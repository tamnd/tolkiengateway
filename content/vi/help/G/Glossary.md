## 0-9 {#0-9}

<dl><dt>+1</dt>
<dd>A comment made to indicate agreement with the previous statement. Frequently used on <a href="#T">talk pages</a>.</dd></dl>

## Một {#A}

<dl><dt>Admin, or Sysop, or Mod</dt>
<dd>An <a href="../../project/A/Administrators.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Administrators">administrator</a>. Administrators are experienced editors of <a href="../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md" title="Tolkien Gateway">Tolkien Gateway</a> who are able to exercise certain technical abilities (such as deleting pages) whilst also being responsible for various &#34;housekeeping&#34; chores on the wiki. They are always happy to help, so please <a href="../../project/A/Administrators.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Administrators">get in touch with them</a> if you feel that you need assistance. <i>See: <a href="../../project/A/Administrators.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Administrators">Tolkien Gateway:Administrators</a>.&#39;</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Anon</dt>
<dd>An anonymous user. As they don&#39;t have a username, their edits show up under their IP address.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>AOTB</dt>
<dd>(The) Adventures of Tom Bombadil</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Article</dt>
<dd>An encyclopaedia entry. All articles are pages, but not all pages are articles.</dd></dl>

## B {#B}

<dl><dt>Blanking</dt>
<dd>Removing all of the content of an article. This differs from deletion, as the article link remains valid.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Ban (also block)</dt>
<dd>An action taken by an admin to prevent an IP or username from editing Tolkien Gateway. Usually exercised for spam or vandalism.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Bot</dt>
<dd>A controlled programme that makes automated or semi-automated edits to the wiki. <i>See: <a href="../../project/B/Bots.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Bots">Tolkien Gateway:Bots</a></i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Broken link</dt>
<dd><i>See <a href="#R">Redlink</a>.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Broken redirect</dt>
<dd>A page that redirects to another page which doesn&#39;t exist. Broken redirects should be avoided at all costs.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Bureaucrat</dt>
<dd>An administrator who has the ability to modify user rights. <i>See: <a href="../../project/B/Bureaucrats.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Bureaucrats">Tolkien Gateway:Bureaucrats</a>.</i></dd></dl>

## C {#C}

<dl><dt><a href="../../main/C/Canon.md" title="Canon">Canon</a></dt>
<dd>The <a href="../../main/L/Legendarium.md" title="Legendarium">legendarium</a> as defined by <a href="../../main/J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>. Anything created by, for example, the <a href="../../main/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a> would likely fall under the realms of <a href="#F">fanon</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Cat</dt>
<dd>Short for category. Every article is put into one or more categories so they can be easily found by users; categories are always listed at the bottom of the page. For instance, this article is in <a href="../../category/H/Help.md" title="Category:Help">Category:Help</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Claimed</dt>
<dd>An article that has been claimed by an editor means that they are temporarily working on an article and request that no one else edit it until they&#39;ve finished their work. <i>See <a href="../../category/C/Claimed.md" title="Category:Claimed">Category:Claimed</a>.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Cleanup</dt>
<dd><i>See <a href="../../category/C/Cleanup.md" title="Category:Cleanup">Category:Cleanup</a>.</i></dd></dl>

## Đ {#D}

<dl><dt>Dead-end page</dt>
<dd>A page that contains no links to other pages.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Del</dt>
<dd>Short for &#34;delete&#34;.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Delink</dt>
<dd>To remove a link on a page. Usually done to remove a <a href="#R">redlink</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Defaultsort</dt>
<dd>By default, MediaWiki software sorts articles by their name. To over-ride that (for example, to sort <i><a href="../../main/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> in &#34;L&#34; rather than &#34;T&#34;). Commonly seen in edit summaries when the defaultsort template has been added (<b>{{DEFAULTSORT:}}</b>).</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Diff</dt>
<dd>Short for &#34;difference.&#34; Used on RecentChanges and page histories to show the difference between two versions of an article.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Disambiguation (or disambig)</dt>
<dd>Resolving the conflict between two or more subjects with identical or similar titles. <i>See our <a href="../../project/N/Naming_policy.md#Disambiguation" title="Tolkien Gateway:Naming policy">disambiguation</a> policy.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Disambiguation page</dt>
<dd>A page solely intended to link to different pages with similar titles. <i>See <a href="../../category/D/Disambiguation.md" title="Category:Disambiguation">Category:Disambiguation</a> for a full list.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Double redirect</dt>
<dd>Where an article redirects to another redirect. These should be avoid as they break after the first redirect. <i>See <a href="#B">broken redirect</a>.</i></dd></dl>

## E {#E}

<dl><dt>Edit conflict</dt>
<dd>When two editors try to change a page at the same time.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Editor, or user, or gatekeeper</dt>
<dd>People who have signed-up to <a href="../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md" title="Tolkien Gateway">Tolkien Gateway</a> to edit the wiki.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Edit summary</dt>
<dd>A small message that can be made when an editor makes an edit. It will be visible in recent changes and on the page&#39;s history. These are to be used for briefly describing the edits made and strongly encouraged.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Edit war</dt>
<dd>When two or more editors undo each other edits for disagreement.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Extensions</dt>
<dd>Additions to the <a href="#M">MediaWiki</a> software which customise and extend the functionality. <i>See <a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Special:Version" title="Special:Version">Special:Version</a> and <a href="../../project/T/Technical_To-do.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Technical To-do">Tolkien Gateway:Technical To-do</a>.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>External link</dt>
<dd>A link to a webpage not on <a href="../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md" title="Tolkien Gateway">Tolkien Gateway</a>. The opposite of an <a href="#I">internal link</a>.</dd></dl>

## F {#F}

<dl><dt>FA</dt>
<dd>Featured article. <i>See <a href="../../project/F/Featured_articles.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Featured articles">Tolkien Gateway:Featured articles</a>.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt><a href="../../main/F/Fanon.md" title="Fanon">Fanon</a></dt>
<dd>Content not created by <a href="../../main/J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>. This content is not allowed on Tolkien Gateway except if it is derived from a licensed adaptation; in which case, it can be included in a &#34;Portrayal in adaptations&#34; section.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>FQ</dt>
<dd>Featured quotes. <i>See <a href="../../project/F/Featured_quotes.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Featured quotes">Tolkien Gateway:Featured quotes</a>.</i></dd></dl>

## G {#G}

<dl><dt>Gatekeeper</dt>
<dd><i>See <a href="#E">Editor</a>.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>GFDL</dt>
<dd><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_Free_Documentation_License" title="wikipedia:GNU Free Documentation License">GNU Free Documentation License</a>, the license under which all <a href="../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md" title="Tolkien Gateway">Tolkien Gateway</a> articles are published. <i>See <a href="../../project/C/Copyrights.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Copyrights">Tolkien Gateway:Copyrights</a>.</i></dd></dl>

## H {#H}

<dl><dt>History</dt>
<dd>All previous <a href="#R">revisions</a> of a page. Accessed by clicking on &#34;History&#34; at the top-right of the page.</dd></dl>

## Tôi {#I}

<dl><dt>Infobox</dt>
<dd>A large template that goes at the beginning of an article which lists a number of key facts. See <a href="../../category/I/Infobox_templates.md" title="Category:Infobox templates">Category:Infobox templates</a> for the full list.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Internal link</dt>
<dd>A link that goes to another page on <a href="../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md" title="Tolkien Gateway">Tolkien Gateway</a> in contradistinction to an <a href="#E">external link</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>InterWiki</dt>
<dd>A link to another wiki.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Intro</dt>
<dd>Introduction of an article, the first paragraph(s).</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>IPA</dt>
<dd><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Phonetic_Alphabetic" title="wikipedia:International Phonetic Alphabetic">International Phonetic Alphabetic</a>, frequently used to indicate correct pronunciation.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>IRC</dt>
<dd>Internet Relay Chat. <i>See <a href="../../project/C/Chat.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Chat">Tolkien Gateway:Chat</a></i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>ISBN</dt>
<dd><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Standard_Book_Number" title="wikipedia:International Standard Book Number">International Standard Book Number</a>.</dd></dl>

## L {#L}

<dl><dt><a href="../../main/L/Legendarium.md" title="Legendarium">Legendarium</a></dt>
<dd>The entirety of J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s works concerning his imagined world of <a href="../../main/A/Arda.md" title="Arda">Arda</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>License template</dt>
<dd>A license template is a requirement on <i>all</i> files uploading to <a href="../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md" title="Tolkien Gateway">Tolkien Gateway</a>. The template indicates under what grounds Tolkien Gateway is allowed to host the file.</dd></dl>

## M {#M}

<dl><dt><a href="../../main/M/Main_Page.md" title="Main Page">Main Page</a></dt>
<dd>The home page.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>MediaWiki</dt>
<dd>The wiki software that runs Tolkien Gateway. Also, the namespace used for system and interface messages.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Merge</dt>
<dd>Turning two articles into one, usually performed by turning one article into a <a href="#R">redirect</a> and moving the content to the other article. <i>See <a href="../../category/A/Articles_to_be_merged.md" title="Category:Articles to be merged">Category:Articles to be merged</a>.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Move</dt>
<dd>When a page on Tolkien Gateway is moved to a different title, in essence renaming the page. A <a href="#R">redirect</a> is automatically created from the original page name to the new one.</dd></dl>

## N {#N}

<dl><dt>Namespace</dt>
<dd>A designated prefix for a segment of Tolkien Gateway that defines the purpose and jurisdiction of a page. A number of namespaces exist, such as Forum, <a href="#U">User</a> and <a href="#T">Talk</a>. The &#34;Main&#34; namespace, which includes all Tolkien Gateway articles, does not have a prefix. By default, when searching, Tolkien Gateway only searches articles in the Main and File namespaces.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>NOTOC</dt>
<dd>No table of contents.</dd></dl>

## O {#O}

<dl><dt>Orphan</dt>
<dd>A page that has no other pages linking to it.</dd></dl>

## P {#P}

<dl><dt>Page</dt>
<dd>Any individual topic at Tolkien Gateway, regardless of <a href="#N">namespace</a>. <a href="#A">Articles</a> are those pages in the &#34;Main&#34; namespace.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Piped link</dt>
<dd>A piped link uses the | character to allow a link to show up differently than the page it is named after. E.g. [[Frodo Baggins|Frodo]] shows up as <a href="../../main/F/Frodo_Baggins.md" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Portals</dt>
<dd>Pages which serve as a &#34;Main Page&#34; for a particular topic. <i>See <a href="../../category/P/Portals.md" title="Category:Portals">Category:Portals</a>.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Project</dt>
<dd>A collaborative effort to improve certain aspects of Tolkien Gateway. <i>See <a href="../../project/P/Projects.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Projects">Tolkien Gateway:Projects</a>.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Protection</dt>
<dd>An <a href="#A">admin</a> action to restrict the editing of a particular article from the public or other <a href="#E">editors</a>. This is usually done in order to prevent vandalism, or because a page is of such high importance/use that it shouldn&#39;t be changed.</dd></dl>

## R {#R}

<dl><dt>Random page</dt>
<dd>A link on the left-hand side which will take the user to a randomly-selected article. Used by editors to find new things to edit.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Recent changes</dt>
<dd>A location where the most recent edits to the wiki are displayed. Most of the frequent editors regard this as their &#34;homepage&#34; as it shows all the recent activity on the wiki.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Redirect</dt>
<dd>A link that exists only to point to another article. For example, &#34;Imladris&#34; redirects to &#34;<a href="../../main/R/Rivendell.md" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>&#34;. The code to do this is <b>#REDIRECT [[Article name]]</b>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Redlink</dt>
<dd>A link to a page that does not exist. This is discouraged unless you realistically believe the page will be created in the future.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Ref</dt>
<dd>A reference; a footnote. These are incredibly important in asserting the credibility of Tolkien Gateway and its articles. A general rule of thumb is to reference virtually all content on Tolkien Gateway articles. <i>See <a href="../R/References.md" title="Help:References">Help:References</a>.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Relink</dt>
<dd>To change which page a link goes to. Commonly seen in <a href="#E">edit summaries</a>, it is usually performed after a page has been <a href="#M">moved</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Restore</dt>
<dd>An admin power that allows the instant resurrection of a deleted article.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Revert</dt>
<dd><i>See <a href="#U">Undo</a>.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Revision</dt>
<dd>Each version of a page. Every time a page is edited this creates a new revision.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>RFA</dt>
<dd>Requests for adminship. The nomination and voting procedure whereby an editor can become an <a href="../../project/A/Administrators.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Administrators">administrator</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Rollback</dt>
<dd>When an <a href="#A">admin</a> undoes an edit, reverting it back to its prevision revision. Admins can do this with one click and it is commonly performed against <a href="#V">vandalism</a>, <a href="#F">fanon</a> and unsourced statements.</dd></dl>

## S {#S}

<dl><dt>Sandbox</dt>
<dd>A place for you to test edits without putting them in an actual article. Some users ever have their own sandbox as a <a href="#S">subpage</a> of their <a href="#U">user page</a>. <i>See <a href="../../project/S/Sandbox.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Sandbox">Tolkien Gateway:Sandbox</a>.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Sig or Signature</dt></dl>

Văn bản được hiển thị khi gõ **\~\~\~\~**. Bạn nên ký tên vào tất cả các bài đăng của mình trên bất kỳ trang thảo luận hoặc diễn đàn nào. Theo mặc định, điều này tạo ra tên người dùng được liên kết với trang người dùng của bạn và dấu thời gian. Chữ ký tùy chỉnh có thể được thực hiện tại "Tùy chọn của tôi" bằng cách chọn tùy chọn "Chữ ký tùy chỉnh" và nhập mã của riêng bạn. *See [Help:Signatures](../S/Signatures.md).*

<dl><dt>Skin</dt>
<dd>The appearance theme of Tolkien Gateway, selected via <a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Special:Preferences" title="Special:Preferences">Special:Preferences</a>. The current default theme is &#34;Mithilien&#34;.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Stub</dt>
<dd>A very short article. These articles need to be expanded. <i>See <a href="../../category/S/Stubs.md" title="Category:Stubs">Category:Stubs</a>.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Subpage</dt>
<dd>A page associated with an article, usually as a subdivision of the subject in question.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Sysop</dt>
<dd><i>See <a href="#A">Admin</a>.</i></dd></dl>

## T {#T}

<dl><dt>Talk page</dt>
<dd>A page set aside for the discussion of an article or page.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Template</dt>
<dd>A piece of code that is essentially an abbreviation for a much larger piece of more complicated code. When in &#34;edit mode&#34;, templates are denoted by curly braces. Hundreds of these are used on Tolkien Gateway, and all of them are located in <a href="../../category/T/Templates.md" title="Category:Templates">Category:Templates</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>TOC</dt>
<dd>Table of contents.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Tolkien Gateway (TG)</dt>
<dd>The world&#39;s best, and largest, Tolkien encyclopaedia.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Tweak</dt>
<dd>A minor edit.</dd></dl>

## U {#U}

<dl><dt>Undo</dt>
<dd>Changing an article back to a previous revision. Anyone can do this.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Userbox</dt>
<dd>Small boxes that display some information about the user; they are used to express an <a href="#E">editor</a>&#39;s personality and individuality. <i>See <a href="../../project/U/Userboxes.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Userboxes">Tolkien Gateway:Userboxes</a>.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>User page</dt>
<dd>Each user&#39;s personal page in the User <a href="#N">namespace</a>; for example, KingAragorn&#39;s user page is at <a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:KingAragorn" title="User:KingAragorn">User:KingAragorn</a>. Editing other people&#39;s user pages is discouraged.</dd></dl>

## V {#V}

<dl><dt>Vandal</dt>
<dd>Someone who deliberately tries to undermine <a href="../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md" title="Tolkien Gateway">Tolkien Gateway</a>. They will be <a href="#B">banned</a>.</dd></dl>

## W {#W}

<dl><dt>Wiki</dt>
<dd>Any website, usually encyclopaedic in nature, that allows easy creation and editing of content. Wikipedia is the most well-known example.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Wikicode</dt>
<dd>The programming language of MediaWiki website, including <a href="../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md" title="Tolkien Gateway">Tolkien Gateway</a>. <i>See <a href="../E/Editing.md" title="Help:Editing">Help:Editing</a>.</i></dd></dl>
