<td>#2
</td>
<td><a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Amroth" title="User:Amroth">Amroth</a>
</td>
<td>How should we structure our <a href="../../main/L/Lego.md" title="Lego">Lego</a> articles? A few possibilities:
<ol><li>One article</li>
<li>Articles about <i>Lego The Lord of the Ring</i> and <i>Lego The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</i> (and a general article?)</li>
<li>Same as above, but with articles about individual sets</li></ol>
</td>
<td>The decision to tone down the old emphasis on collectibles was reaffirmed. Lego, for the time being, would have one article. If, in future, Lego video games were released, these would merit their own and separate articles.
</td></tr>
<tr style="font-size:100%; text-align: center; background-color:#E4F2D9;">
<td>#3
</td>
<td><a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Mith" title="User:Mith">Mith</a>
</td>
<td>What do we do about artists we can&#39;t get hold of?
</td>
<td>It was agreed that a fairuse template (with words akin to &#34;If you&#39;re the copyright owner of this image, please contact X@tolkiengateway.net ...&#34; included therein) should be added to all images by artists we could not get hold of.
</td></tr>
<tr style="font-size:100%; text-align: center; background-color:#E4F2D9;">
<td>#4
</td>
<td><a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Mith" title="User:Mith">Mith</a>
</td>
<td>Do we want to link in with <a class="external text" href="http://www.lotrproject.com" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">LOTR Project</a> somehow? (Not sure how, but every character on there links to a TG article, so might be nice to reciprocate).
</td>
<td>Opinion was mixed, but many agreed that we should link to them somehow. But the &#34;how&#34; was not decided upon. The only thing that was agreed was that an article should be made for the website.
</td></tr>
