## Tóm tắt {#Summary}

Thư của [Christopher Tolkien](../../main/C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) gửi [Findegil](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Findegil)

<div class="poem">
<p>Dear Mr. Livesey,<br/>
  Thank you very much for your letter. The difficulty with the explanation you propose is that following the sentence in question, &#39;If Gandalf is only looking for Frodo, Trotter will have to be an old associate&#39;, my father wrote: &#39;Thus if a Hobbit, make him one who went off under Gandalf&#39;s influence.&#39; This seems to me to show that he meant that Trotter would have to be an old associate of Gandalf&#39;s, not, as you suggest, Frodo&#39;s.<br/>
  In haste,<br/>
  Yours sincerely<br/>
  Christopher Tolkien
</p>
</div>
