## Tóm tắt {#Summary}

Vua Hyarmendacil II của Gondor

Tengwar dòng chữ: **Vinyarion / Hyarmencadil II / Canta Yunquea Aran Ondonóreo** (Vinyarion Hyarmendacil II, thứ hai mươi bốn Vua Gondor)

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Hyarmendacil II, born with the name Vinyarion, was the twenty-fourth King of Gondor. Vinyarion ascended the throne after his father Aldamir was killed in a battle with the Harad and the Corsairs of Umbar in T.A. 1540. Eleven years later, in T.A. 1551, he defeated the Men of the Harad and took the name Hyarmendacil II., meaning &#34;South-victor&#34;.
</p>
