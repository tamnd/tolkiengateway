## Tóm tắt {#Summary}

Người hỗ trợ [Hobbit](../../main/H/Hobbits.md) [Đăng nhanh](../../main/Q/Quick_Post.md) - *[Mithril Miniatures](../../main/M/Mithril_Miniatures.md)*<br>Mô tả sản phẩm:

<div class="poem">
<p>The quickpost, as its name suggests, was a mail delivery service operating exclusively in the shire. It was organised under the auspices of the mayor at Michel Delving.<br/>
<br/>
Only 100 of these figures are produced at this time. This is the 206th release in the Gold Status Fellowship club. This was the winning idea from May 2023 and was submitted by Fellowship member &#34;cjbowser&#34;. This is the nineteenth fully UV resin release, undercoated with matt grey primer. There are three pieces, the base, the two standing Hobbits and the Quickpost rider. This is 32mm scale. The base has the manufacturer name MITHRIL, the sculptor&#39;s name and creation date and MZ number on the sides of the base.
</p>
</div>
