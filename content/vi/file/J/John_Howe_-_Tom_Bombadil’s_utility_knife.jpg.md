<p>Concept art of <a href="../../main/T/Tom_Bombadil.md" title="Tom Bombadil">Tom Bombadil</a>&#39;s utility knife for a <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Syzygy_Forge&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Syzygy Forge (page does not exist)">Syzygy Forge</a> statue.

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Obviously, <a href="../../main/T/Tom_Bombadil.md" title="Tom Bombadil">Tom</a> has little need for a weapon, but he may still want something handy for peeling an apple now and then. The blade isn’t meant to feel like a conventional weapon. There’s a subtle elegance to it, but it doesn’t quite resemble the craftsmanship of <a href="../../main/E/Elves.md" title="Elves">Elves</a> or <a href="../../main/D/Dwarves.md" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a> either. It feels fitting for someone as ancient and mysterious as Tom.
</p>
