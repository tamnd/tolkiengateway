## Tóm tắt {#Summary}

<table class="wikitable" style="width: 100%; direction: ltr;" cellpadding="4">
<tbody><tr style="vertical-align: top">
<th scope="row" id="fileinfotpl_desc" class="fileinfo-paramfield" style="background:var(--tg-color-surface-5)">Description<span class="summary fn" style="display:none">Donato Giancola - J.R.R.Tolkien Portrait.jpg</span></th>
<td class="description">
<p>This portrait represents an idealized version of <a href="../../main/J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>. Tolkien is depicted as he would have looked during the 1950s or 60s, around the time <i><a href="../../main/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> was published. According to <a class="external text" href="http://www.donatoart.com/gallery/jrrtolkien.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">the artist&#39;s description</a>, he is seen inside a <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../main/S/Smial.md" title="Smial">Smial</a>, its round window with a view to a forested nature outside, identifiable as the <a href="../../main/P/Party_Field.md" title="Party Field">Party Field</a> with the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../main/P/Party_Tree.md" title="Party Tree">Party Tree</a>; he is sitting on a chair bearing the <a href="../../main/L/Livery_of_the_heirs_of_Elendil.md" title="Livery of the heirs of Elendil">livery of the heirs of Elendil</a>; he is <a href="../../main/S/Smoking.md" title="Smoking">smoking</a> a pipe whose smoke transforms into an <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../main/E/Eagle.md" title="Eagle">Eagle</a>, while he is working on some manuscripts and drawings; part of the <i><a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Doors_of_Durin.jpg.md" title="File:J.R.R. Tolkien - Doors of Durin.jpg">Doors of Durin</a></i> can be seen, while the drawing he is holding is identifiable as <i><a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Bilbo_comes_to_the_Huts_of_the_Raft-elves_(II).jpg.md" title="File:J.R.R. Tolkien - Bilbo comes to the Huts of the Raft-elves (II).jpg">Bilbo comes to the Huts of the Raft-elves</a></i>. Some papers are empty, which according to the artist, represent the unfinished <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../../main/S/Silmarillion.md" title="Silmarillion">Silmarillion</a></i>.
</p><p>On his library, early covers of <i><a href="../../main/T/The_Hobbit.md" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i> and <i><a href="../../main/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> are visible. On the coffee table there are two cups of <a href="../../main/T/Tea.md" title="Tea">tea</a> (or coffee) bearing the emblems of <a href="../../main/B/Beren.md" title="Beren">Beren</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../main/L/Luthien.md" title="Luthien">Luthien</a> suggesting that they belong to himself and his wife, <a href="../../main/E/Edith_Tolkien.md" title="Edith Tolkien">Edith Tolkien</a>. On the far left there is a book on <i><a href="../../main/B/Beowulf_(poem).md" title="Beowulf (poem)">Beowulf</a></i>.
</p>
