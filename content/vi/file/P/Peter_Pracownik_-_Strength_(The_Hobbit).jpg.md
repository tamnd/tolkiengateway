&#34;Strength&#34; from <i><a href="../../main/T/The_Hobbit_Tarot.md" title="The Hobbit Tarot">The Hobbit Tarot</a></i><br/><blockquote><p><a href="../../main/T/Thorin.md" title="Thorin">Thorin Oakenshield</a>, leader of <a href="../../main/T/Thorin_and_Company.md" title="Thorin and Company">the Companions</a>, aimed to reclaim <a href="../../main/L/Lonely_Mountain.md" title="Lonely Mountain">the Lonely Mountain</a> from the <a href="../../main/D/Dragons.md" title="Dragons">Dragon</a> <a href="../../main/S/Smaug.md" title="Smaug">Smaug</a>. He was haughty and stern in some ways, but had great reserves of courage and strength, which enabled him to fulfill his mission. His especial desire was to reclaim <a href="../../main/A/Arkenstone.md" title="Arkenstone">the Arkenstone</a>. He was the only one of the Dwarves not taken by <a href="../../main/W/William,_Tom,_and_Bert.md" title="William, Tom, and Bert">the Stone-Trolls</a>, and the first of them to be taken by <a href="../../main/E/Elves_of_Mirkwood.md" title="Elves of Mirkwood">the Wood Elves</a>. Always a valiant fighter, he stood by <a href="../../main/G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> in the tunnels of <a href="../../main/G/Great_Goblin.md" title="Great Goblin">the Great Goblin</a>, and of course, at <a href="../../main/B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md" title="Battle of Five Armies">the Battle of the Five Armies</a>. He was furious with <a href="../../main/B/Bilbo_Baggins.md" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo</a> for giving the Arkenstone to the besiegers of <a href="../../main/L/Lake-men.md" title="Lake-men">Men</a> and Elves. Thorin recovered the ancient Elven blade <a href="../../main/O/Orcrist.md" title="Orcrist">Orcrist</a> (Biter) from the Stone-Trolls, then lost it to the Wood Elves. Upon his death he was buried with the Arkenstone in his casket and the sword Orcrist upon it.</p></blockquote>
