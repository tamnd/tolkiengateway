<p>I was thrilled to be asked to paint Smaug, my favourite dragon in all of the fantasy genre. I loved the design of Smaug and his lair, and I had a lot of fun injecting my interpretation of his personality into his pose and expression. These pieces were a fun challenge - as so much of the composition is the environment, much of my attention went into creating a hoard that he would be proud of. I particularly love the way he&#39;s sinking into the gold in this illustration, his coiled form a reference to Tolkien&#39;s own illustration.&#34;
