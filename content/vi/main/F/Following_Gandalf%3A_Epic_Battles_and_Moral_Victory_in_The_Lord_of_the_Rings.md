<i><b>Tiếp theo Gandalf: Những trận chiến lịch sử và chiến thắng đạo đức trong The Lord of the Rings</b></i>, được viết bởi [Matthew Dickerson](../M/Matthew_Dickerson.md), là một cuốn sách về các tác phẩm [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) và đạo đức.

## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>While the success of J. R. R. Tolkien&#39;s Lord of the Rings is remarkable, it&#39;s certainly no mystery. In a culture where truth is relative and morality is viewed as &#34;old-fashioned,&#34; we eagerly welcome the message of these tales: we have free will, our choices matter, and truth can be known. Matthew Dickerson investigates the importance of free will and moral choices in Tolkien&#39;s Middle Earth, where moral victory, rather than military success, is the &#34;real&#34; story. He explores Christian themes throughout, including salvation, grace, and judgment. Following Gandalf will delight veteran Tolkien fans and offer new fans an impressive introduction to his major works. Engaging and theologically thought-provoking, it will interest pastors, students, seminarians, and layreaders.
</p><p><br/>
</p>
</blockquote>
