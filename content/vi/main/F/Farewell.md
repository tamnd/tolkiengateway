<i><b>Farewell</b></i> là ca khúc thứ tám và cũng là ca khúc cuối cùng của album [Let Mortal Heroes Sing Your Fame](../L/Let_Mortal_Heroes_Sing_Your_Fame.md) của ban nhạc [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md).

## Lời bài hát {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Who can find you clear springs of waters, but I can!<br/>
Who can tell you the age of the moon, but I can!<br/>
Who can call the fish from the depths of the sea, yes I can!<br/>
Who can change the shapes of the hills and the headlands, I can!<br/>
<br/>
I have been a sword in the hand,<br/>
I have been a shield in a fight,<br/>
I have been the string of a harp,<br/>
I can shift my shape like a god.<br/>
<br/>
Farewell, leave the shore to an ocean wide and untamed<br/>
Hold your shield high, let the wind bring your enemy your nightmare<br/>
By the bane of my blade, a mighty spell is made and<br/>
Far beyond the battle blood shall fall like a hard rain.
</p>
</div>
