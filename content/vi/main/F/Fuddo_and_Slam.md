<i><b>Fuddo and Slam</b></i> is a Text-Adventure video game designed by [John Wilson](../J/John_Wilson.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Shaun G. McClure](../S/Shaun_G._McClure.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Spectrum_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Spectrum-2">[2]</a></sup> The game is a parody based on *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup> and is a sequel to *[An Everyday Tale of a Seeker of Gold](../A/An_Everyday_Tale_of_a_Seeker_of_Gold.md)* and forms a part of *[The Balrogian Trilogy](../T/The_Balrogian_Trilogy.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Spectrum_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Spectrum-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Câu chuyện {#Story}

Sau khi *Bulbo* đánh bạc mất chiếc nhẫn của mình, bạn bè của ông *Fuddo* và *Slam* bắt tay vào hành trình tìm lại chiếc nhẫn.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup>
