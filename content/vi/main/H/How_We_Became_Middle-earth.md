<i><b>Chúng ta đã trở thành như thế nào Trung Địa</b></i> là cuốn sách thứ mười ba trong bộ *[Cormarë Series](../C/Cormarë_Series.md)* của Cây biết đi. Không giống như hầu hết các tập khác, nó không phải là tuyển tập các bài tiểu luận mà là tác phẩm của Adam Lam và <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Nataliya_Oryshchuk&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nataliya Oryshchuk (page does not exist)">Nataliya Oryshchuk</a>.

## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Following the release in <a href="../_/2001.md" title="2001">2001</a> of the first film of <a href="../P/Peter_Jackson.md" title="Peter Jackson">Peter Jackson</a>&#39;s <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)">adapted trilogy</a> of <a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>&#39;s <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i>: <i><a href="../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Fellowship of the Ring">the Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>, a wave of &#34;Ring Fever&#34; swamped the world, with reprints of the novel, guidebooks, <a href="../../category/W/Websites.md" title="Category:Websites">Internet sites</a>, memorabilia and toys, <a href="../../category/G/Games.md" title="Category:Games">video and computer games</a>, location tours and extended DVDs. Taking a Cultural Studies perspective, this collection of essays examines the cultural issues generated by Tolkien&#39;s novel and Jackson&#39;s films. In particular, by applying a variety of cultural, media and literary theories, the essays in this collection attempt to answer the question: How did we become <a href="../M/Middle-earth.md" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a>? Topics covered range from fan culture in an age of IT, globalization, transnational capitalism and consumerism to the local socio-political implications of the Rings tale, and the formation of a Middle-earth in our real (or, as argued by the French philosopher Jean Beadrillard, our no-longer real but hyperreal) world.This book includes a total of twenty-four chapters, as well as foreword, index, filmography and photo illustrations. It is suitable for broad audience, and can be used for educational and academic purposes.
</p>
</blockquote>
