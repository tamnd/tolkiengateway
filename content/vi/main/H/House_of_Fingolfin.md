**Gia tộc Fingolfin** là những hậu duệ và người đi theo [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md), con trai thứ hai của [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md). Họ nắm giữ [Ngôi vị Đại vương của người Noldor](High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) trong giai đoạn cuối của [Kỷ Đệ Nhất](../F/First_Age.md).

## History {#History}

Fingolfin was accounted [High King of the Noldor](High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) after [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) bestowed the Kingship to him, in gratitude for his son [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) rescuing him from [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md) and as compensation for the acts of [his father](../F/Fëanor.md) against him.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the early years of the [Exile of the Noldor](../E/Exile_of_the_Noldor.md), the House of Fingolfin held the lands to the northwest of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md): [Hithlum](Hithlum.md), [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) and [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). Of these, the city of Gondolin held until [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [510](../F/First_Age_510.md), but Hithlum was overrun by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), where Fingon was slain (Fingolfin had already met his end in the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The remnant of the House, including Fingon's son [Ereinion Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), were succoured by [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) in the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md).

After the Nirnaeth, [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) son of [Huor](Huor.md) was sent as a messenger to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), and there wedded Turgon's daughter [Idril](../I/Idril.md). Thus was the blood of [Men](../M/Men.md) introduced into the House, and the descendants of their line were named half-elven, given the choice of whether they were to considered [Men](../M/Men.md) or [Elves](../E/Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>All these descendants chose to be considered among the [Firstborn](../F/Firstborn.md), except [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md)'s brother [Elros](../E/Elros.md) (who became the first King of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) and was a direct ancestor of [Aragorn Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md)), and Elrond's daughter [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) (Queen to Aragorn at the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md)).

After the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), the survivors of the House lived as exiles at the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md) or in the [Falas](../F/Falas.md), until [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md), descendant of Fingolfin's second son Turgon, sailed into the West to seek the aid of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), who overthrew Morgoth in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) in c. [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=First_Age_583&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="First Age 583 (page does not exist)">583</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The House of Fingolfin was probably no more as an entity; the only known descendants were the two [Half-elven](Half-elven.md) sons of [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md); [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) and [Elros](../E/Elros.md), the patriarch of the [Mannish](../M/Men.md) [House of Elros](House_of_Elros.md) and the [Kings of Númenor](../K/Kings_of_Númenor.md).

## Các phiên bản khác của truyền thuyết {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Vị Đại Vương cuối cùng của người Noldor, Ereinion Gil-galad, vẫn ở lại Trung Địa. Mặc dù trong bản *Silmarillion* đã xuất bản, ông được ghi là con trai của Fingon, Christopher Tolkien sau này nhận xét rằng sẽ tốt hơn nếu ông giữ kín thân thế của Gil-Galad trong bản *Silmarillion* đã xuất bản.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
