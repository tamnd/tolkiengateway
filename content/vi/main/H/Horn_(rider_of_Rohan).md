**Horn** là người của [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) trong [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

Horn đã chiến đấu và hy sinh trong [Trận chiến trên cánh đồng Pelennor](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md). Tên ông được ghi nhớ trong bài thơ *[Song of the Mounds of Mundburg](../S/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Từ nguyên {#Etymology}

*Horn* là tên [tiếng Anh cổ](../O/Old_English.md),<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-IE_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IE-3">[3]</a></sup> có nghĩa là "sừng". Nó chứa yếu tố tiếng Anh cổ *horn*<sup id="cite_ref-IE_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IE-3">[3]</a></sup> ("sừng").

## Miêu tả trong các tác phẩm chuyển thể {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2012: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Riders_of_Rohan.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online: Riders of Rohan">Chúa tể của những chiếc nhẫn trực tuyến: Kỵ sĩ Rohan</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Horn is a prominent character in the game and accompanies the player throughout all of their travels in Riddermark. He is the son of the Reeve of <a href="../E/Entwash_Vale.md" title="Entwash Vale">Entwash Vale</a> and the brother of Ingyth, the betrothed of <a href="../T/Théodred.md" title="Théodred">Théodred</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Horn&#39;s ultimate fate depends on a decision made by the player much earlier in the game. During the summoning of the <a href="../G/Grey_Company.md" title="Grey Company">Grey Company</a>, Halros can be persuaded to either journey south to the aid of <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> or stay and perform the very task that Aragorn instructed him to do. If Halros stays in the Shire, Horn dies in the <a href="../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>. However, if Halros is persuaded to go south, he shields Horn from the mortal blow at the cost of his own life. Horrified by the death around him and the sacrifice of a man he didn&#39;t even know, Horn loses his will to go on and abandons his duty. He forsakes his name and, now a broken man, leaves into paths unknown, telling the player to report him as dead.</dd></dl>
