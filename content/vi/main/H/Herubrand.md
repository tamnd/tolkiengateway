**Herubrand** là người của [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) trong [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Lịch sử {#History}

Herubrand có lẽ là một trong những [Kỵ sĩ của Rohan](../R/Rohirrim.md) đã cưỡi ngựa cùng [Vua](../K/King_of_Rohan.md) [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) của họ từ Rohan đến [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md). Ông ta đã chiến đấu và hy sinh trong [Trận chiến trên cánh đồng Pelennor](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup> vào [15 tháng 3](../_/15_March.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>. Tên ông được ghi nhớ trong bài thơ [Bài ca của những gò đất Mundburg](../S/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Từ nguyên {#Etymology}

*Herubrand* là tên [tiếng Anh cổ](../O/Old_English.md), có nghĩa là "thương hiệu thanh kiếm".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Phần tử đầu tiên có nguồn gốc từ *heoru* ("thanh kiếm"). Phần tử thứ hai là *brand* ("firebrand", "ngọn đuốc", ẩn dụ là "thanh kiếm").<sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Miêu tả trong các tác phẩm chuyển thể {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2013: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">Chúa tể của những chiếc nhẫn trực tuyến</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Herubrand comes from the town of Woodhurst in the Stonedeans, a province in western <a href="../R/Rohan.md" title="Rohan">Rohan</a> where many men have a mix of Rohirrim and Dunlending ancestry. His parents are Briant and Reeve Heremond; his wife is named Cynethryth and their son is <a href="Herefara.md" title="Herefara">Herefara</a>. Under the influence of <a href="../S/Saruman.md" title="Saruman">Saruman</a>, in early March <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_3019.md" title="Third Age 3019">3019</a> a conspiracy of men attacks the Reeve&#39;s family in the Mead Hall of Woodhurst. Heremond and Cynethryth are slain but Herubrand, who is wounded, is saved by his loyalists who hide him and his mother outside the city. Unaware of how many men are involved in the conspiracy, the men loyal to the new Reeve bar the doors of the Mead Hall and keep the attack secret from the people. Herubrand and his advisors enlist the player, an outsider who could not have been involved the attack, to help them in uncovering the conspiracy and eventually take back the control of his city. <a href="Herefara.md" title="Herefara">Herefara</a> is not present during these events, as he is leading the defence of the village of Brockbridge to the south from the forces of <a href="../I/Isengard.md" title="Isengard">Isengard</a>.</dd>
<dd>Herubrand and Herefara are later met in <a class="mw-redirect" href="../D/Druadan_Forest.md" title="Druadan Forest">Druadan Forest</a> during the march of Rohirrim towards <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>. During the <a href="../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a> the father and son fight side by side, but are slain by <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../G/Gothmog.md" title="Gothmog">Gothmog</a>&#39;s orcs.</dd></dl>
