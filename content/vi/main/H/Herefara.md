**Herefara** là người của [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) trong [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

## Lịch sử {#History}

Herefara đã chiến đấu và hy sinh trong [Trận chiến trên cánh đồng Pelennor](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md). Tên ông được ghi nhớ trong bài thơ [Bài ca của những gò đất Mundburg](../S/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Từ nguyên {#Etymology}

*Herefara* là tên [tiếng Anh cổ](../O/Old_English.md), có nghĩa là "người dẫn chương trình"<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup> hoặc "người dẫn chương trình lang thang"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>. Nó chứa các yếu tố tiếng Anh cổ *here* ("quân đội", "chủ nhà", trong tên cá nhân với nghĩa là "chiến binh") và *fara* ("người đi đường", "khách du lịch").<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Miêu tả trong các tác phẩm chuyển thể {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2013: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">Chúa tể của những chiếc nhẫn trực tuyến</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Herefara comes from the town of Woodhurst in the Stonedeans, a province in the western <a href="../R/Rohan.md" title="Rohan">Rohan</a> where many men have a mix of Rohirrim and Dunlending ancestry. His grandparents are Briant and Reeve Heremond; and his parents are Cynethryth and <a href="Herubrand.md" title="Herubrand">Herubrand</a>. Under the influence of <a href="../S/Saruman.md" title="Saruman">Saruman</a>, in early March <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_3019.md" title="Third Age 3019">3019</a> a conspiracy of men attacks the Reeve&#39;s family in the Mead Hall of Woodhurst. Heremond and Cynethryth are slain but Herubrand, who is wounded, is saved by his loyalist who hide him and his mother outside the city. They enlist the player, an outsider who could not have been involved the attack, to help them in uncovering the conspiracy and eventually take back the control of his city. Herefara is not present during these events, as he is leading the defence of Brockbridge, a village ruled by <a href="Háma.md" title="Háma">Háma</a>&#39;s father Léoferth to the south from the forces of <a href="../I/Isengard.md" title="Isengard">Isengard</a>.</dd>
<dd>Herubrand and Herefara are later met in the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../D/Druadan_Forest.md" title="Druadan Forest">Druadan Forest</a> during the march of Rohirrim towards <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>. Herefara joins the player in patrolling the lands around beacons <a href="../N/Nardol.md" title="Nardol">Nardol</a> and <a href="../E/Eilenach.md" title="Eilenach">Eilenach</a>. During the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../B/Battle_of_Pelennor_Fields.md" title="Battle of Pelennor Fields">Battle of Pelennor Fields</a> the father and son fight side by side, but are slain by <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../G/Gothmog.md" title="Gothmog">Gothmog</a>&#39;s orcs.</dd></dl>
