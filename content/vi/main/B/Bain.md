**Bain** là [Vua xứ Dale](../K/King_of_Dale.md), vị vua thứ hai của dòng dõi được khôi phục.

## Lịch sử {#History}

Bain là con trai của [Bard the Bowman](Bard.md), và kế vị cha mình vào [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2977](../T/Third_Age_2977.md). Ông cai trị những vùng đất phía nam [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) trong ba mươi năm, trong thời gian đó [Balin](Balin.md) và [một nhóm](Balin's_folk.md) [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) rời [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) để tái chiếm [Moria](../M/Moria.md).

Ông được con trai mình là [Brand](Brand.md) kế vị vào [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3007](../T/Third_Age_3007.md); người ta nói rằng vương quốc của Brand trải dài xa về phía nam và phía đông của [Esgaroth](../L/Lake-town.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Từ nguyên {#Etymology}

Vì [Tiếng Dale](../L/Language_of_Dale.md) được thể hiện bằng [Tiếng Bắc Âu cổ](../O/Old_Norse.md), nên cái tên này có lẽ đại diện cho *Beinn*, có nghĩa là “người di chuyển”.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Hình tượng trong các bản chuyển thể {#Portrayals_in_adaptations}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d1/The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg/100px-The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="100" height="154" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d1/The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg/150px-The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d1/The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg/200px-The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg 2x" data-file-width="280" data-file-height="432"/></a><figcaption>Bain in <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a></figcaption></figure>

<b>2013: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>While in <a href="../C/Canon.md" title="Canon">canon</a> there is no mention about Bain&#39;s birth year, and there is nothing to indicate that he was alive during the events of <i>The Hobbit</i>, Bain appears in the <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>Hobbit</i> films</a> at age of about 13 and has two sisters, older <a href="../S/Sigrid.md" title="Sigrid">Sigrid</a> and younger <a href="../T/Tilda.md" title="Tilda">Tilda</a>. He is played by <a href="../J/John_Bell.md" title="John Bell">John Bell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Bell_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bell-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Bain assists his father <a href="Bard.md" title="Bard">Bard</a> in smuggling <a href="Bilbo_Baggins.md" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a> and <a href="../T/Thorin_and_Company.md" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and Company</a> into their house. When the <a href="../D/Dwarves.md" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a> see a windlance outside their window, they recall the <a href="../S/Sack_of_Erebor.md" title="Sack of Erebor">Sack of Erebor</a>, and Bain relates the story of how <a href="../G/Girion.md" title="Girion">Girion</a> managed to knock one of <a href="../S/Smaug.md" title="Smaug">Smaug</a>&#39;s scales loose in the attack, though the Dwarves don&#39;t believe him. Just before being arrested, Bard instructs Bain to hide the last <a href="Black_arrow.md" title="Black arrow">black arrow</a> he has in his possession. When <a href="Bolg.md" title="Bolg">Bolg</a>&#39;s <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">Orc</a> pack attacks <a href="../L/Lake-town.md" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a>, Bain assists <a href="../L/Legolas.md" title="Legolas">Legolas</a>, <a href="../T/Tauriel.md" title="Tauriel">Tauriel</a>, <a href="../F/Fíli.md" title="Fíli">Fíli</a>, <a href="../K/Kíli.md" title="Kíli">Kíli</a>, <a href="../_/Óin.md" title="Óin">Óin</a>, and <a href="Bofur.md" title="Bofur">Bofur</a> in fighting off the Orcs, and protects his sisters the best he can.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bain, Sigrid, and Tilda follow Tauriel and the Dwarves to evacuate Lake-town via boat while Smaug is destroying it. When he sees Bard making a last stand against the <a href="../D/Dragons.md" title="Dragons">Dragon</a>, Bain goes off to find the boat in which he hid the black arrow, and manages to deliver it to his father atop the bell tower. Bard constructs a large makeshift bow from the tower to fire the arrow at Smaug, using his son&#39;s shoulder to balance and aim the arrow. Bard urges him to stay calm as he fires the shot that ultimately kills the <a href="../F/Fire-drakes.md" title="Fire-drakes">fire-drake</a>. Later, as the <a href="Battle_of_Five_Armies.md" title="Battle of Five Armies">Battle of Five Armies</a> is getting underway, he and his sisters go into hiding in the ruins of <a href="../D/Dale.md" title="Dale">Dale</a> until the battle is over.</dd></dl>
