**Bữa sáng thứ hai** là bữa ăn của [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) ở [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) vào buổi sáng ông khởi hành [Quest of Erebor](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Roast_Mutton_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roast_Mutton-1">[1]</a></sup> Đó có thể là một trong sáu bữa ăn mỗi ngày của [Shire-hobbit](Shire-hobbits.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<b>2001: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">Chúa tể của những chiếc nhẫn: Hiệp hội những chiếc nhẫn</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>As <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Strider</a> leads the <a href="../T/Travellers.md" title="Travellers">Travellers</a> to <a href="../R/Rivendell.md" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>, the <a href="../H/Hobbits.md" title="Hobbits">Hobbits</a> begin to unpack their cooking supplies. Strider tells them that they won&#39;t stop until night. <a href="../P/Peregrin_Took.md" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a> asks when they will take breakfast and Strider reminds him that they&#39;ve already eaten it. Pippin replies &#34;Well, we&#39;ve had one, yes. What about second breakfast?&#34;. Strider turns around and continues walking. <a href="../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a> says &#34;Don&#39;t think he knows about second breakfast, Pip&#34;.</dd></dl>
