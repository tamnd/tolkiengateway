**Song Of The Ngọn Núi Cô Đơn** là một bài hát do [Howard Shore](../H/Howard_Shore.md) viết và [Neil Finn](../N/Neil_Finn.md) trình bày, có trong phần ghi công cuối của *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md)*. Đây cũng là bài hát thứ mười hai trong đĩa thứ hai của cả *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack.md)* và *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack - Special Edition](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack_-_Special_Edition.md)* (ở dạng mở rộng).

## Lời bài hát {#Lyrics}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<div class="poem">
<p>Far over the <a href="../M/Misty_Mountains.md" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a> rise<br/>
Leave us standing upon the heights<br/>
What was before, we see once more<br/>
Our <a href="../K/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain.md" title="Kingdom under the Mountain">kingdom</a> a distant light<br/>
<br/>
Fiery mountain beneath the <a href="../M/Moon.md" title="Moon">moon</a><br/>
The words unspoken, we&#39;ll be there soon<br/>
For home a song that echoes on<br/>
And all who find us will know the tune<br/>
<br/>
Some folk we never forget<br/>
Some kind we never forgive<br/>
Haven&#39;t seen the back of us yet<br/>
We&#39;ll fight as long as we live<br/>
All eyes on the hidden door<br/>
To the <a href="../L/Lonely_Mountain.md" title="Lonely Mountain">Lonely Mountain</a> borne<br/>
We&#39;ll ride in the gathering storm<br/>
Until we get our long-forgotten gold<br/>
<br/>
We lay under the Misty Mountains cold<br/>
In slumbers deep and dreams of gold<br/>
We must awake, our lives to make<br/>
And in the darkness a torch we hold<br/>
<br/>
From long ago when lanterns burned<br/>
Till this day our hearts have yearned<br/>
Her fate unknown the <a href="../A/Arkenstone.md" title="Arkenstone">Arkenstone</a><br/>
What was stolen must be returned<br/>
<br/>
We must awake and make the day<br/>
To find a song for heart and soul<br/>
<br/>
Some folk we never forget<br/>
Some kind we never forgive<br/>
Haven&#39;t seen the end of it yet<br/>
We&#39;ll fight as long as we live<br/>
All eyes on the hidden door<br/>
To the Lonely Mountain borne<br/>
We&#39;ll ride in the gathering storm<br/>
Until we get our long-forgotten gold<br/>
Far away from Misty Mountains cold
</p>
</div>
