## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>World-building in J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s Works, its Precursors, and Legacies J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s literary cosmos may not be the most elaborate of the imaginary worlds in existence, it is certainly the most influential. The posthumous editorial work of Tolkien&#39;s son Christopher has also shown that Arda remains unrivalled in its consistency and complexity. Additionally, the re-publication of Tolkien&#39;s Andrew Lang lecture &#39;On Fairy-stories&#39; (originally delivered 1939) and its interpretation within the discourse of literary fantasy has further strengthened his position as one of the foremost proponents of literary world-building or, as he himself preferred to call it, (literary) subcreation.<br/>The contributions to this volume by Tom Shippey, John Garth, Mark J.P. Wolf, Kristine Larsen, Andrew Higgins, Allan Turner, Gergely Nagy, Renée Vink, and a dozen other scholars, discuss not only Tolkien&#39;s theoretical concepts as well as his literary work but also explore the relationship between Tolkien’s approach with that of other &#39;literary world-builders&#39; whose imaginary worlds have attracted readers and scholars alike.
</p>
</blockquote>
