<i><b>Shadows of Mordor</b></i>, or <i><b>Shadows of Mordor Software Adventure</b></i> in North-America, is a video game that covers part 4 of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the sequel to *[Lord of the Rings: Game One](../L/Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Game_One.md)* and the precursor to *[Crack of Doom](../C/Crack_of_Doom_Software_Adventure.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> It is part of the *[Tolkien Trilogy](../T/Tolkien_Trilogy.md)*, also known in North-America as [The Tolkien Software Adventure Series](../T/The_Tolkien_Software_Adventure_Series.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Fiction_Database_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fiction_Database-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The game was designed by [Philip Mitchell](../P/Philip_Mitchell.md) and [John Haward](../J/John_Haward.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Fiction_Database_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fiction_Database-3">[3]</a></sup>
