<i><b>Nhật ký Tolkien 2004</b></i> là một cuốn nhật ký trống có hình minh họa của [Ted Nasmith](Ted_Nasmith.md).

## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The annual Tolkien diary, this year containing all-new artwork depicting scenes based on the third book of J.R.R. Tolkien’s The Lord of the Rings<br/><br/>This sumptuous week-to-view desk diary features 13 brand new paintings from Ted Nasmith, acclaimed Tolkien jacket artist and creator of the illustrated Silmarillion. The paintings are all based on the third volume of Tolkien’s classic masterpiece, The Lord of the Rings, and coincides with the release of the second movie from New Line Cinema, guaranteeing greater popularity than ever before.
</p>
</blockquote>
