The **Third Deep** was a level in [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md) that had treasuries and armouries. It was below the [East Gate](../G/Great_Gates.md) of Moria. During [Balin](../B/Balin.md)'s short [recolonization](../B/Balin's_Colony~64118.md) of Moria, he sent [Óin](../_/Óin.md) to find an access to it.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
