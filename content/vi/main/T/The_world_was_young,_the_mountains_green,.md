**Thế giới còn trẻ, núi xanh,** thường được gọi là **Bài hát của Durin**,<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> là một bài thơ được [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) hát khi [Đoàn Hộ Nhẫn](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) đi qua [Moria](../M/Moria.md). Gimli đã đứng dậy hát bài hát này khi nhớ lại sự huy hoàng của [Dwarrowdelf](../D/Dwarrowdelf.md). [Sam Gamgee](../S/Sam_Gamgee.md) thích bài hát này đến nỗi ông muốn học nó.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Bài thơ {#The_poem}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><audio src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/a/a1/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_The_world_was_young%2C_the_mountains_green%2C.mp3" width="300" class="mw-file-element" style="max-width: 100%; width: 300px;" controls=""><a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_The_world_was_young,_the_mountains_green,.mp3">https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_The_world_was_young,_the_mountains_green,.mp3</a></audio><figcaption><a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> reads The world was young, the mountains green,</figcaption></figure>

<br><br><br><br><br>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>The <a href="../A/Arda.md" title="Arda">world</a> was young, the <a href="../M/Mountains.md" title="Mountains">mountains</a> green,<br/>
No stain yet on the <a href="../M/Moon.md" title="Moon">Moon</a> was seen,<br/>
No words were laid on stream or stone<br/>
When <a href="../D/Durin_I.md" title="Durin I">Durin</a> woke and walked alone.<br/>
He named the nameless hills and dells;<br/>
He drank from yet untasted wells;<br/>
He stooped and looked in <a href="../M/Mirrormere.md" title="Mirrormere">Mirrormere</a>,<br/>
And saw <a href="../D/Durin&#39;s_Crown.md" title="Durin&#39;s Crown">a crown of stars</a> appear,<br/>
As gems upon a silver thread,<br/>
Above the shadow of his head.<br/>
<br/>
The world was fair, the mountains tall,<br/>
In <a href="../E/Elder_Days.md" title="Elder Days">Elder Days</a> before the fall<br/>
Of mighty kings in <a href="../N/Nargothrond.md" title="Nargothrond">Nargothrond</a><br/>
And <a href="../G/Gondolin.md" title="Gondolin">Gondolin</a>, who now beyond<br/>
The Western Seas have passed away:<br/>
The world was fair in Durin&#39;s Day.<br/>
<br/>
A king he was on carven throne<br/>
In many-pillared halls of stone<br/>
With golden roof and <a href="../S/Silver.md" title="Silver">silver</a> floor,<br/>
And <a href="../R/Runes.md" title="Runes">runes</a> of power upon the door.<br/>
The light of <a href="../S/Sun.md" title="Sun">sun</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="../S/Star.md" title="Star">star</a> and moon<br/>
In shining lamps of crystal hewn<br/>
Undimmed by cloud or shade of night<br/>
There shone for ever fair and bright.<br/>
<br/>
There hammer on the anvil smote,<br/>
There chisel clove, and graver wrote;<br/>
There forged was blade, and bound was hilt;<br/>
The delver mined, the mason built.<br/>
There beryl, pearl, and opal pale,<br/>
And metal wrought like fishes&#39; mail,<br/>
Buckler and corslet, axe and sword,<br/>
And shining spears were laid in hoard.<br/>
<br/>
Unwearied then were <a class="mw-redirect" href="../D/Durin&#39;s_folk.md" title="Durin&#39;s folk">Durin&#39;s folk</a>;<br/>
Beneath the mountains <a class="mw-redirect" href="../M/Music.md" title="Music">music</a> woke:<br/>
The harpers harped, the minstrels sang,<br/>
And at the <a href="../G/Great_Gates.md" title="Great Gates">gates</a> the trumpets rang.<br/>
<br/>
The world is grey, the mountains old,<br/>
The forge&#39;s fire is ashen-cold;<br/>
No harp is wrung, no hammer falls:<br/>
The darkness dwells in Durin&#39;s halls;<br/>
The shadow lies upon his tomb<br/>
In Moria, in <a class="mw-redirect" href="../K/Khazad-dûm.md" title="Khazad-dûm">Khazad-dûm</a>.<br/>
But still the sunken stars appear<br/>
In dark and windless Mirrormere;<br/>
There lies his crown in water deep,<br/>
Till Durin wakes again from sleep.
</p>
</div>

## Các phiên bản khác của truyền thuyết {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Bản thảo trước đó, được trình bày trong *[The Treason of Isengard](The_Treason_of_Isengard.md)*, có một phiên bản khác của bài thơ

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p><a href="../A/Arda.md" title="Arda">The world</a> was young, the <a href="../M/Mountains.md" title="Mountains">mountains</a> green,<br/>
No mark upon <a href="../M/Moon.md" title="Moon">the moon</a> was seen,<br/>
When <a href="../D/Durin_I.md" title="Durin I">Durin</a> came and gave their name<br/>
To lands where none before had been.<br/>
                           nameless lands had been.<br/>
<br/>
<a href="../A/Arda.md" title="Arda">The world</a> was fair, the <a href="../M/Mountains.md" title="Mountains">mountains</a> tall,<br/>
With gold and silver gleamed his hall,<br/>
When <a href="../D/Durin_I.md" title="Durin I">Durin</a>&#39;s throne of carven stone<br/>
Yet stood behind the guarded wall.<br/>
<br/>
<a href="../A/Arda.md" title="Arda">The world</a> is dark, the <a href="../M/Mountains.md" title="Mountains">mountains</a> old,<br/>
In shadow lies the heaped gold;<br/>
In <a href="../D/Durin_I.md" title="Durin I">Durin</a>&#39;s halls no hammer falls,<br/>
The forges&#39; fires are grey and cold
</p>
</div>

Trong số các dòng hoặc câu ghép nửa hình khác là:

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>When <a href="../D/Durin_I.md" title="Durin I">Durin</a> woke and gave to gold<br/>
its first and secret name of old
</p>
</div>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>When <a href="../D/Durin_I.md" title="Durin I">Durin</a> came to <a href="../D/Dimrill_Dale.md" title="Dimrill Dale">Azanûl</a><br/>
and found and named <a href="../M/Mirrormere.md" title="Mirrormere">the nameless pool</a>
</p>
</div>

## Miêu tả trong các tác phẩm chuyển thể {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b><a href="../_/1981.md" title="1981">1981</a>: <a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (loạt phim phát thanh năm 1981)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gimli performs an abbreviated version of he song, omitting the first verse and portions of the second.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2001.md" title="2001">2001</a>-<a href="../_/2003.md" title="2003">2003</a>: <a class="mw-redirect" href="../P/Pán_prsteňov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series).md" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (Loạt đài phát thanh Slovakia 2001-2003)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gimli (portrayed by Vladimír Hajdu) sings the Song of Durin during the Fellowship&#39;s journey through Moria, as in the novel. The lyrics of the song are not entirely identical with the original English lyrics (especially when it comes to references to First Age events and locations), but follows the structure and ideas of the original lyrics closely. Gimli presents the poem in a half-reciting, half-singing manner. Its slow and melancholy tune is accompanied by flute (the main melody), a subtle sound of a hammer clanging against an anvil in the distance (indicating the rhythm) and occasional string instrument additions (for emphasizing mood).</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2014.md" title="2014">2014</a>: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth%253A_Shadow_of_Mordor.md" title="Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor">Trung Địa: Bóng Tối trong số Mordor</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the <i>Big Game</i> quest, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Torvin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Torvin (page does not exist)">Torvin</a> sings the first stanza, except for &#34;As gems upon a silver thread&#34;, which he hums.</dd></dl>
