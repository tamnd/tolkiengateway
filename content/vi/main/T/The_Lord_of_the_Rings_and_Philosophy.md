<i><b>Chúa tể của những chiếc nhẫn và triết học: Một cuốn sách thống trị tất cả</b></i> là tập hợp các bài viết được viết bởi các giáo sư triết học về [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) và các tác phẩm của ông, được xuất bản trong bộ sách *Popular Culture and Philosophy*.

## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>&#34;The Lord of the Rings&#34; is intended to be applicable to the real world of relationships, religion, pleasure, pain and politics. Tolkien himself said that his grand tale of wizards, orcs, hobbits and elves was aimed at truth and good morals in the actual world. Analysis of the popular appeal of &#34;The Lord of the Rings&#34; shows that Tolkien fans are hungry for discussion of the urgent moral and cosmological issues arising out of this epic story. Can political power be wielded for good, or must it always corrupt? Does technology destroy the truly human? Is it morally wrong to give up hope? Can we find meaning in chance events? In this volume, 17 young philosophy professors all of then ardent Tolkien fans address some of the issues and show how clues to their solutions may be found in the imaginary world of Middle-earth. The book is divided into five sections, concerned with: the power and the Ring, the quest for happiness, good and evil in Middle Earth, time and mortality and philosophical questions and fairytale endings.
</p>
</blockquote>
