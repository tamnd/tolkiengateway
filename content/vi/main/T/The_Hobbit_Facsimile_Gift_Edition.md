<i><b>Phiên bản Quà tặng Fax Hobbit</b></i> được HarperCollins xuất bản và phát hành tại Vương quốc Anh vào [31 tháng 5](../_/31_May.md) [2018](../_/2018.md). Bộ này bao gồm bản sao chép lại phiên bản đầu tiên của *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)*, một CD chứa các bản ghi lưu trữ J.R.R. Tolkien đọc từ *The Hobbit*, một bản sao [bản đồ của Thror](Thror's_map.md) và một tập sách nhỏ có lịch sử ngắn gọn về *The Hobbit* của [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) & [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md).

## Từ Nhà xuất bản {#From_the_Publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This sumptuous gift set includes a replica of the very rare <a href="The_Hobbit_1st_edition.md" title="The Hobbit 1st edition">first edition</a> of The Hobbit, the only edition where one can now read the original version of the story before Tolkien re-edited it to become the one enjoyed by readers since <a href="../_/1951.md" title="1951">1951</a>.
</p><p>The Hobbit was published on <a href="../_/21_September.md" title="21 September">21 September</a> <a href="../_/1937.md" title="1937">1937</a>, with a print run of 1,500 copies. With a beautiful cover design, nearly a dozen black &amp; white illustrations and two black &amp; red maps by the author himself, the book proved to be popular and was reprinted shortly afterwards. History was already being made.
</p><p>The scarcity of the first edition has resulted in copies commanding huge prices, way beyond the reach of most Tolkien <a class="mw-redirect" href="Tolkien_fandom.md" title="Tolkien fandom">fans</a>. In addition, subsequent changes to the text – particularly those to <a href="../R/Riddles_in_the_Dark.md" title="Riddles in the Dark">chapter 5</a>, when Tolkien decided in <a href="../_/1947.md" title="1947">1947</a> to revise the text to bring it better into accord with events as they were developing in its ‘sequel’, <a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a> – mean that the opportunity to read the book in its original form and format has become quite difficult.
</p><p>This special commemorative gift set includes the first edition, so that readers of all ages – not just ‘children between the ages of 5 and 9’, as <a href="../R/Rayner_Unwin.md" title="Rayner Unwin">Rayner Unwin</a> famously declared in his report on the original submission – can finally enjoy Tolkien’s story as it originally appeared. It also includes an exclusive CD of archive recordings that capture Tolkien reading from The Hobbit, a special copy of Thror’s map which reveals the secret moon-runes when held to the light, and an accompanying booklet that relates the history of The Hobbit, and includes connected writings by JRR Tolkien.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>HarperCollins</cite></p>
</blockquote>
