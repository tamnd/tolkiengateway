<i><b>The Art of</b></i> **The Lord of the Rings** <i><b>của J.R.R. Tolkien</b></i> là một cuốn sách nghệ thuật được biên tập bởi [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) và [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md), xuất bản trên [2015](../_/2015.md).

Tác phẩm tái hiện, với lời bình luận sâu rộng, khoảng 190 hình minh họa (phác họa, bản đồ, tengwar và cirth specimina) của [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) liên quan đến *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.

Trong số các bức minh họa, 101 bức chưa được xuất bản trước đây và 42 bức được xuất bản lần đầu tiên bằng màu.

## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>To celebrate the 60th Anniversary of the publication of The Lord of the Rings, a sumptuous full-colour art book containing the complete collection of almost 200 sketches, drawings, paintings and maps by J.R.R. Tolkien.
</p><p>As he wrote The Lord of the Rings, J.R.R. Tolkien’s mental pictures often found expression in drawing, from rough sketches made within the manuscript to more finished illustrations. Only a few of these were meant for publication; most were aids to help Tolkien conceive his complex story and keep it consistent. Many do not illustrate the final text, but represent moments of creation, illuminating Tolkien’s process of writing and design. In addition to pictorial sketches, numerous maps follow the development of <a href="The_Shire.md" title="The Shire">the Shire</a> and the larger landscape of <a href="../M/Middle-earth.md" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a>, while inscriptions in runes and Elvish script, and ‘facsimile’ leaves from the burned and blood-stained <a href="../B/Book_of_Mazarbul.md" title="Book of Mazarbul">Book of Mazarbul</a>, support Tolkien’s pose as an ‘editor’ or ‘translator’ of ancient records.
</p><p>The Art of The Lord of the Rings by J.R.R. Tolkien collects these drawings, inscriptions, maps, and plans in one deluxe volume. More than 180 images are included, all of them printed in colour from high-quality scans and photographs, more than half not previously published. Wayne G. Hammond and Christina Scull, two of the world’s leading Tolkien scholars, have edited the book and provide an expert introduction and comments. Readers who have enjoyed The Art of The Hobbit by J.R.R. Tolkien, with which the new book is uniform, will find much of interest also in The Art of The Lord of the Rings.
</p>
</blockquote>
