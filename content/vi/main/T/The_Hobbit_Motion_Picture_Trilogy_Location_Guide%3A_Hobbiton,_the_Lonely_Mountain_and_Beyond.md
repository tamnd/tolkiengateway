<i><b>Hướng dẫn địa điểm của bộ ba phim điện ảnh Hobbit: Hobbiton, Ngọn núi cô đơn và xa hơn</b></i> là cuốn sách của [Ian Brodie](../I/Ian_Brodie.md) giới thiệu các địa điểm quay phim trong loạt phim *[The Hobbit](<The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)*.

## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Discover the breathtaking locations around New Zealand featured in Peter Jackson&#39;s The Hobbit films with this lush four-color travel guide from the author of the international bestseller The Lord of the Rings Location Guidebook. With a foreword by Sir Peter Jackson and contributions by Andy Serkis, Jared Connon, and Dan Hennah.
</p><p>The Hobbit Motion Picture Trilogy Location Guide showcases the principal filming locations around New Zealand that are captured in Academy Award-winning filmmaker Peter Jackson&#39;s trilogy based on J. R. R. Tolkien&#39;s popular classic The Hobbit. Filled with stunning color photos, this handy reference includes exclusive background information and anecdotes about the filming from the cast and crew.
</p><p>Produced with the full cooperation of New Line Productions and Warner Bros., this beautiful guidebook also features exclusive movie images and location photographs, specialty maps and directions, GPS references, touring information--including accommodation food and entertainment suggestions--and helpful Internet addresses.
</p><p>With The Hobbit Motion Picture Trilogy Location Guide, fans of Peter Jackson&#39;s cinematic masterpieces can enjoy their own imaginative adventures and experience the magic and complexity of Hobbiton and Middle-earth.
</p>
</blockquote>
