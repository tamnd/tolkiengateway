<i><b>Những con thú của Tolkien: Sách tô màu</b></i> là <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coloring_book#Adult_coloring_books" title="wikipedia:Coloring book">sách tô màu dành cho người lớn</a>, được minh họa bởi Mauro Mazzara và Andrea Piparo. Các hình ảnh minh họa dựa trên bài viết của [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AMUK_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AMUK-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This mystical colouring book presents an abundance of famous scenes and characters inspired by Tolkien&#39;s <a href="../M/Middle-earth.md" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a>. From <a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> the Grey to the humble <a href="../H/Hobbits.md" title="Hobbits">Hobbits</a>, take time off from the busy pace of life and indulge your imagination while exploring the enthralling world of <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> and other tales. There&#39;s something new on every page as you discover the intricate elvish designs, fearsome characters and breath-taking landscapes - perfect to satisfy <a href="../F/Fandom.md" title="Fandom">Tolkien fans</a> and beyond. This book is unofficial and not authorised by the <a href="Tolkien_Estate.md" title="Tolkien Estate">Tolkien Estate</a> or <a href="../H/HarperCollins.md" title="HarperCollins">HarperCollins</a> Publishers.<sup id="cite_ref-AMUK_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AMUK-1">[1]</a></sup>
