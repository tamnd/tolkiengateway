<i><b>The Hobbit Đồng hành</b></i> là cuốn sách được viết bởi [David Day](../D/David_Day.md), xuất bản lần đầu vào năm [1997](../_/1997.md).

## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Illustrated in full colour (illustrations by Lidia Postma), including a number of double page prints, this is an intriguing and interesting investigation into the world of words used by <a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">Tolkien</a> in <i><a href="The_Hobbit.md" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i>. It explores the clever web of wordplay Professor Tolkien used to create hidden meanings and delves into the mythical associations that lie beneath the tale&#39;s thrilling surface.
</p><p>Starting with the word &#34;hobbit&#34; itself, the book moves on to examine Bilbo Baggins, hobbit heritage and history, Gandalf, Shire Society, and much more.
</p><p>There is also a chapter on the hobbit characters from <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i>, including <a href="../F/Frodo_Baggins.md" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a>, <a href="../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md" title="Samwise Gamgee">Sam</a>, <a href="../P/Peregrin_Took.md" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a> and <a href="../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a>. Lidia Postma’s painting of the <a href="../P/Party_Field.md" title="Party Field">Party Field</a> from <i><a href="The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Fellowship of the Ring">The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i> is a treasure – it pictures and names 60 of the guests from Adelard Took to Willibald Bolger.
</p>
</blockquote>
