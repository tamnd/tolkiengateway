Lịch <i><b>Tolkien 2021</b></i> là lịch Tolkien chính thức được xuất bản bởi [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md) vào [1 tháng Chín](../_/1_September.md) [2020](../_/2020.md). Nó có tác phẩm nghệ thuật của [John Howe](../J/John_Howe.md), [Alan Lee](../A/Alan_Lee.md) và [Ted Nasmith](Ted_Nasmith.md) gắn liền với việc xuất bản ấn bản minh họa kỷ niệm 40 năm *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*của [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The official Tolkien calendar, this year containing twelve stunning paintings inspired by the stories collected in <i>Unfinished Tales</i> by J.R.R. Tolkien. Three of the world’s finest Tolkien artists present the Three Ages of <a href="../M/Middle-earth.md" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a> in breath-taking glory.<br/><br/>The official Tolkien calendar has become an established publishing event, eagerly anticipated by Tolkien fans the world over. Previous Tolkien calendars have become much sought after collector’s items, and this year’s will doubtless be an essential purchase for Middle-earth fans everywhere, as it celebrates the 40th anniversary of <i>Unfinished Tales</i>.<br/><br/>To commemorate this special occasion, three of the world’s finest Tolkien artists – Ted Nasmith, John Howe, and Alan Lee – have each contributed four full-colour artworks to the Calendar. To accompany each painting, every month features complementary pencil drawings or sketches selected by the artists which are exclusive to this calendar.<br/><br/>In this unique artistic combination, Alan, John and Ted present scenes from the <a href="../F/First_Age.md" title="First Age">First Age</a> of <i><a href="The_Silmarillion.md" title="The Silmarillion">The Silmarillion</a></i>, the <a href="../S/Second_Age.md" title="Second Age">Second Age</a> of <a href="../N/Númenor.md" title="Númenor">Númenor</a> and the fall of <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>, and the <a href="Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">Third Age</a> of <i><a href="The_Hobbit.md" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i> and <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i>, which will take the reader deeper into the world of Middle-earth than ever before.
</p>
</blockquote>
