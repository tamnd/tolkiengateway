<i><b>Tolkien's Oxford</b></i> là cuốn sách [2008](../_/2008.md) của [Robert S. Blackham](../R/Robert_S._Blackham.md). Nó hiển thị các hình ảnh, bản đồ và bản vẽ về [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md) như [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) đã sống ở đó.

## Từ Nhà xuất bản {#From_the_Publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The buildings and people of Oxford were a valuable source of information for <a href="The_Hobbit.md" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a> and <a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a>. This absorbing book charts Tolkien&#39;s life in Oxford from <a href="../_/1911.md" title="1911">1911</a> to <a href="../_/1973.md" title="1973">1973</a>, using old postcards, maps and photographs to paint a picture of the places and times that relate to one of the leading authors of the twentieth century. The book covers such places as <a href="The_Eagle_and_Child.md" title="The Eagle and Child">The Bird and Baby</a>, where the <a href="../I/Inklings.md" title="Inklings">Inklings</a> met to read passages from their work; the Old Ashmolean Building on Broad Street where Tolkien worked on the <a href="../O/Oxford_English_Dictionary.md" title="Oxford English Dictionary">Oxford English Dictionary</a>; the University Botanical Gardens opposite Magdalen College, which contained Tolkiens favourite tree under which to ponder; and the <a href="../W/Wolvercote_Cemetery.md" title="Wolvercote Cemetery">Wolvercote Cemetery</a> where Tolkien was finally buried, with <a href="../E/Edith_Tolkien.md" title="Edith Tolkien">his wife</a>, in <a href="../_/1973.md" title="1973">1973</a>. Presented in a handy guide-book format, this is an essential companion for anyone wanting to find the places that influenced Tolkien and his work.
</p>
</blockquote>
