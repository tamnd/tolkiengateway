**Bộ sưu tập âm thanh J.R.R. Tolkien** là tập hợp các bản ghi âm của [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), cũng như [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) được phát hành dưới dạng [2001](../_/2001.md) trên CD.

## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>For generations, J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s words have brought to thrilling life a world of hobbits, magic, and historic myth, woken from its foggy slumber within our minds. Here, he tells the tales is his own voice.<br/><br/>
Of historic note, these selections from <a href="The_Hobbit.md" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a> and <a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a> are based on a tape recording Tolkien made in <a href="../_/1952.md" title="1952">1952</a>, which inspired him to continue his own quest to see his vision in print. Also included is a never-published poem, &#34;<a href="The_Mirror_of_Galadriel.md" title="The Mirror of Galadriel">The Mirror of Galadriel</a>,&#34; originally intended for inclusion in the trilogy, yet edited out. And, finally, Tolkien&#39;s son, <a href="../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md" title="Christopher Tolkien">Christopher</a>, reads selections from his father&#39;s <a href="The_Silmarillion.md" title="The Silmarillion">The Silmarillion</a>, the epic foundation upon which rests the whole of his work.
</p>
</blockquote>
