<i><b>Thế giới của J.R.R. Tolkien: Những nơi truyền cảm hứng Trung Địa</b></i> là một cuốn sách [2020](../_/2020.md) của [John Garth](../J/John_Garth.md). Trong cuốn sách, Garth dựa trên nghiên cứu của riêng mình về cuộc đời và các bài viết của Tolkien, tài liệu chưa được xuất bản trước đây từ các bài báo Tolkien tại [Thư viện Bodleian](../B/Bodleian_Library.md)và các bản tóm tắt nghiên cứu của [Tolkien học giả](Tolkien_studies.md) và [người hâm mộ](Tolkienists.md) khác.

## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This book takes you to the places that inspired J. R. R. Tolkien to create his fictional locations in <i>The Lord of the Rings</i>, <i>The Hobbit</i>, and other classic works. Written by renowned Tolkien expert John Garth, <i>The Worlds of J. R. R. Tolkien</i> features a wealth of breathtaking illustrations, including Tolkien’s own drawings, contributions from other artists, rare archival images, and spectacular color photos of contemporary locations across Britain and beyond, from the battlefields of World War I to Africa.<br/><br/>Garth identifies the locales that served as the basis for Hobbiton, the elven valley of Rivendell, the Glittering Caves of Helm’s Deep, and many other settings in Middle-earth, from mountains and forests to rivers, lakes, and shorelands. He reveals the rich interplay between Tolkien’s personal travels, his wide reading, and his deep scholarship as an Oxford don. Garth draws on his profound knowledge of Tolkien’s life and work to shed light on the extraordinary processes of invention behind Tolkien’s works of fantasy. He also debunks popular misconceptions about the inspirations for Middle-earth and puts forward strong new claims of his own.<br/><br/>An illustrated journey into the life and imagination of one of the world’s best-loved authors, <i>The Worlds of J. R. R. Tolkien</i> provides a unique exploration of the relationship between the real and the fantastical and is an essential companion for anyone who wants to follow in Tolkien’s footsteps.
</p>
</blockquote>
