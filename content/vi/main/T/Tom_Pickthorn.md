**Tom Pickthorn** was a [man of Bree](../M/Men_of_Bree.md). He was killed in early January, [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md), fighting [Bill Ferny](../B/Bill_Ferny.md) and [Sharkey](../S/Sharkey.md)'s [ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md). [Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md) referred to him as "Little Tom Pickthorn" so he must have been shorter than [Mat Heathertoes](../M/Mat_Heathertoes.md) and [Rowlie Appledore](../R/Rowlie_Appledore.md), two other men of Bree who were killed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
