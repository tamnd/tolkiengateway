<i><b>The Road Goes Ever On và Bật: Bản đồ Trung Địa</b></i> của Tolkien là một cuốn sách bản đồ [2009](../_/2009.md) của [Brian Sibley](../B/Brian_Sibley.md) và [John Howe](../J/John_Howe.md), bao gồm [Trung Địa](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Đây là ấn bản quà tặng bìa cứng của *[The Map of Tolkien's Middle-earth](The_Map_of_Tolkien's_Middle-earth.md)* (1995).

## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The essential companion for any reader journeying through The Lord of the Rings, illustrated by John Howe, conceptual artist on the Lord of the Rings films. Writer and broadcaster Brian Sibley is a foremost expert on The Lord of the Rings (he adapted the novel for the award-winning BBC radio dramatisation in 1980), and here in this clothbound hardback presents an entertaining and informative overview of the writing of The Lord of the Rings, and the creation of the original maps by Tolkien and his son, Christopher.<br/><br/>
</p><p>The book is accompanied by a full-colour illustrated map, which can be removed for reference or even for framing. The map is a real labour of love, illustrated by world-renowned Tolkien artist John Howe, the conceptual artist employed by Peter Jackson to work on his multi-award winning Lord of The Rings film trilogy, and who is soon to work on Guillermo del Toro&#39;s Hobbit film. Each element in this collector&#39;s package is special; together they provide an enchanting and desirable artefact that will be a prized possession of Tolkien readers of all ages.
</p>
</blockquote>
