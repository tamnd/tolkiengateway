<i><b>The Open Door to Poetry: An Anthology</b></i> is a collection of poetry, first published in [1931](../_/1931.md). The anthology includes [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s (acknowledged as "[J.R.R. Tolkein](../J/J.R.R._Tolkein.md)") poem "[Goblin Feet](../G/Goblin_Feet.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
