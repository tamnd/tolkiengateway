**Alcarin** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md): "(glorious"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or "the Glorious", pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈalkarin]</span>)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> was an epithet of [Atanatar II](Atanatar_II.md), who ruled [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Atanatar_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Atanatar-3">[3]</a></sup> from [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1149](../T/Third_Age_1149.md) until [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1226](../T/Third_Age_1226.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> in a time when its wealth and power were legendary<sup id="cite_ref-Atanatar_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Atanatar-3">[3]</a></sup>. <span></span>
