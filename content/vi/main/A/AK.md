**AK** là một [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [gốc](../S/Sundocarme.md) biểu thị "hẹp, hạn chế".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Akr%C4%81&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Akrā (page does not exist)">akrā</a></i>
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[arka](Arca.md)* ("hẹp")
    - [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Agor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Agor (page does not exist)">agor</a></i> (< [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *agr*)

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[aksa](Aksa.md)* ("đường hẹp, khe núi")
- [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Aglond&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aglond (page does not exist)">Aglond</a></i> > *Aglon* ("làm ô uế, vượt qua giữa những bức tường cao"; cũng là một tên riêng)
