**Ardaranye** was the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name for "the **Kingdom of [Arda](Arda.md)**" under the guardianship and headship of [Manwë Súlimo](../M/Manwë.md), the Elder King of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), which is [Ambar](Ambar.md#Ambar) 'the Earth' as a whole planet (including the [Blessed Realm](Aman.md) until its [removal](../C/Changing_of_the_World.md) from the [circles of the world](../C/Circles_of_the_World.md) after the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3319](../S/Second_Age_3319.md)) together with all the celestial objects nearest to it, including the [Moon](../M/Moon.md), the [Sun](../S/Sun.md), and even some '[stars](../S/Stars.md)'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Nói cách khác, *Ardaranye* chính xác là tương đương với toàn bộ <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_System" title="wikipedia:Solar System">Hệ Mặt trời</a></b>.
