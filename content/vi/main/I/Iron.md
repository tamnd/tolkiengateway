Sắt là kim loại được sử dụng phổ biến trong [Trung Địa](../M/Middle-earth.md), mặc dù nó có lẽ được sử dụng phổ biến nhất trong [Vương miện Sắt](Iron_Crown.md). Trong [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) nó được gọi là [ang](../A/Ang.md).

Cổng [Cao Hay](../H/High_Hay.md) có song sắt dày dặn.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Các [Orc của Isengard](../O/Orcs_of_Isengard.md) đội mũ sắt.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Miêu tả trong các tác phẩm chuyển thể {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1994: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="../T/Treasures_of_Middle-earth_(2nd_edition).md" title="Treasures of Middle-earth (2nd edition)">Kho báu của Trung Địa</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Much is made of the purity of iron in the book. Pure iron is silver-white and easiest to work with, though it is rare even <a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Moria</a>, while more common varieties are darker and stronger. The Dwarves considered anything that&#39;s “virtually pure” iron to be iron. The Elves primarily used iron to make <a href="../S/Steel.md" title="Steel">steel</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Barrow-iron ore can be mined by journeyman prospectors in <a href="../C/Cardolan.md" title="Cardolan">Cardolan</a>, <a href="../B/Blue_Mountains.md" title="Blue Mountains">Ered Luin</a>, <a href="../T/The_Shire.md" title="The Shire">the Shire</a>, <a href="../B/Bree-land.md" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a>, the <a href="../L/Lone-lands.md" title="Lone-lands">Lone-lands</a>, and the <a href="../N/North_Downs.md" title="North Downs">North Downs</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Rich iron ore can be mined by expert prospectors in the Lone-lands, the North Downs, <a href="../L/Lake_Evendim.md" title="Lake Evendim">Evendim</a>, Bree-land, and <a href="../T/The_Yondershire.md" title="The Yondershire">the Yondershire</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Dwarf-iron ore can be mined by artisan prospectors in the <a href="../T/Trollshaws.md" title="Trollshaws">Trollshaws</a>, the <a href="../M/Misty_Mountains.md" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a>, <a href="../A/Angmar.md" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>, Evendim, and the Lone-lands.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Ancient iron ore can be mined by master prospectors in the Misty Mountains, Angmar, <a href="../F/Forochel.md" title="Forochel">Forochel</a>, <a href="../E/Eregion.md" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>, and Bree-land.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>
