**Ismael Enrique Cruz Córdova** là một diễn viên người Puerto Rico. Anh ấy đã đóng vai [Arondir](../A/Arondir.md) trong loạt phim *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* của Amazon.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Trích dẫn {#Quotes}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I had quite the harrowing journey to get this role. My desire to be part of the Tolkien world started when I was 14, and I definitely wanted to be an elf. I was struck down, people saying things like, &#34;Elves don&#39;t look like you&#34; and that kind of thing, so it was something I pursued pretty aggressively. When the casting calls came out, I knew it was something I must do. I had a couple of rejections in the process; they said the role wasn&#39;t going to go in my direction. But I kept fighting and fighting and fighting, something like six or seven months of auditions. And I finally made it to the final screen test, where they flew me to New Zealand with six other guys, and I got the part.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Ismael Cruz Córdova, Cnet</cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

</p>
