<b>&lt;blessing&gt;</b> 20-21st century archivists cannot provide item-level control of many collections.  They&#39;re simply to big!  Literary manuscripts are the exception.   That&#39;s why I&#39;m able to tell you exactly how many pages we have for each book.  The financial value requires us to have this kind of intellectual control.  As a result, literary manuscripts are the most expensive collections to preserve.<br/>
<b>&lt;blessing&gt;</b> MU is a medium size academic archives.   We have more than 25,000 cubic feet of archival records.  The JRRT manuscripts account for 12-13 c.f.  Just an example.<br/>
