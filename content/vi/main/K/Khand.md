**Khand** là tên của một vùng đất nằm ở phía đông nam của [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> và phía đông của [Gần Harad](../N/Near_Harad.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> là nơi sinh sống của [Variags](../V/Variags.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Pelennor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pelennor-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Lịch sử {#History}

Người ta biết rất ít về Khand hoặc người dân ở đó, ngoài việc họ liên minh với Mordor.

Người dân Khand và [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md) dường như đã từng là kẻ thù của nhau qua nhiều thời đại. [Những người Phục sinh](../E/Easterlings.md) được gọi là [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) đã đi qua phía nam Mordor và liên minh với những người ở Khand và [Gần Harad](../N/Near_Harad.md). Trong [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md) họ đã thực hiện một cuộc tấn công phối hợp chống lại [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Nhiều năm sau, Variags từ Khand gia nhập lực lượng của [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) trong [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Pelennor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pelennor-3">[3]</a></sup>

Không biết liệu Khand có từng bị [Vương quốc Thống nhất](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md) chinh phục hay nó vẫn độc lập. Người ta cũng không biết Khand có từng gây chiến với người phương Tây sau cái chết của [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) hay không.

## Từ nguyên {#Etymology}

Ý nghĩa của *Khand* không được ghi lại. Nó cùng với *[Variag](../V/Variag.md)* là một trong số ít từ được biết đến trong ngôn ngữ của Người phương Đông và các đồng minh của Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

*Khand* có nghĩa là "vương quốc" hoặc "đất" trong một số ngôn ngữ liên quan ở Nam Á.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Miêu tả trong các tác phẩm chuyển thể {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1984-1997: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Nhập vai Trung Địa</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Khand is a region on a semi-arid plateau between the southeastern arm of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../E/Ephel_Duath.md" title="Ephel Duath">Ephel Duath</a> and the northwestern arm of the Ered Harmal. In the north of Khand is the Gap of Khand, a hundred mile wide pass between those two mountain chains. The region of Nûrad is in the hills northeast of the northern end of the Ered Harmal.<sup id="cite_ref-Khand_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Khand-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Lower Khand, the south-western part of Khand, is hot, dry, and windy with scrub and desert. It is inhabited by horse herders who moved between springs, seasonal pools, and intermittent streams. The only permanent are located along the broad, shallow Noz Peka river (meaning &#34;Knife River&#34; in <a href="../L/Languages_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md#Varadja" title="Languages in Middle-earth Role Playing">Varadja (V.)</a>), a tributary of the <a href="../H/Harnen.md" title="Harnen">Harnen</a> river. The traditional capital city of Lower Khand and largest city of Khand is Sturlurtsa Khand lies at the confluence of the Noz Peka with the with the Medlóshad Peka (meaning &#34;Gold-horse river&#34; in V.). Sturlurtsa Khand is the home of the powerful Ûrpof and Oléna clans.<sup id="cite_ref-Khand_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Khand-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Upper Khand, the north-eastern part of Khand, has cooler temperatures and a bit more fainrall. The tribes of Upper Khand were more numerous, generally richer and more settled than the tribes of Lower Khand. The capital city of Upper Khand is Ûbésêsh-ûta-Pavéter (meaning &#34;Refuge from the Flailing Wind&#34; in V.) lies 170 miles east and upstream from Sturlurtsa Khand. The city receives double the amount of rain than Sturlurtsa Khand, but is smaller. Ûbésêsh-ûta-Pavéter is the home of the Irbo clan. The second city of Upper Khand is Lâorki. Lâorki is the home of the Achef clan. Most of the Variag kings came from Lâorki. <a href="../N/Ninth_of_the_Nazgûl.md" title="Ninth of the Nazgûl">Ûvathar Achef</a>, the greateat conqueror of the line of kings from clan Achef became the ninth of the <a href="../N/Nazgûl.md" title="Nazgûl">Ringwraiths</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Khand_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Khand-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In a mountain spur in the southeastern part of the Ephel Duath are the Caves of Ôlbamarl, vast caverns that wind up and down through the mountain spur. Two permanent towns and seven shrines lie in the Caves of Ôlbamarl. The bones of many Variag kings are entombed in the Caves of Ôlbamarl. The successors of the Variag kings strip the meat from and eat the fallen Variag kings.<sup id="cite_ref-Khand_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Khand-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>A continental map of Middle-earth shows the course of the Noz Peka river with a dot for a principal urban area south of the river near its headwaters (probably the city Ûbésêsh-ûta-Pavéter) and a dot for a principal urban area north of the river further downstream (probably the city Sturlurtsa Khand).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> A map of the northwest of Middle-earth shows the courses of the rivers of Khand. The longest river that flows from the east to the west is probably the Noz Peka and the smaller short river that flows from the northeast to the southwest to join the Noz Peka approximately at the location where the more western dot for the principal ubran area is on the other map is probably the Medlóshad Peka river.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Most of Khand is in a temperature zone with a mean annual temperature of 70 to 80 degrees fahrenheit. The north of Khand along the eastern part of the Ephel Duath is in a temperature zone with a mean annual temperature of 60 to 70 degrees fahrenheit.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Khand ist in a precipitation zone with 25 to 50 cm of precipitartion per year.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> A major highway that functions as a trade route runs parallel to the Noz Peka river on its northern side.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> Khand, the area northwest and northeast of the Gap of Khand (with the exception of Mordor) and the area southeast of Khand were part of the Ringwraith Ûvatha &#39;s Empire of the Kingdom of Khand with a circle indicating the location of Ûvatha&#39;s capital on the western end of the Gap of Khand.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The seven Variag confederations speak Varadja and have a common culture that reveres horses. The Variags are superb riders and warriors and are withouth peer when fighting as cavalry.<sup id="cite_ref-Khand_9-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Khand-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The part of the Ephel Duath that reaches from eastward from Harondor to the plateau of Khand is called Auducar Nurn (&#34;Red wall of Nurn&#34;) in Haradaic. Most of the good passes over it are located in its eastern part.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> The Auducar Nurn is called Caradhram Nurn in Sindarin.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The region of Lurmsakûn (meaning &#34;Basin of flowing waters&#34; in Haradaic) bordering on the plateau of Khand and the foothills of the Ered Harmal is ruled by Haruze lords, but during most of the Third Age control over the region was contested between the Haruze lords of Lurmsakûn and the kings of the Variags and the kings of the Chey.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup></dd></dl>
