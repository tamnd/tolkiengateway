**Gothmog** là trung úy của [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup> Có thể Gothmog là chỉ huy thứ hai sau [Chúa tể Nazgûl](../W/Witch-king.md) hoặc ít có khả năng là chỉ huy thứ hai của quân đội Minas Morgul.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Gothmog cũng là tên của [Chúa tể Balrogs](<Gothmog_(balrog).md>) từ [Thời đại đầu tiên](../F/First_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Lịch sử {#History}

Vào [ngày 15 tháng 3](../M/March_15.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> sau khi Chúa tể Nazgûl bị [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) giết chết trong [Trận chiến trên cánh đồng Pelennor](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Gothmog đã ra lệnh cho lực lượng dự bị bao gồm [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) bằng rìu, [Các biến thể](../V/Variags.md) của [Khand](../K/Khand.md), [Người miền Nam](../H/Haradrim.md) trong trang phục đỏ tươi và những người đàn ông da đen như [nửa quỷ lùn](../H/Half-trolls.md) với mắt trắng và lưỡi đỏ, đã được tích tụ tại thành phố đổ nát [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) để di chuyển vào [Cánh đồng Pelennor](../P/Pelennor_Fields.md) để tấn công kỵ binh của [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) và của [Gondor](Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>

Số phận của Gothmog không bao giờ được xác định. Có thể Gothmog đã bỏ mạng cùng với gần như toàn bộ người hầu của Sauron đã chiến đấu trước cổng [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). Cũng có thể Gothmog đã cùng những người khác chạy trốn khỏi [Ramas Echor](../R/Rammas_Echor.md) và băng qua [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).

Tên *Gothmog* có nguồn gốc từ gốc GOS-/GOTH- ("kinh hãi") và MBAW- ("ép buộc, ép buộc, chủ thể, áp bức") (cũng được tìm thấy trong tiêu đề của Morgoth *Bauglir*: kẻ bạo chúa hoặc kẻ áp bức).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:359, 372</sup>

## Xuất xứ {#Origin}

Hầu như không có gì được biết về Gothmog. Các học giả Tolkien suy đoán rằng Gothmog có thể là một trong những loài sau:

- Một [Người đàn ông](../M/Men.md),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> trong trường hợp này Gothmog có lẽ là một [Người Númenórean da đen](../B/Black_Númenóreans.md) giống như [Miệng Sauron](../M/Mouth_of_Sauron.md)
- Một trong những [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> mặc dù [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) chưa bao giờ đề cập cụ thể đến Gothmog trong số Nazgûl.
- Gothmog có thể là bất cứ thứ gì từ [Orc](../O/Orcs.md) hoặc [Uruk](../U/Uruk-hai.md), (mặc dù sở hữu tên Sindarin) hoặc [Boldog](../B/Boldog.md), một [Maia](../M/Maiar.md) sa ngã trong hình dạng Orc.

<b>1977: <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring_(1977_board_game).md" title="War of the Ring (1977 board game)"><i>War of the Ring</i> (trò chơi board game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gothmog is the second most powerful Nazgûl. He begins the game at Dol Guldur.</dd></dl>

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Nhập vai ở Trung Địa</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gothmog is a <a href="../H/Half-trolls.md" title="Half-trolls">half-troll</a>, a crossbreed of trolls and <a href="../V/Variags.md" title="Variags">Variags</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">Chúa tể của những chiếc nhẫn: Sự trở lại của nhà vua</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gothmog is portrayed as an Orc-general with a misshapen face. He is played by <a href="../L/Lawrence_Makoare.md" title="Lawrence Makoare">Lawrence Makoare</a> and voiced by <a href="../C/Craig_Parker.md" title="Craig Parker">Craig Parker</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> <a href="../P/Peter_Jackson.md" title="Peter Jackson">Peter Jackson</a> wanted to show a hideously deformed orc, one that would aptly convey the &#34;ugliness&#34; of <a href="../M/Mordor.md" title="Mordor">Mordor</a>. The upper left side of Gothmog&#39;s body is swollen and pock-marked from a disease of some sort, as described by the design department. His pale, yellow skin may also be a consequence of this illness. Gothmog&#39;s intelligence is far above that of the common orc and he, understandably, would be the perfect link between the Witch-King and the other planners of the siege of <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> (most likely men in Sauron&#39;s service) and the mindless mass of orc infantry on the front lines. While Gothmog likely did not have the intelligence to design the attack on the White City he certainly would have been able to ensure orders were carried out. Gothmog is clearly a brutal commander, but also an over-confident one. Peter Jackson comments that Gothmog feels powerful with Mordor&#39;s massive army behind him, but in reality is a crippled orc, as seen in his failed dismount from a <a href="../W/Wargs.md" title="Wargs">Warg</a> in the <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)">Extended edition</a>. Gothmog does, however, seem to be a capable warrior, though somewhat inhibited by his crippled left side.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In the midst of the chaos, Gothmog is forced into hand-to-hand combat. He sees a pocket of particularly stiff resistance, primarily from Théoden and <a href="../_/Éowyn.md" title="Éowyn">Éowyn</a>, and decides to fight Éowyn. She is a more skilled fighter than he, however, and soon injures him on his crippled left leg, rendering him essentially unable to walk and useless to Mordor&#39;s army as a whole. After Éowyn has killed the Witch-King, in an act of revenge, Gothmog attempts to kill her with a mace he finds nearby. He is, however, killed just in time by <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> and <a href="Gimli.md" title="Gimli">Gimli</a>. As he is about to strike, Aragorn cuts off his armored right arm, but Gothmog persists and Gimli hits him in the abdomen with his axe. Aragorn then cuts through Gothmog&#39;s armor on the right side with his sword to finally bring the Orc down.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In October 2021, news spread that <a href="../E/Elijah_Wood.md" title="Elijah Wood">Elijah Wood</a> claimed on a podcast that one of the orc masks in the films &#34;was designed to look like <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Weinstein" title="wikipedia:Harvey Weinstein">Harvey Weinstein</a>&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> While Wood did not explicitly refer to Gothmog as portrayed in the movie, Gothmog&#39;s orc mask was widely identified as the orc character in question.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2015: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">Chúa tể của những chiếc nhẫn trực tuyến</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gothmog plays a major role in the game as the chief antagonist of several Epic story-lines and is also combined with the character of <a href="../E/Eärnur.md" title="Eärnur">Eärnur</a>. In the game&#39;s version of events, in <a href="../M/Minas_Morgul.md" title="Minas Morgul">Minas Morgul</a> Eärnur was transformed into a <a href="../W/Wraiths.md" title="Wraiths">wraith</a> named <b>Mordirith</b> in service of the <a href="../W/Witch-king.md" title="Witch-king">Witch-king</a>, who in mockery of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md" title="Stewards of Gondor">Stewards of Gondor</a> made Mordirith his own Steward at <a href="../C/Carn_Dûm.md" title="Carn Dûm">Carn Dûm</a> in <a href="../A/Angmar.md" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>. Long absent from that evil bastion, Mordirith returns to Carn Dûm shortly before <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, launching attacks against the Free Peoples of <a href="../E/Eriador.md" title="Eriador">Eriador</a>. Mordirith is defeated at the end of the game&#39;s original storyline, plunging the other servants of <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> into a civil war over his position before he returns and his identity is revealed at the end of <i>Volume I</i>. He is defeated again, but instead of being sent back to the North he is bestowed upon the name of <b>Gothmog</b> and is ordered to lead the Witch-King&#39;s armies against <a href="Gondor.md" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The player doesn&#39;t meet him again until <a href="../O/Osgiliath.md" title="Osgiliath">Osgiliath</a>, shortly before the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../S/Siege_of_Minas_Tirith.md" title="Siege of Minas Tirith">Siege of Minas Tirith</a>. During the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../B/Battle_of_Pelennor_Fields.md" title="Battle of Pelennor Fields">Battle of Pelennor Fields</a> Gothmog personally kills the Ranger <a href="../H/Halbarad.md" title="Halbarad">Halbarad</a>, but when his master is undone and can no longer bring him back from the <a href="../V/Void.md" title="Void">Void</a>, he suddenly feels fear again and flees the battlefield before <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>. Gothmog barricades himself inside Minas Morgul, refusing even Sauron&#39;s muster to the Black Gate. Though he outlives the Dark Lord&#39;s demise, his other surviving servants blame Gothmog for it and combine their forces to assault of the City of the Dead. This gives the players and a group of <a href="../R/Rangers_of_Ithilien.md" title="Rangers of Ithilien">Rangers of Ithilien</a> the opportunity to sneak inside and challenge Gothmog, delivering a final defeat to him.</dd></dl>

<b>2023: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Tales_of_Middle-earth.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth">Chúa tể của những chiếc nhẫn: Những câu chuyện về Trung Địa</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gothmog is portayed in the <i>Gothmog, Morgul Lieutenant</i> card as a human.</dd></dl>
