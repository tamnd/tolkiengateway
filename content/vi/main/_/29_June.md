## Trên Trái đất {#On_Earth}

- **[1917](1917.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) succumbs to gastritis while at Brocton Camp, and is sent to Brooklands Officers' Hospital in Kingston-upon-Hull.
- **[1935](1935.md)** - Author [Robert Giddings](../R/Robert_Giddings.md) is born.
- **[1944](1944.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 74](../L/Letter_74.md).
- **[1955](1955.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes the Preface to *[The Ancrene Riwle](../T/The_Ancrene_Riwle.md)*.
- **[1955](1955.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 164](../L/Letter_164.md).
- **[1967](1967.md)** - [Gene Deitch](../G/Gene_Deitch.md)'s [adaptation](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1967_film).md>) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is shown to a limited audience in New York City.
- **[1972](1972.md)** - *The Daily Telegraph* describes actions of the Forestry Commission as bringing a "Tolkien gloom". [Tolkien responds](../L/Letter_339.md).
- **[1976](1976.md)** - Actor [Bret McKenzie](../B/Bret_McKenzie.md) is born.
- **[1977](1977.md)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_A_Biography.md)* is published in the US.
