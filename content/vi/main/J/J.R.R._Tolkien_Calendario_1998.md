**J.R.R. Tolkien Lịch 1998** được sản xuất bởi [Hiệp hội Tolkien Ý](../I/Italian_Tolkien_Society.md) và có hình minh họa của 11 nghệ sĩ khác nhau: <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Maura_Boldi&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maura Boldi (page does not exist)">Maura Boldi</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Flavio_Brunetti&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Flavio Brunetti (page does not exist)">Flavio Brunetti</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ambra_Colombrani&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ambra Colombrani (page does not exist)">Ambra Colombrani</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Andreina_D%27Ambrosio&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Andreina D&#39;Ambrosio (page does not exist)">Andreina D'Ambrosio</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Maria_Distefano&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maria Distefano (page does not exist)">Maria Distefano</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Gianfranco_Dutto&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gianfranco Dutto (page does not exist)">Gianfranco Dutto</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Domenico_Franchi&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Domenico Franchi (page does not exist)">Domenico Franci</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Diego_Iaconfcic&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Diego Iaconfcic (page does not exist)">Diego Iaconfcic</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Alessandro_Maccari&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alessandro Maccari (page does not exist)">Alessandro Maccari</a>, [Luca Michelucci](../L/Luca_Michelucci.md) và [Angelo Montanini](../A/Angelo_Montanini.md). Nó có cả phiên bản buộc keo và buộc dây

- Gắn mép trên bằng keo
