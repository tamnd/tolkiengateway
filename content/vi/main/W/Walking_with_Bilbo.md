**Đi cùng Bilbo** là một cuốn sách tâm linh của Sarah Arthur.

## Từ nhà xuất bản {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The author of Walking with Frodo takes readers on an adventure of faith with a new devotional that relates themes from <a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>&#39;s <a href="../T/The_Hobbit.md" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a> to living the <a href="../C/Christianity.md" title="Christianity">Christian life</a>. Unlike the fateful quest <a href="../F/Frodo_Baggins.md" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a> was asked to carry out, <a href="../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a>&#39;s journey came as an unexpected adventure. Readers will be reminded that God chooses us to be unlikely heroes in the adventure of life.
</p>
</blockquote>
