The **Causeway** was the road in the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md) that ran from [Deephallow](../D/Deephallow.md) north through [Rushey](../R/Rushey.md) and [Stock](../S/Stock.md) and ended at the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The road was high banked and ran parallel to the [Brandywine](../B/Brandywine.md) opposite of [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md). [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) rode in [Farmer Maggot](../F/Farmer_Maggot.md)'s wagon on the part of the Causeway that stretched five or more miles from Maggot's lane to the [Bucklebury Ferry](../B/Bucklebury_Ferry.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
