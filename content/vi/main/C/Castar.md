**Castar** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) *Mirian*) là loại tiền tệ chính được sử dụng trong [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md). Đồng xu nhỏ hơn là *[tharni](../T/Tharni.md)* hoặc <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Canath&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Canath (page does not exist)">canath</a></i>, một đồng bạc có giá trị bằng 1/4 *castar*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Từ nguyên {#Etymology}

[Westron](../W/Westron.md) *castar* có sự tương đồng đáng chú ý với từ [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kast&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kast (page does not exist)">kast</a></i> và [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kastu&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kastu (page does not exist)">kastu</a></i>, có nghĩa là "[mathom](../M/Mathom.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Sindarin *mirian* dường như bắt nguồn từ *[mîr](../M/Mîr.md)*, "ngọc quý".

## Miêu tả trong các tác phẩm chuyển thể {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth%253A_Shadow_of_Mordor.md" title="Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor">Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>The Castar, also known as “Gondorian Coin”, was described as being widely used throughout Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Mirian was described as a distinct coin that was once the currency of <a href="../E/Eregion.md" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>. The <a href="../D/Doors_of_Durin.md" title="Doors of Durin">Doors of Durin</a> and the great forge of Khazad-dûm featured on the coins in honour of the Dwarves who provided the <a href="../M/Mithril.md" title="Mithril">mithril</a> to make them. The coins became rare relics after <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> destroyed most of them.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>
