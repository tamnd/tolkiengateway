**Carach Angren** hay **Isenmouthe** là một con đèo ở phía tây bắc [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). Đèo được hình thành nơi các nhánh vươn ra từ các dãy [Ephel Dúath](../E/Ephel_Dúath.md) và [Ered Lithui](../E/Ered_Lithui.md) gặp nhau, chỉ để lại một lối đi hẹp giữa [Cao nguyên Gorgoroth](../P/Plateau_of_Gorgoroth.md) và thung lũng nhỏ hơn [Udûn](<../U/Udûn_(valley).md>) ở phía bắc.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Diện mạo {#Appearance}

Khi đi từ Gorgoroth đến Udûn, Carach Angren được phòng thủ kiên cố, và cả những mỏm đá nhìn ra nó đều có pháo đài và tháp. Bên kia lối đi, một bức tường đất đã được xây dựng và một con mương lớn đã được đào ngang qua khoảng trống được bắc qua bởi một cây cầu duy nhất.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Một hàng rào lớn bằng những cột sắt nhọn đã thu hẹp khoảng trống dẫn vào Udûn.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Từ nguyên {#Etymology}

Cả *Carach Angren* và *Isenmouthe* đều có nghĩa là "Miệng sắt": "*Nó được gọi như vậy vì hàng rào lớn bằng những cột sắt nhọn khép kín khoảng trống dẫn vào Udûn, giống như những chiếc răng trong hàm.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup> Tên [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *Carach Angren* xuất phát từ <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Carach&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Carach (page does not exist)">carach</a></i> ("hàm", "hàng của răng")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> và *[angren](../A/Angren.md)* ("sắt").

*Isen* là một biến thể cũ của sắt trong tiếng Anh; và Mouthe đại diện cho [tiếng Anh cổ](../O/Old_English.md) *mūða* < *mūð* "mở, miệng" đặc biệt được sử dụng ở các cửa sông, nhưng cũng được áp dụng cho các cửa mở khác.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>

Mặc dù có tiếng Anh cổ nhưng tên này không được hiểu là [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md) mà là [Westron](../W/Westron.md) cổ xưa, bản dịch của [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *Carach Angren*.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Miêu tả trong các tác phẩm chuyển thể {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b><a href="../_/1987.md" title="1987">1987</a>-<a href="../_/1996.md" title="1996">96</a>: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the <i><a href="../T/Teeth_of_Mordor_(book).md" title="Teeth of Mordor (book)">Teeth of Mordor</a></i> module in the <i>Fortresses of Middle-earth</i> series it is described that <a href="../I/Isildur.md" title="Isildur">Isildur</a> began a grand plan to seal off Mordor and that the construction of Carach Angren began before the end of <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_1.md" title="Third Age 1">1</a> and was completed during the reign of <a href="../R/Rómendacil_I.md" title="Rómendacil I">Rómendacil I</a> (<a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_492.md" title="Third Age 492">492</a> - <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_541.md" title="Third Age 541">541</a>). It is mentioned that by <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_1640.md" title="Third Age 1640">1640</a> the last citadel guarding Mordor was abandoned. It is said that in the same year of the closing of the Dúnadan fortresses guarding Mordor Sauron sent all <a class="mw-redirect" href="../R/Ringwraiths.md" title="Ringwraiths">Ringwraiths</a> except the <a href="../W/Witch-king.md" title="Witch-king">Witch-king</a> and <a href="../K/Khamûl.md" title="Khamûl">Khamûl</a> to lake <a class="mw-redirect" href="../N/Núrnen.md" title="Núrnen">Núrnen</a> with the mission to ready Mordor for his return. It is described that they sent scouts north who found that the Gondorian fortresses were abandoned.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In the <a href="../G/Gorgoroth_(book).md" title="Gorgoroth (book)">Gorgoroth</a> campaign module it is described that the garrison of Carach Angren succumbed to the Great Plague and only a skeleton crew was left in <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_1635.md" title="Third Age 1635">1635</a>. All contact was lost with these soldiers and their fate was unknown. It is described that the Ringwraith Indûr Dawndeath met the other Ringwraiths except the Witch-king in <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_1640.md" title="Third Age 1640">1640</a> in the castle of Ostigurth to prepare the Black Land for Sauron&#39;s return. It is mentioned that Orcs entered Carach Angren in T.A. 1642 and found that the Gondorian soldiers had blocked the Great Gate by sabotaging its lifting mechanism. It is mentioned that Indûr took control of Carach Angren in T.A. 1652 and remained there and that he joined his companions in the siege of Minas Ithil in <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_2000.md" title="Third Age 2000">2000</a>. Carach Angren is described to consist of a wall with the Great Gate, three middle towers, the tower of Tarmorost (High Black Fort) and the tower of Fuinost (Gloom-Fort). The wall rests on the top of a stone dike (47 feet high, extending 100 feet from the base of the wall). The wall itself is over forty feet high and consists of stone with toothed parapets on its top. The Great Gate consists of double portals made of one foot thick hollow steel on the north and south side of the gatehouse. Inside the gate is a set of porticulli. When the door is closed great masonry fangs are lowered on the outside to further seal the gate. A 1,224 ton stone rests in the western side of the gate in an alcove. It sits on massive rollers and can be slid to seal Carach Angren in a last ditch effort. The three middle towers consist of three levels. Tarmorost is the easternmost end tower of the wall. It stands on a shelf of rock with the peak of the tower nearly 220 feet above the floor of the plateau. Tarmorost consists of five levels. Fuinost is the westernmost end tower of the wall. Fuinost is eighty feet high, but stands on a ridge of stone with the peak of the tower 170 feet above the floor of the plateau. Fuinost also consists of five levels. It is mentioned that the basic garrison of Carach Angren consists of 162 Men, 54 Olog-hai, 81 Uruk-hai and 243 Orcs.</dd></dl>
