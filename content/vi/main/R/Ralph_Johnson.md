**Ralph Johnson** là một diễn viên người Kiwi, người đã đóng vai [Hugin](../H/Hugin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-GCM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GCM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> và [Orc](../O/Orcs.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-3">[3]</a></sup> trong *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-3">[3]</a></sup> một Orc, [Uruk](../U/Uruk-hai.md) và [Rohirrim](Rohirrim.md) trong *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-3">[3]</a></sup> và Rohirrim trong *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-3">[3]</a></sup> He was a stand-in for [John Noble](../J/John_Noble.md) as [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) in *The Return of the King*.<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-3">[3]</a></sup>

Johnson cũng đóng vai 'Laugher' trong bộ phim <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Kong_(2005_film)" title="wikipedia:King Kong (2005 film)">Vua Kong</a>của đạo diễn *[The Lord of the Rings](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)* [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GCM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GCM-1">[1]</a></sup>
