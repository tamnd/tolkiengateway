Vào [11 tháng 5](../_/11_May.md) [1966](../_/1966.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) đã viết **[một lá thư](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) cho ông Walker**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Nội dung:** Trong thư Tolkien có đề cập ngắn gọn về đoạn băng do ông Walker thực hiện.
- **Mô tả:** Thư đánh máy, có chữ ký. Kích thước: 5,5" x 7".
- **Xuất bản:** Chưa xuất bản.

## Trích đoạn {#Excerpts}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Dear Mr. Walker,<br/><br/>I did receive the tape that you sent to me on the 13th July, not the 3rd July, 1965. I regret not having acknowledged receipt, but it arrived just as I was becoming heavily involved in the business of <a href="../A/Ace_Books.md" title="Ace Books">combating &#39;piracy&#39; in the United States</a>, which occupied me most of the remainder of last year. I am afraid it has been wasted on me because I have not now got a recorder and am far too busy to deal with any such matters. If you think of going further with your settings, either making public use of your tapes or considering publication of the music, please consult <a class="mw-redirect" href="../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md" title="Allen and Unwin">Messrs. Allen &amp; Unwin</a>, 40 Museum Street, W.C.1. I return your tape herewith with regrets that I am not able to make any proper return for your interest in my work.<br/><br/>Yours sincerely,<br/>J.R.R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
