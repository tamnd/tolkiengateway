<i><b>Leaf của Niggle</b></i> là một truyện ngắn được viết bởi [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) khoảng [1942](../_/1942.md), xuất bản lần đầu trên *[The Dublin Review](<../T/The_Dublin_Review_(January_1945).md>)* năm [1945](../_/1945.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Nó được đưa vào cuốn sách *[Tree and Leaf](../T/Tree_and_Leaf.md)* (1964) và trong các tài liệu biên soạn khác, chẳng hạn như *[The Tolkien Reader](../T/The_Tolkien_Reader.md)* (1966), *[Poems and Stories](../P/Poems_and_Stories.md)* (1980), và *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm.md)* (1997).

Sự xuất hiện riêng biệt đầu tiên của nó dường như là một [2003](../_/2003.md) bản in riêng của <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_Forum" title="wikipedia:Trinity Forum">Diễn đàn Trinity</a>, trong ấn bản này có lời tựa và "hướng dẫn thảo luận nhóm" ở cuối.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Cuốn sách riêng biệt được [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md) phát hành chính thức vào [2016](../_/2016.md).

## Tổng quan {#Overview}

*Leaf by Niggle* gần như là một câu chuyện ngụ ngôn về quá trình sáng tạo của chính Tolkien, và ở một mức độ nào đó, về cuộc đời của chính ông. Mặc dù Tolkien tích cực bảo vệ việc không mang tính ngụ ngôn, nhưng ông thừa nhận đã làm như vậy trong *Leaf by Niggle* trong [một lá thư](Letter_199.md) gửi Caroline Everett ([ngày 24 tháng 6](../_/24_June.md) [1957](../_/1957.md)):<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><i>&#34;I should say that, in addition to my tree-love (it was originally called The Tree), it arose from my own pre-occupation with the Lord of the Rings, the knowledge that it would be finished in great detail or not at all, and the fear (near certainty) that it would be &#39;not at all&#39;. The war had arisen to darken all horizons. But no such analyses are a complete explanation even of a short story...&#34;</i>
</p>
</blockquote>

## Tóm tắt {#Synopsis}

[Niggle](../N/Niggle.md) là một nghệ sĩ vẽ tranh để làm hài lòng bản thân, sống trong một xã hội ít coi trọng nghệ thuật. Công việc chính của ông là vẽ một bức tranh khổng lồ về cái cây lớn. Ông bắt đầu với một chiếc lá duy nhất và bức tranh phát triển xung quanh nó. Niggle hy vọng có thể vẽ được từng chiếc lá một cách chi tiết. Chẳng bao lâu Niggle tìm thấy những chú chim trên cây, những ngọn đồi hiện rõ qua cành cây. Và thế là bức tranh ngày càng phát triển và chiếm hết thời gian của họa sĩ. Niggle xin nghỉ làm vì lịch sự để giúp đỡ người hàng xóm, một người làm vườn tên là Parish bị què và có một người vợ ốm yếu. Trong quá trình giúp Niggle mắc bệnh.

Sau đó, ông ta buộc phải thực hiện một chuyến đi, nhưng không chuẩn bị sẵn sàng cho chuyến đi đó (một phần do bệnh tật của ông ta) và cuối cùng phải đến Bệnh xá Workhouse, nơi ông ta phải lao động mỗi ngày. Ông ta được tạm tha và gửi đi làm công việc làm vườn ở nông thôn. Ông nhận ra rằng thực tế ông đang làm việc trong khu rừng trong bức tranh của mình, nhưng Cái cây là sự hiện thực hóa thực sự về tầm nhìn của ông chứ không phải là phiên bản thiếu sót trong nghệ thuật của ông.

Niggle được đoàn tụ với Parish, hàng xóm của ông, và họ cùng nhau làm cho khu rừng trở nên đẹp hơn. Cuối cùng, Niggle đi đến những nơi xa xôi trong khu rừng, đến những địa điểm ở rìa bức tranh của mình.

Moved eventually to Niggle&#39;s Parish, finally understanding the art, and waited for his wife to join him.</li>
<li><b><a href="../M/Mrs._Parish.md" title="Mrs. Parish">Mrs. Parish</a></b> - The wife of Mr. Parish. Later, she was mentioned to be mopping up some rain water wondering if Niggle had forgotten to meet with the builder.</li>
<li><b>Doctor</b> - A doctor who worked within the town where Niggle lived; Enlisted by Niggle to cure Mrs. Parish&#39;s cold but took two days to arrive.</li>
<li><b>Builder</b> - A builder who worked within the town where Niggle lived; Enlisted by Niggle to fix Parish&#39;s roof and never arrived at all.</li>
<li><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="../I/Inspector_of_Houses.md" title="Inspector of Houses">Inspector of Houses</a></b> - A very tall man and an enforcer of the <i>houses come first</i> law; Was once in the past called to inspect Niggle&#39;s neglected garden; Threatened to use <i>Niggle&#39;s Picture</i> to fix Parish&#39;s roof.</li>
<li><b>Driver</b> - A very tall man, clad all in black, who forcibly began Niggle&#39;s journey; Drove him by carriage to the Porter&#39;s railway station.</li>
<li><b>Porter</b> - The person who sent Niggle to the Workhouse Infirmary. Later coined the name <i>Niggle&#39;s Parish</i>.</li>
<li><b>Severe doctor</b> - A doctor who worked at the Workhouse Infirmary; Supervised Niggle during his stay there.</li>
<li><b>First Voice</b> - A very severe voice; One of two voices that Niggle heard during a break at the Workhouse Infirmary.</li>
<li><b>Second Voice</b> - A gentle, but authoritative voice, sounding both sad and hopeful; One of two voices that Niggle heard during a break at the Workhouse Infirmary. Later considered Niggle&#39;s Parish to be the perfect holiday spot.</li>
<li><b>Shepherd</b> - A man who lived as a shepherd within the Mountains just beyond the Edge of Niggle&#39;s Parish; Offered to be Niggle&#39;s guide to the mountains beyond and revealed to Parish that this was the land from <i>Niggle&#39;s Picture</i>.</li>
<li><b>Councillor Tompkins</b> - A councillor who lived at Niggle&#39;s old home; Claimed that Niggle had no use to Society at all, and that Society should have &#34;put him away&#34; forever long ago at the &#34;Rubbish Heap&#34;, possibly another name for the Workhouse Infirmary.</li>
<li><b>Atkins</b> - A schoolmaster who claimed that Niggle is pitiful; Saved a piece of <i>Niggle&#39;s Picture</i>, and framed it at the Town Museum.</li>
<li><b>Perkins</b> - A person who never knew that Niggle painted, yet voiced pity regardless.</li></ol>

<div style="-moz-column-width: 400px; -webkit-column-width: 400px; column-width: 400px; -moz-column-gap: 15px; -webkit-column-gap: 15px; column-gap: 15px; -webkit-column-rule: 1px solid #cccccc; -moz-column-rule: 1px solid #cccccc; column-rule: 1px solid #cccccc;">Listed in order of named appearance:
<ol><li><b>Town Council</b> - The local government of the town that Niggle lived in. Niggle tried to save his painting from the Inspector of Houses by suggesting that Parish should file a complaint to the council to fix his roof, damaged by the rain.</li>
<li><b>Emergency Service</b> - An agency run by the Town Council that is responsible for providing disaster relief to the town. According to the Inspector of Houses, they could not deal with Parish&#39;s roof due to more significant damage from a flood in the nearby valley that left many families homeless.</li>
<li><b>Houses come first law</b> - A law in the Society where Niggle lived that says that the fixing of houses must come before leisure activities, such as painting.</li>
<li><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="../W/Workhouse_Infirmary.md" title="Workhouse Infirmary">Workhouse Infirmary</a></b> - A prison like place that locked up people who did not &#34;contribute&#34; to Society and forcing them to work; The place was run by severe officials and attendants who were unfriendly and silent; Possibly referred to by Councilor Tompkins as the <b>Rubbish Heap</b>. There was a railway station just below the green hill the infirmary was on.</li>
<li><b>Medical Board/Court of Inquiry</b> - A council at the Workhouse Infirmary that was comprised of at least the two voices. Their meetings took place in the room next to Niggle&#39;s room with the door open.</li>
<li><b>Records</b> - The name of the documented report on Niggle&#39;s background that the Workhouse Infirmary possessed; It was read and used by the Second Voice to convince the First Voice to allow Niggle to be put through the Gentle Treatment.</li>
<li><b>Calls</b> - A name given by the First Voice to refer to the action in Society of one person asking another person for help; The First Voice claimed that this type of action was called <b>Interruptions</b> by Niggle.</li>
<li><b>Gentle Treatment</b> - The name of the procedure that the Second Voice recommended to the Niggle case where the Workhouse Infirmary provides the subject with new clothes, breakfast, and a treatment for injuries before allowing them to leave for the next stage.</li>
<li><b>Great Tree</b> - A name given by Niggle to the physical representation of the Tree from his painting and the central location within Niggle&#39;s Parish.</li>
<li><b>Forest</b> - A physical representation of the Forest from <i>Niggle&#39;s Picture</i> located within Niggle&#39;s Parish, growing off in the distance on both sides of the Great Tree.</li>
<li><b>Mountains</b> - A physical representation of the Mountains tipped with snow from <i>Niggle&#39;s Picture</i> located just beyond the Edge of Niggle&#39;s Parish, surrounding it in the distance.</li>
<li><b>Spring</b> - A physical representation of the Spring that Niggle imagined, but never had the time to paint within his painting. It was located at the heart of the Forest within Niggle&#39;s Parish, it was the source of a distant lake. The name appeared on the two tonics that Niggle and Parish were provided with.</li>
<li><b>Edge</b> - The name given to the border between Niggle&#39;s Parish and the Mountains beyond.</li>
<li><b>Parish&#39;s Garden</b> - The name given by the shepherd to a small region located within Niggle&#39;s Parish that belonged to Mr. Parish.</li>
<li><i><b>Niggle&#39;s Picture</b></i> - The name given by the shepherd to Niggle&#39;s painting; Given the names <b>Niggle&#39;s Nonsense</b> and <b>That Daubing</b> by Mrs. Parish; It began with a leaf caught within the wind and became a tree with strange birds nesting on it. The canvas was located up a ladder within a tall shed that Niggle had built above an old potato garden he had. It was left incomplete as it was eventually confiscated by the Inspector of Houses in order to fix Parish&#39;s roof.</li>
<li><b><a href="../S/Society.md" title="Society">Society</a></b> - A loose general term for the country and the town that Niggle lived in. This Society placed an emphasis on work over art, confiscating artworks such as <i>Niggle&#39;s Picture</i> to benefit the town. The people, such as Niggle, who violated the laws of Society have their homes and possessions given away after they go on their journey.</li>
<li><b>Town Museum</b> - A museum located within the town that Niggle lived in; The remnant of Niggle&#39;s work was framed there until the museum was burned down.</li>
<li><i><b>Leaf: by Niggle</b></i> - A damaged, but legible corner torn off from <i>Niggle&#39;s Picture</i> (&#34;a mountain-peak and a spray of leaves&#34;) that was found by Atkins in a field and framed within the Town Museum where it had attracted a few people; The remnant was destroyed when the museum was burned to the ground, leaving Niggle to be completely forgotten by his old country.</li>
<li><b><a href="../N/Niggle&#39;s_Parish.md" title="Niggle&#39;s Parish">Niggle&#39;s Parish</a></b> - The name given by the Porter to the region where the Gentle Treatment takes place; Given the name <b>Niggle&#39;s Country</b> by the shepherd and shares the name <i>Niggle&#39;s Picture</i> with Niggle&#39;s original painting; Named after both Niggle and Mr. Parish.</li></ol>

## Miêu tả trong các tác phẩm chuyển thể {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1992: <i><a href="../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md#Leaf_by_Niggle" title="Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)">Tales from the Perilous Realm loạt đài</a></i></b>

<dl><dd><i>Leaf by Niggle</i> Was one of the four stories that the series adapted. The other three adapted stories were: <i><a href="../F/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md" title="Farmer Giles of Ham">Farmer Giles of Ham</a></i>, <i><a href="../S/Smith_of_Wootton_Major.md" title="Smith of Wootton Major">Smith of Wootton Major</a></i>, and <i><a href="../T/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md" title="The Adventures of Tom Bombadil">The Adventures of Tom Bombadil</a></i>. The cast of the <i>Leaf by Niggle</i> part of the series included <a href="../M/Michael_Hordern.md" title="Michael Hordern">Michael Hordern</a> as <a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>, Alfred Molina as <a href="../N/Niggle.md" title="Niggle">Niggle</a>, John Fleming as <a href="../M/Mr._Parish.md" title="Mr. Parish">Parish</a>, and additional voices performed by Jonathan Adams, David Holt, Melanie Hudson, Matthew Morgan, Joanna Wake, and Melinda Walker.</dd></dl>
