Vào [4 tháng 6](../_/4_June.md) [1949](../_/1949.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) đã viết [một lá thư](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) gửi [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md). Nó được viết sau khi hoàn thành bản thảo *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* của Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Xuất bản:** Các đoạn trích từ bức thư đã được xuất bản trên *[Sotheby's Valuable Printed Books and Manuscripts 13 December 2001](../S/Sotheby's_Valuable_Printed_Books_and_Manuscripts_13_December_2001.md)* và trên trang web của Sotheby's (xem liên kết bên ngoài bên dưới). Một đoạn trích khác được xuất bản trong bài viết *The greatest letters ever written* (không rõ tác giả/người biên tập) ngày [ngày 27 tháng 6](../_/27_June.md), [2007](../_/2007.md) (trong ấn bản trực tuyến của *The Independent*) và trong *[Christie's The Albin Schram Collection of Autograph Letters](../C/Christie's_The_Albin_Schram_Collection_of_Autograph_Letters.md)*.

## Phiên âm {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Merton College, Oxford<br/><br/>4 June 1949<br/><br/>Dear Miss Baynes,<br/><br/>I ought to have written to you before to tell you of the great pleasure that your drawings in illustration of “<a href="../F/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md" title="Farmer Giles of Ham">Farmer Giles</a>” have given me. My friends, very justly, said after seeing them that they had reduced the text to a commentary on the pictures.<br/><br/>I have now seen and returned the ‘paste-up’; and though I am glad that so many of the illustrations have been preserved, at least in the sense that they are represented, I am distressed. Ignorant as I am of the costs and of production-processes, I had hoped for a larger page and space. I fear it has done small justice to your beautiful line and pattern to reduce the size so drastically.<br/><br/>Even so, what little value this rather slender ‘squib’ has is much enhanced by your work.<br/><br/>I am hoping soon to get some larger works published, and in a more ample fashion; and if so, I hope you might be interested, or at least have time to consider them. One, a long romance in sequel to <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit.md" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i>, is finished after some years of work, and is being typed. It is held up at the moment, since I am immersed in examinations and other weary business; but when it&#39;s done, I wonder if I could prevail on you to glance at it.<br/><br/>I hope that we may perhaps [have the?] opportunity of meeting ere [?]<br/><br/>Yours sincerely,<br/><br/>J.R.R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
