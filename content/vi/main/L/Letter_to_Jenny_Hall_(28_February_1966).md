Vào [28 tháng 2](../_/28_February.md) [1966](../_/1966.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) đã viết **[một lá thư](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) cho cô Jenny Hall**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Chủ đề:** (1) Từ nguyên của '[Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md)'. (2) Bản chất thực sự của '[cây du](../T/Tree-men.md)' trong [bộ Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).
- **Xuất bản:** Không có. Đấu giá bởi *Christie's* (lô 52).
- **Mô tả:** Một trang, 76 Sandfield Road, Oxford tiêu đề thư, có chữ ký của J.R.R. Tolkien.

## Phiên âm {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I have been in most parts of Wales, but the place names I use are made up from English models or borrowed from books, though Crickhollow was actually meant to resemble Crickhowell.<br/><br/>The walking elms were meant to be <a href="../E/Ents.md" title="Ents">ents</a> (but not <a href="../E/Entwives.md" title="Entwives">entwives</a>). <a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> had asked one or two of them to keep a watch on the Shire, but he did not tell anybody about it.*<br/><br/>[* As can be gathered from <a href="../T/Treebeard.md" title="Treebeard">Treebeard&#39;s</a> conversations with <a href="../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">M[erry] </a> and <a href="../P/Peregrin_Took.md" title="Peregrin Took">P[ippin] </a> he knew a lot more about events than they guessed, and more about &#39;<a href="../H/Hobbits.md" title="Hobbits">hobbits</a>&#39; than he pretended to.]<br/><br/>With best wishes<br/><br/>J.R.R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
