<i><b>Long Lost to Where No Pathway Goes</b></i> là ca khúc thứ hai trong album [Stronghold](../S/Stronghold.md) của ban nhạc [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md). Lời bài hát chịu ảnh hưởng của [The Lays of Beleriand](../T/The_Lays_of_Beleriand.md), [The Lay of the Children of Húrin](../T/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md)và bài thơ [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) [Imram](../I/Imram.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Lời bài hát {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>My star, I saw it high and far<br/>
At the parting of the ways<br/>
A light on the edge of the outer night<br/>
Like silver set ablaze<br/>
Where the round world plunges steeply down<br/>
But on the old roads goes<br/>
As an unseen bridge that on arches runs<br/>
To coasts that no man knows<br/>
<br/>
I would not find the burning domes and sands<br/>
Where reigns the sun, nor dare the snows<br/>
Nor seek in mountains dark the landscapes of the men<br/>
Long lost to where no pathway goes<br/>
<br/>
But where they bloom those flowers fair<br/>
In what air or land they grow<br/>
What words beyond the world I heard<br/>
If you would seek for know<br/>
<br/>
In a boat then, brother, far afloat<br/>
You must labour in the sea<br/>
And find yourself things out of mind:<br/>
You will learn no more of me<br/>
<br/>
I would not find the burning domes and sands<br/>
Where reigns the sun, nor dare the deadly snows<br/>
Nor seek in mountains dark the landscapes of the men<br/>
Of long lost to where no pathway goes<br/>
<br/>
There the twain enfolded,<br/>
Phantom twilight, phantom twilight,<br/>
And dim the mazes dark, unholy.<br/>
<br/>
I would never find the burning domes and sands<br/>
Where reigns the sun, nor dare the snows<br/>
Nor seek in mountains dark, the landscapes of the men<br/>
Long lost to whom no pathway goes
</p>
</div>
