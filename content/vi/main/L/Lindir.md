**Lindir** là Tiên của [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).

## Lịch sử {#History}

Sau khi [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)trình diễn *[Eärendillinwë](../E/Eärendillinwë.md)*của mình, Lindir tuyên bố rằng anh không thể biết phần nào được viết bởi [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md)và phần nào do chính Bilbo viết, đồng thời giải thích rằng anh biết rất ít về [Người phàm](../M/Mortals.md) và cách thức của họ.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Từ nguyên {#Etymology}

Tên *Lindir* dường như được tạo thành từ *[lind](Lind.md)* ("giai điệu") + *dîr* ("người đàn ông"), do đó có nghĩa là "Bài hát".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Lindir can be found in the <a href="../H/Hall_of_Fire.md" title="Hall of Fire">Hall of Fire</a> in <a href="../R/Rivendell.md" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>. He is engaged in a conversation with <a href="../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a> and the two ask the player to join them in a <a href="../R/Riddle-game.md" title="Riddle-game">Riddle-game</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2012-14: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (loạt phim)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Lindir is portrayed by <a href="../B/Bret_McKenzie.md" title="Bret McKenzie">Bret McKenzie</a>. He welcomes Gandalf and the party of Dwarves to Rivendell in place of Elrond. Gandalf asks him where Elrond is and is answered by his horn, signalling Elrond and his companion&#39;s return from hunting Orcs. As Elrond and Gandalf speak, Elrond hands Lindir his spear. Later, after the dwarves have been living in Rivendell for a while, Lindir and Elrond talk. Lindir tells Elrond that the kitchen is under strain and that their supply of wine has almost drained by the Dwarves feasting, asking Elrond how long the Dwarves will stay. Elrond tells Lindir it has yet to be decided. They turn the corner and watch uncomfortably as the Dwarves play naked in the fountain. During a <a href="../W/White_Council.md" title="White Council">White Council</a> meeting, Lindir reports to Lord Elrond that <a href="../T/Thorin.md" title="Thorin">Thorin</a> and his companions have departed in secret.<sup id="cite_ref-IMCasting_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IMCasting-3">[3]</a></sup> McKenzie also appeared in <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a> as an extra dubbed &#34;<a href="../F/Figwit.md" title="Figwit">Figwit</a>&#34; by fans. It is unclear whether that character is intended to be Lindir.</dd></dl>
