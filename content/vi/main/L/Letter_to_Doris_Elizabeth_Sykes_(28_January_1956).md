Vào [28 tháng 1](../_/28_January.md) [1956](../_/1956.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) đã viết **[một lá thư](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) cho Doris Elizabeth Sykes**.<sup id="cite_ref-HS_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Christies_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Christies-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Chủ đề:** Thảo luận về những bức tranh minh họa của Syke lấy cảm hứng từ *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*mà cô đã gửi tới Tolkien vào ngày [5 tháng 1](../_/5_January.md) năm 1956.<sup id="cite_ref-HS_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Mô tả:** Viết tay.<sup id="cite_ref-Christies_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Christies-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Xuất bản:** Các trích dẫn từ bức thư và bản sao chép được đưa vào *[Christie's Fine Printed Books and Manuscripts 18 June 2013](../C/Christie's_Fine_Printed_Books_and_Manuscripts_18_June_2013.md)* (tr. 12).<sup id="cite_ref-HS_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Trích đoạn {#Excerpts}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Tolkien responds in the first letter to the recipient&#39;s drawings for the work, which he finds &#39;extremely good ... Apart from their technical skill ... they not only in many cases correspond closely to my vision, but even enrich it. I was particularly delighted by <a href="../T/Treebeard.md" title="Treebeard">Treebeard</a> with <a href="../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a> and <a href="../P/Peregrin_Took.md" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a> in his arms; but all your <a href="../H/Hobbits.md" title="Hobbits">hobbits</a> are admirable, and also the <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">orcs</a>, and <a href="../G/Gimli.md" title="Gimli">Gimli</a>. I also thought the scene under &#34;<a href="../W/Weathertop.md" title="Weathertop">Weathertop</a>&#34; and the <a href="../W/Witch-king.md" title="Witch-king">Witch-King</a> powerful. It was tackled in the only possible way: making Frodo and the King visible and the other Companions shadowy ... <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> alone does not closely correspond to my vision ... I think of him as sterner, keener and in face less &#34;Greek&#34; and straight-nosed, more Roman&#39;; Tolkien adds with regret however that &#39;the immediate prospects of an illustrated edition are not good&#39;.
</p>
