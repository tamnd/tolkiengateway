**Địa y** là một quần thể lai giữa tảo và [nấm](../F/Funguses.md). Cây cối ở [Cổng rừng](../F/Forest_Gate.md) bị “treo địa y”<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>và nhiều cây ở [Rừng Fangorn](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Miêu tả trong các tác phẩm chuyển thể {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1984-1997: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Many different types of lichen are distinguished in the game:</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Carpet Moss</b></i> (<a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Fangluin</i>, <a href="../Q/Quenya.md" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Laicafanga</i>), despite its name, was a pale green-grey lichen that grew in mats on smooth, damp, calcium-rich rock underground. The patches, often the size of a child or hobbit, released a fine powder when disturbed. When applied as poultices, the moss hastened bone-setting and doubled the rate of healing, retaining its powers for up to a year after harvest.<sup id="cite_ref-p94_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p94-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Chékyûv</b></i> (<a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Ongluin</i>, <a href="../Q/Quenya.md" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Luinondo</i>), also called <i>Blue Rub</i>, was a dark-blue lichen found on the interior of Easterling wells. Scraped and applied directly to a cut, it served as a sedative that also sped up the healing of the wound.<sup id="cite_ref-p94_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p94-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Gefnul</b></i> (<a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Gonhel</i>, <a href="../Q/Quenya.md" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Ondohellë</i>), also known as <i>Earth Ash</i>, was a white lichen found near volcanoes. It could be consumed to restore health.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Gonhithui</b></i> (<a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">S.</a> “Misty Stone,” <a href="../Q/Quenya.md" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Hísimondo</i>) was a small blue lichen that grew on damp rocks along Mordor&#39;s watercourses. Ground and taken as a powder mixed with water it causes severe dehydration over the next day and may be fatal, with no known antidote. The only treatment is constant drinking.<sup id="cite_ref-p107_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p107-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Kingslock</b></i> (<a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Cammaidh</i>, <a href="../Q/Quenya.md" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Malyama</i>), also known as <i>Kly-bane</i>, was a grey-white lichen found on exposed mountain outcrops. It could be ground and consumed to quickly treat organ damage. It also served as an antidote to kly-based poisons.<sup id="cite_ref-p114_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p114-7">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Lawrim</b></i>  (<a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Barmallen</i>, <a href="../Q/Quenya.md" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Culuinassë</i>), also known as <i>Law’r</i>, was a dull-orange lichen of the Ettenmoors. Contact caused painful rashes, while boiled extract produced a poison that induced shock and paralysis. The Hillmen used this extract as a dishonourable poison of last resort.<sup id="cite_ref-tenthpage_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tenthpage-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Lichen Gloriosa</b></i>, also known as <i>Loth-nu-Fuin</i> (<a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">S.</a> Flowers-under-the-Night) was a glowing lichen of Mirkwood. It illuminated dark places and, freshly gathered, could help sharpen blades magically.<sup id="cite_ref-tenthpage_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tenthpage-8">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Nelluin</b></i> (<a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">S.</a> “Blue Tooth,” <a href="../Q/Quenya.md" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Luinelet</i>), also known as <i>Maynact</i>, was a dark-blue lichen that grew on spruce trees. Boiled and strained into a tea, it healed nerve damage as the cost of permanently staining one&#39;s teeth light-blue.<sup id="cite_ref-p125_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p125-12">[12]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Worlclivur</b></i> (<a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Falastir</i>, <a href="../Q/Quenya.md" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Falastir</i>) was a rare reddish-brown flowering lichen found along Eriador&#39;s coastlines. Its processed paste inflicted a range of ocular maladies.<sup id="cite_ref-p146_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p146-13">[13]</a></sup></dd></dl>
