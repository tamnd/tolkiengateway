<b><i>Lembas</i> (Vol. 2, No. 10)</b> is an issue of the journal *[Lembas](<Lembas_(journal).md>)*, published by [Unquendor](../U/Unquendor.md). The issue includes [Renée Vink](../R/Renée_Vink.md)'s article "Tolkien in Rotterdam", about Tolkien's visit to Rotterdam in 1958. The article reproduces a photograph of Tolkien taken at the occasion, and includes a number of quotations from letters by Tolkien (among them, [Letter 205b](Letter_205b.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
