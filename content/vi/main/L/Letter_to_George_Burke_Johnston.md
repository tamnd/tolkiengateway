Vào [24 tháng 5](../_/24_May.md) [1968](../_/1968.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) đã viết **[một lá thư](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) cho George Burke Johnston**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Burke Johnston had sent a "specially printed, specially bound copy of" his booklet *[The Poetry of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Poetry_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* to Tolkien, who responded with a thank you note and explained that in his youth "root" was used as "boot" is now, i.e. slang for "kick".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Tolkien also wrote that he will be moving from [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md) soon.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** None.
