Vào [29 tháng 12](../_/29_December.md) [1966](../_/1966.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) đã viết **[một lá thư](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) cho James S. Marsh**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Chủ đề:** Bức thư thứ hai gửi ông Marsh, bình luận về kế hoạch sản xuất một cuốn sách với [Donald Swann](../D/Donald_Swann.md) (sau này trở thành *[The Road Goes Ever On](<../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book).md>)*) và sự đánh giá cao của cả hai về âm nhạc.
- **Xuất bản:** Không có.

## Bản ghi {#Transcript}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Dear Mr Marsh,<br/><br/>Thank you for your letter. Since music touches you, I spare a moment to let you know that I spent a day last week with Donald Swann he <s>is</s> was on a hasty visit to England in the middle of his tour of the U.S. and Canada. We are hoping to produce <a href="../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book).md" title="The Road Goes Ever On (book)">a book</a>, the main part of which will be songs from <u><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></u>, with his setting to be published by <a class="mw-redirect" href="../H/Houghton_Mifflin.md" title="Houghton Mifflin">Houghton Mifflin</a> of Boston next year. I find his music moving and very close to my own intentions. He is at present very engaged on the difficult task of making a setting for “Errantry”.<br/><br/>With best wishes again,<br/>Yours sincerely<br/>J.R.R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
