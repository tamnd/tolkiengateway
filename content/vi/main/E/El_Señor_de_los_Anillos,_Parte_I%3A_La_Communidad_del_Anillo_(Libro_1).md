**El Señor de los Anillos, Parte I: La Communidad del Anillo (Libro 1)** (Spanish: **The Lord of the Rings, Part I: The Fellowship of the Ring (Book 1)** was a Spanish Text-Adventure covering book I of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> The game has both music and illustrations,<sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> but most are taken from other games<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> mostly from [Interplay](../I/Interplay_Productions.md)'s [The Lord of the Rings, Vol. I](<../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_The_Lord_of_the_Rings,_Vol._I_(1990_video_game).md>) and <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defender_of_the_Crown" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Defender of the Crown</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> The author of the game is [Dimas Caparrós Gómez](../D/Dimas_Caparrós_Gómez.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup>
