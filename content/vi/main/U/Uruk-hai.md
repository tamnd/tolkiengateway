<p><b>Uruk-hai</b> is a <a href="../B/Black_Speech.md" title="Black Speech">Black Speech</a> term meaning &#34;Orc-folk.&#34;<sup id="cite_ref-PM17x_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM17x-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PM17y_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM17y-4">[4]</a></sup> The word &#34;Uruk&#34; can refer generically to any <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">Orc</a>, but there are certain larger, stronger breeds of Orc that are specifically referred to as &#34;Uruks.&#34;<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The term &#34;Uruk-hai&#34; was most notably used by the Uruks of <a href="../S/Saruman.md" title="Saruman">Saruman&#39;s</a> army to describe themselves during the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Uruk_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Uruk-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Deep_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-8">[8]</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Characteristics">Characteristics</span></h2>
<p>Four dead Uruk-hai in the employ of <a href="../O/Orthanc.md" title="Orthanc">Orthanc</a> that lay near <a href="../B/Boromir.md" title="Boromir">Boromir</a> had a greater stature than the surrounding dead Orcs. They were swart, slant-eyed, and had thick legs and large hands. An &#34;<i>S&#34;</i> <a href="../C/Cirth.md" title="Cirth">elf-rune</a> was wrought in white metal on the front of their iron helms. <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> guessed that this &#34;S&#34; stood for Saruman, as their shields were emblazoned with a small <a href="../W/White_Hand.md" title="White Hand">White Hand</a> in the centre of a black field. This stood out in contrast to the Orcs in the service of <a href="../B/Barad-dûr.md" title="Barad-dûr">Barad-dûr</a>, the folk of <a href="../M/Mordor.md" title="Mordor">Mordor</a>, who used the symbol of the Red <a href="../E/Eye_of_Sauron.md" title="Eye of Sauron">Eye of Sauron</a>. Aragorn commented that the four dead Uruk-hai&#39;s gear &#34;<i>is not after the manner of Orcs at all.</i>&#34; Instead of curved scimitars that were usually used by Orcs, they had short, broad-bladed swords. Their <a href="../B/Bows.md" title="Bows">bows</a> were made of yew wood, in length and shape as those of Men.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Origins_of_Uruks">Origins of Uruks</span></h3>

<p>The race of Uruks, described as &#39;black orcs of great strength&#39;, first appeared out of <a href="../M/Mordor.md" title="Mordor">Mordor</a> in the last years of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../R/Ruling_Stewards.md" title="Ruling Stewards">Stewardship</a> of <a href="../D/Denethor_I.md" title="Denethor I">Denethor I</a>, possibly being secretly bred in Mordor by the <a href="../N/Nazgûl.md" title="Nazgûl">Morgul-lords</a> at the behest of their master <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Heirs_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Heirs-1">[1]</a></sup> In <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_2475.md" title="Third Age 2475">2475</a>, these creatures conquered <a href="../I/Ithilien.md" title="Ithilien">Ithilien</a> and destroyed the ancient city of <a href="../O/Osgiliath.md" title="Osgiliath">Osgiliath</a>, but they were eventually defeated and repelled by <a href="../B/Boromir_(Steward_of_Gondor).md" title="Boromir (Steward of Gondor)">Boromir</a>, the son of Denethor I.<sup id="cite_ref-Denethor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Denethor-2">[2]</a></sup>. After about five years (c. <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_2480.md" title="Third Age 2480">2480</a>) since their first appearance, Sauron started to populate <a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Moria</a> with his creatures and Orcs built secret strongholds in the <a href="../M/Misty_Mountains.md" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a> to block all the passes into <a href="../E/Eriador.md" title="Eriador">Eriador</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> In <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_2901.md" title="Third Age 2901">2901</a> most of the remaining inhabitants deserted Ithilien, because of attacks by Uruks of Mordor.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
</p><p>The Uruks in the service of <a href="../B/Barad-dûr.md" title="Barad-dûr">Barad-dûr</a>, the folk of Mordor, used the symbol of the <a href="../E/Eye_of_Sauron.md" title="Eye of Sauron">Red Eye of Sauron</a> on their livery.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-13">[13]</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="War_of_the_Ring">War of the Ring</span></h3>
<p>Around <a href="../T/Third_Age_2990.md" title="Third Age 2990">T.A. 2990</a>, the corrupted <a class="mw-redirect" href="../W/Wizard.md" title="Wizard">Wizard</a> <a href="../S/Saruman.md" title="Saruman">Saruman</a> began to use and possibly breed Orcs in <a href="../I/Isengard.md" title="Isengard">Isengard</a>. As the <a href="../S/Shadow.md" title="Shadow">Shadow</a> of the <a href="../D/Dark_Lord.md" title="Dark Lord">Enemy</a> fell over <a href="../R/Rohan.md" title="Rohan">Rohan</a>, Uruk-hai out of Isengard joined raiding parties of <a href="../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md" title="Orcs of the Misty Mountains">Orcs of the Misty Mountains</a> against the <a href="../R/Rohirrim.md" title="Rohirrim">Rohirrim</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
</p>
<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/J/John_Howe_-_The_Charge_of_the_Rohirrim_at_Helm&#39;s_Deep.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/6e/John_Howe_-_The_Charge_of_the_Rohirrim_at_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg/321px-John_Howe_-_The_Charge_of_the_Rohirrim_at_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="321" height="367" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/6e/John_Howe_-_The_Charge_of_the_Rohirrim_at_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg/482px-John_Howe_-_The_Charge_of_the_Rohirrim_at_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/6/6e/John_Howe_-_The_Charge_of_the_Rohirrim_at_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg 2x" data-file-width="534" data-file-height="610"/></a><figcaption>Uruk-hai face the Charge of the Rohirrim at Helm&#39;s Deep, Art by <a href="../J/John_Howe.md" title="John Howe">John Howe</a></figcaption></figure>
<p>The Uruk-hai (Uruks) of Isengard made up the core of a ten-thousand strong army, mustered by the Wizard Saruman both in service to and in tacit competition with his Lord Sauron. On <a href="../_/25_February.md" title="25 February">25 February</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_3019.md" title="Third Age 3019">T.A. 3019</a>, joined by <a href="../D/Dunlendings.md" title="Dunlendings">Dunlendings</a> and the common <a href="../O/Orcs_of_Isengard.md" title="Orcs of Isengard">Orcs of Isengard</a>, they fought against the Rohirrim in the <a href="../F/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md" title="First Battle of the Fords of Isen">First Battle of the fords of Isen</a>, in which Prince <a href="../T/Théodred.md" title="Théodred">Théodred</a> was slain by a great orc-man.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> It is possible that this &#34;great orc-man&#34; was one of the Orcs who referred to themselves as the Uruk-hai,<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> but it is also possible that he was a <a class="mw-redirect" href="../H/Half-orc.md" title="Half-orc">Half-orc</a>.
</p><p>On <a href="../_/26_February.md" title="26 February">26 February</a> T.A. 3019, Saruman went behind Sauron&#39;s back and sent a smaller troop to capture the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../R/Ring-bearer.md" title="Ring-bearer">Ring-bearer</a> for his own ends. Led by <a href="Uglúk.md" title="Uglúk">Uglúk</a>, these Uruk-hai ambushed the <a href="../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="Fellowship of the Ring">Fellowship of the Ring</a> at <a href="../A/Amon_Hen.md" title="Amon Hen">Amon Hen</a>, slaying <a href="../B/Boromir.md" title="Boromir">Boromir</a> and capturing <a class="mw-redirect" href="../M/Merry.md" title="Merry">Merry</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="../P/Pippin.md" title="Pippin">Pippin</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-17">[17]</a></sup> Though quarreling with Orcs from the north and <a href="../G/Grishnákh.md" title="Grishnákh">Grishnákh</a>&#39;s Mordor-orcs who had joined them, the Uruk-hai imposed Isengard as the destination for the captive <a href="../H/Hobbits.md" title="Hobbits">Hobbits</a>. Aragorn, <a href="../G/Gimli.md" title="Gimli">Gimli</a>, and <a href="../L/Legolas.md" title="Legolas">Legolas</a> would follow the Uruk-hai troop and the other two Orc warbands until the Orcs and Uruk-hai were slain to the last on the eaves of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../F/Fangorn.md" title="Fangorn">Fangorn</a> by <a href="../T/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark.md" title="Third Marshal of the Riddermark">Third Marshal of the Riddermark</a> <a href="../_/Éomer.md" title="Éomer">Éomer</a> and his riders on <a href="../_/27_February.md" title="27 February">27 February</a> T.A. 3019.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>
</p><p>On <a href="../_/29_February.md" title="29 February">29 February</a> T.A. 3019<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>, <a href="../T/Treebeard.md" title="Treebeard">Treebeard</a> was likely referring to the Uruk-hai when he said to <a href="../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a> and <a href="../P/Peregrin_Took.md" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a> that Saruman had done something dangerous to the Orcs and that the Orcs of Saruman were more like wicked Men, because they could tolerate sunlight although they hated it. He openly wondered if they were Men that Saruman had &#34;ruined&#34; or if he had &#34;<a href="../H/Half-orcs.md" title="Half-orcs">blended</a>&#34; Orcs and Men, an act which he considered to be &#34;a black evil.&#34;<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>
</p><p>The Uruk-hai fought the Rohirrim again on <a href="../_/2_March.md" title="2 March">2 March</a> T.A. 3019 at <a href="../S/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md" title="Second Battle of the Fords of Isen">Second Battle of the Fords of Isen</a>, where they defeated and scattered the Rohirrim who were under the command of <a href="../G/Grimbold.md" title="Grimbold">Grimbold</a>. The fortunes of the Uruk-hai would turn for the worst when their army was then sent against <a class="mw-redirect" href="../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md" title="Kings of Rohan">King</a> <a href="../T/Théoden.md" title="Théoden">Théoden</a> and his people at <a href="../H/Helm&#39;s_Deep.md" title="Helm&#39;s Deep">Helm&#39;s Deep</a>, where they faced utter defeat in the <a href="../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md" title="Battle of the Hornburg">Battle of the Hornburg</a> on <a href="../_/3_March.md" title="3 March">3</a>-<a href="../_/4_March.md" title="4 March">4 March</a> T.A. 3019.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup>
</p><p>On <a href="../_/15_March.md" title="15 March">15 March</a> T.A. 3019 <a href="../F/Frodo_Baggins.md" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a> and <a href="../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md" title="Samwise Gamgee">Sam</a> escaped <a href="../C/Cirith_Ungol.md" title="Cirith Ungol">Cirith Ungol</a> and narrowly avoided two Orcs of Mordor who were searching for them. The Orcs speculated on exactly what it was they were searching for, with one of them saying that he had been told that it &#34;<i>must be a pack of rebel Uruk-hai</i>&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-13">[13]</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Etymology">Etymology</span></h2>
<p><i>Uruk-hai</i> is a name in the <a href="../B/Black_Speech.md" title="Black Speech">Black Speech</a>, which means &#34;Orc-folk&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-PM17x_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM17x-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PM17y_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM17y-4">[4]</a></sup> It is a combination of the elements <i>Uruk</i> (&#34;orc&#34;)<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup> and <i>hai</i> (&#34;folk&#34;)<sup id="cite_ref-PM17x_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM17x-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PM17y_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM17y-4">[4]</a></sup>.
</p><p><i>Uruks</i> is the anglicized form of <i>Uruk-hai</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup> <a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> himself used these forms interchangeably.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Usage_of_the_word_Uruk-hai_among_Orcs">Usage of the word Uruk-hai among Orcs</span></h2>
<p><a href="Uglúk.md" title="Uglúk">Uglúk</a>, the commander of a group of <a href="../O/Orcs_of_Isengard.md" title="Orcs of Isengard">Orcs of Isengard</a>, referred to the Orcs under his command as &#34;the fighting Uruk-hai&#34; when he argued with <a href="../G/Grishnákh.md" title="Grishnákh">Grishnákh</a>, an <a href="../O/Orcs_of_Mordor.md" title="Orcs of Mordor">Orc of Mordor</a>, about the fate of the captive <a href="../H/Hobbits.md" title="Hobbits">Hobbits</a>, <a href="../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a> and <a href="../P/Peregrin_Took.md" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a><sup id="cite_ref-Uruk_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Uruk-7">[7]</a></sup>. He also used the phrase &#34;fighting Uruk-hai&#34; when he ordered a group of Orcs from the north to leave the two Hobbits with him when the northern Orcs wished to flee from the riders of the <a href="../R/Rohirrim.md" title="Rohirrim">Rohirrim</a><sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup>. He later referred to the Orcs under his command as &#34;the Uruk-hai of Isengard&#34; when he talked to Grishnákh after Grishnákh&#39;s group had returned<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[26]</a></sup>. Later, during the <a href="../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md" title="Battle of the Hornburg">Battle of the Hornburg</a>, the Orcs of Isengard gathered before the great gates of the Hornburg and referred to themselves twice as &#34;the fighting Uruk-hai&#34; and once simply as &#34;the Uruk-hai&#34; when Aragorn spoke to them from above the gates.<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-8">[8]</a></sup>
</p><p>A large soldier Orc of Mordor bearing the token of the Eye told a small tracker Orc from Mordor that those &#34;Higher Up&#34; had eventually said that it must be &#34;a pack of rebel Uruk-hai&#34; whose scent they were following when they were tracking <a href="../F/Frodo_Baggins.md" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a> and <a href="../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md" title="Samwise Gamgee">Sam</a> after their escape from the <a href="../T/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md" title="Tower of Cirith Ungol">Tower of Cirith Ungol</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_13-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-13">[13]</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Portrayal_in_adaptations">Portrayal in adaptations</span></h2>
<p>1988: <i><a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien&#39;s_War_in_Middle_Earth.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s War in Middle Earth">J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s War in Middle Earth</a></i>:
</p>
<dl><dd>The Uruk-hai are portrayed as figures with purple armor and a shield.</dd></dl>
<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)_-_Lurtz.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/95/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_%28film_series%29_-_Lurtz.jpg/319px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_%28film_series%29_-_Lurtz.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="319" height="186" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/95/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_%28film_series%29_-_Lurtz.jpg/479px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_%28film_series%29_-_Lurtz.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/95/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_%28film_series%29_-_Lurtz.jpg/638px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_%28film_series%29_-_Lurtz.jpg 2x" data-file-width="674" data-file-height="392"/></a><figcaption>The Uruk-hai captain Lurtz in film <i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring.</i></figcaption></figure>
<p>2001-03: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:
</p>
<dl><dd><a href="../P/Peter_Jackson.md" title="Peter Jackson">Peter Jackson&#39;s</a> <i>The Lord of the Rings</i> trilogy makes a more explicit distinction between Uruk-hai and lesser Orcs and Goblins. Uruk-hai are described as a crossbreed between &#34;Orcs and <a href="../G/Goblin-men.md" title="Goblin-men">Goblin-men</a>;&#34; referencing the creatures mentioned by <a href="../G/Gamling.md" title="Gamling">Gamling</a> in the novel that blend the traits of Orcs and Men.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[27]</a></sup> In a scene in Isengard, they are shown as being released from a kind of membrane in the mud deep under Isengard. Special commentary on the DVD edition explained that they were trying to base the scene on an early description<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[28]</a></sup> of Tolkien&#39;s that Orcs &#34;worm their way out of the ground like maggots.&#34; During <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a>,</i> these Uruk-hai are sent after the Fellowship and are led by their captain <a href="../L/Lurtz.md" title="Lurtz">Lurtz</a>, a movie-only character.</dd></dl>
<dl><dd>In <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</i></a>, Uruk-hai forces included Pikemen (who carry long pikes) Swordsmen (who carry single-edged, hook-tipped swords), Archers (who carry <a href="../C/Cross-bows.md" title="Cross-bows">crossbows</a>), and <a href="../B/Berserker.md" title="Berserker">Berserkers</a>. They also use bladed shields as seen in <a href="../A/Amon_Hen.md" title="Amon Hen">Amon Hen</a> during <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>&#39;s fight against Lurtz. During the Battle of the Hornburg, all Uruk-hai (save for Berserkers) are equipped with heavy armor that covers everything other than their elbows and knees. The foot-soldiers&#39; helmets bear crests that spread laterally across the top of helmet. Uruk-hai in the films are also depicted using siege equipment to great effect at Helm&#39;s Deep.</dd></dl>
<p>2002: <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../E/EA&#39;s_The_Two_Towers.md" title="EA&#39;s The Two Towers">EA&#39;s The Two Towers</a></i>:
</p>
<dl><dd>Uruk-hai appear as enemies in the &#34;Battle of Helm&#39;s Deep&#34; and &#34;Fangorn Forest&#34; missions, retaining the movie appearance.</dd></dl>
<p>2004: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring</a></i>:
</p>
<dl><dd>Uruk-hai become available after a storyline mission in which Saruman manages to perfect his creation.</dd></dl>
<p>2004: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth</a></i>:
</p>
<dl><dd>Uruk-hai compose most of the units of Isengard faction: swordsmen, pikemen, crossbowmen, in addition to Uruk-hai operating siege engines are available, following the movie appearance.</dd></dl>
<p>2006: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:
</p>
<dl><dd>Uruk-hai units from the first game return with little if any changes in behavior.</dd></dl>
<p>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:
</p>
<dl><dd><i>Uruk-hai</i> are found in the Marton Farmlands and the surrounding plains in the Westfold in West Rohan.</dd></dl>
<p>2009: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Conquest.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: Conquest">The Lord of the Rings: Conquest</a></i>:
</p>
<dl><dd>Uruk-hai appear as enemies during the Battle of Helm&#39;s Deep.</dd></dl>
<p>2014: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth%253A_Shadow_of_Mordor.md" title="Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor">Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor</a></i>:
</p>
<dl><dd>Uruk-hai are mentioned in a Coded Journal entry.</dd></dl>
