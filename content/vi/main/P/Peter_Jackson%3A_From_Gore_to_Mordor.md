<i><b>Peter Jackson: From Gore to Mordor</b></i> là tập hợp các bài viết về [Peter Jackson](Peter_Jackson.md) và các bộ phim của ông.

## Từ Nhà xuất bản {#From_the_Publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Peter Jackson made cinematic history with the <i>Lord of the Rings</i> trilogy, presenting J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s story onscreen with drama and visual majesty, while shattering box office records. On the surface, his background as a director of low-budget New Zealand splatter movies would seem to make him an odd choice to direct a multimillion-dollar Hollywood film adaptation of a literary epic. A closer look at Jackson&#39;s approach to filmmaking reveals why he was the one of the few directors who could successfully meet the challenge. This collection, the sixth title in the <i>Ultrascreen</i> series, includes the best reviews, essays, and features on Jackson&#39;s films, plus interviews with Jackson himself, offers a comprehensive examination of his career to date, from humble beginnings as a child filmmaker, shooting movies in his backyard, to early successes like <i>Bad Taste</i> and <i>Dead Alive</i>, to his current position as one of the world&#39;s most feted directors.
</p>
</blockquote>
