**Old Ford** là điểm mà [Khu Rừng Cổ Đường](Old_Forest_Road.md) bắc qua Sông [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), cách [Carrock](../C/Carrock.md) khoảng 40 dặm về phía hạ lưu.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Ngày xưa, ở đây có một cây cầu đá bắc qua sông, nhưng đến cuối [Kỷ Đệ Tam](../T/Third_Age.md), cây cầu đã biến mất từ ​​lâu và lối qua sông không khác gì một khúc cạn đơn giản.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Miêu tả trong các tác phẩm chuyển thể {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Old_Ford.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/cd/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Old_Ford.jpg/300px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Old_Ford.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="169" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/cd/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Old_Ford.jpg/450px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Old_Ford.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/cd/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Old_Ford.jpg/600px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Old_Ford.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1920" data-file-height="1080"/></a><figcaption>The Old Ford in <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i></figcaption></figure>

<b>2019: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Trực tuyến</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Old Ford is located close to the remains of <i>Hadhodiant</i>, a stone bridge built by the <a href="../D/Dwarves.md" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a> which merely stands broken rather than being completely gone. On the Western bank is Vegbár, a toll-collecting station of the <a href="../B/Beornings.md" title="Beornings">Beornings</a>, making the Old Ford one and the same with the <a href="../F/Ford_of_Carrock.md" title="Ford of Carrock">Ford of Carrock</a>. Towards the end of the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, <a href="Orcs.md" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> and <a href="../W/Wargs.md" title="Wargs">Wargs</a> came down from the <a href="../M/Misty_Mountains.md" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a> but were defeated in the Battle of the Old Ford by the Beornings and the <a href="../W/Woodmen.md" title="Woodmen">Woodmen</a>, who also suffered many casualties. <a href="../E/Elrond.md" title="Elrond">Elrond</a>, <a href="../A/Arwen.md" title="Arwen">Arwen</a> and their household also travelled by the Old Ford on their way to <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> for Arwen&#39;s wedding. Later in the summer of <a href="../T/Third_Age_3019.md" title="Third Age 3019">T.A. 3019</a> Prince <a href="../D/Durin_VII.md" title="Durin VII">Durin VII</a> led a campaign to reclaim <a href="../G/Gundabad.md" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a>, and a mission shows dwarven builders construct a wooden replacement for the missing section of the <i>Hadhodiant</i> so as to not be reliant on the Old Ford.</dd></dl>
