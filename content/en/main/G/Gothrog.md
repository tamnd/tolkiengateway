**Gothrog** was a [Demon](/wiki/%C3%9Amaiar "Úmaiar") according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)* dating from the [1930s](/wiki/1930 "1930"). Nothing more is known about him since he only appears as a name in a lexical entry.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is not even known if the name *Gothrog* refers to a separate individual or is it another name for a previously existing character.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><b>Gothrog</b></i> is [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) for "Dread Demon", from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gost&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gost (page does not exist)">gost</a></i> ("dread, terror") + the suffixal form -*rog* from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rhaug&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rhaug (page does not exist)">rhaug</a></i> ("demon").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
