**Gimli** was a [Gnome](/wiki/Gnome) in the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. He was liberated from the thraldom of [Tevildo](/wiki/Tevildo) along with [Beren](/wiki/Beren). His captivity had left him blind but with keen hearing.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) word *gimli* ("sense of hearing") is clearly related to this name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
