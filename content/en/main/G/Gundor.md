**Gundor** ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [419](/wiki/First_Age_419 "First Age 419")-[456](/wiki/First_Age_456 "First Age 456"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> aged 37) was the third child and the younger son of [Hador Lórindol](/wiki/Hador "Hador").

Gundor and his father Hador fought in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) and both died, pierced with many arrows at [Eithel Sirion](/wiki/Eithel_Sirion).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
