The **Gwaith-i-Mírdain**, the **People of the Jewel-smiths**, was a brotherhood of [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") master craftsmen of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) who created the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power).

## History {#History}

The Gwaith were founded by [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor) and other [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) who settled in [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion), close to the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") and their *[mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril")* mines. Their capital was [Ost-in-Edhil](/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil) ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>).

Noldor and [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm "Dwarves of Khazad-dûm") cooperated for about a thousand years, and their rare friendship was the closest between [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) in history. Celebrimbor helped to construct the famous and magical [West-gate of Moria](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin "Doors of Durin"). The Gwaith were said to be the most talented artisans to have worked since the time of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) himself (who was Celebrimbor's grandfather).

[Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), after failing with [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) and [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), targeted the Gwaith. In the guise of [Annatar](/wiki/Annatar) the "Lord of Gifts" in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1200](/wiki/Second_Age_1200 "Second Age 1200") he presented himself as an agent of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and taught them how to forge the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power). The Gwaith were easily tempted since they wanted to bring to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) the joys of Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L-3">[3]</a></sup> The Rings would prevent or slow the decay of the mortal world, preserve what was desired or loved and also enhance the natural powers of a possessor even if that would mean disrupting the natural course of the mortal world.<sup id="cite_ref-L_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Gwaith together with "Annatar" made several rings in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1500](/wiki/Second_Age_1500 "Second Age 1500"). Because of their friendship with the Dwarves, Celebrimbor presented King [Durin III](/wiki/Durin_III) with [a Ring of Power](/wiki/Ring_of_Thr%C3%B3r "Ring of Thrór").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> When Annatar had left (actually for [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor)) about [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1590](/wiki/Second_Age_1590 "Second Age 1590") Celebrimbor made the [Three Rings](/wiki/Three_Rings)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> partly using the teachings of "Annatar".

Then Sauron betrayed them and forged [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"). However, the Gwaith realized his plan and denied to hand him their Rings, resulting in the [War of the Elves and Sauron](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron). Eregion was devastated in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697") and the survivors of the Gwaith followed [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and retreated to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gwaith-i-Mírdain* means "Brotherhood of Jewel-smiths" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (from *[gwaith](/wiki/Gwaith "Gwaith")* = "host, people" and *[mírdain](/wiki/M%C3%ADrdain "Mírdain")* = "jewel-smiths").
