The **Gwaith-i-Mírdain**, the **People of the Jewel-smiths**, was a brotherhood of [Elven](../E/Elves.md) master craftsmen of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) who created the [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md).

## History {#History}

The Gwaith were founded by [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md) and other [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) of [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) who settled in [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md), close to the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md) and their *[mithril](../M/Mithril.md)* mines. Their capital was [Ost-in-Edhil](../O/Ost-in-Edhil.md) ([S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [750](../S/Second_Age_750.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>).

Noldor and [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves_of_Khazad-dûm.md) cooperated for about a thousand years, and their rare friendship was the closest between [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) in history. Celebrimbor helped to construct the famous and magical [West-gate of Moria](../D/Doors_of_Durin.md). The Gwaith were said to be the most talented artisans to have worked since the time of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) himself (who was Celebrimbor's grandfather).

[Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), after failing with [Gil-galad](Gil-galad.md) and [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md), targeted the Gwaith. In the guise of [Annatar](../A/Annatar.md) the "Lord of Gifts" in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1200](../S/Second_Age_1200.md) he presented himself as an agent of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and taught them how to forge the [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md). The Gwaith were easily tempted since they wanted to bring to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) the joys of Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L-3">[3]</a></sup> The Rings would prevent or slow the decay of the mortal world, preserve what was desired or loved and also enhance the natural powers of a possessor even if that would mean disrupting the natural course of the mortal world.<sup id="cite_ref-L_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Gwaith together with "Annatar" made several rings in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1500](../S/Second_Age_1500.md). Because of their friendship with the Dwarves, Celebrimbor presented King [Durin III](../D/Durin_III.md) with [a Ring of Power](../R/Ring_of_Thrór.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> When Annatar had left (actually for [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md)) about [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1590](../S/Second_Age_1590.md) Celebrimbor made the [Three Rings](../T/Three_Rings.md)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> partly using the teachings of "Annatar".

Then Sauron betrayed them and forged [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md). However, the Gwaith realized his plan and denied to hand him their Rings, resulting in the [War of the Elves and Sauron](../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md). Eregion was devastated in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1697](../S/Second_Age_1697.md) and the survivors of the Gwaith followed [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) and retreated to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gwaith-i-Mírdain* means "Brotherhood of Jewel-smiths" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) (from *[gwaith](Gwaith.md)* = "host, people" and *[mírdain](../M/Mírdain.md)* = "jewel-smiths").
