**Galdor** can refer to:

- [Galdor (elf of Gondolin)](</wiki/Galdor_(elf_of_Gondolin)>), the lord of the [House of the Tree](/wiki/House_of_the_Tree).

- [Galdor (elf of the Havens)](</wiki/Galdor_(elf_of_the_Havens)>), [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan)'s representative at the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond).
