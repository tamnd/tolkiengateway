The **Grey Company** was a company of thirty [Northern Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor "Dúnedain of Arnor") (clad in grey) commanded by [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)'s kinsman [Halbarad](/wiki/Halbarad).<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Halbarad received a mysterious message requesting to come to the aid of his [Chieftain](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "Chieftain of the Dúnedain") [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) at [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) ([Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) later surmised that it was actually sent by [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) who could read the hearts of men). Gathering thirty of his kin, and aided by [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan) and [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir) who wished to join the [war](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring "War of the Ring"), they rode south to [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). They found Aragorn near [Dol Baran](/wiki/Dol_Baran) before dawn on [6 March](/wiki/6_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> and he admitted that Halbarad was often in his thoughts but had sent no message. Halbarad bore with him a furled standard, which was a gift from [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen), and a message from her; and Elrohir conveyed a message from [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) to take the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead). They journeyed with the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) to [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep), where they parted ways.<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Rangers rode through the plains of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) and reached [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) by morning of [7 March](/wiki/7_March).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> Théoden had not yet arrived, and [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) in vain begged Aragorn to avoid the Paths of the Dead.<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-1">[1]</a></sup>

Spurred only by the strength of Aragorn's will, the Company with [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) entered the dark path at the dawn of [the next day](/wiki/8_March "8 March").<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> At midnight,<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> Aragorn summoned the Dead at a clearing to follow him to the [Stone of Erech](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech). After creeping in the darkness for what seemed like ages, the Company emerged from the Paths and rode quickly through the mountain fields with the Dead behind. They followed the river [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond) briefly to [Erech](/wiki/Erech). The inhabitants of the surrounding countryside fled in fear, calling Aragorn the "King of the Dead".

Arriving at the large, black Stone of Erech at midnight,<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> the legion of the Oathbreakers announced their allegiance to Aragorn. Aragorn unfurled a black flag and pronounced himself the [Heir of Isildur](/wiki/Heir_of_Isildur). The Company rode on to [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [9 March](/wiki/9_March)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> they led the [Oathbreakers](/wiki/Oathbreakers) over [Tarlang's Neck](/wiki/Tarlang%27s_Neck), across the [Ciril](/wiki/Ciril) and [Ethring](/wiki/Ethring) on the way to [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) and reached [Calembel](/wiki/Calembel).<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-1">[1]</a></sup> [A day later](/wiki/10_March "10 March") they passed across the river [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup>

On [11 March](/wiki/11_March) they were in [Lamedon](/wiki/Lamedon), and the Dead drove forth the assailants from [Linhir](/wiki/Linhir), but the staunch defenders fled as well, except for [Angbor](/wiki/Angbor). Aragorn bade him gather his folk and follow them. After Linhir they reached [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar) were beaten back to [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) the [next day](/wiki/12_March "12 March").

On [13 March](/wiki/13_March)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> they found Pelargir overrun by Corsairs once again with fifty great ships and many more smaller vessels ready to sail to [Harlond](</wiki/Harlond_(Gondor)> "Harlond (Gondor)"). The Company and the Dead drove the Corsairs away. After manning the ships, Aragorn released the curse of the Dead.<sup id="cite_ref-Debate_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Debate-3">[3]</a></sup> Halbarad captained a Corsair ship and they sailed toward Harlond.

They arrived at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields) on [15 March](/wiki/15_March),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> where Halbarad fell.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<b>2010: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Grey Company plays a major role in the game beginning with the <i>Volume III: Allies of the King</i>. Other than <a href="/wiki/Halbarad" title="Halbarad">Halbarad</a> and the Sons of <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a>, sixty-two other Rangers and the player form the Grey Company as they journey south. Their path through <a href="/wiki/Dunland" title="Dunland">Dunland</a> is beset by many dangers, as many Rangers are killed and some are forced to either turn back or stay behind until only thirty remain by the time they finally meet with <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>. Other than Halbarad, seven other Rangers are killed in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a> while the rest join the Host of the West on the march towards the <a href="/wiki/Black_Gate" title="Black Gate">Black Gate</a>. Only one of the Rangers falls during the battle and all of the survivors attend the wedding of Aragorn and <a href="/wiki/Arwen" title="Arwen">Arwen</a> after which they finally part ways. Some decide to return north to <a href="/wiki/Eriador" title="Eriador">Eriador</a> while others choose to remain in <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> and serve King Elessar alongside <a href="/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien" title="Rangers of Ithilien">Rangers of Ithilien</a> against the dangers still remaining in <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a>.</dd></dl>
