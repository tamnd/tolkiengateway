**Galadriel's gifts**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> were items [Galadriel](Galadriel.md) gave to the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) on [16 February](../_/16_February.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> to aid them in their journey.

Before the parting feast, the [Galadhrim](Galadhrim.md) gave the members of the Fellowship these items:

- [Lembas](../L/Lembas.md) for food
- [Elven cloaks](../E/Elven_cloaks.md) with [Elven brooches](../E/Elven_brooches.md)
- Some [Elven rope](../E/Elven_rope~74291.md) for [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)
- Boats for the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) (Mostly Celeborn)
- Feast that featured Galadriel's singing

At the parting feast, Galadriel presented specific gifts to each member of the Fellowship:

- The [Elfstone](../E/Elfstone.md) for [Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn.md)
- A [sheath](../A/Andúril's_sheath.md) for [Andúril](../A/Andúril.md) for [Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn.md)
- A [golden belt](../B/Boromir's_belt.md) for [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md)
- A [silver belt](../M/Merry_and_Pippin's_belts.md) for [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md)
- A silver belt for [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md)
- A [Galadhrim bow](../B/Bow_of_the_Galadhrim.md) strung with elf hair and arrows for [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md)
- A [small grey wooden box](../S/Sam's_garden_box.md) with a silver rune "G for Galadriel". Inside the box was earth from Galadriel's orchard. The box also contained a silver nut from a [Mallorn](../M/Mallorn.md) tree, the last east of the sea and west of the mountains. This was given to Sam.
- [Three strands of Galadriel's hair](../H/Hair_of_Galadriel.md) for [Gimli](Gimli.md)
- The [Phial of Galadriel](../P/Phial_of_Galadriel.md) for [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
