**Galadriel's gifts**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> were items [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) gave to the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) on [16 February](/wiki/16_February) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> to aid them in their journey.

Before the parting feast, the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) gave the members of the Fellowship these items:

- [Lembas](/wiki/Lembas) for food
- [Elven cloaks](/wiki/Elven_cloaks) with [Elven brooches](/wiki/Elven_brooches)
- Some [Elven rope](/wiki/Elven_rope) for [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee")
- Boats for the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) (Mostly Celeborn)
- Feast that featured Galadriel's singing

At the parting feast, Galadriel presented specific gifts to each member of the Fellowship:

- The [Elfstone](/wiki/Elfstone) for [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn")
- A [sheath](/wiki/And%C3%BAril%27s_sheath "Andúril's sheath") for [Andúril](/wiki/And%C3%BAril) for [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn")
- A [golden belt](/wiki/Boromir%27s_belt "Boromir's belt") for [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir)
- A [silver belt](/wiki/Merry_and_Pippin%27s_belts "Merry and Pippin's belts") for [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck")
- A silver belt for [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took")
- A [Galadhrim bow](/wiki/Bow_of_the_Galadhrim "Bow of the Galadhrim") strung with elf hair and arrows for [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas)
- A [small grey wooden box](/wiki/Sam%27s_garden_box "Sam's garden box") with a silver rune "G for Galadriel". Inside the box was earth from Galadriel's orchard. The box also contained a silver nut from a [Mallorn](/wiki/Mallorn) tree, the last east of the sea and west of the mountains. This was given to Sam.
- [Three strands of Galadriel's hair](/wiki/Hair_of_Galadriel "Hair of Galadriel") for [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli)
- The [Phial of Galadriel](/wiki/Phial_of_Galadriel) for [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
