**Gollum's Villainy** is the fifth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* and the sixth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

While [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) and [Sam](../S/Sam.md) sleep, [Sméagol](../S/Sméagol.md) mutters in his sleep about killing them. He awakes and appears to be second-guessing his plan to lead them to *her*, before [Gollum](Gollum.md) reminds him they will be able to take the ring back for themselves. As Sam awakes, he hears Sméagol's plan to take the ring *"once the Hobbitses are dead"*. In a rage, Sam attacks him. Frodo awakes and Sam tells him that Gollum means to murder them. Knowing they need a guide to get to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md), Frodo refuses to send Gollum away.
