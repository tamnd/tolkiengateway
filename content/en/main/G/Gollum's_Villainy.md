**Gollum's Villainy** is the fifth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* and the sixth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

While [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) and [Sam](/wiki/Sam) sleep, [Sméagol](/wiki/Sm%C3%A9agol) mutters in his sleep about killing them. He awakes and appears to be second-guessing his plan to lead them to *her*, before [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) reminds him they will be able to take the ring back for themselves. As Sam awakes, he hears Sméagol's plan to take the ring *"once the Hobbitses are dead"*. In a rage, Sam attacks him. Frodo awakes and Sam tells him that Gollum means to murder them. Knowing they need a guide to get to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), Frodo refuses to send Gollum away.
