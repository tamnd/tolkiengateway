**Galadriel** was a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") Lady who witnessed [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) during the Three [Ages](/wiki/Ages).

Galadriel was born in [Aman](/wiki/Aman) as one of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor). She was the daughter of [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin) and [Eärwen](/wiki/E%C3%A4rwen) as well as younger sister to [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") (later [King of Nargothrond](/wiki/King_of_Nargothrond)), [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod), and [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor). She took part in the [Exile of the Noldor](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor), following [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) in his rebellion. However, she opposed the [Kinslaying at Alqualondë](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB) and crossed the [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB) with the rest of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin)'s followers. After arriving in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), she lived mainly in [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), where she befriended [Melian](/wiki/Melian). Later, with her husband [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), she ruled over the [woodland Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves "Silvan Elves") of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), and together they had a daughter, [Celebrían](/wiki/Celebr%C3%ADan), who married [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond).

She is described as "the mightiest and fairest of all the Elves that remained in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth)",<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-5">[3]</a></sup> and the "greatest of Elven women".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[4]</a></sup> Throughout the Third Age it was unknown who wielded the [Three Rings](/wiki/Three_Rings),<sup id="cite_ref-Third_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-7">[5]</a></sup> but during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Galadriel revealed to [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) that she was the bearer of [Nenya](/wiki/Nenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Mirror_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mirror-8">[6]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:365</sup> After the War, she returned to the [Uttermost West](/wiki/Uttermost_West).

Galadriel was born in [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar) in the [Year of the Trees 1362](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1362).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup> She was the fourth child of [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin), prince of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), who named her <i><b>Artanis</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-10">[8]</a></sup> Her mother was [Eärwen](/wiki/E%C3%A4rwen), princess of the [Teleri](/wiki/Falathrim "Falathrim"), who named her <i><b>Nerwen</b></i> ("Man-maiden"). Galadriel grew beautiful and tall, even for the women of the Noldor, being strong of body, will, and mind, "a match for both the [loremasters](/wiki/Loremasters "Loremasters") and the athletes of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) in the days of their youth."<sup id="cite_ref-Case_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-11">[9]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Marya_Filatova_-_Galadriel.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/f/f6/Marya_Filatova_-_Galadriel.jpg/300px-Marya_Filatova_-_Galadriel.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="185" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/f/f6/Marya_Filatova_-_Galadriel.jpg/450px-Marya_Filatova_-_Galadriel.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/f/f6/Marya_Filatova_-_Galadriel.jpg/600px-Marya_Filatova_-_Galadriel.jpg 2x" data-file-width="900" data-file-height="554"/></a><figcaption><i>Galadriel</i> by <a href="/wiki/Marya_Filatova" title="Marya Filatova">Marya Filatova</a></figcaption></figure>

Indeed, "she was then of Amazon disposition and bound up her hair as a crown when taking part in athletic feats". This custom was the origin of her [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) [name](/wiki/Epess%C3%AB "Epessë") <i><b>Galadriel</b></i> ("Maiden crowned with gleaming hair"),<sup id="cite_ref-Amazon_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amazon-12">[10]</a></sup> which was given to (and accepted by) her much later, in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[11]</a></sup> The gleaming of her hair was not a mere poetic reference: [her hair](/wiki/Hair_of_Galadriel "Hair of Galadriel") was held a marvel unmatched. It mingled and surpassed the gold hair of her father and the starlike silver of her mother, so the Eldar said that both [lights](/wiki/Light "Light") of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees) were captured in her tresses. It was also said that this first gave the idea to her uncle [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) of capturing the merged light of the Trees, which would result in the making of the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils). He asked her three times for one of her tresses, but she always refused. Fëanor and Galadriel were always unfriends, both being the greatest Eldar in Valinor; and if Fëanor was greater than she, she was wiser, and her wisdom grew with the long years. For she also had an outstanding gift to see into the minds of others, and, though she judged most with kindness, she hated and feared the darkness in Fëanor, not perceiving that the shadow of the same evil had fallen upon the minds of all the Noldor, including her own.<sup id="cite_ref-Case_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-11">[9]</a></sup>

There was still bliss in Valinor when she was born, but soon she was influenced by the [unrest of the Noldor](/wiki/Of_the_Silmarils_and_the_Unrest_of_the_Noldor "Of the Silmarils and the Unrest of the Noldor") and lost her inner peace ever after. She was proud, like most of the [House of Finwë](/wiki/House_of_Finw%C3%AB), and like her closest brother [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod), "she had dreams of far lands and dominions that might be her own to order as she would without tutelage". But deep in herself, she had the noble spirit of the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar), which did not allow her to forget the reverence for the [Valar](/wiki/Valar).<sup id="cite_ref-Case_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-11">[9]</a></sup>

During the [Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor) and the rebellion of Fëanor, Galadriel was the only woman of the Noldor who stood among the rising princes. She swore no [oath](/wiki/Oath_of_F%C3%ABanor "Oath of Fëanor"), but the [words of Fëanor](/wiki/Speech_of_F%C3%ABanor "Speech of Fëanor") concerning [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) kindled a desire in her heart, as she was eager to see those wide unguarded lands and rule a realm of her own.<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-14">[12]</a></sup> Like the rest of the [House of Finarfin](/wiki/House_of_Finarfin), she was also supportive of the [Exile](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor") by the desire to aid their [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") relatives in Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-Case_11-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-11">[9]</a></sup>

Even though she was one of the leaders in this revolt,<sup id="cite_ref-RGEO_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RGEO-15">[13]</a></sup> during the troubles that followed she fought fiercely against Fëanor by defending her mother's kin during the [Kinslaying at Alqualondë](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB) and the rape of the ships. Afterwards, she even desired to follow Fëanor to thwart him in all the ways that she could, but nothing could have moved her to return since she had begun her way to exile, as her pride was strong then as it was thereafter.<sup id="cite_ref-Case_11-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-11">[9]</a></sup> She kept moving forward even under the [Doom of Mandos](/wiki/Doom_of_Mandos), when her father Finarfin abandoned the march and returned to Valinor, and even when Fëanor left behind the hosts of Fingolfin and the children of Finarfin. Then she, along with Finrod and [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and his sons, led her people into the bitter North and crossed the terrors of [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB). After many losses, they arrived on the northern shores of the [Outer Lands](/wiki/Outer_Lands).<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-14">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[note 3]</a></sup>

#### Arrival to Middle-earth {#Arrival_to_Middle-earth}

Once in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), those of the House of Finarfin were eager to enter [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), but King [Elu Thingol](/wiki/Elu_Thingol) received the Noldor coldly, and only allowed entrance to those invited by him after some inquiries.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-17">[14]</a></sup> Finally in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [52](/wiki/First_Age_52 "First Age 52"),<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[15]</a></sup> Finrod and Galadriel became long guests in Doriath, but her brother eventually departed to the [Caverns of Narog](/wiki/Caverns_of_Narog) to establish the stronghold of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond). Galadriel did not go with him, and remained in Doriath with [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), a kinsman of Thingol, as there was great love between them.<sup id="cite_ref-Noldor_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Noldor-19">[16]</a></sup> Despite their love, they did not marry each other, following the [custom among the Eldar](/wiki/Elven_life_cycle "Elven life cycle") of avoiding marriage and child-bearing during the war,<sup id="cite_ref-Scales_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scales-20">[17]</a></sup> but they were eventually betrothed till the war ended.<sup id="cite_ref-Ages_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ages-21">[18]</a></sup> Meanwhile, she dwelt with Queen [Melian](/wiki/Melian), from whom she learned much of the wisdom of Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-17">[14]</a></sup>

The Western Corn necessary to make [lembas](/wiki/Lembas "Lembas") had been lost in Beleriand until the Noldor brought new grain back, and it flourished by the grace of the Valar. Galadriel was one of the main inheritors of it and of the art of the making of lembas.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[19]</a></sup>

Years passed in Doriath, and Galadriel became dear to Melian, and they spoke often to one another about Valinor. Melian was eager to learn the causes of the [Exile of the Noldor](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor), but Galadriel would tell her nothing of what occurred after the death of the Trees. In the year [66](/wiki/First_Age_66 "First Age 66"),<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[20]</a></sup> Melian asked her directly about this, and Galadriel revealed the story of the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils) and how the Noldor fled from Aman against the will of the Valar, though she still left out the Oath of Fëanor, the Kinslaying, and the burning of the Ships at [Losgar](/wiki/Losgar). Melian was able to discern a darkness that was left unsaid, but Galadriel refused to tell her more. Thus, Thingol became concerned regarding the Noldor, and he was informed soon afterwards of the Noldor's dark deeds in Aman, and it chanced that the children of Finarfin were visiting Galadriel in Doriath. Egged on by his accusing words, [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod) told everything, and Galadriel's brothers departed heavy-hearted.<sup id="cite_ref-Noldor_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Noldor-19">[16]</a></sup>

Despite all this, Galadriel stayed in Doriath until [102](/wiki/First_Age_102 "First Age 102"),<sup id="cite_ref-Nargothrond_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nargothrond-24">[21]</a></sup> when the kingdom of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) was finished and a feast was held for all the children of Finarfin. She dwelt for a while in Nargothrond, and in this time she asked Finrod why he remained unmarried. Her brother answered with dark [foresights](/wiki/Foresight "Foresight") about his kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-Noldor_19-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Noldor-19">[16]</a></sup> Back in Doriath, in [420](/wiki/First_Age_420 "First Age 420"),<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[22]</a></sup> Galadriel heard another foretelling, as Melian told her about [a Man](/wiki/Beren "Beren") who shall cross into Doriath moved by a doom greater than the [Girdle of Melian](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian).<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[23]</a></sup>

Galadriel did not have any significant role in the general course of events in the [War of the Jewels](/wiki/Battles_of_Beleriand "Battles of Beleriand"), but she might have been well informed of the deaths of her brothers [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod) and [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor) during the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach); and of Finrod's, while he was held in captivity by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in the year [465](/wiki/First_Age_465 "First Age 465").<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[24]</a></sup> At some point before the [Fall of Nargothrond](/wiki/Fall_of_Nargothrond) in [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"),<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[25]</a></sup> she and Celeborn passed over [the mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains "Blue Mountains")<sup id="cite_ref-Mirror_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mirror-8">[6]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:357</sup> to [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), far from the turmoil of the last years of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

After the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) at the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), [Eönwë](/wiki/E%C3%B6nw%C3%AB), herald of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB), summoned the [Elves of Beleriand](/wiki/Elves_of_Beleriand) to the West, so they were received in [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) and under the pardon of the Valar. However, some did not wish to leave Middle-earth, such as Celeborn.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-29">[26]</a></sup> Being one of the chief leaders of the Noldorin rebellion, Galadriel was not allowed to return<sup id="cite_ref-L|297_30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L|297-30">[27]</a></sup> and a ban was set upon her, to which she replied that she had no wish to do so.<sup id="cite_ref-RGEO_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RGEO-15">[13]</a></sup> The same pride that led her out of Aman still moved her, and she rejected the pardon of the Valar,<sup id="cite_ref-Case_11-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-11">[9]</a></sup> for as she had once lived in Aman itself, Eressëa seemed only a "second best". She was also moved by love for Celeborn, to whom she probably married around this time.<sup id="cite_ref-Scales_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scales-20">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31">[note 4]</a></sup>

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

At the beginning of the Second Age, Celeborn and Galadriel dwelt for a time in [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), south of the [Lune](/wiki/Lune), while [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) ruled in the north.<sup id="cite_ref-Second_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-32">[28]</a></sup> Both left Lindon before the years 10-20, when [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) became king in that land, apparently thanks to her influence.<sup id="cite_ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33">[29]</a></sup> They entered [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) and dwelt for a while around [Lake Nenuial](/wiki/Lake_Nenuial), being followed by many Noldor, [Grey-elves](/wiki/Grey-elves) and [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves). They were considered the Lord and Lady of the Eldar in Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_34-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-34">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:234</sup> Probably around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [300](/wiki/Second_Age_300 "Second Age 300"),<sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35">[note 5]</a></sup> Galadriel and Celeborn had a daughter: [Celebrían](/wiki/Celebr%C3%ADan).<sup id="cite_ref-Scales_20-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scales-20">[17]</a></sup>

Circa the year [500](/wiki/Second_Age_500 "Second Age 500"), [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) began to stir in Middle-earth again,<sup id="cite_ref-Second_32-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-32">[28]</a></sup> but his name was not known. He was, however, perceived by Galadriel, who noticed there was a controlling evil, and that it was spreading above the world, coming from the East beyond the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). She also thought this 'residue of evil' could only be fought with an alliance of all its enemies. Therefore, she and Celeborn moved eastwards and got established in the realm of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) near [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm).<sup id="cite_ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36">[note 6]</a></sup> Celeborn was not fond of [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), but she saw the importance of getting close to them, for she saw military interests even with them. Also, she was a Noldo, and therefore close to them in mind and in the passion for crafts; and in Valinor she had learned from [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) and [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB), the creator of the Dwarves himself.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_34-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-34">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:235</sup>

Some Noldor were also interested in establishing a realm near Khazad-dûm because they knew [mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril") had been discovered there. Therefore, many Elven-smiths came to Eregion and created good relations with the Dwarves. [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor), a descendant of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) (and therefore a distant cousin of Galadriel), was the greatest of the craft-men and the Lord of Eregion.<sup id="cite_ref-Second_32-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-32">[28]</a></sup> He was its main builder and in [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750") [Ost-in-Edhil](/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil), the main city of the realm, was begun. The power of Galadriel and Celeborn also grew: thanks to the Dwarves of Khazad-dûm, they had contact with the [Nandorin](/wiki/Nandor "Nandor") realm of [Lórinand](/wiki/L%C3%B3rinand), on the other side of the Misty Mountains. Thanks to Galadriel's influence upon Lórinand, Sauron's machinations there were fruitless.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_34-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-34">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:236</sup> She also got in contact with [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), having a meeting with King [Aldarion](/wiki/Aldarion) when he came to [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad) around [883](/wiki/Second_Age_883 "Second Age 883")-884.<sup id="cite_ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37">[31]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Alan_Lee_-_Galadriel_in_Moria.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/d/d3/Alan_Lee_-_Galadriel_in_Moria.jpg/300px-Alan_Lee_-_Galadriel_in_Moria.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="539" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/d/d3/Alan_Lee_-_Galadriel_in_Moria.jpg/450px-Alan_Lee_-_Galadriel_in_Moria.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/d/d3/Alan_Lee_-_Galadriel_in_Moria.jpg/600px-Alan_Lee_-_Galadriel_in_Moria.jpg 2x" data-file-width="756" data-file-height="1359"/></a><figcaption><i>Galadriel in Moria</i> by <a href="/wiki/Alan_Lee" title="Alan Lee">Alan Lee</a></figcaption></figure>

About the year [1200](/wiki/Second_Age_1200 "Second Age 1200"), Sauron came in disguise to Eriador, but he was only welcomed in Eregion by Celebrimbor and the Elven-smiths, who were interested in his advice on craftsmanship.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_34-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-34">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:236</sup> Galadriel was not deceived, and rejected him, saying that he was not in the training of Aulë as he claimed.<sup id="cite_ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38">[32]</a></sup> "*He perceived at once that Galadriel would be his chief adversary and obstacle, and he endeavoured therefore to placate her, bearing her scorn with outward patience and courtesy*". At the same time, without her knowledge, Sauron influenced Celebrimbor and his [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-M%C3%ADrdain) against Galadriel and Celeborn. Finally, he moved them to expell Galadriel from Eregion at some time between [1350](/wiki/Second_Age_1350 "Second Age 1350") and 1400.<sup id="cite_ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39">[note 7]</a></sup> Thus, Galadriel and Celebrían left through Khazad-dûm to Lórinand, although Celeborn would not enter the Dwarvish realm and remained in Eregion.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_34-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-34">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:237</sup>

When Sauron made [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1600](/wiki/Second_Age_1600 "Second Age 1600"), his treachery was discovered by Celebrimbor.<sup id="cite_ref-Second_32-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-32">[28]</a></sup> The elven-smith came to Lórinand, seeking counsel from Galadriel. They did not find the strength to destroy the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power), but she advised him to hide the [Three Rings](/wiki/Three_Rings) of the Elves where Sauron could not find them. Therefore he gave her [Nenya](/wiki/Nenya), the White Ring, and the other Rings were given to other Elven lords.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_34-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-34">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:237</sup> This was done in [1693](/wiki/Second_Age_1693 "Second Age 1693"), the same year Sauron began [the war against the Elves](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron "War of the Elves and Sauron").<sup id="cite_ref-Second_32-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-32">[28]</a></sup>

With the invasion of Eriador, [Eregion was destroyed](/wiki/Sack_of_Eregion "Sack of Eregion") and Celebrimbor killed. Despite using torture, Sauron could not learn from him where the Elven Rings were, although he suspected they were kept by Galadriel and Gil-galad.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_34-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-34">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:238</sup> But thanks to the military intervention of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), Sauron was defeated in [1700](/wiki/Second_Age_1700 "Second Age 1700").<sup id="cite_ref-Second_32-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-32">[28]</a></sup> Soon afterwards, Galadriel was affected by [sea-longing](/wiki/Sea-longing "Sea-longing"), and she decided to leave Lórinand and dwell beside the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_34-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-34">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:240</sup> As one of the [High-elves](/wiki/High-elves), her yearning for the sea was unavoidable, and now she became burdened with the desire to cross to the Blessed Realm, but the ban upon her impeded it.<sup id="cite_ref-RGEO_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RGEO-15">[13]</a></sup> Thus she did not go to the sea yet, but crossed west of the mountains through Khazad-dûm with Celebrían. Seeking Celeborn, they travelled to [Imladris](/wiki/Imladris), a refuge created by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) during the war. Once there, a [Council](/wiki/White_Council "White Council") was held, in which it was decided that Imladris would be the Elvish stronghold in the East, rather than the desolate Eregion.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_34-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-34">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:239–40</sup>

Galadriel, Celeborn and Celebrían dwelt a long time in Imladris, but at some point they left and moved near the sea, to [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas), at the place later known as [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth). There were few inhabitants there, but they were visited by Nandorin parties.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_34-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-34">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:238</sup> Apparently, they returned to Lórien twice before the [Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance) and the end of the Second Age,<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_40-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-40">[33]</a></sup> but of their whereabouts during the final millennia of that Age nothing else is known.

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

#### Rule in Lórien {#Rule_in_Lórien}

In early [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), in the year [109](/wiki/Third_Age_109 "Third Age 109"), Galadriel and Celeborn's daughter [Celebrían](/wiki/Celebr%C3%ADan) wedded [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) the [Half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven).<sup id="cite_ref-Third_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-7">[5]</a></sup> But they did not stay at first with her, for Galadriel was filled with [foreboding](/wiki/Foresight "Foresight"), and was concerned about the growing shadow in [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) and [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur). Therefore she and Celeborn made long journeys investigating [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion), going from [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and the borders of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) to [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) in the north. They also stayed a long time in Lórien with king [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth), for Galadriel saw with her wisdom that this prosperous realm could be a stronghold against the growing Shadow, if it had better rule.<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_40-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-40">[33]</a></sup> At this point, the [Istari](/wiki/Istari) arrived in Middle-earth, and only Galadriel, Elrond and [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) knew [where](/wiki/Valinor "Valinor") they came from.<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-5">[3]</a></sup> Around the year [1100](/wiki/Third_Age_1100 "Third Age 1100"), the [Wise](/wiki/Wise) (including Galadriel as one the chief Eldar) were well aware of the evil dwelling in Dol Guldur, but thought it was one of the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl).<sup id="cite_ref-Third_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-7">[5]</a></sup>

After their enquiries, Galadriel and Celeborn passed over the mountains to Imladris, and there dwelt for many years with their kin.<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_40-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-40">[33]</a></sup>

Between the years [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1980](/wiki/Third_Age_1980 "Third Age 1980")-[1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981"), [a terror](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane "Durin's Bane") appeared in Khazad-dûm and the Dwarves abandoned the realm, while the many Silvan Elves from Lórien had to flee south.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-7">[5]</a></sup> To make things worse, Amroth was lost, leaving no heir, and the Elven realm was left without a ruler. Therefore Celeborn and Galadriel went there, welcomed by the people, and took up the rule jointly, but refused to take the [royal title](/wiki/King_of_L%C3%B3rien "King of Lórien") and were called the [Lord and Lady of Galadhrim](/wiki/Lord_and_Lady_of_Galadhrim). Saving the realm from the perils of the moment, they dwelt and ruled permanently there.<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_40-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-40">[33]</a></sup>

As the shadow of Dol Guldur grew again, the [White Council](/wiki/White_Council) was formed in [2463](/wiki/Third_Age_2463 "Third Age 2463").<sup id="cite_ref-Third_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-7">[5]</a></sup> It was summoned by Galadriel,<sup id="cite_ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41">[34]</a></sup> who, being a good friend of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), recommended that he be made head of the Council. But this distinction fell instead to [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman).<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-5">[3]</a></sup>

In [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"), her daughter Celebrían departed to the West after being wounded by Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_7-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-7">[5]</a></sup> The same year, she aided [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) and his [riders](/wiki/Rohirrim "Rohirrim") in the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant), creating a white mist that drove out the shadows from Dol Guldur.<sup id="cite_ref-42" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-42">[35]</a></sup>

The White Council would not take action until Gandalf discovered that the shadow growing in Dol Guldur was [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). After deliberating, the Council joined forces and drove Sauron out of Dol Guldur in [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941").<sup id="cite_ref-Third_7-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-7">[5]</a></sup>

Some years later, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) entered Lothlórien after many travels, and was admitted by Galadriel. She bade him change his worn-out clothes and dressed him like a proper prince. At that time, Aragorn became betrothed to her granddaughter [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen).<sup id="cite_ref-43" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-43">[36]</a></sup>

#### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Trial.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/a/aa/Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Trial.png/300px-Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Trial.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="424" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/a/aa/Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Trial.png/450px-Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Trial.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/a/aa/Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Trial.png/600px-Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Trial.png 2x" data-file-width="765" data-file-height="1080"/></a><figcaption><i>The Trial</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Ralph_Damiani" title="Category:Images by Ralph Damiani">Ralph Damiani</a></figcaption></figure>

Galadriel and Celeborn were well informed about the [Company of the Ring](/wiki/Company_of_the_Ring), its members and their mission, before its coming to Lothlórien, perhaps informed by messages from Rivendell.<sup id="cite_ref-44" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-44">[37]</a></sup> In the evening of [17 January](/wiki/17_January) of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), the Company arrived at [Caras Galadhon](/wiki/Caras_Galadhon).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_45-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-45">[38]</a></sup> Coming to the [talan](/wiki/Talan "Talan") of the Lady and Lord, each member was greeted by name by Celeborn. Noticing that Gandalf was missing, Galadriel asked where he was, for she wished to speak with him. Hearing that they had encountered [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane) and that this was in fact a [Balrog](/wiki/Balrog) from ancient times, Celeborn criticized the passing of the Company through Moria. However, Galadriel was more sympathetic, especially towards [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), who bowed to her in respect. Before inviting the members of the Company to rest and heal there, she looked deep into their eyes and they all felt their hearts exposed.<sup id="cite_ref-Mirror_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mirror-8">[6]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:354–7</sup>

A month later, in [15 February](/wiki/15_February),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_45-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-45">[38]</a></sup> [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) the [bearer of the One Ring](/wiki/Ringbearer "Ringbearer"), was walking in the evening with [Sam](/wiki/Sam) when they encountered Galadriel. She led them to an enclosed garden and showed them a silver basin. Filling it with water, a [Mirror](/wiki/Mirror_of_Galadriel "Mirror of Galadriel") was formed, which allowed them to see things that were and that might come. Frodo saw the [Eye of Sauron](/wiki/Eye_of_Sauron), and she explained that she knew well Frodo's mind, for Sauron also tried to look into hers. She also [kept](/wiki/Keepers_of_the_Three_Rings "Keepers of the Three Rings") a [Ring of Power](/wiki/Ring_of_Power), and showing him [Nenya](/wiki/Nenya), the Ring of Adamant, she explained that its power would disappear once Frodo destroyed the One, and Lórien would fade. Then Frodo offered the One Ring to her, and she laughed in response, saying: "*I do not deny that my heart has greatly desired to ask what you offer.*" Then she began to ponder how powerful she would become with the Ring's power, and from Nenya came a great light that covered her overwhelming height. But the light faded and she laughed again, saying: "*I pass the test. I will diminish, and go into the West, and remain Galadriel*."<sup id="cite_ref-Mirror_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mirror-8">[6]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:360–6</sup> Indeed, with this rejection of temptation and her long opposition against Sauron, the ban upon her return to Aman was finally lifted.<sup id="cite_ref-RGEO_15-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RGEO-15">[13]</a></sup>

That night, the Lord and Lady summoned the Company and spoke of their departure, which was appointed for the following morning. The next day, she sent the Fellowship off on their Quest, furnished with [gifts](/wiki/Gifts_of_Galadriel "Gifts of Galadriel") for all the members, including [lembas](/wiki/Lembas "Lembas") and [Elven cloaks](/wiki/Elven_cloaks). Later, a farewell party was held, which the Lord and Lady attended in a [swan boat](/wiki/Swan-ships "Swan-ships"); from it, Galadriel sang a [song](/wiki/Galadriel%27s_Song "Galadriel's Song") about [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar). After everyone ate and drank, she proceeded to give [specific gifts](/wiki/Gifts_of_Galadriel "Gifts of Galadriel") to each member.<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_46-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-46">[39]</a></sup>

Among the gifts was the [Elfstone](/wiki/Elfstone), which she gave to Aragorn from Arwen "*as a token of hope*".<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_46-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-46">[39]</a></sup> The jewel was also in part a bridal gift and a token of the future wedding of Arwen and Aragorn, done following the [Noldorin custom](/wiki/Elven_customs "Elven customs") in which the bride's mother should give to the bridegroom a jewel (Arwen's mother Celebrían had departed long ago, so Galadriel did it in her place).<sup id="cite_ref-47" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-47">[40]</a></sup> Galadriel stated she was unsure as to what to give Gimli, and asked what he wanted. After complimenting her beauty, he requested a single strand of [her hair](/wiki/Hair_of_Galadriel "Hair of Galadriel"). Instead, she rewarded him with three strands, which he promised to put into an imperishable crystal in memory of her. Gimli's gift actually consists of what Galadriel had denied to Fëanor. And last, to Frodo, she gave a [a small crystal phial](/wiki/Phial_of_Galadriel "Phial of Galadriel") specially prepared by her, which contained the [light](/wiki/Light_of_Valinor "Light of Valinor") of the [star of Eärendil](/wiki/Star_of_E%C3%A4rendil "Star of Eärendil").<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_46-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-46">[39]</a></sup>

The Company saw her one last time while sailing down to the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin): a shining figure, all clad in white, raising her arms, and Frodo was able to hear her clearly reciting [a long poem in Elvish](/wiki/Nam%C3%A1ri%C3%AB "Namárië") despite the distance.<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_46-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-46">[39]</a></sup>

On [the following day](/wiki/17_February "17 February"), a resurrected Gandalf was brought to Lórien by [Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_45-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-45">[38]</a></sup> who was sent by Galadriel. He was received in Caras Galadhon, and was healed and clothed in white. He also took and gave counsel. Before he left, Galadriel gave him [some prophetic messages](/wiki/Galadriel%27s_messages "Galadriel's messages") for Aragorn, [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and Gimli.<sup id="cite_ref-48" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-48">[41]</a></sup>

The land of Lórien was not exempt from the battles of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring): between [11](/wiki/11_March "11 March") and [18 March](/wiki/18_March), the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) suffered [three assaults](/wiki/Assaults_on_Lothl%C3%B3rien "Assaults on Lothlórien") from Dol Guldur.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_45-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-45">[38]</a></sup> After the [Fall of Barad-dûr](/wiki/Fall_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr), Celeborn crossed the Anduin with a host and began the [destruction of Dol Guldur](/wiki/Fall_of_Dol_Guldur "Fall of Dol Guldur").<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_49-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-49">[42]</a></sup> Soon afterward, Galadriel threw down the walls of the fortress and opened its pits.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_45-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-45">[38]</a></sup>

#### Later history {#Later_history}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Victoria_Clare_-_On_the_Road_to_the_Grey_Havens.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/2/2c/Victoria_Clare_-_On_the_Road_to_the_Grey_Havens.jpg/300px-Victoria_Clare_-_On_the_Road_to_the_Grey_Havens.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="206" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/2/2c/Victoria_Clare_-_On_the_Road_to_the_Grey_Havens.jpg/450px-Victoria_Clare_-_On_the_Road_to_the_Grey_Havens.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/2/2c/Victoria_Clare_-_On_the_Road_to_the_Grey_Havens.jpg/600px-Victoria_Clare_-_On_the_Road_to_the_Grey_Havens.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1200" data-file-height="825"/></a><figcaption><i>On the Road to the Grey Havens</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Victoria_Clare" title="Category:Images by Victoria Clare">Victoria Clare</a></figcaption></figure>

Galadriel, Celeborn and their company attended the wedding of Aragorn and Arwen. Soon afterward they joined the crowded funeral riding of King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) to [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). After the funerals in the [Golden Hall](/wiki/Golden_Hall), they continued their travel northwards with other companions and halted in [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard). [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) reveals that he had freed Saruman, and regrets that he will not see Galadriel and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) again. Celeborn replied that he is unsure but Galadriel affirmed that it would be her last meeting with him, giving Treebeard some [cryptic words](/wiki/Arda_Healed "Arda Healed"). They continued on their way and encountered [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman), who, released by <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Fangorn" title="Fangorn">Fangorn</a>, snapped that she always had hated him, and now he would find comfort knowing that her realm was destroyed as well as his.<sup id="cite_ref-50" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-50">[43]</a></sup>

Two years later, on [22 September](/wiki/22_September) [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021"),<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_49-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-49">[42]</a></sup> Frodo and Sam encountered a [Wandering Company](/wiki/Wandering_Companies "Wandering Companies") while travelling around [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), and found that Elrond, Galadriel and [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo) were part of it. Nenya was visible on Galadriel's finger, and she seemed to shine like the moon. Frodo decided to depart with them and the hobbits joined the company, led by Elrond and Galadriel.<sup id="cite_ref-Havens_51-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Havens-51">[44]</a></sup> On [29 September](/wiki/29_September), they reached the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) and departed over the Sea. Thus ended the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_49-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-49">[42]</a></sup>

Sometime later during the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), she received Celeborn, her husband.<sup id="cite_ref-52" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-52">[45]</a></sup> And in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [120](/wiki/Fourth_Age_120 "Fourth Age 120"),<sup id="cite_ref-53" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-53">[46]</a></sup> it is said that [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) went to the West out of his desire to see Galadriel again. He may have been allowed thanks to her intervention.<sup id="cite_ref-54" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-54">[47]</a></sup>

*Galadriel* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name translated by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") as "maiden crowned with a garland of bright radiance",<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-10">[8]</a></sup> "Maiden crowned with gleaming hair",<sup id="cite_ref-Amazon_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amazon-12">[10]</a></sup> "glittering garland",<sup id="cite_ref-55" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-55">[48]</a></sup> and "maiden crowned with a radiant garland".<sup id="cite_ref-SApp_56-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-56">[49]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-57" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-57">[50]</a></sup> *Galadriel* consists of *[galad](/wiki/Galad "Galad")* ("light, radiance") + *[rî](/wiki/R%C3%AE "Rî")* ("crown").<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-10">[8]</a></sup> It was an [epessë](/wiki/Epess%C3%AB "Epessë") given to her in her youth because, while doing exercise, she usually bound up her long golden hair as a crown.<sup id="cite_ref-Amazon_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amazon-12">[10]</a></sup>

Tolkien notes that the element *galad* had no relation to Sindarin *[galadh](/wiki/Galadh "Galadh")* or [Silvan](/wiki/Silvan) *galað* ("tree").<sup id="cite_ref-SApp_56-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-56">[49]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-58" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-58">[51]</a></sup> Due to this coincidence the name *Galadriel* was occasionally rendered as <i><b>Galadhriel</b></i>, as Tolkien explains:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>When Celeborn and Galadriel became the rulers of the [<a href="/wiki/Galadhrim" title="Galadhrim">Galadhrim</a>], the name of Galadriel became associated with trees, an association that was aided by the name of her husband, which also appeared to contain a tree-word; so that outside <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/L%C3%B3rien" title="Lórien">Lórien</a> among those whose memories of the ancient days and Galadriel&#39;s history had grown dim her name was often altered to &#39;Galadhriel&#39;, though never in Lórien itself.<sup id="cite_ref-59" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-59">[52]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>

## Other names {#Other_names}

At the time of her birth, [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin) named his only daughter <i><b>Artanis</b></i>. Her [father-name](/wiki/Father-name "Father-name") meant "Noble Woman" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), from *[arta](/wiki/Arta "Arta")* + *[nís](/wiki/N%C3%ADs "Nís")*.<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_10-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-10">[8]</a></sup>

Due to Artanis's height and her great strength of body and will, Eärwen chose the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name <i><b>Nerwen</b></i>, meaning "man-maiden".<sup id="cite_ref-Case_11-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-11">[9]</a></sup>

<i><b>Alatáriel</b></i> was the [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) name given to Galadriel by [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), meaning "Maiden Crowned with Radiant Garland", which referred to her hair.<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_10-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-10">[8]</a></sup> The Quenya form of this name was <i><b>Altáriel</b></i>, although the proper form would have been <i><b>Ñaltariel</b></i>. Its [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) cognate <i><b>Galadriel</b></i> was chosen by her to be her Sindarin name, because it was the most beautiful of her names and had been given to her as an [epessë](/wiki/Epess%C3%AB "Epessë") by her lover, Teleporno of the Teleri, whom she wedded later in Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_10-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-10">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-60" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-60">[note 8]</a></sup>

### The History of *The Lord of the Rings* {#The_History_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings}

The first appearance of the character of Galadriel in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") was during the composition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. The first reference [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) ever made to her was in some notes for the "[Lothlórien](</wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien_(chapter)> "Lothlórien (chapter)")" chapter, which mentioned that a "Lord of Galadrim and a Lady" went to the White Council. The following phrases are a stub of them receiving the Company of the Ring, both clad in white and with white hair. In other isolated notes about this chapter, Tolkien wondered "if Galadriel is alone and is wife of Elrond."<sup id="cite_ref-61" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-61">[53]</a></sup>

The first mention of "Keleborn and Galadriel" by name was in the narrative of the [following chapter](/wiki/The_Mirror_of_Galadriel "The Mirror of Galadriel"), although Tolkien considered other Elvish names before writing *Galadriel* in ink.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_62-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-62">[54]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:261</sup> There Galadriel describes Keleborn and herself as "*wise beyond the measure of the Elves of Middle-earth*", and says that they "*have dwelt there since the Mountains were reared and the Sun was young*", from which [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) deduced they were both conceived as [exiled Noldor](/wiki/Exiled_Noldor "Exiled Noldor").<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_62-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-62">[54]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:263</sup> After the episode with the Company, both names are changed, so she became *Galadrien* ("tree-lady") in the next pages.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_62-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-62">[54]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:249</sup> Other notable details that were altered later include the white colour of her hair,<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_62-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-62">[54]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:256</sup> that she was the keeper of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ring_of_Earth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ring of Earth (page does not exist)">Ring of Earth</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_62-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-62">[54]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:252</sup> and that after the departure of the Company, Frodo was supposed to never see Galadriel ever again.<sup id="cite_ref-63" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-63">[55]</a></sup>

In a list of matters "to be explained before the end", Tolkien wondered about what happened with Galadriel after the War of the Ring:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>They learn [...] that Nazgûl razed Lórien and Keleborn fled with a remnant to <a href="/wiki/Mirkwood" title="Mirkwood">Mirkwood</a>. Galadriel was lost or was hidden. Or shall Lórien be left slowly to fade? Yes. Galadriel parts with Keleborn who elects to stay in the world and [?woods]. She is seen by Frodo in old age, when he and Sam see Galadriel and Bilbo...<sup id="cite_ref-64" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-64">[56]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

In sum, earlier and rejected names during the composition of *The Lord of the Rings* were *Finduilas*, *Rhien*, *Galdrin*, *Galdrien*, *Galadhrien*, and *Galadrien*.<sup id="cite_ref-65" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-65">[57]</a></sup>

### Expanding the First Age {#Expanding_the_First_Age}

In the [Appendix F](/wiki/Appendix_F) of *The Lord of the Rings*, Tolkien had already stated that Galadriel was "of the royal house of Finrod, father of Felagund, Lord of Nargothrond" (in the Second Edition, Tolkien changed the names to *Finarfin* and *Finrod Felagund*, respectively). Although not using much detail, Tolkien was thus introducing Galadriel in "The Silmarillion" tradition, and having finished *The Lord of the Rings*, he began the work of expanding the backstory of the character. Galadriel enters this tradition in the *[Annals of Aman](/wiki/Annals_of_Aman)*, in which she is first mentioned when Tolkien decided the children of Finrod (later Finarfin) would be related to Thingol through Eärwen.<sup id="cite_ref-66" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-66">[58]</a></sup> In the *Annals*, written after the publication of *The Lord of the Rings*,<sup id="cite_ref-67" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-67">[59]</a></sup> Galadriel stands out by being valiant and the only woman leading the Exile of the Noldor,<sup id="cite_ref-68" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-68">[60]</a></sup> but there is no explanation of the nature of her rebellion or its later impact on her life.

Indeed, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) explains that when the chapter "Farewell to Lórien" was written, his father had not conceived the idea of a ban upon Galadriel's return due to her role in the Flight of the Noldor.<sup id="cite_ref-History_69-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-History-69">[61]</a></sup> In a letter from [1953](/wiki/1953) (even before *The Lord of the Rings* was published), Tolkien did not object to the comparison of Galadriel with the [Virgin Mary](/wiki/Virgin_Mary),<sup id="cite_ref-70" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-70">[62]</a></sup> [a comparison he would object](#Rebel_exile) to later.

Written in the same years, the *[Grey Annals](/wiki/Grey_Annals)* follow the *Annals of Aman*, telling the chronology of Beleriand, mainly after the Return of the Noldor. Here Galadriel's rol is briefly expanded, limited to her relation with Melian and a conversation with her brother Finrod.<sup id="cite_ref-71" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-71">[63]</a></sup> The darkness pending upon all the Noldor is explicitly mentioned, but the same happens with the innocence of the children of Finrod (later Finarfin) during the discussions with Thingol and Melian. Sadly, Tolkien left these *Annals* unfinished at the point of the [Wanderings of Húrin](/wiki/Wanderings_of_H%C3%BArin), so nothing is told about Galadriel at the end of the First Age, neither about her relation with Celeborn.<sup id="cite_ref-72" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-72">[note 9]</a></sup>

### Expanding the Second Age {#Expanding_the_Second_Age}

After the publication of *The Lord of the Rings*, Tolkien most expanded the story of Galadriel in the Second Age in "[Concerning Galadriel and Celeborn](/wiki/Concerning_Galadriel_and_Celeborn)". This manuscript is the only full narrative dealing with this matter, but sadly it is a stub full of corrections, and Tolkien never solved some contradictions that would be raised later.<sup id="cite_ref-73" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-73">[note 10]</a></sup> Christopher comments that what is now remarkable is that there is still no presence of the idea of a ban upon Galadriel's return: she stays in Middle-earth out of her love for Celeborn. There could be some pride on her part, as she was eager to visit Middle-earth, but later she stays in Eriador after the first fall of Sauron as a sort of duty until he was finally defeated.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_34-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-34">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:233–4</sup>

The first clear mention of pride in Galadriel's decision is probably in one of many manuscripts written c. [1959](/wiki/1959) dealing with Elven growing. In this text it is briefly mentioned that Galadriel rejected coming back to the West and live in [Eressëa](/wiki/Eress%C3%ABa), moved by her love for Celeborn and (told in a footnote) maybe by her pride, as she had lived in Aman as a princess, so the Lonely Isle would be a "second best".<sup id="cite_ref-Scales_20-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scales-20">[17]</a></sup> We see here that she was allowed to return to the West, but limited to Eressëa, far from the blessed lands (this is, Valinor and Eldamar). There is a sort of ban, not particularly against her, but her pride stands out, as it will become in an essential aspect of her character in the following "phase" of her development.

### Rebel exile {#Rebel_exile}

In mid-[1967](/wiki/1967), Tolkien wrote long drafts of a letter, in which he first mentioned the idea of Galadriel not being allowed to return after the First Age as a punishment for being one of the leaders of the Noldor rebellion. Also, in a footnote, he interprets the *[Namárië](/wiki/Nam%C3%A1ri%C3%AB)* as Galadriel's wish that Frodo might go West, as she cannot, believing she is to remain in Middle-earth forever. And more radically, Tolkien interprets the test of the Ring as granting Galadriel permission to return to Aman.<sup id="cite_ref-L|297_30-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L|297-30">[27]</a></sup> Christopher Tolkien does not believe that this letter is proof that the ban was present when such passages were written.<sup id="cite_ref-History_69-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-History-69">[61]</a></sup> Given all this, we can well affirm that what is said in this letter are reinterpretations made a posteriori.

Just two months later, *[The Road Goes Ever On: A Song Cycle](/wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On:_A_Song_Cycle)* was published in the United States, in which Tolkien included a brief biography of Galadriel in notes to the *Namárië*. Not only the Elvish poem, but the line "*What ship will carry me again across such a wide sea?*" of the poem mentioned above, are interpreted here as expressions of Galadriel's longing, unable to return to the West. Her arrogance is connected to her rebellious role, since she replied proudly to the prohibition by saying she had no desire to return.<sup id="cite_ref-RGEO_15-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RGEO-15">[13]</a></sup>

It is strange that Tolkien's new vision was so clear at this time, since there does not seem to be any intermediate text in which he considered this version or others. Now, he would go ahead with this vision, so it would also affect the character's background in the First Age.

Written in [1968](/wiki/1968) or later, *[The Shibboleth of Fëanor](/wiki/The_Shibboleth_of_F%C3%ABanor)* includes a long passage about Galadriel during her life in Aman and during the Exile. It is the first place that highlights her enmity with Fëanor before the Exile, and also her independence from him, since her desire to see and rule distant lands was already present in her before Fëanor's speech. Her role during the Flight of the Noldor is described as courageous and blameless: she had a legitimate desire to help her Teleri kin in Middle-earth, and she fought fiercely against Fëanor in the Kinslaying at Alqualondë. However, she is guilty of pride, not only in her departure, as here she is granted forgiveness after Morgoth's defeat, but she rejects it.<sup id="cite_ref-Case_11-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-11">[9]</a></sup>

A few years later, in [1971](/wiki/1971), we see that this conception of Galadriel still remains. In response to a letter, Tolkien acknowledges the influence of the figure of the Virgin Mary on the character of Galadriel, but now points out a radical difference: Galadriel was a penitent with a past marked by pride and rebellion against divine authorities.<sup id="cite_ref-74" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-74">[64]</a></sup>

### Unstained exile {#Unstained_exile}

In a letter written in August [1973](/wiki/1973) (one month before his death), Tolkien gave a completely different description of Galadriel:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Galadriel was &#39;unstained&#39;: she had committed no evil deeds. She was an enemy of Fëanor. She did not reach Middle-earth with the other Noldor, but independently. Her reasons for desiring to go to Middle-earth were legitimate, and she would have been permitted to depart, but for the misfortune that before she set out the revolt of Fëanor broke out, and she became involved in the desperate measures of Manwë, and the ban on all emigrations.<sup id="cite_ref-75" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-75">[65]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Elena_Kukanova_-_Artanis_sketch.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/4/42/Elena_Kukanova_-_Artanis_sketch.jpg/300px-Elena_Kukanova_-_Artanis_sketch.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="372" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/4/42/Elena_Kukanova_-_Artanis_sketch.jpg/450px-Elena_Kukanova_-_Artanis_sketch.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/4/42/Elena_Kukanova_-_Artanis_sketch.jpg/600px-Elena_Kukanova_-_Artanis_sketch.jpg 2x" data-file-width="633" data-file-height="785"/></a><figcaption><i>Artanis</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Elena_Kukanova" title="Category:Images by Elena Kukanova">Elena Kukanova</a></figcaption></figure>

This was not an isolated idea; around the same time he wrote an outline of the revised backstory of Galadriel, which he could not carry into narrative, as it ended up being probably his last writing related to the legendarium. Here Galadriel is described as totally opposed to Fëanor from the beginning, and she felt confined in Aman and desired to depart to Middle-earth in order to exercise her talents: "for being brilliant in mind and swift in action she had early absorbed all of what she was capable of the teaching which the Valar thought fit to give the Eldar". Manwë was probably aware of her desire and he had not forbidden her to depart, nor given permission yet. Nonetheless, Galadriel did not remain inactive and thought in the ships of the Teleri, so she dwelt in Alqualondë with her mother's kin. There she met Celeborn (here a Telerin prince and her cousin), and both planned to build a boat and seek the permission of the Valar to depart to Middle-earth.

But then Melkor and Ungoliant destroyed the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees), and Fëanor began his rebellion, in which Galadriel had no part. "Indeed, she and Celeborn fought heroically in defence of Alqualondë against the assault of the Noldor, and Celeborn's ship was saved from them". Now Galadriel sailed in despair without asking Manwë, and indeed she would have been told to stay in Valinor in that hour, despite her desire being legitimate; and thus the ban against departure fell upon her and she was forbidden to return. She and Celeborn reached Middle-earth before Fëanor, finding Círdan's haven and being received with joy. They did not join the war against Morgoth, which they considered hopeless without the help of the Valar, and instead they recommended going eastwards and befriending the [Dark Elves](/wiki/Dark_Elves) and Men living there. But the Elves of Beleriand did not heed these counsels, so both of them crossed the Ered Lindon before the end of the First Age. They were granted permission to return by the Valar, but they rejected it.

Christopher Tolkien explains that this change probably had its roots in the philosophical implications of Galadriel's power in Middle-earth. He is certain that his father had the intention of changing all the narrative of "The Silmarillion" related to the backstory of Galadriel, despite what was already written: after all, she was not part of the story of the Noldor in the first place and the book had not been published. The only notable problem is in the backstory of Celeborn, which contradicts what was already published in J.R.R. Tolkien's life.<sup id="cite_ref-History_69-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-History-69">[61]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:231–3</sup>

### Unsolved contradictions {#Unsolved_contradictions}

As can be seen, the development of Galadriel throughout Tolkien's texts is mainly structured around the nature of her exile and ban from Aman. In this subsection we present other aspects of her that are better explained separately for order's sake. In the mentioned texts there are some loose contradictions that can be easily explained as mere mistakes. A noticeable case is Galadriel using [Nenya](/wiki/Nenya) during the Second Age in "[Concerning Galadriel and Celeborn](/wiki/Concerning_Galadriel_and_Celeborn)", which contradicts everything we know about the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power) in *The Lord of the Rings*. On the contrary, the contradictions presented in this subsection result from the several revisions and we cannot know how Tolkien would have solved them.

<dl><dt><dl><dd>Galadriel in Nargothrond</dd></dl></dt></dl>

In the *[Grey Annals](/wiki/Grey_Annals)* it is written that Galadriel went from Doriath to Nargothrond "and dwelt there for a while" after Finrod had completed its building.<sup id="cite_ref-Nargothrond_24-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nargothrond-24">[21]</a></sup> But while expanding the *[Quenta Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Later_Quenta_Silmarillion "The Later Quenta Silmarillion")*, Tolkien wrote: "Yet Galadriel his sister went never to Nargothrond, for she remained long in Doriath", and revised it with "Yet Galaðriel his sister dwelt never in Nargothrond".<sup id="cite_ref-76" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-76">[66]</a></sup> It is unclear which version was written as definitive, but Christopher Tolkien chose the former one for [Chapter 15](/wiki/Of_the_Noldor_in_Beleriand "Of the Noldor in Beleriand") of his edition of *The Silmarillion*.

<dl><dt><dl><dd>Passing over the mountains</dd></dl></dt></dl>

Christopher Tolkien explains that in the first conception, Galadriel went to the east from Beleriand alone and found Celeborn (probably of [Nandorin](/wiki/Nandor "Nandor") origin) in Lórien. Hence in the chapter "[The Mirror of Galadriel](/wiki/The_Mirror_of_Galadriel)" she said Celeborn dwelt in the West before the Dawn, and that she crossed the mountains from Beleriand (apparently alone). This contradicts the information in [Appendix B](/wiki/Appendix_B) of *The Lord of the Rings* and in *[The Road Goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")*, in which Celeborn is a Sinda who accompanied Galadriel from Lindon.<sup id="cite_ref-History_69-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-History-69">[61]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:228</sup>

The time when she passed over the mountains is also contradictory, as Galadriel said that it was "*ere the fall of Nargothrond and Gondolin*", yet in Appendix B it is stated that at the beginning of the Second Age she was still dwelling in Lindon, before passing over the mountains. This contradiction in the same work must have been due to a mistake during revision.<sup id="cite_ref-77" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-77">[note 11]</a></sup>

<dl><dt><dl><dd>Marriage of Galadriel and Celeborn</dd></dl></dt></dl>

In Appendix B, while describing the beginning of the Second Age, it is said that Celeborn dwelt in [south Lindon](/wiki/Harlindon "Harlindon") with his wife Galadriel, so it appears that they were married at this point.<sup id="cite_ref-Second_32-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-32">[28]</a></sup> In a manuscript from c. [1959](/wiki/1959), Tolkien said that Galadriel and Celeborn probably married "soon after the overthrow of Morgoth", before they crossed the Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Scales_20-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scales-20">[17]</a></sup> Another manuscript from the same period states the same, but in its following notes Tolkien wrote that Galadriel did not marry Celeborn until they were settled (in Lake Nenuial, as understood by the context).<sup id="cite_ref-78" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-78">[67]</a></sup> In a manuscript from [1965](/wiki/1965), while Tolkien pondered about Amroth being their child, he wrote that Galadriel and Celeborn were betrothed, but did not marry until the confusion of the War of Wrath and the beginning of the Second Age had ended (till S.A. 24).<sup id="cite_ref-Ages_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ages-21">[18]</a></sup>

<dl><dt><dl><dd>Birth-year of Celebrían</dd></dl></dt></dl>

The year and the place of Celebrían's birth was never definite. Tolkien considered as early as year 9 of the Second Age, and as late as 350 (850 was also considered, but as a mere conjecture). For details and references see *[Celebrían#Other versions of the legendarium](/wiki/Celebr%C3%ADan#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Celebrían")*.

<dl><dt><dl><dd>Mother of Amroth</dd></dl></dt></dl>

In "Concerning Galadriel and Celeborn", both Celebrían and [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth) are children of Galadriel and Celeborn,<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_34-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-34">[30]</a></sup> but in later texts dating from [1969](/wiki/1969), Tolkien made Amroth the son of [Amdír](/wiki/Amd%C3%ADr).<sup id="cite_ref-79" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-79">[68]</a></sup> For more details see *[Amroth#Other versions of the legendarium](/wiki/Amroth#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Amroth")*.

<dl><dt><dl><dd>Founder of Eregion</dd></dl></dt></dl>

In Appendix B, it is said that at the beginning of the Second Age, the Noldor went to [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) out of their interest for the [mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril") in [Moria](/wiki/Moria). Besides, Celebrimbor is called "Lord of Eregion", with no mention of his relation with Galadriel.<sup id="cite_ref-Second_32-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-32">[28]</a></sup> In "Concerning Galadriel and Celeborn", Galadriel and Celeborn are described as the founders of Eregion, a place chosen by Galadriel out of her desire to gather the Elven and the Dwarvish races against the increasing evil in Middle-earth. Celebrimbor is the chief builder of the realm, but is not mentioned as its lord, although he later seizes the power there.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_34-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-34">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:235–7</sup> Celebrimbor as the founder appears only in a linguistic work, in which Tolkien simply mentions "the settlement at Eregion under Celebrimbor".<sup id="cite_ref-80" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-80">[69]</a></sup> It is unclear if these texts are necessarily contradictory, but it is clear that trying to explain the rule in Eregion remains problematic.

### The published *Silmarillion* {#The_published_Silmarillion}

Christopher Tolkien was aware of his father's [latest conception](#Unstained_exile), in which Galadriel left Aman free of any guilt. However, as this was a mere projected idea, he had to make use of the completed narratives for his edition of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, in which she is a rebel leader.<sup id="cite_ref-History_69-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-History-69">[61]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:232–3</sup> It is notable that in this work there is no explanation of Galadriel's reasons for remaining in Middle-earth after the War of Wrath, save that Celeborn was unwilling to leave,<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_29-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-29">[26]</a></sup> nor is there any mention of a ban against her return or her concerns about the remaining evil. This way, Christopher did not choose any particular position and let the reader guess, but also stripped this character of all development in relation to *The Lord of the Rings*.

[Douglas C. Kane](/wiki/Douglas_C._Kane) noted that Christopher Tolkien, for his edition, removed some statements on Galadriel from his father's work: [Chapter 5](/wiki/Of_Eldamar_and_the_Princes_of_the_Eldali%C3%AB "Of Eldamar and the Princes of the Eldalië")'s original text said that Galadriel was "the most valiant" lady of the [house of Finwë](/wiki/House_of_Finw%C3%AB "House of Finwë"); and [Chapter 9](/wiki/Of_the_Flight_of_the_Noldor "Of the Flight of the Noldor")'s mentioned her as "Galadriel the valiant and fair". According to Kane, Galadriel is not the only female character inexplicably diminished by Christopher's editorial choices.<sup id="cite_ref-81" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-81">[70]</a></sup>

### Films {#Films}

<b>1978: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Galadriel is voiced by <a href="/wiki/Annette_Crosbie" title="Annette Crosbie">Annette Crosbie</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Galadriel is played by <a href="/wiki/Cate_Blanchett" title="Cate Blanchett">Cate Blanchett</a>. Galadriel narrates the <a href="/wiki/Prologue:_One_Ring_to_Rule_Them_All..." title="Prologue: One Ring to Rule Them All...">Prologue</a>, explaining the creation of the Rings of Power and the War of the Last Alliance. Earlier plans considered were to have either Frodo or Gandalf narrate the Prologue, but this was dropped: Frodo was not alive until thousands of years after these events happened, and although Gandalf was alive, he was not present in Middle-earth at the time; the Wizards came some one thousand years after the Prologue ends. Thus Galadriel narrates the Prologue, because she had first-hand accounts of this history and actively participated in its events.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Galadriel frequently consults <a href="/wiki/%C3%93sanw%C3%AB" title="Ósanwë">telepathically</a> with Elrond; in the books there is no clear indication of how Galadriel knew about the coming of the Fellowship. In the film, unlike the book, Galadriel and <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a> send an army of <a href="/wiki/Elves" title="Elves">Elves</a> - led by <a href="/wiki/Haldir" title="Haldir">Haldir</a> - to <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg" title="Battle of the Hornburg">Helm&#39;s Deep</a> to aid the <a href="/wiki/Rohirrim" title="Rohirrim">Rohirrim</a>. In the DVD commentary, <a href="/wiki/Peter_Jackson" title="Peter Jackson">Peter Jackson</a> and his fellow writers explain that they worried audiences would wonder why the Elves do not seem to be helping in the war, while at the same time, they felt that actually inserting an entire separate battle scene at Lothlórien would take up too much screen time and resources.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>A spiritual apparition of Lady Galadriel appears to provide Frodo strength following his passage through Shelob&#39;s Lair soon after he uses the Phial that she gave him. She is later seen leaving Middle-earth along with other Ring-bearers, but in film version her husband Celeborn departs with her at the same time.</dd></dl>

<b>2012-14: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Cate Blanchett reprised her role as Galadriel, although the character is absent from the novel.<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting_82-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting-82">[71]</a></sup> She appears as part of the <a href="/wiki/White_Council" title="White Council">White Council</a> alongside <a href="/wiki/Saruman" title="Saruman">Saruman</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> and <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a>. Later, right before <a href="/wiki/Thorin_and_Company" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and his Company</a> enter in <a href="/wiki/Mirkwood" title="Mirkwood">Mirkwood</a>, she telepathically urges Gandalf to further seek the true identity of the <a href="/wiki/Sauron#Sauron&#39;s_Return" title="Sauron">Necromancer</a>. Finally, during the <a href="/wiki/Attack_on_Dol_Guldur" title="Attack on Dol Guldur">attack on Dol Guldur</a>, Galadriel plays a larger role. She first rescues Gandalf from captivity, and is ultimately the one to drive Sauron from the fortress using her <a href="/wiki/Phial_of_Galadriel" title="Phial of Galadriel">phial</a>, although weakened by the amount of power she used.</dd></dl>

### Radio series {#Radio_series}

In addition to the Lothlórien episodes, the voice of Galadriel is heard in Shelob&#39;s Lair as a reminder to use the phial, and she appears with Elrond in the final episode in the Shire, as they journey towards the Grey Havens.</dd></dl>

<b>2001-2003: <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/P%C3%A1n_prste%C5%88ov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series)" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The voice of Galadriel is provided by Soňa Norisová. ### Games {#Games}

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Galadriel, voiced by <a href="/wiki/Jennifer_Hale" title="Jennifer Hale">Jennifer Hale</a>, is the narrator of the Prologue and the Epilogue, and appears in Lothlórien.</dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Galadriel is the strongest of &#34;Hero&#34; units available to the Free Peoples. If a player&#39;s army manages to spot <a href="/wiki/Gollum" title="Gollum">Gollum</a> on the battlefield, take The One Ring from him and deliver it to their forces, the option to &#34;summon&#34; Galadriel unlocks for a large amount of resources. Thus, Lady Galadriel is represented as succumbing to corruption of The Ring, similar to the respective scene in the <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i> movie.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Introduced in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Mines_of_Moria" title="The Lord of the Rings Online: Mines of Moria">The Lord of the Rings Online: Mines of Moria</a></i>, Galadriel plays a prominent role during the Epic storyline with guidance and support. During the final days of the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, Galadriel assembles the Council of Elven Lords, where it is decided to bring their armies against <a href="/wiki/Dol_Guldur" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a>. After she brings down the walls of Dol Guldur, Galadriel accompanies Arwen on her journey south to her wedding, and they visit the site of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Celebrian" title="Celebrian">Celebrian</a>&#39;s captivity in the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a> together. Afterwards, she and Celeborn join the procession of <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a>&#39;s house and travel to <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> to attend the wedding of Arwen and Aragorn.</dd></dl>

**2014: [Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor):**

<dl><dd>Galadriel is featured in the game, although she is a non-playable character. She is voiced by <a href="/wiki/Jennifer_Hale" title="Jennifer Hale">Jennifer Hale</a> and she narrates the prologue in a showing of the game.</dd></dl>

**2017: [Middle-earth: Shadow of War](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_War):**

<dl><dd>Galadriel is featured in the game, although she is a non-playable character, with <a href="/wiki/Jennifer_Hale" title="Jennifer Hale">Jennifer Hale</a> reprising her role from the game.</dd></dl>
