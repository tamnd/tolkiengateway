**Gerontius Took** ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1190](/wiki/Third_Age_2790 "Third Age 2790") - [1320](/wiki/Third_Age_2920 "Third Age 2920"), died aged 130), also known as **The Old Took**, was a renowned [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") and twenty-sixth [Thain](/wiki/Thain) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Gerontius was the only son of [Fortinbras I](/wiki/Fortinbras_Took_I "Fortinbras Took I"). He married [Adamanta Chubb](/wiki/Adamanta_Chubb), who gave birth to twelve children: [Isengrim III](/wiki/Isengrim_Took_III "Isengrim Took III"), [Hildigard](/wiki/Hildigard_Took "Hildigard Took"), [Isumbras IV](/wiki/Isumbras_Took_IV "Isumbras Took IV"), [Hildigrim](/wiki/Hildigrim_Took "Hildigrim Took"), [Isembold](/wiki/Isembold_Took "Isembold Took"), [Hildifons](/wiki/Hildifons_Took "Hildifons Took"), [Isembard](/wiki/Isembard_Took "Isembard Took"), [Hildibrand](/wiki/Hildibrand_Took "Hildibrand Took"), [Belladonna](/wiki/Belladonna_Took "Belladonna Took"), [Donnamira](/wiki/Donnamira_Took "Donnamira Took"), [Mirabella](/wiki/Mirabella_Took "Mirabella Took") and [Isengar](/wiki/Isengar_Took "Isengar Took").

After the death of his father in [1248](/wiki/Third_Age_2848 "Third Age 2848"), Gerontius became the twenty-sixth [Thain](/wiki/Thain) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He was a friend of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), who gave him a pair of magic diamond studs<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and performed firework tricks during Gerontius' [midsummer](/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day "Mid-year's Day")-eve parties.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

Gerontius Took reached the impressive age of 130, which made him the oldest [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") until his grandson [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) celebrated his 131st Birthday.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> He also held the record of most offspring, until [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) bested him with [Tom](/wiki/Tolman_Gardner "Tolman Gardner")'s birth in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1442](/wiki/Fourth_Age_21 "Fourth Age 21").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

He was called *The Old Took* not because of his age, but because of his numerous younger relatives.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-7">[6]</a></sup>

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerontius" title="wikipedia:Gerontius">Gerontius</a></i> is a Latin name, which means "Old-man". It is derived from Greek *Geróntios*. The element *géron* is Greek for "old". *Gerontius* was a popular name in the Late Roman Empire and is rendered as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geraint" title="wikipedia:Geraint">Geraint</a></i> in [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup> *Geraint* is found as a royal name in <i>The <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mabinogion" title="wikipedia:Mabinogion">Mabinogion</a></i> and in Tennyson's <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geraint_and_Enid#Geraint_and_Enid" title="wikipedia:Geraint and Enid">Geraint and Enid</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

*Gerontius* is one of the very few Greco-Latin names appearing in Hobbit lore.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In [a letter to Mr. and Mrs. Kloos](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_and_Mrs_Kloos "Letter to Mr and Mrs Kloos"), Tolkien stated that when he wrote *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, he had imagined Bilbo's grandfather to be old, because both of his grandparents were around a hundred years old when they died.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-7">[6]</a></sup>
