The **Great Hall** was the meeting place of the village [Wootton Major](/wiki/Wootton_Major) wherein the villagers held meetings, debates, public feasts, and family gatherings. Adjoined to the Great Hall were the Cook's House and the Kitchen, for the [Master Cook](/wiki/Master_Cook) was an important person in the village who provided suitable fare for all of the gatherings. It was also the site for the winter festival and [The Feast of Good Children](/wiki/The_Feast_of_Good_Children).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
