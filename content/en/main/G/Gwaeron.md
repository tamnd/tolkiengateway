Following the tradition of their forerunners the [Edain](../E/Edain.md), the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) kept a calendar that took its month-names from the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) language. In that calendar, **Gwaeron** was the third month of the year, with a name equivalent to the more widely used [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) [Súlimë](../S/Súlimë.md). Both these [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) names are a reference to the winds of this season of the year.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
