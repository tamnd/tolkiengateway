Following the tradition of their forerunners the [Edain](/wiki/Edain), the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) kept a calendar that took its month-names from the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) language. In that calendar, **Gwaeron** was the third month of the year, with a name equivalent to the more widely used [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) [Súlimë](/wiki/S%C3%BAlim%C3%AB). Both these [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) names are a reference to the winds of this season of the year.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
