**Gilim** was a [giant](/wiki/Giants "Giants") of [Eruman](/wiki/Araman "Araman"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> apparently noteworthy for his neck. [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) named him in her growth-spell.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), one of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s early conceptions of an [Elven](/wiki/Elvish "Elvish") tongue, *Gilim* is given the translaton "winter".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
