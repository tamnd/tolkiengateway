**Gilim** was a [giant](Giants.md) of [Eruman](../A/Araman.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> apparently noteworthy for his neck. [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) named him in her growth-spell.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Gnomish](Gnomish.md), one of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s early conceptions of an [Elven](../E/Elvish.md) tongue, *Gilim* is given the translaton "winter".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
