**Green Emberü** was the first planet that [Michael George Ramer](/wiki/Michael_George_Ramer) had visited with his mind.

## History {#History}

When Michael George Ramer landed on the most likely green planet, he described to the other members of the [Notion Club](/wiki/Notion_Club) that the world had organic life that was wholesome and longeval and rich.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier drafts, Green Emberü was originally named as **Gyönyörü**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
