**Grafton** (also, **Grafton Books**), a British paperback imprint established circa [1981](/wiki/1981) under <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granada_PLC" title="wikipedia:Granada PLC">Granada Publishing Ltd</a>, published several books by or relating to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). In [1983](/wiki/1983) Granada Publishing Ltd was sold to the publishers William Collins &amp; Co, and in [1989](/wiki/1989) Grafton became a part of [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
