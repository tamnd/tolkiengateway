**Grindwall** was a small hythe (landing) on the north bank of the [Withywindle](../W/Withywindle.md). It was well watched and protected by a *grind* or fence that extended into the water, because it was outside of the [High Hay](../H/High_Hay.md). On rising ground behind the hythe was the village of [Breredon](../B/Breredon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In one hobbit-poem [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md) left his cockle-boat there on his way to the [Marish](../M/Marish.md). After visiting [Farmer Maggot](../F/Farmer_Maggot.md), he vanished and the boat stayed there for three days. [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md) say that otters came to take it back along the Withywindle and with other animals pulled it up to Tom's creek; however they forgot the oars behind, and "Long they lay at Grindwall hythe for Tom to come and find them".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
