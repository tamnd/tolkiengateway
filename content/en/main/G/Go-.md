## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>go-</b></i> (or <i><b>gwa-</b></i>) is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) prefix meaning "together, co-, com-".<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) WO<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- *[gwanûr, gwanūn](Gwanûr.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Noldorin {#Noldorin}

<i><b>go-</b></i> is a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) prefix meaning "together".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
