**Glóin** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [2136](/wiki/Third_Age_2136 "Third Age 2136") – [2385](/wiki/Third_Age_2385 "Third Age 2385"), died age 249) was the King of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) for 96 years, succeeding his father [Thorin I](/wiki/Thorin_I) upon his death in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2289](/wiki/Third_Age_2289 "Third Age 2289"), being the heir of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2210](/wiki/Third_Age_2210 "Third Age 2210") Glóin's father, King [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin_I "Thorin I"), removed the last dwarves from [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") to the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) where the remnants of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) now were. Glóin reigned over the [dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") from there, but otherwise very little is known of his life and reign.

Glóin died in [2385](/wiki/Third_Age_2385 "Third Age 2385"), at the age of 249, and was succeeded by his son, [Óin](</wiki/%C3%93in_(King_of_Durin%27s_Folk)> "Óin (King of Durin's Folk)").<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>
