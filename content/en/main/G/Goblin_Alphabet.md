The **Goblin Alphabet** was invented and used by the [North Polar Bear](/wiki/North_Polar_Bear), based on the marks made by [goblins](/wiki/Orcs#Other_writings "Orcs") of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=North_Pole&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="North Pole (page does not exist)">North Pole</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

[Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) stated that Goblins couldn't draw well, noting that they preferred "nasty queer shapes".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) and [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) have noted that the Goblin alphabet is a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pictogram" title="wikipedia:Pictogram">pictographic</a> writing system,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> conveying its meaning through its pictorial similarity to objects. Arden R. Smith has commented that the Goblin alphabet is:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>quite different from Tolkien’s other invented alphabets. Supposedly based on goblin cave-drawings, it consists mainly of humanoid stick figures. It is essentially alphabetic, but contains a number of characters representing diphthongs and common consonant combinations.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/Arden_R._Smith" title="Arden R. Smith">Arden R. Smith</a><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>
