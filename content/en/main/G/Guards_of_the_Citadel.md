The **Guards of the Citadel** or **Tower of Gondor** were those honoured guards of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) who were charged with protecting the [Citadel](/wiki/Citadel_of_Gondor "Citadel of Gondor"), the highest and most important tier of the city, and participated in official functions.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> When on duty they were not permitted to leave their post for any cause, save at the command of their lord.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup>

They also guarded the [White Tree](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor "White Tree of Gondor") that stood in the [Court of the Fountain](</wiki/Court_of_the_Fountain_(Minas_Tirith)> "Court of the Fountain (Minas Tirith)"). At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) it had been dead for nearly 150 years,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> until the new King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) found a new seedling of the Tree,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and the Guards' great duty was refounded.

## Appearance and Attire {#Appearance_and_Attire}

At the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), they were the only soldiers of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) entitled to wear the [livery of the heirs of Elendil](/wiki/Livery_of_the_heirs_of_Elendil "Livery of the heirs of Elendil") [himself](/wiki/Elendil "Elendil") - a white tree in blossom, surmounted by a silver crown and stars, all embroidered on a field of black. They also bore heirlooms of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)'s ancient wealth, tall helmets wrought out of *[mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril")*, with close-fitting cheek-guards and bearing the carved wings of sea-birds.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> On [10 March](/wiki/10_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) was arrayed as a Guard of the Citadel, at the command of the [Steward](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor") [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor). His appearance in this uniform gave strength to the rumour that he was indeed *[Ernil i Pheriannath](/wiki/Ernil_i_Pheriannath)*, the Prince of the Halflings.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Organisation {#Organisation}

There were at least three companies, each of which had its own storehouse; [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond), son of [Baranor](</wiki/Baranor_(man_of_Gondor)> "Baranor (man of Gondor)") belonged to the [Third Company of the Citadel](/wiki/Third_Company_of_the_Citadel).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> The <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Captain-General&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Captain-General (page does not exist)">Captain-General</a> of the Guards was probably the heir of the throne; during the time of the [Ruling Stewards](/wiki/Ruling_Steward "Ruling Steward") it was the heir of the Steward.
