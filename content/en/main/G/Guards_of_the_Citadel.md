The **Guards of the Citadel** or **Tower of Gondor** were those honoured guards of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) who were charged with protecting the [Citadel](../C/Citadel_of_Gondor.md), the highest and most important tier of the city, and participated in official functions.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> When on duty they were not permitted to leave their post for any cause, save at the command of their lord.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup>

They also guarded the [White Tree](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md) that stood in the [Court of the Fountain](<../C/Court_of_the_Fountain_(Minas_Tirith).md>). At the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) it had been dead for nearly 150 years,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> until the new King [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) and [Gandalf](Gandalf.md) found a new seedling of the Tree,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and the Guards' great duty was refounded.

## Appearance and Attire {#Appearance_and_Attire}

At the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), they were the only soldiers of [Gondor](Gondor.md) entitled to wear the [livery of the heirs of Elendil](../L/Livery_of_the_heirs_of_Elendil.md) [himself](../E/Elendil.md) - a white tree in blossom, surmounted by a silver crown and stars, all embroidered on a field of black. They also bore heirlooms of [Gondor](Gondor.md)'s ancient wealth, tall helmets wrought out of *[mithril](../M/Mithril.md)*, with close-fitting cheek-guards and bearing the carved wings of sea-birds.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> On [10 March](../_/10_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) was arrayed as a Guard of the Citadel, at the command of the [Steward](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md) [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md). His appearance in this uniform gave strength to the rumour that he was indeed *[Ernil i Pheriannath](../E/Ernil_i_Pheriannath.md)*, the Prince of the Halflings.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Organisation {#Organisation}

There were at least three companies, each of which had its own storehouse; [Beregond](../B/Beregond.md), son of [Baranor](<../B/Baranor_(man_of_Gondor).md>) belonged to the [Third Company of the Citadel](../T/Third_Company_of_the_Citadel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> The <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Captain-General&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Captain-General (page does not exist)">Captain-General</a> of the Guards was probably the heir of the throne; during the time of the [Ruling Stewards](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) it was the heir of the Steward.
