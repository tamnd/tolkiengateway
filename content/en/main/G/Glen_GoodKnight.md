**Glen H. GoodKnight** ([1 October](/wiki/1_October) [1941](/wiki/1941) - [3 November](/wiki/3_November) [2010](/wiki/2010)) was the founder of the [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) and the organizer of the first [Mythcons](/wiki/Mythcon "Mythcon").

GoodKnight has made numerous contributions to the Society's publications, as well as several other works.
