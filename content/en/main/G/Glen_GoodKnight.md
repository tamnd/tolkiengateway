**Glen H. GoodKnight** ([1 October](../_/1_October.md) [1941](../_/1941.md) - [3 November](../_/3_November.md) [2010](../_/2010.md)) was the founder of the [Mythopoeic Society](../M/Mythopoeic_Society.md) and the organizer of the first [Mythcons](../M/Mythcon.md).

GoodKnight has made numerous contributions to the Society's publications, as well as several other works.
