**Glow-worms** were creatures associated with lights that glimmered in a dark environment.<sup id="cite_ref-stairs_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stairs-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [10 March](/wiki/10_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> as [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) climbed the [Stairs of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol) to avoid continuing on to [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul), the [wraith-road](/wiki/Morgul-road "Morgul-road") was likened to a "glow-worm thread" in the dark at one point when Frodo glanced down into the [Morgul Vale](/wiki/Morgul_Vale) from high above on the Stairs of Cirith Ungol.<sup id="cite_ref-stairs_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stairs-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) is said to have thought of **glowworms** when seeing the bright light of [Noldo](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") crystal lanterns.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Glowworms also dwelt about the dark borders of the deep pools of [Murmuran](</wiki/L%C3%B3rien_(Valinor)> "Lórien (Valinor)").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Beth_Fajcz_-_Olore_Malle.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/4/4c/Beth_Fajcz_-_Olore_Malle.jpg/250px-Beth_Fajcz_-_Olore_Malle.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="392" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/4/4c/Beth_Fajcz_-_Olore_Malle.jpg/375px-Beth_Fajcz_-_Olore_Malle.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/4/4c/Beth_Fajcz_-_Olore_Malle.jpg/500px-Beth_Fajcz_-_Olore_Malle.jpg 2x" data-file-width="3234" data-file-height="5065"/></a><figcaption><i>Olorë Mallë: Path of Dreams</i> by <a href="/wiki/Beth_Fajcz" title="Beth Fajcz">Beth Fajcz</a></figcaption></figure>

**Great glow-worms** often lurked around the borders of [Olórë Mallë](/wiki/Ol%C3%B3r%C3%AB_Mall%C3%AB), the Path of Dreams.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> As these creatures are mentioned only fleetingly, it is impossible to say if they were related to the great [Worms](/wiki/Worms) (wingless [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons)), or if they were giant versions of regular glowworms. One can assume that they glowed in the dark (and therefore perhaps were related to, or were another name for, the [spark-dragons](/wiki/Spark-dragons "Spark-dragons")).

## Other writings {#Other_writings}

Some glow-worms were seen glowing around the turn of "the crooked fairy lane" in the poem *[Goblin Feet](/wiki/Goblin_Feet)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) mentioned on their website that glow-worms are "a form of beetle" in relation to their appearance in *Goblin Feet*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
