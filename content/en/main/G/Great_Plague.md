The **Great Plague**, also known as the **Dark Plague**, was a deadly plague that spread out of the [east](../R/Rhûn.md) into [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md), [Gondor](Gondor.md), and [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) during the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md). The mortality rate of the epidemic possibly numbered in the hundreds of thousands as it killed untold numbers of people and animals.<sup id="cite_ref-south_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/S/Sage_-_Great_Plague.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/75/Sage_-_Great_Plague.png/300px-Sage_-_Great_Plague.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="211" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/75/Sage_-_Great_Plague.png/450px-Sage_-_Great_Plague.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/75/Sage_-_Great_Plague.png/600px-Sage_-_Great_Plague.png 2x" data-file-width="769" data-file-height="540"/></a><figcaption>The effects of the plague; more intense color shows worse effect (based on interpretation in the <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../A/Atlas_of_Middle-earth.md" title="Atlas of Middle-earth">Atlas of Middle-earth</a></i>).</figcaption></figure>

The Great Plague began beyond [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) in [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md). It was believed by historians to have allegedly been caused by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) as the plague coincided with a [Shadow](../S/Shadow.md) deepening in [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md), foretelling his return in the unguarded [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) and the reappearance of evil things.<sup id="cite_ref-south_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the cold winter of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1635](../T/Third_Age_1635.md), the plague was brought by dark winds to [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md). The [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md) suffered great casualties since they were less skilled in the art of healing than [Gondor](Gondor.md) was. After the plague had passed, more than half of the folk of Rhovanion had been killed along with their horses.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The plague also hit the enemies of Gondor, who otherwise could easily have overwhelmed the weakened kingdom. Though this didn't affect [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), as he could wait.<sup id="cite_ref-south_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-1">[1]</a></sup>

The plague first reached [Gondor](Gondor.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md), just a couple of years after King [Minardil](../M/Minardil.md) had been killed at [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) by the [Corsairs of Umbar](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md). It was devastating in Gondor; [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) and [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) were especially hit hard, with Calenardhon being nearly completely abandoned<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and Osgiliath suffering the highest casualties outside of [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md). Many fled the city and removed to [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) and [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md), and [Minas Anor](../M/Minas_Anor.md) became the [King's seat](../K/King's_House.md). The casualties were so high that "[Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md) was emptied of its people"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and the fortresses guarding [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) were abandoned as the troops were recalled.<sup id="cite_ref-south_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-1">[1]</a></sup> The new king, [Telemnar](../T/Telemnar.md), was killed along with his children, many others of the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md), and the [White Tree of Gondor](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ta_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-5">[5]</a></sup> Coming two centuries after the [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md), this further destroyed the people of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-south_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-1">[1]</a></sup>

From there the plague spread west and then north, desolating many of the lands in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ta_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-5">[5]</a></sup> [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md) suffered less than most, due to having little contact with other regions.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> However, the plague gained strength as it went north, and [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md), [Minhiriath](../M/Minhiriath.md) in particular, was hit especially hard. The joint garrison of the North and South Kingdoms at [Tharbad](../T/Tharbad.md) ceased to exist.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The last of the Dúnedain of Cardolan died on the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md), and evil spirits from [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) and [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md) entered the realm.<sup id="cite_ref-north_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-north-8">[8]</a></sup>

While [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) suffered greatly,<sup id="cite_ref-ta_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-5">[5]</a></sup> the plague lessened as it passed northward until the northern part of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md) was scarcely affected,<sup id="cite_ref-north_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-north-8">[8]</a></sup> still being capable of defending [Fornost Erain](../F/Fornost_Erain.md) from Angmar.

The plague marked the beginning of the desolation of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md),<sup id="cite_ref-ta_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-5">[5]</a></sup> where the population of [Men](../M/Men.md) continued to decline for the rest of the [Age](../A/Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> The exact date that the plague ended is not known, but for the next two centuries, Gondor, as well as its allies and enemies, did little but try to regain strength.<sup id="cite_ref-south_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early draft, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) states that the plague came from the [south](../H/Harad.md) in addition to the east. In a rejected sentence in this version, the plague never passed the [Brandywine River](../B/Brandywine_River.md) into the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Great Plague is revealed to be an evil of <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>, created by his chief poisoner Lhaereth the Stained. Various versions of it were tested in <a href="../R/Rhûn.md" title="Rhûn">Rhûn</a> before a suitable variant was unleashed onto the lands in the west. However, despite the devastation it brought, the plague was deemed a failure by Sauron for not being deadly enough, and Lhaereth fell out of his favor for many years hence. It is revealed that the <a href="../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md" title="Rangers of the North">Rangers of the North</a> could heal those affected by the plague with <a href="../A/Athelas.md" title="Athelas">Kingsfoil</a>. After the downfall of Sauron, the player works with two Rangers to stop Lhaereth from unleashing a new and improved variant of the Great Plague.</dd></dl>
