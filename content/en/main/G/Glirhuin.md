**Glirhuin** was a [seer](../S/Seers.md) and [harp-player](../M/Minstrels.md) of the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md) who lived in the Forest of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md) during the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

Glirhuin composed a song that said that the [Stone of the Hapless](../S/Stone_of_the_Hapless.md) would never be defiled by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) nor sink below the sea. Indeed after [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) fell beneath the waves, still [Tol Morwen](../T/Tol_Morwen.md) stood alone above the waters.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is not glossed, but as Paul Strack notices, it contains the element *glir-* ("sing").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
