**Glirhuin** was a [seer](/wiki/Seers "Seers") and [harp-player](/wiki/Minstrels "Minstrels") of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) who lived in the Forest of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

Glirhuin composed a song that said that the [Stone of the Hapless](/wiki/Stone_of_the_Hapless) would never be defiled by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) nor sink below the sea. Indeed after [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) fell beneath the waves, still [Tol Morwen](/wiki/Tol_Morwen) stood alone above the waters.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is not glossed, but as Paul Strack notices, it contains the element *glir-* ("sing").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
