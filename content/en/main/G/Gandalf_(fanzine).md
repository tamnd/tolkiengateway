<i><b>Gandalf: Tidsskrift for fantasi</b></i> was a Norwegian fanzine (calling itself a "tidsskrift", ("journal")), edited by Øyvind Myhre and published during the 1970s. Although the title was taken from [a character](/wiki/Gandalf "Gandalf") by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), the fanzine covered fantasy literature in general and also included short novels. A few issues had articles about, or relating to, Tolkien (see below).

The fanzine format was of stapled, type-printed A4 paper, and each issue of the magazine featured a hand-drawn illustration on the cover.

## Selected issues {#Selected_issues}

- [1973](/wiki/1973): Nr. 2
    - "Om å skrive Tolkien-kritikk" (Øyvind Myhre) \["On Writing Tolkien Criticism"; the article concerns the political content of Tolkien's writings\]
- [1974](/wiki/1974): Nr. 3 \[see pp. 7, 9\]
- [1975](/wiki/1975): Nr. 1
    - "Moral og umoral" (Giale Hannemyr) \[pp. 5–6; "Morality and Immorality"; the article is a reply to Myhre's article in nr. 2, 1973\]
- [1975](/wiki/1975): Nr. 2
    - "Om Werenskiolds Tolkien-oversettelse" (Roger Humphris) \[pp. 13–14; "On Werenskiold's Translation of Tolkien", see [Nils Werenskiold](/wiki/Nils_Werenskiold)\]
- [1975](/wiki/1975): Nr. 6 \[see p. 6\]
- [1976](/wiki/1976): Nr. 1 \[see p. 12\]
