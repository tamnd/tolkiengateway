**Glaurung** was the first of the [dragons](../D/Dragons.md)<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-1">[1]</a></sup> and one of the foremost lieutenants of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) during the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). He walked on four legs and breathed fire, but he did not have wings. He was sentient, able to understand the speech of others and to speak himself.

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/D/David_Greset_-_The_forces_of_Fingon_against_Glaurung.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/17/David_Greset_-_The_forces_of_Fingon_against_Glaurung.jpg/300px-David_Greset_-_The_forces_of_Fingon_against_Glaurung.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="186" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/17/David_Greset_-_The_forces_of_Fingon_against_Glaurung.jpg/450px-David_Greset_-_The_forces_of_Fingon_against_Glaurung.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/17/David_Greset_-_The_forces_of_Fingon_against_Glaurung.jpg/600px-David_Greset_-_The_forces_of_Fingon_against_Glaurung.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1200" data-file-height="742"/></a><figcaption><i>The forces of Fingon against Glaurung</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_David_Greset.md" title="Category:Images by David Greset">David Greset</a></figcaption></figure>

Glaurung's origin is not known, but he first emerged from [Angband](../A/Angband.md) in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [260](../F/First_Age_260.md)<sup id="cite_ref-GA260_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA260-2">[2]</a></sup> during the [Siege of Angband](../S/Siege_of_Angband.md). Although he was still young according to the long life of dragons and not grown to his full strength, his initial assault pushed the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) from [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md). Glaurung took Ard-galen for his own, defiling the great grassy plain that lay between [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) and the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md).

This victory was short-lived, however, and Glaurung was forced back to Angband when [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) rode against him with a company of mounted archers. Morgoth was not pleased that Glaurung had revealed himself while still immature, and did not allow any more forays from Angband for some two hundred years.<sup id="cite_ref-SReturn_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SReturn-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Two major battles {#Two_major_battles}

The siege of Angband was broken in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [455](../F/First_Age_455.md)<sup id="cite_ref-GADBragollach_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GADBragollach-4">[4]</a></sup> in the *[Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md)*, the Battle of Sudden Flame. Glaurung, now grown to his full might, led the largest army of [balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md) and [orcs](../O/Orcs.md) yet assembled against the leaguer of the Noldor and overthrew it. He then pressed against the [people of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) and, defeating a contingent of their riders upon the plain of [Lothlann](../L/Lothlann.md) and forcing [Maglor's Gap](../M/Maglor's_Gap.md), burned all the land between the arms of [Gelion](Gelion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SRuin_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SRuin-5">[5]</a></sup>

The counter-attack came in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [472](../F/First_Age_472.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> when the [Union of Maedhros](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md) marched in force against Angband in the decisive battle of the *[Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md)*, the Unnumbered Tears. Glaurung and his brood entered the battle in the early hours of the third morning, when Morgoth loosed his last strength against the combined forces of [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md), [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) and [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md):

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The strength and terror of the Great Worm were now great indeed, and Elves and Men withered before him; and he came between the hosts of Maedhros and Fingon and swept them apart.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion.md" title="Quenta Silmarillion">Quenta Silmarillion</a></i>, &#34;<a href="../O/Of_the_Fifth_Battle%253A_Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md" title="Of the Fifth Battle: Nirnaeth Arnoediad">Of the Fifth Battle: Nirnaeth Arnoediad</a>&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote>

But the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of [Belegost](../B/Belegost.md) with their great war masks withstood him. Surrounded and hewed at by their axes, Glaurung was driven into a rage and slew [Azaghâl](../A/Azaghâl.md) the [Lord of Belegost](../L/Lord_of_Belegost.md). But with his last stroke the Dwarf-lord dealt Glaurung a grievous wound to the belly and the dragon and his brood fled back to Angband, leaving the final victory over the Union to [Gothmog](<Gothmog_(balrog).md>) and his balrogs.<sup id="cite_ref-SFifth_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SFifth-7">[7]</a></sup>

### Destruction of Nargothrond {#Destruction_of_Nargothrond}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Turner_Mohan_-_Turin_confronts_Glaurung.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/4/42/Turner_Mohan_-_Turin_confronts_Glaurung.jpg/300px-Turner_Mohan_-_Turin_confronts_Glaurung.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="190" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/4/42/Turner_Mohan_-_Turin_confronts_Glaurung.jpg/450px-Turner_Mohan_-_Turin_confronts_Glaurung.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/4/42/Turner_Mohan_-_Turin_confronts_Glaurung.jpg/600px-Turner_Mohan_-_Turin_confronts_Glaurung.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1194" data-file-height="756"/></a><figcaption><i>Turin confronts Glaurung</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Turner_Mohan.md" title="Category:Images by Turner Mohan">Turner Mohan</a></figcaption></figure>

In autumn of [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md),<sup id="cite_ref-GA495a_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA495a-8">[8]</a></sup> Glaurung led an army of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) against [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md). He passed over [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md) and first assailed the north [Vales of Sirion](../V/Vales_of_Sirion.md), then coming south laid waste to [Eithel Ivrin](../E/Eithel_Ivrin.md) and [Talath Dirnen](../T/Talath_Dirnen.md). His advance was checked by the [Mormegil](../M/Mormegil.md), [Túrin Turambar](../T/Túrin.md), who led the forces of Nargothrond out to meet Glaurung in the [Battle of Tumhalad](../B/Battle_of_Tumhalad.md). The Elves were utterly defeated; [King](../K/King_of_Nargothrond.md) [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) was slain, and although Túrin attempted to rally the survivors to a last defence of Nargothrond itself, Glaurung came there first. The dragon passed swiftly over the bridge over [Narog](../N/Narog.md) and destroyed the [Doors of Felagund](../D/Doors_of_Felagund.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CHFall_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CHFall-9">[9]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/E/Elena_Kukanova_-_Nienor_and_Glaurung.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/87/Elena_Kukanova_-_Nienor_and_Glaurung.jpg/200px-Elena_Kukanova_-_Nienor_and_Glaurung.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="289" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/87/Elena_Kukanova_-_Nienor_and_Glaurung.jpg/300px-Elena_Kukanova_-_Nienor_and_Glaurung.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/87/Elena_Kukanova_-_Nienor_and_Glaurung.jpg/400px-Elena_Kukanova_-_Nienor_and_Glaurung.jpg 2x" data-file-width="450" data-file-height="650"/></a><figcaption><i>Nienor and Glaurung</i> by <a href="../E/Elena_Kukanova.md" title="Elena Kukanova">Elena Kukanova</a></figcaption></figure>

As Glaurung's orcs were sacking the city, Túrin arrived and cut his way to the captives. As he did so Glaurung emerged from the doors and put him under his spell. Túrin was transfixed by the gaze of dragon as the captives, among them [Finduilas](../F/Finduilas.md), were led away. Then Glaurung spoke to Túrin and convinced him not to go after Finduilas but to return to [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) and rescue his mother [Morwen](../M/Morwen.md) and sister [Nienor](../N/Nienor.md) (when in fact they had already fled to [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md)). After weaving the first strand of his plot to bring doom to the children of [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md), who Morgoth had cursed, he drove away his orcs and made his abode in Nargothrond, casting down the bridge and, in true dragon fashion, lying down to sleep on the great hoard he had plundered.<sup id="cite_ref-CHFall_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CHFall-9">[9]</a></sup>

The following year, [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [496](../F/First_Age_496.md),<sup id="cite_ref-GA496a_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA496a-10">[10]</a></sup> Glaurung's slumber was disturbed. Hearing of the fall of Nargothrond a small party from Doriath, including Morwen and Nienor, was sent to seek tidings, especially of the fate of Túrin. His keen sight quickly detected them and, as they approached his dwelling, he issued forth into the Narog and created a steam and reek that scattered the spies in confusion. Glaurung then crept swiftly towards [Amon Ethir](../A/Amon_Ethir.md) where he met Nienor, alone, and placed her under the same spell he had her brother Túrin. But this time rather than subtle deceits he used his power to completely obliterate Niënor's memory and left her to wander in the wild.<sup id="cite_ref-CHJourney_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CHJourney-11">[11]</a></sup>

### Death {#Death}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/G/Guy_Gondron_-_The_Slaying_of_Glaurung.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/b6/Guy_Gondron_-_The_Slaying_of_Glaurung.jpg/300px-Guy_Gondron_-_The_Slaying_of_Glaurung.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="243" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/b6/Guy_Gondron_-_The_Slaying_of_Glaurung.jpg/450px-Guy_Gondron_-_The_Slaying_of_Glaurung.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/b6/Guy_Gondron_-_The_Slaying_of_Glaurung.jpg/600px-Guy_Gondron_-_The_Slaying_of_Glaurung.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1100" data-file-height="890"/></a><figcaption><i>The Slaying of Glaurung</i> by <a href="Guy_Gondron.md" title="Guy Gondron">Guy Gondron</a></figcaption></figure>

For the next several years Glaurung ruled as the 'dragon-king' of what was once the Realm of Nargothrond, gathering many Orcs to him.<sup id="cite_ref-CHComingGlaurung_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CHComingGlaurung-12">[12]</a></sup> Meanwhile, Nienor escaped from [Mablung's](../M/Mablung.md) care and wandered into [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md). There she was found by Túrin, now living amongst the [Halethrim](../H/Halethrim.md), who named her Níniel. Not knowing that they were brother and sister (Túrin having never met his sister and Níniel ignorant of her true identity), the two fell in love and were married.<sup id="cite_ref-CHJourney_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CHJourney-11">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CHNienor_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CHNienor-13">[13]</a></sup> In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [498](../F/First_Age_498.md) Glaurung began to send his orcs against Brethil, and thereby forced Túrin to reveal himself.<sup id="cite_ref-GA498_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA498-14">[14]</a></sup> The next summer<sup id="cite_ref-GA499_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA499-15">[15]</a></sup> he came there himself, but was caught by an ambush before he could reach the Halethrim's dwellings at [Ephel Brandir](../E/Ephel_Brandir.md): Túrin lay in wait in the gorge of [Cabed-en-Aras](../C/Cabed-en-Aras.md), and as Glaurung attempted to leap over it he was dealt a mortal blow by [Gurthang](Gurthang.md), Túrin's black sword.<sup id="cite_ref-CHDeathGlaurung_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CHDeathGlaurung-16">[16]</a></sup>

However even as he lay dying on the banks of [Taeglin](../T/Taeglin.md), the Deceiver completed the doom he had woven for the children of Húrin. Túrin was rendered unconscious by Glaurung's noxious blood and Níniel, coming upon the scene, thought him dead. With his last breath the dragon revealed his plot, and as he died his spells were undone, so Nienor Níniel regained her memory. In despair she leapt to her death into Cabed-en-Aras, and when Túrin awoke and also learned the truth from Brandir, he cast himself upon his own sword.<sup id="cite_ref-CHDeathGlaurung_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CHDeathGlaurung-16">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CHDeathTurin_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CHDeathTurin-17">[17]</a></sup>

## Brood {#Brood}

Glaurung's brood accompanied him in the Nirnaeth Arnoediad<sup id="cite_ref-SFifth_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SFifth-7">[7]</a></sup> and after his death were present during the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), by which time they had become "many and terrible".<sup id="cite_ref-STuor_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-STuor-18">[18]</a></sup>

Exactly what this brood consisted of and, by extension, what Tolkien intended Glaurung's relationship to the rest of the dragons is unclear. The titles "Father of the Dragons" and "first of the *urulóki*" are also ambiguous in terms of whether Glaurung is a progenitor or simply the first to be created. His 'brood' could be all subsequent dragons, just one 'subspecies', or simply his immediate children. Certainly there is a case to be made that [Ancalagon](../A/Ancalagon.md) and the winged-dragons were a separate creation of Morgoth's, based on this passage:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>[Morgoth] loosed upon his foes the last desperate assault that he had prepared, and out of the pits of Angband there issued the winged dragons, that had not before been seen
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion.md" title="Quenta Silmarillion">Quenta Silmarillion</a></i>, &#34;<a href="../O/Of_the_Voyage_of_Eärendil_and_the_War_of_Wrath.md" title="Of the Voyage of Eärendil and the War of Wrath">Of the Voyage of Eärendil and the War of Wrath</a>&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote>

On the other hand, the only two named winged-dragons (Ancalagon and [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md)) were also *urulóki*: they breathed fire. There are no other dragons explicitly stated to be like Glaurung, both fire-breathing but not flying, making it difficult to argue from Tolkien's published works that he intended Glaurung's brood to constitute a discrete lineage. In the end all that can be gleaned of how Tolkien conceived of the 'taxonomy' of dragons comes from isolated references to different types like *urulóki*, *[cold-drake](../C/Cold-drake.md)* and *[long-worms](../L/Long-worms.md)*, so the problem likely does not have a ([canonical](../C/Canon.md)) answer.

The name <i><b>Glaurung</b></i> is in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md). According to *[Eldamo](../E/Eldamo.md)*, the first element of the name is *[glaur](Glaur.md)* ("gold"), but the meaning of the second element is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-Gla_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gla-19">[19]</a></sup> However, there it is still glossed as "Gold-worm".<sup id="cite_ref-Gla_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gla-19">[19]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/J/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Glórund_sets_forth_to_seek_Túrin.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/0d/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Gl%C3%B3rund_sets_forth_to_seek_T%C3%BArin.jpg/200px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Gl%C3%B3rund_sets_forth_to_seek_T%C3%BArin.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="149" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/0d/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Gl%C3%B3rund_sets_forth_to_seek_T%C3%BArin.jpg/300px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Gl%C3%B3rund_sets_forth_to_seek_T%C3%BArin.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/0d/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Gl%C3%B3rund_sets_forth_to_seek_T%C3%BArin.jpg/400px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Gl%C3%B3rund_sets_forth_to_seek_T%C3%BArin.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2000" data-file-height="1492"/></a><figcaption><i>Glórund sets forth to seek Túrin</i> by J.R.R. Tolkien</figcaption></figure>

As the first and greatest of the dragons of Morgoth, Glaurung was sometimes referred to as the **Great Worm**,<sup id="cite_ref-SFifth_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SFifth-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CHComingGlaurung_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CHComingGlaurung-12">[12]</a></sup> the **Worm of Morgoth**,<sup id="cite_ref-CHDeathGlaurung_16-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CHDeathGlaurung-16">[16]</a></sup> the **Great Worm of Angband**<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> and the **Gold-worm of Angband**.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> He bore the epithet **the Golden**.<sup id="cite_ref-SRuin_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SRuin-5">[5]</a></sup> He was also given the title **Father of Dragons**,<sup id="cite_ref-SRuin_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SRuin-5">[5]</a></sup> though whether this implies that Glaurung actually literally sired the rest of his kind is uncertain. However, this seems unlikely due to the differences between individual dragons - for example, Glaurung was wingless, whereas many of the later dragons had wings.

From [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md) to [499](../F/First_Age_499.md), he ruled a host of [orcs](../O/Orcs.md) as the 'dragon-king' of the former Elven-realm of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CHComingGlaurung_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CHComingGlaurung-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Throughout early versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), Glaurung was known by different names: *Glórund* (the c. [1916](../_/1916.md)-[19](../_/1919.md) *[Lost Tales](../L/Lost_Tales.md)*) &gt; *Glórung* (the [1926](../_/1926.md) '[Sketch of the Mythology](../S/Sketch_of_the_Mythology.md)') &gt; *Glómund* (the [1930](../_/1930.md) *[Quenta Noldorinwa](../Q/Quenta_Noldorinwa.md)*; the *[Lay of Leithian](../L/Lay_of_Leithian.md)*) &gt; *Glaurung* (the c. [1951](../_/1951.md) '[Grey Annals](../T/The_Grey_Annals.md)'; the published *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*).<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup>

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, Glórund was a "[Foalókë](../F/Foalókë.md)".

The [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) cognates for *Glorund* were <i><b>Laurundo</b></i> and <i><b>Undolaurë</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup> which [Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) includes as compatible to later [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup>

The name <i><b>Angruin</b></i> was at one time proposed as an alternative name for Glaurung by [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md), however, it was ultimately rejected.<sup id="cite_ref-Root_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Root-26">[26]</a></sup> It is in Sindarin, and consists of the elements *[ang](../A/Ang.md)* ("iron") and *[ruin](../R/Ruin.md)* ("fiery red").<sup id="cite_ref-Ang_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ang-27">[27]</a></sup> It is glossed as "Iron Fire".<sup id="cite_ref-Root_26-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Root-26">[26]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ang_27-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ang-27">[27]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b><a href="../_/1988.md" title="1988">1988</a>, <a href="../_/1994.md" title="1994">1994</a>: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Glaurung was described and given role-playing statistics in the  supplements <i><a href="../C/Creatures_of_Middle-earth.md" title="Creatures of Middle-earth">Creatures of Middle-earth</a></i> and <a href="../C/Creatures_of_Middle-earth_(2nd_edition).md" title="Creatures of Middle-earth (2nd edition)"><i>Creatures of Middle-earth</i> (2nd edition)</a>. His name was given the <a href="../Q/Quenya.md" title="Quenya">Quenya</a> translation &#34;Golden Gloom&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[28]</a></sup> Furthermore, Glaurung was conceived to have bred the cold-drakes <i>Scatha</i> and <i>Ando-anca</i> (whose names were suggested to be revised to <i>Skadi</i> and <i>Skell</i>).<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29">[29]</a></sup></dd></dl>
