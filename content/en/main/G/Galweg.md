**Galweg** was a [Gnome](Gnome.md), one of the [Rodothlim](../R/Rodothlim.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Gal_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gal-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Galweg was the father of [Failivrin](../F/Failivrin.md), who fell in love with [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) when he and his friend [Flinding](../F/Flinding.md) lived in the [caverns of the Rodothlim](../N/Nargothrond.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> ruled by [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md). He was fond of Túrin, and they often aided one another.<sup id="cite_ref-Gal_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gal-1">[1]</a></sup>

Galweg was killed during [Melko](../M/Melko.md)'s [attack on the Rodothlim](../F/Fall_of_Nargothrond.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> His dwelling was later occupied by [Mîm](../M/Mîm.md) the [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md), who was in turn further slain by [Úrin](../_/Úrin.md), Túrin's father.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

While *Galweg* is not directly glossed, in [Gnomish](Gnomish.md) *gal* means "shine (golden, as the Sun)"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:37</sup> and "weg" is a masculine suffix.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](../T/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md)*, written not long after *The Book of Lost Tales*, Failivrin's father became Orodreth, and the character of Galweg had disappeared entirely.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>
