**Gundolpho Bolger** was the patriarch of the [Bolger Family](/wiki/Bolger_Family).

## History {#History}

Gundolpho married [Alfrida of the Yale](/wiki/Alfrida_of_the_Yale) and had three children: [Gundahar](/wiki/Gundahar_Bolger "Gundahar Bolger"), [Rudolph](/wiki/Rudolph_Bolger "Rudolph Bolger") and [Gundahad](/wiki/Gundahad_Bolger "Gundahad Bolger").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Bolger family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Gundolpho* may be an Old German name, derived from the elements *gunda* "war" and *olpho* (classicized spelling "-olf") from "wolf".

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gondulf_of_Provence" title="wikipedia:Gondulf of Provence">Gundulf</a></i> was the name of a patrician of Provence and bishop of Metz who is mentioned in *The History of the Franks* by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/sGregory_of_Tour" title="wikipedia:sGregory of Tour">Gregory of Tours</a>.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In drafts of the [Appendix C](/wiki/Appendix_C), Tolkien at one time had **[Celador Bolger](/wiki/Cellar_door "Cellar door")** within the Hobbit family trees,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> though he was ultimately replaced by Gundolpho Bolger.
