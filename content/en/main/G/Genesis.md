**Genesis** is a [MUD](/wiki/MUDs "MUDs") based on the Norwegian MUD [Ringen](/wiki/Ringen). It was translated into English and converted to MUD-format by [Pål-Kristian Engstad](/wiki/P%C3%A5l-Kristian_Engstad).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> It consists of both old regions like [Moria](/wiki/Moria) (from the original Ringen MUD) and new region like [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> The game contains at least 50 quests and has six different races: [Humans](/wiki/Men "Men"), [Elves](/wiki/Elves), [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits), [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes), [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) and [Goblins](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The game was released in 1988 and is still running. In 1991 a new version was published.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup>
