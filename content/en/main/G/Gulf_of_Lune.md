The **Gulf of Lune** was a sea-arm in the rough shape of an arrowhead<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> that broke through the range of the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Blue_Mountains "Blue Mountains") into [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).

The Gulf of Lune, named after the river [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) or "Lune", was created during the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) when [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) was broken. [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) broke through the mountains of the Ered Luin, creating a gulf. The course of the river Lhûn was altered so that it flowed into the gulf, giving it its name.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The remnant of Beleriand north of the gulf became known as [Forlindon](/wiki/Forlindon), and the part south of it as [Harlindon](/wiki/Harlindon). At the eastern end of the Gulf the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) were established, but smaller havens were established in Forlindon and Harlindon as well: [Forlond](/wiki/Forlond) and [Harlond](</wiki/Harlond_(Lindon)> "Harlond (Lindon)").

During the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) the ships of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) first came to the Gulf of Lune to visit the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) and [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) under [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) who remained behind, and during the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) from it the ships of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) passed away into the [West](/wiki/Aman "Aman").

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth) points out that both the river [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) and the river <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Severn" title="wikipedia:River Severn">Severn</a> flow from mountains (the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) and the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambrian_Mountains" title="wikipedia:Cambrian Mountains">Cambrian Mountains</a>, respectively) in a rough semicircle east, south, and then west into a wide gulf (the <a class="mw-selflink selflink">Gulf of Lune</a>) and the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_Channel" title="wikipedia:Bristol Channel">Bristol Channel</a>, respectively).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> While the Gulf of Lune divides [Harlindon](/wiki/Harlindon) from [Forlindon](/wiki/Forlindon), the Bristol Channel divides <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales" title="wikipedia:Wales">Wales</a> from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerset" title="wikipedia:Somerset">Somerset</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devon" title="wikipedia:Devon">Devon</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornwall" title="wikipedia:Cornwall">Cornwall</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
