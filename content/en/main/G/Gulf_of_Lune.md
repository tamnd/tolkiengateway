The **Gulf of Lune** was a sea-arm in the rough shape of an arrowhead<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> that broke through the range of the [Ered Luin](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) into [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md).

The Gulf of Lune, named after the river [Lhûn](../L/Lhûn.md) or "Lune", was created during the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) when [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) was broken. [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md) broke through the mountains of the Ered Luin, creating a gulf. The course of the river Lhûn was altered so that it flowed into the gulf, giving it its name.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The remnant of Beleriand north of the gulf became known as [Forlindon](../F/Forlindon.md), and the part south of it as [Harlindon](../H/Harlindon.md). At the eastern end of the Gulf the [Grey Havens](Grey_Havens.md) were established, but smaller havens were established in Forlindon and Harlindon as well: [Forlond](../F/Forlond.md) and [Harlond](<../H/Harlond_(Lindon).md>).

During the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) the ships of the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) first came to the Gulf of Lune to visit the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) and [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) under [Gil-galad](Gil-galad.md) who remained behind, and during the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) from it the ships of the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) passed away into the [West](../A/Aman.md).

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[John Garth](../J/John_Garth.md) points out that both the river [Lhûn](../L/Lhûn.md) and the river <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Severn" title="wikipedia:River Severn">Severn</a> flow from mountains (the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) and the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambrian_Mountains" title="wikipedia:Cambrian Mountains">Cambrian Mountains</a>, respectively) in a rough semicircle east, south, and then west into a wide gulf (the <a class="mw-selflink selflink">Gulf of Lune</a>) and the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_Channel" title="wikipedia:Bristol Channel">Bristol Channel</a>, respectively).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> While the Gulf of Lune divides [Harlindon](../H/Harlindon.md) from [Forlindon](../F/Forlindon.md), the Bristol Channel divides <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales" title="wikipedia:Wales">Wales</a> from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerset" title="wikipedia:Somerset">Somerset</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devon" title="wikipedia:Devon">Devon</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornwall" title="wikipedia:Cornwall">Cornwall</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
