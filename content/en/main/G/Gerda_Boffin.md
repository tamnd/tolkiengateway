**Gerda Boffin** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Gerda was the second child of [Uffo Boffin](/wiki/Uffo_Boffin) and [Sapphira Brockhouse](/wiki/Sapphira_Brockhouse).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She married [Adalbert Bolger](/wiki/Adalbert_Bolger) and had one son, [Filibert](/wiki/Filibert_Bolger "Filibert Bolger").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Gerda is a name of the goddess <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ger%C3%B0r" title="wikipedia:Gerðr">Gerðr</a>, an [Old Norse](/wiki/Old_Norse) name meaning "fenced-in".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2025: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Hobbit_Tales" title="Hobbit Tales">Hobbit Tales</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>By <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2960" title="Third Age 2960">2960</a>, Gerda was famed for the mushrooms she grew in a hollowed-out tree in <a href="/wiki/Bindbole_Wood" title="Bindbole Wood">Bindbole Wood</a>. She amassed some wealth selling these to <a href="/wiki/The_Green_Dragon" title="The Green Dragon">The Green Dragon</a>. The inn used these in “Boffin&#39;s Crock”, a mushroom and thyme soup named after her.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>
