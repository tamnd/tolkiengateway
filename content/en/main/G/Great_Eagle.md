The **Great Eagle** was the **[Lord of the Eagles](../L/Lord_of_the_Eagles.md)** of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-fire_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fire-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the Great Eagle resided with fifteen chieftains at the [Great Shelf](Great_Shelf.md) on the east side of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md). At some point in his life, he was wounded by an arrow, but survived after the [wizard](../W/Wizards.md) [Gandalf](Gandalf.md) healed his arrow-wound. Thenceforth, the Great Eagle held Gandalf in friendship.<sup id="cite_ref-fire_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fire-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [Quest of Erebor](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md), the Great Eagle and many of his servants saved [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) from [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Wargs](../W/Wargs.md) and brought them to the Eyrie, where they spent the night.<sup id="cite_ref-fire_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fire-2">[2]</a></sup>

Later that year, the Eagles appeared many hours into the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md) by the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md), and were crucial in the victory over the Orcs and Wargs.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> After the battle, the Great Eagle was proclaimed **King of All Birds**, and was given a golden crown while his fifteen chieftains were each given golden collars.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early draft of the chapter "[Out of the Frying-Pan into the Fire](../O/Out_of_the_Frying-Pan_into_the_Fire.md)", the Great Eagle is referred to as "the **lord of eagles**" and "the **[Lord of Eagles](../L/Lord_of_Eagles.md)**" while his arrow-wound is stated to have come from a goblin's arrow.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In an early draft of the chapter "[Queer Lodgings](../Q/Queer_Lodgings.md)", the Great Eagle's title was changed to the [Lord of the Eagles](../L/Lord_of_the_Eagles.md) and he was given the title **king of all Birds**.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Speculation {#Speculation}

It is often thought that the Great Eagle is the same individual as [Gwaihir](Gwaihir.md), however it is more than likely that they are not the same one. Among several points which separate the two, the Great Eagle is mentioned to wear a crown after *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* but no crown is mentioned with Gwaihir in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. Furthermore, Gandalf states that he was carried by Gwaihir three times, including following the [Battle of the Morannon](../B/Battle_of_the_Morannon.md),<sup id="cite_ref-RKVI4_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKVI4-7">[7]</a></sup> whereas the number would be at least five if Gwaihir and the Lord of the Eagles were one and the same.

It is also possible that the Great Eagle was [Thorondor](../T/Thorondor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-speculation_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-speculation-8">[8]</a></sup> who also bore the title [Lord of the Eagles](../L/Lord_of_the_Eagles.md).
