**Golasgil** was the lord of [Anfalas](/wiki/Anfalas) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-MT184_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT184-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [9 March](/wiki/9_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Golasgil led a long line of men of many sorts to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) before the [Battle of Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_Pelennor_Fields)<sup id="cite_ref-MT184_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT184-1">[1]</a></sup>. With the exception of the men of his household the men that he commanded - hunters, herdsmen, and villagers - were scantily equipped.<sup id="cite_ref-MT184_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT184-1">[1]</a></sup> Since Golasgil was not mentioned among the fallen in the [Song of the Mounds of Mundburg](/wiki/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg),<sup id="cite_ref-Song_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Song-3">[3]</a></sup> it appears that he survived the Battle of the Pelennor Fields.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggests that *Golasgil* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which means "star of leaves" and contains the elements *golas* ("foliage, collection of leaves") and *[gil](/wiki/Gil "Gil")* ("star, (bright) spark, silver glint, twinkle of light").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Golasgil is first met on the second level of <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>, overlooking the training of his soldiers before the battle. He participates in the defence of the city during the siege and later fights in the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Battle_of_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of Pelennor Fields">Battle of Pelennor Fields</a>. During the battle he is separated from his allies, but is able to fend off a party of orcs by himself and survives. He joins the <a href="/wiki/Army_of_the_West" title="Army of the West">Army of the West</a> and follows <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>&#39;s command to the <a href="/wiki/Black_Gate" title="Black Gate">Black Gate</a> of <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a>. During the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon" title="Battle of the Morannon">Battle of the Morannon</a> Golasgil is killed by a large <a href="/wiki/Olog-hai" title="Olog-hai">Olog-hai</a>. He is survived by his wife, Lady Marra, and his young son Orgolas.</dd></dl>
