**Gnomish**, or **I·Lam na·Ngoldathon** ("The Tongue of the [Gnomes](Gnomes.md)"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the name of which is **Goldogrin**, was the language of the Gnomes spoken in the [Great Lands](Great_Lands.md).

This language was the first incarnation of the [Celtic](../C/Celtic.md)-like [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) language invented by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) during the first drafts of his [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) during [1917](../_/1917.md) and later. The language would evolve into the language known as [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) around [1930](../_/1930.md) and finally envisioned as [the language of the Sindar](../S/Sindarin.md) during the writing of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.

Unlike [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), and like [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), Goldogrin was an inflected language with a full case system; For example, the genitive plural "of the Gnomes" is *Ngoldathon* as seen above. The ending *-on* is also known from later Quenya. It is also seen in later names such as [Caras Galadhon](../C/Caras_Galadhon.md) (said to be [Silvan Elvish](../S/Silvan_Elvish.md)) and perhaps [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md).

Gnomish is notable for being one of the very few languages by Tolkien about which he proceeded into assembling a definite [lexicon](Gnomish_Lexicon.md) and grammar notes (published in [*Parma Eldalamberon*, issue 11](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_11.md)).

Since Sindarin is a continuous revision of Gnomish, it kept its Celtic phonology, some common grammar elements (like the <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=-in&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-in (page does not exist)">-in</a></i> plural ending and Welsh style mutations) and much of the vocabulary (eg. <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lam&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lam (page does not exist)">lam</a></i> "language", <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Mab&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mab (page does not exist)">mab</a></i> "hand", *[gloss](Gloss.md)* "white").

Several Gnomish words seem like they would derive from [roots](../S/Sundocarme.md) known from *[The Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* or that they would belong to a dialect of Sindarin; for example the word *gweg* "man" could have been a Sindarin word derived from <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=WEG&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="WEG (page does not exist)">WEG</a> (see [Denweg](../D/Denweg.md)).
