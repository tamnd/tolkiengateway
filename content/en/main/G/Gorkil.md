The **Gorkil**, also known as the **Goblin King**, was a hero within the "[Goblins](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains "Orcs of the Misty Mountains")" faction. Gorkil was invented by [Electronic Arts](/wiki/Electronic_Arts) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II)*, where he was voiced by [Dave Wittenberg](/wiki/Dave_Wittenberg).

## History {#History}

Gorkil was a puppet ruler used by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) to hold sway over the disparate tribes and unions among the [Orcs of the Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains "Orcs of the Misty Mountains"). From his stronghold in the [Ettenmoors](/wiki/Ettenmoors), the Goblin King threatened the [Free Peoples](/wiki/Free_peoples "Free peoples") of the [North](/wiki/Eriador "Eriador") and gathered a great following. After a skirmish at his fortress, the forces of [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in) and [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) put an end to the Goblin King's reign, and the Orc host was dispersed.

## Spells {#Spells}

- Skull Totem - Places a skull totem which will remain on the map until \*destroyed. Reveals the nearby shroud and detects stealth.
- Mount/Dismount - Mount and unmount Gorkil's scorpion.
- Battle Frenzy - Targeted units gain +50% Damage, +50% Armor, and earn experience twice as fast.
- Poisoned Stinger - Targeted enemy becomes stunned and takes poison damage over time.
- Call from the Deep - Summons three Fire Drakes under the caster's control.
