The **Gorkil**, also known as the **Goblin King**, was a hero within the "[Goblins](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md)" faction. Gorkil was invented by [Electronic Arts](../E/Electronic_Arts.md) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md)*, where he was voiced by [Dave Wittenberg](../D/Dave_Wittenberg.md).

## History {#History}

Gorkil was a puppet ruler used by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) to hold sway over the disparate tribes and unions among the [Orcs of the Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md). From his stronghold in the [Ettenmoors](../E/Ettenmoors.md), the Goblin King threatened the [Free Peoples](../F/Free_peoples~19934.md) of the [North](../E/Eriador.md) and gathered a great following. After a skirmish at his fortress, the forces of [Glóin](Glóin.md) and [Glorfindel](Glorfindel.md) put an end to the Goblin King's reign, and the Orc host was dispersed.

## Spells {#Spells}

- Skull Totem - Places a skull totem which will remain on the map until \*destroyed. Reveals the nearby shroud and detects stealth.
- Mount/Dismount - Mount and unmount Gorkil's scorpion.
- Battle Frenzy - Targeted units gain +50% Damage, +50% Armor, and earn experience twice as fast.
- Poisoned Stinger - Targeted enemy becomes stunned and takes poison damage over time.
- Call from the Deep - Summons three Fire Drakes under the caster's control.
