**Graham Faulkner** (1947) is a former British actor. He retired from acting years ago, and before that played mostly minor roles. He voiced [Déagol](/wiki/D%C3%A9agol), [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny) and an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") Captain in the [1981 radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)")*.*
