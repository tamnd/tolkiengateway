**Greenland** may be a name for [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) used by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). It appears only once, in a quick note written c. late 1939, while Tolkien was still brainstorming for his plot in the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Fourth_Phase&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fourth Phase (page does not exist)">Fourth Phase</a>. It states clearly, speaking of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) in the end of the story, "*They go west and set sail to Greenland*". In a more complete set of notes dating to the same approximate time, however, there is similarly written "*a green land by the Sea*".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
