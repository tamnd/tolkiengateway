The **Gondolinic Runes** were a writing system used in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin). They were created by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) between [1920](/wiki/1920)-[1925](/wiki/1925), when he was devising the first writing systems for his Elvish [languages](/wiki/Languages "Languages"). They were the first runic system invented by him, and one of the few alphabets including lower case letters.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Like the [Gnomic Letters](/wiki/Gnomic_Letters), this early writing system was never used.

The only possible glimpse of these runes used within the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") is in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*: [Glamdring](/wiki/Glamdring) and [Orcrist](/wiki/Orcrist), famous swords from Gondolin, had runes that even [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) was unable to read, but [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) (who knew of all types of runes) could.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> However it is clear that Tolkien did not consider using these letters in his writings, as in 1960, while rewriting *The Hobbit*, he found another explanation of why Gandalf could not read the runes: Glamdring was covered in dark blood when Gandalf found it, explaining why the runes were unreadable until cleaned by Elrond.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The sounds they represents are of those that appear in the alphabet, but not attested in any elvish tongue.</span>
</li>
</ol></div>

## Examples {#Examples}

If we accept the values of the runes above, we can apply them to the inscriptions of the famous swords of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*:

- <em style="font-family: Gondolin; font-style: normal;">glamdriñ</em>, *Glamdring* (Foe-hammer)
- <em style="font-family: Gondolin; font-style: normal;">orkrist</em>, *Orcrist* (Orc-cleaver)
