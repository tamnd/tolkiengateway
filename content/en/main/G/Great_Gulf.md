The **Great Gulf** was an immense gulf that divided [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) from southern regions of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Great Gulf was created in the [Battle of the Powers](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers), when the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) waged war against [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor) after they discovered the first [Elves](/wiki/Elves). As a result of the war, [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) broke into Middle-earth, forming the Great Gulf and several lesser bays to its north, including the [Bay of Balar](/wiki/Bay_of_Balar).<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

South of the river [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion), "the land narrowed suddenly" and gave way to the Great Gulf.<sup id="cite_ref-Ambar_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ambar-3">[3]</a></sup> The Great Gulf almost touched the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains),<sup id="cite_ref-Ambar_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ambar-3">[3]</a></sup> but the land around the southern end of the mountain chain was wide enough to allow travellers to pass between [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup>

After the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), most of Beleriand was drowned, and much of the Great Gulf would have vanished with it. Although not explicitly stated, the eastern end of the Great Gulf in Eriador likely became the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas).
