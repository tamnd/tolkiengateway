The <i><b>Golden Book</b></i> ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya")*Parma Kuluina*) was a book about the history of the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days), kept by the Elves of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) in their city of [Kortirion](/wiki/Kortirion). [Pengolodh](/wiki/Pengolodh) consulted the Golden Book when compiling the *[Quenta Silmarillion](/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion)*. [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol)/[Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine) was allowed to read the *Parma Kuluina*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Parma Kuluina* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Golden Book", consisting of *[parma](/wiki/Parma "Parma")* ("book") + *kuluina* ("orange; of gold, golden").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The earlier [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) name of the Golden Book was rendered as *Parma Kuluinen*,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Consisting of *[Parma](/wiki/Parma)* ("skin, bark; parchment; book, writings") from the root PARA.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> It was also known as the **Golden Book of [Heorrenda](/wiki/Heorrenda)**<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
