<i><b>gwaith</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning "'people' associated by place and occupation". The forms <i><b>-weith</b></i> and <i><b>waith</b></i> are often used of "regions in proper names *or* peoples".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

- denoting people
    - <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Danwaith&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Danwaith (page does not exist)">Danwaith</a></i>
    - *[Forodwaith](<../F/Forodwaith_(people).md>)* = "People of the North"
    - *[Gaurwaith](Gaurwaith.md)*
    - *[Gwaith-i-Mírdain](Gwaith-i-Mírdain.md)* = "Brotherhood of Jewel-smiths"
    - *[Tawarwaith](../S/Silvan_Elves.md#Names)*

- denoting region or land
    - *[Drúwaith Iaur](../D/Drúwaith_Iaur.md)*
    - *[Enedwaith](../E/Enedwaith.md)*
    - *[Forodwaith](<../F/Forodwaith_(lands).md>)*
    - *[Haradwaith](../H/Harad.md)*
