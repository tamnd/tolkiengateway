<i><b>gwaith</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "'people' associated by place and occupation". The forms <i><b>-weith</b></i> and <i><b>waith</b></i> are often used of "regions in proper names *or* peoples".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- denoting people
    - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Danwaith&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Danwaith (page does not exist)">Danwaith</a></i>
    - *[Forodwaith](</wiki/Forodwaith_(people)> "Forodwaith (people)")* = "People of the North"
    - *[Gaurwaith](/wiki/Gaurwaith)*
    - *[Gwaith-i-Mírdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-M%C3%ADrdain)* = "Brotherhood of Jewel-smiths"
    - *[Tawarwaith](/wiki/Silvan_Elves#Names "Silvan Elves")*

- denoting region or land
    - *[Drúwaith Iaur](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAwaith_Iaur)*
    - *[Enedwaith](/wiki/Enedwaith)*
    - *[Forodwaith](</wiki/Forodwaith_(lands)> "Forodwaith (lands)")*
    - *[Haradwaith](/wiki/Harad "Harad")*
