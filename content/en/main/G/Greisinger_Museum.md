The **Greisinger Museum** is a foundation housing the Greisinger Collection (GC), a private collection of art, literature and collectibles inspired by [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). The museum is located in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenins" title="wikipedia:Jenins">Jenins</a>, Switzerland. It opened on [7 September](../_/7_September.md) [2013](../_/2013.md) (it was under construction since mid-2008).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Collection {#Collection}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/G/Greisinger_Museum_-_Bag_End.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/b7/Greisinger_Museum_-_Bag_End.jpg/300px-Greisinger_Museum_-_Bag_End.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="132" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/b7/Greisinger_Museum_-_Bag_End.jpg/450px-Greisinger_Museum_-_Bag_End.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/b7/Greisinger_Museum_-_Bag_End.jpg/600px-Greisinger_Museum_-_Bag_End.jpg 2x" data-file-width="860" data-file-height="379"/></a><figcaption><a class="mw-redirect" href="../H/Hobbit-hole.md" title="Hobbit-hole">Hobbit-hole</a> at Greisinger Museum</figcaption></figure>

The Greisinger Collection is Bernd Greisinger's private collection of items related to Middle-earth. A selection of the collection includes:

- over 600 original paintings by more than 100 artists inspired by Tolkien's works (including [Roger Garland](../R/Roger_Garland.md), [the Brothers Hildebrandt](../T/The_Brothers_Hildebrandt.md), [Alan Lee](../A/Alan_Lee.md), [Ted Nasmith](../T/Ted_Nasmith.md), and many others)
- a copy of every impression of the UK first editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, as well as copy of each UK impression of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* from 1937 to 1999 (including many rare, signed books).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
