The **Great Gate** was the guarded doorway in the eastern point of the [City Wall](/wiki/City_Wall), the outer wall on the first and lowest level of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), the capital city of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:751–2</sup>

## Description {#Description}

The Gate itself consisted of large rolling doors<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:751–2</sup> made of steel and iron with posts made of steel. It was protected by towers and bastions of indomitable stone.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:828–9</sup> Behind the Gate was a wide court that ended at the base of the great "ship-keel" of stone that cut through most of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:751–2</sup> Before the Gate was a great paved space where all the roads that led through the [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields) to Minas Tirith converged.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:770</sup>

## History {#History}

The city of Minas Tirith (then known as Minas Anor) was probably built after the founding of the realm of Gondor in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is probable that the Great Gate was built later during the history of the city, because Minas Anor was originally of less importance and only consisted of a small fort on the summit of Amon Anor with the main purpose of guarding [Rath Dínen](/wiki/Rath_D%C3%ADnen), the Tombs of the Kings, while the rest of the rocky hill of Amon Anor was uninhabited.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

On [9 March](/wiki/9_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-5">[5]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") passed through the Great Gates on their way to meet [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor"). Seeing the wizard the men at the Gate knew that "the storm is indeed nigh!" Gandalf did not dissuade them of this conclusion, proclaiming, "It is upon you" and that he had "ridden on its wings".<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:751–2</sup>

[Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) was retreating with his men to the city on [10 March](/wiki/10_March)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-5">[5]</a></sup> when the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) swooped upon them. Just before the Great Gate it appeared that they would reach Faramir when Gandalf rode in and dispelled them. The next day Denethor sent his son to man the [Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor); when the forces of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) overwhelmed the defenders Faramir was again forced to lead his men back to the city. Again the Nazgûl dove upon the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians). Not two furlongs from the Great Gate they attacked and this time managed to wound Faramir.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:820–1</sup>

In the great [siege of Minas Tirith](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor "Siege of Gondor"), on [15 March](/wiki/15_March) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-5">[5]</a></sup> after midnight the host of Sauron attacked the City Wall in many places to test the strength of the defences and to keep the defenders occupied in many places. They concentrated their attack on the Great Gate by moving great siege towers and [Grond](</wiki/Grond_(battering_ram)> "Grond (battering ram)"), a hundred-foot long battering ram towards the gate under the command of the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king). Shortly before dawn, the battering ram reached the gate and pounded the Great Gate four times while the Witch-king spoke words of power during the last three strokes of the ram. At the last stroke there was a flash of lightning and the gate broke and the fragments of the door fell to the ground. The Witch-king rode through the Great Gate where Gandalf awaited him and forbade him to enter,<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:828–9</sup> but then he turned back when the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) arrived to command his troops against the onrushing Rohirrim.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

After the rout of the host of Sauron from the Pelennor, a temporary barricade was erected in place of the Great Gate.<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-7">[7]</a></sup> Later, on [1 May](/wiki/1_May) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) received the [Crown of Gondor](/wiki/Crown_of_Gondor) in front of the ruined Gate and then [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin_the_Tall "Húrin the Tall"), the [Warden of the Keys](/wiki/Warden_of_the_Keys), opened the barricade for him to enter the City.<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-7">[7]</a></sup>

Sometime after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) became the Lord of [Aglarond](/wiki/Glittering_Caves "Glittering Caves"). He, and [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) from [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"), built new Gates of *[mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril")* and steel for Minas Tirith as a replacement for the Great Gate that had been broken during the siege.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The Great Gate of Minas Tirith was also referred to as the Great Gate of the City,<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:750</sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> the Gate of Minas Tirith,<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> the Gate of the City,<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:820</sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> the City Gate,<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:827</sup> the City-gate<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:819</sup> or the Gate of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:829</sup>
