**Gollum's Plan** is the fifty-first scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and the sixty-sixth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) slinks a short distance away from [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) and [Sam](/wiki/Sam). As the two hobbits call out for him, he struggles with himself about the treatment he received at the hands of [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) and his men. Sméagol whimpers "[Master](/wiki/Frodo "Frodo") looks after us", while Gollum says Frodo betrayed them and the two hobbits should be killed, and [the Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") retrieved. Sméagol voices his fear that Sam knows of their villainy, and that it would be too risky to kill them.

As Frodo and Sam come nearer, Gollum says thoughtfully "We could let *her* do it", to which Sméagol eagerly agrees.

Smiling, Gollum comes out from the tree he was hiding behind and says to Frodo and Sam they have a long way to go on their journey yet and that "Sméagol will show you the way".

## Differences {#Differences}

A broader point of divergence between the films and the book is the plan of Gollum. In this scene, Gollum and Sméagol together agree at this point to kill the hobbits and take the Ring back for themselves. In the book however, a passage in the chapter "[The Stairs of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/The_Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol)", where Gollum returns to find Frodo and Sam asleep during their journey up the [stairs](/wiki/Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol "Stairs of Cirith Ungol"), suggests that Gollum had second thoughts about leading the two hobbits to [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> even at this late stage.
