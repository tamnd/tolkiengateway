The **Gate of Wood**, also known as the **Dark Gate**,<sup id="cite_ref-SilmMaeglin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SilmMaeglin-1">[1]</a></sup> was the first of the [Seven Gates of Gondolin](/wiki/Seven_Gates_of_Gondolin).

## History {#History}

The Gate of Wood was placed at the end of the secret passage and the beginning of the [Orfalch Echor](/wiki/Orfalch_Echor), in an arch bordered by pillars. It was essentially a wooden portcullis that rose upon being touched in a certain place, an attribute which may have been cancellable in the case of siege. It was guarded by the [Elemmakil's](/wiki/Elemmakil "Elemmakil") [Outer Guard](/wiki/Dark_Guard "Dark Guard") stationed in a nearby cavern. It was followed by the [Gate of Stone](/wiki/Gate_of_Stone).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
