**Greenfields** or **Green Fields** was a district between the [Norbourn](../N/Norbourn.md) and [Brandywine](../B/Brandywine.md) rivers<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in the [Northfarthing](../N/Northfarthing.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

In the year [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1147](../T/Third_Age_2747.md) there was fought the [Battle of Greenfields](../B/Battle_of_Greenfields.md) between invading [orcs](../O/Orcs.md) from the [Mount Gram](../M/Mount_Gram.md) and the [Hobbitry-in-arms](../H/Hobbitry-in-arms.md). The Hobbits claimed the victory after their leader [Bandobras Took](../B/Bandobras_Took.md) (nicknamed *Bullroarer*) stroke off the head of the [goblin](../O/Orcs.md) king [Golfimbul](Golfimbul.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> This was the only battle fought inside the boundaries of the Shire before the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) and the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
