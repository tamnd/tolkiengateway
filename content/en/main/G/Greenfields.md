**Greenfields** or **Green Fields** was a district between the [Norbourn](/wiki/Norbourn) and [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) rivers<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

In the year [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1147](/wiki/Third_Age_2747 "Third Age 2747") there was fought the [Battle of Greenfields](/wiki/Battle_of_Greenfields) between invading [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") from the [Mount Gram](/wiki/Mount_Gram) and the [Hobbitry-in-arms](/wiki/Hobbitry-in-arms). The Hobbits claimed the victory after their leader [Bandobras Took](/wiki/Bandobras_Took) (nicknamed *Bullroarer*) stroke off the head of the [goblin](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") king [Golfimbul](/wiki/Golfimbul).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> This was the only battle fought inside the boundaries of the Shire before the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) and the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
