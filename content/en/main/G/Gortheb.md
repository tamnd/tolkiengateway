<i><b>gortheb</b></i> is an adjective meaning "horrible" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md). The related noun is *[gorth](Gorth.md)*.

Example:

- [Nan Dungortheb](../N/Nan_Dungortheb.md) = "Valley of the Horrible West"
