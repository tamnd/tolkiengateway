The **Goatleaf Family** was a family of the [Men](/wiki/Men) of [Bree](/wiki/Bree). As was typical among the Men of that region, the name comes from a plant.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Harry Goatleaf](/wiki/Harry_Goatleaf) at one time watched the [West-gate of Bree](/wiki/West-gate_of_Bree), but threw in his lot with [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny) and [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s thugs during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is linked to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/honeysuckle" title="wikipedia:honeysuckle">honeysuckle</a> or <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/woodbine" title="wikipedia:woodbine">woodbine</a>. Its French name, *chèvrefeuille* is also a medieval lay, and would therefore presumably have been familiar to [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
