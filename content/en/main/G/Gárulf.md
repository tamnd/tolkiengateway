**Gárulf** was a Man of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

Gárulf was a rider in the *[Éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored)* of [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer), [Third Marshal of the Riddermark](/wiki/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark). His *Éored* [attacked and slaughtered](/wiki/Ambush_at_Fangorn_Forest "Ambush at Fangorn Forest") the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) of [Uglúk](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk) under the eaves of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest), where Gárulf was killed. His horse [Hasufel](/wiki/Hasufel) was given to [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) by Éomer when they first met in [the Wold](/wiki/The_Wold "The Wold").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gárulf* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name, which means "Spear-wolf".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It contains the Old English elements *gár* ("spear", "weapon") and *wulf* ("wolf").<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup> It was a name of a jutish warrior in the *[Finnsburg Fragment](/wiki/Beowulf_and_the_Finnsburg_Fragment "Beowulf and the Finnsburg Fragment")*.
