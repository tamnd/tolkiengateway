**Gárulf** was a Man of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

## History {#History}

Gárulf was a rider in the *[Éored](../_/Éored.md)* of [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md), [Third Marshal of the Riddermark](../T/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark.md). His *Éored* [attacked and slaughtered](../A/Ambush_at_Fangorn_Forest.md) the [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) of [Uglúk](../U/Uglúk.md) under the eaves of [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md), where Gárulf was killed. His horse [Hasufel](../H/Hasufel.md) was given to [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) by Éomer when they first met in [the Wold](../T/The_Wold.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gárulf* is an [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name, which means "Spear-wolf".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It contains the Old English elements *gár* ("spear", "weapon") and *wulf* ("wolf").<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup> It was a name of a jutish warrior in the *[Finnsburg Fragment](../B/Beowulf_and_the_Finnsburg_Fragment.md)*.
