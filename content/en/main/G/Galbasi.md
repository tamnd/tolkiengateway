<i><b>Galbasi</b></i> is a [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) surname, translated as "Gamwich". It was corrupted as *Galpsi*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The surname comes from the name of the village <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Galabas&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Galabas (page does not exist)">Galabas</a></i> "[Gamwich](/wiki/Gamwich)".

The name *Galabas* uses the elements <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Galab-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Galab- (page does not exist)">galab-</a></i>, "game", and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Bas-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bas- (page does not exist)">bas-</a></i> "village", corresponding somewhat to "-wich" or "-wick".

## Translation {#Translation}

The name was translated as *[Gammidgy](/wiki/Gammidgy)* and the corrupted form as *[Gamgee](/wiki/Gamgee_Family "Gamgee Family")*.
