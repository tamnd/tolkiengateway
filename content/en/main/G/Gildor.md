**Gildor Inglorion** was a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of the [House of Finrod](/wiki/House_of_Finrod), who was one of the few [Exiled Noldor](/wiki/Exiled_Noldor) in the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

## History {#History}

Gildor was probably the son of some [Inglor](/wiki/Inglor) and one of the [Exiles](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor") of the House of Finrod.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> He lived in or near Rivendell.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Alan_Lee_-_Feasting_with_the_Elves.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/e/e7/Alan_Lee_-_Feasting_with_the_Elves.jpg/300px-Alan_Lee_-_Feasting_with_the_Elves.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="492" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/e/e7/Alan_Lee_-_Feasting_with_the_Elves.jpg/450px-Alan_Lee_-_Feasting_with_the_Elves.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/e/e7/Alan_Lee_-_Feasting_with_the_Elves.jpg 2x" data-file-width="488" data-file-height="800"/></a><figcaption><i>Feasting with the Elves</i> by <a href="/wiki/Alan_Lee" title="Alan Lee">Alan Lee</a></figcaption></figure>

Elven [Wandering Companies](/wiki/Wandering_Companies) crossed [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and Gildor knew well [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) when they were out in the woods. Around [September](/wiki/September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001"), as [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) was leaving the Shire for Rivendell, Gildor met the [hobbit](/wiki/Hobbit "Hobbit") as he waved them farewell in one of their camps above [Woodhall](/wiki/Woodhall). The following years he met him once more,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> perhaps at Rivendell.

### Meeting Frodo {#Meeting_Frodo}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395753"/><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Elves seldom give unguarded advice, for advice is a dangerous gift, even from the wise to the wise, and all courses may run ill.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Gildor Inglorion</cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395753"/></div>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

In September [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), he led a Wandering Company of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) travelling eastwards from the [Emyn Beraid](/wiki/Emyn_Beraid), where they went to see [Elbereth](/wiki/Varda "Varda") in the *[palantír](/wiki/Elostirion-stone "Elostirion-stone")* that was kept there. On the [24th](/wiki/24_September "24 September"), they were [singing](/wiki/Elven_Hymn_to_Elbereth "Elven Hymn to Elbereth"), and unknown to him their sounds drove away a [Black Rider](/wiki/Black_Riders "Black Riders") who was searching for [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) on the road near the [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End). As they passed by them, Gildor was the hindmost, and seeing Frodo he greeted him, to his surprise. Gildor initially intended that the elves bid the [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") farewell and continue on their way, until Pippin spoke of the Black Riders pursuing them. Upon hearing this, he invited the hobbits to join them, although it was not their custom. He rejoiced after hearing Frodo's [Quenya greeting](/wiki/Elen_s%C3%ADla_l%C3%BAmenn%27_omentielvo "Elen síla lúmenn' omentielvo") and gave him the title [Elf-friend](/wiki/Elf-friends "Elf-friends").<sup id="cite_ref-3IC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3IC-1">[1]</a></sup>

At night they camped on a hill above [Woodhall](/wiki/Woodhall); they ate and sang, and Gildor discussed the peril that haunted the three hobbits. He sensed Frodo's fears and uncertainties about his travel to Rivendell, and was worried hearing that [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) had not returned as he had promised to Frodo. When Frodo asked whether he ought to wait for Gandalf, Gildor replied that the choice was Frodo's, but if he demanded counsel, then Gildor advised him not to wait but to follow Gandalf's plans, with trusty companions. However, he refused to tell him more of the nature of the mysterious Black Riders, except that they were agents of the [Enemy](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron"), and deadly.<sup id="cite_ref-3IC_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3IC-1">[1]</a></sup>

After separating from the hobbits, he informed other [Wandering Companies](/wiki/Wandering_Companies) to be on the watch,<sup id="cite_ref-3IC_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3IC-1">[1]</a></sup> and sent word to the house of [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil), probably before the [26th](/wiki/26_September "26 September").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> He also spoke with [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and sent a message to [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Two years later, on [22 September](/wiki/22_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021"), he met the hobbits once again. He accompanied a riding of great Lords and Ladies, that planned to sail [West](/wiki/Aman "Aman") on board the [White Ship](/wiki/White_Ship)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and probably he was among the Elves who sailed to the West.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of *Gildor* is not given, but it can be deduced as [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) "Star Lord", from [gil](/wiki/Gil "Gil") ("star") and -dor ("lord, king").<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>

The same applies to *Inglorion*, which means "Son of Inglor", from *[-ion](/wiki/-ion)* ("son of"),<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup> leading to the genealogical discussion explained below.

## Ancestry {#Ancestry}

Gildor is one of the less prominently featured Elves, yet he seems important enough to raise questions concerning his lineage, or the identity of this character. Gildor calls himself "Gildor Inglorion of the House of Finrod". He also says: "*We are [Exiles](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor"), and most of our kindred have long departed and we too are only tarrying here a while, ere we return over the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer").*"<sup id="cite_ref-3IC_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3IC-1">[1]</a></sup>

It should be noted that Tolkien initially used names from his (unpublished) *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* writings rather at random in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and the first drafts of the sequel which would become *The Lord of the Rings*: other examples are the mention of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) and the appearance of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) in *The Hobbit*, which were only later brought into alignment with *The Lord of the Rings* and the unpublished mythology by a third edition of the book.<sup id="cite_ref-L257_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L257-14">[13]</a></sup> This may be the case of the reference to the name "Finrod".

The name "Gildor" first appeared in the outlines of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* in February [1938](/wiki/1938),<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup> and in Tolkien's notes of that time, "Finrod" still meant the character later known as [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin)<sup id="cite_ref-RC3IC_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC3IC-16">[15]</a></sup> and [his son](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") had the older name "[Inglor](/wiki/Inglor)".

However the name *[Inglor](/wiki/Inglor)* reappeared, in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) form, for Finarfin (who in the earlier legendarium, was named Finrod). Finarfin's [mother-name](/wiki/Amiless%C3%AB "Amilessë") was *[Ingalaurë](/wiki/Ingalaur%C3%AB)*.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[16]</a></sup> If Ingalaurë is to be translated in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) according to the [sound-changing rules](/wiki/Sindarization "Sindarization"), this would become *Inglor*. Thus, the connection of Gildor to the house of Finrod/Finarfin still remains in the updated legendarium.

This all suggests that, while Gildor might have been initially intended to be Felagund's son, in the final version he may have been a member of the House of Finrod as some other descendant, or servant, not one of Finrod's sons — perhaps one of the knights of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond).

It is also possible that he was a son of a supposed "Inglor" — a character unconnected to Finrod Felagund.

<b>1978: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Legolas" title="Legolas">Legolas</a>, here an Elf of Rivendell, tells Strider that Elrond had received news of the burden, but it is left unexplained how.</dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Adapter <a href="/wiki/Brian_Sibley" title="Brian Sibley">Brian Sibley</a> wanted to use as much of the original Elvish lines as possible. This meant that the heavily wounded Frodo told the line <i><a href="/wiki/Elen_s%C3%ADla_l%C3%BAmenn%27_omentielvo" title="Elen síla lúmenn&#39; omentielvo">Elen síla lúmenn&#39; omentielvo</a></i> to Glorfindel instead.</dd></dl>

<b>1992: <a href="/wiki/Der_Herr_der_Ringe_(1992_German_radio_series)" title="Der Herr der Ringe (1992 German radio series)"><i>Der Herr der Ringe</i> (1992 German radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gildor is played by Charles Wirths.</dd></dl>

<b>2001-2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Despite not featuring in <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> film series</a>, Decipher produced a card depicting the character.</dd></dl>

<b>2001: <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/P%C3%A1n_prste%C5%88ov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series)" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The voice of Gildor is provided by Marián Geišberg.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>This video game features both <a href="/wiki/Tom_Bombadil" title="Tom Bombadil">Tom Bombadil</a> and <a href="/wiki/Glorfindel" title="Glorfindel">Glorfindel</a>. Tom apparently knows nothing of Frodo carrying the Ring, yet Glorfindel does - he is sent out by Elrond. Elrond had received a message, but from whom this message came is left untold.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gildor makes a brief appearance in the Hobbit introduction to the <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar" title="The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar">Shadows of Angmar</a></i> driving out a <a href="/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl" title="Nazgûl">Black Rider</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Shire" title="The Shire">the Shire</a>. He also appears in the hobbit village of <a href="/wiki/Tighfield" title="Tighfield">Tighfield</a>, interacting with the village&#39;s children.</dd>
<dd>Later he is also the representative for the elves for the Council of the North in the third book to the <i>Shadows of Angmar</i>: &#34;The Council of the North&#34;. Besides the epic story line, players can find him in the Elven refuge Lin Giliath near Esteldín in the <a href="/wiki/North_Downs" title="North Downs">North Downs</a>.</dd></dl>
