The **Great Hall of Thráin** was the vast bottommost cellar of [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) that served as the lair of [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) the [Dragon](../D/Dragons.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Inside_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Inside-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

The Great Hall was located at the mountain's root and was the lowest chamber in the Lonely Mountain.<sup id="cite_ref-Inside_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Inside-1">[1]</a></sup> When [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and the [Dwarves](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) ascended to the [Front Gate](../F/Front_Gate.md) from the Great Hall they had to climb "long stairs, and turned and went down wide echoing ways, and turned again and climbed yet more stairs, and yet more stairs again", which wore out the legs of the hobbit and gives some idea of how deep the Great Hall was.<sup id="cite_ref-Home_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Home-2">[2]</a></sup>

Its exact size is unknown,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> but when Bilbo explored the space the Dwarves saw his light dwindle, disappear, and reappear as he climbed the mounds of treasure.<sup id="cite_ref-Home_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Home-2">[2]</a></sup> There was at least one stairway leading upward that was wide enough for Smaug to reach the Hall and coming down from the west was the tunnel from the secret [Back Door](../B/Back_Door.md), which was too small for the dragon to enter.<sup id="cite_ref-Inside_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Inside-1">[1]</a></sup>

The original function of the Great Hall of Thráin may have been as the throne-room of [Thráin I](../T/Thráin_I.md), but later it became a dungeon/treasury,<sup id="cite_ref-Inside_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Inside-1">[1]</a></sup> possibly when the [Great Chamber of Thrór](Great_Chamber_of_Thrór.md) was excavated. Untouched by the dragon were magical golden harps as well as chainmail and weapons that hung from the walls.<sup id="cite_ref-Home_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Home-2">[2]</a></sup> However, when first seen by Bilbo, the main feature of the hall was the hoard of treasure that Smaug had accumulated.<sup id="cite_ref-Inside_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Inside-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1999](../T/Third_Age_1999.md) [Thráin I](../T/Thráin_I.md) came to Erebor and established the [Kingdom under the Mountain](../K/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-4">[3]</a></sup> Either then or soon thereafter the [Arkenstone](../A/Arkenstone.md) was discovered under the roots of the Mountain<sup id="cite_ref-Inside_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Inside-1">[1]</a></sup> and was kept in the newly made Great Hall (which was named after the kingdom's founder). The gemstone was removed when [Thorin I](../T/Thorin_I.md) departed for the [Grey Mountains](Grey_Mountains.md) in [2210](../T/Third_Age_2210.md), but was brought back in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2590](../T/Third_Age_2590.md) when Thrór returned to Erebor, and the Arkenstone was again stored in the Great Hall.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-4">[3]</a></sup>

In [2770](../T/Third_Age_2770.md) Smaug descended upon the Mountain,<sup id="cite_ref-TA_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-4">[3]</a></sup> killing or routing all of the Dwarves. He took the Great Hall as his abode and piled all of his plunder in it for his bed.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

In [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-4">[3]</a></sup> Bilbo Baggins was the first person to visit the Great Hall of Thráin after the invasion of Smaug. On his first visit the dragon was asleep and the hobbit made off with a golden cup. On his second visit he conversed with Smaug and discovered the Worm's weak spot.<sup id="cite_ref-Inside_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Inside-1">[1]</a></sup> On his third visit, with the Dwarves, the company explored the Hall and escaped from the Mountain.<sup id="cite_ref-Home_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Home-2">[2]</a></sup> After the host of Elves and Men from [Esgaroth](../L/Lake-town.md) arrived the Dwarves passed their time in the Great Hall piling and ordering the treasure, while Thorin searched for the Arkenstone.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md) the Arkenstone was not kept in the Great Hall; instead it was placed on Thorin's tomb, alongside Orcrist; where it is said that the sword gleamed in the dark at the approach of foes, preventing the Dwarf-realm from ever being taken unawares.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>
