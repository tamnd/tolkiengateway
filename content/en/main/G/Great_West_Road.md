The **Great West Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-RKMap_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKMap-1">[1]</a></sup> also referred to as the **West Road**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Index_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-3">[3]</a></sup> or the **horse-road**<sup id="cite_ref-Index_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Isen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-4">[4]</a></sup> was the name of the portion of the **[Great Road](/wiki/Great_Road)**<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> between the eastern shore of the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen)<sup id="cite_ref-Index_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-3">[3]</a></sup> and the [north gate](/wiki/Forannest "Forannest") of the [Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Gate_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-7">[7]</a></sup> north of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).

## Course {#Course}

The Great West Road ran from the eastern shore of the Fords of Isen straight east for approximately two miles, then bent sharply to the south-east<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-4">[4]</a></sup> and ran across [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon)/[Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) roughly parallel to the northern side of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) through the [Westfold](/wiki/Westfold) where it met the [Deeping-road](/wiki/Deeping-road) that ran north-east from [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> continued through the [Folde](/wiki/Folde) on to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) , then through the [Eastfold](/wiki/Eastfold) across the [Mering Stream](/wiki/Mering_Stream) into [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien) where it ran through the northern part of the [Firien Wood](/wiki/Firienholt "Firienholt") and along the northern edge of [Drúadan Forest](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest) to the northern side of [Amon Dîn](/wiki/Amon_D%C3%AEn) where it turned further to the south-east until it reached the north gate of the Rammas Echor in the north of Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-RKMap_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKMap-1">[1]</a></sup>

The road from the Gate of Minas Tirith to the north gate of the Rammas Echor to join the Great West Road<sup id="cite_ref-UI_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Gate_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-7">[7]</a></sup> was called the **North-way**.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

The part of the Great Road that ran from the western shore of the Fords of Isen to [Fornost Erain](/wiki/Fornost_Erain) was called the **North Road** and later called the **[Greenway](/wiki/Greenway)** by the [Bree-folk](/wiki/Bree-folk).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
