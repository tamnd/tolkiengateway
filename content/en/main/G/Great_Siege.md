The **Great Siege** was a conflict, recalled by [Gorbag](/wiki/Gorbag) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) while talking to [Shagrat](/wiki/Shagrat).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He was most likely referring to the [Siege of Barad-dûr](/wiki/Siege_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr) by the [Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> which lasted from [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> until [3441](/wiki/Second_Age_3441 "Second Age 3441").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
