The **Great Siege** was a conflict, recalled by [Gorbag](Gorbag.md) during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) while talking to [Shagrat](../S/Shagrat.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He was most likely referring to the [Siege of Barad-dûr](../S/Siege_of_Barad-dûr.md) by the [Last Alliance](../L/Last_Alliance.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> which lasted from [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3434](../S/Second_Age_3434.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> until [3441](../S/Second_Age_3441.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
