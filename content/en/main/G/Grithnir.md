**Grithnir** was a servant of the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md).

## History {#History}

In the prime of his life, in the time of [Hador Lórindol](../H/Hador.md), Grithnir was said to have travelled widely in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), and come to know its ways well. For this reason, in his old age, he was selected by [Morwen](../M/Morwen.md) as one on the two guides to travel to [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) with her son [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md). They were successful, and Túrin was taken into King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md)'s court, but unlike his companion [Gethron](Gethron.md), Grithnir never saw his northern homelands again. He became sick while a guest in [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md), and died before he could make the journey home.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

This character already appears in the early version of the [Legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) of *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, with the name <i><b>Gumlin</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Here he remains in [Artanor](../A/Artanor.md) after bringing Túrin, as he is too old. But years after Túrin is exiled, he begs [Tinwelint](../T/Tinwelint.md) to depart back to [Hisilómë](../H/Hisilómë.md) and he is granted two guides. He almost dies in the journey, as it was winter, but he is rescued by the men of [Mavwin](../M/Mavwin.md). This way Mavwin knew of Túrin's fame and misfortune, and wished to move to Artanor. The following spring, Gumlin died.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
