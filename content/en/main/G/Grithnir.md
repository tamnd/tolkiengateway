**Grithnir** was a servant of the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador).

## History {#History}

In the prime of his life, in the time of [Hador Lórindol](/wiki/Hador "Hador"), Grithnir was said to have travelled widely in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), and come to know its ways well. For this reason, in his old age, he was selected by [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen) as one on the two guides to travel to [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) with her son [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin). They were successful, and Túrin was taken into King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol)'s court, but unlike his companion [Gethron](/wiki/Gethron), Grithnir never saw his northern homelands again. He became sick while a guest in [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth), and died before he could make the journey home.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

This character already appears in the early version of the [Legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium) of *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, with the name <i><b>Gumlin</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Here he remains in [Artanor](/wiki/Artanor) after bringing Túrin, as he is too old. But years after Túrin is exiled, he begs [Tinwelint](/wiki/Tinwelint) to depart back to [Hisilómë](/wiki/Hisil%C3%B3m%C3%AB) and he is granted two guides. He almost dies in the journey, as it was winter, but he is rescued by the men of [Mavwin](/wiki/Mavwin). This way Mavwin knew of Túrin's fame and misfortune, and wished to move to Artanor. The following spring, Gumlin died.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
