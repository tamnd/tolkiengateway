**Gethron**, like [Grithnir](/wiki/Grithnir), had been young at the time of [Hador Lórindol](/wiki/Hador "Hador"), when they had roamed far and wide throughout [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).

In their old age, they remained in the service of the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador), whose lord at that time was Hador's grandson [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin). After Húrin failed to return from the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), his wife [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen) chose Gethron and Grithnir as guides for the young [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin), to lead him out of the now dangerous land of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min). It was Gethron who stood before Thingol in the halls of [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth) and asked that Túrin be fostered by the King, so playing a small part in Túrin's deadly fate.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

These two "aged servants" are never named in *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, but their names are given in *[Narn i Chîn Húrin](</wiki/Narn_i_Ch%C3%AEn_H%C3%BArin_(tale)> "Narn i Chîn Húrin (tale)")*.
