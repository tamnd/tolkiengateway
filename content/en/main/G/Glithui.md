**Glithui** was a small river that rose beneath [Amon Darthir](/wiki/Amon_Darthir) in the [Mountains of Shadow](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin "Ered Wethrin"), beneath a steep and treacherous pass out of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) into [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand). It flowed southward away from the mountains for less than fifty miles, after which its waters ran into the [Teiglin](/wiki/Teiglin) (north of that river's confluence with the [Malduin](/wiki/Malduin)), and on into the mighty river [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) led [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) across the Glithui as they journeyed east along the Ered Wethrin towards [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Later, when [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) escaped from [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) he used the pass that came down to the Glithui.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Glithui is shown on the map that accompanies *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> but is not labelled there.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> However, the label "Glithui" appears on the general map of [The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>. <span></span>
