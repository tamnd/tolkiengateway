**Glithui** was a small river that rose beneath [Amon Darthir](../A/Amon_Darthir.md) in the [Mountains of Shadow](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md), beneath a steep and treacherous pass out of [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) into [West Beleriand](../W/West_Beleriand.md). It flowed southward away from the mountains for less than fifty miles, after which its waters ran into the [Teiglin](../T/Teiglin.md) (north of that river's confluence with the [Malduin](../M/Malduin.md)), and on into the mighty river [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md) led [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) across the Glithui as they journeyed east along the Ered Wethrin towards [Gondolin](Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Later, when [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) escaped from [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) he used the pass that came down to the Glithui.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Glithui is shown on the map that accompanies *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> but is not labelled there.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> However, the label "Glithui" appears on the general map of [The Children of Húrin](../T/The_Children_of_Húrin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>. <span></span>
