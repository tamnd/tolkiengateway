**Grof** is a [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") from the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) in *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North)*. He appears on the marketplace of [Bree](/wiki/Bree), where he sells several goods. He was asked by a [man of Bree](/wiki/Bree-men "Bree-men"), [Otto Aster](/wiki/Otto_Aster), to sell weapons to him, so he could arm the towns of [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land). Grof originally refused, but after [Eradan](</wiki/Eradan_(video_game_character)> "Eradan (video game character)"), [Andriel](/wiki/Andriel) and [Farin](</wiki/Farin_(video_game_character)> "Farin (video game character)") talked with him, on the request of Otto, he changed his view. Grof then returned to his home in the Blue Mountains to forge weapons for the [Bree-landers](/wiki/Bree-landers).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
