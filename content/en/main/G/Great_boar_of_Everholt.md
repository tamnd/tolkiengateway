The **great boar of Everholt** was a monstrous [boar](/wiki/Boars "Boars") that inhabited [Everholt](/wiki/Everholt), a section of the [Firien Wood](/wiki/Firien_Wood),<sup id="cite_ref-Folca_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folca-1">[1]</a></sup> around the feet of the [Halifirien](/wiki/Halifirien) in the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains).

[Folca](/wiki/Folca), the thirteenth [King of Rohan](/wiki/King_of_Rohan), who was also a renowned hunter, resolved to hunt down and slay the beast. He succeeded, but died in the process due to the tusk-wounds inflicted on him by the boar in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2864](/wiki/Third_Age_2864 "Third Age 2864").<sup id="cite_ref-Folca_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folca-1">[1]</a></sup>
