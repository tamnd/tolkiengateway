**Green Hill Country** was a patch of country in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), marked out by the [Green Hills](/wiki/Green_Hills) that ran west to east through three of the Shire's four [Farthings](/wiki/Farthings).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The western feet of the Green Hills were in the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing), in the lands of the [Tooks](/wiki/Took_Family "Took Family"). The Hills cut through the northern corner of the [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing), where the village of [Pincup](/wiki/Pincup) lay on their southern slopes. Finally, they passed through the border into the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing), where they became densely wooded, and reached their eastern end at the appropriately named [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

From [Tuckborough](/wiki/Tuckborough), a road ran eastwards through the Green Hill Country, and it was this that [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") briefly used in their journey from [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).
