**Gregory Ellis** (born [March 21](/wiki/21_March "21 March"), [1968](/wiki/1968)) is a British screen and voice actor. His most iconic screen roles are that of Michael Amador, the virus broker, in the third season of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/24_(TV_series)" title="wikipedia:24 (TV series)">24</a></i>, and Chief Engineer Olson in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek_(2009_film)" title="wikipedia:Star Trek (2009 film)">Star Trek<i></i></a>.
