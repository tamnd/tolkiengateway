**Greylin** was a river of the far north of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

## Course {#Course}

The Greylin rose in the western [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) and flowed south-westward to meet the [Langwell](/wiki/Langwell) and together they formed the source of the Great River, [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Between the two rivers was the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) built [Framsburg](/wiki/Framsburg), their capital.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The second element of the name derives from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *hlynn* or "torrent", although its literal meaning was probably "the noisy one".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayals in adaptations {#Portrayals_in_adaptations}

<b>2020: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Greylin originates in the region of &#34;Elderslade&#34; which lies to the east of <a href="/wiki/Gundabad" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a>. It flows south through several waterfalls, the largest of them being &#34;the Reikfoss of Greylin&#34;. Along with the two falls of <a href="/wiki/Langwell" title="Langwell">Langwell</a> from the same height, the Reikfoss raises a large cloud of mist into the air, giving the surrounding area its name: &#34;Misthallow&#34;. The two rivers join past <a href="/wiki/Framsburg" title="Framsburg">Framsburg</a>, forming the Great River.</dd></dl>
