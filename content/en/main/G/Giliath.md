<i><b>giliath</b></i> is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "hosts of stars".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The first element is *[gil](Gil.md)* ("star"),<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup> and the second element is likely the class-plural ending *[-ath](../_/-ath.md)*.

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="../O/Osgiliath.md#Etymology" title="Osgiliath">Os<b>giliath</b></a></i> = "Citadel of the Stars"
- <i><a href="../D/Dagor-nuin-Giliath.md" title="Dagor-nuin-Giliath">Dagor-nuin-<b>Giliath</b></a></i> = "Battle-under-Stars"
