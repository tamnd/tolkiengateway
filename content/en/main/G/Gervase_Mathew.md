**Anthony Gervase Mathew** ([14 March](/wiki/14_March) [1905](/wiki/1905) – [4 April](/wiki/4_April) [1976](/wiki/1976)) was a fellow [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). He was a Dominican priest and lecturer in Byzantine studies at the [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford).

In late [1949](/wiki/1949) he introduced editor [Milton Waldman](/wiki/Milton_Waldman) to Tolkien.
