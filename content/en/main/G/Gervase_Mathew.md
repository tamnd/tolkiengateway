**Anthony Gervase Mathew** ([14 March](../_/14_March.md) [1905](../_/1905.md) – [4 April](../_/4_April.md) [1976](../_/1976.md)) was a fellow [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). He was a Dominican priest and lecturer in Byzantine studies at the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md).

In late [1949](../_/1949.md) he introduced editor [Milton Waldman](../M/Milton_Waldman.md) to Tolkien.
