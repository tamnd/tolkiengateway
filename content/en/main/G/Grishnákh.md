**Grishnákh** was a captain of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) from [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He was described as being a short, crook-legged creature, broad, with long arms hanging almost to the ground.<sup id="cite_ref-Uruk_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Uruk-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [26 February](/wiki/26_February) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-Great_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-3">[3]</a></sup> Grishnákh was part of a raiding party that captured [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") at [Parth Galen](/wiki/Parth_Galen). He was the captain of a group of Mordor-orcs which had joined [Uglúk](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk)'s forces on the plains of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). When Pippin awoke and heard the Orcs arguing, he noted that Grishnákh's voice sounded softer but more evil than the others. Grishnákh threatened to report the doings of the mountain-orcs and of Uglúk to Lugbúrz ([Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr)).<sup id="cite_ref-Uruk_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Uruk-2">[2]</a></sup>

On [28 February](/wiki/28_February)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-3">[3]</a></sup> Grishnákh and his followers split from Uglúk's company to head east, but fell back in with the [Isengarders](/wiki/Orcs_of_Isengard "Orcs of Isengard") when [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer)'s riders drew near. In the nighttime when he had a chance, Grishnákh attempted to search the hobbits for [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") (how he knew about it is unknown; possibly he was present at the torture and interrogation of [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) in Barad-dûr). After hearing Pippin imitate Gollum's croaking noise, he believed they had the One Ring and tried to escape with the two hobbits. Before he could accomplish this, he was slain by the Riders of [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) near the eaves of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest).<sup id="cite_ref-Uruk_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Uruk-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The earlier form of the name *Grishnákh* used by [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) was *Gríshnák*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Grishnákh is depicted in the <a href="/wiki/2015" title="2015">2015</a> expansion <i>The Treason of Saruman</i> on the &#34;Devilry of Saruman&#34; card, illustrated by Claudio Pozas. The card has the following effects:</dd></dl>

> Forced: After Grishnákh enters play, the first player attached 1 of the Captives to him. If Grishnákh is defeated, place the attached Captive faceup underneath To Isengard!<br>Forced: At the end of the quest phase, deal 1 damage to the attached Captive.

<b><a href="/wiki/2023" title="2023">2023</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth" title="The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth">The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Grishnákh on the &#34;Grishnákh, Brash Instigator&#34; card, illustrated by Victor Harmatiuk. The card has the following effects:</dd></dl>

> When Grishnákh enters, amass Orcs 2. When you do, until end of turn, gain control of target nonlegendary creature an opponent controls with power less than or equal to the amassed Army’s power. Untap that creature. It gains haste until end of turn.
