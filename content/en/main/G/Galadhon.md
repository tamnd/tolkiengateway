**Galadhon** was a noble [Elf](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), the son of [Elmo](/wiki/Elmo) and thus the nephew of King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) and [Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB). Little is known of him, except for the fact that he was father to [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), later [Lord of Lórien](/wiki/Lord_of_L%C3%B3rien), and [Galathil](/wiki/Galathil), father of [Nimloth](/wiki/Nimloth_of_Doriath "Nimloth of Doriath").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

His name is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and appears to be a combination of *[galadh](/wiki/Galadh "Galadh")* ("tree") + *[-on](/wiki/-on)* (masculine suffix).<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to one late version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), the father of Celeborn is named <i><b><a href="/wiki/Gilit%C4%ABro" title="Gilitīro">Gilitīro</a></b></i>, a [Telerin](/wiki/Falmari "Falmari")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> [Elf](/wiki/Elf) of [Aman](/wiki/Aman). The name is in [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin), meaning "starwatcher". Its [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) equivalent is <i><b>Gildír</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-PE23_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE23-4">[3]</a></sup>
