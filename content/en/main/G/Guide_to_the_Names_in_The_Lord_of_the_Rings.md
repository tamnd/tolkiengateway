The <b>Guide to the Names in <i>The Lord of the Rings</i></b>, later known as <b>Nomenclature of <i>The Lord of the Rings</i></b> or simply **Nomenclature** is a collection of notes made by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) after the [Dutch](../M/Max_Schuchart.md) and [Swedish](../_/Åke_Ohlmarks.md) translations had come out.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Publication history {#Publication_history}

The <i><b>Guide</b></i> was first published in <i><b><a href="../A/A_Tolkien_Compass.md" title="A Tolkien Compass">A Tolkien Compass</a></b></i>, edited by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ATC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ATC-2">[2]</a></sup> It was included in Open Court's first hardcover edition and Del Rey Books' paperback, but was dropped from the second edition at the request of [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md), the legal owners of the text.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

A new edition by [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) – called <i><b>Nomenclature</b></i> – was published in <i><b><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_A_Reader&#39;s_Companion.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: A Reader&#39;s Companion">The Lord of the Rings: A Reader&#39;s Companion</a></b></i>.

## Differences between editions {#Differences_between_editions}

### General differences {#General_differences}

- Christopher Tolkien's edition is a reader's edition rather than the annotated facsimile edition of Hammond and Scull: short, stenographic sentences and abbreviations are written out in full, and it is generally written for a less linguistic audience. Hammond and Scull included a list of abbreviations, as well as extra notes within straight brackets.
- Christopher Tolkien's edition follows his father's text in that it refers to the second, revised edition,<sup id="cite_ref-ATC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ATC-2">[2]</a></sup> while Hammond and Scull also include references to their own [50th Anniversary Edition](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md>) in straight brackets.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The first two sections have been renamed in Christopher Tolkien's edition: *Names of Persons and Peoples* and *Place-names* versus *Persons, Peoples, Creatures* and *Places*. The name of the third section, *Things*, is not changed.

### Smaller differences {#Smaller_differences}

- **Barrow-wight**: Hammond and Scull misspell the Dutch version, as well as its English translation: *grafghest* and "grave-ghast"<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup> rather than proper *grafgeest*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **Beechbone**: Hammond and Scull spelled *Buchenbein* as *Büchenbein*, the former one is the actual German translation.<sup id="cite_ref-ATC_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ATC-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Fair Folk**: Christopher Tolkien moves it below *Fairbairns*.<sup id="cite_ref-ATC_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ATC-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Grey Pilgrim** and **Grey Host**: Christopher Tolkien moves them below *Greyhame*.<sup id="cite_ref-ATC_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ATC-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Greyhame**: Christopher Tolkien refers to the [language of Rohan](../R/Rohanese.md) as "Rohan",<sup id="cite_ref-ATC_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ATC-2">[2]</a></sup> Hammond and Scull as "Rohanese".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the only occurrence of "Rohanese";<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> the rest of the Hammond and Scull-edition uses the word "Rohan".
- **Heathertoes**: Christopher Tolkien spells the suggested German translation as *Heidezhen*,<sup id="cite_ref-ATC_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ATC-2">[2]</a></sup> Hammond and Scull as *Heidezehn*.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup> [Margaret Carroux](../M/Margaret_Carroux.md) would eventually use the plural *Heidezehen*, [Wolfgang Krege](../W/Wolfgang_Krege.md) singular *Heidezeh*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
- **Orc**: Christopher Tolkien misspells Anglo-Saxon *orc-neas* as *orc-nass*<sup id="cite_ref-ATC_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ATC-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Wizard**: Christopher Tolkien omits this entry.<sup id="cite_ref-ATC_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ATC-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Wormtongue**: Christopher Tolkien misspells the Rohan form as *wyrm-tunga*, Hammond and Scull give *wyrm-tunge*.
- **Woses**: Christopher Tolkien mistranscribes the explanation as "old men of the woods". Hammond and Scull give "wild men".
- **Deadman's Dike**: Christopher Tolkien omits this entry.<sup id="cite_ref-ATC_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ATC-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Lithe**: Hammond and Scull combine this with the entries *Yule* and *Midwinter*.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Mathom**: Hammond and Scull omit this entry.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Smials**: Hammond and Scull omit this entry.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Yule**: See *Lithe*. Christopher Tolkien omits the passage about *Midwinter*.<sup id="cite_ref-ATC_2-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ATC-2">[2]</a></sup>

## The manuscript {#The_manuscript}

Several extracts from the manuscript of the *Nomenclature* were quoted in *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_A_Reader's_Companion.md)* (see for example p. 163), including information that is not found in the final version.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>
