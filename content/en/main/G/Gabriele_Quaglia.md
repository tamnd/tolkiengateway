**Gabriele Quaglia** is an Italian author who writes for the second edition of the [*The One Ring* role-playing game](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)") .

Quaglia has a long history with both role-playing games and the work of Tolkien. He was a player of *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* and later *The One Ring*. As a fan, he wrote articles for [Other Minds](/wiki/Other_Minds) and was a major contributor to the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=MERP_Fan_Modules&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MERP Fan Modules (page does not exist)">MERP Fan Modules</a> group. He transitioned towards officially licensed work after [Francesco Nepitello](/wiki/Francesco_Nepitello) approached Quaglia in the early 2020s concerning an article he had written about the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens).<sup id="cite_ref-interview_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-interview-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
