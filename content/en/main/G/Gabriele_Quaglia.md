**Gabriele Quaglia** is an Italian author who writes for the second edition of the [*The One Ring* role-playing game](<../T/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md>) .

Quaglia has a long history with both role-playing games and the work of Tolkien. He was a player of *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)* and later *The One Ring*. As a fan, he wrote articles for [Other Minds](../O/Other_Minds.md) and was a major contributor to the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=MERP_Fan_Modules&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MERP Fan Modules (page does not exist)">MERP Fan Modules</a> group. He transitioned towards officially licensed work after [Francesco Nepitello](../F/Francesco_Nepitello.md) approached Quaglia in the early 2020s concerning an article he had written about the [Grey Havens](Grey_Havens.md).<sup id="cite_ref-interview_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-interview-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
