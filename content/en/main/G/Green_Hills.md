The **Green Hills** was a range of hills that ran west-to-east through [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), through three of the Shire's four [Farthings](/wiki/Farthings), and gave its name to the region known as the [Green Hill Country](/wiki/Green_Hill_Country). To the west, in the [Tookland](/wiki/Tookland), the hills were fairly open and treeless. In contrast, their eastern slopes towards [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End) were densely wooded with trees of all kinds.<sup id="cite_ref-map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-1">[1]</a></sup> The hills were burrowed with [hobbit-holes](/wiki/Hobbit-hole "Hobbit-hole") and [smials](/wiki/Smials "Smials"), like the [Great Smials](/wiki/Great_Smials).<sup id="cite_ref-rk_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rk-2">[2]</a></sup>

At least three rivers and streams had their sources among the Green Hills; the River [Shirebourn](/wiki/Shirebourn), the [Thistle Brook](/wiki/Thistle_Brook) and the [Stock-brook](/wiki/Stock-brook). One of the chief towns of the [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits), [Tuckborough](/wiki/Tuckborough), lay among the western hills, and other villages were to be found among their slopes, or about their feet, including [Tookbank](/wiki/Tookbank), [Woodhall](/wiki/Woodhall) and [Pincup](/wiki/Pincup).<sup id="cite_ref-map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [23 September](/wiki/23_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") passed through the Green Hills on the start of their journey.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Later, in [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), when the [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) were lodged in the Shire they found the deep holes of the [Tooks](/wiki/Took_Family "Took Family") in the Green Hills defeated their attempts to control that part of the Hobbits homeland.<sup id="cite_ref-rk_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rk-2">[2]</a></sup>
