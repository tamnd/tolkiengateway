**Grimbeorn the Old** was the son of [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn).

## History {#History}

Grimbeorn became a great lord in his own right. Like his father, he was the chieftain of the [Beornings](/wiki/Beornings) and he dwelt in the same regions of the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin). During his time, the [High Pass](/wiki/High_Pass) and the [Ford of Carrock](/wiki/Ford_of_Carrock) were kept open and no [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") or [Warg](/wiki/Wargs "Wargs") dared to enter his land.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

**Grimbeorn** is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> which means "fierce-warrior" or "severe-prince"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>.

*Grim* (like in [modern English](/wiki/Modern_English "Modern English")) means "fierce" or "severe" in Old English.<sup id="cite_ref-BT_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BT-4">[4]</a></sup> For the meaning of *beorn* refer to [Beorn - Etymology](/wiki/Beorn#Etymology "Beorn").

<b>2006: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Despite not featuring in <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> film series</a>, Decipher produced a card depicting the character. He was portrayed by sword smith Peter Lyon.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Grimbeorn is the lord of all Beornings and lives at his home, the <a href="/wiki/Beorn%27s_Hall" title="Beorn&#39;s Hall">Beorninghús</a>, with his wife Gírta and three children: the eldest daughter Langhár, the middle son Sterkist and the youngest, who becomes the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Beorning" title="Beorning">Beorning</a> player&#39;s character after Grimbeorn sends them to find the Ranger <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> at the urging of <a href="/wiki/Radagast" title="Radagast">Radagast</a>. During the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a> Grimbeorn visits the camp of Vindurhal in the <a href="/wiki/High_Pass" title="High Pass">High Pass</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a>, but returns home soon after. After the destruction of <a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring" title="The One Ring">The One Ring</a>, Grimbeorn is visited by <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a>, <a href="/wiki/Legolas" title="Legolas">Legolas</a> and the player on their journey back to <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> after a brief visit to the northern lands. Already annoyed by a number of visitors, Grimbeorn is then forced to host a much larger procession when <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a> and much of his house stay at his lodge on their way to <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> for <a href="/wiki/Arwen" title="Arwen">Arwen</a>&#39;s wedding.</dd></dl>
