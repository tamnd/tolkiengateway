The ancient **Grey Mountains** were a series of mountains which continue the line of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) as the western edge of [Endor](/wiki/Endor), but on the [Southlands](/wiki/Dark_Land "Dark Land") of the continent. Their position mirrors symmetrically the [Yellow Mountains](/wiki/Yellow_Mountains) in the east. This range was created when the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) thrust [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) eastward to give more protection to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Since no maps of the entire world exist after the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), it is unknown if this mountain line still existed later. In any case they do not appear in the stories.
