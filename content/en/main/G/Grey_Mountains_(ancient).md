The ancient **Grey Mountains** were a series of mountains which continue the line of the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) as the western edge of [Endor](../E/Endor.md), but on the [Southlands](../D/Dark_Land.md) of the continent. Their position mirrors symmetrically the [Yellow Mountains](../Y/Yellow_Mountains.md) in the east. This range was created when the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) thrust [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) eastward to give more protection to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Since no maps of the entire world exist after the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), it is unknown if this mountain line still existed later. In any case they do not appear in the stories.
