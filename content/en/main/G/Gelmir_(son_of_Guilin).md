**Gelmir**, son of [Guilin](/wiki/Guilin) and brother of [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor), was a [noble Elf](/wiki/Elves_of_Nargothrond "Elves of Nargothrond") of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond). He was captured by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), and as a captive was tortured and blinded.

Years later, he was taken to the fortress of [Barad Eithel](/wiki/Barad_Eithel), where he was maimed and executed before the [elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") host. It was this sight that made Gwindor charge, beginning the great battle of the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), but of unclear meaning. It could be a combination of *gell* ("joy") and *[mîr](/wiki/M%C3%AEr "Mîr")* ("jewel").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier version of the *[Quenta Silmarillion](</wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road)> "Quenta Silmarillion (Lost Road)")*, the character executed by Morgoth was an unnamed herald of [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon). At the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears), Fingon and [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) grew impatient at the delay of [Maidros](/wiki/Maedhros "Maedhros") and sent out their heralds into the plains of [Fauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith "Anfauglith") to summon the hosts of Morgoth to come forth. Morgoth captured these heralds and then sent a relatively small force to bait Fingon into battle. When Fingon did not reveal his forces and attack, Morgoth's forces slew the herald of Fingon and sent the others back with his head. This inflamed the wrath of Fingon, and his army leaped forward; a great part of Turgon's force also joined before he could restrain them.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:§§10-11</sup>
