The **Gates of Angband** were the entrance to a labyrinthine tunnel system under [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md) called [Angband](../A/Angband.md). The great wolf [Carcharoth](../C/Carcharoth.md) was its guard, and it was before these gates that [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) slew [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md).
