The **Gates of Angband** were the entrance to a labyrinthine tunnel system under [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim) called [Angband](/wiki/Angband). The great wolf [Carcharoth](/wiki/Carcharoth) was its guard, and it was before these gates that [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) slew [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin).
