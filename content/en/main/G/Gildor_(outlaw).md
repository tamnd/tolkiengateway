**Gildor** (died [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [460](/wiki/First_Age_460 "First Age 460")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) was a one of [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir)'s [twelve outlaws](/wiki/Barahir%27s_outlaws "Barahir's outlaws").

## History {#History}

Gildor fought in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) under the command of [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir). He was part of a small group of men loyal to their lord that survived.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The survivors formed a small band and lived as outlaws in [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), near [Tarn Aeluin](/wiki/Tarn_Aeluin).

They lived in hiding for some time, until [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s agents captured [Gorlim](/wiki/Gorlim) and coerced him into giving the location of their hideaway. [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) were sent to raid it, and succeeded. Gildor and the other outlaws were killed. Only [Beren](/wiki/Beren) survived, as he had been away on an errand.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of *Gildor* is not given, but it can be deduced that it is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) "Star Lord", from [gil](/wiki/Gil "Gil") ("star") + -dor ("lord, king").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
