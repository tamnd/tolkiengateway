<i><b>gorth</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "horror".<sup id="cite_ref-AC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [NGUR](/wiki/NGUR)<sup id="cite_ref-AC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Gorthaur](/wiki/Gorthaur)

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appears the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) form *goroth* ("horror") and [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin) *ngorth* ("horror").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
