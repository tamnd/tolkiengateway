<i><b>gorth</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning "horror".<sup id="cite_ref-AC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [NGUR](../N/NGUR.md)<sup id="cite_ref-AC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Gorthaur](Gorthaur.md)

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* appears the [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) form *goroth* ("horror") and [Doriathrin](../D/Doriathrin.md) *ngorth* ("horror").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
