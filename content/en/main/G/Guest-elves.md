The **Guest-elves** were a branch of the [Green-elves](Green-elves.md) of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md).

## History {#History}

When [Denethor](<../D/Denethor_(Nando).md>), leader of the [Green-elves](Green-elves.md), was killed by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) on [Amon Ereb](../A/Amon_Ereb.md) in the [First Battle of Beleriand](../F/First_Battle_of_Beleriand.md), some of the [Laiquendi](Green-elves.md) fled westward to [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) and settled in [Arthórien](../A/Arthórien.md). These became known as the Guest-elves.<sup id="cite_ref-WJP1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJP1-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Guest-elves had their own chief, [Ithilbor](../I/Ithilbor.md), who had a permanent place in the council of [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md). Some of the Guest-elves were "darkhearted", and they did not "*mingle happily with the Teleri of Doriath*". However, "*this did not necessarily appear except under strain or provocation*".<sup id="cite_ref-WJP1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJP1-1">[1]</a></sup> Neither were they friends to the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) of [Estolad](../E/Estolad.md)—the [mannish](../M/Men.md) region situated just east of Arthórien, into which the Guest-elves often wandered by crossing the River [Celon](../C/Celon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[2]</a></sup>
