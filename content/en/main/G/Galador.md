**Galador** was the first [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth).

## History {#History}

According to legend, *Galador* was the son of [Imrazôr](/wiki/Imraz%C3%B4r) [the Númenórean](/wiki/The_N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean "The Númenórean") and the Elf-maid [Mithrellas](/wiki/Mithrellas).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-2">[2]</a></sup> The ancestors of the [Princes of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth) were a family of the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) group of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) who had left [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) before its [downfall](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Downfall of Númenor") and settled in the land of Belfalas with a stronghold upon a high promontory and had received the title "[Prince](/wiki/Prince)" from [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) with whom they were related. It is possible that this high promontory was named [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth) after the drowning of [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth), the last [King of Lórien](/wiki/King_of_L%C3%B3rien) who was lost in the sea trying to swim back to the shore of [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas),<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-2">[2]</a></sup> to remember Amroth.<sup id="cite_ref-UTNote_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote-3">[3]</a></sup> *Galador* became known as the first [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Galador* is probably a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. Its meaning and components are not glossed, but it is perhaps a compound of *[galadh](/wiki/Galadh "Galadh")* ("tree") and *[-dor](/wiki/-dor)* ("lord", "king").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
