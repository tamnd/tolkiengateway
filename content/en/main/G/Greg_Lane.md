**Greg Lane** is a New Zealand stuntman. He played various [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") and [Uruk-Hai](/wiki/Uruk-Hai) (including the torch-bearer at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg "Battle of the Hornburg")), and was the stunt double of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) and [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur).
