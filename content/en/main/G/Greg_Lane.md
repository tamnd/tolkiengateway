**Greg Lane** is a New Zealand stuntman. He played various [orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Uruk-Hai](../U/Uruk-Hai~9489.md) (including the torch-bearer at [Helm's Deep](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md)), and was the stunt double of [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) and [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md).
