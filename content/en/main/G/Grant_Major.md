**Grant Major** ([1955](/wiki/1955)) was the production designer for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"). He oversaw the creation of sets such as [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), [Moria](/wiki/Moria), and others. Realism and detail were a must in transforming [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) to the screen, without Major's perfectionism the films would have been greatly different.

## Awards {#Awards}

- [2002](/wiki/2002) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.AFI.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">AFI Award</a></b>, Production Designer of the Year, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*
- 2002 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.nbrmp.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">NBR Award</a></b>, Best Production Design/Art Direction, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2002 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.cinemaclips.com/PFCS.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">PFCS Award</a></b>, Best Production Design, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- [2003](/wiki/2003) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.artdirectors.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Excellence in Production Design Award</a></b>, Feature Film - Period or Fantasy Films, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*
- 2003 - **PFCS Award**, Best Production Design, *Peter Jackson's The Two Towers*
- 2003 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://sdfilmcritics.cinephiles.net/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">SDFCS Award</a></b>, Best Production Design, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*
- [2004](/wiki/2004) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.oscars.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Academy Award</a></b>, Best Art Direction-Set Decoration, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
- 2004 - **Excellence in Production Design Award**, Feature Film - Period or Fantasy Films, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
- 2004 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.lafca.net/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">LAFCA Award</a></b>, Best Production Design, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
