**Grant Major** ([1955](../_/1955.md)) was the production designer for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>). He oversaw the creation of sets such as [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md), [Moria](../M/Moria.md), and others. Realism and detail were a must in transforming [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) to the screen, without Major's perfectionism the films would have been greatly different.

- 2002 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.cinemaclips.com/PFCS.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">PFCS Award</a></b>, Best Production Design, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- [2003](../_/2003.md) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.artdirectors.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Excellence in Production Design Award</a></b>, Feature Film - Period or Fantasy Films, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*

- 2003 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://sdfilmcritics.cinephiles.net/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">SDFCS Award</a></b>, Best Production Design, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*
- [2004](../_/2004.md) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.oscars.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Academy Award</a></b>, Best Art Direction-Set Decoration, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
