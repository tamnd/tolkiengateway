<i><b>George Allen &amp; Unwin Spring Announcements 1937</b></i> is an catalogue from George Allen &amp; Unwin, which includes Tolkien's 'blurb' for The Hobbit.

The blurb was later printed on the front flap of the dustwrapper of the 1st Edition of The Hobbit along with additional remarks by the publisher.

The blurb is also reprinted in [J.R.R. Tolkien: A Descriptive Bibliography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Descriptive_Bibliography) and [The Annotated Hobbit](/wiki/The_Annotated_Hobbit).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
