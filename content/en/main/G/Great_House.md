The **Great House**<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-1">[1]</a></sup> was the hall of the [Master of Lake-town](/wiki/Master_of_Lake-town). It was located next to the central pool within the town and like the other buildings stood upon wooden pilings driven into the bed of the lake.<sup id="cite_ref-Welcome_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Welcome-2">[2]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) entered the Great House where there was a feast in progress. Although the Master was sceptical of [Thorin's](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin") claims, the Dwarves were welcomed by the people of [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town).<sup id="cite_ref-Welcome_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Welcome-2">[2]</a></sup> The building was later smashed by the tail of [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) when he descended upon the township.<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-1">[1]</a></sup>
