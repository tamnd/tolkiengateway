<i><b>Grenadier Models Inc.: 1987 Photographic Compendium</b></i> is a catalogue featuring photographs and figure listings of all the metal miniatures of [Grenadier Models](/wiki/Grenadier_Models_Inc. "Grenadier Models Inc.") available at the time (it was published in [1987](/wiki/1987)). It features photographs and listings of all their *Lord of the Rings* figures.
