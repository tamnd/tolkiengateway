The **Girdle of Melian** was the fence of enchantment set by [Melian](../M/Melian.md) around the kingdom of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), preventing anyone from entering without her or King [Thingol's](../T/Thingol.md) consent. It encompassed the forests of [Neldoreth](../N/Neldoreth.md) and [Region](../F/Forest_of_Region.md), [Nivrim](../N/Nivrim.md) west of the river [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md), and [Aelin-uial](../A/Aelin-uial.md) south of the river [Aros](../A/Aros.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SSindar_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SSindar-2">[2]</a></sup>

The name of *Doriath* meant "Land of the Fence"<sup id="cite_ref-silmindex_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmindex-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-4">[4]</a></sup> or "Land of the Girdle".<sup id="cite_ref-SSindar_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SSindar-2">[2]</a></sup> Accordingly, the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) who dwelt inside Doriath were called *[Iathrim](../I/Iathrim.md)*, the "People of the Fence"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> (*[iâth](../I/Iâth.md)* meaning fence in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)).

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/S/Steamey_-_Making_the_Girdle_of_Melian.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/01/Steamey_-_Making_the_Girdle_of_Melian.jpg/300px-Steamey_-_Making_the_Girdle_of_Melian.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="239" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/01/Steamey_-_Making_the_Girdle_of_Melian.jpg/450px-Steamey_-_Making_the_Girdle_of_Melian.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/01/Steamey_-_Making_the_Girdle_of_Melian.jpg/600px-Steamey_-_Making_the_Girdle_of_Melian.jpg 2x" data-file-width="878" data-file-height="700"/></a><figcaption><i>Making the Girdle of Melian</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Steamey.md" title="Category:Images by Steamey">Steamey</a></figcaption></figure>

Upon the return of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) from [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) he sent his [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) in a great army to assail King Thingol. Although the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) defeated the eastern host of Orcs it was a costly victory (his ally, [Denethor](<../D/Denethor_(Nando).md>) of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md), was killed with all his kin) and the western force of Orcs were successful in driving [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) to the shores. Thingol therefore withdrew his people into Neldoreth and Region, and Melian used her power to encircle this dominion with an invisible wall of shadows and bewilderment. The land of Eglador was thereafter called Doriath, the guarded kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-SSindar_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SSindar-2">[2]</a></sup>

After years of outlawry in [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md), in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [464](../F/First_Age_464.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Beren](../B/Beren.md) passed through the Girdle of Melian, able to do so because (as Melian had foretold) his doom was greater than her power.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> After the [Quest for the Silmaril](../Q/Quest_for_the_Silmaril.md) Beren was in Doriath when the Girdle was again breached, this time by [Carcharoth](../C/Carcharoth.md) the Wolf, who penetrated the Girdle due to his fate and the power of the [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md) he had swallowed.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Upon the death of King Thingol in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [503](../F/First_Age_503.md),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Melian departed from [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) and with her departure the Girdle vanished.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name for "Girdle of Melian" was <i><b>Lest Melian</b></i>, first written by [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) as *List Melian*.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
