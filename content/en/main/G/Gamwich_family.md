The **Gamwich family** was a family of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) from the town of [Gamwich](/wiki/Gamwich) and then [Tighfield](/wiki/Tighfield). Through many generations, the family gave rise to the [Roper family](/wiki/Roper_family) and the [Gamgee family](/wiki/Gamgee_family) (which in turn would later give rise to the [Fairbairn family](/wiki/Fairbairn_family) and [Gardner family](/wiki/Gardner_family)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
