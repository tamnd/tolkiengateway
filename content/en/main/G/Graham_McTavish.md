**Graham McTavish** (born [4 January](/wiki/4_January) [1961](/wiki/1961)) is a Scottish actor who has appeared in a variety of supporting roles in many television programmes, as well as being a prolific voice actor. He portrayed [Dwalin](/wiki/Dwalin) in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
