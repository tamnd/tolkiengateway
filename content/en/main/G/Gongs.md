**Gongs** were evil beings, obscurely related to [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) that appeared within *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, the earliest version of the [Legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), [Melko](../M/Morgoth.md) created Gongs,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> in the image of [Men](../M/Men.md) and [Elves](../E/Elves.md) in order to deceive and betray Men.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

[Mîm](../M/Mîm.md) the [petty-dwarf](../P/Petty-dwarves.md) commanded a guard of Orcs and Gongs to protect the treasures of [Glorund](Glaurung.md) in [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md). They were slain by [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) and his companions.<sup id="cite_ref-Gongs_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gongs-4">[3]</a></sup>

Not long after, a group of Gongs sacked [Linwë's](../T/Thingol.md) [halls](../M/Menegroth.md), killing Linwë, stealing the cursed gold, and fleeing far away. However, they are attacked by [Beren Ermabwed](../B/Beren.md), forcing the gold and [Silmaril](../S/Silmarils.md) into the river [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md), though the Silmaril was eventually found washed up by the [Nauglath](../D/Dwarves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gongs_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gongs-4">[3]</a></sup>

During the [Faring Forth](../F/Faring_Forth.md), a group of Gongs, alongside Orcs, Dwarves, [Trolls](../T/Trolls.md), and evil [Men](../M/Men.md) were among the evil participants of the [Battle of Rôs](../B/Battle_of_Rôs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In his commentary, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) remarks that the Gnomish Lexicon defines a *Gong* as "one of a tribe of the Orcs, a goblin", but notes that *Gongs* might just be another name for *Kaukareldar*,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> an early [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word under the root <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=KAWA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KAWA (page does not exist)">KAWA</a></i> ("stoop") which has five derivations: <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kauka&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kauka (page does not exist)">kauka</a></i> ("crooked, bent, humped"), <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kauko&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kauko (page does not exist)">kauko</a></i> ("humpback"), <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kawin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kawin (page does not exist)">kawin</a></i> ("I bow"), <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kaur%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kaurë (page does not exist)">kaurë</a></i> ("fear"), and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kaur%C3%ABa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kaurëa (page does not exist)">kaurëa</a></i> ("timid").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> In the Quenya Lexicon, *[Eldar](../E/Eldar.md)*, the second element of *Kaukareldar*, is defined as "a beach-fay or [Solosimpë](../S/Solosimpi.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

The Gnomish Lexicon reportedly compares the word *Gongs* to *sithagong* ("dragonfly") derived from *sitha* ("[fly](../F/Flies.md)") and *Gong*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

There are no references to Gongs in the later [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).
