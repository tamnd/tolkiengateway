**Gongs** were evil beings, obscurely related to [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) that appeared within *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the earliest version of the [Legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad "Nirnaeth Arnoediad"), [Melko](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") created Gongs,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> in the image of [Men](/wiki/Men) and [Elves](/wiki/Elves) in order to deceive and betray Men.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

[Mîm](/wiki/M%C3%AEm) the [petty-dwarf](/wiki/Petty-dwarves "Petty-dwarves") commanded a guard of Orcs and Gongs to protect the treasures of [Glorund](/wiki/Glaurung "Glaurung") in [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond). They were slain by [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) and his companions.<sup id="cite_ref-Gongs_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gongs-4">[3]</a></sup>

Not long after, a group of Gongs sacked [Linwë's](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") [halls](/wiki/Menegroth "Menegroth"), killing Linwë, stealing the cursed gold, and fleeing far away. However, they are attacked by [Beren Ermabwed](/wiki/Beren "Beren"), forcing the gold and [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmarils "Silmarils") into the river [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion), though the Silmaril was eventually found washed up by the [Nauglath](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves").<sup id="cite_ref-Gongs_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gongs-4">[3]</a></sup>

During the [Faring Forth](/wiki/Faring_Forth), a group of Gongs, alongside Orcs, Dwarves, [Trolls](/wiki/Trolls), and evil [Men](/wiki/Men) were among the evil participants of the [Battle of Rôs](/wiki/Battle_of_R%C3%B4s).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In his commentary, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) remarks that the Gnomish Lexicon defines a *Gong* as "one of a tribe of the Orcs, a goblin", but notes that *Gongs* might just be another name for *Kaukareldar*,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> an early [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word under the root <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=KAWA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KAWA (page does not exist)">KAWA</a></i> ("stoop") which has five derivations: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kauka&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kauka (page does not exist)">kauka</a></i> ("crooked, bent, humped"), <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kauko&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kauko (page does not exist)">kauko</a></i> ("humpback"), <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kawin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kawin (page does not exist)">kawin</a></i> ("I bow"), <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kaur%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kaurë (page does not exist)">kaurë</a></i> ("fear"), and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kaur%C3%ABa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kaurëa (page does not exist)">kaurëa</a></i> ("timid").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> In the Quenya Lexicon, *[Eldar](/wiki/Eldar)*, the second element of *Kaukareldar*, is defined as "a beach-fay or [Solosimpë](/wiki/Solosimpi "Solosimpi")".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

The Gnomish Lexicon reportedly compares the word *Gongs* to *sithagong* ("dragonfly") derived from *sitha* ("[fly](/wiki/Flies "Flies")") and *Gong*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

There are no references to Gongs in the later [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").
