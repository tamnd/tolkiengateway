<i><b>gobel</b></i> is a noun meaning "walled (fortified) settlement" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word contains the element *[pel](/wiki/P%C3%AAl "Pêl")* "encircle".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

See also the collective prefix *[go-](/wiki/Go- "Go-")*.

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Amon Obel](/wiki/Amon_Obel) = "Hill of the Walled Settlement"
- [Rhosgobel](/wiki/Rhosgobel) = "Brownhay"
- [Tavrobel](/wiki/Tavrobel) = <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Haywood" title="wikipedia:Great Haywood">Great Haywood</a>
