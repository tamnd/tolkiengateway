**Gergely Nagy** ([March 6](../_/6_March.md), [1969](../_/1969.md)) is a Junior Assistant Professor at the Institute of English and American Studies at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Szeged" title="wikipedia:University of Szeged">University of Szeged</a>. He has taught courses on Tolkien and Plato (and the connections between the two), and is also a founding member and the academic vice president of the [Hungarian Tolkien Society](../H/Hungarian_Tolkien_Society.md).
