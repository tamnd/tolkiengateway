**Gergely Nagy** ([March 6](/wiki/6_March "6 March"), [1969](/wiki/1969)) is a Junior Assistant Professor at the Institute of English and American Studies at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Szeged" title="wikipedia:University of Szeged">University of Szeged</a>. He has taught courses on Tolkien and Plato (and the connections between the two), and is also a founding member and the academic vice president of the [Hungarian Tolkien Society](/wiki/Hungarian_Tolkien_Society).
