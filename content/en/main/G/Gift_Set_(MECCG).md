The **Gift Set** was a box set of [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md) products.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><b>Contains All You need for 2 Players:</b>
</p>
<ul><li><i><a href="../M/Middle-earth%253A_The_Wizards.md" title="Middle-earth: The Wizards">Middle-earth: The Wizards</a></i>™ <b>Unlimited Edition</b>
<ul><li>2 Starter Decks</li>
<li>4 Booster Packs</li></ul></li>
<li><i><a href="../M/Middle-earth%253A_The_Dragons.md" title="Middle-earth: The Dragons">Middle-earth: The Dragons</a></i>™ and <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth%253A_Dark_Minions.md" title="Middle-earth: Dark Minions">Middle-earth: Dark Minions</a></i>™ <b>Limited Edition</b>
<ul><li>2 Booster Packs from each set</li>
<li>2 Burning Eye Dice</li>
<li>1 Color Middle-earth Map with scoring track and two scoring markers,Hobbits</li>
<li>1 Sturdy Storage Box for card collectors.</li>
<li>1 Rules Book, Card Listings, and Tips Book.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>This box contains 272 randomly selected cards with a retail value of $43.50 (US). The cards pictured on the box are from the complete card series, and are not necessarily included in this box. This box can be used to sort and to store your card collection. The illustration shows how the box can be used to store additional cards. There is space for 1,000 cards.
</p>
</blockquote>

<br>

## In other countries {#In_other_countries}

The German <i><b>Basis Edition</b></i> had some similarities to both the *Gift Set* and *[Starter Set](../M/Middle-earth%253A_The_Wizards_Starter_Set.md)*.
