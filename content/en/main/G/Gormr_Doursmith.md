**Gormr Doursmith** is a character created for [The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md). He was the [Dourhand](../D/Dourhands.md) steward when the [Longbeards](../L/Longbeards.md) were absent in the [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md). He killed the guards of the Silver Deep Mines and poisoned their leader, [Olin](../O/Olin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When [Dáin](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md), [King under the Mountain](../K/King_under_the_Mountain.md), wanted a tribute of them he refused to give him anything.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Eventually he would make an alliance between him and the [Goblins](Goblins.md) of the Mirkstone Tunnels<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and revived [Skorgrím Dourhand](../S/Skorgrím_Dourhand.md) with the aid of Ivar Bloodhand. But he was slain by [Dwalin](../D/Dwalin.md) and the player shortly after the revival of Skorgrím.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
