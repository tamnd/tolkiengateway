## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>galad</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for "radiance", "glittering", or "reflection", usually connected to water, gems, or a smooth surface.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

Tolkien experimented with several possible etymologies of *galad*:

1. *galad* deriving from [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish) *galatā* ("radiance, radiate"), from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [KAL](/wiki/KAL).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
2. *galad* deriving from [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish) *ñaladā*, from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=%C3%91AL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ÑAL (page does not exist)">ÑGAL</a> ("gleam, sheen").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
3. *galad* deriving from [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) *ñalatā* ("radiance, glittering reflection"), from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=%C3%91AL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ÑAL (page does not exist)">ÑAL</a> ("shine by reflection").<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
