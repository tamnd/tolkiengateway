## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>galad</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word for "radiance", "glittering", or "reflection", usually connected to water, gems, or a smooth surface.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

Tolkien experimented with several possible etymologies of *galad*:

1. *galad* deriving from [Primitive Elvish](../P/Primitive_Elvish.md) *galatā* ("radiance, radiate"), from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [KAL](../K/KAL.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
2. *galad* deriving from [Primitive Elvish](../P/Primitive_Elvish.md) *ñaladā*, from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=%C3%91AL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ÑAL (page does not exist)">ÑGAL</a> ("gleam, sheen").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
3. *galad* deriving from [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) *ñalatā* ("radiance, glittering reflection"), from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=%C3%91AL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ÑAL (page does not exist)">ÑAL</a> ("shine by reflection").<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

## bSilvan {#bSilvan}

<i><b>galad</b></i> is a [Silvan Elvish](../S/Silvan_Elvish.md) word for "tree".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Noldorin {#Noldorin}

<i><b>galad</b></i> is a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) word meaning "light".<sup id="cite_ref-VT45_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT45-5">[5]</a></sup>
