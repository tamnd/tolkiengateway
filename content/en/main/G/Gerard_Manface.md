Sir **Gerard Manface** was a member of the [Notion Club](/wiki/Notion_Club).

## History {#History}

Sir Gerard Manface was educated at All Souls and became a lawyer by occupation. His main hobby was mountaineering and he was well traveled. He had many children, for whom he wrote many unpublished books and tales. He was a special friend of [Philip Frankley](/wiki/Philip_Frankley), though he was not resident in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford) and seldom appeared at the [Notion Club](/wiki/Notion_Club).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
