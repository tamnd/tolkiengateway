<i><b>gwathui</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word,<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup> likely meaning "shadowy" (cf. *[gwathren](/wiki/Gwathren "Gwathren")*).<sup id="cite_ref-Hiswe_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hiswe-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [WATH](/wiki/WATH)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Compounds {#Compounds}

- *[Gwathuirim](/wiki/Dunlendings "Dunlendings")* (*gwathui* + *[rim](/wiki/Rim "Rim")*)<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Hiswe_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hiswe-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- Sindarin *[gwathren](/wiki/Gwathren "Gwathren")*
- Sindarin *[gwath](/wiki/Gwath "Gwath")*
