The **Golden Perch** was an inn in the township of [Stock](/wiki/Stock), north of [Bucklebury](/wiki/Bucklebury) near the eastern borders of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It was said by [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") to serve the best beer in the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing).<sup id="cite_ref-i4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-i4-1">[1]</a></sup> It was probably favoured by anglers hence the name.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>

When the [Travellers](/wiki/Travellers) were leaving [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), on their way to [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow), [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") insisted on making a "shortcut" to the Golden Perch.<sup id="cite_ref-i4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-i4-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name refers to the fish <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perch" title="wikipedia:Perch">perch</a> (and not <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perch_(unit)" title="wikipedia:Perch (unit)">a land-measure</a> or <a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/perch#Noun_2" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">bird-perch</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>
