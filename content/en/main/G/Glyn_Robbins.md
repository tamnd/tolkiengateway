**Glyn Robbins** ([25 April](/wiki/25_April) [1940](/wiki/1940) - [2 January](/wiki/2_January) [2007](/wiki/2007)) was a British writer and actor. He wrote the screenplay for many stage adaptations of classic children’s books. His production of *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(2001_stage_production)> "The Hobbit (2001 stage production)")* was played between 28 November 2001 to 9 February 2002 at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen%27s_Theatre" title="wikipedia:Queen&#39;s Theatre">Queen&#39;s Theatre</a> in London.
