The **Gates of Morning** were situated somewhere in the [East of the World](/wiki/Uttermost_East "Uttermost East"). While it is said that the great [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") mariners, from "*their high prows*", glimpsed the Gates of Morning during their travels across the seas, nothing further is known about these Gates.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PM_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the mythology of *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)* appears the **Gates of Morn** or **Gates of East**, through which the [Sunship](/wiki/Sun "Sun") re-entered the World, after having travelled through the [Outer Dark](/wiki/Void "Void") (the Sun left the World through the [Door of Night](/wiki/Door_of_Night)).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

However, with the "[Sketch of the Mythology](/wiki/Sketch_of_the_Mythology)" [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") re-thought the astronomical foundations of his mythology,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and the Sun passes below the Earth. [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) has therefore remarked that the re-appearance of such a concept in many versions of the story of *[Akallabêth](/wiki/Akallab%C3%AAth)* is astonishing.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
