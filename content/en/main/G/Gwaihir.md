**Gwaihir**, also called the **Windlord**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the greatest of the [Great Eagles](/wiki/Great_Eagles) of northern [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth)<sup id="cite_ref-Council_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-2">[2]</a></sup> and the swiftest of the Great Eagles<sup id="cite_ref-Cormallen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cormallen-3">[3]</a></sup> at the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

Gwaihir was the mightiest of the descendants of [Thorondor](/wiki/Thorondor) and the brother of [Landroval](/wiki/Landroval).<sup id="cite_ref-Cormallen_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cormallen-3">[3]</a></sup> In the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), Gwaihir was the vassal of Thorondor and helped Thorondor and Landroval to rescue [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) from [Angband](/wiki/Angband).<sup id="cite_ref-P1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-4">[4]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Radagast](/wiki/Radagast) rode in the direction of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) where he had many old friends. The Eagles set off and gathered news: [wolves](/wiki/Wolves "Wolves") and [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) gathering and mustering, the movements of the [Nine Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl"), and the escape of [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) from Mirkwood.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-2">[2]</a></sup>

As instructed by Gandalf, Radagast sent Gwaihir to bear the news to him at [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), but Gandalf was at that time imprisoned at the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc). On an autumn night, Gandalf saw the Eagle flying towards him and so Gwaihir rescued the Wizard. But Gwaihir couldn't carry him far, so Gwaihir left him at [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) in order to find a steed.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-2">[2]</a></sup>

Later he was sent by [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) to find Gandalf; he found him on the peak of [Celebdil](/wiki/Celebdil) after the [Battle of the Peak](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Peak), and carried him to [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> After Gandalf recovered, he sent Gwaihir to watch the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) and gather tidings.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Gwaihir, [Landroval](/wiki/Landroval) and [Meneldor](/wiki/Meneldor) and other great eagles helped the [Free peoples](/wiki/Free_peoples) in the [Battle of the Morannon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon) by stooping down on the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) who flew on winged beasts. After the destruction of the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring) Gwaihir, Landroval, and Meneldor flew into [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) to search for Frodo and Sam and carried Frodo and Sam out of Mordor to safety.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><b>Gwaihir</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name,<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-8">[8]</a></sup> which probably means "Windlord",<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-9">[9]</a></sup> because he is often referred to as "Gwaihir the Windlord".<sup id="cite_ref-Council_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> The first element of the name is *[gwae(w)](/wiki/Gwaew "Gwaew")* ("wind").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-8">[8]</a></sup> It is probable that the second element is *[hîr](/wiki/H%C3%AEr "Hîr")* ("lord", "master").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Eagles named "Gwaihir" and "Landroval" (or, in even earlier texts, "Gwaewar" and "Lhandroval") appeared in an early version of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, as vassals of Thorondor who helped to bear [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) from [Angband](/wiki/Angband).<sup id="cite_ref-P1_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-4">[4]</a></sup> These references were removed by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) to escape the seeming discrepancy with *The Lord of the Rings*, although later he admitted that this omission was a mistake.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

In *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, Gwaihir carries Gandalf twice, and by the end of the book Gandalf says that Gwaihir had carried him twice.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> This can be interpreted to imply that Gwaihir never carried him before, but it is also possible that Gandalf only referred to the number of times that Gwaihir carried him in the recent past in the events that took place in *The Lord of the Rings*.

[Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) implies that Gwaihir is the [Lord of the Eagles](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Eagles) of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> although [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") never made a connection nor mentioned that Gwaihir was the Lord of the Eagles. [Douglas Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_Anderson)<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> and [Anders Stenström](/wiki/Anders_Stenstr%C3%B6m) (Beregond)<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> reject that Gwaihir is the Lord of Eagles of *The Hobbit*.

### Films {#Films}

<b>1971: <a href="/wiki/Sagan_om_Ringen_(1971_film)" title="Sagan om Ringen (1971 film)"><i>Sagan om Ringen</i> (1971 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a>, trapped on <a href="/wiki/Saruman" title="Saruman">Saruman</a>&#39;s <a href="/wiki/Orthanc" title="Orthanc">highest tower</a>, sends a message to the birds. Gwaihir, the swiftest of the Great <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Eagles" title="Eagles">Eagles</a>, answers his call and picks Gandalf up with his talons. Gandalf asks Gwaihir how far he will take him and Gwaihir tells him that he can go many more miles, though wizards are heavier than the beings he usually carries. Gandalf decides he needs to go to a place which raises <a href="/wiki/Horses" title="Horses">Horses</a> and settles on <a href="/wiki/Rohan" title="Rohan">Rohan</a>. Just before dawn, Gwaihir sets Gandalf down in Rohan.</dd></dl>

<b>1978: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> explains to <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a> that, while trapped on top of <a href="/wiki/Orthanc" title="Orthanc">Orthanc</a>, the Great Eagle Gwaihir answered his call and bore him away. Gandalf notes that &#34;<a href="/wiki/Saruman" title="Saruman">Saruman</a> has never paid enough attention to animals.&#34;</dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gandalf whispers &#34;Gwaihir&#34; repeatedly to a <a href="/wiki/Moths" title="Moths">moth</a> that arrives at <a href="/wiki/Isengard" title="Isengard">Isengard</a>. Later, Gwaihir rescues <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> from Isengard, and carries him to safety.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gwaihir and his Eagles come to aid at the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon" title="Battle of the Morannon">Battle of the Morannon</a>. They attack the Nazgûl and their <a href="/wiki/Fell_beasts" title="Fell beasts">fell beasts</a>. After the battle is won and the One Ring destroyed, Gwaihir along with <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> and a second Eagle rescue <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo Baggins</a> and <a href="/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee" title="Samwise Gamgee">Samwise Gamgee</a> from the rocks of <a href="/wiki/Mount_Doom" title="Mount Doom">Mount Doom</a>.</dd></dl>

### Video games {#Video_games}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gwaihir is first mentioned by <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> when the player meets up with him at <a href="/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony" title="The Prancing Pony">the Prancing Pony</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> The player first sees Gwaihir when entering Galadriel&#39;s garden in <a href="/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien" title="Lothlórien">Lothlórien</a> for the first time.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> Later, he arrives on the peak of Iorbar in the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a> to reclaim a stolen eagle egg taken by a giant. Finally, he arrives at and participates in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon" title="Battle of the Morannon">Battle of the Morannon</a> with the other eagles, and saves <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Frodo" title="Frodo">Frodo</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Sam" title="Sam">Sam</a> from the slopes of <a href="/wiki/Mount_Doom" title="Mount Doom">Mount Doom</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Gwaihir is first mentioned in chapter 1, in which <a href="/wiki/Beleram" title="Beleram">Beleram</a> tells that he has been sent by Gwaihir to gather news.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> Later on in the game, he makes a full appearance.</dd></dl>
