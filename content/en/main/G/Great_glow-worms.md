**Great glow-worms** often lurked about the borders of [Olórë Mallë](/wiki/Ol%C3%B3r%C3%AB_Mall%C3%AB), the Path of Dreams.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

As these creatures are mentioned only fleetingly, it is impossible to say if they were related to the great [Worms](/wiki/Worms) (wingless [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons)), or if they were giant versions of regular *[glowworms](/wiki/Glow-worms "Glow-worms")*. One can assume that they glowed in the dark (and therefore perhaps were related to, or were another name for, the [spark-dragons](/wiki/Spark-dragons "Spark-dragons")).
