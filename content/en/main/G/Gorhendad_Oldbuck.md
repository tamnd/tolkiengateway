**Gorhendad Oldbuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbit) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), the founder of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) and of the [Brandybuck](/wiki/Brandybuck_Family "Brandybuck Family") family.

## History {#History}

Gorhendad Oldbuck of the [Marish](/wiki/Marish) crossed the [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2340](/wiki/Third_Age_2340 "Third Age 2340")<sup id="cite_ref-TA2340_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2340-1">[1]</a></sup> ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [740](/wiki/Third_Age_2340 "Third Age 2340")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) and founded [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) and constructed [Brandy Hall](/wiki/Brandy_Hall).<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-3">[3]</a></sup> In honour of their new land, he changed his family name from Oldbuck ([H.](/wiki/Hobbitish "Hobbitish") *[Zaragamba](/wiki/Zaragamba)*<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-4">[4]</a></sup>) to [Brandybuck](/wiki/Brandybuck_Family "Brandybuck Family") (H. *[Brandagamba](/wiki/Brandagamba)*<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-4">[4]</a></sup>).<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-3">[3]</a></sup> He ruled the land as [Master of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland)<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-4">[4]</a></sup> and Master of the Hall, virtually a small independent country.<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-3">[3]</a></sup> After his death, the head of the Brandybuck family became the Master of Buckland<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and Master of the Hall.<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-3">[3]</a></sup> When he and his family moved from [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") to Buckland,<sup id="cite_ref-TA2340_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2340-1">[1]</a></sup> the office of [Thain](/wiki/Thain) passed from the Oldbuck family to the [Took Family](/wiki/Took_Family)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and [Isumbras Took I](/wiki/Isumbras_Took_I) became the first [Thain](/wiki/Thain) of the Tooks<sup id="cite_ref-TA2340_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2340-1">[1]</a></sup>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gorhendad* is the translation of [Westron](/wiki/Westron) *Ogmandab*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>. According to Stoor tradition, it used to mean "great-grandfather" or "ancestor".<sup id="cite_ref-granddad_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-granddad-8">[8]</a></sup>

In an earlier manuscript version the Westron name was *Ogforgad*.<sup id="cite_ref-granddad_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-granddad-8">[8]</a></sup>

J.R.R. Tolkien mentioned that he gave the name *Gorhendad* a "Celtic cast" and that the relation of Welsh or British to English was somewhat similar to the relation of the older language of the Stoors and Bree-men to Westron.<sup id="cite_ref-granddad_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-granddad-8">[8]</a></sup> He stated that *Gorhendad* is a "Welsh word meaning 'great-grandfather'".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
