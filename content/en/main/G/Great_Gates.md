The **Great Gates** was the original entrance to [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md), entering from [Dimrill Dale](../D/Dimrill_Dale.md) into the rock.

It was inscribed with [runic](../C/Cirth.md) spells of prohibition and exclusion in [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md). Over time commands were added that all should depart who did not have the leave of the [Lord of Moria](../L/Lord_of_Moria.md), written in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), the [Common Speech](../W/Westron.md), [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md), the [Language of Dale](../L/Language_of_Dale.md), and [Dunlendish](../D/Dunlendish.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Dwarves_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwarves-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/S/Steamey_-_Azog_and_Nár.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/2e/Steamey_-_Azog_and_N%C3%A1r.jpg/150px-Steamey_-_Azog_and_N%C3%A1r.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="150" height="201" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/2e/Steamey_-_Azog_and_N%C3%A1r.jpg/225px-Steamey_-_Azog_and_N%C3%A1r.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/2e/Steamey_-_Azog_and_N%C3%A1r.jpg/300px-Steamey_-_Azog_and_N%C3%A1r.jpg 2x" data-file-width="600" data-file-height="803"/></a><figcaption>Steamey - <i>Azog and Nár</i></figcaption></figure>

When first built near [Mirrormere](../M/Mirrormere.md), where <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../D/Durin.md" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a> saw his vision, the Gate was awe-inspiring. It remained the main (and perhaps) only entry to his Kingdom.

During the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) built a second entrance to Khazad-dûm, the [Doors of Durin](../D/Doors_of_Durin.md), at the western side of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) so as to facilitate access to [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and contact with the [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](Gwaith-i-Mírdain.md).

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2790](../T/Third_Age_2790.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-4">[3]</a></sup> [Thrór](../T/Thrór.md) and his companion [Nár](../N/Nár.md) went to [Moria](../M/Moria.md) to see the ancient halls of their ancestors. Despite the warnings of Nár, Thrór went through the Great Gates. He was captured by Orcs under the command of [Azog](../A/Azog.md), who beheaded him. Azog wrote his own name in [Dwarvish runes](../A/Angerthas_Moria.md) on Thrór's head and threw it outside the gate for Nár to retrieve. He let Nár go to tell all the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) that an [Orc](../O/Orcs.md) ruled [Moria](../M/Moria.md) now, and so Nár did, which started the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-5">[4]</a></sup>

In [2799](../T/Third_Age_2799.md), the War came to its climax with the [Battle of Azanulbizar](../B/Battle_of_Azanulbizar.md),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-4">[3]</a></sup> which was the largest battle of the war. During this battle the doors of the Gate were destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-Dwarves_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwarves-1">[1]</a></sup> After the battle [Dáin II Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md), the victor, would not allow his people to reclaim it, for he had looked through the open Gate, and knew that the [Balrog](../B/Balrogs.md) lurked there still.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-5">[4]</a></sup>

When [Balin's Colony](../B/Balin's_Colony~64118.md) was attacked in [2994](../T/Third_Age_2994.md), it was heavily damaged.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-6">[5]</a></sup>

On [15 January](../_/15_January.md), [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md), after the loss of [Gandalf](Gandalf.md), exited Moria through its eastern access, still broken.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> It was not heavily guarded.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-6">[5]</a></sup> Prior to that, both Gandalf and [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) had passed through it.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>
