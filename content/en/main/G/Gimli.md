**Gimli** was a [dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) of the [House of Durin](../H/House_of_Durin.md) who became famous as the only Dwarven member of the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md).

## History {#History}

Gimli was born in the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) during a time of exile for [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md), about [a century](../T/Third_Age_2770.md) after the tragic destruction of [Dale](../D/Dale.md) and the [Sacking](../S/Sack_of_Erebor.md) of [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) by [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) the Golden. His father was [Glóin](Glóin.md), a direct descendant of [King](../K/King_of_Durin's_Folk~7047.md) [Náin II](../N/Náin_II.md) and, through him, of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../D/Durin.md" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-appdurin_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appdurin-5">[5]</a></sup> His father joined [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) in their [quest](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md) to reclaim the [Kingdom under the Mountain](../K/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain.md), but Gimli was forbidden because he was too young: only 62 at the time.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The Dwarves were successful in their Quest and Gimli and his father were among the Dwarves who returned to Erebor. Years later, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3017](../T/Third_Age_3017.md), Gimli witnessed the arrival of a messenger of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) (possibly a [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md)) inquiring about [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md), old acquaintance of his father, and the [Ring of Power](../O/One_Ring.md), promising tempting offers. Gimli accompanied his troubled father to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) for the dual purpose of warning Bilbo and seeking the counsel of [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) on such weighty matters. He attended the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md) as a representative of the [Dwarves of Erebor](../D/Dwarves_of_Erebor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

### The Fellowship of the Ring {#The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring}

Gimli, as the only young dwarf seen at the council of Elrond, was appointed as a member of the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md). He alone of the company wore a mail-shirt, and carried a broad-bladed axe. He quickly distinguished himself in the company by declaring that he "\[needed\] no map" and naming the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) individually by their [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md) names, inspiring the comment from [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md): "A fair jaw-cracker dwarf-language must be!"<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Anke_Eißmann_-_Balin&#39;s_Tomb.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/c8/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Balin%27s_Tomb.jpg/300px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Balin%27s_Tomb.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="173" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/c8/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Balin%27s_Tomb.jpg/450px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Balin%27s_Tomb.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/c8/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Balin%27s_Tomb.jpg/600px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Balin%27s_Tomb.jpg 2x" data-file-width="837" data-file-height="484"/></a><figcaption><i>Balin&#39;s Tomb</i> by <a href="../A/Anke_Eißmann.md" title="Anke Eißmann">Anke Eißmann</a></figcaption></figure>

Gimli stood more than all the others with [Gandalf](Gandalf.md) on the matter of passing through [Moria](../M/Moria.md). This may be influenced by his curiosity about the fate of his cousin [Balin](../B/Balin.md), who went thither to [refound](../B/Balin's_folk.md) the ancient kingdom of the [Longbeards](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md), and also his vengeful nature. His first clash with [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) occurred before the [Doors of Durin](../D/Doors_of_Durin.md), in a brief dispute over whose fault it was (the Elves’ or the Dwarves’) that the friendship between the two races waned.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-4">[4]</a></sup> Gandalf quickly intervened, though it was not until [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) that they would truly become friends.

He was quite helpful to Gandalf in explaining the Doors to the other curious members of the fellowship. Gandalf showed his appreciation of Gimli’s skills by letting him walk up front through the dark tunnels beside himself, and taking brief counsels with him when the way was unsure. It is probable that here Gandalf was tapping into Dwarven inborn or developed skills rather than actual knowledge of Moria itself, as Gimli had never personally been there.

Gimli reacted most indignantly to Sam’s suggestion that Moria was but "holes", going so far as to chant for him an ancient [song](../T/The_world_was_young,_the_mountains_green,.md). Gimli continued to be helpful to Gandalf on the rest of the dark passage, and boldly aided in the [Battle of the Chamber of Mazarbul](../B/Battle_of_the_Chamber_of_Mazarbul.md), protecting Balin’s tomb. After Gandalf remained behind to halt the Balrog, Gimli led the others across the [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](../B/Bridge_of_Khazad-dûm.md).

He again showed great emotion when they neared [Kheled-zâram](../K/Kheled-zâram.md), taking [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) alone out of all the company with him to see it (though Sam followed as well).<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-8">[8]</a></sup> This hinted at a degree of affection for Frodo, though it may merely have been because he was the most important of the company. Despite his helpfulness, at [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) he became the greatest hindrance to the Company, when he alone was required to be blindfolded and of all of them liked the idea the least (save for perhaps Legolas). The compromise was made that the entire company would go through the Golden Wood in this manner, that Gimli would not suffer alone.<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-8">[8]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/B/Brian_Durfee_-_Three_Golden_Hairs.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/f/fe/Brian_Durfee_-_Three_Golden_Hairs.png/250px-Brian_Durfee_-_Three_Golden_Hairs.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="211" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/f/fe/Brian_Durfee_-_Three_Golden_Hairs.png/375px-Brian_Durfee_-_Three_Golden_Hairs.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/f/fe/Brian_Durfee_-_Three_Golden_Hairs.png 2x" data-file-width="398" data-file-height="336"/></a><figcaption>&#34;Three <a href="../H/Hair_of_Galadriel.md" title="Hair of Galadriel">Golden Hairs</a>&#34; by <a href="../B/Brian_Durfee.md" title="Brian Durfee">Brian Durfee</a></figcaption></figure>

Despite this early struggle, it was Gimli of all the company that was most remembered for his deeds in Lothlórien. For his heart softened towards [Galadriel](Galadriel.md) and as a result toward Elves in general when that great lady sympathised with his sorrows, using the traditional Khuzdul names.<sup id="cite_ref-Mirror_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mirror-9">[9]</a></sup> In return he attempted to compliment her, as he saw her love and understanding. Though slightly clumsy in his first attempt, he distinguished himself when, as Galadriel gave the Company [gifts](Gifts_of_Galadriel.md), he asked only for a single strand of her hair. Galadriel gave him three hairs with a blessing, probably remembering when [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) had requested a similar gift several millennia ago. She later referred to him as "[Lockbearer](../L/Lockbearer.md)". Gimli wept openly at the departure from Lothlórien, calling the light more dangerous than the darkness he had feared.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

By the time the Fellowship departed from Lothlórien, Gimli had become fast friends with Legolas the Elf, a result of Galadriel’s kindness. This, too, made him famous among all the Dwarves of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).

### The Three Hunters {#The_Three_Hunters}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="../T/Three_Hunters.md" title="Three Hunters">Three Hunters</a></b></div></div>

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_Pursuit_in_Rohan.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/1a/Ted_Nasmith_-_Pursuit_in_Rohan.jpg/200px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Pursuit_in_Rohan.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="119" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/1a/Ted_Nasmith_-_Pursuit_in_Rohan.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Pursuit_in_Rohan.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/1a/Ted_Nasmith_-_Pursuit_in_Rohan.jpg/400px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Pursuit_in_Rohan.jpg 2x" data-file-width="884" data-file-height="526"/></a><figcaption><i>Pursuit in Rohan</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

After the [Breaking of the Fellowship](../B/Breaking_of_the_Fellowship.md), during which he and Legolas slew many [orcs](../O/Orcs.md), Gimli listened in silence as his only remaining comrades, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and Legolas, sang a [lament](../S/Song_of_Boromir.md) for Boromir in the character of three of the Winds. Only the East Wind was left, and Gimli would not sing it, which Aragorn declared to be appropriate.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

For most of the chase he served as a commentator, usually leaving the decisions to Aragorn. His reaction was quite hostile to the suspicious [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md), especially after [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md)'s rash comments about Galadriel. Little did either know that they would become close friends in the times to come as fiery words passed between them. After the [Three Hunters](../T/Three_Hunters.md) were lent horses, Gimli and Éomer parted in peace, with promises to one another of reconciliation.

Of the Three Hunters, Gimli seemed the most shaken by [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md) while they searched for traces of [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md). Gimli felt no reluctance to shoot the “unarmed” old man whom they thought was [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md), unlike Aragorn and Legolas. After Gandalf was revealed to them, Gimli fell to his knees. Gandalf put his hand on Gimli’s head, and the dwarf laughed for the first time recorded during the [Quest of the Ring](../Q/Quest_of_the_Ring.md).

Gimli was delighted by the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) and [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md), declaring "This country has tough bones". He declared that if he had a hundred of his kin he could make the fortress invincible.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Glittering_Caves_of_Aglarond.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/33/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Glittering_Caves_of_Aglarond.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Glittering_Caves_of_Aglarond.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="373" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/33/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Glittering_Caves_of_Aglarond.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Glittering_Caves_of_Aglarond.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/33/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Glittering_Caves_of_Aglarond.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Glittering_Caves_of_Aglarond.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1288" data-file-height="1600"/></a><figcaption><i>The Glittering Caves of Aglarond</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

During the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md), Gimli saved Éomer’s life outside the gates. He came in, boasting of his first two orc-kills to his friend Legolas. Legolas estimated his slaughter to amount to twenty, starting the good-hearted orc-killing game that continued through the rest of the battle. Gimli was among the first to the culvert when the orcs crept through, leaping heroically into their midst from the walls. He then proceeded to lead the blocking up of the culvert.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> During the course of the battle, Gimli was one of those forced into the [Glittering Caves](Glittering_Caves.md). He was astonished to see the magnificence of these caverns, moving even Legolas with his glowing description of them.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> This one sight would change his life.

His final kill score number was forty-two in that battle, surpassing his friend the elf by one.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> Gimli shows a hint of humour and affection when he greets the two [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) Merry and Pippin comfortably situated among <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/flotsam" title="wikipedia:flotsam">flotsam</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/jetsam" title="wikipedia:jetsam">jetsam</a> in the ruin of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> He declared himself deep in Pippin’s debt when the hobbit lent him his spare pipe.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

Gimli shows some of the stone hardness of Dwarves when they meet [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) at [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md). Saruman uses the power in his voice to sway [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) to forgive him and make peace, and many of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) are moved by his magic and are spell-bound. Unmoved, Gimli comments that Saruman's words cannot be trusted and that from Orthanc, help would mean ruin. Saruman is angered enough by Gimli that for a moment he loses his charm.

As the fellowship of friends again began to break up – Gandalf and Pippin heading for [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) and his riders for [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md) – Gimli out of love and respect for Aragorn went with him, Legolas, the sons of Elrond, and the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) [Rangers](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) on the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md). He showed great reluctance before the [Dark Door](../D/Dark_Door.md), the final thought that drove him downward was the thought of being bested by an Elf underground.

He participated in the epic [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), returning with Aragorn and his other companions on the ships of the [Corsairs of Umbar](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md), and later recounted their journey to the fascinated Hobbits. He began to fear for Legolas, who seemed entranced by the Sea. His position on Elves completely changed, he remarked "If all the fair folk take to the Havens, it will be a duller world for those who are doomed to stay".<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> He was much more sceptical as to the overall end of [Men](../M/Men.md) than his comrade.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

Gimli fought in the [Battle of the Morannon](../B/Battle_of_the_Morannon.md), passing through unscathed, and finding the alive but bruised Pippin among the dead lying on the hill. He returned to the [Field of Cormallen](../F/Field_of_Cormallen.md) to be reunited with his friends, and there displayed open affection for all of them, even Pippin ("If only because of the pains you have cost me, which I shall never forget").<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

### After the War of the Ring {#After_the_War_of_the_Ring}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_Reach_the_Shores_of_Valinor.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5f/Ted_Nasmith_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_Reach_the_Shores_of_Valinor.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_Reach_the_Shores_of_Valinor.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="207" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5f/Ted_Nasmith_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_Reach_the_Shores_of_Valinor.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_Reach_the_Shores_of_Valinor.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5f/Ted_Nasmith_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_Reach_the_Shores_of_Valinor.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_Reach_the_Shores_of_Valinor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1196" data-file-height="826"/></a><figcaption><i>Gimli and Legolas reach the shores of Valinor</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

After the coronation of Aragorn, Gimli said farewell to his old friend Éomer, who begged for pardon as to his words on Galadriel, though he added that he still didn’t think she was the fairest in the world. When he added that he considered [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) the fairest instead, Gimli was content with this.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

During the return journey he visited many places with Legolas, including Fangorn Forest and [Aglarond](Glittering_Caves.md). Eventually he returned to Erebor, to find it nearly devastated by war. [Dáin II Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) had died, and [Thorin III Stonehelm](../T/Thorin_Stonehelm.md) was now King. Eventually, though, he returned to the Glittering Caves with a contingent of dwarves, becoming the "[Lord of the Glittering Caves](../L/Lord_of_the_Glittering_Caves.md)". He rebuilt the gates of Minas Tirith with *[mithril](../M/Mithril.md)* and steel, and in both [Gondor](Gondor.md) and [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) accomplished great works. There he met his friends Merry and Pippin and the [history of his people](../A/Appendix_A.md) made its way to the [Red Book](../R/Red_Book.md);<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> he also set out the genealogical tree of the [Dwarves of Erebor](../D/Dwarves_of_Erebor.md) for King Elessar.<sup id="cite_ref-appdurin_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appdurin-5">[5]</a></sup>

In Aglarond, it is assumed, he lived on until he was old, and in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md) he sailed with his friend Legolas across [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md) to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), becoming the first and only Dwarf to do so.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup>

In [1978](../_/1978.md), [Jim Allan](../J/Jim_Allan.md) noted that the name *Gimli* appears once in the *[Völuspá](../V/Völuspá.md)* and three times in the *Prose Edda*. Allan suggested that *Gimli* means "One-of-Gimlé" or "One-of-Gimill", where <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giml%C3%A9" title="wikipedia:Gimlé"><i>Gimlé</i>/<i>Gimill</i></a> in the *Eddas* is a hall of the after-life "*inhabited solely by [Light-elves](../C/Calaquendi.md#Inspiration)*" (evoking Gimli's leaving for [Aman](../A/Aman.md)). He also noted that the nominative form *Gimlé* of the dative form *Gimli* consists of the elements *gim* ("fire") and *hlé* ("shelter", "lee").<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup>

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote in a draft letter after he had been questioned about the name *Gimli* that the poetic archaic [Old Norse](../O/Old_Norse.md) word *gim* seems to have meant "fire".<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name "[Gimli](<Gimli_(elf).md>)" was first used by Tolkien for the name of a [Gnome](Gnome.md) in the *[Tale of Tinúviel](../T/Tale_of_Tinúviel.md)*, written in mid-1910s.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup>

In the [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) language described in *[The Notion Club Papers](../T/The_Notion_Club_Papers.md)* ([1945](../_/1945.md)), *gimli* (pl. *gimil*) is the word for "[star](../S/Stars.md)", derived from a [root](../R/Root.md) G-M-L and having the Characteristic Vowel I. From these derive others words such as *igmil* "a star-shaped formation"; and *Gimilnitîr* "Star-kindler" ([Gilthoniel](Gilthoniel.md)), a name for [Varda](../V/Varda.md).<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[26]</a></sup>

<b>1955: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1955 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The part of Gimli was read by <a href="../E/Eric_Lugg.md" title="Eric Lugg">Eric Lugg</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>1971: <a href="../S/Sagan_om_Ringen_(1971_film).md" title="Sagan om Ringen (1971 film)"><i>Sagan om Ringen</i> (1971 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gimli is chosen as the <a href="../D/Dwarves.md" title="Dwarves">Dwarven</a> representative of the <a href="../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="Fellowship of the Ring">Fellowship of the Ring</a>. He departs second-last, in front of <a href="../L/Legolas.md" title="Legolas">Legolas</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>1978: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gimli is portrayed as almost as tall as <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> and <a href="../L/Legolas.md" title="Legolas">Legolas</a>. He has a brown beard and wears a hood. <a href="../D/David_Buck.md" title="David Buck">David Buck</a> provided Gimli&#39;s voice.</dd></dl>

<b>1979: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1979 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1979 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The voice of Gimli is provided by <a href="Gail_Chugg.md" title="Gail Chugg">Gail Chugg</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>1980: <a href="../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)"><i>The Return of the King</i> (1980 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Both Gimli and Legolas are omitted from this adaptation, as it centres on Aragorn.</dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gimli is played by <a href="../D/Douglas_Livingstone.md" title="Douglas Livingstone">Douglas Livingstone</a>, who uses a heavily articulated accent. He travels to Rivendell without his father, who is spoken of as the one to whom the messenger of Mordor came rather than Dain.</dd></dl>

<b>2001-2003: <a class="mw-redirect" href="../P/Pán_prsteňov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series).md" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The voice of Gimli is provided by Vladimír &#34;Ady&#34; Hajdu. Hajdu portrays Gimli&#39;s voice as less gruff than most other adaptations, and though he is a fierce warrior, he is sensitive and insightful and often fairly cheerful. At one point in the series, when his companions in the Fellowship ask him if he heard a suspicious noise, he exclaims &#34;I only hear the whisper of stones and mosses&#34; (a possible reference to Tolkien&#39;s Dwarves being attuned to stone and the mountains as much as Elves are to the woods and sea).</dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="../J/John_Rhys-Davies.md" title="John Rhys-Davies">John Rhys-Davies</a> portrayed Gimli in voice and close-ups; Brett Beattie was his size-double. Jackson used the antagonism between Legolas and Gimli, although he placed most of it on Gimli&#39;s plate.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game).md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gimli is portrayed as a burly lumberjack, slightly smaller than Legolas, but the same size as Boromir. His moment of glory is in <a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Moria</a>, where he aids the player (at this point, Frodo) in trying to find the Bridge. He is voiced by <a href="../J/James_Horan.md" title="James Horan">James Horan</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Now that <a href="../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a> and <a href="../P/Peregrin_Took.md" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a> were no longer around to provide comic relief, Gimli was chosen to fill the spot. This has led to much criticism.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(video_game).md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gimli is one of the three playable characters.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>As in the second film Gimli&#39;s character was somewhat controversially used to insert comic relief into the scenes he appeared in.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(video_game).md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gimli is a playable character, with an aggressive fighting style. He and Legolas accompany Aragorn through Paths of the Dead, later he fights on Pelennor fields and at the Black Gate.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring">Sierra&#39;s War of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="../B/Bob_Papenbrook.md" title="Bob Papenbrook">Bob Papenbrook</a> provided the voice of Gimli, who is a Hero Unit for the Free People side. His first appearance is near the Lonely Mountain, where he and other dwarves fight orcs and later activate a giant catapult.</dd></dl>

<b>2004: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gimil is a Hero Unit for the Rohan faction in skirmishes, though he accompanies the Fellowship in the storyline mode.</dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gimli is now the Hero for the Dwarven faction.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gimli is first met by Dwarven characters in the intro taking place in <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_2941.md" title="Third Age 2941">2941</a>, shortly before the Quest for Erebor. Later, all characters meet him in Rivendell where Gimli accompanies them on a mission to the <a href="../M/Misty_Mountains.md" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a>. The player catches up with Gimli at several points during the Fellowship&#39;s journey and also fights alongside Gimli in the <a href="Glittering_Caves.md" title="Glittering Caves">Glittering Caves</a> during the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../B/Battle_of_Helm&#39;s_Deep.md" title="Battle of Helm&#39;s Deep">Battle of Helm&#39;s Deep</a>, the <a href="../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a> and in Northern <a href="../I/Ithilien.md" title="Ithilien">Ithilien</a> during the <a href="../A/Army_of_the_West.md" title="Army of the West">Army of the West</a>&#39;s march towards the <a href="../B/Black_Gate.md" title="Black Gate">Black Gate</a>. After the downfall of <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>, Gimli and Legolas join the <a href="Gondor.md" title="Gondor">Gondorian</a> army in exploring and securing the Land of Shadow. There, Gimli finds a dwarven clan from the East who have been used as skilled slaves by the Dark Lord for centuries. He leads the survivors to <a class="mw-redirect" href="../E/Erebor.md" title="Erebor">Erebor</a> and secures King <a href="../T/Thorin_Stonehelm.md" title="Thorin Stonehelm">Thorin Stonehelm</a>&#39;s permission for them to reside in his halls, before returning south to <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> for the wedding of Aragorn and <a href="../A/Arwen.md" title="Arwen">Arwen</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2009: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Conquest.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: Conquest">The Lord of the Rings: Conquest</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gimli is a playable hero available in several missions.</dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gimli can be found and talked to in Rivendell, he retains the appearance from the movies. While he provides insight into many events, interactions with him do not affect the main plot.</dd></dl>

<b>2013: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>When Thorin&#39;s Company are arrested in <a href="../M/Mirkwood.md" title="Mirkwood">Mirkwood</a>, Legolas searches Glóin&#39;s possessions and finds a picture wallet. Legolas comments how ugly the portraits are and Glóin points out that it&#39;s his son&#39;s portrait, parodying the future friendship of the two.</dd></dl>

<b>2023: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Return_to_Moria.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria">The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>During the <a href="../F/Fourth_Age.md" title="Fourth Age">Fourth Age</a>, Gimli summons a company of Dwarves and tasks them with reclaiming the lost spoils from the Dwarven homeland of <a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Moria</a>.</dd></dl>
