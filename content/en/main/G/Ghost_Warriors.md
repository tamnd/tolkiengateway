<i><b>Ghost Warriors</b></i> is an adventure module for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*, 1st Edition.

## Cover/Jacket Text {#Cover/Jacket_Text}

Dunland, or Dunfearan, as the region is called by its inhabitants, lies among the rugged western foothills of the southern Misty Mountains. The Great Plague swept these highlands in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636"), but now a worse evil scourges the Dunmen. Ghost Warriors roam by night, taking captives to swell their Undead ranks. The key to their defeat lies beyond Riddle Caves, the mysterious entrance to a dark labyrinth that twists through the roots of the mountains.

GHOST WARRIORS includes:

- 11" x 17" FULL-COLOR MAP depicting the terrain of Dunland.
- 3 EXCITING ADVENTURES involving a clash with Dunnish Wights, a battle against a powerful shaman, and a risky venture into the [Under-deeps](/wiki/Under-deeps) that sprawl beneath the mountains.
- DETAILED LAYOUTS of adventures sites including the Riddle Caves, the Path of Riddles, and the Ghost-caves.
- GAME STATISTICS of the Beasts, Undead, and NPCs that dwell in the hills of Dunfearan.
- RANDOM CAVERN GENERATION SYSTEM provided for travel through the Under-deeps, the vast network of caverns that twists below the surface of Endor.
