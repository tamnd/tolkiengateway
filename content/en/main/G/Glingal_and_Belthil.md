**Glingal** and **Belthil** were two trees made by [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) in the image of the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor).

## History {#History}

After building [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) upon [Amon Gwareth](/wiki/Amon_Gwareth), the [Gondolindrim](/wiki/Gondolindrim) never ceased to work on embellishing the city. [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) himself wrought two images of the Trees of old. These stood in his court, "and the Tree which he made of gold was named *Glingal*, and the Tree whose flowers he made of silver was named *Belthil*".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is also said that they shone with their own light, which filled all the ways of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Both names are [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin):

- *Glingal* means "Hanging Flame",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> from *gling-* ([N](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin"). "hang") and [root](/wiki/Root "Root") [KAL](/wiki/KAL)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) additionally suggests the translation "gleaming light".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

- *Belthil* means "Divine Radiance",<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> from the roots [BAL](/wiki/BAL) and [SIL](/wiki/SIL)/<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=THIL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="THIL (page does not exist)">THIL</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)* they were called <i><b>Glingol</b></i> and <i><b>Bansil</b></i>, and they were not wrought with elven-craft, but were real trees, surviving shoots of the Two Trees destroyed by [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor). They stood in the [Square of the King](/wiki/Square_of_the_King), on either side of the doors of the palace.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:160</sup> During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), the surviving [houses of the Gondothlim](/wiki/Twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim "Twelve houses of the Gondothlim") barricaded themselves in the Square, and there was a rally of men beneath the two trees to hear King Turgon. There he proclaimed the fall of Gondolin, and refusing to fight against [Doom](/wiki/Doom_of_Mandos "Doom of Mandos"), he cast his crown at the roots of Glingol. Later, when the Square was burned down by the enemy, "Glingol was withered to the stock and Bansil was blackened utterly".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:184–6</sup>
