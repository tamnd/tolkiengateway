**Glingal** and **Belthil** were two trees made by [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) in the image of the [Two Trees of Valinor](../T/Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md).

## History {#History}

After building [Gondolin](Gondolin.md) upon [Amon Gwareth](../A/Amon_Gwareth.md), the [Gondolindrim](Gondolindrim.md) never ceased to work on embellishing the city. [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) himself wrought two images of the Trees of old. These stood in his court, "and the Tree which he made of gold was named *Glingal*, and the Tree whose flowers he made of silver was named *Belthil*".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is also said that they shone with their own light, which filled all the ways of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Both names are [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md):

- *Glingal* means "Hanging Flame",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> from *gling-* ([N](../N/Noldorin.md). "hang") and [root](../R/Root.md) [KAL](../K/KAL.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md) additionally suggests the translation "gleaming light".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

- *Belthil* means "Divine Radiance",<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> from the roots [BAL](../B/BAL~31327.md) and [SIL](../S/SIL~59984.md)/<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=THIL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="THIL (page does not exist)">THIL</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)* they were called <i><b>Glingol</b></i> and <i><b>Bansil</b></i>, and they were not wrought with elven-craft, but were real trees, surviving shoots of the Two Trees destroyed by [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md). They stood in the [Square of the King](../S/Square_of_the_King.md), on either side of the doors of the palace.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:160</sup> During the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), the surviving [houses of the Gondothlim](../T/Twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim.md) barricaded themselves in the Square, and there was a rally of men beneath the two trees to hear King Turgon. There he proclaimed the fall of Gondolin, and refusing to fight against [Doom](../D/Doom_of_Mandos.md), he cast his crown at the roots of Glingol. Later, when the Square was burned down by the enemy, "Glingol was withered to the stock and Bansil was blackened utterly".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:184–6</sup>
