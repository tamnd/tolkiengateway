**Gene Wolfe** (born [May 7](../_/7_May.md), [1931](../_/1931.md), died [April 14](../_/14_April.md), [2019](../_/2019.md)) was an American author who primarily wrote fantasy fiction.

Wolfe corresponded with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in the 1960s (see [Letter 290a](../L/Letter_290a.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Tolkien appears to have read his short story, 'Trip, Trap', noting in [Letter 297](../L/Letter_297.md) that the word 'warg' (on whose etymology Wolfe had consulted him) appeared in *Orbit 2*, p. 119, in a story by 'a reader of the L.R.'
