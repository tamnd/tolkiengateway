**Gladden** may refer to:

- [Gladden River](/wiki/Gladden_River), a tributary of the River [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) to the west of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).
- The [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields), marshlands which neighboured the Gladden River.
    - [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields), in which [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) was slain and the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring) was lost.
- [Iris](/wiki/Iris), the flowers that grew near the aforementioned locations, and are referred to as "gladden".
