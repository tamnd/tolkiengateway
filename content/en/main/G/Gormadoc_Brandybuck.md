**Gormadoc "Deepdelver" Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Gormadoc was named Deepdelver in his lifetime. He married [Malva Headstrong](/wiki/Malva_Headstrong), and had three children: [Madoc](/wiki/Madoc_Brandybuck "Madoc Brandybuck"), [Sadoc](/wiki/Sadoc_Brandybuck "Sadoc Brandybuck") and [Marroc](/wiki/Marroc_Brandybuck "Marroc Brandybuck").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is possible that during his office he enlarged [Brandy Hall](/wiki/Brandy_Hall).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Gormadoc* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) suggests that it contains the element *gor*, which means "over" in [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh) as a prefix to [Madoc](/wiki/Madoc_Brandybuck "Madoc Brandybuck"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the first name of Gormadoc Brandybuck's son.
