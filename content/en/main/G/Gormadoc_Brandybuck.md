**Gormadoc "Deepdelver" Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Gormadoc was named Deepdelver in his lifetime. He married [Malva Headstrong](../M/Malva_Headstrong.md), and had three children: [Madoc](../M/Madoc_Brandybuck.md), [Sadoc](../S/Sadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Marroc](../M/Marroc_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is possible that during his office he enlarged [Brandy Hall](../B/Brandy_Hall.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Gormadoc* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](../C/Celtic.md) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](../J/Jim_Allan.md) suggests that it contains the element *gor*, which means "over" in [Welsh](../W/Welsh.md) as a prefix to [Madoc](../M/Madoc_Brandybuck.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the first name of Gormadoc Brandybuck's son.
