**Galathil** was a grandson of King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md)'s brother [Elmo](../E/Elmo.md), and himself the brother of [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md), the [Lord of Lórien](../L/Lord_of_Lórien.md). His daughter [Nimloth](../N/Nimloth_of_Doriath.md) wedded [Dior](../D/Dior.md), Thingol's Heir, and so Galathil was a distant ancestor of both [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) and [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Galathil and his close kin shared names with the <a class="mw-disambig" href="../W/White_Tree_(disambiguation).md" title="White Tree (disambiguation)">White Trees</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PMV_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMV-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:147–8</sup>

The conception of this name was based on Tolkien's idea that Celeborn's name was first devised to mean "Silver Tree", and his close kin all had "tree-names". Although he later changed what he wanted Celeborn's name to mean, referring to "silver" and "tall" from the adjective *ornâ* ("uprising, tall") instead of "silver" and "tree" from the noun *ornê* ("tree"), he did not revise the name meanings of his close kin.<sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-2">[2]</a></sup>

Based on Tolkien's original version of the family's names, *Galathil* is a name that can be seen in the tree name *Galathilion*, which was another name for [Telperion](../T/Telperion.md). This was also the name of the White Tree of [Tirion](../T/Tirion.md), '[Galathilion](Galathilion.md) the Less', and its seedlings were *Celeborn* in [Eressëa](../E/Eressëa.md) and *Nimloth* in [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PMV_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMV-3">[3]</a></sup> In *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, Galathilion was the tree of Tol Eressëa, and [Nimloth](<../N/Nimloth_(tree).md>) was its seedling.<sup id="cite_ref-PMV_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMV-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:163–4</sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
