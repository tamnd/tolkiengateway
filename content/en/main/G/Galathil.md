**Galathil** was a grandson of King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol)'s brother [Elmo](/wiki/Elmo), and himself the brother of [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), the [Lord of Lórien](/wiki/Lord_of_L%C3%B3rien). His daughter [Nimloth](/wiki/Nimloth_of_Doriath "Nimloth of Doriath") wedded [Dior](/wiki/Dior), Thingol's Heir, and so Galathil was a distant ancestor of both [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Galathil and his close kin shared names with the <a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/White_Tree_(disambiguation)" title="White Tree (disambiguation)">White Trees</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PMV_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMV-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:147–8</sup>

The conception of this name was based on Tolkien's idea that Celeborn's name was first devised to mean "Silver Tree", and his close kin all had "tree-names". Although he later changed what he wanted Celeborn's name to mean, referring to "silver" and "tall" from the adjective *ornâ* ("uprising, tall") instead of "silver" and "tree" from the noun *ornê* ("tree"), he did not revise the name meanings of his close kin.<sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-2">[2]</a></sup>

Based on Tolkien's original version of the family's names, *Galathil* is a name that can be seen in the tree name *Galathilion*, which was another name for [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion). This was also the name of the White Tree of [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion), '[Galathilion](/wiki/Galathilion) the Less', and its seedlings were *Celeborn* in [Eressëa](/wiki/Eress%C3%ABa) and *Nimloth* in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-PMV_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMV-3">[3]</a></sup> In *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, Galathilion was the tree of Tol Eressëa, and [Nimloth](</wiki/Nimloth_(tree)> "Nimloth (tree)") was its seedling.<sup id="cite_ref-PMV_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMV-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:163–4</sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
