<i><b>galadh</b></i> (or *galað*) is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) noun for "tree".<sup id="cite_ref-MR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SApp_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-3">[3]</a></sup> A *galadh* signifies a "thick, dense &amp; branching" tree, like [oaks](../O/Oaks.md) and [beeches](../B/Beeches.md) (compare *[orn](../O/Orn.md)* "tall tree", e.g. [birch](../B/Birches.md) and [ash](../A/Ashes.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-PE_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) *galadā*, derived from root [GALA](GALA.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PE_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-3">[3]</a></sup>

Although the form *galadh* is similar to [Silvan](../S/Silvan_Elvish.md) *[galad](Galad.md#Silvan)* ("tree"), it should not be confused with Sindarin *[galad](Galad.md#Sindarin)* ("radiance").<sup id="cite_ref-MR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SApp_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-2">[2]</a></sup>
