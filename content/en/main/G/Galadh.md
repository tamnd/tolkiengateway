<i><b>galadh</b></i> (or *galað*) is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) noun for "tree".<sup id="cite_ref-MR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SApp_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-3">[3]</a></sup> A *galadh* signifies a "thick, dense &amp; branching" tree, like [oaks](/wiki/Oaks "Oaks") and [beeches](/wiki/Beeches "Beeches") (compare *[orn](/wiki/Orn "Orn")* "tall tree", e.g. [birch](/wiki/Birches "Birches") and [ash](/wiki/Ashes "Ashes")).<sup id="cite_ref-PE_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *galadā*, derived from root [GALA](/wiki/GALA).<sup id="cite_ref-PE_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-3">[3]</a></sup>

Although the form *galadh* is similar to [Silvan](/wiki/Silvan_Elvish "Silvan Elvish") *[galad](/wiki/Galad#Silvan "Galad")* ("tree"), it should not be confused with Sindarin *[galad](/wiki/Galad#Sindarin "Galad")* ("radiance").<sup id="cite_ref-MR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SApp_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-2">[2]</a></sup>
