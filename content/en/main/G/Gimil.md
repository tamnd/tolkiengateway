**gimli**, **igmil** means "star-shaped figure" in [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic). Coll. pl. **gimil** "starry sky".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Perhaps related to [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) *[kibil](/wiki/Kibil "Kibil")* "silver"
