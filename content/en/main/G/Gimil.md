**gimli**, **igmil** means "star-shaped figure" in [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md). Coll. pl. **gimil** "starry sky".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Perhaps related to [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md) *[kibil](../K/Kibil.md)* "silver"
