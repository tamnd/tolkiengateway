**Godfrey William Kenton** ([April 13](/wiki/13_April "13 April"), [1902](/wiki/1902) - [April 27](/wiki/27_April "27 April"), [1998](/wiki/1998))<sup id="cite_ref-Ind_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ind-1">[1]</a></sup> was a stage actor, making only rare appearances on big and small screen.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He also made three two-year periods with the [BBC Drama Repertory Company](/wiki/BBC "BBC"), the first being in World War II.<sup id="cite_ref-Ind_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ind-1">[1]</a></sup> During the second, in [1955](/wiki/1955) and [1956](/wiki/1956), he was cast as [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in [Terence Tiller](/wiki/Terence_Tiller)'s adaptation of *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-RT1672_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RT1672-3">[3]</a></sup> In the second half, of [1956](/wiki/1956), he also provided the voice of [Mablung](</wiki/Mablung_(ranger_of_Ithilien)> "Mablung (ranger of Ithilien)").<sup id="cite_ref-RT1725_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RT1725-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Roles {#Roles}

- [1955](/wiki/1955): *[The Fellowship of the Ring](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)")*
    - Episode 3: "Aragorn" - Aragorn
    - Episode 4: "Many Meetings" - Aragorn
    - Episode 5: "The Moria Gate" - Aragorn
    - Episode 6: "The Breaking of the Fellowship" - Aragorn
- [1956](/wiki/1956): *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)")*
    - Episode 1: "Fangorn" - Aragorn
    - Episode 2: "Rohan and Isengard" - Aragorn
    - Episode 3: "Into the Dark" - Mablung
    - Episode 4: "The Siege of Gondor" - Aragorn
    - Episode 5: "Minas Tirith and Mount Doom" - Aragorn
