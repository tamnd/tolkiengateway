**Gorgumoth** was the [hound](/wiki/Hounds "Hounds") of the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. Gorgumoth guarded [Melko](/wiki/Melkor "Melkor") in the castle of [Lumbi](/wiki/Lumbi) after he was overthrown by [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas) in the [battle](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Silent_Pools "Battle of the Silent Pools") of the [Silent Pools](/wiki/Aelin-uial "Aelin-uial") during the [First Faring Forth](/wiki/War_of_Wrath "War of Wrath").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
