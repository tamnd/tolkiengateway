**Gorgumoth** was the [hound](../H/Hounds.md) of the [Vala](../V/Valar.md) [Mandos](../M/Mandos.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. Gorgumoth guarded [Melko](../M/Melkor.md) in the castle of [Lumbi](../L/Lumbi.md) after he was overthrown by [Tulkas](../T/Tulkas.md) in the [battle](../B/Battle_of_the_Silent_Pools.md) of the [Silent Pools](../A/Aelin-uial.md) during the [First Faring Forth](../W/War_of_Wrath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
