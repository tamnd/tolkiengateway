<i><b>Guardians of Middle-earth</b></i> was a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/multiplayer_online_battle_arena" title="wikipedia:multiplayer online battle arena">multiplayer online battle arena</a> video game developed by Monolith Productions for <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_3" title="wikipedia:PlayStation 3">PlayStation 3</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_360" title="wikipedia:Xbox 360">Xbox 360</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Game_info_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Game_info-2">[2]</a></sup>

Between 2018 and 2019, the game was quietly delisted from all marketplaces, and all game servers shut down. No official announcement from the developer or publisher was made.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Gameplay {#Gameplay}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Combat.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/b/bb/Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Combat.png/300px-Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Combat.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="169" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/b/bb/Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Combat.png/450px-Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Combat.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/b/bb/Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Combat.png/600px-Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Combat.png 2x" data-file-width="787" data-file-height="443"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The player fights in a battle of ten combatants, five on each side, who are either controlled by online players or the AI. The player is able to choose out of twenty different "guardians" who have their own strengths and abilities. An in-game currency can be earned during battles and which can be used to buy items. During battles the player will be able to communicate with allied players with in-game voice communication.<sup id="cite_ref-Game_info_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Game_info-2">[2]</a></sup>
