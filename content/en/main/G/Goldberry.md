**Goldberry** was the enigmatic wife of [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil).

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Beyond_the_Old_Forest.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/6/67/Ted_Nasmith_-_Beyond_the_Old_Forest.jpg/175px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Beyond_the_Old_Forest.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="175" height="114" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/6/67/Ted_Nasmith_-_Beyond_the_Old_Forest.jpg/263px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Beyond_the_Old_Forest.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/6/67/Ted_Nasmith_-_Beyond_the_Old_Forest.jpg/350px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Beyond_the_Old_Forest.jpg 2x" data-file-width="530" data-file-height="344"/></a><figcaption>&#34;Beyond the Old Forest&#34; by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

Although her origins are uncertain, it has been speculated that she is a [river-spirit](/wiki/Spirits "Spirits") of the river [Withywindle](/wiki/Withywindle). However, she and Bombadil are enigmas.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

According to the [Bucklandish](/wiki/Buckland "Buckland") poem *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](</wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil_(poem)> "The Adventures of Tom Bombadil (poem)")*, Goldberry was in the [Withywindle](/wiki/Withywindle) when she pulled Tom by his beard under the [water-lilies](/wiki/Water-lilies "Water-lilies") out of mischief, but he ordered her to let him free. One day, he came to the [River-woman](/wiki/River-woman) and asked Goldberry to be his wife, and the creatures of the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest) (the [badger-folk](/wiki/Badger-folk "Badger-folk") and other animals) attended their wedding.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") Tom Bombadil travelled to the lower reaches of the Withywindle to gather white water-lilies for her, and it was while returning from a lily-gathering expedition that he discovered [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and his companions, and rescued them from [Old Man Willow](/wiki/Old_Man_Willow).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Goldberry seems to have used her lilies to recreate her original home in the river: when Tom brought the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) back to his house, they found a seated Goldberry surrounded by water-lilies floating in pots of earthenware.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Goldberry welcomed and tended the [travellers](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") to their [home](</wiki/Underhill_(house)> "Underhill (house)"). The [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") thought of her as a beautiful and calm being, with a beauty resembling an [Elf](/wiki/Elf) but less exotic to their hearts. Her voice was described as "the song of a glad water ... coming down like silver". The hobbits would listen to her sing in a voice like rain, and they would imagine rivers and pools.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) wrote that Goldberry "*represents the actual seasonal changes in \[river-lands\].*"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In her first appearance in the poem, she is displayed as a typical mischievous water-sprite, responsible for pulling and drowning humans into rivers and lakes.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Because adaptor <a href="/wiki/Terence_Tiller" title="Terence Tiller">Terence Tiller</a> thought the age difference between Bombadil and Goldberry was too big, he made them father and daughter.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-8">[8]</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">Tolkien</a> was upset by the change.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> No actress is credited specifically for the part, though <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nicolette_Bernard&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nicolette Bernard (page does not exist)">Nicolette Bernard</a> is the only female in the episode&#39;s cast list, making it likely she voiced Goldberry.<sup id="cite_ref-RT1672_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RT1672-10">[10]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/1992" title="1992">1992</a>: <a href="/wiki/Der_Herr_der_Ringe_(1992_German_radio_series)" title="Der Herr der Ringe (1992 German radio series)"><i>Der Herr der Ringe</i> (1992 German radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Goldberry is played by Donata Höffer.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/1992" title="1992">1992</a>: <a href="/wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)" title="Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)"><i>Tales from the Perilous Realm</i> (1992 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Sorcha_Cusack" title="Sorcha Cusack">Sorcha Cusack</a> played Goldberry in the episode &#34;The Adventures of Tom Bombadil&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2001" title="2001">2001</a>: <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/P%C3%A1n_prste%C5%88ov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series)" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Goldberry is present in the final third of the first episode, along with her husband Tom Bombadil. Unlike Tom (played by Milan Lasica), Goldberry has no spoken lines, and she is only heard singing harmoniously as Tom and his four hobbit guests arrive at his house. Goldberry&#39;s briefly heard singing vocals seem to be portrayed by Soňa Norisová (similar to vocals also used elsewhere in the series), but the actress or singer is uncredited.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2004" title="2004">2004</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Though Goldberry does not appear in <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> film series</a>, <a href="/wiki/Decipher" title="Decipher">Decipher</a> produced a depicting her in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i> as part of the &#34;Book Cards&#34; label, which denotes cards produced by <a href="/wiki/W%C4%93t%C4%81_Workshop" title="Wētā Workshop">Wētā Workshop</a> specifically for the TCG. <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Amanda_Neil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Amanda Neil (page does not exist)">Amanda Neil</a>, Wētā makeup artist, portrayed her<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> in the &#34;Goldberry, River-daughter&#34; card from the &#34;Reflections&#34; set which depicts her near a river.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2002" title="2002">2002</a>: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Goldberry appears at the house of Tom Bombadil. One of the tasks the player has to accomplish is collecting <a href="/wiki/Water-lilies" title="Water-lilies">water-lilies</a> for Tom and Goldberry. She is voiced by <a href="/wiki/Kath_Soucie" title="Kath Soucie">Kath Soucie</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2007" title="2007">2007</a>-: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Goldberry can be found at Goldberry&#39;s Spring in the <a href="/wiki/Old_Forest" title="Old Forest">Old Forest</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> Goldberry&#39;s role in the game is very minor: she is only involved in two quests and only has a few lines in both. Her origin however, is greatly elaborated upon: she is a River-maiden, a spirit that watches over the streams and rivers. Goldberry&#39;s sisters can be found in the <a href="/wiki/Lone-lands" title="Lone-lands">Lone-lands</a>, <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Nenuial" title="Nenuial">Nenuial</a>, the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Gladden" title="Gladden">Gladden</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gilrain" title="Gilrain">Gilrain</a>, <a href="/wiki/Serni" title="Serni">Serni</a> and <a href="/wiki/Erui" title="Erui">Erui</a>. Some show themselves openly, others are secretive and only reveal themselves to the Wise and the descendants of the <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans" title="Númenóreans">Númenóreans</a> who had known of River-maidens in the years of old. But some were driven mad by the bloodshed spilled over their waters, or slain and devoured by the servants of the Enemy who then take on their fair appearance.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2024" title="2024">2024</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a></i>: <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_-_Season_2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power - Season 2 (page does not exist)">Season 2</a></b>

<dl><dd>In episode 4, <i><a href="/wiki/Eldest_(episode)" title="Eldest (episode)">Eldest</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/The_Stranger" title="The Stranger">Gandalf</a> washes in <a href="/wiki/Tom_Bombadil" title="Tom Bombadil">Tom Bombadil</a>&#39;s desert house. He hears Bombadil singing &#34;Down west sinks the sun, soon you will be gropin’&#34;. Bombadil says &#34;Don’t be bashful now, Goldberry&#34;. Goldberry&#39;s voice is heard singing &#34;When the night-shadows fall&#34;. Then, they are heard singing together &#34;Then the door will open&#34;. When Bombadil returns, Gandalf asks him if somebody else is in the house, as he thought he heard a woman singing. Bombadil asks &#34;Woman? What woman?&#34;. Gandalf asks &#34;Is no one else here with you?&#34; and Bombadil replies &#34;You’re here. That is, I think you are. Are you?&#34;. Gandalf replies &#34;Uh, Yes?&#34; and the topic of Goldberry is not brought up again. Goldberry was voiced by <a href="/wiki/Raya_Yarbrough" title="Raya Yarbrough">Raya Yarbrough</a>, wife of <i>The Rings of Power</i> composer <a href="/wiki/Bear_McCreary" title="Bear McCreary">Bear McCreary</a>.</dd></dl>
