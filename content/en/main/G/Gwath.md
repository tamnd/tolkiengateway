<i><b>gwath</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "shade", "shadow" or "dim light".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT42_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-2">[2]</a></sup>

In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)* the word is labelled as [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), and glossed as "stain", coalesced with the notion of "shade".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [WATH](/wiki/WATH)<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Deld%C3%BAwath" title="Deldúwath">Deldú<b>wath</b></a></i> = "Deadly Nightshade"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Ered_Wethrin" title="Ered Wethrin">Ered <b>Weth</b>rin</a></i> = "Mountains of Shadow"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3" title="Gwathló"><b>Gwath</b>ló</a></i> = "Greyflood"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Thuringwethil" title="Thuringwethil">Thurin<b>gweth</b>il</a></i> = "Woman of Secret Shadow"

- ([Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)) <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Agarwaen" title="Agarwaen">Iar<b>wath</b></a></i> = *iar* + *gwath* ("Blood-stained")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
