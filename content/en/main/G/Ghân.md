**Ghân** was the father of the famous leader of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain), [Ghân-buri-Ghân](/wiki/Gh%C3%A2n-buri-Gh%C3%A2n), obviously derived from his own name.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Ghân-buri-Ghân](/wiki/Gh%C3%A2n-buri-Gh%C3%A2n) ("Ghân son of Ghân"; *Ghân* + *[buri](/wiki/Buri "Buri")*)
