**Girion** (d. [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2770](../T/Third_Age_2770.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>) was the last Lord of [Dale](../D/Dale.md).

## History {#History}

Girion was a [Man of Dale](../M/Men_of_Dale.md) and became lord of the city. Not much can be said about his life and rule, other than he had a wife, and at least two sons.<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-1">[1]</a></sup>

He possessed a [Necklace](../N/Necklace_of_Girion.md) made of five hundred emeralds. When the [Dwarves of Erebor](../D/Dwarves_of_Erebor.md) forged a coat of dwarf-linked rings, presumably of [mithril](../M/Mithril.md), for his eldest son, Girion gave the necklace to the Dwarves as a payment.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

He witnessed the arrival of [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) when the [Dragon](../D/Dragons.md) assaulted Dale and the neighbouring [dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) realm of [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md). Girion was killed during that event but his wife and child escaped down the [River Running](../R/River_Running.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

Many years later, his descendant [Bard](../B/Bard.md) was still living in [Esgaroth](../L/Lake-town.md). He took revenge upon the dragon by slaying him with [an arrow](../B/Black_Arrow~68980.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-1">[1]</a></sup> His necklace was found among the dragon's hoard.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Although the names of Dale are [Norse](../N/Norse.md), *Girion* seems to be a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. The meaning is not clear but the *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* give a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) GIR ("quiver, shudder"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> which might be related.
