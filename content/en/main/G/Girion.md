**Girion** (d. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>) was the last Lord of [Dale](/wiki/Dale).

## History {#History}

Girion was a [Man of Dale](/wiki/Men_of_Dale "Men of Dale") and became lord of the city. Not much can be said about his life and rule, other than he had a wife, and at least two sons.<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-1">[1]</a></sup>

He possessed a [Necklace](/wiki/Necklace_of_Girion "Necklace of Girion") made of five hundred emeralds. When the [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor) forged a coat of dwarf-linked rings, presumably of [mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril"), for his eldest son, Girion gave the necklace to the Dwarves as a payment.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

He witnessed the arrival of [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) when the [Dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") assaulted Dale and the neighbouring [dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") realm of [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"). Girion was killed during that event but his wife and child escaped down the [River Running](/wiki/River_Running).<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

Many years later, his descendant [Bard](/wiki/Bard) was still living in [Esgaroth](/wiki/Lake-town "Lake-town"). He took revenge upon the dragon by slaying him with [an arrow](/wiki/Black_Arrow "Black Arrow").<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-1">[1]</a></sup> His necklace was found among the dragon's hoard.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Although the names of Dale are [Norse](/wiki/Norse), *Girion* seems to be a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. The meaning is not clear but the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* give a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") GIR ("quiver, shudder"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> which might be related.
