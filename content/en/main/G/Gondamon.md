**Gondamon** is a city in the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) at the midway point between [Thorin's Hall](/wiki/Thorin%27s_Hall) and [Duillond](/wiki/Duillond) in the game *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*.

## History {#History}

Gondamon was first founded by the survivors of the Fall of [Edhelion](/wiki/Edhelion). After the Elves left it for [Duillond](/wiki/Duillond), the Dwarves settled here and made it to their liking.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> During the War of the Ring the lord of Gondamon was [Mathi Stouthand](/wiki/Mathi_Stouthand), who became lord by fighting in the War of the [Longbeards](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk "Durin's Folk") and the [Dourhands](/wiki/Dourhands).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The city has a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, thanks to it's former Elven inhabitants: *Gondamon*, which means "Hill of Stone" with *gond* meaning "stone"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and *amon* meaning "hill".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
