"**Great beasts**"<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>, "**great monsters**"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>, or **"great [Southron](/wiki/Southrons "Southrons") monsters**"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>, was a vague term applied to various large [animals](/wiki/Kelvar "Kelvar") used in the armies of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). This term was applied several times to [oliphaunts](/wiki/Oliphaunts "Oliphaunts") (also known as *mûmakil* to [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) and [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians)).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The [winged steed](/wiki/Witch-king%27s_winged_steed "Witch-king's winged steed") of the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) was also called "the great beast" twice.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Near the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), different great beasts were used as war-beasts, steeds or beasts of burden by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s armies during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). Some great beasts (described as *mûmakil)* were sent on [14 March](/wiki/14_March) by the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) to pull huge siege-towers and great engines of war to the [City Wall](/wiki/City_Wall) of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> The most notable of these great beasts were ones specifically tasked with pulling the huge battering ram <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Grond" title="Grond">Grond</a> to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith)'s [Great Gate](/wiki/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith "Great Gate of Minas Tirith") to destroy it.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

While the arrows shot from the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Swan-knights&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Swan-knights (page does not exist)">swan-knights</a> of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth) did little to halt Grond, occasionally some of the great beasts "would go mad and spread stamping ruin among the" many [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs_of_Mordor "Orcs of Mordor") which guarded Grond. However the bodies of the tramped Orcs were cast aside and new Orcs replaced the fallen ones just as quickly.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Speculation {#Speculation}

Whether the great beasts used to pull Grond were *mûmakil* or a different type of unknown animals from distant lands, is impossible to say for certain as we lack a more detailed description of the creatures.

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The great beasts pulling <a href="/wiki/Grond_(battering_ram)" title="Grond (battering ram)">Grond</a> are depicted as rhinoceros-like creatures. In the Weta Workshop <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)"><i>Return of The King</i> documentary</a> it is revealed they are based off of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megacerops" title="wikipedia:Megacerops">Brontotherium</a> in appearance.</dd></dl>

<figure typeof="mw:File/Frame"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Yurgs.jpg"><img src="/w/images/a/af/Yurgs.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="192" height="192" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="192" data-file-height="192"/></a><figcaption><i>Yurgs</i> in <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">BFME2</a></figcaption></figure>

<b>2006: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In this video game the basic design of the great beasts from Peter Jackson&#39;s <i>The Lord of the Rings</i> is kept. However, a new idea is introduced: the creatures called &#34;great beasts&#34; were originally used by Dwarves in the Misty Mountains as underground draft animals (depicted as being much smaller than Sauron&#39;s beasts of burden). The Dwarves called them <i>yurgs</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the Mordor expansion pack, the great beasts were given the name <b>Sulokil</b>. They were depicted as being horned, stony-skinned beasts that were a local subspecies of <a href="/wiki/Kine_of_Araw" title="Kine of Araw">Aurochs</a>. Despite living in the &#34;Dor Amarth&#34; region of <a href="/wiki/Plateau_of_Gorgoroth" title="Plateau of Gorgoroth">Plateau of Gorgoroth</a> and being used as beasts of burden by <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>, the Sulokil were  docile unless provoked.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> According to <a href="/wiki/Ingold" title="Ingold">Ingold</a>, their meat was &#34;rather good when cooked and seasoned&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In <a href="/wiki/Minas_Morgul" title="Minas Morgul">Minas Morgul</a> expansion, it is revealed that in the late <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">Second Age</a>, their ancestors, the <b>Sûlokil</b>, wandered the Plateau of Gorgoroth, then named &#34;Mordor Besieged&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> During the <a href="/wiki/Siege_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr" title="Siege of Barad-dûr">Siege of Barad-dûr</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men" title="Last Alliance of Elves and Men">Great Alliance</a>, the Sûlokil were hunted for food since they provided the nourishment that smaller animals did not provide.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup></dd></dl>
