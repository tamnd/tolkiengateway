The **Gates of Sirion** was the cavernous mouth in the southern hills of the [Andram](/wiki/Andram) where the River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) emerged from its underground course.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The Gates were located three leagues southward of the [Falls of Sirion](/wiki/Falls_of_Sirion) where the river plunged below the Andram south of the [Aelin-uial](/wiki/Aelin-uial).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the early *[Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, they were known as the great cave of **Tumultuous Winds**, which the [Exiles of Gondolin](/wiki/Exiles_of_Gondolin) crossed in their wanderings.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
