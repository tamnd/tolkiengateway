**Girdley Island** was an island located in the [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) River, north of the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggests a possible [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") etymology from \**Gyrdlieg* "girdle-island".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> "Girdle" probably refers to its being 'girdled' (enclosed) between the river-branches.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

As in [Rushey](/wiki/Rushey), the ending <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/ey#Old_Norse" title="wiktionary:ey">-ey</a></i> means "island" in [Old Norse](/wiki/Old_Norse) and is found in English island names (cf. <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gurnsey#Etymology" title="wikipedia:Gurnsey">Gurnsey</a>).

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Girdley Island was once a beacon-hill of <a href="/wiki/Arnor" title="Arnor">Arnor</a>, but was abandoned after the founding of the <a href="/wiki/The_Shire" title="The Shire">Shire</a>. It is located on the border with the in-game region of <a href="/wiki/Bree-land" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a>. While identified as a point of interest, no quests take place there, nor are there any enemies.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2025: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Hobbit_Tales" title="Hobbit Tales">Hobbit Tales</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Sometime before <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2960" title="Third Age 2960">2960</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North" title="Rangers of the North">Rangers of the North</a> established a secret refuge on the island. This led to Hobbits believing the place to be haunted.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>
