The **Grey Mountains** (or **Ered Mithrin** in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)) was a large mountain range to the north of [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md). Their western end connected to the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) at the site of [Mount Gundabad](Gundabad.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

North of the Grey Mountains lay the [Northern Waste](../N/Northern_Waste.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UTmap_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTmap-1">[1]</a></sup> In the west, where the Grey Mountains met with the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) rose [Mount Gundabad](Gundabad.md), an ancient [Dwarven](../D/Dwarves.md) holy site and later the capital for the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) of the north. The eastern end of the Grey Mountains was split into two chains, and in between lay the [Withered Heath](../W/Withered_Heath.md), where [Dragons](../D/Dragons.md) bred. After that was a wide hilly plain, beyond which lay the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md). [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md), the Lonely Mountain, lay south of the Grey Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-UTmap_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTmap-1">[1]</a></sup>

From east to west the mountains stretched some 350 miles, and the sources of the Great River [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), the river [Greylin](Greylin.md), and the [Forest River](../F/Forest_River.md) of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) arose in this range.<sup id="cite_ref-UTmap_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTmap-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Early history {#Early_history}

In the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), the Longbeards established mansions in [Moria](../M/Moria.md) and the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md), and they considered the Grey Mountains, which lay between these mansions, to be within their territory. Some [Men](../M/Men.md)—mostly related to the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md) of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md)—settled between the Grey Mountains and [Greenwood](Greenwood.md), and they allied with the Longbeards against Morgoth's [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-relations_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-relations-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:302–303</sup> This alliance ended in the Second Age after [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) destroyed [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md), which prompted the Longbeards to seal Moria. During this time, Orcs took control of the Grey Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-relations_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-relations-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:305</sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

Durin's folk started to gather in the Grey Mountains in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1981](../T/Third_Age_1981.md) after a [Balrog](../B/Balrogs.md) was awakened in their ancestral home of [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md), which its people fled in fear. Another portion of Durin's folk lead by King [Thráin I](../T/Thráin_I.md) established the [Kingdom under the Mountain](../K/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain.md) founded the [Kingdom under the Mountain](../K/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1999](../T/Third_Age_1999.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup> at [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md).

Around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2000](../T/Third_Age_2000.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Scatha](../S/Scatha.md), a mighty [Long-worm](../L/Long-worms.md) of the Grey Mountains and one of the greatest [Dragons](../D/Dragons.md) to infest that range of the north, had plundered treasure from the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) and kept it in his hoard. He was slain by [Fram](../F/Fram.md) son of [Frumgar](../F/Frumgar.md) in the early days of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md). His recovered hoard was the subject of great dispute between the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) and the [Dwarves of the Grey Mountains](../D/Dwarves_of_the_Grey_Mountains.md), who claimed the hoard as their own.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the [Watchful Peace](../W/Watchful_Peace.md) most of [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md) were gathering to the Grey Mountains and in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2210](../T/Third_Age_2210.md) [Thorin I](../T/Thorin_I.md) abandoned Erebor and also joined the others.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-App|TA_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|TA-6">[6]</a></sup> Nearly six hundred years later, [Dragons](../D/Dragons.md) began afflicting the Dwarves. In [2589](../T/Third_Age_2589.md) [Dáin I](../D/Dáin_I.md) was slain by a Dragon and the Grey Mountain strongholds were abandoned after the [Wars of the Dwarves and Dragons](../W/Wars_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons.md). About [2480](../T/Third_Age_2480.md) [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) had begun to infest the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md)<sup id="cite_ref-App|TA_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|TA-6">[6]</a></sup> and spread to the Grey Mountains, such that by [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md) [Gandalf](Gandalf.md) the Wizard could say that the range was "simply stiff with goblins, hobgoblins, and orcs of the worst description".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> However, after the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md) the number of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) in the Grey Mountains were greatly reduced (some three parts of them had perished).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

There still was a remnant of the Dwarves in the Ered Mithrin after the core of the population left,<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup> but were probably few; working whatever mines they could hold from the Orcs and Dragons.

<dl><dd>Ered Mithrin is an area in &#34;The Dwarf-holds&#34; region. The area consists of the southern slopes of the mountains and the land north of <a href="../M/Mirkwood.md" title="Mirkwood">Eryn Lasgalen</a>, and is accessed by a road going through the <a href="../D/Dale.md" title="Dale">Dale</a>-lands west of Erebor.
