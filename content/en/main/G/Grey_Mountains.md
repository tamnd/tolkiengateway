The **Grey Mountains** (or **Ered Mithrin** in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)) was a large mountain range to the north of [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion). Their western end connected to the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) at the site of [Mount Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad "Gundabad").

## Geography {#Geography}

North of the Grey Mountains lay the [Northern Waste](/wiki/Northern_Waste).<sup id="cite_ref-UTmap_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTmap-1">[1]</a></sup> In the west, where the Grey Mountains met with the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) rose [Mount Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad "Gundabad"), an ancient [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") holy site and later the capital for the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of the north. The eastern end of the Grey Mountains was split into two chains, and in between lay the [Withered Heath](/wiki/Withered_Heath), where [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons) bred. After that was a wide hilly plain, beyond which lay the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills). [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"), the Lonely Mountain, lay south of the Grey Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-UTmap_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTmap-1">[1]</a></sup>

From east to west the mountains stretched some 350 miles, and the sources of the Great River [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), the river [Greylin](/wiki/Greylin), and the [Forest River](/wiki/Forest_River) of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) arose in this range.<sup id="cite_ref-UTmap_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTmap-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Early history {#Early_history}

In the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the Longbeards established mansions in [Moria](/wiki/Moria) and the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills), and they considered the Grey Mountains, which lay between these mansions, to be within their territory. Some [Men](/wiki/Men)—mostly related to the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador) of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand)—settled between the Grey Mountains and [Greenwood](/wiki/Greenwood), and they allied with the Longbeards against Morgoth's [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-relations_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-relations-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:302–303</sup> This alliance ended in the Second Age after [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) destroyed [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion), which prompted the Longbeards to seal Moria. During this time, Orcs took control of the Grey Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-relations_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-relations-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:305</sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

Durin's folk started to gather in the Grey Mountains in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981") after a [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") was awakened in their ancestral home of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"), which its people fled in fear. Another portion of Durin's folk lead by King [Thráin I](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_I) established the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) founded the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1999](/wiki/Third_Age_1999 "Third Age 1999")<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup> at [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain").

Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2000](/wiki/Third_Age_2000 "Third Age 2000"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Scatha](/wiki/Scatha), a mighty [Long-worm](/wiki/Long-worms "Long-worms") of the Grey Mountains and one of the greatest [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons) to infest that range of the north, had plundered treasure from the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) and kept it in his hoard. He was slain by [Fram](/wiki/Fram) son of [Frumgar](/wiki/Frumgar) in the early days of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od). His recovered hoard was the subject of great dispute between the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) and the [Dwarves of the Grey Mountains](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Grey_Mountains), who claimed the hoard as their own.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the [Watchful Peace](/wiki/Watchful_Peace) most of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) were gathering to the Grey Mountains and in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2210](/wiki/Third_Age_2210 "Third Age 2210") [Thorin I](/wiki/Thorin_I) abandoned Erebor and also joined the others.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-App|TA_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|TA-6">[6]</a></sup> Nearly six hundred years later, [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons) began afflicting the Dwarves. In [2589](/wiki/Third_Age_2589 "Third Age 2589") [Dáin I](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I) was slain by a Dragon and the Grey Mountain strongholds were abandoned after the [Wars of the Dwarves and Dragons](/wiki/Wars_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons). About [2480](/wiki/Third_Age_2480 "Third Age 2480") [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) had begun to infest the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains)<sup id="cite_ref-App|TA_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|TA-6">[6]</a></sup> and spread to the Grey Mountains, such that by [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) the Wizard could say that the range was "simply stiff with goblins, hobgoblins, and orcs of the worst description".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> However, after the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies) the number of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) in the Grey Mountains were greatly reduced (some three parts of them had perished).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

There still was a remnant of the Dwarves in the Ered Mithrin after the core of the population left,<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup> but were probably few; working whatever mines they could hold from the Orcs and Dragons.

<dl><dd>Ered Mithrin is an area in &#34;The Dwarf-holds&#34; region. The area consists of the southern slopes of the mountains and the land north of <a href="/wiki/Mirkwood" title="Mirkwood">Eryn Lasgalen</a>, and is accessed by a road going through the <a href="/wiki/Dale" title="Dale">Dale</a>-lands west of Erebor. The Grey Mountains have been settled both by the Longbeards and by a house of Easterling dwarves known as the &#34;Zhélruka&#34;. They are now inhabited by many lesser <a href="/wiki/Dragons" title="Dragons">Dragon-kind</a> as well as the true Dragons &#34;Etterfang Foulmaw&#34;, Smaug&#39;s sister, and &#34;Hrímil Frost-heart&#34;. Orcs and Trolls from Gundabad can also be found there.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The slopes and foothills of the Grey Mountains between Gundabad and the sources of the Anduin were introduced in a later update as the region of &#34;Elderslade&#34;, site of a war between the Orcs of Gundabad and an army assembled by &#34;Prince Durin&#34;, son of <a href="/wiki/Thorin_Stonehelm" title="Thorin Stonehelm">Thorin Stonehelm</a>.</dd></dl>
