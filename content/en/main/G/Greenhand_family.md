The **Greenhand family** was a minor family originated by [Holman the greenhanded](/wiki/Holman_the_greenhanded), a [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) gardener. The last known of the Greenhands was apparently Holman's grandson and namesake, [Holman Greenhand](/wiki/Holman_Greenhand), but the family's gardening tradition was carried on by his apprentice, [Hamfast Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee"). No other descendants of the family are documented.<sup id="cite_ref-long_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-long-1">[1]</a></sup>
