**Grinnah** was a [Goblin](../O/Orcs.md#Orcs_and_Goblins) interrogator in [Goblin-town](Goblin-town.md). Grinnah was invented by [Warner Bros.](../W/Warner_Bros..md) for [*The Hobbit* film series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>), where he was portrayed by [Stephen Ure](../S/Stephen_Ure.md), who also portrayed [Fimbul](../F/Fimbul.md).

## History {#History}

Grinnah appeared to be a significant member of the Goblin-town hierarchy, as he directly reported to the [Great Goblin](Great_Goblin.md) when they captured [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) at the [Front Porch](../F/Front_Porch.md). He was responsible for discovering [Orcrist](../O/Orcrist.md) on [Thorin Oakenshield](../T/Thorin.md), and showing it to the Great Goblin, who angrily ordered his subordinates to kill Thorin. When [Gandalf](Gandalf.md) arrived to rescue the Dwarves and they took up arms to fight their way out, Grinnah was stabbed to death by [Kíli](../K/Kíli.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Personality {#Personality}

Grinnah was cunning and vicious, but much like the other goblins, he is a coward. Despite his apparent loyality to the Great Goblin, he actually hated him in secret.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
