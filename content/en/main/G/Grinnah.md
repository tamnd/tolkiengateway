**Grinnah** was a [Goblin](/wiki/Orcs#Orcs_and_Goblins "Orcs") interrogator in [Goblin-town](/wiki/Goblin-town). Grinnah was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for [*The Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), where he was portrayed by [Stephen Ure](/wiki/Stephen_Ure), who also portrayed [Fimbul](/wiki/Fimbul).

## History {#History}

Grinnah appeared to be a significant member of the Goblin-town hierarchy, as he directly reported to the [Great Goblin](/wiki/Great_Goblin) when they captured [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) at the [Front Porch](/wiki/Front_Porch). He was responsible for discovering [Orcrist](/wiki/Orcrist) on [Thorin Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin"), and showing it to the Great Goblin, who angrily ordered his subordinates to kill Thorin. When [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) arrived to rescue the Dwarves and they took up arms to fight their way out, Grinnah was stabbed to death by [Kíli](/wiki/K%C3%ADli).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Personality {#Personality}

Grinnah was cunning and vicious, but much like the other goblins, he is a coward. Despite his apparent loyality to the Great Goblin, he actually hated him in secret.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
