**Grip** was a [dog](/wiki/Dogs "Dogs") inhabitant of the town of [Delving](/wiki/Delving) who appeared in *[Perry-the-Winkle](/wiki/Perry-the-Winkle)*, a nursery rhyme<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup> by [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)<sup id="cite_ref-preface_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-preface-3">[3]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-Perry_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Perry-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the poem, Grip was the companion of the knife-throwing [Bill Butcher](/wiki/Bill_Butcher), but he "turned his tail and ran to save his life" upon seeing the [Lonely Troll](/wiki/Lonely_Troll) peeping over the stalls of the market-place.<sup id="cite_ref-Perry_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Perry-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is possible that Grip may have accompanied Bill Butcher later in the poem as the butcher may have been one of the [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits) who travelled to <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Faraway&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Faraway (page does not exist)">Faraway</a> in order to demand food from the Lonely Troll.<sup id="cite_ref-Perry_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Perry-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Background {#Background}

In the first version of the poem in early [1928](/wiki/1928),<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-4">[4]</a></sup> which was entitled *The Bumpus*, Grip didn't exist, but his role was given to "**[Harry](/wiki/Harry) and his father**", both of whom fled for "their lives" after howling in fear.<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup> No changes to the characters occurred in the second version on [29 March](/wiki/29_March),<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup> in which Tolkien revised the poem as part<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup> of his *[Tales and Songs of Bimble Bay](/wiki/Tales_and_Songs_of_Bimble_Bay)* series. The third version also retained the same details in relation to "Harry and his father".<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-4">[4]</a></sup>

In early [1961](/wiki/1961),<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup> when Tolkien revised the poem for inclusion within *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*, the characters of "Harry and his father" were merged into Grip, Bill Butcher's dog.<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-4">[4]</a></sup>
