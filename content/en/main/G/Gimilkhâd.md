**Gimilkhâd** was the younger son of Queen [Inzilbêth](../I/Inzilbêth.md) and King [Ar-Gimilzôr](../A/Ar-Gimilzôr.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). His older brother [Inziladûn](../I/Inziladûn.md) followed his mother's beliefs (she was secretly one of the [Faithful](../F/Faithful.md)). Inziladûn took the royal title of [Tar-Palantir](../T/Tar-Palantir.md) and attempted to lead the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) back to reverence for the [Valar](../V/Valar.md). However, Gimilkhâd believed in the ways of his father, became the leader of the [King's Men](../K/King's_Men.md), and opposed the policies of his brother. His son usurped the [Sceptre](../S/Sceptre_of_Númenor.md) of Tar-Palantir's daughter [Míriel](../T/Tar-Míriel.md), took the title of [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Palantir_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Palantir-2">[2]</a></sup> and brought destruction upon the island realm.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gimilkhâd* is an [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) name, but its meaning is not glossed. Paul Strack notices the first element must be *[gimil](Gimil.md)* ("stars"), but it is not clear what the last element *khâd* means.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}
