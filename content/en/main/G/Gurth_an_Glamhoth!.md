<i><b>Gurth an Glamhoth!</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) phrase muttered by [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) when he and [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) spotted an [Orc](/wiki/Orc) encampment and he was tempted to attack them and take their meat.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It translates as "Death to the [Glamhoth](/wiki/Glamhoth)!"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
