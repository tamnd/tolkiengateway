<i><b>Gurth an Glamhoth!</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) phrase muttered by [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) when he and [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md) spotted an [Orc](../O/Orc.md) encampment and he was tempted to attack them and take their meat.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It translates as "Death to the [Glamhoth](Glamhoth.md)!"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
