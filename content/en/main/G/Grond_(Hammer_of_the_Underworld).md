**Grond**, called the Hammer of the Underworld, was [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s mighty mace that he bore when he fought [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) before the doors of [Angband](/wiki/Angband). Each time Grond struck the ground it shook the land like a bolt of thunder, creating pits from which smoke and fire erupted. However, Fingolfin evaded these lumbering strokes like a flash of lightning; he only fell when he grew weary and Morgoth was able to "set his left foot upon his neck, and the weight of it was like a fallen hill".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Much later during the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) constructed a great battering ram, also called "[Grond](</wiki/Grond_(battering_ram)> "Grond (battering ram)")", designed solely to crush through the mighty gates of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). Named after his former master's weapon, this ram, powered by the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king), broke the city gates in four strokes.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Grond* means "very weighty and ponderous" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
