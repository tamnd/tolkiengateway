**George Herbert Cowling** ([1881](../_/1881.md)- [23 July](../_/23_July.md) [1946](../_/1946.md)) was a British philologist and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Bay_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bay-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Biography {#Biography}

In [1920](../_/1920.md) Cowling was lecturing at the [University of Leeds](../U/University_of_Leeds.md), as Assistant Lecturer in English Language under Tolkien and [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Bay_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bay-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup> In [1925](../_/1925.md), when Tolkien moved to the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md), Cowling was appointed Reader.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup> He subsequently moved to Australia to take up the Chair of English at the University of Melbourne in [1928](../_/1928.md). The Tolkien and Cowling families remained friends; in [1938](../_/1938.md) they stayed with J.R.R. and [Edith](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) at [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md) when on sabbatical leave.<sup id="cite_ref-Bay_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bay-1">[1]</a></sup>

His wife Muriel (Mollie) maintained contact with the Tolkien family after her husband's death, and visited the Tolkiens in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a> on several occasions. During such a visit in [1955](../_/1955.md), Muriel was given signed copies of three volumes of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* (in which Tolkien included a [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) inscription).<sup id="cite_ref-Bay_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bay-1">[1]</a></sup>
