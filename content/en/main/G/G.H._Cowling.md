**George Herbert Cowling** ([1881](/wiki/1881)- [23 July](/wiki/23_July) [1946](/wiki/1946)) was a British philologist and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-Bay_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bay-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Biography {#Biography}

In [1920](/wiki/1920) Cowling was lecturing at the [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds), as Assistant Lecturer in English Language under Tolkien and [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon).<sup id="cite_ref-Bay_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bay-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup> In [1925](/wiki/1925), when Tolkien moved to the [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford), Cowling was appointed Reader.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup> He subsequently moved to Australia to take up the Chair of English at the University of Melbourne in [1928](/wiki/1928). The Tolkien and Cowling families remained friends; in [1938](/wiki/1938) they stayed with J.R.R. and [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien") at [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford) when on sabbatical leave.<sup id="cite_ref-Bay_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bay-1">[1]</a></sup>

His wife Muriel (Mollie) maintained contact with the Tolkien family after her husband's death, and visited the Tolkiens in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a> on several occasions. During such a visit in [1955](/wiki/1955), Muriel was given signed copies of three volumes of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* (in which Tolkien included a [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) inscription).<sup id="cite_ref-Bay_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bay-1">[1]</a></sup>
