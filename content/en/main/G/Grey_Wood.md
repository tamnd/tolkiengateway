The **Grey Wood** was a wood growing between [Mount Mindolluin](../M/Mount_Mindolluin.md) in [Gondor](Gondor.md) and the beacon-hill of [Amon Dîn](../A/Amon_Dîn.md) to the north. It formed an easterly outlier of the larger [Drúadan Forest](../D/Drúadan_Forest.md), where the remnant of the [Drúedain](../D/Drúedain.md) dwelt in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its trees masked the eastern entrance to the ancient and forgotten [Stonewain Valley](../S/Stonewain_Valley.md) through the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) near the [Great West Road](Great_West_Road.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the Drúedain shared their knowledge of the secret Valley with the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md), and so on [14 March](../_/14_March.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) and his Men passed through the Grey Wood to the relief of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-2">[2]</a></sup> After the War, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) returned to the Grey Wood, and it was here that he granted [Ghân-buri-Ghân](Ghân-buri-Ghân.md) and his people the freedom of the whole Drúadan Forest.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
