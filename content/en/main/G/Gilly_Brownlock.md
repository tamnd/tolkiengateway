**Gilly Brownlock** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Gilly married [Posco Baggins](../P/Posco_Baggins.md), and they had several children: [Ponto](../P/Ponto_Baggins_II.md), [Porto](../P/Porto_Baggins.md) and [Peony](../P/Peony_Baggins.md).

Gilly with her children and grandchildren, was present at [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)' [Birthday Party](../B/Birthday_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
