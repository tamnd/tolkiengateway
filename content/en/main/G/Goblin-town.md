**Goblin-town** was an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs#Goblins_and_Orcs "Orcs") dwelling under the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), which was ruled by the [Great Goblin](/wiki/Great_Goblin).

Goblin-town was a series of tunnels and caverns, which went all the way through the mountains, with a "[back door](/wiki/Goblin-gate "Goblin-gate")" near the [Eagle's Eyrie](/wiki/Eagle%27s_Eyrie) in [Wilderland](/wiki/Rhovanion "Rhovanion"), which served as a means of escape, and an access to the Wilderland.<sup id="cite_ref-pan_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pan-1">[1]</a></sup> A cave with a lake was deep beneath Goblin-town yet was connected to the Orcs' tunnels, with one passage leading to the "back door".<sup id="cite_ref-rid_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rid-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Long before the coming of the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains), [nameless things](/wiki/Nameless_things "Nameless things") were living in the caves.<sup id="cite_ref-rid_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rid-2">[2]</a></sup> Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2480](/wiki/Third_Age_2480 "Third Age 2480") the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains) started making secret strongholds in the mountains blocking the passages towards [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador)<sup id="cite_ref-ta_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-3">[3]</a></sup> and expanded those caves, digging tunnels to connect them; without their knowledge they drove those slimy creatures into dark corners. One of those "original owners" was [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum), who was living at [a nearby underground lake](/wiki/Gollum%27s_lake "Gollum's lake"), feeding himself on raw fish as well as Orcs that he killed.<sup id="cite_ref-rid_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rid-2">[2]</a></sup>

The main gate of the town was in one of the passes of the Misty Mountains, but by [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-ta_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-3">[3]</a></sup> travellers learned to avoid that dangerous road and the Orcs opened another under the safer [High Pass](/wiki/High_Pass): The [Front Porch](/wiki/Front_Porch)).<sup id="cite_ref-pan_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pan-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the journey to [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain), [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) rested in a cave and were captured by the Orcs. They were brought before the Great Goblin, who accused them of spying, and was enraged when he found that Thorin was carrying [Orcrist](/wiki/Orcrist), a sword the Great Goblin remembered from [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin). With the help of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), the Great Goblin was slain and Bilbo and the Dwarves escaped, pursued by a force of Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1977: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)" title="The Hobbit (1977 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1977 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Goblin-town is, like in the book, a set of tunnels and caverns.</dd></dl>

<b>1989: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(comic_book)" title="The Hobbit (comic book)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (comic book)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Goblin-town is, like in the book, a set of tunnels and caverns.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)" title="The Hobbit (2003 video game)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (2003 video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Goblin-town is a giant Dwarven mining complex with many lifts, bridges and train carts to spice up the action of the level.</dd></dl>

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Goblin-town.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/f/f3/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Goblin-town.jpg/250px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Goblin-town.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="141" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/f/f3/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Goblin-town.jpg/375px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Goblin-town.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/f/f3/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Goblin-town.jpg/500px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Goblin-town.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2560" data-file-height="1440"/></a><figcaption>Goblin-town in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i></figcaption></figure>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>One of the first new additions to the game after the initial release, Goblin-town proper is a large network of tunnels and caverns accessible from the eastern part of the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a> region. It is a labyrinthine network of hallways, bridges and caves with many side passages. Great Goblin&#39;s Throne Room and <a href="/wiki/Gollum%27s_lake" title="Gollum&#39;s lake">Gollum&#39;s Cave</a> are two of the natural endpoints of the exploration. Goblin-town was revisited in the &#34;Seat of the Great Goblin&#34; group instance that greatly streamlined the layout with the focus on the Throne Room. Another version of Goblin-town is accessible from the eastern entrance in the <a href="/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin" title="Vales of Anduin">Vales of Anduin</a> region. This version also contains the Throne Room, with all three versions of it being slightly different from one another.</dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Goblin-town is portrayed as a giant system of caverns with sprawling wooden walkways. The goblins have a central hub of dwellings, with the Great Goblin&#39;s throne being in the center.</dd></dl>
