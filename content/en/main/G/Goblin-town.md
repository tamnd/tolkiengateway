**Goblin-town** was an [Orc](../O/Orcs.md#Goblins_and_Orcs) dwelling under the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), which was ruled by the [Great Goblin](Great_Goblin.md).

Goblin-town was a series of tunnels and caverns, which went all the way through the mountains, with a "[back door](Goblin-gate.md)" near the [Eagle's Eyrie](../E/Eagle's_Eyrie.md) in [Wilderland](../R/Rhovanion.md), which served as a means of escape, and an access to the Wilderland.<sup id="cite_ref-pan_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pan-1">[1]</a></sup> A cave with a lake was deep beneath Goblin-town yet was connected to the Orcs' tunnels, with one passage leading to the "back door".<sup id="cite_ref-rid_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rid-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Long before the coming of the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md), [nameless things](../N/Nameless_things.md) were living in the caves.<sup id="cite_ref-rid_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rid-2">[2]</a></sup> Around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2480](../T/Third_Age_2480.md) the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) started making secret strongholds in the mountains blocking the passages towards [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md)<sup id="cite_ref-ta_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-3">[3]</a></sup> and expanded those caves, digging tunnels to connect them; without their knowledge they drove those slimy creatures into dark corners. One of those "original owners" was [Gollum](Gollum.md), who was living at [a nearby underground lake](Gollum's_lake.md), feeding himself on raw fish as well as Orcs that he killed.<sup id="cite_ref-rid_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rid-2">[2]</a></sup>

The main gate of the town was in one of the passes of the Misty Mountains, but by [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md)<sup id="cite_ref-ta_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-3">[3]</a></sup> travellers learned to avoid that dangerous road and the Orcs opened another under the safer [High Pass](../H/High_Pass.md): The [Front Porch](../F/Front_Porch.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-pan_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pan-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the journey to [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md), [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) rested in a cave and were captured by the Orcs. They were brought before the Great Goblin, who accused them of spying, and was enraged when he found that Thorin was carrying [Orcrist](../O/Orcrist.md), a sword the Great Goblin remembered from [Gondolin](Gondolin.md). With the help of [Gandalf](Gandalf.md), the Great Goblin was slain and Bilbo and the Dwarves escaped, pursued by a force of Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1977: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md" title="The Hobbit (1977 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1977 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Goblin-town is, like in the book, a set of tunnels and caverns.</dd></dl>

<b>1989: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(comic_book).md" title="The Hobbit (comic book)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (comic book)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Goblin-town is, like in the book, a set of tunnels and caverns.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game).md" title="The Hobbit (2003 video game)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (2003 video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Goblin-town is a giant Dwarven mining complex with many lifts, bridges and train carts to spice up the action of the level.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>One of the first new additions to the game after the initial release, Goblin-town proper is a large network of tunnels and caverns accessible from the eastern part of the <a href="../M/Misty_Mountains.md" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a> region. It is a labyrinthine network of hallways, bridges and caves with many side passages. Great Goblin&#39;s Throne Room and <a href="Gollum&#39;s_lake.md" title="Gollum&#39;s lake">Gollum&#39;s Cave</a> are two of the natural endpoints of the exploration. Goblin-town was revisited in the &#34;Seat of the Great Goblin&#34; group instance that greatly streamlined the layout with the focus on the Throne Room. Another version of Goblin-town is accessible from the eastern entrance in the <a href="../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md" title="Vales of Anduin">Vales of Anduin</a> region. This version also contains the Throne Room, with all three versions of it being slightly different from one another.</dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Goblin-town is portrayed as a giant system of caverns with sprawling wooden walkways. The goblins have a central hub of dwellings, with the Great Goblin&#39;s throne being in the center.</dd></dl>
