**Galdor** of the Havens was an [Elf](../E/Elf.md) who in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) lived in [Mithlond](../M/Mithlond.md) under [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md).

## History {#History}

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) he was sent in an errand to [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md). He was present in the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md) representing Círdan. He asked about proofs that the ring of [the halfling](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) was indeed the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md), and also inquired why [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) was not with them. It was [Gandalf](Gandalf.md) who answered his questions, narrating his story on how he discovered the One Ring, and Saruman's treachery.

In a later point Galdor mentioned that he knew about the figure of [Iarwain](../I/Iarwain.md), and he agreed with [Glorfindel](Glorfindel.md) that he had not the power to defy [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) and keep the Ring from him.

Then, he agreed with Gandalf that not even the [Sea](../S/Sea.md) could hide the Ring for ever. Galdor expected that the [Ringwraiths](../R/Ringwraiths.md) (who were recently defeated in [Bruinen](../B/Bruinen.md)) would soon return and [Gondor](Gondor.md) could not stop Sauron from coming all the way to [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) and reach the Sea. These were the last comments before Elrond decided that the only option is to destroy the Ring in [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In late writings, Tolkien speculated that this Galdor and the [Galdor](Galdor_of_the_Tree.md) of *[The Fall of Gondolin](<../T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md>)* may have been the same,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but he ultimately rejected the notion: if Galdor had remained behind, he would either have completely rejected the call of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), and thus have Fallen, or he would have had to leave and then return like [Glorfindel](Glorfindel.md) did, which would make him a far more powerful person than his appearance in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* suggests.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> For this reason Tolkien speculated that "Galdor" was simply a common [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name.
