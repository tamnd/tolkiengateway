**Geno Acevedo** (born [6 December](/wiki/6_December) [1964](/wiki/1964)) is an American make-up and digital effects artist, who worked on [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson)'s *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")* and *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>.

He had a cameo role in [*The Fellowship of the Ring*](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring") as a Dwarf lord. His appearance got cut short in the film itself, but did get used in the [The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> A second cameo, as a [Corsair](/wiki/Corsair), did appear in *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King")* as well as several cards for the aforementioned trading card game.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
