**Gorgol the Butcher** was an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), who was mentioned in *[The Lay of Leithian](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Leithian_Recommenced "The Lay of Leithian Recommenced")* as being hewn by [Beren](/wiki/Beren) in [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) during the recounting of Beren's deeds of valor as an outlaw.<sup id="cite_ref-Gorgol_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gorgol-1">[1]</a></sup>
