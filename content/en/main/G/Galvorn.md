**Galvorn** was a dark meteoritic iron that was devised by [Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l) from which he made resistant armour. Black and "shining like jet", it was hard as steel, but supple and malleable, proving resistant to all blades and darts.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-1">[1]</a></sup>

It's possible that galvorn was forgotten or lost with Eöl's death, and possibly also [Maeglin's](/wiki/Maeglin "Maeglin") death,<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-1">[1]</a></sup> as the metal was not mentioned in the [Second](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") and [Third](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [Ages](/wiki/Ages) of the [world](/wiki/Ambar "Ambar").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Galvorn* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "shining black",<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-3">[3]</a></sup> from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gal&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gal (page does not exist)">gal</a></i> ("light; shine, be bright") and *[morn](/wiki/Morn "Morn")* ("black, dark; night").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The original name of *galvorn* was *rodeöl*, the metal of Eöl,<sup id="cite_ref-Old_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Old-6">[6]</a></sup> possibly comprised of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Raud&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Raud (page does not exist)">raud</a></i> ("metal") and *Eöl*.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-3">[3]</a></sup> [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) changed this name many times, to *glindûr* ("dark glance"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> *targlîn*, *morlîn*, back to *glindûr*, and then *maeglin*. Finally *galvorn* was chosen.<sup id="cite_ref-Old_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Old-6">[6]</a></sup>
