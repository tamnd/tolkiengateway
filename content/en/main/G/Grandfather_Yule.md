**Grandfather Yule** is the father of [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) and the [Green Brother](Green_Brother.md). Like Father Christmas, he was named **Nicholas** after <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Nicholas" title="wikipedia:Saint Nicholas">Saint Nicholas</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
