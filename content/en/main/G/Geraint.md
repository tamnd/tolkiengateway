**Geraint** of <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Wales" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">West Wales</a> was one of the companions of [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine) on his journey to [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Geraint* is in [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh). It might be derived from the Latinized Greek *Gerontius* ("old man").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
