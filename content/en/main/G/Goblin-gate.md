The **Goblin-gate**, also known as **Back-door**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> by the [Goblins](/wiki/Orcs#Orcs_and_Goblins "Orcs"), was a stone door serving as the lower eastern passage to the [Goblin-town](/wiki/Goblin-town) in the eastern side of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-h6_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-h6-2">[2]</a></sup> It was located near the [Eagle's Eyrie](/wiki/Eagle%27s_Eyrie) and west of the [Carrock](/wiki/Carrock).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The gate was built by the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains) "ages" ago both as a means of escape from their tunnels and as a way to access and harass the [Wilderland](/wiki/Rhovanion "Rhovanion"). It was always guarded.<sup id="cite_ref-h6_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-h6-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) led [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) through that exit to escape the goblins. [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) also escaped from [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) by squeezing through that stone door and losing his buttons.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
