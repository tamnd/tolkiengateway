The **Goblin-gate**, also known as **Back-door**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> by the [Goblins](../O/Orcs.md#Orcs_and_Goblins), was a stone door serving as the lower eastern passage to the [Goblin-town](Goblin-town.md) in the eastern side of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-h6_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-h6-2">[2]</a></sup> It was located near the [Eagle's Eyrie](../E/Eagle's_Eyrie.md) and west of the [Carrock](../C/Carrock.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The gate was built by the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) "ages" ago both as a means of escape from their tunnels and as a way to access and harass the [Wilderland](../R/Rhovanion.md). It was always guarded.<sup id="cite_ref-h6_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-h6-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Gandalf](Gandalf.md) led [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) through that exit to escape the goblins. [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) also escaped from [Gollum](Gollum.md) by squeezing through that stone door and losing his buttons.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
