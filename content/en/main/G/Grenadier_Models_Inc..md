**Grenadier Models Inc.** was an American company which produced lead miniature figures for wargames and role-playing games with fantasy, science fiction and heroic themes between [1975](/wiki/1975) and [1996](/wiki/1996).

In [1985](/wiki/1985), Grenadier released the *Lord of the Rings Middle Earth* miniature series, a line of official figures for [ICE](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises "Iron Crown Enterprises")'s *[MERP](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing "Middle-earth Role Playing")*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the publisher<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> {#From_the_publisher[2]}

LORD OF THE RINGS™

Middle Earth — Legendary realm of Wizards and Hobbits, danger and wonder, shining heroism and dark evil. Experience J.R.R. Tolkien's magical world with Grenadier's new LORD OF THE RINGS MIDDLE EARTH™ miniature series. Authorized for use with I.C.E.'s MIDDLE EARTH™ ROLE PLAYING game, these incredibly detailed figures faithfully capture the true spirit of Tolkien's fantasy world. These figures are also compatible with many other fantasy role-playing games. Adventure in the greatest fantasy of all. Journey to MIDDLE EARTH™.
