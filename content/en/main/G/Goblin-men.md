**Goblin-men** were a people or race of creatures that were bred by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) in the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age). They were similar to the [Half-orcs](/wiki/Half-orcs), at least in their resistance to sunlight, an attribute that differentiated them from the full [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/b/b3/Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg/200px-Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="151" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/b/b3/Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg/300px-Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/b/b3/Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg/400px-Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg 2x" data-file-width="587" data-file-height="443"/></a><figcaption>Goblin Man - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i></figcaption></figure>

The exact difference between Goblin-men and Half-orcs was unclear, but [Gamling](/wiki/Gamling), a man of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), mentioned both of them during the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg) as creatures of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) that the foul craft of Saruman had bred when he observed that they did not shy back from the sun.<sup id="cite_ref-Gamling_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamling-1">[1]</a></sup> Both goblin-men and half-orcs fought alongside Orcs (the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) and [Wolf-riders](/wiki/Wolf-riders)) marching together with the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) in the rearguard at the Battle of the Hornburg.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) Saruman interbred [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and [Men](/wiki/Men) and produced Orc-men and Men-orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is possible that Goblin-men and Orc-men were the same creatures, since the word 'Goblin' was used as a translation of the [Westron](/wiki/Westron) word 'Orc'.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> At the [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen), an Orc-man slew prince [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred), the son and heir of [King Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden "Théoden") of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), before himself being slain by [Grimbold](/wiki/Grimbold).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
