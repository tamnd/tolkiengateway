**Goblin-men** were a people or race of creatures that were bred by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) in the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md). They were similar to the [Half-orcs](../H/Half-orcs.md), at least in their resistance to sunlight, an attribute that differentiated them from the full [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md).

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/D/Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/b3/Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg/200px-Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="151" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/b3/Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg/300px-Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/b3/Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg/400px-Decipher_-_Goblin_Man.jpg 2x" data-file-width="587" data-file-height="443"/></a><figcaption>Goblin Man - <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i></figcaption></figure>

The exact difference between Goblin-men and Half-orcs was unclear, but [Gamling](Gamling.md), a man of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), mentioned both of them during the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md) as creatures of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) that the foul craft of Saruman had bred when he observed that they did not shy back from the sun.<sup id="cite_ref-Gamling_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamling-1">[1]</a></sup> Both goblin-men and half-orcs fought alongside Orcs (the [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) and [Wolf-riders](../W/Wolf-riders.md)) marching together with the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md) in the rearguard at the Battle of the Hornburg.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) Saruman interbred [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md) and produced Orc-men and Men-orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is possible that Goblin-men and Orc-men were the same creatures, since the word 'Goblin' was used as a translation of the [Westron](../W/Westron.md) word 'Orc'.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> At the [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](../F/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md), an Orc-man slew prince [Théodred](../T/Théodred.md), the son and heir of [King Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), before himself being slain by [Grimbold](Grimbold.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
