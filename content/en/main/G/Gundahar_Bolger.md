**Gundahar Bolger** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Gundahar was the head of the [Bolger Family](../B/Bolger_Family~66348.md) after his father, [Gundolpho](Gundolpho_Bolger.md). He married [Dina Diggle](../D/Dina_Diggle.md), and had two children, [Adalgar](../A/Adalgar_Bolger.md) and [Adaldrida](../A/Adaldrida_Bolger.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Bolger family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gunther" title="wikipedia:Gunther">Gundahar</a></i> is an Old German name, derived from the elements *gunda* "war" and *heri* "army". This was the name of a semi-legendary 5th-century Burgundian king. He appears in the medieval German saga the *Nibelungenlied*.
