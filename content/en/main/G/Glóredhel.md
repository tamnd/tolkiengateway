**Glóredhel** ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [415](/wiki/First_Age_415 "First Age 415")-[472](/wiki/First_Age_472 "First Age 472")) was the eldest child and only daughter of [Hador](/wiki/Hador) and [Gildis](/wiki/Gildis), and she was the sister of [Galdor](</wiki/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min)> "Galdor (Lord of Dor-lómin)") and [Gundor](/wiki/Gundor).<sup id="cite_ref-WJwest_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJwest-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

At a great feast of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain), Glóredhel married [Haldir](</wiki/Haldir_(Chieftain_of_the_Haladin)> "Haldir (Chieftain of the Haladin)") and Galdor married [Hareth](/wiki/Hareth), both children of [Halmir](/wiki/Halmir), chief of the [House of Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Glóredhel was the mother of [Handir](/wiki/Handir), who was in turn the father of [Brandir the Lame](/wiki/Brandir "Brandir").<sup id="cite_ref-WJwest_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJwest-1">[1]</a></sup>

She died of grief after learning her husband had been killed in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad) ("Battle of Unnumbered Tears").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Glóredhel* means "[Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of the Golden Light" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), from *[glaur](/wiki/Glaur "Glaur")-* ("holy golden light") + *[edhel](/wiki/Edhel "Edhel")* ("Elf"). She was so named because of her radiant blonde hair and height, which was comparable to an Elf.
