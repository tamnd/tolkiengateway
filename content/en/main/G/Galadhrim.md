The **Galadhrim** or **Tree-people** were [Elves](../E/Elves.md), mostly [Silvan](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) in origin but also of [Nandorin](../N/Nandorin.md) and [Avarin](../A/Avarin.md) descent, that inhabited the woods of [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).

## History {#History}

### First Age {#First_Age}

Those among the [Telerin Elves](../T/Teleri.md) who abandoned the [Great Journey](Great_Journey.md) of the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) on the eastern side of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) became known as the [Nandor](../N/Nandor.md). Many of these settled permanently in the forests to either side of the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md), and they grew in number throughout the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-POME_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-POME-1">[1]</a></sup> The folk of [Lórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) on the west bank of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) were neighbours and kin to those of [Eryn Galen](../E/Eryn_Galen.md) on the east bank.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

Following the destruction of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) and the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), most of the [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) exiles and remnants of the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) retreated to [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md). The [Valar](../V/Valar.md) invited these [Elves](../E/Elves.md) to [Aman](../A/Aman.md), but [Amdír](../A/Amdír.md), among many others, was unwilling and remained in Lindon.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin-2">[2]</a></sup> Amdír, along with his son [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md), established himself in [Lórinand](../L/Lórinand.md), where he would lead the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AaN_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AaN-3">[3]</a></sup> He took over as their King, while another Sinda, [Oropher](../O/Oropher.md), became King of [Greenwood the Great](../M/Mirkwood.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [Sack of Eregion](../S/Sack_of_Eregion.md), Amroth led a force of Galadhrim and, together with [Dwarves of Khazad-dûm](../D/Dwarves_of_Khazad-dûm.md), attacked [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s force from the rear, saving [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) in the process. The survivors of the attack on [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md), including many [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) and [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), escaped into [Khazad-dûm](../K/Khazad-dûm.md) and were granted passage by their friends the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md). Eventually coming through the [Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) to the woodlands that would long afterwards be called [Lórien](../L/Lórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Galadriel](Galadriel.md) and [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) departed from [Imladris](../R/Rivendell.md) and went to the sparsely inhabited lands between the mouth of the [Gwathló](Gwathló.md) and [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-4">[4]</a></sup> There they dwelt in [Belfalas](../B/Belfalas.md), at [Edhellond](../E/Edhellond.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-4">[4]</a></sup> near the place that was afterwards called [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md); and their company was swelled by [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) from [Lórinand](../L/Lórinand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-4">[4]</a></sup>

Under Amdir's leadership the people of Lórien prospered and their numbers grew. He later led an army of Galadhrim to the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md), where most of them, including Amdír himself, were slain in the [Battle of Dagorlad](../B/Battle_of_Dagorlad.md).

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

The Galadhrim were now ruled by [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md), the son of Amdír. Around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1409](../T/Third_Age_1409.md), some Galadhrim passed over the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) to join forces with [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) under [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) against [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md). Along with assistance with the [Elves of Lindon](../E/Elves_of_Lindon.md), they were successful in subduing the witch-kingdom for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1980](../T/Third_Age_1980.md) with the freeing of [Durin's Bane](../D/Durin's_Bane.md) in [Moria](../M/Moria.md), many of the Galadhrim fled south to [Edhellond](../E/Edhellond.md) and sailed over Sea to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). Amroth grew weary of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) and wished to sail, but he drowned in the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AaN_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AaN-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ta_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-6">[6]</a></sup>

In her wisdom Galadriel saw that Lórien would be a stronghold and a point of power to prevent the [Shadow](../S/Shadow.md) from crossing the Anduin, for the Galadhrim were hardy and valiant. However, they needed a ruler of greater strength and wisdom than they possessed. This is why Galadriel and [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) took up their permanent abode in Lórien and took charge of its governing. Under the leadership of Galadriel and Celeborn, the Galadhrim prospered and their skill grew.

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) the valour of the Galadhrim (with the power of Galadriel) defended their home against [orcs](../O/Orcs.md), who [attacked Lothlórien](../A/Assaults_on_Lothlórien.md) and wrought destruction on its border. Soon thereafter, [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) met his demise, and the Galadhrim with many boats crossed the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) to [attack Dol Guldur](../F/Fall_of_Dol_Guldur.md), [Galadriel](Galadriel.md) herself tearing down its walls.

### Fourth Age {#Fourth_Age}

After the departure of Galadriel to Aman and of Celeborn to [Imladris](../I/Imladris.md), only few of the Galadhrim remained in Lothlórien; many may have moved to [East Lórien](../E/East_Lórien.md) in [Greenwood](Greenwood.md) where the Silvan Elves remained untroubled during the early [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Culture {#Culture}

The Galadhrim prospered and were instructed under their Sindarin rulers, and afterwards by their [Lord and Lady](../L/Lord_and_Lady_of_Galadhrim.md). They became proficient in making [Lembas](../L/Lembas.md), [Elven rope](../E/Elven_rope~74291.md), Elven-cloaks, and many other crafts. They lived in [telain](../T/Telain.md) built in the trees.

They spoke [Silvan Elvish](../S/Silvan_Elvish.md) and a dialect of Sindarin with an "accent".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Some, like [Haldir](../H/Haldir.md), but not all, could speak [Westron](../W/Westron.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Galadhrim* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name meaning "Tree-people". The name consists of *[galadh](Galadh.md)* ("tree") + *[rim(b)](../R/Rim.md)* ("great number").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

In the first edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, *Galadhrim* was spelt *Galadrim*. In later editions, this was amended to the former, which [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) had decided was the correct Sindarin form.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
