The **Galadhrim** or **Tree-people** were [Elves](/wiki/Elves), mostly [Silvan](/wiki/Silvan_Elves "Silvan Elves") in origin but also of [Nandorin](/wiki/Nandorin) and [Avarin](/wiki/Avarin) descent, that inhabited the woods of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).

## History {#History}

### First Age {#First_Age}

Those among the [Telerin Elves](/wiki/Teleri "Teleri") who abandoned the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey) of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) on the eastern side of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) became known as the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor). Many of these settled permanently in the forests to either side of the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin), and they grew in number throughout the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-POME_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-POME-1">[1]</a></sup> The folk of [Lórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien") on the west bank of the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) were neighbours and kin to those of [Eryn Galen](/wiki/Eryn_Galen) on the east bank.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

Following the destruction of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), most of the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") exiles and remnants of the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) retreated to [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon). The [Valar](/wiki/Valar) invited these [Elves](/wiki/Elves) to [Aman](/wiki/Aman), but [Amdír](/wiki/Amd%C3%ADr), among many others, was unwilling and remained in Lindon.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin-2">[2]</a></sup> Amdír, along with his son [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth), established himself in [Lórinand](/wiki/L%C3%B3rinand), where he would lead the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-AaN_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AaN-3">[3]</a></sup> He took over as their King, while another Sinda, [Oropher](/wiki/Oropher), became King of [Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Mirkwood "Mirkwood").<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [Sack of Eregion](/wiki/Sack_of_Eregion), Amroth led a force of Galadhrim and, together with [Dwarves of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm), attacked [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s force from the rear, saving [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) in the process. The survivors of the attack on [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion), including many [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) and [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar), escaped into [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm) and were granted passage by their friends the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). Eventually coming through the [Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains "Misty Mountains") to the woodlands that would long afterwards be called [Lórien](/wiki/L%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) departed from [Imladris](/wiki/Rivendell "Rivendell") and went to the sparsely inhabited lands between the mouth of the [Gwathló](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3) and [Ethir Anduin](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-4">[4]</a></sup> There they dwelt in [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas), at [Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond),<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-4">[4]</a></sup> near the place that was afterwards called [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth); and their company was swelled by [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves) from [Lórinand](/wiki/L%C3%B3rinand).<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-4">[4]</a></sup>

Under Amdir's leadership the people of Lórien prospered and their numbers grew. He later led an army of Galadhrim to the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance), where most of them, including Amdír himself, were slain in the [Battle of Dagorlad](/wiki/Battle_of_Dagorlad).

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

The Galadhrim were now ruled by [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth), the son of Amdír. Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1409](/wiki/Third_Age_1409 "Third Age 1409"), some Galadhrim passed over the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) to join forces with [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) under [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) against [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar). Along with assistance with the [Elves of Lindon](/wiki/Elves_of_Lindon), they were successful in subduing the witch-kingdom for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1980](/wiki/Third_Age_1980 "Third Age 1980") with the freeing of [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane) in [Moria](/wiki/Moria), many of the Galadhrim fled south to [Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond) and sailed over Sea to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). Amroth grew weary of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and wished to sail, but he drowned in the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas).<sup id="cite_ref-AaN_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AaN-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ta_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-6">[6]</a></sup>

In her wisdom Galadriel saw that Lórien would be a stronghold and a point of power to prevent the [Shadow](/wiki/Shadow) from crossing the Anduin, for the Galadhrim were hardy and valiant. However, they needed a ruler of greater strength and wisdom than they possessed. This is why Galadriel and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) took up their permanent abode in Lórien and took charge of its governing. Under the leadership of Galadriel and Celeborn, the Galadhrim prospered and their skill grew.

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) the valour of the Galadhrim (with the power of Galadriel) defended their home against [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs"), who [attacked Lothlórien](/wiki/Assaults_on_Lothl%C3%B3rien "Assaults on Lothlórien") and wrought destruction on its border. Soon thereafter, [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) met his demise, and the Galadhrim with many boats crossed the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) to [attack Dol Guldur](/wiki/Fall_of_Dol_Guldur "Fall of Dol Guldur"), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) herself tearing down its walls.

### Fourth Age {#Fourth_Age}

After the departure of Galadriel to Aman and of Celeborn to [Imladris](/wiki/Imladris), only few of the Galadhrim remained in Lothlórien; many may have moved to [East Lórien](/wiki/East_L%C3%B3rien) in [Greenwood](/wiki/Greenwood) where the Silvan Elves remained untroubled during the early [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Culture {#Culture}

The Galadhrim prospered and were instructed under their Sindarin rulers, and afterwards by their [Lord and Lady](/wiki/Lord_and_Lady_of_Galadhrim "Lord and Lady of Galadhrim"). They became proficient in making [Lembas](/wiki/Lembas), [Elven rope](/wiki/Elven_rope), Elven-cloaks, and many other crafts. They lived in [telain](/wiki/Telain "Telain") built in the trees.

They spoke [Silvan Elvish](/wiki/Silvan_Elvish) and a dialect of Sindarin with an "accent".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Some, like [Haldir](/wiki/Haldir), but not all, could speak [Westron](/wiki/Westron).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Galadhrim* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "Tree-people". The name consists of *[galadh](/wiki/Galadh "Galadh")* ("tree") + *[rim(b)](/wiki/Rim "Rim")* ("great number").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

In the first edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, *Galadhrim* was spelt *Galadrim*. In later editions, this was amended to the former, which [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") had decided was the correct Sindarin form.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
