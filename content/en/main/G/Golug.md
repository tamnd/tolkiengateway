**Golug** was a word from a language of the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), whose only recorded use was during the later [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), used to describe the Deep Elves (the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor)). Its origins are unknown, but it may be related to the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for this same people, [Golodhrim](/wiki/Golodhrim).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2012-14: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Golug is used in <a href="/wiki/Orkish" title="Orkish">Gundabad orkish</a>. Much like in the books, it means &#34;elf&#34;, but unlike in the books, a female form (golgi) and a plural (golgai) also exist.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is also used in <a href="/wiki/Black_Speech" title="Black Speech">Black Speech</a>.</dd></dl>
