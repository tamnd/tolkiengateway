The **Gap of Rohan**, once known as **Gap of Calenardhon** was the opening between the mountain ranges of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) and the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

Through the Gap flowed the river [Isen](../I/Isen.md), which formed the effective western boundary of the region [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md), later Kingdom of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). The ancient [North-South Road](../N/North-South_Road.md) which connected the realms of [Gondor](Gondor.md) and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) of old ran through the Gap, crossing the Isen at the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> Access to the Gap was controlled by the fortress of Angrenost, or [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md), which in the later [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) was controlled by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md).

The triangle between the Isen and Adorn rivers and the White Mountains was a contested area between the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) and the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Both [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and the [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> passed westward through the Gap in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md).

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) chose not to travel through the gap because they had learned Saruman was in league with [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), and instead passed through [Moria](../M/Moria.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Rohirrim fought two battles against the Dunlendings and the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) of Saruman in the gap, during the first of which [Théodred](../T/Théodred.md), son of King [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md), was mortally wounded.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
