**Glǽmscrafu** (the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name for the [Glittering Caves](/wiki/Glittering_Caves)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) is a linguistic website authored by Benjamin Babut and Bertrand Bellet, hosted by Cédric Fockeu on JRRVF and launched in [2006](/wiki/2006). It is dedicated especially to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonoesthetics" title="wikipedia:Phonoesthetics">phonoesthetics</a> of [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien)'s constructed [languages](/wiki/Languages "Languages").

It provides authentic texts written by Tolkien in his constructed languages, [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)/[Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)/[Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic), [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) and others, with sound samples.

The database also provides historical excerpts and sound samples from real-life languages whose philology or phonotactics influenced Tolkien's languages or [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"). There are samples of [Finnish](/wiki/Finnish) (the main source of Quenya), [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh) (the main source of Sindarin), [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) (exemplified as the [Rohan language](/wiki/Rohan_language)), [Old Norse](/wiki/Old_Norse) (exemplified the names of the [Longbeards](/wiki/Longbeards), including a sample of the *["Catalogue of Dwarves"](/wiki/Voluspa "Voluspa")*), [Gothic](/wiki/Gothic) (including a sample of *[Bagmē Blōma](/wiki/Bagm%C4%93_Bl%C5%8Dma)*) and [Latin](/wiki/Latin).

Since [25 March](/wiki/25_March) [2016](/wiki/2016), the site contains "Glǽmscribe", a feature transriber of texts into [Sarati](/wiki/Sarati), [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar), [Gothic](/wiki/Gothic) or [Runes](/wiki/Runes)/[Cirth](/wiki/Cirth).
