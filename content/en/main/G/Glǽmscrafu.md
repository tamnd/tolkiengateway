**Glǽmscrafu** (the [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name for the [Glittering Caves](Glittering_Caves.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) is a linguistic website authored by Benjamin Babut and Bertrand Bellet, hosted by Cédric Fockeu on JRRVF and launched in [2006](../_/2006.md). It is dedicated especially to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonoesthetics" title="wikipedia:Phonoesthetics">phonoesthetics</a> of [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md)'s constructed [languages](../L/Languages.md).

It provides authentic texts written by Tolkien in his constructed languages, [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md)/[Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)/[Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md), [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md), [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md) and others, with sound samples.

The database also provides historical excerpts and sound samples from real-life languages whose philology or phonotactics influenced Tolkien's languages or [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md). There are samples of [Finnish](../F/Finnish.md) (the main source of Quenya), [Welsh](../W/Welsh.md) (the main source of Sindarin), [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) (exemplified as the [Rohan language](../R/Rohan_language.md)), [Old Norse](../O/Old_Norse.md) (exemplified the names of the [Longbeards](../L/Longbeards.md), including a sample of the *["Catalogue of Dwarves"](../V/Voluspa.md)*), [Gothic](Gothic.md) (including a sample of *[Bagmē Blōma](../B/Bagmē_Blōma.md)*) and [Latin](../L/Latin.md).

Since [25 March](../_/25_March.md) [2016](../_/2016.md), the site contains "Glǽmscribe", a feature transriber of texts into [Sarati](../S/Sarati.md), [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md), [Gothic](Gothic.md) or [Runes](../R/Runes.md)/[Cirth](../C/Cirth.md).
