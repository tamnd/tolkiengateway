**Gilfanon a·Davrobel** was one of the oldest [Elves](../E/Elves.md) on [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md). It was he who told [Eriol](../E/Eriol.md) the tale of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) and the [Moon](../M/Moon.md) in the [Cottage of Lost Play](../C/Cottage_of_Lost_Play.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Sun_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sun-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:174 ff</sup>

Gilfanon lived in the [House of the Hundred Chimneys](../H/House_of_the_Hundred_Chimneys.md) in [Tavrobel](../T/Tavrobel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Sun_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sun-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:175</sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

His earlier name was <i><b>Ailios</b></i>, that was mostly amended in the manuscript of the Tale of the Sun and Moon,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but remains in the (earlier) tales given in *[The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

According to an outline, Gilfanon bids Eriol to write down all the tales that he has heard in Tol Eressëa before drinking [limpë](../L/Limpë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Christopher explains that this and all the details about Gilfanon (like being one of the [Noldoli](../N/Noldoli.md)) match with [Pengolodh](../P/Pengolodh.md), so Gilfanon would be his literary origin.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In a [prose fragment following the Lost Tales](../P/Prose_Fragments_Following_the_Lost_Tales.md), there is a [Gilfanon](<Gilfanon_(elf_of_Alqaluntë).md>) mentioned as an Elf of [Alqaluntë](../A/Alqaluntë.md), but Christopher is certain that he cannot be the Gilfanon of [Tavrobel](../T/Tavrobel.md), so probably Tolkien had changed his name.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
