The **Gardner family**, also collectively known as the **Gardners of [the Hill](/wiki/Hobbiton_Hill "Hobbiton Hill")**, was the family name taken by the descendants of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton) in honour of Sam's replanting of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-long_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-long-1">[1]</a></sup>

The family, a branch of which were the [Fairbairns of the Towers](/wiki/Fairbairn_family "Fairbairn family"), became an important and influential family of [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits) in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
