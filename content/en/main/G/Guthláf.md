**Guthláf** was the banner-bearer of King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

## History {#History}

He rode to the relief of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) with his Lord in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), and there fought in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), where he was slain. He fell with the banner in his hand, and it had to be taken from him after death to be given to [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer). He also earned himself a mention in the *[Song of the Mounds of Mundburg](/wiki/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *Guþláf* means "survivor of the battle" (consisting of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/gu%C3%BE#Old_English" title="wiktionary:guþ">gūþ</a></i> + <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/laf#Old_English" title="wiktionary:laf">lāf</a></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It literally means "war-remnant".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
