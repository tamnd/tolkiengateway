**Gorbag** was the captain of the [Uruks](../U/Uruk-hai.md) of [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md). He served the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) who dwelt in the Dead City.

## History {#History}

During the peak of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the [Silent Watchers](../S/Silent_Watchers.md) exhibited unease because a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md), [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), had passed by them. Gorbag and a patrol then, left Minas Morgul and were sent to [Cirith Ungol](../C/Cirith_Ungol.md) where [Shagrat](../S/Shagrat.md) was in command.<sup id="cite_ref-Choices_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Choices-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [13 March](../_/13_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) Gorbag and Shagrat came across Frodo,<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> now paralyzed by [Shelob](../S/Shelob.md). They ordered Frodo carried back into the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](../T/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md) as their prisoner.

During their return to the tower [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), who was wearing the [One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md), overheard them talking. It quickly became apparent that Gorbag was the more observant of the two, and noticed that the paralyzed halfling's cords were cut, meaning that he had a companion: Shagrat said that the Nazgûl were uneasy and that something nearly slipped, and Gorbag corrected him saying that something *had* slipped; Shagrat pointed out that Shelob got the halfling, but Gorbag pointed out that he was not alone, as someone else cut the cords from Frodo. Shagrat dismissed Gorbag's reasoning by claiming that he always took a gloomy view. Shagrat, who had been in the tower longer, was able to tell Gorbag that Frodo was not dead.<sup id="cite_ref-Choices_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Choices-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [14 March](../_/14_March.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> the two Orc leaders stripped Frodo of his possessions. Upon discovering Frodo's [*mithril* coat](../M/Mithril.md#The_Mithril_Coat) Gorbag tried to keep it, which provoked a fight between the underlings of the two leaders. Gorbag knifed Shagrat, who in return attempted to throttle him. Later he attempted to stab Shagrat with a broken spear but Shagrat, hearing him, then stabbed him in the throat and trampled on his body.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Later, he is killed by <a href="../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md" title="Samwise Gamgee">Samwise Gamgee</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Gorbag appears as a boss at the end of the &#34;Cirith Ungol&#34; level. In the game, Gorbag knocks down Shagrat and then Sam must defeat him by stunning him with pikes.</dd></dl>
