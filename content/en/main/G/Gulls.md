**Gulls** or **mews** were birds found on all shores in [Arda](/wiki/Arda).

## History {#History}

Gulls were common in all sea-bordering lands (perhaps especially abundant on the cape of [Bar-in-Mŷl](/wiki/Ras_Mewrim "Ras Mewrim"), the "Home of the Gulls", in the land of the [Falas](/wiki/Falas)<sup id="cite_ref-QaE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QaE-1">[1]</a></sup>), and for that, they were often attributed to [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) that dwelt in the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Havens&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Havens (page does not exist)">Havens</a> were very fond of them, and their mewing welcomed [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) to their land.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> They were also known in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor)<sup id="cite_ref-AaE_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AaE-4">[4]</a></sup> and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-tLD_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tLD-5">[5]</a></sup>

Their association with the sea caused a great sense of longing for the journey to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) among [Elves](/wiki/Elves) that were not familiar with their sound. One of those was [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas).<sup id="cite_ref-tLD_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tLD-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")*, one [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) and one [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for "gull" appear, both from the same root: *[maew](/wiki/Maew "Maew")* and *[maiwë](/wiki/Maiw%C3%AB "Maiwë")*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> A later recorded [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word is *[mŷl](/wiki/M%C5%B7l "Mŷl")*,<sup id="cite_ref-QaE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QaE-1">[1]</a></sup> which replaced *gwael*.<sup id="cite_ref-QaE_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QaE-1">[1]</a></sup>

In manuscripts related to the *Etymologies* Tolkien also experimented with the Noldorin form *cuen*, glossed as "small gull, petrel".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the [house of the Wing](/wiki/House_of_the_Wing "House of the Wing") wore [swan](/wiki/Swans "Swans") or gull-like feathers on their helmets.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [1937](/wiki/1937) *[Quenta Silmarillion](</wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road)> "Quenta Silmarillion (Lost Road)")*, the [Lindar](/wiki/Lindar) were at one point considered the "Lords of the Gulls" among many other names.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other fiction {#Other_fiction}

In the story *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*, a seagull named Mew brings the dog Rover to the moon. On their way, Mew stops to visit other sea-birds, among them the "*oldest and most important of all the Blackbacked Gulls*".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> <span></span>
