**Gulls** or **mews** were birds found on all shores in [Arda](../A/Arda.md).

## History {#History}

Gulls were common in all sea-bordering lands (perhaps especially abundant on the cape of [Bar-in-Mŷl](../R/Ras_Mewrim.md), the "Home of the Gulls", in the land of the [Falas](../F/Falas.md)<sup id="cite_ref-QaE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QaE-1">[1]</a></sup>), and for that, they were often attributed to [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) that dwelt in the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Havens&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Havens (page does not exist)">Havens</a> were very fond of them, and their mewing welcomed [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) to their land.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> They were also known in [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-AaE_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AaE-4">[4]</a></sup> and [Gondor](Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-tLD_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tLD-5">[5]</a></sup>

Their association with the sea caused a great sense of longing for the journey to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) among [Elves](../E/Elves.md) that were not familiar with their sound. One of those was [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-tLD_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tLD-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)*, one [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) and one [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word for "gull" appear, both from the same root: *[maew](../M/Maew.md)* and *[maiwë](../M/Maiwë.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> A later recorded [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word is *[mŷl](../M/Mŷl.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-QaE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QaE-1">[1]</a></sup> which replaced *gwael*.<sup id="cite_ref-QaE_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QaE-1">[1]</a></sup>

In manuscripts related to the *Etymologies* Tolkien also experimented with the Noldorin form *cuen*, glossed as "small gull, petrel".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, the [house of the Wing](../H/House_of_the_Wing.md) wore [swan](../S/Swans.md) or gull-like feathers on their helmets.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [1937](../_/1937.md) *[Quenta Silmarillion](<../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road).md>)*, the [Lindar](../L/Lindar.md) were at one point considered the "Lords of the Gulls" among many other names.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other fiction {#Other_fiction}

In the story *[Roverandom](../R/Roverandom.md)*, a seagull named Mew brings the dog Rover to the moon. On their way, Mew stops to visit other sea-birds, among them the "*oldest and most important of all the Blackbacked Gulls*".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> <span></span>
