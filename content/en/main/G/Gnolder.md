**Gnolder** is a black metal and dungeon synth band from Gislaved, Jönköping, Sweden.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its songs reference [Balin](../B/Balin.md), [Glorfindel](Glorfindel.md), [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).

## Members {#Members}

- Dole – drums
- Fangals – guitars, vocals
- Petronius – keyboards
