**Gnolder** is a black metal and dungeon synth band from Gislaved, Jönköping, Sweden.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its songs reference [Balin](/wiki/Balin), [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel), [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).

## Members {#Members}

- Dole – drums
- Fangals – guitars, vocals
- Petronius – keyboards
