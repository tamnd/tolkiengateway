**Grór** was a [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") and a significant leader among the [people of Durin](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk "Durin's Folk").

## Life {#Life}

### Birth and early Life {#Birth_and_early_Life}

He was born into the [House of Durin](/wiki/House_of_Durin) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2563](/wiki/Third_Age_2563 "Third Age 2563") during its last years in the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains). He was the youngest of the three sons of King [Dáin I](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I), who ruled [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk).<sup id="cite_ref-Line_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-1">[1]</a></sup>

When Grór was seven years old, in [2570](/wiki/Third_Age_2570 "Third Age 2570") dragons began [afflicting the dwarves in the north](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons "War of the Dwarves and Dragons"). Nineteen years later, in [2589](/wiki/Third_Age_2589 "Third Age 2589") his father King [Dáin I](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I) was slain by a [cold-drake](/wiki/Cold-drakes "Cold-drakes") outside [his hall](/wiki/D%C3%A1in%27s_Halls "Dáin's Halls") in the Grey Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> A short time afterwards most of Durin's Folk left the Grey Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Grór_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grór-3">[3]</a></sup> In [2590](/wiki/Third_Age_2590 "Third Age 2590")<sup id="cite_ref-TA2590_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2590-4">[4]</a></sup> Grór left the Grey Mountains with many followers and established a colony in the Iron Hills<sup id="cite_ref-Grór_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grór-3">[3]</a></sup> becoming the Lord of the Iron Hills. In the same year,<sup id="cite_ref-TA2590_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2590-4">[4]</a></sup> his brother [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r), the oldest son<sup id="cite_ref-Line_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-1">[1]</a></sup> and heir of Dáin, and the rest of the Dwarves of the Grey Mountains moved back to the Lonely Mountain<sup id="cite_ref-Thrór_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thrór-5">[5]</a></sup>.

### Lord of the Iron Hills, and later life {#Lord_of_the_Iron_Hills,_and_later_life}

Over the next few centuries a large quantity of ore was traded between the Dwarves of the Lonely Mountain and the Dwarves of the Iron Hills,<sup id="cite_ref-Thrór_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thrór-5">[5]</a></sup> until the Dragon [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) [destroyed](/wiki/Sack_of_Erebor "Sack of Erebor") the Kingdom under the Mountain and scattered the Longbeards who dwelt there<sup id="cite_ref-Thrór_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thrór-5">[5]</a></sup> in [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>. Most of the survivors subsequently went to their kinsmen in the Iron Hills.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In [2799](/wiki/Third_Age_2799 "Third Age 2799")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the final battle of the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs) [occurred in the Dimrill Dale](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar "Battle of Azanulbizar"). Grór's son [Náin](</wiki/N%C3%A1in_(son_of_Gr%C3%B3r)> "Náin (son of Grór)"), accompanied by his son [Dáin](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot") led reinforcements from the Iron Hills to the battle, but was killed by the master of the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of [Moria](/wiki/Moria), [Azog](/wiki/Azog), before the [East Gate](/wiki/Great_Gates "Great Gates") of Moria. The then still quite young Dáin killed Azog and the dwarves won the battle. With the war over, Dáin led his people home to the Iron Hills.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Six years later, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2805](/wiki/Third_Age_2805 "Third Age 2805"), Grór died at 241 years of age and his grandson Dáin became Lord of the Iron Hills.

Many years later, in [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") when Thrór's last descendant, [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin), was killed in the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies), Dáin II Ironfoot became the new [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain) and [King of the Longbeards](/wiki/Kings_of_Durin%27s_Folk "Kings of Durin's Folk"),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> a title that remained with the descendants of Grór<sup id="cite_ref-Line_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-1">[1]</a></sup>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) has suggested that *Grór* (like *[Gróin](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3in#Etymology)*) is derived from [Old Norse](/wiki/Old_Norse) *gróa* ("to grow"), thus meaning "Growing-one".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
