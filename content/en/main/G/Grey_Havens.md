The **Grey Havens**, also known as **Mithlond**, were seaports on both sides of the end of the [Gulf of Lune](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup> near the mouth of the River [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) in western [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

Founded by the [Elves of Lindon](/wiki/Elves_of_Lindon) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1](/wiki/Second_Age_1 "Second Age 1"),<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> the Grey Havens were known for their good harbourage and many ships; these were used by any of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) to leave Middle-earth for [Eressëa](/wiki/Eress%C3%ABa) or [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) was Lord of the Havens from its foundation and into the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [600](/wiki/Second_Age_600 "Second Age 600"), *[Entulessë](/wiki/Entuless%C3%AB)*, a [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") ship headed by [Captain](/wiki/Captain_of_the_King%27s_Ships "Captain of the King's Ships") [Vëantur](/wiki/V%C3%ABantur), arrived in Mithlond and it was there that the Númenóreans first landed in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-Island_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Island-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the [War of the Elves and Sauron](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron), the [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) was held in desperate defence of the Havens after [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) had conquered almost all of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador). The beleaguered defenders were saved with the arrival of [Tar-Minastir](/wiki/Tar-Minastir)'s fleet and Sauron's forces were driven back.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

After the fall of [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) (during the time of the [Kings of Arnor](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor)), the Grey Havens was still home to the largest population of [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), aside from [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador). [Cirdan](/wiki/Cirdan) remained the Lord of the Havens and ruled the [Elves of Lindon](/wiki/Elves_of_Lindon) from Mithlond.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-eriador_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-7">[7]</a></sup>

About [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1000](/wiki/Third_Age_1000 "Third Age 1000"), the [Istari](/wiki/Istari) or Wizards arrived in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) at the Grey Havens. Círdan, upon meeting [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), gave him [Narya](/wiki/Narya), the Ring of Fire.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-8">[8]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975"), Prince [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) brought a fleet of [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) to the Grey Havens to aid [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) [against](/wiki/Angmar_War "Angmar War") [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar). The fleet was so massive that the ships of Gondor filled the Grey Havens, as well as [Forlond](/wiki/Forlond) and <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Harlond" title="Harlond">Harlond</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-9">[9]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-8">[8]</a></sup> Círdan sent [Galdor of the Havens](/wiki/Galdor_of_the_Havens) "on an errand" to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and he became Círdan's representative at the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Later history {#Later_history}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Departure_at_the_Grey_Havens.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/2/22/Ted_Nasmith_-_Departure_at_the_Grey_Havens.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Departure_at_the_Grey_Havens.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="210" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/2/22/Ted_Nasmith_-_Departure_at_the_Grey_Havens.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Departure_at_the_Grey_Havens.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/2/22/Ted_Nasmith_-_Departure_at_the_Grey_Havens.jpg 2x" data-file-width="530" data-file-height="371"/></a><figcaption>Departure at the Grey Havens by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

Following the defeat of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and the crowning of King [Aragorn Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the last of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) set sail from the Grey Havens and left Middle-earth for [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-4">[4]</a></sup> Latest of all were the [Last Riding of the Keepers of the Rings](/wiki/Last_Riding_of_the_Keepers_of_the_Rings); [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel). They were accompanied by [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), who were given the honour of taking the [Straight Road](/wiki/Straight_Road) because they had both been [Ring-bearers](/wiki/Ring-bearers).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Years later, [Fairbairn](/wiki/Fairbairn_Family "Fairbairn Family") family tradition held that [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [61](/wiki/Fourth_Age_61 "Fourth Age 61") also sailed [West](/wiki/Aman "Aman") from the Grey Havens.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-12">[12]</a></sup>

The Grey Havens were maintained into the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) (for the last mentioning of Mithlond was [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)'s departure <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/West" title="West">West</a> in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [61](/wiki/Fourth_Age_61 "Fourth Age 61")),<sup id="cite_ref-Later_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-12">[12]</a></sup> but it was recognized that eventually, its purpose would reach an end when no more Elves wished to cross the [Sea](/wiki/Sea). At that time, Círdan would abandon the Grey Havens and finally travel the [Straight Road](/wiki/Straight_Road) himself. At some unknown date, [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) eventually sought the Grey Havens and with Círdan, they took the [Last Ship](/wiki/Last_Ship) together.<sup id="cite_ref-prologue_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prologue-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppTale_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppTale-14">[14]</a></sup>

*Mithlond* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Grey Haven", from *[mith](/wiki/Mith "Mith")* ("grey") + *[lond](/wiki/Lond "Lond")* ("Haven").<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth) points out that the Grey Havens are located around <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clevedon" title="wikipedia:Clevedon">Clevedon</a> if the river [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) is superimposed on the river <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Severn" title="wikipedia:River Severn">Severn</a>. J.R.R. Tolkien and his wife Edith spent their honeymoon in Clevedon.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> Clevedon is located on the southern shore of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severn_Estuary" title="wikipedia:Severn Estuary">Severn Estuary</a> roughly opposite of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff" title="wikipedia:Cardiff">Cardiff</a>, which is located on the northern shore of the Severn Estuary.
