The **Grey Havens**, also known as **Mithlond**, were seaports on both sides of the end of the [Gulf of Lune](Gulf_of_Lune.md)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup> near the mouth of the River [Lhûn](../L/Lhûn.md) in western [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

Founded by the [Elves of Lindon](../E/Elves_of_Lindon.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1](../S/Second_Age_1.md),<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> the Grey Havens were known for their good harbourage and many ships; these were used by any of the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) to leave Middle-earth for [Eressëa](../E/Eressëa.md) or [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) was Lord of the Havens from its foundation and into the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [600](../S/Second_Age_600.md), *[Entulessë](../E/Entulessë.md)*, a [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) ship headed by [Captain](../C/Captain_of_the_King's_Ships.md) [Vëantur](../V/Vëantur.md), arrived in Mithlond and it was there that the Númenóreans first landed in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Island_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Island-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the [War of the Elves and Sauron](../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md), the [Lhûn](../L/Lhûn.md) was held in desperate defence of the Havens after [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) had conquered almost all of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md). The beleaguered defenders were saved with the arrival of [Tar-Minastir](../T/Tar-Minastir.md)'s fleet and Sauron's forces were driven back.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

After the fall of [Gil-galad](Gil-galad.md) (during the time of the [Kings of Arnor](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md)), the Grey Havens was still home to the largest population of [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), aside from [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md). [Cirdan](../C/Cirdan.md) remained the Lord of the Havens and ruled the [Elves of Lindon](../E/Elves_of_Lindon.md) from Mithlond.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-eriador_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-7">[7]</a></sup>

About [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1000](../T/Third_Age_1000.md), the [Istari](../I/Istari.md) or Wizards arrived in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) at the Grey Havens. Círdan, upon meeting [Gandalf](Gandalf.md), gave him [Narya](../N/Narya.md), the Ring of Fire.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-8">[8]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md), Prince [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md) of [Gondor](Gondor.md) brought a fleet of [Gondorians](Gondorians.md) to the Grey Havens to aid [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md) [against](../A/Angmar_War.md) [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md). The fleet was so massive that the ships of Gondor filled the Grey Havens, as well as [Forlond](../F/Forlond.md) and <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../H/Harlond.md" title="Harlond">Harlond</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-9">[9]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-8">[8]</a></sup> Círdan sent [Galdor of the Havens](Galdor_of_the_Havens.md) "on an errand" to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and he became Círdan's representative at the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Later history {#Later_history}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_Departure_at_the_Grey_Havens.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/22/Ted_Nasmith_-_Departure_at_the_Grey_Havens.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Departure_at_the_Grey_Havens.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="210" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/22/Ted_Nasmith_-_Departure_at_the_Grey_Havens.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Departure_at_the_Grey_Havens.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/2/22/Ted_Nasmith_-_Departure_at_the_Grey_Havens.jpg 2x" data-file-width="530" data-file-height="371"/></a><figcaption>Departure at the Grey Havens by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

Following the defeat of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) and the crowning of King [Aragorn Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) in the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the last of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) set sail from the Grey Havens and left Middle-earth for [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-4">[4]</a></sup> Latest of all were the [Last Riding of the Keepers of the Rings](../L/Last_Riding_of_the_Keepers_of_the_Rings.md); [Gandalf](Gandalf.md), [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md), and [Galadriel](Galadriel.md). They were accompanied by [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), who were given the honour of taking the [Straight Road](../S/Straight_Road.md) because they had both been [Ring-bearers](../R/Ring-bearers.md).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Years later, [Fairbairn](../F/Fairbairn_Family~66364.md) family tradition held that [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [61](../F/Fourth_Age_61.md) also sailed [West](../A/Aman.md) from the Grey Havens.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-12">[12]</a></sup>

The Grey Havens were maintained into the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md) (for the last mentioning of Mithlond was [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)'s departure <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../W/West.md" title="West">West</a> in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [61](../F/Fourth_Age_61.md)),<sup id="cite_ref-Later_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-12">[12]</a></sup> but it was recognized that eventually, its purpose would reach an end when no more Elves wished to cross the [Sea](../S/Sea.md). At that time, Círdan would abandon the Grey Havens and finally travel the [Straight Road](../S/Straight_Road.md) himself. At some unknown date, [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) eventually sought the Grey Havens and with Círdan, they took the [Last Ship](../L/Last_Ship.md) together.<sup id="cite_ref-prologue_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prologue-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppTale_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppTale-14">[14]</a></sup>

*Mithlond* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Grey Haven", from *[mith](../M/Mith.md)* ("grey") + *[lond](../L/Lond.md)* ("Haven").<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[John Garth](../J/John_Garth.md) points out that the Grey Havens are located around <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clevedon" title="wikipedia:Clevedon">Clevedon</a> if the river [Lhûn](../L/Lhûn.md) is superimposed on the river <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Severn" title="wikipedia:River Severn">Severn</a>. J.R.R. Tolkien and his wife Edith spent their honeymoon in Clevedon.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> Clevedon is located on the southern shore of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severn_Estuary" title="wikipedia:Severn Estuary">Severn Estuary</a> roughly opposite of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff" title="wikipedia:Cardiff">Cardiff</a>, which is located on the northern shore of the Severn Estuary.
