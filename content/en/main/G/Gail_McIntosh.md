**Gail McIntosh** is an American artist primarily known for her [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing) cover art, though she also created art for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).<sup id="cite_ref-blackgate_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-blackgate-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Career {#Career}

After studying at the Pennsylvania Academy of Fine Art and at the [Italian](/wiki/Italy "Italy") *Accademia di Belle Arti di Firenze*, she was an apprentice to sculptor <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Berti_(sculptor)" title="wikipedia:Antonio Berti (sculptor)">Antonio Berti</a>. When she returned to the United State, she eventually ended up working for [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises).<sup id="cite_ref-blackgate_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-blackgate-1">[1]</a></sup>
