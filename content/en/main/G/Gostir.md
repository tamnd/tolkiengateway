**Gostir** was the name of one of the [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons) of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth),<sup id="cite_ref-gostir_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gostir-1">[1]</a></sup> about whom almost nothing is known except that they possessed a dragon-name<sup id="cite_ref-gostir_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gostir-1">[1]</a></sup> which had evolved from the older name *Gorsthir*.<sup id="cite_ref-gorsthir_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gorsthir-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gostir* means "dread-glance" in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), from *gost* ("dread, terror")<sup id="cite_ref-gostir_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gostir-1">[1]</a></sup> and *thîr* ("look, face, expression, countenance"), as well as the root *THĒ*, meaning "look (see or seem)".<sup id="cite_ref-gorsthir_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gorsthir-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

### *Middle-earth Role Playing* {#Middle-earth_Role_Playing}

<dl><dd>Gostir appears as a white <a href="/wiki/Cold-drakes" title="Cold-drakes">Cold-drake</a> with red eyes who dwells north of <a href="/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn" title="Rhûn">Rhûn</a> in Barl Syrnac in the <i>Creatures of Middle-earth</i> supplement of <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>. Gostir was said to be the older brother of <a href="/wiki/Lhamthanc" title="Lhamthanc">Lhamthanc</a>.</dd></dl>
