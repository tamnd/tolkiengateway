**Gamwich** was a village in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>

It was the home town of [Hamfast Gamwich](/wiki/Hamfast_of_Gamwich "Hamfast of Gamwich") (and the source of his chosen surname) and as such the origin of the [Gamwich](/wiki/Gamwich_Family "Gamwich Family") and the [Gammidge](/wiki/Gammidge_Family "Gammidge Family") (later [Gamgee](/wiki/Gamgee)) families. The town was also known for its own rope-making.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Gamwich is "translated" from [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Galabas&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Galabas (page does not exist)">Galabas</a></i> meaning "game-village"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) has suggested that the name represents a possible [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") form \**Gamenwic* "Game-village".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

It was delved in the year <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2498" title="Third Age 2498">Third Age 2498</a> and its famed for its hunting.</dd></dl>
