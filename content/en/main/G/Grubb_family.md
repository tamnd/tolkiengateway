The **Grubb family** was an important and wealthy family of the [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md).

Female members of that family married into the [Baggins](../B/Baggins_family.md) and [Boffin families](../B/Boffin_family.md). [Laura Grubb](../L/Laura_Grubb.md) married [Mungo Baggins](../M/Mungo_Baggins.md), and was [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)'s grandmother.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Some of the family were involved in auctioneering. The company [Messrs Grubb, Grubb and Burrowes](../M/Messrs_Grubb,_Grubb_and_Burrowes.md) was set to sell [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)'s belongings.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is meant to recall the English verb grub "dig, root, in the ground".<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>
