The **Grubb family** was an important and wealthy family of the [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits).

Female members of that family married into the [Baggins](/wiki/Baggins_family "Baggins family") and [Boffin families](/wiki/Boffin_family "Boffin family"). [Laura Grubb](/wiki/Laura_Grubb) married [Mungo Baggins](/wiki/Mungo_Baggins), and was [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins")'s grandmother.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Some of the family were involved in auctioneering. The company [Messrs Grubb, Grubb and Burrowes](/wiki/Messrs_Grubb,_Grubb_and_Burrowes) was set to sell [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)'s belongings.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is meant to recall the English verb grub "dig, root, in the ground".<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>
