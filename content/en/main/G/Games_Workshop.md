**Games Workshop** is a British game production and retailing company, founded in [1975](../_/1975.md). One of its key products is the *[Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game](../M/Middle-earth_Strategy_Battle_Game.md)*, along with its more popular titles, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warhammer_Fantasy_Battle" title="wikipedia:Warhammer Fantasy Battle">Warhammer</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warhammer_40,000" title="wikipedia:Warhammer 40,000">Warhammer 40,000</a>

## History of Tolkienia {#History_of_Tolkienia}

Games Workshop's magazine *[White Dwarf](../W/White_Dwarf.md)* (founded in 1977) has featured much material related to game adaptations of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

In [1985](../_/1985.md), Games Workshop introduced the *[MERP](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)*-system in the UK by publishing *[Middle-earth, The Role-playing Game set in J.R.R. Tolkien's World](../M/Middle-earth%253A_The_Role-playing_Game_set_in_J.R.R._Tolkien's_World.md)*.

In [2001](../_/2001.md), Games Workshop introduced *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game.md)*, a miniature game based on [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>).

In [2005](../_/2005.md) was released the board game *[The Battle of Five Armies](<../T/The_Battle_of_Five_Armies_(2005_board_game).md>)*, based on the events of the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md) from *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.

In [2012](../_/2012.md), Games Workshop introduced *[The Hobbit Strategy Battle Game](../T/The_Hobbit_Strategy_Battle_Game.md)*, a miniature game based on [*The Hobbit* (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).

In [2018](../_/2018.md), Games Workshop merged the two strategy battle games together under the new game *[Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game](../M/Middle-earth_Strategy_Battle_Game.md).*
