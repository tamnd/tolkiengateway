**Games Workshop** is a British game production and retailing company, founded in [1975](/wiki/1975). One of its key products is the *[Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Strategy_Battle_Game)*, along with its more popular titles, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warhammer_Fantasy_Battle" title="wikipedia:Warhammer Fantasy Battle">Warhammer</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warhammer_40,000" title="wikipedia:Warhammer 40,000">Warhammer 40,000</a>

## History of Tolkienia {#History_of_Tolkienia}

Games Workshop's magazine *[White Dwarf](/wiki/White_Dwarf)* (founded in 1977) has featured much material related to game adaptations of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

In [1985](/wiki/1985), Games Workshop introduced the *[MERP](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing "Middle-earth Role Playing")*-system in the UK by publishing *[Middle-earth, The Role-playing Game set in J.R.R. Tolkien's World](/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Role-playing_Game_set_in_J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_World "Middle-earth: The Role-playing Game set in J.R.R. Tolkien's World")*.

In [2001](/wiki/2001), Games Workshop introduced *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game)*, a miniature game based on [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").

In [2005](/wiki/2005) was released the board game *[The Battle of Five Armies](</wiki/The_Battle_of_Five_Armies_(2005_board_game)> "The Battle of Five Armies (2005 board game)")*, based on the events of the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies) from *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.

In [2012](/wiki/2012), Games Workshop introduced *[The Hobbit Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/The_Hobbit_Strategy_Battle_Game)*, a miniature game based on [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").

In [2018](/wiki/2018), Games Workshop merged the two strategy battle games together under the new game *[Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Strategy_Battle_Game).*
