**Galhir** was a [man](/wiki/Men "Men") of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) and one of the guards that [Hardang](/wiki/Hardang), the [Halad](/wiki/Halad) of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin), had assigned to keep watch at the [Haudh-en-Elleth](/wiki/Haudh-en-Elleth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Later, when Hardang wanted to send [Manthor](/wiki/Manthor) away while he dealt with Húrin, he told him to go until Captain [Avranc](/wiki/Avranc) came to take command (this was an on-the-spot promotion of Avranc). Manthor immediately quit his service to Hardang and stated that he had left Galhir, "a woodman older and wiser" than Avranc in command.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

When "[The Wanderings of Húrin](/wiki/The_Wanderings_of_H%C3%BArin)" was in draft form, in the second appearance of Sagroth's name it was emended to *Galhir* but then returned to *[Sagroth](/wiki/Sagroth)*. [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) speculated that this wasn't a change of name, but an introduction of a new character in the story.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
