**Galhir** was a [man](../M/Men.md) of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md) and one of the guards that [Hardang](../H/Hardang.md), the [Halad](../H/Halad.md) of the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md), had assigned to keep watch at the [Haudh-en-Elleth](../H/Haudh-en-Elleth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Later, when Hardang wanted to send [Manthor](../M/Manthor.md) away while he dealt with Húrin, he told him to go until Captain [Avranc](../A/Avranc.md) came to take command (this was an on-the-spot promotion of Avranc). Manthor immediately quit his service to Hardang and stated that he had left Galhir, "a woodman older and wiser" than Avranc in command.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

When "[The Wanderings of Húrin](../T/The_Wanderings_of_Húrin.md)" was in draft form, in the second appearance of Sagroth's name it was emended to *Galhir* but then returned to *[Sagroth](../S/Sagroth.md)*. [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) speculated that this wasn't a change of name, but an introduction of a new character in the story.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
