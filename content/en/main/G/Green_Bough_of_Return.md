The **Green Bough of Return** was a branch from the [Fragrant Tree](/wiki/Fragrant_Trees "Fragrant Trees") [Oiolairë](/wiki/Oiolair%C3%AB) which was placed at the prow of the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") ships as a symbol of good fortune (and as a token of friendship with [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB) and [Uinen](/wiki/Uinen)). By custom, the bough had to be brought by a female, often a close relative of the captain.

When [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion"), the son of [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur), finished the great ship [Palarran](/wiki/Palarran) and purposed to go to sea, his father forbade any of his family to bring the bough to his ship. [Erendis](/wiki/Erendis) performed this rite and on that day Aldarion fell in love with her.

Later though, when Erendis had become the wife of Tar-Aldarion and estranged from him, she refused to bring the bough as a show of disagreement with her husband's frequent voyages towards [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Therefore on the prow of Aldarion's later ship [Hirilondë](/wiki/Hirilond%C3%AB) the image of an eagle stood, and not the Green Bough of Return.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
