**Ghül** is a black metal band from Derby, Derbyshire, England, formed in 2023.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its releases include those titled after [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul), [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim) and the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl).
