<i><b>gond</b></i> or <i><b>gonn</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning "rock" or "stone".<sup id="cite_ref-S_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In one manuscript, the word is said to derive from [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) *gon, gondo* ("stone, rock").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In another manuscript, *gond* derives from [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) *gondō* ("stone, general as a substance or material").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)*, the [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *gonn* ("a great stone, or rock") derives from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) GONOD/GONDO ("stone").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
