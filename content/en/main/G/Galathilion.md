**Galathilion**, the **White Tree**, was a tree made by [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) for the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of the city of [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion). It was made in the image of [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion), as the [Amanyar](/wiki/Amanyar) loved it most of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees), although Galathilion gave no light.<sup id="cite_ref-s5_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s5-1">[1]</a></sup>

It grew in the [Great Square](/wiki/Great_Square), a high open courtyard beneath the [Mindon Eldaliéva](/wiki/Mindon_Eldali%C3%A9va), and had many seedlings in [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar). From it came [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn,_White_Tree "Celeborn, White Tree"), the White Tree of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa);<sup id="cite_ref-s5_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s5-1">[1]</a></sup> through which it became the ancestor of [Nimloth of Númenor](/wiki/Nimloth_of_N%C3%BAmenor) and the [White Tree of Gondor](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Ak_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ak-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Galathilion is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "White Tree",<sup id="cite_ref-s5_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s5-1">[1]</a></sup> from *[galadh](/wiki/Galadh "Galadh")* ("tree") + *[thil](/wiki/Sil "Sil")* ("shine silver)".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The final element could be the patronymic *[-ion](/wiki/-ion)*, perhaps indicating the tree descents from Telperion.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

It was also known as the **Tree of Tirion**<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> or **Tree of [Túna](/wiki/T%C3%BAna)**.<sup id="cite_ref-Ak_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ak-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Galathilion* was at first a name for Telperion, "but in after days *Galathilion the Less* was the name of the White Tree of Túna."<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) noted that the [Doors of Durin](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin) displayed the **Tree of the High Elves**,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 1]</a></sup> which probably represented Galathilion.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the poem *[Imram](/wiki/Imram)*, [Saint Brendan](/wiki/Saint_Brendan) in his deathbed is able to remember a White-tree from his travels that cannot be other than Galathilion:<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>In that hidden land we saw there stand<br/>
under a moonlight dim<br/>
a Tree more fair than ever I deemed<br/>
in Paradise might grow:<br/>
its foot was like a great tower&#39;s root,<br/>
its height no man could know;<br/>
and white as winter to my sight<br/>
the leaves of that Tree were;<br/>
they grew more close than swan-wing plumes,<br/>
long and soft and fair.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="/wiki/Imram" title="Imram">Imram</a></i> (The Death of Saint Brendan), vv. 67-76</cite></p>
</blockquote>

In a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) note published in *[Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth](/wiki/Tolkien:_Maker_of_Middle-earth)*, the tree is referred to by the name *[Amalion](/wiki/Amalion)*.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>
