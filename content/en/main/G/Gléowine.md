**Gléowine** was a [minstrel](/wiki/Minstrels "Minstrels") of the [King's House](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan") of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) in the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

## History {#History}

Gléowine composed [an immortalising song](/wiki/Out_of_doubt,_out_of_dark,_to_the_day%E2%80%99s_rising "Out of doubt, out of dark, to the day’s rising") about [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and his ancestors after the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields). It was said that he never composed another song afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early drafts, Gléowine was named Gleowin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Gléowine is first encountered being banished from <a href="/wiki/Edoras" title="Edoras">Edoras</a> at the command of <a href="/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma" title="Gríma">Gríma Wormtongue</a>. The player and Horn later find him drinking his sorrows in the town of <a href="/wiki/Snowbourn" title="Snowbourn">Snowbourn</a>. Gléowine agrees to accompany the player, and they find that the land has been ravaged by the devilry of <a href="/wiki/Saruman" title="Saruman">Saruman</a>. They return after <a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden" title="Théoden">Théoden</a> had been healed by <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> and the King takes Gléowine at his side on the ride to the <a href="/wiki/Fords_of_Isen" title="Fords of Isen">Fords of Isen</a>. During the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Battle_of_Helm%27s_Deep" title="Battle of Helm&#39;s Deep">Battle of Helm&#39;s Deep</a>, Gléowine keeps away from the fighting and later composes a song about the last stand of Théoden&#39;s door-warden <a href="/wiki/H%C3%A1ma" title="Háma">Háma</a>. Gléowine again journeys by the King&#39;s side to <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Mundburg</a> and sees the King&#39;s death at the hands of the <a href="/wiki/Witch-king" title="Witch-king">Witch-king</a> first-hand.</dd></dl>
