**Gundabald Bolger** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Gundabald was the son of [Gundahad Bolger](/wiki/Gundahad_Bolger). He married [Salvia Brandybuck](/wiki/Salvia_Brandybuck) and had one son, [Theobald](/wiki/Theobald_Bolger "Theobald Bolger").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name contains *gund* "battle" and *bald* "bold".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
