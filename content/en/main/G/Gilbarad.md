**Gilbarad** was the father of [Ivorwen](/wiki/Ivorwen), and [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)'s maternal great-grandfather.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gilbarad* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests that it contains the elements *[gil](/wiki/Gil "Gil")* ("star; (bright) spark, silver glint, twinkle of light") and *[barad](/wiki/Barad "Barad")* ("tower, great towering building").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
