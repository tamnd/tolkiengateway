The **Great Barrow** appears in *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)* and *[The One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring)* roleplaying game. In both games, it had become the home of a great wight.<sup id="cite_ref-LOTRO_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LOTRO-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TOR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR-2">[2]</a></sup> It might have been the burial mound of the [Kings of Cardolan](/wiki/Kings_of_Cardolan), and even the place where [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck"), and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") obtained the [Daggers of Westernesse](/wiki/Daggers_of_Westernesse).

## In *The Lord of the Rings Online* {#In_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online}

The **Great Barrow**, also known as *Othrongroth*, was the oldest and largest [Edain](/wiki/Edain) burial mound of the [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs). It was home to the Wight-lord [Sambrog](/wiki/Sambrog), who had a throne room there.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LOTRO_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LOTRO-1">[1]</a></sup>

## In *The One Ring* {#In_The_One_Ring}

The **Great Barrow** was a burial complex in on the highest hills of the Barrow-downs. Sometime before [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2965](/wiki/Third_Age_2965 "Third Age 2965"), the Wight-king had set up a throne room deep inside the burial mound.<sup id="cite_ref-TOR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR-2">[2]</a></sup>
