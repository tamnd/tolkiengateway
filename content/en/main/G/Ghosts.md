**Ghosts** was a name commonly used to describe the [undead](/wiki/Undead "Undead"), beings which did not perish after what would be a natural death, but which continued to haunt the living in their after-life. While likely often the product of a vivid imagination, the dealings of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) with several types of undead testify that ghosts were a reality in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the ruined and deserted [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) was known as "*a city of ghosts.*"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), one of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s early conceptions of an [Elven](/wiki/Elvish "Elvish") language, the word for "a ghost" is *ûl*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Ghosts are one of the most powerful Undead beings. They are altogether incorporeal, and drain constitution points from player characters.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002-5: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game">The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Ghosts come in three types: phantoms, wraiths and wights.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Ghostly beings are referred to as Shades in the game and come in several varieties including Oath-breakers (similar to the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Dead_Men_of_Dunharrow" title="Dead Men of Dunharrow">Dead Men of Dunharrow</a>), fell spirits (ghosts that animate the <a href="/wiki/Barrow-wights" title="Barrow-wights">Barrow-wights</a>), Corpse-candles (like those of the <a href="/wiki/Dead_Marshes" title="Dead Marshes">Dead Marshes</a>), and evil river-spirits called Darkwaters.</dd></dl>
