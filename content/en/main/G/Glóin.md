**Glóin** was a [dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk), the son of [Gróin](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3in), the brother of [Óin](/wiki/%C3%93in), and the father of [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> He is most famous as one of [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin)'s companions on the [quest](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor "Quest of Erebor") to [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"). 77 years after the Quest of Erebor, Glóin attended the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond) with his son Gimli, where the latter would represent dwarves in the Fellowship of the Ring

## History {#History}

Glóin was likely born in [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland), where the Dwarves of Durin's Line dwelt in exile<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> after their expulsion from [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") by the [dragon](/wiki/Dragon "Dragon") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> Glóin was present at the [Battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar),<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> which was odd because Dwarves were considered to be mere striplings in their thirties, yet at the time ([2799](/wiki/Third_Age_2799 "Third Age 2799")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>) Glóin was but 16 (his cousin [Balin](/wiki/Balin) also was among the participants). After the war, in which the dwarves were victorious, Glóin returned to Dunland with the followers of Thorin, who then re-moved the dwarves to the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin), the Blue Mountains, west of the [Shire](/wiki/Shire).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Life_in_Thorin.27s_Halls"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Life_in_Thorin&#39;s_Halls">Life in Thorin&#39;s Halls</span></h3>

During Glóin's life in Ered Luin he saw the departure of King [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in "Thráin"), who tried to go back to [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"), but was captured and tortured for the [Ring of Thrór](/wiki/Ring_of_Thr%C3%B3r), and subsequently died several years later. Under King [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin), Glóin and the other Dwarves living in [Thorin's Halls](/wiki/Thorin%27s_Halls) in the mountains became prosperous in a fashion and trafficked goods with other peoples, and gained what wealth they could. But ever they spoke of the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2879](/wiki/Third_Age_2879 "Third Age 2879"), Glóin has a son, [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), who was considered too young to join [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) at the age of 62.

### The Quest of Erebor {#The_Quest_of_Erebor}

In [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") Thorin happened to meet [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) the Wizard. From this meeting came Thorin's determination to return to the Lonely Mountain with a company of Dwarves, including Glóin, to seek revenge upon Smaug. Gandalf came to a conclave of the Dwarves after visiting [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") (but before seeing [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)) to persuade them to take the hobbit with them. The Dwarves were skeptical, with Glóin calling them "simpletons" and claiming that one of them "would never dare to come within smelling distance of the nakedest dragonet new from the shell!"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Glóin was the fifth Dwarf in the fourth group to arrive at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) for the Unexpected Party, and he came wearing a white hood. Glóin did not assist in setting the table nor did he bring a musical instrument out when Thorin called for music. After Bilbo had his fit of fright he overheard Glóin sneer that the hobbit looked "more like a grocer than a burglar" when he saw him "bobbing and puffing on the mat". This affronted Bilbo enough to make him agree to undertake the quest. Then, when Gandalf proclaimed that Bilbo was his choice he scowled at Glóin so fiercely that the dwarf huddled back in his chair, and later glaring at Bilbo.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On their quest Glóin was quite useful, especially at making fires, although on the night the company met the trolls, [William](/wiki/William), [Bert](/wiki/Bert), and <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Tom" title="Tom">Tom</a>, he and his brother failed to start one and began to fight, yet though first they quarreled.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> On the night when the company came to the glade of the [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs), Glóin along with his brother, Dori, Nori and Ori secured a seat in a huge pine tree.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> At [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn)'s home Glóin and Óin were the fifth pair of Dwarves to appear as Gandalf spun their tale.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> And when released from his barrel near [Esgaroth](/wiki/Lake-town "Lake-town"), Glóin was one of the Dwarves who was waterlogged and seemed but half alive, and needed to be carried ashore.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Glóin fought in and survived the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies). He was given his share of the treasure by [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot), who became King after Thorin's death. Glóin settled in the Lonely Mountain, along with the survivors of the battle, and would grow wealthy in Erebor under the leadership of King Dáin.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

In October [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018")<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Glóin and his son [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) were sent by [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot) to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to seek advice concerning the fate of [Balin's Colony](/wiki/Balin%27s_Colony) in Moria. When [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) recovered from his wounds and attended the feast, it was Glóin who sat next to him and gave him the news from Erebor and surrounding lands since the time of Bilbo.<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-1">[1]</a></sup> Later, at the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond), Glóin spoke of the shadow that had fallen upon his people, Balin's expedition and of the sinister messenger from [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) who had come to inquire about [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) and where they dwelt.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Glóin's greatest contribution to the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) was his son Gimli, who joined the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

It is possible that Glóin fought in the [Battle of Dale](/wiki/Battle_of_Dale) against the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), for he lived until [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [15](/wiki/Fourth_Age_15 "Fourth Age 15").<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> After the War of the Ring it is unknown whether he saw Gimli again after he explored [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest) and the [Glittering Caves](/wiki/Glittering_Caves).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In both the early manuscript of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and in the published version, Glóin had heard of Bilbo's great-great-great-grand-uncle [Bullroarer Took](/wiki/Bandobras_Took "Bandobras Took"), who accidentally invented the game of Golf. However, only in the early manuscript it was mentioned that Glóin was very fond of golf.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

In *The Fellowship of the Ring*, the dwarf [Burin](/wiki/Burin) (also [Frár](/wiki/Fr%C3%A1r)), being Balin's son (from Thorin and Company) was suppose to accompany Glóin to the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond), and that would later join [The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring), though it was changed to Gimli, and also changed to be Glóin's son, in the final draft of the novel.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

<dl><dd><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt"></blockquote></dd></dl>

Of all the members in The Company of Dwarves who set out on the Quest to The Lonely Mountain, Gloin is the most outspoken and opinionated and is not afraid to challenge authority. He has a tendency to be quick-tempered, but he is also strong, brave and loyal. Gloin is one of the only married Dwarfs in the Company (there being a shortage of female Dwarves in general). His wife is an acclaimed beauty with a particularly fine beard. Gloin is also the proud father of a young son, Gimli, who will later grow up to become part of the famous Fellowship of the Ring.

<b><a href="/wiki/2006" title="2006">2006</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Glóin is a major character. Still present in Rivendell after the Council of Elrond, he teams up with <a href="/wiki/Glorfindel" title="Glorfindel">Glorfindel</a> on a campaign throughout <a href="/wiki/Eriador" title="Eriador">Eriador</a> and  <a href="/wiki/Rhovanion" title="Rhovanion">Rhovanion</a>. He is a fierce dwarf with a reddish brown beard.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2007" title="2007">2007</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>During the time of the War of the Ring, Glóin has an official position as emissary of the King Under the Mountain. His first chronological appearance is a cameo in a brief scene showing the departure of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Thorin_Oakenshield" title="Thorin Oakenshield">Thorin Oakenshield</a>&#39;s company from <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ered_Luin" title="Ered Luin">Ered Luin</a>. He then appears in the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a> north of <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>, where after the Council of Elrond Glóin and a few other dwarves establish a camp to deal with the threats in the <a href="/wiki/High_Pass" title="High Pass">High Pass</a> and <a href="/wiki/Goblin-town" title="Goblin-town">Goblin-town</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>After the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Dale" title="Battle of Dale">Battle of Dale</a>, Glóin returns to the Lonely Mountain and reunites with the other surviving members of Thorin&#39;s Company. Soon after <a href="/wiki/Nori" title="Nori">Nori</a> leads an expedition to <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ered_Mithrin" title="Ered Mithrin">Ered Mithrin</a> against the orders of King <a href="/wiki/Thorin_Stonehelm" title="Thorin Stonehelm">Thorin III</a> and Glóin, <a href="/wiki/Bofur" title="Bofur">Bofur</a>, and the player character join <a href="/wiki/Dori" title="Dori">Dori</a> on a mission to rescue his brother from his own folly.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Glóin and his companions travel through the Grey Mountains and eventually find Nori, but unlike the others Glóin does not immediately return to Erebor. Instead, he stays in the Grey Mountains where he becomes involved in Prince Durin&#39;s upcoming war against the <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> of <a href="/wiki/Gundabad" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a>. On Durin&#39;s orders Glóin and some other dwarves track down a raiding party from Gundabad through the wellsprings of <a href="/wiki/Anduin" title="Anduin">Anduin</a>, eventually travelling to the northernmost outposts of the <a href="/wiki/Beornings" title="Beornings">Beornings</a>. When the war for Gundabad begins, Glóin takes the role of Commander of the Longbeard Armies.</dd></dl>
