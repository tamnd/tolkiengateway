The **Glittering Caves**, in Sindarin called **Aglarond**, were the spectacular jewelled caverns that lay in the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) behind [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep) in [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

## Overview {#Overview}

The Glittering Caves were located behind [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep) under the three peaks of the [Thrihyrne](/wiki/Thrihyrne) in the northwestern arm of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains). The Glittering Caves extended deep under the mountains, with many passages, stairs, halls, and chambers. The floors were sandy and the ceilings were high, domed vaults. The walls were polished stone set with gems and crystals and veins of ore.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The entrance to the caves was behind the [Deeping Wall](/wiki/Deeping_Wall) in a narrow gorge that could be defended long against an onslaught, making them an ideal refuge location.

[Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) called the Glittering Caves one of the marvels of the Northern World, and his description of them to [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) was reportedly one of the few times that a dwarf has bested an elf with words; Legolas was left speechless after visiting them.

## History {#History}

The [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) built the fortress of Aglarond during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The meaning of the Sindarin name of the fortress suggests that they already knew of the existence of the caves. Later, after the founding of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) it was used as a storage space and refuge for [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) who used the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg).

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

During the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg) on [3](/wiki/3_March "3 March")-[4 March](/wiki/4_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), many of the women, children, and elderly people of the [Westfold](/wiki/Westfold) took refuge in the Glittering Caves. Livestock and food were stored there as well.

Some [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) crept through a culvert in the Deeping Wall and entered the gorge but were killed or driven back by the defenders. Then the Deeping Wall was breached by an explosive device and the Enemy forces entered the Deep. Many of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) including [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) were driven back to the Glittering Caves, and with them was Gimli.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the war Gimli brought [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) to see the caves; the Elf was so impressed that he was silent and said that only Gimli could fittingly describe them.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

### As a Dwarven colony {#As_a_Dwarven_colony}

When [Gimli Elf-friend](/wiki/Gimli "Gimli") was held up in the caves, he was amazed by their beauty. After the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) he brought from [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") part of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) and founded a [colony](/wiki/Dwarf_realms "Dwarf realms") in the caves, becoming the [Lord of the Glittering Caves](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Glittering_Caves). The Dwarves of the Glittering Caves carefully tended the stone walls and opened new ways and chambers and hung lamps that filled the caverns with light.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The Glittering Caves became one of the most important realms of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) at the beginning of the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aglarond* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and contains the elements *[aglar](/wiki/Aglar "Aglar")* ("brilliance",<sup id="cite_ref-N_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-7">[7]</a></sup> "radiance", glory"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-8">[8]</a></sup>) and *[rond](/wiki/Rond "Rond")* ("vault", "high roofed cavern"),<sup id="cite_ref-N_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> which is translated as "Glittering Caves"<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> or "Caves of Radiance.<sup id="cite_ref-11c_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11c-11">[11]</a></sup> Since the word *aglar* is derived from the stem KAL- ("light")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-8">[8]</a></sup>"Glittering Caves" can be the exact translation.

Its [Rohirric](/wiki/Rohirric) name, which was "translated" as [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *Glǽmscrafu* (the *sc* is pronounced as *\[sh\]*) also meant "Caves of Radiance".<sup id="cite_ref-11c_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11c-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The Glittering Caves are one of very few locations in [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s work that are associated with a real place. The passage bout the Glitting Caves was based on the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheddar_Gorge_and_Caves" title="wikipedia:Cheddar Gorge and Caves">caves in Cheddar Gorge</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerset" title="wikipedia:Somerset">Somerset</a>. J.R.R. Tolkien had written the passage just after he had revisted the caves in 1940. The passage was still coloured by his memory of his vistit of the caves during his honeymoon nearly 30 years before.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
