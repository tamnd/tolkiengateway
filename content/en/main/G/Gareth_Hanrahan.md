**Gareth Hanrahan** (born [1978](/wiki/1978)) is an Irish tabletop game designer who has written for both editions of [*The One Ring* role-playing game](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)"). Beyond his work on licensed role-playing games, he wrote the book *[Tolkien's World](</wiki/Tolkien%27s_World_(by_Gareth_Hanrahan)> "Tolkien's World (by Gareth Hanrahan)")*.
