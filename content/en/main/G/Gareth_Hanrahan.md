**Gareth Hanrahan** (born [1978](../_/1978.md)) is an Irish tabletop game designer who has written for both editions of [*The One Ring* role-playing game](<../T/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md>). Beyond his work on licensed role-playing games, he wrote the book *[Tolkien's World](<../T/Tolkien's_World_(by_Gareth_Hanrahan).md>)*.
