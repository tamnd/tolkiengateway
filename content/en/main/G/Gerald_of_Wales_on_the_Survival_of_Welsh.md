**"Gerald of Wales on the Survival of Welsh"** is an article by W. Rhys Roberts, to which [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) contributed with a translation of a portion of a Latin text into late [twelfth century English of the South West Midlands](../A/AB_language.md). It also notes a suggestion by Tolkien on the significance of "senior quidam".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The article was first published in the Welsh annual journal *[The Transactions of the Honourable Society of Cymmrodorion: Session 1923-24](../T/The_Transactions_of_the_Honourable_Society_of_Cymmrodorion%253A_Session_1923-24.md)* in [1925](../_/1925.md). In [2005](../_/2005.md), Tolkien's contribution was reprinted in [*Tolkien Studies*: Volume 2](../T/Tolkien_Studies%253A_Volume_2.md), in the article "J.R.R. Tolkien and W. Rhys Roberts's 'Gerald of Wales on the Survival of Welsh'" by [Douglas A. Anderson](../D/Douglas_A._Anderson.md).
