The **Great Lake** lay in the center of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) when all the lands and seas were symmetrical. In the middle of the lake was the Isle of [Almaren](../A/Almaren.md), the first dwelling of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) in [Arda](../A/Arda.md).

## History {#History}

After the expulsion of [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) from Arda in the [Valian year](../V/Valian_Years.md) 1,500,<sup id="cite_ref-Annal_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annal-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) brought order to the seas and lands and it may have been at this time that the Great Lake was formed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the Valian year 3,450 Melkor came forth in war and cast down the [Two Lamps](../T/Two_Lamps.md). Their destruction broke the lands and roiled the seas. The dwelling of the Valar upon Almaren was utterly destroyed and the island and the Great Lake disappeared in the tumult.<sup id="cite_ref-Annal_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annal-1">[1]</a></sup>
