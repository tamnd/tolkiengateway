The **Gamgee family** was an old family of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), originally from the village of [Gamwich](/wiki/Gamwich). An offshoot was the [Roper family](/wiki/Roper_family). [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) of the [Company of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") belonged to this family, though his branch was renamed [Gardner](/wiki/Gardner_family "Gardner family") in honour of his replanting of the Shire. Another, matrilineal, offshoot was the [Fairbairn family](/wiki/Fairbairn_family).<sup id="cite_ref-long_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-long-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Gamgee" derives from the earlier name *[Gammidgy](/wiki/Gammidgy)*, referring to the village [Gamwich](/wiki/Gamwich) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). "Gamgee" is the Anglicized translation of [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) surname <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Galpsi&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Galpsi (page does not exist)">Galpsi</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") noted that Gamgee is an uncommon English surname whose origin he did not know. It was also a word for 'cotton-wool', derived from the name of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samson_Gamgee" title="wikipedia:Samson Gamgee">Samson Gamgee</a> who invented '<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamgee_tissue" title="wikipedia:Gamgee tissue">Gamgee tissue</a>'.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[5]</a></sup>
