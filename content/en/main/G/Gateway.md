The **Gateway** was a short roadway from the [Great Gate of Minas Tirith](/wiki/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith) that led into the [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields). On [1 May](/wiki/1_May) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3012](/wiki/Third_Age_3012 "Third Age 3012")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) halted a furlong from the walls of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) at the start of the Gateway before advancing to be crowned King of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Gateway runs for only a short distance before splitting into two roads leading towards the northern and southern gates of <a href="/wiki/Rammas_Echor" title="Rammas Echor">Rammas Echor</a>.</dd></dl>
