**Galdor** was the second child and eldest son of [Hador](/wiki/Hador). He led the [People of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador "House of Hador") when they were at the peak of their power, before the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad). Galdor was noted for his exceptional height, even among the [Edain](/wiki/Edain), and was also called **Galdor the Tall**.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Galdor had a younger brother named [Gundor](/wiki/Gundor) and an older sister named [Glóredhel](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3redhel).<sup id="cite_ref-WJwest_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJwest-2">[2]</a></sup>

At a great feast of the Edain, Galdor married [Hareth](/wiki/Hareth) and Glóredhel married [Haldir](</wiki/Haldir_(Chieftain_of_the_Haladin)> "Haldir (Chieftain of the Haladin)"), both children of [Halmir](/wiki/Halmir), chief of the [House of Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth).<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-1">[1]</a></sup> Galdor and [Hareth](/wiki/Hareth) had two sons, [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) and [Huor](/wiki/Huor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455"), both Galdor's father, Hador, and his younger brother, Gundor, died in the rearguard of the Elf-lord [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin). Galdor then succeeded his father as Lord of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min). In the wake of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s triumph the wife of [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir), [Emeldir](/wiki/Emeldir), gathered the women and children of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) and led them westward; some found refuge in Galdor's domain.

The sons of Galdor, Húrin and Huor, had been taken after the Dagor Bragollach into the hidden city of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) by the eagles of [Thorondor](/wiki/Thorondor). When they departed from the city (again carried by eagles) they returned to Dor-lómin where they would not reveal where they had been, even to their father.

Seven years after the Dagor Bragollach, Morgoth sent a great force against [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum), attacking the passes of the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin). Galdor defended the [Eithel Sirion](/wiki/Eithel_Sirion) and was slain by an arrow.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-1">[1]</a></sup>

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggests it can be a combination of the [root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gal&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gal (page does not exist)">gal</a> ("light") and the suffix *-dor* ("lord").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
