**Griffo Boffin** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Griffo was the son of [Gruffo Boffin](/wiki/Gruffo_Boffin). He married [Daisy Baggins](/wiki/Daisy_Baggins), and was present at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Griffo Boffin is the youngest of the eight children of Prisca Boffin and is met at several family gatherings attended by their first cousin Bingo Boffin. He is also of the assistants to Mayor <a href="/wiki/Will_Whitfoot" title="Will Whitfoot">Will Whitfoot</a>.</dd></dl>
