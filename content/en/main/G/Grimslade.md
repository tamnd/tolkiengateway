**Grimslade** was the ancestral home of [Grimbold](/wiki/Grimbold) of [Westfold](/wiki/Westfold),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> a lesser [Marshal of the Mark](/wiki/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark "Third Marshal of the Riddermark") who commanded the muster of the [West-mark](/wiki/West-mark).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Nothing is known of the location of this structure other that it stood in the [Westfold](/wiki/Westfold) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-3">[3]</a></sup> The word "slade" seems to imply that the house was either in a clearing or on a hillside.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Grimslade* is modernized from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *Grimslaed* representing [Rohirric](/wiki/Rohan_language "Rohan language"). It contains *[Grim](/wiki/Grim)*, which Tolkien describes as "evidently the name of an ancestor" (of Grimbold), and *slade* (from Old English *slaed*, Norwegian dialect *slad*) ("forest, glade, dell (especially on a slope up a hillside"), which is still widely used in English place-names.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayals in Adaptations {#Portrayals_in_Adaptations}

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Grimslade is a small town in <a href="/wiki/Westfold" title="Westfold">Westfold</a>, located between <a href="/wiki/Edoras" title="Edoras">Edoras</a> and <a href="/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep" title="Helm&#39;s Deep">Helm&#39;s Deep</a> but closer to the latter. When Grimbold left with Prince <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Theodred" title="Theodred">Theodred</a> for the <a href="/wiki/Fords_of_Isen" title="Fords of Isen">Fords of Isen</a>, the duties of Grimslade&#39;s Thane fell to his wife Hildith. On <a href="/wiki/3_March" title="3 March">3 March</a> of <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3019" title="Third Age 3019">Third Age 3019</a> Grimslade is attacked by <a href="/wiki/Saruman" title="Saruman">Saruman</a>&#39;s <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Half-orc" title="Half-orc">half-orcs</a> who intend to burn it, but the attackers are repelled by the forces of <a href="/wiki/Elfhelm" title="Elfhelm">Elfhelm</a> during their march towards <a href="/wiki/Edoras" title="Edoras">Edoras</a>.</dd></dl>
