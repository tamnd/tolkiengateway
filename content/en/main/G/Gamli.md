**Gamli** was a [dwarf](/wiki/Dwarf "Dwarf") of the royal line of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) who lived during the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Gamli was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios), where he was portrayed by an unknown actor.

## History {#History}

Gamli and his sister [Gerda](/wiki/Gerda) were born to Durin IV and Disa in the twenty years since [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) last visited [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm). Gamli and Gerda are playing with one another when Elrond visits Durin's chambers.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> While [Disa](/wiki/Disa) spoke to Elrond, Gamli and Gerda sung and punched each other. When Disa told them to stop, they claimed they were playing the 'Knocking Game'. Disa, again, told them to stop.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
