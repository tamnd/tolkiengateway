<i><b>Golden Leaves</b></i> is a song composed by [Bear McCreary](/wiki/Bear_McCreary) for the [first episode](/wiki/Elven_Kings_Under_the_Sky "Elven Kings Under the Sky") of the second season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*. It features lyrics sung in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) by [Benjamin Walker](/wiki/Benjamin_Walker), who plays [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) on the show. Accordings to Bear McCreary, the lyrics were crafted by [John D. Payne](/wiki/John_D._Payne), inspired in part by the lyrics [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") wrote for [Galadriel's Song](/wiki/Galadriel%27s_Song) in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>.

## Poem {#Poem}

<div class="poem">
<p><i>Sís laurië lassi taiter,</i><br/>
<i>yénin linwavandië.</i><br/>
<i>Anpalla Vai Ahtalëa,</i><br/>
<i>sí lantar Eldaniër.</i><br/>
<br/>
<i><a href="/wiki/Eldali%C3%AB" title="Eldalië">Eldalié</a>! Eldalié!</i><br/>
<i>Hrívë túla helda ré úlassëa.</i><br/>
<i>Eldalié! Eldalié!</i><br/>
<i>I lassi lantar celumenna.</i><br/>
<br/>
(children’s choir:) <i>Cormar nelde aranin Eldaron</i><br/>
<br/>
<i>Eldalié and’ amárielvë</i><br/>
<i>ambena solor.</i><br/>
<i>Sí néca riëmancan,</i><br/>
<i>viliën an Valinor</i><br/>
<br/>
<i>Eldalié! Eldalié!</i><br/>
<i>Hrívë túla helda ré úlassëa.</i><br/>
<i>Eldalié! Eldalié!</i><br/>
<i>I lassi lantar celumenna.</i>
</p>
</div>

## Translation {#Translation}

The Quenya text above along with its following translation were published by Amazon's composer Bear McCreary on his personal website<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>.

<div class="poem">
<p><i>Here long the golden leaves grew,</i><br/>
<i>on years branching.</i><br/>
<i>For beyond the Sundering Seas,</i><br/>
<i>now fall Elven-tears.</i><br/>
<br/>
<i>O’ Elven-kind! O’ Elven-kind!</i><br/>
<i>Winter is coming, bare leafless day.</i><br/>
<i>O’ Elven-kind! O’ Elven-kind!</i><br/>
<i>The leaves are falling in the stream.</i><br/>
 <br/>
(children&#39;s choir:) <i>Three rings for the Elven kings</i><br/>
 <br/>
<i>Elven-kind long have we dwelt</i><br/>
<i>upon this hither shore.</i><br/>
<i>Now fading crown I trade,</i><br/>
<i>to sail to Valinor.</i><br/>
<br/>
<i>O’ Elven-kind! O’ Elven-kind!</i><br/>
<i>Winter is coming, bare leafless day.</i><br/>
<i>O’ Elven-kind! O’ Elven-kind!</i><br/>
<i>The leaves are falling in the stream.</i>
</p>
</div>

The series scene actually features only the two last stanzas, however the full song does play during the credits.
