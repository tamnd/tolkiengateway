**Gar Ainion**, the **Place of the Gods**, was a sort of temple in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

It was located nigh the king's halls, going southwards by the [Road of Pomps](/wiki/Road_of_Pomps). It was very open and at its middle was the highest ground of all the city, from which the [Square of the Palace](/wiki/Square_of_the_Palace) could be seen. It was here that [Idril](/wiki/Idril) and [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) were wed, in the Place of Wedding. During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) took a confused Idril to Gar Ainion, where they were found by [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and his group of survivors. From there, they gazed with despair the Square of the King being overran by enemies, the fall of the [king's tower](/wiki/Tower_of_the_King "Tower of the King") and the death of King [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gar Ainion* is [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), from *[gar](/wiki/Gar "Gar")* ("place, district")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> + genitive plural *[Ainu](/wiki/Ainur "Ainur")*. A previous form was *Gar Ainon*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognates were <i><b>Ainurarda</b></i> and <i><b>Arda Valarwa</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
