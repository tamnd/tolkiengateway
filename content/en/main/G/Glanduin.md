The **Glanduin** was a river on the southern border of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-1">[1]</a></sup> It was also known as the Swanfleet river meaning the river that flowed into the [Swanfleet](/wiki/Swanfleet).<sup id="cite_ref-AppendixD_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppendixD-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Course {#Course}

It flowed from the [Hithaeglir](/wiki/Misty_Mountains "Misty Mountains") south of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria")<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup> swiftly<sup id="cite_ref-AppendixD_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppendixD-2">[2]</a></sup> for approximately a hundred miles westwards, then made a bend and continued to the northwest for a few miles where it flowed for approximately seventy miles through a fenland in the plains that was known as the [Swanfleet](/wiki/Swanfleet) (*Nîn-in-Eilph*) until it joined the river [Mitheithel](/wiki/Mitheithel) only a few miles northeast of the town of [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad)<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup>. At their confluence the two rivers formed the river [Gwathló](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3).<sup id="cite_ref-AppendixD_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppendixD-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup>

There was an old ford across the Glanduin east of the falls where the river dropped down into the lowlands where the Swanfleet marshes were located.<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The river received its name Glanduin in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) when it formed the southern border of the [elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") realm of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion), beyond which were the unfriendly people of [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> By the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the name Glanduin only applied to the swift upper course of the river, and it was referred to as the Swanfleet river.<sup id="cite_ref-AppendixD_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppendixD-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck"), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and their company of Elves from [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) came from the south, crossed the old ford over the Glanduin into Eregion<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-4">[4]</a></sup> on September 6 [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Glanduin is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> wich means "border-river"<sup id="cite_ref-AppendixD_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppendixD-2">[2]</a></sup>. It is a compound of the elements *glan(n)* ("boundary") and *\[duin\]\]* ("(large) river").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Note {#Note}

In an annotation to [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) had R. Glanduin placed near the Mountains, and the lower part labelled "Swanfleet".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> It was misunderstood in first printings of *[A Map of Middle-earth](/wiki/A_Map_of_Middle-earth)* that "R. Swanfleet" was the name of the lower part of the river, but it was pointed out as wrong by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) (Swanfleet being the fens the river flows into) and it was corrected in later printings.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

After the publication of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) mentioned in an essay about the names of rivers that the Glanduin was part of the southern border of the North Kingdom of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor).<sup id="cite_ref-AppendixD_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppendixD-2">[2]</a></sup> This is in contradiction with the statement in Appendix A of *The Lord of the Rings* that says that Arnor did not include the lands east of the Greyflood and the Loudwater in which Rivendell and Eregion (Hollin) were located.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayals in adaptations {#Portrayals_in_adaptations}

<b>2008: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Glanduin river forms the border between the in-game regions of <a href="/wiki/Eregion" title="Eregion">Eregion</a> and <a href="/wiki/Enedwaith" title="Enedwaith">Enedwaith</a>. It flows past <a href="/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil" title="Ost-in-Edhil">Mirobel and Caras Gelebren</a> and splits into several streams before joining with <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Mitheithel" title="Mitheithel">Mitheithel</a> at the Wadewater of <a href="/wiki/Swanfleet" title="Swanfleet">Swanfleet</a>.</dd></dl>
