**Gorbulas Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Gorbulas was the son of [Orgulas Brandybuck](/wiki/Orgulas_Brandybuck), the younger son of the [Master of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland). He had one son himself, [Marmadas Brandybuck](/wiki/Marmadas_Brandybuck). He died some time before [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Gorbulas* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) suggests that the name *Gorbulas* combines the first part of *Gorbadoc* with the second part of *Orgulas*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
