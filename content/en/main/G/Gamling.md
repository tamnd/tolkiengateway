**Gamling** (late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age)) was a man of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), probably [Erkenbrand](/wiki/Erkenbrand)'s lieutenant,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the leader of the garrison of [Helm's Dike](/wiki/Helm%27s_Dike).<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

An old man during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Gamling was still a commanding presence at the time of the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg). During the battle Gamling was the first to realize that [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) had penetrated [the Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep "Helm's Deep") through its culvert, and he led the counter-attack himself.

Gamling seems to have been brought up in the western valleys of Rohan; he understood the tongue of [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland) that was still spoken in those regions.<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the [Deeping Wall](/wiki/Deeping_Wall) was taken, Gamling was one of those who retreated into the narrows. When the battle was over Gamling, [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) and others came forth from the caves.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Gamling is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name. It is derived from the stem *gamol*, which means "old", but is only found in Old English in verse-language<sup id="cite_ref-N_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-4">[4]</a></sup>

Gamling is somewhat anglicized/modernized while genuinely it should be *Gameling*. It would be one of the words and names that hobbits recognized as similar to [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) (like *[Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax)* or *[Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma")*).<sup id="cite_ref-N_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Tolkien noted that it is the origin of the surnames Gamlen, Gam(b)lin. It appears in the medieval poem *The Tale of Gamelin* (inspiration of Shakespeare's <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/As_You_Like_It" title="wikipedia:As You Like It">As You Like It</a></i>)<sup id="cite_ref-N_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-4">[4]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Gamling was played by New Zealand actor <a href="/wiki/Bruce_Hopkins" title="Bruce Hopkins">Bruce Hopkins</a>. His role was expanded to that of a lieutenant to <a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden" title="Théoden">Théoden</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2010: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Aragorn%27s_Quest" title="The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn&#39;s Quest">The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn&#39;s Quest</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Despite the game&#39;s heavy reliance on movie audio tracks, Gamling&#39;s speech at the departure from Edoras is not resampled, but dubbed in verbatim by <a href="/wiki/Tom_Kane" title="Tom Kane">Tom Kane</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Gamling&#39;s role is similar to that he has in the book. He welcomes <a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden" title="Théoden">Théoden</a>&#39;s host to <a href="/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep" title="Helm&#39;s Deep">Helm&#39;s Deep</a>, fights on the <a href="/wiki/Helm%27s_Dike" title="Helm&#39;s Dike">Helm&#39;s Dike</a> and on the wall of the <a href="/wiki/Hornburg" title="Hornburg">Hornburg</a> and after the wall is blown retreats into the <a href="/wiki/Glittering_Caves" title="Glittering Caves">Glittering Caves</a> along with <a href="/wiki/Gimli" title="Gimli">Gimli</a> and <a href="/wiki/%C3%89omer" title="Éomer">Éomer</a>. Later, Gamling rides to <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> with Theoden&#39;s riders and survives the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>.</dd></dl>
