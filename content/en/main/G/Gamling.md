**Gamling** (late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md)) was a man of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), probably [Erkenbrand](../E/Erkenbrand.md)'s lieutenant,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the leader of the garrison of [Helm's Dike](../H/Helm's_Dike.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

An old man during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), Gamling was still a commanding presence at the time of the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md). During the battle Gamling was the first to realize that [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) had penetrated [the Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md) through its culvert, and he led the counter-attack himself.

Gamling seems to have been brought up in the western valleys of Rohan; he understood the tongue of [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md) that was still spoken in those regions.<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the [Deeping Wall](../D/Deeping_Wall.md) was taken, Gamling was one of those who retreated into the narrows. When the battle was over Gamling, [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md), [Gimli](Gimli.md) and others came forth from the caves.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Gamling is an [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name. It is derived from the stem *gamol*, which means "old", but is only found in Old English in verse-language<sup id="cite_ref-N_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-4">[4]</a></sup>

Gamling is somewhat anglicized/modernized while genuinely it should be *Gameling*. It would be one of the words and names that hobbits recognized as similar to [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) (like *[Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md)* or *[Wormtongue](Gríma.md)*).<sup id="cite_ref-N_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Tolkien noted that it is the origin of the surnames Gamlen, Gam(b)lin. It appears in the medieval poem *The Tale of Gamelin* (inspiration of Shakespeare's <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/As_You_Like_It" title="wikipedia:As You Like It">As You Like It</a></i>)<sup id="cite_ref-N_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-4">[4]</a></sup>

Later, Gamling rides to <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> with Theoden&#39;s riders and survives the <a href="../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>.</dd></dl>
