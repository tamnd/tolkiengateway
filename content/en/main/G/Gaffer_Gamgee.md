**Hamfast Gamgee**, also known as **the Gaffer**, was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), and the father of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring). He was the chief gardener at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End), though in his later years he had passed most of the work onto his son, Sam.

## History {#History}

Hamfast was born in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2926](/wiki/Third_Age_2926 "Third Age 2926"), the third son of [Hobson Gamgee](/wiki/Hobson "Hobson"), who was a roper by trade. He had three siblings: [Andwise Roper](/wiki/Andwise_Roper), [May](</wiki/May_(hobbit)> "May (hobbit)") and [Halfred of Overhill](/wiki/Halfred_of_Overhill).<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-2">[1]</a></sup>

He entered the service of his relative, "[Cousin Holman](/wiki/Holman_Greenhand "Holman Greenhand")", and became a gardener. One of his first jobs was tending the garden of [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).<sup id="cite_ref-party_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-3">[2]</a></sup> On [22 June](/wiki/22_June), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2942](/wiki/Third_Age_2942 "Third Age 2942"), he and Holman frantically tried to keep the garden in order during the auction of the presumed-dead [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins).

Hamfast resided in Number 3, [Bagshot Row](/wiki/Bagshot_Row) and [Daddy Twofoot](/wiki/Daddy_Twofoot) was his next-door neighbor<sup id="cite_ref-party_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-3">[2]</a></sup> and had never been as far as [Bucklebury](/wiki/Bucklebury).<sup id="cite_ref-three_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-three-4">[3]</a></sup>

Hamfast became a gardener in his own right in about [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2961](/wiki/Third_Age_2961 "Third Age 2961"), and a local expert on [potatoes](/wiki/Potatoes "Potatoes") and other roots; "Master Hamfast" (as Bilbo called him) appreciated Bilbo's politeness and respecting his knowledge.<sup id="cite_ref-party_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-3">[2]</a></sup> He married [Bell Goodchild](/wiki/Bell_Gamgee "Bell Gamgee"), and they had six children: [Hamson](/wiki/Hamson_Gamgee "Hamson Gamgee"), [Halfred](/wiki/Halfred_Gamgee "Halfred Gamgee"), [Daisy](/wiki/Daisy_Gamgee "Daisy Gamgee"), [May](/wiki/May_Gamgee "May Gamgee"), Samwise and [Marigold](/wiki/Marigold_Gamgee "Marigold Gamgee").<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-2">[1]</a></sup> Sam became Hamfast's apprentice.

Sam took over most of his father's work, who had become too old for the laborous work.<sup id="cite_ref-party_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-3">[2]</a></sup> He became known as the "Gaffer", a name Sam uses more often than "Hamfast".

Prior to the [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party"), the younger [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") sought information about the legendary Mr. Bilbo Baggins at the *[Ivy Bush](/wiki/Ivy_Bush)*. Always a man of loquacity and knowledge, he argued with [Sandyman](/wiki/Sandyman), [Old Noakes](/wiki/Old_Noakes) and Daddy Twofoot about the [Bagginses](/wiki/Baggins_Family "Baggins Family") and the [Sackville-Bagginses](/wiki/Sackville-Baggins_Family "Sackville-Baggins Family"). After the party, Bilbo left him two sacks of potatoes, a new spade, a woolen waistcoat, and a bottle of ointment as he suffered from creaking joints.<sup id="cite_ref-party_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-3">[2]</a></sup> By [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") he was beginning to lose his sight.<sup id="cite_ref-three_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-three-4">[3]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), his new neighbor, [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) decided to leave the Shire, and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) had obliged Sam to go with him. Frodo pretended to be moving to [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland), and sold Bag End to Lobelia, for which Hamfast was not so happy. He allowed Sam to go with Frodo to help and tend the garden of his new house, as he said.<sup id="cite_ref-three_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-three-4">[3]</a></sup>

On [23 September](/wiki/23_September), the night of the departure of Frodo and Sam, [Khamûl](/wiki/Kham%C3%BBl)<sup id="cite_ref-Note1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note1-5">[4]</a></sup>, one of the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) rode up the lane as the elderly hobbit was taking his air, and asked about "[Baggins](/wiki/Baggins_Family "Baggins Family")". The Gaffer told him that he had left for [Bucklebury](/wiki/Bucklebury) and it's not their business to know why, and refused to leave any message. The stranger hissed, making Hamfast shudder. Soon after the Nazgûl left, Sam arrived to give Hamfast the keys to Bag End before leaving. Surprised by Sam's arrival, the Gaffer told Sam about the stranger.<sup id="cite_ref-three_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-three-4">[3]</a></sup> [A week later](/wiki/29_September "29 September"), he was visited by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) who he also informed about Frodo's whereabouts.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

When [Lotho](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins "Lotho Sackville-Baggins") and [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey) took over the Shire, the Gaffer was evicted from Bagshot Row.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup> After the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater), in which he had only a marginal role, he moved back into the newly restored Bagshot Row, the "[New Row](/wiki/New_Row)", where he was looked after by the [Widow Rumble](/wiki/Widow_Rumble): a fact which Frodo uses to comfort Sam upon Sam and [Rosie](/wiki/Rosie_Cotton "Rosie Cotton") moving into [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). After Sam's first [child](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner "Elanor Gardner") is born, Sam calls on his father to decide upon a name. Gaffer tells him to stick with the tradition of naming hobbit-girls after flowers which prompts Frodo to suggest the name "[Elanor](</wiki/Elanor_(flower)> "Elanor (flower)")".

After the return of Frodo, Sam and company to the Shire, the Gaffer lived for another 7 years, dying in [Fourth Age 7](/wiki/Fourth_Age_7).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> Four years later, Sam and Rosie named their seventh child [Hamfast](/wiki/Hamfast_Gardner "Hamfast Gardner") in his honour.<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Hamfast* is a modernization of Old English *hám-fæst*, meaning "Stay-at-home", or literally, "Home-fast". It is a translation of his [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name, [Ranugad](/wiki/Ranugad) [Galbasi](/wiki/Galbasi).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup> In the [King's Letter](/wiki/King%27s_Letter), the name (referring to [Hamfast Gardner](/wiki/Hamfast_Gardner), the fourth son of Sam and Rose, not the Gaffer) is translated into [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) as [Baravorn](/wiki/Baravorn).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

"<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/gaffer" title="wiktionary:gaffer">Gaffer</a>" is a colloquial word for "old man". It is a contraction of "grandfather."

## In other stories {#In_other_stories}

In the illustrated short story *[Mr. Bliss](/wiki/Mr._Bliss)*, there is a resident in the village named Gaffer Gamgee who struggles to listen to the discussion between Mr Binks and Sergeant [Boffin](/wiki/Boffin_Family "Boffin Family") about whether Mr Bliss should be locked up for failure to pay for his car. Sergeant Boffin also has a son named [Sam](/wiki/Sam).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In [Letter 257](/wiki/Letter_257), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) recounts a holiday to Lamorna Cave in the early 1930's. To amuse his sons, he named a local Gaffer Gamgee. This "curious local character" was "an old man who used to go about swapping gossip and weather-fashion and such like". The surname was chosen because it alliterated with Gaffer.

Tolkien had several encounters with actual Gamgees, however. <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamgee_Tissue" title="wikipedia:Gamgee Tissue">Gamgee Tissue</a>, a word young Ronald considered comical, was named after <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sampson_Gamgee" title="wikipedia:Sampson Gamgee">J. Sampson Gamgee</a> (1828-1886), a famous [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham) surgeon,<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup> whilst in the Black Country dialect cotton wool was referred to as "gamgee".<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup> The Gamgees were notorious surgeons - years later, Dr. Leonard Gamgee tended those war-wounded, including Lieutenant Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup>

<b>1978: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Gaffer does not appear in the film, though Sam mentions that he saw him thrown out onto the street in his vision as he looks into the <a href="/wiki/Mirror_of_Galadriel" title="Mirror of Galadriel">Mirror of Galadriel</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/John_Church" title="John Church">John Church</a> plays Gaffer Gamgee. He used a thick peasant accent for the role. He is included in early episodes including a full dramatisation of his encounter with the Nazgul. Neither Frodo or Sam learn of this exchange. He also features towards the end of the adaptation when he admonishes the returning Hobbits for wearing &#34;ironmongery.&#34; The Gaffer also is present as the travelers discuss how Sam should use Galadriel’s gift.</dd></dl>

<b>1992: <a href="/wiki/Der_Herr_der_Ringe_(1992_German_radio_series)" title="Der Herr der Ringe (1992 German radio series)"><i>Der Herr der Ringe</i> (1992 German radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Hamfast &#34;Gaffer&#34; Gamgee is played by Wolfgang Reinsch.</dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gaffer appears in the film&#39;s <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)">Extended Edition</a>, where he is played by <a href="/wiki/Norman_Forsey" title="Norman Forsey">Norman Forsey</a>. The conversation in the Ivy Bush is moved to the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Green_Dragon" title="Green Dragon">Green Dragon</a> Inn, though the dialogue remains much the same. He does not appear at Sam&#39;s marriage in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Return of the King</a></i>.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gaffer appears twice; at first, he drinks at the Green Dragon, but during the night, he informs Frodo of the Ringwraith that stalks the Shire. He was voiced by an uncredited <a href="/wiki/Jim_Piddock" title="Jim Piddock">Jim Piddock</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Hamfast Gamgee is found working in his garden just outside Bag End. He only offers one regional Shire quest, but has more story involvement during various seasonal festivals.</dd></dl>
