**Gold** refers to both a valuable mineral in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and golden colour or golden light.

## History {#History}

Gold was present in the deeps of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) since its creation and [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB), the great smith was the first to make use of it. Afterwards, it was to be used by all speaking peoples of earth particularly the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) who mastered it and by the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor).

One of the [Seven Gates of Gondolin](/wiki/Seven_Gates_of_Gondolin) was the [Gate of Gold](/wiki/Gate_of_Gold).

Although valuable, the metal was not nearly as valuable as *[mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril")*. [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) stated that when the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) mined *mithril* in [Moria](/wiki/Moria) it was worth ten times the value of gold and that after the coming of [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane) mithril was beyond price).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) also prized [silver](/wiki/Silver "Silver") (which they called *[telpe](/wiki/Telpe "Telpe")*) above gold.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[The One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring) was made of gold,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> as was [Vilya](/wiki/Vilya), the Elven Ring of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The treasure of [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) also contained gold, wrought and unwrought.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Sometimes, gold was a prerequisite for such '[magic](/wiki/Magic "Magic")' and other evils as [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) practised with it and upon it. It was said that the power of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) was disseminated throughout this element in the matter of [Arda](/wiki/Arda); however, his sway over it was not absolute, since Morgoth did not create gold in the first place.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[malta](/wiki/Malta "Malta")*, *[laurë](/wiki/Laur%C3%AB "Laurë")* (the colour, not the metal), *[culu-](/wiki/KUL "KUL")*
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)/[Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *[malt](/wiki/Malt "Malt")*
- [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic): *[pharaz](/wiki/Pharaz "Pharaz")*

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales) a portion of gold was used in the creation of [Tilkal](/wiki/Tilkal), to construct [Angainor](/wiki/Angainor).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), the word for "gold" is *glôr* (*glôriol* means "golden, like gold", and *glôrin* means "golden, of gold"). The poetic word for "gold" is *Culu*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
