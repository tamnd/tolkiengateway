The **Greater Gelion** was one of the two rivers that flowed together to form the source of long [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion), that flowed along the eastern borders of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). The Greater Gelion rose beneath [Mount Rerir](/wiki/Mount_Rerir) in the northern [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains), and flowed southwestwards to meet the [Little Gelion](/wiki/Little_Gelion).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It formed the eastern boundary of the lands controlled by [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor) during most of the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
