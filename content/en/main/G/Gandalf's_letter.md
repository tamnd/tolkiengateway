**Gandalf's letter** is a letter that [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) wrote and left to [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur), to be delivered to [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-Strider_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strider-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Background {#Background}

Gandalf had told Frodo that they should take [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell); he promised to return to Hobbiton in late September when they would leave. He advised him to adopt the name "[Mr. Underhill](/wiki/Mr._Underhill)" when outside [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was [Mid-year's Day](/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day) and on his way he heard disturbing news from [Radagast](/wiki/Radagast), that [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") were sent from [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) to seek the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-council_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-3">[3]</a></sup> He came hastily to the [*Prancing Pony*](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony "The Prancing Pony") and wrote a warning letter to Frodo.<sup id="cite_ref-Strider_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strider-1">[1]</a></sup> Gandalf then departed for [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) to seek advice from [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman).<sup id="cite_ref-council_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Content {#Content}

In the letter, written in his "strong but graceful script", Gandalf warned Frodo that he might not return when promised and that Frodo should leave as soon as possible, preferably before [August](/wiki/August); whenever Gandalf would be available, he would come to the Shire and try to catch up with them. If Frodo ever came to [Bree](/wiki/Bree) he should trust [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur) and leave a message for Gandalf. He also mentioned that Frodo might meet a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") in the area named [Strider](</wiki/Strider_(Aragorn)> "Strider (Aragorn)") (whom Gandalf had already met and informed on [May 1](/wiki/May_1)) who could assist him.

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_G_for_Gandalf.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/8/84/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_G_for_Gandalf.png/50px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_G_for_Gandalf.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="50" height="72" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/8/84/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_G_for_Gandalf.png/75px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_G_for_Gandalf.png 1.5x, /w/images/8/84/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_G_for_Gandalf.png 2x" data-file-width="90" data-file-height="129"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

He concluded the letter with 3 warnings: that he should not wear the Ring again, and avoid traveling during night; he included [a rhyme](/wiki/Riddle_of_Strider "Riddle of Strider") which would prove Strider's real identity, which is [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn); and finally, he mentioned his worries that the forgetful Barliman might not send this letter in time. The letter was signed with the [certh](/wiki/Cirth "Cirth") "G" for Gandalf.<sup id="cite_ref-Strider_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strider-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Afterwards {#Afterwards}

Before leaving Bree, Gandalf instructed Barliman to send this letter to Hobbiton as soon as possible. He also told him to expect a "Mr. Underhill", whose real name was Baggins, giving Frodo's detailed description. However Gandalf's worries proved to be correct: He was captured in [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc) and therefore could not reach Frodo on their assigned date.<sup id="cite_ref-council_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-3">[3]</a></sup> Furthermore, Barliman could not find someone who would travel to Hobbiton, and day after day, this obligation was forgotten.<sup id="cite_ref-Strider_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strider-1">[1]</a></sup>

Frodo never received the letter and kept on waiting for Gandalf in Hobbiton.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Having no sign from the Wizard, he departed on [September 23](/wiki/September_23),<sup id="cite_ref-great_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-5">[5]</a></sup> much later than Gandalf would have instructed him. Meanwhile Gandalf had escaped Orthanc and rode [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax) to the Shire, less than a week after Frodo's departure.<sup id="cite_ref-great_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-5">[5]</a></sup>

It was not until "Mr. Underhill" came to the *Prancing Pony* with [a company of 3 hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") when Butterbur made the connection. Strider happened also to be there and both he and the hobbits tried to discern each other's trustfullness. Butterbur arrived with the letter which proved to Frodo that Strider was Gandalf's friend, and Strider realized that Frodo was the halfling Gandalf told him about. However all were worried in absence of news from the Wizard.<sup id="cite_ref-Strider_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strider-1">[1]</a></sup>

The hobbits resumed their travel to Rivendell with Strider the next morning,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> without knowing that Gandalf would arrive to Bree that same night.<sup id="cite_ref-great_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-5">[5]</a></sup>
