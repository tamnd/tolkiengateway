The **Great Hall of Feasts** ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) **Merethrond**)<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-1">[1]</a></sup> was a feasting-hall to the right (i.e. north) of the [Tower of Ecthelion](/wiki/Tower_of_Ecthelion) in the [Citadel](/wiki/Citadel_of_Gondor "Citadel of Gondor"), the topmost seventh circle of walls of the city of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [July 18](/wiki/July_18) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [King](/wiki/King_of_Rohan "King of Rohan") [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) returned to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) with an [éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored "Éored") of his knights and participated in a feast that was held by the recently crowned King [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in the Great Hall of Feasts.<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Merethrond* is a Sindarin name,<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-5">[5]</a></sup> which means "Great Hall of Feasts"<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-1">[1]</a></sup> or "Hall of Feasts".<sup id="cite_ref-SA_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-6">[6]</a></sup> The first element of the name is *mereth* ("feast").<sup id="cite_ref-SA_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-6">[6]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests that the second element is *[rond](/wiki/Rond "Rond")* ("vaulted or arched roof", "large hall or chamber so roofed").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-5">[5]</a></sup>
