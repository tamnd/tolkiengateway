The **Great Hall of Feasts** ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) **Merethrond**)<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-1">[1]</a></sup> was a feasting-hall to the right (i.e. north) of the [Tower of Ecthelion](../T/Tower_of_Ecthelion.md) in the [Citadel](../C/Citadel_of_Gondor.md), the topmost seventh circle of walls of the city of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [July 18](../J/July_18.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [King](../K/King_of_Rohan.md) [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) returned to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) with an [éored](../_/Éored.md) of his knights and participated in a feast that was held by the recently crowned King [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) in the Great Hall of Feasts.<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Merethrond* is a Sindarin name,<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-5">[5]</a></sup> which means "Great Hall of Feasts"<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-1">[1]</a></sup> or "Hall of Feasts".<sup id="cite_ref-SA_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-6">[6]</a></sup> The first element of the name is *mereth* ("feast").<sup id="cite_ref-SA_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-6">[6]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests that the second element is *[rond](../R/Rond.md)* ("vaulted or arched roof", "large hall or chamber so roofed").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-5">[5]</a></sup>
