**Gimilzagar** was the second son of [Tar-Calmacil](/wiki/Tar-Calmacil). He was an ancestor of [Inzilbêth](/wiki/Inzilb%C3%AAth), the mother of [Tar-Palantir](/wiki/Tar-Palantir).<sup id="cite_ref-Gimilzagar_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gimilzagar-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gimilzagar* is an [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it likely means "Sword of Stars", being a compound of *[gimil](/wiki/Gimil "Gimil")* ("stars") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Zagar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Zagar (page does not exist)">zagar</a></i> ("sword").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
