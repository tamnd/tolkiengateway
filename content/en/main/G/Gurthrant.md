The **Gurthrant** is the river in [Nurn](/wiki/Nurn) in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) that flows from the southwestern corner of the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) mountains northeastwards into the [Sea of Nurnen](/wiki/Sea_of_Nurnen) on its western shore. The river is unnamed in the [maps](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth "General Map of Middle-earth") that were drayn by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) based on the maps drawn by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) for publication with *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

## Chronology and Creation {#Chronology_and_Creation}

It first appeared on a merchandise map of Mordor for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* that was drawn by [Daniel Reeve](/wiki/Daniel_Reeve)<sup id="cite_ref-reeve-site_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-site-1">[1]</a></sup> in 2003<sup id="cite_ref-reeve-mail_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-mail-2">[2]</a></sup>.

## Fan works {#Fan_works}

The fansite [Thelandofshadow.com](/wiki/Thelandofshadow.com) took on several of the names introduced by Reeve, and expanded on them. The Gurthrant was described as follows:<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Tributary to the Sea of Nurnen - One of four rivers that fed the Sea of Nurnen. Its origin was said to be in the Dark Valley of Nan Ungol at the base of the Mountains of Shadow. It flowed across the plain of Nurn until it fell down across a great rift and then into the Sea of Nurnen. Its waters were dark and cold and bitter to the taste.
</p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

The information from The Land of Shadow was incorporated in a slightly modified form into the fan made *Mordor Gazetteer* of the Middle-earth Role Playing (MERP) fan modules group.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gurthrant* seems to be a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which means "Deathlode". It seems to contain the elements *[gurth](/wiki/Gurth "Gurth")* ("death")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and *[rant](/wiki/Rant "Rant")* ("lode", "water-channel", "course")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>.
