Poor **Galathea** was [Farmer Giles](../F/Farmer_Giles.md)' favorite cow who was squashed flat when the [giant](../T/The_Giant.md) stepped on her as he came blundering across the farmer's lands one fine summer's night. Her demise prompted [Garm](Garm.md) the dog to awaken Giles to tell him of the giant's arrival, claiming that Galathea was as flat as a doormat.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The mythological name *Galathea* "milk-white" (fem.)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> is probably chosen because milk refers to cows in general – or it refers to the actual colour of the cow (which is not mentioned in the story).
