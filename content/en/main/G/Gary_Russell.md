**Gary Russell** ([September 18](/wiki/18_September "18 September"), [1963](/wiki/1963)) is a freelance writer and former child actor (of "<a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0078611/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Famous Five</a>" fame), who has worked as a writer for franchises as Marvel and Dr. Who. He wrote several books relating to [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson)'s [movies](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").
