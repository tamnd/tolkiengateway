**Gelmir** was the son of [Golfinweg](/wiki/Golfinweg), the first [Gnome](/wiki/Gnomes "Gnomes") King, according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)* and *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Gel_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gel-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Following his father's death, Gelmir led the Gnomes to the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands). He was slain in the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium), and his son [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) died trying to save [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), who was caught and tortured after the battle.<sup id="cite_ref-Gel_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gel-1">[1]</a></sup>

Only [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon), Gelmir's grandson, along with a remnant of his people, as well as the [sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor"), managed to escape from the battle.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Gelmir* is in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), but its meaning is unclear. Its [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognate is <i><b>Nelmer</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Gel_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gel-1">[1]</a></sup>
