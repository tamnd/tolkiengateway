The **Grove** was an orchard within eastern [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) somewhere between the [East Bight](../E/East_Bight.md) of the [Greenwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) and [Dorwinion](../D/Dorwinion.md). The Grove was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md).

## History {#History}

The Grove was once a settlement of [Big Folk](../B/Big_Folk.md), who possibly planted the gardens before they abandoned the orchard or were wiped out long ago. The Grove eventually became one of the seasonal havens of a tribe of [Harfoots](../H/Harfoots.md) that migrated there in the autumn relying upon fruit that grows there such as carrots and apricots.

During the late [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), the fruit-trees of the Grove were destroyed by some debris from the eruption of [Orodruin](../M/Mount_Doom.md) within the [Southlands](../M/Mordor.md). Despite this, however, [The Stranger](../T/The_Stranger.md) healed the orchard when asked to do so by [Sadoc Burrows](../S/Sadoc_Burrows.md).

Later, after the Stranger departed the Grove, the [Mystics](../M/Mystics.md) arrived and used a yellow flower growing on the main tree to pinpoint the direction that the Stranger went. When [Elanor Brandyfoot](../E/Elanor_Brandyfoot.md) tried and failed to mislead them, the Dweller plucked a strand of Elanor's hair and burned the Harfoots' carts, she fled after the Stranger with the other Mystics. Yet they were followed by a company of brave Harfoots made up of Sadoc Burrows, [Marigold Brandyfoot](../M/Marigold_Brandyfoot.md), Elanor Brandyfoot, and [Poppy Proudfellow](../P/Poppy_Proudfellow.md).

After the Mystics were defeated and the company came back to the Grove, the Stranger departed for [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md) accompanied by Elanor Brandyfoot while [Malva Meadowgrass](../M/Malva_Meadowgrass.md), [Vilma](../V/Vilma.md), and the other elders departed the Grove, leading the Harfoot community on a migration to presumably the [Old Forest](../M/Mirkwood.md) for winter.
