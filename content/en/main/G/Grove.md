The **Grove** was an orchard within eastern [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) somewhere between the [East Bight](/wiki/East_Bight) of the [Greenwood](/wiki/Mirkwood "Mirkwood") and [Dorwinion](/wiki/Dorwinion). The Grove was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios).

## History {#History}

The Grove was once a settlement of [Big Folk](/wiki/Big_Folk), who possibly planted the gardens before they abandoned the orchard or were wiped out long ago. The Grove eventually became one of the seasonal havens of a tribe of [Harfoots](/wiki/Harfoots) that migrated there in the autumn relying upon fruit that grows there such as carrots and apricots.

During the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the fruit-trees of the Grove were destroyed by some debris from the eruption of [Orodruin](/wiki/Mount_Doom "Mount Doom") within the [Southlands](/wiki/Mordor "Mordor"). Despite this, however, [The Stranger](/wiki/The_Stranger) healed the orchard when asked to do so by [Sadoc Burrows](/wiki/Sadoc_Burrows).

Later, after the Stranger departed the Grove, the [Mystics](/wiki/Mystics) arrived and used a yellow flower growing on the main tree to pinpoint the direction that the Stranger went. When [Elanor Brandyfoot](/wiki/Elanor_Brandyfoot) tried and failed to mislead them, the Dweller plucked a strand of Elanor's hair and burned the Harfoots' carts, she fled after the Stranger with the other Mystics. Yet they were followed by a company of brave Harfoots made up of Sadoc Burrows, [Marigold Brandyfoot](/wiki/Marigold_Brandyfoot), Elanor Brandyfoot, and [Poppy Proudfellow](/wiki/Poppy_Proudfellow).

After the Mystics were defeated and the company came back to the Grove, the Stranger departed for [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn) accompanied by Elanor Brandyfoot while [Malva Meadowgrass](/wiki/Malva_Meadowgrass), [Vilma](/wiki/Vilma), and the other elders departed the Grove, leading the Harfoot community on a migration to presumably the [Old Forest](/wiki/Mirkwood "Mirkwood") for winter.
