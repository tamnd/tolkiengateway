**Guritz**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") Lieutenant who served under [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(Lieutenant_of_Morgul)> "Gothmog (Lieutenant of Morgul)") in the [Siege of Gondor](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor) and [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).

Guritz is an original creation for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* film. He is portrayed physically by [Joel Tobeck](/wiki/Joel_Tobeck)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and his voice is provided by [Craig Parker](/wiki/Craig_Parker).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Biography {#Biography}

When an enemy scarred Guritz across the face, the Orc decapitated him and fixed his head to the top of his helmet.<sup id="cite_ref-TCG_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TCG-4">[4]</a></sup>

During the [Siege of Gondor](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor), [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(Lieutenant_of_Morgul)> "Gothmog (Lieutenant of Morgul)") ordered Guritz to "release the prisoners". Guritz yelled "Catapults", prompting the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) to fire the decapitated heads of the Gondorian prisoners from [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) into [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

During the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), Guritz waited for the [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar) in [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) with a battalion of Orcs. When the Corsairs arrived, he remarked that they were late, telling them to get off the ships, as they were "knifework" needing doing. The Orc was surprised to see the [Three Hunters](/wiki/Three_Hunters) jump from the ship. Smiling, due to the small number of enemies, Guritz readied his Scythe. When [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) charged forward, however, an overwhelming army of [undead warriors](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") followed them. Guritz stumbled back in surprise and was trampled by the army.

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game)'s *Mount Doom* expansion identifies him as an "Advance Captain",<sup id="cite_ref-TCG_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TCG-4">[4]</a></sup> in contrast with [the film's](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King") credits, which give [Tobeck](/wiki/Joel_Tobeck "Joel Tobeck") the credit of "Orc Lieutenant I". The title of Captain may be more accurate, however, as [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(Lieutenant_of_Morgul)> "Gothmog (Lieutenant of Morgul)") holds the position of lieutenant and Guritz is clearly depicted as his subordinate.
