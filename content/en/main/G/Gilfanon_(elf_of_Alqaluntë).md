**Gilfanon** was an [Elf](/wiki/Solosimpi "Solosimpi") of [Alqaluntë](/wiki/Alqalunt%C3%AB), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in a prose fragment following *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Prose_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prose-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) marched with the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) upon [Cú nan Eilch](/wiki/C%C3%BA_nan_Eilch), Gilfanon was one of the [Solosimpi](/wiki/Solosimpi) who fought against them when he saw his swan-feather boat being stolen. He, along with his sons, threatened the Gnomes, and in the ensuing fight, he was thrown into the sea, but not before uttering a curse.<sup id="cite_ref-Prose_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prose-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *The Book of Lost Tales* there appears another character called **[Gilfanon](/wiki/Gilfanon)**, who was a Gnome of [Tavrobel](/wiki/Tavrobel), but [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) is certain that the two are entirely separate characters, and that the *Gilfanon* of Tavrobel had probably ceased to be named as such at this point in the legendarium.<sup id="cite_ref-Prose_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prose-1">[1]</a></sup>
