**Gilfanon** was an [Elf](../S/Solosimpi.md) of [Alqaluntë](../A/Alqaluntë.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in a prose fragment following *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Prose_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prose-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) marched with the [Gnomes](Gnomes.md) upon [Cú nan Eilch](../C/Cú_nan_Eilch.md), Gilfanon was one of the [Solosimpi](../S/Solosimpi.md) who fought against them when he saw his swan-feather boat being stolen. He, along with his sons, threatened the Gnomes, and in the ensuing fight, he was thrown into the sea, but not before uttering a curse.<sup id="cite_ref-Prose_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prose-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *The Book of Lost Tales* there appears another character called **[Gilfanon](Gilfanon.md)**, who was a Gnome of [Tavrobel](../T/Tavrobel.md), but [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) is certain that the two are entirely separate characters, and that the *Gilfanon* of Tavrobel had probably ceased to be named as such at this point in the legendarium.<sup id="cite_ref-Prose_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prose-1">[1]</a></sup>
