**Gelmir** can refer to:

## Elves of the First Age {#Elves_of_the_First_Age}

Two [Elves](/wiki/Elves), who lived during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age):

- [Gelmir (son of Guilin)](</wiki/Gelmir_(son_of_Guilin)>), son of [Guilin](/wiki/Guilin) who was captured by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s servants in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach).
- [Gelmir (messenger of Círdan)](</wiki/Gelmir_(messenger_of_C%C3%ADrdan)>), a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") sent by [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) as a messenger to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond).

## Earlier writings {#Earlier_writings}

Other characters who were named *Gelmir* in earlier writings:

- [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB), father of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), who was named *Gelmir* in *[The Lay of the Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Fall_of_Gondolin)* and in one of the [Prose Fragments Following the Lost Tales](/wiki/Prose_Fragments_Following_the_Lost_Tales).
- [Gelmir (son of Golfinweg)](</wiki/Gelmir_(son_of_Golfinweg)>), a king of the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) in the <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Index_of_Names_for_The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Index of Names for The Lay of the Children of Húrin (page does not exist)">Index of Names for The Lay of the Children of Húrin</a></i>.
