**Gelmir** can refer to:

## Elves of the First Age {#Elves_of_the_First_Age}

Two [Elves](../E/Elves.md), who lived during the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md):

- [Gelmir (son of Guilin)](<Gelmir_(son_of_Guilin).md>), son of [Guilin](Guilin.md) who was captured by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s servants in the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md).
- [Gelmir (messenger of Círdan)](<Gelmir_(messenger_of_Círdan).md>), a [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) [Elf](../E/Elves.md) sent by [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) as a messenger to [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md).

## Earlier writings {#Earlier_writings}

Other characters who were named *Gelmir* in earlier writings:

- [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md), father of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md), who was named *Gelmir* in *[The Lay of the Fall of Gondolin](../T/The_Lay_of_the_Fall_of_Gondolin.md)* and in one of the [Prose Fragments Following the Lost Tales](../P/Prose_Fragments_Following_the_Lost_Tales.md).
- [Gelmir (son of Golfinweg)](<Gelmir_(son_of_Golfinweg).md>), a king of the [Gnomes](Gnomes.md) in the <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Index_of_Names_for_The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Index of Names for The Lay of the Children of Húrin (page does not exist)">Index of Names for The Lay of the Children of Húrin</a></i>.
