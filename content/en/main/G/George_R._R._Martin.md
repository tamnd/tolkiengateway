**George R. R. Martin** (born [20 September](/wiki/20_September), [1948](/wiki/1948))<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> is an American author who primarily writes fantasy and science fiction. He is best known for <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Song_of_Ice_and_Fire" title="wikipedia:A Song of Ice and Fire">A Song of Ice and Fire</a></i>, a series of epic fantasy novels influenced by the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Adam Roberts](/wiki/Adam_Roberts) recognises Tolkien's obvious influences on Martin's work, but considers him an "anti-Tolkien", with the *Song* being a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/grimdark" title="wikipedia:grimdark">grimdark</a> reaction to Tolkien's idealism.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The *Song* was adapted in television as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_Thrones" title="wikipedia:Game of Thrones">Game of Thrones</a></i>, with some cast and crew from [*The Lord of the Rings* trilogy](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") being involved in the series, such as actor [Sean Bean](/wiki/Sean_Bean), and [Weta Digital](/wiki/Weta_Digital) providing the visual effects.
