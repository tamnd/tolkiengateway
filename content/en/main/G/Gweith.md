<i><b>gweith</b></i> is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) noun meaning "manhood". The meaning is often extended to mean "man-power, troop of able-bodied men, host, regiment".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") WEG ("manly vigour")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
