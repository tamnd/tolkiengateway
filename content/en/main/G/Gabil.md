<i><b>gabil</b></i> is a [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md) element only appearing the compound names *[Gabilgathol](Gabilgathol.md)* and *[Gabilān](Gabilān.md)*. As *Gabilān* is glossed as "great river",<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup> and *Gabilgathol* is said to be have been translated into *[Belegost](../B/Belegost.md)* ("Great Fortress"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> one can, as [Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) has suggested, infer that *Gabil* probably means "great".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
