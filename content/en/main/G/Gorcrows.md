**Gorcrows** were a legendary species of birds, only mentioned in passing in [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") verse. These foul [crows](/wiki/Crows "Crows"), who croaked in their sleep, are said to have lived in the same damp marshes as the fearsome [Mewlips](/wiki/Mewlips).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gorcrow*, "gore crow", is an [old English](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") name for <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrion_Crow" title="wikipedia:Carrion Crow">carrion crow</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><i>Gorcrows</i> are said to have 4 ft. wingspan and to be smaller cousins of the <i><a href="/wiki/Crebain" title="Crebain">crebain</a></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and to be attracted to shiny things.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> The <i>northern gorcrow</i>, living in <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Forodwaith_(lands)" title="Forodwaith (lands)">Forodwaith</a>, is described as a larger version of the common gorcrow. The <a href="/wiki/Lossoth" title="Lossoth">Lossoth</a> are said to regard the northern gorcrows as birds of ill-omen.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><i>Gore-crows</i> are a type of crebain native to the <a href="/wiki/Lone-lands" title="Lone-lands">Lone-lands</a>. They also appear in great number in Mordor.</dd></dl>
