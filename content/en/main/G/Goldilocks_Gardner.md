**Goldilocks Gardner** (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1431](../F/Fourth_Age_10.md)) was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Goldilocks was the third daughter of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Rose Cotton](../R/Rose_Cotton.md), so named because her fair hair was rare among the [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md), but not among Sam's children.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup> In [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1463](../F/Fourth_Age_42.md) she married [Thain](../T/Thain.md) [Faramir I](../F/Faramir_Took_I.md), son of [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Although the name refers to her hair, it is also in accordance to the Hobbit custom of giving flower-names to women. Goldilocks sometimes refers to buttercup or daisy flowers.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) translation of her name appearing in the *[King's Letter](../K/King's_Letter.md)* was <i><b>Glorfinniel</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
