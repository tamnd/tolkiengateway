**Goldilocks Gardner** (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1431](/wiki/Fourth_Age_10 "Fourth Age 10")) was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Goldilocks was the third daughter of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton), so named because her fair hair was rare among the [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits), but not among Sam's children.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup> In [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1463](/wiki/Fourth_Age_42 "Fourth Age 42") she married [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Faramir I](/wiki/Faramir_Took_I "Faramir Took I"), son of [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Although the name refers to her hair, it is also in accordance to the Hobbit custom of giving flower-names to women. Goldilocks sometimes refers to buttercup or daisy flowers.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) translation of her name appearing in the *[King's Letter](/wiki/King%27s_Letter)* was <i><b>Glorfinniel</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
