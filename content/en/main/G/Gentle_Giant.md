**Gentle Giant Studios** is a company located in Burbank, California that was founded by animator Karl Meyer. The company is a pioneer in digital 3-D scanning and scanned many of the principal actors from [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") while on set/location in [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) during filming.

The company also has a collectibles division, known as **Gentle Giant, Ltd.**. Founded in 2002, they produce polystone mini-busts, animated maquettes and statues from *Peter Jackson's The Lord of the Rings*.

## Collectibles {#Collectibles}

### Mini-busts {#Mini-busts}

- Strider Ringbearer - Limited to 2000 pieces - release date February 2007
- Ringwraith Ringbearer - Limited to 2000 pieces - release date February 2007
- Gandalf - Limited to 3,000 pieces - release date May 2007
- Glorfindel - Limited to 500 Pieces - San Diego Comic-Con 2007 exclusive

As a special promotion, Gentle Giant Ltd. randomly inserted 100 of the One Ring from [Noble Collection](/wiki/Noble_Collection) - Sterling Edition into the secret compartments of the Strider and Ringwraith busts.
