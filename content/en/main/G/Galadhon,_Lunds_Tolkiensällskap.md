**Caras Galadhon, Lunds Tolkiensällskap** is the Swedish Tolkien Society of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund" title="wikipedia:Lund">Lund</a>. The members of the association, who mainly consist of students and staff at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund_University" title="wikipedia:Lund University">Lund University</a>, assume the role of a character in the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), and meet for parties and other social gatherings (such as studies of the languages and works created by Tolkien, gaming, dancing, etc.).
