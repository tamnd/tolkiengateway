<i><b>golodh</b></i> (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈɡoloð]</span>), plural <i><b>gelydh</b></i> (<span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈɡelyð]</span>, later <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈɡelið]</span>) or older <i><b>gœlydh</b></i> (<span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈɡølyð]</span>), is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for "one of the wise folk", a *Noldo*, pl. *[Noldor](/wiki/Noldor)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The class plural form <i><b>Golodhrim</b></i> (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ɡoˈloðrim]</span>) contains *[-rim](/wiki/Rim "Rim")* as a collective plural ending.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
