The **Great Horn** was an heirloom of the [House of Húrin](../H/House_of_Húrin.md) until its destruction on [26 February](../_/26_February.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md).

The horn was made from the wild white [Kine of Araw](../K/Kine_of_Araw.md) of [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> which was likened to an <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurochs" title="wikipedia:Aurochs">aurochs</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was white itself, tipped with silver and inscribed with ancient characters.<sup id="cite_ref-Window_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Window-3">[3]</a></sup> It was said in legend that if the Horn was blown anywhere within the bounds of ancient Gondor, its call would not pass unheeded.<sup id="cite_ref-Window_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Window-3">[3]</a></sup>

It is generally referred to simply as "the horn of Boromir"<sup id="cite_ref-Window_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Window-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Departure_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Departure-4">[4]</a></sup> as he was its most known (and last) bearer.

## History {#History}

The Great Horn was made from the horn of a [wild-ox](../C/Cows.md) of [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md). [Vorondil the Hunter](../V/Vorondil_the_Hunter.md), the father of [Mardil Voronwë](../M/Mardil.md), hunted the beasts in the far fields of the East. So, after Mardil Voronwë it passed down to every eldest son.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-5">[5]</a></sup> From [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md), it passed down to Boromir, who wore it with him on a baldric.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-6">[6]</a></sup>

Boromir bore the horn with him when he went to [Imladris](../R/Rivendell.md), and held it on his lap during the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Council_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-6">[6]</a></sup> After he was chosen to be one of the [Fellowship](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md), he let the horn ring through Rivendell.<sup id="cite_ref-South_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-7">[7]</a></sup> He would wind the horn twice more. In reply to the meager horns of the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) in [Moria](../M/Moria.md), the Great Horn bellowed like the shout of many throats under the cavernous roof. Though it repelled the attackers for a while, they returned after the last echo disappeared.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_Boromir&#39;s_Last_Stand.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/af/Ted_Nasmith_-_Boromir%27s_Last_Stand.jpg/200px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Boromir%27s_Last_Stand.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="225" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/af/Ted_Nasmith_-_Boromir%27s_Last_Stand.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Boromir%27s_Last_Stand.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/a/af/Ted_Nasmith_-_Boromir%27s_Last_Stand.jpg 2x" data-file-width="400" data-file-height="450"/></a><figcaption><i>Boromir&#39;s Last Stand</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

The last time the horn was rung was shortly before its destruction, on [February 26](../_/26_February.md), on the slopes of [Amon Hen](../A/Amon_Hen.md), near [Parth Galen](../P/Parth_Galen.md). While fighting, Boromir created a sound so loud that it could be heard in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-5">[5]</a></sup> Sadly, the little help that could come - the rest of the Fellowship - could not make it in time. Though the Uruk-hai were at first dismayed and drew back, they returned more fierce than ever.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> In the fight that cost Boromir's life, the horn was cloven in two.

[Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Gimli](Gimli.md) and [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) laid it in the elven boat with Boromir and his weapons and sent it down the falls of [Rauros](../R/Rauros.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Departure_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Departure-4">[4]</a></sup> The two parts of the horn fell out of the boat, and were found several days later. One was found in the reeds near the mouths of [Entwash](../E/Entwash.md) on [February 28](../_/28_February.md), the other further down the river on [February 30](../_/30_February.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> They were brought to Denethor, who held them on his lap, anxiously waiting for news of his beloved son.<sup id="cite_ref-Window_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Window-3">[3]</a></sup> Those answers came, eventually, when [Gandalf](Gandalf.md) brought [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), witness to Boromir's last stand, before the Steward.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Both <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland" title="wikipedia:Roland">Roland</a>, the paladin of Charlemagne, and Boromir bore a white horn.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Like Roland in battle with the Saracens blew his horn to call for Charlemagne, Boromir called for Aragorn. In both cases, help came too late.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

<b>1978: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Boromir blows the horn after he has been hit by many arrows. Boromir&#39;s signal has two tones, the second higher than the first (it&#39;s the same sound heard by other horns, Rohirrim or Hobbits&#39;). It survived the battle without being broken.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Boromir winds the horn in Rivendell, where it produces a clear sound of five notes.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> At the advice of <a href="../P/Peregrin_Took.md" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a>, he blows it again at Amon Hen,<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> which is heard by the others.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Horn is a white horn. Boromir blows it at Amon Hen, three short blows twice. When <a href="../L/Lurtz.md" title="Lurtz">Lurtz</a> shoots arrows at Boromir, the horn appears broken when the third arrow hits him.<sup id="cite_ref-Breaking_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Breaking-17">[17]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game).md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Though Boromir does wear the horn, he does not use it.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>After telling Frodo and Sam of how he found a boat with Boromir&#39;s body on the Anduin,<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> Faramir holds the two pieces in his hands.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Denethor regained possession of the Horn, and showed it to <a href="../P/Peregrin_Took.md" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Horn of Gondor is one of Boromir&#39;s powers. It weakens nearby enemy units.<sup id="cite_ref-bfme_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bfme-21">[21]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="../L/Lego_The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Video_Game.md" title="Lego The Lord of the Rings: The Video Game">Lego The Lord of the Rings: The Video Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Horn of Gondor is among Boromir&#39;s accessories. When blown, it briefly incapacitates enemies, and can also be wielded as a melee weapon. Boromir&#39;s ghost also blows it to announce his presence at the <a href="Grey_Havens.md" title="Grey Havens">Grey Havens</a> as the Fellowship arrives to bid farewell to Frodo.</dd></dl>
