The **Golden Tree**<sup id="cite_ref-appendix_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appendix-2">[2]</a></sup> was one of the most magnificent [mellyrn](/wiki/Mellyrn "Mellyrn") in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), silver-barked and golden-leafed, and the only one of its kind to be found to the west of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-greyhavens_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greyhavens-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [Quest of Mount Doom](/wiki/Quest_of_Mount_Doom)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>, [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) was given a silver nut by [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) in [Lórien](/wiki/L%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-farewell_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-farewell-1">[1]</a></sup> This was the seed of a [mallorn](/wiki/Mallorn "Mallorn") tree.<sup id="cite_ref-greyhavens_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greyhavens-3">[3]</a></sup> These [trees](/wiki/Trees "Trees") were only found in in Lórien.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater) that marked the end of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring),<sup id="cite_ref-chiefdays_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chiefdays-6">[6]</a></sup> however, a single mallorn tree could be found elsewhere beside Lórien. During [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins)'s and [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey)'s takeover of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), the [Party Tree](/wiki/Party_Tree) in the [Party Field](/wiki/Party_Field) was cut down.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Some weeks after [3 November](/wiki/3_November) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-chiefdays_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chiefdays-6">[6]</a></sup>, Sam chose to plant his silver seed in its place. It quickly sprouted into a sapling the next spring and flowered on [6 April](/wiki/6_April) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3020](/wiki/Third_Age_3020 "Third Age 3020")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>. Years later, tales spread far about the only mallorn found west of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), causing people to make long journeys just to see it.<sup id="cite_ref-greyhavens_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greyhavens-3">[3]</a></sup>

At some unspecified time later, the [Shire-folk](/wiki/Shire-folk) living in the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing), especially those close to [Hobbiton Hill](/wiki/Hobbiton_Hill), developed a custom of celebrating [6 April](/wiki/6_April) in the Party Field during clear weather. One of the reasons given is that it may have been the exact day when the Golden Tree first flowered.<sup id="cite_ref-appendix_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appendix-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early version of the chapter "[The Grey Havens](/wiki/The_Grey_Havens), the silver nut was originally described as being "like a nut or a dried berry".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
