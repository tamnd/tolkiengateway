The **Golden Tree**<sup id="cite_ref-appendix_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appendix-2">[2]</a></sup> was one of the most magnificent [mellyrn](../M/Mellyrn.md) in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), silver-barked and golden-leafed, and the only one of its kind to be found to the west of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-greyhavens_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greyhavens-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [Quest of Mount Doom](../Q/Quest_of_Mount_Doom.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>, [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) was given a silver nut by [Galadriel](Galadriel.md) in [Lórien](../L/Lórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-farewell_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-farewell-1">[1]</a></sup> This was the seed of a [mallorn](../M/Mallorn.md) tree.<sup id="cite_ref-greyhavens_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greyhavens-3">[3]</a></sup> These [trees](../T/Trees.md) were only found in in Lórien.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md) that marked the end of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md),<sup id="cite_ref-chiefdays_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chiefdays-6">[6]</a></sup> however, a single mallorn tree could be found elsewhere beside Lórien. During [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](../L/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins.md)'s and [Sharkey](../S/Sharkey.md)'s takeover of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), the [Party Tree](../P/Party_Tree.md) in the [Party Field](../P/Party_Field.md) was cut down.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Some weeks after [3 November](../_/3_November.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-chiefdays_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chiefdays-6">[6]</a></sup>, Sam chose to plant his silver seed in its place. It quickly sprouted into a sapling the next spring and flowered on [6 April](../_/6_April.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3020](../T/Third_Age_3020.md)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>. Years later, tales spread far about the only mallorn found west of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), causing people to make long journeys just to see it.<sup id="cite_ref-greyhavens_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greyhavens-3">[3]</a></sup>

At some unspecified time later, the [Shire-folk](../S/Shire-folk.md) living in the [Westfarthing](../W/Westfarthing.md), especially those close to [Hobbiton Hill](../H/Hobbiton_Hill.md), developed a custom of celebrating [6 April](../_/6_April.md) in the Party Field during clear weather. One of the reasons given is that it may have been the exact day when the Golden Tree first flowered.<sup id="cite_ref-appendix_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appendix-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early version of the chapter "[The Grey Havens](../T/The_Grey_Havens.md), the silver nut was originally described as being "like a nut or a dried berry".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
