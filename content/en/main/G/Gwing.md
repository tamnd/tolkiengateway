<i><b>gwing</b></i> is a noun meaning "foam" or "spray" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). Compare with *[winga](/wiki/Winga "Winga")* in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Elwing](/wiki/Elwing)* = "Star-spray"
- *[Vingilot](/wiki/Vingilot)* = "Foam-flower"
