<i><b>gwing</b></i> is a noun meaning "foam" or "spray" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md). Compare with *[winga](../W/Winga.md)* in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Elwing](../E/Elwing.md)* = "Star-spray"
- *[Vingilot](../V/Vingilot.md)* = "Foam-flower"
