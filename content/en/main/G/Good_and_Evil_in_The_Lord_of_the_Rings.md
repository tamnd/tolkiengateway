**Good and Evil in** <i><b>The Lord of the Rings</b></i> is an article by [W.H. Auden](../W/W.H._Auden.md) discussing the theme of Good and Evil in [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. It was published in *Critical Quarterly*, Volume 10, Issue 1-2, [1968](../_/1968.md), pp. 138–142.

On [28 September](../_/28_September.md), [2007](../_/2007.md), the article was published online by Blackwell Publishing Ltd.
