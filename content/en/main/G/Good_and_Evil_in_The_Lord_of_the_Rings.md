**Good and Evil in** <i><b>The Lord of the Rings</b></i> is an article by [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden) discussing the theme of Good and Evil in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. It was published in *Critical Quarterly*, Volume 10, Issue 1-2, [1968](/wiki/1968), pp. 138–142.

On [28 September](/wiki/28_September), [2007](/wiki/2007), the article was published online by Blackwell Publishing Ltd.
