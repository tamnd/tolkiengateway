**Grey Company Elvish**, often contracted to **Grelvish**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>, is a constructed language loosely based on several sources, though mostly [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). It was developed by Tel'Mithrim, or "the Grey Company", a guild of roleplayers in the game <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online" title="wikipedia:Ultima Online">Ultima Online</a>, who blended mythology from both Tolkien's works and the *Forgotten Realms* setting of [Dungeons and Dragons](../D/Dungeons_and_Dragons.md).<sup id="cite_ref-faq_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-faq-2">[1]</a></sup>

According to the guild's website, “simplified the grammatical structure \[of Tolkien's [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md)\] and expanded the dictionary heavily”.<sup id="cite_ref-faq_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-faq-2">[1]</a></sup> The simplified grammatical structure follows English. The dictionary's origins are [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), though [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) as well as non-Tolkien sources also served as inspiration.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

Despite the website clearly stating that it did not intend to be Tolkien's Elvish, Grelvish was often mistaken for it.<sup id="cite_ref-realelvish_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-realelvish-4">[3]</a></sup> New fans introduced through adaptations like [film series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) often stumbled upon the Grey Company's website (or derivatives thereof), to the consternation of older fans.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

Grelvish has several characterics that distuingish it from Tolkien's languages. Its vocabulary has long words with plural markers (ea, ie, oo), and double vowels which replace Tolkien' long vowels, though not consistently. The pronoun *amin* is used for the first person in all its forms. Apostrophes are also used heavily.<sup id="cite_ref-realelvish_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-realelvish-4">[3]</a></sup>

Sometimes obscurer words from Tolkien have been reutilized with different meanings. *[Vedui](../M/Medui.md)*, meaning "last" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) ([lenited](../L/Lenition.md)), was reinterpreted as a greeting in Grelvish, likely since it occurred in [Glorfindel](Glorfindel.md)'s greeting to [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md).

A well-attested greeting is *vedui il'er* meaning "Greetings everyone". Here, *il'er* which intended to mean "everyone" could be analyzed to *il* for "every, all" (from canonical Elvish [Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) (<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=IL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="IL (page does not exist)">IL</a> cf. *[ilya](../I/Ilya.md)*) and *er* "one" (from <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=ER&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ER (page does not exist)">ER</a>, cf. [Eru](../I/Ilúvatar.md)). Of course, this compounding is not the way Tolkien's Elvish works. The Quenya word for "everybody" is <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ilquen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ilquen (page does not exist)">ilquen</a></i>.
