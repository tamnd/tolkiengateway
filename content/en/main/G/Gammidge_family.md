**Gammidge** ([H.](../H/Hobbitish.md) <i><b>Galbasi</b></i>) was a family name adopted by [Hob Gammidge](../H/Hob_Gammidge.md), in reference of his [Gamwich](Gamwich_family.md) (H. *Galabas*) ancestry. His son [Hobson](../H/Hobson.md) would change it to [Gamgee](Gamgee.md) (H. *Galpsi*).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
