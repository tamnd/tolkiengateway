**Gammidge** ([H.](/wiki/Hobbitish "Hobbitish") <i><b>Galbasi</b></i>) was a family name adopted by [Hob Gammidge](/wiki/Hob_Gammidge), in reference of his [Gamwich](/wiki/Gamwich_family "Gamwich family") (H. *Galabas*) ancestry. His son [Hobson](/wiki/Hobson) would change it to [Gamgee](/wiki/Gamgee) (H. *Galpsi*).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
