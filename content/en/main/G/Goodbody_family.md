The **Goodbody family** was one of the many prominent families of [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits), perhaps of the upper class in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The Goodbodies were connected to the [Baggins family](/wiki/Baggins_family) through [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' great aunt [Lily](/wiki/Lily_Baggins "Lily Baggins"), who married one [Togo Goodbody](/wiki/Togo_Goodbody).<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-2">[2]</a></sup> They were also connected to the [Bolger family](/wiki/Bolger_family); [Cora Goodbody](/wiki/Cora_Goodbody) married [Rudolph Bolger](/wiki/Rudolph_Bolger).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Bilbo acknowledged the descendants of his aunt Lily as his extended family and had invited some of the various Goodbodies at his famous [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party").<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The Goodbodies were added as guests to Bilbo's Farewell Party in the fifth draft of the first chapter of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. However, at that time the family name was *Gawkroger*,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> a Yorkshire surname meaning "clumsy Roger".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
