**Grimbold** was a rider of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), who dwelt in [Grimslade](/wiki/Grimslade). He fought in the [Battles of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Battles_of_the_Fords_of_Isen), and fell in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).

## History {#History}

Grimbold was a valiant captain under the [Second Marshal](/wiki/Second_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark "Second Marshal of the Riddermark"). He was instrumental in the [defence](/wiki/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen "First Battle of the Fords of Isen") of the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen); after his Marshal [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred) fell, he managed to hold the eyot in the river until [Elfhelm](/wiki/Elfhelm) arrived. Soon after, the troops of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) left; their mission was over with the death of the Marshal.

Grimbold and his company were scattered after the [Second Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen), though most of his troops survived. When [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) arrived, he sent Elfhelm to protect [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) and Grimbold to join [Erkenbrand](/wiki/Erkenbrand), who was making his way to the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg) in an attempt to lift the [siege](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg "Battle of the Hornburg").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the victory at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep) and the lighting of the [warning beacons of Gondor](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor"), [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) had to quickly reorganize his troops. The elderly [Erkenbrand](/wiki/Erkenbrand) commanded the defence of Rohan, while Grimbold was elected to take over command of the muster of the [Westfold](/wiki/Westfold) based on the valour he had shown at earlier battles.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> At the [battle](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields "Battle of the Pelennor Fields"), Grimbold fought on the eastern side of the [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields), where he was slain. He was remembered in the *[Song of the Mounds of Mundburg](/wiki/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg)* as "doughty".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Grimbold* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name, which means "fierce-bold".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It probably contains the Old English elements *grim* ("fierce")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and *bold* ("bold", "courageous")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>.

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Grimbold is briefly glimpsed twice in this film. The first time is as <a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden" title="Théoden">Théoden</a> arrives at Dunharrow and calls out to Grimbold. In this scene he appears as an elderly man in the armour of the guard of Edoras. Later on in the film as the Rohirrim arrive at the <a href="/wiki/Pelennor_Fields" title="Pelennor Fields">Pelennor Fields</a> Théoden gives Grimbold orders and tells him to &#34;Go forth and fear no darkness!&#34;. In these scenes he was played by <a href="/wiki/Bruce_Phillips" title="Bruce Phillips">Bruce Phillips</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Contrary to the film&#39;s dialogue, <i>The Return of the King</i> set identifies a different <a href="/wiki/Rohirrim" title="Rohirrim">Rohirrim</a> at <a href="/wiki/Edoras" title="Edoras">Edoras</a> from <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i> as Grimbold, not played by Bruce Phillips.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Grimbold is first met in his encampment in the <a href="/wiki/Gap_of_Rohan" title="Gap of Rohan">Gap of Rohan</a>, where he is concerned with slowing the inevitable advance of the <a href="/wiki/Dunlendings" title="Dunlendings">Dunlendings</a>. During the First Battle of the Fords, Grimbold fights shoulder-to-shoulder with the player for most of the day, but Grimbold is too late to save the Théodred from a mortal blow by a large Uruk, for which he blames himself. In the aftermath of the second battle of the Fords, Gandalf tasks the player with searching for Grimbold, and after a lengthy search the player eventually finds him defending the grave of the Prince at the <a href="/wiki/Fords_of_Isen" title="Fords of Isen">Fords of Isen</a>, his only wish to die defending it.</dd>
<dd>The player does not see Grimbold again until much later, when the Rohirrim are passing through the <a href="/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest" title="Drúadan Forest">Drúadan Forest</a> on their way to the <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>. Grimbold perishes on the fields of Pelennor, mauled to death by a <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Fell_beast" title="Fell beast">fell beast</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2001-2020: <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game" title="Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game">Middle-Earth Strategy Battle Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Grimbold is a hero available to the Rohan faction. He wields a two-handed axe and allows the player to upgrade Warriors of Rohan to Helmingas, making them Strength 4. He is also notable for being one of only a handful of named Rohan heroes to not have the option to take a horse.</dd></dl>
