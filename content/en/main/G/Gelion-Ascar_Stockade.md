The **Gelion-Ascar stockade** was a fortress built by [Haldad](/wiki/Haldad) of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) to defend his people against [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). It was located south of [Sarn Athrad](/wiki/Sarn_Athrad), between the [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion) and [Ascar](/wiki/Ascar) rivers. It was laid siege to in the [Battle of the Gelion-Ascar Stockade](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Gelion-Ascar_Stockade) around [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [375](/wiki/First_Age_375 "First Age 375"), during which Haldad was killed and the stockade badly damaged. Eventually [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth) led the Haladin away, abandoning the refuge.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
