The **Gliding Water** was the name of a river passing through [Kortirion](../K/Kortirion.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Kor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kor-1">[1]</a></sup> It is only mentioned in an early version of the poem *[The Trees of Kortirion](../T/The_Trees_of_Kortirion.md)*.

According to the poem, [elms](../E/Elms.md) grew about it, and it flowed westward from Kortirion, passing through meadows before reaching the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-Kor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The Gliding Water might have been inspired by the river <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Avon,_Warwickshire" title="wikipedia:River Avon, Warwickshire">Avon</a>, which flows through the town of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warwick" title="wikipedia:Warwick">Warwick</a>, the main inspiration behind the city of Kortirion.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In later reworkings of the poem, the river was called the <i><b>River Gliding</b></i> instead.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
