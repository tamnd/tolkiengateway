**Gelmir** and **Arminas** were [Noldorin Elves](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") of the [House of Finarfin](/wiki/House_of_Finarfin), specifically being people of [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod).

They wondered far following the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), and eventually settled at the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion), taking residence with the people of [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan). Círdan then charged them with delivering a message of warning from [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) to [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth), the [Lord of Nargothrond](/wiki/Lord_of_Nargothrond), of its impending peril.

Along their journey they aided [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), who treated them with courtesy, before arriving in Nargothrond where they then met [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) who treated them harshly and with mistrust. Their message of warning was not heeded.

## History {#History}

### Early {#Early}

Little is known of the early history of Gelmir or Arminas. In origin, they were of the folk of Angrod.<sup id="cite_ref-nargothrond_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nargothrond-1">[1]</a></sup> Arminas was acquainted with the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador), having himself at some point seen [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin), and Hurin's forefathers before him.<sup id="cite_ref-nargothrond_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nargothrond-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the Dagor Bragollach in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455"), when [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) was <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Taur-nu-Fuin" title="Taur-nu-Fuin">overtaken and corrupted</a>, they had wandered far.<sup id="cite_ref-utAppendix_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-utAppendix-2">[2]</a></sup> Following the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad) in [472](/wiki/First_Age_472 "First Age 472"), Arminas then long sought for the [hidden kingdom](/wiki/Gondolin "Gondolin") of [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon), though he never found it.<sup id="cite_ref-tuor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tuor-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-nargothrond_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nargothrond-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Dwelling with Círdan {#Dwelling_with_Círdan}

Not long before [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"),<sup id="cite_ref-ga495_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga495-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> they came to dwell among Círdan's people at the Mouths of Sirion. Among those who dwelt with Círdan were [people of Turgon](/wiki/Gondolindrim "Gondolindrim") who had left his realm as messengers; from their guarded speech it seemed to Arminas that Turgon probably still dwelt North in Beleriand, not in the South as most believed.<sup id="cite_ref-nargothrond_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nargothrond-1">[1]</a></sup>

At some point, Ulmo then appeared before Círdan and told him that the "[Evil of the North](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth")" had "defiled the [springs of Sirion](/wiki/Eithel_Sirion "Eithel Sirion")" causing him to withdraw his power from that river. He then claimed that "a [worse thing](/wiki/Glaurung "Glaurung") is yet to come forth" and the [Lord of Nargothrond](/wiki/Lord_of_Nargothrond) should be told to "Shut the doors of [the fortress](/wiki/Nargothrond "Nargothrond"), and go not abroad"; to cast the [Bridge of Nargothrond](/wiki/Bridge_of_Nargothrond) "into the [loud river](/wiki/Narog "Narog")" so the kingdom's gates cannot be found. Círdan sent Gelmir and Arminas to deliver Ulmo's message to Orodreth. Rather than take the overland route, Círdan had the two taken along the coast by ship to [Drengist](/wiki/Drengist) for secrecy and speed.<sup id="cite_ref-nargothrond_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nargothrond-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Crossing paths with Tuor {#Crossing_paths_with_Tuor}

At the beginning of 495,<sup id="cite_ref-ga495_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga495-4">[4]</a></sup> not long into their journey, and shortly after crossing under the [Ered Lómin](/wiki/Ered_L%C3%B3min) and emerging into [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) through the [Gate of the Noldor](/wiki/Annon-in-Gelydh "Annon-in-Gelydh"), they were unexpectedly called to by a lone Man. At first they were weary and drew their swords, but when he drew no weapon and greeted them in Elven-tongue they sheathed them and spoke back courteously. By the Man's speech and his golden hair they deemed that he was an [Edain](/wiki/Edain) of the House of Hador which had previously dwelt in these lands prior to the Nirnaeth. This the Man confirmed, introducing himself as Tuor, son of [Huor](/wiki/Huor), and descended from [Hador](/wiki/Hador).<sup id="cite_ref-tuor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tuor-3">[3]</a></sup>

Tuor stated that he desired to leave Hithlum where he was outlawed and kinless. To this Gelmir confirmed he was on the right road if he sought the havens in the South. When Tour then asked about the Gate of the Noldor, the Elves laughed, and pointed to it right before him, and offered to guide him through them. They could not, however, take him far due to the urgency of their errand. Before entering, Gelmir stated that Tuor should not fear, for a great doom was written upon his brow; one that would lead him far from the lands they were in, and he further guessed even far from [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-tuor_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tuor-3">[3]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Tuor,_Gelmir,_and_Arminas.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/c/c6/Ted_Nasmith_-_Tuor%2C_Gelmir%2C_and_Arminas.jpg/190px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Tuor%2C_Gelmir%2C_and_Arminas.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="190" height="254" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/c/c6/Ted_Nasmith_-_Tuor%2C_Gelmir%2C_and_Arminas.jpg/285px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Tuor%2C_Gelmir%2C_and_Arminas.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/c/c6/Ted_Nasmith_-_Tuor%2C_Gelmir%2C_and_Arminas.jpg 2x" data-file-width="336" data-file-height="450"/></a><figcaption>&#34;Tuor, Gelmir, and Arminas&#34; by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

As they walked through the arch of stone and into darkness, Gelmir brought forth a [Fëanorian lamp](/wiki/F%C3%ABanorian_lamps "Fëanorian lamps") which he used to light the way. After descending down a long flight of stairs, Gelmir and Arminas bade Tuor farewell, noting again their errand and that matters of great peril were moving in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). When Tuor asked if it was then the hour when [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) would come forth, they were amazed, and Arminas asked what Tuor knew of Turgon. Tuor remarked he knew little save the deeds of his father in aiding Turgon's escape from the Nirnaeth, but that Turgon's name stirred his heart and came to his lips, and that he would search for him if he could. Tuor then asked if perhaps the secret road they were on led to Turgon's dwelling, to which Arminas responded that he did not know, as it was hidden, and so to were the ways to it; but even if he did know he would not reveal anything to any among Men. As last words of parting Gelmir then said that he had heard that the House of Hador was favoured by the Lord of the Waters, and that if Ulmo's counsels were leading Tuor to Turgon, then surely he would be brought before the king, and that Tuor should not think that their meeting was by chance; he then [wished him farewell](/wiki/Anar_kaluva_tielyanna "Anar kaluva tielyanna") in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-tuor_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tuor-3">[3]</a></sup>

### In Nargothrond {#In_Nargothrond}

After returning to their journey, and perhaps inspired by their encounter with Tuor, Gelmir and Arminas wandered from the straight path to Nargothrond and searched for the kingdom of Turgon. They travelled through [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) and then turned northeast, rather than south, and traversed all the lands on under the eaves of the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin). On exploring the [Pass of Sirion](/wiki/Pass_of_Sirion) they found a great gathering of [Orcs](/wiki/Orc "Orc") and evil creatures, and that a host was mustering about [Sauron's Isle](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth "Tol-in-Gaurhoth"). They found, however, no sign or rumour of Turgon.<sup id="cite_ref-nargothrond_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nargothrond-1">[1]</a></sup>

By the spring Gelmir and Arminas arrived in [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) and declared their errand. They were then brought before Túrin, who by now ruled all matters of war, but Gelmir insisted that they would speak to Orodreth.<sup id="cite_ref-nargothrond_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nargothrond-1">[1]</a></sup>

When Orodreth came, they informed him of their errand, but he was wary, and questioned them. On answering Orodreth's question about why they came from the North rather than the South, Arminas further revealed his search for Turgon. When Orodreth further inquired why they were seeking Turgon, Arminas answered that it was said that Turgon's kingdom would stand longest against Morgoth, and these words Orodreth took to be ill-omened and they displeased him. Orodreth told Gelmir and Arminas not to tarry long in Nargothrond, for here they would hear no news of Turgon, and he did not need anyone to teach him that Nargothrond stood in peril. Gelmir then told of the gathering of evil to the north they had seen, to which Túrin replied he was already aware of it. At last, Gelmir then delivered Ulmo's message, against which Orodreth turned to Túrin for counsel. Túrin, however, mistrusted the messengers, and scorned them, and said the mariner should look to his ships and if in truth the Lord of Waters actually would send counsel, let him speak more plainly.<sup id="cite_ref-nargothrond_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nargothrond-1">[1]</a></sup>

At this Gelmir bowed to Orodreth, declaring his errand done and turned away. Arminas, however, spoke to Túrin, and asked if he was indeed of the House of Hador as he had heard. To this Túrin confirmed, though he stated that here he was known only as *Agarwaen*, and that it was good Turgon's secret was hidden from Arminas, otherwise, with his loose tongue in guarded speech, it would soon be known in [Angband](/wiki/Angband) itself. Túrin further added a curse on Arminas, that Morgoth should take him and burn out his tongue, if he should learn that Arminas betrayed his secret. Arminas was dismayed by this black wrath, but Gelmir attempted to sooth the situation, stating that Túrin would not be betrayed by them, and reminding that they were in council behind closed doors where speech might be plain. He further clarified that he deemed Arminas only asked since Ulmo had great love for the House of Hador, and that some had said that both Húrin and Huor had once been to Turgon's hidden kingdom. Túrin, however, was not soothed, and declared that he did not believe Arminas asked him this to learn of Turgon, and that he mistrusted such 'messengers of mischief'. At this Arminas spoke back in anger, declaring that Gelmir was mistaken: he asked because he doubted it, given how little Túrin seemed to resemble the kin of Hador. Túrin rebutted this, saying he was not ashamed of his dark, rather than gold, hair colour; in this he bore likeness to his mother, [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen) of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor), and was kindred to [Beren](/wiki/Beren). Arminas further stated that it was not his hair colour he was speaking of, but his bearing: Men of the House of Hador used courtesy, listened to good counsel, and held the [Lords of the West](/wiki/Valar "Valar") in awe; Túrin, however, seemed to take counsel only with his own wisdom and [sword](/wiki/Gurthang "Gurthang"), and spoke haughtily. At this, Túrin cast more insults, calling Arminas a 'runagate from war', and bidding the messengers to get back to the safe shores of the Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-nargothrond_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nargothrond-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Aftermath {#Aftermath}

Gelmir and Arminas then departed, and went back South. Despite Túrin's words, however, they would have gladly awaited battle beside their kin, but Círdan had bidden them otherwise to bring back to him the word of the people of Nargothrond.<sup id="cite_ref-nargothrond_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nargothrond-1">[1]</a></sup>

Gelmir and Arminas were later companions of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) on his voyage,<sup id="cite_ref-ga495n11_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga495n11-6">[5]</a></sup> though details are not given.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gelmir* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), but of unclear meaning. It could be a combination of *gell* ("joy") and *[mîr](/wiki/M%C3%AEr "Mîr")* ("jewel").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> If so, it is unusual that the m was not mutated to v, as would usually have happened. It may thus be that his name is in the [North Sindarin](/wiki/North_Sindarin) dialect where this change did not occur.

*Arminas* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Royal Tower", from *[ar](/wiki/Ar "Ar")* ("high, royal, noble") + *[minas](/wiki/Minas "Minas")* ("tower").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

Tuor marvelled at the sight of Gelmir and Arminas, for they were fairer and more fell to look upon, because of the light of their eyes, than any of the Elven-folk that he yet had known.<sup id="cite_ref-tuor_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tuor-3">[3]</a></sup> Thus, they were likely [Calaquendi](/wiki/Calaquendi) born in Valinor before the exile.

As Gelmir was able to discern a glint of Tuor's fate he appeared to have some gift of [foresight](/wiki/Foresight "Foresight").<sup id="cite_ref-tuor_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tuor-3">[3]</a></sup>

In Túrin's rebuke to Arminas, he states that Arminas claims "the kinship of kings",<sup id="cite_ref-nargothrond_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nargothrond-1">[1]</a></sup> though this is not elaborated on.

In the few lines of dialouge they have, some of their personality can be gleaned: Gelmir appears more soft-spoken and conciliatory, while Arminas is more rash and blunt.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In one plot-synopsis of the Túrin story Gelmir is instead named <i><b>Faramir</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-ga495n11_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga495n11-6">[5]</a></sup>

In *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, it was stated that they had dwelt with Círdan since the Dagor Bragollach.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> In *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*, the wandering of Gelmir and Arminas was changed from the earlier *Bragollach* (in [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455")) to the later *Nirnaeth* (in [472](/wiki/First_Age_472 "First Age 472")).<sup id="cite_ref-nargothrond_1-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nargothrond-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Analysis {#Analysis}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) considers the question of why Círdan sent Gelmir and Arminas so far north to the Drengist rather than the much more expeditious route of the [Narog](/wiki/Narog) where "greater secrecy could surely have been achieved". He speculates that this might have been part of Ulmo's command to Círdan, thus arranging that they should meet Tuor and guide him through the Gate of the Noldor, but that this is nowhere suggested.<sup id="cite_ref-utAppendix_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-utAppendix-2">[2]</a></sup>
