**Gerald Brom** (born [9 March](/wiki/9_March) [1965](/wiki/1965)), known professionally as **Brom**, is an American artist. After a career as a commercial artist, he began illustrating roleplaying games such as [Dungeons and Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons) in [1989](/wiki/1989). In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.

He paints in a gothic fantasy style, primarily using oils.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
