**Gerald Brom** (born [9 March](../_/9_March.md) [1965](../_/1965.md)), known professionally as **Brom**, is an American artist. After a career as a commercial artist, he began illustrating roleplaying games such as [Dungeons and Dragons](../D/Dungeons_and_Dragons.md) in [1989](../_/1989.md). In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.

He paints in a gothic fantasy style, primarily using oils.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
