**Great Hall** can refer to:

- The [Great Hall of Feasts](/wiki/Great_Hall_of_Feasts), the King’s feasting-hall in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).
- The [Great Hall of Osgiliath](/wiki/Great_Hall_of_Osgiliath), the royal hall of the Kings in [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath).
- The [Great Hall of Thráin](/wiki/Great_Hall_of_Thr%C3%A1in), the main hall of [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor) where [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) had his bed of gold.
- The [Great Hall of Wootton Major](/wiki/Great_Hall_of_Wootton_Major) in the town of [Wootton Major](/wiki/Wootton_Major), where the villagers met for various occasions, including [The Feast of Good Children](/wiki/The_Feast_of_Good_Children).
