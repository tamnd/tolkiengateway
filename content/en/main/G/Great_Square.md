The **Great Square**, also known as the **Court of the King**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:§130</sup> was a square at the top of [Túna](/wiki/T%C3%BAna), in [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion), the great city of the [Elves of Valinor](/wiki/Elves_of_Valinor), where the tree [Galathilion](/wiki/Galathilion) stood under the [Mindon Eldaliéva](/wiki/Mindon_Eldali%C3%A9va).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It the location where [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) threatened his half-brother [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) in public,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and where later he gave his [great speech](/wiki/Speech_of_F%C3%ABanor "Speech of Fëanor") and infamous [Oath](/wiki/Oath_of_F%C3%ABanor "Oath of Fëanor").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
