The **Great Hall of Osgiliath** was the joint throne room of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) in the city of [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath). Although the two brothers kept separate houses in [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil) (Isildur) and [Minas Anor](/wiki/Minas_Anor) (Anárion), their thrones were set side by side in the Great Hall.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Presumably after the deaths of both brothers the early [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor) used the Great Hall as their throne room. However, subsequent to the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636") Osgiliath became largely deserted, and in [1640](/wiki/Third_Age_1640 "Third Age 1640") King [Tarondor](</wiki/Tarondor_(King_of_Gondor)> "Tarondor (King of Gondor)") removed the King's House permanently to Minas Anor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
