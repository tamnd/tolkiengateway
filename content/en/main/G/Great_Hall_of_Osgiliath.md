The **Great Hall of Osgiliath** was the joint throne room of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md) in the city of [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md). Although the two brothers kept separate houses in [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md) (Isildur) and [Minas Anor](../M/Minas_Anor.md) (Anárion), their thrones were set side by side in the Great Hall.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Presumably after the deaths of both brothers the early [Kings of Gondor](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) used the Great Hall as their throne room. However, subsequent to the [Great Plague](Great_Plague.md) of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md) Osgiliath became largely deserted, and in [1640](../T/Third_Age_1640.md) King [Tarondor](<../T/Tarondor_(King_of_Gondor).md>) removed the King's House permanently to Minas Anor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
