The **Great Hall of Osgiliath** was the joint throne room of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/Anárion) in the city of [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath). Although the two brothers kept separate houses in [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil) (Isildur) and [Minas Anor](/wiki/Minas_Anor) (Anárion), their thrones were set side by side in the Great Hall.

> Reference: {{S|Rings}}

Presumably after the deaths of both brothers the early [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor) used the Great Hall as their throne room. However, subsequent to the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) of {{TA|1636}} Osgiliath became largely deserted, and in {{TA|1640|n}} King [Tarondor](/wiki/Tarondor_(King_of_Gondor)) removed the King's House permanently to Minas Anor.
