The **North Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> also called the **North-South-Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the **Old South Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> or later the **Greenway** by the [Bree-folk](../B/Bree-folk.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> was the part of the **[Great Road](Great_Road.md)**<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Index_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-7">[7]</a></sup> from [Fornost Erain](../F/Fornost_Erain.md) to the western shore of the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Course {#Course}

The North Road ran from Fornost in the south of the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md) southeast until it crossed the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) just west of the village of [Bree](../B/Bree.md) and then continued south through [Andrath](../A/Andrath.md) between the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md) and the [South Downs](../S/South_Downs.md), met a a road in [Minhiriath](../M/Minhiriath.md) that ran from the [White Downs](../W/White_Downs.md) over [Sarn Ford](../S/Sarn_Ford.md), then turned south-east and traversed the fens in the plains of [Minhiriath](../M/Minhiriath.md) on a long causewway until it crossed the river [Gwathló](Gwathló.md) at [Tharbad](../T/Tharbad.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Glanduin_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glanduin-9">[9]</a></sup> continued on a long<sup id="cite_ref-Glanduin_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glanduin-9">[9]</a></sup> raised<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-10">[10]</a></sup> causeway across the fens in the plains<sup id="cite_ref-Glanduin_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glanduin-9">[9]</a></sup> and then ascended to higher land in the centre and south-east of [Enedwaith](../E/Enedwaith.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-10">[10]</a></sup> until it turned west close to the southern end of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) and ran west to the western shore of the Fords of Isen.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

The part of the Great Road that ran from the eastern shore of the Fords of Isen<sup id="cite_ref-Index_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-7">[7]</a></sup> to the [north gate](../F/Forannest.md) of the [Rammas Echor](../R/Rammas_Echor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> was called the **[Great West Road](Great_West_Road.md)**,<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> the **West Road**<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> or the **horse-road**.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the later [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) after the division of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) [into](../A/Arthedain.md) [three](../C/Cardolan.md) [realms](../R/Rhudaur.md) it became lesser used. Use of the road virtually ended after the [Great Plague](Great_Plague.md) and the [Long Winter](../L/Long_Winter.md) when Tharbad was abandoned and its bridge ruined, and the Old South Road to [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md) was mostly lost. The patch of the road around [Bree-land](../B/Bree-land.md) became overgrown with grass because of disuse and so was called the Greenway.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-utindex_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-utindex-19">[19]</a></sup>

[Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) took this road, crossing Tharbad but losing his horse, on his way to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md). It is possible that the road was restored after the end of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

<dl><dd>&#34;Greenway&#34; specifically refers to the portion of the road running through the <a href="../B/Bree-land.md" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a>, with the rest of it called the &#34;North-South Road&#34;. Running south from <a class="mw-redirect" href="../F/Fornost.md" title="Fornost">Fornost</a>, the road crosses a large chasm called &#34;Cirith Núr&#34; that separates Bree-land from the <a href="../N/North_Downs.md" title="North Downs">North Downs</a> through the wooden &#34;Trestlespan&#34; bridge in the town of &#34;<a href="../T/Trestlebridge.md" title="Trestlebridge">Trestlebridge</a>&#34;. South of <a href="../A/Andrath.md" title="Andrath">Andrath</a>, the Greenway passes through the mannish town of &#34;Herne&#34; where is turns east towards <a href="../T/Tharbad.md" title="Tharbad">Tharbad</a>. With the <a href="../B/Bridge_of_Tharbad.md" title="Bridge of Tharbad">Bridge of Tharbad</a> now broken, the road to the east of Tharbad is considered to be a separate <a href="../O/Old_South_Road.md" title="Old South Road">Old South Road</a>.</dd></dl>
