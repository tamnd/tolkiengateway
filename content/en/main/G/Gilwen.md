**Gilwen** (born [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [397](/wiki/First_Age_397 "First Age 397")) was the fourth child and third daughter of [Bregor](/wiki/Bregor), the fifth chieftain of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor), and the great-great-granddaughter of [Bëor the Old](/wiki/B%C3%ABor "Bëor"). She had two elder sisters *[Bregil](/wiki/Bregil)* and *[Hirwen](/wiki/Hirwen)*, and two brothers [Bregolas](/wiki/Bregolas) the elder and [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir) the younger.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
