The **Guild of Venturers** was a brotherhood of mariners and seamen formed by the Great Captain [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") in the year [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Guild had no fixed guildhouse on land, but was based instead on the ship *[Eämbar](/wiki/E%C3%A4mbar)*, that had been built for the purpose. *Eämbar* was sailed at times to different harbours of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), but was normally anchored off the island of [Tol Uinen](/wiki/Tol_Uinen) in the bay of [Rómenna](/wiki/R%C3%B3menna). The sailors of the Guild went much farther than this, though, steering their vessels east across the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") to the distant shores of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). There, they visited [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) in [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), and explored the coastlands into the far south.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

They were called <i><b>Uinendili</b></i>, the "Lovers of [Uinen](/wiki/Uinen)", the [Maia](/wiki/Maia) of the [Seas](/wiki/Seas).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:176</sup>
