**Gelion** was a river of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the principal river of [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand), being the border of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand).

The vale of Gelion sloped steadily southward and thus had neither falls nor rapids, yet was ever swifter than the River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion). Although Gelion was twice the length of Sirion after its junction with [Ascar](/wiki/Ascar), it was less wide and full, for more rain fell in the west than in the east.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Course {#Course}

Its two sources were at the hill of [Himring](/wiki/Himring), via the [Little Gelion](/wiki/Little_Gelion), and [Mount Rerir](/wiki/Mount_Rerir), via the [Greater Gelion](/wiki/Greater_Gelion) (which also had a tributary connected to [Lake Helevorn](/wiki/Lake_Helevorn)).

It then flowed south for forty leagues, dividing [Estolad](/wiki/Estolad) to the west from [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion) to the east. Gelion met [Sarn Athrad](/wiki/Sarn_Athrad) ("Ford of Stones") just above the inflow of the River Ascar. Gelion then became the western border of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand), the Land of Seven Rivers, from which it was fed by the rivers: [Ascar](/wiki/Ascar), [Thalos](/wiki/Thalos), [Legolin](/wiki/Legolin), [Brilthor](/wiki/Brilthor), [Duilwen](/wiki/Duilwen), and [Adurant](/wiki/Adurant), all rising in the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin).<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup> South of the [Falls of Gelion](/wiki/Falls_of_Gelion), after Ascar met Gelion, that section of the river became swift and ever broader by the tributaries and thus was called **Gabilān** by the [Dwarves of the Blue Mountains](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains) in [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost) and [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod).<sup id="cite_ref-maeg_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-maeg-2">[2]</a></sup>

After its junction with Adurant, Gelion turned southwest, passing by [Taur-im-Duinath](/wiki/Taur-im-Duinath) (the Forest between the Rivers) before emptying its waters into the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> There were at least 300 leagues of total length between Gelion's source and its outfall into Belegaer.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves) who dwelt in Ossiriand lived under the protection of the river, for after Sirion, [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) loved Gelion above all other waters of the western world.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:§115</sup>

Between the arms of Gelion lay the ward of [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor). [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir) made his abode at Lake Helevorn, and the land of Thargelion, which signified the Land beyond Gelion, was also called [Dor Caranthir](/wiki/Dor_Caranthir), the Land of Caranthir.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup>

Due to the open nature of the land about the upper reaches of the River Gelion the region was subjected to many battles. [Haldad](/wiki/Haldad) of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) built a stockade in the corner formed by the Gelion and the Ascar when an orc-raid fell upon his people in Thargelion.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> During the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) the dragon [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) came and destroyed all the land between the arms of Gelion.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Eventually the lands between the rivers [Aros](/wiki/Aros) and Gelion became so dangerous that [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) traveling to and from the Ered Luin and [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth) stopped going in small parties but went in great companies well armed.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

The name *Gelion* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), but it is never glossed. In the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) phase of the language, it is related with the [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin) word *gelion* ("bright"). However, it is noticeable that the same name is glossed as "Merry Singer", but applied to [Tinfang](/wiki/Tinfang).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul), it was known as <i><b>Gabilān</b></i>. The Dwarves used the name only of the river south of the [Falls of Gelion](/wiki/Falls_of_Gelion) where the river became swift and ever broader.<sup id="cite_ref-maeg_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-maeg-2">[2]</a></sup>

The name contains the element *[gabil](/wiki/Gabil "Gabil")* ("great"), that also appears in the Khuzdul name of [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost), *[Gabilgathol](/wiki/Gabilgathol)* ("Great Fortress").

In a later text, [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) used the form <i><b>Gevolon</b></i>, clearly a phonological development from the Dwarf-name *Gabilān*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In later writings, Tolkien decided that the Gelion and [Celon](/wiki/Celon) "*river-names need revision to etymologizable words*". Tolkien proposed many names: **Gelduin**, **Gevilon**, **Gevelon**, **Duin Daer**, and **Duin Dhaer**.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

The [map V of *Ambarkanta*](/wiki/Ambarkanta_maps "Ambarkanta maps"),<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> developed by Tolkien during the 1930s, shows the mouth of the Gelion in the ocean. The river seems to flow (if we do not take into account a pentimento in the sketch) into an unnamed bay comparable in size to the [Bay of Balar](/wiki/Bay_of_Balar).
