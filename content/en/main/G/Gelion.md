**Gelion** was a river of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), the principal river of [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md), being the border of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) and [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md).

The vale of Gelion sloped steadily southward and thus had neither falls nor rapids, yet was ever swifter than the River [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md). Although Gelion was twice the length of Sirion after its junction with [Ascar](../A/Ascar.md), it was less wide and full, for more rain fell in the west than in the east.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Course {#Course}

Its two sources were at the hill of [Himring](../H/Himring.md), via the [Little Gelion](../L/Little_Gelion.md), and [Mount Rerir](../M/Mount_Rerir.md), via the [Greater Gelion](Greater_Gelion.md) (which also had a tributary connected to [Lake Helevorn](../L/Lake_Helevorn.md)).

It then flowed south for forty leagues, dividing [Estolad](../E/Estolad.md) to the west from [Thargelion](../T/Thargelion.md) to the east. Gelion met [Sarn Athrad](../S/Sarn_Athrad.md) ("Ford of Stones") just above the inflow of the River Ascar. Gelion then became the western border of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md), the Land of Seven Rivers, from which it was fed by the rivers: [Ascar](../A/Ascar.md), [Thalos](../T/Thalos.md), [Legolin](../L/Legolin.md), [Brilthor](../B/Brilthor.md), [Duilwen](../D/Duilwen.md), and [Adurant](../A/Adurant.md), all rising in the [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup> South of the [Falls of Gelion](../F/Falls_of_Gelion.md), after Ascar met Gelion, that section of the river became swift and ever broader by the tributaries and thus was called **Gabilān** by the [Dwarves of the Blue Mountains](../D/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains.md) in [Belegost](../B/Belegost.md) and [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md).<sup id="cite_ref-maeg_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-maeg-2">[2]</a></sup>

After its junction with Adurant, Gelion turned southwest, passing by [Taur-im-Duinath](../T/Taur-im-Duinath.md) (the Forest between the Rivers) before emptying its waters into the [Great Sea](../B/Belegaer.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> There were at least 300 leagues of total length between Gelion's source and its outfall into Belegaer.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The [Green-elves](Green-elves.md) who dwelt in Ossiriand lived under the protection of the river, for after Sirion, [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) loved Gelion above all other waters of the western world.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:§115</sup>

Between the arms of Gelion lay the ward of [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md). [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md) made his abode at Lake Helevorn, and the land of Thargelion, which signified the Land beyond Gelion, was also called [Dor Caranthir](../D/Dor_Caranthir.md), the Land of Caranthir.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup>

Due to the open nature of the land about the upper reaches of the River Gelion the region was subjected to many battles. [Haldad](../H/Haldad.md) of the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md) built a stockade in the corner formed by the Gelion and the Ascar when an orc-raid fell upon his people in Thargelion.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> During the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) the dragon [Glaurung](Glaurung.md) came and destroyed all the land between the arms of Gelion.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Eventually the lands between the rivers [Aros](../A/Aros.md) and Gelion became so dangerous that [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) traveling to and from the Ered Luin and [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md) stopped going in small parties but went in great companies well armed.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

The name *Gelion* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), but it is never glossed. In the [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) phase of the language, it is related with the [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md) word *gelion* ("bright"). However, it is noticeable that the same name is glossed as "Merry Singer", but applied to [Tinfang](../T/Tinfang.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md), it was known as <i><b>Gabilān</b></i>. The Dwarves used the name only of the river south of the [Falls of Gelion](../F/Falls_of_Gelion.md) where the river became swift and ever broader.<sup id="cite_ref-maeg_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-maeg-2">[2]</a></sup>

The name contains the element *[gabil](Gabil.md)* ("great"), that also appears in the Khuzdul name of [Belegost](../B/Belegost.md), *[Gabilgathol](Gabilgathol.md)* ("Great Fortress").

In a later text, [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) used the form <i><b>Gevolon</b></i>, clearly a phonological development from the Dwarf-name *Gabilān*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In later writings, Tolkien decided that the Gelion and [Celon](../C/Celon.md) "*river-names need revision to etymologizable words*". Tolkien proposed many names: **Gelduin**, **Gevilon**, **Gevelon**, **Duin Daer**, and **Duin Dhaer**.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

The [map V of *Ambarkanta*](../A/Ambarkanta_maps.md),<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> developed by Tolkien during the 1930s, shows the mouth of the Gelion in the ocean. The river seems to flow (if we do not take into account a pentimento in the sketch) into an unnamed bay comparable in size to the [Bay of Balar](../B/Bay_of_Balar.md).
