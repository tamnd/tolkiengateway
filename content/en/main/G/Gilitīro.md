**Gilitīro** was a [Telerin](../F/Falmari.md) [Elf](../E/Elf.md) of [Aman](../A/Aman.md) and the father of [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) according to one late version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PE23_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE23-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Gilitīro* is in [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md), meaning "starwatcher".<sup id="cite_ref-PE23_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE23-1">[1]</a></sup>

Its [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) equivalent is <i><b>Gildír</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-PE23_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE23-1">[1]</a></sup>
