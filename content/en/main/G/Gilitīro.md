**Gilitīro** was a [Telerin](/wiki/Falmari "Falmari") [Elf](/wiki/Elf) of [Aman](/wiki/Aman) and the father of [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) according to one late version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").<sup id="cite_ref-PE23_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE23-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Gilitīro* is in [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin), meaning "starwatcher".<sup id="cite_ref-PE23_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE23-1">[1]</a></sup>

Its [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) equivalent is <i><b>Gildír</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-PE23_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE23-1">[1]</a></sup>
