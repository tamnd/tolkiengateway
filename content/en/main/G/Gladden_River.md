The **Gladden** (called <i><b>Sîr Ninglor</b></i> "River Goldwater" by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves)) was a short but important river of the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin).

## Course {#Course}

The source of the river begun as two unnamed arms in the central [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), near an important pass.<sup id="cite_ref-south_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-1">[1]</a></sup> It then flowed eastwards to the [Great River Anduin](/wiki/Anduin "Anduin"), which it met in a series of marshes called the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men), [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur), heir of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) and bearer of [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), was assailed by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) near the Gladden Fields, and the One Ring was lost here in the Gladden river.<sup id="cite_ref-Disaster_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Disaster-3">[3]</a></sup>

Much later during the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) some [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors) lived near the streams of Gladden, and from them came [Déagol](/wiki/D%C3%A9agol) who found the ring, and was killed by [Sméagol](/wiki/Sm%C3%A9agol) ([Gollum](/wiki/Gollum)), who long held the Ring. Gollum eventually followed the stream up to its source, ending up in forgotten caves near [Goblin-town](/wiki/Goblin-town).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) searched the Gladden extensively during his search for the Ring, but never found the ring, although he seems to have found [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur)'s remains.<sup id="cite_ref-Disaster_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Disaster-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") some of the scouts sent out by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) after the arrival of the hobbits in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) crossed the Misty Mountains over the pass at the source of the Gladden to seek the wizard [Radagast](/wiki/Radagast) at [Rhosgobel](/wiki/Rhosgobel), but he was not at home, and they return through the [Dimrill Stair](/wiki/Dimrill_Stair).<sup id="cite_ref-south_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gladden* (From [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *glædene*) is another name for the "flag" or "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iris_(plant)" title="wikipedia:Iris (plant)">iris</a>", now usually spelt *gladdon*.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-5">[5]</a></sup>

*Sîr Ninglor* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "River Water-gold", apparently consisting of *[sîr](/wiki/S%C3%AEr "Sîr")* ("river"), *nîn* (pl. of *[nen](/wiki/Nen "Nen")* ("water") + *[glaur](/wiki/Glaur "Glaur")* ("gold").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

<b>2019: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>As it drops from the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a>, the Gladden River flows through the ruins of &#34;Kidzul-kâlah&#34;, an abandoned outpost of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm" title="Khazad-dûm">Khazad-dûm</a>. Immediately past it, the river forms the large lake &#34;Gladdenmere&#34;, from which it drops into the <a href="/wiki/Gladden_Fields" title="Gladden Fields">Gladden Fields</a> through the &#34;Gladden Falls&#34; waterfall.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The present course of the Gladden River is not natural. During the fighting at the end of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">Second Age</a>, massive quakes shook the Misty Mountains. At Kidzul-kâlah, new passages opened leading deep below the surface, where <a href="/wiki/Nameless_things" title="Nameless things">Nameless things</a> dwelt. The <a href="/wiki/River-woman" title="River-woman">River-maiden</a> of Gladden, &#34;Gultháva&#34;, sensed the approach of evil that was about to corrupt her waters. She bade the dwarves leave, and called forth the waters to flood their mines. The waters of the Gladden did not drown the Nameless creatures as she hoped, but their spread to the surface was halted. The changed course of the Gladden carried the sediment downstream, choking the Gladdenmere until its banks overflowed. Water poured over the cliff, flooding the valley and submerging its roads. In this way, the Gladden Fields became a wide marsh of reeds and dark pools. In time the level of the lake was restored, and the Gladden Falls became the narrow torrent that can be observed in the present day.</dd></dl>
