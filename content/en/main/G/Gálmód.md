**Gálmód** was a man of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

## History {#History}

Gálmód must have been born and flourished around the 30th century of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age);<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> he was the father of [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma"), the advisor to [King Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden "Théoden") of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Gálmód* seems to derive from the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) word for "light-minded, licentious"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> or "light, wanton mood".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
