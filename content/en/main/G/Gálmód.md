**Gálmód** was a man of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).

## History {#History}

Gálmód must have been born and flourished around the 30th century of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md);<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> he was the father of [Gríma Wormtongue](Gríma.md), the advisor to [King Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Gálmód* seems to derive from the [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) word for "light-minded, licentious"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> or "light, wanton mood".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
