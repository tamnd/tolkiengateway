The **Gate of Steel**, also known as the **Great Gate**, was the seventh of the [Seven Gates of Gondolin](/wiki/Seven_Gates_of_Gondolin).

## History {#History}

The Gate of Steel was the last gate both in order of entering and construction, having being built by [Maeglin](/wiki/Maeglin) some centuries after the other six as an ultimate defence after the disaster of the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad). Though all but impassable to an enemy, the Gate ultimately failed to prevent the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin).

The gate was like a great steel fence across the Orfalch Echor. The fence had seven great needle-like pillars of steel and between these pillars there were seven cross-bars of steel and forty-nine vertical rods with heads like broad blades of spears. In the centre, above the midmost pillar, was raised a mighty image of the king-helm of [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon), the [Crown of the Hidden Kingdom](/wiki/Crown_of_the_Hidden_Kingdom), set about with diamonds. When [Elemmakil](/wiki/Elemmakil) struck the gate, rather than opening, the current [Warden of the Great Gate](/wiki/Warden_of_the_Great_Gate), [Ecthelion](/wiki/Ecthelion), rode from the north tower on a white horse with an esquire and a company of riders from both towers. The gate opened inward on either side of the pillar of the Crown. This gate followed the [Gate of Gold](/wiki/Gate_of_Gold), and when [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) went through, they came to a high sward that looked over [Tumladen](/wiki/Tumladen). A host of the army of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) were stationed on either side. The host consisted of representatives from each of the guards of the Seven Gates, likely including members of [Elemmakil's](/wiki/Elemmakil "Elemmakil") own [Dark Guard](/wiki/Dark_Guard). The captains and chieftains of each Guard sat upon white and gray horses. As Tuor stood before them in the livery of [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast), they gazed upon him since many of them were with [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) when he first set his armor upon the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=High_Seat_of_Vinyamar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="High Seat of Vinyamar (page does not exist)">High Seat of Vinyamar</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
