**Goldórin** is the [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) word for "[Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)", that means [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya); the Teleri knew it primarily as the tongue of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), for they had few contacts with the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The first element of the word Goldórin, is the compound form of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Golodo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Golodo (page does not exist)">Golodo</a> "Noldo"; the long *ó* of the word reflects the long final vowel of the [primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish "Primitive Elvish") word <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=%C3%91golod%C3%B4&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ñgolodô (page does not exist)">ñgolodô</a></i>.
