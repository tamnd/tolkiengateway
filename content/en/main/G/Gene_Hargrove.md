**Eugene C. Hargrove** (October [1944](../_/1944.md)) is a philosophy professor at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Texas" title="wikipedia:University of North Texas">University of North Texas</a>. He was introduced to [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) by the [Ace paperback](../A/Ace_Books.md), and became interested in music after hearing [Donald Swann](../D/Donald_Swann.md)'s [take](<../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book).md>) and not liking it.
