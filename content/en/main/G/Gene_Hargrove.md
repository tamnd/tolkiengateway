**Eugene C. Hargrove** (October [1944](/wiki/1944)) is a philosophy professor at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Texas" title="wikipedia:University of North Texas">University of North Texas</a>. He was introduced to [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") by the [Ace paperback](/wiki/Ace_Books "Ace Books"), and became interested in music after hearing [Donald Swann](/wiki/Donald_Swann)'s [take](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)") and not liking it.
