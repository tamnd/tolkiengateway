**Gereth** was a [Gnome](/wiki/Gnome) who helped [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing) escape the [ruin of Artanor](/wiki/Second_Kinslaying "Second Kinslaying") by the [sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor"), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

He, along with [Evranin](/wiki/Evranin), Elwing's nurse, also managed to smuggle the [Nauglafring](/wiki/Nauglafring) out of Artanor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
