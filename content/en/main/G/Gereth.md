**Gereth** was a [Gnome](Gnome.md) who helped [Elwing](../E/Elwing.md) escape the [ruin of Artanor](../S/Second_Kinslaying.md) by the [sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

He, along with [Evranin](../E/Evranin.md), Elwing's nurse, also managed to smuggle the [Nauglafring](../N/Nauglafring.md) out of Artanor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
