**Grimir**<sup id="cite_ref-Dwarven_Elder_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwarven_Elder-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> was a [Dwarf of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor "Dwarves of Erebor") who traveled to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) with the envoys who attended the [the Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond "Council of Elrond").

Grimir is an original creation for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* film.

## History {#History}

Grimir was a [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") of [the Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age"), who spent many years in exile with the [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor) while [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) ruled [the Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"). Though old and grey by the time [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), Lord of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), called [the Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond "Council of Elrond"), he could still wield his axe with strength.

The Council invited representatives of factions of [the Free peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/Free_peoples "Free peoples") to the Council, which aimed to answer the growing threat of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). The [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain) [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot) sent multiple emissaries to attend: [Farin](</wiki/Farin_(dwarven_emissary)> "Farin (dwarven emissary)"), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) and [Thrarin](/wiki/Thrarin). Grimir and [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in) accompanied them, and they entered Rivendell with Gimli, Glóin's son.
