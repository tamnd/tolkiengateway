**Galdor** was a [Gnome](/wiki/Gnome)-lord of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

He was the lord of the [folk of the Tree](/wiki/Folk_of_the_Tree "Folk of the Tree"), and like them he probably carried either a great club or spear, and wore green. He was said to be the bravest of the [Gondothlim](/wiki/Gondothlim), save King [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) himself.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:173</sup>

When the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin) began, he and his folk were near the northern gate, where [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)> "Gothmog (balrog)") led the main attack of [Melko](/wiki/Melko)'s armies. When the gates fell, he jumped upon the enemies with his folk and the [house of the Hammer of Wrath](/wiki/House_of_the_Hammer_of_Wrath "House of the Hammer of Wrath").<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:176</sup> But after [Rog](/wiki/Rog) and those of the Hammer died outside the walls, the folk of the Tree retreated to the [Square of the Folkwell](/wiki/Square_of_the_Folkwell). There they fought under the [Arch of Inwë](/wiki/Arch_of_Inw%C3%AB) until [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) entered the square carrying [Ecthelion](/wiki/Ecthelion) and being pursued by Orcs. "There did Galdor become the salvation of Tuor", because "the Orcs had taken them both but for the sudden rush of that champion and the dint of his club."<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:181–2</sup>

Later on, in the [Square of the Palace](/wiki/Square_of_the_Palace), Turgon proclaimed the fall of Gondolin, throwing his crown at the roots of [Glingol](/wiki/Glingol), and it was Galdor who picked the crown up, but the king rejected it.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:185</sup>

He joined the [exiles of Gondolin](/wiki/Exiles_of_Gondolin "Exiles of Gondolin"), and after crossing [Idril's secret way](/wiki/Idril%27s_secret_way), he encouraged them to run to the [Encircling Mountains](/wiki/Encircling_Mountains) before the sunrise.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:189</sup> When they reached the [Cristhorn](/wiki/Cristhorn), he led a group of men ahead of the survivors. They were the first on crossing the narrow pass and had to fight against an ambush of the Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:191–3</sup>

Galdor lived later in the [mouth of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion "Mouths of Sirion") and survived the battle that Melko made against the people dwelling there. It is said that he later visited the ruins of Gondolin with [Eärendel](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendel) and now lives in [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), where he still has followers.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name *Galdor* is never glossed, but in the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) stage of the language, it is related with the [roots](/wiki/Root "Root") [GALA](/wiki/GALA)/[GALAD](/wiki/GALAD), related with "thrive" and "trees".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) name of the character was <i><b>Aldaron</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> which confirms this relation with the word "tree", as it is also the [name](/wiki/Aldaron "Aldaron") of [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) meaning "(King) of the Forests".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an outline of the last Lost Tale, Tolkien considered that both Galdor and [Egalmoth](/wiki/Egalmoth) led the Noldoli back to Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In late writings, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") considered that Galdor of Gondolin and [Galdor of the Havens](/wiki/Galdor_of_the_Havens) may have been the same, but ultimately rejected the notion. If Galdor had remained behind, he would either have completely rejected the call of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), and thus had fallen, or he would have had to leave and then return like [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) did, which would make him a far more powerful person than his appearance in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* suggests. For this reason Tolkien speculated that *Galdor* was simply a common [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
