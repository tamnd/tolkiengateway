**Grip** was a large wolf-like [dog](/wiki/Dogs "Dogs"), one of three kept by [Farmer Maggot](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot) on his farm at [Bamfurlong](/wiki/Bamfurlong) in the [Marish](/wiki/Marish). Like Maggot's other dogs, [Fang](</wiki/Fang_(dog)> "Fang (dog)") and [Wolf](</wiki/Wolf_(dog)> "Wolf (dog)"), Grip was ferocious by nature.

Grip was with Maggot when a stranger in the form of a [Black Rider](/wiki/Black_Rider) came to the farm searching for '[Baggins](/wiki/Baggins_Family "Baggins Family")', and at that time his obedience and ferocity failed: he sniffed the stranger, then yelped in terror and ran from him howling, sensing the [Black Shadow](/wiki/Black_Shadow).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
