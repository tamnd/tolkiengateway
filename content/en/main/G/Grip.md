**Grip** was a large wolf-like [dog](../D/Dogs.md), one of three kept by [Farmer Maggot](../F/Farmer_Maggot.md) on his farm at [Bamfurlong](../B/Bamfurlong.md) in the [Marish](../M/Marish.md). Like Maggot's other dogs, [Fang](<../F/Fang_(dog).md>) and [Wolf](<../W/Wolf_(dog).md>), Grip was ferocious by nature.

Grip was with Maggot when a stranger in the form of a [Black Rider](../B/Black_Rider.md) came to the farm searching for '[Baggins](../B/Baggins_Family~66343.md)', and at that time his obedience and ferocity failed: he sniffed the stranger, then yelped in terror and ran from him howling, sensing the [Black Shadow](../B/Black_Shadow.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
