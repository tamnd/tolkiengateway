**Gilbert Keith Chesterton** ([29 May](../_/29_May.md) [1874](../_/1874.md) – [14 June](../_/14_June.md) [1936](../_/1936.md)) was a British author, poet, lay theologian, and literary and art critic. Chesterton is regarded by scholars such as [Clyde S. Kilby](../C/Clyde_S._Kilby.md) as influential on the [Inklings](../I/Inklings.md).
