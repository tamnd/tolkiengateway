**Gothic Power** is an instrumental piece by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-Ray_Dog" title="wikipedia:X-Ray Dog">X-Ray Dog</a>, a music collective specialized in trailer music. *Gothic Power* was based on the main theme of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Requiem_for_a_Dream" title="wikipedia:Requiem for a Dream">Requiem for a Dream</a> and appeared on some trailers of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.
