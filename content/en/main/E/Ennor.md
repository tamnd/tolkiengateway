<i><b>Ennor</b></i> is the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name for "[Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) equivalent is *[Endor](Endor.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-PE_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- Roots <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=EN(ED)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="EN(ED) (page does not exist)">EN(ED)</a> ("centre, middle") + <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=NDOR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NDOR (page does not exist)">NDOR</a> ("land")<sup id="cite_ref-PE_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Compounds {#Compounds}

- Sindarin *ennorath* ("(all) the Middle-lands"; *ennor* + *[-ath](../_/-ath.md)*)<sup id="cite_ref-PE_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-2">[2]</a></sup>
