**Er Murazor** is a blackened melodic death metal band formed in 2016 by members from Chile and the United States.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The band takes its name from [Er-Mûrazôr](/wiki/Witch-king#Tabletop_Games "Witch-king"), the name of the Witch-king in [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)

## Members {#Members}

- Jared Moran - drums, vocals
- Kveldulf Bjalfason - guitars, bass
