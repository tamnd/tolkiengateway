The **Ered Wethrin**, or the **Mountains of Shadow**, was a mountain range in the north of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

A line of hills to the southwest formed the southern boundary of [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md). In the south, it was an east-west range that divided [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) and [Mithrim](../M/Mithrim.md) to the north from [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) to the south, then in the east it curved around to the northwest, forming the boundary of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) and separating it from [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md). The [Mountains of Mithrim](../M/Mountains_of_Mithrim.md) were a northwesterly spur that separated Dor-lómin from Mithrim.

Several rivers arose in the Ered Wethrin, including [Narog](../N/Narog.md), [Taeglin](../T/Taeglin.md), and [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md) with its [source](Eithel_Sirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

They were among the mountains raised about [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) during the [Battle of the Powers](../B/Battle_of_the_Powers.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-2">[2]</a></sup>

When the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) [followers of Fëanor](../H/House_of_Fëanor.md) arrived to Middle-earth, they were first attacked by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) in [Lake Mithrim](../L/Lake_Mithrim.md). The [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](../D/Dagor-nuin-Giliath.md) began there, but the Noldor pursued the Orcs among the Mountains of Shadow to [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md). However, Fëanor did not stop there and was mortally wounded before reaching [Angband](../A/Angband.md). He was saved by his [sons](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) and brought back to the slopes of the Ered Wethrin, and while going upward the path in Eithel Sirion, he died there while watching [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md) in the distance.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the coming of the host of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) and the rising of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md), [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s armies ran back to Angband, but Fingolfin chose to withdraw to Mithrim, having the Ered Wethrin as a shield.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-3">[3]</a></sup>

Later, [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md), Dor-lómin, Mithrim, and Hithlum were given to Fingolfin and his sons, so all the mountain range became under the protection of the [House of Fingolfin](../H/House_of_Fingolfin.md). Several fortresses were built here,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> with the main fortress being [Barad Eithel](../B/Barad_Eithel.md) from which they watched Ard-galen and their cavalry came upon the plain.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Ered Wethrin disappeared beneath the waves at the end of the First Age.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ered Wethrin* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Shadowy Mountains".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> It consists of *[ered](Ered.md)* ("mountains") and *[gwethrin](../G/Gwathren.md)* ("shadowy").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> <span></span>
