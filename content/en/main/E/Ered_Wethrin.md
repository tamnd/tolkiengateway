The **Ered Wethrin**, or the **Mountains of Shadow**, was a mountain range in the north of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

## Geography {#Geography}

A line of hills to the southwest formed the southern boundary of [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast). In the south, it was an east-west range that divided [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) and [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim) to the north from [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) to the south, then in the east it curved around to the northwest, forming the boundary of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) and separating it from [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith). The [Mountains of Mithrim](/wiki/Mountains_of_Mithrim) were a northwesterly spur that separated Dor-lómin from Mithrim.

Several rivers arose in the Ered Wethrin, including [Narog](/wiki/Narog), [Taeglin](/wiki/Taeglin), and [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) with its [source](/wiki/Eithel_Sirion "Eithel Sirion").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

They were among the mountains raised about [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) during the [Battle of the Powers](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers).<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-2">[2]</a></sup>

When the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) [followers of Fëanor](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor "House of Fëanor") arrived to Middle-earth, they were first attacked by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) in [Lake Mithrim](/wiki/Lake_Mithrim). The [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](/wiki/Dagor-nuin-Giliath) began there, but the Noldor pursued the Orcs among the Mountains of Shadow to [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen). However, Fëanor did not stop there and was mortally wounded before reaching [Angband](/wiki/Angband). He was saved by his [sons](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor") and brought back to the slopes of the Ered Wethrin, and while going upward the path in Eithel Sirion, he died there while watching [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim) in the distance.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the coming of the host of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and the rising of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun), [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s armies ran back to Angband, but Fingolfin chose to withdraw to Mithrim, having the Ered Wethrin as a shield.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-3">[3]</a></sup>

Later, [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast), Dor-lómin, Mithrim, and Hithlum were given to Fingolfin and his sons, so all the mountain range became under the protection of the [House of Fingolfin](/wiki/House_of_Fingolfin). Several fortresses were built here,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> with the main fortress being [Barad Eithel](/wiki/Barad_Eithel) from which they watched Ard-galen and their cavalry came upon the plain.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Ered Wethrin disappeared beneath the waves at the end of the First Age.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ered Wethrin* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Shadowy Mountains".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> It consists of *[ered](/wiki/Ered "Ered")* ("mountains") and *[gwethrin](/wiki/Gwathren "Gwathren")* ("shadowy").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> <span></span>
