**Eric Rücker Eddison** ([24 November](/wiki/24_November) [1882](/wiki/1882) – [18 August](/wiki/18_August) [1945](/wiki/1945)) was an occasional member of the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings).

In a letter to [Caroline Everett](/wiki/Letter_199 "Letter 199"), Tolkien called E.R. Eddison "the greatest and most convincing writer of 'invented worlds' that I have ever read."
