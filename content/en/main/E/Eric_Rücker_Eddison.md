**Eric Rücker Eddison** ([24 November](../_/24_November.md) [1882](../_/1882.md) – [18 August](../_/18_August.md) [1945](../_/1945.md)) was an occasional member of the [Inklings](../I/Inklings.md).

In a letter to [Caroline Everett](../L/Letter_199.md), Tolkien called E.R. Eddison "the greatest and most convincing writer of 'invented worlds' that I have ever read."
