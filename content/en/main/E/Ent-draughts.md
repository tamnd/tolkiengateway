**Ent-draughts** were the mysterious and potent waters consumed by the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) which [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) shared with the [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") in the [Wellinghall](/wiki/Wellinghall).

Though little is known about them in detail, they seem to have been made from river-water, but with unique and subtle flavours reflecting the Ents' association with trees and forests. The first time [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") imbibed an ent-draught it reminded them of the smell of a distant wood brought from afar by a cool night breeze. The second time they drank the flavour was earthier and richer, more food-like.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Their power of growth was most evident when they were drunk by beings other than Ents: the Ent-draughts taken by Merry and Pippin added three inches or more to their heights,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> making them the tallest [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) in the history of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
