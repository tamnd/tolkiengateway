<i><b>ered</b></i> is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for "mountains", being the plural of <i><b>orod</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE1764_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE1764-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [OR](/wiki/OR) ("rise, mount")<sup id="cite_ref-PE1764_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE1764-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

*ered* is the plural form used by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in his writings of the 1930s and 1940s (including *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*) and by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) in the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion "The Silmarillion")*. In his later work on the Silmarillion in the 1950s and 60s, J.R.R. Tolkien often used the plural form <i><b>eryd</b></i> instead, for example *Eryd Lindon*<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and *Eryd Gorgoroth*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> replacing earlier *[Ered Lindon](/wiki/Ered_Lindon)* and *[Ered Gorgoroth](/wiki/Ered_Gorgoroth)*.

In the normal pluralization rules of [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), the form *eryd* would be the proper plural, because an *o* in the final syllable of a singular noun became *y* in the plural, such as *emyn* the plural of *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* ("hill") and *gelydh* the plural of *[golodh](/wiki/Golodh "Golodh")* ("Noldo"). To explain the presence of *ered* in *The Lord of the Rings*, Tolkien considered modifying the internal history of the Sindarin pluralization, so that the *y*-plurals of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) had changed to *e* by the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), except in limited cases such as before nasals like *n* and *m* (to explain *emyn*, which also appeared in *The Lord of the Rings*).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup>
