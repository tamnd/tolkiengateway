The **Elf-fathers** were the three male [Eru-begotten](/wiki/Eru-begotten) [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who [awoke](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves "Awakening of the Elves") at [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen) and then formed the [Three Clans](/wiki/Three_Kindreds "Three Kindreds"). [Imin](/wiki/Imin), [Tata](/wiki/Tata), and [Enel](/wiki/Enel) were their names which translate to "One", "Two", and "Three" respectively. Their followers were thus named [Minyar](/wiki/Minyar) (Firsts), [Tatyar](/wiki/Tatyar) (Seconds), and [Nelyar](/wiki/Nelyar) (Thirds). Their wives were (respectively) [Iminyë](/wiki/Iminy%C3%AB), [Tatië](/wiki/Tati%C3%AB), and [Enelyë](/wiki/Enely%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
