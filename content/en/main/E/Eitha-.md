<i><b>eitha-</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word given three different meanings:

1. "to ease, assist" (root [ATH](/wiki/ATH); cognates: [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Asya-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Asya- (page does not exist)">asya-</a></i>, <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Asi%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Asië (page does not exist)">asië</a></i>)<sup id="cite_ref-PE148_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE148-1">[1]</a></sup>
2. "to prick with a sharp point, to stab"<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup>
3. "to treat with scorn, insult"<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup>
