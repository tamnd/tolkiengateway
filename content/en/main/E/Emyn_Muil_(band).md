**Emyn Muil** is an epic/atmospheric black metal band from Cassano delle Murge, Italy.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The solo project of Saverio Giove (Nartum), it takes its name from the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil), the maze of barren hills in [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) where Frodo and Sam encountered [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum). Its 2013 debut *Túrin Turambar Dagnir Glaurunga* retells the story of [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin).
