The **East Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> also known as the **East-West Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UTNote6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote6-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the **Great Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-5">[5]</a></sup> the **Old Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> or the **great East Road** east of the [Brandywine Bridge](../B/Brandywine_Bridge.md),<sup id="cite_ref-UI_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> was a great ancient road in [Eriador](Eriador.md) that ran from the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Course {#Course}

The western end of the Road was the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-2">[2]</a></sup> near the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-11">[11]</a></sup> The East Road passed through the Shire, and two of its most important towns, [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md) and [Michel Delving](../M/Michel_Delving.md), lay athwart the road.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Just northeast where the East Road crossed the [Greenway](../G/Greenway.md) was the town of [Bree](../B/Bree.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-11">[11]</a></sup> A day's journey east of Bree on the East Road was [The Forsaken Inn](../T/The_Forsaken_Inn.md). The [Ford of Bruinen](../F/Ford_of_Bruinen.md) near [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) was a twelve days long march east of Bree on the East Road.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It is not recorded when and by whom the East Road was built. It is possible that Dwarves had used the East Road or a path that followed the course of the East Road since the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

[Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) had lived in the underground cities of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) and [Belegost](../B/Belegost.md) in the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) since the First Age.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> At the end of the First Age during the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) between the forces of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and the forces of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) great earthquakes caused the western parts of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) west of the Blue Mountains to sink beneath the sea and created a gap in the south of the Blue Mountains into which a gulf of the sea flowed. The earthquakes also caused the river [Lune](../L/Lhûn.md) to change its course and flowed into the new gulf. The Elves built the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) at the eastern end of the gulf, which was named the [Gulf of Lune](../G/Gulf_of_Lune.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-15">[15]</a></sup> As a result of the events Nogrod and Belegost were ruined and many of their inhabitants fled to the underground dwarven city of [Khazad-dûm](../K/Khazad-dûm.md) in the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) in the east.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

In the First Age the [Longbeard Dwarves](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md) of Khazad-dûm and their kin from the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md) began to build a road from the [east gate](../G/Great_Gates.md) of Khazad-dûm north along the eastern skirts of the Misty Mountains over the upper part of the [Gladden River](../G/Gladden_River.md) to the southernmost point where a bridge over the river [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) could be constructed and then eastwards on a stone bridge over the Anduin east across the valley of the Anduin through [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) to a bridge over the [River Running](../R/River_Running.md) and then over open land north-east to the iron mines<sup id="cite_ref-NM_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-17">[17]</a></sup> in the Iron Hills.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

Near the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) at the time of the [Quest of Erebor](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md) a road ran near the valley of Rivendell eastwards to the [High Pass](../H/High_Pass.md).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> It is possible that this road had been made a long time ago. At the time of the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md) at the end of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) an "ancient" road ran down from the High Pass and that the bridge over the Anduin that it led to was enlarged and strengthened so that the armies of the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](../L/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men.md) could pass over it.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

Records from the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) at the end of the Third Age refer to [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) working the fields on both side of the "Dwarves' ancestral road to the" Blue Mountains<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> and state that dwarves had "always" travelled on the East Road to their mines in the Blue Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) realm-in-exile of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) was founded, the East Road from the Grey Havens to Rivendell and the [great road](../N/North-South_Road.md) that connected [Fornost](../F/Fornost.md) with the second Númenorean realm-in-exile of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) were the only "Númenórean roads" in the north-west of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UTNote6_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote6-3">[3]</a></sup>

After Arnor was divided in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [861](../T/Third_Age_861.md), the East Road formed the boundary between two of its successor states, [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md) and [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md). During the [War with Angmar](../A/Angmar_War.md), [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md) and Cardolan tried to maintain a frontier along the [Weather Hills](../W/Weather_Hills.md), the East Road, and the lower [Hoarwell](../H/Hoarwell.md) against [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) and Rhudaur.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup>

By the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the most notable users of the road were the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) who travelled to and from the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-5">[5]</a></sup> Those often stopped at [Bree](../B/Bree.md), and when they traversed [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), it was said that they brought news from the world outside, thus becoming the main link between the Shire and other realms.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Originally, the Road made two large curves: a great loop south of Weathertop to the North-east and then "bent back again southward towards the [River](../B/Bruinen.md)" around the bottom of the [Trollshaws](../T/Trollshaws.md).

Because of [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md)'s "carelessness", in the [1954](../_/1954.md) [published map](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md), the Road has only a small northward curve between Weathertop and the [Last Bridge](../L/Last_Bridge.md), and then runs in a straight line to the [Ford of Bruinen](../F/Ford_of_Bruinen.md). In the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Edition&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Edition (page does not exist)">Second Edition</a> onwards, in order to agree with the map, the portion of the text was corrected to "the Road behind held on its way to the River Bruinen".<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup>

The relevant map in [Barbara Strachey](../B/Barbara_Strachey.md)'s atlas *[Journeys of Frodo](../J/Journeys_of_Frodo.md)* reflects the earlier descriptions.
