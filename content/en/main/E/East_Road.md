The **East Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> also known as the **East-West Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UTNote6_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote6-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the **Great Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-5">[5]</a></sup> the **Old Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> or the **great East Road** east of the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge),<sup id="cite_ref-UI_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> was a great ancient road in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) that ran from the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Course {#Course}

The western end of the Road was the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens)<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-2">[2]</a></sup> near the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-11">[11]</a></sup> The East Road passed through the Shire, and two of its most important towns, [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) and [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving), lay athwart the road.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Just northeast where the East Road crossed the [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway) was the town of [Bree](/wiki/Bree).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-11">[11]</a></sup> A day's journey east of Bree on the East Road was [The Forsaken Inn](/wiki/The_Forsaken_Inn). The [Ford of Bruinen](/wiki/Ford_of_Bruinen) near [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) was a twelve days long march east of Bree on the East Road.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It is not recorded when and by whom the East Road was built. It is possible that Dwarves had used the East Road or a path that followed the course of the East Road since the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

[Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) had lived in the underground cities of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) and [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost) in the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) since the First Age.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> At the end of the First Age during the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) between the forces of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and the forces of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) great earthquakes caused the western parts of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) west of the Blue Mountains to sink beneath the sea and created a gap in the south of the Blue Mountains into which a gulf of the sea flowed. The earthquakes also caused the river [Lune](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn "Lhûn") to change its course and flowed into the new gulf. The Elves built the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) at the eastern end of the gulf, which was named the [Gulf of Lune](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune).<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-15">[15]</a></sup> As a result of the events Nogrod and Belegost were ruined and many of their inhabitants fled to the underground dwarven city of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm) in the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) in the east.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

In the First Age the [Longbeard Dwarves](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk "Durin's Folk") of Khazad-dûm and their kin from the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) began to build a road from the [east gate](/wiki/Great_Gates "Great Gates") of Khazad-dûm north along the eastern skirts of the Misty Mountains over the upper part of the [Gladden River](/wiki/Gladden_River) to the southernmost point where a bridge over the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) could be constructed and then eastwards on a stone bridge over the Anduin east across the valley of the Anduin through [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) to a bridge over the [River Running](/wiki/River_Running) and then over open land north-east to the iron mines<sup id="cite_ref-NM_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-17">[17]</a></sup> in the Iron Hills.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

Near the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) at the time of the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor) a road ran near the valley of Rivendell eastwards to the [High Pass](/wiki/High_Pass).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> It is possible that this road had been made a long time ago. At the time of the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) at the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) an "ancient" road ran down from the High Pass and that the bridge over the Anduin that it led to was enlarged and strengthened so that the armies of the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men) could pass over it.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

Records from the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) at the end of the Third Age refer to [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) working the fields on both side of the "Dwarves' ancestral road to the" Blue Mountains<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> and state that dwarves had "always" travelled on the East Road to their mines in the Blue Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") realm-in-exile of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) was founded, the East Road from the Grey Havens to Rivendell and the [great road](/wiki/North-South_Road "North-South Road") that connected [Fornost](/wiki/Fornost) with the second Númenorean realm-in-exile of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) were the only "Númenórean roads" in the north-west of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-UTNote6_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote6-3">[3]</a></sup>

After Arnor was divided in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [861](/wiki/Third_Age_861 "Third Age 861"), the East Road formed the boundary between two of its successor states, [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan) and [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur). During the [War with Angmar](/wiki/Angmar_War "Angmar War"), [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) and Cardolan tried to maintain a frontier along the [Weather Hills](/wiki/Weather_Hills), the East Road, and the lower [Hoarwell](/wiki/Hoarwell) against [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) and Rhudaur.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup>

By the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the most notable users of the road were the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) who travelled to and from the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-5">[5]</a></sup> Those often stopped at [Bree](/wiki/Bree), and when they traversed [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), it was said that they brought news from the world outside, thus becoming the main link between the Shire and other realms.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Originally, the Road made two large curves: a great loop south of Weathertop to the North-east and then "bent back again southward towards the [River](/wiki/Bruinen "Bruinen")" around the bottom of the [Trollshaws](/wiki/Trollshaws).

Because of [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien)'s "carelessness", in the [1954](/wiki/1954) [published map](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth "General Map of Middle-earth"), the Road has only a small northward curve between Weathertop and the [Last Bridge](/wiki/Last_Bridge), and then runs in a straight line to the [Ford of Bruinen](/wiki/Ford_of_Bruinen). In the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Edition&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Edition (page does not exist)">Second Edition</a> onwards, in order to agree with the map, the portion of the text was corrected to "the Road behind held on its way to the River Bruinen".<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup>

The relevant map in [Barbara Strachey](/wiki/Barbara_Strachey)'s atlas *[Journeys of Frodo](/wiki/Journeys_of_Frodo)* reflects the earlier descriptions.
