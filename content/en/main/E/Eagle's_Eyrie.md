The **Great Shelf**, also known as the **Eyrie**, was the most important "[eagles'](/wiki/Eagles "Eagles") eyrie" located on the north-eastern side of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Pan_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pan-1">[1]</a></sup> Its exact location was unknown, but it was presumably close to the [High Pass](/wiki/High_Pass) and [Goblin-town](/wiki/Goblin-town). It was from this location that the Eagles helped the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) kept watch over the lands east of the Misty Mountains, gathering information about the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) in the region.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

As shown in [Tolkien's own illustration](/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_The_Misty_Mountains_looking_West_from_the_Eyrie_towards_Goblin_Gate.jpg "File:J.R.R. Tolkien - The Misty Mountains looking West from the Eyrie towards Goblin Gate.jpg"), it was not within the main range of the Misty Mountains themselves, but on a peak that stood a little way out into the plain, eastward of the main mountain-chain.

## History {#History}

It is not known when the [Great Eagle](/wiki/Great_Eagle) and his fifteen chieftains<sup id="cite_ref-chieftains_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chieftains-3">[3]</a></sup> first settled the Great Shelf.

On [19 July](/wiki/19_July) in the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles) of the eyrie rescued [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) from the tops of the trees at the meeting-place of the [Goblins](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") and the [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs). After which, [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and [Dori](/wiki/Dori) were taken to one of the "eagles' eyries" of the North before the Eagle who brought them there was bidden to take them to the Great Shelf to see the Great Eagle. Upon arriving at the Great Shelf, Thorin and Company were given leave by the Great Eagle to light a fire to cook rabbits, hares, and sheep. where they spent the night.<sup id="cite_ref-Pan_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pan-1">[1]</a></sup>

The next day, after it was revealed that the Eagles rescued the Dwarves because [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) had previously healed the Great Eagle of an arrow-wound in the past,<sup id="cite_ref-Pan_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pan-1">[1]</a></sup> the Great Eagle and his chieftains took Thorin and Company from the Great Shelf to the [Carrock](/wiki/Carrock).<sup id="cite_ref-chieftains_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chieftains-3">[3]</a></sup>
