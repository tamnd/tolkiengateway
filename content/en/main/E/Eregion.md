**Eregion** or **Hollin** was a realm of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), located near the [Walls of Moria](/wiki/Walls_of_Moria), under the shadow of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). It was the only lasting Noldorin realm outside [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

Eregion was located in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> west of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) near the [West-gate](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin "Doors of Durin") of the underground [dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") realm of [Moria](/wiki/Moria).<sup id="cite_ref-SA1082_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA1082-3">[3]</a></sup> The northern border of Eregion was a low [ridge](/wiki/Hollin_ridge "Hollin ridge") fourty-five leagues (135 miles) south of the elven refuge of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Its southern border was the river [Glanduin](/wiki/Glanduin).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The rivers [Gwathló](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3) and [Bruinen](/wiki/Bruinen) formed its western border<sup id="cite_ref-SA1082_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA1082-3">[3]</a></sup> and the mountain cliff of the West-gate of Moria was its eastern border.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Foundation {#Foundation}

After the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), most of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) who remained in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) dwelt in [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) under the rule of [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), their [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor "High King of the Noldor").<sup id="cite_ref-rings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-1">[1]</a></sup> After some seven centuries, [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor), the grandson of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), chose to depart from Lindon and passed eastward, drawn by the promise of mithril in the mines of the Dwarves. Many Elves followed [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor) and founded the realm of Eregion.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB1_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB1-8">[8]</a></sup> Its capital, [Ost-in-Edhil](/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil), was founded in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750").<sup id="cite_ref-AppB1_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB1-8">[8]</a></sup> It was at this time that [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) travelled from [Lake Nenuial](/wiki/Lake_Nenuial) and settled in Eregion for a while.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-9">[9]</a></sup>

The Elves of Eregion lived in harmony and trading freely with the [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") civilization of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"). A high road ran from Ost-in-Edhil to Khazad-dûm, and an unprecedented friendship between the two races began in that time.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-1">[1]</a></sup> Within Eregion, an important society or guild of craftsmen emerged with Celebrimbor as its leader: these were the *[Gwaith-i-Mírdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-M%C3%ADrdain)*, "the People of the Jewel-smiths", and their work was said to be the greatest since the time of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Creation of the Rings {#Creation_of_the_Rings}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="/wiki/Rings_of_Power" title="Rings of Power">Rings of Power</a></b></div></div>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r398211">.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote{position:relative;margin:0.5rem 0;padding:0.75rem 1rem;box-shadow:var(--tg-border-color-base)0px 0px 0px 1px inset;border-radius:var(--tg-border-radius-medium);background:var(--tg-color-surface-2);font-size:var(--tg-font-size-small);line-height:var(--tg-line-height-xx-small);display:flex;gap:0.5rem;align-items:center}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon{display:flex;align-items:center}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon .mw-file-element{display:block}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon .mw-file-element[src$=".svg"]{filter:var(--filter-invert);opacity:0.5}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--red{background:var(--background-color-destructive-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--yellow{background:var(--background-color-warning-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--green{background:var(--background-color-success-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}</style>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1200](/wiki/Second_Age_1200 "Second Age 1200"), the Elves of Eregion became friends with [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), under the guise of [Annatar](/wiki/Annatar), the "Lord of Gifts".<sup id="cite_ref-AppB1_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB1-8">[8]</a></sup> After Annatar taught and assisted the [Gwaith-i-Mirdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-Mirdain) with creating the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power), he left Eregion. Celebrimbor's smiths proceeded then to forge [Three](/wiki/Three_Rings "Three Rings") more on their own with the knowledge already gained from him. Their forging was completed by around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1590](/wiki/Second_Age_1590 "Second Age 1590").<sup id="cite_ref-AppB1_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB1-8">[8]</a></sup> Annatar didn't learn about them, and they were "unsullied", free from Sauron's corrupting influence.

When Sauron created the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1600](/wiki/Second_Age_1600 "Second Age 1600"), the Elves who wore the Rings of Power heard his voice and realised they had been deceived.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppB1_8-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB1-8">[8]</a></sup> In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1693](/wiki/Second_Age_1693 "Second Age 1693"), Celebrimbor sent his three rings away for safekeeping: he gave [Nenya](/wiki/Nenya) to [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), who advised him to send the other two to [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), away from Sauron, so he gave [Vilya](/wiki/Vilya) to [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) and [Narya](/wiki/Narya) to [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppB1_8-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB1-8">[8]</a></sup>

### War and destruction {#War_and_destruction}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Mark_Poole_-_Ost-in-Edhil.jpg"><img src="/w/images/e/e3/Mark_Poole_-_Ost-in-Edhil.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="296" height="249" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="296" data-file-height="249"/></a><figcaption><i>Eregion</i> by <a href="/wiki/Mark_Poole" title="Mark Poole">Mark Poole</a></figcaption></figure>

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron" title="War of the Elves and Sauron">War of the Elves and Sauron</a></b></div></div>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r398211"/>

The Elves of Eregion did not stand alone against Sauron. From [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) sent a force commanded by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) to lend them aid. Before Elrond could come to Eregion, the Dark Lord demanded that the Rings of Power be turned over to him, and when he was refused he brought his full force against Eregion.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-9">[9]</a></sup> In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697"), [Ost-in-Edhil](/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil) fell, with [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor) himself leading a last desperate defence on the steps of the Jewel-smiths' [guild house](/wiki/House_of_the_M%C3%ADrdain "House of the Mírdain").<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-9">[9]</a></sup> He was taken captive by the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), then shot through with Orc-arrows, and by some accounts, Sauron used Celebrimbor's body as a banner as he turned to face Elrond's army approaching from the north.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-9">[9]</a></sup>

Sauron gathered up the Rings of Power that remained, and laid waste the land of Eregion, bringing an end to its people. The surviving Elves of Eregion seem to have scattered, as there are various accounts of their fates. Some fled northward to join Elrond's host, and that host was itself forced into retreat; some of these Elves of Eregion settled at [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) when Elrond founded it shortly afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_9-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-9">[9]</a></sup> They were joined there by others of their kind who had escaped into the [Wild](/wiki/Rhovanion "Rhovanion"), and only later found their way to safety. Many of these people seem to have later abandoned [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and sailed into the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/West" title="West">West</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_9-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-9">[9]</a></sup>

*Eregion* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-10">[10]</a></sup> It means "Holly-region"<sup id="cite_ref-N_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-11">[11]</a></sup> or "Land of Holly".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> It was called "Hollin-land" or short "Hollin" in [Westron](/wiki/Westron)<sup id="cite_ref-N_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-11">[11]</a></sup> by Men.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> *Hollin* is an old form, still used locally, of "[holly](/wiki/Holly "Holly")". Holly-trees were abundant in the region.<sup id="cite_ref-N_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-11">[11]</a></sup> The name Eregion is a combination of *[ereg](/wiki/Ereg "Ereg")* ("holly") and the suffix *-ion* ("-region").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)* it is stated that "*[Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) therefore went eastwards, about the year 700 of the Second Age, and established the (primarily but by no means solely) [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") realm of Eregion.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_9-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-9">[9]</a></sup> This contradicts the information published in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* which gives the founding of Eregion in the year 750.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> It is possible to reconcile the texts if 700 is seen as the year of their moving eastwards with 750 as the year of the foundation of Eregion; the canonicity of this, however, is unknown.
