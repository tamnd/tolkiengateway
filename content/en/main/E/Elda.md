**Elda** was a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) adjective meaning "of the stars"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>which came to mean the noun **Eldo**, "one of the people of the Stars", later merged and became *Elda* (pl. *[Eldar](/wiki/Eldar)*, p.pl *Eldali*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>)

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) *eldâ*, an adjectival formation meaning "connected or concerned with the stars" itself coming from an earlier [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *eledâ*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=ELED&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ELED (page does not exist)">ELED</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

- poss. sg. *eldava* "Elf's"<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
- gen. pl. *eldaron* "of Elves"<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
- dative pl. *eldain* "for Elves"<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
- poss. pl. *Eldaiva, Eldaive* "Elves'"<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
