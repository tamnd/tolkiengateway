**Edhil** (sing. **Edhel**) is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word referring to the [Elves](Elves.md) who set out on the [Great Journey](../G/Great_Journey.md), a direct equivalent of the common form, [Eldar](Eldar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

There was also a class-plural form **Eldrim** or **Elrim** (where *ll-r* was in secondary contact &gt; *ldr*, later again simplified), as well as the masculine and feminine singular forms **Ellon** and **Elleth**, respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-Q&amp;E_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Q&amp;E-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), who remained behind in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) and thus did not complete the Journey, applied this name to themselves as a description of their people as a whole. In contrast, [those Elves](../C/Calaquendi.md), who eventually completed the Journey and at last settled in the [Blessed Realm](../A/Aman.md), were called **Ódhil** (sing. **Ódhel**).<sup id="cite_ref-Q&amp;E_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Q&amp;E-2">[2]</a></sup>
