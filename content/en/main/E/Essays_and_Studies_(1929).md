<b><i>Essays and Studies</i> (1929)</b> is a collection of essays (vol. 14, [1929](../_/1929.md)) of the series *Essays and Studies by Members of the English Association*. It includes the essay '[Ancrene Wisse and Hali Meiðhad](../A/Ancrene_Wisse_and_Hali_Meiðhad.md)' (pp.\[104\]-126) by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

The collection was reprinted (hardback) in [1966](../_/1966.md) by Wm. Dawson &amp; Sons.
