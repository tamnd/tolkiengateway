<b><i>Essays and Studies</i> (1929)</b> is a collection of essays (vol. 14, [1929](/wiki/1929)) of the series *Essays and Studies by Members of the English Association*. It includes the essay '[Ancrene Wisse and Hali Meiðhad](/wiki/Ancrene_Wisse_and_Hali_Mei%C3%B0had)' (pp.\[104\]-126) by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

The collection was reprinted (hardback) in [1966](/wiki/1966) by Wm. Dawson &amp; Sons.
