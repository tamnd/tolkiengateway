**Esgalduin** was a major tributary of the [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md), which rose in the dark heights of the [Ered Gorgoroth](Ered_Gorgoroth.md) from the [Shadowy Spring](../S/Shadowy_Spring.md), and flowed southwestward to meet the Sirion on the western borders of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md). It marked the boundary between the forests of [Neldoreth](../F/Forest_of_Neldoreth.md) and [Region](../F/Forest_of_Region.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a manuscript dating from the late 1950s and early 1960s, [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote that *Esgalduin* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name meaning "the river under shade".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the earlier *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)*, the name *Esgalduin* was said to be [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md) for "River under Veil (of \[?leaves\])", consisting of the elements *[esgal](Esgal.md)* ("screen, hiding, roof of leaves") + *[duin](../D/Duin.md)* ("water, river").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
