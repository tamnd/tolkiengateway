**Esgalduin** was a major tributary of the [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion), which rose in the dark heights of the [Ered Gorgoroth](/wiki/Ered_Gorgoroth) from the [Shadowy Spring](/wiki/Shadowy_Spring), and flowed southwestward to meet the Sirion on the western borders of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath). It marked the boundary between the forests of [Neldoreth](/wiki/Forest_of_Neldoreth "Forest of Neldoreth") and [Region](/wiki/Forest_of_Region "Forest of Region").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a manuscript dating from the late 1950s and early 1960s, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") wrote that *Esgalduin* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "the river under shade".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the earlier *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")*, the name *Esgalduin* was said to be [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin) for "River under Veil (of \[?leaves\])", consisting of the elements *[esgal](/wiki/Esgal "Esgal")* ("screen, hiding, roof of leaves") + *[duin](/wiki/Duin "Duin")* ("water, river").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
