<i><b>edhel</b></i> (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈeðel]</span>), plural <i><b>edhil</b></i> (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈeðil]</span>), is the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word for *[elf](Elves.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> But it was also an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/endonym" title="wikipedia:endonym">endonym</a>, the name with which the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) referred to their own people, being "the Elves",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> in contrast to the [High Elves](../H/High_Elves.md), the [Dark Elves](../D/Dark_Elves.md) or the [Laiquendi](../L/Laiquendi.md).

It was one of the most common words in Sindarin.
