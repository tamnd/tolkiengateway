"**English to Anglo-Saxon dictionary**" refers to an [unpublished](/wiki/Index:Unpublished_material "Index:Unpublished material") dictionary by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), consisting of a group of more than 400 slips of entries for an English to [Anglo-Saxon](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") dictionary. The entries were arranged alphabetically according to Modern English. The papers are held at the [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library), Manuscript Tolkien A 20/4.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
