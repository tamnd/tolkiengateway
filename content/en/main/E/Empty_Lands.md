The **Empty Lands** were regions in the [East](East.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md) beyond the eastern boundaries of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Along with [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md), they were "cast back" at the [Changing of the World](../C/Changing_of_the_World.md), "and [new lands](../N/New_lands.md) and seas were made".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Location {#Location}

As the Empty Lands were east of Middle-earth, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) inferred that they corresponded to the [Land of the Sun](../L/Land_of_the_Sun.md) and possibly also the [Dark Land](../D/Dark_Land.md).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In their earliest appearance in Tolkien's drafts, the Empty Lands were called the **Barren Land**. When Eru "cast back" Belegaer and the Barren Land, "[new lands](../N/New_lands.md) and seas" were said to appear in areas where previously "nought had been but the paths of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) and [Moon](../M/Moon.md)". In a later draft, the Barren Land was replaced with **Empty Land** before Tolkien eventually made the term plural.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>
