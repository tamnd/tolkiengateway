The **Empty Lands** were regions in the [East](/wiki/East) of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) beyond the eastern boundaries of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Along with [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer), they were "cast back" at the [Changing of the World](/wiki/Changing_of_the_World), "and [new lands](/wiki/New_lands "New lands") and seas were made".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Location {#Location}

As the Empty Lands were east of Middle-earth, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) inferred that they corresponded to the [Land of the Sun](/wiki/Land_of_the_Sun) and possibly also the [Dark Land](/wiki/Dark_Land).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In their earliest appearance in Tolkien's drafts, the Empty Lands were called the **Barren Land**. When Eru "cast back" Belegaer and the Barren Land, "[new lands](/wiki/New_lands "New lands") and seas" were said to appear in areas where previously "nought had been but the paths of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and [Moon](/wiki/Moon)". In a later draft, the Barren Land was replaced with **Empty Land** before Tolkien eventually made the term plural.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>
