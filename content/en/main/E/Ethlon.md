**Ethlon** was a character, possibly one of the [Solosimpi](/wiki/Solosimpi), appearing only in the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in a prose fragment following *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

Ethlon is never mentioned again, and nothing else is known about the character, except that they spoke "wise words" against [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) and the rest of the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) that wanted to leave [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) after the death of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees) and the theft of the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils).<sup id="cite_ref-Eth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ethlon* is in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), according to [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack), but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Another name for the character was <i><b>Dimlint</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Eth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eth-1">[1]</a></sup>
