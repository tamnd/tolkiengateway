**Ethlon** was a character, possibly one of the [Solosimpi](../S/Solosimpi.md), appearing only in the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in a prose fragment following *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

Ethlon is never mentioned again, and nothing else is known about the character, except that they spoke "wise words" against [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) and the rest of the [Gnomes](../G/Gnomes.md) that wanted to leave [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) after the death of the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md) and the theft of the [Silmarils](../S/Silmarils.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ethlon* is in [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md), according to [Paul Strack](../P/Paul_Strack.md), but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Another name for the character was <i><b>Dimlint</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Eth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eth-1">[1]</a></sup>
