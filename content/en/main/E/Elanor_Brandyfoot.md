**Elanor Kellamark Brandyfoot**, also known as **Nori**, is a [Harfoot](/wiki/Harfoots "Harfoots") who was part of a caravan led by [Sadoc Burrows](/wiki/Sadoc_Burrows) during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).

Elanor Kellamark Brandyfoot was invented by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*, where she was portrayed by [Markella Kavenagh](/wiki/Markella_Kavenagh).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the first season, Elanor begins as one of the most adventurous [Harfoots](/wiki/Harfoots), dreaming about the wonders beyond their [wandering](/wiki/Wandering_Days "Wandering Days"). She and her best friend, [Poppy Proudfellow](/wiki/Poppy_Proudfellow), after seeing a meteorite falling from the sky, discover a mysterious figure known as [The Stranger](/wiki/The_Stranger), who is later revealed to be one of the [Istari](/wiki/Istari).

After this mysterious wizard decides to look for answers in the land of [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn), following a hint from the [Hermit's Hat](/wiki/Hermit%27s_Hat), which was a constellation that could only be seen there and was supposedly related to his mission, Nori, with the blessing of her family, goes with him.
