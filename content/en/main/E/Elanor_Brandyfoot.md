**Elanor Kellamark Brandyfoot**, also known as **Nori**, is a [Harfoot](../H/Harfoots.md) who was part of a caravan led by [Sadoc Burrows](../S/Sadoc_Burrows.md) during the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).

Elanor Kellamark Brandyfoot was invented by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)*, where she was portrayed by [Markella Kavenagh](../M/Markella_Kavenagh.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the first season, Elanor begins as one of the most adventurous [Harfoots](../H/Harfoots.md), dreaming about the wonders beyond their [wandering](../W/Wandering_Days.md). She and her best friend, [Poppy Proudfellow](../P/Poppy_Proudfellow.md), after seeing a meteorite falling from the sky, discover a mysterious figure known as [The Stranger](../T/The_Stranger.md), who is later revealed to be one of the [Istari](../I/Istari.md).

After this mysterious wizard decides to look for answers in the land of [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md), following a hint from the [Hermit's Hat](../H/Hermit's_Hat.md), which was a constellation that could only be seen there and was supposedly related to his mission, Nori, with the blessing of her family, goes with him.
