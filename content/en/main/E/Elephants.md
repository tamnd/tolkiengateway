**Elephants** were creatures only mentioned fleetingly. [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) does mention them,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> so it is possible that they shared the wide burning plains of the [Harad](../H/Harad.md) with what would be their giant relatives, the [Mûmakil](../O/Oliphaunts.md).

In [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), the word for "elephant" is *andamunda*. The [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) cognate of the same meaning is *andabon* or *annabon*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
