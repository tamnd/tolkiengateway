**Estelmo** was the squire to [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md)'s eldest son [Elendur](<Elendur_(son_of_Isildur).md>).

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2](../T/Third_Age_2.md), Estelmo accompanied Elendur and Isildur north along the eastern slopes of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md). They were [ambushed](../D/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields.md) by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), and after a blow to the head Estelmo was believed dead. Stunned, the young man survived and was found under Elendur's body. Estelmo recuperated, and was one of only three [Men](../M/Men.md) to survive the Disaster in which Isildur and [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) were lost. He was able to report the words of Isildur and Elendur when they parted.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Estelmo* is clearly [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). Although it is not glossed, [Paul Strack](../P/Paul_Strack.md) explains it could mean "Hope-person", from *[estel](Estel.md)* ("hope") + *[-mo](../_/-mo.md)* (agental suffix).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
