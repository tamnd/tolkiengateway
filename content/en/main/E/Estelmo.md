**Estelmo** was the squire to [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur)'s eldest son [Elendur](</wiki/Elendur_(son_of_Isildur)> "Elendur (son of Isildur)").

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2](/wiki/Third_Age_2 "Third Age 2"), Estelmo accompanied Elendur and Isildur north along the eastern slopes of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). They were [ambushed](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields "Disaster of the Gladden Fields") by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), and after a blow to the head Estelmo was believed dead. Stunned, the young man survived and was found under Elendur's body. Estelmo recuperated, and was one of only three [Men](/wiki/Men) to survive the Disaster in which Isildur and [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") were lost. He was able to report the words of Isildur and Elendur when they parted.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Estelmo* is clearly [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). Although it is not glossed, [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack) explains it could mean "Hope-person", from *[estel](/wiki/Estel "Estel")* ("hope") + *[-mo](/wiki/-mo)* (agental suffix).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
