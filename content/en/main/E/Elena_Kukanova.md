**Elena Kukanova** is a Russian artist<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> most notable for her artworks inspired by the writings of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), particularly *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.

She was born in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow" title="wikipedia:Moscow">Moscow</a> and educated professionally as an artist in <a class="external text" href="http://www.glazunov-academy.ru/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Russian Academy of Painting, Sculpture and Architecture</a> between [1998](../_/1998.md) and [2004](../_/2004.md).
