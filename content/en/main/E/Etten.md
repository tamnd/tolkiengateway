**Etten** is an obscure term in [Westron](../W/Westron.md) that probably refers to monsters like [Trolls](../T/Trolls.md) or [Ogres](../O/Ogres.md).<sup id="cite_ref-nomen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nomen-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-drout_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-drout-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Ettenmoors](Ettenmoors.md), and the [Ettendales](Ettendales.md) which were a part of the Ettenmoors,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> were probably named after these beings. The Ettenmoors were also called the **troll-fells**,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> probably because the region was inhabited by trolls.<sup id="cite_ref-NM_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-5">[5]</a></sup> [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) mentioned that the Ettendales were troll-country and little-known to him.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Specifically [hill-trolls](../H/Hill-trolls.md) were seen in the [lands north](../C/Coldfells.md) of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Explaining the name Ettendales (and Ettenmoors) Tolkien noted the "obsolete element <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/ettin" title="wiktionary:ettin">eten</a></i> ('troll, ogre')".<sup id="cite_ref-nomen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nomen-1">[1]</a></sup> It is a word derived from [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/eoten#Old_English" title="wiktionary:eoten">eōten</a></i> ("[giant](../G/Giants.md), [troll](../T/Trolls.md), [ogre](../O/Ogres.md)").<sup id="cite_ref-nomen_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nomen-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

These locations are probably linked to the trolls. [Michael D.C. Drout](../M/Michael_D.C._Drout.md) noted that Tolkien "probably thought of an 'ettin' as a troll".<sup id="cite_ref-drout_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-drout-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

While writing *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, Tolkien considered the name "Entish Dales" or "Entish Lands" for the Ettenmoors, as the adjective "entish" directly refers to Old English <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/ent#Old_English" title="wiktionary:ent">ent</a></i> "giant", although this was before he conceived the benevolent [Ents](Ents.md) of *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

There is no indication that Tolkien intended his "ettens" to have two heads or that he connected them with the stone-giants.

[C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) introduced the <a class="external text" href="https://narnia.fandom.com/wiki/Ettin" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ettins</a> of <a class="external text" href="https://narnia.fandom.com/wiki/Ettinsmoor" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ettinsmoor</a> in *The Silver Chair*. The ettins are giants, some of whom have two heads; they play the game of Cockshies, throwing rocks at a gorge, much like the stone-[giants](../G/Giants.md) in *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

In many fantasy settings, including some [adaptations](#Portrayal_in_adaptations) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), there are monsters called **Ettins** (or **Ettens**) that are usually portrayed as two-headed.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1998-2004: <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../M/Middle-earth_Online.md" title="Middle-earth Online">Middle-earth Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><i>Ettins</i>, a race of two-headed Trolls, was intended to be included in the game.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002-5: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game">The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><i>Ettens</i> resemble the <a href="../H/Hill-trolls.md" title="Hill-trolls">Hill-trolls</a>, but they possess &#34;not one but two long heads&#34;. The solitary creatures, a result of an ancient experiment by <a href="../M/Morgoth.md" title="Morgoth">Morgoth</a>, live almost exclusively in the Ettenmoors.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><i>Ettens</i> are a race of trolls, primarily native to the <a href="../T/Trollshaws.md" title="Trollshaws">Trollshaws</a> region. They wear animal-skin cloaks that create the illusion of them being two-headed.</dd></dl>
