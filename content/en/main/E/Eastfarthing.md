The **Eastfarthing** was one of the four [Farthings](/wiki/Farthings) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). Its westernmost point was the [Three-Farthing Stone](/wiki/Three-Farthing_Stone), its eastern border was the [Brandywine](/wiki/Baranduin "Baranduin") River, and its southern border was the River [Shirebourn](/wiki/Shirebourn). Within its bounds were the regions of [Bridgefields](/wiki/Bridgefields), the [Marish](/wiki/Marish), and [The Yale](/wiki/The_Yale).<sup id="cite_ref-map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

Geographical features were the forest of [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End), the hills of [Scary](/wiki/Scary) and the [Green Hill Country](/wiki/Green_Hill_Country) and the rivers [Stock-brook](/wiki/Stock-brook), [Thistle Brook](/wiki/Thistle_Brook), and [The Water](/wiki/The_Water).

Hobbit settlements included [Brockenbores](/wiki/Brockenbores), [Budgeford](/wiki/Budgeford), [Deephallow](/wiki/Deephallow), [Dwaling](/wiki/Dwaling), [Frogmorton](/wiki/Frogmorton), [Rushey](/wiki/Rushey), [Scary](/wiki/Scary), [Stock](/wiki/Stock), [Whitfurrows](/wiki/Whitfurrows), [Willowbottom](/wiki/Willowbottom) and [Woodhall](/wiki/Woodhall).<sup id="cite_ref-map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-1">[1]</a></sup>

Several roads that connected the towns and villages were [East Road](/wiki/East_Road), [Causeway](/wiki/Causeway) and [Northway](/wiki/Northway)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

About [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1630](/wiki/Third_Age_1630 "Third Age 1630") many [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors) from [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland) entered and settled in the Eastfarthing.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Throughout that Age, whenever [Bree-hobbits](/wiki/Bree-hobbits) visited the Shire, they mostly reached [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) or the Eastfarthing, as these were closest to their home, and rarely ventured further west.

When [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") travelled from [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) to [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow) much of their trek took place in the Eastfarthing, where they first encountered the [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") and were protected by [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor "Gildor").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Continuing eastward, the crossed the fields of the Marish and met [Farmer Maggot](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot) before they left the Eastfarthing on the [Bucklebury Ferry](/wiki/Bucklebury_Ferry).<sup id="cite_ref-mushroom_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mushroom-5">[5]</a></sup>

Upon their return from their adventures in the south, Frodo, Sam, Pippin, and [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") discovered the evil afoot in the Shire when they entered the Eastfarthing. On the first night they stayed at the uncomfortable guard-house near the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge). At Frogmorton the Travellers were “arrested” and the spent the night at the Shirriff-house. The next morning they journeyed westward and left the Eastfarthing.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Culture {#Culture}

By the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) the inhabitants of the Eastfarthing were still mainly Stoors, especially in the Marish, known by their large build and heavy-legs, and the small beard on their chin; they were known for many peculiar names and strange words ([Stoorish](/wiki/Stoorish)) uncommon in the rest of the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Some months of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), in the Eastfarthing had different names, the ones used in the [Bree Calendar](/wiki/Bree_Calendar).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In muddy regions, such as the Marish, Hobbits wore dwarf-boots and were house-dwellers, like [Farmer Maggot](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot).<sup id="cite_ref-mushroom_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mushroom-5">[5]</a></sup>

*[The Golden Perch](/wiki/The_Golden_Perch)* inn was said by [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") to serve the best beer in the Eastfarthing.<sup id="cite_ref-mushroom_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mushroom-5">[5]</a></sup>
