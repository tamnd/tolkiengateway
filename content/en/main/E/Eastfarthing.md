The **Eastfarthing** was one of the four [Farthings](../F/Farthings.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). Its westernmost point was the [Three-Farthing Stone](../T/Three-Farthing_Stone.md), its eastern border was the [Brandywine](../B/Baranduin.md) River, and its southern border was the River [Shirebourn](../S/Shirebourn.md). Within its bounds were the regions of [Bridgefields](../B/Bridgefields.md), the [Marish](../M/Marish.md), and [The Yale](../T/The_Yale.md).<sup id="cite_ref-map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

Geographical features were the forest of [Woody End](../W/Woody_End.md), the hills of [Scary](../S/Scary.md) and the [Green Hill Country](../G/Green_Hill_Country.md) and the rivers [Stock-brook](../S/Stock-brook.md), [Thistle Brook](../T/Thistle_Brook.md), and [The Water](../T/The_Water.md).

Hobbit settlements included [Brockenbores](../B/Brockenbores.md), [Budgeford](../B/Budgeford.md), [Deephallow](../D/Deephallow.md), [Dwaling](../D/Dwaling.md), [Frogmorton](../F/Frogmorton.md), [Rushey](../R/Rushey.md), [Scary](../S/Scary.md), [Stock](../S/Stock.md), [Whitfurrows](../W/Whitfurrows.md), [Willowbottom](../W/Willowbottom.md) and [Woodhall](../W/Woodhall.md).<sup id="cite_ref-map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-1">[1]</a></sup>

Several roads that connected the towns and villages were [East Road](East_Road.md), [Causeway](../C/Causeway.md) and [Northway](../N/Northway.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

About [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1630](../T/Third_Age_1630.md) many [Stoors](../S/Stoors.md) from [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md) entered and settled in the Eastfarthing.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Throughout that Age, whenever [Bree-hobbits](../B/Bree-hobbits.md) visited the Shire, they mostly reached [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md) or the Eastfarthing, as these were closest to their home, and rarely ventured further west.

When [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) travelled from [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) to [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md) much of their trek took place in the Eastfarthing, where they first encountered the [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) and were protected by [Gildor Inglorion](../G/Gildor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Continuing eastward, the crossed the fields of the Marish and met [Farmer Maggot](../F/Farmer_Maggot.md) before they left the Eastfarthing on the [Bucklebury Ferry](../B/Bucklebury_Ferry.md).<sup id="cite_ref-mushroom_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mushroom-5">[5]</a></sup>

Upon their return from their adventures in the south, Frodo, Sam, Pippin, and [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) discovered the evil afoot in the Shire when they entered the Eastfarthing. On the first night they stayed at the uncomfortable guard-house near the [Brandywine Bridge](../B/Brandywine_Bridge.md). At Frogmorton the Travellers were “arrested” and the spent the night at the Shirriff-house. The next morning they journeyed westward and left the Eastfarthing.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Culture {#Culture}

By the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) the inhabitants of the Eastfarthing were still mainly Stoors, especially in the Marish, known by their large build and heavy-legs, and the small beard on their chin; they were known for many peculiar names and strange words ([Stoorish](../S/Stoorish.md)) uncommon in the rest of the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Some months of the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md), in the Eastfarthing had different names, the ones used in the [Bree Calendar](../B/Bree_Calendar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In muddy regions, such as the Marish, Hobbits wore dwarf-boots and were house-dwellers, like [Farmer Maggot](../F/Farmer_Maggot.md).<sup id="cite_ref-mushroom_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mushroom-5">[5]</a></sup>

*[The Golden Perch](../T/The_Golden_Perch.md)* inn was said by [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) to serve the best beer in the Eastfarthing.<sup id="cite_ref-mushroom_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mushroom-5">[5]</a></sup>
