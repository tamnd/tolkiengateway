**Elvendom**, also called **Elfinesse**, referred to the domain of the [Elves](Elves.md). In [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md), [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) is called "*the heart of Elvendom*".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In the *[Lay of Leithian](../L/Lay_of_Leithian.md)*, [Lúthien Tinúviel](../L/Lúthien.md) is often named the "*fairest maid in Elfinesse*". <span></span>
