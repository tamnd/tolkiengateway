**Elvendom**, also called **Elfinesse**, referred to the domain of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves). In [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings), [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) is called "*the heart of Elvendom*".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In the *[Lay of Leithian](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian)*, [Lúthien Tinúviel](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien "Lúthien") is often named the "*fairest maid in Elfinesse*". <span></span>
