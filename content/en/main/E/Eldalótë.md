**Eldalótë** was the wife of [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod). Their son was [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth), a [lord of Nargothrond](/wiki/Lord_of_Nargothrond "Lord of Nargothrond").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldalote_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldalote-1">[1]</a></sup>

She probably joined the [Exile of the Noldor](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor), as her name was translated into Sindarin in later legend, but she is not mentioned in any of the tales of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eldalótë* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Elven-flower", from *[Elda](/wiki/Elda)* ("Elf") + *[lótë](/wiki/L%C3%B3t%C3%AB "Lótë")* ("flower"). The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) form of her name was <i><b>Eðellos</b></i> (spelled *Edhellos*).<sup id="cite_ref-Eldalote_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldalote-1">[1]</a></sup>
