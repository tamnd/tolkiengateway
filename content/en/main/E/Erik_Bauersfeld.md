**Erik Bauersfeld** ([28 June](/wiki/28_June), [1922](/wiki/1922) - [4 April](/wiki/4_April), [2016](/wiki/2016)) was an American radio dramatist. In the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mind%27s_Eye&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mind&#39;s Eye (page does not exist)">Mind&#39;s Eye</a>'s production of *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1979 radio series)")*, he provided the voices of [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden), [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) and a herbmaster.
