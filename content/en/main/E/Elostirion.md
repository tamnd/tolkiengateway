**Elostirion** was the tallest and westernmost of the three [White Towers](/wiki/White_Towers) that stood on the [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills), built by [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) for [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil). It stood alone upon a green mound.<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-1">[1]</a></sup>

It held [one](/wiki/Elostirion-stone "Elostirion-stone") of the *[palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), which did not communicate with the rest, but looked only westward across the Sundering Seas to [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa). It was the last stone of Arnor until it was removed at the very end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Although no [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbit) was known to ever climb it, those of the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing) had the tradition that from its top one could see as far as [the Sea](/wiki/The_Sea "The Sea"),<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-1">[1]</a></sup> perhaps echoing the knowledge of the palantír there.
