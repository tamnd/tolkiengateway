<i><b>Evernight</b></i> is the fourth full-length album released by the Epic metal band [Battlelore](../B/Battlelore.md). It was released on [February 26th](../_/26_February.md), [2007](../_/2007.md) via Napalm Records. The cover features *Tol Brandir* by [Ted Nasmith](../T/Ted_Nasmith.md), used and modified with permission.
