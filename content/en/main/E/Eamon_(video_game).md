<i><b>Eamon</b></i> is a long-running series of text adventure games originally for the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_II_series" title="wikipedia:Apple II series">Apple II</a> computer family and since ported to other platforms. Launched around 1980 by Donald Brown, the series now consists of approximately 280 adventures including several set in Middle-earth.
