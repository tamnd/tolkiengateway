**Erui** was a river in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). This swift-flowing<sup id="cite_ref-Beacon_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beacon-1">[1]</a></sup> river began near [Mindolluin](/wiki/Mindolluin) in the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains), flowing south through the province of [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach), where it met the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) about 75 miles south of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> Of old, it was part of the border between [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien) and [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The main road from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) to [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) crossed the Erui at the [Crossings of Erui](/wiki/Crossings_of_Erui).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Erui* is a variant of *[ereb](/wiki/Ereb "Ereb")*, which means "single, alone", but with the adjectival ending <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-ui&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-ui (page does not exist)">-ui</a></i>. It was so named because it was the shortest river, and the only one without a tributary.<sup id="cite_ref-Beacon_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beacon-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the "working map" that [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) used while writing *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* this river had the name *Ereg*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Erui was located in Eastern Gondor and was the easternmost of the <a href="/wiki/Five_Rivers_of_Lebennin" title="Five Rivers of Lebennin">Five Rivers of Lebennin</a>. It flowed through <a href="/wiki/Imloth_Melui" title="Imloth Melui">Imloth Melui</a> and divided Upper <a href="/wiki/Lebennin" title="Lebennin">Lebennin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Lossarnach" title="Lossarnach">Lossarnach</a>. The Erui was the domain of the brooding River-maid known as the Lone Lady, one of the Five Sisters, though she and her sisters were widely considered a myth by the end of the <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">Third Age</a>. The Lone Lady had long been detached and troubled due to the blood spilled in her waters during the <a href="/wiki/Kin-strife" title="Kin-strife">Kin-strife</a>, and the invasion by <a href="/wiki/Haradrim" title="Haradrim">Haradrim</a> during the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a> enraged her. She commanded the waters of the Erui to animate the armour of long-dead soldiers of Gondor to drive away any who approached the <a href="/wiki/Crossings_of_Erui" title="Crossings of Erui">Crossings</a>. She was calmed by her sisters, but not before raising the armour of <a href="/wiki/Castamir" title="Castamir">Castamir</a> himself from the riverbed.</dd></dl>
