**Eithel Sirion** was the initial spring and northernmost point of the great River [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md).

## History {#History}

Eithel Sirion was located in the eastern part of the [Ered Wethrin](Ered_Wethrin.md) and overlooked the plains of [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md). From this spot the Sirion flowed south and then slightly east, skirting the plains, to the [Fen of Serech](../F/Fen_of_Serech.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> There was a pass west of Eithel Sirion through the mountains that led to [Mithrim](../M/Mithrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup>

After [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) had been mortally wounded by [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md>) his sons carried him away from [Dor Daedeloth](../D/Dor_Daedeloth.md). As they began climbing the pass near Eithel Sirion, Fëanor had them stop, he cursed [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) three times, and then he died. As it left, Fëanor's fiery spirit consumed his body to ash; no tomb was made for him there or elsewhere.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) built a great fortress in this location called [Barad Eithel](../B/Barad_Eithel.md), from whence a watch was kept upon the plains. However, in the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) a host of Morgoth's assailed this place and other fortresses of the Ered Wethrin. Near the Eithel Sirion [Hador Lórindol](../H/Hador.md) and his son [Gundor](../G/Gundor.md) fell in [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md)'s rearguard. Morgoth withdrew his forces after the Dagor Bragollach, but sent a new host against [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) seven years later. Again the Eithel Sirion and its fortress was a main point of contention and here [Galdor of Dor-lómin](<../G/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-lómin).md>) fell.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

At the beginning of the Fifth Battle, the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) kept watch from the walls of the Barad Eithel for the beacon that would signal the start of the battle. At the bidding of [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md), Fingon held back his strength while the host of Morgoth marched up to the Eithel Sirion. Before the walls of the fortress [Gelmir](<../G/Gelmir_(son_of_Guilin).md>), son of [Guilin](../G/Guilin.md), was cruelly slain. This enraged his brother [Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md) who charged from the Eithel Sirion on horseback.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eithel Sirion* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Sirion's Well",<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> from *[eithel](Eithel.md)* ("spring, well").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
