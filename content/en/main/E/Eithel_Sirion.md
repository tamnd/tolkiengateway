**Eithel Sirion** was the initial spring and northernmost point of the great River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion).

## History {#History}

Eithel Sirion was located in the eastern part of the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin) and overlooked the plains of [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen). From this spot the Sirion flowed south and then slightly east, skirting the plains, to the [Fen of Serech](/wiki/Fen_of_Serech).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> There was a pass west of Eithel Sirion through the mountains that led to [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup>

After [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) had been mortally wounded by [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)> "Gothmog (balrog)") his sons carried him away from [Dor Daedeloth](/wiki/Dor_Daedeloth). As they began climbing the pass near Eithel Sirion, Fëanor had them stop, he cursed [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) three times, and then he died. As it left, Fëanor's fiery spirit consumed his body to ash; no tomb was made for him there or elsewhere.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) built a great fortress in this location called [Barad Eithel](/wiki/Barad_Eithel), from whence a watch was kept upon the plains. However, in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) a host of Morgoth's assailed this place and other fortresses of the Ered Wethrin. Near the Eithel Sirion [Hador Lórindol](/wiki/Hador "Hador") and his son [Gundor](/wiki/Gundor) fell in [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin)'s rearguard. Morgoth withdrew his forces after the Dagor Bragollach, but sent a new host against [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) seven years later. Again the Eithel Sirion and its fortress was a main point of contention and here [Galdor of Dor-lómin](</wiki/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min)> "Galdor (Lord of Dor-lómin)") fell.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

At the beginning of the Fifth Battle, the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) kept watch from the walls of the Barad Eithel for the beacon that would signal the start of the battle. At the bidding of [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin), Fingon held back his strength while the host of Morgoth marched up to the Eithel Sirion. Before the walls of the fortress [Gelmir](</wiki/Gelmir_(son_of_Guilin)> "Gelmir (son of Guilin)"), son of [Guilin](/wiki/Guilin), was cruelly slain. This enraged his brother [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor) who charged from the Eithel Sirion on horseback.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eithel Sirion* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Sirion's Well",<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> from *[eithel](/wiki/Eithel "Eithel")* ("spring, well").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
