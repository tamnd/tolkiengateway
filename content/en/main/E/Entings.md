**Entings** was a name used by the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) to refer to juvenile members of their own kind. At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), due to the loss of the [Entwives](/wiki/Entwives), there had been no new Entings for centuries.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
