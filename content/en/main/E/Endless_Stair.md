The **Endless Stair** was the winding stairway that led from the deepest halls of [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md) up to [Durin's Tower](../D/Durin's_Tower.md) on the peak of [Celebdil](../C/Celebdil.md). It was extremely long, thus its name.

In the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), it was presumed to have been lost or destroyed, and even some [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) thought that it was legendary. [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) proved it not so when he pursued [Durin's Bane](../D/Durin's_Bane.md) up it to the peak, but during the [combat](../B/Battle_of_the_Peak.md) the tower fell and the entry to the stairs was destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
