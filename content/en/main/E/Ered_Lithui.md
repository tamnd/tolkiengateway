The **Ered Lithui**, or the **Ash Mountains**, were a mountain range on the northern border of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) which separated it from [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md). They ran about 500 miles from east to west. One south-western ridge divided the [Plateau of Gorgoroth](../P/Plateau_of_Gorgoroth.md) from [Lithlad](../L/Lithlad.md). A shorter southern spur within Gorgoroth ended at [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md). The west end of the Ash Mountains met the [Mountains of Shadow](Ephel_Dúath.md). Between the Mountains of Shadow and the Ash Mountains was [Udûn](<../U/Udûn_(valley).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Ered Lithui were rugged and forbidding, described as being composed of "broken peaks and barren ridges". Its rocks were coloured grey as ash.<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> King [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) was leading an army to [Dagorlad](../D/Dagorlad.md) to challenge the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md). It was expected that the enemy would assault them from the north or northeast, but the Wainriders came directly from the east, using the cover of the Ered Lithui to conceal their approach.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The Wainriders were greatly speeded by a long-neglected road that ran from the [Morannon](../M/Morannon.md) fifty miles to the east.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On [5 March](../_/5_March.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-6">[6]</a></sup> [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), and [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) looked upon the Ered Lithui before turning aside to journey to [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-2">[2]</a></sup> From [19 March](../_/19_March.md) through [22 March](../_/22_March.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-6">[6]</a></sup> Frodo and Sam crawled slowly east on the road from [Cirith Gorgor](../C/Cirith_Gorgor.md) to Barad-dûr that hugged the slopes of the Ered Lithui.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Name and etymology {#Name_and_etymology}

*Ered Lithui* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. It means "ashen mountains". It is a compound of *[ered](Ered.md)*, the plural of *[orod](../O/Orod.md)* ("mountain") and *[lith](../L/Lith.md)* with the adjectival suffix *ui*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The name was likely taken from their ash-grey hue.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-9">[9]</a></sup>
