**Emeldir the Manhearted** (born [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [406](/wiki/First_Age_406 "First Age 406")) was a woman of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) in [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), the wife of [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir) and the mother of [Beren Erchamion](/wiki/Beren "Beren") and [Hiril](/wiki/Hiril).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Like her husband, Emeldir was of the House of Bëor through the line of [Belen](/wiki/Belen), the youngest son of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor) himself.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), when Dorthonion was ruined and infested, Emeldir's husband Barahir and her son Beren became outlaws fighting the evil creatures. Emeldir would have preferred to stay with her family to fight, but as the women and children of the [First House of the Edain](/wiki/First_House_of_the_Edain) had to flee from their homes, Emeldir finally led them, and after many hardships they reached [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil). Because of this bravery she was known as *Emeldir the Man-hearted*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) has suggested that *Emeldir* possibly consists of [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *emel* ("mother") + *dir* ("man"), yielding a hypothetical translation "manly-mother" or "mother with manly courage" (close in meaning to her epithet the Manhearted).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
