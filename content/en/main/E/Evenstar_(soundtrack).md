**Evenstar** is the eighth track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack.md)* by [Howard Shore](../H/Howard_Shore.md). Named after [Arwen Evenstar](../A/Arwen.md), it's used as Arwen and [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md)'s theme in *[The Lord of the Rings film series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)*.<sup id="cite_ref-AnnotatedScore_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AnnotatedScore-1">[1]</a></sup>

The text was written by [Fran Walsh](../F/Fran_Walsh.md) and [Philippa Boyens](../P/Philippa_Boyens.md), with [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) translation by [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md). Solo vocals are performed by Isabel Bayrakdarian.<sup id="cite_ref-AnnotatedScore_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AnnotatedScore-1">[1]</a></sup>

Evenstar is also featured as a part of [One of the Dunedain](../O/One_of_the_Dunedain.md), the seventh track of disc two on [The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers - The Complete Recordings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_-_The_Complete_Recordings.md).
