**Eluréd** and **Elurín** were the sons, and eldest children of [Dior Eluchíl](/wiki/Dior "Dior") and [Nimloth](/wiki/Nimloth_of_Doriath "Nimloth of Doriath").<sup id="cite_ref-index_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Eluréd and Elurín were [twins](/wiki/Twins "Twins") and had a younger sister [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing), and all three had been born in [Lanthir Lamath](/wiki/Lanthir_Lamath) while their grandparents [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) lived there. However, after the host of the [Naugrim](/wiki/Naugrim) had plundered [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth), their father Dior brought his entire family to the Thousand Caves to renew the kingdom of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).

But soon, after not many years had passed, a messenger brought the [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr) to Dior, which told him that Beren and Lúthien were gone from the world. The news that the Necklace of the Dwarves, which bore one of the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils), was in Dior's keeping provoked the sons of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) to attack Menegroth.

In the ensuing conflict, both Dior and Nimloth were slain and Eluréd and Elurín were captured, while Elwing escaped with the Silmaril. The two young boys were then taken into the woods by the servants of [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm), in retribution for their lord's death, and left to starve.

[Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros), the eldest son of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), however repented and sought for the two but never found them and their fate remained unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was afterwards said that the brothers were lost and died,<sup id="cite_ref-beor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-beor-3">[3]</a></sup> however some accounts say that they were saved by wild birds, and eventually found their way in secret back to [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name of **Eluréd** is said to mean the same as the boy's father's name, [Eluchíl](/wiki/Eluch%C3%ADl), that is, "Heir of Elu".<sup id="cite_ref-index_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-1">[1]</a></sup> Therefore it contains the name [Elu](/wiki/Elu) and the [Bëorian](/wiki/Taliska "Taliska") word *rêda* "heir".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The name **Elurín** in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) means "Remembrance of Elu".<sup id="cite_ref-index_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Eluréd {#Eluréd}

In an early stage, Eluréd was called "<a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Elbereth" title="Elbereth">Elbereth</a>", an [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin) name containing the word *bereth* "valor". During the writing of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, the name was applied to [Varda](/wiki/Varda), and the original meaning<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup> was struck out and the character was renamed "Eldún".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

In the genealogical table of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor), "Eldún"'s date of birth was given as [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") 492 (and he was not a twin). He and his brother did die in [506](/wiki/First_Age_506 "First Age 506") but their father died in [511](/wiki/First_Age_511 "First Age 511"). In commentary following the table, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) explained the year of 511 for Dior's death was incorrect and that in later sources the two brothers were twins born in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [500](/wiki/First_Age_500 "First Age 500").<sup id="cite_ref-beor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-beor-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Elurín {#Elurín}

In an early stage, Eluréd was called "[Elboron](/wiki/Elboron)", and his brother "<a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Elbereth" title="Elbereth">Elbereth</a>", both [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin) names. During the writing of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, the name *Elbereth* was applied to [Varda](/wiki/Varda), and the brothers were renamed as "Eldún" and "Elrún".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[note 2]</a></sup>

According to *[The War of the Jewels](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels)*, the brothers were saved by wild birds after being abandoned in the woods of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), and eventually found their way in secret back to [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand),<sup id="cite_ref-GA_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the genealogical table of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor), "Elrún"'s date of birth was given as [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495") (and he was not a twin). He and his brother did die in [506](/wiki/First_Age_506 "First Age 506") but their father died in [511](/wiki/First_Age_511 "First Age 511"). In commentary following the table, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) explained the year of 511 for Dior's death was incorrect and that in later sources the two brothers were twins born in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [500](/wiki/First_Age_500 "First Age 500").<sup id="cite_ref-beor_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-beor-3">[3]</a></sup>
