**Erech** was a plain<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) situated at the mouth of the [Blackroot Vale](../B/Blackroot_Vale.md) near to the southern entrance to the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md), far to the west of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>. Upon it stood the [Hill of Erech](../H/Hill_of_Erech.md) with a smooth black globe, the [Stone of Erech](../S/Stone_of_Erech.md) on the top of the hill.<sup id="cite_ref-CompanyP789_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CompanyP789-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Near the end of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) set a great [black stone](../S/Stone_of_Erech.md) upon the [Hill of Erech](../H/Hill_of_Erech.md) and made the local hill tribes swear an oath of loyalty over the stone. It was there that the [King of the Mountains](../K/King_of_the_Dead.md) swore allegiance to Isildur's cause at the time of the [Last Alliance](../L/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men.md). However, when he failed to fulfil his oath, he and his [people](../O/Oathbreakers.md) were cursed to wander and haunt the plain of Erech as the Dead until they made good on their promise.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The area near the Hill of Erech became a mysterious and eerie place, shunned by the people of the valley, who claimed that it was haunted by restless spirits.<sup id="cite_ref-CompanyP789_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CompanyP789-4">[4]</a></sup> During the time of King [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md) of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md), a prophecy was made regarding the arising of the dead during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). In this prophecy the Stone of Erech was mentioned:<sup id="cite_ref-CompanyP781_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CompanyP781-6">[6]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The Dead awaken; for the hour is come for the oathbreakers: at the Stone of Erech they shall stand again and hear there a horn in the hills ringing.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../M/Malbeth.md" title="Malbeth">Malbeth</a> the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../S/Seer.md" title="Seer">Seer</a></cite></p>
</blockquote>

When the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md) came to [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), the sons of [Elrond](Elrond.md) brought with them a reminder of these words from their father to [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), who acted upon these words to brave the Paths of the Dead.<sup id="cite_ref-CompanyP781_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CompanyP781-6">[6]</a></sup>

After waiting through the long years of the Third Age, at midnight on [8 March](../_/8_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> the heir of Isildur, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), came to Erech to hold the Dead to their oath again, promising that upon its fulfillment they would have peace.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [The next day](../_/9_March.md) they left Erech for [Calembel](../C/Calembel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Indeed, on [13 March](../_/13_March.md),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> they held true to their oath and formed the Host of the Dead to help Aragorn defeat the [Corsairs of Umbar](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md) at [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md). After the battle at Pelargir, Aragorn released them from their oath and they were allowed to rest.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The origin of the name *Erech*, like that of some other names in Gondor, was forgotten and from a [time before](../P/Pre-Númenórean.md) the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) sailed to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
