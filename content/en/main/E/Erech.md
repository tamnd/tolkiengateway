**Erech** was a plain<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) situated at the mouth of the [Blackroot Vale](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale) near to the southern entrance to the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead), far to the west of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>. Upon it stood the [Hill of Erech](/wiki/Hill_of_Erech) with a smooth black globe, the [Stone of Erech](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech) on the top of the hill.<sup id="cite_ref-CompanyP789_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CompanyP789-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Near the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) set a great [black stone](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech "Stone of Erech") upon the [Hill of Erech](/wiki/Hill_of_Erech) and made the local hill tribes swear an oath of loyalty over the stone. It was there that the [King of the Mountains](/wiki/King_of_the_Dead "King of the Dead") swore allegiance to Isildur's cause at the time of the [Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men "Last Alliance of Elves and Men"). However, when he failed to fulfil his oath, he and his [people](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") were cursed to wander and haunt the plain of Erech as the Dead until they made good on their promise.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The area near the Hill of Erech became a mysterious and eerie place, shunned by the people of the valley, who claimed that it was haunted by restless spirits.<sup id="cite_ref-CompanyP789_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CompanyP789-4">[4]</a></sup> During the time of King [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), a prophecy was made regarding the arising of the dead during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). In this prophecy the Stone of Erech was mentioned:<sup id="cite_ref-CompanyP781_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CompanyP781-6">[6]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The Dead awaken; for the hour is come for the oathbreakers: at the Stone of Erech they shall stand again and hear there a horn in the hills ringing.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/Malbeth" title="Malbeth">Malbeth</a> the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Seer" title="Seer">Seer</a></cite></p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

When the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) came to [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), the sons of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) brought with them a reminder of these words from their father to [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), who acted upon these words to brave the Paths of the Dead.<sup id="cite_ref-CompanyP781_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CompanyP781-6">[6]</a></sup>

After waiting through the long years of the Third Age, at midnight on [8 March](/wiki/8_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> the heir of Isildur, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), came to Erech to hold the Dead to their oath again, promising that upon its fulfillment they would have peace.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [The next day](/wiki/9_March "9 March") they left Erech for [Calembel](/wiki/Calembel).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Indeed, on [13 March](/wiki/13_March),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> they held true to their oath and formed the Host of the Dead to help Aragorn defeat the [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar) at [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir). After the battle at Pelargir, Aragorn released them from their oath and they were allowed to rest.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The origin of the name *Erech*, like that of some other names in Gondor, was forgotten and from a [time before](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean "Pre-Númenórean") the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) sailed to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
