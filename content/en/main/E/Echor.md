<i><b>echor</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) noun which means "encircling"<sup id="cite_ref-silmindex_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmindex-1">[1]</a></sup> or "outer ring".<sup id="cite_ref-lotrindex_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lotrindex-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Echoriath](/wiki/Echoriath)*, "The Encircling Mountains"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- *[Orfalch Echor](/wiki/Orfalch_Echor)*, "great ravine through the Encircling Mountains"<sup id="cite_ref-silmindex_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmindex-1">[1]</a></sup>
- *[Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor)*, "great wall of the outer ring";<sup id="cite_ref-lotrindex_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lotrindex-2">[2]</a></sup> the wall which encircled and enclosed the [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
