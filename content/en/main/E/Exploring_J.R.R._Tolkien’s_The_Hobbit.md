<i><b>Exploring J.R.R. Tolkien’s The Hobbit</b></i> is chapter-by-chapter companion to *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* by [Corey Olsen](/wiki/Corey_Olsen).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

*The Hobbit* has been a childhood classic for decades. Millions have read and loved it before they reached high school; many carry cherished memories of a parent reading the book aloud to them. For some readers, it opens the door to a world of fantasy that will enrich the rest of their lives. The Hobbit is one of the most widely read and best-loved books of the twentieth century.

Exploring J.R.R. Tolkien’s *The Hobbit* is a fun, thoughtful, and an insightful companion volume, designed to bring a thorough and original new reading of this great work to a general audience. Professor Corey Olsen (also known as the Tolkien Professor) will take readers on an in-depth journey through The Hobbit chapter by chapter, revealing the stories within the story: the dark desires of dwarves and the sublime laughter of [elves](/wiki/Elves "Elves"), the nature of evil and its hopelessness, the mystery of divine providence and human choice, and, most of all, the revolutions within the life of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins). Exploring J.R.R. Tolkien’s *The Hobbit* is a book that will make *The Hobbit* come alive for readers as it never has before.
