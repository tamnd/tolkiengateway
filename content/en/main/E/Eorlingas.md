**Eorlingas** or **Eorlings**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a name the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) sometimes used for themselves, as followers and descendants of their first [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan"), [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>.

The word was used in a call to arms chanted by King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) when [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) had healed him:<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">[4]</a></sup>

It was also used by the armed men standing nearby after the healing,<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">[4]</a></sup> by [Háma](/wiki/H%C3%A1ma) when he suggested [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) as the regent of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) during the absence of King Théoden<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>, by Théoden and his riders,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and by Théoden to rally the Rohirrim during the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>and the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eorlingas* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name, which means "People of Eorl". It contains the elements *[eorl](/wiki/Eorl#Etymology "Eorl")* and *[-ingas](/wiki/-ings "-ings")* ("people").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

The word is an Old English translation of the [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) <i><b>þorunahim</b></i> (sg. <i><b>þuron</b></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
