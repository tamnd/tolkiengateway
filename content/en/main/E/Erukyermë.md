The <i><b>Erukyermë</b></i> was one of the [Three Prayers](/wiki/Three_Prayers) held on the summit of the [Meneltarma](/wiki/Meneltarma) in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) (the others being the *[Erulaitalë](/wiki/Erulaital%C3%AB)* and the *[Eruhantalë](/wiki/Eruhantal%C3%AB)*). The *Erukyermë* was held at the beginning of each spring. The ruling [King](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") or [Queen](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Ruling Queen of Númenor"), followed by a silent crowd, would climb the mountain on foot, and at its peak utter a prayer for the approaching year. Traditionally, three [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles), known by the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) as the [Witnesses of Manwë](/wiki/Witnesses_of_Manw%C3%AB), would hover over the King and his people at this time.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was just after the *Erukyermë* of [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [883](/wiki/Second_Age_883 "Second Age 883") that [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur), the sixth King of Númenor, surrendered the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre) to his son [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion").<sup id="cite_ref-Ald_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ald-2">[2]</a></sup>

Since Erukyermë marked the beginning of a new year, this points to the existence of a separate calendar for religious purposes, beginning at vernal equinox. For civil matters the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) started the year in the middle of winter, just after *[Yule](/wiki/Yule)*, a tradition attested as far back as the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Erukyermë* means "Prayer to Eru" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (cf. *[kyermë](/wiki/Kyerm%C3%AB "Kyermë")*).<sup id="cite_ref-Ald_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ald-2">[2]</a></sup>

A misreading of *hy* has been suggested, but the text is a typescript.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
