**Elendil** was the father of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion), a survivor of the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor), and the founder and first [High King](</wiki/High_King_(D%C3%BAnedain)> "High King (Dúnedain)") of the [Realms in Exile](/wiki/Realms_in_Exile).

Elendil was also known as **Elendil the Tall**, as he was the tallest of the Men who escaped the Downfall. Accounts differ as to his exact height, but he was at least seven feet tall.<sup id="cite_ref-linear_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-linear-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-tallnote_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tallnote-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Elendil was born in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). He was the son of [Amandil](/wiki/Amandil), the last [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië") and leader of the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful). He was probably named after his ancestor, [Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil), an ancient [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor); his name also signified his and his family's devotion and friendship to the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) as [Elf-friends](/wiki/Elf-friend "Elf-friend"), preserving the old beliefs in [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) and reverence for the [Valar](/wiki/Valar). Like his father, he stood against the barbarous practices of [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n) the King and [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) his advisor.

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Abe_Papakhian_-_Elendil_and_Sons.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/2/22/Abe_Papakhian_-_Elendil_and_Sons.jpg/300px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Elendil_and_Sons.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="364" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/2/22/Abe_Papakhian_-_Elendil_and_Sons.jpg/450px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Elendil_and_Sons.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/2/22/Abe_Papakhian_-_Elendil_and_Sons.jpg/600px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Elendil_and_Sons.jpg 2x" data-file-width="659" data-file-height="800"/></a><figcaption>Abe Papakhian - <i>Elendil and Sons</i></figcaption></figure>

On the advice of his father, who foresaw the coming destruction of Númenor, Elendil put nine ships off the eastern coast and loaded all his people and possessions aboard them. Amandil then followed the actions of their ancestor, [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) and left Númenor attempting to warn the Valar of their King's folly.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-4">[4]</a></sup>

When the catastrophic [Downfall](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Downfall of Númenor") occurred in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3319](/wiki/Second_Age_3319 "Second Age 3319"), Elendil, his sons [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion), and their supporters fled to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth),<sup id="cite_ref-SA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-5">[5]</a></sup> sailing east in the nine ships. With them they took the *[palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")*, the "Seeing Stones" that were given to the Lords of Andúnië by the Elves of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), the [Sceptre of Annúminas](/wiki/Sceptre_of_Ann%C3%BAminas), the [Ring of Barahir](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir), [Narsil](/wiki/Narsil) which Elendil kept as his sword, and a seedling of [Nimloth](/wiki/White_Tree_of_N%C3%BAmenor "White Tree of Númenor"), the White Tree of Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-4">[4]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Ships_of_the_Faithful.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/0/0d/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Ships_of_the_Faithful.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Ships_of_the_Faithful.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="440" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/0/0d/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Ships_of_the_Faithful.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Ships_of_the_Faithful.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/0/0d/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Ships_of_the_Faithful.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Ships_of_the_Faithful.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1090" data-file-height="1600"/></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a> - <i>The Ships of the Faithful</i></figcaption></figure>

The ships became separated during the tumultuous voyage to Middle-earth; while Isildur and Anárion landed in the more southerly lands near the haven of the Faithful at [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir), Elendil and his people arrived at the northwest near [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon).<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-6">[6]</a></sup> After Elendil finally landed in Middle-earth, he proclaimed an [oath](/wiki/Elendil%27s_Oath "Elendil's Oath") in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): "*Et Eärello Endorenna utúlien. Sinome maruvan ar Hildinyar tenn' Ambar-metta*" ("Out of the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") to Middle-earth I am come. In this place will I abide, and my heirs, unto the ending of the world"). His heir and great-grandson by 39 generations [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") spoke these words again when he took up the crown of Gondor as King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") at the beginning of the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

The Faithful colonists of Middle-earth, and other [Middle Men](/wiki/Middle_Men), gathered around the Númenórean lords at the points they arrived; for this reason there were two realms of the Númenóreans in exile, [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) in the north where Elendil landed, and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in the south where his sons ended up.<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-6">[6]</a></sup> Elendil founded the city of [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas) in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), the capital of Arnor. His son Anárion established the city of [Minas Anor](/wiki/Minas_Anor) in [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien), and Isildur founded [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil) in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien). Elendil, however, ruled as [High King](</wiki/High_King_(D%C3%BAnedain)> "High King (Dúnedain)"). In both realms were built towers for the *palantíri*, with which the lords kept contact.<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-6">[6]</a></sup>

Gil-galad built for Elendil the [White Towers](/wiki/White_Towers) on some [hills](/wiki/Emyn_Beraid "Emyn Beraid") of Eriador. In the [tallest tower](/wiki/Elostirion "Elostirion") he kept a unique *palantír*, the [Stone of Elendil](/wiki/Stone_of_Elendil) that could not communicate with the other six, because it was aligned westwards towards the [Master-stone](/wiki/Master-stone) in the [Tower of Avallonë](/wiki/Tower_of_Avallon%C3%AB). Elendil was able to use it to look west across the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") and see the [Undying Lands](/wiki/Undying_Lands);<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-6">[6]</a></sup> but he could not see Númenor for it was covered by the bent seas for ever.<sup id="cite_ref-App_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-8">[8]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Sauron.27s_return_and_Last_Alliance"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Sauron&#39;s_return_and_Last_Alliance">Sauron&#39;s return and Last Alliance</span></h3>

[Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), having lost his shape in the Downfall, returned to Middle-earth as a shadow and a black wind over the sea. It came to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), next to Gondor, where Sauron wrought a new guise.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-4">[4]</a></sup> In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3429](/wiki/Second_Age_3429 "Second Age 3429") Sauron attacked, seizing Minas Ithil, causing Isildur to flee north to his father, leaving Anárion in charge of Gondor. In response, Elendil formed an [alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men "Last Alliance of Elves and Men") with [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), the [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor), to repel Sauron's assault.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-5">[5]</a></sup> It is said that Elendil bound the [Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance) with an oath and invoked the name of [Eru](/wiki/Eru) to witness it.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-9">[9]</a></sup>

The combined forces marched east to [Imladris](/wiki/Imladris) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3431](/wiki/Second_Age_3431 "Second Age 3431"). In [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434"), they passed the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) and south together and fought in the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) of Elves and Men. Elendil fought valiantly at the [Battle of Dagorlad](/wiki/Battle_of_Dagorlad).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-5">[5]</a></sup>

At the end of the long [Siege of Barad-dûr](/wiki/Siege_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr), in the year [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3441](/wiki/Second_Age_3441 "Second Age 3441"), Sauron came out to personally do battle. Gil-galad and Elendil fought Sauron and cast him down, but were both slain,<sup id="cite_ref-SA_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-5">[5]</a></sup> and Elendil's sword [Narsil](/wiki/Narsil) was broken when he fell. Isildur used the broken sword to cut [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") from Sauron's hand. However, despite the urgings of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and [Cirdan](/wiki/Cirdan), Isildur chose not to destroy the Ring at [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom) and was eventually betrayed by the Ring in the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields), slain by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

Elendil was a great warrior, a wise king, and a heroic figure among the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain). Isildur built a hidden [tomb for his father](/wiki/Tomb_of_Elendil "Tomb of Elendil") on the summit of the beacon hill that was named [Amon Anwar](/wiki/Halifirien "Halifirien") ("hill of awe" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)). Its presence was considered great enough to hallow the entire hill, even to the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), who called it [Halifirien](/wiki/Halifirien) ("holy mountain" in [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese)).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-9">[9]</a></sup>

After the founding of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), the [Steward](/wiki/Steward) [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion) moved Elendil's remains to the [Hallows](/wiki/Hallows) of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-9">[9]</a></sup>

Millennia after Elendil's death, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), the [Heir of Isildur](/wiki/Heir_of_Isildur), invoked the name of Elendil as a battle cry during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the film&#39;s prologue, Elendil is played by <a href="/wiki/Peter_McKenzie" title="Peter McKenzie">Peter McKenzie</a>. Unlike in the books, Elendil is killed by a blow of <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> instead of having a part in destroying him with <a href="/wiki/Gil-galad" title="Gil-galad">Gil-galad</a>. Instead, <a href="/wiki/Isildur" title="Isildur">Isildur</a> deals the fatal blow to the still-living Sauron by cutting off his fingers with the broken blade of <a href="/wiki/Narsil" title="Narsil">Narsil</a>, and, with them, the <a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring" title="The One Ring">ring of power</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (video game)">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (video game)</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Elendil appears in the game&#39;s first level as a non-playable character, where he has the likeness of <a href="/wiki/Peter_Jackson" title="Peter Jackson">Peter Jackson</a>&#39;s <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)">movie counterpart</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Elendil appears in several flashbacks depicting the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance" title="War of the Last Alliance">War of the Last Alliance</a>. His ultimate fate is more accurate to the books than the film version, showing Elendil and <a href="/wiki/Gil-galad" title="Gil-galad">Gil-galad</a> both battling and defeating <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> at the cost of their lives, with Elendil dealing the killing blow.</dd></dl>

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a></i>, Season One:</b>

<dl><dd>Elendil is portrayed by <a href="/wiki/Lloyd_Owen" title="Lloyd Owen">Lloyd Owen</a> as a captain. He first appears in the <a href="/wiki/Adar_(episode)" title="Adar (episode)">third episode</a>, in a plotline created for the series in which he rescues <a href="/wiki/Galadriel" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a> and <a href="/wiki/Halbrand" title="Halbrand">Halbrand</a> from the sea and takes them to <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor" title="Númenor">Númenor</a>. Unlike in the source materials, his Númenórean nobility is uncertain, as he is unknown to <a href="/wiki/Tar-M%C3%ADriel" title="Tar-Míriel">Queen Míriel</a>.</dd></dl>
