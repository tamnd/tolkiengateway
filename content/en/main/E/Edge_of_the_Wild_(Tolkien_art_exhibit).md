**Edge of the Wild** was a free to the public [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) art exhibition sponsored by [ADC Art and Books](/wiki/ADC_Art_and_Books) featuring original artwork and limited edition prints from Tolkien artists [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith), [Ruth Lacon](/wiki/Ruth_Lacon), [Jef Murray](/wiki/Jef_Murray) and [Peter Pracownik](/wiki/Peter_Pracownik) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redesdale_Hall" title="wikipedia:Redesdale Hall">The Redesdale Hall</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moreton-in-Marsh" title="wikipedia:Moreton-in-Marsh">Moreton-in-Marsh</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloucestershire" title="wikipedia:Gloucestershire">Gloucestershire</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a> held from 13–16 August [2010](/wiki/2010)
