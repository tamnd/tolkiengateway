**Ecthelion** may refer to:

- **[Ecthelion](Ecthelion.md) (d. [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [510](../F/First_Age_510.md)), an [Elf](../N/Noldor.md) of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), Lord of the [House of the Fountain](../H/House_of_the_Fountain.md), and slayer of the [Balrog](../B/Balrogs.md) [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md>).**
- [Ecthelion I](Ecthelion_I.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2600](../T/Third_Age_2600.md) – [2698](../T/Third_Age_2698.md)), the seventeenth [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).
    - The [Tower of Ecthelion](../T/Tower_of_Ecthelion.md), the highest tower of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md)'s capital city, [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), which was rebuilt by [Ecthelion I](Ecthelion_I.md).
- [Ecthelion II](Ecthelion_II.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2886](../T/Third_Age_2886.md) – [2984](../T/Third_Age_2984.md)), the twenty-fifth [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and paternal grandfather of [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) and [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md).
