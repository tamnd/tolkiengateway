**Ecthelion** may refer to:

- **[Ecthelion](/wiki/Ecthelion) (d. [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [510](/wiki/First_Age_510 "First Age 510")), an [Elf](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), Lord of the [House of the Fountain](/wiki/House_of_the_Fountain), and slayer of the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)> "Gothmog (balrog)").**
- [Ecthelion I](/wiki/Ecthelion_I) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2600](/wiki/Third_Age_2600 "Third Age 2600") – [2698](/wiki/Third_Age_2698 "Third Age 2698")), the seventeenth [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).
    - The [Tower of Ecthelion](/wiki/Tower_of_Ecthelion), the highest tower of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)'s capital city, [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), which was rebuilt by [Ecthelion I](/wiki/Ecthelion_I).
- [Ecthelion II](/wiki/Ecthelion_II) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2886](/wiki/Third_Age_2886 "Third Age 2886") – [2984](/wiki/Third_Age_2984 "Third Age 2984")), the twenty-fifth [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and paternal grandfather of [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) and [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir).
