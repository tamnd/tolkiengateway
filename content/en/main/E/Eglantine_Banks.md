**Eglantine Took (née Banks)** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Eglantine was the wife of [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Paladin Took](/wiki/Paladin_Took_II "Paladin Took II"), and mother of [Pearl](/wiki/Pearl_Took "Pearl Took"), [Pimpernel](/wiki/Pimpernel_Took "Pimpernel Took"), [Pervinca](/wiki/Pervinca_Took "Pervinca Took") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"). She was present at [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' [Birthday Party](/wiki/Birthday_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

She was named after the flower *[eglantine](/wiki/Eglantine "Eglantine")*.
