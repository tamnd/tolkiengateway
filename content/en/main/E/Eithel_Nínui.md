The **Eithel Nínui**, also called the **Fountain of Tinúviel**, was a fountain found on the plain of [Tumladen](/wiki/Tumladen). It is said to have been created by the tears of [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) falling down on the plain when she and [Beren](/wiki/Beren) were flown over it by [Thorondor](/wiki/Thorondor) and his vassals. Its water was recounted in song as having had healing powers until it was destroyed in the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin).

## Note on occurrence {#Note_on_occurrence}

The fountain and its legend is found in one of [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien)'s drafts of the *[Quenta Silmarillion](</wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road)> "Quenta Silmarillion (Lost Road)")*, but in the published version found in *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) omitted it. He has later admitted that this section should have remained in the published work.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eithel Nínui* is [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), containing *[eithel](/wiki/Eithel "Eithel")* ("spring, well") and *[nínui](/wiki/N%C3%ADnui "Nínui")* ("tearful", as proposed by [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
