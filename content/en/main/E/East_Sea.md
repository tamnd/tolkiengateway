The **East Sea**, also called the **Eastern Sea**, was the sea between [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and the [Land of the Sun](/wiki/Land_of_the_Sun).<sup id="cite_ref-A4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-A4-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Fashion_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fashion-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:293</sup>

## History {#History}

Initially, the size and shape of the East Sea corresponded symmetrically with [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer), the Western Sea separating Middle-earth from [Aman](/wiki/Aman), but when Melkor cast down the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps), this symmetry was lost; Middle-earth was thrust eastward, causing Belegaer to expand and the East Sea to narrow.<sup id="cite_ref-Fashion_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fashion-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:292–3</sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup> At this time, the East Sea was narrowest in the extreme north and south of the world, where only small straits filled with ice separated Middle-earth and the Land of the Sun. These straits connected the East Sea to [Ekkaia](/wiki/Ekkaia), the Outer Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-A4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-A4-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the Battle of the Powers between the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor), the East Sea became connected to Belegaer. The inland [Sea of Ringil](/wiki/Sea_of_Ringil), originally set in the mid-south of Middle-earth, grew in size and "became a great sea flowing north-eastward and joining by straights both the Western and Eastern Seas."<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Fashion_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fashion-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:293–4</sup>

After Melkor defiled the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor), the Valar used the fruit of [Laurelin](/wiki/Laurelin) to create the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and the flower of [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion) to create the [Moon](/wiki/Moon).<sup id="cite_ref-S11_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S11-6">[4]</a></sup> In the evenings, the Sun set in the [Outer Sea](/wiki/Outer_Sea) to the west of Aman, where it was pulled down by [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s servants and sent under the earth<sup id="cite_ref-S11_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S11-6">[4]</a></sup> to the East Sea, where it rose at dawn.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup> The Moon followed the Sun on this path.<sup id="cite_ref-S11_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S11-6">[4]</a></sup>

In the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) sailed about Middle-earth to the [uttermost east](/wiki/Uttermost_east "Uttermost east")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup> and could see the [Gates of Morning](/wiki/Gates_of_Morning) from their ships,<sup id="cite_ref-S_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-9">[7]</a></sup> suggesting that they sailed into the East Sea. It was also in this age that a final change was made to the earth that likely affected the East Sea: when [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) sank Númenor and made the world round, he "cast back ... the [Empty Lands](/wiki/Empty_Lands) east of \[Middle-earth\], and new lands and seas were made".<sup id="cite_ref-S_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-9">[7]</a></sup> How this impacted the geography of the East Sea is unknown.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One)*, the Uttermost East ([Oronto](/wiki/Oronto)) was connected to the [Great Lands](/wiki/Middle-earth "Middle-earth"), directly east of [Palisor](/wiki/Palisor), with no separating water. [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) described the East to the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), saying that "*there is a silent beach and dark empty seas*". It is also told that [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) and [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) "*builded great havens* \[of the Sun and Moon\] *beside the soundless sea*".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[9]</a></sup>

In early texts, [Hildórien](/wiki/Hild%C3%B3rien) - the site where the first [Men](/wiki/Men) awoke in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) - was placed in the central region of Middle-earth near the shore of the East Sea. Hildórien was surrounded by the [Mountains of the Wind](/wiki/Mountains_of_the_Wind), a semi-circular mountain chain that derived its named from the strong winds that blew into them from over the East Sea due to the rising of the Sun.<sup id="cite_ref-A4_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-A4-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Fashion_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fashion-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:293</sup> In later texts, Hildórien is said to be "in the midmost parts of Middle-earth" with no mention of the East Sea or the Mountains of the Wind.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup>
