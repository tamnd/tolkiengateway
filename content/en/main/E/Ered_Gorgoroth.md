The **Ered Gorgoroth** or **Mountains of Terror**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> were a mountain chain in the north of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).

The Ered Gorgoroth was the southern edge of the highlands of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), and separated Dorthonion from Beleriand proper. South of the Gorgoroth lay the unpopulated land of [Nan Dungortheb](/wiki/Nan_Dungortheb) (the Valley of Dreadful Death).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup> The southern wall of the Ered Gorgoroth fell in sheer precipices.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

They were named such by the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) after the [spider](/wiki/Spiders "Spiders")-creature [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) made it her temporary home, and spawned many evil spiders there, which wove webs that took away all light, and all the springs were poisoned.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Gorgoroth had no known passes, and even the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) passed it by. In all of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) [Beren](/wiki/Beren) son of [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir) is said to have passed the mountain range, and he never spoke of this terrible journey,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> though it is said that he fought giant spiders there,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and Beleg was said to cross through the pass of [Anach](/wiki/Anach) at the western end of Ered Gorgoroth in his search for [Turin Turambar](/wiki/Turin_Turambar).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> It is implied that Shelob may have fled from Ered Gorgoroth as Tolkien states, "There agelong she had dwelt, an evil thing in spider-form, even such as once of old had lived in the Land of the Elves in the West that is now under the Sea, such as Beren fought in the Mountains of Terror in Doriath, and so came to Lúthien upon the green sward amid the hemlocks in the moonlight long ago. How Shelob came there, flying from ruin, no tale tells, for out of the Dark Years few tales have come."<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Like the rest of Beleriand, the Gorgoroth were drowned during the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), although the highland of Dorthonion survived as the isle of [Tol Fuin](/wiki/Tol_Fuin).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[ered](/wiki/Ered "Ered")* + *[gorgor](/wiki/Gorgor "Gorgor")* + *oth*

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The Ered Gorgoroth are in one place in the revisions to *[The Lay of Leithian](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Leithian)*, referred to as "Gorgorath" apparently in order to rhyme with "path".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> The variation is also included in the *Beren and Lúthien* glossary,<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> but the reason for the different spelling is not made clear.

In a note to a map in *[The War of the Jewels](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels)*, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) noted that the struck-through name **Goroth\[\]ess** in square D9 contains an illegible letter that he speculated might be an r.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> The name appears near the label **Eryd Orgorath**, which was replaced by the name Ered Gorgoroth.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>
