**Elfstan Fairbairn** was the son of [Fastred of Greenholm](/wiki/Fastred_of_Greenholm) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)'s eldest daughter [Elanor](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner "Elanor Gardner"), born in the year [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [33](/wiki/Fourth_Age_33 "Fourth Age 33") (1454 by the [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He succeeded his father as [Warden of Westmarch](/wiki/Warden_of_Westmarch).<sup id="cite_ref-guide_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

His name means "[Elfstone](/wiki/Elfstone)", and probably he was named so in honour of King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn").<sup id="cite_ref-guide_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
