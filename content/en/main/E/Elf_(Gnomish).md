<i><b>elf</b></i> is a [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) word meaning "five".<sup id="cite_ref-PE11_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE11-1">[1]</a></sup> While appearing without any designated language in the source, [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) has drawn the conclusion that the word is Gnomish.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>
