**Elendil's Stone**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the **Elendil Stone**,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> or the **Stone of Elendil**,<sup id="cite_ref-Varda_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Varda-3">[3]</a></sup> was one of the *[palantíri](../P/Palantíri.md)* brought by the [Faithful](../F/Faithful.md) from [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-4">[4]</a></sup>

The stone was aligned westwards towards the [Master-stone](../M/Master-stone.md) in the [Tower of Avallonë](../T/Tower_of_Avallonë.md), along the length of the [Straight Road](../S/Straight_Road.md); because of this, it could not communicate with the other six of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Elendil was able to use it to look west across the [Sea](../B/Belegaer.md) and see the [Undying Lands](../U/Undying_Lands.md);<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-4">[4]</a></sup> but he could not see Númenor for it was covered by the bent seas for ever.<sup id="cite_ref-App_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-5">[5]</a></sup>

It was stored in the tower of [Elostirion](Elostirion.md) in the [Emyn Beraid](Emyn_Beraid.md), and after [Elendil](Elendil.md)'s death the [High Elves](../C/Calaquendi.md) took it under their care and no [Man](../M/Men.md) used it again.<sup id="cite_ref-Varda_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Varda-3">[3]</a></sup>

Even after the fall of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md), [Wandering Companies](../W/Wandering_Companies.md) of High Elves made pilgrimages to visit the Towers and see the Stone, in order to look at [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) and the Shores of Valinor and sometimes to be rewarded by a vision of [Varda](../V/Varda.md) standing on the mountain [Oiolossë](../O/Oiolossë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Varda_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Varda-3">[3]</a></sup> One such company, led by [Gildor Inglorion](../G/Gildor.md), just returning from Elostirion,<sup id="cite_ref-Varda_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Varda-3">[3]</a></sup> was encountered by [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) on [September 24](../_/24_September.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

It remained in its tower until the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md); it was then removed by [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md), placed on the [White Ship](../W/White_Ship.md) and taken back into the West.<sup id="cite_ref-App_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-5">[5]</a></sup>
