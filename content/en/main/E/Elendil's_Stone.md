**Elendil's Stone**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the **Elendil Stone**,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> or the **Stone of Elendil**,<sup id="cite_ref-Varda_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Varda-3">[3]</a></sup> was one of the *[palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* brought by the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) from [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-4">[4]</a></sup>

The stone was aligned westwards towards the [Master-stone](/wiki/Master-stone) in the [Tower of Avallonë](/wiki/Tower_of_Avallon%C3%AB), along the length of the [Straight Road](/wiki/Straight_Road); because of this, it could not communicate with the other six of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Elendil was able to use it to look west across the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") and see the [Undying Lands](/wiki/Undying_Lands);<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-4">[4]</a></sup> but he could not see Númenor for it was covered by the bent seas for ever.<sup id="cite_ref-App_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-5">[5]</a></sup>

It was stored in the tower of [Elostirion](/wiki/Elostirion) in the [Emyn Beraid](/wiki/Emyn_Beraid), and after [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil)'s death the [High Elves](/wiki/Calaquendi "Calaquendi") took it under their care and no [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") used it again.<sup id="cite_ref-Varda_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Varda-3">[3]</a></sup>

Even after the fall of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), [Wandering Companies](/wiki/Wandering_Companies) of High Elves made pilgrimages to visit the Towers and see the Stone, in order to look at [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) and the Shores of Valinor and sometimes to be rewarded by a vision of [Varda](/wiki/Varda) standing on the mountain [Oiolossë](/wiki/Oioloss%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-Varda_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Varda-3">[3]</a></sup> One such company, led by [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor "Gildor"), just returning from Elostirion,<sup id="cite_ref-Varda_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Varda-3">[3]</a></sup> was encountered by [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") on [September 24](/wiki/24_September "24 September"), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

It remained in its tower until the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age); it was then removed by [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan), placed on the [White Ship](/wiki/White_Ship) and taken back into the West.<sup id="cite_ref-App_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-5">[5]</a></sup>
