**Erien** was the daughter of [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) according to a very late, but ultimately rejected version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").<sup id="cite_ref-Shib_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shib-1">[1]</a></sup>

She had one other sibling, a brother called [Finbor](/wiki/Finbor).<sup id="cite_ref-Shib_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shib-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Erien* is in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), but it has no clear meaning. A possible explanation of her name would be a combination of the Sindarin adjective *[er](/wiki/Er "Er")* ("one, alone") + the feminine suffix *[-ien](/wiki/-ien)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

An earlier, rejected form of her name was *Ernis*.<sup id="cite_ref-Shib_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shib-1">[1]</a></sup>
