**Enderi** (Singular *enderë)* were the 'middle-days' common to the [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") and the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) calendars, and were counted outside the months.

In the [Reckoning of Rivendell](/wiki/Reckoning_of_Rivendell), three *enderi* were set between the seasons of [Yávië](/wiki/Y%C3%A1vi%C3%AB) and [Quellë](/wiki/Quell%C3%AB). Every twelfth year (except in every 432nd year), these *enderi* were doubled to six.<sup id="cite_ref-D_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D-1">[1]</a></sup> This calendar system led to the [yén](/wiki/Y%C3%A9n "Yén") (144 of our years) having more than a day extra. To fix this the doubling of the *Enderi* was skipped, adding 3 days rather than six after 3 yén.<sup id="cite_ref-D_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [Kings' Reckoning](/wiki/Kings%27_Reckoning) and [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning), there was only one *enderë*; the day of the [middle of the year](/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day "Mid-year's Day") between the *[astar](/wiki/Astar "Astar")* of [Nárië](/wiki/N%C3%A1ri%C3%AB) and [Cermië](/wiki/Cermi%C3%AB) was called *[loëndë](/wiki/Lo%C3%ABnd%C3%AB "Loëndë")*. In leap years (divisible by 4 but not 100) it was replaced by two *enderi*;<sup id="cite_ref-D_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D-1">[1]</a></sup> in years divisible by 1000 loëndë was replaced by three *enderi*<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>.

In the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning) each year contained three *enderi*, between [Yavannie](/wiki/Yavannie) and [Narquelie](/wiki/Narquelie); the middle of the three was referred to as *loëndë*.<sup id="cite_ref-D_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D-1">[1]</a></sup>
