**Enderi** (Singular *enderë)* were the 'middle-days' common to the [Elven](Elves.md) and the [Númenórean](../N/Númenórean.md) calendars, and were counted outside the months.

In the [Reckoning of Rivendell](../R/Reckoning_of_Rivendell.md), three *enderi* were set between the seasons of [Yávië](../Y/Yávië.md) and [Quellë](../Q/Quellë.md). Every twelfth year (except in every 432nd year), these *enderi* were doubled to six.<sup id="cite_ref-D_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D-1">[1]</a></sup> This calendar system led to the [yén](../Y/Yén.md) (144 of our years) having more than a day extra. To fix this the doubling of the *Enderi* was skipped, adding 3 days rather than six after 3 yén.<sup id="cite_ref-D_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [Kings' Reckoning](../K/Kings'_Reckoning.md) and [Stewards' Reckoning](../S/Stewards'_Reckoning.md), there was only one *enderë*; the day of the [middle of the year](../M/Mid-year's_Day.md) between the *[astar](../A/Astar.md)* of [Nárië](../N/Nárië.md) and [Cermië](../C/Cermië.md) was called *[loëndë](../L/Loëndë.md)*. In leap years (divisible by 4 but not 100) it was replaced by two *enderi*;<sup id="cite_ref-D_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D-1">[1]</a></sup> in years divisible by 1000 loëndë was replaced by three *enderi*<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>.

In the [New Reckoning](../N/New_Reckoning.md) each year contained three *enderi*, between [Yavannie](../Y/Yavannie.md) and [Narquelie](../N/Narquelie.md); the middle of the three was referred to as *loëndë*.<sup id="cite_ref-D_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D-1">[1]</a></sup>
