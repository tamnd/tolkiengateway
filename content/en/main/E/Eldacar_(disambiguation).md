**Eldacar** (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈeldakar]</span>) is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "[elf-helm](Elfhelm.md)" and may refer to:

- [Eldacar](<Eldacar_(King_of_Arnor).md>) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [87](../T/Third_Age_87.md) - [339](../T/Third_Age_339.md)), fourth [King](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md) of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and grandson of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md).
- [Eldacar](<Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor).md>) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1255](../T/Third_Age_1255.md) - [1490](../T/Third_Age_1490.md)), 21st [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) who had to fight for his throne in the [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md).
- "Eldakar" was an early name of both [Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn.md), and his father [Arathorn II](../A/Arathorn_II.md), in [earlier versions of the legendarium](../T/The_Treason_of_Isengard.md).
- Eldacar was a name used on a [Decipher Card](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md) for one of the Beacon-guards, played by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Christian_Rivers&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Christian Rivers (page does not exist)">Christian Rivers</a>.
