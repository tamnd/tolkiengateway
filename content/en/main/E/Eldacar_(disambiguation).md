**Eldacar** (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈeldakar]</span>) is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "[elf-helm](/wiki/Elfhelm "Elfhelm")" and may refer to:

- [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Arnor)> "Eldacar (King of Arnor)") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [87](/wiki/Third_Age_87 "Third Age 87") - [339](/wiki/Third_Age_339 "Third Age 339")), fourth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor "Kings of Arnor") of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and grandson of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur).
- [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Eldacar (King of Gondor)") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1255](/wiki/Third_Age_1255 "Third Age 1255") - [1490](/wiki/Third_Age_1490 "Third Age 1490")), 21st [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) who had to fight for his throne in the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife).
- "Eldakar" was an early name of both [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"), and his father [Arathorn II](/wiki/Arathorn_II), in [earlier versions of the legendarium](/wiki/The_Treason_of_Isengard "The Treason of Isengard").
- Eldacar was a name used on a [Decipher Card](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game "The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game") for one of the Beacon-guards, played by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Christian_Rivers&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Christian Rivers (page does not exist)">Christian Rivers</a>.
