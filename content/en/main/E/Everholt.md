**Everholt** was the name the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) gave to a section of the [Firien Wood](../F/Firienholt.md). It was notorious for its [boar](../B/Boar_of_Everholt.md), after which the forest was named. In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2864](../T/Third_Age_2864.md), King [Folca](../F/Folca.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) killed the boar and was killed by it.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "boar-wood". The first element comes from [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *eofor*, "boar". The second element appears also in [Dimholt](../D/Dimholt.md), "wood".

[Eofor](Eofor.md) is also a [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md) name.
