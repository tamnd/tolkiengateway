**Everholt** was the name the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) gave to a section of the [Firien Wood](/wiki/Firienholt "Firienholt"). It was notorious for its [boar](/wiki/Boar_of_Everholt "Boar of Everholt"), after which the forest was named. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2864](/wiki/Third_Age_2864 "Third Age 2864"), King [Folca](/wiki/Folca) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) killed the boar and was killed by it.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "boar-wood". The first element comes from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *eofor*, "boar". The second element appears also in [Dimholt](/wiki/Dimholt), "wood".

[Eofor](/wiki/Eofor) is also a [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) name.
