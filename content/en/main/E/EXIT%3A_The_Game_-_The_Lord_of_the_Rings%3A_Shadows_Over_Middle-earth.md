**EXIT: The Game – The Lord of the Rings: Shadows Over Middle-earth** is a cooperative escape room-style board game published by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=KOSMOS&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KOSMOS (page does not exist)">KOSMOS</a> in 2022. Designed by Inka and Markus Brand, the game adapts the award-winning EXIT: The Game formula to J.R.R. Tolkien's fantasy universe, placing players in the role of [hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) carrying out secret missions for the wizard [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) to aid [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md)'s quest to destroy the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md).

## Gameplay {#Gameplay}

The game uses the classic EXIT system, where players solve interconnected puzzles using a combination of cards, a riddle booklet, and a decoder disk. Each puzzle solution produces a three-symbol code that players enter into the decoder wheel to reveal the corresponding answer card number. If correct, players receive new materials and advance the story; if incorrect, they must continue working on the puzzle.

## Components {#Components}

- 1 Decoder Disk
- 90 Cards (including 27 riddle cards, 30 help cards, and 30 answer cards)
- 27 Strange Items (punch-out cardboard pieces)
- 1 Riddle Booklet
- 1 Rulebook
