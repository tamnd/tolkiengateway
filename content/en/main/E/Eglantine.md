**Eglantine** is a type of wild [rose](/wiki/Roses "Roses"). [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") found it growing in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) as they journeyed through that land.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Eglantine Banks](/wiki/Eglantine_Banks), [Pippin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took")'s mother, was named after it,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> so it probably grew in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") as well.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is used for the sweetbriar. It derives from French referring to the "dog rose", ultimately from [Latin](/wiki/Latin) *aculeus* "spine".
