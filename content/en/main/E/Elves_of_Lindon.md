The **Elves of Lindon** were those [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who dwelt in [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) in the west of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), between the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) and the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer").

## History {#History}

### Rule of Gil-galad {#Rule_of_Gil-galad}

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1](/wiki/Second_Age_1 "Second Age 1"),<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) built the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) in the newly-formed [Gulf of Lune](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune) where the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) sailed the [Straight Road](/wiki/Straight_Road), but most of them were unwilling at first to forsake the lands they fought in and preferred to linger there.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup> This was the foundation of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) and its people.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> Among those that remained were [Círdan the Shipwright](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan_the_Shipwright), [Celeborn of Doriath](/wiki/Celeborn "Celeborn"), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), and [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor).<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-3">[3]</a></sup> [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) was the last heir of the [kings of the Noldor](/wiki/King_of_the_Noldor "King of the Noldor") in exile, and acknowledged as [High King of the Elves of the West](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor "High King of the Noldor") and so, he was their king.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the beginning of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), most of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) that remained in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) dwelt in [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) but,<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> many [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri), survivors of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) and [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand), left [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) before [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1000](/wiki/Second_Age_1000 "Second Age 1000") to establish realms among the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-princes_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-princes-4">[4]</a></sup> [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) brought many [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar), and [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves)<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-5">[5]</a></sup> out of Lindon into [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) to dwell there for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-5">[5]</a></sup> Many of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) in Lindon lead by [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor) established the realm of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750") near the [West-gate](/wiki/West-gate) of [Khazad-Dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") due to the recent discovery of [mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril") in [Khazad-Dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria").<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup>

A fair being calling himself [Annatar](/wiki/Annatar), an emissary of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), came seeking entrance. Gil-galad and Master [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) sensed that he was not what he seemed, and denied him. Messengers were sent from [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) with warnings; but they were unheeded, and Annatar was received gladly, mostly in [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion). However, [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) and [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) were proven correct, as Annatar was indeed [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and later, [there was war in Eriador](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron "War of the Elves and Sauron").<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup> [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) led the Elves of Lindon in [battle to defend Eregion](/wiki/Sack_of_Eregion "Sack of Eregion"). With them, and other [Elves](/wiki/Elves_of_Eregion "Elves of Eregion") who fled the destruction of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion), he established [Imladris](/wiki/Imladris) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697").<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> In the [Dark Years](/wiki/Dark_Years), [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) had conquered most of the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands), but in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1700](/wiki/Second_Age_1700 "Second Age 1700") [Tar-Minastir](/wiki/Tar-Minastir) sent a fleet to [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), aiding the Elves of Lindon to drive [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) out of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup> After those events, the Elves of Lindon were diminished and did not extend their realm east of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) anymore.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

When [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n) captured Sauron and took him to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands) found peace, and Gil-galad was free to extend his power and influence so that the Elves of Lindon controlled most of the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands), even to the borders of [Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Greenwood_the_Great), until the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor), when Sauron returned to Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup>

At the end of the Age, Gil-galad joined the [Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance) and led the Elves of Lindon to the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance). Some of the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves) felt hostility towards the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) of Lindon, and did not cooperate, suffering heavy losses.<sup id="cite_ref-princes_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-princes-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Decline {#Decline}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Angelo_Montanini_-_Northern_Waste.jpg"><img src="/w/images/3/35/Angelo_Montanini_-_Northern_Waste.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="253" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="300" data-file-height="253"/></a><figcaption><i>Northern Waste</i> by <a href="/wiki/Angelo_Montanini" title="Angelo Montanini">Angelo Montanini</a></figcaption></figure>

After the fall of Gil-galad (during the time of the [Kings of Arnor](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor)), there was still a remnant of his people, the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), who dwelt at the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) of further inland in Lindon, who, aside from [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), were most of the remaining Noldor of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-eriador_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-7">[7]</a></sup> The Elves of Lindon would often wander into [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) but they mostly dwelt by the sea building the elven-ships in which many [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) would depart to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup> [Cirdan](/wiki/Cirdan) remained the Lord of the Havens and ruled the Elves of Lindon.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-eriador_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-7">[7]</a></sup>

During the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the Elves of Lindon under [Cirdan](/wiki/Cirdan) supported [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) in the [Angmar War](/wiki/Angmar_War). They assisted [Arveleg I](/wiki/Arveleg_I) and [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan) drive off the [Hill-men](/wiki/Hill-men) from the [Weather Hills](/wiki/Weather_Hills). Later, with [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan)'s help, [Arveleg](/wiki/Arveleg_I "Arveleg I")'s young son, [Araphor](/wiki/Araphor), drove [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar)'s armies from [Fornost](/wiki/Fornost) and the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs). Combining forces with [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), and the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) who joined them from beyond the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), they subdued Angmar for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-7">[7]</a></sup> They also assisted King [Araval](/wiki/Araval) in a coalition with [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), in another battle against Angmar in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1851](/wiki/Third_Age_1851 "Third Age 1851").<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-8">[8]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975"), Elves from Lindon brought a ship to [Forochel](/wiki/Forochel) to rescue [Arvedui Last-king](/wiki/Arvedui_Last-king). The crew endured a perilous journey, but Arvedui rashly attempted to return that winter, and all aboard perished. The [next year](/wiki/Third_Age_1976 "Third Age 1976"), the Elves of Lindon joined the [Host of the West](</wiki/Host_of_the_West_(Arnor)> "Host of the West (Arnor)"), and Cirdan led them to victory in the [Battle of Fornost](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost).<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In the later [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) some Elves from Lindon could be seen [traversing](/wiki/Wandering_Companies "Wandering Companies") [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), especially during Spring and Autumn. It was some of the few occasions that [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) could see Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Galdor](/wiki/Galdor_of_the_Havens "Galdor of the Havens") was sent to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) on an errand for [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) and he represented the Elves of Lindon at the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

The [Last Ship](/wiki/Last_Ship), carrying [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), sailed at some unknown date after [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [171](/wiki/Fourth_Age_171 "Fourth Age 171").<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppTale_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppTale-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Population {#Population}

The original Elves of Lindon in the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days), were [Laiquendi](/wiki/Laiquendi), the [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves) of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand). Even after the Elder Days passed, after they were joined by other [Elves of Beleriand](/wiki/Elves_of_Beleriand), the Laiquendi remained; some of them removed to [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador)<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-5">[5]</a></sup> and others went to [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) to live among the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad)'s people were mainly [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor)<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> (especially from [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), but also from the [Houses of Finarfin](/wiki/House_of_Finarfin "House of Finarfin") and [Fëanor](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor "House of Fëanor")); early in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), most of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) that remained in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) lived in Lindon.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> During this time, the few [Exiles](/wiki/Exiles) that remained lived in Lindon.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> Even in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the Elves of Lindon consisted most of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) that still lingered in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-7">[7]</a></sup>

The Elves of [Harlindon](/wiki/Harlindon), the people of Lindon south of the [Gulf of Lhûn](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lh%C3%BBn), were mostly [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar), ruled by [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> Before the building of the [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr), many [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar), survivors of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) uneasy living among the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) of [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), went to east to establish realms among the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-princes_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-princes-4">[4]</a></sup> Some of these [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) also removed to the shores of [Lake Nenuial](/wiki/Lake_Nenuial).<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-5">[5]</a></sup>
