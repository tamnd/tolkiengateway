The **Elves of Lindon** were those [Elves](Elves.md) who dwelt in [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) in the west of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), between the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) and the [Great Sea](../B/Belegaer.md).

## History {#History}

### Rule of Gil-galad {#Rule_of_Gil-galad}

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1](../S/Second_Age_1.md),<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Elves](Elves.md) built the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) in the newly-formed [Gulf of Lune](../G/Gulf_of_Lune.md) where the [Eldar](Eldar.md) sailed the [Straight Road](../S/Straight_Road.md), but most of them were unwilling at first to forsake the lands they fought in and preferred to linger there.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup> This was the foundation of [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) and its people.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> Among those that remained were [Círdan the Shipwright](../C/Círdan_the_Shipwright.md), [Celeborn of Doriath](../C/Celeborn.md), [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md), [Elrond](Elrond.md), and [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-3">[3]</a></sup> [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) was the last heir of the [kings of the Noldor](../K/King_of_the_Noldor.md) in exile, and acknowledged as [High King of the Elves of the West](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) and so, he was their king.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the beginning of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), most of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) that remained in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) dwelt in [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) but,<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> many [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md), survivors of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) and [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md), left [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) before [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1000](../S/Second_Age_1000.md) to establish realms among the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-princes_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-princes-4">[4]</a></sup> [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) brought many [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), and [Green-elves](../G/Green-elves.md)<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-5">[5]</a></sup> out of Lindon into [Eriador](Eriador.md) to dwell there for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-5">[5]</a></sup> Many of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) in Lindon lead by [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md) established the realm of [Eregion](Eregion.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [750](../S/Second_Age_750.md) near the [West-gate](../W/West-gate.md) of [Khazad-Dûm](../M/Moria.md) due to the recent discovery of [mithril](../M/Mithril.md) in [Khazad-Dûm](../M/Moria.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup>

A fair being calling himself [Annatar](../A/Annatar.md), an emissary of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), came seeking entrance. Gil-galad and Master [Elrond](Elrond.md) sensed that he was not what he seemed, and denied him. Messengers were sent from [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) with warnings; but they were unheeded, and Annatar was received gladly, mostly in [Eregion](Eregion.md). However, [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) and [Elrond](Elrond.md) were proven correct, as Annatar was indeed [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) and later, [there was war in Eriador](../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup> [Elrond](Elrond.md) led the Elves of Lindon in [battle to defend Eregion](../S/Sack_of_Eregion.md). With them, and other [Elves](Elves_of_Eregion.md) who fled the destruction of [Eregion](Eregion.md), he established [Imladris](../I/Imladris.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1697](../S/Second_Age_1697.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> In the [Dark Years](../D/Dark_Years.md), [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) had conquered most of the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md), but in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1700](../S/Second_Age_1700.md) [Tar-Minastir](../T/Tar-Minastir.md) sent a fleet to [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md), aiding the Elves of Lindon to drive [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) out of [Eriador](Eriador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup> After those events, the Elves of Lindon were diminished and did not extend their realm east of the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) anymore.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

When [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md) captured Sauron and took him to [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md) found peace, and Gil-galad was free to extend his power and influence so that the Elves of Lindon controlled most of the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md), even to the borders of [Greenwood the Great](../G/Greenwood_the_Great.md), until the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md), when Sauron returned to Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup>

At the end of the Age, Gil-galad joined the [Last Alliance](../L/Last_Alliance.md) and led the Elves of Lindon to the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md). Some of the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) felt hostility towards the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) of Lindon, and did not cooperate, suffering heavy losses.<sup id="cite_ref-princes_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-princes-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Decline {#Decline}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Angelo_Montanini_-_Northern_Waste.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/3/35/Angelo_Montanini_-_Northern_Waste.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="253" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="300" data-file-height="253"/></a><figcaption><i>Northern Waste</i> by <a href="../A/Angelo_Montanini.md" title="Angelo Montanini">Angelo Montanini</a></figcaption></figure>

After the fall of Gil-galad (during the time of the [Kings of Arnor](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md)), there was still a remnant of his people, the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), who dwelt at the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) of further inland in Lindon, who, aside from [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), were most of the remaining Noldor of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-eriador_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-7">[7]</a></sup> The Elves of Lindon would often wander into [Eriador](Eriador.md) but they mostly dwelt by the sea building the elven-ships in which many [Eldar](Eldar.md) would depart to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup> [Cirdan](../C/Cirdan.md) remained the Lord of the Havens and ruled the Elves of Lindon.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-eriador_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-7">[7]</a></sup>

During the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the Elves of Lindon under [Cirdan](../C/Cirdan.md) supported [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) in the [Angmar War](../A/Angmar_War.md). They assisted [Arveleg I](../A/Arveleg_I.md) and [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md) drive off the [Hill-men](../H/Hill-men.md) from the [Weather Hills](../W/Weather_Hills.md). Later, with [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md)'s help, [Arveleg](../A/Arveleg_I.md)'s young son, [Araphor](../A/Araphor.md), drove [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md)'s armies from [Fornost](../F/Fornost.md) and the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md). Combining forces with [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), and the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) who joined them from beyond the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), they subdued Angmar for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-7">[7]</a></sup> They also assisted King [Araval](../A/Araval.md) in a coalition with [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), in another battle against Angmar in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1851](../T/Third_Age_1851.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-8">[8]</a></sup> In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md), Elves from Lindon brought a ship to [Forochel](../F/Forochel.md) to rescue [Arvedui Last-king](../A/Arvedui_Last-king.md). The crew endured a perilous journey, but Arvedui rashly attempted to return that winter, and all aboard perished. The [next year](../T/Third_Age_1976.md), the Elves of Lindon joined the [Host of the West](<../H/Host_of_the_West_(Arnor).md>), and Cirdan led them to victory in the [Battle of Fornost](../B/Battle_of_Fornost.md).<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In the later [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) some Elves from Lindon could be seen [traversing](../W/Wandering_Companies.md) [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), especially during Spring and Autumn. It was some of the few occasions that [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) could see Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), [Galdor](../G/Galdor_of_the_Havens.md) was sent to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) on an errand for [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) and he represented the Elves of Lindon at the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

The [Last Ship](../L/Last_Ship.md), carrying [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) and [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md), sailed at some unknown date after [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [171](../F/Fourth_Age_171.md).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppTale_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppTale-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Population {#Population}

The original Elves of Lindon in the [Elder Days](Elder_Days.md), were [Laiquendi](../L/Laiquendi.md), the [Green-elves](../G/Green-elves.md) of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md). Even after the Elder Days passed, after they were joined by other [Elves of Beleriand](Elves_of_Beleriand.md), the Laiquendi remained; some of them removed to [Eriador](Eriador.md)<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-5">[5]</a></sup> and others went to [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) to live among the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md)'s people were mainly [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> (especially from [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), but also from the [Houses of Finarfin](../H/House_of_Finarfin.md) and [Fëanor](../H/House_of_Fëanor.md)); early in the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), most of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) that remained in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) lived in Lindon.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> During this time, the few [Exiles](Exiles.md) that remained lived in Lindon.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> Even in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the Elves of Lindon consisted most of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) that still lingered in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-7">[7]</a></sup>

The Elves of [Harlindon](../H/Harlindon.md), the people of Lindon south of the [Gulf of Lhûn](../G/Gulf_of_Lhûn.md), were mostly [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), ruled by [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> Before the building of the [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md), many [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), survivors of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) uneasy living among the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) of [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), went to east to establish realms among the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-princes_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-princes-4">[4]</a></sup> Some of these [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) also removed to the shores of [Lake Nenuial](../L/Lake_Nenuial.md).<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-5">[5]</a></sup>
