**Everard Proudfoot** is a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire). Everard Proudfoot was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), where he was portrayed by [Noel Appleby](/wiki/Noel_Appleby).

## History {#History}

Mr. Proudfoot was a grumpy old gaffer, who minded his own business and tended his garden. However, he enjoyed [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)'s fireworks and only expressed his disapproval when under the supervision of his wife. He was present at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He later met the [four hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") as they returned home from their journey.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
