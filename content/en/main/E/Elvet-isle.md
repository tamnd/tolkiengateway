**Elvet-isle** was an island in the [Withywindle](../W/Withywindle.md) in the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md). The isle was inhabited by [swans](../S/Swans.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Elvet* is an old word meaning "swan" from the [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *elfetu* or *ilfetu*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
