**Eldar** (singular **Elda**) was the name given to the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) by the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) when he first found them wandering in the starlight of [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen). At first, the name was applied to all Elves, but after the summons of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), it came to be used only for the **West-elves**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> who followed the summons and began the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey).

The [Three Kindreds](/wiki/Three_Kindreds) of the Eldar were the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar), the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) and the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri). All of the Vanyar and Noldor went to [Aman](/wiki/Aman), though many of the Noldor later returned to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) with [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor).

Many of the Teleri also journeyed to Valinor, but twice a host of this people turned away from the Journey in Middle-earth; these two kindreds are called [Úmanyar](/wiki/%C3%9Amanyar), the Eldar not of Aman. The first of these were the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor), who turned aside east of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), and travelled down the River Anduin. The second, the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar), tarried in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) seeking their lord, [Elwë Singollo](/wiki/Elw%C3%AB_Singollo).

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") notes the accidental similarity between the word "Eldar" and "Elder" (as in "Elder Folk" or "[Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days)") and that it is unrelated to English and therefore should not be any attempt to imitate it.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>

## In popular usage {#In_popular_usage}

The name "Eldar" has been used for Elven-like races in later fiction. <a class="external text" href="http://warhammer40k.wikia.com/wiki/Aeldari" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Eldar</a> is a humanoid being, descendant of an ancient interstellar Empire in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warhammer_40K" title="wikipedia:Warhammer 40K">Warhammer 40K</a> universe. <a class="external text" href="http://midkemia.wikia.com/wiki/Eldar" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Eldar</a> is also a race of Elves in the <a class="external text" href="http://midkemia.wikia.com/wiki/Midkemia" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Midkemia</a> cycle by [Raymond E. Feist](/wiki/Raymond_E._Feist).
