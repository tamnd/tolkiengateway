In *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*, the **Ered Rhívamar** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") **“Edge of the World Mountains”**) were a mountain range in the [Northern Waste](/wiki/Northern_Waste). Its most dangerous spur was the Ered Úmarth ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Mountains of Ill-fate”), which surrounded the valley of [Evermist](/wiki/Evermist). They were inhabited by [Snow-trolls](/wiki/Snow-trolls) and Ice-drakes.<sup id="cite_ref-maininfo_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-maininfo-1">[1]</a></sup>

The mountain range was separated from [Ekkaia](/wiki/Ekkaia) to the north by Gondalf ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Stone Tundra”), a plateau that was known as Angband's Threshold in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) and which collapsed after the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath). It was inhabited by the Jäämiehet, a hardy clan of [Lossoth](/wiki/Lossoth).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

To the south, the mountain range was separated from Spouting Bay and Berg Cradle Bay by the peninsula of Minheldolath ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Between the Bergs”). Protected from the northern storms by the mountain range, and warmed by the by the surrounding waters, it was a fairly temperate peninsula that greened in the summer. It too was inhabited by the Jäämiehet, as well as by another more nomadic clan known as the Merimetsästäjät.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
