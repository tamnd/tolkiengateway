In *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)*, the **Ered Rhívamar** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) **“Edge of the World Mountains”**) were a mountain range in the [Northern Waste](../N/Northern_Waste.md). Its most dangerous spur was the Ered Úmarth ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) “Mountains of Ill-fate”), which surrounded the valley of [Evermist](Evermist.md). They were inhabited by [Snow-trolls](../S/Snow-trolls.md) and Ice-drakes.<sup id="cite_ref-maininfo_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-maininfo-1">[1]</a></sup>

The mountain range was separated from [Ekkaia](Ekkaia.md) to the north by Gondalf ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) “Stone Tundra”), a plateau that was known as Angband's Threshold in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) and which collapsed after the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md). It was inhabited by the Jäämiehet, a hardy clan of [Lossoth](../L/Lossoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

To the south, the mountain range was separated from Spouting Bay and Berg Cradle Bay by the peninsula of Minheldolath ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) “Between the Bergs”). Protected from the northern storms by the mountain range, and warmed by the by the surrounding waters, it was a fairly temperate peninsula that greened in the summer. It too was inhabited by the Jäämiehet, as well as by another more nomadic clan known as the Merimetsästäjät.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
