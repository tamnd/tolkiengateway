**Electronic Arts** or **EA** for short is an American video games developing, publishing and distributing company, founded in [1982](../_/1982.md). Among its successes are *The Sims*, James Bond and various sports games, published by daughter company EA Sports.

## History {#History}

### Early efforts {#Early_efforts}

Electronic Arts had it first fantasy game with <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bard%27s_Tale_(1985)" title="wikipedia:Bard&#39;s Tale (1985)">The Bard&#39;s Tale</a></i> in [1985](../_/1985.md), and was introduced to the world of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in [1990](../_/1990.md), when they held the UK distribution of [Interplay](../I/Interplay_Productions.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings, Vol. I](<../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_The_Lord_of_the_Rings,_Vol._I_(1990_video_game).md>)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In 1994 they published [The Lord of the Rings, Vol. I](<../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_The_Lord_of_the_Rings,_Vol._I_(SNES).md>) for the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Nintendo_Entertainment_System" title="wikipedia:Super Nintendo Entertainment System">SNES</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Movie license {#Movie_license}

Electronic Arts purchased the interactive entertainment rights of [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) in [2002](../_/2002.md). That was after the video game *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game).md>)* by [Vivendi](../V/Vivendi.md) came out, leading several people to believe that was an Electronic Arts game too. EA had to compress both the [first](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) and the [second](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md) film into one game, which became *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](EA's_The_Two_Towers.md)* in [2002](../_/2002.md). A year later, a game based on the [third](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md) film was also made, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(video_game).md>)*. When all movie-based games were published, an "alternative [fellowship](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)"- game, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Third_Age.md)*, was made. Because it did not have rights to the book, it had to fill in the gameplay with movie-like aspects.

### Book license {#Book_license}

In [2005](../_/2005.md), a license to the books could be purchased from [Saul Zaentz](../S/Saul_Zaentz.md), which led the way to *[The Battle for Middle-earth](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth.md)*, *[The Battle for Middle-earth II](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md)* and an expansion, *[The Rise of the Witch-king](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II%253A_The_Rise_of_the_Witch-king.md)*. The popularity of these games made Electronic Arts to extend their license until [2008](../_/2008.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> One more title was planned; at first, this would have been *[The Lord of the Rings: The White Council](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_White_Council.md)*, but that has been cancelled.Its last game which was based on Tolkien's Middle-earth, *[The Lord of the Rings: Conquest](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Conquest.md)* was published in January [2009](../_/2009.md).

### Recent years {#Recent_years}

In [2022](../_/2022.md), it was announced that Electronic Arts will co-operate with [Middle-earth Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md) in order to release a new mobile RPG mobile game named *[The Lord of the Rings: Heroes of Middle-earth](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Heroes_of_Middle-earth.md)* at an unspecified time.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The game was eventually released in [2023](../_/2023.md).

## Peter Jackson {#Peter_Jackson}

Despite borrowing music, character design and character voices from [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), Electronic Arts did not leave room for [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md)'s input. This did not sit well with Jackson, who turned to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubisoft" title="wikipedia:Ubisoft">Ubisoft</a> for the film tie-in game of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Kong_(2005_film)" title="wikipedia:King Kong (2005 film)">King Kong</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
