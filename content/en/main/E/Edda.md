**Edda** was a [woman](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) who served as an assistant to [Queen Regent Míriel](/wiki/Tar-M%C3%ADriel "Tar-Míriel") during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Edda was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios), and she is portrayed by New Zealand actress <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Carmel_McGlone&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Carmel McGlone (page does not exist)">Carmel McGlone</a>.

## History {#History}

After [Queen Regent Míriel](/wiki/Tar-M%C3%ADriel "Tar-Míriel") suddenly wakes up from a [dream](/wiki/Atlantis-haunting "Atlantis-haunting") about a [Great Wave](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Downfall of Númenor") over [Armenelos](/wiki/Armenelos), she is greeted by Edda, who asks her what business she will attend to that day.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Later, shortly before Míriel departed on the expedition to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), Edda came to inform the Queen Regent that her father, [Tar-Palantir](/wiki/Tar-Palantir), desired to speak with her.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
