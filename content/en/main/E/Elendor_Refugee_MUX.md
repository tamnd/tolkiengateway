**Elendor Refugee MUX** was a MUX (Multi-User eXperience), meant to cater to players of *[Elendor](/wiki/Elendor)* when the original was down for a brief period in [1996](/wiki/1996).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Upon *Elendor*'s reactivation, the *Refugee MUX* was closed.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup>
