**Eilinel** was the wife of [Gorlim the Unhappy](/wiki/Gorlim "Gorlim").

## History {#History}

Eilinel was a fair and gentle maiden, and was much loved by Gorlim. When her husband went to the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), their house was "roofless"<sup id="cite_ref-plundered_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-plundered-3">[3]</a></sup> and their "fields and homestead"<sup id="cite_ref-plundered_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-plundered-3">[3]</a></sup> were plundered. Eilinel was killed "by butchers"<sup id="cite_ref-killedby_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-killedby-4">[4]</a></sup> in some "burned woods"<sup id="cite_ref-killedby_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-killedby-4">[4]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-S19_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S19-5">[5]</a></sup>

Upon returning home, Gorlim could not find any trace of Eilinel. He kept returning to their house seeking for his lost wife, and [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) tricked him by devising a [phantom](/wiki/Phantoms "Phantoms") of Eilinel to make him betray the location of [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir).<sup id="cite_ref-S19_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S19-5">[5]</a></sup>
