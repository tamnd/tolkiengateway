The **Ethir Anduin**, also known as the **Mouths of Anduin** and **Anduin's Mouths**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the delta of the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), south of [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## History {#History}

The delta's first settlers were probably [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor). Some of the Nandor who had lived upstream, in the Vales of Anduin under the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), passed to its Mouths southward.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> After they were gone, the "lesser men" settled there,<sup id="cite_ref-AIiv_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AIiv-3">[3]</a></sup> though they were likely in conflict with [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim). After the coming of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans), the confluence of cultures in the Ethir and [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) formed one of the earliest forms of [Westron](/wiki/Westron).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The Númenoreans made a passage taken by the ships going to and from Pelargir.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the [Drowning of Númenor](/wiki/Akallab%C3%AAth "Akallabêth"), the shores of the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas) had retreated a great distance, putting Pelargir much farther inland than it had been in its beginning. Afterwards, the Anduin carved a new path by many mouths to the Bay.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The delta was populated mostly by [fishermen](/wiki/Fish "Fish") and other sea-crafty folk,<sup id="cite_ref-TLD_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TLD-7">[7]</a></sup> and became an important part of Gondor in the days of [Tarannon](/wiki/Tarannon), the first [Ship-king](/wiki/Ship-kings "Ship-kings").<sup id="cite_ref-AIiv_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AIiv-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), some hundred fishermen that could be spared from the boats were sent to the defence of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-8">[8]</a></sup> This left the Mouths themselves open to [Umbarian](/wiki/Umbar "Umbar") conquest,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> but the occupation did not last very long; [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) liberated it several days later.<sup id="cite_ref-TLD_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TLD-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The Ethir Anduin was also known as the "Mouths of Anduin". *[Ethir](/wiki/Ethir)* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "Mouth of a river".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> *[Anduin](/wiki/Anduin)* simply means "Long river".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
