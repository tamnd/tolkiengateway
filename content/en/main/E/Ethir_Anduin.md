The **Ethir Anduin**, also known as the **Mouths of Anduin** and **Anduin's Mouths**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the delta of the river [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), south of [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

## History {#History}

The delta's first settlers were probably [Nandor](../N/Nandor.md). Some of the Nandor who had lived upstream, in the Vales of Anduin under the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), passed to its Mouths southward.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> After they were gone, the "lesser men" settled there,<sup id="cite_ref-AIiv_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AIiv-3">[3]</a></sup> though they were likely in conflict with [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md). After the coming of the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md), the confluence of cultures in the Ethir and [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) formed one of the earliest forms of [Westron](../W/Westron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The Númenoreans made a passage taken by the ships going to and from Pelargir.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the [Drowning of Númenor](../A/Akallabêth.md), the shores of the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md) had retreated a great distance, putting Pelargir much farther inland than it had been in its beginning. Afterwards, the Anduin carved a new path by many mouths to the Bay.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The delta was populated mostly by [fishermen](../F/Fish.md) and other sea-crafty folk,<sup id="cite_ref-TLD_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TLD-7">[7]</a></sup> and became an important part of Gondor in the days of [Tarannon](../T/Tarannon.md), the first [Ship-king](../S/Ship-kings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AIiv_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AIiv-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), some hundred fishermen that could be spared from the boats were sent to the defence of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-8">[8]</a></sup> This left the Mouths themselves open to [Umbarian](../U/Umbar.md) conquest,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> but the occupation did not last very long; [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md) liberated it several days later.<sup id="cite_ref-TLD_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TLD-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The Ethir Anduin was also known as the "Mouths of Anduin". *[Ethir](Ethir.md)* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning "Mouth of a river".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> *[Anduin](../A/Anduin.md)* simply means "Long river".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
