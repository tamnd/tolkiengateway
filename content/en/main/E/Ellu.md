**Ellu Melemno** was the second leader of the [Solosimpi](../S/Solosimpi.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ell_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ell-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After [Tinwë Linto](../T/Tinwë_Linto.md), the original leader of the Solosimpi, was enchanted by the [fay](../F/Fays.md) [Wendelin](../W/Wendelin.md) in [Hisilómë](../H/Hisilómë.md) during the [Elves](Elves.md)'s [march](../G/Great_Journey.md) from [Palisor](../P/Palisor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> the Solosimpi chose one Ellu to be their lord,<sup id="cite_ref-Ell_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ell-1">[1]</a></sup> who eventually led them to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Many years later, when [Melko](../M/Melko.md) started to sow unrest among the [Gnomes](../G/Gnomes.md) in Valinor, their king [Nólemë](../N/Nólemë.md) sought Ellu's advice, who, along with [Inwë](../I/Inwë.md) and [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md), advised him to inform [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) of Melko's machinations.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The names *Ellu* and *Melemno* are, according to [Paul Strack](../P/Paul_Strack.md), in [Solosimpilin](../S/Solosimpilin.md), but their meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The character was originally known only as *Melemno* in the story of *[The Theft of Melko](../T/The_Theft_of_Melko.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to the original version of *[The Tale of Tinúviel](../T/The_Tale_of_Tinúviel.md)*, the name *Ellu* was first applied to Tinwë Linto (or Tinwelint in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md)), as seen in the name *Tinto Ellu*.

However, in a later addition to the tales of *[The Coming of the Elves](../T/The_Coming_of_the_Elves.md)* and of *The Theft of Melko*, the name *Ellu* began to be applied to the lord of the Solosimpi in Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In the *[Sketch of the Mythology](../S/Sketch_of_the_Mythology.md)* from c. [1926](../_/1926.md), the first version of the *Silmarillion*, the name *Ellu* was again applied to the leader of the Solosimpi (now also called [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md)) in Valinor, here changed to *Elu* (or *Elwë* in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

For the later applications of the name *Elu*/*Elwë* in the legendarium, see: [Olwë#Other versions of the legendarium](../O/Olwë.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium) and [Thingol#Other names](../T/Thingol.md#Other_names).
