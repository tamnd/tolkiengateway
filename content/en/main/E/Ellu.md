**Ellu Melemno** was the second leader of the [Solosimpi](/wiki/Solosimpi), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ell_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ell-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After [Tinwë Linto](/wiki/Tinw%C3%AB_Linto), the original leader of the Solosimpi, was enchanted by the [fay](/wiki/Fays "Fays") [Wendelin](/wiki/Wendelin) in [Hisilómë](/wiki/Hisil%C3%B3m%C3%AB) during the [Elves](/wiki/Elves)'s [march](/wiki/Great_Journey "Great Journey") from [Palisor](/wiki/Palisor),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> the Solosimpi chose one Ellu to be their lord,<sup id="cite_ref-Ell_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ell-1">[1]</a></sup> who eventually led them to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Many years later, when [Melko](/wiki/Melko) started to sow unrest among the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) in Valinor, their king [Nólemë](/wiki/N%C3%B3lem%C3%AB) sought Ellu's advice, who, along with [Inwë](/wiki/Inw%C3%AB) and [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), advised him to inform [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) of Melko's machinations.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The names *Ellu* and *Melemno* are, according to [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack), in [Solosimpilin](/wiki/Solosimpilin), but their meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The character was originally known only as *Melemno* in the story of *[The Theft of Melko](/wiki/The_Theft_of_Melko)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to the original version of *[The Tale of Tinúviel](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Tin%C3%BAviel)*, the name *Ellu* was first applied to Tinwë Linto (or Tinwelint in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish)), as seen in the name *Tinto Ellu*.

However, in a later addition to the tales of *[The Coming of the Elves](/wiki/The_Coming_of_the_Elves)* and of *The Theft of Melko*, the name *Ellu* began to be applied to the lord of the Solosimpi in Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In the *[Sketch of the Mythology](/wiki/Sketch_of_the_Mythology)* from c. [1926](/wiki/1926), the first version of the *Silmarillion*, the name *Ellu* was again applied to the leader of the Solosimpi (now also called [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri)) in Valinor, here changed to *Elu* (or *Elwë* in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya)).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

For the later applications of the name *Elu*/*Elwë* in the legendarium, see: [Olwë#Other versions of the legendarium](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Olwë") and [Thingol#Other names](/wiki/Thingol#Other_names "Thingol").
