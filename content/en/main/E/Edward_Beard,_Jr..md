**Edward Beard, Jr.** (born [11 December](../_/11_December.md) [1964](../_/1964.md)) is an American fantasy artist.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup> He was contracted by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create artwork for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).

He tends to work with acrylics for colour work and graphite for black-and-white works.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Career {#Career}

Beard lacks formal training because his school looked down on fantasy art. Despite this, he was seen as promising and initially worked on religious commissions. Later on, he became an artist for [Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md) and many other fantasy games. <sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
