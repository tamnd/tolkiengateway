**Elmo** was an [Elf](Elf.md) of the royal house of the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md). He was the younger brother of the two Kings of the Teleri, [Elwë](../T/Thingol.md) and [Olwë](../O/Olwë.md).

## History {#History}

Elmo and his elder brothers were directly descended through eldest son to eldest son from [Enel](Enel.md), the first of the [Nelyar](../N/Nelyar.md) to [awaken](../A/Awakening_of_the_Elves.md). Unlike his older brothers who had been born by the time the Elves were found by the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), Elmo was born during the [Great Journey](../G/Great_Journey.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

He remained behind in Beleriand after Elwë was lost in [Nan Elmoth](../N/Nan_Elmoth.md) and Olwë departed for [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). After Elwë returned, Elmo remained with and was beloved by him.<sup id="cite_ref-UTG_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTG-3">[3]</a></sup>

Given that Elmo’s grandson [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) was reckoned a prince of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> it is likely that Elmo was likewise a prince. It is unknown if he survived the [sack of Doriath](../S/Sack_of_Doriath.md) or what his fate was otherwise. No specific deeds are ascribed to Elmo,<sup id="cite_ref-UTG_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTG-3">[3]</a></sup> and he is largely notable for his descendants: Celeborn, who was one of the noble Sindar, and [Nimloth](../N/Nimloth_of_Doriath.md), who was briefly Queen of Doriath, when she married [Dior](../D/Dior.md), Thingol's successor.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Elmo* is in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). Its meaning is unclear, but it could be a combination of *[él](EL~31035.md)* (being a poetic word for star) and the suffix *[-mo](../_/-mo.md)* ("person").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Elmo was developed to explain the reference in *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* that [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) was a "kinsman of Thingol". One of the solutions was to establish Celeborn as the son of an unimportant brother of Thingol. Depending on the texts, Elmo is considered as a younger brother of Elwë ([Thingol](../T/Thingol.md)), the grandfather of [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md)<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-7">[7]</a></sup> or his father.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In a draft genealogy of Elwë and Elmo's descendants dated to December [1959](../_/1959.md) that Christopher Tolkien rejected from *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*, Nimloth is the granddaughter of Elmo rather than great-granddaughter (sister rather than daughter of Galathil).<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-9">[9]</a></sup>

In other, later writings, Celeborn is a [Teler of Aman](../F/Falmari.md) rather than a Sinda of Doriath, and a grandson of Olwë rather than of Elmo.<sup id="cite_ref-UTG_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTG-3">[3]</a></sup> Without further revision to Galadriel's own genealogy, this origin for Celeborn would cause the couple to [become first cousins](../C/Celeborn.md#Controversy). Tolkien did not develop this idea further.

### Nelwë {#Nelwë}

In *[The Nature of Middle-earth](../T/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth.md)* there appears a character called **Nelwë**. There, he is presented as an ancestor of [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) (who was an [elf](../F/Falmari.md) of [Aman](../A/Aman.md) in this version of his history), and a younger brother of [Elwë](../T/Thingol.md), [Olwë](../O/Olwë.md), and [Eärwen](Eärwen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Nelw_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nelw-11">[10]</a></sup>

The entire text in which he appears, however, was struck through and rejected.<sup id="cite_ref-Nelw_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nelw-11">[10]</a></sup> While it is not explicitly mentioned anywhere by [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) that the character of *Nelwë* is equivalent to that of *Elmo*, or otherwise the latter's literary successor, he nonetheless fulfills the same role as an ancestor of Celeborn, in which the kinship of Celeborn and Thingol is elaborated on in a more exact manner.

#### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

The name *Nelwë* is in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), but its meaning is not entirely clear. The second element of the name *[-wë](../_/-wë.md)* is a suffix used mainly in masculine names of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), being an old word for "person".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup> According to [Paul Strack](../P/Paul_Strack.md), the first element of the name might be a derivation from the root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=NEL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NEL (page does not exist)">NEL</a> ("three").<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup>
