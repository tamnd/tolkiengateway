**epetai** in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) means "therefore, consequently" (lit. "before that which").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is analyzed as *[epe](/wiki/Epe "Epe")* and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tai&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tai (page does not exist)">tai</a></i>.
