**epetai** in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) means "therefore, consequently" (lit. "before that which").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is analyzed as *[epe](Epe.md)* and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tai&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tai (page does not exist)">tai</a></i>.
