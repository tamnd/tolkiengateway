<i><b>Essays Presented to Charles Williams</b></i> is a collection of six articles by friends of [Charles Williams](../C/Charles_Williams.md), written to present to him on his leaving Oxford, but published in 1947, to honour his death in 1945.

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) contributed his "[On Fairy-Stories](../O/On_Fairy-Stories.md)" (pp. 38–89) essay, which appeared here in print for the first time.
