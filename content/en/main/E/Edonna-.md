<i><b>edonna-</b></i> is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) verb meaning "to beget". It is derived from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ed-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ed- (page does not exist)">ed-</a></i> ("forth, out") + *[onna-](/wiki/Onna- "Onna-")* ("to beget, make born").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
