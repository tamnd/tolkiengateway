The term <i><b>Evendim </b></i> may refer to:

- **[Evendim](Evendim.md)**, the [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) name for the evening dusk.
- [Evendim](../L/Lake_Evendim.md), the [Westron](../W/Westron.md) name for lake [Nenuial](../N/Nenuial.md) ("Water of Twilight") in northern [Eriador](Eriador.md).
- [Hills of Evendim](../H/Hills_of_Evendim.md), a series of hills in northern Eriador, around that lake.
