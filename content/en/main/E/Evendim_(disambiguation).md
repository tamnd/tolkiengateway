The term <i><b>Evendim </b></i> may refer to:

- **[Evendim](/wiki/Evendim)**, the [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) name for the evening dusk.
- [Evendim](/wiki/Lake_Evendim "Lake Evendim"), the [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name for lake [Nenuial](/wiki/Nenuial) ("Water of Twilight") in northern [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).
- [Hills of Evendim](/wiki/Hills_of_Evendim), a series of hills in northern Eriador, around that lake.
