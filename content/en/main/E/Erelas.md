**Erelas** was the fourth of the seven [Beacon-hills](../W/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), which ran west from [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) on the northern side of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) that looked down on the plains of [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

Erelas was was a green hill without trees.<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NM_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Index_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-4">[4]</a></sup> While the fourth beacon-hill, its beacon was small and was not always lit. The lighting of its beacon was a signal of great urgency.<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NM_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-3">[3]</a></sup> The Beacon-hills from [Halifirien](../H/Halifirien.md) (the westernmost and seventh Beacon-hill) to [Nardol](../N/Nardol.md) (the third Beacon-hill) were situated along a shallow curve that bent a little southward so that the three Beacon-hills between them, [Calenhad](../C/Calenhad.md), [Min-Rimmon](../M/Min-Rimmon.md) and Erelas did not block the view.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The beacon-fire on Nardol could be seen from Halifirien on a clear night.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On the night of [7 March](../_/7_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), riding eastward on [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md), saw the beacons being lit to call for aid from Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

On [11 March](../_/11_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> when King [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) of Rohan rode with the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) and [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) through through Anórien on his ride to the aid of Minas Tirith, the signal fire on Erelas was no longer lit.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Despite being [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) in style, *Erelas* has no suitable Sindarin meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NM_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-3">[3]</a></sup> It is probably a [pre-Númenórean](../P/Pre-Númenórean.md) name,<sup id="cite_ref-Index_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-4">[4]</a></sup> but it is not recorded from which language the name *Erelas* was taken, nor what it means.

The hill of Erelas is much smaller than of nearby Beacon-hills <a href="../N/Nardol.md" title="Nardol">Nardol</a> and <a href="../M/Min-Rimmon.md" title="Min-Rimmon">Min-Rimmon</a>. A small village of a few household sits just to the east of the beacon.</dd></dl>
