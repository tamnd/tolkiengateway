**Erelas** was the fourth of the seven [Beacon-hills](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), which ran west from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) on the northern side of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) that looked down on the plains of [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

Erelas was was a green hill without trees.<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NM_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Index_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-4">[4]</a></sup> While the fourth beacon-hill, its beacon was small and was not always lit. The lighting of its beacon was a signal of great urgency.<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NM_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-3">[3]</a></sup> The Beacon-hills from [Halifirien](/wiki/Halifirien) (the westernmost and seventh Beacon-hill) to [Nardol](/wiki/Nardol) (the third Beacon-hill) were situated along a shallow curve that bent a little southward so that the three Beacon-hills between them, [Calenhad](/wiki/Calenhad), [Min-Rimmon](/wiki/Min-Rimmon) and Erelas did not block the view.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The beacon-fire on Nardol could be seen from Halifirien on a clear night.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On the night of [7 March](/wiki/7_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), riding eastward on [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax), saw the beacons being lit to call for aid from Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

On [11 March](/wiki/11_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> when King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) of Rohan rode with the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) and [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") through through Anórien on his ride to the aid of Minas Tirith, the signal fire on Erelas was no longer lit.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Despite being [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) in style, *Erelas* has no suitable Sindarin meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NM_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-3">[3]</a></sup> It is probably a [pre-Númenórean](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean "Pre-Númenórean") name,<sup id="cite_ref-Index_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-4">[4]</a></sup> but it is not recorded from which language the name *Erelas* was taken, nor what it means.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Erelas is located in the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Beacon-hills" title="Beacon-hills">Beacon-hills</a>, across the river to the west from the <a href="/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest" title="Drúadan Forest">Drúadan Forest</a>. The hill of Erelas is much smaller than of nearby Beacon-hills <a href="/wiki/Nardol" title="Nardol">Nardol</a> and <a href="/wiki/Min-Rimmon" title="Min-Rimmon">Min-Rimmon</a>. A small village of a few household sits just to the east of the beacon.</dd></dl>
