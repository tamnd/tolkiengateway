The **Elven-way** was the highroad<sup id="cite_ref-rings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-1">[1]</a></sup> that ran from [Ost-in-Edhil](/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil)<sup id="cite_ref-rings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-1">[1]</a></sup> in the Elven realm of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion),<sup id="cite_ref-elvenway_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-elvenway-2">[2]</a></sup> along the course of the [Sirannon](/wiki/Sirannon) stream<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> to the eastern end of Eregion, which was marked<sup id="cite_ref-elvenway_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-elvenway-2">[2]</a></sup> by two tall Holly trees that stood before the [Doors of Durin](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin) of the underground dwarven realm of [Moria](/wiki/Moria)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>. In the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") of the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) used the Elven-way on their approach to the Doors of Durin.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
