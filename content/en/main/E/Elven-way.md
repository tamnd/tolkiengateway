The **Elven-way** was the highroad<sup id="cite_ref-rings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-1">[1]</a></sup> that ran from [Ost-in-Edhil](../O/Ost-in-Edhil.md)<sup id="cite_ref-rings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-1">[1]</a></sup> in the Elven realm of [Eregion](Eregion.md),<sup id="cite_ref-elvenway_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-elvenway-2">[2]</a></sup> along the course of the [Sirannon](../S/Sirannon.md) stream<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> to the eastern end of Eregion, which was marked<sup id="cite_ref-elvenway_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-elvenway-2">[2]</a></sup> by two tall Holly trees that stood before the [Doors of Durin](../D/Doors_of_Durin.md) of the underground dwarven realm of [Moria](../M/Moria.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>. In the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) of the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) used the Elven-way on their approach to the Doors of Durin.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
