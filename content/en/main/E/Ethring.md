**Ethring** was a bridge<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> in southern [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), across the river [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3).

## History {#History}

Ethring was a major crossing point of the river [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3), along the main road from [Erech](/wiki/Erech) to [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and the [Oathbreakers](/wiki/Oathbreakers) crossed the river here during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ethring* perhaps comes from *[ath](/wiki/Ath "Ath")* meaning "across" (cf. [athrad](/wiki/Athrad "Athrad") "ford, crossing"). *Ring* translates to "cold" (cf. [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3) river, to which it probably refers).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
