**Eilenach** was the second of the seven [Beacon-hills](/wiki/Beacon-hills) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), which ran west from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was the highest point within the [Drúadan Forest](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest).

## Description {#Description}

Eilenach had a sharp summit, incapable of sustaining a large fire. Its main purpose was to relay the fire of the first Beacon-hill, [Amon Dîn](/wiki/Amon_D%C3%AEn), to the third, [Nardol](/wiki/Nardol).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT42_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On the night of [7 March](/wiki/7_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), riding eastward on [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax), saw the beacons being lit to call for aid from Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") wrote *Eilenach* is "probably an alien name, not [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), not [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor "Númenor"), or [Common speech](/wiki/Westron "Westron")."<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-4">[4]</a></sup> Perhaps it was [pre-Númenórean](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean "Pre-Númenórean").<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
