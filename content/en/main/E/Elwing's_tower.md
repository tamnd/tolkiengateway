**Elwing's tower**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the white tower that was built for [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing) "upon the borders of the outer world, in the northern regions of the [Sundering Seas](/wiki/Sundering_Seas)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> She dwelt in the tower where all the sea-birds came to rest, and devised white and silver-grey wings for herself so that she could fly to meet [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) who returned from his journeys from "beyond the confines of the world".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

There is a short mention of "the northern tower on the [Isle of Seabirds](/wiki/Isle_of_Seabirds)" in a note for "[The Tale of Eärendel](/wiki/The_Tale_of_E%C3%A4rendel)" from *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)* period.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the following versions of the story, Eärendel built the tower for himself, in the *[Sketch of the Mythology](/wiki/Sketch_of_the_Mythology)* on an isle in the northern seas,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and in the first version of the chapter 17 of the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](/wiki/Quenta_Noldorinwa)* in the Northern Seas.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> In both of these versions Eärendel is alone and builds the tower when trying to find Elwing who is lost in the seas.

But in the second version of *Quenta Noldorinwa*, chapter 17, the wording is already quite similar to the *Quenta Silmarillion*: Eärendel builds the tower "upon the confines of the outer world in the Northern regions of the Sundering Seas",<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and for the first time Elwing is with him and aids him in his journeys.

The "white tower" is not otherwise named in the *[Quenta Silmarillion](/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion)*, only in the Index of *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings)* it is called "Elwing's tower".
