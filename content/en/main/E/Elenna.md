**Elenna** or **Elenna-nórë**, also **Andor**, the "Land of Gift", was the island on which the realm of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) was founded.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The name meant "Starwards",<sup id="cite_ref-downfall_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-downfall-3">[3]</a></sup> from the fact that the first [Men](/wiki/Men) to inhabit that island came to it by following the light of the [Star of Eärendil](/wiki/Star_of_E%C3%A4rendil).

Generations later, owing to the Númenóreans' arrogance and blasphemy against the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), Elenna was deluged during the [Changing of the World](/wiki/Changing_of_the_World).

## Geography {#Geography}

The island of Númenor had a surface of probably around 180,000 square miles.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> It was located in [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) closer to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) (about 39 days by ship) than [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Its shape was that of a 5-point star. Each point had its own unique geological and physical features, and was therefore considered a separate region of Númenor with its own name:

- [Forostar](/wiki/Forostar) (*Northlands*)
- [Andustar](/wiki/Andustar) (*Westlands*)
- [Hyarnustar](/wiki/Hyarnustar) (*Southwestlands*)
- [Hyarrostar](/wiki/Hyarrostar) (*Southeastlands*)
- [Orrostar](/wiki/Orrostar) (*Eastlands*)
- [Mittalmar](/wiki/Mittalmar) (*Inlands*)

The area between Orrostar and Hyarrostar was the bay of [Rómenna](/wiki/R%C3%B3menna), with [Tol Uinen](/wiki/Tol_Uinen) standing off its coast.

The central region of Mittalmar contained the region of [Emerië](/wiki/Emeri%C3%AB), a grassland with many sheep, and the sacred mountain [Meneltarma](/wiki/Meneltarma), which stood in the very centre of the island. The mountain was the highest location on the entire island, and was a holy place devoted to [Eru Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar "Ilúvatar"). It was said that on a clear day the 'far-sighted' might see [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), the island off the coast of [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar), and the easternmost part of the [Undying Lands](/wiki/Undying_Lands).

The lower slopes of the mountain, the [Tarmasundar](/wiki/Tarmasundar), were gentle and grass-covered. However, near the summit the slopes became more vertical and could not be ascended easily. The kings later built a spiral road to the peak, beginning at the southern tip of the mountain and winding up to the lip of the summit in the north.

The only other known mountain on the island was [Sorontil](/wiki/Sorontil), in Forostar.

Númenor had only two rivers: [Siril](/wiki/Siril) which began at Meneltarma and ended in a small delta near the city of [Nindamos](/wiki/Nindamos), and the [Nunduinë](/wiki/Nunduin%C3%AB), which formed the lake [Nísinen](/wiki/N%C3%ADsinen) before reaching the sea in the [Bay of Eldanna](/wiki/Bay_of_Eldanna) near the haven [Eldalondë](/wiki/Eldalond%C3%AB).

The island itself was tilted southward and a little westward; the northern coasts were all steep sea cliffs.

## Flora {#Flora}

Númenor contained many species of plants that could be found nowhere else in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and many of them were given to the Númenóreans by the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) from [Aman](/wiki/Aman). Most important of these was the [White Tree](/wiki/White_Tree_of_N%C3%BAmenor "White Tree of Númenor") that dwelt in the King's Palace at [Armenelos](/wiki/Armenelos). A [seedling](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor "White Tree of Gondor") from it was later planted in the [Court of the Fountain](</wiki/Court_of_the_Fountain_(Minas_Tirith)> "Court of the Fountain (Minas Tirith)") in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

The other parts of Númenor contained many types of plants, many unique to each of the promontories of the island. Andustar contained great forests of [beech](/wiki/Beeches "Beeches") and birch on the higher ground, and oak and elm forests at lower altitudes. The eastern part of Hyarnustar, being warm and fertile, had great vineyards.

The greatest delight of the Númenóreans, however, were the [trees](/wiki/Fragrant_Trees "Fragrant Trees") given to them by the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar). They grew mostly in the Western portion, Andustar. They are often remembered in song and lore, and few have flowered east of Númenor.

- [Oiolairë](/wiki/Oiolair%C3%AB)
- [Lairelossë](/wiki/Laireloss%C3%AB)
- [Nessamelda](/wiki/Nessamelda)
- [Vardarianna](/wiki/Vardarianna)
- [Taniquelassë](/wiki/Taniquelass%C3%AB)
- [Yavannamírë](/wiki/Yavannam%C3%ADr%C3%AB)

Because of the diversity of wildlife in Andustar, that region was soon called [Nísimaldar](/wiki/N%C3%ADsimaldar), or the [Fragrant Trees](/wiki/Fragrant_Trees). Also only in Andustar could the Golden Tree be found, [Malinornë](/wiki/Malinorn%C3%AB).

In Hyarrostar grew the tree [Laurinquë](/wiki/Laurinqu%C3%AB), which the Númenóreans loved because of their flowers. They incorrectly believed that it came from the Great Tree of Valinor, [Laurelin](/wiki/Laurelin).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *Elenna* is actually the allative form of the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word *[elen](/wiki/Elen "Elen")* meaning "to (a) star". The "longer" name *Elenna-nórë* is glossed as "Land of the Star",<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> literally "Starwards-land". The island was so named because the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) colonists sailed "starwards", following the [Star of Eärendil](/wiki/Star_of_E%C3%A4rendil) to reach it.

[Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) mistakenly identifies the word as being the "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/essive_case" title="wikipedia:essive case">essive</a>" form of *elen*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Essive is a grammatical case that exists in [Finnish](/wiki/Finnish) (but not in Quenya), and is marked by the ending *-na*, like the Quenya allative.

The [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name was *[Gimlad](/wiki/Gimlad)* (cf. *[gimil](/wiki/Gimil "Gimil")*).

## Location {#Location}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Didier_Willis_-_Numenor_recontructed_latitude.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/e/ea/Didier_Willis_-_Numenor_recontructed_latitude.png/300px-Didier_Willis_-_Numenor_recontructed_latitude.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="250" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/e/ea/Didier_Willis_-_Numenor_recontructed_latitude.png/450px-Didier_Willis_-_Numenor_recontructed_latitude.png 1.5x, /w/images/e/ea/Didier_Willis_-_Numenor_recontructed_latitude.png 2x" data-file-width="600" data-file-height="500"/></a><figcaption>Reconstructed latitude of Númenor</figcaption></figure>

Tolkien never drew a map of Arda showing where exactly in Belegaer, or relative to Middle-earth, the island of Númenor stood, but there are some narrative hints.

While sailing for Lindon, [Vëantur](/wiki/V%C3%ABantur) had to fight the north and east winds,<sup id="cite_ref-UT|Wife_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT|Wife-8">[8]</a></sup> possibly suggesting the island was south of the latitude of Lindon (or perhaps simply stating the presence of opposing winds). It is also mentioned that Vëantur was accompanied by (apparently migratory) birds in autumn and spring.<sup id="cite_ref-Didier_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Didier-9">[9]</a></sup>

The Downfall of Númenor, while causing loss in Lindon, also filled much of the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas) so that the city of [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) was left far inland.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> This could suggest the island was more or less around the latitude of the Bay of Belfalas. However, the cataclysmic forces of the Downfall were stated to have impacted "all the coasts and seaward regions of the western world". This could be construed as evidence for Númenor being located in a relatively central position on the north-south axis. However, in the "World Made Round" conception of the Downfall presented in the *Akallabêth*, the cataclysm is by no means localized to Númenor, as the entire earth is warped and "new lands" are created.<sup id="cite_ref-SIV_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIV-11">[11]</a></sup>

As vineyards are attested in the eastern part of the [Hyarnustar](/wiki/Hyarnustar), [Didier Willis](/wiki/Didier_Willis) notes that adequate climatological conditions for viticulture are quite strict<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> and estimates the island to be located around 40° N, indeed facing the Bay of Belfalas.<sup id="cite_ref-Didier2_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Didier2-13">[13]</a></sup>

It is mentioned, however, that after his first voyages to Middle-earth, [Aldarion](/wiki/Aldarion) was no longer content to sail to [Mithlond](/wiki/Mithlond) and began exploring the coasts southwards, past the mouths of [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin).<sup id="cite_ref-UT|Wife_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT|Wife-8">[8]</a></sup> This suggests that the first land to be encountered on the journey of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) east is north of the Brandywine delta - Lindon, which could place the island around that latitude.

As for the distances, we are told simply that the island was closer to Valinor than Endor.<sup id="cite_ref-downfall_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-downfall-3">[3]</a></sup> It was said that from Meneltarma, the keen-eyed Númenóreans could sometimes get a glimpse of [Avallónë](/wiki/Avall%C3%B3n%C3%AB);<sup id="cite_ref-downfall_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-downfall-3">[3]</a></sup> the Fall of Númenor came 39 days after [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n) left,<sup id="cite_ref-downfall_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-downfall-3">[3]</a></sup> but his actual voyage could be shorter.

[Didier Willis](/wiki/Didier_Willis) estimates that as Vëantur could sail directly from Númenor to Lindon - not needing to find a closer coast and follow it up to his destination - the island was close enough to Lindon, with a realistic distance between 2000 to 3000 km.<sup id="cite_ref-Didier_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Didier-9">[9]</a></sup>

[Karen Wynn Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad) placed the island in her Second Age maps south of the latitude of the [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar). It approximates the Númenórean ports of [Vinyalondë](/wiki/Vinyalond%C3%AB), [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) and Umbar on Middle-earth. Her map shows the distance between the Island and Eressëa to be approximately 800 miles.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> Because Eressëa was within eyesight of [Túna](/wiki/T%C3%BAna),<sup id="cite_ref-S5_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S5-15">[15]</a></sup> Túna was located "nigh to the Girdle of Arda" (ie, near the equator),<sup id="cite_ref-S9_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S9-16">[16]</a></sup> and the far-sighted could on a clear day see Eressëa from the summit of Meneltarma,<sup id="cite_ref-downfall_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-downfall-3">[3]</a></sup> Númenor could not be too much further north than the equator itself.
