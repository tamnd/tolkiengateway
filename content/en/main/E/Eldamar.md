**Eldamar**, also known as **Elendë** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md)) or **[Elvenhome](Elvenhome.md)**, was the land of the [Amanyar](../A/Amanyar.md) in [Aman](../A/Aman.md), which encompassed the [Calacirian](../C/Calacirian.md) (the area around the cleft of [Calacirya](../C/Calacirya.md), including the hill of [Túna](../T/Túna.md)), as well as the coastal areas east of the [Pelóri](../P/Pelóri.md) including the island of [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) in the [Bay of Eldamar](../B/Bay_of_Eldamar.md).

The main settlements of the [Elves](Elves.md) in this region were [Tirion](../T/Tirion.md) on Túna (where the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) and the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) dwelt together in ancient times), [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md) (the Swanhaven of the [Teleri](../F/Falmari.md)), and [Avallónë](../A/Avallónë.md) on Tol Eressëa.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the rebellion of the Noldor and the [Kinslaying at Alqualondë](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md), [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) led a great part of the Noldor out of Eldamar, north along shorelands scattered with jewels and crystals. His half-brother [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md) marched with him, but ultimately abandoned the northward journey. He returned at last to Eldamar, to rule as King over the few Noldor that remained in Tirion.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Eldamar means* "**[Elvenhome](Elvenhome.md)**" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> although it directly means ("dwelling of the stars"); composed of *[elda](Elda.md)* ("of the stars") and *[már](../M/Már.md)* ("dwelling").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

An early name for the region was <i><a class="mw-disambig" href="Eglamar_(disambiguation).md" title="Eglamar (disambiguation)">Eglamar</a></i>, having the same meaning as *Eldamar* (*Egla* being the [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) form of the later *[Elda](Eldar.md)*).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeleriand_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeleriand-7">[7]</a></sup>

*Eglavar* ("Elfland") is possibly a variant of *Eglamar*.<sup id="cite_ref-secret_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-secret-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The name is a direct translation of the mythological <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lfheimr" title="wikipedia:Álfheimr">Elfhame</a> (lit. "Elf-home") meaning "Elfland, [Faery](../F/Faery.md)".
