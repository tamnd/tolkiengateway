**Eldamar**, also known as **Elendë** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya")) or **[Elvenhome](/wiki/Elvenhome)**, was the land of the [Amanyar](/wiki/Amanyar) in [Aman](/wiki/Aman), which encompassed the [Calacirian](/wiki/Calacirian) (the area around the cleft of [Calacirya](/wiki/Calacirya), including the hill of [Túna](/wiki/T%C3%BAna)), as well as the coastal areas east of the [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri) including the island of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) in the [Bay of Eldamar](/wiki/Bay_of_Eldamar).

The main settlements of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) in this region were [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion) on Túna (where the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) and the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) dwelt together in ancient times), [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB) (the Swanhaven of the [Teleri](/wiki/Falmari "Falmari")), and [Avallónë](/wiki/Avall%C3%B3n%C3%AB) on Tol Eressëa.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the rebellion of the Noldor and the [Kinslaying at Alqualondë](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB), [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) led a great part of the Noldor out of Eldamar, north along shorelands scattered with jewels and crystals. His half-brother [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin) marched with him, but ultimately abandoned the northward journey. He returned at last to Eldamar, to rule as King over the few Noldor that remained in Tirion.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Eldamar means* "**[Elvenhome](/wiki/Elvenhome)**" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> although it directly means ("dwelling of the stars"); composed of *[elda](/wiki/Elda "Elda")* ("of the stars") and *[már](/wiki/M%C3%A1r "Már")* ("dwelling").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

An early name for the region was <i><a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Eglamar_(disambiguation)" title="Eglamar (disambiguation)">Eglamar</a></i>, having the same meaning as *Eldamar* (*Egla* being the [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) form of the later *[Elda](/wiki/Eldar "Eldar")*).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeleriand_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeleriand-7">[7]</a></sup>

*Eglavar* ("Elfland") is possibly a variant of *Eglamar*.<sup id="cite_ref-secret_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-secret-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The name is a direct translation of the mythological <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%81lfheimr" title="wikipedia:Álfheimr">Elfhame</a> (lit. "Elf-home") meaning "Elfland, [Faery](/wiki/Faery)".
