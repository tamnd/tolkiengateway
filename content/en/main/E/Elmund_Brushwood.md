**Elmund Brushwood** is a [man](/wiki/Men_of_Bree "Men of Bree") of [Bree](/wiki/Bree) in *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North)*. He is a smith, and can be found in his forge near the [Prancing Pony](/wiki/Prancing_Pony). He has known several [Rangers](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North"), in particular one named Hador.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
