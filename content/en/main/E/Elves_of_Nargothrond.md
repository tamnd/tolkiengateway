The **Elves of Nargothrond** were those who followed [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md) and dwelt under his rule in the underground citadel of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) on the banks of the River [Narog](../N/Narog.md).

## History {#History}

For the most part of the late [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), the Elves of Nargothrond remained hidden and isolated, waging a guerilla war against the forces of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), ambushing but never assaulting them directly in open warfare. Spies and scouts of Nargothrond guarded [Talath Dirnen](../T/Talath_Dirnen.md), the plains between Nargothrond and [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The realm of Nargothrond extended westward to the [Sea](../S/Sea.md) and the Elves were allied to the [Falathrim](../F/Falathrim.md). With their help they built ships and some of them explored the [Isle of Balar](../I/Isle_of_Balar.md), thinking to prepare it as a last refuge in time of need.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/H/Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/37/Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg/300px-Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="195" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/37/Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg/450px-Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/37/Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg/600px-Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1200" data-file-height="780"/></a><figcaption><i>Aid on the Fens</i> by <a href="../H/Henning_Janssen.md" title="Henning Janssen">Henning Janssen</a></figcaption></figure>

However they also participated in major events of the Age, as during [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), when Finrod and his army came north to stop the onslaught. In that skirmish, they were ambushed at the [Fen of Serech](../F/Fen_of_Serech.md) but a sortie by [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md) rescued them, and allowed them to retreat. Finrod pledged to Barahir for saving him. After the Battle, [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) attacked [Tol Sirion](../T/Tol_Sirion.md), and it was lost thereafter.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In order to repay this pledge, Finrod and a company of ten Elves of Nargothrond participated also in the [Quest for the Silmaril](../Q/Quest_for_the_Silmaril.md) to assist [Beren](../B/Beren.md), Leaving [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) to rule in his place. These Elves were captured by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) in [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](../T/Tol-in-Gaurhoth.md), and were devoured one by one, including Finrod.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

A small army from Nargothrond also joined the [Union of Maedhros](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md) in order to avenge losses of the previous battle, but [Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md)'s premature assault led to the disastrous [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Demise {#Demise}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_Túrin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/21/Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_T%C3%BArin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_T%C3%BArin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="434" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/21/Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_T%C3%BArin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_T%C3%BArin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/21/Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_T%C3%BArin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_T%C3%BArin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1105" data-file-height="1600"/></a><figcaption><i>Sack of Nargothrond</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

After that battle, most of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) was under the power of [Angband](../A/Angband.md) and there was a massive orc-buildup nearby. [Túrin Turambar](../T/Túrin_Turambar.md) came to Nargothrond and gained influence among the Elves. Against the counsels of [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md), he persuaded the forces of Nargothrond to turn into open war against the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md). This led to the disastrous [Battle of Tumhalad](../B/Battle_of_Tumhalad.md) which all but exterminated the army of Nargothrond, and King Orodreth was killed. [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) the Worm led Orcs to the [Sack of Nargothrond](../S/Sack_of_Nargothrond.md) where all women were taken prisoners, and taken by the [Crossings of Teiglin](../C/Crossings_of_Teiglin.md); the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md) of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md) came to their rescue in vain; the Orcs managed to kill all their captives.<sup id="cite_ref-turin_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-turin-6">[6]</a></sup>

Little is known about the fate of any survivors or their descendants: some fled the slaughter, survived the subsequent <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../F/Fell_Winter.md" title="Fell Winter">Fell Winter</a> and sought refuge to [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), bringing the tidings to King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md), and news of Túrin to [Morwen](../M/Morwen.md), his mother who was staying there.<sup id="cite_ref-turin_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-turin-6">[6]</a></sup> Other than that, Nargothrond and its people disappear from history as it remained deserted until the deluge of Beleriand in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> but some members of the [House of Finrod](../H/House_of_Finrod.md) (cf. [Gildor Inglorion](../G/Gildor_Inglorion.md)) lingered in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) until the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, the realm of Nargothrond had not been named by Tolkien, but the inhabitants were known as the *Rothwarin*, later changed to the <i><b>Rodothlim</b></i>. Their name meant "cavern-dwellers".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> The leader of those Elves had always been [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

In [Eriol's](../_/Ælfwine.md) [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) texts, Nargothrond is translated as *Hlydingaburg*, "City of the **Hlydingas**", taking their name from the river [Narog](../N/Narog.md) (OE: *Hlyda* "Noisy");<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> *Hlydingas* would therefore mean \*"Sons of Narog" or \*"Those belonging to Narog".

While writing *[The Lay of Leithian](../T/The_Lay_of_Leithian.md)*, Tolkien used the term **Nargothronders** once in the preliminary summary of [Canto X](../L/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_X.md), but it was not used in the actual poem.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
