The **Elves of Nargothrond** were those who followed [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") and dwelt under his rule in the underground citadel of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) on the banks of the River [Narog](/wiki/Narog).

## History {#History}

For the most part of the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the Elves of Nargothrond remained hidden and isolated, waging a guerilla war against the forces of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), ambushing but never assaulting them directly in open warfare. Spies and scouts of Nargothrond guarded [Talath Dirnen](/wiki/Talath_Dirnen), the plains between Nargothrond and [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The realm of Nargothrond extended westward to the [Sea](/wiki/Sea) and the Elves were allied to the [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim). With their help they built ships and some of them explored the [Isle of Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar), thinking to prepare it as a last refuge in time of need.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/3/37/Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg/300px-Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="195" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/3/37/Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg/450px-Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/3/37/Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg/600px-Henning_Janssen_-_Aid_on_the_Fens.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1200" data-file-height="780"/></a><figcaption><i>Aid on the Fens</i> by <a href="/wiki/Henning_Janssen" title="Henning Janssen">Henning Janssen</a></figcaption></figure>

However they also participated in major events of the Age, as during [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), when Finrod and his army came north to stop the onslaught. In that skirmish, they were ambushed at the [Fen of Serech](/wiki/Fen_of_Serech) but a sortie by [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir) rescued them, and allowed them to retreat. Finrod pledged to Barahir for saving him. After the Battle, [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) attacked [Tol Sirion](/wiki/Tol_Sirion), and it was lost thereafter.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In order to repay this pledge, Finrod and a company of ten Elves of Nargothrond participated also in the [Quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril) to assist [Beren](/wiki/Beren), Leaving [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) to rule in his place. These Elves were captured by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth), and were devoured one by one, including Finrod.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

A small army from Nargothrond also joined the [Union of Maedhros](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros) in order to avenge losses of the previous battle, but [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor)'s premature assault led to the disastrous [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Demise {#Demise}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_T%C3%BArin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/2/21/Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_T%C3%BArin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_T%C3%BArin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="434" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/2/21/Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_T%C3%BArin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_T%C3%BArin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/2/21/Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_T%C3%BArin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Finduilas_is_Led_Past_T%C3%BArin_at_the_Sack_of_Nargothrond.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1105" data-file-height="1600"/></a><figcaption><i>Sack of Nargothrond</i> by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

After that battle, most of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) was under the power of [Angband](/wiki/Angband) and there was a massive orc-buildup nearby. [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_Turambar) came to Nargothrond and gained influence among the Elves. Against the counsels of [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo), he persuaded the forces of Nargothrond to turn into open war against the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). This led to the disastrous [Battle of Tumhalad](/wiki/Battle_of_Tumhalad) which all but exterminated the army of Nargothrond, and King Orodreth was killed. [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) the Worm led Orcs to the [Sack of Nargothrond](/wiki/Sack_of_Nargothrond) where all women were taken prisoners, and taken by the [Crossings of Teiglin](/wiki/Crossings_of_Teiglin); the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) came to their rescue in vain; the Orcs managed to kill all their captives.<sup id="cite_ref-turin_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-turin-6">[6]</a></sup>

Little is known about the fate of any survivors or their descendants: some fled the slaughter, survived the subsequent <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Fell_Winter" title="Fell Winter">Fell Winter</a> and sought refuge to [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), bringing the tidings to King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol), and news of Túrin to [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen), his mother who was staying there.<sup id="cite_ref-turin_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-turin-6">[6]</a></sup> Other than that, Nargothrond and its people disappear from history as it remained deserted until the deluge of Beleriand in the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> but some members of the [House of Finrod](/wiki/House_of_Finrod) (cf. [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor_Inglorion)) lingered in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) until the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the realm of Nargothrond had not been named by Tolkien, but the inhabitants were known as the *Rothwarin*, later changed to the <i><b>Rodothlim</b></i>. Their name meant "cavern-dwellers".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> The leader of those Elves had always been [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

In [Eriol's](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine "Ælfwine") [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) texts, Nargothrond is translated as *Hlydingaburg*, "City of the **Hlydingas**", taking their name from the river [Narog](/wiki/Narog) (OE: *Hlyda* "Noisy");<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> *Hlydingas* would therefore mean \*"Sons of Narog" or \*"Those belonging to Narog".

While writing *[The Lay of Leithian](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Leithian)*, Tolkien used the term **Nargothronders** once in the preliminary summary of [Canto X](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_X "Lay of Leithian Canto X"), but it was not used in the actual poem.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
