**Everard Took** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Everard was the youngest son of [Adelard Took](/wiki/Adelard_Took).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> At [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001"), he danced the [Springle-ring](/wiki/Springle-ring) with [Melilot Brandybuck](/wiki/Melilot_Brandybuck) when he thought the Speech was over.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning is "boar-strong" (*ever* + *hard*).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Everard's name was originally 'Prospero' until [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s sixth draft version of [A Long-expected Party](/wiki/A_Long-expected_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
