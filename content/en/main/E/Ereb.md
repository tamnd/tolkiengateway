<i><b>ereb</b></i> (pl. *erib*) means *lonely* or *isolated* in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From the [Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=ER&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ER (page does not exist)">ER</a> plus the adjectival ending <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=-eb&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-eb (page does not exist)">-eb</a>.

It's also possible that it derives from [Old Sindarin](../O/Old_Sindarin.md) full word \**eripa* and this from [Primitive Elvish](../P/Primitive_Elvish.md) \**erikwâ*

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="../A/Amon_Ereb.md" title="Amon Ereb">Amon <b>Ereb</b></a></i> = "Lonely Hill"
- <i><a href="../B/Bar-erib.md" title="Bar-erib">Bar <b>Erib</b></a></i> = "Home of the Lonely Ones"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="Erebor.md" title="Erebor"><b>Ereb</b>or</a></i> = "Lonely Mountain"
