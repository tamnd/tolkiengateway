<i><b>ereb</b></i> (pl. *erib*) means *lonely* or *isolated* in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From the [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=ER&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ER (page does not exist)">ER</a> plus the adjectival ending <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-eb&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-eb (page does not exist)">-eb</a>.

It's also possible that it derives from [Old Sindarin](/wiki/Old_Sindarin) full word \**eripa* and this from [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish) \**erikwâ*

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Amon_Ereb" title="Amon Ereb">Amon <b>Ereb</b></a></i> = "Lonely Hill"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Bar-erib" title="Bar-erib">Bar <b>Erib</b></a></i> = "Home of the Lonely Ones"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Erebor" title="Erebor"><b>Ereb</b>or</a></i> = "Lonely Mountain"
