The **Engagement Tablet** was a tablet in [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) used by [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) to mark down the dates of future engagements. He did not remember the dates of his engagements well unless they were on the Tablet. When Bilbo invited the [Wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards") [Gandalf the Grey](/wiki/Gandalf "Gandalf") for tea, he was too flustered to remember to inscribe it on the tablet and nearly forgot.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
