The **Engagement Tablet** was a tablet in [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) used by [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) to mark down the dates of future engagements. He did not remember the dates of his engagements well unless they were on the Tablet. When Bilbo invited the [Wizard](../W/Wizards.md) [Gandalf the Grey](../G/Gandalf.md) for tea, he was too flustered to remember to inscribe it on the tablet and nearly forgot.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
