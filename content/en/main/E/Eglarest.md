**Eglarest** was the southern of the two [Havens of the Falas](../H/Havens_of_the_Falas.md) that lay at the mouth of the River [Nenning](../N/Nenning.md) on the western coasts of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). The northern haven was called [Brithombar](../B/Brithombar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> They were founded during the [Years of the Trees](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) by [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md). Upon the cape west of Eglarest [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) had built [Barad Nimras](../B/Barad_Nimras.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The Havens lasted until they were taken by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [473](../F/First_Age_473.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It has been suggested that the name means "Ravine of the [Eglain](Eglath.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Cf. the [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) word *rhest*<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and [Imladris](../I/Imladris.md).

## Other names {#Other_names}

An earlier name for this city is *Eldorest*, which takes its name from the river *Eldor* whose mouth is located near the city. This name appears on the Westward extension of [The First 'Silmarillion' Map](../T/The_First_'Silmarillion'_Map.md)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>.
