**Eglarest** was the southern of the two [Havens of the Falas](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas) that lay at the mouth of the River [Nenning](/wiki/Nenning) on the western coasts of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). The northern haven was called [Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> They were founded during the [Years of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees) of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) by [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan). Upon the cape west of Eglarest [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) had built [Barad Nimras](/wiki/Barad_Nimras).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The Havens lasted until they were taken by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [473](/wiki/First_Age_473 "First Age 473").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It has been suggested that the name means "Ravine of the [Eglain](/wiki/Eglath "Eglath")".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Cf. the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word *rhest*<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and [Imladris](/wiki/Imladris).

## Other names {#Other_names}

An earlier name for this city is *Eldorest*, which takes its name from the river *Eldor* whose mouth is located near the city. This name appears on the Westward extension of [The First 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_First_%27Silmarillion%27_Map)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>.
