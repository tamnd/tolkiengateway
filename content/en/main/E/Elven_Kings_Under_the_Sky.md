**Elven Kings Under the Sky** is the ninth episode of [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios)'s television series, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*. It is the first episode of the second season and it was released on [29 August](/wiki/29_August), [2024](/wiki/2024).
