In the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*, **Eärcaraxë** the “Sea-fang” was a [Sea-serpent](../S/Sea-serpents.md) who hunted in Úlond, the waters near the coasts of [Andrast](../A/Andrast.md).

Less aggressive than other dragons, her hunts nonetheless meant the doom of many [Corsair](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md) ships, merchant ships and [Swan-ships](../S/Swan-ships.md). Sea-farers considered her to be restless out of loneliness.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She was known to sometimes rescue survivors of her hunts, keeping them as pets to talk to, until she grew tired of them.

Eärcaraxë's lair was located beneath a small island in Úlond.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
