**Ebor** was the chief henchman of [Manthor](/wiki/Manthor), later appointed by him as the Captain of the Guards at the [Ford of Brithiach](/wiki/Ford_of_Brithiach).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Ebor revered his lord, and followed his example in spite of [Hardang](/wiki/Hardang)'s command, as when he returned gear and weapons to [Asgon](/wiki/Asgon)'s company.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
