The **Ettenmoors** were a high barren<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup> region that lay on the western side of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) north of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and south of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> The river [Hoarwell](/wiki/Hoarwell) flowed from its sources in the Ettenmoors<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-3">[3]</a></sup> to the southwest.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Ettenmoors were also called the **troll-fells**,<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-3">[3]</a></sup> probably because the region was inhabited by [Trolls](/wiki/Trolls).<sup id="cite_ref-NM_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-4">[4]</a></sup> The Ettenmoors included the land of the [Ettendales](/wiki/Ettendales), which consisted of valleys reaching up into the foothills of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The realm of [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur) lay between the Ettenmoors in the north and the [Angle](/wiki/Angle) between the rivers [Hoarwell](/wiki/Hoarwell) and [Loudwater](/wiki/Bruinen "Bruinen") in the south.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-6">[6]</a></sup> It is speculated that [Mount Gram](/wiki/Mount_Gram), from where a host of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) [attacked](/wiki/Battle_of_Greenfields "Battle of Greenfields") [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), was located in the Ettenmoors.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Since the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days), [Trolls](/wiki/Trolls) concentrated especially around the Ettenmoors, and they increased during the rule of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar), that was founded in the north.<sup id="cite_ref-NM_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320") when the realm of Arnor was founded by the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the Ettenmoors probably became part of Arnor.

When the kingdom of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) split into three successor kingdoms after the death of King [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(King_of_Arnor)> "Eärendur (King of Arnor)") in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [861](/wiki/Third_Age_861 "Third Age 861"),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> the Ettenmoors probably became a part of the kingdom of Rhudaur.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-6">[6]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975") after his defeat in the [Battle of Fornost](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost)<sup id="cite_ref-TA1975_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1975-10">[10]</a></sup> the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) fled with the remains of his troops to the north to reach his realm of Angmar and the shelter of [Carn Dûm](/wiki/Carn_D%C3%BBm),<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-11">[11]</a></sup> but was pursued to the Ettenmoors<sup id="cite_ref-TA1975_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1975-10">[10]</a></sup> where he was overtaken by the cavalry of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and a force from Rivendell led by Glorfindel.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-11">[11]</a></sup> After the fall of Angmar, the Trolls retreated to the east of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) but returned in the 28th century and even expanded westwards as far as the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs).<sup id="cite_ref-NM_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-4">[4]</a></sup>

It is possible that [Chieftain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "Chieftains of the Dúnedain") [Arador](/wiki/Arador) was slain by trolls in this area<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> in [2930](/wiki/Third_Age_2930 "Third Age 2930").<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

Around [November](/wiki/November) or [December](/wiki/December)<sup id="cite_ref-South_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-14">[14]</a></sup> [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"),<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> scouts from [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) investigated the Ettenmoors for any activities concerning [Ringwraiths](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") or other servants of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), but they came with no warning news.<sup id="cite_ref-South_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-14">[14]</a></sup>

In October [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) after his battle with the Ringwraiths at [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop) [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) fled riding on [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax) to the north in the hope of drawing off some of the Ringwraiths. He had to send Shadowfax away in the Ettenmoors, because he was not able to ride among the rocks there.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-16">[16]</a></sup>

The name *Ettenmoors* consists of *[etten](/wiki/Etten "Etten")* and *moor* ("high barren land").<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup>

"Fells" means hills or moorland, thus the "troll-fells" were hills in which [trolls](/wiki/Trolls "Trolls") lived.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-17">[17]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the "first phase" of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, map sketches label the source of Hoarwell as [Dimrill Dale](/wiki/Dimrill_Dale)(s), but when the name was displaced, the region was renamed Hoardale, changed to "Entish Dales" or "Entish Lands". This was before [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) conceived the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) of *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)* and the adjective "entish" directly refers to Old English *eōten* "giant"<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> (see also [Etymology](#Etymology) section).

Later Tolkien decided to change it into "Trollfells" (capitalized without a hyphen): he wrote on a map: "Alter Entish Lands to \[Trollfells &gt; Bergrisland &gt;\] Ettenmoor". This is also the first mention of Ettenmoor(s) in the legendarium.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The name *Ettenmoors* is similar to the land of <a class="external text" href="http://narnia.wikia.com/wiki/Ettinsmoor" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ettinsmoor</a> of [Narnia](/wiki/The_Chronicles_of_Narnia "The Chronicles of Narnia") by [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis). In *The Silver Chair* Lewis mentions that it is inhabited by <a class="external text" href="http://narnia.wikia.com/wiki/Ettin" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ettins</a>, giants who often play the game of Cockshies, throwing rocks at a gorge, much like the [stone giants](/wiki/Giants "Giants") in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.

<dl><dd>The Ettenmoors are the homeland of the <i><a href="/wiki/Etten" title="Etten">Ettens</a></i> who resemble <a href="/wiki/Hill-trolls" title="Hill-trolls">Hill-trolls</a>, but they possess &#34;not one but two long heads&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Ettenmoors is the region where Player vs Monster Player play takes place, pitting the &#34;Army of <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>&#34; against the &#34;Coldfells Army&#34; of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Free_Peoples" title="Free Peoples">Free Peoples</a>. The two factions each have a home base which cannot be entered by the opposing side and a secondary base which can be assailed but not captured. Between them are five major control points: the keeps of &#34;Lugazag&#34;, &#34;Tirith Rhaw&#34;, and &#34;Tol Ascarnen&#34;, the &#34;Isendeep Mine&#34;, and &#34;Grimwood Lumber Camp&#34;; as well as several smaller outposts, all of which can be captured by either side.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Other sites of interest include the <a href="/wiki/Hobbits" title="Hobbits">Hobbit</a> village &#34;Hoarhallow&#34;, its <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Goblin</a> counterpart, &#34;Grothum&#34;, as well as dens of <a href="/wiki/Bears" title="Bears">bears</a>, <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Eagles" title="Eagles">eagles</a>, <a href="/wiki/Dragons" title="Dragons">drakes</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wargs" title="Wargs">Wargs</a>, and other beasts that align with one side or are hostile to all.</dd></dl>
