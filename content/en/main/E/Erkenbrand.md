**Erkenbrand** was one of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md). He was the Lord of [Deeping-coomb](../D/Deeping-coomb.md) and of much other land in the [Westfold](../W/Westfold.md) during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

## History {#History}

Erkenbrand had been an officer in the [King's Riders](../K/King's_Company.md) in his youth. At the time of the War of the Ring Erkenbrand was Lord of Deeping-coomb and much other land in the Westfold. As such, he was the chief lord in the [West-mark](../W/West-mark.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He was the uncle of [Dúnhere](../D/Dúnhere.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UTnote13_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTnote13-1">[1]</a></sup>

Erkendbrand was in the [Hornburg](../H/Hornburg.md) during the [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](../F/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md). When news of the death of [Théodred](../T/Théodred.md) in the First Battle of the Fords of Isen on [25 February](../_/25_February.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) reached Erkenbrand the next day, he assumed command of the West-mark. Erkenbrand sent errand-riders to [Edoras](Edoras.md) to inform King [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) about the death of Théodred and to request [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) and all forces that could be spared as reinforcements. However, [Gríma](../G/Gríma.md) delayed any actions until he was defeated by [Gandalf.](../G/Gandalf.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> He gave the command in the field to [Grimbold](../G/Grimbold.md) until he could come himself to the battlefield. Erkenbrand did not immediately go to the battlefield due to the fact that he was occupied for three days gathering all those able to bear arms and stores of food to defend the Hornburg.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

On [2 March](../_/2_March.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> the [Second Battle of the Fords of Isen](../S/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md) was fought, and the defences on the [Isen](../I/Isen.md) were driven back by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md)'s forces. The survivors, led by Grimbold and [Elfhelm](Elfhelm.md), were retreating from the fords of the river Isen when they came across Gandalf. Gandalf told them Théoden sought the refuge of the Hornburg, and that they should join Erkenbrand to attack the besiegers.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> They arrived with a thousand men at dawn of [March 4](../_/4_March.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) and turned the tide of the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-7">[7]</a></sup>

Erkenbrand did not come to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) to fight in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md). King Théoden gave him the command of the forces that were left behind to defend Rohan because of Erkenbrand's dignity and authority.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

After the funeral of King Théoden, which took place on 10 August, [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) , King Éomer appointed Erkenbrand to the post of [Marshal of the West-mark](../M/Marshal_of_the_West-mark.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Appearance {#Appearance}

Erkenbrand was said to have been tall and strong with a red shield and a great black horn. King Théoden said that the valour of [Helm Hammerhand](../H/Helm.md) lived again in Erkenbrand.<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Erkenbrand* is a name that possibly was derived from two [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) words: *eorcan* ("precious") and *brand* ("fire-brand, torch; sword").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
