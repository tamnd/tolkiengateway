**Eämbar** was a ship built by [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion) for his dwelling-place, on which was the Guildhouse of the [Venturers](/wiki/Guild_of_Venturers "Guild of Venturers").

## History {#History}

Eämbar was a massive ship, designed to be more of a dwelling-place than a sailing vessel. Originally anchored off the island of [Tol Uinen](/wiki/Tol_Uinen) in the bay of [Rómenna](/wiki/R%C3%B3menna), it was later moved to the ports of [Andúnië](/wiki/And%C3%BAni%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> At some later date the ship was confiscated by the Kings and all of its records (including the accurate charts of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor)) perished in the Downfall.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eämbar* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name meaning "Sea-dwelling",<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> from *[eär](/wiki/E%C3%A4r "Eär")* ("sea") + *[-mbar](/wiki/-mbar)* ("home").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
