**ea** is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) verb meaning "to exist".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is said to be spoken by [Eru](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar "Ilúvatar") creating and giving name to the universe, named after this utterance, [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4).

It is not known if the word comes from Him, or if it is mentioned symbolically.

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

- *ea* "let it be" (imperative), spoken by Eru to create the universe.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- *euva* "will be" (future)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- *enge* "existed" (past)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- *éye/engie* "had been" (perfect)
- *[eala](/wiki/%C3%8Balar "Ëalar")* "being" (present participle)
