**Emyn Arnen** was a cluster of hills located within [South Ithilien](/wiki/South_Ithilien) across the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).

## Description {#Description}

The hills, being an outlier of the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath),<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup> were situated south of [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath), on the east bank of the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), which made a loop around their feet.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Old records preserved in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) indicate that *Arnen* was once used for the larger part of the region later known as Ithilien.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

Emyn Arnen was home to a family of [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") nobles, and from them came [Húrin of Emyn Arnen](/wiki/H%C3%BArin_of_Emyn_Arnen), who became steward to King [Minardil](/wiki/Minardil) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and later founded the line of the [House of Húrin](/wiki/House_of_H%C3%BArin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the events of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) son of Steward [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor") became the [Prince of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien)<sup id="cite_ref-RK_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-4">[4]</a></sup> and the [Lord of Emyn Arnen](/wiki/Lord_of_Emyn_Arnen),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> which became the dwelling-place of the [White Company](/wiki/White_Company).<sup id="cite_ref-RK_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-4">[4]</a></sup>

Many years into the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), [Pen-arduin](/wiki/Pen-arduin), the home of [Borlas](/wiki/Borlas), was located within Emyn Arnen.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Emyn Arnen* translates as "the hills of Arnen" (cf. *[emyn](/wiki/Amon "Amon")*). One historian of Gondor claimed that *Arnen* was likely a word invented by the Númenórean settlers of Gondor, intended to resemble an Elvish tongue (but wrongly mixing [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[ar-](/wiki/Ar "Ar")* "beside" with [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[nen](/wiki/Nen "Nen")* "water"). Other historians derived *Arnen* from an unattested [pre-Númenórean](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean "Pre-Númenórean") word *arn-* ("rock"), although this was based on pure guessing.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Appearance {#Appearance}

The cartographic representation of Emyn Arnen has changed between the [General Map of Middle-earth](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth) published in the *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, where the hills are depicted in clear black lines, and [the map](/wiki/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age "The West of Middle-earth at the End of the Third Age") of *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*, where the hills are depicted as a dark silhouette with three peaks.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name *Emyn Arnen* was originally written as **Haramon**<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and the **Hills of Haramon**,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> though this was replaced by an illegible word before becoming *Emyn Arnen*.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
