**Emyn Arnen** was a cluster of hills located within [South Ithilien](../S/South_Ithilien.md) across the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) from [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).

## Description {#Description}

The hills, being an outlier of the [Ephel Dúath](Ephel_Dúath.md),<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup> were situated south of [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md), on the east bank of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), which made a loop around their feet.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Old records preserved in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) indicate that *Arnen* was once used for the larger part of the region later known as Ithilien.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

Emyn Arnen was home to a family of [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) nobles, and from them came [Húrin of Emyn Arnen](../H/Húrin_of_Emyn_Arnen.md), who became steward to King [Minardil](../M/Minardil.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and later founded the line of the [House of Húrin](../H/House_of_Húrin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the events of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) son of Steward [Denethor II](../D/Denethor.md) became the [Prince of Ithilien](../P/Prince_of_Ithilien.md)<sup id="cite_ref-RK_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-4">[4]</a></sup> and the [Lord of Emyn Arnen](../L/Lord_of_Emyn_Arnen.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> which became the dwelling-place of the [White Company](../W/White_Company.md).<sup id="cite_ref-RK_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-4">[4]</a></sup>

Many years into the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md), [Pen-arduin](../P/Pen-arduin.md), the home of [Borlas](../B/Borlas.md), was located within Emyn Arnen.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Emyn Arnen* translates as "the hills of Arnen" (cf. *[emyn](../A/Amon.md)*). One historian of Gondor claimed that *Arnen* was likely a word invented by the Númenórean settlers of Gondor, intended to resemble an Elvish tongue (but wrongly mixing [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[ar-](../A/Ar.md)* "beside" with [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *[nen](../N/Nen.md)* "water"). Other historians derived *Arnen* from an unattested [pre-Númenórean](../P/Pre-Númenórean.md) word *arn-* ("rock"), although this was based on pure guessing.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Appearance {#Appearance}

The cartographic representation of Emyn Arnen has changed between the [General Map of Middle-earth](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md) published in the *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, where the hills are depicted in clear black lines, and [the map](../T/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age.md) of *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*, where the hills are depicted as a dark silhouette with three peaks.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name *Emyn Arnen* was originally written as **Haramon**<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and the **Hills of Haramon**,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> though this was replaced by an illegible word before becoming *Emyn Arnen*.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
