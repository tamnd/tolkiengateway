**Emily Austin** is an American artist who paints scenes from Tolkien’s stories.

She made cover art for the books *The Inklings and King Arthur* by [Sørina Higgins](../S/Sørina_Higgins.md) and *Why We Love Middle-earth* by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Shawn_Marchese&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Shawn Marchese (page does not exist)">Shawn Marchese</a> and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Alan_Sisto&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alan Sisto (page does not exist)">Alan Sisto</a> (founders of [The Prancing Pony Podcast](../T/The_Prancing_Pony_Podcast.md)).

Her illustrations "The Yellow Door" and "The Vale of Rivendell" were featured in [Tolkien Calendar 2023](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2023.md).
