**Elfhild** was the wife of King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden).

## History {#History}

Elfhild died in childbirth, leaving Théoden a widower and [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred) motherless. At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Rohan had been without a queen for forty years.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
