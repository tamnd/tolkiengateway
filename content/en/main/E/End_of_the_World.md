<i><b>End of the World</b></i> is the title of an illustration by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-AI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

*End of the World* shows an apparently carefree man stepping out from a cliff into an abyss. Beyond the cliff are the [Sun](/wiki/Sun), the [Moon](/wiki/Moon), and [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars") — elements which are pivotal in Tolkien's [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"). The illustration was made in December [1912](/wiki/1912), and was collected by Tolkien with other illustrations from the same period in an envelope labelled "[Earliest Ishnesses](/wiki/The_Book_of_Ishness "The Book of Ishness")".<sup id="cite_ref-AI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

While explicitly claiming no direct link between the two illustrations, [Dimitra Fimi](/wiki/Dimitra_Fimi) has noted the resemblance of Tolkien's *End of the World* to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fool_(Tarot_card)" title="wikipedia:The Fool (Tarot card)">The Fool</a> from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rider-Waite_tarot_deck" title="wikipedia:Rider-Waite tarot deck">Rider-Waite tarot deck</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) have noted that Tolkien's technique in this illustrations has produced a "restless sky drawn as if by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_van_Gogh" title="wikipedia:Vincent van Gogh">Van Gogh</a>".<sup id="cite_ref-AI_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
