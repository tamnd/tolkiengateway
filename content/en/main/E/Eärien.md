**Eärien** was the daughter of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), thus sister to [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur), and an apprentice of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Builder%27s_Guild&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Builder&#39;s Guild (page does not exist)">Builder&#39;s Guild</a> in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). Eärien was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) and is portrayed by [Ema Horvath](/wiki/Ema_Horvath).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Eärien* ("daughter of the sea") is made from the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word *[eär](/wiki/E%C3%A4r "Eär")* ("sea"), and the ending *[-ien](/wiki/-ien)* ("daughter").
