**Eärendur** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") "sea-servant", pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌe.aˈrendur]</span>) was the second son of [Tar-Amandil](/wiki/Tar-Amandil), the third [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor). His brother would take up the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre) as [Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Eärendur is best known by a historical event that did *not* happen. Tar-Elendil's grandson failed to produce a male heir and according to the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans)' law of <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnatic_primogeniture" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">agnatic primogeniture</a> the Sceptre should have passed to Eärendur's grandson [Malantur](/wiki/Malantur). However, the law was abruptly changed so that the eldest *child* would become Ruler of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). This allowed a female to take the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Sceptre of Númenor"), which led to [Tar-Ancalimë](/wiki/Tar-Ancalim%C3%AB) becoming the nation's first [Ruling Queen](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Ruling Queen of Númenor").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Thus, had events played out differently, Eärendur's descendants would have become the royal line of Númenor.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eärendur* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. It means "(professional) mariner".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> As Paul Strack explains, a more literal translation might be "Servant of the Sea". It is a compound of [eär](/wiki/E%C3%A4r "Eär") ("sea") and [-(n)dur](/wiki/-ndur "-ndur") ("servant").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
