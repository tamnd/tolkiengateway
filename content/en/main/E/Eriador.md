**Eriador** was the large region in the north-west of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), defined between [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) and the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) to the west and [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) and the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) to the east.

It was inhabited by all the [Free peoples](/wiki/Free_peoples) of Middle-earth, being the location of many of the most important events of the [Second](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") and [Third](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") Ages. By the end of the Third Age, its main inhabitants were [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and [Men](/wiki/Men) of the surrounding lands.

## History {#History}

### First Age {#First_Age}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a href="/wiki/Great_Journey" title="Great Journey">Great Journey</a></div></div>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r398211">.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote{position:relative;margin:0.5rem 0;padding:0.75rem 1rem;box-shadow:var(--tg-border-color-base)0px 0px 0px 1px inset;border-radius:var(--tg-border-radius-medium);background:var(--tg-color-surface-2);font-size:var(--tg-font-size-small);line-height:var(--tg-line-height-xx-small);display:flex;gap:0.5rem;align-items:center}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon{display:flex;align-items:center}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon .mw-file-element{display:block}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon .mw-file-element[src$=".svg"]{filter:var(--filter-invert);opacity:0.5}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--red{background:var(--background-color-destructive-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--yellow{background:var(--background-color-warning-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--green{background:var(--background-color-success-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}</style>

The [Three Kindreds](/wiki/Three_Kindreds) of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) passed through Eriador on their way to [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), to make the trip to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). First came the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar), followed closely by the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), but the third kindred, the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri), tarried and were only urged forward at the behest of their lord, [Elwë](/wiki/Elw%C3%AB_Singollo "Elwë Singollo"), who was keen to return to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm-Captive_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-Captive-2">[2]</a></sup> Later, some of the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor) who remained behind eventually passed into Eriador, and dwelt there for a time, before [Denethor](</wiki/Denethor_(Nando)> "Denethor (Nando)") gathered as many of the Nandor as possible and they removed to [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand) to become the [Laiquendi](/wiki/Nandor#Lenwë_and_Denethor "Nandor").<sup id="cite_ref-Silm-Captive_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-Captive-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Silm-Sindar_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-Sindar-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [310](/wiki/First_Age_310 "First Age 310"), the first [Men](/wiki/Men), tribes of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) (ancestors of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) and [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador)) traversed Eriador. Many sub-tribes of both peoples stayed behind, and when the shrinking teams (now led by [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor) and [Marach](/wiki/Marach)) reached [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), those who stayed behind occupied many parts of Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-WOTJ_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WOTJ-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-DM_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DM-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Bëorians were discovered by [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod"). These Men quickly made friendship with the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) and entered into their service, but [Bereg](/wiki/Bereg), great-grandson of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor), was discontent and led those of like mind with himself back over the [mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains "Blue Mountains") into Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-Silm-Men_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-Men-6">[6]</a></sup>

Men were still drawn westwards; also living in Eriador during the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) were men known as [Swarthy Men](/wiki/Swarthy_Men), who were led by [Bór](/wiki/B%C3%B3r) and [Ulfang](/wiki/Ulfang_the_Black "Ulfang the Black") into Beleriand ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [463](/wiki/First_Age_463 "First Age 463")), and they met with the Eldar.<sup id="cite_ref-Silm-Fingolfin_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-Fingolfin-7">[7]</a></sup>

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

#### Elves and Númenóreans {#Elves_and_Númenóreans}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans#Dominion_over_the_Men_of_Middle-earth" title="Númenóreans">Númenóreans#Dominion over the Men of Middle-earth</a></div></div>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r398211"/>

Following the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) and the destruction of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), many [Elves](/wiki/Elves) left for [Aman](/wiki/Aman) whilst the remnants of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) removed to live on the newly-formed isle of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). The remaining Elves moved to [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), and in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750") the remnants of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) established the realm of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) in the south-east of Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-App-B1_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B1-8">[8]</a></sup>

In the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) crossed into Eriador with many [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) in their following, together with [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) and [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves); and for a while they dwelt in the country about [Lake Nenuial](/wiki/Lake_Nenuial) ruling the Eldar in Eriador, including the wandering companies of the native [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor).<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[9]</a></sup> Descendants of the Edain also dwelt there, side by side to those Elves. These Men also occupied the [Weather Hills](/wiki/Weather_Hills) and the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs).<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UT-Wife_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-Wife-10">[10]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [600](/wiki/Second_Age_600 "Second Age 600"), the first [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) ships began to visit [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and when news of this reached the [Men](/wiki/Men) of Eriador they were amazed and scared. On the [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills) the Númenóreans met with just twelve [Men](/wiki/Men) from Eriador and both sides recognised their ancient kinship: the Númenóreans discovered that these people lived in the hills east of the [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin) and were likely the descendants of the people of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor) and [Hador](/wiki/Hador) who never crossed the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-UT-Wife_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-Wife-10">[10]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Felix_Sotomayor_-_Ost-in-Edhil_cropped.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/3/32/Felix_Sotomayor_-_Ost-in-Edhil_cropped.jpg/300px-Felix_Sotomayor_-_Ost-in-Edhil_cropped.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="236" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/3/32/Felix_Sotomayor_-_Ost-in-Edhil_cropped.jpg/450px-Felix_Sotomayor_-_Ost-in-Edhil_cropped.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/3/32/Felix_Sotomayor_-_Ost-in-Edhil_cropped.jpg/600px-Felix_Sotomayor_-_Ost-in-Edhil_cropped.jpg 2x" data-file-width="875" data-file-height="689"/></a><figcaption><i>Ost-in-Edhil</i> by <a href="/wiki/Felix_Sotomayor" title="Felix Sotomayor">Felix Sotomayor</a></figcaption></figure>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [725](/wiki/Second_Age_725 "Second Age 725") [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") first visited [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) and Eriador and began a long-lasting friendship with [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad). He made many voyages to Middle-earth and established the haven of [Vinyalondë](/wiki/Vinyalond%C3%AB) (later known as [Lond Daer](/wiki/Lond_Daer)) on the banks of the river [Gwathir](/wiki/Gwathir), a move which proved crucial in defeating Sauron later in the Second Age.<sup id="cite_ref-UT-Wife_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-Wife-10">[10]</a></sup> As a mariner, Aldarion had a great desire to build ships and to do this he began deforesting much of the [Minhiriath](/wiki/Minhiriath) and [Enedwaith](/wiki/Enedwaith); this angered the native forest-dwelling Men who subsequently fled to the forest of [Eryn Vorn](/wiki/Eryn_Vorn).<sup id="cite_ref-UT-Galadriel_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-Galadriel-11">[11]</a></sup>

#### War with Sauron {#War_with_Sauron}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a href="/wiki/Of_the_Rings_of_Power_and_the_Third_Age" title="Of the Rings of Power and the Third Age">Of the Rings of Power and the Third Age</a></div></div>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r398211"/>

The Noldor of Eregion grew in unparalleled friendship with the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"), to the benefit of both peoples, and there was much traffic between the Elven city of [Ost-in-Edhil](/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil) and the [West-gate](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin "Doors of Durin") of Moria. In his attempt to corrupt them, [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) taught them how to create the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm-Rings_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-Rings-12">[12]</a></sup>

The Elves realised they had been tricked and they hid their Rings from him; Sauron, however, demanded that all the Rings be given to him as they were created with his help. When the Elves refused Sauron was vengeful and in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1693](/wiki/Second_Age_1693 "Second Age 1693") began the [War of the Elves and Sauron](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron) with an invasion of Eriador in [1695](/wiki/Second_Age_1695 "Second Age 1695").<sup id="cite_ref-Silm-Rings_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-Rings-12">[12]</a></sup> [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) was sent by [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) with a force to aid [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor), but all Elrond could do was to retreat with some survivors northwards.<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[9]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/7c/Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg/300px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="233" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/7/7c/Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg/450px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/7/7c/Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg/600px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="792" data-file-height="616"/></a><figcaption><i>Death of Celebrimbor</i> by Abe Papakhian</figcaption></figure>

In [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697"), with Eregion lain waste, Elrond founded the refuge of [Imladris](/wiki/Rivendell "Rivendell") as a defence against Sauron, and was joined by Men and Elves, swelling his forces; Sauron had taken over all of Eriador by [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1699](/wiki/Second_Age_1699 "Second Age 1699"), once he drove the Dwarves back into Khazad-dûm and the gates were shut. While Sauron sent most of his army west to attack [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) he had to leave a strong detachment behind to contain Elrond<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[9]</a></sup> and [besieged Imladris](/wiki/First_Siege_of_Imladris "First Siege of Imladris").<sup id="cite_ref-App-B1_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B1-8">[8]</a></sup>

[Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) sent word to [King](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") [Tar-Minastir](/wiki/Tar-Minastir) of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) calling for aid, but when the ships finally arrived in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1700](/wiki/Second_Age_1700 "Second Age 1700") (landing in Lindon and [Vinyalondë](/wiki/Vinyalond%C3%AB)) Sauron was already preparing his invasion of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon). The forces of [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and [Ciryatur](/wiki/Ciryatur) caused heavy losses at [Sarn Ford](/wiki/Sarn_Ford) before Sauron's forces were utterly destroyed at the [Battle of the Gwathló](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Gwathl%C3%B3), where Sauron barely escaped with his life. However, Eriador lay in ruins as many of the native [Men](/wiki/Men), Elves and Númenóreans had been killed by Sauron and his forces had ravaged the lands and destroyed many of the remaining forests.<sup id="cite_ref-UT-Galadriel_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-Galadriel-11">[11]</a></sup>

The Elven lords decided in a Council that Imladris should become the new Elvish stronghold in eastern Eriador after the destruction of Eregion; Gil-galad also bestowed the ring [Vilya](/wiki/Vilya) upon Elrond and made him his vice-regent.<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[9]</a></sup>

#### Last Alliance {#Last_Alliance}

Throughout the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) gradually increased their power in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) by creating coastal dominions; they eventually had the strength to confront [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and take him prisoner to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). Whilst there, Sauron seduced the [King](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") and most of the people, and persuaded [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n) to break the [Ban of the Valar](/wiki/Ban_of_the_Valar), causing [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) to [change the shape](/wiki/Changing_of_the_World "Changing of the World") of the [World](/wiki/Arda "Arda"), destroying Númenor in the process.<sup id="cite_ref-Silm-Akallabeth_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-Akallabeth-13">[13]</a></sup> The [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful), those Númenóreans who remained true to the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and friendly with the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), were spared, and in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320") [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) established the two [Realms in Exile](/wiki/Realms_in_Exile): [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in the south and [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) in Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-App-B1_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B1-8">[8]</a></sup>

Sauron, now unable to take fair form,<sup id="cite_ref-App-Numenor_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Numenor-14">[14]</a></sup> returned to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3429](/wiki/Second_Age_3429 "Second Age 3429") attacked [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Morgul "Minas Morgul"), burning the [White Tree](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor "White Tree of Gondor").<sup id="cite_ref-App-B1_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B1-8">[8]</a></sup> [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) son of Elendil fled north to join his father in Arnor, who took counsel with [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), for both foresaw the need to triumph over Sauron before his power grew unstoppable: in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3430](/wiki/Second_Age_3430 "Second Age 3430") they formed the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men). In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3431](/wiki/Second_Age_3431 "Second Age 3431") Elendil and Gil-galad marched east to [Imladris](/wiki/Rivendell "Rivendell"); the host was the fairest and most splendid in arms ever seen in Middle-earth and the greatest ever assembled since the [Host of the Valar](/wiki/Host_of_the_Valar).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm-Rings_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-Rings-12">[12]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

#### Arnor and Division {#Arnor_and_Division}

Following [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s defeat during the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance), [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) took [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") for himself as a <a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/weregild" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">weregild</a> for his father, but, during the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields) the Ring betrayed him to his death; the Ring was lost and not found again for 2,500 years.<sup id="cite_ref-FR-Council_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR-Council-15">[15]</a></sup> The [kingship](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor "Kings of Arnor") of Arnor passed to Isildur's only surviving son, [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(King_of_Arnor)> "Valandil (King of Arnor)"), whose reign began an uneventful 800 years in the [North-kingdom](/wiki/Arnor "Arnor").<sup id="cite_ref-App-B2_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B2-16">[16]</a></sup>

The unity of Arnor was broken, however, when in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [861](/wiki/Third_Age_861 "Third Age 861"), following the death of the tenth [king](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor "Kings of Arnor"), [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(King_of_Arnor)> "Eärendur (King of Arnor)"), the dissension amongst his three sons led to the division of Arnor into three kingdoms: [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan) and [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur). There was often strife between the kingdoms, especially over ownership of the Weather Hills and the chief *[Palantír](/wiki/Amon_S%C3%BBl-stone "Amon Sûl-stone")* of the North at [Amon Sûl](/wiki/Weathertop "Weathertop"), and this quickened the waning of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain).<sup id="cite_ref-App-Eriador_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Eriador-17">[17]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Arthedain was in the North-west and included the land between <a href="/wiki/Baranduin" title="Baranduin">Brandywine</a> and <a href="/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn" title="Lhûn">Lune</a>, and also the land north of the <a href="/wiki/East_Road" title="East Road">Great Road</a> as far as the <a href="/wiki/Weather_Hills" title="Weather Hills">Weather Hills</a>. Cardolan was in the South, its bounds being the Brandywine, the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Greyflood" title="Greyflood">Greyflood</a>, and the Great Road.

The line of Isildur was maintained through the [Kings of Arthedain](/wiki/Kings_of_Arthedain) all the way to [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"), but the line of kings in Cardolan and Rhudaur quickly dwindled. By [1349](/wiki/Third_Age_1349 "Third Age 1349"), with the rule of [Argeleb I](/wiki/Argeleb_I), the Kings of Arthedain were once again claiming lordship over all of Arnor and prefixed their names with *[ar-](/wiki/Ar "Ar")* in recognition of this.<sup id="cite_ref-App-North_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-North-18">[18]</a></sup>

#### Rise of Angmar {#Rise_of_Angmar}

In the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300")<sup id="cite_ref-App-B2_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B2-16">[16]</a></sup> evil things begin to return to the world with the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) founding the evil realm of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) beyond the [Ettenmoors](/wiki/Ettenmoors) in the far north-east of Eriador, hoping to utterly destroy the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) of the North, seeing hope in the fragmentation of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor).<sup id="cite_ref-App-Eriador_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Eriador-17">[17]</a></sup>

In league with [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) and [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan), [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Arthedain "Kings of Arthedain") [Arveleg I](/wiki/Arveleg_I) of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) successfully fortified the [Weather Hills](/wiki/Weather_Hills) against [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) whilst [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) was being besieged. However, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1409](/wiki/Third_Age_1409 "Third Age 1409"), a great force came out of Angmar, surrounding [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop), defeating the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain), slaying Arveleg and the [last prince of Cardolan](/wiki/Last_prince_of_Cardolan "Last prince of Cardolan") whilst destroying the tower of Amon Sûl. The *[palantír](/wiki/Amon_S%C3%BBl-stone "Amon Sûl-stone")* was saved in retreat to [Fornost](/wiki/Fornost_Erain "Fornost Erain") but Cardolan was ravaged and [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur) became a vassal-state of Angmar. In response, [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) brought [Elves](/wiki/Elves) from [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) and [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) sent aid from [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) to repel Angmar from the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs) and Fornost - for a time, Angmar was subdued.<sup id="cite_ref-App-Eriador_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Eriador-17">[17]</a></sup>

During the [Angmar War](/wiki/Angmar_War), the weather and climate in Eriador became unfriendly, and all these circumstances forced the [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors) to leave the [Angle of Eriador](/wiki/Angle_of_Eriador). In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636") the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) came up from the south-east with many in Cardolan (especially in [Minhiriath](/wiki/Minhiriath)) and [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") perishing; as Cardolan was deserted, the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) sent evil spirits to dwell in the revered [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs).<sup id="cite_ref-App-Eriador_17-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Eriador-17">[17]</a></sup> The Plague marked the beginning of the desolation of Eriador, that continued being depopulated from [Men](/wiki/Men) for the rest of the [Age](/wiki/Age).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

After many years of silence, King [Araphant](/wiki/Araphant) of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) and King [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) renewed communications between the two realms, as they perceived that there was a single power directing the enemies of both [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and Gondor,<sup id="cite_ref-App-Gondor_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Gondor-20">[20]</a></sup> as such in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1940](/wiki/Third_Age_1940 "Third Age 1940") Araphant's son [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) married Ondoher's daughter [Fíriel](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel).<sup id="cite_ref-App-B2_16-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B2-16">[16]</a></sup> Despite the marriage, neither kingdom was able to send aid to the other as Angmar continued to attack Arthedain while the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) attacked Gondor; in the [Battle of the Camp](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Camp) of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944") Ondoher was killed.

Arvedui did not press his claim, but nor did the [Dúnedain of the North](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor "Dúnedain of Arnor") forget it, for their kingdom diminished as foretold by [Malbeth](/wiki/Malbeth) the [Seer](/wiki/Seers "Seers"): in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1974](/wiki/Third_Age_1974 "Third Age 1974") Angmar returned with renewed vigour, attacking the Dúnedain, capturing [Fornost](/wiki/Fornost_Erain "Fornost Erain") and destroying Arnor. After holding out for a while on the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs), Arvedui was forced to flee to the [mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains "Blue Mountains") where he stayed in old [dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") mines; out of hunger, he and his men travelled north and spent the winter with the [Lossoth](/wiki/Lossoth) on the [Icebay of Forochel](/wiki/Icebay_of_Forochel). Arvedui perished and two *[palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* were lost.<sup id="cite_ref-App-Eriador_17-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Eriador-17">[17]</a></sup>

[Eärnil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II "Eärnil II") had told Arvedui, "*I do not forget the royalty of Arnor, nor deny our kinship, nor wish that the realms of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) be estranged. I will send to your aid when you have need, so long as I am able.*"<sup id="cite_ref-App-Gondor_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Gondor-20">[20]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1973](/wiki/Third_Age_1973 "Third Age 1973") he received a request for aid and sent his son, [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur), north with a great host: they arrived at the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) too late. However, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975") [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) and Eärnur amassed a great army and met the forces of Angmar on the plain by [Lake Nenuial](/wiki/Lake_Evendim "Lake Evendim") in the [Battle of Fornost](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost): the force was too strong for the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) and his army fled but his retreat was cut off by Gondor's cavalry and a host of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) led by [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) who had come north from [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-App-B2_16-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B2-16">[16]</a></sup>

Despite the utter destruction of Angmar, [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) was also ended. Arvedui's son, [Aranarth](/wiki/Aranarth), took the title "[Chieftain of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "Chieftains of the Dúnedain")", and the heirlooms of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) were given into the keeping of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond).<sup id="cite_ref-App-B2_16-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B2-16">[16]</a></sup> The Dúnedain continued as a secretive and wandering people, fighting alongside the sons of Elrond to protect their former lands: they mostly lived out their full lives (save for [Aragorn I](/wiki/Aragorn_I) who was slain by [wolves](/wiki/Wolves "Wolves")) until the days of [Arassuil](/wiki/Arassuil) when evil things began to multiply again.<sup id="cite_ref-App-Eriador_17-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Eriador-17">[17]</a></sup>

With the foundation of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), the year [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) 1601 became the year 1 in [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire_Calendar "Shire Calendar").<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-21">[21]</a></sup> On the war with with Angmar the following is recorded:

Thanks to Gandalf they avoided death whilst managing to gain the two [Elvish](/wiki/Elves "Elves") swords [Glamdring](/wiki/Glamdring) and [Orcrist](/wiki/Orcrist), and the dagger [Sting](/wiki/Sting). Later on this quest Bilbo obtained [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum)'s [magic ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").<sup id="cite_ref-H-Riddles_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H-Riddles-25">[25]</a></sup>

#### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

By that time, large portions of Eriador had been deserted, as had other parts of the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands).<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[26]</a></sup>

Following [Bilbo's disappearance](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001"), Bilbo's "nephew", [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), inherited all of Bilbo's possessions, including Bilbo's ring.<sup id="cite_ref-FR-Party_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR-Party-27">[27]</a></sup> Bilbo travelled through Eriador, eventually staying with [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) in Rivendell, whilst Frodo continued to live in [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). However, by [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), Frodo, with [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), was forced to set out from [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") to head towards Rivendell in order to deliver what had been discovered to be [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"). On the way, the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) encountered the [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") and [Barrow-wights](/wiki/Barrow-wights), as well as [Farmer Maggot](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot), [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor "Gildor") and [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil). In [Bree](/wiki/Bree), the [Travellers](/wiki/Travellers) met a man called "[Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn")", who had been sent to safely escort the hobbits to Rivendell.<sup id="cite_ref-FR-Strider_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR-Strider-28">[28]</a></sup> Despite being pursued by Black Riders, they eventually arrived at Rivendell. There, a [Council](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond "Council of Elrond") decided that [a group of nine people](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") should take on a quest to destroy the Ring.<sup id="cite_ref-FR-Council_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR-Council-15">[15]</a></sup>

Following the eventual success of the quest, the destruction of [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") and downfall of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), and the coronation of "Strider" ([Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)) as [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") Elessar of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), the four hobbits and Gandalf returned to Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-RK-Homeward_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-Homeward-29">[29]</a></sup> Gandalf left the hobbits at the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest), choosing to visit [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil), but when they arrived at the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge) they saw that [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") had been badly affected by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) via his puppet "[Chief](/wiki/Chief_Shirriff "Chief Shirriff")" and the use of [ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians"). After breaking many of the new "rules", the four hobbits were escorted to [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) by a troop of [Shirriffs](/wiki/Shirriffs), where they began a rebellion in the form of the successful [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater): the ruffians were killed (as was [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma")) or captured and the [Shire-folk](/wiki/Shire-hobbits "Shire-hobbits") were liberated (at the cost of [19 lives](/wiki/The_Roll "The Roll")).<sup id="cite_ref-RK-Scouring_30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-Scouring-30">[30]</a></sup>

Following the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater) and the end of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Frodo temporarily took the position of Deputy [Mayor](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving "Mayor of Michel Delving") whilst Sam used [his gift](/wiki/Sam%27s_garden_box "Sam's garden box") from [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) to replenish the Shire of its lost flora (bringing a [Mallorn](/wiki/Mallorn) tree to the [Party Field](/wiki/Party_Field)).<sup id="cite_ref-RK-Havens_31-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-Havens-31">[31]</a></sup> On [September 21](/wiki/21_September "21 September"), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021"), Frodo and Sam set out from [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") to meet the [Last Riding of the](/wiki/Last_Riding_of_the_Keepers_of_the_Rings "Last Riding of the Keepers of the Rings") [Keepers of the Rings](/wiki/Three_Keepers "Three Keepers"): on [September 29](/wiki/29_September "29 September") they reached the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) where Frodo, Bilbo, Gandalf, Galadriel and [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) departed [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth); the [Red Book of Westmarch](/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch) was left with Sam.<sup id="cite_ref-App-B4_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B4-32">[32]</a></sup>

### Fourth Age {#Fourth_Age}

In the year [1422](/wiki/Fourth_Age_1 "Fourth Age 1") of [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire_Calendar "Shire Calendar"), the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) began following the departure of the [Ring-bearers](/wiki/Ring-bearers), the defeat of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and re-establishment of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) in the north and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in the south. In [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [6](/wiki/Fourth_Age_6 "Fourth Age 6") [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor "Kings of Arnor") [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) made [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") a [Free Land](/wiki/Free_Land) under the protection of the [Northern Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre_of_Ann%C3%BAminas "Sceptre of Annúminas") and declared that no [man](/wiki/Men "Men") should ever enter the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-App-B5_33-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B5-33">[33]</a></sup>

In [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [15](/wiki/Fourth_Age_15 "Fourth Age 15") Elessar stayed at [Lake Evendim](/wiki/Lake_Evendim), meeting his friends at [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge); in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [31](/wiki/Fourth_Age_31 "Fourth Age 31") he gave the [Westmarch](/wiki/Westmarch), the area between the [Far Downs](/wiki/Far_Downs) and [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills), to the Shire as well as officially adding [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) to the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_21-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-21">[21]</a></sup> In [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [61](/wiki/Fourth_Age_61 "Fourth Age 61") [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") eventually passed over [the sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") and in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [63](/wiki/Fourth_Age_63 "Fourth Age 63") [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") left the Shire and travel south to [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-App-B5_33-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B5-33">[33]</a></sup>

It is unknown how many, if any, [Elves](/wiki/Elves) were left in Eriador during the Fourth Age; presumably very few [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), at least.<sup id="cite_ref-RK-Havens_31-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-Havens-31">[31]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

### Physical characteristics {#Physical_characteristics}

<dl><dd><i>See also: <a href="/wiki/Category:Eriador" title="Category:Eriador">Category:Eriador</a> and <a href="/wiki/Category:Cities,_towns_and_villages_of_the_Shire" title="Category:Cities, towns and villages of the Shire">Category:Cities, towns and villages of the Shire</a></i></dd></dl>

Eriador was a large, inhabited temperate region in the north-west of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) which experienced warm summers and increasingly cool winters.<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_21-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-21">[21]</a></sup> At its widest extent, it stretched for around 675 miles from east-to-west, around 690 miles from north-to-south, 740 miles from northeast-to-southwest and 750 miles from northwest-to-southeast.<sup id="cite_ref-Distance_34-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Distance-34">[34]</a></sup>

Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> showing the <a href="/wiki/Withywindle" title="Withywindle">Withywindle</a></figcaption></figure>

The most important rivers were: the [Lune](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn "Lhûn") ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *Lhûn*) which flowed through the Blue Mountains to form the [Gulf of Lune](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune); the [Brandywine](/wiki/Baranduin "Baranduin") (Sindarin *Baranduin*) which flowed out of [Lake Evendim](/wiki/Lake_Evendim) (*Nenuial*) to form the eastern-boundary of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"); and the [Greyflood](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3 "Gwathló") (*Gwathló*) which formed the southern-border of Eriador. Other rivers included: the [Glanduin](/wiki/Glanduin), which flowed out of [Hollin](/wiki/Eregion "Eregion") (*Eregion*) and into the Greyflood above [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad), forming the [Swanfleet](/wiki/Swanfleet) (*Nîn-in-Eilph*) marsh; the [Loudwater](/wiki/Bruinen "Bruinen") (*Bruinen*), passing [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) (*Imladris*) in the [Ford of Bruinen](/wiki/Ford_of_Bruinen); the [Hoarwell](/wiki/Hoarwell) (*Mitheithel*) which flowed out of the [Ettenmoors](/wiki/Ettenmoors) and over which the [Last Bridge](/wiki/Last_Bridge) passed; the [Withywindle](/wiki/Withywindle) of the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest); and [The Water](/wiki/The_Water), [Stock-brook](/wiki/Stock-brook), [Shirebourn](/wiki/Shirebourn) and [Thistle Brook](/wiki/Thistle_Brook) which all flowed into the Brandywine from in the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-Shire-map_35-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shire-map-35">[35]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ME-map_36-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ME-map-36">[36]</a></sup>

Central Eriador was scattered with many groups of hills including the [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills) (*Emyn Beraid*), [Hills of Evendim](/wiki/Hills_of_Evendim) (*Emyn Uial*), [Weather Hills](/wiki/Weather_Hills) as well as the [Far Downs](/wiki/Far_Downs), [White Downs](/wiki/White_Downs), [South Downs](/wiki/South_Downs), [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs) and [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs) (*Tyrn Gorthad*); [Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad "Karen Wynn Fonstad") noted that the "*longitudinal axes \[of the hills\] formed concentric rings*".<sup id="cite_ref-Atlas-Eriador_37-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Atlas-Eriador-37">[37]</a></sup> Despite large deforestation by the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age)<sup id="cite_ref-UT-Galadriel_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-Galadriel-11">[11]</a></sup> some wooded areas remained, such as the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest), [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End), [Bindbole Wood](/wiki/Bindbole_Wood) (sometimes seen as "Bindbale"), [Chetwood](/wiki/Chetwood) around [Bree-hill](/wiki/Bree-hill), the [Trollshaws](/wiki/Trollshaws), and, largest of all, [Eryn Vorn](/wiki/Eryn_Vorn);<sup id="cite_ref-ME-map_36-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ME-map-36">[36]</a></sup> [Hollin](/wiki/Eregion "Eregion") was so named due to the large numbers of holly trees which grew there.<sup id="cite_ref-FR-Journey_38-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR-Journey-38">[38]</a></sup> Other geographic features included the [Midgewater Marshes](/wiki/Midgewater_Marshes), [Rushock Bog](/wiki/Rushock_Bog), and [Overbourn Marshes](/wiki/Overbourn_Marshes).<sup id="cite_ref-Shire-map_35-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shire-map-35">[35]</a></sup>

The [East-West Road](/wiki/East_Road "East Road") crossed Eriador from Rivendell in the east to the Grey Havens in the west whilst the [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway) ran south from [Fornost](/wiki/Fornost_Erain "Fornost Erain"), linking [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor);<sup id="cite_ref-FR-Sign_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> the Greenway and the East-West Road met at [Bree](/wiki/Bree). Key [Elvish](/wiki/Elves "Elves") settlements included the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens), [Ost-in-Edhil](/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil)<sup id="cite_ref-Silm-Rings_12-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-Rings-12">[12]</a></sup> and [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell); for the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) there were [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop), [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas), and [Fornost Erain](/wiki/Fornost_Erain), as well as the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") ports of [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad) and [Lond Daer](/wiki/Lond_Daer) (earlier known as [Vinyalondë](/wiki/Vinyalond%C3%AB)).

It is known that the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) existed during the First Age more formidable and longer than in the later Ages;<sup id="cite_ref-Silm-map_39-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-map-39">[39]</a></sup> whereas [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") reared the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) before the arrival of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) in order to hinder [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm-Captive_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-Captive-2">[2]</a></sup> These ranges surrounded the eastern and western borders of Eriador.

The [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains) (*Ered Engrin*) stretched across the north of the world in a curve from east to west, standing on the border of the [region of everlasting cold](/wiki/Dor_Daedeloth "Dor Daedeloth")<sup id="cite_ref-Silm-Bel_40-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-Bel-40">[40]</a></sup> and can be considered the northern border of Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41">[41]</a></sup>

It is unclear what the borders of Eriador were to the south; it is unknown whether the [Greyflood](/wiki/Greyflood) or [Lune](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn "Lhûn") rivers even existed in the First Age. Presumably the Greyflood followed the same route through Eriador to the lower end of the extended Blue Mountains, before reaching the sea somewhere to the west of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Atlas_42-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Atlas-42">[42]</a></sup>

## Inhabitants {#Inhabitants}

### Free Peoples {#Free_Peoples}

The five [free peoples](/wiki/Free_peoples "Free peoples") as reckoned by [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard), after the inclusion of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits):-

- [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves):
    - Dwarves dwelt in the east side of the Blue Mountains, especially in the parts south of the [Gulf of Lhûn](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lh%C3%BBn) where they had mines that were still in use.<sup id="cite_ref-App-Eriador_17-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Eriador-17">[17]</a></sup>
- [Elves](/wiki/Elves):
    - [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor) as a wood-land people ([First Age](/wiki/First_Age)).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm-Sindar_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-Sindar-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) in [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750") - [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697")) and [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697") onwards).<sup id="cite_ref-App-B1_8-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B1-8">[8]</a></sup>
- [Ents](/wiki/Ents):
    - The great wood that once spanned the whole of Eriador (largely deforested by [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) for ship-building) used to be traversed by Ents (until sometime during [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age)).<sup id="cite_ref-TT-Treebeard_43-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT-Treebeard-43">[43]</a></sup>
- [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits):
    - First entered Eriador in around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1150](/wiki/Third_Age_1150 "Third Age 1150").<sup id="cite_ref-App-B2_16-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B2-16">[16]</a></sup>
    - At [Bree](/wiki/Bree) (from c. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300")).<sup id="cite_ref-App-B2_16-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B2-16">[16]</a></sup>
    - In [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1601](/wiki/Third_Age_1601 "Third Age 1601") and [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2340](/wiki/Third_Age_2340 "Third Age 2340") onwards, respectively).<sup id="cite_ref-App-B2_16-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B2-16">[16]</a></sup>
    - In the [Westmarch](/wiki/Westmarch) ([Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [31](/wiki/Fourth_Age_31 "Fourth Age 31") onwards).<sup id="cite_ref-App-B5_33-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B5-33">[33]</a></sup>
- [Men](/wiki/Men):
    - [Edain](/wiki/Edain) and [Swarthy Men](</wiki/Easterlings_(First_Age)> "Easterlings (First Age)") during the late First Age - their descendants, known as [Middle Men](/wiki/Middle_Men), stayed into the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age); the [Men of Bree](/wiki/Men_of_Bree) occupied the surrounding [area](/wiki/Bree-land "Bree-land").<sup id="cite_ref-UT-Wife_10-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-Wife-10">[10]</a></sup>
    - [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) in [Vinyalondë](/wiki/Vinyalond%C3%AB) and [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad) (from around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [800](/wiki/Second_Age_800 "Second Age 800")).<sup id="cite_ref-UT-Wife_10-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-Wife-10">[10]</a></sup>
    - [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) founding the realm of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320")).<sup id="cite_ref-App-B2_16-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-B2-16">[16]</a></sup>
    - A small population of native [Middle Men](/wiki/Middle_Men) maintained a hunter-gatherer culture in the remaining woods of [Minhiriath](/wiki/Minhiriath).<sup id="cite_ref-44" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-44">[44]</a></sup>

There are at least two, slightly different, versions of the etymology of *Eriador*:

1. *Eriador* as [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (derived from [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)), whose derivation is quoted by [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) from an unnamed note dated 1949-53. This explains that *Eriador* is derived from *eryā*, "isolated, lonely" and *[dor](/wiki/D%C3%B4r "Dôr")*, "land", thereby translating *Eriador* as "wilderness".<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_47-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-47">[47]</a></sup>
2. In another manuscript, Tolkien stated that *Eriador* was a [Silvan Elvish](/wiki/Silvan_Elvish) name, meaning "Lonely land" (deriving from \**eryā*, S *eir*, *air*).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_48-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-48">[48]</a></sup>

Both translations are noticeably similar to the "[Lone-lands](/wiki/Lone-lands)" mentioned in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*;<sup id="cite_ref-Hob-Mutton_46-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hob-Mutton-46">[46]</a></sup> it is unknown whether they are the same or simply coincidental.

In the [First Map of *The Lord of the Rings*](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The First Map of The Lord of the Rings"), Eriador is called "Forodwaith" - the name which is now applied to the [very cold region](</wiki/Forodwaith_(lands)> "Forodwaith (lands)") in the north of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-Treason_49-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Treason-49">[49]</a></sup>
