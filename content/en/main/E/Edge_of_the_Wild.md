The **Edge of the Wild** was the counceptual boundary between most of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), which was or had been a settled area, and the [dangerous uplands](/wiki/Eastern_Eriador "Eastern Eriador") leading to the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) and the [Wilderland](/wiki/Wilderland) beyond. Conceptually it was a north-south line that ran just to the west of the [Ford of Bruinen](/wiki/Ford_of_Bruinen).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> When one was over, or east, of the Edge of the Wild even the best of plans could easily go astray, as [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) was well aware.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
