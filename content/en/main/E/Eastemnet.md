The **Eastemnet** were the plains of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) east of the river [Entwash](Entwash.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> until it reached the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) and the [Emyn Muil](Emyn_Muil.md), respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [The Wold](../T/The_Wold.md) was the northern part of the Eastemnet.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Before the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), herdsmen of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) lived a nomadic existence on the plains of the Eastemnet, where they would drive their herds across the grasslands and live in temporary camps and tents, even during the winter. When the [Three Hunters](../T/Three_Hunters.md), pursuing the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) who had captured [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), followed them to the Eastemnet on 27 February [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the plains were empty.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the Wold was invaded by Orcs<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Partings_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-7">[7]</a></sup> from the north on [11 March](../_/11_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> but the [Ents](Ents.md) defeated the invading Orcs [the next day](../_/12_March.md)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> in the Wold.<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eastemnet* means "east-plain" in [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> The element *emnet* means "flat land" or "plain" in the language of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> The word *emnet* means "level ground" or "plain" in Old English.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
