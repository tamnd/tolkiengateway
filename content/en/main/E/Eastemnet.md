The **Eastemnet** were the plains of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) east of the river [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> until it reached the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) and the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil), respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [The Wold](/wiki/The_Wold) was the northern part of the Eastemnet.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Before the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), herdsmen of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) lived a nomadic existence on the plains of the Eastemnet, where they would drive their herds across the grasslands and live in temporary camps and tents, even during the winter. When the [Three Hunters](/wiki/Three_Hunters), pursuing the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) who had captured [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), followed them to the Eastemnet on 27 February [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the plains were empty.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the Wold was invaded by Orcs<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Partings_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-7">[7]</a></sup> from the north on [11 March](/wiki/11_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> but the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) defeated the invading Orcs [the next day](/wiki/12_March "12 March")<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> in the Wold.<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eastemnet* means "east-plain" in [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> The element *emnet* means "flat land" or "plain" in the language of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> The word *emnet* means "level ground" or "plain" in Old English.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
