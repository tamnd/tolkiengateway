<i><b>Enquië</b></i> is the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word for *week*. The word derives from the Quenya word for "six", *[enquë](Enquë.md)*, because in the [Reckoning of Rivendell](../R/Reckoning_of_Rivendell.md) the week had six days.

An Elvish [yén](../Y/Yén.md) (144 [solar years](../C/Coranar.md)) contains 8,766 enquier. The observation of the *week* was more for ritual than for practical purposes; the chief day was Valanya, the last day ordinally.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) had renamed *Aldúya* to *[Aldea](../A/Aldea.md)/[Orgaladh](../O/Orgaladh.md)*, and inserted the day *[Earenya](Earenya.md)/[Oraearon](../O/Oraearon.md)* dedicated to [the Sea](../T/The_Sea.md), resulting to a seven-day week in [their reckoning](../K/Kings'_Reckoning.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the early [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), Tolkien used the [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) word *[otsola](../O/Otsola.md)* (*[otso](../O/Otso.md)* "seven") for a seven-day week.

Later, in *[The Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)*, Tolkien mentioned a five-day week, <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lemnar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lemnar (page does not exist)">lemnar</a></i> (*[lempe](../L/Lempe.md)* "five") that was probably used by the [Calaquendi](../C/Calaquendi.md) in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).
