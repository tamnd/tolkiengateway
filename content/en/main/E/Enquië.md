<i><b>Enquië</b></i> is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for *week*. The word derives from the Quenya word for "six", *[enquë](/wiki/Enqu%C3%AB "Enquë")*, because in the [Reckoning of Rivendell](/wiki/Reckoning_of_Rivendell) the week had six days.

An Elvish [yén](/wiki/Y%C3%A9n "Yén") (144 [solar years](/wiki/Coranar "Coranar")) contains 8,766 enquier. The observation of the *week* was more for ritual than for practical purposes; the chief day was Valanya, the last day ordinally.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) had renamed *Aldúya* to *[Aldea](/wiki/Aldea)/[Orgaladh](/wiki/Orgaladh)*, and inserted the day *[Earenya](/wiki/Earenya)/[Oraearon](/wiki/Oraearon)* dedicated to [the Sea](/wiki/The_Sea "The Sea"), resulting to a seven-day week in [their reckoning](/wiki/Kings%27_Reckoning "Kings' Reckoning").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the early [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), Tolkien used the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) word *[otsola](/wiki/Otsola "Otsola")* (*[otso](/wiki/Otso "Otso")* "seven") for a seven-day week.

Later, in *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, Tolkien mentioned a five-day week, <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lemnar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lemnar (page does not exist)">lemnar</a></i> (*[lempe](/wiki/Lempe "Lempe")* "five") that was probably used by the [Calaquendi](/wiki/Calaquendi) in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).
