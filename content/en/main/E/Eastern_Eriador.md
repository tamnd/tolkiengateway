**Eastern Eriador** was a name used for the wild lands in the east of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) on the western side of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). The [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbitish "Hobbitish") name *Wilderland* for the lands on the eastern side of the Misty Mountains, called [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), was often used to include Eastern Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
