**Eastern Eriador** was a name used for the wild lands in the east of [Eriador](Eriador.md) on the western side of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md). The [Hobbit](../H/Hobbitish.md) name *Wilderland* for the lands on the eastern side of the Misty Mountains, called [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), was often used to include Eastern Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
