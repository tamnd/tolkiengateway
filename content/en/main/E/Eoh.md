**Eoh** was the father of [Eriol](Eriol.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Eoh_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eoh-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Eoh lived somewhere in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5th_century" title="wikipedia:5th century">5th century</a> <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglia_(peninsula)" title="wikipedia:Anglia (peninsula)">Anglia</a>, being a descendant of sea-faring ancestors, some of whom visited [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) by the way of [Olórë Mallë](../O/Olórë_Mallë.md) in their sleep.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> His home had a castle with a great tower, and a river running underneath it.<sup id="cite_ref-Sea_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sea-3">[3]</a></sup>

He had a son called Ottor, who would later become known as *Eriol*, inheriting his ancestors' sea-longing.<sup id="cite_ref-Sea_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sea-3">[3]</a></sup>

However, sometime during Eriol's childhood, Eoh's home was attacked by his brother [Beorn](<../B/Beorn_(son_of_Heden).md>), where both Eriol's mother<sup id="cite_ref-Sea_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sea-3">[3]</a></sup> and Eoh died, with Eoh being killed by Beorn himself<sup id="cite_ref-Eoh_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eoh-1">[1]</a></sup> in a "bitter fight about the walls".<sup id="cite_ref-Sea_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sea-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> His grandsons, [Hengest](../H/Hengest.md) and [Horsa](../H/Horsa.md), later avenged his death.<sup id="cite_ref-Eoh_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eoh-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Eoh* is in [Old English](../O/Old_English.md), being a poetic word for "horse".<sup id="cite_ref-Eoh_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eoh-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Eoh's name in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) was <i><b>Évar</b></i>, with <i><b>Echos</b></i> being its [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) equivalent.

The character was also called <i><b>Elvas</b></i> in [Gothic](../G/Gothic.md), *Elvas* probably being a dialectal or archaic form of the Gothic word *aílvs* ("horse") - from which the Qenya and Gnomish equivalents of the name were probably derived from.

An alternative Qenya name for Eoh was <i><b>Eqar</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Elv_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elv-6">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early, rejected sketch, a more elaborate version of the attack on Eoh's home is given, in which Beorn attacked his brother's castle and killed him, *and* took Eriol captive - from which Eriol eventually escaped.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>
