**Evadrien** was a region of the island of [Lúthien](/wiki/Luthany "Luthany"), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in the text called *[Ælfwine of England](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine_of_England)* from *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Eva_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eva-1">[1]</a></sup> It is equated with the legendary drowned kingdom of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyonesse" title="wikipedia:Lyonesse">Lionesse</a>, located between <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornwall" title="wikipedia:Cornwall">Cornwall</a> and the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isles_of_Scilly" title="wikipedia:Isles of Scilly">Scilly Isles</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Éadgifu](/wiki/%C3%89adgifu), mother of [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine), came from these lands. She was a friend of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) that still dwelt in Lúthien, many of whom lived in Evadrien.<sup id="cite_ref-Eva_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eva-1">[1]</a></sup>

It also contained the harbour of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Belerion&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Belerion (page does not exist)">Belerion</a>, from which the Elves of Lúthien sailed to [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Evadrien* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), meaning "Coast of Iron".<sup id="cite_ref-Eva_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eva-1">[1]</a></sup>

An earlier form of the name was *Erenol*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

It was also called <i><b>Lionesse</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Eva_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eva-1">[1]</a></sup>
