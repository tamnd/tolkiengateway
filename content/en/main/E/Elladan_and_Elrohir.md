**Elladan** and **Elrohir** were the [twin](../T/Twins.md) sons, and eldest children, of [Elrond](Elrond.md) and [Celebrían](../C/Celebrían.md).

They were descended from the royal Houses of the [Eldar](Eldar.md) through both their father and mother, but also from the noble Houses of the [Edain](Edain.md) through their father.

## Description {#Description}

The brothers were tall, dark-haired and grey-eyed, and so much alike that only those that knew them well could tell them apart. To [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md), their fair elven faces seemed neither old nor young. While in the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md), they wore cloaks of silver-grey over bright mail.<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2509](../T/Third_Age_2509.md) the twins' mother Celebrían went on a journey into the south to visit her own mother, [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md), in the land of [Lórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Arahad_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arahad-4">[4]</a></sup> In the [Redhorn Pass](../R/Redhorn_Pass.md), she was captured by [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md), and tortured<sup id="cite_ref-Arahad_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arahad-4">[4]</a></sup> in their dens<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-5">[5]</a></sup>. The twins rode to rescue her, but by the time they reached her, she had received a poisonous wound. Though their father healed her, she would not remain in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), and sailed into the [West](../A/Aman.md) the following year.<sup id="cite_ref-Arahad_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arahad-4">[4]</a></sup> After this loss, Elladan and Elrohir never forgot their mother's torture in the dens of the Orcs and often rode far from Rivendell with the [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [2933](../T/Third_Age_2933.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> they saw [Arathorn II](../A/Arathorn_II.md) killed by orcs while hunting with him.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In the early part of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the brothers' main role was to scout the land and prepare the way for the [Fellowship](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md). Around [November](../N/November.md) of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), they were sent by Elrond on a secret errand. They passed down the [Silverlode](../S/Silverlode.md) into a "[strange country](../L/Lothlórien.md)" and were the last to return to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) in [December](../D/December.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Later, they took a more active part. When [Halbarad](../H/Halbarad.md) rode to [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md)'s aid with the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md), Elladan and Elrohir accompanied them and Elrohir gave counsel from [his father](Elrond.md) to Aragorn to "remember the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> They followed Aragorn through the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md), fought with him at [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md), and took part in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), where they fought with stars bound to their brows. Both were present at a council of Aragorn and his captains, and then accompanied the force that fought in the [Battle of the Morannon](../B/Battle_of_the_Morannon.md).

After the celebrations and the coronation of Aragorn, the brothers departed [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) on [8 May](../_/8_May.md). On [14 June](../_/14_June.md) they met with the escort of their [sister](../A/Arwen.md) on her way to [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) to marry Aragorn, and together they went to [Edoras](Edoras.md). They stayed there for [two days](../_/16_June.md) before setting out for Gondor. They arrived to the City on [1 Lithe](../_/1_Lithe.md)<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and the brothers held a silver banner.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

### Later fate {#Later_fate}

After the War, little is known of the brothers' fate. They returned to their father's house at Rivendell and remained there even after their father had passed across the [Sea](../B/Belegaer.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Rec_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rec-12">[12]</a></sup> Like their sister [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md), the sons of Elrond [Half-elven](../H/Half-elven.md) were granted the choice of whether to leave [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) for the [Undying Lands](../U/Undying_Lands.md), or remain there and become [mortal](../G/Gift_of_Men.md) as [Men](../M/Men.md). They chose, for a time at least, to stay in Middle-earth for reasons unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> However, due to the fact that shortly before Aragorn died in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md), he said to Arwen that 'none now walk' in the garden of Elrond, indicating that the last of the [Eldar](Eldar.md) had departed to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> and that [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) dwelt in Rivendell for a while before he eventually departed over the Sea,<sup id="cite_ref-Rec_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rec-12">[12]</a></sup> it seems likely that both twins had left Rivendell before or at the time of their grandfather's last trip for the Undying Lands.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

### Elladan {#Elladan}

*Elladan* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Elf-man", from *[el](El.md)* ("elf") + *[adan](../A/Adan.md)* ("man"). It could also be translated as "Elf-Númenórean", as *adan* refers to the [Edain](Edain.md), who later became [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md).<sup id="cite_ref-twins_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-twins-15">[15]</a></sup>

### Elrohir {#Elrohir}

*Elrohir* is Sindarin for "Elf-man" or "Elf-knight", from *el* ("elf") + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Rohir&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rohir (page does not exist)">rohir</a></i> ("horse-lord").<sup id="cite_ref-twins_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-twins-15">[15]</a></sup>

<b>1979: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1979 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1979 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Elladan and Elrohir follow <a href="../H/Halbarad.md" title="Halbarad">Halbarad</a> and come to <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>&#39;s aid. No actor is credited.</dd></dl>

<b>1980: <a href="../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)"><i>The Return of the King</i> (1980 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Elladan and Elrohir are not mentioned by name. However, two dark-haired individuals of similar dress and appearance appear at the council in Aragorn&#39;s tent, just as Elladan and Elrohir do in the novel.</dd></dl>

<b>2001-2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Despite not featuring in <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> film series</a>, Decipher produced two cards featuring the characters in their <i>Expanded Middle-earth</i> expansion, focusing solely on characters which are not present in the films.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Elladan and Elrohir are first met by Elven and Dwarven characters in <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_3016.md" title="Third Age 3016">3016</a> in <a class="mw-redirect" href="Ered_Luin.md" title="Ered Luin">Ered Luin</a> where they travel to unravel the mystery of their father&#39;s prophetic dream. In <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_3018.md" title="Third Age 3018">3018</a> all players meet them in their camp in the <a href="../T/Trollshaws.md" title="Trollshaws">Trollshaws</a> from which they lead the search for the missing Black Rider and defend the <a href="../F/Ford_of_Bruinen.md" title="Ford of Bruinen">Ford of Bruinen</a> against an attack. Early next year they join the <a href="../G/Grey_Company.md" title="Grey Company">Grey Company</a> and travel with the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../D/Dunedain.md" title="Dunedain">Dunedain</a> Rangers from the wilds of <a href="Eregion.md" title="Eregion">Eregion</a> all the way to the <a href="../P/Pelennor_Fields.md" title="Pelennor Fields">Pelennor Fields</a>. Afterwards they travel with the Host of the West to <a href="../B/Black_Gate.md" title="Black Gate">Black Gate</a> and fight in the <a href="../B/Battle_of_the_Morannon.md" title="Battle of the Morannon">Battle of the Morannon</a>. They later attend their sister Arwen&#39;s wedding in <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>.</dd></dl>

2009: <i><b><a href="../B/Born_of_Hope.md" title="Born of Hope">Born of Hope</a>:</b></i>

<dl><dd>In the late <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">Third Age</a>, Elladan and Elrohir come to visit <a href="../A/Arathorn_II.md" title="Arathorn II">Arathorn</a>, Chieftain of the <a href="../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md" title="Dúnedain of Arnor">Dúnedain</a>, to warn him about orcs approaching the village. Their advice is to bring Arathorn&#39;s son, <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>, to <a href="../R/Rivendell.md" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>, together with Arathorn&#39;s wife, <a href="../G/Gilraen.md" title="Gilraen">Gilraen</a>. However, the decision is delayed and the orcs attack the village. Elladan and Elrohir help in the battle, but later Arathorn and his <a href="../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md" title="Rangers of the North">Rangers</a> follow the orcs in the wood and Arathorn is mortally wounded. Elladan and Elrohir bring back him to the village and later will accompany Gilraen and her son to Rivendell.</dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Elladan and Elrohir were sent by their father, <a href="Elrond.md" title="Elrond">Elrond</a>, to <a class="mw-redirect" href="../F/Fornost.md" title="Fornost">Fornost</a>, to scout for the darkness in that city. They meet with <a href="Eradan_(video_game_character).md" title="Eradan (video game character)">Eradan</a>, <a href="../A/Andriel.md" title="Andriel">Andriel</a> and <a href="../F/Farin_(video_game_character).md" title="Farin (video game character)">Farin</a>, who were sent by <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> to stop <a href="../A/Agandaûr.md" title="Agandaûr">Agandaûr</a>&#39;s army from attacking. As they have the same goal, they collaborate and fight a way to the Citadel of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> There, Elladan and his brother remove the <a href="../M/Magic.md" title="Magic">magic spell</a> of the Citadel gate, which protects Agandaûr against intruders.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> Inside the citadel, Eradan, Andriel and Farin kill <a href="../T/Tharzog.md" title="Tharzog">Tharzog</a> - chieftain of the <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> of <a href="../M/Mount_Gram.md" title="Mount Gram">Mount Gram</a> - while Elladan and Elrohir confront Agandaûr. Agandaûr has the upper hand in the battle, but flees when Eradan, Andriel and Farin arrive. Elladan and Elrohir return to <a href="../R/Rivendell.md" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>, where they inform their father about the events in Fornost.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup></dd></dl>
