**Elladan** and **Elrohir** were the [twin](/wiki/Twins "Twins") sons, and eldest children, of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and [Celebrían](/wiki/Celebr%C3%ADan).

They were descended from the royal Houses of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) through both their father and mother, but also from the noble Houses of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) through their father.

## Description {#Description}

The brothers were tall, dark-haired and grey-eyed, and so much alike that only those that knew them well could tell them apart. To [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck), their fair elven faces seemed neither old nor young. While in the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company), they wore cloaks of silver-grey over bright mail.<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2509](/wiki/Third_Age_2509 "Third Age 2509") the twins' mother Celebrían went on a journey into the south to visit her own mother, [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), in the land of [Lórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien").<sup id="cite_ref-Arahad_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arahad-4">[4]</a></sup> In the [Redhorn Pass](/wiki/Redhorn_Pass), she was captured by [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains), and tortured<sup id="cite_ref-Arahad_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arahad-4">[4]</a></sup> in their dens<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-5">[5]</a></sup>. The twins rode to rescue her, but by the time they reached her, she had received a poisonous wound. Though their father healed her, she would not remain in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and sailed into the [West](/wiki/Aman "Aman") the following year.<sup id="cite_ref-Arahad_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arahad-4">[4]</a></sup> After this loss, Elladan and Elrohir never forgot their mother's torture in the dens of the Orcs and often rode far from Rivendell with the [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North).<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [2933](/wiki/Third_Age_2933 "Third Age 2933"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> they saw [Arathorn II](/wiki/Arathorn_II) killed by orcs while hunting with him.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

In the early part of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the brothers' main role was to scout the land and prepare the way for the [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring"). Around [November](/wiki/November) of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), they were sent by Elrond on a secret errand. They passed down the [Silverlode](/wiki/Silverlode) into a "[strange country](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien")" and were the last to return to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) in [December](/wiki/December).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Later, they took a more active part. When [Halbarad](/wiki/Halbarad) rode to [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)'s aid with the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company), Elladan and Elrohir accompanied them and Elrohir gave counsel from [his father](/wiki/Elrond "Elrond") to Aragorn to "remember the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead)".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> They followed Aragorn through the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead), fought with him at [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir), and took part in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), where they fought with stars bound to their brows. Both were present at a council of Aragorn and his captains, and then accompanied the force that fought in the [Battle of the Morannon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon).

After the celebrations and the coronation of Aragorn, the brothers departed [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) on [8 May](/wiki/8_May). On [14 June](/wiki/14_June) they met with the escort of their [sister](/wiki/Arwen "Arwen") on her way to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) to marry Aragorn, and together they went to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras). They stayed there for [two days](/wiki/16_June "16 June") before setting out for Gondor. They arrived to the City on [1 Lithe](/wiki/1_Lithe)<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and the brothers held a silver banner.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

### Later fate {#Later_fate}

After the War, little is known of the brothers' fate. They returned to their father's house at Rivendell and remained there even after their father had passed across the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer").<sup id="cite_ref-Rec_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rec-12">[12]</a></sup> Like their sister [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen), the sons of Elrond [Half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven) were granted the choice of whether to leave [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) for the [Undying Lands](/wiki/Undying_Lands), or remain there and become [mortal](/wiki/Gift_of_Men "Gift of Men") as [Men](/wiki/Men). They chose, for a time at least, to stay in Middle-earth for reasons unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> However, due to the fact that shortly before Aragorn died in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [120](/wiki/Fourth_Age_120 "Fourth Age 120"), he said to Arwen that 'none now walk' in the garden of Elrond, indicating that the last of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) had departed to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor),<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> and that [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) dwelt in Rivendell for a while before he eventually departed over the Sea,<sup id="cite_ref-Rec_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rec-12">[12]</a></sup> it seems likely that both twins had left Rivendell before or at the time of their grandfather's last trip for the Undying Lands.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

### Elladan {#Elladan}

*Elladan* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Elf-man", from *[el](/wiki/El "El")* ("elf") + *[adan](/wiki/Adan "Adan")* ("man"). It could also be translated as "Elf-Númenórean", as *adan* refers to the [Edain](/wiki/Edain), who later became [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans).<sup id="cite_ref-twins_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-twins-15">[15]</a></sup>

### Elrohir {#Elrohir}

*Elrohir* is Sindarin for "Elf-man" or "Elf-knight", from *el* ("elf") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rohir&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rohir (page does not exist)">rohir</a></i> ("horse-lord").<sup id="cite_ref-twins_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-twins-15">[15]</a></sup>

<b>1979: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1979 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1979 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Elladan and Elrohir follow <a href="/wiki/Halbarad" title="Halbarad">Halbarad</a> and come to <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>&#39;s aid. No actor is credited.</dd></dl>

<b>1980: <a href="/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)"><i>The Return of the King</i> (1980 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Elladan and Elrohir are not mentioned by name. However, two dark-haired individuals of similar dress and appearance appear at the council in Aragorn&#39;s tent, just as Elladan and Elrohir do in the novel.</dd></dl>

<b>2001-2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Despite not featuring in <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> film series</a>, Decipher produced two cards featuring the characters in their <i>Expanded Middle-earth</i> expansion, focusing solely on characters which are not present in the films.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Elladan and Elrohir are first met by Elven and Dwarven characters in <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3016" title="Third Age 3016">3016</a> in <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ered_Luin" title="Ered Luin">Ered Luin</a> where they travel to unravel the mystery of their father&#39;s prophetic dream. In <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3018" title="Third Age 3018">3018</a> all players meet them in their camp in the <a href="/wiki/Trollshaws" title="Trollshaws">Trollshaws</a> from which they lead the search for the missing Black Rider and defend the <a href="/wiki/Ford_of_Bruinen" title="Ford of Bruinen">Ford of Bruinen</a> against an attack. Early next year they join the <a href="/wiki/Grey_Company" title="Grey Company">Grey Company</a> and travel with the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Dunedain" title="Dunedain">Dunedain</a> Rangers from the wilds of <a href="/wiki/Eregion" title="Eregion">Eregion</a> all the way to the <a href="/wiki/Pelennor_Fields" title="Pelennor Fields">Pelennor Fields</a>. Afterwards they travel with the Host of the West to <a href="/wiki/Black_Gate" title="Black Gate">Black Gate</a> and fight in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon" title="Battle of the Morannon">Battle of the Morannon</a>. They later attend their sister Arwen&#39;s wedding in <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>.</dd></dl>

2009: <i><b><a href="/wiki/Born_of_Hope" title="Born of Hope">Born of Hope</a>:</b></i>

<dl><dd>In the late <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">Third Age</a>, Elladan and Elrohir come to visit <a href="/wiki/Arathorn_II" title="Arathorn II">Arathorn</a>, Chieftain of the <a href="/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor" title="Dúnedain of Arnor">Dúnedain</a>, to warn him about orcs approaching the village. Their advice is to bring Arathorn&#39;s son, <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>, to <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>, together with Arathorn&#39;s wife, <a href="/wiki/Gilraen" title="Gilraen">Gilraen</a>. However, the decision is delayed and the orcs attack the village. Elladan and Elrohir help in the battle, but later Arathorn and his <a href="/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North" title="Rangers of the North">Rangers</a> follow the orcs in the wood and Arathorn is mortally wounded. Elladan and Elrohir bring back him to the village and later will accompany Gilraen and her son to Rivendell.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Elladan and Elrohir were sent by their father, <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a>, to <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Fornost" title="Fornost">Fornost</a>, to scout for the darkness in that city. They meet with <a href="/wiki/Eradan_(video_game_character)" title="Eradan (video game character)">Eradan</a>, <a href="/wiki/Andriel" title="Andriel">Andriel</a> and <a href="/wiki/Farin_(video_game_character)" title="Farin (video game character)">Farin</a>, who were sent by <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> to stop <a href="/wiki/Aganda%C3%BBr" title="Agandaûr">Agandaûr</a>&#39;s army from attacking. As they have the same goal, they collaborate and fight a way to the Citadel of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> There, Elladan and his brother remove the <a href="/wiki/Magic" title="Magic">magic spell</a> of the Citadel gate, which protects Agandaûr against intruders.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> Inside the citadel, Eradan, Andriel and Farin kill <a href="/wiki/Tharzog" title="Tharzog">Tharzog</a> - chieftain of the <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> of <a href="/wiki/Mount_Gram" title="Mount Gram">Mount Gram</a> - while Elladan and Elrohir confront Agandaûr. Agandaûr has the upper hand in the battle, but flees when Eradan, Andriel and Farin arrive. Elladan and Elrohir return to <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>, where they inform their father about the events in Fornost.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup></dd></dl>
