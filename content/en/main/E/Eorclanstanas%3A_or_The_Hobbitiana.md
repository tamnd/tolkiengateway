<i><b>Eorclanstanas: or The Hobbitiana</b></i> is an auction catalogue of literature from the library of "A. Himes".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> Included is an incomplete version of [Galadriel's lament](/wiki/Galadriel%27s_lament) (identified as DTS 55 - "Galadriel's Lament" Fragment by the [Mellonath Daeron](/wiki/Forodrim "Forodrim")). The version go "as far as to *airetári-lírinen*" and "is accompanied by a transcription into Roman characters, and an English translation".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> [Måns Björkman](/wiki/M%C3%A5ns_Bj%C3%B6rkman) has provided the following description of this version:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Here a variant occurs in which the diacritic is made up of <i>three</i> dots [...] The variant is used to indicate palatals in two instances out of five, while the remaining have the regular two dots.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><sup id="cite_ref-MB_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MB-1">[1]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>
