**Egalmoth** is a [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md)/[Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name applied to:

- **[Egalmoth](Egalmoth.md)**, an [Elf](Elves.md)-lord of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).
- [Egalmoth](<Egalmoth_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>), the eighteenth [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

- [Saelon](../S/Saelon.md), called *Egalmoth* in early manuscripts.
