**Egalmoth** is a [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish)/[Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name applied to:

- **[Egalmoth](/wiki/Egalmoth)**, an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves")-lord of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).
- [Egalmoth](</wiki/Egalmoth_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Egalmoth (Steward of Gondor)"), the eighteenth [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

- [Saelon](/wiki/Saelon), called *Egalmoth* in early manuscripts.
