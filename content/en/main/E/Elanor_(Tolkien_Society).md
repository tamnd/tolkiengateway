**Elanor**, or **The Flemish Fantasy Society** (<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch" title="wikipedia:Dutch">Dutch</a>: *Het Vlaams Fantasy Genootschap*), is a [Tolkien and Fantasy society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md) based in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium" title="wikipedia:Belgium">Belgium</a>. *Elanor* was founded in [1999](../_/1999.md), at which point it was exclusive a [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-only society, in order to bring a group of committed Tolkien-enthusiasts together over a range of activities.<sup id="cite_ref-AH_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AH-1">[1]</a></sup> Elanor was founded after Flemish Tolkien enthusiasts felt a Tolkien Society independent from [Unquendor](../U/Unquendor.md) would suit them better. Although both Flanders and the Netherlands share the same language, there are considerable cultural differences separating these territories. In recent years Elanor and Unquendor have grown closer and are organizing joint events.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Activities {#Activities}

### Lothelanor {#Lothelanor}

<i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lothelanor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lothelanor (page does not exist)">Lothelanor</a></i> is the thrice-yearly publication of *Elanor*, with a heavy focus on content from its members. *Lothelanor* includes poems, illustrations, interviews, essays, reviews, crosswords, travelogues, and reports of activities both inside and outside the society.<sup id="cite_ref-Lothelanor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lothelanor-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Namarië {#Namarië}

Elanor also includes its own sword-fighting group, "Namarië",<sup id="cite_ref-AH_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AH-1">[1]</a></sup> who provide two-week training courses for members as well as put on shows at various events throughout the year. The shows themselves seek to replicate a well-known fantasy scene, with authentic costumes, for the enjoyment of the spectators and performers.<sup id="cite_ref-Namarie_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Namarie-4">[4]</a></sup>
