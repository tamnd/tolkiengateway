**Emil Johansson** is the founder and creator of the [Lord of the Rings Project](../L/Lord_of_the_Rings_Project.md) (commonly shortened to LotrProject), a creative not-for-profit web project dedicated to the works of J.R.R. Tolkien. His website was launched in January 2012 and provides timelines, genealogies, statistics and maps that are updated with additional information regularly.

He is also a Chemical Engineering student currently living in Gothenburg, Sweden.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
