**Elemmakil** ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Star Sword*) was one of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) and was the captain of the [Dark Guard](/wiki/Dark_Guard) stationed at the [Gate of Wood](/wiki/Gate_of_Wood).<sup id="cite_ref-SilmMaeglin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SilmMaeglin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) appeared, Elemmakil was amazed that his friend Voronwë would bring a mortal man with him. He announced that he should, by law, slay him. Voronwë and Tuor answered that they came at the command of [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo). Elemmakil then guided the two through the [Seven Gates](/wiki/Seven_Gates_of_Gondolin "Seven Gates of Gondolin") of the [Orfalch Echor](/wiki/Orfalch_Echor), until he delivered them unto [Ecthelion of the Fountain](/wiki/Ecthelion "Ecthelion"), then serving as the [Warden of the Great Gate](/wiki/Warden_of_the_Great_Gate).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Elemmakil* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Star Sword", from the elements *[Elen](/wiki/Elen)* ("star") + *[Macil](/wiki/Macil)* ("sword, forged sword blade, cutting sword").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
