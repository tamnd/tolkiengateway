**Elboron** was the second [Prince of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien), and [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor).

## History {#History}

Elboron was the son of [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) and [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-2">[2]</a></sup> Upon his father's death in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [82](/wiki/Fourth_Age_82 "Fourth Age 82"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> he became the second [Prince of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien) and the [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

Elboron was either the father or uncle of [Barahir](</wiki/Barahir_(grandson_of_Faramir)> "Barahir (grandson of Faramir)"), the grandson of Faramir who wrote *[The Tale of Aragorn and Arwen](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Aragorn_and_Arwen)*.<sup id="cite_ref-prologue_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prologue-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Elboron* may mean "Enduring Star", from *[elen](/wiki/Elen "Elen")* ("star") and *[boron](/wiki/Boron "Boron")* ("long-lasting, enduring").
