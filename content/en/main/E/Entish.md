**Entish** or **Old Entish** was the slow, sonorous, rumbling and ancient language of the [Ents](/wiki/Ents).

In ancient days, after meeting the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), the Ents developed the desire for a spoken language. The language that resulted from this desire was unlike any among the other [races](/wiki/Races "Races") of Middle-earth. It was slow, sonorous, agglomerated, repetitive, and extremely long-winded. The lore-masters of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) made no attempt to represent it in writing, as its many vowel-shades and tonal distinctions were far too numerous to do so.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Other examples are limited, with one being *burárum*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3">[3]</a></sup> an Entish noise of disgust.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The recording of *[a-lalla-lalla-rumba-kamanda-lind-or-burúmë](/wiki/A-lalla-lalla-rumba-kamanda-lind-or-bur%C3%BAm%C3%AB "A-lalla-lalla-rumba-kamanda-lind-or-burúmë")*, a part of the name for a *hill,*<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> is described as "the only extant (probably very inaccurate)" fragment of Entish.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Usage of Elvish among the Ents {#Usage_of_Elvish_among_the_Ents}

The Ents found [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) to be a lovely language and adapted it after their fashion to everyday use, along with its vocabulary to Entish grammatical structure.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-6">[6]</a></sup> The individual words were easily understandable, although they formed lengthy run-on sentences of redundant adjectives. When Treebeard said "*Taurelilómëa-tumbalemorna Tumbaletaurëa Lómëanor"*<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>*,* he wanted to tell [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") that "there is a black shadow in the deep dales of the forest", however, the literal translation is "Forestmanyshadowed-deepvalleyblack Deepvalleyforested Gloomyland".<sup id="cite_ref-:1_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-6">[6]</a></sup> Additionally, when Treebeard described the Orcs, he said "evileyed-blackhanded-bowlegged-flinthearted-clawfingered-foulbellied-bloodthirsty," and added some Quenya: *morimaite-sincahonda* ("blackhanded" and "flinthearted").<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3">[3]</a></sup>
