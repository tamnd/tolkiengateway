**Estella Brandybuck, née Bolger**, was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Estella was the second child of [Odovacar Bolger](/wiki/Odovacar_Bolger) and [Rosamunda Took](/wiki/Rosamunda_Took). She was present at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).

Her older brother, [Fredegar](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger "Fredegar Bolger"), was well acquainted with [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> and Estella eventually married him after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) has suggested that *Estella* is related to the Spanish *Estrella*, derived from the Latin *stella*, both meaning "star".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Tolkien added Estella to the family trees in Appendix C in the mid-1960s during the revisions that led to the second edition of *The Lord of the Rings*. For unclear reasons<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> she did not initially appear in the hardcover edition, but only in the mass-market paperback edition published by [Ballantine Books](/wiki/Ballantine_Books). Estella was finally added to the hardcover edition after an update to the text in 1987.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>
