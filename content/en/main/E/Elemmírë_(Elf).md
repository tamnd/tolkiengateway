**Elemmírë** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌelemˈmiːre]</span>) was a [Vanyarin](/wiki/Vanyar "Vanyar") Elf, famed for the creation of a lament for the [Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor), the [Aldudénië](/wiki/Aldud%C3%A9ni%C3%AB). When [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") assaulted the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor"), the Vanyar and other Elves of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) were celebrating a festival on [Taniquetil](/wiki/Taniquetil), and so Elemmírë most likely personally witnessed the destruction of the Trees. The song the elf made to lament the death of the Two Trees was said to be known by all of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Elemmírë is obviously named after the [star](/wiki/Star "Star") [Elemmírë](/wiki/Elemm%C3%ADr%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Note that the form of the word does not allow us to know whether Elemmírë was a male or a female Elf.
