The **Eru-begotten**, otherwise known as the **First Elves**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or **Unbegotten**,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> were the very first 144 [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who were [awakened](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves "Awakening of the Elves") by [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) on the shores of [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen) during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). Each of them slept beside his or her spouse, and there were 72 such pairs.<sup id="cite_ref-Awakening_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Awakening-3">[3]</a></sup>

It should be noted the name **[Firstborn](/wiki/Elves "Elves")** did not apply as a demonym to the Eru-begotten only, but to all Elves *as a whole* instead.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The children of the Eru-begotten were known as the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=First-begotten&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="First-begotten (page does not exist)">First-begotten</a>, the "First Generation".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
