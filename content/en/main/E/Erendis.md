**Erendis** was the wife of [Tar-Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md), the sixth [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md). She came from a family of lower status than Tar-Aldarion, so she had a lesser life-span than he. Erendis loved the land of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), especially her own region of [Emerië](Emerië.md).

## History {#History}

Erendis was born in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [771](../S/Second_Age_771.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Chronology_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chronology-1">[1]</a></sup> being the daughter of [Beregar](../B/Beregar.md), a descendant of the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md), from which she inherited her dark hair and grey eyes. One day, her father brought her to the feast in which Prince Aldarion was proclaimed the [King's Heir](../K/King's_Heir.md). There her eyes were captured by the beauty and splendour of Aldarion. Queen [Almarian](../A/Almarian.md) also noticed her beauty, and later the young lady entered the household of the Queen, where she was held in great esteem. However, Aldarion did not notice her until the day he departed to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) on one of his many travels there.

Years later, Aldarion was forbidden to depart again, and the ladies of his family were not allowed to give him a bough of *[oiolairë](../O/Oiolairë.md)*, a traditional blessing of the Númenóreans. Despite disliking the harbor, Erendis came to Aldarion's ship, bringing the bough. At that moment Aldarion began loving her, and when he returned, he gave her a diamond in gratitude. When their betrothal was announced, Aldarion offered his wife-to-be other jewels but she declared that she only wanted the one jewel. She then set it in a silver fillet and had Aldarion bind it about her forehead. Thus she became known throughout the land as Tar-Elestirnë.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

Erendis and Aldarion were betrothed in the spring of [858](../S/Second_Age_858.md), but their engagement was extended by another voyage of Aldarion, so they married twelve years later.<sup id="cite_ref-Chronology_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chronology-1">[1]</a></sup> Though Erendis resented it, Aldarion insisted on continuing with his voyages to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), causing a rift between the two. Erendis took their daughter [Ancalimë](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md) with her and went to live on her own on her farm. When Aldarion returned, he demanded to have his daughter returned to him, which Erendis did grudgingly, but her pride kept her from returning to the court.

It is unclear how the [story of Aldarion and Erendis](../A/Aldarion_and_Erendis.md) ended. In her old age, Erendis dwelt in Emerië. When she heard that Tar-Aldarion was returning from his last voyage, she journeyed incognito to the haven of [Rómenna](../R/Rómenna.md); there it is said she "perished in the water".<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

After Erendis, the wearing of a white jewel upon the brow became a tradition among the kings and queens of Númenor, who wore it instead of a crown.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Later, in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), this tradition continued in [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) with the wearing of the [Elendilmir](Elendilmir.md), a star-like gem upon the brow.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The meaning of *Erendis* is unclear, but could be [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), being a compound of *[er](Er.md)* ("one, alone") + *[indis](../I/Indis.md#Etymology)* ("wife, bride"), therefore "Lonely Bride",<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> although we cannot know if this was a prophetic name or if it was given to her after her bad relationship with Aldarion.

## Other names {#Other_names}

She was widely known as <i><b>Tar-Elestirnë</b></i> ("Lady of the Star-brow"), given to her because of the white jewel in her forehead, that she wore until sorrow came.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:184</sup>

In one occasion, [Valandil](<../V/Valandil_(Lord_of_Andúnië).md>) called her *Uinéniel* ("Daughter of Uinen"), but she rejected such a name, proclaiming [Uinen](../U/Uinen.md) as her foe.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:182</sup>

While in her youth she was called the **Lady of the Westlands**,<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:180</sup> in her remote dwelling, she was called the **White Lady of Emerië**.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:194</sup>
