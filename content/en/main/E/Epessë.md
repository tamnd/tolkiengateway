**Epessë**, [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for **after-name**, is a nickname or honorific given to an elf at some point in his or her life. It is one of the **[anessi](Essë.md)**, or given names, of [High Elves](../H/High_Elves.md), along with the **[father-name](../F/Father-name.md)**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> and **[amilessë](../A/Amilessë.md)** ("mother-name").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> The epessë was a public name, and the person it was given to sometimes adopted it as one of his or her actual names.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

An example is *Gil-galad* ("Star of Radiance"), by which the last [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) [Ereinion](../G/Gil-galad.md) ("*Scion of Kings*") was chiefly remembered in legend. This eponymous epithet was given to him because his armour and shield were overlaid with silver and set with a device of white stars, shone from afar like a star in sunlight or moonlight and could be seen by Elvish eyes at a great distance if he stood upon a height.<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-4">[3]</a></sup> The element 'galad' also appears in an epessë *Galadriel*, which is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name that belonged to Ereinion's kinswoman [Artanis](../G/Galadriel.md) and translated as "maiden crowned with a garland of bright radiance".<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Epessë* comes from *[epe](Epe.md)* ("after") + *[essë](Essë.md)* ("name").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
