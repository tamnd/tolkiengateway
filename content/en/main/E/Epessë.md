**Epessë**, [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for **after-name**, is a nickname or honorific given to an elf at some point in his or her life. It is one of the **[anessi](/wiki/Ess%C3%AB "Essë")**, or given names, of [High Elves](/wiki/High_Elves), along with the **[father-name](/wiki/Father-name "Father-name")**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> and **[amilessë](/wiki/Amiless%C3%AB "Amilessë")** ("mother-name").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> The epessë was a public name, and the person it was given to sometimes adopted it as one of his or her actual names.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

An example is *Gil-galad* ("Star of Radiance"), by which the last [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor) [Ereinion](/wiki/Gil-galad "Gil-galad") ("*Scion of Kings*") was chiefly remembered in legend. This eponymous epithet was given to him because his armour and shield were overlaid with silver and set with a device of white stars, shone from afar like a star in sunlight or moonlight and could be seen by Elvish eyes at a great distance if he stood upon a height.<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-4">[3]</a></sup> The element 'galad' also appears in an epessë *Galadriel*, which is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name that belonged to Ereinion's kinswoman [Artanis](/wiki/Galadriel "Galadriel") and translated as "maiden crowned with a garland of bright radiance".<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Epessë* comes from *[epe](/wiki/Epe "Epe")* ("after") + *[essë](/wiki/Ess%C3%AB "Essë")* ("name").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
