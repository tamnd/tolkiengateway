**Estirin** was a town in the west of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Qen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Qen-1">[1]</a></sup> It was identified by [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) with <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Exeter</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Qen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Qen-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Estirin* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya). It is glossed as "Outer Tower" by *[Eldamo](/wiki/Eldamo)*, potentially being a compound of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Es-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Es- (page does not exist)">es-</a></i> ("out") + *[tirin](/wiki/Tirin "Tirin")* ("tower").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<i><b>Estirios</b></i>, an alternative form of the name, is given in the *[Qenya Lexicon](/wiki/Qenya_Lexicon)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Qen_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Qen-1">[1]</a></sup>
