**Elendur** means "Servant of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves)" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (from *[elen](/wiki/Elen "Elen")*, "star, Elf" and *[-ndur](/wiki/-ndur)*, "servant of"), and may refer to:

- [Elendur (son of Isildur)](</wiki/Elendur_(son_of_Isildur)>) ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3299](/wiki/Second_Age_3299 "Second Age 3299") - [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2](/wiki/Third_Age_2 "Third Age 2")), who died with [his father](/wiki/Isildur "Isildur") in the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields).
- [Elendur (King of Arnor)](</wiki/Elendur_(King_of_Arnor)>) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [552](/wiki/Third_Age_552 "Third Age 552") - [777](/wiki/Third_Age_777 "Third Age 777")), the ninth and penultimate [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor "Kings of Arnor") of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor).
