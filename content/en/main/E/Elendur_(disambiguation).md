**Elendur** means "Servant of the [Elves](Elves.md)" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) (from *[elen](Elen.md)*, "star, Elf" and *[-ndur](../_/-ndur.md)*, "servant of"), and may refer to:

- [Elendur (son of Isildur)](<Elendur_(son_of_Isildur).md>) ([S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3299](../S/Second_Age_3299.md) - [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2](../T/Third_Age_2.md)), who died with [his father](../I/Isildur.md) in the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](../D/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields.md).
- [Elendur (King of Arnor)](<Elendur_(King_of_Arnor).md>) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [552](../T/Third_Age_552.md) - [777](../T/Third_Age_777.md)), the ninth and penultimate [King](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md) of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md).
