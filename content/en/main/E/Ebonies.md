**Ebonies** were a type of [tree](/wiki/Trees "Trees"). [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins")'s [song of Eärendil](/wiki/Song_of_E%C3%A4rendil "Song of Eärendil") mentioned [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) as having "arrows shorn of ebony".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1994: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="/wiki/Treasures_of_Middle-earth_(2nd_edition)" title="Treasures of Middle-earth (2nd edition)">Treasures of Middle-earth</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>It was described as a rare tropical jet black tree known to the people of Pel, who call it <i>Usamity</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-fourthpage_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fourthpage-2">[2]</a></sup></dd></dl>
