**Nicole Evangeline Lilly** (born [3 August](/wiki/3_August), [1979](/wiki/1979) in Fort Saskatchewan, Alberta) is a Canadian actress. She portrayed [Tauriel](/wiki/Tauriel) in the [*The Hobbit film series*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She was raised in British Columbia by her mother and father and is the middle of three sisters.

## Trivia {#Trivia}

She is fluent in French<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">[2]</a></sup>

Prior to *Lost*, her only acting credits had been commercials and un-credited parts as an extra.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">[2]</a></sup>

From 2004 to 2009 she was in relationship with [Dominic Monaghan](/wiki/Dominic_Monaghan), her co-star from the series <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_(TV_series)" title="wikipedia:Lost (TV series)">Lost</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Returned to work three months after giving birth to begin filming *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)*<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">[2]</a></sup>
