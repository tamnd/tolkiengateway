<i><b>English Studies</b></i> **(December 1947)** is an issue of the journal <i><a class="external text" href="http://www.tandf.co.uk/journals/nest" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">English Studies</a></i>. The 'Notes and News' section includes the essay "'iþþlen' in *Sawles Warde*" by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [S.R.T.O. d'Ardenne](/wiki/Simonne_d%27Ardenne "Simonne d'Ardenne") (see pp. 168–170). The essay discusses a corrupted or misread word in printed editions of an early manuscript of the [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) homily <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sawles_Warde" title="wikipedia:Sawles Warde">Sawles Warde</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>

The essay was published separately as an offprint in paper wrappers in 1947, and the whole journal was reprinted in 1978.<sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>
