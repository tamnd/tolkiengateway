**Eric Velhagen** is an American fantasy artist whose work has been featured in various publications and exhibitions.<sup id="cite_ref-IBA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IBA-1">[1]</a></sup>

Tolkien's books were one the main reasons Velhagen became a fantasy artist, and throughout his career he has painted many scenes set in Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-Rehs_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rehs-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> His painting “[Roar of Bruinen](/wiki/File:Eric_Velhagen_-_Roar_of_Bruinen.jpg "File:Eric Velhagen - Roar of Bruinen.jpg")” was included the 17th edition of the artbook series <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectrum:_The_Best_In_Contemporary_Fantastic_Art" title="wikipedia:Spectrum: The Best In Contemporary Fantastic Art">Spectrum</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Rehs_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rehs-2">[2]</a></sup>

He also illustrated a single piece of alternate art for *[Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
