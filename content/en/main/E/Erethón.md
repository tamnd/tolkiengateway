**Erethón**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) Archer and member of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Naith_Company&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Naith Company (page does not exist)">Naith Company</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He fought in [the Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg "Battle of the Hornburg").

Erethón is an original creation for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* film. He is portrayed by [Alexander Lindsay](/wiki/Alexander_Lindsay).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Erethón was a [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) who lived in [the Naith](/wiki/Naith "Naith") of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien). He was skilled with the bow and, at close range, his accuracy was uncanny. Erethón joined the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Naith_Company&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Naith Company (page does not exist)">Naith Company</a>, a veteran group from Lothlórien's south, and gained the rank of Lieutenant.

When the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), under leadership from [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) [King](/wiki/King_of_Rohan "King of Rohan"), retreated to [the Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg "Hornburg") in [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep), [Saruman the White](/wiki/Saruman "Saruman") sent an army of over ten thousand [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) to assail them. When Lord [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and Lady [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and Lord [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) of Lothlórien heard of this, they decided to honour the ages-old allegiance between [Men](/wiki/Men) and [Elves](/wiki/Elves). They sent a join force of Elves, led by [Haldir](/wiki/Haldir), to assist the Rohirrim in their battle against the Uruks. The Naith Company was part of this force sent to the Hornburg.

The Naith Company were stationed on the front line of the Deeping Wall. As the Uruks reached the fortress, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) walked passed the Elves of the front line, yelling "Show them no mercy! For you shall receive none!" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). The battle commenced and the Company concentrated their fire on the Uruk war machines.

Ultimately, Haldir and every Elven reinforcement were slain during the Battle. Their efforts were not in vain, however, as the few Rohirrim left alive were able to hold out until [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) arrived with [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer)'s [Éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored) and forced the Uruks to retreat into the forest, where they were killed by [Huorns](/wiki/Huorns).
