**eneg** is the number six (6, VI) in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).

In [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), the precursor of Sindarin, "six" is <i><b>eneg</b></i>. The word derives from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") ÉNEK-.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
