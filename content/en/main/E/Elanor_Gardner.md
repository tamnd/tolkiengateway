**Elanor Fairbairn (née Gamgee or Gardner)** was the first child of Master [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and his wife [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton).

## Life {#Life}

Called "The Fair" for her golden hair, she was born 25 March [1421](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021") by [Shire Reckoning](/wiki/Shire_Calendar "Shire Calendar") (which was [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021")), which was at the same time the first day of the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) in Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Elanor was also the only one of Samwise's children known to the Ringbearer, [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), and her name was a suggestion by him.<sup id="cite_ref-Havens_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Havens-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1436](/wiki/Fourth_Age_15 "Fourth Age 15"), Elanor spoke with her father about the importance of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) and the nearing arrival of King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") and Queen Arwen for a visit. Elanor's affectionate name for her father is "Sam-dad", while Sam calls her "*Elanorellë*".<sup id="cite_ref-Epilogue_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Epilogue-1">[1]</a></sup> When Elessar arrived, Elanor became a maid of honour to Queen [Arwen Evenstar](/wiki/Arwen "Arwen"), and in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1442](/wiki/Fourth_Age_21 "Fourth Age 21") she rode to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) with her parents and stayed there for a year. In [1451](/wiki/Fourth_Age_30 "Fourth Age 30") she married [Fastred of Greenholm](/wiki/Fastred_of_Greenholm). [Four years later](/wiki/Fourth_Age_34 "Fourth Age 34"), Fastred was named [Warden of Westmarch](/wiki/Warden_of_Westmarch) by the [Thain](/wiki/Thain) at the request of Samwise, and the couple moved to the [Undertowers](/wiki/Undertowers) on the [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills).<sup id="cite_ref-later_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-later-4">[4]</a></sup>

She last saw her father in [1482](/wiki/Fourth_Age_61 "Fourth Age 61") when he came to the [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills), passing to her the [Red Book](/wiki/Red_Book). so Elanor and her line became the keepers of the [Red Book of Westmarch](/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch), and handed down the tradition that Sam passed to the [Undying Lands](/wiki/Undying_Lands).<sup id="cite_ref-later_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-later-4">[4]</a></sup>

Her two children were [Elfstan](/wiki/Elfstan_Fairbairn "Elfstan Fairbairn"), born in [1454](/wiki/Fourth_Age_33 "Fourth Age 33"),<sup id="cite_ref-later_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-later-4">[4]</a></sup> and [Fíriel](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel_Fairbairn "Fíriel Fairbairn").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Her descendants (becoming known as the [Fairbairns of the Towers](/wiki/Fairbairn_Family "Fairbairn Family")) would live for many generations.<sup id="cite_ref-later_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-later-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is a reference to the *[sun-star](</wiki/Elanor_(flower)> "Elanor (flower)")*, a little golden flower blooming in the land of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) — this perfectly satisfied Sam's desire for a name that was both [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) and yet at the same time a simple flower-name as many hobbit-lasses had.<sup id="cite_ref-Havens_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Havens-3">[3]</a></sup>
