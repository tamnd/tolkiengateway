**Eorl the Young** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) 2485 – 2545, aged 60 years) was the son of [Léod](/wiki/L%C3%A9od) of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), and founder of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Life {#Life}

When Eorl was only 16 years old, his father was killed while trying to tame the horse [Felaróf](/wiki/Felar%C3%B3f), making Eorl [Lord of the Éothéod](/wiki/Lord_of_the_%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) at that young age. Young Eorl swore to avenge his father and managed to tame the horse. He "doomed" the horse to carry him for the rest of their lives, and the horse seemingly accepted this burden.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"), [Borondir Udalraph](/wiki/Borondir "Borondir") came to the north seeking aid, as the [South-kingdom](/wiki/South-kingdom) was attacked by the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and the [Balchoth](/wiki/Balchoth). On April 15, Eorl came to the [Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Field_of_Celebrant), and joined the [battle](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant "Battle of the Field of Celebrant"). For his service to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), he was granted [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) to dwell in. As a return, Eorl and his descendants would have to come to the aid of Gondor when they requested; this was known as the [Oath of Eorl](/wiki/Oath_of_Eorl).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Eorl ruled from a green hill under the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-1">[1]</a></sup>

Even in his older years Eorl never lost his yellow hair. He died in battle against the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2545](/wiki/Third_Age_2545 "Third Age 2545"). He was buried in the first royal mound, and Felaróf was buried with him.<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

Eorl was the founder of Rohan, its first King, the ancestor of the [House of Eorl](/wiki/House_of_Eorl) and all the [Kings of Rohan](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan). His horse Felaróf was the first of the [Mearas](/wiki/Mearas).

Eorl was known as "the Young" because he became [Lord of the Éothéod](/wiki/Lord_of_the_%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) at the age of 16 and because he never lost his yellow hair.

His Oath would be answered at least thrice: once by [Folcred](/wiki/Folcred) and [Fastred](</wiki/Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine)> "Fastred (son of Folcwine)"), once by [Théoden Ednew](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden "Théoden") and by [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *Eorl* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) translation of genuine [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) <i><b>þuron</b></i>, which has perhaps influence from [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [TUR](/wiki/TUR).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

**Eorl** is an Old English word referring to a person of noble rank. It is the ancestor of modern "earl". It was the highest rank of nobility in North Germanic culture, not introduced to Britain until the late 9th century, when it replaced the equivalent Old English title *ealdorman*. The Old Norse equivalent is *jarl.*<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Þórsteinn Thorarensen](/wiki/%C3%9E%C3%B3rsteinn_Thorarensen) used *Jarl* as the name for the character throughout his Icelandic translation of *The Lord of the Rings*, along with [Hjálmur](/wiki/Helm "Helm"), for Helm, [Þengill](/wiki/Thengel "Thengel") for Thengel, [Þjódan](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden "Théoden") for Théoden, and [Jómar](/wiki/%C3%89omer "Éomer") for Éomer.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Compare *[Ceorl](/wiki/Ceorl)*, the second rank.
