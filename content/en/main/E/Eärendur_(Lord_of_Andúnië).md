**Eärendur** (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌe.aˈrendur]</span>) was the fifteenth [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië") in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor)<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Palantir_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup> in the days of king [Ar-Sakalthôr](/wiki/Ar-Sakalth%C3%B4r).<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup> Eärendur was the brother of [Lindórië](/wiki/Lind%C3%B3ri%C3%AB), who would be the grandmother of the wise King [Tar-Palantir](/wiki/Tar-Palantir).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Palantir_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup>

As Lord of [Andúnië](/wiki/And%C3%BAni%C3%AB), Eärendur was also the ancestor of the Kings of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") was his direct descendant through no less than forty-four generations.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eärendur* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. It means "(professional) mariner".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> As Paul Strack explains, a more literal translation might be "Servant of the Sea". It is a compound of [eär](/wiki/E%C3%A4r "Eär") ("sea") and [-(n)dur](/wiki/-ndur "-ndur") ("servant").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
