**Eärendur** (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌe.aˈrendur]</span>) was the fifteenth [Lord of Andúnië](../L/Lords_of_Andúnië.md) in [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Palantir_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup> in the days of king [Ar-Sakalthôr](../A/Ar-Sakalthôr.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup> Eärendur was the brother of [Lindórië](../L/Lindórië.md), who would be the grandmother of the wise King [Tar-Palantir](../T/Tar-Palantir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Palantir_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup>

As Lord of [Andúnië](../A/Andúnië.md), Eärendur was also the ancestor of the Kings of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). [Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn.md) was his direct descendant through no less than forty-four generations.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eärendur* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. It means "(professional) mariner".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> As Paul Strack explains, a more literal translation might be "Servant of the Sea". It is a compound of [eär](Eär.md) ("sea") and [-(n)dur](../_/-ndur.md) ("servant").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
