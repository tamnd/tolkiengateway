The word <i><b>elanor</b></i> means "Star-sun" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (from *[êl](/wiki/%C3%8Al "Êl")*, "star" and *anor*, "[Sun](/wiki/Sun)"), and can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- *[elanor](</wiki/Elanor_(flower)> "Elanor (flower)")*, a flower that grew in the forests of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).
- **[Elanor Gardner](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner) (born [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021")), the first child of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee).**

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- *[Elanor (soundtrack)](</wiki/Elanor_(soundtrack)>)*, the sixth song of Disc 4 of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King - The Complete Recordings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_-_The_Complete_Recordings)*.
- [**Elanor** Brandyfoot](/wiki/Elanor_Brandyfoot "Elanor Brandyfoot"), a character from *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* series.

## Fandom {#Fandom}

- [Elanor (Tolkien Society)](</wiki/Elanor_(Tolkien_Society)>), former Belgian Tolkien Society, succeeded by the Flemish Fantasy Society of the same name.
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Elanor_(journal)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Elanor (journal) (page does not exist)">Elanor (journal)</a></i>, a journal of the Japanese Tolkien Society *[Shiro no Norite](/wiki/Shiro_no_Norite)*.
