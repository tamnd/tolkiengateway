**Ermon** was, along with [Elmir](/wiki/Elmir), one of the first two [Men](/wiki/Men) to be [awakened](/wiki/Awakening_of_Men "Awakening of Men") at [Murmenalda](/wiki/Murmenalda), according to the early [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in some of the outlines for the continuation of *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Mur_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mur-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Parma_Eldalamberon_10_(back_cover).png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/f/f3/Parma_Eldalamberon_10_%28back_cover%29.png/200px-Parma_Eldalamberon_10_%28back_cover%29.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="261" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/f/f3/Parma_Eldalamberon_10_%28back_cover%29.png/300px-Parma_Eldalamberon_10_%28back_cover%29.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/f/f3/Parma_Eldalamberon_10_%28back_cover%29.png/400px-Parma_Eldalamberon_10_%28back_cover%29.png 2x" data-file-width="1614" data-file-height="2110"/></a><figcaption><i>Nuin, Father of Speech</i> by <a href="/wiki/Tom_Loback" title="Tom Loback">Tom Loback</a>. Art from the back cover of <i><a href="/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_10" title="Parma Eldalamberon 10">Parma Eldalamberon 10</a></i>.</figcaption></figure>

Ermon and Elmir were awakened by an [Elf](/wiki/Elf) called [Nuin](/wiki/Nuin), one of the [Ilkorindi](/wiki/Ilkorindi), who, against the warnings of the wizard [Tû](</wiki/T%C3%BB_(king_of_the_Hisildi)> "Tû (king of the Hisildi)") his lord, liked to wander far from his home in [Palisor](/wiki/Palisor) all the way to Murmenalda, and in one of these wanderings he stumbled upon the sleeping bodies of [Men](/wiki/Men), the second children of [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar).

There, after waking Ermon and Elmir, Nuin taught them speech, and they, alone of all Men, witnessed the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) [rising in the West and setting in the East](/wiki/The_Tale_of_the_Sun_and_Moon "The Tale of the Sun and Moon"); sometime after, the rest of mankind awoke as well, and Nuin, Ermon and Elmir taught *them* speech in return - and the race of Men "grew in stature, and gathered knowledge of the [Dark Elves](/wiki/Hisildi "Hisildi")".

Afterwards, a servant of [Melko](/wiki/Melko) called [Fankil](/wiki/Fankil) turned most Men against the Elves, and in the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=War_of_Palisor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="War of Palisor (page does not exist)">first war</a> between Elves and [Goblins](/wiki/Goblins), only the people of Ermon fought on the side of the Elves. Following the war, Men were scattered in all directions, some of them heading for "eastern deserts and the southern forests", but some Men, especially Ermon and his folk, went west, along with some of the Ilkorindi.<sup id="cite_ref-Mur_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mur-2">[2]</a></sup>

Ermon, accompanied by an Elf called [Tareg](/wiki/Tareg), eventually arrived to the river [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) in the northwest of the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands), where they met the exiled [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes), who were about to prepare for a war with Melko. There, they joined forces with them, and, at a place called <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gorfalong&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gorfalong (page does not exist)">Gorfalong</a>, fought a [great battle](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Nirnaeth Arnoediad") with Melko and his forces, many of whom were Men corrupted by him.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Ermon's eventual fate is unknown, but he evidently left descendants, since in one outline, [Ing](/wiki/Ing) King of [Luthany](/wiki/Luthany) was said to be one of them.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ermon* might be in [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin), according to [Christopher Gilson](/wiki/Christopher_Gilson), but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The character of Ermon might have been inspired by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ask_and_Embla" title="wikipedia:Ask and Embla">Askr</a>, the first man created by the gods (out of [ash](/wiki/Ashes "Ashes")-tree) in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norse_mythology" title="wikipedia:Norse mythology">Norse mythology</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>
