**Eduardo Segura Fernández** (born [1967](/wiki/1967)) is a Spanish author and translator. He made his Doctoral thesis on *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, which was later published as *El Viaje del Anillo*.

Segura also donated the [Letter to David L. Sands](/wiki/Letter_to_David_L._Sands) to the [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University).<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>

He currently works as a professor of English language and literature at the University of Granada<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and as an advisor for the Spanish translation of the script and subtitles of [The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
