**Eduardo Segura Fernández** (born [1967](../_/1967.md)) is a Spanish author and translator. He made his Doctoral thesis on *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, which was later published as *El Viaje del Anillo*.

Segura also donated the [Letter to David L. Sands](../L/Letter_to_David_L._Sands.md) to the [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>

He currently works as a professor of English language and literature at the University of Granada<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and as an advisor for the Spanish translation of the script and subtitles of [The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
