**Eldamo** (<i><b>El</b>vish <b>Da</b>ta <b>Mo</b>del</i>), or **An Elvish Lexicon**, is a linguistic website launched in [2008](/wiki/2008), compiling a lexicon of Tolkien's invented [languages](/wiki/Languages "Languages"). The website is maintained by Paul Strack and, despite its name, does include all of Tolkien's languages, not only the [Eldarin](/wiki/Eldarin) family.

The website does not only work as a dictionary, but as a full lexicon that analyzes the relationships between words and the conceptual development of Tolkien's languages through his lifetime. Therefore the invented languages are divided by three periods: Early (1910–30), Middle (1930–50) and Late (1950–73). The entries are extensively sourced.

It also contains a detailed analysis of the grammar and phonetic development of [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic). Optionally, a lexicon of commonly used and well accepted [Neo-Elvish](/wiki/Neo-Elvish) vocabulary is included, drawn from [Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger)'s <i><a class="external text" href="https://folk.uib.no/hnohf/nqnt.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">New Testament in Neo-Quenya</a></i>, or the <i><a class="external text" href="http://www.elvish.org/gwaith/ppq.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Parma Penyanë Quettaron</a></i>, among others.

No new Tolkien word were added since the v0.8.6 update (May 2023); among other rare missing sources, the Eldamo lexicon does not include the [Marquette](/wiki/Marquette_University "Marquette University") [Westron](/wiki/Westron) words published in [*Tyalië Tyelelliéva* 17](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va_17 "Tyalië Tyelelliéva 17") by [Lisa Star](/wiki/Lisa_Star).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Its content is derived from an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XML_database" title="wikipedia:XML database">XML data model</a>, which can be used freely in accordance with the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Commons_license" title="wikipedia:Creative Commons license">Creative Commons license</a> (with attribution).

As can be noticed, [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway) uses Eldamo widely as reference for names that are not glossed in the main sources.
