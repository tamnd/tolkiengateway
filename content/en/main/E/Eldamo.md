**Eldamo** (<i><b>El</b>vish <b>Da</b>ta <b>Mo</b>del</i>), or **An Elvish Lexicon**, is a linguistic website launched in [2008](../_/2008.md), compiling a lexicon of Tolkien's invented [languages](../L/Languages.md). The website is maintained by Paul Strack and, despite its name, does include all of Tolkien's languages, not only the [Eldarin](Eldarin.md) family.

The website does not only work as a dictionary, but as a full lexicon that analyzes the relationships between words and the conceptual development of Tolkien's languages through his lifetime. Therefore the invented languages are divided by three periods: Early (1910–30), Middle (1930–50) and Late (1950–73). The entries are extensively sourced.

It also contains a detailed analysis of the grammar and phonetic development of [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) and [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md). Optionally, a lexicon of commonly used and well accepted [Neo-Elvish](../N/Neo-Elvish.md) vocabulary is included, drawn from [Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md)'s <i><a class="external text" href="https://folk.uib.no/hnohf/nqnt.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">New Testament in Neo-Quenya</a></i>, or the <i><a class="external text" href="http://www.elvish.org/gwaith/ppq.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Parma Penyanë Quettaron</a></i>, among others.

No new Tolkien word were added since the v0.8.6 update (May 2023); among other rare missing sources, the Eldamo lexicon does not include the [Marquette](../M/Marquette_University.md) [Westron](../W/Westron.md) words published in [*Tyalië Tyelelliéva* 17](../T/Tyalië_Tyelelliéva_17.md) by [Lisa Star](../L/Lisa_Star.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Its content is derived from an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XML_database" title="wikipedia:XML database">XML data model</a>, which can be used freely in accordance with the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Commons_license" title="wikipedia:Creative Commons license">Creative Commons license</a> (with attribution).

As can be noticed, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) uses Eldamo widely as reference for names that are not glossed in the main sources.
