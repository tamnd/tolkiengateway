**Elvenesse** was a term whose meaning is not clearly defined, but which seems to refer to all the lands of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), both west and east of the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The ending *-ess* (also in [Westernesse](/wiki/Westernesse)) was used in romance literature for fictional lands that had partly francized names (as in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyonesse" title="wikipedia:Lyonesse">Lyonesse</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matter_of_Britain" title="wikipedia:Matter of Britain">Arthurian legends</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup>
