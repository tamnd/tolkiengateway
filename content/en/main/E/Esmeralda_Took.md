**Esmeralda Brandybuck**, née **Took**, was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Esmeralda was the youngest of the four daughters of [Adalgrim Took](/wiki/Adalgrim_Took). She married [Saradoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Saradoc_Brandybuck), making the [Master of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland) [Rorimac Brandybuck](/wiki/Rorimac_Brandybuck), her father-in-law. She and Saradoc had a son, [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck)

She attended the [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001") with her family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-took_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-took-2">[2]</a></sup> When Bilbo disappeared, Master Rorimac attempted to calm her, saying that the "Mad Baggins" just left for another adventure.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Later Esmeralda herself became mistress of Buckland and of [Brandy Hall](/wiki/Brandy_Hall). Her older brother [Paladin Took II](/wiki/Paladin_Took_II) also became the [Thain](/wiki/Thain). Her son Meriadoc was cousin with the Thain's children,<sup id="cite_ref-took_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-took-2">[2]</a></sup> and especially close friends with his cousin [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
