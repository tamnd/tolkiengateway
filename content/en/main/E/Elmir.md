**Elmir** was, along with [Ermon](Ermon.md), one of the first two [Men](../M/Men.md) to be [awakened](../A/Awakening_of_Men.md) at [Murmenalda](../M/Murmenalda.md), according to the early [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in some of the outlines for the continuation of *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Mur_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mur-4">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/P/Parma_Eldalamberon_10_(back_cover).png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/f/f3/Parma_Eldalamberon_10_%28back_cover%29.png/200px-Parma_Eldalamberon_10_%28back_cover%29.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="261" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/f/f3/Parma_Eldalamberon_10_%28back_cover%29.png/300px-Parma_Eldalamberon_10_%28back_cover%29.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/f/f3/Parma_Eldalamberon_10_%28back_cover%29.png/400px-Parma_Eldalamberon_10_%28back_cover%29.png 2x" data-file-width="1614" data-file-height="2110"/></a><figcaption><i>Nuin, Father of Speech</i> by <a href="../T/Tom_Loback.md" title="Tom Loback">Tom Loback</a>. Art from the back cover of <i><a href="../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_10.md" title="Parma Eldalamberon 10">Parma Eldalamberon 10</a></i>.</figcaption></figure>

Elmir and Ermon were awakened by an [Elf](Elf.md) called [Nuin](../N/Nuin.md), one of the [Ilkorindi](../I/Ilkorindi.md), who, against the warnings of the wizard [Tû](<../T/Tû_(king_of_the_Hisildi).md>) his lord, liked to wander far from his home in [Palisor](../P/Palisor.md) all the way to Murmenalda, and in one of these wanderings he stumbled upon the sleeping bodies of [Men](../M/Men.md), the second children of [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md).

There, after waking Elmir and Ermon, Nuin taught them speech, and they, alone of all Men, witnessed the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) [rising in the West and setting in the East](../T/The_Tale_of_the_Sun_and_Moon.md); sometime after, the rest of mankind awoke as well, and Nuin, Elmir and Ermon taught *them* speech in return - and the race of Men "grew in stature, and gathered knowledge of the [Dark Elves](../H/Hisildi.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-Mur_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mur-4">[3]</a></sup>

Afterwards, a servant of [Melko](../M/Melko.md) called [Fankil](../F/Fankil.md) turned most Men against the Elves, and in the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=War_of_Palisor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="War of Palisor (page does not exist)">first war</a> between Elves and [Goblins](../G/Goblins.md), it is said that *only* the people of Ermon fought on the side of the Elves,<sup id="cite_ref-Mur_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mur-4">[3]</a></sup> possibly implying that, either Elmir and his folk fought on the side of Melko, or that Elmir died before the war.

Ultimately, however, Elmir's fate is unknown.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Elmir* might be in [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md), according to [Christopher Gilson](../C/Christopher_Gilson.md), but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
