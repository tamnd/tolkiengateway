**Esteldín** is the unofficial capital of the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md) in the game *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md).*

## History {#History}

Esteldín is built upon the ruins of the city Dolindîr, one of the Five Towns of Arthedain. The Five Towns were once the most important of the North, but now all but Dolindîr are desterted. They were:<sup id="cite_ref-Arthedain_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arthedain-1">[1]</a></sup>

- Dolindîr, now Esteldín
- Henneth Rhún, established in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1409](../T/Third_Age_1409.md)
- Duintham, established in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1249&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1249 (page does not exist)">1249</a>
- Carnoglin, established between [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1272](../T/Third_Age_1272.md) and [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1349](../T/Third_Age_1349.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Ost Ardúlin, now swarmed with orcs<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Refuge of Esteldín itself was established long after the Five Towns had become ruins, by the [Ranger](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) named Arasuil, son of Arahad.<sup id="cite_ref-Arthedain_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arthedain-1">[1]</a></sup> At the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), [Halbarad](../H/Halbarad.md) was in command of the city. He sends the players out to help the inhabitants of the North Downs and eventually helps in the Battle of the Trestlebridge.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

Esteldín is located in the mountains of Kingsfell in the North Downs. It is made in a vale in the mountains, and it lies hidden, not even being mentioned on the map. The main enterance lies in the west, where you come to the Forecourt after walking down the hidden street. Behind the Forecourt is the Central Courtyard, where Halbarand's library is, and behind that lies the Craftmen's Courtyard, where all the crafting facilities are. From here you can exit the city by the east entrance, leading you to Nan Amlug.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The inhabitants are the [Rangers of Esteldín](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md), also a reputation faction in the game. They are the remnants of the people of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md) and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md). They are the only power, beside the Elves of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) that might still offer resistance against the rise of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Esteldín* is said to mean "Hidden Hope" in Sindarin on the LOTRO-Wiki,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> with *estel* meaning "hope" and *dín* maybe coming from *[din](../D/Din.md)*, meaning "silent".

*Dolindîr* is Sindarin made out of *dolin*, meaning "hidden"<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and *dîr*, meaning "man". *dîr* is an agental ending not typically used for place names but may translate as "Hidden City of Men".
