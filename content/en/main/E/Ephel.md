<i><b>Ephel</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) noun which means "encircling fence"<sup id="cite_ref-silmindex_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmindex-1">[1]</a></sup> or "outer fence".<sup id="cite_ref-silmap_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmap-2">[2]</a></sup> It is a combination of *et* and *pel*.<sup id="cite_ref-silmap_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmap-2">[2]</a></sup>

Examples:

- [Ephel Brandir](/wiki/Ephel_Brandir), "the encircling fence of [Brandir](/wiki/Brandir)";<sup id="cite_ref-silmindex_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmindex-1">[1]</a></sup> often simply called *the Ephel*<sup id="cite_ref-silmtt_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmtt-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath), "Fence of Shadow";<sup id="cite_ref-silmindex_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmindex-1">[1]</a></sup> more commonly known as the "Mountains of Shadow"<sup id="cite_ref-lotrbg_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lotrbg-4">[4]</a></sup>
