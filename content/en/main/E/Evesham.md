[Mabel Suffield](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield)'s family lived in **Evesham**, Worcestershire for generations, and [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s brother [Hilary](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien "Hilary Tolkien")'s farm was located near Evesham.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Tolkien said that Worcestershire was more like home to him than any other part of the world.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
