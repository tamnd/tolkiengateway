**Ened** was a small island that lay straight out from the Firth of [Drengist](/wiki/Drengist), southwest of [Lammoth](/wiki/Lammoth) and northwest of [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast). The island was roughly four times longer than it was wide, and its long axis lined up with the Firth.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> There is no mention of the island in the annals of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) so it may have been uninhabited.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is never glossed, but it is proably related with the word <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Enedh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Enedh (page does not exist)">enedh</a></i> ("centre, middle").

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the "[Map of Beleriand and the Lands to the North](/wiki/Map_of_Beleriand_and_the_Lands_to_the_North)" in *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* the island was shown but unnamed. In *[The War of the Jewels](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels)*, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) said of the [Second *Silmarillion* Map](/wiki/The_Second_%27Silmarillion%27_Map "The Second 'Silmarillion' Map") that over the years it had become covered with alterations and additions of names and features. On Sheet 1 of this map the island bears the name "Ened" - concerning the name Christopher stated that he could cast no light.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
