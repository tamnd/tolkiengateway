**Ened** was a small island that lay straight out from the Firth of [Drengist](../D/Drengist.md), southwest of [Lammoth](../L/Lammoth.md) and northwest of [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md). The island was roughly four times longer than it was wide, and its long axis lined up with the Firth.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> There is no mention of the island in the annals of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) so it may have been uninhabited.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is never glossed, but it is proably related with the word <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Enedh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Enedh (page does not exist)">enedh</a></i> ("centre, middle").

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the "[Map of Beleriand and the Lands to the North](../M/Map_of_Beleriand_and_the_Lands_to_the_North.md)" in *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* the island was shown but unnamed. In *[The War of the Jewels](../T/The_War_of_the_Jewels.md)*, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) said of the [Second *Silmarillion* Map](../T/The_Second_'Silmarillion'_Map.md) that over the years it had become covered with alterations and additions of names and features. On Sheet 1 of this map the island bears the name "Ened" - concerning the name Christopher stated that he could cast no light.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
