**Elemmírë** was one of the lights kindled by [Varda](/wiki/Varda) far in the past, to welcome the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) to [Arda](/wiki/Arda) on their awakening. It is described in [Quenta Silmarillion](/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion) as a star, but in fact it was most likely identified with one of the planets.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is given as the name of a [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) Elf who composed the [Aldudénië](/wiki/Aldud%C3%A9ni%C3%AB) (the Lament for the Two Trees).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Elemmírë means "star-jewel". It's a compound from *[elen](/wiki/Elen "Elen")* and *[mírë](/wiki/M%C3%ADr%C3%AB "Mírë")*. The letters *n+m* from both words are assimilated to long *mm*

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s notes on the subject are open to interpretation, but they appear to identify Elemmírë as the planet we know today as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_(planet)" title="wikipedia:Mercury (planet)">Mercury</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
