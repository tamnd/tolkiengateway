**epe** is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) preposition meaning "before".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Eldar](Eldar.md) imagined future time (time that comes "after" the present) as being "before" them, hence the preposition that means "before" of spatial relationships means "after" when applied to time.

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="Epesse.md" title="Epesse"><b>epe</b>sse</a></i> = "after-name"
- <i><a href="Epetai.md" title="Epetai"><b>epe</b>tai</a></i> = "consequently"
