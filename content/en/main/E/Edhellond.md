**Edhellond** was an ancient harbour in southern [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md). It had been established south-west of where the [Ringló](../R/Ringló.md) entered the [Morthond](../M/Morthond.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> near where the combined waters flowed into [Cobas Haven](../C/Cobas_Haven.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Edhellond was founded in the late [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) by [seafaring Sindar](../F/Falathrim.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> from the [west havens](../H/Havens_of_the_Falas.md) of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) who fled in three small ships when the power of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) overwhelmed the [Eldar](Eldar.md) and the [Edain](Edain.md); but it was later increased by adventurers of the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) seeking for the sea who came down [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

During his sea voyage of [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [735](../S/Second_Age_735.md)-[9](../S/Second_Age_739.md) to the coasts of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), [Aldarion](../A/Aldarion.md), the grandson of King [Tar-Elendil](../T/Tar-Elendil.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), looked at the "country of [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md) where the Nandor Elves still dwell"<sup id="cite_ref-AAE_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AAE-6">[5]</a></sup> which included Edhellond.

From Edhellond, Elves began sailing westward from the time of the [fall of Eregion](../S/Sack_of_Eregion.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1697](../S/Second_Age_1697.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Preface_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Preface-8">[7]</a></sup>

Around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1980](../T/Third_Age_1980.md), with the freeing of [the Balrog](../D/Durin's_Bane.md) in [Moria](../M/Moria.md), many of the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) fled south to Edhellond and sailed over the [Sea](../S/Sea.md) to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md), [King of Lórien](../K/King_of_Lórien.md), was lost at sea near Edhellond in [1981](../T/Third_Age_1981.md) when he attempted to swim to shore to seek his beloved [Nimrodel](../N/Nimrodel.md). The ship from which he jumped overboard was the last ship of [Elves](Elves.md) to leave from Edhellond. The haven was deserted from then on,<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-2">[2]</a></sup> although it was remembered, as were other ancient Elvish dwellings, in the traditions of the [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md) in [Anfalas](../A/Anfalas.md) and [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Preface_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Preface-8">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Edhellond means "Elf Haven" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), from *[edhel](Edhel.md)* ("Elf") + *[lond](../L/Lond.md)* ("harbour, haven").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Another account, in an unfinished note on the origin of the name *[Belfalas](../B/Belfalas.md)*, states that the haven was established in the early [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) by [Elves from Lindon](Elves_of_Lindon.md). They were a remnant of the [people of Doriath](../I/Iathrim.md) who still harboured a grudge against the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-2">[2]</a></sup> They learned the craft of shipbuilding at the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) and then settled at the mouth of the [Morthond](../M/Morthond.md); the fisherfolk of the primitive harbour fled in fear to the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) upon the arrival of the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

In *[Aldarion and Erendis](../A/Aldarion_and_Erendis.md)*, Tolkien described [Aldarion](../A/Aldarion.md)'s sea voyage of [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [735](../S/Second_Age_735.md)-[9](../S/Second_Age_739.md) as passing through the "country of Amroth where the Nandor Elves still dwell".<sup id="cite_ref-AAE_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AAE-6">[5]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) stated that this can be interpreted to imply that the tale of Aldarion and Erendis was recorded in Gondor before the last departure of Elves from Edhellond in T.A. 1981.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>
