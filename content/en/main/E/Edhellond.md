**Edhellond** was an ancient harbour in southern [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). It had been established south-west of where the [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3) entered the [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> near where the combined waters flowed into [Cobas Haven](/wiki/Cobas_Haven).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Edhellond was founded in the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) by [seafaring Sindar](/wiki/Falathrim "Falathrim")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> from the [west havens](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas "Havens of the Falas") of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) who fled in three small ships when the power of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) overwhelmed the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) and the [Edain](/wiki/Edain); but it was later increased by adventurers of the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves) seeking for the sea who came down [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

During his sea voyage of [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [735](/wiki/Second_Age_735 "Second Age 735")-[9](/wiki/Second_Age_739 "Second Age 739") to the coasts of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), [Aldarion](/wiki/Aldarion), the grandson of King [Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil) of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), looked at the "country of [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth) where the Nandor Elves still dwell"<sup id="cite_ref-AAE_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AAE-6">[5]</a></sup> which included Edhellond.

From Edhellond, Elves began sailing westward from the time of the [fall of Eregion](/wiki/Sack_of_Eregion "Sack of Eregion") in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Preface_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Preface-8">[7]</a></sup>

Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1980](/wiki/Third_Age_1980 "Third Age 1980"), with the freeing of [the Balrog](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane "Durin's Bane") in [Moria](/wiki/Moria), many of the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) fled south to Edhellond and sailed over the [Sea](/wiki/Sea) to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth), [King of Lórien](/wiki/King_of_L%C3%B3rien), was lost at sea near Edhellond in [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981") when he attempted to swim to shore to seek his beloved [Nimrodel](/wiki/Nimrodel). The ship from which he jumped overboard was the last ship of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) to leave from Edhellond. The haven was deserted from then on,<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-2">[2]</a></sup> although it was remembered, as were other ancient Elvish dwellings, in the traditions of the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) in [Anfalas](/wiki/Anfalas) and [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-Preface_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Preface-8">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Edhellond means "Elf Haven" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), from *[edhel](/wiki/Edhel "Edhel")* ("Elf") + *[lond](/wiki/Lond "Lond")* ("harbour, haven").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Another account, in an unfinished note on the origin of the name *[Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas)*, states that the haven was established in the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) by [Elves from Lindon](/wiki/Elves_of_Lindon "Elves of Lindon"). They were a remnant of the [people of Doriath](/wiki/Iathrim "Iathrim") who still harboured a grudge against the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor).<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-2">[2]</a></sup> They learned the craft of shipbuilding at the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) and then settled at the mouth of the [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond); the fisherfolk of the primitive harbour fled in fear to the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) upon the arrival of the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar).<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

In *[Aldarion and Erendis](/wiki/Aldarion_and_Erendis)*, Tolkien described [Aldarion](/wiki/Aldarion)'s sea voyage of [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [735](/wiki/Second_Age_735 "Second Age 735")-[9](/wiki/Second_Age_739 "Second Age 739") as passing through the "country of Amroth where the Nandor Elves still dwell".<sup id="cite_ref-AAE_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AAE-6">[5]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) stated that this can be interpreted to imply that the tale of Aldarion and Erendis was recorded in Gondor before the last departure of Elves from Edhellond in T.A. 1981.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>
