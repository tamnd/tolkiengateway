**East Sindar** were the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) who lived in the wide open lands east of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) beyond the [Girdle of Melian](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> They were not under the rule of [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol), and they practiced cattle-rearing and sheep-farming as well as growing grain and other crops - these they sold both to [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) in the west and to the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) to the east.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>
