**East Sindar** were the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) who lived in the wide open lands east of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) beyond the [Girdle of Melian](../G/Girdle_of_Melian.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> They were not under the rule of [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md), and they practiced cattle-rearing and sheep-farming as well as growing grain and other crops - these they sold both to [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) in the west and to the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) to the east.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>
