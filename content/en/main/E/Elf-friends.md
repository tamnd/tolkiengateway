The **Elf-friends** were those, usually among [Men](../M/Men.md), who were recognised by the [Elves](Elves.md) as providing special aid or alliance to their efforts. Elf-friend was given as title or epithet.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other uses {#Other_uses}

The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) terms were technically *[Eldandil](Eldandil.md)* ("Devoted to the [Eldar](Eldar.md)"), or <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Quendil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Quendil (page does not exist)">Quendil</a></i> ("Devoted to the [Quendi](../Q/Quendi.md)") or <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Elesser&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Elesser (page does not exist)">Elesser</a></i>.

Among the [Edain](Edain.md) and [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md), the Elvish words for "Elf" and "[star](../S/Stars.md)" were often confused, since they sounded similar. Thus *[Elendil](Elendil.md)* or *[Elvellon](Elvellon.md)* was used by them to mean "Elf-friend", though it actually signifies "Devoted to the Stars".

The plural form *[Elendili](Elendili.md)* ([Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) *[Nimruzîrim](../N/Nimruzîrim.md)*) was one of the names for the [Faithful](../F/Faithful.md) [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md).

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The concept of the "Elf-friend" is recurrent in Tolkien's legendarium, both in meaning and in name. The name <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%86lfwine" title="wikipedia:Ælfwine">Ælfwine/Elfwine</a> is an actual [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) personal name that means "Elf-friend" and has survived in modern times as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alboin_(disambiguation)" title="wikipedia:Alboin (disambiguation)">Alboin</a> or Alwin/Elwin. This word has been the basis for several characters and terms:

- [Elendil](Elendil.md)
- <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Nimruz%C3%AEr&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nimruzîr (page does not exist)">Nimruzîr</a>
- [Elvellon](Elvellon.md)
- [Elfwine](Elfwine.md) son of [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md)
- [Ælfwine](../_/Ælfwine.md) of England (also known as [Eriol](Eriol.md))
- [Alwin Lowdham](../A/Alwin_Lowdham.md)
- [Alboin](../A/Alboin.md)

"Elwin Ransom" is also the name of the protagonist of [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md)'s [*Space Trilogy*](../S/Space_Trilogy.md). The character is said to be based on Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
