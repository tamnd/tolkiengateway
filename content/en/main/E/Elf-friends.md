The **Elf-friends** were those, usually among [Men](/wiki/Men), who were recognised by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) as providing special aid or alliance to their efforts. Elf-friend was given as title or epithet.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other uses {#Other_uses}

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) terms were technically *[Eldandil](/wiki/Eldandil)* ("Devoted to the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar)"), or <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Quendil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Quendil (page does not exist)">Quendil</a></i> ("Devoted to the [Quendi](/wiki/Quendi)") or <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Elesser&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Elesser (page does not exist)">Elesser</a></i>.

Among the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) and [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain), the Elvish words for "Elf" and "[star](/wiki/Stars "Stars")" were often confused, since they sounded similar. Thus *[Elendil](/wiki/Elendil)* or *[Elvellon](/wiki/Elvellon)* was used by them to mean "Elf-friend", though it actually signifies "Devoted to the Stars".

The plural form *[Elendili](/wiki/Elendili)* ([Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) *[Nimruzîrim](/wiki/Nimruz%C3%AErim)*) was one of the names for the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans).

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The concept of the "Elf-friend" is recurrent in Tolkien's legendarium, both in meaning and in name. The name <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%86lfwine" title="wikipedia:Ælfwine">Ælfwine/Elfwine</a> is an actual [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) personal name that means "Elf-friend" and has survived in modern times as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alboin_(disambiguation)" title="wikipedia:Alboin (disambiguation)">Alboin</a> or Alwin/Elwin. This word has been the basis for several characters and terms:

- [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil)
- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nimruz%C3%AEr&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nimruzîr (page does not exist)">Nimruzîr</a>
- [Elvellon](/wiki/Elvellon)
- [Elfwine](/wiki/Elfwine) son of [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer)
- [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine) of England (also known as [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol))
- [Alwin Lowdham](/wiki/Alwin_Lowdham)
- [Alboin](/wiki/Alboin)

"Elwin Ransom" is also the name of the protagonist of [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis)'s [*Space Trilogy*](/wiki/Space_Trilogy "Space Trilogy"). The character is said to be based on Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
