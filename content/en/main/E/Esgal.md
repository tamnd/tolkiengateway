<i><b>esgal</b></i> is the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word for "veil, screen, roof of leaves".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Possibly [OS](../O/Old_Sindarin.md) \**eskala*, [root](../R/Root.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=SKAL1&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SKAL1 (page does not exist)">SKAL1</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The word is said to be [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md).
