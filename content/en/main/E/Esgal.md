<i><b>esgal</b></i> is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for "veil, screen, roof of leaves".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Possibly [OS](/wiki/Old_Sindarin "Old Sindarin") \**eskala*, [root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=SKAL1&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SKAL1 (page does not exist)">SKAL1</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The word is said to be [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin).
