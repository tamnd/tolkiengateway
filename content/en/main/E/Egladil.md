The **Egladil**, or the **Angle** in [Westron](../W/Westron.md), was the name given by the [people of Lórien](../G/Galadhrim.md) to their lands between the courses of the rivers [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) and [Celebrant](../C/Celebrant.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-1">[1]</a></sup> A part of the [Naith](../N/Naith.md), Egladil was the most populated part of the country, where the tree-city of [Caras Galadhon](../C/Caras_Galadhon.md) was to be found.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

On 16 February [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> a parting feast was held there when the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) departed [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Egladil* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name, which means "Elven-point"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> or "Angle"<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-1">[1]</a></sup>. Paul Strack suggests that it is a combination of the prefixal form *Egla-* of *[Eglan](Eglath.md)* ("Forsaken (Elf)") and the lenited form of *[til](../T/Til.md)* ("point", "spike", "(sharp) horn", "ending").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier drafts to *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, the location was named <i><b>Nelennas</b></i> and <i><b>Nelen</b></i>, sometimes glossed as "Gore", which was part of the larger <i><b>Narthas</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Other candidate, and more specific, names were <i><b>Calennel</b></i> and <i><b>Calendil</b></i>, which is translated as "Green-tine" or "Green-spit".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) notes a confusing fluidity in how his father alternated the names and their meanings, regarding the larger "Angle" region between the rivers, and the specific "[Tongue](../T/Tongue.md)" at their confluence.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2008: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The name &#34;Egladil&#34; is specifically applied to the eastern area of Lothlorien, directly bordering by the Anduin.</dd></dl>
