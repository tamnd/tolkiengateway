The **Ered Lómin** was the grey mountain range that marched north to south between the land of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) to the east and the lands of [Lammoth](/wiki/Lammoth) and [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast) to the west. At its southern end the Ered Lómin met the range of the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin) and so walled off Hithlum and its lesser lands from [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) to the south. The Ered Lómin was broken by the inlet of the Firth of [Drengist](/wiki/Drengist).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was through the Firth of Drengist in these mountains that [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) led his followers among the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) back into [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

At the inlet of the Firth of Drengest, the [Cirith Ninniach](/wiki/Cirith_Ninniach) was a thin pass through the mountains. At the eastern end was a tunnel, the [Annon-in-Gelydh](/wiki/Annon-in-Gelydh), which went under them.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ered Lómin* means *Echoing Mountains*, but the origin of the name is uncertain. According to some sources, it refers to the echoes of the great cry of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) as he returned into Middle-earth with [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) before Fëanor's own return.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> According to others, it was merely in the nature of these mountains to produce great echoes. Whatever the origin of the name, it was clearly shared by the land of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min), that lay to the south-east of these mountains.
