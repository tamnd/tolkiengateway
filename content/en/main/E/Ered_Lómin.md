The **Ered Lómin** was the grey mountain range that marched north to south between the land of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) to the east and the lands of [Lammoth](../L/Lammoth.md) and [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) to the west. At its southern end the Ered Lómin met the range of the [Ered Wethrin](Ered_Wethrin.md) and so walled off Hithlum and its lesser lands from [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) to the south. The Ered Lómin was broken by the inlet of the Firth of [Drengist](../D/Drengist.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was through the Firth of Drengist in these mountains that [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) led his followers among the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) back into [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

At the inlet of the Firth of Drengest, the [Cirith Ninniach](../C/Cirith_Ninniach.md) was a thin pass through the mountains. At the eastern end was a tunnel, the [Annon-in-Gelydh](../A/Annon-in-Gelydh.md), which went under them.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ered Lómin* means *Echoing Mountains*, but the origin of the name is uncertain. According to some sources, it refers to the echoes of the great cry of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) as he returned into Middle-earth with [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md) before Fëanor's own return.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> According to others, it was merely in the nature of these mountains to produce great echoes. Whatever the origin of the name, it was clearly shared by the land of [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md), that lay to the south-east of these mountains.
