**Elessar** ("Elf-stone" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md)) can refer to:

- The **[Elessar of Eärendil](Elessar_of_Eärendil.md)**, two fabled gems, one of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) and one of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).

- "[The Elessar](../T/The_Elessar.md)", a section in the chapter "[The History of Galadriel and Celeborn](../T/The_History_of_Galadriel_and_Celeborn.md)" in *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*.
