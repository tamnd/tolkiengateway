**Elessar** ("Elf-stone" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)) can refer to:

- The **[Elessar of Eärendil](/wiki/Elessar_of_E%C3%A4rendil)**, two fabled gems, one of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) and one of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).
- **King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn")**, originally known as Aragorn II.

- "[The Elessar](/wiki/The_Elessar)", a section in the chapter "[The History of Galadriel and Celeborn](/wiki/The_History_of_Galadriel_and_Celeborn)" in *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*.
