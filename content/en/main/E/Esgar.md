## Ilkorin {#Ilkorin}

<i><b>esgar</b></i> is an [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md) word meaning "reed-bed".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) ESEK- (not given any meaning)<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- *[Esgaroth](../L/Lake-town.md#Etymology)*<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Elvish {#Elvish}

<i><b>esgar</b></i> means "shore", in an unspecified [Elvish](Elvish.md) tongue.<sup id="cite_ref-VT46_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT46-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) SKAR<sup>2</sup> ("\[?\] stop, end; limid, marge")<sup id="cite_ref-VT46_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT46-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples_2}

- *[esgaroth](../L/Lake-town.md#Etymology)*<sup id="cite_ref-VT46_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT46-2">[2]</a></sup>
