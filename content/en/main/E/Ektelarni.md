**Ektelarni** were most likely the [fays](/wiki/Fays "Fays") of fountains, according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ekt_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ekt-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ektelarni* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), probably being related to the word <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ektele&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ektele (page does not exist)">ektele</a></i> or <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kektele&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kektele (page does not exist)">kektele</a></i> ("fountain"),<sup id="cite_ref-Ekt_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ekt-1">[1]</a></sup> with *kektele* being derived from the root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=KELE&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KELE (page does not exist)">KELE</a> ("flow, run; ooze, trickle").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
