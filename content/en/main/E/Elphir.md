**Elphir** was the twenty-third [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-DolAmroth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DolAmroth-1">[1]</a></sup>

<br>

## History {#History}

Elphir was the oldest son of the four children of [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil). He was born in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2987](/wiki/Third_Age_2987 "Third Age 2987"), and was already a man by the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), but no feat of his was recorded. He had two brothers, [Erchirion](/wiki/Erchirion) and [Amrothos](/wiki/Amrothos), and a sister, [Lothíriel](/wiki/Loth%C3%ADriel).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

After his death in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [67](/wiki/Fourth_Age_67 "Fourth Age 67"), he was succeeded by his son [Alphros](/wiki/Alphros).<sup id="cite_ref-DolAmroth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DolAmroth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Elphir* seems to be a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but it seems to mean "Lord of Swans" and to be a combination of the plural *e(i)lph* of *[alph](/wiki/Alph "Alph")* ("swan") and *[hîr](/wiki/H%C3%AEr "Hîr")* ("lord").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
