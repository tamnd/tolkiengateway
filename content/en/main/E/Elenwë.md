**Elenwë** was the wife of [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) and the mother of [Idril Celebrindal](../I/Idril.md). She was one of the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md), and her golden hair passed to her daughter.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

She followed her family to the departure of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) from [Aman](../A/Aman.md). After [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) and his sons departed using the only ships, [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) and [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) took their people across the dangerous [Helcaraxë](../H/Helcaraxë.md), during which Elenwë was lost in the crossing, right before the Noldor returned to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> She and her daughter Idril fell into the bitter waters, and Turgon risked his life to save them. But he could only save Idril, and Elenwë was covered in fallen ice. Therefore, Turgon was thereafter always enmitied with the [House of Fëanor](../H/House_of_Fëanor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Elenwë is the only known full Vanya who accompanied the [Flight of the Noldor](../F/Flight_of_the_Noldor.md) and came under the [Doom of Mandos](../D/Doom_of_Mandos.md). As she died before reaching [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), no Vanya is specifically known to have returned to Middle-earth (excepting the [Host of the Valar](../H/Host_of_the_Valar.md) of the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), which however went home to Valinor).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Originally the name of [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md)'s wife was *Alairë* - later corrected by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in [1970](../_/1970.md) to *Anairë* - who would not leave [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). [Anairë](../A/Anairë.md) became [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md)'s spouse whilst Elenwë became Turgon's in genealogies.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-7">[7]</a></sup>
