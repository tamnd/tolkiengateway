**Elendor** is a “<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MUSH" title="wikipedia:MUSH">Multi-User Shared Hallucination</a>” role-playing game server based on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s writings, especially *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Elendor-home_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendor-home-3">[3]</a></sup> Users are able to create their own characters, with cultures and races of their choice, to play in an environment modelled closely after Tolkien's [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Established in October [1991](../_/1991.md), Elendor is the oldest and most popular Tolkien-based MUSH on the internet.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Elendor-home_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendor-home-3">[3]</a></sup> By May [2005](../_/2005.md), the number of registered users in the game was over 2,000. Middle-earth can be traversed from [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md) to the [Sea of Rhûn](../S/Sea_of_Rhûn.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Elendor-ascalades_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendor-ascalades-4">[4]</a></sup>, with descriptions differing based on the season and time of day.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendor-ascalades_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendor-ascalades-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Characters {#Characters}

New players can either be a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md), [Elf](Elves.md), [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md), [Man](../M/Men.md) or [Orc](../O/Orcs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendor-home_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendor-home-3">[3]</a></sup> As In-Character and Out-of-Character ranks grow, players are able to get the chance to play other races. A full list of races can be found below:

- "Araurak" ([Half-Orcs](../H/Half-Orcs~19882.md))<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

- [Dragons](../D/Dragons.md)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 1]</a></sup>
- [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md)
- Dwarves
- [Eagles](Eagles.md)
- Elves
- [Ents](Ents.md)
- [Half-elves](../H/Half-elves.md)
- Hobbits
- [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md)
- Men
