**Elendor** is a “<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MUSH" title="wikipedia:MUSH">Multi-User Shared Hallucination</a>” role-playing game server based on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s writings, especially *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Elendor-home_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendor-home-3">[3]</a></sup> Users are able to create their own characters, with cultures and races of their choice, to play in an environment modelled closely after Tolkien's [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Established in October [1991](/wiki/1991), Elendor is the oldest and most popular Tolkien-based MUSH on the internet.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Elendor-home_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendor-home-3">[3]</a></sup> By May [2005](/wiki/2005), the number of registered users in the game was over 2,000. Middle-earth can be traversed from [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) to the [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn)<sup id="cite_ref-Elendor-ascalades_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendor-ascalades-4">[4]</a></sup>, with descriptions differing based on the season and time of day.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendor-ascalades_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendor-ascalades-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Characters {#Characters}

New players can either be a [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves"), [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves"), [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits"), [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") or [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs").<sup id="cite_ref-Elendor-home_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendor-home-3">[3]</a></sup> As In-Character and Out-of-Character ranks grow, players are able to get the chance to play other races. A full list of races can be found below:

- "Araurak" ([Half-Orcs](/wiki/Half-Orcs))<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

- [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain)
- Dwarves
- [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles)
- Elves
- [Ents](/wiki/Ents)
- [Half-elves](/wiki/Half-elves)
- Hobbits
- [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar)
- Men
