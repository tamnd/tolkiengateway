The **Wood of Greenleaves** ([S](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin"): <i><b>Eryn Lasgalen</b></i>) was the new name chosen by [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) for the forest that had been known as [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) for much of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Its name is almost the same as the pre-Mirkwood name [Eryn Galen](/wiki/Mirkwood "Mirkwood") ("[Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Mirkwood "Mirkwood")"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but with the addition of the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word *[las](/wiki/Las "Las")* ("leaf"). <span></span>
