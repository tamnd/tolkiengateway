The **Wood of Greenleaves** ([S](../S/Sindarin.md): <i><b>Eryn Lasgalen</b></i>) was the new name chosen by [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) and [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) for the forest that had been known as [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) for much of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Its name is almost the same as the pre-Mirkwood name [Eryn Galen](../M/Mirkwood.md) ("[Greenwood the Great](../M/Mirkwood.md)"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but with the addition of the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word *[las](../L/Las.md)* ("leaf"). <span></span>
