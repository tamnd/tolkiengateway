**Erestor** was an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), and the chief counsellor of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond).

## History {#History}

Erestor, being the chief counsellor of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond)'s household at the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), took part in the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond). He did not support the idea of destroying [the Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), and preferred instead the notion of guarding it from [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), perhaps with the aid of [Iarwain ben-adar](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil "Tom Bombadil"). His suggestion was countered by [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel), as well as [Galdor](/wiki/Galdor_of_the_Havens "Galdor of the Havens"), as they did not believe that Iarwain had the power to defy Sauron's.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Erestor accompanied [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) to her marriage with [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Erestor* is in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest introduction of Erestor, he was one of three counsellors of Elrond's (the other two being unnamed); all three were kinsmen of Elrond's, "of that half-elvish folk whom the Elves named the children of [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien)". In a further update, the other two were made into unnamed Elf-lords of Rivendell, with Erestor being the sole remaining half-elf and kinsman of Elrond.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> However, Erestor is never called a half-elf in the finished text.

In the initial selection for the Nine of the Company of the Ring, Erestor was to represent the half-Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Originally, only Elrond, [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) accompany Arwen to her wedding.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
