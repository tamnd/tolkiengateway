**Edwin Lowdham** was the father of [Alwin Arundel Lowdham](/wiki/Alwin_Arundel_Lowdham).

## History {#History}

Edwin was a wealthy man, and had a great passion for the sea. He spent a great deal of time sailing about <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway" title="wikipedia:Norway">Norway</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland" title="wikipedia:Scotland">Scotland</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland" title="wikipedia:Ireland">Ireland</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iceland" title="wikipedia:Iceland">Iceland</a> and sometimes even going as far south as to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azores" title="wikipedia:Azores">Azores</a>. In [1947](/wiki/1947) he set sail in his ship the [Éarendel](/wiki/%C3%89arendel) with only three sailors accompanying him, and was never seen again.<sup id="cite_ref-Edwin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Edwin-1">[1]</a></sup>

Later, his son Arundel found a manuscript written in [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) which contained an account of the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor) in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) written by Edwin.<sup id="cite_ref-Edwin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Edwin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Appearance {#Appearance}

He is described by his son as being large, tall, powerful and dark.<sup id="cite_ref-Edwin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Edwin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edwin" title="wikipedia:Edwin">Edwin</a> ([Old English](/wiki/Old_English): [Éadwine](/wiki/%C3%89adwine)) contains the elements *ead* "bliss" (seen also in *[Éadig](/wiki/%C3%89adig)*) and *wine* "friend".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
