**Eric George Fraser** ([June 11](/wiki/11_June "11 June"), [1902](/wiki/1902) – [November 15](/wiki/15_November "15 November"), [1983](/wiki/1983)) was a British illustrator and graphic artist.

A specialist in illustrating classic scenes from mythology (such as [Beowulf](</wiki/Beowulf_(poem)> "Beowulf (poem)") fighting the dragon) and classic medieval scenes, Fraser also illustrated [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s books, such as the Folio Society edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* in 1977 and the Folio Society edition of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* in 1979.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
