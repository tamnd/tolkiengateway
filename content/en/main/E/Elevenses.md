**Elevenses** was a meal taken by many of the guests invited to [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)'s [party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party").<sup id="cite_ref-Long_Expected_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Long_Expected-1">[1]</a></sup> It was possibly one of the six meals a day eaten by [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
