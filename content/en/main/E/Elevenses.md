**Elevenses** was a meal taken by many of the guests invited to [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)'s [party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Long_Expected_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Long_Expected-1">[1]</a></sup> It was possibly one of the six meals a day eaten by [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
