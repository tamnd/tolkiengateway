**Ernil i Pheriannath** ("Prince of the Halflings") was a title given to [Peregrin "Pippin" Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") by the people of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). It was rumored that Pippin would offer allegiance to Gondor and 5,000 [Halflings](/wiki/Halflings) for the [defence of the city](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor "Siege of Gondor").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Although the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) had never interacted with [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) before the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), they knew about the "[Halflings](/wiki/Halflings)" from their northern cousins, the [Dúnedain of Arnor](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The rumor that Pippin was their "Prince", was sparked perhaps by the peculiarity of the [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) dialect spoken by Pippin, which enabled him to speak in a familiar form to anyone, even [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor), regardless of rank.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
