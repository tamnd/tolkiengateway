**Ernil i Pheriannath** ("Prince of the Halflings") was a title given to [Peregrin "Pippin" Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) by the people of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). It was rumored that Pippin would offer allegiance to Gondor and 5,000 [Halflings](../H/Halflings.md) for the [defence of the city](../S/Siege_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Although the [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md) had never interacted with [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) before the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), they knew about the "[Halflings](../H/Halflings.md)" from their northern cousins, the [Dúnedain of Arnor](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The rumor that Pippin was their "Prince", was sparked perhaps by the peculiarity of the [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) dialect spoken by Pippin, which enabled him to speak in a familiar form to anyone, even [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md), regardless of rank.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
