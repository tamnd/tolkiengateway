The **Entwade** was a ford and road-crossing over the River [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> some fifty-five miles northeast of [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) in [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>.

On their journey north to [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") in search of [Baggins](/wiki/Baggins_Family "Baggins Family"), the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) secretly and invisibly, on foot, had passed west through [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien), and on over the Entwade<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> before turning eastward again to return to the banks of the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

After [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) and his riders attacked the [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs")-band outside Fangorn,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> he was leading his Riders for this ford when he met [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and his companions in the [Eastemnet](/wiki/Eastemnet). He lent the horses [Hasufel](/wiki/Hasufel) and [Arod](/wiki/Arod) to the [Three Hunters](/wiki/Three_Hunters), and asked that they would follow him at some time across the Entwade to Edoras.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Entwade is a modernisation of [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *Entwaed*. The second element *waed* means "ford". The name is modernised, because it was recognisable by speakers of [Westron](/wiki/Westron). [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) used that name, assimilated to their own language.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Despite the Entwash being the shallowest in this spot, the Entwade crossing is only made possible by the wooded raft-bridges that connect both banks to a small island in the middle. The crossing itself is guarded by the town also called Entwade on the western bank, and only those granted leave are allowed to cross.</dd></dl>
