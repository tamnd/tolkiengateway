The **Entwade** was a ford and road-crossing over the River [Entwash](Entwash.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> some fifty-five miles northeast of [Edoras](Edoras.md) in [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>.

On their journey north to [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) in search of [Baggins](../B/Baggins_Family~66343.md), the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) secretly and invisibly, on foot, had passed west through [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md), and on over the Entwade<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> before turning eastward again to return to the banks of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

After [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) and his riders attacked the [Orc](../O/Orcs.md)-band outside Fangorn,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> he was leading his Riders for this ford when he met [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and his companions in the [Eastemnet](Eastemnet.md). He lent the horses [Hasufel](../H/Hasufel.md) and [Arod](../A/Arod.md) to the [Three Hunters](../T/Three_Hunters.md), and asked that they would follow him at some time across the Entwade to Edoras.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Entwade is a modernisation of [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *Entwaed*. The second element *waed* means "ford". The name is modernised, because it was recognisable by speakers of [Westron](../W/Westron.md). [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md) used that name, assimilated to their own language.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2012: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Despite the Entwash being the shallowest in this spot, the Entwade crossing is only made possible by the wooded raft-bridges that connect both banks to a small island in the middle. The crossing itself is guarded by the town also called Entwade on the western bank, and only those granted leave are allowed to cross.</dd></dl>
