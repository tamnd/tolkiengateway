<i><b>essë</b></i> (pl. *essi*) is a noun meaning "name" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> Compare with *[eneth](/wiki/Eneth "Eneth")* in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).

## Elvish Naming {#Elvish_Naming}

According to the naming traditions of [High Elves](/wiki/High_Elves), an Elf can have four types of names, acquired at various times of their lives. The three first names are called the <i><b>anessi</b></i> ("given-names"): the *[father-name](/wiki/Father-name "Father-name")*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> the *[amilessë](/wiki/Amiless%C3%AB "Amilessë")* ("mother-name"), and the *[epessë](/wiki/Epess%C3%AB "Epessë")* ("after-name" or honorary title). The last type of name is called the <i><b><a href="/wiki/Kilmess%C3%AB" title="Kilmessë">kilmessë</a></b></i>: the *chosen-name* ("self-name").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

#### Anessi {#Anessi}

- [Father-name](/wiki/Father-name): chosen by the father and often based on the father's own name or derived from old legends. Given at or near birth.
- [Amilessë](/wiki/Amiless%C3%AB): chosen by the mother and often prophetic in nature. Given sometimes at or near birth but often bestowed years later.
- [Epessë](/wiki/Epess%C3%AB): often acquired by an Elf as an honorific later in life.

#### Kilmessë {#Kilmessë}

- [Kilmessë](/wiki/Kilmess%C3%AB): a name chosen by an Elf for him- or herself as a disguise or to refer to deeds, talents and personal history.

The *father-name* and the *amilessë* were the Elf's 'true names' and were public names. The *father-name* is the name an Elf used in public until an *epessë* was chosen.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> In *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, when an Elf does not yet have an *epessë*, the Sindarin version of his mother- or father-name is used instead.

### Examples {#Examples}

- **[Maglor](/wiki/Maglor)**: The son of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) of the [House of Finwë](/wiki/House_of_Finw%C3%AB), was given the *essë* *Kanafinwë* ("Strong-voiced \[of\] Finwë"). His amilessë was *[Makalaurë](/wiki/Makalaur%C3%AB)* ("Forging Gold" in a poetic sense).<sup id="cite_ref-Sons_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sons-5">[4]</a></sup> The name Maglor is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) version of his mother-name.

- **[Ereinion Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad "Gil-galad")**: The son of [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) was given the *essë [Ereinion](/wiki/Gil-galad "Gil-galad")* ("Scion of Kings").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> His *epessë* was *[Gil](/wiki/Gil)-[galad](/wiki/Galad "Galad")* ("Star of Radiance") given to him because his armour and shield were overlaid with silver and set with a device of white stars, shone from afar like a star in sunlight or moonlight and could be seen by Elvish eyes at a great distance if he stood upon a height.<sup id="cite_ref-Descendant_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Descendant-7">[6]</a></sup>

- **[Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod")**: The son of [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin) was given the *essë* of *[Findaráto](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod")* ("Golden-haired Champion"). His amilessë was *[Ingoldo](/wiki/Ingoldo)* ("The Noldo", singular for [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor)).<sup id="cite_ref-Descend_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Descend-8">[7]</a></sup> He is usually referred to as *[Felagund](/wiki/Felagund)* ("Hewer of Caves"),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> originally a derogatory nickname given to him because of his dwelling at [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond). Finrod adopted the name as his *epessë* and made it a title of honour. The name Finrod is the Sindarin version of his father-name.

- **[Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel)**: The only daughter of Finarfin was given the *essë* of *[Artanis](/wiki/Artanis)* ("Noble Woman") and the *amilessë* of *[Nerwen](/wiki/Nerwen)* ("Man-maiden") because of her height and strength.<sup id="cite_ref-Descend_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Descend-8">[7]</a></sup> Her *epessë*, *[Alatáriel](/wiki/Alat%C3%A1riel)* ("Maiden Crowned with Radiant Garland"), was given to her by her husband, [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn). The name Galadriel is the Sindarin version of her *epessë*.<sup id="cite_ref-Descendant_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Descendant-7">[6]</a></sup>

- **[Amras](/wiki/Amras)**: The last son of the House of Finwë was given the *essë* *[Telufinwë](/wiki/Telufinw%C3%AB)*, ("Last \[of\] Finwë"). His first *amilessë* was *[Ambarussa](/wiki/Ambarussa)* ("Top-russet" referring to his hair), his twin-brother [Amrod](/wiki/Amrod) had the same *amilessë*. To distinguish the two Amrod was given a second *amilessë*: *[Umbarto](/wiki/Umbarto)* ("The Fated").<sup id="cite_ref-Sons_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sons-5">[4]</a></sup> His mother's choice was prophetic, because Amrod was the first of the [sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor") to die, accidentally killed in the swan ships of the Teleri when his father ordered them to be burnt at [Losgar](/wiki/Losgar). The name Amras is the Sindarin version of *Ambarussa*.

## Tengwa {#Tengwa}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ess%C3%AB.gif"><img src="/w/images/2/20/Ess%C3%AB.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Essë* is also the name in later Quenya of the thirty-first letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet, representing **SS**. The original name of this letter was *Áze*, but over time the phonemes for "z" and "r" merged and the letter was renamed *Áre*. Since *[Óre](/wiki/%C3%93re)* (letter 21) represents untrilled "r" and *[Rómen](/wiki/R%C3%B3men)* (letter 25) represents trilled "r", *Áre* became superfluous. This letter was then renamed *Essë* and represents "ss", but only in written Quenya.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>
