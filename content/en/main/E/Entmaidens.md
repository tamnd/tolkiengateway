**Entmaidens** were young female [Ents](/wiki/Ents) or young [Entwives](/wiki/Entwives). When [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard), talking to [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), remembered old times, when "the world was young, and the woods were wide and wild", he said that "there were Entmaidens then: ah! the loveliness of [Fimbrethil](/wiki/Fimbrethil), of Wandlimb the lightfooted, in the days of our youth!"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
