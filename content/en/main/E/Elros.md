**Elros** was the son of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil), the great hero of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), and his wife [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing). He was the twin brother of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and both were [Half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven).<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-5">[4]</a></sup>

Choosing to live as a mortal [Man](/wiki/Men "Men"), he became a lord of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) and the first [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor), taking the royal name **Tar-Minyatur**.

## History {#History}

### Life in Beleriand {#Life_in_Beleriand}

Elros and Elrond were born in the [Havens of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion "Mouths of Sirion") during the darkest days of the First Age, when the forces of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) controlled most of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). When the twins were just six years old, the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor), bound by their [Oath](/wiki/Oath_of_F%C3%ABanor "Oath of Fëanor"), assaulted the Havens in the [Third Kinslaying](/wiki/Third_Kinslaying) since they desired the [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) on the [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr) held by Elwing. Eärendil was at sea, and Elwing escaped with the Nauglamír and jumped into the sea. [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) and [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor), the only Sons of Fëanor to survive the assault upon the Havens, took Elros and Elrond as captives. But Maglor took pity upon Elrond and Elros and a mutual love grew between Maglor and the young twins afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-5">[4]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_And_Maglor_took_pity_upon_them.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/6/6a/Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_And_Maglor_took_pity_upon_them.jpg/300px-Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_And_Maglor_took_pity_upon_them.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="238" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/6/6a/Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_And_Maglor_took_pity_upon_them.jpg/450px-Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_And_Maglor_took_pity_upon_them.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/6/6a/Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_And_Maglor_took_pity_upon_them.jpg/600px-Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_And_Maglor_took_pity_upon_them.jpg 2x" data-file-width="756" data-file-height="600"/></a><figcaption><i>And Maglor took pity upon them</i> by <a href="/wiki/Catherine_Karina_Chmiel" title="Catherine Karina Chmiel">Catherine Karina Chmiel</a></figcaption></figure>

After the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) and the destruction of Beleriand, the Valar gave the twin *[Peredhil](/wiki/Peredhil)* the choice over their race and fate. Elros chose the [Gift of Men](/wiki/Gift_of_Men),<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-5">[4]</a></sup> but still, he was blessed with a life longer than any other mortal Man's.

### First King of Númenor {#First_King_of_Númenor}

In the first years of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the Valar had prepared for the Edain, as a reward for their struggle against Morgoth, a large island in the western part of the [Great Sea](/wiki/Great_Sea). Now by right a lord of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 2]</a></sup> Elros led his people across the sea, guided by the [Star of Eärendil](/wiki/Star_of_E%C3%A4rendil) his father, to the island. For this the island was called [Elenna](/wiki/Elenna) meaning "starwards", and was the closest of mortal lands to the [Blessed Realm](/wiki/Blessed_Realm), though Men were [forbidden to go](/wiki/Ban_of_the_Valar "Ban of the Valar") so far westward that the coasts of Elenna were no longer visible. The Valar also gave the Edain substantially longer lifespans than those of Men remaining in Middle-earth, and Elros and his descendants had the longest lives of all the people.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-7">[5]</a></sup>

Elros founded the realm of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) and became its first [King](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") in the year 32 of the Second Age. He took a royal name in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), *Tar-Minyatur*, thus setting a tradition for the kings of Númenor to take a royal name in Quenya.<sup id="cite_ref-Kings_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kings-2">[2]</a></sup> He brought with him the [Ring of Barahir](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir), the [Axe of Tuor](/wiki/Dramborleg "Dramborleg"), the [Bow](/wiki/Bow_of_Bregor "Bow of Bregor") of [Bregor](/wiki/Bregor) and [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol)'s sword [Aranrúth](/wiki/Aranr%C3%BAth) as family heirlooms.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup>

The city of [Armenelos](/wiki/Armenelos), including the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=King%27s_house&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="King&#39;s house (page does not exist)">tower and the citadel</a>, were constructed during the reign of Elros.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-7">[5]</a></sup> Elros had four children: three sons, [Vardamir Nólimon](/wiki/Vardamir "Vardamir"), [Manwendil](/wiki/Manwendil), and [Atanalcar](/wiki/Atanalcar); and one daughter, [Tindómiel](/wiki/Tind%C3%B3miel).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[note 3]</a></sup>

### Resignation of life {#Resignation_of_life}

Elros shared the same physical potentiality of life as Elrond. Since, however, he elected to remain among the kindred of Men, he retained the chief human characteristic of "seeking elsewither" (as the Eldar called it), the desire to depart from [the World](/wiki/Arda "Arda").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[8]</a></sup> He remained unwearied until he was five hundred years old and then laid down his life.<sup id="cite_ref-Kings_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kings-2">[2]</a></sup>

Elros's eldest son took up the [Sceptre of Númenor](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor) as [Tar-Vardamir](/wiki/Vardamir "Vardamir"). But because Elros had lived so long, Tar-Vardamir was already old, and he immediately surrendered the Sceptre to his own son, [Tar-Amandil](/wiki/Tar-Amandil).<sup id="cite_ref-Kings_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kings-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a letter from 1958 [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) explained that *Elros* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name that he was given, because he was found playing in the water of a waterfall that fell over the entrance to a cave and that the name is a compound of *eld&gt;ell*, the Sindarin term for the High-Elven exiles, and *ros* based on primitive Elvish *rossē* meaning "dew, spray (of fall or fountain)".<sup id="cite_ref-L211_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L211-12">[9]</a></sup> In an essay that was probably written between 1959 and 1960 J.R.R. Tolkien translated the name as "Star-glitter".<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[10]</a></sup> In an excursus on the names of Finwë's descendants that was written in 1968 or later J.R.R. Tolkien translated the name as "star-foam".<sup id="cite_ref-Shibboleth_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibboleth-14">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[12]</a></sup> The Sindarin elements are *[el](/wiki/El "El")* ("star") and *[ros](/wiki/Ros "Ros")* ("foam").<sup id="cite_ref-Elements_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elements-16">[13]</a></sup> In an essay on "The Problem of Ros" that was written in 1969 or later J.R.R. Tolkien considered the possibility that ros was a Bëorian word for "foam" and for the "white crest of waves", but he rejected this idea, because of the Sindarin name Cair Andros.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[14]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) form of *Elros* was <i><b>Elerossë</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Shibboleth_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibboleth-14">[11]</a></sup>

After Elros ascended the throne of Númenor he was known by the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) title, <i><b>Tar-Minyatur</b></i> in the Scroll of Kings.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[15]</a></sup> Tar-Minyatur means "High first-ruler" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya),<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[16]</a></sup> consisting of *[tar](/wiki/Tar "Tar")* ("high") + *[minya](/wiki/Minya "Minya")* ("first") + *[tur](/wiki/Tur "Tur")* ("master, lord").<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[17]</a></sup> All the Kings and [Queens](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Ruling Queen of Númenor") after Elros also used the prefix *Tar-* in their royal titles in the Scroll of Kings.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[18]</a></sup>

In early versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), Elros' [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name was *Indilzar*,<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[19]</a></sup> which was changed to <i><b>Gimilzôr</b></i>. However, in later versions of the Númenórean tale *[(Ar-)Gimilzôr](/wiki/Ar-Gimilz%C3%B4r "Ar-Gimilzôr")* was the name of the twenty-third King of Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[20]</a></sup>
