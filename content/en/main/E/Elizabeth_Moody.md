**Elizabeth Moody** ([October 29](/wiki/29_October "29 October"), [1939](/wiki/1939) - [January 12](/wiki/12_January "12 January"), [2010](/wiki/2010)) was a New Zealand actress. She played [Lobelia Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins) in the [extended edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)") of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, which turned out to be her last acting credit.
