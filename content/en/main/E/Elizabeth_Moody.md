**Elizabeth Moody** ([October 29](../_/29_October.md), [1939](../_/1939.md) - [January 12](../_/12_January.md), [2010](../_/2010.md)) was a New Zealand actress. She played [Lobelia Sackville-Baggins](../L/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins.md) in the [extended edition](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, which turned out to be her last acting credit.
