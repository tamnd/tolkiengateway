**Eofor** was a prince of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

## History {#History}

Eofor was the third son of king [Brego](/wiki/Brego). His older brothers were [Baldor](/wiki/Baldor) and King [Aldor](/wiki/Aldor). He lived in the town of [Aldburg](/wiki/Aldburg).<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-1">[1]</a></sup> [Éomund](/wiki/%C3%89omund) was his descendant<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Eofor* is likely to have been borrowed from the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) word <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/eofor" title="wiktionary:eofor">eofor</a></i> (meaning "boar").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
