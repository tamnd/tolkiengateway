**Evermind** was a translation of the name [simbelmynë](../S/Simbelmynë.md), the name in [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) for the small white flower that grew especially on the graves of [Men](../M/Men.md). This association with the dead was the source of the flower's name - 'everlasting memory'.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Tolkien noted that the element *-mind* has the sense "memory". Although the name resembles "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forget-me-not" title="wikipedia:Forget-me-not">forget-me-not</a>" it's not the same kind of flower.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
