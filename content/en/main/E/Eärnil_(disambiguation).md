**Eärnil** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), a contracted form of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[eˈarnil]</span>) can refer to:

- [Eärnil I](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_I), thirteenth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and the second of its [Ship-kings](/wiki/Ship-king "Ship-king").
- [Eärnil II](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II), thirty-second King of Gondor, who won the [Battle of the Camp](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Camp).
