**Erchirion** was the son and second child of [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil), [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth).

He had an older brother, [Elphir](/wiki/Elphir), and younger siblings [Amrothos](/wiki/Amrothos) and [Lothíriel](/wiki/Loth%C3%ADriel).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is probably derived from *[ar-](/wiki/Ar- "Ar-") [hîr](/wiki/H%C3%AEr "Hîr")* (both meaning "noble lord") plus the frequent ending *[-ion](/wiki/-ion)* "son of".

Alternatively, it could also derive from [er](/wiki/Er "Er")-[cirion](/wiki/Cirion "Cirion") meaning "lone shipman".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
