<i><b>Elfstone</b></i> is the third track of the album [Dol Guldur](<../D/Dol_Guldur_(album).md>) by the band [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md). The song name is taken from the [Elfstone](Elfstone.md), a gift from [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) to [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md). The lyrics directly quote the [Riddle of Strider](../R/Riddle_of_Strider.md) and [Get Out! You old Wight! Vanish in the Sunlight!](../G/Get_Out!_You_old_Wight!_Vanish_in_the_Sunlight!.md)
