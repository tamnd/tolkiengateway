**Embracer Group** is the parent company of businesses developing and publishing games. With its head office based in Karlstad, Sweden, Embracer Group has a global presence through its ten operative groups: THQ Nordic, Plaion, Coffee Stain, Amplifier Game Invest, Saber Interactive, DECA Games, Gearbox Entertainment, Easybrain, Asmodee (the owner of [Fantasy Flight Games](../F/Fantasy_Flight_Games.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>), and Dark Horse. The Group has 120 internal game development studios and is engaging more than 12,750 employees and contracted employees in more than 40 countries.<sup id="cite_ref-embracertolkien_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-embracertolkien-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [2022](../_/2022.md) the company acquired [Middle-earth Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md) which included the licensing rights to *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* .<sup id="cite_ref-embracertolkien_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-embracertolkien-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [2025](../_/2025.md), after a failed investment deal, Embracer Group split up many of its holdings. In particular among these [Asmodee](../A/Asmodee.md), a massive French board game publisher that was one of Embracer's subsidiaries, was spun out into its own separate company: Asmodee Group AB. Later that year, Middle-earth Enterprises announced that Asmodee had become the exclusive category manager for all MEE-licensed tabletop games.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

On May 20, [2026](../_/2026.md) Embracer announced they would be spinning off <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Fellowship_Entertainment&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fellowship Entertainment (page does not exist)">Fellowship Entertainment</a>, a new company that will be the steward for the *Lord of the Rings* and *The Hobbit* properties (alongside many other non-Tolkien properties)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>. In addition to the new company, [Middle-earth Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md) was also re-organized under the Fellowship Entertainment umbrella.
