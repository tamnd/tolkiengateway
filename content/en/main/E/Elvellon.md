**Elvellon** (pl. *Elvellyn*) is the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) term for "[Elf-friend](Elf-friend.md)"

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From the elements *[el](El.md)* "star-elf" and *[mellon](../M/Mellon.md)* "friend".

[OS](../O/OS.md) could have been \**elmeldondo*
