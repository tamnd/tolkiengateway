**Edrahil** was an [Elf of Nargothrond](/wiki/Elves_of_Nargothrond "Elves of Nargothrond").

## History {#History}

Edrahil was the chief of the ten [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who remained loyal to [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") at the time he set out with [Beren](/wiki/Beren) on the [Quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril). He was the one who lifted Finrod's cast-down crown and asked that it be given to a steward until the king returned. Later he died in the dungeons of [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth), devoured by a [werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf"), alongside his companions.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Edrahil* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and not explicitly defined.<sup id="cite_ref-Paul_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Paul-3">[3]</a></sup> Of the constituent elements, *edra* is a Sindarin verb meaning meaning "open (out)",<sup id="cite_ref-pe17_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pe17-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:45</sup> while *hil* is frequent in Sindarin in words pertaining to "follow", derived from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [KHIL](/wiki/KHIL)<sup id="cite_ref-pe17_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pe17-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:18</sup>

According to an earlier version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), the character's name was *Enedrion*,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) also not explicitly defined.<sup id="cite_ref-Paul_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Paul-3">[3]</a></sup> Of the constituent elements, *ened* means "centre"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:16</sup> and *-[ion](/wiki/Ion "Ion")* is a common masculine suffix for "son of".
