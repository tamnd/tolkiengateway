**Enyalië** (pronounced \[<span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">enˈjalie</span>\] /*en-<b><span style="font-variant:small-caps;">yar</span></b>-ly-eh*/) is an annual activity held as the concluding part of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s [Oxonmoot](/wiki/Oxonmoot) celebration.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On the Sunday morning of the [Oxonmoot](/wiki/Oxonmoot) convention, attendees of the event take a coach trip to [Wolvercote Cemetery](/wiki/Wolvercote_Cemetery) where [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and his wife [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien") are buried. Enyalië is a "sombre" wreath-laying ceremony which involves a "short act of remembrance" at Tolkien's graveside.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The Chairman of the Society reads a passage from Tolkien's works whilst the song [Namárië](/wiki/Nam%C3%A1ri%C3%AB) is sung by one of the Society's long-standing members.<sup id="cite_ref-TS_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-3">[3]</a></sup>

Former Chair of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society), [Shaun Gunner](/wiki/Shaun_Gunner), described the event as "poignant and touching" whilst noting that "many regard it as the single most important activity of Oxonmoot".<sup id="cite_ref-TS_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Enyalië* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) noun meaning "memory"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or "remembrance".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> It features in the [Oath of Eorl](/wiki/Oath_of_Eorl) - in the form *enyalien* - published in *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
