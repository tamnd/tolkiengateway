**Elven-rope**<sup id="cite_ref-Doom_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doom-5">[5]</a></sup> was a rope made by the [Elves](Elves.md) of [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md). During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), when the [Company of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) were set to depart from Lothlórien, the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) presented them with many gifts, to aid them in their quest. Among the gifts there were coils of rope. The rope was slender, but strong, silken to the touch, and grey of hue like the elven-cloaks and was able to withstand the greatest weight. The rope was made of *[hithlain](../H/Hithlain.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-3">[3]</a></sup> which was a strong fibre made from the inner bark of *[mallorn](../M/Mallorn.md)* trees.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Later, when [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) needed to descend a cliff in the [Emyn Muil](Emyn_Muil.md), Sam remembered the Elven rope he was carrying and unpacked it for the task. When Sam measured it, it came to 30 ells<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 1]</a></sup> in length, which was enough for them to reach the bottom of the drop. After they had made the descent Sam gave the rope a last tug and the startled hobbits watched as the rope slithered down. It showed no signs of having frayed so it was Sam's belief that the rope untied itself.<sup id="cite_ref-Taming_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Taming-4">[4]</a></sup>
