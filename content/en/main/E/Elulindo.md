**Elulindo** was the son of [Elwë](../O/Olwë.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium), Lord of the [Teleri](../F/Falmari.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) from a genealogical table created by [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) in the early 1930s.

The table has not been published but [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) mentioned the name in his comments on the genealogies of the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](../Q/Quenta_Noldorinwa.md)* period in the Appendix to *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](../T/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the later versions of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* the name of the father of Elulindo was changed from *Elwë* to *[Olwë](../O/Olwë.md)*. Olwë is said to have had many sons, but none of them are known by name, nor have their deeds been recorded.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Elulindo* was probably conceived as a [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) name at the time, comprised of the elements *[Elu](Elu.md)* (his father's Telerin name) + *[lindo](../L/Lindo.md)* ("singer, singing bird").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
