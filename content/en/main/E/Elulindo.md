**Elulindo** was the son of [Elwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Olwë"), Lord of the [Teleri](/wiki/Falmari "Falmari"), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") from a genealogical table created by [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) in the early 1930s.

The table has not been published but [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) mentioned the name in his comments on the genealogies of the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](/wiki/Quenta_Noldorinwa)* period in the Appendix to *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the later versions of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* the name of the father of Elulindo was changed from *Elwë* to *[Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB)*. Olwë is said to have had many sons, but none of them are known by name, nor have their deeds been recorded.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Elulindo* was probably conceived as a [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) name at the time, comprised of the elements *[Elu](/wiki/Elu)* (his father's Telerin name) + *[lindo](/wiki/Lindo "Lindo")* ("singer, singing bird").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
