**Eithne Patricia Ní Bhraonáin** ([May 17](/wiki/17_May "17 May"), [1961](/wiki/1961)), better known by her stage name **Enya**, is an Irish singer.

## History {#History}

Enya was born in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gweedore" title="wikipedia:Gweedore">Gaoth Dobhair</a> in Ireland, the sixth of nine children. She came from a musical background, and joined the band of some of her siblings and two of her uncles, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clannad" title="wikipedia:Clannad">Clannad</a>, to play keyboards. After a few years, she left together with their manager Nicky Ryan, and continued her career as a solo artist. Since then, Ryan has been Enya's manager, producer, and arranger, while his wife, Roma Ryan, is her primary lyricist. Among other sources of inspiration, Enya shares [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s interest in the Catholic faith. Her music style became an iconic Celtic and Irish inspiration for several films and artists; the music of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titanic_(1997_film)" title="wikipedia:Titanic (1997 film)">Titanic</a></i> was heavily inspired by her music.

Ten years prior to her involvement in [The Fellowship of the Ring movie soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack"), Enya wrote and performed a short instrumental track, *[Lothlórien](</wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien_(Enya_song)> "Lothlórien (Enya song)")*, named for [the Elvish realm](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien") in [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien)'s writings. It is the ninth track on her third solo studio album, *Shepherd Moons*, which was released on [November 4, 1991](/wiki/4_November "4 November").

She performed vocals on two tracks of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)*, which she also composed together with Roma Ryan, her lyricist. *[Aníron](/wiki/An%C3%ADron)* was the love-theme for [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen), and the Academy Award-nominated *[May It Be](/wiki/May_It_Be)* was included in the closing credits. Following her introduction to [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), Roma Ryan created her own language, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loxian" title="wikipedia:Loxian">Loxian</a>.
