**Eithne Patricia Ní Bhraonáin** ([May 17](../_/17_May.md), [1961](../_/1961.md)), better known by her stage name **Enya**, is an Irish singer.

## History {#History}

Enya was born in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gweedore" title="wikipedia:Gweedore">Gaoth Dobhair</a> in Ireland, the sixth of nine children. She came from a musical background, and joined the band of some of her siblings and two of her uncles, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clannad" title="wikipedia:Clannad">Clannad</a>, to play keyboards. After a few years, she left together with their manager Nicky Ryan, and continued her career as a solo artist. Since then, Ryan has been Enya's manager, producer, and arranger, while his wife, Roma Ryan, is her primary lyricist. Among other sources of inspiration, Enya shares [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s interest in the Catholic faith. Her music style became an iconic Celtic and Irish inspiration for several films and artists; the music of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titanic_(1997_film)" title="wikipedia:Titanic (1997 film)">Titanic</a></i> was heavily inspired by her music.

Ten years prior to her involvement in [The Fellowship of the Ring movie soundtrack](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack.md), Enya wrote and performed a short instrumental track, *[Lothlórien](<../L/Lothlórien_(Enya_song).md>)*, named for [the Elvish realm](../L/Lothlórien.md) in [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md)'s writings. It is the ninth track on her third solo studio album, *Shepherd Moons*, which was released on [November 4, 1991](../_/4_November.md).

She performed vocals on two tracks of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack.md)*, which she also composed together with Roma Ryan, her lyricist. *[Aníron](../A/Aníron.md)* was the love-theme for [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md), and the Academy Award-nominated *[May It Be](../M/May_It_Be.md)* was included in the closing credits. Following her introduction to [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), Roma Ryan created her own language, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loxian" title="wikipedia:Loxian">Loxian</a>.
