The **Elves of Beleriand** were those [Elves](/wiki/Elves), predominantly [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar), who inhabited the subcontinent of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and established their realms during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). The Elves of Beleriand were protagonists in the [War of the Jewels](/wiki/Battles_of_Beleriand "Battles of Beleriand") against [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Steamey_-_Oss%C3%AB_and_the_Teleri.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/5/5d/Steamey_-_Oss%C3%AB_and_the_Teleri.jpg/300px-Steamey_-_Oss%C3%AB_and_the_Teleri.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="203" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/5/5d/Steamey_-_Oss%C3%AB_and_the_Teleri.jpg/450px-Steamey_-_Oss%C3%AB_and_the_Teleri.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/5/5d/Steamey_-_Oss%C3%AB_and_the_Teleri.jpg/600px-Steamey_-_Oss%C3%AB_and_the_Teleri.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="692"/></a><figcaption>Steamey - <i>Ossë and the Teleri</i></figcaption></figure>

The Elves first entered Beleriand in [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1125](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1125 "Year of the Trees 1125") when the [Minyar](/wiki/Minyar) and [Tatyar](/wiki/Tatyar) crossed the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin), while the remaining [Nelyar](/wiki/Nelyar) still lagged behind in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> But they were only passing as their final destination was the [Bay of Balar](/wiki/Bay_of_Balar) and finally were ferried to [Aman](/wiki/Aman) by [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo). The Nelyar finally entered Beleriand in [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1128](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1128 "Year of the Trees 1128") on their way to [Balar](/wiki/Balar), but part of them didn't wish to complete the journey: some of them, the **[Eglath](/wiki/Eglath)**, were looking for their leader [Elwë](/wiki/Elw%C3%AB) who was lost in the forests, being enchanted by the [Maia](/wiki/Maia) [Melian](/wiki/Melian); and others were persuaded by the Maia [Osse](/wiki/Osse) to remain with him at the shores of Beleriand. Those became the **[Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim)** and built themselves the [Havens of the Falas](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas) and became the first builders and sailors of ships.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/9/97/Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg/300px-Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="219" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/9/97/Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg/450px-Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/9/97/Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg/600px-Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg 2x" data-file-width="900" data-file-height="658"/></a><figcaption><i>The entrance to <a href="/wiki/Menegroth" title="Menegroth">Menegroth</a></i> by Luis F. Bejarano</figcaption></figure>

At long last Elwë awoke from the spell and returned to his people, founding a kingdom in the midst of Beleriand: [Eglador](/wiki/Eglador), later [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), claiming overlordship of all Beleriand as King Elu Thingol. The Falathrim, along with the Eglath, were considered **[Sindar](/wiki/Sindar)**.<sup id="cite_ref-sindar_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sindar-3">[3]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/a/a6/Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg/300px-Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="409" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/a/a6/Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg/450px-Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/a/a6/Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg/600px-Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg 2x" data-file-width="800" data-file-height="1091"/></a><figcaption><i>Green Elves</i> by Turner Mohan</figcaption></figure>

Some of the Nelyar [who had remained behind](/wiki/Nandor "Nandor") [far in the East](/wiki/Rhovanion "Rhovanion"), resumed their westward journey and after many years entered Beleriand settling at last in the Land of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand). But after the [First Battle of Beleriand](/wiki/First_Battle_of_Beleriand) those became reclusive, camouflaging in green, pulling away from the many troubles of Beleriand, and became the **[Laegrim](/wiki/Laegrim)**, the Green-elves<sup id="cite_ref-sindar_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sindar-3">[3]</a></sup> although some joined their kin in Doriath.<sup id="cite_ref-UT_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJP1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJP1-5">[5]</a></sup>

Although [Moriquendi](/wiki/Moriquendi), never having actually set foot in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), with the [Calaquendi](/wiki/Calaquendi), the Sindar became the fairest and most wise and skillful of the elves of Middle-earth under the rule of Thingol and Melian, and considered themselves *[celbin](/wiki/Calben "Calben")* persons of the Light.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Some [Avari](/wiki/Avari), those of the original Elves who never took the Great Journey and stayed in the far [East](/wiki/East), eventually wandered west; sneaking from the South of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains), they settled caves and deep forests of Beleriand (such as [Taur-im-Duinath](/wiki/Taur-im-Duinath)<sup id="cite_ref-bel_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bel-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-index_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-9">[9]</a></sup>) remaining secretive and isolated from the Eldar; rarely they would be accepted into Sindarin society.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ377_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ377-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Arrival of the Noldor {#Arrival_of_the_Noldor}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin%27s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/3/3b/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin%27s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg/300px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin%27s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="201" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/3/3b/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin%27s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg/450px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin%27s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/3/3b/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin%27s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg/600px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin%27s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg 2x" data-file-width="750" data-file-height="503"/></a><figcaption><i>Turin&#39;s first sight of Eldar</i> by <a href="/wiki/Peter_Xavier_Price" title="Peter Xavier Price">Peter Xavier Price</a></figcaption></figure>

At the rising of the Moon towards the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), many of the Noldor returned to Beleriand from Aman to make war on [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

[Twenty years after](/wiki/First_Age_20 "First Age 20") their arrival, [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor) [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) held a great celebratory feast to unite all Elves of Beleriand in one cause; ambassadors came from all realms of Beleriand, separate branches of the Noldor ([Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) and [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor) alone from the Sons of Feanor), Sindar, Falathrim, the Green-elves, and [two](/wiki/Mablung "Mablung") [ambassadors](/wiki/Daeron "Daeron") from Doriath. The *[Mereth Aderthad](/wiki/Mereth_Aderthad)* was a great success, and the Elves made common cause there against their common enemy, despite some enmity between the Sindar and the Noldor (especially the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor)). Soon after Beleriand plunged into the Wars that would eventually destroy it.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
