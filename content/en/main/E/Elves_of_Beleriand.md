The **Elves of Beleriand** were those [Elves](Elves.md), predominantly [Eldar](Eldar.md), who inhabited the subcontinent of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) and established their realms during the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). The Elves of Beleriand were protagonists in the [War of the Jewels](../B/Battles_of_Beleriand.md) against [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/S/Steamey_-_Ossë_and_the_Teleri.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5d/Steamey_-_Oss%C3%AB_and_the_Teleri.jpg/300px-Steamey_-_Oss%C3%AB_and_the_Teleri.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="203" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5d/Steamey_-_Oss%C3%AB_and_the_Teleri.jpg/450px-Steamey_-_Oss%C3%AB_and_the_Teleri.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5d/Steamey_-_Oss%C3%AB_and_the_Teleri.jpg/600px-Steamey_-_Oss%C3%AB_and_the_Teleri.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="692"/></a><figcaption>Steamey - <i>Ossë and the Teleri</i></figcaption></figure>

The Elves first entered Beleriand in [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1125](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1125.md) when the [Minyar](../M/Minyar.md) and [Tatyar](../T/Tatyar.md) crossed the [Ered Luin](Ered_Luin.md), while the remaining [Nelyar](../N/Nelyar.md) still lagged behind in [Eriador](Eriador.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> But they were only passing as their final destination was the [Bay of Balar](../B/Bay_of_Balar.md) and finally were ferried to [Aman](../A/Aman.md) by [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md). The Nelyar finally entered Beleriand in [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1128](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1128.md) on their way to [Balar](../B/Balar.md), but part of them didn't wish to complete the journey: some of them, the **[Eglath](Eglath.md)**, were looking for their leader [Elwë](Elwë.md) who was lost in the forests, being enchanted by the [Maia](../M/Maia.md) [Melian](../M/Melian.md); and others were persuaded by the Maia [Osse](../O/Osse.md) to remain with him at the shores of Beleriand. Those became the **[Falathrim](../F/Falathrim.md)** and built themselves the [Havens of the Falas](../H/Havens_of_the_Falas.md) and became the first builders and sailors of ships.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/L/Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/97/Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg/300px-Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="219" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/97/Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg/450px-Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/97/Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg/600px-Luis_F._Bejarano_-_The_entrance_to_Menegroth.jpg 2x" data-file-width="900" data-file-height="658"/></a><figcaption><i>The entrance to <a href="../M/Menegroth.md" title="Menegroth">Menegroth</a></i> by Luis F. Bejarano</figcaption></figure>

At long last Elwë awoke from the spell and returned to his people, founding a kingdom in the midst of Beleriand: [Eglador](Eglador.md), later [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), claiming overlordship of all Beleriand as King Elu Thingol. The Falathrim, along with the Eglath, were considered **[Sindar](../S/Sindar.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-sindar_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sindar-3">[3]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a6/Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg/300px-Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="409" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a6/Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg/450px-Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a6/Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg/600px-Turner_Mohan_-_Green_Elves.jpg 2x" data-file-width="800" data-file-height="1091"/></a><figcaption><i>Green Elves</i> by Turner Mohan</figcaption></figure>

Some of the Nelyar [who had remained behind](../N/Nandor.md) [far in the East](../R/Rhovanion.md), resumed their westward journey and after many years entered Beleriand settling at last in the Land of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md). But after the [First Battle of Beleriand](../F/First_Battle_of_Beleriand.md) those became reclusive, camouflaging in green, pulling away from the many troubles of Beleriand, and became the **[Laegrim](../L/Laegrim.md)**, the Green-elves<sup id="cite_ref-sindar_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sindar-3">[3]</a></sup> although some joined their kin in Doriath.<sup id="cite_ref-UT_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJP1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJP1-5">[5]</a></sup>

Although [Moriquendi](../M/Moriquendi.md), never having actually set foot in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), with the [Calaquendi](../C/Calaquendi.md), the Sindar became the fairest and most wise and skillful of the elves of Middle-earth under the rule of Thingol and Melian, and considered themselves *[celbin](../C/Calben.md)* persons of the Light.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Some [Avari](../A/Avari.md), those of the original Elves who never took the Great Journey and stayed in the far [East](East.md), eventually wandered west; sneaking from the South of the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md), they settled caves and deep forests of Beleriand (such as [Taur-im-Duinath](../T/Taur-im-Duinath.md)<sup id="cite_ref-bel_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bel-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-index_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-9">[9]</a></sup>) remaining secretive and isolated from the Eldar; rarely they would be accepted into Sindarin society.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ377_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ377-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Arrival of the Noldor {#Arrival_of_the_Noldor}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/P/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin&#39;s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/3b/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin%27s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg/300px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin%27s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="201" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/3b/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin%27s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg/450px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin%27s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/3b/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin%27s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg/600px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_Turin%27s_first_sight_of_Eldar.jpg 2x" data-file-width="750" data-file-height="503"/></a><figcaption><i>Turin&#39;s first sight of Eldar</i> by <a href="../P/Peter_Xavier_Price.md" title="Peter Xavier Price">Peter Xavier Price</a></figcaption></figure>

At the rising of the Moon towards the end of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), many of the Noldor returned to Beleriand from Aman to make war on [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

[Twenty years after](../F/First_Age_20.md) their arrival, [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) held a great celebratory feast to unite all Elves of Beleriand in one cause; ambassadors came from all realms of Beleriand, separate branches of the Noldor ([Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) and [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md) alone from the Sons of Feanor), Sindar, Falathrim, the Green-elves, and [two](../M/Mablung.md) [ambassadors](../D/Daeron.md) from Doriath. The *[Mereth Aderthad](../M/Mereth_Aderthad.md)* was a great success, and the Elves made common cause there against their common enemy, despite some enmity between the Sindar and the Noldor (especially the [Sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md)). Soon after Beleriand plunged into the Wars that would eventually destroy it.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
