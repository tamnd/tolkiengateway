**Edoras** was the capital of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) that held the Golden Hall of [Meduseld](../M/Meduseld.md).

## History {#History}

Rohan's first capital was at [Aldburg](../A/Aldburg.md) in the [Folde](../F/Folde.md). Sometime after the battle of the [Field of Celebrant](../F/Field_of_Celebrant.md) (T.A. 2510), [King](../K/King_of_Rohan.md) [Eorl](Eorl.md) the Young, or possibly his son [Brego,](../B/Brego.md) built Edoras. In T.A. 2569, Brego built [Meduseld](../M/Meduseld.md), the Golden Hall.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> on the top of the hill on which Edoras was built.

[Wulf](../W/Wulf.md) son of [Freca](../F/Freca.md) the [Dunlending](../D/Dunlendings.md), invaded Rohan in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2758](../T/Third_Age_2758.md) and took Edoras, usurping the throne in Meduseld. But the [Long Winter](../L/Long_Winter.md), which had lasted five long months, ended and [Fréaláf](../F/Fréaláf.md) son of [Hild](../H/Hild.md) came out of [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md) and with a small band of men, surprising Wulf, he attacked **Edoras** and slew Wulf, regaining the city.

It was here that [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) the White met with [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

The city was built on a hill in [Harrowdale](../H/Harrowdale.md), a valley of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) which lay under the great mountain [Starkhorn](../S/Starkhorn.md). The river [Snowbourn](../S/Snowbourn.md) flowed past Edoras on its way west towards the [Entwash](Entwash.md).

Edoras was encircled by a wall, with a dike dug around the hill. It had a spring whose water flowed in a channel along the main road.

Outside, there was the [Barrowfield](../B/Barrowfield.md), with the tombs of the [Rulers of Rohan](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) arrayed in two rows by the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). [Simbelmynë](../S/Simbelmynë.md) grew there. Through the two rows of the [Barrowfield](../B/Barrowfield.md), a rutted road led from the city gates passed the [Snowbourn](../S/Snowbourn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Edoras* is said to mean "the courts" in the Mark-speech of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [Old English](../O/Old_English.md), *edoras* (plural of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/eodor" title="wiktionary:eodor">edor, eodor</a></i>) means "dwellings, houses".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Instead, <a href="../A/Aldburg.md" title="Aldburg">Aldburg</a> functions as the &#34;capital settlement&#34; of the Western Rohan, hosting the hub for swift travel and various player facilities.</dd></dl>
