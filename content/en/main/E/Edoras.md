**Edoras** was the capital of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) that held the Golden Hall of [Meduseld](/wiki/Meduseld).

## History {#History}

Rohan's first capital was at [Aldburg](/wiki/Aldburg) in the [Folde](/wiki/Folde). Sometime after the battle of the [Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Field_of_Celebrant) (T.A. 2510), [King](/wiki/King_of_Rohan "King of Rohan") [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) the Young, or possibly his son [Brego,](/wiki/Brego "Brego") built Edoras. In T.A. 2569, Brego built [Meduseld](/wiki/Meduseld), the Golden Hall.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> on the top of the hill on which Edoras was built.

[Wulf](/wiki/Wulf) son of [Freca](/wiki/Freca) the [Dunlending](/wiki/Dunlendings "Dunlendings"), invaded Rohan in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758") and took Edoras, usurping the throne in Meduseld. But the [Long Winter](/wiki/Long_Winter), which had lasted five long months, ended and [Fréaláf](/wiki/Fr%C3%A9al%C3%A1f) son of [Hild](/wiki/Hild) came out of [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) and with a small band of men, surprising Wulf, he attacked **Edoras** and slew Wulf, regaining the city.

It was here that [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) the White met with [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden).

## Geography {#Geography}

The city was built on a hill in [Harrowdale](/wiki/Harrowdale), a valley of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) which lay under the great mountain [Starkhorn](/wiki/Starkhorn). The river [Snowbourn](/wiki/Snowbourn) flowed past Edoras on its way west towards the [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash).

Edoras was encircled by a wall, with a dike dug around the hill. It had a spring whose water flowed in a channel along the main road.

Outside, there was the [Barrowfield](/wiki/Barrowfield), with the tombs of the [Rulers of Rohan](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan") arrayed in two rows by the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). [Simbelmynë](/wiki/Simbelmyn%C3%AB) grew there. Through the two rows of the [Barrowfield](/wiki/Barrowfield), a rutted road led from the city gates passed the [Snowbourn](/wiki/Snowbourn).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Edoras* is said to mean "the courts" in the Mark-speech of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [Old English](/wiki/Old_English), *edoras* (plural of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/eodor" title="wiktionary:eodor">edor, eodor</a></i>) means "dwellings, houses".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
