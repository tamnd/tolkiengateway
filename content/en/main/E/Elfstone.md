The **Elfstone** ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") <b><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Elessar" title="Elessar">Elessar</a></b>), also known as the **Stone of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil)**, was one, or possibly two, great green jewel(s) of healing power whose legends of creation are conflicting and complex.

## History {#History}

### Origin {#Origin}

The Elfstone, or at least the first of them, was made in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) during the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). Some name [Enerdhil](/wiki/Enerdhil) the jewel-smith as its maker, but others say that it was his friend, [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor) son of [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup> After the completion of the jewel, many [Elves](/wiki/Elves), including the other Noldor, were astonished by it, for the stone was green as the leaves but had the clear light of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) imprisoned within it. It was said that any who looked upon the stone would see withered and aged things as whole and young again. It was also claimed that it granted any who wore the gem the power to heal any hurts of anyone that they touched.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:249</sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Tatyafinwe_-_Idril_Celebrindal.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/6/64/Tatyafinwe_-_Idril_Celebrindal.jpg/260px-Tatyafinwe_-_Idril_Celebrindal.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="260" height="347" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/6/64/Tatyafinwe_-_Idril_Celebrindal.jpg/390px-Tatyafinwe_-_Idril_Celebrindal.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/6/64/Tatyafinwe_-_Idril_Celebrindal.jpg/520px-Tatyafinwe_-_Idril_Celebrindal.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1680" data-file-height="2241"/></a><figcaption>The Elessar was in Gondolin in the First Age with the princess <i>Idril Celebrindal</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Tatyafinwe" title="Category:Images by Tatyafinwe">Tatyafinwe</a></figcaption></figure>

The Elfstone was eventually given to [Idril Celebrindal](/wiki/Idril "Idril"), who wore it upon her breast, and as such, it was saved from perishing during the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin). Eventually, Idril passed it on to her son [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil), whose first memory was of Idril wearing the gem while singing to him. At [Sirion's Haven](/wiki/Havens_of_Sirion "Havens of Sirion"), Eärendil used the Elfstone to heal the hurts of many of the [Men](/wiki/Men), [Elves](/wiki/Elves), and [beasts](/wiki/Kelvar "Kelvar") that dwelled there. Eventually, Eärendil carried it with him on his voyages across the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") to the [Blessed Realm](/wiki/Aman "Aman").<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:249</sup>

A legend says that when the [Wizards](/wiki/Wizards) were sent from Valinor to Middle-earth in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), [Olórin](/wiki/Gandalf "Gandalf") brought back Eärendil's jewel as a token from [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) that the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) had not forsaken them; as [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), he gave it to Galadriel, and remarked prophetically that she would only hold it for a little while, before she passed it to another, who will also be called [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn").<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:250</sup>

However, another legend says that Celebrimbor, who was in love with [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), remade another version of the lost jewel during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) and placed it within a great brooch of silver. It was said that this Elfstone was "*more subtle and clear*" yet had less power than the original. It was made at Galadriel's behest, pained at the state of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). When she wore it, "*all things grew fair*" around her.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:251</sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

Whatever the origins of Galadriel's Elfstone were, she gave it to her daughter [Celebrían](/wiki/Celebr%C3%ADan), who in turn gave it to [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:251</sup>

[Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), during his stay in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), was urged by [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) to include a green jewel in his *[Eärendillinwë](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendillinw%C3%AB)*, possibly anticipating the symbolic importance that the gem would have in his life. Bilbo Baggins, obeying Aragorn, but seemingly unaware of the Elfstone's story, included an inaccurate reference to an emerald.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Tatyafinwe_-_Galadriel_wearing_the_Elessar.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/5/58/Tatyafinwe_-_Galadriel_wearing_the_Elessar.jpg/253px-Tatyafinwe_-_Galadriel_wearing_the_Elessar.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="253" height="337" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/5/58/Tatyafinwe_-_Galadriel_wearing_the_Elessar.jpg/379px-Tatyafinwe_-_Galadriel_wearing_the_Elessar.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/5/58/Tatyafinwe_-_Galadriel_wearing_the_Elessar.jpg/505px-Tatyafinwe_-_Galadriel_wearing_the_Elessar.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1680" data-file-height="2240"/></a><figcaption><i>Galadriel wearing the Elessar</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Tatyafinwe" title="Category:Images by Tatyafinwe">Tatyafinwe</a></figcaption></figure>

When the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) visited the wood of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), the Elfstone was again under Galadriel's possession. When the Fellowship departed and Galadriel offered them [her gifts](/wiki/Gifts_of_Galadriel "Gifts of Galadriel"), the Elfstone was the gift for Aragorn.<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-3">[3]</a></sup> This giving held the function of a wedding gift from the family of the bride to the groom, foretelling his marriage to Arwen.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The Elfstone was worn by Aragorn ever after, and as foretold, he took the name of *Elessar, the Elfstone of the [House of Elendil](/wiki/House_of_Elendil)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-3">[3]</a></sup> Thus, when he manifested his royal blood at [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), he used Elessar, the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for Elfstone, as one of his royal names,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> being crowned as King Elessar.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

An early concept for the Elfstone was the **Green Stone of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor)**. This stone was given from Fëanor to his eldest son [Maidros](/wiki/Maedhros "Maedhros") on his deathbed, and then later given from Maidros to [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Christopher Tolkien noted that this was likely his father "pondering the previous history of the *Elessar*", though this version of the story was not further expanded upon and likely dismissed in favor of the histories present in *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*.

At one point during the development of "*[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*", *[King Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur "Eärnur")*, was named **King Elessar** in an abandoned draft and manuscript.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
