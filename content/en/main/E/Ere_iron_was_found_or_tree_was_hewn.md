"**Ere iron was found or tree was hewn**" is the first line of a short poem recited by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) to [King Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden "Théoden"), when asked about what caused the summoning of the [Huorns](/wiki/Huorns) during the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg). Could it have been an act of "[wizardry](/wiki/Magic "Magic")?", asked Théoden. Gandalf said that it was "a power far older", and went on to recite the poem. Théoden identified Gandalf's lines of verse as a [riddle](/wiki/Riddle-game "Riddle-game").<sup id="cite_ref-TTIII8_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTIII8-1">[1]</a></sup>

## The poem {#The_poem}

*Ere iron was found or tree was hewn,<br>When young was mountain under moon;<br>Ere ring was made, or wrought was woe,<br>It walked the forests long ago.*<sup id="cite_ref-TTIII8_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTIII8-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Composition {#Composition}

The poem contains <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iamb_(poetry)" title="wikipedia:Iamb (poetry)">four iambs in each line</a>, and the lines <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Couplet" title="wikipedia:Couplet">rhyme in pairs</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Kamelen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kamelen-2">[2]</a></sup> [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger) has described the poem as a piece of "folk verse".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Name {#Name}

Among [fans](/wiki/Tolkien_fandom "Tolkien fandom"), the poem is commonly called **Gandalf's Riddle of the Ents**.<sup id="cite_ref-Kamelen_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kamelen-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

It has been noted that Tolkien re-used a phrase from *[The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun)*:<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>ere fire was found or iron hewn,<br/>when young was mountain under moon.
</p>

</blockquote>
