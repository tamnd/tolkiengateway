"**Ere iron was found or tree was hewn**" is the first line of a short poem recited by [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) to [King Théoden](../T/Théoden.md), when asked about what caused the summoning of the [Huorns](../H/Huorns.md) during the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md). Could it have been an act of "[wizardry](../M/Magic.md)?", asked Théoden. Gandalf said that it was "a power far older", and went on to recite the poem. Théoden identified Gandalf's lines of verse as a [riddle](../R/Riddle-game.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TTIII8_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTIII8-1">[1]</a></sup>

## The poem {#The_poem}

*Ere iron was found or tree was hewn,<br>When young was mountain under moon;<br>Ere ring was made, or wrought was woe,<br>It walked the forests long ago.*<sup id="cite_ref-TTIII8_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTIII8-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Composition {#Composition}

The poem contains <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iamb_(poetry)" title="wikipedia:Iamb (poetry)">four iambs in each line</a>, and the lines <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Couplet" title="wikipedia:Couplet">rhyme in pairs</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Kamelen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kamelen-2">[2]</a></sup> [Verlyn Flieger](../V/Verlyn_Flieger.md) has described the poem as a piece of "folk verse".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Name {#Name}

Among [fans](../T/Tolkien_fandom.md), the poem is commonly called **Gandalf's Riddle of the Ents**.<sup id="cite_ref-Kamelen_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kamelen-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

It has been noted that Tolkien re-used a phrase from *[The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun](../T/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun.md)*:<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>ere fire was found or iron hewn,<br/>when young was mountain under moon.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>vv. 299-300<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>
