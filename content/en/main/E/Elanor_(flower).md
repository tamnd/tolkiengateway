<i><b>Elanor</b></i> was a golden, star-shaped flower that grew in abundance in the forest of [Lórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) thought highly of the flower and, at [Frodo's](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") suggestion, named his eldest daughter, [Elanor Gardner](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner), from it.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was one of the flowers brought by the Elves of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Elanor and [Lissuin](/wiki/Lissuin) were used to decorate the ships during the wedding of [Aldarion](/wiki/Aldarion) and [Erendis](/wiki/Erendis).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

It was like "a <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lysimachia_arvensis" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">pimpernel</a> (perhaps a little enlarged) growing sun-golden flowers and star-silver ones on the same plant, and sometimes the two combined".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *elanor* means "Sun-star" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (from *[êl](/wiki/%C3%8Al "Êl")* = "star" and *anor* = "[Sun](/wiki/Sun)").
