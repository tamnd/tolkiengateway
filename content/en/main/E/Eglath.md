The **Eglath** ("The Forsaken") were the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) [Elves](/wiki/Elves) ([Eldar](/wiki/Eldar)) of the [Great March](/wiki/Great_March) who decided to stay in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) seeking their lost leader [Elwë](/wiki/Elw%C3%AB) ([Thingol](/wiki/Thingol)). The main host of the Teleri followed [Olwe](/wiki/Olwe) on their way to [Aman](/wiki/Aman).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Eglath eventually became the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) with Thingol as their King.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name comes from [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) verb <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Egla&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Egla (page does not exist)">egla</a></i> plus the plural ending *[-ath](/wiki/-ath)*. <span></span>
