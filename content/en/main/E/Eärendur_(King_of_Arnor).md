**Eärendur** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌe.aˈrendur]</span>; [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [640](/wiki/Third_Age_640 "Third Age 640")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> - [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [861](/wiki/Third_Age_861 "Third Age 861"),<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup> died aged 221) was the tenth and last [King of Arnor](/wiki/King_of_Arnor).

## History {#History}

Eärendur succeeded his father [Elendur](</wiki/Elendur_(King_of_Arnor)> "Elendur (King of Arnor)") on his death in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [777](/wiki/Third_Age_777 "Third Age 777"). Eärendur's rule was uneventful, but he was the tenth and last [King of Arnor](/wiki/King_of_Arnor) due to dissension amongst his sons.

After his death in [861](/wiki/Third_Age_861 "Third Age 861"), probably due to jealousy on the part of the two younger sons, his three sons split [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) into three separate kingdoms: [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan) and [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur).

His eldest son and rightful heir, [Amlaith](/wiki/Amlaith) of [Fornost](/wiki/Fornost), became the first [King of Arthedain](/wiki/King_of_Arthedain), and it was through Amlaith and his heirs that the line of Isildur endured whilst the line of kings of Rhudaur and Cardolan failed - [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") was a direct descendant of Amlaith, and it wasn't until [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) became king that Arnor was finally reunified.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After Eärendur the [Kings of Arthedain](/wiki/Kings_of_Arthedain)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and the [Chieftains of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain) no longer took names in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (High-elven<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>), but took names in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) instead.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eärendur* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. It means "(professional) mariner".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> As Paul Strack explains, a more literal translation might be "Servant of the Sea". It is a compound of [eär](/wiki/E%C3%A4r "Eär") ("sea") and [-(n)dur](/wiki/-ndur "-ndur") ("servant").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> It is unlikely that Eärendur himself was devoted to the sea: it is more likely that he was named after one of the two previous <a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(disambiguation)" title="Eärendur (disambiguation)">Eärendurs</a>.
