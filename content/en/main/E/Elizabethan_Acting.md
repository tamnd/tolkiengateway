<i><b>Elizabethan Acting</b></i> is volume two in the *Oxford English Monographs* series. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) was one of the General Editors for the series. In the introduction, editor B.L. Joseph thanks Tolkien for his "comment and encouragement".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
