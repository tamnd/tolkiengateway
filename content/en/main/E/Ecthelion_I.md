**Ecthelion I** ([Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) [2600](../T/Third_Age_2600.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> – [2698](../T/Third_Age_2698.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 98 years) was the seventeenth Ruling Steward of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

## History {#History}

He succeeded his father [Orodreth](<../O/Orodreth_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) at his death in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2685](../T/Third_Age_2685.md). He rebuilt the [White Tower](../T/Tower_of_Ecthelion.md) in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), which afterwards carried his name.

Ecthelion had no children. He was followed by [Egalmoth](<Egalmoth_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>), the grandson of Orodreth's sister [Morwen](<../M/Morwen_(daughter_of_Belecthor).md>), at his death in [2698](../T/Third_Age_2698.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

For the meaning of his name refer to [Ecthelion - Etymology](Ecthelion.md#Etymology). It is possible that Ecthelion I was named after the [Noldo](../N/Noldor.md) hero of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
