Eamon was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios), where he was portrayed by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Berynn_Schwerdt&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Berynn Schwerdt (page does not exist)">Berynn Schwerdt</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
