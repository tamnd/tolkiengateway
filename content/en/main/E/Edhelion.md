**Edhelion**, now called the **Refuge of Edhelion**, was an Elven city near [Thorin's Hall](/wiki/Thorin%27s_Hall) in the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) in the game *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*.

## History {#History}

Edhelion was founded on the slopes of the Ered Luin by the Elves of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) thousands of years before the events of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) to make elven wine and gather wood for the ships of [Mithlond](/wiki/Mithlond), probably working closely with the Havens of [Celondim](/wiki/Celondim).

Approximately 600 years before the War of the Ring, the Elven players begin in Edhelion. [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) introduces them to [Talagan Silvertongue](/wiki/Talagan_Silvertongue), lord of Edhelion. While the player is visiting it, it is about to be under attack by [Skorgrím](/wiki/Skorgr%C3%ADm) and the [Dourhands](/wiki/Dourhands). Elrond quickly teaches the player to fight, and when the Dourhands attack the player has to fight them off.<sup id="cite_ref-Refuge_of_Edhelion_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Refuge_of_Edhelion-1">[1]</a></sup> Together with Elrond and the Elf [Dorongúr Whitethorn](/wiki/Dorong%C3%BAr_Whitethorn) the player fights of the enemies. But when Skorgrím comes, Talagan fights him before the Library of Edhelion and, after bringing it down upon them, Talagan kills himself, and Skorgrím with him.<sup id="cite_ref-Skorgr%25C3%25ADm_Dourhand_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Skorgr%25C3%25ADm_Dourhand-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3016](/wiki/Third_Age_3016 "Third Age 3016") Elrond has a dream about Edhelion and he sends his sons [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan) and [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir) to investigate. They camp at Edhelion Watch, where a group of Elves have gathered that Elladan and Elrohir take as companions to investigate the dream, alongside the player. There is also a dwarf, visiting because there were some interesting findings in the Silver Deep mines not far away.<sup id="cite_ref-Edhelion_Watch_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Edhelion_Watch-4">[4]</a></sup> Dorongúr Whitethorn is also of that company and, together with the elf [Dorollas](/wiki/List_of_minor_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_characters_in_the_Ered_Luin#Dorollas "List of minor The Lord of the Rings Online characters in the Ered Luin") he is attempting to reclaim the city of Edhelion, but it is infested with orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the introduction quests, only a Lore-master named Isdi remains behind and Edhelion is deserted once again.

## Legacy {#Legacy}

The survivors of the Fall of Edhelion fled to lower Ered Luin, settling at first in [Gondamon](/wiki/Gondamon) at the midway point between [Thorin's Hall](/wiki/Thorin%27s_Hall) and [Kheledûl](/wiki/Kheled%C3%BBl). This city they built themselves, but when they left for Duillond the Dwarves who came after them changed it to their liking.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondamon_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondamon-6">[6]</a></sup>

After they left Gondamon, desiring to go nearer to the sea, they built [Duillond](/wiki/Duillond) north of [Celondim](/wiki/Celondim) and there was great friendship between these peoples.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondamon_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondamon-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Descprition {#Descprition}

Edhelion lies to the north of the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) in a valley. It was originally built to make elven wine and to gather wood for the construction of ships in [Mithlond](/wiki/Mithlond), by ferrying it via the docks of [Celondim](/wiki/Celondim). In the centre of the city stood the Library of Edhelion, with a great bridge leading to it.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Edhelion* means "Valley of Elves", from *[edhel](/wiki/Edhel "Edhel")* meaning "elf"<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and *ion* meaning "region between mountains".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Edhelion_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Edhelion-2">[2]</a></sup>
