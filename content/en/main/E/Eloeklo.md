**Eloeklo** was a [demon](/wiki/Demons "Demons") invented by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*. In the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), he was [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s guardian of the [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains). During the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), a [Noldo](/wiki/Noldo) bound him to the cold northern wind and he lost the ability to hold a stable [fana](/wiki/Fana "Fana"). Only in blizzards or when explicitly summoned could he somewhat regain his demon-shaped form, in which he carried a whip of barbed ice.<sup id="cite_ref-bio_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bio-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the [Sack of Eregion](/wiki/Sack_of_Eregion), Celebring, a Noldo in service of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), was tasked to pursue the [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor)'s brother Lindor. Lindor and his companions had fled into Forodwaith, and Celebring was able to forge an alliance with Eloeklo. While passing the [Bleak Mountains](/wiki/Bleak_Mountains), Lindor's company was attacked by the demon. Lindor challenged the demon in song, and while the Elf was defeated, the other refugees were able to escape and founded [Evermist](/wiki/Evermist).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Lindor's frozen body still stood on the peak on which Lindor fought the demon, which was afterwards known as Linnarthurras ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Peak of Indomitable Song”).

In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), Eloeklo would wage war on the Noldo of Evermist several times, rallying all the spirits of wind and cold. These were the first and second Wind Wars, known respectively as the [Long](/wiki/Long_Winter "Long Winter") and [Fell Winter](</wiki/Fell_Winter_(Third_Age)> "Fell Winter (Third Age)") in the South.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
