**Elaine Griffiths** ([1909](/wiki/1909)-[1996](/wiki/1996)) was Fellow in English and Chair of the English Faculty Board at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Anne%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Anne&#39;s College, Oxford">St Anne&#39;s College</a>, [Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford").<sup id="cite_ref-Anne_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Anne-1">[1]</a></sup>

After taking her degree in English, Griffiths undertook research under the supervision of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), acquiring an interest in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) Philology.<sup id="cite_ref-Anne_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Anne-1">[1]</a></sup> She soon became one of Tolkien's close friends, and was one of the early readers of a manuscript of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. It was Griffiths who suggested <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Susan_Dagnall&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Susan Dagnall (page does not exist)">Susan Dagnall</a> of [Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/George_Allen_and_Unwin "George Allen and Unwin") to borrow the typescript from Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Tolkien signed a copy of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* to her with a dedication in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *Elainen [tárin](/wiki/T%C3%A1ri "Tári") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Periando&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Periando (page does not exist)">periandion</a> [ar](/wiki/Ar "Ar") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Melde&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Melde (page does not exist)">meldenya</a> [an](/wiki/An- "An-")[yáran](/wiki/Y%C3%A1ra "Yára")* (to Elaine, Queen of Hobbits and my very old friend).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The copy is currently held at collection of the [Greisinger Museum](/wiki/Greisinger_Museum).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
