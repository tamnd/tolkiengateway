**Egalmoth** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) 2626<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> - 2743,<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 117 years) was the eighteenth Ruling Steward of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## History {#History}

Egalmoth was probably named after the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

He succeeded his cousin [Ecthelion I](/wiki/Ecthelion_I) upon his death in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2698](/wiki/Third_Age_2698 "Third Age 2698"). Egalmoth was the grandson of [Morwen](</wiki/Morwen_(daughter_of_Belecthor)> "Morwen (daughter of Belecthor)"), daughter of [Belecthor I](/wiki/Belecthor_I). During his rule there was renewed war with the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2710](/wiki/Third_Age_2710 "Third Age 2710") King [Déor](/wiki/D%C3%A9or) asked his help to drive off the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) from the occupied [Ring of Isengard](/wiki/Ring_of_Isengard), but the Steward was unable to send help.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

Egalmoth was followed by his son [Beren](</wiki/Beren_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Beren (Steward of Gondor)") at his death in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2743](/wiki/Third_Age_2743 "Third Age 2743").<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

For the meaning of his name refer to [Egalmoth - Etymology](/wiki/Egalmoth#Etymology "Egalmoth"). It is possible that Egalmoth was named after [Egalmoth](/wiki/Egalmoth), an elvish lord of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
