**Eglamar** was a major region in the [Falas](../F/Falas.md), encompassing the entire [sea](../B/Belegaer.md)-board from [Cape Andras](../C/Cape_Andras.md) to the northwest all the way to the headland of [Ras Mewrim](../R/Ras_Mewrim.md) in the southeast.<sup id="cite_ref-Egla_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Egla-1">[1]</a></sup> The region of Eglamar also included the [havens](../H/Havens_of_the_Falas.md) of [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) at [Brithombar](../B/Brithombar.md) and [Eglarest](Eglarest.md), the two most important cities of the [Falathrim](../F/Falathrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Egla_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Egla-1">[1]</a></sup>

Eglamar was also where [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) built the tower of [Barad Nimras](../B/Barad_Nimras.md) upon the cape west of Eglarest, guarding against a potential naval attack by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) - however, such an assault never happened.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

However, Eglamar is only named on *[the Second 'Silmarillion' Map](../T/The_Second_'Silmarillion'_Map.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> which formed the basis for the [map of Beleriand](../M/Map_of_Beleriand_and_the_Lands_to_the_North.md) in the published *[Silmarillion](../S/Silmarillion.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> though both Eglamar and Ras Mewrim were not labelled on the published *Silmarillion* map.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Eglamar* is in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), meaning "Home of the [Eglain](Eglain.md)". It consists of the prefixal form *Egla-* (from <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Eglan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Eglan (page does not exist)">Eglan</a></i>, meaning "Forsaken Elf") + *[bar](../B/Bar.md)* ("home").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
