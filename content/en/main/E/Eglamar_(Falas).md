**Eglamar** was a major region in the [Falas](/wiki/Falas), encompassing the entire [sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer")-board from [Cape Andras](/wiki/Cape_Andras) to the northwest all the way to the headland of [Ras Mewrim](/wiki/Ras_Mewrim) in the southeast.<sup id="cite_ref-Egla_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Egla-1">[1]</a></sup> The region of Eglamar also included the [havens](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas "Havens of the Falas") of [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) at [Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar) and [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest), the two most important cities of the [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim).<sup id="cite_ref-Egla_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Egla-1">[1]</a></sup>

Eglamar was also where [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) built the tower of [Barad Nimras](/wiki/Barad_Nimras) upon the cape west of Eglarest, guarding against a potential naval attack by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) - however, such an assault never happened.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

However, Eglamar is only named on *[the Second 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_Second_%27Silmarillion%27_Map "The Second 'Silmarillion' Map")*,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> which formed the basis for the [map of Beleriand](/wiki/Map_of_Beleriand_and_the_Lands_to_the_North "Map of Beleriand and the Lands to the North") in the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)*,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> though both Eglamar and Ras Mewrim were not labelled on the published *Silmarillion* map.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Eglamar* is in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), meaning "Home of the [Eglain](/wiki/Eglain)". It consists of the prefixal form *Egla-* (from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Eglan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Eglan (page does not exist)">Eglan</a></i>, meaning "Forsaken Elf") + *[bar](/wiki/Bar "Bar")* ("home").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
