**Eärendur** (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌe.aˈrendur]</span>) is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Mariner" (lit. "sea-servant"), and can refer to:

- [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(son_of_Tar-Amandil)> "Eärendur (son of Tar-Amandil)") (born [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [361](/wiki/Second_Age_361 "Second Age 361")), the second son of the third [King](/wiki/Kings_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Kings of Númenor") of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), [Tar-Amandil](/wiki/Tar-Amandil).
- [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)> "Eärendur (Lord of Andúnië)") (late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age)), the fifteenth [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië").
- [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(King_of_Arnor)> "Eärendur (King of Arnor)") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [640](/wiki/Third_Age_640 "Third Age 640") - [861](/wiki/Third_Age_861 "Third Age 861")), the tenth, and last, [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor "Kings of Arnor") of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor).
