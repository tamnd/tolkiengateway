**Eärendur** (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌe.aˈrendur]</span>) is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "Mariner" (lit. "sea-servant"), and can refer to:

- [Eärendur](<Eärendur_(son_of_Tar-Amandil).md>) (born [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [361](../S/Second_Age_361.md)), the second son of the third [King](../K/Kings_of_Númenor.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), [Tar-Amandil](../T/Tar-Amandil.md).
- [Eärendur](<Eärendur_(Lord_of_Andúnië).md>) (late [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md)), the fifteenth [Lord of Andúnië](../L/Lords_of_Andúnië.md).
- [Eärendur](<Eärendur_(King_of_Arnor).md>) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [640](../T/Third_Age_640.md) - [861](../T/Third_Age_861.md)), the tenth, and last, [King](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md) of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md).
