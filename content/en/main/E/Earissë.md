**Earissë** was the daughter of [Lindo](../L/Lindo.md) and the wife of [Ælfwine](../_/Ælfwine.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. She was the mother of [Heorrenda](../H/Heorrenda.md) and [Hlúdwine](../H/Hlúdwine.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ear_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ear-2">[1]</a></sup>

Earissë only appears in two of the notes in the <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Notebook_C&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Notebook C (page does not exist)">Notebook C</a></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ear_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ear-2">[1]</a></sup> a small pocket-book which [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) used for notes and suggestions while writing *The Book of Lost Tales*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

Nothing else is known about her.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Earissë* is in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), probably being a phonological imitation of the English name <i><b>Edith</b></i> and its [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) form *Eadgyth*. *Eadgyth* consists of the elements *éad* ("riches, prosperity, good fortune, happiness") + *gyth*, a form of *gúð* ("combat, battle, war").<sup id="cite_ref-Ear_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ear-2">[1]</a></sup>

The first element of the name *Earissë* might be <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ea(r)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ea(r) (page does not exist)">ea(r)</a>/<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Earen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Earen (page does not exist)">earen</a>(-d)</i> ("eagle"), the second being *[-ssë](../_/-ssë.md)*, a feminine name ending occurring in several other names in the early [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ear_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ear-2">[1]</a></sup>

An earlier form of the name was *Eadissë*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ear_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ear-2">[1]</a></sup>
