**Eldest** was a title for the eldest of all beings. Confusingly, it seems to apply to two different individuals; not only does [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md) claim it for himself,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> but [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) also uses it to address the old [Ent](Ents.md) [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Tom Bombadil's nature](../T/Tom_Bombadil%252FNature~74818.md), including what manner of being he is and if he can be considered alive and [incarnate](../I/Incarnate.md), remains unknown. The matter of who is truly the eldest living being in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) is a mystery. <span></span>
