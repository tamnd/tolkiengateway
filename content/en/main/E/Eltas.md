**Eltas** was a [man](/wiki/Man "Man") whose people lived in a vale of [Hisilómë](/wiki/Hisil%C3%B3m%C3%AB) and that he had come to [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) by [Olórë Mallë](/wiki/Ol%C3%B3r%C3%AB_Mall%C3%AB) "in the days before the fall of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin)". The *[Tale of Turambar](/wiki/Turambar_and_the_Foal%C3%B3k%C3%AB "Turambar and the Foalókë")* he had heard from the older men of his people who hated [Melko](/wiki/Melko) and his "evil worms".<sup id="cite_ref-Turambar_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turambar-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:70, 118</sup>

He was in the [Cottage of Lost Play](/wiki/Cottage_of_Lost_Play) on the second day of the feast of [Turuhalmë](/wiki/Turuhalm%C3%AB); in the absence of [Ailios](/wiki/Gilfanon "Gilfanon"), Eltas told the tale of *[Turambar and the Foalókë](/wiki/Turambar_and_the_Foal%C3%B3k%C3%AB)* to [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol).<sup id="cite_ref-Turambar_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turambar-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:69–70</sup>

After the *Tale of Turambar* Eltas was asked to tell the story of [the Nauglafring](/wiki/The_Nauglafring "The Nauglafring"), but he said that the tales of [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and [Beren](/wiki/Beren) should be told first.<sup id="cite_ref-Turambar_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turambar-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:144</sup> However, he didn't tell the story of the Nauglafring, and he doesn't appear any more.
