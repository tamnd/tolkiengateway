The <i><b>Eruhantalë</b></i> was the last of the [Three Prayers](../T/Three_Prayers.md) to [Eru](../I/Ilúvatar.md) spoken each year by the [King](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) or [Queen](../R/Ruling_Queen_of_Númenor.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). In this ceremony, the monarch and his or her people gave thanks to Eru. It was held at the end of each autumn. During the *Eruhantalë*, the King or Queen would ascend the [Meneltarma](../M/Meneltarma.md), accompanied by a silent, white-clad crowd of people. The ruler would make a prayer and a bloodless offering on the mountain's summit, while three great [Eagles](Eagles.md), the [Witnesses of Manwë](../W/Witnesses_of_Manwë.md), hovered overhead.<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eruhantalë* means "Thanksgiving to Eru" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), from the word *[hantalë](../H/Hantalë.md)* ("thanksgiving").<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1996: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="../S/Southern_Gondor%253A_The_People.md" title="Southern Gondor: The People">Southern Gondor: The People</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>The date for the Eruhantalë was fixed at 10 <a href="../_/Úrimë.md" title="Úrimë">Úrivi</a> (1 August). The offering made by the King consisted of the first harvested fruits, as well as bread baked from the first grains.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>
