<i><b>en-</b></i> is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) prefix equivalent to the English prefix "re-, again".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Example {#Example}

- **enquanta-** = "refill" (**quanta-** = "to fill")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}

- [En-keladim](/wiki/En-keladim), a word with a similar prefix
