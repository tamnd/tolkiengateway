The **Entwash Vale** was a green and fenny vale in [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), formed at the lower [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Entwash Vale is one of the four major subdivisions of the <a href="/wiki/Eastemnet" title="Eastemnet">Eastemnet</a>. It is ruled by Reeve Ingbert, rather of <a href="/wiki/Horn_(rider_of_Rohan)" title="Horn (rider of Rohan)">Horn</a>, from the town of &#34;Eaworth&#34;.</dd></dl>
