<i><b>Endor</b></i> or <i><b>Endórë</b></i> was a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for the region much better known as [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) form of this name was *[Ennor](/wiki/Ennor)*.<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Enedh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Enedh (page does not exist)">enedh</a></i> ("middle")<sup id="cite_ref-Etym_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etym-2">[2]</a></sup> + *[nóre](/wiki/N%C3%B3re "Nóre")* ("land")<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Roots](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=EN(ED)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="EN(ED) (page does not exist)">EN(ED)</a> ("centre, middle") + <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NDOR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NDOR (page does not exist)">NDOR</a> ("land")<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>
