<i><b>Explicando Tolkien</b></i> ("Explaining Tolkien") is a Brazilian book written by [Ronald Kyrmse](/wiki/Ronald_Kyrmse).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Tolkien was responsible for the birth of the literary genre of fantasy fiction. Many readers delve with delight in the works of the English author. A better appreciation of his books is only possible if we know more about his life, the motives that inspired him and the messages he wished to convey to us. In &#34;Explicando Tolkien&#34;, Ronald Kyrmse, who has been studying the author for nearly a quarter century, aims to illuminate many facets of this prodigious work, showing how Tolkien works with his sub-created world, as well as the richness of details and issues that make up myths, peoples, languages​​, calendars, etc.
</p>
</blockquote>
