**Ezellohar**, also called **Corollairë**, was the green mound in the midst of the [Ring of Doom](/wiki/M%C3%A1hanaxar "Máhanaxar"), upon which the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees) of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) once grew.

## History {#History}

Ezellohar was the name of a small hill that was located just outside the western gates of [Valimar](/wiki/Valimar), the city of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar). After [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) hallowed it, she sang a powerful song sitting upon the green grass before the [Máhanaxar](/wiki/M%C3%A1hanaxar). Of which, she thought of all the things that could grow from the mound. Afterwards, [Nienna](/wiki/Nienna) pondered in silence and watered Ezellohar with her tears. As the other [Valar](/wiki/Valar) watched, two slender shoots emerged from the mound when the song of Yavanna was the only sound that could be heard. The saplings quickly grew *"fair and tall"*, eventually becoming the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees) of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Later, [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) sat with Yavanna upon Ezellohar beneath the Two Trees, informing her of [Eru's](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar "Ilúvatar") judgment concerning [Ents](/wiki/Ents) and [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During a [high feast](/wiki/High_feast "High feast"), [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") and [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) came before Ezellohar. The [Unlight](/wiki/Unlight) of Ungoliant devoured the roots, while Melkor came onto the mound, slaying the Two Trees with his black spear. The sap of the Two Trees poured like blood upon Ezellohar where Ungoliant sucked it up, before draining the [Wells of Varda](/wiki/Wells_of_Varda) and covering the green mound in a black vapour.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

When the winds of Manwë had cleared away the black vapours, Yavanna came onto Ezellohar, only to find that it had become bare and black. After [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) refused to break the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils) to restore the Two Trees, [Nienna](/wiki/Nienna) came up onto Ezellohar, threw back her grey hood, and washed away the Ungoliant's defilements of the green mound, mourning the [Marring of Arda](/wiki/Arda_Marred "Arda Marred") with a bitter song.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><b>Ezellohar</b></i> is a [Vanyarin](/wiki/Vanyarin) name meaning "Green Mound", which contains the elements *ezel*, *ezella*, *ezello* ("green").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> *Ezellohar* was derived from the [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) name <i><b>Ezellôchâr</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

<i><b>Corollairë</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name meaning "Mound of Summer",<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> which appears to be a compressed version of *Coron Oiolairë* ("Mound Ever-summer"), from *[coron](/wiki/Coron "Coron")* ("mound") + *[oiolairë](/wiki/Oiolair%C3%AB "Oiolairë")* ("ever-summer", also the name of a species of tree).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The first mention of *Ezellohar* in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") is found within *"[The Lay of Leithian Recommenced](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Leithian_Recommenced)"*, where [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) called for the people of [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) to continue on to [Aman](/wiki/Aman) in order to be near the evergreen Ezellohar of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) in [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
