<i><b>ekka</b></i> means "hole" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

Petri Tikka has suggested the spelling *ecca*, to conform with the standard used in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
