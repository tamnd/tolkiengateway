**Elfwine the Fair** was the second [King of the Third Line of Rohan](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan"), ruling in the early [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).

## History {#History}

Elfwine was the son of King [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) of Rohan and [Lothíriel](/wiki/Loth%C3%ADriel) of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He was said to bear a striking likeness to his maternal grandfather, [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil), [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Elfwine* means "[Elf-friend](/wiki/Elf-friend)" in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> being a translation of the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name *[Elendil](/wiki/Elendil)*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Also, it is a reference to the earlier character of Tolkien's [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine) of England.
