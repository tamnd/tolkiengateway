The **Eastfold** was a region in the east of the realm of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), between the [Folde](/wiki/Folde) in the west and the [Fenmarch](/wiki/Fenmarch) in the east, the river Entwash in the north and the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) in the south.<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Eastfold was traversed by the [Great West Road](/wiki/Great_West_Road) parallel to the White Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras), which was located in the *Folde* was also the defensive centre of the Eastfold.<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup>

A similar name, the **East Dales**, are mentioned when it is told how the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) who had fallen in the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg) were buried in two mounds; "those of the [Westfold](/wiki/Westfold) in one and those from the East Dales in another."<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It has been suggested that the name is a synonym for Eastfold.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Eastfold was originally part of the realm of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) until in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510") the fief of [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) was granted to [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) the Young by the [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion). Thereafter the Eastfold was part of Rohan. Initially the guarding and maintenance of the hill [Halifirien](/wiki/Halifirien), which lay east of the [Mering Stream](/wiki/Mering_Stream) in Gondor, was shared by men from Gondor and men from Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-5">[5]</a></sup>

When the realm of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) declined and was under threat by the sea and from the East and the people of Rohan increased, the wardens of the hill [Halifirien](/wiki/Halifirien) were exclusively provided by the people of the Eastfold.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-5">[5]</a></sup>

On March 6 and 7<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) rode with [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) through the Eastfold on the road to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

On March 11 [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the riders of Rohan led by their King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) travelled with [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) through the Eastfold on their way to Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In early August [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> the funeral escort of King Théoden passed through the Eastfold on its way to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eastfold* is a name in the language of Rohan. It is a combination of "East" and *fold* from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *folde* ("earth", "land", "country").<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup>
