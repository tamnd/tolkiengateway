The **Eastfold** was a region in the east of the realm of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), between the [Folde](../F/Folde.md) in the west and the [Fenmarch](../F/Fenmarch.md) in the east, the river Entwash in the north and the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) in the south.<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Eastfold was traversed by the [Great West Road](../G/Great_West_Road.md) parallel to the White Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> [Edoras](Edoras.md), which was located in the *Folde* was also the defensive centre of the Eastfold.<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup>

A similar name, the **East Dales**, are mentioned when it is told how the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) who had fallen in the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md) were buried in two mounds; "those of the [Westfold](../W/Westfold.md) in one and those from the East Dales in another."<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It has been suggested that the name is a synonym for Eastfold.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Eastfold was originally part of the realm of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) until in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md) the fief of [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) was granted to [Eorl](Eorl.md) the Young by the [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md). Thereafter the Eastfold was part of Rohan. Initially the guarding and maintenance of the hill [Halifirien](../H/Halifirien.md), which lay east of the [Mering Stream](../M/Mering_Stream.md) in Gondor, was shared by men from Gondor and men from Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-5">[5]</a></sup>

When the realm of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) declined and was under threat by the sea and from the East and the people of Rohan increased, the wardens of the hill [Halifirien](../H/Halifirien.md) were exclusively provided by the people of the Eastfold.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-5">[5]</a></sup>

On March 6 and 7<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) rode with [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) through the Eastfold on the road to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

On March 11 [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the riders of Rohan led by their King [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) travelled with [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) through the Eastfold on their way to Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In early August [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> the funeral escort of King Théoden passed through the Eastfold on its way to [Edoras](Edoras.md).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eastfold* is a name in the language of Rohan. It is a combination of "East" and *fold* from [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *folde* ("earth", "land", "country").<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup>
