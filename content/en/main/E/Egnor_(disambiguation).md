**Egnor** can refer to:

- *Egnor bo-Rimion*, early name for **[Barahir](../B/Barahir.md)**, father of [Beren](../B/Beren.md).
- *Egnor*, early name for **[Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md)**, an [Elf](Elves.md) of the [Elder Days](Elder_Days.md).
