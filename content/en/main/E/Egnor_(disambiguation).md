**Egnor** can refer to:

- *Egnor bo-Rimion*, early name for **[Barahir](/wiki/Barahir)**, father of [Beren](/wiki/Beren).
- *Egnor*, early name for **[Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor)**, an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days).
