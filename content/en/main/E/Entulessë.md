**Entulessë** was a ship that was sailed by the great [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") mariner [Vëantur](/wiki/V%C3%ABantur), who was [Captain of the King's Ships](/wiki/Captain_of_the_King%27s_Ships) in the time of [Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil), [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor)'s fourth King. After nearly six hundred years, Entulessë was the first Númenórean vessel to make the journey back to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) across the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer").

According to the histories of Númenor, Vëantur sailed the ship into the harbour of the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) in the spring of [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [600](/wiki/Second_Age_600 "Second Age 600"). [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) was still King in [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) at that time, and Vëantur remained in his land for some months. As the following year turned to autumn, Entulessë set sail once again, beginning the long journey home to Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Its name means in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) 'The Return', referring to its famous voyage eastward to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). It incorporates *[en-](/wiki/En- "En-")* "again" and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=T%C3%BBl&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tûl (page does not exist)">Tûl</a></i> "come" as well as the noun ending *[-sse](/wiki/-sse)*. <span></span>
