**Entulessë** was a ship that was sailed by the great [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) mariner [Vëantur](../V/Vëantur.md), who was [Captain of the King's Ships](../C/Captain_of_the_King's_Ships.md) in the time of [Tar-Elendil](../T/Tar-Elendil.md), [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md)'s fourth King. After nearly six hundred years, Entulessë was the first Númenórean vessel to make the journey back to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) across the [Great Sea](../B/Belegaer.md).

According to the histories of Númenor, Vëantur sailed the ship into the harbour of the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) in the spring of [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [600](../S/Second_Age_600.md). [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) was still King in [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) at that time, and Vëantur remained in his land for some months. As the following year turned to autumn, Entulessë set sail once again, beginning the long journey home to Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Its name means in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) 'The Return', referring to its famous voyage eastward to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). It incorporates *[en-](En-.md)* "again" and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=T%C3%BBl&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tûl (page does not exist)">Tûl</a></i> "come" as well as the noun ending *[-sse](../_/-sse.md)*. <span></span>
