**Eldalië** is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) term that refers to the **[Elven-folk](/wiki/Elves "Elves")** in general, or just the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar), the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word can be translated as "people of the Eldar"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and is composed of *[Elda](/wiki/Elda)* + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lie&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lie (page does not exist)">lie</a></i> "people".<sup id="cite_ref-arda_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-arda-4">[4]</a></sup>
