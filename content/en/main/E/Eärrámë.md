**Eärrámë** was the great ship of [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) at the end of his life in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). After feeling the [Unquiet of Ulmo](/wiki/Sea-longing "Sea-longing"), he built the ship in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [525](/wiki/First_Age_525 "First Age 525"), and departed the same year with [Idril](/wiki/Idril) into the West. [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) probably went with them.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Later it was sung that they arrived in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), being Tuor the only [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") who became a member of the [Elder Kindred](/wiki/Firstborn "Firstborn").<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eärrámë* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Sea-Wing",<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-2">[2]</a></sup> from *[eär](/wiki/E%C3%A4r "Eär")* ("sea") + [ráma](/wiki/R%C3%A1ma "Ráma") ("wing").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the ship of Tuor is called <i><b>Alqarámë</b></i> ("Swanwing"), while Eärámë ("Eaglepinion") is the ship of [Eärendel](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil "Eärendil") (being [Wingilot](/wiki/Vingilot "Vingilot") his second ship).<sup id="cite_ref-Earendel_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendel-4">[4]</a></sup> [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) never finished the Lost Tale of Eärendel, so there are contradictory outlines: first Tuor sails alone in secret in Alqarámë, which is described as twilit and with purple sails;<sup id="cite_ref-Earendel_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendel-4">[4]</a></sup> but later it is given a more detailed outline of Tûr's depart:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>One evening he calls Eärendel and they go to the shore. There is a skiff. Tûr bids farewell to Eärendel and bids him thrust it off — the skiff fares away into the West. Eärendel hears a great song swelling from the sea as Tûr&#39;s skiff dips over the world&#39;s rim.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two" title="The Book of Lost Tales Part Two">The Book of Lost Tales Part Two</a></i>, &#34;<a href="/wiki/The_Tale_of_E%C3%A4rendel" title="The Tale of Eärendel">The Tale of Eärendel</a>&#34;, p. 260</cite></p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

Tolkien changed the story as it is in the published *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* in his revision of the *[Sketch of the Mythology](/wiki/The_Earliest_%27Silmarillion%27 "The Earliest 'Silmarillion'")*, in which Eärámë became the ship of Tuor instead of Eärendel's.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
