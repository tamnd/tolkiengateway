**Eärrámë** was the great ship of [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) at the end of his life in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). After feeling the [Unquiet of Ulmo](../S/Sea-longing.md), he built the ship in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [525](../F/First_Age_525.md), and departed the same year with [Idril](../I/Idril.md) into the West. [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md) probably went with them.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Later it was sung that they arrived in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), being Tuor the only [Man](../M/Men.md) who became a member of the [Elder Kindred](../F/Firstborn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Eärrámë* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "Sea-Wing",<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-2">[2]</a></sup> from *[eär](Eär.md)* ("sea") + [ráma](../R/Ráma.md) ("wing").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, the ship of Tuor is called <i><b>Alqarámë</b></i> ("Swanwing"), while Eärámë ("Eaglepinion") is the ship of [Eärendel](Eärendil.md) (being [Wingilot](../V/Vingilot.md) his second ship).<sup id="cite_ref-Earendel_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendel-4">[4]</a></sup> [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) never finished the Lost Tale of Eärendel, so there are contradictory outlines: first Tuor sails alone in secret in Alqarámë, which is described as twilit and with purple sails;<sup id="cite_ref-Earendel_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendel-4">[4]</a></sup> but later it is given a more detailed outline of Tûr's depart:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>One evening he calls Eärendel and they go to the shore. There is a skiff. Tûr bids farewell to Eärendel and bids him thrust it off — the skiff fares away into the West. Eärendel hears a great song swelling from the sea as Tûr&#39;s skiff dips over the world&#39;s rim.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two.md" title="The Book of Lost Tales Part Two">The Book of Lost Tales Part Two</a></i>, &#34;<a href="../T/The_Tale_of_Eärendel.md" title="The Tale of Eärendel">The Tale of Eärendel</a>&#34;, p. 260</cite></p>
</blockquote>

Tolkien changed the story as it is in the published *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* in his revision of the *[Sketch of the Mythology](../T/The_Earliest_'Silmarillion'.md)*, in which Eärámë became the ship of Tuor instead of Eärendel's.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
