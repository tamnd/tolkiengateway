**Entwives** and **Entmaidens** were female [Ents](/wiki/Ents).

## Description {#Description}

[Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) described the Entwives as "fair", especially in youth. Of older Entwives and Entmaidens he said the following:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>[T]he Entwives were bent and browned by their labour; their hair parched by the sun to the hue of ripe corn and their cheeks like red apples. Yet their eyes were still the eyes of our own people.
</p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

The Entwives were described as desiring order, plenty and peace.<sup id="cite_ref-TT476_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT476-1">[1]</a></sup> Treebeard said that they would have liked the lands of [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ralph_Damiani_-_Ancient_Knowledge.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/6/69/Ralph_Damiani_-_Ancient_Knowledge.jpg/250px-Ralph_Damiani_-_Ancient_Knowledge.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="156" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/6/69/Ralph_Damiani_-_Ancient_Knowledge.jpg/375px-Ralph_Damiani_-_Ancient_Knowledge.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/6/69/Ralph_Damiani_-_Ancient_Knowledge.jpg/500px-Ralph_Damiani_-_Ancient_Knowledge.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1920" data-file-height="1197"/></a><figcaption><i>Ancient Knowledge</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Ralph_Damiani" title="Category:Images by Ralph Damiani">Ralph Damiani</a></figcaption></figure>

The Entwives were created along with the male Ents by [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna), the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") responsible for all growing things. Unlike their husbands who were dedicated to [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), the Entwives were dedicated to Yavanna.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> They originally travelled with the Ents, but in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup> they settled down and created gardens because they liked to plant and control small things like vegetables, grass and flowers, while the male Ents tended the larger [trees](/wiki/Trees "Trees") of the forests. Unlike the Ents who wished to speak to the trees and let them grow however they'd like, the Entwives wanted to be obeyed by the plants for they desired order. The rest of the Ents kept on wandering, visiting the gardens occasionally.<sup id="cite_ref-TT476_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT476-1">[1]</a></sup> The Ents wished for the Entwives to return to them but they refused believing their land to be better.<sup id="cite_ref-TT477_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT477-5">[4]</a></sup> When [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) started growing his power in the North, the Entwives passed eastward over the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). After Morgoth was overthrown, their gardens blossomed and they taught agriculture to the primitive [Men](/wiki/Men) and were honoured by them.<sup id="cite_ref-TT476_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT476-1">[1]</a></sup>

To stop the advance of the [Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men "Last Alliance of Elves and Men") down the Anduin, [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) pursued a scorched earth policy.<sup id="cite_ref-L144_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L144-6">[5]</a></sup> The Entwives' lands were burned and uprooted and thereafter Men referred to the land as the Brown Lands.<sup id="cite_ref-TT476_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT476-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Search for the Entwives {#Search_for_the_Entwives}

Sometime during the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance), Treebeard desired to see his wife [Fimbrethil](/wiki/Fimbrethil) but when he reached their lands he found them marred by war and the Entwives no longer there. For a long time, the Ents searched frequently for the Entwives asking all creatures if they had seen them. Some had not seen them, others said they were seen going east, some said west and others south.<sup id="cite_ref-TT476_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT476-1">[1]</a></sup> Many songs were sung by [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and Men about the Ents' search for the Entwives. Over time their searches became more infrequent and they journeyed shorter distances until the Entwives were mere memory to most Ents.<sup id="cite_ref-TT476_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT476-1">[1]</a></sup> The loss of the Entwives had a large effect on the Ents as there were no new [Entings](/wiki/Entings),<sup id="cite_ref-TT475_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT475-7">[6]</a></sup> and survival of the Ents as a race was due only to their long lifespan but some Ents died and others became "treeish" until there were few Ents left.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

### Fate of the Entwives {#Fate_of_the_Entwives}

The Ents held that the Entwives were not dead but merely lost.<sup id="cite_ref-TT475_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT475-7">[6]</a></sup> They believed that they would one day find each other and hoped to go to a land where they could both be happy.<sup id="cite_ref-TT477_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT477-5">[4]</a></sup>

The Entwives may have been wiped out by Sauron during the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) but some could have fled east or become slaves of Sauron used for agriculture. If this were the case, any surviving Entwives would likely "be far estranged from the Ents, and any rapprochement would be difficult".<sup id="cite_ref-L144_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L144-6">[5]</a></sup>

Sometime before the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Halfast Gamgee](/wiki/Halfast_Gamgee) claimed that he saw an [elm](/wiki/Elms "Elms")-like "[Tree-man](/wiki/Tree-men "Tree-men")" walking beyond the [North Moors](/wiki/North_Moors).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> However, it was never learned whether the "Tree-man" was an Ent, an Entwife, "a [troll](/wiki/Trolls "Trolls")"<sup id="cite_ref-unpublished_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-unpublished-10">[9]</a></sup> of some kind, or just a story.
