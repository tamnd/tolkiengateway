<i><b>elen</b></i> (pl. **eleni** or **eldi**) means "star" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Edain](/wiki/Edain), however, equated *elen* and *[elda](/wiki/Elda "Elda")* ("[Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves")"), so in some situations *elen* translates as "Elf".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[PQ](/wiki/PQ) [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") [EL](/wiki/EL).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is said traditionally to come from the exclamation *ele*! ("behold"), being the first word the first Elves spoke at [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen), when they saw the stars.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

- *[elenna](/wiki/Elenna "Elenna")* = "to a star": allative, also a name for [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- *elelli* = "stars": partitive plural<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- *elenion* = "of stars": pl. genitive<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
- *elenillor* = "from stars": pl. ablative<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

*El* means "deity" in some Semitic languages, and is a common element in many [Hebrew](/wiki/Hebrew) names, as happens with [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) names. Dante Alighieri in his *De vulgari eloquentia* suggests that the word *El* was the first sound emitted by Adam: While the first utterance of humans after birth is a cry of pain, Dante assumed that Adam could only have made an exclamation of joy, which at the same time was addressing his Creator.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger) explains that "*neither Tolkien's* ele *nor its derivative* el*, functions in his protolanguage as a name of God. Nevertheless, both are names for the first [light](/wiki/Light "Light"), which suggests God's emanation. The similarity of the fictive first utterance of Tolkien's Elves to Dante's deduced first utterance should not go unremarked*".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
