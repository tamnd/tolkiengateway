<i><b>elen</b></i> (pl. **eleni** or **eldi**) means "star" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Edain](Edain.md), however, equated *elen* and *[elda](Elda.md)* ("[Elf](Elves.md)"), so in some situations *elen* translates as "Elf".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[PQ](../P/PQ.md) [Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) [EL](EL~31035.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is said traditionally to come from the exclamation *ele*! ("behold"), being the first word the first Elves spoke at [Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md), when they saw the stars.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

- *[elenna](Elenna.md)* = "to a star": allative, also a name for [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- *elelli* = "stars": partitive plural<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- *elenion* = "of stars": pl. genitive<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
- *elenillor* = "from stars": pl. ablative<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

*El* means "deity" in some Semitic languages, and is a common element in many [Hebrew](../H/Hebrew.md) names, as happens with [Elvish](Elvish.md) names. Dante Alighieri in his *De vulgari eloquentia* suggests that the word *El* was the first sound emitted by Adam: While the first utterance of humans after birth is a cry of pain, Dante assumed that Adam could only have made an exclamation of joy, which at the same time was addressing his Creator.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Verlyn Flieger](../V/Verlyn_Flieger.md) explains that "*neither Tolkien's* ele *nor its derivative* el*, functions in his protolanguage as a name of God. Nevertheless, both are names for the first [light](../L/Light.md), which suggests God's emanation. The similarity of the fictive first utterance of Tolkien's Elves to Dante's deduced first utterance should not go unremarked*".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
