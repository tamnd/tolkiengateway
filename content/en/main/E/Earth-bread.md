**Earth-bread** was an edible root known only to the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). It was white and fleshy and after boiling was good to eat and similar to bread. Its name in the [dwarf-tongue](/wiki/Khuzdul "Khuzdul") is unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-ch7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ch7-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. 134</sup>

When [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) and his [outlaw band](/wiki/Gaurwaith "Gaurwaith") encountered [Mîm](/wiki/M%C3%AEm) and his sons, the [Petty-dwarves](/wiki/Petty-dwarves) were carrying sacks of the plants,<sup id="cite_ref-ch7_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ch7-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. 123</sup> and possibly gold ingots disguised as roots.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Later, at the hideout of [Bar-en-Danwedh](/wiki/Bar-en-Danwedh), Mîm contributed his earth-bread to the food supply of the outlaws, although he refused to share with [Ulrad](/wiki/Ulrad), who had insulted him.<sup id="cite_ref-ch7_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ch7-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:pp. 134–135</sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor" title="Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor">Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>“Earthbread” was a rare starchy root, which was known to the Rangers of the Black Gate. When properly washed, peeled, and cooked with salt, it yielded a flavour resembling fine milled bread.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>
