The **Evil Breath** was "an evil wind out of Angband" that brought a sickness to [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) under a leaden sky.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Childhood_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Childhood-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the autumn of [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [469](/wiki/First_Age_469 "First Age 469"), many had hope that [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) would turn back [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), sealing the [Doors of Angband](/wiki/Gates_of_Angband "Gates of Angband"). Yet those that were wiser feared that new evils will be devised in Angband. Their fears were true, as "an ill wind from the [North](/wiki/Northlands "Northlands")", known only as the Evil Breath, brought a pestilence to those lands that bordered [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith). Many of those that perished from the sickness were the rising youth and children.<sup id="cite_ref-Childhood_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Childhood-2">[2]</a></sup>

Before the ending of that year, the Evil Breath had reached [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min), striking [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) with a fever and dark dreams. Due to his fate and strength, he survived the sickness. His sister, [Urwen](/wiki/Urwen), was not spared however, and Túrin wept bitterly alone that night, grieving for her.<sup id="cite_ref-Childhood_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Childhood-2">[2]</a></sup>

Later, Túrin mentions the Evil Breath during a conversation he has with [Sador](/wiki/Sador), considering it to have been a curse by the [wicked King](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth").<sup id="cite_ref-Childhood_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Childhood-2">[2]</a></sup>
