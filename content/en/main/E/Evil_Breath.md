The **Evil Breath** was "an evil wind out of Angband" that brought a sickness to [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) under a leaden sky.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Childhood_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Childhood-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the autumn of [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [469](../F/First_Age_469.md), many had hope that [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) would turn back [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), sealing the [Doors of Angband](../G/Gates_of_Angband.md). Yet those that were wiser feared that new evils will be devised in Angband. Their fears were true, as "an ill wind from the [North](../N/Northlands.md)", known only as the Evil Breath, brought a pestilence to those lands that bordered [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md). Many of those that perished from the sickness were the rising youth and children.<sup id="cite_ref-Childhood_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Childhood-2">[2]</a></sup>

Before the ending of that year, the Evil Breath had reached [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md), striking [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) with a fever and dark dreams. Due to his fate and strength, he survived the sickness. His sister, [Urwen](../U/Urwen.md), was not spared however, and Túrin wept bitterly alone that night, grieving for her.<sup id="cite_ref-Childhood_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Childhood-2">[2]</a></sup>

Later, Túrin mentions the Evil Breath during a conversation he has with [Sador](../S/Sador.md), considering it to have been a curse by the [wicked King](../M/Morgoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Childhood_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Childhood-2">[2]</a></sup>
