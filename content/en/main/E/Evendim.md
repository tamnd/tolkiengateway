**Evendim** was a [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) name to refer to the dusk of the evening known to the Elves as *[aduial](/wiki/Aduial "Aduial")* or *[undómë](/wiki/Und%C3%B3m%C3%AB "Undómë")*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is composed by an element for "evening", and *dim* which refers to dusk/gloaming.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
