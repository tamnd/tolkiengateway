**Enerdhil** was a legendary [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) jewel-smith of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) who was responsible for the making of the original [Elessar](Elfstone.md).

## History {#History}

After the death of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md), Enerdhil was considered to be the greatest of the craftsman of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md). Despite this, he took great pleasure in seeing the sunlight between the leaves of trees, as he loved "green things that grew". One day, a desire filled his heart and he sought to make a jewel as "green as leaves" to imprison the "clear light of the sun". After Enerdhil's completion of the jewel, many [Elves](Elves.md), including the other Noldor, were astonished by it. For it was said that any who looked upon the stone would see marred and broken things as whole and healed again. The jewel came to be known as the Elessar and was soon gifted by Enerdhil to [Idril Celebrindal](../I/Idril.md), who wore it upon her breast and as such, it was saved from perishing during the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md). Eventually, Idril passed it on to her son [Eärendil](Eärendil.md), who bore it on his voyages across the [Great Sea](../B/Belegaer.md) to [Aman](../A/Aman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elessar_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elessar-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:249</sup>

According to a legend in which the Elessar was brought back by [Olórin](../G/Gandalf.md), Enerdhil's name was mentioned in passing by [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md), asking about the whereabouts of the Elessar and of Enerdhil "who made it". In response, Olórin claims he does not know of his fate, but reveals that he had brought the Elessar back.<sup id="cite_ref-Elessar_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elessar-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:250</sup>

However, according to another legend, the original Elessar stayed beyond the sea and [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md), at [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md)'s behest, made a second less powerful version of the lost jewel during the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elessar_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elessar-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:251</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) suggests that the name is a loan from [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../A/Anardil.md" title="Anardil">Anardil</a></i> ("Sun-friend"). This [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) form of the name displays what he calls "hyper-[Sindarization](../S/Sindarization.md)", with [i-affection](../I/I-affection.md) before the ending *[-ndil](../_/-ndil.md)* whereas it shouldn't occur in "proper" Sindarin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a final note, Enerdhil was replaced entirely, with both the first and the second Elessar being made by Celebrimbor.<sup id="cite_ref-Elessar_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elessar-1">[1]</a></sup>

The fact that there are two possible Elessars makes Enerdhil fit into the mythos, as the first one could have been made by him and [the second](Elfstone.md) by Celebrimbor, yet not even this has been resolved clearly.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
