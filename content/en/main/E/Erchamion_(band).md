**Erchamion** was a black metal band from Chicago, Illinois.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its name derives from Erchamion ("the One-handed"), an epithet of [Beren](../B/Beren.md) given after [Carcharoth](../C/Carcharoth.md) devoured his hand. Its 2017 debut demo is named after [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md), king of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md).

## Discography {#Discography}

- Thranduil (Demo, 2017)
    1. Orophin (instrumental)
    2. Dagorlad
    3. Thorin
    4. North Mirkwood
- Madness of Trahald (Demo, 2019)
    1. Riddle (instrumental)
    2. The Cave
    3. Dungeon of Barad-dur
    4. Gollum
- Ritualul Antic Al Thuringwethilului (Split, 2019; with Dieseldörf)
    1. The Orc That Marches Alone
    2. Istari
    3. Goblin in Despair
