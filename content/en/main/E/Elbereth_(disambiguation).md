**Elbereth** (pronounced <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈelbereθ]</span>) is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. It can refer to:

- **[Elbereth Gilthoniel](../V/Varda.md)**, the Sindarin name for **Varda, Queen of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md)**.
    - [Snow-white! Snow-white! O Lady clear!](../S/Snow-white!_Snow-white!_O_Lady_clear!.md), a song sung by [Gildor Inglorion](../G/Gildor.md) in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.
    - [A Elbereth Gilthoniel](../A/A_Elbereth_Gilthoniel.md), a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) poem in both *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* and *[The Road Goes Ever On](<../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book).md>)*.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Eluréd](Eluréd.md), son of [Dior Eluchíl](../D/Dior.md), originally referred to as *Elbereth*, an [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md) name containing the word *bereth* "valor". [Elrohir](Elrohir.md), son of [Elrond](Elrond.md), was occasionally referred to as *Elbereth* in earlier writings. The name *Elbereth* was later fixed to Varda while Tolkien was writing *[Three is Company](../T/Three_is_Company.md)* (in the original Elven Hymn, Varda was until then called *Elberil*).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other works {#Other_works}

- [Ilbereth](../I/Ilbereth.md), an elf, the secretary of [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) in *[Letters from Father Christmas](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md)*.
