**Elbereth** (pronounced <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈelbereθ]</span>) is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. It can refer to:

- **[Elbereth Gilthoniel](/wiki/Varda "Varda")**, the Sindarin name for **Varda, Queen of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar)**.
    - [Snow-white! Snow-white! O Lady clear!](/wiki/Snow-white!_Snow-white!_O_Lady_clear!), a song sung by [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor "Gildor") in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.
    - [A Elbereth Gilthoniel](/wiki/A_Elbereth_Gilthoniel), a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) poem in both *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and *[The Road Goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")*.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Eluréd](/wiki/Elur%C3%A9d), son of [Dior Eluchíl](/wiki/Dior "Dior"), originally referred to as *Elbereth*, an [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin) name containing the word *bereth* "valor". [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir), son of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), was occasionally referred to as *Elbereth* in earlier writings. The name *Elbereth* was later fixed to Varda while Tolkien was writing *[Three is Company](/wiki/Three_is_Company)* (in the original Elven Hymn, Varda was until then called *Elberil*).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other works {#Other_works}

- [Ilbereth](/wiki/Ilbereth), an elf, the secretary of [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) in *[Letters from Father Christmas](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas)*.
