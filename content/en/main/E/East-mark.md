The **East-mark** was one of the three military divisions of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), with the others being the [West-mark](/wiki/West-mark), and the King's Lands around [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras). The East-mark was defined as those lands that lay east of the [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash) and [Snowbourn](/wiki/Snowbourn) rivers. In military matters, the command of the [Men](/wiki/Men) of the East-mark would be given to one of Rohan's Marshals of the Riddermark. At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) this was the [Third Marshal](/wiki/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark "Third Marshal of the Riddermark"), the King's nephew [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer), who held his command at the ancient township of [Aldburg](/wiki/Aldburg), the original capital of Rohan and the seat of his family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
