The **East-mark** was one of the three military divisions of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), with the others being the [West-mark](../W/West-mark.md), and the King's Lands around [Edoras](Edoras.md). The East-mark was defined as those lands that lay east of the [Entwash](Entwash.md) and [Snowbourn](../S/Snowbourn.md) rivers. In military matters, the command of the [Men](../M/Men.md) of the East-mark would be given to one of Rohan's Marshals of the Riddermark. At the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) this was the [Third Marshal](../T/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark.md), the King's nephew [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md), who held his command at the ancient township of [Aldburg](../A/Aldburg.md), the original capital of Rohan and the seat of his family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
