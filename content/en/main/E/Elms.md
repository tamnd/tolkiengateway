**Elms** were tall trees that grew throughout the northern regions of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) especially noted the elm-woods of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand), to the east of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)'s cousin [Halfast Gamgee](/wiki/Halfast_Gamgee) said he saw a [Tree-man](/wiki/Tree-men "Tree-men") or [giant](/wiki/Giants "Giants") in the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs) he said it was "as big as an elm tree", although no elms were growing there.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
