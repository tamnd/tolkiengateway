**Elms** were tall trees that grew throughout the northern regions of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md) especially noted the elm-woods of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md), to the east of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)'s cousin [Halfast Gamgee](../H/Halfast_Gamgee.md) said he saw a [Tree-man](../T/Tree-men.md) or [giant](../G/Giants.md) in the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md) he said it was "as big as an elm tree", although no elms were growing there.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
