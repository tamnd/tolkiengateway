The **East Wall of Rohan** was a sheer cliff on the western edge of the hills of the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) where they met the grasslands of the [Eastemnet](/wiki/Eastemnet) on the east side of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Riders_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-2">[2]</a></sup>

The line of the hills ended abruptly in a steep cliff-face,<sup id="cite_ref-Riders_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-2">[2]</a></sup> forming one of the borders of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The cliff was not impassable: a traveller in the hills could pass down onto the plain using a ravine that cut into the rock.<sup id="cite_ref-Riders_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-2">[2]</a></sup>

This was the route used during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) by the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) that captured [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> on [26 February](/wiki/26_February) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and by the [Three Hunters](/wiki/Three_Hunters) who followed them<sup id="cite_ref-Riders_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-2">[2]</a></sup> at dawn the next day.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
