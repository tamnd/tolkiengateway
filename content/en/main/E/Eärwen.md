**Eärwen**, known as the "swan-maiden of [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB)", was the daughter of [Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB), lord of the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1280](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1280 "Year of the Trees 1280"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> she married [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin), the youngest prince of the [House of Finwë](/wiki/House_of_Finw%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup> She became the mother of [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod), [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod), [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor), and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup> She was said to be close friends with [Anairë](/wiki/Anair%C3%AB), the wife of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), her husband's older brother.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-6">[4]</a></sup>

Her children, being also in part [Sea-elves](/wiki/Sea-elves), had a love of the sea and a longing for distant places; and it was in part due to this inheritance that all four of Eärwen's children chose to return to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), [exiling](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor") themselves from [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor),<sup id="cite_ref-Teleri_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Teleri-7">[5]</a></sup> although Eärwen herself stayed in [Aman](/wiki/Aman).

Because of their [Telerin](/wiki/Teleri "Teleri") blood, Eärwen's children were well received in [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), the realm of her father's elder brother, [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol).<sup id="cite_ref-Index_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-8">[6]</a></sup>

*Eärwen* is clearly [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). As [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack) explains, it probably means "Sea-maiden", being a compound of *[eär](/wiki/E%C3%A4r "Eär")* ("sea") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Wende&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Wende (page does not exist)">-wen</a></i> ("maiden").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup>
