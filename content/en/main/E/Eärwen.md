**Eärwen**, known as the "swan-maiden of [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md)", was the daughter of [Olwë](../O/Olwë.md), lord of the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1280](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1280.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> she married [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md), the youngest prince of the [House of Finwë](../H/House_of_Finwë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup> She became the mother of [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md), [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md), [Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md), and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup> She was said to be close friends with [Anairë](../A/Anairë.md), the wife of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md), her husband's older brother.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-6">[4]</a></sup>

Her children, being also in part [Sea-elves](../S/Sea-elves.md), had a love of the sea and a longing for distant places; and it was in part due to this inheritance that all four of Eärwen's children chose to return to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), [exiling](Exile_of_the_Noldor.md) themselves from [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Teleri_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Teleri-7">[5]</a></sup> although Eärwen herself stayed in [Aman](../A/Aman.md).

Because of their [Telerin](../T/Teleri.md) blood, Eärwen's children were well received in [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), the realm of her father's elder brother, [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Index_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-8">[6]</a></sup>

*Eärwen* is clearly [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). As [Paul Strack](../P/Paul_Strack.md) explains, it probably means "Sea-maiden", being a compound of *[eär](Eär.md)* ("sea") + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Wende&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Wende (page does not exist)">-wen</a></i> ("maiden").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup>
