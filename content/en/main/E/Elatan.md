**Elatan** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈɛlatan]</span>) was a nobleman who lived in the city of [Andúnië](../A/Andúnië.md) during the early centuries of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). He wedded [Silmariën](../S/Silmariën.md), the daughter and eldest child of [Tar-Elendil](../T/Tar-Elendil.md), the fourth [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md). Because of the law of <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnatic_primogeniture" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">agnatic primogeniture</a>, Silmariën could not take the [Sceptre](../S/Sceptre.md). However, when Elatan and Silmariën had their first child, [Valandil](<../V/Valandil_(Lord_of_Andúnië).md>), Tar-Elendil created the title [Lord of Andúnië](../L/Lords_of_Andúnië.md) to celebrate his first grandson. Valandil became the first Lord of Andúnië and Elatan's descendants would be titled noblemen.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Elatan's descendants would figure prominently in the history of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). After the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md), [Elendil](Elendil.md), the son of the [last Lord of Andúnië](../A/Amandil.md), would found the Kingdoms of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md). Over thousands of years, Elatan would become the ancestor of [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), the hero of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Elatan* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Star Man" and is a compound of [el](Elen.md) ("star") and [Atan](../A/Atani.md) ("Man").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
