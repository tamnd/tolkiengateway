**Elatan** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈɛlatan]</span>) was a nobleman who lived in the city of [Andúnië](/wiki/And%C3%BAni%C3%AB) during the early centuries of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). He wedded [Silmariën](/wiki/Silmari%C3%ABn), the daughter and eldest child of [Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil), the fourth [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor). Because of the law of <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnatic_primogeniture" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">agnatic primogeniture</a>, Silmariën could not take the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre). However, when Elatan and Silmariën had their first child, [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)> "Valandil (Lord of Andúnië)"), Tar-Elendil created the title [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië") to celebrate his first grandson. Valandil became the first Lord of Andúnië and Elatan's descendants would be titled noblemen.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Elatan's descendants would figure prominently in the history of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). After the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor), [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), the son of the [last Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Amandil "Amandil"), would found the Kingdoms of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). Over thousands of years, Elatan would become the ancestor of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), the hero of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Elatan* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Star Man" and is a compound of [el](/wiki/Elen "Elen") ("star") and [Atan](/wiki/Atani "Atani") ("Man").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
