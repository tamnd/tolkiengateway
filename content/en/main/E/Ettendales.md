The **Ettendales** were the valleys (<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_(landform)" title="wikipedia:Dale (landform)">dales</a>) of the [Ettenmoors](Ettenmoors.md) that extended up into the foothills of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> They were located far north of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-2">[2]</a></sup> on the western side of the Misty Mountains near the source of the river [Hoarwell](../H/Hoarwell.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and were inhabited by [Trolls](../T/Trolls.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-2">[2]</a></sup>.

## History {#History}

In the morning of [17 October](../_/17_October.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> in [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md) in their camp on a stony shelf with a rock-wall behind them [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) told [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) that they had went too far to the north and needed to find a way back to the south. He mentioned that if they continued in the same direction, they would get up into the Ettendales far north of Rivendell. Strider said that the Ettendales were troll-country and little-known to him.<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ettendales* is a [Common Speech](../W/Westron.md) name. It contains the obsolete element <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/ettin" title="wiktionary:ettin">eten</a></i> ('troll, [ogre](../O/Ogres.md)')" from [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *eoten*, which exists in English dialect as *eten*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The second element is *dale* from Old English *dael* ("valley").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

For more, see [Etten#Etymology](Etten.md#Etymology).
