**Elven-cloaks** were given to all nine members of the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md), woven for them by [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) herself and her maidens. Each cloak had a hood, was fitted to its wearer, and was fastened by a green [Elven-brooch](Elven_brooches.md). Their colour was hard to define – grey in twilight but green when moved or brown as fields or dusk-silver in the night. While they could not deflect a shaft or blade they acted as camouflage against unfriendly eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [16 February](../_/16_February.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) as the Fellowship prepared to depart [Lórien](../L/Lothlórien.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) brought the cloaks to the travellers. [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) asked if they were "magic cloaks". The term confused the Elves but they explained their properties, which seemed magical enough – they would hide the wearers, they were light, and warm or cool as needed.<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the Fellowship were attacked at [Sarn Gebir](../S/Sarn_Gebir.md) on [23 February](../_/23_February.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> the grey cloaks defeated the aim of the archers of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md)'s cloak was folded and placed beneath his head in his funeral boat<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> on [26 February](../_/26_February.md). The Elven cloaks hid their wearers on [29 February](../_/29_February.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> when [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and Pippin went unseen as two horsemen and their horses jumped over them just after [Grishnákh](../G/Grishnákh.md) was killed.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> On [30 February](../_/30_February.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> when the [Three Hunters](../T/Three_Hunters.md) were sitting and watching the riders of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) pass by, they were not perceived by [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) or his men, probably due to the Elven-cloaks.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the hall of King [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md), [Gríma](../G/Gríma.md) sneered at the "three ragged wanderers in grey". [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) explained that their raiment was grey for the [Elves](Elves.md) had clad them. Gríma then proclaimed that they were in league with the Sorceress of the Golden Wood (Galadriel), which nearly caused Gimli to attack him.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

On [29 February](../_/29_February.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> after they had descended a cliff by [rope](Elven_rope~74291.md) in the [Emyn Muil](Emyn_Muil.md), [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) thought that [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) might not be able to see them with their Elven cloaks on, but that he could smell and hear them.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

The [Mouth of Sauron](../M/Mouth_of_Sauron.md) displayed Frodo's Elven cloak and brooch to the parley group at the [Morannon](../M/Morannon.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-9">[9]</a></sup> on [25 March](../_/25_March.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> At the end of the parley Gandalf seized the tokens.<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b><a href="../_/2001.md" title="2001">2001</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the movie, in Scene 41, the members of the Fellowship receive their Elven cloaks and brooches in Lórien.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2002.md" title="2002">2002</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the movie, in Scene 18, Sam slides down the rock-strewn slope before the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../M/Morannon.md" title="Morannon">Gates of Mordor</a>. Frodo races down while two <a href="../H/Haradrim.md" title="Haradrim">Southron</a> soldiers, seeing debris falling down the hill, approach the spot where Sam is half buried.  Frodo reaches Sam, cannot free him before the soldiers arrive, and casts his Elven cloak over the two of them.  We see the two soldiers gazing about, perplexed.  When the camera scans downward a large rock is viewed before them, which is Frodo&#39;s Elven cloak perfectly concealing him and Sam.  After they leave the cloak is thrown aside and Frodo pulls Sam free.</dd></dl>
