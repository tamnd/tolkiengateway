The **downs** was a range of hills in [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) in the north of the [Eastemnet](Eastemnet.md). They were located south-east of [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md), south-west of [The Wold](../T/The_Wold.md) and north-west of the [Emyn Muil](Emyn_Muil.md) and the river [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md). A ten miles wide long strip of sunken land lay between them and the river [Entwash](Entwash.md) to the west. The downs ran in a line straight towards the north for eight leagues. They had green slopes that rose to bare ridges. The most northerly of the downs was a smooth and bare round green hill.<sup id="cite_ref-Riders_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [29 February](../_/29_February.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-30February3019_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30February3019-3">[3]</a></sup> [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) reached the downs and spent the night on the top of the northernmost down. On [30 February](../_/30_February.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-30February3019_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30February3019-3">[3]</a></sup> they spotted riders of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) to the north-west and walked down the northward slope to wait for the riders. They met [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) and a company of riders from [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) at the foot of the down.<sup id="cite_ref-Riders_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The downs are included on the [First Map of The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md), the [Second Map of The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Second_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) and the [Third Map of The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Third_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md), that were drawn by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), in the location that is described in the narrative. They do not have a name on the first<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and the second map<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and are simply labelled Downs on the third map.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Tolkien uses the capitalized term **Downs** in notes he made regarding what happened on [27 February](../_/27_February.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The downs are not included on the [General Map of Middle-earth](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md) and on [The West of Middle-earth at the End of the Third Age](../T/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age.md) map that were drawn by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) and published with *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* or *[Unfinished Tales of Númenor and Middle-earth](../U/Unfinished_Tales_of_Númenor_and_Middle-earth.md)*.
