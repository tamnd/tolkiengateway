The **downs** was a range of hills in [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) in the north of the [Eastemnet](/wiki/Eastemnet). They were located south-east of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest), south-west of [The Wold](/wiki/The_Wold) and north-west of the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) and the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). A ten miles wide long strip of sunken land lay between them and the river [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash) to the west. The downs ran in a line straight towards the north for eight leagues. They had green slopes that rose to bare ridges. The most northerly of the downs was a smooth and bare round green hill.<sup id="cite_ref-Riders_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [29 February](/wiki/29_February) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-30February3019_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30February3019-3">[3]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) reached the downs and spent the night on the top of the northernmost down. On [30 February](/wiki/30_February) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-30February3019_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30February3019-3">[3]</a></sup> they spotted riders of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) to the north-west and walked down the northward slope to wait for the riders. They met [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) and a company of riders from [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) at the foot of the down.<sup id="cite_ref-Riders_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The downs are included on the [First Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The First Map of The Lord of the Rings"), the [Second Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Second_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Second Map of The Lord of the Rings") and the [Third Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Third_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Third Map of The Lord of the Rings"), that were drawn by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), in the location that is described in the narrative. They do not have a name on the first<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and the second map<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and are simply labelled Downs on the third map.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Tolkien uses the capitalized term **Downs** in notes he made regarding what happened on [27 February](/wiki/27_February) in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The downs are not included on the [General Map of Middle-earth](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth) and on [The West of Middle-earth at the End of the Third Age](/wiki/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age) map that were drawn by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) and published with *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* or *[Unfinished Tales of Númenor and Middle-earth](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales_of_N%C3%BAmenor_and_Middle-earth)*.
