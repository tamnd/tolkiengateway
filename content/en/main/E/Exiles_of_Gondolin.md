The **Exiles of Gondolin** were survivors of the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin).

## History {#History}

During the confusion of the sack of the city, [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and [Idril](/wiki/Idril) gathered all the remnants of the [Gondolindrim](/wiki/Gondolindrim) they could find. They escaped through [Idril's secret way](/wiki/Idril%27s_secret_way), a tunnel that led to [Tumladen](/wiki/Tumladen). Thanks to the burning of the fountains and the city of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), they were covered in fume and mists, so they could cross the plain unnoticed.

The group ran to [Cirith Thoronath](/wiki/Cirith_Thoronath), where [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) killed the Balrog, saving them. Then they passed over the mountains, reaching the [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) river, which they followed south. After many dangers, they came to [Nan-tathren](/wiki/Nan-tathren), and they could rest there for a while, healing their wounds, but not their sorrow.

Finally, they continued their way to the [Sea](/wiki/Sea), finding refuge in the [Havens of Sirion](/wiki/Havens_of_Sirion), along with the people of [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [Lost Tale](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales "The Book of Lost Tales") of "[The Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")", the escape and wanderings of the Exiles is told with more detail than any other version. Those who escaped the city and reached the [Eagles' Cleft](/wiki/Eagles%27_Cleft) were counted as a nigh eight hundreds. But in their wanderings suffered attacks and diseases, so when they reach the Land of Willows years later they were less: including children, three hundred and twenty men, and two hundred sixty women. When they moved to the mouths of Sirion, they took the name of <i><b>Lothlim</b></i> ("People of the Flower"), as the name Gondothlim saddened their hearts.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
