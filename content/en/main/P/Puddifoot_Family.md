The **Puddifoots** were a family of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits). They were to be found in [Stock](/wiki/Stock) of the [Marish](/wiki/Marish). Unlike most Hobbits, who dwelled in [hobbit-holes](/wiki/Hobbit-hole "Hobbit-hole"), and like most folk of the Marish, the Puddifoots were house-dwellers.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name comes from "puddle-foot" and is connected to the soft soil of the [Marish](/wiki/Marish).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
