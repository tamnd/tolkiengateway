Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>.</div></div>

To flesh out the setting, [The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game.md) introduces many new types of plants and herbs that were used for various purposes, beyond [reeds](../R/Reeds.md).

## Aelgus {#Aelgus}

<i><b>Aelgus</b></i> was a small, green, leafy fern that grew in thick patches throughout [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md). Once cultivated by the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) like [athelas](../A/Athelas.md), it was later considered a nuisance by farmers and shepherds. Properly prepared and eaten, it induced vomiting that purged venom and neutralized poisons.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Eater’s Leaf {#Eater’s_Leaf}

<i><b>Eater’s Leaf</b></i> was an herb found in the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md) and valued in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), especially by the [Brandybucks](../B/Brandybuck_family.md). It fortified the digestive system, allowing heavy consumption of food and drink without ill effects, and granted resistance against poisons.<sup id="cite_ref-secondpage_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-secondpage-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Equial {#Equial}

<i><b>Equial</b></i> ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) *sídhroch*, “horse-rest”) was a hardy weed found on the eastern slopes of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md). Used by the Rohirrim, it served as a mild anaesthetic for calming horses and other mounts. Frequent use, however, could prove deadly.<sup id="cite_ref-secondpage_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-secondpage-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Fresh Dew {#Fresh_Dew}

<i><b>Fresh Dew</b></i> ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) *amrúnan*, “sunrise-gift”) was a small, leafy plant growing high in the Misty Mountains. Crushed, it produced a pungent odour that revived the weary. It could rouse the stunned or even restore those collapsed from exhaustion.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Gwathlas {#Gwathlas}

<i><b>Gwathlas</b></i> ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) “shadow-leaf”) was a leafy plant with a thick root, first cultivated by the [Black Númenóreans](../B/Black_Númenóreans.md) under [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)’s tutelage. Consuming the oil from its compressed roots sharpened the mind, allowing sorcerers to study dark lore more efficiently. In [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), its use was outlawed and punishable by death.<sup id="cite_ref-thirdpage_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-thirdpage-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Naurant {#Naurant}

<i><b>Naurant</b></i> ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) “red-vein”) was a fern with crimson veins, found east of the Sirich River. Gondorian healers employed it as a sedative. <sup id="cite_ref-thirdpage_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-thirdpage-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Soldier’s Root {#Soldier’s_Root}

<i><b>Soldier’s Root</b></i> ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) *dagorthond*, “battle-root”) was a rare plant with an orange, pulpy root, found along the [Loudwater](../B/Bruinen.md) near [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md). It granted great strength but carried the dangerous side-effect of battle-fury, often leading to excessive violence. This led to Elves purging the plant on sights.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
