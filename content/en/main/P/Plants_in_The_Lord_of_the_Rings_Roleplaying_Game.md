To flesh out the setting, [The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game) introduces many new types of plants and herbs that were used for various purposes, beyond [reeds](/wiki/Reeds "Reeds").

## Aelgus {#Aelgus}

<i><b>Aelgus</b></i> was a small, green, leafy fern that grew in thick patches throughout [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor). Once cultivated by the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) like [athelas](/wiki/Athelas "Athelas"), it was later considered a nuisance by farmers and shepherds. Properly prepared and eaten, it induced vomiting that purged venom and neutralized poisons.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Eater’s Leaf {#Eater’s_Leaf}

<i><b>Eater’s Leaf</b></i> was an herb found in the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest) and valued in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), especially by the [Brandybucks](/wiki/Brandybuck_family "Brandybuck family"). It fortified the digestive system, allowing heavy consumption of food and drink without ill effects, and granted resistance against poisons.<sup id="cite_ref-secondpage_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-secondpage-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Equial {#Equial}

<i><b>Equial</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *sídhroch*, “horse-rest”) was a hardy weed found on the eastern slopes of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). Used by the Rohirrim, it served as a mild anaesthetic for calming horses and other mounts. Frequent use, however, could prove deadly.<sup id="cite_ref-secondpage_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-secondpage-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Fresh Dew {#Fresh_Dew}

<i><b>Fresh Dew</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *amrúnan*, “sunrise-gift”) was a small, leafy plant growing high in the Misty Mountains. Crushed, it produced a pungent odour that revived the weary. It could rouse the stunned or even restore those collapsed from exhaustion.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Gwathlas {#Gwathlas}

<i><b>Gwathlas</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “shadow-leaf”) was a leafy plant with a thick root, first cultivated by the [Black Númenóreans](/wiki/Black_N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) under [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)’s tutelage. Consuming the oil from its compressed roots sharpened the mind, allowing sorcerers to study dark lore more efficiently. In [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), its use was outlawed and punishable by death.<sup id="cite_ref-thirdpage_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-thirdpage-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Naurant {#Naurant}

<i><b>Naurant</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “red-vein”) was a fern with crimson veins, found east of the Sirich River. Gondorian healers employed it as a sedative. <sup id="cite_ref-thirdpage_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-thirdpage-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Soldier’s Root {#Soldier’s_Root}

<i><b>Soldier’s Root</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *dagorthond*, “battle-root”) was a rare plant with an orange, pulpy root, found along the [Loudwater](/wiki/Bruinen "Bruinen") near [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell). It granted great strength but carried the dangerous side-effect of battle-fury, often leading to excessive violence. This led to Elves purging the plant on sights.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
