**Peter Roe** (c. [1963](../_/1963.md)-[1979](../_/1979.md)) was a British Tolkien fan. Introduced to [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) at the age of 16, Roe began making his own poems, maps and writing systems, but he died in a traffic accident before his work was done. His unfinished work, the *Dwarvish Chronicles*, was later published by [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) in [*Mallorn* 15](../M/Mallorn_15.md).

A short time later [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) created the [Peter Roe Memorial Fund](Peter_Roe_Memorial_Fund.md).
