**Peter Roe** (c. [1963](/wiki/1963)-[1979](/wiki/1979)) was a British Tolkien fan. Introduced to [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") at the age of 16, Roe began making his own poems, maps and writing systems, but he died in a traffic accident before his work was done. His unfinished work, the *Dwarvish Chronicles*, was later published by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) in [*Mallorn* 15](/wiki/Mallorn_15 "Mallorn 15").

A short time later [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) created the [Peter Roe Memorial Fund](/wiki/Peter_Roe_Memorial_Fund).
