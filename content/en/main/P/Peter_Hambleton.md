**Peter Hambleton** is a New Zealand actor who portrayed [Glóin](../G/Glóin.md), one of the thirteen [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) in [Thorin and his Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md), in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>). He also performed the motion capture and voice work of [William](../W/William.md) the [Troll](../T/Troll.md) in [The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
