**Peth** is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for "word".<sup id="cite_ref-Etymology_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etymology-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Possible [Old Sindarin](/wiki/Old_Sindarin) \**pettha* ([Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") [KWETE](/wiki/KWETE))<sup id="cite_ref-Etymology_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etymology-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Malbeth](/wiki/Malbeth)*, where perhaps is used in the agentive sense, "speaker".
- *[athrabeth](/wiki/Athrabeth "Athrabeth")*
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lasta-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lasta- (page does not exist)">lasto</a> <b>beth</b> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lam&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lam (page does not exist)">lammen</a></i> "Hear the **word** of my tongue"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}
