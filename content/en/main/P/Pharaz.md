<i><b>pharaz</b></i> is an [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) word meaning "[gold](/wiki/Gold "Gold")".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n#Etymology)*

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The similarity with the [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) word *[baraz](/wiki/Baraz "Baraz")* "red" has been noted; possibly *pharaz* represents an ancient [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) loanword from the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
