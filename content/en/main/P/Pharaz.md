<i><b>pharaz</b></i> is an [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) word meaning "[gold](../G/Gold.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md#Etymology)*

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The similarity with the [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md) word *[baraz](../B/Baraz.md)* "red" has been noted; possibly *pharaz* represents an ancient [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) loanword from the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
