**palta** is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) verb meaning "feel with the hand, pass the sensitive palm (*[plata](Plata.md)*) over a surface: feel with the hand".<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

**palta** is a Quenya word meaning "flat of the hand, the hand held upwards or forwards, flat and tensed (with fingers and thumb closed or spread)"<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>
