The **Postmaster** of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") was the head of the [Messenger Service](/wiki/Messenger_Service). The office was attached to the [Mayoralty of Michel Delving](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving "Mayor of Michel Delving").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Position is occupied by &#34;Postmaster Proudfoot&#34;, who is found in <a href="/wiki/Michel_Delving" title="Michel Delving">Michel Delving</a> appropriately right next to <a href="/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving" title="Mayor of Michel Delving">Mayor</a> <a href="/wiki/Will_Whitfoot" title="Will Whitfoot">Will Whitfoot</a> and is involved in multiple quests involving the services of the <a href="/wiki/Quick_Post" title="Quick Post">Quick Post</a>.</dd></dl>
