<i><b>Poems by J.R.R. Tolkien: Volume I-III</b></i> is a 3 volume set issued in a slipcase, containing a selection of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poetry from *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The books feature drawings by Tolkien, coloured by [H.E. Riddett](/wiki/H.E._Riddett).
