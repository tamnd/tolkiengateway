<i><b>Parma Eldalamberon 13: The Alphabet of Rúmil and Early Noldorin Fragments</b></i> is the thirteenth issue of *[Parma Eldalamberon](Parma_Eldalamberon.md)*, a journal of linguistic studies of the Elvish languages and names in the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

## Description {#Description}

The Rúmilian [Sarati](../S/Sarati.md) are the earliest of the Elvish writing systems devised by Tolkien, ultimately envisioned as the historical precursor to the Fëanorian Tengwar, the Elvish script seen in *The Lord of the Rings*. **"The Alphabet of Rúmil"** is an edition of Tolkien's Rúmilian writings, with examples of the script reproduced in facsimile, including charts of the sounds represented by the letters, and both Elvish and English texts written in Rúmilian. Transcriptions of these texts and detailed commentary on the chronology of the documents and evolution of the conception of the writing system are included in this edition.

**"Early Noldorin Fragments"** is a collection of Tolkien's word-lists and grammatical descriptions of the Noldorin language from the 1920s. These trace the evolution the language from its beginnings as the Goldogrin of the "Gnomish Lexicon" (*[Parma 11](Parma_Eldalamberon_11.md)*) to its conception as the Exilic Noldorin that would appear in "The Etymologies" (*[The Lost Road](../T/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings.md)*). These writings reveal the emergence of significant conceptual details, such the use of vowel mutation to mark Noldorin plural nouns, and the place of Old Noldorin in the internal history of the language. Detailed annotations and commentary on these conceptual developments in the documents are included in this edition.
