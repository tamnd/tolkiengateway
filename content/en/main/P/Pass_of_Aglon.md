The **Pass of Aglon**, also called the **Gorge of Aglon**<sup id="cite_ref-GA242_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA242-1">[1]</a></sup> or just **Aglon**, was the pass between [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) and the uplands west of [Himring](/wiki/Himring), to the north-east of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The Pass of Aglon was about six leagues in length, with the western side bordering the [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) plateau rising in very high steep walls. It was chilly since a bitter wind continuously blew through the pass from the lands to the north.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Two of the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor), [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin), heavily fortified this area, augmented by extra forces in [Himlad](/wiki/Himlad).<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-3">[3]</a></sup> About ten leagues to the east, the pair's elder brother [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) had built upon the [Hill of Himring](/wiki/Hill_of_Himring) his stronghold, thus protecting this weak point in the chain of kingdoms created by the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) after the [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](/wiki/Dagor-nuin-Giliath) (Battle-under-Stars).<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [402](/wiki/First_Age_402 "First Age 402"), [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) attempted to break through the Pass, but the [people of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor "House of Bëor") helped Maedhros and Maglor throw back the invaders.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455"),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> during the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) broke through Celegorm and Curufin's fortifications and [Aglon was lost](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pass_of_Aglon "Battle of the Pass of Aglon"), leaving Maedhros' stronghold in Himring the main centre for the forces of Fëanor's sons. Maedhros later took back the Pass for a time.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> However, after [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad) in [472](/wiki/First_Age_472 "First Age 472"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> the Sons of Fëanor were scattered, the Pass of Aglon fell under [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s domination,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and it was garrisoned by Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-GA242_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA242-1">[1]</a></sup> The Pass was never recovered, along with all of the land of northern Beleriand.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aglon* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Narrow Pass".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name **Pass of Aglond** appears in a text and on a map within [J.R.R. Tolkien's](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") final writings. [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) ignored the *d* when redrawing the map, believing it to be an original element. However, he notes that though it may seem like an original element, the *d* could have been added much later.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
