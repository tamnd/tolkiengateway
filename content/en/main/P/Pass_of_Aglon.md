The **Pass of Aglon**, also called the **Gorge of Aglon**<sup id="cite_ref-GA242_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA242-1">[1]</a></sup> or just **Aglon**, was the pass between [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) and the uplands west of [Himring](../H/Himring.md), to the north-east of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The Pass of Aglon was about six leagues in length, with the western side bordering the [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) plateau rising in very high steep walls. It was chilly since a bitter wind continuously blew through the pass from the lands to the north.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Two of the [Sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md), [Celegorm](../C/Celegorm.md) and [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md), heavily fortified this area, augmented by extra forces in [Himlad](../H/Himlad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-3">[3]</a></sup> About ten leagues to the east, the pair's elder brother [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) had built upon the [Hill of Himring](../H/Hill_of_Himring.md) his stronghold, thus protecting this weak point in the chain of kingdoms created by the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) after the [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](../D/Dagor-nuin-Giliath.md) (Battle-under-Stars).<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [402](../F/First_Age_402.md), [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) attempted to break through the Pass, but the [people of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md) helped Maedhros and Maglor throw back the invaders.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [455](../F/First_Age_455.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> during the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) broke through Celegorm and Curufin's fortifications and [Aglon was lost](../B/Battle_of_the_Pass_of_Aglon.md), leaving Maedhros' stronghold in Himring the main centre for the forces of Fëanor's sons. Maedhros later took back the Pass for a time.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> However, after [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md) in [472](../F/First_Age_472.md),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> the Sons of Fëanor were scattered, the Pass of Aglon fell under [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s domination,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and it was garrisoned by Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-GA242_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA242-1">[1]</a></sup> The Pass was never recovered, along with all of the land of northern Beleriand.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aglon* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Narrow Pass".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name **Pass of Aglond** appears in a text and on a map within [J.R.R. Tolkien's](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) final writings. [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) ignored the *d* when redrawing the map, believing it to be an original element. However, he notes that though it may seem like an original element, the *d* could have been added much later.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
