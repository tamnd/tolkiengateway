**Pipe-weed** (also known as Halflings' Leaf or simply Leaf) was a plant that was most notably cultivated by the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## Properties {#Properties}

Pipe-weed was famous for its fragrant flowers. It preferred southern climates and grew abundantly in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md). In northern regions, like [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md), it was not as rich and large, and was never found in the wild; it flourished only in warm sheltered places like the southern slopes of the [Bree-hill](../B/Bree-hill.md), or [Longbottom](../L/Longbottom.md) in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The plant was probably native to [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), and was brought to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) by the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) during the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup> It grew in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) as a wild herb, appreciated primarily for its fragrant blossoms, where it was known as <i><b>sweet galenas</b></i> or more popularly as <i><b>westmansweed</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-healing_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-healing-2">[2]</a></sup>

Pipe-weed reached [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) by the [Greenway](../G/Greenway.md) some time in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Bree-hobbits](../B/Bree-hobbits.md) were likely the first to put it in pipes and use it for [smoking](../S/Smoking.md), but the first "true" pipe-weed was grown by [Tobold Hornblower](../T/Tobold_Hornblower.md) of [Longbottom](../L/Longbottom.md) in the [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md) around [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1070](../T/Third_Age_2670.md). The art of smoking pipe-weed spread from [Bree](../B/Bree.md) to [Men](../M/Men.md), [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) and others who wandered through the crossroads by Bree. [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) therefore considered *[The Prancing Pony](../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md)* as the "home and centre" of the smoking art.<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Wizard](../W/Wizards.md) [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) learned to smoke pipe-weed from the Hobbits and was often seen blowing smoke-rings.<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup> [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) initially derided him for this, but at some point he took up smoking himself, and even had commerce with the Shire; after the destruction of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md), pipe-weed was found among its stores, but the Hobbits [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](Peregrin_Took.md) failed to realize the [sinister implications](../S/Sharkey.md).

The cultivation and exportation of this herb became a key industry in the Shire, especially in the south. Popular varieties of pipe-weed from the Shire included [Longbottom Leaf](../L/Longbottom_Leaf.md), [Old Toby](../O/Old_Toby.md), and [Southern Star](../S/Southern_Star.md).<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup> Perhaps less popular was [Southlinch](../S/Southlinch.md) from Bree.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) authored *[Herblore of the Shire](../H/Herblore_of_the_Shire.md)* discussing the origins and history of the Hobbits' 'art' of [smoking](../S/Smoking.md) pipe-weed.<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In the prologue to *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, Tolkien stated specifically that it is "a variety probably of *Nicotiana*",<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup> i.e. tobacco.

## Other names {#Other_names}

The common Gondorian name of pipe-weed was <b><u><i>westmansweed</i></u></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The more noble name was <i><b>galenas</b></i> (a [Númenórean Sindarin](../N/Númenórean_Sindarin.md) form of the proper [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) form *galanes*), with [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) forms <i><b>alanessë</b></i> or <i><b>alenessë</b></i> derived from [ala-](../G/GALA.md) ("plant, grow") and *nes-* ("sweet smelling").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
