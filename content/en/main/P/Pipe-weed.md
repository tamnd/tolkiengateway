**Pipe-weed** (also known as Halflings' Leaf or simply Leaf) was a plant that was most notably cultivated by the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## Properties {#Properties}

Pipe-weed was famous for its fragrant flowers. It preferred southern climates and grew abundantly in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). In northern regions, like [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), it was not as rich and large, and was never found in the wild; it flourished only in warm sheltered places like the southern slopes of the [Bree-hill](/wiki/Bree-hill), or [Longbottom](/wiki/Longbottom) in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The plant was probably native to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), and was brought to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) by the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup> It grew in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) as a wild herb, appreciated primarily for its fragrant blossoms, where it was known as <i><b>sweet galenas</b></i> or more popularly as <i><b>westmansweed</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-healing_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-healing-2">[2]</a></sup>

Pipe-weed reached [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) by the [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway) some time in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Bree-hobbits](/wiki/Bree-hobbits) were likely the first to put it in pipes and use it for [smoking](/wiki/Smoking "Smoking"), but the first "true" pipe-weed was grown by [Tobold Hornblower](/wiki/Tobold_Hornblower) of [Longbottom](/wiki/Longbottom) in the [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing) around [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1070](/wiki/Third_Age_2670 "Third Age 2670"). The art of smoking pipe-weed spread from [Bree](/wiki/Bree) to [Men](/wiki/Men), [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) and others who wandered through the crossroads by Bree. [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) therefore considered *[The Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony)* as the "home and centre" of the smoking art.<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards") [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) learned to smoke pipe-weed from the Hobbits and was often seen blowing smoke-rings.<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup> [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) initially derided him for this, but at some point he took up smoking himself, and even had commerce with the Shire; after the destruction of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), pipe-weed was found among its stores, but the Hobbits [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") failed to realize the [sinister implications](/wiki/Sharkey "Sharkey").

The cultivation and exportation of this herb became a key industry in the Shire, especially in the south. Popular varieties of pipe-weed from the Shire included [Longbottom Leaf](/wiki/Longbottom_Leaf), [Old Toby](/wiki/Old_Toby), and [Southern Star](/wiki/Southern_Star).<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup> Perhaps less popular was [Southlinch](/wiki/Southlinch) from Bree.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) authored *[Herblore of the Shire](/wiki/Herblore_of_the_Shire)* discussing the origins and history of the Hobbits' 'art' of [smoking](/wiki/Smoking "Smoking") pipe-weed.<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In the prologue to *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, Tolkien stated specifically that it is "a variety probably of *Nicotiana*",<sup id="cite_ref-pipeweed_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup> i.e. tobacco.

## Other names {#Other_names}

The common Gondorian name of pipe-weed was <b><u><i>westmansweed</i></u></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The more noble name was <i><b>galenas</b></i> (a [Númenórean Sindarin](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean_Sindarin) form of the proper [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) form *galanes*), with [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) forms <i><b>alanessë</b></i> or <i><b>alenessë</b></i> derived from [ala-](/wiki/GALA "GALA") ("plant, grow") and *nes-* ("sweet smelling").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
