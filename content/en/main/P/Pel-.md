<i><b>pel-</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) verb meaning "go round, revolve, return."<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [PEL](/wiki/PEL#Other_versions) ("revolve on fixed point").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
