**Patrick Howard Wynne** is one of the leading figures in the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](/wiki/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship), and was co-editor of *[Vinyar Tengwar](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar)* (along with [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter), [Christopher Gilson](/wiki/Christopher_Gilson), [Arden R. Smith](/wiki/Arden_R._Smith) and [Bill Welden](/wiki/Bill_Welden)). He has published numerous articles relating to the invented languages of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), and he is a quite prolific illustrator.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He also frequents the [Lambengolmor mailing list](</wiki/Lambengolmor_(mailing_list)> "Lambengolmor (mailing list)").
