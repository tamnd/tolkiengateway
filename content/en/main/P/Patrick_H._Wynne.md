**Patrick Howard Wynne** is one of the leading figures in the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](../E/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship.md), and was co-editor of *[Vinyar Tengwar](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar.md)* (along with [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md), [Christopher Gilson](../C/Christopher_Gilson.md), [Arden R. Smith](../A/Arden_R._Smith.md) and [Bill Welden](../B/Bill_Welden.md)). He has published numerous articles relating to the invented languages of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), and he is a quite prolific illustrator.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He also frequents the [Lambengolmor mailing list](<../L/Lambengolmor_(mailing_list).md>).
