<i><b>perian</b></i> (lit. "[Halfling](/wiki/Halflings "Halflings")"), plural <i><b>periain</b></i> and collective plural <i><b>Periannath</b></i>, is both the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppE_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppE-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

- *i-Pheriannath*: demonstrating [nasal mutation](/wiki/Nasal_mutation "Nasal mutation") (<i>i<b>n-P</b>eriannath</i>)<sup id="cite_ref-AppE_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppE-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
