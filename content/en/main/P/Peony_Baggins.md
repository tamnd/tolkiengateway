**Peony Baggins** (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1350](../T/Third_Age_2950.md)) was a Hobbit of the Shire.

## History {#History}

Peony was a daughter of [Posco Baggins](Posco_Baggins.md) and [Gilly Brownlock](../G/Gilly_Brownlock.md), the younger sister of [Ponto](Ponto_Baggins_II.md) and [Porto](Porto_Baggins.md). She married [Milo Burrows](../M/Milo_Burrows.md) and they had several children: [Mosco](../M/Mosco_Burrows.md), [Moro](../M/Moro_Burrows.md), [Myrtle](../M/Myrtle_Burrows.md) and [Minto](../M/Minto_Burrows.md). With her family she attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peony" title="wikipedia:Peony">Peony</a> is a genus of the buttercup family of perennial plants with flowers.
