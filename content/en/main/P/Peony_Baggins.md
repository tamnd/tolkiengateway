**Peony Baggins** (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1350](/wiki/Third_Age_2950 "Third Age 2950")) was a Hobbit of the Shire.

## History {#History}

Peony was a daughter of [Posco Baggins](/wiki/Posco_Baggins) and [Gilly Brownlock](/wiki/Gilly_Brownlock), the younger sister of [Ponto](/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_II "Ponto Baggins II") and [Porto](/wiki/Porto_Baggins "Porto Baggins"). She married [Milo Burrows](/wiki/Milo_Burrows) and they had several children: [Mosco](/wiki/Mosco_Burrows "Mosco Burrows"), [Moro](/wiki/Moro_Burrows "Moro Burrows"), [Myrtle](/wiki/Myrtle_Burrows "Myrtle Burrows") and [Minto](/wiki/Minto_Burrows "Minto Burrows"). With her family she attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peony" title="wikipedia:Peony">Peony</a> is a genus of the buttercup family of perennial plants with flowers.
