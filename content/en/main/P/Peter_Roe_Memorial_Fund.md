The **Peter Roe Memorial Fund** (also known as the **Peter Roe Books** or **Peter Roe Booklets**) exists to produce small publications on subject of interest to Tolkien readers and [Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society "The Tolkien Society") members. The Fund exists in memory of [Peter Roe](/wiki/Peter_Roe) of the Tolkien Society.
