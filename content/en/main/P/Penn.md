## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>penn</b></i>, or <i><b>pend</b></i>, is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "slope". The plural form of *penn* is <i><b>pinn</b></i> or <i><b>pind</b></i>. The collective plural form is <i><b>pennath</b></i> (cf. *[-ath](/wiki/-ath)*). A variant (dialectical or [Númenórean Sindarin](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean_Sindarin)) plural form is *[pinnath](/wiki/Pinnath "Pinnath")*.<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [PED](/wiki/PED)<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Noldorin {#Noldorin}

<i><b>penn</b></i>, or <i><b>pend</b></i>, is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word meaning "declivity".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

The word derives from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [PEN, PÉNED](/wiki/PED#Other_versions "PED").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>
