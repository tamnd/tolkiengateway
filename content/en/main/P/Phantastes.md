<i><b>Phantastes: A Faerie Romance for Men and Women</b></i> is fantasy novel written by [George MacDonald](/wiki/George_MacDonald). [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) had read the book (although he did not much like it),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and its notion of animated trees apparently was an inspiration for the creation of the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) *(see further, [MacDonald, Tolkien, and Ents](/wiki/George_MacDonald#Ents "George MacDonald"))*.
