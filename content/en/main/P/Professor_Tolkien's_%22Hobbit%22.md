**Professor Tolkien's "Hobbit"** is the title of a literary review of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. It was written by [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) and originally published in *The Times* on [8 October](/wiki/8_October) [1937](/wiki/1937) (p. 20).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
