**Peter Cuijpers** ([1944](/wiki/1944)) is a Dutch translator, who has translated works of Robin Hobb, Fiona MacIntosh and many others. After the death of [Max Schuchart](/wiki/Max_Schuchart) in [2005](/wiki/2005), a new translator was needed for *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*. Cuijpers had already translated several photo books about the [movie](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") franchise, and was deemed the best option by Mynx publishers. His *De kinderen van Húrin* was presented on [April 28](/wiki/28_April "28 April"), [2007](/wiki/2007).

Cuijpers did not follow Schuchart's translation of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* and *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)* save on [translated names](/wiki/Translated_names "Translated names").

## Translations {#Translations}

- [2001](/wiki/2001) - *De Reisgenoten in beeld en ontwerp* (*[The Art of The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Art_of_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, by [Gary Russell](/wiki/Gary_Russell))
- [2002](/wiki/2002) - *De Twee Torens in beeld en ontwerp* (*[The Art of The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Art_of_The_Two_Towers)*, by Gary Russell)
- [2003](/wiki/2003) - *De Terugkeer van de Koning in beeld en ontwerp* - *[The Art of The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Art_of_The_Return_of_the_King)*, by Gary Russell)
- [2007](/wiki/2007) - *De Kinderen van Húrin* (*[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*)
