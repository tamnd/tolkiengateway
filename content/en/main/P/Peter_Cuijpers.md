**Peter Cuijpers** ([1944](../_/1944.md)) is a Dutch translator, who has translated works of Robin Hobb, Fiona MacIntosh and many others. After the death of [Max Schuchart](../M/Max_Schuchart.md) in [2005](../_/2005.md), a new translator was needed for *[The Children of Húrin](../T/The_Children_of_Húrin.md)*. Cuijpers had already translated several photo books about the [movie](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) franchise, and was deemed the best option by Mynx publishers. His *De kinderen van Húrin* was presented on [April 28](../_/28_April.md), [2007](../_/2007.md).

Cuijpers did not follow Schuchart's translation of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* and *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)* save on [translated names](../T/Translated_names.md).

## Translations {#Translations}

- [2001](../_/2001.md) - *De Reisgenoten in beeld en ontwerp* (*[The Art of The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Art_of_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, by [Gary Russell](../G/Gary_Russell.md))
- [2002](../_/2002.md) - *De Twee Torens in beeld en ontwerp* (*[The Art of The Two Towers](../T/The_Art_of_The_Two_Towers.md)*, by Gary Russell)
- [2003](../_/2003.md) - *De Terugkeer van de Koning in beeld en ontwerp* - *[The Art of The Return of the King](../T/The_Art_of_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*, by Gary Russell)
- [2007](../_/2007.md) - *De Kinderen van Húrin* (*[The Children of Húrin](../T/The_Children_of_Húrin.md)*)
