**Poppy Chubb-Baggins** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Poppy was the daughter of [Falco Chubb-Baggins](/wiki/Falco_Chubb-Baggins). She married [Filibert Bolger](/wiki/Filibert_Bolger). She was a guest at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poppy" title="wikipedia:Poppy">Poppy</a></i> is a common family of annual and perennial plants. The name derives from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *popæg*, ultimately from [Latin](/wiki/Latin) *papaver*. <span></span>
