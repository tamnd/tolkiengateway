**Poppy Chubb-Baggins** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Poppy was the daughter of [Falco Chubb-Baggins](../F/Falco_Chubb-Baggins.md). She married [Filibert Bolger](../F/Filibert_Bolger.md). She was a guest at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poppy" title="wikipedia:Poppy">Poppy</a></i> is a common family of annual and perennial plants. The name derives from [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *popæg*, ultimately from [Latin](../L/Latin.md) *papaver*. <span></span>
