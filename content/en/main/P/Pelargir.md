**Pelargir** was a great city on the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), and the main harbour of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## History {#History}

Pelargir was built in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [2350](/wiki/Second_Age_2350 "Second Age 2350") as a haven of the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful).<sup id="cite_ref-b1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-b1-1">[1]</a></sup> The [King's Men](/wiki/King%27s_Men) established havens farther South.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It had in its early days been a city only a few miles away from the coasts, but after the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor) the coasts along the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas) had retreated a great distance and the city was left far inland.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Pelargir was one of the cities that assembled [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) when it was founded in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320") by the Faithful.<sup id="cite_ref-b1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-b1-1">[1]</a></sup>

It became an even greater haven in the days of the [Ship-kings](/wiki/Ship-kings). [Tarannon Falastur](/wiki/Tarannon_Falastur) built a great house there, with its roots in the water which he so dearly loved. His wife, [Berúthiel](/wiki/Ber%C3%BAthiel), did not, however, and preferred to live in [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) with her [cats](/wiki/Cats_of_Queen_Ber%C3%BAthiel "Cats of Queen Berúthiel").<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-4">[4]</a></sup> Tarannon's nephew [Eärnil I](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_I), who succeeded him, rebuilt Pelargir and built a great navy to conquer [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar).<sup id="cite_ref-Anarion_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Anarion-5">[5]</a></sup>

[Castamir](/wiki/Castamir) was highly loved in the shore regions of the land, and when he usurped the throne, he purposed to move the throne from Osgiliath to Pelargir.<sup id="cite_ref-Anarion_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Anarion-5">[5]</a></sup> Though he did not do so, he did move his base there. [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Eldacar (King of Gondor)") turned the tide in the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife) at the [Battle of the Crossings of Erui](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Crossings_of_Erui) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1447](/wiki/Third_Age_1447 "Third Age 1447"), and Castamir's family, supporters and ships retreated to Pelargir. Eldacar [besieged them by land](/wiki/Siege_of_Pelargir "Siege of Pelargir") until they were driven out, making their way to Umbar.<sup id="cite_ref-Anarion_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Anarion-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-tTA_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tTA-6">[6]</a></sup> Castamir's great-grandsons [Angamaitë](/wiki/Angamait%C3%AB) and [Sangahyando](/wiki/Sangahyando) eventually retook Pelargir in [1634](/wiki/Third_Age_1634 "Third Age 1634"), and slew king [Minardil](/wiki/Minardil).<sup id="cite_ref-Anarion_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Anarion-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-tTA_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tTA-6">[6]</a></sup>

Like his namesake, the great general [Eärnil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II "Eärnil II") took up residence in Pelargir. His Army of the South was stationed there during the [long war with the Wainriders](/wiki/Wainrider/Balchoth_War "Wainrider/Balchoth War"). News of the [Disaster of the Morannon](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Morannon) reached him on [9 July](/wiki/9_July) [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944"), and he set out to [battle](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Camp "Battle of the Camp"). He returned victorious, and with the King and his sons slain, the general (of royal blood) was crowned King Eärnil II.&lt;<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-4">[4]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Pelargir was overrun by [Corsairs](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar "Corsairs of Umbar") once again. Their fleet of some fifty great ships and many more smaller vessels laid in dock, ready to sail to [Harlond](</wiki/Harlond_(Gondor)> "Harlond (Gondor)"). However, it was not to be, for on the [12 March](/wiki/12_March)<sup id="cite_ref-great_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-7">[7]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and the [Dead Men of Dunharrow](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") struck fear into the men of Umbar and drove them towards Pelargir; and on [the next day](/wiki/13_March "13 March"), they met the Corsairs there,<sup id="cite_ref-great_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-7">[7]</a></sup> causing them to flee or dive into the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). After manning the Black Ships with [his own troops](/wiki/Grey_Company "Grey Company"), Aragorn lifted the curse that followed the Dead Men since the day of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur).<sup id="cite_ref-tLD_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tLD-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Pelargir* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Garth of Royal Ships".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) has noted that the first element derives from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) element/root *[pel-](/wiki/PEL "PEL")* ("go round", "encircle").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack) suggests that it is a compound of *[pêl](/wiki/P%C3%AAl "Pêl")* ("fenced field") + the prefix *[ar(a)-](/wiki/Ara "Ara")* ("royal", "noble", "high") + [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") form of the plural *cîr* of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Cair&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cair (page does not exist)">cair</a></i> ("ship").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Writing in a letter](</wiki/Letter_to_Jennifer_Brookes-Smith_(28_July_1955)> "Letter to Jennifer Brookes-Smith (28 July 1955)") about an impending trip in [1955](/wiki/1955), Tolkien identified Pelargir as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice" title="wikipedia:Venice">Venice</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Pelargir was built around the meeting of the rivers <a href="/wiki/Sirith" title="Sirith">Sirith</a> and <a href="/wiki/Anduin" title="Anduin">Anduin</a>, on the eastern border of the Lower <a href="/wiki/Lebennin" title="Lebennin">Lebennin</a> area and the Central <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> region, west of Upper Lebennin and Eastern Gondor. <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>&#39;s retaking of Pelargir was featured in an Epic Battle and served as the climax of Book 2 of Volume IV. The city&#39;s emblem was a blue anchor on a white background, with gold trim. The city was divided into the West-garth, the East-garth, the Market Ward, the Shipwrights&#39; Ward, the Court of the Ship-Kings, and Tol Ciryarani, though the latter was rendered inaccessible by a collapsed bridge by the time Aragorn liberated the city. Argillond, the Harbor of Royal Ships, was partitioned from the rest of the Great River by two sea-gates, one on the east and one on the west.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Sirgon was Lord of the city during the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, and was captured and spirited away by <a href="/wiki/Haradrim" title="Haradrim">Haradrim</a> slavers when the city was captured. Sirgon&#39;s son Faltharan and his captain Thandor took command of the city following its liberation. Faltharan managed to rescue his mother and siblings, only to soon discover his brother Lorvegil had betrayed the city to the <a href="/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar" title="Corsairs of Umbar">Corsairs</a>, allowing it to fall. After apprehending Lorvegil, Faltharan discovered and rescued their father at the <a href="/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor" title="Warning beacons of Gondor">beacon</a> Sirthanc in Upper Lebennin.</dd></dl>

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>After surviving the <a href="/wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(episode)" title="Udûn (episode)">eruption</a> of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Orodruin" title="Orodruin">Orodruin</a> and <a href="/wiki/The_Eye" title="The Eye">escaping</a> the newly-named <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bronwyn" title="Bronwyn">Bronwyn</a> informs <a href="/wiki/Galadriel" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a> upon a hilltop that she was planning on leading the surviving <a href="/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans" title="Pre-Númenóreans">Southlander</a> remnant to Pelargir, which she refers to as &#34;<i>an old <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans" title="Númenóreans">Númenórean</a> colony</i>&#34;.</dd></dl>
