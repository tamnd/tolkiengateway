The **Preface** to *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](../T/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md)* presents the poems as a translation from the *[Red Book of Westmarch](../R/Red_Book_of_Westmarch.md)*, and gives some background information that is not found elsewhere: e.g. the name of the tower at [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md) and the names of the [Seven Rivers](../R/Rivers_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md). The poems carry some fictional backstory, linking them to [Hobbit](../H/Hobbit.md) folklore; they are all supposedly works that Hobbits enjoyed and were preserved in the margins of the Red Book, with several of them being attributed to [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and [Sam Gamgee](../S/Sam_Gamgee.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
