## Quenya {#Quenya}

<i><b>pen</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "without, not having".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Telerin {#Telerin}

<i><b>pen</b></i> in [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) means "person" (as an impersonal pronoun)<sup id="cite_ref-QE_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QE-2">[2]</a></sup>, apparently in analogy to German *man* (used also in Old English) and its French calque *on*.

### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

The word is a descendant form of the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) form *kwene* &gt; [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) *kwēn*, deriving from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [KWENE](/wiki/KWENE).<sup id="cite_ref-QE_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QE-2">[2]</a></sup>
