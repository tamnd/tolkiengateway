<i><b>pār</b></i> is the [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) word for "fist" (mainly the hand using a tool, not punching).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is a descendant form of [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *[kwāră](/wiki/Kw%C4%81ra "Kwāra")*, deriving from root [KWAR](/wiki/KWAR).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[quár](/wiki/Qu%C3%A1r "Quár")*
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[paur](/wiki/Paur "Paur")*
