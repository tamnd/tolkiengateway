<i><b>pār</b></i> is the [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) word for "fist" (mainly the hand using a tool, not punching).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is a descendant form of [Primitive Quendian](Primitive_Quendian.md) *[kwāră](../K/Kwāra.md)*, deriving from root [KWAR](../K/KWAR.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *[paur](Paur.md)*
