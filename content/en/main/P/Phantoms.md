**Phantoms** was a name commonly used to describe evil, shadowy beings, such as [undead](/wiki/Undead "Undead"), [demons](/wiki/Demons "Demons") or delusions.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History and characteristics {#History_and_characteristics}

In the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days), creatures of "*dreadful shapes*", among whom are said to have been phantoms, haunted the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), dragging people into the darkness surrounding the borders of the Elven dwellings never to reappear.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), the forest of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Taur-nu-Fuin" title="Taur-nu-Fuin">Taur-nu-Fuin</a> was an infamous locale of "*phantoms of terror*" said to strangle or pursue lost wanderers.<sup id="cite_ref-S18_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S18-4">[4]</a></sup>

Using wizardry, it was possible to summon phantoms of delusion. [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), "*master of shadows and of phantoms*",<sup id="cite_ref-S18_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S18-4">[4]</a></sup> devised a phantom resembling [Eilinel](/wiki/Eilinel) to entrap [Gorlim](/wiki/Gorlim).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) was rumoured to also be able to craft such phantoms.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

It is also told that disembodied, "phantom-like" Elf-souls (from [Elves](/wiki/Elves) "*who had been slain in the course of some wrong-doing*") could maliciously appear before living beings to induce fear, being envious of people still alive.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Beings called phantoms also appear in the poem *[Oilima Markirya](/wiki/Oilima_Markirya)*.<sup id="cite_ref-VT49_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT49-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Secret_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Secret-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Phantom* (from [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) and Old French *fantosme*, from Latin *phantasma*), means "specter, spirit, ghost" or "illusion, unreality".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word *fairë* is glossed as "phantom; disembodied spirit, when seen as a pale shape" (pl. *fairi*).<sup id="cite_ref-VT49_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT49-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Secret_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Secret-10">[10]</a></sup> The [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech) word *[gûl](/wiki/G%C3%BBl "Gûl")* is glossed (among other things) as "phantom".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> The [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) form *qímar* ("phantom"; *qímari* "phantoms") has been suggested as a reconstructed form deriving from linguistic material.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a "hastily written manuscript on small slips of paper, entitled ‘Reincarnation of Elves’",<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup> [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") discussed different possibilities of how the houseless [*fëa*](/wiki/F%C3%ABar "Fëar") of an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") can have a "memory so strong that it can in another *fëa* (incarnate) induce a picture of it, so that it seems a phantom form". Such a phantom "cannot be touched, but can be walked through".<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup>
