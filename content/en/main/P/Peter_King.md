**Peter King** served as make-up and hair designer for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), for which he won the 2003 <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.oscar.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Academy Award</a></b> for Best Makeup (alongside [Richard Taylor](/wiki/Richard_Taylor)). He reprised this role in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), for which he received a nomination at the 2012 Academy Awards.
