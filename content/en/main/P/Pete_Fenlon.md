**Peter C. Fenlon, Jr.** (born [1955](../_/1955.md)) is an American table-top game designer/author and illustrator. He was the former president and one of the founders of [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md), and contributed extensively to the [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md) game.

Around [2000](../_/2000.md), after ICE went bankrupt, Pete Fenlon and long-time colleague [Coleman Charlton](../C/Coleman_Charlton.md) joined the Mayfair Game company, which ICE had previously bailed out.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When Mayfair's rights to Catan were transferred to [Asmodee](../A/Asmodee.md), Fenlon became the head of the newly created Catan Studio subsidiary.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

As an illustrator, Fenlon is especially known for his maps of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). These were a large-scale map of the continent of Middle-earth (first released in [1982](../_/1982.md) as *[An Artist's Interpretation of Middle Earth](../A/An_Artist's_Interpretation_of_Middle_Earth.md)*) and several smaller-scale maps of different regions of Middle-earth (most of whom were printed in *[Northwestern Middle-earth Map Set](../N/Northwestern_Middle-earth_Map_Set.md)*). The smaller-scale maps were often included as separate color fold-outs with the [MERP](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md) 1st Ed. modules. With MERP 2nd Ed., no new maps were released.

Fenlon's maps have continued to have an explicit influence on later Middle-earth gaming cartographers (e.g., [Thomas Morwinsky](../T/Thomas_Morwinsky.md) and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Sampsa_Rydman&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sampsa Rydman (page does not exist)">Sampsa Rydman</a>), and his continental map has been discussed and revised in the magazines *[Other Hands](../O/Other_Hands.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and *[Other Minds](../O/Other_Minds.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The characteristical maps of Fenlon have even created an expression known as 'Fenlon Style maps'. In January 2008, the *Cartographer's Annual* released a Pete Fenlon style pack for use with ProFantasy Software cartography tools.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
