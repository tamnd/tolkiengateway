**Ponto Baggins** can refer to two members of the [Baggins Family](/wiki/Baggins_Family):

- [Ponto Baggins I](/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_I) ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1216](/wiki/Third_Age_2816 "Third Age 2816")-[1311](/wiki/Third_Age_2911 "Third Age 2911")), son of [Balbo Baggins](/wiki/Balbo_Baggins) and [Berylla Boffin](/wiki/Berylla_Boffin), and great-uncle to [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins").
- [Ponto Baggins II](/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_II) (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1346](/wiki/Third_Age_2946 "Third Age 2946")), son on [Posco Baggins](/wiki/Posco_Baggins) and [Gilly Brownlock](/wiki/Gilly_Brownlock), and great-grandson of [Ponto Baggins I](/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_I).
