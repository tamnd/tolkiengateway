**Ponto Baggins** can refer to two members of the [Baggins Family](../B/Baggins_Family~66343.md):

- [Ponto Baggins I](Ponto_Baggins_I.md) ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1216](../T/Third_Age_2816.md)-[1311](../T/Third_Age_2911.md)), son of [Balbo Baggins](../B/Balbo_Baggins.md) and [Berylla Boffin](../B/Berylla_Boffin.md), and great-uncle to [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md).
- [Ponto Baggins II](Ponto_Baggins_II.md) (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1346](../T/Third_Age_2946.md)), son on [Posco Baggins](Posco_Baggins.md) and [Gilly Brownlock](../G/Gilly_Brownlock.md), and great-grandson of [Ponto Baggins I](Ponto_Baggins_I.md).
