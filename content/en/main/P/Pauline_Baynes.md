**Pauline Diana Gasch, née Baynes** ([9 September](/wiki/9_September) [1922](/wiki/1922) - [1 August](/wiki/1_August) [2008](/wiki/2008)) was an English book illustrator.

## Early life {#Early_life}

Baynes spent her early years in India where her father was commissioner in Agra. She later moved to England for her schooling. Baynes attended the Slade School of Fine Art, but after a year she volunteered to work for the Ministry of Defence to paint camouflage. She was soon transferred to a map-making department (knowledge of which she later employed to good effect when she drew maps of [Narnia](/wiki/The_Chronicles_of_Narnia "The Chronicles of Narnia") for [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) and of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) for [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)).

## Work with Tolkien {#Work_with_Tolkien}

Baynes was J.R.R. Tolkien's chosen illustrator. In 1949, she illustrated *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)*, followed by cover art for the 1961 Puffin edition of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and a triptych for the slip-case of the 1964 deluxe edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. In 1962, she illustrated *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*, and in 1967, *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*. Before Tolkien's death, she also illustrated two posters: "[A Map of Middle-earth](/wiki/A_Map_of_Middle-earth)" ([1970](/wiki/1970)) and "[There and Back Again](</wiki/There_and_Back_Again_(map)> "There and Back Again (map)")" (based on *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, [1971](/wiki/1971)). After Tolkien's death, she provided illustrations for the poem *[Bilbo's Last Song](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Last_Song)* (as a poster in [1974](/wiki/1974), as a book in [1990](/wiki/1990)) and the collection *[Poems and Stories](/wiki/Poems_and_Stories)* ([1980](/wiki/1980)). She also painted the covers for the British [1973](/wiki/1973) one-volume and [1981](/wiki/1981) three-volume paperback editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. The final published appearance of Baynes' Tolkien art was the addition of her map of "The Little Kingdom" to the fiftieth anniversary edition of *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)* ([1999](/wiki/1999)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
