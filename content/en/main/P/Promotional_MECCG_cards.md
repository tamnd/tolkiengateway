**Promotional MECCG cards** is a page on Tolkien Gateway which lists the different promotional cards that were released for [MECCG](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md). Some promotional cards were unique cards that were never included in any of the different basic or expansion sets, others cards where present in the set they promote. For these the promotional version is only distinghuised by a small promo symbol on them.
