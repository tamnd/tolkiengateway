**Potatoes** or **taters** in [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md), were root vegetables that were apparently among the staple foods of the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) ([Hamfast Gamgee](../H/Hamfast_Gamgee.md) was recognized locally as an expert on growing potatoes).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) expected to find them in [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/potato#English" title="wiktionary:potato">Potato</a></i> is a word of Spanish and Native American origins. The term <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/tater#English" title="wiktionary:tater">tater</a></i> seen to be used in [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md), represents UK slang.

Since the potatoes are a New World plant (see below), it has been suggested that "potatoes" is perhaps a "translation" from a [Westron](../W/Westron.md) name of a related but otherwise unrecorded plant of [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Arda_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arda-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Fans have noticed that potatoes are a New World plant, not native to the Old World setting of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). The [pipe-weed](Pipe-weed.md) (another New World plant) is referenced as having being brought from [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) and fans have explored the possibility that this was also the case with potatoes.<sup id="cite_ref-Arda_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arda-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> *[The Children of Húrin](../T/The_Children_of_Húrin.md)* mentions that the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) boiled [a white fleshy root](../E/Earth-bread.md) similar to bread<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> which could have been another instance of potatoes in Middle-earth, from the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

See also [tomatoes](../T/Tomatoes.md)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> concerning a similar apparent "paradox".
