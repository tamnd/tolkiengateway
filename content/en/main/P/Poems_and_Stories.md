<i><b>Poems and Stories</b></i> is a book first published in 1980. It reprints several works by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien): *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*, "[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth Beorhthelm's Son](/wiki/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth_Beorhthelm%27s_Son)", *[On Fairy-Stories](/wiki/On_Fairy-Stories)*, *[Leaf by Niggle](/wiki/Leaf_by_Niggle)*, *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)*, and *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*. The book was illustrated by [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

A new hardback edition of *Poems and Stories* was published by [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) in 1992 (<a class="internal mw-magiclink-isbn" href="/wiki/Special:BookSources/0261102907">ISBN 0261102907</a>),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and by [Houghton Mifflin](/wiki/Houghton_Mifflin) in 1994 (<a class="internal mw-magiclink-isbn" href="/wiki/Special:BookSources/0395689996">ISBN 0395689996</a>),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> with cover art by [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee).
