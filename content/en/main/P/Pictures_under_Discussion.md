<i><b>Pictures under Discussion</b></i> is a [1987](/wiki/1987) book collecting photographs of and commentary on various famous people, by American photographer John Loengard. The book includes a section about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) (misspelled "[Tolkein, J.R.R.](/wiki/Tolkein "Tolkein")" in the index). Loengard's commentary (p. 138) consists of a rather negative [reminiscence](/wiki/Index:Reminiscences_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Reminiscences of J.R.R. Tolkien") about meeting John Ronald and [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien") at a "seaside hotel"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> in [Bournemouth](/wiki/Bournemouth)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> in [1968](/wiki/1968), and a duotone photograph of Tolkien taken by Loengard on the occasion is reproduced on a full-page (p. 139).
