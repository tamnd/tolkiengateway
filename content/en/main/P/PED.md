**PED** is a [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "fall in steep slant, incline, slope, slant down".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *ped-*<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin): *pendā* ("sloping"; "adjective = 'steeply sloping'")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *penda* ("steeply sloping"); *penda-* ("weak verb 'slope, incline'")
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[pend, penn](/wiki/Penn#Sindarin "Penn")*; *penna-* ("come down in a slant, fall")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appears the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) root **PEN, PÉNED**. Among its derivatives are:<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

- Quenya: *pende* ("slope, downslope, declivity")
- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *[penn, pend](/wiki/Penn#Noldorin "Penn")*; *am-benn*<sup id="cite_ref-VT46_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT46-4">[4]</a></sup> ("uphill"); *dadbenn* ("downhill, inclined, prone (to do)"<sup id="cite_ref-VT46_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT46-4">[4]</a></sup>)
