<i><b>parma</b></i> is a noun meaning "book" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Parma.gif"><img src="/w/images/d/d2/Parma.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Parma* is also the name in Quenya of the second letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the second letter of the first grade or [Tyellë](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB), which contains "voiceless stops" and it is the first letter in the labial or *p*-series of consonants, the *[Parmatéma](/wiki/Parmat%C3%A9ma)*. In all modes *Parma* represents **P**.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name for this letter is <i><b>Pí</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<br>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=PAR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="PAR (page does not exist)">PAR</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
