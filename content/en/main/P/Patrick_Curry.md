**Patrick Curry** is an English author. He is the author of *[Defending Middle-Earth: Tolkien: Myth and Modernity](/wiki/Defending_Middle-Earth "Defending Middle-Earth")*, a book that deals with the ideas of the [Middle-Earth](/wiki/Middle-earth "Middle-earth") Universe. Curry has also written on astrology, ethics, and Machiavelli.
