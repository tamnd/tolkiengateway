**Paksu** was a minor character in [The Father Christmas Letters](/wiki/The_Father_Christmas_Letters). He was a polar bear cub who lived at the North Pole. He and his brother [Valkotukka](/wiki/Valkotukka) were nephews of the [North Polar Bear](/wiki/North_Polar_Bear).<sup id="cite_ref-31b_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31b-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Paksu and Valkotukka came to [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas)' house for a visit. They ended up never leaving.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Paksu, along with his brother, was mischievous. Father Christmas wrote of them that "they seem to be growing up just like their uncle."<sup id="cite_ref-31b_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31b-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/paksu" title="wiktionary:paksu">Paksu</a></i> means "fat" in [Finnish](/wiki/Finnish).<sup id="cite_ref-31b_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31b-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
