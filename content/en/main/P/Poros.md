The **Poros** was a river in the south of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## Course {#Course}

Approximately 200 miles long, the Poros began in the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and then flowed south-west for about 150 miles, when it bent to the north-west and met the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) just before the Anduin began its delta. The Poros was crossed by the [Harad Road](/wiki/Harad_Road).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Poros formed the northern border of the region of Harondor ([South Gondor](/wiki/South_Gondor)), and the southern border of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien). During the later [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) it was the effective southern border of Gondor.

Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944") some [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) had crossed the Poros to [South Ithilien](/wiki/South_Ithilien), but they were defeated by the [Southern Army](/wiki/Southern_Army).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2885](/wiki/Third_Age_2885 "Third Age 2885"), the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) crossed the Poros and invaded [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) with great strength. Reinforced by [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), Steward [Túrin II](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_II) won a victory at the [crossings of Poros](/wiki/Crossings_of_Poros "Crossings of Poros"), though the Rohirrim princes [Fastred](</wiki/Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine)> "Fastred (son of Folcwine)") and [Folcred](/wiki/Folcred) were slain.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The *[Haudh in Gwanûr](/wiki/Haudh_in_Gwan%C3%BBr)* was made where the Poros was crossed by the Harad Road.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Poros* is unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

A map that was drawn by J.R.R. Tolkien after the [First Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The First Map of The Lord of the Rings") carries a note where "Boundary" is written after Poros. Other rivers except the Anduin and the Poros on this note are either under a a heading "From East" or under a heading "From West", which probably refers to on which side of the river Anduin in Gondor they are located.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> This probably means that the river Poros was at the boundary of Gondor.

[Karen Wynn Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad) speculated in *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth)* that the [Sea of Helcar](/wiki/Sea_of_Helcar) drained into the [Great Gulf](/wiki/Great_Gulf), and that Poros is what remained of the narrow region called the [Straits of the World](/wiki/Straits_of_the_World) that once separated the Sea from the Gulf. Fonstand's interpretation was predicated on the ideas that [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) did not exist in the First Age, and its location in the Second and later Ages overlapped the area occupied by the Sea of Helcar in the First Age.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

However, in *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)* (which was published after Fonstand's *Atlas*), [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor) was said to have created [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom) in Mordor during the "long [First Age](/wiki/First_Age)".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Additionally, it is told in *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)* that the migration of the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain) from [Hildórien](/wiki/Hild%C3%B3rien) brought them westward through lands south of Mordor, and that they became the first Men to cross the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
