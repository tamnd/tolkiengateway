<i><b>Proceedings of the J.R.R. Tolkien Centenary Conference</b></i> is a book which contains the majority of the material presented at the [J.R.R. Tolkien Centenary Conference](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_Centenary_Conference.md). It was published as *Mythlore* 80 (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/mythlore/vol21/iss1/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Volume 21, Issue 1</a>) and [Mallorn 33](../M/Mallorn_33.md).
