The **Place of the Well**, also known as the **Square of the Folkwell**, was a large square in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

It was located north-west of the [Square of the King](../S/Square_of_the_King.md), connecting it by the [Road of Arches](../R/Road_of_Arches.md), while its western entry was the [Arch of Inwë](../A/Arch_of_Inwë.md). The square had many oak and poplar trees, and also a great well of vast depth with very pure water. During the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), after the fall of the northern gate, many [goblins](../G/Goblins.md) reached the Place and the well became polluted with the carcases of Orcs. Later [Galdor](<../G/Galdor_(elf_of_Gondolin).md>) and his [men of the Tree](../H/House_of_the_Tree.md) expelled the enemy there, saving [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md). Then took place the scattering of many houses, forming a good battalion that Tuor led to the Square of the King.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
