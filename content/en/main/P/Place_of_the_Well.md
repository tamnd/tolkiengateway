The **Place of the Well**, also known as the **Square of the Folkwell**, was a large square in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

It was located north-west of the [Square of the King](/wiki/Square_of_the_King), connecting it by the [Road of Arches](/wiki/Road_of_Arches), while its western entry was the [Arch of Inwë](/wiki/Arch_of_Inw%C3%AB). The square had many oak and poplar trees, and also a great well of vast depth with very pure water. During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), after the fall of the northern gate, many [goblins](/wiki/Goblins "Goblins") reached the Place and the well became polluted with the carcases of Orcs. Later [Galdor](</wiki/Galdor_(elf_of_Gondolin)> "Galdor (elf of Gondolin)") and his [men of the Tree](/wiki/House_of_the_Tree "House of the Tree") expelled the enemy there, saving [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor). Then took place the scattering of many houses, forming a good battalion that Tuor led to the Square of the King.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
