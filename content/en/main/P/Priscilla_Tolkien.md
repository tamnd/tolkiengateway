**Priscilla Mary Anne Reuel Tolkien** ([18 June](/wiki/18_June), [1929](/wiki/1929) – [28 February](/wiki/28_February), [2022](/wiki/2022)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) was [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s only daughter, the sister of [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), [Michael Tolkien](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien), and [John Tolkien](/wiki/John_Tolkien). She was the youngest of the children of J.R.R. Tolkien. She was a social worker, before being retired.

Priscilla Tolkien was Honorary Vice-President of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) from [1986](/wiki/1986) until her death, and for many years she was a host to members of the Society at [Oxonmoot](/wiki/Oxonmoot).

In the biographical drama film, named [Tolkien](</wiki/Tolkien_(film)> "Tolkien (film)"), she appears briefly, and she was portrayed by actresses Mia Woods and Sienna Woods.
