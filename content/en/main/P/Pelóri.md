The **Pelóri** were the mountain-fence of [Aman](../A/Aman.md). The highest peak of the Pelóri was [Taniquetil](../T/Taniquetil.md), on which were the mansions of [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) and [Varda](../V/Varda.md).

## History {#History}

The Pelóri were raised by the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) to defend against the evils of [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) after he destroyed their original dwelling-place at [Almaren](../A/Almaren.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

They were the tallest of all mountains, and the tallest of the Pelóri was [Taniquetil](../T/Taniquetil.md), on which stood the [high mansions](../I/Ilmarin.md) of [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) and [Varda](../V/Varda.md). Behind them to the West, the [Light](../L/Light.md) of the Trees shone, but the narrow shores beneath their eastern feet were in darkness. The second highest of the Pelóri was [Hyarmentir](../H/Hyarmentir.md) above [Avathar](../A/Avathar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

When the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) came to [Aman](../A/Aman.md), the Valar cut a rift in the Mountains, the [Calacirya](../C/Calacirya.md), to let the Light shine through them. In that pass, the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) and the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) built the city of [Tirion](../T/Tirion.md), and the Pass of Light looked out onto the [Bay of Eldamar](../B/Bay_of_Eldamar.md) and lit the isle of [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md), where the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) dwelt in those ancient days.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [Nurtalë Valinóreva](../N/Nurtalë_Valinóreva.md), the Pelóri were rised up higher.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

According to a [prophecy](../S/Second_Prophecy_of_Mandos.md), the Mountains of Valinor shall be leveled after the Last Battle, allowing the [light](../L/Light.md) of the Two rekindled Trees reach all the new world.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name *Pelóri* translates as "the fencing or defensive heights"<sup id="cite_ref-SI_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SI-6">[6]</a></sup> or "Mountain Wall".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> In a manuscript, [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) connects the name to [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *pelo* ("a boundary (fence)") and *pella* ("beyond"), deriving from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [PELE](PEL~34941.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) suggested that the first element derives from *[pel-](PEL~34941.md)* ("fence, enclosure").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> The second element is likely <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Oro&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Oro (page does not exist)">oro</a></i> ("mountain").

## Other names {#Other_names}

An extended version of the name is also known: <i><b>Pelóri Valion</b></i> ("Mountains of Valinor").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

The Pelóri were also called the **Mountains of Aman** and the **Mountains of Defence**.<sup id="cite_ref-SI_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SI-6">[6]</a></sup>

## In other stories {#In_other_stories}

The Pelóri - as the Mountains of Elvenholme - are referenced in Tolkien's *[Roverandom](../R/Roverandom.md)*. In the story, written down in [1927](../_/1927.md), the great whale [Uin](../U/Uin.md) takes the enchanted dog [Roverandom](../R/Rover.md) (formerly known as Rover) on adventures through the seas: Uin takes Roverandom through the [Shadowy Seas](../S/Shadowy_Seas.md) to the [Bay of Fairyland](../B/Bay_of_Eldamar.md) beyond the [Magic Isles](../E/Enchanted_Isles.md) where Rover saw the Mountains of Elvenhome and the light of Faery. Roverandom thought he could see the white glint of [a city of Elves on a green hill far away in the distance](../T/Tirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-11">[11]</a></sup>
