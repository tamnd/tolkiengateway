The **Pelóri** were the mountain-fence of [Aman](/wiki/Aman). The highest peak of the Pelóri was [Taniquetil](/wiki/Taniquetil), on which were the mansions of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) and [Varda](/wiki/Varda).

## History {#History}

The Pelóri were raised by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) to defend against the evils of [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") after he destroyed their original dwelling-place at [Almaren](/wiki/Almaren).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

They were the tallest of all mountains, and the tallest of the Pelóri was [Taniquetil](/wiki/Taniquetil), on which stood the [high mansions](/wiki/Ilmarin "Ilmarin") of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) and [Varda](/wiki/Varda). Behind them to the West, the [Light](/wiki/Light) of the Trees shone, but the narrow shores beneath their eastern feet were in darkness. The second highest of the Pelóri was [Hyarmentir](/wiki/Hyarmentir) above [Avathar](/wiki/Avathar).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

When the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) came to [Aman](/wiki/Aman), the Valar cut a rift in the Mountains, the [Calacirya](/wiki/Calacirya), to let the Light shine through them. In that pass, the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) and the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) built the city of [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion), and the Pass of Light looked out onto the [Bay of Eldamar](/wiki/Bay_of_Eldamar) and lit the isle of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), where the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) dwelt in those ancient days.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [Nurtalë Valinóreva](/wiki/Nurtal%C3%AB_Valin%C3%B3reva), the Pelóri were rised up higher.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

According to a [prophecy](/wiki/Second_Prophecy_of_Mandos "Second Prophecy of Mandos"), the Mountains of Valinor shall be leveled after the Last Battle, allowing the [light](/wiki/Light "Light") of the Two rekindled Trees reach all the new world.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name *Pelóri* translates as "the fencing or defensive heights"<sup id="cite_ref-SI_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SI-6">[6]</a></sup> or "Mountain Wall".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> In a manuscript, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") connects the name to [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *pelo* ("a boundary (fence)") and *pella* ("beyond"), deriving from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [PELE](/wiki/PEL "PEL").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) suggested that the first element derives from *[pel-](/wiki/PEL "PEL")* ("fence, enclosure").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> The second element is likely <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Oro&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Oro (page does not exist)">oro</a></i> ("mountain").

## Other names {#Other_names}

An extended version of the name is also known: <i><b>Pelóri Valion</b></i> ("Mountains of Valinor").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

The Pelóri were also called the **Mountains of Aman** and the **Mountains of Defence**.<sup id="cite_ref-SI_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SI-6">[6]</a></sup>

## In other stories {#In_other_stories}

The Pelóri - as the Mountains of Elvenholme - are referenced in Tolkien's *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*. In the story, written down in [1927](/wiki/1927), the great whale [Uin](/wiki/Uin) takes the enchanted dog [Roverandom](/wiki/Rover "Rover") (formerly known as Rover) on adventures through the seas: Uin takes Roverandom through the [Shadowy Seas](/wiki/Shadowy_Seas) to the [Bay of Fairyland](/wiki/Bay_of_Eldamar "Bay of Eldamar") beyond the [Magic Isles](/wiki/Enchanted_Isles "Enchanted Isles") where Rover saw the Mountains of Elvenhome and the light of Faery. Roverandom thought he could see the white glint of [a city of Elves on a green hill far away in the distance](/wiki/Tirion "Tirion").<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-11">[11]</a></sup>
