**Ponto Baggins II**<sup id="cite_ref-letter_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-letter-1">[1]</a></sup> was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-baggins_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-baggins-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Ponto was the eldest son of [Posco Baggins](/wiki/Posco_Baggins) and [Gilly Brownlock](/wiki/Gilly_Brownlock) and was named after his great-grandfather, <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Ponto_Baggins" title="Ponto Baggins">Ponto</a>. He was the father of [Angelica](/wiki/Angelica_Baggins "Angelica Baggins") and they attended the [Birthday Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") together.<sup id="cite_ref-baggins_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-baggins-2">[2]</a></sup>

After [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) left [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1422](/wiki/Fourth_Age_1 "Fourth Age 1"), Ponto "presumably" became the head of the [Baggins Family](/wiki/Baggins_Family), though in title only, as [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) had passed to [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee).<sup id="cite_ref-letter_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-letter-1">[1]</a></sup>
