**Paula Katherine Marmor** (born [1950](/wiki/1950)) was the founding editor of *[Parma Eldalamberon](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon)* in its first run.

A native of California, Paula Marmor lives in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Fernando_Valley" title="wikipedia:San Fernando Valley">San Fernando Valley</a> and works with design and media.
