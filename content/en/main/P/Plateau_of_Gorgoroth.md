**Gorgoroth** was a high desolate plain<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in north-western [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) enclosed by the [Morgai](/wiki/Morgai) and the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) in the west, the Ephel Dúath and the [Ered Lithui](/wiki/Ered_Lithui) in the north, by a mountain arm that branched off from the Ephel Dúath in the south and by a mountain arm that branched off from the Ered Lithui in the south-east.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

The only known passages into the plateau through these mountain walls were the [Isenmouthe](/wiki/Carach_Angren "Carach Angren") (from the valley of [Udûn](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(valley)> "Udûn (valley)") in the south of the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon)), the rift in the Morgai in the east of the Pass of [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol) and the east of the [Morgul Pass](/wiki/Morgul_Pass) and the gap between the mountain arm that branched off from the Ephel Dúath to the east and the mountain spur that branched off from the Ered Litui to the south-west.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

In this dreary wasteland fumes issued from fissures in the ground and smoke curled and settled in hollows. Centred in the desolation some forty miles east of the Ephel Duath rose [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom). It and [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr), situated on a mountain spur of the Ered Lithui, dominated the landscape of the plateau.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

c. [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1000](/wiki/Second_Age_1000 "Second Age 1000") [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) selected Mordor as his stronghold and began building Barad-dûr above the plateau of Gorgoroth.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434") the host of the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men) entered Gorgoroth and began their siege of Sauron's fortress,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> during which [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) was slain in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3440](/wiki/Second_Age_3440 "Second Age 3440")).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> After seven years the siege ended when Sauron was defeated and his [Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") was taken.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Sauron returned to Mordor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2951](/wiki/Third_Age_2951 "Third Age 2951") and began rebuilding Barad-dûr, which had been torn down after his defeat in the Second Age.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

When [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") first looked upon Gorgoroth on [16 March](/wiki/16_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> they saw innumerable camps of [Men](/wiki/Men) made of huts and drab buildings, connected by a network of roads. In this region were Sauron's mines and forges for equipping his vast armies.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-3">[3]</a></sup>

On [19 March](/wiki/19_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> after escaping the Orcs at the Isenmouthe, the hobbits travelled on the road to Barad-dûr along the northern edge of Gorgoroth. Three days later they left the road and headed south across the plateau to Mount Doom, reaching it<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> on [24 March](/wiki/24_March).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Gorgoroth* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name,<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GS_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GS-14">[14]</a></sup> which is translated as "valley of terror"<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> or more literally "(land of) deadly fear".<sup id="cite_ref-GS_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GS-14">[14]</a></sup> It is a combination of *gor* ("horror", "dread", "fear"<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>) and *goroth* ("horror", "dread", "fear").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>
