**Poisoned weapons** were used by different races throughout the history of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

## Orcs {#Orcs}

[Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) frequently used poisoned blades<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and arrows. [Huor](/wiki/Huor) of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) was slain by a poisoned Orc-arrow that hit him in the eye.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Andróg](/wiki/Andr%C3%B3g) was wounded by poisoned Orc-arrow, but healed by [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Later [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) was killed by several poisoned Orc-arrows that pierced his throat and his heart.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Celebrían](/wiki/Celebr%C3%ADan), wife of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), received a poisoned wound from the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains) and, although Elrond healed her body, she lost all joy in Middle-earth and departed over the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Elves {#Elves}

It is said that [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) never used any poison against their enemies.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> However, there are some examples of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who used poisoned weapons. [Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l) the Dark Elf had a poisoned javelin, which he threw at [Maeglin](/wiki/Maeglin), but hit [Aredhel](/wiki/Aredhel), who died from that wound shortly after.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> [Elves of Nargothrond](/wiki/Elves_of_Nargothrond) also used poisoned darts to protect their kingdom after the departure of [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) and before the coming of [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Dwarves {#Dwarves}

The chieftain of the [Petty-dwarves](/wiki/Petty-dwarves),<sup id="cite_ref-foundnargo_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-foundnargo-9">[9]</a></sup> [Mîm](/wiki/M%C3%AEm), possessed a secret "long knife" that had "runes of venom" on it.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Men {#Men}

[Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain) were famous for their use of poisoned arrows<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> and darts,<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> although they used poisoned weapons only when fighting against the Orcs<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>.
