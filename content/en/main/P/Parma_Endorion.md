<i><b>Parma Endorion: Essays on Middle-earth</b></i> by [Michael Martinez](../M/Michael_Martinez.md) (edited by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Matt_Tinaglia&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Matt Tinaglia (page does not exist)">Matt Tinaglia</a>) is a freely available eBook containing a complete survey of the places, locations, history and peoples of [Arda](../A/Arda.md), from the beginning of days until the *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md).

It also contains some insight into [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s world and Martinez's past essays like for example the similarities (or lack thereof) between the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) and Anglo-Saxons, or how a Middle-earth themed wedding should be planned.

It contains a few illustrations by [Anke Eißmann](../A/Anke_Eißmann.md).
