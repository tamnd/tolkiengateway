## Description {#Description}

"**The Feanorian Alphabet, Part 2**" includes three descriptions of this writing system, dating from around [1948](/wiki/1948) to [1951](/wiki/1951). (A description of the earliest version was published in *[Parma Eldalamberon 22](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_22)*.) Four modes are described in detail representing usage in different regions of Middle-earth: [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), as these modes were applied to the *[Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)*, *[Westron](/wiki/Westron)*, and *English* languages. Charts and examples of the use of the *[tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar "Tengwar")* incorporate scanned images from Tolkien's manuscripts with transcriptions.

"**Eldarin Pronouns**" includes five grammatical texts treating personal, demonstrative, and relative pronouns in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and the definite article in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). "**Qenya Personal Pronouns**" is part of a Qenya Grammar from the 1940s, describing the forms and grammar of these pronouns. The closely contemporary "**Demonstrative, Relative, and Correlative Stems**" describes the forms and grammar of non-personal pronouns in Quenya. "**Eldarin Personal Pronouns**" from the early 1950s describes the etymological origin of the Quenya personal pronouns from [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) bases.

"**Eldarin Pronouns, Demonstrative and Correlatives**" is a revision and elaboration of "Eldarin Personal Pronouns" dating from the late 1950s, in which Tolkien began to incorporate etymological explanations for demonstrative pronouns. "**Common Eldarin Article**" is a typescript from 1968 or 1969 describing the etymological origin of the *Quenya* and *Sindarin* definite articles from the Common Eldarin demonstrative pronoun; the usage of the article in Quenya; and the consonant mutations associated with the article in the grammar of Sindarin.
