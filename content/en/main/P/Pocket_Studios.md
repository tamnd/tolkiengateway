**Pocket Studios** is a video game developer, specialised in developing Game Boy Advance games. It developed the Game Boy Advance version of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)") and *[The Lord of the Rings: The Treason of Isengard](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Treason_of_Isengard)*.
