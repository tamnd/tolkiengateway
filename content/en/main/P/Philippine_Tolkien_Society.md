**The Philippine Tolkien Society**, PTS, was the [Tolkien society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md) in the Philippines.

## History {#History}

As at 2011 the PTS website is in disrepair and the most recent posts date from 2008. Consequently it can be assumed PTS is now defunct.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Magazine {#Magazine}

Whilst active PTS published its magazine Quento.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The 5 issues known to have been released all focused on the Peter Jackson movie.
