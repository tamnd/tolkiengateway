**Prince**, or **Ernil** in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md),<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> was a hereditary title conferred by the kings of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

## History {#History}

The title was first given by [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) to a house of [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) nobles, to whom he was related, that had settled in [Belfalas](../B/Belfalas.md) in or near to [Edhellond](../E/Edhellond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Some two thousand years later, after the drowning of [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md), [Galador](../G/Galador.md) was the first of this line to use the title [Prince of Dol Amroth](Prince_of_Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) King [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) of Gondor created the title [Prince of Ithilien](Prince_of_Ithilien.md) and gave it to [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md), the last [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SaK_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SaK-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Peregrin Took](Peregrin_Took.md) was also informally known as the [Prince of the Halflings](Prince_of_the_Halflings.md) by the people of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) during his time there.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup>
