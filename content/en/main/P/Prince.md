**Prince**, or **Ernil** in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin),<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> was a hereditary title conferred by the kings of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## History {#History}

The title was first given by [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) to a house of [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") nobles, to whom he was related, that had settled in [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas) in or near to [Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Some two thousand years later, after the drowning of [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth), [Galador](/wiki/Galador) was the first of this line to use the title [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") of Gondor created the title [Prince of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien) and gave it to [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), the last [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward).<sup id="cite_ref-SaK_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SaK-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) was also informally known as the [Prince of the Halflings](/wiki/Prince_of_the_Halflings) by the people of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) during his time there.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup>
