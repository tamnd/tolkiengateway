The **Pass of Sirion**, in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), was a steep-sided pass between the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) and the [Echoriad](../E/Echoriad.md), on the upper reaches of the River [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The only road through the pass was narrow and ran along the western bank of the river.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The sheer walls were covered with pines yet the vale was green. In the middle of the pass was the isle of [Tol Sirion](../T/Tol_Sirion.md) where [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) built the tower of [Minas Tirith](<../M/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand).md>). After Finrod departed to [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) the fortress was committed to Finrod's kinsman [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) King [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) was surrounded with a small company in the [Fen of Serech](../F/Fen_of_Serech.md). There he would have fallen but for the arrival of [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md) who came through the Pass of Sirion and rescued him.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> However, by the end of this great battle [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) held the Pass.

During the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md) it was through the Pass of Sirion that [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) led the main host of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) north into battle, and then again south when in retreat. During that retreat [Huor](../H/Huor.md) formed a rearguard south of the Fen of Serech and resisted until all of his men but one were slain. That one was [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) who was captured and then taken to [Angband](../A/Angband.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

[Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) the [dragon](../D/Dragons.md) came through the Pass of Sirion and defiled the waters of the [Eithel Ivrin](../E/Eithel_Ivrin.md) on his way to [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
