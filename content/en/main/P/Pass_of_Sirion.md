The **Pass of Sirion**, in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), was a steep-sided pass between the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin) and the [Echoriad](/wiki/Echoriad), on the upper reaches of the River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The only road through the pass was narrow and ran along the western bank of the river.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The sheer walls were covered with pines yet the vale was green. In the middle of the pass was the isle of [Tol Sirion](/wiki/Tol_Sirion) where [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) built the tower of [Minas Tirith](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand)> "Minas Tirith (Beleriand)"). After Finrod departed to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) the fortress was committed to Finrod's kinsman [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) King [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) was surrounded with a small company in the [Fen of Serech](/wiki/Fen_of_Serech). There he would have fallen but for the arrival of [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir) who came through the Pass of Sirion and rescued him.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> However, by the end of this great battle [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) held the Pass.

During the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad) it was through the Pass of Sirion that [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) led the main host of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) north into battle, and then again south when in retreat. During that retreat [Huor](/wiki/Huor) formed a rearguard south of the Fen of Serech and resisted until all of his men but one were slain. That one was [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) who was captured and then taken to [Angband](/wiki/Angband).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

[Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") came through the Pass of Sirion and defiled the waters of the [Eithel Ivrin](/wiki/Eithel_Ivrin) on his way to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
