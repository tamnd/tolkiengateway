**Prisca Bolger, née Baggins**, was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Prisca was the second child and daughter of [Polo Baggins](/wiki/Polo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She married [Wilibald Bolger](/wiki/Wilibald_Bolger), and had several children: [Wilimar](/wiki/Wilimar_Bolger "Wilimar Bolger"), [Heribald](/wiki/Heribald_Bolger "Heribald Bolger") and [Nora](/wiki/Nora_Bolger "Nora Bolger"). Her husband passed away before [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001"), but she attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party) with her children.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Prisca* is a Latin/Italian word meaning "former, old" from which derives the name [Priscilla](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien "Priscilla Tolkien").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[The 50th Anniversary edition of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition)> "The Lord of the Rings (50th Anniversary Edition)") includes Prisca as a guest at the Farewell Party.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In older editions of [Appendix C](/wiki/Appendix_C), Prisca is not marked as an attendee. [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) in his *[The Complete Guide to Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth)* thus states that Prisca died *before* [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
