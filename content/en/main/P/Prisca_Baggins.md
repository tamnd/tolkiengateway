**Prisca Bolger, née Baggins**, was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Prisca was the second child and daughter of [Polo Baggins](Polo_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She married [Wilibald Bolger](../W/Wilibald_Bolger.md), and had several children: [Wilimar](../W/Wilimar_Bolger.md), [Heribald](../H/Heribald_Bolger.md) and [Nora](../N/Nora_Bolger.md). Her husband passed away before [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1401](../T/Third_Age_3001.md), but she attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md) with her children.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Prisca* is a Latin/Italian word meaning "former, old" from which derives the name [Priscilla](Priscilla_Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[The 50th Anniversary edition of *The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md>) includes Prisca as a guest at the Farewell Party.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In older editions of [Appendix C](../A/Appendix_C.md), Prisca is not marked as an attendee. [Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md) in his *[The Complete Guide to Middle-earth](../T/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth.md)* thus states that Prisca died *before* [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3001](../T/Third_Age_3001.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
