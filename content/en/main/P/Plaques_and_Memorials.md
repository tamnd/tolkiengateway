**Plaques and Memorials** is a list of blue plaques and memorials installed in public places of the United Kingdom to commemorate a link between that location and [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and his family. Often made by [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) and local institutions.

# Plaques {#Plaques}

## Sarehole Mill,Hall Green,Birmingham {#Sarehole_Mill,Hall_Green,Birmingham}

The text of the plaque reads:

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Birmingham Civic Society 2002<br/>
SAREHOLE MILL<br/>
Site of water Mill since 1543<br/>
Used by Matthew Boulton in mid 18th century<br/>
Rebuilt 1768<br/>
INSPIRED J.R.R. Tolkien1896-1900<br/>
The Tolkien Society
</p>
</div>

Birmingham Civic Society with [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) made the blueplaque at 15 August 2002.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## University Parks, Oxford {#University_Parks,_Oxford}

A plaque on a bench dedicated to author J.R.R. Tolkien in the University Parks of Oxford. The bench and two trees ("Laurelin" and "Telperion") planted nearby were donated in 1992 by the Tolkien Centenary Conference. The bench is situated on the western bank of River Cherwell a little bit south of the High Bridge.

<br>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">

# Bust {#Bust}

In the chapel of Exeter College, Oxford, there is a bust of J.R.R. Tolkien made by [Faith Tolkien](../F/Faith_Tolkien.md). Above the bust reads:

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>J.R.R. Tolkien 1892-1973<br/>
Faith Tolkien<br/>
b.1928-<br/>
Sculpted this portrait of her <br/>
Father-in-law<br/>
in 1977
</p>
</div>
