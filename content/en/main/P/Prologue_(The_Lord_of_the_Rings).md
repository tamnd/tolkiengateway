**Prologue**, labeled **Prologue Concerning Hobbits, and Other Matters** in some editions, is the opening section to *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* that provides background to the nature of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) and *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. It includes the *[A Part of the Shire](/wiki/A_Part_of_the_Shire)* map.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

### Concerning Hobbits {#Concerning_Hobbits}

A brief history of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) from their origins in the [Vales](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin "Vales of Anduin") of [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), to their movement into [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), their trusteeship of [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire), and the events leading up to the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). *Concerning Hobbits* also provides a broad description of the physical characteristics of Hobbits, as well as their manners, customs and important families.

### Concerning Pipe-weed {#Concerning_Pipe-weed}

Explanation of the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits)' fascination with [pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed"), its history (as laid out in [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck)'s *[Herblore of the Shire](/wiki/Herblore_of_the_Shire)*) and the notable varieties of [Old Toby](/wiki/Old_Toby), [Longbottom Leaf](/wiki/Longbottom_Leaf) and [Southern Star](/wiki/Southern_Star).

### Of the Ordering of the Shire {#Of_the_Ordering_of_the_Shire}

Describes the division of [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire) ([North](/wiki/Northfarthing "Northfarthing"), [South](/wiki/Southfarthing "Southfarthing"), [East](/wiki/Eastfarthing "Eastfarthing") and [Westfarthings](/wiki/Westfarthing "Westfarthing"); with [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) and [Westmarch](/wiki/Westmarch)), as well as the governing of The Shire (the [Thain](/wiki/Thain), the [Mayor of Michel Delving](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving) and the [Shirriffs](/wiki/Shirriffs)).

### Of the Finding of the Ring {#Of_the_Finding_of_the_Ring}

Covers the chapters in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* which deal with [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins")'s finding of the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring).

### Note on the Shire Records {#Note_on_the_Shire_Records}

Details the documentation in [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire) on the history of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

This entire section entered the Prologue only with the second edition of *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History of composition {#History_of_composition}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) published a detailed description of how his father composed this text in *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)*, in the first chapter "[The Prologue](/wiki/The_Prologue)".

"Concerning Hobbits" derives from a text providing background information on the Hobbits, written in the latter half of [1938](/wiki/1938), while rewriting *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was further worked with more background material, during Tolkien's final proofreading in [1949](/wiki/1949).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The basis of "Concerning Pipe-weed" was a lengthy speech of Merry about pipe-weed in a draft of the chapter *[The Road to Isengard](/wiki/The_Road_to_Isengard)*, eventually removed and reworked.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The "Prologue" would include a description of hobbit gift-giving customs and the *[ribadyan](/wiki/Ribadyan "Ribadyan")* (birthday celebrant) but it was dropped, being too lengthy.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b><a href="/wiki/2001" title="2001">2001</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the first scene, titled &#34;<a href="/wiki/Prologue:_One_Ring_to_Rule_Them_All..." title="Prologue: One Ring to Rule Them All...">Prologue: One Ring to Rule Them All...</a>&#34;, there is a brief description of how <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a> found the One Ring, clearly related to &#34;Of the Finding of the Ring&#34;.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>&#34;Concerning Hobbits&#34; is the name of the <a href="/wiki/Concerning_Hobbits_(scene)" title="Concerning Hobbits (scene)">second scene</a> of the <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)">Extended Edition</a> wherein &#34;Concerning Hobbits&#34; is the first chapter of Bilbo&#39;s <a href="/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch" title="Red Book of Westmarch">book</a>, in which he describes the nature of Hobbits. The audience hears Bilbo reading this aloud as a voiceover on top of scenes of life in <a href="/wiki/The_Shire" title="The Shire">The Shire</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Concerning_Hobbits_(soundtrack)" title="Concerning Hobbits (soundtrack)">scene&#39;s soundtrack</a> composed by <a href="/wiki/Howard_Shore" title="Howard Shore">Howard Shore</a> is also called &#34;Concerning Hobbits&#34; and is used as a primary theme of the Shire.</dd></dl>
