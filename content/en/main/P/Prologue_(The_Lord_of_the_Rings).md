**Prologue**, labeled **Prologue Concerning Hobbits, and Other Matters** in some editions, is the opening section to *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* that provides background to the nature of [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) and *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*. It includes the *[A Part of the Shire](../A/A_Part_of_the_Shire.md)* map.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

### Concerning Hobbits {#Concerning_Hobbits}

A brief history of [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) from their origins in the [Vales](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md) of [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), to their movement into [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md), their trusteeship of [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), and the events leading up to the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). *Concerning Hobbits* also provides a broad description of the physical characteristics of Hobbits, as well as their manners, customs and important families.

### Concerning Pipe-weed {#Concerning_Pipe-weed}

Explanation of the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md)' fascination with [pipe-weed](Pipe-weed.md), its history (as laid out in [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md)'s *[Herblore of the Shire](../H/Herblore_of_the_Shire.md)*) and the notable varieties of [Old Toby](../O/Old_Toby.md), [Longbottom Leaf](../L/Longbottom_Leaf.md) and [Southern Star](../S/Southern_Star.md).

### Of the Ordering of the Shire {#Of_the_Ordering_of_the_Shire}

Describes the division of [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) ([North](../N/Northfarthing.md), [South](../S/Southfarthing.md), [East](../E/Eastfarthing.md) and [Westfarthings](../W/Westfarthing.md); with [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md) and [Westmarch](../W/Westmarch.md)), as well as the governing of The Shire (the [Thain](../T/Thain.md), the [Mayor of Michel Delving](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md) and the [Shirriffs](../S/Shirriffs.md)).

### Of the Finding of the Ring {#Of_the_Finding_of_the_Ring}

Covers the chapters in *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* which deal with [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)'s finding of the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md).

### Note on the Shire Records {#Note_on_the_Shire_Records}

Details the documentation in [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) on the history of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

This entire section entered the Prologue only with the second edition of *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History of composition {#History_of_composition}

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) published a detailed description of how his father composed this text in *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](../T/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md)*, in the first chapter "[The Prologue](../T/The_Prologue.md)".

"Concerning Hobbits" derives from a text providing background information on the Hobbits, written in the latter half of [1938](../_/1938.md), while rewriting *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was further worked with more background material, during Tolkien's final proofreading in [1949](../_/1949.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The basis of "Concerning Pipe-weed" was a lengthy speech of Merry about pipe-weed in a draft of the chapter *[The Road to Isengard](../T/The_Road_to_Isengard.md)*, eventually removed and reworked.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The "Prologue" would include a description of hobbit gift-giving customs and the *[ribadyan](../R/Ribadyan.md)* (birthday celebrant) but it was dropped, being too lengthy.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b><a href="../_/2001.md" title="2001">2001</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the first scene, titled &#34;<a href="Prologue%253A_One_Ring_to_Rule_Them_All....md" title="Prologue: One Ring to Rule Them All...">Prologue: One Ring to Rule Them All...</a>&#34;, there is a brief description of how <a href="../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a> found the One Ring, clearly related to &#34;Of the Finding of the Ring&#34;.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>&#34;Concerning Hobbits&#34; is the name of the <a href="../C/Concerning_Hobbits_(scene).md" title="Concerning Hobbits (scene)">second scene</a> of the <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition).md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)">Extended Edition</a> wherein &#34;Concerning Hobbits&#34; is the first chapter of Bilbo&#39;s <a href="../R/Red_Book_of_Westmarch.md" title="Red Book of Westmarch">book</a>, in which he describes the nature of Hobbits. The audience hears Bilbo reading this aloud as a voiceover on top of scenes of life in <a href="../T/The_Shire.md" title="The Shire">The Shire</a>. The <a href="../C/Concerning_Hobbits_(soundtrack).md" title="Concerning Hobbits (soundtrack)">scene&#39;s soundtrack</a> composed by <a href="../H/Howard_Shore.md" title="Howard Shore">Howard Shore</a> is also called &#34;Concerning Hobbits&#34; and is used as a primary theme of the Shire.</dd></dl>
