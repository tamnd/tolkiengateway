<i><b>Pwyll Prince of Dyved</b></i> is the title of the first of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_Branches_of_the_Mabinogi" title="wikipedia:Four Branches of the Mabinogi">Four Branches</a> of the Welsh medieval manuscript <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mabinogion" title="wikipedia:Mabinogion">Mabinogion</a></i>. [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") worked on an edition and translation of *Pwyll*, which reached to include around the first fifth of the first Branch (Tolkien's edition is held at the [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> A discussion and presentation of (including a brief quote from) Tolkien's manuscript was provided by [Carl Phelpstead](/wiki/Carl_Phelpstead) in *[Tolkien and Wales: Language, Literature and Identity](/wiki/Tolkien_and_Wales "Tolkien and Wales")* (2011; cf. pp. 9, 11, 60-1, 139 n. 33). <span></span>
