Old **Pott** was, according to [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)'s poem "[Perry-the-Winkle](Perry-the-Winkle.md)", a [Mayor of Michel Delving](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md). He witnessed the meeting of [Mrs. Bunce](../M/Mrs._Bunce.md) with the [Lonely Troll](../L/Lonely_Troll.md) and he was in turn frightened. When he learned that the Troll was kind and generous, he and other [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) tried to visit him. But the Troll refused those who had refused his friendship.<sup id="cite_ref-Perry_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Perry-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is unknown if this character was based on an actual person, or just an invention by the poet.<sup id="cite_ref-Perry_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Perry-1">[1]</a></sup>
