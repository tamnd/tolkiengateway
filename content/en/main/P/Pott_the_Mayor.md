Old **Pott** was, according to [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)'s poem "[Perry-the-Winkle](/wiki/Perry-the-Winkle)", a [Mayor of Michel Delving](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving). He witnessed the meeting of [Mrs. Bunce](/wiki/Mrs._Bunce) with the [Lonely Troll](/wiki/Lonely_Troll) and he was in turn frightened. When he learned that the Troll was kind and generous, he and other [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) tried to visit him. But the Troll refused those who had refused his friendship.<sup id="cite_ref-Perry_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Perry-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is unknown if this character was based on an actual person, or just an invention by the poet.<sup id="cite_ref-Perry_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Perry-1">[1]</a></sup>
