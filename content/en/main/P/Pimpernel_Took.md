**Pimpernel Took** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Pimpernel was the second child of [Thain](../T/Thain.md) [Paladin Took II](Paladin_Took_II.md) and [Eglantine Banks](../E/Eglantine_Banks.md). She and her family were guests at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anagallis" title="wikipedia:Anagallis">Pimpernel</a> is a genus of the primrose family and its fruits resemble peppercorns. The name derives from Latin *pimpernella* meaning "little pepper".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
