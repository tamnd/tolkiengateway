**Ponies** were common beasts of burden in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), especially used by [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) and [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), whose small stature made the riding of [horses](/wiki/Horses "Horses") difficult.

## History {#History}

[Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) used ponies for the first part of their journey in the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor). They were loaded with all kinds of burdens, and the smallest one was for [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins). The ponies were captured by [Goblins](/wiki/Orcs#Goblins "Orcs") in the [Front Porch](/wiki/Front_Porch).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn) also used ponies and lent some of his to the Dwarves up to their way to [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood), provided that they would release them by the [Forest Gate](/wiki/Forest_Gate). Although some considered breaking their promise, [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) warned them that Beorn had been following them as a bear, and would be watching them. Reluctantly, they released the ponies and sent them back to Beorn.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The dwarves were provided ponies again by the [people](/wiki/Lake-men "Lake-men") of [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town) on their way to [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) had some ponies in his house at [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow). He had prepared five of them ([Sharp-ears](/wiki/Sharp-ears), [Bumpkin](/wiki/Bumpkin), [Wise-nose](/wiki/Wise-nose), [Swish-tail](/wiki/Swish-tail), and [White-socks](/wiki/White-socks), who at that time were unnamed) for [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and their friends on their way to [Bree](/wiki/Bree).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest), the ponies stayed with [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil)'s pony, [Fatty Lumpkin](/wiki/Fatty_Lumpkin), and became friends.

At Bree, spies let the ponies run to the wild to hinder the hobbits (eventually they returned to Fatty), so [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) bought one from [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny), a sick and mistreated pony, which Sam also named [Bill](/wiki/Bill_the_Pony "Bill the Pony").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Bill was released outside the [Doors of Durin](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin) as he could not follow the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) inside [Moria](/wiki/Moria).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> He found his way back to Bree.

Meriadoc was given the pony [Stybba](/wiki/Stybba) by [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Stybba carried Merry through the mountain passes from the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg) east and south to [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) and thence northward to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).

In [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), Frodo named his new pony [Strider](</wiki/Strider_(pony)> "Strider (pony)") after [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)'s nickname. With him, he rode back to [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> At Bree, the Hobbits found all their former ponies safe at the [*Prancing Pony*](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony "The Prancing Pony"), having returned there.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Frodo and Sam eventually rode Strider and Bill to the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) two years later.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
