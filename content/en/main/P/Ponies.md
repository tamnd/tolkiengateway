**Ponies** were common beasts of burden in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), especially used by [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) and [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), whose small stature made the riding of [horses](../H/Horses.md) difficult.

## History {#History}

[Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) used ponies for the first part of their journey in the [Quest of Erebor](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md). They were loaded with all kinds of burdens, and the smallest one was for [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md). The ponies were captured by [Goblins](../O/Orcs.md#Goblins) in the [Front Porch](../F/Front_Porch.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md) also used ponies and lent some of his to the Dwarves up to their way to [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md), provided that they would release them by the [Forest Gate](../F/Forest_Gate.md). Although some considered breaking their promise, [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) warned them that Beorn had been following them as a bear, and would be watching them. Reluctantly, they released the ponies and sent them back to Beorn.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The dwarves were provided ponies again by the [people](../L/Lake-men.md) of [Lake-town](../L/Lake-town.md) on their way to [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) had some ponies in his house at [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md). He had prepared five of them ([Sharp-ears](../S/Sharp-ears.md), [Bumpkin](../B/Bumpkin.md), [Wise-nose](../W/Wise-nose.md), [Swish-tail](../S/Swish-tail.md), and [White-socks](../W/White-socks.md), who at that time were unnamed) for [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and their friends on their way to [Bree](../B/Bree.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md), the ponies stayed with [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md)'s pony, [Fatty Lumpkin](../F/Fatty_Lumpkin.md), and became friends.

At Bree, spies let the ponies run to the wild to hinder the hobbits (eventually they returned to Fatty), so [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) bought one from [Bill Ferny](../B/Bill_Ferny.md), a sick and mistreated pony, which Sam also named [Bill](../B/Bill_the_Pony.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Bill was released outside the [Doors of Durin](../D/Doors_of_Durin.md) as he could not follow the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) inside [Moria](../M/Moria.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> He found his way back to Bree.

Meriadoc was given the pony [Stybba](../S/Stybba.md) by [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Stybba carried Merry through the mountain passes from the [Hornburg](../H/Hornburg.md) east and south to [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md) and thence northward to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).

In [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), Frodo named his new pony [Strider](<../S/Strider_(pony).md>) after [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md)'s nickname. With him, he rode back to [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> At Bree, the Hobbits found all their former ponies safe at the [*Prancing Pony*](../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md), having returned there.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Frodo and Sam eventually rode Strider and Bill to the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) two years later.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
