**Pinnath Gelin** or **Green Hills** was a fief<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and a line of hills north of Anfalas<sup id="cite_ref-UI_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-3">[3]</a></sup> between the River [Lefnui](/wiki/Lefnui) in the west and by the River [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond) in the east in the southwest of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the lord of this region was [Hirluin the Fair](/wiki/Hirluin "Hirluin"). He led three hundred of his soldiers to the defence of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), all dressed in green.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-5">[5]</a></sup> During the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields) Hirluin rode with [Húrin the Tall](</wiki/H%C3%BArin_(Warden_of_the_Keys)> "Húrin (Warden of the Keys)"), [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil) and the horsemen of Gondor rode eastward from Minas Tirith to the aid of [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) and the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim).<sup id="cite_ref-Battle2_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle2-6">[6]</a></sup> Hirluin did not survive the battle and never went back to the Green Hills of his home.<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Pinnath Gelin* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. It is usually translated as "Green Hills"<sup id="cite_ref-MT_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Battle2_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle2-6">[6]</a></sup> and sometimes as "Green Downs",<sup id="cite_ref-UI_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-3">[3]</a></sup> "Green Slopes", "Green Crests/Ridges"<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>, "Green-crests".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>, or "green ridges"<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>. The first word was the dialectical [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) or [Gondor Sindarin](/wiki/Gondor_Sindarin) plural *[pinnath](/wiki/Pinnath "Pinnath")* ("hill sides", "downs", "slopes", "crests", "ridges").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> The second was a [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") version of the plural form of the word *[calen](/wiki/Calen "Calen")* ("green").<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>
