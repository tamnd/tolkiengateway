**Peter Pracownik** ([19 October](../_/19_October.md), [1951](../_/1951.md) - [August 15](../A/August_15.md), [2024](../_/2024.md)) was a New Age / Fantasy artist and sculptor who was born in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol" title="wikipedia:Bristol">Bristol</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a> and lived most of his life in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glastonbury" title="wikipedia:Glastonbury">Glastonbury</a>, before moving to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Cornwall" title="wikipedia:North Cornwall">North Cornwall</a> later in life. He was an active artist for over 30 years since his professional debut in 1989, providing art for collectible cards, posters and music album covers.

In [1997](../_/1997.md), Pracownik created illustrations for the [Lord of the Rings Tarot Deck &amp; Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Tarot.md) along with artist Terry Donaldson and Mike Fitzgerald. In [2012](../_/2012.md), Donaldson and Pracownik collaborated again on [The Hobbit Tarot](../T/The_Hobbit_Tarot.md). He was commisioned to create a Lord Of The Rings poster for Wizard and Genius.

Pracownik had two children with his first wife, Julie:<sup id="cite_ref-BookBio_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BookBio-1">[1]</a></sup> Rebecca<sup id="cite_ref-BookBio_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BookBio-1">[1]</a></sup> and Oisin.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Peter and Julie separated in 1991.<sup id="cite_ref-BookBio_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BookBio-1">[1]</a></sup> He married artist Nicola-Claire Lydon in 2010<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and they co-owned the Another Green World art gallery and store.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
