**Poldor** was a [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) [man](/wiki/Men "Men") according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)*.

He was a peer of [Herendil](/wiki/Herendil), who complained to his father [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) that Poldor had called him *[Eärendel](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil "Eärendil")* the previous day by way of scorn or mockery.<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup> However, in the lastest draft, Poldor was omitted, and replaced by Herendil's numerous anonymous peers, with Herendil telling his father that his peers called him names such as *Terendul* (meaning "slender and dark") and accusing him of being a "half-Noldo."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Poldor* appears to be a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name, derived from *pol* in *polda* ("strong, burly").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
