**Poldor** was a [Númenórean](../N/Númenórean.md) [man](../M/Men.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Lost Road](../T/The_Lost_Road.md)*.

He was a peer of [Herendil](../H/Herendil.md), who complained to his father [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) that Poldor had called him *[Eärendel](../E/Eärendil.md)* the previous day by way of scorn or mockery.<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup> However, in the lastest draft, Poldor was omitted, and replaced by Herendil's numerous anonymous peers, with Herendil telling his father that his peers called him names such as *Terendul* (meaning "slender and dark") and accusing him of being a "half-Noldo."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Poldor* appears to be a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name, derived from *pol* in *polda* ("strong, burly").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
