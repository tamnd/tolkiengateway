**Polo Baggins** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Polo was the second child, and only son, of [Ponto Baggins](/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_I "Ponto Baggins I") and [Mimosa Bunce](/wiki/Mimosa_Bunce). In his turn, Polo also had a son and a daughter, [Posco](/wiki/Posco_Baggins "Posco Baggins") and [Prisca](/wiki/Prisca_Baggins "Prisca Baggins").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Polo* was meaningless in contemporary [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) [Westron](/wiki/Westron). It represented an original name *Pola*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
