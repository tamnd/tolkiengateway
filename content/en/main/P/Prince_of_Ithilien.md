The **Prince of Ithilien** was a title granted to [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md), the [Steward](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), by [Aragorn Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md), the [King](<../H/High_King_(Dúnedain).md>) of Gondor and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md), after the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the War of the Ring the Prince of Ithilien was the greatest noble in Gondor after the [Prince of Dol Amroth](Prince_of_Dol_Amroth.md). In his capacity as the Steward of Gondor, the Prince of Ithilien was the chief counsellor of the [Great Council of Gondor](../C/Council_of_Gondor.md). When the King of Gondor was absent, the Prince of Ithilien or the Prince of Dol Amroth acted as the military commander at home in Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-L244_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L244-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Prince of Ithilien lived in the hills of [Emyn Arnen](../E/Emyn_Arnen.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-1">[1]</a></sup> and held the title of [Lord of Emyn Arnen](../L/Lord_of_Emyn_Arnen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> He was guarded by the [White Company](../W/White_Company.md) who was also based in Emyn Arnen.<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the restoration of the realm of Gondor after the War of the Ring, the Prince of Ithilien was the march-warden of the main eastward outpost of Gondor. In that period his duties included clearing Ithilien of outlaws, remnants of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and the dreadful [Morgul Vale](../M/Morgul_Vale.md) as well as rehabilitating the land.<sup id="cite_ref-L244_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L244-2">[2]</a></sup>
