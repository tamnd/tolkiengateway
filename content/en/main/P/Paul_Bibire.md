**Paul Bibire** is an author and former lecturer at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews" title="wikipedia:University of St Andrews">University of St Andrews</a> in Scotland and [Cambridge University](/wiki/Cambridge "Cambridge"). His area is [Anglo-Saxon](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") and [Old Norse](/wiki/Old_Norse), and he has written many articles on these and related subjects. Bibire is also a keen enthusiast of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), whom he credits as the motivation for his academic career.
