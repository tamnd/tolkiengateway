**Prince of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth)** was a title used by a noble house of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## History {#History}

The Princes are the male heirs of the [House of Dol Amroth](/wiki/House_of_Dol_Amroth). That House was of noble [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") origin from the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age); they were [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) akin to the [Lords of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB) in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), and later [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) gave to their ruler the title of [Prince](/wiki/Prince).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The first Prince of Dol Amroth was [Galador](/wiki/Galador), who, according to legend, was the son of [Mithrellas](/wiki/Mithrellas), an Elf maid.<sup id="cite_ref-GalCel_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GalCel-2">[2]</a></sup> This supposed elven strain was noticed in [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil) many years later by [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
