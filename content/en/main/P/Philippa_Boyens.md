**Philippa Boyens** co-wrote the screenplays for [*The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and [*The Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)") with [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) and [Fran Walsh](/wiki/Fran_Walsh). She also co-produced *The Hobbit* (film series). Her son [Calum Gittins](/wiki/Calum_Gittins) played [Haleth](</wiki/Haleth_(film_character)> "Haleth (film character)"), son of [Háma](/wiki/H%C3%A1ma) in *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers")* and her daughter Phoebe played a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits").

- [2001](/wiki/2001) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.sefca.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">SEFCA Award</a></b>, Best Screenplay, Adapted, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*
- [2002](/wiki/2002) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.chlotrudis.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Chloturdis Award</a></b>, Best Screenplay - Adapted, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2002 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.cinemaclips.com/PFCS.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">PFCS Award</a></b>, Best Screenplay - Adaptation, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- [2003](/wiki/2003) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.dvdexclusive.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">DVD Exclusive Award</a></b>, Best Audio Commentary, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2003 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.sfwa.org/awards/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Nebula Award</a></b>, Best Script, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2003 - **Chloturdis Award**, Audience Award, Best Screenplay - Adapted, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*
- 2003 - **PFCS Award**, Best Screenplay - Adaptation, *Peter Jackson's The Two Towers*
- [2004](/wiki/2004) - **Nebula Award**, Best Script, *Peter Jackson's The Two Towers*
- 2004 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.oscars.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Academy Award</a></b>, Best Writing, Adapted Screenplay, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*
- 2004 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.bafta.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">BAFTA</a></b>, Best Screenplay - Adapted, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
- 2004 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.saturnawards.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Saturn Award</a></b>, Best Writing, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
- 2004 - **PFCS Award**, Best Screenplay - Adaptation, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
- 2004 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.ofcs.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">OFCS Award</a></b>, Best Screenplay, Adapted, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
