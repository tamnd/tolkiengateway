**Philippa Boyens** co-wrote the screenplays for [*The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) and [*The Hobbit* film series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>) with [Peter Jackson](Peter_Jackson.md) and [Fran Walsh](../F/Fran_Walsh.md). She also co-produced *The Hobbit* (film series). Her son [Calum Gittins](../C/Calum_Gittins.md) played [Haleth](<../H/Haleth_(film_character).md>), son of [Háma](../H/Háma.md) in *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* and her daughter Phoebe played a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md).

- 2002 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.cinemaclips.com/PFCS.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">PFCS Award</a></b>, Best Screenplay - Adaptation, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- [2003](../_/2003.md) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.dvdexclusive.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">DVD Exclusive Award</a></b>, Best Audio Commentary, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2003 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.sfwa.org/awards/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Nebula Award</a></b>, Best Script, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2003 - **Chloturdis Award**, Audience Award, Best Screenplay - Adapted, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*
- 2003 - **PFCS Award**, Best Screenplay - Adaptation, *Peter Jackson's The Two Towers*
- [2004](../_/2004.md) - **Nebula Award**, Best Script, *Peter Jackson's The Two Towers*
- 2004 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.oscars.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Academy Award</a></b>, Best Writing, Adapted Screenplay, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*
- 2004 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.bafta.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">BAFTA</a></b>, Best Screenplay - Adapted, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
