To flesh out the setting, [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing) introduces many new types of plants and herbs that were used for various purposes, beyond [lilies](/wiki/Lilies "Lilies"), [mosses](/wiki/Mosses "Mosses"), [pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed"), [reeds](/wiki/Reeds "Reeds"), and [water-lilies](/wiki/Water-lilies "Water-lilies").

[Aloe](/wiki/Aloe) is an adaptation-exclusive plant introduced in Middle-earth Role Playing, but which was later also used in another adaptation.

## Acána {#Acána}

<i><b>Acána</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Gurthmallen*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Malinuru*) was a small yellow-flowered herb found on rocky outcroppings above the treeline. The flower, ground into a paste with a little vinegar, produced an instantly lethal poison. If ground with water instead, the preparation induced a deep coma lasting days, with ironbine being the only antidote.<sup id="cite_ref-p83_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p83-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Akbutegé {#Akbutegé}

<i><b>Akbutegé</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Dordos*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Nornatussa*) was a laurel shrub that grew in the Elorna Wetlands of the distant south, noted for its gnarly black trunk. When its dark green leaves showed a pink underside in winter, herbalists gathered them and used them for healing.<sup id="cite_ref-firstpage_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-firstpage-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p83_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p83-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Alambas {#Alambas}

<i><b>Alambas</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Thargeber*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Narnass*), also known as *Spike*, was a succulent that grew as short gold grass on the Talath Mallen in Mirëdor. Its juices could be used to treat burns.<sup id="cite_ref-firstpage_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-firstpage-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Aldaka {#Aldaka}

<i><b>Aldaka</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Cenetholch*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Cenyasulca*), also known as *Baby-sitter*, was a plant that grew in cold mountain regions. Its most valuable part was its orange tuber-like root, which could be used to treat eye injuries.<sup id="cite_ref-p84_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p84-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Ancalthur {#Ancalthur}

<i><b>Ancalthur</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Henungol*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Liantehen*) was a flower that grew along cold freshwater shores. Itts white petals were edged in black, while its centre was gold and red. Grounding its stem into a powder and mixing with water, and subsequently leaving it overnight in a dead man's mouth, resulted in a poison favoured by assasins. It warped the victim's sense of time.<sup id="cite_ref-p84_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p84-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Arfandas {#Arfandas}

<i><b>Arfandas</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Lebellas*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Lempelassë*), also known as *Keltakukka*, was a flower that grew by shady stream banks. It was used in poultices to stimulate the healing of fractured bones.<sup id="cite_ref-firstpage_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-firstpage-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-firstpage_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-firstpage-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p85_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p85-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Arlan {#Arlan}

<i><b>Arlan</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Orluin*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Oroluin*), also known as *White Slipper*, was a blue-flowered mountain plant that was cultivated for its leaves, which were used in poultices to treat bruises and concussions.<sup id="cite_ref-firstpage_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-firstpage-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p85_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p85-5">[5]</a></sup>

<h2><span id="Arlan.27s_Slipper"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Arlan&#39;s_Slipper">Arlan&#39;s Slipper</span></h2>

<i><b>Arlan&#39;s Slipper</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Arlandal*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Ninguetal*) was a white wildflower that grew in woodlands and slopes of mountains. When its roots were dried and subsequently boiled, the vapours served as a decongestant.<sup id="cite_ref-firstpage_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-firstpage-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p85_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p85-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Arnuminas {#Arnuminas}

<i><b>Arnuminas</b></i> was a tall long-leaved garden plant. Its leaves were used in poultices and salves to treat bruises and sprains.<sup id="cite_ref-firstpage_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-firstpage-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Arroweed {#Arroweed}

<i><b>Arroweed</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Calembilin*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Laicapilin*), also called *Nura*, was a red-flowered plant grew in grass along forest edges. Its flame-shaped flowers yielded black, pea-sized seeds which restored health when eaten.<sup id="cite_ref-p86_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p86-7">[6]</a></sup>

## Árunya {#Árunya}

<i><b>Árunya</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Orroen*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") “Sunlight’s Trace”), also known as *Red Sleep* or *Orrain*, was a red flower that grew on warm hills. Juice from its roots induce an especially restful sleep, though it is difficult to extract.<sup id="cite_ref-secondpage_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-secondpage-8">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p86_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p86-7">[6]</a></sup>

## Atigax {#Atigax}

<i><b>Atigax</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Gailgened*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Alcenië*), called *Allsee*, *Firegaze*, *Punasalvia*, or *Metsästäjän Ystävä*, was a hardy, clumping grass found in cold scrubland. Many different-looking leaf forms sprouted from this plant. Burning the root resulted in a white powder forming in its center, which could be boiled in water to create a draught that protecred against blinding flashes of light for a while.<sup id="cite_ref-p87_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p87-9">[8]</a></sup>

## Avhail {#Avhail}

<i><b>Avhail</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Noryldos*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Malinyávë*) was a bush from [Saralainn](/wiki/Saralainn) and sometimes the rest of [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan). Its delicious fruits are used in a brandy named *biotaille*.<sup id="cite_ref-secondpage_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-secondpage-8">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p87_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p87-9">[8]</a></sup>

## Baldfoot {#Baldfoot}

<i><b>Baldfoot</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Rúdal*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Rúlatal*), also known as *Splort* or *Carahsá*, was a loww shrub that favoured deciduous woods near granite outcrops. Its exposed white roots gave the plant its name. Its black berries, that appear in early summer, would restore health when eaten, but eating more than one at a time would cause severe vertigo for several hours.<sup id="cite_ref-p88_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p88-10">[9]</a></sup>

## Barnaië {#Barnaië}

<i><b>Barnaië</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[note 2]</a></sup> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Cumidh*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Cumbusérë*) was a small yellow-flowered ground plant used in medicinal teas to treat nausea.<sup id="cite_ref-secondpage_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-secondpage-8">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p88_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p88-10">[9]</a></sup>

## Bitterwood {#Bitterwood}

<i><b>Bitterwood</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Celebrif*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Telperíma*), also known as *Ket*, was a large, tree-like shrub that grew in jungles and rain forests. Its rough, brown-green bark was streaked with silver, and its leaves had silver edges as well. Its tuberous root could be smoked in an elaborate process, which if consumed halted bleeding and cured organ damage.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup>

## Black Vines {#Black_Vines}

<i><b>Black Vines</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Oldur*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Mornolos*), also known as *Vision-makers*, were a dangerous weed found in [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur)'s Yfelwid, growing among the roots of great trees. It had a thin stem with a few black leaves. Long contact with the leaves caused a dream-like state, during which the plant would quickly grow around and try to smother the victim.<sup id="cite_ref-thirdpage_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-thirdpage-13">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-page90_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page90-14">[12]</a></sup>

## Bluedrake {#Bluedrake}

<i><b>Bluedrake</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Urli*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Úrelis*), also known as *Bluetongue*, was a blue marsh flower. Its vase-shaped flower was filled with nectar that trapped insects, and this nectar could also be used to treat severe burns.<sup id="cite_ref-page90_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page90-14">[12]</a></sup>

## Bodezlist {#Bodezlist}

<i><b>Bodezlist</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Sigillas*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Sicillassë*), also known as *Daggerleaf*, was a thorny bush in [Far Harad](/wiki/Far_Harad). Its blade-like leaves were easily spotted during the day, but at night they could cause serious injuries. The plant's heart consisted of succulent water-holding tissue, which experienced desert travellers could forage.<sup id="cite_ref-thirdpage_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-thirdpage-13">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-page90_14-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page90-14">[12]</a></sup>

## Boglock {#Boglock}

<i><b>Boglock</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Aechaew*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Sangwaica*), also known as *Snakegrass*, was a thorned vine that grew in bogs, swamps and marshes. When the vine was touched, it would grow and coil around whatever touched it. Its black thorns would inject a toxin that caused muscular spasms.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[13]</a></sup>

## Bog-land Grass {#Bog-land_Grass}

<i><b>Bog-land Grass</b></i> was an invasive weed that had spread from the marshes of [The Wold](/wiki/The_Wold) and [Brown Lands](/wiki/Brown_Lands) to the Plains of [Wilderland](/wiki/Rhovanion "Rhovanion"). This is because it didn't require as much water as other marsh plants. If pulled loose, the plant could be woven into waterproof ropes and baskets.<sup id="cite_ref-thirdpage_13-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-thirdpage-13">[11]</a></sup>

## Boneset {#Boneset}

<i><b>Boneset</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Lasgorn*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Axosulca*) was a low bushy plant found only in [Blackroot Vale](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale). Its roots helped heal fractured bones.<sup id="cite_ref-fourthpage_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fourthpage-16">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-page91_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page91-17">[15]</a></sup>

## Boskone {#Boskone}

<i><b>Boskone</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Amlothluin*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Aldaseron*) was a blue orchid found in the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest) and [Eryn Vorn](/wiki/Eryn_Vorn), where it grew among leaves of tall trees. Its leaved could be dried and ground into an addictive powder that enhances the senses.<sup id="cite_ref-fourthpage_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fourthpage-16">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-page91_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page91-17">[15]</a></sup>

## Boxdeath {#Boxdeath}

<i><b>Boxdeath</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Cennilloth*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Cantillótë*), also called *Blackbox* or *Blackwish*, was a small spiky bush that grew in arid deserts. Each year it produced a single small black, box-shaped flower. It was used as a remedy of last resort: when ingested it induced a three-day coma that either fully healed the patient or ended their life.<sup id="cite_ref-page91_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page91-17">[15]</a></sup>

## Breldiar {#Breldiar}

<i><b>Breldiar</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Saunor*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Náresáva*), also called *Bréala* or *Sinitähti*, was a black-flowered vine found in volcanic cliffs. Consumption of a flower induced intense euphoria which might improve magical prowess.<sup id="cite_ref-page92_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page92-18">[16]</a></sup>

## Bright Blue Eyes {#Bright_Blue_Eyes}

<i><b>Bright Blue Eyes</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Luingil*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Luinelen*), also known as *Bluestar* or *Blu* was a tiny wildflower that grew in the plains of Talath Harroch (Southern Rhovanion). Tea brewed of these flowers was said to enhance one's vision.<sup id="cite_ref-fourthpage_16-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fourthpage-16">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-page92_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page92-18">[16]</a></sup>

## Butterfoot {#Butterfoot}

<i><b>Butterfoot</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Melindal*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Malintal*), also known as Putot, was a small meadow flower with bright yellow petals. When crushed and administered as an eye preparation over the course of a week, the herb could restore sight lost through injury.<sup id="cite_ref-page92_18-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page92-18">[16]</a></sup>

## Caj {#Caj}

<i><b>Caj</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Gurthand*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Andanuru*), also known as *Kagg*, was a tough red-leaved shrub found in the woods of Gaj in Far Harad. Its resin was a core ingredient of an addictive tea.<sup id="cite_ref-fourthpage_16-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fourthpage-16">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-page92_18-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page92-18">[16]</a></sup>

## Carefree mustard {#Carefree_mustard}

<i><b>Carefree mustard</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Sauruin*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Roinasáva*), also known as *Roan Mustard*, was an offshoot of a crop grown long ago in the fields of Gladden, Thorlorién and the Undeeps. The seeds were used to soothe pains.<sup id="cite_ref-fourthpage_16-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fourthpage-16">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p93_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p93-19">[17]</a></sup>

## Carlong {#Carlong}

<i><b>Carlong</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Heavy Red,” [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Carnilunga*), also known as *Bursis* or *Bonis*, was a red-flowered plant that grew in temperate grasslands. Its hairy brown stalk could be chopped and boiled into a thick soup which aided with bone healing.<sup id="cite_ref-p93_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p93-19">[17]</a></sup>

## Caradhrain {#Caradhrain}

<i><b>Caradhrain</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Red Border,” [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Carniréna*), also known as *Heat-bane*, *Flame-thief*, or *Kilmakur*, was a tropical grass notable for red margins on its leaves. It had a large tuber which, when prepared by both an internal draught and an exterior oil extracted from a second tuber blackened in fire, protected against non-magical heat and flame.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[18]</a></sup>

## Cashdir {#Cashdir}

<i><b>Cashdir</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Malhen*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Hendemalin*), also known as *Argsbargies*, was a small white desert flower. Eaten raw it counteracted some muscle-paralysing poisons, but it was addictive, with repeated use leading to lethargy and yellowing of the eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[19]</a></sup>

## Cicino {#Cicino}

<i><b>Cicino</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Onodlas*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Ontolassë*) was a leafy plant exclusively found in [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest), where [Ents](/wiki/Ents) used it to cure blights by soaking the herb in spring water for ten days. It resembled cabbages.<sup id="cite_ref-fourthpage_16-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fourthpage-16">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-page95_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page95-22">[20]</a></sup>

## Cockfan {#Cockfan}

<i><b>Cockfan</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Gwaethar*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Súrisara*), also known as *Bushwhip* or *Sárgrass*, was a greenish-yellow palm frond that grew in arid scrubland. When boiled into a bitter tea the leaves eased nausea. The whole plant could also be dried and used as a fan.<sup id="cite_ref-page95_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page95-22">[20]</a></sup>

## Coraco {#Coraco}

<i><b>Coraco</b></i>([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Lisdos*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Lissetussa*) was a bush in the Forest of Tears in Far Harad. Its berries were the basis of an expensive juice to treat wounds.<sup id="cite_ref-fifthpage_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fifthpage-23">[21]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-page95_22-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page95-22">[20]</a></sup>

## Creagach-Chnàimh {#Creagach-Chnàimh}

<i><b>Creagach-Chnàimh</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Carond*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Ondoinatar*), also known as *Craeg-Cerflun, Stone-bone, also known as* Stone-broke, was a laurel-like bush found in Blackroot Vale and the White Mountains. It had a silvery bark, and its blue-green leaves bore golden flowers in the spring and the autumn. Squeezing the leaves releases a poisonous mist, which can be harvested by boiling the leaves or roots.<sup id="cite_ref-fifthpage_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fifthpage-23">[21]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-page95_22-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page95-22">[20]</a></sup>

## Cuildolthor {#Cuildolthor}

<i><b>Cuildolthor</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Lifebringer,” [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Cuiletultar*), also known as *Chebkuilë*, was a herb that clung as a moss to the highest nooks and crannies of the Grey Mountains. Its colours changed with its life cycle: green in summer (a very potent healer with a high risk of amnesia) and blue-green in spring (fatal). It had a silvery underside year-round.<sup id="cite_ref-page96_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page96-24">[22]</a></sup>

## Culan {#Culan}

<i><b>Culan</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Urinc*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Urincë*), also known as *Karlan*, was a vine-like plant found near watering holes in arid regions. It had light-green vines and red-edged arrowhead-shaped leaves. Its roots could be processed into a foul-tasting powdered drug that relieved spasms and some poisons.<sup id="cite_ref-fifthpage_23-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fifthpage-23">[21]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-page96_24-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page96-24">[22]</a></sup>

## Curfalaka {#Curfalaka}

<i><b>Curfalaka</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Nimbeg*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Ninquipica*), also known as *Pogalaka* or *Meriasë*, was a fruit-bearing bush that grew in the swamps of Pel's jungles and woods. Its round purple fruits were said to heal muscle injuries.<sup id="cite_ref-fifthpage_23-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fifthpage-23">[21]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-page96_24-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page96-24">[22]</a></sup>

## Curuloth {#Curuloth}

<i><b>Curuloth</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Cunning Blossom,” [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Curwelótë*), was a small pale-blue flower that grew in wetlands. When combined in salves or injected preparations it could enable herbs normally effective only when eaten to work as a salve or injection.<sup id="cite_ref-page97_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page97-25">[23]</a></sup>

## Dagmather {#Dagmather}

Dried and ground into powder its stalks served as an oral poison, mixed with oil and slowly heated it caused slowly-healing blisters that could only be treated with stag’s purse.<sup id="cite_ref-p135_83-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p135-83">[77]</a></sup>

## Sindiluin {#Sindiluin}

<i><b>Sindiluin</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Mithluin*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") “Grey-blue”), also known as *Sanso* or *Sindoluin*, was a small blue meadow flower. Through a complex process, skilled healers could prepare Sindiluin into a coagulant capable of halting severe bleeding.<sup id="cite_ref-14thpage_35-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14thpage-35">[32]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p135_83-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p135-83">[77]</a></sup>

## Siran {#Siran}

<i><b>Siran</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Eithanem*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Nastanengwë*), also known as *Ragarlik*, was a plant found in semi-arid grasslands. Its brownish bulb resembled a garlic, and its cloves could heal organ damage, though it also caused a mild skin disease consisting of green pussy sores on the hands and face.<sup id="cite_ref-p136_84-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p136-84">[78]</a></sup>

## Siriena {#Siriena}

<i><b>Siriena</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Fernhebor*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Loicotir*), also known as *Keeper of the Dead*, was a pallid, short grass growing in semi-arid grasslands. It was boiled into a fluid that in skilled hands aided the embalming process.<sup id="cite_ref-p136_84-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p136-84">[78]</a></sup>

## Snapcane {#Snapcane}

<i><b>Snapcane</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Lothmalen*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Lossemalin*), also known as *Popper*, was a segmented vine found in cold northern climes. Ground snapcane stems are used in treatments for the disease shutinis.<sup id="cite_ref-p137_85-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p137-85">[79]</a></sup>

## Snowbane {#Snowbane}

<i><b>Snowbane</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Seron-ethuil*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Tuilendil*), also known as *Spring-friend*, was a small herb found in the shadows of cliffs in colder climates. Its leaves warmed the the consumer and restored health.<sup id="cite_ref-p137_85-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p137-85">[79]</a></sup>

## Snowdragon {#Snowdragon}

<i><b>Snowdragon</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Lothlug*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Losselócë*), also known as *Mindmaker* or *Jruthick*, was a hardy vine found in mountains. A brew mixing its stem and true tarnas was said to cure the disease juth.<sup id="cite_ref-p137_85-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p137-85">[79]</a></sup>

## Splayfoot Goodwort {#Splayfoot_Goodwort}

<i><b>Splayfoot Goodwort</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Linithil*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Linisil*), also known simply as *Goodwort*, was a tall marsh plant whose seed pods contained black seeds. Consumed, they bolstered confidence in the good but disoriented the wicked. They were found in the Gladden Fields and the bogs of the Undeeps.<sup id="cite_ref-14thpage_35-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14thpage-35">[32]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-86" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-86">[80]</a></sup>

<h2><span id="Stag.27s_Purse"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Stag&#39;s_Purse">Stag&#39;s Purse</span></h2>

<i><b>Stag&#39;s Purse</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Calpharas*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Arassecalpa*), also known as *Treebladder*, was a vine found in deciduous forests. As its made it way up the trunk of a tree and along its branchesm it developed a fuzzy brown bladder with a white flower. The inside of this bladder could be used to treat burns caused by the poision silmirë.<sup id="cite_ref-p138_87-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p138-87">[81]</a></sup>

## Stonelance {#Stonelance}

<i><b>Stonelance</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Angolf*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Angolva*), also known as *Ironstem*, grew near rocky outcrops in coniferous woods. Its light stem was as strong as iron for several years, and it saw lots of use because of this property. The flower could be ground into a paste that reduced fevers and cured colds. Its leaves were used in a remedy for the disease vemák.<sup id="cite_ref-p138_87-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p138-87">[81]</a></sup>

## Súrinië {#Súrinië}

<i><b>Súrinië</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-88" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-88">[note 7]</a></sup> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Gwaenir*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") “Wind-tear”), was a bush found along temperate stream beds. Its red berries could be crushed to act as smelling salts, because of their pungent odour.<sup id="cite_ref-14thpage_35-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14thpage-35">[32]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-89" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-89">[82]</a></sup>

## Sweet Sorrel {#Sweet_Sorrel}

<i><b>Sweet Sorrel</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Nellas*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Nellassë*), also known as *Greater Sorrel*, was a woodland plant, which had white bell-shaped flowers and trefoil leaves. Heating the leaves and inhaling the steam served as an antidote to many poisons.<sup id="cite_ref-14thpage_35-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14thpage-35">[32]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p139_90-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p139-90">[83]</a></sup>

## Sweetstar {#Sweetstar}

<i><b>Sweetstar</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Glisgil*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Lisselen*), also known as *Quickheal* or *Momatar*, was a mountain-side bush found in coniferous forests. Its delicious yellow star-shaped fruits were used in baked goods. A preparation from Sweetstar's stem induced a deep refreshing sleep.<sup id="cite_ref-p139_90-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p139-90">[83]</a></sup>

## Tartella {#Tartella}

<i><b>Tartella</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Immabron*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Mapindo*), also known as *Tar-web* or *Tartec*, was a swamp herb found in the Gwathló wetlands. It induced euphoria and was highly addictive, with dangerous withdrawal symptoms. It resembled pipeweed, though it was much smaller.<sup id="cite_ref-15thpage_91-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15thpage-91">[84]</a></sup> A more purified form called *Tartec* had both stronger effects and stronger side-effects.<sup id="cite_ref-14thpage_35-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14thpage-35">[32]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p139_90-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p139-90">[83]</a></sup>

## Telek {#Telek}

<i><b>Telek</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Nestachel*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Nestahellë*), also known as *Icebane*, visually differed from strawberry plant only in that its leaves are smooth. It grew in the tundra, and appropriately, its berries helped treat cold-related injuries such as frostbite.<sup id="cite_ref-p140_92-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p140-92">[85]</a></sup>

## Terbas {#Terbas}

<i><b>Terbas</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Nimros*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Ninquerossë*), also known as *Bas*, was a low shrub found in moist deciduous woods. Wrapped tightly and doused with hot water around an affected limb, its long white-spotted leaves hastened nerve healing.<sup id="cite_ref-p140_92-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p140-92">[85]</a></sup>

## Thurl {#Thurl}

<i><b>Thurl</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Hadron*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Hatar*), also known as *Thorl*, *Tharl*, *Thell*, and *Thile*, resembled a small red-tipped onion plant. Many of its names are onomatopoeia for the sounds one makes when vomiting, which happens when not eaten on an empty stomach. When eaten correctly it restores some health. Hillmen also call it *“Mae e’n gwneud fy mod i'n glaf. Lladdwch fe.”*, meaning “He makes me sick. Kill him”. This was said by an ancient chieftain who suffered from a medicine man who treated him with the plant.<sup id="cite_ref-p140_92-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p140-92">[85]</a></sup>

## Touch-grass {#Touch-grass}

<i><b>Touch-grass</b></i> was a prairie plant from southern Rhovanian that changed shape based on who touched it, allowing experienced scouts to ascertain who came through the area. It was inedible.<sup id="cite_ref-15thpage_91-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15thpage-91">[84]</a></sup>

## True Tarnas {#True_Tarnas}

<i><b>True Tarnas</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Arthond*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Arasunda*), also known as *Greater Tarnas*, was a flower from the Suza Sumar of Far Harad whose bulbs, when boiled, healed organ damage. They were hard to find and often confused with [Hopper Mushrooms](/wiki/Mushrooms#Portrayal_in_adaptations "Mushrooms").<sup id="cite_ref-15thpage_91-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15thpage-91">[84]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p141_93-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p141-93">[86]</a></sup>

## Tulaxar {#Tulaxar}

<i><b>Tulaxar</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Finhereg*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Sercefin*), also known as *Raybled*, was a healer’s garden herb. Its brewed leaves acted as a styptic, stopping bleeding quickly.<sup id="cite_ref-15thpage_91-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15thpage-91">[84]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p141_93-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p141-93">[86]</a></sup>

## Tyr-Fira {#Tyr-Fira}

<i><b>Tyr-Fira</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Gwinguil*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Vinyacuilë*), also known as *Lifegiver*, was a herb of the White Mountains. According to legends it could be life-giving if a petal is put in the deceased's mouth.<sup id="cite_ref-15thpage_91-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15thpage-91">[84]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p141_93-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p141-93">[86]</a></sup>

## Ul-Naza {#Ul-Naza}

<i><b>Ul-Naza</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Baladhroen*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Valarunya*), was a rare desert shrub said to only grow where a Vala had walked. Dried and brewed as a tea, it induced euphoria and allowed kinsfolk to share their dreams. It was very addictive.<sup id="cite_ref-p142_95-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p142-95">[88]</a></sup>

<i><b>Urána</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Lasdol*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Lassecar*), also known as *Ána*, was a leafy herb that grew in temperate grasslands. Its base resembled a cabbage, but in the middle it grew a stalk with purple flowers. Its inner leaves were used to make a poisonous paste that damaged the victim, with no known antidote.<sup id="cite_ref-p142_95-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p142-95">[88]</a></sup>

## Usquelótë {#Usquelótë}

<i><b>Usquelótë</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Osphalab*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") “Smoke-flower”) was a small white summer flower found in Dunfearan. It had blood-red roots and red-edged leaves. It could be dried and smoked as a relaxant.<sup id="cite_ref-p142_95-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p142-95">[88]</a></sup>

## Vinuk {#Vinuk}

<i><b>Vinuk</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Erchrim*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Linercëa*), also known as *Vasha*, was a low spiny bush found in semi-arid grasslands. When chopped and simmered, the root yields a thick brew that granted relief from being stunned.<sup id="cite_ref-p143_96-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p143-96">[89]</a></sup>

## Vinyanestalë {#Vinyanestalë}

<i><b>Vinyanestalë</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Gwinnestad*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") “New Healing”), also known as *Oltack* or *Meristark*, was a light-blue flowered herb found near sub-arcitict seawater. Ingesting it stabilized the patient's condition and allowed all healing magics and herbs to work at full potency.<sup id="cite_ref-p143_96-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p143-96">[89]</a></sup>

## Vrel {#Vrel}

<i><b>Vrel</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Norethar*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Nuriesalquë*), also known as *Runningweed*, was a small temperate meadow plant. Contact with its oily trianguler leaves contracted the disease grelnixar.<sup id="cite_ref-p143_96-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p143-96">[89]</a></sup>

## Welwal {#Welwal}

<i><b>Welwal</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-97" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-97">[note 8]</a></sup> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Corollas*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Corollassë*), was a desert shrub with spherical leaves. These leaves reduced the duration of being stunned when placed in the mouth.<sup id="cite_ref-tenthpage_43-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tenthpage-43">[39]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p144_98-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p144-98">[90]</a></sup>

<i><b>White Mountain Poppy</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Orluth*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Orolicé*), also known as *White Lotus*, was a plant found solely in the eponymous mountains. Dried and powdered juice of its unripe seed was made into a drug, which induced trances that aided magic. It was addictive and debilitating in the long term.<sup id="cite_ref-16thpage_99-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16thpage-99">[91]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p144_98-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p144-98">[90]</a></sup>

<i><b>White-Berried Yew</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Nimgurth*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Nuruninguë*), also known as *White Death*, was a shrub found in Eriador’s coniferous forests, especially Rhudaur. Its ripe pink berries caused painless death and were used historically in ritual suicides.<sup id="cite_ref-16thpage_99-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16thpage-99">[91]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p144_98-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p144-98">[90]</a></sup>

## Wifwif {#Wifwif}

<i><b>Wifwif</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Tuthollen*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Holdatuima*) was a small nocturnal grassland flower. Pollen scraped from an open flower could be inhaled to induce a deep sleep, though at higher doses risked comas.<sup id="cite_ref-p145_100-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p145-100">[92]</a></sup>

<i><b>Wight Orchid</b></i> was a mystical herb from the high meadows in the White Mountains. Its root, when eaten, allowed the spirit to leave the body and travel afar. Used in ancient rituals, it was highly dangerous.<sup id="cite_ref-17thpage_101-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17thpage-101">[93]</a></sup>

## Winclamit {#Winclamit}

<i><b>Winclamit</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Calengil*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Laicelen*), also known as *Bloodkeep*, was a vine from the White Mountains, which grew among its spruce forests. Its boiled fruit healed even grievous wounds.<sup id="cite_ref-16thpage_99-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16thpage-99">[91]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p145_100-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p145-100">[92]</a></sup>

## Witan {#Witan}

<i><b>Witan</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Puilas*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Piutalassë*) was a red-leafed plant of the Forest of Tears in Far Harad. Its leaves, chewed in battle by the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Bozishnarod&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bozishnarod (page does not exist)">Bozishnarod</a>, relieved stunning effects. This led to them given it the name *Drugo-sreca* (second chance).<sup id="cite_ref-17thpage_101-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17thpage-101">[93]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p145_100-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p145-100">[92]</a></sup>

## Wolfstooth {#Wolfstooth}

<i><b>Wolfstooth</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Draugarch*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Rácarca*), also known as *Wolf-fang*, was a woodland plant with poisonous roots. Deep inhalation of Yaran greatly heightened smell and taste. <i><b>Zaganzar</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Henhaew*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Hendesangwa*), also known as *Zagána*, was a woody vine found near temperate mountains. The large white taproot yielded an oily liquid that, when administered orally or by blade, dealt rapid damage and caused loss of sight. The sigh loss could be counteracted with antidotes such as Aldaka or Nightfoot.<sup id="cite_ref-p147_103-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p147-103">[95]</a></sup>
