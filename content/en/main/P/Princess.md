**Princess**, also known as **The New Princess and Girl**, was a weekly magazine aimed at girls, published by Fleetway. From [10 October](/wiki/10_October), [1964](/wiki/1964) to [16 January](/wiki/16_January), [1965](/wiki/1965), it featured an abridged, fifteen part serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.

Each episode was published on two pages, and featured five images by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ferguson_Dewar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ferguson Dewar (page does not exist)">Ferguson Dewar</a>.
