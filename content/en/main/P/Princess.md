**Princess**, also known as **The New Princess and Girl**, was a weekly magazine aimed at girls, published by Fleetway. From [10 October](../_/10_October.md), [1964](../_/1964.md) to [16 January](../_/16_January.md), [1965](../_/1965.md), it featured an abridged, fifteen part serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.

Each episode was published on two pages, and featured five images by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ferguson_Dewar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ferguson Dewar (page does not exist)">Ferguson Dewar</a>.
