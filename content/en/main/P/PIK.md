**PIK** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") without any given signification, except for what can be determined from its derivatives.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

Before arriving at the form PIK, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") experimented with the form PEK.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin"): *pika* ("small spot, dot")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *peg* ("small spot, dot")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin"): *pikina* ("tiny")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *pigen* ("tiny")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
