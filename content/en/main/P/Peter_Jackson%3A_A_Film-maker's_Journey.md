<i><b>Peter Jackson: A Film-maker&#39;s Journey</b></i> is a biography of [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson), written by [Brian Sibley](/wiki/Brian_Sibley).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This work presents an authorised and fully illustrated insight into the life and career of the award-winning director, from his childhood film projects up to &#34;King Kong&#34;, together with Jackson&#39;s revealing personal account of his six-year quest to film &#34;<a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)">The Lord of the Rings</a>&#34;. 
</p><p>Once, Peter Jackson was a name unknown to all but a small band of loyal fans and fellow film-makers. Now, he is the newest member of Hollywood&#39;s elite fellowship, with his name on the most successful movie trilogy of all time.
</p><p>Written with Jackson&#39;s full participation, this extensive biography, illustrated with never-before-seen photos from Jackson&#39;s personal collection, tells the inside story of how a <a href="/wiki/New_Zealand" title="New Zealand">New Zealander</a> became Hollywood&#39;s hottest property - from the early cult classics, through Academy Award -winning success with Kate Winslet&#39;s &#34;Heavenly Creatures&#34;, the abandoned &#34;King Kong&#34; remake, and the filming of &#34;The Lord of the Rings&#34;, a project which was abandoned two years into pre-production, rejected by most of the other studios and then picked up by <a href="/wiki/New_Line_Cinema" title="New Line Cinema">New Line Cinema</a> in the biggest gamble in film history.
</p><p>Drawing upon interviews with fifty of Peter Jackson&#39;s colleagues and contemporaries, author Brian Sibley paints a portrait of a true auteur, a man gifted with single-minded determination and an artist&#39;s vision. Jackson himself is both revealing and insightful about his entire film-making life, from his first childhood steps filming in Super 8 to the grand realisation of his life&#39;s dream: &#34;King Kong&#34;. Together, these joint narratives provide a truly unique and compelling insight into one of the finest cinematic minds at work today.
</p>
</blockquote>
