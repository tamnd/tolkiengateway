**Primula Brandybuck** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2920](/wiki/Third_Age_2920 "Third Age 2920") - [2980](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980")) was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins)' mother.

## History {#History}

Primula was the youngest daughter<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup> of [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck) and [Mirabella Took](/wiki/Mirabella_Took). She married [Drogo Baggins](/wiki/Drogo_Baggins), with whom she had one child, [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In late [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1380](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980"), she and her husband drowned when they went boating on the [Brandywine River](/wiki/Baranduin "Baranduin").<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primula" title="wikipedia:Primula">Primula</a> is a genus of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primulaceae" title="wikipedia:Primulaceae">primrose family</a> of plants. It is the Latin name of the primrose and means "little first" because it flowers in early spring.
