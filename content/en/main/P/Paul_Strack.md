**Paul Strack** is a Tolkien computational linguist. He is the creator of *[Eldamo](../E/Eldamo.md)*, the Elvish Data Model, launched in 2008, which is a lexicon of all of Tolkien’s invented languages.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
