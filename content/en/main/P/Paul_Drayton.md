**Paul Drayton** (born [28 December](/wiki/28_December) [1944](/wiki/1944)) is a British musician who composed the [1967 stage adaptation of *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_stage_adaptation)> "The Hobbit (1967 stage adaptation)") with [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter).

Drayton is the Musical Director of Duchy Opera in Cornwall.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
