**Paul Drayton** (born [28 December](../_/28_December.md) [1944](../_/1944.md)) is a British musician who composed the [1967 stage adaptation of *The Hobbit*](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1967_stage_adaptation).md>) with [Humphrey Carpenter](../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md).

Drayton is the Musical Director of Duchy Opera in Cornwall.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
