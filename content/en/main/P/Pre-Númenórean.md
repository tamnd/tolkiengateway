**Pre-Númenórean** is a term used to refer to several [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) tongues and dialects in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) that predate [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) settlement. Its speakers were the [Pre-Númenóreans](Pre-Númenóreans.md), including the folk of [Agar](../A/Agar.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Drúedain](../D/Drúedain.md), the [Men of Dunharrow](../O/Oathbreakers.md), the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md)<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-2">[2]</a></sup> and several other inhabitants of [Lamedon](../L/Lamedon.md) and [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CE51_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CE51-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Roots {#Roots}

Pre-Númenórean languages derived from the settlers who were separated relatives of the [House of Haleth](../H/House_of_Haleth.md). Their members stayed back in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) while the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) migrated to the west during the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Drú_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drú-4">[4]</a></sup>

Their language was foreign to [Taliska](../T/Taliska.md), the language of the other two tribes, and as a result, the evolved languages became distinctly alien from [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md),<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-2">[2]</a></sup> being closer to the tongue of the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Drú_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drú-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Survival {#Survival}

The only pre-Númenórean tongues that survived into the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) as active languages were [Dunlendish](../D/Dunlendish.md)<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-2">[2]</a></sup> and the [Drúedain](../D/Drúedain.md) tongue.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> For the former, it was the result of a long enmity and a reluctance to speak [Westron](../W/Westron.md),<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HD_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HD-6">[6]</a></sup> while in the latter case it was isolation.<sup id="cite_ref-Drú_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drú-4">[4]</a></sup>

The pre-Númenórean tongue of the [Ethir](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md) and [Pelargir](Pelargir.md) merged with [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) into Westron.<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-2">[2]</a></sup> Under the Númenórean dominion, the language only survived in placenames and personal names, such as [Eilenach](../E/Eilenach.md),<sup id="cite_ref-CE51_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CE51-3">[3]</a></sup> [Rimmon](../M/Min-Rimmon.md) and [Forlong](../F/Forlong.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
