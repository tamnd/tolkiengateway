**Pre-Númenórean** is a term used to refer to several [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) tongues and dialects in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) that predate [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") settlement. Its speakers were the [Pre-Númenóreans](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans), including the folk of [Agar](/wiki/Agar),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain), the [Men of Dunharrow](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers"), the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings)<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-2">[2]</a></sup> and several other inhabitants of [Lamedon](/wiki/Lamedon) and [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-CE51_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CE51-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Roots {#Roots}

Pre-Númenórean languages derived from the settlers who were separated relatives of the [House of Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth). Their members stayed back in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) while the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) migrated to the west during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-Drú_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drú-4">[4]</a></sup>

Their language was foreign to [Taliska](/wiki/Taliska), the language of the other two tribes, and as a result, the evolved languages became distinctly alien from [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic),<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-2">[2]</a></sup> being closer to the tongue of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin).<sup id="cite_ref-Drú_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drú-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Survival {#Survival}

The only pre-Númenórean tongues that survived into the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) as active languages were [Dunlendish](/wiki/Dunlendish)<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-2">[2]</a></sup> and the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain) tongue.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> For the former, it was the result of a long enmity and a reluctance to speak [Westron](/wiki/Westron),<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HD_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HD-6">[6]</a></sup> while in the latter case it was isolation.<sup id="cite_ref-Drú_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drú-4">[4]</a></sup>

The pre-Númenórean tongue of the [Ethir](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin "Ethir Anduin") and [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) merged with [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) into Westron.<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-2">[2]</a></sup> Under the Númenórean dominion, the language only survived in placenames and personal names, such as [Eilenach](/wiki/Eilenach),<sup id="cite_ref-CE51_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CE51-3">[3]</a></sup> [Rimmon](/wiki/Min-Rimmon "Min-Rimmon") and [Forlong](/wiki/Forlong).<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
