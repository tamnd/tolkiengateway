The **Phial of Galadriel**, or **star-glass** or **Lady's glass**, was the [gift](../G/Gifts_of_Galadriel.md) that [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) bestowed to [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md).

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Tim_Kirk_-_Galadriel.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7b/Tim_Kirk_-_Galadriel.jpg/300px-Tim_Kirk_-_Galadriel.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="385" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7b/Tim_Kirk_-_Galadriel.jpg/450px-Tim_Kirk_-_Galadriel.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7b/Tim_Kirk_-_Galadriel.jpg/600px-Tim_Kirk_-_Galadriel.jpg 2x" data-file-width="973" data-file-height="1250"/></a><figcaption><i>Galadriel</i> by <a href="../T/Tim_Kirk.md" title="Tim Kirk">Tim Kirk</a></figcaption></figure>

Galadriel gave Frodo this gift upon the Fellowship's departure from [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-1">[1]</a></sup> It would stay among his possessions unused until reaching the edge of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).

Soon after the [Breaking of the Fellowship](../B/Breaking_of_the_Fellowship.md), the night fell upon the [Three Hunters](../T/Three_Hunters.md), so [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) wished that the Lady had given to them a light like Frodo's, but [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) remarked that Frodo would need it more.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

As Frodo, [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) approached the [Stairs of Cirith Ungol](../S/Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol.md), Frodo saw a great mass of cavalrymen led by the [Lord of the Nazgûl](../W/Witch-king.md). When the Witch-king stopped, Frodo feared they had been spotted; against his will, Frodo moves his hand towards the [ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md). Instead, he touched the phial - which he had forgotten - and the Witch-king continued on his way. The next morning, he made use of the phial again while leaving [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md) behind.<sup id="cite_ref-Stairs_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stairs-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:706–708</sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/I/Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Samwise_the_Stouthearted.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/9c/Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Samwise_the_Stouthearted.jpg/300px-Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Samwise_the_Stouthearted.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="234" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/9c/Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Samwise_the_Stouthearted.jpg/450px-Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Samwise_the_Stouthearted.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/9c/Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Samwise_the_Stouthearted.jpg/600px-Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Samwise_the_Stouthearted.jpg 2x" data-file-width="4000" data-file-height="3126"/></a><figcaption><i>Samwise the Stouthearted</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Irvin_Rodriguez.md" title="Category:Images by Irvin Rodriguez">Irvin Rodriguez</a></figcaption></figure>

Frodo used the phial to fend off the attacks of [Shelob](../S/Shelob.md), but he was eventually poisoned by her.<sup id="cite_ref-Lair_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lair-4">[4]</a></sup> Sam could defeat the spider thanks to the Phial, and escaped her [lair](../S/Shelob's_Lair.md) after taking the star-glass with him, excusing himself to Frodo's body.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Sam and Frodo later used it to overcome the will of the [Two Watchers](../T/Two_Watchers.md) at the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](../T/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Sam also attempted to use the light in the [Crack of Doom](../C/Cracks_of_Doom.md), but the light from the glass faded because they were in the heart of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s domain.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Upon Frodo's departure from [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), the light from the phial faded as it reached the shores of [Eldamar](../E/Eldamar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

### Relation with Eärendil {#Relation_with_Eärendil}

Galadriel explained that her phial contained the [light](../L/Light.md) of the [Star of Eärendil](../S/Star_of_Eärendil.md) as reflected in her [Mirror](../M/Mirror_of_Galadriel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-1">[1]</a></sup> As Sam remembered to Frodo, the light of this star was the same [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md) recovered by [Beren](../B/Beren.md), now hold by [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md). This made them part of the great tales of the past.<sup id="cite_ref-Stairs_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stairs-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:712</sup>

Thus, when [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) led Frodo and Sam into Shelob's Lair, Frodo exclaimed in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), "*[Aiya Eärendil elenion ancalima!](../A/Aiya_Eärendil_elenion_ancalima!.md)*",<sup id="cite_ref-Lair_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lair-4">[4]</a></sup> translating as "Hail Eärendil, brightest of stars!".

### Films {#Films}

<b>1978: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Phial is absent as Galadriel does not give any gifts to the Fellowship.</dd></dl>

<b>1980: <a href="../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)"><i>The Return of the King</i> (1980 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Phial is found by Sam in Frodo&#39;s cloak and is used to pass the Watchers&#39; barrier in Cirith Ungol. Its origins are never touched on in the film, with Sam being unaware of it until finding it. Frodo identifies it as the Phial of Galadriel, but refuses to say anymore about it, claiming that doing so will destroy its powers.</dd></dl>

<b>2001-03: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Phial is given to Frodo by Galadriel in the first film. It reappears in <i>Return of the King</i>, used by Frodo to ward off Shelob although he drops it in his escape. It is retrieved by Sam and wielded in his battle against Shelob.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies"><i>The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</i></a>:</b>

<dl><dd>When the <a href="../W/White_Council.md" title="White Council">White Council</a> arrives to rescue <a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a>, Galadriel use the phial to cast Sauron out of <a href="../D/Dol_Guldur.md" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a>, rebuking him as a &#34;servant of <a class="mw-redirect" href="../M/Melkor.md" title="Melkor">Morgoth</a>&#34; without name, face, or form.</dd></dl>
