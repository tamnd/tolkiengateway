**Pelloini** were most likely the [fays](/wiki/Fays "Fays") of towns or hedged fields, according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Pel_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pel-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Pelloini* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), probably being related to the word <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Pelle&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Pelle (page does not exist)">pelle</a></i> ("town").<sup id="cite_ref-Pel_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pel-1">[1]</a></sup> Both *pelle* and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Pelto&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Pelto (page does not exist)">pelto</a></i> ("hedge, hedged field") are derived from the root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=PELE&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="PELE (page does not exist)">PELE</a> ("fence in").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
