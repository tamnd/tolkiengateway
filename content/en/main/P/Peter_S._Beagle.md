**Peter Soyer Beagle** ([April 20](/wiki/20_April "20 April"), [1939](/wiki/1939)) is an American fantasist and author of novels, nonfiction, and screenplays.

## Bilbiography, selected {#Bilbiography,_selected}

### Books {#Books}

- [1978](/wiki/1978): *[The Film Book of J.R.R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Film_Book_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* (based on screenplay)
- [1991](/wiki/1991): *[After the King: Stories In Honor of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/After_the_King "After the King")* (as contributor)

### Articles {#Articles}

- [1966](/wiki/1966): *Holiday*
    - (June:) "Tolkien's Magic Ring"
- [1966](/wiki/1966): *[The Tolkien Reader](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Reader)*
    - "Tolkien's Magic Ring"
- [1982](/wiki/1982): *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* (US revised edition)
    - "About the *Lord of the Rings* Trilogy by J. R. R. Tolkien"
- [1984](/wiki/1984): *Fantastists on Fantasy*
    - "Tolkien's Magic Ring"

## Filmography {#Filmography}

- [1978](/wiki/1978): [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)") (screenplay, with Chris Conkling)
- [2005](/wiki/2005): *[Ringers: Lord of the Fans](/wiki/Ringers:_Lord_of_the_Fans)* (interviewee)
