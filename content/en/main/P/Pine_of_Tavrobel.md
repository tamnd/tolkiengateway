The **Pine of Tavrobel** was a great, star-reaching [pine](Pines.md) located in [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Pine_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pine-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In one of the outlines for the continuation of the *Lost Tales*, it is said that before the [Inwir](../I/Inwir.md) left [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) for the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md) to free the enslaved [Gnomes](../G/Gnomes.md), [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md) gave them a seed to guard, for she foretold that great things should come of it - however, it was eventually forgotten and cast into the garden of [Gilfanon](../G/Gilfanon.md) in [Tavrobel](../T/Tavrobel.md). There it grew to an incredible height, even reaching [Ilwë](../I/Ilwë.md) (the part of air in which the stars are located<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>).<sup id="cite_ref-Pine_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pine-2">[2]</a></sup>

When [Melko](../M/Melko.md) broke free of his second imprisonment, he went to Tol Eressëa in order to sow dissension between the Gnomes and the [Solosimpi](../S/Solosimpi.md), who sent for help to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). However, the only aid that came from the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) was that of [Tulkas](../T/Tulkas.md) who in secret has sent his son [Telimektar](../T/Telimektar.md) to Tol Eressëa to aid them.<sup id="cite_ref-Pine_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pine-2">[2]</a></sup>

There, Telimektar, with the help of [Ingil](../I/Ingil.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> son of [Inwë](../I/Inwë.md), surprised Melko and wounded him, and chased him up the Pine of Tavrobel all the way to Ilwë. However, Melko kept attacking the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) and the [stars](../S/Stars.md), and in the end caused the death of [Urwendi](../U/Urwendi.md) the steerer of the Sun-vessel.<sup id="cite_ref-Pine_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pine-2">[2]</a></sup>

Finally, the Valar decided to cut down the great Pine, which left Melko stranded out of the world until his return in the [Great End](../G/Great_End.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Pine_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pine-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In one note associated with the outline given above, [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) suggested that perhaps the Pine should be located somewhere other than Tol Eressëa.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

According to the slightly later conception of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), in which the context of the *Lost Tales* is shifted so that Tol Eressëa is no longer identified with (what would become) the British Isles, there is a mention of the **Pine of Belawryn**, whose offspring still grows in [Tavrobel the Old](../T/Tavrobel_the_Old.md) in [Luthany](../L/Luthany.md), after which the Tavrobel in Tol Eressëa was named after.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>
