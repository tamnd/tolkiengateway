The **Pine of Tavrobel** was a great, star-reaching [pine](/wiki/Pines "Pines") located in [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Pine_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pine-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In one of the outlines for the continuation of the *Lost Tales*, it is said that before the [Inwir](/wiki/Inwir) left [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) for the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands) to free the enslaved [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes), [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) gave them a seed to guard, for she foretold that great things should come of it - however, it was eventually forgotten and cast into the garden of [Gilfanon](/wiki/Gilfanon) in [Tavrobel](/wiki/Tavrobel). There it grew to an incredible height, even reaching [Ilwë](/wiki/Ilw%C3%AB) (the part of air in which the stars are located<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>).<sup id="cite_ref-Pine_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pine-2">[2]</a></sup>

When [Melko](/wiki/Melko) broke free of his second imprisonment, he went to Tol Eressëa in order to sow dissension between the Gnomes and the [Solosimpi](/wiki/Solosimpi), who sent for help to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). However, the only aid that came from the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) was that of [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas) who in secret has sent his son [Telimektar](/wiki/Telimektar) to Tol Eressëa to aid them.<sup id="cite_ref-Pine_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pine-2">[2]</a></sup>

There, Telimektar, with the help of [Ingil](/wiki/Ingil)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> son of [Inwë](/wiki/Inw%C3%AB), surprised Melko and wounded him, and chased him up the Pine of Tavrobel all the way to Ilwë. However, Melko kept attacking the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and the [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars"), and in the end caused the death of [Urwendi](/wiki/Urwendi) the steerer of the Sun-vessel.<sup id="cite_ref-Pine_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pine-2">[2]</a></sup>

Finally, the Valar decided to cut down the great Pine, which left Melko stranded out of the world until his return in the [Great End](/wiki/Great_End).<sup id="cite_ref-Pine_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pine-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In one note associated with the outline given above, [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) suggested that perhaps the Pine should be located somewhere other than Tol Eressëa.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

According to the slightly later conception of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), in which the context of the *Lost Tales* is shifted so that Tol Eressëa is no longer identified with (what would become) the British Isles, there is a mention of the **Pine of Belawryn**, whose offspring still grows in [Tavrobel the Old](/wiki/Tavrobel_the_Old) in [Luthany](/wiki/Luthany), after which the Tavrobel in Tol Eressëa was named after.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>
