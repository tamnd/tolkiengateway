Professor **Rashbold** was a [Man](/wiki/Man) living in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_century" title="wikipedia:20th century">20th century</a> <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>.

## History {#History}

Rashbold transliterated and translated the [Edwin Lowdham](/wiki/Edwin_Lowdham)'s manuscript on the behest of [Michael George Ramer](/wiki/Michael_George_Ramer). The text was in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) written in [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar). It described the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Night_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Night-1">[1]</a></sup>

He was described by [Lowdham](/wiki/Alwin_Arundel_Lowdham "Alwin Arundel Lowdham") as a "grumpy old bear".<sup id="cite_ref-Night_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Night-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The name *Rashbold* is a pun on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s own surname, being a translation from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language" title="wikipedia:German language">German</a> *Toll-kühn* (meaning "foolhardy").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
