**Pembroke College** is one of the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md)'s 39 constituent colleges.

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) became [Rawlinson and Bosworth Professor of Anglo-Saxon](../R/Rawlinson_and_Bosworth_Professor_of_Anglo-Saxon.md) in [1925](../_/1925.md). Despite being a fellow at the college he chose not to live at the college. During this period he wrote *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* and *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md)* as well as [Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics](../B/Beowulf%253A_The_Monsters_and_the_Critics.md). He kept the position for twenty years before moving to Merton College in [1945](../_/1945.md) and taking up the position of Professor of English Language and Literature.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Tolkien seemed to have disliked Pembroke. In a <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../L/Letter.md" title="Letter">letter</a> ([Letter 96](../L/Letter_96.md)) to his son, he described Pembroke as "miserable" and said he desired be in a "real college".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Since 2013 Pembroke Colleges has delivered an annual lecture called The J.R.R. Tolkien Lecture on Fantasy.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
