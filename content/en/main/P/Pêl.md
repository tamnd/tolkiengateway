<i><b>pêl</b></i> (plural *peli*) is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) noun meaning "fenced field".<sup id="cite_ref-etym_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The word *pêl* or *pel* often appear as a suggested [Neo-Sindarin](/wiki/Neo-Elvish "Neo-Elvish") word, retaining the same meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Noldorin *pêl* is a descendant form of [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin") *pele*, deriving from root [PEL(ES)](/wiki/PEL#Other_versions "PEL"). [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") also notes the word <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/tun#Old_English" title="wiktionary:tun">tūn</a></i> ("fenced field"; identified as [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien)) in connection with *pêl*.<sup id="cite_ref-etym_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-1">[1]</a></sup>
