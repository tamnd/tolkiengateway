**Ponórir** was the name of the northern portion of the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands), later known as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scandinavia" title="wikipedia:Scandinavia">Scandinavia</a> (with **Ponórë** being the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) name for <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway" title="wikipedia:Norway">Norway</a>), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Pon_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pon-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ponórir* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), glossed as "the Northlands, Scandinavia" in the *[Qenya Lexicon](/wiki/Qenya_Lexicon)*,<sup id="cite_ref-Pon_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pon-1">[1]</a></sup> a combination of the root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=PO&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="PO (page does not exist)">PO</a> (associated with the north) + *[nórë](/wiki/N%C3%B3r%C3%AB "Nórë")* ("land").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
