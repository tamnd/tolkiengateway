**Pengolodh** was a [Loremaster](../L/Loremaster.md) of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).

## History {#History}

Pengolodh was an [Elf](../E/Elves.md), born in [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) to a [Sindarin](../S/Sindar.md) and a [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) parent; when [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) built his Hidden City in [Tumladen](../T/Tumladen.md), he moved with his people there. As a member of the [Lambengolmor](../L/Lambengolmor.md), he was known as the "Sage of the Noldor", and counted as the greatest Loremaster since [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) and [Rúmil](<../R/Rúmil_(elf_of_Tirion).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-2">[1]</a></sup>

Pengolodh escaped the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md) with [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) and [Idril](../I/Idril.md), and followed them to the [Havens of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-2">[1]</a></sup> The *[Annals of Beleriand](../A/Annals_of_Beleriand.md)* are attributed to him, as well as the edited *[Annals of Valinor](../A/Annals_of_Valinor.md)* (furthering the work of Rúmil).

It was during his stay at the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md) that Pengolodh did the majority of his work. Basing on information obtained from the refugees of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), he made copies and extracts of documents written in [Cirth](../C/Cirth.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-2">[1]</a></sup> possibly preserving them as an active writing system; possibly he must have stayed in [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) for at least a while after the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), so that the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) could copy his work.

Later, in the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), he dwelt in the Kingdom of [Ereinion Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md). Pengolodh was one of the few Elves admitted into [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-2">[1]</a></sup> where he might have learned [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 2]</a></sup>

Unlike the early [loremasters](../L/Loremasters.md) who did not give much importance to *[hwermë](../H/Hwermë.md)*, it is said that Pengolodh collected much material concerning gesture-systems.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[2]</a></sup>

It was during the [War of the Elves and Sauron](../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md) and after the fall of [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) when Pengolodh left [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) for [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md), last of the Loremasters to leave Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-2">[1]</a></sup>

Ages later, he accepted [Ælfwine](../_/Ælfwine.md) the traveler and taught him about the Elves and the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md). Some of their discussions were recorded as *[Dangweth Pengoloð](../D/Dangweth_Pengoloð.md)*.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name *Pengolodh* is glossed as "'teaching sage', doctor of lore",<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-5">[3]</a></sup> consisting of the words *[pen](Pen.md)* ("somebody") and *[golodh](../G/Golodh.md)* ("lore-master, sage").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[4]</a></sup> The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) version of his name was <i><b>Quendingoldo</b></i> or <i><b>Quengoldo</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup>

*Pengolodh* also appears spelled <i><b>Pengolod</b></i>, <i><b>Pengoloð</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-5">[3]</a></sup> <i><b>Pengoloth</b></i>, and <i><b>Pengoloþ</b></i>.

In some of his [later writings](../Q/Quendi_and_Eldar.md) (c. 1959/1960), Tolkien proposed changing the character's name to <i><b>Thingódhel</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup> The name is Sindarin for "Grey Noldo", from <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Thind&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Thind (page does not exist)">thind</a></i> ("grey") + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=G%C3%B3dhel&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gódhel (page does not exist)">Gódhel</a></i> ("\[Exiled\] Noldo").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Pengolodh does not appear in any of the canon narratives of Middle-earth, but is mentioned in texts published in *[The History of Middle-earth](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)*; he is given as the fictional author of many works, including the *[Annals of Beleriand](../A/Annals_of_Beleriand.md)*, a work which was developed by Tolkien at the same time as *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, and from which [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) drew much information to establish the published *Silmarillion*. Various late essays by Tolkien dealing with linguistics are presented as being the work of Pengolodh, including the remarkable work *[Quendi and Eldar](../Q/Quendi_and_Eldar.md)*.

Because of Pengolodh's nature as a literary device, he does not appear actively as a protagonist in any of the stories. All information about his life come from biographical notes written by Tolkien.

Early Tolkien texts stated that, after removing to Tol Eressëa, Pengolodh lived in a village called *[Tavrobel](../T/Tavrobel.md)* (or *Tathrobel*). Centuries later [Ælfwine](../_/Ælfwine.md) spoke with him there. The figure of [Gilfanon](../G/Gilfanon.md), which fulfilled a similar role as a chronicler of the annals of Beleriand in earlier works, probably became this character as well in Tolkien's mind.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[9]</a></sup>

### Possible erasure from the legendarium {#Possible_erasure_from_the_legendarium}

In the late 1950s Tolkien apparently decided that the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) is not Elvish, but a Mannish tradition.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup> Dawn M. Walls-Thumma notes that after that point, Pengolodh's name fails to be mentioned by Tolkien in the later works and revisions; for example the *[Grey Annals](../G/Grey_Annals.md)* are attributed to Sindarin and Noldorin sources but Pengolodh is not mentioned; Pengolodh was mentioned in the first draft of *[The Annals of Aman](../T/The_Annals_of_Aman.md)*, along with [Quennar](../Q/Quennar.md) and Rúmil, but in the second draft only Rúmil remains; Pengolodh's name is also removed in a revision of *[The Tale of Years](<../T/The_Tale_of_Years_(The_War_of_the_Jewels).md>)* and while Pengolodh was attributed in the First Phase of [The Later *Quenta Silmarillion*](../T/The_Later_Quenta_Silmarillion.md), his name is removed from the Second Phase. Walls-Thumma however notes that the texts are still Elf-focused and they still include elements that wouldn't be known by Rúmil alone.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[11]</a></sup>
