**Paladin Took I** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") who held the position of [Thain](/wiki/Thain) some time between [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [740](/wiki/Third_Age_2340 "Third Age 2340") and [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1083](/wiki/Third_Age_2683 "Third Age 2683").

## History {#History}

There are no direct records of Paladin I but he was evidently the namesake of [Paladin II](/wiki/Paladin_Took_II "Paladin Took II").<sup id="cite_ref-App|Took_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|Took-1">[1]</a></sup> Paladin I's reign as Thain came between the beginning of the Took line of Thains in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [740](/wiki/Third_Age_2340 "Third Age 2340") and the reign of [Isengrim II](/wiki/Isengrim_Took_II "Isengrim Took II"), which began in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1083](/wiki/Third_Age_2683 "Third Age 2683"), after which there were no gaps in the Took family's genealogy.<sup id="cite_ref-App|B2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|B2-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paladin" title="wikipedia:Paladin">paladin</a>" is a high office which existed in medieval Europe - which entered into many tales - and was a noble office held by an honourable and valiant knight. In particular, a paladin was one of the twelve peers of Charlemagne's court. The term comes from the Latin *palatinus* which means "of the palace".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
