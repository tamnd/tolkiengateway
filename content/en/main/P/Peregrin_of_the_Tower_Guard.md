**Peregrin of the Tower Guard** is the twenty-fifth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Pippin](Pippin.md) sits on a bench in the uniform of the [Guards of the Citadel](../G/Guards_of_the_Citadel.md) reflecting on his decision to offer his allegiance to [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md), and wondering what service he, a [hobbit](../H/Hobbit.md), can offer [the Steward](../D/Denethor.md). [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) walks towards him and tells him that *"a generous deed should not be checked with cold counsel"*. Smiling at Pippin, Faramir tells him that the livery he is wearing was once his own when he was a child. He tells the hobbit that [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) was always the soldier and his older brother and father were very alike.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I think you have strength of a different kind. And one day, your father will see it.

## Differences {#Differences}

In the book, it is Gandalf who tells Pippin that *"generous deed should not be checked by cold counsel"*, in response to Pippin asking the wizard if he is angry with him for swearing himself to the service of Denethor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
