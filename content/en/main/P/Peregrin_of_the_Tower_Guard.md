**Peregrin of the Tower Guard** is the twenty-fifth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)"). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Pippin](/wiki/Pippin) sits on a bench in the uniform of the [Guards of the Citadel](/wiki/Guards_of_the_Citadel) reflecting on his decision to offer his allegiance to [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor), and wondering what service he, a [hobbit](/wiki/Hobbit "Hobbit"), can offer [the Steward](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor"). [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) walks towards him and tells him that *"a generous deed should not be checked with cold counsel"*. Smiling at Pippin, Faramir tells him that the livery he is wearing was once his own when he was a child. He tells the hobbit that [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) was always the soldier and his older brother and father were very alike.

## Differences {#Differences}

In the book, it is Gandalf who tells Pippin that *"generous deed should not be checked by cold counsel"*, in response to Pippin asking the wizard if he is angry with him for swearing himself to the service of Denethor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
