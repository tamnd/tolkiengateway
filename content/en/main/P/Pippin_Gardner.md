**Pippin Gardner** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Pippin was the third son and fifth child of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton), born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1429](/wiki/Fourth_Age_8 "Fourth Age 8").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He was named after [Pippin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") at the suggestion of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Pippin* is a translation of *Razan*, a [Westron](/wiki/Westron) word for a small type of apple.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), the name *Pippin* is *Cordof*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>
