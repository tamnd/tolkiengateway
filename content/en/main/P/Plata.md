**plata** is a [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) word meaning "flat of the hand, the hand held upwards or forwards, flat and tensed (with fingers and thumb closed or spread). The significance of this was that it was a common way of saying "there is no weapon in my hand"."<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
