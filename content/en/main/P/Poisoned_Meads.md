The **Poisoned Meads**<sup id="cite_ref-name_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-1">[1]</a></sup> were wide meadows on both sides of the [Morgulduin](/wiki/Morgulduin) in the [Morgul Vale](/wiki/Morgul_Vale). The pale white flowers that grew in the meads were luminous and beautiful, though their shapes were horrific. They had a rotten odour and emitted noxious vapours which poisoned the river.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [10 March](/wiki/10_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> as [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) were approaching the bridge over the [Morgulduin](/wiki/Morgulduin), cold vapours issued from the river which sickened Frodo.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

On [19 March](/wiki/19_March),<sup id="cite_ref-name_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-1">[1]</a></sup> when the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Host_of_the_West" title="Host of the West">Host of the West</a> came to and passed the [Cross-roads](/wiki/Cross-roads), a vanguard led by [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) rode to the Morgul Vale and there destroyed the bridge and set fire to the Poisoned Meads<sup id="cite_ref-name_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-1">[1]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The flowers of the Poisoned Meads<sup id="cite_ref-name_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-1">[1]</a></sup> may have been inspired by the *Arum* genus.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
