**pedo** (root [KWETE](../K/KWETE.md)) is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning "speak" or "say" (imperative).

The [Doors of Durin](../D/Doors_of_Durin.md) had the inscription *Pedo [mellon](../M/Mellon.md) a minno* meaning "Say 'friend' and enter".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
