**Privata Kodo Skauta** (Modern English: **Private Scout Code**) is the first known script created by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and used in [Book of the Foxrook](/wiki/Book_of_the_Foxrook).

## Description {#Description}

Words are written using ideographic symbols, "monographs" that represent words or spelled out using a phonetic code-alphabet. Tolkien noted that Privata Kodo Skauta was phonetically the same as [Esperanto](/wiki/Esperanto) (with the exception of six spelling conventions), which he was also learning at the time and was also included in Book of the Foxrook.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Seven_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Seven-2">[2]</a></sup>
