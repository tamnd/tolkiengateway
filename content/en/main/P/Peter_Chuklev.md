**Peter Chuklev** (born [1936](/wiki/1936)) is a Bulgarian illustrator. Graduated from the Art Academy in Sofia in 1960, he has been a teacher at the National Art Academy since 1964 and professor of printmaking since 1993.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Working primarily in the area of printmaking, illustration, and drawing, Chuklev made the illustrations for the 1979 version of the 1975 Bulgarian edition of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
