**Penni** was a term used by some [Avari](/wiki/Avari) to describe themselves. It is unknown if the name "Penni" was applied o a particular group of Avari specifically or to all Avari as a whole. The name was especially known among the [Wood-elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves "Silvan Elves") of the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin).

It is suggested that the group of Avari calling themselves "Penni" belonged initially to the [Nelyar](/wiki/Nelyar), and the shift of [PQ](/wiki/PQ) *kw* to *p* had already occurred among the [Lindar](/wiki/Lindar) at the [Sundering of the Elves](/wiki/Sundering_of_the_Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-Author_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Author-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*penni* is an [Avarin](/wiki/Avarin) word meaning "the People". It derives from [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *[kwendī](/wiki/Kwende "Kwende")*.<sup id="cite_ref-Author_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Author-1">[1]</a></sup>
