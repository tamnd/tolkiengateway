**Penni** was a term used by some [Avari](../A/Avari.md) to describe themselves. It is unknown if the name "Penni" was applied o a particular group of Avari specifically or to all Avari as a whole. The name was especially known among the [Wood-elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) of the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md).

It is suggested that the group of Avari calling themselves "Penni" belonged initially to the [Nelyar](../N/Nelyar.md), and the shift of [PQ](PQ.md) *kw* to *p* had already occurred among the [Lindar](../L/Lindar.md) at the [Sundering of the Elves](../S/Sundering_of_the_Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Author_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Author-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*penni* is an [Avarin](../A/Avarin.md) word meaning "the People". It derives from [Primitive Quendian](Primitive_Quendian.md) *[kwendī](../K/Kwende.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Author_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Author-1">[1]</a></sup>
