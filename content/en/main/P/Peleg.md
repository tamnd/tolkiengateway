**Peleg** was the son of [Indor](</wiki/Indor_(grandfather_of_Tuor)> "Indor (grandfather of Tuor)") and the father of [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. Therefore, like Tuor, he probably belonged to the [House of the Swan](/wiki/House_of_the_Swan).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Peleg* is in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), meaning "axe",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and its [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognate is <i><b>Pelek</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In *[The Lay of the Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Fall_of_Gondolin)*, from c. [1920](/wiki/1920), his name was changed to <i><b>Fengel</b></i>, a name that was later used for the [fifteen King of Rohan](/wiki/Fengel "Fengel").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
