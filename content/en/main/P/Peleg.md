**Peleg** was the son of [Indor](<../I/Indor_(grandfather_of_Tuor).md>) and the father of [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. Therefore, like Tuor, he probably belonged to the [House of the Swan](../H/House_of_the_Swan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Peleg* is in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md), meaning "axe",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and its [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) cognate is <i><b>Pelek</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In *[The Lay of the Fall of Gondolin](../T/The_Lay_of_the_Fall_of_Gondolin.md)*, from c. [1920](../_/1920.md), his name was changed to <i><b>Fengel</b></i>, a name that was later used for the [fifteen King of Rohan](../F/Fengel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
