**Perry-the-Winkle** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbit) of [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving), and the main character of the hobbit-poem *[Perry-the-Winkle](/wiki/Perry-the-Winkle)*.

In the poem, Perry was the only hobbit who treated the [Lonely Troll](/wiki/Lonely_Troll) kindly, and in him the Troll found the friend he always wanted; the Troll often fed him and taught him how to be a great cook.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The poem [Perry-the-Winkle](/wiki/Perry-the-Winkle) is a revision of a earlier story named *The Bumpus*. In *The Bumpus*, Perry is named William "Bill" Winkle.
