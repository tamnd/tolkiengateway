The **Pastures of [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna)** were in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), in the southern part of the land. To the east were, successively, the [Woods of Oromë](/wiki/Woods_of_Orom%C3%AB), the [Hyamentir](/wiki/Hyamentir), and the narrow land of [Avathar](/wiki/Avathar). These pastures were golden with the tall wheat of the gods.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
