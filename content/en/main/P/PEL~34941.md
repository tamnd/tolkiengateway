**PEL**, or **PELE**, is a [Primitive Quendian](Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "edge, bound, fence, limit, border".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

In his Appendix to *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) noted the Elvish element or root *pel-* (signifying "go round, encircle").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is unknown if he reconstructed this element from writings by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) which have appeared in print after [1977](../_/1977.md) (when the work was first published), or if he used manuscripts which still are unpublished.

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- PEL
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *peltakse-* ("a fence of fixed stakes etc., or a 'pale' and fencing stakes"); *pelma* ("a border, fringe, edge, limiting device")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>

- PELE
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *pelo* ("a boundary (fence)"); *[pella](Pella.md)* ("beyond"); cf. *[Pelóri](Pelóri.md#Etymology)*<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root PELE, signifying "fence in".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

In the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md) appears the roots PEL ("revolve on fixed point") and PEL(ES) (no signification given).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup> Among the derivatives are:<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup>

- PEL
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[pel-](Pel-.md)*; *peltas* ("pivot")
    - [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *pelthaes* ("pivot")

- PEL(ES)
    - [Old Noldorin](../K/Kornoldorin.md): *pele* ("fenced field")
        - Noldorin: *[pêl](Pêl.md)*
    - Quenya: *peler*, *opele* ("walled house or village, 'town')
    - Noldorin: *[gobel](../G/Gobel.md)*
