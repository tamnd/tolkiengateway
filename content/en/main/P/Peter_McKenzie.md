**Peter Leo McKenzie** ([10 August](/wiki/10_August) [1943](/wiki/1943) - [9 September](/wiki/9_September) [2023](/wiki/2023)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) was a New Zealand horse trainer and breeder and a part-time actor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He played [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.

Peter McKenzie was the father of [Bret McKenzie](/wiki/Bret_McKenzie).
