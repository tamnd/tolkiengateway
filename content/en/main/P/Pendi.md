**Pendi** is the [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) name for (the entire race of) the [Elves](/wiki/Elves). It has no singular and was "a learned word of the historians"<sup id="cite_ref-QE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QE-1">[1]</a></sup>

The word was later dropped since it was confused to mean "lacking, poor" (root [PEN](/wiki/PEN))<sup id="cite_ref-QE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word comes from [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish) *[kwendî](/wiki/Kwende "Kwende")* (singular *kwende*),<sup id="cite_ref-QE_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QE-1">[1]</a></sup> [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") [KWE](/wiki/KWE)NE. See also *[pen](/wiki/Pen "Pen")*.
