**Paladin Took II** was the thirty-first [Thain](/wiki/Thain) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). He was the father of [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took).

## History {#History}

### Family {#Family}

Paladin was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1333](/wiki/Third_Age_2933 "Third Age 2933") being the son and heir of [Adalgrim Took](/wiki/Adalgrim_Took), and a great-grandchild of the [Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took"). He married [Eglantine Banks](/wiki/Eglantine_Banks), and they had several children together: [Pearl](/wiki/Pearl_Took "Pearl Took") (born [1375](/wiki/Third_Age_2975 "Third Age 2975")) followed by [Pimpernel](/wiki/Pimpernel_Took "Pimpernel Took") ([1379](/wiki/Third_Age_2979 "Third Age 2979")) and [Pervinca](/wiki/Pervinca_Took "Pervinca Took") ([1385](/wiki/Third_Age_2985 "Third Age 2985")). [Peregrin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") ([1390](/wiki/Third_Age_2990 "Third Age 2990")) was younger, but would be Paladin's heir.<sup id="cite_ref-Took_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Becoming Thain {#Becoming_Thain}

Paladin was a direct descendant of the [Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took"), one of his great grandsons. He was member of a junior branch of the Took family, where [Hildigrim](/wiki/Hildigrim_Took "Hildigrim Took") was head of that branch.<sup id="cite_ref-Took_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-1">[1]</a></sup> He became a farmer, farming the lands around [Whitwell](/wiki/Whitwell) near [Tuckborough](/wiki/Tuckborough).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Never did he expect to become Thain as at the time of his birth the Thainship was in the hands of, [Isumbras IV](/wiki/Isumbras_Took_IV "Isumbras Took IV"), his great uncle. Isumbras IV's succession appeared secure since he had both a son and grandson, [Fortinbras II](/wiki/Fortinbras_Took_II "Fortinbras Took II") and [Ferumbras III](/wiki/Ferumbras_Took_III "Ferumbras Took III"). However, Ferumbras III died without an heir in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1415](/wiki/Third_Age_3015 "Third Age 3015") and Fortinbras II had had no other sons, so the Thainship passed to Paladin, the most senior male-line relative.<sup id="cite_ref-L214_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L214-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001") he attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party) with his children.<sup id="cite_ref-Took_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-1">[1]</a></sup>

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

Paladin never agreed with [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins)' attempt to take over [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") as he believed that if anyone were going to the "Chief" it should be the legitimate [Thain of the Shire](/wiki/Thain_of_the_Shire), therefore Paladin and the other Tooks stood up to Lotho and his [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians). The [Tooks](/wiki/Took_Family "Took Family") hid in their deep dwellings of [Great Smials](/wiki/Great_Smials), and the ruffians could not get them, and if any ruffians were found in the [Tookland](/wiki/Tookland) they were shot and killed, an act which led the ruffians to become harsher.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-4">[4]</a></sup>

At this time, Paladin's son [Peregrin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") and [three other hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers"), were lost in the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest) by all accounts. Their return was unexpected, but Paladin fully embraced their suggestion to drive out the Ruffians. He sent a hundred Tooks with his son to the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater), and commanded the remainder of his troops cleaning up the area south of the village.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Death {#Death}

Paladin died in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1434](/wiki/Fourth_Age_13 "Fourth Age 13") at the age of 101, and after having been Thain for nineteen years, he was succeeded by his son who became Thain Peregrin I.<sup id="cite_ref-Took_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Paladin* is derived from Latin *palatinus*, which means "palace officer".<sup id="cite_ref-IE_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IE-5">[5]</a></sup> It was used for the twelve peers of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlemagne" title="wikipedia:Charlemagne">Charlemagne</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-IE_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IE-5">[5]</a></sup> the emperor of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolingian_Empire" title="wikipedia:Carolingian Empire">Carolingian Empire</a>, an empire in Western and Central Europe that was dominated by the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franks" title="wikipedia:Franks">Franks</a>.

A "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paladin" title="wikipedia:Paladin">paladin</a>" is a high office which existed in medieval Europe - which entered into many tales - and was a noble office held by an honourable and valiant knight.
