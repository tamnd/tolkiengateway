**Pierre Vanderweerd** is an artist who specializes in character design, visual development and traditional 2D animation. He was born in the Netherlands and moved to Canada in [2005](../_/2005.md). Pierre started drawing in [2009](../_/2009.md) fueled by all things whimsical and musical. He is currently working as a senior traditional animator in Montréal. He started a visual development project based on [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) to further develop his skills in character design, posing, rendering and more. Not wanting to be influenced by the [2001](../_/2001.md)-[2003](../_/2003.md) [trilogy](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) (which he loves,) he began by re-reading the books and drew his inspiration largely from there.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
