The **Party Field** was a large field south of [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End)'s front door adjacent to [Bagshot Row](/wiki/Bagshot_Row) near [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton).

## History {#History}

In the preparations for [Bilbo's 111th birthday party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party"), a special entrance was cut into the bank, and a large white gate and steps connected it to the road, and an open-air kitchen was installed in its north corner, manned with cooks from many inns and taverns of the vicinity.

The field was covered with tents and pavilions; underneath the largest pavilion was the [Party Tree](/wiki/Party_Tree), a decorated tree that stood at the head of the table. Bilbo made his farewell speech beneath it.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the destruction of the Party Tree by [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey) and his [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) planted a [mallorn](/wiki/Mallorn "Mallorn") tree, upon his return from the [Quest of the Ring](/wiki/Quest_of_the_Ring)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and it blossomed on [6 April](/wiki/6_April) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3020](/wiki/Third_Age_3020 "Third Age 3020")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Afterwards it was used for holidays and celebrations by the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) of that region including a new holiday celebrated on April 6.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
