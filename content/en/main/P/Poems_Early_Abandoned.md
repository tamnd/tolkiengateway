**Poems Early Abandoned** is the second chapter of *[The Lays of Beleriand](/wiki/The_Lays_of_Beleriand)*. During his time at the [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds) ([1920](/wiki/1920)-[1925](/wiki/1925)), Tolkien embarked on five poetic works concerned as part of his mythology, but only the *[Lay of Leithian](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian)* and the *[Lay of the Children of Húrin](/wiki/Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)* were more than just beginnings.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This chapter treats in turn those abandoned poems.
