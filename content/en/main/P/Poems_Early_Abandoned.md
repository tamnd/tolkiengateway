**Poems Early Abandoned** is the second chapter of *[The Lays of Beleriand](../T/The_Lays_of_Beleriand.md)*. During his time at the [University of Leeds](../U/University_of_Leeds.md) ([1920](../_/1920.md)-[1925](../_/1925.md)), Tolkien embarked on five poetic works concerned as part of his mythology, but only the *[Lay of Leithian](../L/Lay_of_Leithian.md)* and the *[Lay of the Children of Húrin](../L/Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md)* were more than just beginnings.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This chapter treats in turn those abandoned poems.
