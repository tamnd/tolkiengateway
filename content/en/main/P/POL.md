**POL** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "large, big (strong)" or "can, have physical power and ability". At one time [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") seems to have hesitated about this sense of the root, writing: "\[√POL\] cannot refer to *strength*", since it is "\[t\]oo obvious a reminiscence of *pollens*"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> and "also it does not account for *poli-* 'meal'". He therefore proposed the revised signification "'pound up', break small, reduced to powder etc". However, the original sense of POL ultimately seems to have been retained, and Tolkien instead proposed a change of the word *pole* ("meal"; cf. *poli-* above) to *mŭle*.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[1]</a></sup>

In [*Parma Eldalamberon* 17](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_17 "Parma Eldalamberon 17") (p. 115) "*pol*" appears to be cited as a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word. However, it has been argued that: "\[s\]ince this would be the sole example of a monosyllabic Quenya adjective, it may be that Tolkien is here citing the root POL rather than a complete word."<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> The argument also gains plausibility as *pol* is listed among the roots in the "large &amp; small" group.

POL is said to have been close in meaning to one of the senses of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=LER&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LER (page does not exist)">LER</a> "am free to do, there being no obstacle", as the part "no obstacle" could sometimes signify "no physical obstacle".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *polda* ("big")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LR_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the roots POL-I (yielding the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) derivative *pole* "oats") and POLO (signifying "have strength").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies") appears the root POL-, POLOD-, signifying "physically strong".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-5">[4]</a></sup>
