<br> **Sir Peter Robert Jackson** ([October 31](/wiki/31_October "31 October"), [1961](/wiki/1961)) is a [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand)-born Academy Award-winning director, screenwriter and producer. He is one of the co-creators of [Wētā FX](/wiki/W%C4%93t%C4%81_FX), formerly known as *Weta Digital*. He directed, co-wrote and produced [*The Lord of the Rings* trilogy](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and [*The Hobbit* trilogy](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").
