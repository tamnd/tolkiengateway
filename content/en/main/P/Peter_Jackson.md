<br> **Sir Peter Robert Jackson** ([October 31](../_/31_October.md), [1961](../_/1961.md)) is a [New Zealand](../N/New_Zealand.md)-born Academy Award-winning director, screenwriter and producer. He is one of the co-creators of [Wētā FX](../W/Wētā_FX.md), formerly known as *Weta Digital*. He directed, co-wrote and produced [*The Lord of the Rings* trilogy](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) and [*The Hobbit* trilogy](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).
