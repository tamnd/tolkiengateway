**Penlod** was a [Gnome](/wiki/Gnome)-lord of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

He was marshall of both the [house of the Pillar](/wiki/House_of_the_Pillar "House of the Pillar") and the [house of the Tower of Snow](/wiki/House_of_the_Tower_of_Snow "House of the Tower of Snow"), and he is described as the tallest of the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:173</sup>

During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), the [Gondothlim](/wiki/Gondothlim) suffered a hard attack from the enemy after the death of [Rog](/wiki/Rog) and the [folk of the Hammer of Wrath](/wiki/Folk_of_the_Hammer_of_Wrath "Folk of the Hammer of Wrath"). Having to retreat further back into the city, Penlod perished "in a lane with his back to the wall", and with him many of his men.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:179</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Penlod* is clearly [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), but it is not glossed. The [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognate was <i><b>Pendelot</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
