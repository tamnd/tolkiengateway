**Pippin Looks After Merry** is the forty-sixth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* and the fifty-seventh scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)"). It was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") searches for [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck"). He finds him lying, half-conscious, near a slain <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Oliphaunt" title="Oliphaunt">Oliphaunt</a>. Pippin pushes off a dead orc that had fallen on Merry and sits him up. Merry wakes up and tells Pippin he knew he'd find him and then asks if Pippin is going to leave him to which Pippin responds, "No Merry. I'm going to look after you."

## Differences {#Differences}

- Pippin was sent by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) to look for Merry.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He searched inside the city, finding him inside the [Gate of Minas Tirith](/wiki/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith "Great Gate of Minas Tirith").<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Merry asked, "Are you going to bury me?" and Merry responded "No, indeed!...No, we are going to the Houses of Healing."<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">[2]</a></sup>
