<i><b>Parma Eldalamberon 5</b></i> is the fifth issue of *[Parma Eldalamberon](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon)*, a journal of linguistic studies of the Elvish languages and names in the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

It is the last issue of its initial run.
