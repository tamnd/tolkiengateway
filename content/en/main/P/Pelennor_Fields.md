The **Pelennor Fields** were the townlands and fields of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), capital of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

## Description {#Description}

The Pelennor Fields lay just outside the city of Minas Tirith, on the west bank of the river [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md). The land ran down toward the river in slopes and terraces. The City of Minas Tirith was located on the south-western corner of the fields, at the foot of [Mount Mindolluin](../M/Mindolluin.md).

A road ran north-east across the Pelennor Fields to the [Causeway Forts](../C/Causeway_Forts.md) on the river bank; a distance of 12 miles. The [North-way](../G/Great_West_Road.md) led from the City through the fields to the [Forannest](../F/Forannest.md), or north-gate, before turning westward to become the [Great West Road](../G/Great_West_Road.md) to [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). The [South Road](../S/South_Road.md) came from the southern provinces of Gondor and passed through the Pelennor Fields on the way to Minas Tirith. A number of other paths also crisscrossed the fields.

The Pelennor Fields were home to farmers and herdsmen who had barns, pens, livestock, granaries, and kilns for drying hops and malt which were located on the Pelennor. The fields were fertile farmland, with tilled fields, orchards, and small brooks flowing down to the Anduin.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md) had [fallen](../S/Second_Fall_of_Minas_Ithil.md) and been renamed [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md), the Fields were walled by the great wall of [Rammas Echor](../R/Rammas_Echor.md) by [Ecthelion II](../E/Ecthelion_II.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2954](../T/Third_Age_2954.md), to prevent an invasion.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Presumably, the Fields took their name because of this enclosure.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the people and livestock evacuated the lands, seeing the coming invasion of the forces of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) from [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md). Indeed, [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and evil [Men](../M/Men.md) overran the Rammas Echor on [13 March](../_/13_March.md) and on [the next day](../_/14_March.md) [besieged the city](../S/Siege_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Fields_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fields-5">[5]</a></sup> Homes and trees were burned and trenches were carved through the fields and filled with fire. The Pelennor Fields were the location of the ensuing [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), when [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md), [King of Rohan](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) and the [Lord of the Nazgûl](../W/Witch-king.md) and his [Fell beast](../F/Fell_beasts.md) were slain.<sup id="cite_ref-Fields_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fields-5">[5]</a></sup>

### After the Battle {#After_the_Battle}

After the battle, the grass began to grow back on the fields. [Snowmane's Howe](../S/Snowmane's_Howe.md), where [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md)'s [horse](../S/Snowmane.md) was buried, grew especially green. The location at which the Witch-king and his steed had been killed was stained black and no grass grew from the scorched earth.<sup id="cite_ref-Fields_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fields-5">[5]</a></sup>

On [1 May](../_/1_May.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) the coronation of [King Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) took place, and those of Gondor and [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) gathered on the Pelennor Fields to witness the event.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Pelennor* translates to "fenced, encircled land" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-lotrindex_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lotrindex-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) has noted that the first element derives from the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) root/element *[pel-](PEL~34941.md)* ("go round, encircle");<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> the other elements appear to be *end* (from *enedh* "middle")' + *[(n-)dor](../D/Dôr.md)* ("land, dwelling").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> The field was called by several other names as well, such as *Fields of Pelennor*, *the Pelennor*, and *the townlands*.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-8">[8]</a></sup>
