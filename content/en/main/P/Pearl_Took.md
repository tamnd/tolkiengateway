**Pearl Took** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Pearl was the eldest child of [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Paladin Took II](/wiki/Paladin_Took_II) and [Eglantine Banks](/wiki/Eglantine_Banks).<sup id="cite_ref-Took_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-1">[1]</a></sup> She was the caretaker of [Lalia the Fat](/wiki/Lalia_Clayhanger "Lalia Clayhanger"), the rotund, aged, and immensely annoying (to the Tooks) matriarch of the family. Each morning, Pearl was to push her wheelchair outside so Lalia could experience fresh air.<sup id="cite_ref-L214_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L214-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001") she was a guest at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-Took_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-1">[1]</a></sup>

One morning in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1402](/wiki/Third_Age_3002 "Third Age 3002"), Lalia's attendant was a little bit careless in her custom of tending the old Hobbit, and the matriarch flew across the steps to her death. It was widely rumuored that Pearl was the attendant in question. As a formal expression of displeasure, Pearl was excluded from the accession ceremony of [Ferumbras](/wiki/Ferumbras_Took_III "Ferumbras Took III"), who had been waiting to become head of the clan for quite a while (he had already been Thain since the death of his father). She was, however, afterwards spotted with a necklace of pearls believed to be a Took family heirloom.<sup id="cite_ref-L214_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L214-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Pearl's name follows the Hobbit custom of high-class women being named after jewels.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
