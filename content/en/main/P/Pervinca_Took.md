**Pervinca Took** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Pervinca was the third child and youngest daughter of [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Paladin Took II](/wiki/Paladin_Took_II) and [Eglantine Banks](/wiki/Eglantine_Banks), and elder sister to [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took).

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001") she was a guest at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/pervinca" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Pervinca</a> refers to periwinkle, trailing or erect evergreen plants such as the European creeper.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
