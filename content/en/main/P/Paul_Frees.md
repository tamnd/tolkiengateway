**Solomon Hersh Frees** (1920-1986, aged 66) was an American actor and author, but above all else a legendary voice actor, nicknamed "The Man of a Thousand Voices". He lent four of those to characters in the [Rankin/Bass](/wiki/Rankin/Bass) animated films: in [The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)"), he voiced [Bombur](/wiki/Bombur) and the first [Troll](/wiki/Trolls "Trolls"); in [The Return of the King](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)") he took over [Cyril Ritchard](/wiki/Cyril_Ritchard)'s part of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), and voiced an unnamed [Goblin](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs").
