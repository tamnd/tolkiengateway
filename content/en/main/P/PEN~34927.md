**PEN**, **PENE** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "lack, have not; be without"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[pen](/wiki/Pen#Quenya "Pen")* ("without, not having"); *[penin](/wiki/Penin "Penin")* ("no; I haven't")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[pen-](/wiki/Pen- "Pen-")*<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin): *[Pendi](/wiki/Pendi)* ("the lacking, the poor")<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup>
