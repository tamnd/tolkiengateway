<i><b>Pesma o Otruu i Itrun</b></i>, published in [2002](/wiki/2002), is a bilingual (Serbian/English) edition of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem *[The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun)*. The book was printed in 500 copies, and it is the first reprint of the poem since its publication in *[The Welsh Review](</wiki/The_Welsh_Review_(Vol.IV,_No.4)> "The Welsh Review (Vol.IV, No.4)")* ([1945](/wiki/1945)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

An expanded edition was published in [2015](/wiki/2015).
