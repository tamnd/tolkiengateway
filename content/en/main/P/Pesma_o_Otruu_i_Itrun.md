<i><b>Pesma o Otruu i Itrun</b></i>, published in [2002](../_/2002.md), is a bilingual (Serbian/English) edition of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem *[The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun](../T/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun.md)*. The book was printed in 500 copies, and it is the first reprint of the poem since its publication in *[The Welsh Review](<../T/The_Welsh_Review_(Vol.IV,_No.4).md>)* ([1945](../_/1945.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

An expanded edition was published in [2015](../_/2015.md).
