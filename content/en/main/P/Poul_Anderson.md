**Poul William Anderson** ([November 25](../_/25_November.md), [1926](../_/1926.md) – [July 31](../_/31_July.md), [2001](../_/2001.md)) was an American author who primarily wrote science fiction.

In 1972, he was Guest of Honour at [Mythcon 3](../M/Mythcon_3.md).
