**Poul William Anderson** ([November 25](/wiki/25_November "25 November"), [1926](/wiki/1926) – [July 31](/wiki/31_July "31 July"), [2001](/wiki/2001)) was an American author who primarily wrote science fiction.

In 1972, he was Guest of Honour at [Mythcon 3](/wiki/Mythcon_3).
