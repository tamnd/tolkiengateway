**Percy** was a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") of [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town) in the [*Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)") film trilogy, who served as a porter. He was played by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nick_Blake&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nick Blake (page does not exist)">Nick Blake</a>.

## History {#History}

It is Percy who checked [Bard](/wiki/Bard) in as he re-entered Lake-town on his barge with [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) stowed away in wine barrels from the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Later, after [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug)'s destruction of Lake-town, Percy served as one of Bard's chief assistants in aiding the town's refugees, and was essentially Bard's second-in-command of the Army of Men during the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
