**Posco Baggins** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Posco was the son of [Polo Baggins](/wiki/Polo_Baggins) and a cousin of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins). He married [Gilly Brownlock](/wiki/Gilly_Brownlock), and together they had several children: [Ponto](/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_II "Ponto Baggins II"), [Porto](/wiki/Porto_Baggins "Porto Baggins"), [Peony](/wiki/Peony_Baggins "Peony Baggins"). While his family and descendants attended his cousin's [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001"), he did not.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
