**Posco Baggins** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Posco was the son of [Polo Baggins](Polo_Baggins.md) and a cousin of [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md). He married [Gilly Brownlock](../G/Gilly_Brownlock.md), and together they had several children: [Ponto](Ponto_Baggins_II.md), [Porto](Porto_Baggins.md), [Peony](Peony_Baggins.md). While his family and descendants attended his cousin's [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md) in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1401](../T/Third_Age_3001.md), he did not.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
