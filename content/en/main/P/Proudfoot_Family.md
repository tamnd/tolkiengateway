The **Proudfoot Family** was a family of [Hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits "Shire-hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

If [Odo Proudfoot](/wiki/Odo_Proudfoot) is to be taken as an example, at least some members of the family boasted large and exceptionally (even for Hobbits) hairy feet.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-1">[1]</a></sup>

There is some uncertainty among [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) about the plural form of "Proudfoot" - the family themselves apparently used "Proudfeet", but [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) pointedly preferred "Proudfoots".<sup id="cite_ref-Party_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-1">[1]</a></sup>
