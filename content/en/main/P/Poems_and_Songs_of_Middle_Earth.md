<i><b>Poems and Songs of Middle Earth</b></i> is a collection of verses from *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)* read by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), together with [Donald Swann](/wiki/Donald_Swann)'s (piano) and [William Elvin](/wiki/William_Elvin)'s (song) performance of *[The Road Goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")*, issued by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caedmon_Audio" title="wikipedia:Caedmon Audio">Caedmon Records</a> on a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gramophone_record" title="wikipedia:Gramophone record">gramophone record</a> in [1967](/wiki/1967). Tolkien recorded the verses on [15 June](/wiki/15_June) [1967](/wiki/1967).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

The front of the album cover features an illustration by [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes), an artist Tolkien appreciated and frequently used.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> On the back [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden) contributed with an introduction to Tolkien.
