<th><h2 style="margin:0;background:#cef2e0;font-size:120%;font-weight:bold;border:1px solid #a3bfb1;text-align:left;color:#000;padding:0.2em 0.4em;"><span class="mw-headline" id="Featured_Location_Article">Featured Location Article</span></h2>
</th></tr>
<tr>
<td style="color:#000">
<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:John_Howe_-_The_Drowning_of_Numenor.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/8/82/John_Howe_-_The_Drowning_of_Numenor.jpg/260px-John_Howe_-_The_Drowning_of_Numenor.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="260" height="260" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/8/82/John_Howe_-_The_Drowning_of_Numenor.jpg/390px-John_Howe_-_The_Drowning_of_Numenor.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/8/82/John_Howe_-_The_Drowning_of_Numenor.jpg/520px-John_Howe_-_The_Drowning_of_Numenor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="534" data-file-height="533"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure><b><a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor" title="Númenor">Númenor</a></b> was a Kingdom founded on the Isle of <a href="/wiki/Elenna" title="Elenna">Elenna</a>, an island raised from the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Great_Sea" title="Great Sea">Great Sea</a> as a gift to the <a href="/wiki/Edain" title="Edain">Edain</a> after the <a href="/wiki/War_of_Wrath" title="War of Wrath">War of Wrath</a>. It became one of the most powerful realms during the <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">Second Age</a> and was very influential on the events of the <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">Third Age</a>. Númenoreans were forbidden - by the <a href="/wiki/Valar" title="Valar">Valar</a> - from sailing so far westward that Númenor was no longer visible; however they would eventually begin to resent the <a href="/wiki/Ban_of_the_Valar" title="Ban of the Valar">Ban of the Valar</a>, compensating by travelling and establishing colonies eastwards instead. Nonetheless this resentment grew. After King <a href="/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n" title="Ar-Pharazôn">Ar-Pharazôn</a> took <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> hostage in <a href="/wiki/Second_Age_3262" title="Second Age 3262">S.A. 3262</a>, Sauron soon began to corrupt the King claiming that Númenoreans could achieve eternal life by worshipping <a href="/wiki/Morgoth" title="Morgoth">Melkor</a>. Upon Saurons bidding, Ar-Pharazôn built a great armada which set sail to wage war on the Valar in the west. <a href="/wiki/Manw%C3%AB" title="Manwë">Manwë</a> called upon <a href="/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar" title="Ilúvatar">Ilúvatar</a>, who destroyed the <a href="/wiki/Great_Armament" title="Great Armament">Great Armament</a> and reshaped the world; making the world round, drowning Elenna and Númenor with it.
