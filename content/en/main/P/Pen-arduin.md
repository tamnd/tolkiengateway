**Pen-arduin** was the dwelling of [Borlas](../B/Borlas.md) that was located within the [Emyn Arnen](../E/Emyn_Arnen.md) of [South Ithilien](../S/South_Ithilien.md).

## History {#History}

Pen-arduin was a small grey-stone house that was located atop the hills of [Arnen](../E/Emyn_Arnen.md), which was the region where his father, [Beregond](../B/Beregond.md), had moved to after becoming the Captain of the [White Company](../W/White_Company.md) at the beginning of the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md). The house had a fenced arbour which lay slightly below it near the steep east bank of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md). At the central point of Borlas's garden was a tree which Borlas called [Neldor](../N/Neldor.md). Sometime during Borlas's life, Pen-arduin started being used in connection with his name: Borlas of Pen-arduin.<sup id="cite_ref-House_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-House-2">[2]</a></sup>

One day, when Borlas cought a youth named [Saelon](../S/Saelon.md) stealing an unripe apple from the orchard, he brought the boy inside Pen-arduin, explaining to him that stealing unripe fruit solely to play with before tossing it aside was "[Orcs](../O/Orcs.md)' work". While Saelon eventually grew out of petty thievery, he harbored thoughts of revenge and sometimes thought about cutting down all of the trees in Pen-arduin, but never went though with the thought.<sup id="cite_ref-House_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-House-2">[2]</a></sup>

Sometime during the month of [June](../J/June.md) in the year [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [103](../F/Fourth_Age_103.md), Borlas was trimming a whistle of green willow with a sharp nail-knife at the arbour of Pen-arduin in the late afternoon. During which, Saelon came upon him and they began having a discussion about the nature of evil. Soon afterwards, Borlas talked rhetorically to his tree, [Neldor](../N/Neldor.md), before deciding to go inside his house.<sup id="cite_ref-House_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-House-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Pen-arduin* appears to be a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack and David Salo suggest a combination of *[pend](Pend.md)* ("fall"), *[ar(a)-](../A/Ar.md)* ("royal"), and *[duin](../D/Duin.md)* ("river").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [The History of Middle-earth Index](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth_Index.md), *Pen-arduin* was written as **Pen-Arduin**, though it is unknown whether or not this is an intentional change.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
