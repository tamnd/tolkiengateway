<i><b>Palantir</b></i> is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "far-sighted", and can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Palantíri](Palantíri.md) (singular *Palantír*), Seeing stones.
- [Tar-Palantir](../T/Tar-Palantir.md), the twenty-fourth [King](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).

## Chapters {#Chapters}

- "[The Palantír](../T/The_Palantír.md)", the eleventh chapter in *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md)*.
- "[The Palantíri](../T/The_Palantíri.md)", the last chapter in *[Unfinished Tales of Númenor and Middle-earth](../U/Unfinished_Tales_of_Númenor_and_Middle-earth.md)*.
