<i><b>Palantir</b></i> is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "far-sighted", and can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri) (singular *Palantír*), Seeing stones.
- [Tar-Palantir](/wiki/Tar-Palantir), the twenty-fourth [King](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).

## Chapters {#Chapters}

- "[The Palantír](/wiki/The_Palant%C3%ADr)", the eleventh chapter in *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*.
- "[The Palantíri](/wiki/The_Palant%C3%ADri)", the last chapter in *[Unfinished Tales of Númenor and Middle-earth](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales_of_N%C3%BAmenor_and_Middle-earth)*.
