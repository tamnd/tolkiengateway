**Foen** was a high mountain peak in the center of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), east of [Rivil's Well](/wiki/Rivil%27s_Well) and west of [Aeluin](/wiki/Aeluin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Foen* signified "Long Sight" due to its height. It was stated in a philological fragment of an uncertain date that Dorthonion "<i>was called also <b>Taur-na-Foen</b>, the Forest of the Foen</i>".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appears the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word *foen*, meaning "radiant, white".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
