<i><b>From Abjection to Alchemy: Tolkien&#39;s Middle-earth Legendarium</b></i> is the title of Sara Brown's PhD thesis, presented at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Salford" title="wikipedia:University of Salford">University of Salford</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

For the work on her thesis, Brown was permitted access to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s private papers held at the [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library): "*There she was able to study transcripts of lectures and drafts of some of Tolkien's essays and literary works, including a small note in the author's handwriting ... concerning the character of Gandalf*".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
