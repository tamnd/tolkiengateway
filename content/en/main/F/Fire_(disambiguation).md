**Fire** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Fire-drakes](/wiki/Fire-drakes), fire-breathing [dragons](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons").
- The [Hall of Fire](/wiki/Hall_of_Fire), a large meeting-place in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).
- The Mountain of Fire, [Orodruin](/wiki/Mount_Doom "Mount Doom").
- The Ring of Fire, [Narya](/wiki/Narya).
- The [Secret Fire](/wiki/Secret_Fire), a mysterious concept concerning the power of creation.
    - Also known as: [Fire of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Fire_of_Il%C3%BAvatar); [Flame Imperishable](/wiki/Flame_Imperishable).
