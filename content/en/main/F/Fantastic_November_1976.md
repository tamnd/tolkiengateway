<b><i>Fantastic</i> November 1976</b> is an issue of the magazine <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantastic_(magazine)" title="wikipedia:Fantastic (magazine)">Fantastic: Sword &amp; Sorcery and Fantasy Stories</a></i>. The article "White Wizard in Tweeds" by [L. Sprague de Camp](../L/L._Sprague_de_Camp.md) includes a few quotes and recollections of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted by de Camp during his visit in [1967](../_/1967.md) (pp. 84–6).
