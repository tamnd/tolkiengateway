**First Age 499** (abbreviated to **F.A. 499**) is the 499th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Notable events in this year include:

## Spring {#Spring}

- [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) issues forth from [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) and approaches [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil).<sup id="cite_ref-GA320_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA320-1">[1]</a></sup>
- <a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Ni%C3%ABnor_N%C3%ADniel" title="Niënor Níniel">Níniel</a> and [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) conceive a child.<sup id="cite_ref-GA320_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA320-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Summer {#Summer}

- [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin), with [Hunthor](/wiki/Hunthor) and [Dorlas](/wiki/Dorlas), goes in search of [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung). [Hunthor](/wiki/Hunthor) is killed by a falling rock and [Dorlas](/wiki/Dorlas) is killed by [Brandir](/wiki/Brandir) for deserting Túrin.<sup id="cite_ref-CH17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CH17-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Túrin finds Glaurung and kills the dragon.<sup id="cite_ref-CH17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CH17-2">[2]</a></sup>
- With his dying words, Glaurung reveals to Níniel that she is Nienor and Túrin is her brother. She commits suicide by throwing herself off the [Cabed-en-Aras](/wiki/Cabed-en-Aras) into the river [Taeglin](/wiki/Taeglin). Brandir witnesses this.<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Brandir tells Túrin that his wife is also his sister. Túrin disbelieves him and kills Brandir in anger.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Túrin learns from [Mablung](/wiki/Mablung) that Brandir spoke the truth. Túrin commits suicide by falling on [his sword](/wiki/Gurthang "Gurthang").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- The [Haladin](/wiki/House_of_Haleth "House of Haleth") erect a grave to Túrin and Nienor.<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Later {#Later}

- The [Petty-dwarf](/wiki/Petty-dwarves "Petty-dwarves") [Mîm](/wiki/M%C3%AEm) goes to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) and takes its treasure for his own.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
