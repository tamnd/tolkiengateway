**First Age 60** (abbreviated to **F.A. 60**) is the 60th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The Third [Battle of Beleriand](../B/Battles_of_Beleriand.md): [Dagor Aglareb](../D/Dagor_Aglareb.md) (*Glorious Battle*). [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) is decisively defeated by the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), led by [High King](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) [Fingolfin](Fingolfin.md) and [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md). The [Siege of Angband](../S/Siege_of_Angband.md) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Annals_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-2">[2]</a></sup>
- About this time, [Minas Tirith](<../M/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand).md>) on [Tol Sirion](../T/Tol_Sirion.md) is constructed.<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-2">[2]</a></sup>
