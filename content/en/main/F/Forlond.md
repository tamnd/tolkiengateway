**Forlond** was a harbour that lay on the northern shore of the [Gulf of Lune](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune), in the [Elvish](/wiki/Elves "Elves") land of [Forlindon](/wiki/Forlindon). It had a counterpart on the southern shore: the [Harlond](</wiki/Harlond_(Lindon)> "Harlond (Lindon)").

Forlond was located at the mouth of an unnamed river that traversed Forlindon, some two hundred miles to the northwest of the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the ships of the [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") general [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur) arrived in [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) during the [Angmar War](/wiki/Angmar_War), it is told that they filled the Forlond, Harlond and the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster), *Forlond* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "north-haven".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name used by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") on the [First Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The First Map of The Lord of the Rings") was *Forlorn*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
