**From an "Outsider"** is the title of a memoir written by [James Dundas-Grant](/wiki/James_Dundas-Grant) and originally published in *[C.S. Lewis at the Beakfast Table and Other Reminiscences](/wiki/Remembering_C.S._Lewis "Remembering C.S. Lewis")* (1979). Although foremostly a recollection of his teacher [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), Dundas-Grant also recalls memories of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). They first met at Lewis's rooms in Oxford in [1944](/wiki/1944):

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395753">.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider{margin-top:1rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem;color:var(--tg-color-subtle);display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:space-between}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider:not(:empty){gap:1rem;white-space:nowrap}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::before,.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::after{content:"";height:1px;width:100%;background-color:var(--tg-border-color-base)}</style><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>It was thus I came to meet [C.S. Lewis&#39;s] great friend Tolkien: tall, swept-back grey hair, restless. He read to us parts of his manuscript for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i>, asking for criticism.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>&#34;From an &#39;Outsider&#39;&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395753"/></div>

After <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=World_War_II&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="World War II (page does not exist)">World War II</a>, Dundas-Grant joined the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings) at the [Eagle and Child](/wiki/Eagle_and_Child):

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395753"/><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>We met every Tuesday morning over a glass of beer. <a href="/wiki/Warren_Lewis" title="Warren Lewis">Warnie</a>, [C.S. Lewis&#39;s] brother, was there; MacCallum of Pembroke; Father <a href="/wiki/Gervase_Mathew" title="Gervase Mathew">Gervase Mathew</a>, O.P., from Blackfriars; Tolkien of Merton and Havard. Others came and went. We sat in a small back room with a fine coal fire in winter. Back and forth the conversation would flow. Latin tags flying around. Homer quoted in the original to make a point. And Tolkien, jumping up and down, declaiming in <a href="/wiki/Old_English" title="Old English">Anglo-Saxon</a>.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>&#34;From an &#39;Outsider&#39;&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395753"/></div>

After the death of Lewis, Dundas-Grant finally recalls that he attended a Mass in memory of Lewis, together with Tolkien and [Robert Havard](/wiki/Robert_Havard).
