The **Folkmoot for Judgment**, also known as the **Moot of Brethil**, was a custom among the [Haladin](/wiki/House_of_Haleth "House of Haleth") where matters of high importance would be judged at the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Moot-ring&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Moot-ring (page does not exist)">Moot-ring</a> in a meeting attended by at least five hundred headmen.<sup id="cite_ref-Moot_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Moot-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

## History {#History}

It was during the three hundred and first Moot of Brethil that [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) was judged by a gathering of nearly a thousand Haladin under the charge of wounding [Hardang](/wiki/Hardang) and the false charge of being sent by [Angband](/wiki/Angband) to do harm to the [Men](/wiki/Men) of [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Forest_of_Brethil). [Manthor](/wiki/Manthor), acted as Húrin's "friend" during the moot.<sup id="cite_ref-Moot_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Moot-1">[1]</a></sup>

Near the end, Húrin accused the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) of mistreating [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) and [Niënor](/wiki/Ni%C3%ABnor); while accusing Hardang of causing [Morwen's](/wiki/Morwen "Morwen") death. Before Hardang was able to present his side of the matter, a revolt broke out in the Moot-ring, before moving to the wider [town](/wiki/Obel_Halad "Obel Halad"). In the ensuing battle, the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hall_of_the_Chieftains&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hall of the Chieftains (page does not exist)">Hall of the Chieftains</a> was burned down, Hardang was killed, Manthor was shot, and Avranc gained power over the remnant of the Haladin.<sup id="cite_ref-Moot_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Moot-1">[1]</a></sup>
