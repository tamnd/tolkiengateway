The **Folkmoot for Judgment**, also known as the **Moot of Brethil**, was a custom among the [Haladin](../H/House_of_Haleth.md) where matters of high importance would be judged at the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Moot-ring&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Moot-ring (page does not exist)">Moot-ring</a> in a meeting attended by at least five hundred headmen.<sup id="cite_ref-Moot_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Moot-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

## History {#History}

It was during the three hundred and first Moot of Brethil that [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) was judged by a gathering of nearly a thousand Haladin under the charge of wounding [Hardang](../H/Hardang.md) and the false charge of being sent by [Angband](../A/Angband.md) to do harm to the [Men](../M/Men.md) of [Forest of Brethil](Forest_of_Brethil.md). [Manthor](../M/Manthor.md), acted as Húrin's "friend" during the moot.<sup id="cite_ref-Moot_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Moot-1">[1]</a></sup>

Near the end, Húrin accused the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md) of mistreating [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) and [Niënor](../N/Niënor.md); while accusing Hardang of causing [Morwen's](../M/Morwen.md) death. Before Hardang was able to present his side of the matter, a revolt broke out in the Moot-ring, before moving to the wider [town](../O/Obel_Halad.md). In the ensuing battle, the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hall_of_the_Chieftains&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hall of the Chieftains (page does not exist)">Hall of the Chieftains</a> was burned down, Hardang was killed, Manthor was shot, and Avranc gained power over the remnant of the Haladin.<sup id="cite_ref-Moot_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Moot-1">[1]</a></sup>
