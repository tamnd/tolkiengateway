**First Age 313** (abbreviated to **F.A. 313**) is the 313th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [people of Marach](../H/House_of_Hador.md) enter [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) and settle in [Estolad](../E/Estolad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
