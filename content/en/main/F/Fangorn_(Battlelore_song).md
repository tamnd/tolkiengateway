<i><b>Fangorn</b></i> is a song by the epic metal band [Battlelore](../B/Battlelore.md) about the forest of [Fangorn](Fangorn_Forest.md). It appears in their first full-lenght album, [...Where the Shadows Lie](../_/%252E..Where_the_Shadows_Lie.md), released in [2002](../_/2002.md).
