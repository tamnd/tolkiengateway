**Fourth Age 67** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 67** or **F.O.**) is the 67th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 67 was also known as **S.R. 1488** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Elphir](../E/Elphir.md), [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
- [Alphros](../A/Alphros.md), son of Elphir, becomes the twenty-fourth [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-1">[1]</a></sup>
