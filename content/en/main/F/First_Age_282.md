**First Age 282** (abbreviated to **F.A. 282**) is the 282nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Marach](../M/Marach.md), founder of the [House of Marach/Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
