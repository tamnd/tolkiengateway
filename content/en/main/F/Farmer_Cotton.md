**Tolman "Tom" Cotton**, also known as **Farmer Cotton**, ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") – [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) [19](/wiki/Fourth_Age_19 "Fourth Age 19"), aged 99 years) was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Old Tom was a renowned farmer living on [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater)'s [South Lane](/wiki/South_Lane).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-1">[1]</a></sup> He was married to [Lily Brown](/wiki/Lily_Brown), and they had five children. [Young Tom](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Junior "Tolman Cotton Junior") was the eldest, and his siblings were [Rosie](/wiki/Rose_Cotton "Rose Cotton"), [Jolly](/wiki/Wilcome_Cotton_II "Wilcome Cotton II"), [Nick](/wiki/Bowman_Cotton "Bowman Cotton") and [Nibs](/wiki/Carl_Cotton "Carl Cotton").<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-2">[2]</a></sup>

Farmer Cotton played an important part in the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater), where he rallied many hobbits to fight [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey)'s men.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-1">[1]</a></sup>

At some point during the next two years, between [13 March](/wiki/13_March) and [6 October](/wiki/6_October), Cotton found [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) lying ill upon a bed, clutching a white gem in a manner reminiscent of the One Ring.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tolman* comes from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *tōl* + *mann* meaning "tool person".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In early phases of writing [Appendix F](/wiki/Appendix_F), Farmer Cotton's original [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name was given as *Tomacca Lothran*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or (earlier) *Tomakli Lothron*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
