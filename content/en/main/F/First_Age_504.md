**First Age 504** (abbreviated to **F.A. 504**) is the 504th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- With the [power](../L/Light_of_Valinor.md) of the [Silmaril](../S/Silmarils.md), [King](../K/King_of_Doriath.md) [Dior](../D/Dior.md) manages to restore the former glory of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md). He now wears the [Nauglamír](../N/Nauglamír.md) and the Jewel in public.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:351</sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
