**Findegil** was the [King's Writer](/wiki/King%27s_Writer) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).

## History {#History}

Findegil presumably served under the kingship of [Eldarion](/wiki/Eldarion)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).

In [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [172](/wiki/Fourth_Age_172 "Fourth Age 172"), Findegil wrote a copy of the [Thain's Book](/wiki/Thain%27s_Book) for the [Thain](/wiki/Thain) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), probably at the request of [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took)'s great-grandson. This copy was the only one to include [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)'s complete *[Translations from the Elvish](/wiki/Translations_from_the_Elvish)*, which Bilbo had composed while at [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) during his 'retirement' there.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The character is described as "King's Writer", but the exact meaning of the name is unglossed, although it is clearly [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggests the first element could be <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Find&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Find (page does not exist)">find</a></i> ("hair"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and Paul Strack suggests the last one could be [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") *tegil* ("pen").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
