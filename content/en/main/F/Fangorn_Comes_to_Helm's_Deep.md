**Fangorn Comes to Helm's Deep** is the sixty-first scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)"). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The scene begins with the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) retreating from the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg), now defeated. The [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) drives them away until they come to a forest, where [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) shouts, "Do not enter the forest! Keep away from the trees!" The army halts, and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and the rest of the army watch as the Uruk-hai and [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) head into the forest, where they hear the [Huorns](/wiki/Huorns) killing the retreating army of [Goblins](/wiki/Goblins). The Rohirrim cheer as [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s army is at last defeated and Gandalf smiles happily.
