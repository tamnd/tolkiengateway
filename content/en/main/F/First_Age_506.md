**First Age 506** (abbreviated to **F.A. 506**) is the 506th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Early {#Early}

- [Celegorm](../C/Celegorm.md) inflames [his brethren](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md), and they prepare an assault on [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), coming up at unawares in winter.<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-2">[2]</a></sup>

## [Yule](../Y/Yule.md) {#Yule}

- The [Second Kinslaying](../S/Second_Kinslaying.md) occurs, when the Sons of Fëanor attack [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) on its east marches, seeking for the [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Celegorm is slain by [Dior](../D/Dior.md). Deaths of [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md) and [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md). Both Dior and his wife [Nimloth](<../N/Nimloth_(elf_of_Doriath).md>) are killed.<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>
- [Eluréd](../E/Eluréd.md) and [Elurín](../E/Elurín.md) are abandoned to die in the forest by the cruel servants of Celegorm.<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-2">[2]</a></sup>
