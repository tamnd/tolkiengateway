**First Age 459** (abbreviated to **F.A. 459**) is the 459th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) and [Huor](../H/Huor.md) depart [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) and return home to [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md). They tell no-one where they have been or what they saw, keeping an oath of silence given to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
