The **Field of Cormallen** was a tree-lined field in [North Ithilien](/wiki/North_Ithilien), near the bank of the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) near [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros) and [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn).<sup id="cite_ref-RKCorm_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKCorm-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is told that the tree *[culumalda](/wiki/Culumalda "Culumalda")*, having yellow flowers, grew in the Field of Cormallen.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After returning from the [Battle of the Morannon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon) on [25 March](/wiki/25_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) set up his camp on the Field of Cormallen. There, he healed the exhausted [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") after their rescue from the wreckage of [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom) and put them in a long sleep.<sup id="cite_ref-RKCorm_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKCorm-1">[1]</a></sup> On [8 April](/wiki/8_April) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> after waking up from a long sleep the [Ringbearers](/wiki/Ringbearers) were greeted by many friends, and were received by Aragorn and praised before the Host of the West in a celebration of the victory in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-RKCorm_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKCorm-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Cormallen* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. It means "golden circle" from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Cor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cor (page does not exist)">cor</a></i> ("ring") and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mallen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mallen (page does not exist)">mallen</a></i> ("golden"). Apparently the name derived from the tree *[culumalda](/wiki/Culumalda "Culumalda")* that grew there.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) likely used the name *Cormalthen* as in [Gondor Sindarin](/wiki/Gondor_Sindarin) compounds retained the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fricative_consonant" title="wikipedia:Fricative consonant">spirant</a>. The spelling *Cormallen* follows the "proper" Elvish Sindarin, *malt* ("gold") and ending *-en* became \**mallen*, however in Gondor this remained *malthen*.<sup id="cite_ref-VT4227_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT4227-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
