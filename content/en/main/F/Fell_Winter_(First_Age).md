The **Fell Winter** of [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495")/[496](/wiki/First_Age_496 "First Age 496") was especially long and bitter, with ice and snow from November to March.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ-WH_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-WH-1">[1]</a></sup> It came immediately after the [Fall of Nargothrond](/wiki/Fall_of_Nargothrond), hindering both [Túrin's](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin") journey to [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) and [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and [Voronwë's](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB "Voronwë") to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-CH-RD_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CH-RD-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UT-TG_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-TG-4">[4]</a></sup>
