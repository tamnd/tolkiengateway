The **Devilry of Saruman**, or the **fire of Orthanc**, was a type of mysterious explosive substance that was supposedly created by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) at [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It was used by Saruman's army of [Uruk-Hai](../U/Uruk-Hai~9489.md) and [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md) to blast the culvert of the [Deeping Wall](../D/Deeping_Wall.md) during the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md) at [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-1">[1]</a></sup> It is unknown whether it was just a single substance, or if it was made up of a list of an assortment of ingredients, which would potentially most likely would have included gunpowder. Saruman is also said to have possibly have used it in the [Battle of Isengard](../B/Battle_of_Isengard.md) against the [Ents](../E/Ents.md). It may possibly have been the cause of the death of [Beechbone](../B/Beechbone.md).

It is possible that a similar substance was used by the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md)'s forces to blast breaches in the [Rammas Echor](../R/Rammas_Echor.md) at the onset of the [Siege of Gondor](../S/Siege_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Wild Men](../D/Drúedain.md) when later speaking with the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) said the destruction of the wall was caused by "earth-thunder".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<b><a href="../_/1978.md" title="1978">1978</a>: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>During the <a href="../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md" title="Battle of the Hornburg">Battle of the Hornburg</a>, magical projectiles travel out of <a href="../O/Orthanc.md" title="Orthanc">Orthanc</a> and fly over <a href="../H/Helm&#39;s_Deep.md" title="Helm&#39;s Deep">Helm&#39;s Deep</a>. <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> cries out &#34;Fire of Isengard!&#34; and one of the projectiles strikes the outer wall of Helm&#39;s Deep, breaching it and allowing the <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> to enter. The Eorlings retreat to the <a href="../G/Glittering_Caves.md" title="Glittering Caves">Glittering Caves</a>.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2002.md" title="2002">2002</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the <i><a href="../I/Isengard_Unleashed.md" title="Isengard Unleashed">Isengard Unleashed</a></i> scene, <a href="../G/Gríma.md" title="Gríma">Gríma</a> tells <a href="../S/Saruman.md" title="Saruman">Saruman</a> that <a href="../H/Hornburg.md" title="Hornburg">the Hornburg</a> was a weakness in a small culvert in the wall. Saruman fills an orb with powder and Gríma asks him &#34;How can fire undo stone?&#34;. In the scene <i><a href="../T/The_Breach_of_the_Deeping_Wall.md" title="The Breach of the Deeping Wall">The Breach of the Deeping Wall</a></i>, an <a href="../U/Uruk-hai.md" title="Uruk-hai">Uruk-hai</a> place the powder, sealed in a spiked ball, in the culvert. A torch-bearing Uruk runs forward and <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> orders <a href="../L/Legolas.md" title="Legolas">Legolas</a> to &#34;bring him down&#34;. The Uruk, unfazed by Legolas&#39; arrows,  dives into the culvert, igniting the fire and triggering an explosion that breaches the wall of the fortress.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2014.md" title="2014">2014</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Card_Game.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game">The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The fire is depicted in the <a href="../_/2015.md" title="2015">2015</a> expansion <i>The Treason of Saruman</i> on the &#34;Devilry of Saruman&#34; card, illustrated by Álvaro Calvo Escudero. The card has the following effects:</dd></dl>

> When Revealed: Place 3 progress on the main quest (bypassing any active location). Until the end of the round, treat each character's text box as if it was blank (except for Traits).<br>Shadow: Attacking enemy gets +2 Attack and gains Toughness 2 until the end of the round.

<b><a href="../_/2023.md" title="2023">2023</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Tales_of_Middle-earth.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth">The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The fire is depicted on the &#34;Fire of Orthanc&#34; card, illustrated by Jeremy Paillotin. The card has the following effects:</dd></dl>

> Destroy target artifact or land. Creatures without flying can’t block this turn.
