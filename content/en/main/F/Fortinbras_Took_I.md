**Fortinbras Took I** was the twenty-fifth [Thain](../T/Thain.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Fortinbras was the son of [Ferumbras II](Ferumbras_Took_II.md). He inherited the Thainship upon his father's death in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1201](../T/Third_Age_2801.md), and passed it on to his son [Gerontius](../G/Gerontius_Took.md) after his death.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortinbras" title="wikipedia:Fortinbras">Fortinbras</a> is the name of two characters in Shakespeare's <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlet" title="wikipedia:Hamlet">Hamlet</a></i>. It is French for "strong in arms".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
