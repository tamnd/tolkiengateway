**Frances Walsh** ([January 10](/wiki/10_January "10 January"), [1959](/wiki/1959)) is a New Zealand screenwriter and producer. She is the wife of [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson), director of *[The Lord of the Rings (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)>)* and *[The Hobbit (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)>)*. She, Jackson, and [Philippa Boyens](/wiki/Philippa_Boyens) are credited with writing the screenplays for all six films, as well as [Guillermo del Toro](/wiki/Guillermo_del_Toro) for *The Hobbit (film series)*. She also wrote the songs "[Gollum's Song](/wiki/Gollum%27s_Song)", "[Into the West](/wiki/Into_the_West)" and "<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=A_Shadow_Lies_Between_Us&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="A Shadow Lies Between Us (page does not exist)">A Shadow Lies Between Us</a>". Walsh also provided the "Ringwraith scream" sound effect for the films.
