**Frances Walsh** ([January 10](../_/10_January.md), [1959](../_/1959.md)) is a New Zealand screenwriter and producer. She is the wife of [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md), director of *[The Lord of the Rings (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)* and *[The Hobbit (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)*. She, Jackson, and [Philippa Boyens](../P/Philippa_Boyens.md) are credited with writing the screenplays for all six films, as well as [Guillermo del Toro](../G/Guillermo_del_Toro.md) for *The Hobbit (film series)*. She also wrote the songs "[Gollum's Song](../G/Gollum's_Song.md)", "[Into the West](../I/Into_the_West.md)" and "<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=A_Shadow_Lies_Between_Us&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="A Shadow Lies Between Us (page does not exist)">A Shadow Lies Between Us</a>". Walsh also provided the "Ringwraith scream" sound effect for the films.
