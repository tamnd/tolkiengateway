**Fourth Age 10** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 10** or **F.O.**) is the 10th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 10 was also known as **S.R. 1431** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Goldilocks Gardner](../G/Goldilocks_Gardner.md), sixth child and third daughter of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Rose Cotton](../R/Rose_Cotton.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Odovacar Bolger](../O/Odovacar_Bolger.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
