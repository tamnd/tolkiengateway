**Frár** was a [dwarf of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor "Dwarves of Erebor").

## History {#History}

Frár was part of [Balin's Colony](/wiki/Balin%27s_Colony) to reclaim [Moria](/wiki/Moria). Their mission at first seemed successful, and [Balin](/wiki/Balin) went so far as to claim the title [Lord of Moria](/wiki/Lord_of_Moria). This all changed in their fifth year there. On November 10, the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) came up the [Silverlode](/wiki/Silverlode) and killed Balin while he was alone, and unaware and looking into [Mirrormere](/wiki/Mirrormere). The Dwarves fought back but eventually lost [East-gate](/wiki/East-gate) and [First Hall](/wiki/First_Hall). The Orcs made it across the [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm) and there was a [battle in the Second Hall](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Second_Hall "Battle of the Second Hall"), and Frár and his companions [Lóni](/wiki/L%C3%B3ni) and [Náli](/wiki/N%C3%A1li) died bravely while the rest of the colonists retreated.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Frár* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. His name most likely means "swift".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
