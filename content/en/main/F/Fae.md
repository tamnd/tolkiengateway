<i><b>fae</b></i> is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "spirit".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[fëa](Fëa.md)*

<dl><dd>Tolkien mentions that the name <i><a href="Fëanor.md" title="Fëanor">Fëanor</a></i> is a Sindarinized version of the Quenya <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="Fëanáro.md" title="Fëanáro">Fëanáro</a></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> so is not proper Sindarin. His genuine Sindarin name would be <i>Faenor</i> instead.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

<b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/fae" title="wiktionary:fae">Fae</a></b> is an archaic English word referring to "magic" or "[fairies](Fairies.md)". This word was used by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in the early version of his [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) (*[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*) to refer to the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and the [Elves](../E/Elves.md). <span></span>
