**First Age 402** (abbreviated to **F.A. 402**) is the 402nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) attempt to pierce the [Pass of Aglon](../P/Pass_of_Aglon.md), but they are defeated by [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) and [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md), who are aided by the sons of [Finarfin](Finarfin.md) and [Edain](../E/Edain.md) of the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Annals_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-2">[1]</a></sup>
