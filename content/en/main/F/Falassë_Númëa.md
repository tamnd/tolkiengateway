**Falassë Númëa** was the western shore of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. In these shores and beaches, the [Solosimpi](/wiki/Solosimpi) sailed in hosts upon their [swanships](/wiki/Swanships "Swanships").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Falassë Númëa* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), meaning the "Western Surf".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is a combination of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Falass%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Falassë (page does not exist)">falassë</a></i> ("shore, beach, surf") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=N%C3%BAm%C3%ABa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Númëa (page does not exist)">númëa</a></i> ("in the west, western").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
