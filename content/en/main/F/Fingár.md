**Fingár** was a son of [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) in the early versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").<sup id="cite_ref-Fin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fin-1">[1]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about him.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Fingár* is in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English), since it was translated by [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine), but no [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) equivalent is given.<sup id="cite_ref-Fin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fin-1">[1]</a></sup>
