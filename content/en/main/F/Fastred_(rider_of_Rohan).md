**Fastred** was a man of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

Fastred rode with his king [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) to the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), where he was killed. His name was remembered in the *[Song of the Mounds of Mundburg](/wiki/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fastred* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name, which means "firm-counsel".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It contains the Old English elements *fæst* ("firm", "solid") and *rǽd* ("counsel", "advice").<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Fastred is a major character in the <a href="/wiki/Rohan" title="Rohan">Rohan</a> storyline. A great warrior, Fastred is also stubborn and rash, always seeking to revenge against the <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> for the death of his father. When <a href="/wiki/%C3%89omer" title="Éomer">Éomer</a> orders all people to abandon <a href="/wiki/Eastemnet" title="Eastemnet">Eastemnet</a> and move across the <a href="/wiki/Entwash" title="Entwash">Entwash</a> river, Fastred openly defies his order, keeping his family in the city and leading his riders against the raiding parties of the <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a> Orcs instead. Fastred is next met by the player in the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Druadan_Forest" title="Druadan Forest">Druadan Forest</a> during the ride of the Rohirrim to <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>, and he later fights in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>.</dd></dl>
