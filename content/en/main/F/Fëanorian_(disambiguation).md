**Fëanorian** can refer to:

- The **[House of Fëanor](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor)**, whose members were **[Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor)** himself, his [Seven Sons](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor") and their followers.
- The Feanorian script, more usually known as the <i><b><a href="/wiki/Tengwar" title="Tengwar">Tengwar</a></b></i>.
- The **[Fëanorian dialect](/wiki/F%C3%ABanorian_dialect)** of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) in [East-Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand "East Beleriand").
- The **[Fëanorian lamps](/wiki/F%C3%ABanorian_lamps)**, a rare Noldorin artifact.
