**Faniel** was a [Ñoldorin Elf](/wiki/%C3%91oldor "Ñoldor") that was the third daughter of [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB) and [Indis](/wiki/Indis) in an apparently discarded concept of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She had two brothers, [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin), two sisters, [Findis](/wiki/Findis) and [Finvain](/wiki/%C3%8Drim%C3%AB "Írimë") and a half-brother, [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Faniel was born during the [Noontide of Valinor](/wiki/Noontide_of_Valinor), probably in [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion). She did not leave [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) during the [Flight of the Noldor](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Noldor),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> remaining instead in [Aman](/wiki/Aman).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Faniel* was a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name comprised of an unknown element and *[-iel](/wiki/-iel)* ("daughter, feminine suffix").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
