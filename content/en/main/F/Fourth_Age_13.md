**Fourth Age 13** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 13** or **F.O.**) is the 13th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 13 was also known as **S.R. 1434** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:==Earlier this Year==

- Death of [Thain](../T/Thain.md) [Paladin Took II](../P/Paladin_Took_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Took_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), son of Paladin and [Eglantine Banks](../E/Eglantine_Banks.md), becomes the thirty-second Thain of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Took_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [King Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) makes the Thain, the [Master](../M/Master_of_Buckland.md), and the Mayor Counsellors of the North-Kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-2">[2]</a></sup>

<h2><span id="Mid-year.27s_Day"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Mid-year&#39;s_Day"><a href="../M/Mid-year&#39;s_Day.md" title="Mid-year&#39;s Day">Mid-year&#39;s Day</a></span></h2>

- [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) is elected [Mayor of Michel Delving](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md) for the second time.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-2">[2]</a></sup>
