The **First Marshal of the Riddermark** was the first such Marshall, the highest military rank among the [Riders of Rohan](../_/Éored.md), and title of the lieutenants of the [King of Rohan](../K/King_of_Rohan.md).

The First Marshal's duty was to command the [Muster of Edoras](../M/Muster_of_Edoras.md), comprising the Riders of the capital [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) and the [surrounding lands](Folde.md), including the [King's Lands](../K/King's_Lands.md) and [Harrowdale](../H/Harrowdale.md). This contrasted with the duties of the [Second](../S/Second_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark.md) and [Third](../T/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark.md) marshals, whose duties were assigned by the King as the times demanded.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

## During the War of the Ring {#During_the_War_of_the_Ring}

Coming to the throne at a relatively young age, King [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md), the seventeenth King of Rohan, led the Riders of the Muster of Edoras himself, so that no First Marshal was needed, even in times of war. When the King later fell under [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md)'s power, [Gríma Wormtongue](../G/Gríma.md) worked to ensure that no First Marshal was appointed, leaving a vacuum of power for him to exploit. It was thus that at the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), there was no First Marshal of the Riddermark.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
