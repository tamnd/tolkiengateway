**Fankil**, also known as **Fangli** and **Fúkil** in the earlier stages of the writing, was a servant of [Melko](../M/Melko.md), and only appeared in the early outlines of the [Gilfanon](../G/Gilfanon.md)'s tale. He acted as the lieutenant of the Dark Lord,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> a role that would be taken by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) in the final version of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.

Fankil is first mentioned in a note regarding the [Awakening of the Elves](../A/Awakening_of_the_Elves.md) as having escaped into the world with the other dark shapes when the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) broke [Utumna](../U/Utumno.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He then came in order to live among the First [Men](../M/Men.md), presumably in [Palisor](../P/Palisor.md). He, along with his [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> and [goblins](../G/Goblins.md) mingled among Men in order to estrange them from the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and some joined the evil [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md). This resulted to the [Battle of Palisor](../B/Battle_of_Palisor.md), the first war between Elves and goblins. In some earlier sketch, Fangli and his evil Dwarves, conquered Palisor. The Men corrupted by him became wild and savage peoples, worshiping Melko.
