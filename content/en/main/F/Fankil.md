**Fankil**, also known as **Fangli** and **Fúkil** in the earlier stages of the writing, was a servant of [Melko](/wiki/Melko), and only appeared in the early outlines of the [Gilfanon](/wiki/Gilfanon)'s tale. He acted as the lieutenant of the Dark Lord,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> a role that would be taken by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in the final version of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.

Fankil is first mentioned in a note regarding the [Awakening of the Elves](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves) as having escaped into the world with the other dark shapes when the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) broke [Utumna](/wiki/Utumno "Utumno").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He then came in order to live among the First [Men](/wiki/Men), presumably in [Palisor](/wiki/Palisor). He, along with his [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> and [goblins](/wiki/Goblins "Goblins") mingled among Men in order to estrange them from the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and some joined the evil [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). This resulted to the [Battle of Palisor](/wiki/Battle_of_Palisor), the first war between Elves and goblins. In some earlier sketch, Fangli and his evil Dwarves, conquered Palisor. The Men corrupted by him became wild and savage peoples, worshiping Melko.
