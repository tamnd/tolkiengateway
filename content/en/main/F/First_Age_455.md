**First Age 455** (abbreviated to **F.A. 455**) is the 455th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). This year is also known as the **Fell Year**.

Notable events in this year include:

## Winter {#Winter}

- The Fourth [Battle of Beleriand](/wiki/Battles_of_Beleriand "Battles of Beleriand"): [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) (*Battle of the Sudden Flame*). [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) breaks the [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband) marking the end of [Long Peace](/wiki/Long_Peace).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen) is destroyed by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s rivers of fire and is renamed the [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith), the Gasping Dust.<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) leads forth an army of [balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") and [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs"). The [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) suffer heavy losses and are scattered across [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") is cut off from the rest of army in the [Fen of Serech](/wiki/Fen_of_Serech); he is saved by [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir). Thereafter, oaths are made between Barahir and Finrod and Barahir [receives a ring](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir "Ring of Barahir") which becomes an heirloom.<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) is lost and its people remove to [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum). [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) are defeated and flee to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond). Orcs capture [Mount Rerir](/wiki/Mount_Rerir), destroy [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion) and defile [Lake Helevorn](/wiki/Lake_Helevorn); [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir) removes to [Amon Ereb](/wiki/Amon_Ereb).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Deaths of [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod), [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor), [Bregolas](/wiki/Bregolas), [Hador Lórindol](/wiki/Hador "Hador") and [Gundor](/wiki/Gundor).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Gelmir](</wiki/Gelmir_(son_of_Guilin)> "Gelmir (son of Guilin)") is captured.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon), learning of the battle, sends ships to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) to ask for aid. No aid comes and few ships return.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)*, the capture of [Minas Tirith](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand)> "Minas Tirith (Beleriand)") upon [Tol Sirion](/wiki/Tol_Sirion) and the defeat of [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) took place in this year, even before [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) was slain in his [last stand](/wiki/Fall_of_Fingolfin "Fall of Fingolfin") against Morgoth in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [456](/wiki/First_Age_456 "First Age 456").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Nevertheless, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) eventually did not include this change in the chronology into the [published *Silmarillion*](/wiki/The_Silmarillion "The Silmarillion"), preferring an earlier version from *[The Later Annals of Beleriand](/wiki/The_Later_Annals_of_Beleriand)*, where the city was captured in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [457](/wiki/First_Age_457 "First Age 457").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
