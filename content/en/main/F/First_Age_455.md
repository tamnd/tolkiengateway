**First Age 455** (abbreviated to **F.A. 455**) is the 455th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). This year is also known as the **Fell Year**.

Notable events in this year include:

## Winter {#Winter}

- The Fourth [Battle of Beleriand](../B/Battles_of_Beleriand.md): [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) (*Battle of the Sudden Flame*). [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) breaks the [Siege of Angband](../S/Siege_of_Angband.md) marking the end of [Long Peace](../L/Long_Peace.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md) is destroyed by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s rivers of fire and is renamed the [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md), the Gasping Dust.<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) leads forth an army of [balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md) and [orcs](../O/Orcs.md). The [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) suffer heavy losses and are scattered across [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Finrod Felagund](Finrod.md) is cut off from the rest of army in the [Fen of Serech](Fen_of_Serech.md); he is saved by [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md). Thereafter, oaths are made between Barahir and Finrod and Barahir [receives a ring](../R/Ring_of_Barahir.md) which becomes an heirloom.<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) is lost and its people remove to [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md). [Celegorm](../C/Celegorm.md) and [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md) are defeated and flee to [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md). Orcs capture [Mount Rerir](../M/Mount_Rerir.md), destroy [Thargelion](../T/Thargelion.md) and defile [Lake Helevorn](../L/Lake_Helevorn.md); [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md) removes to [Amon Ereb](../A/Amon_Ereb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Deaths of [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md), [Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md), [Bregolas](../B/Bregolas.md), [Hador Lórindol](../H/Hador.md) and [Gundor](../G/Gundor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Gelmir](<../G/Gelmir_(son_of_Guilin).md>) is captured.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md), learning of the battle, sends ships to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) to ask for aid. No aid comes and few ships return.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to *[The Grey Annals](../T/The_Grey_Annals.md)*, the capture of [Minas Tirith](<../M/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand).md>) upon [Tol Sirion](../T/Tol_Sirion.md) and the defeat of [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) took place in this year, even before [Fingolfin](Fingolfin.md) was slain in his [last stand](Fall_of_Fingolfin.md) against Morgoth in [F.A.](First_Age.md) [456](First_Age_456.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Nevertheless, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) eventually did not include this change in the chronology into the [published *Silmarillion*](../T/The_Silmarillion.md), preferring an earlier version from *[The Later Annals of Beleriand](../T/The_Later_Annals_of_Beleriand.md)*, where the city was captured in [F.A.](First_Age.md) [457](First_Age_457.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
