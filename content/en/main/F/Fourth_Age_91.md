**Fourth Age 91** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 91** or **F.O.**) is the 91st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 91 was also known as **S.R. 1512** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Dwalin](../D/Dwalin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Note {#Note}

The family tree of the [House of Durin](../H/House_of_Durin.md) in [Appendix A](../A/Appendix_A.md) also gives this year as "[3112](../T/Third_Age_3112.md)" of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md). <span></span>
