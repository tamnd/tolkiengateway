**First Age 590** (abbreviated to **F.A. 590**) is the 590th, and last, [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- After the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) is banished forever into the [Void](../V/Void.md) through the [Door of Night](../D/Door_of_Night.md),<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup> which will be guarded by [Eärendil the Mariner](../E/Eärendil.md) until the coming of the [Dagor Dagorath](../D/Dagor_Dagorath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The [Host of the West](../H/Host_of_the_Valar.md) finally leaves sunken [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) and summons the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) to remove to [Aman](../A/Aman.md) and settle on [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md), but [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md), [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md), [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md), [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), and many [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) elect to remain in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-2">[2]</a></sup>
- As a gift to the [Men](../M/Men.md) of the Three Houses of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) (as well as the remaining [Drúedain](../D/Drúedain.md) of Beleriand<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>) who had helped to defeat Morgoth, the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) raise the island of [Elenna](../E/Elenna.md) amidst the [Great Sea](../B/Belegaer.md). Guided by the [Star of Eärendil](../S/Star_of_Eärendil.md), most of them start migrating there to establish a [new kindom](../N/Númenor.md) removed from the dangers of Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-LotR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LotR-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-5">[5]</a></sup>
- The choice of the [Half-elven](../H/Half-elven.md): [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) chooses to be of the [Elven-kind](../E/Elves.md), and to remain in Middle-earth beside Gil-galad; while his brother [Elros](../E/Elros.md) chooses to be a [mortal Man](../G/Gift_of_Ilúvatar.md) and thus remains with the Edain.<sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LotR_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LotR-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-5">[5]</a></sup>
- The [First Age](First_Age.md) ends.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
