**First Age 457** (abbreviated to **F.A. 457**) is the 457th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Minas Tirith](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand)> "Minas Tirith (Beleriand)") upon [Tol Sirion](/wiki/Tol_Sirion) is captured by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), who makes the island his residence; it becames renamed [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth), "*Isle of Werewolves*". Although defeated, [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) is saved by [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin); they escape to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) together.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)*, the capture of Minas Tirith by Sauron took place in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455"), even before [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) was slain in his [last stand](/wiki/Fall_of_Fingolfin "Fall of Fingolfin") against [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> However, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) eventually did not include this change in the chronology into the [published *Silmarillion*](/wiki/The_Silmarillion "The Silmarillion"), preferring an earlier version from *[The Later Annals of Beleriand](/wiki/The_Later_Annals_of_Beleriand).*
