**First Age 472** (abbreviated to **F.A. 472**) is the 472nd [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). This year is also known as the **Year of Lamentation**.

Notable events in this year include:

## Spring {#Spring}

- Marriage of [Huor](/wiki/Huor) and [Rían](/wiki/R%C3%ADan).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) is setting out for [Barad Eithel](/wiki/Barad_Eithel).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Midsummer {#Midsummer}

- **The Fifth [Battle of Beleriand](/wiki/Battles_of_Beleriand "Battles of Beleriand"): [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad)** (*Battle of Unnumbered Tears*). The [army](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros "Union of Maedhros") of [elves](/wiki/Elves "Elves"), [men](/wiki/Men "Men") and [dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") are betrayed by [Ulfang](/wiki/Ulfang) causing their defeat. The armies suffer heavy losses in the face of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs"), [dragons](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") and [balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs").<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) dies in battle with [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)> "Gothmog (balrog)"), Lord of [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs).<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Azaghâl](/wiki/Azagh%C3%A2l) dies in battle with [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung).<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Deaths of [Huor](/wiki/Huor), [Hundar](/wiki/Hundar), [Haldir](</wiki/Haldir_(Chieftain_of_the_Haladin)> "Haldir (Chieftain of the Haladin)"), [Gelmir](</wiki/Gelmir_(son_of_Guilin)> "Gelmir (son of Guilin)"), [Uldor](/wiki/Uldor), [Ulfast](/wiki/Ulfast), [Ulwarth](/wiki/Ulwarth), [Borlach](/wiki/Borlach), [Borlad](/wiki/Borlad) and [Borthand](/wiki/Borthand).<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) and [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor) are captured. [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) refuses to tell [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) the location of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin); Morgoth taunts Húrin at the [Haudh-en-Ndengin](/wiki/Hill_of_the_Slain "Hill of the Slain").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) succeeds [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) as [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and returns to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Late {#Late}

- Birth of [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor). [Rían](/wiki/R%C3%ADan) commits Tuor to the care of the elf [Annael](/wiki/Annael).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-5">[5]</a></sup>
- Rían goes to the [Haudh-en-Nirnaeth](/wiki/Hill_of_the_Slain "Hill of the Slain") and dies.<sup id="cite_ref-GA_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-5">[5]</a></sup>
- Death of [Glóredhel](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3redhel).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
- [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) sent to [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) by his mother [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
