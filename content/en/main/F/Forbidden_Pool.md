The **Forbidden Pool** was a basin of water created by the waterfall that concealed one of the two entrances into [Henneth Annûn](../H/Henneth_Annûn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the days of [Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Túrin II](../T/Túrin_II.md), most of the remaining people of Ithilien departed due to the influx of [Mordor-orcs](../M/Mordor-orcs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">[2]</a></sup> In response, he had many secret outposts and refuges established for [his soldiers](../R/Rangers_of_Ithilien.md) and in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2901](../T/Third_Age_2901.md) Henneth Annûn was constructed.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">[2]</a></sup> It remained the longest guarded and manned.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Rangers fiercely protected the secrecy of their refuge. The pool itself was not particularly special however it was deemed to be one of its boundaries so to swim in it was seen as trespassing upon the stronghold and held the penalty of death.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

While [Frodo](Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) are in Henneth Annûn, [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md), searching for fish, chanced to find the pool by accident. Frodo convinced Faramir to spare him and instead let him go down and rescue him.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
