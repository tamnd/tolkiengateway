**First Age 501** (abbreviated to **F.A. 501**) is the 501st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) leaves [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) with [Asgon](../A/Asgon.md) and six others. They seek [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) and accidentally reveal its rough location to [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Hurin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hurin-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) heads to [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md) and meets [Morwen](../M/Morwen.md) at [their children's grave](../S/Stone_of_the_Hapless.md) just before she dies.<sup id="cite_ref-Hurin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hurin-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Húrin is imprisoned by the [Haladin](../H/House_of_Haleth.md) and accuses them of failing to help his family. A revolt occurs and the Chieftain, [Hardang](../H/Hardang.md), is killed as is Húrin's friend [Manthor](../M/Manthor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Hurin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hurin-1">[1]</a></sup>
- With the Haladin leaderless, and [Ephel Brandir](../E/Ephel_Brandir.md) in ruins, Húrin heads towards his children's grave and leaves Brethil.<sup id="cite_ref-Hurin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hurin-1">[1]</a></sup>
