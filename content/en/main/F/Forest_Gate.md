The **Forest Gate** was the western entrance to the [Elf-path](/wiki/Elf-path) that led across [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) to the [Elvenking's Halls](/wiki/Elvenking%27s_Halls). It was formed as a sort of arch made by two great trees leaning together that were old, [ivy](/wiki/Ivies "Ivies")-strangled, hung with [lichen](/wiki/Lichen "Lichen"), and bearing but a few blackened leaves. Beyond the gate the path became a gloomy tunnel, such that from within the forest the gate appeared as a small bright hole.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was a four-day ride by horse from [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn)'s house directly north to the Forest Gate, which was also to the northeast of the confluence of the [Rhimdath](/wiki/Rhimdath) and [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) Rivers. When [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) and company came to the gate, they camped there overnight before entering the forest. It was at this point that they released [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn)'s [ponies](/wiki/Ponies "Ponies"), and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) left the party, to the consternation of the dwarves and [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
