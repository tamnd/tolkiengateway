The **Forest Gate** was the western entrance to the [Elf-path](../E/Elf-path.md) that led across [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) to the [Elvenking's Halls](../E/Elvenking's_Halls.md). It was formed as a sort of arch made by two great trees leaning together that were old, [ivy](../I/Ivies.md)-strangled, hung with [lichen](../L/Lichen.md), and bearing but a few blackened leaves. Beyond the gate the path became a gloomy tunnel, such that from within the forest the gate appeared as a small bright hole.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was a four-day ride by horse from [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md)'s house directly north to the Forest Gate, which was also to the northeast of the confluence of the [Rhimdath](../R/Rhimdath.md) and [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) Rivers. When [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) and company came to the gate, they camped there overnight before entering the forest. It was at this point that they released [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md)'s [ponies](../P/Ponies.md), and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) left the party, to the consternation of the dwarves and [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
