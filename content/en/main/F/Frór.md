**Frór** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2552](/wiki/Third_Age_2552 "Third Age 2552") - [2589](/wiki/Third_Age_2589 "Third Age 2589"), aged 37 years) was a [dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") of [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk) and the second son of King [Dáin I](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I).

## History {#History}

During his short life his people lived in the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains), where they were threatened by and at [war](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons "War of the Dwarves and Dragons") with [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons). In 2589 both Frór and his father were killed by a great [Cold-drake](/wiki/Cold-drakes "Cold-drakes") outside their [hall](/wiki/D%C3%A1in%27s_Halls "Dáin's Halls").

Frór's older brother [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) then became [King of Durin's Folk](/wiki/Kings_of_Durin%27s_Folk "Kings of Durin's Folk") and led part of his house back to [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain). The rest of those who left traveled East to the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) with the youngest brother [Grór](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3r), who founded his own [realm](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills "Dwarves of the Iron Hills").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Frór* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. His name most likely means "swift".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<b>2002: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i>: <i>Mines of Moria</i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Decipher" title="Decipher">Decipher</a> gave names to many of the unnamed characters in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Fellowship of the Ring">The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>, including those at the council. One card is named &#34;Frór, Gimli&#39;s Kinsman&#34;, however the Dwarf depicted is not a character in the film. Decipher used a photo of Gimli&#39;s scale double <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Brett_Beattie&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Brett Beattie (page does not exist)">Brett Beattie</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Council_of_Elrond" title="Council of Elrond">Council of Elrond</a> and presented him as though he was another character. This is incompatible with the film, as only four Dwarves are seen during the Council and &#34;Frór&#34; is sitting in Gimli&#39;s spot.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/D%C3%A1in%27s_Halls" title="Dáin&#39;s Halls">Thikil-gundu</a>, &#34;The Steel Keep&#34;, it is revealed that Frór, along with a number of other deceased dwarves, were brought back to life as dwarf-wights, and now stalk the halls of the Steel Keep. He is encountered by <a href="/wiki/Dori" title="Dori">Dori</a>&#39;s Company inside Afhân-binnîn, &#34;The Lore Trove&#34;.</dd></dl>
