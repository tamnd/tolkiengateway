The **Front Gate** was a common term for the **Gate of Erebor**, the wide arched main entrance of the Dwarf-realm of [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md). The [Great Chamber of Thrór](../G/Great_Chamber_of_Thrór.md) was near the Gate.

The entrance was at the head of the valley formed by the two southern ridges of rock thrust out from the southern slopes of Erebor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Out of the gateway sprang the stream that grew into the [River Running](../R/River_Running.md).<sup id="cite_ref-home_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-home-2">[2]</a></sup>

Through the gate passed a stone-paved road, wide enough for many men abreast. The gate itself was a tall arch and at one time displayed carven work, but during [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md)'s residence in the mountain the opening had become worn, splintered, and blackened. From the gate one could look out upon [Dale](../D/Dale.md) (or its ruins during the time of the dragon).<sup id="cite_ref-home_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-home-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2770](../T/Third_Age_2770.md), the [dragon](../D/Dragons.md) Smaug descend upon Erebor and entered the Mountain via the Front Gate, which unfortunately was wide enough to accommodate him.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> Over time Smaug had broken and blocked all of the other gates to the Mountain (except for the secret [Back Door](../B/Back_Door.md)), leaving only the Front Gate for his use.<sup id="cite_ref-Gather_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gather-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md), Smaug was slain.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) and company, having entered the Mountain through the [Back Door](../B/Back_Door.md), came out the Front Gate not knowing the fate of the dragon. When the dwarves learned of Smaug's death they returned to the Mountain and built a high thick wall of squared stones across the gate.<sup id="cite_ref-Gather_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gather-4">[4]</a></sup> This barrier allowed them to hold off the forces of the [Woodland Realm](../W/Woodland_Realm.md) and the [Lake-men](../L/Lake-men.md) while Thorin awaited the coming of [Dáin Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md). However, hard upon Dáin's arrival the goblins of [Bolg](../B/Bolg.md) also appeared and the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md) began. In the middle of the battle Thorin and his companion dwarves levered the wall at the Front Gate to fall outwards and they then rushed into the battle.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> After the battle was over it was at the Front Gate that Bilbo took his leave of the surviving dwarves before heading home.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Dáin II Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) was less fortunate: he fell in battle during the [Battle of Dale](../B/Battle_of_Dale.md) at the threshold of the Gate, but the [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) could not take it; it protected his people in the short and unsuccessful siege that followed.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> <span></span>
