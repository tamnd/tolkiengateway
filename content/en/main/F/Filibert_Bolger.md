**Filibert Bolger** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Filibert married [Poppy Chubb-Baggins](/wiki/Poppy_Chubb-Baggins). With his wife, he was present at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Bolger family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Filibert* is a Germanic name that contains the elements *fili* ("very") and *berht* ("bright"). Its modern English form is *Fullbright*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
