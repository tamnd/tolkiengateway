**Folca** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2804](/wiki/Third_Age_2804 "Third Age 2804") – [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2864](/wiki/Third_Age_2864 "Third Age 2864"), aged 60 years) was the thirteenth [King of Rohan](/wiki/King_of_Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-Folca_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folca-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Folca was the son of King [Walda](/wiki/Walda), who was born in a time when his country was still plagued by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs)<sup id="cite_ref-Walda_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walda-2">[2]</a></sup> who had invaded Rohan after the [War of Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_Dwarves_and_Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> At the time of his grandfather [Brytta](/wiki/Brytta)'s death it was believed that Rohan had been freed of the Orcs. However, some Orcs remained, and in [2851](/wiki/Third_Age_2851 "Third Age 2851") after ruling just nine years Walda and all his companions were killed by Orcs when they rode from [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) on mountain paths.<sup id="cite_ref-Walda_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walda-2">[2]</a></sup>

Folca was a great hunter, but he swore not to hunt wild beasts as long as there were Orcs left in Rohan. During his life all orc-holds in Rohan were destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-Folca_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folca-1">[1]</a></sup>

After Rohan had been cleared of Orcs, Folca went to the [Firienwood](/wiki/Firienholt "Firienholt") to hunt the great [Boar of Everholt](/wiki/Boar_of_Everholt). He managed to kill the boar, but suffered a mortal wound from the beast's tusks.<sup id="cite_ref-Folca_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folca-1">[1]</a></sup> He was buried in the fourth mound on the east-side of the Barrow-field<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> on the left side of the road that approached Edoras<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> from the north-west.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Folca was succeeded by his son [Folcwine](/wiki/Folcwine).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Folca* is derived from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *folc*, which means "folk" or "people".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
