**Fimbrethil** or **Wandlimb** was [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard)'s long-lost [entwife](/wiki/Entwives "Entwives").<sup id="cite_ref-Treebeard_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Treebeard-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The pair were beloved since before even [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) first arose in power during the youth of the world. As with all of the other Entwives, Fimbrethil had been missing since [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s forces destroyed the [gardens of the Entwives](/wiki/Brown_Lands "Brown Lands") during the Second Age.<sup id="cite_ref-Treebeard_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Treebeard-1">[1]</a></sup> At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Treebeard had not seen his beloved Fimbrethil for over 3,000 years.

During the [last march of the Ents](/wiki/Battle_of_Isengard "Battle of Isengard") against [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), Treebeard felt that many of the Ents were marching to their doom, and that indeed, he might never see his Fimbrethil ever again. Treebeard told [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") that Saruman had to be stopped, although he would dearly have liked to see Fimbrethil once again.<sup id="cite_ref-Treebeard_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Treebeard-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name *Fimbrethil* consists of the words *[fim](/wiki/Fim "Fim")* and *[brethil](</wiki/Brethil_(Sindarin_word)> "Brethil (Sindarin word)")*.

Different translations given by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) were: "slender-beech",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> "slim-birch",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and "slender princess".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
