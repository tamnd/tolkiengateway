**Fourth Age 21** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 21** or **F.O.**) is the 21st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 21 was also known as **S.R. 1442** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), [Rose](../R/Rose_Cotton.md) and [Elanor](../E/Elanor_Gardner.md) ride to [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and stay there for a year. During this period, Master [Tolman Cotton](../T/Tolman_Cotton_Junior.md) acts as deputy [Mayor](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Tolman Gardner](../T/Tolman_Gardner.md), thirteenth child and seventh son of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Rose Cotton](../R/Rose_Cotton.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
