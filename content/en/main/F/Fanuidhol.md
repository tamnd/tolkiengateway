**Fanuidhol** was one of the three peaks in the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) (with [Caradhras](../C/Caradhras.md) and [Celebdil](../C/Celebdil.md)) that lay above the ancient dwarf-city of [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

A short name used by the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) was **[Shathûr](../S/Shathûr.md)** - in full **[Bundushathûr](../B/Bundushathûr.md)**. It has the same meaning in [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md) as **Fanuidhol** in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) - they both mean **Cloudyhead**.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the common tongue, *Fanuidhol* was called "Cloudyhead". Both *Bundushathûr* and *Fanuidhol* translate to this.

*Bundushathûr* is Khuzdul - the first element "[bund](../B/Bund.md)" means "head". *Fanuidhol* is Sindarin, where the second element "[dol](../D/Dol.md)" means "head".
