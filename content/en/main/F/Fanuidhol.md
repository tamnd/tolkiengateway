**Fanuidhol** was one of the three peaks in the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) (with [Caradhras](/wiki/Caradhras) and [Celebdil](/wiki/Celebdil)) that lay above the ancient dwarf-city of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

A short name used by the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) was **[Shathûr](/wiki/Shath%C3%BBr)** - in full **[Bundushathûr](/wiki/Bundushath%C3%BBr)**. It has the same meaning in [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) as **Fanuidhol** in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) - they both mean **Cloudyhead**.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the common tongue, *Fanuidhol* was called "Cloudyhead". Both *Bundushathûr* and *Fanuidhol* translate to this.

*Bundushathûr* is Khuzdul - the first element "[bund](/wiki/Bund "Bund")" means "head". *Fanuidhol* is Sindarin, where the second element "[dol](/wiki/Dol "Dol")" means "head".
