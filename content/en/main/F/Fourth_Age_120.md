**Fourth Age 120** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 120** or **F.O.**) is the 120th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 120 was also known as **S.R. 1541** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## March {#March}

- **1** - Death of King [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) of the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md). [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md), son of Elessar and [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md), becomes King.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Later {#Later}

- It is said that the beds of [Meriadoc](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Peregrin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) were set beside the bed of the great king.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) builds a grey ship in [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md), and with [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) sails down [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) and so over [Sea](../B/Belegaer.md). This marks the end in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) of the [Fellowship of the Ring](Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other version of the legendarium {#Other_version_of_the_legendarium}

In the second edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, the date of [Aragorn's](../A/Aragorn.md) death was ultimately changed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) from [Fo.A.](Fourth_Age.md) [100](Fourth_Age_100.md) (as in the first edition) to [Fo.A.](Fourth_Age.md) <a class="mw-selflink selflink">120</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Note {#Note}

The family tree of the [House of Durin](../H/House_of_Durin.md) in [Appendix A](../A/Appendix_A.md) also gives this year as "[3141](../T/Third_Age_3141.md)" of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).
