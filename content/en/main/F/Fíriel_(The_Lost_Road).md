**Fíriel** was the daughter of [Orontor](/wiki/Orontor) of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)*.

## History {#History}

She was a fair [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") maiden of the household of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil). While Elendil and his son [Herendil](/wiki/Herendil) were approaching their house, they heard Fíriel sing a [sad song](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel%27s_song "Fíriel's song") from a high window in the [Eressëan](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") tongue that Elendil and [Herendil](/wiki/Herendil) heard the first two lines of, beginning with "*Ilu Ilúvatar en káre*" ("The Father made the World")<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and going until her voice fell into the question "*O Ilúvatar, give me in that day beyond the end, when my Sun faileth?*" Then Elendil said "*E man antaváro? What will he give indeed?*" Herendil complained, saying that she should not be singing such things, as people in Númenor were now singing that [Melko](/wiki/Melko) would come back and give them the Sun forever, but his father despised those words.<sup id="cite_ref-Firiel_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Firiel-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fíriel* is only glossed in *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)* as a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) name of [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien), meaning "Mortal Maiden", from *feir* ("mortal"), and *[-iel](/wiki/-iel)* ("daughter").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

While Tolkien eventually discarded the character of Fíriel, the name would later be used for <a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/F%C3%ADriel_(disambiguation)" title="Fíriel (disambiguation)">other characters</a> within the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").
