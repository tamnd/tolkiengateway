**Fíriel** was the daughter of [Orontor](../O/Orontor.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Lost Road](../T/The_Lost_Road.md)*.

## History {#History}

She was a fair [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) maiden of the household of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md). While Elendil and his son [Herendil](../H/Herendil.md) were approaching their house, they heard Fíriel sing a [sad song](Fíriel's_song.md) from a high window in the [Eressëan](../Q/Quenya.md) tongue that Elendil and [Herendil](../H/Herendil.md) heard the first two lines of, beginning with "*Ilu Ilúvatar en káre*" ("The Father made the World")<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and going until her voice fell into the question "*O Ilúvatar, give me in that day beyond the end, when my Sun faileth?*" Then Elendil said "*E man antaváro? What will he give indeed?*" Herendil complained, saying that she should not be singing such things, as people in Númenor were now singing that [Melko](../M/Melko.md) would come back and give them the Sun forever, but his father despised those words.<sup id="cite_ref-Firiel_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Firiel-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fíriel* is only glossed in *[The Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* as a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) name of [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md), meaning "Mortal Maiden", from *feir* ("mortal"), and *[-iel](../_/-iel.md)* ("daughter").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

While Tolkien eventually discarded the character of Fíriel, the name would later be used for <a class="mw-disambig" href="Fíriel_(disambiguation).md" title="Fíriel (disambiguation)">other characters</a> within the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).
