**First Age 436** (abbreviated to **F.A. 436**) is the 436th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- At a feast, a double marriage is held between [Galdor](<../G/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-lómin).md>) and [Hareth](../H/Hareth.md), and between [Haldir](<../H/Haldir_(Chieftain_of_the_Haladin).md>) and [Glóredhel](../G/Glóredhel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
