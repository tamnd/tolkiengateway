**First Age 470** (abbreviated to **F.A. 470**) is the 470th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Dior](../D/Dior.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Hardang](../H/Hardang.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- *Possible* death of [Ulfang](../U/Ulfang.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>
