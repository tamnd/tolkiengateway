The **Falathrim** were the shoreland Elves of the western shores of the [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) who took [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) as their lord from the beginning. They dwelt mainly in the ancient havens of [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest) and [Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar) of the [Falas](/wiki/Falas), and their last home on Middle-earth before finally leaving for the Undying Lands was the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens).

## History {#History}

The [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) were the most numerous Elven clan and for this reason they were led by two brothers, [Elwë](/wiki/Elw%C3%AB) and [Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB) in the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey) to [Aman](/wiki/Aman). They were also the most slow and reluctant and many turned back or stayed behind. When Elwë became lost in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), many of his people refused to carry on until he had been found; those were named [Eglath](/wiki/Eglath), the forsaken.

It was Olwë who led the rest to the shores of Beleriand. As they lingered at the [Bay of Balar](/wiki/Bay_of_Balar) they were befriended and taught by [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s vassal, the [Maia](/wiki/Maia) [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB). When Ulmo returned with the [floating island](/wiki/Tol_Eressea "Tol Eressea") to take the bulk of the Teleri to the West, Ossë convinced some of them to forsake Valinor and the other Elves and stay behind with him.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

These became the Falathrim, and their leader was [Nowë](/wiki/Now%C3%AB),<sup id="cite_ref-LastWritingsN_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LastWritingsN-2">[2]</a></sup> kin of both Elwë and Olwë, who stayed behind despite his great eagerness to see the [light of Valinor](/wiki/Light_of_Valinor "Light of Valinor").<sup id="cite_ref-Cirdan_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirdan-3">[3]</a></sup> He was later known as Círdan. The Falathrim settled on Beleriand's western shores, building themselves Havens at [Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar) and [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest) and became the first builders and sailors of ships in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth)'s history.

When Elwë returned, he established the Kingdom of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), claiming overlordship of all Beleriand as King Elu Thingol. The Falathrim, along with the Eglath, were considered [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar), and found many pearls that they sent to him as gifts.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After the [Return of the Noldor](/wiki/Return_of_the_Noldor), the Elves of the Falas became fast in friendship with the [Deep Elves](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor"). Círdan attended the [Mereth Aderthad](/wiki/Mereth_Aderthad) with many of his people, where he swore oaths of friendship.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> With [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod)'s aid, the Havens of the Falas were strengthened and rebuilt. The Elves of the Havens enjoyed peaceful relations with all the [Elves of Beleriand](/wiki/Elves_of_Beleriand), and were particularly closely allied with the people of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond). As one of the most remote peoples from [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s forces in [Angband](/wiki/Angband), the other Elvish lords would often send their children to the Elves of the Havens for safekeeping.

They fought beside the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), too: in the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_assault_on_Hithlum&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second assault on Hithlum (page does not exist)">Second assault on Hithlum</a>, their mariners rescued High King [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) from overwhelming hordes in [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum). After the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), Morgoth sent his hosts to besiege them, and both the Havens were overthrown. Most of the Elves of the Falas perished in the [fall of the Falas](/wiki/Fall_of_the_Falas "Fall of the Falas"), or were captured, but Círdan escaped. With his surviving followers and the young [Ereinion Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad "Gil-galad"), who was at that time lodging with the people of the Havens, he sailed southwards, and settled for a time on the [Isle of Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar) maintaining a haven.

As [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s power overwhelmed the Eldar (perhaps soon after the Nirnaeth), some "seafaring Sindar from the west havens" fled with 3 small ships to the east. They ended up at the confluence of [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond) and [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3). There they established [Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond) and were eventually joined by their Telerin kin, [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves) from the north.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

During the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), Círdan and Gil-galad sailed east to [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) where Cirdan founded the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) on the newly formed [Gulf of Lhûn](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lh%C3%BBn), and the remnant of the Elves of the Havens must surely have accompanied him.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Falathrim* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which means "people of the foaming shore".<sup id="cite_ref-Cirdan_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirdan-3">[3]</a></sup> It is a combination of the element *[falas](/wiki/Falas "Falas")*<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> ("beach", "shore", "strand", "surf(line)") and probably the element *[rim](/wiki/Rim "Rim")* ("host", "great number", "people (of one kind or origin)").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In one text, the names **[Falmari](/wiki/Falmari)** (Quenya) and **Falathrim** (Sindarin) both refer to the people of [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand)<sup id="cite_ref-Cirdan_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirdan-3">[3]</a></sup> however elsewhere and in the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)*, the Quenya name refers to those in Valinor,<sup id="cite_ref-p3i3_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p3i3-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-index_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-11">[11]</a></sup> and the Sindarin name to those of the [Falas](/wiki/Falas).<sup id="cite_ref-index_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The people of Círdan retained their name as Teleri and were known in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) as **Telir** or **Telerrim**.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirdan_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirdan-3">[3]</a></sup>

Another name for the Falathrim was the **Egladhrim**, or the **Eglain**, a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "The Forsaken". While originally used as a name for the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) by themselves, it came to refer only to the Elves of the Falas, as distinguished from the "inland Elves" of Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
