The **Falathrim** were the shoreland Elves of the western shores of the [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) who took [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) as their lord from the beginning. They dwelt mainly in the ancient havens of [Eglarest](../E/Eglarest.md) and [Brithombar](../B/Brithombar.md) of the [Falas](Falas.md), and their last home on Middle-earth before finally leaving for the Undying Lands was the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md).

## History {#History}

The [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) were the most numerous Elven clan and for this reason they were led by two brothers, [Elwë](../E/Elwë.md) and [Olwë](../O/Olwë.md) in the [Great Journey](../G/Great_Journey.md) to [Aman](../A/Aman.md). They were also the most slow and reluctant and many turned back or stayed behind. When Elwë became lost in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), many of his people refused to carry on until he had been found; those were named [Eglath](../E/Eglath.md), the forsaken.

It was Olwë who led the rest to the shores of Beleriand. As they lingered at the [Bay of Balar](../B/Bay_of_Balar.md) they were befriended and taught by [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md)'s vassal, the [Maia](../M/Maia.md) [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md). When Ulmo returned with the [floating island](../T/Tol_Eressea.md) to take the bulk of the Teleri to the West, Ossë convinced some of them to forsake Valinor and the other Elves and stay behind with him.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

These became the Falathrim, and their leader was [Nowë](../N/Nowë.md),<sup id="cite_ref-LastWritingsN_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LastWritingsN-2">[2]</a></sup> kin of both Elwë and Olwë, who stayed behind despite his great eagerness to see the [light of Valinor](../L/Light_of_Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirdan_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirdan-3">[3]</a></sup> He was later known as Círdan. The Falathrim settled on Beleriand's western shores, building themselves Havens at [Brithombar](../B/Brithombar.md) and [Eglarest](../E/Eglarest.md) and became the first builders and sailors of ships in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md)'s history.

When Elwë returned, he established the Kingdom of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), claiming overlordship of all Beleriand as King Elu Thingol. The Falathrim, along with the Eglath, were considered [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), and found many pearls that they sent to him as gifts.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After the [Return of the Noldor](../R/Return_of_the_Noldor.md), the Elves of the Falas became fast in friendship with the [Deep Elves](../N/Noldor.md). Círdan attended the [Mereth Aderthad](../M/Mereth_Aderthad.md) with many of his people, where he swore oaths of friendship.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> With [Finrod](Finrod.md)'s aid, the Havens of the Falas were strengthened and rebuilt. The Elves of the Havens enjoyed peaceful relations with all the [Elves of Beleriand](../E/Elves_of_Beleriand.md), and were particularly closely allied with the people of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md). As one of the most remote peoples from [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s forces in [Angband](../A/Angband.md), the other Elvish lords would often send their children to the Elves of the Havens for safekeeping.

They fought beside the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), too: in the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_assault_on_Hithlum&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second assault on Hithlum (page does not exist)">Second assault on Hithlum</a>, their mariners rescued High King [Fingon](Fingon.md) from overwhelming hordes in [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md). After the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), Morgoth sent his hosts to besiege them, and both the Havens were overthrown. Most of the Elves of the Falas perished in the [fall of the Falas](Fall_of_the_Falas.md), or were captured, but Círdan escaped. With his surviving followers and the young [Ereinion Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), who was at that time lodging with the people of the Havens, he sailed southwards, and settled for a time on the [Isle of Balar](../I/Isle_of_Balar.md) maintaining a haven.

As [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s power overwhelmed the Eldar (perhaps soon after the Nirnaeth), some "seafaring Sindar from the west havens" fled with 3 small ships to the east. They ended up at the confluence of [Morthond](../M/Morthond.md) and [Ringló](../R/Ringló.md). There they established [Edhellond](../E/Edhellond.md) and were eventually joined by their Telerin kin, [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) from the north.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

During the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), Círdan and Gil-galad sailed east to [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) where Cirdan founded the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) on the newly formed [Gulf of Lhûn](../G/Gulf_of_Lhûn.md), and the remnant of the Elves of the Havens must surely have accompanied him.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Falathrim* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name, which means "people of the foaming shore".<sup id="cite_ref-Cirdan_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirdan-3">[3]</a></sup> It is a combination of the element *[falas](Falas.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> ("beach", "shore", "strand", "surf(line)") and probably the element *[rim](../R/Rim.md)* ("host", "great number", "people (of one kind or origin)").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In one text, the names **[Falmari](Falmari.md)** (Quenya) and **Falathrim** (Sindarin) both refer to the people of [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Cirdan_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirdan-3">[3]</a></sup> however elsewhere and in the published *[Silmarillion](../S/Silmarillion.md)*, the Quenya name refers to those in Valinor,<sup id="cite_ref-p3i3_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p3i3-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-index_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-11">[11]</a></sup> and the Sindarin name to those of the [Falas](Falas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-index_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The people of Círdan retained their name as Teleri and were known in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) as **Telir** or **Telerrim**.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirdan_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirdan-3">[3]</a></sup>

Another name for the Falathrim was the **Egladhrim**, or the **Eglain**, a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name meaning "The Forsaken". While originally used as a name for the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) by themselves, it came to refer only to the Elves of the Falas, as distinguished from the "inland Elves" of Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
