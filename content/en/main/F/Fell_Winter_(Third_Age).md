The **Fell Winter** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2911](/wiki/Third_Age_2911 "Third Age 2911") – [2912](/wiki/Third_Age_2912 "Third Age 2912")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>) was an extremely cold and long-lasting winter in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Like the earlier [Long Winter](/wiki/Long_Winter) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758") – [2759](/wiki/Third_Age_2759 "Third Age 2759")), the Fell Winter began in [November](/wiki/November). This time [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) was not able to launch a full attack, as the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs) that had taken place more than a century earlier had eliminated most of the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) in the north, and the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) had been badly defeated in [2885](/wiki/Third_Age_2885 "Third Age 2885").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

The winter was also less harsh than the Long Winter, and snow and ice stopped in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) at the [Greyflood](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3 "Gwathló") River, and in [Wilderland](/wiki/Rhovanion "Rhovanion") in the middle of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood). Nevertheless, there were severe problems, as food again ran short, and the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") were attacked by [White Wolves](/wiki/White_Wolves), which crossed the frozen [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) river.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was on this occasion that the [Horn-call of Buckland](/wiki/Horn-call_of_Buckland) had been sounded; it was not heard again until [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") invaded [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) in [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and the [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North) had to provide food for the Hobbits to prevent them from dying of hunger.

In March when the winter ended great floods of meltwater rushed down [Greyflood](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3 "Gwathló"), and the city of [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad) was ruined and its bridge broken. The people of [Enedwaith](/wiki/Enedwaith) also suffered greatly as their lands were flooded.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
