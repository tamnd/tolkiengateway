The **Fell Winter** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2911](../T/Third_Age_2911.md) – [2912](../T/Third_Age_2912.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>) was an extremely cold and long-lasting winter in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Like the earlier [Long Winter](../L/Long_Winter.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2758](../T/Third_Age_2758.md) – [2759](../T/Third_Age_2759.md)), the Fell Winter began in [November](../N/November.md). This time [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) was not able to launch a full attack, as the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs.md) that had taken place more than a century earlier had eliminated most of the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) in the north, and the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md) had been badly defeated in [2885](../T/Third_Age_2885.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

The winter was also less harsh than the Long Winter, and snow and ice stopped in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) at the [Greyflood](../G/Gwathló.md) River, and in [Wilderland](../R/Rhovanion.md) in the middle of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md). Nevertheless, there were severe problems, as food again ran short, and the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) were attacked by [White Wolves](../W/White_Wolves.md), which crossed the frozen [Brandywine](../B/Brandywine.md) river.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was on this occasion that the [Horn-call of Buckland](../H/Horn-call_of_Buckland.md) had been sounded; it was not heard again until [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) invaded [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md) in [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and the [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) had to provide food for the Hobbits to prevent them from dying of hunger.

In March when the winter ended great floods of meltwater rushed down [Greyflood](../G/Gwathló.md), and the city of [Tharbad](../T/Tharbad.md) was ruined and its bridge broken. The people of [Enedwaith](../E/Enedwaith.md) also suffered greatly as their lands were flooded.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
