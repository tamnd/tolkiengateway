The **Feast of Felling** is possibly a type of event or activity in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) or [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) as a whole.

The term is mentioned by [Borlas](../B/Borlas.md) in his conversation with [Saelon](../S/Saelon.md) as an example of a place not to leave axes hanging from the walls in order to be able to constantly hew the [Dark Tree](../D/Dark_Tree.md), a description of the growing threat of evil in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
