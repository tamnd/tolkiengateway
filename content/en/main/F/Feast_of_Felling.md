The **Feast of Felling** is possibly a type of event or activity in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) or [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) as a whole.

The term is mentioned by [Borlas](/wiki/Borlas) in his conversation with [Saelon](/wiki/Saelon) as an example of a place not to leave axes hanging from the walls in order to be able to constantly hew the [Dark Tree](/wiki/Dark_Tree), a description of the growing threat of evil in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
