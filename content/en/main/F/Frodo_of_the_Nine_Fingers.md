**Of Frodo of the Nine Fingers and the Ring of Doom** was an epic unnamed lay that was sung by an unnamed [minstrel](../M/Minstrel_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Song_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Song-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On the [Field of Cormallen](Field_of_Cormallen.md), a minstrel of Gondor begged the king for his permission to sing. Before waiting for [Aragorn's](../A/Aragorn.md) leave, the minstrel announced that he was going to relate, in his lay, the [tale](../Q/Quest_of_the_Ring.md) "of [Frodo of the Nine Fingers](Frodo_Baggins.md) and the [Ring of Doom](../R/Ring_of_Doom.md)",<sup id="cite_ref-Song_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Song-1">[1]</a></sup> possibly meaning that this was the name of the lay, but this is unclear.

This pleased [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) so greatly that he wept immediately after hearing this, and the rest of the listeners joined him. The minstrel's clear voice was said to alternate between one of the [elven-tongues](../E/Elvish.md) and [Westron](../W/Westron.md). The lay made the hearts of the listeners overflow with joy at the sweet words and they began weeping.<sup id="cite_ref-Song_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Song-1">[1]</a></sup>

The minstrel's lay lasted almost that entire day, and he concluded it just as the evening began.<sup id="cite_ref-Song_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Song-1">[1]</a></sup>

The lyrics created for the film are given as follows:</dd></dl>
