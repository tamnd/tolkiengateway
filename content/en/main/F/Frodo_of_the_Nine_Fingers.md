**Of Frodo of the Nine Fingers and the Ring of Doom** was an epic unnamed lay that was sung by an unnamed [minstrel](/wiki/Minstrel_of_Gondor "Minstrel of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Song_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Song-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On the [Field of Cormallen](/wiki/Field_of_Cormallen), a minstrel of Gondor begged the king for his permission to sing. Before waiting for [Aragorn's](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") leave, the minstrel announced that he was going to relate, in his lay, the [tale](/wiki/Quest_of_the_Ring "Quest of the Ring") "of [Frodo of the Nine Fingers](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and the [Ring of Doom](/wiki/Ring_of_Doom)",<sup id="cite_ref-Song_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Song-1">[1]</a></sup> possibly meaning that this was the name of the lay, but this is unclear.

This pleased [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) so greatly that he wept immediately after hearing this, and the rest of the listeners joined him. The minstrel's clear voice was said to alternate between one of the [elven-tongues](/wiki/Elvish "Elvish") and [Westron](/wiki/Westron). The lay made the hearts of the listeners overflow with joy at the sweet words and they began weeping.<sup id="cite_ref-Song_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Song-1">[1]</a></sup>

The minstrel's lay lasted almost that entire day, and he concluded it just as the evening began.<sup id="cite_ref-Song_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Song-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1980: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)">The Return of the King (1980 film)</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The unnamed lay in the original text was turned into a song referred to in the soundtrack as <b>Frodo of the Nine Fingers</b>.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> It was put to music by <a href="/wiki/Maury_Laws" title="Maury Laws">Maury Laws</a> and given lyrics by <a href="/wiki/Jules_Bass" title="Jules Bass">Jules Bass</a>. While the <a href="/wiki/Minstrel_of_Gondor" title="Minstrel of Gondor">Minstrel of Gondor</a> (voiced by <a href="/wiki/Glenn_Yarbrough" title="Glenn Yarbrough">Glenn Yarbrough</a>) still sang it, the lay appeared as the film&#39;s framing device and became a song used as entertainment at <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a>&#39; 129th birthday party in <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>. The lyrics created for the film are given as follows:</dd></dl>

And the ring of doom...
</p>
</div>
