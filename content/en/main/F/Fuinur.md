**Fuinur** was a [Black Númenórean](/wiki/Black_N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Black Númenóreans") who lived in the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> Fuinur was among those [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) who sailed east from [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) to establish fortresses and dwellings along the coasts during Sauron's stay in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) (from [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3262](/wiki/Second_Age_3262 "Second Age 3262") to [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3319](/wiki/Second_Age_3319 "Second Age 3319")) and were already bent to Sauron's will. Fuinur rose to power among the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim). It is possible that Fuinur was among those Númenórean servants from the south that Sauron gathered to him when he prepared to attack [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3429](/wiki/Second_Age_3429 "Second Age 3429") after he had taken shape again after the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>

*Fuinur* seems to be a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-eldamo_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eldamo-3">[2]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) suggests that the initial element of this name is S. *[fuin](/wiki/Fuin "Fuin")* ("night", "gloom", "darkness"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> but the meaning of the suffix is unclear.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

In the defunct [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing) game from the 1980s, Fuinur is given an extended history. [Herumor](/wiki/Herumor) is portrayed as his younger brother.
