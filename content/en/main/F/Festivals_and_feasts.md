Various **festivals and feasts** were celebrated in [Arda](/wiki/Arda).

The [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) observed the [Three Prayers](/wiki/Three_Prayers) of *[Erukyermë](/wiki/Erukyerm%C3%AB)*, *[Erulaitalë](/wiki/Erulaital%C3%AB)*, and *[Eruhantalë](/wiki/Eruhantal%C3%AB)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Additionally, in the autumn the [Great Bear-dance](/wiki/Great_Bear-dance) was held.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) were known to observe [Yule](/wiki/Yule)<sup id="cite_ref-D2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-3">[3]</a></sup>, and held the [Free Fair](/wiki/Free_Fair) during the [Lithedays](/wiki/Lithedays).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After the destruction of [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), *[Cormarë](/wiki/Cormar%C3%AB)* was instituted as a festival in the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom) to commemorate [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-D2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1996: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="/wiki/Southern_Gondor:_The_People" title="Southern Gondor: The People">Southern Gondor: The People</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>In addition to the days of the Three Prayers and <a href="/wiki/Yule" title="Yule">Midwinter</a>, <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor" title="Númenor">Númenor</a> also had a late harvest festival known as <i>Serkerë</i> (<a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Q.</a> “Day of Blood”) on 12 <a href="/wiki/Hithui" title="Hithui">Hithui</a> (1 November). Animals were slaughtered to preserve for the winter, and the dead were remembered. In <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>, offers at the Hallow of <a href="/wiki/Mindolluin" title="Mindolluin">Mindolluin</a> were added, leading to the feast being renamed to <i>Airilaitalë</i> (<a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Q.</a> “Hallowmas”). In addition to departed ancestors, the fall of Númenor was now also commemorated.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The game has five seasonal events: a Spring Festival, a Midsummer Festival, a Farmers Faire, a Harvest Festival, and a Yule Festival. <a href="/wiki/Durin%27s_Day" title="Durin&#39;s Day">Durin&#39;s Day</a> and the <a href="/wiki/22_September" title="22 September">birthday</a> of <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo</a> and Frodo are also marked in the game.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>
