<i><b>Firland in Flames</b></i> is the third book in the fantasy series [The Firland Saga](/wiki/The_Firland_Saga), inspired by the fictional works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). "Firland in Flames" is a translation of the Danish title, *Firland i flammer* (the book appears to have only been published in Denmark).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
