**First Age 498** (abbreviated to **F.A. 498**) is the 498th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- **[Mid-year's Day](../M/Mid-year's_Day.md)** - After becoming betrothed in the spring, [Túrin Turambar](../T/Túrin.md) weds <a class="mw-disambig" href="../N/Niënor_Níniel.md" title="Niënor Níniel">Niënor Níniel</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md)'s [orcs](../O/Orcs.md) begin raiding [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md). Túrin initially refuses to go into battle, but is persuaded by [Dorlas](../D/Dorlas.md). Túrin takes up [Gurthang](../G/Gurthang.md) and defeats the orcs; news of the Black Sword reaches Glaurung.<sup id="cite_ref-GA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-2">[2]</a></sup>
