**First Age 545** (abbreviated to **F.A. 545**) is the 545th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) - The [Host of the Valar](../H/Host_of_the_Valar.md), led by [Eönwë](../E/Eönwë.md), reach [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) aboard ships made by the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md). The Great Battle against [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) are defeated and driven from the [shores](Falas.md) of [West Beleriand](../W/West_Beleriand.md) by the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md), led by [Ingwion](../I/Ingwion.md), son of King [Ingwë](../I/Ingwë.md), at the [Battle of Eglarest](../B/Battle_of_Eglarest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
