The **Far Downs** were the downlands that marked the former western borders of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), forty leagues (approximately 140 miles) west from the [Brandywine Bridge](../B/Brandywine_Bridge.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Of the geography of the Far Downs, almost nothing is known, except that they were at the border of the [Westfarthing](../W/Westfarthing.md) and the town of [Greenholm](../G/Greenholm.md) seems to have been built among the Downs.

[Elanor Gardner](../E/Elanor_Gardner.md) married [Fastred of Greenholm](Fastred_of_Greenholm.md) there in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1451](Fourth_Age_30.md). The next year ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1452](Fourth_Age_31.md)) King [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) extended the Shire's borders westward to the [Tower Hills](../T/Tower_Hills.md) with the [Westmarch](../W/Westmarch.md) lying in between.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Fastred of Greenholm](Fastred_of_Greenholm.md) became the first [Warden of Westmarch](../W/Warden_of_Westmarch.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Within the [Ballantine Books](../B/Ballantine_Books.md)' paperback edition of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* there was a misprint in the [Prologue](../P/Prologue_Concerning_Hobbits,_and_other_matters.md) that incorrectly named the Far Downs as the **Fox Downs**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Some families claimed descent from Hobbits who had “taken a fairy wife”, meaning that an ancestor had supposedly been whisked away by fairies and returned with a “strain of madness in the blood”.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>
