The **Far Downs** were the downlands that marked the former western borders of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), forty leagues (approximately 140 miles) west from the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Of the geography of the Far Downs, almost nothing is known, except that they were at the border of the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing) and the town of [Greenholm](/wiki/Greenholm) seems to have been built among the Downs.

[Elanor Gardner](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner) married [Fastred of Greenholm](/wiki/Fastred_of_Greenholm) there in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1451](/wiki/Fourth_Age_30 "Fourth Age 30"). The next year ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1452](/wiki/Fourth_Age_31 "Fourth Age 31")) King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") extended the Shire's borders westward to the [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills) with the [Westmarch](/wiki/Westmarch) lying in between.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Fastred of Greenholm](/wiki/Fastred_of_Greenholm) became the first [Warden of Westmarch](/wiki/Warden_of_Westmarch).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Within the [Ballantine Books](/wiki/Ballantine_Books)' paperback edition of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* there was a misprint in the [Prologue](/wiki/Prologue_Concerning_Hobbits,_and_other_matters "Prologue Concerning Hobbits, and other matters") that incorrectly named the Far Downs as the **Fox Downs**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2025: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Hobbit_Tales" title="Hobbit Tales">Hobbit Tales</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Hobbits from Greenholm believed there were fairies hiding in the heavy nightly fog. Some families claimed descent from Hobbits who had “taken a fairy wife”, meaning that an ancestor had supposedly been whisked away by fairies and returned with a “strain of madness in the blood”.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>
