**First Age 405** (abbreviated to **F.A. 405**) is the 405th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Hador](../H/Hador.md) enters the service of [Fingolfin](Fingolfin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
