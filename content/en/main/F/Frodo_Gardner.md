**Frodo Gardner** was the second child and eldest son of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton).<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It was always Sam's intention to name one of his children after his best friend, [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins). Though his first child was [a girl](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner "Elanor Gardner"), he named the second one Frodo.<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-1">[1]</a></sup>

Frodo became the head of [Gardner Family](/wiki/Gardner_Family) and inherited [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). His son was [Holfast Gardner](/wiki/Holfast_Gardner).<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *[King's Letter](/wiki/King%27s_Letter)*, [King Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") mentions his name in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) as *Iorhael*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
