**Fréawine** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) 2594 – 2680, aged 86 years) was fifth [King of Rohan](/wiki/King_of_Rohan).

## History {#History}

Fréawine was the son of [Fréa](/wiki/Fr%C3%A9a) and grandson of [Aldor](/wiki/Aldor) the Old. On account of his grandfather's immensely long reign, Fréawine, like his father before him, became [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan)'s King in his old age, when he was already more than sixty years old. Little is said of his twenty-one years as King, except that it was a time of peace and plenty in the Mark. Fréawine was succeeded by his son [Goldwine](/wiki/Goldwine).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It's possible that Fréawine had other descendants who eventually were mingled with the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) who returned after the time of Goldwine. Lord [Freca](/wiki/Freca) claimed to be one of his descendants.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fréawine* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name. It means "dear or beloved lord" and consists of *fréa* ("lord", "master") and *wine* ("friend" in the sense 'one who can help or protect').<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
