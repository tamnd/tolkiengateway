**Finduilas** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2950](/wiki/Third_Age_2950 "Third Age 2950") - [2988](/wiki/Third_Age_2988 "Third Age 2988"))<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-1">[1]</a></sup> was the daughter of [Adrahil](/wiki/Adrahil) the [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth). She was the sister of [Ivriniel](/wiki/Ivriniel) and Prince [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil) of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-1">[1]</a></sup> She became the wife of [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor") and the mother of [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) and [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir).

Named after [Finduilas](/wiki/Finduilas), an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves")-maiden of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), she was of great beauty and gentle heart.<sup id="cite_ref-A_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-A-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2976](/wiki/Third_Age_2976 "Third Age 2976") she was wed to the much older [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor), son of [Ecthelion II](/wiki/Ecthelion_II) the [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-B_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2978](/wiki/Third_Age_2978 "Third Age 2978") she gave birth to [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir),<sup id="cite_ref-B_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B-3">[3]</a></sup> most loved by his father. In [2983](/wiki/Third_Age_2983 "Third Age 2983") she gave birth to her second son, [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir),<sup id="cite_ref-B_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B-3">[3]</a></sup> and afterwards grew weak. Faramir was ignored by his father, and she did her best to raise him on her own.

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2984](/wiki/Third_Age_2984 "Third Age 2984") Ecthelion died, and Denethor became Steward of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-B_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B-3">[3]</a></sup> Nevertheless the health of the Lady Finduilas dwindled in the city. The [shadow](/wiki/Shadow "Shadow") of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) filled her with horror while she never stopped missing the sea,<sup id="cite_ref-A_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-A-2">[2]</a></sup> and four years later she died at the very young age of thirty-eight (considering her heritage as a [Dúnadan](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain "Dúnedain")).

## Legacy {#Legacy}

Denethor never remarried, and became more grim and silent than before. It was not long after her death that he would sit long alone in his tower deep in thought, foreseeing that the assault from Mordor would come in his time.<sup id="cite_ref-A_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-A-2">[2]</a></sup> Thus he began using the *[palantír](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), and the despair fed to his mind led to his ultimate insanity and suicide some thirty-one years later.
