**Francesco Mattioli** is an Italian illustrator. He illustrated many of the maps for *[The One Ring](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)")*’s second edition and *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He also illustrated for *[The Battle of Five Armies](</wiki/The_Battle_of_Five_Armies_(2014_board_game)> "The Battle of Five Armies (2014 board game)")*, *[Hunt for the Ring](</wiki/Hunt_for_the_Ring_(2017_board_game)> "Hunt for the Ring (2017 board game)")*, and *[War of the Ring: The Card Game](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring:_The_Card_Game)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
