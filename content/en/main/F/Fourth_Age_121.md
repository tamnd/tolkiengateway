**Fourth Age 121** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 121** or **F.O.**) is the 121st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 121 was also known as **S.R. 1542** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## March {#March}

- **[1](../_/1_March.md)**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> - [Arwen Undómiel](../A/Arwen.md) lays down her life at [Cerin Amroth](../C/Cerin_Amroth.md) in abandoned [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
