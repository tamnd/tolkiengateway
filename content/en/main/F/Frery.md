**Frery** was the first month of the [Bree](../B/Bree.md) and [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md) calendars, corresponding to the [Afteryule](../A/Afteryule.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md). It was the thirty days immediately following the holiday of [Yule](../Y/Yule.md), approximately corresponding to modern [23 December](../_/23_December.md) through [21 January](../_/21_January.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Frery represents [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *frēorig* meaning "freezing".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
