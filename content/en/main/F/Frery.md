**Frery** was the first month of the [Bree](/wiki/Bree) and [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) calendars, corresponding to the [Afteryule](/wiki/Afteryule) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar). It was the thirty days immediately following the holiday of [Yule](/wiki/Yule), approximately corresponding to modern [23 December](/wiki/23_December) through [21 January](/wiki/21_January).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Frery represents [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *frēorig* meaning "freezing".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
