**First Age 467** (abbreviated to **F.A. 467**) is the 467th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) lays down her life and goes to the [Halls of Mandos](../H/Halls_of_Mandos.md) to plead for [Beren](../B/Beren.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Hunthor](../H/Hunthor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
