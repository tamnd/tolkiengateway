The **Falls of Gelion** were located below the last ford over Gelion, i.e. [Sarn Athrad](../S/Sarn_Athrad.md), and above the junction with the river [Ascar](../A/Ascar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>. South of the Falls, river Gelion became more swift and large due to its five tributaries and was name Gabilān by the Dwarves<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>.

This location does not appear on the [The Second 'Silmarillion' Map](../T/The_Second_'Silmarillion'_Map.md), nor in any map of the Beleriand.
