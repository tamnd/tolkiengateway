**Fiona Apple McAfee-Maggart** (born [September 13](../S/September_13.md), [1977](../_/1977.md)) is an American singer-songwriter. She sung the [Ring Verse](../R/Ring_Verse.md) for [Bear McCreary](../B/Bear_McCreary.md)'s "[Where the Shadows Lie](../W/Where_the_Shadows_Lie.md)" composition, which played during the end credits of *[Alloyed](../A/Alloyed.md)*, the eighth episode of the first season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
