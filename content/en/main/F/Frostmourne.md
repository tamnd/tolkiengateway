**Frostmourne** is a Mexican black metal band from Morelia/Zacapu in Michoacán de Ocampo.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The band released the EP *Nazgul*, featuring tracks such as "La batalla en el abismo de Helm" (referring to [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg)) and "La última marcha de los ents" (referring to the [Ents](/wiki/Ent "Ent")).

## Members {#Members}

- Grond – drums
- Lord – guitars
- Soldado del Odio – vocals

## Discography {#Discography}

- *Nazgul* (2009, EP)
    1. Melancolía
    2. La batalla en el abismo de Helm
    3. La ciénaga de los muertos
    4. La última marcha de los ents
    5. Nazgul
    6. Pulpo-gore (instrumental)
