**Fen Hollen** or the **Steward's Door** was on the sixth level of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), in its rearward wall to the west. It led to [Rath Dínen](/wiki/Rath_D%C3%ADnen) (the Silent Street) and the [Hallows](/wiki/Hallows) where the tombs of the [Stewards](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor") and [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor) lay.

Guarded by a porter, the door was also called the **Closed Door** as it opened only for funerals, or to those who tended the tombs and had the token.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Around the time of the [Siege of Gondor](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor), [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond) killed the porter in order to save wounded [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) and took his key. After he locked the Door again, [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) gave the key to the injured [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fen Hollen* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Closed Door"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> or "Shut Door", and consists of *fen* ("door") + *hollen* ("closed", "shut").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Fen Hollen is closed in the pre-battle version of Minas Tirith. After the battle, the door is opened to the Silent Street for the mourning.</dd></dl>
