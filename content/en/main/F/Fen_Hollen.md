**Fen Hollen** or the **Steward's Door** was on the sixth level of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), in its rearward wall to the west. It led to [Rath Dínen](../R/Rath_Dínen.md) (the Silent Street) and the [Hallows](../H/Hallows.md) where the tombs of the [Stewards](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md) and [Kings of Gondor](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) lay.

Guarded by a porter, the door was also called the **Closed Door** as it opened only for funerals, or to those who tended the tombs and had the token.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Around the time of the [Siege of Gondor](../S/Siege_of_Gondor.md), [Beregond](../B/Beregond.md) killed the porter in order to save wounded [Faramir](Faramir.md) and took his key. After he locked the Door again, [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) gave the key to the injured [Faramir](Faramir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fen Hollen* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Closed Door"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> or "Shut Door", and consists of *fen* ("door") + *hollen* ("closed", "shut").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Fen Hollen is closed in the pre-battle version of Minas Tirith. After the battle, the door is opened to the Silent Street for the mourning.</dd></dl>
