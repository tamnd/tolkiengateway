**Fire-drakes** ([Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[Urulóki](../U/Urulókë.md)*) were fire breathing [Dragons](../D/Dragons.md).

## History and characteristics {#History_and_characteristics}

The fire-drakes were the more powerful of the two fundamental divisions of dragon-kind (the other being the [cold-drakes](../C/Cold-drakes.md)), who, as the name suggests, were able to breathe fire. [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md), the [Father of Dragons](Father_of_Dragons.md), was the first of the species.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He first appeared during the late [First Age](First_Age.md), but the Urulóki were known to have survived even after the [downfall](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) of the Dark Lord [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), troubling the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md). Another dragon considered to be of the fire-drakes during the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md) was [Ancalagon the Black](../A/Ancalagon.md).

[Smaug](../S/Smaug.md), the last of the truly powerful dragons, was slain by [Bard](../B/Bard.md) in the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> We can be sure that he was not the last of the fire-drakes, though, because [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) refers to fire-breathing dragons in the time before the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), nearly eighty years after Smaug's death.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In "[The Fall of Gondolin](<../T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md>)", the first tale of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), multiple Fire-drakes are mentioned. Most of the time it is not clear if they are different from the [Iron Dragons](../I/Iron_Dragons.md), although some of them are clearly living creatures, like the "creature of fire with [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md) upon it" or the Fire-drake that dried the [Fountain of the King](Fountain_of_the_King.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
