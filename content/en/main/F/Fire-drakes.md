**Fire-drakes** ([Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[Urulóki](/wiki/Urul%C3%B3k%C3%AB "Urulókë")*) were fire breathing [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons).

## History and characteristics {#History_and_characteristics}

The fire-drakes were the more powerful of the two fundamental divisions of dragon-kind (the other being the [cold-drakes](/wiki/Cold-drakes "Cold-drakes")), who, as the name suggests, were able to breathe fire. [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung), the [Father of Dragons](/wiki/Father_of_Dragons), was the first of the species.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He first appeared during the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), but the Urulóki were known to have survived even after the [downfall](/wiki/War_of_Wrath "War of Wrath") of the Dark Lord [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), troubling the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). Another dragon considered to be of the fire-drakes during the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days) was [Ancalagon the Black](/wiki/Ancalagon "Ancalagon").

[Smaug](/wiki/Smaug), the last of the truly powerful dragons, was slain by [Bard](/wiki/Bard) in the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> We can be sure that he was not the last of the fire-drakes, though, because [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) refers to fire-breathing dragons in the time before the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), nearly eighty years after Smaug's death.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In "[The Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")", the first tale of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), multiple Fire-drakes are mentioned. Most of the time it is not clear if they are different from the [Iron Dragons](/wiki/Iron_Dragons), although some of them are clearly living creatures, like the "creature of fire with [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) upon it" or the Fire-drake that dried the [Fountain of the King](/wiki/Fountain_of_the_King).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
