**Fíli** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2859](../T/Third_Age_2859.md) – [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Line_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-1">[1]</a></sup> 82 years old) was one of the thirteen [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) who took part in the [quest](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md) for [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md), which was [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)'s great adventure. He was one of the three Dwarves of [Thorin's company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) who did not survive the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md) (the other two being his uncle [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) and his brother [Kíli](../K/Kíli.md).)

## History {#History}

Fíli and Kíli were the sons of [Dís](../D/Dís.md), the sister of [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md). Before the quest to Erebor, they probably lived in the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) where their uncle resided after the disappearance of [Thráin II](../T/Thráin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Line_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-1">[1]</a></sup> As Thorin's sister-sons, they accompanied their uncle when he set out to challenge the [dragon](../D/Dragons.md) [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md). Before the Dwarves met Bilbo, [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) met with them and persuaded them to bring the hobbit on the quest. At this meeting Fíli thought that Bilbo sounded like a strange creature and laughed at his name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### The Quest of Erebor {#The_Quest_of_Erebor}

When Bilbo first met Fíli and Kíli (in the third group of Dwarves to arrive at [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md)) they were wearing blue hoods. After supper, Fíli and Kíli played small fiddles that they had brought in bags.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The brothers were the youngest of the company by some 50 years,<sup id="cite_ref-Hill_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hill-4">[4]</a></sup> with Kíli being five years younger than his brother.<sup id="cite_ref-Line_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-1">[1]</a></sup> Although not twins, the two brothers were inseparable throughout the adventure.

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Adlpictures_-_Fili.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a1/Adlpictures_-_Fili.jpg/200px-Adlpictures_-_Fili.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="283" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a1/Adlpictures_-_Fili.jpg/300px-Adlpictures_-_Fili.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a1/Adlpictures_-_Fili.jpg/400px-Adlpictures_-_Fili.jpg 2x" data-file-width="900" data-file-height="1273"/></a><figcaption>Adlpictures - <i>Fíli</i></figcaption></figure>

Early on the night when the company encountered the [Trolls](../T/Trolls.md) one of the ponies became frightened and bolted into the river; Fíli and Kíli nearly drowned getting the beast out of the water. After the trolls had turned to stone and the company explored their hideout, it was Fíli who encouraged the others to get out of the "horrible smell" in the troll's lair.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Up in the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) during the thunder-battle the two brothers, being the youngest Dwarves with the keenest eyes, were sent ahead to look for shelter. They found a small dry cave which was perfect for the company (until the [Goblins](../O/Orcs.md#Orcs_and_Goblins) arrived). After [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) had rescued the company and was counting the members he found Fíli and Kíli, as usual, paired together.<sup id="cite_ref-Hill_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hill-4">[4]</a></sup>

When the company rushed up into trees to escape the [wargs](../W/Wargs.md), Fíli and Kíli scrambled to the top of a tall larch.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The next day, when the company arrived at [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md)'s [Hall](../B/Beorn's_Hall.md), Fíli and Kíli were again together as the fourth pair of Dwarves to appear during Gandalf's story.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

When Bilbo spied the boat on the far side of the [Enchanted River](../E/Enchanted_River.md), Thorin chose Fíli as the youngest<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 1]</a></sup> with the best sight (instead of [Dori](../D/Dori.md), the strongest) to throw a rope with a hook attached to snag the small vessel. Fíli did manage to snag the boat on the second try but could not pull it across. Kíli came to his aid first, followed by [Óin](../_/Óin.md) and [Glóin](../G/Glóin.md), before the boat came loose (after its painter snapped). When the company made the crossing they used another rope that Fíli threw ahead into the trees since the boat had no oars.<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-9">[8]</a></sup>

After the Dwarves had been captured by [spiders](../S/Spiders.md) Bilbo climbed out on the branch where the spiders had trussed up the Dwarves. The first bundle that Bilbo reached was Fíli, whom the hobbit recognized by the tips of his blue hood and long nose poking out of the threads.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[note 2]</a></sup> Once freed, Fíli helped Bilbo free the other Dwarves despite being sickened by spider-poison.<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-9">[8]</a></sup>

After the escape from the halls of the [Elvenking](../T/Thranduil.md), Fíli and Kíli came out smiling since they were young and had but few bruises. The two brothers helped Thorin and Bilbo locate and free the other Dwarves. Next, they accompanied Thorin and Bilbo to the bridge into [Esgaroth](../L/Lake-town.md) while the others recovered and Fíli was the most impatient to see the [Master of Lake-town](../M/Master_of_Lake-town.md) at his feast.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[9]</a></sup>

When the company reached the Lonely Mountain, Thorin sent out a scouting party to view the Front Gate (or where it once stood). Fíli and Kíli went along with [Balin](../B/Balin.md) and Bilbo on this expedition. On another scouting trip Bilbo was with Fíli and Kíli when the three discovered the rough steps that led up the mountain to the little bay that contained the secret door.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup>

Of the thirteen Dwarves, Fíli and Kíli looked uncomfortable when Bilbo asked for volunteers to accompany him on the first trip into the mountain (only Balin went with the hobbit for part of the way). Once the dragon woke up though Fíli and Kíli, along with Bilbo and Balin, were the first to go in the tunnel (sent in by Thorin).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[11]</a></sup>

While searching the treasure when the full company descended into Erebor, Fíli and Kíli were in such exuberant spirits that they took down magic golden harps and played music on them.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[12]</a></sup>

Fíli was slain with his brother in the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md), while defending their mortally wounded uncle.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[13]</a></sup> After Thorin's death, the brothers would have been the only remaining descendants of [Thrór](../T/Thrór.md)'s line and Fíli would have inherited the title of [King under the Mountain](../K/King_under_the_Mountain.md). Their deaths ended the dynasty, and the title passed to [Dáin II](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md). It was their heroic last stand that made their mother the only [Dwarf-woman](../D/Dwarf-women.md) to be mentioned in the genealogies.<sup id="cite_ref-Line_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fíli* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](../V/Völuspá.md)*. It can mean "File" or "Filer"; it is of Frisian or Low German origin rather than Old Norse.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[14]</a></sup>

### Films {#Films}

<b>1967: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(1967_film).md" title="The Hobbit (1967 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1967 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Fíli is omitted. <a href="../T/Thorin.md" title="Thorin">Thorin Oakenshield</a> only travels with an unnamed guard and the princess of Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-Deitch_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deitch-17">[15]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1977: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md" title="The Hobbit (1977 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1977 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Fíli&#39;s voice is provided by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Jack_DeLeon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Jack DeLeon (page does not exist)">Jack DeLeon</a>. However, he only ever speaks in unison with the rest of the Dwarves.</dd></dl>

<b>2012-14: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="../D/Dean_O&#39;Gorman.md" title="Dean O&#39;Gorman">Dean O&#39;Gorman</a> portrays Fíli.<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting1_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting1-18">[16]</a></sup> O&#39;Gorman joined the cast on <a href="../_/30_April.md" title="30 April">30 April</a> <a href="../_/2011.md" title="2011">2011</a> after Rob Kazinsky, who was originally cast as Fíli, left the production for personal reasons.<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting1_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting1-18">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting2_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting2-19">[17]</a></sup> A description of Fíli in <i>The Hobbit</i> films was released by the studio:</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt"></blockquote></dd></dl>

Fíli was born into the royal line of Durin and raised under the stern guardianship of his uncle, Thorin Oakenshield. Along with his brother Kíli, Fíli is one of the youngest in The Company of the Dwarves. He has never traveled far, nor ever seen the fabled Dwarf City of Erebor. A skilled fighter, Fíli off on the adventure ahead with little idea of the challenges and dangers that lie before him.

<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../W/Warner_Bros..md" title="Warner Bros.">Warner Bros.</a><sup id="cite_ref-HobbitApp_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HobbitApp-20">[18]</a></sup></cite></p>

<dl><dd>He carries many weapons, such as throwing axes, war hammers, knives and swords.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[19]</a></sup> When Thorin refuses to allow Kíli to go with the others to the Lonely Mountain due to his weakened state (from being struck by a poisoned arrow), Fíli opts to stay behind in <a href="../L/Lake-town.md" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a> (along with de facto group healer <a href="../_/Óin.md" title="Óin">Óin</a>) and look after his younger brother. In the <a href="../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md" title="Battle of Five Armies">Battle of Five Armies</a>, he goes to assist <a href="../T/Thorin.md" title="Thorin">Thorin</a> (along with <a href="../K/Kíli.md" title="Kíli">Kíli</a> and <a href="../D/Dwalin.md" title="Dwalin">Dwalin</a>) at the top of <a href="../R/Ravenhill.md" title="Ravenhill">Ravenhill</a> in an attempt to kill the <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">Orc</a> army&#39;s leader, <a href="../A/Azog.md" title="Azog">Azog</a>. However, when searching for the Pale Orc, Fíli is cornered by him and some of his soldiers. Azog then takes Fíli out in front of the other Dwarves, impales him with the sword attached to his left arm, and drops his body onto the rock below him as the others watch in horror.</dd></dl>
