<p>[…] this meta-text is sometimes referred to as “<b>fanon</b>.” Within an individual fandom, certain plotlines may be reinvented so many times and by so many people—or alternately may be written so persuasively by a few writers—that they take on the status of fan-produced canon.<sup id="cite_ref-parrish_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-parrish-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>

**Fanon** is a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/portmanteau" title="wikipedia:portmanteau">portmanteau</a> of "fan [canon](../C/Canon.md)", referring to interpretations, explanations, or even subversions of fictional works that widely permeate a [fandom](Fandom.md). These can originate from [fanfiction](Fanfiction.md), parodies, adaptations (such as the [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)), or even misconceptions. Their permeation means their non-canonical nature is not always clear to fans.<sup id="cite_ref-parrish_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-parrish-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Eason_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eason-2">[2]</a></sup>

Examples of non-canonical concepts considered common in fanfiction by the TV Tropes community are, among others, the usage of terms like “dwarfling” and “elfling”, the notion of [Elves](../E/Elves.md) being strictly vegetarian, and usage of modern military ranks.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

A major fanon undertaking in the *Lord of the Rings* fandom is the <a class="external text" href="https://notionclubarchives.fandom.com" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">New Notion Club Archives</a>, which is a project that seeks to weld together a coherent, denser and complexer [Arda](../A/Arda.md), using concepts from various adaptations reinterpreted and adjusted for consistency.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It, in turn, was used as a source for other fan projects such as *[The Lord of the Rings: Realms in Exile](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Realms_in_Exile.md)*.
