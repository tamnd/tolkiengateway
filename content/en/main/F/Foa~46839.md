## Quenya {#Quenya}

<i><b>foa</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word, which in a ca. [1968](/wiki/1968) text is said to mean "breath, puff of breath", deriving from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onomatopoeia" title="wikipedia:Onomatopoeia">onomatopoetic</a> [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) word *phā*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In a ca. [1959](/wiki/1959) text, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") noted the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") PHAW-, said to signify "emit (foul breath etc.)" and to have yielded the derivatives "*phawalōkō* &gt; *[foalókë](/wiki/Foal%C3%B3k%C3%AB "Foalókë")*".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Qenya {#Qenya}

<i><b>foa</b></i> is a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) word meaning "hoard, treasure". It is found in the compound *[foalókë](/wiki/Foal%C3%B3k%C3%AB#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Foalókë")* (*foa* + *[lókë](/wiki/L%C3%B3k%C3%AB "Lókë")*).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
