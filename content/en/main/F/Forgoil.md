The **Forgoil**, or the **Strawheads** as it reportedly translated in [Westron](/wiki/Westron), were the enemies of the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings). In the rest of Middle-earth, they were known as [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Forgoil* is the only known word in the language of the Dunlendings. It is possible that the final part of the word, *-il*, is a plural marker, such as in the case of [Westron](/wiki/Westron) *il* cf. *[mûmakil](/wiki/M%C3%BBmakil "Mûmakil")* (which is perhaps <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Haradric&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Haradric (page does not exist)">Haradric</a>); similar formation with the ending *-li* is also seen in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> There is also a possibility of a plural formation with infixion from \**forgol*

The (possible) element *go* has been tied to the [Pre-Númenórean](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) word *[Go-hilleg](/wiki/Go-hilleg)*,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> which is untranslated, but refers to the [Númenórean settlers](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans"). It could mean "head", in this sense.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> However, both these interpretations remain speculation; [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") never left any notes on the subject.
