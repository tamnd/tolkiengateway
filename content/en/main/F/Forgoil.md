The **Forgoil**, or the **Strawheads** as it reportedly translated in [Westron](../W/Westron.md), were the enemies of the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md). In the rest of Middle-earth, they were known as [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Forgoil* is the only known word in the language of the Dunlendings. It is possible that the final part of the word, *-il*, is a plural marker, such as in the case of [Westron](../W/Westron.md) *il* cf. *[mûmakil](../M/Mûmakil.md)* (which is perhaps <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Haradric&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Haradric (page does not exist)">Haradric</a>); similar formation with the ending *-li* is also seen in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> There is also a possibility of a plural formation with infixion from \**forgol*

The (possible) element *go* has been tied to the [Pre-Númenórean](../P/Pre-Númenórean.md) word *[Go-hilleg](../G/Go-hilleg.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> which is untranslated, but refers to the [Númenórean settlers](../N/Númenóreans.md). It could mean "head", in this sense.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> However, both these interpretations remain speculation; [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) never left any notes on the subject.
