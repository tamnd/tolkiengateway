**First Age 468** (abbreviated to **F.A. 468**) is the 468th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Union of Maedhros](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md) is formed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
