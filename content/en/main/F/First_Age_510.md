**First Age 510** (abbreviated to **F.A. 510**) is the 510th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Midsummer {#Midsummer}

- The [Fall of Gondolin](Fall_of_Gondolin.md): [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) attacks [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) with an army of [orcs](../O/Orcs.md), [dragons](../D/Dragons.md), [wolves](../W/Wolves.md) and [balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Ecthelion](../E/Ecthelion.md) dies killing [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md>), Lord of Balrogs.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Deaths of King [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) and many chieftains of the [noble houses](../T/Twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim.md) of Gondolin.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Maeglin](../M/Maeglin.md) takes [Idril](../I/Idril.md) and [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md). [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) kills Maeglin.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Idril](../I/Idril.md) and [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) lead a group of [survivors](../E/Exiles_of_Gondolin.md) out a [secret tunnel](../I/Idril's_secret_way.md) and across the [Cirith Thoronath](../C/Cirith_Thoronath.md). [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) dies there killing a Balrog. The exiles wander towards the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) becomes [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup>
