The **Fourth Age** began after [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) was finally defeated, when his [Ruling Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") was destroyed, and the [Keepers of the Three Rings](/wiki/Keepers_of_the_Three_Rings) left Middle-earth for the [Uttermost West](/wiki/Aman "Aman").

## History {#History}

The [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) was held to have ended when [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) left Middle-earth on [29 September](/wiki/29_September) of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021").<sup id="cite_ref-Calendars_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Calendars-2">[2]</a></sup>

This age was marked by the recovery of the [Númenorean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor "Númenor") kingdoms of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), while the [House of Durin](/wiki/House_of_Durin) retook [Moria](/wiki/Moria) until the race of [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) failed.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Last Ship](/wiki/Last_Ship) of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), carrying [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan), [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), and all remaining Elves of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) left Middle-earth some time after <a class="mw-selflink selflink">Fo.A.</a> [171](/wiki/Fourth_Age_171 "Fourth Age 171").<sup id="cite_ref-Records_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Records-4">[4]</a></sup> Eventually that would lead to the final [Dominion of Men](/wiki/Dominion_of_Men) over [Arda](/wiki/Arda), coming together with the total waning of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and other dwindling races, such as the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) and probably the Dwarves.

[Eldarion](/wiki/Eldarion)'s reign would have lasted for about 100 years after the death of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn).<sup id="cite_ref-L338_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L338-5">[5]</a></sup>

There is no concrete information on more than the first few centuries of this Age, so it is not known when it ended, although it was probably shorter than 3 millennia,<sup id="cite_ref-Letter211_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letter211-6">[6]</a></sup> around c. 2,430 years or more,<sup id="cite_ref-Sixty_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sixty-7">[7]</a></sup> and was followed by the [Fifth Age](/wiki/Later_Ages "Later Ages").<sup id="cite_ref-Letter211_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letter211-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Sixty_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sixty-7">[7]</a></sup>

Additionally, according to one of the drafts of the *[Appendices](/wiki/Appendices)*, it is said:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Of <a href="/wiki/Eldarion" title="Eldarion">Eldarion</a> son of <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Elessar</a> it was foretold that he should rule a great <a href="/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom" title="Reunited Kingdom">realm</a>, and <b>that it should endure for a hundred generations of <a href="/wiki/Men" title="Men">Men</a> after him, that is until a new age brought in again new things</b>; and from him should come the kings of many realms in long days after.
</p>

## Reckoning of years {#Reckoning_of_years}

Not all calendars in Middle-earth reset their count of years to <a class="mw-selflink selflink">Fo.A.</a> [1](/wiki/Fourth_Age_1 "Fourth Age 1") in the same year, on the same date, or indeed at all. As such, years given for certain events in the Fourth Age differ according to the calendar used.

The [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning) is the calendar system used for all dates in the [Red Book](/wiki/Red_Book), with [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1](/wiki/Third_Age_1601 "Third Age 1601") corresponding to [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1601](/wiki/Third_Age_1601 "Third Age 1601").<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-9">[9]</a></sup> Years in Shire-reckoning did not restart at the end of the Third Age; they continued uninterrupted from [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1421](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021") to [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1422](/wiki/Fourth_Age_1 "Fourth Age 1"), and "in so far as the Hobbits took any account of the change of Age, they maintained that it began with [2 Yule](/wiki/2_Yule) 1422."<sup id="cite_ref-Calendars_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Calendars-2">[2]</a></sup> This means that [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1422](/wiki/Fourth_Age_1 "Fourth Age 1") is also [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3022](/wiki/Third_Age_3022 "Third Age 3022") and <a class="mw-selflink selflink">Fo.A.</a> [1](/wiki/Fourth_Age_1 "Fourth Age 1") as far as dates in the Red Book are concerned.

However, in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and the rest of the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom) outside the Shire, the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning) calendar entered use starting in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"). Under this calendar, [25 March](/wiki/25_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021") is the first day of <a class="mw-selflink selflink">Fo.A.</a> [1](/wiki/Fourth_Age_1 "Fourth Age 1"), some 9 months earlier than the new year in the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-Calendars_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Calendars-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) and the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning) begin their years on different days: 2 Yule for the Shire and 25 March for the rest of the Reunited Kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[note 1]</a></sup>

There are few dates given in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") that require the reader to account for this difference, because most dates of the Fourth Age are already explicitly expressed in terms of the Shire Reckoning. For example, [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli)'s departure from Middle-earth is given as [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1541](/wiki/Fourth_Age_120 "Fourth Age 120").<sup id="cite_ref-Later_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-11">[10]</a></sup> The exceptions are both from the [Note on the Shire Records](/wiki/Note_on_the_Shire_Records) and are as follows:

- Tolkien explains that the text of *The Lord of the Rings* descends from a copy of the Red Book made in Gondor that bore the note "Findegil, King’s Writer, finished this work in IV 172."<sup id="cite_ref-Records_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Records-4">[4]</a></sup> The accompanying text notes the equivalent year [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1592](/wiki/Fourth_Age_171 "Fourth Age 171"), which is <a class="mw-selflink selflink">Fo.A.</a> [171](/wiki/Fourth_Age_171 "Fourth Age 171") in the Shire Reckoning, so this note must have been written with regard to the New Reckoning calendar before 7 Narvinyë.
- In the same paragraph, Tolkien refers to [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took)'s retirement to Gondor in "IV 64."<sup id="cite_ref-Records_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Records-4">[4]</a></sup> We are told elsewhere that Peregrin and his companion [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) arrived in Gondor sometime between the autumn and the ending of [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1484](/wiki/Fourth_Age_63 "Fourth Age 63"), which is <a class="mw-selflink selflink">Fo.A.</a> [63](/wiki/Fourth_Age_63 "Fourth Age 63") in the Shire Reckoning.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-11">[10]</a></sup> As such, the stated "IV 64" must be in reference to the New Reckoning calendar, again, prior to 7 Narvinyë.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The Prologue of the first edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* mistakenly mentions that the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) "ended with the great years [1418](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") and [1419](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") of the Shire."<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup> whereas the Age ended in [1421](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021") and [1422](/wiki/Fourth_Age_1 "Fourth Age 1"). This reference was removed in the second edition.
