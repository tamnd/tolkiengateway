The **Free Peoples of the World**,<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup> also known as the **Free Folk**, were those people who were not under [Sauron's](../S/Sauron.md) domination.

By the later [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) Sauron had [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), [Trolls](../T/Trolls.md) and other creatures under his control, and other peoples like the [Men of Darkness](../M/Men_of_Darkness.md) enslaved or allied with him. As such, the Free Peoples were the [Elves](../E/Elves.md), [High](../D/Dúnedain.md) and [Middle Men](../M/Middle_Men.md), [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md), [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), and any folk who opposed him.

In the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md), [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) made a mention of the **Free Lords of the Free**, possibly referring to the rulers of the Free Peoples, such as [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) or [Denethor II](../D/Denethor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> while suggesting that they should use the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md) against Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The members of the [Fellowship of the Ring](Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) were specifically chosen so that at least one representative of all the free peoples would be there: one for the [Elves](../E/Elves.md), one for the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) and two for [Men](../M/Men.md).<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup> As they had appeared relatively late in the knowledge of most, [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) were generally not reckoned among them until the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the prologue, the term &#34;Free Peoples of Middle-earth&#34; is used to describe those that formed the <a href="../L/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men.md" title="Last Alliance of Elves and Men">Last Alliance of Elves and Men</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2004: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>&#34;Free Peoples&#34; is one of the playable factions, the other being &#34;Minions of Sauron&#34;. Its troops include Men of <a href="../G/Gondor.md" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> and <a href="../R/Rohan.md" title="Rohan">Rohan</a>, <a href="../D/Dwarves.md" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a>, <a href="../E/Elves.md" title="Elves">Elves</a>, <a href="../H/Huorns.md" title="Huorns">Huorns</a> and <a href="../B/Beornings.md" title="Beornings">Beornings</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The term &#34;Free Peoples&#34; is generally used to identify the &#34;good&#34; faction in the MonsterPlay sessions. It is often abbreviated as &#34;Freeps&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>
