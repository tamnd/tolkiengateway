The **Free Peoples of the World**,<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup> also known as the **Free Folk**, were those people who were not under [Sauron's](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron") domination.

By the later [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) Sauron had [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), [Trolls](/wiki/Trolls) and other creatures under his control, and other peoples like the [Men of Darkness](/wiki/Men_of_Darkness) enslaved or allied with him. As such, the Free Peoples were the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), [High](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain "Dúnedain") and [Middle Men](/wiki/Middle_Men), [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits), [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), and any folk who opposed him.

In the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond), [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) made a mention of the **Free Lords of the Free**, possibly referring to the rulers of the Free Peoples, such as [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) or [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> while suggesting that they should use the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring) against Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The members of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) were specifically chosen so that at least one representative of all the free peoples would be there: one for the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), one for the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) and two for [Men](/wiki/Men).<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup> As they had appeared relatively late in the knowledge of most, [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) were generally not reckoned among them until the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the prologue, the term &#34;Free Peoples of Middle-earth&#34; is used to describe those that formed the <a href="/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men" title="Last Alliance of Elves and Men">Last Alliance of Elves and Men</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2004: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>&#34;Free Peoples&#34; is one of the playable factions, the other being &#34;Minions of Sauron&#34;. Its troops include Men of <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> and <a href="/wiki/Rohan" title="Rohan">Rohan</a>, <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a>, <a href="/wiki/Elves" title="Elves">Elves</a>, <a href="/wiki/Huorns" title="Huorns">Huorns</a> and <a href="/wiki/Beornings" title="Beornings">Beornings</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The term &#34;Free Peoples&#34; is generally used to identify the &#34;good&#34; faction in the MonsterPlay sessions. It is often abbreviated as &#34;Freeps&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>
