**Findis** was a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldo "Noldo") [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves"). She was the first child and daughter of [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB), the [King of the Noldor](/wiki/King_of_the_Noldor), and [Indis](/wiki/Indis), his second wife.

She did not follow the [Noldor into exile](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor"), instead abiding amongst the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) with her mother.<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is stated that they were in grief until [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) deemed to restore Finwë to life,<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-1">[1]</a></sup> but that he may not have, as little is actually known of what occurred in [Aman](/wiki/Aman) following the departure of the Noldor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Her name was a combination of her parents' names.<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-1">[1]</a></sup>
