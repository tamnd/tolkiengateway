**First Age 507** (abbreviated to **F.A. 507**) is the 507th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Early {#Early}

- [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) is utterly destroyed following the [Second Kinslaying](../S/Second_Kinslaying.md) to never rise again.<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Keeping the [Nauglamír](../N/Nauglamír.md) and the [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md), [Elwing](../E/Elwing.md) arrives at the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md) amongst a remnant of the [people of Doriath](../E/Elves_of_Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>
- [Eluréd](../E/Eluréd.md) and [Elurín](../E/Elurín.md) are lost in the woods,<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-2">[2]</a></sup> but [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) repents and seeks unavailingly for the sons of [Dior](../D/Dior.md). Nothing certain is known of their fate, but some say that the birds succoured them and led them to [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup>
