**Father Christmas** is the name of the gift-giving figure of Christmas. He sent letters to the Tolkien children from [1920](/wiki/1920) to [1943](/wiki/1943) and they are collected in a book called *[Letters from Father Christmas](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas)*.

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

Father Christmas was magical and his magic was especially powerful on Christmas.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Father Christmas had very shaky handwriting being over 1900 years old by the time he started writing the letters.<sup id="cite_ref-l23_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l23-2">[2]</a></sup> He was prone to stress which made his handwriting even worse.<sup id="cite_ref-l25_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l25-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Father Christmas wrote his first letter to [John Tolkien](/wiki/John_Tolkien) in [1920](/wiki/1920) after the then three-year-old,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> John had asked what Father Christmas was like and where he lived.<sup id="cite_ref-l20_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l20-5">[5]</a></sup>

Christmas in [1924](/wiki/1924) was very busy so he didn't have time to write a full letter but he wrote a short letter to both John and [Hilary](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien "Hilary Tolkien") wishing them well and explaining he didn't have time to write a letter because he had to go deliver presents.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In [1925](/wiki/1925), Father Christmas had to move from the [Christmas House](/wiki/Christmas_House) to the [Cliff House](/wiki/Cliff_House). The North Polar Bear had tried to rescue his hood that had been blown onto the roof and fell through the roof and made a hole in the living room.<sup id="cite_ref-l25_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l25-3">[3]</a></sup>

[1926](/wiki/1926) was a very stressful year. The [North Polar Bear](/wiki/North_Polar_Bear) set off a firework which turned the North Pole black, shook the stars and cracked the moon into four and the [Man in the Moon](/wiki/Man_in_the_Moon) had fallen. He ate a lot of Father Christmas' chocolates before returning to the moon. Then the reindeer got loose and ran wild around the North Pole. The North Polar Bear then turned on two years worth of Northern Lights.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Because of this there were no Northern Lights until [1928](/wiki/1928)<sup id="cite_ref-l27_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l27-9">[9]</a></sup>

In [1932](/wiki/1932), Father Christmas and the North Polar Bear discovered a tunnel in their cellar leading from the caves that had been dug by [Goblins](/wiki/Orcs#Other_writings "Orcs"). The Goblins stole all of the items from the main cellar and although they were able to get the items back, [Mr Cave Bear](/wiki/Mr_Cave_Bear), his sons and the Gnome-ladies had to help prepare for Christmas in order to be done in time.<sup id="cite_ref-1932b_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1932b-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Father Christmas also signed his letters as **Father Nicholas Christmas**,<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> or just **Nicholas Christmas**.<sup id="cite_ref-1932b_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1932b-10">[10]</a></sup> In one letter, the [North Polar Bear](/wiki/North_Polar_Bear) calls him **Daddy Noel**.<sup id="cite_ref-1932b_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1932b-10">[10]</a></sup>
