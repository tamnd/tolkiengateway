**Finrún Felageómor** was the fourth child of [Finwë](Finwë.md) in the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Fin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fin-1">[1]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about this character.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Finrún* is in [Old English](../O/Old_English.md), since it was translated by [Ælfwine](../_/Ælfwine.md), but no [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) equivalent is given.<sup id="cite_ref-Fin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fin-1">[1]</a></sup> *Felageómor* is likewise an Old English name (meaning "very sorrowful").<sup id="cite_ref-Fin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fin-1">[1]</a></sup>

In Old Norse "rún" meant "rune" or "secret";<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> from this "rūn" in Old English was derived with the same meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Names ending with "rún" were more commonly associated with females (cf. "Guðrún" and "Sigrún" from the Poetic Edda).
