**Fangorn** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) the [Ent](/wiki/Ents "Ents"), also known by the name *Fangorn*.
- [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest), a large forest to the north of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) where [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) and the other Ents live.

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- *Fangorn*, the first episode of the second half (and seventh overall) of [The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)>), aired [November 19](/wiki/19_November "19 November"), [1956](/wiki/1956).
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Treebeard_Of_Fangorn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Treebeard Of Fangorn (page does not exist)">Treebeard Of Fangorn</a></i>, the twelfth episode of [*The Lord of the Rings* (1981 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), aired [May 24](/wiki/24_May "24 May"), [1981](/wiki/1981).
- *[Night Camp at Fangorn](/wiki/Night_Camp_at_Fangorn)*, the tenth scene of the [Extended Edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)") of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*.
- *[Fangorn comes to Helm's Deep](/wiki/Fangorn_Comes_to_Helm%27s_Deep "Fangorn Comes to Helm's Deep")*, the sixty-first scene of the [Extended Edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)") of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*.
- "[Fangorn (song)](</wiki/Fangorn_(soundtrack)> "Fangorn (soundtrack)")", the tenth track of Disc 1 of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers - The Complete Recordings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_-_The_Complete_Recordings)*.
