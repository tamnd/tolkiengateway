**Fangorn** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md) the [Ent](../E/Ents.md), also known by the name *Fangorn*.
- [Fangorn Forest](Fangorn_Forest.md), a large forest to the north of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) where [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md) and the other Ents live.

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- *Fangorn*, the first episode of the second half (and seventh overall) of [The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>), aired [November 19](../_/19_November.md), [1956](../_/1956.md).
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Treebeard_Of_Fangorn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Treebeard Of Fangorn (page does not exist)">Treebeard Of Fangorn</a></i>, the twelfth episode of [*The Lord of the Rings* (1981 radio series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), aired [May 24](../_/24_May.md), [1981](../_/1981.md).
- *[Night Camp at Fangorn](../N/Night_Camp_at_Fangorn.md)*, the tenth scene of the [Extended Edition](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*.
- *[Fangorn comes to Helm's Deep](Fangorn_Comes_to_Helm's_Deep.md)*, the sixty-first scene of the [Extended Edition](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*.
- "[Fangorn (song)](<Fangorn_(soundtrack).md>)", the tenth track of Disc 1 of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers - The Complete Recordings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_-_The_Complete_Recordings.md)*.
