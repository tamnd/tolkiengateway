**Framsburg** was a fortified burg<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup> north of the confluence of the rivers [Greylin](/wiki/Greylin) and [Langwell](/wiki/Langwell).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It is possible that Framsburg was founded shortly after the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), the ancestors of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), arrived in their new land in the far north of [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) between the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) in the west and the [Forest River](/wiki/Forest_River) in the east<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup> in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1977](/wiki/Third_Age_1977 "Third Age 1977"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> during the rule of [Frumgar](/wiki/Frumgar), but it was presumably named after his son [Fram](/wiki/Fram),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> the famed slayer of the [Dragon](/wiki/Long-worms "Long-worms") [Scatha](/wiki/Scatha).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> It was the only fortified burg of the Éothéod, while they lived in the far north of Rhovanion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is possible that Framsburg was abandoned circa [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510") when [Eorl the Young](/wiki/Eorl_the_Young) led the Éothéod south to settle in [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon), the land later known as [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
