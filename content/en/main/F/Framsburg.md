**Framsburg** was a fortified burg<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup> north of the confluence of the rivers [Greylin](../G/Greylin.md) and [Langwell](../L/Langwell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It is possible that Framsburg was founded shortly after the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md), the ancestors of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md), arrived in their new land in the far north of [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) between the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) in the west and the [Forest River](Forest_River.md) in the east<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup> in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1977](../T/Third_Age_1977.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> during the rule of [Frumgar](Frumgar.md), but it was presumably named after his son [Fram](Fram.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> the famed slayer of the [Dragon](../L/Long-worms.md) [Scatha](../S/Scatha.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> It was the only fortified burg of the Éothéod, while they lived in the far north of Rhovanion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is possible that Framsburg was abandoned circa [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md) when [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl_the_Young.md) led the Éothéod south to settle in [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md), the land later known as [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
