**First Age 456** (abbreviated to **F.A. 456**) is the 456th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Fingolfin](Fingolfin.md) in [single combat](Fall_of_Fingolfin.md) with [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). [Thorondor](../T/Thorondor.md) bears Fingolfin's body away and he is buried by his son [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) in a [cairn](Fingolfin's_Cairn.md) overlooking [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), a young child, is sent to the [Havens of the Falas](../H/Havens_of_the_Falas.md) for protection.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md) refuses to give up on [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) and forms a [band of outlaws](../B/Barahir's_outlaws.md) who seek to thwart the designs of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). There are thirteen members of the group: [Dagnir](../D/Dagnir.md), [Ragnor](../R/Ragnor.md), [Radhruin](../R/Radhruin.md), [Dairuin](../D/Dairuin.md), [Gildor](<../G/Gildor_(outlaw).md>), [Gorlim the Unhappy](../G/Gorlim.md), [Urthel](../U/Urthel.md), [Arthad](../A/Arthad.md), [Hathaldir](../H/Hathaldir.md); [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md), his son [Beren](../B/Beren.md), and his kinsmen [Belegund](../B/Belegund.md) and [Baragund](../B/Baragund.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Emeldir](../E/Emeldir.md), wife of [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md), leads [their people](../H/House_of_Bëor.md) out of [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) to [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-4">[4]</a></sup> Some, including [Morwen](../M/Morwen.md) and [Rían](../R/Rían.md), continue on to [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-1">[1]</a></sup>
