**First Age 456** (abbreviated to **F.A. 456**) is the 456th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) in [single combat](/wiki/Fall_of_Fingolfin "Fall of Fingolfin") with [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth). [Thorondor](/wiki/Thorondor) bears Fingolfin's body away and he is buried by his son [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) in a [cairn](/wiki/Fingolfin%27s_Cairn "Fingolfin's Cairn") overlooking [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), a young child, is sent to the [Havens of the Falas](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas) for protection.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir) refuses to give up on [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) and forms a [band of outlaws](/wiki/Barahir%27s_outlaws "Barahir's outlaws") who seek to thwart the designs of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth). There are thirteen members of the group: [Dagnir](/wiki/Dagnir), [Ragnor](/wiki/Ragnor), [Radhruin](/wiki/Radhruin), [Dairuin](/wiki/Dairuin), [Gildor](</wiki/Gildor_(outlaw)> "Gildor (outlaw)"), [Gorlim the Unhappy](/wiki/Gorlim "Gorlim"), [Urthel](/wiki/Urthel), [Arthad](/wiki/Arthad), [Hathaldir](/wiki/Hathaldir); [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir), his son [Beren](/wiki/Beren), and his kinsmen [Belegund](/wiki/Belegund) and [Baragund](/wiki/Baragund).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Emeldir](/wiki/Emeldir), wife of [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir), leads [their people](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor "House of Bëor") out of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) to [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-4">[4]</a></sup> Some, including [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen) and [Rían](/wiki/R%C3%ADan), continue on to [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min).<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-1">[1]</a></sup>
