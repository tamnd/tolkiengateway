**Firith** was the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for the season of late autumn, and called [Quellë](/wiki/Quell%C3%AB) in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). In the [Reckoning of Rivendell](/wiki/Reckoning_of_Rivendell) it was fourth of the six "seasons", and corresponded more or less to the 54 days lying between [7 October](/wiki/7_October) and [30 November](/wiki/30_November) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
