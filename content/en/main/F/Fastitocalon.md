**Fastitocalon** was a legendary sea monster, apparently the last of the mighty [Turtle-fish](../T/Turtle-fish.md). He is only mentioned in [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) verse.<sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup>

Fastitocalon was the size of a small island and vegetation would often grow on his back when not submerged, adding to his deceitful appearance as an actual island. Because of his huge size, sailors landed on his back only to drown when he dove underwater.<sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fastitocalon* derives from the word *astitocalon* of the [Shire-speech](../H/Hobbitish.md) (rendered in modern English), which itself is an alteration or corruption of the [High-Elven](../E/Elvish.md) *Aspido-chelōne* (rendered in Greek, meaning "turtle with a round shield (of hide)"), and "the F was put on by the versifier simply to make the name alliterate".<sup id="cite_ref-letter_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-letter-2">[2]</a></sup>
