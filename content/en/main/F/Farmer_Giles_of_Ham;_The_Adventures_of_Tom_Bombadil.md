<i><b>Farmer Giles of Ham; The Adventures of Tom Bombadil</b></i> is a [1975](/wiki/1975) anthology, published by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Unwin_Books&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Unwin Books (page does not exist)">Unwin Books</a>, collecting the books *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)* and *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)* under the same covers.
