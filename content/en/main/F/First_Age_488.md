**First Age 488** (abbreviated to **F.A. 488**) is the 488th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Annael](../A/Annael.md) and the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of [Androth](../A/Androth.md) leave [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) for the [Havens of Sirion](../H/Havens_of_Sirion.md). They escape but [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) is captured.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) is enthralled by [Lorgan](../L/Lorgan.md), chief of the [Easterlings](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>) in Hithlum.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Around this time, the [Elves of Nargothrond](../E/Elves_of_Nargothrond.md) become yet more wary and secret, and will not suffer even Elves to stray in their lands.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
