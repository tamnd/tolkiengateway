**First Age 587** (abbreviated to **F.A. 587**) is the 587th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) is destroyed during the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) and [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) is defeated and nearly all the [dragons](../D/Dragons.md) and [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md) are killed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Ancalagon the Black](../A/Ancalagon.md) is destroyed by [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md). Ancalagon's body falls from the sky and destroys [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md).
- Much of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) sinks beneath [the sea](../B/Belegaer.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Eönwë](../E/Eönwë.md) takes the [Silmarils](../S/Silmarils.md) from [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). Morgoth's [Iron Crown](../I/Iron_Crown.md) is beat into a collar around his neck and attached to [Angainor](../A/Angainor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) and [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md) steal the Silmarils. The Silmarils burn their hands and Maedhros jumps into a fiery chasm with his Silmaril whilst Maglor casts his Silmaril into the sea. The fate of Maglor is unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-2">[2]</a></sup>
