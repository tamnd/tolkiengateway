**First Age 443** (abbreviated to **F.A. 443**) is the 443rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Hunleth](../H/Hunleth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:237</sup>
- Birth of [Morwen Eledhwen](../M/Morwen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:231</sup>
- *Possible* birth of [Borlad](../B/Borlad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>
