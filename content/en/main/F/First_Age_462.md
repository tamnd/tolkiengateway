**First Age 462** (abbreviated to **F.A. 462**) is the 462nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) renewed his assault against [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md).
    - At [Barad Eithel](../B/Barad_Eithel.md), [Galdor](<../G/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-lómin).md>) was slain and [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) became [Lord of Dor-lómin](../L/Lord_of_Dor-lómin.md); he drove the Orcs back from the walls and into the [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Fingon](Fingon.md) faced a separate army coming down from the north and, outnumbered, was hard pressed to hold them back and battle spilled into the plains of Hithlum. [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md), however, sent aid and landed his forces in [Drengist](../D/Drengist.md) which then fell upon the west flank of the [Orcs](../O/Orc.md). The Orcs then broke and fled, pursued by horsed archers.<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Between 455 and 462 {#Between_455_and_462}

- At was noted that at sometime prior to this year, Morgoth's army which had driven into [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md) following the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) had been utterly destroyed. It was initially broken by [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) on the borders of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), with the scattered part that departed south never seen again, that which went north destroyed by [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md), and that which went east near the mountains hunted by [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
