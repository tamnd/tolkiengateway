**Foalókë** is a type of [dragon](/wiki/Dragon "Dragon"). The name is only attested in [a manuscript](/wiki/Words,_phrases_and_passages_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "Words, phrases and passages in The Lord of the Rings") without context, so although it clearly means "breath-dragon",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> it is unclear what kind of breath it means. If the trait referred to breathing fire or cold, then it was another name for [dragons](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), *[foa](/wiki/Foa "Foa")* ("breath") + *[lókë](/wiki/L%C3%B3k%C3%AB "Lókë")*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name was first conceived for "a serpent that guarded a treasure", as back then the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) word *foa* referred to "hoard".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) form was *Fuithlug* ("a dragon (who guards treasure)").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The name was mainly applied to the dragon [Glórund](/wiki/Glaurung#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium "Glaurung").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
