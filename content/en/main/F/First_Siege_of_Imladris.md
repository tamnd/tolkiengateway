The **first siege of Imladris** was a siege of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond)'s refuge by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s forces during the [War of the Elves and Sauron](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron) in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).

## Background {#Background}

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1200](/wiki/Second_Age_1200 "Second Age 1200") [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) came to [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) in disguise, claiming to be an emissary of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) sent to offer aid to the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar). Although rejected by [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), he was able to win over [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor) and the smiths of Eregion. The brotherhood of smiths, the [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-M%C3%ADrdain), profited from his knowledge<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-1">[1]</a></sup> and around [1500](/wiki/Second_Age_1500 "Second Age 1500") they began forging the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power), finishing them in [1590](/wiki/Second_Age_1590 "Second Age 1590").<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup> Unbeknownst to them, Sauron (who had returned to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor)) created a master [Ring](/wiki/One_Ring "One Ring") to rule the others. However, Celebrimbor perceived the designs of Sauron and prevented the use of the [Three Rings](/wiki/Three_Rings).

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r398211"/>

Infuriated by Celebrimbor's defiance, Sauron led a host out of Mordor.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-1">[1]</a></sup> In [1695](/wiki/Second_Age_1695 "Second Age 1695") this army invaded [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) from the south.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup> Gil-galad sent out a force under the command of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) which joined with [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) in defence of Eregion, but they were unable to prevent Sauron from conquering the realm. Celebrimbor was captured and killed. Elrond too would have been overwhelmed but for an attack upon Sauron's force from the rear by [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) from [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") and many [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) led by [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-1">[1]</a></sup> Elrond gathered what remained of the [Elves of Eregion](/wiki/Elves_of_Eregion) and retreated northward.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Siege {#Siege}

In [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697") Elrond founded the refuge of [Imladris](/wiki/Imladris). Sauron did not immediately pursue Elrond, turning his attention upon the [Dwarves of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm) and [Elves of Lórinand](/wiki/Elves_of_L%C3%B3rinand). Once they were driven back into Khazad-dûm and the gates were shut, Sauron sought to overrun all of Eriador. While Sauron sent most of his army west to attack [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), he had to leave a strong detachment behind to contain Elrond.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-1">[1]</a></sup>

The siege lasted until [1700](/wiki/Second_Age_1700 "Second Age 1700"), when a great [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) navy, sent by [Tar-Minastir](/wiki/Tar-Minastir), arrived in Lindon.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup> The might of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) broke Sauron's army at the [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) and crushed his forces at the [Battle of the Gwathló](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Gwathl%C3%B3). The army he had left besieging Imladris was caught between Elrond and Gil-galad and utterly destroyed, thus ending the siege.

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

A [Council](/wiki/White_Council "White Council") was held in the wake of Sauron's defeat. Given the devastation of Eregion, it was decided that Imladris should become the new Elvish stronghold in eastern [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador). In recognition of his efforts, Gil-galad bestowed the ring [Vilya](/wiki/Vilya) upon Elrond at this time and made him his vice-regent.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-1">[1]</a></sup> Elrond maintained Imladris from then on until the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), when he departed from [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
