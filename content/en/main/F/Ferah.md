**Ferah** is a black/thrash metal band from Schwaigern, Germany, formed in 2011.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its 2017 album *Yalme* includes songs named after [Erech](/wiki/Erech) and the Iron Crown ("Eisenkrone").

## Members {#Members}

- Ûrfang - drums
- Morgond - guitars
- Pethron - vocals, bass

## Discography {#Discography}

- Yalme (Full-length, 2017)
    1. Erech
    2. Ein Grabenlied
    3. Marie
    4. Uns folgt der Tod
    5. Eisenkrone
    6. Angriff
    7. Furor Maniacus
    8. Silberfaust
    9. Kostnice
