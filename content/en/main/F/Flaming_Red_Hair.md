**Flaming Red Hair** is the fifth track of disc one of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - The Complete Recordings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_The_Complete_Recordings)* by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore). The track was composed by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Plan_9&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Plan 9 (page does not exist)">Plan 9</a> with <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=David_Long&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="David Long (page does not exist)">David Long</a>, a group of Kiwi musicians who had previously collaborated with [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) and [Fran Walsh](/wiki/Fran_Walsh). The piece was performed on-set, before Shore joined the project, to provide music for the actors to dance to. When the time came to compose the score, Shore and others decided it was a great composition and left it in the final film.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
