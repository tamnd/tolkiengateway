**Fourth Age 185** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 185** or **F.O.**) is the 185th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 185 was also known as **S.R. 1606** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Harding of the Hill](../H/Harding_of_the_Hill.md), [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)'s heir, dies.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
