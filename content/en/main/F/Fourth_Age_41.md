**Fourth Age 41** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 41** or **F.O.**) is the 41st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 41 was also known as **S.R. 1462** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:==[Mid-year's Day](../M/Mid-year's_Day.md)==

- [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) is elected [Mayor of Michel Delving](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md) for the sixth time.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>
- At Samwise's request, [Thain](../T/Thain.md) [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) makes [Fastred](Fastred_of_Greenholm.md) the [Warden of Westmarch](../W/Warden_of_Westmarch.md). Fastred and [Elanor](../E/Elanor_Gardner.md) make their dwelling at [Undertowers](../U/Undertowers.md) on the [Tower Hills](../T/Tower_Hills.md), where their descendants, the [Fairbairns of the Towers](Fairbairn_Family~66364.md), dwelt for many generations.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Within the Year {#Within_the_Year}

- Birth of [Holfast Gardner](../H/Holfast_Gardner.md), son of [Frodo Gardner](Frodo_Gardner.md) and grandson of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to the "[Prologue](<../P/Prologue_(The_Lord_of_the_Rings).md>)" to *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, the [Westmarch](../W/Westmarch.md) was added to [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) in this year ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) <a class="mw-selflink selflink">1462</a>), contradicting the [Tale of Years](../T/Tale_of_Years.md) that gave [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1452](Fourth_Age_31.md). The date was corrected to 1452 in the Prologue of [50th Anniversary Edition](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md>). <span></span>
