The **Falas** was an area on the west coast of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), facing the [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md). The [Falathrim](Falathrim.md), [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) [Elves](../E/Elves.md) ruled by [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md), dwelt here. It was widely regarded as one of the most peaceful and beautiful areas of Beleriand.

## Geography {#Geography}

The River [Brithon](../B/Brithon.md) and the River [Nenning](../N/Nenning.md) ran into the sea here. The Elves built the [havens](../H/Havens_of_the_Falas.md) of [Brithombar](../B/Brithombar.md) and [Eglarest](../E/Eglarest.md). [Finrod](Finrod.md) built the tower of [Barad Nimras](../B/Barad_Nimras.md) ("White Horn Tower") on a cape of the Falas between Brithombar and Eglarest.

## History {#History}

The Falas only occasionally enters into the events of the [First Age](First_Age.md); in the [First Battle](First_Battle.md) of Beleriand, Morgoth's host overran the Falas and besieged the two havens, but lifted the siege in order to join in the Second Battle.

[Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) had an alliance with the Falas. During the [Siege of Angband](../S/Siege_of_Angband.md) ([F.A.](First_Age.md) [65](First_Age_65.md)),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Elves of Nargothrond](../E/Elves_of_Nargothrond.md) aided the Falathrim in rebuilding the two Havens and made them fair and strong. They also built Barad Nimras to watch for attacks from the sea by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Havens of the Falas were finally [destroyed](Fall_of_the_Falas.md) in [F.A.](First_Age.md) [473](First_Age_473.md), and Círdan's people fled to the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md) and the [Isle of Balar](../I/Isle_of_Balar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *falas* means "surfline, sea-shore" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or "surf, wave-beaten shore"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> deriving from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) PHAL ("foam")
