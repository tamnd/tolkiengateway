The **Falas** was an area on the west coast of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), facing the [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer). The [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim), [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) [Elves](/wiki/Elves) ruled by [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan), dwelt here. It was widely regarded as one of the most peaceful and beautiful areas of Beleriand.

## Geography {#Geography}

The River [Brithon](/wiki/Brithon) and the River [Nenning](/wiki/Nenning) ran into the sea here. The Elves built the [havens](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas "Havens of the Falas") of [Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar) and [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest). [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) built the tower of [Barad Nimras](/wiki/Barad_Nimras) ("White Horn Tower") on a cape of the Falas between Brithombar and Eglarest.

## History {#History}

The Falas only occasionally enters into the events of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age); in the [First Battle](/wiki/First_Battle) of Beleriand, Morgoth's host overran the Falas and besieged the two havens, but lifted the siege in order to join in the Second Battle.

[Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) had an alliance with the Falas. During the [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband) ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [65](/wiki/First_Age_65 "First Age 65")),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Elves of Nargothrond](/wiki/Elves_of_Nargothrond) aided the Falathrim in rebuilding the two Havens and made them fair and strong. They also built Barad Nimras to watch for attacks from the sea by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Havens of the Falas were finally [destroyed](/wiki/Fall_of_the_Falas "Fall of the Falas") in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [473](/wiki/First_Age_473 "First Age 473"), and Círdan's people fled to the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion) and the [Isle of Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *falas* means "surfline, sea-shore" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or "surf, wave-beaten shore"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> deriving from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") PHAL ("foam")
