**First Age 64** (abbreviated to **F.A. 64**) is the 64th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) secretly removes some of his people from [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) to begin construction on [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
