**First Age 486** (abbreviated to **F.A. 486**) is the 486th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Seeking [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md), [Beleg](../B/Beleg.md) finds the [Gaurwaith](../G/Gaurwaith.md), but as his friend isn't there, they mistake the [Elf](../E/Elf.md) for a spy and torture him, until Túrin returns.
- Beleg is unable to persuade Túrin to come with him, despite that he has been pardoned by [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md). Beleg leaves to return to [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Increasing numbers of [orcs](../O/Orcs.md) enter [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md); the [Gaurwaith](../G/Gaurwaith.md) are forced southwards down the [old South Road](<../O/Old_South_Road_(Beleriand).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-CH_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CH-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The Outlaws encounter the [petty-dwarf](../P/Petty-dwarves.md) [Mîm](../M/Mîm.md) who offers them his home [Amon Rûdh](../A/Amon_Rûdh.md) in return for his life. It is known as Bar-en-Danwedh, the [House of Ransom](../H/House_of_Ransom.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CH_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CH-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Khîm](../K/Khîm.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CH_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CH-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Winter {#Winter}

- Beleg sets out again to return to Túrin.<sup id="cite_ref-CH_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CH-2">[2]</a></sup>
