The **Fords of Isen**<sup id="cite_ref-Nazgul_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nazgul-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Battle_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-2">[2]</a></sup> also called **Isenford**<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> or the **Crossings of Isen**<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> were fords across the river [Isen](/wiki/Isen) over which travellers on the [Great Road](/wiki/Great_Road) could cross the river.<sup id="cite_ref-UI_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-6">[6]</a></sup>

The fords were located a few miles north of where the Isen turned west towards the [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) in the [Gap of Rohan](/wiki/Gap_of_Rohan), a strategic location where the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) in the north were close to the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) in the south. They were the main entrance to the realm of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) from [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) in the west.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

Just north to the westward bent of the River Isen ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) **[Sîr Angren](/wiki/S%C3%AEr_Angren)**) towards [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer),<sup id="cite_ref-Map_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-7">[7]</a></sup> it became broad and shallow, flowing across a stony shelf and branching into two arms about a large island. Only here could large forces effectively cross the river, and the Fords were the only place where the river could be crossed south of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-8">[8]</a></sup> The road from [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) to Isengard passed over the fords and western part of the road continued on the western side of the Isen.<sup id="cite_ref-UI_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-6">[6]</a></sup> On both sides of the river the road descended between banks of turf down to the edge of the water. Three series of flat stepping stones with fords for horses between them led from each side of the river to a bare island in the middle of the river.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the time of the [Realms in Exile](/wiki/Realms_in_Exile), the [North-South Road](/wiki/North-South_Road), which connected [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), crossed the Isen, and the Fords of Isen—the [Ethraid Engrin](/wiki/Ethraid_Engrin) (or *Athrad Angren*), marked the entrance into the South Kingdom of Gondor. The Fords were closely guarded from the Gondorian fortresses of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) and the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg) at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Map_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-7">[7]</a></sup>

After the fall of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) at the end of the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3441](/wiki/Second_Age_3441 "Second Age 3441"),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and while [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) was still in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> the larger part of the army of Arnor returned to Arnor on the Great Road crossing the Isen at the Fords of Isen.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510")<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> the Steward [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion) granted the land of [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) to [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) the Young. The River Isen then became the western boundary of the new realm of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) and responsibility for the great road and the Fords of Isen fell upon the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), although it was agreed that free passageway would be maintained for all travelers of either people.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

In [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758")<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> a force of [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) led by [Wulf](/wiki/Wulf) invaded Rohan through the Crossings of Isen. King [Helm](/wiki/Helm) attempted to fight them off at that point but was defeated and retreated to [Suthburg](/wiki/Hornburg "Hornburg") while Rohan was overran.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

On [18 September](/wiki/18_September), [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), the [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") crossed the Fords of Isen on their way to [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-Nazgul_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nazgul-1">[1]</a></sup> [Six days later](/wiki/24_September "24 September"), Gandalf crossed the Isen on his way to [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire), probably using the Fords of Isen, because he wanted to get to the Shire quickly and he wanted to avoid Isengard after having been imprisoned there.<sup id="cite_ref-Nazgul_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nazgul-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [25 February](/wiki/25_February), [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-2">[2]</a></sup> the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman), reinforced by [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) and wolfriders, plus two battalions of [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai), rushed down upon [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred), King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden)'s son, and killed Prince Théodred in the [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen). This blow against the leadership of Rohan was Saruman's main objective in this battle and his forces then withdrew.<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-8">[8]</a></sup>

[Erkenbrand](/wiki/Erkenbrand) of [Westfold](/wiki/Westfold) maintained a defence at the Fords after the First Battle, under the immediate command of [Grimbold](/wiki/Grimbold), strengthened by [Elfhelm](/wiki/Elfhelm) and his riders. However, on [2 March](/wiki/2_March),<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> the host of Saruman overwhelmed the defenders and swept across the Fords in the [Second Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen).<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-8">[8]</a></sup>

[Three days later](/wiki/5_March "5 March"), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) crossed over the Fords of Isen on their way to Minas Tirith. The [next day](/wiki/6_March "6 March"), Théoden, Aragorn, Legolas, Gimli and riders from Rohan took the Fords of Isen to return to Rohan. Not lang after they had croosed over the river, [Halbarad](/wiki/Halbarad) and thirty [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North) accompanied by [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan) and [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir), the sons of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) also crossed the Fords of Isen and caught up with them.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

In August,<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) took leave of the reduced company that had formed King Théoden's funeral escort near the Fords of Isen.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for the Fords of Isen was <i><b>Ethraid Engrin</b></i> (singular <i><b>Athrad Angren</b></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup>

The literal meaning of *Ethraid Engrin* is "fords of iron" (*[ethraid](/wiki/Athrad "Athrad")* + *[engrin](/wiki/Angren "Angren")*).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
