**Fourth Age 171** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 171** or **F.O.**) is the 171st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 171 was also known as **S.R. 1592** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- A copy of the [Red Book of Westmarch](../R/Red_Book_of_Westmarch.md) is completed in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) by [Findegil](Findegil.md), the [King's Writer](../K/King's_Writer.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Note {#Note}

The *[Note on the Shire Records](../N/Note_on_the_Shire_Records.md)* gives this date as "F.A. 17**2**" and "IV 17**2**," but also states its [Shire Reckoning](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) equivalent to be S.R. 1592. Since S.R. 1592 is equivalent to Fo.A. 171 under the Shire Reckoning, the year number 172 must refer to the [New Reckoning](../N/New_Reckoning.md) of Gondor. The New Reckoning count of years in the Fourth Age is [ahead of the Shire count](Fourth_Age.md#Reckoning_of_years) by one for all days of the year between 25 [March](../M/March.md) and 1 [Yule](../Y/Yule.md) (these days are named [Yestarë](../Y/Yestarë.md) and 6 [Narvinyë](../N/Narvinyë.md) respectively in the New Reckoning). <span></span>
