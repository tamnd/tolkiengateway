The **First Deep** was the level in [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") immediately beneath the [Great Gates](/wiki/Great_Gates) on the eastern side of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). After the battle in the [Chamber of Mazarbul](/wiki/Chamber_of_Mazarbul) the members of the [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") fled down seven flights of stairs and traveled about a mile, arriving at the [Second Hall](/wiki/Second_Hall) on the First Deep. It was on this level that the [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm) lay.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
