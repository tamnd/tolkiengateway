The **First Deep** was the level in [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md) immediately beneath the [Great Gates](../G/Great_Gates.md) on the eastern side of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md). After the battle in the [Chamber of Mazarbul](../C/Chamber_of_Mazarbul.md) the members of the [Fellowship](Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) fled down seven flights of stairs and traveled about a mile, arriving at the [Second Hall](../S/Second_Hall.md) on the First Deep. It was on this level that the [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](../B/Bridge_of_Khazad-dûm.md) lay.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
