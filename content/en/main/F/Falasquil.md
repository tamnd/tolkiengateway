**Falasquil** was a home of [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

After Tuor had left [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim), he reached the shores of the [Sea](/wiki/Sea) and marvelled at what he saw. There he dwelt some time in a cove covered with rocks, whose floor was of white sand. But the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) from [Dor Lómin](/wiki/Dor_L%C3%B3min) began to sent him wood through the hidden river of [Glorfalc](/wiki/Glorfalc), so Tuor could make a cottage himself.

The walls were embellished by Tuor with animals, birds, trees and flowers. His favourite carving was a swan, which eventually became the emblem of his [house](/wiki/House_of_the_Swan "House of the Swan"). Tuor dwelt there for a while, until Ulmo appointed him with the mission to reach [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Falasqil* is [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), meaning "Beach of Peace". The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate was <i><b>Falas-a-&#39;Wilb</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
