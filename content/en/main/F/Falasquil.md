**Falasquil** was a home of [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

## History {#History}

After Tuor had left [Mithrim](../M/Mithrim.md), he reached the shores of the [Sea](../S/Sea.md) and marvelled at what he saw. There he dwelt some time in a cove covered with rocks, whose floor was of white sand. But the [Gnomes](../G/Gnomes.md) from [Dor Lómin](../D/Dor_Lómin.md) began to sent him wood through the hidden river of [Glorfalc](../G/Glorfalc.md), so Tuor could make a cottage himself.

The walls were embellished by Tuor with animals, birds, trees and flowers. His favourite carving was a swan, which eventually became the emblem of his [house](../H/House_of_the_Swan.md). Tuor dwelt there for a while, until Ulmo appointed him with the mission to reach [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Falasqil* is [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), meaning "Beach of Peace". The [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) cognate was <i><b>Falas-a-&#39;Wilb</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
