**First Age 320** (abbreviated to **F.A. 320**) is the 320th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Maeglin](../M/Maeglin.md), son of [Aredhel](../A/Aredhel.md) and [Eöl](../E/Eöl.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
