**fanga** is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for "beard".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[PQ](/wiki/PQ) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Spang%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Spangâ (page does not exist)">spangâ</a></i>, perhaps from even earlier \**spanagâ*; [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=SP%C3%81NAG&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SPÁNAG (page does not exist)">SPÁNAG</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}
