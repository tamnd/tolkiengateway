**Aegnor** was an [Elf](../E/Elves_of_Rivendell.md) of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), affectionately known to his contemporaries in Rivendell as **Figwit**.<sup id="cite_ref-Decipher_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Decipher-1">[1]</a></sup> He attended [the Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md) and later travelled to [the Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) with [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) to pass into [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).

Figwit is an original creation for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) and is portrayed by [Bret McKenzie](../B/Bret_McKenzie.md). Figwit, while later made official by Topps<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and [Decipher](../D/Decipher.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Decipher_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Decipher-1">[1]</a></sup> was initially a fan-created name. Figwit is an acronym for "[Frodo](Frodo_Baggins.md) is grea...who is THAT?!?", with the latter part referring to the viewer's surprise at the character's beauty during the Council of Elrond scene.

## History {#History}

### Early life {#Early_life}

Aegnor was an [Elf](../E/Elves_of_Rivendell.md) of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) during the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), affectionately known to his contemporaries in Rivendell as Figwit.<sup id="cite_ref-Decipher_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Decipher-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Participation in the Council of Elrond {#Participation_in_the_Council_of_Elrond}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_-_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Figwit.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/5/52/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_-_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Figwit.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="193" height="248" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="193" data-file-height="248"/></a><figcaption>Figwit&#39;s appearance at the Council of Elrond in The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</figcaption></figure>

When [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md), the Lord of Rivendell, invited representatives of factions of [the Free peoples of Middle-earth](Free_peoples~19934.md) to a Council which aimed to answer the growing threat of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md), Figwit attended. He sat in between another [Elf](../E/Elves.md) and [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Council_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Council erupted into chaos as its members disagreed on what to do with [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md). Figwit stood up and began yelling. He and the Elf next to him argued with [Járnsmid](../J/Járnsmid.md) and [Hugin](../H/Hugin.md) of [Dale](../D/Dale.md). The Council's furious argument raged on until [Frodo Baggins](Frodo_Baggins.md), the [Shire-hobbit](../S/Shire-hobbits.md) who brought the Ring to Rivendell, volunteered to take the Ring to Mordor and destroy it. Figwit watched as eight others offered to join Frodo on his quest. He sat back down and watched Lord Elrond dub Frodo and his eight companions "[The Fellowship of the Ring](Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-Council_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Travelling to the Grey Havens {#Travelling_to_the_Grey_Havens}

Later, Elrond formed a [wandering company](../W/Wandering_Companies.md) of [Elves](../E/Elves.md). He ordered them to set out for [the Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) with [Arwen Undómiel](../A/Arwen.md), his daughter. Elrond ordered them to take her by the safest road and leave for [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) on the ship anchored at the Havens. On the road, Arwen had a vision of [her son](../E/Eldarion.md), should she remain in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) with Aragorn. Brought to tears, she halted. Figwit told her "Lady Arwen, we cannot delay". Arwen turned her horse around and rode back to Rivendell. In confusion, Figwit asked "My lady?".<sup id="cite_ref-Vision_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vision-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Fandom {#Fandom}

Figwit's rise to fame began shortly after the first movie, and some fans believe that he was given dialog in the third movie because [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) was aware of the attention given to this extra. Jackson mentions the phenomenon in the commentary track on the [extended edition of the *Return of the King*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>):

> *The decision to give him a speaking role was developed after the scene was scripted. Originally just a random cast extra was to give the lines, but it was decided that it would be fun if the Figwit actor was brought in to deliver them.*

In 2004, a 50-minute documentary on the Figwit phenomenon, <a class="external text" href="http://www.enzedff.co.nz/filmsynopsis.asp?FilmID" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">= 1802&amp;Archive = 0&amp;RegionID = 1&amp;EventID = 8 <i>&#34;Frodo is great... Who is that?!!&#34;</i></a>, was made by three [Wellington](../W/Wellington.md) filmmakers - Stan Alley, Nick Booth, and Hannah Clarke - all of whom worked on Peter Jackson's *The Lord of the Rings* adaptation. The documentary follows Bret McKenzie to Edinburgh where he meets the fans of his trademark pout and "elvish good looks" and features extensive interviews with fans behind various Figwit fan sites as well as [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md), <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Barry_Osbourne&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Barry Osbourne (page does not exist)">Barry Osbourne</a>, [Mark Ordesky](../M/Mark_Ordesky.md), [Ian McKellen](../I/Ian_McKellen.md), and a pre-fame <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Taika_Waititi&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Taika Waititi (page does not exist)">Taika Waititi</a>.

Figwit has also appeared in official merchandise of the movie franchise: once on an autograph card, and once on a card for [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md). Both of these cards are made by <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topps" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Topps</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-website_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-website-7">[7]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Figwit.27s_.22brother.22"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Figwit&#39;s_&#34;brother&#34;">Figwit&#39;s &#34;brother&#34;</span></h3>

Figwit has a lesser known "brother", played by extra Blair Morton. **Meorof**, also known as the "Mystery Elf On (The) Right Of Figwit", appears at the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md) sitting in the seat on Figwit's right<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name **Figwit** is a humorous acronym created by fans which stands for "[Frodo](Frodo_Baggins.md) is grea...who is THAT?!?"<sup id="cite_ref-website_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-website-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Figwit is credited in the credits of *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)* under the name **Elf Escort**, and his [Decipher Card](../D/Decipher_Card.md) gives the name **[Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md)**, a name derived from *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-website_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-website-7">[7]</a></sup>

In [fan fiction](Fanfiction.md), Figwit is sometimes called **Melpomaen**, a shoddy translation of the elements "fig" (*melpo* in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md)) and "wit" (*maen* in [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-website_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-website-7">[7]</a></sup>

Bret McKenzie's character in *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md)* is **[Lindir](../L/Lindir.md)**, who shares similarities with Figwit. Both are dark-haired Elves of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and courtiers of [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md). It is unclear if the two characters are intended to be the same, which would establish a film "canon" name for Figwit.
