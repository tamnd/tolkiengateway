**Flambard Took** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Flambard was the son of [Isembard Took](/wiki/Isembard_Took), one of [the Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took")'s younger sons. He had one son, [Adelard](/wiki/Adelard_Took "Adelard Took").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Like many other members of the [Took](/wiki/Took_Family "Took Family") clan, Flambard's name has military connections: a flambard (*flam* "flame" + *bard* "battle-axe") is a large sword with a waved or flame-shaped blade.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
