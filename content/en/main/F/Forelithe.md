**Forelithe**, or **[Lithe](/wiki/Lithe)** in [Bree](/wiki/Bree), was the sixth month of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), lying between [Thrimidge](/wiki/Thrimidge) and the midsummer [Lithedays](/wiki/Lithedays), and approximately corresponding to modern [22 May](/wiki/22_May) through [20 June](/wiki/20_June).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a [modernization](/wiki/Hobbitish "Hobbitish") of the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name for June, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%C3%A6rra_li%C3%BEa" title="wiktionary:ærra liþa">Æ&#39;rra Líþa</a></i> meaning "before lithe".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
