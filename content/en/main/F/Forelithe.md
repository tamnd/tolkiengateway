**Forelithe**, or **[Lithe](../L/Lithe.md)** in [Bree](../B/Bree.md), was the sixth month of the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md), lying between [Thrimidge](../T/Thrimidge.md) and the midsummer [Lithedays](../L/Lithedays.md), and approximately corresponding to modern [22 May](../_/22_May.md) through [20 June](../_/20_June.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a [modernization](../H/Hobbitish.md) of the [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name for June, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%C3%A6rra_li%C3%BEa" title="wiktionary:ærra liþa">Æ&#39;rra Líþa</a></i> meaning "before lithe".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
