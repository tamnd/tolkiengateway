**Fourth Age 220** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 220** or **F.O.**) is the 220th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 220 was also known as **S.R. 1641** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Some "secret societies" emerge in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) "practising [dark cults](../D/Dark_Tree.md), and '[orc-cults](../O/Orcs.md)' among adolescents".<sup id="cite_ref-letter_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-letter-1">[1]</a></sup>
- This "secret [Satanistic](../M/Morgoth.md) religion" is later discovered and overthrown in Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- About this time, [Eldarion's](../E/Eldarion.md) reign as [High King](<../H/High_King_(Dúnedain).md>) comes to an end.<sup id="cite_ref-letter_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-letter-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The New Shadow](../T/The_New_Shadow.md)*, there is only one single [dark cult](../D/Dark_Tree.md) that arose in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md). It is connected to the term [Herumor](<../H/Herumor_(The_New_Shadow).md>). Before the events of the narrative, it is mentioned that a few Shipmen and a small vessel of the [King's Ships](../K/King's_Ships.md) mysteriously disappeared.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [April](../A/April.md), [Berelach](../B/Berelach.md) visited his father [Borlas](../B/Borlas.md) at [Pen-arduin](../P/Pen-arduin.md), telling him about the recent incident with the Shipmen, believing that they were unskilled and foolish; and as such, deserved their fate.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [June](../J/June.md), Borlas quoted Berelach's tale to [Saelon](../S/Saelon.md), only for Saelon to put suspicion on Berelach's story, leading to a discussion about the nature of evil.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-3">[3]</a></sup>

Since the events of *The New Shadow* is described taking place during the reign of Eldarion and "after the death of [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md)", this dating contradicts to the second edition of *The Lord of the Rings*, where in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) the date of Aragorn's death was ultimately changed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) from [Fo.A.](Fourth_Age.md) [100](Fourth_Age_100.md) to [Fo.A.](Fourth_Age.md) [120](Fourth_Age_120.md).

The text from which the date of Eldarion's death as the year 125 derives was written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in [1959](../_/1959.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and thus predates the second edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, where Tolkien ultimately changed the date of [Aragorn's](../A/Aragorn.md) death from [Fo.A.](Fourth_Age.md) [100](Fourth_Age_100.md) to [Fo.A.](Fourth_Age.md) [120](Fourth_Age_120.md).
