**Fisher Blue** was a [Kingfisher](../K/Kingfishers.md) and one of the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Forest-folk&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Forest-folk (page does not exist)">Forest-folk</a><sup id="cite_ref-Bombadil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bombadil-1">[1]</a></sup> in the poem *[Bombadil Goes Boating](../B/Bombadil_Goes_Boating.md)*.

## History {#History}

One day Fisher Blue was flying above a river when he came across [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md) in his boat. He asked Tom to bring back tidings of the place Tom was going. Tom did not want to answer back. This angered Fisher Blue very much that he threatened to tip over Tom's boat. Not very pleased at hearing this Tom said he would cut Fisher's beak off and use it as a weather vane. After that Fisher Blue left.

Later the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Otter-folk&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Otter-folk (page does not exist)">Otter-folk</a> found Tom's boat, docked it along side the shore and loosed it with the help of Fisher Blue, [Willow-wren](../W/Willow-wren.md), and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Old_Swan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Old Swan (page does not exist)">Old Swan</a>. They waited for Tom to come and look for it, but he was already heading for home by the time they loosed his boat, and so they waited for a long time.<sup id="cite_ref-Bombadil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bombadil-1">[1]</a></sup>
