**First Age 7** (abbreviated to **F.A. 7**) is the 7th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) hold a [council in Mithrim](../C/Council_in_Mithrim.md) to discuss their relations with the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md); [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md) and [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md) have an argument.<sup id="cite_ref-year_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-year-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-return_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-return-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Fingolfin](Fingolfin.md) is chosen as the first [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), since [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) waives his claim.<sup id="cite_ref-year_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-year-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-return_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-return-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The [Sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) decide to relocate to [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-year_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-year-1">[1]</a></sup>
