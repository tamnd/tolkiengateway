**Felaróf** was the famous steed of [Eorl the Young](/wiki/Eorl_the_Young), the first [King of Rohan](/wiki/Kings_of_the_Mark "Kings of the Mark"), and to whom all of the [Mearas](/wiki/Mearas), mighty horses of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), were descendants.

## History {#History}

According to later tradition among the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), the ancestors of the Mearas were brought from the [West](/wiki/Uttermost_West "Uttermost West") by [Béma](/wiki/B%C3%A9ma).<sup id="cite_ref-eorl_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eorl-1">[1]</a></sup> Therefore, Felaróf might be a descendant of those horses according to said tradition.

[Léod](/wiki/L%C3%A9od) was a tamer of horses among the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), who captured this wild white horse while it was still a foal. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2501](/wiki/Third_Age_2501 "Third Age 2501")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> he tried to tame it himself, but when he attempted to mount it, it threw him and escaped. So Léod died, leaving a sixteen-year-old son, [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl). Eorl hunted the white horse, found it, called it "Mansbane" and demanded that it give up its freedom in payment for the death of his father. The horse agreed, and took the name that Eorl gave it: Felaróf.

It was on Felaróf that Eorl rode to the aid of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and earned a famous victory,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> one that would lead [Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward "Ruling Steward") [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion) to grant them the wide empty land of [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon), which would come to be called [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). For more than thirty years afterwards, Felaróf had the freedom of those wide grasslands, but a new invasion of [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) saw Eorl ride to battle once again. In [The Wold](/wiki/The_Wold), the far northern reach of Rohan, Eorl and Felaróf met their ends, and were laid together in a mound raised outside the gate of [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).

Felaróf was a very remarkable horse indeed. It is even recorded that he could understand the speech of [Men](/wiki/Men). He gave rise in turn to a race of wonderful horses, the [Mearas](/wiki/Mearas), who according to tradition could only be ridden by the [Lord of the Mark](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Mark), Eorl's descendants. It was from this line that [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)'s horse [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax) came, making him a descendant of Felaróf himself.<sup id="cite_ref-eorl_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eorl-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Felaróf* is a poetic word in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English): "very valiant, very strong".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
