**Flies** were tiresome flying insects. When [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") struggled northward through the glens of the [Morgai](/wiki/Morgai) in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) they encountered flies, dun or grey or black, which were marked like [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) with a [red eye](/wiki/Eye_of_Sauron "Eye of Sauron")-shaped blotch.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for "small insect, fly" is *pī*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), one of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s early conceptions of an [Elven](/wiki/Elvish "Elvish") language, the word for "fly" is *sitha*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> A [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) name for a "small fly" is *mí*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> The [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) word for "large fly" was *buzbō*, with derivatives being [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) *buspo*, [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *puspo, pupso*, and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *buðu*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
