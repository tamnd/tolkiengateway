**Flies** were tiresome flying insects. When [Frodo](Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) struggled northward through the glens of the [Morgai](../M/Morgai.md) in [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) they encountered flies, dun or grey or black, which were marked like [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) with a [red eye](../E/Eye_of_Sauron.md)-shaped blotch.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name for "small insect, fly" is *pī*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md), one of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s early conceptions of an [Elven](../E/Elvish.md) language, the word for "fly" is *sitha*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> A [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) name for a "small fly" is *mí*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> The [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) word for "large fly" was *buzbō*, with derivatives being [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) *buspo*, [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *puspo, pupso*, and [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *buðu*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
