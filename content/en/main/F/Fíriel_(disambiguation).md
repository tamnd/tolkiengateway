**Fíriel** can refer to:

- [Míriel Þerindë](/wiki/M%C3%ADriel_%C3%9Eerind%C3%AB), the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") queen whose name was changed to *Fíriel*.
- [Fíriel](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel), a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") princess.
- [Fíriel Fairbairn](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel_Fairbairn), a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits").
- [Fíriel (The Lost Road)](</wiki/F%C3%ADriel_(The_Lost_Road)>), a maiden in an early stage of the [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) myth.

- [Firiel (poem)](</wiki/Firiel_(poem)>), [1934](/wiki/1934) poem.
- [Fíriel (The Last Ship)](</wiki/F%C3%ADriel_(The_Last_Ship)>), a fictional character from the poem *[The Last Ship](/wiki/The_Last_Ship)*.
