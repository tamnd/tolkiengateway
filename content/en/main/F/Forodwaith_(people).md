The **Forodwaith** were a people of [Men](/wiki/Men) who dwelt in the far north of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), apparently dating back to the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days). Not much is known about them.

## History {#History}

The Forodwaith were noted as being a hardy people, accustomed to cold, as indeed would be necessary living in the icy lands close to [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s realm.<sup id="cite_ref-Lossoth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lossoth-1">[1]</a></sup> The land they lived in was called [Forodwaith](/wiki/Northern_Waste "Northern Waste"), after its inhabitants.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

By the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the [Lossoth](/wiki/Lossoth), a remnant of the Forodwaith, lived mostly on the great [Cape of Forochel](/wiki/Cape_of_Forochel) north-west of the [Icebay of Forochel](/wiki/Icebay_of_Forochel). However, they often set up their camps on the southern shore of the bay at the feet of the [Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains "Blue Mountains").<sup id="cite_ref-Lossoth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lossoth-1">[1]</a></sup> The region north of the [Mountains of Angmar](/wiki/Mountains_of_Angmar) and the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) was still called Forodwaith on maps of Middle-earth at the end of the Third Age.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Forodwaith* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which means "Northmen".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> It is a combination of the element *[forod](/wiki/Forod "Forod")*<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> ("north")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and the element *[gwaith](/wiki/Gwaith "Gwaith")*<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> ("people" associated by place and occupation)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In some drafts about [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine), Tolkien uses the word *Forodwaith* to refer to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikings" title="wikipedia:Vikings">Vikings</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
