**Frogmorton** was a village in the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It stood on the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road), between the [Three-Farthing Stone](/wiki/Three-Farthing_Stone) (fourteen miles to the west) and the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge) (twenty-two miles to the east). Immediately to the north of the village, the stream known as [The Water](/wiki/The_Water) broke into two, creating a wide watery region.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Until the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the village seems to have been most notable for its inn, [The Floating Log](/wiki/The_Floating_Log). During the brief time when [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) took over the Shire, though, it became the home of the [First Eastfarthing Troop](/wiki/First_Eastfarthing_Troop) of Shirriffs, and it was here that the returning [Travellers](/wiki/Travellers) were "arrested" on [1 November](/wiki/1_November) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and brought to the [Shirriff-house](/wiki/Shirriff-house) of the village on their journey back to [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is analysed as *frog* + *moor* ("marshy land") and *ton* ("town, village")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> obviously referring to a marshy land at the division of [The Water](/wiki/The_Water). Based on this, [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) has suggested a speculative [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) etymology from \**Froggameretun* meaning "town by the frog-pool".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

There is a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frogmore" title="wikipedia:Frogmore">Frogmore</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckinghamshire" title="wikipedia:Buckinghamshire">Buckinghamshire</a>, which contains an estate (and burial ground) of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Royal_Family" title="wikipedia:British Royal Family">British Royal Family</a>.
