**Ferdibrand Took** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Ferdibrand was the son of [Ferdinand Took](/wiki/Ferdinand_Took). He was present at [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") in September [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is composed of *ferdi* "peace" and *brand* "sword"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Wikipedia gives the etymology *frith* "protection"/ *farð* "journey" and *nanth*
