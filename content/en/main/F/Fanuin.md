**Fanuin** was an [Ainu](/wiki/Ainur "Ainur") representing Year, who visited Valinor together with his brothers. He made for [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) an invisible rope that bound both the Sun and the Moon.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
