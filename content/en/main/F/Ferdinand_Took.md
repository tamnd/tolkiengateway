**Ferdinand Took** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Ferdinand was the son of [Sigismond Took](../S/Sigismond_Took.md). He was the father of [Ferdibrand](Ferdibrand_Took.md). They both attended [Bilbo Baggins'](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) [Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md) in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1401](../T/Third_Age_3001.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is composed of *ferdi* "peace" + *nand* "daring, brave".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
