**Ferdinand Took** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Ferdinand was the son of [Sigismond Took](/wiki/Sigismond_Took). He was the father of [Ferdibrand](/wiki/Ferdibrand_Took "Ferdibrand Took"). They both attended [Bilbo Baggins'](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is composed of *ferdi* "peace" + *nand* "daring, brave".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
