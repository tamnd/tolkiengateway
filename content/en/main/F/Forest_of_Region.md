The **Forest of Region** was a forest, primarily made up of [holly](/wiki/Holly "Holly"). Early in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the [Tatyar](/wiki/Tatyar) rested in its woods.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Populated by [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar), Region formed the southern and greater part of the Kingdom of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath). It was bounded by the rivers [Aros](/wiki/Aros) and [Esgalduin](/wiki/Esgalduin), south of [Dor Dínen](/wiki/Dor_D%C3%ADnen) and the [Girdle of Melian](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian). [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth) lay in its forest.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Region* is given as [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The base is the word <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Reg&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Reg (page does not exist)">reg</a></i> ("holly tree"), with perhaps the toponymical ending *[-ion](/wiki/-nd "-nd")* (\*"holly-land") or a plural genitive ending *[-ion](/wiki/-ion)* (as in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)), having the meaning \*"(Land) of Hollies".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Note that the name is unrelated to the English word "region (of land)", and thus is to be pronounced with a hard *g*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
