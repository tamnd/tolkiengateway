The **Forest of Region** was a forest, primarily made up of [holly](../H/Holly.md). Early in the [First Age](First_Age.md), the [Tatyar](../T/Tatyar.md) rested in its woods.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Populated by [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), Region formed the southern and greater part of the Kingdom of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md). It was bounded by the rivers [Aros](../A/Aros.md) and [Esgalduin](../E/Esgalduin.md), south of [Dor Dínen](../D/Dor_Dínen.md) and the [Girdle of Melian](../G/Girdle_of_Melian.md). [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md) lay in its forest.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Region* is given as [Doriathrin](../D/Doriathrin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The base is the word <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Reg&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Reg (page does not exist)">reg</a></i> ("holly tree"), with perhaps the toponymical ending *[-ion](../_/-nd.md)* (\*"holly-land") or a plural genitive ending *[-ion](../_/-ion.md)* (as in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md)), having the meaning \*"(Land) of Hollies".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Note that the name is unrelated to the English word "region (of land)", and thus is to be pronounced with a hard *g*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
