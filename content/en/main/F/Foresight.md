**Foresight** ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") <i><b>apacen</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) is a gift or power apparently given to picked [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Men](/wiki/Men). Many elves appear to have varying amounts of foresight, while some of the more noble men ([Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans)/[Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain)) appear to have degrees of foresight on special occasions. In turn, the Númenóreans credited the Drúedain with a "strange foresight" .

Elven mothers would give their children a name, known as *[essi apacenye](/wiki/Amiless%C3%AB "Amilessë")* or just *apacenye*, having insight into the characters and abilities of their children, and many also had the gift of prophetic foresight.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

There are several mentions of *[Seers](/wiki/Seers)* or *Prophets* namely [Amnon](/wiki/Amnon), [Glirhuin](/wiki/Glirhuin) and [Malbeth](/wiki/Malbeth). Foresight was also seen in dreams: [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) saw prophetic dreams during his adventure,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) and [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) saw a puzzling dream, prompting Boromir to travel to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to seek counsel.<sup id="cite_ref-Countil_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Countil-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Notable cases {#Notable_cases}

- Although not clearly stated, [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) was probably moved by foreknowledge to preserve the [light](/wiki/Light "Light") of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees), creating the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> This was later celebrated by [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) as providential foresight.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
- The building of [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth) before the return of [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor) to Middle-earth was done out of [Melian](/wiki/Melian)'s intense foresight "after the manner of the [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar)".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
- In his last moments, Fëanor "with his last sight he beheld far off the peaks of [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim), mightiest of the towers of Middle-earth, and knew with the foreknowledge of death that no power of the Noldor would ever overthrow them".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
- After being inquired by his sister [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) stated nothing would remain of his kingdom to be inherited by a child.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
- [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) foretold [Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l) that if he chased his runaway wife and child, he would not come back to [Nan Elmoth](/wiki/Nan_Elmoth).<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-10">[10]</a></sup>
- Eöl cursed his son [Maeglin](/wiki/Maeglin) to die in the same way as him.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-10">[10]</a></sup>
- After the coming of [Men](/wiki/Men) in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), Melian told Galadriel that [a man](/wiki/Beren "Beren"), probably of the [house of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor "House of Bëor") would be able to cross the [Girdle of Melian](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian) moved by a doom greater than her power, "and the songs that shall spring from that coming shall endure when all Middle-earth is changed".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
- [Huor](/wiki/Huor)'s last words to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad) foretold the birth of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil): "*This I say to you, lord, with the eyes of death: though we part here for ever, and I shall not look on your [white walls](/wiki/Gondolin "Gondolin") again, from you and from me a new star will arise*".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
- Before dying, [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor) had the foresight that if [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) managed to save [Finduilas](/wiki/Finduilas), she would save him from his [úmarth](/wiki/%C3%9Amarth "Úmarth"). Túrin, however, came too late, and the loss of Finduilas enabled him to meet [Níniel](/wiki/N%C3%ADniel) much later, and follow his dark fate.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>
- [Idril](/wiki/Idril) felt a dark shadow upon her heart in the last years of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), which moved her to build a [secret tunnel](/wiki/Idril%27s_secret_way "Idril's secret way") as a way to escape in case of siege.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
- On [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain) were noted for their "strange foresight"; already since the time of [Aldarion](/wiki/Aldarion) they felt the land "unstable under their feet", millennia before the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor), and gradually abandoned it to return to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). By the time of the Downfall, they all had left the island.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>
- [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) foretold [King](/wiki/King_of_Gondor "King of Gondor") [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur) that the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) would not fall by the hand of man.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>
- [Dírhael](/wiki/D%C3%ADrhael) and his wife [Ivorwen](/wiki/Ivorwen), grandparents of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), were gifted in foresight and had premonitions about [Arathorn II](/wiki/Arathorn_II) who wanted to marry [their daughter](/wiki/Gilraen "Gilraen"). These premonitions were a reason at first to oppose the marriage, but later to concede to it.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> Ivorwen also saw a vision of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Elessar" title="Elessar">Elessar</a> on the chest of baby Aragorn.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>While Arwen is on her way to the <a href="/wiki/Grey_Havens" title="Grey Havens">Grey Havens</a> to leave Middle-earth and Aragorn, she has a <i>vision</i> of her <a href="/wiki/Eldarion" title="Eldarion">future son</a> which persuades her to return to <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>. Then she asks her father, who has the gift of foresight to tell her what he saw about her; he replies he saw only death, but she replies that there is also life.</dd></dl>
