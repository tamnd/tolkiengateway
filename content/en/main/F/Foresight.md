**Foresight** ([Q.](../Q/Quenya.md) <i><b>apacen</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) is a gift or power apparently given to picked [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md). Many elves appear to have varying amounts of foresight, while some of the more noble men ([Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md)/[Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md)) appear to have degrees of foresight on special occasions. In turn, the Númenóreans credited the Drúedain with a "strange foresight" .

Elven mothers would give their children a name, known as *[essi apacenye](../A/Amilessë.md)* or just *apacenye*, having insight into the characters and abilities of their children, and many also had the gift of prophetic foresight.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

There are several mentions of *[Seers](../S/Seers.md)* or *Prophets* namely [Amnon](../A/Amnon.md), [Glirhuin](../G/Glirhuin.md) and [Malbeth](../M/Malbeth.md). Foresight was also seen in dreams: [Frodo Baggins](Frodo_Baggins.md) saw prophetic dreams during his adventure,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) and [Faramir](Faramir.md) saw a puzzling dream, prompting Boromir to travel to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to seek counsel.<sup id="cite_ref-Countil_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Countil-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Notable cases {#Notable_cases}

- Although not clearly stated, [Fëanor](Fëanor.md) was probably moved by foreknowledge to preserve the [light](../L/Light.md) of the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md), creating the [Silmarils](../S/Silmarils.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> This was later celebrated by [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md) as providential foresight.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
- The building of [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md) before the return of [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md) to Middle-earth was done out of [Melian](../M/Melian.md)'s intense foresight "after the manner of the [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
- In his last moments, Fëanor "with his last sight he beheld far off the peaks of [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md), mightiest of the towers of Middle-earth, and knew with the foreknowledge of death that no power of the Noldor would ever overthrow them".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
- After being inquired by his sister [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md), [Finrod](Finrod.md) stated nothing would remain of his kingdom to be inherited by a child.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
- [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md) foretold [Eöl](../E/Eöl.md) that if he chased his runaway wife and child, he would not come back to [Nan Elmoth](../N/Nan_Elmoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-10">[10]</a></sup>
- Eöl cursed his son [Maeglin](../M/Maeglin.md) to die in the same way as him.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-10">[10]</a></sup>
- After the coming of [Men](../M/Men.md) in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), Melian told Galadriel that [a man](../B/Beren.md), probably of the [house of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md) would be able to cross the [Girdle of Melian](../G/Girdle_of_Melian.md) moved by a doom greater than her power, "and the songs that shall spring from that coming shall endure when all Middle-earth is changed".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
- [Huor](../H/Huor.md)'s last words to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md) foretold the birth of [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md): "*This I say to you, lord, with the eyes of death: though we part here for ever, and I shall not look on your [white walls](../G/Gondolin.md) again, from you and from me a new star will arise*".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
- Before dying, [Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md) had the foresight that if [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) managed to save [Finduilas](Finduilas.md), she would save him from his [úmarth](../_/Úmarth.md). Túrin, however, came too late, and the loss of Finduilas enabled him to meet [Níniel](../N/Níniel.md) much later, and follow his dark fate.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>
- [Idril](../I/Idril.md) felt a dark shadow upon her heart in the last years of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), which moved her to build a [secret tunnel](../I/Idril's_secret_way.md) as a way to escape in case of siege.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
- On [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), the [Drúedain](../D/Drúedain.md) were noted for their "strange foresight"; already since the time of [Aldarion](../A/Aldarion.md) they felt the land "unstable under their feet", millennia before the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md), and gradually abandoned it to return to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). By the time of the Downfall, they all had left the island.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>
- [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) foretold [King](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md) that the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) would not fall by the hand of man.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>
- [Dírhael](../D/Dírhael.md) and his wife [Ivorwen](../I/Ivorwen.md), grandparents of [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), were gifted in foresight and had premonitions about [Arathorn II](../A/Arathorn_II.md) who wanted to marry [their daughter](../G/Gilraen.md). These premonitions were a reason at first to oppose the marriage, but later to concede to it.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> Ivorwen also saw a vision of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../E/Elessar.md" title="Elessar">Elessar</a> on the chest of baby Aragorn.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>
