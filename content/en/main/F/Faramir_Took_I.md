**Faramir Took I** was the thirty-third [Thain](/wiki/Thain) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), the twentieth of the [Took](/wiki/Took_Family "Took Family") line.

## History {#History}

Faramir was the son of [Thain Peregrin Took I](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") and [Diamond of Long Cleeve](/wiki/Diamond_of_Long_Cleeve).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He was named after [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), the [Prince of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien) in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) far South, a good friend of his father. In [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1463](/wiki/Fourth_Age_42 "Fourth Age 42") he married [Goldilocks Gardner](/wiki/Goldilocks_Gardner), the third daughter of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee). He succeeded his father when Peregrin relocated to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1484](/wiki/Fourth_Age_63 "Fourth Age 63").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Faramir I was named after [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), who [Pippin Took](/wiki/Pippin_Took), Faramir I's father, saved from being burnt to death alongside his despairing father, [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor), during the [Siege of Gondor](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor).
