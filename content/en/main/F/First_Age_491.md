**First Age 491** (abbreviated to **F.A. 491**) is the 491st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- After three years of captivity, [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) escapes from his thralldom under [Lorgan](../L/Lorgan.md) the [Easterling](../E/Easterling.md). Tuor hides in the caves of [Androth](../A/Androth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
