**Francis de Wolff** (1913 - 1984) was a British actor. He played in many Hammer Horror films, including <i><a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0051542/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Corridors of Blood</a></i>, in which he played half of a comic duo (the other half was [Christopher Lee](../C/Christopher_Lee.md)). De Wolff was, like Lee, often cast as an antagonist, and he voiced [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) in the [1968 radio series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.
