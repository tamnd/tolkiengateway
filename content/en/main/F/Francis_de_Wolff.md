**Francis de Wolff** (1913 - 1984) was a British actor. He played in many Hammer Horror films, including <i><a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0051542/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Corridors of Blood</a></i>, in which he played half of a comic duo (the other half was [Christopher Lee](/wiki/Christopher_Lee)). De Wolff was, like Lee, often cast as an antagonist, and he voiced [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) in the [1968 radio series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)") of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.
