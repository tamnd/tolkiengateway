**Forostar** was the northern promontory of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), a land of rocks, cliffs and high moors with woods of fir and larch.<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-1">[1]</a></sup> It stretched some two hundred miles from its southern borders with the [Mittalmar](../M/Mittalmar.md) to its [North Cape](../N/North_Cape.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The great road from [Rómenna](../R/Rómenna.md) to [Andúnië](../A/Andúnië.md) passed through its southern parts, at [Ondosto](../O/Ondosto.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-1">[1]</a></sup>

The location known as [Tompollë](../T/Tompollë.md) was also there.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The lands farther north, toward the Cape, became rockier and more mountainous. In the far north was a region around the peak of [Sorontil](../S/Sorontil.md), where the landscape fell sheer into the sea forming tremendous cliffs. This was the abode of [Eagles](../E/Eagles.md), and also the dwelling-place of [Meneldur Elentirmo](../T/Tar-Meneldur.md), heir to Númenor's throne and a watcher of the stars, who built a great tower here to follow the movements of the heavens.<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-1">[1]</a></sup>
