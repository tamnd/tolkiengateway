The **Fairbairn family** was the family name of the descendants of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), through his eldest daughter [Elanor](../E/Elanor_Gardner.md) and her husband [Fastred of Greenholm](Fastred_of_Greenholm.md), because her elvish beauty was long inherited by her descendants.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup>

Fastred became the first [Warden of Westmarch](../W/Warden_of_Westmarch.md), a title handed down to his son [Elfstan Fairbairn](../E/Elfstan_Fairbairn.md) and his descendants. The family seat was at [Undertowers](../U/Undertowers.md) on the [Tower Hills](../T/Tower_Hills.md) (moved there in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1462](Fourth_Age_41.md)), so that the family are often referred to as the 'Fairbairns of the Towers'.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Fairbairns were also the heirs of the [Red Book of Westmarch](../R/Red_Book_of_Westmarch.md) to which they made additions and also created copies.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
