The **Fairbairn family** was the family name of the descendants of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), through his eldest daughter [Elanor](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner "Elanor Gardner") and her husband [Fastred of Greenholm](/wiki/Fastred_of_Greenholm), because her elvish beauty was long inherited by her descendants.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup>

Fastred became the first [Warden of Westmarch](/wiki/Warden_of_Westmarch), a title handed down to his son [Elfstan Fairbairn](/wiki/Elfstan_Fairbairn) and his descendants. The family seat was at [Undertowers](/wiki/Undertowers) on the [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills) (moved there in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1462](/wiki/Fourth_Age_41 "Fourth Age 41")), so that the family are often referred to as the 'Fairbairns of the Towers'.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Fairbairns were also the heirs of the [Red Book of Westmarch](/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch) to which they made additions and also created copies.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
