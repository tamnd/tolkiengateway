**Forlindon** was a name for the northern part of [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md), north of the [Gulf of Lune](../G/Gulf_of_Lune.md) and west of the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md). The land was traversed by a river which ended on the northern shore of the Gulf; the haven of [Forlond](Forlond.md) was built there, on its mouths.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Off its north-western coasts stood the island of [Himring](../H/Himring.md), where [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md)'s fortress had once stood.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Prior to the [Great Battle](../G/Great_Battle.md) the land of Lindon had been part of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md); afterwards the remnant was called Lindon, split into Forlindon and [Harlindon](../H/Harlindon.md) by the newly created Gulf of Lune.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Gil-Galad](../G/Gil-Galad~20494.md) lived in Forlindon in the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Forlindon* likely means "North Lindon",<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> apparently derived from *forn* ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md); "right, north") + *[lindon](../L/Lindon.md#Etymology)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
