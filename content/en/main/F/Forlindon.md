**Forlindon** was a name for the northern part of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), north of the [Gulf of Lune](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune) and west of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains). The land was traversed by a river which ended on the northern shore of the Gulf; the haven of [Forlond](/wiki/Forlond) was built there, on its mouths.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Off its north-western coasts stood the island of [Himring](/wiki/Himring), where [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros)'s fortress had once stood.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Prior to the [Great Battle](/wiki/Great_Battle) the land of Lindon had been part of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand); afterwards the remnant was called Lindon, split into Forlindon and [Harlindon](/wiki/Harlindon) by the newly created Gulf of Lune.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Gil-Galad](/wiki/Gil-Galad) lived in Forlindon in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Forlindon* likely means "North Lindon",<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> apparently derived from *forn* ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin); "right, north") + *[lindon](/wiki/Lindon#Etymology "Lindon")*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
