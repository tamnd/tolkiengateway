**Fourth Age 34** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 34** or **F.O.**) is the 34th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 34 was also known as **S.R. 1455** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:==[Mid-year's Day](../M/Mid-year's_Day.md)==

- [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) is elected [Mayor of Michel Delving](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md) for the fifth time.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Within the Year {#Within_the_Year}

- Death of [Imrahil](../I/Imrahil.md), [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Elphir](../E/Elphir.md), son of Imrahil, becomes the twenty-third [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) gives this as the year when [Fastred of Greenholm](Fastred_of_Greenholm.md) became [Warden of Westmarch](../W/Warden_of_Westmarch.md) and relocated to the [Undertowers](../U/Undertowers.md). In the [50th Anniversary Edition](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md>) this event happened in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1462](Fourth_Age_41.md). <span></span>
