<i><b>Fifty New Poems for Children: An Anthology Selected from Books Recently Published by Basil Blackwell</b></i> is a book which includes [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[Goblin Feet](/wiki/Goblin_Feet)" (erroneously attributed to "J.R.R. [Tolkein](/wiki/Tolkein)"), which had previously been published in *[Oxford Poetry 1915](/wiki/Oxford_Poetry "Oxford Poetry")*. It also contains the poem "Romance" by E. F. A. Geach, which [Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson) has suggested as a possible inspiration for "[The Road Goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(song)> "The Road Goes Ever On (song)")," various versions of which are included in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-AH_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AH-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Publication history {#Publication_history}

Although the book does not give a publication date, 1922 has been assumed.<sup id="cite_ref-AH_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AH-1">[1]</a></sup> However, [Pieter Collier](/wiki/Pieter_Collier) has dated it as [1924](/wiki/1924).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It contains pieces published in 1922 and a copy with a January 1923 dated inscription has been seen. The British Library catalogues also list it as 1922, and the book was advertised in the *Asiatic Review* (April 1922).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

It was reprinted in [1924](/wiki/1924) and [1929](/wiki/1929), and American editions have been seen published by Bretano’s (undated) and Appleton &amp; Co. (1924).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
