<i><b>fëa</b></i> (pl. <i><b>fëar</b></i>) is the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word for "soul, indwelling <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../S/Spirit.md" title="Spirit">spirit</a> of an incarnate being".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The union of *fëa* and *[hröa](../H/Hröa.md)* ("body") was the essential characteristic of the [Incarnates](../I/Incarnates.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<br> The *fëar* were sent into [Eä](../E/Eä.md) directly by [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md); and unlike the *hröa*, they are indestructible and unable to be dominated by any force within Eä, unless they give consent.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) equivalent is <i><b><a href="Fae.md" title="Fae">fae</a></b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-MR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-4">[4]</a></sup>

The word *fëar* is only used in reference to souls whose natural state is to be incarnated; those [spirits](../S/Spirits.md) that do not require a *hröa* in order to be complete are called *[ëalar](../_/Ëalar.md)* ("beings").<sup id="cite_ref-MR_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-4">[4]</a></sup>

*Fëar* were sent to inhabit the [Ents](../E/Ents.md).<sup id="cite_ref-L247_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L247-5">[5]</a></sup> [Unbodied](../U/Unbodied.md) *fëar*, could be controlled by necromancy<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> (See also: [undead](../U/Undead.md)).

Animals,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and according to some lore beings like the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), were soulless (did not possess *fea*).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
