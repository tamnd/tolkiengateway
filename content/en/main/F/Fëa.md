<i><b>fëa</b></i> (pl. <i><b>fëar</b></i>) is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for "soul, indwelling <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Spirit" title="Spirit">spirit</a> of an incarnate being".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The union of *fëa* and *[hröa](/wiki/Hr%C3%B6a "Hröa")* ("body") was the essential characteristic of the [Incarnates](/wiki/Incarnates).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<br> The *fëar* were sent into [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4) directly by [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar); and unlike the *hröa*, they are indestructible and unable to be dominated by any force within Eä, unless they give consent.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) equivalent is <i><b><a href="/wiki/Fae" title="Fae">fae</a></b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-MR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-4">[4]</a></sup>

The word *fëar* is only used in reference to souls whose natural state is to be incarnated; those [spirits](/wiki/Spirits "Spirits") that do not require a *hröa* in order to be complete are called *[ëalar](/wiki/%C3%8Balar "Ëalar")* ("beings").<sup id="cite_ref-MR_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-4">[4]</a></sup>

*Fëar* were sent to inhabit the [Ents](/wiki/Ents).<sup id="cite_ref-L247_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L247-5">[5]</a></sup> [Unbodied](/wiki/Unbodied) *fëar*, could be controlled by necromancy<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> (See also: [undead](/wiki/Undead "Undead")).

Animals,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and according to some lore beings like the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), were soulless (did not possess *fea*).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
