**First Age 503** (abbreviated to **F.A. 503**) is the 503rd [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Notable events in this year include:

## Within this Year {#Within_this_Year}

- [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) is killed by the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> who steal the [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr).<sup id="cite_ref-L247_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L247-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-3">[2]</a></sup> In grief, [Melian](/wiki/Melian) vanishes to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) and thus withdraws [her Girdle](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian "Girdle of Melian"), leaving [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) unprotected to the enemies.<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>
- The Dwarves are pursued by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves_of_Doriath "Elves of Doriath") and killed except two of them, who return to [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod). The Nauglamír is brought back to [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth).<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>
- The [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Nogrod "Dwarves of Nogrod") of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) invade Doriath and attack Menegroth, defeating the Elves in a [battle](/wiki/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves "Battle in the Thousand Caves") within the [Thousand Caves](/wiki/Thousand_Caves).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup> [Mablung](/wiki/Mablung) is slain, the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) are scattered, and the Dwarves take the [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr).<sup id="cite_ref-L247_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L247-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>
- Aided by some [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves) and the [Ents](/wiki/Ents),<sup id="cite_ref-L247_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L247-2">[1]</a></sup> [Beren](/wiki/Beren) defeats the Dwarven host at the [Battle of Sarn Athrad](/wiki/Battle_of_Sarn_Athrad).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 2]</a></sup> He then returns to [Tol Galen](/wiki/Tol_Galen) with the Nauglamír for [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) to wear it.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing) in [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>
- Accompanied by his family, [Dior](/wiki/Dior) removes to Doriath to reign over it as [its King](/wiki/King_of_Doriath "King of Doriath").<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Spring {#Spring}

- Birth of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup>

## Autumn {#Autumn}

- [Final death](/wiki/Gift_of_Men "Gift of Men") of Beren and Lúthien. King Dior receives the Nauglamír.<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>
