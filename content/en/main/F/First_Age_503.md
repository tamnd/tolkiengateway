**First Age 503** (abbreviated to **F.A. 503**) is the 503rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Within this Year {#Within_this_Year}

- [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) is killed by the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> who steal the [Nauglamír](../N/Nauglamír.md).<sup id="cite_ref-L247_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L247-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-3">[2]</a></sup> In grief, [Melian](../M/Melian.md) vanishes to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) and thus withdraws [her Girdle](../G/Girdle_of_Melian.md), leaving [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) unprotected to the enemies.<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>
- The Dwarves are pursued by the [Elves](../E/Elves_of_Doriath.md) and killed except two of them, who return to [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md). The Nauglamír is brought back to [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>
- The [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves_of_Nogrod.md) of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) invade Doriath and attack Menegroth, defeating the Elves in a [battle](../B/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves.md) within the [Thousand Caves](../T/Thousand_Caves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup> [Mablung](../M/Mablung.md) is slain, the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) are scattered, and the Dwarves take the [Nauglamír](../N/Nauglamír.md).<sup id="cite_ref-L247_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L247-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>
- Aided by some [Green-elves](../G/Green-elves.md) and the [Ents](../E/Ents.md),<sup id="cite_ref-L247_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L247-2">[1]</a></sup> [Beren](../B/Beren.md) defeats the Dwarven host at the [Battle of Sarn Athrad](../B/Battle_of_Sarn_Athrad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 2]</a></sup> He then returns to [Tol Galen](../T/Tol_Galen.md) with the Nauglamír for [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) to wear it.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Elwing](../E/Elwing.md) in [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>
- Accompanied by his family, [Dior](../D/Dior.md) removes to Doriath to reign over it as [its King](../K/King_of_Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Spring {#Spring}

- Birth of [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md) in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup>

## Autumn {#Autumn}

- [Final death](../G/Gift_of_Men.md) of Beren and Lúthien. King Dior receives the Nauglamír.<sup id="cite_ref-Years_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_5-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>
