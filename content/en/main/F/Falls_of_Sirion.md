The **Falls of Sirion** were the mighty thundering falls of the River [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md). Formed at the southernmost end of the [Aelin-uial](../A/Aelin-uial.md), its base drained to flow under the hills of the [Andram](../A/Andram.md) for three leagues before emerging again at the [Gates of Sirion](../G/Gates_of_Sirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
