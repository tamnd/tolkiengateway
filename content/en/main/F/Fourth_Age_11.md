**Fourth Age 11** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 11** or **F.O.**) is the 11th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 11 was also known as **S.R. 1432** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Hamfast Gardner](../H/Hamfast_Gardner.md), seventh child and fourth son of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Rose Cotton](../R/Rose_Cotton.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Saradoc Brandybuck](../S/Saradoc_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md), son of Saradoc and [Esmeralda Took](../E/Esmeralda_Took.md), becomes the [Master of Buckland](../M/Master_of_Buckland.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-2">[2]</a></sup> Great gifts are sent to him by [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) and the Lady [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) of [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
