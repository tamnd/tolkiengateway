**Fëanorian lamps** were lamps which emitted a blue light from a flame within a white crystal. They are described as "crystals hung in a fine chain net, the crystals being ever shining with an inner blue radiance". The lamps were created in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), and the flame could not be extinguished by wind or water. The [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) were famous for the flames, but unfortunately the craft was lost with them. [Gelmir](<../G/Gelmir_(messenger_of_Círdan).md>) possessed one of these lamps when he met [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Some of the Noldor who had been captured by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) and put to work in his mines had Fëanorian lamps with them. [Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md) was one of these enslaved Elves, and when he escaped he took with him one of these lamps.<sup id="cite_ref-CH9_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CH9-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the [Elves of Lothlórien](../G/Galadhrim.md) used similar devices: small lamps that gave out slender silver beams.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The lamps were part of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) from very early stages, but [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) did not mention them in the published *[Silmarillion](../S/Silmarillion.md)*. For example, in one version of the story, [Túrin Turambar](../T/Túrin_Turambar.md) recognised the face of [Beleg](../B/Beleg.md) whom he had slain by the light of Gwindor's lamp;<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> yet in the version included in the *Silmarillion*, it was lightning which revealed Beleg's face to Túrin.<sup id="cite_ref-CH9_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CH9-2">[2]</a></sup>

A detailed description of the nature of the lamps is described in the early *[Lay of the Children of Húrin](../L/Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md)*, when Beleg finds Gwindor in the forest of [Taur-nu-Fuin](<../T/Taur-nu-Fuin_(Dorthonion).md>) after seeing his lamp shining in the distance:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>But little lanterns   of lucent crystal<br/>
and silver cold   with subtlest cunning<br/>
they strangely fashioned,   and steadfast a flame<br/>
burnt unblinking   there blue and pale,<br/>
unquenched for ever.   The craft that lit them<br/>
was the jewel-makers&#39;   most jealous secret.<br/>
Not Morgoth&#39;s might,   nor meed nor torment<br/>
them vowed, availed   to reveal that lore;<br/>
yet lights and lamps   of living radiance,<br/>
many and magical,   they made for him.<br/>
No dark could dim them   the deeps wandering;<br/>
whose lode they lit   was lost seldom<br/>
in groundless grot,   or gulfs far under.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="../T/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md" title="The Lay of the Children of Húrin">The Lay of the Children of Húrin</a></i>, II. Beleg, vv. 787-799</cite></p>
</blockquote>

This scene of Beleg finding Gwindor with one of these lamps was painted by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and published in *[Pictures by J.R.R. Tolkien](../P/Pictures_by_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* (1979), [no. 37](../../file/J/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Fangorn_Forest.jpg.md).
