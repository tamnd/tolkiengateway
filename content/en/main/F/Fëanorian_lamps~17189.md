**Fëanorian lamps** were lamps which emitted a blue light from a flame within a white crystal. They are described as "crystals hung in a fine chain net, the crystals being ever shining with an inner blue radiance". The lamps were created in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), and the flame could not be extinguished by wind or water. The [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) were famous for the flames, but unfortunately the craft was lost with them. [Gelmir](</wiki/Gelmir_(messenger_of_C%C3%ADrdan)> "Gelmir (messenger of Círdan)") possessed one of these lamps when he met [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Some of the Noldor who had been captured by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) and put to work in his mines had Fëanorian lamps with them. [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor) was one of these enslaved Elves, and when he escaped he took with him one of these lamps.<sup id="cite_ref-CH9_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CH9-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the [Elves of Lothlórien](/wiki/Galadhrim "Galadhrim") used similar devices: small lamps that gave out slender silver beams.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The lamps were part of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") from very early stages, but [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) did not mention them in the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)*. For example, in one version of the story, [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_Turambar) recognised the face of [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) whom he had slain by the light of Gwindor's lamp;<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> yet in the version included in the *Silmarillion*, it was lightning which revealed Beleg's face to Túrin.<sup id="cite_ref-CH9_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CH9-2">[2]</a></sup>

A detailed description of the nature of the lamps is described in the early *[Lay of the Children of Húrin](/wiki/Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*, when Beleg finds Gwindor in the forest of [Taur-nu-Fuin](</wiki/Taur-nu-Fuin_(Dorthonion)> "Taur-nu-Fuin (Dorthonion)") after seeing his lamp shining in the distance:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>But little lanterns   of lucent crystal<br/>
and silver cold   with subtlest cunning<br/>
they strangely fashioned,   and steadfast a flame<br/>
burnt unblinking   there blue and pale,<br/>
unquenched for ever.   The craft that lit them<br/>
was the jewel-makers&#39;   most jealous secret.<br/>
Not Morgoth&#39;s might,   nor meed nor torment<br/>
them vowed, availed   to reveal that lore;<br/>
yet lights and lamps   of living radiance,<br/>
many and magical,   they made for him.<br/>
No dark could dim them   the deeps wandering;<br/>
whose lode they lit   was lost seldom<br/>
in groundless grot,   or gulfs far under.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin" title="The Lay of the Children of Húrin">The Lay of the Children of Húrin</a></i>, II. Beleg, vv. 787-799</cite></p>
</blockquote>
