"**Free Land**" was the political designation of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) from [Fo.A.](Fourth_Age.md) [6](Fourth_Age_6.md) ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1427](Fourth_Age_6.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-App-Later_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

Following the re-establishment of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) by King [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-RK-Field_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-Field-2">[2]</a></sup> the King declared that the Shire was to become a Free Land under the protection of the [Northern Sceptre](../S/Sceptre_of_Annúminas.md). Furthermore, he declared that no [Men](../M/Men.md) are to enter the Shire - a policy he even upheld himself.<sup id="cite_ref-App-Later_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>
