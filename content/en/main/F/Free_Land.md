"**Free Land**" was the political designation of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") from [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [6](/wiki/Fourth_Age_6 "Fourth Age 6") ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1427](/wiki/Fourth_Age_6 "Fourth Age 6")).<sup id="cite_ref-App-Later_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

Following the re-establishment of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) by King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-RK-Field_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-Field-2">[2]</a></sup> the King declared that the Shire was to become a Free Land under the protection of the [Northern Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre_of_Ann%C3%BAminas "Sceptre of Annúminas"). Furthermore, he declared that no [Men](/wiki/Men) are to enter the Shire - a policy he even upheld himself.<sup id="cite_ref-App-Later_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>
