**Fimbul** was a [Orc](../O/Orcs.md) who was part of [Azog's](../A/Azog.md) pack. Fimbul was invented by [Warner Bros.](../W/Warner_Bros..md) for [*The Hobbit* film series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>), where he was portrayed by [Stephen Ure](../S/Stephen_Ure.md), who also portrayed [Grinnah](../G/Grinnah.md).

## History {#History}

When Azog had [Yazneg](../Y/Yazneg.md) killed for his failure to catch [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) and his [Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) as they headed toward [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), Fimbul became Azog's new lieutenant.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Once [Bolg](../B/Bolg.md) took over the leadership of the pack when Azog was summoned back to [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) by the [Necromancer](../S/Sauron.md), Fimbul continued to serve as lieutenant. When the pack finally caught up to the Company again in [Lake-town](../L/Lake-town.md), he was the one who notified Bolg that Thorin and most of the Company had already gone. Almost immediately after that, a slain Orc fell into the boat that Fimbul was sitting in at that moment, launching him into the air, where he was then decapitated by [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), who happened to be on the balcony of [Bard's](../B/Bard.md) house at that very moment.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

His name is old norse for "great" or "mighty".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It could also be a reference to [Golfimbul](../G/Golfimbul.md).
