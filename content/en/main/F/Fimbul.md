**Fimbul** was a [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") who was part of [Azog's](/wiki/Azog "Azog") pack. Fimbul was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for [*The Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), where he was portrayed by [Stephen Ure](/wiki/Stephen_Ure), who also portrayed [Grinnah](/wiki/Grinnah).

## History {#History}

When Azog had [Yazneg](/wiki/Yazneg) killed for his failure to catch [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) and his [Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") as they headed toward [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), Fimbul became Azog's new lieutenant.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Once [Bolg](/wiki/Bolg) took over the leadership of the pack when Azog was summoned back to [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) by the [Necromancer](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron"), Fimbul continued to serve as lieutenant. When the pack finally caught up to the Company again in [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town), he was the one who notified Bolg that Thorin and most of the Company had already gone. Almost immediately after that, a slain Orc fell into the boat that Fimbul was sitting in at that moment, launching him into the air, where he was then decapitated by [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), who happened to be on the balcony of [Bard's](/wiki/Bard "Bard") house at that very moment.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

His name is old norse for "great" or "mighty".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It could also be a reference to [Golfimbul](/wiki/Golfimbul).
