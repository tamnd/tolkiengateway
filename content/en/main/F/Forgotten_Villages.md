The **forgotten villages** were the settlements of the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) after they had migrated westward across the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) but before they had settled [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

When Greenwood the Great became darkened and was renamed [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) the Hobbits who had lived in the upper vales of [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) crossed the Misty Mountains into [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md). The [Harfoots](../H/Harfoots.md) moved in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1050](../T/Third_Age_1050.md) while the [Fallohides](Fallohides.md) and [Stoors](../S/Stoors.md) made the journey c. [1150](../T/Third_Age_1150.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-1">[1]</a></sup> The Harfoots settled between [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md) and the mountains, the Stoors dwelt between [Tharbad](../T/Tharbad.md) and [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md), and the Fallohides mingled with the others (often becoming leaders among the Harfoot and Stoor clans).<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-2">[2]</a></sup>

Around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1300](../T/Third_Age_1300.md) the realm of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) arose and the Hobbits began moving west again, with many settling in [Bree](../B/Bree.md). Finally, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1601](../T/Third_Age_1601.md), King [Argeleb II](../A/Argeleb_II.md) granted the Hobbits land beyond the [Baranduin](../B/Baranduin.md) when the majority of them moved and established the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-1">[1]</a></sup> By the time of [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) all of the earlier settlements had vanished and been forgotten, except for those Hobbits still living in Bree and its environs.<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Note {#Note}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) never referred to the "Forgotten Villages" by name; they were given this identification in *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](../T/The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
