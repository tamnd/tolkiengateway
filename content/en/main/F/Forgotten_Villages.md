The **forgotten villages** were the settlements of the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) after they had migrated westward across the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) but before they had settled [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

When Greenwood the Great became darkened and was renamed [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) the Hobbits who had lived in the upper vales of [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) crossed the Misty Mountains into [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador). The [Harfoots](/wiki/Harfoots) moved in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1050](/wiki/Third_Age_1050 "Third Age 1050") while the [Fallohides](/wiki/Fallohides) and [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors) made the journey c. [1150](/wiki/Third_Age_1150 "Third Age 1150").<sup id="cite_ref-Great_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-1">[1]</a></sup> The Harfoots settled between [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop) and the mountains, the Stoors dwelt between [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad) and [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland), and the Fallohides mingled with the others (often becoming leaders among the Harfoot and Stoor clans).<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-2">[2]</a></sup>

Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300") the realm of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) arose and the Hobbits began moving west again, with many settling in [Bree](/wiki/Bree). Finally, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1601](/wiki/Third_Age_1601 "Third Age 1601"), King [Argeleb II](/wiki/Argeleb_II) granted the Hobbits land beyond the [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin) when the majority of them moved and established the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-1">[1]</a></sup> By the time of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) all of the earlier settlements had vanished and been forgotten, except for those Hobbits still living in Bree and its environs.<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Note {#Note}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) never referred to the "Forgotten Villages" by name; they were given this identification in *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
