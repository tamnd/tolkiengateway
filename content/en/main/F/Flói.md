**Flói** was a [dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") of [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain").

## History {#History}

Flói was a member of [Balin's folk](/wiki/Balin%27s_folk) who tried to reclaim [Moria](/wiki/Moria) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2989](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989"). When they reached the [Mirrormere](/wiki/Mirrormere), there was a great battle. Flói was valorous and killed the great chieftain, but was killed by a [Goblin](/wiki/Goblin) arrow.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Although against the Dwarven traditions,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> he was buried under the grass near the Mirrormere. His death was recorded in the [Book of Mazarbul](/wiki/Book_of_Mazarbul).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) suggests that the name *Flói* could be connected with the Old Norse verb *flóa* ("to boil", "to flood") and that the Old Norse word *Flói* means "marshy moor", "bay" or "large firth".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
