**Forfalas** was the northern half of the region of [Falas](/wiki/Falas), located south of [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast), and northwest of [Eglamar](</wiki/Eglamar_(Falas)> "Eglamar (Falas)").<sup id="cite_ref-For_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-For-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Bel_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bel-2">[2]</a></sup>

The region of Forfalas was marked on *[the Second 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_Second_%27Silmarillion%27_Map "The Second 'Silmarillion' Map")*,<sup id="cite_ref-For_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-For-1">[1]</a></sup> which formed the basis for the [map of Beleriand](/wiki/Map_of_Beleriand_and_the_Lands_to_the_North "Map of Beleriand and the Lands to the North") in the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

However, apart from being labeled on the aforementioned map, Forfalas was never mentioned again, nor did it appear in any of the narratives, annals or essays set in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-Bel_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bel-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Forfalas* is in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), meaning "North [Falas](/wiki/Falas)". It is a combination of the prefixal form <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Forn_(Sindarin_word)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Forn (Sindarin word) (page does not exist)">for-</a></i> ("north") + *[falas](/wiki/Falas#Etymology "Falas")* ("shore").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
