**Fourth Age 27** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 27** or **F.O.**) is the 27th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 27 was also known as **S.R. 1448** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:
