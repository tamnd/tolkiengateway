**Fuiyáru** was a deadly nightshade that grew about the [courts of Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos "Halls of Mandos") in the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. It is said to have been particularly abundant before the doors of [Fui](/wiki/Nienna "Nienna").<sup id="cite_ref-PE15_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE15-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Fuiyáro* is [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), likely a literal translation of "nightshade". Its composition probably relates to the elements *Fui*/*hui* ("fog, dark, murk, night") + *yaru*/*yara* ("gloom, blight"). The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name of the flower was <i><b>Fuior</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-PE15_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE15-1">[1]</a></sup>
