**Farewell to Lórien** is the eighth chapter of the second book in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.

## Summary {#Summary}

[Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) gave the [Company](Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) boats in order to float down the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md). She gave each of them a [gift](../G/Gifts_of_Galadriel.md), in order to help them on their way. She gave [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) a [sheath](../A/Andúril's_sheath.md) made to fit his sword [Andúril](../A/Andúril.md) and a silver broach that held a clear green stone, the [Elfstone](../E/Elfstone.md) of the house of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md). To [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md), she gave a [belt](../B/Boromir's_belt.md) of gold. To [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) she gave a [bow](../B/Bow_of_the_Galadhrim.md) strung with elf-hair and a quiver of arrows. To [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Peregrin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), she gave silver [belts](../M/Merry_and_Pippin's_belts.md) with golden clasps. To Sam she gave neither something to keep him on the road nor to defend himself; instead she gave him a [box](../S/Sam's_garden_box.md) of earth from her gardens, so that if he returned to his home in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), his garden would bloom like none other. When she asked [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) what he wanted of her. After much modest refusal, he asked for a strand of [her hair](../H/Hair_of_Galadriel.md). She gave him three, as well as a blessing that his hands would ever flow with gold, though it would have no dominion over his mind. To [Frodo](Frodo_Baggins.md) she bequeathed a small [crystal vial](../P/Phial_of_Galadriel.md) in which was caught the [light](../L/Light.md) of the [Star of Eärendil](../S/Star_of_Eärendil.md), amid the waters of a fountain. It would be a light for him in dark places and make the night brighter.

The Company then left on their way down the river, and Galadriel followed them for a while, singing them a [song](../G/Galadriel's_Song.md). They had chosen a difficult, but a valiant path.

## Composition {#Composition}

Initially Tolkien considered that [Time](../T/Time.md) was "frozen" for the Fellowship while staying in Lórien, and it was still [January](../J/January.md) when they departed it. Much later Tolkien decided that Time still passed, and set the departure on [14 February](../_/14_February.md), and some days later Frodo observes the full [moon](../M/Moon.md) of [8 March](../_/8_March.md) in *[The Forbidden Pool](../T/The_Forbidden_Pool.md)*. However much later Tolkien introduced the [Shire Reckoning](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md), in which both [January](../J/January.md) and [February](February.md) have 30 days (instead of 31 and 28). He changed the date of the chapter to [15](../_/15_February.md), and then [16 February](../_/16_February.md), in order to still have Frodo observe the full moon on 8 March.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
