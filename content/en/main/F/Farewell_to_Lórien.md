**Farewell to Lórien** is the eighth chapter of the second book in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.

## Summary {#Summary}

[Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) gave the [Company](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") boats in order to float down the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). She gave each of them a [gift](/wiki/Gifts_of_Galadriel "Gifts of Galadriel"), in order to help them on their way. She gave [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) a [sheath](/wiki/And%C3%BAril%27s_sheath "Andúril's sheath") made to fit his sword [Andúril](/wiki/And%C3%BAril) and a silver broach that held a clear green stone, the [Elfstone](/wiki/Elfstone) of the house of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil). To [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir), she gave a [belt](/wiki/Boromir%27s_belt "Boromir's belt") of gold. To [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) she gave a [bow](/wiki/Bow_of_the_Galadhrim "Bow of the Galadhrim") strung with elf-hair and a quiver of arrows. To [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Peregrin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), she gave silver [belts](/wiki/Merry_and_Pippin%27s_belts "Merry and Pippin's belts") with golden clasps. To Sam she gave neither something to keep him on the road nor to defend himself; instead she gave him a [box](/wiki/Sam%27s_garden_box "Sam's garden box") of earth from her gardens, so that if he returned to his home in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), his garden would bloom like none other. When she asked [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) what he wanted of her. After much modest refusal, he asked for a strand of [her hair](/wiki/Hair_of_Galadriel "Hair of Galadriel"). She gave him three, as well as a blessing that his hands would ever flow with gold, though it would have no dominion over his mind. To [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") she bequeathed a small [crystal vial](/wiki/Phial_of_Galadriel "Phial of Galadriel") in which was caught the [light](/wiki/Light "Light") of the [Star of Eärendil](/wiki/Star_of_E%C3%A4rendil), amid the waters of a fountain. It would be a light for him in dark places and make the night brighter.

The Company then left on their way down the river, and Galadriel followed them for a while, singing them a [song](/wiki/Galadriel%27s_Song "Galadriel's Song"). They had chosen a difficult, but a valiant path.

## Composition {#Composition}

Initially Tolkien considered that [Time](/wiki/Time) was "frozen" for the Fellowship while staying in Lórien, and it was still [January](/wiki/January) when they departed it. Much later Tolkien decided that Time still passed, and set the departure on [14 February](/wiki/14_February), and some days later Frodo observes the full [moon](/wiki/Moon "Moon") of [8 March](/wiki/8_March) in *[The Forbidden Pool](/wiki/The_Forbidden_Pool)*. However much later Tolkien introduced the [Shire Reckoning](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning), in which both [January](/wiki/January) and [February](/wiki/February) have 30 days (instead of 31 and 28). He changed the date of the chapter to [15](/wiki/15_February "15 February"), and then [16 February](/wiki/16_February), in order to still have Frodo observe the full moon on 8 March.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
