The **Fens of Sirion** were the fens and marshes that formed around the lakes of [Aelin-uial](/wiki/Aelin-uial) on the River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Beren](/wiki/Beren) passed through the Fens when he departed from [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) on the beginning of his quest to win a [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
