The **Fens of Sirion** were the fens and marshes that formed around the lakes of [Aelin-uial](../A/Aelin-uial.md) on the River [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Beren](../B/Beren.md) passed through the Fens when he departed from [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) on the beginning of his quest to win a [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
