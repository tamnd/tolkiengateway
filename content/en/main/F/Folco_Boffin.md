**Folco Boffin** (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1378](/wiki/Third_Age_2978 "Third Age 2978")) was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-Boffin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Boffin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Folco was the son of [Vigo Boffin](/wiki/Vigo_Boffin) and one of the guests at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-Boffin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Boffin-1">[1]</a></sup>

He was a friend of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and in [September](/wiki/September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") he stayed in [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) assisting in Frodo's move to [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) by packing and loading carts. He stayed to celebrate [Frodo's birthday](/wiki/22_September "22 September") with [Merry](/wiki/Merry), [Pippin](/wiki/Pippin) and [Fatty Bolger](/wiki/Fatty_Bolger). [The next day](/wiki/23_September "23 September")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> they loaded the last of the luggage leaving for [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow), and after lunch he left for his home.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
