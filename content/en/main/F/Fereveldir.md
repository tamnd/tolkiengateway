**Fereveldir**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) Archer who fought in [the Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md).

Fereveldir is an original creation for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* film. He is portrayed by [Ben Britton](../B/Ben_Britton.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Fereveldir was a [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) of [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md). He was the son of [Thandronen](../T/Thandronen.md) and the twin brother of [Ferevellon](Ferevellon.md). Together with Ferevellon, he patrolled the borders of Lothlórien.

When the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md), under leadership from [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) [King](../K/King_of_Rohan.md), retreated to [the Hornburg](../H/Hornburg.md) in [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md), [Saruman the White](../S/Saruman.md) sent an army of over ten thousand [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) to assail them. When Lord [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and Lady [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and Lord [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) of Lothlórien heard of this, they decided to honour the ages-old allegiance between [Men](../M/Men.md) and [Elves](../E/Elves.md). They sent a join force of Elves, led by [Haldir](../H/Haldir.md), to assist the Rohirrim in their battle against the Uruks. [Thandronen](../T/Thandronen.md), [Ferevellon](Ferevellon.md) and Fereveldir were part of this force sent to the Hornburg.

Fereveldir was stationed on the front line of the Deeping Wall, to the left of [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md). The battle commenced and Fereveldir fired arrows at the Uruk-hai.

Ultimately, Haldir and the rest of the Elven reinforcements were slain during the Battle. Their efforts were not in vain, however, as the few Rohirrim left alive were able to hold out until [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) arrived with [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md)'s [Éored](../_/Éored.md) and forced the Uruks to retreat into the forest, where they were killed by [Huorns](../H/Huorns.md).
