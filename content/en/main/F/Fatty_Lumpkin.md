**Fatty Lumpkin** was a [pony](../P/Ponies.md) kept by [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md).

## History {#History}

Fatty Lumpkin was rarely ridden by Tom and spent much of his time roaming free on the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md). While [Frodo](Frodo_Baggins.md) and his friends stayed at Tom's house, Fatty became friends with their ponies, who Tom later named [Sharp-ears](../S/Sharp-ears.md), [Bumpkin](../B/Bumpkin.md), [Wise-nose](../W/Wise-nose.md), [Swish-tail](../S/Swish-tail.md), and [White-socks](../W/White-socks.md).

After encountering a [Barrow-wight](../B/Barrow-wights.md), the hobbits' ponies became so frightened that they ran off to find Fatty. Tom called them all back with the names he had given them, and he rode on Fatty Lumpkin alongside the hobbits and their ponies until reaching the borders of his country.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the hobbits' ponies escaped from [Bree](../B/Bree.md), they ran out again in the Downs to find Fatty. They stayed with Tom for a while, until he had heard news of the events at Bree.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) notes that *-kin* is in this name used as a diminutive suffix.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Pop Culture {#Pop_Culture}

In Pinky and the Brain episode Brainania, Pinky and the Brain travel on a ship named "SS Fatty Lumpkin."
