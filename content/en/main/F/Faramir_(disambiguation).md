**Faramir** can refer to the following:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Gelmir](<../G/Gelmir_(messenger_of_Círdan).md>), a [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) [Elf](../E/Elves.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md), who was named *Faramir* in a plot-synopsis of the *[Narn](../N/Narn.md)*.
- [Faramir](<Faramir_(son_of_Ondoher).md>) (died [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md)), second son of the [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), slain in the [Battle of the Camp](../B/Battle_of_the_Camp.md).
- **[Faramir](Faramir.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2983](../T/Third_Age_2983.md) - [Fo.A.](Fourth_Age.md) [82](Fourth_Age_82.md)), son of [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) and younger brother of [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md), a hero of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).**
- [Faramir Took I](Faramir_Took_I.md) (born [Fo.A.](Fourth_Age.md) [9](Fourth_Age_9.md)), son of [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) who succeeded his father as [Thain](../T/Thain.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).
