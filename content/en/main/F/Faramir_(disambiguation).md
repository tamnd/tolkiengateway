**Faramir** can refer to the following:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Gelmir](</wiki/Gelmir_(messenger_of_C%C3%ADrdan)> "Gelmir (messenger of Círdan)"), a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), who was named *Faramir* in a plot-synopsis of the *[Narn](/wiki/Narn)*.
- [Faramir](</wiki/Faramir_(son_of_Ondoher)> "Faramir (son of Ondoher)") (died [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944")), second son of the [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), slain in the [Battle of the Camp](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Camp).
- **[Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2983](/wiki/Third_Age_2983 "Third Age 2983") - [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [82](/wiki/Fourth_Age_82 "Fourth Age 82")), son of [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) and younger brother of [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir), a hero of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).**
- [Faramir Took I](/wiki/Faramir_Took_I) (born [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [9](/wiki/Fourth_Age_9 "Fourth Age 9")), son of [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) who succeeded his father as [Thain](/wiki/Thain) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").
