**First Age 502** (abbreviated to **F.A. 502**) is the 502nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Second Union of Elves and Men](../U/Unions_of_Elves_and_Men.md) occurs in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), where [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) weds [Idril](../I/Idril.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) comes to [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) and slays [Mîm](../M/Mîm.md) the [petty-dwarf](../P/Petty-dwarves.md) in revenge for [his son's](../T/Túrin.md) death. He then seizes the treasure of [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) and takes the [Nauglamír](../N/Nauglamír.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Hurin_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hurin-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Arriving to [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md), Húrin casts the Nauglamír at [Thingol's](../T/Thingol.md) feet. Realising from [Melian](../M/Melian.md) that he has been acting evilly due to [Morgoth's](../M/Morgoth.md) curse, Húrin leaves [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) and throws himself into the [Sea](../B/Belegaer.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Hurin_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hurin-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- Thingol commissions the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves_of_Nogrod.md) of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) to embed the [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md) into the Nauglamír.<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-3">[3]</a></sup>
