**First Age 502** (abbreviated to **F.A. 502**) is the 502nd [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Second Union of Elves and Men](/wiki/Unions_of_Elves_and_Men "Unions of Elves and Men") occurs in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), where [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) weds [Idril](/wiki/Idril).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) comes to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) and slays [Mîm](/wiki/M%C3%AEm) the [petty-dwarf](/wiki/Petty-dwarves "Petty-dwarves") in revenge for [his son's](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin") death. He then seizes the treasure of [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) and takes the [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr).<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Hurin_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hurin-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Arriving to [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth), Húrin casts the Nauglamír at [Thingol's](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") feet. Realising from [Melian](/wiki/Melian) that he has been acting evilly due to [Morgoth's](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") curse, Húrin leaves [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) and throws himself into the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer").<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Hurin_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hurin-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- Thingol commissions the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Nogrod "Dwarves of Nogrod") of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) to embed the [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) into the Nauglamír.<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-3">[3]</a></sup>
