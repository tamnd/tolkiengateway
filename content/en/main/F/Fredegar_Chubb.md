**Fredegar Chubb** is a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") who appears in the [Extended Edition](</wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_(extended_edition)> "The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey (extended edition)") of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey).* [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) visited the market outside of the [Green Dragon](/wiki/Green_Dragon) Inn shortly after [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)'s visit and bought a fish from Fredegar while there. He was portrayed by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Eric_Vespe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Eric Vespe (page does not exist)">Eric Vespe</a>.
