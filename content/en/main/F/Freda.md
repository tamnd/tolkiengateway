**Freda** is a [girl](/wiki/Rohirrim "Rohirrim") of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) who was sent to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) with her brother. Freda was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), where she was portrayed by [Olivia Tennet](/wiki/Olivia_Tennet).

## History {#History}

Freda and her older brother [Éothain](</wiki/%C3%89othain_(film_character)> "Éothain (film character)") were sent by their mother [Morwen](</wiki/Morwen_(film_character)> "Morwen (film character)") to alert [King Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden "Théoden") that the [Wild Men](/wiki/Dunlendings "Dunlendings") were pillaging through [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). Éothain and Freda were kept safe at [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) until Théoden led his people to [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep), where they were reunited with their mother.
