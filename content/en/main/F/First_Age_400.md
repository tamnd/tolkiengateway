**First Age 400** (abbreviated to **F.A. 400**) is the 400th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Whilst [Eöl](../E/Eöl.md) is in the mountains attending a midsummer <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Feast&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Feast (page does not exist)">Feast</a><sup id="cite_ref-journeys_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-journeys-1">[1]</a></sup> with the [dwarves](../D/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains.md), [Aredhel](../A/Aredhel.md) and [Maeglin](../M/Maeglin.md) escape [Nan Elmoth](../N/Nan_Elmoth.md) and go to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). They are secretly followed by [Eöl](../E/Eöl.md) who is then given the choice to either stay in Gondolin or to die. Eöl attempts to kill Maeglin but Aredhel jumps in front of the dart. Death of Aredhel.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Eöl](../E/Eöl.md) is cast off the [Caragdûr](../C/Caragdûr.md) for killing Aredhel. Death of Eöl.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- *Possible* birth of [Ulfang](../U/Ulfang.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 1]</a></sup>
