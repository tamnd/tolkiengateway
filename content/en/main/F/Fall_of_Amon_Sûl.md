The **Fall of Amon Sûl** was a siege that took place in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1409](../T/Third_Age_1409.md).

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

In 1409 the Witch-King of Angmar led a great host across the river [Hoarwell](../H/Hoarwell.md) against the land of [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md), after being beaten back out of the lands of the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) many years before.

### Siege {#Siege}

When the forces of Angmar reached Weathertop they besieged it, until they broke through. The attackers subsequently burned, then razed the fortress to the ground. King Arveleg I was killed, but some of the Dúnedain retreated and escaped with the *[Palantír](../P/Palantír.md)* that was stationed there.

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

Amon Sûl was the main defensive outpost in the [Weather Hills](../W/Weather_Hills.md) and northern Cardolan. With it gone the Witch-King was able to take and ravage Cardolan, as well as consolidate his new conquests in [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md), which his <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hill-Men&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hill-Men (page does not exist)">Hill-Men</a> allies and subjects had taken, as well as launch attacks against the whole of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md).

However despite permanently ending the threat that was Cardolan, Angmar was unable to ride its momentum, and was driven away from the city of [Fornost](Fornost.md) and the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md) by the forces of the new King [Araphor](../A/Araphor.md) and Lord [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) of [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md).
