The **Fall of Amon Sûl** was a siege that took place in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1409](/wiki/Third_Age_1409 "Third Age 1409").

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

In 1409 the Witch-King of Angmar led a great host across the river [Hoarwell](/wiki/Hoarwell) against the land of [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan), after being beaten back out of the lands of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) many years before.

### Siege {#Siege}

When the forces of Angmar reached Weathertop they besieged it, until they broke through. The attackers subsequently burned, then razed the fortress to the ground. King Arveleg I was killed, but some of the Dúnedain retreated and escaped with the *[Palantír](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADr)* that was stationed there.

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

Amon Sûl was the main defensive outpost in the [Weather Hills](/wiki/Weather_Hills) and northern Cardolan. With it gone the Witch-King was able to take and ravage Cardolan, as well as consolidate his new conquests in [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur), which his <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hill-Men&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hill-Men (page does not exist)">Hill-Men</a> allies and subjects had taken, as well as launch attacks against the whole of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain).

However despite permanently ending the threat that was Cardolan, Angmar was unable to ride its momentum, and was driven away from the city of [Fornost](/wiki/Fornost) and the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs) by the forces of the new King [Araphor](/wiki/Araphor) and Lord [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon).
