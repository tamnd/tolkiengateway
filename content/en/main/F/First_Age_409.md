**First Age 409** (abbreviated to **F.A. 409**) is the 409th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Brandir](<../B/Brandir_(son_of_Arachon).md>), son of [Arachon](../A/Arachon.md) and [Bregil](../B/Bregil.md), uncle to [Brandir the Lame](../B/Brandir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- "[Athrabeth Finrod ah Andreth](../A/Athrabeth_Finrod_ah_Andreth.md)", the Debate of [Finrod](Finrod.md) and [Andreth](../A/Andreth.md), takes place.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
