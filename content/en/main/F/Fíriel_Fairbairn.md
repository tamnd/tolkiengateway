**Fíriel Fairbairn** was a Hobbit of the [Westmarch](/wiki/Westmarch) of the [Shire](/wiki/Shire).

## History {#History}

Fíriel was the daughter of [Fastred of Greenholm](/wiki/Fastred_of_Greenholm) and [Elanor Gardner](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner) and flourished in the first century of the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-Preface_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Preface-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fíriel* was likely named after [Fíriel](</wiki/F%C3%ADriel_(The_Last_Ship)> "Fíriel (The Last Ship)"), the character from the song *[The Last Ship](/wiki/The_Last_Ship)* and/or [the Gondorian princess](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel "Fíriel") she was based on. The name means "mortal woman" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Preface_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Preface-1">[1]</a></sup>
