**Fluithuin** was an [ogress](/wiki/Ogres "Ogres") according to the early version of the [Legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

[Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium> "Gothmog (balrog)") is described as the son of [Melko](/wiki/Melko) and the ogress Fluithuin or Ulbandi.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Both 'ogresses' and the idea that the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) had [children](/wiki/Valarindi "Valarindi") were discarded by Tolkien fairly early on.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Both the [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name *Fluithuin* and the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) *Ulbandi* are of unclear meaning, although the element *ulban(d)* means "monster".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
