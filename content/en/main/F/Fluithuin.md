**Fluithuin** was an [ogress](../O/Ogres.md) according to the early version of the [Legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

[Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium>) is described as the son of [Melko](../M/Melko.md) and the ogress Fluithuin or Ulbandi.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Both 'ogresses' and the idea that the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) had [children](../V/Valarindi.md) were discarded by Tolkien fairly early on.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Both the [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) name *Fluithuin* and the [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) *Ulbandi* are of unclear meaning, although the element *ulban(d)* means "monster".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
