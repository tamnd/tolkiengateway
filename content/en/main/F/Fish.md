**Fish** were common throughout the bodies of water in [Arda](/wiki/Arda).

## History {#History}

Fish formed the chief source of food in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), as it was abundant throughout the coast of the island. The fisherfolk dwelt mainly in [Nindamos](/wiki/Nindamos), built on the hards of a marshy land. [Mariners](/wiki/Guild_of_Venturers "Guild of Venturers") of Númenor were mostly drawn from the fisher folk.<sup id="cite_ref-Descr_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Descr-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_It_is_fish!.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/1/1b/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_It_is_fish%21.jpg/300px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_It_is_fish%21.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="334" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/1/1b/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_It_is_fish%21.jpg/450px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_It_is_fish%21.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/1/1b/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_It_is_fish%21.jpg/600px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_It_is_fish%21.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1280" data-file-height="1423"/></a><figcaption><i>It is fish!</i> by <a href="/wiki/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil" title="Matěj Čadil">Matěj Čadil</a></figcaption></figure>

When the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) travelled to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), they encountered the [primitive tribes](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Pre-Númenóreans") there. Though some of them, along the mouths of [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond) and [Gwathló](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3), had crude fisheries, they had fallen out of use as its people fled towards the mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors) that dwelt in the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin) also had fish as their main food. Two of them, [Déagol](/wiki/D%C3%A9agol) and [Sméagol](/wiki/Sm%C3%A9agol), were fishing when they found [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), lost in the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-5">[5]</a></sup>

The [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians), much like their forefathers, ate fish. The [Ethir](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin "Ethir Anduin") was populated by fisher-folk, who often went out to [sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") on boats,<sup id="cite_ref-MT_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-6">[6]</a></sup> because fishing was the most profitable business.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Though [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) did not generally hunt or fish for pleasure,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> they did angle for food now and then. One of the favourite spots for anglers was near *[The Golden Perch](/wiki/The_Golden_Perch)*, which took its name for the golden fish that swam there.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for "fish" is <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lingw%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lingwë (page does not exist)">lingwë</a></i> (Quenya also has a word for "(small) fish", <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hala&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hala (page does not exist)">hala</a></i>). The [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) name, derived from the same [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") is <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lhimb&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lhimb (page does not exist)">lhimb</a></i> or *lhim*, and [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin) has <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=L%C3%ADw&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Líw (page does not exist)">líw</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), one of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s early conceptions of an [Elven](/wiki/Elvish "Elvish") language, "fish" is *ing*, and "young of fish, small fry" is *igli*.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> A [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) name for "little fish" (also "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnow" title="wikipedia:Minnow">minnow</a>") is given as *nis*.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
