In a manuscript by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), a [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) simply labeled "**F**" accompanied [Khamûl](../K/Khamûl.md) in tracking the [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md) during the [Hunt for the Ring](../H/Hunt_for_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-name_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Like the other Nazgûl, "F"<sup id="cite_ref-name_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-2">[2]</a></sup> was one of nine [Men](../M/Men.md) to whom [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) gave one of the [Rings of Power](../N/Nine_Rings.md), who became mighty kings, sorcerers or warriors, and eventually faded into a [wraith](../W/Wraiths.md) that was under the rule of Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> By [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [2251](../S/Second_Age_2251.md) "F" and the other eight Men who had received rings of power first appeared as Ringwraiths.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2951](../T/Third_Age_2951.md), when Sauron declared himself openly to his enemies, "F" was probably<sup id="cite_ref-RC97_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC97-1">[1]</a></sup> one of the two Ringwraiths under [Khamûl](../K/Khamûl.md)'s command that were sent to [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Black_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Black-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the evening of [23 September](../_/23_September.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-7">[7]</a></sup> after the Nazgûl approach near the [Three-Farthing Stone](../T/Three-Farthing_Stone.md) in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md),<sup id="cite_ref-RC97_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC97-1">[1]</a></sup> "F"<sup id="cite_ref-name_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-2">[2]</a></sup> probably lurked south of [Whitfurrows](../W/Whitfurrows.md), watching the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) and the [Stock Road](../S/Stock_Road.md) as Khamûl arrived in [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md) and asked [Hamfast Gamgee](../H/Hamfast_Gamgee.md) about "Baggins".<sup id="cite_ref-Hunt99_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hunt99-8">[8]</a></sup>

On [25 September](../_/25_September.md), "F" was summoned by Khamûl with cries after failing to find [Frodo Baggins](Frodo_Baggins.md) on the ridge above [Woodhall](../W/Woodhall.md). Together, they ride east over the fields and visit [Farmer Maggot](Farmer_Maggot.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> After leaving Farmer Maggot, "F" was sent off by Khamûl to head down the [Causeway](../C/Causeway.md) in the direction of the [Overbourn Marshes](../O/Overbourn_Marshes.md). "F" reached the [Bucklebury Ferry](../B/Bucklebury_Ferry.md) at night soon after Khamûl arrived too late to the catch the Hobbits before they crossed the [Brandywine River](../B/Brandywine_River.md).<sup id="cite_ref-RC116_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC116-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early draft of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* labeled "Scheme D", Tolkien identified each Nazgûl by letters between A and I. It is not known which of these early draft labels correspond with "F". The Nazgûl trailing "the hobbits in the Shire" were labeled "**D**" and "<b><a class="mw-disambig" href="../E/E.md" title="E">E</a></b>" respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-treason_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-treason-11">[11]</a></sup>
