**Firienfeld** was what the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) called the flat upland of [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md); a large grassy area for the encampment of soldiers and refuge-seekers.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md) passed through the Firienfeld on [8 March](../_/8_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md); King [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) and his riders arrived there the [next day](../_/9_March.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Firien represents [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *firgen*, "mountain"; *-feld,* means "field" and [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) left it unmodernised; the Firienfield of the Index is in error.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2013: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Firienfeld sits atop the Dunharrow plateau, but has relatively little quest involvement as the player does not participate in the Muster of Rohan but rather is investigating activities of <a href="../W/White_Mountains.md" title="White Mountains">White Mountains</a> orcs in the area.</dd></dl>
