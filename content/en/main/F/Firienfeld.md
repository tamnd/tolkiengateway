**Firienfeld** was what the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) called the flat upland of [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow); a large grassy area for the encampment of soldiers and refuge-seekers.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) passed through the Firienfeld on [8 March](/wiki/8_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"); King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and his riders arrived there the [next day](/wiki/9_March "9 March").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Firien represents [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *firgen*, "mountain"; *-feld,* means "field" and [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") left it unmodernised; the Firienfield of the Index is in error.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Firienfeld sits atop the Dunharrow plateau, but has relatively little quest involvement as the player does not participate in the Muster of Rohan but rather is investigating activities of <a href="/wiki/White_Mountains" title="White Mountains">White Mountains</a> orcs in the area.</dd></dl>
