**First Age 374** (abbreviated to **F.A. 374**) is the 374th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Beren](<../B/Beren_(son_of_Belemir).md>), son of [Belemir](../B/Belemir.md) and grandfather of [Beren](../B/Beren.md) (lover of [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
