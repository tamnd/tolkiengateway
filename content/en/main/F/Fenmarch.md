The **Fenmarch**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> was a marshy border-land along the [Mering Stream](/wiki/Mering_Stream)<sup id="cite_ref-Nomenclature_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomenclature-3">[3]</a></sup> in the east of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). It stretched from the [Firien Wood](/wiki/Firienholt "Firienholt") to the confluence of the Mering Sream with the river [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash), and was therefore located along a part of Rohan's eastern border with [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fenmarch* is a name in the [language of Rohan](/wiki/Rohanese "Rohanese").<sup id="cite_ref-Nomenclature_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomenclature-3">[3]</a></sup> The first element *fen* means "marsh",<sup id="cite_ref-Nomenclature_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomenclature-3">[3]</a></sup> probably a reference to the marshlands along the [Mering Stream](/wiki/Mering_Stream). The second element *march* is an old word for "borderland",<sup id="cite_ref-Nomenclature_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomenclature-3">[3]</a></sup> probably a reference to the border with [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien). It has the same meaning as the element *mark* in [Riddermark](/wiki/Riddermark), a "borderland", especially one that serves as a defence of the inner lands of a realm.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

J.R.R. Tolkien wrote in his [Nomenclature of *The Lord of the Rings*](/wiki/Guide_to_the_Names_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "Guide to the Names in The Lord of the Rings"), which he had nearly completed by 2 January 1967 after the publication of the second edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* in October 1965, that the *Fenmarch* should have been called the **Fenmark**, but that he had retained the name *Fenmarch*, because the name *Fenmarch* had been used in the text of *The Lord of the Rings* and in the [Map of Rohan, Gondor, and Mordor](/wiki/Map_of_Rohan,_Gondor,_and_Mordor). He wrote that the name *Fenmark* should not be translated in translations of *The Lord of the Rings* into other languages.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomenclature_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomenclature-3">[3]</a></sup>

J.R.R. Tolkien already used the name *Fenmarch* in his manuscripts of what would later become the chapter *The Muster of Rohan*<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and on his draft map of Rohan, Gondor and Mordor.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
