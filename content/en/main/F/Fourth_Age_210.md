**Fourth Age 210** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 210** or **F.O.**) is the 210th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 210 was also known as **S.R. 1631** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

- Death of [Borlas's](../B/Borlas.md) wife.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-1">[1]</a></sup> In the second edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, the date of [Aragorn's](../A/Aragorn.md) death was ultimately changed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) from [Fo.A.](Fourth_Age.md) [100](Fourth_Age_100.md) (as in the first edition) to [Fo.A.](Fourth_Age.md) [120](Fourth_Age_120.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
