The **Ford of Brithiach** was an important ford of the River [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md). The crossing was in the upper part of the river's course, on the northern borders of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md) just south of where the [Dry River](../D/Dry_River.md) once flowed into the Sirion. A road led across it from [West Beleriand](../W/West_Beleriand.md) into [Dimbar](../D/Dimbar.md), and then on into the perilous valley of [Nan Dungortheb](../N/Nan_Dungortheb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Below the ford there was no means of crossing the river until it reached [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) and [Huor](../H/Huor.md) were pursued by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) to the ford where they would have perished but that [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) raised a mist from the Sirion that hid them while they crossed the Brithiach into Dimbar.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The ford is often referred to simply as "The Brithiach", a name that comes from the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) for 'gravel crossing' ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *[brith](../B/Brith.md)* "gravel" + *[iach](../I/Iach.md)* "ford"). <span></span>
