The **Ford of Brithiach** was an important ford of the River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion). The crossing was in the upper part of the river's course, on the northern borders of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) just south of where the [Dry River](/wiki/Dry_River) once flowed into the Sirion. A road led across it from [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand) into [Dimbar](/wiki/Dimbar), and then on into the perilous valley of [Nan Dungortheb](/wiki/Nan_Dungortheb).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Below the ford there was no means of crossing the river until it reached [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) and [Huor](/wiki/Huor) were pursued by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) to the ford where they would have perished but that [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) raised a mist from the Sirion that hid them while they crossed the Brithiach into Dimbar.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The ford is often referred to simply as "The Brithiach", a name that comes from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) for 'gravel crossing' ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[brith](/wiki/Brith "Brith")* "gravel" + *[iach](/wiki/Iach "Iach")* "ford"). <span></span>
