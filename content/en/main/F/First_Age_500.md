**First Age 500** (abbreviated to **F.A. 500**) is the 500th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Following the death of his children, [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) releases [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) from captivity. [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) goes to [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Hurin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hurin-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Eluréd](../E/Eluréd.md) and [Elurín](../E/Elurín.md), twin sons of [Dior](../D/Dior.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Hurin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hurin-1">[1]</a></sup>
