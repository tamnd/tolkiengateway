**Flotsam and Jetsam** is the sixty-third scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition).md>). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) stand waist-deep in water following the aftermath of the [Battle of Isengard](../B/Battle_of_Isengard.md). They watch as [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) looks hopelessly out of a window of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md) at the destruction of his work. Pippin realises he is hungry; at this moment, an apple happens to float across his path. Pippin follows a trail of apples and turkey to the storeroom of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md). The two hobbits look in wonder at the multitude of food before them, however their gaze falls on two barrels of pipe-weed; they joyously discover that they have found [Longbottom Leaf](../L/Longbottom_Leaf.md).

They debate whether they should share it with [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md) before deciding not to. Merry and Pippin then smoke by themselves in the storeroom in delight.
