The **Ford of [Carrock](/wiki/Carrock)** was, according to [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in)'s words in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), a ford that the [Beornings](/wiki/Beornings) kept open for high tolls during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Theories {#Theories}

It is not clear exactly what the Ford of Carrock is. In *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) traverse a small ford made of "huge flat stones" that connected only the [Carrock](/wiki/Carrock) with "the grass-land beyond the stream" at the eastern bank of the river. According to [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) "it is easy enough \[...\] to get from \[the eastern\] bank to the Carrock by ford, but on the other side is a cliff standing up from a swirling channel." Gandalf could not cross the river there when he was following the track of [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

This small ford remains unnamed (without capitals) in *The Hobbit* and it is not clear if the "Ford of Carrock" mentioned by Glóin in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* refers to that ford. [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) supposed that Glóin's reference should be to the [Old Ford](/wiki/Old_Ford).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
