The **Ford of [Carrock](../C/Carrock.md)** was, according to [Glóin](../G/Glóin.md)'s words in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), a ford that the [Beornings](../B/Beornings.md) kept open for high tolls during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Theories {#Theories}

It is not clear exactly what the Ford of Carrock is. In *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) traverse a small ford made of "huge flat stones" that connected only the [Carrock](../C/Carrock.md) with "the grass-land beyond the stream" at the eastern bank of the river. According to [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) "it is easy enough \[...\] to get from \[the eastern\] bank to the Carrock by ford, but on the other side is a cliff standing up from a swirling channel." Gandalf could not cross the river there when he was following the track of [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

This small ford remains unnamed (without capitals) in *The Hobbit* and it is not clear if the "Ford of Carrock" mentioned by Glóin in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* refers to that ford. [Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md) supposed that Glóin's reference should be to the [Old Ford](../O/Old_Ford.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
