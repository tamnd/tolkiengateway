**Frank Kelley Freas** ([27 August](/wiki/27_August) [1922](/wiki/1922)-[2 January](/wiki/2_January) [2005](/wiki/2005)) was an American science fiction artist.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.
