**First Age 1** (abbreviated to **F.A. 1**) is the 1st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The first rising of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) marks a new [reckoning of time](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> and the end of the [Sleep of Yavanna](../S/Sleep_of_Yavanna.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Men_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Men-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-3">[2]</a></sup> [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) and his servants cannot bear the light of the Sun.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
- The Second [Children](../C/Children_of_Ilúvatar.md) of [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md), [Men](../M/Men.md), [awake](../A/Awakening_of_Men.md) in the far east in [Hildórien](../H/Hildórien.md). Morgoth then leaves [Angband](../A/Angband.md) in secret, committing command of the [war](../B/Battles_of_Beleriand.md) to [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md); he seeks to corrupt Men.<sup id="cite_ref-Men_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Men-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-3">[2]</a></sup>
- With the first sunrise, the host of [Fingolfin](Fingolfin.md) arrives in [Mithrim](../M/Mithrim.md) after the crossing of the [Helcaraxë](../H/Helcaraxë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Return_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-6">[5]</a></sup>
- Fingolfin marches unopposed across [Dor Daedeloth](../D/Dor_Daedeloth.md) and bangs on the [Gates of Angband](../G/Gates_of_Angband.md); however, Morgoth remains hidden.<sup id="cite_ref-GA_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Return_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-6">[5]</a></sup>
