**First Age 1** (abbreviated to **F.A. 1**) is the 1st [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Notable events in this year include:

- The first rising of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) marks a new [reckoning of time](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> and the end of the [Sleep of Yavanna](/wiki/Sleep_of_Yavanna).<sup id="cite_ref-Men_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Men-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-3">[2]</a></sup> [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) and his servants cannot bear the light of the Sun.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
- The Second [Children](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar "Children of Ilúvatar") of [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar), [Men](/wiki/Men), [awake](/wiki/Awakening_of_Men "Awakening of Men") in the far east in [Hildórien](/wiki/Hild%C3%B3rien). Morgoth then leaves [Angband](/wiki/Angband) in secret, committing command of the [war](/wiki/Battles_of_Beleriand "Battles of Beleriand") to [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron); he seeks to corrupt Men.<sup id="cite_ref-Men_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Men-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-3">[2]</a></sup>
- With the first sunrise, the host of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) arrives in [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim) after the crossing of the [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Return_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-6">[5]</a></sup>
- Fingolfin marches unopposed across [Dor Daedeloth](/wiki/Dor_Daedeloth) and bangs on the [Gates of Angband](/wiki/Gates_of_Angband); however, Morgoth remains hidden.<sup id="cite_ref-GA_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Return_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-6">[5]</a></sup>
