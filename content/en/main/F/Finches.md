**Finches** was one of the many species of birds found in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). In the song *[In Western Lands beneath the Sun](../I/In_Western_Lands_beneath_the_Sun.md)* sung by [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) in the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](../T/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md), he mentions that in western lands "the merry finches sing".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

After [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md)'s death many birds were gathering around [Erebor](../E/Erebor.md). [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) saw among them "[starlings](../S/Starlings.md) and flocks of finches".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a linguistic manuscript dating from the 1930s, appears the [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) gloss *liri* ("finch").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other writings {#Other_writings}

In *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](../T/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudrún.md)*, a raven and a finch speak two of the verses,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> reminiscent of the sentient [thrushes](../T/Thrushes.md) and [ravens](../R/Ravens.md) in *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.
