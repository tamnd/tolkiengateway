**Finches** was one of the many species of birds found in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). In the song *[In Western Lands beneath the Sun](/wiki/In_Western_Lands_beneath_the_Sun)* sung by [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) in the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol), he mentions that in western lands "the merry finches sing".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

After [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug)'s death many birds were gathering around [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor). [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) saw among them "[starlings](/wiki/Starlings "Starlings") and flocks of finches".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a linguistic manuscript dating from the 1930s, appears the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) gloss *liri* ("finch").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other writings {#Other_writings}

In *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudr%C3%BAn)*, a raven and a finch speak two of the verses,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> reminiscent of the sentient [thrushes](/wiki/Thrushes "Thrushes") and [ravens](/wiki/Ravens "Ravens") in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.
