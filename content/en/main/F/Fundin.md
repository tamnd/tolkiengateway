**Fundin** was a [dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") of the royal line of [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk), the great-grandson of King [Náin II](/wiki/N%C3%A1in_II). He was the son of [Farin](/wiki/Farin) and the older brother of [Gróin](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3in).

## History {#History}

Fundin was most likely born in the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) and went into exile with his people after the attack by the [Dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug). He fathered two sons, [Balin](/wiki/Balin) and [Dwalin](/wiki/Dwalin).

Fundin was killed beneath the [East-gate of Moria](/wiki/East-gate_of_Moria) in the [Battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar), the final conflict of the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs): He was part of the first assault of the vanguard that was thrown back with loss, and was driven into a wood by lake [Mirrormere](/wiki/Mirrormere).

He was slain there along with his kinsman [Frerin](/wiki/Frerin), and many others. After the battle his body was burned on a wooden pyre along with the rest of the [dead](/wiki/Burned_Dwarves "Burned Dwarves").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Years later, his sons, as members of [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company), were involved with the [regaining of Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor "Quest of Erebor"). [Roäc](/wiki/Ro%C3%A4c) recognised Balin as the son of Fundin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fundin* is one of the dwarf names found in the *[Völuspá](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> His name means "Found".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2021: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Fundin appears in an extended flashback depicting the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar" title="Battle of Azanulbizar">Battle of Azanulbizar</a>, in which he fights alongside his sons <a href="/wiki/Balin" title="Balin">Balin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Dwalin" title="Dwalin">Dwalin</a> and the rest of <a href="/wiki/Farin" title="Farin">Farin</a>&#39;s kin. He is slain by arrows while covering the retreat of <a href="/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in" title="Thráin">Thráin</a>&#39;s vanguard.</dd></dl>
