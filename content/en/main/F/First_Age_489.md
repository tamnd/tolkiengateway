**First Age 489** (abbreviated to **F.A. 489**) is the 489th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md) escapes from [Angband](../A/Angband.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Mîm](../M/Mîm.md) betrays [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) who is taken captive by [orcs](../O/Orcs.md). [Beleg](../B/Beleg.md) pursues the orcs and encounters Gwindor on the road.<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Túrin is rescued by Beleg; Túrin mistakenly kills Beleg and takes his sword, [Anglachel](../A/Anglachel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-2">[2]</a></sup>
