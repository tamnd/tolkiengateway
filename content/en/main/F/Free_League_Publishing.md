**Free League Publishing** is a Swedish-based publisher and game creator company, founded in [2011](/wiki/2011). Since 2015, the company has published role-playing games from a list of licensed and home-grown properties.

## *The One Ring* {#The_One_Ring}

On February 11, 2021, the company launched a Kickstarter campaign for the publishing of a second edition of [*The One Ring*](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)"), of which was later delivered to supporters at the beginning of October of the same year.<sup id="cite_ref-TOR2posts_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2posts-1">[1]</a></sup> The game was subsequently released for retail purchase, on the [22nd of March](/wiki/22_March "22 March") [2022](/wiki/2022).<sup id="cite_ref-TOR2R1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2R1-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TOR2R2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2R2-3">[3]</a></sup>

## *The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying* {#The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying}

On the [29th of May](/wiki/29_May "29 May") [2023](/wiki/2023), Free League released [The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying) game, differentiated from the *The One Ring* system by utilizing the [Dungeons &amp; Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons "Dungeons and Dragons") Fifth Edition ruleset via the Open-Gaming License (OGL).<sup id="cite_ref-LOTR5E_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LOTR5E-4">[4]</a></sup>
