**Free League Publishing** is a Swedish-based publisher and game creator company, founded in [2011](../_/2011.md). Since 2015, the company has published role-playing games from a list of licensed and home-grown properties.

## *The One Ring* {#The_One_Ring}

On February 11, 2021, the company launched a Kickstarter campaign for the publishing of a second edition of [*The One Ring*](<../T/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md>), of which was later delivered to supporters at the beginning of October of the same year.<sup id="cite_ref-TOR2posts_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2posts-1">[1]</a></sup> The game was subsequently released for retail purchase, on the [22nd of March](../_/22_March.md) [2022](../_/2022.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TOR2R1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2R1-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TOR2R2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2R2-3">[3]</a></sup>

## *The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying* {#The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying}

On the [29th of May](../_/29_May.md) [2023](../_/2023.md), Free League released [The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying.md) game, differentiated from the *The One Ring* system by utilizing the [Dungeons &amp; Dragons](../D/Dungeons_and_Dragons.md) Fifth Edition ruleset via the Open-Gaming License (OGL).<sup id="cite_ref-LOTR5E_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LOTR5E-4">[4]</a></sup>
