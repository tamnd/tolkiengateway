The **Five Guardians** were a group of [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar) appointed by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) to guard the firstborn [Elves](/wiki/Elves).

## History {#History}

After [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) found the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) in [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen), the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) planned to make [War](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers "Battle of the Powers") against [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor). Oromë and [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas) had dwelt with the Elves for many years, protecting them, so when they left to make war preparations,<sup id="cite_ref-nome_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nome-1">[1]</a></sup> the Valar put a guard about Cuiviénen to protect the Elves from battle for the following two [Valian Years](/wiki/Valian_Years).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was a group of great [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar) to guard the Elves meanwhile. They were [Tarindor](/wiki/Tarindor) (later Saruman), [Olórin](/wiki/Ol%C3%B3rin) (Gandalf), [Hrávandil](/wiki/Hr%C3%A1vandil) (Radagast), [Palacendo](/wiki/Palacendo) and [Haimenar](/wiki/Haimenar). They were led by [Melian](/wiki/Melian), who had travelled to Cuiviénen before the Five Guardians; she was the sixth Maiar guardian and was the only female spirit among them.<sup id="cite_ref-nome_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nome-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

*[The Annals of Aman](/wiki/The_Annals_of_Aman)* mention the Valian guard set to protect the Elves, but its nature is not specified. The Five Guardians appear in a late and unfinished version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) while revising key dates of the [Valian Years](/wiki/Valian_Years). As the editor [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) comments, these five Maiar were apparently the later [Istari](/wiki/Istari), but the last two names are not specified,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> so it is unclear if they correspond to the earlier [Blue Wizards](/wiki/Blue_Wizards). <span></span>
