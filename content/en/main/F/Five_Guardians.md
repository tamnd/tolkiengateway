The **Five Guardians** were a group of [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md) appointed by the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) to guard the firstborn [Elves](../E/Elves.md).

## History {#History}

After [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) found the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) in [Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md), the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) planned to make [War](../B/Battle_of_the_Powers.md) against [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md). Oromë and [Tulkas](../T/Tulkas.md) had dwelt with the Elves for many years, protecting them, so when they left to make war preparations,<sup id="cite_ref-nome_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nome-1">[1]</a></sup> the Valar put a guard about Cuiviénen to protect the Elves from battle for the following two [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was a group of great [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md) to guard the Elves meanwhile. They were [Tarindor](../T/Tarindor.md) (later Saruman), [Olórin](../O/Olórin.md) (Gandalf), [Hrávandil](../H/Hrávandil.md) (Radagast), [Palacendo](../P/Palacendo.md) and [Haimenar](../H/Haimenar.md). They were led by [Melian](../M/Melian.md), who had travelled to Cuiviénen before the Five Guardians; she was the sixth Maiar guardian and was the only female spirit among them.<sup id="cite_ref-nome_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nome-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

*[The Annals of Aman](../T/The_Annals_of_Aman.md)* mention the Valian guard set to protect the Elves, but its nature is not specified. The Five Guardians appear in a late and unfinished version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) while revising key dates of the [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md). As the editor [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md) comments, these five Maiar were apparently the later [Istari](../I/Istari.md), but the last two names are not specified,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> so it is unclear if they correspond to the earlier [Blue Wizards](../B/Blue_Wizards.md). <span></span>
