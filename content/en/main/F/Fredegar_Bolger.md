**Fredegar "Fatty" Bolger** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). He was one of the close friends of [Frodo](Frodo_Baggins.md) who knew that he had the [Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) and helped him with his mission to leave the Shire.

## History {#History}

### Ancestry {#Ancestry}

Fatty was a descendant of [Hildibrand Took](../H/Hildibrand_Took.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2849](../T/Third_Age_2849.md) - [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2934](../T/Third_Age_2934.md)), one of the many sons of the [Old Took](../G/Gerontius_Took.md).<sup id="cite_ref-took_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-took-2">[2]</a></sup> He was the son of [Odovacar Bolger](../O/Odovacar_Bolger.md) and [Rosamunda Took](../R/Rosamunda_Took.md). Fatty was born in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2980](../T/Third_Age_2980.md) and his family was from [Budgeford](../B/Budgeford.md) in [Bridgefields](../B/Bridgefields.md) of the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md). He also had a younger sister, [Estella Bolger](../E/Estella_Brandybuck.md) (b. [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2985](../T/Third_Age_2985.md)), who would eventually marry [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md).

Fredegar was a good friend of [Frodo Baggins](Frodo_Baggins.md). In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3001](../T/Third_Age_3001.md) he was a guest at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-took_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-took-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Conspiracy {#Conspiracy}

Fatty was one of the [Conspirators](../C/Conspirators.md), the friends of Frodo who knew that he was in an important mission to leave the Shire, and they assisted him without his knowledge. Fatty, along with [Folco Boffin](Folco_Boffin.md) and Frodo's close circle, in [September](../S/September.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md) helped him pack and send his things to [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md), and stayed to celebrate his last [birthday](../_/22_September.md) before his departure from [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md); Fatty and Merry left [the next morning](../_/23_September.md) for the house that Frodo had bought, in order to prepare it to be ready and habitable for his arrival.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Frodo eventually came to his house, along with [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md). It was there when the conspirators revealed themselves to Frodo, and expressed their will to follow him in his mission. Fatty however was a typical hobbit who had never left the Shire and was unwilling to do so, but would play his role; he stayed behind to keep up the pretence that Frodo was there, by wearing his clothes and keep the house occupied, and deal with any "inquisitive folk".

After hearing that Frodo was pursued by the [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md), Fatty was given the option to flee with the others into the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md), but that didn't seem any more inviting to him despite Merry's attempts to persuade him that the Riders were far more terrifying than the stories about the Old Forest. Fredegar opted to remain behind as a decoy for the Nazgûl and as a messenger for [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) in case he showed up.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The Ringwraiths did eventually come in the night of [30 September](../_/30_September.md) to Crickhollow for Frodo and [the Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md). But Fatty, though frightened nearly witless, escaped to raise the alarm of [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md), causing the [Brandybucks](../B/Brandybucks.md) to sound the [Horn-call of Buckland](../H/Horn-call_of_Buckland.md). This caused the Nazgûl to flee, but they had learned that the Ring was gone.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Resistance {#Resistance}

During [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md)'s rule of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), Fatty led a group of partisans fighting against the [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) in the [Brockenbores](../B/Brockenbores.md) around the hills of [Scary](../S/Scary.md) before eventually being captured. He was imprisoned in the [Lockholes](../L/Lockholes.md) and starved, (and could no longer be called "Fatty") but was rescued after the victory of the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Havens_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Havens-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Bolger family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The name *Fredegar* contains *ferdi*, *frede* ("peace") and *gar* ("spear").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Fredegar was the name of an author of a Frankish chronicle from the seventh century, known as the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chronicle_of_Fredegar" title="wikipedia:Chronicle of Fredegar">Chronicle of Fredegar</a>.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early versions of the text (cf. *[The Return of the Shadow](../T/The_Return_of_the_Shadow.md)*), his name was **Hamilcar Bolger** and was rescued by [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) from [Black Riders](../B/Black_Riders.md) before reaching [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), narrating to the protagonists Gandalf's adventures. His larger role was later abandoned.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-8">[8]</a></sup>

His role survives as a minor anomaly in the published *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* - before the four Hobbits go into the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md), they have *six* ponies prepared (Fatty was originally supposed to go with them). The number was corrected to *five* in the [50th Anniversary Edition](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
