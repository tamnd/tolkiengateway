The **Fifth of the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md)** was never differentiated in Tolkien's works, but several adaptations did attribute individual traits to this specific Nazgûl.

[Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) constructed an elaborate non-[canonical](../C/Canon.md) backstory for [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md), identifying the character as *Akhôrahil*, a backstory which was also used in the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ICE_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ICE-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Decipher](../D/Decipher.md), for their [Trading Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md), instead limited this individualization to naming the character *[Úlairë](../N/Nazgûl.md#Other_names) Lemenya* ([Q.](../Q/Quenya.md) Fifth Nazgûl), and assigning the epithet of *Black Enemy*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

While some other adaptations also individualized the Nine, none are known to have assigned specific numbers.

## Backstory in Middle-earth Role Playing {#Backstory_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing}

**Akhôrahil** was born in [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1888&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1888 (page does not exist)">1888</a> to a wealthy family. He was a cousin of Prince Mûrazôr (the later [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md)), and was noted as brilliant but spoiled.

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1904&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1904 (page does not exist)">1904</a>, his family moved to Middle-earth to found the kingdom of [Ciryatanor](../C/Ciryatanor.md), with his father, Ciryamir, as its client-king. Akhôrahil enjoyed the authority his family held, but in his greed he sought to usurp his father's throne. In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1918&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1918 (page does not exist)">1918</a>, he made a pact with a [Haradan](../H/Haradrim.md) priest, exchanging his eyes for two magical sapphire gems known as the *Eyes of the Well*. Though now physically blind, these gems granted him magical powers. Using his new abilities, he cast a spell on his father, who was overcome with despair and took his own life.

Akhôrahil ascended to the throne of Ciryatanor in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1919&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1919 (page does not exist)">1919</a> and proclaimed himself the Storm King. He married his sister, Akhôraphil, and began a campaign of conquest, expanding his kingdom into the lands of the Haradrim and along the Bay of Ormal.

His campaigns and growing power caught the attention of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md). In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2000&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2000 (page does not exist)">2000</a>, an emissary offered Akhôrahil a [Ring of Power](../N/Nine_Rings.md), and thus he became the fifth King to fall under Sauron's sway.

Already eccentric before his transformation, the Storm King now became a recluse. His wife fled with his children, after which he purged his court of both those too loyal to the [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) as well as those who acted too independently. Still, he maintained a nominal allegiance to Númenor and kept sending tributes for 250 years.

As [Tar-Ancalimon](../T/Tar-Ancalimon.md) ascended the throne, he request all colonies to reaffirm their loyalty. His envoy arrived in Ciryatanor in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2250&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2250 (page does not exist)">2250</a>. Realizing that Númenor would seek to expand again, he held the emissary as a hostage and officially allied himself with [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). Tar-Ancalimon, after ransoming his envoy, responded by launching an invasion, which led to a crushing defeat for the Storm King in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [2280](../S/Second_Age_2280.md).

Having fled to Mordor, he would spend the next ten centuries directing the slaves of [Nurn](../N/Nurn.md) to supply food for Sauron's armies. He fled after Sauron's surrender to [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md), though after the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) he returned to Nurn, later leading its host in the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md). He was defeated with his master and passed into the shadows.

Akhôrahil reappeared around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1050](../T/Third_Age_1050.md), residing in the Far South and reestablishing his [host](../A/Army_of_the_Southern_Dragon.md) before being summoned back to Mordor by Sauron in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1640](../T/Third_Age_1640.md). He helped rebuild Sauron's strongholds and became a close companion and trusted lieutenant to the Witch-king, sharing a special rapport due to their similar backgrounds as powerful sorcerers. He stayed with the Witch-king at [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md) after the fortress's capture in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2002](../T/Third_Age_2002.md).

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), Akhôrahil rode with the Black Riders in search of the One Ring. He fought in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), later joining the remaining wraiths at the [Battle of the Morannon](../B/Battle_of_the_Morannon.md), where he perished with Sauron's defeat.<sup id="cite_ref-ICE_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ICE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Weaponry in Middle-earth Role Playing {#Weaponry_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing}

Beyond a [Morgul-knife](../M/Morgul-knife.md), the Ringwraith is noted to have wielded the **Yellow Hammer**, a gold steel mace inlaid with copper.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
