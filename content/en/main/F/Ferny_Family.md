**Ferny** is a [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) surname of [Bree](/wiki/Bree). One of the family's members was [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Ferny refers to the plant name "fern".<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>

Fern and Ferny, Fernie are real-life English surnames.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>
