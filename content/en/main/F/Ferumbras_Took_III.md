**Ferumbras Took III** was the thirtieth [Thain](/wiki/Thain) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Ferumbras was the son of [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Fortinbras II](/wiki/Fortinbras_Took_II "Fortinbras Took II") and [Lalia Clayhanger](/wiki/Lalia_Clayhanger). As Lalia outlived her husband, in accordance with [Took](/wiki/Took_Family "Took Family") tradition she continued as matriarch of the family, and despite Ferumbras being Thain he was only accorded a small bachelor pad in [Great Smials](/wiki/Great_Smials). He remained unmarried, and according to legend it was because no woman could stand living in with Lalia.<sup id="cite_ref-L214_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L214-1">[1]</a></sup>

Ferumbras attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001"). He died in the year [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1415](/wiki/Third_Age_3015 "Third Age 3015").<sup id="cite_ref-Took_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-2">[2]</a></sup> As Ferumbras left no heir the Thainship passed to his second cousin, [Paladin Took II](/wiki/Paladin_Took_II).<sup id="cite_ref-L214_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L214-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferumbras" title="wikipedia:Ferumbras">Ferumbras</a> is a name from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland" title="wikipedia:Roland">legends of Roland</a>, a corruption of Frankish *fier à bras*, "brave arm".
