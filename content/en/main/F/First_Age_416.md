**First Age 416** (abbreviated to **F.A. 416**) is the 416th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Fingolfin](Fingolfin.md) grants [Hador](../H/Hador.md) the [Lordship](../L/Lords_of_Dor-lómin.md) of [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
