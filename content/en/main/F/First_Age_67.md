**First Age 67** (abbreviated to **F.A. 67**) is the 67th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) learns of the [Kinslaying](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md) at [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md). He bans the speaking of [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md); the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) adopt [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) and only speak Quenya in private and in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
