**Fraud of the Rings** was a satirical comic strip by John Cook based on *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. The website the comic was hosted in is no longer active, however some strips can be viewed using the <a class="external text" href="https://web.archive.org/web/20240000000000*/http://cartoons.sev.com.au/Sev-Space/Fraud-of-the-Rings/%7C" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Wayback Machine</a>.
