**Falmaríni** were the male [sea](/wiki/Sea "Sea")-[fays](/wiki/Fays "Fays"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

They arrived to the world with [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB) and [Ónen](/wiki/%C3%93nen), along with some other types of fays.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Falmaríni* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), being the plural of *falmarin* ("sea-fay; spirit of the sea foam"). It probably consists of the elements *[falma](/wiki/Falma "Falma")* ("foam, wave") + the noun suffix *[-rin](/wiki/-rin)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
