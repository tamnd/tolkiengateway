**felek** means "to hew rock" in [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Stated to be a "root".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) is \*F-L-K
