**Farin** was a [dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) of [Durin's folk](../D/Durin's_folk.md).

## History {#History}

Farin was one of the [Dwarves of the Grey Mountains](../D/Dwarves_of_the_Grey_Mountains.md). When his uncle King [Dáin I](../D/Dáin_I.md) and cousin [Frór](Frór.md) had been slain by a [Cold-drake](../C/Cold-drakes.md), his people were forced to flee the [dragon](../D/Dragons.md)-plagued [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md) while he was just 29 years old. Farin's father, [Borin](../B/Borin.md), joined [Thrór](../T/Thrór.md) when he rebuilt the [Kingdom under the Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) in [2590](../T/Third_Age_2590.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[2]</a></sup>

Farin was the father of [Fundin](Fundin.md) and [Gróin](../G/Gróin.md), and therefore an ancestor to both [Balin](../B/Balin.md) of [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) and [Gimli Elf-friend](../G/Gimli.md), one of the [Fellowship of the Ring](Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[2]</a></sup>

In [2770](../T/Third_Age_2770.md) the dragon [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) [sacked the Lonely Mountain](../S/Sack_of_Erebor.md), driving its people into exile.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Jim Allan](../J/Jim_Allan.md) has suggested that *Farin* is derived from [Old Norse](../O/Old_Norse.md) *farinn* ("travelled-one").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
