**Farin** was a [dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") of [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk).

## History {#History}

Farin was one of the [Dwarves of the Grey Mountains](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Grey_Mountains). When his uncle King [Dáin I](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I) and cousin [Frór](/wiki/Fr%C3%B3r) had been slain by a [Cold-drake](/wiki/Cold-drakes "Cold-drakes"), his people were forced to flee the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons")-plagued [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) while he was just 29 years old. Farin's father, [Borin](/wiki/Borin), joined [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) when he rebuilt the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") in [2590](/wiki/Third_Age_2590 "Third Age 2590").<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[2]</a></sup>

Farin was the father of [Fundin](/wiki/Fundin) and [Gróin](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3in), and therefore an ancestor to both [Balin](/wiki/Balin) of [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) and [Gimli Elf-friend](/wiki/Gimli "Gimli"), one of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[2]</a></sup>

In [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770") the dragon [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) [sacked the Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Sack_of_Erebor "Sack of Erebor"), driving its people into exile.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) has suggested that *Farin* is derived from [Old Norse](/wiki/Old_Norse) *farinn* ("travelled-one").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
