**Forlong** was the wide-shouldered, old, and grey-bearded<sup id="cite_ref-MT182_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT182-2">[2]</a></sup> [Lord of Lossarnach](/wiki/Lord_of_Lossarnach)<sup id="cite_ref-MT181_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT181-3">[3]</a></sup> during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

In addition to his age, Forlong had a huge girth,<sup id="cite_ref-MT182_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT182-2">[2]</a></sup> which led [Bergil](/wiki/Bergil) of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) to refer to him as both "Forlong the Fat" and "Good old Forlong"<sup id="cite_ref-MT181_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT181-3">[3]</a></sup> and led to him being referred to as "Forlong the old" in the [Song of the Mounds of Mundburg](/wiki/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg)<sup id="cite_ref-Song_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Song-4">[4]</a></sup>.

On [9 March](/wiki/9_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Forlong rode into the Minas Tirith clad in mail with a black helm and a long heavy spear on a big thick-limbed horse<sup id="cite_ref-MT182_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT182-2">[2]</a></sup> with two hundred of his men to help to defend the city<sup id="cite_ref-MT183_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT183-1">[1]</a></sup>. His men were grim-faced, shorter and swarthier than any men that [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) had seen in Gondor before. They were well-armed and bore great battle-axes.<sup id="cite_ref-MT182_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT182-2">[2]</a></sup> On [15 March](/wiki/15_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Forlong fought in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields) during which he was killed by strokes of axes when he fought alone and unhorsed.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> He was remembered in the [Song of the Mounds of Mundburg](/wiki/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg).<sup id="cite_ref-Song_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Song-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Forlong* was a name of forgotten origin that descended from the days before the ships of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) sailed the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Its meaning is unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> It is also unknown if the element *for* is related to the same element in the [Dunlendish](/wiki/Dunlendish) name *[Forgoil](/wiki/Forgoil)*.

<dl><dd>Forlong is first found in <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> before the battle in the &#34;Merry Swan&#34; tavern on the third level of the city, reminiscing on their youth with his friends <a href="/wiki/Hirluin" title="Hirluin">Hirluin</a> and Neldir. Later, both Forlong and Hirluin fight in the defence of the White City on the walls of the First Circle. After the Rohirrim army arrives, both of them rush outside the city gates and faces their foes together before Hirluin is slain in view of his friend. Forlong himself is gravely wounded and after the battle is over is found by the player on the field. He shares that he saw the visage of Thorongil on the field of battle but believes it to be his dying fever until Aragorn comes to his side. Amazed to see the man from the days of his youth untouched by years, Forlong departs in peace.</dd></dl>
