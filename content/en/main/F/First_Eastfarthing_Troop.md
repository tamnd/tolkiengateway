The **First Eastfarthing Troop** was a troop of the [Watch](../W/Watch.md) formed during the troubled times in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) under the rule of [Sharkey](../S/Sharkey.md). The [Shirriffs](../S/Shirriffs.md) of the troop was based in the [Shirriff-house](../S/Shirriff-house.md) at [Frogmorton](Frogmorton.md) on the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md). It was the First Eastfarthing Troop that attempted to arrest [Frodo](Frodo_Baggins.md) and the [Travellers](../T/Travellers.md) on their return journey to [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md). They could not keep up with the mounted Travellers on foot so they parted at [Three-Farthing Stone](../T/Three-Farthing_Stone.md). They arrived at Bywater shortly before the first skirmish with the [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md). Most of them took off their feathers and joined in the revolt aside from a few who slunk away. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
