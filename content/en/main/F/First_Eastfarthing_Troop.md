The **First Eastfarthing Troop** was a troop of the [Watch](/wiki/Watch) formed during the troubled times in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") under the rule of [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey). The [Shirriffs](/wiki/Shirriffs) of the troop was based in the [Shirriff-house](/wiki/Shirriff-house) at [Frogmorton](/wiki/Frogmorton) on the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road). It was the First Eastfarthing Troop that attempted to arrest [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and the [Travellers](/wiki/Travellers) on their return journey to [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). They could not keep up with the mounted Travellers on foot so they parted at [Three-Farthing Stone](/wiki/Three-Farthing_Stone). They arrived at Bywater shortly before the first skirmish with the [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians). Most of them took off their feathers and joined in the revolt aside from a few who slunk away. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
