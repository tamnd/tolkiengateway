<i><b>Fumellar</b></i>, the **flowers of sleep**, were <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poppy" title="wikipedia:Poppy">poppies</a> according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

They grew in the gardens of [Lórien](</wiki/L%C3%B3rien_(Valinor)> "Lórien (Valinor)") in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). The *fumellar* are said to have glowed "redly in the dusk". The [Vala Lórien](/wiki/Irmo "Irmo") often used them in his enchantments.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *fumellar* is [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya): *fúmella*, *fúmellot* means "poppy". The first element is related to *fúmë* ("sleep"; noun).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Variant [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) names for "poppy" are *humilos*, *humlos*, *humethla*, and *humloth*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other occurences {#Other_occurences}

In early linguistic writings, *[Tethil](/wiki/Tethil)* was a [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name for a flower [fay](/wiki/Sprites "Sprites") that dwelt in a poppy.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
