**First Age 466** (abbreviated to **F.A. 466**) is the 466th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Spring {#Spring}

- Birth of [Lalaith](../L/Lalaith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## [Quest for the Silmaril](../Q/Quest_for_the_Silmaril.md) {#Quest_for_the_Silmaril}

- [Beren](../B/Beren.md) travels to [Angband](../A/Angband.md) by himself but [Huan](../H/Huan.md) brings [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) to him. Beren and Lúthien travel to Angband disguised as [Draugluin](../D/Draugluin.md) and [Thuringwethil](../T/Thuringwethil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Passing [Carcharoth](../C/Carcharoth.md), [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) puts [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) under a spell and [Beren](../B/Beren.md) takes a [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md) from the [Iron Crown](../I/Iron_Crown.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Trying to escape, [Carcharoth](../C/Carcharoth.md) bites off Beren's hand holding the Silmaril. The Silmaril hurts Carcharoth. Beren and Lúthien are rescued by [Thorondor](../T/Thorondor.md) and the [eagles](../E/Eagles.md) who take them to [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-2">[2]</a></sup>

## [Hunting of the Wolf](../H/Hunting_of_the_Wolf.md) {#Hunting_of_the_Wolf}

- [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md), [Beren](../B/Beren.md), [Beleg](../B/Beleg.md), [Mablung](../M/Mablung.md) and [Huan](../H/Huan.md) hunt [Carcharoth](../C/Carcharoth.md) to retrieve the [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Carcharoth mortally wounds Beren. Huan kills Carcharoth but dies in the process.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Thingol receives the Silmaril.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Death of [Beren](../B/Beren.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
