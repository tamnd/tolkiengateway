**Francesco Nepitello** (born [29 December](/wiki/29_December) [1967](/wiki/1967)) is an Italian tabletop game designer, who often works together with his long-time friend [Marco Maggi](/wiki/Marco_Maggi).<sup id="cite_ref-Juegos_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Juegos-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-roliste_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-roliste-2">[2]</a></sup>

Having grown up playing [Dungeons and Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons), he worked on the 1993 roleplaying game *Lex Arcana*. The popularity of *[Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering)* meant the end of the small Italian roleplaying game market, after which he moved on to designing board games. He still continued playing roleplaying games, and in February 2003 he mentioned being an avid player of [Decipher](/wiki/Decipher)'s [The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game)<sup id="cite_ref-roliste_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-roliste-2">[2]</a></sup> A year later, *[War of the Ring](</wiki/War_of_the_Ring_(2004_board_game)> "War of the Ring (2004 board game)")*, a board game he co-designed, was released.<sup id="cite_ref-Juegos_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Juegos-1">[1]</a></sup>

Under [Cubicle 7 Entertainment](/wiki/Cubicle_7_Entertainment), he was the lead designer of *[The One Ring](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)")* roleplaying game.<sup id="cite_ref-Juegos_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Juegos-1">[1]</a></sup> He would continue in that role after [Free League Publishing](/wiki/Free_League_Publishing) took over the license.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
