The **Fall of Fornost** was an event that resulted from the defeat of the forces of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md), and the taking of their capital city of [Fornost Erain](Fornost_Erain.md) by the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) and his armies.

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

By [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1974](../T/Third_Age_1974.md), the [Dúnedain of Arnor](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md) were on the brink of ruin. The [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) three hundred years previously had killed most of the people of [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md), which later fell to the Witch-king. Even with the aid of [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md), most of the diminished and totally demoralized people of Arnor were waiting to make their final stand at Fornost, capital city of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Opposing Forces {#Opposing_Forces}

For the forces of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md), exact numbers are not known. It can be estimated that, at most, ten thousand [Men of Arnor](../M/Men_of_Arnor.md) were present at the city, along with possibly several thousand Elves of [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md), [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md), and five hundred Hobbits archers from [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

The forces of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) can be estimated as between forty and sixty thousand [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), [Trolls](../T/Trolls.md), Evil Men, Wolves, and all other manner of evil creatures.

### The Battle {#The_Battle}

Not much is known of the battle, and it is simply stated that the Witch-king overran Fornost and the King was driven into the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md). The Witch-king's forces occupied Fornost and pillaged it.

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

With Arthedain in his hands, the Witch-king had conquered all of Arnor. [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md) perished in the [Icebay of Forochel](../I/Icebay_of_Forochel.md) when a ship sent to rescue him sank in a storm. The Witch-king took the throne in the king's palace, until a year later in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md) when the forces of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) had regrouped with newly arrived reinforcements from [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md). In the [Battle of Fornost](../B/Battle_of_Fornost.md), his army was destroyed, and he himself fled east over the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

Fornost was not resettled after the war. It became a deserted place, feared by the men of [Bree](../B/Bree.md), who called it Deadmen's Dike. It was not until a thousand years later after the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) under King [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) was it refounded.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
