The **Fall of Fornost** was an event that resulted from the defeat of the forces of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), and the taking of their capital city of [Fornost Erain](/wiki/Fornost_Erain) by the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) and his armies.

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

By [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1974](/wiki/Third_Age_1974 "Third Age 1974"), the [Dúnedain of Arnor](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor) were on the brink of ruin. The [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) three hundred years previously had killed most of the people of [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan), which later fell to the Witch-king. Even with the aid of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits), most of the diminished and totally demoralized people of Arnor were waiting to make their final stand at Fornost, capital city of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Opposing Forces {#Opposing_Forces}

For the forces of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), exact numbers are not known. It can be estimated that, at most, ten thousand [Men of Arnor](/wiki/Men_of_Arnor) were present at the city, along with possibly several thousand Elves of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), and five hundred Hobbits archers from [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

The forces of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) can be estimated as between forty and sixty thousand [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), [Trolls](/wiki/Trolls), Evil Men, Wolves, and all other manner of evil creatures.

### The Battle {#The_Battle}

Not much is known of the battle, and it is simply stated that the Witch-king overran Fornost and the King was driven into the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs). The Witch-king's forces occupied Fornost and pillaged it.

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

With Arthedain in his hands, the Witch-king had conquered all of Arnor. [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) perished in the [Icebay of Forochel](/wiki/Icebay_of_Forochel) when a ship sent to rescue him sank in a storm. The Witch-king took the throne in the king's palace, until a year later in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975") when the forces of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) had regrouped with newly arrived reinforcements from [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). In the [Battle of Fornost](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost), his army was destroyed, and he himself fled east over the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

Fornost was not resettled after the war. It became a deserted place, feared by the men of [Bree](/wiki/Bree), who called it Deadmen's Dike. It was not until a thousand years later after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) under King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") was it refounded.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
