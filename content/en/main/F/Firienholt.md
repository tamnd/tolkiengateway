**Firienholt**, originally known as **Eryn Fuir**<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup> was a small forest of [oaks](/wiki/Oaks "Oaks") that stood on the borders of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), about and upon the slopes of the [Halifirien](/wiki/Halifirien) (Amon Anwar in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Mering Stream](/wiki/Mering_Stream) arose in the cleft between the Halifirien and the rest of the [Ered Nimrais](/wiki/White_Mountains "White Mountains"). It then turned northwest and watered the densest stands of trees in the forest. The stream marked the precise border between the [Eastfold](/wiki/Eastfold), and the [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") land of [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien) and thus part of the woods belonged to either realm. The great West Road passed through a cutting in the wood to avoid the wet lands to the north. From the road there was also a path to the summit of the Halifirien maintained by the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Beacon-wardens&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Beacon-wardens (page does not exist)">Beacon-wardens</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The road that cut through the northern bounds of the wood had been built by the [Exiles of Númenor](/wiki/Exiles_of_N%C3%BAmenor) soon after the founding of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

At the end of the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance), [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) took his nephew [Meneldil](/wiki/Meneldil) and a company of trusted friends on a ride about the borders of Gondor. Returning from the northern boundary, Isildur came to the Eryn Fuir and had a path made to the summit of Amon Anwar, which was green and treeless. There they raised a mound for a casket to be the [tomb and memorial](/wiki/Tomb_of_Elendil "Tomb of Elendil") of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil). This spot was chosen because it stood near to the center of the lands claimed by the South Kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"), after [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl), lord of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), and his riders had saved the Steward [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion) and the army of Gondor at the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Cirion brought Eorl through the Eryn Fuir to Amon Anwar to grant him the land of [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon), thus founding the realm of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup> With the coming of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), the forest became known as "Firienholt" in [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese).<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2864](/wiki/Third_Age_2864 "Third Age 2864") King [Folca](/wiki/Folca) hunted and slew the great boar of [Everholt](/wiki/Everholt) in the Firien Wood, but died of the wounds the beast had given him.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In March of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and the host of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) passed to the north of Firienholt on their way to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Months later the funeral escort of Théoden would travel through the Firienholt on the way back to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The older [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for the wood had been <i><b>Eryn Fuir</b></i>, "North Wood" (becoming obsolete with the coming of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim)).<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup> Because of the unnatural silence that lay upon the land in the Common Speech the Firienholt acquired the name "the Whispering Wood".<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*firien holt* means "mountain wood", though it is rather more commonly referred to simply as the <i><b>Firien Wood</b></i>, taking its name from Halifirien.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-10">[10]</a></sup>
