**Firienholt**, originally known as **Eryn Fuir**<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup> was a small forest of [oaks](../O/Oaks.md) that stood on the borders of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), about and upon the slopes of the [Halifirien](../H/Halifirien.md) (Amon Anwar in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Mering Stream](../M/Mering_Stream.md) arose in the cleft between the Halifirien and the rest of the [Ered Nimrais](../W/White_Mountains.md). It then turned northwest and watered the densest stands of trees in the forest. The stream marked the precise border between the [Eastfold](../E/Eastfold.md), and the [Gondorian](../G/Gondor.md) land of [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md) and thus part of the woods belonged to either realm. The great West Road passed through a cutting in the wood to avoid the wet lands to the north. From the road there was also a path to the summit of the Halifirien maintained by the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Beacon-wardens&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Beacon-wardens (page does not exist)">Beacon-wardens</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The road that cut through the northern bounds of the wood had been built by the [Exiles of Númenor](../E/Exiles_of_Númenor.md) soon after the founding of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

At the end of the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md), [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) took his nephew [Meneldil](../M/Meneldil.md) and a company of trusted friends on a ride about the borders of Gondor. Returning from the northern boundary, Isildur came to the Eryn Fuir and had a path made to the summit of Amon Anwar, which was green and treeless. There they raised a mound for a casket to be the [tomb and memorial](../T/Tomb_of_Elendil.md) of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md). This spot was chosen because it stood near to the center of the lands claimed by the South Kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md), after [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md), lord of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md), and his riders had saved the Steward [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md) and the army of Gondor at the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](../B/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Cirion brought Eorl through the Eryn Fuir to Amon Anwar to grant him the land of [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md), thus founding the realm of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup> With the coming of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md), the forest became known as "Firienholt" in [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md).<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2864](../T/Third_Age_2864.md) King [Folca](Folca.md) hunted and slew the great boar of [Everholt](../E/Everholt.md) in the Firien Wood, but died of the wounds the beast had given him.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In March of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) and the host of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) passed to the north of Firienholt on their way to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Months later the funeral escort of Théoden would travel through the Firienholt on the way back to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The older [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name for the wood had been <i><b>Eryn Fuir</b></i>, "North Wood" (becoming obsolete with the coming of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup> Because of the unnatural silence that lay upon the land in the Common Speech the Firienholt acquired the name "the Whispering Wood".<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*firien holt* means "mountain wood", though it is rather more commonly referred to simply as the <i><b>Firien Wood</b></i>, taking its name from Halifirien.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-10">[10]</a></sup>
