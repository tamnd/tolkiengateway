**Fladweth Amrod** was a place in [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) near [Tavrobel](/wiki/Tavrobel), where [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol) stayed temporarily, according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Fladweth Amrod* is [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) for "Nomad's Green",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> being a compound of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Fladweth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fladweth (page does not exist)">fladweth</a></i> ("meadow, grassland") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Amrog&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Amrog (page does not exist)">amrog</a></i> ("nomad, wanderer").<sup id="cite_ref-Green_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Green-3">[3]</a></sup>

Another, earlier, name for the place was <i><b>Fladweth-amrog</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In the spring of [1918](/wiki/1918), [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) was posted again to Rugeley Camp, in <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penkridge" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Penkridge</a>, a village in <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staffordshire" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Staffordshire</a>. While visiting him, [Edith Bratt](/wiki/Edith_Bratt), and several of her companions, found accommodation in a nearby house in Gipsy Green, a small hamlet. There, Tolkien would occasionally visit them and stay for a while.<sup id="cite_ref-Gib_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gib-5">[5]</a></sup>

It is possible that the name of *Gipsy Green* may be reflected in the name *Fladweth Amrod* (whose translation from Gnomish would be *Nomad's Green*),<sup id="cite_ref-Gib_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gib-5">[5]</a></sup> a connection [John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth) proposed in his book *[Tolkien and the Great War](/wiki/Tolkien_and_the_Great_War)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
