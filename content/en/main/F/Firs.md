**Firs** were Evergreen trees that grew across the northern parts of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and especially in the upper [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin). On [23 September](/wiki/23_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") encountered firs at midnight in the [Green Hill Country](/wiki/Green_Hill_Country). The hobbits made a fire of fir sticks and cones and slept in angles between a great fir-tree's roots.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1992-1997: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="/wiki/The_Grey_Mountains" title="The Grey Mountains">The Grey Mountains</a></i> &amp; <i><a href="/wiki/Hands_of_the_Healer" title="Hands of the Healer">Hands of the Healer</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>The <i><b>Silver Fir</b></i> (<a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Celepthon</i>, <a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Telpesono</i>), also known as <i>Argenak</i>, was a type of fir with silver-green needles, found in the Grey Mountains. Its sap, scraped from an incision in the bark, could be melted and mixed with wine to relieve severe constipation .<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>
