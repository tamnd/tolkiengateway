**Flotsam and Jetsam** is the ninth chapter of the first book in *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*.

## Summary {#Summary}

[Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") told [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) how they had come to be at [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) after escaping the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and being helped by [Ents](/wiki/Ents). Merry told them how the whole forest had marched to Isengard and torn up the gates. Pippin then told them how [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) had almost managed to escape but had been spotted in time and taken refuge in the stronghold of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc) that even the iron-tearing ents could not destroy. He also told them how [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) had come by and asked for [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard)'s help, which he had given. Pippin told them how [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma") had come to give Saruman a message, but seeing the destruction he had wanted to leave. At that point Treebeard had captured him and that was how he had come to be cloistered with Saruman.

## Composition {#Composition}

Tolkien wrote this chapter, along with the five final chapter of Book III were writen as a whole (not in consequence) during his spare time in summer and autumn [1942](/wiki/1942). He wrote three versions of Merry's monologue on [pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed"). Eventually, while making a fair copy, much of Merry's speech was removed to form a separate text, "[Concerning Pipe-weed](/wiki/Concerning_Pipe-weed)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
