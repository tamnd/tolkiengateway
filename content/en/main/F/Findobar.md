**Findobar** was the son of [Fingon](Fingon.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) from a genealogical table created by [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) in the early 1930s.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The table has not been published but [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) mentioned the name in his comments on the genealogies of the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](../Q/Quenta_Noldorinwa.md)* period in the Appendix to *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](../T/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the later versions of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* the character of Findobar was discarded from the legendarium.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Findobar* was a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) name appearing as a compound of *[phinde](../P/Phinde.md)* ("skill"), and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=B%C3%A1r&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bár (page does not exist)">bár</a></i> ("home; earth").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
