<i><b>faloth</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) noun meaning "foam".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=PHAL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="PHAL (page does not exist)">PHAL</a> ("foam").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Example {#Example}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Asfaloth" title="Asfaloth">As<b>faloth</b></a></i> = "Sunlit Foam"
