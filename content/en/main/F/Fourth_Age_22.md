**Fourth Age 22** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 22** or **F.O.**) is the 22nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 22 was also known as **S.R. 1443** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), [Rose](../R/Rose_Cotton.md) and [Elanor](../E/Elanor_Gardner.md) leave [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and return to [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Filibert Bolger](Filibert_Bolger.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
