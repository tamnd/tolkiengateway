The **Five Rivers of [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin)** are the following:

1. The river [Erui](/wiki/Erui),
2. The river [Sirith](/wiki/Sirith), which met the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) at [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir),
3. The river [Celos](/wiki/Celos), which started in the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) and became a tributary of the river Sirith,
4. The river [Serni](/wiki/Serni), which began in the plains of Lebennin and emptied into the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas) west of the [Ethir Anduin](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin) and southwest of the city of [Linhir](/wiki/Linhir), and
5. The river [Gilrain](/wiki/Gilrain), which started as two unnamed tributaries in the White Mountains, and flowed south meeting the Serni at Linhir.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Five Rivers were the Gilrain, the Serni, the Sirith, the Celos, and the <a href="/wiki/Erui" title="Erui">Erui</a>. Each of the rivers was the domain of a River-Maiden, one of the Five Sisters of Lebennin. By the time of the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, the Five Sisters were considered a myth by most of the <a href="/wiki/Men" title="Men">Men</a> of Lebennin, though it remained traditional to leave baskets of flowers as offerings to the rivers.</dd></dl>
