The **Five Rivers of [Lebennin](../L/Lebennin.md)** are the following:

1. The river [Erui](../E/Erui.md),
2. The river [Sirith](../S/Sirith.md), which met the river [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) at [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md),
3. The river [Celos](../C/Celos.md), which started in the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) and became a tributary of the river Sirith,
4. The river [Serni](../S/Serni.md), which began in the plains of Lebennin and emptied into the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md) west of the [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md) and southwest of the city of [Linhir](../L/Linhir.md), and
5. The river [Gilrain](../G/Gilrain.md), which started as two unnamed tributaries in the White Mountains, and flowed south meeting the Serni at Linhir.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Five Rivers were the Gilrain, the Serni, the Sirith, the Celos, and the <a href="../E/Erui.md" title="Erui">Erui</a>. Each of the rivers was the domain of a River-Maiden, one of the Five Sisters of Lebennin. By the time of the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, the Five Sisters were considered a myth by most of the <a href="../M/Men.md" title="Men">Men</a> of Lebennin, though it remained traditional to leave baskets of flowers as offerings to the rivers.</dd></dl>
