The **First Fall of Minas Ithil** was a battle that occurred in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3429](../S/Second_Age_3429.md) between the forces of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) in [3319](../S/Second_Age_3319.md), the sons of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) brought their ships up the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) and together founded the realm of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md). While [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md) set up his house in [Minas Anor](../M/Minas_Anor.md), his brother [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) established his house in [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md) in a pass in the [Ephel Dúath](../E/Ephel_Dúath.md) on the border of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).

But [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) also escaped the Downfall and returned to Mordor. There he built up his strength until he could assault the exiled [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md). In [3429](../S/Second_Age_3429.md) a great force took Minas Ithil, drove out Isildur, and burnt the [White Tree](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md). Isildur escaped with his wife, his sons, and a seedling of the Tree. They traveled to Elendil in the north while Anárion defended [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) and kept Sauron at bay.

Sauron's attack and conquest of Minas Ithil was the spark that touched off the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
