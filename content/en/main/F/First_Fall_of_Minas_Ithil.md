The **First Fall of Minas Ithil** was a battle that occurred in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3429](/wiki/Second_Age_3429 "Second Age 3429") between the forces of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor) in [3319](/wiki/Second_Age_3319 "Second Age 3319"), the sons of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) brought their ships up the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) and together founded the realm of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). While [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) set up his house in [Minas Anor](/wiki/Minas_Anor), his brother [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) established his house in [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil) in a pass in the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) on the border of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).

But [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) also escaped the Downfall and returned to Mordor. There he built up his strength until he could assault the exiled [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans). In [3429](/wiki/Second_Age_3429 "Second Age 3429") a great force took Minas Ithil, drove out Isildur, and burnt the [White Tree](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor "White Tree of Gondor"). Isildur escaped with his wife, his sons, and a seedling of the Tree. They traveled to Elendil in the north while Anárion defended [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) and kept Sauron at bay.

Sauron's attack and conquest of Minas Ithil was the spark that touched off the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
