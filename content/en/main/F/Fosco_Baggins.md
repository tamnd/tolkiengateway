**Fosco Baggins** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Fosco was the son of [Largo Baggins](../L/Largo_Baggins.md) and [Tanta Hornblower](../T/Tanta_Hornblower.md). He married [Ruby Bolger](../R/Ruby_Bolger.md), and had three children: [Dora](../D/Dora_Baggins.md), [Drogo](../D/Drogo_Baggins.md) and [Dudo](../D/Dudo_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Fosco is the grandfather of [Frodo Baggins](Frodo_Baggins.md).
