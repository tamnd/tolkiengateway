**Fosco Baggins** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Fosco was the son of [Largo Baggins](/wiki/Largo_Baggins) and [Tanta Hornblower](/wiki/Tanta_Hornblower). He married [Ruby Bolger](/wiki/Ruby_Bolger), and had three children: [Dora](/wiki/Dora_Baggins "Dora Baggins"), [Drogo](/wiki/Drogo_Baggins "Drogo Baggins") and [Dudo](/wiki/Dudo_Baggins "Dudo Baggins").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Fosco is the grandfather of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).
