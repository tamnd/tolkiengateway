**First Age 490** (abbreviated to **F.A. 490**) is the 490th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md) leads [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) to [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md). Túrin, known as [Mormegil](../M/Mormegil.md), becomes a great captain of the people of Nargothrond.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
