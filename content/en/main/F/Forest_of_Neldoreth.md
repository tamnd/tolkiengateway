The **Forest of Neldoreth** was the [beech](/wiki/Beeches "Beeches")-forest that lay between the banks of the [Esgalduin River](/wiki/Esgalduin "Esgalduin") and the [Mindeb River](/wiki/Mindeb "Mindeb") that formed the northern and lesser part of the Kingdom of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It was in the Forest of Neldoreth that [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) was born and later these woods were part of the enclosure called the "[Girdle of Melian](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was also under the trees of Neldoreth that [Beren](/wiki/Beren) first beheld Lúthien dancing in the moonlight. Later, when [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) had cast Beren into a pit and Lúthien resolved to rescue him, [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) had her imprisoned in a house built high in the branches of the highest tree of the forest, called [Hírilorn](/wiki/H%C3%ADrilorn).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

When [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) was carrying [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") to [Wellinghall](/wiki/Wellinghall) he chanted to them about walking in the woods of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). He recalled coming to Neldoreth in Autumn and enjoying the gold and red and sighing leaves of *Taur-na-neldor* (an alternate name for the forest).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Neldoreth* is unclear, but seems to be a compound of *[neldor](/wiki/Neldor "Neldor")* ("beech") and the abstract noun ending *-eth*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

*Taur-na-neldor* more clearly means "Beech-forest": *[taur](/wiki/Taur "Taur")* ("forest"), *[na](/wiki/Na "Na")* ("of") and *neldor*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

On an early draft map for *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, Tolkien indicated that *[Neldoreth](</wiki/Neldoreth_(Rh%C3%BBn)> "Neldoreth (Rhûn)")* was the name of the forest bordering the north-eastern shore of the [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn). This forest went unnamed in later maps.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

[Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard)'s home was also called the **Forest of Neldoreth** in an early draft.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
