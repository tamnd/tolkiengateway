**Fingon** was a [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) prince, and later second [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md), noted for his skill in battle and was thus named "the Valiant" by his cousin [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md).

Fingon was the eldest son of [Fingolfin](Fingolfin.md) and [Anairë](../A/Anairë.md), and he was the older brother of [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md), [Aredhel](../A/Aredhel.md), and [Argon](../A/Argon.md). Fingon led the largest host in the [Flight of the Noldor](Flight_of_the_Noldor.md) from [Aman](../A/Aman.md) to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) where he then ruled over [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) and [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md). He ruled the Noldor as High King from [F.A.](First_Age.md) [456](First_Age_456.md), following the death of his father in single combat with [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). However, Fingon is mostly remembered for his friendship with Maedhros, who he rescued from the peaks of [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md), and for the manner of his death in battle with the [Balrog](../B/Balrog.md) [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md>) during the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md). He was succeeded as High King by his brother [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md), who ruled the Noldor from [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).

## History {#History}

### In Aman {#In_Aman}

Fingon was born in the [Year of the Trees 1260](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1260.md),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[5]</a></sup> being the eldest child of [Anairë](../A/Anairë.md) and [Fingolfin](Fingolfin.md), second son of [King of the Noldor](../K/King_of_the_Noldor.md) [Finwë](Finwë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-7">[4]</a></sup> Whilst in [Aman](../A/Aman.md) he lived in [Tirion](../T/Tirion.md), home of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) in [Eldamar](../E/Eldamar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamar_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamar-9">[6]</a></sup>

In [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1495](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1495.md), [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), with the help of [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md), destroyed the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md) of [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), stole the [Silmarils](../S/Silmarils.md) from the fortress of [Formenos](Formenos.md) and killed Finwë.<sup id="cite_ref-AA_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-10">[7]</a></sup> In anger, Fingon's uncle [Fëanor](Fëanor.md) made a [speech in Tirion](../S/Speech_of_Fëanor.md) and, with [his sons](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md), made [an oath](../O/Oath_of_Fëanor.md) to recover the Silmarils and lead the Noldor in to Beleriand. Fingon did not support Fëanor, however he was moved by Fëanor's words, and he desired to see wide unguarded lands and to rule there a realm at his own will.<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-11">[8]</a></sup>

During the [Flight of the Noldor](Flight_of_the_Noldor.md), Fingon led the largest host and his people supported Fëanor in the [First Kinslaying](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md) at [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md). Once the host reached [Araman](../A/Araman.md) in [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1496](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1496.md) there was great debate about how to reach [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Fëanor's people secretly took all the [Swan-ships](../S/Swan-ships.md) and passed over the sea to land at [Firth of Drengist](Firth_of_Drengist.md); [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) - who was friendly with Fingon - wanted to send the ships back first to retrieve "Fingon the valiant" but Fëanor burnt the ships at [Losgar](../L/Losgar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-11">[8]</a></sup> Fingon, with the rest of the Noldor of Fingolfin and his cousin [Finrod](Finrod.md), had to cross the dangerous grinding ice of [Helcaraxë](../H/Helcaraxë.md) with much loss of life. They arrived in Middle-earth in [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1500](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1500.md), after Fëanor had been killed in the second [battle](../B/Battles_of_Beleriand.md) of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](../D/Dagor-nuin-Giliath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Return_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-13">[10]</a></sup>

### Rescue of Maedhros {#Rescue_of_Maedhros}

In [F.A.](First_Age.md) [1](First_Age_1.md), soon after their arrival in Middle-earth following the rising of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) and [Moon](../M/Moon.md), the host of [Fingolfin](Fingolfin.md) were attacked in the [Battle of the Lammoth](../B/Battle_of_the_Lammoth.md). Although victorious in battle against the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), Fingon's brother Argon was surrounded and killed.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[11]</a></sup>

There was no great love between the Houses of Fingolfin and Fëanor following the latter's burning of the ships, causing the former to cross the Helcaraxë. Fingon resolved to rebuild the relationship between these two great houses of the Noldor, so in [F.A.](First_Age.md) [5](First_Age_5.md) went on a noble quest to Thangorodrim to rescue his cousin and friend Maedhros who had previously been captured by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). Travelling alone, and unseen in the darkness, Fingon climbed across the cracks and crevices of Thangorodrim but could not find Maedhros. Then, in both despair and defiance, he took out his harp and sang a fair song of Valinor. Suddenly, above him,his song was taken up, and Maedhros sang among his torment. However, Fingon could not climb up to him and so Maedhros begged Fingon to shoot him with his bow. But, as Fingon cried to [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) to "speed now this feathered shaft", the great [eagle](../E/Eagles.md) [Thorondor](../T/Thorondor.md) swooped down and lifted Fingon up. Fingon could not break Maedhros's chains - being of evil design by Morgoth - and although Maedhros begged Fingon to kill him, Fingon cut Maedhros's hand off at the wrist to free him. Borne back to [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) by Thorondor, Fingon's deed was praised as one of the most mighty amongst the Noldor. The hurt between the two houses was healed, and Maedhros waived the [High Kingship of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) which then passed to Fingolfin.<sup id="cite_ref-Realms_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Realms-15">[12]</a></sup>

[Fingolfin](Fingolfin.md)'s people made their home in [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md), mostly around [Lake Mithrim](../L/Lake_Mithrim.md), and built the fortress of [Barad Eithel](../B/Barad_Eithel.md) to keep watch; in [F.A.](First_Age.md) [7](First_Age_7.md), at Maedhros's suggestion, Fëanor's sons moved to [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[13]</a></sup> The region of [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) was taken by Fingon's brother [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) (until he moved to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md)), whilst the south-western region of Hithlum, [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md), was given to Fingon to rule over.<sup id="cite_ref-Realms_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Realms-15">[12]</a></sup>

### In Hithlum {#In_Hithlum}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/V/Victoria_Clare_-_Fingon_and_Glaurung.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a3/Victoria_Clare_-_Fingon_and_Glaurung.jpg/300px-Victoria_Clare_-_Fingon_and_Glaurung.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="227" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a3/Victoria_Clare_-_Fingon_and_Glaurung.jpg/450px-Victoria_Clare_-_Fingon_and_Glaurung.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a3/Victoria_Clare_-_Fingon_and_Glaurung.jpg/600px-Victoria_Clare_-_Fingon_and_Glaurung.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1200" data-file-height="909"/></a><figcaption><i>Fingon and Glaurung</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Victoria_Clare.md" title="Category:Images by Victoria Clare">Victoria Clare</a></figcaption></figure>

Following the great victory of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) in the [Dagor Aglareb](../D/Dagor_Aglareb.md), the Glorious Battle, Fingon lived in a watchful peace known as the [Siege of Angband](../S/Siege_of_Angband.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-13">[10]</a></sup> In [F.A.](First_Age.md) [155](First_Age_155.md), [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) sent a force of along the coast to [Drengist](../D/Drengist.md) to attack [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) from the west. Having been seen by the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), Fingon heavily defeated the Orcs, causing Morgoth to realize that orcs alone were no match for the Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[14]</a></sup> Over a hundred years later in [F.A.](First_Age.md) [260](First_Age_260.md), the great [dragon](../D/Dragons.md) [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) came forth from [Angband](../A/Angband.md) to attack the Elves;<sup id="cite_ref-GA260_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA260-18">[15]</a></sup> although a new breed of [fire-drake](Fire-drakes.md), Glaurung was still relatively young and only half-grown. The Elves fled to [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) and [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) as Glaurung defiled the [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md), but Fingon led a troop of archers on horseback to attack Glaurung. He could not withstand their arrows and fled, and for the next 200 years the peoples of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) enjoyed the [Long Peace](../L/Long_Peace.md) and grew prosperous.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_13-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-13">[10]</a></sup> In [F.A.](First_Age.md) [416](First_Age_416.md), [Fingolfin](Fingolfin.md) granted [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) to the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md), the third house of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md), and Fingon presented [Hador](../H/Hador.md) with a [Dragon-helm](../D/Dragon-helm_of_Dor-lómin.md) which eventually passed to [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Narn_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narn-20">[17]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/M/Marya_Filatova_-_Fingon_and_Rochallor.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/6f/Marya_Filatova_-_Fingon_and_Rochallor.jpg/300px-Marya_Filatova_-_Fingon_and_Rochallor.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="179" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/6f/Marya_Filatova_-_Fingon_and_Rochallor.jpg/450px-Marya_Filatova_-_Fingon_and_Rochallor.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/6f/Marya_Filatova_-_Fingon_and_Rochallor.jpg/600px-Marya_Filatova_-_Fingon_and_Rochallor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="612"/></a><figcaption>Fingon and <a href="../R/Rochallor.md" title="Rochallor">Rochallor</a> by <a href="../M/Marya_Filatova.md" title="Marya Filatova">Marya Filatova</a></figcaption></figure>

In winter [F.A.](First_Age.md) [455](First_Age_455.md), rivers of fire suddenly sprang forth from [Angband](../A/Angband.md), turning the Ard-galen into the [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md). This began the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), the Battle of Sudden Flame.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-21">[18]</a></sup> The dragon Glaurung, now fully grown, led armies of [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md) and [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) in battle that resulted in the deaths of many Elves and Men. The remaining forces of the Noldor were scattered following this heavy defeat. Perceiving the ruin of the Noldor, Fingon's father Fingolfin challenged Morgoth to single combat. Fingolfin fought valiantly, and although he died in the battle, Morgoth himself was wounded and never again fought in single combat. Fingon then became [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Realms_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Realms-15">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-21">[18]</a></sup>

Seven years later, in [F.A.](First_Age.md) [462](First_Age_462.md), Morgoth sent a force of Orcs to attack Fingon's people in Hithlum. Galdor, son of Hador, died defending [Eithel Sirion](../E/Eithel_Sirion.md) and his son [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) took up the [lordship of Dor-lómin](../L/Lords_of_Dor-lómin.md) and served Fingon thereafter. To the west, battle took place in Hithlum, and Fingon would have succumbed had it not been for a force of [Falathrim](Falathrim.md) elves under the command of [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) who sailed up the [Firth of Drengist](Firth_of_Drengist.md) and helped defeat the Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_21-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-21">[18]</a></sup>

### Nirnaeth Arnoediad {#Nirnaeth_Arnoediad}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/S/Steamey_-_Fingon_in_a_battle_with_the_Balrogs.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7c/Steamey_-_Fingon_in_a_battle_with_the_Balrogs.jpg/300px-Steamey_-_Fingon_in_a_battle_with_the_Balrogs.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="287" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7c/Steamey_-_Fingon_in_a_battle_with_the_Balrogs.jpg/450px-Steamey_-_Fingon_in_a_battle_with_the_Balrogs.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7c/Steamey_-_Fingon_in_a_battle_with_the_Balrogs.jpg/600px-Steamey_-_Fingon_in_a_battle_with_the_Balrogs.jpg 2x" data-file-width="889" data-file-height="850"/></a><figcaption><i>Fingon in a battle with the Balrogs</i> by Steamey</figcaption></figure>

Following the success of [Beren](../B/Beren.md) and [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) in the [Quest for the Silmaril](../Q/Quest_for_the_Silmaril.md), [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) saw that Morgoth was not unassailable and was emboldened to form the [Union of Maedhros](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md) of Elves, Men and [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md). Maedhros would attack from the east and Fingon from the west. They planned to trap Morgoth between the two armies.<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-22">[19]</a></sup> Unlooked for, Fingon's brother [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) unleashed a force of ten thousand Elves from [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). On seeing this, Fingon cried:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>&#39;<a href="../U/Utúlie&#39;n_aurë!.md" title="Utúlie&#39;n aurë!">Utúlie&#39;n aurë!</a> Aiya Eldalië ar Atanatári, utúlie&#39;n aurë! The day has come! Behold, people of the Eldar and Fathers of Men, the day has come!&#39; And all those who heard his great voice echo in the hills answered crying: &#39;Auta i lómë! The night is passing!&#39;
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion.md" title="Quenta Silmarillion">Quenta Silmarillion</a></i>, &#34;<a class="mw-redirect" href="../O/Of_the_Fifth_Battle.md" title="Of the Fifth Battle">Of the Fifth Battle</a>&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote>

However, Maedhros' army had been delayed following the betrayal of [Uldor](../U/Uldor.md). [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) therefore sent his Orcs swiftly over the Anfauglith to taunt Fingon and draw out his forces. They held firm until the Orcs brought forth [Gelmir](<../G/Gelmir_(son_of_Guilin).md>) and cut off his legs, arms and head before them. [Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md), Gelmir's brother, was enraged by this; and he, with Fingon, led the charge. Gwindor and the elves of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) reached the doors of Angband but they were all slain save Gwindor, who was taken captive, for Fingon could not come to his aid in time.<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-22">[19]</a></sup>

On the fourth day, Fingon retreated and met his brother Turgon on the plains of Anfauglith. Some hours later, the banners of [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) appeared on the battlefield and the Elves were heartened, believing victory was in their grasp. However, Morgoth then unleashed [wolves](../W/Wolves.md), dragons and Balrogs led by [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md), who separated the hosts of Fingon and Turgon from Maedhros. This battle, the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), the Battle of Unnumbered Tears, was the most grievous for the Elves. Fingon, with a host of Men from [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) led by [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md), was surrounded. Eventually, being pushed back, Fingon fought [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md>), lord of [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md) in battle, but was killed by Gothmog's black axe after another Balrog bound him with a whip. They beat him into the dust with their maces, and his banner, they trod into the mire of his blood. This brought an end to Fingon's realm in [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md), and his brother Turgon became High King of the Noldor,<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_22-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-22">[19]</a></sup> whilst Húrin was captured and tortured personally by Morgoth.<sup id="cite_ref-Narn_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narn-20">[17]</a></sup>

*Fingon* is the [Sindarized](../S/Sindarized.md) version of version of his [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) [father-name](Father-name.md), <i><b>Findecáno</b></i> ("Hair-shout"), from *[findë](Findë.md)* ("hair") + *[káno](../K/Káno.md)* ("commander"). It is said it was given by his father as an echo of *Finwë* and because it fitted him due his long dark hair, combed in great plaits braided with gold.<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-7">[4]</a></sup>

Read as proper [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), *Fingon* is composed of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Fin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fin (page does not exist)">fin</a></i> ("hair") + *-gon* ("lord, prince").<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[20]</a></sup>

At some point, [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) considered and rejected the name as *Fingorn*, from *fin(wë)* + *[gorn](../G/Gorn.md)* ("revered").<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[21]</a></sup>

In the [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) phase of the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) languages, the name contains the elements *find-* ("skill") + *-gon* ("valour").<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[22]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Earliest versions {#Earliest_versions}

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) observed that in the very earliest versions of the legendarium, there is no mention of Fingon (or Fingolfin, Finarfin or [Finrod](Finrod.md))<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[23]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[24]</a></sup> However, when he first appears in "[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](../T/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md)" in the 1920s he is named <i><b>Finweg</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[25]</a></sup> (and on one occasion *Fingweg*),<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29">[26]</a></sup> a name he retains in the "[The Quenta](../T/The_Quenta.md)" of the early 1930s even though his story closely matches that of the published *Silmarillion*. A more significant difference is that originally Fingon spoke against [Fëanor](Fëanor.md) at [Tirion](../T/Tirion.md),<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">[27]</a></sup> whilst in the later 1951 version of *Quenta Silmarllion*, Fingon and [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) did not take part in the Kinslaying at [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31">[28]</a></sup>

Fingon is renamed from *Finweg* the 1930s around the time of the *[Annals of Beleriand](../A/Annals_of_Beleriand.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32">[29]</a></sup> and was firmly *Fingon* by the time [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) reworked the *[Quenta Silmarillion](<../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road).md>)* in 1937-8.<sup id="cite_ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33">[30]</a></sup> In an [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) version of the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](../Q/Quenta_Noldorinwa.md)*, Fingon's name is given as <i><b>Finbrand</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34">[31]</a></sup>

### Later versions {#Later_versions}

In the published *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, Fingon was the father of [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), which was a brief idea that Tolkien had, but this inclusion was an editorial decision by Christopher Tolkien who admitted it would have been better had his parentage remained obscure and that Gil-galad being the son of [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) was Tolkien's "last word on the subject". Furthermore, before his death Tolkien corrected the last of the genealogical tables, where Fingon's wife and chidren (including [Erien](../E/Erien.md) and [Finbor](Finbor.md)) were struck out, and thus in *[The Shibboleth of Fëanor](../T/The_Shibboleth_of_Fëanor.md)* Fingon was once and for all mentioned as wifeless and childless elf-lord.<sup id="cite_ref-Shibboleth_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibboleth-4">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Galad_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-5">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35">[32]</a></sup>
