The **Folde** was a region in the centre of the realm of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), between the [Westfold](/wiki/Westfold) in the west, the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) in the south and a line from the confluence of the river [Snowbourn](/wiki/Snowbourn) with the river [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash) running approximately south-west to the White Mountains in the east. The [Eastfold](/wiki/Eastfold) lay beyond that line in the east.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomenclature_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomenclature-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Folde formed a part of the King's Lands.<sup id="cite_ref-Aldburg_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aldburg-3">[3]</a></sup> The town of [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras), the defensive centre of the Folde and the seat of the King<sup id="cite_ref-Nomenclature_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomenclature-1">[1]</a></sup> and Aldburg, the former seat of the King were located in the Folde<sup id="cite_ref-Aldburg_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aldburg-3">[3]</a></sup>..

## History {#History}

The Folde was originally part of the realm of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) until in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510") the fief of [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) was granted to [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) the Young by the [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) of Gondor, [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion). Thereafter the Folde was part of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-4">[4]</a></sup> The first King of Rohan, Eorl the Young lived in a house in Aldburg. After his son Brego moved to Edoras, Aldburg passed into the hands of the third son of Brego, [Eofor](/wiki/Eofor) and his descendants.<sup id="cite_ref-Aldburg_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aldburg-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer), the nephew of King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) of Rohan, who claimed to be descended from Eofor had his home in Aldburg.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On March 6 and 7<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) rode with [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) through the Folde on the road to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

On March 11 [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the riders of Rohan led by their King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) travelled with [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) through the Folde on their way to Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In early August [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> the funeral escort of King Théoden passed through the Folde on its way to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Folde* is a name in the language of Rohan, which is represented by [Old English](/wiki/Old_English). *Folde* means "earth", "land", or "country" in Old English\]\].<sup id="cite_ref-Nomenclature_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomenclature-1">[1]</a></sup>
