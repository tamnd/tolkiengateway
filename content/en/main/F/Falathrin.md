**Falathrin** was the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) dialect of the [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is told that the dialect "was in many ways different" from other forms of Sindarin, but not enough "to interfere with intercourse".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days), the host of the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) [Elves](/wiki/Elves) was split after King [Elwë](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") became lost in the woods of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). Some remained behind to search for their missing lord, while others followed his brother [Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB) to the western shores of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). There they were befriended by the [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar") [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB), who convinced some of them to forsake the journey to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). These Elves fell in love with the shore-lands of the [Falas](/wiki/Falas) and wished to remain there. As such, they became known as the Falathrim.

Both the Falathrim and the other Teleri of Beleriand (who became known as the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar)) spoke a language descended from [Common Telerin](/wiki/Common_Telerin). It had no name, nor did it need one, for it was the only language they had ever heard. However, now that the Falathrim were separated from their inland kin, their tongue began to diverge in subtle ways. The exiled [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) Elves (who spoke [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)) designated the language of Beleriand as Sindarin. They considered the Falathrin variant to be a dialect of Sindarin. The two branches of the language had many differences but were mutually intelligible.

Following the tumultuous [Battles of Beleriand](/wiki/Battles_of_Beleriand) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the [Havens of the Falas](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas) were destroyed. The surviving Falathrim fled to the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion) and established secret havens there. As [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) and other Elvish kingdoms were decimated by the wars, refugees from these lands also fled to the Havens of Sirion and the [Isle of Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar). There they mingled with the Falathrim and the various Sindarin dialects began to merge. A new dialect developed, largely based on Falathrin, which allowed for better communication between the various peoples of the Havens.

In the [Second](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") and [Third Ages](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age"), this new version of Sindarin became the common tongue for all Elves. Falathrin Sindarin, as it had existed in the First Age, was no longer spoken.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The language of the Falathrim was never given a definite name by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"). Fans and [Tolkienists](/wiki/Tolkienists) often use the term Falathrin, which combines the Sindarin word *falas*, meaning "surfline, sea-shore" with the suffix *-in*, used in the names of other Elvish languages (e.g. Sindarin, [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin), etc.).
