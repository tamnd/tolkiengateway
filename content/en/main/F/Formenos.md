**Formenos** was the mighty stronghold of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), his [sons](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor"), and their followers in the north of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), built after his banishment from [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion). A great number of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), including their king [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB), went with Fëanor into exile.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Fëanor's treasury was in Formenos, and [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") came to the stronghold searching for the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils). Here he slew Finwë before stealing the Jewels and escaping to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Formenos means "Northern Fortress" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (from *[formen](/wiki/Formen "Formen")* = "north" and *[osto](/wiki/Osto "Osto")* = "fortress"). Its [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) cognate is *[Fornost](/wiki/Fornost)*, the name of a different city.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The precursor of Formenos is [Sirnúmen](/wiki/Sirn%C3%BAmen), a location which appears in an early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In this version, [Melko](/wiki/Melko) sows discord among the [Noldoli](/wiki/Noldoli), who are then ordered by [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) to leave their home of [Kôr](/wiki/K%C3%B4r) and build a new dwelling elsewhere in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). Their new dwelling, Sirnúmen, is later plundered by Melko, who kills Fëanor's father, [Bruithwir](/wiki/Bruithwir), and steals the gems of the Noldoli, including the Silmarils.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
