<i><b>findë</b></i> means "hair", or mor specifically "a tress or plait of hair", in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) *[phini-](/wiki/Phini- "Phini-")* ("a single hair") and *[phindē](/wiki/Phind%C4%93 "Phindē")* ("a tress").<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
