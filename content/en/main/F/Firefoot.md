**Firefoot** was the horse ridden by [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) on the journey from [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) to [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep). During the journey, Éomer invited [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) to be a passenger on Firefoot's back with him, a proposition which Gimli accepted with great pleasure.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
