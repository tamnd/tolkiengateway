**Firefoot** was the horse ridden by [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) on the journey from [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) to [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md). During the journey, Éomer invited [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) to be a passenger on Firefoot's back with him, a proposition which Gimli accepted with great pleasure.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
