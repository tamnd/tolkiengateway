**First Age 538** (abbreviated to **F.A. 538**) is the 538th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Third Kinslaying](/wiki/Third_Kinslaying): the remaining [sons](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor") of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), desiring the [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril), attack the remnants of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) and [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) at the [New Havens](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion "Mouths of Sirion").<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) and [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) send aid to the Havens. They arrive too late and the people are defeated; the refugees move to the [Isle of Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar).<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Death of [Amrod](/wiki/Amrod) and [Amras](/wiki/Amras).<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and [Elros](/wiki/Elros) are taken captive by [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor), but Maglor comes to love the boys and fosters them.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing), bearing the Silmaril, casts herself into [the sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer"). [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) bears her up in the likeness of a swan and she finds [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup>
