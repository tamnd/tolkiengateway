**Frumgar** was a chieftain of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) who led his people from the middle to the northern [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-Frumgar_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Frumgar-1">[1]</a></sup> It is possible that Frumgar was the fourth [Lord of the Éothéod](/wiki/Lord_of_the_%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) and the grandson of [Forthwini](/wiki/Forthwini).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Before [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1977](/wiki/Third_Age_1977 "Third Age 1977") the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) lived in the middle vales of Anduin between the [Carrock](/wiki/Carrock) and the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields) with most of them living on the west side of the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> At that time many men lived in the middle vales of Anduin and the shadow of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) was lengthening.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975")<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-5">[5]</a></sup> the evil [Kingdom of Angmar](/wiki/Angmar "Angmar") was [destroyed](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost "Battle of Fornost"). The Éothéod heard of the destruction of Angmar,<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-4">[4]</a></sup> probably because some of them had fought with their horses with the expeditionary force from [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) against the troops from Angmar<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975")<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-5">[5]</a></sup>. As a consequence, Frumgar, then the chieftain of the Éothéod, led the Éothéod,<sup id="cite_ref-Frumgar_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Frumgar-1">[1]</a></sup> who sought more room, to the North and drove away the remnants of the people of Angmar on the east side of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains)<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-4">[4]</a></sup> in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1977](/wiki/Third_Age_1977 "Third Age 1977")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>.

The Éothéod settled in the land between the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) on the west, the [Forest River](/wiki/Forest_River) in the east and the confluence of the rivers [Greylin](/wiki/Greylin) and [Langwell](/wiki/Langwell) in the south.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The only fortified *burg* of the Éothéod was located at the confluence of the Greylin and the Langwell.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> It is possible that this *burg* was the settlement known as [Framsburg](/wiki/Framsburg)<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> and that it was named after Frumgar's son, [Fram](/wiki/Fram).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Frumgar was remembered in the songs of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim).<sup id="cite_ref-Frumgar_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Frumgar-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Frumgar* means "chieftain", "leader", "prince" or "patriarch", literally "first spear" in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

It its a compound of *frum* ("first")<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> and *gár* ("spear")<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2020: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Frumgar appears in a flashback narrating the history of the <a href="/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od" title="Éothéod">Éothéod</a>. He is shown befriending <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">dwarves</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wizards" title="Wizards">Wizards</a> at a young age, and his decision to move his people to the Wells of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Langflood" title="Langflood">Langflood</a> is partially motivated by his wife being killed in an <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orc</a> raid. It was he who gave <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> his northern name by calling him Wandalb, &#34;the Elf of the Wand&#34;.</dd></dl>
