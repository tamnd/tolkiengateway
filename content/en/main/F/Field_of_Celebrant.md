The **Field of Celebrant** lay between the Rivers [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) and [Limlight](/wiki/Limlight), to the southeast of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> this was the site of the decisive [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant), at which [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) the Young rode from the north to the aid of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
