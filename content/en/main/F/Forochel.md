**Forochel** was one of the regions of the [Northern Waste](/wiki/Northern_Waste). It was the name of the icy tundra in the northern parts of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and especially of the great [icebay](/wiki/Icebay_of_Forochel "Icebay of Forochel") and the lands that surrounded it, such as the [Cape of Forochel](/wiki/Cape_of_Forochel).

It was inhabited by the [Lossoth](/wiki/Lossoth), a hardy tribe of [Men](/wiki/Men) descended from the [Forodwaith](</wiki/Forodwaith_(people)> "Forodwaith (people)"). Most Men of the region lived on the Cape of Forochel near the Great Sea of [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) but some built their villages on the shores of the Icebay itself.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Forochel* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. It is a compound of *[forod](/wiki/Forod "Forod")* ("north") and *khel* ("ice").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Therefore it means "Northern Ice".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Forochel is one of the regions of the game, accessed via the northern end of <a href="/wiki/Evendim" title="Evendim">Evendim</a>. The language and culture of its people, the Lossoth, are modeled after the Finnish. An <i>aurora borealis</i> effect can be seen in the sky. Prior to the release of Western <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>, it was the only region in the game world where the ocean was visible, but players will freeze to death if attempting to swim in it.</dd>
<dd>The capital of the region is <b><a href="/wiki/S%C3%BBri-kyl%C3%A4" title="Sûri-kylä">Sûri-kylä</a></b>, said to mean &#34;Large Village&#34;; at the westernmost point of the settlement stands the Great Lodge with the Council of Elders and the Lossoth leader Yrjänä.</dd></dl>
