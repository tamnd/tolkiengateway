The **flame of Anor** is a mysterious element or power that [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) claimed to wield during his battle with [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The specific nature of this flame is obscure, as it is not referenced directly elsewhere, but there is contextual evidence to support some inferences.

*[Anor](/wiki/Anor)* is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for the Sun, and so literally the "flame of Anor" would be the light of the Sun,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> which originated in the fiery fruit of [Laurelin](/wiki/Laurelin), one of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor") of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Alternatively, the "flame of Anor" may refer to Gandalf's possession of [Narya](/wiki/Narya) the Great, whose stone was red as fire.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The expression might metaphorically refer to some power wielded by the [Wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards"), as a [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar") and servant of the [Flame Imperishable](/wiki/Flame_Imperishable); with the pure light of the Sun standing as the antithesis to the "dark fire" of the [fallen](/wiki/%C3%9Amaiar "Úmaiar") [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs").
