**First Age 375** (abbreviated to **F.A. 375**) is the 375th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Orcs besiege the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md) in [Thargelion](../T/Thargelion.md). In the [ensuing battle](../B/Battle_of_the_Gelion-Ascar_Stockade~66779.md), the orcs are defeated by [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md)'s people.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Haldad](../H/Haldad.md) and [Haldar](../H/Haldar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Caranthir suggested to [Haleth](../H/Haleth.md), daughter of [Haldad](../H/Haldad.md) and leader of the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md), that her people move to his lands. She thanks Caranthir but leads her people westwards.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup>
