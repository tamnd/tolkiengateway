The **First Hall** of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") was on the eastern side of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), above the [Dimrill Dale](/wiki/Dimrill_Dale) and apparently the first hall built by [Durin I](/wiki/Durin_I). The [Great Gates](/wiki/Great_Gates) of Moria led directly into the First Hall, and from this space there was a wide passage and stairs leading to the [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm), the [Second Hall](/wiki/Second_Hall), and the rest of the city. In contrast to much of Khazad-dûm, the First Hall was brightly lit due to high windows on the eastern side.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Balin's folk](/wiki/Balin%27s_folk) managed to claim the First Hall after driving out Orcs from the [Great Gate](/wiki/Great_Gate) and the guard room. [Flói](/wiki/Fl%C3%B3i) killed an orc chieftain during one of those skirmishes, before being killed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the fall of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), the remaining members of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) ran through the First Hall to escape from Moria on [January 15](/wiki/January_15) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2008: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The <a href="/wiki/Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm" title="Bridge of Khazad-dûm">Bridge of Khazad-dûm</a> is now broken, so to access the First Hall one has to make a long and dangerous journey north of the chasm.</dd></dl>

<b>2024: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Moria_-_Through_the_Doors_of_Durin" title="Moria - Through the Doors of Durin">Moria: Through the Doors of Durin</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Divided into an Outer Hall and an Inner Hall by a stone partition known as Durin&#39;s Door, the inner shrine held the original coronation throne of the Longbeards and ancient wall carvings carved by Durin the Deathless himself. When Moria fell, <a href="/wiki/N%C3%A1in_I" title="Náin I">Náin I</a> ordered the craftsmen to cut the carvings from the walls and hide them to prevent their destruction. Despite never being crowned, Náin&#39;s name was scratched onto the slabs of royal names, though as the fifth of his name, a clue towards the lost Fifth Hall.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>
