**First Age 272** (abbreviated to **F.A. 272**) is the 272nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- *Possible* birth of [Finduilas](Finduilas.md) in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Notes {#Notes}
