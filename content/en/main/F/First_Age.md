The **First Age** was the longest of the recorded [ages of Middle-earth](/wiki/Ages "Ages"),<sup id="cite_ref-PM_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-2">[2]</a></sup> and its events were chronicled between the coming of the Elder [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar) into the world and the [Downfall of Angband](/wiki/War_of_Wrath "War of Wrath").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Commonly known as the **[Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days)**,<sup id="cite_ref-SilmIndex_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SilmIndex-4">[4]</a></sup> the First Age thus saw the creation of the [races](/wiki/Category:Races "Category:Races") of [Arda](/wiki/Arda), their flourishing both in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) and [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), their feats against [Dark Lord](/wiki/Dark_Lord) [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) and his eventual overthrow by the combined armies of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), [Elves](/wiki/Elves), and [Edain](/wiki/Edain).<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor) kept [track of time](/wiki/Time "Time") for most of the First Age. With the creation of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and [Moon](/wiki/Moon), however, the time became measured with the *[coranar](/wiki/Coranar "Coranar")*, a much shorter unit of time within the [Years of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun), which replaced long [Years of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees).<sup id="cite_ref-Atani_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Atani-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Definition {#Definition}

According to the Valinorëan [loremasters](/wiki/Lambengolmor "Lambengolmor"), the First Age ended precisely when the Sun first rose in [heaven](/wiki/Ilmen "Ilmen").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AV2_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AV2-9">[9]</a></sup> However, for the loremasters of the [Noldorin Exiles](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor"), the Age also included the next six centuries of [their War](/wiki/Battles_of_Beleriand "Battles of Beleriand") against Morgoth until his final defeat.<sup id="cite_ref-AB_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AB-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Thus, according to the latter definition, the First Age lasted 4,902 solar years in total, out of which 4,312 solar years had passed between the Awakening of the Elves and the first sunrise, since one [Valian Year](/wiki/Valian_Years "Valian Years") was equal to 9.582 Years of the Sun.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Years of the Trees {#Years_of_the_Trees}

The First Age began with the [Awakening of the Elves](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves),<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Nature_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nature-14">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Guide_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guide-15">[15]</a></sup> which occurred in [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1050](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1050 "Year of the Trees 1050").<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> Aimed to protect them, the Valar [battled](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers "Battle of the Powers") against [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth"), besieged his fortress [Utumno](/wiki/Utumno) and defeated his servants. Then the Elves [were invited](/wiki/Great_Journey "Great Journey") by the Valar to the West, and while [some](/wiki/Avari "Avari") remained in far eastern [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), the [others](/wiki/Eldar "Eldar") answered the summon and dwelt in peace and bliss of the Trees in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) for a three ages of the [Chaining of Melkor](/wiki/Chaining_of_Melkor) until the latter was released from his bondage.<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-18">[18]</a></sup> In the end, he managed to sow an awful unrest among the Elves and kill the Two Trees alongside [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant), and then fled back to [Angband](/wiki/Angband).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> Seeking revenge, the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") Exiles led by [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) persued their Enemy and thus came to north-western Middle-earth at the end of the Age.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Elves_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elves-21">[21]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Peoples_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Peoples-22">[22]</a></sup>

Soon after the arrival of the Noldor the [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](/wiki/Dagor-nuin-Giliath) (*Battle under the Stars*, so named because it was fought before the rising of the Sun) occurred, and Fëanor was killed at his last stand against the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs). It was considered the second battle in the Wars of Beleriand, after [one that](/wiki/First_Battle "First Battle") took place towards the end of the Years of the Trees.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-23">[23]</a></sup>

Meanwhile, the Valar managed to restore the last silver flower and golden fruit of the Two Trees, from which they created the Moon and the Sun to give a new light to the world, and with the first sunrise, the [Awakening of Men](/wiki/Awakening_of_Men), the Younger Children of Ilúvatar, occurred in [Hildórien](/wiki/Hild%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup> Like the Eldar before them, they started to migrate to the [West](/wiki/Westlands "Westlands"), but most remained in the [East](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn "Rhûn") or either in [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) and [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).<sup id="cite_ref-Atani_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Atani-5">[5]</a></sup>

The following events of the last six centuries of the First Age were centered around a series of wars waged by those Noldor, the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) and the [Three Houses](/wiki/Three_Houses) of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) (their [Mannish](/wiki/Men "Men") allies who entered Beleriand three hundred years after the Awakening), against the armies of Angband. The [Elves in Beleriand](/wiki/Moriquendi "Moriquendi") sought merely to exist, and Morgoth had little to do with them, but the Noldor, on the other hand, had come with the express purpose to defeat Morgoth and, particularly the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor), retrieve the treasured [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils) stolen by him.

Eighty-eight years after the first battle, Morgoth again attacked the Noldor, and again without any success. The [Dagor Aglareb](/wiki/Dagor_Aglareb) (*Glorious Battle*) made the Noldor so bold as to [besiege Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband "Siege of Angband") for almost four hundred years.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-23">[23]</a></sup> Within the first century since the arrival of the Noldor, the settlements of [Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar) and [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest), [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), and [Minas Tirith](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand)> "Minas Tirith (Beleriand)") were built across all Beleriand, and both the Noldor and the Sindar were prospering under the young Sun.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup>

It was during the [Long Peace](/wiki/Long_Peace), in the fourth century after the arrival of the Noldor, when some Men (the [Edain](/wiki/Edain)) reached Beleriand eager to find the "[Gods](/wiki/Gods)" as they believed, and entered the service of the Eldar. The [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Brethil "Brethil") was given to the [House of Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth) and [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) to the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador).<sup id="cite_ref-Atani_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Atani-5">[5]</a></sup>

However, the Siege of Angband was of limited effectiveness since the northern side of Angband was on the opposite side of the [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains) and thus unapproachable. Indeed in the fifth century, Morgoth poured rivers of fire out of Angband, ruining the besieging Noldorin armies. The green plain of [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen) had been permanently laid waste by the rivers of fire, and was now called *[Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith)*, the Choking Dust; and the highlands of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), which had been inhabited by the Edain of the [folk of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor "House of Bëor"), were made inhospitable. The [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) (*Battle of Sudden Flame*) began, and the Noldor eventually mustered a defence, but their losses were severe.<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[26]</a></sup> It was in the years following that battle when the Man [Beren](/wiki/Beren) rescued a Silmaril for the [Elven King](/wiki/King_of_Doriath "King of Doriath") [Elu Thingol](/wiki/Elu_Thingol).<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[27]</a></sup>

Later the Noldor initiated a battle for a first time. They massed an army composed of Elves, Edain, [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), and the [Easterling](</wiki/Easterlings_(First_Age)> "Easterlings (First Age)") houses of [Bór](/wiki/B%C3%B3r) and [Ulfang](/wiki/Ulfang) allied to the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor). The Elves and their allies advanced very close to Angband, but Morgoth's trickery had upset their battle plan since the folk of Ulfang proved treacherous. Τhe battle became known thereafter as [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad) (*Unnumbered Tears*) from the destruction of the Elves' last hope of victory. The land of Hithlum was lost, the Sons of Fëanor were largely scattered, and the peoples of Beleriand had been decimated. [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of Morgoth made a [heap of the Elven and Mannish dead](/wiki/Hill_of_the_Slain "Hill of the Slain") in the centre of Anfauglith. Realms such as the [Falas](/wiki/Falas), Nargothrond, and Gondolin were later destroyed one by one. The [Havens of Sirion](/wiki/Havens_of_Sirion) were constructed as a hidden refuge by [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan).<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[28]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Alan_Lee_-_Ruin_of_Angband.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/8/82/Alan_Lee_-_Ruin_of_Angband.jpg/450px-Alan_Lee_-_Ruin_of_Angband.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="450" height="770" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/8/82/Alan_Lee_-_Ruin_of_Angband.jpg/675px-Alan_Lee_-_Ruin_of_Angband.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/8/82/Alan_Lee_-_Ruin_of_Angband.jpg/900px-Alan_Lee_-_Ruin_of_Angband.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1022" data-file-height="1748"/></a><figcaption><i>Ruin of Angband</i>, by <a href="/wiki/Alan_Lee" title="Alan Lee">Alan Lee</a></figcaption></figure>

At last, the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) took place after [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) sailed to Valinor and persuaded the Valar to help those whom they had forsaken. The Valar gathered the [Host of the West](/wiki/Host_of_the_Valar "Host of the Valar") composed of the [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar), [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar), and those Noldor who had stayed in Valinor. The [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) refused their aid, due to an [old offense](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB "Kinslaying at Alqualondë") dealt them by the Noldor of Beleriand, but consented to ferry the armies of Valinor in their famous ships. This battle marked the first appearance of the winged dragons, most notably [Ancalagon the Black](/wiki/Ancalagon "Ancalagon"), but the Valar had the day. Morgoth's realm of tyranny was obliterated, and the Dark Lord himself captured and cast out of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) into the [Timeless Void](/wiki/Void "Void") in [Y.S.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") [590](/wiki/First_Age_590 "First Age 590"), the last year of the First Age.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29">[29]</a></sup> However, the entire landmass of Beleriand had been also destroyed and sunk under the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") in the heat of battle.<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">[30]</a></sup>

Events that marked the end of the Age were the breaking of the [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim),<sup id="cite_ref-Years_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-1">[1]</a></sup> the loss of the Silmarils, and the choice of the [Half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven).<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31">[31]</a></sup>

## Terminology {#Terminology}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395753"/><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The title of this second part, <i>The War of the Jewels</i>, is an expression that my father often used of the <b>last</b> six centuries of the First Age: the history of Beleriand after the return of Morgoth to Middle-earth and the coming of the Noldor, until its end.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien" title="Christopher Tolkien">Christopher Tolkien</a><sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32">[32]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395753"/></div>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

In the [Appendix B](/wiki/Appendix_B) and the *[Quenta Silmarillion](/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion)* [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) never gives dates of the First Age. In works such as *[The Annals of Aman](/wiki/The_Annals_of_Aman)* and *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)*, Tolkien measured the First Age with the Years of the Trees (YT),<sup id="cite_ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33">[33]</a></sup> or the Valian Years (VY) and then Years of the Sun (YS),<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34">[34]</a></sup> respectively.

[Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) among other scholars attempted to chronicle the First Age; by convention these sources use the Years of the Sun as "First Age" keeping a format similar to Appendix B. For example, the [twentieth Year of the Sun](/wiki/First_Age_20 "First Age 20") is referred to as I 20 or F.A. 20, and the [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway) also uses this format. It should be however pointed out that according to Foster's own remark the definition YS 1 would be more accurate than FA 1,<sup id="cite_ref-Foster_35-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foster-35">[35]</a></sup> and that his text itself does not clarify when exactly the First Age began.<sup id="cite_ref-Guide_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guide-15">[15]</a></sup>

Unfortunately, this convention creates the widespread [misconception](/wiki/Misconceptions#Beginning_of_the_First_Age "Misconceptions") that F.A. 1 was the first year of the First Age, or its beginning, marked as such by the first sunrise. This led to the [fanonical](/wiki/Fanon "Fanon") term "Ages of the Sun" and the misconception that the Ages measured from the first rising of the Sun. According to that definition, the First Age 'proper' followed the Years of the Trees and lasted only c. 600 years until the [beginning](/wiki/Second_Age_1 "Second Age 1") of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).

However, in the [Appendix F](/wiki/Appendix_F) to the *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* J.R.R. Tolkien himself wrote explicitly that the [Return of the Noldor](/wiki/Return_of_the_Noldor) to Middle-earth, which resulted from their Exile, took place at at the <i><b>end</b></i> of the First Age, thus meaning that the year labeled F.A. 1 was in fact placed near the end of that long Age;<sup id="cite_ref-Elves_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elves-21">[21]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Peoples_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Peoples-22">[22]</a></sup> in the same Appendix, there is also mentioned that the Orcs were first bred by Morgoth in the Elder Days,<sup id="cite_ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36">[36]</a></sup> i.e. in the First Age,<sup id="cite_ref-SilmIndex_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SilmIndex-4">[4]</a></sup> but this happened before Utumno was broken and demolished.<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-18">[18]</a></sup>

Furthermore, in *The Annals of Aman* Tolkien mentioned that the First Age ([of the Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Ages_of_the_Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar "Ages of the Children of Ilúvatar")) began when the Elves awoke at Cuiviénen in [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1050](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1050 "Year of the Trees 1050"),<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-13">[13]</a></sup> while in the [twelfth volume](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth "The Peoples of Middle-earth") of *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* he also pointed out that the First Age was the <i><b>longest</b></i> (of all Ages),<sup id="cite_ref-PM_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-2">[2]</a></sup> as well as in his [Letter 131](/wiki/Letter_131) to [Milton Waldman](/wiki/Milton_Waldman), where Tolkien described the First Age as *long*.<sup id="cite_ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37">[37]</a></sup>

Additionally, in *[The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth)* Tolkien also described the First Age beginning with the Awakening of the Elves and ending with the Downfall of Angband.<sup id="cite_ref-Nature_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nature-14">[14]</a></sup> Therefore, all of this signifies that the term 'First Age' is required to be expanded <i><b>long before</b></i> the first rising of the Sun, and indeed Robert Foster in his *[Complete Guide of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth "The Complete Guide to Middle-earth")* undoubtedly defined events of the Years of the Trees following the Awakening of the Elves as an integral part of the First Age,<sup id="cite_ref-Foster_35-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foster-35">[35]</a></sup> as well as both [J.E.A. Tyler](/wiki/J.E.A._Tyler) in his *[Complete Tolkien Companion](/wiki/The_Complete_Tolkien_Companion "The Complete Tolkien Companion")* and [Paul H. Kocher](/wiki/Paul_H._Kocher) in his *[Reader's Guide to The Silmarillion](/wiki/A_Reader%27s_Guide_to_The_Silmarillion "A Reader's Guide to The Silmarillion")*.<sup id="cite_ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38">[38]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39">[39]</a></sup> [Karen Wynn Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad) also attributed the events before the first rising of the Sun to the First Age in her *[Atlas of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth "The Atlas of Middle-earth")*.<sup id="cite_ref-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-40">[40]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41">[note 1]</a></sup>

It is quite possible that the term "Age of the Sun" was in fact "invented" by [David Day](/wiki/David_Day),<sup id="cite_ref-42" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-42">[41]</a></sup> a Canadian author whose books are widely criticized for their inaccuracies and misinterpretations of [J.R.R. Tolkien's mythology](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").<sup id="cite_ref-43" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-43">[42]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-44" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-44">[43]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-45" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-45">[44]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The "very archaic" [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name for this Age that "came" to [Arundel Lowdham](/wiki/Alwin_Arundel_Lowdham "Alwin Arundel Lowdham") was *frumaeldi*.<sup id="cite_ref-46" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-46">[45]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier texts, such as *[The Later Annals of Valinor](/wiki/The_Later_Annals_of_Valinor)*, J.R.R. Tolkien referred to the 'First Age(s) of the World' rather than the 'First Age of the Children of Ilúvatar'. This variation had much earlier starting point, extending the First Age back to the creation of Arda,<sup id="cite_ref-AV2_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AV2-9">[9]</a></sup> but consistently ended with Morgoth's defeat in Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-AB_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AB-10">[10]</a></sup>
