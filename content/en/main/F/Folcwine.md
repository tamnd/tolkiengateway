**Folcwine** ([Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) [2830](../T/Third_Age_2830.md) – [2903](../T/Third_Age_2903.md), aged 73 years) was the fourteenth [King of Rohan](../K/King_of_Rohan.md).

## History {#History}

Folcwine was the son of [Folca](Folca.md) "The Hunter", and great-grandfather of [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md). Aided by [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), he drove out [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md) who occupied the area between [Adorn](../A/Adorn.md) and [Isen](../I/Isen.md) and recovered that area.

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2885](../T/Third_Age_2885.md) he also fulfilled the [Oath of Eorl](../O/Oath_of_Eorl.md) and sent many Rohirrim to the aid of Gondor against the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md). However, his twin sons [Folcred](Folcred.md) and [Fastred](<Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine).md>) were both killed in that war, and Folcwine was succeeded by his youngest child, [Fengel](Fengel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Folcwine* is an [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name. It means "friend of the people" and is composed of *folc* ("folk" or "people") and *wine* ("friend").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
