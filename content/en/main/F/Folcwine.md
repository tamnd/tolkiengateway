**Folcwine** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [2830](/wiki/Third_Age_2830 "Third Age 2830") – [2903](/wiki/Third_Age_2903 "Third Age 2903"), aged 73 years) was the fourteenth [King of Rohan](/wiki/King_of_Rohan).

## History {#History}

Folcwine was the son of [Folca](/wiki/Folca) "The Hunter", and great-grandfather of [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden). Aided by [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), he drove out [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) who occupied the area between [Adorn](/wiki/Adorn) and [Isen](/wiki/Isen) and recovered that area.

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2885](/wiki/Third_Age_2885 "Third Age 2885") he also fulfilled the [Oath of Eorl](/wiki/Oath_of_Eorl) and sent many Rohirrim to the aid of Gondor against the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim). However, his twin sons [Folcred](/wiki/Folcred) and [Fastred](</wiki/Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine)> "Fastred (son of Folcwine)") were both killed in that war, and Folcwine was succeeded by his youngest child, [Fengel](/wiki/Fengel).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Folcwine* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name. It means "friend of the people" and is composed of *folc* ("folk" or "people") and *wine* ("friend").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
