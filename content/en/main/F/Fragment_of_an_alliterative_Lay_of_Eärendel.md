[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) attempted to write a **Lay of Eärendel**, resulting in a mere fragment included in the chapter "[Poems Early Abandoned](/wiki/Poems_Early_Abandoned)" in *[The Lays of Beleriand](/wiki/The_Lays_of_Beleriand)*. It was written and left unfinished during his time at the [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds), probably in [1925](/wiki/1925). It has no title and does not extend far enough to make clear what could be its subject, but at the end of the text Tolkien wrote in different scripts 'Earendel', 'Earendel son of Fengel', 'Earendel Fengelsson'; so [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) states that this poem was the beginning of a *Lay of Eärendel*.<sup id="cite_ref-LB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LB-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Analysis {#Analysis}

Along with *[The Flight of the Noldoli](/wiki/The_Flight_of_the_Noldoli_from_Valinor "The Flight of the Noldoli from Valinor")* and *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*, this was other piece of alliterative verse concerned with the matter of the *[Lost Tales](/wiki/Lost_Tales)*, but this is in the first stage of composition and is exceedingly rough. It narrates quickly the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), the escape of the fugitives down the [secret tunnel](/wiki/Idril%27s_secret_way "Idril's secret way") and the flight at [Cristhorn](/wiki/Cristhorn), ending with the long wandering in the wilds thereafter. These deeds would serve as the introduction to the Lay. The only differences respect to "[The Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")" are: Cristhorn is not in the south of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), but in the north, and the exiles sought for the [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) during thirty moons.<sup id="cite_ref-LB_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LB-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Poem excerpt {#Poem_excerpt}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Lo! the flame of fire and fierce hatred<br/>
engulfed Gondolin and its glory fell,<br/>
its tapering towers and its tall rooftops<br/>
were laid all low, and its leaping fountains<br/>
made no music more on the mount of Gwareth,<br/>
and its whitehewn walls were whispering ash.
</p>
</div>
