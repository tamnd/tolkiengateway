**First Age 6** (abbreviated to **F.A. 6**) is the 6th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Following a visit from [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md), [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) allows the [House of Finarfin](../H/House_of_Finarfin.md) to enter [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) due to their close kinship with Thingol via Finarfin's wife, [Eärwen](../E/Eärwen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md), as the self-styled Lord of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), grants the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) permission to occupy [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) and [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md), as well as the lands to the east of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
