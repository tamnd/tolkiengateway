The **First [Shirriff](/wiki/Shirriff)** of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") was the head of the [Watch](/wiki/Watch). The office was attached to the [Mayoralty of Michel Delving](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving "Mayor of Michel Delving").<sup id="cite_ref-Ordering_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ordering-1">[1]</a></sup>

When [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins) briefly seized control of the Shire during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) he called himself the [Chief Shirriff](/wiki/Chief_Shirriff).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-2">[2]</a></sup>
