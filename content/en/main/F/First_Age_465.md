**First Age 465** (abbreviated to **F.A. 465**) is the 465th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Notable events in this year include:

## Spring {#Spring}

- [Beren](/wiki/Beren) hears [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) singing and awakes from his trance. They fall in love.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) commands [Beren](/wiki/Beren) to return with a [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) in exchange for [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien)'s hand in marriage. [Quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Autumn {#Autumn}

- [Beren](/wiki/Beren) departs [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) with [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) and ten companions (including [Edrahil](/wiki/Edrahil)). Finrod appoints [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) as regent.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Arriving at [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth), Beren, Finrod and companions are captured by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) leaves [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) seeking [Beren](/wiki/Beren); she encounters [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) who take her to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond). She escapes Nargothrond with the aid of [Huan](/wiki/Huan).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) and his ten companions in Tol-in-Gaurhoth.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Huan](/wiki/Huan) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) arrive at [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth). Huan kills [Draugluin](/wiki/Draugluin) and defeats wolf-Sauron in battle; Sauron flees in the form of a [vampire](/wiki/Vampires "Vampires") and goes to <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Taur-nu-Fuin" title="Taur-nu-Fuin">Taur-nu-Fuin</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) are banished from [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) and subsequently attack Beren and Lúthien. Huan betrays his master out of love for Lúthien. Beren leaves for [Angband](/wiki/Angband).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Unknown {#Unknown}

- [Brandir](/wiki/Brandir) is born.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
