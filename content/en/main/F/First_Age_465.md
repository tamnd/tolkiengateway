**First Age 465** (abbreviated to **F.A. 465**) is the 465th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Spring {#Spring}

- [Beren](../B/Beren.md) hears [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) singing and awakes from his trance. They fall in love.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) commands [Beren](../B/Beren.md) to return with a [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md) in exchange for [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md)'s hand in marriage. [Quest for the Silmaril](../Q/Quest_for_the_Silmaril.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Autumn {#Autumn}

- [Beren](../B/Beren.md) departs [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) with [Finrod](Finrod.md) and ten companions (including [Edrahil](../E/Edrahil.md)). Finrod appoints [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) as regent.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Arriving at [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](../T/Tol-in-Gaurhoth.md), Beren, Finrod and companions are captured by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) leaves [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) seeking [Beren](../B/Beren.md); she encounters [Celegorm](../C/Celegorm.md) and [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md) who take her to [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md). She escapes Nargothrond with the aid of [Huan](../H/Huan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Finrod](Finrod.md) and his ten companions in Tol-in-Gaurhoth.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Huan](../H/Huan.md) and [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) arrive at [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](../T/Tol-in-Gaurhoth.md). Huan kills [Draugluin](../D/Draugluin.md) and defeats wolf-Sauron in battle; Sauron flees in the form of a [vampire](../V/Vampires.md) and goes to <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../T/Taur-nu-Fuin.md" title="Taur-nu-Fuin">Taur-nu-Fuin</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Celegorm](../C/Celegorm.md) and [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md) are banished from [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) and subsequently attack Beren and Lúthien. Huan betrays his master out of love for Lúthien. Beren leaves for [Angband](../A/Angband.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Unknown {#Unknown}

- [Brandir](../B/Brandir.md) is born.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
