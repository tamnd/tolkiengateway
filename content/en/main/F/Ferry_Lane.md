The **Ferry Lane** was a short road that led from the [Causeway](../C/Causeway.md), the high-banked roadway west of the [Brandywine River](../B/Baranduin.md), down to the ferry landing opposite [Buck Hill](../B/Buck_Hill.md) in [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The lane was south of the town of [Stock](../S/Stock.md) and five or more miles north of [Farmer Maggot's](Farmer_Maggot.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Maggot%27s_Lane&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maggot&#39;s Lane (page does not exist)">lane</a>. The entrance to the lane was marked by two tall white posts.<sup id="cite_ref-Short_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Short-2">[2]</a></sup> The hundred yards of Ferry Lane were edged with large white-washed stones and ended at a broad wooden landing-stage for the ferry to Buckland.<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-3">[3]</a></sup>

It was at the entrance to Ferry Lane that [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) caught up with [Frodo Baggins](Frodo_Baggins.md) and the other hobbits after their ride from Farmer Maggot's home.<sup id="cite_ref-Short_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Short-2">[2]</a></sup> After crossing the river the hobbits saw a [Black Rider](../N/Nazgûl.md) that had followed them down Ferry Lane to the landing.<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-3">[3]</a></sup>
