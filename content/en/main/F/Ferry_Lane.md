The **Ferry Lane** was a short road that led from the [Causeway](/wiki/Causeway), the high-banked roadway west of the [Brandywine River](/wiki/Baranduin "Baranduin"), down to the ferry landing opposite [Buck Hill](/wiki/Buck_Hill) in [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The lane was south of the town of [Stock](/wiki/Stock) and five or more miles north of [Farmer Maggot's](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot "Farmer Maggot") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Maggot%27s_Lane&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maggot&#39;s Lane (page does not exist)">lane</a>. The entrance to the lane was marked by two tall white posts.<sup id="cite_ref-Short_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Short-2">[2]</a></sup> The hundred yards of Ferry Lane were edged with large white-washed stones and ended at a broad wooden landing-stage for the ferry to Buckland.<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-3">[3]</a></sup>

It was at the entrance to Ferry Lane that [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) caught up with [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and the other hobbits after their ride from Farmer Maggot's home.<sup id="cite_ref-Short_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Short-2">[2]</a></sup> After crossing the river the hobbits saw a [Black Rider](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") that had followed them down Ferry Lane to the landing.<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-3">[3]</a></sup>
