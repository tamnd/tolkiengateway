**First Age 316** (abbreviated to **F.A. 316**) is the 316th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Aredhel](../A/Aredhel.md) leaves [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). She gets separated from her escort in [Nan Dungortheb](../N/Nan_Dungortheb.md) and finds herself in the forest of [Nan Elmoth](../N/Nan_Elmoth.md). She encounters the dark elf [Eöl](../E/Eöl.md), who forces her to be his wife.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Beldir](../B/Beldir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
