**Fritz Möser** ([19 October](/wiki/19_October) [1932](/wiki/1932) - February [2013](/wiki/2013)) was a German artist.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Apparently [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) was aware of Möser's artwork, as he was seemingly interested in Möser creating illustrations for translations of his works in Germany.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
