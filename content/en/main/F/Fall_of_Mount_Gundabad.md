The **Fall of Mount Gundabad** took place shortly after [Eregion was destroyed](/wiki/Sack_of_Eregion "Sack of Eregion") (c. [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>).

After the doors of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm) were [shut](/wiki/Sack_of_Eregion "Sack of Eregion"), [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), reinforced and commanded by the servants of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), invaded [Mount Gundabad](/wiki/Mount_Gundabad) and drove the [Longbeard](/wiki/Longbeard) [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) from their revered site and dwelling. As a result, the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) were infested, and communication between [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm) and the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) was cut off for a time.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is not recorded how long the Orcs occupied Mount Gundabad. Mount Gundabad was one of the strongholds of the Orcs when a great force of Dwarves of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) and of the Houses of other [Fathers of the Dwarves](/wiki/Fathers_of_the_Dwarves) [sacked](/wiki/Sacking_of_Mount_Gundabad "Sacking of Mount Gundabad") it<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup> at a point in time between [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2793](/wiki/Third_Age_2793 "Third Age 2793")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2799](/wiki/Third_Age_2799 "Third Age 2799")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>.
