**First Age 53** (abbreviated to **F.A. 53**) is the 53rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- With the help of [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md), [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) travels up the [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md) and discovers the hidden valley of [Tumladen](../T/Tumladen.md). He plans to create the city of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), a city similar to [Tirion](../T/Tirion.md) upon [Túna](../T/Túna.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
