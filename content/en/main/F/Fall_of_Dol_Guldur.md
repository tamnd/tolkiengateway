The **Fall of Dol Guldur** was the epic conflict that culminated the [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) campaign of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

In [March](../M/March.md) of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), Sauron directed his armies in [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) to assail the [Elves of Lórien](../E/Elves_of_Lórien.md) and the [Woodland Realm](../W/Woodland_Realm.md) as part of his northern campaign in the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). These armies made three assaults across the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) against [Lórien](../L/Lórien.md). Although grievous harm was done to the borders of the forest, all these attacks were driven back for the valour of the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) was great and the power that lay in the woods was far too powerful to be overcome unless [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) had come there himself.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>

Upon the destruction of the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md) that same day, the [Shadow](../S/Shadow.md) of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) was lifted from the hearts of his enemies and fear and despair fell upon his servants and allies.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Battle {#Battle}

On [28 March](../_/28_March.md), [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) led the host of [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) across the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) on many boats and took Dol Guldur.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> After taking the fortress, [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) threw down its walls and laid bare its pits, cleansing the forest.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

On [April 6](../_/6_April.md) Celeborn and [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) met in the midst of the forest and renamed it [Eryn Lasgalen](../E/Eryn_Lasgalen.md), the "Wood of Green Leaves." Celeborn took the land south of [the Narrows](../T/The_Narrows.md) for himself and named it [East Lórien](../E/East_Lórien.md), while Thranduil took all the forest north of the [Mountains of Mirkwood](../M/Mountains_of_Mirkwood.md). They gave the middle of the wood to the [Beornings](../B/Beornings.md) and [Woodmen](../W/Woodmen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>
