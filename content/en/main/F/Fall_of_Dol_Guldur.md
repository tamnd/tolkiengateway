The **Fall of Dol Guldur** was the epic conflict that culminated the [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) campaign of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

In [March](/wiki/March) of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), Sauron directed his armies in [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) to assail the [Elves of Lórien](/wiki/Elves_of_L%C3%B3rien) and the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm) as part of his northern campaign in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). These armies made three assaults across the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) against [Lórien](/wiki/L%C3%B3rien). Although grievous harm was done to the borders of the forest, all these attacks were driven back for the valour of the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) was great and the power that lay in the woods was far too powerful to be overcome unless [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) had come there himself.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>

Upon the destruction of the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring) that same day, the [Shadow](/wiki/Shadow) of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) was lifted from the hearts of his enemies and fear and despair fell upon his servants and allies.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Battle {#Battle}

On [28 March](/wiki/28_March), [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) led the host of [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) across the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) on many boats and took Dol Guldur.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> After taking the fortress, [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) threw down its walls and laid bare its pits, cleansing the forest.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

On [April 6](/wiki/6_April "6 April") Celeborn and [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) met in the midst of the forest and renamed it [Eryn Lasgalen](/wiki/Eryn_Lasgalen), the "Wood of Green Leaves." Celeborn took the land south of [the Narrows](/wiki/The_Narrows "The Narrows") for himself and named it [East Lórien](/wiki/East_L%C3%B3rien), while Thranduil took all the forest north of the [Mountains of Mirkwood](/wiki/Mountains_of_Mirkwood). They gave the middle of the wood to the [Beornings](/wiki/Beornings) and [Woodmen](/wiki/Woodmen).<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>
