The **Fountains of the South** were fountains in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. They were located south of the city, along the [Way of Running Waters](/wiki/Way_of_Running_Waters). Passing them were the walls, where [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor)'s house was. The [people of the Fountain](/wiki/People_of_the_Fountain "People of the Fountain") came from the south of the city when summoned, so they probably dwelt nearby.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
