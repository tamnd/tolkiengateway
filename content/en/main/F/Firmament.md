The **Firmament** encompasses the highest spaces of the infinite [abode](/wiki/Timeless_Halls "Timeless Halls") of [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) and the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In both [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), the word for firmament is *[Menel](/wiki/Menel)*, though it has other senses too.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
