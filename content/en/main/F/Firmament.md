The **Firmament** encompasses the highest spaces of the infinite [abode](../T/Timeless_Halls.md) of [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md) and the [Ainur](../A/Ainur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In both [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), the word for firmament is *[Menel](../M/Menel.md)*, though it has other senses too.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
