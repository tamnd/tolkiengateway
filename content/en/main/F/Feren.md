**Feren** was an [Elf](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood "Elves of Mirkwood") of the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm) who appears in the [*Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"). He was played by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Simon_London&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Simon London (page does not exist)">Simon London</a>.

## History {#History}

When it is discovered that [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) have escaped from their prison cells via wine barrels, Feren blows a horn to alert the Elves guarding the gate that leads to the [River Running](/wiki/River_Running) and orders them to close the gate and bring the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) back into custody.

Later, Feren is sent by [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) to where the [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town) refugees are staying to summon [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) back to [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood), and to also inform [Tauriel](/wiki/Tauriel) that the elven-king has banished her from the realm.

He also serves as one of Thranduil's chief lieutenants in the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Feren is a Quenya word for "beech" or "beech-tree," which has the alternate spelling "fernë."<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
