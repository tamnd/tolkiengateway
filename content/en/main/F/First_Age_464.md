**First Age 464** (abbreviated to **F.A. 464**) is the 464th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Winter {#Winter}

- [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) over-runs [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) with [wraiths](../W/Wraiths.md), [werewolves](../W/Werewolves.md) and other foul creatures. With a price on his head, [Beren](../B/Beren.md) is forced to flee Dorthonion and comes to [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Spring {#Spring}

- Marriage of [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) and [Morwen](../M/Morwen.md). [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) is born later in the year.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Summer {#Summer}

- [Beren](../B/Beren.md) sees [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) for the first time and becomes enchanted.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>
