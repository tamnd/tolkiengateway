**Fourth Age 9** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 9** or **F.O.**) is the 9th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 9 was also known as **S.R. 1430** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Faramir Took](Faramir_Took_I.md), son of [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) and [Diamond of Long Cleeve](../D/Diamond_of_Long_Cleeve.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Merimac Brandybuck](../M/Merimac_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Death of [Vigo Boffin](../V/Vigo_Boffin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
