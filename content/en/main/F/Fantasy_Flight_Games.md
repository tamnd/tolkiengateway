**Fantasy Flight Games** is a tabletop game developer and publisher, founded in 1995 in Minnesota by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Christian_T._Petersen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Christian T. Petersen (page does not exist)">Christian T. Petersen</a>. FFG rose to fame with its *Twilight Imperium* board game in 1997 and in more recent years, acquired licenses to make games using the Star Wars, Marvel, Warhammer and Lord of the Rings properties.

It's a subdivision of Asmodee (since 2014)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and in turn, of the [Embracer Group](/wiki/Embracer_Group).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Games {#Games}

- [The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Card_Game)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> a "Living Card Game" (LCG) with over a hundred unique products released from 2011 until its retirement in 2024. Some of the refreshed content repackaged in 2023 is still in print.
- [The Lord of the Rings: Journeys in Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Journeys_in_Middle-earth)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> a cooperative app-based adventure board game, released in 2019 and still in print.
- [Lord of the Rings: The Confrontation](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Confrontation).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>, a two-player competitive game with Sauron facing the armies of the Free-peoples, originaly released in 2013. Currently out of print.
- [Lord of the Rings (board game)](</wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings_(board_game)>),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>, a revised version of the cooperative adventure game originally released by [Sophisticated Games](/wiki/Sophisticated_Games), published by Fantasy Flight in 2000 who also released an limited edition Anniversary Edition in 2020<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>. Currently out of print.
