The **Four Wise Clerks of Oxenford** were the authorities whom the author of *[Farmer Giles of Ham](Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md)* apparently consulted for the exact definition of a [Blunderbuss](../B/Blunderbuss.md), which was the weapon [Farmer Giles](Farmer_Giles.md) used to chase away the [giant](../T/The_Giant.md) who came to the village of [Ham](../H/Ham.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The definition of "blunderbuss" is directly taken from the *[Oxford English Dictionary](../O/Oxford_English_Dictionary.md)*, and the four clerks are presumably <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=James_A._H._Murray&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="James A. H. Murray (page does not exist)">James A. H. Murray</a>, [Henry Bradley](../H/Henry_Bradley.md), <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=W._A._Craigie&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="W. A. Craigie (page does not exist)">W. A. Craigie</a>, and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=C._T._Onions&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="C. T. Onions (page does not exist)">C. T. Onions</a>, the four editors of the dictionary.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
