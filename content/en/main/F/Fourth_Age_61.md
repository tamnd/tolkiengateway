**Fourth Age 61** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 61** or **F.O.**) is the 61st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 61 was also known as **S.R. 1482** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

<h2><span id="Midyear.27s_Day"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Midyear&#39;s_Day"><a class="mw-redirect" href="../M/Midyear&#39;s_Day.md" title="Midyear&#39;s Day">Midyear&#39;s Day</a></span></h2>

- [Rose Cotton](../R/Rose_Cotton.md), wife of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), dies.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

## [September](../S/September.md) {#September}

- **22** - Samwise Gamgee rides out from [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Later {#Later}

- Samwise comes to the [Tower Hills](../T/Tower_Hills.md) and gives the [Red Book of Westmarch](../R/Red_Book_of_Westmarch.md) to [Elanor](../E/Elanor_Gardner.md). Samwise leaves westwards. According to tradition of the [Fairbairn](Fairbairn_Family~66364.md), he went to the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) to pass over the [Sea](../B/Belegaer.md) and into the [Uttermost West](../A/Aman.md), last of the [Ring-bearers](../R/Ring-bearers.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>
