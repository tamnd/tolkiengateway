**Falathren** is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for [Westron](/wiki/Westron). It is derived from the word *[falas](/wiki/Falas "Falas")*, "shore", as its first speakers settled on the shores of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
