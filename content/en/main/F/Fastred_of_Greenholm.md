**Fastred of [Greenholm](/wiki/Greenholm)** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Fastred married [Elanor Gardner](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner) in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [30](/wiki/Fourth_Age_30 "Fourth Age 30") on the [Far Downs](/wiki/Far_Downs). Together they had a son, [Elfstan](/wiki/Elfstan_Fairbairn "Elfstan Fairbairn"),<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup> and a daughter [Fíriel](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel_Fairbairn "Fíriel Fairbairn").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

He was made [Warden of Westmarch](/wiki/Warden_of_Westmarch) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1462](/wiki/Fourth_Age_41 "Fourth Age 41") by [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took), and he relocated to the [Undertowers](/wiki/Undertowers) with his family.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fastred* in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) means "fast-rede, firm-counsel".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
