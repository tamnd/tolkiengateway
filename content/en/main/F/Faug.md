<i><b>faug</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word, meaning "gape".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In context, it also carries the meanings "gasping", "choking", and "thirst".

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Anfauglir](/wiki/Anfauglir)* - "Jaws of Thirst"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- *[Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith)* - "Gasping Dust"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- *[Dor-nu-Fauglith](/wiki/Dor-nu-Fauglith)* - "Land under Choking Ash"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
