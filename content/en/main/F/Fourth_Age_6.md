**Fourth Age 6** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 6** or **F.O.**) is the 6th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 6 was also known as **S.R. 1427** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:==[Mid-year's Day](../M/Mid-year's_Day.md)==

- [Will Whitfoot](../W/Will_Whitfoot.md) resigns as [Mayor of Michel Delving](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md). [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) is elected as Mayor for the first of seven terms.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Within this Year {#Within_this_Year}

- [King Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) issues and edict that [Men](../M/Men.md) are not to enter either [Drúadan Forest](../D/Drúadan_Forest.md) or [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). Elessar makes the Shire a Free Land under the protection of the [Northern Sceptre](../S/Sceptre_of_Annúminas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) marries [Diamond of Long Cleeve](../D/Diamond_of_Long_Cleeve.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Merry Gardner](../M/Merry_Gardner.md), fourth child and second son of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Rose Cotton](../R/Rose_Cotton.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
