**Fourth Age 63** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 63** or **F.O.**) is the 63rd [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway) follows [Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") usage in [Appendix B](/wiki/Appendix_B) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 63 was also known as **S.R. 1484** in [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning).

Notable events in this year include:

## Spring {#Spring}

- [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) receives a message from [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan") [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) that he wishes to see him.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Meriadoc takes counsel with [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took); they leave their belongings to their sons, Pippin takes with him a copy of the [Thain's Book](/wiki/Thain%27s_Book)<sup id="cite_ref-records_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-records-2">[2]</a></sup> and they depart [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") from [Sarn Ford](/wiki/Sarn_Ford) into the south.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Faramir Took I](/wiki/Faramir_Took_I) succeeds his father Peregrin Took as [Thain](/wiki/Thain) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Autumn {#Autumn}

- Death of [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan") [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-mark_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mark-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-note_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>
- [Elfwine](/wiki/Elfwine), son of Éomer, becomes the nineteenth King of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-mark_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mark-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Meriadoc Brandybuck and Peregrin Took then travel on to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup> Peregrin brings the copy of the [Thain's Book](/wiki/Thain%27s_Book).<sup id="cite_ref-records_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-records-2">[2]</a></sup>
