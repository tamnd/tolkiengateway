**Fourth Age 63** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 63** or **F.O.**) is the 63rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 63 was also known as **S.R. 1484** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Spring {#Spring}

- [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) receives a message from [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) that he wishes to see him.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Meriadoc takes counsel with [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md); they leave their belongings to their sons, Pippin takes with him a copy of the [Thain's Book](../T/Thain's_Book.md)<sup id="cite_ref-records_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-records-2">[2]</a></sup> and they depart [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) from [Sarn Ford](../S/Sarn_Ford.md) into the south.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Faramir Took I](Faramir_Took_I.md) succeeds his father Peregrin Took as [Thain](../T/Thain.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Autumn {#Autumn}

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-mark_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mark-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-note_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>
- [Elfwine](../E/Elfwine.md), son of Éomer, becomes the nineteenth King of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-mark_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mark-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Meriadoc Brandybuck and Peregrin Took then travel on to [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup> Peregrin brings the copy of the [Thain's Book](../T/Thain's_Book.md).<sup id="cite_ref-records_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-records-2">[2]</a></sup>
