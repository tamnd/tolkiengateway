**Felix Felton** ([August 12](/wiki/12_August "12 August"), [1911](/wiki/1911) - [October 21](/wiki/21_October "21 October"), [1972](/wiki/1972)) was an English actor. He played various parts in the [first radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)")*.*

## Roles {#Roles}

- [1954](/wiki/1954): *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth, Beorhthelm's Son](</wiki/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth,_Beorhthelm%27s_Son_(radio_play)> "The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth, Beorhthelm's Son (radio play)")* - Tidwald
- [1955](/wiki/1955): *[The Fellowship of the Ring](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)")*
    - Episode 3: "Aragorn" - Unspecified
    - Episode 4: "Many Meetings" - Unspecified
    - Episode 5: "The Moria Gate" - [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)
- [1956](/wiki/1956): *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)")*
    - Episode 1: "Fangorn" - [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs)
    - Episode 2: "Rohan and Isengard" - Voice of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)
    - Episode 5: "Minas Tirith and Mount Doom" - [The Black Captain](/wiki/Witch-king "Witch-king")
