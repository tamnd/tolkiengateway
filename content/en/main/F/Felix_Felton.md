**Felix Felton** ([August 12](../_/12_August.md), [1911](../_/1911.md) - [October 21](../_/21_October.md), [1972](../_/1972.md)) was an English actor. He played various parts in the [first radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>)*.*

## Roles {#Roles}

- [1954](../_/1954.md): *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth, Beorhthelm's Son](<../T/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth,_Beorhthelm's_Son_(radio_play).md>)* - Tidwald
- [1955](../_/1955.md): *[The Fellowship of the Ring](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>)*
    - Episode 3: "Aragorn" - Unspecified
    - Episode 4: "Many Meetings" - Unspecified
    - Episode 5: "The Moria Gate" - [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)
- [1956](../_/1956.md): *[The Lord of the Rings](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>)*
    - Episode 1: "Fangorn" - [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md)
    - Episode 2: "Rohan and Isengard" - Voice of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)
    - Episode 5: "Minas Tirith and Mount Doom" - [The Black Captain](../W/Witch-king.md)
