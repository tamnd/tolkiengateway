<i><b>formen</b></i> is a noun meaning "north" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Formen.gif"><img src="/w/images/1/16/Formen.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Formen* is also the name in Quenya of the tenth letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the second letter of the third grade or [Tyellë](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB), which contains "voiceless spirants" and it is the third letter in the labial or *p*-series of consonants, the *[Parmatéma](/wiki/Parmat%C3%A9ma)*. In all modes *Formen* represents **F**.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was one of the most widely known and used of all the Tengwar since it indicated "north" even in languages that used a very different term.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Fí</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the West-lands of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) the four cardinal directions were *[Númen](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen), [Hyarmen](/wiki/Hyarmen), [Rómen](/wiki/R%C3%B3men),* and *Formen*, and they were named in this order, beginning with (and facing) west. *Hyarmen* meant a left-hand region and *Formen* meant a right-hand region.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> Thus the word for "north" derives from the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=PHOR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="PHOR (page does not exist)">PHOR</a>, meaning "right hand", plus [MEN](/wiki/MEN) indicating direction.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
