**First Age 441** (abbreviated to **F.A. 441**) is the 441st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Handir](../H/Handir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
