**Fortinbras Took II** was the twenty-ninth [Thain](/wiki/Thain) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Fortinbras was the son of [Isumbras IV](/wiki/Isumbras_Took_IV "Isumbras Took IV").<sup id="cite_ref-Took_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-1">[1]</a></sup> He married [Lalia Clayhanger](/wiki/Lalia_Clayhanger) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1314](/wiki/Third_Age_2914 "Third Age 2914"), and had one son, [Ferumbras III](/wiki/Ferumbras_Took_III "Ferumbras Took III"), born two years later.<sup id="cite_ref-L214_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L214-2">[2]</a></sup>

Fortinbras became Thain after his father's death in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1339](/wiki/Third_Age_2939 "Third Age 2939").<sup id="cite_ref-Took_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-1">[1]</a></sup> It was during his rule when his cousin, [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), left for his [famous adventure](/wiki/Quest_for_Erebor "Quest for Erebor").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

He was Thain for 41 years, and after his death in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") at the age of 102 he was succeeded as Thain by his son Ferumbras; however, in accordance with Took tradition, Lalia continued to be head of the family until her death in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1402](/wiki/Third_Age_3002 "Third Age 3002").<sup id="cite_ref-L214_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L214-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortinbras" title="wikipedia:Fortinbras">Fortinbras</a> is the name of two characters in Shakespeare's <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamlet" title="wikipedia:Hamlet">Hamlet</a></i>. It is French for "strong in arms".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
