**First Age 495** (abbreviated to **F.A. 495**) is the 495th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Spring {#Spring}

- [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) leaves [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) and encounters [Gelmir and Arminas](../G/Gelmir_and_Arminas.md) who show Tuor the way to the [Gate of the Noldor](../G/Gate_of_the_Noldor.md). Tuor passes through [Drengist](../D/Drengist.md) and, coming to [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md), becomes the first [Man](../M/Man.md) to see [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md), the Great Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Gelmir and Arminas, messengers sent by [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md), come to [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) bearing a message from [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md). They advise [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) to close the gate and throw down the bridge. [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) rejects the advice.<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) attack the Forest of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md) and kill [Handir](../H/Handir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-2">[2]</a></sup> His son [Brandir](../B/Brandir.md) becomes the 6th [Halad](../H/Halad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Autumn {#Autumn}

- [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) unleashes an army on [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) led by [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md). After burning the [Talath Dirnen](../T/Talath_Dirnen.md), The [Battle of Tumhalad](../B/Battle_of_Tumhalad.md) takes place. The elves are defeated, and [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) and [Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md). [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) is protected by the [Dragon-helm](../D/Dragon-helm_of_Dor-lómin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GAS275_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GAS275-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Túrin returns to witness the [Fall of Nargothrond](Fall_of_Nargothrond.md). Nargothrond is completely destroyed and all remaining elves are killed or captured.<sup id="cite_ref-GAS275_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GAS275-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Túrin falls under Glaurung's spell and instead of protecting [Finduilas](Finduilas.md) he returns to [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) to seek [Morwen](../M/Morwen.md) and [Nienor](../N/Nienor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Finduilas is taken by orcs and is killed at the [Crossings of the Taeglin](../C/Crossings_of_the_Taeglin.md). The [Haladin](../H/House_of_Haleth.md) build a mound over her grave: [Haudh-en-Elleth](../H/Haudh-en-Elleth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- Glaurung takes possession of Nargothrond and its treasures.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## October {#October}

- **[11](../_/11_October.md)** - [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) encounters [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) who instructs him to deliver a message to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[12](../_/12_October.md)** - Tuor meets [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md) who agrees to take Tuor to Gondolin.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## November {#November}

- The [Fell Winter](<Fell_Winter_(First_Age).md>) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[8](../_/8_November.md)** - [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) and [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md) reach the [Pools of Ivrin](../P/Pools_of_Ivrin.md) and spot [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[17](../_/17_November.md)** - Tuor and Voronwë hide from [orcs](../O/Orcs.md) travelling on the [Old South Road](<../O/Old_South_Road_(Beleriand).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[18](../_/18_November.md)** - Tuor and Voronwë travel up the [Dry River](../D/Dry_River.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[19](../_/19_November.md)** - After passing the [Seven Gates](../S/Seven_Gates_of_Gondolin.md), Tuor and Voronwë come to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). Tuor delivers [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md)'s message to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) but Turgon decides to stay.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 1]</a></sup>
