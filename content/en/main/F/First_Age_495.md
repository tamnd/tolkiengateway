**First Age 495** (abbreviated to **F.A. 495**) is the 495th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Notable events in this year include:

## Spring {#Spring}

- [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) leaves [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) and encounters [Gelmir and Arminas](/wiki/Gelmir_and_Arminas) who show Tuor the way to the [Gate of the Noldor](/wiki/Gate_of_the_Noldor). Tuor passes through [Drengist](/wiki/Drengist) and, coming to [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast), becomes the first [Man](/wiki/Man) to see [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer), the Great Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Gelmir and Arminas, messengers sent by [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan), come to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) bearing a message from [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo). They advise [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) to close the gate and throw down the bridge. [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) rejects the advice.<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) attack the Forest of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) and kill [Handir](/wiki/Handir).<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-2">[2]</a></sup> His son [Brandir](/wiki/Brandir) becomes the 6th [Halad](/wiki/Halad).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Autumn {#Autumn}

- [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) unleashes an army on [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) led by [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung). After burning the [Talath Dirnen](/wiki/Talath_Dirnen), The [Battle of Tumhalad](/wiki/Battle_of_Tumhalad) takes place. The elves are defeated, and [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) and [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor). [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) is protected by the [Dragon-helm](/wiki/Dragon-helm_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min "Dragon-helm of Dor-lómin").<sup id="cite_ref-GAS275_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GAS275-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Túrin returns to witness the [Fall of Nargothrond](/wiki/Fall_of_Nargothrond). Nargothrond is completely destroyed and all remaining elves are killed or captured.<sup id="cite_ref-GAS275_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GAS275-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Túrin falls under Glaurung's spell and instead of protecting [Finduilas](/wiki/Finduilas) he returns to [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) to seek [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen) and [Nienor](/wiki/Nienor).<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Finduilas is taken by orcs and is killed at the [Crossings of the Taeglin](/wiki/Crossings_of_the_Taeglin). The [Haladin](/wiki/House_of_Haleth "House of Haleth") build a mound over her grave: [Haudh-en-Elleth](/wiki/Haudh-en-Elleth).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- Glaurung takes possession of Nargothrond and its treasures.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## October {#October}

- **[11](/wiki/11_October "11 October")** - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) encounters [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) who instructs him to deliver a message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[12](/wiki/12_October "12 October")** - Tuor meets [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) who agrees to take Tuor to Gondolin.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## November {#November}

- The [Fell Winter](</wiki/Fell_Winter_(First_Age)> "Fell Winter (First Age)") begins.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[8](/wiki/8_November "8 November")** - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) reach the [Pools of Ivrin](/wiki/Pools_of_Ivrin) and spot [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[17](/wiki/17_November "17 November")** - Tuor and Voronwë hide from [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") travelling on the [Old South Road](</wiki/Old_South_Road_(Beleriand)> "Old South Road (Beleriand)").<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[18](/wiki/18_November "18 November")** - Tuor and Voronwë travel up the [Dry River](/wiki/Dry_River).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[19](/wiki/19_November "19 November")** - After passing the [Seven Gates](/wiki/Seven_Gates_of_Gondolin "Seven Gates of Gondolin"), Tuor and Voronwë come to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin). Tuor delivers [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) but Turgon decides to stay.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 1]</a></sup>
