**First Age 496** (abbreviated to **F.A. 496**) is the 496th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Early {#Early}

- [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) returns to [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) and discovers that [Morwen](../M/Morwen.md) and [Nienor](../N/Nienor.md) had left for [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md). In a rage, Túrin kills [Brodda](../B/Brodda.md) the [Easterling](../E/Easterlings.md). [Aerin](../A/Aerin.md) dies in the fire in [Brodda's hall](../B/Brodda's_hall.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## March {#March}

- The [Fell Winter](<Fell_Winter_(First_Age).md>) ends.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## ? {#?}

- [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md), searching for [Finduilas](Finduilas.md), comes to the woods of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md) and discovers that [Finduilas](Finduilas.md) had been killed by orcs. He stays in Brethil and takes the name *Turambar*.<sup id="cite_ref-GA_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Mablung the Hunter](../M/Mablung.md) leads a party from [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) to [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md); it is waylaid by [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) and [Niënor Níniel](../N/Niënor.md) and [Morwen Eledhwen](../M/Morwen_Eledhwen.md) are lost in the wild.<sup id="cite_ref-GA_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Niënor falls under Glaurung's spell and forgets her identity. She is found by [Túrin Turambar](../T/Túrin.md) and comes to [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-4">[4]</a></sup>
