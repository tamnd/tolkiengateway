**Frank Frazetta** was an American comic artist and illustrator who explored science fiction and fantasy themes. Frazetta started his career as a comic strip artist in the 1940s and worked on the comic strip <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li%27l_Abner" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Li&#39;l Abner</a> while working at <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Capp" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Al Capp&#39;s</a> studio. He would go on to transition to creating illustrations for popular culture in the 1960s to include creating several illustrations for [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) and [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit) in the 1970s with his illustration of [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) vs the [Witch-King](/wiki/Witch-king "Witch-king") being the most famous. Frazetta also would work with director [Ralph Bakshi](/wiki/Ralph_Bakshi) as a producer for the rotoscoped sequences in Bakshi's animated movie <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_and_Ice_(1983_film)" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Fire and Ice</a>, a technology used for Baskhi's 1978 [animated adaptation](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)") of The Lord of the Rings.
