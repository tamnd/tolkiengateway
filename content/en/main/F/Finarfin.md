**Finarfin** was the youngest child and son of [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB) and [Indis](/wiki/Indis) and the third [King of the Noldor](/wiki/King_of_the_Noldor) in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). After the [Flight of the Noldor](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Noldor), he remained in [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion) and ruled the Noldor who remained in [Aman](/wiki/Aman).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Finarfin was born in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) in [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1230](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1230 "Year of the Trees 1230").<sup id="cite_ref-MR|P2j_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR|P2j-3">[3]</a></sup> He was the youngest of the five children of [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB), the [King of the Noldor](/wiki/King_of_the_Noldor). Finarfin's mother was [Indis](/wiki/Indis), Finwë's second wife.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Finarfin's full siblings were [Findis](/wiki/Findis), [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), and [Írimë](/wiki/%C3%8Drim%C3%AB) while his half-brother was the great [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") Lord [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor). Fëanor disapproved of his father's second marriage and had little love for Indis and her children, although Finarfin remained far from those disputes.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1280](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1280 "Year of the Trees 1280") Finarfin married [Eärwen](/wiki/E%C3%A4rwen), daughter of [Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB), King of the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-MR|P2j_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR|P2j-3">[3]</a></sup> They had four children: [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod), [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod), [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> Finarfin's mother was of the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar), and he inherited her fair hair, which he passed on to his children;<sup id="cite_ref-NM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup> this trait of the **[House of Finarfin](/wiki/House_of_Finarfin)** was unique among the normally dark haired Noldor.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

In [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1495](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1495 "Year of the Trees 1495").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup> [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") destroyed the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees),<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup> slaughtered Finarfin's father Finwë, and stole the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils) of Fëanor. Enraged, Fëanor came to the city of [Tirion upon Túna](/wiki/Tirion "Tirion") and, in a [fiery speech](/wiki/Speech_of_F%C3%ABanor "Speech of Fëanor"), convinced many of his kinsmen to leave Valinor for [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), to recover the Silmarils and defeat Morgoth. Though Finarfin argued for calm and to avoid making a rash decision, the Noldor were persuaded by Fëanor and all but a tithe [chose to leave Aman](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor") and return to Middle-earth.

The [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) followed Fëanor in groups, and Fingolfin and Finarfin led the last host. As such they did not participate in the [First Kinslaying](/wiki/First_Kinslaying) or know its true cause at the time. While they were travelling up the coast of [Araman](/wiki/Araman), the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos) appeared and pronounced the [Doom of the Noldor](/wiki/Doom_of_Mandos "Doom of Mandos"). Finarfin, dismayed by the prophecy and already contemplating return because of the tragedy of the Kinslaying of his wife's people at [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB), returned to Valinor with a small group of his people; his sons and daughter, though, would not forsake the sons of Fingolfin and went on.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup>

The name *Finarfin* is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) version of his [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) [father-name](/wiki/Father-name "Father-name") *Arafinwë*. The direct equivalence is <i><b>Arfin</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-PM|Finwe_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM|Finwe-15">[14]</a></sup> Like his brother Fingolfin, the Sindarized name of his father *fin* was added at the beginning.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup>

Finarfin's primary name being in Sindarin, a language indigenous to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), was a unique occurrence among the [High Elves](/wiki/High_Elves) who remained in the [Undying Lands](/wiki/Undying_Lands). Other such Amanya High Elves who stayed behind were primarily known by their [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) or [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) names. This Sindarin naming was a result of the special position his children had among the Exiles, especially by their being related to King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol), and their coming to be collectively known as *[Nost Finarfin](/wiki/Nost_Finarfin)* ("the children of Finarfin").<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[16]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

His [father-name](/wiki/Father-name "Father-name") was <i><b>Arafinwë</b></i>, with the stem *[ara](/wiki/Ara "Ara")-* ("noble") added to the name of his father.<sup id="cite_ref-PM|Finwe_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM|Finwe-15">[14]</a></sup>

His [mother-name](/wiki/Amiless%C3%AB "Amilessë") was <i><b><a href="/wiki/Ingoldo" title="Ingoldo">Ingoldo</a></b></i>, meaning "*the Noldo*, one eminent in the kindred", which also became the mother-name of [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod).<sup id="cite_ref-Ingoldo_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ingoldo-18">[17]</a></sup>

In earlier texts *Ingoldo* was the mother-name of Fingolfin, whereas Finarfin's was <i><b>Ingalaurë</b></i>, given to him due his Vanyarin golden hair,<sup id="cite_ref-Ingoldo_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ingoldo-18">[17]</a></sup> which was even more golden than the Vanyar. Tolkien considered many variants of this name including: *Lauringa*, *Lavaringa*, *Inglawar*, *Loaringo*, *Lawarindo*, *Ingilaur*, *Inglavar*, *Vanelaure*, *Lawaring*, *Laurin*, *Ingalaur*, *Ingwelaure*, and *Ingalaure*.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[18]</a></sup> The Sindarin version of this name was <i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Inglor" title="Inglor">Inglor</a></b></i>.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Finarfin was called *[Finrod](/wiki/Finrod)* in earlier versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), and his son was named <i><b><a href="/wiki/Inglor_Felagund" title="Inglor Felagund">Inglor Felagund</a></b></i>. As such he appears in the 1st edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* as *Finrod*. This was changed in later editions, but not all references to **Inglor** were removed (see [Gildor **Inglor**ion](/wiki/Gildor "Gildor")).

In some early works, his name is spelled *Finarphin*.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[19]</a></sup> An early version of [Appendix F](/wiki/Appendix_F), mentions the "royal house of Finarphir" which was corrected in later editions.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[20]</a></sup>
