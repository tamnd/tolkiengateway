**Fréa** ([Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) 2570 – 2659, aged 89 years) was the fourth of the Kings of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).

## History {#History}

Fréa was the great-grandson of [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl_the_Young.md), and succeeded the long-lived [Aldor](../A/Aldor.md). He was already seventy-five years old when he came to the throne. His fourteen-year reign is recorded as one of peace and prosperity.<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fréa* means "lord" or "master" in [Old English](../O/Old_English.md).<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The element *Fréa* appears also in the names [Fréaláf](Fréaláf.md) and [Fréawine](Fréawine.md).<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup>

Fréan is also the name that [Eriol](../_/Ælfwine.md) gave to the Valar in his Old English "translation" of the *[Quenta Silmarillion](../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
