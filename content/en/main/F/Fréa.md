**Fréa** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) 2570 – 2659, aged 89 years) was the fourth of the Kings of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

## History {#History}

Fréa was the great-grandson of [Eorl the Young](/wiki/Eorl_the_Young), and succeeded the long-lived [Aldor](/wiki/Aldor). He was already seventy-five years old when he came to the throne. His fourteen-year reign is recorded as one of peace and prosperity.<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fréa* means "lord" or "master" in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English).<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The element *Fréa* appears also in the names [Fréaláf](/wiki/Fr%C3%A9al%C3%A1f) and [Fréawine](/wiki/Fr%C3%A9awine).<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup>

Fréan is also the name that [Eriol](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine "Ælfwine") gave to the Valar in his Old English "translation" of the *[Quenta Silmarillion](/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
