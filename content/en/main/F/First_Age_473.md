**First Age 473** (abbreviated to **F.A. 473**) is the 473rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Early {#Early}

- [Nienor](../N/Nienor.md) is born.<sup id="cite_ref-GA254_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA254-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) arrives in [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) and is welcomed by [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) as a foster-son.<sup id="cite_ref-GA254_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA254-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Unknown {#Unknown}

- [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) sends a force to [Brithombar](../B/Brithombar.md) and [Eglarest](../E/Eglarest.md) which are [subsequently destroyed](Fall_of_the_Falas.md), and [Barad Nimras](../B/Barad_Nimras.md) is cast down.<sup id="cite_ref-S_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md), [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) and other survivors retreat to the [Isle of Balar](../I/Isle_of_Balar.md) but keep a base at the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Círdan builds seven ships and [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) sends them [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) to ask for help. All are lost save [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md), who is washed ashore in [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md).<sup id="cite_ref-S_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-2">[2]</a></sup>
