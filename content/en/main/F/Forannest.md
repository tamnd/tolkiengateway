**Forannest** was the northern gate of the [Rammas Echor](../R/Rammas_Echor.md), the great encircling wall of the [Pelennor Fields](../P/Pelennor_Fields.md).

## History {#History}

The Forannest was located in the north-west part of the Rammas Echor and the northern road from [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) ran through it joining the [Great West Road](../G/Great_West_Road.md).

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) passed through the gate which was under the command of [Ingold](../I/Ingold.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Several days later the forces of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) coming from the [Morannon](../M/Morannon.md), took [Cair Andros](../C/Cair_Andros.md) and breached the Rammas Echor, destroying the Forannest, and Ingold and his men were the last to retreat from the wall to Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Two days later the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) passed through Forannest and the breaches around it to take part in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is apparently [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) (which was later re-envisioned as [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)) and is glossed by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) as "North Entrance ..." with an obscure final word looking as "in".<sup id="cite_ref-wr354_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wr354-4">[4]</a></sup>

The name seems to be composed of the Noldorin/Sindarin elements *forn* ("north"), *[annon](../A/Annon.md)* ("gate") and *nest* which probably means "heart, core".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Earlier names of the gate that Tolkien rejected were *Fornest* and *Anfornest*.<sup id="cite_ref-wr354_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wr354-4">[4]</a></sup>

The name *Forannest* appears only in drafts from the 1940s and didn't make it to the final book *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Forannest is only referred to as the &#34;North-gate&#34; in the game. <a href="../I/Ingold.md" title="Ingold">Ingold</a> stands watch over the game and gives the player several task to scout and undermine the enemy before the Siege of Gondor beings in earnest.</dd></dl>
