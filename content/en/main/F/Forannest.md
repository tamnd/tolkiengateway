**Forannest** was the northern gate of the [Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor), the great encircling wall of the [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields).

## History {#History}

The Forannest was located in the north-west part of the Rammas Echor and the northern road from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) ran through it joining the [Great West Road](/wiki/Great_West_Road).

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) passed through the gate which was under the command of [Ingold](/wiki/Ingold).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Several days later the forces of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) coming from the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon), took [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros) and breached the Rammas Echor, destroying the Forannest, and Ingold and his men were the last to retreat from the wall to Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Two days later the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) passed through Forannest and the breaches around it to take part in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is apparently [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) (which was later re-envisioned as [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)) and is glossed by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") as "North Entrance ..." with an obscure final word looking as "in".<sup id="cite_ref-wr354_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wr354-4">[4]</a></sup>

The name seems to be composed of the Noldorin/Sindarin elements *forn* ("north"), *[annon](/wiki/Annon "Annon")* ("gate") and *nest* which probably means "heart, core".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Earlier names of the gate that Tolkien rejected were *Fornest* and *Anfornest*.<sup id="cite_ref-wr354_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wr354-4">[4]</a></sup>

The name *Forannest* appears only in drafts from the 1940s and didn't make it to the final book *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}
