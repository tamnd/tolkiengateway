**Fengel** was the father of [Indor](</wiki/Indor_(grandfather_of_Tuor)> "Indor (grandfather of Tuor)") and a great-grandfather of [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in a prose fragment following *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Fen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fen-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Fengel* seems to be in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), but its meaning is unclear.

An earlier name for the character was *Eär*.<sup id="cite_ref-Fen_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fen-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Early legendarium {#Early_legendarium}

In the early [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), the name *Fengel* was also used for two different characters at some point.

In *[The Lay of the Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Fall_of_Gondolin)*, from c. [1920](/wiki/1920), *Fengel* replaced *[Peleg](/wiki/Peleg)* as the name of Tuor's father.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

The name *Fengel* also appears as the name of Tuor himself, in a [fragment of an alliterative Lay of Eärendel](/wiki/Fragment_of_an_alliterative_Lay_of_E%C3%A4rendel "Fragment of an alliterative Lay of Eärendel") from c. [1925](/wiki/1925).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

### Later legendarium {#Later_legendarium}

In the later legendarium from *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, **[Fengel](/wiki/Fengel)** was the name of the fifteenth king of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
