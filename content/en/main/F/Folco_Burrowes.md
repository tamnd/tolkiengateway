**Folco Burrowes**<sup id="cite_ref-burrowes_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-burrowes-1">[1]</a></sup> was one of [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo)'s lawyers in *[The Return of the Shadow](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_Shadow)* (at that stage of the writing Frodo was called Bingo). Folco, along with Bingo's other lawyer, [Iago Grubb](/wiki/Iago_Grubb), eject [Sancho Proudfoot](/wiki/Sancho_Proudfoot) as he tries to excavate the pantry at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
