**Folco Burrowes**<sup id="cite_ref-burrowes_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-burrowes-1">[1]</a></sup> was one of [Frodo](Frodo.md)'s lawyers in *[The Return of the Shadow](../T/The_Return_of_the_Shadow.md)* (at that stage of the writing Frodo was called Bingo). Folco, along with Bingo's other lawyer, [Iago Grubb](../I/Iago_Grubb.md), eject [Sancho Proudfoot](../S/Sancho_Proudfoot.md) as he tries to excavate the pantry at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
