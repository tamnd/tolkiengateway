**Fourth Age 125** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 125** or **F.O.**) is the 125th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 125 was also known as **S.R. 1546** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md), son of King [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) of the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and Queen [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of legendarium {#Other_versions_of_legendarium}

The text from which the date of Eldarion's death derives was written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in [1959](../_/1959.md) and thus predates the second edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, where Tolkien ultimately changed the date of [Aragorn's](../A/Aragorn.md) death from [Fo.A.](Fourth_Age.md) [100](Fourth_Age_100.md) to [Fo.A.](Fourth_Age.md) [120](Fourth_Age_120.md).

## Note {#Note}

In the [reckoning](../N/New_Reckoning.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), Fourth Age 1 began on [25 March](../_/25_March.md) of [Third Age 3021](../T/Third_Age_3021.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
