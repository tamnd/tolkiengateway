**Fingolfin's cairn** was the resting place for the body of King [Fingolfin](Fingolfin.md), the High King of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), after his single-handed combat with [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). It was built upon a mountain top in the [Echoriad](../E/Echoriad.md), to the north of the valley of [Tumladen](../T/Tumladen.md), overlooking the city of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).

## History {#History}

As the forces of Morgoth achieved victory in the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), King Fingolfin believed that he was witnessing the end of the Noldor. He rode to the gates of [Angband](../A/Angband.md) and challenged the Enemy to battle. Morgoth came forth, they [fought](Fall_of_Fingolfin.md), and Fingolfin died after wounding his opponent. Morgoth broke the King's body, and was about to cast it to his wolves when the eagle [Thorondor](../T/Thorondor.md) swooped in and snatched the body away.

The great eagle laid Fingolfin's body upon a mountain above Gondolin. Fingolfin's son, [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md), then came and piled a high cairn over the body. It is said that no [Orc](../O/Orcs.md) dared to come near the tomb.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

On a list of names from the 1930s, the cairn is called <i><b>Sarnas Fingolfin</b></i> ("Cairn of Fingolfin") in [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
