**Fingolfin's cairn** was the resting place for the body of King [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), the High King of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), after his single-handed combat with [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth). It was built upon a mountain top in the [Echoriad](/wiki/Echoriad), to the north of the valley of [Tumladen](/wiki/Tumladen), overlooking the city of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).

## History {#History}

As the forces of Morgoth achieved victory in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), King Fingolfin believed that he was witnessing the end of the Noldor. He rode to the gates of [Angband](/wiki/Angband) and challenged the Enemy to battle. Morgoth came forth, they [fought](/wiki/Fall_of_Fingolfin "Fall of Fingolfin"), and Fingolfin died after wounding his opponent. Morgoth broke the King's body, and was about to cast it to his wolves when the eagle [Thorondor](/wiki/Thorondor) swooped in and snatched the body away.

The great eagle laid Fingolfin's body upon a mountain above Gondolin. Fingolfin's son, [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon), then came and piled a high cairn over the body. It is said that no [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") dared to come near the tomb.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

On a list of names from the 1930s, the cairn is called <i><b>Sarnas Fingolfin</b></i> ("Cairn of Fingolfin") in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
