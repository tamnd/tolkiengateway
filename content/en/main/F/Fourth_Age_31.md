**Fourth Age 31** (abbreviated to **Fo.A. 31** or **F.O.**) is the 31st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Fourth Age](Fourth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Unless otherwise stated, [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) follows [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) usage in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) for reckoning Fourth Age years: Fourth Age 31 was also known as **S.R. 1452** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Westmarch](../W/Westmarch.md) becomes a part of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) by the gift of the [King](../A/Aragorn.md). Many [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) remove to it.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
