In early versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), <i><b>Fôs&#39; Almir</b></i> (the **Bath of Flame**),<sup id="cite_ref-LT2II_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2II-1">[1]</a></sup> also called <i><b>Faskalan</b></i>, <i><b>Faskala-númen</b></i> (the **Bath of the Setting Sun**) and <i><b>Tanyasalpë</b></i> (the bowl of fire),<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-2">[2]</a></sup> was a purifying bath.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2II_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2II-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LT2IIn_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2IIn-3">[3]</a></sup>

The bath was fashioned as a great basin with golden floor and walls of polished bronze. An "arcade of golden pillars topped with fires engirdled it" and [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md) "set a great and nameless [spell](../M/Magic.md) around it, so that therein was poured the most of the waters of the fruit of the noon and it became a bath of fire".<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was told that [Urwendi](../U/Urwendi.md) and her maidens, before the rising of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md), entered into Faskalan.<sup id="cite_ref-LT1Sun_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1Sun-4">[4]</a></sup> [Turambar](../T/Túrin.md) and [Nienóri](../N/Nienor.md) entered Faskalan after their death.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2II_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2II-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LT2IIn_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2IIn-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fôs' Almir* is a [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) name, meaning "Galmir's bath, i.e. [Sun](../S/Sun.md)'s bath".<sup id="cite_ref-PE11_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE11-5">[5]</a></sup>

*Tanyasalpë* is a [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) name, containing *tanya* ("fire")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and *salpa* "bowl".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

A rejected name used in earlier texts for the bath was <i><b>Fauri</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2IIn_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2IIn-3">[3]</a></sup>

*Fôs' Almir* appears to also have been used by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) as a name for the [Western Sea](../B/Belegaer.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PE11_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE11-5">[5]</a></sup>
