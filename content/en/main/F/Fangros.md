**Fangros** was an unspecified place where the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) burned their stolen ships out of repentance, during their [flight](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor") from [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in a prose fragment following *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, and in *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Fan_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fan-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

An earlier name for the place was *Fangair*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
