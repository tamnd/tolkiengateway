**Faery**, called **Fairyland** by [Nokes](/wiki/Nokes), was the land that [Smith Smithson](/wiki/Smith_Smithson) alone of [Wootton Major](/wiki/Wootton_Major) could enter due to the [fay-star](/wiki/Fay-star "Fay-star") that he had eaten as a young boy at [The Feast of Good Children](/wiki/The_Feast_of_Good_Children).<sup id="cite_ref-History_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-History-1">[1]</a></sup>

Faery was bounded by the [Sea of Windless Storm](/wiki/Sea_of_Windless_Storm). In the land were fair valleys filled with woods and meads, and on a wide plain there was a shadowy hill from which sprang the [King's Tree](/wiki/King%27s_Tree). Faery had two ranges of mountains, the Outer Mountains and the Inner Mountains. In the Outer Mountains of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Outer_Faery&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Outer Faery (page does not exist)">Outer Faery</a>, there was a [Lake of Tears](/wiki/Lake_of_Tears)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> filled with fiery creatures. Its surface was harder than stone and slipperier than glass. In the Inner Mountains, there was the [Vale of Evermorn](/wiki/Vale_of_Evermorn) where danced the Elven maidens.<sup id="cite_ref-History_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-History-1">[1]</a></sup>

Due to the Fay-star that Smith had eaten (which was transferred to his brow on his tenth birthday), Smith was able to visit Faery and explore its various regions. In the Vale of Evermorn he was allowed to dance with the maidens upon the lawn and eventually in a high place he met the Queen of Faery.<sup id="cite_ref-History_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-History-1">[1]</a></sup>
