**Fataneh Howe** is a French illustrator and actor who has created artwork for and appeared in adaptations of the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). She also frequently collaborates with her husband, [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe), and appeared in *[Lord of the Brush](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Brush)*, a documentary about his work in the Legendarium. She is credited for contributing artwork to [War of the Ring: The Card Game](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring:_The_Card_Game) and played an uncredited role as a woman from [Laketown](/wiki/Laketown) in [The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>.
