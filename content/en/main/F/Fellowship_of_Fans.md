**Fellowship of Fans** is a community of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) fans who are often first to release information about <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../T/The_Rings_of_Power.md" title="The Rings of Power">The Rings of Power</a></i> and *[The War of the Rohirrim](../T/The_War_of_the_Rohirrim.md)* adaptations. They are active on various social sites such as Reddit, Twitter, YouTube and Discord.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
