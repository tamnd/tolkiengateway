**First Age 116** (abbreviated to **F.A. 116**) is the 116th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) is completed and [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) secretly removes his people from [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Before [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) leaves [Vinyamar](../V/Vinyamar.md), [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) comes to him and instructs Turgon to leave armour and a sword behind. They will be worn as a token by [a future warrior](../T/Tuor.md) to warn him of impending Doom.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
