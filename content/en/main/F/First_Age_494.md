**First Age 494** (abbreviated to **F.A. 494**) is the 494th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) stirs up the [Easterlings](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>) to greater hatred of [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Edain](../E/Edain.md), sending [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) to aid and impel them. Hearing of [Nienor's](../N/Nienor.md) beauty, [Lorgan](../L/Lorgan.md) is eager to take her by force.<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Morwen](../M/Morwen.md) and Nienor flee to [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) and seek news of [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup> There is no news of Túrin in Doriath, but [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) allows Morwen and Nienor to stay in Doriath as his guests.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
