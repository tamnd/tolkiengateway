**First Age 417** (abbreviated to **F.A. 417**) is the 417th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Beleth](../B/Beleth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:231</sup>
- Birth of [Galdor the Tall](<../G/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-lómin).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:234</sup>
