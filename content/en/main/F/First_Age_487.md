**First Age 487** (abbreviated to **F.A. 487**) is the 487th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](First_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Beleg](../B/Beleg.md) returns to [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) with the [Dragon-helm of Dor-lómin](../D/Dragon-helm_of_Dor-lómin.md). Fighting side-by-side around [Amon Rûdh](../A/Amon_Rûdh.md), the area becomes known as [Dor-Cúarthol](../D/Dor-Cúarthol.md), the Land of Bow and Helm.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
