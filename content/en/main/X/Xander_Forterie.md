**Xander Forterie** is a Dutch make-up artist and sculptor, who runs the Unreal studio with his business partner Rogier Samuels. They were both employed by [Weta Workshop](../W/Weta_Workshop.md) for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>).

Forterie worked on creature prosthetics in all three films and made several [Sideshow Collectibles](../S/Sideshow_Collectibles.md) statuettes. He also appeared in the [prologue](../P/Prologue%253A_One_Ring_to_Rule_Them_All....md) of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* as one of the seven Dwarf lords.
