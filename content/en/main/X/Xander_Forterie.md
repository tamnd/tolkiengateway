**Xander Forterie** is a Dutch make-up artist and sculptor, who runs the Unreal studio with his business partner Rogier Samuels. They were both employed by [Weta Workshop](/wiki/Weta_Workshop) for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").

Forterie worked on creature prosthetics in all three films and made several [Sideshow Collectibles](/wiki/Sideshow_Collectibles) statuettes. He also appeared in the [prologue](/wiki/Prologue:_One_Ring_to_Rule_Them_All... "Prologue: One Ring to Rule Them All...") of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* as one of the seven Dwarf lords.
