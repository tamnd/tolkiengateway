**Xabi Gaztelua** is a Spanish illustrator. As a [Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md) artist, she created art for [The Hobbit](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit.md) expansion.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Gaztelua attributes his interest in the fantasy genre to his mother, who read *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* to him and gifted him the [comic book adaptation](<../T/The_Hobbit_(comic_book).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
