A timeline of the life of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

## [2968](/wiki/Third_Age_2968 "Third Age 2968") {#2968}

- [22 September](/wiki/22_September): Birth of Frodo Baggins.

## [2980](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980") {#2980}

- Death of Frodo's parents, [Drogo Baggins](/wiki/Drogo_Baggins) and [Primula Brandybuck](/wiki/Primula_Brandybuck).

## [2989](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989") {#2989}

- Frodo is adopted by [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and moves to [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) around this time.

## [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001") {#3001}

- 22 September: Frodo comes of age and inherits Bag End and the [Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") from Bilbo.

## [3004](/wiki/Third_Age_3004 "Third Age 3004") {#3004}

- [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) visits Frodo in the [Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") several times over the next four years.

## [3008](/wiki/Third_Age_3008 "Third Age 3008") {#3008}

- Gandalf visits Frodo for the last time for many years.

## [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") {#3018}

- [12 April](/wiki/12_April): Gandalf arrives at Bag End.
- [13 April](/wiki/13_April): Frodo learns he has the One Ring and decides to leave the Shire.

- [22 September](/wiki/22_September): Frodo turns 50.
- [23 September](/wiki/23_September): Frodo leaves Bag End.
- [24 September](/wiki/24_September): Frodo is nearly caught twice by one of the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) and meets [Gildor](/wiki/Gildor) in the [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End).
- [25 September](/wiki/25_September): Frodo learns of his friends' intention to accompany him.
- [26 September](/wiki/26_September): Frodo and his companions travel through the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest) and come to the House of [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil).
- [28 September](/wiki/28_September): The Hobbits are trapped by a [Barrow-wight](/wiki/Barrow-wights "Barrow-wights"). Frodo resists putting on the Ring.
- [29 September](/wiki/29_September): Frodo meets [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in [Bree](/wiki/Bree).

- [6 October](/wiki/6_October): Frodo is wounded by the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) at [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop).
- [20 October](/wiki/20_October): Frodo crosses the [Ford of Bruinen](/wiki/Ford_of_Bruinen) pursued by the Nazgûl.
- [23 October](/wiki/23_October): [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) removes the sliver of the [Morgul-blade](/wiki/Morgul-knife "Morgul-knife") from Frodo's body.
- [24 October](/wiki/24_October): Frodo wakes in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).
- [25 October](/wiki/25_October): At the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond), Frodo volunteers to take the Ring to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).

- [25 December](/wiki/25_December): Frodo and the [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") leave Rivendell.

## [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") {#3019}

- [13 January](/wiki/13_January): The Fellowship enters [Moria](/wiki/Moria).
- [15 January](/wiki/15_January): Gandalf battles the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") and falls into [shadow](/wiki/Shadow "Shadow"). The Fellowship enters [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).
- [17 January](/wiki/17_January): Frodo meets [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel).

- [14 February](/wiki/14_February): Frodo looks into the [Mirror of Galadriel](/wiki/Mirror_of_Galadriel).
- [16 February](/wiki/16_February): Frodo and the Fellowship leave Lothlórien.
- [26 February](/wiki/26_February): At Amon Hen, [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) tries to take the Ring. Frodo decides to go to Mordor alone but is followed by [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee").
- [29 February](/wiki/29_February): Frodo meets [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) and spares his life.

- [1 March](/wiki/1_March)-[2 March](/wiki/2_March): Gollum leads the Hobbits through the [Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes).
- [5 March](/wiki/5_March): The Hobbits reach the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) and realize it's impassable. Frodo agrees to follow Gollum to a [secret way](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol "Cirith Ungol") into Mordor.
- [7 March](/wiki/7_March): Frodo meets [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), brother of Boromir.
- [8 March](/wiki/8_March): Frodo asks Faramir to spare Gollum's life at the [Forbidden Pool](/wiki/Forbidden_Pool). The Hobbits part company with Faramir in the morning.
- [9 March](/wiki/9_March): The Hobbits reach the [Morgul-road](/wiki/Morgul-road) at dusk.
- [10 March](/wiki/10_March): The Hobbits reach the [Cross-roads](/wiki/Cross-roads) and continue to the [Morgul Vale](/wiki/Morgul_Vale). Frodo sees the Witch-king leading an army from [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) and resists putting on the Ring.
- [12 March](/wiki/12_March): Gollum leads Frodo and Sam into [Shelob's Lair](/wiki/Shelob%27s_Lair).
- [13 March](/wiki/13_March): Frodo is wounded by [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob) and is taken prisoner by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs_of_Mordor "Orcs of Mordor") to the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol).
- [14 March](/wiki/14_March): Sam finds Frodo in the Tower of Cirith Ungol.
- [15 March](/wiki/15_March): Frodo and Sam escape from the Tower.
- [16 March](/wiki/16_March): Frodo and Sam look toward [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom) from the [Morgai](/wiki/Morgai).
- [18 March](/wiki/18_March): Frodo and Sam are forced to join a company of Orcs marching toward Udun.
- [19 March](/wiki/19_March): Frodo and Sam escape from the Orcs and continue on.
- [22 March](/wiki/22_March): Frodo and Sam leave the road and turn south toward Mount Doom.
- [23 March](/wiki/23_March): The Hobbits cast away their gear.
- [24 March](/wiki/24_March): The Hobbits reach the foot of Mount Doom.
- [25 March](/wiki/25_March): Frodo reaches the [Cracks of Doom](/wiki/Cracks_of_Doom) and claims the Ring for himself. Gollum bites off Frodo's finger and falls into the fiery chasm. The Ring is destroyed.

- [8 April](/wiki/8_April): Frodo and Sam are honoured at the [Field of Cormallen](/wiki/Field_of_Cormallen).

- [1 May](/wiki/1_May): Frodo carries the crown at Aragorn's coronation.

- [15 July](/wiki/15_July): [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) tells Frodo that he may pass into the [West](/wiki/Aman "Aman") in her stead.

- [6 October](/wiki/6_October): Frodo feels pain in his shoulder on the anniversary of his confrontation with the Witch-king.
- [30 October](/wiki/30_October): The Hobbits arrive at the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge).

- [3 November](/wiki/3_November): The [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater). Frodo says that [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s life should be spared, but the Wizard is killed by [Gríma](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma).
- [4 November](/wiki/4_November): Frodo agrees to be Deputy Mayor.

<br>

## [3020](/wiki/Third_Age_3020 "Third Age 3020") {#3020}

- [13 March](/wiki/13_March): Frodo is ill on the anniversary of his encounter with Shelob.

- [1 May](/wiki/1_May): Sam marries [Rosie Cotton](/wiki/Rosie_Cotton) and moves into Bag End.

- [Mid-year's Day](/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day): Frodo resigns as Deputy Mayor.

- [6 October](/wiki/6_October): Frodo is ill again.

## [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021") {#3021}

- [13 March](/wiki/13_March): Frodo is ill again.

- [21 September](/wiki/21_September): Frodo and Sam set out for the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens).
- [22 September](/wiki/22_September): Frodo meets Galadriel and Elrond in the Woody End.
- [29 September](/wiki/29_September): Frodo sails into the West to the [Undying Lands](/wiki/Undying_Lands).
