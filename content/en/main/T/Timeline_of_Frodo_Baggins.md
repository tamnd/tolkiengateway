A timeline of the life of [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) in the [Third Age](Third_Age.md).

## [2968](Third_Age_2968.md) {#2968}

- [22 September](../_/22_September.md): Birth of Frodo Baggins.

## [2980](Third_Age_2980.md) {#2980}

- Death of Frodo's parents, [Drogo Baggins](../D/Drogo_Baggins.md) and [Primula Brandybuck](../P/Primula_Brandybuck.md).

## [2989](Third_Age_2989.md) {#2989}

- Frodo is adopted by [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and moves to [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) around this time.

## [3001](Third_Age_3001.md) {#3001}

- 22 September: Frodo comes of age and inherits Bag End and the [Ring](The_One_Ring.md) from Bilbo.

## [3004](Third_Age_3004.md) {#3004}

- [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) visits Frodo in the [Shire](The_Shire.md) several times over the next four years.

## [3008](Third_Age_3008.md) {#3008}

- Gandalf visits Frodo for the last time for many years.

## [3018](Third_Age_3018.md) {#3018}

- [12 April](../_/12_April.md): Gandalf arrives at Bag End.
- [13 April](../_/13_April.md): Frodo learns he has the One Ring and decides to leave the Shire.

- [22 September](../_/22_September.md): Frodo turns 50.
- [23 September](../_/23_September.md): Frodo leaves Bag End.
- [24 September](../_/24_September.md): Frodo is nearly caught twice by one of the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) and meets [Gildor](../G/Gildor.md) in the [Woody End](../W/Woody_End.md).
- [25 September](../_/25_September.md): Frodo learns of his friends' intention to accompany him.
- [26 September](../_/26_September.md): Frodo and his companions travel through the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md) and come to the House of [Tom Bombadil](Tom_Bombadil.md).
- [28 September](../_/28_September.md): The Hobbits are trapped by a [Barrow-wight](../B/Barrow-wights.md). Frodo resists putting on the Ring.
- [29 September](../_/29_September.md): Frodo meets [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) in [Bree](../B/Bree.md).

- [6 October](../_/6_October.md): Frodo is wounded by the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) at [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md).
- [20 October](../_/20_October.md): Frodo crosses the [Ford of Bruinen](../F/Ford_of_Bruinen.md) pursued by the Nazgûl.
- [23 October](../_/23_October.md): [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) removes the sliver of the [Morgul-blade](../M/Morgul-knife.md) from Frodo's body.
- [24 October](../_/24_October.md): Frodo wakes in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).
- [25 October](../_/25_October.md): At the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md), Frodo volunteers to take the Ring to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).

- [25 December](../_/25_December.md): Frodo and the [Fellowship](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) leave Rivendell.

## [3019](Third_Age_3019.md) {#3019}

- [13 January](../_/13_January.md): The Fellowship enters [Moria](../M/Moria.md).
- [15 January](../_/15_January.md): Gandalf battles the [Balrog](../B/Balrogs.md) and falls into [shadow](../S/Shadow.md). The Fellowship enters [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).
- [17 January](../_/17_January.md): Frodo meets [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md).

- [14 February](../_/14_February.md): Frodo looks into the [Mirror of Galadriel](../M/Mirror_of_Galadriel.md).
- [16 February](../_/16_February.md): Frodo and the Fellowship leave Lothlórien.
- [26 February](../_/26_February.md): At Amon Hen, [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) tries to take the Ring. Frodo decides to go to Mordor alone but is followed by [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md).
- [29 February](../_/29_February.md): Frodo meets [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) and spares his life.

- [1 March](../_/1_March.md)-[2 March](../_/2_March.md): Gollum leads the Hobbits through the [Dead Marshes](../D/Dead_Marshes.md).
- [5 March](../_/5_March.md): The Hobbits reach the [Black Gate](../B/Black_Gate.md) and realize it's impassable. Frodo agrees to follow Gollum to a [secret way](../C/Cirith_Ungol.md) into Mordor.
- [7 March](../_/7_March.md): Frodo meets [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md), brother of Boromir.
- [8 March](../_/8_March.md): Frodo asks Faramir to spare Gollum's life at the [Forbidden Pool](../F/Forbidden_Pool.md). The Hobbits part company with Faramir in the morning.
- [9 March](../_/9_March.md): The Hobbits reach the [Morgul-road](../M/Morgul-road.md) at dusk.
- [10 March](../_/10_March.md): The Hobbits reach the [Cross-roads](../C/Cross-roads.md) and continue to the [Morgul Vale](../M/Morgul_Vale.md). Frodo sees the Witch-king leading an army from [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md) and resists putting on the Ring.
- [12 March](../_/12_March.md): Gollum leads Frodo and Sam into [Shelob's Lair](../S/Shelob's_Lair.md).
- [13 March](../_/13_March.md): Frodo is wounded by [Shelob](../S/Shelob.md) and is taken prisoner by [Orcs](../O/Orcs_of_Mordor.md) to the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md).
- [14 March](../_/14_March.md): Sam finds Frodo in the Tower of Cirith Ungol.
- [15 March](../_/15_March.md): Frodo and Sam escape from the Tower.
- [16 March](../_/16_March.md): Frodo and Sam look toward [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md) from the [Morgai](../M/Morgai.md).
- [18 March](../_/18_March.md): Frodo and Sam are forced to join a company of Orcs marching toward Udun.
- [19 March](../_/19_March.md): Frodo and Sam escape from the Orcs and continue on.
- [22 March](../_/22_March.md): Frodo and Sam leave the road and turn south toward Mount Doom.
- [23 March](../_/23_March.md): The Hobbits cast away their gear.
- [24 March](../_/24_March.md): The Hobbits reach the foot of Mount Doom.
- [25 March](../_/25_March.md): Frodo reaches the [Cracks of Doom](../C/Cracks_of_Doom.md) and claims the Ring for himself. Gollum bites off Frodo's finger and falls into the fiery chasm. The Ring is destroyed.

- [8 April](../_/8_April.md): Frodo and Sam are honoured at the [Field of Cormallen](../F/Field_of_Cormallen.md).

- [1 May](../_/1_May.md): Frodo carries the crown at Aragorn's coronation.

- [15 July](../_/15_July.md): [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) tells Frodo that he may pass into the [West](../A/Aman.md) in her stead.

- [6 October](../_/6_October.md): Frodo feels pain in his shoulder on the anniversary of his confrontation with the Witch-king.
- [30 October](../_/30_October.md): The Hobbits arrive at the [Brandywine Bridge](../B/Brandywine_Bridge.md).

- [3 November](../_/3_November.md): The [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md). Frodo says that [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md)'s life should be spared, but the Wizard is killed by [Gríma](../G/Gríma.md).
- [4 November](../_/4_November.md): Frodo agrees to be Deputy Mayor.

<br>

## [3020](Third_Age_3020.md) {#3020}

- [13 March](../_/13_March.md): Frodo is ill on the anniversary of his encounter with Shelob.

- [1 May](../_/1_May.md): Sam marries [Rosie Cotton](../R/Rosie_Cotton.md) and moves into Bag End.

- [Mid-year's Day](../M/Mid-year's_Day.md): Frodo resigns as Deputy Mayor.

- [6 October](../_/6_October.md): Frodo is ill again.

## [3021](Third_Age_3021.md) {#3021}

- [13 March](../_/13_March.md): Frodo is ill again.

- [21 September](../_/21_September.md): Frodo and Sam set out for the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md).
- [22 September](../_/22_September.md): Frodo meets Galadriel and Elrond in the Woody End.
- [29 September](../_/29_September.md): Frodo sails into the West to the [Undying Lands](../U/Undying_Lands.md).
