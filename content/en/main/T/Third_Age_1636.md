**Third Age 1636** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1636**) is the 1636th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1636 was also known as **S.R. 36** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) devastates [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), especially [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md). Watch on [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) ceases.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-gondor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Death of King [Telemnar](Telemnar.md) and his children.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Tarondor](<Tarondor_(King_of_Gondor).md>), the nephew of [Telemnar](Telemnar.md), becomes the twenty-seventh [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The [White Tree](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md) dies in [Minas Anor](../M/Minas_Anor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The plague spreads north and west, hits [the Shire](The_Shire.md) and many parts of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) become desolate.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Around the same time the [Shadow](../S/Shadow.md) in [Greenwood](../G/Greenwood.md) grows deeper and evil things reappear.<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Around the same time the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) sent the [Barrow-wights](../B/Barrow-wights.md) that haunted the hills,<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-4">[4]</a></sup> preventing any resettlement.
