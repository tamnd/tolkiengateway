**They're taking the Hobbits to Isengard** is a 2005 music video made by Erwin Beekveld ([1969](/wiki/1969) - [31 March](/wiki/31_March) [2022](/wiki/2022)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is a mix of footage from *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers")*. The song is formed around the phrase, uttered by [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), "They're taking the Hobbits to Isengard". This line is spoken in *The Two Towers*, when Legolas spots the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) and extrapolates their objective from the direction in which they run. It should be noted that Legolas states the Uruks turned north-east--a direction which would not lead them to Isengard, but straight back at the pursuers. The actual direction should be north-west.

The lyrics are scrambled lines from Legolas, [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn). Footage of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took), [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck), [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) is also shown. The music is a computerized mix of two themes of [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore)'s music; "[The Shire](</wiki/The_Shire_(soundtrack)> "The Shire (soundtrack)")" theme and "[The Ring Goes South](</wiki/The_Ring_Goes_South_(soundtrack)> "The Ring Goes South (soundtrack)")" theme.
