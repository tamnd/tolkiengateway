**The Burning Eye** is a [MUD](/wiki/MUDs "MUDs"). It opened in [1997](/wiki/1997) and is set in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) at the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-official_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-official-2">[2]</a></sup> The mud has more than 300 skills, 12 races and 10 classes.<sup id="cite_ref-official_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-official-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Races {#Races}

The Burning Eye has twelve races, of which there are eight good and four evil.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [Avari Elf](/wiki/Avari "Avari")
- [Beorning](/wiki/Beornings "Beornings")
- [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain)
- [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves")
- [Half Orc](/wiki/Half_Orcs "Half Orcs")
- [Half Troll](/wiki/Half-trolls "Half-trolls")
- [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits")
- [Human](/wiki/Men "Men")
- [Noldor Elf](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor")
- [Olog-Hai](/wiki/Olog-hai "Olog-hai")
- [Teleri Elf](/wiki/Teleri "Teleri")
- [Uruk-Hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai")

## Classes {#Classes}

This mud has ten classes.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

- Archmage
- Bashkar
- Cleric
- Monk
- Paladin
- Ranger
- Sorcerer
- Thief
- Warrior
