**The Burning Eye** is a [MUD](../M/MUDs.md). It opened in [1997](../_/1997.md) and is set in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) at the [Third Age](Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-official_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-official-2">[2]</a></sup> The mud has more than 300 skills, 12 races and 10 classes.<sup id="cite_ref-official_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-official-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Races {#Races}

The Burning Eye has twelve races, of which there are eight good and four evil.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [Avari Elf](../A/Avari.md)
- [Beorning](../B/Beornings.md)
- [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md)
- [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md)
- [Half Orc](../H/Half_Orcs.md)
- [Half Troll](../H/Half-trolls.md)
- [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md)
- [Human](../M/Men.md)
- [Noldor Elf](../N/Noldor.md)
- [Olog-Hai](../O/Olog-hai.md)
- [Teleri Elf](Teleri.md)
- [Uruk-Hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md)

## Classes {#Classes}

This mud has ten classes.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

- Archmage
- Bashkar
- Cleric
- Monk
- Paladin
- Ranger
- Sorcerer
- Thief
- Warrior
