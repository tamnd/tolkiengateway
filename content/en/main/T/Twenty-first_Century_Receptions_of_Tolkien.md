<i><b>Twenty-first Century Receptions of Tolkien</b></i> is a collection of the proceedings of the 2021 [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Society) Winter Seminar. The book was published in [2022](/wiki/2022) as the twenty-first volume in the [Peter Roe Memorial Fund](/wiki/Peter_Roe_Memorial_Fund) series.

## Contents {#Contents}

- Introduction by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Will_Sherwood&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Will Sherwood (page does not exist)">Will Sherwood</a>
- "Middle-earth: Shadow of War as the new shadow" by Jelena Filipovic
- "Artists from Middle-earth: a 21st-century dive into J.R.R. Tolkien’s Secondary World" by Marie Bretagnolle
- "Nazgûl Taller Than Night: Tolkien and Speculative Realism" by Nick Groom
- "Tolkien’s Grave as a Site of Memory" by Mina D. Lukić and Dejan M. Vukelić

## From the Publisher {#From_the_Publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><br/>
Hosted online, the Tolkien Society 2021 winter seminar sought to explore how J.R.R. Tolkien has been received in the twenty-first century. With a broad range of Tolkien&#39;s writings being published in the last twenty-two years, adaptions embellishing on Tolkien&#39;s stories, and new philosophies offering innovative ways to read Tolkien, the twenty-first century continues to bring us revised visions of Tolkien. This proceedings offers a range of insights into how recent adaptions and philosophies have expanded Tolkien&#39;s legendarium. It was also the first of three seminars that the Tolkien Society hosted online in 2021, collectively welcoming over 1600 people from across the globe.
</p><p>Published under the auspices of the Society&#39;s Peter Roe Memorial Fund, this proceedings features a collection of four papers delivered at the Tolkien Society 2021 winter seminar.
</p><p><br/>
</p>
</blockquote>
