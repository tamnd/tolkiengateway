**The Great River** is the ninth and penultimate chapter of the second book in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.

## Summary {#Summary}

The journey by boat down the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) was uneventful for some days. The [Company](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") passed through barren lands laid by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and came across no living things except black swans flying overhead. The company decided for the most part to follow [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) along the path to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) that he had recommended. [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) disagreed, wanting to return to his home with the [Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), because of the dream he had had. Aragorn and [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) went on ahead to find a safe route past the [Falls of Rauros](/wiki/Rauros "Rauros"). They returned to tell the company that they must leave the river and go by road for a while.

## Composition {#Composition}

Perhaps Tolkien wrote this narration at the end of [1941](/wiki/1941), being part of a larger chapter titled "*The Scattering of the Company*" before dividing it in two. The passage of time spent by the Company in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) fluctuated in Tolkien's mind (unsure whether the [Time](/wiki/Time) proceeded at different pace in the Golden Wood, or had stopped altogether) and considered several time-schemes, reflected to different versions of the conversation among the Fellowship about it.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. xxiv</sup>

It was while writing this portion when Tolkien decided to consult a 1942 calendar to describe the [lunar](/wiki/Moon "Moon") phases accurately.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. xlvi</sup> <span></span>
