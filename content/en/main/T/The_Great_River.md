**The Great River** is the ninth and penultimate chapter of the second book in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.

## Summary {#Summary}

The journey by boat down the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) was uneventful for some days. The [Company](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) passed through barren lands laid by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) and came across no living things except black swans flying overhead. The company decided for the most part to follow [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) along the path to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) that he had recommended. [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) disagreed, wanting to return to his home with the [Ring](The_One_Ring.md), because of the dream he had had. Aragorn and [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) went on ahead to find a safe route past the [Falls of Rauros](../R/Rauros.md). They returned to tell the company that they must leave the river and go by road for a while.

## Composition {#Composition}

Perhaps Tolkien wrote this narration at the end of [1941](../_/1941.md), being part of a larger chapter titled "*The Scattering of the Company*" before dividing it in two. The passage of time spent by the Company in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) fluctuated in Tolkien's mind (unsure whether the [Time](Time.md) proceeded at different pace in the Golden Wood, or had stopped altogether) and considered several time-schemes, reflected to different versions of the conversation among the Fellowship about it.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. xxiv</sup>

It was while writing this portion when Tolkien decided to consult a 1942 calendar to describe the [lunar](../M/Moon.md) phases accurately.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. xlvi</sup> <span></span>
