<i><b>Tolkien Centenary Conference 1992</b></i> is a souvenir book which was published in conjunction to the [J.R.R. Tolkien Centenary Conference](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_Centenary_Conference). It was limited to five hundred fifty copies.

The booklet features cover and title page art by [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne), leaf ornament drawn by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tony_Curtis&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tony Curtis (page does not exist)">Tony Curtis</a>, and [centrefold art](</wiki/File:Kay_Woollard_-_Party_Piece_(full).jpg> "File:Kay Woollard - Party Piece (full).jpg") by [Kay Woollard](/wiki/Kay_Woollard).
