<i><b>Tolkien Centenary Conference 1992</b></i> is a souvenir book which was published in conjunction to the [J.R.R. Tolkien Centenary Conference](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_Centenary_Conference.md). It was limited to five hundred fifty copies.

The booklet features cover and title page art by [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md), leaf ornament drawn by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tony_Curtis&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tony Curtis (page does not exist)">Tony Curtis</a>, and [centrefold art](<../../file/K/Kay_Woollard_-_Party_Piece_(full).jpg.md>) by [Kay Woollard](../K/Kay_Woollard.md).
