**Third Age 2691** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2691**) is the 2691st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2691 was also known as **S.R. 1091** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Helm](../H/Helm.md), son of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Gram](../G/Gram.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
