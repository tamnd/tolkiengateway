**Teak** is a type of tree that appears in [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing) and [The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online).

## In *Middle-earth Role Playing* {#In_Middle-earth_Role_Playing}

<i><b>Teak</b></i> ([S](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin"). *Maldawar*, [Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"). *Malintavar*), known as *Gumumiti* in the language of [Pel](/wiki/Dominions_of_the_Seven#Pel "Dominions of the Seven"), was a tropical hardwood that grew up to 120 ft. The tree was common in the forests of Pel. It had yellow-brown wood that was resinous, and was used for inlays, furniture, boats, and shields.<sup id="cite_ref-secondpage_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-secondpage-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Its leaves were called *witav* and lessened the duration of being stunned when ingested.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## In *The Lord of the Rings Online* {#In_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online}

**Teak** was found in Mûr Ghala in northern [Harad](/wiki/Harad). Wood of the tree could be foraged by *Sul Madásh* foresters.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
