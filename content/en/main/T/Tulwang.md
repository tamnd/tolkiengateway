In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), **Tulwang** was a land on a peninsula west of [Ciryatanor](/wiki/Ciryatanor), north of [Hyarn](/wiki/Hyarn), and southeast of [Far Harad](/wiki/Far_Harad).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

As the Chaialla [woses](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain "Drúedain") were pushed away from their homeland in Chy by the migrations of Men, they dwelled in Tulwang during the first two decades of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). When Men later settled in [Far Harad](/wiki/Far_Harad), the woses moved to what later became Hyarn by [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_20&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 20 (page does not exist)">20</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-timelinesouth_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timelinesouth-2">[2]</a></sup>

Around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [700](/wiki/Second_Age_700 "Second Age 700"), [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) established embassies in Tulwang. It is in Tulwang that its fleet landed in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_2280&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 2280 (page does not exist)">2280</a>, when [Tar-Ancalimon](/wiki/Tar-Ancalimon) moved against the rebellious [Akhôrahil](/wiki/Fifth_of_the_Nazg%C3%BBl "Fifth of the Nazgûl") of [Ciryatanor](/wiki/Ciryatanor).<sup id="cite_ref-timelinesouth_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timelinesouth-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1399&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1399 (page does not exist)">1399</a>, the [Army of the Southern Dragon](/wiki/Army_of_the_Southern_Dragon) attacked Tulwang in its first known campaign.<sup id="cite_ref-timelinesouth_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timelinesouth-2">[2]</a></sup> After centuries, the Army finally conquered the land in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1657&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1657 (page does not exist)">1657</a>. Tulwang later split apart in various petty kingdoms during the civil war of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_2013&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 2013 (page does not exist)">2013</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-timelinecitadel_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timelinecitadel-3">[3]</a></sup>

The [Sun Lord](/wiki/Far_Harad#Portrayal_in_adaptations "Far Harad") Pon Ifta attacked Tulwang in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_2224&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 2224 (page does not exist)">2224</a> and swiftly conquered it in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_2229&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 2229 (page does not exist)">2229</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-timelinecitadel_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timelinecitadel-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
