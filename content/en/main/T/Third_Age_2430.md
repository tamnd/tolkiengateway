**Third Age 2430** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2430**) is the 2430th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2430 was also known as **S.R. 830** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- About this time, [Sméagol](../S/Sméagol.md) is born near [Gladden Fields](../G/Gladden_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-guide_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
