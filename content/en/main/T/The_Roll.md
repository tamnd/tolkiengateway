The **Roll** was a list made and learned by heart by Shire-historians. The Roll contained the names of all those [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) who fought in the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater). In all accounts of the Roll, Captains [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) always appear to be listed first at the top of the list. Some of the other Hobbits on the list include the likes of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), [Farmer Cotton](/wiki/Farmer_Cotton), [Young Tom](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Junior "Tolman Cotton Junior"), [Jolly](/wiki/Wilcome_Cotton_II "Wilcome Cotton II") and [Nick Cotton](/wiki/Bowman_Cotton "Bowman Cotton").

In latter years, the rise of fame and fortune of the [Cottons](/wiki/Cotton_Family "Cotton Family") dates from this time.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
