**The Lord of the Rings: Duel for Middle-earth** is a two-player card game, re-implementing the popular <a class="external text" href="https://www.rprod.com/en/games/7-wonders-duel" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">7 Wonders Duel</a> with the Lord of the Rings license. It features art by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Vincent_Dutrait&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Vincent Dutrait (page does not exist)">Vincent Dutrait</a>.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

An Epic Duel for the Fate of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth)! In The Lord of the Rings - Duel for Middle-earth, two players face off to determine the fate of Middle-earth. One player embodies the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring), seeking to protect the free peoples and destroy the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring), while the other plays as [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), attempting to capture [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) and [Sam](/wiki/Sam) and overwhelm Middle-earth with his evil hordes.
