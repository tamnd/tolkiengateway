**The Road to Isengard** is the third scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* and [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)"). It was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden), and [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) ride through [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest), approaching [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).

[Merry](/wiki/Merry) and [Pippin](/wiki/Pippin) sit atop a wall, laughing and smoking the [Longbottom Leaf](/wiki/Longbottom_Leaf) that [they found in Saruman's storeroom](</wiki/Flotsam_and_Jetsam_(scene)> "Flotsam and Jetsam (scene)"). As they see Gandalf and the others ride out of the forest, Merry stands and greets them, much to the delight of Aragorn.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>You young rascals! A merry hunt you&#39;ve led us on, and now we find you, feasting and smoking!
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/Gimli" title="Gimli">Gimli</a> to <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Merry" title="Merry">Merry</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Pippin" title="Pippin">Pippin</a></cite></p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

Merry tells them that [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) has taken over Isengard.
