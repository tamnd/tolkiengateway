**Thomas Morwinsky** (born [1967](/wiki/1967)), German Doctor in natural sciences, is editor of the journal *[Other Minds](/wiki/Other_Minds)*. He has created several maps and written a number of articles about role-playing adaptations of Tolkien's legendarium.

He co-authored the *[Rivendell](</wiki/Rivendell_(The_One_Ring_role-playing_game)> "Rivendell (The One Ring role-playing game)")* supplement for the first edition of the *[The One Ring](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)")* role-playing game.
