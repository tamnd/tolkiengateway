**Thomas Morwinsky** (born [1967](../_/1967.md)), German Doctor in natural sciences, is editor of the journal *[Other Minds](../O/Other_Minds.md)*. He has created several maps and written a number of articles about role-playing adaptations of Tolkien's legendarium.

He co-authored the *[Rivendell](<../R/Rivendell_(The_One_Ring_role-playing_game).md>)* supplement for the first edition of the *[The One Ring](<The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md>)* role-playing game.
