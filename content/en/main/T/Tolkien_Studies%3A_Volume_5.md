<b><i>Tolkien Studies</i>: Volume 5</b>, published in [2008](/wiki/2008), is the fifth volume of the annual review *[Tolkien Studies](</wiki/Tolkien_Studies_(journal)> "Tolkien Studies (journal)")*.

This issue reprints two texts by J.R.R. Tolkien: essay "[Chaucer as a Philologist: The Reeve's Tale](/wiki/Chaucer_as_a_Philologist:_The_Reeve%27s_Tale)", first published in 1934, and a version of "[The Reeve's Tale](/wiki/The_Reeve%27s_Tale)" by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Chaucer" title="wikipedia:Geoffrey Chaucer">Chaucer</a>, first published in 1939.
