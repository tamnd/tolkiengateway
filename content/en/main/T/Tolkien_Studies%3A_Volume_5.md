<b><i>Tolkien Studies</i>: Volume 5</b>, published in [2008](../_/2008.md), is the fifth volume of the annual review *[Tolkien Studies](<Tolkien_Studies_(journal).md>)*.

This issue reprints two texts by J.R.R. Tolkien: essay "[Chaucer as a Philologist: The Reeve's Tale](../C/Chaucer_as_a_Philologist%253A_The_Reeve's_Tale.md)", first published in 1934, and a version of "[The Reeve's Tale](The_Reeve's_Tale.md)" by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Chaucer" title="wikipedia:Geoffrey Chaucer">Chaucer</a>, first published in 1939.

## Contents {#Contents}

- Articles
    - Brian Rosebury, "Revenge and Moral Judgement in Tolkien"
    - Douglas A. Anderson, "Brian Rosebury on J.R.R. Tolkien: A Checklist"
    - Carl Phelpstead, "'With chunks of poetry in between': *The Lord of the Rings* and Saga Poetics"
    - Corey Olsen, "The Myth of the Ent and the Entwife"
    - James G. Davis, "Showing Saruman as Faber: Tolkien and Peter Jackson"
    - Lynn Forest-Hill, "Boromir, Byrhtnoth, and Bayard: Finding a Language for Grief in J.R.R. Tolkien's *The Lord of the Rings*"
    - Jason Fisher, "Three Rings for—Whom Exactly? And Why?: Justifying the Disposition of the Three Elven Rings"

- Notes and Documents
    - J.R.R. Tolkien, "[Chaucer as a Philologist: The Reeve's Tale](../C/Chaucer_as_a_Philologist%253A_The_Reeve's_Tale.md)"
    - J.R.R. Tolkien, "[The Reeve's Tale: Version Prepared for Recitation at the 'Summer Diversions'](The_Reeve's_Tale.md) Oxford: 1939"
    - Ross Smith, "Steiner on Tolkien"
    - George Steiner, "Tolkien, Oxford's Eccentric Don"

- Book Reviews
    - *[The Children of Húrin](The_Children_of_Húrin.md), Narn i Chîn Húrin: The Tale of the Children of Húrin*, by J.R.R Tolkien, edited by Christopher Tolkien, reviewed by Nicholas Birns
    - *Early Elvish Poetry and Pre-Fëanorian Alphabets* (*[Parma Eldalamberon 16](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_16.md)*), by J.R.R. Tolkien, edited by Arden R. Smith, Christopher Gilson, Bill Welden, Carl F. Hostetter, and Patrick H. Wynne, reviewed by John Garth
    - *The Evolution of Tolkien's Mythology: A Study of the History of Middle-earth*, by Elizabeth Whittingham, reviewed by Deidre A. Dawson
    - *The Frodo Franchise: "The Lord of the Rings" and Modern Hollywood*, by Kristen Thompson, reviewed by Dyrk Ashton
    - *[The History of The Hobbit](The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)*, by John D. Rateliff, reviewed by Tom Shippey
    - *[Hither Shore](<../H/Hither_Shore_(journal).md>): Interdisciplinary Journal of Modern Fantasy Literature, Jahrbuch der Deutschen Tolkien Gesellschaft e.V.*, edited by Thomas Fornet-Ponse (editor-in-chief), Marcel Bülles, Thomas Honegger, Rainer Nagel, Alexandra Velten, and Frank Weinreich, reviewed by Mark T. Hooker
    - *[Inside Language](../I/Inside_Language.md): Linguistic and Aesthetic Theory in Tolkien*, by Ross Smith, reviewed by Dimitra Fimi
    - *[Roots and Branches](../R/Roots_and_Branches.md): Selected Papers on Tolkien*, by Tom Shippey, reviewed by Kelley M. Wickham-Crowley
    - *[Tolkien and Modernity 1](Tolkien_and_Modernity_1.md)*, and *[Tolkien and Modernity 2](Tolkien_and_Modernity_2.md)*, edited by Frank Weinreich and Thomas Honegger, reviewed by Shaun F. D. Hughes
    - *[Tolkien and Shakespeare](Tolkien_and_Shakespeare.md): Essays on Shared Themes and Language*, edited by Janet Brennan Croft, reviewed by Jared Lobdell
    - *[Tree of Tales](Tree_of_Tales.md): Tolkien, Literature, and Theology*, by Trevor Hart and Ivan Khovacs, reviewed by Michael J. Brisbois
    - Book Notes, by Douglas A. Anderson

- David Bratman, "The Year's Work in Tolkien Studies 2005"
- Michael D.C. Drout, Jason Rea, Rebecca Epstein, and Lauren Provost, "Bibliography (in English) for 2006"
