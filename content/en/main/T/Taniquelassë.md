**Taniquelassë** was a [fragrant evergreen tree](../F/Fragrant_Trees.md) brought to [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) from [Tol Eressëa](Tol_Eressëa.md) by the [Elves](../E/Elves.md). After the downfall of Númenor they were not found within the circles of the world.<sup id="cite_ref-UTNum_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNum-1">[1]</a></sup>

Taniquelassë, among the other trees brought to Númenor, grew mostly up the sheltered seaward slopes of [Nísimaldar](../N/Nísimaldar.md) in the [Bay of Eldanna](../B/Bay_of_Eldanna.md) but they also spread far east into the land.<sup id="cite_ref-UTNum_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNum-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *taniquelassë* means "high-snow-leaf" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) (from <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ta-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ta- (page does not exist)">ta-</a></i> (cf. <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=T%C3%A1ra&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tára (page does not exist)">tára</a></i>)= "high", <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Nique&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nique (page does not exist)">nique</a></i>= "snow" and *[lassë](../L/Lassë.md)*= "leaf").<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
