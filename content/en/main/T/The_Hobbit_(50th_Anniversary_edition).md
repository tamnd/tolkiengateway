<b><i>The Hobbit</i> (50th Anniversary edition)</b> is an edition of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* published in [1987](/wiki/1987), featuring a new Foreword by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien). Included in the Foreword are [reminiscences](/wiki/Index:Reminiscences_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Reminiscences of J.R.R. Tolkien") by Christopher of his father, and reproductions of two pages from the manuscript of *The Hobbit*, together with six further illustrations and several extracts from letters written by J.R.R. Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

A similar edition was published in the same year by Guild Publishing.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
