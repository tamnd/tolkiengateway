**The Tale of Years of the Third Age** is the eighth chapter of *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md)*, the twelfth book in the series *[The History of Middle-earth](The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)*. This chapter describes the writing and development of the history of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) that would be published in the second part of [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md).

## Earliest Timeline {#Earliest_Timeline}

The first version of the Tale of Years of the Third Age begins with the death of Isildur, declaring it Year 0. From there, [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) is said to have reawoken and entered Mirkwood in 1000, where he established Dol Dugul (in the final version this will become [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md)).

In 1100, Deagol and Smeagol came across the One Ring. Smeagol killed Deagol, became [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md), and entered the Misty Mountains by 1105. Separately, the brothers' people multiplied, crossed the Misty Mountains in 1300, and became [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md). (At around this time Orcs began to reappear.) In 1500 the Hobbits settled at [Bree](../B/Bree.md). A century later, Marco and Cavallo crossed the Brandywine and the king at Fornost Erain granted them the Shire. (This event marks the beginning of Shire-reckoning.)

## About the Text {#About_the_Text}

The earliest version of the Tale of Years of the Third Age was a short text that seemed closely related to the version of the Tale of Years of the Second Age which [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) had designated T2 in the sixth chapter, *[The Tale of Years of the Second Age](The_Tale_of_Years_of_the_Second_Age.md)*. Because of this connection, although the two manuscripts were clearly distinct from each other, Christopher designated the relevant Third Age document T2 as well.

JRR Tolkien provided Shire-reckoning dates for all events that had them in this first timeline, but Christopher declined to include these.
