**The Tale of Years of the Third Age** is the eighth chapter of *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)*, the twelfth book in the series *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)*. This chapter describes the writing and development of the history of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) that would be published in the second part of [Appendix B](/wiki/Appendix_B).

## Earliest Timeline {#Earliest_Timeline}

The first version of the Tale of Years of the Third Age begins with the death of Isildur, declaring it Year 0. From there, [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) is said to have reawoken and entered Mirkwood in 1000, where he established Dol Dugul (in the final version this will become [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur)).

In 1100, Deagol and Smeagol came across the One Ring. Smeagol killed Deagol, became [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum), and entered the Misty Mountains by 1105. Separately, the brothers' people multiplied, crossed the Misty Mountains in 1300, and became [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits). (At around this time Orcs began to reappear.) In 1500 the Hobbits settled at [Bree](/wiki/Bree). A century later, Marco and Cavallo crossed the Brandywine and the king at Fornost Erain granted them the Shire. (This event marks the beginning of Shire-reckoning.)

## About the Text {#About_the_Text}

The earliest version of the Tale of Years of the Third Age was a short text that seemed closely related to the version of the Tale of Years of the Second Age which [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) had designated T2 in the sixth chapter, *[The Tale of Years of the Second Age](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Years_of_the_Second_Age)*. Because of this connection, although the two manuscripts were clearly distinct from each other, Christopher designated the relevant Third Age document T2 as well.

JRR Tolkien provided Shire-reckoning dates for all events that had them in this first timeline, but Christopher declined to include these.
