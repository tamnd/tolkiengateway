**Thorin** is a name [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") adapted from the <i><a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Völuspá</a></i>, a part of the epic *Poetic Edda*, and it means "Daring" in Old Norse. Here *Thorin* can refer to:

- [Thorin I](/wiki/Thorin_I) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2035](/wiki/Third_Age_2035 "Third Age 2035") - [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2289](/wiki/Third_Age_2289 "Third Age 2289")), a [dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves")-king left [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") in favour of the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains).
- <b><a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin II <i>Oakenshield</i></a> (<a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2746" title="Third Age 2746">2746</a> - <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2941" title="Third Age 2941">2941</a>), a companion of <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i>.</b>
- [Thorin III *Stonehelm*](/wiki/Thorin_Stonehelm "Thorin Stonehelm") (born [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2866](/wiki/Third_Age_2866 "Third Age 2866")), [King under the Mountain](/wiki/Kings_under_the_Mountain "Kings under the Mountain") during the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).
