"**The cold hard lands**" is the first line of a [poem](/wiki/Poems_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "Poems in The Lord of the Rings") written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) that was sung by [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) in *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*.<sup id="cite_ref-towers_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-towers-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Poem excerpt {#Poem_excerpt}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>The cold hard lands<br/>
they bites our hands,<br/>
  they gnaws our feet.<sup id="cite_ref-towers_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-towers-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</div>

## History {#History}

In the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age),<sup id="cite_ref-year_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-year-2">[2]</a></sup> part of the poem was used by [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) as a riddle when he and [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo) partook in a [Riddle-game](/wiki/Riddle-game):<sup id="cite_ref-riddles_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-riddles-3">[3]</a></sup>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Alive without breath,<br/>
As cold as death,<br/>
Never thirsting, ever drinking,<br/>
All in mail never clinking.<sup id="cite_ref-riddles_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-riddles-3">[3]</a></sup>
</p>
</div>

On [1 March](/wiki/1_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-month_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-month-4">[4]</a></sup> Gollum, while guiding [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) through the [Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes), occasionally chuckled or sang portions of the poem. He may have been delighted at the feeling of the water "in the shallow stony stream" his feet splashed in. At one point, Gollum interrupted himself to allude to the Riddle-game many [decades before](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor "Quest of Erebor"). Gollum's singing caused Samwise to think more pressingly about "the problem of food" and to speculate that Gollum was thinking of this as well.<sup id="cite_ref-towers_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-towers-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In Tolkien's first draft of the chapter, the first line, "The cold hard lands", is the only part of the poem that remained unaltered.<sup id="cite_ref-ring_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ring-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2002: <i><a href="/wiki/P%C3%A1n_prste%C5%88ov" title="Pán prsteňov">Pán prsteňov</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gollum (portrayed by Ibrahim Maiga) sings this ditty during an episode of the second series (season) of the Slovak radio series. The song is accompanied by a nursery rhyme-like melody with some subtle percussions.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>An alternate version of this song is heard in the <a href="/wiki/The_Forbidden_Pool_(scene)" title="The Forbidden Pool (scene)">forty-second scene</a> when Gollum catches a fish in the <a href="/wiki/Forbidden_Pool" title="Forbidden Pool">Forbidden Pool</a>:</dd></dl>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>The rock and pool<br/>
Is nice and cool,<br/>
So juicy-sweet!<br/>
Our only wish,<br/>
To catch a fish,<br/>
So juicy-sweet!
</p>
</div>
