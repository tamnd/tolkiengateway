<i><b>Tolkien - The Centenary Calendar 1992</b></i> was the official Tolkien calendar published by [Grafton](/wiki/Grafton) on [25 July](/wiki/25_July) [1991](/wiki/1991). It features art by [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith) based on *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.
