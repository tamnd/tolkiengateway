<b><i>Tyalië Tyelelliéva</i> 8</b> is an issue of the journal *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va)*.

## Contents {#Contents}

- Editorial, Celestial Events
- Bree Moot Report by [Lisa Star](/wiki/Lisa_Star) (with pictures)
- Elvish Language Poetry Prize, announcing the winners
- Book Reviews (reviews by Lisa Star)
    - *J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist &amp; Illustrator*, by Wayne Hammond and Christina Scull
    - *The Oxford Book of Fantasy Stories*, selected by Tom Shippey
    - *The Road to Middle-earth* (revised), by Tom Shippey
    - *Les langues Elfiques, Vol. I (Quenya and Lindarin)*, by [Edouard Kloczko](/wiki/Edouard_Kloczko)
- Leonid Korablev's AElfwine Note (review of artwork)
- Comments from Leonid Korablev (on Salo's analysis of [Fíriel's song](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel%27s_song)), [Rhona Beare](/wiki/Rhona_Beare) (on intensives, including a list!), [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) (on the correct form of 'twelve' in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin))
- Ring Poem in Quenya, translation by [Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger), with calligraphy by Leonid Korablev
- Lussi Lomesse by Helge Fauskanger (a poem in Quenya)
- Ring Poem in Egyptian by [Ivan Derzhanski](/wiki/Ivan_Derzhanski) (in Egyptian hieroglyphs with analysis)
