<b><i>Tyalië Tyelelliéva</i> 8</b> is an issue of the journal *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](Tyalië_Tyelelliéva.md)*.

## Contents {#Contents}

- Editorial, Celestial Events
- Bree Moot Report by [Lisa Star](../L/Lisa_Star.md) (with pictures)
- Elvish Language Poetry Prize, announcing the winners
- Book Reviews (reviews by Lisa Star)
    - *J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist &amp; Illustrator*, by Wayne Hammond and Christina Scull
    - *The Oxford Book of Fantasy Stories*, selected by Tom Shippey
    - *The Road to Middle-earth* (revised), by Tom Shippey
    - *Les langues Elfiques, Vol. I (Quenya and Lindarin)*, by [Edouard Kloczko](../E/Edouard_Kloczko.md)
- Leonid Korablev's AElfwine Note (review of artwork)
- Comments from Leonid Korablev (on Salo's analysis of [Fíriel's song](../F/Fíriel's_song.md)), [Rhona Beare](../R/Rhona_Beare.md) (on intensives, including a list!), [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) (on the correct form of 'twelve' in [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md))
- Ring Poem in Quenya, translation by [Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md), with calligraphy by Leonid Korablev
- Lussi Lomesse by Helge Fauskanger (a poem in Quenya)
- Ring Poem in Egyptian by [Ivan Derzhanski](../I/Ivan_Derzhanski.md) (in Egyptian hieroglyphs with analysis)
