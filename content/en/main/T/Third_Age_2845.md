**Third Age 2845** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2845**) is the 2845th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2845 was also known as **S.R. 1245** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Thráin](Thráin.md) the [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) is imprisoned in [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md); the [Ring of Thrór](../R/Ring_of_Thrór.md), the last of the [Seven Rings](../S/Seven_Rings.md), is taken from him.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
