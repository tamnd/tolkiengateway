**Tookbank** was a small village in the [Tookland](Tookland.md), in the [Westfarthing](../W/Westfarthing.md) of [the Shire](The_Shire.md), not far west of the larger township of [Tuckborough](Tuckborough.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) suggests that the name represents a possible [Old Hobbitish](../O/Old_English.md) form *Tucbanca* meaning "slope of the [Tooks](Took.md)" (cf. <i>river <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bank#Etymology_2" title="wiktionary:bank">bank</a></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
