**Tookbank** was a small village in the [Tookland](/wiki/Tookland), in the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), not far west of the larger township of [Tuckborough](/wiki/Tuckborough).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggests that the name represents a possible [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") form *Tucbanca* meaning "slope of the [Tooks](/wiki/Took "Took")" (cf. <i>river <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bank#Etymology_2" title="wiktionary:bank">bank</a></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
