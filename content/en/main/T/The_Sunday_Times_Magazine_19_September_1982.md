<b><i>The Sunday Times Magazine</i> 19 September 1982</b> is an issue of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sunday_Times_Magazine" title="wikipedia:The Sunday Times Magazine">The Sunday Times Magazine</a></i>. Sue Seddon's article "The Return of Tolkien" (pp. 82-85) includes extracts from an interview with [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) together with reproductions of twelve illustrations by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) from *[Mr. Bliss](/wiki/Mr._Bliss)*. The illustrations are from pages 6, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 31, 33, 38, 39, 49 and 50 of the manuscript. This magazine was published the day before the publication of *Mr. Bliss*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
