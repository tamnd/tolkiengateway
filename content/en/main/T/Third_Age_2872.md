**Third Age 2872** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2872**) is the 2872nd [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Third Age 2872 was also known as **S.R. 1272** in [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) [Belecthor II](/wiki/Belecthor_II) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Thorondir](/wiki/Thorondir), son of Belecthor II, becomes the twenty-second Ruling Steward.<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The [White Tree](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor "White Tree of Gondor") dies, and no seedling can be found. The [Dead Tree](/wiki/Dead_Tree) is left standing.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Notes {#Notes}

In editions prior to the [50th Anniversary edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition)> "The Lord of the Rings (50th Anniversary Edition)") the information for this year in [Appendix B](/wiki/Appendix_B) was assigned to [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2852](/wiki/Third_Age_2852 "Third Age 2852"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> which contradicted the death of Belecthor II in 2872 found in [Appendix A](/wiki/Appendix_A) in the list of the dates of death of the ruling stewards.<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>
