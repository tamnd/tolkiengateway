**Third Age 2872** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2872**) is the 2872nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2872 was also known as **S.R. 1272** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Belecthor II](../B/Belecthor_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Thorondir](Thorondir.md), son of Belecthor II, becomes the twenty-second Ruling Steward.<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The [White Tree](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md) dies, and no seedling can be found. The [Dead Tree](../D/Dead_Tree.md) is left standing.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Notes {#Notes}

In editions prior to the [50th Anniversary edition](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md>) the information for this year in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) was assigned to [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2852](Third_Age_2852.md),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> which contradicted the death of Belecthor II in 2872 found in [Appendix A](../A/Appendix_A.md) in the list of the dates of death of the ruling stewards.<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>
