**Tulkastor** was the father of [Vairë](</wiki/Vair%C3%AB_(wife_of_Lindo)> "Vairë (wife of Lindo)"), an [Elf](/wiki/Elf) of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

Although he belonged to [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB)'s kindred, he had dwelt long with the [Solosimpi](/wiki/Solosimpi) and was thus one of the first [Elves](/wiki/Elves) to come to Tol Eressëa.<sup id="cite_ref-Tulk_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tulk-2">[2]</a></sup>

He was also the grandfather of [Naimi](/wiki/Naimi), and an ancestor of [Heorrenda](/wiki/Heorrenda) son of [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol).<sup id="cite_ref-Naim_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Naim-1">[1]</a></sup> His daughter Vairë was married to [Lindo](/wiki/Lindo), the keeper of the [Cottage of Lost Play](/wiki/Cottage_of_Lost_Play).<sup id="cite_ref-Tulk_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tulk-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Tulkastor* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya). Its meaning is unclear, but it appears to contain the name of the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas) (from the root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=TULUK&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TULUK (page does not exist)">TULUK</a>-, meaning "steady, firm").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate of *Tulkastor* is <i><b>Tulchathron</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Originally the name of the character was *Turenbor*, later changed to *Tulkassë* and finally to *Tulkastor*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
