**Third Age 1437** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1437**) is the 1437th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Burning of Osgiliath](../B/Burning_of_Osgiliath.md) and loss of the *[palantír](../M/Master_Stone_of_Osgiliath.md)* in the river [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppA2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA2-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Eldacar](<../E/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor).md>) flees to [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md); his son [Ornendil](../O/Ornendil.md) is murdered.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Castamir "the Usurper"](../C/Castamir.md), [Captain of Ships](../C/Captain_of_Ships.md), takes the throne of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) to become its twenty-second [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA1-3">[3]</a></sup>
