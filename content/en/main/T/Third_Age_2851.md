**Third Age 2851** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2851**) is the 2851st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2851 was also known as **S.R. 1251** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [White Council](../W/White_Council.md) meets.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) urges an attack on [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md). [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) (who desired the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md) and hoped that it would reveal itself with [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s presence) overrules him.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) begins to search near the [Gladden Fields](../G/Gladden_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Walda](../W/Walda.md) with his companions ride by mountain-paths from [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md). They are trapped and slain by [orcs](../O/Orcs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Folca](../F/Folca.md), son of Walda, becomes the thirteenth King of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Halfred Greenhand](../H/Halfred_Greenhand.md), second child and first son of [Holman the greenhanded](../H/Holman_the_greenhanded.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
