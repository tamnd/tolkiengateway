<i><b>The Tolkien Calendar 1989</b></i> was the official Tolkien calendar published by [Unwin Paperbacks](/wiki/Unwin_Hyman "Unwin Hyman") on [25 August](/wiki/25_August) [1988](/wiki/1988). It features art by [Roger Garland](/wiki/Roger_Garland) based on *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* and *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.
