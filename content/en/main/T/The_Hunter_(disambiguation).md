**The Hunter** could be referring to the following:

- [Folca](/wiki/Folca), thirteenth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan") of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) who died capturing the [Boar of Everholt](/wiki/Boar_of_Everholt).
- [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth), female [Chieftain of the Haladin](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_Haladin) during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).
- [Hunter](/wiki/Hunter_and_Rider "Hunter and Rider"), an illusive figure of Elven legends.
- [Mablung](/wiki/Mablung), a [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) who accompanied [Beren](/wiki/Beren) in the [Hunting of the Wolf](/wiki/Hunting_of_the_Wolf).
- [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), one of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), known as *Huntsman of the Valar*.
- [Vorondil](/wiki/Vorondil), a [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor") who killed the [Kine of Araw](/wiki/Kine_of_Araw).
- [Thû](/wiki/Th%C3%BB), the Necromancer of the [Wizard's Isle](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth "Tol-in-Gaurhoth").
