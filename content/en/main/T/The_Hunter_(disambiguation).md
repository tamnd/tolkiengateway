**The Hunter** could be referring to the following:

- [Folca](../F/Folca.md), thirteenth [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) who died capturing the [Boar of Everholt](../B/Boar_of_Everholt.md).
- [Haleth](../H/Haleth.md), female [Chieftain of the Haladin](../C/Chieftain_of_the_Haladin.md) during the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).
- [Hunter](../H/Hunter_and_Rider.md), an illusive figure of Elven legends.
- [Mablung](../M/Mablung.md), a [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) [Elf](../E/Elves.md) of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) who accompanied [Beren](../B/Beren.md) in the [Hunting of the Wolf](../H/Hunting_of_the_Wolf.md).
- [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md), one of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), known as *Huntsman of the Valar*.
- [Vorondil](../V/Vorondil.md), a [Steward of Gondor](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md) who killed the [Kine of Araw](../K/Kine_of_Araw.md).
- [Thû](Thû.md), the Necromancer of the [Wizard's Isle](Tol-in-Gaurhoth.md).
