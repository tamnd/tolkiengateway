**Tolkien's Monsters** is the ninth episode of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_(TV_channel)" title="wikipedia:History (TV channel)">the History channel</a>'s "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clash_of_the_Gods_(TV_series)" title="wikipedia:Clash of the Gods (TV series)">Clash of the Gods</a>" series. The first seven episodes were on Greek mythology, followed by one on *[Beowulf](<../B/Beowulf_(poem).md>)*. The tenth and last episode was on Thor, the Norse god of Thunder.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The episode features expert interviews and reenactments, as it endeavours to explain the sources and origins behind the story. The story explores *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)*, with reenactments and computer simulations.

## Errors {#Errors}

- Not much attention was paid to make-up; Dwarves are portrayed beardless, Hobbits are just bare footed (Bilbo even has a beard) and Elves all have an unexplained tattoo on their right cheek, the only thing that distinguishes them from Men.
- The actor playing the older [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) has a mustache. Though he did have a mustache during [the Great War](../W/World_War_I.md), he did not retain it. Ironically, reenactments of his war years show him clean shaven, and wearing the insignia of a US Army corporal rather than a British second lieutenant.
- Though the experts and the narrator do a generally good job with pronunciation, [Ainur](../A/Ainur.md) is pronounced "Aniur".
