**Théodwyn** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2963](/wiki/Third_Age_2963 "Third Age 2963")-[3002](/wiki/Third_Age_3002 "Third Age 3002")) was the mother of [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) and [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn).

## History {#History}

Théodwyn was a noble woman of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), the sister of [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden), and the daughter of [Thengel](/wiki/Thengel) and [Morwen Steelsheen](/wiki/Morwen_Steelsheen). She was married to [Éomund](/wiki/%C3%89omund) of [Eastfold](/wiki/Eastfold) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2989](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989"). Her son Éomer was born in [2991](/wiki/Third_Age_2991 "Third Age 2991"), and her daughter Éowyn in [2995](/wiki/Third_Age_2995 "Third Age 2995").

Éomund was slain in [3002](/wiki/Third_Age_3002 "Third Age 3002"). Not long after that Théodwyn died of illness. Théoden raised her two children after her death.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Théodwyn* is [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) for "joy of the people".
