**Théodwyn** ([T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2963](Third_Age_2963.md)-[3002](Third_Age_3002.md)) was the mother of [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) and [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md).

## History {#History}

Théodwyn was a noble woman of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), the sister of [Théoden](Théoden.md), and the daughter of [Thengel](Thengel.md) and [Morwen Steelsheen](../M/Morwen_Steelsheen.md). She was married to [Éomund](../_/Éomund.md) of [Eastfold](../E/Eastfold.md) in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2989](Third_Age_2989.md). Her son Éomer was born in [2991](Third_Age_2991.md), and her daughter Éowyn in [2995](Third_Age_2995.md).

Éomund was slain in [3002](Third_Age_3002.md). Not long after that Théodwyn died of illness. Théoden raised her two children after her death.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Théodwyn* is [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) for "joy of the people".
