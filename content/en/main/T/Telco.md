<i><b>telco</b></i> is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for "leg".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Hence <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Telcontar" title="Telcontar"><b>Telco</b>ntar</a></i> ("Strider").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Telco* also refers to the straight 'stem' line that appears in written [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar), which can extend above or below the writing line (although in the original system there was a third option, a telco that extended both above and below the line). Combined with the *[lúva](/wiki/L%C3%BAva "Lúva")* ("bow") stroke, it forms the basis of all the standard characters of the [Fëanorian alphabet](/wiki/F%C3%ABanorian_alphabet).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
