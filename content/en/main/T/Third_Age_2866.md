**Third Age 2866** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2866**) is the 2866th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2866 was also known as **S.R. 1266** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Thorin III](Thorin_Stonehelm.md), son of [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Dáin II Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_II_Ironfoot.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Angelimir](../A/Angelimir.md), son of [Aglahad](../A/Aglahad.md), [Prince of Dol Amroth](../P/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
