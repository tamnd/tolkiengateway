**Tumunzahar** is the [Dwarvish](/wiki/Khuzdul "Khuzdul") name for [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod), the citadel of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) that lay in the central part of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains), near [Mount Dolmed](/wiki/Mount_Dolmed).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tumunzahar* is apparently [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) for "Hollowbold".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Magnus Åberg](/wiki/Magnus_%C3%85berg) has suggested that the name consists of the unattested elements *tûm* (?"bold/delving") and *[zahar](/wiki/Zahar "Zahar")* (?"hollow").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
