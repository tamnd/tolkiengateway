<i><b>The Lord Of The Rings (Original Motion Picture Soundtrack)</b></i> is an album comprising the soundtrack from the 1978 film *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)")*. It was released in [1978](/wiki/1978) as a double LP and in [2001](/wiki/2001) as a CD with more tracks, which is the track listing below
