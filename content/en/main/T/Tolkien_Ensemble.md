The **Tolkien Ensemble** is a Danish ensemble with the aim to create "the world's first complete musical interpretation of the poems and songs from [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)". They published four studio albums from 1997 to 2005. The project was given approval by both the [Tolkien family](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate "Tolkien Estate") and [Harper Collins](/wiki/Harper_Collins) Publishers. Queen [Margrethe II of Denmark](/wiki/Margrethe_II_of_Denmark) gave permission for her illustrations to be used as cover art for their albums.

The ensemble was founded by guitarist and composer <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Caspar_Reiff&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Caspar Reiff (page does not exist)">Caspar Reiff</a> in 1995. Other permanent members were Peter Hall, Signe Asmussen, Øyvind Ougaard, Katja Nielsen, and Morten Ryelund Sørensen.

According to their <a class="external text" href="https://www.facebook.com/thetolkienensemble/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Facebook page</a>, the ensemble continued to perform sporadically until Covid-19 lockdowns prevented further concerts.
