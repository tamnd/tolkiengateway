**Third Age 1635** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1635**) is the 1635th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1635 was also known as **S.R. 35** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) hits [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Around this time the [Shadow](../S/Shadow.md) deepens in [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md), and evil things reappear.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
