<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Online: Helm&#39;s Deep</b></i> is the fifth expansion to *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)* game. It expands the world by opening up the West [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) region, including the city of [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras), from the [Gap of Rohan](/wiki/Gap_of_Rohan) south and east towards the [Eastfold](/wiki/Eastfold). It also introduced expansions to gameplay through the Big Battles system (the first of which being the expansion's namesake), raising the level cap to 95, class changes, and the extension of the epic story. It marked the last large paid expansion Turbine would release for three years, instead opting to release quarterly free updates with bonuses for VIP subscribers.

## Updates {#Updates}

### Update 13: The Breaking of Isengard {#Update_13:_The_Breaking_of_Isengard}

*The Breaking of Isengard* is the thirteenth update and was released [14 April](/wiki/14_April), [2014](/wiki/2014).<sup id="cite_ref-Update_13_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_13-1">[1]</a></sup>

**Book XIV - The Waters of Isengard**

- Release of a new landscape consisting of post-[Battle of Isengard](/wiki/Battle_of_Isengard) Nan Curunir as well as the Entwood,<sup id="cite_ref-Update_13_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_13-1">[1]</a></sup> an area consisting of the south-western part of Fangorn.
- The fourteenth and final book of Epic Volume III: Allies of the King, The Waters of Isengard.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Misty Mountains, North Downs and Trollshaws region revamps.<sup id="cite_ref-Update_13_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_13-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Resizable inventory bags.<sup id="cite_ref-Update_13_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_13-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Level scaling from level 20 up for players in the Ettenmoors and temporary Ettenmoors passes for free-to-play purchasable with Mithril Coins.<sup id="cite_ref-Update_13_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_13-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Update 14: Paths of the Dead {#Update_14:_Paths_of_the_Dead}

*Paths of the Dead* is the fourteenth update and was released [14 July](/wiki/14_July), [2014](/wiki/2014).<sup id="cite_ref-Update_14_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_14-3">[3]</a></sup>

**Volume IV: The Strength of Sauron** **Book I - The Road to Gondor**

- Release of the West Gondor region, consisting of the areas the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead), the [Blackroot Vale](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale), [Lamedon](/wiki/Lamedon), the Havens of [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas), and [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-Update_14_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_14-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- A new Epic series,<sup id="cite_ref-Update_14_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_14-3">[3]</a></sup> Volume IV: The Strength of Sauron, beginning with Book 1: The Road to Gondor.
- The Essence System, allowing specially slotted armors to be fitted with a variety of Essences for customizable stats.<sup id="cite_ref-Update_14_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_14-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Level cap increase to 100.<sup id="cite_ref-Update_14_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_14-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The [Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes) region was added later in the minor Update 14.2, released September 15, 2014. This area included a Session Play featuring Frodo, Samwise, and Gollum's passage through the marshes, casting the player character as Sam.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Update 15: Gondor Aflame {#Update_15:_Gondor_Aflame}

*Gondor Aflame*<sup id="cite_ref-U15_Announcement_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-U15_Announcement-6">[6]</a></sup> is the fifteeth update and was released 4 November 2014.<sup id="cite_ref-Update_15_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_15-7">[7]</a></sup>

**Book II - The Dawnless Day**

- The region of Central Gondor, consisting of [Ringló Vale](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3_Vale), [Dor-en-Ernil](/wiki/Dor-en-Ernil), [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin) and [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir).<sup id="cite_ref-U15_Announcement_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-U15_Announcement-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Update_15_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_15-7">[7]</a></sup>
- Beorning race and class.<sup id="cite_ref-U15_Announcement_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-U15_Announcement-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Update_15_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_15-7">[7]</a></sup>
- Epic Volume IV Book 2: The Dawnless Day.<sup id="cite_ref-Update_15_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_15-7">[7]</a></sup>
- A new Epic Battle, Retaking Pelargir.<sup id="cite_ref-Update_15_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_15-7">[7]</a></sup> Initially only available in solo/duo mode, a full fellowship version was released in 4 February 2015 with Update 15.2.<sup id="cite_ref-Update_15.2_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_15.2-8">[8]</a></sup>
- Roving Threats, a new type of open landscape group content.<sup id="cite_ref-Update_15_7-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_15-7">[7]</a></sup> Additional Roving Threats were added to other regions of the game beginning with Update 15.2<sup id="cite_ref-Update_15.2_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_15.2-8">[8]</a></sup>

### Update 16: Ashes of Osgiliath {#Update_16:_Ashes_of_Osgiliath}

*Ashes of Osgiliath* is the sixteenth update and was released [1 May](/wiki/1_May), [2015](/wiki/2015).<sup id="cite_ref-Update_16_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_16-9">[9]</a></sup>

**Book III - Voices of the Past**

- The region of Eastern Gondor, consisting of Upper Lebennin, Lossarnach, South Ithilien and Osgiliath.<sup id="cite_ref-Update_16_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_16-9">[9]</a></sup>
- Epic Volume IV Book 3: Voices of the Past.<sup id="cite_ref-Update_16_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update_16-9">[9]</a></sup>
- Cosmetic weapons and shields, more Roving Threats, and new PvMP map set in Osgiliath were released with Update 16.2 on 17 July 2015. A new quest series of weekly episodic content, following the adventures of the Hobbit Bingo Boffin, was begun the Wednesday after the update, on 22 July.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Update 17: The Siege of Minas Tirith {#Update_17:_The_Siege_of_Minas_Tirith}

*The Siege of Minas Tirith* is the seventeenth update and was released [27 October](/wiki/27_October), [2015](/wiki/2015).

**Book IV - Siege of the White City**

- The region of Old Anórien, including [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), the [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields), and Talath Anor.
- Epic Volume IV Book 4: Siege of the White City
- New crafting tier, hidden cosmetic weapon toggle.
- New Epic Battles: Defence of Minas Tirith, Hammer of the Underworld.

### Update 18: The Battle of Pelennor Fields {#Update_18:_The_Battle_of_Pelennor_Fields}

*The Battle of Pelennor fields* is the eighteenth update and was released [11 April](/wiki/11_April) [2016](/wiki/2016).

**Book V - The Ride of the Rohirrim** **Book VI - The Battle of Pelennor Fields**

- The region of Far Anórien, including [Taur Drúadan](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest "Drúadan Forest"), [Beacon hills](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor"), and the Pelennor Fields during the battle.
- Instance cluster including two 3-man instances (The Quays of the Harlond, The Blood of the Black Serpent), one 6-man instance (The Silent Street) and a 12-man raid (Throne of the Dread Terror).
- Epic Volume IV Book 5: The Ride of the Rohirrim and Book 6: The Battle of Pelennor Fields.

### Update 19: March of the King {#Update_19:_March_of_the_King}

*March of the King* is the nineteenth update and was released [14 October](/wiki/14_October), [2016](/wiki/2016).

**Book VII - March of the King**

- The region March of the King, including [North Ithilien](/wiki/North_Ithilien), after battle Minas Tirith, after battle Osgiliath, and after battle Pelennor Fields.
- Flora barter system to exchange new gear for flowers.
- Epic Volume IV Book 7: March of the King.

### Update 20: Battle of the Black Gate {#Update_20:_Battle_of_the_Black_Gate}

*Battle of the Black Gate* is the twentieth update and was released [21 March](/wiki/21_March), [2017](/wiki/2017).

**Book VIII - Battle of the Black Gate**

- The region The Wastes, including Noman-lands, [Slag-hills](/wiki/Slag-hills) and [Dagorlad](/wiki/Dagorlad) which surround the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate).
- New reputation items to exchange for armour.
- Epic Volume IV Book 8: Battle of the Black Gate.
