<i><b>The Grey Wizard</b></i> is a song by the epic metal band [Battlelore](../B/Battlelore.md) about [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md). It appears in their first full-length album, *[...Where the Shadows Lie](../_/%252E..Where_the_Shadows_Lie.md)*, released in [2002](../_/2002.md).
