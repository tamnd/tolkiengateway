<i><b>The Grey Wizard</b></i> is a song by the epic metal band [Battlelore](/wiki/Battlelore) about [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf). It appears in their first full-length album, *[...Where the Shadows Lie](/wiki/...Where_the_Shadows_Lie)*, released in [2002](/wiki/2002).
