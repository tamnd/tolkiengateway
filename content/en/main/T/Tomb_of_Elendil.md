The **Tomb of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil)** was the tomb of the first King of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) built by [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) on [Eilenaer](/wiki/Halifirien "Halifirien"), the mid-point of the Kingdom while the Kingdom endured. In an oval lawn upon the summit of the hill, on the eastern side, was placed a low mound which in time became covered with *[alfirin](/wiki/Alfirin "Alfirin")*. Before the mound was a black stone with the letters *[lambe](/wiki/Lambe "Lambe")*, *[ando](/wiki/Ando "Ando")*, *lambe* engraved upon it. After that time the hill was renamed [Amon Anwar](/wiki/Halifirien "Halifirien"), the “Hill of Awe”.<sup id="cite_ref-Tradition_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tradition-1">[1]</a></sup>

Its location was a secret kept for many centuries among the [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor) and the [Stewards](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor"). At Isildur's command, the hill would be a hallow and none would disturb it other than an heir of Elendil. The King should visit the hallow from time to time, especially when he felt the need of wisdom in days of danger or distress. It was also the place where he should bring his heir full-grown to manhood, and tell him of the making of the hallow and other matters that he should know.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Stewards were considered to have the rights and duties of the King, so they too followed this tradition even after the time of Kings, although they visited the place seldomly.<sup id="cite_ref-Tradition_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tradition-1">[1]</a></sup>

On this site, [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion) made his oath of alliance between [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) with [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>

When [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) was founded, Cirion judged that the "Tradition of Isildur" was now made void: The hallow was no longer "at the midpoint" and Gondor was not the Kingdom as it was when Isildur spoke. Cirion removed the casket that Isildur had set within the mound and sent it to the [Hallows](/wiki/Hallows) of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).

The green mound remained as the memorial and even when the hill had become a [Beacon hill](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor"), it was still a place of reverence to Gondor and to the Rohirrim, who named it in their own tongue [Halifirien](/wiki/Halifirien), the Holy Mount.<sup id="cite_ref-Tradition_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tradition-1">[1]</a></sup>
