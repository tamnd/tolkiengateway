**Third Age 2552** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2552**) is the 2552nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2552 was also known as **S.R. 952** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Frór](../F/Frór.md), the second of the three sons of [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Dáin I](../D/Dáin_I.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
