<i><b>The Hidden Door: Songs in the Key of Enchantment</b></i> is the first music album by artist [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith). It was released in [2007](/wiki/2007) with original art created by Nasmith for its cover art.<sup id="cite_ref-BandCamp_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BandCamp-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HiddenDoorArt_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HiddenDoorArt-2">[2]</a></sup>
