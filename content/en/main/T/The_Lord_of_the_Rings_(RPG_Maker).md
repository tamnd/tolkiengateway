<i><b>The Lord of the Rings</b></i> is a fan-made role-playing game using the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RPG_Maker#RPG_Maker_2000_and_2003" title="wikipedia:RPG Maker">RPG Maker 2000 engine</a>, adapting [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)* film trilogy. The game features over 30 hours of content, allowing players to control over 15 playable characters. The game mixes pixel art with edited pictures taken from the films.<sup id="cite_ref-rpgmaker.net_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rpgmaker.net-2">[2]</a></sup>

A demo for the game was released in 2005, with the full game being released in 2007. An updated version was released in 2022.<sup id="cite_ref-rmarchiv_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rmarchiv-1">[1]</a></sup>
