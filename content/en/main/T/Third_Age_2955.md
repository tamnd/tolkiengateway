**Third Age 2955** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2955**) is the 2955th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2955 was also known as **S.R. 1355** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Imrahil](../I/Imrahil.md), third child and only son of [Adrahil](../A/Adrahil.md), [Prince of Dol Amroth](../P/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
