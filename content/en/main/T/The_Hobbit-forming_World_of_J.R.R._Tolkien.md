<i><b>The Hobbit-forming World of J.R.R. Tolkien</b></i> refers to the title of an article which includes extracts from an interview with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). The interview was conducted by Henry Resnik over the telephone, for the magazine the *Saturday Evening Post* (published in [the issue of 2 July 1966](<../S/Saturday_Evening_Post_(2_July_1966).md>)). The interview was perhaps conducted on [2 March](../_/2_March.md) [1966](../_/1966.md) and according to Resnik they spoke for about half an hour. According to [Clyde S. Kilby](../C/Clyde_S._Kilby.md), Tolkien disliked the published article and had hung up on Resnik.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Kilby also explains that Tolkien denied to have said some affirmations about [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) being Europe, although these do not seem to be false per se.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

A more complete transcription of the interview was published in <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Niekas_18&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Niekas 18 (page does not exist)"><i>Niekas</i> Issue 18 (Late Spring 1967)</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The issue is readable <a class="external text" href="http://efanzines.com/Niekas/Niekas-18.pdf#37" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">online</a>. <span></span>
