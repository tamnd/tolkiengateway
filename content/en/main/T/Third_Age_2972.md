**Third Age 2972** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2972**) is the 2972nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2972 was also known as **S.R. 1372** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Daisy Gamgee](../D/Daisy_Gamgee.md), third child and first daughter of [Hamfast Gamgee](../H/Hamfast_Gamgee.md) and [Bell Goodchild](../B/Bell_Goodchild.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Halfast Gamgee](../H/Halfast_Gamgee.md), son of [Halfred of Overhill](../H/Halfred_of_Overhill.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-1">[1]</a></sup>
