<i><b>Tolkien&#39;s Hobbit: A Book of 20 Postcards</b></i> is a book containing twenty postcards with illustrations inspired by *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.

## Contents {#Contents}

1. [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe): *An Unexpected Party*
2. Gerd Renshof and Ron Ploeg: *Rough Steps Going up the Lonely Mountain*
3. [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee): *Captured by Spiders*
4. [Roger Garland](/wiki/Roger_Garland): *Gandalf*
5. [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee): *Bridge to Rivendell*
6. Capucine Mazille: *Smaug*
7. [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith): *Eagles to the Rescue*
8. Gerd Renshof and Ron Ploeg: *The Trolls*
9. [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe): *A Hobbit Dwelling*
10. [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee): *Thorin's Map*
11. [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes): *Barrels out of Bond*
12. [Cor Blok](/wiki/Cor_Blok): *The Game of Riddles*
13. [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee): *Into the Mountains*
14. [David Wenzel](/wiki/David_Wenzel): *An Unexpected Party*
15. Capucine Mazille: *In the House of Beorn*
16. [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee): *Thorin's Arrival*
17. [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe): *Gandalf*
18. Capucine Mazille: *The Battle of Five Armies*
19. [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee): *View over Mirkwood*
20. Gerd Renshof and Ron Ploeg: *Bilbo Flies on Eagle's Wings*

## From the back {#From_the_back}

A book of 20 postcards featuring a selection of artists' interpretations of JRR Tolkien's enduring classic work *The Hobbit*.
