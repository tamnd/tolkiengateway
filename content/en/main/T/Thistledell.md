The **Thistledell** was a valley that thistles grew in eastwards beyond the [Trout Bend](/wiki/Trout_Bend) and lay between the [Rhovanion Hills](/wiki/Rhovanion_Hills) and the northern parts of the [Southlands](/wiki/Mordor "Mordor"). The Thistledell was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios).

## History {#History}

In the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the Thistledell was a valley that a certain [Harfoot community](/wiki/Harfoots "Harfoots") passed through when migrating from one seasonal haven to the next, particularly from the [Norfield Glen](/wiki/Norfield_Glen) to the [Grove](/wiki/Grove).

During one such migration, the Brandyfoot family, [Poppy Proudfellow](/wiki/Poppy_Proudfellow), and [The Stranger](/wiki/The_Stranger) passed through the Thistledell while following in their haste after the main caravan of the Harfoot community which was far ahead of them.

Later, a group of three [Mystics](/wiki/Mystics) possibly passed through the Thistledell when they were stalking the Harfoot migration trail, seeking to find the Stranger.
