The **Thistledell** was a valley that thistles grew in eastwards beyond the [Trout Bend](Trout_Bend.md) and lay between the [Rhovanion Hills](../R/Rhovanion_Hills.md) and the northern parts of the [Southlands](../M/Mordor.md). The Thistledell was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md).

## History {#History}

In the late [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), the Thistledell was a valley that a certain [Harfoot community](../H/Harfoots.md) passed through when migrating from one seasonal haven to the next, particularly from the [Norfield Glen](../N/Norfield_Glen.md) to the [Grove](../G/Grove.md).

During one such migration, the Brandyfoot family, [Poppy Proudfellow](../P/Poppy_Proudfellow.md), and [The Stranger](The_Stranger.md) passed through the Thistledell while following in their haste after the main caravan of the Harfoot community which was far ahead of them.

Later, a group of three [Mystics](../M/Mystics.md) possibly passed through the Thistledell when they were stalking the Harfoot migration trail, seeking to find the Stranger.
