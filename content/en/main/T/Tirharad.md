**Tirharad** was a [Southlander](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Pre-Númenóreans") village that was located within the [Southlands](/wiki/Mordor "Mordor") of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) during the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Tirharad was invented by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The village was located in the [Southlands](/wiki/Southlands) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and specifically in the [Plateau of Gorgoroth](/wiki/Plateau_of_Gorgoroth). It was destroyed by the eruption of [Orodruin](/wiki/Mount_Doom "Mount Doom"), causing the survivors to abandon the place.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tirharad* is likely a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning *southwatch*, being possibly comprised of the elements *[Tir](/wiki/Tir)* ("watch") and *[Harad](/wiki/Harad)* ("south").
