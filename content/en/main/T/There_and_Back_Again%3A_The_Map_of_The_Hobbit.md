<i><b>There and Back Again: The Map of Hobbit</b></i> is a [1995](/wiki/1995) map-book by [Brian Sibley](/wiki/Brian_Sibley) and [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe), primarily covering the area of [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This beautiful colour version of Tolkien&#39;s map of Wilderland, lovingly re-created by artist John Howe, will take you to all the places Bilbo the Hobbit travelled through on his great Adventure.<br/><br/>
</p><p>The Map of The Hobbit comes with an illustrated explanatory booklet by Brian Sibley, creator of the highly successful radio dramatisation of The Lord of the Rings and is a companion map to The Map of Tolkien&#39;s Middle-earth.
</p>
</blockquote>
