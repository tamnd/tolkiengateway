**Third Age 1637** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1637**) is the 1637th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1637 was also known as **S.R. 37** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) ends in [the Shire](The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Periannath](../P/Periannath.md) survive, but suffer great loss.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>
