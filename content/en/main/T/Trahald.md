<i><b>trahald</b></i> means "burrowing" in [Northern Mannish](/wiki/Northern_Mannish). It was also the name of the [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") [Sméagol](/wiki/Sm%C3%A9agol).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A possible [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") \*TRAH.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
