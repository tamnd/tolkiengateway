**Tol Fuin** was an island off the coast of [Forlindon](/wiki/Forlindon).

## History {#History}

When the waves consumed [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and the regions around it after the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), not all of the western lands were lost. The highlands that had been [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), and came to be called [Taur-nu-Fuin](</wiki/Taur-nu-Fuin_(Dorthonion)> "Taur-nu-Fuin (Dorthonion)"), in part survived the inrush of the sea, and remained as an island far off the western coasts of Forlindon.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for Dark Isle from *[tol](/wiki/Tol "Tol")* and *[fuin](/wiki/Fuin "Fuin")*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
