**Third Age 2788** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2788**) is the 2788th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2788 was also known as **S.R. 1188** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Roäc](../R/Roäc.md) son of [Carc](../C/Carc.md) is born.

## Calculation of the Date {#Calculation_of_the_Date}

[Thorin and Company](Thorin_and_Company.md) came to [Erebor](../E/Erebor.md) in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2941](Third_Age_2941.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When they met Roäc he told them that it was "a hundred years and three and fifty since I came out of the egg".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Subtracting 153 years from 2941 yields the year 2788. <span></span>
