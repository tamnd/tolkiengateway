The **Trolls' lair**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> was a troll-hole<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> that was made by three [trolls -](/wiki/Trolls "Trolls") <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Tom" title="Tom">Tom</a>, [Bert](/wiki/Bert) and [William](/wiki/William) - who captured [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") and [the dwarves](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") as described in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. Made from a cave, the lair served as the three trolls hideout from the [Sun](/wiki/Sun), their treasury and storeroom.

## Description {#Description}

The cave floor had bones and an untidy litter of plunder, from brass button to pots of gold. On shelves was a good deal of food, along with a barrel of ale. Clothing of victims hung on the walls as well as several swords. There was also a horrible smell in the air.<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395753">.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider{margin-top:1rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem;color:var(--tg-color-subtle);display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:space-between}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider:not(:empty){gap:1rem;white-space:nowrap}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::before,.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::after{content:"";height:1px;width:100%;background-color:var(--tg-border-color-base)}</style><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>One may guess that your <a href="/wiki/Trolls" title="Trolls">trolls</a> had plundered, other plunderers, or come on the remnants of old robberies in some hole in the mountains of old. I have heard that there are still forgotten treasures to be found in the deserted caverns of the mines of <a href="/wiki/Moria" title="Moria">Moria</a>, since the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs" title="War of the Dwarves and Orcs">dwarf and goblin war</a>.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a><sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-4">[4]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395753"/></div>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

After [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) had rescued [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) and his party from the trolls during the [Quest for Erebor](/wiki/Quest_for_Erebor) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>, he reminded them that the trolls must have a cave or hole to hide in from the sun. Searching about, they followed the marks of stony boots uphill to a big stone door hidden by bushes. Pushing and incantations by Gandalf did not open it. Then Bilbo asked if the key he had found would help. It did.<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-4">[4]</a></sup>

The party took much of the food, the barrel of ale, and the gold. Gandalf and Thorin took two swords with beautiful scabbards and jewelled hilts, and Bilbo took a knife in a leather sheath (which for a hobbit was as good as a short sword). Outside the cave the party ate and rested, and then buried the pots of gold near the river with many spells put over them.<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-4">[4]</a></sup>

Later, in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) read the runes on the swords of Gandalf and Thorin. He revealed that they were very old and had been made by the High Elves of the West, in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin). Thus from the Trolls' lair Thorin obtained [Orcrist](/wiki/Orcrist) (meaning Goblin-cleaver in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)) and Gandalf got [Glamdring](/wiki/Glamdring) (meaning Foe-hammer in Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Much later Bilbo would name his short sword from the Trolls' lair "[Sting](/wiki/Sting)".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) on October 18 [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) and [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) passed by the Trolls' lair by accident and saw the three trolls that had been turned to stone outside.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the manuscript for *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, the Company found something else in the Trolls' lair besides the three swords - they also found the key to the [Back Door](/wiki/Back_Door) of the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain), although they did not know it at the time.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Gandalf took charge of the key in the cave and gave it to Thorin just before he departed from the Company at the gates of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> On [Durin's Day](/wiki/Durin%27s_Day) it was Bilbo who recalled the troll-key and they found that it was the right one to open the door.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1968: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)" title="The Hobbit (1968 radio series)">The Hobbit (1968 radio series)</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Trolls&#39; lair features in this adaptation without any difference from book.</dd></dl>

<b>1977: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)" title="The Hobbit (1977 film)">The Hobbit (1977 film)</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In this film, the Trolls&#39; lair does not have a locked door as in the book.</dd></dl>

<b>1979: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1979_radio_series)" title="The Hobbit (1979 radio series)">The Hobbit (1979 radio series)</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Trolls&#39; lair appears in this adaptation with the same role as in the book.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)" title="The Hobbit (2003 video game)">The Hobbit (2003 video game)</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the game, the Trolls&#39; lair is explored by Bilbo, and it is implied that it was built by <a href="/wiki/Witch-king" title="Witch-king">Witch-king</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Trolls&#39; lair, along with the Stone Trolls&#39; Glade, is located in the Trollshaws region. The cave is inaccessible.</dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Trolls&#39; lair appears in the first film  of <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> film series</a> as an open tunnel with no locked door blocking it. Gandalf finds <a href="/wiki/Sting" title="Sting">Sting</a> inside the cave and gives it to Bilbo.</dd></dl>
