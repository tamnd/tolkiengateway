**Third Age 2002** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2002**) is the 2002nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2002 was also known as **S.R. 402** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Fall](../S/Second_Fall_of_Minas_Ithil.md) of [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md), afterwards known as [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md). The [palantír](../I/Ithil-stone.md) is captured.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Minas Anor](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), the *Tower of the Setting Sun*, is renamed Minas Tirith, the *Tower of the Guard*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
