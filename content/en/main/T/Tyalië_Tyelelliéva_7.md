<b><i>Tyalië Tyelelliéva</i> 7</b> is an issue of the journal *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va)*.

## Contents {#Contents}

- Editorial, Celestial Events, News and Notes
- Journal Reviews
- I-Lam na-Ngoldathon (or \[complete\] Gnomish Lexicon) review by Lisa Star
- Letters to the Editor from Andreas Bigger (on Helge's Elvish names), [David Doughan](/wiki/David_Doughan) (on Russian controversies), [Nancy Martsch](/wiki/Nancy_Martsch) (on the Plotz declension et alia) and [Rhona Beare](/wiki/Rhona_Beare) (on oaken doors)
- Comments on relative pronouns (article in last issue), from [Ivan Derzhanski](/wiki/Ivan_Derzhanski), Lisa Star, and Maciej Garbowski
- Scroll of the Kings of Númenor by Lisa Star (detail of a Quenya text in tengwar)
- Commentary on [Fíriel's song](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel%27s_song) by [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) (detailed analysis of Tolkien's Quenya text from the Lost Road)
- Rules of the Game, translated by Sergey Bratus (excerpts from Rules of "Hobbitish Games" played in Moscow)
- The Ever-Horned One by Lisa Star (intensives and reduplication in Elvish and Indo-European languages)
- Tengwar Inscriptions from D &amp; D (unreadable)
