<b><i>Tyalië Tyelelliéva</i> 7</b> is an issue of the journal *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](Tyalië_Tyelelliéva.md)*.

## Contents {#Contents}

- Editorial, Celestial Events, News and Notes
- Journal Reviews
- I-Lam na-Ngoldathon (or \[complete\] Gnomish Lexicon) review by Lisa Star
- Letters to the Editor from Andreas Bigger (on Helge's Elvish names), [David Doughan](../D/David_Doughan.md) (on Russian controversies), [Nancy Martsch](../N/Nancy_Martsch.md) (on the Plotz declension et alia) and [Rhona Beare](../R/Rhona_Beare.md) (on oaken doors)
- Comments on relative pronouns (article in last issue), from [Ivan Derzhanski](../I/Ivan_Derzhanski.md), Lisa Star, and Maciej Garbowski
- Scroll of the Kings of Númenor by Lisa Star (detail of a Quenya text in tengwar)
- Commentary on [Fíriel's song](../F/Fíriel's_song.md) by [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) (detailed analysis of Tolkien's Quenya text from the Lost Road)
- Rules of the Game, translated by Sergey Bratus (excerpts from Rules of "Hobbitish Games" played in Moscow)
- The Ever-Horned One by Lisa Star (intensives and reduplication in Elvish and Indo-European languages)
- Tengwar Inscriptions from D &amp; D (unreadable)
