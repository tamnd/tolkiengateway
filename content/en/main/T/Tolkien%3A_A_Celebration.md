<i><b>Tolkien: A Celebration: Collected Writings on a Literary Legacy</b></i> is a collection of essays on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [his works](../../index/W/Writings_by_J.R.R._Tolkien.md). Many of the chapters focus on [Catholic](../C/Christianity.md) themes.

The article "Tolkien and C.S. Lewis: An Interview with [Walter Hooper](../W/Walter_Hooper.md)" includes quotes attributed to J.R.R. Tolkien.
