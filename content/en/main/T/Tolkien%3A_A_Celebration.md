<i><b>Tolkien: A Celebration: Collected Writings on a Literary Legacy</b></i> is a collection of essays on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [his works](/wiki/Index:Writings_by_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Writings by J.R.R. Tolkien"). Many of the chapters focus on [Catholic](/wiki/Christianity "Christianity") themes.

The article "Tolkien and C.S. Lewis: An Interview with [Walter Hooper](/wiki/Walter_Hooper)" includes quotes attributed to J.R.R. Tolkien.
