<i><b>The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack - Special Edition</b></i> is a special edition album comprising of the soundtrack from *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)*, by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore). It was released digitally and on CD on [9 December](/wiki/9_December) [2014](/wiki/2014).
