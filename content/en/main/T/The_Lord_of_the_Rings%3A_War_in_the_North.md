<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</b></i> (commonly abbreviated to **WITN**) is a role-playing video-game developed by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snowblind_Studios" title="wikipedia:Snowblind Studios">Snowblind Studios</a> and published by [Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment](../W/Warner_Bros._Interactive_Entertainment.md) for <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_3" title="wikipedia:PlayStation 3">PlayStation 3</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_360" title="wikipedia:Xbox 360">Xbox 360</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Windows" title="wikipedia:Microsoft Windows">Windows</a>. The game takes place during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) but adapts the seldom-mentioned battles that took place across [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md). The game was released between [1 November](../_/1_November.md) and [25 November](../_/25_November.md) [2011](../_/2011.md).<sup id="cite_ref-FAQ_Regions_Release_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FAQ_Regions_Release-3">[3]</a></sup>

The <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OS_X" title="wikipedia:OS X">Mac OS X</a> version of the game was developed and published by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feral_Interactive" title="wikipedia:Feral Interactive">Feral Interactive</a> and released on [18 September](../_/18_September.md) [2013](../_/2013.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mac_version_announcement_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mac_version_announcement-1">[1]</a></sup>

The game is based on both the books and the films.<sup id="cite_ref-sourceE_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceE-6">[6]</a></sup>

Although the game includes familiar locations from [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) it does not revolve around the core story of *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*; instead, it creates an original tale.<sup id="cite_ref-Dev_Interview1_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dev_Interview1-7">[7]</a></sup> The player controls his own fellowship, a trio of fighters - [Eradan](<../E/Eradan_(video_game_character).md>), a [Dúnadan](../D/Dúnedain.md) "[Ranger of the North](../R/Ranger_of_the_North.md)," [Andriel](../A/Andriel.md), an [elven](../E/Elves.md) "Loremaster of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md)"<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 1]</a></sup> and [Farin](<../F/Farin_(video_game_character).md>), a [dwarvern](../D/Dwarves.md) "Champion of Erebor" - to get behind enemy lines and have to stop [Agandaûr](../A/Agandaûr.md), the game's antagonist, before his plan succeeds.

## Plot {#Plot}

### Prologue {#Prologue}

The game starts with an introduction, narrated by [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md). Gandalf explains that heroes like [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and Gandalf are honoured much, but that without a few, not well-known heroes - [Eradan](<../E/Eradan_(video_game_character).md>), [Andriel](../A/Andriel.md) and [Farin](<../F/Farin_(video_game_character).md>) - the north of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) would have been lost.<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-9">[8]</a></sup>

The game then switches to [Bree](../B/Bree.md), where the three heroes make their way to the [Prancing Pony](../P/Prancing_Pony.md), just a few days before Frodo arrives in Bree. Arrived in the Prancing Pony, they find [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) in a corner of the inn. They tell Aragorn that the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) attacked and defeated the [Rangers](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) at [Sarn Ford](../S/Sarn_Ford.md), and a flashback shown in which the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) meets a [Black Númenórean](../B/Black_Númenóreans.md), [Agandaûr](../A/Agandaûr.md). Eradan, Andriel and Farin overhear their conversation: Agandaûr says he has summoned an army of [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) and assembled an army in [Fornost](../F/Fornost.md), to aid the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) in the Hunt of the Ring. The Witch-king sends Agandaûr back to Fornost, so he can attack immediately. The flashback ends, and the game returns to the Prancing Pony<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-9">[8]</a></sup>

Farin asks Aragorn why the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) attack [the Shire](The_Shire.md), to which he answers that [a Hobbit](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) with [an important burden](The_One_Ring.md) is making his way to Bree. And that this "must be protected at all cost". Aragorn sends Eradan, Andriel and Farin to Fornost, where they have to stop [Agandaûr](../A/Agandaûr.md)'s army before it can attack.<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-9">[8]</a></sup>

### Chapter 1: Fornost {#Chapter_1:_Fornost}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Beleram_&amp;_Orcs.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/dd/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Beleram_%26_Orcs.jpg/300px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Beleram_%26_Orcs.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="169" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/dd/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Beleram_%26_Orcs.jpg/450px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Beleram_%26_Orcs.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/dd/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Beleram_%26_Orcs.jpg/600px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Beleram_%26_Orcs.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1280" data-file-height="720"/></a><figcaption><a href="../B/Beleram.md" title="Beleram">Beleram</a> - captured by <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="../G/Goblins.md" title="Goblins">Goblins</a> - in <a class="mw-redirect" href="../F/Fornost.md" title="Fornost">Fornost</a>.</figcaption></figure>

As soon as Eradan, Andriel and Farin enter the ruins of Fornost they are attacked by a band of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Goblins](../G/Goblins.md). After clearing the main gate, they end up in the pits under Fornost. There they hear an animal screaming. After finding their way out of the pits, they find the source of the noise - a [giant Eagle](../E/Eagles.md). The three heroes unchain the Eagle and kill its jailers. The Eagle introduces himself as [Beleram](../B/Beleram.md), a servant of [Gwaihir](../G/Gwaihir.md) who gathered news but was taken out of the air by siege engines and sorcerers. Together the Eagle and the three heroes make a plan: while Beleram would attract the enemy's attention, Eradan, Andriel and Farin would make their way up to the battlements to destroy the siege weapons.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup> The plan works out successfully, as all siege weapons as well as two Orc sorcerers are destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup> The player, furthermore, obtains a scroll with unreadable text.<sup id="cite_ref-Cult_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cult-12">[11]</a></sup>

When reaching the outer wards of Fornost, the players meet again with Beleram. The eagle thanks his rescuers again and tells them that he saw [Elladan](../E/Elladan.md) and [Elrohir](../E/Elrohir.md) within the ruins. While searching for the two [Half-elves](../H/Half-elves.md), they defeat a giant crossbow, used by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), and are trapped by a Troll. Just shortly after the death of the Troll, the sons of [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) appear.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Eradan,_Andriel_&amp;_Farin2.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/ed/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Eradan%2C_Andriel_%26_Farin2.jpg/250px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Eradan%2C_Andriel_%26_Farin2.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="141" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/ed/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Eradan%2C_Andriel_%26_Farin2.jpg/375px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Eradan%2C_Andriel_%26_Farin2.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/ed/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Eradan%2C_Andriel_%26_Farin2.jpg/500px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Eradan%2C_Andriel_%26_Farin2.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1280" data-file-height="720"/></a><figcaption><a href="../E/Eradan_(video_game_character).md" title="Eradan (video game character)">Eradan</a>, <a href="../F/Farin_(video_game_character).md" title="Farin (video game character)">Farin</a> and <a href="../A/Andriel.md" title="Andriel">Andriel</a>.</figcaption></figure>

Andriel introduces Eradan and Farin to Elladan and Elrohir, and informs them about their task. They collaborate in this task, but instead of just stopping the army to attack they decide to kill Agandaûr. The sons of Elrond guide the player to the citadel. They separate, however, after defeating another giant crossbow. Eventually, they are locked on a bridge between two gates. After surviving an ambush, the sons of Elrond appear again and open the gate.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup> After defeating another ambush, they arrive at the gate of the citadel, which is protected by a magic spell to stop intruders. While the sons of Elrond undo the magic, the player defends them against an attacking force of Orcs, Goblins and a Troll. As Eradan, Andriel, Farin, Elrohir and Elladan enter the citadel, Beleram remains outside to protect them against enemy reinforcements.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup>

Inside, they overhear a conversation between Agandaûr and [Tharzog](Tharzog.md), chieftain of the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) of [Mount Gram](../M/Mount_Gram.md). Tharzog tells Agandaûr that there are intruders in Fornost, after which Agandaûr angrily commands him to find and kill them immediately. After Agandaûr leaves, the player and the sons of Elrond go onward in the tower. Tharzog, however, notices them and as a gate is closed, the player is cut off from the sons of Elrond. Tharzog and his guard attack Eradan, Andriel and Farin, but are defeated and killed.<sup id="cite_ref-Citadel_Tower_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Citadel_Tower-16">[15]</a></sup>

At the same time, Elladan and Elrohir fight Agandaûr on the top of the Citadel tower. Agandaûr is no match for the twins alone, but when Eradan, Andriel and Farin join them he flees on the back of a [Fell beast](../F/Fell_beast.md). Beleram attempts to chase him, but Agandaûr summons a thunderstorm and escapes.<sup id="cite_ref-Citadel_Tower_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Citadel_Tower-16">[15]</a></sup>

Beleram returns to Gwaihir, in the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), to inform him about the events in Fornost, Elladan and Elrohir return to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and Eradan, Andriel and Farin go back to Sarn's Ford to inform Halbarad.<sup id="cite_ref-Citadel_Tower_16-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Citadel_Tower-16">[15]</a></sup>

### Chapter 2: The Barrow Downs {#Chapter_2:_The_Barrow_Downs}

The leader of the camp, [Halbarad](../H/Halbarad.md), sends the party to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), to report on the events at Fornost to [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md). As they are to travel through the [Barrow Downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md), Halbarad asks them to keep an eye out for two rangers, Luin and Kilaran, who went missing in the area. Traversing the Barrow Downs, Eradan, Andriel and Farin encounter many wights as they fight their way through the tombs, following ranger clues into Amon Gorthad, the Great Barrow. There, they find Kilaran ensnared in nightmares. Luin has fallen to a great wight, controlling him, and they must defeat him to make their way out and finally head to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-sourceF_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceF-17">[16]</a></sup> The fellowship travels through the Barrow Downs, where they have to find two lost rangers.<sup id="cite_ref-sourceF_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceF-17">[16]</a></sup>

### Chapter 3: Ettenmoors {#Chapter_3:_Ettenmoors}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/L/LOTR-WITN-Agandaur2.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/ad/LOTR-WITN-Agandaur2.png/200px-LOTR-WITN-Agandaur2.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="113" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/ad/LOTR-WITN-Agandaur2.png/300px-LOTR-WITN-Agandaur2.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/ad/LOTR-WITN-Agandaur2.png/400px-LOTR-WITN-Agandaur2.png 2x" data-file-width="1280" data-file-height="720"/></a><figcaption><a href="../A/Agandaûr.md" title="Agandaûr">Agandaûr</a>, the game&#39;s main antagonist.</figcaption></figure>

In [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) the fellowship meets several members of the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md),<sup id="cite_ref-sourceF_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceF-17">[16]</a></sup> scout the land around Rivendell,<sup id="cite_ref-sourceG_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceG-18">[17]</a></sup> and fight [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) with [Elladan](../E/Elladan.md) and [Elrohir](../E/Elrohir.md).

### Chapter 4: Gundabad {#Chapter_4:_Gundabad}

Whilst the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) travels towards [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) to destroy [the One Ring](The_One_Ring.md), [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) has sent one of his most cruel servants, the [Black Númenórean](../B/Black_Númenóreans.md) [Agandaûr](../A/Agandaûr.md), to the North to destroy [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and defeat the [Free peoples](../F/Free_peoples~19934.md) of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md). Eradan, Andriel and Farin travel to [Gundabad](../G/Gundabad.md) to discover the strength of Agandaûr's army.<sup id="cite_ref-Gundabadtrailer_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gundabadtrailer-19">[18]</a></sup>

### Chapter 5: Mirkwood {#Chapter_5:_Mirkwood}

Eradan, Andriel and Farin travel with Beleram to [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) in search of [Radagast](../R/Radagast.md). In the air they’re attacked by Wolfram, a sorcerer and a [Fell Beast](../F/Fell_Beast~21258.md) rider, after which they fall from the Eagle’s back. After slaying Wolfram, Wolfram’s Fell Beast tries to escape but is killed by Beleram. Andriel leads the others deeper into Mirkwood. They are ambushed by a group of Orcs and an Orc shaman. Eventually the fellowship enter a cave where they defeat a [Cave-troll](../C/Cave-trolls.md).<sup id="cite_ref-sourceJ_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceJ-20">[19]</a></sup>

Commentators have noted that this is the first iteration of *The Lord of the Rings*-based games which is aimed at an older audience - predicted to be the first game in the series to be given an M-rating - with the inclusion of graphic violence and gory bloody detail<sup id="cite_ref-Kotaku_Throats_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kotaku_Throats-21">[20]</a></sup> with players being able to decapitate orcs and slash limbs off enemies.<sup id="cite_ref-Kotaku_Blood_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kotaku_Blood-22">[21]</a></sup> When defeating enemies you can gain experience and loot. With experience you can gain new skills; there are twenty skills for each race.<sup id="cite_ref-sourceE_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceE-6">[6]</a></sup> Every skill has five different ranks. Loot can also be found in chests. Some items can only be used by a certain race, therefore it is possible to trade with the other characters of your fellowship.<sup id="cite_ref-sourceH_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceH-23">[22]</a></sup> The gameplay - in both single and multiplayer modes - revolves around strategically combining the different abilities of the threesome to good effect, for instance, the dwarf can find treasure and spot weaknesses in structures, which lead to alternate roads,<sup id="cite_ref-sourceJ_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceJ-20">[19]</a></sup> whereas the elf can find herbs to heal other party members. When in single-player, the other two characters will be controlled by computer AI.<sup id="cite_ref-Kotaku_Party_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kotaku_Party-5">[5]</a></sup> Working together with the other characters of your fellowship is very important to defeat stronger enemies.

It is possible to co-op (cooperative mode) both local and online;<sup id="cite_ref-sourceE_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceE-6">[6]</a></sup> players can join the game at every moment.<sup id="cite_ref-sourceH_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceH-23">[22]</a></sup> While the Xbox 360 and the PlayStation 3 support split-screen, online split-screen and online multiplayer, the PC only includes online multiplayer.<sup id="cite_ref-sourceK_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceK-24">[23]</a></sup>

The game will not be open world, nevertheless, some areas will be more open than others.<sup id="cite_ref-sourceB_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceB-25">[24]</a></sup> It is still unknown if it is possible to return to earlier locations. There will be several NPC's (Non-playable characters) in the game, some of them will have a more passive role while others fight beside you.<sup id="cite_ref-sourceG_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceG-18">[17]</a></sup> Like many RPGs, the dialogue system is very important, and affects the game's storyline.<sup id="cite_ref-sourceE_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceE-6">[6]</a></sup>

If you are in need of help you can call the eagle Beleram, who can help you to defeat bigger or stronger enemies. Calling him is limited in the beginning, but while leveling you can find items which make it possible to call him. It is impossible to call Beleram underground or in buildings and there is a cool down for using him.<sup id="cite_ref-sourceD_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sourceD-26">[25]</a></sup>

## Development {#Development}

Development on War in the North started in early [2010](../_/2010.md) or late [2009](../_/2009.md), [Saul Zaentz Company](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md) claimed the name *The Lord of the Rings: War in the North* in [June](../J/June.md) 2009.<sup id="cite_ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39">[38]</a></sup> However, the game was not officially announced until [18 March](../_/18_March.md) 2010.<sup id="cite_ref-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-40">[39]</a></sup> Since its announcement several [images](../../category/I/Images_from_The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md), <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Traillers">traillers</a> and concept art of several locations and characters has been released.

### Continuity issues {#Continuity_issues}

*War in the North* is considered to be a tie-in to Peter Jackson's film adaptations of *The Lord of the Rings*. But since this game was developed before the [film adaptations](<The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>) of *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* entered production, *War in the North* features minor continuity errors in relation to those movies.

Elrond summarizes the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs.md) as it is described in the novel; with [Azog](../A/Azog.md) perishing during the [Battle of Azanulbizar](../B/Battle_of_Azanulbizar.md) at the hands of Dain Ironfoot. However, in the movies, Azog survives that battle, but was killed many years later by [Thorin Oakenshield](Thorin_Oakenshield.md) at the Battle of Five Armies.

The winged fire-drake [Úrgost](../_/Úrgost.md) is depicted as having four legs and a separate pair of wings, as with most traditional depictions of Western dragons. This contradicts the movies' portrayal of [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md), who is depicted in *The Hobbit* film trilogy as having wings attached to his forelegs like a wyvern (though Smaug was originally intended to have separate wings as well, as shown in the theatrical cut of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md)*).

<h3><span id="Collector.27s_Edition"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Collector&#39;s_Edition">Collector&#39;s Edition</span></h3>

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/L/LOTR-WITN-Collector&#39;s_Edition.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/38/LOTR-WITN-Collector%27s_Edition.png/150px-LOTR-WITN-Collector%27s_Edition.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="150" height="201" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/38/LOTR-WITN-Collector%27s_Edition.png/225px-LOTR-WITN-Collector%27s_Edition.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/38/LOTR-WITN-Collector%27s_Edition.png/300px-LOTR-WITN-Collector%27s_Edition.png 2x" data-file-width="620" data-file-height="832"/></a><figcaption>The collector&#39;s edition of <b>The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</b>.</figcaption></figure>

Besides the normal version of the game, a collector's edition will be released. This collector's edition will include an artbook, a behind-the-scenes music DVD, a quiver case as well as access to digital gubbins: icons, avatars and themes. Some retailers give extra add-ons. Both the collector's Edition and the special add-ons will be available in North-America and Europe; it is still unknown if it will be available in other parts of the world.<sup id="cite_ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41">[40]</a></sup>

## Reception {#Reception}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>It&#39;s a very handsome game with well-voiced cutscenes, and places the compelling Lord of the Rings realm in the hands of a developer with solid RPG credentials. Fans of both have good reason to look forward to a unique entry in the series.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a class="external text" href="http://www.kotaku.com" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Kotaku</a><sup id="cite_ref-Kotaku_Party_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kotaku_Party-5">[5]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>

Commentators immediately picked up on Snowblind Studios' experience with role-playing games (such as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate:_Dark_Alliance" title="wikipedia:Baldur&#39;s Gate: Dark Alliance">Baldur&#39;s Gate</a></i> and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champions_of_Norrath:_Realms_of_EverQuest" title="wikipedia:Champions of Norrath: Realms of EverQuest">Champions of Norrath: Realms of EverQuest</a></i>) along with frequent mentions of the more "mature" and "brutal"<sup id="cite_ref-CVG_E3_42-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CVG_E3-42">[41]</a></sup> design of the game, and the desire for an action RPG in the manner of previous Snowblind games.<sup id="cite_ref-Kotaku_Untold_43-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kotaku_Untold-43">[42]</a></sup>
