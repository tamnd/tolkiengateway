**The Grey Annals**, first part in *[The War of the Jewels](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels)* book, is an account of the early events in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), such as: the awakening of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), the tale of [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and the story of [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin").
