**The Drúedain** is a chapter in [Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales), a posthumously published work of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) edited and published by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien). It is in its entirety an excerpt from a much longer unfinished essay titled *[Of Dwarves and Men](/wiki/Of_Dwarves_and_Men)*, the rest of which was published in *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The essay speaks about the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain), little-appearing in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. It explores their history during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) being a part of the [Second House](/wiki/House_of_Haleth "House of Haleth") of [Men](/wiki/Men), and later their temporary presence on [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) and then their staying in [Andrast](/wiki/Andrast).

It is mentioned about their being confused with [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) hunting and killing them in their ignorance.

There is a mention of their peculiar "[magic](/wiki/Magic "Magic")" abilities and customs, exemplified in the First Age story *[The Faithful Stone](/wiki/The_Faithful_Stone)*.

Among the [Atani](/wiki/Atani), the [Folk of Haleth](/wiki/Folk_of_Haleth) were the strangest. They kept to themselves, and had their own language. Among the Folk of Haleth, a small group of people named the Druedain were even stranger.
