**Tuckborough** was an old and important settlement in the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), nestled in the [Green Hill Country](/wiki/Green_Hill_Country). A road connected Tuckborough with [the Yale](/wiki/The_Yale "The Yale") and [Stock](/wiki/Stock) (with the [Causeway](/wiki/Causeway)) to the east.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was the location of [Great Smials](/wiki/Great_Smials), the ancestral seat of the [Took clan](/wiki/Took_Family "Took Family"), the hereditary [Thains](/wiki/Thain "Thain") of the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
