**Tom Sherry** is a Scottish artist.

Sherry joined [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) at the age of thirteen, following the [BBC](/wiki/BBC) [radio production of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"). He was a regular contributor to many different publications submitting scores of black and white ink illustrations. His art has been published in <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_York_Shire_Post&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The York Shire Post (page does not exist)">The York Shire Post</a></i>, <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tasarion_(journal)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tasarion (journal) (page does not exist)">Tasarion</a></i>, <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Aglared_(journal)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aglared (journal) (page does not exist)">Aglared</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Amon_Hen_(journal)" title="Amon Hen (journal)">Amon Hen</a></i> - for which he did numerous cover drawings.
