**Third Age 1981** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1981**) is the 1981st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1981 was also known as **S.R. 381** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Náin I](../N/Náin_I.md) slain by the [Balrog](../D/Durin's_Bane.md) in Moria.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppA3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA3-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Thráin I](Thráin_I.md), son of Náin I, becomes the King of [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA3_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA3-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) flee from [Moria](../M/Moria.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA3_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA3-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Many [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) of [Lórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) flee south.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ut_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ut-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md) and [Nimrodel](../N/Nimrodel.md) are lost<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup> on their way to [Edhellond](../E/Edhellond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ut_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ut-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Around that time [Imrazôr](../I/Imrazôr.md) meets and marries [Mithrellas](../M/Mithrellas.md), a maiden of Nimrodel.<sup id="cite_ref-ut_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ut-3">[3]</a></sup>
