The **Two Lamps** of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) were **[Illuin](/wiki/Illuin)** and **[Ormal](/wiki/Ormal)**, they stood upon pillars in the far north and south of [the world](/wiki/Arda "Arda") during the [Spring of Arda](/wiki/Spring_of_Arda).

## History {#History}

The Two Lamps were wrought by [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) at the request of [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) for she had planted many seeds that needed light for growth. The lamps were filled with light by [Varda](/wiki/Varda), hallowed by [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) and set on great pillars, taller than any mountains of the later days.

- Illuin was set in the north of Middle-earth, placed upon the tower [Helcar](/wiki/Helcar)<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-1">[1]</a></sup> and shone with a blue light.
- Ormal was set in the far south, placed upon the tower [Ringil](</wiki/Ringil_(tower)> "Ringil (tower)")<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-1">[1]</a></sup> and shone with a golden light.

In the centre of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), where the light of the Two Lamps mingled and the vegetation of [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) was richer, was the [Great Lake](/wiki/Great_Lake) and the island [Almaren](/wiki/Almaren), where the Valar first dwelt. The light of the Lamps of the Valar flowed out over the Earth, so that all was lit as it were in a changeless day.<sup id="cite_ref-Days_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Days-2">[2]</a></sup>

However Melkor was aware of all that was done for he had spies among the Maiar. Whilst the Valar feasted and [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas) slept Melkor came forth suddenly to war and striking the first blow against the lights of Illuin and Ormal, cast down the mighty pillars and broke the Two Lamps.

It was due to the destruction of the pillars that the lands were broken and new seas arose; the shape of Arda and the symmetry of its waters and its lands were marred so that the first designs of the Valar were never after restored.<sup id="cite_ref-Days_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Days-2">[2]</a></sup> The great northern inland sea of [Helcar](/wiki/Sea_of_Helcar "Sea of Helcar") was formed as a result of the destruction of Illuin and it is believed that the [Sea of Ringil](/wiki/Sea_of_Ringil) to the south was formed from the fall of Ormal.

With the Two Lamps gone and their dwellings on Almaren destroyed the Valar retreated to [Aman](/wiki/Aman) and founded [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). There they would go on to create the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor"), which were a pair of gold and silver light, similar to the Two Lamps before them.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) noted that the story of the Lamps in its early stage was very different from the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)*. According to older writings not used in the published *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, the Valar wanted peace with Melkor, and decided to ask his assistance: they wished to fix the lamps upon Arda's ground. Melkor, black with hate and envy of the rest of the Valar, agreed to help. He gave Aulë a substance which was both sturdy and strong: ice, which was unknown to the Valar. Melkor bided his time and allowed the Valar to do as they wished until the fateful day when the Lamps' light and heat melted the ice and crashed upon Arda, flooding it with water and darkness.

The one remaining influence from the older version of the story was that the fall of the towers created inland seas, a vestige of the notion that the towers had been made of ice that melted.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In Tolkien's latest writings where Arda was a [round world](/wiki/Round_World_version_of_the_Silmarillion "Round World version of the Silmarillion") from its beginning, the Two Lamps never truly existed and were instead a subject of Númenórean mythology.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
