The **Tea Club, Barrovian Society**, also known as the **T.C.B.S.**, was an organization that met regularly at Barrow Stores at [King Edward's School](../K/King_Edward's_School.md) in [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md). The core members were considered to be the "big four" of Tolkien, [Geoffrey Bache Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md), [Christopher Wiseman](../C/Christopher_Wiseman.md), and [Robert Gilson](../R/Robert_Gilson.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The friends continued to stay in contact with each other's literary work until [1916](../_/1916.md). [Sidney Barrowclough](../S/Sidney_Barrowclough.md) and the Payton brothers, [Ralph](../R/Ralph_Payton.md) and [Wilfrid](../W/Wilfrid_Payton.md), were younger than Tolkien and continued it for some time after they left.

Almost all of them died in [World War I](../W/World_War_I.md). Of Tolkien's close personal friends, only [Christopher Wiseman](../C/Christopher_Wiseman.md) survived the war,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> a fact which greatly affected him.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
