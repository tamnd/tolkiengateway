The **Tea Club, Barrovian Society**, also known as the **T.C.B.S.**, was an organization that met regularly at Barrow Stores at [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School) in [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham). The core members were considered to be the "big four" of Tolkien, [Geoffrey Bache Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith), [Christopher Wiseman](/wiki/Christopher_Wiseman), and [Robert Gilson](/wiki/Robert_Gilson).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The friends continued to stay in contact with each other's literary work until [1916](/wiki/1916). [Sidney Barrowclough](/wiki/Sidney_Barrowclough) and the Payton brothers, [Ralph](/wiki/Ralph_Payton "Ralph Payton") and [Wilfrid](/wiki/Wilfrid_Payton "Wilfrid Payton"), were younger than Tolkien and continued it for some time after they left.

Almost all of them died in [World War I](/wiki/World_War_I). Of Tolkien's close personal friends, only [Christopher Wiseman](/wiki/Christopher_Wiseman) survived the war,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> a fact which greatly affected him.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
