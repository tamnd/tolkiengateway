<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King - The Complete Recordings</b></i> is an album, by [Howard Shore](../H/Howard_Shore.md), comprising the soundtrack from the [extended edition](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.
