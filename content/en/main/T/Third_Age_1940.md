**Third Age 1940** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1940**) is the 1940th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1940 was also known as **S.R. 340** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) renew communications and form an alliance. [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md) weds [Fíriel](../F/Fíriel.md) daughter of [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
