**The Brothers Hildebrandt** were a duo of fantasy artists consisting of the twin brothers **Greg Hildebrandt** ([January 23](/wiki/23_January "23 January"), [1939](/wiki/1939) - [October 31](/wiki/October_31), [2024](/wiki/2024)) and **Tim Hildebrandt** (January 23, [1939](/wiki/1939) – [June 11](/wiki/11_June "11 June"), [2006](/wiki/2006)).

The brothers have been recognized artists in the world of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-inspired art. Other art includes the original poster art of <i><a class="external text" href="http://starwars.wikia.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_The_Art_of_the_Brothers_Hildebrandt" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Star Wars</a></i> and over a hundred <i><a class="external text" href="http://marvel.wikia.com/wiki/Hildebrandt_Brothers/Gallery" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Marvel Masterpieces</a></i>, including *Swimsuit Specials*.

They created reference photographs prior to painting many of their pieces and both preferred to work on Masonite rather than Canvas.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Until Tim's death, the twins painted together, or in shifts, dependent of the time they had for a piece of art. After a struggle with diabetes, Tim passed away on [June 11](/wiki/11_June "11 June"), [2006](/wiki/2006).

## The Lord of the Rings {#The_Lord_of_the_Rings}

After seeing [a Tolkien calendar](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar_1975 "The J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar 1975") with [Tim Kirk](/wiki/Tim_Kirk)'s art, they responded to a call for artists by [Ballantine](/wiki/Ballantine_Books "Ballantine Books"). Their art was well-liked, and they were commissioned to illustrate the [1976 Ballantine calendar](/wiki/1976_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar "1976 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar").

More calendars followed: [1977](/wiki/1977_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar "1977 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar"), [1978](/wiki/1978_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar "1978 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar") - and they sold a record one million calendars. They moved their scope to other large projects, but kept producing Tolkien artwork. In [2001](/wiki/2001), <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Greg_and_Tim_Hildebrandt:_The_Tolkien_Years&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Greg and Tim Hildebrandt: The Tolkien Years (page does not exist)">Greg and Tim Hildebrandt: The Tolkien Years</a></i> was published by Greg's son, Gregory Jr. Following the release of this collection, Ballantine began to reissue their popular Tolkien Calendars starting in [2002](/wiki/The_Brothers_Hildebrandt_2002_Calendar "The Brothers Hildebrandt 2002 Calendar").
