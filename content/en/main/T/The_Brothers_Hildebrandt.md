**The Brothers Hildebrandt** were a duo of fantasy artists consisting of the twin brothers **Greg Hildebrandt** ([January 23](../_/23_January.md), [1939](../_/1939.md) - [October 31](../O/October_31.md), [2024](../_/2024.md)) and **Tim Hildebrandt** (January 23, [1939](../_/1939.md) – [June 11](../_/11_June.md), [2006](../_/2006.md)).

The brothers have been recognized artists in the world of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-inspired art. Other art includes the original poster art of <i><a class="external text" href="http://starwars.wikia.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_The_Art_of_the_Brothers_Hildebrandt" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Star Wars</a></i> and over a hundred <i><a class="external text" href="http://marvel.wikia.com/wiki/Hildebrandt_Brothers/Gallery" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Marvel Masterpieces</a></i>, including *Swimsuit Specials*.

They created reference photographs prior to painting many of their pieces and both preferred to work on Masonite rather than Canvas.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Until Tim's death, the twins painted together, or in shifts, dependent of the time they had for a piece of art. After a struggle with diabetes, Tim passed away on [June 11](../_/11_June.md), [2006](../_/2006.md).

## The Lord of the Rings {#The_Lord_of_the_Rings}

After seeing [a Tolkien calendar](The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar_1975.md) with [Tim Kirk](Tim_Kirk.md)'s art, they responded to a call for artists by [Ballantine](../B/Ballantine_Books.md). Their art was well-liked, and they were commissioned to illustrate the [1976 Ballantine calendar](../_/1976_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar.md).

More calendars followed: [1977](../_/1977_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar.md), [1978](../_/1978_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar.md) - and they sold a record one million calendars. They moved their scope to other large projects, but kept producing Tolkien artwork. In [2001](../_/2001.md), <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Greg_and_Tim_Hildebrandt:_The_Tolkien_Years&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Greg and Tim Hildebrandt: The Tolkien Years (page does not exist)">Greg and Tim Hildebrandt: The Tolkien Years</a></i> was published by Greg's son, Gregory Jr. Following the release of this collection, Ballantine began to reissue their popular Tolkien Calendars starting in [2002](The_Brothers_Hildebrandt_2002_Calendar.md).
