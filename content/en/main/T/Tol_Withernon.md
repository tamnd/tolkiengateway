**Tol Withernon**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> might have been an island off the east coast of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Wit_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wit-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Tol Withernon* is in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish). The first element of the name is *[tol](/wiki/Tol "Tol")* ("an isle with high-steep coasts")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>; however, the second part, *Withernon*, is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Tol Withernon might have been inspired by the town of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Withernsea" title="wikipedia:Withernsea">Withernsea</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkshire" title="wikipedia:Yorkshire">Yorkshire</a>, where, not far from it, [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) was stationed during <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I" title="wikipedia:World War I">World War I</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> While the town of Withernsea isn't located on an island presently, a local tradition claims that at some point in history it actually was an island.<sup id="cite_ref-Wit_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wit-2">[2]</a></sup>
