**Third Age 913** (abbreviated to **T.A. 913**) is the 913th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Tarannon Falastur](Tarannon_Falastur.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppSouth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppSouth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Tarannon](Tarannon_Falastur.md)'s nephew, [Eärnil I](../E/Eärnil_I.md) (son of Tarannon's younger brother, [Tarciryan](Tarciryan.md)), becomes the thirteenth [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), second in the line of [Ship-kings](../S/Ship-kings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppSouth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppSouth-1">[1]</a></sup>
