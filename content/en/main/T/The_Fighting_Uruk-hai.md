**The Fighting Uruk-hai** is the thirty-fourth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and the fortieth of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

In the tower of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc) in [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) is telling [Lurtz](/wiki/Lurtz) about how the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) came into being. Formerly elves, they were taken by the Dark Powers, tortured and mutilated into a terrible form of life, now perfected by Saruman as the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai). He then asks Lurtz who he serves, and he answers, "Saruman."

Under Isengard, the Uruk-hai are readying for war, painting their faces with the [White Hand](/wiki/White_Hand) of Saruman, and arming and armoring themselves. Saruman gives them a rousing speech, to which the Uruk-hai cheer. He then turns to Lurtz, giving him the orders that [one of the halflings](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") carries [an item of great value](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), and that they are to be brought to him alive and unspoiled. They are to kill the others.

The orders given, Lurtz leads the Uruk-hai out of Isengard, beginning the hunt for the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring).
