**The Fighting Uruk-hai** is the thirty-fourth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* and the fortieth of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition).md>).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

In the tower of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md) in [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md), [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) is telling [Lurtz](../L/Lurtz.md) about how the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) came into being. Formerly elves, they were taken by the Dark Powers, tortured and mutilated into a terrible form of life, now perfected by Saruman as the [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md). He then asks Lurtz who he serves, and he answers, "Saruman."

Under Isengard, the Uruk-hai are readying for war, painting their faces with the [White Hand](../W/White_Hand.md) of Saruman, and arming and armoring themselves. Saruman gives them a rousing speech, to which the Uruk-hai cheer. He then turns to Lurtz, giving him the orders that [one of the halflings](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) carries [an item of great value](The_One_Ring.md), and that they are to be brought to him alive and unspoiled. They are to kill the others.

The orders given, Lurtz leads the Uruk-hai out of Isengard, beginning the hunt for the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md).
