**The Songs of the Entwives** is the sixteenth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)"). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Merry](/wiki/Merry) and [Pippin](/wiki/Pippin) continue to travel with [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) towards his home in the [forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest "Fangorn Forest"). The ent recites verses<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> to them as they sit on his branches, although the two hobbits appear somewhat bored. Merry asks him if it is much further, to which he replies "Bru-ra-hroom. Don't be hasty," and that he told [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) he would keep them safe. He begins reciting another verse.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Beneath the roof of sleeping leaves <br/> And the dreams of trees unfold <br/> When woodland halls are green and cool <br/> And the wind is in the West <br/> Come back to me <br/> Come back to me <br/> And say my land is best
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/Treebeard" title="Treebeard">Treebeard</a><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 2]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

Upon finishing, Treebeard notices the two hobbits have fallen asleep. He lays them down on the ground, bidding them sleep and says there are "many to call", and that "the withering of all woods is drawing near".
