The **Index** was first published in the Ballantine Books edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* in 1965. This index was a revision of an index that was compiled by [Nancy Smith](/wiki/Nancy_Smith) in [1958](/wiki/1958) based on the requirements of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) for "an alphabetical list of all proper names of persons, places, or things .. in the *text* (including Foreword and Prologue, but *not* the Appendices)". It was combined with an index of songs and verses that was compiled by [Baillie Klass](/wiki/Baillie_Klass).<sup id="cite_ref-Index_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-1">[1]</a></sup>

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) added some notes and 'translations' and selected citations to the appendices to the index for the Allen &amp; Unwin second edition of [1966](/wiki/1966).<sup id="cite_ref-Index_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [2005](/wiki/2005) a new, enlarged index<sup id="cite_ref-Index_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-1">[1]</a></sup> was compiled by [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) independent of the previous index, but J.R.R. Tolkien's added notes and translations were retained within square brackets.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
