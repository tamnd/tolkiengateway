The **Index** was first published in the Ballantine Books edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* in 1965. This index was a revision of an index that was compiled by [Nancy Smith](../N/Nancy_Smith.md) in [1958](../_/1958.md) based on the requirements of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) for "an alphabetical list of all proper names of persons, places, or things .. in the *text* (including Foreword and Prologue, but *not* the Appendices)". It was combined with an index of songs and verses that was compiled by [Baillie Klass](../B/Baillie_Klass.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Index_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-1">[1]</a></sup>

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) added some notes and 'translations' and selected citations to the appendices to the index for the Allen &amp; Unwin second edition of [1966](../_/1966.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Index_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [2005](../_/2005.md) a new, enlarged index<sup id="cite_ref-Index_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-1">[1]</a></sup> was compiled by [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) independent of the previous index, but J.R.R. Tolkien's added notes and translations were retained within square brackets.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
