**Third Age 2978** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2978**) is the 2978th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2978 was also known as **S.R. 1378** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md), son of [Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Denethor II](../D/Denethor_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Finduilas](<../F/Finduilas_(wife_of_Denethor).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Théodred](Théodred.md), only child and son of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Théoden](Théoden.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) and [Elfhild](../E/Elfhild.md).
- Death of [Elfhild](../E/Elfhild.md), wife of Théoden.
- Birth of [Folco Boffin](../F/Folco_Boffin.md), son of [Vigo Boffin](../V/Vigo_Boffin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Calculation of the Dates for Théodred and Elfhild {#Calculation_of_the_Dates_for_Théodred_and_Elfhild}

In [Appendix A](../A/Appendix_A.md) it is stated that [Éomund](../_/Éomund.md), father of [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) and [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md), died in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3002](Third_Age_3002.md). His wife [Théodwyn](Théodwyn.md), the beloved sister of King Théoden, died soon after. The king took in his sister's children for he only had one child, Théodred, who in 3002 was 24 years old and thus was born in 2978. It is also stated that Théoden's wife Elfhild died in childbirth.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
