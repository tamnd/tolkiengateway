**The Last Ballad of Damrod (Feat. Jens Kidman)** is a death metal song composed by Bear McCreary for Damrod the troll in [The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power). It features Jens Kidman on vocals. According to Bear McCreary, he saw how Damrod brutalized friend and foe alike and that brought sound of death metal into his head. The lyrics are loosely based on verses from *[The Stone Troll](/wiki/The_Stone_Troll)*, a poem by [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee).
