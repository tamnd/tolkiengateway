<b>The Later <i>Quenta Silmarillion</i></b> is the third and largest part of *[Morgoth's Ring](/wiki/Morgoth%27s_Ring)*.

The First Phase of the *Quenta* was written around [1951](/wiki/1951)-[1952](/wiki/1952) while the Second Phase around [1958](/wiki/1958).
