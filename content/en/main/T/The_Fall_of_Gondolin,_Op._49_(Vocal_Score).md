Published by Zarathustra Music in 2019, the complete Vocal Score of [Paul Corfield Godfrey](/wiki/Paul_Corfield_Godfrey)'s *[The Fall of Gondolin, Op. 49 (Opera)](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin,_Op.49_(Opera)> "The Fall of Gondolin, Op.49 (Opera)")* updated to reflect amendments made during the production of Volante Opera Production's Demo Recording of the work.

This is a 114 page A4 sized book with full gloss covers.

This is a complete piano reduction of the score with introduction by the composer, synopsis and translations of the Elvish.
