**The Curse of Fëanor** is the sixth track for [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian)'s Tolkien themed album, *[Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth)*. [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) expresses anger, but has no regret and doesn't stray from his goal to win the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils) back.
