**The Lord of the Rings: Realms in Exile** is a modification for the PC-game <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crusader_Kings_III" title="wikipedia:Crusader Kings III">Crusader Kings III</a></i> of the Crusader King series by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paradox_Development_Studio" title="wikipedia:Paradox Development Studio">Paradox Development Studio</a>. It changes Europe's map to a map of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), while retaining grand strategy and character-based simulation of the original.

It features an extensive, detailed map of Middle-earth for players to engage with, including extensive non-canon south-western geography. It has drawn (non canon) supplemental material from the [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing) tabletop RPG and [Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings_Online) to flesh out the expansive world.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Realms in Exile refers to the kingdoms that formed after the destruction of the isle of <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor" title="Númenor">Númenor</a>. These are <a href="/wiki/Arnor" title="Arnor">Arnor</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>, <a href="/wiki/Umbar" title="Umbar">Umbar</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bellakar" title="Bellakar">Bellakar</a> and <a href="/wiki/Army_of_the_Southern_Dragon" title="Army of the Southern Dragon">Thôn an-Khârlôkh</a>. Gondor features in the third book of The Lord of the Rings. It is ruled by the stewards of <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>, as their king was lost in a duel with the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Witch_King" title="Witch King">Witch King</a> of <a href="/wiki/Minas_Morgul" title="Minas Morgul">Minas Morgul</a>. Arnor is the lost realm of the north, <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> is chieftain of its few survivors. Umbar was once a city of the <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans" title="Númenóreans">Númenóreans</a> at the height of their power in Middle-earth, now it is a mighty haven of corsairs. Bellakar and Thôn an-Khârlôkh are former colonies, far to the south, and the books do not mention their names.
</p>

</blockquote>

The current version of the game allows players to begin simulating the world starting in [T.A. 3000](/wiki/Third_Age_3000 "Third Age 3000"), with future plans to allow for playing as early as [S.A. 3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320") and as late as [Fo.A. 120](/wiki/Fourth_Age_120 "Fourth Age 120"). It includes lore and quotes from [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings), with character adaptations and interstitial stills inspired from the [Lord of the Rings film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").
