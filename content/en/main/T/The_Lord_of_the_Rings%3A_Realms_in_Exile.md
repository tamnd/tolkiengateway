**The Lord of the Rings: Realms in Exile** is a modification for the PC-game <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crusader_Kings_III" title="wikipedia:Crusader Kings III">Crusader Kings III</a></i> of the Crusader King series by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paradox_Development_Studio" title="wikipedia:Paradox Development Studio">Paradox Development Studio</a>. It changes Europe's map to a map of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), while retaining grand strategy and character-based simulation of the original.

It features an extensive, detailed map of Middle-earth for players to engage with, including extensive non-canon south-western geography. It has drawn (non canon) supplemental material from the [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md) tabletop RPG and [Lord of the Rings Online](../L/Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md) to flesh out the expansive world.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Realms in Exile refers to the kingdoms that formed after the destruction of the isle of <a href="../N/Númenor.md" title="Númenor">Númenor</a>. These are <a href="../A/Arnor.md" title="Arnor">Arnor</a>, <a href="../G/Gondor.md" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>, <a href="../U/Umbar.md" title="Umbar">Umbar</a>, <a href="../B/Bellakar.md" title="Bellakar">Bellakar</a> and <a href="../A/Army_of_the_Southern_Dragon.md" title="Army of the Southern Dragon">Thôn an-Khârlôkh</a>. Gondor features in the third book of The Lord of the Rings. It is ruled by the stewards of <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>, as their king was lost in a duel with the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../W/Witch_King.md" title="Witch King">Witch King</a> of <a href="../M/Minas_Morgul.md" title="Minas Morgul">Minas Morgul</a>. Arnor is the lost realm of the north, <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> is chieftain of its few survivors. Umbar was once a city of the <a href="../N/Númenóreans.md" title="Númenóreans">Númenóreans</a> at the height of their power in Middle-earth, now it is a mighty haven of corsairs. Bellakar and Thôn an-Khârlôkh are former colonies, far to the south, and the books do not mention their names.
</p>

</blockquote>

The current version of the game allows players to begin simulating the world starting in [T.A. 3000](Third_Age_3000.md), with future plans to allow for playing as early as [S.A. 3320](../S/Second_Age_3320.md) and as late as [Fo.A. 120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md). It includes lore and quotes from [The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md), with character adaptations and interstitial stills inspired from the [Lord of the Rings film series](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>).
