The **Túrin Wrapper** comprises three [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> texts pertaining to the [Narn i Chîn Húrin](<../N/Narn_i_Chîn_Húrin_(tale).md>) of the 1950s composed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). It was published, along with translations and analysis by [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md), in [Vinyar Tengwar 50](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_50.md).

## Area IV {#Area_IV}

Area IV on the wrapper contains text detailing dialogue from [Rían](../R/Rían.md) to her infant son [Tuor](Tuor.md).
