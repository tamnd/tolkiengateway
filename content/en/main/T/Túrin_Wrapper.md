The **Túrin Wrapper** comprises three [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> texts pertaining to the [Narn i Chîn Húrin](</wiki/Narn_i_Ch%C3%AEn_H%C3%BArin_(tale)> "Narn i Chîn Húrin (tale)") of the 1950s composed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). It was published, along with translations and analysis by [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter), in [Vinyar Tengwar 50](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_50).

## Area IV {#Area_IV}

Area IV on the wrapper contains text detailing dialogue from [Rían](/wiki/R%C3%ADan) to her infant son [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor).
