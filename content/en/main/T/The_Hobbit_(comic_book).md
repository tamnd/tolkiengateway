**The Hobbit: A Graphic Novel** is a [1989](/wiki/1989) three issue comic book series, published by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Eclipse_Comics&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Eclipse Comics (page does not exist)">Eclipse Comics</a>. In January [1990](/wiki/1990), they were bound as one in a trade paperback, and a revised edition was published in [2006](/wiki/2006). To coincide with the then [upcoming *The Hobbit* movies](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), a new edition, with six new pages of artwork, was published on [12 September](/wiki/12_September) [2012](/wiki/2012).<sup id="cite_ref-collier_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collier-1">[1]</a></sup> Another new edition was published in [2025](/wiki/2025).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The story was adapted from the [original](/wiki/The_Hobbit "The Hobbit") by [Chuck Dixon](/wiki/Charles_Dixon "Charles Dixon"), and illustrated by [David T. Wenzel](/wiki/David_T._Wenzel).<sup id="cite_ref-collier_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collier-1">[1]</a></sup>

In 1992, Eclipse Comics released trading cards based on the comic, the backs of which contained texts written by Sean Deming.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Publication history {#Publication_history}

The three published issues released in [1989](/wiki/1989) were called **Book One**, **Book Two** and **Book Three**<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup>. The issues are numbered continuously: part one comprises pages 1 to 44 (up until the [riddle-game](/wiki/Riddles_in_the_Dark "Riddles in the Dark")), part two pages 45 to 88 (up until the [rafts of the Lake-men](/wiki/Barrels_Out_of_Bond "Barrels Out of Bond")), and part three pages 89 to 133.

The issues were bound in one volume trade paperback in [1990](/wiki/1990), and newer editions have been released in the following years.<sup id="cite_ref-collier_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collier-1">[1]</a></sup>
