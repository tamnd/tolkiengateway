The **Tower Hall**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> **Hall of the White Tower**,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> **Hall of the Tower**<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> or **Hall of the Kings**<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-4">[4]</a></sup> was the throne room<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> of the [Kings of Gondor](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md), the [Ruling Stewards](../R/Ruling_Steward.md)<sup id="cite_ref-MT_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-6">[6]</a></sup> and the Kings of the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-4">[4]</a></sup> in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).

## Description {#Description}

The Tower Hall was at the base of the [Tower of Ecthelion](Tower_of_Ecthelion.md),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> which stood in the centre of the [Citadel](../C/Citadel_of_Gondor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the topmost seventh circle of walls of Minas Tirith, beyond the [Court of the Fountain](<../C/Court_of_the_Fountain_(Minas_Tirith).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> A visitor first passed through an outer door guarded by silent door-wardens and walked down a long and empty paved passage. Next came the inner door, tall and fashioned of polished metal, which was opened by unseen means. Inside, rows of tall pillars, carved monoliths of black marble crowned with capitals exhibiting figures of beasts and leaves, upheld the vaulted ceiling of gold and flowing traceries. Flanking the pillars were spacious aisles lit by deep windows and between the pillars were tall images of long-dead kings made of cold stone.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-6">[6]</a></sup>

At the far end of the hall was a dais of many steps which supported a high throne with a marble canopy shaped like a crowned helm. Behind this was the image of a flowering tree set in gems. Upon the lowest step of the dais was an unadorned black stone chair, the seat of the Steward.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-6">[6]</a></sup>

## The War of the Ring and thereafter {#The_War_of_the_Ring_and_thereafter}

On [9 March](../_/9_March.md) [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) reached Minas Tirith and met Steward [Denethor II](../D/Denethor.md) in the Tower Hall, where Pippin pledged service to the aged Steward.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> On the next day Gandalf and Pippin returned to the Hall where the wizard and the steward discussed the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) and their policies.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

In early May [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> after victory in the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the new [King Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) sat upon the high throne in the Tower Hall. There he received embassies and pronounced judgements, such as his merciful banishment of [Beregond](../B/Beregond.md) to the [White Company](../W/White_Company.md), Lord [Faramir's](../F/Faramir.md) guard in [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
