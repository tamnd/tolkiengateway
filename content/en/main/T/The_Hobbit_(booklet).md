This refers unofficially to two booklets that came with two certain editions of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. They are similar in format and content, containing miscellaneous things concerning *The Hobbit*.

They most notably contain a lecture titled "[Dragons](</wiki/Dragons_(lecture)> "Dragons (lecture)")" by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), which has not been published fully anywhere else.

## *The Hobbit 1937-2017* {#The_Hobbit_1937-2017}

The first of these came with *[The Hobbit Facsimile Gift Edition](/wiki/The_Hobbit_Facsimile_Gift_Edition)* box (<a class="internal mw-magiclink-isbn" href="/wiki/Special:BookSources/9780008259549">ISBN 9780008259549</a>) in [2018](/wiki/2018). It has 62 pages, and has the following content:

- The History of *The Hobbit*
- Publication
- Later Editions
- 'The Hoard' by J.R.R. Tolkien
- Lecture on Dragon
- 'Dragons' by J.R.R. Tolkien

- The History of *The Hobbit*
- Thorin's letter to Bilbo\*
- Publication
- Later Editions
- 'The Hoard' by J.R.R. Tolkien
- Lecture on Dragon
- 'Dragons' by J.R.R. Tolkien
