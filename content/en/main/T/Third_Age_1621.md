**Third Age 1621** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1621**) is the 1621st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1621 was also known as **S.R. 21** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Hyarmendacil II](../H/Hyarmendacil_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Hyarmendacil II](../H/Hyarmendacil_II.md)'s son, [Minardil](../M/Minardil.md), becomes the twenty-fifth [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>
