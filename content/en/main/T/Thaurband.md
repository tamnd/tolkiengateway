**Thaurband** is a place in [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) that appears in several adaptations.

## Chronology and Creation {#Chronology_and_Creation}

The name was originally created by cartographer and calligrapher [Daniel Reeve](../D/Daniel_Reeve.md) while working on licensed map assets for the film trilogy and related merchandise.

Its first appearance is in the [Maps of Middle-earth](../M/Maps_of_Middle-earth.md) map set for [Decipher](../D/Decipher.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game.md)*, where it appears as a minor label below what seems to be a fortress near the western shore of the [Sea of Nurnen](../S/Sea_of_Nurnen.md) without further exposition.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Reeve subsequently used the Decipher map as a template when tasked with designing the expanded merchandise map for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-reeve-site_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-site-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-reeve-mail_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-mail-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Further adaptations {#Further_adaptations}

### Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor {#Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor}

Thaurband is mentioned in a coded journal entry in [Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](../M/Middle-earth%253A_Shadow_of_Mordor.md):<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I was going to scout Thaurband, the slave city. The Orcs got a prison there, and enough people in chains to run the mines and the mills and make the city go.
</p>
</blockquote>

### The Lord of the Rings Online {#The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online}

While the location is not visited in [The Lord of the Rings Online](The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md), Thaurband is mentioned during a quest to fight the Gloom of Nurn, the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) who dwelt there:<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>He dwelt in Thaurband, a fortress in southern Mordor, not far from the inland Sea. It was from that keep that the fear of him spread, giving him a new title to replace the name he no longer needed. He became the Gloom of Núrn, though he did not stay there, and roved far and wide at Sauron&#39;s command.
</p>
</blockquote>

### Fan works {#Fan_works}

The fansite [Thelandofshadow.com](Thelandofshadow.com.md) took on several of the names introduced by Reeve, and expanded on them. Thaurband was described as follows:<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>A fortress along the Sea of Nurnen. Thaurband is one of the largest of the southern guard posts of Mordor. Raised along the banks of the Sea of Nurnen, it was a logical outpost for troops, slaves and goods coming into Northern Mordor. [...] Many ships moved goods across the sea landing at Thaurband and then moving north along the Nurn road.
</p>
</blockquote>

The information from The Land of Shadow was incorporated in a slightly modified form into the fan made *Mordor Gazetteer* of the Middle-earth Role Playing (MERP) fan modules group.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Thaurband* seems to be a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name, which means "Horrbile Prison". It seems to contain the elements *[thaur](Thaur.md)* ("abominable", "horrible")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and *[band](../B/Band.md)* ("prison", "custody", "duress")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>.
