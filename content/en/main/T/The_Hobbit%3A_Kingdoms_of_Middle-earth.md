<i><b>The Hobbit: Kingdoms of Middle-earth</b></i> is a strategy and management game, set in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).It was developed by Kabam with the involment of [Warner Bros.](../W/Warner_Bros..md), and it is based on [The Hobbit movies](<The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).It was released on [8 November](../_/8_November.md) [2012](../_/2012.md) for Android and iOS.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In this game, players can rule entire cities of elves and dwarves and lead armies into battle.

## Gameplay {#Gameplay}

The game, like almost all games of this kind, has an online MMO component that allows players to stay in constant contact with other players. Thus you can form unbreakable alliances with your friends and also fight shoulder to should with your band of brothers.

[Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), [Thorin](Thorin.md)... some of the most important heroes of Middle-earth will be at the head of your armies, which will ultimately have to charge the orcs and goblins in battle. You can also kit out all your characters with different pieces of equipment that you'll find throughout the game.
