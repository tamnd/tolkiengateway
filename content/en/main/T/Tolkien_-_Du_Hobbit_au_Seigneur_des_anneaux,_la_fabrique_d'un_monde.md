## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

Immerse yourself in the extraordinary work of a world creator! Unique in the history of literature, J. R. R. Tolkien wrote thousands of poetic, narrative and speculative texts throughout his life, which, when brought together, build an entire universe. This collection will take you on a tour of the best-known regions – those of *The Lord of the Rings* and *The Hobbit* – and the least explored lands, revealing their conceptual treasures. Finally, it will show you an author very different from what the great adventures he staged might suggest.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>
