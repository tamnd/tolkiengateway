<i><b>The Tolkien Diary 2000</b></i> was a blank diary book featuring illustrations by [Ted Nasmith](Ted_Nasmith.md).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The Tolkien millennium desk diary includes paintings by Ted Nasmith, illustrator of the new edition of The Silmarillion, together with artwork and quotes by JRR Tolkien.<br/><br/>This sumptuous week-to-view desk diary is illustrated with brand new full colour paintings and previously unpublished black and white drawings by Ted Nasmith, as well as many heraldic devices designed by Tolkien himself. Designed to complement the annual Tolkien calendar, which is this year based on The Silmarillion, this is a diary which will appeal to Tolkien enthusiasts and art lovers alike.
</p>
</blockquote>
