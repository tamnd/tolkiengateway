**Tar-Alcarin** was the seventeenth [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Alcarin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Alcarin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Tar-Alcarin was the son of [Ruling Queen](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Ruling Queen of Númenor") [Tar-Vanimeldë](/wiki/Tar-Vanimeld%C3%AB) and [Herucalmo](/wiki/Herucalmo), a descendant of [Tar-Atanamir](/wiki/Tar-Atanamir). When Tar-Vanimeldë died in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [2637](/wiki/Second_Age_2637 "Second Age 2637"), Herucalmo took the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre) for his own and assumed the name Tar-Anducal. Tar-Alcarin had to wait for twenty years until his father died before he could become the rightful King. "Tar-Anducal" being an usurper was not reckoned in the Line of Kings as the seventeenth ruler of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Vanimelde_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Vanimelde-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Alcarin* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. It means "the Glorious".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Paul Strack explains that *Alcarin* is the shortened form of *alcarin(qua)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
