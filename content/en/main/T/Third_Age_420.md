**Third Age 420** (abbreviated to **T.A. 420**) is the 420th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Ostoher](../O/Ostoher.md) rebuilds [Minas Anor](../M/Minas_Anor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup>
