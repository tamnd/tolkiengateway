**The Languages of Tolkien's Middle-earth** is a book by [Ruth S. Noel](/wiki/Ruth_S._Noel) studying Tolkien's languages. The book is heavily outdated, being published before *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* and *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)*, and even contained errors in the material that already was available at the time.

## From the Publisher {#From_the_Publisher}

This is the book on all of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s invented languages, spoken by [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits), [Elves](/wiki/Elves), and [Men](/wiki/Men) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth)—a dictionary of fourteen languages, an English-[Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) glossary, all the [Runes](/wiki/Runes) and alphabets, and material on Tolkien the linguist.
