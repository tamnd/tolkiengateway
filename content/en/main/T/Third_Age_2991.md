**Third Age 2991** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2991**) is the 2991st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2991 was also known as **S.R. 1391** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md), first child and son of [Éomund](../_/Éomund.md) and [Théodwyn](Théodwyn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Ilberic Brandybuck](../I/Ilberic_Brandybuck.md), second child and second son of [Seredic Brandybuck](../S/Seredic_Brandybuck.md) and [Hilda Bracegirdle](../H/Hilda_Bracegirdle.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Moro Burrows](../M/Moro_Burrows.md), second child and second son of [Milo Burrows](../M/Milo_Burrows.md) and [Peony Baggins](../P/Peony_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Death of [Sigismond Took](../S/Sigismond_Took.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Took_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-4">[4]</a></sup>
