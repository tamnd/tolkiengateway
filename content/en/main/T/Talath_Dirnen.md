**Talath Dirnen**, the **Guarded Plain**, was the forested empty land roughly between [Narog](../N/Narog.md) and [Teiglin](Teiglin.md) in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) and belonged to the Realm of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md). In its eastern part was the lone hill of [Amon Rûdh](../A/Amon_Rûdh.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The plain was guarded by rangers and hidden towers. When [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) conquered [Tol Sirion](Tol_Sirion.md), the [West Beleriand](../W/West_Beleriand.md) was open to his forces. Although uninhabited, the plain was defended by scouts and spies of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md) of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md), and the [Gaurwaith](../G/Gaurwaith.md). Under the [Two Captains](Two_Captains.md), the Gaurwaith controlled the area around Amon Rûdh, named [Dor-Cúarthol](../D/Dor-Cúarthol.md) after [The Helm](../G/Gorthol.md) and [The Bow](../B/Belthronding.md).

After the capture of [Túrin Turambar](Túrin_Turambar.md), the plain was ravaged by [Orc](../O/Orc.md) raiders and King [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) protected Nargothrond with ambushes, not wishing to draw Morgoth's attention to his hidden city. That was until Túrin, now known as [Mormegil](../M/Mormegil.md), persuaded him to fight openly, and they managed to clear the Plain from Orcs. The Plain was lost after the [Battle of Tumhalad](../B/Battle_of_Tumhalad.md) and the [fall of Nargothrond](../F/Fall_of_Nargothrond.md).

Years later, after his Orcs were defeated in [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md), [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) headed across the plain in a straight line, burning all in his way and poisoning the air.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
