**Talath Dirnen**, the **Guarded Plain**, was the forested empty land roughly between [Narog](/wiki/Narog) and [Teiglin](/wiki/Teiglin) in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and belonged to the Realm of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond). In its eastern part was the lone hill of [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The plain was guarded by rangers and hidden towers. When [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) conquered [Tol Sirion](/wiki/Tol_Sirion), the [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand) was open to his forces. Although uninhabited, the plain was defended by scouts and spies of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil), and the [Gaurwaith](/wiki/Gaurwaith). Under the [Two Captains](/wiki/Two_Captains), the Gaurwaith controlled the area around Amon Rûdh, named [Dor-Cúarthol](/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol) after [The Helm](/wiki/Gorthol "Gorthol") and [The Bow](/wiki/Belthronding "Belthronding").

After the capture of [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_Turambar), the plain was ravaged by [Orc](/wiki/Orc) raiders and King [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) protected Nargothrond with ambushes, not wishing to draw Morgoth's attention to his hidden city. That was until Túrin, now known as [Mormegil](/wiki/Mormegil), persuaded him to fight openly, and they managed to clear the Plain from Orcs. The Plain was lost after the [Battle of Tumhalad](/wiki/Battle_of_Tumhalad) and the [fall of Nargothrond](/wiki/Fall_of_Nargothrond "Fall of Nargothrond").

Years later, after his Orcs were defeated in [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil), [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) headed across the plain in a straight line, burning all in his way and poisoning the air.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
