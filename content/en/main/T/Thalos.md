**Thalos** was the second of the six [rivers](/wiki/Category:Rivers "Category:Rivers") that rushed down from the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) through [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand) to meet the great [River Gelion](/wiki/Gelion "Gelion"), between the rivers [Ascar](/wiki/Ascar) and the [Legolin](/wiki/Legolin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was in the high valley of its upper course that [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor) and his followers, the first Men to cross into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), made their camp, and where they were discovered by [Elf](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") King [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The name comes from the [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin) word *thalos* ("torrent") used as a name.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
