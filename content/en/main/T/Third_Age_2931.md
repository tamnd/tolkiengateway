**Third Age 2931** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2931**) is the 2931st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2931 was also known as **S.R. 1331** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## [March](../M/March.md) {#March}

- [1](../_/1_March.md): Birth of [Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn.md), son of [Chieftain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md) [Arathorn II](../A/Arathorn_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
