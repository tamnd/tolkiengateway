**Third Age 2172** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2172**) is the 2172nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2172 was also known as **S.R. 572** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of the unnamed fourth [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
