<i><b>Third Age of the Sun</b></i> is the third album released by the Epic metal band [Battlelore](/wiki/Battlelore), released in [2005](/wiki/2005). The sound of the album differs from the first two Battlelore albums as the instrumentation has a more live and organic feel and also due to the absence of clean male vocals.
