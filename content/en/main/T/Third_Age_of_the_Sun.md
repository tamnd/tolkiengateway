<i><b>Third Age of the Sun</b></i> is the third album released by the Epic metal band [Battlelore](../B/Battlelore.md), released in [2005](../_/2005.md). The sound of the album differs from the first two Battlelore albums as the instrumentation has a more live and organic feel and also due to the absence of clean male vocals.
