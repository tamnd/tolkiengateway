**The Peril to the Shire** is a [fan film](/wiki/Category:Fan_films "Category:Fan films") written and directed by Douglas Dunklin, produced by Pointy Stick Productions.

## Production Information {#Production_Information}

*The Peril to the Shire* was created as an experience project by a group of home-schooled students and their families from Coweta and Fayette counties, Georgia.

Written by Colonel Douglas Dunklin, the original screenplay explores a "what if" scenario based on *[The Appendices](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Appendices "The Lord of the Rings Appendices")*. It was originally written as a Christmas present to his daughter in 2005.

The project to turn it into a film started in March 2006, by gathering several friends and families and forming "Pointy Stick Productions". The film was completed in June 2007, and premiered to a private audience that same month.

## Plot {#Plot}

The story takes place some four months after [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") left [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") (December [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018")); main players are [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton) and her (original) cousin Hamfoot. On their road to [Budgeford](/wiki/Budgeford), they come across a wounded Elf maid with a dire message.

Rosie and her cousin, along with some friends set out to save the Shire from imminent destruction. The danger is far bigger than they could ever imagine
