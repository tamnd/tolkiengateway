**The Green Dragon** was one of the many inns of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It was located in [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater) on the [Bywater Road](/wiki/Bywater_Road) and was the building nearest to [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton),<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-1">[1]</a></sup> being one mile south-east from the bridge over [the Water](/wiki/The_Water "The Water") that led to [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-2">[2]</a></sup> As such, [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) from both villages could be seen there.

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> when [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) looked at the letter that [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) [Oakenshield](/wiki/Oakenshield) had left on his mantelpiece after the Unexpected Party, he found that he only had ten minutes to arrive punctually for the 11 a.m. start of the quest to the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) at the Green Dragon Inn. Bilbo made it, barely, and found that the [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) were packed and ready to go.<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-2">[2]</a></sup>

On [12 April](/wiki/12_April) [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), the same day that [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) returned to the Shire after many years,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) was visiting the Green Dragon when the talk turned to Tree-men, one of whom had been seen by Sam's [cousin Hal](/wiki/Halfast_Gamgee "Halfast Gamgee"). Although queer tales had begun to be heard in the Shire the hobbits at the inn, especially [Ted Sandyman](/wiki/Ted_Sandyman), laughed at Sam's story.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

When [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck"), [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), and Sam returned to Bywater on [2 November](/wiki/2_November) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> they found the Green Dragon closed with broken windows and a half dozen [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) loitering nearby. When they insulted Frodo the other three hobbits drove the men away.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the Ruffians and [Sharkey](/wiki/Saruman "Saruman") had either been killed or driven away the Green Dragon Inn was undoubtedly restored.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the original version of [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin)'s letter to Bilbo, the rendezvous point for the start of the journey to the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) was the [Great Mill](/wiki/Old_Mill "Old Mill").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Not long before the story was published this was changed to the inn, first named the **Green Man** before changing to the familiar Green Dragon Inn.<sup id="cite_ref-TrollNotes_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TrollNotes-8">[8]</a></sup>
