**The Green Dragon** was one of the many inns of [the Shire](The_Shire.md). It was located in [Bywater](../B/Bywater.md) on the [Bywater Road](../B/Bywater_Road.md) and was the building nearest to [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-1">[1]</a></sup> being one mile south-east from the bridge over [the Water](The_Water.md) that led to [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-2">[2]</a></sup> As such, [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) from both villages could be seen there.

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2941](Third_Age_2941.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> when [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) looked at the letter that [Thorin](Thorin.md) [Oakenshield](../O/Oakenshield.md) had left on his mantelpiece after the Unexpected Party, he found that he only had ten minutes to arrive punctually for the 11 a.m. start of the quest to the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) at the Green Dragon Inn. Bilbo made it, barely, and found that the [Thorin and Company](Thorin_and_Company.md) were packed and ready to go.<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-2">[2]</a></sup>

On [12 April](../_/12_April.md) [3018](Third_Age_3018.md), the same day that [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) returned to the Shire after many years,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) was visiting the Green Dragon when the talk turned to Tree-men, one of whom had been seen by Sam's [cousin Hal](../H/Halfast_Gamgee.md). Although queer tales had begun to be heard in the Shire the hobbits at the inn, especially [Ted Sandyman](Ted_Sandyman.md), laughed at Sam's story.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

When [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md), [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), and Sam returned to Bywater on [2 November](../_/2_November.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> they found the Green Dragon closed with broken windows and a half dozen [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) loitering nearby. When they insulted Frodo the other three hobbits drove the men away.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the Ruffians and [Sharkey](../S/Saruman.md) had either been killed or driven away the Green Dragon Inn was undoubtedly restored.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the original version of [Thorin](Thorin.md)'s letter to Bilbo, the rendezvous point for the start of the journey to the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) was the [Great Mill](../O/Old_Mill.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Not long before the story was published this was changed to the inn, first named the **Green Man** before changing to the familiar Green Dragon Inn.<sup id="cite_ref-TrollNotes_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TrollNotes-8">[8]</a></sup>
