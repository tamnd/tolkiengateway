The **Taeglin** was a river in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), a tributary of [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md).

## Course {#Course}

It rose in the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) and flowed southeast through [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md) to join Sirion on the borders of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md). It had three minor tributaries: [Glithui](../G/Glithui.md), [Malduin](../M/Malduin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Celebros](../C/Celebros.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In its pass by the southeast of Brethil, it descended through the [Ravines of Taeglin](../R/Ravines_of_Taeglin.md) that skirted the eaves of the Forest. Further south, the river could be forded at the [Crossings of the Taeglin](../C/Crossings_of_the_Taeglin.md), near the western border of Brethil. A road ran through it from [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) to [Minas Tirith](<../M/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand).md>).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The element <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Taeg&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Taeg (page does not exist)">taeg</a></i> means "boundary".<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the *[Etymologies](../E/Etymologies.md)* the name appears in its earlier form "Taiglin" and its second element appears to be *[lin](../L/Lin.md)* "pool".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier writings, the river was called **Teiglin**. As revealed in the *[The History of Middle-earth](The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* series, the name of the river should actually have been spelt *Taeglin* ("Boundary River").<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-3">[3]</a></sup> This was a relatively late change which was not adopted into the published *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)*, which uses the old name, as does *[The Children of Húrin](The_Children_of_Húrin.md)*. <span></span>
