The **Taeglin** was a river in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), a tributary of [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion).

## Course {#Course}

It rose in the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin) and flowed southeast through [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) to join Sirion on the borders of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath). It had three minor tributaries: [Glithui](/wiki/Glithui), [Malduin](/wiki/Malduin)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Celebros](/wiki/Celebros).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In its pass by the southeast of Brethil, it descended through the [Ravines of Taeglin](/wiki/Ravines_of_Taeglin) that skirted the eaves of the Forest. Further south, the river could be forded at the [Crossings of the Taeglin](/wiki/Crossings_of_the_Taeglin), near the western border of Brethil. A road ran through it from [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) to [Minas Tirith](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand)> "Minas Tirith (Beleriand)").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The element <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Taeg&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Taeg (page does not exist)">taeg</a></i> means "boundary".<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/Etymologies)* the name appears in its earlier form "Taiglin" and its second element appears to be *[lin](/wiki/Lin "Lin")* "pool".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier writings, the river was called **Teiglin**. As revealed in the *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* series, the name of the river should actually have been spelt *Taeglin* ("Boundary River").<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-3">[3]</a></sup> This was a relatively late change which was not adopted into the published *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, which uses the old name, as does *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*. <span></span>
