<i><b>tarkil</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word, used in the [Common Tongue](../W/Westron.md) as a name for a [Man](../M/Men.md) of [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) descent.<sup id="cite_ref-App_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

The [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) corrupted the word, and used *[tark](Tark.md)* as an insulting term for a [Gondorian](../G/Gondorians.md).<sup id="cite_ref-App_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The spelling [Tarcil](Tarcil.md) is used for the name of the sixth [King of Arnor](../K/King_of_Arnor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Arda_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arda-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the [*Etymologies*](The_Etymologies.md), *tark* derives from [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) *tāra-khil* ([roots](../S/Sundocarme.md) [TUR](TUR~21398.md) and [KHIL](../K/KHIL.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 1]</a></sup> It has been suggested that *tarkil* means "literally 'high follower' (\[since\] the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) referred to Men as *[followers](../M/Men.md)* because they were the [Afterborn](../A/Afterborn.md) [Children of Ilúvatar](../C/Children_of_Ilúvatar.md))".<sup id="cite_ref-Arda_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arda-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, the word appears only in a footnote (added in the [Allen &amp; Unwin](../G/George_Allen_and_Unwin.md) second edition, 1966) in [Appendix F](../A/Appendix_F.md). However, at one time it was destined for more prominence: in the early drafts of the work, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) himself is known as "the Tarkil", but this was changed to "the Dúnadan" in the published text.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup>
