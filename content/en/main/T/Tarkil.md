<i><b>tarkil</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word, used in the [Common Tongue](/wiki/Westron "Westron") as a name for a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") of [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") descent.<sup id="cite_ref-App_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

The [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) corrupted the word, and used *[tark](/wiki/Tark "Tark")* as an insulting term for a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians").<sup id="cite_ref-App_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The spelling [Tarcil](/wiki/Tarcil) is used for the name of the sixth [King of Arnor](/wiki/King_of_Arnor).<sup id="cite_ref-Arda_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arda-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), *tark* derives from [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *tāra-khil* ([roots](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [TUR](/wiki/TUR) and [KHIL](/wiki/KHIL)).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 1]</a></sup> It has been suggested that *tarkil* means "literally 'high follower' (\[since\] the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) referred to Men as *[followers](/wiki/Men "Men")* because they were the [Afterborn](/wiki/Afterborn) [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar))".<sup id="cite_ref-Arda_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arda-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, the word appears only in a footnote (added in the [Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/George_Allen_and_Unwin "George Allen and Unwin") second edition, 1966) in [Appendix F](/wiki/Appendix_F). However, at one time it was destined for more prominence: in the early drafts of the work, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) himself is known as "the Tarkil", but this was changed to "the Dúnadan" in the published text.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup>
