**Third Age 1934** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1934**) is the 1934th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1934 was also known as **S.R. 334** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Thráin](Thráin_I.md), son of [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Náin I](../N/Náin_I.md) and founder of the [Dwarf kingdom](../K/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain.md) at [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md), is born.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
