<i><b>Tolkien: La Luce e l&#39;Ombra</b></i> ("Tolkien: The Light and the Shadow") is an Italian-language collection of essays on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"). Authors contributing include: Giovanni Agnoloni, Roberto Arduini, Patrick Curry, [Michaël Devaux](/wiki/Micha%C3%ABl_Devaux), [Michael D.C. Drout](/wiki/Michael_D.C._Drout), [Colin Duriez](/wiki/Colin_Duriez), [John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth), [Thomas Honegger](/wiki/Thomas_Honegger), Gino Scatasta, and Guglielmo Spirito.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Many of the articles have appeared before in other languages.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
