**Thargelion**, earlier known as **Talath Rhúnen** and later known as **Dor Caranthir**, was a flat wooded region of [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md), between the river [Gelion](../G/Gelion.md) and the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md). It was north of the river [Ascar](../A/Ascar.md), and therefore not counted as part of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md). At its northern end was Lake [Helevorn](../H/Helevorn.md) and Mount [Rerir](../R/Rerir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-bel_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bel-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) called this land *Talath Rhúnen*. After the Rebellion of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) this was the land of [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md), one of the [Sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md), and after him it was often called *[Dor](../D/Dor.md) Caranthir*, "Land of Caranthir". There the Noldor met the [Dwarves of the Blue Mountains](../D/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-bel_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bel-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md) briefly dwelt there independently, near the river Ascar, until they were nearly destroyed in an [Orc](../O/Orcs.md)-raid. Caranthir offered part of his land to their leader, [Haleth](../H/Haleth.md), but she refused and instead led her people to [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), Orcs broke the defences of [Maglor's Gap](../M/Maglor's_Gap.md), defiled Helevorn, and ravaged all Thargelion before passing over Gelion farther into East Beleriand. Caranthir fled to the south with the remainder of his people.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Caranthir later gave some of his former lands to [Uldor](../U/Uldor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Thargelion* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name, consisting of *thar-* ("across, over") and *[Gelion](../G/Gelion.md#Etymology)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

*[Talath](Talath.md) [Rhúnen](../R/Rhûn.md)* is glossed as "East Vale".<sup id="cite_ref-bel_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bel-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
