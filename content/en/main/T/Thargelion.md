**Thargelion**, earlier known as **Talath Rhúnen** and later known as **Dor Caranthir**, was a flat wooded region of [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand), between the river [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion) and the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains). It was north of the river [Ascar](/wiki/Ascar), and therefore not counted as part of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand). At its northern end was Lake [Helevorn](/wiki/Helevorn) and Mount [Rerir](/wiki/Rerir).<sup id="cite_ref-bel_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bel-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) called this land *Talath Rhúnen*. After the Rebellion of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) this was the land of [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir), one of the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor), and after him it was often called *[Dor](/wiki/Dor) Caranthir*, "Land of Caranthir". There the Noldor met the [Dwarves of the Blue Mountains](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-bel_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bel-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) briefly dwelt there independently, near the river Ascar, until they were nearly destroyed in an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs")-raid. Caranthir offered part of his land to their leader, [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth), but she refused and instead led her people to [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), Orcs broke the defences of [Maglor's Gap](/wiki/Maglor%27s_Gap), defiled Helevorn, and ravaged all Thargelion before passing over Gelion farther into East Beleriand. Caranthir fled to the south with the remainder of his people.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Caranthir later gave some of his former lands to [Uldor](/wiki/Uldor).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Thargelion* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, consisting of *thar-* ("across, over") and *[Gelion](/wiki/Gelion#Etymology)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

*[Talath](/wiki/Talath) [Rhúnen](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn "Rhûn")* is glossed as "East Vale".<sup id="cite_ref-bel_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bel-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
