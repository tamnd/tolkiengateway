<b><i>The Daily Telegraph Magazine</i> (22 March 1968)</b> is a supplement magazine of the newspaper <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Daily_Telegraph" title="wikipedia:The Daily Telegraph">The Daily Telegraph</a></i>. It includes the article "[The Man Who Understands Hobbits](/wiki/The_Man_Who_Understands_Hobbits)" (pp. 31–5), featuring extracts from an interview with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
