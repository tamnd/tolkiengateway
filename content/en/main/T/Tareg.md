**Tareg** *the Ilkorin* was an [Elf](/wiki/Ilkorindi "Ilkorindi"), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Gilfanon_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gilfanon-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the disastrous <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=War_of_Palisor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="War of Palisor (page does not exist)">War of Palisor</a>, Tareg led his hosts north-westwards upon hearing the rumour of the newly arrived [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes). His hosts were then joined by many of the [lost kindreds](/wiki/Ilkorindi "Ilkorindi").<sup id="cite_ref-Gilfanon_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gilfanon-1">[1]</a></sup>

Tareg and his host eventually arrived in the northwest of the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands), where they met the exiled Gnomes at the [Feast of Reunion](/wiki/Mereth_Aderthad "Mereth Aderthad"), as well as the [Men](/wiki/Men) led by [Ermon](/wiki/Ermon), who were about to prepare for a war with [Melko](/wiki/Melko). There, Tareg joined forces with the host of [Nólemë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB "Finwë") (the leader of the Gnomes), along with the sons of Ermon, and, at a place called <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gorfalong&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gorfalong (page does not exist)">Gorfalong</a>, fought a [great battle](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad "Nirnaeth Arnoediad") with Melko and his forces, many of whom were Men corrupted by Melko.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

However, nothing else is known about Tareg's fate after the battle.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Tareg* seems to be in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) according to [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack), but no etymology is given.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
