**Theodor Meir Bikel** ([2 May](/wiki/2_May) [1924](/wiki/1924)-[21 July](/wiki/21_July) [2015](/wiki/2015)) was an Austrian-American actor, folk singer and president of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associated_Actors_and_Artistes_of_America" title="wikipedia:Associated Actors and Artistes of America">Associated Actors and Artistes of America</a>. In [1980](/wiki/1980), he voiced the part of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in [*The Return of the King* (1980 film)](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)").
