**Tar-Ancalimon** was the fourteenth [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Ancalimon_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Ancalimon-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the reign of Tar-Ancalimon the rift between the [King's Men](/wiki/King%27s_Men), the larger part of the population that followed the Kings in their growing opposition to the [Ban of the Valar](/wiki/Ban_of_the_Valar), and the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful), those who maintained friendship with the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar), became wider. Many of the King's Men began to stop using the Elven-tongues and no longer taught them to their children. Despite the rejection of the Elven-tongues, the royal titles were still given in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). This was done out of custom and for fear that breaking the time-honoured usage would create ill-fortune.<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Ancalimon_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Ancalimon-1">[1]</a></sup>

Several notable events occurred during his reign:

- The [Ringwraiths](/wiki/Ringwraiths) first appeared in Middle-earth<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar), a haven of the Númenórean King's Men, was turned into a fortress<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- The city of [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) was built and became the chief haven of the Faithful Númenóreans<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
- The great [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles) of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) left Númenór; previously a pair had dwelt in an eyrie in the summit of the tower of the King's palace in [Armenelos](/wiki/Armenelos), living on the bounty of the King, since the days of [Elros](/wiki/Elros).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ancalimon* (<span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌtaraŋˈkalimon]</span>) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Brightest", and seems to be a masculinised form of *ancalima* ("brightest"),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> which consists of the superlative or intensive prefix *[an-](/wiki/An- "An-")* and the element *[calima](/wiki/Kalima "Kalima")* ("bright").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Like all the rulers of Númenor who took their royal names in Quenya, *Ancalimon* added the prefix *tar-* ("high") to his name when he received the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Sceptre of Númenor").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Tale of Years](/wiki/Appendix_B "Appendix B")*, it said in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [2251](/wiki/Second_Age_2251 "Second Age 2251") "Tar-Atanamir takes the sceptre", however, Atanamir died in 2221. 2221 is itself an emendation of 2251, and the former (2221) appears in the later tables, while the latter (2251) in the earlier tables: therefore 2251 (properly 2221) should have read "Death of Tar-Atanamir. Tar-Ancalimon takes the sceptre".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
