<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power (Season Two: Amazon Original Series Soundtrack)</b></i> is an album composed by [Bear McCreary](/wiki/Bear_McCreary) for the second season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* television series. It was released online on [23 August](/wiki/23_August), [2024](/wiki/2024) by Amazon Music.
