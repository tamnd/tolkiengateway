<b>The 1943 Map of <i>The Lord of the Rings</i></b> is a large elaborate map that was drawn in pencil and coloured chalks by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) in [1943](/wiki/1943)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> based on the state at that time of the [First Map of *The Lord of the Rings*](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The First Map of The Lord of the Rings") that was drawn by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-1943Map_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1943Map-3">[3]</a></sup>

On the 1943 map Christopher Tolkien used pictoral forms for the mountains and hills, which are less precise.<sup id="cite_ref-1943Map_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1943Map-3">[3]</a></sup> The map-squares of the 1943 map are of 4 centimetres side. The scale of the 1943 map is 4 centimetres for 100 miles.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The location of [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost) was included on the 1943 Map in the map square L 5, but was not included on subsequent maps.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In addition, the island in the [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn) is marked as wooded. Furthermore the [Nargil Pass](/wiki/Nargil_Pass) is included on the 1943 Map on map square S 17, but was not included in subsequent maps. J.R.R. Tolkien replaced the label [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas), which was approximately in the location of [Anfalas](/wiki/Anfalas) with the label Anfalas. In addition, J.R.R. Tolkien replaced the label Anarion on map square Q 14, which was approximately in the location of [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach) with the label Lebennin.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The 1943 map is not included in *The History of Middle-earth* series of books, but a description of its design and of some of its contents is included in chapter XVI The First Map of *The Lord of the Rings* in *[The Treason of Isengard](/wiki/The_Treason_of_Isengard)* volume of *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* series of books.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
