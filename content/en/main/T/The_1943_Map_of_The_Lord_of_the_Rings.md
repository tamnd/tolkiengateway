<b>The 1943 Map of <i>The Lord of the Rings</i></b> is a large elaborate map that was drawn in pencil and coloured chalks by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) in [1943](../_/1943.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> based on the state at that time of the [First Map of *The Lord of the Rings*](The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) that was drawn by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1943Map_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1943Map-3">[3]</a></sup>

On the 1943 map Christopher Tolkien used pictoral forms for the mountains and hills, which are less precise.<sup id="cite_ref-1943Map_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1943Map-3">[3]</a></sup> The map-squares of the 1943 map are of 4 centimetres side. The scale of the 1943 map is 4 centimetres for 100 miles.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The location of [Belegost](../B/Belegost.md) was included on the 1943 Map in the map square L 5, but was not included on subsequent maps.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In addition, the island in the [Sea of Rhûn](../S/Sea_of_Rhûn.md) is marked as wooded. Furthermore the [Nargil Pass](../N/Nargil_Pass.md) is included on the 1943 Map on map square S 17, but was not included in subsequent maps. J.R.R. Tolkien replaced the label [Belfalas](../B/Belfalas.md), which was approximately in the location of [Anfalas](../A/Anfalas.md) with the label Anfalas. In addition, J.R.R. Tolkien replaced the label Anarion on map square Q 14, which was approximately in the location of [Lossarnach](../L/Lossarnach.md) with the label Lebennin.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The 1943 map is not included in *The History of Middle-earth* series of books, but a description of its design and of some of its contents is included in chapter XVI The First Map of *The Lord of the Rings* in *[The Treason of Isengard](The_Treason_of_Isengard.md)* volume of *[The History of Middle-earth](The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* series of books.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
