"**The Great**" was a very common title, applied to (among others) the River [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>, the Ring [Narya](/wiki/Narya)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>, the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>, [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>, [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>, [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and [Tar-Atanamir](/wiki/Tar-Atanamir)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). <span></span>
