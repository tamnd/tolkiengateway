**Third Age 2645** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2645**) is the 2645th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2645 was also known as **S.R. 1045** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Aldor](../A/Aldor.md) [the Old](The_Old.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Fréa](../F/Fréa.md), son of Aldor, becomes the fourth King of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup>
