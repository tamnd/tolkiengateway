**Third Age 2754** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2754**) is the 2754th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2754 was also known as **S.R. 1154** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Freca](../F/Freca.md), struck down by a blow from the hand of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Helm](../H/Helm.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Calculation of the Date {#Calculation_of_the_Date}

Freca died four years before great troubles came to Rohan in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2758](Third_Age_2758.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
