**Third Age 3012** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3012**) is the 3012th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 3012 was also known as **S.R. 1412** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Asphodel Brandybuck](../A/Asphodel_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Otho Sackville-Baggins](../O/Otho_Sackville-Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](../L/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins.md), son of Otho, inherits [Leaf](../P/Pipe-weed.md) plantations in the [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md) of [the Shire](The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
