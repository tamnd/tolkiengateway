The <i><b>Tolkien Calendar 2026</b></i> is the official Tolkien calendar published by [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) on [12 August](/wiki/12_August) [2025](/wiki/2025). It features thirteen paintings of the three "[Great Tales](/wiki/Great_Tales)" by [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee), including new pieces. Lee also wrote the indroduction.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The official Tolkien calendar, this year containing 13 breathtaking paintings inspired by J.R.R. Tolkien’s three &#39;Great Tales&#39; of Middle-earth: THE CHILDREN OF HÚRIN, BEREN AND LÚTHIEN and THE FALL OF GONDOLIN. Featuring beautiful artwork by Alan Lee, including a selection of stunning new paintings and drawings appearing here for the first time.
</p>
</blockquote>
