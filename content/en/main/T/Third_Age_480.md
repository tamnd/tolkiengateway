**Third Age 480** (abbreviated to **T.A. 480**) is the 480th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Atanatar I](../A/Atanatar_I.md), son of [Turambar](<Turambar_(King_of_Gondor).md>) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PMElendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMElendil-1">[1]</a></sup>
