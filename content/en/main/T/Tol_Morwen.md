**Tol Morwen** was one of the three islands off the coast of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon). It was a remnant of the drowning of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), and on it stood the memorial stone of [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_Turambar), [Nienor](/wiki/Nienor), and [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen).

## History {#History}

During the drowning of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) at the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the site of the [Stone of the Hapless](/wiki/Stone_of_the_Hapless) did not drown but, as predicted by [Glirhuin](/wiki/Glirhuin), remained as an island, Tol Morwen, which was the grave of [Morwen Eledhwen](/wiki/Morwen "Morwen"), hence its name. It was the westernmost of the islands in [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) located off the coast of [Forlindon](/wiki/Forlindon).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Location {#Location}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Didier_Willis_-_Location_of_Tol_Morwen.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/b/bc/Didier_Willis_-_Location_of_Tol_Morwen.png/300px-Didier_Willis_-_Location_of_Tol_Morwen.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="128" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/b/bc/Didier_Willis_-_Location_of_Tol_Morwen.png/450px-Didier_Willis_-_Location_of_Tol_Morwen.png 1.5x, /w/images/b/bc/Didier_Willis_-_Location_of_Tol_Morwen.png 2x" data-file-width="500" data-file-height="214"/></a><figcaption>Approximate (speculative) location of Tol Morwen.</figcaption></figure>

The place is only attested in the texts, and neither the grave of Morwen nor the later island are represented on any of Tolkien's maps published so far. Being close to the gorge of [Cabed-en-Aras](/wiki/Cabed-en-Aras), the location of this site may nevertheless reasonably be inferred near the gorges of the [Teiglin](/wiki/Teiglin) and above its ravines.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Superimposing the map of Beleriand in the First Age and the map of the Westlands in the Third Age therefore allows to approximate the location of the island.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2023: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Tales_From_the_Lone-lands" title="Tales From the Lone-lands">Tales From the Lone-lands</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Known as the Isle of the Mother, it became a pilgrimage site for Númenóreans, who also built a fortress there due to its strategic location. During the <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">Third Age</a>, the island gradually became forgotten and the fortress fell into ruin, although mariners and knights still found it from time to time. By <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2965" title="Third Age 2965">2965</a>, a tribe of <a href="/wiki/Lossoth" title="Lossoth">Lossoth</a> established a camp on the island.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>
