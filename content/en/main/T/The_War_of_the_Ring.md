<i><b>The War of the Ring</b></i>, published in [1990](/wiki/1990), is the eighth volume of [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien)'s 12-volume book series, *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)*, in which he analyzes the unpublished manuscripts of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

It is also the third volume of *[The History of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, which documents the writing process of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

This book covers part of *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)* and part of *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*. Following the development of the previous books, the story continues to the opening of the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate).

The title *The War of the Ring* was the original title for Book 5 of *TLotR*, later discarded by Tolkien.

## Inscriptions {#Inscriptions}

There is an inscription in the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) characters in the first pages of every *History of Middle-earth* volume, written by Christopher Tolkien and describing the contents of the book. The inscription in Book VIII reads:

<dl><dd>&#34;<i>In the War of the Ring is traced the story of the history at Helm&#39;s Deep and the drowning of Isengard by the Ents, then is told of the journey of Frodo with Samwise and Gollum to the Morannon, of the meeting with Faramir and the stairs of Cirith Ungol, of the Battle of the <a href="/wiki/Pelennor_Fields" title="Pelennor Fields">Pelennor Fields</a>, and of the coming of Aragorn in the <a href="/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar" title="Corsairs of Umbar">fleet of Umbar</a>.</i>&#34;</dd></dl>

## From the Publisher {#From_the_Publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The third part of The History of The Lord of the Rings, an enthralling account of the writing of the Book of the Century which contains many additional scenes and includes the unpublished Epilogue in its entirety.<br/>
The War of the Ring takes up the story of The Lord of the Rings with the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Battle_of_Helm%27s_Deep" title="Battle of Helm&#39;s Deep">Battle of Helm&#39;s Deep</a> and the drowning of <a href="/wiki/Isengard" title="Isengard">Isengard</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Ents" title="Ents">Ents</a>, continues with the journey of Frodo, Sam and Gollum to the Pass of <a href="/wiki/Cirith_Ungol" title="Cirith Ungol">Cirith Ungol</a>, describes the war in Gondor, and ends with the parley between Gandalf and the ambassador of the Dark Lord before the Black Gate of Mordor.<br/>
</p><p>The book is illustrated with plans and drawings of the changing conceptions of <a href="/wiki/Orthanc" title="Orthanc">Orthanc</a>, <a href="/wiki/Dunharrow" title="Dunharrow">Dunharrow</a>, <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>, and the tunnels of <a href="/wiki/Shelob%27s_Lair" title="Shelob&#39;s Lair">Shelob&#39;s Lair</a>.
</p>
</blockquote>
