The **Three Elderwomen of the Elves**<sup id="cite_ref-nature_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nature-1">[1]</a></sup> were the spouses to the three [Elf-fathers](../E/Elf-fathers.md) who were among the [first](../E/Eru-begotten.md) 144 [Elves](../E/Elves.md) to [awaken](../A/Awakening_of_the_Elves.md) by the bay of [Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md) on the inland [Sea of Helcar](../S/Sea_of_Helcar.md). Their names were [Iminyë](../I/Iminyë.md), [Tatië](Tatië.md), and [Enelyë](../E/Enelyë.md). Their husbands are (respectively) [Imin](../I/Imin.md), [Tata](Tata.md), and [Enel](../E/Enel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Nature of Middle-earth](The_Nature_of_Middle-earth.md)*, [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) brought the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Western_Corn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Western Corn (page does not exist)">Western Corn</a> seed that was used in art of the making of lembas. He taught this art to the Three Elderwomen of the Elves, rather than to the [Yavannildi](../Y/Yavannildi.md).<sup id="cite_ref-nature_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nature-1">[1]</a></sup>
