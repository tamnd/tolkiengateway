The **Three Elderwomen of the Elves**<sup id="cite_ref-nature_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nature-1">[1]</a></sup> were the spouses to the three [Elf-fathers](/wiki/Elf-fathers) who were among the [first](/wiki/Eru-begotten "Eru-begotten") 144 [Elves](/wiki/Elves) to [awaken](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves "Awakening of the Elves") by the bay of [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen) on the inland [Sea of Helcar](/wiki/Sea_of_Helcar). Their names were [Iminyë](/wiki/Iminy%C3%AB), [Tatië](/wiki/Tati%C3%AB), and [Enelyë](/wiki/Enely%C3%AB). Their husbands are (respectively) [Imin](/wiki/Imin), [Tata](/wiki/Tata), and [Enel](/wiki/Enel).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth)*, [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) brought the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Western_Corn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Western Corn (page does not exist)">Western Corn</a> seed that was used in art of the making of lembas. He taught this art to the Three Elderwomen of the Elves, rather than to the [Yavannildi](/wiki/Yavannildi).<sup id="cite_ref-nature_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nature-1">[1]</a></sup>
