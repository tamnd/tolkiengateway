## The Tolkien Trilogy {#The_Tolkien_Trilogy}

The Tolkien Trilogy is a series of games which cover *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* which also included paperback editions of *The Hobbit*, *[The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* and *[The Two Towers](The_Two_Towers.md)*. It was only published in Europe.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The following games are part of the series:

1. [The Hobbit](<The_Hobbit_(1982_video_game).md>)
2. [Lord of the Rings: Game One](../L/Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Game_One.md)
3. [Shadows of Mordor](../S/Shadows_of_Mordor.md)

## The Tolkien Software Adventure Trilogy {#The_Tolkien_Software_Adventure_Trilogy}

The Tolkien Software Adventure Series is a couple of Software Adventure games that cover *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and included the European paperback editions, *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)*, *[The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, *[The Two Towers](The_Two_Towers.md)* and *[The Return of the King](The_Return_of_the_King.md)*. It was only published in North-America and Australia.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The following games are part of the series:

1. [The Hobbit Software Adventure](The_Hobbit_Software_Adventure.md)
2. [The Fellowship of the Ring Software Adventure](The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_Software_Adventure.md)
3. [Shadows of Mordor Software Adventure](../S/Shadows_of_Mordor_Software_Adventure.md)
4. [Crack of Doom Software Adventure](../C/Crack_of_Doom_Software_Adventure.md)
