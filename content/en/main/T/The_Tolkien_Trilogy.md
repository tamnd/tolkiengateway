## The Tolkien Trilogy {#The_Tolkien_Trilogy}

The Tolkien Trilogy is a series of games which cover *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* which also included paperback editions of *The Hobbit*, *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*. It was only published in Europe.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The following games are part of the series:

1. [The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1982_video_game)> "The Hobbit (1982 video game)")
2. [Lord of the Rings: Game One](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings:_Game_One)
3. [Shadows of Mordor](/wiki/Shadows_of_Mordor)

## The Tolkien Software Adventure Trilogy {#The_Tolkien_Software_Adventure_Trilogy}

The Tolkien Software Adventure Series is a couple of Software Adventure games that cover *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and included the European paperback editions, *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)* and *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*. It was only published in North-America and Australia.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The following games are part of the series:

1. [The Hobbit Software Adventure](/wiki/The_Hobbit_Software_Adventure)
2. [The Fellowship of the Ring Software Adventure](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_Software_Adventure)
3. [Shadows of Mordor Software Adventure](/wiki/Shadows_of_Mordor_Software_Adventure)
4. [Crack of Doom Software Adventure](/wiki/Crack_of_Doom_Software_Adventure)
