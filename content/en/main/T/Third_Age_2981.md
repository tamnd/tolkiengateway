**Third Age 2981** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2981**) is the 2981st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2981 was also known as **S.R. 1381** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Merimas Brandybuck](../M/Merimas_Brandybuck.md), first child and son of [Marmadas Brandybuck](../M/Marmadas_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Angelica Baggins](../A/Angelica_Baggins.md), daughter of [Ponto Baggins](../P/Ponto_Baggins_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
