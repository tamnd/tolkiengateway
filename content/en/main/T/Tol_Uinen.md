**Tol Uinen** was a small island that stood off the coasts of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), in the long narrow bay of [Rómenna](/wiki/R%C3%B3menna) that separated the regions of [Orrostar](/wiki/Orrostar) and [Hyarrostar](/wiki/Hyarrostar). Legend said that the island had been placed there by [Uinen](/wiki/Uinen) herself, the [Lady of the Seas](/wiki/Lady_of_the_Seas), who was held in particular reverence by the mariners of Númenor. Indeed, the [Guild of Venturers](/wiki/Guild_of_Venturers) called themselves *[Uinendili](/wiki/Uinendili)* - devotees of Uinen - and often moored their great ship [Eämbar](/wiki/E%C3%A4mbar) off Tol Uinen. Their <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Great_Captain&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Great Captain (page does not exist)">Great Captain</a>, [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion"), raised a tower on the island named [Calmindon](/wiki/Calmindon), to shine a light across the waters of the bay of Rómenna.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
