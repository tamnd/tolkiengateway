**Tol Uinen** was a small island that stood off the coasts of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), in the long narrow bay of [Rómenna](../R/Rómenna.md) that separated the regions of [Orrostar](../O/Orrostar.md) and [Hyarrostar](../H/Hyarrostar.md). Legend said that the island had been placed there by [Uinen](../U/Uinen.md) herself, the [Lady of the Seas](../L/Lady_of_the_Seas.md), who was held in particular reverence by the mariners of Númenor. Indeed, the [Guild of Venturers](../G/Guild_of_Venturers.md) called themselves *[Uinendili](../U/Uinendili.md)* - devotees of Uinen - and often moored their great ship [Eämbar](../E/Eämbar.md) off Tol Uinen. Their <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Great_Captain&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Great Captain (page does not exist)">Great Captain</a>, [Aldarion](Tar-Aldarion.md), raised a tower on the island named [Calmindon](../C/Calmindon.md), to shine a light across the waters of the bay of Rómenna.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
