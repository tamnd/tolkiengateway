<i><b>The First and Second Ages</b></i> is a collection of the proceedings of the 5th Tolkien Society Workshop, held in Milton Keynes in 1990. It was published by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) in [1992](/wiki/1992) under the auspices of the [Peter Roe Memorial Fund](/wiki/Peter_Roe_Memorial_Fund) in [1992](/wiki/1992); it was republished as a second edition in [2020](/wiki/2020).

## Contents {#Contents}

- "From Fëanor to Doctor Faustus: A "Creator", destroys himself", by [John Ellison](/wiki/John_Ellison)
- "Before the Moon, Before the Sun: The Big Time in Tolkien's Mythology", by Denis Bridoux
- "Purity and Danger in Middle-earth", by [Chris Seeman](/wiki/Chris_Seeman)
- "'Good People' - Elves of the First Age", by [Alex Lewis](/wiki/Alex_Lewis)
