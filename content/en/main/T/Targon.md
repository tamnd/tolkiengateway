**Targon** was a man of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

Targon worked at the storehouse and buttery for the [Guards of the Citadel](/wiki/Guards_of_the_Citadel) in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). On the morning of [9 March](/wiki/9_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> he gave [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) food and drink for a picnic.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
