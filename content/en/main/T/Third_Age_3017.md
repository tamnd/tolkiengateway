**Third Age 3017** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3017**) is the 3017th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 3017 was also known as **S.R. 1417** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## January {#January}

- [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) is released from [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> <sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

## February {#February}

- **1** - [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) captures [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) in the [Dead Marshes](../D/Dead_Marshes.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Hunt_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hunt-3">[2]</a></sup>

## February/March {#February/March}

- On his way to Mirkwood, Aragorn crosses the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) assisted by the [Beornings](../B/Beornings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
- [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) visits [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) and reads the [scroll of Isildur](../S/Scroll_of_Isildur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> He then leaves for [the Shire](The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-council_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-5">[4]</a></sup>
- On his way northwards, Gandalf gets word from [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) that Aragorn passed by with captured Gollum, and changes his course to meet him.<sup id="cite_ref-council_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-5">[4]</a></sup>

## March {#March}

- **21** - [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) brings Gollum to [Thranduil](Thranduil.md) in [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Hunt_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hunt-3">[2]</a></sup>
- **23** - Gandalf comes to Thranduil and questions Gollum.<sup id="cite_ref-Hunt_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hunt-3">[2]</a></sup>
- **29** - Gandalf leaves Mirkwood and resumes his course west for [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Hunt_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hunt-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Unknown {#Unknown}

- Birth of [Alphros](../A/Alphros.md), son of [Elphir](../E/Elphir.md), [Prince of Dol Amroth](../P/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
- [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) becomes [Third Marshal of the Riddermark](Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The dates concerning Gollum are taken from *[The Hunt for the Ring](The_Hunt_for_the_Ring.md)* in the *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*, where those events are said to take place a year later, in 3018; however the published *[The Tale of Years](The_Tale_of_Years.md)* ([Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md)) places the capture in the year 3017. According to Hammond and Scull, Tolkien changed the date because he probably thought that more time was needed between the capture of Gollum and the arrival of Gandalf in Hobbiton (12 April, 3018).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup> This article reflects Tolkien's later decision, but uses the exact dates from *The Hunt for the Ring*.
