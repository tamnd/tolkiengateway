In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), **Tûl Isra** was the administrative capital of [Sîrayn](/wiki/S%C3%AErayn), located in the province of Isra. It sits on the east bank of the Hayk Sarzain ([Ta.](/wiki/Languages_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing#Tazinain "Languages in Middle-earth Role Playing") "Swollen-waters in the Earth-sands"), a deep lake formed by the Maudar river. As the most powerful city in the region, its ruler, the Tarb, long held dominion over six of the seven cities of Sîrayn, with only the distant seaport of [Tûl Harar](/wiki/T%C3%BBl_Harar) remaining independent.

A census in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1622&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1622 (page does not exist)">1622</a> recorded over 17,000 residents within the city proper, with another 6,000 to 10,000 living in the surrounding province. The population included a mix of Sirani citizens, Ayten nomads, and foreign merchants.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Tûl Isra was originally founded in the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Its location was strategic, situated where the *Yûl Isra* trade route branched off from the Camel Road near the confluence of the river Siresha and the Maudar River, leading south to the [Yellow Mountains](/wiki/Yellow_Mountains). The original city stood northwest of the Hayk Sarzain until its destruction during in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1933&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1933 (page does not exist)">1933</a> by the [Storm King](/wiki/Fifth_of_the_Nazg%C3%BBl "Fifth of the Nazgûl")'s armies.

Later in the Second Age, a village named Mumett was established on the eastern shore of the lake. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_83&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 83 (page does not exist)">83</a>, members of Clan Másra refounded Tûl Isra just south of Mumett. This new location featured a defensible bluff that augmented the city's twenty-foot high walls. Stone for the reconstruction was quarried from the nearby [Brû Isra](/wiki/S%C3%AErayn#Isra "Sîrayn") or salvaged from ancient ruins.

Over the centuries, the city outgrew its initial boundaries. The administrative centre was relocated to a new, expansive plaza featuring a palace, and the city expanded southward with new outer walls.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

Bazaud Izain and his Bulgan clan were secuded by the Storm King in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1375&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1375 (page does not exist)">1375</a>, and in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1380&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1380 (page does not exist)">1380</a> took control of the court. After [Tartas Izain](/wiki/Tartas_Izain) was appointed Taraskon in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1385&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1385 (page does not exist)">1385</a>, he established a temple to the [Silent One](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron") by [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1397&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1397 (page does not exist)">1397</a>. Driven off in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1436&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1436 (page does not exist)">1436</a>, he became Taraskon again from [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1629&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1629 (page does not exist)">1629</a> until [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1641&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1641 (page does not exist)">1641</a>, when the Bulgan clan falls.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

By [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1630](/wiki/Third_Age_1630 "Third Age 1630"), Tûl Isra was divided into several distinct sections:

### The Inner City {#The_Inner_City}

The *Hask Katarn* ([Ta.](/wiki/Languages_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing#Tazinain "Languages in Middle-earth Role Playing") "Heart of the City") contained the oldest and most affluent districts. It was characterized by broad avenues lined with fine shops, spacious residences, and ornately carved temples built primarily of marble. The architecture featured abstract curvilinear designs on exteriors and geometric mosaics on interiors. The palace of the Tarb and administrative buildings were located here.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

The city also hosted various temples. The Temple of the [Silent One](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron") was an unadorned stone edifice with black wooden doors, avoided by most citizens due to its ominous atmosphere. In contrast, the Tayee Temple was filled with frescoes and music. The Temple of the True Flame featured gold-sheathed domes, while the Temple of Saubaiz was adorned with grotesque figures and the depiction of an eight-eyed squid-like monster.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

### The Outer City {#The_Outer_City}

The outer districts were less opulent, constructed primarily of brick rather than stone. It is here that the Tayee school was located, which rivaled the libraries of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) and [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas). The Outer City also housed the Tár Ribausk ([Ta.](/wiki/Languages_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing#Tazinain "Languages in Middle-earth Role Playing") "Wizard's Tower"), the residence of Court Mage Kasair.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

### The Ruins {#The_Ruins}

The eastern edge of the city remained in ruins, the result of the magical fires unleashed by the sorcerers of [Charnesra](/wiki/Charnesra) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1457&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1457 (page does not exist)">1457</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

### The Outer City Market {#The_Outer_City_Market}

Located outside the city gates to the north were the caravan grounds. They were frequented by Haradan traders, Dwarves from the Yellow Mountains, and Elves from the southern archipelago.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
