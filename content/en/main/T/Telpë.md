<i><b>telpë</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-SApp_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-1">[1]</a></sup> or <i><b>tyelpë</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-2">[2]</a></sup> <i><b>tyelep-</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-3">[3]</a></sup> <i><b>telep</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-SApp_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-1">[1]</a></sup> are [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) words for "[silver](/wiki/Silver "Silver")".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a letter, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") stated that the form *tyelpe* derives from [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kyelep%C4%93&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kyelepē (page does not exist)">kyelepē</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-L347_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-4">[4]</a></sup>

It appears like *tyelpe* is the most ancient (or "pure") Quenya word for silver; the form *telpe*, which became the usual form among the Quenya-speaking [Elves](/wiki/Elves), was borrowed from (or influenced by) [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) *[telpe](/wiki/Telepi "Telepi")* (itself apparently a later form of the original Telerin form *[telepi](/wiki/Telepi "Telepi")*).<sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L347_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LR_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-5">[5]</a></sup>

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) has noted that *tyelep-*, *tyelpe* derive from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [KYELEP](/wiki/KYELEP).<sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-3">[3]</a></sup> In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, *telpe*, *tyelpe* derive from root [KYELEP/TELEP](/wiki/KYELEP "KYELEP").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-5">[5]</a></sup>
