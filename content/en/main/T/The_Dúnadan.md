**The Dúnadan** was a title applied to [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) by [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") meaning 'Man of the West'. [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") learned of this name when Aragorn found the two hobbits discussing news of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
