## Contents {#Contents}

- Articles
    - Philip Irving Mitchell, "'Legend and History Have Met and Fused': The Interlocution of Anthropology, Historiography, and Incarnation in J.R.R. Tolkien's '[On Fairy-stories](../O/On_Fairy-stories~23735.md)'"
    - John M. Bowers, "Tolkien's Goldberry and *The Maid of the Moor*"
    - Lucas Annear, "Language in Tolkien's '[Bagme Bloma](../B/Bagme_Bloma.md)'"
    - [José Manuel Ferrández Bru](../J/José_Manuel_Ferrández_Bru.md), "'Wingless fluttering': Some Personal Connections in Tolkien's Formative Years"

- Notes and Documents
    - [John Garth](../J/John_Garth.md), "Robert Quilter Gilson, T.C.B.S.: A Brief Life in Letters"
    - [Janet Brennan Croft](../J/Janet_Brennan_Croft.md), "The Hen that Laid the Eggs: Tolkien and the Officers Training Corps"

- Book Reviews
    - *Quenya Phonology: Comparative Tables, Outline of Phonetic Development, Outline of Phonology* (*[Parma Eldalamberon 19](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_19.md)*), by J.R.R. Tolkien, edited by [Christopher Gilson](../C/Christopher_Gilson.md), reviewed by John Garth
    - *[Hither Shore](<../H/Hither_Shore_(journal).md>): Interdisciplinary Journal of Modern Fantasy Literature, Jahrbuch der Deutschen Tolkien Gesellschaft e.V.*, edited by Thomas Fornet-Ponse (editor-in-chief), [Marcel Bülles](../M/Marcel_Bülles.md), [Thomas Honegger](Thomas_Honegger.md), Rainer Nagel, Alexandra Velten, and Frank Weinreich, and *Volume four, 2007 (2008): "Tolkiens Kleinere Werke" \[Tolkien's Lesser Works\]*, and *Volume five, 2008 (2009): "Der Hobbit" \[The Hobbit\]*, reviewed by Mark T. Hooker
    - *[Music in Middle-earth](../M/Music_in_Middle-earth.md)*, edited by Heidi Steimel and Friedhelm Schneidewind, reviewed by Gerald Seaman
    - *[Middle-earth Minstrel](../M/Middle-earth_Minstrel.md): Essays on Music in Tolkien*, edited by Bradford Lee Eden, reviewed by Gerald Seaman
    - *The Power of Tolkien's Prose: Middle-Earth's Magical Style*, by Steve Walker, Reviewed by Richard C. West
    - *The Saga of King Heidrek the Wise*, edited by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), reviewed by [Tom Shippey](Tom_Shippey.md)
    - Book Notes, by [Douglas A. Anderson](../D/Douglas_A._Anderson.md)

- Review-Essay
    - *[The Ring Goes Ever On](The_Ring_Goes_Ever_On%253A_Proceedings_of_the_Tolkien_2005_Conference.md)*, edited by Sarah Wells, reviewed by Deidre A. Dawson

- [David Bratman](../D/David_Bratman.md) and Merlin DeTardo, "The Year's Work in Tolkien Studies 2008"
- Rebecca Epstein, David Bratman, [Michael D.C. Drout](../M/Michael_D.C._Drout.md), Merlin DeTardo, and Douglas A. Anderson, "Bibliography (in English) for 2009"
