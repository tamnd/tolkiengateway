<i><b>Trāgu</b></i> is the name of the [dragon](../D/Dragons.md) [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) in the [language of Dale](../L/Language_of_Dale.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It has been suggested that the word seems to come from a [Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) \*TAR'H.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
