<i><b>Trāgu</b></i> is the name of the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) in the [language of Dale](/wiki/Language_of_Dale "Language of Dale").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It has been suggested that the word seems to come from a [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") \*TAR'H.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
