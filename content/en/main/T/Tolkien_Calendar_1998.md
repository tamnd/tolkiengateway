The <i><b>Tolkien Calendar 1998</b></i> was the official Tolkien calendar published by [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) on [12 June](/wiki/12_June) [1997](/wiki/1997). It features art from 12 different artists based on *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
