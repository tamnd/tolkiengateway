**Third Age 2952** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2952**) is the 2952nd [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Third Age 2952 was also known as **S.R. 1352** in [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) reveals to '[Estel](/wiki/Estel)' his true name and ancestry, and delivers to him the [Shards of Narsil](/wiki/Shards_of_Narsil).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HS_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-error_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-error-3">[note 1]</a></sup>
- [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen), newly returned from [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), meets [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in the woods of [Imladris](/wiki/Rivendell "Rivendell").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HS_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-error_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-error-3">[note 1]</a></sup>
- [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) goes out into the Wild.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HS_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-error_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-error-3">[note 1]</a></sup>
