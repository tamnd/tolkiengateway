<i><b>The Horns of the Host of Doriath</b></i> is a poem composed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) roughly between [1915](../_/1915.md) and [1930](../_/1930.md). It was first called <i><b>The Trumpet of Faery</b></i> and <i><b>The Trumpets of Faery</b></i> in *[The Book of Ishness](The_Book_of_Ishness.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Within the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), the phrase itself is possibly meant to allude to the ambush at [Sarn Athrad](../S/Sarn_Athrad.md), when the [Laiquendi](../G/Green-elves.md) and the [Iathrim](../I/Iathrim.md) filled the woods with the sound of elven-horns during their ambush of the [Dwarves of Nogrod](../D/Dwarves_of_Nogrod.md), who were fleeing from [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md) with the stolen [Nauglamir](../N/Nauglamír.md).
