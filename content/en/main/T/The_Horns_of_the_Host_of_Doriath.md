<i><b>The Horns of the Host of Doriath</b></i> is a poem composed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) roughly between [1915](/wiki/1915) and [1930](/wiki/1930). It was first called <i><b>The Trumpet of Faery</b></i> and <i><b>The Trumpets of Faery</b></i> in *[The Book of Ishness](/wiki/The_Book_of_Ishness)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Within the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), the phrase itself is possibly meant to allude to the ambush at [Sarn Athrad](/wiki/Sarn_Athrad), when the [Laiquendi](/wiki/Green-elves "Green-elves") and the [Iathrim](/wiki/Iathrim) filled the woods with the sound of elven-horns during their ambush of the [Dwarves of Nogrod](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Nogrod), who were fleeing from [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth) with the stolen [Nauglamir](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr "Nauglamír").
