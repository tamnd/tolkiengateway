**The Lord of the Rings: Circle of Conflict** is a competitive dice-driven game for two to four players. Players will attempt to score the most victory points through pushing their Fellowship's progress while hindering opponents with Shadow abilities.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

It is the Third Age of Middle-earth and a great war between the many nations is unfolding.

Will you recruit iconic characters for The Free Peoples or perhaps muster the troops of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) to fight for Shadow in one of two pivotal battles? Will you hunt your opponent’s Fellowship with your [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) or secretly move your Fellowship to evade [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)’s watchful Eye?

To win as the Free Peoples, be the first player to get The Fellowship to reach [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md) and defeat Shadow in Battle. As the Shadow, be the first player to fully corrupt the Fellowship and defeat them in Battle.
