The **Travellers** was the name in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") for the four [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) who travelled with the [Company of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring"): [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) and [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
