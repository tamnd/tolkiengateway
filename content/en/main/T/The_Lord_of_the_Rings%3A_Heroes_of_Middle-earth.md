<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: Heroes of Middle-earth</b></i> is a collectable RPG video game developed by Capital Games and produced by [Electronic Arts](/wiki/Electronic_Arts) for iOS and Android devices. It is based on [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit) and [The Lord of the Rings books](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Lord of the Rings"). The game was released on [10 May](/wiki/10_May) of [2023](/wiki/2023).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

On [24 February](/wiki/24_February) of [2024](/wiki/2024), it was announced that the game will no longer become available for use generally on [24 May](/wiki/24_May) of the same year.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Plot {#Plot}

The game explores an original, never-before-told story. It features immersive storytelling, turn-based combat, deep collection systems, and a wide roster of characters based from both The Lord of The Rings and The Hobbit, and it is set during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
