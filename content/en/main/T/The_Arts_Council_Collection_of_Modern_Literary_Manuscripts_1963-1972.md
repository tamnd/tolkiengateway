<i><b>The Arts Council Collection of Modern Literary Manuscripts 1963-1972</b></i> is a catalogue of manuscripts collected by the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_Council_of_Great_Britain" title="wikipedia:Arts Council of Great Britain">Arts Council of Great Britain</a> (through its National Manuscript Collection of Contemporary Writers), acquired mainly by the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Museum" title="wikipedia:British Museum">British Museum</a>.

A [letter from J.R.R. Tolkien to Elizabeth Jennings](/wiki/Letter_to_Elizabeth_Jennings "Letter to Elizabeth Jennings") is illustrated in plate 3.
