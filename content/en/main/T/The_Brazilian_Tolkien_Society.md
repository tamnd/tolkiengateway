**Sociedade de Tolkien Brasileira** (Brazilian: **Brazilian Tolkien Society**), abbreviated STB or BTS, is a Tolkien Society in Brazil. It was founded on [1996](/wiki/1999) by Daniel Cossi.

> Reference: [http://cultura.estadao.com.br/noticias/cinema,fa-funda-sociedade-para-discutir-senhor-dos-aneis,20011211p2899 Fã funda sociedade para discutir Senhor dos Anéis]

Daniel Cossi in 2001  "Annexed the Society to the company of which he is a partner, investing $ 275,000" . Since then, the Brazilian Tolkien Society has been acting as a consultant for products related to Peter Jackson movies.

> Reference: [http://www.terra.com.br/istoegente/125/reportagem/senhor_aneis_ouro.htm Interview with the founder]

Daniel Cossi was also official consultant for Warner Bros Brasil and New Line Cinema, designer and creator of all licensed products of the trilogy movies The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit, as well as translation revisor of both movies.
