**Third Age 2004** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2004**) is the 2004th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2004 was also known as **S.R. 404** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Galador](../G/Galador.md), son of [Imrazôr](../I/Imrazôr.md) and [Mithrellas](../M/Mithrellas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_Amroth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol_Amroth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Gilmith](../G/Gilmith.md), daughter of [Imrazôr](../I/Imrazôr.md) and [Mithrellas](../M/Mithrellas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_Amroth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol_Amroth-1">[1]</a></sup>
