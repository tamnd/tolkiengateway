**The Trumpets of Faerie** refers to an [unpublished](../../index/U/Unpublished_material.md) book of [poems](../../index/P/Poems_by_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). In [1916](../_/1916.md) Tolkien sent the original to *Sidgwick &amp; Jackson*, for publication but was rejected. According to Douglas A. Anderson, Tolkien offered it to the Swan Press while he was at Leeds in the early 1920s *"and was again turned down"*; and after he returned to Oxford *‘he made one further attempt, and in the spring of 1926 the Oxford publisher Basil Blackwell turned it down’* .<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
