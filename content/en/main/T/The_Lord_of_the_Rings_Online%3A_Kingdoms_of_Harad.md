<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Online: Kingdoms of <a href="/wiki/Harad" title="Harad">Harad</a></b></i> (**Update 46**) is the thirteenth expansion for *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*.

## From the Developer {#From_the_Developer}

The Kingdoms of Harad stand at a crossroads, their rivalries sharpening as a greater threat gathers strength. [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) has come with a warning of a darkness rising in the South. The Thardúth, ancient servants of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), rise once more, harnessing powers that could unmake the balance of Middle-earth.

Rally the Kindred of the Coins along with the fierce Mizâdi, Shâra of Renewed Hamât, as rival kingdoms clash and old grudges resurface. From arid wastelands, blighted forests, and perilous ruins, war looms on every horizon. Who will rise to lead the Kingdoms of Harad against the coming darkness, or deeper into it?
