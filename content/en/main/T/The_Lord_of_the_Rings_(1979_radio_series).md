**The Lord of the Rings** was a [1979](/wiki/1979) radio adaptation by [Bernard Mayes](/wiki/Bernard_Mayes), produced by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mind%27s_Eye_(publisher)" title="wikipedia:The Mind&#39;s Eye (publisher)">The Mind&#39;s Eye</a>.

It is an abridged version of the story, voiced by local Virginia theatre actors, who show serious problems pronouncing the names correctly. Due to a low budget, effects were home made, and music was low-key.

The production was overshadowed by that of the [BBC](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), and is usually considered inferior in quality, music and acting.
