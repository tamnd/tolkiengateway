The **Treegarth of Orthanc** was the name of the forest established around the tower of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc) in the valley of [Nan Curunír](/wiki/Nan_Curun%C3%ADr).

## History {#History}

Following the [Entish](/wiki/Ents "Ents") victory at the [Battle of Isengard](/wiki/Battle_of_Isengard), many trees stayed around Orthanc to guard [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman).<sup id="cite_ref-RKPartings_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKPartings-3">[3]</a></sup> Shortly before [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and company departed [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) on [5 March](/wiki/5_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) declared that trees would return and that the new forest would be called the "Watchwood".<sup id="cite_ref-voice_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-voice-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

When Gandalf and company returned on [22 August](/wiki/22_August) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") Treebeard welcomed them to the "Treegarth of Orthanc". The Ents had destroyed the [Ring of Isengard](/wiki/Ring_of_Isengard) and filled the land with orchards and trees. A stream ran into a clear lake, in the middle of which stood Orthanc the impregnable tower.<sup id="cite_ref-great_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RKPartings_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKPartings-3">[3]</a></sup> However despite the "watch", Treebeard allowed Saruman to leave the Watchwood.<sup id="cite_ref-RKPartings_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKPartings-3">[3]</a></sup>

Within the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom), [King Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") granted the Ents complete self-governance of the Treegarth of Orthanc.<sup id="cite_ref-RKPartings_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKPartings-3">[3]</a></sup>
