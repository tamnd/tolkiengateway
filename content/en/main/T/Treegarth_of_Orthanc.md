The **Treegarth of Orthanc** was the name of the forest established around the tower of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md) in the valley of [Nan Curunír](../N/Nan_Curunír.md).

## History {#History}

Following the [Entish](../E/Ents.md) victory at the [Battle of Isengard](../B/Battle_of_Isengard.md), many trees stayed around Orthanc to guard [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-RKPartings_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKPartings-3">[3]</a></sup> Shortly before [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), [Théoden](Théoden.md) and company departed [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) on [5 March](../_/5_March.md) [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md), [Treebeard](Treebeard.md) declared that trees would return and that the new forest would be called the "Watchwood".<sup id="cite_ref-voice_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-voice-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

When Gandalf and company returned on [22 August](../_/22_August.md) [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md) Treebeard welcomed them to the "Treegarth of Orthanc". The Ents had destroyed the [Ring of Isengard](../R/Ring_of_Isengard.md) and filled the land with orchards and trees. A stream ran into a clear lake, in the middle of which stood Orthanc the impregnable tower.<sup id="cite_ref-great_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RKPartings_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKPartings-3">[3]</a></sup> However despite the "watch", Treebeard allowed Saruman to leave the Watchwood.<sup id="cite_ref-RKPartings_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKPartings-3">[3]</a></sup>

Within the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md), [King Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) granted the Ents complete self-governance of the Treegarth of Orthanc.<sup id="cite_ref-RKPartings_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKPartings-3">[3]</a></sup>
