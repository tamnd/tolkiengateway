**The Hobbit** was a video game made by [Bryce Fitzsimons](/wiki/Bryce_Fitzsimons). The story covers *[the Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit "The Hobbit")*, in which the player must reclaim the treasures of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) from [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) the [Dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons"). It was written by [Bryce Fitzsimons](/wiki/Bryce_Fitzsimons).
