**The Coming of the Elves and the Making of Kôr** is the fifth chapter of *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Eriol](../E/Eriol.md) asked about [Melko](../M/Melko.md)'s release, and [Meril](../M/Meril-i-Turinqi.md) explained that it happened close to the end of the third century of his imprisonment. At the time, [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) and his [hawks](../H/Hawks.md) saw the first [elves](../E/Elves.md) - when the inhabitants of [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) heard the news they cried happily *I Eldar tulier* - "the Eldar have come". Manwë decided to unchain Melko early, however leaving manacles and fetters from the *[tilkal](Tilkal.md)* on him.

[Nornorë](../N/Nornorë.md) took the three leaders of Elves to Valinor: these were [Isil Inwë](../I/Ingwë.md), ruling over the [Teleri](../V/Vanyar.md); [Finwë Nólomë](../F/Finwë.md), ruling over the [Noldoli](../N/Noldoli.md); and [Tinwë Linto](Thingol.md), ruling over the [Solosimpi](../S/Solosimpi.md). Meril explained she was a descendant of Inwë, while Finwë was the father of [Turgon](Turgon.md), and Tinwë later stayed with [Wendelin](../M/Melian.md) in [Hisilómë](../H/Hithlum.md).

The [Valar](../V/Valar.md) decided to invite the Elves to Valinor. Only [Makar](../M/Makar.md), [Meássë](../M/Meássë.md), [Mandos](../M/Mandos.md), [Fui](../F/Fui.md), and [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md) were less enthusiastic. Melko joined the majority, but later lied that he was against "imprisoning" the Elves.

[Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) and his whale [Uin](../U/Uin.md) used [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md)'s island as a ferry, bringing first the Teleri, then the Noldoli, and finally the Solosimpi. Using his island angered Ossë, who stopped it in the middle of the [Great Sea](../G/Great_Sea.md), where it become known as [Tol Eressëa](Tol_Eressëa.md), the Lonely Island. Afterwards, the Teleri and Noldoli built [Kôr](../K/Kôr.md). Eventually, after many years, the Solosimpi used swans and sea birds to take them on ships to Valinor. In Valinor, the Noldoli made a large quantity of gems, of which last and most beautiful were opals, until [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) made [Silmarili](../S/Silmarils.md).
