**The Coming of the Elves and the Making of Kôr** is the fifth chapter of *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Eriol](/wiki/Eriol) asked about [Melko](/wiki/Melko)'s release, and [Meril](/wiki/Meril-i-Turinqi "Meril-i-Turinqi") explained that it happened close to the end of the third century of his imprisonment. At the time, [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) and his [hawks](/wiki/Hawks "Hawks") saw the first [elves](/wiki/Elves "Elves") - when the inhabitants of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) heard the news they cried happily *I Eldar tulier* - "the Eldar have come". Manwë decided to unchain Melko early, however leaving manacles and fetters from the *[tilkal](/wiki/Tilkal "Tilkal")* on him.

[Nornorë](/wiki/Nornor%C3%AB) took the three leaders of Elves to Valinor: these were [Isil Inwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB "Ingwë"), ruling over the [Teleri](/wiki/Vanyar "Vanyar"); [Finwë Nólomë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB "Finwë"), ruling over the [Noldoli](/wiki/Noldoli); and [Tinwë Linto](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol"), ruling over the [Solosimpi](/wiki/Solosimpi). Meril explained she was a descendant of Inwë, while Finwë was the father of [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon), and Tinwë later stayed with [Wendelin](/wiki/Melian "Melian") in [Hisilómë](/wiki/Hithlum "Hithlum").

The [Valar](/wiki/Valar) decided to invite the Elves to Valinor. Only [Makar](/wiki/Makar), [Meássë](/wiki/Me%C3%A1ss%C3%AB), [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos), [Fui](/wiki/Fui), and [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB) were less enthusiastic. Melko joined the majority, but later lied that he was against "imprisoning" the Elves.

[Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) and his whale [Uin](/wiki/Uin) used [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB)'s island as a ferry, bringing first the Teleri, then the Noldoli, and finally the Solosimpi. Using his island angered Ossë, who stopped it in the middle of the [Great Sea](/wiki/Great_Sea), where it become known as [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), the Lonely Island. Afterwards, the Teleri and Noldoli built [Kôr](/wiki/K%C3%B4r). Eventually, after many years, the Solosimpi used swans and sea birds to take them on ships to Valinor. In Valinor, the Noldoli made a large quantity of gems, of which last and most beautiful were opals, until [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) made [Silmarili](/wiki/Silmarils "Silmarils").
