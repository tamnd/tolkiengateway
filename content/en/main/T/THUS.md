**THUS** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "evil mist, fog, Darkness".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *thūsē*
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *thû* ("horrible darkness, black mist")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), the root THUS (given no signification) yields the Primitive Quendian form *thausā*, from which derives Quenya ([Qenya](/wiki/Qenya)) *[saura](/wiki/Saura "Saura")*. From this root also derives [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *thaw* "corrupt, rotten", and *thû* "stench" (used as a proper name, *[Thû](/wiki/Th%C3%BB)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
