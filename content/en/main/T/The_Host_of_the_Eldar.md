**The Host of the Eldar** is the thirty-seventh scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and the forty-eighth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The men of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) continue to get ready for the coming battle. [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) sits and sees a boy holding a sword nearby. He tells the boy to show him the sword. As Aragorn examines the sword, the boy tells him his name is [Haleth](</wiki/Haleth_(film_character)> "Haleth (film character)"), son of [Háma](/wiki/H%C3%A1ma), and that the men are saying the coming battle is hopeless. Aragorn returns his sword to him and tells him that *"there is always hope"*.

As Aragorn is readying himself for battle indoors, [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) enters and apologies to him for [despairing at the situation earlier](/wiki/The_Glittering_Caves "The Glittering Caves"). Aragorn smiles and tells him there is nothing to forgive. As [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) puts on his chainmail shirt, which is too long for him, he remarks that is it *"a little tight across the chest"*. At that moment, they hear a horn sound.

<p>That is no Orc horn.

<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/Legolas" title="Legolas">Legolas</a></cite></p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

An Elven host led by [Haldir](/wiki/Haldir) enters [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep) to the joy of the men of Rohan. [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) meets them in surprise. Haldir tells them that the host was sent by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond). Aragorn and Legolas happily greet him as he tells them all that the Elves are *"happy to fight alongside Men once more"*.
