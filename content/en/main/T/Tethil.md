**Tethil** was the name of a poppy-inhabiting flower [fay](../F/Fays.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tet_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tet-1">[1]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about her, and she doesn't appear in any of the narratives.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Tethil* is in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md), and it includes the element <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Teth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Teth (page does not exist)">teth</a></i> ("a bud").<sup id="cite_ref-Tet_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tet-1">[1]</a></sup> Its [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) cognate is <i><b>Tetillë</b></i>, an elaboration on <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tetl&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tetl (page does not exist)">tetl</a></i> ("small flower"), which is itself derived from the root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=TETE&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TETE (page does not exist)">TETE</a> ("bud, blossom").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
