**Town Hole** was a building in [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving) where the [mayor](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving "Mayor of Michel Delving") resided.

Some time between [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3013](/wiki/Third_Age_3013 "Third Age 3013") and [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") the building collapsed. Mayor [Will Whitfoot](/wiki/Will_Whitfoot) emerged safe and sound from the ruins covered in white dust, and thus earned the affectionate nickname "Flourdumpling".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name must be a pun on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Town Hall">Town Hall</a>, and also suggests that it was a [Hobbit-hole](/wiki/Hobbit-hole).
