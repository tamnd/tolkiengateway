**Third Age 1447** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1447**) is the 1447th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Eldacar](<../E/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor).md>) returns from [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md); his army is joined by supporters from [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md), [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md) and [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) as he travels south.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[2]</a></sup>
- At the [Battle of the Crossings of Erui](../B/Battle_of_the_Crossings_of_Erui.md), [Eldacar](<../E/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor).md>)'s army defeats [Castamir](../C/Castamir.md)'s forces. Eldacar personally slays Castamir to reclaim the throne of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The sons of [Castamir](../C/Castamir.md), and their supporters, are besieged in the [Siege of Pelargir](../S/Siege_of_Pelargir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[2]</a></sup>
