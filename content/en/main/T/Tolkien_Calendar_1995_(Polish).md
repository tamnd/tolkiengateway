**Tolkien Calendar 1995** was published by Seckji Tolkienowskiej Slaskiego Kluba Fantastyki, Poland ([Polish Tolkien Society](../P/Polish_Tolkien_Society.md)). The calendar features pen and ink illustrations by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Maciej_Wygnanski&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maciej Wygnanski (page does not exist)">Maciej Wygnanski</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Aleksandra_Jagielowica&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aleksandra Jagielowica (page does not exist)">Aleksandra Jagielowica</a>, [Małgorzata Pudlik](../M/Małgorzata_Pudlik.md), Marek Dabrowski, and [Karolina Stopa](../K/Karolina_Stopa.md).

- Dimensions: 11 5/8 x 8 1/4 inches
- Heavy, clear plastic cover sheet
- Blue plastic comb-bound
