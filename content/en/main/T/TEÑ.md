**TEÑ** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "show, sign, indicate, signify, to point at".<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup> The root is related to [TEK](/wiki/TEK) ("sign").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tenwe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tenwe (page does not exist)">tenwe</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup> or *teñwa* &gt; <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Teiw&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Teiw (page does not exist)">teiw</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tengwe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tengwe (page does not exist)">tengwe</a></i> ("indication, sign, token"); <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tengwesta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tengwesta (page does not exist)">tengwesta</a></i>; *[tengwa](/wiki/Tengwa "Tengwa")*<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

Note that the roots <a href="/wiki/TEN#Other_versions" title="TEN">TEÑ<sup>1</sup> and TEÑ<sup>2</sup></a>, with different significations, appear in the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")*.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root TEŊE ("know, understand").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
