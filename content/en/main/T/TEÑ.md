**TEÑ** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "show, sign, indicate, signify, to point at".<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup> The root is related to [TEK](TEK.md) ("sign").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tenwe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tenwe (page does not exist)">tenwe</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup> or *teñwa* &gt; <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Teiw&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Teiw (page does not exist)">teiw</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tengwe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tengwe (page does not exist)">tengwe</a></i> ("indication, sign, token"); <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tengwesta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tengwesta (page does not exist)">tengwesta</a></i>; *[tengwa](Tengwa.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

Note that the roots <a href="TEN.md#Other_versions" title="TEN">TEÑ<sup>1</sup> and TEÑ<sup>2</sup></a>, with different significations, appear in the *[Etymologies](The_Etymologies.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>
