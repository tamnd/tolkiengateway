**Third Age 2700** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2700**) is the 2700th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2700 was also known as **S.R. 1100** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Beregond](<../B/Beregond_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>), son of [Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Beren](<../B/Beren_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
