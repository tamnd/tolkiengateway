**Third Age 1100** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1100**) is the 1100th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- About this time, [the Wise](The_Wise.md) (the [Istari](../I/Istari.md) and the chief [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md)) discover that an evil power has made a stronghold at [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md). It is thought to be one of the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-1">[1]</a></sup>
