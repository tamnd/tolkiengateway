**Thráin I** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [1934](/wiki/Third_Age_1934 "Third Age 1934") – [2190](/wiki/Third_Age_2190 "Third Age 2190"), died aged 256) was the [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Durin%27s_Folk "Kings of Durin's Folk") of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) for 209 years, succeeding his father, [Náin I](/wiki/N%C3%A1in_I), when he was killed by [the Balrog](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane "Durin's Bane") in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981"). Thráin founded the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) at [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"), and was a descendant of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a> via his grandfather [Durin VI](/wiki/Durin_VI).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Thrain_II_Discovers_the_Lonely_Mountain.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/9/91/Ted_Nasmith_-_Thrain_II_Discovers_the_Lonely_Mountain.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Thrain_II_Discovers_the_Lonely_Mountain.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="186" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/9/91/Ted_Nasmith_-_Thrain_II_Discovers_the_Lonely_Mountain.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Thrain_II_Discovers_the_Lonely_Mountain.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/9/91/Ted_Nasmith_-_Thrain_II_Discovers_the_Lonely_Mountain.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Thrain_II_Discovers_the_Lonely_Mountain.jpg 2x" data-file-width="960" data-file-height="595"/></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a> - <i>Thrain Discovers the Lonely Mountain</i></figcaption></figure>

Thráin's grandfather, Durin VI, was [King of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/King_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm) when the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) appeared in [Moria](/wiki/Moria); this Balrog has forever been known as [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane) as Durin was killed by it in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1980](/wiki/Third_Age_1980 "Third Age 1980"). Durin's son - and Thráin's father - [Náin I](/wiki/N%C3%A1in_I) was then King until he was himself killed in [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981") and the Dwarves left Moria.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup>

Thráin - at the tender age of 47 - became King of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk). He led his wandering people until [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1999](/wiki/Third_Age_1999 "Third Age 1999") when he founded the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) in [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"),<sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup> whilst some settled instead in the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains). In Erebor the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) began building a mighty kingdom, discovering the prized possession of King Thráin: the [Arkenstone](/wiki/Arkenstone), the Heart of the Mountain.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>

Thráin died in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2190](/wiki/Third_Age_2190 "Third Age 2190") at the age of 256 and was succeeded by his son [Thorin I](/wiki/Thorin_I). With a rule of 209 years, Thráin I was the longest-ruling of all the known [Kings of Durin's Folk](/wiki/Kings_of_Durin%27s_Folk) with, perhaps, the only exception being <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Þráinn* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. It means "Stubborn".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

It is not clear whether Thráin I existed in Tolkien's mind while he was writing *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* or whether he was invented later, when writing the back-story of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. The phrase on [Thrór's Map](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r%27s_Map) "*Here of old was Thrain - [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain)*"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> causes some fans to claim that Tolkien wrote it for Thráin I, since [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in "Thráin") was never a King under the Mountain.

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), [John Rateliff](/wiki/John_Rateliff) and [Douglas Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_Anderson) say that Tolkien accidentally reversed the family tree Thrór --&gt; Thráin --&gt; Thorin to Thráin --&gt; Thrór --&gt; Thorin and then reverted it before final publication of the book. Rateliff argues in his book that the original tree was favoured but that it is impossible to say which was intended to be the correct genealogy. According to Rateliff, the first edition uses the name "Thráin" in two distinct contexts that support either point of view and that Tolkien only added a third element to the story (the forenote) in 1947 when he sent suggested changes to the publisher.
