**Third Age 2939** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2939**) is the 2939th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2939 was also known as **S.R. 1339** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) discovers that [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s servants are searching the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) near [Gladden Fields](../G/Gladden_Fields.md), and that Sauron therefore has learned of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md)'s end. He is alarmed, but says nothing to the [White Council](../W/White_Council.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Thain](Thain.md) [Isumbras Took IV](../I/Isumbras_Took_IV.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Took_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Fortinbras Took II](../F/Fortinbras_Took_II.md), son of Isumbras, becomes the twenty-ninth Thain of [the Shire](The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Took_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-2">[2]</a></sup>
