**Third Age 2795** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2795**) is the 2795th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2795 was also known as **S.R. 1195** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Basso Boffin](../B/Basso_Boffin.md) leaves [the Shire](The_Shire.md) and supposedly goes to sea.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
