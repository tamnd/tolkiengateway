**Third Age 2827** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2827**) is the 2827th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2827 was also known as **S.R. 1227** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Aglahad](../A/Aglahad.md), nineteenth [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
