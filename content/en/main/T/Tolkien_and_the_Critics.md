<i><b>Tolkien and the Critics: Essays on J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s The Lord of the Rings</b></i> is a collection of essays related to J.R.R. Tolkien and his works. Several of the essays were reprinted in the editors' 2004 collection *[Understanding The Lord of the Rings](../U/Understanding_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.

The cover illustration was made by Rainey Bennett.
