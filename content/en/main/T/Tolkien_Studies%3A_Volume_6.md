<b><i>Tolkien Studies</i>: Volume 6</b>, published in [2009](../_/2009.md), is the sixth volume of the annual review *[Tolkien Studies](<Tolkien_Studies_(journal).md>)*.

This volume published for the first time J.R.R. Tolkien's essay "[Fate and Free Will](../F/Fate_and_Free_Will.md)" (edited by [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md)), it was reprinted in *[The Nature of Middle-earth](The_Nature_of_Middle-earth.md)* in [2021](../_/2021.md). <br>[Stuart D. Lee](../S/Stuart_D._Lee.md)'s article "J.R.R. Tolkien and *The Wanderer*: From Edition to Application" includes formerly unpublished extracts from Tolkien's teaching notes on <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wanderer_(Old_English_poem)" title="wikipedia:The Wanderer (Old English poem)">The Wanderer</a></i>, held at the [Bodleian Library](../B/Bodleian_Library.md).
