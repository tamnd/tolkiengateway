<b><i>Tolkien Studies</i>: Volume 6</b>, published in [2009](/wiki/2009), is the sixth volume of the annual review *[Tolkien Studies](</wiki/Tolkien_Studies_(journal)> "Tolkien Studies (journal)")*.

This volume published for the first time J.R.R. Tolkien's essay "[Fate and Free Will](/wiki/Fate_and_Free_Will)" (edited by [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter)), it was reprinted in *[The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth)* in [2021](/wiki/2021). <br>[Stuart D. Lee](/wiki/Stuart_D._Lee)'s article "J.R.R. Tolkien and *The Wanderer*: From Edition to Application" includes formerly unpublished extracts from Tolkien's teaching notes on <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wanderer_(Old_English_poem)" title="wikipedia:The Wanderer (Old English poem)">The Wanderer</a></i>, held at the [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library).
