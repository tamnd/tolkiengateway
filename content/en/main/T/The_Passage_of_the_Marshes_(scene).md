**The Passage of the Marshes** is the twelfth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* and the fourteenth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition).md>). This scene was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) leads [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) out of [Emyn Muil](../E/Emyn_Muil.md) and into the [Dead Marshes](../D/Dead_Marshes.md), which stretch far towards [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). He tells them that he knows safe paths through the marshes and that [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) do not, and so go around instead.

As they rest and Frodo and Sam eat [*lembas*](../L/Lembas.md), Gollum complains that he is *"famished"*. Taking pity on him, Frodo gives him some of the *lembas*, but he is unable to eat it and complains he must starve. Sam bids him "*starve then, and good riddance*". In response Gollum calls him cruel and says he does not care, but then turns to Frodo, and says "*Master cares. Master knows ... Once it takes hold of us, it never lets go.*" As Gollum reaches out to touch Frodo, Frodo bats his hand away, telling him not to touch him.

After their stop, Gollum continues leading them through the Marshes and tells them to be careful. Sam notices dead bodies in the water.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>All dead. All rotten. Elves and Men and Orcses. <a href="../B/Battle_of_Dagorlad.md" title="Battle of Dagorlad">A great battle</a> long ago.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../G/Gollum.md" title="Gollum">Gollum</a></cite></p>
</blockquote>

Frodo stops and is drawn to one of the dead [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and falls into the water. Under water, he sees hands trying to grab him and a screaming face getting closer to him, before Gollum pulls him out of the water and back onto land.

At night, Frodo lies stroking [the Ring](The_One_Ring.md) and hears Gollum speaking of the beauty of his precious. Frodo quickly puts the Ring away and asks Gollum who he is. The conversation between the two moves backwards and forwards between them before Frodo says to him "*You were not so very different from a hobbit once, were you Sméagol?*" After a moment of shock, Gollum smiles as he remembers his name.

The shriek of a [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) forces the three to hide. Gollum is terrified at the wraith flying atop a [fell beast](../F/Fell_beast.md), but the pain of Frodo's wound immobilises him. Sam helps him hide and says he thought they were dead,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> but Gollum tells him they cannot be killed and that they are calling for the Ring. Frodo's hand reaches for the Ring, but Sam stops it. After a short time, the Nazgûl flies away. Gollum tells them the [Black Gate](../B/Black_Gate.md) is now "*very close*".
