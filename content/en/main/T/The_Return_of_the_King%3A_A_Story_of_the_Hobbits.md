**The Return of the King: A Story of the Hobbits** is the soundtrack of [The Return of the King (1980 film)](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)>), released in [1980](/wiki/1980) by [Disneyland](/wiki/Disney "Disney") / Vista. Originally produced on vinyl, a subsequent compact disc (CD) version was released.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
