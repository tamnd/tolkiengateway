**Third Age 1409** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1409**) is the 1409th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Angmar conflict](../A/Angmar_conflict.md): The [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) invades [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) with a great host. [Fornost Erain](../F/Fornost_Erain.md) and [Tyrn Gorthad](Tyrn_Gorthad.md) are defended.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The [Tower](Tower_of_Amon_Sûl.md) of [Amon Sûl](../W/Weathertop.md) destroyed, but its *[palantír](../P/Palantíri.md)* was saved and moved to [Fornost](../F/Fornost_Erain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md) became a fief of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md), occupied by evil [Men](../M/Men.md); [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md) was ravaged with some fleeing to the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) were defeated and [King](../K/Kings_of_Arthedain.md) [Arveleg I](../A/Arveleg_I.md) of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md) slain.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppA_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Arveleg I](../A/Arveleg_I.md)'s son, [Araphor](../A/Araphor.md), becomes the ninth [King](../K/Kings_of_Arthedain.md) of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppNorh_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppNorh-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Fornost](../F/Fornost_Erain.md) and the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md) are defended by Araphor with help from [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The [Elves of Lindon](../E/Elves_of_Lindon.md) and [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md), assisted by the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) who came from beyond the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), subdue [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The [last prince of Cardolan](../L/Last_prince_of_Cardolan.md) is killed and buried in a [barrow](../B/Barrows.md) of Tyrn Gorthad.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
