**The Caverns of Isengard** is the sixteenth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* and the twentieth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition).md>).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

Gandalf sits atop the spire of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md). He grabs a moth that is fluttering by and imparts a message to it before sending it away. Below, the ring of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) has been transformed into a foundry and breeding ground for new [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and the large scale production of equipment. Saruman watches on as new [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) are bred, including a formidible Uruk named [Lurtz](../L/Lurtz.md).

## Differences {#Differences}

- Lurtz is an original character to this adaptation.
- Gandalf does not send a message to the outer world at Orthanc.
- The entirety of this scene is fabricated for the movie in order to depict Saruman's rise as a power, though it largely adheres to how Tolkien describes Isengard.
