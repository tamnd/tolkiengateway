<i><b>The Transactions of the Honourable Society of Cymmrodorion: Session 1923-24</b></i> is an issue of the Welsh annual journal *Transactions of the Honourable Society of Cymmrodorion*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The journal includes W. Rhys Roberts's essay "[Gerald of Wales on the Survival of Welsh](../G/Gerald_of_Wales_on_the_Survival_of_Welsh.md)", to which [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) contributed. (See pages 58–59.)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
