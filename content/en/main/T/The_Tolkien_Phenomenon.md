**The Tolkien Phenomenon** was a conference in honour of the 100th anniversary of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). The papers from the conference were published in *[Scholarship and Fantasy: Proceedings of the Tolkien Phenomenon, Turku, May 1992](/wiki/Scholarship_and_Fantasy "Scholarship and Fantasy")*.
