**Third Age 1974** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1974**) is the 1974th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1974 was also known as **S.R. 374** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Winter {#Winter}

- [Angmar conflict](../A/Angmar_conflict.md): The [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) overruns [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md) and takes [Fornost](../F/Fornost.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-eriador_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-2">[2]</a></sup>
- End of the [North-kingdom](../A/Arnor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-eriador_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md) protects the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md) then flees to the Dwarf-mines of the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) and then north to the [Icebay of Forochel](../I/Icebay_of_Forochel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-2">[2]</a></sup>
