The **Tower of Avallónë** was raised to overlook the haven of [Avallónë](../A/Avallónë.md) on the island of [Tol Eressëa](Tol_Eressëa.md). There the *[Master Palantír](../M/Master-stone.md)* was kept. It is said that when a mariner sailed to [Aman](../A/Aman.md) he saw the tower first of all on the horizon.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Elostirion-stone](../E/Elostirion-stone.md), one of the *[palantíri](../P/Palantíri.md)* erected in the tallest of the towers of [Emyn Beraid](../E/Emyn_Beraid.md), could show the tower when used by King [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md). This would comfort him when he longed for the West.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
