The **Tower of Avallónë** was raised to overlook the haven of [Avallónë](/wiki/Avall%C3%B3n%C3%AB) on the island of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa). There the *[Master Palantír](/wiki/Master-stone "Master-stone")* was kept. It is said that when a mariner sailed to [Aman](/wiki/Aman) he saw the tower first of all on the horizon.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Elostirion-stone](/wiki/Elostirion-stone), one of the *[palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* erected in the tallest of the towers of [Emyn Beraid](/wiki/Emyn_Beraid), could show the tower when used by King [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil). This would comfort him when he longed for the West.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
