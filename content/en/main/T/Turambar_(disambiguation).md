<i><b>Turambar</b></i> is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Master of Doom", from *[tur](/wiki/Tur "Tur")* ("master, lord") + *[ambar](/wiki/Ambar "Ambar")* ("doom") and can refer to:

- **[Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin") ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [464](/wiki/First_Age_464 "First Age 464") - [499](/wiki/First_Age_499 "First Age 499")), the name taken by the [son](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin") of [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) when he dwelt among the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) of the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Forest_of_Brethil).**
- [Turambar](</wiki/Turambar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Turambar (King of Gondor)") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [397](/wiki/Third_Age_397 "Third Age 397") - [667](/wiki/Third_Age_667 "Third Age 667")), the ninth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) who expanded Gondor's territories into [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn).

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Turamarth" title="Turamarth">turamarth</a></b></i>, the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) equivalent of *Turambar*.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In the *[Qenya Lexicon](/wiki/Qenya_Lexicon)*, Tolkien equated it with the Norse name *Sigurðr*.
