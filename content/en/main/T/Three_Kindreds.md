The **Three Kindreds** were the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar), the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), and the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri); the three hosts of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who followed the summons of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and began the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey) to [Aman](/wiki/Aman).
