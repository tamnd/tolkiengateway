**The Name Coventry** is the published title of a [letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to the Editor of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Catholic_Herald" title="wikipedia:The Catholic Herald">The Catholic Herald</a></i>, written on [10](/wiki/10_February "10 February")-[11 February](/wiki/11_February) [1945](/wiki/1945).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien discusses the derivations of "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/convent" title="wiktionary:convent">convent</a>" and "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Coventry" title="wiktionary:Coventry">Coventry</a>", and English place-names in general.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TB1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB1-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** The letter was published in [*The Catholic Herald* (23 February 1945)](</wiki/The_Catholic_Herald_(23_February_1945)> "The Catholic Herald (23 February 1945)").<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TB1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB1-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Extracts from the letter also appeared in [*The Catholic Herald* (18 September 1981)](</wiki/The_Catholic_Herald_(18_September_1981)> "The Catholic Herald (18 September 1981)").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
