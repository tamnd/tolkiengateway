**Titan** is <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn" title="wikipedia:Saturn">Saturn</a>'s largest moon.

## Naming conventions {#Naming_conventions}

Titan's mountains (called *montes*)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> are named after mountains in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) while *colles*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 2]</a></sup> are named after characters created by [Tolkien](Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[1]</a></sup>
