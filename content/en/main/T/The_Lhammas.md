**The Lhammas** is the fifth chapter of the second section, "Part Two: [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) and [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) before *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)"*, of *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The *Lhammas* comprises a linguistic account attributed to [Pengolod](/wiki/Pengolod) of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), which he authored on [Tol Eressea](/wiki/Tol_Eressea), based on some earlier work of [Rúmil of Tun](</wiki/R%C3%BAmil_(elf_of_Tirion)> "Rúmil (elf of Tirion)"). A note suggests that it was one of the sources of [Aelfwine](/wiki/Aelfwine), who had seen the work. *Lhammas* is actually composed of two versions of the same revised text. The later and third form of the text, having the title *Lammasethen*, is shorter and deviates from the *Lhammas* at some points.

The essay represents an old linguistic view of the [Languages](/wiki/Languages) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) (which was later dropped) in which all languages belonged to either the [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin), [Oromëan](/wiki/Orom%C3%ABan), [Aulëan](/wiki/Aul%C3%ABan), or [Melkian](/wiki/Melkian) phylums:

- The *Valarin* phylum is the origin of all other phylums and led to [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) (the language of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar).)
- *[Oromëan](/wiki/Orom%C3%ABan)* is named after the Vala [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) and is used for all languages of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), because Oromë taught the Elves language. Most languages of [Men](/wiki/Men) also belong to this phylum.
- *[Aulëan](/wiki/Aul%C3%ABan)* is named after the Vala [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB), father of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), and is the origin of the [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) language. It has had some influences on the tongues of Men.
- *[Melkian](/wiki/Melkian)* is named after the fallen Vala [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") (or Morgoth), and is the origin of the [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech) of the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and other evil beings.

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") later revised and simplified the linguistic history of his world, and some elements cannot be applied to the later languages of Middle-earth. However, Tolkien in later writings still held that [Rúmil](</wiki/R%C3%BAmil_(elf_of_Tirion)> "Rúmil (elf of Tirion)") wrote <i><b>L</b>ammas</i>, but himself never wrote an "updated" form that would be coherent with his later mythology.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *lhammas* is [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) and roughly means "assortment of languages" (cf. [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word *[lambë](/wiki/Lamb%C3%AB "Lambë")*). When Tolkien revised the history of the languages and Noldorin became [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), he renamed the word as *lammas* (initial *lh* changed to *l*. See also: [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)).
