**The Lhammas** is the fifth chapter of the second section, "Part Two: [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) and [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) before *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)"*, of *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The *Lhammas* comprises a linguistic account attributed to [Pengolod](../P/Pengolod.md) of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), which he authored on [Tol Eressea](Tol_Eressea.md), based on some earlier work of [Rúmil of Tun](<../R/Rúmil_(elf_of_Tirion).md>). A note suggests that it was one of the sources of [Aelfwine](../A/Aelfwine.md), who had seen the work. *Lhammas* is actually composed of two versions of the same revised text. The later and third form of the text, having the title *Lammasethen*, is shorter and deviates from the *Lhammas* at some points.

The essay represents an old linguistic view of the [Languages](../L/Languages.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) (which was later dropped) in which all languages belonged to either the [Valarin](../V/Valarin.md), [Oromëan](../O/Oromëan.md), [Aulëan](../A/Aulëan.md), or [Melkian](../M/Melkian.md) phylums:

- The *Valarin* phylum is the origin of all other phylums and led to [Valarin](../V/Valarin.md) (the language of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md).)
- *[Oromëan](../O/Oromëan.md)* is named after the Vala [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) and is used for all languages of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md), because Oromë taught the Elves language. Most languages of [Men](../M/Men.md) also belong to this phylum.
- *[Aulëan](../A/Aulëan.md)* is named after the Vala [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md), father of the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), and is the origin of the [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md) language. It has had some influences on the tongues of Men.
- *[Melkian](../M/Melkian.md)* is named after the fallen Vala [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) (or Morgoth), and is the origin of the [Black Speech](../B/Black_Speech.md) of the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and other evil beings.

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) later revised and simplified the linguistic history of his world, and some elements cannot be applied to the later languages of Middle-earth. However, Tolkien in later writings still held that [Rúmil](<../R/Rúmil_(elf_of_Tirion).md>) wrote <i><b>L</b>ammas</i>, but himself never wrote an "updated" form that would be coherent with his later mythology.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *lhammas* is [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) and roughly means "assortment of languages" (cf. [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word *[lambë](../L/Lambë.md)*). When Tolkien revised the history of the languages and Noldorin became [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), he renamed the word as *lammas* (initial *lh* changed to *l*. See also: [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md)).
