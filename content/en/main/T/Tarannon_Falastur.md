**Tarannon Falastur** ([T.A.](Third_Age.md) [654](Third_Age_654.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> – [913](Third_Age_913.md),<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 259 years) was the twelfth [King of Gondor](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>.

## History {#History}

Tarannon was [Captain of the Hosts](../C/Captain_of_the_Hosts.md) and the first of the [Ship-kings](../S/Ship-kings.md) of Gondor, who extended the realm along the shores west and south of the [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md). Commemorating his victories, Tarannon assumed the name "Falastur" when he took the crown.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-4">[4]</a></sup>

Tarannon was also famous for his Queen, the "nefarious, solitary, and loveless" [Berúthiel](../B/Berúthiel.md). Tarannon had built a house downstream of [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) that rested upon arches sunk into the waters of the [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md), but the queen refused to live within the sound and smell of the sea. She lived in the King's House in [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) where she owned cats said to be able to spy upon men. Eventually Tarannon placed her upon a boat cast adrift at sea.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> He was then the first king to die without an heir, and was succeeded by his nephew, the second of the Ship-kings, [Eärnil I](../E/Eärnil_I.md).<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tarannon* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). Its meaning is not glossed, but Helge Fauskanger suggests that it means "High-gift" or "Lord or the Gate" and is a compound of *[tar](Tar.md)* ("high", "king") and a masculinized form of *[anna](../A/Anna.md)* ("gift") or a Sindarin-influenced form of [annon](../A/Annon.md) ("great gate").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The epithet *Falastur* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). It means "Lord of the Coasts".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-4">[4]</a></sup> As Paul Strack explains, it is a compound of *falassë* ("coast") and *-tur* ("lord").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
