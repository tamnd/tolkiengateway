**Tarannon Falastur** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [654](/wiki/Third_Age_654 "Third Age 654")<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> – [913](/wiki/Third_Age_913 "Third Age 913"),<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 259 years) was the twelfth [King of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>.

## History {#History}

Tarannon was [Captain of the Hosts](/wiki/Captain_of_the_Hosts) and the first of the [Ship-kings](/wiki/Ship-kings) of Gondor, who extended the realm along the shores west and south of the [Ethir Anduin](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin). Commemorating his victories, Tarannon assumed the name "Falastur" when he took the crown.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-4">[4]</a></sup>

Tarannon was also famous for his Queen, the "nefarious, solitary, and loveless" [Berúthiel](/wiki/Ber%C3%BAthiel). Tarannon had built a house downstream of [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) that rested upon arches sunk into the waters of the [Ethir Anduin](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin), but the queen refused to live within the sound and smell of the sea. She lived in the King's House in [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) where she owned cats said to be able to spy upon men. Eventually Tarannon placed her upon a boat cast adrift at sea.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> He was then the first king to die without an heir, and was succeeded by his nephew, the second of the Ship-kings, [Eärnil I](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_I).<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tarannon* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). Its meaning is not glossed, but Helge Fauskanger suggests that it means "High-gift" or "Lord or the Gate" and is a compound of *[tar](/wiki/Tar "Tar")* ("high", "king") and a masculinized form of *[anna](/wiki/Anna "Anna")* ("gift") or a Sindarin-influenced form of [annon](/wiki/Annon "Annon") ("great gate").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The epithet *Falastur* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). It means "Lord of the Coasts".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-4">[4]</a></sup> As Paul Strack explains, it is a compound of *falassë* ("coast") and *-tur* ("lord").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}
