**Taras-ness** was the headland of [Mount Taras](/wiki/Mount_Taras), on the shores of the land of [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast), where [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) had his original citadel of [Vinyamar](/wiki/Vinyamar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In place names, the suffix -ness comes from the Old English *næss* (or *ness*) and means "cape", "promontory", or "land jutting out into the sea"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>.
