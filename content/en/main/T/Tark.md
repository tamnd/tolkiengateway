**Tark** is an [Orkish](/wiki/Orkish) word that was often used by the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of the later [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) to describe a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") of [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") heritage (such as the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians)). Considering the enmity between the Orcs and the Men of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), the word *tark* tended to be used in an insulting way.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

There exists at least two, only slightly different, etymological explanations of *tark*:

1. a corrupted version of *[tarkil](/wiki/Tarkil "Tarkil")*, a [Westron](/wiki/Westron) borrowing from [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) that literally meant "[High Man](/wiki/High_Man)".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
2. unknown etymology, but "possibly a mere abbreviation of *[tarkil](/wiki/Tarkil "Tarkil")*".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Whenever a keep is flipped in favor of the Servants of the Eye, an orc calls out &#34;look at the feeble târks run!&#34;</dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Tharzog mentions Tarks in Fornost.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor" title="Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor">Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Orcs and Orc Captains refer to Talion, a ranger of the Black Gates as &#34;the Tark&#34;.</dd></dl>

<b>2017: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_War" title="Middle-earth: Shadow of War">Middle-earth: Shadow of War</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>As in the previous installment, Orcs and Orc Captains continue to refer to Talion as a &#34;Tark&#34;.</dd></dl>
