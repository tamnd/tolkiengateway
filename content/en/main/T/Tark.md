**Tark** is an [Orkish](../O/Orkish.md) word that was often used by the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) of the later [Third Age](Third_Age.md) to describe a [Man](../M/Men.md) of [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) heritage (such as the [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md)). Considering the enmity between the Orcs and the Men of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), the word *tark* tended to be used in an insulting way.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

There exists at least two, only slightly different, etymological explanations of *tark*:

1. a corrupted version of *[tarkil](Tarkil.md)*, a [Westron](../W/Westron.md) borrowing from [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) that literally meant "[High Man](../H/High_Man.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
2. unknown etymology, but "possibly a mere abbreviation of *[tarkil](Tarkil.md)*".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Whenever a keep is flipped in favor of the Servants of the Eye, an orc calls out &#34;look at the feeble târks run!&#34;</dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Tharzog mentions Tarks in Fornost.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth%253A_Shadow_of_Mordor.md" title="Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor">Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Orcs and Orc Captains refer to Talion, a ranger of the Black Gates as &#34;the Tark&#34;.</dd></dl>

<b>2017: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth%253A_Shadow_of_War.md" title="Middle-earth: Shadow of War">Middle-earth: Shadow of War</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>As in the previous installment, Orcs and Orc Captains continue to refer to Talion as a &#34;Tark&#34;.</dd></dl>
