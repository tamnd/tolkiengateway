<i><b>The Hobbit Facsimile Gift Edition</b></i> was published by HarperCollins and released in the UK on [31 May](/wiki/31_May) [2018](/wiki/2018). This set included a facsimile-reproduction of the first edition of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, a CD of archive recordings of J.R.R. Tolkien reading from *The Hobbit*, a copy of [Thror's map](/wiki/Thror%27s_map), and a booklet with a brief history of *The Hobbit* by [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) &amp; [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond).

## From the Publisher {#From_the_Publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This sumptuous gift set includes a replica of the very rare <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_1st_edition" title="The Hobbit 1st edition">first edition</a> of The Hobbit, the only edition where one can now read the original version of the story before Tolkien re-edited it to become the one enjoyed by readers since <a href="/wiki/1951" title="1951">1951</a>.
</p><p>The Hobbit was published on <a href="/wiki/21_September" title="21 September">21 September</a> <a href="/wiki/1937" title="1937">1937</a>, with a print run of 1,500 copies. With a beautiful cover design, nearly a dozen black &amp; white illustrations and two black &amp; red maps by the author himself, the book proved to be popular and was reprinted shortly afterwards. History was already being made.
</p><p>The scarcity of the first edition has resulted in copies commanding huge prices, way beyond the reach of most Tolkien <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Tolkien_fandom" title="Tolkien fandom">fans</a>. In addition, subsequent changes to the text – particularly those to <a href="/wiki/Riddles_in_the_Dark" title="Riddles in the Dark">chapter 5</a>, when Tolkien decided in <a href="/wiki/1947" title="1947">1947</a> to revise the text to bring it better into accord with events as they were developing in its ‘sequel’, <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a> – mean that the opportunity to read the book in its original form and format has become quite difficult.
</p><p>This special commemorative gift set includes the first edition, so that readers of all ages – not just ‘children between the ages of 5 and 9’, as <a href="/wiki/Rayner_Unwin" title="Rayner Unwin">Rayner Unwin</a> famously declared in his report on the original submission – can finally enjoy Tolkien’s story as it originally appeared. It also includes an exclusive CD of archive recordings that capture Tolkien reading from The Hobbit, a special copy of Thror’s map which reveals the secret moon-runes when held to the light, and an accompanying booklet that relates the history of The Hobbit, and includes connected writings by JRR Tolkien.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>HarperCollins</cite></p>
</blockquote>
