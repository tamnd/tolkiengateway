**The Problem of Ros** is the twelfth chapter of *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)*.

The chapter is dedicated to a [1968](/wiki/1968) [linguistic](/wiki/Languages "Languages") essay by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), concerning the word [ros](/wiki/Ros "Ros"), as seen in the name *[Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros)*.

While writing it, Tolkien realised that what he is explaining had already a solution in the [Appendices](/wiki/Appendices), and rejected "most of it".
