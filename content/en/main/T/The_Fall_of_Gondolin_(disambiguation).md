**The Fall of Gondolin** refers to more than one concept:

- <i><b><a href="/wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin" title="The Fall of Gondolin">The Fall of Gondolin</a></b></i>, the [2018](/wiki/2018) publication covering the entire story and its history, including the 1984 chapter.
- [Of Tuor and the Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Of_Tuor_and_the_Fall_of_Gondolin), the twenty-third chapter in the [1977](/wiki/1977) book *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.
- [The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)>), the chapter in the [1984](/wiki/1984) book *[The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two)* covering the very first version of the story.
- [The Lay of the Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Fall_of_Gondolin), an unfinished poem included in *[The Lays of Beleriand](/wiki/The_Lays_of_Beleriand)*.

- The **[Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin)**, the actual event itself about the downfall of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) and the escape of [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and [Idril](/wiki/Idril).
