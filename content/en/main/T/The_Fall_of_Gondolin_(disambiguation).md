**The Fall of Gondolin** refers to more than one concept:

- <i><b><a href="The_Fall_of_Gondolin.md" title="The Fall of Gondolin">The Fall of Gondolin</a></b></i>, the [2018](../_/2018.md) publication covering the entire story and its history, including the 1984 chapter.
- [Of Tuor and the Fall of Gondolin](../O/Of_Tuor_and_the_Fall_of_Gondolin.md), the twenty-third chapter in the [1977](../_/1977.md) book *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)*.
- [The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)](<The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md>), the chapter in the [1984](../_/1984.md) book *[The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two.md)* covering the very first version of the story.
- [The Lay of the Fall of Gondolin](The_Lay_of_the_Fall_of_Gondolin.md), an unfinished poem included in *[The Lays of Beleriand](The_Lays_of_Beleriand.md)*.

- The **[Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md)**, the actual event itself about the downfall of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) and the escape of [Tuor](Tuor.md) and [Idril](../I/Idril.md).
