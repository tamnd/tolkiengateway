**Ted Nasmith** is a Canadian artist, illustrator, and musician most known for illustrating many works by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

## Early life {#Early_life}

In his childhood and teens, Nasmith was introduced to *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* by his sister and was absorbed. In [1972](/wiki/1972) he mailed to Tolkien photographs of his work. Tolkien replied with praise, but also with criticism, saying that his rendition of Bilbo in the painting <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tednasmith.com/tolkien/the-unexpected-party/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Unexpected Party</a></i> was "too childlike".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<h2><span id="Peter_Jackson.27s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Peter_Jackson&#39;s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings">Peter Jackson&#39;s The Lord of the Rings</span></h2>

Many fans of Ted Nasmith have expressed disappointment in his lack of involvement in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").

However, I was going through a personal crisis unrelated to my art, and in the end, being that it would also force me to abandon my freelance obligations and be away indefinitely, I reluctantly declined, settling the question in my mind after very careful deliberation. It was a difficult decision.
</p>
