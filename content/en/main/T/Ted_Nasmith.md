**Ted Nasmith** is a Canadian artist, illustrator, and musician most known for illustrating many works by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

## Early life {#Early_life}

In his childhood and teens, Nasmith was introduced to *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* by his sister and was absorbed. In [1972](../_/1972.md) he mailed to Tolkien photographs of his work. Tolkien replied with praise, but also with criticism, saying that his rendition of Bilbo in the painting <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tednasmith.com/tolkien/the-unexpected-party/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Unexpected Party</a></i> was "too childlike".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<h2><span id="Peter_Jackson.27s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Peter_Jackson&#39;s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings">Peter Jackson&#39;s The Lord of the Rings</span></h2>

Many fans of Ted Nasmith have expressed disappointment in his lack of involvement in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>).

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I was contacted by a producer in early &#39;99. They invited me to be there with the others in New Zealand to help with conceptual art, and made me a nice offer. However, I was going through a personal crisis unrelated to my art, and in the end, being that it would also force me to abandon my freelance obligations and be away indefinitely, I reluctantly declined, settling the question in my mind after very careful deliberation. It was a difficult decision.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Ted Nasmith <sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

One fan has created a website known as <a class="external text" href="http://tednasmith.narod.ru" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ted Nasmith: The Uncredited Lord of the Rings Films Concept Artist</a>. The website displays side-by-side illustrations by Nasmith with later scenes in The Lord of the Rings films. [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) was inspired by Nasmith - as [Alan Lee](../A/Alan_Lee.md) described:

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>&#34;[T]here were certain pictures of Ted&#39;s that Peter quite liked. He actually bought one of Nasmith&#39;s originals --- a very striking picture of Mordor&#34;
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Alan Lee <sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>
