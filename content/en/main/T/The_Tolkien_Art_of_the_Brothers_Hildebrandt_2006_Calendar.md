**The Tolkien art of the Brothers Hildebrandt 2006 Calendar** was published by Ronnie Sellers and is the fourth of a series of four calendars that features original Tolkien artwork by the [Brothers Hildebrandt](../B/Brothers_Hildebrandt.md). The sketches and photos featured were created from posing sessions during the 1970's.

<a class="internal mw-magiclink-isbn" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Special:BookSources/1416210717">ISBN 1-4162-1071-7</a>

- Dimensions: 12 x 12 inches
- Stapled
- Punched hanging hole
- Shrink-wrapped
