<i><b>Beasts of Tolkien: A Colouring Book</b></i> is an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coloring_book#Adult_coloring_books" title="wikipedia:Coloring book">adult colouring book</a>, illustrated by Mauro Mazzara and Andrea Piparo. The illustrations are based on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s writings.<sup id="cite_ref-AMUK_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AMUK-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This mystical colouring book presents an abundance of famous scenes and characters inspired by Tolkien&#39;s <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a>. From <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> the Grey to the humble <a href="/wiki/Hobbits" title="Hobbits">Hobbits</a>, take time off from the busy pace of life and indulge your imagination while exploring the enthralling world of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> and other tales. There&#39;s something new on every page as you discover the intricate elvish designs, fearsome characters and breath-taking landscapes - perfect to satisfy <a href="/wiki/Fandom" title="Fandom">Tolkien fans</a> and beyond. This book is unofficial and not authorised by the <a href="/wiki/Tolkien_Estate" title="Tolkien Estate">Tolkien Estate</a> or <a href="/wiki/HarperCollins" title="HarperCollins">HarperCollins</a> Publishers.<sup id="cite_ref-AMUK_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AMUK-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>
