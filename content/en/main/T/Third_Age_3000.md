**Third Age 3000** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3000**) is the 3000th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 3000 was also known as **S.R. 1400** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- From that time on the [shadow](../S/Shadow.md) of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) lengthens.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> The following years see many [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) from the East traversing [the Shire](The_Shire.md) seeking refuge to the West and the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) bringing news about [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md); and [Elves](../E/Elves.md) traversing the Shire on their way to the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Around this time [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) dares to use the *[palantír](../P/Palantíri.md)* of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md), but becomes ensnared by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), who has the [Ithil-stone](../I/Ithil-stone.md). He becomes a traitor to the [White Council](../W/White_Council.md). His spies report that [the Shire](The_Shire.md) is being closely guarded by the [Rangers](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- One day, [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) sees the [Sackville-Bagginses](../S/Sackville-Bagginses.md) approaching along the road. Using [his Ring](../O/One_Ring.md) he evades an unwanted meeting with them. Unbeknownst to him, the whole scene is witnessed by a young [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>
- In preparation for [his party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md) next year, Bilbo orders special gifts from [Erebor](../E/Erebor.md) and [Dale](../D/Dale.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
- Death of [Wilibald Bolger](../W/Wilibald_Bolger.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
