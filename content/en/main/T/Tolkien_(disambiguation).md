**Tolkien** is an English name of Saxon origin. It can refer to the following people:

- <b><a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> (<a href="../_/1892.md" title="1892">1892</a> - <a href="../_/1973.md" title="1973">1973</a>), author of <i><a href="The_Hobbit.md" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i>, <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> and <i><a href="The_Silmarillion.md" title="The Silmarillion">The Silmarillion</a></i>, among other works.</b>
- [Adam Tolkien](../A/Adam_Tolkien.md) (born [1969](../_/1969.md)), the son of [Christopher](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) and [Baillie Tolkien](../B/Baillie_Tolkien.md), who translated several of J.R.R. Tolkien's texts into French.
- [Arthur Tolkien](../A/Arthur_Tolkien.md) ([1857](../_/1857.md) - [1896](../_/1896.md)), husband of [Mabel Tolkien](../M/Mabel_Suffield.md) and father of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- [Baillie Tolkien](../B/Baillie_Tolkien.md) (born [1941](../_/1941.md)), the second wife of [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), and editor of *[The Father Christmas Letters](The_Father_Christmas_Letters.md)*.
- <b><a href="../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md" title="Christopher Tolkien">Christopher Tolkien</a> (<a href="../_/1924.md" title="1924">1924</a> - <a href="../_/2020.md" title="2020">2020</a>), J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s third son and the editor of his posthumous publications. His editorial contributions include <i><a href="../U/Unfinished_Tales.md" title="Unfinished Tales">Unfinished Tales</a></i>, <i><a href="The_Silmarillion.md" title="The Silmarillion">The Silmarillion</a></i>, and the twelve volumes of <i><a href="The_History_of_Middle-earth.md" title="The History of Middle-earth">The History of Middle-earth</a></i>.</b>
- [Edith Tolkien](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) ([1889](../_/1889.md) - [1971](../_/1971.md)), wife of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- [Hilary Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md) ([1894](../_/1894.md) - [1976](../_/1976.md)), younger brother of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- [John Tolkien](../J/John_Tolkien.md) ([1917](../_/1917.md) - [2003](../_/2003.md)), eldest son of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- [John Benjamin Tolkien](../J/John_Benjamin_Tolkien.md) (1807 - [1896](../_/1896.md)), father of [Arthur Tolkien](../A/Arthur_Tolkien.md) and grandfather of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- [Mabel Tolkien](../M/Mabel_Suffield.md) ([1870](../_/1870.md) - [1904](../_/1904.md)), wife of [Arthur Tolkien](../A/Arthur_Tolkien.md) and mother of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- [Michael Tolkien](../M/Michael_Tolkien.md) ([1920](../_/1920.md) - [1984](../_/1984.md)), J.R.R. Tolkien's second son.
- [Priscilla Tolkien](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md) (born [1929](../_/1929.md)), J.R.R. Tolkien's daughter.
- [Rachel Tolkien](../R/Rachel_Tolkien.md) (born [1971](../_/1971.md)), the daughter of [Christopher](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) and [Baillie Tolkien](../B/Baillie_Tolkien.md).
- [Royd Tolkien](../R/Royd_Tolkien.md) (born [1969](../_/1969.md)), great-grandson of J.R.R. Tolkien, grandson of [Michael Tolkien](../M/Michael_Tolkien.md), and noted publisher.
- [Simon Tolkien](../S/Simon_Tolkien.md) (born [1959](../_/1959.md)), eldest son of [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) and an author in his own right.
- [Tim Tolkien](Tim_Tolkien.md) (born [1962](../_/1962.md)), grandson of [Hilary Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md) and noted sculptor.
