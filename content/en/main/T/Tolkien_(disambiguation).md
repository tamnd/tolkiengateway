**Tolkien** is an English name of Saxon origin. It can refer to the following people:

- <b><a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> (<a href="/wiki/1892" title="1892">1892</a> - <a href="/wiki/1973" title="1973">1973</a>), author of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i>, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/The_Silmarillion" title="The Silmarillion">The Silmarillion</a></i>, among other works.</b>
- [Adam Tolkien](/wiki/Adam_Tolkien) (born [1969](/wiki/1969)), the son of [Christopher](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien "Christopher Tolkien") and [Baillie Tolkien](/wiki/Baillie_Tolkien), who translated several of J.R.R. Tolkien's texts into French.
- [Arthur Tolkien](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien) ([1857](/wiki/1857) - [1896](/wiki/1896)), husband of [Mabel Tolkien](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield "Mabel Suffield") and father of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- [Baillie Tolkien](/wiki/Baillie_Tolkien) (born [1941](/wiki/1941)), the second wife of [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), and editor of *[The Father Christmas Letters](/wiki/The_Father_Christmas_Letters)*.
- <b><a href="/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien" title="Christopher Tolkien">Christopher Tolkien</a> (<a href="/wiki/1924" title="1924">1924</a> - <a href="/wiki/2020" title="2020">2020</a>), J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s third son and the editor of his posthumous publications. His editorial contributions include <i><a href="/wiki/Unfinished_Tales" title="Unfinished Tales">Unfinished Tales</a></i>, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Silmarillion" title="The Silmarillion">The Silmarillion</a></i>, and the twelve volumes of <i><a href="/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth" title="The History of Middle-earth">The History of Middle-earth</a></i>.</b>
- [Edith Tolkien](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien) ([1889](/wiki/1889) - [1971](/wiki/1971)), wife of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- [Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien) ([1894](/wiki/1894) - [1976](/wiki/1976)), younger brother of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- [John Tolkien](/wiki/John_Tolkien) ([1917](/wiki/1917) - [2003](/wiki/2003)), eldest son of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- [John Benjamin Tolkien](/wiki/John_Benjamin_Tolkien) (1807 - [1896](/wiki/1896)), father of [Arthur Tolkien](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien) and grandfather of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- [Mabel Tolkien](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield "Mabel Suffield") ([1870](/wiki/1870) - [1904](/wiki/1904)), wife of [Arthur Tolkien](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien) and mother of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- [Michael Tolkien](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien) ([1920](/wiki/1920) - [1984](/wiki/1984)), J.R.R. Tolkien's second son.
- [Priscilla Tolkien](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien) (born [1929](/wiki/1929)), J.R.R. Tolkien's daughter.
- [Rachel Tolkien](/wiki/Rachel_Tolkien) (born [1971](/wiki/1971)), the daughter of [Christopher](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien "Christopher Tolkien") and [Baillie Tolkien](/wiki/Baillie_Tolkien).
- [Royd Tolkien](/wiki/Royd_Tolkien) (born [1969](/wiki/1969)), great-grandson of J.R.R. Tolkien, grandson of [Michael Tolkien](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien), and noted publisher.
- [Simon Tolkien](/wiki/Simon_Tolkien) (born [1959](/wiki/1959)), eldest son of [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) and an author in his own right.
- [Tim Tolkien](/wiki/Tim_Tolkien) (born [1962](/wiki/1962)), grandson of [Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien) and noted sculptor.
