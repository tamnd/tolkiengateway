Over the years, many visual and verbal **references** have been made to the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). While some of them predated [Peter Jackson's film trilogy](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), most of them are references to the movie (especially in the movie's portrayal of [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md)).

## Books {#Books}

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Song_of_Ice_and_Fire" title="wikipedia:A Song of Ice and Fire">A Song of Ice and Fire</a> {#A_Song_of_Ice_and_Fire}

- Dareon, a recruit of the Night's Watch and a singer, could be a reference to [Daeron](../D/Daeron.md), the [minstrel](../M/Minstrels.md) of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) from *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)*, though the *e* and the *r* are swapped.
- Morghul, a young [dragon](../D/Dragons.md) bound to Jaehaera Targaryen and the word for *death* in High Valyrian, could be a reference to [Morgul](../M/Morgul.md), the word for black arts, [sorcery](../M/Magic.md), and necromancy within the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).
- Leobald Tallhart's youngest son is named Beren, most likely as a light nod to [Beren](../B/Beren.md).

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Strange_%26_Mr_Norrell" title="wikipedia:Jonathan Strange &amp; Mr Norrell">Jonathan Strange &amp; Mr Norrell</a> {#Jonathan_Strange_&_Mr_Norrell}

- In chapter 25 "The education of a magician", Mr. Norrel explains the bad idea of infusing magical power into physical objects in order to preserve it: "*Rings have long been considered peculiarly suitable for this sort of magic by virtue of their small size. A man may keep a ring continually upon his finger for years, without exciting the smallest comment —which would not be the case if he showed the same attachment to a book or a pebble— and yet there is scarcely a magician in history who, having once committed some of his skill and power to [a magic ring](../R/Rings_of_Power.md), did not somehow lose that ring and was put to a world of trouble to get it back again*".

### <a class="external text" href="https://books.google.cl/books/about/Boy_in_a_White_Room.html?id=0DADzwEACAAJ&amp;redir_esc=y" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Boy in a White Room</a> {#Boy_in_a_White_Room}

- The main character's alleged father builds him a digital version of Middle-earth he can explore freely. He notably takes pity on [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) he is about to attack, as he sees them living normal lives in a village of their own.

### <a class="external text" href="https://www.thefirstcatinspace.com/books" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The First Cat in Space Ate Pizza</a> {#The_First_Cat_in_Space_Ate_Pizza}

- The Ship's Computer, while attempting to insert itself into the heroes' party, compares itself to [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md), whom it identifies as "everyone's favorite character from *The Lord of the Rings*!"
- In the second book, The First Cat in Space and the Soup of Doom, the Ship's Computer makes a surprise return appearance which it compares to that of [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md).
- In the third book, The First Cat in Space and the Wrath of the Paperclip, the Ship's Computer mentions having difficulty getting through *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)* despite having analyzed over 35 million stories.

## Movies {#Movies}

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/17_Again_(film)" title="wikipedia:17 Again (film)">17 Again</a> {#17_Again}

- Ned Gold (Thomas Lennon), best friend of the protagonist 17 year-old Mike O'Donnell (Zac Efron), courts Jane Masterson (Melora Hardin), the high school principal who shares a mutual interest in The Lord of the Rings. They even discuss the return of [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) the Grey as Gandalf the White in [The Two Towers](The_Two_Towers.md) and converse in elvish.

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Wedding" title="wikipedia:American Wedding">American Wedding</a> {#American_Wedding}

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bones_and_All" title="wikipedia:Bones and All">Bones and All</a> {#Bones_and_All}

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Date_Movie" title="wikipedia:Date Movie">Date Movie</a> {#Date_Movie}

Ignoring him, Frodo accepts fifty dollars; they then beat up Gandalf and run off to "get some wine coolers... and hookers!". <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Lenk" title="wikipedia:Tom Lenk">Tom Lenk</a> plays Frodo, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Fitzpatrick" title="wikipedia:Thomas Fitzpatrick">Tom Fitzpatrick</a> Gandalf and Scott Bridges is Sam.

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadpool_(film)" title="wikipedia:Deadpool (film)">Deadpool</a> {#Deadpool}

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donnie_Darko" title="wikipedia:Donnie Darko">Donnie Darko</a> {#Donnie_Darko}

She explains: *"This famous linguist once said that of all the phrases in the English language, of all the endless combinations of words in all of history, that Cellar Door is the most beautiful."* In the DVD commentary, director Richard Kelly vaguely attributes this remark to Edgar Allan Poe.

<h3><span id="Futurama:_Bender.27s_Game"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Futurama:_Bender&#39;s_Game"><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Futurama:_Bender%27s_Game" title="wikipedia:Futurama: Bender&#39;s Game">Futurama: Bender&#39;s Game</a></span></h3>

- In this tv-movie, Bender comes up with the world "Cornwood", and the entire plot then shifts to *The Lord of the Rings*.
- The map of Cornwood is actually one of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) mirrored, where [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md) is replaced by the "Geysers of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Gygax" title="wikipedia:Gary Gygax">Gygax</a>".
- A toilet-bowl version of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) is called "Wipe Castle"; it even has a mad leader.
- Main characters Fry and Leela are renamed Frydo and Leegola.
- Professor Farnsworth becomes the Great Wizard Greyfarn.
- Amy Wong is Gynacaladriel, queen of the Nympho's
- The object that needs to be destroyed ("in the Geysers of Gygax from whence it came") is an anti-backwards crystal. It has the shape of a [D&amp;D](../D/Dungeons_and_Dragons.md)-die, and is therefore called "the Die of Power". A heavily Gollum-like Frydo refers to it as "my duodecalicious" (referring to its twelve sides).
- The Die doubles as a *[palantír](../P/Palantír.md)*.
- The character Mom has been renamed to Momon, mirroring *[Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)*.
- The [Argonath](../A/Argonath.md) are one heroic statue and a stack of garden gnomes. A rickety bridge hangs in between them.
- A walking tree, named Treedledum, appears. He ends up as firewood.
- Moria is portrayed as "the Cave of Hopelessness". The gang is attacked by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mork_%26_Mindy" title="wikipedia:Mork &amp; Mindy">Morks</a> there.

<h3><span id="Freud.27s_Last_Session"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Freud&#39;s_Last_Session"><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freud%27s_Last_Session" title="wikipedia:Freud&#39;s Last Session"> Freud&#39;s Last Session</a></span></h3>

- The movie, based on a play of the same name, represents a fictional encounter between Sigmund Freud and [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md). In the play Tolkien is merely mentioned as a friend of Lewis, but in the movie he appears in a flashback scene, played by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Campbell_Moore" title="wikipedia:Stephen Campbell Moore"> Stephen Campbell Moore</a>. The [Inklings](../I/Inklings.md) are also mentioned several times.

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Heartbreak_Kid_(2007_film)" title="wikipedia:The Heartbreak Kid (2007 film)">The Heartbreak Kid</a> {#The_Heartbreak_Kid}

- Eddie refers to a couple of kids as "homophobic hobbits".

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_House_That_Dripped_Blood" title="wikipedia:The House That Dripped Blood">The House That Dripped Blood</a> {#The_House_That_Dripped_Blood}

- During the thunderstorm, John Reid ([Christopher Lee](../C/Christopher_Lee.md)) reads the <a class="external text" href="http://www.tolkienlibrary.com/dmiller/000467.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">1968 one volume paperback</a> of *The Lord of the Rings*. It was purportedly Lee's own copy.

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imaginationland_Episode_I" title="wikipedia:Imaginationland Episode I">Imaginationland: The Movie</a> {#Imaginationland:_The_Movie}

- This South Park three part episode turned movie features several homages to *The Lord of the Rings*.:
- Episode Two, "The Drying Of The Balls", opens with an aerial shot of mountains, followed by the titles in ["Ringbearer" font](../F/Fonts.md), similar to the opening of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*. The music accompanying the scene is based on the film's soundtrack.
- There's an [Ent](../E/Ent.md) among the good characters.
- [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) and the [Balrog](../B/Balrogs.md) are one of the evil imaginations.
- One of the orcs resembles those from the [Rankin/Bass](../R/Rankin%252FBass.md) productions.
- Castle Sunshine is an exact replica of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) as featured in the movie trilogy.
- Gandalf is a member of the Council of Nine, along with Jesus, Aslan, Popeye, Wonder Woman, Luke Skywalker, Zeus, Morpheus (from *The Matrix*) and the Tooth Fairy.
- During the Battle of Castle Sunshine, several visual references are made to Peter Jackson's [Battle of Helm's Deep](../B/Battle_of_Helm's_Deep.md).

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inkheart_(film)" title="wikipedia:Inkheart (film)">Inkheart</a> {#Inkheart}

The last part is a reference to [Sean Astin](../S/Sean_Astin.md), who made his big break in the 1985 movie* The Goonies*.*

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judge_John_Deed" title="wikipedia:Judge John Deed">Judge John Deed</a> {#Judge_John_Deed}

That is impressive for seven. What book you are?" boy said: "Two Towers" and the Judge "Wow! I was eleven before I read the first book".

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiss_Kiss_Bang_Bang" title="wikipedia:Kiss Kiss Bang Bang">Kiss Kiss Bang Bang</a> {#Kiss_Kiss_Bang_Bang}

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lizzie_McGuire_Movie" title="wikipedia:The Lizzie McGuire Movie">The Lizzie McGuire Movie</a> {#The_Lizzie_McGuire_Movie}

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss_Cast_Away_and_the_Island_Girls" title="wikipedia:Miss Cast Away and the Island Girls">Miss Cast Away and the Island Girls</a> {#Miss_Cast_Away_and_the_Island_Girls}

The outside of this fortress looks like [Barad-dur](../B/Barad-dûr.md), the chamber where Gollum was tortured in the first movie of LOTR.

## Television Series {#Television_Series}

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Amazing_World_of_Gumball" title="wikipedia:The Amazing World of Gumball">The Amazing World of Gumball</a> {#The_Amazing_World_of_Gumball}

- The episode "The Helmet", which resolves around a hat with magical abilities, takes several cues from Peter Jackson's movies. Gumball's obsessive behaviour mirrors Gollum's, and Darwin and Anais' destruction of the hat by throwing it in a garbage compactor is based on Sam and Frodo's scene at the [Sammath Naur](../C/Cracks_of_Doom.md).

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Dad!" title="wikipedia:American Dad!">American Dad!</a> {#American_Dad!}

- "Dungeons and Wagons" features a *World of Warcraft*-like game. The "heroes" fight a troll in a cave, which turns to stone after being exposed to sunlight. Will responds that he likes any movie in which "two boys named Merry and Pippin risk their lives for a good piece of jewelry". The client makes various other references to the films - he wants to retire and count his precious money and he wants Jack to be "the Lord of his Ring".

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W.I.T.C.H._(TV_series)" title="wikipedia:W.I.T.C.H. (TV series)">W.I.T.C.H.</a> {#W.I.T.C.H.}

- In "G is for Garbage", the character [Blunk](../S/Steven_Jay_Blum.md) does a Gollum-like "precious" when someone tries to steal a prized piece of garbage from him.

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonderfalls" title="wikipedia:Wonderfalls">Wonderfalls</a> {#Wonderfalls}

- Because Jaye lacks hometown pride, Mahandra calls her "a Hobbit that hates the Shire" in episode 6, "Barrel Bear".

## Video games {#Video_games}

<h3><span id="Assassin.27s_Creed_series"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Assassin&#39;s_Creed_series"><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassin%27s_Creed" title="wikipedia:Assassin&#39;s Creed">Assassin&#39;s Creed series</a></span></h3>

#### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassin%27s_Creed_Origins" title="wikipedia:Assassin&#39;s Creed Origins">Origins</a> {#Origins}

- In the quest "Precious Bonds" the player helps a group of scavengers retrieve a valuable ring that they are obsessed with. Their obsession with the ring mirrors Gollum and the One Ring. A further reference is from the quest title, *precious*, a word Gollum often uses to refer to the Ring.

#### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassin%27s_Creed_Odyssey" title="wikipedia:Assassin&#39;s Creed Odyssey">Odyssey</a> {#Odyssey}

- In the quest "The Ordering of the Kosmos", a Cult Guard greets three Order Representatives saying "You're late". One of the representatives replies "We arrived precisely when we meant to." This is a reference to Frodo and Gandalf's exchange at the beginning of The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring.

#### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassin%27s_Creed_Valhalla" title="wikipedia:Assassin&#39;s Creed Valhalla">Valhalla</a> {#Valhalla}

- In the town of Glowecestre, inside one of the houses is a note and beside it an envelope with a ring on top of it. The note references Bilbo and Gandalf.
    - "One of the little folk asked me to make the door smaller, as he wants to keep unruly houseguests from his house. After the nonsense with the druid last fall. I can see his point."
- In the world event "Hyrrokin's Gift" the player encounters a dwarf named "Frodri" and you help him destroy a cursed ring. To destroy the ring the player and Frodri have to climb a mountain and throw the ring into the lava. The quest is a reference to Frodo's quest to destroy the Ring in *The Lord of the Rings*.

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DC_Universe_Online" title="wikipedia:DC Universe Online">DC Universe Online</a> {#DC_Universe_Online}

- One of the challenge boss Tiger Eye's lines is "Meat is back on the menu," taken from *The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers*.

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hearthstone" title="wikipedia:Hearthstone">Hearthstone</a> {#Hearthstone}

- When you play the card "Prophet Velen" his summon quote is "Not all who wander are lost", a reference to [The Riddle of Strider](The_Riddle_of_Strider.md) from *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*
- The flavour text of the card "Safeguard" reads, "Keep it secret. Keep it safeguarded!". Similar to the line "Keep it secret. Keep it safe" Gandalf says to Frodo in The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring.
- The summon quote of the card "Shieldbearer" is "You shall not pass!", a line Gandalf says to the Balrog in The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring.
- When you play the card "Tichondrius" in Battlegrounds his play sound is similar to "ash nazg gimbatul" from the Ring Verse.
- The flavour text of the card "Eredar Deceptor" reads, "But they were all of them deceived, for another Eredar was made". The line references the prologue of The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring where Galadriel says, "But they were all of them deceived, for another ring was made".
- List of achievements:
    - "The One Ring" - named after Sauron's [ring](The_One_Ring.md)
    - "Return of the King" - named after the third volume of *The Lord of the Rings*.
    - "What About Sixteenth Breakfast?" - a reference to the line Pippin says in The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring, "But what about second breakfast?"
    - "One To Twist Them All" - a reference to "One Ring to rule them all" [inscription](../R/Ring_Verse.md) on the One Ring.

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minecraft" title="wikipedia:Minecraft">Minecraft</a> {#Minecraft}

- One of the languages that the game is playable in is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).
- In "The Garden Awakens" game drop update, the Pale Garden biome is reminiscent of the descriptions of the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md) from [chapter VI](The_Old_Forest.md) in <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Book_One&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Book One (page does not exist)">Book One</a> of *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_IV:_Oblivion" title="wikipedia:The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion">The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion</a> {#The_Elder_Scrolls_IV:_Oblivion}

- The Dark Brotherhood initiation quest is named "A Knife in the Dark", the same as the [chapter](../A/A_Knife_in_the_Dark.md) in *The Fellowship of the Ring*.
- When doing the quest "Unfriendly Competition", the player comes across the book "Macabre Manifest" which contains a list of recently deceased persons in Cyrodil. One of the entries is "Oford Gabings", an anagram of Frodo Baggins and a list of several items that he carried during *The Lord of the Rings*.
    - "Travel Cloak with Silver and Green Leaf Fastener", "Enchanted Shortsword with Inlaid Writing", "Gold Ring with Inscription (Cursed?)" and "Leather Bound Travel Journal"
- Havilstein Hoar-Blood has a pet wolf named "Redmaw", which is the direct translation of [Carcharoth](../C/Carcharoth.md), the wolf in *The Silmarillion*.

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Longest_Journey" title="wikipedia:The Longest Journey">The Longest Journey</a> {#The_Longest_Journey}

- In the world of Arcadia, the lair of the Gribbler creature is described as "a cozy hobbit-hole".
- Still in Arcadia, April can find *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* among the books of Abnaxus' library. She will comment that the classics are common in both worlds.
- The fifth chapter of the game is titled *[There and Back Again](There_and_Back_Again.md)*.

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pandora_Directive" title="wikipedia:The Pandora Directive">The Pandora Directive</a> {#The_Pandora_Directive}

- When Archie Elis asks Tex Murphy his identity, the player can select among three responses, one being described "Hobbit Humor". Selecting it, Tex says "I am Gandalf, Wielder of the [Glamdring](../G/Glamdring.md) and Slayer of the [Balrog](../D/Durin's_Bane.md)."

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_Days_(visual_novel)" title="wikipedia:School Days (visual novel)">School Days</a> {#School_Days}

- [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md) is one of the movies Makoto can choose to have he and Kotonoha go to on their date, which, if chosen, strengthens Makoto's relationship with her. During the movie Kotonoha tells Makoto that it is based off a book written by a 19th century professor.

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_the_Sorcerer" title="wikipedia:Simon the Sorcerer">Simon the Sorcerer</a> {#Simon_the_Sorcerer}

- A creature called Gollum can be found fishing. He will help the player get a [ring of invisibility](../O/One_Ring.md).
- Talking with Gollum, will also refer to stories such as "*[The Short Creature with Furry Feet](The_Hobbit.md)*" and "*[The Master of the Finger Ornaments](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*".

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_a_Killing_Moon" title="wikipedia:Under a Killing Moon">Under a Killing Moon</a> (novelization) {#Under_a_Killing_Moon_(novelization)}

- When Tex Murphy tastes a cigarette he doesn't like, he describes it as "The kind of creation [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) and his minions worked through the night creating in the foul-stench bowels of ".

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warcraft_III:_The_Frozen_Throne" title="wikipedia:Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne">Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne</a> {#Warcraft_III:_The_Frozen_Throne}

- Lord Garithos has the quote "Never trust an elf!" said by Gimli in*The Fellowship of the Ring*.
- One of the quotes for the Spell Breaker unit is "I stole your precious."
- One of Tichondrius quotes is "Ash nath kimbatuul!" a reference to The One Ring's inscription in [Black Speech](../B/Black_Speech.md).

### <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Warcraft" title="wikipedia:World of Warcraft">World of Warcraft</a> {#World_of_Warcraft}

- There is an item called "The 1 Ring" that can only be acquired from Fishing. This is a reference to The One Ring being found by [Déagol](../D/Déagol.md) and [Sméagol](../S/Sméagol.md) while on a fishing trip.
- There is a mob named "Gorum" that can be found in Bloodthorn Cave, Shadowmoon Valley. He drops the item "Precious Bloodthorn Loop" which turns the player invisible when used.
- One of the voice emote jokes for a Human male character is about an idea of a great movie. The plot line is very similar to *The Lord of the Rings*.
    - "So, I have this idea for a great movie. It's about two gnomes who find a bracelet of power, and they have to take it to the Burning Steppes and cast it into the Cauldron. They form the Brotherhood of the Bracelet. Along the way they're trailed by a murloc named Gottom, who's obsessed with the bracelet, and nine bracelet bogeymen. It could be a three-parter, called 'Ruler of the Bracelet'. The first part would be called 'The Brotherhood of the Bracelet', followed by 'A Couple of Towers', with the climactic ending called 'Hey, the King's Back!'"
- In Draenor there is a campfire with three ogrons named "Toom", "Bort" and "Wullim". They are guarding a chest which contains 12 "Dwarf Beard Rings" and a "Burglar's Vest".
- In the Ghostlands there is a quest named "Anok'suten" where you have to kill an elite mob (a spider) and one of the rewards for completing the quest is a one-handed sword named "Stung". This is a reference to [Sting](../S/Sting.md) from *The Hobbit* and *The Lord of the Rings*.
- In Gnomeregan there is a quest you can obtain named "Return of the Ring", a pun on *Return of the King*.
- One of the rewards for completing the quest, "Escape from the Winterfin Caverns", is the "Mrgl Blade" a reference to the [weapon](../M/Morgul-knife.md) the Witch-king used to stab Frodo on Weathertop. Note that Aragorn identifies the weapon as "Morgul *blade*" only in .
- In the quest "Excuse the Mess" the player must sign the Embassy Visitor Log in Wingrest Embassy. The player is given a list of what they want to write for each column. The middle column asks the player for their arrival time, one of the options to choose is "Precisely when I mean to".
- In Stormwind City there is an NPC named "Thorfin Stoneshield".
