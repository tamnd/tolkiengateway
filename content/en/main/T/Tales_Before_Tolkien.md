<i><b>Tales Before Tolkien</b></i> is a collection of fantasy fiction which inspired [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

Editor [Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson) has noted that the title *Tales before Tolkien* was suggested by the publisher; his original title had been *Roots of the Mountain: Fantasy before Tolkien*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>For those interested in <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>&#39;s sources comes Tales Before Tolkien: The Roots of Modern Fantasy, edited by <a href="/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson" title="Douglas A. Anderson">Douglas A. Anderson</a> (<a href="/wiki/The_Annotated_Hobbit" title="The Annotated Hobbit">The Annotated Hobbit</a>), which collects 22 classic stories by such masters as <a href="/wiki/George_MacDonald" title="George MacDonald">George MacDonald</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Andrew_Lang&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Andrew Lang (page does not exist)">Andrew Lang</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lord_Dunsany&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lord Dunsany (page does not exist)">Lord Dunsany</a> and <a href="/wiki/James_Branch_Cabell" title="James Branch Cabell">James Branch Cabell</a>. Arthur Machen aficionados will especially appreciate &#34;The Coming of the Terror&#34; (an abridgement of his short novel The Terror), hitherto unreprinted since its initial magazine appearance in 1917. Copyright 2003 Reed Business Information.
</p>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "The Elves" by Ludwig Tieck
- "The Golden Key" by George Macdonald
- "Puss-Cat Mew" by E. H. Knatchbull-Hugessen
- "The Griffin and the Minor Canon" by Frank R. Stockton
- "The Demon Pope" by Richard Garnett
- "The Story of Sigurd" by Andrew Lang
- "The Folk of the Mountain Door" by William Morris
- "Black Heart and White Heart" by H. Rider Haggard
- "The Dragon Tamers" by E. Nesbit
- "The Far Islands" by John Buchan
- "The Drawn Arrow" by Clemence Housman
- "The Enchanted Buffalo" by L. Frank Baum
- "Chu-bu and Sheemish" by Lord Dunsany
- "The Baumhoff Explosive" by William Hope Hodgson
- "The Regent of the North" by Kenneth Morris
- "The Coming of the Terror" by Arthur Machen
- "The Elf Trap" by Francis Stevens
- "The Thin Queen of Elfhame" by James Branch Cabell
- "The Woman of the Wood" by A. Merritt
- "Golithos the Ogre" by E. A. Wyke-Smith
- "The Story of Alwina" by Austin Tappan Wright
- "A Christmas Play" by David Lindsay
