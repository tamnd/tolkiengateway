**Third Age 2980** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2980**) is the 2980th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Third Age 2980 was also known as **S.R. 1380** in [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning).

Notable events in this year include:

## April {#April}

- **6** - Birth of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), fifth child and third son of [Hamfast Gamgee](/wiki/Hamfast_Gamgee) and [Bell Goodchild](/wiki/Bell_Goodchild).<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-1">[1]</a></sup>

## During 2980 {#During_2980}

- [Thorongil](/wiki/Thorongil) ([Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)) with the leave of [Ecthelion II](/wiki/Ecthelion_II), [attacks Umbar](/wiki/Surprise_Attack_on_Umbar "Surprise Attack on Umbar"), destroys the fleet and kills the [Captain of the Haven](/wiki/Captain_of_the_Haven).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-stew_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Thorongil leaves his companions at [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) and goes on alone for other tasks. His adventures end.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-stew_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Aragorn enters [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) and there meets again [Arwen Undómiel](/wiki/Arwen "Arwen"). Aragorn gives her the [ring of Barahir](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir "Ring of Barahir"), and they plight their troth upon the hill of [Cerin Amroth](/wiki/Cerin_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- About this time [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) reaches the confines of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and becomes acquainted with [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Everard Took](/wiki/Everard_Took), fourth child and second son of [Adelard Took](/wiki/Adelard_Took).<sup id="cite_ref-Took_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Berilac Brandybuck](/wiki/Berilac_Brandybuck), son of [Merimac Brandybuck](/wiki/Merimac_Brandybuck).<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-5">[5]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Fredegar Bolger](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger), first child and son of [Odovacar Bolger](/wiki/Odovacar_Bolger) and [Rosamunda Took](/wiki/Rosamunda_Took).<sup id="cite_ref-Took_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Tolman Cotton Junior](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Junior), first child and son of [Tolman Cotton Senior](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior) and [Lily Brown](/wiki/Lily_Brown).<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan") [Thengel](/wiki/Thengel).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-6">[6]</a></sup>
- [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden), son of Thengel, becomes the seventeenth King of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-6">[6]</a></sup>
- Death of [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Fortinbras Took II](/wiki/Fortinbras_Took_II).<sup id="cite_ref-Took_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Ferumbras Took III](/wiki/Ferumbras_Took_III), son of Fortinbras, becomes the thirtieth Thain of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-Took_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Death of [Drogo Baggins](/wiki/Drogo_Baggins) and [Primula Brandybuck](/wiki/Primula_Brandybuck); they are drowned in the [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine). Their son, young [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), is adopted by his uncle [Rorimac Brandybuck](/wiki/Rorimac_Brandybuck).<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
