<i><b>Tolkien, the Sea and Scandinavia</b></i> is a collection of the proceedings of the 11st Tolkien Society Seminar held in Colchester in 1996. The book was published by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) in [1999](/wiki/1999) under the auspices of the [Peter Roe Memorial Fund](/wiki/Peter_Roe_Memorial_Fund). In 2021, a second edition was issued by Luna Press Publishing under the title *Tolkien and the Sea*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "Seas and Shores: A Study of Cataclysms in Middle-earth" by [Alex Lewis](/wiki/Alex_Lewis)
- "The Sea-Bell: A Voyage of Exploration" by Christine Davidson
- "Searching for an Earthly Paradise: Some Common Images in the Works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis)" by Maria Kuteeva
- "The Great Wave and Other Dreams in The Lord of the Rings" by [Patricia Reynolds](/wiki/Patricia_Reynolds)
- "Tolkien-on-Sea: The View from the Shores of Middle-earth" by [John Ellison](/wiki/John_Ellison)
