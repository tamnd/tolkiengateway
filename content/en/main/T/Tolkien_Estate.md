The **Tolkien Estate** is the legal body which manages the copyrights of most of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s works. It mainly holds the copyright over the literary texts. [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) and his sister, [Priscilla Tolkien](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien), were executors. Current executors of the estate include Christopher's second wife [Baillie Tolkien](/wiki/Baillie_Tolkien) and Christopher's nephew [Michael George Tolkien](/wiki/Michael_George_Tolkien). Cathleen Blackburn of Manches &amp; Co. has been the estate's solicitor for many years.

The film and merchandise rights for *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* were sold by J.R.R. Tolkien, and are currently owned by [Middle-earth Enterprises](/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises), formerly known as Tolkien Enterprises, a company currently controlled by [Embracer Group](/wiki/Embracer_Group), while the television rights of these two books remained with Tolkien Estate.

On [13 November](/wiki/13_November) [2017](/wiki/2017), it was announced that [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) had acquired the television rights of *The Lord of the Rings* and *The Hobbit*, committing to a [multi-season television series](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power"). The series is not a direct adaptation of both of these novels, but instead it features stories that are set before the events of *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Amazon](/wiki/Amazon.com_Incorporated "Amazon.com Incorporated") said that the deal included the potential production for a spin-off series as well.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The press release referred to "previously unexplored stories based on J.R.R. Tolkien’s original writings". Amazon is the producer in conjunction with the Tolkien Estate itself, [The Tolkien Trust](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Trust), [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) and [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
