The **Tolkien Estate** is the legal body which manages the copyrights of most of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s works. It mainly holds the copyright over the literary texts. [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) and his sister, [Priscilla Tolkien](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md), were executors. Current executors of the estate include Christopher's second wife [Baillie Tolkien](../B/Baillie_Tolkien.md) and Christopher's nephew [Michael George Tolkien](../M/Michael_George_Tolkien.md). Cathleen Blackburn of Manches &amp; Co. has been the estate's solicitor for many years.

The film and merchandise rights for *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* were sold by J.R.R. Tolkien, and are currently owned by [Middle-earth Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md), formerly known as Tolkien Enterprises, a company currently controlled by [Embracer Group](../E/Embracer_Group.md), while the television rights of these two books remained with Tolkien Estate.

On [13 November](../_/13_November.md) [2017](../_/2017.md), it was announced that [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md) had acquired the television rights of *The Lord of the Rings* and *The Hobbit*, committing to a [multi-season television series](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md). The series is not a direct adaptation of both of these novels, but instead it features stories that are set before the events of *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Amazon](../A/Amazon.com_Incorporated.md) said that the deal included the potential production for a spin-off series as well.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The press release referred to "previously unexplored stories based on J.R.R. Tolkien’s original writings". Amazon is the producer in conjunction with the Tolkien Estate itself, [The Tolkien Trust](The_Tolkien_Trust.md), [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md) and [New Line Cinema](../N/New_Line_Cinema.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
