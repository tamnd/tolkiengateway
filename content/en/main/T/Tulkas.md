**Tulkas** was a [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar"). He was the most warlike of the Valar and the spouse of [Nessa](/wiki/Nessa).

## Attributes {#Attributes}

Tulkas was so strong and quick that he needed no weapon or steed. He delighted in deeds of prowess and strength such as wrestling and fighting. But he was not dreadful; he laughed loudly in sport and war and his anger dispelled [darkness](/wiki/Darkness "Darkness") and clouds.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-s1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-2">[2]</a></sup>

He was a hardy friend and slow to wrath, but also slow to forgive. He was however not forward-thinking and did not learn from the past; always quick in action, he was not good in counselling.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:206</sup>

His flesh was ruddy and his hair and beard golden.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup>

Tulkas represents the good side of ‘violence’ in the war against evil. This is an absence of all compromise which will even face apparent evils (such as war) rather than parley; and does not (in any kind of pride) think that any one less than Eru can redress this, or rewrite the tale of Arda.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:522</sup>

## History {#History}

Tulkas was [created](/wiki/Creation_of_the_Ainur "Creation of the Ainur") by [Eru](/wiki/Eru) with the other [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) and sang in the [Song of the Ainur](/wiki/Song_of_the_Ainur), and chose to enter [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4). His spirit was wandering Ea and did not participate in the formation of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) with the other [Valar](/wiki/Valar): He heard of their [First War](/wiki/First_War) with [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor) [1500](/wiki/Valian_Year_1500 "Valian Year 1500") [Valian Years](/wiki/Valian_Years) after their arrival, and descended to them from the far heavens. The Dark Lord might have defeated the Valar by himself, but hearing the sound of Tulkas' laughter and beholding his wrath, Melkor fled before him.<sup id="cite_ref-s1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-2">[2]</a></sup>

Thanks to him, the Valar now continued their work in peace, and the [Spring of Arda](/wiki/Spring_of_Arda) was begun. After the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps) were erected and the Valar had made their first dwelling at [Almaren](/wiki/Almaren), Tulkas wedded [Nessa](/wiki/Nessa) in a great feast. Being weary and content he slept, and Melkor decided his hour to retaliate had come, and ruined the Lamps, causing a cataclysm.<sup id="cite_ref-s1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Antonio_Vinci_-_Tulkas.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/0/05/Antonio_Vinci_-_Tulkas.jpg/300px-Antonio_Vinci_-_Tulkas.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="143" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/0/05/Antonio_Vinci_-_Tulkas.jpg/450px-Antonio_Vinci_-_Tulkas.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/0/05/Antonio_Vinci_-_Tulkas.jpg/600px-Antonio_Vinci_-_Tulkas.jpg 2x" data-file-width="3310" data-file-height="1575"/></a><figcaption><i>Tulkas</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Antonio_Vinci" title="Category:Images by Antonio Vinci">Antonio Vinci</a></figcaption></figure>

After the cataclysm, the Valar and their people went to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). Anticipating the [Awakening of the Elves](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves) Tulkas urged the other Valar to wage war against Melkor.

But when [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) announced that the [first Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Elves "Elves") were awake, Manwë called a council at the [Ring of Doom](/wiki/Ring_of_Doom) and announced the will of Eru that, for the sake of the Elves, the Valar should regain guardianship of the world from Melkor. Tulkas was glad of this decision. In the [Battle of the Powers](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers), after a long siege at [Utumno](/wiki/Utumno), it was Tulkas who wrestled with Melkor and cast him upon his face. Tulkas [chained](/wiki/Chaining_of_Melkor "Chaining of Melkor") Melkor with the enchanted chain [Angainor](/wiki/Angainor), forged by [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB). For a while the world was free from Melkor's influence.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

When his sentence was fulfilled, Melkor repented of his actions and was pardoned by [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB); Tulkas was disappointed with this decision, ever distrustful. But having fought Melkor in the name of authority, he could not rebel himself, and accepted Manwë's decision.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

After the lies spread by Melkor were known, Tulkas and Oromë seached him in the north of Aman, but he was already escaped south. And when Melkor and [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) caused the [Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor), Tulkas and Oromë searched in vain for them, unable to penetrate the unlight cast by Ungoliant.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Shortly after, Tulkas hurried [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) to take a decision about surrendering the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

It is said that in the [Dagor Dagorath](/wiki/Dagor_Dagorath), Tulkas will once more oppose [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor), and will directly fight him in the battle. The victor is unknown, But probably Melkor, For it will be [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) who will slay Melkor.<sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:333</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Tulkas* means in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) "steady, firm".<sup id="cite_ref-Etymologies_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etymologies-9">[9]</a></sup> <i><b>Tulukhastāz</b></i> ("the golden-haired")<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[note 1]</a></sup> was the original [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) name for Tulkas.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

He was also called <i><b>Astaldo</b></i> ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"): "the Valiant").<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup> *Astaldo* replaced the earlier name *Poldórëa*.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LT1III_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1III-14">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup>

In [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol)'s [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) translations, Tulkas is referred as *Afodfrea* "Strength-ruler".<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup>

In an early manuscript, a considered surname was: <i><b>Ender</b></i>, derived from <i><b>Enderō</b></i> ('\[?virile\] young bridegroom').<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[16]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In some versions it is said that Tulkas was of the spirits who didn't come into [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4). In other versions, it is said that Tulkas was one of the spirits who entered the vast regions of Eä, until he came to Arda to help Manwë.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[17]</a></sup>

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)* he did not come last and he was young but not the youngest of the great Valar ([Ómar Amillo](/wiki/%C3%93mar "Ómar") was).<sup id="cite_ref-LT1III_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1III-14">[13]</a></sup> [Telimektar](/wiki/Telimektar) was his son.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[18]</a></sup>

In [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), his name was <i><b>Tolchas</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Etymologies_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etymologies-9">[9]</a></sup>
