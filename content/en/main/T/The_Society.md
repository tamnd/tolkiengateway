**The Society** was a dining club associated with the [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford). [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) became a member in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilary_term" title="wikipedia:Hilary term">Hilary Term</a> [1935](/wiki/1935) and attended most dinners until moving from [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The papers (including manuscripts by Tolkien) of the Society are held at the [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
