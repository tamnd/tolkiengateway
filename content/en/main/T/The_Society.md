**The Society** was a dining club associated with the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md). [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) became a member in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilary_term" title="wikipedia:Hilary term">Hilary Term</a> [1935](../_/1935.md) and attended most dinners until moving from [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The papers (including manuscripts by Tolkien) of the Society are held at the [Bodleian Library](../B/Bodleian_Library.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
