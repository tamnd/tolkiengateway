<i><b>The Boggit</b></i> is a parody of the [1982](../_/1982.md) video game *[The Hobbit](<The_Hobbit_(1982_video_game).md>)*, being the sequel to the [1985](../_/1985.md) game *[Bored of the Rings](<../B/Bored_of_the_Rings_(video_game).md>)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup> The game was written by [Fergus McNeill](../F/Fergus_McNeill.md) and [Judith Child](../J/Judith_Child.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Spectrum_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Spectrum-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup>

It is divided into three parts, and includes a hidden game.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
