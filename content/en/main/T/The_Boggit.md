<i><b>The Boggit</b></i> is a parody of the [1982](/wiki/1982) video game *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1982_video_game)> "The Hobbit (1982 video game)")*, being the sequel to the [1985](/wiki/1985) game *[Bored of the Rings](</wiki/Bored_of_the_Rings_(video_game)> "Bored of the Rings (video game)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup> The game was written by [Fergus McNeill](/wiki/Fergus_McNeill) and [Judith Child](/wiki/Judith_Child).<sup id="cite_ref-Spectrum_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Spectrum-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup>

It is divided into three parts, and includes a hidden game.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
