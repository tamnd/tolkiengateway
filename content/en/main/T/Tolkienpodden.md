<i><b>Tolkienpodden</b></i> is a Swedish [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related podcast hosted by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Adam_Westlund_De_La_Torre&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Adam Westlund De La Torre (page does not exist)">Adam Westlund De La Torre</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Elisabet_Bergander&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Elisabet Bergander (page does not exist)">Elisabet Bergander</a> and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Daniel_M%C3%B6ller&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Daniel Möller (page does not exist)">Daniel Möller</a>. The podcast has been released monthly since 2017. Every episode covers a different Tolkien-related subject.
