**Third Age 2204** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2204**) is the 2204th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2204 was also known as **S.R. 604** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Belegorn](../B/Belegorn.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Húrin I](../H/Húrin_I.md), son of Belegorn, becomes the fifth Ruling Steward.<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>
