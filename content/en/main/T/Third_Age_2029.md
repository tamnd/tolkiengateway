**Third Age 2029** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2029**) is the 2029th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2029 was also known as **S.R. 429** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of Steward [Vorondil](../V/Vorondil.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Mardil](../M/Mardil.md), son of Vorondil, becomes the Steward of [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Eärnil II](../E/Eärnil_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>
