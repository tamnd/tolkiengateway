**Tû** was a [fay](/wiki/Fays "Fays"), and the king of the [Hisildi](/wiki/Hisildi), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

His dwelling was in an underground lake near [Koivië-néni](/wiki/Koivi%C3%AB-n%C3%A9ni).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuv_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuv-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Tû was a wizard, "more skilled in magics than any that have dwelt ever yet beyond the land of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor)", who became a king among the [Ilkorindi](/wiki/Ilkorindi), the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who never left [Palisor](/wiki/Palisor). Those Elves that followed him afterwards became known as the [Hisildi](/wiki/Hisildi), and he taught them much lore and wisdom.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuv_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuv-1">[1]</a></sup>

According to one text, when an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") called [Nuin](/wiki/Nuin) discovered the sleeping bodies of [Men](/wiki/Men) at [Murmenalda](/wiki/Murmenalda), Tû subsequently forbade the Elves to wake them or even to visit them, fearing the wrath of [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

However, despite this warning, Nuin still visited the sleeping Men, and eventually woke two of them, later called [Ermon](/wiki/Ermon) and [Elmir](/wiki/Elmir), and taught them [his](/wiki/Ilkorin "Ilkorin") speech. After the [awakening of Men](/wiki/Awakening_of_Men "Awakening of Men"), [Melko](/wiki/Melko) learned of them, and sent his creatures after the Elves and the newly awakened Men, from which Tû protected them.<sup id="cite_ref-Thu_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thu-3">[3]</a></sup>

According to another text, Tû eventually "faded before the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and hid in the bottomless caverns" after Men "grew in stature".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The character was originally called *Túvo*, before being changed to *Tû*.<sup id="cite_ref-Glo_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glo-5">[5]</a></sup>

He was also called the <i><b>Lord of Gloaming</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuv_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuv-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earlier versions of his story, Tû (called *Túvo* in that text) was said to have met Melko in the [halls of Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos "Halls of Mandos") during Melko's imprisonment, and was corrupted by him. Túvo was said to have learned "much black [magic](/wiki/Magic "Magic")" from Melko, and that after the [darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor "Darkening of Valinor"), he went to the Great Lands where he set up a "wizard kingship".<sup id="cite_ref-Glo_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glo-5">[5]</a></sup>

However, in later texts, the character of Tû lost these negative connotations,<sup id="cite_ref-Glo_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glo-5">[5]</a></sup> and instead became the protector of Elves and Men.<sup id="cite_ref-Thu_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thu-3">[3]</a></sup>
