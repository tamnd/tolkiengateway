**Tû** was a [fay](../F/Fays.md), and the king of the [Hisildi](../H/Hisildi.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

His dwelling was in an underground lake near [Koivië-néni](../K/Koivië-néni.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuv_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuv-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Tû was a wizard, "more skilled in magics than any that have dwelt ever yet beyond the land of [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md)", who became a king among the [Ilkorindi](../I/Ilkorindi.md), the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) who never left [Palisor](../P/Palisor.md). Those Elves that followed him afterwards became known as the [Hisildi](../H/Hisildi.md), and he taught them much lore and wisdom.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuv_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuv-1">[1]</a></sup>

According to one text, when an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) called [Nuin](../N/Nuin.md) discovered the sleeping bodies of [Men](../M/Men.md) at [Murmenalda](../M/Murmenalda.md), Tû subsequently forbade the Elves to wake them or even to visit them, fearing the wrath of [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

However, despite this warning, Nuin still visited the sleeping Men, and eventually woke two of them, later called [Ermon](../E/Ermon.md) and [Elmir](../E/Elmir.md), and taught them [his](../I/Ilkorin.md) speech. After the [awakening of Men](../A/Awakening_of_Men.md), [Melko](../M/Melko.md) learned of them, and sent his creatures after the Elves and the newly awakened Men, from which Tû protected them.<sup id="cite_ref-Thu_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thu-3">[3]</a></sup>

According to another text, Tû eventually "faded before the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) and hid in the bottomless caverns" after Men "grew in stature".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The character was originally called *Túvo*, before being changed to *Tû*.<sup id="cite_ref-Glo_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glo-5">[5]</a></sup>

He was also called the <i><b>Lord of Gloaming</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuv_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuv-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earlier versions of his story, Tû (called *Túvo* in that text) was said to have met Melko in the [halls of Mandos](../H/Halls_of_Mandos.md) during Melko's imprisonment, and was corrupted by him. Túvo was said to have learned "much black [magic](../M/Magic.md)" from Melko, and that after the [darkening of Valinor](../D/Darkening_of_Valinor.md), he went to the Great Lands where he set up a "wizard kingship".<sup id="cite_ref-Glo_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glo-5">[5]</a></sup>

However, in later texts, the character of Tû lost these negative connotations,<sup id="cite_ref-Glo_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glo-5">[5]</a></sup> and instead became the protector of Elves and Men.<sup id="cite_ref-Thu_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thu-3">[3]</a></sup>
