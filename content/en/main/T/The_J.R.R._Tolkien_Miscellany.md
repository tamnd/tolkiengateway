## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

Entertaining and unusual facts, trivia and quotes about Tolkien's life and works. Covers the characters, films, books and life of the great author. Looks at the places that inspired his fictional world of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) and the problems that fame caused him in the 1960s. Author is foremost Tolkien expert This miscellany explores the fascinating and enigmatic world of J.R.R. Tolkien, examining his place in literary history, his books and his iconic characters. The reader can explore facts and trivia from Tolkien's life and works, including his early life in southern Africa and Birmingham, Tolkien on the silver screen, his role in the two world wars and his friendship with C.S. Lewis, as well as the places that inspired his fictional world of Middle-earth. Both light-hearted and highly informative, this miscellany offers an insight for new and old Tolkien fans into one of the great writers of the twentieth century.
