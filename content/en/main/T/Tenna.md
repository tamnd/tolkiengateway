<i><b>tenna</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "unto",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or "'to the object' hence 'up to, to (reach), as far as'", said to be a "preposition originally adverb".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-C_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-3">[3]</a></sup>

When followed by a word beginning in a similar vowel, *tenna* takes an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elision" title="wikipedia:Elision">elided</a> form (*tenn’*).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- Root [TEN](/wiki/TEN)<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Compounds {#Compounds}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[tennoio](/wiki/Tennoio "Tennoio")* ("for ever"; *tenna* + *[oio](/wiki/Oio "Oio")*)<sup id="cite_ref-C_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- "<i><b>Tenn(’/a)</b> <a href="/wiki/Ambar-metta" title="Ambar-metta">Ambar-metta</a></i>" ("Until World's-end")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
