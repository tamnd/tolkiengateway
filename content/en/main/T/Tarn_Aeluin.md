**Tarn Aeluin**,<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup> sometimes referred to as just **Aeluin**,<sup id="cite_ref-MR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-2">[2]</a></sup> was a clear blue mountain lake in the eastern highlands of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), southwest of [Ladros](/wiki/Ladros).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It was situated among wild heather hills, themselves surrounded by trackless and uninhabited forests and highland plains of heather. [Alder-trees](/wiki/Alders "Alders") were known to grow beside the lake.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Tarn Aeluin was said to have been hallowed by [Melian](/wiki/Melian) the [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar").<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>

When [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor) saw the reflection of [Andreth](/wiki/Andreth) in Tarn Aeluin, he fell in love with her.<sup id="cite_ref-MR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Barahir](/wiki/Barahir) and his [twelve followers](/wiki/XII_B%C3%ABorings "XII Bëorings") hid from [Morgoth's](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") forces after the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) and made their lair at Tarn Aeluin. There they remained hidden until they were betrayed by [Gorlim](/wiki/Gorlim).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aeluin* is clearly [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), being never glossed. [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack) explains it means "Blue Lake", from *[ael](/wiki/Ael "Ael")* ("lake, pool") + *[luin](/wiki/Luin "Luin")* ("blue").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> *Tarn* is an [archaic English word](/wiki/Uncommon_words "Uncommon words") meaning "mountain lake".

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to the text in "[The Complaint of Mîm the Dwarf](/wiki/The_Complaint_of_M%C3%AEm_the_Dwarf)", [Mîm](/wiki/M%C3%AEm), the last of the [Petty-dwarves](/wiki/Petty-dwarves), had made his first work of art at Tarn Aeluin during his youth.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
