<i><b>thôl</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word, meaning "helm"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or "helmet". Plural forms were *thely*, *thuil*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol" title="Dor-Cúarthol">Dor-Cúar<b>thol</b></a></i> = "Land of Bow and Helm"
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Castol&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Castol (page does not exist)">cas<b>tol</b></a></i> = "helmet"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Gorthol" title="Gorthol">Gor<b>thol</b></a></i> = "Dread Helm"
