**Third Age 3019** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3019**) is the 3019th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 3019 was also known as **S.R. 1419** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## January {#January}

- **8** - The [Company](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) reach [Hollin](../H/Hollin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **11** - The Company ascend the [Redhorn Gate](../R/Redhorn_Gate.md)<sup id="cite_ref-chronology1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology1-2">[2]</a></sup> and are overtaken by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Snow&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Snow (page does not exist)">Snow</a><sup id="cite_ref-chronology1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology1-2">[2]</a></sup> on [Caradhras](../C/Caradhras.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **12** - Snowstorm<sup id="cite_ref-chronology1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology1-2">[2]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **13** - Attack by [Wargs](../W/Wargs.md)<sup id="cite_ref-chronology1_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology1-2">[2]</a></sup> in the early hours. The [Company](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) reaches the [West-gate](../W/West-gate.md) of [Moria](../M/Moria.md) at nightfall. [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) begins to trail the [Ring-bearer](../R/Ring-bearer.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **14** - Night in [Hall Twenty-one](Twenty-first_Hall.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **15** - The [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](../B/Bridge_of_Khazad-dûm.md), and fall of [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md). The [Company](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) reaches [Nimrodel](../N/Nimrodel_the_river.md) late at night.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **17** - The [Company](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) comes to [Caras Galadhon](../C/Caras_Galadhon.md) at evening.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **23** - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) pursues the [Balrog](../B/Balrogs.md) to the peak of [Zirakzigil](../C/Celebdil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **24** - Gollum is captured and questioned by [Uglúk](../U/Uglúk.md), but he escapes later. [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) sends reinforcements to Uglúk; his troop is now about 180 strong.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **25** - Gandalf casts down the [Balrog](../B/Balrogs.md), and passes away. His body lies on the peak.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

## February {#February}

- **15** - The [Mirror of Galadriel](../M/Mirror_of_Galadriel.md). [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) returns to life, and lies in a trance.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **16** - Farewell to [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md). [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) in hiding on the west bank observes the departure.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **17** - [Gwaihir](../G/Gwaihir.md) bears [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) to [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **23** - The boats are attacked at night near [Sarn Gebir](../S/Sarn_Gebir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md) returns to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **25** - The [Company](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) pass the [Argonath](../A/Argonath.md) and camp at [Parth Galen](../P/Parth_Galen.md). [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](../F/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md); [Théodred](Théodred.md) son of [Théoden](Théoden.md) slain.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **26** - [Breaking of the Fellowship](../B/Breaking_of_the_Fellowship.md). Death of [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md); his horn is heard in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). [Meriadoc](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Peregrin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) captured. [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) enter the eastern Emyn Muil. [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) sets out in pursuit of the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) at evening. [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) hears of the descent of the Orc-band from the [Emyn Muil](../E/Emyn_Muil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **27** - [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) reaches the west-cliff at sunrise. [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) against [Théoden](Théoden.md)'s orders sets out from [Eastfold](../E/Eastfold.md) about midnight to pursue the Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **28** - [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) overtakes the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) just outside [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **29** - [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) escape and meet [Treebeard](Treebeard.md). The [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) attack at sunrise and destroy the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md). [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) descends from the [Emyn Muil](../E/Emyn_Muil.md) and meets [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md). [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) sees the funeral boat of [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **30** - [Entmoot](../E/Entmoot.md) begins. [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) returning to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) meets [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

## March {#March}

- **1** - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) begins the passage of the [Dead Marshes](../D/Dead_Marshes.md) at dawn. [Entmoot](../E/Entmoot.md) continues. [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) meets [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), and forgets to celebrate his 88th birthday. They set out for [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md). [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) leaves [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) on an errand to [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **2** - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) comes to the end of the [Dead Marshes](../D/Dead_Marshes.md). [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) comes to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) and heals [Théoden](Théoden.md). The [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) ride west against [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md). [Second Battle of the Fords of Isen](../S/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md). [Erkenbrand](../E/Erkenbrand.md) defeated. [Entmoot](../E/Entmoot.md) ends in afternoon. The [Ents](../E/Ents.md) march on [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) and reach it at night.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **3** - [Théoden](Théoden.md) retreats to [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md). [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md) begins. [Ents](../E/Ents.md) complete the destruction of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **4** - [Théoden](Théoden.md) and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) set out from the [Hornburg](../H/Hornburg.md) for [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md). [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) reaches the slag-mounds on the edge of the [Desolation of the Morannon](../D/Desolation_of_the_Morannon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **5** - [Théoden](Théoden.md) reaches [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) at noon. Parley with [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) in [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md). [Winged Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) passes over the camp at [Dol Baran](../D/Dol_Baran.md). [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) sets out with [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) for [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) hides in sight of the [Morannon](../M/Morannon.md), and leaves at dusk.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **6** - At dawn [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Pippin](../P/Pippin.md) reach [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Minas_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Minas-5">[5]</a></sup> [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) overtaken by the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md) in the early hours.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) uses the [Orthanc-stone](../O/Orthanc-stone.md) to reveal himself and [Andúril](../A/Andúril.md) to [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Théoden](Théoden.md) sets out from the [Hornburg](../H/Hornburg.md) for [Harrowdale](../H/Harrowdale.md). [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) sets out later.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **7** - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) taken by [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) to [Henneth Annûn](../H/Henneth_Annûn.md). [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) comes to [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md) at nightfall.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), riding through [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md), see the [Warning beacons of Gondor](../W/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor.md) lit.<sup id="cite_ref-Minas_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Minas-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **8** - [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) takes the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md) at daybreak; he reaches the [Stone of Erech](../S/Stone_of_Erech.md) at midnight. [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) leaves [Henneth Annûn](../H/Henneth_Annûn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **9** - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) reaches [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) leaves Henneth Annûn. [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) sets out from [Erech](../E/Erech.md) and comes to [Calembel](../C/Calembel.md). At dusk [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) reaches the [Morgul-road](../M/Morgul-road.md). [Théoden](Théoden.md) comes to [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Red Arrow](../R/Red_Arrow.md) is presented by [Hirgon](../H/Hirgon.md) to [Théoden](Théoden.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> [Darkness](../D/Darkness.md) begins to flow out of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **10** - The [Dawnless Day](../D/Dawnless_Day.md). The <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Muster_of_Rohan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Muster of Rohan (page does not exist)">Muster of Rohan</a>: the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) ride from [Harrowdale](../H/Harrowdale.md). [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) rescued by [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Imrahil](../I/Imrahil.md) outside the gates of the City. [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) crosses [Ringló](../R/Ringló.md). An army from the [Morannon](../M/Morannon.md) takes [Cair Andros](../C/Cair_Andros.md) and passes into [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md). [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) passes the [Cross-roads](../C/Cross-roads.md), and sees the [Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md)-host set forth.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **11** - [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) visits [Shelob](../S/Shelob.md), but seeing [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) asleep nearly repents. [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) sends [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) to [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md). [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) reaches [Linhir](../L/Linhir.md) and crosses into [Lebennin](../L/Lebennin.md). Eastern [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) is invaded from the north. First [assault](../A/Assaults_on_Lothlórien.md) on [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **12** - [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) leads [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) into [Shelob's Lair](../S/Shelob's_Lair.md). [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) retreats to the [Causeway Forts](../C/Causeway_Forts.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> [Théoden](Théoden.md) camps under [Min-Rimmon](../M/Min-Rimmon.md). [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) drives the [Southrons](../S/Southrons.md) towards [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md). The [Ents](../E/Ents.md) defeat the invaders of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **13** - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) captured by the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) of [Cirith Ungol](Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md), including [Shagrat](../S/Shagrat.md) and [Gorbag](../G/Gorbag.md). The [Pelennor](../P/Pelennor.md) is over-run. [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) is wounded. [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) reaches [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) and captures the fleet. [Théoden](Théoden.md) in [Drúadan Forest](../D/Drúadan_Forest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **14** - [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) finds [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) in [the Tower](Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md). [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) is besieged. The [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) led by the [Woses](../W/Woses.md) come to the [Grey Wood](../G/Grey_Wood.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) have passed the [Carnen](../C/Carnen.md) and pushed King [Brand](../B/Brand.md); [Battle of Dale](../B/Battle_of_Dale.md) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 1]</a></sup>
- **15** - In the early hours the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) breaks the Gates of the City. [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) burns himself on a pyre. The horns of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) are heard at cockcrow. [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md). [Théoden](Théoden.md) is slain. [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) and [Meriadoc](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) wounded. [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) raises the standard of [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md). Frodo and Sam escape and begin their journey north along the [Morgai](../M/Morgai.md). [Battle under the trees](../B/Battle_under_the_trees.md) in [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md); [Thranduil](Thranduil.md) repels the forces of [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md). Second [assault](../A/Assaults_on_Lothlórien.md) on [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **16** - [Last Debate](../L/Last_Debate.md) of the commanders in Minas Tirith. [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) from the [Morgai](../M/Morgai.md) looks out over the camp to [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **17** - [Battle of Dale](../B/Battle_of_Dale.md). King [Brand](../B/Brand.md) and King [Dáin Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) fall. Many [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md) take refuge in [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) and are besieged. [Shagrat](../S/Shagrat.md) brings [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)'s cloak, mail-shirt, and sword to [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md),<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> where Sauron slays him.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>
- **18** - The Host of the West marches from [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) comes in sight of the [Isenmouthe](../I/Isenmouthe.md); he is over-taken by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) on the road from [Durthang](../D/Durthang.md) to [Udûn](<../U/Udûn_(valley).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **19** - The Host comes to [Morgul Vale](../M/Morgul_Vale.md). [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) escape and begin their journey along the road to the [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
