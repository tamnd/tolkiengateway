**Third Age 3019** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3019**) is the 3019th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Third Age 3019 was also known as **S.R. 1419** in [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning).

Notable events in this year include:

## January {#January}

- **8** - The [Company](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") reach [Hollin](/wiki/Hollin).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **11** - The Company ascend the [Redhorn Gate](/wiki/Redhorn_Gate)<sup id="cite_ref-chronology1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology1-2">[2]</a></sup> and are overtaken by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Snow&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Snow (page does not exist)">Snow</a><sup id="cite_ref-chronology1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology1-2">[2]</a></sup> on [Caradhras](/wiki/Caradhras).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **12** - Snowstorm<sup id="cite_ref-chronology1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology1-2">[2]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **13** - Attack by [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs)<sup id="cite_ref-chronology1_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology1-2">[2]</a></sup> in the early hours. The [Company](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") reaches the [West-gate](/wiki/West-gate) of [Moria](/wiki/Moria) at nightfall. [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) begins to trail the [Ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearer).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **14** - Night in [Hall Twenty-one](/wiki/Twenty-first_Hall "Twenty-first Hall").<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **15** - The [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm), and fall of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf). The [Company](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") reaches [Nimrodel](/wiki/Nimrodel_the_river "Nimrodel the river") late at night.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **17** - The [Company](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") comes to [Caras Galadhon](/wiki/Caras_Galadhon) at evening.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **23** - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) pursues the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") to the peak of [Zirakzigil](/wiki/Celebdil "Celebdil").<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **24** - Gollum is captured and questioned by [Uglúk](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk), but he escapes later. [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) sends reinforcements to Uglúk; his troop is now about 180 strong.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **25** - Gandalf casts down the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs"), and passes away. His body lies on the peak.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

## February {#February}

- **15** - The [Mirror of Galadriel](/wiki/Mirror_of_Galadriel). [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) returns to life, and lies in a trance.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **16** - Farewell to [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien). [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) in hiding on the west bank observes the departure.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **17** - [Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir) bears [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) to [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **23** - The boats are attacked at night near [Sarn Gebir](/wiki/Sarn_Gebir).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax) returns to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **25** - The [Company](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") pass the [Argonath](/wiki/Argonath) and camp at [Parth Galen](/wiki/Parth_Galen). [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen); [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred) son of [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) slain.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **26** - [Breaking of the Fellowship](/wiki/Breaking_of_the_Fellowship). Death of [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir); his horn is heard in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). [Meriadoc](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Peregrin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") captured. [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") enter the eastern Emyn Muil. [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) sets out in pursuit of the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) at evening. [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) hears of the descent of the Orc-band from the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **27** - [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) reaches the west-cliff at sunrise. [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) against [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden)'s orders sets out from [Eastfold](/wiki/Eastfold) about midnight to pursue the Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **28** - [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) overtakes the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) just outside [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **29** - [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") escape and meet [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard). The [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) attack at sunrise and destroy the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") descends from the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) and meets [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum). [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) sees the funeral boat of [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **30** - [Entmoot](/wiki/Entmoot) begins. [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) returning to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) meets [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

## March {#March}

- **1** - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") begins the passage of the [Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes) at dawn. [Entmoot](/wiki/Entmoot) continues. [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) meets [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), and forgets to celebrate his 88th birthday. They set out for [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras). [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) leaves [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) on an errand to [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **2** - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") comes to the end of the [Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes). [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) comes to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) and heals [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden). The [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) ride west against [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman). [Second Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen). [Erkenbrand](/wiki/Erkenbrand) defeated. [Entmoot](/wiki/Entmoot) ends in afternoon. The [Ents](/wiki/Ents) march on [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) and reach it at night.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **3** - [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) retreats to [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep). [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg) begins. [Ents](/wiki/Ents) complete the destruction of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **4** - [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) set out from the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg) for [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard). [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") reaches the slag-mounds on the edge of the [Desolation of the Morannon](/wiki/Desolation_of_the_Morannon).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **5** - [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) reaches [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) at noon. Parley with [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) in [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc). [Winged Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") passes over the camp at [Dol Baran](/wiki/Dol_Baran). [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) sets out with [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") for [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") hides in sight of the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon), and leaves at dusk.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **6** - At dawn [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Pippin](/wiki/Pippin) reach [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).<sup id="cite_ref-Minas_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Minas-5">[5]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) overtaken by the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) in the early hours.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) uses the [Orthanc-stone](/wiki/Orthanc-stone) to reveal himself and [Andúril](/wiki/And%C3%BAril) to [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) sets out from the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg) for [Harrowdale](/wiki/Harrowdale). [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) sets out later.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **7** - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") taken by [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) to [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn). [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) comes to [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) at nightfall.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), riding through [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien), see the [Warning beacons of Gondor](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor) lit.<sup id="cite_ref-Minas_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Minas-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **8** - [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) takes the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead) at daybreak; he reaches the [Stone of Erech](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech) at midnight. [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") leaves [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **9** - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) reaches [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) leaves Henneth Annûn. [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) sets out from [Erech](/wiki/Erech) and comes to [Calembel](/wiki/Calembel). At dusk [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") reaches the [Morgul-road](/wiki/Morgul-road).
