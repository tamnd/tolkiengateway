**Tar-Meneldur** was the fifth [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md).

## History {#History}

Meneldur's birth name was *Írimon*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Meneldur_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Meneldur-1">[1]</a></sup> He was the only son of [Tar-Elendil](Tar-Elendil.md) and had two older sisters, [Silmariën](../S/Silmariën.md) and [Isilmë](../I/Isilmë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> At this time, [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) began to sail eastwards and in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [600](../S/Second_Age_600.md) the [Captain of the King's Ships](../C/Captain_of_the_King's_Ships.md), [Vëantur](../V/Vëantur.md), reached the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-3">[3]</a></sup>

His great passion was stargazing and learning all he could about the Heavens from the lore of [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md). To study the movements of the stars, he built a tower in the [Forostar](../F/Forostar.md), the northernmost part of Númenor, since the sky was clearer there than anywhere else on the isle he also received the title [Elentirmo](../E/Elentirmo.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Númenor_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Númenor-5">[5]</a></sup> Although Meneldur was not interested in the sea,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> he married [Almarian](../A/Almarian.md), the daughter of Vëantur.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Together they had three children, a son [Anardil](../A/Anardil_of_Númenor.md) and two daughters, [Ailinel](../A/Ailinel.md) and [Almiel](../A/Almiel.md), all born before Meneldur became king in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [740](../S/Second_Age_740.md).<sup id="cite_ref-earlier_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-earlier-8">[8]</a></sup>

As the eldest son, but the youngest child of [Tar-Elendil](Tar-Elendil.md), Tar-Meneldur received the [Sceptre of Númenor](../S/Sceptre_of_Númenor.md) due to the old law of royal [succession](../L/Law_of_Succession_in_Númenor.md) that existed at that time.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-3">[3]</a></sup> Meneldur only left the tower when he inherited the Sceptre, but even after becoming King his interest in the stars did not cease.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-3">[3]</a></sup>

Meneldur's son Anardil grew swiftly and became known as [Aldarion](Tar-Aldarion.md). Like his grandfather Vëantur, Aldarion became enamoured of the sea: in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [725](../S/Second_Age_725.md) Vëantur invited Aldarion to sail with him to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md); to this plan Meneldur gave his reluctant consent. When the voyagers returned in [727](../S/Second_Age_727.md) Meneldur's joy was great, but soon his son begged leave to sail again. Two more long voyages followed in [730](../S/Second_Age_730.md) and [735](../S/Second_Age_735.md). However, when Tar-Elendil relinquished the Sceptre to Tar-Meneldur in [740](../S/Second_Age_740.md), Aldarion remained at home for awhile for the comfort of his father.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

After a time the sea-longing came upon Aldarion anew. He formed a [Guild of Venturers](../G/Guild_of_Venturers.md) and left [Armenelos](../A/Armenelos.md) to reside on his ship [Eämbar](../E/Eämbar.md). Then Tar-Meneldur looked coldly upon the sea-enterprises of his son, believing that they engendered restlessness and a desire for dominion over other lands. Father and son became estranged but Almarian supported Aldarion and thus Tar-Meneldur let matters proceed unhindered. Tar-Meneldur did try to curb his son's activities by prohibiting the cutting of trees for shipbuilding, but this only resulted in Aldarion's seeking lumber in Middle-earth.

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [800](../S/Second_Age_800.md), Tar-Meneldur commanded his son to cease voyaging, for Aldarion was of an age (one hundred years old) to be proclaimed the King's Heir. The two were then reconciled, for a time. However, six years later Aldarion sought and obtained Tar-Meneldur's grudging leave to go to sea again. At the same time Almarian and Tar-Meneldur were encouraging Aldarion to marry, and in the Queen's household there was one, [Erendis](../E/Erendis.md), who attracted the prince’s attention. Although Aldarion did fall in love with Erendis it did not prevent him from sailing again. When he returned from this journey Tar-Meneldur forbade his son from leaving, yet Aldarion did so in defiance of the King. Hard upon his return Aldarion left for a twenty-year voyage which disquieted the King.

Tar-Meneldur rejoiced when Aldarion and Erendis were finally betrothed in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [858](../S/Second_Age_858.md), yet they were not married until [870](../S/Second_Age_870.md) after Aldarion had gone on yet another sea venture. Aldarion and Erendis' marriage proved to be unhappy and only produced one child, their daughter [Ancalimë](Tar-Ancalimë.md).

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [882](../S/Second_Age_882.md), Aldarion brought [a letter](../G/Gil-galad's_letter.md) from King [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) to Tar-Meneldur. In it the King of the Elves warned Tar-Meneldur that a new shadow was arisen in the East and beseeched him for aid. From this letter Tar-Meneldur learned that his son had aided the Elves and knew much of what these matters portended. Recognizing that his son was better equipped to handle this menace, Tar-Meneldur resolved to resign the Sceptre to his son; this he did in the year [883](../S/Second_Age_883.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-3">[3]</a></sup>

Tar-Meneldur passed the Sceptre to his son long before due time. He lived for 59 more years before he died in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [942](../S/Second_Age_942.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Meneldur_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Meneldur-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Meneldur* is Quenya. Its meaning is not glossed, but as Paul Strack explains, it means "Servant of the Heavens", being a compound of *[menel](../M/Menel.md)* ("the heavens") + *[-(n)dur](../_/-ndur.md)* ("servant").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> He took this royal name because of his love of starlore.<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Meneldur_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Meneldur-1">[1]</a></sup> Like all the rulers of Númenor who took their royal names in Quenya, Meneldur added the prefix *tar-* ("high") to his name when he received the [Sceptre](../S/Sceptre_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

His birthname <i><b>Írimon</b></i> is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). Paul Strack says that the elements are difficult to discern but it might be <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=%C3%8Dr%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Írë (page does not exist)">írë</a></i> ("desire") + a variant of the agental suffix *[-mo](../_/-mo.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Irimon_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Irimon-12">[12]</a></sup>

<i><b>Elentirmo</b></i> is Quenya for "Star-watcher".<sup id="cite_ref-UT|4a_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT|4a-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> As Paul Strack suggests it is a compound of *[elen](../E/Elen.md)* ("star") + otherwise unattested *tirmo* ("watcher").<sup id="cite_ref-Elentirmo_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elentirmo-14">[14]</a></sup> The noun *tirmo* is formed from the verb *[tir](Tir.md)* ("to watch") with the agental suffix *[-mo](../_/-mo.md)* ("one", "anyone", "someone", "somebody").<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

Gil-galad's letter refers to him as a "<b><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../H/High_King.md" title="High King">High King</a></b>",<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-3">[3]</a></sup> whereas no other King of Númenor is mentioned to have this status. It possibly implies an overlordship of the King of Númenor not only on his island, but also the Númenórean colonists of Middle-earth.
