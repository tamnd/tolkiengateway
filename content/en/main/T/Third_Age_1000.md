**Third Age 1000** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1000**) is the 1000th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Around this year, the [Istari](../W/Wizards.md) appear in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-2">[2]</a></sup>
