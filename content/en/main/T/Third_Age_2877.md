**Third Age 2877** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2877**) is the 2877th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2877 was also known as **S.R. 1277** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Madoc Brandybuck](../M/Madoc_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Marmadoc Brandybuck](../M/Marmadoc_Brandybuck.md), son of Madoc and [Hanna Goldworthy](../H/Hanna_Goldworthy.md), becomes the [Master of Buckland](../M/Master_of_Buckland.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-1">[1]</a></sup>
