**Thomas Robins** is a [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) actor who portrayed [Déagol](/wiki/D%C3%A9agol) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*. He also portrayed Young [Thráin](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in) in *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
