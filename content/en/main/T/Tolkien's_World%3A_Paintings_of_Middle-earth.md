<i><b>Tolkien&#39;s World: Paintings of Middle-earth</b></i> is a 1992 book collecting various pieces of art inspired by the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). Each painting is accompanied by a page of text drawn from the work that inspired it. Artists include [Inger Edelfeldt](/wiki/Inger_Edelfeldt), [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe), [Roger Garland](/wiki/Roger_Garland), [Michael Hague](/wiki/Michael_Hague), and others. Due to the success of this book, in [1996](/wiki/1996) *[Realms of Tolkien](/wiki/Realms_of_Tolkien)* was published, featuring even more art.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

[Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), the world created by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in his timeless classics *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, has been an inspiration for generations of artists. This beautiful volume of artwork celebrates the legacy of his imagination. *Tolkien's World* includes paintings by artists from all over the world. From the powerful depictions of the darker side of Middle-earth by [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe), to the evocative landscapes by [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee); from [Michael Hague](/wiki/Michael_Hague)'s delightful paintings in *The Hobbit* to the strange and compelling vision of [Roger Garland](/wiki/Roger_Garland); from the engaging simplicity of [Inger Edelfeldt](/wiki/Inger_Edelfeldt)'s work to the intricacy of dramatic realism of [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith) - Tolkien's World spans the whole of Middle-earth. Each painting is accompanied by a page of text drawn from the book that inspired it, describing the scene the artist has chosen to illustrate. Also included is a short essay by each of the artists about Tolkien's influence upon them.
