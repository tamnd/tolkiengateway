<i><b>Tolkien&#39;s World: Paintings of Middle-earth</b></i> is a 1992 book collecting various pieces of art inspired by the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). Each painting is accompanied by a page of text drawn from the work that inspired it. Artists include [Inger Edelfeldt](../I/Inger_Edelfeldt.md), [John Howe](../J/John_Howe.md), [Roger Garland](../R/Roger_Garland.md), [Michael Hague](../M/Michael_Hague.md), and others. Due to the success of this book, in [1996](../_/1996.md) *[Realms of Tolkien](../R/Realms_of_Tolkien.md)* was published, featuring even more art.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

[Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), the world created by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in his timeless classics *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)*, *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)*, has been an inspiration for generations of artists. This beautiful volume of artwork celebrates the legacy of his imagination. *Tolkien's World* includes paintings by artists from all over the world. From the powerful depictions of the darker side of Middle-earth by [John Howe](../J/John_Howe.md), to the evocative landscapes by [Alan Lee](../A/Alan_Lee.md); from [Michael Hague](../M/Michael_Hague.md)'s delightful paintings in *The Hobbit* to the strange and compelling vision of [Roger Garland](../R/Roger_Garland.md); from the engaging simplicity of [Inger Edelfeldt](../I/Inger_Edelfeldt.md)'s work to the intricacy of dramatic realism of [Ted Nasmith](Ted_Nasmith.md) - Tolkien's World spans the whole of Middle-earth. Each painting is accompanied by a page of text drawn from the book that inspired it, describing the scene the artist has chosen to illustrate. Also included is a short essay by each of the artists about Tolkien's influence upon them.
