**Turuhalmë**, or the **Logdrawing**, was an [Elvish](../E/Elves.md) holiday of [Yule](../Y/Yule.md),<sup id="cite_ref-app_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-app-1">[1]</a></sup> centered on the bringing in of wood to [Mar Vanwa Tyaliéva](../M/Mar_Vanwa_Tyaliéva.md) to feed the [Tale-fire](Tale-fire.md), according to the earliest version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

As described by [Lindo](../L/Lindo.md) in the [Cottage of Lost Play](../C/Cottage_of_Lost_Play.md), it was a day of sports in the snow, and logs were drawn from the woods, followed by singing and drinking, after which tales were told around the [Tale-fire](Tale-fire.md). During this night, [Eriol](../E/Eriol.md) heard the tale of the [Travail of the Noldoli](../G/Gilfanon's_Tale%253A_The_Travail_of_the_Noldoli_and_the_Coming_of_Mankind.md) from [Ailios](../A/Ailios.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Turuhalmë* is [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) for "Logdrawing", from <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Turu&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Turu (page does not exist)">turu</a></i> ("wood; mainly firewood") + *halm* ("drawing"). The [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) cognates, <i><b>Duruchalmo</b></i>(<i><b>s</b></i>) and <i><b>Halm na-dhuruthon</b></i>, were glossed as "[Yule](../Y/Yule.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-app_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-app-1">[1]</a></sup> In the *[Gnomish Lexicon](../G/Gnomish_Lexicon.md)*, it is said that the last term referred to the morning following the Yule night: *Durufui* or *Tonfui*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Tolkien also considered the terms *Halmadhurwion* and *Duruchalm* in a note, but struck out *Duruchalm*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
