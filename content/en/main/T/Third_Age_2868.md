**Third Age 2868** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2868**) is the 2868th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2868 was also known as **S.R. 1268** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Orgulas Brandybuck](../O/Orgulas_Brandybuck.md), fourth child and younger son of [Marmadoc Brandybuck](../M/Marmadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Adaldrida Bolger](../A/Adaldrida_Bolger.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
