Famous **Twins** had a significant role in the history of [Arda](../A/Arda.md), both in [Elven](../E/Elves.md), [Half-elven](../H/Half-elven.md), and [Mannish](../M/Men.md) lines.

## Known twins {#Known_twins}

### Elven and Half-elven {#Elven_and_Half-elven}

- [Amrod](../A/Amrod.md) and [Amras](../A/Amras.md)
- [Eluréd](../E/Eluréd.md) and [Elurín](../E/Elurín.md)
- [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) and [Elros](../E/Elros.md)
- [Elrohir](../E/Elrohir.md) and [Elladan](../E/Elladan.md)

### Mannish {#Mannish}

- [Haleth](../H/Haleth.md) and [Haldar](../H/Haldar.md)
- [Folcred](../F/Folcred.md) and [Fastred](<../F/Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine).md>)

## Elvish language {#Elvish_language}

In [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), the term was *ónoni* (sing. *onóna* "twin born", "one of a pair of twins"). The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) cognate is *[gwanûr](../G/Gwanûr.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>
