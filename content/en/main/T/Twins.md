Famous **Twins** had a significant role in the history of [Arda](/wiki/Arda), both in [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves"), [Half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven), and [Mannish](/wiki/Men "Men") lines.

## Known twins {#Known_twins}

### Elven and Half-elven {#Elven_and_Half-elven}

- [Amrod](/wiki/Amrod) and [Amras](/wiki/Amras)
- [Eluréd](/wiki/Elur%C3%A9d) and [Elurín](/wiki/Elur%C3%ADn)
- [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and [Elros](/wiki/Elros)
- [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir) and [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan)

### Mannish {#Mannish}

- [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth) and [Haldar](/wiki/Haldar)
- [Folcred](/wiki/Folcred) and [Fastred](</wiki/Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine)> "Fastred (son of Folcwine)")

## Elvish language {#Elvish_language}

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), the term was *ónoni* (sing. *onóna* "twin born", "one of a pair of twins"). The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) cognate is *[gwanûr](/wiki/Gwan%C3%BBr "Gwanûr")*.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>
