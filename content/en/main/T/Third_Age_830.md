**Third Age 830** (abbreviated to **T.A. 830**) is the 830th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Siriondil](<../S/Siriondil_(King_of_Gondor).md>) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppSouth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppSouth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Siriondil](<../S/Siriondil_(King_of_Gondor).md>)'s son, [Falastur](Tarannon_Falastur.md), becomes the twelfth [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) and begins the line of [Ship-kings](../S/Ship-kings.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-2">[2]</a></sup>
