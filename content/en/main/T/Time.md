**Time** is the life of [Eä](../E/Eä.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Timeless Halls](Timeless_Halls.md) and the [Void](../V/Void.md) are implied to be outside Time. The Time began with Eä and the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) descending to [Arda](../A/Arda.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ain_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ain-3">[3]</a></sup> Time perhaps represents the sequence of the chords sung by [Eru](../E/Eru.md) in the [Ainulindalë](../A/Ainulindalë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The **Count of Time** begun with the first blooming of the [Two Trees of Valinor](Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md) at the [Opening Hour](../O/Opening_Hour.md) (see further: *[Years of the Trees](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md)*).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The expression **Deeps of Time** probably loosely refers to the vast extent of Time<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> in the unfathomable past or future, and each specific point is an immeasurable space in the Deeps of Time.<sup id="cite_ref-ain_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ain-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an excerpt related to *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, Tolkien introduced some [Ainur](../A/Ainur.md) who represented the concept of time, and presented themselves to [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md): **[Aluin](../A/Aluin.md)** was the eldest of the Ainur and represented Time itself, and his children were [Fanuin](../F/Fanuin.md) (year), [Ranuin](../R/Ranuin.md) (month) and [Danuin](../D/Danuin.md) (day).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The excerpt however was soon rejected and the concept was not developed.

While writing drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, Tolkien considered that Time is [magically](../M/Magic.md) "frozen", or passes in a different pace in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) , and the calendar didn't advance while the [Company of the Ring](../C/Company_of_the_Ring.md) was staying there; however he changed his mind later,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> with the characters just losing some sense of time, which continued to flow normally.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> <span></span>
