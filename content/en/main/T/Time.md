**Time** is the life of [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Timeless Halls](/wiki/Timeless_Halls) and the [Void](/wiki/Void) are implied to be outside Time. The Time began with Eä and the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) descending to [Arda](/wiki/Arda).<sup id="cite_ref-ain_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ain-3">[3]</a></sup> Time perhaps represents the sequence of the chords sung by [Eru](/wiki/Eru) in the [Ainulindalë](/wiki/Ainulindal%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The **Count of Time** begun with the first blooming of the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor) at the [Opening Hour](/wiki/Opening_Hour) (see further: *[Years of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees)*).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The expression **Deeps of Time** probably loosely refers to the vast extent of Time<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> in the unfathomable past or future, and each specific point is an immeasurable space in the Deeps of Time.<sup id="cite_ref-ain_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ain-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an excerpt related to *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, Tolkien introduced some [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) who represented the concept of time, and presented themselves to [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB): **[Aluin](/wiki/Aluin)** was the eldest of the Ainur and represented Time itself, and his children were [Fanuin](/wiki/Fanuin) (year), [Ranuin](/wiki/Ranuin) (month) and [Danuin](/wiki/Danuin) (day).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The excerpt however was soon rejected and the concept was not developed.

While writing drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, Tolkien considered that Time is [magically](/wiki/Magic "Magic") "frozen", or passes in a different pace in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) , and the calendar didn't advance while the [Company of the Ring](/wiki/Company_of_the_Ring) was staying there; however he changed his mind later,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> with the characters just losing some sense of time, which continued to flow normally.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> <span></span>
