<b><i>Tolkien Studies</i>: Volume 12</b>, published in [2015](../_/2015.md), is the twelfth volume of the annual review *[Tolkien Studies](<Tolkien_Studies_(journal).md>)*. From this point on the journal is printed with softcover instead of hardcover, along with a more striking cover design.

This issue reprints an obituary written by J.R.R. Tolkien in [1923](../_/1923.md), a memorial to his senior Henry Bradley.

## Contents {#Contents}

- Articles
    - Kelley M. Wickham-Crowley, "'Mind to Mind': Tolkien's Faërian Drama and the Middle English *[Sir Orfeo](<../S/Sir_Orfeo_(booklet).md>)"*
    - Kris Swank, "The Irish Otherworld Voyage of [Roverandom](../R/Roverandom.md)"
    - Simon Cook, "The Peace of Frodo: On the Origin of an English Mythology"
    - Carrol Fry, "'Two Musics about the Throne of Ilúvatar': Gnostic and Manichaean Dualism in *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)*"
    - Alban Gautier, "From Dejection in Winter to Victory in Spring: Aragorn and Alfred, Parallel Episodes?"
    - Sherrylyn Branchaw, "Boromir: Breaker of the Fellowship?"

- Notes and Documents
    - J.R.R. Tolkien, "Henry Bradley, 3 Dec. 1845-23 May 1923," an obituary for his supervisor at the *[Oxford English Dictionary](../O/Oxford_English_Dictionary.md)*, with commentary by Tom Shippey and Peter Gilliver

- Book Reviews
    - *[Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary](../B/Beowulf%253A_A_Translation_and_Commentary.md) together with Sellic Spell*, by J.R.R. Tolkien, edited by Christopher Tolkien, reviewed by Michael D.C. Drout
    - *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md) and Other Verses from the Red Book*, by J.R.R. Tolkien, edited by Christina Scull and Wayne G. Hammond, reviewed by Richard C. West
    - *[A Companion to J.R.R. Tolkien](../A/A_Companion_to_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*, edited by Stuart D. Lee, reviewed by Jorge Luis Bueno-Alonso
    - *[Tolkien: The Forest and the City](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_the_Forest_and_the_City.md)*, edited by Helen Conrad-O'Briain and Gerard Hynes, reviewed by Patrick Curry
    - *[Tolkien](<Tolkien_(2014_book).md>)*, by Raymond Edwards, reviewed by David Bratman

- Merlin DeTardo, Jason Fisher, David Bratman, Marjorie Burns, John Wm. Houghton, and John Magoun, "The Year's Work in Tolkien Studies 2012"
- Rebecca Epstein and David Bratman, "Bibliography (in English) for 2013"
