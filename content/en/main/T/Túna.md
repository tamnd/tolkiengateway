**Túna** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈtuːna]</span>) was the green hill in [Aman](/wiki/Aman), raised by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) within the [Calacirya](/wiki/Calacirya), upon which the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) and the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) founded their fair city of [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion). In the [Years of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees) the light of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor") fell upon its western face and its shadow lay ever to the east, reaching [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar) and [the Sea](/wiki/The_Sea "The Sea").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

When [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) came to Aman the people of the city had gone to a festival in [Valimar](/wiki/Valimar) so the city was empty. Fearing some evil had befallen the Blessed Realm he turned away until [Eönwë](/wiki/E%C3%B6nw%C3%AB), the herald of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB), called to him and invited him to Valimar.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

A host of [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) camped around Túna when [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n) invaded Aman.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early versions it was known as **Tûn**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
