**Third Age 1711** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1711**) is the 1711th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1711 was also known as **S.R. 111** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Araval](../A/Araval.md), son of [Arveleg II](../A/Arveleg_II.md) of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
