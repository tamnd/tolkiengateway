**Tolfalas** (also **Tol Falas**<sup id="cite_ref-Index_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) was a large island that stood beyond the [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md) in the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The storms and waves that followed the [Drowning of Númenor](../D/Drowning_of_Númenor.md) almost destroyed Tolfalas and left it like a barren and lonely mountain in the water not far from the new mouths of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), because the east of the bay of Belfalas was filled with new land and the river Anduin had to carve a new path by many river mouths to the bay. Before the Drowning of Númenor the city of [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) had been only a few miles from the sea and was far inland afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-Second_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-4">[4]</a></sup>

Ships that wanted to sail up the Anduin from the Bay of Belfalas passed Tolfalas on its eastern side and then took a canal that had been made by the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) in the middle of the delta of the Anduin.<sup id="cite_ref-Index_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Tol Falas* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Island of the Shore", being a clear combination of *[tol](Tol.md)* ("island") and *[falas](../F/Falas.md)* ("coast").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The spelling *Tolfalas* is used in an earlier typescript version of what would later become [Appendix A](../A/Appendix_A.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Second_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-4">[4]</a></sup> in an outline that was made during the writing of *[The Two Towers](The_Two_Towers.md)* where it is mentioned that the [Men of Harad](../M/Men_of_Harad.md) (then called the Men of Harrowland and by other names) had taken the island<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and on the [First Map of *The Lord of the Rings*](../F/First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
