**Third Age 1191** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1191**) is the 1191st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Arthedain.md) [Celepharn](../C/Celepharn.md) of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppNorth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppNorth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Celepharn](../C/Celepharn.md)'s son, [Celebrindor](../C/Celebrindor.md), becomes the fifth [King](../K/Kings_of_Arthedain.md) of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppNorth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppNorth-1">[1]</a></sup>
