**Tar-Elendil** was the fourth [King](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Elendil_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> He was the eldest son of [Tar-Amandil](Tar-Amandil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-earlier_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-earlier-2">[2]</a></sup> Tar-Elendil was also called **[Parmaitë](../P/Parmaitë.md)**, because he made many books from the legends and lore gathered by his grandfather [Vardamir Nólimon](../V/Vardamir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Elendil_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During Tar-Elendil's reign, in the year [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [600](../S/Second_Age_600.md) [Vëantur](../V/Vëantur.md), the [Captain of the King's Ships](../C/Captain_of_the_King's_Ships.md), sailed his ship *[Entulessë](../E/Entulessë.md)* (which means "Return") to the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md), the first [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) ship that returned [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The Númenórean mariners were welcomed by [Ereinion Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) and a friendship and alliance between Númenor and the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) of [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) began. When news of the arrival of a ship with men reached Men of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) who were of the same stock as the Peoples of [Hador](../H/Hador.md) and [Bëor](../B/Bëor.md), they asked Gil-galad if they could meet the shipmen. Twelve Men of Eriador met the Númenóreans at the [Tower Hills](Tower_Hills.md) and the two groups were able to converse haltingly, because their languages were related.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Vëantur's daughter, [Almarian](../A/Almarian.md), married [Tar-Meneldur](Tar-Meneldur.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and their son [Aldarion](Tar-Aldarion.md) inherited his grandfather's love of the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Tar-Elendil had three children: two daughters, [Silmariën](../S/Silmariën.md) and [Isilmë](../I/Isilmë.md); and one son, [Írimon](../_/Írimon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-earlier_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-earlier-2">[2]</a></sup> Silmariën was the eldest child, but because of the law of <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnatic_primogeniture" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">agnatic primogeniture</a> that existed in Númenor at that time,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> her brother Írimon inherited the Sceptre and ruled as [Tar-Meneldur](Tar-Meneldur.md). Silmariën became important in her own right as the link of the [Lords of Andúnië](../L/Lords_of_Andúnië.md) to the royal house; her son was [Valandil](<../V/Valandil_(Lord_of_Andúnië).md>), the first Lord of Andúnië.<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Elendil_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> Furthermore, Tar-Elendil also passed the [Ring of Barahir](../R/Ring_of_Barahir.md) down to Silmariën instead of his son<sup id="cite_ref-3a_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3a-3">[3]</a></sup>.; this choice, over *many* generations, allowed [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) to give the ring to [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) in [Lórien](../L/Lórien.md) in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2980](Third_Age_2980.md) on their engagement.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Elendil is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). It means "Elf-friend" or "Star-lover".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> The meaning "Elf-friend" was common among the [Edain](../E/Edain.md). Properly in Quenya it meant "a lover or student of the stars". "Elf-friend" in proper Quenya would be represented by Eldandil.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> The name Elendil is a compound of *[elen](../E/Elen.md)* ("star") and *[-(n)dil](../_/-ndil.md)* ("friend, lover, devoted to"). Like all the rulers of Númenor who took their royal names in Quenya, Elendil added the prefix *tar-* ("high") to his name when he received the Sceptre.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

His sobriquet *Parmaitë* is Quenya. It means "Book-handed"<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> or "Book Handy".<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

If he had the same [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) name as his distant descendent [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md), Tar-Elendil's [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) name may have been "Ar-<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Nimruz%C3%AEr&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nimruzîr (page does not exist)">Nimruzîr</a>", although this is not mentioned by Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>
