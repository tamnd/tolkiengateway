**Tar-Elendil** was the fourth [King](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Elendil_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> He was the eldest son of [Tar-Amandil](/wiki/Tar-Amandil).<sup id="cite_ref-earlier_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-earlier-2">[2]</a></sup> Tar-Elendil was also called **[Parmaitë](/wiki/Parmait%C3%AB)**, because he made many books from the legends and lore gathered by his grandfather [Vardamir Nólimon](/wiki/Vardamir "Vardamir").<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Elendil_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During Tar-Elendil's reign, in the year [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [600](/wiki/Second_Age_600 "Second Age 600") [Vëantur](/wiki/V%C3%ABantur), the [Captain of the King's Ships](/wiki/Captain_of_the_King%27s_Ships), sailed his ship *[Entulessë](/wiki/Entuless%C3%AB)* (which means "Return") to the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens), the first [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") ship that returned [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The Númenórean mariners were welcomed by [Ereinion Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad "Gil-galad"), [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor) and a friendship and alliance between Númenor and the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) began. When news of the arrival of a ship with men reached Men of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) who were of the same stock as the Peoples of [Hador](/wiki/Hador) and [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor), they asked Gil-galad if they could meet the shipmen. Twelve Men of Eriador met the Númenóreans at the [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills) and the two groups were able to converse haltingly, because their languages were related.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Vëantur's daughter, [Almarian](/wiki/Almarian), married [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and their son [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") inherited his grandfather's love of the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Tar-Elendil had three children: two daughters, [Silmariën](/wiki/Silmari%C3%ABn) and [Isilmë](/wiki/Isilm%C3%AB); and one son, [Írimon](/wiki/%C3%8Drimon).<sup id="cite_ref-earlier_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-earlier-2">[2]</a></sup> Silmariën was the eldest child, but because of the law of <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnatic_primogeniture" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">agnatic primogeniture</a> that existed in Númenor at that time,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> her brother Írimon inherited the Sceptre and ruled as [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur). Silmariën became important in her own right as the link of the [Lords of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB) to the royal house; her son was [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)> "Valandil (Lord of Andúnië)"), the first Lord of Andúnië.<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Elendil_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> Furthermore, Tar-Elendil also passed the [Ring of Barahir](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir) down to Silmariën instead of his son<sup id="cite_ref-3a_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3a-3">[3]</a></sup>.; this choice, over *many* generations, allowed [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) to give the ring to [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) in [Lórien](/wiki/L%C3%B3rien) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2980](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980") on their engagement.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Elendil is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). It means "Elf-friend" or "Star-lover".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> The meaning "Elf-friend" was common among the [Edain](/wiki/Edain). Properly in Quenya it meant "a lover or student of the stars". "Elf-friend" in proper Quenya would be represented by Eldandil.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> The name Elendil is a compound of *[elen](/wiki/Elen "Elen")* ("star") and *[-(n)dil](/wiki/-ndil "-ndil")* ("friend, lover, devoted to"). Like all the rulers of Númenor who took their royal names in Quenya, Elendil added the prefix *tar-* ("high") to his name when he received the Sceptre.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

His sobriquet *Parmaitë* is Quenya. It means "Book-handed"<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> or "Book Handy".<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

If he had the same [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name as his distant descendent [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), Tar-Elendil's [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name may have been "Ar-<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nimruz%C3%AEr&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nimruzîr (page does not exist)">Nimruzîr</a>", although this is not mentioned by Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>
