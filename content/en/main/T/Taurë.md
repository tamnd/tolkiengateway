<i><b>taure</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "forest".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

It has been suggested that a standardized spelling of the word would be *taurë*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): *[taur](Taur.md)*

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Tauremorna](Tauremorna.md)*
- ?*[Tauron](../O/Oromë.md#Etymology)*

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Etymologies](The_Etymologies.md)*, Quenya *taure* is glossed as "great wood, forest".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
