**The Prancing Pony** was a large inn and important gathering place at [Bree](../B/Bree.md).

## Description {#Description}

The inn was located just where the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) bent round the foot of [Bree-hill](../B/Bree-hill.md), within the dike that stretched around the town. The building was three stories tall with many windows. Its front faced the Road and it had two wings that ran back towards the elevated ground of the hill, such that in the rear the second floor was at ground level.

Between the wings was a courtyard that was accessible through an archway. Under the arch a few steps on the left led to main door. Above the arch was a lamp that illuminated the sign beneath, which showed a fat pony rearing on its hind legs. A white-lettered inscription over the door announced "The Prancing Pony by [Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md)".

In the north wing there were rooms on the ground floor that had been especially built for [hobbit](../H/Hobbit.md) guests. The centre of activity was the big common-room where drinks were served near a cheery log fire,<sup id="cite_ref-Pony_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pony-1">[1]</a></sup> and lanterns were hanging on the roof beams.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/M/Matěj_Čadil_-_The_Prancing_Pony.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/58/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_The_Prancing_Pony.jpg/300px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_The_Prancing_Pony.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="402" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/58/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_The_Prancing_Pony.jpg/450px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_The_Prancing_Pony.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/58/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_The_Prancing_Pony.jpg/600px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_The_Prancing_Pony.jpg 2x" data-file-width="896" data-file-height="1200"/></a><figcaption><i>The Prancing Pony</i> by <a href="../M/Matěj_Čadil.md" title="Matěj Čadil">Matěj Čadil</a></figcaption></figure>

The Prancing Pony had been built long before the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), when traffic on the roads had been much greater. Despite diminished numbers of visitors the inn had remained an important centre of news, since Bree was at the crossroads of the East Road and the [Greenway](../G/Greenway.md), which came up from the south and went onwards to [Fornost](../F/Fornost_Erain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Pony_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pony-1">[1]</a></sup>

The inn had been kept by the [Butterbur Family](../B/Butterbur_Family.md) for generations as a convivial gathering place for [Men](../M/Men.md), [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), and Hobbits (both those living in Bree and travellers from [the Shire](The_Shire.md)). [Tobold Hornblower](Tobold_Hornblower.md) was one of the Shire Hobbits who visited Bree and probably the Prancing Pony, where he learned about [Pipe-weed](../P/Pipe-weed.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Weed_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Weed-3">[3]</a></sup> Around [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2670](Third_Age_2670.md) Old Toby brought back samples of the plant to the [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-4">[4]</a></sup> where it became a major product of Hobbit agriculture, but the centre of the "art" of smoking remained at the old inn.<sup id="cite_ref-Weed_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Weed-3">[3]</a></sup>

It was perhaps in the *Pony* where the [Wizard](../W/Wizards.md) [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) met Dwarven [King-in-Exile](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Thorin](Thorin.md) [Oakenshield](../O/Oakenshield.md) in the spring of [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2941](Third_Age_2941.md). That meeting set the stage for the [Quest of Erebor](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UT9_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT9-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup> When [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and the [dwarves](Thorin_and_Company.md) travelled eastward on their journey to the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) later that year,<sup id="cite_ref-TA_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-4">[4]</a></sup> it is very probable that they stayed at the Prancing Pony, although Bilbo did not specifically mention it in his memoir.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 2]</a></sup>

At the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the inn was owned and managed by Barliman Butterbur, a man who was somewhat absent-minded at times. He had two hobbit assistants, [Nob](../N/Nob.md) and [Bob](../B/Bob.md). In [Mid-year's Day](../M/Mid-year's_Day.md) [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3018](Third_Age_3018.md) the inn was once again visited by Gandalf, who left Barliman [a letter](../G/Gandalf's_letter.md) for a [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) in [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md). Barliman did not find anyone willing to bring it to the Shire, and it was soon forgotten.<sup id="cite_ref-Strider_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strider-8">[6]</a></sup>

On the last [Monday](../M/Monday.md) of [September](../S/September.md) [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3018](Third_Age_3018.md)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[note 3]</a></sup> two "[black men](../N/Nazgûl.md)" knocked, asking Nob for one [Baggins](../B/Baggins_family.md). Barliman noticed that their presence made the dogs and geese of the inn noisy and terrified Nob. He slammed the door on them, and later heard that they continued all the way to [Archet](../A/Archet.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Strider_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strider-8">[6]</a></sup>

The [night before](../_/28_September.md) Frodo Baggins and his companions arrived another party (of men) came to the inn, travelling up the [Greenway](../G/Greenway.md) from the South.<sup id="cite_ref-Pony_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pony-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [29 September](../_/29_September.md) [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3018](Third_Age_3018.md), Frodo, [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), and [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) arrived at Bree and came to the Prancing Pony.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[7]</a></sup> The hobbits had followed Gandalf's instructions and expected to meet him there, but the Wizard was nowhere to be found. After supper in their room all except Merry went to the big common-room. It was here that "[Mr. Underhill](../M/Mr._Underhill.md)" (Frodo) first met [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) and where [the One Ring](The_One_Ring.md) got onto his finger when he fell off of a table.<sup id="cite_ref-Pony_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pony-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the parlour of their rooms the hobbits met Strider, who eventually (aided by Gandalf's letter that was now brought in by Barliman) convinced Frodo that he was a friend who could guide them. When they learned that Merry had seen a [Black Rider](../N/Nazgûl.md) within Bree they abandoned their lodgings and spent the night in Strider's room.<sup id="cite_ref-Strider_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strider-8">[6]</a></sup>

In the early hours of [30 September](../_/30_September.md) the Hobbits' room was attacked, although no one heard the intruders. It was also discovered that the hobbits' ponies and the horses of all of the other guests had been driven off. While new mounts were being found the travellers managed to get a breakfast at the inn. Many hours later, Frodo, Sam, Merry, and Pippin left the Prancing Pony, guided by Strider.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[8]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/M/Miriam_Ellis_-_Homeward_Bound_from_The_Prancing_Pony_Inn.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7a/Miriam_Ellis_-_Homeward_Bound_from_The_Prancing_Pony_Inn.jpg/250px-Miriam_Ellis_-_Homeward_Bound_from_The_Prancing_Pony_Inn.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="208" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7a/Miriam_Ellis_-_Homeward_Bound_from_The_Prancing_Pony_Inn.jpg/375px-Miriam_Ellis_-_Homeward_Bound_from_The_Prancing_Pony_Inn.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7a/Miriam_Ellis_-_Homeward_Bound_from_The_Prancing_Pony_Inn.jpg/500px-Miriam_Ellis_-_Homeward_Bound_from_The_Prancing_Pony_Inn.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1000" data-file-height="830"/></a><figcaption><i>Homeward Bound from The Prancing Pony Inn</i> by <a href="../M/Miriam_Ellis.md" title="Miriam Ellis">Miriam Ellis</a></figcaption></figure>

That same night, Gandalf, who was trying to catch up with Frodo, came to the inn. He was furious with Barliman's forgetfulness, who was very worried when he saw the hobbits leaving with the suspicious Ranger; but that made Gandalf forgive him as the Hobbits were alive and in good hands, and put a blessing on his beer. He left the [next morning](../_/1_October.md) for [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md).

On [28 October](../_/28_October.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md), Gandalf and the hobbits returned to Bree and stayed at the Prancing Pony.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[9]</a></sup> There they learned of the troubles Bree had had since their departure over a year before. In return they told Barliman of their adventures and the news that the King had returned, who was none other than Strider. Best of all for Sam was the news the [Bill the Pony](../B/Bill_the_Pony.md) had returned safely there.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In 1960 [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) began a complete rewrite of *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* in which he sought to refashion the story in the same "epic" mode as *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. As part of this refashioning, Tolkien added elements that would tie the landscape of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) seen by Bilbo to that seen by Frodo. The stopping-place in Bree was specifically mentioned: "There \[Bree\] they spent their last comfortable night for many a day to come, in the great inn of Bree, the Prancing Pony, well-known to the hobbits of the east side of the Shire. "<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[11]</a></sup> In this version of the story Tolkien also sought to maintain an exact timeline, and the arrival at Bree occurred on [2 May](../_/2_May.md) [2941](Third_Age_2941.md).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[12]</a></sup> Eventually though Tolkien abandoned this effort to rewrite Bilbo's tale.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

According to research conducted by a branch of [the Tolkien Society](The_Tolkien_Society.md), the Bell Inn in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moreton-in-Marsh" title="wikipedia:Moreton-in-Marsh">Moreton-in-Marsh</a> was a source of inspiration when [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) created the Prancing Pony, since the former also has three storeys and an "entrance via a courtyard, and Moreton's similarities to the town of Bree".<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[13]</a></sup>

The inn is visited by several <a href="../B/Bree-landers.md" title="Bree-landers">Bree-landers</a>, including <a href="../O/Otto_Aster.md" title="Otto Aster">Otto Aster</a> and <a href="Tedder_Hedgerow.md" title="Tedder Hedgerow">Tedder Hedgerow</a>, and outlanders.<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-19">[16]</a></sup><br/>According to Barliman Butterbur, the inn has existed almost as long as Bree has and has been in the possession of the <a href="../B/Butterbur_Family.md" title="Butterbur Family">Butterbur Family</a> for many generations.<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-19">[16]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2013.md" title="2013">2013</a>: <i><a href="The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Prancing Pony appears in the opening scene of the film. It is the place where Gandalf and Thorin first meet and make their plans to restore <a href="../L/Lonely_Mountain.md" title="Lonely Mountain">Erebor</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In <a href="Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="Third_Age_2965.md" title="Third Age 2965">2965</a>, Barnabas Butterbur was the innkeeper.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[17]</a></sup></dd></dl>
