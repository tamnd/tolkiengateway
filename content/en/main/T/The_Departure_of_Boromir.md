**The Departure of Boromir** is the first chapter of the first book in *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*.

## Summary {#Summary}

The camp having been attacked by [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) found [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) mortally wounded. Before Boromir died, he told Aragorn that the Orcs had carried off the other [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits). The remaining members of the party (Aragorn, [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli)) searched through the bodies of dead Orcs and found many weapons, as well as the blades of [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"). Because they did not have time to bury him or build a cairn, they put Boromir's body into one of the boats gifted to the fellowship in Lothlórien and pushed it off into the flow of the Anduin, while Legolas and Aragorn sang a beautiful [lament](/wiki/Song_of_Boromir "Song of Boromir") for Boromir, taking the voices of three of the winds.

Aragorn told Gimli and Legolas that he thought [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") had gone alone to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and that they themselves should follow the Orcs and not Frodo. So saying, these adventurers continued on their journey, travelling in search of their missing companions.
