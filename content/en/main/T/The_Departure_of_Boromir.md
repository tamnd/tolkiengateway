**The Departure of Boromir** is the first chapter of the first book in *[The Two Towers](The_Two_Towers.md)*.

## Summary {#Summary}

The camp having been attacked by [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) found [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) mortally wounded. Before Boromir died, he told Aragorn that the Orcs had carried off the other [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md). The remaining members of the party (Aragorn, [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md)) searched through the bodies of dead Orcs and found many weapons, as well as the blades of [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md). Because they did not have time to bury him or build a cairn, they put Boromir's body into one of the boats gifted to the fellowship in Lothlórien and pushed it off into the flow of the Anduin, while Legolas and Aragorn sang a beautiful [lament](../S/Song_of_Boromir.md) for Boromir, taking the voices of three of the winds.

Aragorn told Gimli and Legolas that he thought [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) had gone alone to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) and that they themselves should follow the Orcs and not Frodo. So saying, these adventurers continued on their journey, travelling in search of their missing companions.

## Composition {#Composition}

The chapter was probably written at the end of [1941](../_/1941.md) and the start of [1942](../_/1942.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
