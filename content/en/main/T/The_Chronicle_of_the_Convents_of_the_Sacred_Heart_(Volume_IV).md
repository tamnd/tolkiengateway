<b><i>The Chronicle of the Convents of the Sacred Heart</i> (Volume IV)</b> was a literary publication; it was perhaps issued by the Roman Catholic school "Convent of the Sacred Heart" that was located in Roehampton in the 1930s (currently renamed <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woldingham_School" title="wikipedia:Woldingham School">Woldingham School</a> and situated in Surrey).

The fourth volume includes [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[Firiel](</wiki/Firiel_(poem)> "Firiel (poem)")" (pp. 30–2). The poem is illustrated by an unidentified artist.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
