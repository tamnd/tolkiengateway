<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: Legends of Middle-earth</b></i> was an app-based game inspired by *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. It was released on 16 October 16, 2014, and permanently shut down on 10 March 10, 2016.
