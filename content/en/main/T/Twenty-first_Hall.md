The **Twenty-first Hall** was a great hall on the [Seventh Level](../S/Seventh_Level.md) in the [North End](../N/North_End.md) of the [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) realm of [Moria](../M/Moria.md).

<br> [Balin's Colony](../B/Balin's_Colony~64118.md) reclaimed the Hall from the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md). There is a mention that there was a "good air" and "the shaft was clear", and allowed the Dwarves to watch their surroundings.

Years later the [Fellowship](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) camped in the hall for the night on their trek through Moria. [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) was able to identify the hall after the Fellowship had found the [Chamber of Mazarbul](../C/Chamber_of_Mazarbul.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
