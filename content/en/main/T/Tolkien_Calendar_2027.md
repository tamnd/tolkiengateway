The <i><b>Tolkien Calendar 2027: The Hobbit 90th Anniversary</b></i> is the official Tolkien calendar for [2027](/wiki/2027), published by William Morrow Paperbacks, an imprint of [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins), on [11 August](/wiki/11_August) [2026](/wiki/2026). It features thirteen paintings by a variety of artists based on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and a introduction by [Brian Sibley](/wiki/Brian_Sibley).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

> **The official Tolkien calendar, this year containing 13 stunning illustrations and paintings inspired by [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit) - featuring work by [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee), [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith), [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes), and for the first time [Maurice Sendak](/wiki/Maurice_Sendak)!**
>
> [The official Tolkien calendar](/wiki/Category:Official_calendars "Category:Official calendars") has become an established publishing event, eagerly anticipated by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") fans the world over. Previous Tolkien calendars have become much sought after collector's items, and this year's will doubtless be an essential purchase for [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) fans everywhere.
>
> Featuring 13 full-color paintings of scenes inspired by the story, some of which are unique to this calendar, a world-famous roll call of artists including [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee), [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith) and [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes) explores [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s beloved work of fantasy. Published for the first time, this calendar also includes a sketch by [Maurice Sendak](/wiki/Maurice_Sendak), creator of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Where_the_Wild_Things_Are" title="wikipedia:Where the Wild Things Are">Where the Wild Things Are</a>.
>
> With a variety of artistic styles on display, this year's calendar is a celebration of the remarkable influence [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") and his work has had on artists around the world since its publication, inspiring a creative legacy that has endured across the generations.
