The <i><b>Tolkien Calendar 2027: The Hobbit 90th Anniversary</b></i> is the official Tolkien calendar for [2027](../_/2027.md), published by William Morrow Paperbacks, an imprint of [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md), on [11 August](../_/11_August.md) [2026](../_/2026.md). It features thirteen paintings by a variety of artists based on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* and a introduction by [Brian Sibley](../B/Brian_Sibley.md).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

> **The official Tolkien calendar, this year containing 13 stunning illustrations and paintings inspired by [The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md) - featuring work by [Alan Lee](../A/Alan_Lee.md), [Ted Nasmith](Ted_Nasmith.md), [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md), and for the first time [Maurice Sendak](../M/Maurice_Sendak.md)!**
>
> [The official Tolkien calendar](../../category/O/Official_calendars.md) has become an established publishing event, eagerly anticipated by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) fans the world over. Previous Tolkien calendars have become much sought after collector's items, and this year's will doubtless be an essential purchase for [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) fans everywhere.
>
> Featuring 13 full-color paintings of scenes inspired by the story, some of which are unique to this calendar, a world-famous roll call of artists including [Alan Lee](../A/Alan_Lee.md), [Ted Nasmith](Ted_Nasmith.md) and [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md) explores [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s beloved work of fantasy. Published for the first time, this calendar also includes a sketch by [Maurice Sendak](../M/Maurice_Sendak.md), creator of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Where_the_Wild_Things_Are" title="wikipedia:Where the Wild Things Are">Where the Wild Things Are</a>.
>
> With a variety of artistic styles on display, this year's calendar is a celebration of the remarkable influence [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and his work has had on artists around the world since its publication, inspiring a creative legacy that has endured across the generations.
