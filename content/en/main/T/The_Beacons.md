**The Beacons** is a [Fan Film](/wiki/Category:Fan_films "Category:Fan films") produced by a group of French Lord of the Rings enthusiasts. They shot the short film in Auvergne, France in 2024<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was released on 2 October 2025

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

Borlas, a soldier of Gondor, is alerted: the beacons are lit. Danger lurks. After lighting his own, he realizes the next one does not ignite. The chain is broken. The kingdom is in peril.
