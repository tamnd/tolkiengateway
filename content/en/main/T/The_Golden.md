"**The Golden**" was a title held by the [Dragons](../D/Dragons.md) [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and of [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the last [King](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). <span></span>
