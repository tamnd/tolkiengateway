"**The Golden**" was a title held by the [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons) [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and of [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the last [King](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). <span></span>
