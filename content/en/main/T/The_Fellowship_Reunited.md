**The Fellowship Reunited** is the fifty-eighth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* and the seventy-third scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) slowly awakes in a white bed, bathed in light. In amazement and disbelief, he finds himself looking at [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> The wizard and the hobbit both laugh joyfully, and [Merry](/wiki/Merry) and [Pippin](/wiki/Pippin) rush in to the room and jump on Frodo's bed to hug him, thrilled to see him awake. [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), and [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) join Frodo, reuniting the remaining members of the [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring").

Last of all, [Sam](/wiki/Sam) enters, standing in the doorway. As Frodo sees him, the two hobbits share a long, meaningful look, and simply smile at each other.

## Differences {#Differences}

The events in this scene correspond to parts of the chapter [The Field of Cormallen](/wiki/The_Field_of_Cormallen). In the books, the events are told from the point-of-view of Sam, who wakes up in a bed in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) next to Frodo, with Gandalf watching over them. He had been asleep and awoke on "the [eighth day of April](/wiki/8_April "8 April") in the [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning)", according to Gandalf. The two hobbits are reunited with the rest of the surviving members of the Fellowship in the woods, where Aragorn bows before them and praises them<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> (much like he does in the following scene, [The Return of the King](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(scene)> "The Return of the King (scene)")).
