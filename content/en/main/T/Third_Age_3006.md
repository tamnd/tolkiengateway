**Third Age 3006** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3006**) is the 3006th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 3006 was also known as **S.R. 1406** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) continues visiting [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) at intervals.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Dora Baggins](../D/Dora_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
