**Third Age 2948** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2948**) is the 2948th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2948 was also known as **S.R. 1348** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Théoden](Théoden.md) in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), son of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Thengel](Thengel.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Seredic Brandybuck](../S/Seredic_Brandybuck.md), son of [Saradas Brandybuck](../S/Saradas_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Porto Baggins](../P/Porto_Baggins.md), second child and son of [Posco Baggins](../P/Posco_Baggins.md) and [Gilly Brownlock](../G/Gilly_Brownlock.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Death of [Donnamira Took](../D/Donnamira_Took.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- Death of [Rudigar Bolger](../R/Rudigar_Bolger.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
