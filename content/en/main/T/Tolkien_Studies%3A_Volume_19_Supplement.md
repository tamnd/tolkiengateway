<b><i>Tolkien Studies</i>: Volume 19 Supplement</b> is the supplement issue to [*Tolkien Studies 19*](Tolkien_Studies%253A_Volume_19.md), published in [2022](../_/2022.md).

This volume publishes the important paper "[The Chronology of The Lord of the Rings](The_Chronology_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)", edited with notes and commentary by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=William_Cloud_Hicklin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="William Cloud Hicklin (page does not exist)">William Cloud Hicklin</a>. The paper presents [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s unpublished manuscripts of the time-scheme, or "Chronology", on which his *The Lord of the Rings* is based.
