**Third Age 2757** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2757**) is the 2757th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2757 was also known as **S.R. 1157** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Argonui](../A/Argonui.md), son of [Chieftain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md) [Arathorn I](../A/Arathorn_I.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
