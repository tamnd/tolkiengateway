**Thimbalt** was probably a fortress of Morgoth in a early version of the Legendarium, located near Angband on the slopes of Thangorodrim. The name only appears on the north part of the [First 'Silmarillion' Map](</wiki/The_First_%27Silmarillion%27_Map_(map)> "The First 'Silmarillion' Map (map)") and nowhere else. Thimbalt was struck out using a pencil on the map.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

While its meaning is unclear, [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack) suggests on [Eldamo](/wiki/Eldamo) that it is a rejected [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
