**Tarlang's Neck** was a pass over a southern mountain spur of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) that separated the [Blackroot Vale](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale) from [Lamedon](/wiki/Lamedon).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

A local legend among the [indigenous people](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Pre-Númenóreans") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) told of [giants](/wiki/Giants "Giants") and one of them called [Tarlang](/wiki/Tarlang) who tripped and broke his neck. The other giants did not clean up his body, which became incorporated in the land instead. The giant's neck became Tarlang's Neck, his head [Dol Tarlang](/wiki/Dol_Tarlang), and the stones he was carrying [Cûl Veleg](/wiki/C%C3%BBl_Veleg) and [Cûl Bîn](/wiki/C%C3%BBl_B%C3%AEn).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Tarlang's Neck formed part of the route by which [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) led the [Army of the Dead](/wiki/Army_of_the_Dead) on their journey to the relief of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tarlang* was the original name of this pass, but it was later taken as a personal name.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup>
