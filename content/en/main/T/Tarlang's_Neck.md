**Tarlang's Neck** was a pass over a southern mountain spur of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) that separated the [Blackroot Vale](../B/Blackroot_Vale.md) from [Lamedon](../L/Lamedon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

A local legend among the [indigenous people](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) told of [giants](../G/Giants.md) and one of them called [Tarlang](Tarlang.md) who tripped and broke his neck. The other giants did not clean up his body, which became incorporated in the land instead. The giant's neck became Tarlang's Neck, his head [Dol Tarlang](../D/Dol_Tarlang.md), and the stones he was carrying [Cûl Veleg](../C/Cûl_Veleg.md) and [Cûl Bîn](../C/Cûl_Bîn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Tarlang's Neck formed part of the route by which [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md) led the [Army of the Dead](../A/Army_of_the_Dead~6352.md) on their journey to the relief of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) in the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tarlang* was the original name of this pass, but it was later taken as a personal name.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup>
