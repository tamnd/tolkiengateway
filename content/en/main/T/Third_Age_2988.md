**Third Age 2988** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2988**) is the 2988th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2988 was also known as **S.R. 1388** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Tosto Boffin](Tosto_Boffin.md), son of [Griffo Boffin](../G/Griffo_Boffin.md) and [Daisy Baggins](../D/Daisy_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Finduilas](<../F/Finduilas_(wife_of_Denethor).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
