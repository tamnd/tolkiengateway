<i><b>The Trilogy</b></i> was a planned video game that covered both *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, designed by a developer under the pseudonym "Smeagol 88". It was developed with Klik &amp; Play, and later with *Multimedia Fusion*, with game-development engines designed by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clickteam" title="wikipedia:Clickteam">Clickteam</a>. Early beta versions of the game could be downloaded but the full game was cancelled in [2000](../_/2000.md) and never released.
