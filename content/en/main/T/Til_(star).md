**Til** (or **Til<sup>(1) </sup>-l-**) is the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) name of a [star](/wiki/Stars "Stars") only appearing in an early linguistic writing by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"). Til is said to be "a small star near [Sirius](/wiki/Helluin "Helluin")".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
