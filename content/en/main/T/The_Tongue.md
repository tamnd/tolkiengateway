**The Tongue** was the narrow strip of land at end of [Egladil](/wiki/Egladil), forming the tip where the [Celebrant](/wiki/Celebrant) flowed into the Great River [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).

The Tongue itself was south-eastern tip of the Angle (or Egladil) in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), specifically the place where the two rivers met.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was a completely treeless, being covered in a green lawn with the bright golden *[elanor](</wiki/Elanor_(flower)> "Elanor (flower)")*. On the southern bank was a landing-place for bets to set out onto the Celebrant and beyond to either river; it was here where [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) held a farewell feast for the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring), before they departed via the Anduin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
