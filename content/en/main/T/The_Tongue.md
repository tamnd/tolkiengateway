**The Tongue** was the narrow strip of land at end of [Egladil](../E/Egladil.md), forming the tip where the [Celebrant](../C/Celebrant.md) flowed into the Great River [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).

The Tongue itself was south-eastern tip of the Angle (or Egladil) in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md), specifically the place where the two rivers met.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was a completely treeless, being covered in a green lawn with the bright golden *[elanor](<../E/Elanor_(flower).md>)*. On the southern bank was a landing-place for bets to set out onto the Celebrant and beyond to either river; it was here where [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) held a farewell feast for the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md), before they departed via the Anduin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
