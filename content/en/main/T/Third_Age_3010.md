**Third Age 3010** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3010**) is the 3010th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 3010 was also known as **S.R. 1410** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Adrahil](../A/Adrahil.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Imrahil](../I/Imrahil.md), son of Adrahil, becomes the twenty-second [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Bruno Bracegirdle](../B/Bruno_Bracegirdle.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
