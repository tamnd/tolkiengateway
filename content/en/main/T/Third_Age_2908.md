**Third Age 2908** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2908**) is the 2908th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2908 was also known as **S.R. 1308** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Drogo Baggins](../D/Drogo_Baggins.md), second child and first son of [Fosco Baggins](../F/Fosco_Baggins.md) and [Ruby Bolger](../R/Ruby_Bolger.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Saradas Brandybuck](../S/Saradas_Brandybuck.md), third child and second son of [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](../G/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Mirabella Took](../M/Mirabella_Took.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Gorbulas Brandybuck](../G/Gorbulas_Brandybuck.md), son of [Orgulas Brandybuck](../O/Orgulas_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-2">[2]</a></sup>
