**The King beneath the mountains** is a poem about the [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This song was sung by the [people](/wiki/Lake-men "Lake-men") of [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town) upon the arrival of [Thorin Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin_Oakenshield) and the [Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company"). It was a prophecy made after the [fall](/wiki/Sack_of_Erebor "Sack of Erebor") of [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor) and [Dale](/wiki/Dale) about [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) and [Thráin](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in).

## Text {#Text}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>The King beneath the mountains,<br/>
    The King of carven stone,<br/>
The lord of silver fountains<br/>
    Shall come into his own!<br/>
<br/>
His crown shall be upholden,<br/>
    His harp shall be restrung,<br/>
His halls shall echo golden<br/>
    To songs of yore re-sung.<br/>
<br/>
The woods shall wave on mountains<br/>
    And grass beneath the sun;<br/>
His wealth shall flow in fountains<br/>
    And the rivers golden run.<br/>
<br/>
The streams shall run in gladness,<br/>
    The lakes shall shine and burn,<br/>
All sorrow fail and sadness<br/>
    At the Mountain-king&#39;s return!
</p>
</div>

## Other {#Other}

Then suddenly a great light appeared in the low place in the hills and the northern end of the lake turned golden.

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>&#34;The King beneath the mountain!&#34;
</p>
</div>

They shouted.

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>    &#34;His wealth is like the Sun,<br/>
His silver like a fountain,<br/>
    His rivers golden run!&#34;
</p>
</div>

They cried, and everywhere windows were opening and feet were hurrying.

<b>1985: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1985_television_film)" title="The Hobbit (1985 television film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1985 television film)</a></b>

<dl><dd>The first three verses are sang in the movie, with considerable changes from the book translation the movie is based upon.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2013" title="2013">2013</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i></b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Bard" title="Bard">Bard</a> notices that one of the <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a> he is hosting is named <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin</a>, and realizes their identity after consulting an old tapestry showing the King&#39;s Line. Upon hearing someone mention the &#39;Lord of Silver Fountains&#39;, he remembers the &#34;prophecy&#34;. The words of the prophecy in the film mirror the first and fourth stanza of the poem but re-arranged and with the focus of the second part altered to make it a prophecy of dread rather than a celebration of joy:</dd></dl>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>The lord of silver fountains<br/>
    The King of carven stone,<br/>
The King beneath the mountains,<br/>
    Shall come into his own!<br/>
<br/>
And the bells shall ring in gladness,<br/>
    At the Mountain-king&#39;s return!<br/>
But all shall fail in sadness<br/>
    And the lake will shine and burn,
</p>
</div>

As the final line is delivered, the camera lingers on the Sunset over the Long Lake.
