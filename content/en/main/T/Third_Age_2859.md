**Third Age 2859** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2859**) is the 2859th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2859 was also known as **S.R. 1259** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Fíli](../F/Fíli.md), elder son of [Dís](../D/Dís.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Hending](../H/Hending.md), fourth child and third son of [Holman the greenhanded](../H/Holman_the_greenhanded.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Death of the unnamed seventeenth [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The seventeenth Prince's unnamed son becomes the eighteenth [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-3">[3]</a></sup>
