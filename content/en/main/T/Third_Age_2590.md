**Third Age 2590** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2590**) is the 2590th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2590 was also known as **S.R. 990** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [War of the Dwarves and Dragons](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons.md): [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Thrór](Thrór.md) returns to [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md). [Grór](../G/Grór.md) his brother goes to the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
