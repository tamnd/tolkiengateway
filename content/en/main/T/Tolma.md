<i><b>tolma</b></i> is a [Telerin](Telerin.md) word for "knob, short, rounded handle".<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) has suggested that the word (and its meaning) could be valid [Neo-Quenya](../N/Neo-Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [TOL](TOL~34330.md)<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>
