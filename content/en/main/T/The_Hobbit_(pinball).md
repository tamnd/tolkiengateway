**The Hobbit** is a pinball machine by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jersey_Jack_Pinball" title="wikipedia:Jersey Jack Pinball">Jersey Jack Pinball</a>. It is licensed merchandise of [*The Hobbit* trilogy](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), and was released in March of [2016](/wiki/2016).
