<i><b>Tolkien: In de Ban van zijn Werk</b></i> (Dutch: *Tolkien: Under the Spell of his Works*) is an illustrated, introductory booklet to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s works in Dutch. Its title is a play on *In de Ban van de Ring* ("Under the Spell of the Ring"), the Dutch title of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

Tolkien's visit to the Netherlands is treated on pp. 1–4; pp. 4–6 deals with Tolkien's popularity, pp. 6–7 with Tolkien and translators and finally pp. 7–16 with Tolkien's life and work in general.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
