**Time Stands Still (at the Iron Hill)** is the thirteenth track for [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian)'s Tolkien themed album, *[Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth)*. It tells the story of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin)'s ride to [Angband](/wiki/Angband) after the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), his challenge to [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), and his battle and defeat.
