**Time Stands Still (at the Iron Hill)** is the thirteenth track for [Blind Guardian](../B/Blind_Guardian.md)'s Tolkien themed album, *[Nightfall in Middle-Earth](../N/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth.md)*. It tells the story of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md)'s ride to [Angband](../A/Angband.md) after the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), his challenge to [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), and his battle and defeat.
