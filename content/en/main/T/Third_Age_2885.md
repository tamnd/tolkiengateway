**Third Age 2885** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2885**) is the 2885th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2885 was also known as **S.R. 1285** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Stirred up by emissaries of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md) cross the [Poros](../P/Poros.md) and attack [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ta_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Folcwine](../F/Folcwine.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) fulfills the [Oath of Eorl](../O/Oath_of_Eorl.md) and sends aid to Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-stew_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-mark_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mark-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Battle of the Crossings of Poros](../B/Battle_of_the_Crossings_of_Poros.md): [Folcred](../F/Folcred.md) and [Fastred](<../F/Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine).md>), the [twin](Twins.md) sons of Folcwine, are slain in the service of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ta_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-stew_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-mark_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mark-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) erect the [Haudh in Gwanûr](../H/Haudh_in_Gwanûr.md) in their honor, by the [Crossings of Poros](../C/Crossings_of_Poros.md).<sup id="cite_ref-stew_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Hobson Gamgee](../H/Hobson_Gamgee.md), son of [Hob Gammidge](../H/Hob_Gammidge.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
