<i><b>Tilkal</b></i> was a metal crafted by the [Vala](../V/Valar.md) [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. It was used in the chain [Angaino](../A/Angainor.md) that bound [Melko](../M/Melko.md):

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Behold, Aulë now gathered six metals, copper, silver, tin, lead, iron, and gold, and taking a portion of each made with his magic a seventh which he named therefore tilkal, and this had all the properties of the six and many of its own. It&#39;s colour was bright green or red in varying lights and it could not be broken, and Aulë alone could forge it. Thereafter he forged a mighty chain, making of it all seven metals welded with spells to a substance of uttermost hardness and brightness and smoothness, but of tilkal he had not sufficient to add more than a little to each link.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a class="mw-redirect" href="The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One.md" title="The Book of Lost Tales Part One">The Book of Lost Tales Part One</a></i>, &#34;<a href="The_Chaining_of_Melko.md" title="The Chaining of Melko">The Chaining of Melko</a>&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote>

The name *tilkal* is an acronym from the [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) names of the metals mentioned above:<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- <i><b>t</b>ambe</i> = "copper, bronze"
- <i><b>i</b>lsa</i> = "silver"
- <i><b>l</b>atúken</i> = "tin"
- <i><b>k</b>anu</i> = "lead"
- <i><a href="../A/Anga.md" title="Anga"><b>a</b>nga</a></i> = "iron"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../L/Laure.md" title="Laure"><b>l</b>aure</a></i> = "gold"
