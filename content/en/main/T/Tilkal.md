<i><b>Tilkal</b></i> was a metal crafted by the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. It was used in the chain [Angaino](/wiki/Angainor "Angainor") that bound [Melko](/wiki/Melko):

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Behold, Aulë now gathered six metals, copper, silver, tin, lead, iron, and gold, and taking a portion of each made with his magic a seventh which he named therefore tilkal, and this had all the properties of the six and many of its own. It&#39;s colour was bright green or red in varying lights and it could not be broken, and Aulë alone could forge it. Thereafter he forged a mighty chain, making of it all seven metals welded with spells to a substance of uttermost hardness and brightness and smoothness, but of tilkal he had not sufficient to add more than a little to each link.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One" title="The Book of Lost Tales Part One">The Book of Lost Tales Part One</a></i>, &#34;<a href="/wiki/The_Chaining_of_Melko" title="The Chaining of Melko">The Chaining of Melko</a>&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

The name *tilkal* is an acronym from the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) names of the metals mentioned above:<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- <i><b>t</b>ambe</i> = "copper, bronze"
- <i><b>i</b>lsa</i> = "silver"
- <i><b>l</b>atúken</i> = "tin"
- <i><b>k</b>anu</i> = "lead"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Anga" title="Anga"><b>a</b>nga</a></i> = "iron"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Laure" title="Laure"><b>l</b>aure</a></i> = "gold"
