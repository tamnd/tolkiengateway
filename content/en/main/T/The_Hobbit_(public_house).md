**The Hobbit** is a Tolkien-themed <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/public_house" title="wikipedia:public house">public house</a> in Southampton, United Kingdom.

## Copyright infringement accusation {#Copyright_infringement_accusation}

In March 2012 it drew international attention after it was accused of copyright infringement by [Middle-earth Enterprises](/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises).<sup id="cite_ref-BBC1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC1-1">[1]</a></sup> In response, a "Save The Hobbit" campaign was established.<sup id="cite_ref-FB1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FB1-2">[2]</a></sup> This caught the attention of [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)") cast members [Stephen Fry](/wiki/Stephen_Fry) and [Ian McKellen](/wiki/Ian_McKellen), who both criticised the action taken by [Saul Zaentz's](/wiki/Saul_Zaentz "Saul Zaentz") company.<sup id="cite_ref-BBC2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC2-3">[3]</a></sup> Stephen Fry tweeted:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Honestly, @savethehobbit, sometimes I&#39;m ashamed of the business I&#39;m in. What pointless, self-defeating bullying.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Stephen Fry<sup id="cite_ref-Twitter1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Twitter1-4">[4]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

On his website, Ian McKellen commented:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>With no apparent explanation they [Middle-earth Enterprises] are insisting that The Hobbit pub stop trading under that name. As if it were possible to control the way Tolkien and his characters have entered the culture. Did anyone try to ban &#34;Gandalf for President&#34; buttons? I wear mine with pride.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Ian McKellen<sup id="cite_ref-IM1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IM1-5">[5]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

Lawyers representing Middle-earth Enterprises subsequently offered The Hobbit a reprieve, saying that they would be open to giving them a license to trade as "The Hobbit" for a fee of $100 per year. Stephen Fry and Ian McKellen have since announced that they would be willing to pay this fee. Both actors hope to visit the public house once filming on *The Hobbit* films ends in July.<sup id="cite_ref-BBC3_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC3-6">[6]</a></sup>
