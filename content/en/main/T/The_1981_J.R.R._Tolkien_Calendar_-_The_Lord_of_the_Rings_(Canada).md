This version of **The 1981 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar - The Lord of the Rings** (To Honor the Silver Anniversary) was published specifically for Canada by [Methuen Publications](/wiki/Methuen "Methuen"). It is illustrated by [Paul Rivoche](/wiki/Paul_Rivoche) with images loosely based on the images from [The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)>).

- ISBN 458 946109
- Dimensions: 13 x 11.75 inches
- Stapled
- Punched hanging hole
- Glossy white, medium-weight paperboard sleeve
- Shrink-wrapped
