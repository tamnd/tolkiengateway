Various **dice sets** have been released for *[The One Ring](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)")* role-playing game.

Contrary to games like *[Dungeons and Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons)* and its *Lord of the Rings* adaptations *[Adventures in Middle-earth](/wiki/Adventures_in_Middle-earth)* and *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying)*, which use a [wider variety of dice](/wiki/RPG_Dice "RPG Dice"), *The One Ring* uses six-sided and twelve-sided dice exclusively. Both dice have game-specific markings: the six-sided dice have an additional [Lambë](/wiki/Lamb%C3%AB) letter on the six, while on the twelve-sided dice the eleven shows the [Eye of Sauron](/wiki/Eye_of_Sauron) and the twelve shows Gandalf's rune.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## First Edition {#First_Edition}

The bone-colored dice were released first, with a green set following in 2015.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Two sets of marbled dice, one in white and blue, and another in black and red, were released at some later point.

## Second Edition {#Second_Edition}

Dice sets in white or black were offered, both having red engravings. The initial printings of the twelve-sided dice were incorrect, with the face that should show 1 showing 11 instead.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Both starter sets come with the white dice set included.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Mytago, the Czech distributor, has a related webshop named Imago, which sells two sets of exclusive dice, though it's not clear whether these fall under Mytago's license. These sets have less stylized lettering for the numbers, but do have the One Ring embedded in their resin. The red set is exclusively distributed as part of a collector's set, together with miniatures and a pencil.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
