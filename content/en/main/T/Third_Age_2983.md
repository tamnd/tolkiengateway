**Third Age 2983** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2983**) is the 2983rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2983 was also known as **S.R. 1383** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md), son of [Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Denethor II](../D/Denethor_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Finduilas](<../F/Finduilas_(wife_of_Denethor).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Ferdibrand Took](../F/Ferdibrand_Took.md), son of [Ferdinand Took](../F/Ferdinand_Took.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Mentha Brandybuck](../M/Mentha_Brandybuck.md), second child and first daughter of [Marmadas Brandybuck](../M/Marmadas_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Marigold Gamgee](../M/Marigold_Gamgee.md), sixth child and third daughter of [Hamfast Gamgee](../H/Hamfast_Gamgee.md) and [Bell Goodchild](../B/Bell_Goodchild.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The second edition of [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) to *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* had [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)'s birth this year, which contradicted other information. The [50th Anniversary Edition](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md>) corrected it to [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2980](Third_Age_2980.md). <span></span>
