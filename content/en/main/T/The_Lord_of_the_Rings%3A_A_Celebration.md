<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: A Celebration</b></i> is a booklet issued as a supplement to the January 2004 edition of the film magazine <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_(film_magazine)" title="wikipedia:Empire (film magazine)">Empire</a></i>. The booklet features portraits and interview excerpts for 27 cast and crew members of [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson)'s [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
