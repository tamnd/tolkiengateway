**Third Age 541** (abbreviated to **T.A. 541**) is the 541st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Rómendacil I](../R/Rómendacil_I.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) slain in battle with new hordes of [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Rómendacil I](../R/Rómendacil_I.md)'s son, [Turambar](<Turambar_(King_of_Gondor).md>), becomes the ninth [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppSouth_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppSouth-3">[3]</a></sup>
