**The Passing of the Elves** is the tenth track of disc one of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - The Complete Recordings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_The_Complete_Recordings)* by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore). The song was written and performed by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Plan_9&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Plan 9 (page does not exist)">Plan 9</a> and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=David_Long&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="David Long (page does not exist)">David Long</a> for the film, and produced by Howard Shore. It is a rendition of the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) poem *[A Elbereth Gilthoniel](/wiki/A_Elbereth_Gilthoniel)*.
