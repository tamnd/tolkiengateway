<b><i>Tolkien Studies</i>: Volume 3</b>, published in [2006](../_/2006.md), is the third volume of the annual review *[Tolkien Studies](<Tolkien_Studies_(journal).md>)*.

## Contents {#Contents}

- Articles
    - Ross Smith, "Fitting Sense to Sound: Linguistic Aesthetics and Phonosemantics in the Work of J.R.R. Tolkien"
    - Maria Prozesky, "The Text Tale of Frodo the Nine-fingered: Residual Oral Patterning in *The Lord of the Rings*"
    - Amy Amendt-Raduege, "Dream Visions in J.R.R. Tolkien's *The Lord of the Rings*"
    - Gergely Nagy, "The 'Lost' Subject of Middle-earth: The Constitution of the Subject in the Figure of Gollum in *The Lord of the Rings*"
    - Martin Simonson, "Three is Company: Novel, Fairy Tale, and Romance on the Journey through the Shire"
    - Richard W. Fehrenbacher, "*[Beowulf](<../B/Beowulf_(poem).md>)* as Fairy-story: Enchanting the Elegiac in *The Two Towers*"
    - James Obertino, "Barbarians and Imperialism in Tacitus and *The Lord of the Rings*"

- Notes and Documents
    - Verlyn Flieger, "[Karen Wynn Fonstad](../K/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad.md)"
    - Karen Wynn Fonstad, "Writing 'TO' the Map"
    - Douglas A. Anderson, "[R. W. Chambers](../R/R._W._Chambers.md) and *The Hobbit*"
    - Michael D.C. Drout, "A Spliced Old English Quotation in '[Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics](../B/Beowulf%253A_The_Monsters_and_the_Critics.md)'"
    - James Ignatius McNelis, "'The tree took me up from ground and carried me off': A Source for Tolkien's Ents in Ludvig Holberg's *Journey of Niels Klim to the World Underground*"

- Book Reviews
    - *Sí Qente Feanor &amp; Other Elvish Writings* (*[Parma Eldalamberon 15](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_15.md)*), by J.R.R. Tolkien, edited by Arden R. Smith, Christopher Gilson, Patrick H. Wynne, and Bill Welden, reviewed by John Garth
    - *[Smith of Wootton Major](../S/Smith_of_Wootton_Major.md): Extended Edition*, by J.R.R. Tolkien, edited by Verlyn Flieger, reviewed by Paul Edmund Thomas
    - *[Gateway to Sindarin](../A/A_Gateway_to_Sindarin.md)*, by David Salo, reviewed by Sandra Ballif Straubhaar
    - *[Interrupted Music](../I/Interrupted_Music.md): The Making of Tolkien's Mythology*, by Verlyn Flieger, reviewed by Gergely Nagy
    - *"J.R.R. Tolkien Special Issue", Mfs: Modern Fiction Studies, 50 no. 4 (Winter 2004)*, edited by Shaun F. D. Hughes, reviewed by Robin Anne Reid
    - *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_A_Reader's_Companion.md)*, by Wayne G. Hammond and Christina Scull, reviewed by David Bratman
    - *[Perilous Realms: Celtic and Norse in Tolkien's Middle-earth](../P/Perilous_Realms%253A_Celtic_and_Norse_in_Tolkien's_Middle-earth.md)*, by Marjorie Burns, reviewed by Dimitra Fimi
    - *Reconsidering Tolkien*, edited by Thomas Honegger, reviewed by Janet Brennan Croft
    - *The Science of Middle-earth*, by Henry Gee, reviewed by Amy Amendt-Raduege
    - *Tolkien, les racines du légendaire*, edited by Michaël Devaux, and *Tolkien, trente ans après (1973-2003)*, edited by Vincent Ferré, reviewed by Shaun F. D. Hughes
    - *[Tolkien and the Invention of Myth](Tolkien_and_the_Invention_of_Myth.md): A Reader*, edited by Jane Chance, reviewed by Margaret Sinex
    - *[Tolkien in Translation](Tolkien_in_Translation.md)*, and *[Translating Tolkien: Text and Film](Translating_Tolkien%253A_Text_and_Film.md)*, edited by Thomas Honegger, reviewed by David Doughan
    - *Tolkien on Film: Essays on Peter Jackson's "The Lord of the Rings"*, edited by Janet Brennan Croft, reviewed by Kristin Thompson
    - *Translating Tolkien: Philological Elements in "The Lord of the Rings"*, by Allan Turner, reviewed by Arden R. Smith
    - *Unsung Heroes of "The Lord of the Rings": From the Page to the Screen*, by Lynnette R. Porter, reviewed by Richard C. West
    - *[War and the Works of J.R.R. Tolkien](../W/War_and_the_Works_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*, by Janet Brennan Croft, reviewed by John Garth
    - Book Notes, by Douglas A. Anderson

- David Bratman, "The Year's Work in Tolkien Studies 2003"
- Michael D.C. Drout, Marcel R. Bulles, and Rebecca Epstein, "Bibliography (in English) for 2004"
