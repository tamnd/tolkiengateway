**Thorondor**, the **King of Eagles**,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Return_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-fingolfin_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fingolfin-7">[7]</a></sup> was the greatest of the [Great Eagles](/wiki/Great_Eagles) of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB). It is said that Thorondor had a wingspan of thirty <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fathom" title="wikipedia:Fathom">fathoms</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-q8_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-q8-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Thorondor was sent by Manwë to watch over the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) after they arrived in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). The [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) first encountered him when he helped [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) rescue [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) from imprisonment from [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-3">[3]</a></sup>

After that, Thorondor and the rest of the Eagles settled in the [Crissaegrim](/wiki/Crissaegrim).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> When [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) was built, Thorondor became the ever vigilant guardian of the city. He rescued [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin)'s body from defilement after [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) killed him, giving the Dark Lord a scar on his face.<sup id="cite_ref-fingolfin_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fingolfin-7">[7]</a></sup>

He and two of his vassals, [Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir) and [Landroval](/wiki/Landroval),<sup id="cite_ref-P1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-9">[9]</a></sup> rescued [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) when [Angband](/wiki/Angband) was aroused.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> When Gondolin fell, Thorondor rescued the survivors.<sup id="cite_ref-gondolin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondolin-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), Thorondor and [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) led the Eagles in battle with the [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons), and may have fought with [Ancalagon](/wiki/Ancalagon) the Black.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

Thorondor is not mentioned after the War of Wrath, but his descendants played part in some events of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Thorondor* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "King of Eagles", being the combination of *[thoron](/wiki/Thoron "Thoron")* ("eagle") + *[taur, -dor](/wiki/Taur "Taur")* ("king"). The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) cognate is <i><b>Sorontar</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

In the earlier versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), the character was called <i><b>Sorontur</b></i> in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=SORO&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SORO (page does not exist)">SORO</a> ("eagle") + *tur* ("king"). The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognates were <i><b>Thorndor</b></i> and <i><b>Throndor</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

A discarded Qenya name was *Ramandur*/*Ramandor* ("Shouter").<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>
