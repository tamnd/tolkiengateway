The **twelve houses of the Gondothlim** were the kindreds of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. Their lords and deeds are described in the tale of "[The Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")" in great detail.

## History {#History}

When the [Gondothlim](/wiki/Gondothlim) knew that [Melko](/wiki/Melko)'s armies were approaching Gondolin, all the folks and lords were reunited coming from all parts of the city. Here are named in the order they came when King Turgon called them to council:<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:172–4</sup>

During the council, nearly all the lords agreed with Tuor that the Gondothlim should leave the city before the siege began, but Turgon was convinced by Meglin and Salgant to stay and fight in the city that they loved. Thus, all the houses prepared to battle.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:175–6</sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Mysilvergreen_-_The_Fall_of_Gondolin.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/5/55/Mysilvergreen_-_The_Fall_of_Gondolin.jpg/200px-Mysilvergreen_-_The_Fall_of_Gondolin.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="141" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/5/55/Mysilvergreen_-_The_Fall_of_Gondolin.jpg/300px-Mysilvergreen_-_The_Fall_of_Gondolin.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/5/55/Mysilvergreen_-_The_Fall_of_Gondolin.jpg/400px-Mysilvergreen_-_The_Fall_of_Gondolin.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="724"/></a><figcaption><i>The Fall of Gondolin</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Mysilvergreen" title="Category:Images by Mysilvergreen">Mysilvergreen</a></figcaption></figure>

When the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin) began, all of them suffered great losses or were completely destroyed, except the House of the King, who remained in the palace to protect the King.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:183</sup> First, the folk of the Mole were scattered after they attacked the folk of the Wing and Meglin was killed by Tuor. Later, after the northern gate was lost, Duilin was killed, but the house of the Swallow kept fighting without their lord. Soon the folk of the Hammer and their lord Rog were butchered outside the walls; Penlod suffered the next attack, so he was killed and both of his houses lost many men.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:178–9</sup> Meanwhile, the host of the Harp abandoned their treacherous lord Salgant and went to save the folk of the Golden Flower, being mostly destroyed in the [Great Market](/wiki/Great_Market).<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:182–3</sup>

Thereafter the survivors of all kindreds were reunited in the [Square of the Palace](/wiki/Square_of_the_Palace), save those of the Hammer of Wrath alone. In that square, Ecthelion and [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)> "Gothmog (balrog)") slew each other. There also King Turgon proclaimed the fall of Gondolin and ordered the survivors to follow Tuor, but his royal folk stayed with him.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:183–4</sup> Later he and his house were killed when the [Tower of the King](/wiki/Tower_of_the_King) fell.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:187</sup> After the [exiles of Gondolin](/wiki/Exiles_of_Gondolin "Exiles of Gondolin") escaped, Glorfindel died saving them from a [Balrog](/wiki/Balrog).<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:194</sup>

Thus, apart from Tuor, the only lords who survived the Fall of Gondolin were Egalmoth and Galdor. Egalmoth was later killed in a battle at the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion), but Galdor would reach [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) and even keep some followers.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Tolkien never wrote again about the houses of Gondolin in later texts, because his writings never include details about the city. The only exception is in the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](/wiki/Quenta_Noldorinwa)*, where there is a simple mention of "the chieftains of the noble houses and their warriors"<sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-3">[3]</a></sup> (notice that this is the text used in the [twenty-third chapter](/wiki/Of_Tuor_and_the_Fall_of_Gondolin "Of Tuor and the Fall of Gondolin") of the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)*).

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) explains that the idea would have re-emerged if his father had finished *[Of Tuor and his coming to Gondolin](/wiki/Of_Tuor_and_his_coming_to_Gondolin)*: in the description of the [Seven Gates](/wiki/Seven_Gates), there are seven groups of guards, each one with rich 'heraldic' descriptions.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> But Tolkien did not finish the tale, so beyond the Lost Tales the only vestiges are the titles Ecthelion 'of the Fountain' and Glorfindel 'chief of the House of the Golden Flower of Gondolin'.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> They are the only captains of Turgon mentioned in the published *Silmarillion*, but there are also later texts of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") that include individual mentions to Rog<sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-3">[3]</a></sup> and Egalmoth.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name *Gondothlim* for the people of Gondolin was later changed by the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) <i><b><a href="/wiki/Gondolindrim" title="Gondolindrim">Gondolindrim</a></b></i>.
