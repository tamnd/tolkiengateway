<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Painting Guide</b></i> is a magazine-format booklet published in 1979. It describes techniques to paint miniatures produced by [Heritage Models](/wiki/Heritage_Models). It also contains information on the recommended use of materials (colors, brushes, etc.) to paint the models presented in the book. Heritage Models miniatures are based on the work of [Ralph Bakshi](/wiki/Ralph_Bakshi) in his [animated film](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)").
