<i><b>The Hobbit Companion</b></i> is a book writen by [David Day](/wiki/David_Day), first published in [1997](/wiki/1997).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Illustrated in full colour (illustrations by Lidia Postma), including a number of double page prints, this is an intriguing and interesting investigation into the world of words used by <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">Tolkien</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i>. It explores the clever web of wordplay Professor Tolkien used to create hidden meanings and delves into the mythical associations that lie beneath the tale&#39;s thrilling surface.
</p><p>Starting with the word &#34;hobbit&#34; itself, the book moves on to examine Bilbo Baggins, hobbit heritage and history, Gandalf, Shire Society, and much more.
</p><p>There is also a chapter on the hobbit characters from <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i>, including <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a>, <a href="/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee" title="Samwise Gamgee">Sam</a>, <a href="/wiki/Peregrin_Took" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a>. Lidia Postma’s painting of the <a href="/wiki/Party_Field" title="Party Field">Party Field</a> from <i><a href="/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Fellowship of the Ring">The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i> is a treasure – it pictures and names 60 of the guests from Adelard Took to Willibald Bolger.
</p>
</blockquote>
