<i><b>torech</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word glossed as "tunnel; hole, excavation",<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> and "secret hole, lair".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *torech* is a derivative of the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") TOR ("secrete, hide"). An associated word is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[ekka](/wiki/Ekka "Ekka")*.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>
