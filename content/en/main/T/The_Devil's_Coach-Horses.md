<i><b>The Devil&#39;s Coach-Horses</b></i> is an essay written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) that was published in *[The Review of English Studies](<The_Review_of_English_Studies_(Vol._1).md>)* in [July](../J/July.md) of [1925](../_/1925.md).<sup id="cite_ref-chronology1925_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology1925-1">[1]</a></sup>

The essay discusses *eaueres*, a word found in the [1922](../_/1922.md) Early English Text Society Edition of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Group" title="wikipedia:Katherine Group">Hali Meiðhad</a></i>. Tolkien suggests that rather than meaning boar, the word should be translated as draught horse.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Devil's Coach-Horses was first published in [*The Review of English Studies*, Vol. 1 No. 3](<The_Review_of_English_Studies_(Vol._1_No._3).md>) (July 1925), and was reprinted in [*The Review of English Studies*, Vol. 1](<The_Review_of_English_Studies_(Vol._1).md>) ([1969](../_/1969.md)). The essay also appeared separately as a [six-page pamphlet](<The_Devil's_Coach-Horses_(booklet).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
