<i><b>The Devil&#39;s Coach-Horses</b></i> is an essay written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) that was published in *[The Review of English Studies](</wiki/The_Review_of_English_Studies_(Vol._1)> "The Review of English Studies (Vol. 1)")* in [July](/wiki/July) of [1925](/wiki/1925).<sup id="cite_ref-chronology1925_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology1925-1">[1]</a></sup>

The essay discusses *eaueres*, a word found in the [1922](/wiki/1922) Early English Text Society Edition of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Group" title="wikipedia:Katherine Group">Hali Meiðhad</a></i>. Tolkien suggests that rather than meaning boar, the word should be translated as draught horse.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Devil's Coach-Horses was first published in [*The Review of English Studies*, Vol. 1 No. 3](</wiki/The_Review_of_English_Studies_(Vol._1_No._3)> "The Review of English Studies (Vol. 1 No. 3)") (July 1925), and was reprinted in [*The Review of English Studies*, Vol. 1](</wiki/The_Review_of_English_Studies_(Vol._1)> "The Review of English Studies (Vol. 1)") ([1969](/wiki/1969)). The essay also appeared separately as a [six-page pamphlet](</wiki/The_Devil%27s_Coach-Horses_(booklet)> "The Devil's Coach-Horses (booklet)").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
