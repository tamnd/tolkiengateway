Published by Zarathustra Music in 2020, the complete Full Score of [Paul Corfield Godfrey](/wiki/Paul_Corfield_Godfrey)'s *[The Children of Húrin, Op. 48 (Opera)](</wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin,_Op._48_(Opera)>)* updated to reflect amendments made during the production of Volante Opera Production's Demo Recording of the work.

This is a 388 page A4 sized book with full gloss covers.

This is a complete orchestral score with introduction by the composer and synopsis.
