Published by Zarathustra Music in 2020, the complete Full Score of [Paul Corfield Godfrey](../P/Paul_Corfield_Godfrey.md)'s *[The Children of Húrin, Op. 48 (Opera)](<The_Children_of_Húrin,_Op._48_(Opera).md>)* updated to reflect amendments made during the production of Volante Opera Production's Demo Recording of the work.

This is a 388 page A4 sized book with full gloss covers.

This is a complete orchestral score with introduction by the composer and synopsis.
