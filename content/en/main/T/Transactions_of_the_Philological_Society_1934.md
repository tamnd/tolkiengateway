<i><b>Transactions of the Philological Society 1934</b></i> is a journal issue published in 1934.

It publishes Tolkien's essay "[Chaucer as a Philologist: The Reeve's Tale](../C/Chaucer_as_a_Philologist%253A_The_Reeve's_Tale.md)". In the lecture (delivered on 16 May 1931) Tolkien discusses Chaucer’s use of the Northern Middle English dialect as a linguistic joke in The Reeve’s Tale. The essay was reprinted in [*Tolkien Studies*: Volume 5](Tolkien_Studies%253A_Volume_5.md) in [2008](../_/2008.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
