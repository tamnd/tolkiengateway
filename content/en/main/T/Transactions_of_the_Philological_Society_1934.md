<i><b>Transactions of the Philological Society 1934</b></i> is a journal issue published in 1934.

It publishes Tolkien's essay "[Chaucer as a Philologist: The Reeve's Tale](/wiki/Chaucer_as_a_Philologist:_The_Reeve%27s_Tale)". In the lecture (delivered on 16 May 1931) Tolkien discusses Chaucer’s use of the Northern Middle English dialect as a linguistic joke in The Reeve’s Tale. The essay was reprinted in [*Tolkien Studies*: Volume 5](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_5 "Tolkien Studies: Volume 5") in [2008](/wiki/2008).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
