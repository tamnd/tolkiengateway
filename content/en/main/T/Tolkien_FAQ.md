<b>Tolkien <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FAQ" title="wikipedia:FAQ">FAQ</a></b> aka **The Tolkien Frequently Asked Questions List** has been an electronic document online since the early 1990s. Its purpose is to give answers and debate common questions and misunderstandings usually occurring among readers of Tolkien's [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md). The topics range between the role of the [Shire](../S/Shire.md) as England, the pointed ears of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) or the fate the [Entwives](../E/Entwives.md). While the FAQ points out clear answers that are covered from the books, some of the answers rely on less known sources, like *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*.

The FAQ has been hailed as "superb ... well written and well documented" and generally acceptable by the [Tolkien fandom](Tolkien_fandom.md). Also, most of their conclusions have even been strengthened by more recently-published information.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The FAQ was compiled in [1993](../_/1993.md) by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=William_D.B._Loos&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="William D.B. Loos (page does not exist)">William D.B. Loos</a>, while [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md) and [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) are credited among the contributors of the FAQ.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Presumably, it originated from the Tolkien Newsgroups in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usenet" title="wikipedia:Usenet">Usenet</a> and from there circulated through mailing lists, BBSs and several websites. Traditional addresses of the FAQ of around that time were <a class="external free" href="http://ganet.acns.fssu.edu/~gsl9286/aft/faq/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">http://ganet.acns.fssu.edu/~gsl9286/aft/faq/</a> and <a class="external free" href="http://www.daimi.aau.dk/~bouvin/tolkienfaq.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">http://www.daimi.aau.dk/~bouvin/tolkienfaq.html</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> One version of the FAQ was updated on [March 28](../_/28_March.md) of [1994](../_/1994.md), while the last (and as of yet, final) revision was made on [July 8](../_/8_July.md) of [1996](../_/1996.md).

## Structure {#Structure}

The document is composed of two parts; each part contains the following sections:

- Tolkien And His Work
- (General History of Middle-earth)
- Hobbits
- Elves
- Dwarves
- Istari (Wizards)
- Enemies
- Miscellaneous

The main part contains questions (and answers) concerning Tolkien, his work, and details concerning *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)*; the second part, called LessFAQ, contains questions on more obscure topics, usually having to do with *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)* or *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)* and contains the section "General History of Middle-earth". Some websites reproduced the FAQ as one single part.

As of the last (1996) version of the FAQ, some entries are noted to be "in their final form" while others are noted as "being unrevised" and still remain so.

## Legacy {#Legacy}

In the late 1990s, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Steuard_Jensen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Steuard Jensen (page does not exist)">Steuard Jensen</a>, created a "Newsgroups FAQ" which contains questions, hints and guidelines concerning membership and behavior in the Usenet newgroups rec.arts.books.tolkien and alt.fan.tolkien. The third section contained both parts of the FAQ, and expanded by a new third part titled "The Great Debates"; this new part summarized common hot topics of discussion and possible solutions to the controversies.

As of [2009](../_/2009.md), the Newsgroups FAQ is still expanded, maintained and updated and presently has a different structure. <span></span>
