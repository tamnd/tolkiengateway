**The Tall** was a title used of [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Son of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor"), [Galdor](</wiki/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min)> "Galdor (Lord of Dor-lómin)") of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Húrin](</wiki/H%C3%BArin_(Warden_of_the_Keys)> "Húrin (Warden of the Keys)"), Warden of the Keys of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir), son of [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor_II).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The Sindarin translation of "the Tall" is given as <i><b>Orchal</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
