<b><i>Tolkien Studies</i>: Volume 2</b>, published in [2005](../_/2005.md), is the second volume of the annual review *[Tolkien Studies](<Tolkien_Studies_(journal).md>)*.

This issue reprints W. Rhys Roberts's "[Gerald of Wales on the Survival of Welsh](../G/Gerald_of_Wales_on_the_Survival_of_Welsh.md)", an essay which J.R.R. Tolkien contributed with a translation of a Latin passage into the "[AB Language](../A/AB_Language~26937.md)", a dialect speculated by Tolkien to had existed in the West Midlands of England in the 13th century.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
