<i><b>tharni</b></i> is a [Westron](/wiki/Westron) word which means "quarter". Dependant on where it was spoken, it has two meanings:

- A division of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It was translated *[Farthing](/wiki/Farthings "Farthings")* in the text.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
- In [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), it was a silver coin ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Canath&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Canath (page does not exist)">canath</a></i>), worth one fourth of a [Castar](/wiki/Castar), or *Mirian*.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It has an older form <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tharant%C4%ABn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tharantīn (page does not exist)">tharantīn</a></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup> from possible [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) \**tharan* "four".<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
