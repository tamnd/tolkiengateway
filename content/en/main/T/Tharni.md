<i><b>tharni</b></i> is a [Westron](../W/Westron.md) word which means "quarter". Dependant on where it was spoken, it has two meanings:

- A division of [the Shire](The_Shire.md). It was translated *[Farthing](../F/Farthings.md)* in the text.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
- In [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), it was a silver coin ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Canath&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Canath (page does not exist)">canath</a></i>), worth one fourth of a [Castar](../C/Castar.md), or *Mirian*.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It has an older form <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tharant%C4%ABn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tharantīn (page does not exist)">tharantīn</a></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup> from possible [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) \**tharan* "four".<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
