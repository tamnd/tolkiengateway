**tinnu** is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning *dusk* or *[twilight](Twilight.md)*. It is derived from *[tinu](Tinu.md)* "spark, small star" and *[dû](../D/Dû.md)* "nightfall, evening, dimness*.*
