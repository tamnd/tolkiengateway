**Tom Kidd** (born [10 August](/wiki/10_August) [1955](/wiki/1955)) is an American fantasy artist. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create art for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).

He also designed at least one collectible knife based on the Lord of the Rings.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
