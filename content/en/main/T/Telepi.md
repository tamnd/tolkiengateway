<i><b>telepi</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-L347_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-1">[1]</a></sup> <i><b>telpe</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-2">[2]</a></sup> (or <i><b>telpë</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>), and <i><b>telep-</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-2">[2]</a></sup> are [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) words for "[silver](/wiki/Silver "Silver")".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a letter, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") stated that the form *telepi* derives from [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kyelep%C4%93&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kyelepē (page does not exist)">kyelepē</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-L347_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) has noted that *telpe*, *telep-* derive from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [KYELEP](/wiki/KYELEP).<sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-2">[2]</a></sup>
