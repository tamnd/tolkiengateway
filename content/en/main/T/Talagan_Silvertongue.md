**Talagan Silvertongue** was the lord of the Edhelion. In c. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") 2500 an alliance of [Goblins](/wiki/Goblins) and [Dourhands](/wiki/Dourhands) attacked Edhelion to gain its relics under the leadership of [Skorgrím Dourhand](/wiki/Skorgr%C3%ADm_Dourhand). Talagan sent messengers to [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) for aid. Just before [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond)'s forces arrived, the [Dourhands](/wiki/Dourhands) managed to break through Edhelion's defences and surrounded Talagan. In a last attempt to save Edhelion's relics Talagan destroys the entrance of the halls. The stones crush both Talagan and [Skorgrím](/wiki/Skorgr%C3%ADm_Dourhand "Skorgrím Dourhand").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
