**Talagan Silvertongue** was the lord of the Edhelion. In c. [T.A.](Third_Age.md) 2500 an alliance of [Goblins](../G/Goblins.md) and [Dourhands](../D/Dourhands.md) attacked Edhelion to gain its relics under the leadership of [Skorgrím Dourhand](../S/Skorgrím_Dourhand.md). Talagan sent messengers to [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) for aid. Just before [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md)'s forces arrived, the [Dourhands](../D/Dourhands.md) managed to break through Edhelion's defences and surrounded Talagan. In a last attempt to save Edhelion's relics Talagan destroys the entrance of the halls. The stones crush both Talagan and [Skorgrím](../S/Skorgrím_Dourhand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
