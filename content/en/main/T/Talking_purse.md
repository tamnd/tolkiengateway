The **talking purse** was the purse of the [troll](/wiki/Trolls "Trolls") [William](/wiki/William).

## History {#History}

When [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) tried to pick the trolls' purse from William's pocket, the purse squeaked, alerting William of the attempted burglary and captured Bilbo.<sup id="cite_ref-H2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H2-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Background {#Background}

[Fans](/wiki/Tolkien_fandom "Tolkien fandom") have suggested that the purse was perhaps not made by trolls, but that it was stolen and was of [dwarvish](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") or [elvish](/wiki/Elves "Elves") origin due to it having the ability to speak.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) certainly guessed that the trolls had some plunder from [Moria](/wiki/Moria), although he was specifically referring to [Glamdring](/wiki/Glamdring) and [Orcrist](/wiki/Orcrist).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
