**Third Age 1900** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1900**) is the 1900th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1900 was also known as **S.R. 300** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Calimehtar](<../C/Calimehtar_(King_of_Gondor).md>) builds the [White Tower](Tower_of_Ecthelion.md) in [Minas Anor](../M/Minas_Anor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
