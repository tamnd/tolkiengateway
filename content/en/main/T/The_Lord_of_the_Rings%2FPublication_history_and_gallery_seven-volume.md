Here listed are the seven-volume editions of <i><b><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></b></i>.

In 1999, HarperCollins released a "Millennium edition" of *The Lord of the Rings*, consisting of seven volumes which were originally conceived by J.R.R. Tolkien, with book titles suggested by him:

1. *The Ring Sets Out*
2. *The Ring Goes South*
3. *The Treason of Isengard*
4. *The Ring Goes East*
5. *The War of the Ring*
6. *The End of the Third Age*
7. Appendices
