<i><b>The Inklings Handbook: A Comprehensive Guide to the Lives, Thought and Writings of C.S. Lewis, J.R.R. Tolkien, Charles Williams, Owen Barfield and their friends</b></i>, written by [Colin Duriez](/wiki/Colin_Duriez) and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=David_Porter&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="David Porter (page does not exist)">David Porter</a>, is a biographical book about the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings).

## From the publishers {#From_the_publishers}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The Oxford Inklings was an informal group of literary friends who met weekly to talk about ideas and pieces they were writing and enjoy a good evening of &#34;the cut and parry of prolonged, fierce and masculine argument.&#34;
This comprehensive guide to the lives, thoughts, and writings of C.S. Lewis, J. R. R. Tolkien, Charles Williams, and Owen Barfield provides an introduction to the Inklings and a second A-Z section that contains many biographical articles, as well as entries on the group&#39;s publications, themes, and theology.
</p>
</blockquote>
