**The Lord of the Rings MUSH II** is a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MUSH" title="wikipedia:MUSH">MUSH</a> created by [Christopher Anderson](../C/Christopher_Anderson.md).

The game takes place in the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md), during [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md)'s Kingship. He sends a group of explorers - the players - to [the east](../R/Rhûn.md) to search for the [Blue Wizards](../B/Blue_Wizards.md). Programming began in [1999](../_/1999.md), but it has not been released yet. If it ever will is uncertain.
