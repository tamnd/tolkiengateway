**The Lord of the Rings MUSH II** is a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MUSH" title="wikipedia:MUSH">MUSH</a> created by [Christopher Anderson](/wiki/Christopher_Anderson).

The game takes place in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), during [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)'s Kingship. He sends a group of explorers - the players - to [the east](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn "Rhûn") to search for the [Blue Wizards](/wiki/Blue_Wizards). Programming began in [1999](/wiki/1999), but it has not been released yet. If it ever will is uncertain.
