There have been many **exhibitions** about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s life and works, and new exhibitions are constantly being hold worldwide. Here is a list of some of them.

Many of Tolkien's [unpublished materials](/wiki/Index:Unpublished_material "Index:Unpublished material") (writings, drawings, manuscripts...) were first revealed to the public through these exhibitions, and are documented and commentated in their respective catalogues. One can expect to see still more of them in exhibitions in the coming years.
