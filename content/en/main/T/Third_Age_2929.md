**Third Age 2929** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2929**) is the 2929th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2929 was also known as **S.R. 1329** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Arathorn](../A/Arathorn_II.md) son of [Arador](../A/Arador.md) of the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) weds [Gilraen](../G/Gilraen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
