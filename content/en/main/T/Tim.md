**Tim**, said to be the "[nuncle](../U/Unusual_words.md)" of [Tom](<Tom_(The_Stone_Troll).md>), is only mentioned in verse composed by [Sam Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md). In the poem, Tom encounters an old [troll](../S/Stone-trolls.md), gnawing for years on the shinbone of Tim, and Tom demands to let it down.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Most likely, Tim was only an invention by [Sam](../S/Sam.md).<sup id="cite_ref-RF_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RF-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md) who illustrated *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md)*, depicted Tom who kicked the troll with his *boots*, as [Tom Bombadil](Tom_Bombadil.md); although the name was most probably generic and there is no evidence that the author intended Tom to be Bombadil, as having an uncle and father would be absurd.

[Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md) considers a possibility that Tom was somehow modelled after Bombadil, considering that Sam composed the poem soon after their meeting.<sup id="cite_ref-RF_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RF-3">[3]</a></sup>
