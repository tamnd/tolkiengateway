<i><b>Tolkien and C.S. Lewis: The Gift of Friendship</b></i> is a book which explores the friendship of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This book explores their lives, unfolding the extraordinary story of their complex friendship that lasted, with its ups and downs, until Lewis&#39;s death in 1963. Despite their differences - of temperament, spiritual emphasis, and storytelling style - what united them was much stronger: A shared vision that continues to inspire their millions of readers throughout the world
</p>
</blockquote>
