**Telimektar** was a [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar"), the son of [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-War_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-War-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Early history {#Early_history}

Telimektar is first mentioned in the *Lost Tales* in the story of *[the Chaining of Melko](/wiki/The_Chaining_of_Melko "The Chaining of Melko")*. There, he was "but just war-high" when he fought at his father's side against [Melko](/wiki/Melko) in the war which ended with the captivity of the Enemy. In that story Telimektar had "a long sword girt about his waist by a silver girdle."<sup id="cite_ref-War_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-War-1">[1]</a></sup>

His next mention is during the [Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor), where he was pursuing Melko along with the other [Valar](/wiki/Valar).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Later history {#Later_history}

According to one text, when Melko broke free of his second imprisonment (after the [March of Liberation](/wiki/March_of_Liberation)), he went to [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) in order to sow dissension between the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) and the [Solosimpi](/wiki/Solosimpi), who sent for help to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). However, the only aid that came from the Valar was that of Tulkas, who in secret sent his son Telimektar to Tol Eressëa to aid them.

There, Telimektar, with the help of [Ingil](/wiki/Ingil)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> son of [Inwë](/wiki/Inw%C3%AB), surprised Melko and wounded him, and chased him up the [Pine of Tavrobel](/wiki/Pine_of_Tavrobel) all the way to [Ilwë](/wiki/Ilw%C3%AB). However, Melko kept attacking the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and the [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars"), and in the end caused the death of [Urwendi](/wiki/Urwendi) the steerer of the Sun-vessel.

Finally, the Valar decided to cut down the great Pine, which left Melko stranded out of the world.

However, Telimektar and Ingil had to remain in the sky, guarding ceaselessly until Melko's return in the [Great End](/wiki/Great_End). There, [Varda](/wiki/Varda) periodically gives him [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars") to replace the ones Melko destroys and to remind the world of the Valar's watch - and the constellation of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orion_(constellation)" title="wikipedia:Orion (constellation)">Orion</a> is afterwards seen as his image in the sky.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Telimektar*<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup> is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Eld_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eld-6">[4]</a></sup> It is glossed as "Orion, literally Swordsman of Heaven" in the [Qenya Lexicon](/wiki/Qenya_Lexicon), under the root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=TELE&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TELE (page does not exist)">TELE</a> ("cover in").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 3]</a></sup> The name is comprised of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Telimbo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Telimbo (page does not exist)">telimbo</a></i> ("canopy, sky") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ektar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ektar (page does not exist)">ektar</a></i> ("swordsman").<sup id="cite_ref-Eld_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eld-6">[4]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate of *Telimektar* is <i><b>Telumaithor</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[note 4]</a></sup> with the earlier forms of the name being *Telumaithron* and *Telumaithweg*.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[6]</a></sup>

One of his names was <i><b>Mordo</b></i>. It is a Gnomish word adopted into Qenya, being derived from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mavar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mavar (page does not exist)">mavar</a></i> ("shepherd") through Gnomish <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mord&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mord (page does not exist)">mord</a></i> - its meaning being a poetic one for "man, warrior" or otherwise possibly derived from the "warlike qualities of the shepherds". A similar name was <i><b>Taimordo</b></i> (<i><b>Taimondo</b></i> being a corrupt form of the name), with <i><b>Daimort</b></i> (changed from *Daimord*<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[7]</a></sup>) being its Gnomish cognate.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[note 5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[9]</a></sup>

Another late name for the character was <i><b>Telmaithron</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In one of the later outlines, which might have formed the basis for *The Book of Lost Tales*, Telimektar was said to have a son called [Lúthien](</wiki/L%C3%BAthien_(son_of_Telimektar)> "Lúthien (son of Telimektar)").<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[11]</a></sup>

The idea of Telimektar and Ingil (known to [Men](/wiki/Men) in later ages as Orion and Sirius respectively) guarding against Melko's eventual return was ultimately abandoned. In its place, the idea of [Eärendel](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendel) keeping the watch on the [Door of Night](/wiki/Door_of_Night) through which Melko/Morgoth was thrust at the end of the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), was adopted instead.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[12]</a></sup>

Of the name *Telimektar* remained only the mention of [Telumehtar](/wiki/Telumehtar) as a name of Orion in the [Appendices to *The Lord of the Rings*](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Appendices "The Lord of the Rings Appendices").<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[13]</a></sup>
