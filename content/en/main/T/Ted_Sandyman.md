**Ted Sandyman** was the miller of [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md).

## History {#History}

Ted was a miller after [his father](../S/Sandyman.md). [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) did not like him, and even less when they were arguing in *[The Green Dragon](The_Green_Dragon.md)* about the strange news reaching [the Shire](The_Shire.md) from afar. Ted claimed he didn't believe in [dragons](../D/Dragons.md) (except the green one) derided Sam's story that his [cousin Hal](../H/Hal_Gamgee.md) had seen an [elm](../E/Elms.md)-like [Tree-man](../E/Ent.md) walking in the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md), and the bystanding hobbits laughed and clapped at his remarks. He also laughed with Sam's sadness for the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) leaving [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), saying that nobody saw them doing so, and considered [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo.md) and [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) "cracked" who told Sam about those topics. With that, he drained his mug and went out noisily.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Ted collaborated with [Lotho](../L/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins.md) and [Sharkey](../S/Saruman.md), in exchange for a new and much improved mill. When Sam and the [Travellers](Travellers.md) returned to Hobbiton, Ted taunted Sam over his grief at seeing the [Party Tree](The_Party_Tree.md) felled and questioned why he returned. When an angry Sam threatened him, Ted boasted of being a friend to the Boss. When he saw the reinforcements riding up behind the Travelers, Ted sounds a horn to warn [Sharkey](../S/Sharkey.md) but Merry promptly winded the [Horn of the Mark](../H/Horn_of_the_Mark.md) to summon the inhabitants of Hobbiton.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Ted's fate afterwards is unmentioned.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

It is possible [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) was inspired by the miller's son, George Andrew junior, at [Sarehole Mill](../S/Sarehole_Mill.md), Warwickshire, England.

Ronald named him &#39;the White <a href="../O/Ogres.md" title="Ogres">Ogre</a>&#39;. When he yelled at them to clear off they would scamper away from the yard...
</p>
