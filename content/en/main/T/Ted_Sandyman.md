**Ted Sandyman** was the miller of [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton).

## History {#History}

Ted was a miller after [his father](/wiki/Sandyman "Sandyman"). [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") did not like him, and even less when they were arguing in *[The Green Dragon](/wiki/The_Green_Dragon)* about the strange news reaching [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") from afar. Ted claimed he didn't believe in [dragons](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") (except the green one) derided Sam's story that his [cousin Hal](/wiki/Hal_Gamgee "Hal Gamgee") had seen an [elm](/wiki/Elms "Elms")-like [Tree-man](/wiki/Ent "Ent") walking in the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs), and the bystanding hobbits laughed and clapped at his remarks. He also laughed with Sam's sadness for the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) leaving [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), saying that nobody saw them doing so, and considered [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo) and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) "cracked" who told Sam about those topics. With that, he drained his mug and went out noisily.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Ted collaborated with [Lotho](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins "Lotho Sackville-Baggins") and [Sharkey](/wiki/Saruman "Saruman"), in exchange for a new and much improved mill. When Sam and the [Travellers](/wiki/Travellers) returned to Hobbiton, Ted taunted Sam over his grief at seeing the [Party Tree](/wiki/The_Party_Tree "The Party Tree") felled and questioned why he returned. When an angry Sam threatened him, Ted boasted of being a friend to the Boss. When he saw the reinforcements riding up behind the Travelers, Ted sounds a horn to warn [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey) but Merry promptly winded the [Horn of the Mark](/wiki/Horn_of_the_Mark) to summon the inhabitants of Hobbiton.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Ted's fate afterwards is unmentioned.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

It is possible [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) was inspired by the miller's son, George Andrew junior, at [Sarehole Mill](/wiki/Sarehole_Mill), Warwickshire, England.
