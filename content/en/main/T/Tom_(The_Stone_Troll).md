**Tom** is a character only mentioned in a verse composed by [Sam Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"). In the poem, Tom encounters an old [troll](/wiki/Stone-trolls "Stone-trolls"), gnawing for years on the shinbone of [Tim](/wiki/Tim), his [nuncle](/wiki/Unusual_words "Unusual words"), and Tom demands to let it down.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Most likely, Tim was only an invention by the poet.<sup id="cite_ref-RF_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RF-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes) who illustrated *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*, depicted Tom who kicked the troll with his *boots*, as [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil); although the name was most probably generic and there is no evidence that the author intended Tom to be Bombadil, as having an uncle and father would be absurd.

[Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) considers a possibility that Tom was somehow modelled after Bombadil, considering that Sam composed the poem soon after their meeting.<sup id="cite_ref-RF_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RF-3">[3]</a></sup>
