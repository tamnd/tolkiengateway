**The Pyre of Denethor** is the seventh chapter of the first book in *[The Return of the King](The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.

## Summary {#Summary}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Domes_of_the_Dead.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/1d/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Domes_of_the_Dead.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Domes_of_the_Dead.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="218" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/1d/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Domes_of_the_Dead.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Domes_of_the_Dead.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/1d/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Domes_of_the_Dead.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Domes_of_the_Dead.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1600" data-file-height="1160"/></a><figcaption><i>The Domes of the Dead</i> by <a href="Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

The narrative returns to the perspective of [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md). When the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) disappeared from the gate of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup> Pippin ran to [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and told him of [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md)'s madness and the situation in the tower. Gandalf wished to pursue the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) but knew he must save [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md). As they raced to the [Citadel](../C/Citadel_of_Gondor.md), Gandalf lamented the Enemy's ability to bring evil and discord to the inner circle of Minas Tirith. Reaching the door to the [House of the Stewards](../H/House_of_the_Stewards.md), the two found Denethor’s servants bearing swords and torches, standing before the lone figure of [Beregond](../B/Beregond.md), who held the door against them. Two servants had already fallen to Beregond's sword. The men cowered at the sight of Gandalf, whose appearance was like a burst of white light.

Denethor threw open the door, drawing his sword, but Gandalf lifted his hand and the sword flew from Denethor’s grip. The wizard decried Denethor’s madness, but the Steward said that Faramir had already burned. Rushing past, Gandalf found Faramir still alive on the funeral pyre. To Denethor's protests and tears, Gandalf lifted Faramir and carried him away with a strength that surprised Pippin. Denethor, the wizard said, did not have the authority to order Faramir's death.

Denethor laughed. Standing proudly, he produced from his cloak a *palantír*, similar to the [Orthanc-stone](../O/Orthanc-stone.md). He warned that the West was doomed, as he had foreseen the black ships of the Enemy approaching.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The Steward condemned Gandalf for bringing a young upstart Ranger to replace him as ruler. Denethor wished that things would remain as they always had been in Minas Tirith. He sprang for Faramir, but Beregond stopped him. Grabbing a torch from a servant, Denethor lit the funeral pyre. He threw himself into the raging fire, clutching the *palantír*.

Gandalf and Beregond carried Faramir to the [Houses of Healing](../H/Houses_of_Healing.md). As they exited, the House of Stewards collapsed in flames, and Denethor's servants ran out. Soon after, they heard a great cry from the battlefield—the sound of [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) and [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md)’s defeat of the Lord of the Nazgûl. A sense of hope returned as the sun broke through the Darkness. Gandalf discussed Denethor’s *palantír* with Pippin and Beregond. Gandalf said he had always suspected that the Steward possessed one of the [seven seeing-stones](../P/Palantíri.md). The wizard surmised that Denethor, in his growing distress, had begun to use the stone, and through it he had fallen prey to the lies of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md). <span></span>
