**Tolkien Calendar 1996** was published by Slaski Klub Fantasyki, Poland ([Polish Tolkien Society](../P/Polish_Tolkien_Society.md)). The calendar features illustrations by [Ryszard Derdziński](../R/Ryszard_Derdziński.md), <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Arkadiusz_Jubala&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Arkadiusz Jubala (page does not exist)">Arkadiusz Jubala</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Helena_Strolkowska&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Helena Strolkowska (page does not exist)">Helena Strolkowska</a>, [Małgorzata Pudlik](../M/Małgorzata_Pudlik.md), <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Maciej_Wygnanski&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maciej Wygnanski (page does not exist)">Maciej Wygnanski</a>, and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Aleksandra_Jagielowica&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aleksandra Jagielowica (page does not exist)">Aleksandra Jagielowica</a>. Reckonings in standard dates, the [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md) calendar, new reckoning of [King Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md), and the [Calendar of Imladris](../C/Calendar_of_Imladris.md).

- Dimensions: 11 3/4 x 16 1/2 inches
- White comb-bound at top
