**Tolkien Calendar 1996** was published by Slaski Klub Fantasyki, Poland ([Polish Tolkien Society](/wiki/Polish_Tolkien_Society)). The calendar features illustrations by [Ryszard Derdziński](/wiki/Ryszard_Derdzi%C5%84ski), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Arkadiusz_Jubala&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Arkadiusz Jubala (page does not exist)">Arkadiusz Jubala</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Helena_Strolkowska&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Helena Strolkowska (page does not exist)">Helena Strolkowska</a>, [Małgorzata Pudlik](/wiki/Ma%C5%82gorzata_Pudlik), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Maciej_Wygnanski&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maciej Wygnanski (page does not exist)">Maciej Wygnanski</a>, and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Aleksandra_Jagielowica&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aleksandra Jagielowica (page does not exist)">Aleksandra Jagielowica</a>. Reckonings in standard dates, the [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) calendar, new reckoning of [King Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"), and the [Calendar of Imladris](/wiki/Calendar_of_Imladris).

- Dimensions: 11 3/4 x 16 1/2 inches
- White comb-bound at top
