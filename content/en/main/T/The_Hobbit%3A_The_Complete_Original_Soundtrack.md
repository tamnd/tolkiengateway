**The Hobbit: The Complete Original Soundtrack** is the soundtrack of [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](<The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>), published in [1977](../_/1977.md) by Buena Vista records (a division of [Disney](../D/Disney.md)). It features many of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s original poems, adapted by [Jules Bass](../J/Jules_Bass.md) and set to music by [Maury Laws](../M/Maury_Laws.md). A 16 page booklet of full-color illustrations from the motion picture was included.

- Format: Boxed set including three vinyl LP's and booklet
- Product number: Buena Vista records 103
