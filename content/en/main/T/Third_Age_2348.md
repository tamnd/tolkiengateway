**Third Age 2348** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2348**) is the 2348th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2348 was also known as **S.R. 748** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of the unnamed fifth [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The fifth Prince's unnamed son becomes the sixth [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>
