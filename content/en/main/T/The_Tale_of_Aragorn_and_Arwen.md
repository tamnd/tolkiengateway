<i><b>The Tale of Aragorn and Arwen</b></i> was a [Gondorian](../G/Gondorians.md) tale written in the early [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md). It told the story of the romance of [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) from the days before Aragorn's birth until Arwen's despair at his loss.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Tale was written in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) by [Barahir](<../B/Barahir_(grandson_of_Faramir).md>), the grandson of [Prince](../P/Prince.md) [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) of [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md). It was inserted into a copy of the [Thain's Book](Thain's_Book.md) by [Findegil](../F/Findegil.md), but in a shortened form - the passages relating to the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) were removed to avoid repeating the main tale of that Book.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
