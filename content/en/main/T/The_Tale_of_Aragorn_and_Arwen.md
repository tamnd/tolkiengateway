<i><b>The Tale of Aragorn and Arwen</b></i> was a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") tale written in the early [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age). It told the story of the romance of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) from the days before Aragorn's birth until Arwen's despair at his loss.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Tale was written in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) by [Barahir](</wiki/Barahir_(grandson_of_Faramir)> "Barahir (grandson of Faramir)"), the grandson of [Prince](/wiki/Prince) [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien). It was inserted into a copy of the [Thain's Book](/wiki/Thain%27s_Book) by [Findegil](/wiki/Findegil), but in a shortened form - the passages relating to the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) were removed to avoid repeating the main tale of that Book.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
