**The Dweller** was a [Mystic](/wiki/Mystics "Mystics") acolyte who served the [Dark Wizard](/wiki/Dark_Wizard) during the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). The Dweller was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios), where she is portrayed by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Bridie_Sisson&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bridie Sisson (page does not exist)">Bridie Sisson</a>. She appeared in the first and second seasons of the series.

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Rings_of_Power_(TV_series)_-_Mystics2.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/9/9a/The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Mystics2.png/345px-The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Mystics2.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="345" height="286" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/9/9a/The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Mystics2.png/518px-The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Mystics2.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/9/9a/The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Mystics2.png/690px-The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Mystics2.png 2x" data-file-width="1040" data-file-height="861"/></a><figcaption>The three Mystics.</figcaption></figure>

At the command of the Dark wizard, the Dweller travelled west from [Caras Gaer](/wiki/Caras_Gaer) in [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn) alongside two other [Mystics](/wiki/Mystics) known as <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Nomad&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Nomad (page does not exist)">The Nomad</a> and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Ascetic&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Ascetic (page does not exist)">The Ascetic</a>. The three were in persuit of [the Stranger](/wiki/The_Stranger "The Stranger"), whom they believed to be a reembodied [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). After the Stranger's meteoric fall, the Mystics came upon the smoldering crater where he had landed in [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion).

The Mystics trailed the Stranger and an accompanying band of [Harfoots](/wiki/Harfoots) east to the place called the [Grove](/wiki/Grove). Amidst a patch of apple trees that had been renewed by the Stranger's [magic](/wiki/Magic "Magic"), [Nori Brandyfoot](/wiki/Elanor_Brandyfoot "Elanor Brandyfoot") attempted to cause a distraction to lead them away from the Stranger. Having been successfully distracted by Nori, the Dweller used her sorcery to set flame to the Harfoot caravan.

Upon finding the Stranger in the [Greenwood](/wiki/Mirkwood "Mirkwood"), the Mystics bowed to the Stranger and told him of his presumed identity as Sauron. The Stranger resisted this revelation and, once again utilizing her sorcery, the Dweller cast a spell to cause him to fall into a deep slumber. Realizing that a band of Harfoots were attempting to rescue the Stranger, the Dweller used her magic to adopt his likeness and lured them into a trap.

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Rings_of_Power_(TV_series)_-_Mystics_Defeated.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/0/0c/The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Mystics_Defeated.png/367px-The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Mystics_Defeated.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="367" height="177" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/0/0c/The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Mystics_Defeated.png/551px-The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Mystics_Defeated.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/0/0c/The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Mystics_Defeated.png/734px-The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Mystics_Defeated.png 2x" data-file-width="1492" data-file-height="720"/></a><figcaption>The Dweller (center) withering alongside the Nomad and the Ascetic from the onslaught of the Stranger&#39;s magic.</figcaption></figure>

The Stranger then awoke from his slumber and engaged in a magical battle with the Mystics. Though he initially struggled, Nori managed to bring The Stranger the staff of the Dweller. Wielding her staff, he used his overwhelming magical power to reveal that, to the horror of the Mystics, he was not Sauron but was instead one of the [Istari](/wiki/Istari). The Mystics then withered and burst into a swarm of moths.

Later, in [Caras Gaer](/wiki/Caras_Gaer), Dark Wizard resurrected the Dweller through a blood magic ritual and admonished her for not succeeding in capturing The Stranger. Her later fate is unknown.
