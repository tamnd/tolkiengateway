**Third Age 2818** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2818**) is the 2818th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2818 was also known as **S.R. 1218** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Adaldrida Bolger](../A/Adaldrida_Bolger.md), second child and daughter of [Gundahar Bolger](../G/Gundahar_Bolger.md) and [Dina Diggle](../D/Dina_Diggle.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
