**Third Age 2783** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2783**) is the 2783rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2783 was also known as **S.R. 1183** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Glóin](../G/Glóin.md), second son of [Gróin](../G/Gróin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
