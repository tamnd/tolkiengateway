**Thangorodrim** was a group of three volcanic mountains in the [Iron Mountains](../I/Iron_Mountains.md) in the north of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) during the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). The highest peaks in Middle-earth, they were raised by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), who delved his fortress of [Angband](../A/Angband.md) beneath them, and far back into the Iron Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-LazyMorgan_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LazyMorgan-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/K/Kenneth_Sofia_-_Fingolfin_rides_to_Angband.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/81/Kenneth_Sofia_-_Fingolfin_rides_to_Angband.png/300px-Kenneth_Sofia_-_Fingolfin_rides_to_Angband.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="169" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/81/Kenneth_Sofia_-_Fingolfin_rides_to_Angband.png/450px-Kenneth_Sofia_-_Fingolfin_rides_to_Angband.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/81/Kenneth_Sofia_-_Fingolfin_rides_to_Angband.png/600px-Kenneth_Sofia_-_Fingolfin_rides_to_Angband.png 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="576"/></a><figcaption><i>Fingolfin rides to Angband</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Kenneth_Sofia.md" title="Category:Images by Kenneth Sofia">Kenneth Sofia</a></figcaption></figure>

Thangorodrim was said to have been the piles of slag from Morgoth's furnaces and rubble from the delving of Angband,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> though they were solid enough to form sheer precipices; [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) was chained to a cliff of Thangorodrim,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) imprisoned on a high terrace.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The tops of Thangorodrim perpetually smoked, and sometimes spewed forth lava. The three peaks of Thangorodrim functioned as furnaces for Morgoth's great smithies deep in Angband.

For a time the [Eagles](../E/Eagles.md) lived on Thangorodrim, but at some time during the First Age they removed to the [Crissaegrim](../C/Crissaegrim.md) near [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).

At the base of the south face of the middle peak was the Great [Gates of Angband](../G/Gates_of_Angband.md), a deep canyon leading into the mountain, lined with towers and forts. There were also a number of secret gates scattered around the sides of the mountain group, from which Morgoth's hosts could issue forth and surprise their foes.

Thangorodrim was destroyed in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) at the end of the First Age when the defeated dragon [Ancalagon](../A/Ancalagon.md) the Black fell on them.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/J/John_Howe_-_Thangorodrim.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/af/John_Howe_-_Thangorodrim.jpg/300px-John_Howe_-_Thangorodrim.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="298" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/af/John_Howe_-_Thangorodrim.jpg/450px-John_Howe_-_Thangorodrim.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/af/John_Howe_-_Thangorodrim.jpg/600px-John_Howe_-_Thangorodrim.jpg 2x" data-file-width="674" data-file-height="670"/></a><figcaption><i>Thangorodrim</i> by <a href="../J/John_Howe.md" title="John Howe">John Howe</a></figcaption></figure>

The position and size of Thangorodrim are unclear. One drawing by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), if to scale, would have made Thangorodrim 35,000 ft high, and the statement that it lay 150 leagues (450 [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) miles) north of [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md) puts it too far away for some of the action in *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)* to make sense; a distance of 150–200 miles would have been more consistent. It is possible that with the higher figure Tolkien was not referring to 'as the eagle flies', but rather 'as the wolf runs': the plateau of [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) forced a long detour which added the extra 200, 250 miles to the distance.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Thangorodrim* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Mountains of Tyranny"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-7">[7]</a></sup> or "mountain-chain of tyranny".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The name consists of *[thang](Thang.md)* ("compulsion, oppression") + *[orodrim](../O/Orodrim.md)* ("mountain-chain").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In earlier [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md), *Thangorodrim* is glossed as "Mountains of Duress".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
