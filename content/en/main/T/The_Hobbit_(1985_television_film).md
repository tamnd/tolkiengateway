**Сказочное путешествие мистера Бильбо Бэггинса, Хоббита** (English: The Fairytale Journey of Mr. Bilbo Baggins, The Hobbit) is an unlicensed television film of *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* from the Soviet Union. The complete name of the film is *Сказочное путешествие мистера Бильбо Бэггинса, Хоббита, через дикий край, чёрный лес, за туманные горы. Туда и обратно. По сказочной повести Джона Толкина "Хоббит"*.

This children's film featured Zinovy ​​Gerdt as "The Professor" (a narrator stand-in for [Tolkien](Tolkien.md)), Mikhail Danilov as [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo.md), Anatoly Ravikovich as [Thorin](Thorin.md), Ivan Krasko as [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and Igor Dmitriev as [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md). [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) and the [Spiders](../S/Spiders.md) were portrayed by puppets. Missing in this version are the [Trolls](Trolls.md), [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md), [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md) and the [Wood-elves](../W/Wood-elves.md). The [Goblins](../G/Goblins.md) are human-like with little makeup and portrayed by dancers from the Leningrad State Academic Opera and Ballet Theatre (as were the inhabitants of Lake Town).

The film was shot in [1984](../_/1984.md) as a teleplay and produced in the framework of the children's TV series *Tale after Tale* (Russian: Сказка за сказкой) aired on the Leningrad TV Channel in the 1980s and the 1990s.
