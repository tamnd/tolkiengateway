**Сказочное путешествие мистера Бильбо Бэггинса, Хоббита** (English: The Fairytale Journey of Mr. Bilbo Baggins, The Hobbit) is an unlicensed television film of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* from the Soviet Union. The complete name of the film is *Сказочное путешествие мистера Бильбо Бэггинса, Хоббита, через дикий край, чёрный лес, за туманные горы. Туда и обратно. По сказочной повести Джона Толкина "Хоббит"*.

This children's film featured Zinovy ​​Gerdt as "The Professor" (a narrator stand-in for [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien)), Mikhail Danilov as [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo), Anatoly Ravikovich as [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin), Ivan Krasko as [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and Igor Dmitriev as [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum). [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) and the [Spiders](/wiki/Spiders) were portrayed by puppets. Missing in this version are the [Trolls](/wiki/Trolls), [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn) and the [Wood-elves](/wiki/Wood-elves). The [Goblins](/wiki/Goblins) are human-like with little makeup and portrayed by dancers from the Leningrad State Academic Opera and Ballet Theatre (as were the inhabitants of Lake Town).

The film was shot in [1984](/wiki/1984) as a teleplay and produced in the framework of the children's TV series *Tale after Tale* (Russian: Сказка за сказкой) aired on the Leningrad TV Channel in the 1980s and the 1990s.
