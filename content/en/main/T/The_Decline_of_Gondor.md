**The Decline of Gondor** is the twelfth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)"). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) exits the [throne-room](/wiki/Hall_of_the_Kings "Hall of the Kings") of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) in a tempered fury, bringing [Pippin](/wiki/Pippin) with him. He is at odds with [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) and believes it likely that the city will fall due to the madness of the steward. As a consequence, Gandalf says, the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/White_Tree" title="White Tree">White Tree</a> will not bloom again. He tells Pippin that the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) around it are guarding it out of a faint hope that the king will come and it will flower again. They walk along the bastion of stone as Gandalf tells the young hobbit of the decay of Gondor.

From their vantage-point, Pippin can see [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) clearly. A large bulk of dark cloud is seen coming from Mordor. Gandalf explains that the unnatural weather is of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s making; it protects his army of [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") who shun daylight. When the cloud reaches the city, war will begin, Gandalf says. He tells Pippin they will not be leaving the city and that help must come to Minas Tirith once the war begins.

## Differences {#Differences}

Rather than discuss the decline of Gondor after their audience with Denethor, in the books — specifically, in the chapter [Minas Tirith](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(chapter)> "Minas Tirith (chapter)") — Gandalf and Pippin are led to a house in the city. There, Pippin meets [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond). Some of the dialogue regarding Mordor from the discussion towards the end of this scene was taken from a conversation between Pippin and Beregond, as was the description of the "gathering gloom in the East".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
