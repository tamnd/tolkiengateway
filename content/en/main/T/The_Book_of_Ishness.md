<i><b>The Book of Ishness</b></i> was a sketchbook, begun by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) on [6 January](/wiki/6_January) [1914](/wiki/1914), where he collected illustrations devoted to "imaginative subjects".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff) has suggested that Tolkien's creation of the word *ishness* might have been inspired by a passage in chapter 7 of [Lewis Carroll](/wiki/Lewis_Carroll)'s *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*: "did you ever see a thing as a drawing of a muchness!".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Many of the paintings and sketches have been published in various artwork collections, such as *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Life and Legend](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Life_and_Legend)* and *[Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth](/wiki/Tolkien:_Maker_of_Middle-earth)*.
