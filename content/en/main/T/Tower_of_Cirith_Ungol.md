The **Tower of Cirith Ungol** ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), "Spider's cleft") was a fortress on the left side of the pass of [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol) in the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) on the western border of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") was taken into the Tower and searched by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs); however, he was rescued by [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") before they descended onto the [Plateau of Gorgoroth](/wiki/Plateau_of_Gorgoroth). It is possible that the Tower of Cirith Ungol was originally called by a different name, because the pass of Cirith Ungol was previously called Cirith Dúath.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Appearance {#Appearance}

The Tower stood against the eastern mountain face of the Ephel Dúath on the mountain slope on the left side of the pass of [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> It looked down on the trough between the Ephel Dúath and the [Morgai](/wiki/Morgai) and on the plateau of [Gorgoroth](/wiki/Gorgoroth) beyond the Morgai. From the west only the round, topmost turret was visible. The turret was supported by three great tiers with pointed bastions that stood on a shelf in the mountain-wall far below and looked north-east and south-east into Mordor. The lowest tier was encircled by a thirty-foot high wall with overhanging battlements that enclosed a narrow paved courtyard. The main gate on the south-eastern side of the wall opened on to a broad road that ran along a precipice down to a southward bend and down to join the road that came over the [Morgul Pass](/wiki/Morgul_Pass). A narrower path led down from Cirith Ungol by stairs to meet the broad road near the gate. In the gate were the [Two Watchers](/wiki/Two_Watchers).

Across the courtyard a great door at the foot of the tower led to its interior. A wide passage with doors on either side led from the door to the mountain-side. At the end of the passage was a great arched door that was the inner side of the [Undergate](/wiki/Undergate). To the right of the Undergate was a winding stairway that led to the upper levels of the tower. The top of the stairs was covered by a domed chamber, with low doors facing east and west leading out onto the flat roof of the third tier of the tower. The roof was about 20 yards across and was surrounded by a parapet. On the western side of the roof stood the turret of the tower whose top rose high above the crest of the hills behind the tower. Another winding stairway inside the turret led up to the first and second storey of the turret. The turret had window-slits facing westward and eastward through which torchlight glowed like red eyes. In the ceiling of the passage, a trap-door led to the middle of the floor of a large round chamber at the top of the tower.<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-4">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Third Age before the War of the Ring {#Third_Age_before_the_War_of_the_Ring}

The Tower of Cirith Ungol was originally built by the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) after the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) as an eastern outpost of the defences of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien)<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-4">[3]</a></sup> and of [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> Its purpose was to defend Ithilien and Minas Ithil from attacks from [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s remaining servants by guarding the pass of [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol) and watching movements in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-4">[3]</a></sup>

Later the vigilance of Gondor failed and the Tower of Cirith Ungol was yielded up to the [Lord of the Ringwraiths](/wiki/Witch-king "Witch-king") due to treachery.<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 2]</a></sup>

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

On [13 March](/wiki/13_March) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), Orcs from the Tower, under the command of [Shagrat](/wiki/Shagrat), captured Frodo after finding his unconscious body in the Pass below.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-7">[5]</a></sup> They carried his body through the [Under-way](/wiki/Under-way) and into the [Undergate](/wiki/Undergate) that, besides the main gate, was the only entrance to the Tower. After overhearing Shagrat and [Gorbag](/wiki/Gorbag) talking, Sam realized that Frodo had only been paralysed by [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob) and not killed as he had first thought. Sam was too late to gain entry through the Undergate and knocked himself senseless trying to get in.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup>

When Sam awoke on [14 March](/wiki/14_March) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") he determined to enter the main gate to find Frodo.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-7">[5]</a></sup> Mastering his fears, Sam came to the main gate and was halted by the malevolent will of the [Two Watchers](/wiki/Two_Watchers), stone statues flanking either side of the gate, topped with three vulture-faces each and endowed with spirits of evil. Holding aloft the [Phial of Galadriel](/wiki/Phial_of_Galadriel) Sam overcame their will and thrust through the gate, but the Watchers let forth a shrill cry that was echoed by a harsh bell above.

In the meantime, the orcs had searched Frodo. When they found the [shirt of mithril](/wiki/Mithril-coat "Mithril-coat") mail Frodo was wearing, a quarrel over the spoils erupted. A contingent of orcs from [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) led by [Gorbag](/wiki/Gorbag) fought Shagrat and his company for possession of the mithril. The fighting spread throughout the tower and most of the orcs on both sides were killed, making it possible for Sam to proceed and rescue Frodo. Although Sam fought Shagrat, the Orc got away, bearing Frodo's gear to [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr). Sam finally found Frodo in the topmost chamber after killing [Snaga](</wiki/Snaga_(orc_of_Mordor)> "Snaga (orc of Mordor)"). Sam returned the [One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") to Frodo, dressed him and gathered food and gear, and they left the tower. On the way out their use of the Phial caused the gate to crumble behind them. Again a bell clanged and the watchers issued a high and dreadful wail. This was answered far above in the darkness and out of the sky dropped the winged shape of a [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup> with a ghastly shriek.<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-4">[3]</a></sup>

In the first draft ('A') of the chapter The Tower of Kirith Ungol the tower was described to consist of four great tiers and Tolkien drew a sketch of the tower with four tiers. It is mentioned that it was made after the overthrow of Sauron in the days of the Last Alliance. It is described to have been the easternmost outpost of the defence of not only Ithilien, but also of Minas Ithil.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[8]</a></sup>

It is said that in the same year of the closing of the Dúnadan fortresses guarding Mordor Sauron sent all <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ringwraiths" title="Ringwraiths">Ringwraiths</a> except the <a href="/wiki/Witch-king" title="Witch-king">Witch-king</a> and <a href="/wiki/Kham%C3%BBl" title="Khamûl">Khamûl</a> to lake <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/N%C3%BArnen" title="Núrnen">Núrnen</a> with the mission to ready Mordor for his return. It is described that they sent scouts north who found that the Gondorian fortresses were abandoned and that the Ringwraiths Ren and Indûr occupied the Tower of Cirith Ungol. It is further said that Gondor concerned with internal matters scarcely noted the activity.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In the <a href="/wiki/Gorgoroth_(book)" title="Gorgoroth (book)">Gorgoroth</a> campaign module it is described that the Ringwraiths Indûr Dawndeath and Ren The Unclean met the other Ringwraiths except the Witch-king in <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_1640" title="Third Age 1640">1640</a> in the castle of Ostigurth to prepare the Black Land for Sauron&#39;s return. It is described that Indûr joined his companions in the siege of Minas Ithil in <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2000" title="Third Age 2000">2000</a>. It is further mentioned that Ren often travelled between Barad-wath and Minas Morgul after <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2002" title="Third Age 2002">2002</a>. It is mentioned that the basic garrison of the three-tiered Barad Ungol (the Tower of Cirith Ungol) consists of 48 Uruk-hai and 160 Orcs.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In Scene 43, &#34;The Choices of Master Samwise&#34;, Frodo first sees the turret at the top of the tower through a narrow cleft, with a single red light glowing. Frodo is stung by <a href="/wiki/Shelob" title="Shelob">Shelob</a>, Sam fights and defeats the spider, and then Shagrat and orcs come upon Frodo&#39;s body. There is no Under-way or Undergate; the Orcs carry the body up the path with Sam following behind.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In Scene 58, &#34;The Tower of Cirith Ungol&#34; (Extended Version), the view zooms into a window of the turret to show Frodo, still unconscious, lying on the floor while Shagrat and Gorbag go through his possessions. Frodo awakes while the two Orcs find the mithril coat. Unlike their roles in the book, Shagrat wants the coat for himself while Gorbag insists it goes to Sauron. A fight breaks out that soon envelopes all of the Orcs in the Tower. As the Orcs slaughter each other, Sam approaches the gate and spies the Two Watchers. The scene jumps to Sam inside the gate without showing the contest of wills. Sam climbs the inside of the tower and his shadow scares four Orcs, including Shagrat himself, descending the stairs. Sam kills three of the Orcs while Shagrat escapes with the mithril coat. In the turret room Frodo struggles to free himself, attracting the attention of Gorbag, who threatens to bleed him &#34;like a stuck pig&#34;. However, the point of <a href="/wiki/Sting" title="Sting">Sting</a> suddenly pops out of Gorbag&#39;s chest as Sam kills the Orc. Sam reluctantly returns the Ring to Frodo and they dress themselves as Orcs before they leave the Tower. Their escape past the Two Watchers is not shown.</dd></dl>
