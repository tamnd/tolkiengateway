The **Third Marshal of the Riddermark** was the third such Marshal, the highest military rank among the [Riders of Rohan](/wiki/%C3%89ored "Éored"), and title of the lieutenants of the [King of Rohan](/wiki/King_of_Rohan).

The Third Marshal‘s duties, like those of the [Second Marshal of the Riddermark](/wiki/Second_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark), were not fixed, but assigned by the King as the times demanded.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

## During the War of the Ring {#During_the_War_of_the_Ring}

Prior to the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) was under [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)’s power and the influence of [Grima Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma"), through whose voice the King’s orders were made. At this time and during the War of the Ring, the Third Marshal was [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer), son of [Éomund](/wiki/%C3%89omund) (also a Marshal) and nephew of King Théoden.  Éomer’s base of operations was at [Aldburg](/wiki/Aldburg) in the [Folde](/wiki/Folde).

After Théodred was slain and King Théoden had regained his health, Éomer effectively became First Marshall of the Riddermark, although did not officially take that title. [Erkenbrand](/wiki/Erkenbrand) took command of the Western Muster.  At the Full Muster ahead of the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), [Grimbold](/wiki/Grimbold) took command of the Muster of the West-mark, however the title of Third Marshal was not given.  Grombold was slain at the Battle of Pelennor Fields.

After the War of the Ring, Éomer became King and revised the old tradition of the three marshals, giving Erkenbrand the new title of [Marshal of the West-mark](/wiki/Marshal_of_the_West-mark).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup><span></span>
