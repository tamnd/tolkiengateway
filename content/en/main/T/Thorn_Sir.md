**Thorn Sir** ("Eagle-stream") was a river according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. It was located beneath [Cristhorn](/wiki/Cirith_Thoronath "Cirith Thoronath"), at the bottom of a gorge in the [Encircling Mountains](/wiki/Encircling_Mountains) into which [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) and the [balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") fell. After this battle the river was stained black by the balrog's blood for many days.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognate was <i><b>Eäsindi</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

This river was later mentioned as **Thornsir** in an unfinished [lay of Eärendel](/wiki/Fragment_of_an_alliterative_Lay_of_E%C3%A4rendel "Fragment of an alliterative Lay of Eärendel"),<sup id="cite_ref-LB_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LB-3">[3]</a></sup> but was not used by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in any other text. <span></span>
