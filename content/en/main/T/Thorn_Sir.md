**Thorn Sir** ("Eagle-stream") was a river according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. It was located beneath [Cristhorn](../C/Cirith_Thoronath.md), at the bottom of a gorge in the [Encircling Mountains](../E/Encircling_Mountains.md) into which [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) and the [balrog](../B/Balrogs.md) fell. After this battle the river was stained black by the balrog's blood for many days.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) cognate was <i><b>Eäsindi</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

This river was later mentioned as **Thornsir** in an unfinished [lay of Eärendel](../F/Fragment_of_an_alliterative_Lay_of_Eärendel.md),<sup id="cite_ref-LB_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LB-3">[3]</a></sup> but was not used by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in any other text. <span></span>
