**Third Age 2858** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2858**) is the 2858th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2858 was also known as **S.R. 1258** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Folcred](../F/Folcred.md) and [Fastred](<../F/Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine).md>), first two children and twin sons of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Folcwine](../F/Folcwine.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Balbo Baggins](../B/Balbo_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Death of [Bosco Boffin](../B/Bosco_Boffin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
