**The Departure of the Fellowship** is the thirtieth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition).md>). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

With the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) ready to depart [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) re-iterates the [Ring-bearer's](../F/Frodo.md) quest is for [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md) and that there is no obligation for those travelling with him to go further than they wish. He bids them blessings for their journey and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) then tells [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) they are ready to leave. The hobbit looks around somewhat nervously as he asks "[Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) Gandalf, is it left or right?" In response, the wizard tells him "Left".

[Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) shares a last long look with [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) before he gives her a slight nod and smile and follows the rest of the [Fellowship](../F/Fellowship.md) over the bridge out of Rivendell.
