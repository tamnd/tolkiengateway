**The Walt Disney Company**, also known simply as **Disney**, is an American media and entertainment conglomerate, founded in 1923 by the brothers Walter Elias Disney and Roy Oliver Disney.

<h2><span id="Tolkien.27s_thoughts"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Tolkien&#39;s_thoughts">Tolkien&#39;s thoughts</span></h2>

For [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), the products of the Disney company were an especially prominent example of bad style.

<br> A remarkable critique of Walt Disney was given by Tolkien in a [1964](../_/1964.md) letter to a fan:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I recognize [Walt Disney&#39;s] talent, but it has always seemed to me hopelessly corrupted. Though in most of the &#39;pictures&#39; proceeding from his studios there are admirable or charming passages, the effect of all of them to me is disgusting. Some have given me nausea
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../L/Letter_to_Miss_J.L._Curry.md" title="Letter to Miss J.L. Curry">Letter to Miss J.L. Curry</a>, of 15 July 1964]]</cite></p>
</blockquote>

In [1946](../_/1946.md), he rejected suggestions for illustrations by [Horus Engels](../H/Horus_Engels.md) for the German edition of the *Hobbit* as *"too Disnified"*,

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Bilbo with a dribbling nose, and Gandalf as a figure of vulgar fun rather than the Odinic wanderer that I think of.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../L/Letter_107.md" title="Letter 107">Letter 107</a></cite></p>
</blockquote>

Moreover, throughout his life Tolkien had a deep concern and strong opinions about adaptations of his [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md). when certain producers became interested in movie adaptations of Tolkien's works, (he had sold the film, stage and merchandise rights of *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* to United Artists in 1968), he forbade Disney should ever be involved:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>It might be advisable [...] to let the Americans do what seems good to them — as long as it was possible [...] to veto anything from or influenced by the Disney studios (for all whose works I have a heartfelt loathing).
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../L/Letter_13.md" title="Letter 13">Letter 13</a></cite></p>
</blockquote>

In [another letter](../L/Letter_to_Jane_Dixon.md), Tolkien writes in [1965](../_/1965.md) that neither he nor his publishers ([George Allen and Unwin](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md)) intend to give Walt Disney permission to make a film based on his books.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Tolkien-related Disney products {#Tolkien-related_Disney_products}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Hobbit_Disney.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/84/The_Hobbit_Disney.jpg/300px-The_Hobbit_Disney.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="296" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/8/84/The_Hobbit_Disney.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="304" data-file-height="300"/></a><figcaption>Cover of the <i>The Hobbit Read-Along</i></figcaption></figure>

Nevertheless, Disney's Disneyland and Buena Vista record labels were to release six audio products based on the [Rankin/Bass *The Hobbit*](<The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>) and two based on [Rankin/Bass *The Return of the King*](<The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md>) in the 1970s and 1980s.

- [1977](../_/1977.md): *[The Hobbit: The Complete Original Soundtrack](The_Hobbit%253A_The_Complete_Original_Soundtrack.md)*
- [1977](../_/1977.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Hobbit_(Disneyland_Read-Along)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Hobbit (Disneyland Read-Along) (page does not exist)">The Hobbit (Disneyland Read-Along)</a></i>
- [1977](../_/1977.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Hobbit_(Disneyland_Storyteller)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Hobbit (Disneyland Storyteller) (page does not exist)">The Hobbit (Disneyland Storyteller)</a></i>
- [1980](../_/1980.md): *[The Return of the King: A Story of the Hobbits](The_Return_of_the_King%253A_A_Story_of_the_Hobbits.md)*

Disney has also in its possession the television network *ABC*, the original television network of Rankin/Bass *The Return of the King*, which acquired it in [1996](../_/1996.md).

Moreover, Disney has released through Fox Searchlight Pictures the biographical film *[Tolkien](<Tolkien_(film).md>)* ([2019](../_/2019.md)), which covers the early years of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) until the [World War I](../W/World_War_I.md).
