**Third Age 2510** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2510**) is the 2510th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2510 was also known as **S.R. 910** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Within this year {#Within_this_year}

- [Celebrían](../C/Celebrían.md) departs over [Sea](../B/Belegaer.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## March {#March}

- 10 [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md) sends the first messengers to the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA2510_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2510-2">[2]</a></sup>
- 25 [Borondir](../B/Borondir.md) delivers Cirion's plea for help to [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl_the_Young.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA2510_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2510-2">[2]</a></sup>

## March - April {#March_-_April}

- [Wainrider/Balchoth War](../W/Wainrider%252FBalchoth_War.md): [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Balchoth](../B/Balchoth.md) overrun [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## April {#April}

- 6 [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl_the_Young.md) and his *[éoherë](../_/Éoherë.md)* set out for [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA2510_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2510-2">[2]</a></sup>
- 15 A [Gondorian](../G/Gondorian.md) army moves north and fights the [Balchoth](../B/Balchoth.md) north of the [Limlight](../L/Limlight.md); a horde of [Orcs](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) from the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) surrounds them.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl_the_Young.md) comes to their aid and [wins the victory](../B/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md) of the [Field of Celebrant](../F/Field_of_Celebrant.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA2510_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2510-2">[2]</a></sup>

## August {#August}

- The [Oaths](../O/Oath_of_Cirion.md) of [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md) and [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl_the_Young.md) are spoken; [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) is ceded to the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) and becomes the Kingdom of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA2510_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2510-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl_the_Young.md) returns to the North; he brings the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) to settle in [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
