**THON** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) without any given signification, except for what can be determined from its derivatives.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>
