**Third Age 2780** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2780**) is the 2780th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2780 was also known as **S.R. 1180** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Walda](../W/Walda.md), son of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Brytta](../B/Brytta.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Gundahad Bolger](../G/Gundahad_Bolger.md), third son of [Gundolpho Bolger](../G/Gundolpho_Bolger.md) and [Alfrida of the Yale](../A/Alfrida_of_the_Yale.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
