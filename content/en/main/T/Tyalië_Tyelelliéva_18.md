<b><i>Tyalië Tyelelliéva</i> 18</b> is an issue of the journal *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va)*. The issue includes two prayers in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya):

- *Átaremma* (Pater Noster - The Lord's Prayer)
- *Aia María* (Ave Maria - Hail Mary)

They are reproduced in [Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger)'s article *Syntactical and Etymological Analysis of J.R.R. Tolkien's Quenya translation of the Lord's Prayer and Hail Mary*.
