**Third Age 1979** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1979**) is the 1979th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1979 was also known as **S.R. 379** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Bucca](../B/Bucca_of_the_Marish.md) of the [Marish](../M/Marish.md) becomes first [Thain](Thain.md) of [the Shire](The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
