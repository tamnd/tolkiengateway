<i><b>The Year&#39;s Work in English Studies</b></i> was a journal published annually by the English Association. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) contributed three review-essays for the section *Philology: General Works*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The first review-essay was published in the first issue of Volume IV for 1923 (published on [8 January](../_/8_January.md) in [1925](../_/1925.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> though the imprint date states 1924), pages 20–37.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The second review-essay was published in the first issue of Volume 5 for 1924 (published in March of 1926), pages 26–65.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The third review-essay was published in the first issue of Volume VI for 1925 (published in February of 1927), pages 32–66.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Tolkien wrote about writing the W sections for the [Oxford English Dictionary](../O/Oxford_English_Dictionary.md) as well as publications concerning names of places. Throughout the essays he attempted to inform the reader while including bits of humor to entertain.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
