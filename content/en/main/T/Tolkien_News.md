**Tolkien News** was a not-for-profit website which sought to aggregate [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related news, in direct competition with the more commercial <a class="external text" href="http://www.theonering.net" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">TheOneRing.net</a>.

Tolkien News was founded on [1 January](/wiki/1_January), [2006](/wiki/2006), however, as [Hyarion](/wiki/User:Hyarion "User:Hyarion") (the founder) was tasked with the sole responsibility of its upkeep the site closed in [2008](/wiki/2008).
