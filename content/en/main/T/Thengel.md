**Thengel the Thrice-renowned** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [2905](/wiki/Third_Age_2905 "Third Age 2905") – [2980](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980"), aged 75 years) was the sixteenth [King of Rohan](/wiki/King_of_Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-Thengel_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thengel-2">[2]</a></sup> Thengel was the only son of [Fengel](/wiki/Fengel), and became king after the death of his father in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2953](/wiki/Third_Age_2953 "Third Age 2953").<sup id="cite_ref-Fengel_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fengel-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

His father king Fengel was a greedy man who quarreled with his marshals and children. When he reached his manhood Thengel left Rohan and went to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), where he won great renown in the service of Steward [Turgon](</wiki/Turgon_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Turgon (Steward of Gondor)").<sup id="cite_ref-Fengel_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fengel-1">[1]</a></sup> Eventually he met and married [Morwen Steelsheen](/wiki/Morwen_Steelsheen) of [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach) in [2943](/wiki/Third_Age_2943 "Third Age 2943"), and he lived with her there for a while. Morwen bore him three children during their time in Gondor - his only son (his second child) [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) in [2948](/wiki/Third_Age_2948 "Third Age 2948") and two daughters.<sup id="cite_ref-Thengel_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thengel-2">[2]</a></sup>

After Fengel died in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2953](/wiki/Third_Age_2953 "Third Age 2953"),<sup id="cite_ref-Fengel_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fengel-1">[1]</a></sup> Thengel was called to come back to Rohan and reluctantly returned with his wife and three children from Gondor. After his return [Westron](/wiki/Westron),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the speech of Gondor, was used in his house, which some men disapproved.<sup id="cite_ref-Thengel_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thengel-2">[2]</a></sup> Soon afterwards the [wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards") [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) declared himself as Lord of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) and began to support the enemies of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-Thengel_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thengel-2">[2]</a></sup> Around [2957](/wiki/Third_Age_2957 "Third Age 2957") or later, a mysterious Northerner calling himself [Thorongil](/wiki/Thorongil) came to Rohan and served Thengel for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Later Thorongil went to Gondor where he entered the service of Steward [Ecthelion II](/wiki/Ecthelion_II) and became a great captain and great leader of men by land or by sea.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In Rohan Morwen bore Thengel two more daughters, including [Théodwyn](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odwyn) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2963](/wiki/Third_Age_2963 "Third Age 2963"),<sup id="cite_ref-Thengel_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thengel-2">[2]</a></sup> who would be the mother of [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) and [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

After Thengel the kings of Rohan and their descendants also spoke [Sindarin](/wiki/Gondor_Sindarin "Gondor Sindarin"), the language of nobles in Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Thengel* is a modernized spelling of the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) word *Þengel*, which means "prince".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
