The <i><b>Tolkien Calendar 2025</b></i> is the official Tolkien calendar published by [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md) on [13 August](../_/13_August.md) [2024](../_/2024.md). It features art by [John Howe](../J/John_Howe.md), including a new illustration. HarperCollins began shipping calendars without plastic wrappings.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The official Tolkien calendar, this year containing 13 stunning paintings inspired by the Three Ages of J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s Middle-earth. Featuring breathtaking paintings and drawings by John Howe, with new pieces unique to this calendar.
</p>
</blockquote>
