<b><i>The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</i> (extended edition)</b> is an extended version of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)*. It has 13 minutes of additional footage added back into the film (bringing its total running time up to 182 minutes), as well as multiple documentaries about the making of the film as supplements.

## Special Extended Edition (disc 1) {#Special_Extended_Edition_(disc_1)}

The first disc contains the extended version of the film with the following audio commentary:

- [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) and [Philippa Boyens](/wiki/Philippa_Boyens)

NOTE: The film is split over 2 discs for both the DVD and Blu-ray 3-D editions; the breaking point coming between chapters 20 &amp; 21 (the Blu-ray 3-D edition also contains the regular 2-D Blu-ray version of the film as disc 3).
