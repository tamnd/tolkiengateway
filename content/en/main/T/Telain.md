**Telain** ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), plural of *Talan*<sup id="cite_ref-UT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-1">[1]</a></sup>), known in [Westron](/wiki/Westron) as **flets**, were platforms built in the trees by the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).

## History {#History}

*Telain* were built high up [Mallorn](/wiki/Mallorn) trees, created in the space where the trunk of the tree divided into many branches; they lacked walls, or even rails, but portable screens were put to keep out the wind; they were only accessible via a lowered *[hithlain](/wiki/Hithlain "Hithlain")* rope-ladder. They were mainly used as guard-posts and watch-towers around the edges of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-Loth_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Loth-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Caras Galadhon](/wiki/Caras_Galadhon), however, *telain* were more common and served more of a ceremonial function: it was on a house built on a *talan* that the [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") met with [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn).<sup id="cite_ref-Mirror_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mirror-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Flet* is an archaic English word referring to the floor of a house or a flat.

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word *talan* or *tâl* ("flat space, platform"; pl. *telain*) is a descendant form of [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) *talam-*. The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) form is *talma* or *talan*, pl. *talami*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><i>Telain</i> were retained in the film, although there were no moveable plaited screens or rope-ladders visible. In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> the meeting with <a href="/wiki/Galadriel" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a> and <a href="/wiki/Celeborn" title="Celeborn">Celeborn</a> took place inside a large hall built on a <i>talan</i>,<sup id="cite_ref-Mirror_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mirror-3">[3]</a></sup> whereas in the film it took place on an open <i>talan</i>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>All Telain outside of Caras Galadhon have open sides and perform mostly defensive function, although some are recreational. Within Caras Galadhon itself, the larger telain are enclosed from the outside, while the wooden platforms themselves still provide an open walkable area.</dd></dl>
