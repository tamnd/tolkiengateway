**Third Age 2060** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2060**) is the 2060th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2060 was also known as **S.R. 460** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The power of [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) grows. [The Wise](The_Wise.md) fear that it may be [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) taking shape again.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Mardil](../M/Mardil.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) issues a [Revised Calendar](../R/Revised_Calendar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of the unnamed second [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
