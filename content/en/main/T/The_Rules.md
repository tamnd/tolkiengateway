**The Rules** were the laws that the [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits) followed within [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire).<sup id="cite_ref-Order_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Order-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the time of the [Kings of Arthedain](/wiki/King_of_Arthedain "King of Arthedain"), the Shire-hobbits were obliged to acknowledge their lordship, to speed their messengers, and to maintain the Shire's bridges and roads.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Possibly their obligations included allowing the King to visit the land three times a year for hunting, as the woods and moorlands of the Shire had been a royal park.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The Shire-hobbits kept these Rules of their own free will long after Arthedain fell, recognizing their justness and antiquity, though they still attributed them to the old King of Arthedain, never forgetting that they gave them the Rules.<sup id="cite_ref-Order_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Order-1">[1]</a></sup>

Throughout the history of the Shire, the [Shirriffs](/wiki/Shirriffs) of the [Watch](/wiki/Watch) had no need to enforce thes Rules.<sup id="cite_ref-Order_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Order-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), after [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) sold [Bag-End](/wiki/Bag-End) to the [Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Sackville-Baggins_Family "Sackville-Baggins Family"), [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins) took advantage of the lack of enforcement and established himself as the [Chief Shirriff](/wiki/Chief_Shirriff) after locking up [Will Whitfoot](/wiki/Will_Whitfoot). He then had his own version of *The Rules* written with multiple copies posted on every wall in every building within the Shire. Anyone who did not follow these new rules got sent to the [Lockholes](/wiki/Lockholes). He also added an unknown number of Shirriffs to the Watch to enforce his rules and established the [First Eastfarthing Troop](/wiki/First_Eastfarthing_Troop).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-4">[4]</a></sup>

Some of Lotho's new rules included "Gate-breaking, and Tearing up of Rules, and Assaulting <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gate-keepers&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gate-keepers (page does not exist)">Gate-keepers</a>, and Trespassing, and Sleeping in Shire-buildings without Leave, and Bribing <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gate-keepers&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gate-keepers (page does not exist)">Guards</a> with Food". Most of Lotho's new rules were broken by the [Travellers](/wiki/Travellers) when they returned to the Shire. [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) in particular broke Rule 4 by using the majority of the next day's wood allowance that very night.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-4">[4]</a></sup>

After the Shire was retaken, Lotho's new Rules were never mentioned again,<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-4">[4]</a></sup> possibly implying that the Shire removed all of Lotho's contributions to the Rules.
