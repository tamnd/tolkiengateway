**Third Age 3001** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3001**) is the 3001st [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Third Age 3001 was also known as **S.R. 1401** in [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning).

Notable events in this year include:

## September {#September}

- [Gaffer Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee), [Old Noakes](/wiki/Old_Noakes), [Daddy Twofoot](/wiki/Daddy_Twofoot), [Sandyman](/wiki/Sandyman) and other strangers are at the *[Ivy Bush](/wiki/Ivy_Bush)* and gossip about [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>
- (two days later) Rumors begin that Bilbo's party will have fireworks.<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>
- A [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor "Dwarves of Erebor")-wagon loaded with packages comes to [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>
- c. **[15](/wiki/15_September "15 September")**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) rides a cart over the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge), through [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater) and reaches Bag End.<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The following day and week more wagons and carts arrive. Bilbo sends orders for provisions from the neighboring regions.<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")** - Cloudy weather over [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton).<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")** - [Bilbo's farewell feast](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party"). [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) suspects Bilbo's ring to be [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **[23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")** - [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) inherits [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) and has a hard time managing the inheritance and distributing Bilbo's gifts. At the evening [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) departs seeking for news of [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum).<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Gandalf calls on the help of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn). The [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North) double their guard on [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
