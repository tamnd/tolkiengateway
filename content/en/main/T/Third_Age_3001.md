**Third Age 3001** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3001**) is the 3001st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 3001 was also known as **S.R. 1401** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## September {#September}

- [Gaffer Gamgee](../G/Gaffer_Gamgee.md), [Old Noakes](../O/Old_Noakes.md), [Daddy Twofoot](../D/Daddy_Twofoot.md), [Sandyman](../S/Sandyman.md) and other strangers are at the *[Ivy Bush](../I/Ivy_Bush.md)* and gossip about [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>
- (two days later) Rumors begin that Bilbo's party will have fireworks.<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>
- A [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves_of_Erebor.md)-wagon loaded with packages comes to [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md).<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>
- c. **[15](../_/15_September.md)**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) rides a cart over the [Brandywine Bridge](../B/Brandywine_Bridge.md), through [Bywater](../B/Bywater.md) and reaches Bag End.<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The following day and week more wagons and carts arrive. Bilbo sends orders for provisions from the neighboring regions.<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[21](../_/21_September.md)** - Cloudy weather over [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md).<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[22](../_/22_September.md)** - [Bilbo's farewell feast](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md). [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) suspects Bilbo's ring to be [the One Ring](The_One_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **[23](../_/23_September.md)** - [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) inherits [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) and has a hard time managing the inheritance and distributing Bilbo's gifts. At the evening [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) departs seeking for news of [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md).<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Gandalf calls on the help of [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md). The [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) double their guard on [the Shire](The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
