"**The Gods Return to Earth**" is the title of the first literary review of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. It was written by [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) and originally published in *Time and Tide* on [14 August](/wiki/14_August) [1954](/wiki/1954) (p. 1082) reviewing *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.

In the article Lewis likened the book with a "lightning from a clear sky", and compared it to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Blake" title="wikipedia:William Blake">Blake&#39;s</a> <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songs_of_Innocence" title="wikipedia:Songs of Innocence">Songs of Innocence</a></i>. For him it marked a triumphant and relieving return of the heroic romance in the anti-romantic age, while at the same time being a revolutionary work in that genre.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
