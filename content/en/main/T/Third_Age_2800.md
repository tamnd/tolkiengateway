**Third Age 2800** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2800**) is the 2800th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2800 was also known as **S.R. 1200** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md), fleeing from the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs.md), seek refuge to the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) from the North start to trouble [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Wiseman Gamwich](../W/Wiseman_Gamwich.md), son of [Hamfast of Gamwich](../H/Hamfast_of_Gamwich.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
