**Third Age 2769** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2769**) is the 2769th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2769 was also known as **S.R. 1169** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Basso Boffin](../B/Basso_Boffin.md), second son of [Buffo Boffin](../B/Buffo_Boffin.md) and [Ivy Goodenough](../I/Ivy_Goodenough.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
