**Third Age 640** (abbreviated to **T.A. 640**) is the 640th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Eärendur of Arnor](<../E/Eärendur_(King_of_Arnor).md>), son of [Elendur](<../E/Elendur_(King_of_Arnor).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-PMElendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMElendil-1">[1]</a></sup>
