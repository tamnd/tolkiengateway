<i><b>tal</b></i> (unattested plural *tail*) means "foot" or "end" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). The word is rendered *dal* with [lenition](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") (lenited plural form *dail*).<sup id="cite_ref-S_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Compounds {#Compounds}

- *[Celebrindal](/wiki/Celebrindal)* ("silver-foot")
- *[Labadal](/wiki/Labadal)* ("Hopafoot")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- *[Ramdal](/wiki/Ramdal)*
- *[Levain tad-dail](/wiki/Petty-dwarves "Petty-dwarves")*
