**Taur-nu-Fuin** was a name for the northern parts of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), and sometimes for the forest as a whole after it was corrupted by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) after the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach). The kingdoms of Dorthonion were destroyed at that point, including [Ladros](/wiki/Ladros) and the realm of [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod) and [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor). [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir) remained as an outlaw in Taur-nu-Fuin, and after his death [Beren](/wiki/Beren) his son dwelt in this forest for several years, before crossing the [Ered Gorgoroth](/wiki/Ered_Gorgoroth) and forsaking Dorthonion to its captors. Then it was truly wholly "under nightshade".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) was defeated by [Huan](/wiki/Huan) in their battle upon [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth) he took the form of a vampire and fled to Taur-nu-Fuin, filling the forest with horror.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After the formation of the [Union of Maedhros](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros), in [468](/wiki/First_Age_468 "First Age 468"), Dorthonion was briefly cleared of evil when the Orcs were driven out of all the northward regions of Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> At some point before [489](/wiki/First_Age_489 "First Age 489"), though, it had returned to a place so full of dread and evil that not even the [Orcs](/wiki/Orc "Orc") would go through it save by a single Orc-road, and even then only when in great need and haste.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

After [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) was captured upon [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh), he was taken by the Orcs through Taur-nu-Fuin northwards on the Orc-road. In those woods the pursuing [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) came upon [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor), who told him of seeing Túrin being whipped and driven towards [Angband](/wiki/Angband). Together the two Elves followed the orcs out onto the barren dunes of [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Taur-nu-Fuin* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Forest under Night(shade)",<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> from *[taur](/wiki/Taur "Taur")* ("great forest") *[nu](/wiki/Nu "Nu")* ("under") and *[fuin](/wiki/Fuin "Fuin")* ("night, gloom").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) phase of the language, it was spelled *Taur-n*a*-Fuin* (as seen in the excerpt from the Lay above), meaning "Forest of Night".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

A variation of the name was <i><b>Dor nu Fuin</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

Another Sindarin name was <i><b>Deldúwath</b></i>, translated as "Horror of Night-shadow".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Book of Lost Tales {#Book_of_Lost_Tales}

In [The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales), **Taurfuin** is described as "a dark and perilous region so thick with pines of giant growth that none but the [goblins](/wiki/Goblins "Goblins") might find a track, having eyes that pierced the deepest gloom, yet were many even of these lost long time in those regions; and they were called by the [Noldoli](/wiki/Noldoli) Taurfuin, the Forest of Night."<sup id="cite_ref-turambar_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-turambar-13">[13]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:104</sup>

In *[The Tale of Tinúviel](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Tin%C3%BAviel)*, Beren and [Tinúviel](/wiki/Tin%C3%BAviel) passed the glooms of the forest as they were fleeing from the pursuit of [Melko's](/wiki/Melko "Melko") [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) after retrieving a [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) and Beren had his hand bitten off by [Karkaras](/wiki/Karkaras).<sup id="cite_ref-Tinúviel_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tinúviel-14">[14]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:48</sup> It was also in Taurfuin that [Dairon](/wiki/Dairon), while seeking for Tinúviel after she escaped from [Thingol's](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") captivity, became wrapped in the deceit and utterly lost, and turned towards [Palisor](/wiki/Palisor) and came never back to Elfinesse.<sup id="cite_ref-Tinúviel_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tinúviel-14">[14]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:63</sup>

In *[Turambar and the Foalókë](/wiki/Turambar_and_the_Foal%C3%B3k%C3%AB)* it was in Taurfuin where [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg), pursing the Orcs which had captured [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin), came upon the Gnome [Flinding](/wiki/Flinding) who had escaped from Melko.<sup id="cite_ref-turambar_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-turambar-13">[13]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:104</sup>

### Other {#Other}

In *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*, Taur-na-Fuin is described as follows:<sup id="cite_ref-beleg_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-beleg-15">[15]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:753-767</sup>

<div class="poem">
<p>Never-dawning night   was netted clinging<br/>
in the black branches   of the beetling trees;<br/>
oppressed by pungent   pinewood’s odours, <br/>
and drowsed with dreams   as the darkness thickened,<br/>
he strayed steerless.   The stars were hid,<br/>
and the moon mantled.   There magic foundered<br/>
in the gathering glooms,   there goblins even<br/>
(whose deep eyes drill   the darkest shadows)<br/>
bewildered wandered,   who the way forsook<br/>
to grope in the glades,   there greyly loomed<br/>
of girth unguessed   in growth of ages<br/>
the topless trunks   of trees enchanted.<br/>
That fathomless fold   by folk of Elfland<br/>
is Taur-na-Fuin,   the Trackless Forest<br/>
of Deadly Nightshade,   dreadly naméd.
</p>
</div>

As before, Beleg encountered Flinding while pursuing the Orcs who had taken Túrin captive.<sup id="cite_ref-beleg_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-beleg-15">[15]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:773-780</sup> Unique details include that the Orc-road "is beaten broad through the black shadows by wizardry warded from wandering Elves".<sup id="cite_ref-beleg_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-beleg-15">[15]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:834-836</sup>

In *[The Earliest 'Silmarillion'](/wiki/The_Earliest_%27Silmarillion%27)*, following the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears), Isfin ([Aredhel](/wiki/Aredhel)) was lost in Taur-na-Fuin where she was trapped by [Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l).<sup id="cite_ref-sketch_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sketch-16">[16]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:section 15</sup>

In *[The Later Annals of Beleriand](/wiki/The_Later_Annals_of_Beleriand)*, [Turgon's](/wiki/Turgon "Turgon") host was delayed in joining the Battle of Unnumbered Tears as they had come through Taur-na-Fuin and "were delayed by the deceit and evil of the forest". Later, during the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), Morgoth himself came forth from Angband, and passed over Taur-na-Fuin, on his way to confront the [Host of the Valar](/wiki/Host_of_the_Valar).<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}

- [Nan Dungortheb](/wiki/Nan_Dungortheb): another dark forest closely located to Tuar-nu-Fuin; as the legendarium developed, elements initially associated with Nan Dungortheb were transitioned to Taur-nu-Fuin.
- [Tol Fuin](/wiki/Tol_Fuin): an island, located where Taur-nu-Fuin was situated, which survived the submerging of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand)
