**Taur-nu-Fuin** was a name for the northern parts of [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md), and sometimes for the forest as a whole after it was corrupted by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) after the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md). The kingdoms of Dorthonion were destroyed at that point, including [Ladros](../L/Ladros.md) and the realm of [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md) and [Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md). [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md) remained as an outlaw in Taur-nu-Fuin, and after his death [Beren](../B/Beren.md) his son dwelt in this forest for several years, before crossing the [Ered Gorgoroth](../E/Ered_Gorgoroth.md) and forsaking Dorthonion to its captors. Then it was truly wholly "under nightshade".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) was defeated by [Huan](../H/Huan.md) in their battle upon [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](Tol-in-Gaurhoth.md) he took the form of a vampire and fled to Taur-nu-Fuin, filling the forest with horror.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After the formation of the [Union of Maedhros](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md), in [468](../F/First_Age_468.md), Dorthonion was briefly cleared of evil when the Orcs were driven out of all the northward regions of Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> At some point before [489](../F/First_Age_489.md), though, it had returned to a place so full of dread and evil that not even the [Orcs](../O/Orc.md) would go through it save by a single Orc-road, and even then only when in great need and haste.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

After [Túrin](Túrin.md) was captured upon [Amon Rûdh](../A/Amon_Rûdh.md), he was taken by the Orcs through Taur-nu-Fuin northwards on the Orc-road. In those woods the pursuing [Beleg](../B/Beleg.md) came upon [Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md), who told him of seeing Túrin being whipped and driven towards [Angband](../A/Angband.md). Together the two Elves followed the orcs out onto the barren dunes of [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Taur-nu-Fuin* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Forest under Night(shade)",<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> from *[taur](Taur.md)* ("great forest") *[nu](../N/Nu.md)* ("under") and *[fuin](../F/Fuin.md)* ("night, gloom").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In the [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) phase of the language, it was spelled *Taur-n*a*-Fuin* (as seen in the excerpt from the Lay above), meaning "Forest of Night".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

A variation of the name was <i><b>Dor nu Fuin</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

Another Sindarin name was <i><b>Deldúwath</b></i>, translated as "Horror of Night-shadow".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Book of Lost Tales {#Book_of_Lost_Tales}

In [The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md), **Taurfuin** is described as "a dark and perilous region so thick with pines of giant growth that none but the [goblins](../G/Goblins.md) might find a track, having eyes that pierced the deepest gloom, yet were many even of these lost long time in those regions; and they were called by the [Noldoli](../N/Noldoli.md) Taurfuin, the Forest of Night."<sup id="cite_ref-turambar_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-turambar-13">[13]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:104</sup>

In *[The Tale of Tinúviel](The_Tale_of_Tinúviel.md)*, Beren and [Tinúviel](Tinúviel.md) passed the glooms of the forest as they were fleeing from the pursuit of [Melko's](../M/Melko.md) [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) after retrieving a [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md) and Beren had his hand bitten off by [Karkaras](../K/Karkaras.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tinúviel_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tinúviel-14">[14]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:48</sup> It was also in Taurfuin that [Dairon](../D/Dairon.md), while seeking for Tinúviel after she escaped from [Thingol's](Thingol.md) captivity, became wrapped in the deceit and utterly lost, and turned towards [Palisor](../P/Palisor.md) and came never back to Elfinesse.<sup id="cite_ref-Tinúviel_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tinúviel-14">[14]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:63</sup>

In *[Turambar and the Foalókë](Turambar_and_the_Foalókë.md)* it was in Taurfuin where [Beleg](../B/Beleg.md), pursing the Orcs which had captured [Túrin](Túrin.md), came upon the Gnome [Flinding](../F/Flinding.md) who had escaped from Melko.<sup id="cite_ref-turambar_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-turambar-13">[13]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:104</sup>

### Other {#Other}

In *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md)*, Taur-na-Fuin is described as follows:<sup id="cite_ref-beleg_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-beleg-15">[15]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:753-767</sup>

<div class="poem">
<p>Never-dawning night   was netted clinging<br/>
in the black branches   of the beetling trees;<br/>
oppressed by pungent   pinewood’s odours, <br/>
and drowsed with dreams   as the darkness thickened,<br/>
he strayed steerless.   The stars were hid,<br/>
and the moon mantled.   There magic foundered<br/>
in the gathering glooms,   there goblins even<br/>
(whose deep eyes drill   the darkest shadows)<br/>
bewildered wandered,   who the way forsook<br/>
to grope in the glades,   there greyly loomed<br/>
of girth unguessed   in growth of ages<br/>
the topless trunks   of trees enchanted.<br/>
That fathomless fold   by folk of Elfland<br/>
is Taur-na-Fuin,   the Trackless Forest<br/>
of Deadly Nightshade,   dreadly naméd.
</p>
</div>

As before, Beleg encountered Flinding while pursuing the Orcs who had taken Túrin captive.<sup id="cite_ref-beleg_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-beleg-15">[15]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:773-780</sup> Unique details include that the Orc-road "is beaten broad through the black shadows by wizardry warded from wandering Elves".<sup id="cite_ref-beleg_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-beleg-15">[15]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:834-836</sup>

In *[The Earliest 'Silmarillion'](The_Earliest_'Silmarillion'.md)*, following the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](../B/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears.md), Isfin ([Aredhel](../A/Aredhel.md)) was lost in Taur-na-Fuin where she was trapped by [Eöl](../E/Eöl.md).<sup id="cite_ref-sketch_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sketch-16">[16]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:section 15</sup>

In *[The Later Annals of Beleriand](The_Later_Annals_of_Beleriand.md)*, [Turgon's](Turgon.md) host was delayed in joining the Battle of Unnumbered Tears as they had come through Taur-na-Fuin and "were delayed by the deceit and evil of the forest". Later, during the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), Morgoth himself came forth from Angband, and passed over Taur-na-Fuin, on his way to confront the [Host of the Valar](../H/Host_of_the_Valar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}

- [Nan Dungortheb](../N/Nan_Dungortheb.md): another dark forest closely located to Tuar-nu-Fuin; as the legendarium developed, elements initially associated with Nan Dungortheb were transitioned to Taur-nu-Fuin.
- [Tol Fuin](Tol_Fuin.md): an island, located where Taur-nu-Fuin was situated, which survived the submerging of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md)
