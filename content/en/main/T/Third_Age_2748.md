**Third Age 2748** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2748**) is the 2748th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2748 was also known as **S.R. 1148** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) driven from [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
