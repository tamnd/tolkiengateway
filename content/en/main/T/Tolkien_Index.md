The **Tolkien Index** is a not-for-profit website which aims to produce a comprehensive index of names from writings by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) (and associated editorial matter) on [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) which do not already have an index.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Morgan Thomsen](../M/Morgan_Thomsen.md) is the owner and main contributor of the website, whilst Daniel Helen is its webmaster.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was founded on [24 June](../_/24_June.md), [2011](../_/2011.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Reception {#Reception}

Noting the website Tolkien Index in November 2011, *Tolkien Transactions*, an on-line review journal, wrote: "*\[t\]rusting that the authors will continue the work, this promises to be a very valuable index resource*".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
