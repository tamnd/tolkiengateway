**The Floating Log** was an inn in the village of [Frogmorton](/wiki/Frogmorton), in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")'s [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing). The Floating Log stood on the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) that passed through [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), about halfway between [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) and the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge), and so must have been a busy place. In the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), though, [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey) and his [Men](/wiki/Men) took over the Shire, and shut the inn down.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
