The **Towers of the Teeth** were two towers that stood on two sheer hills that were thrust forward on either side of the mouth of the pass of [Cirith Gorgor](/wiki/Cirith_Gorgor) at the northwestern end of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) where the [Ered Lithui](/wiki/Ered_Lithui) met the [Ephel Duath](/wiki/Ephel_Duath). They were strong, tall, stony-faced and had dark window-holes facing north, east and west.<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-1">[1]</a></sup> The tower on the western side of the pass was called **Narchost** and the tower on the eastern side of the pass was called **Carchost**.<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434"), the [Battle of Dagorlad](/wiki/Battle_of_Dagorlad)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> took place at the plain [Dagorlad](/wiki/Dagorlad) in front of the Black Gate.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After the defeat of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) the [Men of Gondor](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") built the Towers of the Teeth to prevent [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) from returning to Mordor<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-1">[1]</a></sup> and to keep watch on Mordor where the creatures of Sauron still lurked.<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) devastated [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636"), the watch on the borders of Mordor ceased and the fortresses that guarded the passes were unmanned in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1640](/wiki/Third_Age_1640 "Third Age 1640") because of a lack of troops.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> It is possible that the Towers of the Teeth and the other fortresses that guarded the passes into Mordor were only abandoned temporarily and were manned again by troops from Gondor when sufficient troops became available again later.

At the time of the death of king [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) the Towers of the Teeth were still manned by the Men of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

As the strength of Gondor failed, the Towers of the Teeth were abandoned by the Men of Gondor and were empty for long years and fell into decay. After the return of Sauron they were repaired and garrisoned with the forces of Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-1">[1]</a></sup> The Towers of the Teeth fell down during an earthquake<sup id="cite_ref-Cormallen_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cormallen-9">[9]</a></sup> after an eruption of [Orodruin](/wiki/Orodruin) when the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring) fell into the [Sammath Naur](/wiki/Sammath_Naur) in the Orodruin<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> on [25 March](/wiki/25_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Other names of the Towers of the Teeth were the *Teeth of Mordor*<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-1">[1]</a></sup> or the *Towers of the Black Gate*.<sup id="cite_ref-Cormallen_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cormallen-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Narchost* is translated as "Bitter-biting Fort".<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup> Its initial element *narch* is not defined, but a similar ([Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)) verb <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Narcha-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Narcha- (page does not exist)">narcha-</a></i> ("to rend") appears in *The Etymologies* as a derivative of the root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=N%C3%81RAK&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NÁRAK (page does not exist)">NÁRAK</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> The second element is *[ost](/wiki/Ost "Ost")* ("fortress").<sup id="cite_ref-ost_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ost-13">[13]</a></sup>

The name *Carchost* is translated as "Fang Fort".<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-carak_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-carak-14">[14]</a></sup> It contains two [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) elements: *carch* ("fang")<sup id="cite_ref-carak_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-carak-14">[14]</a></sup> and *[ost](/wiki/Ost "Ost")* ("fortress").<sup id="cite_ref-ost_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ost-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early drafts, these were known as the Dire-castles **Gorgos** and **Nargos**.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:299</sup>

An earlier version of what would later become Appendix A and an earlier version of what would later become Appendix B mention the watch on Mordor was "neglected" and "relaxed", respectively and the fortresses guarding the passes became emptied with the first mentioning the fewness of the people after the plague as the reason.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>
