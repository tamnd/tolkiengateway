A timeline of the life of **[Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md)** throughtout the [Ages](../A/Ages.md). Important dates of close relatives are also included. Many dates and events here presented were not clearly defined, but they follow the narrative proposed in the main article.

## [First Age](../F/First_Age.md): [Years of the Trees](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) {#First_Age:_Years_of_the_Trees}

### [1362](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1362.md) {#1362}

- [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) is born in [Eldamar](../E/Eldamar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [1490](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1490.md) {#1490}

- Unrest of the Noldor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Galadriel loses her inner peace forever.<sup id="cite_ref-Case_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-3">[3]</a></sup>

### [1495](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1495.md) {#1495}

- [Darkening of Valinor](../D/Darkening_of_Valinor.md) and speech of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) in [Tirion](Tirion.md). Galadriel supports the [Exile of the Noldor](../E/Exile_of_the_Noldor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Kinslaying at Alqualondë](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Galadriel fights against Fëanor defending her kin.<sup id="cite_ref-Case_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-3">[3]</a></sup>

### [1496](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1496.md) {#1496}

- [Doom of Mandos](../D/Doom_of_Mandos.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md) retreats to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), but Galadriel keeps forward, wishing to make Fëanor's life impossible.<sup id="cite_ref-Case_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-3">[3]</a></sup>

### [1497](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1497.md) {#1497}

- Fëanor and [his followers](../H/House_of_Fëanor.md) abandone the rest of the Noldorin hosts in Aman, burning the stolen ships. Galadriel leads her people along with [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md), and [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) and his sons, and they begin their hard travel through [Helcaraxë](../H/Helcaraxë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

### [1500](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1500.md) {#1500}

- The [Moon](../M/Moon.md) rises for the first time, lighting the hosts of Fingolfin when reaching the Northern Lands.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## [First Age](../F/First_Age.md): [Years of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) {#First_Age:_Years_of_the_Sun}

### [52](../F/First_Age_52.md) {#52}

- Galadriel and [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) are guests in [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md). Finrod soon departs to the [Caves of Narog](../C/Caves_of_Narog.md), but she stays.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> She befriends [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) and [Melian](../M/Melian.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Noldor_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Noldor-10">[10]</a></sup>

### [66](../F/First_Age_66.md) {#66}

- Galadriel is enquired by Melian, and the rebelious nature of the Exile of the Noldor is exposed.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

### [67](../F/First_Age_67.md) {#67}

- Galadriel is visited by her brothers in Doriath, and [Thingol](Thingol.md) reproaches them having concealed the story of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../K/Kinslaying.md" title="Kinslaying">Kinslaying</a> from him. They depart, but she stays.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

### [102](../F/First_Age_102.md) {#102}

- [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) is finished and the [children of Finarfin](../C/Children_of_Finarfin.md), are reunited in a feast there, including Galadriel coming from Doriath. She stays there for a while and asks Finrod about his love life.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

### [420](../F/First_Age_420.md) {#420}

- Melian foretells Galadriel about [a Man](../B/Beren.md) who shall cross into Doriath moved by a doom greater than the [Girdle of Melian](../G/Girdle_of_Melian.md).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

### [455](../F/First_Age_455.md) {#455}

- [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md). Deaths of [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md) and [Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

### [465](../F/First_Age_465.md) {#465}

- Autumn: Death of Finrod while being in captivity by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

### [495](../F/First_Age_495.md) {#495}

- Autumn: [Fall of Nargothrond](../F/Fall_of_Nargothrond.md). Death of [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

### [545](../F/First_Age_545.md) {#545}

- The [Host of the Valar](../H/Host_of_the_Valar.md) arrives to [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md),<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> Finarfin leading the Noldor for the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

## [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) {#Second_Age}

### c. [1](../S/Second_Age_1.md) {#c._1}

- Galadriel and Celeborn are married "soon after the overthrow of Morgoth".<sup id="cite_ref-Scales_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scales-20">[20]</a></sup>

### 1-20 {#1-20}

- Galadriel and Celeborn leave [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) before [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) becomes its king, apparently thanks to her influence.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> They settled at [Lake Nenuial](../L/Lake_Nenuial.md) along with many followers.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:235</sup>

### c. [300](../S/Second_Age_300.md) {#c._300}

- Her daughter [Celebrían](../C/Celebrían.md) is born.<sup id="cite_ref-Scales_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scales-20">[20]</a></sup>

### c. [500](../S/Second_Age_500.md) {#c._500}

- [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) begins to stir Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-Second_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-23">[23]</a></sup> Galadriel pervices this evil coming from the East.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:235</sup>

### c. [700](../S/Second_Age_700.md) {#c._700}

- Galadriel and Celeborn move eastwards and establish themselves in [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) near [Moria](../M/Moria.md). She seeks the friendship of the [Dwarves of Khazad-dûm](../D/Dwarves_of_Khazad-dûm.md) and the [Nandor](../N/Nandor.md) of [Lórinand](../L/Lórinand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:236</sup>

### [750](../S/Second_Age_750.md) {#750}

- The realm of Eregion is founded,<sup id="cite_ref-Second_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-23">[23]</a></sup> and the building of its chief city, [Ost-in-Edhil](../O/Ost-in-Edhil.md), is begun.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:236</sup>

### [883](../S/Second_Age_883.md) {#883}

- She has a meeting with King [Aldarion](../A/Aldarion.md) when he came to [Tharbad](Tharbad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup>

### [1200](../S/Second_Age_1200.md) {#1200}

- Sauron tries to seduce the Eldar,<sup id="cite_ref-Second_23-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-23">[23]</a></sup> but finds in Galadriel his main adversary.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:237</sup>

### [1350](../S/Second_Age_1350.md)-1400 {#1350-1400}

- Sauron moves [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md) and the [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](../G/Gwaith-i-Mírdain.md) against Galadriel, who leaves Eregion via Moria to Lórinand.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:237</sup>

### c. [1600](../S/Second_Age_1600.md) {#c._1600}

- The treachery of Sauron is discovered when he forges [the One Ring](The_One_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Second_23-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-23">[23]</a></sup>

### [1693](../S/Second_Age_1693.md) {#1693}

- The [Three Rings](Three_Rings.md) are hidden<sup id="cite_ref-Second_23-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-23">[23]</a></sup> by counsel of Galadriel. Celebrimbor gives her [Nenya](../N/Nenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:237</sup>

### [1700](../S/Second_Age_1700.md) {#1700}

- Sauron is defeated with the help of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) after the [War of the Elves and Sauron](../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md) and the [Sack of Eregion](../S/Sack_of_Eregion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Second_23-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-23">[23]</a></sup>

### c. [1701](../S/Second_Age_1701.md) {#c._1701}

- "Soon after" the war, she is affected with [Sea-longing](../S/Sea-longing.md) and decides to dwell near the Sea. Instead she first travels to Imladris with Celebrían, seeking for Celeborn.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:240</sup>
- In Imladris she attends the [first Council](../W/White_Council.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:239</sup>

## [Third Age](Third_Age.md) {#Third_Age}

### [109](Third_Age_109.md) {#109}

- Celebrían and [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) are married.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1085</sup>

### c. [1100](Third_Age_1100.md) {#c._1100}

- The [Wise](../W/Wise.md) (including Galadriel, who is one of the chief Eldar), discover an evil dwelling in [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) and they believed it is one of the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1085</sup>

### [1981](Third_Age_1981.md) {#1981}

- After the apparition of [Durin's Bane](../D/Durin's_Bane.md), Moria is emptied. Many [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) of Lórien fly south.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1087</sup>
- Around this time, Galadriel and Celeborn become the [Lord and Lady of Lórien](../L/Lord_and_Lady_of_Lórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[26]</a></sup>

### [2463](Third_Age_2463.md) {#2463}

- The [White Council](../W/White_Council.md) is formed.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1087</sup>

### [2510](Third_Age_2510.md) {#2510}

- Celebrían departs to the West.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1087</sup>
- She aids [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md) and his [riders](../R/Rohirrim.md) in the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](../B/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md), creating a white mist that drives out the shadows from Dol Guldur.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[27]</a></sup>

### [2851](Third_Age_2851.md) {#2851}

- The White Council meets. The idea of attacking [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) is rejected by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1088</sup>

### [2941](Third_Age_2941.md) {#2941}

- The White Council meets and [attack Dol Guldur](../A/Attack_on_Dol_Guldur.md), expelling Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1089</sup>

### [2953](Third_Age_2953.md) {#2953}

- The White Council meets for last time, debating about the [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1089</sup>

### [2980](Third_Age_2980.md) {#2980}

- Aragorn enters [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) after many travels,<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1090</sup> admitted by Galadriel, who bades him to change his worn out clothes and dresses him like a proper prince.<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[28]</a></sup>

### [3019](Third_Age_3019.md) {#3019}

- [17 January](../_/17_January.md): Galadriel and Celeborn receive the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) in Lothlórien.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-29">[29]</a></sup>

- [15 February](../_/15_February.md):<sup id="cite_ref-Great_29-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-29">[29]</a></sup> Galadriel shows [her Mirror](../M/Mirror_of_Galadriel.md) to Frodo Baggins. She is tempted with the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md), but rejects it.<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">[30]</a></sup>
- [16 February](../_/16_February.md):<sup id="cite_ref-Great_29-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-29">[29]</a></sup> Galadriel gives [gifts](../G/Gifts_of_Galadriel.md) and [lembas](../L/Lembas.md) to the Fellowship, and gives them farewell reciting the *[Namárië](../N/Namárië.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31">[31]</a></sup>
- [17 February](../_/17_February.md): Resurrected Gandalf is beared to Lórien by [Gwaihir](../G/Gwaihir.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_29-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-29">[29]</a></sup> who was sent by Galadriel. Gandalf is received in [Caras Galadhon](../C/Caras_Galadhon.md) and cloathed in white. Galadriel gives him [some prophetic messages](../G/Galadriel's_messages.md) for [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md).<sup id="cite_ref-White_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-White-32">[32]</a></sup>

- [28 March](../_/28_March.md): After the [assaults on Lothlórien](../A/Assaults_on_Lothlórien.md), Celeborn crosses the Anduin and the [destruction of Dol Guldur](../F/Fall_of_Dol_Guldur.md) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup> Soon after Galadriel throws down the walls of Dol Guldur.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_29-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-29">[29]</a></sup>

- [20 May](../_/20_May.md): Elrond and Arwen come to Lórien in their way to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup>
- [27 May](../_/27_May.md): The escort of Arwen leaves Lórien.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup>

- [6 September](../_/6_September.md): Celeborn and Galadriel, Gandalf and the [four Hobbits](Travellers.md) halt under the [Mountains of Moria](../M/Mountains_of_Moria.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup>
- [13 September](../_/13_September.md): Celeborn and Galadriel go their way.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup>

### [3021](Third_Age_3021.md) {#3021}

- [22 September](../_/22_September.md): The [Last Riding of the Keepers of the Rings](../L/Last_Riding_of_the_Keepers_of_the_Rings.md) meets Frodo and Sam in [Woody End](../W/Woody_End.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup>
- [29 September](../_/29_September.md): The company arrives to the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md). The [Three Keepers](Three_Keepers.md), Bilbo and Frodo leave Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup>

## [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md) {#Fourth_Age}

### [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md) {#120}

- It is said that [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) sailed West with [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md),<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34">[34]</a></sup> maybe thanks to Galadriel.<sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35">[35]</a></sup>
