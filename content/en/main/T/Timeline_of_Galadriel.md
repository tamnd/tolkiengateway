A timeline of the life of **[Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel)** throughtout the [Ages](/wiki/Ages). Important dates of close relatives are also included. Many dates and events here presented were not clearly defined, but they follow the narrative proposed in the main article.

## [First Age](/wiki/First_Age): [Years of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees) {#First_Age:_Years_of_the_Trees}

### [1362](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1362 "Year of the Trees 1362") {#1362}

- [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) is born in [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [1490](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1490 "Year of the Trees 1490") {#1490}

- Unrest of the Noldor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Galadriel loses her inner peace forever.<sup id="cite_ref-Case_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-3">[3]</a></sup>

### [1495](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1495 "Year of the Trees 1495") {#1495}

- [Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor) and speech of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) in [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion). Galadriel supports the [Exile of the Noldor](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Kinslaying at Alqualondë](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Galadriel fights against Fëanor defending her kin.<sup id="cite_ref-Case_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-3">[3]</a></sup>

### [1496](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1496 "Year of the Trees 1496") {#1496}

- [Doom of Mandos](/wiki/Doom_of_Mandos).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin) retreats to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), but Galadriel keeps forward, wishing to make Fëanor's life impossible.<sup id="cite_ref-Case_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Case-3">[3]</a></sup>

### [1497](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1497 "Year of the Trees 1497") {#1497}

- Fëanor and [his followers](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor "House of Fëanor") abandone the rest of the Noldorin hosts in Aman, burning the stolen ships. Galadriel leads her people along with [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod), and [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and his sons, and they begin their hard travel through [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

### [1500](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1500 "Year of the Trees 1500") {#1500}

- The [Moon](/wiki/Moon) rises for the first time, lighting the hosts of Fingolfin when reaching the Northern Lands.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## [First Age](/wiki/First_Age): [Years of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun) {#First_Age:_Years_of_the_Sun}

### [52](/wiki/First_Age_52 "First Age 52") {#52}

- Galadriel and [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) are guests in [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath). Finrod soon departs to the [Caves of Narog](/wiki/Caves_of_Narog), but she stays.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> She befriends [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Melian](/wiki/Melian).<sup id="cite_ref-Noldor_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Noldor-10">[10]</a></sup>

### [66](/wiki/First_Age_66 "First Age 66") {#66}

- Galadriel is enquired by Melian, and the rebelious nature of the Exile of the Noldor is exposed.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

### [67](/wiki/First_Age_67 "First Age 67") {#67}

- Galadriel is visited by her brothers in Doriath, and [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) reproaches them having concealed the story of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Kinslaying" title="Kinslaying">Kinslaying</a> from him. They depart, but she stays.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

### [102](/wiki/First_Age_102 "First Age 102") {#102}

- [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) is finished and the [children of Finarfin](/wiki/Children_of_Finarfin "Children of Finarfin"), are reunited in a feast there, including Galadriel coming from Doriath. She stays there for a while and asks Finrod about his love life.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

### [420](/wiki/First_Age_420 "First Age 420") {#420}

- Melian foretells Galadriel about [a Man](/wiki/Beren "Beren") who shall cross into Doriath moved by a doom greater than the [Girdle of Melian](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

### [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455") {#455}

- [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach). Deaths of [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod) and [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

### [465](/wiki/First_Age_465 "First Age 465") {#465}

- Autumn: Death of Finrod while being in captivity by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

### [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495") {#495}

- Autumn: [Fall of Nargothrond](/wiki/Fall_of_Nargothrond). Death of [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth).<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

### [545](/wiki/First_Age_545 "First Age 545") {#545}

- The [Host of the Valar](/wiki/Host_of_the_Valar) arrives to [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand),<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> Finarfin leading the Noldor for the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

## [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) {#Second_Age}

### c. [1](/wiki/Second_Age_1 "Second Age 1") {#c._1}

- Galadriel and Celeborn are married "soon after the overthrow of Morgoth".<sup id="cite_ref-Scales_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scales-20">[20]</a></sup>

### 1-20 {#1-20}

- Galadriel and Celeborn leave [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) before [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) becomes its king, apparently thanks to her influence.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> They settled at [Lake Nenuial](/wiki/Lake_Nenuial) along with many followers.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:235</sup>

### c. [300](/wiki/Second_Age_300 "Second Age 300") {#c._300}

- Her daughter [Celebrían](/wiki/Celebr%C3%ADan) is born.<sup id="cite_ref-Scales_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scales-20">[20]</a></sup>

### c. [500](/wiki/Second_Age_500 "Second Age 500") {#c._500}

- [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) begins to stir Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-Second_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-23">[23]</a></sup> Galadriel pervices this evil coming from the East.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:235</sup>

### c. [700](/wiki/Second_Age_700 "Second Age 700") {#c._700}

- Galadriel and Celeborn move eastwards and establish themselves in [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) near [Moria](/wiki/Moria). She seeks the friendship of the [Dwarves of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm) and the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor) of [Lórinand](/wiki/L%C3%B3rinand).<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:236</sup>

### [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750") {#750}

- The realm of Eregion is founded,<sup id="cite_ref-Second_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-23">[23]</a></sup> and the building of its chief city, [Ost-in-Edhil](/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil), is begun.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:236</sup>

### [883](/wiki/Second_Age_883 "Second Age 883") {#883}

- She has a meeting with King [Aldarion](/wiki/Aldarion) when he came to [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad).<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup>

### [1200](/wiki/Second_Age_1200 "Second Age 1200") {#1200}

- Sauron tries to seduce the Eldar,<sup id="cite_ref-Second_23-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-23">[23]</a></sup> but finds in Galadriel his main adversary.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:237</sup>

### [1350](/wiki/Second_Age_1350 "Second Age 1350")-1400 {#1350-1400}

- Sauron moves [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor) and the [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-M%C3%ADrdain) against Galadriel, who leaves Eregion via Moria to Lórinand.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:237</sup>

### c. [1600](/wiki/Second_Age_1600 "Second Age 1600") {#c._1600}

- The treachery of Sauron is discovered when he forges [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").<sup id="cite_ref-Second_23-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-23">[23]</a></sup>

### [1693](/wiki/Second_Age_1693 "Second Age 1693") {#1693}

- The [Three Rings](/wiki/Three_Rings) are hidden<sup id="cite_ref-Second_23-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-23">[23]</a></sup> by counsel of Galadriel. Celebrimbor gives her [Nenya](/wiki/Nenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:237</sup>

### [1700](/wiki/Second_Age_1700 "Second Age 1700") {#1700}

- Sauron is defeated with the help of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) after the [War of the Elves and Sauron](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron) and the [Sack of Eregion](/wiki/Sack_of_Eregion).<sup id="cite_ref-Second_23-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Second-23">[23]</a></sup>

### c. [1701](/wiki/Second_Age_1701 "Second Age 1701") {#c._1701}

- "Soon after" the war, she is affected with [Sea-longing](/wiki/Sea-longing) and decides to dwell near the Sea. Instead she first travels to Imladris with Celebrían, seeking for Celeborn.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:240</sup>
- In Imladris she attends the [first Council](/wiki/White_Council "White Council").<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_22-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:239</sup>

## [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) {#Third_Age}

### [109](/wiki/Third_Age_109 "Third Age 109") {#109}

- Celebrían and [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) are married.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1085</sup>

### c. [1100](/wiki/Third_Age_1100 "Third Age 1100") {#c._1100}

- The [Wise](/wiki/Wise) (including Galadriel, who is one of the chief Eldar), discover an evil dwelling in [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) and they believed it is one of the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl).<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1085</sup>

### [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981") {#1981}

- After the apparition of [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane), Moria is emptied. Many [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves) of Lórien fly south.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1087</sup>
- Around this time, Galadriel and Celeborn become the [Lord and Lady of Lórien](/wiki/Lord_and_Lady_of_L%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[26]</a></sup>

### [2463](/wiki/Third_Age_2463 "Third Age 2463") {#2463}

- The [White Council](/wiki/White_Council) is formed.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1087</sup>

### [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510") {#2510}

- Celebrían departs to the West.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1087</sup>
- She aids [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) and his [riders](/wiki/Rohirrim "Rohirrim") in the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant), creating a white mist that drives out the shadows from Dol Guldur.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[27]</a></sup>

### [2851](/wiki/Third_Age_2851 "Third Age 2851") {#2851}

- The White Council meets. The idea of attacking [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) is rejected by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman).<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1088</sup>

### [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") {#2941}

- The White Council meets and [attack Dol Guldur](/wiki/Attack_on_Dol_Guldur "Attack on Dol Guldur"), expelling Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1089</sup>

### [2953](/wiki/Third_Age_2953 "Third Age 2953") {#2953}

- The White Council meets for last time, debating about the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power).<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1089</sup>

### [2980](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980") {#2980}

- Aragorn enters [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) after many travels,<sup id="cite_ref-Third_25-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-25">[25]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1090</sup> admitted by Galadriel, who bades him to change his worn out clothes and dresses him like a proper prince.<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[28]</a></sup>

### [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") {#3019}

- [17 January](/wiki/17_January): Galadriel and Celeborn receive the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) in Lothlórien.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-29">[29]</a></sup>

- [15 February](/wiki/15_February):<sup id="cite_ref-Great_29-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-29">[29]</a></sup> Galadriel shows [her Mirror](/wiki/Mirror_of_Galadriel "Mirror of Galadriel") to Frodo Baggins. She is tempted with the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring), but rejects it.<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">[30]</a></sup>
- [16 February](/wiki/16_February):<sup id="cite_ref-Great_29-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-29">[29]</a></sup> Galadriel gives [gifts](/wiki/Gifts_of_Galadriel "Gifts of Galadriel") and [lembas](/wiki/Lembas "Lembas") to the Fellowship, and gives them farewell reciting the *[Namárië](/wiki/Nam%C3%A1ri%C3%AB)*.<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31">[31]</a></sup>
- [17 February](/wiki/17_February): Resurrected Gandalf is beared to Lórien by [Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_29-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-29">[29]</a></sup> who was sent by Galadriel. Gandalf is received in [Caras Galadhon](/wiki/Caras_Galadhon) and cloathed in white. Galadriel gives him [some prophetic messages](/wiki/Galadriel%27s_messages "Galadriel's messages") for [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli).<sup id="cite_ref-White_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-White-32">[32]</a></sup>

- [28 March](/wiki/28_March): After the [assaults on Lothlórien](/wiki/Assaults_on_Lothl%C3%B3rien "Assaults on Lothlórien"), Celeborn crosses the Anduin and the [destruction of Dol Guldur](/wiki/Fall_of_Dol_Guldur "Fall of Dol Guldur") begins.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup> Soon after Galadriel throws down the walls of Dol Guldur.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_29-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-29">[29]</a></sup>

- [20 May](/wiki/20_May): Elrond and Arwen come to Lórien in their way to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup>
- [27 May](/wiki/27_May): The escort of Arwen leaves Lórien.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup>

- [6 September](/wiki/6_September): Celeborn and Galadriel, Gandalf and the [four Hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") halt under the [Mountains of Moria](/wiki/Mountains_of_Moria).<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup>
- [13 September](/wiki/13_September): Celeborn and Galadriel go their way.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup>

### [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021") {#3021}

- [22 September](/wiki/22_September): The [Last Riding of the Keepers of the Rings](/wiki/Last_Riding_of_the_Keepers_of_the_Rings) meets Frodo and Sam in [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End).<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup>
- [29 September](/wiki/29_September): The company arrives to the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens). The [Three Keepers](/wiki/Three_Keepers), Bilbo and Frodo leave Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_33-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-33">[33]</a></sup>

## [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) {#Fourth_Age}

### [120](/wiki/Fourth_Age_120 "Fourth Age 120") {#120}

- It is said that [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) sailed West with [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas),<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34">[34]</a></sup> maybe thanks to Galadriel.<sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35">[35]</a></sup>
