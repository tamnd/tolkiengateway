**Third Age 1944** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1944**) is the 1944th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1944 was also known as **S.R. 344** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- **[9 July](../_/9_July.md)**: News of the oncoming of the enemy reach [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Disaster of the Morannon](../D/Disaster_of_the_Morannon.md)
- [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) falls in battle.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> His sons [Artamir](../A/Artamir.md) and [Faramir](<../F/Faramir_(son_of_Ondoher).md>) are also killed.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-gondor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **[13 July](../_/13_July.md)**: [Minohtar](../M/Minohtar.md) is overwhelmed by [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) and slain by an arrow.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Eärnil](../E/Eärnil_II.md) defeats the enemy in South [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md). He then wins the [Battle of the Camp](../B/Battle_of_the_Camp.md), and drives [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) into the [Dead Marshes](../D/Dead_Marshes.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Pelendur](../P/Pelendur.md) briefly rules [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md) claims the crown of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-gondor_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>
