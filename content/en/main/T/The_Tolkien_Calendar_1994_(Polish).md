**The Tolkien Calendar 1994** is a Polish calendar published by Seckja Tolkienowska, Poland ([Polish Tolkien Society](/wiki/Polish_Tolkien_Society)). The calendar features pen and ink illustrations by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Helena_Strokowska&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Helena Strokowska (page does not exist)">Helena Strokowska</a>, [Ryszard Derdziński](/wiki/Ryszard_Derdzi%C5%84ski), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Maciej&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maciej (page does not exist)">Maciej</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Wygnanski&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Wygnanski (page does not exist)">Wygnanski</a>, [Karolina Stopa](/wiki/Karolina_Stopa), and [Małgorzata Pudlik](/wiki/Ma%C5%82gorzata_Pudlik).

- Dimensions: 8 1/4 x 11 1/2 inches
- Clear plastic cover (front and back)
- Red comb bound
