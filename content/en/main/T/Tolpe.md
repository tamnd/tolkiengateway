**tolpe** is the [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) number "eighteen"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Possible [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish) \**tolokwê*. The [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") for "eight" is <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=TOLOD&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TOLOD (page does not exist)">TOLOD</a> and we see the suffix <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-pe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-pe (page does not exist)">-pe</a></i> "-teen".
