<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Rings Photo Guide</b></i> includes over 70 images of the (at the time of publication) unreleased film, [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The official children&#39;s photo guide to The Fellowship of the Ring. Meet Frodo Baggins and enter a world of hobbits, wizards, dwarves and elves. Discover the secrets of the One Ring and the terrifying creatures of the Dark Lord, Sauron. Follow the Companions as they embark on their perilous quest.
</p>
</blockquote>
