**The Steward of Gondor** is the fifth track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)* by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore). It features [Billy Boyd](/wiki/Billy_Boyd) singing a brief song that has lyrics based off of the final two stanzas of [A Walking Song](/wiki/A_Walking_Song).
