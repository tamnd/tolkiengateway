<i><b>The Children&#39;s Treasury of Literature in Colour</b></i> is a collection of children's stories. The anthology includes the chapter "[An Unexpected Party](/wiki/An_Unexpected_Party)" from *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, illustrated by Robert J. Lee.

Tolkien received copy of the book from [Joy Hill](/wiki/Joy_Hill) in the beginning of January [1967](/wiki/1967), and [wrote to her on 5 January](</wiki/Letter_to_Joy_Hill_(5_January_1967)> "Letter to Joy Hill (5 January 1967)") that while he considered "a great many of the illustrations" to be "very good", Robert J. Lee's *Hobbit* illustrations appeared to him as "vulgar, stupid and entirely out of keeping with the text".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He also wrote that he will give the book to his grandson, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien)'s son [Simon](/wiki/Simon_Tolkien "Simon Tolkien") (aged 8).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
