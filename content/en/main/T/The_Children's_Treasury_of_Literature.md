<i><b>The Children&#39;s Treasury of Literature in Colour</b></i> is a collection of children's stories. The anthology includes the chapter "[An Unexpected Party](../A/An_Unexpected_Party.md)" from *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)*, illustrated by Robert J. Lee.

Tolkien received copy of the book from [Joy Hill](../J/Joy_Hill.md) in the beginning of January [1967](../_/1967.md), and [wrote to her on 5 January](<../L/Letter_to_Joy_Hill_(5_January_1967).md>) that while he considered "a great many of the illustrations" to be "very good", Robert J. Lee's *Hobbit* illustrations appeared to him as "vulgar, stupid and entirely out of keeping with the text".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He also wrote that he will give the book to his grandson, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md)'s son [Simon](../S/Simon_Tolkien.md) (aged 8).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
