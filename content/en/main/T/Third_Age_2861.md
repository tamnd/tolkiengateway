**Third Age 2861** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2861**) is the 2861st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2861 was also known as **S.R. 1261** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Theobald Bolger](Theobald_Bolger.md), son of [Gundabald Bolger](../G/Gundabald_Bolger.md) and [Salvia Brandybuck](../S/Salvia_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) gives King [Walda](../W/Walda.md)'s death this year, who would be 81 years old. However the "Kings of the Mark" section of [Appendix A](../A/Appendix_A.md) puts his death 10 years earlier, in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2851](Third_Age_2851.md). <span></span>
