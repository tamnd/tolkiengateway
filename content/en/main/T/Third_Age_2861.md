**Third Age 2861** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2861**) is the 2861st [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Third Age 2861 was also known as **S.R. 1261** in [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Theobald Bolger](/wiki/Theobald_Bolger), son of [Gundabald Bolger](/wiki/Gundabald_Bolger) and [Salvia Brandybuck](/wiki/Salvia_Brandybuck).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Appendix B](/wiki/Appendix_B) gives King [Walda](/wiki/Walda)'s death this year, who would be 81 years old. However the "Kings of the Mark" section of [Appendix A](/wiki/Appendix_A) puts his death 10 years earlier, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2851](/wiki/Third_Age_2851 "Third Age 2851"). <span></span>
