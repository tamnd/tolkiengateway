<i><b>Tolkien : his language, a guide to Quenya</b></i>, written by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=George_Horton&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="George Horton (page does not exist)">George Horton</a>, is a book about J.R.R. Tolkien's language.

# From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>&#34;This is a guide to Quenya, intended for the use primarily of linguists who want a quick guide to a case study for invented language, and of enthusiasts for the work of Tolkien. Part 1, intended to form the core of the book, is a fast guide to speaking the language, with full information on grammar and pronunciation, as well as a basic vocabulary. Part 2, introduces written Quenya, and extends the vocabulary; as well as presenting passage translation. Part 3, gives the full vocabulary, and additional passage work of a more difficult standard.&#34;
</p>
</blockquote>
