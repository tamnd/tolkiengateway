**Third Age 2923** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2923**) is the 2923rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2923 was also known as **S.R. 1323** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Gróin](../G/Gróin.md), son of [Farin](../F/Farin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Andwise Roper](../A/Andwise_Roper.md), first child and son of [Hobson Gamgee](../H/Hobson_Gamgee.md).<sup id="cite_ref-C4_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C4-2">[2]</a></sup>
