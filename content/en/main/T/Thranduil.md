**Thranduil**, also known as the **Elvenking**, was a [Sindarin](../S/Sindar.md) [Elf](../E/Elves.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-2">[2]</a></sup> and King of the [Silvan](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) [Elves of Mirkwood](../E/Elves_of_Mirkwood.md) of the [Woodland Realm](../W/Woodland_Realm.md). Thranduil was son of [Oropher](../O/Oropher.md) and father of [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md).

The Elvenking is a name used for the ruler of the [Woodland Realm](../W/Woodland_Realm.md), the realm of the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) in the far northeast of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) by the late [Third Age](Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Early History {#Early_History}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a href="#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium">#Other versions of the legendarium</a></div></div>

Thranduil's birth date and early life are not recorded. He may have been born in the First Age, or early Second Age. He was of Sindarin descent. His father [Oropher](../O/Oropher.md) was originally one of the [Iathrim](../I/Iathrim.md) of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Anna_Lee_-_Thranduil_and_Legolas.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/c0/Anna_Lee_-_Thranduil_and_Legolas.jpg/300px-Anna_Lee_-_Thranduil_and_Legolas.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="434" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/c0/Anna_Lee_-_Thranduil_and_Legolas.jpg/450px-Anna_Lee_-_Thranduil_and_Legolas.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c0/Anna_Lee_-_Thranduil_and_Legolas.jpg 2x" data-file-width="588" data-file-height="850"/></a><figcaption><i>Thranduil and Legolas</i> by <a href="../A/Anna_Lee.md" title="Anna Lee">Anna Lee</a></figcaption></figure>

Many of the Sindar left [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) and travelled eastward before the building of the [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1000](../S/Second_Age_1000.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-6">[4]</a></sup> Thranduil is first recorded in this event, when he and his father, [Oropher](../O/Oropher.md), arrived "with only a handful of Sindar"<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-2">[2]</a></sup> in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [750](../S/Second_Age_750.md), in [Greenwood the Great](../M/Mirkwood.md).<sup id="cite_ref-foundation_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-foundation-7">[5]</a></sup> Oropher was taken by the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) as their lord and founded the [Woodland Realm](../W/Woodland_Realm.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Elves of Mirkwood](../E/Elves_of_Mirkwood.md) joined the [Last Alliance](../L/Last_Alliance.md) and in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3434](../S/Second_Age_3434.md), Thranduil followed his father and numerous lightly armed Elves in the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md). In the [Battle of Dagorlad](../B/Battle_of_Dagorlad.md), Oropher was slain in the first assault upon Mordor, "rushing forward at the head of his most doughty warriors before Gil-galad had given the signal for the advance."<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-2">[2]</a></sup> His son survived, but over the course of the war, two-thirds of his people had perished. After the [Siege of Barad-dûr](../S/Siege_of_Barad-dûr.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3441](../S/Second_Age_3441.md), when [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) was defeated, Thranduil led the remainder of his people north back to the Woodland Realm, where he was crowned king.<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

#### The Shadow Returns {#The_Shadow_Returns}

Upon the return of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) - disguised as the [Necromancer](../S/Sauron.md) - in around [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [1050](Third_Age_1050.md), southern Greenwood became increasingly dangerous; evil creatures such as the great [spiders](../S/Spiders.md) came to dwell in it and the forest soon came to be known as "[Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup> Thranduil's folk retreated to the north of the forest, where they established themselves near the [Forest River](../F/Forest_River.md). Thranduil had [his halls](../E/Elvenking's_Halls.md) delved underground, fashioned partly in memory (it was said) of the mighty, but long-lost, [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md) of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[note 3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Princes_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[7]</a></sup> In north-eastern Mirkwood, they were near the Forest River behind great, stone gates.

#### Battle of Five Armies {#Battle_of_Five_Armies}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Anke_Eißmann_-_Thorin_and_Thranduil.jpeg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5d/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thorin_and_Thranduil.jpeg/300px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thorin_and_Thranduil.jpeg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="227" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5d/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thorin_and_Thranduil.jpeg/450px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thorin_and_Thranduil.jpeg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5d/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thorin_and_Thranduil.jpeg/600px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thorin_and_Thranduil.jpeg 2x" data-file-width="1121" data-file-height="850"/></a><figcaption><i>Thorin and Thranduil</i> by <a href="../A/Anke_Eißmann.md" title="Anke Eißmann">Anke Eißmann</a></figcaption></figure>

In [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2941](Third_Age_2941.md), Thranduil and some of his folk were feasting in the woods when they were repeatedly disturbed by [a party](Thorin_and_Company.md) of [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md). After the third disturbance, the Elves captured [Thorin](Thorin.md) who refused to reveal to Thranduil the reason for their journey through Mirkwood.<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-11">[8]</a></sup> Following their battle with the spiders, the rest of [the company](Thorin_and_Company.md) was also captured by the Elves. Following their repeated failure to explain their presence in Mirkwood, Thranduil placed all the Dwarves under lock and key. They escaped, however, with the aid of a Hobbit, [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md). Baggins had evaded capture by Thranduil's people through the use of [his magic ring](The_One_Ring.md).

After the Dwarves' escape, Thranduil received word of what had transpired from the [Raft-elves](../R/Raft-elves.md) who returned up the [Forest River](../F/Forest_River.md). Thus he was now aware of the Dwarves' quest. Upon learning this, he stated, "no treasure will come back through Mirkwood without my having something to say in the matter."<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[9]</a></sup> He believed the Dwarves were incapable of slaying the Dragon. However, he soon heard from his own messengers (including the birds) that the [Dragon](../D/Dragons.md) [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) had been felled (and the [Lake-town](../L/Lake-town.md) was destroyed).

Thranduil was aware that Smaug had a massive hoard of treasure. Believing the Dwarves to be dead, and desiring a share of the treasure, he set out towards the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) with a company of Elves. On the way, they met messengers from [Bard](../B/Bard.md) who was seeking aid for his destroyed town. Thranduil gave aid to these Men, as the [Lake-men](../L/Lake-men.md) had been friends with the Wood-elves, and left elven craftsmen to aid in the labour and building of huts to fortify them against the coming winter.<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-13">[10]</a></sup> Together, Thranduil and Bard led their forces towards the Lonely Mountain and were surprised when they found out that the Dwarves not only survived Smaug's attacks, but had taken possession of the Mountain and its treasures; the [King under the Mountain](../K/King_under_the_Mountain.md) had returned.

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/eb/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="217" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/eb/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/eb/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg 2x" data-file-width="650" data-file-height="470"/></a><figcaption><i>The Arkenstone</i> by <a href="Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

As Thorin refused to give away any part of the treasure for [Dale](../D/Dale.md) and Lake-town under armed threat, Thranduil and Bard besieged the mountain. After a few days, a Dwarvish host led by [Dáin II Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md), who had been summoned by [raven](../R/Ravens.md) messengers, approached to support Thorin. But, two evenings before his arrival, Bilbo came before Thranduil and Bard bringing the [Arkenstone](../A/Arkenstone.md), a great jewel that Thorin valued above all, in order to make Thorin open to negotiations again. Thranduil was impressed by Bilbo and urged him to remain in order to avoid Thorin's wrath, but Bilbo returned to his friends. The next morning Bard and Thranduil entered into negotiations with an angered Thorin, who agreed to pay one-fourteenth share of the treasure in exchange for the stone. Thranduil was reluctant to start a war over gold, but when the forces of Dáin arrived the next day, before the trade had been made for the Arkenstone, the Dwarves proceeded to attack.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Anke_Eißmann_-_The_Battle.jpeg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/3b/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Battle.jpeg/300px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Battle.jpeg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="252" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/3b/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Battle.jpeg/450px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Battle.jpeg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/3b/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Battle.jpeg/600px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Battle.jpeg 2x" data-file-width="809" data-file-height="680"/></a><figcaption><i>The Battle</i> by <a href="../A/Anke_Eißmann.md" title="Anke Eißmann">Anke Eißmann</a></figcaption></figure>

At that moment, the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) and [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md) under [Bolg](../B/Bolg.md) were using the opportunity to march after the hoard, and he was accompanied by a cloud of great bats. After skirmishing among themselves, under the council of Gandalf, the three commanders agreed that the Goblins were the enemies of all. So the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md) began, "upon one side were the Goblins and the Wild Wolves, and upon the other were Elves and Men and Dwarves."<sup id="cite_ref-Burst_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Burst-14">[11]</a></sup> Thranduil's host was positioned on the southern side of the Mountain, and they were the first to charge. Many Elves and allies were slain and things looked grim when the [Eagles](../E/Eagles.md) and [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md) arrived on the battlefield. They turned the tide and the battle was won.

Thorin died soon after the battle. Thranduil laid [Orcrist](../O/Orcrist.md) on Thorin's tomb, where it was said to glow in warning when foes approached.

The victors divided the treasure, with Bard giving Thranduil the emeralds of [Girion](../G/Girion.md). When Bilbo and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) bade farewell to Thranduil, Bilbo gave him a necklace of silver and pearls. Thranduil gave the [hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) the title [Elf-friend](../E/Elf-friends.md) and returned with the remainder of his host to his realm in Mirkwood.

#### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

On [21 March](../_/21_March.md), [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3018](Third_Age_3018.md) [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) delivered [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) as a prisoner to Thranduil; but in June he later escaped. Thranduil sent his son [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) to Rivendell to inform [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md), and during the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md) Legolas was selected as one of the nine members of the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-council_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-15">[12]</a></sup> Another member of the Company was the Dwarf, [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md), the son of [Glóin](../G/Glóin.md) of [Thorin's company](Thorin_and_Company.md), who had previously been imprisoned by Thranduil. The unprecedented friendship between Elf and Dwarf helped to reconcile Thranduil's people and the Dwarves.

On [15 March](../_/15_March.md), [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md), [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s army from [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) attacked Mirkwood. There was a long [battle under the trees](../B/Battle_under_the_trees.md) and the woods were set on fire, but in the end Thranduil was victorious. And after the passing of Sauron, the [forces](../G/Galadhrim.md) of the [Lord and Lady of Galadhrim](../L/Lord_and_Lady_of_Galadhrim.md) stormed Dol Guldur and threw down its walls, finally cleansing the forest.<sup id="cite_ref-great_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-16">[13]</a></sup>

On [6 April](../_/6_April.md)<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[14]</a></sup> (the [day of the Elven New Year](../Y/Yestarë.md)), the Elvenking met with [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) in the midst of Mirkwood and renamed it *[Eryn Lasgalen](../E/Eryn_Lasgalen.md)*, "The Wood of the Greenleaves". With the forest cleansed and Sauron's forces destroyed, the forest was divided; Celeborn claimed the southern part of the forest north to the Narrows, Thranduil took the northern part of the forest south to the [Mountains](../M/Mountains_of_Mirkwood.md), and the two Elf-lords granted the middle to the [Beornings](../B/Beornings.md) and [Woodmen](../W/Woodmen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-great_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-16">[13]</a></sup>

### Fourth Age {#Fourth_Age}

The partition of Eryn Lasgalen is the last reference to Thranduil in the histories of Middle-earth. It is not said that he joined his son Legolas in settling Ithilien or in departing over the Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-18">[15]</a></sup> Presumably, he continued to rule the re-established Woodland Realm into the Fourth Age, but nothing of this is known for certain, as he could have sailed to the West at some unknown date.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup><sup id="cite_ref-Departure_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Departure-19">[note 4]</a></sup>

## Personality {#Personality}

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/M/Marya_Filatova_-_Thranduil.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/bb/Marya_Filatova_-_Thranduil.jpg/200px-Marya_Filatova_-_Thranduil.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="287" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/bb/Marya_Filatova_-_Thranduil.jpg/300px-Marya_Filatova_-_Thranduil.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/bb/Marya_Filatova_-_Thranduil.jpg/400px-Marya_Filatova_-_Thranduil.jpg 2x" data-file-width="500" data-file-height="717"/></a><figcaption><i>Thranduil by <a href="../M/Marya_Filatova.md" title="Marya Filatova">Marya Filatova</a></i></figcaption></figure>

Although his concern was primarily for his realm, the memory of the end of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) and what lay outside his borders haunted him:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>But there was in Thranduil&#39;s heart a still deeper shadow. He had seen the horror of <a href="../M/Mordor.md" title="Mordor">Mordor</a> and could not forget it. If ever he looked south its memory dimmed the light of the Sun, and though he knew that it was now broken and deserted and under the vigilance of the Kings of Men, fear spoke in his heart that it was not conquered for ever; it would arise again.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="../U/Unfinished_Tales.md" title="Unfinished Tales">Unfinished Tales</a></i>, &#34;<a href="The_History_of_Galadriel_and_Celeborn.md" title="The History of Galadriel and Celeborn">The History of Galadriel and Celeborn</a>&#34;, &#34;Appendix B: The Sindarin Princes of the Silvan Elves&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote>

Thranduil lived in atunement with nature, wearing a crown of woodland flowers, or autumn berries and red leaves, according to the season.<sup id="cite_ref-Barrels_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Barrels-20">[16]</a></sup> His banner was green in colour.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[17]</a></sup> He loved the forest, though it was dark and dangerous in many parts; and enjoyed hunting and feasting among the trees with his people.<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-11">[8]</a></sup> He was distrustful of strangers for the most part, although he had business dealings with the men of [Lake-town](../L/Lake-town.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Barrels_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Barrels-20">[16]</a></sup> He was mostly unconcerned with affairs of the world beyond Mirkwood unless a common enemy was shared or for trade with "their kinsfolk in the South, or \[...\] Men in distant lands."<sup id="cite_ref-Barrels_20-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Barrels-20">[16]</a></sup>

He had a particular fondness for white gems and wanted to acquire more: "if the elf-king had a weakness it was for treasure, especially for silver and white gems; and though his hoard was rich, was eager for more, since he had not yet as great a treasure as other elf-lords of old."<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-11">[8]</a></sup> Despite this weakness, he was wise and would not wantonly go to war, risking his people's lives, over treasure. This was evidenced when the Elvenking said, "long will I tarry, ere I begin this war for gold \[...\] let us hope still for something that will bring reconciliation."<sup id="cite_ref-Burst_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Burst-14">[11]</a></sup>

The name <i><b>Thranduil</b></i> means "Vigorous spring" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), from <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tharan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tharan (page does not exist)">tharan</a></i> ("vigorous") and [lenited](../L/Lenition.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tuil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tuil (page does not exist)">tuil</a></i> ("spring"). ## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The published [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) of *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, as well as one of its drafts,<sup id="cite_ref-foundation_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-foundation-7">[5]</a></sup> mention Thranduil as King of the Woodland realm, founded in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [750](../S/Second_Age_750.md), suggesting (but not explicitly mentioning) that he was possibly its founder and first king.

His father Oropher was invented later, while Tolkien was fleshing out the background of Thranduil and the history of the Silvan realms, given a far older origin, explicitly clarifying that Thranduil was not the founder of the realm, and he only succeeded his father after the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md). Oropher and Thranduil's backstory were never incorporated in more "canonical" narratives, and the drafts exploring them were published in the *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Birth_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Birth-3">[note 1]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Thranduil is played by <a href="../L/Lee_Pace.md" title="Lee Pace">Lee Pace</a> in the <i>Hobbit</i> <a href="The_Hobbit_(film_series).md" title="The Hobbit (film series)">film trilogy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting-23">[19]</a></sup> Thranduil is first seen in the prologue paying homage to King <a href="Thrór.md" title="Thrór">Thrór</a> inside within the <a href="../L/Lonely_Mountain.md" title="Lonely Mountain">Lonely Mountain</a>, though leaves in a huff when the <a href="../D/Dwarves.md" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a> refuse to give him a box of white jewels. He is next seen with an army outside of <a href="../L/Lonely_Mountain.md" title="Lonely Mountain">Erebor</a> riding an elk, seemingly ready to help during the <a href="../S/Sack_of_Erebor.md" title="Sack of Erebor">Sack of Erebor</a>. However, he decides not to help and turns away.</dd></dl>

<b>2013: <i><a href="The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Mentioning his past, Thranduil is portrayed as a widower and also an isolationist king who isn&#39;t concerned with the evil that happens beyond his borders. He has generously raised <a href="Tauriel.md" title="Tauriel">Tauriel</a> for the last 600 years, and also mentions that he has fought the &#34;<a href="../D/Dragons.md" title="Dragons">serpents of the North</a>&#34;.</dd>
<dd>When <a href="Thorin.md" title="Thorin">Thorin</a> is taken before Thranduil in the <a href="../E/Elvenking&#39;s_Halls.md" title="Elvenking&#39;s Halls">Elvenking&#39;s Halls</a>, he surmises that he and his <a href="Thorin_and_Company.md" title="Thorin and Company">Company</a> are going to try to reclaim the <a href="../L/Lonely_Mountain.md" title="Lonely Mountain">Lonely Mountain</a> from <a href="../S/Smaug.md" title="Smaug">Smaug</a> (unlike in the book, where he had no idea as to their purpose). He offers a deal to Thorin, saying he will let the Company go if they will pay him the white jewels he desires inside the Mountain. When the Dwarf refuses and chastises him for not offering aid to the <a href="../D/Dwarves_of_Erebor.md" title="Dwarves of Erebor">Dwarves</a> the day of the Sack, the Elven-king reveals a large disfigurement on his face underneath his skin, mentioning his fighting against the &#39;great serpents of the North.&#39;</dd>
<dd>Later, an <a href="../U/Unseen.md" title="Unseen">invisible</a> Bilbo Baggins spies his discussion with Tauriel, having ordered her company of Elves to destroy the <a href="../S/Spiders.md" title="Spiders">spiders</a>&#39;s nests, but not beyond the borders of the Woodland Realm, displaying his disregard for the outside world.</dd>
<dd>He and Legolas interrogate <a href="../N/Narzug.md" title="Narzug">Narzug</a>, a captured <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">Orc</a> who claims that <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">&#34;The One&#34;</a> is returning, and will unleash a powerful weapon (possibly Smaug). Despite his own promise to &#34;set him free&#34;, he beheads the orc, and orders the kingdom to be completely sealed off from the outside world.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="The_Hobbit%253A_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>After learning of <a href="../S/Smaug.md" title="Smaug">Smaug</a>&#39;s demise, Thranduil leads his Elven army to claim the white jewels from the Dwarves of Erebor, even by force. It is revealed that these jewels were meant for his wife, who had been taken to <a href="../G/Gundabad.md" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a> and tortured by Orcs to death many years prior. When the army arrives in <a href="../D/Dale.md" title="Dale">Dale</a>, he forges an allegiance with <a href="../B/Bard.md" title="Bard">Bard</a>, also offering the <a href="../L/Lake-town.md" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a> refugees food and supplies. When Gandalf arrives at Erebor to warn them of the impending attack by the approaching Orc army, the Elven-king refuses to listen. Thranduil&#39;s soldiers come to clashes with those of <a href="../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md" title="Dáin Ironfoot">Dáin Ironfoot</a> right before the Orcs arrive, leading to the <a href="../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md" title="Battle of Five Armies">Battle of Five Armies</a>. Thranduil himself fights in the battle against the Orcs, but withdraws his forces when defence of Dale results in many Elven casualties. This leads to a confrontation between him and <a href="Tauriel.md" title="Tauriel">Tauriel</a> before she and <a href="../L/Legolas.md" title="Legolas">Legolas</a> go to Thorin&#39;s aid at <a href="../R/Ravenhill.md" title="Ravenhill">Ravenhill</a>. The event causes a rift between Thranduil and his son, to the point where Legolas decides not to return to the kingdom. Thranduil then advises him to go and find the <a href="../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md" title="Rangers of the North">Ranger</a> known as &#34;<a class="mw-redirect" href="../S/Strider_(Aragorn).md" title="Strider (Aragorn)">Strider</a>&#34; among the <a href="../D/Dúnedain.md" title="Dúnedain">Dúnedain</a>. The Elven-king also accepts Tauriel&#39;s love for <a href="../K/Kíli.md" title="Kíli">Kíli</a> when he finds her mourning over the Dwarf&#39;s death.</dd></dl>

### Games {#Games}

<b>2003: <a href="The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game).md" title="The Hobbit (2003 video game)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (2003 video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Thranduil is referred to as &#34;Elvenking Thranduil&#34;, using both his title from <i>The Hobbit</i> and his name from <i>The Lord of the Rings</i>, to accommodate players who have only read <i>The Hobbit</i>. Thranduil first appears in the level &#34;Barrels Out of Bond&#34;, in which he can be overheard speaking about the <a href="../W/White_Council.md" title="White Council">White Council</a> and their attack on <a href="../D/Dol_Guldur.md" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a>. He returns as a conversation partner in the last level, &#34;The Clouds Burst&#34;, in which he and Gandalf ask the player, in the persona of Bilbo, to deliver a message to Bard. No voice actor is specified for this part.</dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Thranduil is a hero for the Elven faction. In the good campaign, he shows up after the <a href="../B/Battle_of_Dale.md" title="Battle of Dale">Battle of Dale</a>, and participates in the Siege of <a href="../D/Dol_Guldur.md" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2017: <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Thranduil&#39;s earliest appearances are during the War of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../L/Last_Alliance.md" title="Last Alliance">Last Alliance</a>. He can be found during the <a href="../B/Battle_of_Dagorlad.md" title="Battle of Dagorlad">Battle of Dagorlad</a> and later is seen during the <a href="../S/Siege_of_Barad-dûr.md" title="Siege of Barad-dûr">Siege of Barad-dûr</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In the present time, Thranduil is first encountered fighting in <a href="../M/Mirkwood.md" title="Mirkwood">Mirkwood</a> during the last days of the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>. He joins the forces of Lord <a href="../C/Celeborn.md" title="Celeborn">Celeborn</a> and Lady <a href="../G/Galadriel.md" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a> and their combined host moves on <a href="../D/Dol_Guldur.md" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a> where Galadriel brings down the fortress. Afterwards he and Celeborn renamed the forest <a href="../E/Eryn_Lasgalen.md" title="Eryn Lasgalen">Eryn Lasgalen</a> and divide it into three parts, giving the middle section to the <a href="../W/Woodmen.md" title="Woodmen">Woodmen</a>. Thranduil then returns to his halls in Felegoth where his son <a href="../L/Legolas.md" title="Legolas">Legolas</a> and the player join him after the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../O/One_Ring.md" title="One Ring">One Ring</a> is destroyed. Thranduil&#39;s people have to deal with an unexpected situation when most of spiders in Mirkwood, except for the very young or very old ones, suddenly depart the forest en masse, travelling south to answer the call of <a href="../S/Shelob.md" title="Shelob">their mother</a>. In their place come the Wood-trolls from the north and Thranduil tasks the player with finding the source of the new threat and preventing it from reaching his borders.</dd></dl>

<b>2021: <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Rise_to_War.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: Rise to War">The Lord of the Rings: Rise to War</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Thraduil is a flawless rarity commander for the <a href="../L/Lothlórien.md" title="Lothlórien">Elves</a> Faction and is extremely hard to get loyalty for.</dd></dl>
