**Thrór's Map** is a map of the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) that proved important in the [retaking of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor "Quest of Erebor").

## History {#History}

The [Dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) attacked and destroyed the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) went into exile with his kin and sometime until, old and desperate, he went to [Moria](/wiki/Moria) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2790](/wiki/Third_Age_2790 "Third Age 2790")<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> he drew this Map, and wrote the [moon-letters](/wiki/Moon-letters "Moon-letters") on a day of [1 Lithe](/wiki/1_Lithe), when the moon was "a silver broad crescent".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> When [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) read the letters in [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> he stated that they had to have been written under a moon in the same phase on the same date in some past year.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Audrey_Corman_-_Thror%27s_Map.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/3/38/Audrey_Corman_-_Thror%27s_Map.jpg/300px-Audrey_Corman_-_Thror%27s_Map.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="225" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/3/38/Audrey_Corman_-_Thror%27s_Map.jpg/450px-Audrey_Corman_-_Thror%27s_Map.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/3/38/Audrey_Corman_-_Thror%27s_Map.jpg/600px-Audrey_Corman_-_Thror%27s_Map.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="768"/></a><figcaption><i>Thror&#39;s Map</i> by <a href="/wiki/Audrey_Corman" title="Audrey Corman">Audrey Corman</a>.</figcaption></figure>

In his old age, Thrór set out to wander the lands of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), but before departing he gave the heirlooms of his house to his son Thráin, including his Map. Years later, Thráin too set out into Middle-earth, seeking to recover Erebor himself, and taking with him the Map to guide his way. He was captured in the Wild by the servants of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), but Sauron captured Thráin for one of the [Seven](/wiki/Seven_Rings "Seven Rings") [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power) he bore, and so overlooked the Map he carried too. This proved to be a serious oversight, because [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) secretly entered Sauron's lair at [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) while Thráin was a prisoner there, and managed to escape with the Map and the key that opened the secret door.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-5">[4]</a></sup>

Using Thrór's Map, his grandson [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) was able to lead [a small group of Dwarves](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") (and one [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits"), a certain [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)) on his own quest to refound the ancient Dwarf-realm.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-5">[4]</a></sup> Through many adventures, they were able to recover Erebor and bring about the destruction of Smaug, dealing Sauron a serious reverse.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

As was traditional among the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), it was drawn with East at the top of the map and showed the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) of [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"), the [Running](/wiki/River_Running "River Running") River that flowed from it, and the lands about. It also contained mention of a [secret entrance](/wiki/Back_Door "Back Door") to the Mountain, and (written in secret [Moon-letters](/wiki/Moon-letters)) instructions as to how it might be opened.

The Map's Runes are written in Anglo-Saxon <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Saxon_runes" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Futhorc</a> however it is spelt out in modern English, not in [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul). The small text on the left, below the hand, reads.

</blockquote>

"Thrór's Map" is reproduced in facsimile in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* (and as plate No. 86 in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Artist_and_Illustrator)*). The map was drawn by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), and a preliminary version of the map appears as plate No. 85 in *Artist &amp; Illustrator*.<sup id="cite_ref-AI_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-9">[7]</a></sup>

In [1979](/wiki/1979) a coloured version by [H.E. Riddett](/wiki/H.E._Riddett) was published as a poster by [Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin "Allen and Unwin") (together with the "[Wilderland](/wiki/Map_of_Wilderland "Map of Wilderland")" map).<sup id="cite_ref-AH_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AH-10">[8]</a></sup>
