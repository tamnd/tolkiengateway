**Thrór's Map** is a map of the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) that proved important in the [retaking of Erebor](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md).

## History {#History}

The [Dragon](../D/Dragons.md) [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) attacked and destroyed the [Kingdom under the Mountain](../K/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain.md) in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2770](Third_Age_2770.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> [Thrór](Thrór.md) went into exile with his kin and sometime until, old and desperate, he went to [Moria](../M/Moria.md) in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2790](Third_Age_2790.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> he drew this Map, and wrote the [moon-letters](../M/Moon-letters.md) on a day of [1 Lithe](../_/1_Lithe.md), when the moon was "a silver broad crescent".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> When [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) read the letters in [2941](Third_Age_2941.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> he stated that they had to have been written under a moon in the same phase on the same date in some past year.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Audrey_Corman_-_Thror&#39;s_Map.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/38/Audrey_Corman_-_Thror%27s_Map.jpg/300px-Audrey_Corman_-_Thror%27s_Map.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="225" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/38/Audrey_Corman_-_Thror%27s_Map.jpg/450px-Audrey_Corman_-_Thror%27s_Map.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/38/Audrey_Corman_-_Thror%27s_Map.jpg/600px-Audrey_Corman_-_Thror%27s_Map.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="768"/></a><figcaption><i>Thror&#39;s Map</i> by <a href="../A/Audrey_Corman.md" title="Audrey Corman">Audrey Corman</a>.</figcaption></figure>

In his old age, Thrór set out to wander the lands of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), but before departing he gave the heirlooms of his house to his son Thráin, including his Map. Years later, Thráin too set out into Middle-earth, seeking to recover Erebor himself, and taking with him the Map to guide his way. He was captured in the Wild by the servants of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), but Sauron captured Thráin for one of the [Seven](../S/Seven_Rings.md) [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md) he bore, and so overlooked the Map he carried too. This proved to be a serious oversight, because [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) secretly entered Sauron's lair at [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) while Thráin was a prisoner there, and managed to escape with the Map and the key that opened the secret door.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-5">[4]</a></sup>

Using Thrór's Map, his grandson [Thorin](Thorin.md) was able to lead [a small group of Dwarves](Thorin_and_Company.md) (and one [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md), a certain [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)) on his own quest to refound the ancient Dwarf-realm.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-5">[4]</a></sup> Through many adventures, they were able to recover Erebor and bring about the destruction of Smaug, dealing Sauron a serious reverse.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

As was traditional among the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), it was drawn with East at the top of the map and showed the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) of [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md), the [Running](../R/River_Running.md) River that flowed from it, and the lands about. It also contained mention of a [secret entrance](../B/Back_Door.md) to the Mountain, and (written in secret [Moon-letters](../M/Moon-letters.md)) instructions as to how it might be opened.

The Map's Runes are written in Anglo-Saxon <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Saxon_runes" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Futhorc</a> however it is spelt out in modern English, not in [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md). The small text on the left, below the hand, reads.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>five<br/>
feet high<br/>
the door an<br/>
d three may<br/>
walk abre<br/>
ast.<br/>
Th. Th. <sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 2]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>

The [moon-letters](../M/Moon-letters.md) read by Elrond are visible in the sketch and read:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Stand by the grey st<br/>
one when the thrush kn<br/>
ocks and the setting s<br/>
un with the last light<br/>
of Durin&#39;s day will sh<br/>
ine upon the keyhole<br/>
Th.
</p>
</blockquote>

"Thrór's Map" is reproduced in facsimile in *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* (and as plate No. 86 in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_Artist_and_Illustrator.md)*). The map was drawn by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), and a preliminary version of the map appears as plate No. 85 in *Artist &amp; Illustrator*.<sup id="cite_ref-AI_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-9">[7]</a></sup>

In [1979](../_/1979.md) a coloured version by [H.E. Riddett](../H/H.E._Riddett.md) was published as a poster by [Allen &amp; Unwin](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md) (together with the "[Wilderland](../M/Map_of_Wilderland.md)" map).<sup id="cite_ref-AH_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AH-10">[8]</a></sup>
