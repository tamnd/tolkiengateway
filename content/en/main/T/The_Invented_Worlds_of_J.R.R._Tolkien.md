<i><b>The Invented Worlds of J.R.R. Tolkien: Drawings and Original Manuscripts from the Marquette University Collection</b></i> was first published in 2004 by [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md) as the catalogue from the exhibition of the same name held at the Haggerty Museum of Art (October 21, 2004 - January 30, 2005).

### Manuscripts {#Manuscripts}

- Illustration - The first draft version of the first page of The Book of Mazarbul.
- Manuscript page - includes the first draft version of Thror’s Map.
- Illustration - Isengard &amp; Orthanc.
- Manuscript page - includes the earliest sketch of the inscription and signs on the Doors of Durin.
- Manuscript page - The Ring Verse in Tolkien’s characteristic handwriting, and also in tengwar.
- Illustration - A page from Mr. Bliss - So he turned sharp to the right at the next turning, and ran straight into Mr.Day.
- Manuscript page - includes a plan of Minas Tirith, and a topographical plan of the city and the surrounding mountains.
- Illustration - A [code letter from Tolkien to Father Francis Morgan](../L/Letter_to_Father_Francis_Morgan.md).
- Illustration - Cave paintings from The Father Christmas Letters.
- Manuscript page - A synoptic time scheme used while writing LotR. It summarizes the movements of characters between the 13th and 17 March.
