**Third Age 2784** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2784**) is the 2784th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2784 was also known as **S.R. 1184** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Chieftain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md) of the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md) [Arassuil](../A/Arassuil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-North_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Arathorn I](../A/Arathorn_I.md), the son of Arassuil, becomes twelfth Chieftain.<sup id="cite_ref-North_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-1">[1]</a></sup>
