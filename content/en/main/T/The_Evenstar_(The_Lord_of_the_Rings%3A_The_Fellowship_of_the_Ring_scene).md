**The Evenstar** is the twenty-second scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* and the twenty-sixth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition).md>).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) and [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) pledge their love to each other on a bridge in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md). Arwen hands Aragorn the Evenstar necklace as a token of her choice to become mortal for him. Aragorn is aware of how weighty this gift is, but accepts it upon her insistence. They share a kiss.
