**Trees** were the tallest of the *[olvar](/wiki/Olvar "Olvar")* of [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna), plentiful throughout [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and formed [forests](/wiki/Category:Forests "Category:Forests"). Although the trees were *olver*, some trees were animate, like the [Huorns](/wiki/Huorns). There was also the [race](/wiki/Portal:Characters "Portal:Characters") known as [Ents](/wiki/Ents), herders of the trees.

Trees, being living things, were never regarded as gender-neutral in the [High-elven tongue](/wiki/Eldarin "Eldarin").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Trees were of special importance to Tolkien - in his short story "[Leaf by Niggle](/wiki/Leaf_by_Niggle)", which in a sense was an elaborate allegory explaining his own creative process, the protagonist, Niggle, spends his life painting a single Tree.
