**Trees** were the tallest of the *[olvar](../O/Olvar.md)* of [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md), plentiful throughout [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) and formed [forests](../../category/F/Forests.md). Although the trees were *olver*, some trees were animate, like the [Huorns](../H/Huorns.md). There was also the [race](../../portal/C/Characters.md) known as [Ents](../E/Ents.md), herders of the trees.

Trees, being living things, were never regarded as gender-neutral in the [High-elven tongue](../E/Eldarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Trees were of special importance to Tolkien - in his short story "[Leaf by Niggle](../L/Leaf_by_Niggle.md)", which in a sense was an elaborate allegory explaining his own creative process, the protagonist, Niggle, spends his life painting a single Tree.
