<i><b>The Eldar</b></i> is a song by [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian), appearing in their 1998 album, [Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth). It is a ballad, with piano serving as the main instrument.
