<i><b>The Nature of Middle-earth: Late Writings on the Lands, Inhabitants, and Metaphysics of Middle-earth</b></i> is a compilation of minor writings by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), edited by [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) and published in [2021](/wiki/2021) in a single book.

Its many texts discuss a variety of topics related to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), such as its "nature" and landscapes, the characteristics of different [races](/wiki/Portal:Characters "Portal:Characters"), theology, chronology, and many other miscellaneous matters in Tolkien's [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"). Most of these writings were previously [unpublished material](/wiki/Unpublished_material "Unpublished material"), but some had been already published in the linguistic journals *[Parma Eldalamberon](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon)* and *[Vinyar Tengwar](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar)*.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The first ever publication of J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s final writings on <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a>, covering a wide-range of subjects, and the perfect next read for those who have enjoyed <i><a href="/wiki/Unfinished_Tales" title="Unfinished Tales">Unfinished Tales</a></i> and the <i><a href="/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth" title="The History of Middle-earth">History of Middle-earth</a></i> series and are hungry for more.
</p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><i>The Nature of Middle-earth</i> comprises numerous late (c. 1959-73) and previously unpublished writings by J.R.R. Tolkien on the &#34;nature&#34; of Middle-earth, in both chief senses of that word: both metaphysical and natural/historical.<br/>
It is for Tolkien fans, readers, and scholars interested in learning more about Tolkien&#39;s own views on Middle-earth. It will appeal in particular to those readers who enjoyed <i>Unfinished Tales</i>, and some of the later volumes of the <i>History of Middle-earth</i>. Indeed, many of the texts to be included are closely associated with materials published in those places, and were sent to Hostetter, specifically, in photocopy by <a href="/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien" title="Christopher Tolkien">Christopher Tolkien</a> for potential publication.<br/>
Much as <i>Unfinished Tales</i> forms an unofficial thirteenth volume of the <i>History of Middle-earth</i>, this new book will sit very nicely alongside as an unofficial 14th volume. Of particular note, given the impending <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">Amazon series</a>, are several texts detailing the lands, flora, and fauna of <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor" title="Númenor">Númenor</a>, and the lives of <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans" title="Númenóreans">Númenóreans</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>
