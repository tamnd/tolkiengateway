<i><b>Tolkien: A Mythology for England?</b></i> is a collection of the proceedings of the 13th Tolkien Society Seminar held in Cambridge in 1999. The book was published by [The Tolkien Society](The_Tolkien_Society.md) in [2000](../_/2000.md) under the auspices of the [Peter Roe Memorial Fund](../P/Peter_Roe_Memorial_Fund.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- "The Romans in Middle-earth: Classical Precedents for Tolkien's Early Conception of Mythology" by Allan Turner
- "Monsters, Maldon and Holy Maidenhood: Tolkien's Real Mythology for England" by Andrew Wells
- "Reflections on Reflections on Fairy Stories" by Gwydion M. Williams
- "England? Which England?: Concepts Of Englishness 1982-1954" by [David Doughan](../D/David_Doughan.md)
- "Some English Myth-Makers: Success and Failure Prior to Tolkien" by Christine Davidson
- "Tolkien's Mythology of England: The Shire as 'Local Community'" by [John Ellison](../J/John_Ellison.md)
