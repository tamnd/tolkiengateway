**Tar-** was the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) title taken on by [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") rulers meaning "high" as in "royal" or "noble". The feminine form *tári* meant "queen".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Annatar](/wiki/Annatar)* - "Lord of Gifts"
- *[Elentári](/wiki/Elent%C3%A1ri)* - "Star-Queen"
- *[Kementári](/wiki/Kement%C3%A1ri)* - "Queen of the Earth"
- *[Tarcil](/wiki/Tarcil)* - "High Man"
- *[Sorontar](/wiki/Sorontar)* - "[Lord of the Eagles](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Eagles)"

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)* mention [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *tāro* ("king: only used of the legitimate kings of the whole tribes"), from root TĀ. The same root yields [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)/[Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[taur](/wiki/Taur "Taur")*.<sup id="cite_ref-LR2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR2-2">[2]</a></sup>= <span></span>
