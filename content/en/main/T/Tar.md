**Tar-** was the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) title taken on by [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) rulers meaning "high" as in "royal" or "noble". The feminine form *tári* meant "queen".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Annatar](../A/Annatar.md)* - "Lord of Gifts"
- *[Elentári](../E/Elentári.md)* - "Star-Queen"
- *[Kementári](../K/Kementári.md)* - "Queen of the Earth"
- *[Tarcil](Tarcil.md)* - "High Man"
- *[Sorontar](../S/Sorontar.md)* - "[Lord of the Eagles](../L/Lord_of_the_Eagles.md)"

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*[The Etymologies](The_Etymologies.md)* mention [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) *tāro* ("king: only used of the legitimate kings of the whole tribes"), from root TĀ. The same root yields [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md)/[Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *[taur](Taur.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-LR2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR2-2">[2]</a></sup>= <span></span>
