The **Trollshaws** were the upland woods, consisting at least partly of [beech](/wiki/Beeches "Beeches") trees, that lay to the west of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) between the Rivers [Hoarwell](/wiki/Hoarwell) and [Loudwater](/wiki/Bruinen "Bruinen").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On the hills of the Trollshaws were shallow caves, such as the [Trolls' lair](/wiki/Trolls%27_lair), and Mannish castles and towers.

## History {#History}

The Trollshaws were within the borders of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and later of the smaller realm of [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur). The [Men](/wiki/Men) of Rhudaur built castles and towers on the top of some of its hills.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

They were the haunt of [Trolls](/wiki/Trolls), especially after the fall of Arnor; [Stone-trolls](/wiki/Stone-trolls) from "the mountains" came to the woodlands. This was the case of [William, Tom, and Bert](/wiki/William,_Tom,_and_Bert) who, having eaten a village and a half on their way, complained about the scarcity of people in the area and instead had to make do with mutton. While they made their camp, they famously waylaid [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") and [his companions](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") who were on their [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor). Thanks to the distractions of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), the Trolls were turned to stone by the [Sun](/wiki/Sun).<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-3">[3]</a></sup> Years later, [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and his companions traversed the woodland and found the Trolls' petrified forms on their way to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Shaw is an archaic word meaning "thicket, small wood, copse, or grove". The Trollshaws are referred to in the narrative as "the Trolls' wood".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> According to the Index of The Lord of the Rings the "Trolls' wood" is equivalent to the "Trollshaws".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Trollshaws means Trollwoods.

The woods geographically belonged to where the ancient realm of [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur) used to be,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and were perhaps named after it. The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name *Rhudaur* means "Troll shaw".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The Trollshaws is a region of the game located between the <a href="/wiki/Lone-lands" title="Lone-lands">Lone-lands</a> across the <a href="/wiki/Last_Bridge" title="Last Bridge">Last Bridge</a> in the west, the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a> beyond <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a> to the north-east, and <a href="/wiki/Eregion" title="Eregion">Eregion</a> to the south. The river <a href="/wiki/Bruinen" title="Bruinen">Bruinen</a> plays a prominent role there, with both the &#34;Bruinen Gorges&#34; and the hills of &#34;Tâl Bruinen&#34; being important areas. The &#34;<a href="/wiki/Angle" title="Angle">Angle</a> of <a href="/wiki/Hoarwell" title="Hoarwell">Mitheithel</a>&#34; between the Hoarwell and the Loudwater is also featured.</dd></dl>

There are also many ruins inhabited by <a href="/wiki/Trolls" title="Trolls">Trolls</a>, hostile <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Hill-men" title="Hill-men">Hill-men</a>, and other foes. The <a href="/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North" title="Rangers of the North">Ranger</a> village &#34;Tornhad&#34; is another settlement, located in the Angle.</dd></dl>
