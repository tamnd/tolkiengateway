**The Friendship Onion** is a podcast hosted by actors [Dominic Monaghan](/wiki/Dominic_Monaghan) and [Billy Boyd](/wiki/Billy_Boyd). It was launched in 2021 by Kast Media. In the podcast, Monaghan and Boyd share stories about their time filming *[The Lord of the Rings (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)>)*, interview members of the cast and crew, and answer questions from fans.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Notable guests who have appeared on the podcast include [Elijah Wood](/wiki/Elijah_Wood), [Richard Taylor](/wiki/Richard_Taylor), [Stephen Colbert](/wiki/Stephen_Colbert), [Sean Astin](/wiki/Sean_Astin), [Corey Olsen](/wiki/Corey_Olsen), [Mark Ordesky](/wiki/Mark_Ordesky), and [Bear McCreary](/wiki/Bear_McCreary).
