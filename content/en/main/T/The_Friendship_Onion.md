**The Friendship Onion** is a podcast hosted by actors [Dominic Monaghan](../D/Dominic_Monaghan.md) and [Billy Boyd](../B/Billy_Boyd.md). It was launched in 2021 by Kast Media. In the podcast, Monaghan and Boyd share stories about their time filming *[The Lord of the Rings (film series)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)*, interview members of the cast and crew, and answer questions from fans.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Notable guests who have appeared on the podcast include [Elijah Wood](../E/Elijah_Wood.md), [Richard Taylor](../R/Richard_Taylor.md), [Stephen Colbert](../S/Stephen_Colbert.md), [Sean Astin](../S/Sean_Astin.md), [Corey Olsen](../C/Corey_Olsen.md), [Mark Ordesky](../M/Mark_Ordesky.md), and [Bear McCreary](../B/Bear_McCreary.md).
