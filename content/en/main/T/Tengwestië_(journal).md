<i><b>Tengwestië</b></i> is the online journal of the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](/wiki/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship) focusing primarily on the invented languages of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). It was started in [2003](/wiki/2003) and is edited by [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) and [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne).<sup id="cite_ref-ELF_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ELF-1">[1]</a></sup>
