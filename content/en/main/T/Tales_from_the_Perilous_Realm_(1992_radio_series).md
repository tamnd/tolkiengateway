**Tales from the Perilous Realm** is a [1992](/wiki/1992) radio series of *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](/wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm)*, adapted by [Brian Sibley](/wiki/Brian_Sibley), produced by John Taylor and aired on [BBC Radio 5](/wiki/BBC "BBC"). It is often called an [audiobook](/wiki/Audiobooks "Audiobooks"), though it is not.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The series contains the four stories in *Tales from the Perilous Realm*: *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)*, *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*, *[Leaf by Niggle](/wiki/Leaf_by_Niggle)* and *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*. *The Adventures of Tom Bombadil* is the dramatization of Tom's scenes with the four Hobbits in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, rather than a retelling of the events of the poems *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](</wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil_(poem)> "The Adventures of Tom Bombadil (poem)")* and *[Bombadil Goes Boating](/wiki/Bombadil_Goes_Boating)*. Brian Sibley had expressed some pity in not including it in his [radio adaptation](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), and decided to include it here <a class="external autonumber" href="http://briansibleytheworks.blogspot.com/2008/01/this-page-is-still-under-construction_23.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[1]</a>.

## Air dates {#Air_dates}

*Farmer Giles of Ham* was aired in two parts, the first on [August 16](/wiki/16_August "16 August"), [1992](/wiki/1992), and the second on [August 23](/wiki/23_August "23 August"). *Smith of Wootton Major* was broadcast a week later, on [August 30](/wiki/30_August "30 August"). *The Adventures of Tom Bombadil* was also aired in two parts, on [September 6](/wiki/6_September "6 September") and [September 13](/wiki/13_September "13 September"), and *Leaf by Niggle* on [September 20](/wiki/20_September "20 September").
