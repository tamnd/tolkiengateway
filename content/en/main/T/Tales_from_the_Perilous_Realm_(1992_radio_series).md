**Tales from the Perilous Realm** is a [1992](../_/1992.md) radio series of *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm.md)*, adapted by [Brian Sibley](../B/Brian_Sibley.md), produced by John Taylor and aired on [BBC Radio 5](../B/BBC.md). It is often called an [audiobook](../A/Audiobooks.md), though it is not.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The series contains the four stories in *Tales from the Perilous Realm*: *[Farmer Giles of Ham](../F/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md)*, *[Smith of Wootton Major](../S/Smith_of_Wootton_Major.md)*, *[Leaf by Niggle](../L/Leaf_by_Niggle.md)* and *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md)*. *The Adventures of Tom Bombadil* is the dramatization of Tom's scenes with the four Hobbits in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, rather than a retelling of the events of the poems *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](<The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil_(poem).md>)* and *[Bombadil Goes Boating](../B/Bombadil_Goes_Boating.md)*. Brian Sibley had expressed some pity in not including it in his [radio adaptation](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), and decided to include it here <a class="external autonumber" href="http://briansibleytheworks.blogspot.com/2008/01/this-page-is-still-under-construction_23.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[1]</a>.

## Air dates {#Air_dates}

*Farmer Giles of Ham* was aired in two parts, the first on [August 16](../_/16_August.md), [1992](../_/1992.md), and the second on [August 23](../_/23_August.md). *Smith of Wootton Major* was broadcast a week later, on [August 30](../_/30_August.md). *The Adventures of Tom Bombadil* was also aired in two parts, on [September 6](../_/6_September.md) and [September 13](../_/13_September.md), and *Leaf by Niggle* on [September 20](../_/20_September.md).
