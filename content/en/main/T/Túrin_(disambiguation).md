**Túrin** means "he who desires mastery" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) (from *[tûr](Tûr.md)*, "mastery" or "lord" and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ind&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ind (page does not exist)">ind</a></i>, "mood, desire, heart") and can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- <b><a href="Túrin.md" title="Túrin">Túrin Turambar</a> (<a href="../F/First_Age.md" title="First Age">F.A.</a> <a href="../F/First_Age_464.md" title="First Age 464">464</a> - <a href="../F/First_Age_499.md" title="First Age 499">499</a>), the tragic hero of the <a href="../F/First_Age.md" title="First Age">First Age</a> who is the main protagonist of <i><a href="The_Children_of_Húrin.md" title="The Children of Húrin">The Children of Húrin</a></i>.</b>
- [Túrin I](Túrin_I.md) ([T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2165](Third_Age_2165.md) - [2278](Third_Age_2278.md)), sixth [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) who ruled during the [Watchful Peace](../W/Watchful_Peace.md).
- [Túrin II](Túrin_II.md) ([T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2815](Third_Age_2815.md) - [2914](Third_Age_2914.md)), twenty-third [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) whose reign marked the loss of [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).
