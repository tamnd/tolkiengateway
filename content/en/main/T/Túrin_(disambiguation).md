**Túrin** means "he who desires mastery" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (from *[tûr](/wiki/T%C3%BBr "Tûr")*, "mastery" or "lord" and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ind&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ind (page does not exist)">ind</a></i>, "mood, desire, heart") and can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- <b><a href="/wiki/T%C3%BArin" title="Túrin">Túrin Turambar</a> (<a href="/wiki/First_Age" title="First Age">F.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/First_Age_464" title="First Age 464">464</a> - <a href="/wiki/First_Age_499" title="First Age 499">499</a>), the tragic hero of the <a href="/wiki/First_Age" title="First Age">First Age</a> who is the main protagonist of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin" title="The Children of Húrin">The Children of Húrin</a></i>.</b>
- [Túrin I](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_I) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2165](/wiki/Third_Age_2165 "Third Age 2165") - [2278](/wiki/Third_Age_2278 "Third Age 2278")), sixth [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) who ruled during the [Watchful Peace](/wiki/Watchful_Peace).
- [Túrin II](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_II) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2815](/wiki/Third_Age_2815 "Third Age 2815") - [2914](/wiki/Third_Age_2914 "Third Age 2914")), twenty-third [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) whose reign marked the loss of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).
