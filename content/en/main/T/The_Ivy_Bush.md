**The Ivy Bush** was an inn located on [Bywater Road](/wiki/Bywater_Road) in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

The Ivy Bush was frequented by the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) of [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) and [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater). Prior to [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party), many people gathered around [Gaffer Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee), were interested in the stories he told about [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins). [Daddy Twofoot](/wiki/Daddy_Twofoot), [Old Noakes](/wiki/Old_Noakes), the miller [Sandyman](/wiki/Sandyman) and others, such as a stranger from [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving) gossipped about the mysterious Bagginses and the rumored treasure in [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The Ivy Bush is the name of a public house located on Hagley Road, [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham), England.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> When [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") lived on Stirling Road this would have been his local hostelry and could be found halfway between his house and the [Birmingham Oratory](/wiki/Birmingham_Oratory), where he attended Mass for a time.
