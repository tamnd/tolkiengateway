**The Ivy Bush** was an inn located on [Bywater Road](../B/Bywater_Road.md) in [the Shire](The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

The Ivy Bush was frequented by the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) of [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md) and [Bywater](../B/Bywater.md). Prior to [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md), many people gathered around [Gaffer Gamgee](../G/Gaffer_Gamgee.md), were interested in the stories he told about [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md). [Daddy Twofoot](../D/Daddy_Twofoot.md), [Old Noakes](../O/Old_Noakes.md), the miller [Sandyman](../S/Sandyman.md) and others, such as a stranger from [Michel Delving](../M/Michel_Delving.md) gossipped about the mysterious Bagginses and the rumored treasure in [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The Ivy Bush is the name of a public house located on Hagley Road, [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md), England.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> When [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) lived on Stirling Road this would have been his local hostelry and could be found halfway between his house and the [Birmingham Oratory](../B/Birmingham_Oratory.md), where he attended Mass for a time.
