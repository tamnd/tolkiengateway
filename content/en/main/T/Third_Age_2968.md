**Third Age 2968** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2968**) is the 2968th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2968 was also known as **S.R. 1368** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), son of [Drogo Baggins](../D/Drogo_Baggins.md) and [Primula Brandybuck](../P/Primula_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
