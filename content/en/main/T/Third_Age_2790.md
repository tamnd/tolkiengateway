**Third Age 2790** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2790**) is the 2790th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2790 was also known as **S.R. 1190** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Thrór](Thrór.md) and [Nár](../N/Nár.md) go to [Moria](../M/Moria.md).<sup id="cite_ref-durin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-durin-1">[1]</a></sup> Thrór slain by [an Orc](../A/Azog.md) in [Moria](../M/Moria.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Nár returns to [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md).<sup id="cite_ref-durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-durin-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) gather for [a war of vengeance](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Gerontius](../G/Gerontius_Took.md), son of [Fortinbras Took I](../F/Fortinbras_Took_I.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
