<i><b>Tales From the Lone-lands</b></i> is a set of adventures for the second edition of the [*The One Ring* Roleplaying Game](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)"). A version for [The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying), titled <i><b>Tales From Eriador</b></i>, was also released.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><i><b>Tales from the Lone-lands</b></i> contains six adventures for <i>The One Ring</i>™, the official tabletop roleplaying game based on the works of J.R.R. Tolkien. All set in the lone-lands of north-western Middle-earth around the year 2965 of the Third Age, the adventures can be played individually, or as part of a larger tale.
</p><p>Ancient evils wake as the Dark Lord stretches forth his hand. Over the course of these adventures, the heroes may thwart the spies and stratagems of the Enemy, explore long-lost wonders of the north, and travel far from hearth and home. Preserve the spark of hope, and one day these empty, wounded lands shall be healed! Fail, and all will be destroyed…
</p>
</blockquote>

## Reception {#Reception}

In a review for [Other Minds 27](/wiki/Other_Minds_27), José Enrique Vacas de la Rosa praised the book for its production quality and the gameplay provided, but had strong reservations regarding the severity of the threat the Hill of Fear posed to Eriador. He also noted that while the material for gaming was overall very detailed, many locations that appear in the adventures are only superficially described.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## In other languages {#In_other_languages}

For the Polish localization of *The One Ring*, a unique grey collector's edition was released by publisher Black Monk.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
