**Tumhalad** was a field in [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand) that lay between the [Narog River](/wiki/Narog "Narog") and its tributary, the [Ginglith](/wiki/Ginglith).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Here the army of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), led by [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) and [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth), fought the **[Battle of Tumhalad](/wiki/Battle_of_Tumhalad)**, a last desperate defence against an onslaught led by the [Dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung). The [Elves](/wiki/Elves) were overrun and [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth), King of Nargothrond, was slain. Túrin carried the dying [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor) from the battle before speeding back to the defence of Nargothrond.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
