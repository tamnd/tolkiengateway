**Tumhalad** was a field in [West Beleriand](../W/West_Beleriand.md) that lay between the [Narog River](../N/Narog.md) and its tributary, the [Ginglith](../G/Ginglith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Here the army of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md), led by [Túrin](Túrin.md) and [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md), fought the **[Battle of Tumhalad](../B/Battle_of_Tumhalad.md)**, a last desperate defence against an onslaught led by the [Dragon](../D/Dragons.md) [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md). The [Elves](../E/Elves.md) were overrun and [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md), King of Nargothrond, was slain. Túrin carried the dying [Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md) from the battle before speeding back to the defence of Nargothrond.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
