**Third Age 2856** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2856**) is the 2856th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2856 was also known as **S.R. 1256** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Belba Baggins](../B/Belba_Baggins.md), second child and first daughter of [Mungo Baggins](../M/Mungo_Baggins.md) and [Laura Grubb](../L/Laura_Grubb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Rosa Baggins](../R/Rosa_Baggins.md), first child and daughter of [Ponto Baggins](../P/Ponto_Baggins_I.md) and [Mimosa Bunce](../M/Mimosa_Bunce.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Donnamira Took](../D/Donnamira_Took.md), tenth child and third daughter of [Thain](Thain.md) [Gerontius Took](../G/Gerontius_Took.md) and [Adamanta Chubb](../A/Adamanta_Chubb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
