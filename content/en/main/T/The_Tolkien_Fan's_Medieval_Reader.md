<i><b>The Tolkien Fan&#39;s Medieval Reader</b></i> is a collection of medieval texts which were studied by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

Brought together in one volume for the first time, **The Tolkien Fan's Medieval Reader** presents many of the medieval texts that were important to Tolkien's professional work and to his fiction. Prose versions have been selected to introduce Tolkien's modern readers to such marvel-filled works as [Beowulf](<../B/Beowulf_(poem).md>), <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight.md" title="Sir Gawain and the Green Knight">Sir Gawain and the Green Knight</a>, and the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Prose_Edda&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Prose Edda (page does not exist)">Prose Edda of Snorri Sturluson</a> - three classics that every fantasy fan will enjoy on their own merits. Many will also appreciate seeing firsthand how these works, and others in this book - from Old English, Middle English, Old Norse, the Celtic languages and Finnish - served as inspiration for elements in Tolkien's great imaginary world of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) dragons, elves, dwarves, monsters, magical swords and heroes.
