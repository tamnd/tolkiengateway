**The Shire** is a video game made for a mainframe or mini-computer, probably the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PLATO_(computer_system)" title="wikipedia:PLATO (computer system)">Plato System</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is probably the first videogame ever made that is based generally on Tolkien's books.
