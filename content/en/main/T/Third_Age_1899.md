**Third Age 1899** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1899**) is the 1899th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1899 was also known as **S.R. 299** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Wainrider/Balchoth War](../W/Wainrider%252FBalchoth_War.md): Around that time [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md) revolt against the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- King [Calimehtar](<../C/Calimehtar_(King_of_Gondor).md>) defeats the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) on [Dagorlad](../D/Dagorlad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
