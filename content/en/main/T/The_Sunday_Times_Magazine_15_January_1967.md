<b><i>The Sunday Times Magazine</i> 15 January 1967</b> is an issue of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sunday_Times_Magazine" title="wikipedia:The Sunday Times Magazine">The Sunday Times Magazine</a></i>.

The article "The Hobbit Man" by Philip Norman includes extracts from an [interview with Tolkien](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") (pp. 34-6).

Another (similar but not identical) article based on the same interview was published on the same date in *[The New York Times Magazine](/wiki/The_New_York_Times_Magazine_15_January_1967 "The New York Times Magazine 15 January 1967")*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
