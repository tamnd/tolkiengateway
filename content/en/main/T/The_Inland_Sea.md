<i><b>The Inland Sea</b></i> was a supplement intended to be published for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)*. Mike Campbell and Luke Potter were commissioned during the 1990s to write the supplement. But as the publisher [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) lost the exploitation license to *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)* in September 1999, Campbell and Potter stopped working on the Inland Sea project around 1999/2000.

The manuscripts of MERP the Inland Sea (including several maps) were in the end published free of charge by [Chris Seeman](../C/Chris_Seeman.md) (from the fanzine [Other Hands](../O/Other_Hands.md)). They can be reached through the Yahoo Fan modules web site on Internet (now located at <a class="external text" href="http://groups.io/g/merp-fan-modules" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">MERP Fan Modules</a>). The present state of these manuscripts is that of a playable supplement.
