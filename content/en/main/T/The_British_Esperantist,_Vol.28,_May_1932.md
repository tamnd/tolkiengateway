<b><i>The British Esperantist</i>, Vol.28, May 1932</b> is an issue of the magazine *The British Esperantist*. The issue contains "[A Philologist on Esperanto](/wiki/A_Philologist_on_Esperanto)" (p. 182) — extracts from a letter by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) written to the secretary of the Education Committee of the British Esperanto Association.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
