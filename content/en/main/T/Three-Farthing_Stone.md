The **Three-Farthing Stone** was a stone by the side of the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) that marked the point where the borders of the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing), [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing) and [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing) of the Shire came together.<sup id="cite_ref-Part_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Part-1">[1]</a></sup> It was also said to mark a point near the centre of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") itself.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The stone stood about five miles south-east from [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater), and exactly fourteen miles west from [Frogmorton](/wiki/Frogmorton).

The fourth farthing, the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing), did not meet the others at the Three-Farthing Stone.<sup id="cite_ref-Part_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Part-1">[1]</a></sup> Instead, its borders met those of the Westfarthing and Eastfarthing about ten miles north of the Stone, though there is no record of a second three-farthing stone to mark this spot.

In late [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) stood near the stone and cast the remnants of the dust of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) into the air, sharing [Galadriel's gift](/wiki/Sam%27s_garden_box "Sam's garden box") with a [Shire](/wiki/Shire) suffering from the effects of [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey) and his men.

## Inspirations {#Inspirations}

Tolkien may have got this idea from the [Four Shire Stone](/wiki/Four_Shire_Stone).
