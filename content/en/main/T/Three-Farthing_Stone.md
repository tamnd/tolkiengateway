The **Three-Farthing Stone** was a stone by the side of the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) that marked the point where the borders of the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md), [Westfarthing](../W/Westfarthing.md) and [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md) of the Shire came together.<sup id="cite_ref-Part_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Part-1">[1]</a></sup> It was also said to mark a point near the centre of [the Shire](The_Shire.md) itself.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The stone stood about five miles south-east from [Bywater](../B/Bywater.md), and exactly fourteen miles west from [Frogmorton](../F/Frogmorton.md).

The fourth farthing, the [Northfarthing](../N/Northfarthing.md), did not meet the others at the Three-Farthing Stone.<sup id="cite_ref-Part_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Part-1">[1]</a></sup> Instead, its borders met those of the Westfarthing and Eastfarthing about ten miles north of the Stone, though there is no record of a second three-farthing stone to mark this spot.

In late [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md), [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) stood near the stone and cast the remnants of the dust of [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) into the air, sharing [Galadriel's gift](../S/Sam's_garden_box.md) with a [Shire](../S/Shire.md) suffering from the effects of [Sharkey](../S/Sharkey.md) and his men.

## Inspirations {#Inspirations}

Tolkien may have got this idea from the [Four Shire Stone](../F/Four_Shire_Stone.md).
