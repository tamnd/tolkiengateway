<i><b>The Hobbit Calendar 1976</b></i> was the official Tolkien calendar published by [George Allen &amp; Unwin](../G/George_Allen_and_Unwin.md) in [1975](../_/1975.md). It features art by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) based on *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)*.
