<i><b>The Hunt for Gollum</b></i> is the soundtrack to *[The Hunt for Gollum](/wiki/The_Hunt_for_Gollum)*. The soundtrack was composed by Adam Langston and Andrew Skrabutenas, based on music by Howard Shore and Clint Mansell. It was released for free on the internet.
