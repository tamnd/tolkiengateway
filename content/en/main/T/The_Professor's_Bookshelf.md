<i><b>The Professor&#39;s Bookshelf</b></i> is a collection of publications by the *Leaves of Gold Press* company. Their aim is to publish classic literary titles known to be enjoyed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), by authors who were the forefathers of modern fantasy fiction, and [inspired](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_inspirations "J.R.R. Tolkien's inspirations") Tolkien's works. There is the aim to include original illustrations by Tolkien's contemporary artists whom he admired.

Fantasy author Cecilia Dart-Thornton signs the introduction of each book.
