<i><b>The Professor&#39;s Bookshelf</b></i> is a collection of publications by the *Leaves of Gold Press* company. Their aim is to publish classic literary titles known to be enjoyed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), by authors who were the forefathers of modern fantasy fiction, and [inspired](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_inspirations.md) Tolkien's works. There is the aim to include original illustrations by Tolkien's contemporary artists whom he admired.

Fantasy author Cecilia Dart-Thornton signs the introduction of each book.
