<i><b>tum</b></i> means "valley"<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-1">[1]</a></sup> or "deep valley, under or among hills"<sup id="cite_ref-Etym_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etym-2">[2]</a></sup> in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (and [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, Noldorin *tum* derives from [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tumbu&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tumbu (page does not exist)">tumbu</a></i>, [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=TUB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TUB (page does not exist)">TUB</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Etym_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etym-2">[2]</a></sup>

- <i><a href="/wiki/Carn_D%C3%BBm" title="Carn Dûm">Carn <b>Dûm</b></a></i> = "Red Valley"

- <i><a href="/wiki/Tumladen" title="Tumladen"><b>Tum</b>laden</a></i> = "Wide Valley"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Tum_Gondregain" title="Tum Gondregain"><b>Tum</b> Gondregain</a></i> = "Stonewain Valley"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Tum_Orchorod" title="Tum Orchorod"><b>Tum</b> Orchorod</a></i> = "Vale of Tall Mountains"
