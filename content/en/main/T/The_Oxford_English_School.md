**The Oxford English School** is an essay written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) that was published in *The Oxford Magazine* on [29 May](/wiki/29_May) [1930](/wiki/1930). In it, he discusses how the English syllabus fails students, as well as his suggestions to fix it. He also talks about how "literature" and "language" are divided. It is difficult to find information about this particular essay, but it excerpts of it were reprinted in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Reader's Guide](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> released on 11 November 2006 for the UK, and 6 October 2006 for the US.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
