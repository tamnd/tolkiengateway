**TOL** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "stick out or up" or "stick up, stand up (stiff), raise the head".<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[tol](/wiki/Tol "Tol")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[tol](/wiki/Tol "Tol")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>

- ?[Telerin](/wiki/Telerin)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>/?[Quenya](/wiki/Quenya):<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> *[tolma](/wiki/Tolma "Tolma")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies") appears the root TOL<sup>2</sup>, from which derives:<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *tollo*
    - Quenya *tol*
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *[toll](/wiki/Toll "Toll")*

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root TOLO, yielding a variety of derivatives (among them [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) *tolome* "island" and *tolda* "hill with a flat top, town on a hill").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
