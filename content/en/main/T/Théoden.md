**Théoden** was the seventeenth [King of Rohan](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md), ruling for 39 years, from [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2980](Third_Age_2980.md) until his death. He was the last of the Second Line.<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Early life {#Early_life}

Théoden was the only son of [Thengel](Thengel.md), the heir of king [Fengel](../F/Fengel.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), and [Morwen of Lossarnach](../M/Morwen_of_Lossarnach.md), a descendant of a former [prince](../P/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of the fief of [Belfalas](../B/Belfalas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Linear_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Linear-4">[3]</a></sup> His father left Rohan when he came to manhood and lived long in Gondor. Théoden had been born in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and spent at least the first five years of his life there with his mother and father until his father was called back to Rohan after the death of Théoden's grandfather Fengel.

[Westron](../W/Westron.md),<sup id="cite_ref-DwarvesIntro_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DwarvesIntro-5">[4]</a></sup> the "speech of Gondor", was used in his father's house in Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1069</sup> Théoden spoke Westron as his native language,<sup id="cite_ref-DwarvesIntro_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DwarvesIntro-5">[4]</a></sup> and also spoke [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md) and [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> It is unknown whether Sindarin and Rohanese were also his native languages or acquired languages. His mother Morwen was a "lady of Gondor of high Númenórean descent".<sup id="cite_ref-Linear_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Linear-4">[3]</a></sup> In Gondor all men of high lineage and all those who learned how to read and write used Sindarin, even as a daily language, and it is said that in some families Sindarin was the native language.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

Théoden loved his sister [Théodwyn](Théodwyn.md) dearly. After she and her husband both died, he adopted her children [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) and [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) as his own. He had a son, [Théodred](Théodred.md), whose mother [Elfhild](../E/Elfhild.md) died in childbirth.<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup>

Théoden became king after the death of his father in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2980](Third_Age_2980.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3014](Third_Age_3014.md), Théoden's health began to decline, caused by the spells of [Saruman the White](../S/Saruman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup> Other factors may have contributed to his worsening condition, such as older age, as he was then 66. Even so, Rohirrim often lived past 80. His decline could also have been hastened by subtle poisons administered by [Gríma](../G/Gríma.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup> Gríma (or *Wormtongue* as most others in the [Mark](../R/Riddermark.md) called him) was secretly in the service of Saruman.

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

As the war approached, Théoden was increasingly misled by his chief adviser Gríma. In the last years before the War of the Ring, Théoden let his rule slip out of his hands completely, and Gríma became increasingly powerful. Rohan was troubled again by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md), who operated under the will of Saruman, who ruled from [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md).

His only son, Théodred, had only recently been slain by Saruman's forces during the [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](../F/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md). Théoden ordered Éomer to no longer hunt Orcs that crossed into Rohan, but Éomer did not heed his uncle’s instructions.<sup id="cite_ref-appB_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appB-9">[8]</a></sup> On Gríma's orders, Éomer was arrested and imprisoned for his disobedience.<sup id="cite_ref-Hall_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hall-3">[2]</a></sup>

When [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) appeared before him on [2 March](../_/2_March.md), the [Wizard](../W/Wizards.md) healed the king. He then restored his nephew, who would become his heir, took up his [sword](../H/Herugrim.md), and led the [Riders of Rohan](../R/Rohirrim.md) to the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md), against Saruman.<sup id="cite_ref-appB_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appB-9">[8]</a></sup>

On their way, they received news that [the border was lost](../S/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md) and he retreated into [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md) where they were attacked by Saruman's forces on [3 March](../_/3_March.md).<sup id="cite_ref-appB_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appB-9">[8]</a></sup> Théoden won the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md) and after this he became known as **Théoden Ednew**, the Renewed,<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup> because he had thrown off the yoke of Saruman.

After the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md), Théoden visited [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md), where he witnessed [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) deposing Saruman from [their order](../W/Wizards.md). During the parley before the tower of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md), [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) attempted to deceive Théoden with false offers of peace and friendship. When Théoden rejected him, [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) mockingly referred to him as a "**Horsemaster**" as an insult<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>. On their way back to Rohan, they stopped by the [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md), where he noticed that the lonely [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of their company, [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) felt out of place, and offered to ride with him for the rest of the journey. The hobbit was delighted, as he felt useless among the Riders and offered Théoden his sword in service of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>

On [6 March](../_/6_March.md) Théoden and the Riders left Hornburg for [Harrowdale](../H/Harrowdale.md) taking a safe path to Edoras, and soon after [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) left the Rohirrim's company for [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md).<sup id="cite_ref-appB_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appB-9">[8]</a></sup> Near the outer hills of Rohan, Théoden dismissed Éomer's urge not to go to the war, and with the remaining Riders he rode to the Hold of Dunharrow where his people took shelter at [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md).<sup id="cite_ref-muster_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-muster-13">[12]</a></sup>

### Fulfilling the Oath of Eorl {#Fulfilling_the_Oath_of_Eorl}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/P/Paula_DiSante_-_The_Red_Arrow.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/80/Paula_DiSante_-_The_Red_Arrow.jpg/300px-Paula_DiSante_-_The_Red_Arrow.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="399" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/80/Paula_DiSante_-_The_Red_Arrow.jpg/450px-Paula_DiSante_-_The_Red_Arrow.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/8/80/Paula_DiSante_-_The_Red_Arrow.jpg 2x" data-file-width="600" data-file-height="797"/></a><figcaption><a href="../P/Paula_DiSante.md" title="Paula DiSante">Paula DiSante</a> - <i>The Red Arrow</i></figcaption></figure>

After reuniting with Éowyn and while resting, a [Gondorian](../G/Gondorian.md) named [Hirgon](../H/Hirgon.md) entered his tent, bringing the [Red Arrow](../R/Red_Arrow.md) from [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) II. Théoden responded to him that 6000 Riders would reach [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) in the week. Indeed they set off the [next morning](../D/Dawnless_Day.md); before leaving he ordered his squire, Meriadoc, to stay behind at Edoras.<sup id="cite_ref-muster_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-muster-13">[12]</a></sup>

On their way to Minas Tirith, Théoden's forces were alerted to a host of Orcs upon the road from the city moving towards them. A leader of the [Woses](../W/Woses.md), [Ghân-buri-Ghân](../G/Ghân-buri-Ghân.md), offered to lead the Rohirrim by secret paths through the [Drúadan Forest](../D/Drúadan_Forest.md) so they could avoid the Orcs. Taking the forgotten road down [Stonewain Valley](../S/Stonewain_Valley.md) the riders of Rohan were able to come to the [Pelennor Fields](../P/Pelennor_Fields.md) undetected.

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Abe_Papakhian_-_The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/ca/Abe_Papakhian_-_The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.jpg/300px-Abe_Papakhian_-_The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="224" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/ca/Abe_Papakhian_-_The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.jpg/450px-Abe_Papakhian_-_The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/ca/Abe_Papakhian_-_The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.jpg/600px-Abe_Papakhian_-_The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2400" data-file-height="1789"/></a><figcaption><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Abe_Papakhian&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Abe Papakhian (page does not exist)">Abe Papakhian</a> - <i>The Ride of the Rohirrim</i></figcaption></figure>

To his dismay, they found Hirgon dead and understood that the Gondorians were unaware of their arrival; Minas Tirith [was being destroyed](../S/Siege_of_Gondor.md). Théoden was reinvigorated by a sudden great flash of light from the city. He ordered to charge as the [Darkness](../S/Storm_of_Mordor.md) was fading. He led the Rohirrim to the aid of Gondor at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md). In that battle he challenged a chieftain of the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md) who led their cavalry and had a standard with a "black serpent upon scarlet", and unhorsed him and hewed down his standard-bearer.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Angus_McBride_-_Eowyn_against_Witch-king.gif.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/bf/Angus_McBride_-_Eowyn_against_Witch-king.gif/300px-Angus_McBride_-_Eowyn_against_Witch-king.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="308" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/bf/Angus_McBride_-_Eowyn_against_Witch-king.gif/450px-Angus_McBride_-_Eowyn_against_Witch-king.gif 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/b/bf/Angus_McBride_-_Eowyn_against_Witch-king.gif 2x" data-file-width="509" data-file-height="523"/></a><figcaption><a href="../A/Angus_McBride.md" title="Angus McBride">Angus McBride</a> - <i>Eowyn against Witch-king</i></figcaption></figure>

The [Lord of the Nazgûl](../W/Witch-king.md) attacked him, and he was mortally wounded when his horse [Snowmane](../S/Snowmane.md) fell upon him after being frightened by the Ringwraith's [Fell beast](../F/Fell_beasts.md).

He was immediately avenged by Éowyn and the [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md), both of whom had ridden to war in secret. He claimed to Merry at his death on the field that he was satisfied, for he had felled the Black Serpent.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

The body of Théoden the Renowned remained in the [Hallows](../H/Hallows.md) of Minas Tirith<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup> while [Snowmane](../S/Snowmane.md) was buried where they fell.

His nephew Éomer succeeded him as King of Rohan. In [July](../J/July.md) he returned to Minas Tirith and brought Théoden's body back to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md). On [10 August](../_/10_August.md), the funeral of Théoden took place. [Gléowine](../G/Gléowine.md) composed a poem for him and other Kings of the line of [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md).<sup id="cite_ref-partings_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-partings-17">[16]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

Théoden had long, braided<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[17]</a></sup> white hair.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[18]</a></sup> He had a long<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[19]</a></sup> beard,<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[20]</a></sup> blue eyes<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[21]</a></sup> and long<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[22]</a></sup> and wrinkled hands.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[23]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Théoden* is derived from the [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) word <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%C3%BEeoden" title="wiktionary:þeoden">þeoden</a></i>, which means "chief of a people", "prince" or "king".<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[24]</a></sup> The word *þeoden* contains the element *þeod*, which means "people".<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[25]</a></sup>

His epithet *Ednew* is derived from the Old English word *edneowe*, which means "renewed".<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[26]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[27]</a></sup>

In Tolkien's fictional etymology, the name *Théoden* is an Old English translation of the original [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md) *Tûrac*, an old word for King.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-29">[28]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In one of Tolkien's early drafts, Théoden also had a daughter by the name of [Idis](../I/Idis.md), but she was eventually removed when her character was eclipsed by that of Éowyn.

<b>1955: <a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1955 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="../V/Valentine_Dyall.md" title="Valentine Dyall">Valentine Dyall</a> voiced the part of Théoden.</dd></dl>

<b>1978: <a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The voice of Théoden was provided by <a href="../P/Philip_Stone.md" title="Philip Stone">Philip Stone</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>1979: <a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1979 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1979 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="../E/Erik_Bauersfeld.md" title="Erik Bauersfeld">Erik Bauersfeld</a> provided the voice of Théoden.</dd></dl>

<b>1980: <a href="The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)"><i>The Return of the King</i> (1980 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Théoden is voiced by <a href="../D/Don_Messick.md" title="Don Messick">Don Messick</a>, though he speaks very little. His death is narrated by <a href="../J/John_Huston.md" title="John Huston">John Huston</a> as Gandalf.</dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Theoden played by <a href="../J/Jack_May.md" title="Jack May">Jack May</a> appears much earlier in the narrative than in the book, allowing Gandalf to borrow a horse on his escape from Isengard.</dd></dl>

<b>1992: <a href="../D/Der_Herr_der_Ringe_(1992_German_radio_series).md" title="Der Herr der Ringe (1992 German radio series)"><i>Der Herr der Ringe</i> (1992 German radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Théoden is played by Werner Rundshagen.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>This film deviates from Tolkien&#39;s story by having Théoden (played by <a href="../B/Bernard_Hill.md" title="Bernard Hill">Bernard Hill</a>) actually possessed by Saruman rather than simply deceived by Gríma. He then goes to <a href="../H/Helm&#39;s_Deep.md" title="Helm&#39;s Deep">Helm&#39;s Deep</a> to take his people to safety rather than to make a stand against the enemy.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Théoden at first refuses to come to the aid of Gondor saying that Gondor did not come to the aid of Rohan at the <a href="../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md" title="Battle of the Hornburg">Battle of the Hornburg</a>. His death is placed after the coming of the <a href="../H/Haradrim.md" title="Haradrim">Haradrim</a>, and is depicted differently; he is not crushed by <a href="../S/Snowmane.md" title="Snowmane">Snowmane</a>, but wounded by the <a href="../F/Fell_beasts.md" title="Fell beasts">fell beast</a>. In general, his personality is changed from a &#34;kindly old man&#34; to that of a callous and somewhat obstinate character, though he has several tender moments at the grave of his son and near Éowyn.</dd></dl>

<b>2002-2003: <a class="mw-redirect" href="../P/Pán_prsteňov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series).md" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The voice of Théoden is provided by Ladislav Chudík.</dd></dl>

<b>2004: <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Théoden is a hero in the Rohan faction. He is voiced by <a href="../P/Phil_Proctor.md" title="Phil Proctor">Phil Proctor</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Théoden is a hero in the Men faction. He is voiced by <a href="../P/Phil_Proctor.md" title="Phil Proctor">Phil Proctor</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2009: <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Conquest.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: Conquest">The Lord of the Rings: Conquest</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Théoden is voiced by <a href="../B/Brian_George.md" title="Brian George">Brian George</a>.</dd></dl>
