**Tarciryan** was the son of [King](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) [Siriondil](<../S/Siriondil_(King_of_Gondor).md>) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), the younger brother of [Tarannon Falastur](Tarannon_Falastur.md), and the father of [Eärnil I](../E/Eärnil_I.md). When his elder brother died without an heir, his son ascended the throne.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Tarciryan was presumably born after the year [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [654](Third_Age_654.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md), the year of his elder brother [Tarannon Falastur](Tarannon_Falastur.md)'s birth.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In the year [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [736](Third_Age_736.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Tarciryan became the father of [Eärnil I](../E/Eärnil_I.md).<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup> In the year [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [830](Third_Age_830.md), [Siriondil](<../S/Siriondil_(King_of_Gondor).md>),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Tarciryan's father,<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup> died and his brother Tarannon became the [King of Gondor](../K/King_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> When Tarannon died without an heir in the year [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [913](Third_Age_913.md), the throne passed directly to Tarciryan's son, Eärnil I.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tarciryan* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but as [Paul Strack](../P/Paul_Strack.md) explains, it may be a compound of *[tar](Tar.md)* ("high") "and a reduced form of *[ciryando](../C/Cirya.md)*" ("sailor").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The significance of the final *n* is unclear.
