**Tarciryan** was the son of [King](/wiki/King_of_Gondor "King of Gondor") [Siriondil](</wiki/Siriondil_(King_of_Gondor)> "Siriondil (King of Gondor)") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), the younger brother of [Tarannon Falastur](/wiki/Tarannon_Falastur), and the father of [Eärnil I](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_I). When his elder brother died without an heir, his son ascended the throne.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Tarciryan was presumably born after the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [654](/wiki/Third_Age_654 "Third Age 654") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the year of his elder brother [Tarannon Falastur](/wiki/Tarannon_Falastur)'s birth.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [736](/wiki/Third_Age_736 "Third Age 736"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Tarciryan became the father of [Eärnil I](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_I).<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup> In the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [830](/wiki/Third_Age_830 "Third Age 830"), [Siriondil](</wiki/Siriondil_(King_of_Gondor)> "Siriondil (King of Gondor)"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Tarciryan's father,<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup> died and his brother Tarannon became the [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> When Tarannon died without an heir in the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [913](/wiki/Third_Age_913 "Third Age 913"), the throne passed directly to Tarciryan's son, Eärnil I.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tarciryan* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but as [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack) explains, it may be a compound of *[tar](/wiki/Tar "Tar")* ("high") "and a reduced form of *[ciryando](/wiki/Cirya "Cirya")*" ("sailor").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The significance of the final *n* is unclear.
