The **Third Age** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- **[Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age)**, the era between the first fall of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and his final destruction.
    - [Any year within the Third Age](/wiki/Category:Third_Age_years "Category:Third Age years").

## Books {#Books}

- *[Of the Rings of Power and the Third Age](/wiki/Of_the_Rings_of_Power_and_the_Third_Age)*, the last chapter of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.
- *[The End of the Third Age](/wiki/The_End_of_the_Third_Age)*, the short version of *[Sauron Defeated](/wiki/Sauron_Defeated)* that formed the fourth, and last, part of *[The History of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
- The End of the Third Age, published as Book VI in some editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- *[Battles of the Third Age](/wiki/Battles_of_the_Third_Age)*, an expansion pack to *[War of the Ring (board game)](</wiki/War_of_the_Ring_(board_game)>)*.
- *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Third_Age)*, a [2004](/wiki/2004) game by [Electronic Arts](/wiki/Electronic_Arts).
- *[Third Age of the Sun](/wiki/Third_Age_of_the_Sun)*, a [2005](/wiki/2005) album by [Battlelore](/wiki/Battlelore).
- *[Third Age: Total War](/wiki/Third_Age:_Total_War)*, a [2009](/wiki/2009) strategy [game mod](/wiki/Category:Modifications "Category:Modifications").
- *[The Last Days (of the Third Age of Middle-earth)](</wiki/The_Last_Days_(of_the_Third_Age_of_Middle-earth)>)*, a [2011](/wiki/2011) action RPG [game mod](/wiki/Category:Modifications "Category:Modifications").

## People and groups {#People_and_groups}

- [The Third Age (Modding Community)](</wiki/The_Third_Age_(Modding_Community)>), a large modding community that hosts mods for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth Anthology](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_Anthology)*
