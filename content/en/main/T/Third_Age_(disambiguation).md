The **Third Age** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- **[Third Age](Third_Age.md)**, the era between the first fall of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) and his final destruction.
    - [Any year within the Third Age](../../category/T/Third_Age_years.md).

## Books {#Books}

- *[Of the Rings of Power and the Third Age](../O/Of_the_Rings_of_Power_and_the_Third_Age.md)*, the last chapter of *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)*.
- *[The End of the Third Age](The_End_of_the_Third_Age.md)*, the short version of *[Sauron Defeated](../S/Sauron_Defeated.md)* that formed the fourth, and last, part of *[The History of The Lord of the Rings](The_History_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.
- The End of the Third Age, published as Book VI in some editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- *[Battles of the Third Age](../B/Battles_of_the_Third_Age.md)*, an expansion pack to *[War of the Ring (board game)](<../W/War_of_the_Ring_(board_game)~25771.md>)*.
- *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Third_Age.md)*, a [2004](../_/2004.md) game by [Electronic Arts](../E/Electronic_Arts.md).
- *[Third Age of the Sun](Third_Age_of_the_Sun.md)*, a [2005](../_/2005.md) album by [Battlelore](../B/Battlelore.md).
- *[Third Age: Total War](Third_Age%253A_Total_War.md)*, a [2009](../_/2009.md) strategy [game mod](../../category/M/Modifications.md).
- *[The Last Days (of the Third Age of Middle-earth)](<The_Last_Days_(of_the_Third_Age_of_Middle-earth).md>)*, a [2011](../_/2011.md) action RPG [game mod](../../category/M/Modifications.md).

## People and groups {#People_and_groups}

- [The Third Age (Modding Community)](<The_Third_Age_(Modding_Community).md>), a large modding community that hosts mods for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth Anthology](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_Anthology.md)*
