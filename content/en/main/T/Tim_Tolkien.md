**Tim Tolkien** (born [13 October](/wiki/13_October) [1962](/wiki/1962)) is [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s great-nephew, the son of [Julian Tolkien](/wiki/Julian_Tolkien), [Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien)'s son. He has a brother named Nick.

Tim Tolkien is a successful sculptor.
