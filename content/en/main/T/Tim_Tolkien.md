**Tim Tolkien** (born [13 October](../_/13_October.md) [1962](../_/1962.md)) is [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s great-nephew, the son of [Julian Tolkien](../J/Julian_Tolkien.md), [Hilary Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md)'s son. He has a brother named Nick.

Tim Tolkien is a successful sculptor.
