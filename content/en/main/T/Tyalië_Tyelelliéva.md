<i><b>Tyalië Tyelelliéva</b></i> is a journal on Tolkienian linguistics edited by [Lisa Star](../L/Lisa_Star.md) since [1994](../_/1994.md). It publishes poems and other texts in the Elvish languages as well as articles about the languages and alphabets of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

It appeared three to four times per year at first, but after November [1995](../_/1995.md), issues appeared more irregularly, roughly twice per year, before the current hiatus in publication began in December [2001](../_/2001.md). How long this hiatus will last is unknown - no-one seems to be able to contact her<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> - but the official website received an update in March [2009](../_/2009.md).
