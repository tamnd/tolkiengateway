<i><b>Tyalië Tyelelliéva</b></i> is a journal on Tolkienian linguistics edited by [Lisa Star](/wiki/Lisa_Star) since [1994](/wiki/1994). It publishes poems and other texts in the Elvish languages as well as articles about the languages and alphabets of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

It appeared three to four times per year at first, but after November [1995](/wiki/1995), issues appeared more irregularly, roughly twice per year, before the current hiatus in publication began in December [2001](/wiki/2001). How long this hiatus will last is unknown - no-one seems to be able to contact her<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> - but the official website received an update in March [2009](/wiki/2009).
