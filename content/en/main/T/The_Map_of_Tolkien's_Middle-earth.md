<i><b>The Map of Tolkien&#39;s Middle-earth</b></i> is a [1995](../_/1995.md) map-book by [Brian Sibley](../B/Brian_Sibley.md) and [John Howe](../J/John_Howe.md), covering [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Written by Brian Sibley, this slipcase features Tolkien&#39;s maps of &#34;The Hobbit&#34;, Beleriand and Middle-earth. The maps, presented with individual books and wallets show Tolkien&#39;s mythical lands in detail - they are also bound with fewer folds, making them suitable for portfolios or framing.
</p>
</blockquote>
