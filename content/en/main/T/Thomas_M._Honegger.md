**Thomas M. Honegger**, born in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z%C3%BCrich" title="wikipedia:Zürich">Zurich</a>, is a professor of [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) Languages at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena" title="wikipedia:University of Jena">Friedrich Schiller University</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jena" title="wikipedia:Jena">Jena</a>, Germany. He is an editor and contributor to [Walking Tree Publishers](../W/Walking_Tree_Publishers.md)' *[Cormarë](../C/Cormarë_Series.md)* series and a member of the board of advisors of *[Hither Shore](<../H/Hither_Shore_(journal).md>)*.
