**Thomas M. Honegger**, born in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z%C3%BCrich" title="wikipedia:Zürich">Zurich</a>, is a professor of [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) Languages at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena" title="wikipedia:University of Jena">Friedrich Schiller University</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jena" title="wikipedia:Jena">Jena</a>, Germany. He is an editor and contributor to [Walking Tree Publishers](/wiki/Walking_Tree_Publishers)' *[Cormarë](/wiki/Cormar%C3%AB_Series "Cormarë Series")* series and a member of the board of advisors of *[Hither Shore](</wiki/Hither_Shore_(journal)> "Hither Shore (journal)")*.
