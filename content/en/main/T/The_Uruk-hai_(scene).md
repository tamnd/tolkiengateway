**The Uruk-hai** is the third scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* and the fourth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition).md>). This scene was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

A band of [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) run across the plains of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). They are carrying [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), who were [taken prisoner](<The_Breaking_of_the_Fellowship_(scene).md>) by the Uruk-hai at [Parth Galen](../P/Parth_Galen.md). Pippin tries to rouse Merry who is unconscious. The Uruk-hai are stopped by a company of [orcs](../O/Orcs.md) who urge them to quickly deliver the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) to [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md).

Unable to wake Merry, Pippin tells the Uruk-hai that his friend is sick; they proceed to give Merry "some medicine" consisting of a brown liquid. This rouses Merry who tells Pippin that he is fine and that he is not hurt.

One of the Uruk-hai sniffs the air and tells the others he can smell "Man-flesh". They realise that their trail has been picked up and they quickly hurry on after stopping. Pippin understands that it is likely [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) who is tracking them, and so he discreetly tears the leaf-shaped brooch tying his [Elven cloak](../E/Elven_cloaks.md) using his mouth and drops it on the floor, hoping to leave a token for Aragorn to know he is on the right trail.
