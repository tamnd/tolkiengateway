**The Uruk-hai** is the third scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and the fourth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)"). This scene was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

A band of [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) run across the plains of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). They are carrying [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), who were [taken prisoner](</wiki/The_Breaking_of_the_Fellowship_(scene)> "The Breaking of the Fellowship (scene)") by the Uruk-hai at [Parth Galen](/wiki/Parth_Galen). Pippin tries to rouse Merry who is unconscious. The Uruk-hai are stopped by a company of [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") who urge them to quickly deliver the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) to [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman).

Unable to wake Merry, Pippin tells the Uruk-hai that his friend is sick; they proceed to give Merry "some medicine" consisting of a brown liquid. This rouses Merry who tells Pippin that he is fine and that he is not hurt.

One of the Uruk-hai sniffs the air and tells the others he can smell "Man-flesh". They realise that their trail has been picked up and they quickly hurry on after stopping. Pippin understands that it is likely [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) who is tracking them, and so he discreetly tears the leaf-shaped brooch tying his [Elven cloak](/wiki/Elven_cloaks "Elven cloaks") using his mouth and drops it on the floor, hoping to leave a token for Aragorn to know he is on the right trail.
