**Tom Loback** ([1949](../_/1949.md) — [5 March](../_/5_March.md) [2015](../_/2015.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) was a linguist and artist from New York. His artwork has been published in *[Beyond Bree](../B/Beyond_Bree.md)*, *[Vinyar Tengwar](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar.md)*, *[Mythlore](../M/Mythlore.md)*, *[Parma Eldalamberon](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon.md)*, <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Little_Gwaihir&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Little Gwaihir (page does not exist)">Little Gwaihir</a></i> and several other journals.<sup id="cite_ref-ELF_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ELF-2">[2]</a></sup> He also had work in the *[Halls of the Elven-king](../H/Halls_of_the_Elven-king.md)*, a supplement in the [MERP](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md) series.

His relevant artwork features [tengwar](Tengwar.md)-inscriptions in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ELF_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ELF-2">[2]</a></sup>
