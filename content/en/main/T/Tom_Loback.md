**Tom Loback** ([1949](/wiki/1949) — [5 March](/wiki/5_March) [2015](/wiki/2015)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) was a linguist and artist from New York. His artwork has been published in *[Beyond Bree](/wiki/Beyond_Bree)*, *[Vinyar Tengwar](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar)*, *[Mythlore](/wiki/Mythlore)*, *[Parma Eldalamberon](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon)*, <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Little_Gwaihir&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Little Gwaihir (page does not exist)">Little Gwaihir</a></i> and several other journals.<sup id="cite_ref-ELF_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ELF-2">[2]</a></sup> He also had work in the *[Halls of the Elven-king](/wiki/Halls_of_the_Elven-king)*, a supplement in the [MERP](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing "Middle-earth Role Playing") series.

His relevant artwork features [tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar "Tengwar")-inscriptions in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-ELF_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ELF-2">[2]</a></sup>
