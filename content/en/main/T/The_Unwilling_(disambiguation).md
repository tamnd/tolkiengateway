**The Unwilling** may refer to:

- The [Avari](/wiki/Avari), a branch of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) that refused to embark on the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey).
- [Tar-Atanamir](/wiki/Tar-Atanamir), the thirteenth [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor) and the first King to refuse to surrender his life willingly.
