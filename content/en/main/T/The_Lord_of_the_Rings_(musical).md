**The Lord of the Rings Musical** has been the largest, and most expensive, effort to adapt [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* into a musical. It was first shown in Toronto but to mixed reviews. It closed early and re-opened in London with a new script although reviews were still far from perfect.

## Productions {#Productions}

### Toronto {#Toronto}

The Toronto musical was three-and-a-half hours long, had 65 actors, and was located at the Princess of Wales Theatre in Toronto, Canada. The show cost approximately $26.9 million and ran from [March 23rd](../_/23_March.md) (press opening), 2006 to [September 3rd](../_/3_September.md), 2006, closing at a loss.

#### Cast {#Cast}

- [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) - Brent Carver
- [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) - Michael Therriault
- [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) - Rebecca Jackson Mendoza

### London {#London}

After the musical moved to London, the stage was rebuilt, the script rewritten, and shortened to three hours. Previews began at the Theatre Royal, Drury Lane on [May 9th](../_/9_May.md), [2007](../_/2007.md) and the premiere was on [June 19th](../_/19_June.md), 2007. The musical had the same creative team but had minor changes in the cast, with only 50 actors in this version. The reported total cost was approximately $49 million, making it one of the most expensive musicals outside of Las Vegas.

### Newbury {#Newbury}

In 2023 the musical was revived at the Watermill Theatre in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newbury,_Berkshire" title="wikipedia:Newbury, Berkshire">Newbury</a>, West Berkshire. It ran for 12 weeks, from Tuesday [25th July](../_/25_July.md) until Sunday [15th October](../_/15_October.md) [2023](../_/2023.md). Due to the small size of the theatre (seating just 220 people) the production was set across both the Watermill auditorium and its gardens *"immersing audiences in the magical landscape of Tolkien featuring an ensemble cast and large-scale puppets"*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The production received favourable reviews, with particular praise for the casts performances.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) - Matthew Bugg
- [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) - Peter Marinker

### Chicago {#Chicago}

In 2024, the Watermill production was announced to be making it's US premiere at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Shakespeare_Theater" title="wikipedia:Chicago Shakespeare Theater">Chicago Shakespeare Theater</a>, running from [19 July](../_/19_July.md) to [1 September](../_/1_September.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It was also confirmed that the production will go on an international tour, with further dates and venues to be announced in June.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
