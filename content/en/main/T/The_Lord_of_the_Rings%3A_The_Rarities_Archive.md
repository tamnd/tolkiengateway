<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: The Rarities Archive</b></i> is an album by [Howard Shore](../H/Howard_Shore.md) comprising of mock-up, alternate, and previously unreleased versions of tracks from the score of *[The Lord of the Rings film series](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was compiled for and bundled with the book *[The Music of the Lord of the Rings Films](The_Music_of_the_Lord_of_the_Rings_Films.md)* by author [Doug Adams](../D/Doug_Adams.md).
