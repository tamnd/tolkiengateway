<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: The Rarities Archive</b></i> is an album by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore) comprising of mock-up, alternate, and previously unreleased versions of tracks from the score of *[The Lord of the Rings film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was compiled for and bundled with the book *[The Music of the Lord of the Rings Films](/wiki/The_Music_of_the_Lord_of_the_Rings_Films)* by author [Doug Adams](/wiki/Doug_Adams).
