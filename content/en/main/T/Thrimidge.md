**Thrimidge**, often written as **Thrimich**, is the fifth month of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), lying between [Astron](/wiki/Astron) and [Forelithe](/wiki/Forelithe), and approximately corresponding to modern [22 April](/wiki/22_April) through [21 May](/wiki/21_May).<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a [modernization](/wiki/Hobbitish "Hobbitish") of the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name for May, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%C3%BErimilce" title="wiktionary:þrimilce">þrimilce</a></i> meaning "Three milk-givings".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> As such, an archaic form of the name was **Thrimilch**.<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
