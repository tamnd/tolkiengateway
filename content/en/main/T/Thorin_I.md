**Thorin I** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [2035](/wiki/Third_Age_2035 "Third Age 2035") – [2289](/wiki/Third_Age_2289 "Third Age 2289"), died aged 254) was the [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Durin%27s_Folk "Kings of Durin's Folk") of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) for 99 years, succeeding his father, [Thráin I](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_I), on his death in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2190](/wiki/Third_Age_2190 "Third Age 2190"). Thorin was the heir of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a> being the great-grandson of [Durin VI](/wiki/Durin_VI).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Thorin was born after his father Thráin had led the [Dwarves of Moria](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Moria) away from [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") and had founded the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) in [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"). Thorin himself became [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2190](/wiki/Third_Age_2190 "Third Age 2190"). However, he heard that the majority of the remnants of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) were now gathering in the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains). In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2210](/wiki/Third_Age_2210 "Third Age 2210") he decided to leave the Lonely Mountain and reunited its people with [their brethren](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Grey_Mountains "Dwarves of the Grey Mountains") in the Grey Mountains, which were rich and largely unexplored.

Thorin I died in [2289](/wiki/Third_Age_2289 "Third Age 2289"), at the age of 254, and was succeeded by his son [Glóin](</wiki/Gl%C3%B3in_(King_of_Durin%27s_Folk)> "Glóin (King of Durin's Folk)"). His sixth great-grandson, [Thorin II Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin"), was the much famed leader of the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor), which reclaimed the Lonely Mountain from the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Thorin's name is one from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. It means "Bold".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
