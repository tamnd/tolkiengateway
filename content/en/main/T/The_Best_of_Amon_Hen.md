<i><b>The Best of Amon Hen</b></i> is a edited compilation of articles from [The Tolkien Society](The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s bulletin *[Amon Hen](<../A/Amon_Hen_(journal).md>)*. There have been two volumes of *The Best of Amon Hen* covering issues [131](../A/Amon_Hen_131.md)-[150](../A/Amon_Hen_150.md) (Part 1) and issues [91](../A/Amon_Hen_91.md)-[120](../A/Amon_Hen_120.md) (Part 2). *The Best of Amon Hen* has been published by [The Tolkien Society](The_Tolkien_Society.md) as volumes 10 and 13 in the [Peter Roe Memorial Fund](../P/Peter_Roe_Memorial_Fund.md) series.
