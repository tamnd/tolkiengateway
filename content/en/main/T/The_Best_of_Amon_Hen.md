<i><b>The Best of Amon Hen</b></i> is a edited compilation of articles from [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s bulletin *[Amon Hen](</wiki/Amon_Hen_(journal)> "Amon Hen (journal)")*. There have been two volumes of *The Best of Amon Hen* covering issues [131](/wiki/Amon_Hen_131 "Amon Hen 131")-[150](/wiki/Amon_Hen_150 "Amon Hen 150") (Part 1) and issues [91](/wiki/Amon_Hen_91 "Amon Hen 91")-[120](/wiki/Amon_Hen_120 "Amon Hen 120") (Part 2). *The Best of Amon Hen* has been published by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) as volumes 10 and 13 in the [Peter Roe Memorial Fund](/wiki/Peter_Roe_Memorial_Fund) series.
