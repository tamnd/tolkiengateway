**Third Age 1332** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1332**) is the 1332nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Possible death of [Vidumavi](../V/Vidumavi.md), wife of [Valacar](../V/Valacar.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and mother of [Eldacar](<../E/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
