The <b>First Map of <i>The Lord of the Rings</i></b> refers to the earliest map of the western regions of Middle-earth that was drawn by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The map has squares of 2 centimetre side and a map scale where 2 centimetres are the equivalent of 100 miles.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was redrawn and commented on by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) in *[The Treason of Isengard](The_Treason_of_Isengard.md)* (1989; chapter 15).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The First Map of the Lord of the Rings covers a larger area of Middle-earth than the [General Map of Middle-earth](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md) or [The West of Middle-earth at the End of the Third Age](The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age.md) map. It also shows the lands to the north of the [Icebay of Forochel](../I/Icebay_of_Forochel.md) and the island of [Tol Fuin](Tol_Fuin.md) west of the island of [Himling](../H/Himling.md) off the coast of [Forlindon](../F/Forlindon.md) in the [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md) ocean. The sea west of the [Icebay of Forochel](../I/Icebay_of_Forochel.md) was labeled as *[North Sea](../N/North_Sea.md)* in pencil.

## Early concepts {#Early_concepts}

A label "Forodwaith (Northerland)" is written in the area between the river [Baranduin](../B/Baranduin.md) and the river [Gwathló](../G/Gwathló.md) and extends from south of the [Sarn Ford](../S/Sarn_Ford.md) to beyond the northern end of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md). There is no label "Eriador" on the map. However, J.R.R. Tolkien added "(or Eriador)" after "Forodwaith" on The 1943 Map of The Lord of the Rings.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> This and the absence of the name Eriador from earlier manuscripts suggest that Forodwaith was initially used as a name of the region that was later called Eriador.

A label "[Westfold](../W/Westfold.md)" was written to the south of a label "[Dunland](../D/Dunland.md)" west of the southern end of the Misty Mountains and north of the river [Isen](../I/Isen.md), but was struck through<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and reinserted west of [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md), north of the northern foothills of the ["Black Mountains"](../W/White_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> This and the use of the name Westfold for the land of the Westfolders, the invaders of Rohan in earlier drafts of *[The Two Towers](The_Two_Towers.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> suggest that Westfold was initially used as a name of the region that was later called Dunland.
