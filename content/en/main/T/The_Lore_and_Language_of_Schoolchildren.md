<i><b>The Lore and Language of Schoolchildren</b></i> is a scholarly work on childhood culture, derived from folklorists <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iona_and_Peter_Opie" title="wikipedia:Iona and Peter Opie">Iona and Peter Opie</a>'s collecting of rhymes, games, riddles in the United Kingdom during the 1950s. The discussion of '*Fains or fainites*' (p. 151) includes quotations from a [1951 letter](../L/Letter_to_Iona_and_Peter_Opie.md) by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
