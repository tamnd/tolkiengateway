<b><i>Tolkien Studies</i>: Volume 4</b>, published in [2007](/wiki/2007), is the fourth volume of the annual review *[Tolkien Studies](</wiki/Tolkien_Studies_(journal)> "Tolkien Studies (journal)")*.

This issue reprints J.R.R. Tolkien's essay "[The Name 'Nodens'](/wiki/The_Name_%27Nodens%27)", first published in [1932](/wiki/1932). <br>Thomas Honegger's article includes extracts from unpublished drafts of *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth](/wiki/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth)*. <br>The front cover reproduces Tolkien's [handwritten notes](/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Ae_Adar_Nin_2.png "File:J.R.R. Tolkien - Ae Adar Nin 2.png") about *[Ae Adar Nín](/wiki/Ae_Adar_N%C3%ADn)* on the back of a postcard.<sup id="cite_ref-TB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-1">[1]</a></sup>

    - *The Power of the Ring: The Spiritual Vision Behind the Lord of the Rings*, by Stratford Caldecott, reviewed by Mike Foster
    - *Reading "The Lord of the Rings": New Writings on Tolkien's Classic*, edited by Robert Eaglestone, reviewed by Patrick Curry
    - *[The Ring of Words](/wiki/The_Ring_of_Words): Tolkien and the Oxford English Dictionary*, by Peter Gilliver, Jeremy Marshall, and Edmund Weiner, reviewed by Douglas A. Anderson
    - *The Rise of Tolkienian Fantasy*, by Jared Lobdell, reviewed by Matthew T. Dickerson
    - *[The Roots of Tolkien's Middle Earth](/wiki/The_Roots_of_Tolkien%27s_Middle_Earth)*, by Robert S. Blackham, reviewed by Tom Shippey
    - *A Tolkien Mathomium: A Collection of Articles about J.R.R. Tolkien and His Legendarium*, by Mark T. Hooker, reviewed by Sandra Ballif Straubhaar
