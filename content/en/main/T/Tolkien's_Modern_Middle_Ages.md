**Tolkien's Modern Middle Ages** is a collection of essays edited by [Jane Chance](/wiki/Jane_Chance) and Alfred K. Siewers.

## From the Publisher {#From_the_Publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> delved into the Middle Ages to create a critique of the modern world in his fantasy, yet did so in a form of modernist literature with postmodern implications and huge commercial success. These essays examine that paradox and its significance in understanding the intersection between traditionalist and counter-culture criticisms of the modern. The approach helps to explain the popularity of his works, the way in which they continue to be brought into dialogue with twenty-first century issues, and their contested literary significance in the academy.
</p>
</blockquote>
