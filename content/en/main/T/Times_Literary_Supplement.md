The <i><b>Times Literary Supplement</b></i> is a literary journal.

## Notable issues {#Notable_issues}

### 1954 {#1954}

Upon the publication of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, in the article "Heroic Endeavour" of [27 August](/wiki/27_August) [1954](/wiki/1954) (p. 541), an anonymous reviewer thought that Tolkien managed to balance between the "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_Programme" title="wikipedia:Light Programme">Light Programme</a>" and the "Domain of the Nibelungs", seeming like the former intruding into the latter. Considering the book an allegory of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_War" title="wikipedia:Cold War">Cold War</a>, some criticism was that the Good is not defined, as the protagonists function unter "the warrior's code of courage".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

With the publication of *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*, a reviewer wrote the article "The Epic of Westernesse" for the [17 December](/wiki/17_December) issue (p. 817). According to the review, the "allegory" of this 'prose epic in praise of courage', was becoming plainer. The reviewer considered convincing and delightful the described imaginary places; but also criticised the lack of some realistic elements of the [Secondary World](/wiki/Secondary_World), such as jobs and [money](/wiki/Money "Money"), the role of women, the [Sea](/wiki/Sea), while nowhere is explained how evil is [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### 1955 {#1955}

*[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)* was reviewed in the article "The Saga of Middle Earth" on [25 November](/wiki/25_November) [1955](/wiki/1955) (p. 704). The reviewer considered the work memorable, but not readable more than once; he likened the whole story to a shiny and magnificent edifice, stretching beyond view, with "further spacious halls as yet unvisited", but also criticised how Good and Evil were distibguished.

In the issue of [9 December](/wiki/9_December) (p. 743), responding to a reader's letter, the reviewer wrote that all the book was about the good and the bad, both brave, only trying to kill each other and that "Morally there seems nothing to choose between them".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

### 2012 {#2012}

The issue of [14 September](/wiki/14_September) [2012](/wiki/2012) (published online [two days earlier](/wiki/12_September "12 September")) includes the article "A vision of Middle-earth: Mary Fairburn — Tolkien illustrator" by Paul Tankard, about the artist [Mary Fairburn](/wiki/Mary_Fairburn), her illustrations inspired by [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and friendship with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). Several quotations from formerly [unpublished letters by Tolkien](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") are provided. <span></span>
