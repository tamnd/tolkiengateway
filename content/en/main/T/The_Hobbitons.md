**The Hobbitons** was the name given to members of [Unquendor, the Dutch Tolkien Society](../U/Unquendor.md) who released the CD *[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Songs from Middle-earth* in [1996](../_/1996.md). The CD contains 16 tracks of songs and poems from *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)*, *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md)*, and *[Bilbo's Last Song](../B/Bilbo's_Last_Song.md)*. *J.R.R. Tolkien's Songs from Middle-earth* was produced with permission of the [Tolkien Estate](Tolkien_Estate.md) on the condition that it would not be sold commercially.<sup id="cite_ref-ourCD_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ourCD-1">[1]</a></sup>
