**The Hobbitons** was the name given to members of [Unquendor, the Dutch Tolkien Society](/wiki/Unquendor "Unquendor") who released the CD *[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s Songs from Middle-earth* in [1996](/wiki/1996). The CD contains 16 tracks of songs and poems from *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*, and *[Bilbo's Last Song](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Last_Song)*. *J.R.R. Tolkien's Songs from Middle-earth* was produced with permission of the [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate) on the condition that it would not be sold commercially.<sup id="cite_ref-ourCD_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ourCD-1">[1]</a></sup>
