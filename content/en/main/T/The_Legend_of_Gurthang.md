<i><b>The Legend of Gurthang</b></i> is a [fan film](/wiki/Category:Fan_films "Category:Fan films") directed, written, and produced by Jared Belt. It was released online.

## Plot {#Plot}

From the official website:

> ​"The Legend of Gurthang" is an original story set in [Tolkien's](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") Middle Earth, inspired by and loosely based on [The Children of Hurin](/wiki/The_Children_of_Hurin), by J.R.R. Tolkien and [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien). Set in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) at the height of the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance), this story follows a man from the kingdom of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), on his lone mission to the far island of [Tol Morwen](/wiki/Tol_Morwen), to find and retrieve [Gurthang](/wiki/Gurthang), a legendary sword of great power once owned by Turin, son of Hurin.
