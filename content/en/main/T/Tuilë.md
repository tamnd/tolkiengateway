**Tuilë** was the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for the season of spring; the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) called it [Ethuil](/wiki/Ethuil). In the [Reckoning of Rivendell](/wiki/Reckoning_of_Rivendell) it was the first season of the year, and corresponded more or less to the 54 days between [7 April](/wiki/7_April) and [30 May](/wiki/30_May) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
