"**The earliest map**" refers to a map of the world as conceived in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the earliest version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"). The map as reproduced in *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One)* (page 81) was redrawn by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) from a sketch by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> being "no more than a quick scribble, in soft pencil ... in many features difficult or impossible to interpret".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Features redrawn by Christopher include (listed roughly from north to south):

- Utumna (later [Utumno](/wiki/Utumno))
- [Ringil](</wiki/Ringil_(tower)> "Ringil (tower)")
- [Mountains of Valinor](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri "Pelóri") (marked *j*)
- [domain of Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos "Halls of Mandos") (marked *c*)
- [Hisilome](/wiki/H%C3%ADsil%C3%B3m%C3%AB "Hísilómë") (marked *g*; [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) form of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum)<sup id="cite_ref-Tumblr_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tumblr-3">[3]</a></sup>)
- The [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains) (marked f*)*
- A river and an unmarked dot next to it
- [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor") (marked *b*)
- [Magic Isles](/wiki/Enchanted_Isles "Enchanted Isles") (marked *k*)
- [Kôr](/wiki/K%C3%B4r) (marked *d*)
- [Valmar](/wiki/Valmar) (marked *a*)
- [Eruman](/wiki/Araman "Araman")/[Arvalin](/wiki/Avathar "Avathar") (marked *h*; later [Avathar](/wiki/Avathar)<sup id="cite_ref-Tumblr_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tumblr-3">[3]</a></sup>)
- [Helkar](/wiki/Helkar)
- [Helkar](/wiki/Sea_of_Helcar "Sea of Helcar")?
