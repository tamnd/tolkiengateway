**Third Age 2953** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2953**) is the 2953rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2953 was also known as **S.R. 1353** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Dark Tower](../D/Dark_Tower.md) rises again.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Turgon](<Turgon_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Last meeting of the [White Council](../W/White_Council.md). They debate the [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md). [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) feigns that he has discovered that [the One Ring](The_One_Ring.md) has passed down [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) to [the Sea](../B/Belegaer.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ta_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Saruman withdraws to [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md), which he takes as his own, and fortifies it. Being jealous and afraid of [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) he sets spies to watch all his movements; and notes his interest in [the Shire](The_Shire.md). He soon begins to keep agents in [Bree](../B/Bree.md) and the [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ta_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Ecthelion II](../E/Ecthelion_II.md), son of Turgon, becomes the twenty-fifth Ruling Steward.<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Fengel](../F/Fengel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-5">[5]</a></sup>
- [Thengel](Thengel.md), son of Fengel, becomes the sixteenth King of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-5">[5]</a></sup>
