The **Tower Hills** ([Sind.](../S/Sindarin.md) *Emyn Beraid*) were a range of hills at the [west end](../W/Westmarch.md) of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md). They lay east of [Mithlond](../G/Grey_Havens.md), the Grey Havens of [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md), and west of the [Far Downs](../F/Far_Downs.md), of old the border of [the Shire](The_Shire.md).

High upon the Tower Hills stood three tall [Elven](../E/Elves.md) towers, the [White Towers](../W/White_Towers.md), the highest of which was called *[Elostirion](../E/Elostirion.md)*, which held a *[Palantír](../P/Palantíri.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> before Cirdan removed it to the [White Ship](../W/White_Ship.md).

## History {#History}

Although [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md)'s kingdom did not have clear bounds south of the [Lune](../L/Lune.md), the [Elves of Lindon](../E/Elves_of_Lindon.md) maintained an outpost there.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [600](../S/Second_Age_600.md), when the first ship of the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) sailed up the [Gulf of Lune](../G/Gulf_of_Lune.md) there was a meeting between the men from the sea and their [distant kinsmen](../M/Middle_Men.md) in the Hills.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The White Towers upon the Tower Hills were built before the end of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) by [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) for his friend [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

During the [Third Age](Third_Age.md), [bands of Elves](../W/Wandering_Companies.md) would go in pilgrimage to Elostirion in order to have a glimpse of the West through the [Elostirion-stone](../E/Elostirion-stone.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The Stone remained in its tower until the end of the Age; it was then taken back into the West.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [31](../F/Fourth_Age_31.md), the [Westmarch](../W/Westmarch.md) was added to [the Shire](The_Shire.md), making the Tower Hills the new western border of the land. Three years later [Thain](Thain.md) [Peregrin I](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) made [Fastred of Greenholm](../F/Fastred_of_Greenholm.md) the first Warden of Westmarch. He and his wife [Elanor](../E/Elanor_Gardner.md), daughter of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), moved to the Tower Hills and founded the town of [Undertowers](../U/Undertowers.md) on the eastern slopes.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Emyn Beraid* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Tower Hills", from *[emyn](../A/Amon.md)* ("hills") and *[beraid](../B/Barad.md)* ("towers").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to J.R.R. Tolkien's [Unfinished index](../U/Unfinished_index.md) for *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* the towers on the Hills were reputed to have been built by the elves, but they were actually built by Elendil.<sup id="cite_ref-RC1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC1-9">[9]</a></sup>
