The **Tower Hills** ([Sind.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Emyn Beraid*) were a range of hills at the [west end](/wiki/Westmarch "Westmarch") of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador). They lay east of [Mithlond](/wiki/Grey_Havens "Grey Havens"), the Grey Havens of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), and west of the [Far Downs](/wiki/Far_Downs), of old the border of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

High upon the Tower Hills stood three tall [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") towers, the [White Towers](/wiki/White_Towers), the highest of which was called *[Elostirion](/wiki/Elostirion)*, which held a *[Palantír](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")*,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> before Cirdan removed it to the [White Ship](/wiki/White_Ship).

## History {#History}

Although [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad)'s kingdom did not have clear bounds south of the [Lune](/wiki/Lune), the [Elves of Lindon](/wiki/Elves_of_Lindon) maintained an outpost there.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [600](/wiki/Second_Age_600 "Second Age 600"), when the first ship of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) sailed up the [Gulf of Lune](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune) there was a meeting between the men from the sea and their [distant kinsmen](/wiki/Middle_Men "Middle Men") in the Hills.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The White Towers upon the Tower Hills were built before the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) by [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) for his friend [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

During the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), [bands of Elves](/wiki/Wandering_Companies "Wandering Companies") would go in pilgrimage to Elostirion in order to have a glimpse of the West through the [Elostirion-stone](/wiki/Elostirion-stone).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The Stone remained in its tower until the end of the Age; it was then taken back into the West.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [31](/wiki/Fourth_Age_31 "Fourth Age 31"), the [Westmarch](/wiki/Westmarch) was added to [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), making the Tower Hills the new western border of the land. Three years later [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Peregrin I](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") made [Fastred of Greenholm](/wiki/Fastred_of_Greenholm) the first Warden of Westmarch. He and his wife [Elanor](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner "Elanor Gardner"), daughter of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), moved to the Tower Hills and founded the town of [Undertowers](/wiki/Undertowers) on the eastern slopes.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Emyn Beraid* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Tower Hills", from *[emyn](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* ("hills") and *[beraid](/wiki/Barad "Barad")* ("towers").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to J.R.R. Tolkien's [Unfinished index](/wiki/Unfinished_index) for *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* the towers on the Hills were reputed to have been built by the elves, but they were actually built by Elendil.<sup id="cite_ref-RC1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC1-9">[9]</a></sup>
