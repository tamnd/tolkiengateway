The following is a list of all portrayals found in the [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering) [Tales of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth "The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth") set, including the base set, the Commander product, reprints of previous Magic cards and reprints that were given a different name unique to this expansion.

Each card is organized by the primary focus of the artwork. If it (and the card) are describing only an individual, it is organized as portraying them. If the artwork (and along with context of the card) is depicting a specific scene, than it is organized under the specific event.
