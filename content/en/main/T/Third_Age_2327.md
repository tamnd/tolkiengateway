**Third Age 2327** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2327**) is the 2327th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2327 was also known as **S.R. 727** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The fifth [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md), [Aragorn I](../A/Aragorn_I.md), is killed by [wolves](../W/Wolves.md) in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Aragorn's son, [Araglas](../A/Araglas.md), becomes the sixth Chieftain of the Dúnedain.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
