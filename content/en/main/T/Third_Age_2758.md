**Third Age 2758** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2758**) is the 2758th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2758 was also known as **S.R. 1158** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Wulf%27s_rebellion&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Wulf&#39;s rebellion (page does not exist)">Wulf&#39;s rebellion</a>: [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) attacked from west and east and overrun.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Corsair wars](../C/Corsair_wars.md): [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) attacked by fleets of the [Corsairs](../C/Corsairs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Helm](../H/Helm.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) takes refuge in [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Wulf](../W/Wulf.md), son of [Freca](../F/Freca.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> seizes [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Winter of 2758-2759 {#Winter_of_2758-2759}

- The [Long Winter](../L/Long_Winter.md) follows, starting in [November](../N/November.md). Great suffering and loss of life in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) comes to the aid of the [Shire-folk](../S/Shire-folk.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
