<i><b>The Individuated Hobbit: Jung, Tolkien, and the Archetypes of Middle-earth</b></i> is a critical study of the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) by [Timothy R. O'Neill](Timothy_R._O'Neill.md). It was written from a Jungian perspective, with particular emphasis put on archetypes.
