<i><b>The Individuated Hobbit: Jung, Tolkien, and the Archetypes of Middle-earth</b></i> is a critical study of the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) by [Timothy R. O'Neill](/wiki/Timothy_R._O%27Neill). It was written from a Jungian perspective, with particular emphasis put on archetypes.
