**Tomatoes** were supposedly a plant known to the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Tomatoes were referenced in the first edition of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> but [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) changed this to "pickles" in the third edition (1966).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The most usual explanation for the change is that the American plant-life would not fit in his setting of ancient [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). However, as pointed out by [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff), it may have been simply that Tolkien felt that it was too early in the year for tomatoes and substituted a preserved food instead.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i></b>:

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee" title="Samwise Gamgee">Samwise Gamgee</a>, <a href="/wiki/Peregrin_Took" title="Peregrin Took">Peregrin Took</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Meriadoc Brandybuck</a> are all cooking tomatoes, along with sausages and bacon, over their campfire at <a href="/wiki/Weathertop" title="Weathertop">Weathertop</a> when <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a> wakes up. In a panic, Frodo stamps his foot on the fire to put it out before the <a href="/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl" title="Nazgûl">Black Riders</a> see it, and Pippin complains that Frodo got ash all over his tomatoes.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Denethor" title="Denethor">Denethor</a> asks <a href="/wiki/Peregrin_Took" title="Peregrin Took">Peregrin</a> to sing to him while he feasts. He eats tomatoes as <a href="/wiki/Faramir" title="Faramir">Faramir</a> charges on <a href="/wiki/Osgiliath" title="Osgiliath">Osgiliath</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i></b>:

<dl><dd>Tomatoes are among the food stocked in the Bag End pantry.</dd></dl>
