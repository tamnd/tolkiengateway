**Third Age 3** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3**) is the 3rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Ohtar](../O/Ohtar.md) brings the [shards of Narsil](../S/Shards_of_Narsil.md) to [Imladris](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup>
