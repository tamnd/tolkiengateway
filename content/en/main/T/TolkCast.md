**TolkCast** is a German-language podcast produced by the [German Tolkien Society](/wiki/German_Tolkien_Society). The first episode aired on [22 September](/wiki/22_September) [2018](/wiki/2018), making it the oldest Tolkien-related German podcast.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is co-hosted by Annika Röttinger, Tobias M. Eckrich, Jonas Reinhard, Johny, and Marie-Noelle. Since October [2020](/wiki/2020), two episodes per month are released.
