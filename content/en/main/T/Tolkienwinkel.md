**Tolkienwinkel**, also operating in English as **Tolkien Shop**, is a store in the Dutch city <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden" title="wikipedia:Leiden">Leiden</a> selling [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") related books and merchandise. It has been run by [René van Rossenberg](/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_van_Rossenberg) since [1986](/wiki/1986), who also showcases his own collection as a sort of museum.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Tolkienwinkel originated as a mail order service by Netherlands Tolkien Society [Unquendor](/wiki/Unquendor) to its members. In the 1990s however, the commercial nature of Tolkienwinkel led to it being split from Unquendor. In 1996 Van Rossenberg began selling items online, and the actual physical store opened in 2004.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In recent decades the Tolkienwinkel has sold interesting collections: letters and books signed by J.R.R. Tolkien, [Joy Hill](/wiki/Joy_Hill)'s (Tolkien's private secretary) library, as well as many [Cor Blok](/wiki/Cor_Blok), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sergei_Iukhimov&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sergei Iukhimov (page does not exist)">Sergei Iukhimov</a>, [Roger Garland](/wiki/Roger_Garland) and other paintings.
