**Theobald Bolger** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Theobald was the son of [Gundabald Bolger](../G/Gundabald_Bolger.md) and [Salvia Brandybuck](../S/Salvia_Brandybuck.md). He married [Nina Lightfoot](../N/Nina_Lightfoot.md) and had one son, [Wilibald](../W/Wilibald_Bolger.md), who was born in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1304](Third_Age_2904.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theudebald" title="wikipedia:Theudebald">Theobald</a></i> contains *theud* "people" and *bald* "bold".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
