**Theobald Bolger** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Theobald was the son of [Gundabald Bolger](/wiki/Gundabald_Bolger) and [Salvia Brandybuck](/wiki/Salvia_Brandybuck). He married [Nina Lightfoot](/wiki/Nina_Lightfoot) and had one son, [Wilibald](/wiki/Wilibald_Bolger "Wilibald Bolger"), who was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1304](/wiki/Third_Age_2904 "Third Age 2904").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theudebald" title="wikipedia:Theudebald">Theobald</a></i> contains *theud* "people" and *bald* "bold".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
