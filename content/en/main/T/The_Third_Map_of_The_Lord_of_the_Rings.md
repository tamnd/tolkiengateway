**The Third Map of The Lord of the Rings** is the last general small-scale map that was drawn by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) during the writing of [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The Third Map of The Lord of the Rings is not included in [The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth) series of books, but some of its contents are described in [Sauron Defeated](/wiki/Sauron_Defeated) and in [The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth). Based on the statements that the distance due East from the [Morgai](/wiki/Morgai) to [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom) is 80 miles on The Third Map<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and that the [Little Lune](/wiki/Little_Lune) was first marked on the third and last of J.R.R. Tolkien's maps on which Christopher Tolkien's map that was published with the Lord of the Rings was closely based,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> it is possible that the map titled *MIDDLE-EARTH*, previously available in the [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate) website's <a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkienestate.com/painting/maps" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">maps section</a>, is The Third Map of The Lord of the Rings, because it contains those two features and its features are very close to the [General Map of Middle-earth](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth). Contour lines are used for the mountains and hills on that map that was drawn by J.R.R. Tolkien. That map uses map-squares of 2 centimetres side and a scale of 2 centimetres (one square) for 100 miles.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
