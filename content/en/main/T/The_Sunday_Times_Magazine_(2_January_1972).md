<i><b>The Sunday Times Magazine</b></i> **(2 January 1972)** is an issue of the magazine <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sunday_Times_Magazine" title="wikipedia:The Sunday Times Magazine"><i>The Sunday Times Magazine</i></a>.

The article "The Lord of the Legends" (pp. 24–28) by [Bill Cater](/wiki/William_Cater "William Cater") includes extracts from an interview with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). The article was reprinted with a title of "More and More Readers Are Getting the J.R.R. Tolkien Habit" in <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Los_Angeles_Times_Calendar_(9_April_1972)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Los Angeles Times Calendar (9 April 1972) (page does not exist)"><i>The Los Angeles Times Calendar</i> (9 April 1972)</a> magazine.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
