The **Tree of Tongues** is a sketch by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) found in the chapter "[The Lhammas](The_Lhammas.md)" of *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings.md)*. It shows the relationship between the multitude of languages described in that section of the book. Three similar sketches entitled *Table of the Descent of Tongues* are included with the *Tengwesta Qenderinwa* in [Parma Eldalamberon 18](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_18.md), with some dating later than those in [The Lhammas](The_Lhammas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The later revisions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) show a considerably different and simplified history of the languages. For example, we know from *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)* that the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) languages do not descend from [Valarin](../V/Valarin.md), taught to them by [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md), but from [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md), spoken before Oromë found the [Elves](../E/Elves.md).
