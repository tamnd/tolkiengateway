The **Tree of Tongues** is a sketch by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) found in the chapter "[The Lhammas](/wiki/The_Lhammas)" of *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings)*. It shows the relationship between the multitude of languages described in that section of the book. Three similar sketches entitled *Table of the Descent of Tongues* are included with the *Tengwesta Qenderinwa* in [Parma Eldalamberon 18](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_18), with some dating later than those in [The Lhammas](/wiki/The_Lhammas).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The later revisions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") show a considerably different and simplified history of the languages. For example, we know from *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* that the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) languages do not descend from [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin), taught to them by [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), but from [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian), spoken before Oromë found the [Elves](/wiki/Elves).
