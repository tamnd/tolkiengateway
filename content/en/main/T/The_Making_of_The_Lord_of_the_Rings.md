<i><b>The Making of The Lord of the Rings</b></i> is an 8-page booklet by [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin), published in [August](/wiki/August) [1992](/wiki/1992) by W.A.Meeuws.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Originally, only 250 copies were made for the Centenary celebrations at Keble College, [Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford"). 12 copies contained Italian hand-made marble paper covered boards and a linen spine.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In [2007](/wiki/2007), 100 more copies were printed.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
