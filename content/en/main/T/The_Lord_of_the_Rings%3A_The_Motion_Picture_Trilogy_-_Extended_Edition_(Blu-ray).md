**The Lord of the Rings: The Motion Picture Trilogy - Extended Edition** is a Blu-ray pack of [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") released on June 28, 2011 by [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema). It includes the extended editions of the films in high-definition and nine DVDs of bonus features. *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring")* also received a remaster from the 2K digital files, supervised by director [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) and cinematographer [Andrew Lesnie](/wiki/Andrew_Lesnie). It received a perfect user rating on multiple sites including blu-ray.com.
