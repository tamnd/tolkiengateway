**The Lord of the Rings: The Motion Picture Trilogy - Extended Edition** is a Blu-ray pack of [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) released on June 28, 2011 by [New Line Cinema](../N/New_Line_Cinema.md). It includes the extended editions of the films in high-definition and nine DVDs of bonus features. *[The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* also received a remaster from the 2K digital files, supervised by director [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) and cinematographer [Andrew Lesnie](../A/Andrew_Lesnie.md). It received a perfect user rating on multiple sites including blu-ray.com.
