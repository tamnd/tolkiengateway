<i><b>The Story of Kullervo</b></i> is a collection of three texts by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), including a prose story of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kullervo" title="wikipedia:Kullervo">Kullervo</a>, an ill-fated character in the Finnish epic *[Kalevala](/wiki/Kalevala)*, originally compiled by [Elias Lönnrot](/wiki/Elias_L%C3%B6nnrot). The texts were first published in an article in the [*Tolkien Studies* vol. 7](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_7 "Tolkien Studies: Volume 7") in [2010](/wiki/2010), together with commentaries and notes by [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger). These were revised and enlarged, and published as a separate book in [2015](/wiki/2015).

These texts were written by Tolkien when he was an undergraduate at Exeter College, Oxford, from [1914](/wiki/1914) to [1915](/wiki/1915). It was an unsettled period for him and this is thought to be reflected in the story's dark subject matter.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The story is regarded significant in the development of Tolkien’s [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") as it provided the basis for [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_Turambar), a tragic hero of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* and *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*.

## Pre-publication history {#Pre-publication_history}

In a [1914](/wiki/1914) letter to [Edith Bratt](/wiki/Edith_Bratt), Tolkien mentions that he is "trying to turn one of the stories \[of the *Kalevala*\] — which is really a very great story and most tragic — into a short story".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter) notes that *"'The Story of Kullervo', was never finished, but proved to be the germ of the story of Túrin Turambar in The Silmarillion"*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The manuscript remained unpublished for many years, but was finally published in [2010](/wiki/2010) in *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 7](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_7)*, edited and with commentaries by [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger), who has also edited *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)* and *[Tolkien On Fairy-stories](/wiki/Tolkien_On_Fairy-stories)*.

In [2013](/wiki/2013), Eduardo Oliveira Ferreira presented an academic project to The [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate), entitled "The Story of Kullervo", with contributions from Verlyn Flieger, [John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth), [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond), [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) and [Shaun Gunner](/wiki/Shaun_Gunner). The English and Portuguese bilingual book was approved by The Tolkien Estate on the condition that it be limited to 100 copies with the final text being subject to the Estate's scrutiny. However, when *The Story of Kullervo* was confirmed by [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) publishers, Oliveira Ferreira's project was cancelled.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

</p>
