**Tar-Vanimeldë** was the daughter of [Tar-Telemmaitë](Tar-Telemmaitë.md) and the sixteenth Ruler of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md); the third and last [Ruling Queen](../R/Ruling_Queen_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Vanimelde_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Vanimelde-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Tar-Vanimeldë gave little thought to the practicalities of government, preferring music and dancing. The day-to-day affairs of state she left to her husband [Herucalmo](../H/Herucalmo.md), who was of noble descent himself (of the same degree from [Tar-Atanamir](Tar-Atanamir.md)), and who effectively ruled Númenor while his wife held the [Sceptre](../S/Sceptre.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Vanimelde_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Vanimelde-1">[1]</a></sup>

When she died, her son [Alcarin](Tar-Alcarin.md) should have taken up the Sceptre, but Herucalmo refused to give up his power. Instead, though he had no legal claim to the throne, he took the name [Tar-Anducal](Tar-Anducal.md) and ruled as King for the next twenty years. After Herucalmo's death, the kingship passed back to its rightful heir, his son, who became [Tar-Alcarin](Tar-Alcarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Vanimelde_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Vanimelde-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Vanimeldë* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests that it may mean "elven-fair" as a feminised form of *[vanimelda](../V/Vanimelda.md)* ("beautiful and beloved", "elven-fair").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Like all the rulers of Númenor who took their royal names in Quenya, *Vanimeldë* added the prefix *tar-* ("high") to her name when she received the [Sceptre](../S/Sceptre_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
