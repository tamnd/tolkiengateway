**The Etymologies** is the third part of *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings)*. In his preface to that volume, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) describes *The Etymologies* as "a kind of historical dictionary", and associates the work with the years [1937](/wiki/1937)-[1938](/wiki/1938), prior to [his father](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s work on *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Etymologies_folio_93.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/8/8a/Etymologies_folio_93.png/175px-Etymologies_folio_93.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="175" height="218" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/8/8a/Etymologies_folio_93.png/263px-Etymologies_folio_93.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/8/8a/Etymologies_folio_93.png/350px-Etymologies_folio_93.png 2x" data-file-width="412" data-file-height="514"/></a><figcaption><i>Etymologies</i> folio 93</figcaption></figure>

In *The Etymologies*, Tolkien provides a series of [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) linguistic root syllables, or "bases". He then shows how each base evolved into the vocabularies of approximately twelve variations of the Elvish languages, primarily [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) and [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin). Although the derived words were not necessarily in their final forms, as Tolkien's invented languages continued to evolve throughout his life, Christopher Tolkien stated that this document "*enormously increases the known vocabularies of the Elvish tongues*".

## Addenda and corrigenda {#Addenda_and_corrigenda}

In [2003](/wiki/2003) and [2004](/wiki/2004), [*Vinyar Tengwar* issues 45](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_45 "Vinyar Tengwar 45") and [46](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_46 "Vinyar Tengwar 46") provided addenda and corrigenda to the original published text of *The Etymologies*, with previously unknown entries and roots. In [2007](/wiki/2007), *[Parma Eldalamberon](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon)* issue [17](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_17 "Parma Eldalamberon 17") provided even more entries.
