**The Etymologies** is the third part of *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings.md)*. In his preface to that volume, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) describes *The Etymologies* as "a kind of historical dictionary", and associates the work with the years [1937](../_/1937.md)-[1938](../_/1938.md), prior to [his father](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s work on *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/E/Etymologies_folio_93.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/8a/Etymologies_folio_93.png/175px-Etymologies_folio_93.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="175" height="218" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/8a/Etymologies_folio_93.png/263px-Etymologies_folio_93.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/8a/Etymologies_folio_93.png/350px-Etymologies_folio_93.png 2x" data-file-width="412" data-file-height="514"/></a><figcaption><i>Etymologies</i> folio 93</figcaption></figure>

In *The Etymologies*, Tolkien provides a series of [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) linguistic root syllables, or "bases". He then shows how each base evolved into the vocabularies of approximately twelve variations of the Elvish languages, primarily [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) and [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md). Although the derived words were not necessarily in their final forms, as Tolkien's invented languages continued to evolve throughout his life, Christopher Tolkien stated that this document "*enormously increases the known vocabularies of the Elvish tongues*".

## Addenda and corrigenda {#Addenda_and_corrigenda}

In [2003](../_/2003.md) and [2004](../_/2004.md), [*Vinyar Tengwar* issues 45](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_45.md) and [46](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_46.md) provided addenda and corrigenda to the original published text of *The Etymologies*, with previously unknown entries and roots. In [2007](../_/2007.md), *[Parma Eldalamberon](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon.md)* issue [17](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_17.md) provided even more entries.
