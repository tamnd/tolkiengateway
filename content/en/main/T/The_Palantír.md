**The Palantír** is the eleventh chapter of the first book in *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*.

## Summary {#Summary}

As [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) retreated from [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), the wizard carried [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") with him on [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax), while [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) carried [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"). Merry and Gandalf chatted. They rode late into the night and then stopped to camp. Pippin asked Merry whether Gandalf seemed different now that he had come back from the dead, and Merry replied that the wizard seemed both happier and more serious.

Merry was sleepy and tired of Pippin’s questioning, and he soon fell asleep. But Pippin, unable to sleep, was tormented by curiosity about the spherical stone that [Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma") had thrown out of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc). Pippin sneaked over to the sleeping Gandalf and snatched the stone. Pippin looked into it, unable to avert his gaze as the stone showed him [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), who questioned him. Gandalf awakened, angered at Pippin for taking the stone. After questioning him, Gandalf learned that the stone cast from Orthanc was one of the seven *[palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")*. This had allowed [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) to communicate with Sauron. During Pippin's interaction with Sauron through the *palantír*, he managed not to reveal any important information about his company's quest. Sauron thought that Pippin was the hobbit with [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") imprisoned at Isengard, so he ordered Pippin to tell Saruman that the Ring would soon be retrieved, and Saruman could not have it.

Expecting Sauron's messengers to come to Isengard soon, the host made plans to quickly leave. While discussing this, a winged [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) flew overhead to Orthanc, the first time that any of the Nine had been allowed to go west of the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) after the loss of their horses and shapes at the [Ford of Bruinen](/wiki/Ford_of_Bruinen). This prompted Gandalf to immediate action: he commanded everyone to leave the area as rapidly as possible, and then he mounted [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax) with Pippin and started riding to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## Composition {#Composition}

Tolkien wrote this chapter, along with the five final chapters of Book III were writen as a whole (not in consequence) during his spare time in summer and autumn [1942](/wiki/1942).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The [rhyme of lore](/wiki/Rhymes_of_Lore "Rhymes of Lore") was already in Tolkien's mind for some time before even inventing the Palantir into the story.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

By that time, Tolkien thought that the book needed six more chapters to be completed, which he had already outlined; however he entered a long hiatus and wouldn't resume before the Spring [1944](/wiki/1944), when he made a clean copy and "polished" the chapter.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
