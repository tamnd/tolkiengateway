**Third Age 1250** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1250**) is the 1250th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Regent](../R/Regent_of_Gondor.md) [Rómendacil](../R/Rómendacil_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) sends his son, [Valacar](../V/Valacar.md), to dwell with King [Vidugavia](../V/Vidugavia.md) of [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) as an ambassador in order to understand the language, manners and policies of the [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>
