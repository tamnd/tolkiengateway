<i><b>The Return of the King: The Best of White Dwarf Magazine</b></i> is a collection of articles, originally published in various issues of *[White Dwarf](/wiki/White_Dwarf)*, concerned with [Games Workshop](/wiki/Games_Workshop)'s *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game)*.

## From the back {#From_the_back}

This compilation is a collection of the best articles from the pages of White Dwarf magazine and forms an invaluable resource for anyone involved in The Lord of The Rings battle game. Inside you will find rules, scenarios, painting &amp; modelling advice and battle reports.
