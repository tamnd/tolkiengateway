**The White** was a title apparently used to denote the head of the [Wizards](/wiki/Wizards) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), originally borne by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> but claimed by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) after his return.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

This title was also given (with a more literal meaning) to [Celebdil](/wiki/Celebdil), the snow-capped mountain above [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") where [Durin's Tower](/wiki/Durin%27s_Tower) was built.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
