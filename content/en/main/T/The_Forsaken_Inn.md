The **Forsaken Inn** was an inn on the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) where it approached the old borders of the [Bree-land](../B/Bree-land.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> According to [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md), it was "a day's journey east of [Bree](../B/Bree.md)" and he doubted "if the Road has ever been measured in miles beyond the *Forsaken Inn*".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is possible that it was abandoned or ruined by [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2941](Third_Age_2941.md) as it is said that [Thorin and Company](Thorin_and_Company.md) on the quest to [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) found a deserted building where they camped.<sup id="cite_ref-bridge_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bridge-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Around [1960](../_/1960.md) [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) embarked on a detailed revision of *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)*, with a goal of reconciling it with *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In the retelling, the sketchy details of the trip from [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) were filled in and made to fit the geography of the later story. After Thorin's company left [Bree](../B/Bree.md), a day's journey brought them to the **Last Inn**, which they found deserted.<sup id="cite_ref-bridge_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bridge-3">[3]</a></sup> There they camped on [3 May](../_/3_May.md) [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2941](Third_Age_2941.md), being too depressed to proceed further than the twenty miles they had progressed.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [John Rateliff](../J/John_Rateliff.md) (author of *[The History of The Hobbit](The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)*) states that the Last Inn (later called the Forsaken Inn) was brought in to emphasize the desolation which had grown east of Bree.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

*[The Treason of Isengard](The_Treason_of_Isengard.md)* included the [First Map of The Lord of the Rings](The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md). On Map II, in the square K-8, there is a dot with the initials "F.I." on the road east of Bree about one-quarter of the distance to [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) stated that these initials did stand for the Forsaken Inn.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/L/Landmark-The_Forsaken_Inn.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/0d/Landmark-The_Forsaken_Inn.jpg/300px-Landmark-The_Forsaken_Inn.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="225" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/0d/Landmark-The_Forsaken_Inn.jpg/450px-Landmark-The_Forsaken_Inn.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/0d/Landmark-The_Forsaken_Inn.jpg/600px-Landmark-The_Forsaken_Inn.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1600" data-file-height="1200"/></a><figcaption>The Forsaken Inn as depicted in <a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a>.</figcaption></figure>

<b>2007: <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In this adaptation, the role of the inn is greatly expanded. It is the <a href="../L/Lone-lands.md" title="Lone-lands">Lone-lands</a> settlement closest to <a href="../B/Bree-land.md" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a> and lies in the shadow of <a href="../W/Weathertop.md" title="Weathertop">Weathertop</a>. A respite for travelers, the Inn has a milestone, mail service, and many merchants, in addition to a great number of people in need of travellers&#39; assistance. These include the Inn&#39;s proprietor, Anlaf, and his staff as well as travellers, guests, and some of the Eglain, meaning the Forsaken, that have recently been displaced from Minas Eriol.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>To the north and northeast lie the ruins of the fortifications of the war between <a href="../A/Arthedain.md" title="Arthedain">Arthedain</a> and <a href="../R/Rhudaur.md" title="Rhudaur">Rhudaur</a>, now occupied and spanned by camps of <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">orcs</a> and goblins. Ruins also lie to the south-east, and along both sides of the road to the East. A long journey across the Lone-lands in that direction eventually leads to Ost Guruth, the last settlement before the <a href="../H/Hoarwell.md" title="Hoarwell">Hoarwell</a>, which marks the borders of the <a href="Trollshaws.md" title="Trollshaws">Trollshaws</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2021: <i><a href="The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="../C/Core_Rules_(The_One_Ring).md" title="Core Rules (The One Ring)">Core Rules</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Around <a href="Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="Third_Age_2965.md" title="Third Age 2965">2965</a>, the Forsaken Inn was an inn patronized by criminals and other low-lifes. The Rangers of the North tended to avoid it. The innkeeper at that time was named Jack the Forsaken.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup></dd></dl>
