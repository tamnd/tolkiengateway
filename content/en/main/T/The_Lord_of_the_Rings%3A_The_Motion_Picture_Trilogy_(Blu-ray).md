**The Lord of the Rings: The Motion Picture Trilogy** is a Blu-ray pack of [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") released on April 6, 2010 by [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema). It consists of the theatrical editions in high-definition and six DVDs of bonus features, music videos, captions in both Spanish and English, including over an hour of deleted scenes and a documentary about the making of the film (trailers are included).
