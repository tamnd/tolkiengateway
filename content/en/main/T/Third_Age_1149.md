**Third Age 1149** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1149**) is the 1149th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Hyarmendacil I](../H/Hyarmendacil_I.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), last of the [Ship-kings](../S/Ship-kings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppSouth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppSouth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Hyarmendacil I](../H/Hyarmendacil_I.md)'s son, [Atanatar II Alcarin](../A/Atanatar_II.md), becomes the sixteenth [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppSouth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppSouth-1">[1]</a></sup>
