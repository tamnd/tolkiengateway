**Third Age 2385** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2385**) is the 2385th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2385 was also known as **S.R. 785** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Glóin](<../G/Glóin_(King_of_Durin's_Folk).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Óin](<../_/Óin_(King_of_Durin's_Folk).md>), son of Glóin, becomes the King of [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>
