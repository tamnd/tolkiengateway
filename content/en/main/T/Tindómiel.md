**Tindómiel** was the second child, and only daughter, of [Elros Tar-Minyatur](/wiki/Elros "Elros"), the first [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor). Almost nothing is known about her life.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tindómiel* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but it's listed along with Tinúviel among the examples of the ending *[-iel](/wiki/-iel)* for "female".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack) suggests it means "Daughter of Twilight" and is a compound of *[tindómë](/wiki/Tind%C3%B3m%C3%AB "Tindómë")* ("twilight") and *[-iel](/wiki/-iel)* ("daughter", feminine suffix). The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name *Tinúviel* has the same meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

It could also mean "Morning Star" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and consist of an assimilation of *[tindómë](/wiki/Tind%C3%B3m%C3%AB "Tindómë")* and *[el](/wiki/Elen "Elen")* ("star"),<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> analogous to [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) [Undómiel](/wiki/Und%C3%B3miel) "Evenstar", who was a cousin of Tindómiel, while <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tind%C3%B3merel&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tindómerel (page does not exist)">Tindómerel</a> is better documented as meaning "Daughter of Twilight".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
