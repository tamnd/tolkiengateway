**Tumladen** was a valley in southern [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) between the rivers [Celos](/wiki/Celos) and [Sirith](/wiki/Sirith)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> in the east of the fief of [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-3">[3]</a></sup>

A road went from [Pelennor](/wiki/Pelennor) to the vales of Tumladen and [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach), the mountain-villages, and then to [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin). During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Tumladen was made a refuge for some of the women, children, and elderly who were evacuated from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Tumladen is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-UI_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-5">[5]</a></sup> It means "a flat valley with steep sides"<sup id="cite_ref-UI_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-3">[3]</a></sup> or "Wide Valley".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack) suggests that it is a compound of *[tum](/wiki/Tum "Tum")* ("valley")<sup id="cite_ref-S_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-7">[7]</a></sup> and *laden* ("flat", "wide").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Tumladen was located in Upper <a href="/wiki/Lebennin" title="Lebennin">Lebennin</a> in Eastern <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>. The river <a href="/wiki/Sirith" title="Sirith">Sirith</a> flowed through it. &#34;Imladir&#34; was the Captain of the Guard of Tumladen, but in the days before the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a> the &#34;Lady Terenis&#34;, sister of Steward <a href="/wiki/Denethor" title="Denethor">Denethor</a>, dwelt in Tumladen and held the true power in the valley.</dd></dl>
