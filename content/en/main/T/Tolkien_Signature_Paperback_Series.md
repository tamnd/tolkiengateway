**Tolkien 'Signature' Paperback Series** unofficially refers to a series of new editions (or reprints) of some core Tolkien-books published by [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md), it started in [2022](../_/2022.md) and is ongoing as of [2025](../_/2025.md).

This new format is a trade paperback with a uniform dimension and matching cover designs, which features an elegant mono-colored background with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s signature on the top, in the center is a pattern or artwork relevant to the book's content.

The series started in [2022](../_/2022.md) with *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* and the 3-volume *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, then in [2023](../_/2023.md) extending to *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)*, *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*, 12 volumes of *[The History of Middle-earth](The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* plus the *[Index](The_History_of_Middle-earth_Index.md)*, and also the three "Great Tales".

In [2024](../_/2024.md) shorter works *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm.md)*, *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md)*, and *[Farmer Giles of Ham](../F/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md)* were released in this format, and from [2025](../_/2025.md) it also includes *[Roverandom](../R/Roverandom.md)*, *[Smith of Wootton Major](../S/Smith_of_Wootton_Major.md)*, and *[Tree and Leaf](Tree_and_Leaf.md)*.

Also released in 2025 are *[The Road to Middle-earth](The_Road_to_Middle-earth.md)*, *[Tolkien On Fairy-stories](Tolkien_On_Fairy-stories~23734.md)*, *[The Nature of Middle-earth](The_Nature_of_Middle-earth.md)*, and then *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight](../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl,_and_Sir_Orfeo.md)*, *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudrún.md)*, *[The Story of Kullervo](The_Story_of_Kullervo.md)*, *[A Secret Vice](../A/A_Secret_Vice%253A_Tolkien_on_Invented_Languages.md)*, and then *[Finn and Hengest](../F/Finn_and_Hengest%253A_The_Fragment_and_the_Episode.md)*, *[The Monsters and the Critics](The_Monsters_and_the_Critics,_and_Other_Essays.md)*.

Added in [2026](../_/2026.md) to this series is *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Author of the Century](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_Author_of_the_Century.md)*.
