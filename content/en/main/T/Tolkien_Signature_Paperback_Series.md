**Tolkien 'Signature' Paperback Series** unofficially refers to a series of new editions (or reprints) of some core Tolkien-books published by [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins), it started in [2022](/wiki/2022) and is ongoing as of [2025](/wiki/2025).

This new format is a trade paperback with a uniform dimension and matching cover designs, which features an elegant mono-colored background with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s signature on the top, in the center is a pattern or artwork relevant to the book's content.

The series started in [2022](/wiki/2022) with *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and the 3-volume *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, then in [2023](/wiki/2023) extending to *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*, 12 volumes of *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* plus the *[Index](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth_Index "The History of Middle-earth Index")*, and also the three "Great Tales".

In [2024](/wiki/2024) shorter works *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](/wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm)*, *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*, and *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)* were released in this format, and from [2025](/wiki/2025) it also includes *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*, *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*, and *[Tree and Leaf](/wiki/Tree_and_Leaf)*.

Also released in 2025 are *[The Road to Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Road_to_Middle-earth)*, *[Tolkien On Fairy-stories](/wiki/Tolkien_On_Fairy-stories)*, *[The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth)*, and then *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight](/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl,_and_Sir_Orfeo "Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, Pearl, and Sir Orfeo")*, *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudr%C3%BAn)*, *[The Story of Kullervo](/wiki/The_Story_of_Kullervo)*, *[A Secret Vice](/wiki/A_Secret_Vice:_Tolkien_on_Invented_Languages "A Secret Vice: Tolkien on Invented Languages")*, and then *[Finn and Hengest](/wiki/Finn_and_Hengest:_The_Fragment_and_the_Episode "Finn and Hengest: The Fragment and the Episode")*, *[The Monsters and the Critics](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics,_and_Other_Essays "The Monsters and the Critics, and Other Essays")*.

Added in [2026](/wiki/2026) to this series is *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Author of the Century](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Author_of_the_Century)*.
