## Reception

In a review of *Trolls of the Misty Mountains* in *[White Dwarf](/wiki/White_Dwarf)* magazine, Graham Staplehurst remarked that the adventures have some interesting twists and characters, but that the "*language and ideas get somewhat childish*" and that the description of the setting (including maps) is a bit confusing. In conclusion Staplehurst says that "*the module slips a little from the excellent standards previously set by ICE*."

> Reference: "Open Box", in *[White Dwarf](/wiki/White_Dwarf)*, issue 87 (March 1987), p. 4
