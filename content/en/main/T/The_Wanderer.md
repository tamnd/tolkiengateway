<i><b>The Wanderer</b></i> is a book containing a critical edition of the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) poem <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wanderer_(Old_English_poem)" title="wikipedia:The Wanderer (Old English poem)"><i>The Wanderer</i></a>, edited by two of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s former students: [T.P. Dunning](/wiki/T.P._Dunning) and [A.J. Bliss](/wiki/A.J._Bliss). In their Introduction, the editors provide a reference to Tolkien's essay "[Ofermod](/wiki/Ofermod)" (cf. page 94, footnote 3).
