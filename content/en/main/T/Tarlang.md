**Tarlang** was a legendary [giant](../G/Giants.md).<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

According to folklore, Tarlang belonged to a group of giants who in "ancient days" were building the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) in order to prevent [Men](../M/Men.md) from entering the giants' land close by the [Sea](../B/Beleriand.md). While carrying a heavy load of rocks, Tarlang stumbled and fell on his face, fatally breaking his neck. His corpse was used by the other giants to finish the wall: his remnants formed the pass [Tarlang's Neck](Tarlang's_Neck.md) (from the break in his neck), [Dol Tarlang](../D/Dol_Tarlang.md) (consisting of his head), and [Cûl Veleg](../C/Cûl_Veleg.md) and [Cûl Bîn](../C/Cûl_Bîn.md) (remnants of his load of rocks).<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Tarlang* is glossed as "stiff neck", consisting of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tarch&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tarch (page does not exist)">tarch</a></i> ("stiff, tough") + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lang&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lang (page does not exist)">lang</a></i> ("neck").<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-DW_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DW-2">[2]</a></sup>
