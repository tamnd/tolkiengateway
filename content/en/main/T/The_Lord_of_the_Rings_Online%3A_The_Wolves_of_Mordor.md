<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Online: The Wolves of <a href="../M/Mordor.md" title="Mordor">Mordor</a></b></i> (**Update 47**) is the fourteenth expansion for *[The Lord of the Rings Online](The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)*.

## From the Developer {#From_the_Developer}

The Free Peoples of Middle-earth recover from the hardships of the War of the Ring, believing their victory over Sauron's forces to be complete. But in a distant corner of Mordor, beyond the plateau of [Gorgoroth](../G/Gorgoroth.md), the fiery hatred of an ancient bloodline has been rekindled.

Not every shadow fled when Mordor’s fires dimmed, and some of the Dark Lord's oldest servants issue forth to threaten those who caused their master to fall. As terrible howls pierce the night, the were-wolves of Mordor emerge to rend the peace with teeth and claw!
