**Theo** was a young [Southlander](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md) of [Tirharad](Tirharad.md) in the [Southlands](../M/Mordor.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Theo was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md) and he is portrayed by [Tyroe Muhafidin](Tyroe_Muhafidin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Theo is the son of [Bronwyn](../B/Bronwyn.md), and he is at first skeptical of the Elf [Arondir's](../A/Arondir.md) feelings toward his mother.
