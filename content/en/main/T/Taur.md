## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>taur</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning "forest".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

Sindarin *taur* is a descendant form of [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) *tau-rē*, derived from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [TAW](TAW.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[taure](Taure.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Noldorin {#Noldorin}

### Etymology 1 {#Etymology_1}

<i><b>taur</b></i> is a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) word meaning "great wood, forest",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> a descendant form of [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Taur%C4%93&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Taurē (page does not exist)">taurē</a></i>, derived from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [TÁWAR](TAW.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium).<sup id="cite_ref-LR1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR1-4">[3]</a></sup>

### Etymology 2 {#Etymology_2}

<i><b>taur</b></i> is a poetic/archaic (†) [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) word meaning "king", derived from Common Eldarin <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=T%C4%81ro&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tāro (page does not exist)">tāro</a></i> ("king: only used of the legitimate kings of the whole tribes"), from root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=T%C4%80&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TĀ (page does not exist)">TĀ, TAƷ</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-LR2_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR2-5">[4]</a></sup>

### Etymology 3 {#Etymology_3}

<i><b>taur</b></i> is a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) adjective meaning "vast, mighty, overwhelming, awful - also high, sublime" (poetic only or in ancient titles and names), from roots <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=T%C4%80&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TĀ (page does not exist)">TĀ, TAƷ</a>/[TUR](TUR~21398.md).<sup id="cite_ref-LR1_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR1-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LR3_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR3-6">[5]</a></sup>

## Ilkorin {#Ilkorin}

The [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md) word <i><b>taur</b></i> means "great wood, forest" or "wood (place and material), a descendant form of [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) *taurē*, derived from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [TÁWAR](TAW.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium).<sup id="cite_ref-LR1_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR1-4">[3]</a></sup>
