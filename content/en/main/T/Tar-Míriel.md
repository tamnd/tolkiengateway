**Tar-Míriel** was the only child of the late-marrying [Tar-Palantir](/wiki/Tar-Palantir), the twenty-fourth [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

By the [Law of Succession](/wiki/Law_of_Succession_in_N%C3%BAmenor "Law of Succession in Númenor"), established by [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion), she should have become the fourth [Ruling Queen of Númenor](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor). However, after her father's death, Míriel's cousin [Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n "Ar-Pharazôn") took her unwillingly as his wife and seized the [Sceptre of Númenor](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor) for himself, taking the title of [Ar-Pharazôn the Golden](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n "Ar-Pharazôn"). He changed Míriel's name to [Ar-Zimraphel](/wiki/Zimraphel "Zimraphel").

Ar-Pharazôn in his pride brought a mighty fleet to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) to challenge [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) for dominion of the land. Sauron pretended to humble himself before the King and was taken as captive to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). Soon Sauron had fully corrupted the King and had persuaded the aging king to seek immortality and assail [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) itself. The [Valar](/wiki/Valar) called upon [the One](/wiki/The_One "The One"), who cast Númenor into the sea. It is said that Tar-Míriel strove to reach the sacred peak of the [Meneltarma](/wiki/Meneltarma) before the inundation of the island, but the great wave swept her away from the steep side of the Holy Mountain.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Míriel*, a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name, had an approximate meaning of "Jewel-daughter". The first element *[mírë](/wiki/M%C3%ADr%C3%AB "Mírë")* most probably means "jewel" while the second element *[-iel](/wiki/-iel)* either means "daughter" or is a general feminine suffix.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The prefix *[tar-](/wiki/Tar "Tar")* means "high" in Quenya.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

If her [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name Ar-Zimraphel, has the same meaning, the first element *[zimra](/wiki/Zimra "Zimra")* may mean "jewel" and the second element *-phel* may mean "daughter" or could be a general feminine suffix.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The meaning of the Adûnaic prefix *Ar-* is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "king" and is a prefixal form of *ârû* ("king") or derived from the same root.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first ever writings about Númenor, the last queen of Númenor, who witnessed the coming of [Thû](/wiki/Th%C3%BB)/Sauron, was "Queen **Istar**", wife of "King [Angor](/wiki/Angor)".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> In a rewrite, their names are changed to "Tar-kalion" and "**Tar-ilien**". Unlike in the later [Akallabêth](/wiki/Akallab%C3%AAth), both Tar-ilien and her husband believe Sauron's lies.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In one version of the *[Akallabêth](/wiki/Akallab%C3%AAth)*, Míriel was named **Zimrahil** and was betrothed to [Elentir](/wiki/Elentir), the brother of [Amandil](/wiki/Amandil) (the last [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)). However, she fell desperately in love with Pharazôn and chose to marry him when he took up the Sceptre. This story is very different than the tale in the published *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, where Ar-Pharazôn takes Míriel very much against her will in order to claim the Sceptre.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
