**Third Age 1973** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1973**) is the 1973rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1973 was also known as **S.R. 373** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Autumn {#Autumn}

- [King](../K/Kings_of_Arthedain.md) [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md) of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md) sends word to [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Eärnil II](../E/Eärnil_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) that they are struggling against the threat of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md). Eärnil sends a fleet north carrying his son [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md) with an army.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>
