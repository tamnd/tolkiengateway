**Túro** is an enigmatic character, apparently old ("*[Yára](/wiki/Y%C3%A1ra) Túro*"). Although the lore surrounding him deals with [Quenya grammar](/wiki/Quenya/Grammar "Quenya/Grammar"), it is unknown if he belongs to the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"). Some people were interested in him, due to his coming and making unspecified things, and he ended up annoying them by eating all of the bread.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
