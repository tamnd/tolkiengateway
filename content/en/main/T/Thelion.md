<i><b>thelion</b></i> is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) word, only appearing as an element in the names *[Ecthelion](/wiki/Ecthelion)* and *Aegthelion*.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Didier Willis](/wiki/Didier_Willis) notes *thelion* as an unattested [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) noun meaning "one who remains firm in his purpose".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}
