**Third Age 3018** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3018**) is the 3018th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Third Age 3018 was also known as **S.R. 1418** in [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning).

Notable events in this year include:

## April {#April}

- **12** - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) reaches [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **13** - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) reveals to [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") that Bilbo's ring is [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## May {#May}

- **1** - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) meets with [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) at [Sarn Ford](/wiki/Sarn_Ford).<sup id="cite_ref-Strid_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strid-3">[3]</a></sup>

## June {#June}

- **19** - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) and [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) receive the [riddle](/wiki/Seek_for_the_Sword_that_was_broken "Seek for the Sword that was broken") in their sleep.
- **20** - [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) [attacks](/wiki/Sauron%27s_attack_on_Osgiliath "Sauron's attack on Osgiliath") [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath). About the same time [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) is attacked, and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) escapes.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Fugitives from the South reach [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador). At the southern borders of the [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing) Gandalf receives news of war and defeat and the [Black Shadow](/wiki/Black_Shadow). He starts wandering aimlessly and then turns east and north.<sup id="cite_ref-council_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-4">[4]</a></sup>

<h2><span id="Midyear.27s_Day"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Midyear&#39;s_Day">Midyear&#39;s Day</span></h2>

- Along the [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway) [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) finds [Radagast](/wiki/Radagast) with news from [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) that the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) have crossed the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) and look for [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). Gandalf goes to [Bree](/wiki/Bree) and leaves [a letter](/wiki/Gandalf%27s_letter "Gandalf's letter") for [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") with [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur).<sup id="cite_ref-Strid_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strid-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-council_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-4">[4]</a></sup>

## July {#July}

- **1** - At dawn Gandalf leaves Bree for [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).<sup id="cite_ref-council_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-4">[4]</a></sup> The [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") set forth to search for [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"). They pass northward across [the Wold](/wiki/The_Wold "The Wold")
- **4** - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) sets out from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **10** - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) imprisoned in [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

## August {#August}

- All trace of [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) is lost. It is thought that at about this time, being hunted both by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s servants, he took refuge in [Moria](/wiki/Moria); but when he had at last discovered the way to the [West-gate](/wiki/West-gate) he could not get out.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

## September {#September}

- Having failed to find the Shire, the Black Riders return to the [Wold](/wiki/Wold) empty-handed; messengers from Sauron send them to [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).
- Frodo worries about not having heard from Gandalf, as his birthday approaches. Some of his friends, [Folco Boffin](/wiki/Folco_Boffin), [Fredegar Bolger](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger), [Merry Brandybuck](/wiki/Merry_Brandybuck) and [Pippin Took](/wiki/Pippin_Took) stay in [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) and help him pack for his removal to [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow).<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Three_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Three-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **18** - [Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir) comes and rescues [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) from [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc) in the early hours. The [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") cross the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **19** - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) comes to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) as a beggar, and is refused admittance.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **20** - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) gains entrance to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras). [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) commands him to take any horse and go within a day<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") come to [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard). Frodo's furniture is moved to [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow).<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Three_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Three-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **21** - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) meets [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax), but the horse will not allow him to come near, and follows him far over the fields.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") encounter [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma"), who tells them of the location of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). Frodo is anxious for Gandalf's absence and keeps a constant look-out for him.<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Three_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Three-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **22** - The [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") reach [Sarn Ford](/wiki/Sarn_Ford) at evening; they drive off the guard of [Rangers](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North"). [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) overtakes [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> Frodo celebrates his birthday with friends but without Gandalf.<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Three_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Three-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **23** - Four Riders enter [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") before dawn. The others pursue the [Rangers](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North") eastward, and then return to watch the [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway). Merry and Fatty leave for Crickhollow. [Khamûl](/wiki/Kham%C3%BBl) comes to [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) at nightfall. [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") leaves [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) having tamed [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax) rides from [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **24** - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) crosses the [Isen](/wiki/Isen).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> The hobbits are followed by a Rider on a road near [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End); a [Wandering Company](/wiki/Wandering_Companies "Wandering Companies") of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) under [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor_Inglorion) returns from [Emyn Beraid](/wiki/Emyn_Beraid) and the Rider runs away the chants to <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Elbereth" title="Elbereth">Elbereth</a>. The hobbits camp with the Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Three_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Three-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **25** - A Black Rider approaches [Farmer Maggot](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot) in [Bamfurlong](/wiki/Bamfurlong) but he is ousted. The Hobbits later reach him who guides them with his wagon until the [Bucklebury Ferry](/wiki/Bucklebury_Ferry). The evening they meet Merry and reach [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow). [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") reveals them his plans, already known.
- **26** - The [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest). The Hobbits are trapped by the [Old Man Willow](/wiki/Old_Man_Willow) but are rescued by [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil). He invites them home.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Two Riders come to the *[Prancing Pony](/wiki/Prancing_Pony)* and talk with [Nob](/wiki/Nob) who closes the door; the Riders go towards [Archet](/wiki/Archet).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
- **27** - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) crosses [Greyflood](/wiki/Greyflood). Second night with [Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil "Tom Bombadil").<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **28** - The [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) reach the [Barrow Downs](/wiki/Barrow_Downs) and are captured by a [Barrow-wight](/wiki/Barrow-wights "Barrow-wights") in a [barrow](/wiki/Barrows "Barrows"), but are rescued again by Bombadil.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) reaches [Sarn Ford](/wiki/Sarn_Ford).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **29** - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") reaches [Bree](/wiki/Bree) at night. [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) visits the Gaffer.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **30** - [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow) and the [Inn](/wiki/Prancing_Pony "Prancing Pony") at [Bree](/wiki/Bree) are raided in the early hours. [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") leaves [Bree](/wiki/Bree). [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) comes to [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow), and reaches [Bree](/wiki/Bree) at night.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

## October {#October}

- **1** - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) leaves [Bree](/wiki/Bree).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **3** - He is attacked at night on [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") in the [Midgewater Marshes](/wiki/Midgewater_Marshes) sees flashes of light from the battle.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
- **4** - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) escapes to the north from [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop), followed by four [Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl").<sup id="cite_ref-council_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **6** - The camp under [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop) attacked at night; the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) stabs [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") with his [Morgul-blade](/wiki/Morgul-blade), causing a [Morgul-wound](/wiki/Morgul-wound).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
- **9** - [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) leaves [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **11** - He drives the [Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") off the [Last Bridge](/wiki/Last_Bridge) of [Mitheithel](/wiki/Mitheithel)<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> and leaves a beryl as a token that it is safe.<sup id="cite_ref-council_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **13** - Aragorn and the hobbits find it, and cross the Bridge.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
