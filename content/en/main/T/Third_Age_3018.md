**Third Age 3018** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3018**) is the 3018th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 3018 was also known as **S.R. 1418** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## April {#April}

- **12** - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) reaches [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **13** - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) reveals to [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) that Bilbo's ring is [the One Ring](The_One_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## May {#May}

- **1** - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) meets with [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) at [Sarn Ford](../S/Sarn_Ford.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Strid_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strid-3">[3]</a></sup>

## June {#June}

- **19** - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) and [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) receive the [riddle](../S/Seek_for_the_Sword_that_was_broken.md) in their sleep.
- **20** - [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) [attacks](../S/Sauron's_attack_on_Osgiliath.md) [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md). About the same time [Thranduil](Thranduil.md) is attacked, and [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) escapes.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Fugitives from the South reach [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md). At the southern borders of the [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md) Gandalf receives news of war and defeat and the [Black Shadow](../B/Black_Shadow.md). He starts wandering aimlessly and then turns east and north.<sup id="cite_ref-council_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-4">[4]</a></sup>

<h2><span id="Midyear.27s_Day"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Midyear&#39;s_Day">Midyear&#39;s Day</span></h2>

- Along the [Greenway](../G/Greenway.md) [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) finds [Radagast](../R/Radagast.md) with news from [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) that the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) have crossed the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) and look for [the Shire](The_Shire.md). Gandalf goes to [Bree](../B/Bree.md) and leaves [a letter](../G/Gandalf's_letter.md) for [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) with [Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Strid_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strid-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-council_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-4">[4]</a></sup>

## July {#July}

- **1** - At dawn Gandalf leaves Bree for [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md).<sup id="cite_ref-council_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-4">[4]</a></sup> The [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) set forth to search for [the One Ring](The_One_Ring.md). They pass northward across [the Wold](The_Wold.md)
- **4** - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) sets out from [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **10** - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) imprisoned in [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

## August {#August}

- All trace of [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) is lost. It is thought that at about this time, being hunted both by the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s servants, he took refuge in [Moria](../M/Moria.md); but when he had at last discovered the way to the [West-gate](../W/West-gate.md) he could not get out.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

## September {#September}

- Having failed to find the Shire, the Black Riders return to the [Wold](../W/Wold.md) empty-handed; messengers from Sauron send them to [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md).
- Frodo worries about not having heard from Gandalf, as his birthday approaches. Some of his friends, [Folco Boffin](../F/Folco_Boffin.md), [Fredegar Bolger](../F/Fredegar_Bolger.md), [Merry Brandybuck](../M/Merry_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin Took](../P/Pippin_Took.md) stay in [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) and help him pack for his removal to [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md).<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Three_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Three-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **18** - [Gwaihir](../G/Gwaihir.md) comes and rescues [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) from [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md) in the early hours. The [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) cross the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **19** - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) comes to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) as a beggar, and is refused admittance.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **20** - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) gains entrance to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md). [Théoden](Théoden.md) commands him to take any horse and go within a day<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) come to [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md). Frodo's furniture is moved to [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md).<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Three_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Three-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **21** - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) meets [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md), but the horse will not allow him to come near, and follows him far over the fields.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) encounter [Gríma Wormtongue](../G/Gríma.md), who tells them of the location of [the Shire](The_Shire.md). Frodo is anxious for Gandalf's absence and keeps a constant look-out for him.<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Three_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Three-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **22** - The [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) reach [Sarn Ford](../S/Sarn_Ford.md) at evening; they drive off the guard of [Rangers](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md). [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) overtakes [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> Frodo celebrates his birthday with friends but without Gandalf.<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Three_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Three-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **23** - Four Riders enter [the Shire](The_Shire.md) before dawn. The others pursue the [Rangers](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) eastward, and then return to watch the [Greenway](../G/Greenway.md). Merry and Fatty leave for Crickhollow. [Khamûl](../K/Khamûl.md) comes to [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md) at nightfall. [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) leaves [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md). [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) having tamed [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md) rides from [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **24** - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) crosses the [Isen](../I/Isen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> The hobbits are followed by a Rider on a road near [Woody End](../W/Woody_End.md); a [Wandering Company](../W/Wandering_Companies.md) of [Elves](../E/Elves.md) under [Gildor Inglorion](../G/Gildor_Inglorion.md) returns from [Emyn Beraid](../E/Emyn_Beraid.md) and the Rider runs away the chants to <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../E/Elbereth.md" title="Elbereth">Elbereth</a>. The hobbits camp with the Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Three_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Three-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **25** - A Black Rider approaches [Farmer Maggot](../F/Farmer_Maggot.md) in [Bamfurlong](../B/Bamfurlong.md) but he is ousted. The Hobbits later reach him who guides them with his wagon until the [Bucklebury Ferry](../B/Bucklebury_Ferry.md). The evening they meet Merry and reach [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md). [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) reveals them his plans, already known.
- **26** - The [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md). The Hobbits are trapped by the [Old Man Willow](../O/Old_Man_Willow.md) but are rescued by [Tom Bombadil](Tom_Bombadil.md). He invites them home.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Two Riders come to the *[Prancing Pony](../P/Prancing_Pony.md)* and talk with [Nob](../N/Nob.md) who closes the door; the Riders go towards [Archet](../A/Archet.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
- **27** - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) crosses [Greyflood](../G/Greyflood.md). Second night with [Bombadil](Tom_Bombadil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **28** - The [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) reach the [Barrow Downs](../B/Barrow_Downs~11356.md) and are captured by a [Barrow-wight](../B/Barrow-wights.md) in a [barrow](../B/Barrows.md), but are rescued again by Bombadil.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) reaches [Sarn Ford](../S/Sarn_Ford.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **29** - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) reaches [Bree](../B/Bree.md) at night. [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) visits the Gaffer.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **30** - [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md) and the [Inn](../P/Prancing_Pony.md) at [Bree](../B/Bree.md) are raided in the early hours. [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) leaves [Bree](../B/Bree.md). [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) comes to [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md), and reaches [Bree](../B/Bree.md) at night.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

## October {#October}

- **1** - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) leaves [Bree](../B/Bree.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **3** - He is attacked at night on [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) in the [Midgewater Marshes](../M/Midgewater_Marshes.md) sees flashes of light from the battle.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
- **4** - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) escapes to the north from [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md), followed by four [Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md).<sup id="cite_ref-council_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **6** - The camp under [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md) attacked at night; the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) stabs [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) with his [Morgul-blade](../M/Morgul-blade.md), causing a [Morgul-wound](../M/Morgul-wound.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
- **9** - [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) leaves [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **11** - He drives the [Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) off the [Last Bridge](../L/Last_Bridge.md) of [Mitheithel](../M/Mitheithel.md)<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> and leaves a beryl as a token that it is safe.<sup id="cite_ref-council_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **13** - Aragorn and the hobbits find it, and cross the Bridge.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **18** - [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) finds [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) at dusk. [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) reaches [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **20** - Escape across the [Ford of Bruinen](../F/Ford_of_Bruinen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) release a flood that drowns the horses of the Riders.<sup id="cite_ref-council_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **24** - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) recovers and wakes. [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) arrives in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) at night.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **25** - [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

## December {#December}

- **18** - The membership of the [Company of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) is chosen.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
- **25** - The [Company of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) leaves [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) at dusk.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Within the year {#Within_the_year}

- [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) completes work on his [Translations from the Elvish](Translations_from_the_Elvish.md).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
