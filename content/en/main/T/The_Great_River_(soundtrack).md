**The Great River** is the fifteenth track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack.md)* by [Howard Shore](../H/Howard_Shore.md). The first part of the lyrics are taken from the [Namárië](../N/Namárië.md) and the second half from [Elendil's Oath](../E/Elendil's_Oath.md).

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div class="poem">
<p><i>Ai! laurië lantar lassi súrinen,</i><br/>
<i>yéni únótimë ve rámar aldaron!</i><br/>
<i>yéni ve lintë...</i><br/>
<br/>
<i>Et Eärello Endorenna utúlien.</i><br/>
<i>Sinome maruvan ar Hildinyar tenn&#39; Ambar-metta!</i>
</p>
</div>

## Translation {#Translation}

<div class="poem">
<p>Alas! golden leaves fall in the wind,<br/>
long years numberless as [the] wings of trees!<br/>
Long years like swift...<br/>
<br/>
Out of the Great Sea to Middle-earth I am come.<br/>
In this place I will abide, and my heirs, unto the ending of the world
</p>
</div>
