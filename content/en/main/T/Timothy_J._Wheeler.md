Wheeler** ([June 25](/wiki/25_June "25 June"), [1937](/wiki/1937) - [August 4](/wiki/4_August "4 August"), [2007](/wiki/2007)),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> founder of the magazine *Rally*, corresponded with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in late 1965 and early 1966.
