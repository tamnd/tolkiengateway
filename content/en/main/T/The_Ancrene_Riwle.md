<i><b>The Ancrene Riwle</b></i> is a Modern English translation of the [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) work <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ancrene_Riwle&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ancrene Riwle (page does not exist)">Ancrene Riwle</a></i>, by M.B. Salu.

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) conributed with a Preface (page v), dated [29 June](/wiki/29_June) [1955](/wiki/1955). The two paragraph preface discusses the version of the *Ancrene Riwle* chosen for translation by Salu.<sup id="cite_ref-TB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-1">[1]</a></sup> As described by [Richard C. West](/wiki/Richard_C._West) (who quotes from the preface), Tolkien calls the "Middle English dialect of the original '*a natural, easy, and cultivated speech, familiar with the courtesy of letters, able to combine colloquial liveliness with a reverence for the already long tradition of English writing*,' and thinks the translator has succeeded in representing this in modern terms."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

A paperback edition of the book was published by Burns &amp; Oates in 1963 (reprinted in 1967), and another paperback edition by the University of Exeter Press in 1990 (<a class="internal mw-magiclink-isbn" href="/wiki/Special:BookSources/0859893413">ISBN 0859893413</a>; reprinted in 2001).<sup id="cite_ref-TB_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
