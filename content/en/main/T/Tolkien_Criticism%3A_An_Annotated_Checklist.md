<i><b>Tolkien Criticism: An Annotated Checklist</b></i> is a descriptive bibliography of books and articles by and about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), compiled by [Richard C. West](/wiki/Richard_C._West).

The book includes some quotes from hard-to-find writings by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien): (1) Entry A24 on page 5 quotes from the Preface published in *[The Ancrene Riwle](/wiki/The_Ancrene_Riwle)*; (2) Entry B109 on page 34 reprints the brief extract from [a letter to Richard Lupoff](/wiki/Letter_to_Richard_Lupoff "Letter to Richard Lupoff") first printed in *[Edgar Rice Burroughs: Master of Adventure](/wiki/Edgar_Rice_Burroughs:_Master_of_Adventure)*.

A revised edition of the work was published in 1981 (177 pages, <a class="internal mw-magiclink-isbn" href="/wiki/Special:BookSources/9780873382564">ISBN 978-0873382564</a>).
