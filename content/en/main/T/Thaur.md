<i><b>thaur</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning *abhorrent, abominable*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its [Old Sindarin](../O/Old_Sindarin.md) form would be *t<sup>h</sup>aura*.
