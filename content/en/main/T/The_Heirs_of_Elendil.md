**The Heirs of Elendil** is the chapter from the *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)* volume, part of *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* series, which deals with the descendants of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil). It is the basis of [Appendix A](/wiki/Appendix_A) in the published *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

The chapter begins as a list of dates of events concerning [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) and his sons, [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion), listing their births and then events concerning them in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) after the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor). It then lists the Northern and Southern Lines that began with Isildur and Anárion respectively. The Northern Line lists the names, lifespans, birth years, and death years of the [Kings of Arnor](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor), [Kings of Arthedain](/wiki/Kings_of_Arthedain), and [Chieftains of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain). The Southern Line lists the same for the [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor) and [Ruling Stewards of Gondor](/wiki/Ruling_Steward "Ruling Steward"). Interspersed throughout are notes about the reigns of some, but not all, of these figures. Following these a separate list of the lifespans, dates of birth, and dates of death are given for the [Princes of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth "Prince of Dol Amroth"), but in this case most of the names are not given.
