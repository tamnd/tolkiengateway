<i><b>ten-</b></i>, meaning "arrive", is a scrapped [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) verb. Tolkien subsequently emended *ten-* and its gloss were struck out, and was changed to *men-* in all the other derivatives.<sup id="cite_ref-VT49_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT49-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

- *tene* (aorist: "comes, arrives, is with us")
- *etēnie* (perfect: "has just arrived")
- *tenuva* (future: "will arrive")
