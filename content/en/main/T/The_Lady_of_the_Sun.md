**The Lady of the Sun**<sup id="cite_ref-spirits_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-spirits-1">[1]</a></sup> was a character that exists within the folk-tales of the [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits),<sup id="cite_ref-tlotr_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tlotr-4">[4]</a></sup> the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans),<sup id="cite_ref-spirits_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-spirits-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians).<sup id="cite_ref-preface_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-preface-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Lady of the Sun<sup id="cite_ref-spirits_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-spirits-1">[1]</a></sup> in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) folklore may have her origins in the legend of [Arien](/wiki/Arien) the [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar").

The [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) believed that The Lady of the Sun, like *[Nîlû](/wiki/N%C3%AEl%C3%BB)*, was a [guardian spirit](/wiki/Spirits "Spirits") and named her <i><b>Ûrî</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-spirits_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-spirits-1">[1]</a></sup>

According to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) folklore, The Lady of the Sun<sup id="cite_ref-spirits_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-spirits-1">[1]</a></sup> had fiery eyes. After the [Man in the Moon](/wiki/Man_in_the_Moon)'s misadventure on [Arda](/wiki/Arda) in [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)'s<sup id="cite_ref-preface_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-preface-2">[2]</a></sup> song *[The Man in the Moon Stayed Up Too Late](/wiki/The_Man_in_the_Moon_Stayed_Up_Too_Late)*, The Lady of the Sun<sup id="cite_ref-spirits_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-spirits-1">[1]</a></sup> appeared surprised that no one was awake despite it being daytime.<sup id="cite_ref-poem5_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem5-3">[3]</a></sup>

In *[The Man in the Moon Came Down Too Soon](/wiki/The_Man_in_the_Moon_Came_Down_Too_Soon)*, a song derived from [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") lore,<sup id="cite_ref-preface_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-preface-2">[2]</a></sup> it is said that the Man in the Moon "longed for **Fire**" in his heart, possibly implying that he longed for The Lady of the Sun<sup id="cite_ref-spirits_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-spirits-1">[1]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-poem6_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem6-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name *Ûrî* is given as the female personification of *ûre* ("the Sun"), referring to its guardian spirit.<sup id="cite_ref-spirits_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-spirits-1">[1]</a></sup>
