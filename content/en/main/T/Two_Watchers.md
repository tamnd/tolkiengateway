The **Two Watchers** were two carved figures that guarded the arched gateway through the outer wall that led to the courtyard of the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md).

## Description {#Description}

The Two Watchers were great two figures that were sitting on thrones on both sides of the gateway. Their figures were formed of three bodies with heads with the faces of vultures with eyes of black stone. One head looked outward, one head looked inward and the third head stared across the gateway itself. They were inhabited by "some [dreadful spirit](../S/Spirits.md) of evil vigilance". Not a [visible](../S/Seen.md) or [invisible](../U/Unseen.md) creature was unnoticed by them. They had the power to identify enemies and to block them from entering or from exiting through the gate with the force of their will alone, and were able to send up a shrill alarm call if their guard was breached.<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:902</sup>

## History {#History}

It is possible that the Two Watchers were not created by the Men of Gondor, because a "dreadful spirit of evil vigilance" existed in them, they perceived a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbit.md) as an enemy and made a high shrill cry when he passed them, but let [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) pass the gateway without making such a cry.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

On [14 March](../_/14_March.md) [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> the Two Watchers blocked [Sam](../S/Sam.md)'s initial attempt to pass through the gate. However, Sam was able to overcome the will of the Two Watchers with the help of the [Phial of Galadriel](../P/Phial_of_Galadriel.md) and was able to pass through the gate into the court of the Tower of Cirith Ungol in a second attempt. When he passed the gate, the Two Watchers uttered a high shrill cry, which was answered by a single clang of a bell high up in The Tower of Cirith Ungol.<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:902–903</sup> After hearing the bell the orc [Snaga](<../S/Snaga_(orc_of_Mordor).md>) said to the orc [Shagrat](../S/Shagrat.md) that a great fighter, an Elf or a Man of Gondor ("[tark](Tark.md)") got past the Watchers, and that's *tark'*s work.<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:906</sup> It is probable that he meant that the feat of getting past the Watchers was the work of a Man of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Kirith_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kirith-6">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:26</sup>

Later that day, in order to leave the Tower, Frodo and Sam held up again the Phial while invoking [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md) and <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../E/Elbereth.md" title="Elbereth">Elbereth</a>, which broke the will of the Two Watchers so that they were able to pass through the gate to the outside. The keystone of the arch and the wall above the gate crumbled into ruin after they passed, but the Two Watchers uttered a high and dreadful wail.<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:915</sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) treats it like a fact that "that's tark's work" refers to "the getting past the Watchers". Earlier manuscripts of what would later become the chapter The Tower of Cirith Ungol have the orc saying "that's Elvish work" or "that's wizard's work" and the latter has him assuming that the great fighter may be "one of these filthy wizards".<sup id="cite_ref-Kirith_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kirith-6">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:26</sup>

<b>1980: <a href="The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)"><i>The Return of the King</i> (1980 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md" title="Samwise Gamgee">Samwise Gamgee</a> encounters the Watchers while attempting to enter the tower to rescue <a href="../F/Frodo_Baggins.md" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo Baggins</a>. He sets off the alarm, but gets by them by using the <a href="../P/Phial_of_Galadriel.md" title="Phial of Galadriel">Phial of Galadriel</a>. When he and Frodo encounter them once again when trying to leave the tower, they put up more of a resistance to the Phial, but give in when Frodo helps Sam use it.</dd></dl>

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Two Watchers that guard the Tower of Cirith Ungol are referred to as the Silent Watchers. They are described to be a type of Pûkel-creatures, animated stone monsters that are magically instilled with enchanted spirits, that were produced by Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> The Watchers-in-Stone of Moria are enchanted statues, inhabited by magical Spirits said to resemble the &#34;<i>spell-sewn Power that inhabits the &#39;Two Watchers&#39;</i>&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>
