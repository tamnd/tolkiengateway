**The Dwarf Lords** is the sixteenth track of disc two of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack - Special Edition](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack_-_Special_Edition)*, by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore). It is the third of four 'exclusive bonus tracks'.
