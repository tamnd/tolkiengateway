**Turtle-fish** was a legendary race of giant sea monsters, mentioned only in [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) verse. The last of the Turtle-fish was named *[Fastitocalon](../F/Fastitocalon.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Poem_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Poem-1">[1]</a></sup>

Turtle-fish appeared as treacherous islands, really being monsters capable of drowning seafarers.<sup id="cite_ref-Poem_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Poem-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L255_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L255-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) was inspired by an [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) bestiary, appearing in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_Book" title="wikipedia:Exeter Book">Exeter Book</a>. The idea of giant marine turtles ultimately derives from Eastern myths, and in the European versions the monster is often confused with whales.<sup id="cite_ref-L255_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L255-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other version of the legendarium {#Other_version_of_the_legendarium}

It is never explained whether the turtle-fish was an actual race or just a fictional character for the poem "[Fastitocalon](<../F/Fastitocalon_(poem).md>)". In an earlier version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md), Lord of the Waters, uses his giant whale, [Uin](../U/Uin.md), to drag [Tol Eressëa](Tol_Eressëa.md) towards [Aman](../A/Aman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> This whale reappears in [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s story *[Roverandom](../R/Roverandom.md)*. It is thinkable that Tolkien, in an early phase of the mythology, imagined the fastitocalon to be another one of Ulmo's creatures.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-2007: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Turtle-fish, also called <i>Fell Turtles</i> and <i>Festitycelyn</i>, are described as monsters that can grow as large as fifty feet in length, and that live both in the seas and in the large rivers and lakes.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1995-1997: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The card &#34;Fell Turtle&#34;, released in the set <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth%253A_The_Wizards.md" title="Middle-earth: The Wizards">METW</a></i>, is a Hazard Creature.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><i>Dread-turtles</i>, huge reptiles with spiky shells, are possibly inspired by the Turtle-fish. The largest dread-turtle, found in <a href="../R/Rohan.md" title="Rohan">Rohan</a>, is referred to as the Fastitocalon.</dd></dl>
