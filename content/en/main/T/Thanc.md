<i><b>thanc</b></i> is a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) word meaning "forked".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

Since *thanc* appears in the name *[Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md#Etymology)*, [Didier Willis](../D/Didier_Willis.md) has suggested that *thanc* (retaining the same meaning) would be valid [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from [Old Noldorin](../K/Kornoldorin.md) *sthanka*, from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [STAK](../S/STAK.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Compounds {#Compounds}

- *[Lhamthanc](../S/Serpents.md#Names)* ("forked tongue")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
- *[Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md#Etymology)* ("forked-height")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
