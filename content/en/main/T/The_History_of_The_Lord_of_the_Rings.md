<i><b>The History of The Lord of the Rings</b></i> is a set of four books by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), also published in *[The History of Middle-earth](The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* series. The independent 4-volume set was first published in [1998](../_/1998.md), and it was republished in [2002](../_/2002.md), by HarperCollins.

It documents the writing of *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, except for the [Foreword](The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Foreword.md), the [Prologue](<../P/Prologue_(The_Lord_of_the_Rings).md>) and the [Appendices](../A/Appendices.md) (which came later in *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md)*). Other early versions, drafts and schemes (including the only complete scheme of the entire book) have been published in *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_A_Reader's_Companion.md)*.

The idea of separating the four volumes from *The History of Middle-earth* came first from the Spanish edition in [1993](../_/1993.md), which was made with consent by Christopher Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Volumes and contents {#Volumes_and_contents}

- I. <i><b><a href="The_Return_of_the_Shadow.md" title="The Return of the Shadow">The Return of the Shadow</a></b></i> ([1988](../_/1988.md)) begins with the initial composition, and goes through to the [Mines of Moria](../K/Khazad-dûm.md).

- II. <i><b><a href="The_Treason_of_Isengard.md" title="The Treason of Isengard">The Treason of Isengard</a></b></i> ([1989](../_/1989.md)) continues to the meeting with [Théoden](Théoden.md).

- III. <i><b><a href="The_War_of_the_Ring.md" title="The War of the Ring">The War of the Ring</a></b></i> ([1990](../_/1990.md)) continues to the opening of the [Black Gate](../M/Morannon.md).

- IV. <i><b><a href="The_End_of_the_Third_Age.md" title="The End of the Third Age">The End of the Third Age</a></b></i> ([1998](../_/1998.md)) finishes the story.
    - This is significantly shorter than its *The History of Middle-earth* counterpart, <i><b><a href="../S/Sauron_Defeated.md" title="Sauron Defeated">Sauron Defeated</a></b></i> ([1992](../_/1992.md)), as it omits *[The Notion Club Papers](The_Notion_Club_Papers.md)* and *[The Drowning of Anadûnê](The_Drowning_of_Anadûnê.md)*.

In general, the books are organized as chapters corresponding to the chapters in the final *The Lord of the Rings*, with additional chapters describing the "[First Map](The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)", the "[Second Map](The_Second_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)", and other matters. Each chapter begins with some context, then the text of a first or second draft, possibly some alternate drafts if there were especially large changes, and interspersed with extended discussion of confusing or contradictory situations. The end of each chapter includes a set of notes about points of interest, such as words that were used originally and then partially erased or struck out.

## Book titles {#Book_titles}

The titles of the volumes derive from discarded titles for the separate books of *The Lord of the Rings*. J.R.R. Tolkien conceived the work as a single volume comprising six "books" plus extensive appendices, but the [original publisher](../G/George_Allen_&_Unwin.md) split the work into three, publishing two books per volume with the appendices included in the third.

The titles proposed by Tolkien for the six books were:

- [Book 1](The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md#Book_I) — *The First Journey* or *The Ring Sets Out*
- [Book 2](The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md#Book_II) — *The Journey of the Nine Companions* or *The Ring Goes South*
- [Book 3](The_Two_Towers.md#Book_III) — *The Treason of Isengard*
- [Book 4](The_Two_Towers.md#Book_IV) — *The Journey of the Ring-Bearers* or *The Ring Goes East*
- [Book 5](The_Return_of_the_King.md#Book_V) — *The War of the Ring*
- [Book 6](The_Return_of_the_King.md#Book_VI) — *The End of the Third Age*

The title *The Return of the Shadow* was a discarded title for Volume 1 — *[The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.

Three of the titles were also used as book titles for the seven-volume edition of *The Lord of the Rings*: *The Treason of Isengard* for Book 3, *The War of the Ring* for Book 5, and *The End of the Third Age* for Book 6.
