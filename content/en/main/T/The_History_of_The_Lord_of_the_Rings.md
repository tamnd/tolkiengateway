<i><b>The History of The Lord of the Rings</b></i> is a set of four books by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), also published in *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* series. The independent 4-volume set was first published in [1998](/wiki/1998), and it was republished in [2002](/wiki/2002), by HarperCollins.

It documents the writing of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, except for the [Foreword](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Foreword "The Lord of the Rings Foreword"), the [Prologue](</wiki/Prologue_(The_Lord_of_the_Rings)> "Prologue (The Lord of the Rings)") and the [Appendices](/wiki/Appendices) (which came later in *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)*). Other early versions, drafts and schemes (including the only complete scheme of the entire book) have been published in *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_A_Reader%27s_Companion)*.

The idea of separating the four volumes from *The History of Middle-earth* came first from the Spanish edition in [1993](/wiki/1993), which was made with consent by Christopher Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Volumes and contents {#Volumes_and_contents}

- I. <i><b><a href="/wiki/The_Return_of_the_Shadow" title="The Return of the Shadow">The Return of the Shadow</a></b></i> ([1988](/wiki/1988)) begins with the initial composition, and goes through to the [Mines of Moria](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm "Khazad-dûm").

- II. <i><b><a href="/wiki/The_Treason_of_Isengard" title="The Treason of Isengard">The Treason of Isengard</a></b></i> ([1989](/wiki/1989)) continues to the meeting with [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden).

- III. <i><b><a href="/wiki/The_War_of_the_Ring" title="The War of the Ring">The War of the Ring</a></b></i> ([1990](/wiki/1990)) continues to the opening of the [Black Gate](/wiki/Morannon "Morannon").

- IV. <i><b><a href="/wiki/The_End_of_the_Third_Age" title="The End of the Third Age">The End of the Third Age</a></b></i> ([1998](/wiki/1998)) finishes the story.
    - This is significantly shorter than its *The History of Middle-earth* counterpart, <i><b><a href="/wiki/Sauron_Defeated" title="Sauron Defeated">Sauron Defeated</a></b></i> ([1992](/wiki/1992)), as it omits *[The Notion Club Papers](/wiki/The_Notion_Club_Papers)* and *[The Drowning of Anadûnê](/wiki/The_Drowning_of_Anad%C3%BBn%C3%AA)*.

In general, the books are organized as chapters corresponding to the chapters in the final *The Lord of the Rings*, with additional chapters describing the "[First Map](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The First Map of The Lord of the Rings")", the "[Second Map](/wiki/The_Second_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Second Map of The Lord of the Rings")", and other matters. Each chapter begins with some context, then the text of a first or second draft, possibly some alternate drafts if there were especially large changes, and interspersed with extended discussion of confusing or contradictory situations. The end of each chapter includes a set of notes about points of interest, such as words that were used originally and then partially erased or struck out.

## Book titles {#Book_titles}

The titles of the volumes derive from discarded titles for the separate books of *The Lord of the Rings*. J.R.R. Tolkien conceived the work as a single volume comprising six "books" plus extensive appendices, but the [original publisher](/wiki/George_Allen_%26_Unwin "George Allen & Unwin") split the work into three, publishing two books per volume with the appendices included in the third.

The titles proposed by Tolkien for the six books were:

- [Book 1](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring#Book_I "The Fellowship of the Ring") — *The First Journey* or *The Ring Sets Out*
- [Book 2](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring#Book_II "The Fellowship of the Ring") — *The Journey of the Nine Companions* or *The Ring Goes South*
- [Book 3](/wiki/The_Two_Towers#Book_III "The Two Towers") — *The Treason of Isengard*
- [Book 4](/wiki/The_Two_Towers#Book_IV "The Two Towers") — *The Journey of the Ring-Bearers* or *The Ring Goes East*
- [Book 5](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King#Book_V "The Return of the King") — *The War of the Ring*
- [Book 6](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King#Book_VI "The Return of the King") — *The End of the Third Age*

The title *The Return of the Shadow* was a discarded title for Volume 1 — *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.

Three of the titles were also used as book titles for the seven-volume edition of *The Lord of the Rings*: *The Treason of Isengard* for Book 3, *The War of the Ring* for Book 5, and *The End of the Third Age* for Book 6.
