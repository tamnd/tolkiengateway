<i><b>The Ruins of Osgiliath: The Complete Tales of the First City of Gondor</b></i> is a collection of stories set in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), written by [Alex Lewis](../A/Alex_Lewis.md) and illustrated by [Ruth Lacon](../R/Ruth_Lacon.md). The book was first issued in a series of 3 booklets for members of [The Tolkien Society](The_Tolkien_Society.md) in 1990, created to fund a quality present for Miss [Priscilla Tolkien](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md) during the 1992 Tolkien Society Conference, celebrating Tolkien's 100th birthday. Like *[The Tale of Gondolin](The_Tale_of_Gondolin.md)*, the project was not backed by the [Tolkien Estate](Tolkien_Estate.md).

The book was released in [2005](../_/2005.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and was limited to 100 copies worldwide.
