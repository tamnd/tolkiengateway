<i><b>The Ruins of Osgiliath: The Complete Tales of the First City of Gondor</b></i> is a collection of stories set in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), written by [Alex Lewis](/wiki/Alex_Lewis) and illustrated by [Ruth Lacon](/wiki/Ruth_Lacon). The book was first issued in a series of 3 booklets for members of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) in 1990, created to fund a quality present for Miss [Priscilla Tolkien](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien) during the 1992 Tolkien Society Conference, celebrating Tolkien's 100th birthday. Like *[The Tale of Gondolin](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Gondolin)*, the project was not backed by the [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate).

The book was released in [2005](/wiki/2005)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and was limited to 100 copies worldwide.
