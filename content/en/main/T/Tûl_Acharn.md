<i><b>Tûl Acharn!</b></i> was a phrase that [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) shouted to [Lorgan](../L/Lorgan.md) in [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) after he was released from [Angband](../A/Angband.md) by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).

## History {#History}

After the [Eastron](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>), [Lorgan](../L/Lorgan.md), told [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) to leave [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) and wished him ill, Húrin shouted the phrase "*Tûl Acharn*", warning Lorgan that he was not the last of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md), regardless of his fate.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tûl Acharn!* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) phrase translating to "*Vengeance comes*", and is made up of the words <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=T%C3%BBl&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tûl (page does not exist)">Tûl</a></i> ("to come") and *[Acharn](../A/Acharn.md)* ("vengeance, an act of revenge").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a note, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) mentions that the phrase was originally **Tôl Acharn**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
