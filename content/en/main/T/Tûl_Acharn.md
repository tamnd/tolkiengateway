<i><b>Tûl Acharn!</b></i> was a phrase that [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) shouted to [Lorgan](/wiki/Lorgan) in [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) after he was released from [Angband](/wiki/Angband) by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).

## History {#History}

After the [Eastron](</wiki/Easterlings_(First_Age)> "Easterlings (First Age)"), [Lorgan](/wiki/Lorgan), told [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) to leave [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) and wished him ill, Húrin shouted the phrase "*Tûl Acharn*", warning Lorgan that he was not the last of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain), regardless of his fate.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tûl Acharn!* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) phrase translating to "*Vengeance comes*", and is made up of the words <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=T%C3%BBl&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tûl (page does not exist)">Tûl</a></i> ("to come") and *[Acharn](/wiki/Acharn)* ("vengeance, an act of revenge").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a note, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) mentions that the phrase was originally **Tôl Acharn**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
