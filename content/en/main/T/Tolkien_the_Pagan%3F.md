<i><b>Tolkien the Pagan? Reading Middle-earth through a Spiritual Lens</b></i> is a collection of the proceedings of the 31st Tolkien Society Seminar held in Leeds in 2018. The book was published by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) in [2019](/wiki/2019) under the auspices of the [Peter Roe Memorial Fund](/wiki/Peter_Roe_Memorial_Fund).

## Contents {#Contents}

- "Introduction" by Anna Milon
- "On the Providential Historicism of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth)" by Ryan Haecker
- "The Nature of Arda: An Artwork as the Embodiment of the [Flame Imperishable](/wiki/Secret_Fire "Secret Fire")" by Aslı Bülbül Candaş
- "Honouring the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), Seeking the Elf Within: The Curious History of Tolkien Spirituality and the Religious Affordance of Tolkien's Literary Mythology" by Markus Altena Davidsen
- "[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos), [Pratchett](/wiki/Terry_Pratchett "Terry Pratchett")'s Death" by Justin Lewis-Anthony
- "Also Sprach [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), Spirit of Fire: A [Nietzschean](/wiki/Friedrich_Nietzsche "Friedrich Nietzsche") Reading of Tolkien's Mythology?" by Giovanni Carmine Costabile

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This volume of proceedings contains papers from the largest and, perhaps, most diverse Tolkien Society Seminar to date. Following a much-contested Call for Papers, Tolkien the Pagan? Reading Middle-earth through a Spiritual Lens explores Tolkien&#39;s complex use of religious ideology, the readers’ approach to their beloved fictional world via unusual spiritual and philosophical channels, and how Middle-earth almost became a faith unto itself.
</p>
</blockquote>
