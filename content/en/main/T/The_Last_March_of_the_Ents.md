**The Last March of the Ents** is the forty-fifth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and the fifty-sixth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)"). This scene was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

As [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) talks with [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") about a family of field mice who used to *"tickle him awfully"*, the two [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) stare at the desolation of south [Fangorn](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest "Fangorn Forest"). Treebeard stops short and stares as he sees that much of the greenery surrounding [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) has been destroyed. He mourns for the loss of the trees, saying *"Many of these trees were my friends. Creatures I had known from nut and acorn. They had voices of their own."* While Merry looks around and Pippin tries to comfort him, Treebeard's gaze falls upon Isengard, and he says in anger, *"[Saruman](/wiki/Saruman). A [wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards") should know better!"*

He summons [Ents](/wiki/Ents) and [Huorns](/wiki/Huorns) out of Fangorn Forest with a great cry. Pippin notices that trees in the distance are moving. Treebeard says that their business is with the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), but that his business is with Isengard. A host of Ents emerges from the forest to join him, and they begin their approach to Isengard; Treebeard remarks to the two Hobbits that this could be their doom, and that it is likely to be the last march of the Ents.
