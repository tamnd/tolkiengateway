<i><b>The Devil&#39;s Coach-Horses</b></i> is a separate printing of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s essay "[The Devil's Coach-Horses](/wiki/The_Devil%27s_Coach-Horses)" as a six-page pamphlet. It was published under the imprint "Jersey: Edward Brothers, 1925" (likely an unauthorised reprint). Although dated 1925 the text is printed in Monotype Bembo, a typeface cut in 1929. Therefore it can be assumed that this publication dates from after this time, possibly from the 1980s.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
