**The Field of Cormallen** is the fourth chapter of the second book in *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*.

## Summary {#Summary}

The narrative returns to [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and those outside the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate). To the north, the [Captains of the West](/wiki/Captains_of_the_West) foundered on the hills outside the Gate, surrounded by a dark, rolling sea of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and [Wild Men](/wiki/Men_of_Darkness "Men of Darkness"). Gandalf stood proudly, white and calm, with no shadow falling upon him. Suddenly, a great cry rose up: “The [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles) are coming!”<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Out of the north arrived a company of great eagles, led by [Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir) the [Windlord](/wiki/Windlord). The will of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) faltered, and all the armies of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) quailed in terror. A great roar shook the hills. Gandalf cried in victory that the [Ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearers "Ring-bearers") had completed his quest, and that the reign of Sauron has ended.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> As Gandalf spoke, a huge shadow rose in the south, extending across the sky like a giant hand, and then vanished in the wind with a great rush.

[Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) led the Captains in a great sweep over the plains. Gandalf then soared into Mordor on the back of Gwaihir. Meanwhile, [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), still in the heart of Mordor, had given up all hope of survival. As they talked quietly below the ruin of [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom), Gwaihir spotted them before they lost consciousness. Two eagles swept down and lifted the hobbits into the air.

When Sam awoke, he found himself on a soft bed in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien), the eastern lands of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). He first commented on the extraordinary dream he had just had and then cried out in astonishment that his dream actually happened. Frodo slept next to Sam, and Gandalf watched over the two of them. The wizard said that a great Shadow had departed, asked the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) to dress in their worn and ragged attire, and escorted them out of the wood. They were to attend a reception hosted by the [King of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor").

A great throng of people awaited the Hobbits. At their emergence, the crowd burst into thunderous applause, singing [songs in praise of the Hobbits](/wiki/Long_live_the_Halflings!_Praise_them_with_great_praise! "Long live the Halflings! Praise them with great praise!"). Frodo and Sam approached a great throne, where Aragorn welcomed them. He lifted them and set them on the throne, and the joy of the people flowed over them like a warm wind. In a regal ceremony, Frodo bequeathed his knife [Sting](/wiki/Sting) to Sam, who initially resisted but finally accepted the gift. That evening, Frodo and Sam attended a generous feast. They reunited with their old companions. Sam was greatly surprised by [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), who seemed to have grown several inches. The next morning, King Aragorn prepared to enter the great city of Minas Tirith as its rightful ruler. <span></span>
