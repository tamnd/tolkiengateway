The **Wold** was the northern part of the [Eastemnet](../E/Eastemnet.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> windy upland<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> plains<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup> between [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md) and the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), bordered to the north by the [Limlight](../L/Limlight.md).<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The Wold was famous for its grass where the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) grazed their herds.<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Prior to the late years of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) the Wold was part of no kingdom; the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of [Lórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) never claimed any land south of the [Limlight](../L/Limlight.md) as part of their realm. When the kingdom of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) was established in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3320](../S/Second_Age_3320.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> the Wold became part of its province [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppendixC_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppendixC-7">[7]</a></sup>

After victory in the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md), [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md), his nephew [Meneldil](../M/Meneldil.md), and a company of trusted friends rode about the borders of Gondor. The Wold was one of their last stops before they journeyed to [Amon Anwar](../H/Halifirien.md) where the tomb of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) was made.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2510](Third_Age_2510.md) the Wold was invaded by a host of the [Balchoth](../B/Balchoth.md) who crossed over the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) from the [Brown Lands](../B/Brown_Lands.md). At the same time Orcs descended upon the Wold from the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) in the west. The [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md) sent messengers north to seek aid and then led Gondor's northern army to repel the invaders. This army had been driven into the [Field of Celebrant](../F/Field_of_Celebrant.md) and was facing annihilation when [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl.md) and his mounted host burst upon the rear of the enemy. The invaders fled across the Limlight and were hunted by Eorl's riders across the Wold.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

### Rohan {#Rohan}

It was after that victory when Calenardhon, including the Wold, was given to Eorl's people and became an independent kingdom. However, in [2545](Third_Age_2545.md) [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) once more attempted to invade the area and King [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md) of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) fell in battle in the Wold.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-10">[10]</a></sup>

The main inhabitants were nomadic Men of Rohan who used the land to graze cattle, although during the years up to the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) the land had become deserted due to [orc-raids](../O/Orcs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [July](../J/July.md) [3018](Third_Age_3018.md) the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) passed northward across the Wold as they began searching for the land of the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md). They returned to the Wold in [September](../S/September.md) empty-handed, where they met messengers from Sauron with orders to ride in haste to [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

From 18<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup> to 20<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup> [February](../F/February.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) passed by the eastern edge of the Wold as they coursed down the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup> Later that month the Fellowship was broken and first the [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md), who had captured [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md),<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> followed by the [Three Hunters](Three_Hunters.md), skirted the western edge of the Wold as they raced towards Fangorn Forest. It was here that [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) met [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md), who was riding back after destroying the Orcs who had crossed the northern plains of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the Wold was invaded by Orcs<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Partings_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-16">[16]</a></sup> from the north on [11 March](../_/11_March.md) [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> but the [Ents](../E/Ents.md) defeated the invading Orcs on [the next day](../_/12_March.md)<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> in the Wold.<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *wold* means high, open and uncultivated land in English.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>
