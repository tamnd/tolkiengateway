The **Wold** was the northern part of the [Eastemnet](/wiki/Eastemnet) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> windy upland<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> plains<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup> between [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest) and the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), bordered to the north by the [Limlight](/wiki/Limlight).<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The Wold was famous for its grass where the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) grazed their herds.<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Prior to the late years of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) the Wold was part of no kingdom; the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Lórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien") never claimed any land south of the [Limlight](/wiki/Limlight) as part of their realm. When the kingdom of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) was established in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> the Wold became part of its province [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon).<sup id="cite_ref-AppendixC_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppendixC-7">[7]</a></sup>

After victory in the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance), [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur), his nephew [Meneldil](/wiki/Meneldil), and a company of trusted friends rode about the borders of Gondor. The Wold was one of their last stops before they journeyed to [Amon Anwar](/wiki/Halifirien "Halifirien") where the tomb of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) was made.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510") the Wold was invaded by a host of the [Balchoth](/wiki/Balchoth) who crossed over the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) from the [Brown Lands](/wiki/Brown_Lands). At the same time Orcs descended upon the Wold from the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) in the west. The [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion) sent messengers north to seek aid and then led Gondor's northern army to repel the invaders. This army had been driven into the [Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Field_of_Celebrant) and was facing annihilation when [Eorl the Young](/wiki/Eorl "Eorl") and his mounted host burst upon the rear of the enemy. The invaders fled across the Limlight and were hunted by Eorl's riders across the Wold.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

### Rohan {#Rohan}

It was after that victory when Calenardhon, including the Wold, was given to Eorl's people and became an independent kingdom. However, in [2545](/wiki/Third_Age_2545 "Third Age 2545") [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) once more attempted to invade the area and King [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) fell in battle in the Wold.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-10">[10]</a></sup>

The main inhabitants were nomadic Men of Rohan who used the land to graze cattle, although during the years up to the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) the land had become deserted due to [orc-raids](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs").<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [July](/wiki/July) [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) passed northward across the Wold as they began searching for the land of the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits). They returned to the Wold in [September](/wiki/September) empty-handed, where they met messengers from Sauron with orders to ride in haste to [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

From 18<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup> to 20<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup> [February](/wiki/February) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) passed by the eastern edge of the Wold as they coursed down the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-River_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-3">[3]</a></sup> Later that month the Fellowship was broken and first the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai), who had captured [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"),<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> followed by the [Three Hunters](/wiki/Three_Hunters), skirted the western edge of the Wold as they raced towards Fangorn Forest. It was here that [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) met [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer), who was riding back after destroying the Orcs who had crossed the northern plains of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the Wold was invaded by Orcs<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Partings_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-16">[16]</a></sup> from the north on [11 March](/wiki/11_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> but the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) defeated the invading Orcs on [the next day](/wiki/12_March "12 March")<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> in the Wold.<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *wold* means high, open and uncultivated land in English.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>
