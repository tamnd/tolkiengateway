The **Trout Bend** was a river valley that lay between the [hills](../R/Rhovanion_Hills.md) of [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) and an unnamed range of hills just slightly north of the [Southlands](../M/Mordor.md). The Trout Bend was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

The Trout Bend was named for the bend in a illegibly named river (--OG) that originated just at the eastern end of the river valley.

## History {#History}

In the late [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), the Trout Bend was a location that a certain [Harfoot community](../H/Harfoots.md) passed by when migrating from one seasonal haven to the next, particularly from the [Norfield Glen](../N/Norfield_Glen.md) to the [Grove](../G/Grove.md).

During one such migration, the Brandyfoot family, [Poppy Proudfellow](../P/Poppy_Proudfellow.md), and [The Stranger](The_Stranger.md) crossed above a waterfall following after the main caravan of the Harfoot community which was far ahead of them.

Later, a group of three [Mystics](../M/Mystics.md) possibly passed through the Trout Bend when they were stalking the Harfoot migration trail, seeking to find the Stranger.
