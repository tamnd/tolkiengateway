**Third Age 2963** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2963**) is the 2963rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2963 was also known as **S.R. 1363** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Théodwyn](Théodwyn.md), fifth child and fourth daughter of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Thengel](Thengel.md) and [Morwen Steelsheen](../M/Morwen_Steelsheen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](../G/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Rorimac Brandybuck](../R/Rorimac_Brandybuck.md), son of Gorbadoc and [Mirabella Took](../M/Mirabella_Took.md), becomes the [Master of Buckland](../M/Master_of_Buckland.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Death of [Linda Baggins](../L/Linda_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Note {#Note}

In the second edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)'s year of birth was added to *[The Tale of Years](../A/Appendix_B.md)* as 2963. This contradicted both the *Longfather Tree of Master Samwise*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and a later entry in *The Tale of Years*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The incorrect date has been corrected (to [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2980](Third_Age_2980.md)) in the [50th anniversary edition](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md>). See also *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_A_Reader's_Companion.md)*, p. 716.

In most editions, [Appendix C](../A/Appendix_C.md) gives [Bingo Baggins](../B/Bingo_Baggins.md)'s death as [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1360](Third_Age_2960.md), but in some later editions, including the 1994 and 50th anniversary editions, the year is mistakenly given as <a class="mw-selflink selflink">1363</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
