**Tol Galen** was the green isle set amidst the waters of the River [Adurant](../A/Adurant.md) in [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After their return to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), [Beren](../B/Beren.md) and [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) dwelt in Tol Galen for the remainder of their time within the confines of [Eä](../E/Eä.md). It was here that their son, [Dior](../D/Dior.md), was born. The country surrounding Tol Galen later came to be known as *[Dor Firn-i-Guinar](../D/Dor_Firn-i-Guinar.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the death of [Thingol](Thingol.md), Beren left Tol Galen and, with his son and a host of Green-elves, came to the north of the River [Ascar](../A/Ascar.md). At [Sarn Athrad](../S/Sarn_Athrad.md) they assailed the [Dwarves of Nogrod](../D/Dwarves_of_Nogrod.md) returning from [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) with their plunders. The Dwarves were defeated and all the treasure was thrown into the Ascar, save one. The [Silmaril](../S/Silmarils.md) from the crown of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), set within the [Nauglamír](../N/Nauglamír.md), came back into the hands of Beren. He took it back to Tol Galen. It is told that when Lúthien wore the Necklace of the Dwarves 'the Land of the Dead that Live became like a vision of the land of the Valar, and no place has been since so fair, so fruitful, or so filled with light.'<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tol Galen* means "The Green Isle",<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> consisting of the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) words *[tol](Tol.md)* ("island") + *[calen](../C/Calen.md)* ("green").<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkiendil_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkiendil-5">[5]</a></sup>
