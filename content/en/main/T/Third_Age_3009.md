**Third Age 3009** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3009**) is the 3009th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 3009 was also known as **S.R. 1409** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) renew their hunt for [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) at intervals during the next eight years, searching in the vales of [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md), and [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) to the confines of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). At some time during these years Gollum himself ventured into [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md), and was captured by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Dudo Baggins](../D/Dudo_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Note {#Note}

As of [2015](../_/2015.md), the published version of *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* includes an entry for the year 3009 that [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) called for [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) to return to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md). Following research by [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md), [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) agrees that this is in error and the correct year should be [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3016](Third_Age_3016.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Tolkien Gateway reflects this. <span></span>
