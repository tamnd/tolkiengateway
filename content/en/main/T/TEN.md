**TEN** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "direction".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[tenna](Tenna.md)*;<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup> *[tenne](Ten~56220.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

### TEƷ {#TEƷ}

In the [*Etymologies*](The_Etymologies.md) appears the root TEƷ- ("line, direction"). From this root derives:<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md): *teʒē*
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[tie](Tië.md)*
    - [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *tê* ("line, way")

- \[see TEÑ<sup>1</sup>\]
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *téra* ("straight, right")
    - [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *tîr* ("straight, right")

### TEÑ<sup>1</sup> and TEÑ<sup>2</sup> {#TEÑ1_and_TEÑ2}

According to [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), the root TEƷ- was changed to TEÑ. In this new entry, Quenya *téra* and Noldorin *tîr* derived from Primitive Quendian *teñrā*,<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup> where an intermediate form appears to be *tēra*.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

There exists furthermore an additional entry on TEÑ in the *Etymologies* (for the sake of clarity, here called TEÑ<sup>2</sup>), yielding the derivatives Noldorin *tî* ("line, row"), *tiwdi* ("alphabet"), *tær*, *taer* ("straight"), and [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *téma* ("row, series, line"), *tea* ("straight line, road").<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

Note that there also exists a root [TEÑ](TEÑ.md), with a different signification, in the manuscript "[Eldarin Roots and Stems](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_17.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

### √*ten* {#√ten}

The complexity of the root TEN is has been discussed at length by [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md). A very late (ca. 1968) manuscript by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) includes an almost identical rendering of √*ten* as given in the version from "[Eldarin Roots and Stems](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_17.md)" (that is, the version used as the first definition in this article), which, however, was changed to a √*men* (yielding different derivatives). An earlier manuscript (late 1950s or early 1960s) by Tolkien also contains a mention of a √*ten-*, having a similar (but not identical) description.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
