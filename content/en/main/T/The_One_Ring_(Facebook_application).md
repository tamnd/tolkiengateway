**The One Ring** is a licensed game produced by Microcosm Games. It is published as a Facebook application.

## Overview {#Overview}

Players take on the role of any of several heroes from *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. As the game progresses, their character will grow in power, improving their abilities, gaining companions, and finding new equipment.
