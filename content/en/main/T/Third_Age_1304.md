**Third Age 1304** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1304**) is the 1304th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Calmacil](<../C/Calmacil_(King_of_Gondor).md>) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Calmacil](<../C/Calmacil_(King_of_Gondor).md>)'s son, [Regent](../R/Regent_of_Gondor.md) Minalcar, becomes the nineteenth [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) as [Rómendacil II](../R/Rómendacil_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>
