**Third Age 2996** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2996**) is the 2996th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2996 was also known as **S.R. 1396** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Minto Burrows](../M/Minto_Burrows.md), fourth child and third son of [Milo Burrows](../M/Milo_Burrows.md) and [Peony Baggins](../P/Peony_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
