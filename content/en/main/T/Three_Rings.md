The **Three Rings** were magical artifacts, three of the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power). They were forged by [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor) and granted the power to preserve<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L131_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L131-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L144_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L144-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Council_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-5">[5]</a></sup> the beauty<sup id="cite_ref-L131_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L131-3">[3]</a></sup> of things by stopping change and warding off the decay of time<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and postponing the weariness of the world.<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup> They had the greatest power of the Rings of Power. The Three Rings were named **[Vilya](/wiki/Vilya)**, **[Narya](/wiki/Narya)**, and **[Nenya](/wiki/Nenya)** after the principal elements of air, fire, and water (respectively).<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup>

The other [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power) had powers, which were more directly derived from Sauron, like making their wearers invisible or making things from the invisible world visible for their wearers, but the Three Rings did not make their wearers invisible.<sup id="cite_ref-L131_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L131-3">[3]</a></sup> [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) did not assist in their making nor ever did he touch them, and his taint was not directly upon them, but, as they were partly created according to the craft taught by him,<sup id="cite_ref-L144_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L144-4">[4]</a></sup> they were under the control of [The One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L144_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L144-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Names and descriptions {#Names_and_descriptions}

- **[Nenya](/wiki/Nenya)**, the **Ring of Adamant**<sup id="cite_ref-Mirror_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mirror-7">[7]</a></sup> and the **Ring of Water**,<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup> was made of *[mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril")* with a stone of adamant;<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup> it was the chief of the Three,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> originally — and only ever — worn by [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel).
- **[Narya](/wiki/Narya)**, the **Ring of Fire** and the **Red Ring**, set with a ruby,<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup> originally worn by [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan)<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup> who gave it to [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-9">[9]</a></sup>
- **[Vilya](/wiki/Vilya)**, the **Ring of Sapphire**, **Blue Ring**, and the **Ring of Air**, and mightiest of the Three,<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-10">[10]</a></sup> a ring of gold with a sapphire stone,<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup> originally worn by [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) but given to [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond).<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Rings were named after the three principal elements, each matching the colour of their stones. They were originally given to the three greatest [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) at that time.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup> Their names also match the fates of the three [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup> However, not much is known about their specific powers and whether they had anything to do with each element.

## History {#History}

After "[Annatar](/wiki/Annatar)" taught and assisted the [Gwaith-i-Mirdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-Mirdain) with creating the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power), he left [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion). Celebrimbor went on to forge another Three Rings alone, with the knowledge he had gained from Annatar. This was completed by around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1590](/wiki/Second_Age_1590 "Second Age 1590").<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> These Three were "unsullied," never touched by the hand of Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup> Sauron learned of their making, and desired to possess these Three over all the others.<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup>

When Sauron created the One Ring, the Elves who wore the Rings of Power heard his voice, realized they had been deceived, and took off their rings.<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Jenny_Dolfen_-_Ringbearers.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/7b/Jenny_Dolfen_-_Ringbearers.jpg/300px-Jenny_Dolfen_-_Ringbearers.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="400" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/7/7b/Jenny_Dolfen_-_Ringbearers.jpg/450px-Jenny_Dolfen_-_Ringbearers.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/7/7b/Jenny_Dolfen_-_Ringbearers.jpg 2x" data-file-width="500" data-file-height="667"/></a><figcaption><i>Ringbearers</i> by <a href="/wiki/Jenny_Dolfen" title="Jenny Dolfen">Jenny Dolfen</a></figcaption></figure>

As the Elves failed him, Sauron [waged war](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron "War of the Elves and Sauron") against Eregion in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1693](/wiki/Second_Age_1693 "Second Age 1693"). Celebrimbor immediately sent his three rings away for safekeeping:<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> he gave Nenya to [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), who advised him to send the other two to [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), away from Sauron. He gave Vilya to [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor "High King of the Noldor") [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) and Narya to [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan).<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup>

Sauron succeeded in claiming the Rings of Power. But the Three remained outside his grasp and their whereabouts were seldom revealed.

After Sauron's defeat in the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) at the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the [Keepers of the Three Rings](/wiki/Keepers_of_the_Three_Rings) were free to wear them. During the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the Rings were not idle.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup> They were used to create enclaves of peace and protection where memory and beauty were preserved from wear and decay.<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_2-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L131_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L131-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) found out that Galadriel held Nenya, using it to preserve [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) from withering, and [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) reprimanded him for revealing this. The other two rings were revealed at the end of the Third Age, after Frodo destroyed [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), and the Dark Lord Sauron was completely overthrown. It transpired that [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) had wielded [Vilya](/wiki/Vilya) and presumably used its powers to keep [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) a haven safe from attack. It was [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) who wielded [Narya](/wiki/Narya), given to him by [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup>

With the destruction of the One Ring on [25 March](/wiki/25_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup> the Three Rings lost their power.<sup id="cite_ref-L144_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L144-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Council_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[16]</a></sup> Not being able to hold back time and preserve the High-elven lore and beauty, their bearers, in the [Last Riding of the Keepers of the Rings](/wiki/Last_Riding_of_the_Keepers_of_the_Rings), left [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) with the Three Rings on the [White Ship](/wiki/White_Ship) on [29 September](/wiki/29_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021").<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[18]</a></sup> The [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) ended and the [Dominion of Men](/wiki/Dominion_of_Men) began.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early version of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* the Three Rings were known as [Kemen](/wiki/Kemen), [Ëar](/wiki/E%C3%A4r "Eär"), and [Menel](/wiki/Menel). <sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[19]</a></sup>

### Films {#Films}

<b>1978: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)">Ralph Bakshi&#39;s <i>The Lord of the Rings</i></a>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the introduction of the movie, the Three Rings are said to be made &#34;for the tall Elf Kings&#34; as three unidentified silhouettes are seen holding and lifting the Rings.</dd></dl>

<b>2001-3: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the prologue to the <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">first film</a>, the Three Rings are show briefly being borne by <a href="/wiki/Galadriel" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gil-galad" title="Gil-galad">Gil-galad</a> and <a href="/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan" title="Círdan">Círdan</a>; later in the film Galadriel reveals to <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a> that she possesses <a href="/wiki/Nenya" title="Nenya">Nenya</a>. In the final film, although <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> can be seen wearing <a href="/wiki/Narya" title="Narya">Narya</a>, its significance is never stated.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies_(extended_edition)" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies (extended edition)">Extended Edition</a> of the film, we see Narya on Gandalf&#39;s finger as an <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orc</a> attempts to cut off his hand in an attempt to claim it for <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>. However, Galadriel arrives to rescue Gandalf and vanquishes the Orc in the nick of time. She can be seen wielding Nenya on her finger throughout the scene. During the subsequent battle against the <a href="/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl" title="Nazgûl">Nazgûl</a>, Vilya can be glimpsed on Elrond&#39;s finger.</dd></dl>

### Television {#Television}

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a></i>, Season One:</b>

<dl><dd><b>Episode 8: <i><a href="/wiki/Alloyed" title="Alloyed">Alloyed</a></i>:</b></dd>
<dd>In this adaptation, various events found in the books are compressed, rearranged and/or omitted. The Three Rings are forged immediately after the departure of Annatar/<a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> from Eregion, but the other sixteen Rings of Power are either not discussed or not yet created. In this version, the Three Rings are not presented as the culmination of the <a href="/wiki/Gwaith-i-M%C3%ADrdain" title="Gwaith-i-Mírdain">Elven-smiths</a>&#39; prior work on rings. The new, magical properties of <a href="/wiki/Mithril" title="Mithril">mithril</a> made up for the series serve as the main representation of all <a href="/wiki/Magic" title="Magic">magical craft</a> done by the Elven-smiths.</dd>
<dd>The concept that became the Three Rings was first thought of as a crown to be put upon the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Gil-Galad" title="Gil-Galad">High King Gil-Galad</a>&#39;s brow, as they needed something circular to keep the power going in an unbroken round. Yet, since there ultimately was not enough <a href="/wiki/Mithril" title="Mithril">mithril</a> to do this, the concept changed to something smaller: two <a href="/wiki/Rings_of_Power" title="Rings of Power">Rings of Power</a>. After Galadriel found out about <a href="/wiki/Halbrand" title="Halbrand">Halbrand</a> being Sauron from an <a href="/wiki/Lambengolmor" title="Lambengolmor">Elven Loremaster</a>, she tells <a href="/wiki/Celebrimbor" title="Celebrimbor">Lord Celebrimbor</a> that they cannot make two rings, for she says that &#34;one will only corrupt&#34; and that &#34;two will always divide.&#34; &#34;With three,&#34; Celebrimbor responds, &#34;there is balance.&#34;</dd>
<dd>However, at the same time, Celebrimbor reveals that the mithril will not bond with the lesser ores, unless they be pure, naming <a href="/wiki/Gold" title="Gold">gold</a> and <a href="/wiki/Silver" title="Silver">silver</a> from <a href="/wiki/Valinor" title="Valinor">Valinor</a> as an example, but clearly meaning that he needs <a href="/wiki/Finrod" title="Finrod">Finrod&#39;s dagger</a>. Galadriel somewhat reluctantly agrees. When the melting is done, Galadriel states that the power that they craft here must be solely for the <a href="/wiki/Elves" title="Elves">Elves</a>. Upon taking the melted dagger out of the furnace and onto a spinning device, Elrond provides the mithril ore that <a href="/wiki/Durin_IV" title="Durin IV">Prince Durin IV</a> had given him as a token of their friendship. The mithril melts into the dagger, and the liquid is poured into three small steel cups to outline the physical appearance of the future rings. These outlines are then taken by the Elven-smiths, and bent into the shape of the Three Rings. Celebrimbor then binds jewels to each ring. The Ring&#39;s names and properties are never mentioned.</dd></dl>
