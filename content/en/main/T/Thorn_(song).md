**Thorn** is the fifteenth track of the [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-themed album [Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth) by [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian). The song tells of [Maeglin](/wiki/Maeglin), the son of [Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l). Maeglin reflects on his place in the world and his decision to betray the location of the hidden city [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) to [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
