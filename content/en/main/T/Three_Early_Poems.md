<i><b>Three Early Poems</b></i> collects three [poems](/wiki/Index:Poems_by_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Poems by J.R.R. Tolkien") by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) first published in the 1920s:

- "[The Cat and the Fiddle](/wiki/The_Man_in_the_Moon_Stayed_Up_Too_Late "The Man in the Moon Stayed Up Too Late")" - from *[Yorkshire Poetry](/wiki/Yorkshire_Poetry)*
- "[The Princess Ní](/wiki/The_Princess_N%C3%AD)" - from *[Leeds University Verse 1914-24](/wiki/Leeds_University_Verse_1914-24)*
- "[An Evening in Tavrobel](/wiki/An_Evening_in_Tavrobel)" - from *[Leeds University Verse 1914-24](/wiki/Leeds_University_Verse_1914-24)*

The pamphlet was privately printed in a numbered edition of ten copies.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
