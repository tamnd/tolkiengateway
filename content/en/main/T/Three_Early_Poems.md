<i><b>Three Early Poems</b></i> collects three [poems](../../index/P/Poems_by_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) first published in the 1920s:

- "[The Cat and the Fiddle](The_Man_in_the_Moon_Stayed_Up_Too_Late.md)" - from *[Yorkshire Poetry](../Y/Yorkshire_Poetry.md)*
- "[The Princess Ní](The_Princess_Ní.md)" - from *[Leeds University Verse 1914-24](../L/Leeds_University_Verse_1914-24.md)*
- "[An Evening in Tavrobel](../A/An_Evening_in_Tavrobel.md)" - from *[Leeds University Verse 1914-24](../L/Leeds_University_Verse_1914-24.md)*

The pamphlet was privately printed in a numbered edition of ten copies.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
