**Third Age 1601** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1601**) is the 1601st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1601 was also known as **S.R. 1** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Led by the [Fallohide](../F/Fallohides.md) brothers [Marcho](../M/Marcho.md) and [Blanco](../B/Blanco.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-1">[1]</a></sup> many [Periannath](../H/Hobbits.md) migrate from [Bree](../B/Bree.md), and are granted land beyond [Baranduin](../B/Baranduin.md) by [Argeleb II](../A/Argeleb_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-2">[2]</a></sup>
