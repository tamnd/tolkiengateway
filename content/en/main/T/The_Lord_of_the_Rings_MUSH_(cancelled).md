**The Lord of the Rings MUSH** is a cancelled video game. It was to be the succesor of a [game with the same name](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_MUSH_(1993_video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings MUSH (1993 video game)"). In story and design it was similar to the original, but the coding was significantly improved in comparison with the other game.It was cancelled for unknown reasons.
