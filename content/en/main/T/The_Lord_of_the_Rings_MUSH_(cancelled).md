**The Lord of the Rings MUSH** is a cancelled video game. It was to be the succesor of a [game with the same name](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_MUSH_(1993_video_game).md>). In story and design it was similar to the original, but the coding was significantly improved in comparison with the other game.It was cancelled for unknown reasons.
