The **Two Captains** was a title that was applicable to both [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) and [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin), during the time in which they established the land of [Dor-Cúarthol](/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol) in resistance to [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in [Angband](/wiki/Angband).<sup id="cite_ref-Captains_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captains-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Two Captains were the two guardians of [Dor-Cúarthol](/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol) and the leaders of the [Old Company](/wiki/Gaurwaith "Gaurwaith"). The title was first given to both [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) and [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) by the leaderless [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Men](/wiki/Men) who, upon finding themselves dispossessed through battles and defeats, came to [Dor-Cúarthol](/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol) seeking the Bow and Helm that were believed to have supposedly fallen during the many battles within [Dimbar](/wiki/Dimbar).<sup id="cite_ref-Captains_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captains-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the following of the Two Captains had become a great force, word had reached as far as [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) of their success in hindering and resisting the forces of Angband. So much so that the [King of Nargothrond](/wiki/King_of_Nargothrond), [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) himself, sent messengers to the Two Captains, offering his aid in any matter that they should have any need in other than in arms. Yet, despite this, he not only forbade the Two Captains from setting foot in Nargothrond, but charged them that they may not drive [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) to do so either.<sup id="cite_ref-Captains_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captains-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
