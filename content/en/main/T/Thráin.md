**Thráin II** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [2644](/wiki/Third_Age_2644 "Third Age 2644") – [2850](/wiki/Third_Age_2850 "Third Age 2850"), aged 206 years) was King of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) for 60 years, from [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2790](/wiki/Third_Age_2790 "Third Age 2790") to [2850](/wiki/Third_Age_2850 "Third Age 2850"), during their exile from [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain). He was the son of [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) and father of [Thorin II](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin"), [Frerin](/wiki/Frerin), and [Dís](/wiki/D%C3%ADs). Thorin II would later be known as [Thorin Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin").<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Thráin fled with his father and a small group of companions when the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770") descended on Lonely Mountain and sacked the [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") kingdom. King Thrór, Thráin, and the others eventually settled in [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland) and scratched out a meager living. Thrór was devastated by the loss of [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") and left his people to journey north. He took a single companion, [Nár](/wiki/N%C3%A1r), but left his son with his [Ring of Power](/wiki/Ring_of_Thr%C3%B3r "Ring of Thrór"), along with the [map](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r%27s_Map "Thrór's Map") and key to Lonely Mountain.

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2790](/wiki/Third_Age_2790 "Third Age 2790")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> Nár returned to tell Thráin that his father had been captured and butchered by the [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs")-chieftain [Azog](/wiki/Azog) when they had journeyed to the mines of [Moria](/wiki/Moria). Even worse, Azog had beheaded Thrór and carved his own name on Thrór's forehead to show the Dwarves that an Orc now ruled their ancestral home.

Thráin sat for seven days without speaking, until he stood and said "This cannot be borne!".<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

### War of the Dwarves and Orcs {#War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs}

Filled with righteous anger, by [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2793](/wiki/Third_Age_2793 "Third Age 2793")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> Thráin had gathered together a massive army of Dwarves to wage the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs). The army included the exiles of the Lonely Mountain, Thráin's [kin](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills "Dwarves of the Iron Hills") from the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) under his uncle [Grór](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3r), and even some Dwarves not of Durin's folk. One by one they assaulted the Orc-holds of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), destroying their great warrens in [Mount Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad "Gundabad") and eventually facing Azog himself in [Dimrill Dale](/wiki/Dimrill_Dale) (*Azanulbizar* in [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul)) before the [East-gate of Moria](/wiki/Great_Gates "Great Gates").

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2799](/wiki/Third_Age_2799 "Third Age 2799")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> Thráin and his army fought the bloody [Battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar). Thráin was blinded in one eye and suffered a leg-wound. While the Orcs were vanquished and Azog slain, the Dwarves took heavy losses, including Thráin's son Frerin, his cousin [Náin](/wiki/N%C3%A1in_son_of_Gr%C3%B3r "Náin son of Grór"), and [Fundin](/wiki/Fundin) father of Dwarven hero [Balin](/wiki/Balin).

King Thráin II wanted to enter Moria and reclaim it, but the Dwarves not of Durin's folk refused, saying that the city was not their Fathers' House; they had honoured Thrór's memory by fighting, and this was enough. [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot) had peered within the [East-gate](/wiki/Great_Gates "Great Gates") and also warned Thráin that [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane) still dwelt within Khazad-dûm.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Wanderings and imprisonment {#Wanderings_and_imprisonment}

After the war, Thráin and Thorin led the exiles of Lonely Mountain west to live in the southern [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) where they settled in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2802](/wiki/Third_Age_2802 "Third Age 2802").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> Over the next forty years Thráin grew increasingly consumed with the lost riches of the Lonely Mountain. This may have been partly because of the [Ring of Thrór](/wiki/Ring_of_Thr%C3%B3r).

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2841](/wiki/Third_Age_2841 "Third Age 2841")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> he left the Blue Mountains with a small group that included [Balin](/wiki/Balin) and [Dwalin](/wiki/Dwalin). Travelling east, the group was constantly harassed by wolves, Orcs, and other fell creatures. In [2845](/wiki/Third_Age_2845 "Third Age 2845"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> while camped under the eaves of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood), Thráin disappeared and would never be seen again by his kin. The King of Durin's folk had been captured by Sauron's agents and was taken to the fortress of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) in southern Mirkwood. There Sauron tortured Thráin, took back the last of the [seven Dwarf-rings](/wiki/Seven_Rings "Seven Rings"), and left him in the dungeon to die.

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2850](/wiki/Third_Age_2850 "Third Age 2850"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> while on a reconnaissance mission to Dol Guldur, [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) came upon Thráin, who was so diminished that he could not even remember his own name. Thráin gave Gandalf his last two possessions, the key and map to the Lonely Mountain, and shortly thereafter died. Gandalf left him without knowing who he was.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Legacy {#Legacy}

Much later Gandalf met Thorin Oakenshield near [Bree](/wiki/Bree) and realized that the Dwarf he had found in Dol Guldur was Thráin, the vanished King of Durin's folk. Gandalf and Thorin discussed reclaiming Lonely Mountain, and Gandalf gave much assistance to [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company), including the map and key to Lonely Mountain, along with a suggestion that they include a [burglar](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") in their quest.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

Gandalf mentioned to the Fellowship, as they were debating about going into Moria, that he had been in the mines before looking for Thráin.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Þráinn* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. It means "Stubborn".<sup id="cite_ref-IE_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IE-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) noted that *Thráin* went stubbornly off to Erebor again.<sup id="cite_ref-IE_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IE-4">[4]</a></sup>

<b>2012-14: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The young Thráin  during the Dwarves&#39; reign at <a href="/wiki/Lonely_Mountain" title="Lonely Mountain">Erebor</a> is portrayed by <a href="/wiki/Thomas_Robins" title="Thomas Robins">Thomas Robins</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>  During the scenes from the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar" title="Battle of Azanulbizar">Battle of Azanulbizar</a> in the same film, he is allegedly played by <a href="/wiki/Mike_Mizrahi" title="Mike Mizrahi">Mike Mizrahi</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting-7">[7]</a></sup> though no footage seems to appear of him in the Theatrical Version of the films. Finally, in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_(extended_edition)" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug (extended edition)">Extended Edition</a> of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>, he is portrayed by <a href="/wiki/Antony_Sher" title="Antony Sher">Antony Sher</a> at old age in Dol Guldur, and by Mike Mizrahi at young age, during the Battle of Azanulbizar.</dd></dl>

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the Extended Edition of this film, Thráin Is first seen in a flashback to the Battle of Azanulbizar during the prologue while <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin</a> discuss his possible whereabouts. In this flashback, Thráin insists Thorin stay back as he leads a charge toward the <a href="/wiki/Great_Gates" title="Great Gates">Great Gates</a> (though he is not seen again during the battle or its aftermath).</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Old and delirious, Thráin is again seen later at <a href="/wiki/Dol_Guldur" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a> when Gandalf arrives to investigate. He tracks the <a href="/wiki/Wizards" title="Wizards">Wizard</a>, and ultimately attacks him in a fit of madness. Gandalf is able to overtake him and calm him. Thráin warns Gandalf to not let Thorin near the <a href="/wiki/Lonely_Mountain" title="Lonely Mountain">Lonely Mountain</a> because he has learned that <a href="/wiki/Smaug" title="Smaug">Smaug</a> is in league with <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">The One</a>. He ultimately helps Gandalf navigate through the fortress before <a href="/wiki/Azog" title="Azog">Azog</a> and several other <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> attack. The two of them try to escape but are eventually stopped by the shadow of the <a href="/wiki/Sauron#Sauron&#39;s_Return" title="Sauron">Necromancer</a>. Thráin tells Gandalf to tell Thorin that he loved him, and is swallowed up the shadow, which then reveals itself as <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> to Gandalf.</dd></dl>

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/Guardians_of_Middle-earth" title="Guardians of Middle-earth">Guardians of Middle-earth</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Thráin is a warrior-type &#34;guardian&#34; with four abilities: <i>Hammer throw</i>, <i>Dwarven valor</i>, <i>Lethal blow</i> and <i>Durin&#39;s Wrath</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2021: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Thráin appears in an extended flashback depicting the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar" title="Battle of Azanulbizar">Battle of Azanulbizar</a>, which closely follows the description given in the books.</dd></dl>
