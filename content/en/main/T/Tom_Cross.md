**Tom Cross** ([8 February](/wiki/8_February) [1954](/wiki/1954) - [27 December](/wiki/27_December) [2008](/wiki/2008)) was an American environmental activist and fantasy artist with a focus on [Fairies](/wiki/Fairies).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tribune_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tribune-2">[2]</a></sup> He created created numerous [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-inspired illustrations, some of them for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).
