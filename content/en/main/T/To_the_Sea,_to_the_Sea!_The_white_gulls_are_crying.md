"**To the Sea, to the Sea! The white gulls are crying**" is a poem sung by [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) while leaving his partners at the end of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Cormallen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cormallen-1">[1]</a></sup>

As warned by [Galadriel's messages](../G/Galadriel's_messages.md), [sea-longing](../S/Sea-longing.md) had appeared in Legolas's heart after seeing the sea at [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) and hearing the song of the [gulls](../G/Gulls.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Here he is saying goodbye to the Fellowship, before following the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) to the [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md) to fulfill his desire.<sup id="cite_ref-Cormallen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cormallen-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Poem {#Poem}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>To the Sea, to the Sea! The white gulls are crying,<br/>
The wind is blowing, and the white foam is flying.<br/>
West, west away, the round sun is falling.<br/>
Grey ship, grey ship, do you hear them calling,<br/>
The voices of my people that have gone before me?<br/>
I will leave, I will leave the woods that bore me;<br/>
For our days are ending and our years failing.<br/>
I will pass the wide waters lonely sailing.<br/>
Long are the waves on the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../L/Last_Shore.md" title="Last Shore">Last Shore</a> falling,<br/>
Sweet are the voices in the <a href="Tol_Eressëa.md" title="Tol Eressëa">Lost Isle</a> calling,<br/>
In Eressëa, in <a href="../E/Elvenhome.md" title="Elvenhome">Elvenhome</a> that no man can discover,<br/>
Where the leaves fall not: land of my people for ever!
</p>
</div>
