The **prefatory note**, labeled **Author's Note** in some editions, refers to the untitled preface placed before the first chapter of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.

The prefatory note starts with *THE HOBBIT OR THERE AND BACK AGAIN* written in [Cirth](/wiki/Cirth). It proceeds to discuss some issues related to the words *[dwarf](/wiki/Dwarf "Dwarf")* and *[orc](/wiki/Orc "Orc")*. Then it gives a short description of the Cirth writing system used in *The Hobbit*. The letters of the [moon-runes](/wiki/Moon-runes "Moon-runes"), read by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), are provided in the end.

## History {#History}

The note was first added to the <a class="external text" href="http://tolkienbooks.net/php/details.php?reference=21200" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">second edition of <i>The Hobbit</i></a> ([1951](/wiki/1951)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In [1966](/wiki/1966), a completely revised prefatory note first appeared in the <a class="external text" href="http://tolkienbooks.net/php/details.php?reference=21450" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Longmans/Unwin</a> edition.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The latter text has been used since.
