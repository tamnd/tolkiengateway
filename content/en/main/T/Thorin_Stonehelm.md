**Thorin III Stonehelm** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) 2866 – [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age)) was the son and heir of [Dáin II Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot") of [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk), who was king of the [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor) and the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) in [Wilderland](/wiki/Rhovanion "Rhovanion").

## History {#History}

During the [Lordship](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Iron_Hills "Lord of the Iron Hills") of his father, Thorin was more than likely born in the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) to Dáin II Ironfoot. During his younger years he saw his people able to finally return to the Lonely Mountain after many years of further exile, and wandering. He himself became a prince with the rise of his father as [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain), and over all the [Longbeards](/wiki/Longbeards).

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) at the age of one hundred fifty-three, Thorin fought the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) in the [Battle of Dale](/wiki/Battle_of_Dale), and took refuge in Erebor after his father was killed at its gates, along with [Brand](/wiki/Brand) [king](/wiki/King_of_Dale "King of Dale") of [Dale](/wiki/Dale). Thorin then became [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain).

When the news of Sauron's [defeat in the South](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon "Battle of the Morannon") came to the ears of the enemy, they became dismayed and the Dwarves and Men came forth from the mountain and routed them driving them back of the river [Carnen](/wiki/Carnen), never troubling them again.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-3">[2]</a></sup>

Under Thorin's leadership Erebor and Dale were rebuilt along with Dale's new king [Bard II](/wiki/Bard_II), son of Brand, and once again both their people prospered, and their realms became close allies with the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom) of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) of King [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn").

During his rule [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), son of [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in), led a number of Dwarves South to [Aglarond](/wiki/Aglarond), where a new colony was settled. Also during his rule mining for *[mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril")* in [Moria](/wiki/Moria) slowly began again, although Moria was not yet permanently settled.

Thorin III had a descendant who was held to be the reincarnation of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a>, and would rule as [Durin VII](/wiki/Durin_VII) the Last.<sup id="cite_ref-Making_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Making-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Thorin's name is one from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. It means "Bold".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

The origins of the epithet "[Stonehelm](/wiki/Stonehelm)" are mysterious: [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") doesn't provide us with any account that would shed light on its meaning.

<dl><dd>A Decipher card identifies one of the Dwarves at the Council of Elrond in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i> as Thorin III.</dd></dl>
<p><b>2018: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>
</p>
<dl><dd>Thorin III Stonehelm rules <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Erebor" title="Erebor">Erebor</a> after the passing of his father <a href="/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot" title="Dáin Ironfoot">Dáin Ironfoot</a>, and is central to various questlines and stories taking place in <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a>, <a href="/wiki/Eryn_Lasgalen" title="Eryn Lasgalen">Eryn Lasgalen</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Dale" title="Dale">Dale</a> lands, the <a href="/wiki/Iron_Hills" title="Iron Hills">Iron Hills</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ered_Mithrin" title="Ered Mithrin">Ered Mithrin</a>. &#34;Stonehelm&#34; was originally a derisive nickname used by those who disagreed with his decision to retreat and seal the gates of Erebor, but after proving his worthiness he adopts it as a symbol of steadfastness and strength.</dd></dl>
<dl><dd>His son is named Durin, but it remains to be seen if this Durin will become King <a href="/wiki/Durin_VII" title="Durin VII">Durin VII</a>. His younger brother is &#34;Náin the Slakeless&#34;, Lord of the Iron Hills.</dd></dl>
<p><b>2023: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Return_to_Moria" title="The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria">The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria</a></i>:</b>
</p>
<dl><dd>Thorin is first mentioned by an Emissary from <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Erebor" title="Erebor">Erebor</a>, stating that Thorin objected Gimli&#39;s call to return to <a href="/wiki/Moria" title="Moria">Moria</a>. Thorin later appears congratulating Gimli and the player character, proclaiming the player character the new Lord of Moria.</dd></dl>
