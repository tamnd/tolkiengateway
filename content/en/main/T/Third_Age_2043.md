**Third Age 2043** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2043**) is the 2043rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2043 was also known as **S.R. 443** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Eärnil II](../E/Eärnil_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md), son of Eärnil II, becomes the thirty-third King of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md) is challenged by the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Mardil](../M/Mardil.md) the Steward restrains the King's wrath.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
