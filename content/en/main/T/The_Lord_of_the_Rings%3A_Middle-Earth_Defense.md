<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: Middle-Earth Defense</b></i> is a tower-defence game for the iPhone and iPod Touch, created by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Glu_Games&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Glu Games (page does not exist)">Glu Games</a>. It offers 18 stages, stretched across seven memorable locations from the books and film trilogy, such as the Tower of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc) and [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).
