<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Online: Rise of Isengard</b></i> is an expansion for *[The Lord of the Rings Online](The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)* covering the rise of [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) and the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md)'s arrival in [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md). Three new regions - [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md), Dunland and the [Gap of Rohan](../G/Gap_of_Rohan.md) - will be released, the level cap will be increased up to level 75, and other new additions, including a new monster play region and a 24-man raid.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The game was released on [September 27](../_/27_September.md), [2011](../_/2011.md).

## Storyline {#Storyline}

### Book IV - Rise of Isengard {#Book_IV_-_Rise_of_Isengard}

Passing into the lands of Dunland, the Rangers investigate the loyalties of the local tribes of [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md). Some are fiercely loyal to Saruman, other openly oppose him and many have not yet been convinced to do either. The Falcon-clan of Tur Morva village offers the Rangers shelter in exchange for their help against Saruman. The Rangers busy themselves with preparations for war while the player is sent to scout the rest of Dunland. Finding a surprise ally in Nona, who insists that she does not need [Halbarad](../H/Halbarad.md)'s permission to travel over her own land, the player and the Rangers come into first contact with the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md). Riders led by [Grimbold](../G/Grimbold.md) hold the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md) against inevitable assault, while [Théodred](Théodred.md), son of King [Théoden](Théoden.md), leads a smaller scouting party into the lands of the Dunlendings, who have been sworn enemies of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) for centuries. Returning to the village of Tur Morva, the player finds a betrayal waiting for them: the clan decided that more is to be gained by siding with Saruman than by opposing him. The Grey Company is caught unprepared, many Rangers are killed, and the rest captured.

The player and Ranger Lothrandir are sent directly to Isengard as a gift to Saruman. Separated from the Ranger, the player is sent to work in the pits, observed by merciless [Uruks](../U/Uruk-hai.md). Using the dislike of Uruks for each other, the player fakes total submission and is eventually allowed more freedom, as none believe him able to escape any longer. The player finds other prisoners, among them captured Rohirrim and Dunlendings - sworn enemies once, both find something in common after being made slaves to Saruman. Staging a diversion and freeing many prisoners at once, the player is able to sneak past the confusion and escape Isengard.

Théodred offers the Rangers Saeradan and Radanir help in rescuing their captive friends and avenging the betrayal. The player and Rohirrim storm the village of traitors and rescue many Rangers, including Halbarad and the sons of [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md). Yet they are still outnumbered - the leader of the traitorous clan gloats over Ranger's weaknesses and allows them to leave, telling them to spread the tale of how the Falcon-clan beat them. Of almost 60 named Rangers barely 30 made it out alive: Halbarad takes an oath to avenge the traitors and proclaims himself in eternal debt to Théodred, before planning the next move.

## Updates {#Updates}

### Update 5: Armies of Isengard {#Update_5:_Armies_of_Isengard}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_5_Armies_of_Isengard.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/67/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_5_Armies_of_Isengard.png/220px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_5_Armies_of_Isengard.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="220" height="190" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/67/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_5_Armies_of_Isengard.png/330px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_5_Armies_of_Isengard.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/67/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_5_Armies_of_Isengard.png/440px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_5_Armies_of_Isengard.png 2x" data-file-width="480" data-file-height="415"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Armies of Isengard* was the first update published after the release of *The Lord of the Rings Online: Rise of Isengard*. It was released on [12 December](../_/12_December.md) [2011](../_/2011.md), and included Book V - The Prince of Rohan.

The secrecy required by Grey Company's errand and their weakened state following capture and imprisonment prevent them from continuing their journey for the time being. Finishing his scouting of Dunlending lands, Prince Théodred returns to the Fords of Isen and Halbarad bids the player to go with him, to repay the debt. Saying farewell to old friends, the player arrives at [Grimbold](../G/Grimbold.md)'s encampment and joins the preparations for the upcoming battle. Théodred shares his plan: the Rohirrim are to journey north to [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) and smash the vanguard of Saruman's forces, then retreat to the Ford, forcing the Wizard to delay his assault on Rohan and buy themselves valuable time.

At dawn, the Prince leaves a small company to guard the Ford and leads his Éored into battle, with Grimbold and the player at his side. They encounter the vanguard of the Uruks and quickly cut through their lines, but then reinforcements come out of Isengard and outflank the cavalry, nearly surrounding them. Théodred orders a retreat, covered by Grimbold and the player, who have to cut down their pursuers many times. Unknown to the Rohirrim, Saruman has already sent his main force to the Ford of Isen by the eastern bank of the river while the riders attacked from the west. The defenders of the Ford are caught between two forces and are surrounded on a small island amidst the river by the time Théodred returns. Their forces are already failing by the time Grimbold and the player arrive. They cut through the enemies, but as they reach Théodred, he is struck by a mighty Uruk. It is then that the reinforcements from [Riddermark](../R/Riddermark.md) finally arrive, led by [Elfhelm](../E/Elfhelm.md) from [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md), and charge the attackers. The Uruks retreat to Isengard, while Elfhelm and Grimbold tend to the gravely wounded Prince, who orders them to bury him at the Ford - to guard it until such time as [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) comes. At nightfall, the Prince of the Riddermark passes away.

Other features included(<a class="external text" href="http://lorebook.lotro.com/wiki/Update_5,_Armies_of_Isengard,_Official" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">complete release notes</a>):

- Four new instances and a 12-man raid in and around [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md).
- Book 5: The Prince of Rohan of **Volume III: Allies of the King** is released.
- A new Instance finder, to form groups more easily.
- Barter currencies of skirmishes, classic and instances are unified.
- Changes to Reputation items.

### Update 6: Shores of the Great River {#Update_6:_Shores_of_the_Great_River}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_6_The_Great_River.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/13/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_6_The_Great_River.png/220px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_6_The_Great_River.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="220" height="202" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/13/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_6_The_Great_River.png/330px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_6_The_Great_River.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/13/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_6_The_Great_River.png/440px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Update_6_The_Great_River.png 2x" data-file-width="480" data-file-height="440"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Shores of the Great River* is the sixth update and was released on [12 March](../_/12_March.md) [2012](../_/2012.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Update6&amp;Riders_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update6&amp;Riders-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Update6_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update6-4">[4]</a></sup>It included the continuation of the Epic Story, Book VI - Mists of Anduin.

Regrouping at Grimbold's encampment at night, Rohirrim and the player take a rest after a long day of battle. The player experiences a strange lucid dream, seeing many friends and enemies, both living and already dead. An apparition of Lady [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) bids the player to come and visit her urgently, adding a request to "bring the ghost" at the end. "The Ghost" refers to the Dunlending girl Nona, who had dubbed herself "Wadu's Ghost" in memory of her late brother and began waging her own vendetta over the Dunlendings who sold themselves over to [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md). The players track Nona in the [Gap of Rohan](../G/Gap_of_Rohan.md) and convince her to undertake a journey to [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md). While the Galadhrim border sentries are even more distrusting of an outsider from [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md) than usual, the two are granted an audience. Galadriel shocks the player by saying that the dream had nothing to do with her, when [Gandalf the White](../G/Gandalf_the_White.md) enters the conversation, having barely missed the Fellowship as they departed down the Great River days earlier. The Wizard insists that the dream must have had meaning and bids the player to explore the shores of Anduin downstream. After passing through Rohirrim border settlement of Stangard, the player and Nona arrive at the [Brown Lands](../B/Brown_Lands.md), where they find a new threat: a corpse of a fell beast, slain by an arrow fired by [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) as described in [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md), with its rider missing. On their way back to Rohirrim town, the player and Nona are attacked first by a band of [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) and later the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) himself. Using the fire, the two manage to hold their ground, yet Nona is wounded - and whether by an Easterling blade or a [Morgul one](../M/Morgul-knife.md) is unclear. With all haste, the player takes her back to Lady Galadriel, who heals the wound, leaving Nona out of danger. Later, Galadriel reveals to the player that in her [mirror](../M/Mirror_of_Galadriel.md), she foresaw Nona's demise and that she was saved only by the player's actions.

Other features:

- A new region, the [The Great River](../A/Anduin.md), was added, featuring seven new areas - including Parth Celebrant - in the upper [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Update6_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update6-4">[4]</a></sup>
- of Volume III, was released, in which the player seeks the wisdom of [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) about troubling dreams and fights [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md), Brigands and an ancient enemy of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-Update6_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update6-4">[4]</a></sup>
- The ability to summon skirmish warriors outside skirmishes.<sup id="cite_ref-Update6_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update6-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Updates to the Instance finder.<sup id="cite_ref-Update6_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Update6-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Currency-change in Monster Play: "Destiny" will be replaced with "Commendations". Commendations will be available for both Freeps and Creeps.<sup id="cite_ref-MP_Currency_change_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MP_Currency_change-5">[5]</a></sup>
