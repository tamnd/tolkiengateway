## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

Has all been said on J.R.R. Tolkien, author of *The Lord of the Rings* - a work reprinted and imitated in fantasy literature for half a century and made into an Oscar-winning film?

Certainly not! French and European specialists here confront the challenge of turning their approaches and readings to reveal less studied aspects of Tolkien's work, by crossing literature, music, movies, theology, philosophy, history and games to situate Tolkien in his time and in the genre he helped to reestablish, the literature of fantasy. Within this book is examined, in a new and challenging fashion, the dialogue of Tolkien with Lewis, Stephen King and Wagner, and subjects such as the role of women and of the Hobbits, temptation and heroism.

Through these innovative approaches, this book intends to show how Tolkien is relevant and protean, and avoids reducing the analyses to clichés. *Tolkien Today* is a perfect opportunity to discover a new angle of a world thought to be exhaustively explored.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>
