The **Oratory School** is Roman Catholic independent boarding school in Woodcote, Oxfordshire. [Michael](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien "Michael Tolkien") and [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) were pupils there in the 1930s when it was located in Caversham in Berkshire.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-1">[1]</a></sup> Michael was later a master at the school and when staying with him [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) occasionally covered lessons for absent teachers.
