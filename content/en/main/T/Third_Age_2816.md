**Third Age 2816** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2816**) is the 2816th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2816 was also known as **S.R. 1216** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Ponto Baggins](../P/Ponto_Baggins_I.md), third child and second son of [Balbo Baggins](../B/Balbo_Baggins.md) and [Berylla Boffin](../B/Berylla_Boffin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
