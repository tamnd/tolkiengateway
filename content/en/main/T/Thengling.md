**Thengling** is a name used in the [Song of the Mounds of Mundburg](/wiki/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg) poems, used in reference to [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden)'s death at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields). It identifies Théoden by his father [Thengel](/wiki/Thengel)'s name with the diminuative ending 'ling' to denote him being Thengel's child.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
