<i><b>tû</b></i> is a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) word meaning "muscle, sinew; vigour, physical strength".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

Retaining the same form and meaning, *tû* is commonly considered a valid [Neo-Sindarin](../N/Neo-Elvish.md) word.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
