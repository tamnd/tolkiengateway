**Tolkien WordSearch** is an online puzzle game in which the player needs to find the [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related words.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> It was made by [Eric Harshbarger](/wiki/Eric_Harshbarger) and [Benjamin Milder](/wiki/Benjamin_Milder) of [Sun Microsystems](/wiki/Sun_Microsystems).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup>
