<b><i>Tyalië Tyelelliéva</i> 16</b> is an issue of the journal *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va)*.

## Contents {#Contents}

- Editorial, Celestial Events
- Letters to the Editor, from Ales Bican, Didier Willis and [Ryszard Derdziński](/wiki/Ryszard_Derdzi%C5%84ski)
- Conferences, Demise of [ICE](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises "Iron Crown Enterprises"), ELPP Update, Movie Rumors
- Review of a Sarati font with a small text about unicorns
- Namarie-linde Luineloteva by Ales Bican with annotation by [H.K. Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger "Helge Fauskanger"), (poem in Quenya)
- Morn'ellui by Didier Willis, (poem in Sindarin)
- Turbeth Lossfin (Snowmane's Epitaph) by Ryszard Derdziński, (translation into Sindarin and Anglo-Saxon with tengwar and cirth)
- Bilbo by Manuel Colunga-Hernandez, (artwork)
- Comparison of Adjectives in Quenya, linguistic analysis by Lisa Star
- Tolkien Collection at [Marquette](/wiki/Marquette_University "Marquette University"), Part 2, report by Lisa Star
- New and Updated Websites, especially those good for linguistic information
- Rootplay: AD-NO \`door' (historical analysis of reflexes)
- Bas du Page Homework on doors and farm animals
- Obscure Publications, GC, KoDT, Hisweloke, OH, PE, and VT
