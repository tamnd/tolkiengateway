**The Last Debate** is the forty-eighth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* and the fifty-ninth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)"). It was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), and [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) are gathered in the [throne-room](/wiki/Hall_of_the_Kings "Hall of the Kings") of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) after the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).

Debating their next steps, Gandalf worries that Frodo has passed beyond his sight and that he has sent the hobbit to his death. He says that [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) is re-marshalling his troops in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). Gimli questions why they should care; in response, Gandalf tells him that "10,000 orcs now stand between Frodo and [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom)". Aragorn puts forward the idea of drawing them away from the [plains of Gorgoroth](/wiki/Plateau_of_Gorgoroth "Plateau of Gorgoroth") to give Frodo a chance to get the [Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") to Mount Doom. "A diversion," Legolas calls it. Gandalf warns that Sauron will suspect a trap, however Aragorn assures the wizard that the bait will be taken.

## Differences {#Differences}

In the books, [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil), [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan), and [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir) are also present while Legolas and Gimli are not. Gandalf is the one who suggests that the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Host_of_the_West" title="Host of the West">Host of the West</a> be created while others have doubts. The [Last Debate](/wiki/Last_Debate) takes place outside of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
