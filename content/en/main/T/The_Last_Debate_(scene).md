**The Last Debate** is the forty-eighth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* and the fifty-ninth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>). It was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md), and [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) are gathered in the [throne-room](../H/Hall_of_the_Kings.md) of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) after the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).

Debating their next steps, Gandalf worries that Frodo has passed beyond his sight and that he has sent the hobbit to his death. He says that [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) is re-marshalling his troops in [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). Gimli questions why they should care; in response, Gandalf tells him that "10,000 orcs now stand between Frodo and [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md)". Aragorn puts forward the idea of drawing them away from the [plains of Gorgoroth](../P/Plateau_of_Gorgoroth.md) to give Frodo a chance to get the [Ring](The_One_Ring.md) to Mount Doom. "A diversion," Legolas calls it. Gandalf warns that Sauron will suspect a trap, however Aragorn assures the wizard that the bait will be taken.

## Differences {#Differences}

In the books, [Imrahil](../I/Imrahil.md), [Elladan](../E/Elladan.md), and [Elrohir](../E/Elrohir.md) are also present while Legolas and Gimli are not. Gandalf is the one who suggests that the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../H/Host_of_the_West.md" title="Host of the West">Host of the West</a> be created while others have doubts. The [Last Debate](../L/Last_Debate.md) takes place outside of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
