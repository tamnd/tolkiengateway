<i><b>tirith</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "watching, guarding"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or "guard",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> or "vigilance".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word <i><b>tirith</b></i> means "watch, guard".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")*, *tirith* derives from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [TIR](/wiki/TIR).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) = "Tower of the Guard"
