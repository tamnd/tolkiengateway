**Third Age 2961** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2961**) is the 2961st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2961 was also known as **S.R. 1361** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Anson Roper](../A/Anson_Roper.md), son of [Andwise Roper](../A/Andwise_Roper.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Gaffer Gamgee](../G/Gaffer_Gamgee.md) begins tending the garden of [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
