**Tar-Anárion** was the eighth ruler of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Anarion_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Anarion-1">[1]</a></sup> succeeding his mother, [Tar-Ancalimë](Tar-Ancalimë.md) (being the first ruling Queen).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Ancalime_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Ancalime-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Little is said about Tar-Anárion's rule.<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Anarion_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Anarion-1">[1]</a></sup> His two eldest children were female, but both refused to be heirs to the throne for unknown reasons, and they disliked and feared [Ancalimë](Tar-Ancalimë.md), who would in revenge not allow them to marry. Therefore he was succeeded by his son, [Tar-Súrion](Tar-Súrion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Anárion* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it likely means "Son of the Sun" as a compound of *[anar](../A/Anar.md)* ("sun") and the patronymic suffix *[-ion](../_/-ion.md)* ("son of").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Like all the rulers of Númenor who took their royal names in Quenya, Anárion added the prefix *tar-* ("high") to his name when he received the [Sceptre](../S/Sceptre_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
