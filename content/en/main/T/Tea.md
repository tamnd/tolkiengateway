**Tea** was a beverage that the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) (specifically [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits)) enjoyed alone or socially.

A time of resting and relaxing, the **tea-time**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was considered a meal on its own, between lunch and dinner.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was customary to offer guests for a cup of tea.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) invited [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) for a cup of tea when he unexpectedly arrived to [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). After his invitation, Gandalf showed up with [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [a poem](/wiki/Perry-the-Winkle "Perry-the-Winkle") written by [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), the [Lonely Troll](/wiki/Lonely_Troll) served [Perry-the-Winkle](</wiki/Perry-the-Winkle_(hobbit)> "Perry-the-Winkle (hobbit)") some "fulsome tea" and the two friends regularly met in weekly tea sessions.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Names {#Names}

In the early stages of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) created two [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) words for "tea": in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) <i><b>tyé</b></i> (clearly adapted from English),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) <i><b>suithlas</b></i> (literally "drink-leaf").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
