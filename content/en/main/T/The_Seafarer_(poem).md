**The Seafarer** is an [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) poem recorded in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_Book" title="wikipedia:Exeter Book">Exeter Book</a>, one of the four surviving manuscripts of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_English_literature" title="wikipedia:Old English literature">Old English poetry</a>.

Together with [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) prepared an edition of the poem. The work was revised and completed by [Ida Gordon](../I/Ida_Gordon.md) and published in [1960](../_/1960.md) (see *[The Seafarer](<The_Seafarer_(I.L._Gordon).md>)*).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Tolkien's poem '<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Mona%C3%B0_Modes_Lust_mid_Mereflode&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Monað Modes Lust mid Mereflode (page does not exist)">Monað Modes Lust mid Mereflode</a>', appearing in the "[The Lost Road](The_Lost_Road.md)" and "[The Notion Club Papers](The_Notion_Club_Papers.md)", is derived from *The Seafarer*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
