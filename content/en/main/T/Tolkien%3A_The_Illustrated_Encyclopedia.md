<i><b>Tolkien: The Illustrated Encyclopedia</b></i> is an encyclopedia-style book by [David Day](/wiki/David_Day), first published in [1991](/wiki/1991).

The book has been reissued in many different covers by different publishers.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The first encyclopedic illustrated guide to the world of <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle Earth</a> [sic] and the <a href="/wiki/Undying_Lands" title="Undying Lands">Undying Lands</a>, this book brings together every important aspect of <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">Tolkien</a>&#39;s vast cosmology. More than five hundred alphabetical entries cover five major subject areas: history, geography, sociology, natural history and biography.
</p><p>The maps, genealogies and time-charts, together with the illustrations of characters, places and events, reveal to the reader the full dramatic sweep and splendor of Tolkien&#39;s world.
</p>
</blockquote>
