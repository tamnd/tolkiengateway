**The Firland Saga** is a children's fantasy book series by [Norman S. Power](../N/Norman_Power.md). The series contain a reference to [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), and the author was inspired by the fictional works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The stories of Firland were expanded from bedtime stories Power told to his four children.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Firland is an [Atlantis](../A/Atlantis-haunting.md)-like country that is visited by some modern day children. Characters of Firland include the evil Queen Ivis, Greylin the Wizard, a talking dragon, and King Mark in the Castle of Casterbor. The narrative also includes cameo appearances of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes" title="wikipedia:Sherlock Holmes">Sherlock Holmes</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Watson_(Sherlock_Holmes)" title="wikipedia:John Watson (Sherlock Holmes)">Dr. Watson</a>.

Tolkien himself seems to have read the first book in the series (*[The Forgotten Kingdom: The Firland Saga](The_Forgotten_Kingdom.md)*, 1970), as he wrote a letter to Norman Power in the 1970s, commenting on The Firland Saga.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
