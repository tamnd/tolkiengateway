**Tyler Jacobson** is an American illustrator. As a [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering) artist, he created art for the [Tales of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth "The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth") and [The Hobbit](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit "Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit") expansions.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Outside of his commercial work, he sketched a portrait of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in addition to other works of fan art.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
