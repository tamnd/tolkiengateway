<i><b>Travel and Communication in Tolkien&#39;s Worlds</b></i> is a collection of the proceedings of the 10th Tolkien Society Seminar, held in Leicester in 1995. The book was published by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) in [1996](/wiki/1996) under the auspices of the [Peter Roe Memorial Fund](/wiki/Peter_Roe_Memorial_Fund) in [1996](/wiki/1996) and re-published in [1998](/wiki/1998).
