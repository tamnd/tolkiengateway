**Third Age 2793** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2793**) is the 2793rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2793 was also known as **S.R. 1193** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs.md) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
