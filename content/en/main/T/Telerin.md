**Telerin** was a word used to refer to the tongues of any Elf-people that are of [Lindar](/wiki/Lindar) origin but is best reserved for the language of the Teleri that came to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), the [Falmari](/wiki/Falmari);<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> it is a tongue related to both [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).

It is also called <i><b>Lindalambe</b></i> or <i><b>Lindárin</b></i>.

## History {#History}

The language of the Falmari descended from [Common Telerin](/wiki/Common_Telerin), an ancient dialect of the [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) spoken during the [Great March](/wiki/Great_March). From the viewpoint of the speakers of [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (who considered their language the main direct descendant of Common Eldarin), they considered Telerin (a direct descendant of Common Telerin) a "dialect of Quenya". Telerin was therefore considered a closely related language still largely intelligible.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

However, the phonology of the two languages differed much, with Telerin often being more conservative than [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). In [Aman](/wiki/Aman) the speakers of Telerin outnumbered the speakers of [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), much as the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) outnumbered the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) and [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor).

Also, the Teleri prized silver above gold, and their skill as silversmiths was esteemed even by the Noldor who seemed to prefer the Telerin word *[telpe](/wiki/Telpe "Telpe")* ("silver") intead the Quenya form *[tyelpë](/wiki/Tyelp%C3%AB "Tyelpë")*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin) is said to have learned Telerin. He also gave his sons Telerin-influenced Quenya names.

## Grammar {#Grammar}

A notable difference from Quenya was that the possessive endings were suffixed to a word of something possessed, not possessing. For example "[Olwe](/wiki/Olwe)'s house" is <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Cava&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cava (page does not exist)">cavaria</a> Olue</i> (lit. "house-his Olue").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

*Telerin* is a name of a language appearing in *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, where about 25 words of that language are given.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> This version of Telerin was more related to [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin) than to [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) or [Kornoldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
