**Tol-in-Gaurhoth** or **Isle of Werewolves**, also called **Wizard's Isle**<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> was a fortress held by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), once called [Tol Sirion](Tol_Sirion.md).

## History {#History}

Tol-in-Gaurhoth began as [Minas Tirith](<../M/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand).md>), constructed by [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md), and was held by [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) for his uncle to guard the vale of [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md). However, Orodreth was powerless to stop [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) from taking the isle and fled south. Sauron began breeding large [wolves](../W/Wolves.md) in the isle, and, by sending evil spirits into the fell beasts, turned them into [werewolves](../W/Werewolves.md).

Ten years later during the [Quest for the Silmaril](../Q/Quest_for_the_Silmaril.md), [Beren Erchamion](../B/Beren.md), seeking the [Silmaril](../S/Silmarils.md), enlisted Finrod's aid, but the two of them and their group were captured while passing the isle. Sauron defeated Finrod in a contest of spells and songs, and their companions were slain one-by-one by werewolves. Finrod saved Beren's life by killing the werewolf that came for him, but in doing so he sacrificed his own life.

[Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) and [Huan](../H/Huan.md), [Hound](../H/Hound.md) of [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), came to Beren's rescue, and Huan killed the wolves sent against him. After [Draugluin](../D/Draugluin.md) was killed, Sauron went out in the form of a great wolf but was defeated. Lúthien gained control over the isle from Sauron, who fled from her. After releasing those who had been captive there under Sauron, Lúthien reduced Minas Tirith to ruins. Finrod was buried there by Beren and Lúthien.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The isle remained clean for a time, but in the aftermath of the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md) it was retaken by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) who rebuilt the towers of Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

It was about this isle that a great host of Orcs were gathered before the [Battle of Tumhalad](../B/Battle_of_Tumhalad.md) which shattered the forces of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-nargothrond_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nargothrond-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tol-in-Gaurhoth* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Isle of Werewolves", from *[tol](Tol.md)* ("isle") + plural *[i](../I/I.md)* (definite article) + *[gaur](../G/Gaur.md)* ("werewolf") + *[hoth](../H/Hoth.md)* ("host").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> It is also mentioned simply as "Gaurhoth Isle".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
