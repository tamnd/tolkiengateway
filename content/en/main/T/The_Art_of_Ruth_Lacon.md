<i><b>The Art of Ruth Lacon: Illustrations inspired by the works of JRR Tolkien</b></i> is an art book which collects illustrations made by [Ruth Lacon](../R/Ruth_Lacon.md). The book includes commments on the pieces of art, and a detailed biography on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), and Ruth Lacon. Only 1000 copies were published.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

A beautiful book covering Ruth’s variety of media and styles. The illustrations range from black &amp; white pen &amp; ink to full colour, very bright, with much attention to details and borders, and covers the whole Tolkien spectrum from Lord of the Rings through Hobbit to Silmarillion, Unfinished Tales &amp; Farmer Giles.
