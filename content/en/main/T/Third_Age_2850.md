**Third Age 2850** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2850**) is the 2850th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2850 was also known as **S.R. 1250** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) again enters [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md), and discovers that its master is indeed [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), who is gathering all the [Rings](../R/Rings_of_Power.md) and seeking for news of [the One](The_One_Ring.md), and of [Isildur's Heir](../I/Isildur's_Heir.md). He finds [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Thráin II](Thráin.md) and receives the key of [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md), but doesn't know who the Dwarf was as he had forgotten his own name.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of King Thráin II in [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md). Loss of the [Ring of Thrór](../R/Ring_of_Thrór.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Around that time [Thorin II Oakenshield](Thorin_II_Oakenshield.md), son of Thráin II, becomes the [King](../K/King_of_Durin's_Folk~7047.md) of [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>&lt;/ref&gt;

Thráin died in capture five years later in 2850 but nobody knew it, as it was Gandalf the only one to witness it, and didn&#39;t know who he was. According to <a href="../A/Appendix_A.md" title="Appendix A">Appendix A</a>, it was only in <a href="Third_Age_2941.md" title="Third Age 2941">91 years later</a>, meeting Thorin, when he realised that the captured Dwarf he saw dying in <a href="../D/Dol_Guldur.md" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a> was Thráin, and presumably (not mentioned in the narrative) he reported it to Thorin. It is hard to believe that the Longbeards knew when their King died, or that they remained kingless almost a century until Gandalf confirmed it to Thorin; it is reasonable that Thorin succeeded his father not too long after his disappearance.</span>
