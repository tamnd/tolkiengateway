<i><b>Tolkien: A Cultural Phenomenon</b></i> is a book by Brian Rosebury in the field of [Tolkien Studies](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies). It was published in 2003.

### From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This comprehensive and discriminating account of Tolkien&#39;s work has been revised and expanded to take account both of recent developments in scholarship, and of the recent films directed by Peter Jackson. Tracing the development of Tolkien&#39;s creative technique over several decades, it explores the bewildering profusion of shorter works, as well as devoting an extended analysis to The Lord of the Rings. Chapters consider Tolkien&#39;s contribution to the history of ideas, and review the critical reception of the Lord of the Rings film adaptations and other popular adaptations of his work.
</p>
</blockquote>
