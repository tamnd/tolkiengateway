In [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md), **Tantûrak** was a [Númenórean](../N/Númenor.md) colony established in the [Mûmakan](../M/Mûmakan.md) on the northeast corner of the Usakan bay. Founded circa [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1300&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1300 (page does not exist)">1300</a>, it was the southernmost of the Númenórean settlements on [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Its capital was the port of Sarûl, located on the southern bank of the Uvar River.<sup id="cite_ref-tanturak_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tanturak-1">[1]</a></sup>

The city of Sarûl was extreme in its opulence with massive structures, barren avenues and gaudy decroration. The main military based of the army of Tantûrak was located on an island to the left of the city with barracks and a large field for daily drills.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The formidable navy of Tantûrak was also based at Sarûl.<sup id="cite_ref-tanturak_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tanturak-1">[1]</a></sup>

Unlike other inhabitants of the Mûmakan who adapted to the jungle, the colonists cleared vast swathes of the rainforest and coastal vegetation by burning it down.<sup id="cite_ref-tanturak_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tanturak-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Black Númenóreans of Tantûrak were probably referred to as Saruleans.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The colony was founded around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1300&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1300 (page does not exist)">1300</a><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> as Lond Hallacar.<sup id="cite_ref-nazgul_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nazgul-5">[5]</a></sup> While the climate of the southern Mûmakan was far more humid and oppressive than the cool air of Númenor, the colonists chose this location out of necessity rather than preference. Initially, they attempted to settle further north in the Usakal Forest. However, they were soon plagued by the Uvag-Aak ("Drinkers of Blood"), terrifying ape-like creatures that inhabited the northern woods.<sup id="cite_ref-tanturak_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tanturak-1">[1]</a></sup>

For approximately seven hundred years, Tantûrak was ruled by a royal governor who answered to the King of Númenor. This system remained stable through eleven governors until the arrival of a mysterious figure known as the "Magician" circa [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2000&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2000 (page does not exist)">2000</a>. Rising quickly through the ranks of advisers to the rank of chief advisor to the governor, the Magician corrupted the sitting governor, a cousin of the King who harboured a lust for power and glory.<sup id="cite_ref-tanturak_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tanturak-1">[1]</a></sup> This Magician was in fact an elf called Valmorgûl, of the [Court of Ardor](../C/Court_of_Ardor.md), who appeared to look as an "ageless" man with silver-white hair and gold eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Under the Magician's influence, the Governor declared himself King Tar-Kinjaarn in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2040&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2040 (page does not exist)">2040</a>, severing all political ties with Númenor. This rebellion was initially welcomed by the populace, as it freed them from the increasingly inflated taxes imposed by the mother country.<sup id="cite_ref-tanturak_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tanturak-1">[1]</a></sup> For this, they gained the support of [Jí Indûr](../F/Fourth_of_the_Nazgûl.md)'s Mûmakan empire. [Ar-Pharazon](../A/Ar-Pharazon.md) ultimately reconquered Tantûrak in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3262](../S/Second_Age_3262.md), but Númenor itself would fall in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_3319&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 3319 (page does not exist)">3319</a>, leaving the colony independent again.<sup id="cite_ref-nazgul_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nazgul-5">[5]</a></sup>

As their lifespans began to wane, the people of Tantûrak grew increasingly jealous of the immortal Elves and even the indigenous Kirani. This envy transformed from disdain into outward hatred.<sup id="cite_ref-tanturak_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tanturak-1">[1]</a></sup> Again under the Magician's sway, Tantûrak declared war on [Koronandë](../K/Koronandë.md) in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1365&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1365 (page does not exist)">1365</a>. Through Indûr's intervention, a peace was reached in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1372&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1372 (page does not exist)">1372</a>, which increased his influence.<sup id="cite_ref-nazgul_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nazgul-5">[5]</a></sup>

By [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3000](Third_Age_3000.md) the Court of Ardor had been defeated and Valmorgûl, the Magician wass dead. However, the emperor of Tantûrak contined to be advised by the "Magician", because the role of the Magician was assumed by the disguised Khelekar, an agent of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md). Thus, Tantûrak was under his sway. In this time, Tantûrak had grown and dominated [Dûshera](../D/Dûshera.md) and northern Taliraan. In addition, it repeatedly raided Hathor by sea.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> <span></span>
