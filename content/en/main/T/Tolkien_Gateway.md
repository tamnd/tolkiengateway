**Tolkien Gateway** (or simply **TG**) is a not-for-profit collaborative wiki devoted to the [works](../../index/W/Writings_by_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), being the largest Tolkien-related encyclopedia on the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Wide_Web" title="wikipedia:World Wide Web">World Wide Web</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-List_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-List-1">[1]</a></sup> It strives to be the most extensive and complete online resource with content relating not just to the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) but also information on Tolkien-related images, adaptations, people, places, events, societies, and other works of scholarly and academic interest.

Although still criticised for its "wiki" ethos, Tolkien Gateway's growth and increased scholarship have been lauded by Tolkien scholars in recent years.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkienseminariet_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkienseminariet-2">[2]</a></sup> Its increased status has led to greater popularity as Tolkien Gateway is now the most popular Tolkien website in the world, ahead of [TheOneRing.net](TheOneRing.net.md) and [The Tolkien Society](The_Tolkien_Society.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Alexa_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Alexa-3">[3]</a></sup> Tolkien Gateway is recognised as one of the world's largest wikis, ranking 368th in the world by total number of articles, 529th by total number of edits and 656th by total number of images.<sup id="cite_ref-WikiStats_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WikiStats-4">[4]</a></sup>

Inspired by the release of [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), Tolkien Gateway was founded by [Hyarion](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Hyarion) on [1 January](../_/1_January.md) [2003](../_/2003.md) as <a class="external text" href="http://web.archive.org/web/*/http://tolkiengateway.cjb.net" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">http://tolkiengateway.cjb.net</a>, then <a class="external text" href="http://web.archive.org/web/*/http://tolkiengateway.tk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">http://tolkiengateway.tk</a>, before eventually opening up as <a class="external free" href="http://tolkiengateway.net" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">http://tolkiengateway.net</a> on [3 June](../_/3_June.md) [2005](../_/2005.md). Tolkien Gateway uses the same <a class="external text" href="http://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/MediaWiki" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">MediaWiki</a> software as <a class="external text" href="http://www.fandom.com" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Fandom</a> and <a class="external text" href="http://www.wikipedia.org" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Wikipedia</a> whilst operating under a similar <a class="external text" href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">CC BY-SA 4.0</a> license. Tolkien Gateway has a <a class="external text" href="http://wikiindex.org/Category:WikiFactor" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">wikifactor</a> of 220.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>

### History by year {#History_by_year}

#### 2003 {#2003}

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/TG_2003.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5b/TG_2003.jpg/200px-TG_2003.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="200" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5b/TG_2003.jpg/300px-TG_2003.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5b/TG_2003.jpg/400px-TG_2003.jpg 2x" data-file-width="800" data-file-height="800"/></a><figcaption>Tolkien Gateway in April 2003</figcaption></figure>

Inspired by the recent release of [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), [Hyarion](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Hyarion) created TolkienGateway on [1 January](../_/1_January.md) [2003](../_/2003.md) as a community and an encyclopedia at <a class="external text" href="http://web.archive.org/web/*/http://tolkiengateway.cjb.net" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">http://tolkiengateway.cjb.net</a>. Sporting a tan-coloured skin, TolkienGateway made use of the free <a class="external text" href="http://www.phpnuke.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">PHP-Nuke</a> software.<sup id="cite_ref-Archive_CJB_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Archive_CJB-21">[20]</a></sup>

In [February](../F/February.md), Hyarion decide to move the web-hosting of TolkienGateway from <a class="external text" href="http://Angelfire.com" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Angelfire</a> to <a class="external text" href="http://FuturePoint.com" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">FuturePoint</a>. By [August](../A/August.md), Hyarion migrated to the free, and more aesthetically-pleasing, domain of <a class="external text" href="http://web.archive.org/web/*/http://tolkiengateway.tk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">http://tolkiengateway.tk</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Archive_TK_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Archive_TK-22">[21]</a></sup>

#### 2004 {#2004}

On [6 November](../_/6_November.md), Hyarion purchased the domain <a class="external free" href="http://tolkiengateway.net" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">http://tolkiengateway.net</a> and begins work on creating a proper wiki. By [March](../M/March.md) [2005](../_/2005.md), the original TolkienGateway has gone offline so Hyarion can focus on the new website.<sup id="cite_ref-Archive_TK_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Archive_TK-22">[21]</a></sup>

#### 2005 {#2005}

In [January](../J/January.md) [2005](../_/2005.md) Tolkien Gateway's [IRC chat](../../project/C/Chat%252FFormer.md) was opened; this enabled both Tolkien Gateway editors and users from other websites to congregate in a single place for discussion of their respective websites and the works of Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-Chat_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chat-23">[22]</a></sup> Tolkien Gateway's IRC, irc.tolkiengateway.net. The IRC network was eventually retired roughly a decade later.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[23]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/TG-logo.gif.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c1/TG-logo.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="160" height="214" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="160" data-file-height="214"/></a><figcaption>Tolkien Gateway&#39;s original logo</figcaption></figure>

After a long hiatus, Tolkien Gateway opened to the public on [3 June](../_/3_June.md) with Hyarion as the sole administrator and bureaucrat. Around the same time, Wikipedia began removing and pruning many of their Tolkien-related articles that were deemed not noteworthy enough. In order to preserve the content and for Tolkien Gateway to be seeded with some articles ready for its release, many were copied from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject_Middle-earth" title="wikipedia:Wikipedia:WikiProject Middle-earth">Wikipedia</a> or <a class="external text" href="http://www.glyphweb.com/arda/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Encyclopedia of Arda</a>; this has since been criticised by later editors who have struggled to identify and update all such articles.<sup id="cite_ref-Meeting_5Sep_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meeting_5Sep-25">[24]</a></sup>

Notable editors during this time include [BrightSideoftheDark](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:BrightSideoftheDark), [Bokkie](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Bokkie), [Elemmakil](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Elemmakil), [Gandalf](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Gandalf), [Hyarion](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Hyarion) and [Tik](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Tik).

#### 2006 {#2006}

On [1 January](../_/1_January.md) [2006](../_/2006.md) Hyarion opened a second website, [Tolkien News](Tolkien_News.md), as a another source of information, in competition with <a class="external text" href="http://www.theonering.net" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">TheOneRing.net</a>, but without the commercial advertising. The website closed in [2008](../_/2008.md).

Technically, 2006 was a very important year for Tolkien Gateway: it saw the introduction of the fresh Cavendish skin, the creation of a forum for editors to discuss important issues, a community portal, and the ability to embed video. Furthermore, 2006 witnessed impressive growth in page views: from less than 200,000 page views at the start of the year to a million by October, by this point Tolkien Gateway was also witnessing 3,000 pages views per day.<sup id="cite_ref-2006u_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2006u-8">[7]</a></sup>

2006 also witnessed modest growth in members from about 180 at the start of year to 422 at the end of the end of the year.<sup id="cite_ref-April2007_12-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-April2007-12">[11]</a></sup> Important editors during this time include [Ardamir](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Ardamir), [Earendilyon](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Earendilyon), [Ebakunin](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Ebakunin), [Hyarion](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Hyarion), [Mith](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Mith), [Narfil Palùrfalas](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Narfil_Pal%C3%B9rfalas), [Tar-Telperien](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Tar-Telperien) and [Tik](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Tik).

Although technically relatively uneventful, [2007](../_/2007.md) saw attempts by Hyarion to increase Tolkien Gateway's user base and reach. In particular, coinciding with the release of *[The Children of Húrin](The_Children_of_Húrin.md)* Hyarion arranged [The Children of Húrin Release Party](The_Children_of_Húrin_Release_Party.md) an online gathering of artists, collectors, scholars and fans to celebrate the publication of this fresh story; a record-breaking 69 new users joined Tolkien Gateway in [April](../A/April.md) 2007 as a result of this.<sup id="cite_ref-April2007_12-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-April2007-12">[11]</a></sup> Coinciding with The Children of Húrin Release Party, Hyarion also introduced <a class="external text" href="http://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Main_Page&amp;oldid=42029" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">a brand new Main Page layout</a> which still remains the basis for the current [Main Page](../M/Main_Page.md).

On [4 October](../_/4_October.md), Hyarion introduced interwiki links allowing Tolkien Gateway's English articles to be connected to the fellow wikis <a class="external text" href="http://ardapedia.herr-der-ringe-film.de/index.php/Hauptseite" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ardapedia</a> (German) and <a class="external text" href="http://kontu.wiki/Etusivu" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Kontu</a> (Finnish).<sup id="cite_ref-Interwiki_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interwiki-10">[9]</a></sup> Since then, interwiki links have also been expanded to the French website <a class="external text" href="http://www.tolkiendil.com/encyclo" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkiendil</a> and Farsi website <a class="external text" href="http://wiki.valimar.ir/index.php?title=%D8%B5%D9%81%D8%AD%D9%87%D9%94_%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%84%DB%8C" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Valimar</a>.

Influential editors during this period include [Dr Death](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Dr_Death), [Dwarf Lord](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Dwarf_Lord), [Earendilyon](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Earendilyon), [Elemmakil](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Elemmakil) [Fleela](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Fleela), [Hyarion](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Hyarion), [Mith](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Mith), [Narfil Palùrfalas](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Narfil_Pal%C3%B9rfalas), [Tar-Telperien](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Tar-Telperien), [Theoden1](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Theoden1) and [Tik](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Tik).

#### 2008 {#2008}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/TG_2008.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/06/TG_2008.png/300px-TG_2008.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="534" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/06/TG_2008.png/450px-TG_2008.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/06/TG_2008.png/600px-TG_2008.png 2x" data-file-width="1002" data-file-height="1785"/></a><figcaption>Tolkien Gateway in June 2008</figcaption></figure>

The first major event of [2008](../_/2008.md) was a server move, "*Due to the rapid growth of Tolkien Gateway we will be migrating to a new quad-core server with 8 gigs of RAM.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Updates_Move_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Updates_Move-26">[25]</a></sup> Tolkien Gateway moves server on roughly a year basis every time resulting in considerable downtime and residual problems; this move was no exception!<sup id="cite_ref-Updates_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Updates-27">[26]</a></sup> The following month, Tolkien Gateway was featured as the Site of the Week for the 8th February on the fan website <a class="external text" href="http://www.thereandbackagain.net/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">ThereAndBackAgain.net</a><sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[27]</a></sup>

On [30 April](../_/30_April.md) [2008](../_/2008.md) the first major extension to the wiki's content was the creation of <a class="external text" href="http://tolkiengateway.net/map/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">a map</a> which seeks to list all the important locations in the world relevant to Tolkien's life, publications and adaptations.<sup id="cite_ref-Updates_Map_11-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Updates_Map-11">[10]</a></sup> The expansion of the wiki continues, reaching 7,000 articles by [12 July](../_/12_July.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Updates_7000_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Updates_7000-13">[12]</a></sup> whilst gaining an extra 366 members over the course of the year, taking the total membership to 788.<sup id="cite_ref-April2007_12-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-April2007-12">[11]</a></sup>

In the summer, Hyarion revealed to editors that [he had been in discussions](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Forums:One_Wiki_Merge) with the administrators of <a class="external text" href="http://lotr.wikia.com/wiki/Main_Page" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">One Wiki to Rule Them All</a>, the Tolkien encyclopedia of <a class="external text" href="http://www.wikia.com" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Wikia</a>, to merge the two sites together. Although the proposal wasn't fully fleshed-out, members of both communities got so far as <a class="external text" href="http://lotr.wikia.com/wiki/LOTR:Merge_Vote" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">registering their intention to vote</a> on the issue before Wikia staff decided they were unwilling to let go of their own site - the merger never took place.<sup id="cite_ref-Merge_talk_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Merge_talk-29">[28]</a></sup> A happy side-effect, however, is that many One Wiki editors migrated permanently to Tolkien Gateway, including the One Wiki bureaucrat [KingAragorn](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:KingAragorn).<sup id="cite_ref-Merge_talk2_30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Merge_talk2-30">[29]</a></sup>

The second seminal event of 2008 was the introduction of [Meetings](../../project/M/Meetings.md). The [first meeting](../../project/M/Meetings%252F17_August_2008.md) was held on 17 August 2008; following the first meeting, Tolkien Gateway saw its very first promotion of [Ederchil](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Ederchil) to [administrator](../../project/A/Administrators.md), an expansion of [Projects](../../project/P/Projects.md) to focus the activities of members, and a more extensive [Community Portal](../../project/C/Community_Portal.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Meeting_1_31-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meeting_1-31">[30]</a></sup> Meetings continued on a weekly basis until [October](../O/October.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-32">[31]</a></sup>

On [25 August](../_/25_August.md), [KingAragorn](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:KingAragorn) contacted [TheOneRing.net](TheOneRing.net.md) with a proposition that Tolkien Gateway and TheOneRing.net should work in co-operation. The general premise was that the two online Tolkien communities were consubstantial and should, therefore, work together for each other's benefit. The discussion was, at first, progressive; it was mooted that, in effect, Tolkien Gateway would become TheOneRing.net's encyclopaedia, and that TheOneRing.net would become Tolkien Gateway's news portal. However, discussions came to an end because of an apparent bad history between [Hyarion](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Hyarion), the owner of Tolkien Gateway, and TheOneRing.net. Whilst there was reciprocal praise for the work achieved by both communities, no further discussions took place.<sup id="cite_ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33">[32]</a></sup>

Finally, on [26 August](../_/26_August.md), the "Gateway to Ithilien" was made the default skin for Tolkien Gateway, and - with minor alterations - still remains the default skin today.<sup id="cite_ref-Ithilien_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ithilien-15">[14]</a></sup>

Top contributors during this period include [Aule the Smith](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Aule_the_Smith), [Dwarf Lord](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Dwarf_Lord), [Earendilyon](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Earendilyon), [Ederchil](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Ederchil), [Eldarion Telcontar](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Eldarion_Telcontar), [KingAragorn](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:KingAragorn), [Linathiel](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Linathiel), [Mith](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Mith), [Narfil Palùrfalas](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Narfil_Pal%C3%B9rfalas), [Quidon88](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Quidon88), [Sage](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Sage), [Þelma](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:%C3%9Eelma), [Theoden1](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Theoden1), and [Tik](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Tik) as well as [KingAragorn Bot](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:KingAragorn_Bot)

#### 2010 {#2010}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/TG_2010.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/05/TG_2010.png/300px-TG_2010.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="410" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/05/TG_2010.png/450px-TG_2010.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/05/TG_2010.png/600px-TG_2010.png 2x" data-file-width="1007" data-file-height="1376"/></a><figcaption>Tolkien Gateway in November 2010</figcaption></figure>

[2010](../_/2010.md) started eventfully for Tolkien Gateway as a new editor, [Gilgamesh](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Gilgamesh), including [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) declension templates in the majority of articles - this led to an intense discussion between Gilgamesh on the one hand, and [Ederchil](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Ederchil), [Mith](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Mith) and [Sage](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Sage) on the other (arguing that their appearance and canonicity were questionable). Eventually, all the templates were removed.<sup id="cite_ref-Quenya_Template_35-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenya_Template-35">[34]</a></sup>

Despite reaching its fifth birthday (as a wiki) this was left uncelebrated by the editors. Indeed, [some editors expressed disquiet](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Forums:The_future_of_Tolkien_Gateway) about existing technical problems, the paucity of editors and the inactivity of [Hyarion](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Hyarion) with some editors suggesting that the project should be forked. Following this, Tolkien Gateway witness a re-emergence of its meetings,<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_32-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-32">[31]</a></sup> Hyarion became more active (updating the MediaWiki software), Ederchil was promoted to a bureaucrat and Mith was promoted to administrator/sysop.<sup id="cite_ref-MithPromote_36-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MithPromote-36">[35]</a></sup> It was also at this time that, with great reluctance,<sup id="cite_ref-Reluctance_37-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Reluctance-37">[36]</a></sup> that members of the community decided to lock Tolkien Gateway to anonymous editors; this was in order to prevent the large numbers of spammers who were vandalising articles.<sup id="cite_ref-TG_lock_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TG_lock-16">[15]</a></sup>

In the summer of 2010, <a class="external text" href="http://twitter.com/#!/kriskowal" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Kris Kowal</a> from <a class="external text" href="http://3rin.gs" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">3rin.gs</a> added links to Tolkien Gateway - alongside existing ones to <a class="external text" href="http://www.glyphweb.com/arda/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">the Encyclopedia of Arda</a> - on his interactive map.<sup id="cite_ref-3rin.gs_38-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3rin.gs-38">[37]</a></sup>

During this time, many key projects were embarked upon by editors: the relatively new editor [Morgan](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Morgan) working on bibliographical information;<sup id="cite_ref-Works_39-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Works-39">[38]</a></sup> Mith working on the [Timeline](Timeline.md) and years; Ederchil and Mith updated and expanded infoboxes; Mith and [KingAragorn](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:KingAragorn) trying to remove five-years of redirected links (with the aid of their bots);<sup id="cite_ref-Redirects_40-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Redirects-40">[39]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Redirects1_41-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Redirects1-41">[40]</a></sup> KingAragorn re-categorisation all location articles; [Sage](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Sage) working on language-related articles; and [Amroth](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Amroth) and [Pinkkeith](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Pinkkeith) working on video-games articles. Other high-frequency editors include: [Aule the Smith](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Aule_the_Smith), [Gilgamesh](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Gilgamesh), [Grond](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Grond), [Mthomas](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Mthomas) and [Theoden1](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Theoden1), along with the aforementioned [KingAragorn Bot](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:KingAragorn_Bot) and [Mithbot](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Mithbot).

#### 2012 {#2012}

In January [2012](../_/2012.md), news spread about <a class="external text" href="http://twitter.com/lotrproject" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Emil Johansson</a>'s <i><a class="external text" href="http://lotrproject.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Lord of the Rings Family Tree Project</a></i> — a genealogy of characters from Tolkien's [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), where each character carries a link to the respective Tolkien Gateway article.<sup id="cite_ref-47" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-47">[46]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-48" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-48">[47]</a></sup>

On [20 August](../_/20_August.md) 2012, [Shaun Gunner](../S/Shaun_Gunner.md) ([User:Mith](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Mith)) took part in a panel discussion with [Corey Olsen](../C/Corey_Olsen.md) called 'Lifelong Learning Tolkien: Face-to-face and Online'. Tolkien Gateway was cited has an example of the power of the online Tolkien community, with noted scholar [Dimitra Fimi](../D/Dimitra_Fimi.md) claiming to be an editor.<sup id="cite_ref-ROTR-Troels_49-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ROTR-Troels-49">[48]</a></sup>

In September 2012, the [Mythgard Institute](../M/Mythgard_Institute.md) podcast "[Riddles in the Dark](../R/Riddles_in_the_Dark.md)" episode 17, featuring [Corey Olsen](../C/Corey_Olsen.md), Dave Kale, and Trish Lambert as hosts, included a positive mention of articles (particularly [Radagast](../R/Radagast.md)) on Tolkien Gateway.<sup id="cite_ref-50" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-50">[49]</a></sup>

On 22 November 2012, Tolkienseminariet ("The Tolkien Seminary") in Sweden, with notable members [Anders Stenström](../A/Anders_Stenström.md) and [Åke Bertenstam](../_/Åke_Bertenstam.md), positively reviewed Tolkien Gateway, saying that since 2003 the wiki "*has grown rapidly and now provides copious and dependable information about Middle-earth and not least about Tolkien's life and writings*".<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkienseminariet_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkienseminariet-2">[2]</a></sup>

*C.S. Lewis: An Annotated Bibliography and Resource* by P. H. Brazier is published in 2012 with multiple references to Tolkien Gateway.<sup id="cite_ref-51" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-51">[50]</a></sup>

#### 2013 {#2013}

In December 2013, surrounding the release of *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md)*, a surge in traffic meant that Tolkien Gateway briefly became a top-50,000 website worldwide.<sup id="cite_ref-Alexa_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Alexa-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the Brazilian translation of Michael White's <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tolkien:_A_Biography&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien: A Biography (page does not exist)">Tolkien: A Biography</a></i> (*J.R.R. Tolkien, O Senhor da Fantasia*), Tolkien Gateway is listed in the editor's appendix as one of the best websites about Tolkien.

#### 2014 {#2014}

On [18 May](../_/18_May.md) [2014](../_/2014.md), the online version of *The New York Times* provided a link to Tolkien Gateway's page about *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)*, in Ethan Gilsdorf's article "Waving His Wand at ‘Beowulf’".<sup id="cite_ref-52" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-52">[51]</a></sup> On 21 July 2014, the website Inquisitr referenced Tolkien Gateway's article on the [Inklings](../I/Inklings.md) in its news story about forthcoming Tolkien biopics.<sup id="cite_ref-53" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-53">[52]</a></sup>

On [20 May](../_/20_May.md) 2014, [Stuart D. Lee](../S/Stuart_D._Lee.md)'s book *[A Companion to J.R.R. Tolkien](../A/A_Companion_to_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* is released. It lists Tolkien Gateway under recommended "Further Reading", stating that it is *"An indespensible resource for Tolkien references and explanations"*.

On [7 August](../_/7_August.md) 2014, [Shaun Gunner](../S/Shaun_Gunner.md), Chairman of [The Tolkien Society](The_Tolkien_Society.md), made reference to Tolkien Gateway in an interview with [TheOneRing.net](TheOneRing.net.md), saying the website has "transformed the accessibility of Tolkien".<sup id="cite_ref-54" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-54">[53]</a></sup>

#### 2016 {#2016}

[Janet Brennan Croft](../J/Janet_Brennan_Croft.md) includes Tolkien Gateway in her article "Bibliographic Resources for Literature Searches on J.R.R Tolkien", noting that although the website is not mainly intended for academic use, it can be "*useful for locating the source of a quotation or piece of information within Tolkien's works, or for a brief bibliography of a particular scholar's works*".<sup id="cite_ref-55" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-55">[54]</a></sup>

*The Science of Middle-earth: A New Understanding of Tolkien and His World* by Jean-Sebastien Steyer, Loic Mangin, and Roland Lehoucq references Tolkien Gateway on its Acknowledgements page.<sup id="cite_ref-60" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-60">[59]</a></sup>

## Criticism {#Criticism}

<dl><dd><i>See also: <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiki#Trustworthiness" title="wikipedia:Wiki">Wiki#Trustworthiness</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criticism_of_Wikipedia" title="wikipedia:Criticism of Wikipedia">Criticism of Wikipedia</a> for criticism of wikis in general</i></dd></dl>

Like other wikis, Tolkien Gateway has been criticised for the reliability of its information, its lack of sources and the frequency of its revisions. In particular, in the summer of [2010](../_/2010.md), Tolkien Gateway was criticised for confusing the two articles for [Norman Power](../N/Norman_Power.md) and [Joseph Power](../J/Joseph_Power.md); the prominent scholars [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) said:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The Tolkien Gateway entry for Norman Power makes a terrible hash of it by conjoining Norman and Joseph. How on earth could the writer think that someone said to be born in 1925 (Joseph) could be a student at Oxford in the late 1930s? [..] Well, call us old-fashioned (we do), and we admit to having only a passing knowledge of the ways of wiki-creation, but it would never occur to us to post a work-in-progress, to be cleaned up by someone else, especially since people often take wiki articles, in any state of completion, as gospel truth.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md" title="Wayne G. Hammond">Wayne G. Hammond</a> and <a href="../C/Christina_Scull.md" title="Christina Scull">Christina Scull</a><sup id="cite_ref-Power_65-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Power-65">[64]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Power2_66-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Power2-66">[65]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>

Despite [Morgan](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Morgan) launching a defence of Tolkien Gateway on The Lord of the Rings Fanatics Plaza, although accepting that Tolkien Gateway was better than other online encyclopaedias, the members generally complained about the unreliability of Tolkien Gateway - and wikis in general - and the ephemeral nature of the Internet, with an agreement that there was a "*distressing lack of sources*".<sup id="cite_ref-Plaza_Critic_67-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Plaza_Critic-67">[66]</a></sup> The article <a class="external text" href="http://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=English_and_Welsh&amp;oldid=105660" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">English and Welsh</a> was paraded as a particularly bad example. In response to the criticisms raised, both the "Joseph Power"/"Norman Power" and "[English and Welsh](../E/English_and_Welsh.md)" articles were amended.<sup id="cite_ref-EandW_68-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EandW-68">[67]</a></sup>

In a review of Tolkien Gateway, Kristine Kastle claimed that it was only appropriate for the younger reader, it suffered from poor navigation, and the design was basic, lacking colour and that the life was sucked out of it; she gave Tolkien Gateway a score of 1.5 out of 4 which translates as, "*Well now at least the website is recognizable as a website; style is still not pretty much nonexistant.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Kastle_69-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kastle-69">[68]</a></sup> The skin has gone through several iterations since this review.<sup id="cite_ref-Updates_27-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Updates-27">[26]</a></sup>

Commenting on Tolkien Gateway, Troels Forchhammer says that "*\[i\]t suffers from the same problems as any wiki when the subject is one of some fame or notoriety and in particular when the subject lends itself to strongly held opinions*". He also marks that Tolkien Gateway is "*trying to add thorough references to their articles, but has still a way to go*."<sup id="cite_ref-70" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-70">[69]</a></sup>

After a suggestion from [User:Morgan](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Morgan), [Hawke Robinson](../H/Hawke_Robinson.md) and [Michael Martinez](../M/Michael_Martinez.md) discussed Tolkien Gateway on the *Middle-earth Radio Talk Show* on [8 August](../_/8_August.md) [2010](../_/2010.md). The radio hosts put forward a "*positive critique*", praising Tolkien Gateway for "*a great effort*", "*a nice layout*", and that the website should be "*recognized for all the work that has gone into this wiki*". However, they also addressed the issue that "*as all wikis, there's always room for improvement*", especially pointing out the need for "*better citation standards*".<sup id="cite_ref-71" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-71">[70]</a></sup>
