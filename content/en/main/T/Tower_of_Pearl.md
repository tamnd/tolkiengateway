The **Tower of Pearl** was located in one of the [Twilit Isles](/wiki/Twilit_Isles) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

Like the Twilit Isles, it was in the [Shadowy Seas](/wiki/Shadowy_Seas), west of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa). There the tower rose pale upon the most western cape of the Twilit Isles.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was built in later days after Tol Eressëa was established, and was much sung in song.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> During his travels, [Eärendel](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendel) was driven there, where he found the [Sleeper](/wiki/Sleeper) in the Tower of Pearl and woke them up with [Littleheart](/wiki/Littleheart)'s [gong](/wiki/Gong_of_the_Children "Gong of the Children").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Its name in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) was <i><b>Tirimbrithla</b></i>, from *[tirin](/wiki/Tirin "Tirin")* ("tower") + *[brithla](/wiki/Brithla "Brithla")* ("pearl").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [1915](/wiki/1915), before any narrative, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote a poem whose main subject is the Tower of Pearl in the Twilit Isles, titled *[The Happy Mariners](/wiki/The_Happy_Mariners)*, which gives some details about its appeareance: the tower was white, glimmering like a spike in the evening, and it stood upon a dark rock.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
