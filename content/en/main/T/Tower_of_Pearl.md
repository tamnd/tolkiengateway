The **Tower of Pearl** was located in one of the [Twilit Isles](Twilit_Isles.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

Like the Twilit Isles, it was in the [Shadowy Seas](../S/Shadowy_Seas.md), west of [Tol Eressëa](Tol_Eressëa.md). There the tower rose pale upon the most western cape of the Twilit Isles.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was built in later days after Tol Eressëa was established, and was much sung in song.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> During his travels, [Eärendel](../E/Eärendel.md) was driven there, where he found the [Sleeper](../S/Sleeper.md) in the Tower of Pearl and woke them up with [Littleheart](../L/Littleheart.md)'s [gong](../G/Gong_of_the_Children.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Its name in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) was <i><b>Tirimbrithla</b></i>, from *[tirin](Tirin.md)* ("tower") + *[brithla](../B/Brithla.md)* ("pearl").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [1915](../_/1915.md), before any narrative, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote a poem whose main subject is the Tower of Pearl in the Twilit Isles, titled *[The Happy Mariners](The_Happy_Mariners.md)*, which gives some details about its appeareance: the tower was white, glimmering like a spike in the evening, and it stood upon a dark rock.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
