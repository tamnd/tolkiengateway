**Thomas Kane Roberts** (1962) is an American voice actor. He has voiced a wide variety of recognizable characters in cartoons, including Professor Utonium of *The Powerpuff Girls*, Monkey Fist in *Kim Possible*, Darwin in *The Wild Thornberries* and Mr. Herriman in *Foster's Home for Imaginary Friends*. He also provides the voices for Yoda and [C-3PO](/wiki/Anthony_Daniels "Anthony Daniels") whenever their actors are not available.
