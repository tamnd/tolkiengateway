**Telemnar**, [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1516](/wiki/Third_Age_1516 "Third Age 1516")<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> - [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636"),<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup> was the twenty-sixth [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor).

## History {#History}

Telemnar came to the throne unexpectedly when his father [Minardil](/wiki/Minardil) was killed in a surprise raid by [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar). In response, he began outfitting a fleet to reduce the [Haven of Umbar](/wiki/Umbar "Umbar"), but this work was halted when the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) struck his realm.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> The King and all his children died in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> as did a large number of the people of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). The [White Tree](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor#Second_White_Tree "White Tree of Gondor") withered and died, [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) was partially deserted and began to fall into ruin, and the watch on [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) ceased.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

With Telemnar and all his heirs deceased, the Kingship passed on to his nephew [Tarondor](</wiki/Tarondor_(King_of_Gondor)> "Tarondor (King of Gondor)"), the son of Telemnar's younger brother [Minastan](/wiki/Minastan).<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Telemnar* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). Its meaning is not glossed, but as Paul Strack explains, it seems to mean "Silver Fire" and be a compound of an assimilated form [telep](/wiki/Telep "Telep") of [telpë](/wiki/Telp%C3%AB "Telpë") ("silver") and the element [nár](</wiki/N%C3%A1r_(Quenya)> "Nár (Quenya)") ("fire").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
