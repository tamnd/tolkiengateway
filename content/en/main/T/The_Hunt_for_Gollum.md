**The Hunt for Gollum** is a [fan film](/wiki/Category:Fan_films "Category:Fan films") based on *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. The film focuses on the events prior to *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* but after *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* as [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) tracks down [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum). Featured characters include [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum), with new characters *Arithir*, and *Goblok*. The film was made available to download for free on [May 3](/wiki/3_May "3 May"), [2009](/wiki/2009).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

As Gandalf learns the truth about the ring in Frodo's possession, he dispatches Aragorn to find and capture the creature Gollum, the ring's former carrier.
