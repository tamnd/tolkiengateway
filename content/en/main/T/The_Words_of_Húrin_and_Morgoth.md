**The Words of Húrin and Morgoth** is the third chapter of *[The Children of Húrin](The_Children_of_Húrin.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

No tidings of what had befallen in the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md) ever came to [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md)'s home, [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md).

Húrin was brought swiftly to [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) in [Angband](../A/Angband.md), for Morgoth had heard rumour that Húrin knew of the whereabouts of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) - and he desired to know the location of [Turgon](Turgon.md)'s city so he might have a fuller victory. But Húrin was undaunted by the power of the [Enemy](../M/Morgoth.md) and would not give up his knowledge, even under slow pain and torture. So Morgoth took Húrin to [Haudh-en-Nirnaeth](../H/Haudh-en-Nirnaeth.md), which was a mound freshly made of the bodies of the fallen at the very heart of the plain of [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md). There again Morgoth could not master Húrin's will and so in anger he cursed Húrin and [Morwen](../M/Morwen.md) and their offspring saying, *"the shadow of my thought shall lie upon them wherever they go."*

Yet still Húrin would not yield the secret of Turgon and indeed challenged and mocked Morgoth's power, feeling that the Enemy did not now have the authority in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) to master the fates of Men. Thus Morgoth took Húrin and set him in a chair on a high place of [Thangorodrim](Thangorodrim.md) until such a time as he would release him. And there Morgoth granted Húrin his own sight, that he might see and hear to the ends of the land of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), for Morgoth wished that Húrin would despair to see the evil tidings that would now befall Húrin's kin.
