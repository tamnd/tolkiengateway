**The Words of Húrin and Morgoth** is the third chapter of *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

No tidings of what had befallen in the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad "Nirnaeth Arnoediad") ever came to [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin)'s home, [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min).

Húrin was brought swiftly to [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in [Angband](/wiki/Angband), for Morgoth had heard rumour that Húrin knew of the whereabouts of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) - and he desired to know the location of [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon)'s city so he might have a fuller victory. But Húrin was undaunted by the power of the [Enemy](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") and would not give up his knowledge, even under slow pain and torture. So Morgoth took Húrin to [Haudh-en-Nirnaeth](/wiki/Haudh-en-Nirnaeth), which was a mound freshly made of the bodies of the fallen at the very heart of the plain of [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith). There again Morgoth could not master Húrin's will and so in anger he cursed Húrin and [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen) and their offspring saying, *"the shadow of my thought shall lie upon them wherever they go."*

Yet still Húrin would not yield the secret of Turgon and indeed challenged and mocked Morgoth's power, feeling that the Enemy did not now have the authority in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) to master the fates of Men. Thus Morgoth took Húrin and set him in a chair on a high place of [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim) until such a time as he would release him. And there Morgoth granted Húrin his own sight, that he might see and hear to the ends of the land of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), for Morgoth wished that Húrin would despair to see the evil tidings that would now befall Húrin's kin.
