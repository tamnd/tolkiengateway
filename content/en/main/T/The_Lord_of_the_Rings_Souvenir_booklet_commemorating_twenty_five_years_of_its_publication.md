<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Souvenir booklet commemorating twenty five years of its publication</b></i> is a booklet written by [Humphrey Carpenter](../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md). He recounts how *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* came to be written and published, and of its reception and fortunes since publication.

The booklet was distributed both separately and with the 25th Anniversary three-volume paperback edition of *The Lord of the Rings*.

It includes numerous brief excerpts from letters and reproduces:

- 1\) A manuscript page from *The Lord of the Rings* chapter "[The Shadow of the Past](The_Shadow_of_the_Past.md)".

- 2\) Page one of a letter to [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) dated 24 October 1952 in which Tolkien discusses the possible publication of *The Lord of the Rings*. Much of the letter was later included in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* (1981; see [Letter 135](../L/Letter_135.md)).

- 3\) Part of "The Gods Return to Earth", a review of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* by [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md).

- 4\) Part of "A Boy's World", a review of *[The Return of the King](The_Return_of_the_King.md)* by Edwin Muir.

- 5\) Part of "At the End of the Quest, Victory", a review of *[The Return of the King](The_Return_of_the_King.md)* by [W.H. Auden](../W/W.H._Auden.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
