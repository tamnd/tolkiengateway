<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Souvenir booklet commemorating twenty five years of its publication</b></i> is a booklet written by [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter). He recounts how *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* came to be written and published, and of its reception and fortunes since publication.

The booklet was distributed both separately and with the 25th Anniversary three-volume paperback edition of *The Lord of the Rings*.

It includes numerous brief excerpts from letters and reproduces:

- 1\) A manuscript page from *The Lord of the Rings* chapter "[The Shadow of the Past](/wiki/The_Shadow_of_the_Past)".

- 2\) Page one of a letter to [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) dated 24 October 1952 in which Tolkien discusses the possible publication of *The Lord of the Rings*. Much of the letter was later included in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)* (1981; see [Letter 135](/wiki/Letter_135)).

- 3\) Part of "The Gods Return to Earth", a review of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* by [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis).

- 4\) Part of "A Boy's World", a review of *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)* by Edwin Muir.

- 5\) Part of "At the End of the Quest, Victory", a review of *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)* by [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
