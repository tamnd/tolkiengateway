**Third Age 1879** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1879**) is the 1879th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1879 was also known as **S.R. 279** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Pelendur](../P/Pelendur.md) born, who served as steward to [Kings](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) and [Eärnil II](../E/Eärnil_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
