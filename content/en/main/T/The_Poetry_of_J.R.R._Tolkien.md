<i><b>The Poetry of J.R.R. Tolkien</b></i> is booklet by George Burke Johnston, containing an essay about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poetry. The essay features a reprint of Tolkien's poem "[The Root of the Boot](/wiki/The_Root_of_the_Boot)" (pages 10–11) which was first published in *[Songs for the Philologists](/wiki/Songs_for_the_Philologists)*. Burke Johnston's essay is a slightly revised version of that published in <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Tolkien_Papers&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Tolkien Papers (page does not exist)">The Tolkien Papers</a></i>.

The booklet was not intended for sale, and was privately circulated by Burke Johnston. A "specially printed, specially bound copy of the booklet" was sent to Tolkien, who [responded on 24 May 1968](/wiki/Letter_to_George_Burke_Johnston "Letter to George Burke Johnston").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The booklet was also printed in a 1968 edition, with red lettering on the cover instead of black.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
