The **Third Company of the Citadel**, was one group of [Guards of the Citadel](../G/Guards_of_the_Citadel.md) under the command of the 26th [Steward of Gondor](../S/Steward_of_Gondor.md), [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md)

[Beregond](../B/Beregond.md), one of the few known guards, was assigned the task of guiding and befriending [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) during the time before [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s siege on [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
