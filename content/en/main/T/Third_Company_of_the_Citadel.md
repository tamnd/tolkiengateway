The **Third Company of the Citadel**, was one group of [Guards of the Citadel](/wiki/Guards_of_the_Citadel) under the command of the 26th [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor), [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor)

[Beregond](/wiki/Beregond), one of the few known guards, was assigned the task of guiding and befriending [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) during the time before [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s siege on [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
