**Third Age 2570** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2570**) is the 2570th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2570 was also known as **S.R. 970** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Baldor](../B/Baldor.md), son of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Brego](../B/Brego.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), enters the [Forbidden Door](../D/Dark_Door.md) and is lost.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of King Brego, who dies of grief because of the loss of Baldor.<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Aldor](../A/Aldor.md), the second son of Brego, becomes the third King of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Fréa](../F/Fréa.md), fourth child and son of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Aldor](../A/Aldor.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [War of the Dwarves and Dragons](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons.md): About this time [Dragons](../D/Dragons.md) reappear in the [far North](../N/Northern_Waste.md) and begin to afflict the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
