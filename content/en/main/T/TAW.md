**TAW**, or **TAWA**, is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "wood".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin): *tau-rē* ("forest")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[taure](/wiki/Taure "Taure")*<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[taur](/wiki/Taur#Sindarin "Taur")*<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *töa* ("wood as material"); *tauka* ("stiff, wooden"); *toina* ("wood - of material" or "wooden of make")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *taw* ("wood as material"); *tāva* ("great tree"); *tawen, tewin* ("wood - of material" or "wooden of make"); *taug* ("firm, strong")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root TAVA, signifying "beam".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies") appears the root TÁWAR-, signifying "wood, forest".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
