<b><i>Tolkien Studies</i>: Volume 10</b>, published in [2013](/wiki/2013), is the tenth volume of the annual review *[Tolkien Studies](</wiki/Tolkien_Studies_(journal)> "Tolkien Studies (journal)")*.

This issue reprints J.R.R. Tolkien's poem "[Once upon a Time](/wiki/Once_upon_a_Time)", first published in [1969](/wiki/1969). It was later included in *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)* [2014](/wiki/2014) edition.

    - *[Exploring J.R.R. Tolkien and the Origins of The Hobbit")*, by [Mark Atherton](/wiki/Mark_Atherton), reviewed by [Jason Fisher](/wiki/Jason_Fisher)
    - *[Green Suns and Faërie](/wiki/Green_Suns_and_Fa%C3%ABrie)*, by [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger), reviewed by [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff)
    - *[The Broken Scythe](/wiki/The_Broken_Scythe)*, edited by Roberto Arduini and Claudio A. Testi, reviewed by [John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth)
    - *A Hobbit Journey*, by [Matthew Dickerson](/wiki/Matthew_Dickerson), and *A Hobbit Devotional*, by Ed Strauss, reviewed by Donald T. Williams
