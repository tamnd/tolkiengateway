**Taniquetil** or **[Oiolossë](../O/Oiolossë.md)**, the great **Holy Mountain**, was the highest of the mountains of [Pelóri](../P/Pelóri.md) and the tallest peak in [Arda](../A/Arda.md). On its summit was raised [Ilmarin](../I/Ilmarin.md), the mansions of [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) and [Varda](../V/Varda.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-1">[1]</a></sup>

According to *[The Annals of Aman](The_Annals_of_Aman.md)*, it was raised 3,450 [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md) (or 33,000 solar years) after the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) arrived in [Aman](../A/Aman.md).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Taniquetil* (or *Taníquetil*) is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) (pronounced <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[taˈnikʷetil]</span>) and means "high-snow-peak"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> or "High White Peak".<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-1">[1]</a></sup> The name can be analyzed as <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=T%C4%80&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TĀ (page does not exist)">ta-</a></i> (cf. *tára*) "high", <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Nique&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nique (page does not exist)">nique</a></i> "white" and *[til](TIL~40097.md)* "point".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The mountain was also called **[Amon Uilos](../A/Amon_Uilos.md)**, the White Mountain, the Mountain of Manwë,<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-1">[1]</a></sup> *Arfanyarassë* (or *Arfanyaras*; [Q](../Q/Quenya.md). "High shining white peak"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> *Taniquetilde Tára*,<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> and *[Elerrína](../E/Elerrína.md)*.

Taniquetil is also called *Oiolossë* (also spelled *Oiolosse*),<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-5">[5]</a></sup> which is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and means "Ever-snow-white",<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-1">[1]</a></sup> "Everwhite/snowy",<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-5">[5]</a></sup> or "Everlasting snow".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety379_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety379-6">[6]</a></sup> The name seems to consist of *[oio](../O/Oio.md)* "ever" (cf. root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=OY&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="OY (page does not exist)">OY-</a>)<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ety379_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety379-6">[6]</a></sup> and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Losse&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Losse (page does not exist)">losse</a></i> "snow".<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [Eriol](../E/Eriol.md)'s [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) translations, Oiolossë is referred to as *Sinsnáw, Sinsnǽwen* "Ever-snow".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In the *[Eärendillinwë](../E/Eärendillinwë.md)* it is named **Hill of [Ilmarin](../I/Ilmarin.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In [Valarin](../V/Valarin.md) it was called *dāhan-igwiš-telgūn*, or less properly *Dahanigwistilgūn*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

*Elerrína* was another name for Taniquetil, translating to "Crowned with Stars" ([elen](../E/Elen.md) + <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=R%C3%ADna&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rína (page does not exist)">rína</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> It is spelt "Elerína" in *[Etymologies](The_Etymologies.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
