**The 3rd Age**, also known as the **T3A Community**, is a large modding community on the internet which hosts mods for *[The Battle for Middle-Earth Anthology](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_Anthology "The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth Anthology")* and has over one hundred tutorials about making modifications, modeling, coding, skinning, scripting and mapping.
