**The 3rd Age**, also known as the **T3A Community**, is a large modding community on the internet which hosts mods for *[The Battle for Middle-Earth Anthology](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_Anthology.md)* and has over one hundred tutorials about making modifications, modeling, coding, skinning, scripting and mapping.
