**Timothy R. O'Neill** ([1943](../_/1943.md)-[2023](../_/2023.md)) was a retired US Army lieutenant-colonel and former professor of Engineering Psychology at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Point" title="wikipedia:West Point">West Point</a>. He was credited with groundbreaking research in digital camouflage known as *DualTex*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He wrote two books in his life, one fictional set in West Point, titled *Shades of Grey*, and one on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Jung" title="wikipedia:Carl Jung">Jungian</a> elements in [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s work. O'Neill passed away in November 2023 at the age of 80.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
