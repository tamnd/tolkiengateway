**Tar-Anducal** was the royal name assumed by **Herucalmo** after the death of his wife, [Tar-Vanimeldë](Tar-Vanimeldë.md), the third [Ruling Queen of Númenor](../R/Ruling_Queen_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Vanimelde_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Vanimelde-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Herucalmo was a descendant of [Tar-Atanamir](Tar-Atanamir.md) and married [Tar-Vanimeldë](Tar-Vanimeldë.md), the daughter of [Tar-Telemmaitë](Tar-Telemmaitë.md) and an older relative and descendant of the same degree from Tar-Atanamir. Together they had a son, [Alcarin](Tar-Alcarin.md). Tar-Vanimeldë had little interest in her duties as a ruler (preferring to indulge in music and dance) so Herucalmo wielded the power of the [Sceptre of Númenor](../S/Sceptre_of_Númenor.md). When his wife died, Herucalmo usurped the kingship that should have passed on to their son, and ruled [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) for twenty years until he died in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [2657](../S/Second_Age_2657.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Vanimelde_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Vanimelde-1">[1]</a></sup>

Upon his death the [Kingship](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) reverted to the rightful heir, [Tar-Alcarin](Tar-Alcarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Vanimelde_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Vanimelde-1">[1]</a></sup>

"Tar-Anducal" being an usurper was not reckoned in the Line of Kings as the seventeenth ruler of Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Vanimelde_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Vanimelde-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Herucalmo* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. As Paul Strack suggests it seems to be a combination of [heru](../H/Heru.md) ("lord"), [cala](../C/Cala.md) ("light") with the masculine agentive suffix [-mo](../_/-mo.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Anducal* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests that it perhaps means "Light of the West" as a combination of *andu* ("west") and [cala](../C/Cala.md) ("light").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Like all the rulers of Númenor who took their royal names in Quenya, *Anducal* added the prefix *tar-* ("high") to his name when he received the [Sceptre](../S/Sceptre_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
