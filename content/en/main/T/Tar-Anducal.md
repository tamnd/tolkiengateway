**Tar-Anducal** was the royal name assumed by **Herucalmo** after the death of his wife, [Tar-Vanimeldë](/wiki/Tar-Vanimeld%C3%AB), the third [Ruling Queen of Númenor](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Vanimelde_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Vanimelde-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Herucalmo was a descendant of [Tar-Atanamir](/wiki/Tar-Atanamir) and married [Tar-Vanimeldë](/wiki/Tar-Vanimeld%C3%AB), the daughter of [Tar-Telemmaitë](/wiki/Tar-Telemmait%C3%AB) and an older relative and descendant of the same degree from Tar-Atanamir. Together they had a son, [Alcarin](/wiki/Tar-Alcarin "Tar-Alcarin"). Tar-Vanimeldë had little interest in her duties as a ruler (preferring to indulge in music and dance) so Herucalmo wielded the power of the [Sceptre of Númenor](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor). When his wife died, Herucalmo usurped the kingship that should have passed on to their son, and ruled [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) for twenty years until he died in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [2657](/wiki/Second_Age_2657 "Second Age 2657").<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Vanimelde_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Vanimelde-1">[1]</a></sup>

Upon his death the [Kingship](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") reverted to the rightful heir, [Tar-Alcarin](/wiki/Tar-Alcarin).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Vanimelde_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Vanimelde-1">[1]</a></sup>

"Tar-Anducal" being an usurper was not reckoned in the Line of Kings as the seventeenth ruler of Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Vanimelde_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Vanimelde-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Herucalmo* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. As Paul Strack suggests it seems to be a combination of [heru](/wiki/Heru "Heru") ("lord"), [cala](/wiki/Cala "Cala") ("light") with the masculine agentive suffix [-mo](/wiki/-mo).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Anducal* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests that it perhaps means "Light of the West" as a combination of *andu* ("west") and [cala](/wiki/Cala "Cala") ("light").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Like all the rulers of Númenor who took their royal names in Quenya, *Anducal* added the prefix *tar-* ("high") to his name when he received the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Sceptre of Númenor").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
