**The Istari** is a chapter in [Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales), a posthumously published work of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) edited and published by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

In the section is described the background of the five wizards:

- [Alatar](/wiki/Blue_Wizards "Blue Wizards")
- [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)
- [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)
- [Pallando](/wiki/Blue_Wizards "Blue Wizards")
- [Radagast](/wiki/Radagast)

The main focus however is on Gandalf and Saruman and specifically their names in the various languages of Middle-earth.

It is important to note that the section is a composition of various manuscripts and printed texts from [The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion) and [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) with comments by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) rather than a singular document. In the notes some information on [Queen Berúthiel](/wiki/Queen_Ber%C3%BAthiel) is also disclosed. There is also a <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Wilt_thou_learn_the_lore&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Wilt thou learn the lore (page does not exist)">poem</a> about [Dagor Dagorath](/wiki/Dagor_Dagorath).
