**Tar-Telemmaitë** was the fifteenth [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Telemmaite_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Telemmaite-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

From his time onwards, the Kings ruled from the death of their predecessor to their own death, but actual power passed often to their sons and counsellors when they reached old age. The lifetime of the descendants of [Elros](/wiki/Elros) decreased because the Shadow had fallen on Númenor. Tar-Telemmaitë loved silver and had his servants always searching for *mithril*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Telemmaite_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Telemmaite-1">[1]</a></sup>

Tar-Telemmaitë either had only one child or his eldest child was a daughter, for upon his death he was succeeded by [Tar-Vanimeldë](/wiki/Tar-Vanimeld%C3%AB), the third [Ruling Queen](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Ruling Queen of Númenor").<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Vanimelde_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Vanimelde-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Telemmaitë* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. According to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) it means "Silver-handed".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests that it seems to be a compound of an assimilated form of [telpë](/wiki/Telp%C3%AB "Telpë") ("silver") and the adjective element [maitë](/wiki/Mait%C3%AB "Maitë") ("handed").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> He was called *Telemmaitë*, because of his love of silver.<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Telemmaite_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Telemmaite-1">[1]</a></sup> Like all the rulers of Númenor who took their royal names in Quenya, *Telemmaitë* added the prefix *tar-* ("high") to his name when he received the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Sceptre of Númenor").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
