The **Tower of Ecthelion**,<sup id="cite_ref-MT751_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT751-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> also known as the **White Tower**<sup id="cite_ref-MT752_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT752-3">[3]</a></sup> or the **Tower of Denethor**<sup id="cite_ref-Palantir_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantir-4">[4]</a></sup> was the chief tower of the [Citadel](/wiki/Citadel_of_Gondor "Citadel of Gondor") of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_At_the_Court_of_the_Fountain.jpg"><img src="/w/images/e/ef/Ted_Nasmith_-_At_the_Court_of_the_Fountain.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="272" height="450" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="272" data-file-height="450"/></a><figcaption><i>At the Court of the Fountain</i> by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

The Tower of Ecthelion stood in the centre of the Citadel<sup id="cite_ref-SD_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SD-6">[6]</a></sup> on the topmost seventh circle of walls of the city. The tower was 300 feet tall with a banner at the top.<sup id="cite_ref-MT752_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT752-3">[3]</a></sup> Under the sun, the Tower's shapely form shone and glimmered like a spike of crystals, pearl and silver against the sky. White banners waved from its battlements.<sup id="cite_ref-MT751_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT751-1">[1]</a></sup> At the base of the tower was the [Tower Hall](/wiki/Tower_Hall), a great hall where the throne<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> of the [King of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") stood upon a dais of many steps, and at the foot the dais on the lowest step was a black stone chair where the [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor") sat.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The tower also contained a high chamber<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> where probably the [palantír](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri") of Minas Tirith, the [Anor-stone](/wiki/Anor-stone), was kept.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

In front (i.e. east) of the tower was the [Court of the Fountain](</wiki/Court_of_the_Fountain_(Minas_Tirith)> "Court of the Fountain (Minas Tirith)")<sup id="cite_ref-MT752_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT752-3">[3]</a></sup> where the [White Tree of Gondor](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor) grew.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> To the right (i.e. north) of the tower was [Merethrond](/wiki/Merethrond), the Great Hall of Feasts and behind (i.e. west) of the tower was the [King's House](/wiki/King%27s_House).<sup id="cite_ref-SD_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SD-6">[6]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The White Tower was first built by King [Calimehtar](</wiki/Calimehtar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Calimehtar (King of Gondor)") in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1900](/wiki/Third_Age_1900 "Third Age 1900").<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2698](/wiki/Third_Age_2698 "Third Age 2698"), the White Tower was rebuilt by Steward [Ecthelion I](/wiki/Ecthelion_I).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

On [March 9](/wiki/March_9), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) got his first glimpse of the Tower of Ecthelion as he rode with [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-MT751_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT751-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [May 1](/wiki/May_1), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) was crowned King and when he entered the Citadel, the [standard of Elendil](/wiki/Standard_of_Elendil "Standard of Elendil") bearing the Tree and the Stars was unfurled on the pinnacle of the Tower of Ecthelion.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

A late manuscript version mentions that Calimehtar (spelled Kalimehtar in that version) built the White Tower to house the palantír.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>
