**The Fellowship** was a web-based Dutch language Tolkien community, with real life meetings, centred around [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md)'s [*The Lord of the Rings* films](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>).

## History {#History}

The Fellowship commenced prior to the release of the films in 2002.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Fellowship, and especially its forum, was very active through 2006. When the server crashed in 2006 however without available backup almost all information was lost.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> A smaller core of active participants continued with an alternative website that was started earlier in 2004.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Fellowship organized in person events, usually centered around the movies, during its existence.

As at 2011 there is very little activity on the new website and its forum. As at 2014 the new website was also defunct.
