<i><b>The Tolkien Scrapbook</b></i> is a book which contains poems, stories, songs, recipes, puzzles and essays about [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) from a multitude of authors ([W.H. Auden](../W/W.H._Auden.md), <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Edmund_Wilson&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Edmund Wilson (page does not exist)">Edmund Wilson</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Colin_Wilson&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Colin Wilson (page does not exist)">Colin Wilson</a>, and others), interspersed with short quotes from [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), color illustrations by [Tim Kirk](Tim_Kirk.md) and black and white illustrations by [Michael Green](../M/Michael_Green.md). The book also includes a drawing by Tolkien,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> "<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Lonely_Mountain_(J.R.R._Tolkien_drawing&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Lonely Mountain (J.R.R. Tolkien drawing (page does not exist)">The Lonely Mountain</a>", (pp. 114–15).

In [1989](../_/1989.md), the title was changed to "A Tolkien Treasury".

- Note: In [2001](../_/2001.md), samples from some of the texts and pictures were published in *[A Tolkien Treasury (miniature edition)](<../A/A_Tolkien_Treasury_(miniature_edition).md>)*.
