## Back cover {#Back_cover}

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s magical world of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) - a land inhabited by [tobacco](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed")-smoking, ale-brewing, [Halfling](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") superheroes, eon-old [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") kings, chanting time-travellers, messianic [wizards](/wiki/Istari "Istari") and satanic [war-lords](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron") - remains unparalleled in its scope and vision and unsurpassed in its appeal and fascination for countless millions of people of all ages the world over.

Now, in this long-awaited study, Professor Helms shows how the master story-teller succeeded in combining a scholarly knowledge of traditional mythological literature with an imaginative genius to create fantasy epics of universal significance.
