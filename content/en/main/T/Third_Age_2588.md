**Third Age 2588** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2588**) is the 2588th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2588 was also known as **S.R. 988** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Chieftain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md) of the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md) [Aragost](../A/Aragost.md).<sup id="cite_ref-North_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Aravorn](../A/Aravorn.md), the son of Aragost, becomes the ninth Chieftain.<sup id="cite_ref-North_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of the unnamed thirteenth [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
