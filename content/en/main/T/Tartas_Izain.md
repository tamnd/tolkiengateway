**Tartas Izain** was a sorcerer invented for [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing). A disciple of the [Blind Sorcerer](/wiki/Fifth_of_the_Nazg%C3%BBl "Fifth of the Nazgûl"), he was a central figure in the political and religious subversion of the region of [Sîrayn](/wiki/S%C3%AErayn) during the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), serving as the *Taraskon* ([Ta.](/wiki/Languages_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing#Tazinain "Languages in Middle-earth Role Playing") "Wisdom-lord") of the city of [Tûl Isra](/wiki/T%C3%BBl_Isra).

## Biography {#Biography}

Tartas Izain was born in Isra to a Haradan lord and an abducted Elven maiden in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1350&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1350 (page does not exist)">1350</a>. At some point<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>, his mother died of a disease, and the following year he was sent to his uncle Shuftas Gabar in [Charnesra](/wiki/Charnesra). Under his uncle's tutelage, Tartas began his training in the dark arts, quickly surpassing his instructor.

At the age of 23, Tartas received an emissary from [Ny Chennacatt](/wiki/Ny_Chennacatt) inviting him to train under the Blind Sorcerer. He departed a year later. During this time, he was tutored in advanced sorcery and initiated into the worship of the [Dark Lord](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron"). Upon his departure at the age of 34, Akhôrahil gifted him a [lesser ring](/wiki/Lesser_rings "Lesser rings") to prolong his life.

Tartas then infiltrated the ruling council of Tûl Isra together with clan Bulgan, and was appointed Taraskon in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1385&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1385 (page does not exist)">1385</a>. He revitalized the floundering cult of Taimaraud, a God of the Dead, by corrupting them into worship [Silent One](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron"), with temples to the Silent One appearing by [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1397&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1397 (page does not exist)">1397</a>.

His reign lasted until [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1436&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1436 (page does not exist)">1436</a>, when Clan Másra regained the throne of Tûl Isra, forcing the sorcerer to flee to Charnesra. Seeking revenge, Tartas convinced his uncle Shuftas Gabar to launch an unannounced attack on his former city, but Charnesra ends up being destroyed.

Following his military failure, Tartas returned to Ny Chennacatt. He remained there until [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1627&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1627 (page does not exist)">1627</a>, when he returned to Tûl Isra to serve again as the Taraskon.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

When clan Bulgan was once again overthrown in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1641&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1641 (page does not exist)">1641</a>, he fled to Ny Chennacatt. As punishment, he was decapitated and his ring was given to warlord [Utchû](/wiki/Army_of_the_Southern_Dragon#Utchû "Army of the Southern Dragon").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
