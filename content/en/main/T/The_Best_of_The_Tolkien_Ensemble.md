<i><b>The Best of The Tolkien Ensemble</b></i> is a compilation album by the [Tolkien Ensemble](/wiki/Tolkien_Ensemble). It features fourteen tracks from their previously released albums and four newly released live tracks.

## Track listing {#Track_listing}

1. Verse of the Rings
2. Galadriel's Song of Eldamar (I)
3. A Walking Song (I)
4. The Old Walking Song (I)
5. There is an inn, a merry old inn...
6. Gollum's Song / Riddle
7. Song of Gondor
8. The Ents' Marching Song
9. Treebeard's Song
10. Song of Lebennin
11. Sam's Song in the Orc-tower
12. A Walking Song (II)
13. Elven Hymn to Elbereth Gilthoniel (III)
14. Farewell Song of Merry &amp; Pippin
15. Hobbit tunes: Jigs 'n' Reels, Live from Ledreborg Castle Concerts 2005
16. The Eagle's Song, Live from Ledreborg Castle Concerts 2005
17. Galadriel's Song of Eldamar (I), Live from Musical Theater Bremen 2008
18. Jigs 'n' Reels (encore version), Live from Musical Theater Bremen 2008
