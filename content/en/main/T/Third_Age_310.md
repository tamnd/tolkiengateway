**Third Age 310** (abbreviated to **T.A. 310**) is the 310th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Tarostar](Tarostar.md) (the future [Rómendacil I](../R/Rómendacil_I.md)), son of [Ostoher](../O/Ostoher.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PMElendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMElendil-1">[1]</a></sup>
