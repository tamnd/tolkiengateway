**Third Age 2935** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2935**) is the 2935th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2935 was also known as **S.R. 1335** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Odovacar Bolger](../O/Odovacar_Bolger.md), son of [Herugar Bolger](../H/Herugar_Bolger.md) and [Jessamine Boffin](../J/Jessamine_Boffin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
