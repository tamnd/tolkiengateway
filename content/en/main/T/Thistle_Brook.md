**Thistle Brook** was a stream of the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). From [Green Hill Country](/wiki/Green_Hill_Country) it flowed south-east round [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End) and through the village of [Willowbottom](/wiki/Willowbottom) before its waters emptied into the River [Shirebourn](/wiki/Shirebourn).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Thistles presumably grew around it.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
