<i><b>The Fellows Hip: Rise of the Gamers</b></i> is feature-length comedy adventure film which features gamers on a journey to a *[The Lord of the Rings Online](The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)* tournament, featuring many parody references to *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* along the way.<sup id="cite_ref-official_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-official-1">[1]</a></sup> It had its UK première at [The Return of the Ring](The_Return_of_the_Ring.md) on [16 August](../_/16_August.md), [2012](../_/2012.md).

## Plot {#Plot}

A group of friends embark on an epic journey to find a new world they have only heard of, encountering dangerous obstacles and threats around every corner. During their quest they lose one of their most precious possessions and must display depths of bravery and heroism that they never thought possible. With the aid of some unexpected allies along the way, the companions fiercely look for certainty that they can reach their destination.

## Production {#Production}

The writing of the script started in 2007, while production of the actual film began on 1 May 2008 with the founding of Opening Act Productions. Newcomb received permission from both [Turbine](Turbine.md) and [Middle-earth Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md) to reference the Lord of the Rings. Turbine especially was noted to be of great help, letting the team use a test server and special characters for footage.<sup id="cite_ref-official_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-official-1">[1]</a></sup>
