**The Old** was a title of three [Men](../M/Men.md) who lived to advanced age: [Bëor](../B/Bëor.md), [Aldor](../A/Aldor.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) and [Grimbeorn](../G/Grimbeorn.md) the son of [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md).

Apparently the youngest of the three was [Grimbeorn](../G/Grimbeorn.md), who is reported to have ruled in the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md) in the year [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3018](Third_Age_3018.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The evidence points strongly to his being born after [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)'s visit to his father in [2941](Third_Age_2941.md), so his age could be no more than 77 at the time he is called 'The Old'. He may, of course, have lived longer that this.

Bëor was the first of Men to encounter the [Elves](../E/Elves.md), having led his people across the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) into [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). He was 49 at the time of that meeting, and died after another forty-four years of service to [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md), at the age of 93.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The oldest of the three, though, was Aldor, the third King of Rohan. He lived for more than a century, 101 years to be precise, and ruled the land of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) for no less than seventy-five years.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The epithet was also applied to [Forlong](../F/Forlong.md), [Lord of Lossarnach](../L/Lord_of_Lossarnach.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> though he was more commonly identified as '<a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="The_Fat.md" title="The Fat">The Fat</a>'.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
