<i><b>Tolkien&#39;s Legendarium: Essays on The History of Middle-earth</b></i> is a collection of scholarly essays edited by [Verlyn Flieger](../V/Verlyn_Flieger.md) and [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md) on the *[History of Middle-earth](The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)*, a series of books relating to the fiction of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), compiled and edited by his son, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md). It was published by Greenwood Press in [2000](../_/2000.md).

It includes a bibliography of works by Christopher Tolkien compiled by [Douglas A. Anderson](../D/Douglas_A._Anderson.md).

The book is the winner of the 2002 Mythopoeic Scholarship Award for Inklings Studies.
