<i><b>Tolkien&#39;s Legendarium: Essays on The History of Middle-earth</b></i> is a collection of scholarly essays edited by [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger) and [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) on the *[History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth "The History of Middle-earth")*, a series of books relating to the fiction of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), compiled and edited by his son, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien). It was published by Greenwood Press in [2000](/wiki/2000).

It includes a bibliography of works by Christopher Tolkien compiled by [Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson).

The book is the winner of the 2002 Mythopoeic Scholarship Award for Inklings Studies.
