<i><b>The Tolkien Quiz Book</b></i> is a [1981](/wiki/1981) book by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nigel_Robinson&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nigel Robinson (page does not exist)">Nigel Robinson</a> and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Linda_Wilson&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Linda Wilson (page does not exist)">Linda Wilson</a>. It features questions on all works by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") that were published at the time: *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*, *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*, *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)* and *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)*.
