**Third Age 2957** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2957**) is the 2957th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2957 was also known as **S.R. 1357** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn.md) begins his great journeys and errantries as [Thorongil](Thorongil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ta_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Around this time he enters the service of King [Thengel](Thengel.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ta_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-1">[1]</a></sup>
