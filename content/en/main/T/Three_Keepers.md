The **Three Keepers** were the guardians of the [Three Rings](Three_Rings.md) of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md), a term used particularly to refer to those who held the Rings at the end of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md), and departed over the Sea with them.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The guardianship was unknown throughout the whole Age and it was only known later that they were held by the greatest among the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Third_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-2">[2]</a></sup> The Three Keepers were [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) (who received [Vilya](../V/Vilya.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Havens_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Havens-3">[3]</a></sup> from [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md)), [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) (who held [Nenya](../N/Nenya.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Havens_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Havens-3">[3]</a></sup>) and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) (who received [Narya](../N/Narya.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> from [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-Third_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-2">[2]</a></sup>
