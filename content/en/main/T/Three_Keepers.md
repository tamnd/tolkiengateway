The **Three Keepers** were the guardians of the [Three Rings](/wiki/Three_Rings) of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), a term used particularly to refer to those who held the Rings at the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), and departed over the Sea with them.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The guardianship was unknown throughout the whole Age and it was only known later that they were held by the greatest among the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar).<sup id="cite_ref-Third_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-2">[2]</a></sup> The Three Keepers were [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) (who received [Vilya](/wiki/Vilya)<sup id="cite_ref-Havens_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Havens-3">[3]</a></sup> from [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad)), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) (who held [Nenya](/wiki/Nenya)<sup id="cite_ref-Havens_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Havens-3">[3]</a></sup>) and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) (who received [Narya](/wiki/Narya)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> from [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan)).<sup id="cite_ref-Third_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-2">[2]</a></sup>
