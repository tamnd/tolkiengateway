**Third Age 2190** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2190**) is the 2190th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2190 was also known as **S.R. 590** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Thráin I](Thráin_I.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Thorin I](Thorin_I.md) becomes [King of Durin's Folk](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) and [King under the Mountain](../K/Kings_under_the_Mountain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>
