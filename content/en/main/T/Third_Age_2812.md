**Third Age 2812** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2812**) is the 2812th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2812 was also known as **S.R. 1212** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Otto Boffin](../O/Otto_Boffin.md), son of [Bosco Boffin](../B/Bosco_Boffin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Pansy Baggins](../P/Pansy_Baggins.md), second child and first daughter of [Balbo Baggins](../B/Balbo_Baggins.md) and [Berylla Boffin](../B/Berylla_Boffin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
