**Tim Kirk** is an American artist who has illustrated various scenes from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s works.

Kirk's illustrations became known to Tolkien fans when they appeared in early American fanzines. He completed a series of twenty-six paintings of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* for his Master's Degree in Illustration, some of which were published by Ballantine Books in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar 1975](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar_1975)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
