**Tanta Hornblower** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Tanta married [Largo Baggins](../L/Largo_Baggins.md), and was the mother of [Fosco Baggins](../F/Fosco_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Tanta* was meaningless in contemporary [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) [Westron](../W/Westron.md). It represented an original name *Tante* or *Tanto*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
