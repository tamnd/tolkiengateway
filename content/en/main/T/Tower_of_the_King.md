The **Tower of the King** or the **Tower of Turgon**,<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup> was a high and powerful tower in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). In its fountain-filled gardens stood images of the [Two Trees of Valinor](Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md), made by [Turgon](Turgon.md) himself, called [Glingal and Belthil](../G/Glingal_and_Belthil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), the Tower was defended by the men of the king's house-hold until it was overthrown, "and great and mighty was its fall and the fall of Turgon in its ruin."<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

There are more details told in the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) of *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. It is said that it was the loftiest of all towers in Gondolin. When [Tuor](Tuor.md) and [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md) came to Gondolin, they were received by the King on the stairs, with [Meglin](../M/Meglin.md) at his right hand and [Idril](../I/Idril.md) at his left. There Tuor gave [Ulmo's warning](../U/Ulmo's_warning.md) to the King with prophetical words.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:160–1</sup>

During the siege of the city, Turgon watched most of the battle from his tower, sending troops where they were needed.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:180</sup> Once the King ordered the survivors to leave the city, he remained behind, climbing to the topmost pinnacle of the white tower where he cried: "*Great is the victory of the [Noldoli](../N/Noldoli.md)!*". While the [exiles](../E/Exiles_of_Gondolin.md) left, the [house of the King](../H/House_of_the_King.md) gathered around the base of the king's tower to fight till the end. Later, [Idril](../I/Idril.md) and Tuor saw from [Gar Ainion](../G/Gar_Ainion.md) how the tower was beset. Then the [dragons](../D/Dragons.md) crushed the base and the tower fell with a terrible clangour, and thus died King Turgon.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:185–7</sup>
