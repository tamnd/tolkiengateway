The **Tower of the King** or the **Tower of Turgon**,<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup> was a high and powerful tower in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin). In its fountain-filled gardens stood images of the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor), made by [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) himself, called [Glingal and Belthil](/wiki/Glingal_and_Belthil).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), the Tower was defended by the men of the king's house-hold until it was overthrown, "and great and mighty was its fall and the fall of Turgon in its ruin."<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

There are more details told in the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") of *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. It is said that it was the loftiest of all towers in Gondolin. When [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) came to Gondolin, they were received by the King on the stairs, with [Meglin](/wiki/Meglin) at his right hand and [Idril](/wiki/Idril) at his left. There Tuor gave [Ulmo's warning](/wiki/Ulmo%27s_warning) to the King with prophetical words.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:160–1</sup>

During the siege of the city, Turgon watched most of the battle from his tower, sending troops where they were needed.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:180</sup> Once the King ordered the survivors to leave the city, he remained behind, climbing to the topmost pinnacle of the white tower where he cried: "*Great is the victory of the [Noldoli](/wiki/Noldoli)!*". While the [exiles](/wiki/Exiles_of_Gondolin "Exiles of Gondolin") left, the [house of the King](/wiki/House_of_the_King "House of the King") gathered around the base of the king's tower to fight till the end. Later, [Idril](/wiki/Idril) and Tuor saw from [Gar Ainion](/wiki/Gar_Ainion) how the tower was beset. Then the [dragons](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") crushed the base and the tower fell with a terrible clangour, and thus died King Turgon.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:185–7</sup>
