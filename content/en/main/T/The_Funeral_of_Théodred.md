**The Funeral of Théodred** is the twenty-first scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)"). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

After [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) has released [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) from the enchantment placed upon him by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) - and Saruman's lackey [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma") has been banished from [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) - the king learns that his son [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred) has died. He leads the people of [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras), along with Gandalf and the members of the Fellowship, in a funeral procession outside the walls of the city. As Théodred body is lowered into its burial mound, [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) performs a lament in [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese).
