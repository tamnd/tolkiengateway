<i><b>The J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar 1984</b></i> was the official Tolkien calendar published by [Unwin Paperbacks](../U/Unwin_Hyman.md) in [1983](../_/1983.md). It features art by [Roger Garland](../R/Roger_Garland.md) based on *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)*.
