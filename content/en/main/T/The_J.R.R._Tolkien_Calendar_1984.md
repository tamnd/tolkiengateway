<i><b>The J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar 1984</b></i> was the official Tolkien calendar published by [Unwin Paperbacks](/wiki/Unwin_Hyman "Unwin Hyman") in [1983](/wiki/1983). It features art by [Roger Garland](/wiki/Roger_Garland) based on *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.
