**Third Age 2883** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2883**) is the 2883rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2883 was also known as **S.R. 1283** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Lalia Clayhanger](../L/Lalia_Clayhanger.md) is born.

## Calculation of the Date {#Calculation_of_the_Date}

[Fortinbras Took II](../F/Fortinbras_Took_II.md) married Lalia in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1314](Third_Age_2914.md) when she was 31 years old.<sup id="cite_ref-L214_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L214-1">[1]</a></sup> Subtracting 31 from 1314 places Lalia's birth in 1283 by the [Shire Reckoning](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md); adding 1600 converts the year to the reckoning of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
