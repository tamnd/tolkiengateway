**The Whole Experience** is a North American independent video game developer. It developed the Xbox port of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (video game)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)").
