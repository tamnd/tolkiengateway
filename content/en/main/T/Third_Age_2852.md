**Third Age 2852** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2852**) is the 2852nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2852 was also known as **S.R. 1252** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Belladonna Took](../B/Belladonna_Took.md), ninth child and second daughter of [Thain](Thain.md) [Gerontius Took](../G/Gerontius_Took.md) and [Adamanta Chubb](../A/Adamanta_Chubb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Notes {#Notes}

Editions prior to the [50th Anniversary edition](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md>) also included the following, which was moved to [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2872](Third_Age_2872.md):<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Belecthor II](../B/Belecthor_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) dies. The [White Tree](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md) dies, and no seedling can be found. The [Dead Tree](../D/Dead_Tree.md) is left standing.
