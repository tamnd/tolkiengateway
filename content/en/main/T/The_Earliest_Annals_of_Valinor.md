**The Earliest Annals of Valinor** is the sixth chapter of *[The Shaping of Middle-earth](The_Shaping_of_Middle-earth.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

This chapter details the events occurring in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) from The creation of all thing to approximately year 3000 of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), or roughly 30000 [years of the sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md). It details the creation of the world, the coming of [elves](../E/Elves.md), the [chaining](../C/Chaining_of_Melkor.md) of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), his release and the death of the [trees](Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md), the [March of the Noldor](../E/Exile_of_the_Noldor.md) begins, and the section ends with the arrival of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md)'s forces in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) and the Rising of the [sun](../S/Sun.md).
