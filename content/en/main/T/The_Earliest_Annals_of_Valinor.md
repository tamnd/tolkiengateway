**The Earliest Annals of Valinor** is the sixth chapter of *[The Shaping of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Shaping_of_Middle-earth)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

This chapter details the events occurring in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) from The creation of all thing to approximately year 3000 of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), or roughly 30000 [years of the sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun"). It details the creation of the world, the coming of [elves](/wiki/Elves "Elves"), the [chaining](/wiki/Chaining_of_Melkor "Chaining of Melkor") of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), his release and the death of the [trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor"), the [March of the Noldor](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor") begins, and the section ends with the arrival of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin)'s forces in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and the Rising of the [sun](/wiki/Sun "Sun").
