**Third Age 2746** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2746**) is the 2746th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2746 was also known as **S.R. 1146** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Thorin II Oakenshield](Thorin_II_Oakenshield.md), son of [Thráin II](Thráin.md), son of [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Thrór](Thrór.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The unnamed fifteenth [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md) is slain by the [Corsairs](../C/Corsairs.md) of [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The fifteenth Prince's unnamed son becomes the sixteenth [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of the unnamed seventeenth [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
