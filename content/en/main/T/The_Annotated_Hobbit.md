<i><b>The Annotated Hobbit</b></i> is an edition of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* with commentaries by [Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson). It was first published in [1988](/wiki/1988) in celebration of the 50th anniversary of the first American publication of *The Hobbit* by [Houghton Mifflin](/wiki/Houghton_Mifflin), and later in [1989](/wiki/1989) by [Unwin Hyman](/wiki/Unwin_Hyman).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the winner of the 1990 [Mythopoeic Scholarship Award](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society "Mythopoeic Society") in Inklings Studies.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Overview {#Overview}

The book includes more than 150 black-and-white illustrations from foreign editions and some that were drawn by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") himself, as well as some photographs. The commentary notably includes a breakdown of all the differences between the original and revised version of chapter five, *[Riddles in the Dark](/wiki/Riddles_in_the_Dark)*, printing the altered sentences and sometimes full paragraphs where necessary.

### Second edition {#Second_edition}

In [2002](/wiki/2002), a revised and expanded edition was published by [Houghton Mifflin](/wiki/Houghton_Mifflin). This version included maps and colour paintings. It also provided many new sources, due to the amount of new literature published. The British edition was published in [2003](/wiki/2003) by [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> This edition includes several rare or previously unpublished poems and writings by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien):

- Previously unpublished:
    - "[Elvish Song in Rivendell](/wiki/Elvish_Song_in_Rivendell)"
    - "[Glip](/wiki/Glip)"

- Had been published elsewhere:
    - "[Goblin Feet](/wiki/Goblin_Feet)"
    - "[Enigmata Saxonica Nuper Inventa Duo](/wiki/Enigmata_Saxonica_Nuper_Inventa_Duo)"
    - "[Iúmonna Gold Galdre Bewunden](/wiki/I%C3%BAmonna_Gold_Galdre_Bewunden)"
    - "[Progress in Bimble Town](/wiki/Progress_in_Bimble_Town)"
    - "[The Root of the Boot](/wiki/The_Root_of_the_Boot)"
    - "[The Dragon's Visit](/wiki/The_Dragon%27s_Visit)"
    - "[The Quest of Erebor](/wiki/The_Quest_of_Erebor)" (a longer version)

### Third edition {#Third_edition}

Since at least 2019, rumors have been circulating about a third edition being in the works.<sup id="cite_ref-interview_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-interview-4">[4]</a></sup> Anderson confirmed that he was working on it in a [2021](/wiki/2021) interview, but that he had also lost a lot of his progress when his house was struck by lightning.<sup id="cite_ref-interview_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-interview-4">[4]</a></sup>
