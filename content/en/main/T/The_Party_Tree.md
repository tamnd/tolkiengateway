The **Party Tree** was a tree in the center of the [Party Field](../P/Party_Field.md) south of [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md).

## History {#History}

On preparation of the [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md), a pavilion was erected that was large enough to accommodate it and it stood at the head of the main table during the celebration with lanterns hanging from its branches. Bilbo stood underneath the Party Tree when he announced he would be leaving [the Shire](The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

By November of [3019](Third_Age_3019.md) the Party Tree had been cut down by [Sharkey](../S/Sharkey.md) and his men and was lying on the field.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> After the [Scouring of the Shire](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md), Sam planted the silver nut from the [wooden box](../S/Sam's_garden_box.md) given to him by [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md). In the spring of [3020](Third_Age_3020.md), the sapling of a [mallorn](../M/Mallorn.md) tree emerged; the [Golden Tree](../G/Golden_Tree.md) was the only mallorn between the [mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) and the [Sea](../B/Belegaer.md), and people came from miles around to gaze upon it.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) of that region held holidays and celebrations around the Mallorn tree.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

- The Party Tree may have been inspired by a willow hanging over the mill-pool at Sarehole, Warwickshire, England
