**Torhir Ifant** wrote the *[Dorgannas Iaur](/wiki/Dorgannas_Iaur)*, a book of lore of the realms of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). Nothing else is known about this character.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) has suggested that *Ifant* is the same gloss as the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word *[ifant](/wiki/Ifant "Ifant")* ("old").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
