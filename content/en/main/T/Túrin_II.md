**Túrin II** ([Third Age](Third_Age.md) [2815](Third_Age_2815.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> – [2914](Third_Age_2914.md),<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 99 years) was the twenty-third [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

## History {#History}

Túrin succeeded his father [Thorondir](Thorondir.md) at his death in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2882](Third_Age_2882.md).<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup> [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) was further deserted because of infestation by [Orcs of Mordor](../O/Orcs_of_Mordor.md). It was Túrin who built secret refuges for his soldiers in Ithilien, of which [Henneth Annûn](../H/Henneth_Annûn.md) was the longest guarded and manned. He also fortified the isle of [Cair Andros](../C/Cair_Andros.md) to defend [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-stew_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-3">[3]</a></sup>

During his time also the fief [South Gondor](../S/South_Gondor.md) was occupied by the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md) and there was fighting along the river [Poros](../P/Poros.md), until in [2885](Third_Age_2885.md) they invaded Ithilien was invaded in great strength. His ally King [Folcwine](../F/Folcwine.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) fulfilled the [Oath of Eorl](../O/Oath_of_Eorl.md) and sent many men to Gondor. With their aid Túrin won a victory at the crossing of the river, though the princes [Fastred](<../F/Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine).md>) and [Folcred](../F/Folcred.md) were slain.<sup id="cite_ref-stew_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Túrin was followed by his son [Turgon](<Turgon_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) at his death in [2914](Third_Age_2914.md).<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

For the meaning of his name refer to [Túrin - Etymology](Túrin.md#Etymology). Túrin II was probably named after [Túrin Turambar](Túrin.md), a tragic hero of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
