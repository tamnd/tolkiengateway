<b><i>Tolkien Studies</i>: Volume 19</b>, published in [2023](../_/2023.md), is the nineteenth volume of the annual review *[Tolkien Studies](<Tolkien_Studies_(journal).md>)*.

This is the [2022](../_/2022.md) issue, its publication was delayed to 2023 due to the preparation of the [*Issue 19 Supplement*](Tolkien_Studies%253A_Volume_19_Supplement.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- Articles
    - Verlyn Flieger, "In Memoriam: [Priscilla Tolkien](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md), 1929-2022"
    - Paul Acker, "A Rabble of Uninvited Dwarves"
    - Riley McGuire, "The Place of Allegory in Tolkien's Understanding of the [Old English *Exodus*](The_Old_English_Exodus.md)"
    - Nathan Kowalsky, "*The Hobbit* and the Hermeneutics of the Barnyard"
    - Perry Neil Harrison, "Tolkien, the Medieval Robin Hood, and the Matter of the Greenwood"
    - J.M. Silk, "A Faërie Ring: Poetry and the Metaphor of Music as Devices of Enchantment in Tolkien's Fiction"
    - Vincent E. Rone, "The Musical Continuity between Howard Shore and J.R.R. Tolkien"
    - Christopher Crane, "Early Drafts and Carbon Copies: Composing and Editing *[Smith of Wootton Major](../S/Smith_of_Wootton_Major.md)*"
    - Ewan Cameron, "Tolkien, Thompson, English Modernity, and the Left"
    - John Rosegrant, "When the Search for Enchantment is Bent: 'The Scouring of the Shire'"

- Book Reviews
    - *[The Nature of Middle-earth](The_Nature_of_Middle-earth.md)*, by J.R.R. Tolkien, edited by Carl F. Hostetter, reviewed by John Garth
    - *[Tolkien and the Classical World](Tolkien_and_the_Classical_World.md)*, edited by Hamish Williams, and *[Tolkien and the Classics](Tolkien_and_the_Classics.md)*, edited by Roberto Arduini, Giampaolo Canzonieri, and Claudio A. Testi, reviewed by Victor Parker
    - <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Musical_Scores_and_the_Eternal_Present&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Musical Scores and the Eternal Present (page does not exist)">Musical Scores and the Eternal Present</a>: Theology, Time, and Tolkien</i>, by Chiara Bertoglio, reviewed by Eileen Marie Moore
    - <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=A_Sense_of_Tales_Untold&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="A Sense of Tales Untold (page does not exist)">A Sense of Tales Untold</a>: Exploring the Edges of Tolkien&#39;s Literary Canvas</i>, by Peter Grybauskas, reviewed by Maria K. Alberto
    - *[Middle-earth, or There and Back Again](../M/Middle-earth,_or_There_and_Back_Again.md)*, edited by Łukasz Neubauer, reviewed by Merlin DeTardo
    - Book Notes, by David Bratman

- David Bratman, Kate Neville, Jennifer Rogers, Jonathan Evans, Robin Anne Reid, John Wm. Houghton, and John Magoun, "The Year's Work in Tolkien Studies 2019"
- David Bratman, "Bibliography (in English) for 2020"
