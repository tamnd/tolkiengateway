**The Houses of Healing** is the fifty-sixth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)"). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.

<br>

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

After the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), soldiers search the battlefield for the wounded and dead, among them is [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"). He finds [Merry's](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") [Elven cloak](/wiki/Elven_cloaks "Elven cloaks") and fears he is dead. [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) finds an unconscious [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn), who he had believed was safe in [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) and brings her to the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing). There she is healed by [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn). Éowyn sees [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) who is also wounded and in the Houses of Healing.

## Differences {#Differences}

This scene is based off [The Houses of Healing](/wiki/The_Houses_of_Healing), some events from [The Pyre of Denethor](/wiki/The_Pyre_of_Denethor) are modified and included in this scene.

- Éomer saw Éowyn's body during the battle and believed her to be dead causing him and his army to fight even more fiercely.<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Éowyn's body was brought to the Houses of Healing, Éomer did not discover that she was still alive until he asked [Prince Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil "Imrahil") where her body was and was told that she was still alive.<sup id="cite_ref-Houses_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Houses-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Pippin was sent by Gandalf to look for Merry.<sup id="cite_ref-Houses_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Houses-2">[2]</a></sup> He searched inside the city and found him inside the [Gate of Minas Tirith](/wiki/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith "Great Gate of Minas Tirith").<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup> In the subsequent scene ([Pippin Looks After Merry](/wiki/Pippin_Looks_After_Merry)) Pippin finds Merry in the Pelennor Fields.
- Aragorn was not in the Houses of Healing until Gandalf summoned him upon hearing the prophecy "*The hands of a king are the hands of a healer*".<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Those who had suffered from the [Black Breath](/wiki/Black_Breath) were unable to be healed by any ruler but Aragorn. This is not shown in the movie.
