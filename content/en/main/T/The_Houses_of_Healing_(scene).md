**The Houses of Healing** is the fifty-sixth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.

<br>

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

After the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), soldiers search the battlefield for the wounded and dead, among them is [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md). He finds [Merry's](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) [Elven cloak](../E/Elven_cloaks.md) and fears he is dead. [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) finds an unconscious [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md), who he had believed was safe in [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) and brings her to the [Houses of Healing](../H/Houses_of_Healing.md). There she is healed by [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md). Éowyn sees [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) who is also wounded and in the Houses of Healing.

## Differences {#Differences}

This scene is based off [The Houses of Healing](The_Houses_of_Healing.md), some events from [The Pyre of Denethor](The_Pyre_of_Denethor.md) are modified and included in this scene.

- Éomer saw Éowyn's body during the battle and believed her to be dead causing him and his army to fight even more fiercely.<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Éowyn's body was brought to the Houses of Healing, Éomer did not discover that she was still alive until he asked [Prince Imrahil](../I/Imrahil.md) where her body was and was told that she was still alive.<sup id="cite_ref-Houses_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Houses-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Pippin was sent by Gandalf to look for Merry.<sup id="cite_ref-Houses_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Houses-2">[2]</a></sup> He searched inside the city and found him inside the [Gate of Minas Tirith](../G/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup> In the subsequent scene ([Pippin Looks After Merry](../P/Pippin_Looks_After_Merry.md)) Pippin finds Merry in the Pelennor Fields.
- Aragorn was not in the Houses of Healing until Gandalf summoned him upon hearing the prophecy "*The hands of a king are the hands of a healer*".<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Those who had suffered from the [Black Breath](../B/Black_Breath.md) were unable to be healed by any ruler but Aragorn. This is not shown in the movie.
