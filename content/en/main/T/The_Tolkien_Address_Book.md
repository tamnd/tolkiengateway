<i><b>The Tolkien Address Book</b></i> is a blank address book featuring a selection of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s illustrations (from *[Pictures by J.R.R. Tolkien](../P/Pictures_by_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*) and verse (from *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Return of the King](The_Return_of_the_King.md)*). It was published to celebrate the centenary of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) ([1892](../_/1892.md)-[1992](../_/1992.md)).
