<i><b>The Tolkien Address Book</b></i> is a blank address book featuring a selection of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s illustrations (from *[Pictures by J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Pictures_by_J.R.R._Tolkien)*) and verse (from *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*). It was published to celebrate the centenary of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) ([1892](/wiki/1892)-[1992](/wiki/1992)).
