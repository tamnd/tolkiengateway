1. **The Wise** was a title claimed by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman).
2. **The [Wise](/wiki/Wise)** was a title given to the greatest [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and the chief [Wizards](/wiki/Wizards), who formed the [White Council](/wiki/White_Council) in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).
3. **The Wise** were persons esteemed among [Men](/wiki/Men) for their knowledge of lore. This epithet is referenced in [Athrabeth Finrod Ah Andreth](/wiki/Athrabeth_Finrod_Ah_Andreth). Members include [Andreth](/wiki/Andreth) and [Adanel](/wiki/Adanel).
4. **The Wise** was also a title of [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon), [King of Gondolin](/wiki/King_of_Gondolin).
5. A descriptor applied to [Nerdanel](/wiki/Nerdanel)
