1. **The Wise** was a title claimed by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md).
2. **The [Wise](../W/Wise.md)** was a title given to the greatest [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) and the chief [Wizards](../W/Wizards.md), who formed the [White Council](../W/White_Council.md) in the [Third Age](Third_Age.md).
3. **The Wise** were persons esteemed among [Men](../M/Men.md) for their knowledge of lore. This epithet is referenced in [Athrabeth Finrod Ah Andreth](../A/Athrabeth_Finrod_Ah_Andreth~66012.md). Members include [Andreth](../A/Andreth.md) and [Adanel](../A/Adanel.md).
4. **The Wise** was also a title of [Turgon](Turgon.md), [King of Gondolin](../K/King_of_Gondolin.md).
5. A descriptor applied to [Nerdanel](../N/Nerdanel.md)
