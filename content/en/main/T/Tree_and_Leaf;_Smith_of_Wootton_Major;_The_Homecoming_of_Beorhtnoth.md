<i><b>Tree and Leaf; Smith of Wootton Major; The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth</b></i> is a [1975](/wiki/1975) anthology, published by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Unwin_Books&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Unwin Books (page does not exist)">Unwin Books</a>, collecting the books *[Tree and Leaf](/wiki/Tree_and_Leaf)*, *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*, and poem *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth](/wiki/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth)* under the same covers.
