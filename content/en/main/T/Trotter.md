In the [earliest drafts](The_History_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) of *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, the character who became [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) was called **Trotter** instead of [Strider](<../S/Strider_(Aragorn).md>) and was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) instead of [Man](../M/Man.md). His name was derived from the fact that he had wooden feet or wore wooden shoes after being tortured in [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).

In some drafts Trotter's real name was **[Peregrin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) [Boffin](../B/Boffin_Family~66342.md)** and was a cousin of [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md). He was present at the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md) and appointed as a member of the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-comp_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-comp-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:xxii</sup>

Trotter eventually became a Man whose true name was [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md). While in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md), Tolkien rejected *Aragorn* in favor of *[Elfstone](../E/Elfstone.md)* and briefly *[Ingold](../I/Ingold.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-comp_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-comp-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:xxiv</sup> However, the name "Trotter" continued an alias for Aragorn until the drafting of *The Lord of the Rings* was almost complete, not being changed to "Strider" until [1948](../_/1948.md), in changes to the drafts of the last chapters.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
