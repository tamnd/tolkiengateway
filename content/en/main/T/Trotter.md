In the [earliest drafts](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The History of The Lord of the Rings") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, the character who became [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) was called **Trotter** instead of [Strider](</wiki/Strider_(Aragorn)> "Strider (Aragorn)") and was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") instead of [Man](/wiki/Man). His name was derived from the fact that he had wooden feet or wore wooden shoes after being tortured in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).

In some drafts Trotter's real name was **[Peregrin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") [Boffin](/wiki/Boffin_Family "Boffin Family")** and was a cousin of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins). He was present at the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond) and appointed as a member of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-comp_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-comp-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:xxii</sup>

Trotter eventually became a Man whose true name was [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn). While in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), Tolkien rejected *Aragorn* in favor of *[Elfstone](/wiki/Elfstone)* and briefly *[Ingold](/wiki/Ingold)*.<sup id="cite_ref-comp_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-comp-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:xxiv</sup> However, the name "Trotter" continued an alias for Aragorn until the drafting of *The Lord of the Rings* was almost complete, not being changed to "Strider" until [1948](/wiki/1948), in changes to the drafts of the last chapters.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
