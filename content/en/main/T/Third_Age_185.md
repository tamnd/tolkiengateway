**Third Age 185** (abbreviated to **T.A. 185**) is the 185th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Arantar](../A/Arantar.md), son of [Eldacar](<../E/Eldacar_(King_of_Arnor).md>) of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
