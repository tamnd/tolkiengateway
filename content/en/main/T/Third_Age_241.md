**Third Age 241** (abbreviated to **T.A. 241**) is the 241st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Arwen Undómiel](../A/Arwen.md), youngest child and only daughter of [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) and [Celebrían](../C/Celebrían.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
