**Telperion** was the elder of the [Two Trees of Valinor](Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md), called the **White Tree**, which shed silver light on the domain of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md). His leaves were of dark green, shining silver beneath, and his boughs were decked with brilliant flowers that shed a rain of silver dew, which was collected as a source of water and of light.<sup id="cite_ref-Days_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Days-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Telperion was the elder of the Two Trees, and created by the [Vala](../V/Vala.md) [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md). It bloomed during the first part of the Valian day, followed by [Laurelin](../L/Laurelin.md). It endured throughout the [Years of the Trees](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md), but came to an end in the dreadful [Darkening of Valinor](../D/Darkening_of_Valinor.md).

However one of Telperion's flowers survived the Darkening, and was set aloft by the Valar; this was the light of the [Moon](../M/Moon.md), carried by [Tilion](Tilion.md) the [Maia](../M/Maia.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), [Turgon](Turgon.md) made images of the Two Trees that stood in his court of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), and the one made in memory of Telperion was [Belthil](../B/Belthil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Telperion.27s_descendants"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Telperion&#39;s_descendants">Telperion&#39;s descendants</span></h3>

Even though the elder tree did not survive, he was not the last of the White Trees. Because the Elves that first came to Valinor especially loved Telperion, Yavanna made a second tree like it to stand in the city of [Tirion](Tirion.md) where the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) and [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) dwelt together at first. This tree, named *[Galathilion](../G/Galathilion.md)*, was identical to Telperion except that it gave no light of its own. It had many seedlings, one of which was [Celeborn](<../C/Celeborn_(White_Tree).md>) on the isle of [Tol Eressëa](Tol_Eressëa.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), a seedling of Celeborn was brought as a gift to the [Númenoreans](../N/Númenoreans.md) — that was [Nimloth](../N/Nimloth_of_Númenor.md), the White Tree of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). It lasted through the vast majority of the realm's duration, but when [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) took control of the island he had king [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md) chop it down. Fortunately, [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) managed to save a single fruit of that tree. Of this fruit later came the [White Tree of Gondor](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Telperion* comes from the root *[telepi](Telepi.md)*/*[telpë](Telpë.md)* ("silver").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The exact etymology of the ending *-rion* is not clear. Latter elements may include *[rî](../R/Rî.md)* ("garland, wreath"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and/or *ere* ("the one" or "first")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> along with the suffix *-on* or *-ion*.

The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) form was actually *Tyelperion* but Quenya adopted *[telpë](Telpë.md)* from [Telerin](Telerin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Another [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name for Telperion was <i><b>Silpion</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> It is derived from the root [SIL](../S/SIL~59984.md) (shine (with white or silver light))"<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> or from the extended root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=SILIP&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SILIP (page does not exist)">SILIP</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-SILIP_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SILIP-13">[13]</a></sup> and the suffix *-ion*.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> It was sometimes glossed the "White Tree of Valinor",<sup id="cite_ref-SILIP_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SILIP-13">[13]</a></sup> or simply the "White Tree".

<i><b>Ninquelótë</b></i> was a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) title of Telperion.<sup id="cite_ref-Days_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Days-1">[1]</a></sup> *Ninquelótë* means "White Flower" or (perhaps more likely in this context) "White Blossom". The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) equivalent of this Quenya name was *[Nimloth](../N/Nimloth_of_Númenor.md)*, and indeed that name was inherited by the White Tree that grew in the [King's Court](../K/King's_Court.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).

<i><b>Ibrīniðilpathānezel</b></i> was the name in [Valarin](../V/Valarin.md) of Telperion.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

In early writings of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) Telperion's names were <i><b>Silpion</b></i>, <i><b>Bansil</b></i> and <i><b>Belthil</b></i>.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
