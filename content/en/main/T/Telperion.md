**Telperion** was the elder of the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor), called the **White Tree**, which shed silver light on the domain of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar). His leaves were of dark green, shining silver beneath, and his boughs were decked with brilliant flowers that shed a rain of silver dew, which was collected as a source of water and of light.<sup id="cite_ref-Days_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Days-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Telperion was the elder of the Two Trees, and created by the [Vala](/wiki/Vala) [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna). It bloomed during the first part of the Valian day, followed by [Laurelin](/wiki/Laurelin). It endured throughout the [Years of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees), but came to an end in the dreadful [Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor).

However one of Telperion's flowers survived the Darkening, and was set aloft by the Valar; this was the light of the [Moon](/wiki/Moon), carried by [Tilion](/wiki/Tilion) the [Maia](/wiki/Maia).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) made images of the Two Trees that stood in his court of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), and the one made in memory of Telperion was [Belthil](/wiki/Belthil).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Telperion.27s_descendants"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Telperion&#39;s_descendants">Telperion&#39;s descendants</span></h3>

Even though the elder tree did not survive, he was not the last of the White Trees. Because the Elves that first came to Valinor especially loved Telperion, Yavanna made a second tree like it to stand in the city of [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion) where the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) and [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) dwelt together at first. This tree, named *[Galathilion](/wiki/Galathilion)*, was identical to Telperion except that it gave no light of its own. It had many seedlings, one of which was [Celeborn](</wiki/Celeborn_(White_Tree)> "Celeborn (White Tree)") on the isle of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), a seedling of Celeborn was brought as a gift to the [Númenoreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenoreans) — that was [Nimloth](/wiki/Nimloth_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Nimloth of Númenor"), the White Tree of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). It lasted through the vast majority of the realm's duration, but when [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) took control of the island he had king [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n) chop it down. Fortunately, [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) managed to save a single fruit of that tree. Of this fruit later came the [White Tree of Gondor](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Telperion* comes from the root *[telepi](/wiki/Telepi "Telepi")*/*[telpë](/wiki/Telp%C3%AB "Telpë")* ("silver").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The exact etymology of the ending *-rion* is not clear. Latter elements may include *[rî](/wiki/R%C3%AE "Rî")* ("garland, wreath"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and/or *ere* ("the one" or "first")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> along with the suffix *-on* or *-ion*.

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) form was actually *Tyelperion* but Quenya adopted *[telpë](/wiki/Telp%C3%AB "Telpë")* from [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Another [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for Telperion was <i><b>Silpion</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> It is derived from the root [SIL](/wiki/SIL) (shine (with white or silver light))"<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> or from the extended root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=SILIP&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SILIP (page does not exist)">SILIP</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-SILIP_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SILIP-13">[13]</a></sup> and the suffix *-ion*.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> It was sometimes glossed the "White Tree of Valinor",<sup id="cite_ref-SILIP_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SILIP-13">[13]</a></sup> or simply the "White Tree".

<i><b>Ninquelótë</b></i> was a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) title of Telperion.<sup id="cite_ref-Days_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Days-1">[1]</a></sup> *Ninquelótë* means "White Flower" or (perhaps more likely in this context) "White Blossom". The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) equivalent of this Quenya name was *[Nimloth](/wiki/Nimloth_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Nimloth of Númenor")*, and indeed that name was inherited by the White Tree that grew in the [King's Court](/wiki/King%27s_Court) of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).

<i><b>Ibrīniðilpathānezel</b></i> was the name in [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) of Telperion.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

In early writings of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") Telperion's names were <i><b>Silpion</b></i>, <i><b>Bansil</b></i> and <i><b>Belthil</b></i>.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
