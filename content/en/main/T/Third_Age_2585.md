**Third Age 2585** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2585**) is the 2585th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2585 was also known as **S.R. 985** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Náin II](../N/Náin_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Dáin I](../D/Dáin_I.md), son of Náin II, becomes the King of [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>
