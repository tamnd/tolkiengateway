**Tolkien 2019** was a conference hosted by [The Tolkien Society](The_Tolkien_Society.md) from [7](../_/7_August.md)-[11 August](../_/11_August.md) [2019](../_/2019.md) in Birmingham, England. The conference marked the 50th anniversary of the Tolkien Society’s foundation.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
