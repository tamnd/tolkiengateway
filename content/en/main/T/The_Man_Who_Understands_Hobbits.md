**The Man Who Understands Hobbits** is the title of an [interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) by Charlotte and Denis Plimmer, published in [*The Daily Telegraph Magazine*](</wiki/The_Daily_Telegraph_Magazine_(22_March_1968)> "The Daily Telegraph Magazine (22 March 1968)"), on [22 March](/wiki/22_March) [1968](/wiki/1968). The interview was conducted on [30 November](/wiki/30_November) 1966.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [Letter 294](/wiki/Letter_294), Tolkien replies to a draft of the article, writing about suggestions for changes.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The article was reprinted in the *The Daily Times-Journal* of Fort William, Ontario and [*Moose Jaw Times-Herald*](</wiki/Moose_Jaw_Times-Herald:_Weekend_Magazine_(Vol._18,_No._35)> "Moose Jaw Times-Herald: Weekend Magazine (Vol. 18, No. 35)").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<p>&#34;<a href="/wiki/Spiders" title="Spiders">Spiders</a>,&#34; observed Professor J.R.R. Tolkien, cradling the word with the same affection that he cradled the pipe in his hand, &#34;are the particular terror of northern imaginations.&#34;
</p>
</blockquote>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Discussing one of his own monsters, a man-devouring, spider-like female, he said, &#34;The <a href="/wiki/Shelob" title="Shelob">female monster</a> is certainly no deadlier than the male, but she is different. She is a sucking, strangling, trapping creature.&#34;
</p>
</blockquote>

<p>&#34;I never expected a money success,&#34; said Tolkien, pacing the room, as he does constantly when he speaks &#34;in fact, I never even thought of commercial publication when I wrote <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit" title="The Hobbit">THE HOBBIT</a> back in the Thirties.&#34;
</p>
