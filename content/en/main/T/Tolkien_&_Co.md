**Tolkien &amp; Co** was a [Tolkien society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies") in France.

## History {#History}

Tolkien &amp; Co was active from the late 1990s through early 2000s. It can be assumed it is now defunct as no recent internet presence or activities are known to exist.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
