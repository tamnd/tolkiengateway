**Third Age 2767** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2767**) is the 2767th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2767 was also known as **S.R. 1167** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Balbo Baggins](../B/Balbo_Baggins.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Bosco Boffin](../B/Bosco_Boffin.md), first son of [Buffo Boffin](../B/Buffo_Boffin.md) and [Ivy Goodenough](../I/Ivy_Goodenough.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Dáin II Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md), son of [Náin](<../N/Náin_(son_of_Grór).md>)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
