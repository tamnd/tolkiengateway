## Contents {#Contents}

- Articles
    - [Peter Grybauskas](../P/Peter_Grybauskas.md), "Untold Tales: Solving a Literary Dilemma"
    - Gerard Hynes, "'Beneath the Earth's dark keel': Tolkien and Geology"
    - [Douglas C. Kane](../D/Douglas_C._Kane.md), "Law and Arda"
    - Amelia A. Rutledge, "'Justice is not Healing': J.R.R. Tolkien's Pauline Constructs in 'Finwë and Míriel'"

- Book Reviews
    - *[Tolkien and Wales](Tolkien_and_Wales.md): Language, Literature and Identity*, by Carl Phelpstead, reviewed by Marjorie Burns
    - *[Middle-earth and Beyond](../M/Middle-earth_and_Beyond.md): Essays on the World of J.R.R. Tolkien*, edited by Kathleen Dubs and Janka Kascáková, reviewed by John D. Rateliff
    - *[The Ecological Augury in the Works of J.R.R. Tolkien](The_Ecological_Augury_in_the_Works_of_JRR_Tolkien.md)*, by Liam Campbell, reviewed by Kristine Larsen
    - *[Tolkien and the Study of His Sources](Tolkien_and_the_Study_of_His_Sources.md)*, edited by Jason Fisher, reviewed by Paul Edmund Thomas
    - *[Picturing Tolkien](../P/Picturing_Tolkien.md): Essays on Peter Jackson's The Lord of the Rings Film Trilogy*, edited by Janice M. Bogstad and Philip E. Kaveny, reviewed by Anne C. Petty
    - *[The Ring and the Cross](The_Ring_and_the_Cross.md): Christianity and The Lord of The Rings*, edited by Paul E. Kerry, and *[Light Beyond All Shadow](../L/Light_Beyond_All_Shadow.md): Religious Experience in Tolkien's Work*, edited by Paul E. Kerry and Sandra Miesel, reviewed by Jonathan Evans

- David Bratman and Merlin DeTardo, "The Year's Work in Tolkien Studies 2009"
- Rebecca Epstein, Michael D.C. Drout, David Bratman, and Merlin DeTardo, "Bibliography (in English) for 2010"
