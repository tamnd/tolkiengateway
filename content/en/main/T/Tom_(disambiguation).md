**Tom** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

**Tom** was a common name of [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md), usually an abbreviation of [Westron](../W/Westron.md) **Tombûran**, **Tomacca**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> **Tomba** or **Tolma**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> (all of unknown meaning). It can refer to the following characters:

- <b><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="Tom.md" title="Tom">Tom</a>, a troll in <i><a href="The_Hobbit.md" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i>;</b>
- **[Tom Bombadil](Tom_Bombadil.md), a mysterious character who lived in the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md);**
- [Tom Bombadil (chapter)](<Tom_Bombadil_(chapter).md>), the sixth chapter within *[The Return of the Shadow](The_Return_of_the_Shadow.md)*.
- [Tolman "Tom" Cotton Senior](Tolman_Cotton_Senior.md) ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1341](Third_Age_2941.md) - [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1440](../F/Fourth_Age_19.md)), also known as Farmer Cotton, father of [Rose Cotton](../R/Rose_Cotton.md);
- [Tolman "Tom" Cotton Junior](Tolman_Cotton_Junior.md) (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1380](Third_Age_2980.md)), eldest son of Tom Cotton Senior;
- [Tolman "Tom" Gardner](Tolman_Gardner.md) (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1442](../F/Fourth_Age_21.md)), youngest son of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Rose Cotton](../R/Rose_Cotton.md);
- [Tom](<Tom_(The_Stone_Troll).md>), the protagonist of Sam Gamgee's poem "[The Stone Troll](The_Stone_Troll.md)";
- <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tomnoddy&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tomnoddy (page does not exist)">Tomnoddy</a>, a name used for a spider in *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)*.
