**Tom** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

**Tom** was a common name of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits), usually an abbreviation of [Westron](/wiki/Westron) **Tombûran**, **Tomacca**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> **Tomba** or **Tolma**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> (all of unknown meaning). It can refer to the following characters:

- <b><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Tom" title="Tom">Tom</a>, a troll in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i>;</b>
- **[Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil), a mysterious character who lived in the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest);**
- [Tom Bombadil (chapter)](</wiki/Tom_Bombadil_(chapter)>), the sixth chapter within *[The Return of the Shadow](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_Shadow)*.
- [Tolman "Tom" Cotton Senior](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior") ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1341](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") - [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1440](/wiki/Fourth_Age_19 "Fourth Age 19")), also known as Farmer Cotton, father of [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton);
- [Tolman "Tom" Cotton Junior](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Junior "Tolman Cotton Junior") (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1380](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980")), eldest son of Tom Cotton Senior;
- [Tolman "Tom" Gardner](/wiki/Tolman_Gardner "Tolman Gardner") (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1442](/wiki/Fourth_Age_21 "Fourth Age 21")), youngest son of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton);
- [Tom](</wiki/Tom_(The_Stone_Troll)> "Tom (The Stone Troll)"), the protagonist of Sam Gamgee's poem "[The Stone Troll](/wiki/The_Stone_Troll)";
- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tomnoddy&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tomnoddy (page does not exist)">Tomnoddy</a>, a name used for a spider in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.
