**Third Age 667** (abbreviated to **T.A. 667**) is the 667th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Turambar](<Turambar_(King_of_Gondor).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Turambar](<Turambar_(King_of_Gondor).md>)'s son, [Atanatar I](../A/Atanatar_I.md), becomes the tenth [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>
