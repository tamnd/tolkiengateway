<i><b>Treasures from the Misty Mountains: A Collector&#39;s Guide to Tolkien</b></i> is a 2001 book by James H. Gilam, offering an overview of collectibles based on Tolkien's legendarium.

Includes a reproduction of a [postcard from Tolkien to Rayner Unwin](</wiki/Letter_to_Rayner_Unwin_(28_December_1953)> "Letter to Rayner Unwin (28 December 1953)") dated 28 December 1953 (p. 126).<sup id="cite_ref-TB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

In addition to giving an overview on collecting and the various movies, including a general preview of the as-of-then upcoming *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, the book also covers licensed products from the 1970s onward, including [books](/wiki/Category:Books "Category:Books"), [calendars](/wiki/Category:Calendars "Category:Calendars"), games, and collectibles from various companies ([Kinder Surprise](/wiki/Kinder_Surprise), [Mithril Miniatures](/wiki/Mithril_Miniatures), [Sideshow-WETA Collectibles](/wiki/Sideshow_Collectibles "Sideshow Collectibles")).

## Reception {#Reception}

TolkienBooks.net writes that "it is far from complete and contains many errors."<sup id="cite_ref-TB_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-1">[1]</a></sup>
