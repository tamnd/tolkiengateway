<i><b>The Hobbit Birthday Book</b></i> is a blank diary featuring illustrations by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) from *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* (coloured by [H.E. Riddett](/wiki/H.E._Riddett)). It was published to celebrate the centenary of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) ([1892](/wiki/1892)-[1992](/wiki/1992)).
