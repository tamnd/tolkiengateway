<i><b>The Open Door to Poetry: An Anthology</b></i> is a collection of poetry, first published in [1931](/wiki/1931). The anthology includes [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s (acknowledged as "[J.R.R. Tolkein](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkein)") poem "[Goblin Feet](/wiki/Goblin_Feet)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
