<i><b>The Trees, the Jewels and the Rings: A Discursive Enquiry Into Things Little Known on Middle-earth</b></i> is a work of speculation by [Charles E. Noad](/wiki/Charles_E._Noad) about the possible content of *The Silmarillion*. It was published in January [1977](/wiki/1977),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> just a few months before the published version of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.
