**Tookland** was a wide region of [the Shire](The_Shire.md)'s [Westfarthing](../W/Westfarthing.md). It lay to the south of the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> with its chief and central township at [Tuckborough](Tuckborough.md) in the [Green Hills](../G/Green_Hills.md), where, burrowed into the hillsides, was the famous and historic home of the Tooks, [Great Smials](../G/Great_Smials.md). Somewhat to the west of Tuckborough lay the smaller of the Tookland's main settlements, [Tookbank](Tookbank.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Tookland was the historical province of the [Took clan](Took_Family.md), and so of the hereditary [Thains](Thain.md) of the Shire. By the late [Third Age](Third_Age.md) it was one of those folklands who were still home to their ancestral clan, as most Tooks were still found there.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The [Travellers](Travellers.md) passed from Tookland on their way to the [Green Hill Country](../G/Green_Hill_Country.md).

During [Sharkey](../S/Sharkey.md)'s occupation, the Tooks were fortified in their smials and didn't allow any [ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) to enter, having shot three for prowling and robbing. They became nastier and kept a close watch. Around the time of the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md), [Thain](Thain.md) [Paladin Took II](../P/Paladin_Took_II.md) raised all the country and commanded some troops to drive them away to the south, and sent a messenger to [Bywater](../B/Bywater.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
