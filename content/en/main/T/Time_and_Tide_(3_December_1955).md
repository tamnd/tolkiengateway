<b><i>Time and Tide</i> (3 December 1955)</b> is an issue of the British magazine <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_and_Tide_(magazine)" title="wikipedia:Time and Tide (magazine)">Time and Tide</a></i>. Included is the first publication of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem "[Imram](../I/Imram.md)" (page 1561).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup>

The issue also contains reviews of Elizabeth Jenning's *[A Way of Looking](../L/Letter_to_Elizabeth_Jennings.md)* and [W.H. Auden](../W/W.H._Auden.md)'s *[The Shield of Achilles](../L/List_of_books_in_Tolkien's_library.md#The_Shield_of_Achilles)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup>
