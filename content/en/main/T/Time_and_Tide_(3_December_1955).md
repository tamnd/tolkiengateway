<b><i>Time and Tide</i> (3 December 1955)</b> is an issue of the British magazine <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_and_Tide_(magazine)" title="wikipedia:Time and Tide (magazine)">Time and Tide</a></i>. Included is the first publication of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[Imram](/wiki/Imram)" (page 1561).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup>

The issue also contains reviews of Elizabeth Jenning's *[A Way of Looking](/wiki/Letter_to_Elizabeth_Jennings "Letter to Elizabeth Jennings")* and [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden)'s *[The Shield of Achilles](/wiki/List_of_books_in_Tolkien%27s_library#The_Shield_of_Achilles "List of books in Tolkien's library")*.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup>
