**Third Age 1975** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1975**) is the 1975th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1975 was also known as **S.R. 375** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Early {#Early}

- Having learned from [Aranarth](../A/Aranarth.md) about [King](../K/Kings_of_Arthedain.md) [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md), [Cirdan](../C/Cirdan.md) sends an elven ship to [Forochel](../F/Forochel.md) to rescue him.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-1">[1]</a></sup>

## March {#March}

- Long winter, the ice of the north remains longer than usual.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md) drowned in the [Icebay of Forochel](../I/Icebay_of_Forochel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The *[palantíri](../P/Palantíri.md)* of [Annúminas](../A/Annúminas.md) and [Amon Sûl](../W/Weathertop.md) are lost.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Ring of Barahir](../R/Ring_of_Barahir.md) stays with the chief of the [Lossoth](../L/Lossoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Later {#Later}

- [Angmar conflict](../A/Angmar_conflict.md): [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md) brings a fleet to [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-gondor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The [Host of the West](<../H/Host_of_the_West_(Arnor).md>) is assembled; [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) with a force from [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) joins them.<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) defeated at the [Battle of Fornost](../B/Battle_of_Fornost.md). He is pursued to the [Ettenmoors](../E/Ettenmoors.md) where Eärnur fails to fight him. He vanishes from the North.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-gondor_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>
