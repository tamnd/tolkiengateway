**Third Age 1975** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1975**) is the 1975th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Third Age 1975 was also known as **S.R. 375** in [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning).

Notable events in this year include:

## Early {#Early}

- Having learned from [Aranarth](/wiki/Aranarth) about [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Arthedain "Kings of Arthedain") [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui), [Cirdan](/wiki/Cirdan) sends an elven ship to [Forochel](/wiki/Forochel) to rescue him.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-1">[1]</a></sup>

## March {#March}

- Long winter, the ice of the north remains longer than usual.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) drowned in the [Icebay of Forochel](/wiki/Icebay_of_Forochel).<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The *[palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* of [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas) and [Amon Sûl](/wiki/Weathertop "Weathertop") are lost.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Ring of Barahir](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir) stays with the chief of the [Lossoth](/wiki/Lossoth).<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Later {#Later}

- [Angmar conflict](/wiki/Angmar_conflict): [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur) brings a fleet to [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-gondor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The [Host of the West](</wiki/Host_of_the_West_(Arnor)> "Host of the West (Arnor)") is assembled; [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) with a force from [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) joins them.<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) defeated at the [Battle of Fornost](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost). He is pursued to the [Ettenmoors](/wiki/Ettenmoors) where Eärnur fails to fight him. He vanishes from the North.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-gondor_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>
