**The Breaking of the Fellowship** is the thirty-eighth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* and the forty-fourth of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition).md>). This scene was extended in the latter edition.
