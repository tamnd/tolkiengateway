<i><b>The Tolkien Diary 1992</b></i> is a blank diary featuring illustrations by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) (first published in *[Pictures by J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Pictures_by_J.R.R._Tolkien)*) and many quotations (from *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)*) and biographic details.

Except for the introduction,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the diary was reprinted as *[A Tolkien Book of Days](/wiki/A_Tolkien_Book_of_Days)* (HarperCollins 1996).
