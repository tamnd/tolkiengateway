The **Tehtar** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈteçtar]</span>; singular **Tehta**) were a series of signs and symbols used when writing with the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar), consisting of a variety of dots, curls and other marks written above or below [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) letters. Most commonly, they were used to represent vowel-sounds, since [Fëanorian script](/wiki/Tengwar "Tengwar") as it was commonly used did not provide characters to represent vowels. However, some of the tehtar were used to abbreviate writing, such as a bar above a consonant that added a preceding nasal of the same *[téma](/wiki/T%C3%A9mar "Témar")*, or a bar below that indicated consonantal doubling, or a downward hook added to the bow that meant a following 's' (especially favoured in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
