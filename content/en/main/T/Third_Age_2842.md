**Third Age 2842** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2842**) is the 2842nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2842 was also known as **S.R. 1242** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Isembold Took](../I/Isembold_Took.md), fifth child and fourth son of [Thain](Thain.md) [Gerontius Took](../G/Gerontius_Took.md) and [Adamanta Chubb](../A/Adamanta_Chubb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Brytta Léofa](../B/Brytta.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Walda](../W/Walda.md), son of Brytta, becomes the twelfth King of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-2">[2]</a></sup>
