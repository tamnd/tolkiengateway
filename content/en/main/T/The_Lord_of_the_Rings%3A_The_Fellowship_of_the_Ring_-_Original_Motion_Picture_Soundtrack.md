<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack</b></i> is an album comprising of the soundtrack from the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*. It was released [20 November](../_/20_November.md), [2001](../_/2001.md). An extended soundtrack, based on the extended edition of the film, was later released as *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - The Complete Recordings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_The_Complete_Recordings.md)*.
