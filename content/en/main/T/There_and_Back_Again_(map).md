<i><b>There and Back Again: A Map of Bilbo&#39;s Journey through Eriador and Rhovanion</b></i> is a [1971](/wiki/1971) map illustrated by [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes) based on *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.

It reproduces the *[Map of Wilderland](/wiki/Map_of_Wilderland)* of *The Hobbit*, but also extends to [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador). Thus it includes all the regions through which [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) passed during the [quest](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor "Quest of Erebor") from [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) to [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain").

## Mistakes {#Mistakes}

There are a couple of spelling errors on the map: Dimril Dale instead of [Dimrill Dale](/wiki/Dimrill_Dale) and Gladuin instead of [Glanduin](/wiki/Glanduin).
