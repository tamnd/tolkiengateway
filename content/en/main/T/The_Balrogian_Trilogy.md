<i><b>The Balrogian Trilogy</b></i> is a series Text-Adventure video games that are parodies of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. It includes the following games:

1. [*An Everyday Tale of a Seeker of Gold*](/wiki/An_Everyday_Tale_of_a_Seeker_of_Gold "An Everyday Tale of a Seeker of Gold")
2. [*Bulbo and the Lizard King*](/wiki/Bulbo_and_the_Lizard_King "Bulbo and the Lizard King")
3. [*Fuddo and Slam*](/wiki/Fuddo_and_Slam "Fuddo and Slam")

A later game in the series, [*Retarded Creatures and Caverns*](/wiki/Retarded_Creatures_and_Caverns "Retarded Creatures and Caverns"), was not included in the trilogy.
