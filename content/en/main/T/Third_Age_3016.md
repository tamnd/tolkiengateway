**Third Age 3016** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3016**) is the 3016th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Third Age 3016 was also known as **S.R. 1416** in [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) sends for [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen), and she returns to [Imladris](/wiki/Rivendell "Rivendell"); the [Mountains](/wiki/Mountains) and all lands eastward are becoming dangerous.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>
