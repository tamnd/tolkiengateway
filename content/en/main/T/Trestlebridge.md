**Trestlebridge** is a city north of [Bree](/wiki/Bree) with a bridge called the Trestlespan, connecting [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land) with the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs) in the game *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online).*

## History {#History}

Once there was a great stone bridge made to serve the [North-South Road](/wiki/North-South_Road), but together with the Kingdoms of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), it fell, and it was replaced with the Trestlespan.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the time of the War of the Rings, Nellie Boskins is the mayor of Trestlebridge, following her father Lovelle Boskins who was killed by Orcs. Because of this attack, half of Trestlebridge lies in ashes. The Orcs of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) have made encampments to the north of the Trestlespan to attack the city.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Originally, Nellie Boskins was not willing to participate with the newly formed Council, but when the [Rangers](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North") of [Esteldín](/wiki/Esteld%C3%ADn) - and the player - came to help them unlooked for, she was grateful. The Orcs had just gathered to strike Trestlebridge, and this battle became known as the Battle of Trestlebridge. The Council won and Nellie Boskins joined it.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

Trestlebridge lies in the north of [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land), at the midway point between [Bree](/wiki/Bree) and [Fornost](/wiki/Fornost), with the [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway) running trough it. To the north lies the chasm Cirith Núr, which is bridged by the wooden bridge the Trestlespan. Half of the city is burned down and it is here where one can start the Wildwood content, an area you can buy.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A trestle bridge is a type of bridge that carries the load in multiple shorter spans.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

*Cirith Núr* comes from the word *cirith*, meaning "cleft" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and *núr*, coming from the rivers name *Núrsir*, which is made of the words *núr*, meaning "sad"<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and *sîr*, meaning "stream".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> That makes "Cleft of the Sad Stream". <span></span>
