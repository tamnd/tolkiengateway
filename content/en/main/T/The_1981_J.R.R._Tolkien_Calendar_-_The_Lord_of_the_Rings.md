**The 1981 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar - The Lord of the Rings** (To Honor the Silver Anniversary) was produced by [Tolkien Enterprises](/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises "Middle-earth Enterprises") and illustrated by [Paul Rivoche](/wiki/Paul_Rivoche) with images loosely based on those of [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)").

- <a class="internal mw-magiclink-isbn" href="/wiki/Special:BookSources/0969089015">ISBN 0-9690890-15</a>
- Dimensions: 13 x 11.75 inches
- Stapled
- Punched hanging hole
- Glossy white, medium-weight paperboard sleeve
- Shrink-wrapped
