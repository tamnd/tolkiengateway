**The 1981 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar - The Lord of the Rings** (To Honor the Silver Anniversary) was produced by [Tolkien Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md) and illustrated by [Paul Rivoche](../P/Paul_Rivoche.md) with images loosely based on those of [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>).

- <a class="internal mw-magiclink-isbn" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Special:BookSources/0969089015">ISBN 0-9690890-15</a>
- Dimensions: 13 x 11.75 inches
- Stapled
- Punched hanging hole
- Glossy white, medium-weight paperboard sleeve
- Shrink-wrapped
