**Third Age 2718** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2718**) is the 2718th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2718 was also known as **S.R. 1118** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Déor](../D/Déor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Gram](../G/Gram.md), son of Déor, becomes the eighth King of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup>
