**Third Age 2247** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2247**) is the 2247th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2247 was also known as **S.R. 647** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Chieftain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md) of the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md) [Aranuir](../A/Aranuir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-North_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Aravir](../A/Aravir.md), the son of Aranuir, becomes fourth Chieftain.<sup id="cite_ref-North_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-1">[1]</a></sup>
