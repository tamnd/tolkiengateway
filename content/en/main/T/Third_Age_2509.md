**Third Age 2509** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2509**) is the 2509th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2509 was also known as **S.R. 909** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Celebrían](../C/Celebrían.md), journeying to [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md), is waylaid in the [Redhorn Pass](../R/Redhorn_Pass.md), and receives a poisoned wound.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
