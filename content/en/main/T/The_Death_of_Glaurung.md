**The Death of Glaurung** is the seventeenth chapter of *[The Children of Húrin](The_Children_of_Húrin.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Alan_Lee_-_The_Death_of_Glaurung.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/31/Alan_Lee_-_The_Death_of_Glaurung.jpg/300px-Alan_Lee_-_The_Death_of_Glaurung.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="212" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/31/Alan_Lee_-_The_Death_of_Glaurung.jpg/450px-Alan_Lee_-_The_Death_of_Glaurung.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/31/Alan_Lee_-_The_Death_of_Glaurung.jpg/600px-Alan_Lee_-_The_Death_of_Glaurung.jpg 2x" data-file-width="702" data-file-height="495"/></a><figcaption><i>Glaurung approaches</i> by <a href="../A/Alan_Lee.md" title="Alan Lee">Alan Lee</a></figcaption></figure>

It was night when <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="Turambar.md" title="Turambar">Turambar</a>, [Dorlas](../D/Dorlas.md) and [Hunthor](../H/Hunthor.md) arrived to [Cabed-en-Aras](../C/Cabed-en-Aras.md) and began to go down the ravine. The [Teiglin](Teiglin.md) was noisy, stony and very dangerous, and Dorlas cowed and chose not to follow Turambar. While the two men climbed the narrow, they heard the coming of [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md): the dragon crossed the ravine directly from above. With the passing of the dragon, the whole ground shook and the air was hot and poisoned. In that moment a rock hit Hunthor's head and his body fell in to the river. Turambar lamented, but carried on and with all his will climbed up the narrow to the dragon's position. Then he took his sword [Gurthang](../G/Gurthang.md) and stabbed Glaurung's belly to the hilt. Crying in pain, the dragon crossed to the other cliff with the sword still embedded. He twisted in agony, falling in the ground. Turambar almost fainted, but with great strength he left the ravine and approached the dragon. Mocking the dragon, Turambar took back his sword, but a blood stream sprayed his hand, and he fell unconscious.

Meanwhile, in [Nen Girith](../N/Nen_Girith.md) the dragon's cry was heard by [Níniel](../N/Níniel.md) and those who followed her, and everybody panicked and refused to move, believing that the [Black Sword](../B/Black_Sword.md) had died. Then [Brandir](../B/Brandir.md) arrived and found Níniel shaking and petrified; he took her hand and tried to run away with her from [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md). When Níniel realised what was happening, she walked away from him and ran in despair through the woods, with Bradir limping behind her.

Suddenly, Níniel saw Glaurung's body under the moonlight, and near it was Turambar's. She ran to help him, and bandaged his injured hand, but he did not wake. The dragon, however, did wake up, and with his last words he greeted her as the daughter of [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) and sister of [Túrin](Túrin.md). Then Glaurung died and his enchantment upon her faded away. Thus [Niënor](../N/Niënor.md) remembered all her past and cried: *"Farewell, O twice beloved! A Túrin Turambar turún' ambartanen: Master of Doom by Doom mastered!"*

Brandir heard everything, and tried to stop her from running away. But she didn't wait for him and jumped into the [Teiglin](Teiglin.md). Thus Cabed-en-Aras was known since then as *Cabed Naeramarth*, "Leap of Dreadful Doom". Brandir left the place horrified, thinking that Túrin was indeed dead. On the way back he found Dorlas, and realised Dorlas had left Turambar and Hunthor in their quest, and blamed him for everything. Dorlas attacked Brandir, but Brandir drew his sword and killed Dorlas.

Brandir arrived at Nen Girith. He explained to the people what happened: the dragon was dead, and also Turambar, and both were indeed good news, because Turambar and Níniel were actually Túrin and Niënor, the children of Húrin, bringing their dark fate to Brethil. Thus Brandir called Brethil *Sarch nia Hin Húrin*, "Grave of the Children of Húrin". The people was startled by the news, but they decided to build a grave for their saviour and headed to Cabed-en-Aras.
