**Third Age 2956** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2956**) is the 2956th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2956 was also known as **S.R. 1356** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) meets [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and their friendship begins.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Belba Baggins](../B/Belba_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
