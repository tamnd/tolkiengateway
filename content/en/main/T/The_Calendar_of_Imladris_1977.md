**The Calendar of Imladris [1977](/wiki/1977)** was produced by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Permanent_Press&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Permanent Press (page does not exist)">Permanent Press</a> a division of [Thorin and Co.](/wiki/Thorin_and_Co.). It contained fourteen black and white illustrations by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=John_Pivovarnick&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="John Pivovarnick (page does not exist)">John Pivovarnick</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Michael_Logan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Michael Logan (page does not exist)">Michael Logan</a>, and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tom_Santoski&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tom Santoski (page does not exist)">Tom Santoski</a>.

- Dimensions: 11 x 8.5 inches
- Stapled on the fold.
