**This Wandering Day** is the twenty-fifth track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power (Season One: Amazon Original Series Soundtrack)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power_(Season_One%253A_Amazon_Original_Series_Soundtrack).md>)*. The song was composed by David Donaldson, David Long, Janet Roddick, and Steve Roche, with lyrics by J.D. Payne. The song was sung during a montage by the [Harfoot](../H/Harfoots.md) [Poppy Proudfellow](../P/Poppy_Proudfellow.md) ([Megan Richards](../M/Megan_Richards.md)) in scene six of [episode five](../P/Partings.md). The song also appears over the credits of the episode, this time sung by composer Janet Roddick.

<p><a href="../S/Sun.md" title="Sun">The sun</a> is fast fallin&#39;<br/>
Beneath trees of stone,<br/>
The light in the tower,<br/>
No longer my home,<br/>
Passed eyes of pale fire,<br/>
Black sand for my bed,<br/>
I trade all I&#39;ve known for the unknown ahead.<br/>
<br/>
Call to me, call to me, lands far away!<br/>
For I must now wander, this wandering day,<br/>
Away I must wander, this wandering day.<br/>
<br/>
Of drink I have little<br/>
And food I have less,<br/>
My strength tells me no,<br/>
But the path demands yes.<br/>
<br/>
My legs are so short and the way is so long,<br/>
I&#39;ve no rest nor comfort,<br/>
No comfort but song.<br/>
<br/>
Sing to me, sing to me, lands far away!<br/>
Oh rise up and guide me this wandering day,<br/>
Please promise to find me this wandering day.<br/>
<br/>
At last comes their answer,<br/>
Through cold and through frost,<br/>
That not all who wonder or wander are lost,<br/>
No matter the sorrow,<br/>
No matter the cost,<br/>
That not all who wonder or wander are lost.
