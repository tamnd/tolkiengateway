**The Lord of the Rings: Fate of the Fellowship** is a strategy game published by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Z-Man_Games&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Z-Man Games (page does not exist)">Z-Man Games</a> in 2025, retelling the journey of [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) and [Sam](/wiki/Sam) as they make their way to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) with [The One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring), as told in the [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings). It reimplements mechanics from the immensely popular *Pandemic* board games.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

The Lord of the Rings: Fate of the Fellowship™ is a cooperative game where each player controls 2 characters, lending their unique abilities to protect Frodo, battle enemies in pivotal locations, and evade the menacing [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) and [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)’s searching Eye. Each play presents new challenges every time with 24 different objectives, 14 events, and 13 playable characters.
