**Tolkien Quest** (TQ) was a series of solo adventure paperback books based on the *QuestGame* System, published by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md).

After the release of the second book of the series, [Allen &amp; Unwin](../A/Allen_&_Unwin.md) claimed [ICE](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) and Tolkien Enterprises had violated their contract. All books had to be recalled and destroyed, with the loss being estimated between 2.25 and 2.5 million dollars. ICE was able to negotiate a gamebook license with Allen &amp; Unwin in [1986](../_/1986.md), which resulted in the *[Middle-earth Quest](../M/Middle-earth_Quest.md)* series.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Publications {#Publications}

- [1985](../_/1985.md): *[Night of the Nazgûl](../N/Night_of_the_Nazgûl.md)*
- [1985](../_/1985.md): *[The Legend of Weathertop](The_Legend_of_Weathertop.md)*

Two sequel books were planned to be published in January and February of [1986](../_/1986.md): *[Rescue in Mirkwood](../R/Rescue_in_Mirkwood.md)* (which was later published as part of the [Middle-earth Quest](../M/Middle-earth_Quest.md) series) and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Murder_at_Minas_Tirith&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Murder at Minas Tirith (page does not exist)">Murder at Minas Tirith</a></i> which was never published.
