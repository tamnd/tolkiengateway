**Tobold "Old Toby" Hornblower** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) famous for introducing pipe-weed to the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-Pipeweed_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Road_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Road-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Tobold Hornblower came from [Longbottom](../L/Longbottom.md) in the [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md) of [the Shire](The_Shire.md). Around [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1070](Third_Age_2670.md) ([T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2670](Third_Age_2670.md))<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> he started to grow true pipe-weed in his gardens, the first recorded Hobbit to do so.<sup id="cite_ref-Pipeweed_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Road_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Road-2">[2]</a></sup> He was not much of a traveller, but in his youth he often visited [Bree](../B/Bree.md). He already had a keen interest in herbs and it is speculated that in one of these visits he learned of [pipe-weed](../P/Pipe-weed.md) and of [smoking](../S/Smoking.md) from the [Bree-hobbits](../B/Bree-hobbits.md) (who claim to be the first smokers of that herb).<sup id="cite_ref-Pipeweed_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup> Toby however never revealed how he came by the weed.

His name, "[Old Toby](../O/Old_Toby.md)", was given to a variety of weed that was still popular in the late Third Age.<sup id="cite_ref-Pipeweed_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Tobold is a form of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theudebald" title="wikipedia:Theudebald">Theobald</a></i> which contains *theud* "people" and *bald* "bold".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a draft version of the chapter "[The Road to Isengard](The_Road_to_Isengard.md)", the name of this hobbit was Elias Tobiasson of [Mugworth](../M/Mugworth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> *Elias Tobiasson* is a common name in modern Swedish.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In another early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), Toby was named *Tobias Hornblower*, and his [Westron](../W/Westron.md) name was *Zāra-tōbi*<sup id="cite_ref-Languages§50_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Languages§50-8">[8]</a></sup> *Raspūta*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
