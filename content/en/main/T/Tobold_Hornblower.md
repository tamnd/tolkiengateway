**Tobold "Old Toby" Hornblower** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") famous for introducing pipe-weed to the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-Pipeweed_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Road_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Road-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Tobold Hornblower came from [Longbottom](/wiki/Longbottom) in the [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). Around [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1070](/wiki/Third_Age_2670 "Third Age 2670") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2670](/wiki/Third_Age_2670 "Third Age 2670"))<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> he started to grow true pipe-weed in his gardens, the first recorded Hobbit to do so.<sup id="cite_ref-Pipeweed_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Road_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Road-2">[2]</a></sup> He was not much of a traveller, but in his youth he often visited [Bree](/wiki/Bree). He already had a keen interest in herbs and it is speculated that in one of these visits he learned of [pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed") and of [smoking](/wiki/Smoking "Smoking") from the [Bree-hobbits](/wiki/Bree-hobbits) (who claim to be the first smokers of that herb).<sup id="cite_ref-Pipeweed_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup> Toby however never revealed how he came by the weed.

His name, "[Old Toby](/wiki/Old_Toby)", was given to a variety of weed that was still popular in the late Third Age.<sup id="cite_ref-Pipeweed_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Tobold is a form of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theudebald" title="wikipedia:Theudebald">Theobald</a></i> which contains *theud* "people" and *bald* "bold".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a draft version of the chapter "[The Road to Isengard](/wiki/The_Road_to_Isengard)", the name of this hobbit was Elias Tobiasson of [Mugworth](/wiki/Mugworth).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> *Elias Tobiasson* is a common name in modern Swedish.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In another early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), Toby was named *Tobias Hornblower*, and his [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name was *Zāra-tōbi*<sup id="cite_ref-Languages§50_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Languages§50-8">[8]</a></sup> *Raspūta*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
