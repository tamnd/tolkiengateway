**The Fall of Osgiliath** is the sixteenth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* and the twenty-first scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>). It was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The soldiers of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) fight the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md). [Madril](../M/Madril.md) tells [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) that they cannot fight them back any more, or they will all die. Faramir orders the men to retreat to Minas Tirith. [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(Lieutenant_of_Morgul).md>) kills Madril with a spear as he lies on the ground. While the soldiers retreat, they are attacked by the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md).

Gandalf and [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md) ride out from Minas Tirith and hold off the Nazgûl to enable the soldiers to safely return to Minas Tirith. Inside the walls, Faramir approaches Gandalf with information that the orcs have broken their defenses and taken [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md). [Irolas](../I/Irolas.md) adds that [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) has foreseen those events happening, and Gandalf adds that the steward has foreseen and done nothing.

Faramir tells Gandalf that he has seen [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) in [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) two days ago.
