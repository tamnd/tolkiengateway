The **Yondershire** is a division of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") invented for the game *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*. Located north-west of [Needlehole](/wiki/Needlehole), it connects the rest of the Shire to the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Blue_Mountains "Blue Mountains") region.<sup id="cite_ref-U33_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-U33-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is described as 'a sparsely populated region of moor, thicket, and fen that has long been home to Hobbit recluses and troublemakers'.<sup id="cite_ref-U33_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-U33-1">[1]</a></sup> It encompasses the northern part of the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing) and the western part of the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing), and also includes the wilderness between the Bounds and the [River Lune](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn "Lhûn") in the west and the [Twilight Hills](/wiki/Hills_of_Evendim "Hills of Evendim") in the north.

The inhabitants are called "Yonder-hobbits" by the inhabitants of the Shire proper, which folk of the Yondershire in turn call the "Hithershire".<sup id="cite_ref-Quests_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quests-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was settled by [Blanco](/wiki/Blanco), who continued to wander while his brother [Marcho](/wiki/Marcho) became fat and comfortable in Michel Delving. Blanco built his home at [Long Cleeve](/wiki/Long_Cleeve), near the source of [the Water](/wiki/The_Water "The Water"). Since the time of [Bandobras Took](/wiki/Bandobras_Took), Long Cleeve has been the home of the [North-tooks](/wiki/North-took_Family "North-took Family") and the seat of their leader, the Reeve of the Yondershire.<sup id="cite_ref-Sites_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sites-3">[3]</a></sup>

Another important village is [Nobottle](/wiki/Nobottle), founded in the time of [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Ferumbras Took I](/wiki/Ferumbras_Took_I) and located near Needlehole on the edge of the Hithershire. The other two settlements are the hunting village of [Gamwich](/wiki/Gamwich),<sup id="cite_ref-Sites_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sites-3">[3]</a></sup> and [Tighfield](/wiki/Tighfield), known for its rope-making.<sup id="cite_ref-FTtG_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FTtG-4">[4]</a></sup>
