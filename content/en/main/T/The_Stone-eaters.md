<i><b>The Stone-eaters</b></i> was a book written by [Michael George Ramer](/wiki/Michael_George_Ramer) of the [Notion Club](/wiki/Notion_Club)<sup id="cite_ref-Book_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Book-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

At some point in time many years prior to <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Seventh_Age_1987&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Seventh Age 1987 (page does not exist)">1987</a>, Ramer wrote an opening scene to the book that he would later name *The Stone-eaters*. The scene was about "a great morain high up in the barren mountains" that was somehow connected to a Glacier-foot.<sup id="cite_ref-Book_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Book-1">[1]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>and the chief difference (since both were now inner) is that the one is tinged with sadness for it is past, but the other, the Glacier, is not so tinged, has only its own proper flavour, because it is not past or present with reference to the world.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="/wiki/Sauron_Defeated" title="Sauron Defeated">Sauron Defeated</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/The_Notion_Club_Papers_Part_One" title="The Notion Club Papers Part One">The Notion Club Papers Part One</a><sup id="cite_ref-Glacier_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glacier-2">[2]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>
