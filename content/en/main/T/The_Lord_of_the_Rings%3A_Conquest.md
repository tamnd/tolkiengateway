**The Lord of the Rings: Conquest** is the last video game set in Middle-earth that was made by [Electronic Arts](/wiki/Electronic_Arts). It was developed by Pandemic Studios for the PC, Xbox 360, PlayStation 3, and Nintendo DS consoles. [Weta Digital](/wiki/Weta_Digital) worked alongside Pandemic, and has provided several digital models for them to use, including the [fell beasts](/wiki/Fell_beasts "Fell beasts").

## Gameplay {#Gameplay}

### Good Campaign {#Good_Campaign}

The Good Campaign begins at the [Battle of the Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men "Last Alliance of Elves and Men") as a training stage, and continues with loosely-based settings from the films, such as the [Battle of Isengard](/wiki/Battle_of_Isengard) as an [Ent](/wiki/Ent), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) fighting [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) in [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc), battling the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") in the [Mines of Moria](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm "Khazad-dûm"), battling the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) as [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) while protecting [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), and fighting inside [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul). There is also a mission that has [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) and his attempts to take back Moria.

### Evil Campaign {#Evil_Campaign}

The Evil Campaign begins immediately in an alternate universe, where the Nazgûl have just killed Frodo and stopped him from destroying [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"). Players will have the chance to fight as [Oliphaunts](/wiki/Oliphaunts) at [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), as [Trolls](/wiki/Trolls) at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep), as the Balrog, as the Nazgûl against [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) at [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop), against [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), and lastly, as [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) himself against [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).
