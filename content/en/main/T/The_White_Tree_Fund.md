**The White Tree Fund** was a non-profit organisation which raised funds from fans of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to help those who are harmed by natural disasters. It closed down in early [2013](/wiki/2013).<sup id="cite_ref-1.1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1.1-1">[1]</a></sup> Its journal, *[Silver Leaves](/wiki/Silver_Leaves)*, was acquired by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Oloris_Publishing&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Oloris Publishing (page does not exist)">Oloris Publishing</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1.1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1.1-1">[1]</a></sup>
