<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: A Book of 20 Postcards</b></i> is a book containing twenty postcards with illustrations inspired by *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

## From the back {#From_the_back}

A book of 20 postcards featuring paintings inspired by the work of J.R.R. Tolkien. Artists include Ted Nasmith, John Howe, Alan Lee, Roger Garland, Cor Blok and Stephen Hickman.
