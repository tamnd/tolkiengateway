**Third Age 1144** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1144**) is the 1144th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Malvegil](../M/Malvegil.md), son of [Celebrindor](../C/Celebrindor.md) of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
