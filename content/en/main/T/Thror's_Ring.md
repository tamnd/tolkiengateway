<i><b>Thror&#39;s Ring</b></i> is a text-adventure, published by [Eamon](</wiki/Eamon_(video_game)> "Eamon (video game)")<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> and written by [Tom Zuchowski](/wiki/Tom_Zuchowski).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The objective of the game is to find [Thrór's Ring](/wiki/Ring_of_Thr%C3%B3r "Ring of Thrór") in the depths of [Moria](/wiki/Moria).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup>
