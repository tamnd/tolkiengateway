**Tumladen** was the hidden valley within the [Encircling Mountains](/wiki/Echoriad "Echoriad") where the [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") city of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) was built during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

## Description {#Description}

The valley was hidden between the mountain range of the [Echoriad](/wiki/Echoriad), with the [Crissaegrim](/wiki/Crissaegrim) in the south. The only entry to the valley was [Orfalch Echor](/wiki/Orfalch_Echor), the pass formed by the [Dry River](/wiki/Dry_River).<sup id="cite_ref-Noldor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Noldor-1">[1]</a></sup> In the middle of the green plain there was the rocky height of [Amon Gwareth](/wiki/Amon_Gwareth), and upon it the city of Gondolin.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In ancient days, the valley was a great lake between the mountains, but was emptied through the [Dry River](/wiki/Dry_River).<sup id="cite_ref-Noldor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Noldor-1">[1]</a></sup> In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [53](/wiki/First_Age_53 "First Age 53"), [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon), a lord of the exiled [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), discovered Tumladen under the divine guidance of the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo), Lord of Waters.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> There he began to build the city of Gondolin in the top of [Amon Gwareth](/wiki/Amon_Gwareth), and after fifty years of work he moved there from [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast) with all his people.<sup id="cite_ref-Noldor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Noldor-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the [Quest of the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_of_the_Silmaril), [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) could see the valley while being carried by [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles). Tears fell from Lúthien's eyes into the plain, and from them a fountain sprang to life: the Fountain of Tinúviel, or [Eithel Nínui](/wiki/Eithel_N%C3%ADnui).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), the valley was covered with mists due the fume of the burning and the steam of the fountains in the flame of the [dragons](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons"). This allowed the [survivors](/wiki/Exiles_of_Gondolin "Exiles of Gondolin") to escape from the slaughter through the plain without being noticed and reach the mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tumladen* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Wide Valley"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> or "a flat valley with steep sides",<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> from *[tum](/wiki/Tum "Tum")* ("valley, vale")<sup id="cite_ref-S_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-7">[7]</a></sup> + *laden* ("flat, wide"). An alternate Sindarin spelling is *Tumladhen*.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-8">[8]</a></sup>

In the early *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")*, *Tumladen* was given as [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) for "Level Vale".<sup id="cite_ref-Etymologies_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etymologies-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for the vale was *i Tumbo*, short form of <i><b>i Tumbo Tarmacorto</b></i> ("the Vale of the High Mountain Circle"). Its Sindarin cognate was <i><b>Tum Orchorod</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-8">[8]</a></sup>

An early [Q(u)enya](/wiki/Qenya "Qenya") name was <i><b>Tumbolatsin</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Etymologies_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etymologies-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the vale was called <i><b>Tumladin</b></i> ("Valley of Smoothness") in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish). The vale had been leveled by the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) with great toil, so everything there could be watched from Amon Gwareth.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-10">[10]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:163</sup> [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) had to walk a day's light march to reach Amon Gwareth from the entrance of the [Way of Escape](/wiki/Way_of_Escape). The plain "was of a marvellous level, broken but here and there by boulders round and smooth which lay amid a sward, or by pools in rocky beds. Many fair pathways lay across that plain."<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-10">[10]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:158</sup>
