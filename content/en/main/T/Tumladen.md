**Tumladen** was the hidden valley within the [Encircling Mountains](../E/Echoriad.md) where the [Elven](../E/Elves.md) city of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) was built during the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

## Description {#Description}

The valley was hidden between the mountain range of the [Echoriad](../E/Echoriad.md), with the [Crissaegrim](../C/Crissaegrim.md) in the south. The only entry to the valley was [Orfalch Echor](../O/Orfalch_Echor.md), the pass formed by the [Dry River](../D/Dry_River.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Noldor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Noldor-1">[1]</a></sup> In the middle of the green plain there was the rocky height of [Amon Gwareth](../A/Amon_Gwareth.md), and upon it the city of Gondolin.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In ancient days, the valley was a great lake between the mountains, but was emptied through the [Dry River](../D/Dry_River.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Noldor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Noldor-1">[1]</a></sup> In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [53](../F/First_Age_53.md), [Turgon](Turgon.md), a lord of the exiled [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), discovered Tumladen under the divine guidance of the [Vala](../V/Valar.md) [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md), Lord of Waters.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> There he began to build the city of Gondolin in the top of [Amon Gwareth](../A/Amon_Gwareth.md), and after fifty years of work he moved there from [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) with all his people.<sup id="cite_ref-Noldor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Noldor-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the [Quest of the Silmaril](../Q/Quest_of_the_Silmaril.md), [Beren](../B/Beren.md) and [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) could see the valley while being carried by [Eagles](../E/Eagles.md). Tears fell from Lúthien's eyes into the plain, and from them a fountain sprang to life: the Fountain of Tinúviel, or [Eithel Nínui](../E/Eithel_Nínui.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

During the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), the valley was covered with mists due the fume of the burning and the steam of the fountains in the flame of the [dragons](../D/Dragons.md). This allowed the [survivors](../E/Exiles_of_Gondolin.md) to escape from the slaughter through the plain without being noticed and reach the mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tumladen* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Wide Valley"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> or "a flat valley with steep sides",<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> from *[tum](Tum.md)* ("valley, vale")<sup id="cite_ref-S_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-7">[7]</a></sup> + *laden* ("flat, wide"). An alternate Sindarin spelling is *Tumladhen*.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-8">[8]</a></sup>

In the early *[Etymologies](The_Etymologies.md)*, *Tumladen* was given as [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) for "Level Vale".<sup id="cite_ref-Etymologies_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etymologies-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name for the vale was *i Tumbo*, short form of <i><b>i Tumbo Tarmacorto</b></i> ("the Vale of the High Mountain Circle"). Its Sindarin cognate was <i><b>Tum Orchorod</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-8">[8]</a></sup>

An early [Q(u)enya](../Q/Qenya.md) name was <i><b>Tumbolatsin</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Etymologies_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etymologies-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, the vale was called <i><b>Tumladin</b></i> ("Valley of Smoothness") in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md). The vale had been leveled by the [Gnomes](../G/Gnomes.md) with great toil, so everything there could be watched from Amon Gwareth.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-10">[10]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:163</sup> [Tuor](Tuor.md) and [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md) had to walk a day's light march to reach Amon Gwareth from the entrance of the [Way of Escape](../W/Way_of_Escape.md). The plain "was of a marvellous level, broken but here and there by boulders round and smooth which lay amid a sward, or by pools in rocky beds. Many fair pathways lay across that plain."<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-10">[10]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:158</sup>
