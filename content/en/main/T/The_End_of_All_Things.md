**The End of All Things** is the fifty-seventh scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* and the seventy-second scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The two hobbits barely escape the destruction of the [Sammath Naur](../S/Sammath_Naur.md), with [Sam](../S/Sam.md) half-dragging [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) out through the doorway. They find a raised boulder for what they believe will be their final respite. Frodo, no longer weighed down with the burden of the [Ring](The_One_Ring.md), exclaims *"It's done"*. He lays down, with a sea of molten lava surrounding them, and closes his eyes. *"I can see [The Shire](The_Shire.md)"*, he says. Sam looks on, and reminisces about the life he could have had, had he not left The Shire with Frodo on the quest that led them to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). He speaks of [Rosie Cotton](../R/Rosie_Cotton.md), and weeps as he says *"If ever I was to marry someone... it would have been her."* Frodo gets up, wraps an arm around Sam's shoulder, and says *"I'm glad to be with you Samwise Gamgee. Here at the end of all things"*. The screen fades to black for a few seconds.

As Frodo and Sam lie on a large boulder awaiting death in the fiery cataclysm, Gandalf, upon [Gwaihir](../G/Gwaihir.md), flies towards them, with two other eagles. The eagles take Frodo and Sam from the large rock. A dazed Frodo looks around before his eyes slowly close.
