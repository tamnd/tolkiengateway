**The End of All Things** is the fifty-seventh scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* and the seventy-second scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The two hobbits barely escape the destruction of the [Sammath Naur](/wiki/Sammath_Naur), with [Sam](/wiki/Sam) half-dragging [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) out through the doorway. They find a raised boulder for what they believe will be their final respite. Frodo, no longer weighed down with the burden of the [Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), exclaims *"It's done"*. He lays down, with a sea of molten lava surrounding them, and closes his eyes. *"I can see [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire)"*, he says. Sam looks on, and reminisces about the life he could have had, had he not left The Shire with Frodo on the quest that led them to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). He speaks of [Rosie Cotton](/wiki/Rosie_Cotton), and weeps as he says *"If ever I was to marry someone... it would have been her."* Frodo gets up, wraps an arm around Sam's shoulder, and says *"I'm glad to be with you Samwise Gamgee. Here at the end of all things"*. The screen fades to black for a few seconds.

As Frodo and Sam lie on a large boulder awaiting death in the fiery cataclysm, Gandalf, upon [Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir), flies towards them, with two other eagles. The eagles take Frodo and Sam from the large rock. A dazed Frodo looks around before his eyes slowly close.
