**Third Age 2846** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2846**) is the 2846th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2846 was also known as **S.R. 1246** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Bungo Baggins](../B/Bungo_Baggins.md), son of [Mungo Baggins](../M/Mungo_Baggins.md) and [Laura Grubb](../L/Laura_Grubb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Hob Gammidge](../H/Hob_Gammidge.md), son of [Wiseman Gamwich](../W/Wiseman_Gamwich.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
