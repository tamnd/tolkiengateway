**Tolman Cotton** refers to two members of the [Cotton Family](/wiki/Cotton_Family):

- [Tolman Cotton Senior](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior) ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1341](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") - [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1440](/wiki/Fourth_Age_19 "Fourth Age 19")), [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton)'s father.
- [Tolman Cotton Junior](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Junior) ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1380](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980")), [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton)'s eldest brother.
