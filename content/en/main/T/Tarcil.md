**Tarcil** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [280](/wiki/Third_Age_280 "Third Age 280")<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup> – [515](/wiki/Third_Age_515 "Third Age 515"),<sup id="cite_ref-App_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 235 years) was the sixth [King of Arnor](/wiki/King_of_Arnor).

## History {#History}

Tarcil succeeded his father, [Arantar](/wiki/Arantar), when he died in [Third Age 435](/wiki/Third_Age_435). Tarcil was great-great-grandson of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur).

Tarcil ruled uneventfully for eighty years, and was succeeded by his son, [Tarondor](</wiki/Tarondor_(King_of_Arnor)> "Tarondor (King of Arnor)"), upon his death in [Third Age 515](/wiki/Third_Age_515).<sup id="cite_ref-App_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tarcil* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but the spelling variant [Tarkil](/wiki/Tarkil) also appears in early drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* as the name of the sixth king of Arnor.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Based on its etymology, it means "[High Man](/wiki/High_Man)" or "Númenorean" and is a compound of *[tar-](/wiki/Tar "Tar")* ("high") and a shortened from of *[Hildo](/wiki/Hildo)* ("Man").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> See *[tarkil](/wiki/Tarkil "Tarkil")* for more.

In early drafts "the Tarkil" was a nickname for [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) before replaced by "the Dúnadan".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
