**Third Age 2459** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2459**) is the 2459th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2459 was also known as **S.R. 859** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Léod](../L/Léod.md), later a [Lord of the Éothéod](../L/Lord_of_the_Éothéod.md).

## Calculation of the Date {#Calculation_of_the_Date}

Léod was 42 years old when he died and his son [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md) was but 16.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Eorl was born in [T.A.](Third_Age.md) [2485](Third_Age_2485.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> so when he was 16 it was the year [2501](Third_Age_2501.md). Forty-two years before was 2459, the year of Léod's birth.
