<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers Creatures</b></i> is a tie-in book released for [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson)'s *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Packed with photos, this fascinating introduction to the monsters and peoples in the Lord of the Rings film trilogy shows how the filmmakers have brought the inhabitants of Middle-earth to life for the big screen.
</p><p>The book contains creatures familiar from the first film, from the benign hobbits to Lurtz, the ill-fated leader of the Uruk-hai, and includes races prominent in the new movie, The Two Towers, including the Rohirrim, the Easterlings, the Haradrim and the tree-like Ents.
</p><p>Both an exciting introduction for children and a reference for adult movie buffs, there are also behind-the-scenes details on how each creature was designed and brought to life.
</p>
</blockquote>
