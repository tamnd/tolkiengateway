**The Fellowship of the Ring** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- **[Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring), a fairly uncommon (though highly popular) name for the [Ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearer) and his eight companions.**

## Books {#Books}

- <b><i><a href="/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Fellowship of the Ring">The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>, being the First Part of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i>.</b>
- *[The Art of The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Art_of_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, a film tie-in photobook by [Gary Russell](/wiki/Gary_Russell).
- *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring Visual Companion](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_Visual_Companion)*, a film tie-in photobook by [Jude Fisher](/wiki/Jude_Fisher).
