**The Tolkien Experience** is a two-fold project by Luke Shelton, that encompases the **Tolkien Experience Project** and **Podcast**, both focused on collecting the experiences by fans and scholars.

## Tolkien Experience Project {#Tolkien_Experience_Project}

"It is an attempt to catalogue the experience of different Tolkien fans and share them in a public forum to grow community around Tolkien's work!"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Fans who wish to participate must answer five questions:

1. How were you introduced to Tolkien's work?
2. What is your favorite part of Tolkien's work?
3. What is your fondest experience of Tolkien's work?
4. Has the way you approach Tolkien's work changed over time?
5. Would you ever recommend Tolkien's work? Why/Why not?

## Tolkien Experience Podcast {#Tolkien_Experience_Podcast}

Hosted by Luke Shelton, Sara Brown and Sarah Westvik. "The TEP is an attempt to bring the fan and scholarly communities together around our shared passion: the works of J.R.R. Tolkien! Each episode features a notable scholar or member of the fan community sharing their responses to the original Tolkien Experience Project questions".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The guests are asked the same five questions from the Project, with an added sixth question:

<dl><dd>6. What Tolkien work, project, or community are you currently working on that we should know about?</dd></dl>

In [2021](/wiki/2021), the podcast received [the Tolkien Society Award](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society_Award "The Tolkien Society Award") for best online content.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
