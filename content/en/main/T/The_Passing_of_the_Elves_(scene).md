**The Passing of the Elves** is the eleventh scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition).md>). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) and [Sam](../S/Sam.md) are at a camping site in the forest in the evening. While Sam is cooking food over a fire, they hear [Wood-elves](../W/Wood-elves.md) singing in the distance and run to see them. The Elves are walking in a solemn procession on their way to the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md). Sam says it makes him sad that the Elves are leaving Middle-earth.

Later, when Frodo and Sam have bedded down for the night, Sam complains about lying on the ground among tree roots, to Frodo's amusement.

A [Black Rider](../B/Black_Rider.md) is briefly seen in an empty landscape of the [Shire](../S/Shire.md). In the theatrical edition of the film, this shot was included in [The Shadow of the Past](<The_Shadow_of_the_Past_(scene).md>).

## Differences {#Differences}

This scene is not directly based on anything in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, but may be inspired by the episode in the chapter [Three is Company](Three_is_Company.md) in which Frodo and his companions meet a party of wood-elves led by [Gildor](../G/Gildor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is also said that Sam "believed he had once seen an Elf in the woods."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> There is also a reference to the discomfort of sleeping on the ground: "Frodo woke up first, and found that a tree-root had made a hole in his back, and that his neck was stiff."<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
