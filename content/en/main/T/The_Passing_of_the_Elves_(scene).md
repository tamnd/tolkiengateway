**The Passing of the Elves** is the eleventh scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)"). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) and [Sam](/wiki/Sam) are at a camping site in the forest in the evening. While Sam is cooking food over a fire, they hear [Wood-elves](/wiki/Wood-elves) singing in the distance and run to see them. The Elves are walking in a solemn procession on their way to the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens). Sam says it makes him sad that the Elves are leaving Middle-earth.

Later, when Frodo and Sam have bedded down for the night, Sam complains about lying on the ground among tree roots, to Frodo's amusement.

A [Black Rider](/wiki/Black_Rider) is briefly seen in an empty landscape of the [Shire](/wiki/Shire). In the theatrical edition of the film, this shot was included in [The Shadow of the Past](</wiki/The_Shadow_of_the_Past_(scene)> "The Shadow of the Past (scene)").

## Differences {#Differences}

This scene is not directly based on anything in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, but may be inspired by the episode in the chapter [Three is Company](/wiki/Three_is_Company) in which Frodo and his companions meet a party of wood-elves led by [Gildor](/wiki/Gildor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is also said that Sam "believed he had once seen an Elf in the woods."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> There is also a reference to the discomfort of sleeping on the ground: "Frodo woke up first, and found that a tree-root had made a hole in his back, and that his neck was stiff."<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
