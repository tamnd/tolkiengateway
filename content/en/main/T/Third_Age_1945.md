**Third Age 1945** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1945**) is the 1945th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1945 was also known as **S.R. 345** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Council of Gondor](../C/Council_of_Gondor.md) refutes [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md)'s claim;<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-1">[1]</a></sup> [Eärnil II](../E/Eärnil_II.md) receives the crown of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Angmar conflict](../A/Angmar_conflict.md): Around the same time [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) continues its assaults against [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md); King [Araphant](../A/Araphant.md) continues to hold off with dwindling strength, for the rest of his reign.<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-1">[1]</a></sup>
