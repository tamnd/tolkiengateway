<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Movie Trilogy Coloring Book</b></i> is a [2016](../_/2016.md) <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coloring_book#Adult_coloring_books" title="wikipedia:Coloring book">adult colouring book</a>, illustrated by Nicolette Caven. The illustrations are based on [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md)'s [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-HC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Experience your favorite characters and enchanted scenes from one of the most famous fantasy worlds every created—Middle-earth—in a brilliant new way with this first authorized coloring book based on the Academy Award-winning epic motion picture trilogy <i>The Lord of the Rings</i> directed by Peter Jackson.<br/><br/>The groundbreaking, award-winning <i>The Lord of the Rings</i> film trilogy directed by Peter Jackson brought <a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>&#39;s magical world of <a href="../M/Middle-earth.md" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a> radiantly to life for millions worldwide. Now, fans can explore this enchanted universe as never before with the official <i>The Lord of the Rings Movie Trilogy Coloring Book</i>.<br/><br/>Embark on your own coloring adventure through the lands of Middle-earth, from the grim landscape of <a href="../M/Mordor.md" title="Mordor">Mordor</a> to the majestic realms of <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> and <a href="../R/Rivendell.md" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>, from the Fellowship&#39;s frightening journey through the mines of <a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Moria</a> to the Battle of Pelennor Fields. <i>The Lord of the Rings Movie Trilogy Coloring Book</i> is packed with black-and-white line illustrations-all breathtaking in detail-that showcase favorite characters, including <a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a>, <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>, <a href="../L/Legolas.md" title="Legolas">Legolas</a>, Frodo, Sam, and <a href="../G/Gollum.md" title="Gollum">Gollum</a>, as well as iconic creatures such as the tree-like Ents, the fearsome Balrog and the hideous Shelob. This companion book also features some of the unforgettable props used in the films, such as the One Ring, Frodo&#39;s sword <a href="../S/Sting.md" title="Sting">Sting</a>, and <a href="../A/Arwen.md" title="Arwen">Arwen</a>&#39;s beautiful Evenstar.<br/><br/>The line drawings in <i>The Lord of the Rings Movie Trilogy Coloring Book</i> are printed on heavy stock paper that absorb colors and prevents them from bleeding through no matter which medium-paint, marker, pen, pencil, or crayon-you choose. Whether you want to color each image true to life or prefer to add your own imaginative flourish, the choice is up to you!<br/><br/>An exciting, artistic journey through Middle-earth, <i>The Lord of the Rings Movie Trilogy Coloring </i>Book is ideal for dedicated Rings fans, coloring enthusiasts, and anyone looking for a few hours of creative fun.<sup id="cite_ref-HC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HC-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>
