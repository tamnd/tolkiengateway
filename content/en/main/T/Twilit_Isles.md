The **Twilit Isles** were located west of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

These islands already "floated" in the West before the falling of the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps), and were located in the [Shadowy Seas](/wiki/Shadowy_Seas), later being considered the first of the [Outer Lands](/wiki/Aman "Aman") (the lands of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar)) when travelling westwards.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> They were also the first place from where a faint [light](/wiki/Light "Light") of [Silpion](/wiki/Silpion) could be seen in the distance.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:125</sup> When [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) was established, the Twilit Isles were far dim in the West, whereas the [Magic Isles](/wiki/Magic_Isles) were east of it.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:121</sup> Later, some birds from [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna)'s gardens came to the seas and [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB) showed them dwellings in these Isles.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:123</sup> In later days, the [Tower of Pearl](/wiki/Tower_of_Pearl) was built upon the most western isle of the archipelago.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:125</sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), they were known as <i><b>Tolli Kimpelear</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) comments on the possibility that the [Enchanted Isles](/wiki/Enchanted_Isles) mentioned in *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* were also inspired by the Twilit Isles, as well as the Magic Isles.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
