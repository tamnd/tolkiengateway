The **Twilit Isles** were located west of [Tol Eressëa](Tol_Eressëa.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

## History {#History}

These islands already "floated" in the West before the falling of the [Two Lamps](Two_Lamps.md), and were located in the [Shadowy Seas](../S/Shadowy_Seas.md), later being considered the first of the [Outer Lands](../A/Aman.md) (the lands of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md)) when travelling westwards.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> They were also the first place from where a faint [light](../L/Light.md) of [Silpion](../S/Silpion.md) could be seen in the distance.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:125</sup> When [Tol Eressëa](Tol_Eressëa.md) was established, the Twilit Isles were far dim in the West, whereas the [Magic Isles](../M/Magic_Isles.md) were east of it.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:121</sup> Later, some birds from [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md)'s gardens came to the seas and [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md) showed them dwellings in these Isles.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:123</sup> In later days, the [Tower of Pearl](Tower_of_Pearl.md) was built upon the most western isle of the archipelago.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:125</sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), they were known as <i><b>Tolli Kimpelear</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) comments on the possibility that the [Enchanted Isles](../E/Enchanted_Isles.md) mentioned in *[The Silmarillion](The_Silmarillion.md)* were also inspired by the Twilit Isles, as well as the Magic Isles.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
