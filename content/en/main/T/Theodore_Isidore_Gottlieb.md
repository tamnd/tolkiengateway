**Theodore Isidore Gottlieb** ([1906](/wiki/1906)-[2001](/wiki/2001)), mostly known as **Brother Theodore**, was an American actor and comedian. He was the voice for [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) in [The Hobbit (1977 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)>) and [The Return of the King (1980 film)](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)>) animated films.
