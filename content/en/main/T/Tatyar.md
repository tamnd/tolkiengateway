**Tatyar** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈtacar]</span>) or Tatalië<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the original name of the second Clan of the Elves, before the [Great March](../G/Great_March.md). The Clan-name, meaning 'Seconds' (q. cardinal *(t)atya* 'second') was derived from the name of the father of the Clan, [Tata](Tata.md) 'Two', the second [Elf-father](../E/Elf-fathers.md) to awake at [Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md).

The other original clans in Cuiviénen were the [Minyar](../M/Minyar.md) 'Firsts', and [Nelyar](../N/Nelyar.md) 'Thirds' according to the Elf-fathers [Imin](../I/Imin.md) 'One' and [Enel](../E/Enel.md) 'Three'. The Tatyar were 56 in number out of the original 144 Elves who awoke at Cuiviénen. Half of them followed [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md) in the March and became the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) and half of them [stayed back](../A/Avari.md) with [Morwë](../M/Morwë.md) and/or [Nurwë](../N/Nurwë.md).

Some of the Avari who were of the Tatyarin origin eventually reached [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) and acknowledged their kinship with the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), but were unfriendly, jealous of their more exalted kin, and considered them arrogant.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
