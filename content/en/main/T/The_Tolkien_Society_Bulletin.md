<i><b>The Tolkien Society Bulletin</b></i> was the first bulletin of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society). All four issues were edited by Phil Spencer. It was to be followed by *[Andúril](</wiki/And%C3%BAril_(UK)> "Andúril (UK)")*, the precursor to *[Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Issues {#Issues}

### *The Tolkien Society Bulletin* 1 {#The_Tolkien_Society_Bulletin_1}

- Published: October [1970](/wiki/1970)

### *The Tolkien Society Bulletin*2 {#The_Tolkien_Society_Bulletin2}

- Published: November 1970

### *The Tolkien Society Bulletin* 3 {#The_Tolkien_Society_Bulletin_3}

- Published: January [1971](/wiki/1971)

### *The Tolkien Society Bulletin* 4 {#The_Tolkien_Society_Bulletin_4}

- Published: June 1971
