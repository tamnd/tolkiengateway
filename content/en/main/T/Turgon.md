**Turgon** was an [Elven](../E/Elves.md) king of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), second son of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md), brother to [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md), [Aredhel](../A/Aredhel.md) and [Argon](../A/Argon.md). He was lord of the [Elves of Nevrast](../G/Gondolindrim.md), and later of the Hidden City of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).

He was often called "*the Wise*" and is described as "tallest of all the [Children of the World](../C/Children_of_the_World.md), save [Thingol](Thingol.md)"; with a white and gold [sword](../G/Glamdring.md) in an ivory sheath.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-4">[2]</a></sup> He sat in a high throne, holding his staff of doom.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-5">[3]</a></sup>

### Life in Aman {#Life_in_Aman}

Turgon was born in the [Year of the Trees 1300](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1300.md), the same year as his cousin [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[4]</a></sup> At some point, he married [Elenwë](../E/Elenwë.md) of the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md), and they had a child: [Idril](../I/Idril.md).

After the [Darkening of Valinor](../D/Darkening_of_Valinor.md), [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) gave his famous speech in [Tirion](Tirion.md) and proclaimed his [Oath](../O/Oath_of_Fëanor.md). Turgon and his father [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) spoke against Fëanor, and the Fëanorians almost unsheathed their swords, but [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md) intervened and invited the Noldor to ponder the matter. Turgon and his friend Finrod were together against the departure of the Noldor from [Aman](../A/Aman.md), but the Noldorin assembly chose [Exile](../E/Exile_of_the_Noldor.md). So Turgon set out on the journey, and although he was originally against it, he did not come back after the [Doom of Mandos](../D/Doom_of_Mandos.md) because "Fingon and Turgon were bold and fiery of heart, and loath to abandon any task to which they had put their hands until the bitter end, if bitter it must be". After Fëanor and his [sons](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) departed using the only ships, abandoning the remaining Noldor, Turgon went with his people across the dangerous [Helcaraxë](../H/Helcaraxë.md) alongside his father Fingolfin.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup> During the crossing, Turgon's wife Elenwë, and their daughter Idril fell into the bitter waters. Turgon risked his life to save them, but he only could save Idril, and Elenwë was lost. Turgon was always hostile to the [House of Fëanor](../H/House_of_Fëanor.md) thereafter.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup>

### Building of Gondolin {#Building_of_Gondolin}

Once they arrived in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), Turgon and his followers settled at [Vinyamar](../V/Vinyamar.md) in [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md). In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [50](../F/First_Age_50.md), he and Finrod journeyed together along [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md), and while they slept near the [Aelin-uial](../A/Aelin-uial.md), [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) sent both of them a dream to look for a hidden place where they could be safe from the power of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). Then, in the following year, Ulmo appeared directly to Turgon and guided him to the vale of [Tumladen](Tumladen.md) in the [Encircling Mountains](../E/Encircling_Mountains.md). Initially, Turgon left Tumladen and returned to Vinyamar, keeping his discovery a secret for many years and planning the construction of a city.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/M/Mysilvergreen_-_Turgon_and_Idril.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a9/Mysilvergreen_-_Turgon_and_Idril.jpg/300px-Mysilvergreen_-_Turgon_and_Idril.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="212" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a9/Mysilvergreen_-_Turgon_and_Idril.jpg/450px-Mysilvergreen_-_Turgon_and_Idril.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a9/Mysilvergreen_-_Turgon_and_Idril.jpg/600px-Mysilvergreen_-_Turgon_and_Idril.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1754" data-file-height="1240"/></a><figcaption><i>Turgon and Idril</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Mysilvergreen.md" title="Category:Images by Mysilvergreen">Mysilvergreen</a></figcaption></figure>

After the [Dagor Aglareb](../D/Dagor_Aglareb.md), Turgon began to once again feel the restlessness set in his heart by Ulmo, and his thought turned to Tumladen. He secretly moved many of the most skilled Noldor to the valley, and had them build [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). When it was completed in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [116](../F/First_Age_116.md),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[8]</a></sup> he abandoned Vinyamar with all his people and led them to Tumladen in secret, hidden by the power of Ulmo. Before leaving, however, he left in Vinyamar a sword, mail and helm for one who would come in the hour of direst need of the Noldor and deliver hope, following Ulmo's prophecy. In his Hidden City, Turgon was King of the [Gondolindrim](../G/Gondolindrim.md), and all kept working on embellishing Gondolin. He even personally created the two trees [Glingal and Belthil](../G/Glingal_and_Belthil.md) in the image of the [Two Trees](Two_Trees.md).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[9]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [400](../F/First_Age_400.md),<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup> after the unfortunate events concerning his sister [Aredhel](../A/Aredhel.md), Turgon welcomed his nephew [Maeglin](../M/Maeglin.md), who became a close counsellor.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-5">[3]</a></sup>

### Doom of the Noldor {#Doom_of_the_Noldor}

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [458](../F/First_Age_458.md) Turgon took in [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) and [Huor](../H/Huor.md), who were saved by the [Eagles](../E/Eagles.md). They forged a strong friendship, but after a year he allowed them to leave the city, although it was against his law.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[11]</a></sup> Turgon and the two brothers met again in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md): in the middle of the battle, they rejoiced in their meeting. When Fingon was killed and the battle seemed lost, Huor urged Turgon to leave, prophesying that from both of them a new [star](../E/Eärendil.md) would rise. Thanks to the men of the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md) in the [Fen of Serech](../F/Fen_of_Serech.md), the Gondolindrim escaped, but Huor was killed and Húrin captured. Although the battle was still lost, Turgon's unexpected intervention prevented the rout from completely destroying the armies of the Noldor and their allies.<sup id="cite_ref-Nirnaeth_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nirnaeth-14">[12]</a></sup>

With the death of his brother Fingon, Turgon became the new [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md), and he was the most feared of the enemies of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), as he remained hidden.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[13]</a></sup> In those times, [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) settled in the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md), and with his help, Turgon sent seven boats to the [West](../A/Aman.md) to ask help to the Valar, but all of them were wrecked.<sup id="cite_ref-Nirnaeth_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nirnaeth-14">[12]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/C/Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_Turgon,_Idril,_and_Maeglin_together.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/88/Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_Turgon%2C_Idril%2C_and_Maeglin_together.jpg/300px-Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_Turgon%2C_Idril%2C_and_Maeglin_together.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="264" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/88/Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_Turgon%2C_Idril%2C_and_Maeglin_together.jpg/450px-Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_Turgon%2C_Idril%2C_and_Maeglin_together.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/88/Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_Turgon%2C_Idril%2C_and_Maeglin_together.jpg/600px-Catherine_Karina_Chmiel_-_Turgon%2C_Idril%2C_and_Maeglin_together.jpg 2x" data-file-width="700" data-file-height="617"/></a><figcaption><i>Turgon, Idril, and Maeglin together</i> by <a href="../C/Catherine_Karina_Chmiel.md" title="Catherine Karina Chmiel">Catherine Karina Chmiel</a></figcaption></figure>

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md),<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[14]</a></sup> [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md), the only survivor of the shipwrecks, brought into the Hidden City a man: [Tuor](Tuor.md), son of Huor. The King received him in the [Tower of the King](Tower_of_the_King.md), and when Tuor spoke, his cloak fell, revealing the arms Turgon had left in Vinyamar following Ulmo's orders nearly 400 years earlier.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-4">[2]</a></sup> Tuor gave him [Ulmo's warning](../U/Ulmo's_warning.md): the [Doom of the Noldor](../D/Doom_of_the_Noldor~13942.md) was approaching, so the [Gondolindrim](../G/Gondolindrim.md) had to leave their fair city and go down Sirion to the sea. But the King had become proud and loved his kingdom, so he rejected the counsel of the Vala. However, he ordered the blocking up of the [Seven Gates](../S/Seven_Gates.md), so that no one could enter the city, and the only news that came in was that brought by [Thorondor](Thorondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-17">[15]</a></sup>

Some years later, Húrin was released by Morgoth, and he approached the [Echoriad](../E/Echoriad.md) with the hope of returning to Gondolin. Thorondor informed Turgon, but he distrusted Húrin and did not help him. Turgon later regretted it, but it was too late; through Húrin's wanderings, the location of the Hidden City became known to Morgoth.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[16]</a></sup>

Seven years after his coming, Tuor married Idril. Turgon blessed their union, as he held Tuor in great esteem and he had not forgotten the words of Huor in the Nirnaeth Arnoediad.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-17">[15]</a></sup>

Morgoth attacked the city in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [510](../F/First_Age_510.md).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[17]</a></sup> The tale of this battle was told in <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium"><i>The Fall of Gondolin</i></a>, including Turgon's defence of the Tower of the King together with the people of his stronghold: "and mighty was its fall and the fall of Turgon in its ruin".<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-17">[15]</a></sup>

A group of [Exiles](../E/Exiles_of_Gondolin.md) survived the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md) and reached the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md). There [Ereinion Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) was named the next High King of the Noldor. Turgon's grandson, [Eärendil the Blessed](../E/Eärendil.md), would later bring salvation to Middle-earth, thus fulfilling Morgoth's fears about him.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Turgon* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), meaning "Ruling Lord" or "Victory Prince",<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-20">[18]</a></sup> probably a combination of *[tur](Tur.md)* ("master")<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[19]</a></sup> and *-gon* ("lord, prince").<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-22">[20]</a></sup>

However, in *[The Shibboleth of Fëanor](The_Shibboleth_of_Fëanor.md)*, Tolkien translated *Turgon* as "Master Shout". There it is explained that the name is the [Sindarized](../S/Sindarized.md) version of his [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) [father-name](../F/Father-name.md), <i><b>Turukáno</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-22">[20]</a></sup> clearly meaning "Victory Commander".<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[21]</a></sup>

A rejected Quenya name was *Turondo* ("Lord of Stone").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-20">[18]</a></sup> In the earliest stage of the Elvish [languages](../L/Languages.md), the [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) name was the same, and the [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) name was also *Turgon*, which [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) suggests to be derived from the [root](../R/Root.md) [TURU](TUR~21398.md) ("be strong").<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[22]</a></sup>

At one point *Sarafinwë* was considered for his father name, with the name *Turgond (Turukond)* as a title.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_20-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-20">[18]</a></sup> While "Sarafinwë" was not defined, it is notable that the root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=SAR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SAR (page does not exist)">SAR</a> had the meaning of "stone".<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[23]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)* {#The_Book_of_Lost_Tales}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>But I Turgon will not leave my city, and will burn with it.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a class="mw-redirect" href="The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two.md" title="The Book of Lost Tales Part Two">The Book of Lost Tales Part Two</a></i>, &#34;<a href="The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md" title="The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)">The Fall of Gondolin</a>&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

Turgon already appears in the earliest stage of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. He is the son of Finwë Nólemë, lord of the [Noldoli](../N/Noldoli.md) (precursor of both [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md) and [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md)). Before Turgon (or *Turondo* in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md)) was born, [Amnon](../A/Amnon.md) prophesied the fall of Gondolin and the fade of Turgon.<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[24]</a></sup> Turgon was born soon after the [Flight of the Noldoli](../E/Exile_of_the_Noldor.md) in [Lake Mithrim](../L/Lake_Mithrim.md) or near [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md). Later he participated in the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](../B/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears.md), and there his father was isolated and slain by the enemy, and the Orcs cut out Nólemë's heart. But Turgon rescued the body, and the [scarlet heart](../S/Scarlet_heart.md) became his emblem. With terrible wrath, he ran out of the battle, and went to save the women and children of the camps. Then he fled south along Sirion, and aided by its magic waters, he escaped into a secret place away from [Melko](../M/Melko.md). There the Noldoli built the secret city of Gondolin and Turgon became their king.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[25]</a></sup>

Many years later, when [Tuor](Tuor.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium) came to the city, he found Turgon in the [Square of the Palace](../S/Square_of_the_Palace.md). "Then Turgon king of Gondolin robed in white with a belt of gold, and a coronet of garnets upon his head, stood before his doors and spake from the head of the white stairs that led thereto." Tuor was well received, as his coming was prophesied, but [Ulmo's warning](../U/Ulmo's_warning.md) was not attended by the king, who answered that he had already sent boats to the West which had never come back. However, Turgon knew that Tuor had the favour of the Valar, and invited him to dwell in Gondolin, even in the royal halls.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-28">[26]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:160–2</sup>

Many years later, Turgon allowed the wedding of Tuor and his daughter Idril. All the [Gondothlim](../G/Gondothlim.md) and the king were overjoyed when [Eärendel](../E/Eärendel.md) was born, except [Meglin](../M/Meglin.md), the king's nephew and close counsellor.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_28-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-28">[26]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:164–5</sup> One year later, news came about spies of [Melko](../M/Morgoth.md) near [Tumladin](Tumladin.md), and the king remembered the words of Tuor, so he tripled the watch and ordered preparations for war.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_28-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-28">[26]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:167</sup> However, more years passed and his fear softened, and the watch was reduced again, as the Gondothlim became confident after Melko withdrew the spies.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_28-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-28">[26]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:170</sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/N/Natalie_Chen_-_Turgon_King_of_Gondolin.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/de/Natalie_Chen_-_Turgon_King_of_Gondolin.jpg/300px-Natalie_Chen_-_Turgon_King_of_Gondolin.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="378" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/de/Natalie_Chen_-_Turgon_King_of_Gondolin.jpg/450px-Natalie_Chen_-_Turgon_King_of_Gondolin.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/de/Natalie_Chen_-_Turgon_King_of_Gondolin.jpg/600px-Natalie_Chen_-_Turgon_King_of_Gondolin.jpg 2x" data-file-width="761" data-file-height="960"/></a><figcaption><i>Turgon King of Gondolin</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Natalie_Chen.md" title="Category:Images by Natalie Chen">Natalie Chen</a></figcaption></figure>

During the [Tarnin Austa](Tarnin_Austa.md), news came about the armies of Melko approaching the city, so Turgon called a council and all the [twelve houses of the Gondothlim](Twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim.md) came to his palace. Among them were his personal guard, the [House of the King](../H/House_of_the_King.md), who wore his emblems: the [sun](../S/Sun.md), the [moon](../M/Moon.md) and the [scarlet heart](../S/Scarlet_heart.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_28-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-28">[26]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:172</sup> In the council, all the lords of Gondolin supported Tuor, who wanted to leave the city to save all the people; but the king was convinced by Meglin and [Salgant](../S/Salgant.md) alone, because they appealed to the wealth and strength of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_28-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-28">[26]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:175–6</sup>

Thus, the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md) began. Turgon watched most of the battle from [his tower](Tower_of_the_King.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_28-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-28">[26]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:180</sup> while his folk was held in reserve in the Square of the Palace, waiting in order to defend him.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_28-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-28">[26]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:183</sup> Throughout the siege, the king sent the different reserved hosts to help where necessary, until the remnants of all the surviving houses finally gathered in the Square. After [Ecthelion of the Fountain](../E/Ecthelion_of_the_Fountain.md) there killed [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md>), the king came down in splendour and helped his house to cleanse the square. But the steam of the fountains killed some men, so the king was saved and taken under [Glingol](../G/Glingol.md) and [Bansil](../B/Bansil.md). Then said the king: "*Great is the fall of Gondolin!*", the words of the prophet [Amnon](../A/Amnon.md). Seeing he had brought ruin upon his city, he proclaimed he would not fight against [Doom](../D/Doom_of_the_Noldor~13942.md), so he ordered the survivors to follow Tuor and threw down his [crown](../C/Crown_of_the_Hidden_Kingdom.md) at the roots of Glingol. [Galdor](<../G/Galdor_(elf_of_Gondolin).md>) picked it up, but Turgon rejected it and climbed to the pinnacle of his tower.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_28-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-28">[26]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:184–5</sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>There he shouted in a voice like a horn blown among the mountains, and all that were gathered beneath the Trees and the foemen in the mists of the square heard him: &#34;Great is the victory of the Noldoli!&#34; And &#39;tis said that it was then middle night, and that the Orcs yelled in derision.
</p>
</blockquote>

Now, Tuor wanted to escape from the city to save the women and children, but he could not leave the king, so messengers were sent to convince Turgon to flee. But Turgon refused thrice, saying he would not leave his city and would burn with it. His royal house remained at his side, protecting the tower.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_28-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-28">[26]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:185</sup> Later, Tuor and Idril could see from the [Gar Ainion](../G/Gar_Ainion.md) how the Square of the King was taken by enemies. Dragons crushed the base of the tower and this fell, covered in flames. "Great was the clangour of that terrible fall, and therein passed Turgon King of the Gondothlim". Idril wept for her father, saying "*Sad is the blindness of the wise*"; and Tuor answered "*Sad too is the stubbornness of those we love — yet 'twas a valiant fault*".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_28-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-28">[26]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:187</sup>
