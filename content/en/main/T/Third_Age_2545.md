**Third Age 2545** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2545**) is the 2545th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2545 was also known as **S.R. 945** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Belecthor I](../B/Belecthor_I.md), son of [Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Húrin II](../H/Húrin_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Renewed attacks of the [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-2">[2]</a></sup> who falls in battle in [the Wold](The_Wold.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Brego](../B/Brego.md), son of [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md), becomes the second King of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-2">[2]</a></sup>
