**Third Age 1050** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1050**) is the 1050th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Hyarmendacil](../H/Hyarmendacil_I.md) conquers the [Harad](../H/Harad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) reaches the height of its power.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-1">[1]</a></sup>
- About this time a [shadow](../S/Shadow.md) falls on [Greenwood](../M/Mirkwood.md), and [men](../M/Men.md) begin to call it [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-1">[1]</a></sup>
- About this time [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s spirit moves some [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) into [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) up to the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Alerted by the shadow of [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the [Periannath](../P/Periannath.md) started to [migrate westwards](../W/Wandering_Days.md). They are first mentioned in records with the coming of the [Harfoots](../H/Harfoots.md) to [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-1">[1]</a></sup>
