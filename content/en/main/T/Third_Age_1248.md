**Third Age 1248** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1248**) is the 1248th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Regent](../R/Regent_of_Gondor.md) [Minalcar](../R/Rómendacil_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) - with the aid of [Vidugavia](../V/Vidugavia.md), King of [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) - leads a great force of troops and defeats the Easterlings in the lands between [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) and the [Inland Sea](../S/Sea_of_Rhûn.md). All [Easterling](../E/Easterlings.md) camps and settlements east of the Inland Sea are destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Minalcar takes the name [Rómendacil](../R/Rómendacil_II.md) (*East-victor*) in celebration of his great victory over the Easterlings.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- About this time, the western shores of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) are fortified and the pillars of the [Argonath](../A/Argonath.md) are erected at the entrance to [Nen Hithoel](../N/Nen_Hithoel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>
