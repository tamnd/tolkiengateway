<i><b>The Roots of the Mountains</b></i> is a novel by [William Morris](/wiki/William_Morris), which follows the Gothic theme from its predecessor, *[The House of the Wolfings](/wiki/The_House_of_the_Wolfings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) stated in a letter that he was inspired by *The Roots of the Mountains* in his depiction of the [Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes) and the approaches to the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon) in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It has also been suggested that "almost certainly J.R.R. Tolkien remembered *The Roots of the Mountains* when he created [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum)".<sup id="cite_ref-TS_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
