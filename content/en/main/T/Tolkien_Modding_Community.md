The **Tolkien Modding Community** is a large online community of indepedent mod developers and modding teams, on the modding database and mod promotion website <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ModDB.com" title="wikipedia:ModDB.com">ModDB.com</a>. The community focuses on Tolkien-themed mods, addons and total conversions for a variety of video game titles across a plethora of game genres and game publishers.
