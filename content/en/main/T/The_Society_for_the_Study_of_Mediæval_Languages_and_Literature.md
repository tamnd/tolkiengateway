**The Society for the Study of Mediæval Languages and Literature** is a society that exists for the encouragement of reasearch in medieval languages and literature and for the publication of results of that research (usually in its journal, *Medium Ævum*). Originally called the *Arthurian Society*, it was based at the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md). During his tenure as Merton Professor of the English language, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) served as the president of the Society, and he was also on the editorial board of *Medium Ævum* (along with <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Talbut_Onions" title="wikipedia:Charles Talbut Onions">C.T. Onions</a> and [J.A.W. Bennett](../J/J.A.W._Bennett.md)). [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md) printer <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Basil_Blackwell&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Basil Blackwell (page does not exist)">Basil Blackwell</a> served as the Society's treasurer for some time during the early 1950's, and all publications were printed at his facilities.

For the Society, Tolkien produced *[Sigelwara Land](../S/Sigelwara_Land.md)* in two parts, one in [1932](../_/1932.md) and one in [1935](../_/1935.md).
