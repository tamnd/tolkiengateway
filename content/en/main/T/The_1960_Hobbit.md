**The 1960 Hobbit**, or **Fifth Phase**, is a chapter in *[The History of The Hobbit](The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)* about an abandoned attempt by Tolkien to reconcile the differences between *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, including discrepancies in chronology, geography, and style, which resulted from *The Hobbit* having been written years before *The Lord of the Rings*, and in a much more whimsical tone.<sup id="cite_ref-History_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-History-1">[1]</a></sup>

The existence of the rewrite was not known to the general public for a long time, aside from [Humphrey Carpenter](../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md)'s *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_A_Biography.md)* referring to "some revision notes" that Tolkien had wished to use to help him in writing the third edition of *The Hobbit* (1966) but had been unable to find. It was first brought to light when [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) read a portion of it at the 1987 Marquette Tolkien Conference ([Mythcon 18](../M/Mythcon_18.md)), and at last became available to the public when it was published in [John D. Rateliff](../J/John_D._Rateliff.md)'s *[The History of The Hobbit](The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-History_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-History-1">[1]</a></sup>

The 1960 rewrite was a typescript consisting of several updated passages intended to replace corresponding portions of the already-published second edition. Tolkien did not complete the project, however, abandoning the rewrite just before Thorin and Company reach Rivendell.<sup id="cite_ref-History_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-History-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
