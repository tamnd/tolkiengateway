The **Timeless Halls** are the dwelling of [Eru](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar "Ilúvatar"), as distinct from the [Void](/wiki/Void). They have existed before and outside the boundaries of the [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4), the created universe, and may or may not have a physical form. Although without limit, they are said to stretch from the [Abyss](/wiki/Abyss) to the [Firmament](/wiki/Firmament). It is where the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) [were created](/wiki/Creation_of_the_Ainur "Creation of the Ainur") and then sang the [Song of the Ainur](/wiki/Song_of_the_Ainur).

## Other uses {#Other_uses}

In [Bilbo's](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") *[Eärendillinwë](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendillinw%C3%AB)*, the [halls](/wiki/Ilmarin "Ilmarin") of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) and [Varda](/wiki/Varda) are referred to as "The Timeless Halls."

## Outside the legendarium {#Outside_the_legendarium}

In *[Átaremma](/wiki/%C3%81taremma)*, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s translation of the Lord's Prayer, he renders "in Heaven" as *han Eä*, lit. "beyond Creation \[the Material Universe\]"; this expression replaces the more literal Quenya *[menel](/wiki/Menel "Menel")* "heaven" which implies the physical sky.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
