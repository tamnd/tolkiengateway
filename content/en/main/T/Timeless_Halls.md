The **Timeless Halls** are the dwelling of [Eru](../I/Ilúvatar.md), as distinct from the [Void](../V/Void.md). They have existed before and outside the boundaries of the [Eä](../E/Eä.md), the created universe, and may or may not have a physical form. Although without limit, they are said to stretch from the [Abyss](../A/Abyss.md) to the [Firmament](../F/Firmament.md). It is where the [Ainur](../A/Ainur.md) [were created](../C/Creation_of_the_Ainur.md) and then sang the [Song of the Ainur](../S/Song_of_the_Ainur.md).

## Other uses {#Other_uses}

In [Bilbo's](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) *[Eärendillinwë](../E/Eärendillinwë.md)*, the [halls](../I/Ilmarin.md) of [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) and [Varda](../V/Varda.md) are referred to as "The Timeless Halls."

## Outside the legendarium {#Outside_the_legendarium}

In *[Átaremma](../_/Átaremma.md)*, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s translation of the Lord's Prayer, he renders "in Heaven" as *han Eä*, lit. "beyond Creation \[the Material Universe\]"; this expression replaces the more literal Quenya *[menel](../M/Menel.md)* "heaven" which implies the physical sky.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
