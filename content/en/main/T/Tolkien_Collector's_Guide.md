**Tolkien Collector's Guide**, also known as TolkienGuide.com is a website devoted to the collecting of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related materials, primarily books and fanzines. It was the winner of the [2019 Tolkien Society award for Best Website](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society_Awards "The Tolkien Society Awards").
