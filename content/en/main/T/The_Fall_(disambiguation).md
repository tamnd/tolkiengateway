In general, the term "**The Fall**" is used as an abbreviation for the following events:

- The [Fall of Barad-dûr](../D/Downfall_of_Barad-dûr.md) ([T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md)), the ruin of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s fortress due to the destruction of the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md).
- The [Fall of Dol Guldur](../F/Fall_of_Dol_Guldur.md) ([T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md)), a battle in the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) which lead to the collapse of [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md).
- The [Fall of the Falas](../F/Fall_of_the_Falas.md) ([F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [473](../F/First_Age_473.md)), the destruction of [Falas](../F/Falas.md) by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s armies.
- The [Fall of Fingolfin](../F/Fall_of_Fingolfin.md) ([F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [455](../F/First_Age_455.md)), the death of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md), [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md), in single combat with [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).
- *[The Fall of Gil-galad](The_Fall_of_Gil-galad.md)*, a lay sung by [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* which tells of the death of [Ereinion Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) ([S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3441](../S/Second_Age_3441.md)).
- The [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), ([F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [510](../F/First_Age_510.md)), the destruction of the last Noldorin realm in Beleriand.
- The <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Fall_of_H%C3%BArin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fall of Húrin (page does not exist)">Fall of Húrin</a>, ([F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [472](../F/First_Age_472.md)), the capture of [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) during the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md).
- The [Fall of Nargothrond](../F/Fall_of_Nargothrond.md) ([F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [494](../F/First_Age_494.md)), the defeat of the city of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) by forces led by [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md).
- The [Fall of the Noldor](../F/Fall_of_the_Noldor.md) ([Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1495](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1495.md)), a name given to the event where the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) left [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) and committed the [Kinslaying](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md); it was made into a song by [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md), known as the *[Noldolantë](../N/Noldolantë.md)*.

It is occasionally used for one of the following:

- The destruction of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) ([F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [587](../F/First_Age_587.md)), in which most of the lands sunk beneath the sea after the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md).
- The [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) ([S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3319](../S/Second_Age_3319.md)), the destruction of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) by the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), told in the *[Akallabêth](../A/Akallabêth.md)*.
- The Fall of Sauron ([T.A.](Third_Age.md) [3019](Third_Age_3019.md)), the [death of Sauron](../S/Sauron.md#Third_Age) or the [Fall of Barad-dûr](../D/Downfall_of_Barad-dûr.md).
