In general, the term "**The Fall**" is used as an abbreviation for the following events:

- The [Fall of Barad-dûr](/wiki/Downfall_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr "Downfall of Barad-dûr") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")), the ruin of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s fortress due to the destruction of the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring).
- The [Fall of Dol Guldur](/wiki/Fall_of_Dol_Guldur) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")), a battle in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) which lead to the collapse of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur).
- The [Fall of the Falas](/wiki/Fall_of_the_Falas) ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [473](/wiki/First_Age_473 "First Age 473")), the destruction of [Falas](/wiki/Falas) by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s armies.
- The [Fall of Fingolfin](/wiki/Fall_of_Fingolfin) ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455")), the death of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor), in single combat with [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).
- *[The Fall of Gil-galad](/wiki/The_Fall_of_Gil-galad)*, a lay sung by [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* which tells of the death of [Ereinion Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad "Gil-galad") ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3441](/wiki/Second_Age_3441 "Second Age 3441")).
- The [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [510](/wiki/First_Age_510 "First Age 510")), the destruction of the last Noldorin realm in Beleriand.
- The <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Fall_of_H%C3%BArin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fall of Húrin (page does not exist)">Fall of Húrin</a>, ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [472](/wiki/First_Age_472 "First Age 472")), the capture of [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) during the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad).
- The [Fall of Nargothrond](/wiki/Fall_of_Nargothrond) ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [494](/wiki/First_Age_494 "First Age 494")), the defeat of the city of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) by forces led by [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung).
- The [Fall of the Noldor](/wiki/Fall_of_the_Noldor) ([Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1495](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1495 "Year of the Trees 1495")), a name given to the event where the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) left [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) and committed the [Kinslaying](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB "Kinslaying at Alqualondë"); it was made into a song by [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor), known as the *[Noldolantë](/wiki/Noldolant%C3%AB)*.

It is occasionally used for one of the following:

- The destruction of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [587](/wiki/First_Age_587 "First Age 587")), in which most of the lands sunk beneath the sea after the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath).
- The [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor) ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3319](/wiki/Second_Age_3319 "Second Age 3319")), the destruction of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), told in the *[Akallabêth](/wiki/Akallab%C3%AAth)*.
- The Fall of Sauron ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")), the [death of Sauron](/wiki/Sauron#Third_Age "Sauron") or the [Fall of Barad-dûr](/wiki/Downfall_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr "Downfall of Barad-dûr").
