**Third Age 109** (abbreviated to **T.A. 109**) is the 109th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) weds [Celebrían](../C/Celebrían.md), daughter of [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup>
