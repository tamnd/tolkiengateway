The **[Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Society)** grants every year five **Awards** as a way to recognise excellence in [Tolkien studies](/wiki/Tolkien_studies), art and [fandom](/wiki/Fandom "Fandom"). Winners are announced during the Annual Dinner in the Annual General Meeting of the Society.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> If any retribution is granted, it is not indicated.

Every year, the members of the Tolkien Society can propose nominees and vote for them in four categories:

- Best Artwork
- Best Article
- Best Book
- Best Online Content

The Outstanding Contribution Award is selected by the Trustees.
