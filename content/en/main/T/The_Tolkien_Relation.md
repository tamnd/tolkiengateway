<i><b>The Tolkien Relation: A Personal Inquiry</b></i> is a book by [William Ready](../W/William_Ready.md), the former Director of Libraries at [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md). The paperback edition of [1969](../_/1969.md) was renamed into <b>Understanding Tolkien and <i>The Lord of the Rings</i></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ake_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ake-1">[1]</a></sup>

The book includes extracts from letters to William Ready from both [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and his son [Christopher](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), as well as some unattributed quotes. See especially pp. 55–56 and 57-60 (1st edition).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

1. The Early Cycle
2. The Inklings and the Myth
3. The Days of the Dons
4. The Hobbits of Tolkien
5. The Tale of the Ring
6. Craft and Art
7. Man for Tolkien
8. The Once and the Future
9. As It Was and Will Be
10. Epilogue
