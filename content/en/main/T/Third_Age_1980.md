**Third Age 1980** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1980**) is the 1980th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1980 was also known as **S.R. 380** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) comes to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) and there gathers the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup>
- A [Balrog](../D/Durin's_Bane.md) appears in [Moria](../M/Moria.md), and slays [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Durin VI](../D/Durin_VI.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Náin I](../N/Náin_I.md), son of Durin VI, becomes the King of [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
