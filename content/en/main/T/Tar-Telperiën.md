**Tar-Telperiën** was the tenth monarch of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) and its second [Ruling Queen](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Ruling Queen of Númenor"). She was the eldest child of [Tar-Súrion](/wiki/Tar-S%C3%BArion).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Telperien_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Telperien-1">[1]</a></sup>

During her rule (S.A. 1556 - S.A. 1731), the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power) were forged ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1600](/wiki/Second_Age_1600 "Second Age 1600"))<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>, and the [War of the Elves and Sauron](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron) began ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1693](/wiki/Second_Age_1693 "Second Age 1693"))<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>. When [King Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad "Gil-galad") called on Númenor for aid against Sauron in [S.A. 1695](/wiki/Second_Age_1695 "Second Age 1695"), a great navy was sent forth under the admiral [Ciryatur's](/wiki/Ciryatur "Ciryatur") command<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>. It was delayed in reaching [Middle Earth](/wiki/Middle-earth "Middle-earth") until [S.A. 1700](/wiki/Second_Age_1700 "Second Age 1700"), but reinforced Gil-galad's imperiled forces at the river [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) and was key to the subsequent rout of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s forces at the [Battle of the Gwathló](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Gwathl%C3%B3)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>.

Tar-Telperiën "would wed with no man" and was long-lived even for one of [Elros](/wiki/Elros)' line<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Telperien_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Telperien-1">[1]</a></sup>. She died within a year of giving up the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre) to her nephew and successor [Tar-Minastir](/wiki/Tar-Minastir), the son of her younger brother [Isilmo](/wiki/Isilmo)<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Telperien_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Telperien-1">[1]</a></sup>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Telperien* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it could mean "Silver-daughter" as a compound of *telpë* ("silver") and the feminine suffix *[-ien](/wiki/-ien)* ("-daughter"); though it could also be a feminised form of [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion), the name of the White Tree of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Like all the rulers of Númenor who took their royal names in Quenya, Telperien added the prefix *tar-* ("high") to her name when she received the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Sceptre of Númenor").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2024: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Tar-Telperiën ordered the construction of a massive wall that completely encircled the cape of <a href="/wiki/Umbar" title="Umbar">Umbar</a> (at the time called <i>Vëalondë</i> by Numenorians) to protect it from the peoples of <a href="/wiki/Harad" title="Harad">Harad</a> to the East. &#34;Telperiën&#39;s Wall&#34; would mark the furthermost extent of the realms of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Numenor" title="Numenor">Numenor</a> and later <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> in <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Haradwaith" title="Haradwaith">Haradwaith</a>, though the passage of millennia eventually made it useless as defensive structure.</dd></dl>
