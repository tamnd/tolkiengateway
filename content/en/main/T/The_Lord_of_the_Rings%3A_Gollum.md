<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: Gollum</b></i> is a video game for PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Microsoft Windows, Nintendo Switch, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X/S, featuring [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) as the main protagonist. It is set between the events of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, during the time in which Gollum is searching for the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring). The game was originally scheduled for release in [1 September](/wiki/1_September) [2022](/wiki/2022),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> but on [25 July](/wiki/25_July) 2022, it was announced that there would be a further delay of "a few months".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was later announced on [24 March](/wiki/24_March) [2023](/wiki/2023) that the game would release on [25 May](/wiki/25_May), with the Nintendo Switch version releasing later in the year.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

A special edition called the **Precious Edition** was announced on [14 April](/wiki/14_April) 2023. The contents include the full game, an <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Art_Exhibition&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Art Exhibition (page does not exist)">Art Exhibition</a> application, the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lore_Compendium&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lore Compendium (page does not exist)">Lore Compendium</a> DLC, a 45 minute <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Gollum_(Original_Soundtrack)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Lord of the Rings: Gollum (Original Soundtrack) (page does not exist)">original soundtrack</a>, and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) voice acting. Early access to the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Emotes_Pack&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Emotes Pack (page does not exist)">Emotes Pack</a> DLC is also included as a pre-order bonus.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Plot {#Plot}

The game explores a never-before-told story about [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum), and is set eight years before the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

After Gollum is tortured in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), he is enslaved to work in the mines of the [Ephel Duath](/wiki/Ephel_Duath) on the edge of Mordor. Here Gollum fulfils his tasks, and sometimes stealthily causes some mischief.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Upon escaping enslavement, Gollum enters the tunnels of [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

The game cuts to the [Elvenking's Halls](/wiki/Elvenking%27s_Halls) upon fleeing Shelob, where Gollum is brought before [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil). Gollum is granted some freedoms, and befriends an [Elf](/wiki/Elf) named [Mell](</wiki/Mell_(Gollum_Game)> "Mell (Gollum Game)"). She helps him find <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gwendil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gwendil (page does not exist)">Gwendil</a>, the Riddlemaster of the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm) and a relative of the Elvenking. Depending on whether or not he is killed, Gollum either escapes [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) or is released by the Riddlemaster and Mell, and the game ends with a cutscene in the [Mines of Moria](/wiki/Mines_of_Moria).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Gameplay {#Gameplay}

The game was designed with a focus on stealth,<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> platforming,<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum)'s split personality.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Reception {#Reception}

The game has received overwhelmingly negative feedback, with some calling it the worst game of the year.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> Headlines include *boil it, mash it, stick it in the bin*,<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> referencing [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee")'s line in the film, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*. On Metacritic, it got a Metascore of 39/100, and a user score of 2.0/10 as of [5 July](/wiki/5_July) 2023.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> <span></span>
