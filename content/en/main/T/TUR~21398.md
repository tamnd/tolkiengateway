**TUR** is an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) signifying "power, control, mastery, victory", to "dominate, master, conquer" or to be "strong, mighty, in power".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

This root has been affected by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=TA%C6%B7&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TAƷ (page does not exist)">TAƷ</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
