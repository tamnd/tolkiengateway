**Tales and Songs of Bimble Bay** are a series of poems written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). Although [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter) wrote that they were written in [1922](/wiki/1922),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) and [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) place the time of composition as c. [1928](/wiki/1928).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The poems tell stories about an imaginary English coastal town and harbour, Bimble Bay.

## List of poems {#List_of_poems}

- "[The Bumpus](/wiki/The_Bumpus)" (revised as "[Perry-the-Winkle](/wiki/Perry-the-Winkle)")
- "[The Dragon's Visit](/wiki/The_Dragon%27s_Visit)"
- "[Glip](/wiki/Glip)"
- "[Old Grabbler](/wiki/Old_Grabbler)"
- "[Progress in Bimble Town](/wiki/Progress_in_Bimble_Town)"
- "[A Song of Bimble Bay](/wiki/A_Song_of_Bimble_Bay)"
