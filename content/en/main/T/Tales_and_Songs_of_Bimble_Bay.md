**Tales and Songs of Bimble Bay** are a series of poems written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). Although [Humphrey Carpenter](../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md) wrote that they were written in [1922](../_/1922.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) and [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) place the time of composition as c. [1928](../_/1928.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The poems tell stories about an imaginary English coastal town and harbour, Bimble Bay.

## List of poems {#List_of_poems}

- "[The Bumpus](The_Bumpus.md)" (revised as "[Perry-the-Winkle](../P/Perry-the-Winkle.md)")
- "[The Dragon's Visit](The_Dragon's_Visit.md)"
- "[Glip](../G/Glip.md)"
- "[Old Grabbler](../O/Old_Grabbler.md)"
- "[Progress in Bimble Town](../P/Progress_in_Bimble_Town.md)"
- "[A Song of Bimble Bay](../A/A_Song_of_Bimble_Bay.md)"
