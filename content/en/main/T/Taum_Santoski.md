**Taum J.R. Santoski** ([November 2](../_/2_November.md), [1958](../_/1958.md) - [August 19](../_/19_August.md), [1991](../_/1991.md), aged 32) was a prominent member of the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](../E/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship.md). He assisted [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) with the *[History of Middle-earth](The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* series,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and planned to do a similar book on the writing process of *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* himself, but died of cancer before he could finish it. [The work](The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md) was continued and published by [John D. Rateliff](../J/John_D._Rateliff.md).

## Dedications {#Dedications}

- [*Vinyar Tengwar* 19](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_19.md) was dedicated to his memory, and featured an editiorial *In Memoriam* by [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md), in which his perseverance, generosity and enthuisiasm were praised.
- [*Vinyar Tengwar* 24](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_24.md) was dedicated to him in recognition on his work on [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) and in assisting Christopher Tolkien with the *History of Middle-earth*.
- *[Sauron Defeated](../S/Sauron_Defeated.md)*, Volume IX of *[The History of Middle-earth](The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* was dedicated to his memory by Christopher Tolkien in recognition of his assistance with *The History of Middle-earth* series.
