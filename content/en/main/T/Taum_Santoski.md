**Taum J.R. Santoski** ([November 2](/wiki/2_November "2 November"), [1958](/wiki/1958) - [August 19](/wiki/19_August "19 August"), [1991](/wiki/1991), aged 32) was a prominent member of the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](/wiki/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship). He assisted [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) with the *[History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth "The History of Middle-earth")* series,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and planned to do a similar book on the writing process of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* himself, but died of cancer before he could finish it. [The work](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit "The History of The Hobbit") was continued and published by [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff).

## Dedications {#Dedications}

- [*Vinyar Tengwar* 19](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_19 "Vinyar Tengwar 19") was dedicated to his memory, and featured an editiorial *In Memoriam* by [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne), in which his perseverance, generosity and enthuisiasm were praised.
- [*Vinyar Tengwar* 24](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_24 "Vinyar Tengwar 24") was dedicated to him in recognition on his work on [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) and in assisting Christopher Tolkien with the *History of Middle-earth*.
- *[Sauron Defeated](/wiki/Sauron_Defeated)*, Volume IX of *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* was dedicated to his memory by Christopher Tolkien in recognition of his assistance with *The History of Middle-earth* series.
