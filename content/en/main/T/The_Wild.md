**The Wild** was an untamed, dangerous region of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its boundaries are somewhat indefinite, but it seems to have been approximately equivalent to the region of [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion), although in [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' memoirs a map showed the [Edge of the Wild](/wiki/Edge_of_the_Wild) west of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
