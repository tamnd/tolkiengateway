**The Wild** was an untamed, dangerous region of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its boundaries are somewhat indefinite, but it seems to have been approximately equivalent to the region of [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md), although in [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)' memoirs a map showed the [Edge of the Wild](../E/Edge_of_the_Wild.md) west of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
