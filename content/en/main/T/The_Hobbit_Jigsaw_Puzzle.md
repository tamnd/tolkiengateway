**The Hobbit Jigsaw Puzzle** was a series of 200-piece jigsaw puzzles featuring snapshot images from the [1977 film *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)") produced by [Rankin/Bass](/wiki/Rankin/Bass).

The boxes measure 13" x 8" x 1 3/4", and the finished puzzles would measure 11" x 17".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
