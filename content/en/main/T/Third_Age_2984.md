**Third Age 2984** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2984**) is the 2984th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2984 was also known as **S.R. 1384** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Rose Cotton](../R/Rose_Cotton.md), second child and only daughter of [Tolman Cotton Senior](Tolman_Cotton_Senior.md) and [Lily Brown](../L/Lily_Brown.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Wilcome Cotton](../W/Wilcome_Cotton_II.md), third child and second son of [Tolman Cotton Senior](Tolman_Cotton_Senior.md) and [Lily Brown](../L/Lily_Brown.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Ecthelion II](../E/Ecthelion_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Denethor II](../D/Denethor.md), son of Ecthelion II, becomes the twenty-sixth Ruling Steward.<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Denethor II begins to use the [Anor-stone](../A/Anor-stone.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Death of [Hobson Gamgee](../H/Hobson_Gamgee.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-1">[1]</a></sup>
