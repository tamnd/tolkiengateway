**Toy Biz** (currently known as **Marvel Toys**) is the toy division of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Entertainment" title="wikipedia:Marvel Entertainment">Marvel Entertainment</a>. They produced licensed action figures for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").
