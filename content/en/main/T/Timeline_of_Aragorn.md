A timeline of the life of [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn_II) in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

## [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) {#Third_Age}

### [2931](/wiki/Third_Age_2931 "Third Age 2931") {#2931}

- [1 March](/wiki/1_March): Birth of Aragorn II.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [2933](/wiki/Third_Age_2933 "Third Age 2933") {#2933}

- Death of Aragorn's father [Arathorn II](/wiki/Arathorn_II).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Aragorn's mother [Gilraen](/wiki/Gilraen) takes him to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) where [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) accepts him as a foster-son and calls him [Estel](/wiki/Estel) meaning "hope."<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") {#2941}

- June: [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) arrive at Rivendell.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> It is unknown whether they interact with 10-year old Aragorn.

### [2952](/wiki/Third_Age_2952 "Third Age 2952") {#2952}

- Aragorn learns his true name and ancestry from Elrond. He is given the shards of [Narsil](/wiki/Narsil).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Aragorn meets [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) at woods of Rivendell.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Aragorn embarks into the wild on many adventures.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [2956](/wiki/Third_Age_2956 "Third Age 2956") {#2956}

- Aragorn meets [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [2957](/wiki/Third_Age_2957 "Third Age 2957")-[2980](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980") {#2957-2980}

- Aragorn journeys throughout [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Aragorn serves King [Thengel](/wiki/Thengel) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) and [Ecthelion II](/wiki/Ecthelion_II), [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor), going by the name [Thorongil](/wiki/Thorongil).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [2980](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980") {#2980}

- Aragorn defeats the [Corsairs](/wiki/Corsairs) while in the service of Ecthelion.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Aragorn faces perils on the confines of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) before turning homeward.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Aragorn meets Arwen in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Midsummer's Eve: Aragorn and Arwen become betrothed.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001") {#3001}

- Aragorn begins the search for [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) at the request of Gandalf.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [3007](/wiki/Third_Age_3007 "Third Age 3007") {#3007}

- Spring: Death of Aragorn's mother Gilraen.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [3009](/wiki/Third_Age_3009 "Third Age 3009") {#3009}

- Aragorn and Gandalf renew their hunt for [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [3017](/wiki/Third_Age_3017 "Third Age 3017") {#3017}

- [1 February](/wiki/1_February): Aragorn captures Gollum in the [Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [21 March](/wiki/21_March): Aragorn delivers Gollum to King [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) of the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm) to be held captive.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

### [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") {#3018}

- [1 May](/wiki/1_May): Aragorn meets Gandalf at [Sarn Ford](/wiki/Sarn_Ford) and learns of [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins")'s plan to leave [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") in September.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [29 September](/wiki/29_September): Aragorn meets Frodo Baggins in [Bree](/wiki/Bree).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [30 September](/wiki/30_September): Aragorn leads the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) out of Bree.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [6 October](/wiki/6_October): Five [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) attack the camp at [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [13 October](/wiki/13_October): Aragorn finds a token left by [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) on the [Last Bridge](/wiki/Last_Bridge).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [20 October](/wiki/20_October): Frodo escapes across the [Ford of Bruinen](/wiki/Ford_of_Bruinen) to Rivendell; Aragorn and the others follow.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [25 October](/wiki/25_October): The [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond); Aragorn shows the shards of Narsil and declares his intention to go to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [18 December](/wiki/18_December): Aragorn joins the [Company of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring").<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [25 December](/wiki/25_December): The [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") leaves Rivendell.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

### [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") {#3019}

- [11](/wiki/11_January "11 January")-[12 January](/wiki/12_January): Blizzard on [Caradhras](/wiki/Caradhras).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [13 January](/wiki/13_January): The Fellowship enters [Moria](/wiki/Moria) against Aragorn's advice.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [15 January](/wiki/15_January): Gandalf falls facing the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs"); Aragorn leads the Fellowship out of Moria to the edge of Lothlorien.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [17 January](/wiki/17_January): The Fellowship are greeted by [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [16 February](/wiki/16_February): The Fellowship leaves Lothlorien; Aragorn receives [Andúril's sheath](/wiki/And%C3%BAril%27s_sheath).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [25 February](/wiki/25_February): The Fellowship passes the [Argonath](/wiki/Argonath).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [26 February](/wiki/26_February): The Breaking of the Fellowship. Aragorn decides to follow the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) who captured [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took").<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [30 February](/wiki/30_February): The [Three Hunters](/wiki/Three_Hunters) meet [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) and then travel to the edge of [Fangorn](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest "Fangorn Forest").<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [1 March](/wiki/1_March): The Three Hunters are reunited with Gandalf and set out for [Meduseld](/wiki/Meduseld).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [2 March](/wiki/2_March): Gandalf and the Three Hunters arrive at Meduseld.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [3 March](/wiki/3_March): The [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) retreat to [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep) and the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [4 March](/wiki/4_March): Aragorn rides out at dawn with King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden). The [Huorns](/wiki/Huorns) of [Fangorn](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest "Fangorn Forest") and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) arrive and the battle is won.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [5 March](/wiki/5_March): The Three Hunters are reunited with Merry and Pippin at [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard). Aragorn takes possession of the *palantir* of Orthanc.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [6 March](/wiki/6_March): Aragorn is met by the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) and Elrond's sons. He reveals himself to [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in the *[palantir](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* and decides to take the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [7 March](/wiki/7_March): The [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) arrives at [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow). Aragorn takes leave of [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [8 March](/wiki/8_March): The Grey Company takes the Paths of the Dead. At midnight, Aragorn summons the [Oathbreakers](/wiki/Oathbreakers) at the [Stone of Erech](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [9 March](/wiki/9_March): Aragorn sets out from [Erech](/wiki/Erech) and comes to [Calembel](/wiki/Calembel).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [10 March](/wiki/10_March): Aragorn, leading the Dead Men and the Grey Company, crosses the river [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [11 March](/wiki/11_March): Aragorn reaches [Linhir](/wiki/Linhir) and crosses into [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [12 March](/wiki/12_March): Aragorn drives the enemy towards Pelargir.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [13 March](/wiki/13_March): The Grey Company reaches [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) with the army of the Dead and they capture the Corsairs' fleet.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [14 March](/wiki/14_March): Aragorn sails towards Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [15 March](/wiki/15_March): Aragorn sails north and arrives at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields). After the battle he heals [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), Éowyn and Merry.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [16 March](/wiki/16_March): The Captains of the West decide to march to the Black Gate.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [18 March](/wiki/18_March): Aragorn leads the army of the West out of Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [23 March](/wiki/23_March): Aragorn sends those too frightened to proceed to retake [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [25 March](/wiki/25_March): Aragorn commands the army of the West in the [Battle of the Morannon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon). The Ring is destroyed and Sauron is defeated.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [8 April](/wiki/8_April): The [Ring-bearers](/wiki/Ring-bearers) are honored by Aragorn and the Host of the West at the [Field of Cormallen](/wiki/Field_of_Cormallen).<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-4">[4]</a></sup>

- [1 May](/wiki/1_May): Aragorn is crowned King.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-4">[4]</a></sup>

- [25 June](/wiki/25_June): Aragorn finds the sapling of the [White Tree](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor "White Tree of Gondor").<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Midsummer's Eve: Arwen arrives in Minas Tirith. Aragorn receives the [Sceptre of Annúminas](/wiki/Sceptre_of_Ann%C3%BAminas) signifying the Kingship of Arnor from Elrond.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Midsummer's Day](/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day "Mid-year's Day"): Wedding of Aragorn and Arwen.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-4">[4]</a></sup>

- [22 August](/wiki/22_August): Aragorn bids farewell to the other members of the Fellowship.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Fourth Age {#Fourth_Age}

### [6](/wiki/Fourth_Age_6 "Fourth Age 6") {#6}

- King Elessar makes the Shire a Free Land under the protection of the [Northern Sceptre](/wiki/Northern_Sceptre) and forbids Men from entering it.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-5">[5]</a></sup>

### [13](/wiki/Fourth_Age_13 "Fourth Age 13") {#13}

- King Elessar makes the Thain, the [Master of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland), and the [Mayor of Michel Delving](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving) Counsellors of the North-kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-5">[5]</a></sup>

### [15](/wiki/Fourth_Age_15 "Fourth Age 15") {#15}

- King Elessar and Queen Arwen come north to dwell at Annuminas. They meet with Pippin, Merry and Sam at the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-5">[5]</a></sup>

### [31](/wiki/Fourth_Age_31 "Fourth Age 31") {#31}

- King Elessar adds the [Westmarch](/wiki/Westmarch) to the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-5">[5]</a></sup>

### [120](/wiki/Fourth_Age_120 "Fourth Age 120") {#120}

- [1 March](/wiki/1_March): Death of King Elessar. He is succeeded by his son Eldarion.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-5">[5]</a></sup>
