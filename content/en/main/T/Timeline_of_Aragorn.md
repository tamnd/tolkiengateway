A timeline of the life of [Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn_II.md) in the [Third Age](Third_Age.md).

## [Third Age](Third_Age.md) {#Third_Age}

### [2931](Third_Age_2931.md) {#2931}

- [1 March](../_/1_March.md): Birth of Aragorn II.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [2933](Third_Age_2933.md) {#2933}

- Death of Aragorn's father [Arathorn II](../A/Arathorn_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Aragorn's mother [Gilraen](../G/Gilraen.md) takes him to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) where [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) accepts him as a foster-son and calls him [Estel](../E/Estel.md) meaning "hope."<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [2941](Third_Age_2941.md) {#2941}

- June: [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and [Thorin and Company](Thorin_and_Company.md) arrive at Rivendell.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> It is unknown whether they interact with 10-year old Aragorn.

### [2952](Third_Age_2952.md) {#2952}

- Aragorn learns his true name and ancestry from Elrond. He is given the shards of [Narsil](../N/Narsil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Aragorn meets [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) at woods of Rivendell.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Aragorn embarks into the wild on many adventures.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [2956](Third_Age_2956.md) {#2956}

- Aragorn meets [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [2957](Third_Age_2957.md)-[2980](Third_Age_2980.md) {#2957-2980}

- Aragorn journeys throughout [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Aragorn serves King [Thengel](Thengel.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) and [Ecthelion II](../E/Ecthelion_II.md), [Steward of Gondor](../S/Steward_of_Gondor.md), going by the name [Thorongil](Thorongil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [2980](Third_Age_2980.md) {#2980}

- Aragorn defeats the [Corsairs](../C/Corsairs.md) while in the service of Ecthelion.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Aragorn faces perils on the confines of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) before turning homeward.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Aragorn meets Arwen in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Midsummer's Eve: Aragorn and Arwen become betrothed.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [3001](Third_Age_3001.md) {#3001}

- Aragorn begins the search for [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) at the request of Gandalf.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [3007](Third_Age_3007.md) {#3007}

- Spring: Death of Aragorn's mother Gilraen.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [3009](Third_Age_3009.md) {#3009}

- Aragorn and Gandalf renew their hunt for [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

### [3017](Third_Age_3017.md) {#3017}

- [1 February](../_/1_February.md): Aragorn captures Gollum in the [Dead Marshes](../D/Dead_Marshes.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [21 March](../_/21_March.md): Aragorn delivers Gollum to King [Thranduil](Thranduil.md) of the [Woodland Realm](../W/Woodland_Realm.md) to be held captive.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

### [3018](Third_Age_3018.md) {#3018}

- [1 May](../_/1_May.md): Aragorn meets Gandalf at [Sarn Ford](../S/Sarn_Ford.md) and learns of [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)'s plan to leave [the Shire](The_Shire.md) in September.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [29 September](../_/29_September.md): Aragorn meets Frodo Baggins in [Bree](../B/Bree.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [30 September](../_/30_September.md): Aragorn leads the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) out of Bree.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [6 October](../_/6_October.md): Five [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) attack the camp at [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [13 October](../_/13_October.md): Aragorn finds a token left by [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) on the [Last Bridge](../L/Last_Bridge.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [20 October](../_/20_October.md): Frodo escapes across the [Ford of Bruinen](../F/Ford_of_Bruinen.md) to Rivendell; Aragorn and the others follow.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [25 October](../_/25_October.md): The [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md); Aragorn shows the shards of Narsil and declares his intention to go to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [18 December](../_/18_December.md): Aragorn joins the [Company of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [25 December](../_/25_December.md): The [Fellowship](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) leaves Rivendell.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

### [3019](Third_Age_3019.md) {#3019}

- [11](../_/11_January.md)-[12 January](../_/12_January.md): Blizzard on [Caradhras](../C/Caradhras.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [13 January](../_/13_January.md): The Fellowship enters [Moria](../M/Moria.md) against Aragorn's advice.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [15 January](../_/15_January.md): Gandalf falls facing the [Balrog](../B/Balrogs.md); Aragorn leads the Fellowship out of Moria to the edge of Lothlorien.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [17 January](../_/17_January.md): The Fellowship are greeted by [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [16 February](../_/16_February.md): The Fellowship leaves Lothlorien; Aragorn receives [Andúril's sheath](../A/Andúril's_sheath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [25 February](../_/25_February.md): The Fellowship passes the [Argonath](../A/Argonath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [26 February](../_/26_February.md): The Breaking of the Fellowship. Aragorn decides to follow the [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) who captured [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [30 February](../_/30_February.md): The [Three Hunters](Three_Hunters.md) meet [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) and then travel to the edge of [Fangorn](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [1 March](../_/1_March.md): The Three Hunters are reunited with Gandalf and set out for [Meduseld](../M/Meduseld.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [2 March](../_/2_March.md): Gandalf and the Three Hunters arrive at Meduseld.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [3 March](../_/3_March.md): The [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) retreat to [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md) and the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [4 March](../_/4_March.md): Aragorn rides out at dawn with King [Théoden](Théoden.md). The [Huorns](../H/Huorns.md) of [Fangorn](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md) and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) arrive and the battle is won.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [5 March](../_/5_March.md): The Three Hunters are reunited with Merry and Pippin at [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md). Aragorn takes possession of the *palantir* of Orthanc.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [6 March](../_/6_March.md): Aragorn is met by the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) and Elrond's sons. He reveals himself to [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) in the *[palantir](../P/Palantíri.md)* and decides to take the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [7 March](../_/7_March.md): The [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md) arrives at [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md). Aragorn takes leave of [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [8 March](../_/8_March.md): The Grey Company takes the Paths of the Dead. At midnight, Aragorn summons the [Oathbreakers](../O/Oathbreakers.md) at the [Stone of Erech](../S/Stone_of_Erech.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [9 March](../_/9_March.md): Aragorn sets out from [Erech](../E/Erech.md) and comes to [Calembel](../C/Calembel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [10 March](../_/10_March.md): Aragorn, leading the Dead Men and the Grey Company, crosses the river [Ringló](../R/Ringló.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [11 March](../_/11_March.md): Aragorn reaches [Linhir](../L/Linhir.md) and crosses into [Lebennin](../L/Lebennin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [12 March](../_/12_March.md): Aragorn drives the enemy towards Pelargir.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [13 March](../_/13_March.md): The Grey Company reaches [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) with the army of the Dead and they capture the Corsairs' fleet.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [14 March](../_/14_March.md): Aragorn sails towards Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [15 March](../_/15_March.md): Aragorn sails north and arrives at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md). After the battle he heals [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md), Éowyn and Merry.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [16 March](../_/16_March.md): The Captains of the West decide to march to the Black Gate.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [18 March](../_/18_March.md): Aragorn leads the army of the West out of Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [23 March](../_/23_March.md): Aragorn sends those too frightened to proceed to retake [Cair Andros](../C/Cair_Andros.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [25 March](../_/25_March.md): Aragorn commands the army of the West in the [Battle of the Morannon](../B/Battle_of_the_Morannon.md). The Ring is destroyed and Sauron is defeated.<sup id="cite_ref-GY_3-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GY-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [8 April](../_/8_April.md): The [Ring-bearers](../R/Ring-bearers.md) are honored by Aragorn and the Host of the West at the [Field of Cormallen](../F/Field_of_Cormallen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-4">[4]</a></sup>

- [1 May](../_/1_May.md): Aragorn is crowned King.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-4">[4]</a></sup>

- [25 June](../_/25_June.md): Aragorn finds the sapling of the [White Tree](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Midsummer's Eve: Arwen arrives in Minas Tirith. Aragorn receives the [Sceptre of Annúminas](../S/Sceptre_of_Annúminas.md) signifying the Kingship of Arnor from Elrond.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Midsummer's Day](../M/Mid-year's_Day.md): Wedding of Aragorn and Arwen.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-4">[4]</a></sup>

- [22 August](../_/22_August.md): Aragorn bids farewell to the other members of the Fellowship.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Fourth Age {#Fourth_Age}

### [6](../F/Fourth_Age_6.md) {#6}

- King Elessar makes the Shire a Free Land under the protection of the [Northern Sceptre](../N/Northern_Sceptre.md) and forbids Men from entering it.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-5">[5]</a></sup>

### [13](../F/Fourth_Age_13.md) {#13}

- King Elessar makes the Thain, the [Master of Buckland](../M/Master_of_Buckland.md), and the [Mayor of Michel Delving](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md) Counsellors of the North-kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-5">[5]</a></sup>

### [15](../F/Fourth_Age_15.md) {#15}

- King Elessar and Queen Arwen come north to dwell at Annuminas. They meet with Pippin, Merry and Sam at the [Brandywine Bridge](../B/Brandywine_Bridge.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-5">[5]</a></sup>

### [31](../F/Fourth_Age_31.md) {#31}

- King Elessar adds the [Westmarch](../W/Westmarch.md) to the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-5">[5]</a></sup>

### [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md) {#120}

- [1 March](../_/1_March.md): Death of King Elessar. He is succeeded by his son Eldarion.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-5">[5]</a></sup>
