The **Third Deep** was a level in [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") that had treasuries and armouries. It was below the [East Gate](/wiki/Great_Gates "Great Gates") of Moria. During [Balin](/wiki/Balin)'s short [recolonization](/wiki/Balin%27s_Colony "Balin's Colony") of Moria, he sent [Óin](/wiki/%C3%93in) to find an access to it.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2024: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Moria_-_Through_the_Doors_of_Durin" title="Moria - Through the Doors of Durin">Moria: Through the Doors of Durin</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Third Deep is contextualized as arsenal and garrison of the Dwarven Tunnel-guards. All of them perished when <a href="/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane" title="Durin&#39;s Bane">Durin&#39;s Bane</a> unleashed deadly poison during the fall of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm" title="Khazad-dûm">Khazad-dûm</a>. By <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2965" title="Third Age 2965">2965</a>, due to the lingering poison the armouries remained largely intact. The Lower Armoury, locked with a special key, held the <a href="/wiki/Durin%27s_Axe" title="Durin&#39;s Axe">Axe of Durin</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup></dd></dl>
