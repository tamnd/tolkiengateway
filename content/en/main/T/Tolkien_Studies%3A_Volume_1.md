<b><i>Tolkien Studies</i>: Volume 1</b>, published in [2004](../_/2004.md), is the first volume of the annual review *[Tolkien Studies](<Tolkien_Studies_(journal).md>)*.

[Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md)'s article reprints the [1944](../_/1944.md) Middle English version of *[Sir Orfeo](<../S/Sir_Orfeo_(booklet).md>)* by J.R.R. Tolkien, it analyses the differences and similarities between this version and the Modern English version of it, also by Tolkien. The latter was published in *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, Pearl, and Sir Orfeo](../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl,_and_Sir_Orfeo.md)* in [1975](../_/1975.md).
