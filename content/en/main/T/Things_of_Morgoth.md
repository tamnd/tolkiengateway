The **Things of Morgoth** were a group of unspecified creatures or beings that were associated with [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).<sup id="cite_ref-name_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> the Things of Morgoth roamed freely alongside "[wolfriders](/wiki/Wolf-riders "Wolf-riders") and wandering [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs)" throughout [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-name_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
