**Third Age 2338** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2338**) is the 2338th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2338 was also known as **S.R. 738** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Náin II](../N/Náin_II.md), son of [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Óin](<../_/Óin_(King_of_Durin's_Folk).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
