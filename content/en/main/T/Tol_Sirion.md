**Tol Sirion** was the island on the upper reaches of the River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) in the middle of the [Pass of Sirion](/wiki/Pass_of_Sirion), between the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin) and [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion).

There [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") built the tower of [Minas Tirith](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand)> "Minas Tirith (Beleriand)") on this strategic position, as the guarded Pass connected [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen) and [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand). It was renamed [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth) (Isle of the Werewolves) after its capture by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was called Wizard's Isle in the [Lay of Leithian](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The loss of Tol Sirion allowed [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) to invade West Beleriand and harass [Talath Dirnen](/wiki/Talath_Dirnen) and [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tol Sirion* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Isle of the Great River", from *[tol](/wiki/Tol "Tol")* ("isle")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and *[Sirion](/wiki/Sirion)* ("The Great River").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
