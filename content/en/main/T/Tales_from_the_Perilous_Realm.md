<i><b>Tales from the Perilous Realm</b></i> is a compilation, first published in [1997](/wiki/1997), of several stories and poems by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), which had been previously published separately.

Its only major relation to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) is *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*, which features the character [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil).

## First edition {#First_edition}

The first edition of the book ([1997](/wiki/1997)) includes:

- *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)*
- *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*
- *[Leaf by Niggle](/wiki/Leaf_by_Niggle)*
- *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*

## Expanded edition {#Expanded_edition}

An expanded edition was published in September [2008](/wiki/2008), followed by the [full-cast dramatization](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)") in October. In this edition is included the story *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*, essay *[On Fairy-Stories](/wiki/On_Fairy-Stories)*, as well as an introduction and afterword. It is illustrated by [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee).

- Introduction by [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey)
- *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*
- [*Farmer Giles of Ham*](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham "Farmer Giles of Ham")
- *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*
- *[Leaf by Niggle](/wiki/Leaf_by_Niggle)*
- *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*
- Appendix: *[On Fairy-Stories](/wiki/On_Fairy-Stories)*
- Afterword by [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee)

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The five tales are written with the same skill, quality and charm that made The Hobbit a classic. Largely overlooked because of their short lengths, they are finally together in a volume which reaffirms Tolkien&#39;s place as a master storyteller for readers young and old.<br/>
</p>
<ul><li>Roverandom is a toy dog who, enchanted by a sand sorcerer, gets to explore the world and encounter strange and fabulous creatures.</li>
<li>Farmer Giles of Ham is fat and unheroic, but – having unwittingly managed to scare off a short-sighted giant – is called upon to do battle when a dragon comes to town;</li>
<li>The Adventures of Tom Bombadil tells in verse of Tom&#39;s many adventures with hobbits, princesses, dwarves and trolls;</li>
<li>Leaf by Niggle recounts the strange adventures of the painter Niggle who sets out to paint the perfect tree;</li>
<li>Smith of Wootton Major journeys to the Land of Faery thanks to the magical ingredients of the Great Cake of the Feast of Good Children.</li></ul>
</blockquote>
