**The Houses of Healing** is the eleventh track of disc three of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King - The Complete Recordings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_-_The_Complete_Recordings)* by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore). The song was sang by actress [Liv Tyler](/wiki/Liv_Tyler). It was written originally for *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers")*, but the creators could not find a place for it. So, they decided to put it in *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King")*, as a song played in "[The Houses of Healing](</wiki/The_Houses_of_Healing_(scene)> "The Houses of Healing (scene)")" scene.
