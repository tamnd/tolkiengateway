**The Return of the Ring: Celebrating Tolkien in 2012** was a convention hosted by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society). It was held at Loughborough University, England, [16](/wiki/16_August "16 August")-[20 August](/wiki/20_August), [2012](/wiki/2012). The convention marked the seventy-fifth anniversary of the publication of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.

## Events {#Events}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<ul><li><i>Academic streams (two or three in lecture theatres): lectures, panels, visual presentations, etc.</i></li>
<li><i>Creative stream: hand-on doing things, workshops and the like.</i></li>
<li><i>Fandom stream: anything that is inspired through fandom.</i></li>
<li><i>Dealers’ Room: there is a good sized room for the dealers on the ground floor with space for signings.</i></li>
<li><i>Art Show: we have a nice light, airy room to display art to good advantage.</i></li>
<li><i>Bar and hospitality facilities: a large bar in a central area on the ground floor and a caffeteria </i>[sic]<i> on the first floor.</i></li>
<li><i>Child-friendly activities for younger people with their parents (we won’t be providing child minding or a crèche)</i></li>
<li><i>Tours: we&#39;re planning to repeat the popular coach trips to Birmingham and Oxford, and possibly elsewhere too.</i></li></ul>

## Video from Peter Jackson, Alan Lee, and John Howe {#Video_from_Peter_Jackson,_Alan_Lee,_and_John_Howe}

In addition to donating two items for the auction (A *The Lord of the Rings* (extended edition) Blu-ray boxset and a ComicCon 2012 [*The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)") poster, both signed by Jackson), [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson), [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee), and [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe) sent their greetings and best wishes to the Return of the Ring. The video was played at the opening ceremony to the event.
