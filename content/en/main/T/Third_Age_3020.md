**Third Age 3020** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3020**) is the 3020th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 3020 was also known as **S.R. 1420** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## March {#March}

- **13** - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) is taken ill (on the anniversary of his poisoning by [Shelob](../S/Shelob.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB4-1">[1]</a></sup>

## April {#April}

- **6** - The [mallorn](../M/Mallorn.md) flowers in the [Party Field](../P/Party_Field.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB4-1">[1]</a></sup>

## In Spring {#In_Spring}

- Death of [Lobelia Bracegirdle](../L/Lobelia_Bracegirdle.md) (Sackville-Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-havens_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-havens-2">[2]</a></sup>

## May {#May}

- **1** - [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) marries [Rose](../R/Rose_Cotton.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB4_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB4-1">[1]</a></sup>

<h2><span id="Midyear.27s_Day"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Midyear&#39;s_Day"><a class="mw-redirect" href="../M/Midyear&#39;s_Day.md" title="Midyear&#39;s Day">Midyear&#39;s Day</a></span></h2>

- [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) resigns office of mayor, and [Will Whitfoot](../W/Will_Whitfoot.md) is restored.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB4_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB4-1">[1]</a></sup>

## [Overlithe](../O/Overlithe.md) {#Overlithe}

- This was a leap year in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md). The merrymaking in that year is said to have been the greatest in memory or record, owing to the wonderful summer of that year.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## September {#September}

- **22** - [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)'s hundred and thirtieth birthday.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB4_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB4-1">[1]</a></sup>

## October {#October}

- **6** - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) is again ill.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB4_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB4-1">[1]</a></sup>

## During the Year {#During_the_Year}

- This year was remembered as "The Great Year of Plenty" in [the Shire](The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB4_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB4-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) weds [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Frodo begins writing *The Downfall of the Lord of the Rings and the Return of the King*, his account of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), in the [Red Book](../R/Red_Book.md).<sup id="cite_ref-havens_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-havens-2">[2]</a></sup>
