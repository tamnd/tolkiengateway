<i><b>The Children of Húrin: Deluxe Edition</b></i> is the special edition of *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)* which was released at the same time. It is the same as the regular edition with the exception that it is bound in higher quality material and comes in a slipcase with a gold foil stamp.

[HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) printed 10,000 copies of the deluxe edition.

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) signed approximately 900 adhesive book plates which have been glued to some copies of this deluxe edition.
