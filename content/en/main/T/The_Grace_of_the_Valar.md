**The Grace of the Valar** is the twenty-eighth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* and the thirty-seventh scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition).md>).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md)'s body floats downstream before coming to rest at a rocky shore. [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) is shown lying in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md). The scene then returns to Aragorn, who is unconscious. Arwen fades into view above Aragorn and, as she gently kisses him, saying "May the grace of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) protect you", Aragorn opens his eyes.

[Brego](<../B/Brego_(horse).md>) finds Aragorn lying unconscious on the riverbank. The horse nudges him and he wearily grips a part of Brego's mane, pulling himself up. Brego rides away with him, taking him towards [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md).
