<i><b>tinco</b></i> is a noun meaning "metal" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Tinco.gif"><img src="/w/images/e/ec/Tinco.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Tinco* is also the name in Quenya of the first letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the first letter of the first grade or [Tyellë](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB), which contains "voiceless stops" and it is the first letter in the dental or *t*-series of consonants, the *[Tincotéma](/wiki/Tincot%C3%A9ma)*. In all modes *Tinco* represents **T**.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Tó</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<br>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=TINK%C5%8C&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TINKŌ (page does not exist)">TINKŌ</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
