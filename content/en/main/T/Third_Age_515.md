**Third Age 515** (abbreviated to **T.A. 515**) is the 515th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md) [Tarcil](Tarcil.md) of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppANorth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppANorth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Tarcil](Tarcil.md)'s son, [Tarondor](<Tarondor_(King_of_Arnor).md>), becomes the seventh [King](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md) of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppANorth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppANorth-1">[1]</a></sup>
