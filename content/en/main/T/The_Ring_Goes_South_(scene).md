**The Ring Goes South** is the twenty-fifth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* and the thirty-first of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition).md>). This scene was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

Having set off from [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) is making their way south, following the line of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md). [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) states that the goal is to travel south for forty days, then turn east at the [Gap of Rohan](../G/Gap_of_Rohan.md) to travel straight to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). They later stop to set up a camp, with [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) preparing a meal there.

[Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) grouses that they're taking the long way around, and recommends that they go through the Mines of [Moria](../M/Moria.md), where his brother [Balin](../B/Balin.md) would give them a royal welcome. Gandalf, however, vetoes this idea, stating he would not pass through Moria unless they had no other choice. On the other side of the camp, [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) is training [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) in swordplay, but accidentally strikes Pippins' hand. Pippin retaliates by kicking Boromir in the shin, and the two hobbits tackle him in a friendly tussle. [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn_II.md) tries to break them up, but gets tripped by the hobbits and swept up into the tussle.

The levity quickly ends when [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) sees something in the sky. Gimli writes it off as a wisp of cloud, but Boromir notes, with concern, that it's moving fast, and against the wind. The Fellowship is forced to hide both their camp and themselves as a flock of [crows](../C/Crebain.md) fly past then.

Gandalf notes with dismay that the crows are spies of [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md), which means the passage south is being watched. Needing a new path forward, Gandalf redirects the Fellowship to the pass of [Caradhras](../C/Caradhras.md).
