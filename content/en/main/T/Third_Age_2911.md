**Third Age 2911** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2911**) is the 2911th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2911 was also known as **S.R. 1311** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Fell Winter](<../F/Fell_Winter_(Third_Age).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The [Baranduin](../B/Baranduin.md) and other rivers are frozen.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [White Wolves](../W/White_Wolves.md) invade [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) from the North.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Wolves pass the frozen Baranduin and invade [the Shire](The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Horn-call of Buckland](../H/Horn-call_of_Buckland.md) is heard to alert for the wolf-invasion.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Dudo Baggins](../D/Dudo_Baggins.md), third child and second son of [Fosco Baggins](../F/Fosco_Baggins.md) and [Ruby Bolger](../R/Ruby_Bolger.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Death of [Ponto Baggins](../P/Ponto_Baggins_I.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-4">[4]</a></sup>
