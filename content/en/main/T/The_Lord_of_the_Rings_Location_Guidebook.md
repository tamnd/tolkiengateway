<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Location Guidebook</b></i> is a book by [Ian Brodie](../I/Ian_Brodie.md) that provides navigation directions and visiting tips for the filming locations of *[The Lord of the Rings](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)* film series. It is available in both pocketbook and full-size formats.

## Editions {#Editions}

*The Lord of the Rings Location Guidebook* has been released in several consecutively expanded editions:

- First Edition (2002) - only filming locations from *[The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* and *[The Two Towers](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* are included.
- Revised Edition (2003) - extended with the filming locations for *[The Return of the King](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.
- Extended Edition (2004) - larger size and different, square format, also includes general behind-the-scenes information about the filming process.
