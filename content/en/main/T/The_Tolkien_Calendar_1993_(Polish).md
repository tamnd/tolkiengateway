**The Tolkien Calendar 1993** was published in Poland by Sekji Tolkienowskiej. This calendar features pen and ink illustrations by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Aleksandra_Kosciuszczuk&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aleksandra Kosciuszczuk (page does not exist)">Aleksandra Kosciuszczuk</a>, [Ryszard Derdziński](/wiki/Ryszard_Derdzi%C5%84ski), [Małgorzata Pudlik](/wiki/Ma%C5%82gorzata_Pudlik), and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Helena_Strokowska&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Helena Strokowska (page does not exist)">Helena Strokowska</a>.

- Dimensions: 8 1/4 x 11 5/8 inches
- Not stapled
