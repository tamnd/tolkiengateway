**The Three Hunters** is the fourth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and the fifth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) lies on the ground, ear pressed to the dirt, listening for the faraway feet of their enemies whom they have been tracking. He tells [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) that the pace of the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) [who captured Merry and Pippin](</wiki/The_Breaking_of_the_Fellowship_(scene)> "The Breaking of the Fellowship (scene)") has increased, and that they should make haste. The Three Hunters carry on, following the tracks of the Uruk-hai, until Aragorn suddenly stops, as he has found an [elven brooch](/wiki/Elven_brooch "Elven brooch") on the ground, the same as those that they themselves wear on their [Elven-cloaks](/wiki/Elven_cloaks "Elven cloaks") that they were given as gifts by [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Not idly do the leaves of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/L%C3%B3rien" title="Lórien">Lórien</a> fall.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Aragorn</cite></p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

They realize that they are catching up with the Uruk-hai who have captured their young friends and continue on, with Gimli struggling in the chase. Upon climbing to the top of a hill, they look out over the plains of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). Aragorn asks Legolas what his Elf-eyes can see. The Elf replies, "The Uruks turn northeast. They are taking the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) to [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard)!"
