<i><b>The Hobbit Tarot</b></i> is a book describing a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarot" title="wikipedia:Tarot">Tarot deck</a> with illustrations of characters and scenes from *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. The book is accompanied by deck of Tarot cards called <i><b>The Hobbit Tarot</b></i>.

This is the second Tolkien tarot-related collaboration between Terry Donaldson and Peter Pracownik, who also authored [The Lord of the Rings Tarot](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Tarot), released in 1997. Unlike that release, *The Hobbit Tarot* does not feature instructions for a game to be played with the cards.

## Synopsis from the publisher {#Synopsis_from_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>In anticipation of two new feature film releases, enter the world of The Hobbit and allow the powerful imagery of the tarot to guide you through this mystic realm. Following the hero s quest along his many adventures, The Hobbit Tarot helps us understand the meaning of our own life journeys.
</p>
</blockquote>

## Cards {#Cards}

### Major Arcana {#Major_Arcana}

- 0 - *The Fool*: [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) begins his journey
- I - *The Magician*: [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) sitting near a lake, gazing into a sphere, perhaps a [Palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri)
- II - *The High Priestess*: Book reading 'The lost key to the Book of Dain, Lord of the Misty Mountain." in [Cirth](/wiki/Cirth).
- III - *The Empress*: Bilbo, Gandalf, [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company).
- IV - *The Emperor*: [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) sits in a tree
- V - *The Hierophant*: [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in a trance
- VI - *The Lovers*: [Belladonna Took](/wiki/Belladonna_Took) and [Bungo Baggins](/wiki/Bungo_Baggins) converse.
- VII - *The Chariot*: [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) on horses, travelling to the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain)
- VIII - *Strength*: [Thorin Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin") rests.
- IX - *The Hermit*: [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn) with an axe.
- X - *Wheel of Fortune*: [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum)’s [Riddle-game](/wiki/Riddle-game) with the [Ring-inscription](/wiki/Ring-inscription) superimposed.
- XI - *Justice*: [Thorin Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin"), [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and The [Arkenstone](/wiki/Arkenstone).
- XII - *The Hanged Man*: [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) hunting for food.
- XIII - *Death*: Death of the [Great Goblin](/wiki/Great_Goblin).
- XIV - *Temperance*: [Bolg](/wiki/Bolg)
- XV - *The Devil*: [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) attacks [Laketown](/wiki/Laketown).
- XVI - *The Tower*: [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) emerges from the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain), breathing fire.
- XVII - *The Star*: The [Back Door](/wiki/Back_Door) of the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain)
- XVIII - *The Moon*: [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) and the [Moon](/wiki/Moon)
- XIX - *The Sun*: The [Sun](/wiki/Sun) shines over a valley, [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) can be seen in the distance.
- XX - *Judgement*: The [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies)
- XXI - *The World*: [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) on his horde of gold
