**Tolkien metal** is a sub-genre of *black metal* music, influenced by the work of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). It is more atmospheric and ambient than original black metal, and the lyrics are about Tolkien's work. The band [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md) is the most notable Tolkien metal band, while [Isengard](<../I/Isengard_(band).md>) has influenced many.

Although their genre is not black metal, bands like [Blind Guardian](../B/Blind_Guardian.md), [Battlelore](../B/Battlelore.md) and [Rivendell](<../R/Rivendell_(band).md>) can be described as Tolkien metal because of their lyrics. However, the name was specifically created for Summoning and its kind.
