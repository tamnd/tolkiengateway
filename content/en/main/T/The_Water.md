**The Water**, short for the **Shire-water**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was an important river in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-Part_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Part-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The Water arose to the north-west of [Needlehole](/wiki/Needlehole) where it turned to flow to the south. After a short distance at the [Rushock Bog](/wiki/Rushock_Bog) it split into two branches, which soon rejoined and then turned eastwards, southwards and again eastwards before [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton).<sup id="cite_ref-Part_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Part-2">[2]</a></sup> Before flowing through Hobbiton the Water was quite narrow and could be crossed by a narrow plank bridge, and was shadowed by alder-trees.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> From there, it flowed through Hobbiton and on into the [Bywater Pool](/wiki/Bywater_Pool) at [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater), where it was joined by a stream from the north. From then on, it flowed eastward alongside the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road), which ran through the [Water-valley](/wiki/Water-valley).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> North of [Frogmorton](/wiki/Frogmorton) it split again into two branches that rejoined after a short distance. It continued eastwards where it could be crossed at the Budge Ford by the village of [Budgeford](/wiki/Budgeford)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and then continued eastwards to join the [Brandywine](/wiki/Baranduin "Baranduin") just north of the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge).<sup id="cite_ref-Part_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Part-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The naming of the Water may be a parody of some sorts of Celtic hydronyms that mean simply "river" or "water".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
