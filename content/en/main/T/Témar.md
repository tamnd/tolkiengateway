**Témar** was the "series" of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar). In normal usage, each of these contained six *[tyeller](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB "Tyellë")* or "grades" that gave different qualities to a basic sound.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
