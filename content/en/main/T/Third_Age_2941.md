**Third Age 2941** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2941**) is the 2941st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2941 was also known as **S.R. 1341** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## [March](../M/March.md) {#March}

- **[15](../_/15_March.md)**: [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) meets [Thorin](Thorin.md) near [Bree](../B/Bree.md) and eventually decide about the [Quest of Erebor](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## [April](../A/April.md) {#April}

- **[6](../_/6_April.md)**: [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) is out for *[Yestare](../Y/Yestare.md)*; [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) visits [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md), talks with [Holman Greenhand](../H/Holman_Greenhand.md) and realises Bilbo is the right person for the Quest.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Thorin](Thorin.md) and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) visit [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) in [the Shire](The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- The next day [Thorin and Company](Thorin_and_Company.md) depart for the *[Green Dragon](../G/Green_Dragon~72852.md)*. Bilbo catches up at 11 a.m. Quest for Erebor begins.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Spring-Summer {#Spring-Summer}

- Bilbo meets [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) and finds [the One Ring](The_One_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The [White Council](../W/White_Council.md) meets.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) agrees to an [Attack on Dol Guldur](../A/Attack_on_Dol_Guldur.md), since he now wishes to prevent [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) from searching the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) having made his plans abandons [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>

## [September](../S/September.md) {#September}

- Bilbo and [Thorin and Company](Thorin_and_Company.md) escape the [Elvenking's Halls](../E/Elvenking's_Halls.md) on barrels.
- **[22](../_/22_September.md)**: Bilbo's birthday; that day he arrives in [Lake-town](../L/Lake-town.md).

## Later {#Later}

- [Bard](../B/Bard.md) of [Esgaroth](../L/Lake-town.md) slays [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md) in [Dale](../D/Dale.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Death of [Thorin](Thorin.md) Oakenshield, [Fíli](../F/Fíli.md), and [Kíli](../K/Kíli.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Death of [Bolg](../B/Bolg.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
- [Dáin II Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) of the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md) becomes [King under the Mountain](../K/King_under_the_Mountain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Unknown {#Unknown}

- Birth of [Tolman Cotton](Tolman_Cotton_Senior.md), first child and son of [Holman Cotton](../H/Holman_Cotton.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
- Death of [Hildigrim Took](../H/Hildigrim_Took.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
