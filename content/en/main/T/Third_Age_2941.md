**Third Age 2941** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2941**) is the 2941st [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Third Age 2941 was also known as **S.R. 1341** in [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning).

Notable events in this year include:

## [March](/wiki/March) {#March}

- **[15](/wiki/15_March "15 March")**: [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) meets [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) near [Bree](/wiki/Bree) and eventually decide about the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## [April](/wiki/April) {#April}

- **[6](/wiki/6_April "6 April")**: [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) is out for *[Yestare](/wiki/Yestare)*; [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) visits [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End), talks with [Holman Greenhand](/wiki/Holman_Greenhand) and realises Bilbo is the right person for the Quest.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) visit [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- The next day [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) depart for the *[Green Dragon](/wiki/Green_Dragon)*. Bilbo catches up at 11 a.m. Quest for Erebor begins.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Spring-Summer {#Spring-Summer}

- Bilbo meets [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) and finds [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The [White Council](/wiki/White_Council) meets.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) agrees to an [Attack on Dol Guldur](/wiki/Attack_on_Dol_Guldur), since he now wishes to prevent [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) from searching the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) having made his plans abandons [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>

## [September](/wiki/September) {#September}

- Bilbo and [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) escape the [Elvenking's Halls](/wiki/Elvenking%27s_Halls) on barrels.
- **[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")**: Bilbo's birthday; that day he arrives in [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town).

## Later {#Later}

- [Bard](/wiki/Bard) of [Esgaroth](/wiki/Lake-town "Lake-town") slays [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies) in [Dale](/wiki/Dale).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Death of [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) Oakenshield, [Fíli](/wiki/F%C3%ADli), and [Kíli](/wiki/K%C3%ADli).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Death of [Bolg](/wiki/Bolg).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
- [Dáin II Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot") of the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) becomes [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Unknown {#Unknown}

- Birth of [Tolman Cotton](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior"), first child and son of [Holman Cotton](/wiki/Holman_Cotton).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
- Death of [Hildigrim Took](/wiki/Hildigrim_Took).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
