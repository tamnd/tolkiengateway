<i><b>The Bridge of Catzad-Dum</b></i> is a text adventure in the *[Eamon](</wiki/Eamon_(video_game)> "Eamon (video game)")* series, written by [Nathan Segerlind](/wiki/Nathan_Segerlind)<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> and released in September 1988. The adventure parodies chapters [4](/wiki/A_Journey_in_the_Dark "A Journey in the Dark") and [5](/wiki/The_Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm "The Bridge of Khazad-dûm") of Book 2 from *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, and is largely adapted from the first story in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Samurai_Cat" title="wikipedia:The Adventures of Samurai Cat">The Adventures of Samurai Cat</a></i>, a collection of humorous short stories written by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_E._Rogers" title="wikipedia:Mark E. Rogers">Mark E. Rogers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> In the adventure you journey to Catzad-Dum to avenge the death of your master.<sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup>

The title is a parody of "[The Bridge of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/The_Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm)" and the dwarves and orcs of the original story are replaced by cats and "porks".
