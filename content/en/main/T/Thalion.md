<i><b>thalion</b></i> is a word glossed as "strong, dauntless, steadfast" ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and "hero, dauntless man" ([Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md); pl. *thelyn*)<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *[Etymologies](The_Etymologies.md)*, Noldorin *thalion* derives from [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) *stalgondō*, from root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=ST%C3%81LAG&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="STÁLAG (page does not exist)">STÁLAG</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

## As a Name {#As_a_Name}

- [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) son of [Galdor](<../G/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-lómin).md>), the last [Lord of Dor-lómin](../L/Lord_of_Dor-lómin.md) was called **Thalion**, "The Steadfast".
- [Mardil Voronwë](../M/Mardil.md), the first [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) was called "The Steadfast" for preventing civil war by taking up the rulership of Gondor.
