<i><b>thalion</b></i> is a word glossed as "strong, dauntless, steadfast" ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and "hero, dauntless man" ([Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin); pl. *thelyn*)<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")*, Noldorin *thalion* derives from [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *stalgondō*, from root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=ST%C3%81LAG&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="STÁLAG (page does not exist)">STÁLAG</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

## As a Name {#As_a_Name}

- [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) son of [Galdor](</wiki/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min)> "Galdor (Lord of Dor-lómin)"), the last [Lord of Dor-lómin](/wiki/Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min) was called **Thalion**, "The Steadfast".
- [Mardil Voronwë](/wiki/Mardil "Mardil"), the first [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) was called "The Steadfast" for preventing civil war by taking up the rulership of Gondor.
