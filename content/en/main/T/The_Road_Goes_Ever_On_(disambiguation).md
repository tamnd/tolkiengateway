**The Road Goes Ever On** can refer to:

## Poems {#Poems}

- "[The Road Goes Ever On (song)](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(song)>)", a walking song featured in both *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

## Books {#Books}

- *[The Road Goes Ever On, A Song Cycle](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")*, a book containing a set of poems by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") set to music by [Donald Swann](/wiki/Donald_Swann).
- *[The Road Goes Ever On and On: The Map of Tolkien's Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_and_On:_The_Map_of_Tolkien%27s_Middle-earth)*, a book by [Brian Sibley](/wiki/Brian_Sibley) and [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe).

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- "[The Road Goes Ever On... (scene)](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On..._(scene)>)", the last scene of both the theatrical and extended editions of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.
- "[Roads (song)](</wiki/Roads_(song)>)", an adaptation of the song, sung by [Glenn Yarbrough](/wiki/Glenn_Yarbrough) and published on *[The Hobbit: The Complete Original Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Complete_Original_Soundtrack)*.
- "[Roads Go Ever, Ever On](/wiki/Roads_Go_Ever,_Ever_On)", an adaptation of the song from the [1980](/wiki/1980) film *[The Return of the King](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)")* by [Arthur Rankin, Jr.](/wiki/Arthur_Rankin,_Jr.) and [Jules Bass](/wiki/Jules_Bass).

## Popular media {#Popular_media}

- "[Mountain Live: The Road Goes Ever On](/wiki/Mountain_Live:_The_Road_Goes_Ever_On)", an album by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mountain&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mountain (page does not exist)">Mountain</a>.
