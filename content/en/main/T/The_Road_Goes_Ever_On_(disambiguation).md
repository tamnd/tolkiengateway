**The Road Goes Ever On** can refer to:

## Poems {#Poems}

- "[The Road Goes Ever On (song)](<The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(song).md>)", a walking song featured in both *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.

## Books {#Books}

- *[The Road Goes Ever On, A Song Cycle](<The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book).md>)*, a book containing a set of poems by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) set to music by [Donald Swann](../D/Donald_Swann.md).
- *[The Road Goes Ever On and On: The Map of Tolkien's Middle-earth](The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_and_On%253A_The_Map_of_Tolkien's_Middle-earth.md)*, a book by [Brian Sibley](../B/Brian_Sibley.md) and [John Howe](../J/John_Howe.md).

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- "[The Road Goes Ever On... (scene)](<The_Road_Goes_Ever_On..._(scene).md>)", the last scene of both the theatrical and extended editions of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.
- "[Roads (song)](<../R/Roads_(song).md>)", an adaptation of the song, sung by [Glenn Yarbrough](../G/Glenn_Yarbrough.md) and published on *[The Hobbit: The Complete Original Soundtrack](The_Hobbit%253A_The_Complete_Original_Soundtrack.md)*.
- "[Roads Go Ever, Ever On](../R/Roads_Go_Ever,_Ever_On~44338.md)", an adaptation of the song from the [1980](../_/1980.md) film *[The Return of the King](<The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md>)* by [Arthur Rankin, Jr.](../A/Arthur_Rankin,_Jr..md) and [Jules Bass](../J/Jules_Bass.md).

## Popular media {#Popular_media}
