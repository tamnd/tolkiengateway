**toloth** is the number eight (8, VIII) in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and perhaps [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin). <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), the precursor of Sindarin, "eight" is <i><b>toloth</b></i>. The word derives from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") TOL (-OTH/OT).<sup id="cite_ref-TOL_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOL-2">[2]</a></sup>
