**toloth** is the number eight (8, VIII) in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) and perhaps [Telerin](Telerin.md). <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md), the precursor of Sindarin, "eight" is <i><b>toloth</b></i>. The word derives from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) TOL (-OTH/OT).<sup id="cite_ref-TOL_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOL-2">[2]</a></sup>
