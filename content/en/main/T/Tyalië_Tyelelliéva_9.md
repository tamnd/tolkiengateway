<b><i>Tyalië Tyelelliéva</i> 9</b> is an issue of the journal *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va)*.

## Contents {#Contents}

- Editorial, Celestial Events, News and Notes
- Celeg Aithorn by Lisa Star (news from the Internet)
- Letters to the Editor from Dr. Rhona Beare (on mythological geography), Edouard Kloczko (on review of his book), Nancy Martsch (on intensives)
- Painted Caves and Yggdrasil, by Dr. [Rhona Beare](/wiki/Rhona_Beare) (article on sources for Tolkien's Father Christmas Letters art; enclosed with this issue - until they run out - is a postcard with Tolkien's cave painting on it)
- Three Kinds of Elves by Leonid Korablev (article on the historical origins of elf folk lore with calligraphy)
- Four Calligraphic Styles of Tengwar by Leonid Korablev (calligraphy)
- Ilkorin and North Sindarin by [Édouard Kloczko](/wiki/%C3%89douard_Kloczko) (article on phonological analysis of early dialects)
- Inspired by Art: William Morris, by Lisa Star (article on [Morris](/wiki/William_Morris "William Morris")' influence on Tolkien)
- Catamando Lire Mincarme by David Salo (translation of Caedmon's Hymn with calligraphy and explanation)
- Reviews (reviews by Lisa Star)
    - The Quenya Dictionary by Harold Ensle
    - The Sindarin Dictionary by Harold Ensle
    - Tengwar Fonts designed by Dan Smith (software review)
- Poetry Competition Rules.
