<i><b>The Fair</b></i> can refer to:

- [Celegorm the Fair](/wiki/Celegorm "Celegorm") ([Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") - [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [506](/wiki/First_Age_506 "First Age 506")), [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") prince and third son of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor);
- [Dior the Fair](/wiki/Dior "Dior") ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [470](/wiki/First_Age_470 "First Age 470") - [506](/wiki/First_Age_506 "First Age 506")), [half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven "Half-elven") son of [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien);
- [Elanor the Fair](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner "Elanor Gardner") (b. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021")), eldest child of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton);
- [Elfwine the Fair](/wiki/Elfwine "Elfwine") ([Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age)), nineteenth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan") of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan);
- [Hirluin the Fair](/wiki/Hirluin "Hirluin") (d. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")), captain of the men of [Pinnath Gelin](/wiki/Pinnath_Gelin) during the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).
