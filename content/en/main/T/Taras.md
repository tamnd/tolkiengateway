**Mount Taras** was a tall mountain at the western end of a line of hills that were outliers of the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin). It towered over a green cape between [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast) and [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand) on the shores of [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Below its slopes [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) built his halls of [Vinyamar](/wiki/Vinyamar) when he first returned to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Long after Turgon had deserted Nevrast for [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) came there and spoke with [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*taras* means "great towering building, mount, hill".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
