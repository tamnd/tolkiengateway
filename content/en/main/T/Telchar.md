**Telchar** was a [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Nogrod "Dwarves of Nogrod") of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) in the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains), and one of the greatest smiths in the history of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

## History {#History}

Telchar was trained by [Gamil Zirak](/wiki/Gamil_Zirak), another great smith.<sup id="cite_ref-Narn_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narn-1">[1]</a></sup> Among his works were [Angrist](/wiki/Angrist) (the knife that freed the [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmarils "Silmarils") from the [Iron Crown](/wiki/Iron_Crown)),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Narsil](/wiki/Narsil) (the sword of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), later reforged for [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) as [Andúril](/wiki/And%C3%BAril)),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and the [Dragon-helm of Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dragon-helm_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min).<sup id="cite_ref-Narn_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narn-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is not defined whether the name Telchar is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) or [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul). The formation seems to suggest to be Sindarin since the digraph *ch* is not used in Khuzdul.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) notes a resemblance to the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telchines" title="wikipedia:Telchines">Telchines</a></i> of Greek mythology, a mythological race of divine craftsmen.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Telchar appears for the first time in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*, simply named as the creator of the dragon-helm.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> In the later *[Quenta Noldorinwa](/wiki/Quenta_Noldorinwa)*, Telchar is said to be from [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost), instead of Nogrod as in the *[Narn](/wiki/Narn_i_H%C3%AEn_H%C3%BArin "Narn i Hîn Húrin")*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
