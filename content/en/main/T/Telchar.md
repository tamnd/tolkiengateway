**Telchar** was a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves_of_Nogrod.md) of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) in the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md), and one of the greatest smiths in the history of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

## History {#History}

Telchar was trained by [Gamil Zirak](../G/Gamil_Zirak.md), another great smith.<sup id="cite_ref-Narn_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narn-1">[1]</a></sup> Among his works were [Angrist](../A/Angrist.md) (the knife that freed the [Silmaril](../S/Silmarils.md) from the [Iron Crown](../I/Iron_Crown.md)),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Narsil](../N/Narsil.md) (the sword of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md), later reforged for [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) as [Andúril](../A/Andúril.md)),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and the [Dragon-helm of Dor-lómin](../D/Dragon-helm_of_Dor-lómin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Narn_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narn-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is not defined whether the name Telchar is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) or [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md). The formation seems to suggest to be Sindarin since the digraph *ch* is not used in Khuzdul.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](../J/Jim_Allan.md) notes a resemblance to the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telchines" title="wikipedia:Telchines">Telchines</a></i> of Greek mythology, a mythological race of divine craftsmen.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Telchar appears for the first time in the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md)*, simply named as the creator of the dragon-helm.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> In the later *[Quenta Noldorinwa](../Q/Quenta_Noldorinwa.md)*, Telchar is said to be from [Belegost](../B/Belegost.md), instead of Nogrod as in the *[Narn](../N/Narn_i_Hîn_Húrin.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
