**Tosser Grubb** is a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) in [*The Hobbit* film series](<The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>). He was was portrayed by Merv Smith.

## History {#History}

Tosser Grubb was the auctioneer for [Messrs Grubb, Grubb and Burrowes](../M/Messrs_Grubb,_Grubb_and_Burrowes.md) in charge of selling [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)'s possessions and [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md). When Bilbo himself arrived, Tosser was apprehensive. Bilbo gave him the contract he had with the Dwarves, which Tosser found sufficient to prove his identity.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
