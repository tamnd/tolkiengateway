**The Lord of the Rings MUSH** is a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MUSH" title="wikipedia:MUSH">MUSH</a><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> released in 1993 that was based totally on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s works. Previous MUSHes typically only contained one Tolkien-based area.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FAQ_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FAQ-3">[3]</a></sup> It was made by [Christopher Anderson](../C/Christopher_Anderson.md), nicknamed as Sagar, and his team. In [1994](../_/1994.md) it was closed.<sup id="cite_ref-FAQ_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FAQ-3">[3]</a></sup> There were plans for two succesors. One with the [same name](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_MUSH_(cancelled).md>) was planned to be mostly the same but with improved code until it was cancelled.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FAQ_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FAQ-3">[3]</a></sup> The other, named [The Lord of the Rings MUSH II](The_Lord_of_the_Rings_MUSH_II.md), takes place in the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md) but has yet to be released.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FAQ_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FAQ-3">[3]</a></sup>
