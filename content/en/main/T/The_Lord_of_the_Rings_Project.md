**The Lord of the Rings Project** is a website which aims to 'to place every character in [J.R.R. Tolkien's](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) fictional universe in a family tree'.<sup id="cite_ref-LOTRPAbout_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LOTRPAbout-1">[1]</a></sup>

In addition to the family tree, the Lord of the Rings Project features a timeline of important events of the history of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> an interactive map of Middle-earth in the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) and various detailed statistics.

It was founded by Chemical Engineering student [Emil Johansson](../E/Emil_Johansson.md).<sup id="cite_ref-LOTRPAbout_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LOTRPAbout-1">[1]</a></sup>
