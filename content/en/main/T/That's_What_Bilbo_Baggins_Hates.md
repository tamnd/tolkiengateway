**That's What [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) Hates** is a song from the [1977 adaptation](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)") of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, the lyrics are based on [the poem from the book](/wiki/Chip_the_glasses_and_crack_the_plates! "Chip the glasses and crack the plates!"). The lyrics were adapted by [Jules Bass](/wiki/Jules_Bass) and the music by [Maury Laws](/wiki/Maury_Laws). *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)* features [a similar adaptation](/wiki/Blunt_the_Knives "Blunt the Knives") of the poem.

That&#39;s what Bilbo Baggins hates,<br/>
So carefully, carefully with the plates!<br/>
<br/>
Blunt the knives and bend the forks!<br/>
Smash the bottles, burn the corks!<br/>
That&#39;s what Bilbo Baggins hates,<br/>
So carefully, carefully with the plates!
</p>
