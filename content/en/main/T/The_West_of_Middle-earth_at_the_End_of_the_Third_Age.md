<i><b>The West of Middle-earth at the End of the Third Age</b></i> is the title of a map drawn by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) in [1980](/wiki/1980), it is the new version of [an older map](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth "General Map of Middle-earth") made back in [1953](/wiki/1953).

It depicts the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands) and all northwestern [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## Conception and creation {#Conception_and_creation}

The map is a re-drawing by Christopher of his [own original map](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth "General Map of Middle-earth") made in late [1953](/wiki/1953) for the first edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-intro_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-intro-3">[3]</a></sup> It was redrawn in order to include new locations mentioned in the *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)* (such as "[Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond)" and "[Undeeps](/wiki/Undeeps)"), but also to correct some of the minor defects of the earlier map drawn "twenty-five years ago".<sup id="cite_ref-intro_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-intro-3">[3]</a></sup>

The size of the new drawing was "half as large again as the old map in its published dimensions" and its smaller scope allowed for better clarity. Christopher Tolkien preserved the "style and detail" of the original not because of its accuracy, but because of its acceptable faithfulness to his father's conceptions.<sup id="cite_ref-intro_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-intro-3">[3]</a></sup>

Since [2005](/wiki/2005), it has been included in the [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age_(colorized).jpeg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/4/4f/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age_%28colorized%29.jpeg/300px-The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age_%28colorized%29.jpeg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="229" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/4/4f/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age_%28colorized%29.jpeg/450px-The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age_%28colorized%29.jpeg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/4/4f/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age_%28colorized%29.jpeg/600px-The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age_%28colorized%29.jpeg 2x" data-file-width="3765" data-file-height="2879"/></a><figcaption>This colorized version can be found in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Fall_of_N%C3%BAmenor" title="The Fall of Númenor">The Fall of Númenor</a></i>.</figcaption></figure>

Smaller in scope than the original, this map excludes locations such as the [Cape of Forochel](/wiki/Cape_of_Forochel) in the north, or [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) and [Far Harad](/wiki/Far_Harad) in the south. However the island of [Himling](/wiki/Himling) is now included.<sup id="cite_ref-intro_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-intro-3">[3]</a></sup>

The [Sea](/wiki/Sea) is now filled with contour lines radiating from the coasts, as has been the case with the inland bodies of water.

The [Icebay of Forochel](/wiki/Icebay_of_Forochel) is now labelled with an arrow pointing to the larger northeast part of the bay, and not the small gulf. [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) stated that his original map was marked incorrectly.<sup id="cite_ref-intro_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-intro-3">[3]</a></sup>

The areas of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) are more detailed with streets and roads, as seen in the *[Map of Rohan, Gondor, and Mordor](/wiki/Map_of_Rohan,_Gondor,_and_Mordor)*: [Westfold](/wiki/Westfold) and [Eastfold](/wiki/Eastfold), separated by the [Snowbourn](/wiki/Snowbourn) are marked, as well as the [Mering Stream](/wiki/Mering_Stream). The [Old South Road](/wiki/Old_South_Road) is renamed [North-South Road](/wiki/North-South_Road) and extends along the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains), although part of its course was conjectured by Christopher Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-intro_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-intro-3">[3]</a></sup>

Other names not shown in the original map include:

- [Greylin](/wiki/Greylin) and [Langwell](/wiki/Langwell)
- [Carnen](/wiki/Carnen)
- [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) and [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan) are now included, alongside [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur)
- [Old Ford](/wiki/Old_Ford)
- [East Bight](/wiki/East_Bight)
- [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas)
- [Crossings of Poros](/wiki/Crossings_of_Poros)
- [Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond) (conjectural)<sup id="cite_ref-intro_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-intro-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Lond Daer](/wiki/Lond_Daer) (conjectural)<sup id="cite_ref-intro_3-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-intro-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Harnen](/wiki/Harnen)
- [Eryn Vorn](/wiki/Eryn_Vorn) now textured as a wooded area
- [Swanfleet](/wiki/Swanfleet) and [Glanduin](/wiki/Glanduin)
- [Adorn](/wiki/Adorn)
- [Andrast](/wiki/Andrast)
- [Drúwaith Iaur](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAwaith_Iaur)

### Errors {#Errors}

On the original print of the *Unfinished Tales* carried over a mistake from the *general map*: the river [Sirith](/wiki/Sirith) was marked as the western tributary of [Celos](/wiki/Celos), whereas the reverse is true.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The mistake was corrected in later (but not all) printings of the map.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
