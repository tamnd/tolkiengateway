**The Battle of the Pelennor Fields** is the sixth chapter of the first book in *[The Return of the King](The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.

## Summary {#Summary}

Suddenly, a massive black beast swooped down upon [Théoden](Théoden.md), hitting his horse with a poisoned dart. The steed reared up, and the king fell beneath his [horse](../S/Snowmane.md), crushed. The [Lord of the Nazgûl](../W/Witch-king.md) loomed above on the back of his flying steed. Terrified, Théoden's guards fled in panic—all but [Dernhelm](../D/Dernhelm.md). Thrown from his horse but unharmed, Dernhelm challenged the Witch-king. [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md), crawling on all fours in a daze, heard Dernhelm speak, and he recognized the warrior’s voice. Dernhelm threw back his hood and revealed to the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) that he was in fact [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md), the Lady of Rohan, in disguise.

The winged steed struck at Éowyn, but she dealt it a fatal wound, cutting off its head. The Witch-king leapt off his dead mount and shattered Éowyn’s shield with a blow from his club, breaking her arm. He raised his spiked club again, but just before he struck, Merry sneaked up behind him and stabbed the Nazgûl through the leg with an [ancient dagger](../D/Daggers_of_Westernesse.md) that [Tom Bombadil](Tom_Bombadil.md) gave him at the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Bowed over, the creature let out a terrible shriek. Éowyn, with her final strength, slashed at his face with her sword, the blade shattering upon impact. The Witch-king's armour fell shapeless at Éowyn’s feet, and his crown rolled away. Éowyn collapsed on top of the Nazgûl’s remains.

The dying King Théoden appointed [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) as his heir. Éomer, seeing his sister Éowyn’s fallen body, led the Rohirrim in a furious attack. The men of Minas Tirith, led by [Imrahil](../I/Imrahil.md), [Prince of Dol Amroth](../P/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth.md), emerged from the city and drove the enemy from the gate. Théoden's body was taken to the city, along with Éowyn, but Imrahil alerted her rescuers that she was not dead.

The allies of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) reassembled as new soldiers of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) arrived from [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md). The men of Rohan and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) dwindled. As the tide turned against Gondor again, a fleet of black ships appeared on the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) River. The defenders of Gondor turned for the city at the sight of the enemy ships. Éomer, though he realized he was defeated, continued to fight bravely, laughing in a mix of hope and despair.

Suddenly, the frontmost black ship unfurled the [standard of Elendil](../S/Standard_of_Elendil.md) bearing the white tree of Gondor and the seven stars and crown of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md)— the symbols of the ancient high-kingdom of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md). [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) had arrived in the black ships, along with the [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md), and reinforcements from the southern kingdoms.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Wielding the legendary sword [Andúril](../A/Andúril.md), reforged and burning like a star, Aragorn led a fierce battle to save Gondor. The armies of Mordor were defeated, and Aragorn, Éomer, and Imrahil returned to the city. <span></span>
