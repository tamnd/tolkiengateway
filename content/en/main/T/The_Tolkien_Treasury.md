<i><b>The Tolkien Treasury</b></i> is a [2015](/wiki/2015) pocket boxed set published by [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins). The collection includes four pocket-edition books:

- *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*
    - (2014 extended edition, eds. [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) &amp; [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond))
- *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)*
    - (2014 edition, eds. Christina Scull &amp; Wayne G. Hammond)
- *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*
    - (2013 edition, eds. Christina Scull &amp; Wayne G. Hammond)
- *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*
    - (2015 extended edition, ed. [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger))

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>De luxe boxed gift set of Tolkien’s most popular and charming tales; full of wit and humour, giants, dragons, magic and more, they are collected together for the first time and will delight readers of all ages.<br/>
Farmer Giles did not look like a hero. He was fat and red-bearded and enjoyed a slow, comfortable life. Then one day a rather deaf and short-sighted giant blundered on to his land…<br/>
The Adventures of Tom Bombadil collects hobbit-verses from the ‘Red Book’ and other poems relating legends and jests of the Shire at the end of the Third Age.<br/>
Smith of Wootton Major tells of the preparation of the Great Cake to mark the Feast of Good Children and the magical events which follow.<br/>
Roverandom is a real dog who is magically transformed into a toy and is forced to seek out the wizard who wronged him in order to be returned to normal. The story was written to console Tolkien&#39;s four-year-old son, Michael, who lost a beloved toy dog on the beach.
</p>
</blockquote>
