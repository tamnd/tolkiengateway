<i><b>thoron</b></i> / <i><b>thôr</b></i> is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for "eagle".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Arathorn" title="Arathorn">Arathorn</a></i> (-*thor(o)n*; another etymology with *thorn* 'steadfast' also mentioned by Tolkien, see [Arathorn II](/wiki/Arathorn_II#Etymology))
- *[Thorondir](/wiki/Thorondir)*
- *[Thorondor](/wiki/Thorondor)*
- *[Thorongil](/wiki/Thorongil)*
