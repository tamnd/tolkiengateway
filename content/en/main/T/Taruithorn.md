**Taruithorn** was a town in [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. It later became known as **[Oxford](/wiki/Oxford)**.<sup id="cite_ref-Oxf_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Oxf-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the [Faring Forth](/wiki/Faring_Forth), and the removal of Tol Eressëa to the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands), it was conquered by [Horsa](/wiki/Horsa), one of the sons of [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol), the [Mannish](/wiki/Men "Men") mariner who visited the Lonely Isle years prior.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Taruithorn* is in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish). It consists of the elements <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tarog&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tarog (page does not exist)">tarog</a></i> ("ox") + *[tarn](/wiki/Tarn "Tarn")* ("gate"). Its [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognate is <i><b>Taruktarna</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Some of its earlier, rejected names are *Taruitharn* and *Taruithron*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The town was called <i><b>Oxenaford</b></i> in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English).<sup id="cite_ref-Oxf_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Oxf-1">[1]</a></sup>
