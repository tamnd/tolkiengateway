**Taruithorn** was a town in [Tol Eressëa](Tol_Eressëa.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. It later became known as **[Oxford](../O/Oxford.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-Oxf_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Oxf-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the [Faring Forth](../F/Faring_Forth.md), and the removal of Tol Eressëa to the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md), it was conquered by [Horsa](../H/Horsa.md), one of the sons of [Eriol](../E/Eriol.md), the [Mannish](../M/Men.md) mariner who visited the Lonely Isle years prior.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Taruithorn* is in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md). It consists of the elements <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tarog&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tarog (page does not exist)">tarog</a></i> ("ox") + *[tarn](Tarn.md)* ("gate"). Its [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) cognate is <i><b>Taruktarna</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Some of its earlier, rejected names are *Taruitharn* and *Taruithron*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The town was called <i><b>Oxenaford</b></i> in [Old English](../O/Old_English.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Oxf_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Oxf-1">[1]</a></sup>
