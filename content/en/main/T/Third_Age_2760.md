**Third Age 2760** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2760**) is the 2760th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2760 was also known as **S.R. 1160** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- In [The Shire](The_Shire.md), the famine known as the [Days of Dearth](../D/Days_of_Dearth.md) ends.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Dís](../D/Dís.md), third child and daughter of [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Thráin II](Thráin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Hamfast of Gamwich](../H/Hamfast_of_Gamwich.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
