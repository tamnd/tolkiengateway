**The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring** is a 2003 real-time strategy game developed by Liquid Entertainment and published by [Sierra Entertainment](/wiki/Sierra_Entertainment). It expands upon the events of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

*War of the Ring* is based solely on the books, not the [films](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") by [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson). Thus, characters like [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) look and sound different from their counterparts as depicted in the films and the game. The game is officially licensed by [Tolkien Enterprises](/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises "Middle-earth Enterprises").
