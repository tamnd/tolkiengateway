**The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring** is a 2003 real-time strategy game developed by Liquid Entertainment and published by [Sierra Entertainment](../S/Sierra_Entertainment.md). It expands upon the events of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

*War of the Ring* is based solely on the books, not the [films](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) by [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md). Thus, characters like [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) and [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) look and sound different from their counterparts as depicted in the films and the game. The game is officially licensed by [Tolkien Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md).
