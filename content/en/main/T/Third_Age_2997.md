**Third Age 2997** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2997**) is the 2997th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2997 was also known as **S.R. 1397** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Adalbert Bolger](../A/Adalbert_Bolger.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
