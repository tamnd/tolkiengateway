**The Ambarkanta** is the fifth chapter of *[The Shaping of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Shaping_of_Middle-earth)*. The chapter includes the text "Ambarkanta: The Shape of the World" (attributed to [Rúmil](</wiki/R%C3%BAmil_(elf_of_Tirion)> "Rúmil (elf of Tirion)")) and the associated [*Ambarkanta* maps](/wiki/Ambarkanta_maps "Ambarkanta maps").

Developed during the 1930s, according to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), the Ambarkanta represents the "next phase" of the cosmology after the earlier world described in the *[Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
