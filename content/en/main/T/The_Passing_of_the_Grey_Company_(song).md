<i><b>The Passing of the Grey Company</b></i> is the third track of the album [Minas Morgul](</wiki/Minas_Morgul_(album)> "Minas Morgul (album)") by the band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning). The song name is taken from the chapter of the [same name](/wiki/The_Passing_of_the_Grey_Company "The Passing of the Grey Company") in [The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King). The lyrics directly reference [Aragorn taking the Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Aragorn_Takes_the_Paths_of_the_Dead "Aragorn Takes the Paths of the Dead"), including the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company), [Stone of Erech](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech), and [Oathbreakers](/wiki/Oathbreakers).

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Over the land there lies a long shadow,<br/>
westward reaching wings of darkness.<br/>
The tower trembles,to the tombs of kings<br/>
doom approaches.The dead awaken,<br/>
for the hour is come for the oathbreakers;<br/>
at the stone of Erech they shall stand again<br/>
and hear there a horn in the hills ringing.<br/>
<br/>
Whose shall the horn be? Who shall call them<br/>
from grey twilight,the forgotten people?<br/>
the heir of him to whom the oath they swore.<br/>
From the North shall he come,need shall drive him.<br/>
He shall pass the door to the path of the dead.<br/>
<br/>
On your knees...<br/>
The grey company is arriving now...
</p>
