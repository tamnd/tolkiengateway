<i><b>The Iron Prison</b></i> is a text-adventure game designed by [Sam Ruby](../S/Sam_Ruby.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in which the player needs to infiltrate [Angband](../A/Angband.md), and find and bring back the [Silmaril](../S/Silmarils.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup> You can kill [Ancalagon](../A/Ancalagon.md), [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md>), [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md), [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) and [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup>
