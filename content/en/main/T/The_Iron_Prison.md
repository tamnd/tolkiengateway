<i><b>The Iron Prison</b></i> is a text-adventure game designed by [Sam Ruby](/wiki/Sam_Ruby),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in which the player needs to infiltrate [Angband](/wiki/Angband), and find and bring back the [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmarils "Silmarils").<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup> You can kill [Ancalagon](/wiki/Ancalagon), [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)> "Gothmog (balrog)"), [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant), [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup>
