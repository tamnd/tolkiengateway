**Third Age 1864** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1864**) is the 1864th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 1864 was also known as **S.R. 264** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md), son of [Araphant](../A/Araphant.md) of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
