**The Foundations of Stone** is the first scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The film begins with an aerial view of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). We then see a recap of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)'s confrontation with the [Balrog](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane "Durin's Bane") in the scene "[The Bridge of Khazad-dûm](</wiki/The_Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm_(scene)> "The Bridge of Khazad-dûm (scene)")" from [*The Fellowship of the Ring*](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring"). Gandalf tells the Balrog it cannot pass, then breaks the [bridge](/wiki/Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm "Bridge of Khazad-dûm"), sending it falling into the abyss. However, as it falls, it swings its whip upwards and pulls Gandalf over the edge.

Gandalf and the Balrog fall together, fighting, into a seemingly bottomless chasm, until finally they plunge into water. At that moment, the scene switches to [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") in the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil), where Frodo jerks awake, calling Gandalf's name. Reassuring Sam that nothing is wrong, just a dream, Frodo lies down again.

## Differences {#Differences}

The confrontation at the Bridge of Khazad-dûm follows the account in the chapter [The Bridge of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/The_Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm) from *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* fairly closely. Gandalf's fall into the abyss with the Balrog reflects the beginning of Gandalf's account in the chapter [The White Rider](/wiki/The_White_Rider) from *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*: "*Long I fell, and he fell with me. His fire was about me. I was burned. Then we plunged into the deep water and all was dark*."<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The main deviation from the source material is presenting this fall separately from the [Battle of the Peak](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Peak), which will be shown later.

Frodo does not dream about Gandalf's fight with the Balrog in the book. However, in *The Fellowship of the Ring*, he has what appears to be a visionary dream about Gandalf's imprisonment in [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
