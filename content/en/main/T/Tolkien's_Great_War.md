<i><b>Tolkien&#39;s Great War</b></i> is a [2014](/wiki/2014) documentary film about the experiences of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and his closest friends in the First World War. Made by two former pupils of [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School), Zander and Elliot Weaver, the film-makers interview [John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth), Simon Stacey and Paul Golightly.
