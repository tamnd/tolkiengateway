**Twilight** was the soft light in the sky seen before the rising and (especially) after the setting of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun).

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) it is *[uial](/wiki/Uial "Uial")* or *[tinnu](/wiki/Tinnu "Tinnu")*. There are several words for it in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), including <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Y%C3%BAcal%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Yúcalë (page does not exist)">yúcalë</a></i>, <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Yual%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Yualë (page does not exist)">yualë</a></i>, and *[lómë](/wiki/L%C3%B3m%C3%AB "Lómë")*. Morning twilight is also designated in Quenya: the name *[tindómë](/wiki/Tind%C3%B3m%C3%AB "Tindómë")*; and the evening twilight, *[undómë](/wiki/Und%C3%B3m%C3%AB "Undómë")*.
