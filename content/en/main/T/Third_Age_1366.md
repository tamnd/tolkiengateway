**Third Age 1366** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1366**) is the 1366th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Rómendacil II](../R/Rómendacil_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-App_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Valacar](../V/Valacar.md) becomes the twentieth [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-App_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-1">[1]</a></sup>
