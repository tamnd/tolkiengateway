<i><b>The Mythology of Middle-earth</b></i> is [Ruth S. Noel](/wiki/Ruth_S._Noel)'s first book on the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). It is largely overshadowed by her second work, *[The Languages of Tolkien's Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Languages_of_Tolkien%27s_Middle-earth)*. The book focuses on the basics of Tolkien's mythological influences. It does not go as in-depth into the analysis as some other published works.
