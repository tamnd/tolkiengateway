**Taurnil**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was an [Elven](../E/Elves.md) warrior who fought in [the Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md).

Taurnil is an original creation for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* film.

## History {#History}

Taurnil was an [Elven](../E/Elves.md) archer of [the Third Age](Third_Age.md). He was sharp-eyed and his precise aim helped him disarm enemies at long ranges.

When the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md), under leadership from [Théoden](Théoden.md) [King](../K/King_of_Rohan.md), retreated to [the Hornburg](../H/Hornburg.md) in [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md), [Saruman the White](../S/Saruman.md) sent an army of over ten thousand [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) to assail them. When Lord [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and Lady [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and Lord [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) of Lothlórien heard of this, they decided to honour the ages-old allegiance between [Men](../M/Men.md) and [Elves](../E/Elves.md). They sent a join force of Elves, led by [Haldir](../H/Haldir.md), to assist the Rohirrim in their battle against the Uruks. Taurnil was part of this force sent to the Hornburg. When the battle commenced, Taurnil fired arrows at the Uruk-hai.

Ultimately, Haldir and the rest of the Elven reinforcements were slain during the Battle. Their efforts were not in vain, however, as the few Rohirrim left alive were able to hold out until [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) arrived with [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md)'s [Éored](../_/Éored.md) and forced the Uruks to retreat into the forest, where they were killed by [Huorns](../H/Huorns.md).
