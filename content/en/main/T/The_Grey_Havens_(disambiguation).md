## Locations {#Locations}

- *[Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens)*, or *Mithlond*, the harbours on the western shores of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

## Chapters {#Chapters}

- *[The Grey Havens](/wiki/The_Grey_Havens)*, the final chapter of the second book of *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*.

- *[The Grey Havens (scene)](</wiki/The_Grey_Havens_(scene)>)*, the scene from [The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King).
