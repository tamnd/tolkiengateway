<i><b>The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack</b></i> is an album comprising of the soundtrack from *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)*, by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore). It was released digitally and on CD on [11 December](/wiki/11_December) [2012](/wiki/2012).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
