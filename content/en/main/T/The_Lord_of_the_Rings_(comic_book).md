**The Lord of the Rings** is a [1989](/wiki/1989) three issue comic book series, illustrated by Spanish artist [Luis Bermejo](/wiki/Luis_Bermejo), with the story adapted by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Cuti" title="wikipedia:Nick Cuti">Nicola Cuti</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was published in several European languages, but not in English, likely due to copyright issues.<sup id="cite_ref-tolkienlibrary_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tolkienlibrary-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Dutch version states that these were based on the [1978 film](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"), and like that adaptation the story ends halfway through *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*.<sup id="cite_ref-tolkienlibrary_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tolkienlibrary-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
