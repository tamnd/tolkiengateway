**Third Age 3013** (abbreviated to **T.A. 3013**) is the 3013th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 3013 was also known as **S.R. 1413** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Will Whitfoot](../W/Will_Whitfoot.md) is elected [Mayor of Michel Delving](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ordering_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ordering-1">[1]</a></sup>
