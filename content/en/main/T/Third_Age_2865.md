**Third Age 2865** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2865**) is the 2865th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2865 was also known as **S.R. 1265** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Primrose Boffin](../P/Primrose_Boffin.md), fourth child and only daughter of [Otto Boffin](../O/Otto_Boffin.md) and [Lavender Grubb](../L/Lavender_Grubb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
