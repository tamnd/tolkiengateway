**Tolman "Tom" Cotton, Jr** was the eldest son of [Farmer Cotton](Tolman_Cotton_Senior.md).

## History {#History}

When Tom was young, he was known as "Young Tom" to distinguish him from his father with whom they had the same name. Father and son fought in the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> After the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), he married [Sam Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)'s youngest sister, [Marigold](../M/Marigold_Gamgee.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

He briefly served as [Deputy-Mayor](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md) in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1442](../F/Fourth_Age_21.md), when Sam traveled to [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tolman* comes from [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *tōl* + *mann* meaning "tool person".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In early phases of writing [Appendix F](../A/Appendix_F.md), his father's original [Westron](../W/Westron.md) name was given as *Tomacca Lothran*<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> or (earlier) *Tomakli Lothron*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
