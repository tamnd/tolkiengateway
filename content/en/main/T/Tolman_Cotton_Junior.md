**Tolman "Tom" Cotton, Jr** was the eldest son of [Farmer Cotton](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior").

## History {#History}

When Tom was young, he was known as "Young Tom" to distinguish him from his father with whom they had the same name. Father and son fought in the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> After the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), he married [Sam Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee")'s youngest sister, [Marigold](/wiki/Marigold_Gamgee "Marigold Gamgee").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

He briefly served as [Deputy-Mayor](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving "Mayor of Michel Delving") in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1442](/wiki/Fourth_Age_21 "Fourth Age 21"), when Sam traveled to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tolman* comes from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *tōl* + *mann* meaning "tool person".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In early phases of writing [Appendix F](/wiki/Appendix_F), his father's original [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name was given as *Tomacca Lothran*<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> or (earlier) *Tomakli Lothron*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
