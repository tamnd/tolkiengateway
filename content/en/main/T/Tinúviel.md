**Tinúviel** ([S](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[tiˈnuːvjel]</span>) is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) poetic term, though not a literal name, for the '[Nightingale](/wiki/Nightingale)'. This name was first given to [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) by [Beren](/wiki/Beren) when he first saw her dancing in the forest.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *tindômiselde*. Literally it means "Daughter of Twilight".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It can be analyzed as <i><a href="/wiki/Tinnu" title="Tinnu">tinnu</a>(w) + <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sell&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sell (page does not exist)">sell</a></i>.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
