**The Grey Havens** is the ninth and final chapter of the second book of *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*, the final volume of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

## Summary {#Summary}

Now that [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")'s brief police state had been overthrown,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) rebuilt the villages of the region. [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") opened the box [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) had given him<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and found a small silver seed, which he planted. In the [Party Field](/wiki/Party_Field), a [Mallorn](/wiki/Mallorn) sprang up to replace the old tree that had been cut down. Many children were born that year. [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") became heroes in the Shire, but [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") quietly retired. That spring, Sam married [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton), [Farmer Cotton](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior")'s daughter, and they lived at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) with Frodo.

Frodo decided to travel to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to see [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins). Frodo entrusted to Sam a history of the War, written in part by Bilbo. Frodo, Sam, and others set out. As they entered the [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End), they met [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), who now wore two of the [Three Elven Rings](/wiki/Three_Rings "Three Rings"). Riding slowly behind the two elves was Bilbo himself. Sam and Frodo accompanied the travellers to the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer"). When they reached the gates of the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens), they found [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), wearing the [last](/wiki/Narya "Narya") of the Three Rings, waiting for them. Beyond him was a great white ship, ready to sail to the West across the sea.

Pippin and Merry appeared, wishing to be present at Frodo's departure. Frodo sadly bade farewell to his three friends, kissed them, and boarded the ship. Gandalf entreated the three hobbits to enjoy each other's friendship as they quietly returned to the Shire. Sam entered his warm home, where he found [Rosie](/wiki/Rosie_Cotton "Rosie Cotton") waiting. She put their young daughter, [Elanor](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner "Elanor Gardner"), in his lap, and Sam drew a deep breath and said, "Well, I'm back." <span></span>
