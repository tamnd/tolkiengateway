Dr. theol. **Thomas Fornet-Ponse** is a research assistant at the seminar of Fundamental Theology at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn" title="wikipedia:University of Bonn">University of Bonn</a>. Among his research interests are philosophical and theological analyses of J.R.R. Tolkien and other authors of fantasy works. He is a committee member of the [German Tolkien Society](/wiki/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft_e.V. "Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft e.V.") and is editor of the journal *[Hither Shore](</wiki/Hither_Shore_(journal)> "Hither Shore (journal)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
