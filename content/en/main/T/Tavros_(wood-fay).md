**Tavros** was the chief [wood-fay](/wiki/Tavari "Tavari"), described as the "Blue Spirit of the Woods", according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tav_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tav-1">[1]</a></sup>

His home was called [Inthavros](/wiki/Inthavros).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Tavros* is in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish). It is based on the word <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tavros_(Gnomish_word)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tavros (Gnomish word) (page does not exist)">tavros</a></i> ("forest, wooded land"), used as a proper name.<sup id="cite_ref-Tav_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tav-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In later versions of the legendarium, *Tavros* (and afterwards *[Tauros](/wiki/Tauros)* and *[Tauron](/wiki/Tauron)*) became a name of [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
