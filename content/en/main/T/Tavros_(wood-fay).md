**Tavros** was the chief [wood-fay](Tavari.md), described as the "Blue Spirit of the Woods", according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tav_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tav-1">[1]</a></sup>

His home was called [Inthavros](../I/Inthavros.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Tavros* is in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md). It is based on the word <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tavros_(Gnomish_word)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tavros (Gnomish word) (page does not exist)">tavros</a></i> ("forest, wooded land"), used as a proper name.<sup id="cite_ref-Tav_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tav-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In later versions of the legendarium, *Tavros* (and afterwards *[Tauros](Tauros.md)* and *[Tauron](Tauron.md)*) became a name of [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
