<i><b>tári</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "queen". The literal meaning is "her highness", from *târa* ("high") + feminine personal ending *-ī*.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Airë Tári](../A/Airë_Tári.md)* = "Holy Queen"
- *[Elentári](../E/Elentári.md)* = "Queen of the Stars"
- *[Kementári](../K/Kementári.md)* = "Queen of the Earth"
