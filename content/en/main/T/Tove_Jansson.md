**Tove Jansson** ([1914](/wiki/1914)-[2001](/wiki/2001)) was a Finnish author and artist, most known for her books on <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moomin" title="wikipedia:Moomin">Moomin</a></i>.

In [1962](/wiki/1962), Jansson contributed with the interior illustrations (more than 50 individual pieces) and cover art for the second Swedish edition of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* (*Bilbo – en hobbits äventyr*). These illustrations were also used in the 1973,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> 2003 and 2012<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Finnish editions of *The Hobbit*. Jansson wrote to Mikael Ahlström of the Finnish Tolkien Society in 1992 that "for me, illustrating *The Hobbit* was an adventure". The Moominvalley Museum in Tampere, Finland, foremostly devoted to Jansson's Moomin characters, also holds her original artwork for *The Hobbit*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
