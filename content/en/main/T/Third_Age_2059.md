**Third Age 2059** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2059**) is the 2059th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2059 was also known as **S.R. 459** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Mardil](../M/Mardil.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) adds two leap days before implementing a [revised calendar](../S/Stewards'_Reckoning.md) [the following year](Third_Age_2060.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
