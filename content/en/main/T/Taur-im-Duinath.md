**Taur-im-Duinath**, called the "Forest between Rivers" since it lay between the rivers [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) and [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion),<sup id="cite_ref-index_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-2">[2]</a></sup> was the dark and gloomy forest that extended over a vast area of land to the south of the [Andram](/wiki/Andram).<sup id="cite_ref-map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-1">[1]</a></sup> None but a few [Dark Elves](/wiki/Dark_Elves) ever wandered there.<sup id="cite_ref-bel_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bel-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Taur-im-Duinath* literally means "forest between rivers" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), consisting of *[taur](/wiki/Taur "Taur")* ("forest") + *[im](/wiki/Im "Im")* ("between") + *[duin](/wiki/Duin "Duin")* ("river") + *[-ath](/wiki/-ath)* (collective plural suffix).<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the revised version of the second *Silmarillion* map appears the names <i><b>Taur-i-Melegyrn</b></i> ("Forest of the Great Trees") and <i><b>Taur-na-Chardhîn</b></i> ("Forest of the Southern Silence") for this region,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> although it is unknown if they were intended as replacements or alternative names.<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-4">[4]</a></sup>
