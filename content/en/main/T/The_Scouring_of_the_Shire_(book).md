<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: The Scouring of the Shire</b></i> is a sourcebook supplement for [Games Workshop](../G/Games_Workshop.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game](The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game.md)*.

<br>

## From the back {#From_the_back}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Hobbits really are amazing creatures! You can learn all that there is to know about their ways in a month, and yet after a hundred years, they can still surprise you.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Gandalf to Frodo: The Fellowship of the Ring</cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>
<p><i>This supplement is an invaluable guide to collecting, painting and gaming with Games Workshop&#39;s detailed The Lord of The Rings miniatures. Within its colourfully illustrated pages you will find essential rules, information, and inspiration to bring the battles of Middle-earth to your own tabletop.</i>
</p><p>RULES
</p><p>Full rules for new Good and Evil forces, including Hobbit Warriors and Heroes, Sharkey and his band of Ruffians and the likes of Golfimbul and the dreaded White Wargs.
</p><p>SCENARIOS
</p><p>A linked campaign of six scenarios tells the story of Sharkey&#39;s occupation of the Shire and the desperate plight of the Hobbits, from the first Ruffians sneaking through the Shire&#39;s bounds to the Battle of Bywater, the greatest battle ever fought in the Shire. An appendix details some of the more memorable events in the Shire&#39;s history through six additional scenarios, such as the Battle of Greenfields and the Fell Winter.
</p><p>MODELLING TIPS
</p><p>Detailed advice is given on creating the colourful scenery of the Shire, from Hobbit-holes to traps and snares, as well as painting guides for Hobbits, Sharkey&#39;s Ruffians and Warg Riders.
</p>
</blockquote>
