**The Hobbit** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits), the inhabitants of [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire) and [Bree](/wiki/Bree).
- [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), the hobbit referred to by the title of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* book.

## Books {#Books}

- <b><i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i>, the <a href="/wiki/1937" title="1937">1937</a> book by <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>.</b>
- *[The Hobbits: The Many Lives of Bilbo, Frodo, Sam, Merry and Pippin](/wiki/The_Hobbits "The Hobbits")*, a [2012](/wiki/2012) book by [Lynnette Porter](/wiki/Lynnette_Porter).
- [The Hobbit (booklet)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(booklet)>), a commemorative booklet that contains miscellaneous writings.

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

### Films {#Films}

- [*The Hobbit* (1967 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_film)> "The Hobbit (1967 film)"), a 12 minute adaptation by [Gene Deitch](/wiki/Gene_Deitch).
- [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)"), the [1977](/wiki/1977) cartoon adaptation by [Rankin/Bass](/wiki/Rankin/Bass).
- [*The Hobbit* (Russian television film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1985_television_film)> "The Hobbit (1985 television film)"), a [1985](/wiki/1985) Russian television film.
- <b><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>, a film series based on the book,directed by <a href="/wiki/Peter_Jackson" title="Peter Jackson">Peter Jackson</a>:</b>
    - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)*, the [2012](/wiki/2012) film.
    - *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)*, the [2013](/wiki/2013) film.
    - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)*, the [2014](/wiki/2014) film.
