<i><b>The One Ring</b></i> is a role-playing game designed by [Francesco Nepitello](/wiki/Francesco_Nepitello) and published under license from [Sophisticated Games](/wiki/Sophisticated_Games), first by [Cubicle 7 Entertainment](/wiki/Cubicle_7_Entertainment) and later by [Free League](/wiki/Free_League_Publishing "Free League Publishing").

## History {#History}

The game was first released in August [2011](/wiki/2011), featuring artwork by [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe), [Jon Hodgson](/wiki/Jon_Hodgson), and other artists.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TOR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR-2">[2]</a></sup> In November [2019](/wiki/2019), the publisher decided to end the licensing agreement with Sophisticated Games, due to contractual differences.<sup id="cite_ref-TORE_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TORE-3">[3]</a></sup> In [2021](/wiki/2021), a new edition was announced on Kickstarter, for crowdfunding, under the publisher [Free League](/wiki/Free_League_Publishing "Free League Publishing") and began to be shipped to supporters in October of the same year.<sup id="cite_ref-TOR2_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2-4">[4]</a></sup> Having fulfilled all deliveries to the backers, Free League then officially released the RPG for wider retail purchase, on the [22 March](/wiki/22_March) [2022](/wiki/2022).<sup id="cite_ref-TOR2R1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2R1-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TOR2R2_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2R2-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Setting {#Setting}

The initial release (originally planned to be first of a trilogy), *The One Ring: Adventures over the Edge of the Wild*, is based in the area of northern [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood), after the events of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and before the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-TOR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TORC_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TORC-7">[7]</a></sup> The Second Edition of The One Ring is now set in the lone-lands of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), in the year 2965 of the Third Age, moving the focus of the game to the lands of Old [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), with a full description of the town of [Bree](/wiki/Bree), including also famous locales like [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop), [Fornost](/wiki/Fornost) and [Annuminas](/wiki/Annuminas), providing the players with a new starting point for their adventures.<sup id="cite_ref-TOR2_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Game system {#Game_system}

*The One Ring* uses custom dice, specifically a twelve-sided die numbered 1-10, the "Eye of Sauron", and the "Gandalf" Rune (a [Cirth](/wiki/Cirth) G rune, <em style="font-family: &#39;Cirth Erebor&#39;; font-style: normal;">r</em>), and 6 sided dice numbered normally, but with the 1-3 being in outline, 4-5 solid, and 6 solid having also the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) [Lambe](/wiki/Lambe) rune, <span class="tg-tengwar tg-tengwar--tengwar_eldamar">j</span><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395196">@font-face{font-family:"TemplateStyles-TengwarEldamar";src:local("Tengwar Eldarmar"),url("https://tolkiengateway.net/w/resources/assets/fonts/tengwar-eldamar.woff2")format("woff2");font-style:normal;font-weight:normal}.mw-parser-output .tg-tengwar--tengwar_eldamar{font-family:"TemplateStyles-TengwarEldamar"}</style>. Standard 12-sided dice can be used, counting 11 as Sauron, and 12 as Gandalf, and standard 6 sided dice as well.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## In other languages {#In_other_languages}

For the second edition, some publishers of localized versions released exclusive products. Publisher Black Monk released several unique collector's editions, including for titles which never received this treatment in English.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> For the French translation, publisher Edge Studio distributed its own exclusive set of dice.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Mytago, the Czech distributor, has a related webshop named Imago, which sells exclusive dice and resin-printed miniatures, though it's not clear whether these fall under Mytago's license.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
