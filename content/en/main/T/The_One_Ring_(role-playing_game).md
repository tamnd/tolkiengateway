<i><b>The One Ring</b></i> is a role-playing game designed by [Francesco Nepitello](../F/Francesco_Nepitello.md) and published under license from [Sophisticated Games](../S/Sophisticated_Games.md), first by [Cubicle 7 Entertainment](../C/Cubicle_7_Entertainment.md) and later by [Free League](../F/Free_League_Publishing.md).

## History {#History}

The game was first released in August [2011](../_/2011.md), featuring artwork by [John Howe](../J/John_Howe.md), [Jon Hodgson](../J/Jon_Hodgson.md), and other artists.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TOR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR-2">[2]</a></sup> In November [2019](../_/2019.md), the publisher decided to end the licensing agreement with Sophisticated Games, due to contractual differences.<sup id="cite_ref-TORE_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TORE-3">[3]</a></sup> In [2021](../_/2021.md), a new edition was announced on Kickstarter, for crowdfunding, under the publisher [Free League](../F/Free_League_Publishing.md) and began to be shipped to supporters in October of the same year.<sup id="cite_ref-TOR2_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2-4">[4]</a></sup> Having fulfilled all deliveries to the backers, Free League then officially released the RPG for wider retail purchase, on the [22 March](../_/22_March.md) [2022](../_/2022.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TOR2R1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2R1-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TOR2R2_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2R2-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Setting {#Setting}

The initial release (originally planned to be first of a trilogy), *The One Ring: Adventures over the Edge of the Wild*, is based in the area of northern [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md), after the events of *[The Hobbit](The_Hobbit.md)* and before the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TOR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TORC_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TORC-7">[7]</a></sup> The Second Edition of The One Ring is now set in the lone-lands of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md), in the year 2965 of the Third Age, moving the focus of the game to the lands of Old [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md), with a full description of the town of [Bree](../B/Bree.md), including also famous locales like [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md), [Fornost](../F/Fornost.md) and [Annuminas](../A/Annuminas.md), providing the players with a new starting point for their adventures.<sup id="cite_ref-TOR2_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TOR2-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Game system {#Game_system}

*The One Ring* uses custom dice, specifically a twelve-sided die numbered 1-10, the "Eye of Sauron", and the "Gandalf" Rune (a [Cirth](../C/Cirth.md) G rune, <em style="font-family: &#39;Cirth Erebor&#39;; font-style: normal;">r</em>), and 6 sided dice numbered normally, but with the 1-3 being in outline, 4-5 solid, and 6 solid having also the [Tengwar](Tengwar.md) [Lambe](../L/Lambe.md) rune, <span class="tg-tengwar tg-tengwar--tengwar_eldamar">j</span>. Standard 12-sided dice can be used, counting 11 as Sauron, and 12 as Gandalf, and standard 6 sided dice as well.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## In other languages {#In_other_languages}

For the second edition, some publishers of localized versions released exclusive products. Publisher Black Monk released several unique collector's editions, including for titles which never received this treatment in English.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> For the French translation, publisher Edge Studio distributed its own exclusive set of dice.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Mytago, the Czech distributor, has a related webshop named Imago, which sells exclusive dice and resin-printed miniatures, though it's not clear whether these fall under Mytago's license.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
