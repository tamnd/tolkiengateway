<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack</b></i> is an album comprising of the soundtrack from the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*. It was released [10 December](/wiki/10_December) [2002](/wiki/2002). An extended soundtrack, based on the extended edition of the film, was later released as *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers - The Complete Recordings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_-_The_Complete_Recordings)*.
