<i><b>Tolkien and the Spirit of the Age</b></i> is a collection of the papers presented at the Lustrum Celebration of the Dutch Tolkien Society "[Unquendor](/wiki/Unquendor)" (1: 1986: Leiden).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The publication also includes J.R.R. Tolkien's poem "[Progress in Bimble Town](/wiki/Progress_in_Bimble_Town)" (p. 22).
