**Third Age 2840** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2840**) is the 2840th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2840 was also known as **S.R. 1240** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Hildigrim Took](../H/Hildigrim_Took.md), fourth child and third son of [Thain](Thain.md) [Gerontius Took](../G/Gerontius_Took.md) and [Adamanta Chubb](../A/Adamanta_Chubb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
