**Third Age 2986** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2986**) is the 2986th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2986 was also known as **S.R. 1386** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Bowman Cotton](../B/Bowman_Cotton.md), fourth child and third son of [Tolman Cotton Senior](Tolman_Cotton_Senior.md) and [Lily Brown](../L/Lily_Brown.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Death of [Jago Boffin](../J/Jago_Boffin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
