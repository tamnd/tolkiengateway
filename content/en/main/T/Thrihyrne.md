The **Thrihyrne** was a mountain with three peaks<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> with steep faces that looked like jagged horns<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-2">[2]</a></sup> at the head of the deep gorge of [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> on the northernmost arm of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Thrihyrne cast its shadow over the gorge of Helm's Deep.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Thrihyrne* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name,<sup id="cite_ref-RC_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-4">[4]</a></sup> which probably means "Three Horns".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> It is a combination of *thri* ("three") and *hyrne* ("horn", "corner", "angle").<sup id="cite_ref-RC_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-4">[4]</a></sup>
