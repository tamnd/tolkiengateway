**Tarnost** was a location in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) on the northern foothills of the mountains of [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas) between the river [Gilrain](/wiki/Gilrain) to the east and [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth) to the west. To the northwest was the crossing over the river [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3). The location is not mentioned in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Tarnost does not appear in the close-up map of Gondor in *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The first reference is in *[The War of the Ring](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Ring)*: On the western part of [the Second Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Second_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Second Map of The Lord of the Rings") there is a dot visible in square Q12 at the northern tip of the hills southeast of the crossings of the Ringló,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) states that the map had penciled note calling it *Tarnost*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The second reference appears in *[Sauron Defeated](/wiki/Sauron_Defeated)*: In a manuscript Tolkien stated that "From [Erech](/wiki/Erech) a road ran by \[the\] sea, skirting in a loop the **hills of Tarnost**, and so to [Ethir Anduin](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin) and the [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin)".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond "Wayne G. Hammond") and [Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull "Christina Scull") suggest that *Tarnost* appears to be an earlier name for *[Ethring](/wiki/Ethring)*, as they share a "similar position on the published map".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> However, on the published [Map of Rohan, Gondor, and Mordor](/wiki/Map_of_Rohan,_Gondor,_and_Mordor) Ethring is directly at the crossing over the river Ringló and directly at the road that runs from Erech to [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) instead of the foothills of the mountains of Belfalas, which are further to the south.

Tarnost appears on the map of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) in *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth)*, identified by [Wynn Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad "Karen Wynn Fonstad") as a city in approximately the same location as on the Second Map of The Lord of the Rings. The nearby hills are also named as the Hills of Tarnost.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It has been suggested that *Tarnost* is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) name, the second element perhaps being *[ost](/wiki/Ost "Ost")* ("city").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
