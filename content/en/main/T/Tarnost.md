**Tarnost** was a location in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) on the northern foothills of the mountains of [Belfalas](../B/Belfalas.md) between the river [Gilrain](../G/Gilrain.md) to the east and [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md) to the west. To the northwest was the crossing over the river [Ringló](../R/Ringló.md). The location is not mentioned in *[The Lord of the Rings](The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Tarnost does not appear in the close-up map of Gondor in *[The Return of the King](The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The first reference is in *[The War of the Ring](The_War_of_the_Ring.md)*: On the western part of [the Second Map of The Lord of the Rings](The_Second_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) there is a dot visible in square Q12 at the northern tip of the hills southeast of the crossings of the Ringló,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) states that the map had penciled note calling it *Tarnost*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The second reference appears in *[Sauron Defeated](../S/Sauron_Defeated.md)*: In a manuscript Tolkien stated that "From [Erech](../E/Erech.md) a road ran by \[the\] sea, skirting in a loop the **hills of Tarnost**, and so to [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md) and the [Lebennin](../L/Lebennin.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) suggest that *Tarnost* appears to be an earlier name for *[Ethring](../E/Ethring.md)*, as they share a "similar position on the published map".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> However, on the published [Map of Rohan, Gondor, and Mordor](../M/Map_of_Rohan,_Gondor,_and_Mordor.md) Ethring is directly at the crossing over the river Ringló and directly at the road that runs from Erech to [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) instead of the foothills of the mountains of Belfalas, which are further to the south.

Tarnost appears on the map of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) in *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth.md)*, identified by [Wynn Fonstad](../K/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad.md) as a city in approximately the same location as on the Second Map of The Lord of the Rings. The nearby hills are also named as the Hills of Tarnost.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It has been suggested that *Tarnost* is a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) name, the second element perhaps being *[ost](../O/Ost.md)* ("city").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
