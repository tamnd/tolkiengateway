**Third Age 2954** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2954**) is the 2954th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2954 was also known as **S.R. 1354** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md) bursts into flame again. The last inhabitants of [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) flee over [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Hilda Bracegirdle](../H/Hilda_Bracegirdle.md), second child and only daughter of [Bruno Bracegirdle](../B/Bruno_Bracegirdle.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
