<b><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</i> (extended edition)</b> is an extended version of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*. It has 44 minutes of additional footage added back into the film (bringing its total running time up to 223 minutes), as well as multiple documentaries about the making of the film as supplements.

## Special Extended Edition: Part One (disc 1) {#Special_Extended_Edition:_Part_One_(disc_1)}

The first disc contains the extended version of the film with the following audio commentaries:

- [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson), [Fran Walsh](/wiki/Fran_Walsh), and [Philippa Boyens](/wiki/Philippa_Boyens)
- The Design Team
- The Production and Post-Production Team
- The Cast
