**Third Age 2913** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2913**) is the 2913th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2913 was also known as **S.R. 1313** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Asphodel Brandybuck](../A/Asphodel_Brandybuck.md), fifth child and first daughter of [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](../G/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Mirabella Took](../M/Mirabella_Took.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Bruno Bracegirdle](../B/Bruno_Bracegirdle.md), first child and son of [Blanco Bracegirdle](../B/Blanco_Bracegirdle.md) and [Primrose Boffin](../P/Primrose_Boffin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
