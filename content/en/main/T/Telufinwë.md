<i><b>Telufinwë</b></i> was the [essë](/wiki/Ess%C3%AB "Essë") of [Amras](/wiki/Amras), youngest [son](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor") of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor). It means "Last \[of\] Finwë " in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The compound *Telufinwë* likely consists of a form of *tella* ("hindmost, last")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> + *[Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB)*.
