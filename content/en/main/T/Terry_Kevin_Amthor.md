**Terry Kevin Amthor** ([18 October](/wiki/18_October) [1958](/wiki/1958) – [25 September](/wiki/25_September) [2021](/wiki/2021)) was an American author of role-playing games. In [1980](/wiki/1980), he was one of the founders of [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises). Though primarily known for his Shadow World campaign setting, he also worked on many supplements for [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
