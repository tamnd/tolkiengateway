<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Online: Mordor</b></i> is the sixth expansion for *[The Lord of the Rings Online](The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)*. Features included:

- The region Plateau of [Gorgoroth](../G/Gorgoroth.md) includes five different areas, <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../U/Udûn.md" title="Udûn">Udûn</a>, Dor Amarth, Lhingris, Talath Urui, and Agarnaith.
- The conclusion of the Epic Questline (and Volume IV), with Book IX - The Ring of Power.
- A new series of quests, the "Black Book of Mordor", becomes the main storyline after the conclusion of the Epic Quests. There are four chapters introduced in this update.
- The Allegiance System is introduced, players can pledge to one of four factions to gain greater rewards in [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). They are the Kingdom of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), the Court of [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md), [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md), and the Hobbits of the Company.
- A new instance cluster is introduced, featuring a 3-man instance (The Court of Seregost), a 6-man instance (The Dungeons of Naerband), and a 12-man raid (The Abyss of Mordath).

## Updates {#Updates}

### Update 22: Legacy of the Necromancer {#Update_22:_Legacy_of_the_Necromancer}

*Legacy of the Necromancer* is the twenty-second update, released on [6 March](../_/6_March.md), [2018](../_/2018.md).

- The new region "[Eryn Lasgalen](../E/Eryn_Lasgalen.md) and the Dale-lands" (otherwise known as the "Strongholds of the North" or "Northern Mirkwood"), features the Forest of Eryn Lasgalen (including the [Halls of the Elven-king](../H/Halls_of_the_Elven-king.md) (known as Felegoth), The Dale-lands (featuring iconic locations such as The [Long Lake](../L/Long_Lake.md), [Lake-town](../L/Lake-town.md), [Dale](../D/Dale.md), and [Ravenhill](../R/Ravenhill.md)), and [Erebor](../E/Erebor.md), the Lonely Mountain.
- Three new reputation factions are introduced; the "Elves of Felegoth", the "Men of Dale", and the "Dwarves of Erebor".
- The Black Book of Mordor continues with two new chapters exploring the new region.

### Update 23: Where Dragons Dwell {#Update_23:_Where_Dragons_Dwell}

*Where Dragons Dwell* is the twenty-third update, released on [9 October](../_/9_October.md), [2018](../_/2018.md).

- The new area "The Dwarf-holds", consists of two areas; The [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md), and [Ered Mithrin](../E/Ered_Mithrin.md). An expanded area of [Erebor](../E/Erebor.md) is also opened.
- A new instance cluster is introduced, featuring two 3-man instances (Caverns of Thrumfall and Glimmerdeep), a six-man instance (Thikil-gundu), and a 12-man raid (The Anvil of Winterstith).
- The Black Book of Mordor continues with two new chapters exploring the new areas.

### Update 24: Vales of Anduin {#Update_24:_Vales_of_Anduin}

*Vales of Anduin* is the twenty-fourth update, released on [4 June](../_/4_June.md), [2019](../_/2019.md).

- A new region, the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md) was released, containing several key locations such as the Beorninghus ([Beorn's Hall](../B/Beorn's_Hall.md)), The [Carrock](../C/Carrock.md), the [Eagle's Eyrie](../E/Eagle's_Eyrie.md), the [Gladden Fields](../G/Gladden_Fields.md), [Rhosgobel](../R/Rhosgobel.md), and various settlements of the [Woodmen](../W/Woodmen.md).
- Update 24.2 brought the "Gladdenmere" sub-region to the Vales, along with a single six-man instance, "The Depths of Kidzul-kalah.
- The Black Book of Mordor continues with two new chapters exploring the new area, in addition to a "Prelude" to the Minas Morgul Expansion.
