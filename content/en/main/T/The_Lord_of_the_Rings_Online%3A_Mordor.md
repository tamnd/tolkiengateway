<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Online: Mordor</b></i> is the sixth expansion for *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*. Features included:

- The region Plateau of [Gorgoroth](/wiki/Gorgoroth) includes five different areas, <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Ud%C3%BBn" title="Udûn">Udûn</a>, Dor Amarth, Lhingris, Talath Urui, and Agarnaith.
- The conclusion of the Epic Questline (and Volume IV), with Book IX - The Ring of Power.
- A new series of quests, the "Black Book of Mordor", becomes the main storyline after the conclusion of the Epic Quests. There are four chapters introduced in this update.
- The Allegiance System is introduced, players can pledge to one of four factions to gain greater rewards in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). They are the Kingdom of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), the Court of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk), and the Hobbits of the Company.
- A new instance cluster is introduced, featuring a 3-man instance (The Court of Seregost), a 6-man instance (The Dungeons of Naerband), and a 12-man raid (The Abyss of Mordath).

## Updates {#Updates}

### Update 22: Legacy of the Necromancer {#Update_22:_Legacy_of_the_Necromancer}

*Legacy of the Necromancer* is the twenty-second update, released on [6 March](/wiki/6_March), [2018](/wiki/2018).

- The new region "[Eryn Lasgalen](/wiki/Eryn_Lasgalen) and the Dale-lands" (otherwise known as the "Strongholds of the North" or "Northern Mirkwood"), features the Forest of Eryn Lasgalen (including the [Halls of the Elven-king](/wiki/Halls_of_the_Elven-king) (known as Felegoth), The Dale-lands (featuring iconic locations such as The [Long Lake](/wiki/Long_Lake), [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town), [Dale](/wiki/Dale), and [Ravenhill](/wiki/Ravenhill)), and [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor), the Lonely Mountain.
- Three new reputation factions are introduced; the "Elves of Felegoth", the "Men of Dale", and the "Dwarves of Erebor".
- The Black Book of Mordor continues with two new chapters exploring the new region.

### Update 23: Where Dragons Dwell {#Update_23:_Where_Dragons_Dwell}

*Where Dragons Dwell* is the twenty-third update, released on [9 October](/wiki/9_October), [2018](/wiki/2018).

- The new area "The Dwarf-holds", consists of two areas; The [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills), and [Ered Mithrin](/wiki/Ered_Mithrin). An expanded area of [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor) is also opened.
- A new instance cluster is introduced, featuring two 3-man instances (Caverns of Thrumfall and Glimmerdeep), a six-man instance (Thikil-gundu), and a 12-man raid (The Anvil of Winterstith).
- The Black Book of Mordor continues with two new chapters exploring the new areas.

### Update 24: Vales of Anduin {#Update_24:_Vales_of_Anduin}

*Vales of Anduin* is the twenty-fourth update, released on [4 June](/wiki/4_June), [2019](/wiki/2019).

- A new region, the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin) was released, containing several key locations such as the Beorninghus ([Beorn's Hall](/wiki/Beorn%27s_Hall)), The [Carrock](/wiki/Carrock), the [Eagle's Eyrie](/wiki/Eagle%27s_Eyrie), the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields), [Rhosgobel](/wiki/Rhosgobel), and various settlements of the [Woodmen](/wiki/Woodmen).
- Update 24.2 brought the "Gladdenmere" sub-region to the Vales, along with a single six-man instance, "The Depths of Kidzul-kalah.
- The Black Book of Mordor continues with two new chapters exploring the new area, in addition to a "Prelude" to the Minas Morgul Expansion.
