<i><b>The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power (Season One: Amazon Original Series Soundtrack)</b></i> is an album which was composed by [Bear McCreary](/wiki/Bear_McCreary) for the first season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* television series. It was released online on [19 August](/wiki/19_August), [2022](/wiki/2022) by Amazon Music.
