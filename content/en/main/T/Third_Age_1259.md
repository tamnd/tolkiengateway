**Third Age 1259** (abbreviated to **T.A. 1259**) is the 1259th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Castamir](../C/Castamir.md), great-grandson of [Calmacil](<../C/Calmacil_(King_of_Gondor).md>) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
