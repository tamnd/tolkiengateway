**Thuringwethil** was a [Vampire](/wiki/Vampire) servant of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), and a possible [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar").<sup id="cite_ref-etymology_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etymology-1">[1]</a></sup><sup><a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Notes">NB</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Thuringwethil was Sauron's messenger, but was caught in the battle between her master, [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) and [Huan](/wiki/Huan) at [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth) ("Isle of Werewolves").

She was slain either by Huan or in the collapse of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith_in_Beleriand "Minas Tirith in Beleriand"). Lúthien later used her cloak to sneak into [Angband](/wiki/Angband) during the [Quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Because of Thuringwethil's ability to change forms, she may have been a [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar")<sup id="cite_ref-etymology_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etymology-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> or some sort of [skin-changer](/wiki/Skin-changer "Skin-changer")<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name <i><b>Thuringwethil</b></i> is glossed as "Woman of Secret Shadow"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> or "she of hidden shadow".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

The above can be analyzed as *[thurin](/wiki/Thurin "Thurin")* ("secret, hidden"), *[gwath](/wiki/Gwath "Gwath")* ("shadow") and an obscure ending *[-il](/wiki/-il)* which perhaps denotes feminine gender (and which causes "[umlauting](/wiki/I-affection "I-affection")" (or "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affection_(linguistics)" title="wikipedia:Affection (linguistics)">affection</a>") of *gwath* to *gweth*).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

In the earlier versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), *Thuringwethil* was an [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin) name, with *Dolwethil* being its [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) cognate.<sup id="cite_ref-etymology_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etymology-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The character of Thuringwethil appears for the first time in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in the *[Lay of Leithian](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian)*. Thus, in *[The Tale of Tinúviel](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Tin%C3%BAviel)* (the first version of the tale of Beren and Lúthien), [Tinúviel](/wiki/Tin%C3%BAviel) simply wears her dark cloak of magic sleep when entering [Angamandi](/wiki/Angamandi).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>
