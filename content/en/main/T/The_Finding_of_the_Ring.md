**The Finding of the Ring** is the first scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* and [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>). It was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The scene begins with [Sméagol](../G/Gollum.md) inspecting a worm while fishing with [Déagol](../D/Déagol.md) on a riverboat in the middle of a peaceful, forested area. Déagol hooks onto a large fish which drags him underwater. There he sees a glint of gold half buried in the riverbed and, entranced by it, he grabs it.

On coming to the surface and returning to the bank, he discovers in his hand a plain golden [ring](The_One_Ring.md), covered in mud. A cheerful Sméagol comes to Déagol, but upon seeing the ring, immediately lusts for it, saying *"It's my birthday, and I wants it"*. Déagol refuses and the two begin to fight. Sméagol takes it by force, murdering Déagol to do so. Taking it out of Déagol's lifeless hand, a look of pure joy crosses Sméagol's face. *"My precious!"* he says, before putting the ring on for the first time and vanishing.

The scene cuts to a Sméagol who has become entirely dependent upon the ring, uttering *"Gollum!"* while holding the ring. He gradually forgets his name and we witness his transformation and decline into his present-day condition.

## Differences {#Differences}

This scene follows the account of [Sméagol](../G/Gollum.md) and [Déagol](../D/Déagol.md) as told by [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) to [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) in the book chapter [The Shadow of the Past](The_Shadow_of_the_Past.md) very closely in terms of both narrative, and dialogue (after Déagol emerges from underwater). The two [Stoor-hobbits](../S/Stoors.md) indeed go fishing, Déagol is dragged under water and finds the [ring](The_One_Ring.md), and Sméagol strangles him to take it.<sup id="cite_ref-FRShadow_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRShadow-1">[1]</a></sup> Where this scene *differs* from the source material is in what is *not* included, which mainly covers Sméagol's malicious use of the ring upon returning to his family, before being expelled by his grandmother. This results in him eventually ending up under the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-FRShadow_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRShadow-1">[1]</a></sup>
