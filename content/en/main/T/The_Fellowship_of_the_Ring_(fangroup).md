**The Fellowship of the Ring** was the first organized Tolkien club, it was founded by [Ted Johnstone](Ted_Johnstone.md) in [4 September](../_/4_September.md) [1960](../_/1960.md) and held its first meeting at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/18th_World_Science_Fiction_Convention" title="wikipedia:18th World Science Fiction Convention">18th World Science Fiction Convention</a> or *Pittcon*. It dissolved in the mid-60s.
