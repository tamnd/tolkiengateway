The **Magnificent** was a title used of [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) after his return from the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Elvenking](Thranduil.md) called [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) "Bilbo the Magnificent" when Bilbo before departing gave him a present as a return for his "hospitality".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
