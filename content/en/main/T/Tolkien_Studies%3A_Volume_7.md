<b><i>Tolkien Studies</i>: Volume 7</b>, published in [2010](/wiki/2010), is the seventh volume of the annual review *[Tolkien Studies](</wiki/Tolkien_Studies_(journal)> "Tolkien Studies (journal)")*.

[Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger)'s article "'The Story of Kullervo' and Essays on *Kalevala*" published three formerly unpublished texts by J.R.R. Tolkien, one of "The Story of Kullervo" and two drafts of "On the Kalevala". The whole article, revised and enlarged, was published as the book *[The Story of Kullervo](/wiki/The_Story_of_Kullervo)* in [2015](/wiki/2015).
