**The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring – Trick-Taking Game** is a cooperative card game for one to four players. Similar to classic trick-taking games like Bridge and Spades, players play cards matching a lead suit along with characters with unique abilities to get through one of eighteen unique scenarios. Features art by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Elaine_Ryan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Elaine Ryan (page does not exist)">Elaine Ryan</a> and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Samuel_R._Shimota&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Samuel R. Shimota (page does not exist)">Samuel R. Shimota</a>.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

Follow the key story beats of the iconic fantasy tale by [J. R. R. Tolkien](/wiki/J._R._R._Tolkien), starting with [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)’ [Eleventy-First birthday party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") in [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). Players are taken through eighteen total chapters chronologically, focusing on big story moments and the beloved literary characters among them. Each chapter introduces new challenges, characters, and items; slowly revealing cards that build toward the forming of [The Fellowship](/wiki/The_Fellowship). Story cards are used at the beginning of each chapter, featuring actual text from the book, to set the mood as you begin your adventure.

Middle-earth awaits!
