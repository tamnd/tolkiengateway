<i><b>The Evolution of Tolkien&#39;s Mythology: A Study of the History of Middle-earth</b></i> is a book by Elizabeth A. Whittingham on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The History of Middle-earth traces the evolution of J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s literary world, stories, and characters from their earliest written forms to the final revisions Tolkien penned shortly before his death in 1973. Published posthumously by Tolkien&#39;s son Christopher, the extensively detailed 12-volume work allows readers to follow the development of the texts that eventually became Tolkien&#39;s immensely popular The Hobbit, The Lord of The Rings, The Silmarillion, and Unfinished Tales.<br/>
This work provides a thorough study of Tolkien&#39;s life and influences through an analysis of The History of Middle-earth. The work begins with a brief biography and an analysis of the major influences in Tolkien&#39;s life. Following chapters deal with elements common to Tolkien&#39;s popular works, including the cosmogony, theogony, cosmology, metaphysics, and eschatology of Middle-earth. The study also reviews some of the myths with which Tolkien was most familiar--Greek, Roman, Finnish, and Norse--and reveals the often overlapping relationship between mythology, biblical stories, and Tolkien&#39;s popular works.
</p>
</blockquote>
