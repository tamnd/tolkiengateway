**Tolkien 2005: The Ring Goes Ever On** was a convention hosted by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) in conjunction with the [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society). It was held at Aston University (Birmingham, England) [11](/wiki/11_August "11 August")-[15 August](/wiki/15_August), [2005](/wiki/2005). The convention marked the fiftieth anniversary of the complete publication of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
