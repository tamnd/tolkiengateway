<i><b>The Lord of the Rings</b></i> is a film memorabilia card of [Ralph Bakshi](../R/Ralph_Bakshi.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](<The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>)*. The card was released in Italy circa [1993](../_/1993.md)-[1994](../_/1994.md) in the set *Super Cinema Cards* by a company called Due Emme Publishing. In total, 144 cards were released.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The front of the card reproduces the poster of the film. The writing on the back of the card is in English, where is stated the director, producer, writer, music composer and cast, as well as the year of release, of the film. The card measures 4 x 3 1/4" (10 x 8 cm).
