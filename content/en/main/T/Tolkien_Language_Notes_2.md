The magazine includes [Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan)'s\[?\] article "The Quenya Noun" - this includes a transcription of the manuscript page "[The Plotz Declension](/wiki/The_Plotz_Declension)" by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
