**Third Age 2076** (abbreviated to **T.A. 2076**) is the 2076th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Third Age](Third_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Third Age 2076 was also known as **S.R. 476** in [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Imrazôr](../I/Imrazôr.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Galador](../G/Galador.md), son of Imrazôr, becomes first [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-1">[1]</a></sup>
