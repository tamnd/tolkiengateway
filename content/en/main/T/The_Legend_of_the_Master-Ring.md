<i><b>The Legend of the Master-Ring</b></i> is the tenth track of the album [Minas Morgul](</wiki/Minas_Morgul_(album)> "Minas Morgul (album)") by the band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning). The song name is taken from the [One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"). The lyrics are directly taken from the [Ring Verse](/wiki/Ring_Verse).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
