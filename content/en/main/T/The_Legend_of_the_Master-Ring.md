<i><b>The Legend of the Master-Ring</b></i> is the tenth track of the album [Minas Morgul](<../M/Minas_Morgul_(album).md>) by the band [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md). The song name is taken from the [One Ring](The_One_Ring.md). The lyrics are directly taken from the [Ring Verse](../R/Ring_Verse.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
