<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar</b></i> is the soundtrack to *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*. The soundtrack was originally released as part of the special edition to the game. In June 2008 Turbine released a download manager where players who owned the game could download the mp3's of the game which included all these titles plus additional tracks. The majority of the tracks were composed by [Chance Thomas](/wiki/Chance_Thomas).
