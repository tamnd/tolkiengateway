**Lammoth**, the "Great Echo", was a shoreland region far to the northwest of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), north of [Drengist](/wiki/Drengist) and west of the [Ered Lómin](/wiki/Ered_L%C3%B3min).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was where [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) set upon [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in an attempt to gain the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils). As Morgoth was being strangled he cried out in anguish and the echoes of this shout remained in the land thereafter. His cry was heard by the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) from [Angband](/wiki/Angband) who came and rescued their lord from the great spider.<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the first moonrise, when the host of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) arrived in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) after crossing the [Grinding Ice](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB "Helcaraxë"),<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the [Battle of the Lammoth](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Lammoth) was fought here between the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) and a large host of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) as the Elves marched southwards to enter into [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum), resulting in a crushing defeat for Morgoth. However, [Argon](/wiki/Argon), the third son of Fingolfin, perished in that combat.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word, a combination of *lamm* ("echo", related with *lóm* as in *[Dor Lómin](/wiki/Dor_L%C3%B3min)*) with the augmentative suffix *-oth*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> <span></span>
