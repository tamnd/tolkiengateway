The **songs of lamentation** were [elvish](/wiki/Elves "Elves") songs sung in lamentation for [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien). The only excerpt from these songs is "*Mithrandir, Mithrandir, O Pilgrim Grey!*".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

This was expanded and set to music by [Philippa Boyens](/wiki/Philippa_Boyens) and [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore), respectively, for [The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring). It was sung in the said film by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Elizabeth_Fraser&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Elizabeth Fraser (page does not exist)">Elizabeth Fraser</a> in the track <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lothl%C3%B3rien_(song)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lothlórien (song) (page does not exist)">Lothlórien</a></i>. Notable about this song is that it assumes that the elves of Lothlórien were aware that Gandalf was an incarnate Maia. This is debatable. As well, the lyrics ask "*What drove you to leave/That which you loved?*". This suggests that Gandalf was well aware that he would fall in [Moria](/wiki/Moria). Other debatable verses include the claims that Gandalf was the wisest of the Maiar, and that with him the [Flame of Anor](/wiki/Flame_of_Anor) would leave the world (assuming that it and he were one, or he was the only wielder of the Flame).

<h2><span id="Lyrics_in_Peter_Jackson.27s_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Lyrics_in_Peter_Jackson&#39;s_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring">Lyrics in Peter Jackson&#39;s The Fellowship of the Ring</span></h2>

The first part is in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya):

<dl><dd><i>A <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Olórin</a> i yáresse</i><br/><i>Mentaner i Númenherui</i><br/><i>Tírien i Rómenóri </i><br/><i><a href="/wiki/Maiar" title="Maiar">Maiaron</a> i Oiosaila</i><br/><i>Manan elye etevanne</i><br/><i>Nórie i melanelye?</i></dd></dl>

The Second part is in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin):

<dl><dd><i>Mithrandir, Mithrandir, A Randir Vithren</i><br/><i>ú-reniathach i amar galen</i><br/><i>I reniad lín ne mór, nuithannen</i><br/><i>In gwidh ristennin, i fae  narchannen</i><br/><i>I lach <a href="/wiki/Sun" title="Sun">Anor</a> ed ardhon gwannen</i><br/><i>Caled veleg, ethuiannen.</i></dd></dl>

### Translation {#Translation}

<dl><dd><i>Olórin, who once was...</i><br/><i>Sent by the <a href="/wiki/Valar" title="Valar">Lords of the West</a></i><br/><i>To guard the <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">lands of the East</a></i><br/><i>Wisest of all <a href="/wiki/Maiar" title="Maiar">Maiar</a></i><br/><i>What drove you to leave</i><br/><i>That which you loved?</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i>Mithrandir, Mithrandir O Pilgrim Grey</i><br/><i>No more will you wander the green fields of this earth</i><br/><i>Your journey has ended in darkness.</i><br/><i>The bonds cut, the spirit broken</i><br/><i>The <a href="/wiki/Flame_of_Anor" title="Flame of Anor">Flame of Anor</a> has left this <a href="/wiki/Arda" title="Arda">World</a></i><br/><i>A great light, extinguished.</i></dd></dl>

See also *[Frodo's Lament](/wiki/Frodo%27s_Lament)*, another song for Gandalf.
