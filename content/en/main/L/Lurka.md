**Lurka** is an [Orc of the Southlands](/wiki/Orcs_of_Mordor "Orcs of Mordor") who oversaw the captives in the [trenches](/wiki/Orc-holds "Orc-holds"). Lurka was invented by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*, where he is portrayed by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Robert_Strange&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Robert Strange (page does not exist)">Robert Strange</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
