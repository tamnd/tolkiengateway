The **Last Alliance of Elves and Men** was formed in [3430](../S/Second_Age_3430.md) of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) in response to the threat of conquest by the Dark Lord [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Sauron had been contending with the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) for mastery of Middle-earth for over 1200 years. Fearing the establishment of the powerful kingdoms in exile — [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) — by his hated enemies, the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md), Sauron launched a pre-emptive attack on Gondor in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3429](../S/Second_Age_3429.md).

[Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) left [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md) for the north to meet his father and [High King of the Dúnedain](../H/High_King_of_the_Dúnedain.md) [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md), whilst [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md), Elendil's younger son, defended [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) against the hosts of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup>

In response, Elendil formed an alliance with [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), the [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md), to repel Sauron's assault in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3430](../S/Second_Age_3430.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup> [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md) also joined. It is said that Elendil bound the Alliance with an oath and invoked the name of [Eru](../E/Eru.md) to witness it.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup> Eru's name was very rarely invoked in oaths, and among the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) it was held that only the King could call upon Him. This would happen again only thousands of years later during the [Oath of Cirion](../O/Oath_of_Cirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup>

It is said that the hosts of Gil-galad joined Elendil at [Amon Sûl](../W/Weathertop.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-4">[4]</a></sup> and marched towards Imladris in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3431](../S/Second_Age_3431.md) where they camped for three years,<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup> forging armour and making plans.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

Finally in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3434](../S/Second_Age_3434.md) the armies crossed the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) at various locations; Gil-galad and [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) crossed the [Pass of Imladris](../P/Pass_of_Imladris.md) and the [Pass of Caradhras](../P/Pass_of_Caradhras.md), and their hosts were so large that the [Orcs of Mordor](../O/Orcs_of_Mordor.md) sent by Sauron to harry them hid themselves instead.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Their forces were strengthened in the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md) by Elves from [Lothlórien](Lothlórien.md) and [Greenwood the Great](../M/Mirkwood.md) under the command of [Oropher](../O/Oropher.md) and [Amdír](../A/Amdír.md) and by the Dwarves of [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md).

When Sauron learned of their advance, he burned the former [Gardens](../B/Brown_Lands.md) of the [Entwives](../E/Entwives.md) to hinder them down the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Soon they reached [Dagorlad](../D/Dagorlad.md) where they met and defeated Sauron's army in the [Battle of Dagorlad](../B/Battle_of_Dagorlad.md) after months of fierce fighting. Sauron retreated, and the Alliance breached the [Morannon](../M/Morannon.md) into [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md), marching to besiege the Dark Lord's fortress of [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md). The siege lasted for seven years, during which the Alliance sustained heavy losses; among others, [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md) was slain. It culminated in Sauron leaving his fortress and breaking the siege himself. Sauron's last sortie pushed the army of the Alliance back to the slopes of [Orodruin](../O/Orodruin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup>

At the last stage of the [Siege of Barad-dûr](../S/Siege_of_Barad-dûr.md), Sauron slew Gil-galad and Elendil in single combat, but was himself defeated.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup> Isildur cut the Ring from Sauron's finger.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Sauron's body died and his spirit fled. Without the power of the One Ring, Sauron could not take form again in Middle-earth for a thousand years.

With the victory over Sauron and the death of Gil-galad and Elendil, the Last Alliance was dissolved. The remaining Númenóreans resettled their [kingdoms](../R/Realms_in_Exile.md). <span></span>
