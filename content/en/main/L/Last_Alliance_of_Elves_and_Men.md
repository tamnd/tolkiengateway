The **Last Alliance of Elves and Men** was formed in [3430](/wiki/Second_Age_3430 "Second Age 3430") of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) in response to the threat of conquest by the Dark Lord [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Sauron had been contending with the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) for mastery of Middle-earth for over 1200 years. Fearing the establishment of the powerful kingdoms in exile — [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) — by his hated enemies, the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain), Sauron launched a pre-emptive attack on Gondor in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3429](/wiki/Second_Age_3429 "Second Age 3429").

[Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) left [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil) for the north to meet his father and [High King of the Dúnedain](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain) [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), whilst [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion), Elendil's younger son, defended [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) against the hosts of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup>

In response, Elendil formed an alliance with [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), the [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor), to repel Sauron's assault in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3430](/wiki/Second_Age_3430 "Second Age 3430").<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup> [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) also joined. It is said that Elendil bound the Alliance with an oath and invoked the name of [Eru](/wiki/Eru) to witness it.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup> Eru's name was very rarely invoked in oaths, and among the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) it was held that only the King could call upon Him. This would happen again only thousands of years later during the [Oath of Cirion](/wiki/Oath_of_Cirion).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup>

It is said that the hosts of Gil-galad joined Elendil at [Amon Sûl](/wiki/Weathertop "Weathertop")<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-4">[4]</a></sup> and marched towards Imladris in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3431](/wiki/Second_Age_3431 "Second Age 3431") where they camped for three years,<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup> forging armour and making plans.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Angel_Falto_-_Elf_Last_Alliance.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/4/4a/Angel_Falto_-_Elf_Last_Alliance.jpg/200px-Angel_Falto_-_Elf_Last_Alliance.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="86" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/4/4a/Angel_Falto_-_Elf_Last_Alliance.jpg/300px-Angel_Falto_-_Elf_Last_Alliance.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/4/4a/Angel_Falto_-_Elf_Last_Alliance.jpg/400px-Angel_Falto_-_Elf_Last_Alliance.jpg 2x" data-file-width="627" data-file-height="270"/></a><figcaption><i>Last Alliance</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Angel_Falto" title="Category:Images by Angel Falto">Angel Falto</a></figcaption></figure>

Finally in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434") the armies crossed the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) at various locations; Gil-galad and [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) crossed the [Pass of Imladris](/wiki/Pass_of_Imladris) and the [Pass of Caradhras](/wiki/Pass_of_Caradhras), and their hosts were so large that the [Orcs of Mordor](/wiki/Orcs_of_Mordor) sent by Sauron to harry them hid themselves instead.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Their forces were strengthened in the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin) by Elves from [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) and [Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Mirkwood "Mirkwood") under the command of [Oropher](/wiki/Oropher) and [Amdír](/wiki/Amd%C3%ADr) and by the Dwarves of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria").

When Sauron learned of their advance, he burned the former [Gardens](/wiki/Brown_Lands "Brown Lands") of the [Entwives](/wiki/Entwives) to hinder them down the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Soon they reached [Dagorlad](/wiki/Dagorlad) where they met and defeated Sauron's army in the [Battle of Dagorlad](/wiki/Battle_of_Dagorlad) after months of fierce fighting. Sauron retreated, and the Alliance breached the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon) into [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), marching to besiege the Dark Lord's fortress of [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr). The siege lasted for seven years, during which the Alliance sustained heavy losses; among others, [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) was slain. It culminated in Sauron leaving his fortress and breaking the siege himself. Sauron's last sortie pushed the army of the Alliance back to the slopes of [Orodruin](/wiki/Orodruin).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup>

At the last stage of the [Siege of Barad-dûr](/wiki/Siege_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr), Sauron slew Gil-galad and Elendil in single combat, but was himself defeated.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup> Isildur cut the Ring from Sauron's finger.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Sauron's body died and his spirit fled. Without the power of the One Ring, Sauron could not take form again in Middle-earth for a thousand years.

With the victory over Sauron and the death of Gil-galad and Elendil, the Last Alliance was dissolved. The remaining Númenóreans resettled their [kingdoms](/wiki/Realms_in_Exile "Realms in Exile"). <span></span>
