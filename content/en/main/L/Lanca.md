<i><b>lanca</b></i> is a [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) word meaning "sharp edge (not of tools), sudden end, as in e.g. a cliff-edge, or the clean edge of things made by hand or built".<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) (G)LAN ("rim, border, boundary, limit")<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>
