<b><i>Lembas</i> (Vol. 2, No. 10)</b> is an issue of the journal *[Lembas](</wiki/Lembas_(journal)> "Lembas (journal)")*, published by [Unquendor](/wiki/Unquendor). The issue includes [Renée Vink](/wiki/Ren%C3%A9e_Vink)'s article "Tolkien in Rotterdam", about Tolkien's visit to Rotterdam in 1958. The article reproduces a photograph of Tolkien taken at the occasion, and includes a number of quotations from letters by Tolkien (among them, [Letter 205b](/wiki/Letter_205b)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
