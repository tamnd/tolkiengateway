On [5 January](/wiki/5_January) [1961](/wiki/1961), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Madam Ossendrijver**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien writes about the [Dutch translation](/wiki/Max_Schuchart "Max Schuchart") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, and replies to a question about the etymology of the name *[Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor)*, among other things.
- **Description**: Handwritten letter.
- **Publication:** A large excerpt from the letter was published as [Letter 227](/wiki/Letter_227) in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)*.

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

"Númenor, shortened form of *Númenórë*, is my own invention, compounded from *nūme-n* "going down" (√ndū, nū), sunset, West, and *nōre* "land, country" = *Westernesse*"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
