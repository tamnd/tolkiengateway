On [5 January](../_/5_January.md) [1961](../_/1961.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Madam Ossendrijver**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien writes about the [Dutch translation](../M/Max_Schuchart.md) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, and replies to a question about the etymology of the name *[Númenor](../N/Númenor.md)*, among other things.
- **Description**: Handwritten letter.
- **Publication:** A large excerpt from the letter was published as [Letter 227](Letter_227.md) in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*.

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

"Númenor, shortened form of *Númenórë*, is my own invention, compounded from *nūme-n* "going down" (√ndū, nū), sunset, West, and *nōre* "land, country" = *Westernesse*"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
