On [6 December](/wiki/6_December) [1965](/wiki/1965), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to G.S. Rigby Jr.** (of Lansdowne, Pennsylvania).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Sothebys_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sothebys-3">[3]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Acknowledging theological elements in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
- **Description:** Typed letter signed ("J.R.R. Tolkien"), one page (6 3/4 x 5 1/4 in.; 172 x 130 mm).<sup id="cite_ref-Sothebys_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sothebys-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of the letter appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*.

## From the auction {#From_the_auction}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The Lord of the Rings and its Christian/Catholic underpinnings. Tolkien, a devout Catholic, readily admits to theological elements in his famous trilogy. &#34;There is, in fact, quite a lot of theology included in The Lord of the Rings (I was surprised to find how much when the work was analysed some time ago in a theological periodical), though perhaps it  is made more palatable by a sugar coating.&#34; <sup id="cite_ref-Sothebys_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sothebys-3">[3]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>

## Transcript {#Transcript}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>6th December, 1965.<br/><br/>Dear Mr. Rigby,<br/><br/>Thank you very much for your appreciative letter. It gave me much pleasure.<br/><br/>There is, in fact, quite a lot of theology included in The Lord of the Rings (I was surprised to find now much when the work was analysed some time ago in a theological periodical), though perhaps it is made more palatable by a sugar  coating.<br/><br/>With best wishes and thanks.<br/>Yours sincerely,<br/>J. R. R. Tolkien<br/><br/>
</p>
</blockquote>
