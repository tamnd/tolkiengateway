On [7 January](/wiki/7_January) [1966](/wiki/1966), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to Roger Shaw**.

- **Contents:** Tolkien responds to the suggestion that the landscape of *The Lord of the Rings* is Icelandic.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Typed, 1 page, 8vo, Oxford
- **Publication:** A description of the letter was presumably printed in <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sotheby%27s_English_Literature,_History,_Private_Press_and_Children%27s_Books_12_December_2002&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sotheby&#39;s English Literature, History, Private Press and Children&#39;s Books 12 December 2002 (page does not exist)">Sotheby&#39;s English Literature, History, Private Press and Children&#39;s Books 12 December 2002</a></i>.

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

"I have never been to Iceland. Something has always interfered with my plans to go there. So my acquaintance with its landscape is derived entirely from photographs... One point of influence at any rate is certain, namely, the [Midgewater Marshes](/wiki/Midgewater_Marshes), the name of which is of course merely a translation of Mývatn..."
