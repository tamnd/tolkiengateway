The **Lithedays** or just **The Lithe** refers to the three (sometimes four) feast days between the months of [Forelithe](/wiki/Forelithe) and [Afterlithe](/wiki/Afterlithe) of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar); in purpose and function the Lithedays were equivalent to the *[enderi](/wiki/Enderi "Enderi")* of the [Reckoning of Rivendell](/wiki/Reckoning_of_Rivendell).

They were:

- 1 Lithe (midsummer-eve)
- [Mid-year's Day](/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day)
    - [Overlithe](/wiki/Overlithe) (observed only in leap-years)
- 2 Lithe

They were called [Summerdays](/wiki/Summerdays) in [Bree](/wiki/Bree).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Events that occurred on 1 Lithe:

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2778](/wiki/Third_Age_2778 "Third Age 2778") (circa)
    - [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) draws his [Map](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r%27s_Map "Thrór's Map")
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"):
    - The [moon](/wiki/Moon "Moon") is "a broad silver crescent".
    - [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) discovers the [moon-letters](/wiki/Moon-letters "Moon-letters") on [Thrór's Map](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r%27s_Map).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel), [Erestor](/wiki/Erestor), [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan), [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir), [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) arrive with [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Each midsummer-eve the [Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took") held parties, where [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) impressed the [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") with fireworks.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Old English](/wiki/Old_English) <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/li%C3%BEa" title="wiktionary:liþa">líða</a></i>, is the old name for the months June and July.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-5">[5]</a></sup> It probably referred to the first new moon around the summer solstice.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
