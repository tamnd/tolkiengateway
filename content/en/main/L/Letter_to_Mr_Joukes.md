On [28 August](/wiki/28_August) [1967](/wiki/1967), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Mr S.E.O. Joukes** (apparently a native of the Netherlands<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>).

- **Contents:** Mr. Joukes wants to name his daughter *[Ioreth](/wiki/Ioreth)*, but Tolkien writes that it means 'old woman' in Elvish (Tolkien also notes that the name probably does not occur outside *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*). Furthermore, Tolkien writes about the translation of the name *[Arwen](/wiki/Arwen)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** *[Hobbits in Holland: Leven en werk van JRR Tolkien (1892-1973)](/wiki/Hobbits_in_Holland "Hobbits in Holland")*. An excerpt was also published in *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_A_Reader%27s_Companion)* (p. 68).

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

"\[*Arwen*\] means in Elvish 'noble maiden', but ... it is also a word in [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh) meaning 'greatly blessed'" <span></span>
