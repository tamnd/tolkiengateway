On [28 August](../_/28_August.md) [1967](../_/1967.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Mr S.E.O. Joukes** (apparently a native of the Netherlands<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>).

- **Contents:** Mr. Joukes wants to name his daughter *[Ioreth](../I/Ioreth.md)*, but Tolkien writes that it means 'old woman' in Elvish (Tolkien also notes that the name probably does not occur outside *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*). Furthermore, Tolkien writes about the translation of the name *[Arwen](../A/Arwen.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** *[Hobbits in Holland: Leven en werk van JRR Tolkien (1892-1973)](../H/Hobbits_in_Holland.md)*. An excerpt was also published in *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_A_Reader's_Companion.md)* (p. 68).

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

"\[*Arwen*\] means in Elvish 'noble maiden', but ... it is also a word in [Welsh](../W/Welsh.md) meaning 'greatly blessed'" <span></span>
