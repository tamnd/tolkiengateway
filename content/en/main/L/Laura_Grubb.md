**Laura Baggins (née Grubb)**<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-1">[1]</a></sup> was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Laura was a sister of [Lavender Grubb](/wiki/Lavender_Grubb).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> She married [Mungo Baggins](/wiki/Mungo_Baggins) and among their children was [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' father [Bungo](/wiki/Bungo_Baggins "Bungo Baggins").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

From [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2900](/wiki/Third_Age_2900 "Third Age 2900") to [2916](/wiki/Third_Age_2916 "Third Age 2916") Laura Grubb was the official matriarch of the [Baggins Family](/wiki/Baggins_Family).<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_(given_name)" title="wikipedia:Laura (given name)">Laura</a> is the female form of Latin *laurus* "bay, laurel tree".
