**Laura Baggins (née Grubb)**<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-1">[1]</a></sup> was a Hobbit of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Laura was a sister of [Lavender Grubb](Lavender_Grubb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> She married [Mungo Baggins](../M/Mungo_Baggins.md) and among their children was [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)' father [Bungo](../B/Bungo_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

From [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2900](../T/Third_Age_2900.md) to [2916](../T/Third_Age_2916.md) Laura Grubb was the official matriarch of the [Baggins Family](../B/Baggins_Family~66343.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_(given_name)" title="wikipedia:Laura (given name)">Laura</a> is the female form of Latin *laurus* "bay, laurel tree".
