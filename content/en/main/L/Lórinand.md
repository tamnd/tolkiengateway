<br> **Lórinand** was one of the many old names for the land usually called [Lothlórien](Lothlórien.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a manuscript composed by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) sometime after *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, *Lórinand* is said to be a [Nandorin](../N/Nandorin.md) name, meaning "valley of gold" (containing the "Elvish word meaning 'golden light'").<sup id="cite_ref-UTG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTG-1">[1]</a></sup>

However, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) notes that in a later manuscript the name *Lórinand* was reconceptualized as "a transformation, after the introduction of the [mallorns](../M/Mallorn.md), of a yet older name *[Lindórinand](Lothlórien.md#Names)*".<sup id="cite_ref-UTG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTG-1">[1]</a></sup>
