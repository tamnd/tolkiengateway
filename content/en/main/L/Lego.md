Lego (stylised as "LEGO") is a plastic toy brand based in Denmark, mainly consisting of interlocking plastic bricks. Among other themes <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lego_Group" title="wikipedia:The Lego Group">the Lego Group</a> also produces sets based on *[The Lord of the Rings (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)>)* and *[The Hobbit (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)>)*.

## Overview {#Overview}

On [16 December](/wiki/16_December), [2011](/wiki/2011) <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Consumer_Products" title="wikipedia:Warner Bros. Consumer Products">Warner Bros. Consumer Products</a> and the Lego Group announced their partnership and the rolling global launch of the first *The Lord of The Rings* sets to start in June 2012.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Lego_-_Gandalf_Arrives.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/b/b9/Lego_-_Gandalf_Arrives.png/230px-Lego_-_Gandalf_Arrives.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="230" height="199" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/b/b9/Lego_-_Gandalf_Arrives.png/345px-Lego_-_Gandalf_Arrives.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/b/b9/Lego_-_Gandalf_Arrives.png/460px-Lego_-_Gandalf_Arrives.png 2x" data-file-width="1150" data-file-height="996"/></a><figcaption>&#34;Gandalf Arrives&#34; set box</figcaption></figure>

The first *The Lord of The Rings* sets were revealed at the 2012 <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_International_Toy_Fair" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">International Toy Fair</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City" title="wikipedia:New York City">New York</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and released couple of months later, in June [2012](/wiki/2012)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> aimed for children between 8 and 14 years old. The previously announced *The Hobbit* sets, six in total, were released in December 2012.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The following years several more sets were released in both themes: five *The Lord of The Rings* and four *The Hobbit* in 2013, four *The Hobbit* in 2014 and four <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lego_Dimensions" title="wikipedia:Lego Dimensions"><i>Lego Dimensions</i></a> packs in 2015 which included *The Lord of The Rings* characters.

2016 and later saw no releases until the revival of *The Lord of The Rings* theme as a subtheme of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lego_BrickHeadz" title="wikipedia:Lego BrickHeadz">BrickHeadz</a></i> and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lego_Icons" title="wikipedia:Lego Icons">Icons</a></i> themes in 2023.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Since then at least one new *The Lord of The Rings* themed set was released each year.
