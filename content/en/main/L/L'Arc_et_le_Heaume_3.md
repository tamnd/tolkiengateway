<b><i>L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</i> n°3</b> (*[Númenor](../N/Númenor.md)*) is the third issue of the fanzine <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkiendil.com/asso/mag" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</a></i> by the [Tolkiendil](<../T/Tolkiendil_(Tolkien_society).md>) French association. It was published in [2012](../_/2012.md) and it is dedicated to *[Númenor](../N/Númenor.md)*.
