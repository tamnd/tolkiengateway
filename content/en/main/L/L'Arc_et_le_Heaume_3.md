<b><i>L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</i> n°3</b> (*[Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor)*) is the third issue of the fanzine <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkiendil.com/asso/mag" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</a></i> by the [Tolkiendil](</wiki/Tolkiendil_(Tolkien_society)> "Tolkiendil (Tolkien society)") French association. It was published in [2012](/wiki/2012) and it is dedicated to *[Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor)*.
