On [4 December](../_/4_December.md) [1967](../_/1967.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_%2522The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%2522.md) to Roger Verhulst**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Tolkien thanks Mr. Verhulst for sending him two copies of [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md)'s *[Letters to an American Lady](Letters_to_an_American_Lady.md)*, which he finds deeply interesting, and mentions that he is now recovering from an illness.
- **Publication:** None. A description of the letter has been provided in *[W.H. Auden: A Legacy](../W/W.H._Auden%253A_A_Legacy.md)* and on the website [Tolkien Library](../T/Tolkien_Library.md). A description of the letter has appeared in the catalogue *Christie's Fine Printed Books and Manuscripts 28 November 2011* (lot 258).
- **Description:** One page, 76 Sandfield Road, Oxford letterhead, signed by J.R.R. Tolkien.
