On [13 January](/wiki/13_January) [1960](/wiki/1960), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) sent a <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/procuration" title="wikipedia:procuration">per procuration</a></i> **[letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Elizabeth Jones**. The letter was typed and signed by his secretary Elizabeth Lumsden.<sup id="cite_ref-Sothebys_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sothebys-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Description:** 1 page, small 8vo.<sup id="cite_ref-Sothebys_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sothebys-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the auction {#From_the_auction}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>[The letter conatains a] printed statement about <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> and a typed paragraph stating that he is &#34;working on a new book which I hope will tell you some of the things you would like to know.&#34;
