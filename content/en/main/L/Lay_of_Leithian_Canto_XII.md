This [Canto](/wiki/Cantos_of_the_Lay_of_Leithian "Cantos of the Lay of Leithian") is one of the most popular, due to its opening 93 lines (3538-3631) concerning the duel of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth). After this interlude the lay recommences on the [Quest](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril "Quest for the Silmaril"), where [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) reach the Gates of [Angband](/wiki/Angband), and meet the great [werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf") called [Carcharoth](/wiki/Carcharoth) the Red Maw. Carcharoth hails Beren as [Draugluin](/wiki/Draugluin), whose shape Beren bears, asking how he escaped. Beren responds that he has urgent business with [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), and prays that Carcharoth let them aside. The wolf agrees uneasily, but is suspicious of Lúthien in her [vampire](/wiki/Vampire "Vampire") form. But Lúthien swift casts her hideous raiment aside and throws a spell of sleep upon Carcharoth, and he is "as lightning stroke/had felled a huge o'ershadowing oak".
