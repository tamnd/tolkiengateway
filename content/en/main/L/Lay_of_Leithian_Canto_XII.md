This [Canto](../C/Cantos_of_the_Lay_of_Leithian.md) is one of the most popular, due to its opening 93 lines (3538-3631) concerning the duel of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) and [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). After this interlude the lay recommences on the [Quest](../Q/Quest_for_the_Silmaril.md), where [Beren](../B/Beren.md) and [Lúthien](Lúthien.md) reach the Gates of [Angband](../A/Angband.md), and meet the great [werewolf](../W/Werewolf.md) called [Carcharoth](../C/Carcharoth.md) the Red Maw. Carcharoth hails Beren as [Draugluin](../D/Draugluin.md), whose shape Beren bears, asking how he escaped. Beren responds that he has urgent business with [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), and prays that Carcharoth let them aside. The wolf agrees uneasily, but is suspicious of Lúthien in her [vampire](../V/Vampire.md) form. But Lúthien swift casts her hideous raiment aside and throws a spell of sleep upon Carcharoth, and he is "as lightning stroke/had felled a huge o'ershadowing oak".
