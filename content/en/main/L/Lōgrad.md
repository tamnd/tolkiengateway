<i><b>Lōgrad</b></i> is a [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) word, being the native word for *Éo-marc* ("the Horse-mark" or "Borderland of the Horsemen"; called [Riddermark](/wiki/Riddermark) in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*). The name *[Rohan](/wiki/Rohan)* is said to be a translation of *Lōgrad*.<sup id="cite_ref-PMII_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMII-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word likely derives from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Loho&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Loho (page does not exist)">loho-, lō</a></i> ("horse")<sup id="cite_ref-PMII_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMII-1">[1]</a></sup> and \*<i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rad&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rad (page does not exist)">rad</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>
