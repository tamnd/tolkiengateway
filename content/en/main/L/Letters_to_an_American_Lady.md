<i><b>Letters to an American Lady</b></i> is a collection of over 100 letters that [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) wrote to an American woman between 1950 until he died in 1963.

<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Roger_Verhulst&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Roger Verhulst (page does not exist)">Roger Verhulst</a> sent two copies of the book to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), who expressed both in a letter to the former and to [Clyde S. Kilby](/wiki/Clyde_S._Kilby) that he found the book "*deeply interesting*".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
