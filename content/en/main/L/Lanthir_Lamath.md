**Lanthir Lamath**, the "Waterfall of Echoing Voices"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>, was the shimmering, glittering waterfall that ran beside the house of [Dior Eluchíl](../D/Dior.md) in [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md). It was probably part of the stream of the River [Adurant](../A/Adurant.md).

Lanthir Lamath was most famous for inspiring the name of Dior's daughter [Elwing](../E/Elwing.md), whose name - meaning 'Star-spray' - was inspired by the falling water on the night of stars when she was born.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lanthir Lamath* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Waterfall of Echoing Voices", from <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lamthir&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lamthir (page does not exist)">lamthir</a></i> ("waterfall") + plural <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lam&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lam (page does not exist)">lam</a></i> ("echo").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
