On [7 July](../_/7_July.md) [1946](../_/1946.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to G.E. Selby**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Academic matters and a recent holiday with [Edith](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>
