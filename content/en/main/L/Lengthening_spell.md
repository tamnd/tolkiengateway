The **lengthening spell** was a [magic song](/wiki/Magic "Magic") that was sung by [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) to grow out her hair while she was kept in [Hírilorn](/wiki/H%C3%ADrilorn).

The published version of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* only references the *[Lay of Leithian](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian)* and provides a brief summary: "for she put forth her arts of enchantment, and caused her hair to grow to great length, and of it she wove a dark robe that wrapped her beauty like a shadow, and it was laden with a spell of sleep".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The earlier versions of the spell in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") provide some more detailed accounts of this enchantment.

### The Book of Lost Tales {#The_Book_of_Lost_Tales}

In the first version of the story in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, it is said that [Tinúviel](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien "Lúthien"), being the daughter of [Gwendeling](/wiki/Gwendeling), knew of spells, so she devised a plan to escape from Hirilorn: she asked for clear water from the stream below, which should be collected at midnight in a silver bowl, and brought to her in complete silence. After receiving this, she asked for wine, which should be brought to her at noon in a golden flagon while the bearer sang. Then she asked her mother for a spinning wheel, and asked [Dairon](/wiki/Dairon) for a tiny loom.

Having all she needed, when she was alone, she took the water and wine, and mixed them together. While the mixture was in the golden bowl, she sang a song of growth; and while it was in the silver one, she sang a song about all the tallest and longest things upon Earth: "the beards of the [Indravangs](/wiki/Indravangs), the tail of [Karkaras](/wiki/Karkaras), the body of [Glorund](/wiki/Glorund), the bole of Hirilorn, and the sword of Nan she named, nor did she forget the chain [Angainu](/wiki/Angainu) that [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) and [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas) made or the neck of Gilim the giant, and last and longest of all she spake of the hair of [Uinen](/wiki/Uinen) the lady of the [sea](/wiki/Sea "Sea") that is spread through all the waters.” Then Tinúviel washed her hair with the enchanted water and wine, and meanwhile she sang a third song about deep sleep. Her hair began growing swiftly, and in half a day, it almost covered the whole room, and when she woke up the next morning, her hair was flowing out of the windows. She worked long and hard to weave a magical robe of sleepiness and a rope to escape from her prison.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### The Lay of Leithian {#The_Lay_of_Leithian}

In *[The Lay of Leithian](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Leithian)*, Lúthien's father [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) placed her in a guarded treehouse. Through a spell of invoking the longest things in the world, she grew out her hair and made a robe and a rope of it: "*a magic dress / that all was drenched in drowsiness*". Lowering the rope, she put her guards to sleep and escaped.<sup id="cite_ref-Lengthening_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lengthening-3">[3]</a></sup>

Below is a list of invocations within the lengthening spell.<sup id="cite_ref-Lengthening_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lengthening-3">[3]</a></sup>

- **locks of the [Longbeard](/wiki/Longbeards "Longbeards") [dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves")** - One of the seven clans of Dwarves.
- **tail of [Draugluin](/wiki/Draugluin)** - The father of the [Werewolves](/wiki/Werewolves).
- **body of [Glómund](/wiki/Glaurung "Glaurung")** - A great snake with a long body.
- **vast upsoaring peaks** - The mountains of [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim) above [Angband](/wiki/Angband).
- **[Angainor](/wiki/Angainor)** - The great chain that the [Gods](/wiki/Valar "Valar") will contain [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) with. In a note, **Engainor** was written in the original draft.<sup id="cite_ref-LB|C5_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LB|C5-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **Glend** - The sword of the [Giant](/wiki/Giants "Giants") Nan.<sup id="cite_ref-Glend_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glend-6">[6]</a></sup>
- **Nan** - A [Giant](/wiki/Giants "Giants") who wielded Glend.<sup id="cite_ref-Glend_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glend-6">[6]</a></sup> In a note, **Nann** was written as an alternative name to *Nan*.<sup id="cite_ref-LB|C5_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LB|C5-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
- **Gilim** ([G.](/wiki/Gnomish "Gnomish") *Winter*) - The [Giant](/wiki/Giants "Giants") of [Eruman](/wiki/Araman "Araman"), noteworthy for having a long neck.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
- **endless hair of [Uinen](/wiki/Uinen)** - The Lady of the [Sea](/wiki/Sea).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
