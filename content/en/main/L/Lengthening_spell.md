The **lengthening spell** was a [magic song](../M/Magic.md) that was sung by [Lúthien](Lúthien.md) to grow out her hair while she was kept in [Hírilorn](../H/Hírilorn.md).

The published version of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* only references the *[Lay of Leithian](Lay_of_Leithian.md)* and provides a brief summary: "for she put forth her arts of enchantment, and caused her hair to grow to great length, and of it she wove a dark robe that wrapped her beauty like a shadow, and it was laden with a spell of sleep".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The earlier versions of the spell in the [legendarium](Legendarium.md) provide some more detailed accounts of this enchantment.

### The Book of Lost Tales {#The_Book_of_Lost_Tales}

In the first version of the story in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, it is said that [Tinúviel](Lúthien.md), being the daughter of [Gwendeling](../G/Gwendeling.md), knew of spells, so she devised a plan to escape from Hirilorn: she asked for clear water from the stream below, which should be collected at midnight in a silver bowl, and brought to her in complete silence. After receiving this, she asked for wine, which should be brought to her at noon in a golden flagon while the bearer sang. Then she asked her mother for a spinning wheel, and asked [Dairon](../D/Dairon.md) for a tiny loom.

Having all she needed, when she was alone, she took the water and wine, and mixed them together. While the mixture was in the golden bowl, she sang a song of growth; and while it was in the silver one, she sang a song about all the tallest and longest things upon Earth: "the beards of the [Indravangs](../I/Indravangs.md), the tail of [Karkaras](../K/Karkaras.md), the body of [Glorund](../G/Glorund.md), the bole of Hirilorn, and the sword of Nan she named, nor did she forget the chain [Angainu](../A/Angainu.md) that [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) and [Tulkas](../T/Tulkas.md) made or the neck of Gilim the giant, and last and longest of all she spake of the hair of [Uinen](../U/Uinen.md) the lady of the [sea](../S/Sea.md) that is spread through all the waters.” Then Tinúviel washed her hair with the enchanted water and wine, and meanwhile she sang a third song about deep sleep. Her hair began growing swiftly, and in half a day, it almost covered the whole room, and when she woke up the next morning, her hair was flowing out of the windows. She worked long and hard to weave a magical robe of sleepiness and a rope to escape from her prison.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### The Lay of Leithian {#The_Lay_of_Leithian}

In *[The Lay of Leithian](../T/The_Lay_of_Leithian.md)*, Lúthien's father [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) placed her in a guarded treehouse. Through a spell of invoking the longest things in the world, she grew out her hair and made a robe and a rope of it: "*a magic dress / that all was drenched in drowsiness*". Lowering the rope, she put her guards to sleep and escaped.<sup id="cite_ref-Lengthening_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lengthening-3">[3]</a></sup>

Below is a list of invocations within the lengthening spell.<sup id="cite_ref-Lengthening_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lengthening-3">[3]</a></sup>

- **locks of the [Longbeard](Longbeards.md) [dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md)** - One of the seven clans of Dwarves.
- **tail of [Draugluin](../D/Draugluin.md)** - The father of the [Werewolves](../W/Werewolves.md).
- **body of [Glómund](../G/Glaurung.md)** - A great snake with a long body.
- **vast upsoaring peaks** - The mountains of [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md) above [Angband](../A/Angband.md).
- **[Angainor](../A/Angainor.md)** - The great chain that the [Gods](../V/Valar.md) will contain [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) with. In a note, **Engainor** was written in the original draft.<sup id="cite_ref-LB|C5_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LB|C5-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **Glend** - The sword of the [Giant](../G/Giants.md) Nan.<sup id="cite_ref-Glend_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glend-6">[6]</a></sup>
- **Nan** - A [Giant](../G/Giants.md) who wielded Glend.<sup id="cite_ref-Glend_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glend-6">[6]</a></sup> In a note, **Nann** was written as an alternative name to *Nan*.<sup id="cite_ref-LB|C5_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LB|C5-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
- **Gilim** ([G.](../G/Gnomish.md) *Winter*) - The [Giant](../G/Giants.md) of [Eruman](../A/Araman.md), noteworthy for having a long neck.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
- **endless hair of [Uinen](../U/Uinen.md)** - The Lady of the [Sea](../S/Sea.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
