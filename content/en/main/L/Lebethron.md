**Lebethron** was a hardwood tree that grew in [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).

## History {#History}

Lebethron was said to be fair, and beloved by the woodworkers of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-XR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-XR-1">[1]</a></sup> The staves given by [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) to [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) were made of lebethron,<sup id="cite_ref-XR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-XR-1">[1]</a></sup> as was the casket, described as made of "black *lebethron*", in which he brought the [Crown of Gondor](../C/Crown_of_Gondor.md) to the coronation of [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name was first invented by Tolkien at the time of writing as *melinon*, then *lebendron* and finally *lebethras*. It was replaced by *lebethron* on the fair manuscript copy.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

According to a later source, *lebethron* was a [Númenórean Sindarin](../N/Númenórean_Sindarin.md) word. The first element, <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lebeth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lebeth (page does not exist)">lebeth</a></i>, was related to [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Leps%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lepsë (page does not exist)">lepsë</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-4">[4]</a></sup> ("finger"<sup id="cite_ref-etym_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-5">[5]</a></sup>). The second element was said to be derived from *[oron](../O/Oron.md)* ("tree"), though a later addition also ties it to the [root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) [RUN](../R/RUN.md) ("rub, grind, smooth, polish"). In this light, the tree was named *lebethorn*, and the wood of the tree *lebethron*, and the two words merged into one over time. The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) cognate was <i><b>lepetta</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-4">[4]</a></sup>
