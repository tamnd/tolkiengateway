<i><b>Literary Swordsmen and Sorcerers: The Makers of Heroic Fantasy</b></i> is a collection of articles by [L. Sprague de Camp](/wiki/L._Sprague_de_Camp).

Chapter IX, "Merlin in Tweeds: J.R.R. Tolkien" (based on the article "White Wizard in Tweeds", *Fantastic Stories* November 1976), is a literary analysis of the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). The article also includes recollections of and a quote from Tolkien noted by de Camp during his visit in [1967](/wiki/1967) (see pp. 243–4).

Tolkien's reaction was described thus:

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395753">.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider{margin-top:1rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem;color:var(--tg-color-subtle);display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:space-between}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider:not(:empty){gap:1rem;white-space:nowrap}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::before,.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::after{content:"";height:1px;width:100%;background-color:var(--tg-border-color-base)}</style><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>[Tolkien] said he found [the anthology] interesting but did not much like the stories in it [...] We sat in the garage for a couple of hours, smoking pipes, drinking beer, and talking about a variety of things. Practically anything in English literature, from Beowulf down, Tolkien had read and could talk intelligently about. He indicated that he &#39;rather liked&#39; Howard&#39;s Conan stories.
</p>
