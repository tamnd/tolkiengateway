**Lockbearer** was the name that [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) used of [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) in her message delivered by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in the [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> so chosen because Galadriel had given [three golden strands of her hair](/wiki/Hair_of_Galadriel "Hair of Galadriel") to Gimli as a farewell present (at his request) in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-Rider_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rider-1">[1]</a></sup>
