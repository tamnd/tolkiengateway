<i><b>Leaf by Niggle</b></i> is the first separate reprint of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s short story "[Leaf by Niggle](/wiki/Leaf_by_Niggle)". The publication features a foreword by Alonzo L. McDonald and a four page discussion guide at rear.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
