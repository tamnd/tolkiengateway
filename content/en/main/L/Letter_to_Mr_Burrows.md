On [1 August](/wiki/1_August) [1969](/wiki/1969), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Mr Burrows**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien discusses the signing of a copy of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, his change of address and recent health issues.<sup id="cite_ref-Bloom_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bloom-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Autograph letter, 1 page.<sup id="cite_ref-Bloom_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bloom-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description and photograph of the letter was included in *[Bloomsbury Modern First Editions, Manuscripts and Autograph Letters, English and Continental Literature and History 24 May 2007](/wiki/Bloomsbury_Modern_First_Editions,_Manuscripts_and_Autograph_Letters,_English_and_Continental_Literature_and_History_24_May_2007)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Dear Mr Burrows,<br/><br/>Thank you for kind &amp; appreciative letter. I return your &#39;Hobbit&#39; cover signed.<br/><br/>You have not discovered my address: I left Oxford a year ago. I now live in &#39;retirement&#39; —in an endeavour to preserve some time free from interruptions. All letters will be forwarded from the <s>above</s> below address. I have been unwell during July and this added to the forwarding by the P.Office must be my excuse for delay. Best wishes. Yours sincerely<br/><br/>J.R.R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
