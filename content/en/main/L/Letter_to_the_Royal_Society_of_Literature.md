On [10 January](/wiki/10_January) [1964](/wiki/1964), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote <b><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22" title="Letters not published in &#34;The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien&#34;">a letter</a> to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Literature" title="wikipedia:Royal Society of Literature">Royal Society of Literature</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien declines to write an obituary of [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) for the Society's journal *Report*, but suggests that [Owen Barfield](/wiki/Owen_Barfield) do so instead. Tolkien writes: "I feel his loss so deeply that I have since his death refused to write or speak about him".<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A quote from the letter appeared in the article "Literary Greats Exposed as Gossips and Snipes" by Martin Bentham, published in the *Sunday Telegraph*, 7 February 1999.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
