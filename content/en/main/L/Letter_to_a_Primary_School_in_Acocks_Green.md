On [17 October](/wiki/17_October) [1966](/wiki/1966), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to a primary school class in Acocks Green**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subjects:** *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, personal relation to [Acocks Green](/wiki/Acocks_Green).
- **Description:** 1 page, [76 Sandfield Road](/wiki/76_Sandfield_Road), [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford). Typed letter with postscript in blue ink.
- **Publication:** Excerpts from the letter have appeared in *[Sotheby's English Literature, History, Children's Books, and Illustrations 16 December 2004](/wiki/Sotheby%27s_English_Literature,_History,_Children%27s_Books,_and_Illustrations_16_December_2004)*.
