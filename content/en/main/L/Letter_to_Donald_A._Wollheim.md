On [10 February](/wiki/10_February) [1966](/wiki/1966), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Donald A. Wollheim](/wiki/Donald_A._Wollheim)**, editor for [Ace Books](/wiki/Ace_Books).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** The [Ace Books](/wiki/Ace_Books) controversy.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Autographed, (full signature &amp; initialed) 1 page (front only) handwritten letter.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Transcription {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Dear Mr. Wollheim,<br/><br/>I regret the delay in replying to your letter of Jan 27th.  I have been away from home - I was am an advance victim of the influenza epidemic now widespread in this country. Thanks for your expressions of esteem,<br/><br/>Yours Sincerely<br/>J.R.R. Tolkien.<br/><br/>Enclosed:  two copies of the proposed agreement, signed.  Also a copy of an &#39;agreed statement&#39; which I will send out as soon as I hear that it is acceptable. JRRT.
</p>
</blockquote>
