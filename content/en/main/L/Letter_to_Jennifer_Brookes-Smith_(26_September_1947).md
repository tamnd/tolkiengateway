On [26 September](/wiki/26_September) [1947](/wiki/1947), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Jennifer Brookes-Smith**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-3">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Signing the letter "Uncle Ronald", Tolkien offers advice about Oxford colleges, and discusses misprints in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, the development of [runes](/wiki/Runes "Runes"), and the various editions of *The Hobbit*.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-3">[2]</a></sup> Tolkien also expresses a dissatisfaction with [Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin "Allen and Unwin").<sup id="cite_ref-TB_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-4">[3]</a></sup>
- **Description:** 4 pages.<sup id="cite_ref-TB_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-4">[3]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of the letter appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*. Extracts from the letter were quoted in *[Christie's 20th Century Books and Manuscripts 2 December 2003](/wiki/Christie%27s_20th_Century_Books_and_Manuscripts_2_December_2003)*.
