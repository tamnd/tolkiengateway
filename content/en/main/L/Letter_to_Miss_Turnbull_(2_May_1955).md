On [2 May](/wiki/2_May) [1955](/wiki/1955), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Miss Turnbull** in Whitby, Yorkshire.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Tolkien writes that he is currently "*putting final touches on the [third volume](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King "The Return of the King") of* [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) *(evidently in [Appendices](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Appendices "The Lord of the Rings Appendices") [D](/wiki/Appendix_D "Appendix D") and [E](/wiki/Appendix_E "Appendix E")).*"<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup> He remarks that delays in the publication of *The Return of the King* have been his fault, not the publishers. Furthermore, Tolkien mentions other writings.<sup id="cite_ref-Dacobra_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dacobra-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Handwritten letter, signed, Tolkien's Sandfield Road stationery.<sup id="cite_ref-Dacobra_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dacobra-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** The letter was quoted in *[Sotheby's English Literature and History 18 July 1991](/wiki/Sotheby%27s_English_Literature_and_History_18_July_1991)*.
