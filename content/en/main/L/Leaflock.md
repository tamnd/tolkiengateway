**Leaflock**, called **Finglas** in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), was, with [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) and [Skinbark](/wiki/Skinbark), one of the three oldest [Ents](/wiki/Ents) of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest).

## History {#History}

Finglas, [Fladrif](/wiki/Skinbark "Skinbark"), and [Fangorn](/wiki/Treebeard "Treebeard") were the oldest of the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) of Fangorn Forest still alive in the later [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

According to [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard), Leaflock had become tired, sometimes sleeping through entire seasons (although formerly he used to rouse up in winter), and tree-like with a covering of leafy hair.<sup id="cite_ref-iii4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-iii4-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Finglas* is apparently composed by the two elements <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Fing&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fing (page does not exist)">fing</a>/<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Find&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Find (page does not exist)">find</a></i> "lock of hair" and *[las](/wiki/Las "Las")* "leaf".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The element *lock* in *Leaflock* here has the meaning of "hair" since it is a translation of his [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>

The name obviously refers to his being "Covered with leafy hair".<sup id="cite_ref-iii4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-iii4-1">[1]</a></sup>
