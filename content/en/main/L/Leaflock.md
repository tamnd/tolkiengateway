**Leaflock**, called **Finglas** in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), was, with [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md) and [Skinbark](../S/Skinbark.md), one of the three oldest [Ents](../E/Ents.md) of [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md).

## History {#History}

Finglas, [Fladrif](../S/Skinbark.md), and [Fangorn](../T/Treebeard.md) were the oldest of the [Ents](../E/Ents.md) of Fangorn Forest still alive in the later [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).

According to [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md), Leaflock had become tired, sometimes sleeping through entire seasons (although formerly he used to rouse up in winter), and tree-like with a covering of leafy hair.<sup id="cite_ref-iii4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-iii4-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Finglas* is apparently composed by the two elements <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Fing&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fing (page does not exist)">fing</a>/<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Find&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Find (page does not exist)">find</a></i> "lock of hair" and *[las](Las.md)* "leaf".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The element *lock* in *Leaflock* here has the meaning of "hair" since it is a translation of his [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>

The name obviously refers to his being "Covered with leafy hair".<sup id="cite_ref-iii4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-iii4-1">[1]</a></sup>
