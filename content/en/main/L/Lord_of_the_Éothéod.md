The **Lord of the Éothéod**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> was the hereditary ruler of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md), the horsemen of the North. The Lords of the Éothéod were descended from the [Kings of Rhovanion](../K/King_of_Rhovanion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> With the formation of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md), the title was abandoned in favor of "[King of the Mark](../K/King_of_Rohan.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

- [Marhwini](../M/Marhwini.md), fl. [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1856](../T/Third_Age_1856.md)
- [Forthwini](../F/Forthwini.md), fl. [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md)
- Third Lord, fl. mid-20th century
- [Frumgar](../F/Frumgar.md), fl. [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1977](../T/Third_Age_1977.md)
- [Fram](../F/Fram.md), fl. 21st century
- Unknown number of unnamed lords
- [Léod](Léod.md), d. [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2501](../T/Third_Age_2501.md)
- [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2501](../T/Third_Age_2501.md) - [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md)

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2020: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>This game provides names and interesting backstories for all of the un-named Lords of the Éothéod and their kin. They were Forthári, Gifica, Gundamári, Gisilhári, Gundahári, Gundáric, Hildáric, Gundobad, Hildigar, Gundolávi, Thíudric, Gunthar, Lóthar, Gundric, Bóra, Maera, Théodric, Haldend, Théodwyne Sternbrow, Frúma, Cendric, and Hárda.</dd></dl>
