<i><b>La Conexion Española de J.R.R. Tolkien: El Tío &#34;Curro&#34;</b></i> ("J.R.R. Tolkien's Spanish Connection: Uncle 'Curro'") is a Spanish-language biography about [Francis Xavier Morgan](/wiki/Francis_Xavier_Morgan), with an emphasis on his relationship to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The book was both reedited in Spanish and translated into English by Luna Press Publishing.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
