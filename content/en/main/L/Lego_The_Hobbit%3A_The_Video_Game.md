**Lego The Hobbit: The Video Game** is a video game by [Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment](/wiki/Warner_Bros._Interactive_Entertainment) and [Traveller's Tales](/wiki/Traveller%27s_Tales). The game is based on [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)") and was released in April 2014. It features the story lines of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)* and *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)*. Downloadable content covering *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)* was cancelled in March 2015 for unknown reasons after the game's release.<sup id="cite_ref-HBTitles_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HBTitles-1">[1]</a></sup>
