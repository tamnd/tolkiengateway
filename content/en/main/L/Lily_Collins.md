**Lily Jane Collins** ([18 March](/wiki/18_March), [1989](/wiki/1989)) is an English-American actress, who played [Edith Bratt](/wiki/Edith_Bratt) in *[Tolkien](</wiki/Tolkien_(film)> "Tolkien (film)")* (2019). She had previously auditioned for the role of [Tauriel](/wiki/Tauriel) in [The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)") film series.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
