**Lily Jane Collins** ([18 March](../_/18_March.md), [1989](../_/1989.md)) is an English-American actress, who played [Edith Bratt](../E/Edith_Bratt.md) in *[Tolkien](<../T/Tolkien_(film).md>)* (2019). She had previously auditioned for the role of [Tauriel](../T/Tauriel.md) in [The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>) film series.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
