On [21 September](/wiki/21_September) [1937](/wiki/1937), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to [Simonne d'Ardenne](/wiki/Simonne_d%27Ardenne)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Description:** 1-page, handwritten letter, enclosed with a copy of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* (published on this day) sent by Tolkien as a gift to Simonne d'Ardenne.
- **Archive:** The letter is kept at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Li%C3%A8ge" title="wikipedia:University of Liège">University of Liège</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-JRRVF_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-JRRVF-2">[2]</a></sup>
