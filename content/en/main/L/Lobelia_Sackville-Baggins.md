**Lobelia Sackville-Baggins**, (née **[Bracegirdle](/wiki/Bracegirdle_Family "Bracegirdle Family")**) was the second child, and only daughter of [Primrose Boffin](/wiki/Primrose_Bracegirdle "Primrose Bracegirdle") and [Blanco Bracegirdle](/wiki/Blanco_Bracegirdle) and then the wife of [Otho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Otho_Sackville-Baggins), a distant relative of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), known for her want of the hobbit-hole [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).

## History {#History}

Lobelia was born in [Hardbottle](/wiki/Hardbottle) to [Primrose Boffin](/wiki/Primrose_Bracegirdle "Primrose Bracegirdle") and [Blanco Bracegirdle](/wiki/Blanco_Bracegirdle) and the sister of [Bruno Bracegirdle](/wiki/Bruno_Bracegirdle). Lobelia was renowned in the [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire) for her temper and greed. She later married [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)'s cousin [Otho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Otho_Sackville-Baggins), and they had one child together, [Lotho](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins "Lotho Sackville-Baggins"), who was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1364](/wiki/Third_Age_2964 "Third Age 2964").<sup id="cite_ref-Bag_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bag-1">[1]</a></sup>

Her envy of [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) was well-known; when Bilbo left for [his legendary journey](/wiki/Quest_for_Erebor "Quest for Erebor") in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1341](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup> she coveted Bag End and found an oportunity to steal some of Bilbo's spoons, as Bilbo suspected after his return, and Lobelia knew well his suspicions. Lobelia and Otho were furious when Bilbo made [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) his heir, him being a more distant relative. However, he did invite them to [his farewell feast](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party"), with magnificent invitations written with golden ink; Lobelia could not ignore it. The [next day](/wiki/23_September "23 September") she and Otho came to Bag End, hearing that Bilbo's household was being given out, but Frodo said that what was directed to her was only a case of silver spoons. She took "the point", and took the gift. But later as Otho left, she was still there, investigating nooks and corners and tapping the floors, she had snatched some small valuable things in her umbrella, until Frodo relieved her and drove her out, as she shouted that he should also leave, being half [Brandybuck](/wiki/Brandybuck_Family "Brandybuck Family"), did not belong here. [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) later saw her driving a pony-trap towards [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Her angry words to Frodo turned out to be true: he decided to leave for [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow) (a ruse to escape the Shire) he sold Bag End to Lobelia by [June](/wiki/June) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), but unfortunately Lobelia was now centennial, and Otho had died by then. The titles would pass to her on [24 September](/wiki/24_September), but she went there [the previous day](/wiki/23_September "23 September") with Lotho, and an inventory list to inspect that nothing she had paid for had been carried off to Buckland. She went right though her list, and in the end they departed with the spare key; Frodo said that his own would be left with the [Gamgee Family](/wiki/Gamgee_Family) and Lobelia reacted as if she thought that the Gamgees would rob the household the next day.<sup id="cite_ref-Company_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Company-4">[4]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Lobelia was imprisoned in [Lockholes](/wiki/Lockholes) for arguing with the [Chief](/wiki/Chief_Shirriff "Chief Shirriff")'s Men, and attacking one of them with an umbrella.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-5">[5]</a></sup> After finding [Fredegar Bolger](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger), Frodo finds Lobelia, old and frail. As Frodo lets her out, Lobelia was popular for the first time for bravely standing up to the [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s [Men](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians"), receiving cheers and applauds. However, she was crushed to find out her son had been murdered whilst she was in prison. After the War of the Ring, Lobelia returned to her home village to live with the other [Bracegirdles](/wiki/Bracegirdle_Family "Bracegirdle Family"), giving Bag End to Frodo. When she died in the Spring of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3020](/wiki/Third_Age_3020 "Third Age 3020"), she gave her money to Frodo to be used to help [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") left homeless by Saruman and her son, which deeply moved Frodo.<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobelia" title="wikipedia:Lobelia">Lobelia</a> is a genus of the bellflower family, named after <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthias_de_L%27Obel" title="wikipedia:Matthias de L&#39;Obel">Matthias de L&#39;Obel</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Some species are commonly grown as garden flowers.

<b>1981: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Although she is only named in passing, Diana Bishop plays Lobelia at Bilbo&#39;s 111th Birthday party.  She and <a href="/wiki/Otho_Sackville-Baggins" title="Otho Sackville-Baggins">Otho</a> are given a brief exchange complaining about being part of &#34;one gross&#34;, and questioning what &#34;Frodo coming into his inheritance&#34; means.</dd></dl>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the expansion <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_Dark_Minions" title="Middle-earth: Dark Minions">Dark Minions</a></i>, Lobelia appears as two different cards: &#34;Mistress Lobelia&#34; is a Resource Ally, and &#34;Lobelia Sackville-Baggins&#34; is an Agent.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Lobelia (portrayed by <a href="/wiki/Elizabeth_Moody" title="Elizabeth Moody">Elizabeth Moody</a>) appears briefly along with Otho in the Extended edition of the film, attempting to confront <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo</a> during his <a href="/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party" title="Bilbo&#39;s Farewell Party">farewell party</a>, though Bilbo manages to elude their presence. In the theatrical release, Lobelia is only heard knocking at Bilbo&#39;s door before the farewell party. Bilbo also makes a dismissive reference to “some Bracegirdle from Hardbottle.”</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Lobelia (voiced by <a href="/wiki/Jennifer_Hale" title="Jennifer Hale">Jennifer Hale</a>) appears in Bywater. Frodo has to give her the Bag End Deed as one of the first missions. When Frodo approaches her she insists he first warn <a href="/wiki/Robin_Smallburrow" title="Robin Smallburrow">Robin Smallburrow</a> of the wolves in the Shire by ringing the warning bell. Frodo does so, before presenting an exhilirated Lobelia with the Bag End Deed.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Found outside <a href="/wiki/Bag_End" title="Bag End">Bag End</a>, Lobelia is involved in several quests dealing with surprises left for her by the previous owner.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2012" title="2012">2012</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Lobelia is seen briefly in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_(extended_edition)" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey (extended edition)">Extended Edition</a> (along with Otho) in the marketplace outside of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Green_Dragon" title="Green Dragon">Green Dragon</a> Inn. The character&#39;s costume is deliberately ostentatious to be &#34;bright and show-offy&#34; as &#34;she aspires to the best dressed lady in Hobbiton&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2014" title="2014">2014</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>She and Otho are also seen at the end of the film when Bilbo&#39;s possessions are being auctioned off as he returns to <a href="/wiki/The_Shire" title="The Shire">the Shire</a>. Lobelia pretends not to know who Bilbo is, so he accuses her of being dishonest about it. She is portrayed by Erin Banks.</dd></dl>
