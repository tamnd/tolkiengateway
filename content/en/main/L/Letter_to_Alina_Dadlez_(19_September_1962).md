On [19 September](/wiki/19_September) [1962](/wiki/1962), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Alina Dadlez](/wiki/Alina_Dadlez)**.<sup id="cite_ref-G_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-G-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Reply to a letter from Dadlez (of [17 September](/wiki/17_September)), concerning a translation into Spanish of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* (see <i><b><a href="/wiki/El_hobito" title="El hobito">El hobito</a></b></i>). The Argentinian publishers (in a letter of 14 September<sup id="cite_ref-RG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RG-2">[2]</a></sup>), commenting on Tolkien's suggestion of the form *hobitos*, suggest in their turn the form *jobitos* (since *h* is mute in Spanish). The publishers also write that they "are unable to find the quotation cited by him in the copy of *The Hobbit* they are using", concerning *gnomos*/*gnomes* (see [Letter to Alina Dadlez (20 July 1962)](</wiki/Letter_to_Alina_Dadlez_(20_July_1962)>)).<sup id="cite_ref-G_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-G-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Archive:** Tolkien-George Allen &amp; Unwin archive, HarperCollins.<sup id="cite_ref-G_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-G-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Excerpts {#Excerpts}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I prefer <i>hobitos</i> since it preserves to the eye more relationship to the original word. I do not much mind the <i>h</i> being &#39;mute&#39;; I am sure many <a href="/wiki/Hobbits" title="Hobbits">hobbits</a> drop their <i>h</i>s like most rural folk in England.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>J.R.R. Tolkien<sup id="cite_ref-RG_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RG-2">[2]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>

In the words of [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) and [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond), Tolkien also writes that he "gave the wrong reference in *The Hobbit* because he had used an older edition".<sup id="cite_ref-G_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-G-1">[1]</a></sup>
