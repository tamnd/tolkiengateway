On [2 December](/wiki/2_December) [1955](/wiki/1955), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Elizabeth Jennings**, Tolkien's childhood friend.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien congratulates Jennings on the publication of her poetry book *A Way of Looking* and discusses the merits of [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden)'s *[Shield of Achilles](/wiki/List_of_books_in_Tolkien%27s_library#The_Shield_of_Achilles "List of books in Tolkien's library")*, and mentions the review of both books in *[Time and Tide](</wiki/Time_and_Tide_(3_December_1955)> "Time and Tide (3 December 1955)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** The letter is reproduced in *[The Arts Council Collection of Modern Literary Manuscripts 1963-1972](/wiki/The_Arts_Council_Collection_of_Modern_Literary_Manuscripts_1963-1972)*. A description of the letter appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*.
