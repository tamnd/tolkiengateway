On [8 February](/wiki/8_February) [1967](/wiki/1967), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote <b><a href="/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien" title="Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien">a letter</a> to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Manlove" title="wikipedia:Colin Manlove">C.N. Manlove</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Discussion of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
- **Publication:** Quotes from the letter appeared in *[Modern Fantasy: Five Studies](/wiki/Modern_Fantasy:_Five_Studies)*.
