<i><b>luin</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "blue".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Tarn_Aeluin" title="Tarn Aeluin">Ae<b>luin</b></a></i> = "Blue Lake"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ered_Luin" title="Ered Luin">Ered <b>Luin</b></a></i> = "Blue Mountains"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Blue_Wizards" title="Blue Wizards">Ithryn <b>Luin</b></a></i> = "Blue Wizards"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Mindolluin" title="Mindolluin">Mindol<b>luin</b></a></i> = "Towering-head-blue"

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[luine](/wiki/Luine "Luine")*
