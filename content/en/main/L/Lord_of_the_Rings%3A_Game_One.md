<i><b>Lord of the Rings: Game One</b></i> (published in North America as <i><b>The Fellowship of the Rings Software Adventure</b></i>) is the sequel of *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1982_video_game)> "The Hobbit (1982 video game)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> It is divided in two parts and has also a beginner's game.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup> *Lord of the Rings: Game One* is a part of "[The Tolkien Trilogy](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Trilogy)"<sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup> and "[The Tolkien Software Adventure Series](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Software_Adventure_Series)".<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup> It was licensed by the [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup>

Included in the initial release was a paperback copy of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup>

Playable characters include [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Pippin](/wiki/Pippin_Took "Pippin Took") and [Merry](/wiki/Merry_Brandybuck "Merry Brandybuck").
