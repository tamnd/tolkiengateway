On [28 May](../_/28_May.md) [1965](../_/1965.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_%2522The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%2522.md) to an unknown reader**.<sup id="cite_ref-Flavin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flavin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Description:** A young girl from New Zealand had written to Tolkien "*a letter about genealogy and hobbit recipes for cooking rabbits, Tolkien's secretary wrote back a nice reply and Tolkien personally added a few lines and signed the letter*".<sup id="cite_ref-Flavin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flavin-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** The letter is mentioned by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) in *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](../T/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md)*:

"*On one of his copies of the First Edition he added to the genealogy of the [Tooks](../T/Tooks.md) (Fredegar's mother being [Rosamunda Took](../R/Rosamunda_Took.md)) 'Estella' as the sister of [Fredegar](../F/Fredegar_Bolger.md) and her birth-date [1385](../T/Third_Age_2985.md); and to the [Brandybuck](../B/Brandybuck.md) genealogy he added to Meriadoc's '= [Estella Bolger](../E/Estella_Bolger.md) 1385', noting beside this that he had told a correspondent in [1965](../_/1965.md) that 'I believe he married a sister of Fredegar Bolger of the Bolgers of [Budgeford](../B/Budgeford.md)'. These corrections, for a reason unknown to me, were not incorporated in the [Allen and Unwin](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md) Second Edition, but they did occur in a later impression of the [Ballantine](../B/Ballantine.md) edition of [1966](../_/1966.md), and hence Estella Bolger and her marriage to Merry Brandybuck are entered in* [The Complete Guide to Middle-earth](../T/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth.md) *by [Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md).*

*These additions to the family trees were made at the instance of [Douglas A. Anderson](../D/Douglas_A._Anderson.md) in the [Houghton Mifflin](../H/Houghton_Mifflin.md) edition of [1987](../_/1987.md), to which he contributed a note on the history of the text. Estella Bolger and her marriage to Meriadoc have finally entered the British 'tradition' in the re-set edition published by [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md) in [1994](../_/1994.md) (see Douglas Anderson's 'Note on the text' in this edition, p. xii).*"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

\[Tolkien's added lines:\]

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I believe he married a sister of<br/><a href="../F/Fredegar_Bolger.md" title="Fredegar Bolger">Fredegar (Fatty) Bolger</a>, but I<br/>will look into the matter.  I have some<br/>records of the family of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../B/Bolger_Family~66348.md" title="Bolger Family">Bolgers</a> of <a href="../B/Budgeford.md" title="Budgeford">Budgeford</a>,<br/>whose lands were not far from <a href="../B/Buckland.md" title="Buckland">Buckland</a>, but they were<br/>for lack of space not included.  J. R. R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
