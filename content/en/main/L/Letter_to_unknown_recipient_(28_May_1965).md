On [28 May](/wiki/28_May) [1965](/wiki/1965), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to an unknown reader**.<sup id="cite_ref-Flavin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flavin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Description:** A young girl from New Zealand had written to Tolkien "*a letter about genealogy and hobbit recipes for cooking rabbits, Tolkien's secretary wrote back a nice reply and Tolkien personally added a few lines and signed the letter*".<sup id="cite_ref-Flavin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flavin-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** The letter is mentioned by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) in *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)*:

"*On one of his copies of the First Edition he added to the genealogy of the [Tooks](/wiki/Tooks) (Fredegar's mother being [Rosamunda Took](/wiki/Rosamunda_Took)) 'Estella' as the sister of [Fredegar](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger "Fredegar Bolger") and her birth-date [1385](/wiki/Third_Age_2985 "Third Age 2985"); and to the [Brandybuck](/wiki/Brandybuck) genealogy he added to Meriadoc's '= [Estella Bolger](/wiki/Estella_Bolger) 1385', noting beside this that he had told a correspondent in [1965](/wiki/1965) that 'I believe he married a sister of Fredegar Bolger of the Bolgers of [Budgeford](/wiki/Budgeford)'. These corrections, for a reason unknown to me, were not incorporated in the [Allen and Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin) Second Edition, but they did occur in a later impression of the [Ballantine](/wiki/Ballantine) edition of [1966](/wiki/1966), and hence Estella Bolger and her marriage to Merry Brandybuck are entered in* [The Complete Guide to Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth) *by [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster).*

*These additions to the family trees were made at the instance of [Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson) in the [Houghton Mifflin](/wiki/Houghton_Mifflin) edition of [1987](/wiki/1987), to which he contributed a note on the history of the text. Estella Bolger and her marriage to Meriadoc have finally entered the British 'tradition' in the re-set edition published by [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) in [1994](/wiki/1994) (see Douglas Anderson's 'Note on the text' in this edition, p. xii).*"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

\[Tolkien's added lines:\]

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I believe he married a sister of<br/><a href="/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger" title="Fredegar Bolger">Fredegar (Fatty) Bolger</a>, but I<br/>will look into the matter.  I have some<br/>records of the family of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Bolger_Family" title="Bolger Family">Bolgers</a> of <a href="/wiki/Budgeford" title="Budgeford">Budgeford</a>,<br/>whose lands were not far from <a href="/wiki/Buckland" title="Buckland">Buckland</a>, but they were<br/>for lack of space not included.  J. R. R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
