**Lothron** was an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) name for the fifth month of the year. Lothron comes from the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) language, and in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) the name was used only by the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md). Most other calendars in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) at that time used the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) equivalent, which was [Lótessë](Lótessë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Both these names come ultimately from the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) root-word lot(h), 'flower', and refer to the springtime blossoming associated with this month.
