**Lothron** was an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) name for the fifth month of the year. Lothron comes from the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) language, and in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) the name was used only by the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain). Most other calendars in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) at that time used the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) equivalent, which was [Lótessë](/wiki/L%C3%B3tess%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Both these names come ultimately from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) root-word lot(h), 'flower', and refer to the springtime blossoming associated with this month.
