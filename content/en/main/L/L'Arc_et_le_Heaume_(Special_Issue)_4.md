<b><i>L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</i> (Special Issue) n°4</b> (*La Chronologie du Seigneur des Anneaux de J.R.R. Tolkien*) is the fourth special issue of the fanzine <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkiendil.com/asso/mag" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</a></i> by the [Tolkiendil](<../T/Tolkiendil_(Tolkien_society).md>) French association. It was published in [2023](../_/2023.md) and it is dedicated to the chronology of *The Lord of the Rings*. The fanzine is for the most part the translation of the article of William Cloud Hicklin, *The Chronology of The Lord of the Rings*, *Tolkien Studies*, [2022](../_/2022.md), vol. 19, no 19, p. 22-152.
