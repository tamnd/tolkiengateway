**Lindon** was a region of the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md). Initially populated by [Laiquendi](../G/Green-elves.md) in the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md), it became an important Elvish realm in the following Ages, known for its havens and [ships](../S/Ships.md) that would sail for the [West](../A/Aman.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

Lindon was a name of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md), a region west of the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md), in [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md). After the deluge of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), Lindon became the westernmost land of the continent of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

The regions of [Forlindon](../F/Forlindon.md) and [Harlindon](../H/Harlindon.md) were the chief parts of Lindon, which extended north of the [Gulf of Lune](../G/Gulf_of_Lune.md) to include the lands east of the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md), and west of the River [Lune](Lune.md) as far as the inflow of the [Little Lune](Little_Lune.md) (beyond that was [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains.md) territory).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-eriador_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-4">[3]</a></sup> South of the Lune it had no clear bounds, but the [Tower Hills](../T/Tower_Hills.md) (as they were later called) were maintained as an outpost.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

When [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md) captured Sauron and took him to [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md) found peace, and Gil-galad was free to extend his power and influence so that the Elves of Lindon controlled most of the Westlands, even to the borders of [Greenwood the Great](../G/Greenwood_the_Great.md), until the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md), when Sauron returned to Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-6">[5]</a></sup>

In the tumult following the Downfall of Númenor, Lindon suffered great loss as "the sea rode in upon the land", and it shrank by the end of the Second Age.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Elder Days {#Elder_Days}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="../O/Ossiriand.md" title="Ossiriand">Ossiriand</a></b></div></div>

The name Lindon was first used by the exiled [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) for the region of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-8">[7]</a></sup>

Lindon was the only part of Beleriand that survived the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), the rest of the land having been broken or submerged.<sup id="cite_ref-PMII78_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMII78-9">[8]</a></sup> However, [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md) the Great Sea broke through the mountain chain, creating the [Gulf of Lhûn](../G/Gulf_of_Lhûn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-6">[5]</a></sup>

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/L/Liz_Danforth_-_Elves_of_Lindon.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/25/Liz_Danforth_-_Elves_of_Lindon.jpg/200px-Liz_Danforth_-_Elves_of_Lindon.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="169" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/2/25/Liz_Danforth_-_Elves_of_Lindon.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="260" data-file-height="220"/></a><figcaption><a href="Liz_Danforth.md" title="Liz Danforth">Liz Danforth</a> - <i>Elves of Lindon</i></figcaption></figure>

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1](../S/Second_Age_1.md), the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) built the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) in the newly-formed Gulf of Lhûn where the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) sailed the [Straight Road](../S/Straight_Road.md), but most of them were unwilling at first to forsake the lands they fought in and preferred to linger there. This was the foundation of Lindon. [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) was the last heir of the [kings of the Noldor](../K/King_of_the_Noldor.md) in exile, and acknowledged as [High King of the Elves of the West](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md), so he was the king in Lindon.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rings_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-6">[5]</a></sup>

In the beginning of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), most of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) that remained in Middle-earth dwelt in Lindon, but many [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md), survivors of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) and [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md), left Lindon before [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1000](../S/Second_Age_1000.md) to establish realms among the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-6">[5]</a></sup> [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) brought many Noldor, [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), and [Green-elves](../G/Green-elves.md) out of Lindon into [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) to dwell there for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-10">[9]</a></sup> Many of the Noldor in Lindon led by [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md) established the realm of [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [750](../S/Second_Age_750.md) near the [West-gate](../W/West-gate.md) of [Khazad-Dûm](../K/Khazad-Dûm~69981.md) due to the recent discovery of [mithril](../M/Mithril.md) in Khazad-Dûm.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [600](../S/Second_Age_600.md), [Entulessë](../E/Entulessë.md), a ship from [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), arrived in Mithlond where Gil-galad welcomed the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md), before reestablishing contact with their [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) kin, the [Middle Men](../M/Middle_Men.md).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>

A fair being calling himself [Annatar](../A/Annatar.md), an emissary of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), came seeking entrance. Gil-galad and Master [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) sensed that he was not what he seemed, and denied him. Messengers were sent from Lindon with warnings, but they were unheeded, and Annatar was received gladly, mostly in Eregion. However, Gil-galad and Elrond were proven correct, as Annatar was indeed [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) and later, [there was war in Eriador](../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-6">[5]</a></sup> Elrond led the forces of Lindon to [battle to defend Eregion](../S/Sack_of_Eregion.md). With them, and other [Elves](../E/Elves_of_Eregion.md) who fled the destruction of Eregion, he established [Imladris](../I/Imladris.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1697](../S/Second_Age_1697.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Elves called that time [Days of Flight](../D/Dark_Years.md) as many fled to Lindon and thence over the [Sea](../S/Sea.md) to the [Uttermost West](../U/Uttermost_West.md). But in Lindon Gil-galad still maintained his power, and Sauron dared not as yet to pass the [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md) nor to assail the [Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md); and Gil-galad was aided by the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md). In the [Dark Years](../D/Dark_Years.md), Sauron had conquered most of the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md), but in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1700](../S/Second_Age_1700.md) [Tar-Minastir](../T/Tar-Minastir.md) sent a fleet to Lindon and the combined army of Lindon and Númenor drove Sauron's forces out of Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rings_6-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-6">[5]</a></sup>

### Later history {#Later_history}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Angelo_Montanini_-_Northern_Waste.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/3/35/Angelo_Montanini_-_Northern_Waste.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="253" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="300" data-file-height="253"/></a><figcaption><i>Northern Waste</i> by Angelo Montanini</figcaption></figure>

After the fall of Gil-galad (during the time of the [Kings of Arnor](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md)), there was still a remnant of his people, the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), who dwelt at the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) of further inland in Lindon, who, aside from [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), were most of the remaining Noldor of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). [Cirdan](../C/Cirdan.md) remained the Lord of the Havens and ruled Lindon from the Grey Havens.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rings_6-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-6">[5]</a></sup>

During the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), Lindon supported [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) in the [Angmar War](../A/Angmar_War.md). It assisted [Arveleg I](../A/Arveleg_I.md) and [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md) drive off the [Hill-men](../H/Hill-men.md) from the [Weather Hills](../W/Weather_Hills.md). Later, with [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md)'s help, [Arveleg](../A/Arveleg_I.md)'s young son, [Araphor](../A/Araphor.md), drove [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md)'s armies from [Fornost](../F/Fornost.md) and the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md). Combining forces with [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), and the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) who joined them from beyond the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), they subdued Angmar for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-4">[3]</a></sup> Lindon also assisted King [Araval](../A/Araval.md) in a coalition with [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), in another battle against Angmar in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1851](../T/Third_Age_1851.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-13">[12]</a></sup> In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md), Elves from Lindon brought a ship to [Forochel](../F/Forochel.md) to rescue [Arvedui Last-king](../A/Arvedui_Last-king.md). The crew endured a perilous journey, but Arvedui rashly attempted to return that winter, and all aboard perished. The [next year](../T/Third_Age_1976.md), the forces of Lindon joined the [Host of the West](<../H/Host_of_the_West_(Arnor).md>), and Cirdan led them to victory in the [Battle of Fornost](../B/Battle_of_Fornost.md).<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup>

By [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [171](../F/Fourth_Age_171.md), few [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) remained in Lindon, if any at all. [Men](../M/Men.md) did not settle in Lindon.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Names and etymology {#Names_and_etymology}

In a post-[LotR](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) writing, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) says that the name *Lindon* was coined by the Green-elves, derived from [Nandorin](../N/Nandorin.md)(?) \**Lindānā*. The Sindar called the country *Dor Lindon* and the Noldor *Lindóne* (or *Lindónë*<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup>) but both also adopted the name *Lindon*.<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-16">[15]</a></sup>

The name *Lindon* contains the element *lin-* ("sing, song") as also seen in [Lindar](Lindar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[16]</a></sup> *Lindon* means "land of music".<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-8">[7]</a></sup>

The translation "Land of the singers", conceived to be a name in the [Ossiriandic](../N/Nandorin.md) tongue, has been suggested by [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md), [Arden Smith](../A/Arden_R._Smith.md), [Patrick Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md), et al. in their linguistic contribution to *[Arnor: The Land](../A/Arnor%253A_The_Land.md)*

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early versions of the *[The Lord of the Rings Appendices](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Appendices.md)*, Gil-galad is said to have founded Lindon in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) 10.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[17]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[John Garth](../J/John_Garth.md) points out that both the river [Lhûn](Lhûn.md) and the river <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Severn" title="wikipedia:River Severn">Severn</a> flow from mountains (the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) and the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambrian_Mountains" title="wikipedia:Cambrian Mountains">Cambrian Mountains</a>, respectively) in a rough semicircle east, south, and then west into a wide gulf (the [Gulf of Lune](../G/Gulf_of_Lune.md)) and the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol_Channel" title="wikipedia:Bristol Channel">Bristol Channel</a>, respectively).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[18]</a></sup> While the Gulf of Lune divides [Harlindon](../H/Harlindon.md) from [Forlindon](../F/Forlindon.md), the Bristol Channel divides <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales" title="wikipedia:Wales">Wales</a> from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerset" title="wikipedia:Somerset">Somerset</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devon" title="wikipedia:Devon">Devon</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornwall" title="wikipedia:Cornwall">Cornwall</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[19]</a></sup> He suggests that this supports the notion that the elven <a class="mw-selflink selflink">Lindon</a> reflects the Celtic west of Britain (i.e. Wales and Cornwall).<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[20]</a></sup>

Erwin and Oliver Schick were commissioned to write supplements on Lindon and Mithlond, respectively (both supplements were nearly finished at the time ICE lost their license from <a href="../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md" title="Middle-earth Enterprises">Tolkien Enterprises</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[22]</a></sup> However, scattered information on Lindon and its prominent individuals can be found in several supplements.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[23]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[24]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[25]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[26]</a></sup></dd></dl>

## Notes {#Notes}

<div class="mw-references-wrap"><ol class="references">
<li id="cite_note-2"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-2">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text">In <i><a href="../T/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md" title="The Peoples of Middle-earth">The Peoples of Middle-earth</a></i>, it is stated that after the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md" title="War of the Elves and Sauron">War of the Elves and Sauron</a> and the establishment of <a class="mw-redirect" href="../I/Imladris.md" title="Imladris">Imladris</a>, the realm of Lindon was diminished and did not extend beyond the <a href="../B/Blue_Mountains.md" title="Blue Mountains">Blue Mountains</a> any more. This is contradictory to <i><a href="../A/Appendix_A.md" title="Appendix A">Appendix A</a></i> which states that by the time the <a href="../R/Red_Book_of_Westmarch.md" title="Red Book of Westmarch">Red Book of Westmarch</a> was written in the late <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">Third Age</a>, the lands beyond the <a class="mw-redirect" href="Lune.md" title="Lune">Lune</a> were still part of Lindon.</span>
</li>
