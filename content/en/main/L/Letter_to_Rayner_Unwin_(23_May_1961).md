On [23 May](/wiki/23_May) [1961](/wiki/1961), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** The art of [Cor Blok](/wiki/Cor_Blok).
- **Publication:** In [2011](/wiki/2011), a quotation from the letter appeared in *[A Tolkien Tapestry](/wiki/A_Tolkien_Tapestry)* (pp. 7, 25).
