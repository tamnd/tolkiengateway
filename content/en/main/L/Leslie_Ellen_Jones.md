**Leslie Ellen Jones** holds a Ph.D. in Folklore and Mythology Studies from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA" title="wikipedia:UCLA">UCLA</a>, with a specialization in comparative and Celtic mythology. She has written extensively on modern uses of mythology in popular fiction, film, and television.
