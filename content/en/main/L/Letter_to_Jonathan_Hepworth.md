On [18 December](/wiki/18_December) [1963](/wiki/1963), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote a **[letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Jonathan Hepworth** and his father.<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Publication:** None.
- **Notes:** Contains a previously unknown phrase in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *nai lye hiruva airea [amanar](/wiki/Amanar "Amanar")!*, translated by Tolkien as "may thee find a blessed Amanar", referring to "the [Yule](/wiki/Yule) and the beginning of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun)'s return" and representing an "ancient [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish)" equivalent to "merry Christmas".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Autograph letter signed by J.R.R. Tolkien. In English and Elvish. 2 pages, 87 x 112mm, notecard and half page, 175 x 136mm.<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the auction<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup> {#From_the_auction[1]}

‘I have one criticism and that is that you show the Three Rings inside the unbroken One Ring’: Tolkien discusses his cover art illustrations for the *Lord of the Rings* trilogy with a young fan, adding a Yuletide greeting in Elvish. Tolkien thanks Jonathan for his folding card, ‘especially for the (horrible) view of Mordor and the destruction of Orodruin’, though he does note that the Three Rings should not be shown appearing within the One Ring. ‘I drew “jackets”, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=File:Jrrt_lotr_cover_design.jpg&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="File:Jrrt lotr cover design.jpg (page does not exist)">a separate one for each volume</a>, but they proved too expensive, and what was used was a (defective) version of the first’, the design for which Tolkien goes on to describe. Having read Jonathan’s message in Runes, he replies with one of his own, wishing his young correspondent a merry Christmas in ancient Elvish, English (‘May thee find a blessed Amanar’) and in Runes.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> Tolkien adds a covering letter to Jonathan’s father, thanking him for his kind words and for Jonathan’s card: ‘I see that he has been studying with effect the rather difficult [Runes](/wiki/Runes). How about the [Elvish script](/wiki/Tengwar "Tengwar")?’.

Tolkien originally designed [three individual dust-jackets](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings/Original_dust-jacket_designs "The Lord of the Rings/Original dust-jacket designs") for *The Fellowship of the Ring*, *The Two Towers*, and *The Return of the King*; the illustration for the first of these, which showed the One Ring threatened by three hostile rings, was later adapted as the cover artwork for all three titles. As he complains here, the eventual design showed only one of the three hostile rings.
