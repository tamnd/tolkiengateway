<i><b>lind</b></i> or <i><b>lin</b></i> is an element in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). In Quenya it appears sometimes as a contraction of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Linde&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Linde (page does not exist)">linde</a></i> "air, tune" or "song, music".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Quenya {#Quenya}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Ainulindal%C3%AB_(R%C3%BAmil%27s_work)" title="Ainulindalë (Rúmil&#39;s work)">Ainu<b>lind</b>alë</a></i> = "The Music of the Ainur" (*lindele* = music)
- <i><a href="/wiki/Laurelin" title="Laurelin">Laure<b>lin</b></a></i> = "Song of Gold"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Lindon" title="Lindon"><b>Lind</b>on</a></i> = "Land of music"
- <i><b>lind</b>e</i> = "singer"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Lindir" title="Lindir"><b>Lind</b>ir</a></i> = "Song Man"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Teiglin" title="Teiglin">Teig<b>lin</b></a></i> = "Boundary Singer"
