<i><b>lemin</b></i> is a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) word meaning "five".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") also suggested the form *lempe* ("five"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> but in later manuscripts emended that form to *lemin*.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
