On [12 December](/wiki/12_December) [1955](/wiki/1955), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to David Masson** (librarian at the [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds)).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Reactions to a review of *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)* published in the *Times Literary Supplement* (25 November 1955).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TS_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A quotation from the letter has been published in *[The Road to Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Road_to_Middle-earth)*.<sup id="cite_ref-TS_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Tolkien remarked that the reviewer should not have made such a fuss over giving quarters to <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">orcs</a>. &#39;Surely how often &#34;quarter&#34; is given is off the point in a book that breathes Mercy from start to finish: in which the central hero is at last divested of all arms, except his will? &#34;Forgive us our trespasses as we forgive them that trespass against us. Lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil&#34;, are words that occur to me, and of which the scene, in the <a href="/wiki/Cracks_of_Doom" title="Cracks of Doom">Sammath Naur</a> was meant to be a &#34;fairy-story&#34; exemplum ...&#39;
</p>

</blockquote>
