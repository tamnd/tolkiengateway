**Lúthien** can refer to:

- [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien), a heroine of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) and the daughter of [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) and [Melian](/wiki/Melian) the [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar").
- [Lúthien](</wiki/L%C3%BAthien_(son_of_Gelmir)> "Lúthien (son of Gelmir)"), a [Gnome](/wiki/Gnome) in a prose fragment following *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.
- [Lúthien](</wiki/L%C3%BAthien_(son_of_Telimektar)> "Lúthien (son of Telimektar)"), a [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar"), the son of [Telimektar](/wiki/Telimektar) in *The Book of Lost Tales*.
- [Lúthien](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine "Ælfwine"), a name given to [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine) by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) in *The Book of Lost Tales*.
- [Lúthien](/wiki/Luthany "Luthany"), a name for <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a> in *The Book of Lost Tales*.
- [Lúthien](/wiki/Hendwine "Hendwine"), a [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name for Hendwine, a son of [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol) in a text associated with *The Book of Lost Tales*.
