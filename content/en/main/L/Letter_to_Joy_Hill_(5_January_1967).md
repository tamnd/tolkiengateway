On [5 January](/wiki/5_January) [1967](/wiki/1967), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Joy Hill](/wiki/Joy_Hill)**.<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien thanked Hill for sending [a copy](/wiki/List_of_books_in_Tolkien%27s_library#The_Children's_Treasury_of_Literature "List of books in Tolkien's library") of *[The Children's Treasury of Literature](/wiki/The_Children%27s_Treasury_of_Literature)*, which included a chapter from *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* illustrated by Robert J. Lee. Tolkien did not like Lee's illustrations for the chapter, calling them "vulgar, stupid and entirely out of keeping with the text." He ended the letter by discussing his *[Beowulf](</wiki/Beowulf_(poem)> "Beowulf (poem)")* lecture.
- **Publication:** A description of the letter was published in [*The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology*](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide") (p. 685).
