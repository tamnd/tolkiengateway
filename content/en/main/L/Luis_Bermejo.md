**Luis Bermejo** ([12 August](/wiki/12_August) [1931](/wiki/1931) - [12 December](/wiki/12_December) [2015](/wiki/2015)) was a Spanish comic book artist who illustrated the [1979](/wiki/1979)–[1981](/wiki/1981) *[Lord of the Rings comic book adaptation](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(comic_book)> "The Lord of the Rings (comic book)").*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
