The **Little Lune** was a western tributary of the river [Lune](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn "Lhûn"). It flowed from its source on the eastern side of the northern part of the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) eastwards until its confluence with the Lune.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

The Little Lune was the northern border of the [elven](/wiki/Elven "Elven") realm of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) and north of it was [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") territory.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> This territory included dwarf-mines close to the northern end of the Ered Luin,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> in which [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui), the last king of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) fled after the conquest of [Fornost Erain](/wiki/Fornost_Erain) by the troops of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1974](/wiki/Third_Age_1974 "Third Age 1974").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Lune is a [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name. It is an alteration of the elvish name *Lhûn*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>
