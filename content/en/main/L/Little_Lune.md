The **Little Lune** was a western tributary of the river [Lune](Lhûn.md). It flowed from its source on the eastern side of the northern part of the [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md) eastwards until its confluence with the Lune.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

The Little Lune was the northern border of the [elven](../E/Elven.md) realm of [Lindon](Lindon.md) and north of it was [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) territory.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> This territory included dwarf-mines close to the northern end of the Ered Luin,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> in which [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md), the last king of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md) fled after the conquest of [Fornost Erain](../F/Fornost_Erain.md) by the troops of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1974](../T/Third_Age_1974.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Lune is a [Westron](../W/Westron.md) name. It is an alteration of the elvish name *Lhûn*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>
