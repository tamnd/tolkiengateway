**Lilies** were large flowers that grow on slender stalks. Given many references to these flowers they must have been widespread in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth): In [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") there were two [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) who were named "Lily", [Lily Baggins](/wiki/Lily_Baggins)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Lily Cotton](/wiki/Lily_Cotton).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Among [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)'s amazing array of fireworks was one in the shape of a lily, which could famously hang in the air throughout an entire evening.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") found lilies when they came to [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn), ill in the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing), was described as "white as a lily".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) sang of the green fields of [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin) where "the while lilies sway".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1984-1997: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Two types of lily are distinguished:</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>White Lilies</b></i> grew around the lakes and streams of Lebennin. They were brewed by the Dunnish into drinks and substances like <a href="/wiki/Metals_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing#Cranor" title="Metals in Middle-earth Role Playing">Cranor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Ladylock</b></i> (<a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Finhiril</i>, <a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Herifindë</i>), also known as <i>Hanging Lily</i>, was a grey-green moss-like plant that hung from ancient deciduous branches. It could be placed briefly in hot water and then laid on a wound to stop bleeding. Dried and brewed as tea it could rouse patients from a <a href="/wiki/Plants_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing#Saewedhil" title="Plants in Middle-earth Role Playing">saewedhil</a>-induced coma.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><i><b>Lily-of-the-Valley</b></i>, a plant with small white flowers, can be grown by apprentice farmers<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>, while scholars can use its leaves to create dyes and paints in various green shades.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> A <i><b>wood lily</b></i> is a red flower that players earn as a reward during their quests.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup></dd></dl>
