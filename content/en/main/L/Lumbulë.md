<i><b>lumbulë</b></i> (or *lumbule*) is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "(heavy) shadow; dark shadow".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word seems to be an extended/augmentative form of the word <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lumbe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lumbe (page does not exist)">lumbe</a></i> "gloom" (cf. <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tumba&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tumba (page does not exist)">tumba</a></i> and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tumbale&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tumbale (page does not exist)">tumbale</a></i>), from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=LUM&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LUM (page does not exist)">LUM</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
