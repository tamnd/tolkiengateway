**Lothlórien** may refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- The **Kingdom of [Lothlórien](Lothlórien.md)** in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), often referred to as *Lórien*.
    - [Elves of Lórien](../G/Galadhrim.md), the Silvan Elves - or *Galadhrim* - who lived there.
    - [King of Lórien](../K/King_of_Lórien.md), the former title of the rulers of Lothlórien.
    - [Lord and Lady of Lórien](Lord_and_Lady_of_Lórien.md), the titles taken by [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md).
