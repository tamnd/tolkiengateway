\*<i><b>linta</b></i> (pl. **linte**) is a [quenya](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") word meaning "swift".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The etymology is not clear, but perhaps hints to a [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") \*LIN or \*LIT

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

This word is a relic from [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s juvenile language called [Nevbosh](/wiki/Nevbosh). In that language *lint* means "quick, clever, nimble"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
