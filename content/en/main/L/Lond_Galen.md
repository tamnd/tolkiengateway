In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), **Lond Galen** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Green Haven”) was the capital of [Anfalas](/wiki/Anfalas).<sup id="cite_ref-desc_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-desc-1">[1]</a></sup> It later re-appeared, though without further context, on a map of West Gondor in *[Maps of Middle-earth](/wiki/Maps_of_Middle-earth)* for [Decipher](/wiki/Decipher)'s *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Founded by Dúnedain in the 19th century of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) as a small trading harbour, the fickle nature of its Daen neighbours led to the harbour being surrounded by a fifteen foot wall. As times became more peaceful and the town expanded, the walls came down. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1652&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1652 (page does not exist)">1652</a>, [Corsair](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar "Corsairs of Umbar") raids led to the construction of a new wall.<sup id="cite_ref-desc_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-desc-1">[1]</a></sup>
