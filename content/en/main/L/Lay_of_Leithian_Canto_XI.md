This [Canto](/wiki/Cantos_of_the_Lay_of_Leithian "Cantos of the Lay of Leithian") starts with a description of [Dor-na-Fauglith](/wiki/Dor-na-Fauglith) ("Land under Choking Ash"), but changes its name to "Land of Thirst". [Beren](/wiki/Beren) gazes across the plain and sees afar [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim). He gives [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin)'s horse leave to depart; the last [horse](/wiki/Horse "Horse") to stand upon that plain. Then he sings his [Song of Parting](/wiki/Song_of_Parting), "grim-hearted, bitter, fierce and fey." But as he ends and begins to stride down toward death, [Lúthien Tinúviel](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien "Lúthien") comes. Beren wonders and asks where [Huan](/wiki/Huan) is, but Lúthien knows not.

Finally Huan comes, and brings forth two bodies and lays them at their feet: that of [wolf](/wiki/Werewolves "Werewolves") [Draugluin](/wiki/Draugluin), and that of a [vampire](/wiki/Vampire "Vampire") [Thuringwethil](/wiki/Thuringwethil). Beren asks why he brought them; were they just trophies of their victory over [Thû](/wiki/Th%C3%BB)? But Huan spoke for only the second time in his life, and declares that they must don the bodies as raiments and sneak into [Angband](/wiki/Angband). With [Elvish](/wiki/Elves "Elves") magic Lúthien causes them to take on the shape of the bodies, and then they stumble across the thirty desert of Dor-na-Fauglith. At last they reach the gates and towers of Angband, and enter the roots of Thangorodrim.
