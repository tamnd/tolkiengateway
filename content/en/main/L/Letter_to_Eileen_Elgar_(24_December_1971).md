On [24 December](../_/24_December.md) [1971](../_/1971.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Mrs Eileen Elgar**.

- **Contents:** Tolkien writes about the death of [Edith](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md). He also writes that he thinks [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md)'s *Hobbit* poster map is better than *A Map of Middle-earth*; he is glad Elgar liked the [Narnia](../T/The_Chronicles_of_Narnia.md) books, although he himself is not fond of them; and he discusses the naming of pets after [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) (commenting that [Hilary](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md) has a dog which is called [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) when being good, and Baggins when being bad).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Descriptions of the letter appeared in *[Sotheby's English Literature and English History 6-7 December 1984](../S/Sotheby's_English_Literature_and_English_History_6-7_December_1984.md)* and *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md): Chronology*. An excerpt concerning Narnia was published in [*Mythlore* 121/122](../M/Mythlore_121%252F122.md).

## Excerpts {#Excerpts}

"\[Edith\] was my *Lúthien Tinúviel*, with her river \[*sic*? as transcribed, for "raven"\] hair and fair face and bright starry eyes"

"I am glad that you have discovered <u>Narnia</u>. These stories are deservedly very popular; but since you ask if <u>I</u> like them I am afraid the answer is <u>No</u>. I do <u>not</u> like 'allegory', and least of all religious allegory of this kind. But that is a difference of taste which we both recognized and did not interfere with our friendship."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
