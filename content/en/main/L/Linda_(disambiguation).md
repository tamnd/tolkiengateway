**Linda** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word *[linda](/wiki/Linda "Linda")* meaning "fair".
- The element *[lind](/wiki/Lind "Lind")*, related with "music" both in Quenya and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (not to be confused with *[lin](/wiki/Lin "Lin")*).
- One of the *[Lindar](/wiki/Lindar)*, the name the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) used for themselves.
- [Linda Proudfoot](/wiki/Linda_Proudfoot) ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1262](/wiki/Third_Age_2862 "Third Age 2862") - [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1363](/wiki/Third_Age_2963 "Third Age 2963")), aunt of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins).
- [Lindo](/wiki/Lindo), the wise keeper of the [Cottage of Lost Play](/wiki/Cottage_of_Lost_Play).
