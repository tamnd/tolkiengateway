The **Limlight** was a river at the north of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) that ran through northern [Fangorn](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest "Fangorn Forest") and emptied into the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It formed the southern boundary of the [Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Field_of_Celebrant), and was claimed by [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) as the southern boundary of their realm.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was also the historic northern border of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and later of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-River_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-5">[5]</a></sup> the Northern Army of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) had been driven by the [Balchoth](/wiki/Balchoth) and [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) across the Limlight. The men of Gondor were facing destruction when [Eorl the Young](/wiki/Eorl "Eorl"), leading the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), fell upon the rear of the enemy and drove them southward with heavy losses over the Limlight into [the Wold](/wiki/The_Wold "The Wold").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), at the behest of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), hunted and captured [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) in [3017](/wiki/Third_Age_3017 "Third Age 3017");<sup id="cite_ref-TA_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-5">[5]</a></sup> on his journey north to [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) they crossed the Limlight since Aragorn was taking a route west of the Great River.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In [February](/wiki/February) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) floated past the mouth of the Limlight as they headed south upon the Anduin.<sup id="cite_ref-River_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Limlight* is a modernized form of the word *Limliht* from [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

The first element of the name is from an alien [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) or [Pre-Númenórean](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) form which was obscure in [Westron](/wiki/Westron). The second element refers to the clarity of its bright waters.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") provided different explanations of the exact meaning and even languages used in the name, including *Limlich*, *Limliht*, *Limlaith*<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> and *Limhîr*.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Limlight River forms the southern border of &#34;The Great River&#34; region. It gives name to the &#34;Limlight Gorge&#34; on the northern bank, &#34;Limlight Glade&#34; on the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Fangorn" title="Fangorn">Fangorn</a> side, and the &#34;Limlight Falls&#34; through which is plummets below. The river then flows past &#34;Stangard&#34;, a Rohirrim military town outside the borders of <a href="/wiki/Rohan" title="Rohan">Rohan</a> proper. It eventually joins the <a href="/wiki/Anduin" title="Anduin">Anduin</a> at the &#34;Rushgore&#34; marsh.</dd></dl>
