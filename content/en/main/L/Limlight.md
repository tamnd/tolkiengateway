The **Limlight** was a river at the north of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) that ran through northern [Fangorn](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md) and emptied into the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It formed the southern boundary of the [Field of Celebrant](../F/Field_of_Celebrant.md), and was claimed by [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) of [Lothlórien](Lothlórien.md) as the southern boundary of their realm.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was also the historic northern border of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and later of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-River_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-5">[5]</a></sup> the Northern Army of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) had been driven by the [Balchoth](../B/Balchoth.md) and [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) across the Limlight. The men of Gondor were facing destruction when [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl.md), leading the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md), fell upon the rear of the enemy and drove them southward with heavy losses over the Limlight into [the Wold](../T/The_Wold.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), at the behest of [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), hunted and captured [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) in [3017](../T/Third_Age_3017.md);<sup id="cite_ref-TA_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-5">[5]</a></sup> on his journey north to [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) they crossed the Limlight since Aragorn was taking a route west of the Great River.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In [February](../F/February.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) floated past the mouth of the Limlight as they headed south upon the Anduin.<sup id="cite_ref-River_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Limlight* is a modernized form of the word *Limliht* from [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

The first element of the name is from an alien [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) or [Pre-Númenórean](../P/Pre-Númenórean.md) form which was obscure in [Westron](../W/Westron.md). The second element refers to the clarity of its bright waters.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) provided different explanations of the exact meaning and even languages used in the name, including *Limlich*, *Limliht*, *Limlaith*<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> and *Limhîr*.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2012: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Limlight River forms the southern border of &#34;The Great River&#34; region. It gives name to the &#34;Limlight Gorge&#34; on the northern bank, &#34;Limlight Glade&#34; on the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../F/Fangorn.md" title="Fangorn">Fangorn</a> side, and the &#34;Limlight Falls&#34; through which is plummets below. The river then flows past &#34;Stangard&#34;, a Rohirrim military town outside the borders of <a href="../R/Rohan.md" title="Rohan">Rohan</a> proper. It eventually joins the <a href="../A/Anduin.md" title="Anduin">Anduin</a> at the &#34;Rushgore&#34; marsh.</dd></dl>
