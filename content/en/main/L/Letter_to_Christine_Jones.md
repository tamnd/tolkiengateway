On [9 December](/wiki/9_December) [1965](/wiki/1965), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Christine Jones**, a reader in New Jersey.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-3">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Reply to Jones' comments on the climax of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, which he found interesting. Apologies for his short letter.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of and quotation from the letter appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*.
- **Archive:** [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>If I answered all the letters I receive as they deserved, I should write nothing else.
</p>
