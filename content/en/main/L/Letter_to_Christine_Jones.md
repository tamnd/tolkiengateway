On [9 December](../_/9_December.md) [1965](../_/1965.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Christine Jones**, a reader in New Jersey.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-3">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Reply to Jones' comments on the climax of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, which he found interesting. Apologies for his short letter.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of and quotation from the letter appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md)*.
- **Archive:** [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>If I answered all the letters I receive as they deserved, I should write nothing else.
</p>
