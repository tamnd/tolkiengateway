<i><b>lempë</b></i> is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for the number five.<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT47_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In a late manuscript, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") also suggested the Quenya form <i><b>lemen</b></i> ("five"), which had been rejected in other manuscripts.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
