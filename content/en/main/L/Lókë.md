<i><b>lókë</b></i> is a noun meaning "[snake](/wiki/Snakes "Snakes"), [serpent](/wiki/Serpents "Serpents")" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The ([Qenya](/wiki/Qenya)) form *lóke (-ī)* ("dragon") also appears.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety370_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety370-2">[2]</a></sup> In earlier manuscripts appear the forms *lókë* ("drake")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and *lōke* ("dragon").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

It has been suggested that the spelling according to the Quenya style of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* would be *lócë*, meaning "dragon, snake, serpent, drake".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is derived from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=LOK&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LOK (page does not exist)">LOK</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety370_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety370-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
