**Lond Daer Enedh** (also spelt **Ened**) was a great harbour in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) founded by the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans).

## History {#History}

Originally, the harbour, under the name <i><b><a href="/wiki/Vinyalond%C3%AB" title="Vinyalondë">Vinyalondë</a></b></i>, or "New Haven", was founded by [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor)'s crown prince [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") on the estuary of the river [Gwathló](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3), between [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750") and [800](/wiki/Second_Age_800 "Second Age 800").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Númenórean hunger for ever more wealth shifted focus to the establishment of newer havens to the south, [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) and [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar). Following this, the haven was one of several major ports in Middle-earth, and was known as Lond Daer Enedh, or "Great Middle Haven", between the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) and Pelargir.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> Despite this, Númenor still maintained traffic on the [Gwathló](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3) as far as the fortified town of [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad), and Lond Daer was expanded and fortified.<sup id="cite_ref-6d_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6d-4">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. 264</sup>

After the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor), [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) founded the realm of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) in the lands north of Lond Daer, which was still part of the safest and most efficient route between the [Realms in Exile](/wiki/Realms_in_Exile) by sea, in conjunction with [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad) and the [Great Road](/wiki/Great_Road); but as Lond Daer fell into ruin,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup> Tharbad succeeded it in importance.<sup id="cite_ref-6d_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6d-4">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. 264</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lond Daer Enedh* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Great Middle Haven",<sup id="cite_ref-6d_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6d-4">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:264</sup> clearly composed of *[lond](/wiki/Lond "Lond")* ("haven"), <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Daer&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Daer (page does not exist)">daer</a></i> ("great, large") and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Enedh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Enedh (page does not exist)">enedh</a></i> ("middle").

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Ruins_of_the_Lost_Realm" title="Ruins of the Lost Realm">Ruins of the Lost Realm</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Some decades before <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2955" title="Third Age 2955">2955</a>, a mix of refugees and descendants of old inhabitants built a settlement in and near the ruins of Lond Daer. In that year, a sea serpent began terrorizing the new citizens. A woman named Nimue was able to find the beast&#39;s lair, possibly advised by <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a>, and through luck managed to slay it with an ancient spear of <a href="/wiki/Westernesse" title="Westernesse">Westernesse</a> found in the ruins. In gratitude, the people acclaimed her as queen.<sup id="cite_ref-history_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-6">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-gandalf_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gandalf-7">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>A longhouse built on the ruins of an old villa served as the Queen&#39;s Hall, with the skull of the sea serpent hanging above her throne. The only other major building of note was that of Johan Fleet, a merchant whose fortune was built on silver his grandfather, a former master of <a href="/wiki/Tharbad" title="Tharbad">Tharbad</a>, stole.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-history_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-6">[4]</a></sup> Gandalf, who occasionally visited, was considered the queen&#39;s court wizard.<sup id="cite_ref-gandalf_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gandalf-7">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2972" title="Third Age 2972">2972</a>, either due to <a href="/wiki/Ruins_of_the_Lost_Realm#Plot" title="Ruins of the Lost Realm">an attack from the Kathu­phaz­gân or Dunlending raids</a>, the settlement fell. Queen Nimue and her hall perished in the flames.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>
