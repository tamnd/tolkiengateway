**Lily** can refer to:

## Flowers {#Flowers}

- [Lilies](/wiki/Lilies), large white flowers seen throughout [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth)
- [Water-lilies](/wiki/Water-lilies), large flowers seen on the surface of water.

## People {#People}

- [Lily Baggins](/wiki/Lily_Baggins) ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1222](/wiki/Third_Age_2822 "Third Age 2822") - [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1312](/wiki/Third_Age_2912 "Third Age 2912")), great-aunt of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins).
- [Lily Brown](/wiki/Lily_Brown), mother of [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton).
- [Lily](/wiki/Lily), the Draper's daughter, in *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*.

## Places {#Places}

- "Lily of the Valley", translation of *Lothengriol*, one of the seven names of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).
