**Lothlórien** is the fourteenth track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)* by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore). It was composed and performed by Elizabeth Fraser.

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div class="poem">
<p>Verse 1 (<a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Quenya</a>):<br/>
<br/>
A Olórin i yáresse<br/>
Mentaner i Númeherui<br/>
Tírien i Rómenóri<br/>
Maiaron i Oiosaila<br/>
Manan elye etevanne<br/>
Nórie i melanelye?<br/>
<br/>
Verse 2 and 3 (<a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">Sindarin</a>):<br/>
<br/>
Mithrandir, Mithrandir, A Randir Vithren<br/>
ú-reniathach i amar galen<br/>
I reniad lín ne môr, nuithannen<br/>
In gwidh ristennin, i fae narchannen<br/>
I lach Anor ed ardhon gwannen<br/>
Calad veleg, ethuiannen.
</p>
</div>

## Translation {#Translation}

<div class="poem">
<p>Olórin, who once was...<br/>
Sent by the Lords of the West<br/>
To guard the lands of the East<br/>
Wisest of all Maiar<br/>
What drove you to leave<br/>
That which you loved?<br/>
<br/>
Mithrandir, Mithrandir, O Pilgrim Grey<br/>
No more will you wander the green fields of this earth<br/>
Your journey has ended in darkness.<br/>
The bonds but, the spirit broken<br/>
The Flame of Anor has left this World<br/>
A great light, has gone out.
</p>
</div>
