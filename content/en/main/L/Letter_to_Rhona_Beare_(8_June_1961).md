On [8 June](../_/8_June.md) [1961](../_/1961.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Rhona Beare](../R/Rhona_Beare.md)**.

This is one of the two letters that were printed in the *[J.R.R. Tolkien's Letters to Rhona Beare](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_Letters_to_Rhona_Beare.md)*, being the original of [Letter 230](Letter_230.md).

- **Difference from Letter 230**: Compared to this, Letter 230 omitted its first two paragraphs and final paragraph. The second paragraph is a translation of the [Elvish hymn to Elbereth](../A/A_Elbereth_Gilthoniel.md) in *The Lord of the Rings*, which differs in detail from the literal translation given in *[The Road Goes Ever On](<../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book).md>)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
