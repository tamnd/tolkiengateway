The **Lamb and Flag** is a public house located in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford).

## History {#History}

After c. [1960](/wiki/1960), members of the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings) changed their meeting place to the Lamb and Flag after ["The Bird and Baby"](/wiki/The_Eagle_and_Child "The Eagle and Child") 'modernised' to their dislike.<sup id="cite_ref-HCInklings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HCInklings-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the death of [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) in [1963](/wiki/1963) they continued to meet at the Lamb and Flag, but without Lewis the meetings were eventually abandoned.<sup id="cite_ref-HCInklings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HCInklings-1">[1]</a></sup>
