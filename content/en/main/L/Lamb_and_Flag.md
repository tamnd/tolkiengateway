The **Lamb and Flag** is a public house located in [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md).

## History {#History}

After c. [1960](../_/1960.md), members of the [Inklings](../I/Inklings.md) changed their meeting place to the Lamb and Flag after ["The Bird and Baby"](../T/The_Eagle_and_Child.md) 'modernised' to their dislike.<sup id="cite_ref-HCInklings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HCInklings-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the death of [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) in [1963](../_/1963.md) they continued to meet at the Lamb and Flag, but without Lewis the meetings were eventually abandoned.<sup id="cite_ref-HCInklings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HCInklings-1">[1]</a></sup>
