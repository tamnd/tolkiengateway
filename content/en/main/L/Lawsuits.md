This article lists most of the lawsuits or legal actions relating to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and his works.

## Domain disputes {#Domain_disputes}

### jrrtolkien.com {#jrrtolkien.com}

- **Dispute:** JRR Tolkien Estate Limited v. Network Operations Center, Alberta Hot Rods <a class="external autonumber" href="http://www.wipo.int/amc/en/domains/decisions/html/2003/d2003-0837.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[1]</a>
- **Summary:** No attempt of non-commercial use of the domain was made.
- **Outcome:** The [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md) wins control of the domain.

### tolkien.net {#tolkien.net}

- **Dispute:** JRR Tolkien Estate Limited v. tolkien.net <a class="external autonumber" href="http://www.wipo.int/amc/en/domains/decisions/html/2003/d2003-0833.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[2]</a>
- **Summary:** There was no attempt by the owner to create an actual Tolkien-related website, thus it was assumed the owner was hoping to profit by most likely selling the domain.
- **Outcome:**The Tolkien Estate wins control of the domain.

### shiremail.com {#shiremail.com}

- **Dispute:** JRR Tolkien Estate v. Tarrant Costelloe <a class="external autonumber" href="http://www.theregister.co.uk/2004/07/14/one_domain_to_rule_them_all/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[3]</a> <a class="external autonumber" href="http://www.planet-tolkien.com/news/article_84.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[4]</a>
- **Summary:** The email service was not free and thus attempting to profit from Tolkien's works.
- **Outcome:** The domain name expired and now just displays advertisements.

### lotrfanclub.com {#lotrfanclub.com}

- **Dispute:** The Saul Zaentz Company v. Siarhei Chyzhevich <a class="external autonumber" href="http://www.wipo.int/amc/en/domains/decisions/html/2006/d2006-0370.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[5]</a>
- **Summary:** After the [The Lord of the Rings Fan Club](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Fan_Club.md) was shut down by [Decipher](../D/Decipher.md), they forgot to renew the domain name, lotrfanclub.com, after expiration a pornographic snatched up the domain.
- **Outcome:** [Tolkien Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md) wins control of the domain.

### hobbit.es {#hobbit.es}

- **Dispute:** The Saul Zaentz Company doing business as Tolkien Enterprises v. Marc Rodríguez Negro <a class="external autonumber" href="http://www.wipo.int/amc/en/domains/decisions/html/2006/des2006-0043.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[6]</a>
- **Summary:** Not a non-commercial site.
- **Outcome:** Tolkien Enterprises wins control of the domain.

## Image disputes {#Image_disputes}

### fan.theonering.net/rolozo {#fan.theonering.net/rolozo}

(Ryan Lovett)

- **Date:** June 14, 2004
- **Dispute:** [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md) asked webmaster to remove all maps.
- **Outcome:** All maps were removed.

### tolkienion.com &amp; tolkien-maps.com {#tolkienion.com_&_tolkien-maps.com}

([Steven White](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Smeagol))

- **Date:** June 16, 2004<b></b>
- **Dispute:** [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md) asked website to remove material that infringes their copyrights, specifically the maps.
- **Outcome:** Website complied and removed maps.

### lotrmaps.cjb.net &amp; lotrmaps.middle-earth.us {#lotrmaps.cjb.net_&_lotrmaps.middle-earth.us}

(Jeroen Backx)

- **Dispute:** [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md) asked website to remove copyright infringing maps.
- **Outcome:** Website complies and shuts down.

### 7up.org/gecko/gallery2 {#7up.org/gecko/gallery2}

(Jonatan Alvarsson)

- **Dispute:** [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md) asked website to remove copyright infringing maps.
- **Outcome:** Website complies and removes Tolkien-related maps.

### isildur.com {#isildur.com}

- **Dispute:** [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md) asked website to remove copyright infringing maps.
- **Outcome:** Website complies and removes maps section.

## Content disputes {#Content_disputes}

### merp.com {#merp.com}

- **Dispute:** [Tolkien Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md) asked website to remove all material relating to ICE Roleplaying material.
- **Outcome:** Website complied and removed material.

### esnips.com {#esnips.com}

- **Dispute:** The [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md) asked the website to remove PDFs of [The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md), [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md), and [The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md) from their website. The Estate also asked the website to hand over information on all of the users who downloaded the books.
- **Outcome:** The website complied, removed the PDFs, the Estate went on to "politely and firmly" ask the downloaders to stop. <a class="external autonumber" href="http://www.bloomberg.com/apps/news?pid=20601102&amp;sid=aI.oEehDIdK8" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[7]</a>

### shirepost.com {#shirepost.com}

- **Dispute:** [Tolkien Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md) asked the website to remove all Tolkien-related information and cease selling their Tolkien-related coins.
- **Outcome:** The website initially complied and ceased selling coins, and then became officially licensed.
