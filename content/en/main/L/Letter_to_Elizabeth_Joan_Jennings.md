In [1964](../_/1964.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Elizabeth Joan Jennings**, the daughter of Tolkien's friend <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Henry_Cecil_Jennings&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Henry Cecil Jennings (page does not exist)">Henry Cecil Jennings</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** An autograph note on a first edition of *[Tree and Leaf](../T/Tree_and_Leaf.md)*.

## Transcription {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Dear Elizabeth,<br/>Very best wishes.<br/>I hope you have some<br/>cheer at <a class="mw-redirect" href="../C/Christmas.md" title="Christmas">Christmas</a>.<br/><br/>Remember me to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cecil_Day-Lewis" title="wikipedia:Cecil Day-Lewis">C. D. L.</a><br/>I believe I have only once<br/>met him and wish it<br/>were more often.<br/><br/>Yours ever affectionately,<br/>Ronald
</p>
</blockquote>
