<b><i>L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</i> (Special Issue) n°1</b> (*Tolkien 1892-2012*) is the first special issue of the fanzine <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkiendil.com/asso/mag" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</a></i> by the [Tolkiendil](<../T/Tolkiendil_(Tolkien_society).md>) French association. It was published in July [2012](../_/2012.md) and it is dedicated to the centenary of the birth of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
