**Lake Evendim**, or **Nenuial** ("Lake of Twilight") in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), was a lake in northern [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).

The lake was about 100 miles north of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It was approximately 50 miles from north to south and 20 miles east to west<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> and was bounded on the west and south by the Emyn Uial ([Hills of Evendim](/wiki/Hills_of_Evendim)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin) or Brandywine River flowed out of the lake to the east.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It is said that after the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), both [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) crossed into [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) from [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) with many Noldor in their following, together with [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) and [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves); and for a while they dwelt in the country about Lake Nenuial.<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-3">[3]</a></sup> They departed for [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750").<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), there were [lesser men](/wiki/Middle_Men "Middle Men") living about Lake Evendim, some of whom met with the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) when they first sailed back to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor), [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) established the capital of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas), on the southern shore of the lake in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> the [Battle of Fornost](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost) was fought on the plain between Lake Evendim and the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs) between the [Host of the West](</wiki/Host_of_the_West_(Arnor)> "Host of the West (Arnor)") and the forces of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [15](/wiki/Fourth_Age_15 "Fourth Age 15") [King Elessar](/wiki/King_Elessar) rode from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) to spend some time dwelling by the Lake.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Lake Evendim is a translation of [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *Nenuial*, literally “Lake Twilight", from *[nen](/wiki/Nen "Nen")* ("water", specifically lakes, pools, and lesser rivers)<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> + *[uial](/wiki/Uial "Uial")* ("[twilight](/wiki/Twilight "Twilight")")<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
