**Lavender Grubb** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Lavender was the sister of [Laura Grubb](/wiki/Laura_Grubb) and married [Otto Boffin](/wiki/Otto_Boffin). They had four children: [Hugo](/wiki/Hugo_Boffin "Hugo Boffin"), [Uffo](/wiki/Uffo_Boffin "Uffo Boffin"), [Rollo](/wiki/Rollo_Boffin "Rollo Boffin") and [Primrose](/wiki/Primrose_Boffin "Primrose Boffin").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lavender" title="wikipedia:Lavender">Lavender</a></i> is a fair-scented purple flower.
