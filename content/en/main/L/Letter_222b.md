'Letter 222b is a letter written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and published in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien: Revised and Expanded edition](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien#Revised_and_Expanded_edition "The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien")*.

<br>

## Summary {#Summary}

Tolkien wrote to Unwin to inform him that work on his translations of *Sir Gawain and the Green Knight* and *Pearl* had to be paused while he was getting *The Silmarillion* typed out. He also indicated that he was intending to return to the translations of *Gawain* and *Pearl* but a few days earlier, he received proofs of *[Ancrene Wisse](/wiki/Ancrene_Wisse)*. <br>
