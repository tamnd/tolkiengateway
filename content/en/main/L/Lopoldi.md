The **lopoldi** were an animal species named by the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) on the island of [Elenna](/wiki/Elenna).

## History {#History}

Their description is not known, but they appear to have been [rabbits](/wiki/Rabbits "Rabbits"), which where unknown in the north-western regions of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> The lopoldi were voracious herbivores and multiplied easily quickly, so they were found in large numbers on [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). The Númenóreans did not view the lopoldi as food, leaving them to be the chief food source of [Foxes](/wiki/Foxes), which were viewed as the best natural way to keep the lopoldi population levels under control. As a result of the abundance of the lopoldi, foxes never hunted animals domesticated by the Númenóreans.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> The lopoldi most likely perished during the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lopoldi* was the plural [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for "Rabbits".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

In a linguistic manuscript dating from the 1930s, appears the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) gloss *lopo* ("rabbit") from the primitive *lopō*<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> and connected to the root *LOP*. In earlier [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), it was *lapatte*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
