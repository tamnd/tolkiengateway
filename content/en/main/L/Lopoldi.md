The **lopoldi** were an animal species named by the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) on the island of [Elenna](../E/Elenna.md).

## History {#History}

Their description is not known, but they appear to have been [rabbits](../R/Rabbits.md), which where unknown in the north-western regions of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> The lopoldi were voracious herbivores and multiplied easily quickly, so they were found in large numbers on [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). The Númenóreans did not view the lopoldi as food, leaving them to be the chief food source of [Foxes](../F/Foxes.md), which were viewed as the best natural way to keep the lopoldi population levels under control. As a result of the abundance of the lopoldi, foxes never hunted animals domesticated by the Númenóreans.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> The lopoldi most likely perished during the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lopoldi* was the plural [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word for "Rabbits".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

In a linguistic manuscript dating from the 1930s, appears the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) gloss *lopo* ("rabbit") from the primitive *lopō*<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> and connected to the root *LOP*. In earlier [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), it was *lapatte*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
