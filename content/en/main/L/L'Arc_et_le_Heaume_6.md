<b><i>L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</i> n°6</b> (*Ainulindalë et Valaquenta*) is the sixth issue of the fanzine <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkiendil.com/asso/mag" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</a></i> by the [Tolkiendil](</wiki/Tolkiendil_(Tolkien_society)> "Tolkiendil (Tolkien society)") French association. It was published in September [2019](/wiki/2019) and it is dedicated to the chapters *[Ainulindalë](/wiki/Ainulindal%C3%AB)* and *[Valaquenta](/wiki/Valaquenta)* and their relatives, such as the powers in Arda ([Eru](/wiki/Eru), [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar)).
