[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to an unknown Dutchman**, postmarked [3 April](/wiki/3_April) [1958](/wiki/1958).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** A letter of reply to a Dutchman who had invited Tolkien for a cup of tea.
- **Description:** Autograph letter, 1 page. Signed as [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) above a [tengwa](/wiki/Tengwar "Tengwar") *[ungwë](/wiki/Ungw%C3%AB "Ungwë")*. At the bottom Tolkien wrote "2 [Astron](/wiki/Astron)" (see [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar)).

- **Publication:** Not published.

## Transcription {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Keep the kettle handy! Look out for me especially at unlikely times. But I have unfortunately had to return over the Water on business.<br/><br/>Gandalf<br/><br/>2 <a href="/wiki/Astron" title="Astron">Astron</a>
</p>
</blockquote>
