**Lalia Took, née Clayhanger**, was the wife of [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Fortinbras Took II](/wiki/Fortinbras_Took_II).

## History {#History}

### Life {#Life}

Lalia was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1283](/wiki/Third_Age_2883 "Third Age 2883"), and married Fortinbras Took in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1314](/wiki/Third_Age_2914 "Third Age 2914"). They had one son, [Ferumbras](/wiki/Ferumbras_Took_III "Ferumbras Took III"), who was born in [1316](/wiki/Third_Age_2916 "Third Age 2916") before Fortinbras became [Thain](/wiki/Thain) in [1339](/wiki/Third_Age_2939 "Third Age 2939"). In accordance with [Took](/wiki/Took_Family "Took Family") family tradition, Lalia became the head of the family once her husband Fortinbras died in [1380](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980"). Ferumbras was unable to find a wife and it was alleged that this was because he could not find someone willing to share a house with Lalia, and Ferumbras was only accorded a small bachelor pad in Great Smials until after Lalia's death.

Lalia long outlived her husband and ruled the [Tooks](/wiki/Took_Family "Took Family") of [Great Smials](/wiki/Great_Smials) for a further twenty-two years, becoming the great and memorable matriarch of the family known as *Lalia the Great* (or least courteously by those with less love for her, *Lalia the Fat*). She was famous for her tremendous girth, which was rumoured to have prevented her attendance of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' famous [Birthday Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") in [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001").<sup id="cite_ref-L214_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L214-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Death {#Death}

In her later - and fattest - years it was her immobility rather than age which was the biggest problem for her so she went around in a wheelchair. In particular, she liked to be wheeled to the Great Door in the mornings so she could experience the fresh air.

She died in [1402](/wiki/Third_Age_3002 "Third Age 3002") at the grand old age of 119, after her clumsy attendant let Lalia's wheelchair run over the threshold which tipped Lalia down a flight of garden steps. It is rumoured that this attendant was [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took")'s sister, [Pearl Took](/wiki/Pearl_Took), as she was excluded from the celebrations of Ferumbras's accession to Thain, but the family tried to keep the details to themselves. However, shortly after Lalia's demise, she was seen with a priceless pearl necklace, believed to be a Took heirloom, implying she may have been rewarded for the "accident".<sup id="cite_ref-L214_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L214-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Lalia" is the latinized form of the classical Greek word <i><a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%CE%BB%CE%B1%CE%BB%CE%B9%CE%AC#Ancient_Greek" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">λαλιά</a></i> meaning "speech".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
