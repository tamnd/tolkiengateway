**Lalia Took, née Clayhanger**, was the wife of [Thain](../T/Thain.md) [Fortinbras Took II](../F/Fortinbras_Took_II.md).

## History {#History}

### Life {#Life}

Lalia was born in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1283](../T/Third_Age_2883.md), and married Fortinbras Took in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1314](../T/Third_Age_2914.md). They had one son, [Ferumbras](../F/Ferumbras_Took_III.md), who was born in [1316](../T/Third_Age_2916.md) before Fortinbras became [Thain](../T/Thain.md) in [1339](../T/Third_Age_2939.md). In accordance with [Took](../T/Took_Family.md) family tradition, Lalia became the head of the family once her husband Fortinbras died in [1380](../T/Third_Age_2980.md). Ferumbras was unable to find a wife and it was alleged that this was because he could not find someone willing to share a house with Lalia, and Ferumbras was only accorded a small bachelor pad in Great Smials until after Lalia's death.

Lalia long outlived her husband and ruled the [Tooks](../T/Took_Family.md) of [Great Smials](../G/Great_Smials.md) for a further twenty-two years, becoming the great and memorable matriarch of the family known as *Lalia the Great* (or least courteously by those with less love for her, *Lalia the Fat*). She was famous for her tremendous girth, which was rumoured to have prevented her attendance of [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)' famous [Birthday Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md) in [1401](../T/Third_Age_3001.md).<sup id="cite_ref-L214_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L214-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Death {#Death}

In her later - and fattest - years it was her immobility rather than age which was the biggest problem for her so she went around in a wheelchair. In particular, she liked to be wheeled to the Great Door in the mornings so she could experience the fresh air.

She died in [1402](../T/Third_Age_3002.md) at the grand old age of 119, after her clumsy attendant let Lalia's wheelchair run over the threshold which tipped Lalia down a flight of garden steps. It is rumoured that this attendant was [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md)'s sister, [Pearl Took](../P/Pearl_Took.md), as she was excluded from the celebrations of Ferumbras's accession to Thain, but the family tried to keep the details to themselves. However, shortly after Lalia's demise, she was seen with a priceless pearl necklace, believed to be a Took heirloom, implying she may have been rewarded for the "accident".<sup id="cite_ref-L214_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L214-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Lalia" is the latinized form of the classical Greek word <i><a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%CE%BB%CE%B1%CE%BB%CE%B9%CE%AC#Ancient_Greek" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">λαλιά</a></i> meaning "speech".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
