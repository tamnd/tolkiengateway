On [2 December](../_/2_December.md) [1968](../_/1968.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md)**.

- **Contents:** Tolkien responds to Unwin regarding negotiations of film rights with [United Artists](../U/United_Artists.md). He also mentions his unpublished story *[The End of Bovadium](../T/The_End_of_Bovadium.md)*, which he has "no intention of publishing now (if ever)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Publication:** Short quotes and a summary were published by [Wayne Hammond](../W/Wayne_Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) in the *Chronology* volume of [The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md).
