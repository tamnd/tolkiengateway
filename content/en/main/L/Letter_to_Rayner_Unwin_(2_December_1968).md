On [2 December](/wiki/2_December) [1968](/wiki/1968), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin)**.

- **Contents:** Tolkien responds to Unwin regarding negotiations of film rights with [United Artists](/wiki/United_Artists). He also mentions his unpublished story *[The End of Bovadium](/wiki/The_End_of_Bovadium)*, which he has "no intention of publishing now (if ever)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Publication:** Short quotes and a summary were published by [Wayne Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) in the *Chronology* volume of [The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide).
