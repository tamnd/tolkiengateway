On [13 December](/wiki/13_December) [1954](/wiki/1954), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote <b><a href="/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien" title="Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien">a letter</a> to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayner_Heppenstall" title="wikipedia:Rayner Heppenstall">Rayner Heppenstall</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Critical points about the [radio play *The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth*](</wiki/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth,_Beorhthelm%27s_Son_(radio_play)> "The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth, Beorhthelm's Son (radio play)") (broadcast on [3 December](/wiki/3_December)). While the letter has not been kept in the [BBC](/wiki/BBC) Written Archives Centre, it can be inferred from Heppenstall's reply on the same day that Tolkien appears to have "included comments that the readers ignored the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliterative_verse" title="wikipedia:Alliterative verse">alliterative metre</a> of the work, and delivered the verse as if it were <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iambic_pentameter" title="wikipedia:Iambic pentameter">iambic pentameter</a>".<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
