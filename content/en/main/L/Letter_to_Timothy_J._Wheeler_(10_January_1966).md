On [10 January](/wiki/10_January) [1966](/wiki/1966), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to [Timothy J. Wheeler](/wiki/Timothy_J._Wheeler)**.

- **Publication:** None. A photo and transcription of the letter was posted on the web site *[Tolkien Library](/wiki/Tolkien_Library)*, maintained by [Pieter Collier](/wiki/Pieter_Collier).

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Dear Mr. Wheeler,<br/><br/>Thank you for your letter. I hope you will not think that I am ungrateful for your attention and good wishes if I say that I do not feel able to write anything such as you wish. I do wish you well, but I do not think that it would really do you much good to have special good wishes from me.<br/><br/>Yours sincerely,<br/><br/>JRR Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
