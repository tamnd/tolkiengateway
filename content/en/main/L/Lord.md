**Lord** is a title used by most societies to denote a ruler. The [Elves](../E/Elves.md) used the words **[Heru](../H/Heru.md)** ([Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md)) and **[Hîr](../H/Hîr.md)** ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)), and the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) **[Uzbad](../U/Uzbad.md)**.
