**Lord** is a title used by most societies to denote a ruler. The [Elves](/wiki/Elves) used the words **[Heru](/wiki/Heru)** ([Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)) and **[Hîr](/wiki/H%C3%AEr)** ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)), and the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) **[Uzbad](/wiki/Uzbad)**.
