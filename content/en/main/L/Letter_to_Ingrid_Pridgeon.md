In August [1968](/wiki/1968), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Ingrid Pridgeon**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Description:** Autograph letter, 2pp. &amp; envelope, 8vo, Oxford address crossed out and typed c/o Messrs Geo. Allen and Unwin, postmarked Poole, August 1968.<sup id="cite_ref-Bloom_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bloom-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Excerpts, description, and photograph of the letter was included in *[Bloomsbury Auctions: Modern First Editions, English &amp; Continental Literature &amp; History, Emblem Books, Economics &amp; Law 13 March 2008](/wiki/Bloomsbury_Modern_First_Editions,_English_and_Continental_Literature_and_History,_Emblem_Books,_Economics_and_Law_13_March_2008 "Bloomsbury Modern First Editions, English and Continental Literature and History, Emblem Books, Economics and Law 13 March 2008")*.

## Contents {#Contents}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ingrid_Pridgeon_August_1968.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/7b/Ingrid_Pridgeon_August_1968.jpg/300px-Ingrid_Pridgeon_August_1968.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="395" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/7/7b/Ingrid_Pridgeon_August_1968.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="400" data-file-height="526"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

\[Tolkien apologises\] for not having replied more fully, "I am having rather a bad time. I have left Oxford, and in the middle of the move I fell downstairs and damaged my right leg so badly that it is in plaster from toe to hip, and I am on crutches" and \[gives\] a reference to *The Lord of the Rings*, "I notice Spire Hollin: very Shire-like. Hollin is northern form of holly: and in Book I of The L.R: I lived for a while in Leeds in a Hollin Lane".<sup id="cite_ref-Bloom_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bloom-2">[2]</a></sup>
