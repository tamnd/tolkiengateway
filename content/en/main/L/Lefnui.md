The **Lefnui** was the westernmost river of southern [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Course {#Course}

The *Lefnui* flowed from its source in the far west of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) southwest in a partially erratic course through the lands west of the hills of [Pinnath Gelin](../P/Pinnath_Gelin.md), until it emptied into the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md) through a long narrow firth.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> It was the longest and broadest of the five rivers [Erui](../E/Erui.md), [Sirith](../S/Sirith.md), [Serni](../S/Serni.md), [Morthond](../M/Morthond.md) and *Lefnui*. The *Lefnui* formed the western border of Gondor, because the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) had never established permanent settlements in the promontory of [Andrast](../A/Andrast.md) to its west where they only kept a coast-guard force and beacons at the end of the cape of Andrast.<sup id="cite_ref-Rivers_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivers-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Lefnui played little part in history, though it was remembered by the people of Gondor and the [Men](../M/Men.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). When the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md) invaded Rohan<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup> in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2758](../T/Third_Age_2758.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> during the rule of King [Helm](../H/Helm.md) of Rohan and Steward [Beren](<../B/Beren_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) of Gondor,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> they were aided by Gondor's enemies from the south, the [Corsairs of Umbar](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup>. These sea-going marauders landed at Lefnui's mouth on their mission to overrun and destroy the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md), though they would eventually be defeated.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lefnui* (also spelt *Levnui*) is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name, which is said to mean "fifth". It received its name, because it was the fifth river south of the White Mountains in Gondor counting the rivers westwards from the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) after the rivers *Erui*, *Sirith*, *Serni* and *Morthond*.<sup id="cite_ref-Rivers_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivers-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the "working map" that [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) used while writing *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* this river had the name *Lhefneg*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> <span></span>
