The **Lefnui** was the westernmost river of southern [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Course {#Course}

The *Lefnui* flowed from its source in the far west of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) southwest in a partially erratic course through the lands west of the hills of [Pinnath Gelin](/wiki/Pinnath_Gelin), until it emptied into the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas) through a long narrow firth.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> It was the longest and broadest of the five rivers [Erui](/wiki/Erui), [Sirith](/wiki/Sirith), [Serni](/wiki/Serni), [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond) and *Lefnui*. The *Lefnui* formed the western border of Gondor, because the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) had never established permanent settlements in the promontory of [Andrast](/wiki/Andrast) to its west where they only kept a coast-guard force and beacons at the end of the cape of Andrast.<sup id="cite_ref-Rivers_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivers-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Lefnui played little part in history, though it was remembered by the people of Gondor and the [Men](/wiki/Men) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). When the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) invaded Rohan<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup> in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> during the rule of King [Helm](/wiki/Helm) of Rohan and Steward [Beren](</wiki/Beren_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Beren (Steward of Gondor)") of Gondor,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> they were aided by Gondor's enemies from the south, the [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar)<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup>. These sea-going marauders landed at Lefnui's mouth on their mission to overrun and destroy the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), though they would eventually be defeated.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lefnui* (also spelt *Levnui*) is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which is said to mean "fifth". It received its name, because it was the fifth river south of the White Mountains in Gondor counting the rivers westwards from the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) after the rivers *Erui*, *Sirith*, *Serni* and *Morthond*.<sup id="cite_ref-Rivers_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivers-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the "working map" that [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) used while writing *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* this river had the name *Lhefneg*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> <span></span>
