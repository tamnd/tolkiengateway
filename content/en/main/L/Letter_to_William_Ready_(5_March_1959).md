On [5 March](/wiki/5_March) [1959](/wiki/1959), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to [William Ready](/wiki/William_Ready)**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Gratitude for Ready's arrangement of the purchase by the [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University) of Tolkien's manuscripts for *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of and quotations from the letter appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*.
