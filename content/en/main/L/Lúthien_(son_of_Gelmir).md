**Lúthien** son of [Gelmir](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB#Other_version_of_the_legendarium "Finwë") was a [Gnome](/wiki/Gnome), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in a prose fragment following *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Prose_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prose-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/8/87/Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg/300px-Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="146" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/8/87/Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg/450px-Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/8/87/Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg/600px-Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1533" data-file-height="744"/></a><figcaption><i>Gnome Banners</i> by <a href="/wiki/User:Quentandil" title="User:Quentandil">Quentandil</a></figcaption></figure>

After the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) returned to the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands) to wage war with [Melko](/wiki/Melko), Lúthien led one of the three great armies of his father Gelmir. His standard (banner) was that of a "golden swallow that winged through an azure field as it were the sky set with silver stars", and his army preferred bows over other weapons.<sup id="cite_ref-Prose_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prose-1">[1]</a></sup>

Beyond this point in the story, nothing else is known about him.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Lúthien* seems to be in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), but its meaning is uncertain.
