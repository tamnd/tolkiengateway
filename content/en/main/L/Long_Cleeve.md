**Long Cleeve** was a town in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Long Cleeve was the home of the [North-tooks](/wiki/North-took_Family "North-took Family"), a distant branch of the [Took Family](/wiki/Took_Family) who were descended from [Bandobras](/wiki/Bandobras_Took "Bandobras Took") the [Bullroarer](/wiki/Bandobras_Took "Bandobras Took"). It was the home of [Diamond](/wiki/Diamond_of_Long_Cleeve "Diamond of Long Cleeve"), who married [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) eight years after his return from the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-Took_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name "Cleeve" comes from the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) word *clif*, which means "cliff" or "hill".<sup id="cite_ref-rc_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. lvii</sup>

This, and the name "North-Took" implies that it lay in [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> much of which was rocky in nature (though none of this is stated specifically by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") himself).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Long Cleeve appears in the [A Part of the Shire](/wiki/A_Part_of_the_Shire) map drawn by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) in [1943](/wiki/1943), along with many other names, that didn't make it to the published version of the map.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. lvi</sup>

<dl><dd>Long Cleeve is one of the four settlements of &#34;<a href="/wiki/The_Yondershire" title="The Yondershire">the Yondershire</a>&#34;, in the Shire&#39;s wilder north-western reaches. It was founded by <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Blanco" title="Blanco">Blanco</a> and served as the seat of <a href="/wiki/Bandobras_Took" title="Bandobras Took">Bandobras Took</a> and the <a href="/wiki/North-took_Family" title="North-took Family">North-took Family</a>. Located near the <a href="/wiki/North_Moors" title="North Moors">North Moors</a> and the southern <a href="/wiki/Hills_of_Evendim" title="Hills of Evendim">Hills of Evendim</a>, it is the seat of a local leader known as the &#34;Reeve of the Yondershire&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>
