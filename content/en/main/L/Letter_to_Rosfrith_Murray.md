On [14 December](/wiki/14_December) [1967](/wiki/1967), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to [Rosfrith Murray](/wiki/Rosfrith_Murray)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Christmas greetings. Tolkien encloses a signed copy of *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*, first American edition.
- **Description:** 2 pages, 16mo, signed.<sup id="cite_ref-TL_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Excerpts {#Excerpts}

"*This is the American edition (better than ours, though I do not think much of the photograph\[)\]*". Before signing himself "*Yrs. affectionately Ronald*", the author states "*...I remember you ... in prayers.*"<sup id="cite_ref-TL_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL-2">[2]</a></sup>
