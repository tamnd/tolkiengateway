On [14 December](../_/14_December.md) [1967](../_/1967.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_%2522The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%2522.md) to [Rosfrith Murray](../R/Rosfrith_Murray.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Christmas greetings. Tolkien encloses a signed copy of *[Smith of Wootton Major](../S/Smith_of_Wootton_Major.md)*, first American edition.
- **Description:** 2 pages, 16mo, signed.<sup id="cite_ref-TL_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Excerpts {#Excerpts}

"*This is the American edition (better than ours, though I do not think much of the photograph\[)\]*". Before signing himself "*Yrs. affectionately Ronald*", the author states "*...I remember you ... in prayers.*"<sup id="cite_ref-TL_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL-2">[2]</a></sup>
