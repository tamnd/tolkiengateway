On [15 December](../_/15_December.md) [1937](../_/1937.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote <b><a href="Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien">a letter</a> to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Ransome" title="wikipedia:Arthur Ransome">Arthur Ransome</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** A reply to Ransome's letter of [13 December](../_/13_December.md). Tolkien acknowledges Ransome's suggestion about changing some words in *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*. Ransome had suggested that it might be inappropriate to use the word *men* when referring to [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) and [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md): Tolkien justifies the character [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md)'s use of *man* by claiming it was "*the language in those days*". Tolkien promises to send him a revised copy of the book "*if there is a reprint*", adding that "*sales are not very great*". He also writes: "*To be fancied by you, that is more than any hobbit could have expected*", and that his children have kept Ransome's books from when they were young.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Leeds_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Leeds-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Handwritten letter.<sup id="cite_ref-Leeds_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Leeds-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Archive:** The letter is kept at the [University of Leeds](../U/University_of_Leeds.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Leeds_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Leeds-2">[2]</a></sup>
