On [18 January](/wiki/18_January) [1948](/wiki/1948), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to Rosemary**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject**: Tolkien replies to a letter of appreciation of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* from Rosemary.
- **Publication:** Mentioned in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide): Chronology* (p. 328) and a facsimile was published in [Mallorn 36](/wiki/Mallorn_36)
- **Description:** Two pages, handwritten, signed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
