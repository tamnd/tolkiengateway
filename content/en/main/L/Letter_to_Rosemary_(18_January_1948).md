On [18 January](../_/18_January.md) [1948](../_/1948.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_%2522The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%2522.md) to Rosemary**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject**: Tolkien replies to a letter of appreciation of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* from Rosemary.
- **Publication:** Mentioned in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md): Chronology* (p. 328) and a facsimile was published in [Mallorn 36](../M/Mallorn_36.md)
- **Description:** Two pages, handwritten, signed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
