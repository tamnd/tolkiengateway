On [7 September](../_/7_September.md) [1937](../_/1937.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [George Allen &amp; Unwin](../G/George_Allen_and_Unwin.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents**: Tolkien writes to his publishers regarding the promotion of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*. Notable is Tolkien's description of his friend [Wilfred Rowland Childe](../W/Wilfred_Rowland_Childe.md).
- **Archive**: Tolkien-George Allen &amp; Unwin archive, HarperCollins.

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

*"\[Wilfred Rowland Childe is\] specially interested in elves and related creatures. He was (once at any rate) a fairly well-known poet, and is still a good one; and is in close touch with various literary and journalistic sets &amp; folk, in the North specially. He would probably do something, if he got a copy. He saw the \[manuscript\] and is well disposed"* <span></span>
