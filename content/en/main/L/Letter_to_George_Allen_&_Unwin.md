On [7 September](/wiki/7_September) [1937](/wiki/1937), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [George Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/George_Allen_and_Unwin "George Allen and Unwin")**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents**: Tolkien writes to his publishers regarding the promotion of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. Notable is Tolkien's description of his friend [Wilfred Rowland Childe](/wiki/Wilfred_Rowland_Childe).
- **Archive**: Tolkien-George Allen &amp; Unwin archive, HarperCollins.

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

*"\[Wilfred Rowland Childe is\] specially interested in elves and related creatures. He was (once at any rate) a fairly well-known poet, and is still a good one; and is in close touch with various literary and journalistic sets &amp; folk, in the North specially. He would probably do something, if he got a copy. He saw the \[manuscript\] and is well disposed"* <span></span>
