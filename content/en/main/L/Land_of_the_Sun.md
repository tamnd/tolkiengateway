The **Land of the Sun**, also known as the **Burnt Land of the Sun**, **Easterness**, **Eastland**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and **Eastern Land**,<sup id="cite_ref-5b_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5b-2">[2]</a></sup> was a continent in the far east of [Arda](../A/Arda.md) that corresponded symmetrically to [Aman](../A/Aman.md) in the far west. The land was separated from [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) by the waters of the [East Sea](../E/East_Sea.md). In the Land of the Sun was a great, curve-shaped mountain range called the [Walls of the Sun](../W/Walls_of_the_Sun.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5b_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5b-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Land of the Sun likely comprised all or part of the [Empty Lands](../E/Empty_Lands.md) that [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md) "cast back" during the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One.md)*, the easternmost land of Arda was called <i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="../O/Oronto.md" title="Oronto">Oronto</a></b></i> (*Oronto*, a [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) word for "East", was a name for the Uttermost East).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> This region was connected to the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md), laying directly east of [Palisor](../P/Palisor.md), with no separating water. The huge peak [Kalormë](../K/Kalormë.md) stood in Oronto.<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:212</sup>

[Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) described the East to the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), saying that "*there is a silent beach and dark empty seas*".<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:214</sup> It is also told that [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) and [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) "*builded great havens* \[of the Sun and Moon\] *beside the soundless sea*".<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:215</sup>
