The **Land of the Sun**, also known as the **Burnt Land of the Sun**, **Easterness**, **Eastland**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and **Eastern Land**,<sup id="cite_ref-5b_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5b-2">[2]</a></sup> was a continent in the far east of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) that corresponded symmetrically to [Aman](/wiki/Aman) in the far west. The land was separated from [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) by the waters of the [East Sea](/wiki/East_Sea). In the Land of the Sun was a great, curve-shaped mountain range called the [Walls of the Sun](/wiki/Walls_of_the_Sun).<sup id="cite_ref-5b_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5b-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Land of the Sun likely comprised all or part of the [Empty Lands](/wiki/Empty_Lands) that [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) "cast back" during the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One)*, the easternmost land of Arda was called <i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Oronto" title="Oronto">Oronto</a></b></i> (*Oronto*, a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) word for "East", was a name for the Uttermost East).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> This region was connected to the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands), laying directly east of [Palisor](/wiki/Palisor), with no separating water. The huge peak [Kalormë](/wiki/Kalorm%C3%AB) stood in Oronto.<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:212</sup>

[Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) described the East to the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), saying that "*there is a silent beach and dark empty seas*".<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:214</sup> It is also told that [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) and [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) "*builded great havens* \[of the Sun and Moon\] *beside the soundless sea*".<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:215</sup>
