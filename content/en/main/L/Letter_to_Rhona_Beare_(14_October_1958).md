On [14 October](../_/14_October.md) [1958](../_/1958.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Rhona Beare](../R/Rhona_Beare.md)**.

This is one of the two letters that were printed in the *[J.R.R. Tolkien's Letters to Rhona Beare](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_Letters_to_Rhona_Beare.md)*, being the original of [Letter 211](Letter_211.md).

- **Difference from Letter 211**: Compared to this, Letter 211 omitted some marginal comments, replaced a paragraph with one from a draft (on the same point), and provided a prefatory note summarizing Dr. Beare's questions to which the letter is a response.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
