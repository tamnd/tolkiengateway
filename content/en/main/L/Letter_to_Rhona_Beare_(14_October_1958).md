On [14 October](/wiki/14_October) [1958](/wiki/1958), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Rhona Beare](/wiki/Rhona_Beare)**.

This is one of the two letters that were printed in the *[J.R.R. Tolkien's Letters to Rhona Beare](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_Letters_to_Rhona_Beare)*, being the original of [Letter 211](/wiki/Letter_211).

- **Difference from Letter 211**: Compared to this, Letter 211 omitted some marginal comments, replaced a paragraph with one from a draft (on the same point), and provided a prefatory note summarizing Dr. Beare's questions to which the letter is a response.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
