<b><i>L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</i> n°7</b> (*Tolkien au féminin*) is the seventh issue of the fanzine <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkiendil.com/asso/mag" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</a></i> by the [Tolkiendil](</wiki/Tolkiendil_(Tolkien_society)> "Tolkiendil (Tolkien society)") french association. It was published in august [2021](/wiki/2021) and it is dedicated to the feminine characters and point of views in the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
