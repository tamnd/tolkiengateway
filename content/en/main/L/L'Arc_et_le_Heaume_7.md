<b><i>L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</i> n°7</b> (*Tolkien au féminin*) is the seventh issue of the fanzine <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkiendil.com/asso/mag" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</a></i> by the [Tolkiendil](<../T/Tolkiendil_(Tolkien_society).md>) french association. It was published in august [2021](../_/2021.md) and it is dedicated to the feminine characters and point of views in the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
