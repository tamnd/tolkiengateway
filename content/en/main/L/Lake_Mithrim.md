**Lake Mithrim** was a body of water in the land of [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim) with its long axis stretching from east to west.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) first returned to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) encamped upon the northern shore until [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin)'s host arrived; then the Sons of Fëanor removed to the southern shore to avoid conflict.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, this body of water is known as **Lake Asgon**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
