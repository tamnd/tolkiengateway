In [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md), **Lóchas Drûs** ("Dominion of the Lochaw") was a region in the south of the farthest East. It encompassed all areas once ruled by the Lochaw empire, at the southeast of the Mountains of the Wind.<sup id="cite_ref-ldrus_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ldrus-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

### Brôd {#Brôd}

**Brôd** was a realm situated between the rivers Kirmóas (north), Runk (south), and Breacna (east). The Mountains of the Wind served as its western boundary. The Brôdim culture was a mix of local Braric traditions with Lochan and Wôlim influences. The Brôdim spoke the same language as the Wôlim, sharing a common social organization. Their political organization resembled that of the Lochaw.

Its major urban centers were Sôgôl, Runk, and Brôd. Sôgôl, located on the south bank of the Kirmóas river, was a wealthy town that guarded the Wind Gates which was a pass through the mountains. Brôd was located more centrally, and while the least populated, it still served as the realm's political and religious center. Runk was named after the river and was situated near a waterfall. It guarded the sole bridge crossing the river.

The Braric symbol, the plain white orb, was displayed on Brôd's Lochan-inspired flag. It was set on a field diagonally divided into four. Each division represented a cardinal direction: the east was red, the south was green, the west was purple, and the north was blue.<sup id="cite_ref-brod_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-brod-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Locha {#Locha}

**Locha** was the core of the Lochaw empire, located east of the river Breacna. Before being settled, it was inhabited by *Lakhab* (“Coast-folk”) and likely known as Cleeac.<sup id="cite_ref-locha_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-locha-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Wôl {#Wôl}

Wôl was the most southern part of Lóchas Drûs. It was originally inhabited by the Wôlim, who spoke a tongue related to [Pûkael](../D/Drúadan_language.md).<sup id="cite_ref-wol_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wol-4">[4]</a></sup> The Wôlim were described as war-like, often fighting against the Brôdan and neighbouring Ts'dan.<sup id="cite_ref-dwar_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwar-5">[5]</a></sup> They also settled [Waw](../W/Waw.md) in the second and third centuries of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-waw_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-waw-6">[6]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Chief Woma Dral of the Womaw tribe Surk sailed to Cleeac in South Eastern Middle-earth in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_347&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 347 (page does not exist)">347</a>. His Vuulmaw warriors immediately came into conflict with the local Lakhab, culminating in the *Cunaaci-aac-Aalkbroda* ("Battle of Hillholds"). This resulted in him being proclaimed the High Chief in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_348&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 348 (page does not exist)">348</a>, leading the first push towards centralization in the region, settling his Vuulmaw throughout the various parts of his new Lochas (“Never-ending Glen”).<sup id="cite_ref-locha_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-locha-3">[3]</a></sup>

At some point before [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1051&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1051 (page does not exist)">1051</a>, Waw had been conquered by the Lochaw, but it declared itself independent again in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1617&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1617 (page does not exist)">1617</a> after centuries of rebellion fomented by the [Ringwraith](../R/Ringwraith.md) [Dwar of Waw](../T/Third_of_the_Nazgûl.md).<sup id="cite_ref-dwar_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwar-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Lochaw empire was divided in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1794&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1794 (page does not exist)">1794</a>, leading to the rise of successor states that often followed previous cultural and political lines.<sup id="cite_ref-ldrus_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ldrus-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
