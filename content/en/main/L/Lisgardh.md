**Lisgardh** was a fenland region at the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion) consisting of large areas where [reeds](/wiki/Reeds "Reeds") and rushes grew Man-high.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lisgardh* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), meaning "Land of Reeds",<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup> from *lisg* ("reed") + *gardh* ("region").

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")*, *Lisgardh* was called <i><b>Arlisgion</b></i> in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), also meaning "the place of reeds".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is described as being located to the south of [Dor Lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min "Dor-lómin") but separated from it by the [Mountains of Shadow](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin "Ered Wethrin"). [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) stayed there for a while before departing for the "yet more lovely" [Land of Willows](/wiki/Nan-tathren "Nan-tathren").

Other variant of the Gnomish name was <i><b>Garlisgion</b></i>, and its [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognate was <i><b>Ardaliskea</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
