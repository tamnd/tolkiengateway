New Zealand actor **Lawrence Makoare** (born 1968) played [Lurtz](/wiki/Lurtz) and [Gothmog](/wiki/Gothmog,_Lieutenant_of_Morgul "Gothmog, Lieutenant of Morgul") in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), and [Bolg](/wiki/Bolg) via performance-capture in *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)*. He also played the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) during the battle scene with [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) in *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King")*.
