On [28 April](/wiki/28_April) [1967](/wiki/1967), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to John Leyerle**.<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Reply to Leyerle's letter, delivered to Tolkien by hand from [William Ready](/wiki/William_Ready) sometime between [21](/wiki/21_April "21 April") and [28 April](/wiki/28_April): at present, Tolkien has given up translating *[The Owl and the Nightingale](/wiki/The_Owl_and_the_Nightingale)*, and his translations of <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight" title="Sir Gawain and the Green Knight">Sir Gawain and the Green Knight</a></i> and <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Pearl" title="Pearl">Pearl</a></i> will soon be published by [Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin "Allen and Unwin"). However, he may be able to submit a "reasonably successful translation of <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Sir_Orfeo" title="Sir Orfeo">Sir Orfeo</a></i>", made some years ago, to Leyerle's anthology.
- **Publication:** A description of the letter was published in [*The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology*](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide") (p. 697).
- **Archive:** Tolkien Papers, [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library), Oxford.<sup id="cite_ref-RG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RG-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

"I have at present given up the task \[of translating *The Owl and the Nightingale*\] It comes off well enough in certain passages, but in general octo-syllabic couplets are defeating for a translator; there is no room to move".<sup id="cite_ref-RG_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RG-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
