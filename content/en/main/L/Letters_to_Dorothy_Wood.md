Between [1960](/wiki/1960) and [1973](/wiki/1973), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[12 letters](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to [Dorothy Wood](/wiki/Dorothy_Wood)**.

- **Subject(s):** Among the subjects discussed are <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_II" title="wikipedia:Elizabeth II">the Queen</a> ("I am one of her devoted subjects and greatly admire her, though her voice is not one of her graces"), wealth, the consequences of fame, handwriting, *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, the Tolkien name and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antony_Armstrong-Jones,_1st_Earl_of_Snowdon" title="wikipedia:Antony Armstrong-Jones, 1st Earl of Snowdon">Lord Snowdon</a> ("...a very thin almost '[elvish](/wiki/Elves "Elves")' figure... we had much in common - especially a devotion to [trees](/wiki/Trees "Trees")...").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of and quotes from the letters appeared in *[Sotheby's English Literature, History, Children's Books, Illustrations and Photographs 8 July 2004](/wiki/Sotheby%27s_English_Literature,_History,_Children%27s_Books,_Illustrations_and_Photographs_8_July_2004)*.
