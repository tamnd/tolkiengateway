Between [1960](../_/1960.md) and [1973](../_/1973.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[12 letters](Letters_not_published_in_%2522The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%2522.md) to [Dorothy Wood](../D/Dorothy_Wood.md)**.

- **Subject(s):** Among the subjects discussed are <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_II" title="wikipedia:Elizabeth II">the Queen</a> ("I am one of her devoted subjects and greatly admire her, though her voice is not one of her graces"), wealth, the consequences of fame, handwriting, *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, the Tolkien name and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antony_Armstrong-Jones,_1st_Earl_of_Snowdon" title="wikipedia:Antony Armstrong-Jones, 1st Earl of Snowdon">Lord Snowdon</a> ("...a very thin almost '[elvish](../E/Elves.md)' figure... we had much in common - especially a devotion to [trees](../T/Trees.md)...").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of and quotes from the letters appeared in *[Sotheby's English Literature, History, Children's Books, Illustrations and Photographs 8 July 2004](../S/Sotheby's_English_Literature,_History,_Children's_Books,_Illustrations_and_Photographs_8_July_2004.md)*.
