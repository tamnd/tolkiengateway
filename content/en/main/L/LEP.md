**LEP** is a [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "finger",<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup> or "pick (up, out) with the fingers".<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin): *leper-*<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *lepta-* ("finger, feel with fingertips"); *leper*<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin): *lepta-* ("finger, feel with fingertips"); *leper*<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *leber*<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-2">[2]</a></sup>

- Quenya: *[lempe](/wiki/Lempe "Lempe")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT47_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Telerin *[lepen](/wiki/Lepen "Lepen")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT47_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Sindarin *[leben](/wiki/Leben "Leben")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT47_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the manuscript "Words, Phrases and Passages in *The Lord of the Rings*" (late 1950, early 1960s) appears the root LEPEN (or LENEP), yielding the derivatives Quenya *[lempe](/wiki/Lempe "Lempe")* and Sindarin *[leben](/wiki/Leben "Leben")*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* (ca. [1937](/wiki/1937)) appears the root LEP-, LEPET signifying "finger", yielding the derivatives Quenya *lepse*, [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *lhebed*. The extended forms LEPEN, LEPEK, signifying "five", yield Quenya *[lempe](/wiki/Lempe "Lempe")*, Quenya *lemnar* ("week"), and Noldorin *[lheben](/wiki/Lheben "Lheben")*, Noldorin *lhevnar* ("week").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT48_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT48-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the *Gnomish Lexicon* (ca. [1917](/wiki/1917)) appears the root *lef-*, yielding the derivatives [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) *[lempe](/wiki/Lemin "Lemin")* and [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) *[elf](</wiki/Elf_(Gnomish)> "Elf (Gnomish)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-VT48_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT48-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE11_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE11-7">[7]</a></sup>

In the *Qenya Lexicon* (ca. [1915](/wiki/1915)) appears the root LEPE, with the derivative [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) *let* ("finger").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
