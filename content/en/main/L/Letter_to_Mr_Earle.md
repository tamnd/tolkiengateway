On [12 April](/wiki/12_April) [1956](/wiki/1956), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Mr Earle**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Discussion of the sources of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, and the origin and pronunciation of the surname "Tolkien".<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Description:** According to the records of the Marquette Unviersity, the letter was "offered for sale by DMK Books with *The Lord of the Rings* in 3 volumes, First Editions in dust jackets, George Allwen &amp; Unwin, London 1954-1955".<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of the letter appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*.
