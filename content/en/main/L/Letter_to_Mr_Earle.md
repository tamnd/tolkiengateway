On [12 April](../_/12_April.md) [1956](../_/1956.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Mr Earle**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Discussion of the sources of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, and the origin and pronunciation of the surname "Tolkien".<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Description:** According to the records of the Marquette Unviersity, the letter was "offered for sale by DMK Books with *The Lord of the Rings* in 3 volumes, First Editions in dust jackets, George Allwen &amp; Unwin, London 1954-1955".<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of the letter appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md)*.
