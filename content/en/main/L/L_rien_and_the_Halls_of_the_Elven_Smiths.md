***Lórien and the Halls of the Elven Smiths*** is a module (in the *Campaign*-series) for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*, 1st Edition.

For use with this supplement, [Mithril Miniatures](/wiki/Mithril_Miniatures) released a set of metal miniatures (M205 - M214 *Lórien Elves*).
