The **Last Riding of the Keepers of the Rings** was the procession of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), and other [High Elves](/wiki/Calaquendi "Calaquendi") that rode westward to the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens)<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-1">[1]</a></sup> in [September](/wiki/September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021"). On [22 September](/wiki/22_September) [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) joined them in the [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and on [29 September](/wiki/29_September) the Riding came to the Grey Havens<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> where they found [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf). All of the riders save Sam then boarded a [White Ship](/wiki/White_Ship). At that time [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) and [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) arrived and after the ship had sailed the three friends returned to the Shire. Those aboard the ship sailed to [Aman](/wiki/Aman) and never returned to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-1">[1]</a></sup>
