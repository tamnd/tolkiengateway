On [11 November](../_/11_November.md) [1964](../_/1964.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Clyde S. Kilby](../C/Clyde_S._Kilby.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subjects:** Reply to question on acquiring manuscripts to the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md). The sales of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* in the USA.
- **Publication:** Excerpts from the letter have been published in [*SEVEN*, Volume 27](../S/SEVEN_27.md) (pp. 48–9).
