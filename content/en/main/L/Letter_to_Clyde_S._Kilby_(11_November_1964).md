On [11 November](/wiki/11_November) [1964](/wiki/1964), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Clyde S. Kilby](/wiki/Clyde_S._Kilby)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subjects:** Reply to question on acquiring manuscripts to the [Marion E. Wade Center](/wiki/Marion_E._Wade_Center). The sales of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* in the USA.
- **Publication:** Excerpts from the letter have been published in [*SEVEN*, Volume 27](/wiki/SEVEN_27 "SEVEN 27") (pp. 48–9).
