**Lissuin** was a fragrant flower of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), and was said to bring the heart ease. It was one of the types of flowers brought by a ship of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) to grace the wedding of [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") and [Erendis](/wiki/Erendis).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is related to the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word *[lissë](/wiki/Liss%C3%AB "Lissë")* meaning "sweet".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
