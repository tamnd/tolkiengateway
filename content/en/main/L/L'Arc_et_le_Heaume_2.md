<b><i>L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</i> n°2</b> (*Les Animaux de Tolkien*) is the second issue of the fanzine <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkiendil.com/asso/mag" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</a></i> by the [Tolkiendil](<../T/Tolkiendil_(Tolkien_society).md>) French association. It was published in [2010](../_/2010.md) and it is dedicated to the animals.
