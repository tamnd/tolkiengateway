On [4 October](/wiki/4_October) [1965](/wiki/1965), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) sent **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Agatha Carew Hunt**.<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** A stock reply (written by Tolkien's secretary on 11 May) to a letter of appreciation. However, Tolkien discovers the letter on 4 October, and adds a note of apology.
- **Publication:** [Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond "Wayne G. Hammond") and [Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull "Christina Scull") published a description of the letter in their [*The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology*](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide") (p. 644). A photograph of the letter was reproduced in *[Dominic Winter Children's and Illustrated Books, Modern First Editions 2003](/wiki/Dominic_Winter_Children%27s_and_Illustrated_Books,_Modern_First_Editions_2003)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Transcription {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I am v sorry that this note was not posted until now 4/10/65. A change of secretary and a load of work suddenly imposed on me by <a href="/wiki/Ace_Books" title="Ace Books">USA &#34;pirates&#34;</a> is responsible. I have just found this in a pile of neglect, clearing up before going away.<br/><br/>Best wishes<br/><br/>J.R.R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
