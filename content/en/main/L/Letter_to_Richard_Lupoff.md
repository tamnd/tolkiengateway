At an unknown date, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Richard Lupoff](/wiki/Richard_A._Lupoff "Richard A. Lupoff")**.

- **Contents:** Tolkien denies any influence from the writings of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgar_Rice_Burroughs" title="wikipedia:Edgar Rice Burroughs">Edgar Rice Burroughs</a> on the creation of [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob).
- **Publication:** A brief extract from the letter was published in *[Edgar Rice Burroughs: Master of Adventure](/wiki/Edgar_Rice_Burroughs:_Master_of_Adventure)* (written by Richard Lupoff) and in *[Tolkien Criticism: An Annotated Checklist](/wiki/Tolkien_Criticism:_An_Annotated_Checklist)*. Part of the extract was reprinted in [*Orcrist* (Issue 4, 1969-70)](/wiki/Orcrist_4 "Orcrist 4").
