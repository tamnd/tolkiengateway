At an unknown date, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Richard Lupoff](../R/Richard_A._Lupoff.md)**.

- **Contents:** Tolkien denies any influence from the writings of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgar_Rice_Burroughs" title="wikipedia:Edgar Rice Burroughs">Edgar Rice Burroughs</a> on the creation of [Shelob](../S/Shelob.md).
- **Publication:** A brief extract from the letter was published in *[Edgar Rice Burroughs: Master of Adventure](../E/Edgar_Rice_Burroughs%253A_Master_of_Adventure.md)* (written by Richard Lupoff) and in *[Tolkien Criticism: An Annotated Checklist](../T/Tolkien_Criticism%253A_An_Annotated_Checklist.md)*. Part of the extract was reprinted in [*Orcrist* (Issue 4, 1969-70)](../O/Orcrist_4.md).
