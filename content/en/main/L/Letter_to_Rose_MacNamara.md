On [12 September](/wiki/12_September) [1966](/wiki/1966), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to Rose MacNamara** (née Murphy).<sup id="cite_ref-Burren_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Burren-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Comments:** Tolkien wrote Rose the letter just before she took her vows as a Nun in India.<sup id="cite_ref-Burren_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Burren-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Handwritten letter.

## Transcript {#Transcript}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>My dear Rose,<br/><br/>I hear from your dear mother that you made your final profession, and I send you my congratulations (a weak word alas!), and best wishes: I should say my deep and respectful admiration, and sense of privilege in having known you and being still remembered by you. God bless you in all your days on earth – now so unhappy, though I think it has often (or usually) been quite as unhappy, if perhaps never in such great peril. Your mother has perhaps told you that my eldest son, <a href="/wiki/John_Tolkien" title="John Tolkien">Fr John</a>, said a (Latin) mass for your dear father, and I served it. Spare me a prayer of your sweet charity: I have need.<br/><br/>Yours very sincerely<br/><br/>JRR Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
