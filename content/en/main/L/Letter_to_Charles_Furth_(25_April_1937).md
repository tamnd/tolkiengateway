On [25 April](/wiki/25_April) [1937](/wiki/1937), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Charles Furth**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien replies to a letter from Furth from [15 April](/wiki/15_April), regarding the jacket design for *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.
- **Publication:** Quotes from the letter appears in *[The Hobbit 50th Anniversary](/wiki/The_Hobbit_50th_Anniversary)*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Archive:** The letter is kept at the Tolkien-George Allen &amp; Unwin archive.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
