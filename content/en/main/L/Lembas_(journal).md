<i><b>Lembas</b></i> is the title of the journal of [Dutch Tolkien Society Unquendor](../D/Dutch_Tolkien_Society.md) (Tolkien Genootschap Unquendor). *Lembas* is released four to five times a year. *[Beyond Bree](../B/Beyond_Bree.md)* usually has a summary of the contents in English.

## Issues of *Lembas Extra* {#Issues_of_Lembas_Extra}

Listing of <i><b>Lembas Extra</b></i> (known as <i><b>Lembas-extra</b></i> till 2008), the irregularly published journal of *Lembas*.

- [1985](../_/1985.md): (no subtitle, three articles in English, two in Dutch)
- [1986](../_/1986.md): (no subtitle, one Dutch article in an otherwise English issue)
- [1987](../_/1987.md): *[Tolkien and the Spirit of the Age](../T/Tolkien_and_the_Spirit_of_the_Age.md)*, Proceedings of Unquendor's First Lustrum Conference 1986 (completely in English, this was officially not part of the Lembas Extra series, in practice however it can be regarded as Lembas Extra 1987 in all but name)
- [1988](../_/1988.md): *Motieven in Midden-aarde* \[Motifs in Middle-earth\] (Dutch only)
- [1990](../_/1990.md): *[Elrond's Holy Round Table](../E/Elrond's_Holy_Round_Table.md)*
- [1991](../_/1991.md): (no subtitle, actually the proceedings of Unquendor's second Lustrum Conference 1991)
- [1992](../_/1992.md): (no subtitle, one article in Dutch)

- [2009](../_/2009.md): *[Lembas Extra 2009: Tolkien in Poetry and Song](Lembas_Extra_2009.md)*
