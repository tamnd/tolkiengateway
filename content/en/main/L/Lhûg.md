<i><b>lhûg</b></i> is a noun meaning "[snake](/wiki/Snakes "Snakes"), [serpent](/wiki/Serpents "Serpents")" in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)<sup id="cite_ref-Ety370_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety370-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")*, Noldorin *lhûg* derives from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=LOK&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LOK (page does not exist)">LOK</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety370_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety370-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Amlug" title="Amlug">am<b>lug</b></a></i> = "dragon"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Lhimlug" title="Lhimlug">lhim<b>lug</b></a></i> = "sea-serpent"

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[lókë](/wiki/L%C3%B3k%C3%AB "Lókë")*

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) appears the forms <i>fuith<b>lug</b></i> ("a dragon who guards treasure") and *lûg* ("[worm](/wiki/Worms "Worms"), [serpent](/wiki/Serpents "Serpents")").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
