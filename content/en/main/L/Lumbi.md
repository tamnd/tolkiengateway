**Lumbi** was a castle that was built in [Aman](/wiki/Aman) to be the third dwelling place of [Melko](/wiki/Melkor "Melkor") according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Lum_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lum-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After Melko was overthrown by [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas) in the [battle](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Silent_Pools "Battle of the Silent Pools") of the [Silent Pools](/wiki/Aelin-uial "Aelin-uial") during the [First Farthing Forth](/wiki/War_of_Wrath "War of Wrath"), the castle of Lumbi was used to imprison him after he was bound and chained. Lumbi was guarded by [Gorgumoth](/wiki/Gorgumoth), the [hound](/wiki/Hounds "Hounds") of [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos).<sup id="cite_ref-Lum_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lum-1">[1]</a></sup>

At some unknown time after, [Tevildo](/wiki/Tevildo) gnawed on Melko's bonds until eventually, many centuries later, Melko was released from his imprisonment. After which, Melko was pursued up the [Pine of Tavrobel](/wiki/Pine_of_Tavrobel) by [Telimektar](/wiki/Telimektar).<sup id="cite_ref-pine_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pine-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Lumbi* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), probably being derived from the root <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=LUVU&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LUVU (page does not exist)">LUVU</a></i> ("dark weather")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and has the same meaning as its [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <i><b>Lumfod</b></i>, meaning "gloomy-end" or "Gloomy End"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>, from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lum&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lum (page does not exist)">lum</a></i> ("cloud")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Fod&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fod (page does not exist)">fod</a></i> ("end, butt, bottom")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>. <i><b>Lumfad</b></i> was either an early spelling or a variation of *Lumfod*.<sup id="cite_ref-Lum_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lum-1">[1]</a></sup>
