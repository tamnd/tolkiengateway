<i><b>lómë</b></i> (stem *lómi-*) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word which means "dusk", "twilight" and also "night".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Auta](/wiki/Auta) [i](/wiki/I "I") lómë!* "The Night is passing!"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- *[Lómion](/wiki/L%C3%B3mion)*
