<i><b>lómë</b></i> (stem *lómi-*) is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word which means "dusk", "twilight" and also "night".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Auta](../A/Auta.md) [i](../I/I.md) lómë!* "The Night is passing!"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- *[Lómion](Lómion.md)*
