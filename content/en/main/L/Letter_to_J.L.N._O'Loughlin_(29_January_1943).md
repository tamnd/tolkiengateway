On [29 January](../_/29_January.md) [1943](../_/1943.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to J.L.N. O'Loughlin**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien will likely have no vacations in the near future, since he is organizing courses for Navy and Air Force cadets.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of the letter appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md)*.
