The **Light of Valinor** was a term that referred not only to the glorious, unsullied light of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor"), but also to the pure wisdom and understanding that those living in the [Blessed Realm](/wiki/Aman "Aman") possessed.

The Light of Valinor derived from light before any [fall](/wiki/Arda_Marred "Arda Marred") and is the light of art bound with reason and good bound with beauty.

The Light of Valinor appeared to be a quality that the inhabitants of [Aman](/wiki/Aman) could spread to others: the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) who went there and were instructed by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) gained much wisdom and beauty, so that they surpassed all other [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") races in greatness. When the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) came back to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) at the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), they spread the Light of Valinor that was in them to the [Edain](/wiki/Edain), so that the Edain became a nobler people.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Christianity](/wiki/Christianity) has a concept called "Uncreated Light", being the emminence of God itself, before any fall, or anything else created. There is a branch of Orthodox theology that examines the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabor_Light" title="wikipedia:Tabor Light">Tabor Light</a>.
