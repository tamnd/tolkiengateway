**Luvailin** or **Shadowmere** was a mere in [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar) under the shadow of [Oiolossë](/wiki/Oioloss%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup>

Shadowmere is mentioned in [Bilbo's](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") [song](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil_was_a_mariner "Eärendil was a mariner") of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell):<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>where pale as glass<br/>
beneath the Hill of <a href="/wiki/Ilmarin" title="Ilmarin">Ilmarin</a><br/>
a-glimmer in a valley sheer<br/>
the lamplit towers of <a href="/wiki/Tirion" title="Tirion">Tirion</a><br/>
are mirrored on the Shadowmere.
</p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

As the mere reflected the lights of the city of Tirion, "it must have lain in or near to" the valley of [Calacirya](/wiki/Calacirya).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Luvailin* appears only in the *[Unfinished index](/wiki/Unfinished_index)*. The name *Shadowmere* is said to be its "translation".<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> As Paul Strack explains, it could be a compound of root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=LUB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LUB (page does not exist)">LUB</a> ("shadow, darkness") + *[ailin](/wiki/Ailin "Ailin")* ("lake").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early version of [Galadriel's Song](/wiki/Galadriel%27s_Song), appears the lines:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>And by the strand of Tirion there grew a golden Tree.<br/>
Beneath the stars of Ever eve in Eldamar it shone,<br/>
In Eldamar beside the walls of Elven Tirion.<br/>
But far away and far away beyond the <b>Shadow-meres</b><i></i>
</p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

A different version of the second line above was: *Beneath the Hill of Ilmarin lies Aelinuial*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup>
