<i><b>Les aventures de Tom Bombadil</b></i> is the French translation of *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*. The work was translated by Dashiell Hédayat and presented in a bilingual edition in English and French.

*Les Aventures de Tom Bombadil* reproduces two manuscript pages by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). One includes a version of '[A Elbereth Gilthoniel](/wiki/A_Elbereth_Gilthoniel)' and partial and full versions of '[Namárië](/wiki/Nam%C3%A1ri%C3%AB)'. The other includes short extracts from the chapters '[Many Meetings](/wiki/Many_Meetings)' and '[The Council of Elrond](/wiki/The_Council_of_Elrond)' in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. Also included is a reproduction of a handwritten description by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) of the two manuscript pages.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
