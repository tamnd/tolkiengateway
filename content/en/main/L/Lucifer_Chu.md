**Lucifer Hsueh-Heng "Luc" Chu** (<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanzi" title="wikipedia:Hanzi">Hanzi</a> **朱學恆**) is a Taiwanese translator, philanthropist and internet entrepreneur. Chu graduated from Taiwan's National Central University in [1998](../_/1998.md), with a BS in Electrical Engineering.

With news of an upcoming [live action film series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) about *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* in the late 1990's, Chu approached a local publisher to translate the works for a minimal fee; 9 percent if 10.000 box sets (or 40.000 individual copies) were sold; if not, he donated his services for free.

The gamble paid off, and Chu became a millionnaire. He donated most of his money to his own organization <a class="external text" href="http://www.myoops.org/en/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">OOPS</a>, translating many educational books into Chinese.
