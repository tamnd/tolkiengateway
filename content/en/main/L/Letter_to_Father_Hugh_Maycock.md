On [14 December](../_/14_December.md) [1966](../_/1966.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Father Hugh Maycock**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Description:** In an inscribed a copy of the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hobitto_no_B%C5%8Dken&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hobitto no Bōken (page does not exist)">Japanese <i>Hobbit</i></a> ("With best wishes \| to Fr Hugh Maycock \| and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pusey_House,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Pusey House, Oxford">Pusey House</a> \| Dec. 14. 1966"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>), Tolkien loosely inserted a brief note to Maycock, apologising for the delay "in performing my promise" and now sending "a copy of the Japanese 'Hobbit' with best wishes including Christmas blessings".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>
