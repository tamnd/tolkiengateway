**Legolas** was an [Elf](/wiki/Elf) of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

He was one of the [house of the Tree](/wiki/House_of_the_Tree "House of the Tree"), a scout under [Galdor](</wiki/Galdor_(elf_of_Gondolin)> "Galdor (elf of Gondolin)"). Because of his keen eyes and knowledge of the mountainous area, he was chosen by the escapees of the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin) to be in front and guide them across the [Cristhorn](/wiki/Cirith_Thoronath "Cirith Thoronath").<sup id="cite_ref-III_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-III-1">[1]</a></sup>

Following the fall, he moved to [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), where he was named <i><b>Laiqalassë</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-IIIn_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IIIn-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of *Legolas* was twofold in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish): it could mean "Greenleaf", from *laigos* ("greenness") + *las* ("leaf"); but in the form <i><b>Legolast</b></i> it could mean "Keen sight", from *leg, lêg* ("keen, piercing") + *last* ("look, glance").<sup id="cite_ref-app_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-app-3">[3]</a></sup>
