On an unknown date, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_%2522The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%2522.md) to [Simonne d'Ardenne](../S/Simonne_d'Ardenne.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-JRRVF_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-JRRVF-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fagrskinna" title="wikipedia:Fagrskinna">Fagrskinna</a></i>, philological discussion of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/berserk" title="wiktionary:berserk">berserk</a></i>.
- **Publication:** None.
- **Archive:** The letter is kept at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Li%C3%A8ge" title="wikipedia:University of Liège">University of Liège</a>, with the following description in French: "\[J.R.R. Tolkien\], extrait d’une lettre \[adressée à S.R.T.O. d’Ardenne\], non datée, sur papier jaune pâle."<sup id="cite_ref-JRRVF_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-JRRVF-1">[1]</a></sup>
