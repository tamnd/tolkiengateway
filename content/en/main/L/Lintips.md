**Lintips** were mysterious creatures only mentioned in verse. They appear at night under the moon; Tom wondered whether they slide down from the [Moon](/wiki/Moon), or come in [star](/wiki/Star "Star")-winks. Apparently they are very small, and smell mousy.

[Tom](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil "Tom Bombadil") noticed them from their smell, one night they came too early; he told them to drink up the dew from the grass, but minding his feet. They just ran away and laughed at him to Tom's dismay who wished them to stay, leaving him complaining that they are the only things that won't talk to him, and wondered if they hide something.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

As in the case of the [Mewlips](/wiki/Mewlips), [readers](/wiki/Tolkien_fandom "Tolkien fandom") have "failed to identify a source of the 'lintips'".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Rhona Beare](/wiki/Rhona_Beare) has suggested that the lintips correspond to the "gleaming [spirits](/wiki/Spirits "Spirits")" that danced and sipped radiance from the buttercups, in Tolkien's earlier poem *[An Evening in Tavrobel](/wiki/An_Evening_in_Tavrobel)*, which was probably the basis of *Once upon a Time*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
