On [30 January](/wiki/30_January) [1972](/wiki/1972), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Michael George Tolkien](/wiki/Michael_George_Tolkien)**.<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject(s):** Private life, problems with [fans](/wiki/Tolkien_fandom "Tolkien fandom") and press.<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** [Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond "Wayne G. Hammond") and [Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull "Christina Scull") published a description of the letter in their [*The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology*](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide") (p. 759). Quotes from the letter have appeared in Michael Tolkien's transcript of his lecture delivered at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews" title="wikipedia:University of St Andrews">University of St Andrews</a> (section 26).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Add_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Add-3">[3]</a></sup>
