<i><b>Lugburz</b></i> is the first full-length album by [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md) and was released in [1995](../_/1995.md). The name is taken from [Lugbúrz](../B/Barad-dûr.md), meaning "*dark tower*" in the [Black Speech](../B/Black_Speech.md), referring to [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md). Despite the album name, the cover artwork does not depict the tower of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) but is taken from the Roger Taylor novel *The Fall of Fyorlund.*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
