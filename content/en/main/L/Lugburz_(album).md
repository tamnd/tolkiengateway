<i><b>Lugburz</b></i> is the first full-length album by [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning) and was released in [1995](/wiki/1995). The name is taken from [Lugbúrz](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr "Barad-dûr"), meaning "*dark tower*" in the [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech), referring to [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr). Despite the album name, the cover artwork does not depict the tower of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) but is taken from the Roger Taylor novel *The Fall of Fyorlund.*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
