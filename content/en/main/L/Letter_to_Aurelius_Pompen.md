On [27 September](/wiki/27_September) [1936](/wiki/1936), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Aurelius Pompen** (Professor of English at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_Nijmegen" title="wikipedia:Radboud University Nijmegen">Catholic University of Nijmegen</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Personal life.
- **Publication:** None.
- **Description:** Handwritten letter signed by J.R.R. Tolkien, 20 Northmoor Road, Oxford.
