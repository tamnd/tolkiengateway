On [27 September](../_/27_September.md) [1936](../_/1936.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Aurelius Pompen** (Professor of English at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_Nijmegen" title="wikipedia:Radboud University Nijmegen">Catholic University of Nijmegen</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Personal life.
- **Publication:** None.
- **Description:** Handwritten letter signed by J.R.R. Tolkien, 20 Northmoor Road, Oxford.
