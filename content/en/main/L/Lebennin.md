**Lebennin** (prounounced \[<span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">lɛˈbɛn.nɪn</span>\] *le-<b><span style="font-variant:small-caps;">ben</span></b>-nin*) was a fief<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> and region in the south of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

Lebennin was the land between the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) in the north, the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) in the south and the river [Gilrain](/wiki/Gilrain) in the west<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> from [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien) to [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was known as *Fair Lebennin of the Five Streams*, which referred to the [Five Rivers of Lebennin](/wiki/Five_Rivers_of_Lebennin).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> The city of [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) was in the south of Lebennin.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) a large number of hardy [Men](/wiki/Men)<sup id="cite_ref-MT765_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT765-4">[4]</a></sup> of mixed blood resided there, including short and swarthy people who were more descended from the forgotten men who lived in the shadows of the hills before the coming of the [kings](/wiki/King_of_Gondor "King of Gondor").<sup id="cite_ref-MT_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-5">[5]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Lebennin did not send men to the aid of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) before the [Siege of Minas Tirith](/wiki/Siege_of_Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> This was probably because Lebennin was under attack by the [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar).<sup id="cite_ref-MT765_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT765-4">[4]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), leading the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) and the [Dead Men](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") of [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow), came to [Linhir](/wiki/Linhir). There men from [Lamedon](/wiki/Lamedon) defended the fords agains men from [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) and [Harad](/wiki/Harad) who had sailed up the river, but all of them fled when Aragorn arrived and cried that the King of the Dead had come. Only [Angbor](/wiki/Angbor), the Lord of [Lamedon](/wiki/Lamedon) had the courage to stay and Aragorn asked him to gather his men and follow him because he would need them at [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir). They crossed over the river Gilrain into Lebennin<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> on [11 March](/wiki/11_March)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> driving their fleeing enemies before them and defeated the Corsairs of Umbar and the Haradrim near the city of Pelargir<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> on [13 March](/wiki/13_March).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> On the next day troops from Lebennin travelled with Aragorn and the Grey Company up the Anduin on the captured ships of the Corsairs of Umbar<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> to aid the forces of Minas Tirith and the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lebennin* means "five rivers"<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-13">[13]</a></sup> It consists of *[leben](/wiki/Leben "Leben")* ("five") and *[nin](/wiki/Nen "Nen")* ("rivers", "waters").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>For game development purposes, Lebennin was divided into Lower Lebennin and Upper Lebennin, divided by <a href="/wiki/Pelargir" title="Pelargir">Pelargir</a> and the river <a href="/wiki/Sirith" title="Sirith">Sirith</a>. Lower Lebennin was the eastern-most area in Central Gondor and Upper Lebennin was the western-most area in Eastern Gondor. <a href="/wiki/Dor-en-Ernil" title="Dor-en-Ernil">Dor-en-Ernil</a> lay to the west across the river <a href="/wiki/Gilrain" title="Gilrain">Gilrain</a> and <a href="/wiki/Lossarnach" title="Lossarnach">Lossarnach</a> lay to the east across the river <a href="/wiki/Erui" title="Erui">Erui</a>. The emblem of Lebennin was five wavy blue lines representing the <a href="/wiki/Five_Rivers_of_Lebennin" title="Five Rivers of Lebennin">Five Rivers</a> on a white field.</dd></dl>

Aside from Pelargir, major settlements in Lebennin included "Ost Anglebed", "Glaniath", and [Tumladen](</wiki/Tumladen_(Gondor)> "Tumladen (Gondor)"), along with [Linhir](/wiki/Linhir) which was also part of [Dor-en-Ernil](/wiki/Dor-en-Ernil). The smaller settlements, some of which had fallen to enemy forces before the breaking of the [Siege of Gondor](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor), included "Ethilorn", "Sarnhad", "Malbarth", "Aerthir", "Lornost", "Erynos", and "Halach". A local tradition in Lebennin was the offering of gift baskets to the rivers in honor of the Five Sisters of Lebennin, though by the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) few believed the Sisters were any more than a myth.
