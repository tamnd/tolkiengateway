**Lída Holubová** known also by her nickname **Aegeri** or **Gnóme the Artist** is a Czech artist and active member of the Tolkien community.

She resides in Prague and has a deep connection to Tolkien's world. She discovered Middle-earth through books from her local library as a child and was captivated by the depth and elaboration of Tolkien's work. She is a self-taught artist and was influenced by renowned illustrators like [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe) and [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee). She collaborates and finds mutual inspiration with other Czech Tolkien artists, especially [Matěj Čadil](/wiki/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil) and [Šárka Škorpíková](/wiki/%C5%A0%C3%A1rka_%C5%A0korp%C3%ADkov%C3%A1).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Her illustration *Lúthien Tinúviel* was featured on the cover of *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")* (Issue 62, Winter 2021), the journal of the [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Society). Her illustration *Vingilot* has been featured as the front cover artwork of the eponymous digital publication of The Tolkien Society.

In 2024 she illustrated the booklet for a musical album *Místa Středozemě* ("Places of Middle-earth") by Czech group <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Poetick%C3%A9_hudebn%C3%AD_spole%C4%8Denstvo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Poetické hudební společenstvo (page does not exist)">Poetické hudební společenstvo</a>.

Her illustration *Tilion* was featured as the cover art of Polish Tolkien journal [*Aiglos*](</wiki/Aiglos_(journal)> "Aiglos (journal)") (Issue 26, Summer 2024).

Beyond her Tolkien-inspired art, one of her common subject have been illustrations of fungi. In 2025, her work was included in *Fungi Fest – Houby v umění* ("Fungi in Art"), a group exhibition at Zámek Veleslavín in Prague that showcased mushroom-themed art across diverse media.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
