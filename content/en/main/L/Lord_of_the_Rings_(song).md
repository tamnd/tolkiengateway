**Lord of the Rings** is the fourth track of [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian)'s 1990 album, *Tales from the Twilight World*. It also appeared on *The Forgotten Tales* album and *Imaginations Through the Looking Glass* DVD.
