**Lugdush** was an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs"), and apparently an [Uruk](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai"), in the service of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman). He was a member of [Uglúk](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk)'s raiding band that captured [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") beneath [Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen), and drove them westwards to the eaves of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest). There, Uglúk ordered him to put them down, find two other guards and stand watch over them, unless the [Whiteskins](/wiki/Whiteskins) broke through. Uglúk told them to bind their legs. They later left the Hobbits and followed Uglúk, leaving [Grishnákh](/wiki/Grishn%C3%A1kh) to guard them. It was there that Lugdush met his end, at the hands of [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer)'s [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It's possible that his name contains the [Orcish](/wiki/Orcish) element *lug-* ("fortress, lock-up, prison"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) seen in the name for the [Dark Tower](/wiki/Dark_Tower), *Lugburz*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
