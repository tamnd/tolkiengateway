On [29 September](/wiki/29_September) [1968](/wiki/1968), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Billy Callahan**, a schoolboy in Philadelphia.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Reply to a reader. Tolkien apologizes for not being able to send a photograph, but encloses signatures for Billy to attach in books.
- **Publication:** A reproduction and transcription of the letter appeared in [*Wisterian* (20 December 1968)](</wiki/Wisterian_(20_December_1968)> "Wisterian (20 December 1968)").
