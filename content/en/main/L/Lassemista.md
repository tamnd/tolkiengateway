**Lassemista** was one of the [rowans](/wiki/Rowans "Rowans") of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest), much beloved by the [Ent](/wiki/Ent) [Bregalad](/wiki/Quickbeam "Quickbeam"). It was cut down by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), arousing the anger of this impulsive Ent.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lassemista* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "leaf-grey",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> being a compound of *[lassë](/wiki/Lass%C3%AB "Lassë")* ("leaf") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mista&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mista (page does not exist)">mista</a></i> ("grey").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
