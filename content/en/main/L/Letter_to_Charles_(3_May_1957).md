On [3 May](../_/3_May.md) [1957](../_/1957.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote a letter to **Charles Blackwood**.

- **Subject:** Dwarven linguistics.
- **Publication:** None. Appeared in an episode of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiques_Roadshow" title="wikipedia:Antiques Roadshow">Antiques Roadshow</a> aired in 2021.
