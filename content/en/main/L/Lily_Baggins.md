**Lily Baggins** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Lily was a daughter of [Balbo Baggins](/wiki/Balbo_Baggins) and [Berylla Boffin](/wiki/Berylla_Baggins "Berylla Baggins"). She married [Togo Goodbody](/wiki/Togo_Goodbody) and had many descendants.<sup id="cite_ref-tree_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tree-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the last years of her life, the Shire suffered from the [Fell Winter](</wiki/Fell_Winter_(Third_Age)> "Fell Winter (Third Age)"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and around that time she lost her elder brother [Ponto](/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_I "Ponto Baggins I"). Lily died the next year, as did her other brother, [Largo](/wiki/Largo_Baggins "Largo Baggins").<sup id="cite_ref-tree_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tree-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A *lily* is a plant of the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilium" title="wikipedia:Lilium">lilium</a></i> genus, or to visually similar plants. It derives from Greek *λείριον* through Latin, ultimately of ancient Egyptian origin.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
