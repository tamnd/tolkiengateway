<i><b>lond</b></i> or <i><b>lonn</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) noun meaning "land-locked haven".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) form was <i><b>lhonn</b></i>, meaning "narrow path, strait, pass".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Aglon" title="Aglon">Ag<b>lon</b></a></i> = "Narrow Pass"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Edhellond" title="Edhellond">Edhel<b>lond</b></a></i> = "Elf Haven"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Forlond" title="Forlond">For<b>lond</b></a></i> = "North Haven"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Lond_Daer" title="Lond Daer"><b>Lond</b> Daer</a></i> = "Great Haven"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Mithlond" title="Mithlond">Mith<b>lond</b></a></i> = "Foggy (Grey) Havens"
