On [19 December](../_/19_December.md) [1937](../_/1937.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Charles Furth**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien replies to a letter from Furth from [17 December](../_/17_December.md), regarding the reprint of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.
- **Publication:** The largest part of the letter appeared as [Letter 20](Letter_20.md) in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*. An additional paragraph and a reproduction of the second page was included in *[The Hobbit 50th Anniversary](../T/The_Hobbit_50th_Anniversary.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
