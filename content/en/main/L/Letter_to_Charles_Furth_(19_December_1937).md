On [19 December](/wiki/19_December) [1937](/wiki/1937), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Charles Furth**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien replies to a letter from Furth from [17 December](/wiki/17_December), regarding the reprint of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.
- **Publication:** The largest part of the letter appeared as [Letter 20](/wiki/Letter_20) in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)*. An additional paragraph and a reproduction of the second page was included in *[The Hobbit 50th Anniversary](/wiki/The_Hobbit_50th_Anniversary)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
