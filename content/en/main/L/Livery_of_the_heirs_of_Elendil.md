The **livery of the heirs of Elendil** was a black surcoat with an embroidered blossoming [white tree](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor "White Tree of Gondor") beneath a silver crown and many-pointed stars.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) nobody, except the [Guards of the Citadel](/wiki/Guards_of_the_Citadel) wore it in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Standard of Elendil {#Standard_of_Elendil}

The **standard of Elendil** was a banner attached to a staff with a version of the design of the livery of Elendil that was made by [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) for [Aragorn Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It had a black field,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> with a flowering white tree for Gondor, [seven stars](/wiki/Seven_Stars "Seven Stars") wrought of gems about it, and a crown wrought of *[mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril")* and gold above it.<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-4">[4]</a></sup> These were the signs of the house of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), which Aragorn unfurled at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-4">[4]</a></sup> The royal banner of Gondor had been black with a blossoming white tree beneath seven stars.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
