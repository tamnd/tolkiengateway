The **livery of the heirs of Elendil** was a black surcoat with an embroidered blossoming [white tree](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md) beneath a silver crown and many-pointed stars.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) nobody, except the [Guards of the Citadel](../G/Guards_of_the_Citadel.md) wore it in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Standard of Elendil {#Standard_of_Elendil}

The **standard of Elendil** was a banner attached to a staff with a version of the design of the livery of Elendil that was made by [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) for [Aragorn Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It had a black field,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> with a flowering white tree for Gondor, [seven stars](../S/Seven_Stars.md) wrought of gems about it, and a crown wrought of *[mithril](../M/Mithril.md)* and gold above it.<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-4">[4]</a></sup> These were the signs of the house of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md), which Aragorn unfurled at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-4">[4]</a></sup> The royal banner of Gondor had been black with a blossoming white tree beneath seven stars.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
