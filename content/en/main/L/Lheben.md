<i><b>lheben</b></i> is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word meaning "five".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AI_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- Phrase on a draft of [Thrór's Map](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r%27s_Map): "<i><b>Lheben</b> <a href="/wiki/T%C3%A2l" title="Tâl">teil</a> <a href="/wiki/Brand_(word)#Noldorin" title="Brand (word)">brann</a> <a href="/wiki/I" title="I">i</a> <a href="/wiki/Annon" title="Annon">annon</a> <a href="/wiki/Ar" title="Ar">ar</a> <a href="/wiki/N%C3%AAl" title="Nêl">neledh</a> neledhi <a href="/wiki/Gar" title="Gar">gar</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Godrebh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Godrebh (page does not exist)">godrebh</a></i>" ("**Five** feet high the gate and three by three they go through together"<sup id="cite_ref-AI_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-2">[2]</a></sup> or "**Five** feet high the door and three may walk abreast"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>)
