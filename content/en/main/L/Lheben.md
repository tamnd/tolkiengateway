<i><b>lheben</b></i> is a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) word meaning "five".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AI_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [LEP](LEP.md#Other_versions)<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- Phrase on a draft of [Thrór's Map](../T/Thrór's_Map.md): "<i><b>Lheben</b> <a href="../T/Tâl.md" title="Tâl">teil</a> <a href="../B/Brand_(word).md#Noldorin" title="Brand (word)">brann</a> <a href="../I/I.md" title="I">i</a> <a href="../A/Annon.md" title="Annon">annon</a> <a href="../A/Ar.md" title="Ar">ar</a> <a href="../N/Nêl.md" title="Nêl">neledh</a> neledhi <a href="../G/Gar.md" title="Gar">gar</a> <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Godrebh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Godrebh (page does not exist)">godrebh</a></i>" ("**Five** feet high the gate and three by three they go through together"<sup id="cite_ref-AI_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-2">[2]</a></sup> or "**Five** feet high the door and three may walk abreast"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>)
