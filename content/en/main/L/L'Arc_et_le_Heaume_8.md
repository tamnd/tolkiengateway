<b><i>L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</i> n°8</b> (*Autour du Seigneur des Anneaux*) is the eigth issue of the fanzine <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkiendil.com/asso/mag" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</a></i> by the [Tolkiendil](</wiki/Tolkiendil_(Tolkien_society)> "Tolkiendil (Tolkien society)") french association. It was published in february [2025](/wiki/2025) and it is dedicated to *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
