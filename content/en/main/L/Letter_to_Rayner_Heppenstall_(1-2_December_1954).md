On [1](../_/1_December.md)-[2 December](../_/2_December.md) [1954](../_/1954.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote <b><a href="Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien">a letter</a> to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayner_Heppenstall" title="wikipedia:Rayner Heppenstall">Rayner Heppenstall</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** On [22 November](../_/22_November.md), Heppenstall had written to inform Tolkien that his radio play will be preceded by Gavin Bone's translation of *The Battle of Maldon*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> and hence *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth](<../T/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth,_Beorhthelm's_Son_(radio_play).md>)* shouldn't need any introduction. Heppenstall had also written that they have booked an actor for the "Voice in the Dark". Tolkien now replies that Heppenstall did not mention anything about Bone's *Battle of Maldon* when he paid Tolkien a visit in Oxford, and that he does not believe that such a strategy would help to make the play more understandable:

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>...even to such readers as heard it. <i>I do earnestly hope that it may even now be possible to include such a</i> brief addition to the preliminary announcement as I have appended on the <i>enclosed</i> copy. [He thought that they had agreed that it would be best to omit the &#39;Voice in the Dark&#39;. The lines he selected from the <i>Dies Irae</i>] make sense, are apt, and preserve the rhyme-scheme. They are either recited in speaking voice, or sung to simple tone or tune (which preserves the metre and emphasizes the rhyme) [He adds several pages of notes on stress, speaking alliteration, the pronunciation of names, etc.]
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>
