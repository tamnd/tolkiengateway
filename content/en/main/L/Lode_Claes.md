**Lode Claes** ([1931](/wiki/1931) - [14 March](/wiki/14_March) [2019](/wiki/2019)) was a Belgian artist who created fine line color scene pictures based on Lord of the Rings.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> His work was featured in various exhibitions in Belgium, the USA (Milwaukee, WI), the [1998 Tolkien calendar](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_1998 "Tolkien Calendar 1998"), [The Lord of the Rings: A Book of 20 Postcards](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_A_Book_of_20_Postcards) and [Realms of Tolkien](/wiki/Realms_of_Tolkien). His art was owned by [Priscilla Tolkien](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien). Lode Claes was a close personal friend of [Jan Boom](/wiki/Jan_Boom).
