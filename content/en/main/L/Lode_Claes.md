**Lode Claes** ([1931](../_/1931.md) - [14 March](../_/14_March.md) [2019](../_/2019.md)) was a Belgian artist who created fine line color scene pictures based on Lord of the Rings.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> His work was featured in various exhibitions in Belgium, the USA (Milwaukee, WI), the [1998 Tolkien calendar](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_1998.md), [The Lord of the Rings: A Book of 20 Postcards](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_A_Book_of_20_Postcards.md) and [Realms of Tolkien](../R/Realms_of_Tolkien.md). His art was owned by [Priscilla Tolkien](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md). Lode Claes was a close personal friend of [Jan Boom](../J/Jan_Boom.md).
