<i><b>leben</b></i> is the number five (5, V) in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT47_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md), the precursor of Sindarin, "five" is <i><b>lheben</b></i>. The word derives from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) LEP-.<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT47_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
