In *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* and the *[Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game "Middle-earth Collectible Card Game")*, **Leucaruth** the Wrathful was a bad-tempered [Fire-drake](/wiki/Fire-drakes "Fire-drakes") in the [Withered Heath](/wiki/Withered_Heath). She had a lair ten miles to the north, a complex of caverns called Ire-rock.<sup id="cite_ref-bio_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bio-1">[1]</a></sup>

Rarely at her lair, she spent most of her time hunting Dwarves and Orcs. Dwarves in particular were the subject of her ire: they had slain her mother, sister, and her newborn daughter.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Throkmaw, Angurth and [Itangast](/wiki/Itangast) all sought to claim her. It was Throkmaw who emerged victorious from the mating duels, and together with him she produced a single offspring in the early Third Age: a worm later known as Turukulon.<sup id="cite_ref-bio_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bio-1">[1]</a></sup>

Leucaruth participated in the [War of the Dwarves and Dragons](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons). During the assault on the Dwarven colony of Norr-Dûm in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_2580&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 2580 (page does not exist)">2580</a>, she was slain by [Nain II](/wiki/Nain_II).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
