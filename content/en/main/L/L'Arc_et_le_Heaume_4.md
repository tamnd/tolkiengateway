<b><i>L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</i> n°4</b> (*Histoire d'un aller et retour*) is the fourth issue of the fanzine <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkiendil.com/asso/mag" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</a></i> by the [Tolkiendil](</wiki/Tolkiendil_(Tolkien_society)> "Tolkiendil (Tolkien society)") French association. It was published in february [2014](/wiki/2014) and it is dedicated to *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.
