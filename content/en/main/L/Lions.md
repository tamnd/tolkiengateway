**Lions** were <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felidae" title="wikipedia:Felidae">feline creatures</a> only mentioned in [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") poetry and [Elvish](/wiki/Elves "Elves") linguistic writings.<sup id="cite_ref-ATB12_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ATB12-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup> Apparently the lions roamed the lands of the East (perhaps referring to the lands beyond [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn), the [Uttermost East](/wiki/Uttermost_East)), hunting "beasts and tender men".<sup id="cite_ref-ATB12_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ATB12-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), the name for "lion" is *rá* (pl. *rávi*). The [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) name is *rhaw* (pl. *rhui*).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), one of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s early conceptions of an [Elven](/wiki/Elvish "Elvish") language, the word for "lion" is *rau*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
