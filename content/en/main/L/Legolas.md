**Legolas** was a [Silvan Elf](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) of [Sindarin](../S/Sindar.md) origin, the prince of the [Woodland Realm](../W/Woodland_Realm.md) of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md). His grandfather [Oropher](../O/Oropher.md) was of the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), and his father [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) was king of Mirkwood ([Greenwood the Great](../G/Greenwood_the_Great.md)). His birthdate is unknown, as are his earliest exploits. His most notable role is the part he played in the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), during which he represented the [Elven](../E/Elves.md) in the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md). His Elven abilities, such as superior sight and hearing, lightness of foot, and skilled archery, were invaluable to his eight companions.

Legolas' unlikely friendship with the [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md), [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) son of [Glóin](../G/Glóin.md) was considered odd. As such a friendship was rare between the two races, due to the long-standing grievances between [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) and [Elves](../E/Elves.md) dating back to the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md). And, unlikely because of the grudge between their two particular houses for the rough treatment Glóin and the rest of [Thorin Oakenshield](../T/Thorin_Oakenshield.md)'s company was given by Thranduil on an earlier occasion.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

There are nothing to say about Legolas’s birthdate and early upbringing, except for the very few things he himself has said on the matter.

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the creature [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) was captured by [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), and put under lock and key by the [Elves of Mirkwood](../E/Elves_of_Mirkwood.md). Through their over-kindliness to him, however, Gollum managed an escape with the help of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md). Legolas was dispatched to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to deliver this ill news to [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Council_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-3">[3]</a></sup>

Legolas was present at the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md) and related the news of Gollum's escape, quickly adding that it was not through lack of vigilance that he slipped their grasp, and provided a detailed account of the whole affair to the Council.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-3">[3]</a></sup> He spoke little if at all after his report, but was later chosen to represent the Elves in the Nine Walkers of the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) to participate in the [Quest of the Ring](../Q/Quest_of_the_Ring.md). His skills would prove invaluable to the Fellowship.

### Fellowship of the Ring {#Fellowship_of_the_Ring}

For the early part of the quest, the nine companions walked single file, Gandalf leading, Legolas, with his keen eyes, providing the rearguard. When the Company came to [Hollin](../H/Hollin.md), Legolas was very deeply moved: the wholesome air yet spoke of the Elves that dwelt there long ago, and his sharp ears heard the very stones crying out for grief at their departure and memory of their ways.<sup id="cite_ref-South_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-4">[4]</a></sup>

As they ascended [Caradhras](../C/Caradhras.md), Legolas in his light shoes could walk on top of the snow, whereas the others were forced to trudge and wade their way forward. But snow or no snow, nothing could dampen his buoyant Elven spirit, and he joked lightly with Gandalf even at the darkest moments. He scouted ahead and behind the company, dancing over the snow with great ease, and brought news that the storm was, as they had been fearing, put out to stop them by some Power greater than they. When this report arrived, Gandalf reluctantly decided to abandon the idea of crossing by the [Redhorn Gate](../R/Redhorn_Gate.md). Legolas stayed near the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) on the dangerous descent.<sup id="cite_ref-South_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-4">[4]</a></sup>

At the bottom of the mountain, the company took a vote on attempting passage through [Moria](../M/Moria.md). Legolas was silent until asked for his opinion, and then said simply and seemingly reluctantly, "I do not wish to go to Moria." His loyalty and respectful deference to the decisions of the two leaders, Aragorn and Gandalf, extended even to following them through darkness and unknown horror; of all the Company, he was arguably the most loyal to leadership. When the voices of the [Wargs](../W/Wargs.md) were heard and the attack began, Legolas did perhaps more than anyone save Gandalf, shooting numerous Wargs and even collecting his spent arrows to fire again, retrieving them, like a responsible bowman, after the fight ended.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Fellowship reached the West Gate of Moria, and were halted by the doors fast shut. At last gaining entrance, the Company, following Gandalf's staff, began their march through Moria, a pit so profoundly dark that even Legolas' eyes could see nothing. He served his turn, like the others, in lookout duty through the night on the march. When they came to the [Chamber of Mazarbul](../C/Chamber_of_Mazarbul.md) and were attacked by the Orcs, Legolas dispatched at least two before following the others out the east door, having to drag Gimli away from [Balin](../B/Balin.md)'s tomb. When [Durin's Bane](../D/Durin's_Bane.md) made its frightful appearance, Legolas was the first to spot it when he turned to shoot an arrow towards the Orcs. He recognized it immediately as a [Balrog](../B/Balrog.md), and it is the only recorded time he was ever truly afraid: a Balrog held far more terror for an Elf than for any other, for only they remembered where the Demons of Fire had come from and whom they had first served.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-6">[6]</a></sup>

After the fall of Gandalf, Aragorn led the Fellowship eastwards to the borders of the forest of Lórien. Legolas was seized with a deep excitement, for none of his kindred had been into the wood for many years, and he himself had only heard of it from tales. He was grieved, though, that it was winter, and the full glory of the *[Mellyrn](../M/Mellyrn.md)* was departed. The Fellowship splashed across the [Nimrodel](../N/Nimrodel.md) river, feeling their weariness drained away by its cool waters. As they rested on the shore, Legolas told the tales of [Lothlórien](Lothlórien.md) still remembered by the Mirkwood Elves and sang to them part of the [Lay of Nimrodel](Lay_of_Nimrodel.md). At last, the Company turned aside from the path in order to shelter in the trees for the night, not caring to be caught on the ground by the Orcs pursuing them from Moria. Upon being surprised and questioned by the Elves of Lórien who were watching from a tree, Legolas responded cautiously in their own tongue. When [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) queried as to what they were saying, Legolas slyly responded, "They say that you breathe so loud they could shoot you in the dark." He hastened to add that they need not fear the Elves.

Legolas was called up to meet with the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md), bringing Frodo, though Sam followed, as always. The Galadhrim had heard Legolas' singing and knew him for one of their northern kindred. They had had tidings from the sons of Elrond as to the Quest upon which the Company was embarked, and readily accepted all of the fellowship save Gimli, who was only grudgingly allowed, for the suspicion of the Elves of Lórien towards the Dwarves was especially acute. Legolas was forced to answer for the company, with the reminder to keep an eye on "that dwarf". The next morning, when the Elves took the company across the river [Celebrant](../C/Celebrant.md) on their way to [Caras Galadhon](../C/Caras_Galadhon.md), they told Gimli he would have to be blindfolded, but he was outraged and refused. When Aragorn offered for all of the Company to wear blindfolds, Gimli said if only Legolas would wear one, he would. Legolas was outraged in turn, but Aragorn settled the dispute by asking to blindfold the whole of the Company. In the end, Legolas had no choice but to agree.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In Lothlórien, many Elves sang of Gandalf, and their language was such that only Legolas could understand. Legolas would not translate the lamentations for the rest of the Company, saying that he had neither the skill nor the heart. During their time in Lórien, however, with the influence of the Lady [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) permeating the air, he became fast friends with Gimli, a friendship that would never be broken.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> He was one of the Company that could handle boats, and when the Fellowship prepared to leave Lórien, he was assigned to paddle one with Gimli. From the lady Galadriel, he received an Elven cloak and brooch, like the others, and a long, stout bow and quiver of arrows, such as the Galadhrim used.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

When the Company was ambushed by orc-archers on the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), Legolas quickly leaped out onto dry ground and up the riverbank with his bow, searching in the darkness for any sign of the Orcs. From Frodo's low position in the boats, he appeared to be crowned with white stars as he stood tall upon the bank. Suddenly, the south wind chased the clouds away, and a chilling dread fell on the Company. Legolas looked up, and sighed, "[Elbereth Gilthoniel](../E/Elbereth_Gilthoniel.md)!" as if to draw strength to face the terror riding high in the wind. As the Shadow approached, he bent the great bow of Lórien and shot the descending [Fell beast](../F/Fell_beasts.md) from the sky, one of his most masterful deeds. He was praised by the rest of the Company for this, especially Gimli.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Three Hunters {#Three_Hunters}

When the company was ambushed on [Amon Hen](../A/Amon_Hen.md), Legolas shot many Orcs until his arrows ran out, and then used his knife.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Upon the [breaking of the Fellowship](../B/Breaking_of_the_Fellowship.md), when he learned that Boromir had fallen, he sang a lament with Aragorn, taking the part of the South Wind, which came from the Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Legolas was of great aid to Aragorn in the days following, as he helped to track the [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) across [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). His eyes could see many leagues, and for a while he could see their quarry far ahead of them.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

When accosted by the [Riders of Rohan](../R/Riders_of_Rohan.md) led by [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md), Legolas stood by his friend Gimli when confronted by the haughty Marchwarden, threatening him with death if he attempted to harm the dwarf. When Éomer lent them horses to speed them on their way, Legolas was given [Arod](../A/Arod.md), a very high-spirited horse. But Legolas had the Elvish way with beasts, and after he had leaped lightly upon Arod, the horse was docile beneath him. Legolas let Gimli ride behind him on the way to [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md) in their search for [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md). When they arrived by the smoking pile of Orc ashes, they combed the battlefield for any sign of the Hobbits for several hours, but gave up as night approached. Camping under the eaves of the forest, Legolas noticed how the tree beneath which they sat seemed glad of the fire they lit, stretching out its limbs and leaves to the heat. Though the night was very dark, he was also the first to notice the absence of the horses. Later he asserted to Aragorn that the beasts sounded joyful, confirming Aragorn’s own guess.<sup id="cite_ref-White_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-White-14">[14]</a></sup>

Upon entering Fangorn, Legolas declared that he almost felt young again beside those trees. He commented that in earlier days he could have been happy there. Gimli snorted, saying, “*I dare say you could. You are a Wood-elf, anyway, though Elves of any kind are strange folk.*” Legolas would later reverse this declaration at the [Hornburg](../H/Hornburg.md). When the [Three Hunters](../T/Three_Hunters.md) met with the apparition of an old man, whom they believed to be [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md), despite Gimli’s encouragement Legolas did not shoot him. The old man declared, "*Put away that bow, Master Elf.*" Legolas dropped his bow, but later picked it up again, and was about to shoot when it was seen that beneath the old man’s robes there was white. Yet he recognised that it was [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) just in time, and shot his arrow high in the air to be consumed by fire. Gandalf coolly added, "*Well met, I say to you again, Legolas!*"<sup id="cite_ref-White_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-White-14">[14]</a></sup>

### With the Rohirrim {#With_the_Rohirrim}

Legolas was the first to ask Gandalf about Merry and Pippin, and Gandalf’s apparently miraculous escape. After the story, Gandalf delivered [Galadriel's messages](../G/Galadriel's_messages.md) to each of them, Legolas' being:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Legolas Greenleaf long under tree<br/>
In joy thou hast lived. Beware of the Sea!<br/>
If thou hearest the cry of the gull on the shore,<br/>
Thy heart shall then rest in the forest no more.<sup id="cite_ref-White_14-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-White-14">[14]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>

Later, Legolas again used his eyes for the help of the company, as he spied both of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) and [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) from afar. He played only a passive role in the healing of [Théoden King](../T/Théoden.md), and later was arrayed in shining mail beside Aragorn. Gimli would not ride on Éomer's horse unless Legolas rode beside them, which he did gladly. Éomer declared, "*Legolas upon my left, and Aragorn upon my right, and none will dare to stand before us!*"<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/J/John_Howe_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_at_Helm&#39;s_Deep.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/f/fb/John_Howe_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_at_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg/300px-John_Howe_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_at_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="412" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/f/fb/John_Howe_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_at_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg/450px-John_Howe_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_at_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/f/fb/John_Howe_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_at_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg/600px-John_Howe_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_at_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1000" data-file-height="1374"/></a><figcaption><i>Legolas and Gimli at Helm&#39;s Deep</i> <a href="../J/John_Howe.md" title="John Howe">John Howe</a></figcaption></figure>

As Legolas stood at the [Hornburg](../H/Hornburg.md) at the eve of battle, he said that he did not like the place. Gimli comforted him, and he was glad that the dwarf stood by his side. He also wished that a hundred archers of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) were there, noting the small number of bowmen among the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md). At the opening of the battle, Legolas shot twenty at least, this figure being taken as precise by Gimli. When Gimli returned to the elf for the second time to declare that he slew twenty-one, Legolas counted his kills as twenty-four. By the time the [Fire of Orthanc](../F/Fire_of_Orthanc.md) blew out a piece of the wall, his quiver was nearly empty. With the last arrow the elf saved Aragorn’s life when he stumbled while pursued. At the end of the battle, Legolas had shot a total of forty-one, though Gimli surpassed his count by one.<sup id="cite_ref-Road_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Road-16">[16]</a></sup>

Legolas showed great interest in the [Huorns](../H/Huorns.md) on the way to [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md), discussing them with Gandalf and a less willing Gimli. Legolas promised Gimli that he would go to [Aglarond](../G/Glittering_Caves.md) after the war upon hearing the dwarf’s eloquence, if only Gimli would accompany him on a return to Fangorn. At Isengard he enjoyed a meal in the company of Gimli, Aragorn, and the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md), Merry and Pippin.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

### Return of the King {#Return_of_the_King}

When Aragorn made clear his purpose as to the taking of the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md), Legolas and Gimli willingly volunteered to go with him. Legolas predicted, when Gimli suggested that Galadriel might have sent them soldiers from their own lands, that they need not ride away to find war.

Amidst the Paths of the Dead, riding with the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md), Legolas alone, save for [Elladan](../E/Elladan.md) and [Elrohir](../E/Elrohir.md), [Elrond's](../E/Elrond.md) sons, felt no fear of the [Oathbreakers](../O/Oathbreakers.md),<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> and it may be remembered that the High Elves had power both in the worlds of the seen and unseen. Yet his turn came to be struck to the heart in the opposite sense – when he heard the gulls at [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md), fulfilling Galadriel’s prediction and warning. While telling this story later, he stopped there, while Gimli promptly said, "*For my part I heeded them not*". Legolas saw as Aragorn led the Dead Men what a mighty lord he might have been if he had taken the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Debate_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Debate-19">[19]</a></sup>

Legolas came with Aragorn from the ships during the [Battle of Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), and fought there beside his comrades. He survived the battle, and afterward commented on the longevity and eventual domination of [Men](../M/Men.md) to the dubious Gimli.<sup id="cite_ref-Debate_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Debate-19">[19]</a></sup>

### Battle of the Morannon and aftermath {#Battle_of_the_Morannon_and_aftermath}

Legolas rode for the last time into battle beside his friends to the [Battle of the Morannon](../B/Battle_of_the_Morannon.md). He witnessed the Fall of the Dark Tower, and after the battle attended the ceremonies of the [Field of Cormallen](../F/Field_of_Cormallen.md) in honour of Frodo and Samwise and their victory. That night Legolas would not go to bed, but instead went away to walk in the woods, singing of the Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

At the urging of Aragorn, Legolas remained in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) for a time, after attending the coronation of [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md). During the parting of the [Fellowship](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md), Legolas went with Gimli to [Aglarond](../A/Aglarond.md). After that, he rode off with Gimli to return to [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup>

### After the War of the Ring {#After_the_War_of_the_Ring}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Turner_Mohan_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_depart.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/eb/Turner_Mohan_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_depart.jpg/300px-Turner_Mohan_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_depart.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="432" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/eb/Turner_Mohan_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_depart.jpg/450px-Turner_Mohan_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_depart.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/eb/Turner_Mohan_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_depart.jpg/600px-Turner_Mohan_-_Legolas_and_Gimli_depart.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="1473"/></a><figcaption><i>Legolas and Gimli depart</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Turner_Mohan.md" title="Category:Images by Turner Mohan">Turner Mohan</a></figcaption></figure>

In the early Fourth Age Legolas brought south [Elves out of Greenwood](../E/Elves_of_Mirkwood.md), and they dwelt in Ithilien, which became once again the fairest country in all the westlands.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1080</sup> During his stay in Ithilien, Legolas devised the renowned gardens of Prince Faramir's new home in the Hills of [Emyn Arnen](../E/Emyn_Arnen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup>

Legolas and the [Wood-Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) later worked together with Gimli and the Dwarves to rebuild and improve [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1080</sup> After King Elessar died in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md), Legolas built a grey ship in Ithilien and sailed down the Anduin and onwards into the [West](../A/Aman.md),<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-24">[24]</a></sup> reportedly taking Gimli with him.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_22-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-22">[22]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1081</sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppB_24-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-24">[24]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

### Appearance and equipment {#Appearance_and_equipment}

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) recounts that his father wrote the following "wrathful" comment protesting against a "pretty" or "ladylike" depiction of Legolas:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>He was tall as a young tree, lithe, immensely strong, able swiftly to draw a great war-bow and shoot down a Nazgûl, endowed with the tremendous vitality of Elvish bodies, so hard and resistant to hurt that he went only in light shoes over rock or through snow, the most tireless of all the Fellowship.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>, <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two.md" title="The Book of Lost Tales Part Two">The Book of Lost Tales Part Two</a></i>, p. 327</cite></p>
</blockquote>

At Rivendell, Legolas was dressed in green and brown garb,<sup id="cite_ref-Council_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-3">[3]</a></sup> and he was probably dressed similarly for the duration of the quest. He bore a bow from Mirkwood, and later a [Bow of the Galadhrim](../B/Bow_of_the_Galadhrim.md). He also had a long white knife.<sup id="cite_ref-South_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-4">[4]</a></sup> He was a master archer, unmatched by any other during his time, capable of shooting adversaries from afar, and on occasion kill more than one with a single arrow, as shown when he nailed two orcs through the throat in Moria.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-6">[6]</a></sup> His skill with a bow was even more formidable due to his keen eyesight. At the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md), he also proved skilled at knife-fighting in close quarters.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup>

### Personality {#Personality}

Legolas showed almost irrepressible cheerfulness throughout the journey, passing through Caradhras and even the Paths of the Dead without hesitation or complaint. His youthful nature can be seen from the mocking way he spoke of the "strong men" on Caradhras, who Boromir (meaning himself and Aragorn) had said could forge a way out through the snow.<sup id="cite_ref-South_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-4">[4]</a></sup> He is perhaps most remembered for his friendship with Gimli the Dwarf, and it was during this friendship that we see his faithfulness, and also his love of beauty. Gimli's words moved him when the dwarf spoke of the [Glittering Caves](../G/Glittering_Caves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Road_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Road-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Legolas* is a [Silvan](../S/Silvan_Elvish.md) dialect form of pure [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *Laegolas*, which means "Greenleaf". At one point he is called "Legolas **Greenleaf**" by Gandalf, coupling his name and its translation like an epithet.<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[note 1]</a></sup>

*Legolas* consists of the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) words *laeg*, a very rare, archaic word for "green" (cf. *Laegrim, Laegel(d)rim*, the [Green Elves](../G/Green_Elves.md)), which is normally replaced by *calen* (cf. *[Calenhad](../C/Calenhad.md), [Parth Galen](../P/Parth_Galen.md)* and *[Pinnath Gelin](../P/Pinnath_Gelin.md)*); and *golas*, a collection of leaves, foliage (being a prefixed collective form of *[las(s)](Lass.md)*, "leaf").<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[26]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-pe17_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pe17-28">[27]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:84</sup>

In one note, [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) considered *Legolas* to be a non-Sindarin name, instead being [Ossiriandish](../O/Ossiriand.md) in which *laiquā* &gt; *lēgo* (shortened in trisyllabic names to *lěgo*).<sup id="cite_ref-pe17_28-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pe17-28">[27]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:153</sup>

The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) cognate of *Laegolas* was <i><b>Laicolasse</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-pe17_28-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pe17-28">[27]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:56</sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name "*[Legolas Greenleaf](<Legolas_(elf_of_Gondolin).md>)*" appeared first in "[The Fall of Gondolin](<../T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md>)" in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, applied to a character who guided some survivors of the sack of the city to safety.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29">[28]</a></sup> However, this character had no further development in the [Legendarium](Legendarium.md) and is unrelated to the Legolas of the Fellowship of the Ring.

While writing *The Lord of the Rings*, Tolkien first considered [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) to be the Elven character of the Fellowship, however he dropped the idea, and Legolas was created to replace him. Perhaps this is the reason why Legolas is considered the most underdeveloped character of the Fellowship, playing only a minor role in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">[29]</a></sup>

<b>2001-03: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Legolas was portrayed by <a href="../O/Orlando_Bloom.md" title="Orlando Bloom">Orlando Bloom</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In the &#34;official movie guide&#34; for <i>The Lord of the Rings</i>, a birthdate for Legolas is set to 87 of the <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">Third Age</a>. This would make him 2931 years old at the time of the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>. This date for Legolas&#39; birth was made up by the movie writers. Curiously, the year 2931 was the year Aragorn was born; the writers may have picked the number at random from the <i>Tale of Years</i> in the <a href="../A/Appendix_B.md" title="Appendix B">Appendices</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>He is presented as an unstoppable fighter, arguably to the point of stealing the show; he performs show-stopping yet implausible stunts in battle scenes. For example, in the <a href="../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md" title="Battle of the Hornburg">Battle of the Hornburg</a>, he slides down a staircase using a shield, shooting arrows all the while, and in the <a href="../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>, he takes down an <a href="../O/Oliphaunts.md" title="Oliphaunts">Oliphaunt</a> all by himself. However, in the books, Legolas&#39;s exploits in battle are not presented in great detail. Aside from shooting the fell beast, he undertakes no major actions other than to make peace with Gimli, overcoming their longstanding mutual racial animosity — he and Gimli are followers, rather than leaders. The film-makers later stated that the entire scene of Legolas killing the Oliphaunt and its entire crew was filmed during pick-ups (months after original filming) to insert a major action scene showcasing him, because at that point they realized that he simply doesn&#39;t get to do much in the third part of the trilogy.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Legolas bears two long knives, while in the book he bears only one. Another, more trivial change, was the number of Orcs he and Gimli kill at <a href="../H/Helm&#39;s_Deep.md" title="Helm&#39;s Deep">Helm&#39;s Deep</a>: 42 and 43, respectively. It is not clear whether this was an intentional change on the part of the filmmakers, though it seems likely that it was, since the original numbers were so clear in Tolkien&#39;s own text.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Playing Legolas in the trilogy was Orlando Bloom&#39;s breakout route to superstardom. His handsome features and Legolas&#39;s &#34;coolness&#34;, so to speak, as depicted in the film, have led to the character becoming an unprecedented fan favourite with both fangirls and fanboys, not to mention other Tolkien fans. Many debaters on the Internet during earlier stages of production were worried that a film portrayal of Legolas might render him as far too effeminate for popular consumption. Later, many felt that Bloom was able to avoid this entirely.</dd></dl>
