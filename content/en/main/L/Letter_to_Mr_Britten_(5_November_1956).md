On [5 November](../_/5_November.md) [1956](../_/1956.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to a Mr Britten**.<sup id="cite_ref-HS_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TS_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-BBC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC-3">[3]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Tolkien replies to questions, chiefly about *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-HS_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Hand-written, eight-page letter.<sup id="cite_ref-TS_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-BBC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC-3">[3]</a></sup>
