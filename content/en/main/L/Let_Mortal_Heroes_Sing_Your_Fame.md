<i><b>Let Mortal Heroes Since Your Fame</b></i> is the fifth full-length studio album by [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning) and was released in [2001](/wiki/2001). This is the first Summoning album to make use of *clean* vocals and to make use of audio samples from radio productions of [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Many of the lyrics are taken from, or are influenced by, [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit).

## Lineup {#Lineup}

- Protector: *Vocals (tracks 3, 4, 6-8), Drum Programming, Keyboards, Guitars*
- Silenius: *Vocals (tracks 2, 5, 8), Keyboards, Bass*
