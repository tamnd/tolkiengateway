The so-called **Lockholes** were the storage tunnels of [Michel Delving](../M/Michel_Delving.md), converted into prison cells when [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) was taken over by [Sharkey's Men](../R/Ruffians.md) during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In them were kept those who rebelled against the new regime, among whose inhabitants were [Mayor](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md) [Will Whitfoot](../W/Will_Whitfoot.md), [Lobelia Sackville-Baggins](Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins.md), and [Fredegar "Fatty" Bolger](../F/Fredegar_Bolger.md).

On [4 November](../_/4_November.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), the day after the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md), [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) rode to the Lockholes and released all of the prisoners. Those kept in the Lockholes were treated to harsh conditions and starved. Fredegar was no longer fat; in fact [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) had to help him to walk out. Lobelia looked old and thin although she was able to hobble out on her own two feet; still, she died a few months later. Will Whitfoot was debilitated enough that Frodo took on the job of Deputy Mayor until Will could regain his strength.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)'s poem, *Perry-the-Winkle*,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the [Lonely Troll](Lonely_Troll.md) sat and wept outside of the Lockholes gate because he had no friends. There [Perry-the-Winkle](../P/Perry-the-Winkle.md) befriended the [Troll](../T/Troll.md) who in gratitude took him home where they had a "fulsome [tea](../T/Tea.md)". The inclusion of the word *Lockholes* indicates that Sam wrote the poem after the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
