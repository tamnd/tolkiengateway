The so-called **Lockholes** were the storage tunnels of [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving), converted into prison cells when [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") was taken over by [Sharkey's Men](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians") during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In them were kept those who rebelled against the new regime, among whose inhabitants were [Mayor](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving "Mayor of Michel Delving") [Will Whitfoot](/wiki/Will_Whitfoot), [Lobelia Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins), and [Fredegar "Fatty" Bolger](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger "Fredegar Bolger").

On [4 November](/wiki/4_November) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), the day after the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater), [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") rode to the Lockholes and released all of the prisoners. Those kept in the Lockholes were treated to harsh conditions and starved. Fredegar was no longer fat; in fact [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") had to help him to walk out. Lobelia looked old and thin although she was able to hobble out on her own two feet; still, she died a few months later. Will Whitfoot was debilitated enough that Frodo took on the job of Deputy Mayor until Will could regain his strength.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)'s poem, *Perry-the-Winkle*,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the [Lonely Troll](/wiki/Lonely_Troll) sat and wept outside of the Lockholes gate because he had no friends. There [Perry-the-Winkle](/wiki/Perry-the-Winkle) befriended the [Troll](/wiki/Troll) who in gratitude took him home where they had a "fulsome [tea](/wiki/Tea "Tea")". The inclusion of the word *Lockholes* indicates that Sam wrote the poem after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
