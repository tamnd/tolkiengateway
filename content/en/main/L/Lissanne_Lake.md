**Lissanne Lake** is an American artist. After working in the advertising industry, she transitioned to freelance illustration work in the late 1980s. She's known for her many trading card illustrations. In the 1990s, she was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.

She signs her works, mostly made using acrylics, with a stylized LL monogram that looks like a bow and arrow. She has cited fellow artist [Frank Kelley Freas](../F/Frank_Kelley_Freas.md) as an inspiration.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
