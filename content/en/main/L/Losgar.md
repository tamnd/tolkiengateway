**Losgar** was a place at the mouth of the Firth of [Drengist](../D/Drengist.md), in [Lammoth](Lammoth.md), the cold lands north-west of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was here that [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) landed during the [Long Night](Long_Night.md) with the stolen [swan-ships](../S/Swan-ships.md) of the [Falmari](../F/Falmari.md), and gave the order to burn them, because he considered the host of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) and [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) to be "*needless baggage on the road*", saying: "*Let those that cursed my name, curse me still, and whine their way back to the cages of the Valar*". In the event, the Noldor left behind chose instead to march across the deadly [Helcaraxë](../H/Helcaraxë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

According to a legend, another reason to burn the ships at Losgar was to prevent some Noldor from turning back, such as his own son [Amrod](../A/Amrod.md), who was uncomfortable with his father's deeds. According to this legend, Amrod died as he was still sleeping inside the first ship burned.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Losgar* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), but it is never glossed. However, the name appears in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, meaning "Place of Flowers" in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md), but it is a rejected name for [Alalminórë](../A/Alalminórë.md), a region of [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
