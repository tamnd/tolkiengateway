**Losgar** was a place at the mouth of the Firth of [Drengist](/wiki/Drengist), in [Lammoth](/wiki/Lammoth), the cold lands north-west of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was here that [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) landed during the [Long Night](/wiki/Long_Night) with the stolen [swan-ships](/wiki/Swan-ships "Swan-ships") of the [Falmari](/wiki/Falmari), and gave the order to burn them, because he considered the host of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) to be "*needless baggage on the road*", saying: "*Let those that cursed my name, curse me still, and whine their way back to the cages of the Valar*". In the event, the Noldor left behind chose instead to march across the deadly [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

According to a legend, another reason to burn the ships at Losgar was to prevent some Noldor from turning back, such as his own son [Amrod](/wiki/Amrod), who was uncomfortable with his father's deeds. According to this legend, Amrod died as he was still sleeping inside the first ship burned.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Losgar* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), but it is never glossed. However, the name appears in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, meaning "Place of Flowers" in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), but it is a rejected name for [Alalminórë](/wiki/Alalmin%C3%B3r%C3%AB), a region of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
