On [8 August](/wiki/8_August) [1904](/wiki/1904), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Father Francis Morgan](/wiki/Francis_Xavier_Morgan "Francis Xavier Morgan")**.

- **Description:** The letter, a "remarkable code letter" written by the 12-year-old Tolkien, is kept at the [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library) in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford), and ends with [a limerick](/wiki/There_was_an_old_priest_nam%C3%A9d_Francis "There was an old priest naméd Francis").<sup id="cite_ref-AB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AB-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** An excerpt of the letter was published in *[The Invented Worlds of J.R.R. Tolkien: Drawings and Original Manuscripts from the Marquette University Collection](/wiki/The_Invented_Worlds_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "The Invented Worlds of J.R.R. Tolkien")*.<sup id="cite_ref-AB_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AB-1">[1]</a></sup> The full letter in colour was published in *[Beyond Bree](/wiki/Beyond_Bree)*, October 2020 issue, together with a commentary by Denis Bridoux.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
