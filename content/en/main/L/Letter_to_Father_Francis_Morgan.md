On [8 August](../_/8_August.md) [1904](../_/1904.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Father Francis Morgan](../F/Francis_Xavier_Morgan.md)**.

- **Description:** The letter, a "remarkable code letter" written by the 12-year-old Tolkien, is kept at the [Bodleian Library](../B/Bodleian_Library.md) in [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md), and ends with [a limerick](../T/There_was_an_old_priest_naméd_Francis.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AB-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** An excerpt of the letter was published in *[The Invented Worlds of J.R.R. Tolkien: Drawings and Original Manuscripts from the Marquette University Collection](../T/The_Invented_Worlds_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-AB_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AB-1">[1]</a></sup> The full letter in colour was published in *[Beyond Bree](../B/Beyond_Bree.md)*, October 2020 issue, together with a commentary by Denis Bridoux.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
