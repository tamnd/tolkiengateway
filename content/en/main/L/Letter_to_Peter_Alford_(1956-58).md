Sometime between [14 January](/wiki/14_January) [1956](/wiki/1956) and [2 April](/wiki/2_April) [1958](/wiki/1958), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Peter Alford**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Notes:** While Tolkien's other three known letters to Mr Alford can be dated,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> the date of this fourth letter has not been revealed.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Beregond, Anders Stenström](/wiki/Anders_Stenstr%C3%B6m "Anders Stenström") noted an Elvish passage in one of the letters in this group (and none of the other letters appear to fit the description): "*Another letter* \[among Sotheby's 14 actual Tolkien items to be auctioned by on December 13\] *(to Peter Alford, from the years 1956-58) provides a lengthy discussion of the Elvish greeting: Elen síla lúmenn' ómentielmo. One thing attracts our attention: Tolkien explains the pronominal possessive suffix -lma as a variety of -lme 'we' which in fact means: 'you and I/we'. The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) verb [sil-](/wiki/Sil "Sil") 'shine white' is derived - according to this letter - from the stem ÞIL-*."<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Excerpts were published in *[Sotheby's Valuable Printed Books and Manuscripts 13 December 2001](/wiki/Sotheby%27s_Valuable_Printed_Books_and_Manuscripts_13_December_2001)*.
