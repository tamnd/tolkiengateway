Sometime between [14 January](../_/14_January.md) [1956](../_/1956.md) and [2 April](../_/2_April.md) [1958](../_/1958.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Peter Alford**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Notes:** While Tolkien's other three known letters to Mr Alford can be dated,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> the date of this fourth letter has not been revealed.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Beregond, Anders Stenström](../A/Anders_Stenström.md) noted an Elvish passage in one of the letters in this group (and none of the other letters appear to fit the description): "*Another letter* \[among Sotheby's 14 actual Tolkien items to be auctioned by on December 13\] *(to Peter Alford, from the years 1956-58) provides a lengthy discussion of the Elvish greeting: Elen síla lúmenn' ómentielmo. One thing attracts our attention: Tolkien explains the pronominal possessive suffix -lma as a variety of -lme 'we' which in fact means: 'you and I/we'. The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) verb [sil-](../S/Sil.md) 'shine white' is derived - according to this letter - from the stem ÞIL-*."<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Excerpts were published in *[Sotheby's Valuable Printed Books and Manuscripts 13 December 2001](../S/Sotheby's_Valuable_Printed_Books_and_Manuscripts_13_December_2001.md)*.
