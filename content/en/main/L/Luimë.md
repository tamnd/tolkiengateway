**luimë** in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) means "flood" and "high tide" possibly used also for any tide, or for the spring tide<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Possible [PQ](../P/PQ.md) form \**luimê* from [Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) \*LUY.

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

Tolkien considered also the glosses "floodwater, flooded land" but they were struck out.
