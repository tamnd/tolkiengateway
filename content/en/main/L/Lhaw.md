<i><b>lhaw</b></i> is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for a pair of ears.<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup> See also *[lhewig](/wiki/Lhewig "Lhewig")*.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- Derives from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") SLAS<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>
