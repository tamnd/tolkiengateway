The **Lord of Dale** was the title held by the ruler of [Dale](../D/Dale.md) until its destruction in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2770](../T/Third_Age_2770.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> The only recorded Lord was the final one, [Girion](../G/Girion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> His descendant [Bard I](../B/Bard.md) later founded the hereditary title of [King of Dale](../K/King_of_Dale.md) in [2944](../T/Third_Age_2944.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
