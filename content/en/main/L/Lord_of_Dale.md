The **Lord of Dale** was the title held by the ruler of [Dale](/wiki/Dale) until its destruction in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> The only recorded Lord was the final one, [Girion](/wiki/Girion).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> His descendant [Bard I](/wiki/Bard "Bard") later founded the hereditary title of [King of Dale](/wiki/King_of_Dale) in [2944](/wiki/Third_Age_2944 "Third Age 2944").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>
