**Lemberin** was a name in early versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") for what would later be called the [Avarin](/wiki/Avarin) languages.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In Tolkien's early writings about Middle-earth, the Elvish, or Quendian, languages were divided into three groups, Lemberin, [Eldarin](/wiki/Eldarin), and [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin). The people who spoke Lemberin were known as the Lembi, or Lingerers.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the earlier *[The Tree of Tongues](/wiki/The_Tree_of_Tongues)* and *[The Lhammas](/wiki/The_Lhammas)*, Tolkien has the [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) languages deriving from Lemberin; in the later *Tree* and *Lhammas*, Lemberin has only influenced the Mannish languages (as did [Danian](/wiki/Danian), [Orc-speech](/wiki/Orkish "Orkish") and [Dwarf-speech](/wiki/Khuzdul "Khuzdul"))<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> a concept that is followed in the published *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.

Lemberin was originally going to be [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn)'s native language, but this was later changed to [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin).
