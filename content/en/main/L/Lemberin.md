**Lemberin** was a name in early versions of the [legendarium](Legendarium.md) for what would later be called the [Avarin](../A/Avarin.md) languages.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In Tolkien's early writings about Middle-earth, the Elvish, or Quendian, languages were divided into three groups, Lemberin, [Eldarin](../E/Eldarin.md), and [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md). The people who spoke Lemberin were known as the Lembi, or Lingerers.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the earlier *[The Tree of Tongues](../T/The_Tree_of_Tongues.md)* and *[The Lhammas](../T/The_Lhammas.md)*, Tolkien has the [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) languages deriving from Lemberin; in the later *Tree* and *Lhammas*, Lemberin has only influenced the Mannish languages (as did [Danian](../D/Danian.md), [Orc-speech](../O/Orkish.md) and [Dwarf-speech](../K/Khuzdul.md))<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> a concept that is followed in the published *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.

Lemberin was originally going to be [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md)'s native language, but this was later changed to [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md).
