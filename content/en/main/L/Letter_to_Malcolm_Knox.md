On [17 December](/wiki/17_December) [1947](/wiki/1947), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Mr Thomas Malcom Knox**.

- **Contents:** A letter of thanks for Knox's hospitality when Tolkien delivered his lecture *[On Fairy-Stories](/wiki/On_Fairy-Stories)* at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews" title="wikipedia:University of St Andrews">University of St Andrews</a> in [1939](/wiki/1939). Tolkien also sends Knox a copy of *[Essays Presented to Charles Williams](/wiki/Essays_Presented_to_Charles_Williams)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Description:** One page, handwritten, signed.
- **Publication:** A facsimile was published in *[Tree of Tales](/wiki/Tree_of_Tales)*.
