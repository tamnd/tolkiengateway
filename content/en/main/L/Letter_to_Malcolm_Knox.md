On [17 December](../_/17_December.md) [1947](../_/1947.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Mr Thomas Malcom Knox**.

- **Contents:** A letter of thanks for Knox's hospitality when Tolkien delivered his lecture *[On Fairy-Stories](../O/On_Fairy-Stories.md)* at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews" title="wikipedia:University of St Andrews">University of St Andrews</a> in [1939](../_/1939.md). Tolkien also sends Knox a copy of *[Essays Presented to Charles Williams](../E/Essays_Presented_to_Charles_Williams.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Description:** One page, handwritten, signed.
- **Publication:** A facsimile was published in *[Tree of Tales](../T/Tree_of_Tales.md)*.
