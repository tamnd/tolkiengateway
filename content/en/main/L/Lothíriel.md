**Lothíriel** was the daughter of [Prince Imrahil](../I/Imrahil.md) of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md), and later the wife of King [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).

## History {#History}

Lothíriel was born in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2999](../T/Third_Age_2999.md). She had three older brothers: [Elphir](../E/Elphir.md), [Erchirion](../E/Erchirion.md), and [Amrothos](../A/Amrothos.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Lothíriel married [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md), King of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3021](../T/Third_Age_3021.md). They had at least one son, [Elfwine the Fair](../E/Elfwine.md), who became the 19th King of Rohan upon the death of his father in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [63](../F/Fourth_Age_63.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Whether Lothíriel still lived by that date, or died after, is unknown.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Lothíriel is probably a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but David Salo suggests that it means "lady of flowers" and contains the elements *[loth](Loth.md)* ("flower", blossom"), *[hîr](../H/Hîr.md)* ("lord", "master") and the suffix *[-iel](../_/-iel.md)* ("daugher", feminine suffix).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the published version of *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)*, Lothíriel and Éomer were wedded in "the last year of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md)". In [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md), "The Tale of Years" states that 3021 was the last of the Third Age,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and thus the wedding took place in that year. However, in a late manuscript of what would later become Appendix B the wedding of Lothíriel and Éomer was set in "3022 (or [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md) 1)".<sup id="cite_ref-Third_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-1">[1]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Lothíriel first appeared in &#34;The Road to Gondor&#34;, the first book of Volume 4 of <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>, and can be found in the Great Hall of the Prince in Dol Amroth.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Denethor II proposed to Imrahil a betrothal between Boromir and Lothíriel. Because the betrothal was between close kin, it was disapproved by both Imrahil and Boromir - as well as a part of the population - but neither dared to refuse his request. Both Lothíriel and Boromir reluctantly consented as obedient children, though both were unhappy with the betrothal.<sup id="cite_ref-SOS_1.10_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOS_1.10-6">[6]</a></sup> The marriage was to be in the summer of <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_3018.md" title="Third Age 3018">3018</a>, but was postponed after Boromir suddenly had to travel to <a href="../R/Rivendell.md" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> After the betrothal she often left Dol Amroth and had dealings with the Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-SOS_1.10_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOS_1.10-6">[6]</a></sup> She met Dorthaneth, leader of the Avorrim, who became one of her closest friends. Dorthaneth counseled her concerning her betrothal to Boromir and - during the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a> - weapons, armies and war.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> She mourned the death of Boromir, though she was relieved that she would not have to marry him.<sup id="cite_ref-SOS_1.10_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SOS_1.10-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>When her father answered the summon of Denethor II and marched to Minas Tirith with a large part of the army, Lothíriel was left in charge of Dol Amroth. She sent detachments of <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Swan-knights&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Swan-knights (page does not exist)">Swan-knights</a> to <a href="Lamedon.md" title="Lamedon">Lamedon</a> and the <a href="../B/Blackroot_Vale.md" title="Blackroot Vale">Blackroot Vale</a> against her father&#39;s wishes.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>  She had two purposes in mind, firstly, to protect the local peoples against evil, and secondly, to rebel against her father. When she learned of the threat of the <a href="../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md" title="Corsairs of Umbar">Corsairs of Umbar</a> to Dol Amroth, she recalled the Swan-knights back to protect Dol Amroth.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup></dd></dl>
