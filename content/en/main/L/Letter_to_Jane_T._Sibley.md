On [30 May](../_/30_May.md) [1964](../_/1964.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Jane T. Sibley** of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haddam,_Connecticut" title="wikipedia:Haddam, Connecticut">Haddam, Connecticut</a>. It informs the reader that the [runes](../R/Runes.md) in *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* are not the same set. Typed on Tolkien's own, preprinted paper (self-addressed to [76 Sandfield Road](../_/76_Sandfield_Road.md). Signed and accompanied by a handwritten note suggesting further reading. Copyright by the [Tolkien Trust](../T/Tolkien_Trust.md).

- **Publication:** The letter was first printed in an article by Jane T. Sibley in *Niekas* 38, 1989 (pp. 13, 67). A revised version of the material was published in [*Vinyar Tengwar* 6](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_6.md) ([July](../J/July.md) [1989](../_/1989.md)): a photocopy reproduction of the letter is featured on the back, and commentaries and a transcription (by Sibley) are given on pages 7–8.
