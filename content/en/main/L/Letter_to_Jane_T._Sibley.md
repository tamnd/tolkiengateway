On [30 May](/wiki/30_May) [1964](/wiki/1964), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Jane T. Sibley** of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haddam,_Connecticut" title="wikipedia:Haddam, Connecticut">Haddam, Connecticut</a>. It informs the reader that the [runes](/wiki/Runes "Runes") in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* are not the same set. Typed on Tolkien's own, preprinted paper (self-addressed to [76 Sandfield Road](/wiki/76_Sandfield_Road). Signed and accompanied by a handwritten note suggesting further reading. Copyright by the [Tolkien Trust](/wiki/Tolkien_Trust).

- **Publication:** The letter was first printed in an article by Jane T. Sibley in *Niekas* 38, 1989 (pp. 13, 67). A revised version of the material was published in [*Vinyar Tengwar* 6](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_6 "Vinyar Tengwar 6") ([July](/wiki/July) [1989](/wiki/1989)): a photocopy reproduction of the letter is featured on the back, and commentaries and a transcription (by Sibley) are given on pages 7–8.
