**LINI** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "pool, mere, lake".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[ailin](../A/Ailin.md)* ([AYA(R)](<../A/AYA(R).md>) + LINI) = "a large lake"
- [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=L%C3%AEn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lîn (page does not exist)">lîn</a></i> = "pool, mere"
- [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *lhîn* = "pool"

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In the *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* appears the root LIN<sup>1</sup>- signifying "pool". LIN<sup>1</sup>- yields the following derivatives of the same meaning: [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *linya*, [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *lhîn*, and [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md) *line*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-2">[2]</a></sup> The root is related to [LINKWI](LINKWI.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
