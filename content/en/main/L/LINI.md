**LINI** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "pool, mere, lake".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[ailin](/wiki/Ailin "Ailin")* ([AYA(R)](</wiki/AYA(R)>) + LINI) = "a large lake"
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=L%C3%AEn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lîn (page does not exist)">lîn</a></i> = "pool, mere"
- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *lhîn* = "pool"

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appears the root LIN<sup>1</sup>- signifying "pool". LIN<sup>1</sup>- yields the following derivatives of the same meaning: [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *linya*, [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *lhîn*, and [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin) *line*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-2">[2]</a></sup> The root is related to [LINKWI](/wiki/LINKWI).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
