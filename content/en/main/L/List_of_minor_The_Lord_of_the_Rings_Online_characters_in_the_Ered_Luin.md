This list contains all minor characters in the [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md) that were specifically created for [The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md).

## Anglor {#Anglor}

**Anglor** is a female [Elf](../E/Elves.md) and guardian trainer in Edhelion. She was a member of Dorongúr Whitethorn's company that attempted to restore Edhelion. During the attempt Dorongúr sent the guardian player to her so he could learn from her.<sup id="cite_ref-meditating_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-meditating-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Bogi {#Bogi}

**Bogi** is a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) from Erebor. He was a member of the company of Dwarves who were sent by [Dáin II](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) to investigate Thorin's Hall.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup> On his request <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Orodlin">Orodlin</a> and <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Selur">Selur</a> sent the player inside the Silver Deep Mines to kill a few of the Cave-claws, who were destroying the land by digging.<sup id="cite_ref-fear_of_collapse_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fear_of_collapse-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Bolli {#Bolli}

**Bolli** was a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) from [Erebor](Lonely_Mountain.md) who was a member of the company of [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) that was sent to investigate Thorin's Gate by [Dáin II](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md). He sends the dwarf player to [Nos Grimsong](../N/Nos_Grimsong.md) in the Introduction.<sup id="cite_ref-return_lonely_mountain_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-return_lonely_mountain-4">[4]</a></sup> After Gondamon was reconquered by the [Longbeards](Longbeards.md) he became captain of the guard of Gondamon.<sup id="cite_ref-Goblins_in_the_South_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Goblins_in_the_South-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Calthendin {#Calthendin}

**Calthendin** is an [Elven](../E/Elves.md) warden trainers in Edhelion during [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md)'s attempt to restore Edhelion. In the Introduction he improves the warden player's skills on the request of Dorongúr Whitethorn.<sup id="cite_ref-meditating_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-meditating-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Dorollas {#Dorollas}

**Dorollas** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) in Edhelion who wants to restore Edhelion to its former glory, and therefore joined the company of Elves that attempted to restore Edhelion in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) 3016. [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md), the leader of the company, sent the player to Dorollas to aid in cleaning Edhelion.<sup id="cite_ref-plague_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-plague-6">[6]</a></sup> On Dorollas's request the player kills several slugs that inhabit a befouled pool<sup id="cite_ref-befouled_pool_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-befouled_pool-7">[7]</a></sup> and afterwards sends him to [Elladan](../E/Elladan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-scouting_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-scouting-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Dúri Bold-Hammer {#Dúri_Bold-Hammer}

**Dúri Bold-Hammer** is a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) in Frerin's Court. Dúri was one of the Dwarves that accompenied [Dwalin](../D/Dwalin.md) to investigate Thorin's Gate. He is a weaponsmith.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Edenithil {#Edenithil}

**Edenithil** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) and bowyer. He was a member of [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md)'s company who tried to restore Edhelion. During this failed attempt he was at an outpost of Edhelion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup> When the attempt failed he went to [Celondim](../C/Celondim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Edhelben {#Edhelben}

**Edhelben** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) from Edhelion. She fought in the Battle of Edhelion when [Dourhands](../D/Dourhands.md) and [Goblins](../O/Orcs.md) attacked the city.<sup id="cite_ref-refuge_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-refuge-10">[10]</a></sup> It is unknown if he survived the battle or not.

## Erynwen {#Erynwen}

**Erynwen** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) in Frerin's Court. She was a member of the company of [Elves](../E/Elves.md), led by [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md), that attempted to restore Edhelion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup> The player helped her to externimate the poisonous plant Skorgrím's Bloom, with threatened the animals from Thorin's Gate.<sup id="cite_ref-deadly_bloom_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-deadly_bloom-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Fanhir {#Fanhir}

**Fanhir** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) who could be found at an outpost of Edhelion during the Introduction.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup> After [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md)'s attempt to restore Edhelion failed she went to Duilond. She is an one-handed weaponsmith.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Gaden of Lindon {#Gaden_of_Lindon}

**Gaden of Lindon** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) from [Lindon](Lindon.md) who accompenied [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md) when he attempted to restore Edhelion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Gailthin {#Gailthin}

**Gailthin** is one of the orginal [Elves](../E/Elves.md) who inhabitted Edhelion.<sup id="cite_ref-lost_lore_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lost_lore-12">[12]</a></sup> She fought during the Battle of Edhelion and when the city was left she lived in Duilond. She was part of the company that [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md) led back to Edhelion with the attempt to restore the city. There Gailthin tried to collect and catalogue the lore of Edhelion with the aid of the player, who collects many seeds of the trees in Edhelion for her.<sup id="cite_ref-lost_lore_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lost_lore-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Glanwen {#Glanwen}

**Glanwen** is an [Elf](../E/Elven.md) hunter trainer in Edhelion. She was a member of [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md)'s company that tried to restore Edhelion. Dorongúr Whitethorn sends the hunter player to her so he can improve his skills.<sup id="cite_ref-meditating_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-meditating-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Grímr {#Grímr}

**Grímr** is a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) and champion trainer in Frerin's Court. He was a member of the company of Dwarves that were sent by [Dáin II](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) to investigate Thorin's Gate.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup> He thaught the champion player about the champion-class on the request of [Nos Grimsong](../N/Nos_Grimsong.md).<sup id="cite_ref-sharpen_your_skills_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sharpen_your_skills-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Grithur {#Grithur}

**Grithur** is a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) and light armoursmith in Frerin's Court.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Haerendir {#Haerendir}

**Haerendir** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) from Edhelion, during the Battle of Edhelion he was killed by a [Goblin](../O/Orcs.md). The player took her weapons in the Introduction to defend himself against [Dourhands](../D/Dourhands.md) and Goblins.<sup id="cite_ref-refuge_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-refuge-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Hithbar {#Hithbar}

**Hithbar** is an [Elven](../E/Elves.md) armoursmith who sells medium armour. During the Introduction he could be found at an outpost near Edhelion<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup> but after [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md)'s attempt to restore Edhelion failed he went to Duilond.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Laergil {#Laergil}

**Laergil** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) at an outpost of Edhelion. She asked the player to kill a few lynxes because they were threatening the [Thrushes](../T/Thrushes.md).<sup id="cite_ref-restoring_the_balance_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-restoring_the_balance-14">[14]</a></sup> When the attempt to restore Edhelion failed she moved to Celondim.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Lennasthrieln {#Lennasthrieln}

**Lennasthrieln** is an [Elven](../E/Elves.md) rune-keeper trainers in Edhelion during the Introduction. She participated in the failed attempt to restore Edhelion, she helped the rune-keeper player by teaching him more about his class on the request of [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-meditating_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-meditating-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Malthendring {#Malthendring}

**Malthendring** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) and a champion trainer during the Introduction. He participated in [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md)'s attempt to restore the Elven city Edhelion. The champion player was sent to him to learn more about the skills of a champion by Dorongúr Whitethorn.<sup id="cite_ref-meditating_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-meditating-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Merethen {#Merethen}

**Merethen** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) and one of the orginal inhabitians of Edhelion. She fought in the Battle of Edhelion and survived. She was part of [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md)'s company that wanted to restore Edhelion in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) 3016. She told the player that Dorongúr wanted to speak with him/her.<sup id="cite_ref-elrond&#39;s_premonition_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-elrond&#39;s_premonition-15">[15]</a></sup>

## Methgil {#Methgil}

**Methgil** is an [Elven](../E/Elves.md) warden trainer in Frerin's Court.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup> During the Introduction he taught the warden player, on request of [Nos Grimsong](../N/Nos_Grimsong.md), about warden skills.<sup id="cite_ref-sharpen_your_skills_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sharpen_your_skills-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Narl {#Narl}

**Narl** is a [Dwarven](../D/Dwarves.md) rune-keeper trainer in Frerin's Court.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup> On the request of [Nos Grimsong](../N/Nos_Grimsong.md) he taught the dwarf player about the rune-keeper class.<sup id="cite_ref-sharpen_your_skills_13-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sharpen_your_skills-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Nial {#Nial}

**Nial** is a [Dwarven](../D/Dwarves.md) minstrel trainer in Frerin's Court.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup> He was a member of the company of Dwarves who were led by [Dwalin](../D/Dwalin.md) to investigate Thorin's Gate on [Dáin II](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md)'s command.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup> During the Introduction he taught the dwarf player about minstrel skills on the request of [Nos Grimsong](../N/Nos_Grimsong.md).<sup id="cite_ref-sharpen_your_skills_13-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sharpen_your_skills-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Orodlin {#Orodlin}

**Orodlin** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) from [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) who travelled with [Elladan](../E/Elladan.md) and [Elrohir](../E/Elrohir.md) to Thorin's Gate, where he was positioned at an outpost near Edhelion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup> On the request of the [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Bogi">Bogi</a> he sends the elf player inside the Silver Deep Mines to kill some Cave-claws, who are destroying the land by digging.<sup id="cite_ref-fear_of_collapse_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fear_of_collapse-3">[3]</a></sup> Latter he returned back to Rivendell, where he can be found at the market.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Otur {#Otur}

**Otur** is a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) from [Thorin's Hall](../T/Thorin's_Halls.md). In the Ered Luin Introduction he gave the player a weapon while they went deep in the Silver Deep Mines in search of [Tvistur](#Tvistur).<sup id="cite_ref-into_the_silver_deep_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-into_the_silver_deep-17">[17]</a></sup>

## Ovi Redforge {#Ovi_Redforge}

**Ovi Redforge** is a [Dwarven](../D/Dwarves.md) medium armoursmith in Frerin's Court. Ovi Redforge was a member of the company of [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) who were sent to investigate Thorin's Gate by [Dáin II](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Pennastan {#Pennastan}

**Pennastan** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) from Duilond and lore-master trainer. She was a member of [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md)'s company that attempted to restore Edhelion. During the Introduction she teaches the player about fire-lore<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> on the request of Dorongúr Whitethorn.<sup id="cite_ref-meditating_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-meditating-1">[1]</a></sup> When the attempt failed Pennastan returned to Duilond.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Perphant {#Perphant}

**Perphant** is an [Elven](../E/Elves.md) light armoursmith in an outpost near Edhelion.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Reklar {#Reklar}

**Reklar** is a [Dwarven](../D/Dwarves.md) hunter trainer in Frerin's Court and a member of the company of [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) who were sent to investigate Thorin's Gate by [Dáin II](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup> On the request of [Nos Grimsong](../N/Nos_Grimsong.md) he taught the hunter player about the hunter class during the Introduction.<sup id="cite_ref-sharpen_your_skills_13-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sharpen_your_skills-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Selur {#Selur}

**Selur** is a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) in Frerin's Court.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup> On the request of <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Bogi">Bogi</a> he sends the dwarf player inside the Silver Deep Mines to kill the Cave-claws, who are destroying the land.<sup id="cite_ref-fear_of_collapse_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fear_of_collapse-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Snorri {#Snorri}

**Snorri** is a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) and guardian trainer in Frerin's Court.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup> Snorri taught the guardian trainer about the guardian class on [Nos Grimsong](../N/Nos_Grimsong.md)'s request.<sup id="cite_ref-sharpen_your_skills_13-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sharpen_your_skills-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Thalchyl {#Thalchyl}

**Thalchyl** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) and a minstrel trainer in Edhelion. Thalchyl was part of [Dorongúr Whitethorn](../D/Dorongúr_Whitethorn.md)'s company that tried to restore Edhelion. He taught the minstrel player about the minstrel-class on the request of Dorongúr Whitethorn.<sup id="cite_ref-meditating_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-meditating-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Tombes {#Tombes}

**Tombes** is a [Elven](../E/Elves.md) lore-master in Frerin's Court.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup> During the Introduction he taught the minstrel player, on the request of [Nos Grimsong](../N/Nos_Grimsong.md), about the minstrel class and skills.<sup id="cite_ref-sharpen_your_skills_13-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sharpen_your_skills-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Torri {#Torri}

**Torri** is a [Dwarven](../D/Dwarves.md) bowyer in Frerin's Court and one of the members of the company of Dwarves who were led by [Dwalin](../D/Dwalin.md) to investigate Thorin's Gate.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Tryggwi {#Tryggwi}

**Tryggwi** is a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) from [Erebor](Lonely_Mountain.md). He was a member of the company of [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) who were sent by [Dáin II](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) to Thorin's Gate. He was one of the guards of [Dwalin](../D/Dwalin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_9-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Tvistur {#Tvistur}

**Tvistur** is a [Dourhand](../D/Dourhands.md) [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) and a miner in the Silver Deep Mines. He ignored the rule of the [Longbeards](Longbeards.md) and mined in search of Edhelion's relics. In his search he found an entrance to the Halls of Edhelion but accidentally freed a [Stone-troll](../T/Trolls.md); Tvistur decided to fight the creature but was killed.<sup id="cite_ref-into_the_silver_deep_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-into_the_silver_deep-17">[17]</a></sup>

## Unnarr {#Unnarr}

**Unnarr** is a dwarf from [Erebor](Lonely_Mountain.md) and the captain of the guard in Thorin's Hall.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> He was a member of the company of [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) that were sent by [Dwalin](../D/Dwalin.md) to investigate Thorin's Hall.<sup id="cite_ref-Ered_Luin_introduction_2-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ered_Luin_introduction-2">[2]</a></sup>
