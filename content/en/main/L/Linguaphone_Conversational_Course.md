<i><b>Linguaphone Conversational Course</b></i> is an English language course originally released in [1930](/wiki/1930) as a set of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gramophone_record#Speeds" title="wikipedia:Gramophone record">78 rpm records</a> by the Linguaphone Institute of London. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) read parts of Lesson 20 "At the Tobacconist's" and Lesson 30 "Wireless", which is the earliest known recording of his voice.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup> Tolkien made the recording in July [1929](/wiki/1929).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
