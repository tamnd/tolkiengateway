The **last prince of Cardolan** was a noble during the [demise](../F/Fall_of_Cardolan.md) of [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md).

## History {#History}

Almost 500 years before his death, the Kingdom of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) had been divided after the death of King [Eärendur](<../E/Eärendur_(King_of_Arnor).md>) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [861](../T/Third_Age_861.md) into the realms of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md), [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md) and [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md), because of disagreements among the sons of Eärendur.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Not long before the death of the last prince of Cardolan,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> King [Argeleb I](../A/Argeleb_I.md) of Arthedain claimed to be the King of all of Arnor, and thus to also be the King of Cardolan, because there were no remaining [descendants](../H/Heir_of_Isildur.md) of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) in Cardolan and Rhudaur.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

It is possible that Cardolan had accepted this claim and that the ruler of Cardolan became a vassal ruler with the nominal title "prince". In any case, it appears that Cardolan was not actively opposing Arthedain, but rather assisting it in the fight against Angmar and the now corrupted Rhudaur.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>

The last prince of Cardolan died in a [war](../A/Angmar_conflict.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1409](../T/Third_Age_1409.md) when a great army from [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) attacked Cardolan. He was buried in a mound in the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mounds_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mounds-6">[5]</a></sup>

In that tomb was a pile of treasures, including a blue-jewelled brooch belonging to a "fair" woman that [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md) seemingly knew long ago.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> Whether that lady was related to the last prince, no tale tells.

## Legacy {#Legacy}

The [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) of Angmar rose and empowered the [Barrow-wights](../B/Barrow-wights.md) of Cardolan<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup> and slew the [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) in order to trap the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../R/Ring.md" title="Ring">Ring</a>-bearer.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup> Indeed, on [28 September](../_/28_September.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md)<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>, [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and his comrades were trapped in the Barrow-downs by a [Barrow-wight](../B/Barrow-wight.md) in a mound,<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup> which some say was the grave of the last prince of Cardolan<sup id="cite_ref-Mounds_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mounds-6">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-13">[12]</a></sup>.

While [Merry](../M/Merry.md) was trapped in the mound, he dreamed that he and others were attacked and defeated by "the men of [Carn Dûm](../C/Carn_Dûm.md)" at night and that his heart had been pierced with a spear<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup>, which probably was a memory of the last moments of the prince<sup id="cite_ref-RC_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-13">[12]</a></sup>.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earlier versions of the story, the barrow where the Hobbits were trapped was said to belong to a prince of the men that "fought against the evil foes" long ago, and was slain during the war. The identification of the buried prince with the last prince of Cardolan, as well as the appearance of Angmar and the men of Carn Dûm, are subsequent.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup>

Also, in the earlier versions Tom Bombadil's statement regarding the blue-jewelled brooch he took for Goldberry, and the mysterious lady that once wore it, was almost identical to the published version, except that he also mentioned that "they shall not forget" the kings, the warriors and the fair maidens of the past.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[16]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The Last Prince of Cardolan is named Ostir, son of Ostecthel. Like his father and grandfather before him, he ruled in fealty to <a href="../A/Argeleb_I.md" title="Argeleb I">Argeleb I</a> after the direct line of Isildur in Cardolan had died out. He fought bravely in the <a href="../F/Fall_of_Amon_Sûl.md" title="Fall of Amon Sûl">Fall of Amon Sûl</a>, only to return to find his home razed in his absence. His men fell into an ambush, and Ostir was the last to fall in battle. His mother, princess-regent Luilloth went to <a href="../T/Tom_Bombadil.md" title="Tom Bombadil">Tom Bombadil</a> and Lady <a href="../G/Goldberry.md" title="Goldberry">Goldberry</a> to plead for aid against the evil raised by the <a href="../W/Witch-king.md" title="Witch-king">Witch-king</a> in the <a href="../B/Barrow-downs.md" title="Barrow-downs">Barrow-downs</a>. Though she did not come back, the evil had subsided for a time; the blue-jewelled brooch belonged to her.</dd></dl>
