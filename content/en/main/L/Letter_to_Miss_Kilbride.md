On [24 December](/wiki/24_December) [1938](/wiki/1938), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Miss Kilbride** (Katherine or Kitty), a friend and former student.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Personal greetings. News of the hardships suffered that year and of his family, and complaining abut the state of Oxford in the age of Morris Motors.<sup id="cite_ref-Bonhams_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bonhams-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Postcard (reproducing in colours [Josef Madlener](/wiki/Josef_Madlener)'s Heilige Familie), one page, closely written in a minute hand.<sup id="cite_ref-Bonhams_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bonhams-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A reproduction of and quotes from the letter appeared in the auction catalogue *[Bonhams Books, Maps, Manuscripts and Historical Photographs 12 June 2012](/wiki/Bonhams_Books,_Maps,_Manuscripts_and_Historical_Photographs_12_June_2012)*.
