**Lomendánar** (also **Lome Danar**), "The Days of Gloaming", was a name in early versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") for the period before the making of the [Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps "Two Lamps").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
