**Lomendánar** (also **Lome Danar**), "The Days of Gloaming", was a name in early versions of the [legendarium](Legendarium.md) for the period before the making of the [Lamps](../T/Two_Lamps.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
