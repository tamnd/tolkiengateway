**Lord Lovidicus** is an American Dungeon Synth musician, and has released 17 albums<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> since 2009. Most of his music has no vocals, but a few of the songs and titles are named after events and characters in the Sillmarillion and Lord of the Rings.

<br>

<br>
