**Lethuin** was a [Silvan](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) [Elf](../E/Elves.md) of the [Woodland Realm](../W/Woodland_Realm.md) in the [*Hobbit* films](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>), portrayed by Eli Kent.

## History {#History}

Lethuin helped capture [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) while they were in [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md), and was later seen in the cellar conversing with [Galion](../G/Galion.md) while [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) was searching for a means of helping the Dwarves escape.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lethuin* is an anagram of the name [Lúthien](Lúthien.md), which is similar to how other background characters were given names of that nature throughout the [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) films.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> However, there is no actual background or etymology behind it.
