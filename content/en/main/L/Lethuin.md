**Lethuin** was a [Silvan](/wiki/Silvan_Elves "Silvan Elves") [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm) in the [*Hobbit* films](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), portrayed by Eli Kent.

## History {#History}

Lethuin helped capture [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) while they were in [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood), and was later seen in the cellar conversing with [Galion](/wiki/Galion) while [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) was searching for a means of helping the Dwarves escape.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lethuin* is an anagram of the name [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien), which is similar to how other background characters were given names of that nature throughout the [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) films.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> However, there is no actual background or etymology behind it.
