On [17 January](/wiki/17_January) [1973](/wiki/1973), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) sent a <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procuration" title="wikipedia:Procuration">per procurationem</a></i> **[letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to N.J. Kyle**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Reply to letter from Kyle from [14 January](/wiki/14_January). Apologies for not being able to offer a longer reply.
- **Description:** Type-written letter (1 page).
- **Publication:** None.

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

Tolkien writes that he has not "*in fact looked at [The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion) for over a year and a great deal needs to be done on it ... As far as I can see, there can be no more books about [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") but of course hobbits will feature in this new book The Silmarillion though, as you know, the tale will deal with the age prior to that in [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings).*"
