On [15 November](/wiki/15_November) [1961](/wiki/1961), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Jane Neave](/wiki/Jane_Neave)**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Discussion about the forthcoming *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)* (1962),<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup> as well as his former chair at Oxford: "I wasn't a professor of "Literature" - which I still think is not a good basis for academic study".<sup id="cite_ref-TB_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **Description:** 2 pages.<sup id="cite_ref-TB_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of the letter appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*. Extracts from the letter were quoted in *[Christie's 20th Century Books and Manuscripts 2 December 2003](/wiki/Christie%27s_20th_Century_Books_and_Manuscripts_2_December_2003)*.
- **Archive:** The letter is kept at the [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University).<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>
