**Lamedon** was a fief<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and region near the upper waters of the rivers [Ciril](/wiki/Ciril) and [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-3">[3]</a></sup> on the southern side of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Tarlang's Neck](/wiki/Tarlang%27s_Neck), a long ridge of rock between the main ridge of the White Mountains and a mountain branch in the south was the border<sup id="cite_ref-Company_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Company-5">[5]</a></sup> between Lamedon and the plain of [Erech](/wiki/Erech) in the [Morthond Vale](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale "Blackroot Vale").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) the Lord of Lamedon was [Angbor](/wiki/Angbor).<sup id="cite_ref-Debate_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Debate-8">[8]</a></sup>

[Beregond](/wiki/Beregond), a guard of the Citadel of Gondor, thought that the [beacons](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor") were ignited on 7 March [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), because of news from [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin) that a great fleet of the [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar) was approaching the mouths of the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

On 9 March [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) followed by the [Army of the Dead](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") took the road from Erech over Tarlang's Neck into Lamedon and discovered that the town of [Calembel](/wiki/Calembel) had been deserted by its inhabitants, because many men had left to fight in the war and the remaining population had fled into the hills after they had heard a rumour that the [King of the Dead](/wiki/King_of_the_Dead) was coming.<sup id="cite_ref-Company_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Company-5">[5]</a></sup> On the same day only a few hillmen from Lamedon without a captain arrived at Minas Tirith to aid in the defence of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> It is probable that this was due to the fact that Angbor needed most of his troops in the south in order to defend it against the attack by troops from [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) and [Harad](/wiki/Harad), because on 11 March [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> men of Lamedon led by Angbor defended the fords over the river [Gilrain](/wiki/Gilrain) at Linhir against attackers from [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) and [Harad](/wiki/Harad) who had sailed up the river. When Aragorn arrived at the battle at Linhir with the Grey Company and the Army of the Dead the defenders and the attackers fled in fear of the Dead with the exception of Angbor who was brave enough to stay. Then Aragorn requested Angbor to gather his troops and to follow them to [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir).<sup id="cite_ref-Debate_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Debate-8">[8]</a></sup> The Grey Company departed first, and by the time Angbor joined them at Pelargir with all the horsemen he could assemble, the [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar) and the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) had been driven away, and the Dead released, because they had fulfilled their oath.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> Many of the men of Lamedon, as well as soldiers from [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin) and various other southern fiefs, came in ships to Minas Tirith and fought in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> But Angbor, leading four thousand, went by land, coming to garrison Minas Tirith after the hosts departed for the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) and the ensuing [Battle of the Morannon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lamedon* is a [pre-Númenórean](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean "Pre-Númenórean") name (it is *not* [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-3">[3]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Lamedon was an area in the region of Western <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>, located northeast of the Havens of <a href="/wiki/Belfalas" title="Belfalas">Belfalas</a>, west of the <a href="/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3_Vale" title="Ringló Vale">Ringló Vale</a> in Central Gondor, and southeast of the <a href="/wiki/Blackroot_Vale" title="Blackroot Vale">Blackroot Vale</a> via <a href="/wiki/Tarlang%27s_Neck" title="Tarlang&#39;s Neck">Tarlang&#39;s Neck</a>. Its emblem was a black fist on a yellow field. The capital was <a href="/wiki/Calembel" title="Calembel">Calembel</a> and other settlements included &#34;Dínadab&#34;, &#34;Lothgobel&#34; and &#34;Bar Elir&#34;. The river <a href="/wiki/Ciril" title="Ciril">Ciril</a> was fed by several tributaries and all were a source of freshwater fish for <a href="/wiki/Dol_Amroth" title="Dol Amroth">Dol Amroth</a>. South of the river <a href="/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3" title="Ringló">Ringló</a> on the edges of the Emyn Ernil were two <a href="/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor" title="Warning beacons of Gondor">beacons</a>, &#34;Maegond&#34; and another only identified as the Fallen Beacon.</dd></dl>
