**Marh-**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> was a military leader of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) who served under [Lord](Lord_of_the_Éothéod.md) [Forthwini](../F/Forthwini.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Leader_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Leader-2">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

He aided King [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) against the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) in the summer of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Leader_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Leader-2">[1]</a></sup>

During the battle, he rode to the rescue of a party of Éothéod as they retreated to­wards the [Dead Marshes](../D/Dead_Marshes.md). When a soldier died in his arms, he searched the body and found some tokens which identified the dead soldier as [Faramir](<../F/Faramir_(son_of_Ondoher).md>), the Prince of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Leader_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Leader-2">[1]</a></sup>

The leader went to join [Minohtar](../M/Minohtar.md) at the head of the [North Road](../N/North_Road.md) in [North Ithilien](../N/North_Ithilien.md). When Minohtar ordered for a message to be carried to the prince whom he believed to be at [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), the leader of the Éothéod revealed to him that the prince had joined the battle in disguise and was killed.<sup id="cite_ref-Leader_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Leader-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Marh- is a [Gothic](../G/Gothic.md) name element meaning "horse" (see also [Marhari](../M/Marhari.md), [Marhwini](../M/Marhwini.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>
