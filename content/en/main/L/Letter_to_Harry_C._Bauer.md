On [24 November](../_/24_November.md) [1966](../_/1966.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Professor Harry C. Bauer**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Sac_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sac-2">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien thanks Bauer for an article or reprint Bauer had sent him. While the text Bauer sent is unknown, [John D. Rateliff](../J/John_D._Rateliff.md) has noted that "it must have had something to do with the work of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinclair_Lewis" title="wikipedia:Sinclair Lewis">Sinclair Lewis</a>, since Tolkien immediately segues into saying that he is now inclined to think that the word *[hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md)* owes something to Lewis's <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babbitt_(novel)" title="wikipedia:Babbitt (novel)"><i>Babbitt</i></a>". Tolkien also states in the letter that "he read all of Sinclair Lewis" (apparently referring to Lewis's famous books).<sup id="cite_ref-Sac_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sac-2">[1]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Typed letter.<sup id="cite_ref-Sac_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sac-2">[1]</a></sup>
