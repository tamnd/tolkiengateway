**Lord of the Iron Hills** was probably the title given to the rulers of the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md) in eastern [Wilderland](../R/Rhovanion.md). The only two known Dwarves who probably held this title were [Grór](../G/Grór.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and his grandson and successor [Dáin II Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>. In a council immediately before the [Battle of the Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md) after which Dáin became [King under the Mountain](../K/King_under_the_Mountain.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> and [King of Durin's Folk](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>, Dáin was referred to as a "dwarf-lord"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>. It is not known if anyone else took up the title of Lord of the Iron Hills after Dáin became a king.
