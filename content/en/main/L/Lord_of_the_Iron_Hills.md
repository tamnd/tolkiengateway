**Lord of the Iron Hills** was probably the title given to the rulers of the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) in eastern [Wilderland](/wiki/Rhovanion "Rhovanion"). The only two known Dwarves who probably held this title were [Grór](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3r)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and his grandson and successor [Dáin II Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>. In a council immediately before the [Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Five_Armies) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") after which Dáin became [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> and [King of Durin's Folk](/wiki/Kings_of_Durin%27s_Folk "Kings of Durin's Folk")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>, Dáin was referred to as a "dwarf-lord"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>. It is not known if anyone else took up the title of Lord of the Iron Hills after Dáin became a king.
