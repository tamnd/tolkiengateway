In December [1959](/wiki/1959), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](/wiki/Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Publication:** A description of the letter was included in *[Christie's Fine Printed Books and Manuscripts 1 June 2009](/wiki/Christie%27s_Fine_Printed_Books_and_Manuscripts_1_June_2009)*.

## Contents {#Contents}

\[Description and excerpt:\] "In December 1959 \[Tolkien\] complains that he finds 'compulsory retirement both distressing, and extremely laborious', complaining about his 'very inadequate pension'"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
