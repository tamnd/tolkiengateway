On [11 November](../_/11_November.md) [1956](../_/1956.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to a Mr Britten**.<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-BBC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Among topics included, the letter discusses [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) and [Legolas](Legolas.md), and on "the top of one page Tolkien mentions the nomenclature regarding names for places in the [Shire](../S/Shire.md)" apparently "crediting [Christopher](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) for one or more of them, explaining that it was Christopher who drew the map". Further, at "the bottom of the same page, JRR expresses his delight at a question regarding the verse [*Oliphaunt*](<../O/Oliphaunt_(poem).md>), saying that almost none of the reviewers had mentioned his poetry; except [Auden](../W/W.H._Auden.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-BBC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Hand-written letter.<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-BBC_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC-2">[2]</a></sup>
