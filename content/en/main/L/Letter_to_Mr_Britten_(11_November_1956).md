On [11 November](/wiki/11_November) [1956](/wiki/1956), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to a Mr Britten**.<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-BBC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Among topics included, the letter discusses [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) and [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), and on "the top of one page Tolkien mentions the nomenclature regarding names for places in the [Shire](/wiki/Shire)" apparently "crediting [Christopher](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien "Christopher Tolkien") for one or more of them, explaining that it was Christopher who drew the map". Further, at "the bottom of the same page, JRR expresses his delight at a question regarding the verse [*Oliphaunt*](</wiki/Oliphaunt_(poem)> "Oliphaunt (poem)"), saying that almost none of the reviewers had mentioned his poetry; except [Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden "W.H. Auden")".<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-BBC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Hand-written letter.<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-BBC_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC-2">[2]</a></sup>
