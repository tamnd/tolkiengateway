<b><i>L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</i> n°1</b> (*Les Hobbits*) is the first issue of the fanzine <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkiendil.com/asso/mag" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</a></i> by the [Tolkiendil](</wiki/Tolkiendil_(Tolkien_society)> "Tolkiendil (Tolkien society)") french association. It was published in [2005](/wiki/2005) and it is dedicated to the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits).
