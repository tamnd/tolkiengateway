**Lord of the Eagles** was a title of two [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles): the [Great Eagle](/wiki/Great_Eagle)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Thorondor](/wiki/Thorondor)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>.

The Great Eagle was also known as the **lord of the eagles of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains)**<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and the **lord of the eagles**.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Thorondor was also known as the **King of Eagles**<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> or the **king of eagles**<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>. He was also called the **Lord of Eagles**.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

While [Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir) was never called the Lord of the Eagles directly, his name was often translated as the Windlord.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:0_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-15">[15]</a></sup> It is possible that Gwaihir and his brother [Landroval](/wiki/Landroval) were two of the Great Eagle's fifteen chieftains<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>, since all the other Eagles were called their vassals.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-15">[15]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early draft of the chapter "[Out of the Frying-Pan into the Fire](/wiki/Out_of_the_Frying-Pan_into_the_Fire)", the Great Eagle is referred to as "the **lord of eagles**" and "the **[Lord of Eagles](/wiki/Lord_of_Eagles)**".<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> In an early draft of the chapter "[Queer Lodgings](/wiki/Queer_Lodgings)", the Great Eagle's title was changed to the <a class="mw-selflink selflink">Lord of the Eagles</a> and he was given the title **king of all Birds**.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Gwaihir" title="Gwaihir">Gwaihir</a> bears the title &#34;Lord of the Eagles&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/Lego" title="Lego">LEGO The Hobbit</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gwaihir is conflated with the Lord of the Eagles.</dd></dl>
