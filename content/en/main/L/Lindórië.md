**Lindórië** was one of the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), being the daughter of the fourteenth [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië") (and the sister of [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)> "Eärendur (Lord of Andúnië)"), the fifteenth Lord of that line).

Lindórië was born during the dark times of Númenor when the Númenorians, led by their [Kings](/wiki/Kings_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Kings of Númenor") were turning against the [Valar](/wiki/Valar). However she belonged to the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) and instilled her beliefs in her daughter [Inzilbêth](/wiki/Inzilb%C3%AAth), who passed these beliefs on to her elder son [Inziladûn](/wiki/Inzilad%C3%BBn). Due to this upbringing, when Lindórië's grandson took the [Sceptre of Númenor](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor), he adopted the name [Tar-Palantir](/wiki/Tar-Palantir) in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and attempted to reverse the influences of the [King's Men](/wiki/King%27s_Men).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lindórië* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but [Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) suggests that it means "She that arises in beauty" (compare with *[Melkor](/wiki/Melkor)*, which means "He that arises in Might") and consists of the compounds *linda* ("fair", "beautiful") and a feminine form of the ending in *Melkor*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> According to Paul Strack the meaning of the name is unclear, but he suggests that its initial element might be derived from *[lind](/wiki/Lind "Lind")ë* ("singing", "song", "musical sound").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
