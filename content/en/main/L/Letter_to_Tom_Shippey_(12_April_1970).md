On c. [12 April](/wiki/12_April) [1970](/wiki/1970), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey)**. Shippey received the letter on [13 April](/wiki/13_April) [1970](/wiki/1970).<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Guide_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guide-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Comments on Shippey's paper "The Author as Philologist" (sent by Shippey to Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Quotes from the letter were published in *[The Road to Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Road_to_Middle-earth)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Shippey also briefly describes the letter in "Long Evolution: *The History of Middle-earth* and Its Merits" (in [*Arda*](</wiki/Arda_(annual)> "Arda (annual)") 1987, Vol. VII, p. 29)<sup id="cite_ref-Guide_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guide-2">[2]</a></sup> and in *[Roots and Branches](/wiki/Roots_and_Branches)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
