**Sir Lenworth George Henry**, [CBE](/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire "Order of the British Empire"), known as **Lenny Henry**, is an English actor, comedian, singer, television presenter and writer. He has portrayed [Sadoc Burrows](/wiki/Sadoc_Burrows) in Amazon's *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* series.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
