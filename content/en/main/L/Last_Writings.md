**Last Writings** is the thirteenth chapter of *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](../T/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md)*. It contains three manuscripts from the last year of Tolkien's life: "Glorfindel" (two separate texts and two brief notes), "The Five Wizards", and "Círdan".
