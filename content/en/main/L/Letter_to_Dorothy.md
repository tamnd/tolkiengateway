Some years before [1968](/wiki/1968), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) allegedly wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Dorothy**.

- **Authenticity:** "...most likely genuine. If it isn't, it is a particularly well-informed and elaborate hoax" - [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter).<sup id="cite_ref-Carl_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Carl-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Only one [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) sentence published in mailing lists.

## Comment and Excerpt {#Comment_and_Excerpt}

Dorothy had sent [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) a letter asking him how to say "Merry Christmas" in [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish); Tolkien replied that the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) did not celebrate [Christmas](/wiki/Christmas) but they had a generic wish: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mer-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mer- (page does not exist)">Meri[n]</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sa (page does not exist)">sa</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Harya-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Harya- (page does not exist)">haryalye</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Alasse&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alasse (page does not exist)">alasse</a></i> "I hope that you have happiness" (adding in a case of a mortal: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=N%C3%B3&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nó (page does not exist)">nó</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Vanya-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Vanya- (page does not exist)">vanyalye</a> <a href="/wiki/Ambar" title="Ambar">Ambarello</a></i> "before you pass from the world").

## Legacy {#Legacy}

In a letter dated [October 14](/wiki/14_October "14 October") [1968](/wiki/1968) Dorothy mentioned Tolkien's letter and the Elvish phrase to a Ms Dawson with whom they shared love for Tolkien's work. In her letter, she transcribed the first word as *Meriu*.

On [April 1](/wiki/1_April "1 April") [1999](/wiki/1999), Dawson's son, Michael sent a mail to the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tolkien_Mailing_List&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien Mailing List (page does not exist)">Tolkien Mailing List</a> inquiring the identity of his mother's friend. He asked whether such a letter by Tolkien has been published in [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter)'s "*[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)*" so that he would identify and trace that Dorothy. Nobody could help Michael Dawson identify Dorothy, since the letter had never been published.

### Significance and authenticity {#Significance_and_authenticity}

Linguists of [Tolklang](/wiki/Tolklang) and [Elfling](/wiki/Elfling) considered the Elvish sentence another piece of the Quenya puzzle; most importantly, the previously unknown words *sa* "that" (introducing a secondary sentence) and *nó* "before". They also corrected the first word *meriu* as a misread *merin*: u and n tended to look identical in Tolkien's handwriting,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the word was supposed to be a verb in [1st person singular](/wiki/Quenya#Pronouns "Quenya").

The Quenya sentence, known as "Merin Sentence", has been critically analyzed in publications such as [*Tyalië Tyelelliéva* 14](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va_14 "Tyalië Tyelelliéva 14") by [Lisa Star](/wiki/Lisa_Star).

Some of the oddities concerning the phrase were that Michael Dawson sent his mail on April 1 (<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fool%27s_Day" title="wikipedia:Fool&#39;s Day">Fool&#39;s Day</a>), and afterwards nobody could contact him.<sup id="cite_ref-Helge_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helge-3">[3]</a></sup> [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) and [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) (who was forwarded Dawson's mail by Hostetter) said that they had not encountered these 'new' words as such in any of Tolkien's unpublished papers.<sup id="cite_ref-Carl_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Carl-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is of note that the vocabulary of the sentence fits *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)* suggesting that the letter to Dorothy was not written later than *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Helge_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helge-3">[3]</a></sup> In later publications, the word *sa* has been seen again in a text as "that"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> but the word *nó* was given as "but".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
