On [29 January](../_/29_January.md) [1954](../_/1954.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote <b><a href="Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien">a letter</a> to the Rector of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Li%C3%A8ge" title="wikipedia:University of Liège">University of Liège</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:**The letter concerns the festivities of Tolkien's reception of an honorary doctorate at the University of Liège on [October 2nd](../_/2_October.md), [1954](../_/1954.md) (he was suggested as a candidate by [Simonne d'Ardenne](../S/Simonne_d'Ardenne.md)).
- **Publication:** The letter was reprinted in full in the article "Tolkien and Belgium" by [Johan Vanhecke](../J/Johan_Vanhecke.md), published in *[Lembas Extra: Proceedings of the 5th Unquendor Lustrum](Lembas_Extra%253A_Proceedings_of_the_5th_Unquendor_Lustrum.md)*.
- **Archive**: The letter is kept at the University Archives in Liège.

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I look forward with great pleasure to the opportunity of again visiting the renowned University of Liège, for which I entertain feelings of affection and the highest regard.
</p>
