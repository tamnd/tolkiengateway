On [8 March](/wiki/8_March) [1956](/wiki/1956), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to William Galbraith**.<sup id="cite_ref-hammond_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hammond-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Description:** 5 pages.<sup id="cite_ref-Marion_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marion-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Archive:** [Marion E. Wade Center](/wiki/Marion_E._Wade_Center).<sup id="cite_ref-Marion_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marion-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Having received a letter from William Galbraith dated <a href="/wiki/7_March" title="7 March">7 March</a> with questions about <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i>, Tolkien immediately writes a reply, but mislays it and does not send it until <a href="/wiki/13_April" title="13 April">13 April</a>. He hopes that it may be possible to publish the index he had begun, as well as other things omitted from <i>The Lord of the Rings</i> for reasons of space and cost. All that now impedes the publication of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Silmarillion" title="The Silmarillion">The Silmarillion</a></i> is his finding time to prepare it.
</p>
