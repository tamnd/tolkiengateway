On [30 April](../_/30_April.md) [1954](../_/1954.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_%2522The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%2522.md) to [Stanley Unwin](../S/Stanley_Unwin.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Hughes_(writer)" title="wikipedia:Richard Hughes (writer)">Richard Hughes</a>'s opinion of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.
- **Publication:** Quotes from the letter appears in *[Sotheby's Nineteenth Century and Modern First Editions, Presentation Copies, Autograph Letters and Literary Manuscripts](../S/Sotheby's_Nineteenth_Century_and_Modern_First_Editions,_Presentation_Copies,_Autograph_Letters_and_Literary_Manuscripts.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Transcription {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Dear Unwin,<br/><br/>It was most kind of you to send me a copy of Richard Hughes&#39; remarks. It has done much to lighten the despondency into which I have fallen. 1953 and 54 have been specially unkind to me in providing every kind of obstacle to my giving proper attention to the <i>Lord of the Rings</i>.<br/><br/>It should be an honour and advantage to be reviewed by Hughes, and I hope he will manage it.<br/><br/>With best wishes,<br/><br/>Yours sincerely<br/><br/>Ronald Tolkien.
</p>
</blockquote>
