<i><b>lalaith</b></i> is a noun meaning "laughter" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is possible that this word shows influence from *[lala](Lala.md)-* in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), such as, in the name *Lalwendë* or, more commonly, *Lalwen* (laughing maiden).<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:343</sup>

In Common Eldarin the stem for 'laugh' is *g*-*lada*-, in Quenya *lala*-, in Telerin *glada*, and in Sindarin *glað*.<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:359, Note 29</sup>
