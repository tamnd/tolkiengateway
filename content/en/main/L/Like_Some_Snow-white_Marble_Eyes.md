<i><b>Like Some Snow-white Marble Eyes</b></i> is the fourth track of the album [Stronghold](/wiki/Stronghold) by the band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning). The lyrics are influenced by the Robert Frost poem *Stars*. but are also influenced by captivity of [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) at the hands of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>How countless they congregate<br/>
O&#39;er our tumultuous snow<br/>
Which flows in shapes as tall as trees<br/>
When wintry winds do blow<br/>
<br/>
As if with keenness for our fate<br/>
Our faltering few steps on<br/>
To white rest, and a place of rest<br/>
Invisible at dawn<br/>
<br/>
And yet with neither love nor hate<br/>
Those stars like some snow-white<br/>
Morgoth snow-white marble eyes<br/>
Without the gift of sight<br/>
<br/>
Upon this star I fixed my eye<br/>
All over the wide land<br/>
My horse moved on, hoof after hoof<br/>
He raised and never stopped<br/>
When down behind the cottage roof<br/>
At once the planet dropped
</p>
</div>
