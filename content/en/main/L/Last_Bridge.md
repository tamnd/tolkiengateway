The **Last Bridge**,<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-3">[3]</a></sup> also known as the **Bridge of Mitheithel**<sup id="cite_ref-Mitheithel_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mitheithel-2">[2]</a></sup> or the **Bridge of Hoarwell**,<sup id="cite_ref-chronology_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology-1">[1]</a></sup> was an ancient stone bridge<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-4">[4]</a></sup> that carried the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) over the River [Hoarwell](/wiki/Hoarwell) (*Mitheithel*), about a hundred miles east of [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop) and roughly the same distance west of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-FRmap_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRmap-5">[5]</a></sup> Its three great arches crossed the river at the bottom of a short steep slope<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-6">[6]</a></sup> and to the east the land rose again into the wooded hills of the [Trollshaws](/wiki/Trollshaws).<sup id="cite_ref-FRmap_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRmap-5">[5]</a></sup> It was the only practicable crossing point south of the river's sources in the [Ettenmoors](/wiki/Ettenmoors).<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

How ancient the bridge was is unknown. It may have been as old as the East-West Road, which possibly dated to the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) or or it may have been built or improved by the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-UTNote6_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote6-7">[7]</a></sup> In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the bridge was within the bounds of the realm of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor),<sup id="cite_ref-FRmap_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRmap-5">[5]</a></sup> and then on the border of two of its three successor realms, [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan) and [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and probably these two realms kept up the maintenance of this strategic structure.

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) passed over the bridge on their way to the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain). After the crossing one of the ponies bolted back into the river and its load of food was lost. This loss of provisions and the dwarves' inability to ignite a fire in the wind and rain led to the encounter of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and his companions with the unfriendly trio of trolls.<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-4">[4]</a></sup>

On [11 October](/wiki/11_October) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> three [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") were guarding the bridge against [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and the hobbits on their journey to Rivendell. Their plan was foiled by [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel), who drove them away and left his token,<sup id="cite_ref-Mitheithel_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mitheithel-2">[2]</a></sup> a green beryl, on the Bridge. Two days later,<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Aragorn found the beryl, took it as a sign that the bridge was safe, and led the hobbits over it.<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the 1960 abandoned rewrite of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit),* the Last Bridge was more prominent in the story. When the Company came to the bridge they found its single arch broken ([J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) had forgotten in this draft that the bridge had three arches in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* - see above). The company had to cross the dangerous ford instead. [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) (not yet vanished as in the published story) tried the crossing first on his horse [Rohald](/wiki/Rohald). His success led to the dwarves crossing, followed by Bilbo, although one of the pack-ponies still bolted back into the river.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

The 1960 rewrite ended in the next chapter just as the Company came to the edge of the valley of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell). In a note regarding contents yet to write it was stated "Ch. III should make clear [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond)'s care for roads etc. from Greyflood to &lt;Mountains&gt;".<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> This would explain how the Last Bridge got fixed before [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) arrived and found the structure intact.
