<i><b>lin</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word that was the meaning "pool, mere".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Root](/wiki/Root) [LINI](/wiki/LINI)
