**Letter 150**, published in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*, is actually an excerpt of several notes which [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) included in his letter to [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md), written on [9 September](../_/9_September.md) 1954, ([Letter 149](Letter_149.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Summary {#Summary}

Tolkien regretted that no copy for the Appendices had been sent. He was doing his best to produce them before the end of the month. His trouble was indecision and conflicting advice in selecting from too abundant matter, and spending too much time trying to satisfy an unfortunate promise made in Volume I of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup> His index had reached (in rough form) the middle of *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md)*. The "alphabets" would need blocks even in simplest form. A map of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) was most urgent and he hoped to get his son [Christopher](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) to produce one from his drafts.
