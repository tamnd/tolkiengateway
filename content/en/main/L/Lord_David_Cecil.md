**Lord Edward Christian David Gascoyne-Cecil** ([April 9](/wiki/9_April "9 April"), [1902](/wiki/1902) – [January 1](/wiki/1_January "1 January"), [1986](/wiki/1986)), was an English aristocrat, literary scholar, biographer and academic. He was also a fellow [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). His title was a courtesy title: he was a younger son of the 4th Marquess of Salisbury.
