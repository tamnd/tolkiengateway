**Lothlann**, a "wide and empty" plain,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> lay to the east of [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen) and north of [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand), beyond the [March of Maedhros](/wiki/March_of_Maedhros) and [Maglor's Gap](/wiki/Maglor%27s_Gap).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) the riders of the people of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) were overwhelmed in Lothlann when the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) came and passed through [Maglor's Gap](/wiki/Maglor%27s_Gap). Many of [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor)'s cavalry were burnt alive on the plain as [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) set fire to it.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

As with most of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), it was drowned by the [Great Sea](/wiki/Great_Sea) at the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "empty and wide". In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)* the name is identified as [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) and spelled *Lhothlann* according to the earlier conception, with the "Lh" later being dropped in favour of "L" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It is derived from the Noldorin elements *lhost* "empty" and *lhand/lhann* \*"wide".

In that document the name appears both as *Lhothland* and *Lhothlann*, but under the entry LUS it also appears in its later form <i><b>L</b>othlann</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early drafts of *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*, the name Lothlann, also Lothland desert or [Desert of Lostladen](/wiki/Desert_of_Lostladen),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> was to be a region south of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
