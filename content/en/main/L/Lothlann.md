**Lothlann**, a "wide and empty" plain,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> lay to the east of [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md) and north of [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md), beyond the [March of Maedhros](../M/March_of_Maedhros.md) and [Maglor's Gap](../M/Maglor's_Gap.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) the riders of the people of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) were overwhelmed in Lothlann when the [dragon](../D/Dragons.md) [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) came and passed through [Maglor's Gap](../M/Maglor's_Gap.md). Many of [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md)'s cavalry were burnt alive on the plain as [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) set fire to it.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

As with most of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), it was drowned by the [Great Sea](../G/Great_Sea.md) at the end of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "empty and wide". In *[The Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* the name is identified as [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) and spelled *Lhothlann* according to the earlier conception, with the "Lh" later being dropped in favour of "L" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It is derived from the Noldorin elements *lhost* "empty" and *lhand/lhann* \*"wide".

In that document the name appears both as *Lhothland* and *Lhothlann*, but under the entry LUS it also appears in its later form <i><b>L</b>othlann</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early drafts of *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)*, the name Lothlann, also Lothland desert or [Desert of Lostladen](../D/Desert_of_Lostladen.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> was to be a region south of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
