On [17 November](/wiki/17_November) [1954](/wiki/1954), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote <b><a href="/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien" title="Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien">a letter</a> to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayner_Heppenstall" title="wikipedia:Rayner Heppenstall">Rayner Heppenstall</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Tolkien will not be able to attend the rehearsal and recording of the [radio play *The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth*](</wiki/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth,_Beorhthelm%27s_Son_(radio_play)> "The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth, Beorhthelm's Son (radio play)") on [3 December](/wiki/3_December). He wonders if it could be of any help if sent some notes on the text. Instead of the current conclusion (the *Dirige*), Tolkien sends the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dies_Irae" title="wikipedia:Dies Irae">Dies Irae</a></i>. Tolkien would like his copy of *[Essays and Studies](</wiki/Essays_and_Studies_(1953)> "Essays and Studies (1953)")* to be returned (since it is his only copy), and would be thankful for receiving a copy of the script for the radio play.
- **Archive:** [BBC](/wiki/BBC) Written Archives Centre.

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>[Tolkien remains] rather in the dark as to how you propose to treat the thing, and what part — if any; but some hint of &#34;background&#34; seems necessary — of the historical notes [included with the verse-drama in <i>Essays and Studies</i>] is to be included (as preface)<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>
