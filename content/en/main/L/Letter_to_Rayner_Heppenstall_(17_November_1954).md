On [17 November](../_/17_November.md) [1954](../_/1954.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote <b><a href="Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien">a letter</a> to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayner_Heppenstall" title="wikipedia:Rayner Heppenstall">Rayner Heppenstall</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Tolkien will not be able to attend the rehearsal and recording of the [radio play *The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth*](<../T/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth,_Beorhthelm's_Son_(radio_play).md>) on [3 December](../_/3_December.md). He wonders if it could be of any help if sent some notes on the text. Instead of the current conclusion (the *Dirige*), Tolkien sends the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dies_Irae" title="wikipedia:Dies Irae">Dies Irae</a></i>. Tolkien would like his copy of *[Essays and Studies](<../E/Essays_and_Studies_(1953).md>)* to be returned (since it is his only copy), and would be thankful for receiving a copy of the script for the radio play.
- **Archive:** [BBC](../B/BBC.md) Written Archives Centre.

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>[Tolkien remains] rather in the dark as to how you propose to treat the thing, and what part — if any; but some hint of &#34;background&#34; seems necessary — of the historical notes [included with the verse-drama in <i>Essays and Studies</i>] is to be included (as preface)<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>
