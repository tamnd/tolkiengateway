**Lithe** was the sixth month of the calendar of [Bree](../B/Bree.md), corresponding to [Forelithe](../F/Forelithe.md) of the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md), and approximately corresponding to modern [22 May](../_/22_May.md) through [20 June](../_/20_June.md) in a normal year. It was followed by the [Summerdays](../S/Summerdays.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Old English](../O/Old_English.md) <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/li%C3%BEa" title="wiktionary:liþa">líða</a></i>, is the old name for the months June and July.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup> It probably referred to the first new moon around the summer solstice.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
