On [30 August](/wiki/30_August) [1964](/wiki/1964), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [L. Sprague de Camp](/wiki/L._Sprague_de_Camp)**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien writes about his opinion of the contributions to the anthology *[Swords &amp; Sorcery](/wiki/Swords_%26_Sorcery)*.
- **Publication:** Quotes from the letter were published in [*Mythlore* 50](/wiki/Mythlore_50 "Mythlore 50").

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>all the items seem poor in the subsidiary (but to me not unimportant) matters of nomenclature. Best when inventive, least good when literary or archaic
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>J.R.R. Tolkien<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>
