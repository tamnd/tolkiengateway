On [30 August](../_/30_August.md) [1964](../_/1964.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [L. Sprague de Camp](L._Sprague_de_Camp.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien writes about his opinion of the contributions to the anthology *[Swords &amp; Sorcery](../S/Swords_&_Sorcery.md)*.
- **Publication:** Quotes from the letter were published in [*Mythlore* 50](../M/Mythlore_50.md).

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>all the items seem poor in the subsidiary (but to me not unimportant) matters of nomenclature. Best when inventive, least good when literary or archaic
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>J.R.R. Tolkien<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>
