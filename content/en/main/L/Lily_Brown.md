**Lily Cotton (née Brown)** was the wife of [Farmer Cotton](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior").

## History {#History}

Lily married Tolman Cotton of Bywater. Together, they had five children: [Young Tom](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Junior "Tolman Cotton Junior"), [Rose](/wiki/Rose_Cotton "Rose Cotton"), [Jolly](/wiki/Wilcome_Cotton_II "Wilcome Cotton II"), [Nick](/wiki/Bowman_Cotton "Bowman Cotton") and [Nibs](/wiki/Carl_Cotton "Carl Cotton").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Like many female hobbits,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Lily was named after a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilium" title="wikipedia:Lilium">flower</a>.
