**Lily Cotton (née Brown)** was the wife of [Farmer Cotton](../T/Tolman_Cotton_Senior.md).

## History {#History}

Lily married Tolman Cotton of Bywater. Together, they had five children: [Young Tom](../T/Tolman_Cotton_Junior.md), [Rose](../R/Rose_Cotton.md), [Jolly](../W/Wilcome_Cotton_II.md), [Nick](../B/Bowman_Cotton.md) and [Nibs](../C/Carl_Cotton.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Like many female hobbits,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Lily was named after a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilium" title="wikipedia:Lilium">flower</a>.
