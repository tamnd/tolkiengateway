**Lembi** is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "Lingerers". Early in Tolkien's writings, it was used as a collective term for the [Avari](/wiki/Avari), Elves who stayed in Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is likely derived from the earlier Quenya adjective <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lemba&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lemba (page does not exist)">lemba</a></i>, which means "left behind".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
