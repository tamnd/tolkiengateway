<i><b>Lembas Extra 2019: The World Tolkien Built</b></i> is an issue of the journal *[Lembas Extra](<Lembas_(journal).md>)*. The issue contains eight articles, the proceedings of the conference held at the University of Leiden.

## Contents {#Contents}

- "Introduction" by [Renée Vink](../R/Renée_Vink.md) (pp. 5–6)
- Thijs Porck: "Reshaping the Germanic Economy of Honour: Gift Giving in J.R.R. Tolkien's *The Lord of the Rings*" (pp. 7–26)
- Chiara Marchetti: "Tolkien's Medieval Monsters: Grendel, Gollum and Boromir" (pp. 27–47)
- Berber Bossenbroek: "Ides Ælfscinu, Lúthien, and Judith: Towards a New Anglo-Saxon Analogue of J.R.R. Tolkien's Fiction" (pp. 49–57)
- Lettie Dorst: "Fair, Fierce, Feigned: Gender Representations in *The Lord of the Rings*" (pp. 59–78)
- Renée Vink: "(Un)happily Ever After: The Many Woes of Tolkien's Lovers" (pp. 79–92)
- Jan van Breda: "*The Statute of Finwë and Míriel*: Justice, Pity and the Irrevocable Free Will of the Dead" (pp. 93–110)
- Hamish Williams: "Tolkien's Trolls: Intertextuality in 'Roast Mutton' and Monstrous Incarnations after *The Hobbit*" (pp. 111–27)
- [Łukasz Neubauer](../_/Łukasz_Neubauer.md): "'This local family game played in the country just round us': Some Remarks Concerning the Genesis of *Farmer Giles of Ham*" (pp. 129–46)
- "Biographies" (pp. 147–9)
