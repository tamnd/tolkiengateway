**Lewis MacLeod** is a Scottish actor. In the realm of voice acting, he is best known as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebulba" title="wikipedia:Sebulba">Sebulba</a> in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars_Episode_I:_The_Phantom_Menace" title="wikipedia:Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace">The Phantom Menace</a></i> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postman_Pat" title="wikipedia:Postman Pat">Postman Pat</a> in the eponymous show.

## Roles {#Roles}

- [2006](/wiki/2006): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II)*: Hadhod, Dwarf Sage, Additional voices
- [2004](/wiki/2004): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Third_Age)*: Hadhod, [Sharku](/wiki/Sharku)
