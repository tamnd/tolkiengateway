<b><i>Locus</i> 149</b> is an issue of the American science fiction and fantasy magazine <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locus_(magazine)" title="wikipedia:Locus (magazine)">Locus</a></i>. Issue #149 includes: [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) obituary; Tolkien tributes by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_del_Rey" title="wikipedia:Lester del Rey">Lester del Rey</a>, [L. Sprague de Camp](/wiki/L._Sprague_de_Camp), <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_E._Lanier" title="wikipedia:Sterling E. Lanier">Sterling E. Lanier</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_Leiber" title="wikipedia:Fritz Leiber">Fritz Leiber</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederik_Pohl" title="wikipedia:Frederik Pohl">Frederik Pohl</a>, and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Zelazny" title="wikipedia:Roger Zelazny">Roger Zelazny</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

L. Sprague de Camp briefly describes his [meeting](/wiki/Index:Reminiscences_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Reminiscences of J.R.R. Tolkien") with Tolkien, and Sterling Lanier mentions his correspondence with Tolkien (consisting of a "dozen or so" [letters](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"")).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
