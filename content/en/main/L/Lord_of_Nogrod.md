**Lord of Nogrod** was a title given to the one who ruled over the [Dwarven](../D/Dwarf.md) city of [Nogrod](../D/Dwarf.md).

Though there were presumably many Lords through the history of that [Age](../F/First_Age.md), only one appears in records, and even that Lord is not named. He led an [attack](../B/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves.md) against [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md) to capture the [Nauglamir](../N/Nauglamir.md). Later he fought with [Beren](../B/Beren.md) in the [Battle of Sarn Athrad](../B/Battle_of_Sarn_Athrad.md) and would have slain him, but stumbled and Beren slew him to reclaim the Nauglamir. With his dying words, he cursed the treasure that he was bringing back to Nogrod, which Beren threw into the river.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, [Naugladur](../N/Naugladur.md) was the king of the [Dwarves of Nogrod](../D/Dwarves_of_Nogrod.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> who fought a [battle in the Thousand Caves](../B/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves.md) against [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) and fought with [Beren](../B/Beren.md) in the [Battle of Sarn Athrad](../B/Battle_of_Sarn_Athrad.md).
