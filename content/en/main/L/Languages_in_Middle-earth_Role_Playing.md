To flesh out the setting, [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing) introduces many new languages.

## Apysaic {#Apysaic}

The Apysaic languages were spoken by [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim), and dominate south-west [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). It was originally the language of the Apysan peoples, who migrated through Ered Harmal in two waves. The first wave settled in Hyarn, Elorna, Mirëdor, Drel, and Pel. A second wave of settlers 1500 years late took control of Far Harad, and their Haradaic-influenced speech is named *New* or *Northern Apysaic*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rulebook_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rulebook-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Tazinain {#Tazinain}

A language spoken by city dwellers of [Sîrayn](/wiki/S%C3%AErayn), based on Apysaic and Talatherin.<sup id="cite_ref-greaterharad_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greaterharad-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Atliduk {#Atliduk}

Atliduk was a language spoken by [Beornings](/wiki/Beornings).<sup id="cite_ref-rulebook_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rulebook-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Blarm {#Blarm}

Blarm was the language of the [Hillmen](/wiki/Hillmen). It was represented by [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Fornen {#Fornen}

Fornen was a language of the Northmen spoken before the 1800s of the Third Age.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Haradaic {#Haradaic}

Haradaic was a language spoken by Haradrim, [Corsairs](/wiki/Corsairs), and [Black Númenóreans](/wiki/Black_N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans), though [Variags](/wiki/Variags) also had some knowledge of it.<sup id="cite_ref-rulebook_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rulebook-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Labba {#Labba}

Labba was a language spoken by the [Lossoth](/wiki/Lossoth), though the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Umli&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Umli (page does not exist)">Umli</a> also had some knowledge of it.<sup id="cite_ref-rulebook_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rulebook-2">[2]</a></sup> It was influenced by Lossidilrin, the language of the Snow-elves.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup> rather than being related to other Mannish tongues.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

## Logathig {#Logathig}

Logathig was a language spoken by [Dorwinrim](/wiki/Dorwinion "Dorwinion") and [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings).<sup id="cite_ref-rulebook_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rulebook-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Nahaiduk {#Nahaiduk}

Logathig was a language spoken by Beornings and [Woodmen](/wiki/Woodmen).<sup id="cite_ref-rulebook_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rulebook-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Talatherin {#Talatherin}

Talatherin was the language of the ancient Chyans.<sup id="cite_ref-greaterharad_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greaterharad-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Umitic {#Umitic}

Umitic was a language spoken by the Umli.<sup id="cite_ref-rulebook_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rulebook-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Varadja {#Varadja}

Varadja was a language spoken by the Variags.<sup id="cite_ref-rulebook_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rulebook-2">[2]</a></sup>
