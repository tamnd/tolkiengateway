**Lord of the Rings: Friends &amp; Foes** is the first expansion to the [Lord of the Rings](<Lord_of_the_Rings_(board_game).md>) board game.

It introduced two new locations, [Bree](../B/Bree.md) and [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md), and a new deck of foe cards, such as [Barrow-wights](../B/Barrow-wights.md) and [Wargs](../W/Wargs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

A small package of three new cards expanding *Friends &amp; Foes*, titled *The Black Gate*, was given away at conventions. Two of them were later included in the *Sauron* expansion, while one remains exclusive.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
