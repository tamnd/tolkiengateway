**Liv Rundgren Tyler** (born 1977) played [Arwen Evenstar](/wiki/Arwen "Arwen") in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") trilogy. She also recorded a song, which is featured in the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing) scene in [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)").
