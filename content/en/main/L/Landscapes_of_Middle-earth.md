<i><b>Landscapes of Middle-earth</b></i>, also published as **Songs Of Middle Earth: A Tribute To Lord Of The Rings**, is a [2001](/wiki/2001) album by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rick_Wakeman&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rick Wakeman (page does not exist)">Rick Wakeman</a>, former member of progressive rock band <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yes_(band)" title="wikipedia:Yes (band)">Yes</a>. It contains tracks selected and retitled from his earlier albums *The Seven Wonders of the World* and *Heritage*.

A CD of this album was included with the DVD release of *[Master of the Rings](/wiki/Master_of_the_Rings:_The_Unauthorized_Story_Behind_J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_%27Lord_of_the_Rings%27 "Master of the Rings: The Unauthorized Story Behind J.R.R. Tolkien's 'Lord of the Rings'")*.
