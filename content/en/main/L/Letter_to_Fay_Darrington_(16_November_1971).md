On [16 November](/wiki/16_November) [1971](/wiki/1971), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Miss Fay Darrington**.<sup id="cite_ref-Deweatts_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deweatts-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Publication:** None.
- **Description:** One page, printed heading, handwritten signed letter.

## From the auction {#From_the_auction}

Autograph Note signed to Fay Darrington,1p., 8vo, n.p, 16th November, 1971, thanking her for her letter, returning two books signed and sending her a copy of the new Hobbit map poster.
