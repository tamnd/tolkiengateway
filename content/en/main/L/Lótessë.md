**Lótessë** was the name for the month of the year (*[asta](/wiki/Asta "Asta")*) that followed the month [Víressë](/wiki/V%C3%ADress%C3%AB) and preceded the month [Nárië](/wiki/N%C3%A1ri%C3%AB). It was the fifth month in the [King's Reckoning](/wiki/King%27s_Reckoning) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning). After the introduction of [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning), the year started with Víressë so Lótessë became the second month.<sup id="cite_ref-app_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-app-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Lótessë* in the Kings' Reckoning (and for the most part in the Stewards' Reckoning) corresponded to [Thrimidge](/wiki/Thrimidge) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) (approximately modern [22 April](/wiki/22_April) through [21 May](/wiki/21_May)).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word includes the word *[lótë](/wiki/L%C3%B3t%C3%AB "Lótë")*, "blossom". Lótessë was the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name inherited from [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) and traditionally used by the [Westron](/wiki/Westron)-speaking peoples. The [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) themselves however used the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name of the month, [Lothron](/wiki/Lothron).<sup id="cite_ref-app_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-app-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

As the Númenorean calendars seem to be based on the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Republican_Calendar" title="wikipedia:French Republican Calendar">French Republican Calendar</a>, the name and meaning of the month corresponds to the month <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flor%C3%A9al" title="wikipedia:Floréal">Floréal</a>, meaning "flowery".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
