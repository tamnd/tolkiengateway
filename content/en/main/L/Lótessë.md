**Lótessë** was the name for the month of the year (*[asta](../A/Asta.md)*) that followed the month [Víressë](../V/Víressë.md) and preceded the month [Nárië](../N/Nárië.md). It was the fifth month in the [King's Reckoning](../K/King's_Reckoning.md) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](../S/Stewards'_Reckoning.md). After the introduction of [New Reckoning](../N/New_Reckoning.md), the year started with Víressë so Lótessë became the second month.<sup id="cite_ref-app_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-app-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Lótessë* in the Kings' Reckoning (and for the most part in the Stewards' Reckoning) corresponded to [Thrimidge](../T/Thrimidge.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) (approximately modern [22 April](../_/22_April.md) through [21 May](../_/21_May.md)).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word includes the word *[lótë](Lótë.md)*, "blossom". Lótessë was the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name inherited from [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) and traditionally used by the [Westron](../W/Westron.md)-speaking peoples. The [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) themselves however used the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name of the month, [Lothron](Lothron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-app_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-app-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

As the Númenorean calendars seem to be based on the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Republican_Calendar" title="wikipedia:French Republican Calendar">French Republican Calendar</a>, the name and meaning of the month corresponds to the month <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flor%C3%A9al" title="wikipedia:Floréal">Floréal</a>, meaning "flowery".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
