On [26 June](../_/26_June.md) [1957](../_/1957.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Deirdre Levinson** (his former student, who at this time was in [South Africa](../O/Orange_Free_State.md)).

- **Publication:** A description of the letter was included in *[Michael Silverman Catalogue 23](../M/Michael_Silverman_Catalogue_23.md)* and in *Bonhams Books, Maps and Manuscripts 23 March 2004*.<sup id="cite_ref-Books_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Books-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Tolkien comments on conditions in South Africa, and gives Levinson news of various appointments in [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md), including [Nevill Coghill](../N/Nevill_Coghill.md) as the Merton Professor of English Literature, and [Elaine Griffiths](../E/Elaine_Griffiths.md) as a Lecturer. Tolkien was shocked by the death of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roy_Campbell_(poet)" title="wikipedia:Roy Campbell (poet)">Roy Campbell</a> but relieved by the general tone of his obituaries. He is sorry "*to be pinned down to beer, especially in quantity. On the whole it goes down better with tobacco than other drinks; but I have never been a gargantuan swiller of it*".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
