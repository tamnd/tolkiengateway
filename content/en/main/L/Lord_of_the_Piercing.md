**Lord of the Piercing** is a short 4 minute parody of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, created for the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_MTV_Movie_Awards" title="wikipedia:2002 MTV Movie Awards">2002 MTV Movie Awards</a>. It was broadcast [June 6](/wiki/6_June "6 June"), [2002](/wiki/2002).

The parody was also included as an easter egg on the American [Extended Edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)") release of *The Fellowship of the Ring*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Plot {#Plot}

The parody features Jack Black and Sarah Michelle Gellar at the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond). When asked to bring forth [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), Jack confesses that while drunk, he got it pierced to his private parts. He demonstrates this by pulling down his trousers. A discussion then ensues, after which Gellar's character rips off the Ring.
