**Lord of the Rings vol. 1** (French: *Seigneur de la Bague vol. 1*) is a video game written by Vector.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-介_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-介-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-3">[3]</a></sup> It's a mix of a puzzle-game and RPG.<sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-3">[3]</a></sup> The French version won the first prize for the computer game category in the The Mississippi Foreign Language Fair.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> It was later followed by <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lord_of_the_Rings_vol._2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lord of the Rings vol. 2 (page does not exist)">Lord of the Rings vol. 2</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup>
