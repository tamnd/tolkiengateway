**Livia Rusz** (also, Livia Rusz) is a Romanian and Hungarian graphic artist, best known for her work in illustration, comic strip and comic book genres.

Livia Rusz became the first Romanian illustrator of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s work, when she contributed drawings for the first Romanian-language edition of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* ([*O poveste cu un hobbit*](<../O/O_poveste_cu_un_hobbit_(1975).md>), 1975). While working on this project, she was reportedly unable to compare her renditions of [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md), [Elves](../E/Elves.md) or [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) with any previous portrayals by other artists, owing to the scarcity of foreign literature sources in Romania — her work therefore owed much to imagination.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
