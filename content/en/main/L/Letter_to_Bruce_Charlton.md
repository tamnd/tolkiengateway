On [14 June](/wiki/14_June) [1973](/wiki/1973), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **a <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procuration" title="wikipedia:Procuration">per procurationem</a></i> [letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Bruce Charlton**.<sup id="cite_ref-Notion_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Notion-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Dear Mr Charlton<br/><br/>Thank you very much for your kind letter. I am delighted that my books have given you pleasure.<br/><br/>THE SILMARILLION is far from complete and I have not looked at it for some considerable time. Therefore I regret that I cannot predict when it will see the light of day.<br/><br/>Yours sincerely, <br/><br/>pp. JRR Tolkien<br/><br/>[initials] MJH<sup id="cite_ref-Notion_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Notion-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>
