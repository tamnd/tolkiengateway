**Lenition** is a phonological and grammatical aspect of [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) which reflects the phonological mutations of the language in its evolution from [Old Sindarin](../O/Old_Sindarin.md).

## Historical background {#Historical_background}

In the phonological development of Sindarin, lenition refers (primarily) to the mutation of medial consonants, occurring usually between two vowels (VCV). It affected the Old Sindarin sounds **p, t, k, b, d, g, m** and **s**.

More specifically, the evolution of these sounds can be visualized in these examples:

<dd>OS *<i>kele<b>p</b>e</i> &gt; S <i><a href="../C/Celeb.md" title="Celeb">celeb</a></i> &#34;silver&#34;</dd>
<dt>t &gt; d</dt>
<dd>OS *<i>a<b>t</b>ar</i> &gt; S <i><a href="../A/Adar.md" title="Adar">adar</a></i> &#34;father&#34;</dd>
<dt>k &gt; g</dt>
<dd>OS <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Beleka&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Beleka (page does not exist)">bele<b>k</b>a</a></i> &gt; S <i><a href="../B/Beleg.md" title="Beleg">beleg</a></i> &#34;great&#34;</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>b &gt; v</dt>
<dd>OS *<i>ja<b>b</b>asse</i> &gt; S <i><a href="../I/Iavas.md" title="Iavas">Iavas</a></i> &#34;autumn&#34;<sup id="cite_ref-Q_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Q-1">[note 1]</a></sup></dd>
<dt>d &gt; dh</dt>
<dd>OS *<i>e<b>d</b>elo</i> &gt; S <i><a href="../E/Edhel.md" title="Edhel">Edhel</a></i> &#34;Elf&#34;</dd>
<dt>g &gt; ∅</dt>
<dd>OS *<i>khu<b>g</b>o</i> &gt; *<i>huȝo</i><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 2]</a></sup> &gt; S <i><a href="../H/Hû.md" title="Hû">hû</a></i> &#34;dog&#34;<sup id="cite_ref-Q_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Q-1">[note 1]</a></sup></dd></dl>

## In compounds {#In_compounds}

As a consequence, this process took place also in Old Sindarin compounds, when the initial letter of a word was no longer initial in a compound. Even in later compounds that would be pure Sindarin, the lenition was solidified as a rule, envisioning retroactively the lenition process from a theoretical Old Sindarin compound.

For example the word *[athrabeth](../A/Athrabeth.md)* "debate, converse (lit. 'across-word')" can be analyzed both as a derivative of OS word \*<i>athra<b>p</b>ettha</i> but also as a compound of Sindarin words <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Athra-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Athra- (page does not exist)">athra-</a></i> "across" + [peth](../P/Peth.md) *"word".*

The fact that the above sounds mutated when medially was solidified as a rule

- <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Thind&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Thind (page does not exist)">thind</a> + <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Coll&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Coll (page does not exist)"><b>c</b>oll</a> &gt; [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) (&lt; ?OS \*<i>Thinda<b>k</b>ollo</i>)
- <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Cair&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cair (page does not exist)">cair</a> + <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=T%C3%A2n&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tân (page does not exist)"><b>t</b>ân</a> &gt; [Cirdan](../C/Cirdan.md) (&lt; ?OS \*<i>Kiria<b>t</b>âno</i>)
- <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Fanui&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fanui (page does not exist)">fanui</a> + [**d**ol](../D/Dol.md) &gt; [Fanuidhol](../F/Fanuidhol.md) (&lt; ?OS \*<i>Fanoi<b>d</b>olo</i>)
- [Edhel](../E/Edhel.md) + [**s**arn](../S/Sarn.md) &gt; [Edhelharn](../E/Edhelharn.md) (&lt; ?OS \*<i>Edelo<b>s</b>arno</i>)
- [har-](../H/Harad.md) + [**G**ondor](../G/Gondor.md) &gt; [Harondor](../H/Harondor.md) (&lt; ?OS \*<i>Khara<b>g</b>onddor</i>)
- [ar-](../A/Ar.md) + [**g**wend](../G/Gwend.md) &gt; [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) (&lt; ?OS \**Arwende*; no *g* would evolve between *ar-* and *wende*)
- <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Or-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Or- (page does not exist)">or-</a> + <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hall&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hall (page does not exist)"><b>h</b>all</a> &gt; [orchal](../O/Orchal.md) (&lt; ?OS \**orkhalna*; *kh* would not lenite as *h* inside a word)

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Sindarin, being inspired from the Welsh language, also borrowed heavily from its <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welsh_morphology#Initial_consonant_mutation" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">mutation system</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[1]</a></sup> The <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenition#Grammatical_lenition" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">grammatical lenition</a> is generally prominent in the [Celtic](../C/Celtic.md) languages.
