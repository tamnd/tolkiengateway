On [11 December](/wiki/11_December) [1971](/wiki/1971), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [George and Moira Sayer](/wiki/George_Sayer "George Sayer")**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Brief reply to letter of condolence, on the occasion of [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien")'s death.
- **Publication:** Excerpts from the letter appeared in *[Christie's 20th Century Books and Manuscripts 16 November 2001](/wiki/Christie%27s_20th_Century_Books_and_Manuscripts_16_November_2001)*. A description of the letter appears in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: I. Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*.
