On [7 July](/wiki/7_July) [1960](/wiki/1960), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [H.W. Donner](/wiki/H.W._Donner)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Abo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Abo-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

- **Subject(s):** Tolkien disliked the cover illustration of the Swedish translation of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* (*[Sagan om ringen](/wiki/H%C3%A4rskarringen "Härskarringen")*, 1st Swedish edition [1959](/wiki/1959)).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** None. The letter is kept in the collections of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%85bo_Akademi_University" title="wikipedia:Åbo Akademi University">Åbo Akademi University</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Abo_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Abo-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Comments:** The cover was illustrated by Rolf Lagerson.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
