On [8 February](/wiki/8_February) [1967](/wiki/1967), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to David L. Sands**.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Description:** Handwritten letter.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Subject:** Tolkien is expressing approval for a proposed Spanish language translation of his works.
- **Publication:** None.
- **Archive:** A Photocopy of original typewritten letter, donated by [Eduardo Segura](/wiki/Eduardo_Segura), is kept at [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University).<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>
