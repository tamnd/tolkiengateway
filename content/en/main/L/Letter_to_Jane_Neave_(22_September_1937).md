On [22 September](../_/22_September.md) [1937](../_/1937.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Jane Neave](../J/Jane_Neave.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien intends to send her a copy of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*. Personal greetings.
- **Publication:** The letter first appeared in an episode of the [BBC](../B/BBC.md)'s <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Antiques_Roadshow&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Antiques Roadshow (page does not exist)">Antiques Roadshow</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Antiques_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Antiques-2">[2]</a></sup> The opening was reproduced as a picture in *[Sotheby's English Literature, History, Fine Bindings, Private Press Books, Children's Books, Illustrated Books and Drawings 10 July 2003](../S/Sotheby's_English_Literature,_History,_Fine_Bindings,_Private_Press_Books,_Children's_Books,_Illustrated_Books_and_Drawings_10_July_2003.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> A short summary of the letter is included in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md)*.

## Transcription {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Sept 22. 1937<br/>
My dearest Jane
<br/>
Here&#39;s a copy of my little book which I send you with much love; I hope it will amuse you. It arrived yesterday, and is now at last &#39;published&#39;. We are <u>all</u> so much looking forward to your coming again this winter. It was not like Christmas without you. I have bought another second-hand car partly with money already brought in by &#34;The Hobbit&#34;. It is quieter, quicker, and very much steadier and less bumpy, so [?now] I think I shall come and fetch you this time, and bring you safe and unshaken direct from door to door.<br/>
I hope you are better,<br/>
Your loving 
Ronald.
</p>
</blockquote>

## History {#History}

Tolkien temporarily lost the letter and ended up sending his [aunt Jane](../J/Jane_Neave.md) another copy of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* with another letter dated [October 6](../O/October_6.md). He eventually found the original letter and kept it in his collection.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The letter was stuck to the front page of the book with tape and was passed on to [Simon Tolkien](../S/Simon_Tolkien.md) after Tolkien's death.<sup id="cite_ref-Antiques_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Antiques-2">[2]</a></sup> The book was featured in an episode of <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Antiques_Roadshow&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Antiques Roadshow (page does not exist)">Antiques Roadshow</a> aired in 1990 (series 12 or 13), brought by Simon's wife Tracy Steinberg.<sup id="cite_ref-Antiques_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Antiques-2">[2]</a></sup> The book was afterwards sold together with the letter in 2003.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
