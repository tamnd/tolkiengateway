On [4 January](/wiki/4_January) [1968](/wiki/1968), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Ken Jackson**.,<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TL_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL-2">[2]</a></sup> that was a biology master at New College School.

- **Subject:** In 1967, Humphrey Carpenter produced a [stage adaptation of *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_stage_adaptation)> "The Hobbit (1967 stage adaptation)"). Tolkien provided autographs for the actors but had neglected the technical crew. In response to this situation, Tolkien wrote a letter to the technical director enclosing "some stick-in autographs" and apologises to "the back-room boys of the drama" noting their "necessary, indeed very important, functions". He explains that his eldest son "used to perform the same functions at his school".<sup id="cite_ref-TL_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Descrition:** Handwritten, two pages, on blindstamped headed paper.<sup id="cite_ref-TL_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** The letter is described in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
