Written from [18 April](../_/18_April.md) to [6 May](../_/6_May.md) [1963](../_/1963.md) (suffering from symptoms of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibromyalgia" title="wikipedia:Fibromyalgia">fibrositis</a>, he could not finish the letter at once), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Mr and Mrs Kloos**. Anneke C. Kloos-Adriaansen and P. Kloos were <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropology" title="wikipedia:Anthropology">anthropologists</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Replying to their letter from [23 March](../_/23_March.md), Tolkien writes at length about "his sources and inspiration for *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*".<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Excerpts from the letter were published in *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_A_Reader's_Companion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Excerpts {#Excerpts}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>[The <a href="../G/Gerontius_Took.md" title="Gerontius Took">Old Took</a>] has part of his origin in the fact that both my grandfathers were longeval. My <a href="../J/John_Benjamin_Tolkien.md" title="John Benjamin Tolkien">father&#39;s father</a> was in his eleventh year when <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Waterloo" title="wikipedia:Battle of Waterloo">Waterloo</a> was fought; my <a href="../T/Tolkien_Family.md" title="Tolkien Family">mother&#39;s father</a>, a much younger man, was born before <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Victoria" title="wikipedia:Queen Victoria">Queen Victoria</a> came to the throne, and survived till his ninety-ninth year, missing his &#39;hundred&#39; (with which he was as much concerned as <a class="mw-redirect" href="../B/Bilbo.md" title="Bilbo">Bilbo</a> was to surpass the Old Took) only because he mowed a large lawn that spring and then sat in the wind without a jacket.
</p>

</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>[The incidents of the <a href="../W/Witch-king.md" title="Witch-king">Witch-king</a>&#39;s <a href="../M/Morgul-knife.md" title="Morgul-knife">knife</a> in <a href="../F/Flight_to_the_Ford.md" title="Flight to the Ford">Book I, Chapter 12</a>, and of <a href="../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a>&#39;s <a class="mw-redirect" href="../D/Daggers_of_Westernesse.md" title="Daggers of Westernesse">sword</a> in <a href="../T/The_Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md" title="The Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Book V, Chapter 6</a>] were intended to be integrated with the entire mytho-historical background, events in an agelong war. <a class="mw-redirect" href="../F/Frodo.md" title="Frodo">Frodo</a> received his wound from the Witchking under <a href="../W/Weathertop.md" title="Weathertop">Weathertop</a>, the bulwark of the ancient fortified line made by the <a href="../N/Númenóreans.md" title="Númenóreans">Numenoreans</a> against his kingdom; Meriadoc&#39;s dagger was taken from the gravemounds of the same people. It was made by smiths who knew all about <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> and his servants, and made in prophetic vision or hope of ending just as it did. [spellings <i>sic</i>]
</p>
