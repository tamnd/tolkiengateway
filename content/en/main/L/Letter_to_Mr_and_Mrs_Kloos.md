Written from [18 April](/wiki/18_April) to [6 May](/wiki/6_May) [1963](/wiki/1963) (suffering from symptoms of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fibromyalgia" title="wikipedia:Fibromyalgia">fibrositis</a>, he could not finish the letter at once), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Mr and Mrs Kloos**. Anneke C. Kloos-Adriaansen and P. Kloos were <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropology" title="wikipedia:Anthropology">anthropologists</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Replying to their letter from [23 March](/wiki/23_March), Tolkien writes at length about "his sources and inspiration for *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*".<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Excerpts from the letter were published in *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_A_Reader%27s_Companion)*.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup>
