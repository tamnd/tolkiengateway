<i><b>lúmë</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "time, hour".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Forms {#Forms}

- *lúmenna* = "upon the time"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
- *lúmesse* = "at the hour"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Elen_s%C3%ADla_l%C3%BAmenn%27_omentielvo" title="Elen síla lúmenn&#39; omentielvo">Elen síla <b>lúmenn</b>(<b>a</b>) omentielvo</a></i> = "a star shines on the hour of our meeting"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Onnal%C3%BAm%C3%AB" title="Onnalúmë">Onna<b>lúmë</b></a></i> = "Time of the Children"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Aia_Mar%C3%ADa" title="Aia María">sí ar <b>lúmessë</b> ya firuvammë</a></i> = "now and at the hour of our death"
