**Lars Gustafsson** (born [May 17](/wiki/17_May "17 May"), [1936](/wiki/1936)) is a Swedish poet, novelist and scholar. From 1983 he served as a professor at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin" title="wikipedia:University of Texas at Austin">University of Texas at Austin</a>, where he taught Philosophy and Creative Writing, until May 2006, when he retired.

In [1961](/wiki/1961), Gustafsson conducted an interview with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in Headington.
