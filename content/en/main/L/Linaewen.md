**Linaewen** was a long, narrow lake within the vast marshland of reeds and pools that formed the [Marshes of Nevrast](/wiki/Marshes_of_Nevrast), which were in the eastern lowlands of the country of [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The margins of Linaewen abounded with wading birds of every kind.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was from these birds that it took its name: *Linaewen* is [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) for "lake of birds".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Linaewen* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, composed of *[lind](/wiki/Lind "Lind")* ("pool, mere") + *[aew](/wiki/Aew "Aew")* ("bird") + *[en](/wiki/En "En")* (adjective suffix), therefore "Lake of Birds".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
