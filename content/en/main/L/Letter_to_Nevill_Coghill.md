On [21 August](../_/21_August.md) [1954](../_/1954.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Nevill Coghill](../N/Nevill_Coghill.md)**.

- **Subject:** [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) have stated that: "[Nevill Coghill](../N/Nevill_Coghill.md) had written to Tolkien asking for explanations, some of which the author felt should be left until the later volumes of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* appeared and his friend was able to read them". Tolkien did, however, offer some comments about [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md) in his letter.<sup id="cite_ref-WC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WC-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A section from the letter was published in <a class="external text" href="http://wayneandchristina.wordpress.com/2014/12/30/tom-bombadil-addenda-corrigenda/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">a blog post</a> by [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WC-1">[1]</a></sup>
