On [11 June](../_/11_June.md) [1972](../_/1972.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote <b><a class="mw-redirect" href="Letters_not_published_in_%2522The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%2522.md" title="Letters not published in &#34;The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien&#34;">a letter</a> to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Burchfield" title="wikipedia:Robert Burchfield">Robert Burchfield</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-Addenda_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Addenda-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Apologies for not having returned a book (*Kentish Place-names* by J.K. Wallenberg, 1931) which Tolkien had borrowed.
- **Description:** Signed letter, written from Merton College.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Excerpts {#Excerpts}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>My dear Burchfield<br/><br/>In unpacking my books from shore<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> I discovered this book which belongs to you. You lent it me a long while ago - but as I was in hospital at the time of my reward from Oxford<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 2]</a></sup> I was not guilty of removing it to Poole, though guilty (with more excuse) for not returning it later. Forgive me.<br/><br/>Yours...<br/><br/>J.R.R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
