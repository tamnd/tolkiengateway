On [13](/wiki/13_June "13 June")-[15 June](/wiki/15_June) [1964](/wiki/1964), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to Mr W.R. Matthews**.

- **Descriptions:** Tolkien made a carbon copy of the letter and included it in the manuscript folder containing *Words, Phrases &amp; Passages in* The Lord of the Rings*. The letter contains a discussion of the [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech).*
- **Publication:** A large section of the letter was published in [*Parma Eldalamberon*, issue 17](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_17 "Parma Eldalamberon 17").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
