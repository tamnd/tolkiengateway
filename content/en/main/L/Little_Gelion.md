**Little Gelion** was a lesser river that rose beneath [Himring](../H/Himring.md) in the north marches of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), whose spring was close by that of the [Celon](../C/Celon.md). Where the Celon ran southwest towards the borders of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), the Little Gelion followed a twisting course to the southeast, where it joined with the [Greater Gelion](../G/Greater_Gelion.md) to form the long river [Gelion](../G/Gelion.md) itself.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The river formed the southwest border of the ward of [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
