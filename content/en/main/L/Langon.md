**Langon** was a servant of [Melko](../M/Melko.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

## History {#History}

Langon's race and the meaning of his name are unknown. During the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md) he became a servant of Melko.

When the [Gods](../G/Gods.md) went to the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md) to attack Melko, he ran north and locked himself in [Utumna](../U/Utumna.md). Then the Valar opened the gates and asked him to come forth. However, Melko sent Langon to negotiate with their enemies. His message was full of insolence and false flattery, causing wrath among the Gods.<sup id="cite_ref-Langon_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Langon-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Although *Langon* is never glossed, in the *[Gnomish Lexicon](../G/Gnomish_Lexicon.md)* there are two entries that could be related: *lang* ("to blare, clang, ring"), *langon* ("great bell").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> So perhaps the name has something to do with Langon "blaring forth" Melko's message.
