**Larks** were melodious songbirds found in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). A "lark" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) was a *[lirulin](Lirulin.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md)'s first wife [Míriel](../M/Míriel.md) died he lived in grief for many years. [Ingwë](../I/Ingwë.md), wishing to lighten his heart, invited him to leave [Túna](../T/Túna.md) and come visit him where the light of the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md) might bring him healing. As he approached the house of Ingwë on the west slopes of [Oiolossë](../O/Oiolossë.md), Ingwë's daughter [Indis](../I/Indis.md) saw Finwë and sang a song of joy to him, which was "as a song of the *lirulin* in the sky." Seeing Indis at this moment caused Finwë to fall in love with her and a year later the two were wed.<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier version of the story above [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) used the word *aimenel* or *aimenal* but changed it to *lirulin* and wrote the word "lark" in the margin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
