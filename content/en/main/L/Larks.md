**Larks** were melodious songbirds found in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). A "lark" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) was a *[lirulin](/wiki/Lirulin "Lirulin")*.<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB)'s first wife [Míriel](/wiki/M%C3%ADriel) died he lived in grief for many years. [Ingwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB), wishing to lighten his heart, invited him to leave [Túna](/wiki/T%C3%BAna) and come visit him where the light of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees) might bring him healing. As he approached the house of Ingwë on the west slopes of [Oiolossë](/wiki/Oioloss%C3%AB), Ingwë's daughter [Indis](/wiki/Indis) saw Finwë and sang a song of joy to him, which was "as a song of the *lirulin* in the sky." Seeing Indis at this moment caused Finwë to fall in love with her and a year later the two were wed.<sup id="cite_ref-Finwe_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Finwe-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier version of the story above [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) used the word *aimenel* or *aimenal* but changed it to *lirulin* and wrote the word "lark" in the margin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
