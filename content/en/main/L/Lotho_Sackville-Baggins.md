**Lotho Sackville-Baggins** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2964](../T/Third_Age_2964.md) - [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), died aged 55) was the only child of [Otho](../O/Otho_Sackville-Baggins.md) and [Lobelia](Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins.md) [Sackville-Baggins](../S/Sackville-Baggins_Family.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) at the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), he established a totalitarian regime within [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

After Lotho's father, Otho, died in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3012](../T/Third_Age_3012.md), Lotho inherited his [pipe-weed](../P/Pipe-weed.md) plantations in the [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md), ensuring his status as a wealthy Hobbit.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-3">[3]</a></sup>

According to [Tolman Cotton](../T/Tolman_Cotton_Senior.md), Lotho had been quietly selling the best [Pipe-weed](../P/Pipe-weed.md) for one or two years before the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) and he seemingly bought the [Old Mill](../O/Old_Mill.md) before [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) sold [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) to his family.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-3">[3]</a></sup>

On [23 September](../_/23_September.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-4">[4]</a></sup> Lotho and Lobelia arrived at Bag End after lunch to take stock of the property and to obtain the keys.<sup id="cite_ref-Three_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Three-1">[1]</a></sup> Soon thereafter they moved into the residence, Lotho demolished the Old Mill and recruited outsiders to build a bigger mill and others like it in order to efficiently grind corn faster. By the end of that year, Lotho began buying an excessive amount of property as well as selling goods beyond Pipe-weed to many [Men](../M/Men.md) who came up from the south, mostly [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md). When folk began to complain about shortages that winter, Lotho had some Ruffians stay to assert his dominance over the Shire. At first, Lotho paid for all the stolen goods and damages that the Ruffians were responsible for. However, Lotho eventually stopped doing this. When [Will Whitfoot](../W/Will_Whitfoot.md), the [Mayor of the Shire](../M/Mayor_of_the_Shire.md), set out to protest against Lotho's activities, he had his [Men](../S/Sharkey's_Men.md) incarcerate him in the [Lockholes](Lockholes.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-3">[3]</a></sup>

Soon after <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=New_Year&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="New Year (page does not exist)">New Year</a>, with no Mayor of the Shire, Lotho began calling himself [Chief Shirriff](../S/Shirriffs.md), and demanded to be acknowledged as the Chief, and did whatever he wanted with no one to stop one. He closed all of the inns and made his own version of *[The Rules](../T/The_Rules.md)*, turning the Shire into a totalitarian regime. When [Thain](../T/Thain.md) [Paladin II](../P/Paladin_II.md) refused to acknowledge Lotho as the Chief, Lotho sent his Men to [Tookland](../T/Tookland.md), though the [Tooks](../T/Took_Family.md) did not give in and resisted.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-3">[3]</a></sup>

Lotho's regime within the Shire ended in [September](../S/September.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) when [Sharkey](../S/Sharkey.md) arrived at Bag End<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and took over the Shire from there. Soon Lotho was no longer seen in public and his mother, Lobelia, was taken to the Lockholes.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-3">[3]</a></sup>

It was later revealed that Lotho was stabbed (and possibly also eaten) by [Gríma](../G/Gríma.md) in his sleep at the order of [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

After Sharkey usurped the rule of the Shire from Lotho, he had the Ruffians industrialize the new mill in order to pollute [the Water](../T/The_Water.md) and the [Brandywine](../B/Brandywine.md), and life in the Shire became worse. Eventually however, when the [Travellers](../T/Travellers.md) returned, the Shire was retaken in the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Lotho* represents an original [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) [Westron](../W/Westron.md) name **Lotha** which was meaningless in contemporary speech.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Lotho was called **Cosimo** within two variants of an early draft of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, where he fought [Sancho Proudfoot](../S/Sancho_Proudfoot.md) at [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) being supported by his mother, Lobelia, whose umbrella broke upon Sancho's head.<sup id="cite_ref-Early_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Early-7">[7]</a></sup> In a note to the draft, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) stated that this was Lotho's first appearance, and that in both of these variants, he had an overshadowed wife named [Miranda](../C/Cora_Goodbody.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Early_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Early-7">[7]</a></sup> though she was removed within the next draft of the text.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> In another early draft, Cosimo's epithet was **sandy-haired** rather than Pimple.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In an isolated outline foreseeing the scouring, it is only Cosimo rather than Saruman, who industrialized the Shire with factories and smoke.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> In a draft of [The Mirror of Galadriel](../T/The_Mirror_of_Galadriel.md) chapter, Frodo sees a very wealthy Cosimo buying a lot of land.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> In another outline of plot notes, Tolkien wrote that the Sackville-Bagginses are kicked out of the Shire to become pot-boys in [Bree](../B/Bree.md),<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> presumably including Cosimo.

In an early version of [The Land of Shadow](../T/The_Land_of_Shadow.md) chapter, Sam speculated that Cosimo may have made a mess in the Shire and that there would be trouble if they ever got back.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> In an outline of plot points, Tolkien wrote that when the Hobbits returned to the Shire, they drove out Cosimo.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

In an early version of the [Many Partings](../M/Many_Partings.md) chapter, Saruman genuinely warned the Travellers about the Shire, directly mentioning Cosimo by name.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

In an early draft where Tolkien mapped out a conversation, Cosimo was originally going to be the **New Mayor** before Tolkien immediately changed his mind and made him the Chief Shirriff. In another draft, Merry mentions that none of the [Bucklanders](../B/Bucklanders.md) would allow Cosimo to dictate them.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>
