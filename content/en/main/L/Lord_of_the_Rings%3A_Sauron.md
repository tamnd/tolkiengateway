**Lord of the Rings: Sauron** is the second expansion to the [Lord of the Rings](</wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings_(board_game)> "Lord of the Rings (board game)") board game.

It introduced the ability to play as [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), while other players got extra resources to deal with this new threat.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
