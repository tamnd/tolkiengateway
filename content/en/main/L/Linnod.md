The **Linnod** was one of the three Elvish [Verse Modes](/wiki/Verse_Modes). The other two being the *[Ann-thennath](/wiki/Ann-thennath)*, and the *[Minlamad thent/estent](/wiki/Minlamad_thent/estent)*. The Linnod was a type of poetic meter intended for short sayings.

## History {#History}

The verse form was used by [Gilraen](/wiki/Gilraen), the mother of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), in her linnod: *Ónen i-Estel Edain, ú-chebin estel anim*, which translates to this: "I gave [Hope](/wiki/Aragorn#Names_and_titles "Aragorn") to the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain), I have kept no hope for myself."<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The *linnod* can be broken up as follows: [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[lind](/wiki/Lind "Lind")* = *song, chant, singing; singer*. *[odog](/wiki/Odog "Odog")* = *seven*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
