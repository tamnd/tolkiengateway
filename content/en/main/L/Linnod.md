The **Linnod** was one of the three Elvish [Verse Modes](../V/Verse_Modes.md). The other two being the *[Ann-thennath](../A/Ann-thennath.md)*, and the *[Minlamad thent/estent](../M/Minlamad_thent%252Festent.md)*. The Linnod was a type of poetic meter intended for short sayings.

## History {#History}

The verse form was used by [Gilraen](../G/Gilraen.md), the mother of [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), in her linnod: *Ónen i-Estel Edain, ú-chebin estel anim*, which translates to this: "I gave [Hope](../A/Aragorn.md#Names_and_titles) to the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md), I have kept no hope for myself."<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The *linnod* can be broken up as follows: [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *[lind](Lind.md)* = *song, chant, singing; singer*. *[odog](../O/Odog.md)* = *seven*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
