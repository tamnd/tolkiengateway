On [30 May](/wiki/30_May) [1973](/wiki/1973), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Philip Brown** of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Merton College, Oxford">Merton College</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Arranging a meeting. Tolkien also gives the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*: *i Túrin i Cormaron*.
