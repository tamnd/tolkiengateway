On [8 July](/wiki/8_July) [1973](/wiki/1973), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Norman Power](/wiki/Norman_Power)**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup> The letter was published in 1975 in the journal *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Description and Contents:** Tolkien sends Power a signature, a spare copy of a publication, and "*comments on his childhood in [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham).*"<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
