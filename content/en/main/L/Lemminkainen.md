**Lemminkainen** is a character based on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemmink%C3%A4inen" title="wikipedia:Lemminkäinen">Lemminkäinen</a> from the *[Kalevala](/wiki/Kalevala)*,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> who appears in two poems by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien): *[The New Lemminkainen](/wiki/The_New_Lemminkainen)* and *[Lemminkainen Goeth to the Fords of Oxen](/wiki/Lemminkainen_Goeth_to_the_Fords_of_Oxen)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
