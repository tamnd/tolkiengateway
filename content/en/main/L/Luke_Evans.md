**Luke George Evans** (born [April 15](../_/15_April.md), [1979](../_/1979.md) in Aberbargoed, Wales) is a Welsh theatre and film actor. He portrayed [Bard](../B/Bard.md) as well as [Girion](../G/Girion.md) (Bard's ancestor) in [*The Hobbit* films](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).
