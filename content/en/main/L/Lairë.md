**Lairë** is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for the season of summer, known in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) as [Laer](/wiki/Laer). This was also the name of the second season in the [Calendar of Imladris](/wiki/Reckoning_of_Rivendell "Reckoning of Rivendell"), 72 days that fell more or less between [1 June](/wiki/1_June) and [9 August](/wiki/9_August) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
