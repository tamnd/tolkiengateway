In [1963](/wiki/1963), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to [T.P. ("Tom") Dunning](/wiki/T.P._Dunning "T.P. Dunning")**, Tolkien's former student.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Bloom_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bloom-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Description:** Handwritten letter, one page, loosely inserted in Dunning's copy of *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)*, signed by Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-Bloom_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bloom-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The letter and book sold for £3,500 (£4,340 including feeds fees) at Bloomsbury on 30 May 2013.
