**Lord of the Rings: The Confrontation** is a two-player board game, made in 2013<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> by [Fantasy Flight Games](/wiki/Fantasy_Flight_Games) in which one player controls the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) and the other player wields the forces of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It features art from <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Daarken&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Daarken (page does not exist)">Daarken</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Chris_Dien&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Chris Dien (page does not exist)">Chris Dien</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=John_Gravato&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="John Gravato (page does not exist)">John Gravato</a>, [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Michael_Komarck&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Michael Komarck (page does not exist)">Michael Komarck</a> and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sedone_Thongvilay&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sedone Thongvilay (page does not exist)">Sedone Thongvilay</a>.
