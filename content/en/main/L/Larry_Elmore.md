**Larry Elmore** (born [5 August](../_/5_August.md) [1948](../_/1948.md)) is an American fantasy artist, most notable in helping to define the look of *[Dungeons and Dragons](../D/Dungeons_and_Dragons.md)* and contributing art and comics to the related *[Dragon Magazine](../D/Dragon_Magazine.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup> He was contracted by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create artwork for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.

## Career {#Career}

After getting a fine arts degree at Western Kentucky University, he became an early key staff artist at [TSR](../T/TSR.md). After leaving the company in [1987](../_/1987.md), as a freelancer he worked for many other games, such as *[Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md)* and the *Middle-earth Collectible Card Game*.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
