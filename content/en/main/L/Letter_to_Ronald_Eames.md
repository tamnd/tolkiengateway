On [15 August](/wiki/15_August) [1953](/wiki/1953), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to Ronald Eames** of [George Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin "Allen and Unwin").<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject**: Reply to a letter from Eames, dated [14 August](/wiki/14_August), concerning the typescript of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** None.
