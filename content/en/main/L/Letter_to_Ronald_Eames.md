On [15 August](../_/15_August.md) [1953](../_/1953.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_%2522The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%2522.md) to Ronald Eames** of [George Allen &amp; Unwin](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject**: Reply to a letter from Eames, dated [14 August](../_/14_August.md), concerning the typescript of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** None.
