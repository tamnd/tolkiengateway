On [28 October](/wiki/28_October) [1965](/wiki/1965), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) sent **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to a Mr P.M. Cowan**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien answers a young fan (aged 9 in 1965) concerning the *[The Lord of the Rings Index](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Index)*.
- **Publication:** None.
