The **Lonely Troll**, or the **Troll**, was an old inhabitant of <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Faraway&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Faraway (page does not exist)">Faraway</a> who appeared in *[Perry-the-Winkle](../P/Perry-the-Winkle.md)*, a nursery rhyme<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup> by [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)<sup id="cite_ref-preface_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-preface-4">[4]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-Perry_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Perry-3">[3]</a></sup>

This was a very unusual [Troll](../T/Trolls.md): He wanted friends, he did not steal, drink beer, or even eat meat, and most amazingly of all, he was a very good cook. The Lonely Troll lived in the hills of Faraway, which seems to have been somewhere in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md), for he was the last troll "from [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md) to the [Sea](../B/Belegaer.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-Perry_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Perry-3">[3]</a></sup>

One day the Lonely Troll decided to seek friendship in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). When he came to [Delving](../D/Delving.md), though, the [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md) ran and hid. The old troll sat down outside of the [Lockholes](Lockholes.md) and wept. It was then that a [hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) lad, [Perry-the-Winkle](<../P/Perry-the-Winkle_(hobbit).md>), came up and befriended him. The happy troll took Perry home for [tea](../T/Tea.md) and fed him generously.<sup id="cite_ref-Perry_3-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Perry-3">[3]</a></sup>

When the other hobbits learned that Perry-the-Winkle had been so handsomely fed they all went to the Troll's house to demand food for themselves. The Lonely Troll refused to feed them though since they had refused to be his friend. Instead he invited Perry to [tea-time](../T/Tea-time.md) [Every Thursday](../M/Mersday.md) and taught him to be a great <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Baker&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Baker (page does not exist)">Baker</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Perry_3-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Perry-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Background {#Background}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Bumpus_(no._105,_text_A).jpeg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d2/The_Bumpus_%28no._105%2C_text_A%29.jpeg/150px-The_Bumpus_%28no._105%2C_text_A%29.jpeg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="150" height="190" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d2/The_Bumpus_%28no._105%2C_text_A%29.jpeg/225px-The_Bumpus_%28no._105%2C_text_A%29.jpeg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d2/The_Bumpus_%28no._105%2C_text_A%29.jpeg/300px-The_Bumpus_%28no._105%2C_text_A%29.jpeg 2x" data-file-width="1284" data-file-height="1630"/></a><figcaption>&#34;<i>The Bumpus</i>&#34; by <a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>.</figcaption></figure>

In the first version of the poem, entitled *The Bumpus*, in early [1928](../_/1928.md),<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-5">[5]</a></sup> which was unconnected to the [legendarium](Legendarium.md), the Lonely Troll was a being known only as the **Bumpus**. On the manuscript of the poem, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) drew an unintentionally sinister<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup> sketch of the Bumpus.<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-5">[5]</a></sup> On [29 March](../_/29_March.md),<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup> the Bumpus became part<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup> of Tolkien's *[Tales and Songs of Bimble Bay](../T/Tales_and_Songs_of_Bimble_Bay.md)* series<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-5">[5]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-guide_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the third version of the poem, entitled *William and the Bumpus*, Tolkien added an extra 111 lines,<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup> which incorporated the scene where the Bumpus/Lonely Troll teaches William/Perry the art of baking, bringing the poem more in line with the final version.<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-5">[5]</a></sup>

In early [1961](../_/1961.md),<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup> when Tolkien revised the poem for inclusion within *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](../T/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md)*, the Bumpus was changed to the **Lonely [Worm](../W/Worm.md)**<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup> and then to the Lonely Troll. Despite the name change of the protagonist, Tolkien included the place-name [Bumpus Head](../B/Bumpus_Head.md) in the published version of *[The Dragon's Visit](../T/The_Dragon's_Visit.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-5">[5]</a></sup>
