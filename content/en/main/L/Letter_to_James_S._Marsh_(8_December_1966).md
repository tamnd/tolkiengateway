On [8 December](/wiki/8_December) [1966](/wiki/1966), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to James S. Marsh**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** A reply to a letter of appreciation and three signatures on a separately enclosed sheet.
- **Publication:** None.

## Transcript {#Transcript}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Dear Mr Marsh,<br/><br/>Thank you for your letter. I am pleased to hear that you like my work. I am extremely busy trying to complete my next book, <u>The Silmarillion</u>, and I therefore enclose three autographs for you to stick into your books.<br/><br/>With best wishes,<br/>Yours sincerely<br/>J.R.R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
