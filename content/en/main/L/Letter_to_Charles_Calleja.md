In [1966](/wiki/1966), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Charles Calleja**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Tolkien explained that the second edition [*Hobbit*](/wiki/The_Hobbit "The Hobbit") was different to the original.
- **Description:** Typewritten letter with handwritten annotations, 2 pages, Oxford.
- **Publication:** None.

## Excerpts {#Excerpts}

“Being influenced by, or making use of, events or experience of one’s life (which is inevitable) has nothing to do with allegory.”

“You will not find it possible, I think, to acquire a copy of the first edition... they are very scarce, most of the stocks having perished in the London blitz.”
