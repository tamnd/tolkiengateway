The **Lesser Market** was a market in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

It was in the southern part of the city, and [Salgant](../S/Salgant.md) dwelt near its square. During the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) sent Salgant to the [Great Market](../G/Great_Market.md) to reinforce [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) and the [house of the Golden Flower](../H/House_of_the_Golden_Flower.md), but being deep in his cowardice, Salgant lied to his company, the [host of the Harp](../H/Host_of_the_Harp.md), and took them to the Lesser Market. There his men fretted and rebelled from his orders, and came back to the king's halls in time to save Glorfindel.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
