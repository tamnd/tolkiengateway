The **Lesser Market** was a market in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

It was in the southern part of the city, and [Salgant](/wiki/Salgant) dwelt near its square. During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) sent Salgant to the [Great Market](/wiki/Great_Market) to reinforce [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) and the [house of the Golden Flower](/wiki/House_of_the_Golden_Flower "House of the Golden Flower"), but being deep in his cowardice, Salgant lied to his company, the [host of the Harp](/wiki/Host_of_the_Harp "Host of the Harp"), and took them to the Lesser Market. There his men fretted and rebelled from his orders, and came back to the king's halls in time to save Glorfindel.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
