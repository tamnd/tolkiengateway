**Lóni** was a [dwarf of Erebor](../D/Dwarves_of_Erebor.md).

## History {#History}

Lóni was part of [Balin's Colony](../B/Balin's_Colony~64118.md) to reclaim [Moria](../M/Moria.md). Their mission at first seemed successful, and [Balin](../B/Balin.md) went so far as to claim the title [Lord of Moria](Lord_of_Moria.md). This all changed in their fifth year there. On November 10, the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) came up the [Silverlode](../S/Silverlode.md) and killed Balin while he was alone, unaware, and looking into [Mirrormere](../M/Mirrormere.md). The Dwarves fought back but eventually lost [East-gate](../E/East-gate.md) and [First Hall](../F/First_Hall.md). The Orcs made it across the [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](../B/Bridge_of_Khazad-dûm.md), there was a [battle in the Second Hall](../B/Battle_of_the_Second_Hall.md), and Lóni and his companions [Frár](../F/Frár.md) and [Náli](../N/Náli.md) died bravely while the rest of the colonists retreated.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lóni* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](../V/Völuspá.md)*. His name most likely means "lazy person".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
