The **lesser rings** were magic rings, forged before the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power), by the elves as essays in the craft of ring-making.

## History {#History}

The lesser rings were made sometime between [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1200](/wiki/Second_Age_1200 "Second Age 1200"), when [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) came in disguise to [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion), and [1500](/wiki/Second_Age_1500 "Second Age 1500"), when they began crafting the greater [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Unlike the greater rings, these were round and unadorned, without gems, much like the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-Rivendell_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivendell-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) of the [Shire](/wiki/Shire) had old tales of magic rings that turned people invisible. When [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) found the One Ring in the tunnels near [Gollum's lake](/wiki/Gollum%27s_lake), he believed that it was one of them.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) mentioned these rings to [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") when recounting the origins of the Rings of Power.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Supposedly, the lesser rings were also mentioned by a messenger of Sauron, who asked [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot) to find and return to Sauron "a little ring, the least of rings", that is, the ring of Bilbo Baggins.<sup id="cite_ref-Rivendell_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivendell-2">[2]</a></sup>

During his conversation with Gandalf in [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc), [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) wore a ring on his finger and claimed himself the '[Ring-maker](/wiki/Ring-maker)'.<sup id="cite_ref-Rivendell_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivendell-2">[2]</a></sup> It was possible that he indeed forged one of the lesser rings.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
