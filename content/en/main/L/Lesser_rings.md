The **lesser rings** were magic rings, forged before the [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md), by the elves as essays in the craft of ring-making.

## History {#History}

The lesser rings were made sometime between [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1200](../S/Second_Age_1200.md), when [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) came in disguise to [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md), and [1500](../S/Second_Age_1500.md), when they began crafting the greater [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Unlike the greater rings, these were round and unadorned, without gems, much like the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Rivendell_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivendell-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) of the [Shire](../S/Shire.md) had old tales of magic rings that turned people invisible. When [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) found the One Ring in the tunnels near [Gollum's lake](../G/Gollum's_lake.md), he believed that it was one of them.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) mentioned these rings to [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) when recounting the origins of the Rings of Power.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Supposedly, the lesser rings were also mentioned by a messenger of Sauron, who asked [Dáin Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) to find and return to Sauron "a little ring, the least of rings", that is, the ring of Bilbo Baggins.<sup id="cite_ref-Rivendell_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivendell-2">[2]</a></sup>

During his conversation with Gandalf in [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md), [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) wore a ring on his finger and claimed himself the '[Ring-maker](../R/Ring-maker.md)'.<sup id="cite_ref-Rivendell_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivendell-2">[2]</a></sup> It was possible that he indeed forged one of the lesser rings.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
