On [14 December](../_/14_December.md) [1970](../_/1970.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote <b><a class="mw-redirect" href="Letters_not_published_in_%2522The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%2522.md" title="Letters not published in &#34;The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien&#34;">a letter</a> to <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=W.A.R._Hadley&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="W.A.R. Hadley (page does not exist)">W.A.R. Hadley</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-TL_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Christmas letter with updates on the Tolkien family happenings of 1970.
- **Publication:** None.

## Contents {#Contents}

The letter includes references to:<sup id="cite_ref-TL_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL-1">[1]</a></sup>

- The death of [Hilary Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md)'s wife Magdalen on 5 April
- Her requiem and burial perfomed by [Edith](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) and Ronald's son [John](../J/John_Tolkien.md)
- Hilary's son Gabriel, wife and children moving from Wesham to live with him
- Edith's March fall and resultant injuries
- Pending visits from son John and daughter [Priscilla](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md)
- The "Canadian daughter-in-law" [Baillie](../B/Baillie_Tolkien.md) in Winnipeg
- Ronald's [1968](../_/1968.md) "accident" and their move to Poole
- Canada's "troubles" in 1970
- Ronald's desire to visit [Canada](../../category/C/Canada.md) and [South Africa](../../category/S/South_Africa.md)
- A happy summer visit from "[Ding](../D/Dorothy_Wood.md)" — "an amazing person"
