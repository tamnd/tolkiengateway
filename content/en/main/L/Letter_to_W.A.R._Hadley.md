On [14 December](/wiki/14_December) [1970](/wiki/1970), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote <b><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22" title="Letters not published in &#34;The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien&#34;">a letter</a> to <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=W.A.R._Hadley&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="W.A.R. Hadley (page does not exist)">W.A.R. Hadley</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-TL_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Christmas letter with updates on the Tolkien family happenings of 1970.
- **Publication:** None.

## Contents {#Contents}

The letter includes references to:<sup id="cite_ref-TL_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL-1">[1]</a></sup>

- The death of [Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien)'s wife Magdalen on 5 April
- Her requiem and burial perfomed by [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien") and Ronald's son [John](/wiki/John_Tolkien "John Tolkien")
- Hilary's son Gabriel, wife and children moving from Wesham to live with him
- Edith's March fall and resultant injuries
- Pending visits from son John and daughter [Priscilla](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien "Priscilla Tolkien")
- The "Canadian daughter-in-law" [Baillie](/wiki/Baillie_Tolkien "Baillie Tolkien") in Winnipeg
- Ronald's [1968](/wiki/1968) "accident" and their move to Poole
- Canada's "troubles" in 1970
- Ronald's desire to visit [Canada](/wiki/Category:Canada "Category:Canada") and [South Africa](/wiki/Category:South_Africa "Category:South Africa")
- A happy summer visit from "[Ding](/wiki/Dorothy_Wood "Dorothy Wood")" — "an amazing person"
