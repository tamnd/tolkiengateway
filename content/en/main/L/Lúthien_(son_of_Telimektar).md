**Lúthien** was the name of the son of the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Telimektar](/wiki/Telimektar),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in one of the outlines of *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

In that outline, [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) is no longer identified with England as is the case in most of the *Lost Tales*. And instead of [Ottor Wǽfre](/wiki/Eriol "Eriol"), it is a mariner called [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine) from [Luthany](/wiki/Luthany) who arrives to Tol Eressëa - who in one of the notes associated with the above outline, is called *Lúthien* by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) living there, for his love of the constellation of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orion_(constellation)" title="wikipedia:Orion (constellation)">Orion</a> (identified with Telimektar, the father of Lúsion/Lúthien).<sup id="cite_ref-Lus_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lus-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Lúthien* is in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish). It is explained as meaning "friend" in most of the contemporary outlines, and "wanderer" in others.<sup id="cite_ref-Lus_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lus-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

An alternative name <i><b>Lúsion</b></i> is also given in the same text in which the character appears,<sup id="cite_ref-Lus_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lus-2">[1]</a></sup> which might be the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognate of *Lúthien*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup>
