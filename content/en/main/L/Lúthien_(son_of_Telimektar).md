**Lúthien** was the name of the son of the [Vala](../V/Valar.md) [Telimektar](../T/Telimektar.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> according to the early version of the [legendarium](Legendarium.md) in one of the outlines of *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

In that outline, [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) is no longer identified with England as is the case in most of the *Lost Tales*. And instead of [Ottor Wǽfre](../E/Eriol.md), it is a mariner called [Ælfwine](../_/Ælfwine.md) from [Luthany](Luthany.md) who arrives to Tol Eressëa - who in one of the notes associated with the above outline, is called *Lúthien* by the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) living there, for his love of the constellation of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orion_(constellation)" title="wikipedia:Orion (constellation)">Orion</a> (identified with Telimektar, the father of Lúsion/Lúthien).<sup id="cite_ref-Lus_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lus-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Lúthien* is in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md). It is explained as meaning "friend" in most of the contemporary outlines, and "wanderer" in others.<sup id="cite_ref-Lus_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lus-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

An alternative name <i><b>Lúsion</b></i> is also given in the same text in which the character appears,<sup id="cite_ref-Lus_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lus-2">[1]</a></sup> which might be the [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) cognate of *Lúthien*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup>
