**Little Delving** was a village of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It is situated to the north-east of the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing), perhaps north to its bigger counterpart, [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a possible [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") form *Lytel Delfing*. It means "small digging".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Little Delving is a small village north-west of <a href="/wiki/Michel_Delving" title="Michel Delving">Michel Delving</a>. Hobbit characters start their journey there after finishing the introductory instance, but afterwards very few errands ever lead the players back to Little Delving.</dd></dl>
