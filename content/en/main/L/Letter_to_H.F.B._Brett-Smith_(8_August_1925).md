On [8 August](../_/8_August.md) [1925](../_/1925.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to** H.F.B. Brett-Smith<b>.<sup id="cite_ref-wheaton_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wheaton-1">[1]</a></sup></b>

- **Description:** Handwritten letter, 2 pages.
- **Subject:** Tolkien is discussing academia at Leeds University and Tolkien’s poetry.
