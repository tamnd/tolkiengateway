**Liz Danforth** (born [16 November](../_/16_November.md) [1953](../_/1953.md)) is an American fantasy artist, author and librarian. In the 1980s and 1990s, Danforth was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create illustrations for *[Rescue in Mirkwood](../R/Rescue_in_Mirkwood.md)*, *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)* and to make card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.

While generally working in oils and mixed media, time constraints often led her to use ink and acrylics when doing card art.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
