This list contains all minor characters in [Bree-land](../B/Bree-land.md) that were created specifically for [The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md).

## Aldred Cartwright {#Aldred_Cartwright}

**Aldred Cartwright** is the champion trainer in [Archet](../A/Archet.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> In the Bree-land Introduction, [Amdir](<../A/Amdir_(Shadows_of_Angmar).md>) sends the champion player to him so he can improve the player's skills.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceB-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Ann Granger {#Ann_Granger}

**Ann Granger** is a woman of [Archet](../A/Archet.md) found near the Hunter's Lodge.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> She originally held a farm in Archet-Dale but was driven out by [Blackwolds](../B/Blackwolds.md) and joined [Jon Brackenbrook](../J/Jon_Brackenbrook.md)'s hunters.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Aran Greyfeather {#Aran_Greyfeather}

**Aran Greyfeather** is a bowyer from [Archet](../A/Archet.md). He sells his products west of Archet's trainings fields.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Argirion {#Argirion}

**Argirion** was the first mate at the Gilminuial, a ship of the [Rangers](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) that supplies the [Wardens of Annúminas](../W/Wardens_of_Annúminas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-shipwrecked_mariners_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shipwrecked_mariners-4">[4]</a></sup> When a storm destroyed the ship, Argirion and <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Mindambeth">Mindambeth</a>, the quarter master, were drowned near [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md). He asks you to follow the [Baranduin](../B/Baranduin.md) to the north to find the drowned ship and to learn the fate of the other crew members, especially of [Aranhir](List_of_minor_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_characters_in_Evendim.md#Aranhir), the captain of the Gilminuial.<sup id="cite_ref-shipwrecked_mariners_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shipwrecked_mariners-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Cal Sprigley {#Cal_Sprigley}

**Cal Sprigley** is a farmer in southern [Archet](../A/Archet.md)-Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> He successfully defended his farm against the [Blackwolds](../B/Blackwolds.md) but fled to Archet with his wife, <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Holey_Sprigley">Holey Sprigley</a>, when the spiders overrun his farm.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> He's a friend of <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Constable_Thistlewool">Constable Thistlewool</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Constable Thistlewool {#Constable_Thistlewool}

**Constable Thistlewool** is the constable of [Archet](../A/Archet.md) and can be found at its training grounds.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> He is a friend of <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Cal_Sprigley">Cal Sprigley</a>, a farmer in the south of Archet-Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Cuthbert Rook {#Cuthbert_Rook}

**Cuthbert Rook** is a lore-master trainer of [Archet](../A/Archet.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> In the Bree-land Introduction, he improves the player's skills on [Amdir](<../A/Amdir_(Shadows_of_Angmar).md>)'s request.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceB_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceB-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Daren Mills {#Daren_Mills}

**Daren Mills** is a hunter trainer in [Archet](../A/Archet.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> He improved the player's skills in the Bree-land Introduction.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceB_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceB-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Dirk Mudbrick {#Dirk_Mudbrick}

**Dirk Mudbrick** is a member of [Archet](../A/Archet.md)'s garrison. His original post was at Archet's West Gate but because he had too much comentare on [Captain Brackenbrook](../C/Captain_Brackenbrook.md)'s strategy, he was removed to a farm in Archet-Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-Spies_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Spies-8">[8]</a></sup> While he was defending the sheep-farms against the wolves, he saw many [Blackwolds](../B/Blackwolds.md) passing through the dale.<sup id="cite_ref-Spies_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Spies-8">[8]</a></sup> He sent the player into the Archet-Dale to kill one of them and bring back their orders. One of these orders described the treason of [Calder Cob](../C/Calder_Cob.md) and a planned raid against Archet.<sup id="cite_ref-Spies_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Spies-8">[8]</a></sup> He immediately sent the Blackwold-orders to Captain Brackenbrook and fought in the assault of Archet.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> When Archet's garrison drove the Blackwolds back to Blackwold Roost, he was one of those that was slain.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceC_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceC-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Edgar Standish {#Edgar_Standish}

**Edgar Standish** is a captain trainer in [Archet](../A/Archet.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> He teaches the captain player how to use his class in the Bree-land Introduction.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Fastred Burrows {#Fastred_Burrows}

**Fastred Burrows** is a burglar trainer in [Archet](../A/Archet.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> On [Amdir](<../A/Amdir_(Shadows_of_Angmar).md>)'s request he teaches the player more about his class in the Bree-land Introduction.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceB_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceB-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Fenton Marshly {#Fenton_Marshly}

**Fenton Marshly** is one of [Jon Brackenbrook](../J/Jon_Brackenbrook.md)'s hunters.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> He started hunting game as soon as he was strong enough to wield a bow.<sup id="cite_ref-Fento_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fento-12">[12]</a></sup> When the [Blackwolds](../B/Blackwolds.md) started to assault [Archet](../A/Archet.md), he was stationed at the Hunter's Lodge.<sup id="cite_ref-Fento_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fento-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Godwig the Lazy {#Godwig_the_Lazy}

**Godwig the Lazy** is a man from [Archet](../A/Archet.md), who can be found in Archet's Jailyard.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Hodor Thornberry {#Hodor_Thornberry}

**Hodor Thornberry** is a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) weapon-trader from [Archet](../A/Archet.md). He sells both one-handed as two-handed weapons.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Holey Sprigley {#Holey_Sprigley}

**Holey Sprigley** is the wife of <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Cal_Sprigley">Cal Sprigley</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Reason_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Reason-13">[13]</a></sup> a farmer in the south of [Archet](../A/Archet.md)-Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> When their farm was overrun they fled to Archet.<sup id="cite_ref-Reason_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Reason-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Jasper Greensmith {#Jasper_Greensmith}

**Jasper Greensmith** is a light armoursmith in [Archet](../A/Archet.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Kate Henseed {#Kate_Henseed}

**Kate Henseed** is a female supplier from [Archet](../A/Archet.md)<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> who informs the player that [Amdir](<../A/Amdir_(Shadows_of_Angmar).md>) is looking for him.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Mildred Catchfly {#Mildred_Catchfly}

**Mildred Catchfly** is the medium armoursmith in [Archet](../A/Archet.md). She is found west of the training fields.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Mindambeth {#Mindambeth}

**Mindambeth** is a [Ranger](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) and the quartermaster of the Gilminuial. She and <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Argirion">Argirion</a> drowned near [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md) when the ship was drowned.<sup id="cite_ref-shipwrecked_mariners_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shipwrecked_mariners-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Nate Whisperwood {#Nate_Whisperwood}

**Nate Whisperwood** is a male hunter of [Archet](../A/Archet.md). He could be found inside the Hunter's Lodge.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> He fought in the assault of Archet and was slain while defending the Hunter's Lodge.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceC_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceC-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Old Bloodtusk {#Old_Bloodtusk}

**Old Bloodtusk** was a boar in [Archet-dale](../A/Archet.md) between <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Cal_Sprigley">Cal Sprigley</a>'s Farm and [Blackwold's Roost](../B/Blackwold's_Roost.md). He lived in his den, built in a rotten, old tree stump.<sup id="cite_ref-Old_Bloodtusk_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Old_Bloodtusk-15">[15]</a></sup> He always went to the walls of Cal Sprigley's Farm in search of food. One day, he went into the farm and killed one of the farmhands. Afterwards, Cal Sprigley sent the player to Old Bloodtusk's Den to kill him.<sup id="cite_ref-Old_Bloodtusk_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Old_Bloodtusk-15">[15]</a></sup>

## Otto the Brigand {#Otto_the_Brigand}

**Otto the Brigand** was leader of the [brigands](../B/Blackwolds.md) that attacked [Archet](../A/Archet.md).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> He was imprisoned by [Calder Cob](../C/Calder_Cob.md) but was freed in the Assault of Archet where he takes Amdir as prisoner to their hideout.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> When the player and [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) assault the Blackwolds in Ost Baranor he is turned into a wraith by [Skorgrím Dourhand](../S/Skorgrím_Dourhand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

## Peg Pruner {#Peg_Pruner}

**Peg Pruner** is [Archet](../A/Archet.md)'s provisioner<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Captain Brackenbrook](../C/Captain_Brackenbrook.md)'s quartermaster.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> She is [Ned Pruner](../N/Ned_Pruner.md)'s wife.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

## Sergeant Redroot {#Sergeant_Redroot}

**Sergeant Redroot** is a member of [Captain Brackenbrook](../C/Captain_Brackenbrook.md)'s garrison. He was originally posted at [Archet](../A/Archet.md)'s gate<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> but was removed to the road between Archet and [Combe](../C/Combe.md).<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup>

## Teal Saddler {#Teal_Saddler}

**Teal Saddler** is a guardian trainer who can be found west of [Archet](../A/Archet.md)'s training grounds.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> She improves the player's skills in the Bree-land Introduction.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceB_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceB-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Tolman Gardner {#Tolman_Gardner}

**Tolman Gardner** is a minstrel trainer in [Archet](../A/Archet.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> During the Bree-land Introduction, he teaches the player how to use his class.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceB_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceB-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Will Wheatley {#Will_Wheatley}

**Will Wheatley** is <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Cal_Sprigley">Cal Sprigley</a>'s farmhand, and can be found at Cal Sprigley's farm in southern [Archet](../A/Archet.md)-Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_1-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-1">[1]</a></sup> He fought in the assault of Archet and was slain by [Blackwolds](../B/Blackwolds.md) near [Blackwold's Roost](../B/Blackwold's_Roost.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceC_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceC-10">[10]</a></sup>
