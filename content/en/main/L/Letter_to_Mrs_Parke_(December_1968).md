On [December](../D/December.md) [1968](../_/1968.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Mrs Parke**.<sup id="cite_ref-dreweattss_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dreweattss-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wishes a merry Christmas and a fortunate new year to Mrs Parke.
- **Publication:** None.
- **Description:** One page, signed.

## From the auction {#From_the_auction}

1pp., 6 lines with hand-drawn Christmas holly to one corner and signed "J.R.R.T.", \[1968\], wishing Mrs Parke the best for Christmas and a happier and more fortunate 1969, folds, 8vo . Mrs Parke acted as driver and general helper to the Tolkiens for several hours a week, after responding to an ad in a local Bournemouth newspaper placed by Edith Tolkien.
