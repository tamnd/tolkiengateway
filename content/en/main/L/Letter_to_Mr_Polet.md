On [1 January](/wiki/1_January) [1956](/wiki/1956), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Mr Polet**.

- **Description:** Handwritten and signed by Tolkien on his Sandfield Road letter paper. 5x7"/13x17 cm.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Dear Mr Polet,<br/><br/>I cannot resist the temptation to disobey your kind command not to answer, and write to thank you for your letter of 22 December.<br/><br/>I am most grateful for your expression of pleasure; and I hope that you will continue to enjoy Vols. ii and iii.<br/><br/>Yours sincerely<br/><br/>J.R.R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
