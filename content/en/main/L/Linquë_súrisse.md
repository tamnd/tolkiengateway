<i><b>Linquë súrisse</b></i> is the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) name of a grass.<sup id="cite_ref-AI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name likely means "grass in the wind". It has been suggested that the first element is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Linque&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Linque (page does not exist)">linque</a></i> ("grass, reed").<sup id="cite_ref-AI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-1">[1]</a></sup> The second element would likely be related to <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=S%C3%BAre&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Súre (page does not exist)">súre</a></i>.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
