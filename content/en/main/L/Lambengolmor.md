The **Lambengolmor** were the **Loremasters** of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves). They were skilled linguists and historians, and created many works about the Elves and their [languages](/wiki/Elvish "Elvish").

Known members were [Rúmil](</wiki/R%C3%BAmil_(elf_of_Tirion)> "Rúmil (elf of Tirion)"), who invented the first alphabet; [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), who invented the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar); and [Pengolodh](/wiki/Pengolodh), who is credited with many works that are the basis for *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>

A similar concept were the **Quettúri** ("Word-masters"), those Noldor who exercised [lámatyávë](/wiki/L%C3%A1maty%C3%A1v%C3%AB "Lámatyávë") creating new word systems.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In Valinor, Fëanor was chief of the linguistic loremasters.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The loremasters were not a distinct guild or gentle scribes; they comprised of the greatest kings, princes, and warriors such as Fëanor, the captains of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod), and [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lambengolmor* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word, glossed as "Loremasters of Tongues" in one manuscript.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup> In another manuscript, the alternative spelling *Lambeñgolmor* is seen, and also glossed as "lingustic loremasters".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has noted that only the plural form is attested, of a possible singular form *Lambengolmo*. The word consists of *[lambe](/wiki/Lambe "Lambe")* ("tongue, language") + *ingolmo* ("loremaster").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
