The **Lambengolmor** were the **Loremasters** of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md). They were skilled linguists and historians, and created many works about the Elves and their [languages](../E/Elvish.md).

Known members were [Rúmil](<../R/Rúmil_(elf_of_Tirion).md>), who invented the first alphabet; [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md), who invented the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md); and [Pengolodh](../P/Pengolodh.md), who is credited with many works that are the basis for *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>

A similar concept were the **Quettúri** ("Word-masters"), those Noldor who exercised [lámatyávë](Lámatyávë.md) creating new word systems.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In Valinor, Fëanor was chief of the linguistic loremasters.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The loremasters were not a distinct guild or gentle scribes; they comprised of the greatest kings, princes, and warriors such as Fëanor, the captains of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md), and [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lambengolmor* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word, glossed as "Loremasters of Tongues" in one manuscript.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup> In another manuscript, the alternative spelling *Lambeñgolmor* is seen, and also glossed as "lingustic loremasters".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) has noted that only the plural form is attested, of a possible singular form *Lambengolmo*. The word consists of *[lambe](Lambe.md)* ("tongue, language") + *ingolmo* ("loremaster").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
