**Lorri Holt** ([1953](../_/1953.md)) is an award-winning <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Bay" title="wikipedia:San Francisco Bay">Bay Area</a> stage actress.

## History {#History}

Holt graduated from Bennington College, in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bennington,_Vermont" title="wikipedia:Bennington, Vermont">Bennington, Vermont</a> with a Master's degree in Writing and Literature. She started acting in the seventies, and has since appeared as Angel in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angels_in_America:_A_Gay_Fantasia_on_National_Themes" title="wikipedia:Angels in America: A Gay Fantasia on National Themes">Angels in America</a></i>, Sister Aloysius in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Patrick_Shanley" title="wikipedia:John Patrick Shanley">John Patrick Shanley</a>'s <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doubt:_A_Parable" title="wikipedia:Doubt: A Parable">Doubt</a></i>, and also starred in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Vagina_Monologues" title="wikipedia:The Vagina Monologues">The Vagina Monologues</a></i>. In the world of film, she appeared as a nurse in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patch_Adams" title="wikipedia:Patch Adams">Patch Adams</a></i>.
