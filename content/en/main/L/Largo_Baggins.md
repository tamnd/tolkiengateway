**Largo Baggins** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Largo was a son of [Balbo Baggins](/wiki/Balbo_Baggins). He married [Tanta Hornblower](/wiki/Tanta_Hornblower), and had one child, [Fosco](/wiki/Fosco_Baggins "Fosco Baggins").<sup id="cite_ref-tree_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tree-1">[1]</a></sup>

The last years of his life the Shire suffered from the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Fell_Winter" title="Fell Winter">Fell Winter</a><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and around that time he lost his elder brother [Ponto](/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_I "Ponto Baggins I"). Largo died the next year, as did his younger sister [Lily](/wiki/Lily_Baggins "Lily Baggins").<sup id="cite_ref-tree_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tree-1">[1]</a></sup>
