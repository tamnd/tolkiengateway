**Lee Grinner Pace** (born [25 March](/wiki/25_March) [1979](/wiki/1979)) is an American actor who portrayed the Elvenking [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) in [*The Hobbit* film trilogy](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and *[The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Hunt_for_Gollum)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Twitter_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Twitter-2">[2]</a></sup> His break began playing Calpernia Addams, a male-to-female transsexual, in the film <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soldier%27s_Girl" title="wikipedia:Soldier&#39;s Girl">Soldier&#39;s Girl</a></i>, but he is best known for playing the starring role, Ned, in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pushing_Daisies" title="wikipedia:Pushing Daisies">Pushing Daisies</a></i>.
