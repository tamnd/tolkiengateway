On [9 December](/wiki/9_December) [1965](/wiki/1965), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Jane Dixon** of Ohio.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien is grateful for Dixon having sent him a copy of an article in the *Saturday Review* about the [Ace Books](/wiki/Ace_Books) edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. He also writes that neither he nor his publishers ([George Allen and Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin "Allen and Unwin")) intend to give [Walt Disney](/wiki/The_Walt_Disney_Company "The Walt Disney Company") permission to make a film of his books.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of the letter appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*.
