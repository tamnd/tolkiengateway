On [9 December](../_/9_December.md) [1965](../_/1965.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Jane Dixon** of Ohio.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien is grateful for Dixon having sent him a copy of an article in the *Saturday Review* about the [Ace Books](../A/Ace_Books.md) edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. He also writes that neither he nor his publishers ([George Allen and Unwin](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md)) intend to give [Walt Disney](../T/The_Walt_Disney_Company.md) permission to make a film of his books.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of the letter appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md)*.
