<i><b>loth</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "inflorescence, a head of small flowers",<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup> "flower, a single bloom",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> or "a flower (of defined shape)".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") LOT ("flower")<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

It is a cognate of [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *lóte* (large single flower).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
