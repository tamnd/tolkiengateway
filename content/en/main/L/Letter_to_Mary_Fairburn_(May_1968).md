In May [1968](/wiki/1968), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Mary Fairburn](/wiki/Mary_Fairburn)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Fairburn had sent illustrations of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) to Tolkien, who replies saying that he considers them to be "*splendid. They are better pictures in themselves and also show far more attention to the text than any that have yet been submitted to me*". Fairburn's illustrations included a pen-and-ink illustration of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) on the tower of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc), and "*a little sketch of [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum)*", and Tolkien continues: "*After seeing your specimens I am beginning to \[...\] think that an illustrated edition might be a good thing*". Tolkien ends the letter by telling Fairburn that he would be "*very pleased indeed*" to see her other pictures when they are finished.

- **Description:** Typewritten letter.
- **Publication:** [*The Times Literary Supplement* 14 September 2012](/wiki/The_Times_Literary_Supplement_14_September_2012 "The Times Literary Supplement 14 September 2012").
