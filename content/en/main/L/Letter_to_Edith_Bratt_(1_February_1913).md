On [1 February](../_/1_February.md) [1913](../_/1913.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Edith Bratt](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** The postcard picture depicts the dining hall at [Exeter College](../U/University_of_Oxford.md#Exeter_College), and Tolkien has marked his place at the table with an "X".
- **Publication:** A reproduction of the postcard appeared in *[The Tolkien Family Album](../T/The_Tolkien_Family_Album.md)*.
