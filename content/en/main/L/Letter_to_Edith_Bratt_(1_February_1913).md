On [1 February](/wiki/1_February) [1913](/wiki/1913), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Edith Bratt](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien")**.<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** The postcard picture depicts the dining hall at [Exeter College](/wiki/University_of_Oxford#Exeter_College "University of Oxford"), and Tolkien has marked his place at the table with an "X".
- **Publication:** A reproduction of the postcard appeared in *[The Tolkien Family Album](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Family_Album)*.
