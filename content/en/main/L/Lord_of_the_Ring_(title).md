**Lord of the Ring** or **Lord of the Rings** was a title for **[Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)**, the master of [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"). The singular form appeared twice when [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) tried to apply it to [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), but the [Wizard](/wiki/Istari "Istari") [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) corrected him.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The plural form appeared thrice: once spoken by Gandalf in the aforementioned conversation, a second time by [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) during the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and a third time by Frodo while writing the [Red Book of Westmarch](/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
