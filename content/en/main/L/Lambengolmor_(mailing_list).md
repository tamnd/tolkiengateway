**Lambengolmor** was a mailing list for the scholarly study and discussion of the invented languages of Tolkien, launched by [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md) of the [E.L.F.](../E/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship.md) on [29 May](../_/29_May.md) [2002](../_/2002.md).

With the shut down of <a class="external text" href="http://ph.help.yahoo.com/kb/yahoo-groups-shut-sln35505.html?redirect=true" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Yahoo Groups</a> in [15 December](../_/15_December.md) [2020](../_/2020.md), the group was closed. Carl Hostetter announced that it would reopen as a forum on the E.L.F website and that official archives would be made available<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> but neither has happened so far. An archive was made available by Harri Perälä though (see below).
