**LORD** is a text-adventure which covers *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, as well as utilising information from other sources.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup> It has 550 separate locations,<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup> and was the first text-adventure based on a book.<sup id="cite_ref-Brasslantern_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brasslantern-2">[2]</a></sup> The game was written and designed by Olli J. Paavola, at the time a student at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalto_University_School_of_Science_and_Technology" title="wikipedia:Aalto University School of Science and Technology">Helsinki University of Technology</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Brasslantern_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brasslantern-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup>
