On [30 June](../_/30_June.md) [1969](../_/1969.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Paul Bibire](../P/Paul_Bibire.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Comment:** Provided by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) and [Paul Bibire](../P/Paul_Bibire.md) for [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md)'s introduction to "[The Rivers and Beacon-hills of Gondor](../T/The_Rivers_and_Beacon-hills_of_Gondor~21248.md)".
- **Publication:** A partial transcription of the letter is found in [*Vinyar Tengwar*, issue 42](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_42.md), pp. 6–7. It was reprinted along with the rest of the article in *[The Nature of Middle-earth](../T/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth.md)* pp.  377—379.
