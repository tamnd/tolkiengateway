On [30 June](/wiki/30_June) [1969](/wiki/1969), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Paul Bibire](/wiki/Paul_Bibire)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Comment:** Provided by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) and [Paul Bibire](/wiki/Paul_Bibire) for [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter)'s introduction to "[The Rivers and Beacon-hills of Gondor](/wiki/The_Rivers_and_Beacon-hills_of_Gondor)".
- **Publication:** A partial transcription of the letter is found in [*Vinyar Tengwar*, issue 42](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_42 "Vinyar Tengwar 42"), pp. 6–7. It was reprinted along with the rest of the article in *[The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth)* pp.  377—379.
