The **Long Winter** (November [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758") to March [2759](/wiki/Third_Age_2759 "Third Age 2759")<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-1">[1]</a></sup>) was an extremely cold and long-lasting winter in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), covering [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland) and [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

The Winter came as cold and great snows from the north and the [East](/wiki/East). It lasted for 5 months. There was misery and death in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) and [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) but it affected less the region of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) south of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758"), [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) and [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) (led by [Wulf](/wiki/Wulf)) attacked [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) from both the north-west and east. Meanwhile, three great and long-prepared fleets of [Corsairs](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar "Corsairs of Umbar") and [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) began to assail the coasts of Gondor as far north as the [River Isen](/wiki/Isen "Isen"), and some of them sailed up to Isen and [Lefnui](/wiki/Lefnui) into Rohan and strengthened the Dunlendings. Because of the multiple problems, the two kingdoms were unable to help each other.<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Rohan {#Rohan}

The [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) were defeated by the invasion of the Dunlendings; [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan") [Helm Hammerhand](/wiki/Helm "Helm")'s son, [Haleth](</wiki/Haleth_(son_of_Helm)> "Haleth (son of Helm)"), was killed; and Wulf sat in [Meduseld](/wiki/Meduseld) declaring himself King.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Long Winter compounded the problems: Rohan was under snow for five months with both the Rohirrim and their enemies suffering grievously from famine. Meanwhile, King Helm took refuge in the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg) and the ravine behind (becoming known as [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep)); at [Yule](/wiki/Yule) a great counsel was held, and, against the King's advice, [Háma](</wiki/H%C3%A1ma_(son_of_Helm)> "Háma (son of Helm)"), Helm's younger son, went out in a sortie and was lost in the snow. King Helm himself would always blow his great horn, striking fear into the hearts of his enemies, and discreetly go out to enemy camps to slay enemies with his bare hands. During one such sortie he died, but remained standing upright.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the winter, the melted snow caused great floods with the [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash) becoming a vast fen. Rohan recovered very slowly.

### Gondor {#Gondor}

Although the Long Winter did not affect [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) directly, it did have an indirect effect: in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758"), three fleets of [Corsairs](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar "Corsairs of Umbar") and [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) began to assail the coasts of Gondor as far north as the [River Isen](/wiki/Isen "Isen");<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-2">[2]</a></sup> due to the dual perils of the Long Winter and the war with the Dunlendings, the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) were unable to send help.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-3">[3]</a></sup>

### The Shire {#The_Shire}

The Long Winter resulted in the deaths of many thousands of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) in [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire).<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-4">[4]</a></sup> Although the [wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards") [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) came to the aid of the Hobbits,<sup id="cite_ref-B_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B-5">[5]</a></sup> a dreadful famine followed the Long Winter, known as the [Days of Dearth](/wiki/Days_of_Dearth), which lasted into [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2760](/wiki/Third_Age_2760 "Third Age 2760").

The Long Winter had a more indirect effect on the history of The Shire and [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth): it was during the Long Winter that Gandalf first became fond of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) and their affairs, which indirectly led to Gandalf's selection of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) to go on the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor):

## Aftermath {#Aftermath}

Before the spring, [Beregond](</wiki/Beregond_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Beregond (Steward of Gondor)") had defeated the Corsair invaders of Gondor and afforded to send help to Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-2">[2]</a></sup> Come March [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2759](/wiki/Third_Age_2759 "Third Age 2759"), the Long Winter ended and [Fréaláf Hildeson](/wiki/Fr%C3%A9al%C3%A1f "Fréaláf"), Helm's sister-son, came from [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) and took back [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras), catching the Dunlendings unaware and killing [Wulf](/wiki/Wulf); the Dunlendings and Easterlings were driven out of Rohan (including [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard)).

Gondor began to recover to its former power, but it wasn't so easy for Rohan. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758"), the [Steward](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor"), [Beren](</wiki/Beren_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Beren (Steward of Gondor)") gave the keys to [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc) to the [wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards") [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman), hoping that he would help the Rohirrim.<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-2">[2]</a></sup>
