**The Litany of Loreto** is an unfinished translation of the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litany_of_the_Blessed_Virgin_Mary" title="wikipedia:Litany of the Blessed Virgin Mary">Litany of Loreto</a></i> from [Latin](Latin.md) into [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) made by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in the 1950s. It was published in [*Vinyar Tengwar*, Number 44](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_44.md), June 2002 in [2002](../_/2002.md), edited by [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md), [Arden R. Smith](../A/Arden_R._Smith.md), and [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md). It only covers the invocation to the Trinity and the first five titles of the [Virgin Mary](../V/Virgin_Mary.md).

In [1944](../_/1944.md), Tolkien recommended his son [Christopher](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) to learn "*the Litany of Loretto (with the prayer Sub tuum praesidium)*" by heart.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was therefore logical that Tolkien created his own Quenya translation, which immediately follows in the same manuscript as the *[Ortírielyanna](../O/Ortírielyanna.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<dd><i>A Eruion Mardorunando Eru órava omesse.</i></dd>

<dd><i>Amille Hristo arca atarme.</i></dd>

<dd>O Holy Spirit, God, have mercy on us.</dd>
<dd>O Holy Trinity, one God, have mercy on us.</dd>
<dd>O Holy Mary, pray for us.</dd>
<dd>Holy Mother of God, pray for us.</dd>
<dd>Holy Virgin of virgins, pray for us.</dd>
