**The Litany of Loreto** is an unfinished translation of the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litany_of_the_Blessed_Virgin_Mary" title="wikipedia:Litany of the Blessed Virgin Mary">Litany of Loreto</a></i> from [Latin](/wiki/Latin) into [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) made by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in the 1950s. It was published in [*Vinyar Tengwar*, Number 44](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_44 "Vinyar Tengwar 44"), June 2002 in [2002](/wiki/2002), edited by [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne), [Arden R. Smith](/wiki/Arden_R._Smith), and [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter). It only covers the invocation to the Trinity and the first five titles of the [Virgin Mary](/wiki/Virgin_Mary).

In [1944](/wiki/1944), Tolkien recommended his son [Christopher](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien "Christopher Tolkien") to learn "*the Litany of Loretto (with the prayer Sub tuum praesidium)*" by heart.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was therefore logical that Tolkien created his own Quenya translation, which immediately follows in the same manuscript as the *[Ortírielyanna](/wiki/Ort%C3%ADrielyanna)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
