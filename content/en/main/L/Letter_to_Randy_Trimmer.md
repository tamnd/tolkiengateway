On [7 April](/wiki/7_April) [1971](/wiki/1971), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Randy Trimmer**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien apologises for not being able to reply because he is working on *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.
- **Publication:** The letter was reproduced in [*Amon Dîn* (Vol.2, No.1)](</wiki/Amon_D%C3%AEn_(Vol.2,_No.1)> "Amon Dîn (Vol.2, No.1)").
