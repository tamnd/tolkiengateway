<i><b>linnon</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word appearing in "The [Lay of Leithian](Lay_of_Leithian.md) Recommenced" (ca. [1950](../_/1950.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-LB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LB-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Didier Willis](../D/Didier_Willis.md) has suggested that *linnon* is the 1st pres. form ("I sing") of an unattested verb *linna-* ("to sing").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Phrases {#Phrases}

"*le linnon im Tinúviel!*"<sup id="cite_ref-LB_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LB-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
