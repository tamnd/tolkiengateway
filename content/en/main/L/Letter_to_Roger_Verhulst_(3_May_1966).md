On [3 May](/wiki/3_May) [1966](/wiki/1966), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to Roger Verhulst**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** This card is a brief thank you note to the Eerdmans Publishing Co. for sending a book about [Charles Williams](/wiki/Charles_Williams) to Tolkien.
- **Publication:** None. A description of the letter has been provided in *[W.H. Auden: A Legacy](/wiki/W.H._Auden:_A_Legacy)* and on the website [Tolkien Library](/wiki/Tolkien_Library). A description of the letter has appeared in the catalogue *Christie's Fine Printed Books and Manuscripts 28 November 2011* (lot 258).
- **Description:** Embossed 76 Sandfield Road stationery card. Signed by J.R.R. Tolkien.
