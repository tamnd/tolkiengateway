On [3 May](../_/3_May.md) [1966](../_/1966.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_%2522The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%2522.md) to Roger Verhulst**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** This card is a brief thank you note to the Eerdmans Publishing Co. for sending a book about [Charles Williams](../C/Charles_Williams.md) to Tolkien.
- **Publication:** None. A description of the letter has been provided in *[W.H. Auden: A Legacy](../W/W.H._Auden%253A_A_Legacy.md)* and on the website [Tolkien Library](../T/Tolkien_Library.md). A description of the letter has appeared in the catalogue *Christie's Fine Printed Books and Manuscripts 28 November 2011* (lot 258).
- **Description:** Embossed 76 Sandfield Road stationery card. Signed by J.R.R. Tolkien.
