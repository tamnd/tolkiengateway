<i><b>lassë</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "leaf".

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i>Laurië lantar <b>lassi</b> súrinen</i> = Like gold fall **the leaves** in the wind<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
