On [5 December](/wiki/5_December) [1956](/wiki/1956), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to a Mr Britten**.<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-BBC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** In the letter, Tolkien appears to mainly be discussing a portrait at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King%27s_College,_Cambridge" title="wikipedia:King&#39;s College, Cambridge">King&#39;s College</a>, [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge).<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-BBC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Description:** One-page, handwritten letter.<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-BBC_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC-2">[2]</a></sup>
