On [21 October](../_/21_October.md) [1926](../_/1926.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_%2522The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%2522.md) to Willard G. Harding**.

- **Publication:** None
- **Description:** Tolkien replies to an enquiry from Willard G. Harding about the etymology of "sag".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
