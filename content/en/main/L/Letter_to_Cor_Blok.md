On [1 February](../_/1_February.md) [1962](../_/1962.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Cor Blok](../C/Cor_Blok.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** The letter is a reply from Tolkien, concerning an exhibition. Tolkien also asks if he may buy two pieces of artwork from Blok.
- **Publication:** In [2011](../_/2011.md), a facsimile of the letter appeared in *[A Tolkien Tapestry](../A/A_Tolkien_Tapestry.md)* (page \[6\]).
