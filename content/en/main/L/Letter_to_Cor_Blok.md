On [1 February](/wiki/1_February) [1962](/wiki/1962), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Cor Blok](/wiki/Cor_Blok)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** The letter is a reply from Tolkien, concerning an exhibition. Tolkien also asks if he may buy two pieces of artwork from Blok.
- **Publication:** In [2011](/wiki/2011), a facsimile of the letter appeared in *[A Tolkien Tapestry](/wiki/A_Tolkien_Tapestry)* (page \[6\]).
