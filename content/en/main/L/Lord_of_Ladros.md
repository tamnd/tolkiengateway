The **Lords of Ladros** ruled [Ladros](Ladros.md), a realm of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) in the highlands of [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Boromir, the older son of [Boron](../B/Boron.md), became the fourth Chieftain of the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md) in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [408](../F/First_Age_408.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-2">[2]</a></sup> Since his House had long been loyal in service to the [House of Finarfin](../H/House_of_Finarfin.md), in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [410](../F/First_Age_410.md),<sup id="cite_ref-WJWest_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJWest-3">[3]</a></sup> he was given the region of [Ladros](Ladros.md) in [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) by the [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) Elf-lords [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md) and [Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md), the younger sons of the Elven-king [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup>

Bregolas served with the Elf-lords [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md) and [Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md) in the northern land of [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md), and he was by their side when the forces of [Angband](../A/Angband.md) launched the sudden and devastating attack that would become known as the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [455](../F/First_Age_455.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-4">[4]</a></sup> Bregolas died quickly in that assault, but his sons survived to join [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-4">[4]</a></sup> With Dorthonion under [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s control, they became outlaws in the land of their father.<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-4">[4]</a></sup>

Before the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) expressed the hope that, should the [Union of Maedhros](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md) prove victorious, the fiefs previously held by the house of Bëor might be returned to his wife [Morwen](../M/Morwen.md), granddaughter of Bregolas, or through her to their son [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
