The **Lords of Ladros** ruled [Ladros](/wiki/Ladros), a realm of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) in the highlands of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion).<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Boromir, the older son of [Boron](/wiki/Boron), became the fourth Chieftain of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [408](/wiki/First_Age_408 "First Age 408").<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-2">[2]</a></sup> Since his House had long been loyal in service to the [House of Finarfin](/wiki/House_of_Finarfin), in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [410](/wiki/First_Age_410 "First Age 410"),<sup id="cite_ref-WJWest_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJWest-3">[3]</a></sup> he was given the region of [Ladros](/wiki/Ladros) in [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) by the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) Elf-lords [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod) and [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor), the younger sons of the Elven-king [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin).<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup>

Bregolas served with the Elf-lords [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod) and [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor) in the northern land of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), and he was by their side when the forces of [Angband](/wiki/Angband) launched the sudden and devastating attack that would become known as the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455").<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-4">[4]</a></sup> Bregolas died quickly in that assault, but his sons survived to join [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir).<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-4">[4]</a></sup> With Dorthonion under [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s control, they became outlaws in the land of their father.<sup id="cite_ref-Silm_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silm-4">[4]</a></sup>

Before the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) expressed the hope that, should the [Union of Maedhros](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros) prove victorious, the fiefs previously held by the house of Bëor might be returned to his wife [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen), granddaughter of Bregolas, or through her to their son [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
