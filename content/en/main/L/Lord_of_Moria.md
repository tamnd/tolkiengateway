The **Lord of Moria** ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): <i><b>Aran Moria</b></i>) is the title of the ruler of [Moria](../M/Moria.md) such as the [King of Khazad-dûm](../K/Kings_of_Khazad-dûm.md) who was typically the [King of Durin's Folk](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md).

The title *Aran Moria*, translated as **Lord of Moria**, refers to [Durin III](../D/Durin_III.md) and appears in the [Doors of Durin](../D/Doors_of_Durin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The kingdom had been abandoned in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1980](../T/Third_Age_1980.md) but [Balin](../B/Balin.md), a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) of the [House of Durin](../H/House_of_Durin.md), led [a small colony](../B/Balin's_Colony~64118.md) to reclaim it. His rule lasted only briefly ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2989](../T/Third_Age_2989.md) - [2995](../T/Third_Age_2995.md)) until his death. The [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md) phrase <i><b>Uzbad Khazaddûmu</b></i> meaning **Lord of Moria** was inscribed on [his tomb](../B/Balin's_Tomb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The phrase is translated as "Lord of Moria"; *uzbad* seems to be translated as "Lord", however since Khuzdul words of three consonants normally don't begin with a vowel, [Magnus Åberg](../M/Magnus_Åberg.md) proposes that the word is enclitic, and \**u-* is actually a prefix meaning "and".<sup id="cite_ref-magnus_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-magnus-3">[3]</a></sup>

If this is so, *uzbad* means "...and lord", the full phrase being "...son of [Fundin](../F/Fundin.md) *and* lord...". Åberg suggests that the basic form of "Lord" would be \**zâbad* and "Lord of Moria" would be \**zəbad Khazaddûmu*.<sup id="cite_ref-magnus_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-magnus-3">[3]</a></sup>

The ending <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=-u&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-u (page does not exist)">-u</a></i> in *Khazaddûmu* is probably "an ending that gives the noun an objective or locative meaning".<sup id="cite_ref-magnus_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-magnus-3">[3]</a></sup>
