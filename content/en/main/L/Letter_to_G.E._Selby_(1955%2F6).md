Sometime between late [1955](/wiki/1955) and early [1956](/wiki/1956), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to G.E. Selby**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien writes about the publication of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, which he says is not a trilogy and not an allegory. He also "comments on reviews, [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo)'s failure, the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) languages, and the poem *[Errantry](/wiki/Errantry)*".<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Publication:** Extracts from the letter appeared in <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sotheby%27s_Nineteenth_Century_and_Modern_First_Editions,_Presentation_Copies,_Autograph_Letters_and_Literary_Manuscripts_28-29_July_1977&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sotheby&#39;s Nineteenth Century and Modern First Editions, Presentation Copies, Autograph Letters and Literary Manuscripts 28-29 July 1977 (page does not exist)">Sotheby&#39;s Nineteenth Century and Modern First Editions, Presentation Copies, Autograph Letters and Literary Manuscripts 28-29 July 1977</a></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup> and in <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sotheby%27s_Catalogue_of_Autograph_Letters,_Literary_Manuscripts_and_Historical_Documents_16_October_1978&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sotheby&#39;s Catalogue of Autograph Letters, Literary Manuscripts and Historical Documents 16 October 1978 (page does not exist)">Sotheby&#39;s Catalogue of Autograph Letters, Literary Manuscripts and Historical Documents 16 October 1978</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> A summary of the letter was provided by [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) and [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
