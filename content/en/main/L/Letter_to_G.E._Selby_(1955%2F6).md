Sometime between late [1955](../_/1955.md) and early [1956](../_/1956.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to G.E. Selby**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien writes about the publication of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, which he says is not a trilogy and not an allegory. He also "comments on reviews, [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md)'s failure, the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) languages, and the poem *[Errantry](../E/Errantry.md)*".<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Publication:** Extracts from the letter appeared in <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Sotheby%27s_Nineteenth_Century_and_Modern_First_Editions,_Presentation_Copies,_Autograph_Letters_and_Literary_Manuscripts_28-29_July_1977&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sotheby&#39;s Nineteenth Century and Modern First Editions, Presentation Copies, Autograph Letters and Literary Manuscripts 28-29 July 1977 (page does not exist)">Sotheby&#39;s Nineteenth Century and Modern First Editions, Presentation Copies, Autograph Letters and Literary Manuscripts 28-29 July 1977</a></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup> and in <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Sotheby%27s_Catalogue_of_Autograph_Letters,_Literary_Manuscripts_and_Historical_Documents_16_October_1978&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sotheby&#39;s Catalogue of Autograph Letters, Literary Manuscripts and Historical Documents 16 October 1978 (page does not exist)">Sotheby&#39;s Catalogue of Autograph Letters, Literary Manuscripts and Historical Documents 16 October 1978</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> A summary of the letter was provided by [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) and [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
