On [28 July](/wiki/28_July) [1955](/wiki/1955), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Jennifer Brookes-Smith** (name also given as "Jennifer Paxman").<sup id="cite_ref-TB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject(s):** Replying to a letter written in March, Tolkien apologises for having been busy.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup> Among the subjects Tolkien discusses in the letter are the production of the Appendices and map for *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)* and an impending trip to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor): [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) and [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach) - identified as Italy: Venice and Assisi.<sup id="cite_ref-TB_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Two pages.<sup id="cite_ref-TB_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of the letter appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*. Extracts from the letter were reproduced in *[Christie's 20th Century Books and Manuscripts 2 December 2003](/wiki/Christie%27s_20th_Century_Books_and_Manuscripts_2_December_2003)*.
