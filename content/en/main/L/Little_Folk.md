**Little Folk** was a term for the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits), especially the **[Bree-hobbits](/wiki/Bree-hobbits)** (q.v.), contrasting them to the [Men](/wiki/Men_of_Bree "Men of Bree") (the [Big Folk](/wiki/Big_Folk)) as the two races live together in [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

At other points, [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> referred to the Hobbits of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) as "little folk". [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey)'s [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> also referred to the [Halflings](/wiki/Halflings) in general as such, and thus it may be assumed that the term was in common use.
