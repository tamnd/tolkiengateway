**Lindens** were trees in [Arda](/wiki/Arda). Some of the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) looked like lindens.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Lindens were apparently known for their fair and light leaves: both [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and [Nimrodel](/wiki/Nimrodel)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> were said in song to move like linden-leaves.
