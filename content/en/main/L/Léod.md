**Léod** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2459](/wiki/Third_Age_2459 "Third Age 2459") - [2501](/wiki/Third_Age_2501 "Third Age 2501")) was a [Lord of the Éothéod](/wiki/Lord_of_the_%C3%89oth%C3%A9od).

## History {#History}

Léod was a great horseman and during his rule he captured a white foal that was wild and proud. When the horse was grown, Léod tried to break him, but instead was thrown off of the horse and he hit his head on a rock and died (he was but 42 and his son 16). His son, [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl), later tamed the horse, naming him [Felaróf](/wiki/Felar%C3%B3f). He would become the first of the great *[Mearas](/wiki/Mearas)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Léod* means "prince",<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup> "man" or "poet"<sup id="cite_ref-Bosworth_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bosworth-3">[3]</a></sup> in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English)<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Bosworth_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bosworth-3">[3]</a></sup>.

Tolkien originally referred to Léod as Garman.
