<i><b>Laer Cú Beleg</b></i> was a song made by [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) in memory of his friend [Beleg Cúthalion](/wiki/Beleg "Beleg"), whom he unintentionally slew.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

With the aid of [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor), Beleg freed Turin from Orc captivity during a thunderstorm. Upon being freed, however, Turin mistook Beleg for an Orc and murdered him with Beleg's own sword, [Anglachel](/wiki/Anglachel). Upon realizing the horror of what he had just done, Turin fell into a frozen state of shock and allowed himself to be led by Gwindor to the [Pools of Ivrin](/wiki/Pools_of_Ivrin). Once there, he drank from the pure waters and was healed, and was at last able to weep, mourning Beleg. Then he composed and sang this lament as a form of release in his grief, singing it aloud regardless of peril.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleg_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleg-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Laer Cú Beleg* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Song of the Great Bow",<sup id="cite_ref-Beleg_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleg-2">[2]</a></sup> from *[laer](/wiki/Laer "Laer")* ("song"), *[cú](/wiki/C%C3%BA "Cú")* ("bow") and *[beleg](</wiki/Beleg_(word)> "Beleg (word)")* ("great, mighty; large, big").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The concept of Túrin composing an elegy for his friend is present since early stages of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"). It appears for the first time in *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*, in which it is called the **Bowman's Friendship**, and it is said that the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) remembered it in [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), and that it was a song used in battles against [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The same name is given to it in the following versions of the story in the *[Sketch of the Mythology](/wiki/Sketch_of_the_Mythology)*<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](/wiki/Quenta_Noldorinwa)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
