On [2 April](/wiki/2_April) [1958](/wiki/1958), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Peter Alford**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien describes the recent dinner in Rotterdam and encloses a copy of the menu and programme, with manuscript alterations.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Excerpts appeared in *[Sotheby's Valuable Printed Books and Manuscripts 13 December 2001](/wiki/Sotheby%27s_Valuable_Printed_Books_and_Manuscripts_13_December_2001)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
