In November [1969](/wiki/1969), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Leslie Holloway**, producer of the event "An Afternoon in Middle-earth".<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Publication:** Parts of the letter were published in the programme booklet *[An Afternoon in Middle-earth](/wiki/An_Afternoon_in_Middle-earth)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Contents:** Tolkien "*writes to decline the invitation, and comments on his connections with the West Midlands*".<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
