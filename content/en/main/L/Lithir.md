The **Lithir** River<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a tributary of the [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). Its source was in the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin) south of [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim). It flowed southeast until it raced over falls and joined the [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) about halfway between [Tol Sirion](/wiki/Tol_Sirion) and the [Dry River](/wiki/Dry_River).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

When [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) left [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum), [Asgon](/wiki/Asgon) guided him over the mountains and down the course of the Lithir to reach the vale of Sirion.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lithir* probably means "Ash River" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), from *[lith](/wiki/Lith "Lith")* ("ash") and *[sîr](/wiki/S%C3%AEr "Sîr")* ("river").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
