The **Lithir** River<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a tributary of the [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md) in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). Its source was in the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) south of [Mithrim](../M/Mithrim.md). It flowed southeast until it raced over falls and joined the [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md) about halfway between [Tol Sirion](../T/Tol_Sirion.md) and the [Dry River](../D/Dry_River.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

When [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) left [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md), [Asgon](../A/Asgon.md) guided him over the mountains and down the course of the Lithir to reach the vale of Sirion.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lithir* probably means "Ash River" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), from *[lith](Lith.md)* ("ash") and *[sîr](../S/Sîr.md)* ("river").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
