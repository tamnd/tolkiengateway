The **Lord of Andúnië** was the [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) lord who ruled the shoreland region of [Andustar](../A/Andustar.md) in western [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) from the great city of [Andúnië](../A/Andúnië.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

As rulers of one of the major regions of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), the Lords of Andúnië were members of the [Council of the Sceptre](../C/Council_of_the_Sceptre.md). Being the chief councellors of the [Kings of Númenor](../K/Kings_of_Númenor.md), they were the second most honourable lords in the kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup> From their beginning, these lords revered and were ever-loyal to the [Kings of Númenor](../K/Kings_of_Númenor.md); in addition to this, they had great love for the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) and the [Valar](../V/Valar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup>

Their symbol of office was a [silver sceptre](../S/Sceptre_of_Annúminas.md) which was afterwards held by the [Kings of Arnor](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The house inherited the [Ring of Barahir](../R/Ring_of_Barahir.md) (given by [Tar-Elendil](../T/Tar-Elendil.md) to his daughter [Silmarien](../S/Silmarien.md)),<sup id="cite_ref-note_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-note-5">[5]</a></sup> the original [Elendilmir](../E/Elendilmir.md) from [Silmariën](../S/Silmariën.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-6">[6]</a></sup> and the [palantíri](../P/Palantíri.md) of [Amandil](../A/Amandil.md) (gifted by the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-7">[7]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Tar-Elendil](../T/Tar-Elendil.md), the fourth [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md), was the first King whose eldest child was a daughter. [Silmariën](../S/Silmariën.md), as a woman, could not receive the [Sceptre of Númenor](../S/Sceptre_of_Númenor.md) at the time because of the [law of succession](Law_of_Succession_in_Númenor.md) that then existed in the realm; she could not succeed her father as [Queen of Númenor](../Q/Queen_of_Númenor.md) and instead her younger brother, [Tar-Meneldur](../T/Tar-Meneldur.md), took up the Sceptre.<sup id="cite_ref-UTWife_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTWife-8">[8]</a></sup> Instead, Silmariën married [Elatan of Andúnië](../E/Elatan.md) and their son was [Valandil](<../V/Valandil_(Lord_of_Andúnië).md>), who became the first of the Lords of Andúnië.<sup id="cite_ref-UTWife_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTWife-8">[8]</a></sup>

When the [Shadow](../S/Shadow.md) fell on [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), the Lords remained [Faithful](../F/Faithful.md) to the Valar and covertly gave aid to the other Faithful, but retained their office.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup> They could not declare their true allegiance openly, but they attempted to dissuade the [Kings of Númenor](../K/Kings_of_Númenor.md) from their wickedness with wiser counsel.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup>

In these dark times, [Lindórië](Lindórië.md), daughter of the 14th Lord of Andúnië, wed [Gimilzagar](../G/Gimilzagar.md), a descendant of [Tar-Calmacil](../T/Tar-Calmacil.md). Lindórië bore a daughter, [Inzilbêth](../I/Inzilbêth.md), and raised her in the ways of the Faithful.

Once grown to womanhood Inzilbêth wed [Ar-Gimilzôr](../A/Ar-Gimilzôr.md)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and bore him two sons, [Inziladûn](../I/Inziladûn.md) and [Gimilkhâd](../G/Gimilkhâd.md). She passed her Faithful ways on to her elder son Inziladûn, who took the throne as [Tar-Palantir](../T/Tar-Palantir.md). He was the last Faithful King of Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Amandil](../A/Amandil.md) was the last Lord of Andúnië and a dear friend of [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md) in their youth. He was stripped of his titles for supporting the Faithful when [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) came to power in Númenor. The Lordship of Andúnië was not revived before the [Downfall](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) claimed Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup>

Before the Downfall, a family of Faithful related to [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) settled in [Belfalas](../B/Belfalas.md). After the Downfall Elendil granted the title [Prince](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) to the lord of that family who had a stronghold on the high promontory of Dol Amroth. From that ancient family arose the [House of Dol Amroth](../H/House_of_Dol_Amroth.md) and its hereditary Princes.<sup id="cite_ref-prince_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prince-10">[10]</a></sup>

[Elendil](../E/Elendil.md), son of [Amandil](../A/Amandil.md), escaped Númenor before the Downfall with his sons and followers. He and his sons [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md) founded the kingdoms of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) when they arrived in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Though the Lordship of Andúnië would never return, the House of its Lords lived on into the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md) in the distant descendants of Elendil, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and his son [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-note_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Name of House {#Name_of_House}

All known descendants of Silmariën and Elatan are also descendants of Valandil, whose male-line descendants were known as the "House of Valandil."<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Later generations of the male-line descendants of Silmariën and Elatan were also named as members of the "House of the Lords of Andúnië."<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
