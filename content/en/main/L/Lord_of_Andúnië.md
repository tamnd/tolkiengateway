The **Lord of Andúnië** was the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") lord who ruled the shoreland region of [Andustar](/wiki/Andustar) in western [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) from the great city of [Andúnië](/wiki/And%C3%BAni%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

As rulers of one of the major regions of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), the Lords of Andúnië were members of the [Council of the Sceptre](/wiki/Council_of_the_Sceptre). Being the chief councellors of the [Kings of Númenor](/wiki/Kings_of_N%C3%BAmenor), they were the second most honourable lords in the kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup> From their beginning, these lords revered and were ever-loyal to the [Kings of Númenor](/wiki/Kings_of_N%C3%BAmenor); in addition to this, they had great love for the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) and the [Valar](/wiki/Valar).<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup>

Their symbol of office was a [silver sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre_of_Ann%C3%BAminas "Sceptre of Annúminas") which was afterwards held by the [Kings of Arnor](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The house inherited the [Ring of Barahir](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir) (given by [Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil) to his daughter [Silmarien](/wiki/Silmarien)),<sup id="cite_ref-note_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-note-5">[5]</a></sup> the original [Elendilmir](/wiki/Elendilmir) from [Silmariën](/wiki/Silmari%C3%ABn),<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-6">[6]</a></sup> and the [palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri") of [Amandil](/wiki/Amandil) (gifted by the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar)).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-7">[7]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil), the fourth [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor), was the first King whose eldest child was a daughter. [Silmariën](/wiki/Silmari%C3%ABn), as a woman, could not receive the [Sceptre of Númenor](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor) at the time because of the [law of succession](/wiki/Law_of_Succession_in_N%C3%BAmenor "Law of Succession in Númenor") that then existed in the realm; she could not succeed her father as [Queen of Númenor](/wiki/Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor) and instead her younger brother, [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur), took up the Sceptre.<sup id="cite_ref-UTWife_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTWife-8">[8]</a></sup> Instead, Silmariën married [Elatan of Andúnië](/wiki/Elatan "Elatan") and their son was [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)> "Valandil (Lord of Andúnië)"), who became the first of the Lords of Andúnië.<sup id="cite_ref-UTWife_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTWife-8">[8]</a></sup>

When the [Shadow](/wiki/Shadow) fell on [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), the Lords remained [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) to the Valar and covertly gave aid to the other Faithful, but retained their office.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup> They could not declare their true allegiance openly, but they attempted to dissuade the [Kings of Númenor](/wiki/Kings_of_N%C3%BAmenor) from their wickedness with wiser counsel.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup>

In these dark times, [Lindórië](/wiki/Lind%C3%B3ri%C3%AB), daughter of the 14th Lord of Andúnië, wed [Gimilzagar](/wiki/Gimilzagar), a descendant of [Tar-Calmacil](/wiki/Tar-Calmacil). Lindórië bore a daughter, [Inzilbêth](/wiki/Inzilb%C3%AAth), and raised her in the ways of the Faithful.

Once grown to womanhood Inzilbêth wed [Ar-Gimilzôr](/wiki/Ar-Gimilz%C3%B4r)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and bore him two sons, [Inziladûn](/wiki/Inzilad%C3%BBn) and [Gimilkhâd](/wiki/Gimilkh%C3%A2d). She passed her Faithful ways on to her elder son Inziladûn, who took the throne as [Tar-Palantir](/wiki/Tar-Palantir). He was the last Faithful King of Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Amandil](/wiki/Amandil) was the last Lord of Andúnië and a dear friend of [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n) in their youth. He was stripped of his titles for supporting the Faithful when [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) came to power in Númenor. The Lordship of Andúnië was not revived before the [Downfall](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Downfall of Númenor") claimed Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup>

Before the Downfall, a family of Faithful related to [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) settled in [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas). After the Downfall Elendil granted the title [Prince](/wiki/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth "Princes of Dol Amroth") to the lord of that family who had a stronghold on the high promontory of Dol Amroth. From that ancient family arose the [House of Dol Amroth](/wiki/House_of_Dol_Amroth) and its hereditary Princes.<sup id="cite_ref-prince_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prince-10">[10]</a></sup>

[Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), son of [Amandil](/wiki/Amandil), escaped Númenor before the Downfall with his sons and followers. He and his sons [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) founded the kingdoms of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) when they arrived in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Though the Lordship of Andúnië would never return, the House of its Lords lived on into the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) in the distant descendants of Elendil, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and his son [Eldarion](/wiki/Eldarion).<sup id="cite_ref-note_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Name of House {#Name_of_House}

All known descendants of Silmariën and Elatan are also descendants of Valandil, whose male-line descendants were known as the "House of Valandil."<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Later generations of the male-line descendants of Silmariën and Elatan were also named as members of the "House of the Lords of Andúnië."<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
