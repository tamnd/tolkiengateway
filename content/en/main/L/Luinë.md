<i><b>luinë</b></i> (pl. *luini*) is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "blue".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The word occurred mainly in [Noldorin](../E/Exilic_Quenya.md) Quenya; the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) in their [dialect](../V/Vanyarin.md) used <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ulban&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ulban (page does not exist)">ulban</a></i> for blue, adopted and adapted from [Valarin](../V/Valarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="../N/Namárië.md" title="Namárië">Vardo tellumar nu <b>luini</b></a></i> = "beneath the blue vaults of [Varda](../V/Varda.md)"
- <i><a href="../H/Helluin.md" title="Helluin">Hel<b>luin</b></a></i> = "Ice Blue"
- <i><a href="Luinil.md" title="Luinil"><b>Luin</b>il</a></i> = "Blue Star"
- <i><a href="../M/Menelluin.md" title="Menelluin">Menel<b>luin</b></a></i> = "Sky-blue"

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *[luin](Luin.md)*

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In some linguistic manuscripts, [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) experimented with *luine* being a cognate of [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *[duin](../D/Duin.md)* ("river"), but noted that *luine* as meaning "river" was not used. [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md) has suggested that *luine* meaning "river" perhaps only survived in compounds as <i><b>-duinë</b></i> (cf. *[Nunduinë](../N/Nunduinë.md#Etymology)*).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
