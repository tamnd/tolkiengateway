<i><b>luinë</b></i> (pl. *luini*) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "blue".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The word occurred mainly in [Noldorin](/wiki/Exilic_Quenya "Exilic Quenya") Quenya; the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) in their [dialect](/wiki/Vanyarin "Vanyarin") used <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ulban&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ulban (page does not exist)">ulban</a></i> for blue, adopted and adapted from [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Nam%C3%A1ri%C3%AB" title="Namárië">Vardo tellumar nu <b>luini</b></a></i> = "beneath the blue vaults of [Varda](/wiki/Varda)"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Helluin" title="Helluin">Hel<b>luin</b></a></i> = "Ice Blue"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Luinil" title="Luinil"><b>Luin</b>il</a></i> = "Blue Star"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Menelluin" title="Menelluin">Menel<b>luin</b></a></i> = "Sky-blue"

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[luin](/wiki/Luin "Luin")*

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In some linguistic manuscripts, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") experimented with *luine* being a cognate of [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[duin](/wiki/Duin "Duin")* ("river"), but noted that *luine* as meaning "river" was not used. [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) has suggested that *luine* meaning "river" perhaps only survived in compounds as <i><b>-duinë</b></i> (cf. *[Nunduinë](/wiki/Nunduin%C3%AB#Etymology)*).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
