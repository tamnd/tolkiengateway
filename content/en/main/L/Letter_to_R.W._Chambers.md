On [21 December](../_/21_December.md) [1933](../_/1933.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [R.W. Chambers](../R/R.W._Chambers.md)**. Either with the letter or separately, Tolkien also sends Chambers a manuscript version of his poem [Visio de Doworst](../V/Visio_de_Doworst.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Christmas greetings.
- **Description:** 1 page, handwritten.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A transcript of the letter was published in the [*Monash Review*, Nr. 3 (1975)](<../M/Monash_Review_3_(1975).md>).

## Transcription {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>My Dear Chambers,<br/><br/>Thank you for your note. I am sorry you are — or were — unwell. My very best wishes for a happy feast and for all blessings in 1934.<br/><br/>Yrs very sincerely,<br/>J.R.R. Tolkien<br/><br/>I send you - it may possibly amuse you — a report on last year&#39;s Examinations in Oxford: held by myself, <a href="../C/Charles_Leslie_Wrenn.md" title="Charles Leslie Wrenn">Wrenn</a>, Brett-Smith &amp; <a href="../C/C.S._Lewis.md" title="C.S. Lewis">C.S. Lewis</a> (whose <i>Pilgrim&#39;s Regress</i> you may have read). The howlers are genuine except the nonsense about Percy S.<br/><br/>Yrs<br/>J.R.R.T.
</p>
</blockquote>
