On [21 December](../_/21_December.md) [1955](../_/1955.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Rachael Feichtmeir**.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup> <sup id="cite_ref-Marq2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq2-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Description:** 2 page, handwritten letter.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Subject:** Reply to Fan Letter.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq2-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** None.
- **Archive:** The original letter is kept at [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md) as gift of Ms. Rachael Balyeat in May 2014.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Summary {#Summary}

This letter was a reply to Rachael Feichtmeir's letter from [September](../S/September.md) 1955 answering her questions about [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) and offered insights into his writing process.
