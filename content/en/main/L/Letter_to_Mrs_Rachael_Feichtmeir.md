On [21 December](/wiki/21_December) [1955](/wiki/1955), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Rachael Feichtmeir**.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup> <sup id="cite_ref-Marq2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq2-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Description:** 2 page, handwritten letter.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Subject:** Reply to Fan Letter.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq2-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** None.
- **Archive:** The original letter is kept at [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University) as gift of Ms. Rachael Balyeat in May 2014.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Summary {#Summary}

This letter was a reply to Rachael Feichtmeir's letter from [September](/wiki/September) 1955 answering her questions about [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) and offered insights into his writing process.
