The **Last Desert** was a possibly fictitious place located within the "East of East" where wild [Were-worms](../W/Were-worms.md) possibly dwelled.<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbit_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbit-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Last Desert is mentioned only in *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*. When [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) insisted to [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) that he was fit to be the burglar on their quest, he said he was willing to travel to the "East of East" which, according to him, was the location of a region known as the "Last Desert" where the wild [Were-worms](../W/Were-worms.md) lived.<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbit_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbit-1">[1]</a></sup> However, this is the only mention of the Last Desert and the Were-worms, and [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) (who rarely left [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md)) had an extensive folklore, so the existence of the Last Desert is debatable.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>who?</i></a>]</sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest drafts of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, Bilbo offered to walk from [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) "to *\[cancelled: <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_Kush" title="wikipedia:Hindu Kush">Hindu Kush</a>\]* the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gobi_Desert" title="wikipedia:Gobi Desert">Great Desert of Gobi</a> and fight the Wild Wire worm(s) of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China" title="wikipedia:China">Chinese</a>."<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In a slightly later version, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) altered this to say "to the last desert in the East and fight the Wild Wireworms of the Chinese",<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and in the final version it was altered once more to say "to the East of East and fight the wild [Were-worms](../W/Were-worms.md) in the Last Desert.".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Although the Last Desert is mentioned only in *The Hobbit*, there is another mention of the greater region where it was located. In the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](../Q/Quenta_Noldorinwa.md)*, an early version of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, the first [Men](../M/Men.md) were said to have awoke in the land of [Eruman](../E/Eruman.md), which lay in the "East of East."<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
