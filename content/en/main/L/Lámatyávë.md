**Lámatyávë** (pl. **lámatyáver**) is a noun in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) which refers to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonaesthetics" title="wikipedia:Phonaesthetics">phonaesthesia</a> of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md). It means "sound-taste" and refers to individual pleasure in the sounds and forms of words.

Lámatyávë was practiced when Elves [named their children](../E/Essë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The names of persons and places in this story [...] [have] given perhaps more pleasure to more readers than anything else in it.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>J.R.R. Tolkien<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

Sound-taste was an important factor in [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md)'s created names and languages and by extension, his [legendarium](Legendarium.md). His first contact with [Welsh](../W/Welsh.md) "pierced his linguistic heart"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Finnish](../F/Finnish.md) language was to him like "bottles of an amazing wine of a kind and flavour never tasted before" that "quite intoxicated" him.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> These two languages have been the basis for his major [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) languages, [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) and [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).

Throughout his work, Tolkien emphasizes on how the [Elven languages](../E/Elvish.md) are heard as beautiful by the characters; he intended them to be particularly "pleasant".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
