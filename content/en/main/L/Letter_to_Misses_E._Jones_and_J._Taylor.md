On [20 October](/wiki/20_October) [1958](/wiki/1958), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Misses E. Jones and J. Taylor**.<sup id="cite_ref-Sothebys_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sothebys-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Description:** 4 pages, small 4to, autograph letter.<sup id="cite_ref-Sothebys_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sothebys-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the auction {#From_the_auction}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>[In the letter, Tolkien is] apologising for his lack of reply and describing himself as &#34;a much harassed man&#34;. He notes that since publication of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> &#34;it was discovered how I had wasted my time for the last many years&#34;, stating his &#34;intention of publishing the legends of the Beginning and the <a href="/wiki/First_Age" title="First Age">First</a> and <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">Second Age</a>&#34;, that &#34;co-ordinating the large mass of written material&#34; will be &#34;a lot of work&#34; and that &#34;with luck (which has not notably attended me since <a href="/wiki/1956" title="1956">1956</a>) they may appear in <a href="/wiki/1960" title="1960">1960</a>&#34;.
</p>

</blockquote>
