On [3 July](/wiki/3_July) [1961](/wiki/1961), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Mr. Gilmore**. The one-page typed and signed letter, in which Tolkien explains why there cannot be [a sequel](/wiki/The_New_Shadow "The New Shadow") to *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, was put up for auction in early 2019 at Bonhams.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
