**Landroval** was one of the [Great Eagles](/wiki/Great_Eagles). He was the brother of [Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir) and a descendant of [Thorondor](/wiki/Thorondor).

## History {#History}

A descendant of [Thorondor](/wiki/Thorondor), it is said that Landroval was his vassal in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) and with his brother [Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir) helped him rescue [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) from [Angband](/wiki/Angband).<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) he stayed out of the war for most of the conflict, but he followed his brother to the [Battle of the Morannon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon), where he and his vassals helped turn the tide.

After the Ring was destroyed, Landroval, Gwaihir and [Meneldor](/wiki/Meneldor), flew into [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) to retrieve [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") from the ruins of [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Landroval* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Broad Winged", containing the element *[roval](/wiki/Rov- "Rov-")* ("wing").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
