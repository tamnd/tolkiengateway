The **Lords of Dor-lómin** ruled [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md), a realm of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) in northern [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).

## History {#History}

As a young man, [Hador](../H/Hador.md) gained the favour of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md), [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md), while serving in his household. Fingolfin granted him the lordship of Dor-lómin, where Hador then gathered his kin. His son and grandson succeeded him as Lords and were vassals of the High King [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup>

Within their own household, the Lords of Dor-lómin spoke [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), but at times still used their [native tongue](../T/Taliska.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup> They were on good terms with the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md), and formed marital ties with the houses of [Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md) and [Haleth](../H/House_of_Haleth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Dor-lómin ceased to exist as a realm of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) following the battle of the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), when all of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) was occupied by [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md), in the service of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), who enslaved the Edain.<sup id="cite_ref-Departure_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Departure-3">[3]</a></sup> Húrin was held prisoner in [Angband](../A/Angband.md) while his son [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) was sent away to [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Departure_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Departure-3">[3]</a></sup> From this point, Dór-lomin was ruled by [Brodda](../B/Brodda.md) of the Easterlings, until he was killed by Túrin in his own hall. Despite a brief uprising by the Edain, the Easterlings retained control of the land.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
