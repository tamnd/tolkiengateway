The **Lords of Dor-lómin** ruled [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min), a realm of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) in northern [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).

## History {#History}

As a young man, [Hador](/wiki/Hador) gained the favour of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor), while serving in his household. Fingolfin granted him the lordship of Dor-lómin, where Hador then gathered his kin. His son and grandson succeeded him as Lords and were vassals of the High King [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon).<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup>

Within their own household, the Lords of Dor-lómin spoke [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), but at times still used their [native tongue](/wiki/Taliska "Taliska").<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup> They were on good terms with the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar), and formed marital ties with the houses of [Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor "House of Bëor") and [Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth "House of Haleth").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Dor-lómin ceased to exist as a realm of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) following the battle of the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), when all of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) was occupied by [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings), in the service of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), who enslaved the Edain.<sup id="cite_ref-Departure_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Departure-3">[3]</a></sup> Húrin was held prisoner in [Angband](/wiki/Angband) while his son [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) was sent away to [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-Departure_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Departure-3">[3]</a></sup> From this point, Dór-lomin was ruled by [Brodda](/wiki/Brodda) of the Easterlings, until he was killed by Túrin in his own hall. Despite a brief uprising by the Edain, the Easterlings retained control of the land.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
