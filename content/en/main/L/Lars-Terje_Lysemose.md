**Lars-Terje Lysemose** (born [1974](../_/1974.md)) is a Danish journalist, editor and educator. A member of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) in the early 1990s, he founded what is now known as [Imladris, Danmarks Tolkienforening](../I/Imladris,_Danmarks_Tolkienforening.md) (then, Danmarks Tolkienforening) together with <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Inge_S%C3%B8b%C3%A6k&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Inge Søbæk (page does not exist)">Inge Søbæk</a> in 1993.

He also became the editor for *[Athelas](<../A/Athelas_(journal).md>)*, the journal of the Society. As the [Appendices](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Appendices.md) were not included in the Danish edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, Lysemose published a translation running through several issues of *Athelas*.

In 1997, Lysemose moved to the United States and left the post as editor for *Athelas*. Lysemose has lived in Sweden and is now back in Copenhagen.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
