**Lars-Terje Lysemose** (born [1974](/wiki/1974)) is a Danish journalist, editor and educator. A member of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) in the early 1990s, he founded what is now known as [Imladris, Danmarks Tolkienforening](/wiki/Imladris,_Danmarks_Tolkienforening) (then, Danmarks Tolkienforening) together with <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Inge_S%C3%B8b%C3%A6k&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Inge Søbæk (page does not exist)">Inge Søbæk</a> in 1993.

He also became the editor for *[Athelas](</wiki/Athelas_(journal)> "Athelas (journal)")*, the journal of the Society. As the [Appendices](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Appendices "The Lord of the Rings Appendices") were not included in the Danish edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, Lysemose published a translation running through several issues of *Athelas*.

In 1997, Lysemose moved to the United States and left the post as editor for *Athelas*. Lysemose has lived in Sweden and is now back in Copenhagen.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
