The **Langwell** was one of the two rivers, along with [Greylin](../G/Greylin.md), that met in the northern [Vale of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md) to form the beginnings of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) River. The Langwell flowed east from the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1977](../T/Third_Age_1977.md), [Frumgar](../F/Frumgar.md) led the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) from the middle to the upper part of the Vale of Anduin<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> to escape from the growing shadow of [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md), and to take advantage of the fall of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) in [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Upon their arrival, they built their capital city of [Framsburg](../F/Framsburg.md) (named after Frumgar's son [Fram](../F/Fram.md)) between the Langwell and the Greylin.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The Éothéod left the vicinity of the Langwell in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md) when they became the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md), settling in [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayals in adaptations {#Portrayals_in_adaptations}

<b>2022: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Langwell falls from the <a href="../M/Misty_Mountains.md" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a> into the large &#34;Dvergvát&#34; lake that forms a natural defensive barrier beyond the gates of <a href="../G/Gundabad.md" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a>. Beyond the lake, Langwell and the nearby Wyrmsgráf river both drop from a great height in two spectacular waterfalls collectively called the &#34;Twainmantle&#34;. Together with the nearby &#34;Reikfoss of <a href="../G/Greylin.md" title="Greylin">Greylin</a>&#34; waterfall, they are known as &#34;the Three Sisters&#34; and raise a large cloud of mist into the air, giving the surrounding area its name: &#34;Misthallow&#34;.</dd></dl>
