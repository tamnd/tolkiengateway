The **Langwell** was one of the two rivers, along with [Greylin](/wiki/Greylin), that met in the northern [Vale of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin "Vales of Anduin") to form the beginnings of the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) River. The Langwell flowed east from the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1977](/wiki/Third_Age_1977 "Third Age 1977"), [Frumgar](/wiki/Frumgar) led the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) from the middle to the upper part of the Vale of Anduin<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> to escape from the growing shadow of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur), and to take advantage of the fall of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) in [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Upon their arrival, they built their capital city of [Framsburg](/wiki/Framsburg) (named after Frumgar's son [Fram](/wiki/Fram)) between the Langwell and the Greylin.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The Éothéod left the vicinity of the Langwell in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510") when they became the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), settling in [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayals in adaptations {#Portrayals_in_adaptations}

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Langwell falls from the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a> into the large &#34;Dvergvát&#34; lake that forms a natural defensive barrier beyond the gates of <a href="/wiki/Gundabad" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a>. Beyond the lake, Langwell and the nearby Wyrmsgráf river both drop from a great height in two spectacular waterfalls collectively called the &#34;Twainmantle&#34;. Together with the nearby &#34;Reikfoss of <a href="/wiki/Greylin" title="Greylin">Greylin</a>&#34; waterfall, they are known as &#34;the Three Sisters&#34; and raise a large cloud of mist into the air, giving the surrounding area its name: &#34;Misthallow&#34;.</dd></dl>
