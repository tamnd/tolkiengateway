The <i><b>Line of Elros</b></i> was a document that listed the names of the [Kings](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) and [Queens](../R/Ruling_Queen_of_Númenor.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), and contained a brief account of their birthdate, length of rule, death date, and a summary of events that occurred during their reigns. The *Line of Elros* was probably made in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) (most likely in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md)) after the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md), as it contains a reference to [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md)'s writing of the *[Akallabêth](../A/Akallabêth.md)*, which is an account of that disaster.
