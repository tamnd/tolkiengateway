The <i><b>Line of Elros</b></i> was a document that listed the names of the [Kings](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") and [Queens](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Ruling Queen of Númenor") of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), and contained a brief account of their birthdate, length of rule, death date, and a summary of events that occurred during their reigns. The *Line of Elros* was probably made in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) (most likely in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)) after the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor), as it contains a reference to [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil)'s writing of the *[Akallabêth](/wiki/Akallab%C3%AAth)*, which is an account of that disaster.
