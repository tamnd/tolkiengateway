On [28 July](../_/28_July.md) [1955](../_/1955.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Mrs Dixey** of Cape Town, [South Africa](../../category/S/South_Africa.md).<sup id="cite_ref-hammond_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hammond-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UpFront_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UpFront-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien expresses the hope that Mrs Dixey will enjoy the forthcoming *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-UpFront_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UpFront-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Handwritten letter, Tolkien's personal stationery.<sup id="cite_ref-UpFront_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UpFront-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

"I do not suppose I will write any more about [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md), but the success of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* has been quite as surprising to [the publishers](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md) as to me (possibly more so) that it seems likely now that they will wish to publish the legend of the [First](../F/First_Age.md) and [Second Ages](../S/Second_Age.md) (written first but refused)." The rejected material in question, of course, was published as *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*. Tolkien admits rather reluctantly to his correspondent that he is a native of South Africa. "I...belong really to the N. West of the Old World," he says, "...\[but\] I suppose that even in '[The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md)' they had heard some tales of the [far south](../F/Far_Harad.md)."<sup id="cite_ref-hammond_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hammond-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UpFront_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UpFront-2">[2]</a></sup>
