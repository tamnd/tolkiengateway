On [28 July](/wiki/28_July) [1955](/wiki/1955), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Mrs Dixey** of Cape Town, [South Africa](/wiki/Category:South_Africa "Category:South Africa").<sup id="cite_ref-hammond_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hammond-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UpFront_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UpFront-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien expresses the hope that Mrs Dixey will enjoy the forthcoming *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*.<sup id="cite_ref-UpFront_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UpFront-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Handwritten letter, Tolkien's personal stationery.<sup id="cite_ref-UpFront_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UpFront-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

"I do not suppose I will write any more about [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits), but the success of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* has been quite as surprising to [the publishers](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin "Allen and Unwin") as to me (possibly more so) that it seems likely now that they will wish to publish the legend of the [First](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") and [Second Ages](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") (written first but refused)." The rejected material in question, of course, was published as *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*. Tolkien admits rather reluctantly to his correspondent that he is a native of South Africa. "I...belong really to the N. West of the Old World," he says, "...\[but\] I suppose that even in '[The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire)' they had heard some tales of the [far south](/wiki/Far_Harad "Far Harad")."<sup id="cite_ref-hammond_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hammond-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UpFront_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UpFront-2">[2]</a></sup>
