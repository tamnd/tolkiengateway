**Lascelles Abercrombie** ([9 January](/wiki/9_January), [1881](/wiki/1881) – [27 October](/wiki/27_October), [1938](/wiki/1938)) became Professor of English Literature at the University of [Leeds](/wiki/Leeds), ahead of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), who was then Reader in English Language. When Tolkien applied for the position of [Rawlinson and Bosworth Professorship of Anglo-Saxon](/wiki/Rawlinson_and_Bosworth_Professorship_of_Anglo-Saxon) at Oxford, Abercrombie wrote a letter of recommendation praising Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>[J.R.R. Tolkien] has throughout acted as my advisor and collaborator in the conduct and policy of the department as a whole....I have never consulted him without gaining an illumination that can penetrate as well as expatiate. But I must not omit to mention that I have gained at least as much from the keen artistic sensibility as from the science of his scholarship.
</p>

</blockquote>
