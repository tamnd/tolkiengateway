On [12 October](/wiki/12_October) [1966](/wiki/1966), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Kenneth I. Rasmussen, Jr.**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** A stock reply to a letter of appreciation, to which Tolkien added: "*If you use one of the modern steel [bows](/wiki/Bows "Bows"), you should be developing a good torso!*".
- **Publication:** None.
