<b><i>L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</i> (Special Issue) n°3</b> (*Prières &amp; Calligraphies elfiques*) is the first special issue of the fanzine <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkiendil.com/asso/mag" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">L&#39;Arc et le Heaume</a></i> by the [Tolkiendil](</wiki/Tolkiendil_(Tolkien_society)> "Tolkiendil (Tolkien society)") French association. It was published in December [2022](/wiki/2022) and it is dedicated to the [Catholic prayers](/wiki/Christianity "Christianity") translated in [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish).
