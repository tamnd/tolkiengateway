<i><b>La hobito</b></i> ("The Hobbit") is a translation of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* to [Esperanto](../E/Esperanto.md). The poems and songs were translated by [William Auld](../W/William_Auld.md), while the rest of the text was translated by Christopher Gledhill.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the retailer<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> {#From_the_retailer[2]}

J. R. R. TOLKIEN - Trans. Christopher GLEDHILL, poetry trans. William AULD. The Hobbit is the novel that introduces Middle Earth, Tolkien's fantasy land of hobbits, elves, wizards and many other wondrous creatures. It sets the stage for the Lord of the Rings trilogy, one of the most-loved fantasy adventures of the 20th century.
