On [5 March](/wiki/5_March) [1964](/wiki/1964), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Mrs Eileen Elgar**.

- **Contents:** The letter is a reply to Mrs Elgar's questions concerning some poems from *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil and Other Verses from the Red Book](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil "The Adventures of Tom Bombadil")*. Tolkien also encloses the manuscripts [Concerning ... 'The Hoard'](/wiki/Concerning_..._%27The_Hoard%27) and [Kinship of the Half-elven](/wiki/Kinship_of_the_Half-elven).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Reproduced in *[Sotheby's English Literature and English History 6-7 December 1984](/wiki/Sotheby%27s_English_Literature_and_English_History_6-7_December_1984)*. An excerpt from the letter, concerning the poem "[Fastitocalon](</wiki/Fastitocalon_(poem)> "Fastitocalon (poem)")", was published as [letter 255](/wiki/Letter_255 "Letter 255") in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)*.
