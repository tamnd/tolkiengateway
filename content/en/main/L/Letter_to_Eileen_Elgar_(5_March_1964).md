On [5 March](../_/5_March.md) [1964](../_/1964.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Mrs Eileen Elgar**.

- **Contents:** The letter is a reply to Mrs Elgar's questions concerning some poems from *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil and Other Verses from the Red Book](../T/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md)*. Tolkien also encloses the manuscripts [Concerning ... 'The Hoard'](../C/Concerning_..._'The_Hoard'.md) and [Kinship of the Half-elven](../K/Kinship_of_the_Half-elven.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Reproduced in *[Sotheby's English Literature and English History 6-7 December 1984](../S/Sotheby's_English_Literature_and_English_History_6-7_December_1984.md)*. An excerpt from the letter, concerning the poem "[Fastitocalon](<../F/Fastitocalon_(poem).md>)", was published as [letter 255](Letter_255.md) in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*.
