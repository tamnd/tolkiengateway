On [17 May](/wiki/17_May) [1954](/wiki/1954), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote <b><a href="/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien" title="Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien">a letter</a> to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Henry_Willink,_1st_Baronet" title="wikipedia:Sir Henry Willink, 1st Baronet">Henry Willink</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Discussion about [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), academic matters.
- **Publication:** A substantial portion of the letter was quoted by Brian Barbour in his article "Lewis and Cambridge", published in the journal [*Modern Philology 96*, no. 4](/wiki/Modern_Philology_96,_no._4 "Modern Philology 96, no. 4") (cf., pp. 463–4). A shorter and slightly edited version of the extract was published in *[The Collected Letters of C.S. Lewis: Narnia, Cambridge, and Joy 1950-1963](/wiki/The_Collected_Letters_of_C.S._Lewis:_Narnia,_Cambridge,_and_Joy_1950-1963)* (p. 475; here wrongly dated 18 May).<sup id="cite_ref-Addenda_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Addenda-2">[2]</a></sup>
