On [8 April](/wiki/8_April) [1968](/wiki/1968), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to** [Dick Plotz](/wiki/Dick_Plotz), president of the [Tolkien Society of America](/wiki/Tolkien_Society_of_America)<b>.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup></b>

- **Description:** Handwritten letter.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Subject:** Tolkien is expressing displeasure about the upcoming publication by [William Ready](/wiki/William_Ready) on The Lord of the Rings.
- **Publication:** None.
- **Archive:** A Photocopy of original typewritten letter is kept at [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University).<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>
