On [8 April](../_/8_April.md) [1968](../_/1968.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to** [Dick Plotz](../D/Dick_Plotz.md), president of the [Tolkien Society of America](../T/Tolkien_Society_of_America.md)<b>.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup></b>

- **Description:** Handwritten letter.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Subject:** Tolkien is expressing displeasure about the upcoming publication by [William Ready](../W/William_Ready.md) on The Lord of the Rings.
- **Publication:** None.
- **Archive:** A Photocopy of original typewritten letter is kept at [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>
