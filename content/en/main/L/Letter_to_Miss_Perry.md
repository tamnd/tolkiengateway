On [29 December](../_/29_December.md) [1961](../_/1961.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Miss Perry**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Replying to several letters from Perry, Tolkien explains the delay by not being able to remember addresses and that his papers are not in order. However, "*recently I found someone willing to go through the confusion; and she had special orders to look out for certain addresses. Just before Christmas she unearthed yours*".<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup> Tolkien also discusses his busy schedule and his doubts about a sequel to *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, ending the letter with "*forgive this gloomy note*".<sup id="cite_ref-Dacobra_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dacobra-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Description:** Handwritten letter, signed, Tolkien's Sandfield Road stationery.<sup id="cite_ref-Dacobra_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dacobra-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Quotes and a photograph appeared in *[Michael Silverman Catalogue Nine](../M/Michael_Silverman_Catalogue_Nine.md)* (p. 32 and pl. 8).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
