On [28 February](/wiki/28_February) [1966](/wiki/1966), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Miss Jenny Hall**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** (1) Etymology of '[Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow)'. (2) The true nature of the '[walking elms](/wiki/Tree-men "Tree-men")' in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").
- **Publication:** None. Auctioning by *Christie's* (lot 52).
- **Description:** One page, 76 Sandfield Road, Oxford letterhead, signed by J.R.R. Tolkien.

## Transcription {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I have been in most parts of Wales, but the place names I use are made up from English models or borrowed from books, though Crickhollow was actually meant to resemble Crickhowell.<br/><br/>The walking elms were meant to be <a href="/wiki/Ents" title="Ents">ents</a> (but not <a href="/wiki/Entwives" title="Entwives">entwives</a>). <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> had asked one or two of them to keep a watch on the Shire, but he did not tell anybody about it.*<br/><br/>[* As can be gathered from <a href="/wiki/Treebeard" title="Treebeard">Treebeard&#39;s</a> conversations with <a href="/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">M[erry] </a> and <a href="/wiki/Peregrin_Took" title="Peregrin Took">P[ippin] </a> he knew a lot more about events than they guessed, and more about &#39;<a href="/wiki/Hobbits" title="Hobbits">hobbits</a>&#39; than he pretended to.]<br/><br/>With best wishes<br/><br/>J.R.R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
