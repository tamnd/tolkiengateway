On [14 February](../_/14_February.md) [1896](../_/1896.md), a four-year-old [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Arthur Tolkien](../A/Arthur_Tolkien.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Description:** The letter was dictated by Tolkien to his nurse, but was never sent since his mother [Mabel](../M/Mabel_Suffield.md) received a telegram on the same day saying that Arthur had <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bleeding" title="wikipedia:Bleeding">haemorrhaged</a> (he died on the following day). It is the earliest known letter composed by Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-Life_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Life-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** The letter was quoted in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_A_Biography.md)* (p. 16). A reproduction of the letter appeared in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Life and Legend](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_Life_and_Legend.md)* (p. 11).
- **Archive:** [Bodleian Library](../B/Bodleian_Library.md) (Family papers 1/33(9)).<sup id="cite_ref-Life_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Life-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I am so glad I am coming back to see you it is such a long time since we came away from you I hope the ship will bring us all back to you Mamie and <a href="../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md" title="Hilary Tolkien">Baby</a> and me ... I am got such a big man now because I have got a man&#39;s coat and a mans [<i>sic</i>] bodice ... Auntie Gracie [Grace Mountain <i>née</i> Tolkien] has been to see us I walk every day and only ride in my mailcart a little bit ... your loving Ronald.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>J.R.R. Tolkien</cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>
