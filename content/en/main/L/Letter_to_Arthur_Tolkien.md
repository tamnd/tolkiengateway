On [14 February](/wiki/14_February) [1896](/wiki/1896), a four-year-old [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Arthur Tolkien](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Description:** The letter was dictated by Tolkien to his nurse, but was never sent since his mother [Mabel](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield "Mabel Suffield") received a telegram on the same day saying that Arthur had <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bleeding" title="wikipedia:Bleeding">haemorrhaged</a> (he died on the following day). It is the earliest known letter composed by Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-Life_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Life-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** The letter was quoted in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography)* (p. 16). A reproduction of the letter appeared in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Life and Legend](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Life_and_Legend)* (p. 11).
- **Archive:** [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library) (Family papers 1/33(9)).<sup id="cite_ref-Life_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Life-2">[2]</a></sup>
