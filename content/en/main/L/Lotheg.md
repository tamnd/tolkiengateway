<i><b>lotheg</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning "a single flower".<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*loth* is the singular form of the collective noun *[loth](Loth.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> deriving from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) LOT ("flower").<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}

- [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *[loth](Loth.md)* ("flower")
- [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *[lúth](Lúth.md)* ("blossom")
