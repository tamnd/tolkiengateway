On [8 April](../_/8_April.md) [1966](../_/1966.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_%2522The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%2522.md) to Roger Verhulst**.<sup id="cite_ref-Hammond_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hammond-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Tolkien objects (and declines) to participate in the Eerdmans’ book project. The letter also mentions the Charles Williams book project and Charles Williams’ wife. Tolkien talkes about The Silmarillion, mentioning C.S. Lewis is one of the few people who has read parts of the manuscript and that the chief obstacle to its publication is the mass of Tolkien's correspondence.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Quotes from the letter have been provided in *[Tolkien and The Silmarillion](../T/Tolkien_and_The_Silmarillion.md)* and in *[W.H. Auden: A Legacy](../W/W.H._Auden%253A_A_Legacy.md)*. A description of the letter has appeared in the catalogue *Christie's Fine Printed Books and Manuscripts 28 November 2011* (lot 258).
- **Description:** One page, 76 Sandfield Road, dictated by Tolkien and signed by Phyllis M. Jenkinson (Tolkien's assistant)
