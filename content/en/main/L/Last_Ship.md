The **Last Ship** was the last ship, built by [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan), that sailed from the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) into the [Uttermost West](/wiki/Uttermost_West) carrying the last [Elves](/wiki/Elves) from [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

[Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) maintained the haven at least into the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) (for the last mentioning the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) was [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)'s departure <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/West" title="West">West</a> in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [61](/wiki/Fourth_Age_61 "Fourth Age 61")),<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup> but it was recognized that eventually, its purpose would reach an end when no more Elves wished to cross the [Sea](/wiki/Sea). At that time, Círdan would abandon the Grey Havens and finally travel the [Straight Road](/wiki/Straight_Road) himself.

At some unknown date,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppTale_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppTale-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-prologue_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prologue-4">[3]</a></sup> [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) eventually sought the Grey Havens, taking with him the last memory of the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Since Círdan and Celeborn are among the only known Elves of the Elder Days in Middle-earth, they would have taken the Last Ship together.<sup id="cite_ref-prologue_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prologue-4">[3]</a></sup>

<li id="cite_note-2"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-2">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text">The Last Ship could not have sailed before <a href="/wiki/Fourth_Age" title="Fourth Age">Fo.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Fourth_Age_120" title="Fourth Age 120">120</a> based on <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>&#39;s final words to <a href="/wiki/Arwen" title="Arwen">Arwen</a>. Aragorn suggests Arwen might still take passage on a ship from Mithlond into the West, but by this time Arwen had long forfeited the right to live in the <a href="/wiki/Undying_Lands" title="Undying Lands">Undying Lands</a>. The words &#34;and some say [Círdan] dwells there still, until the Last Ship sets sail into the West&#34; derive from the <a href="/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch" title="Red Book of Westmarch">Red Book of Westmarch</a>, and not from the <i>original</i> <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Red_Book" title="Red Book">Red Book</a>, which has not been preserved, but from a copy of it, the first of them being the <a href="/wiki/Thain%27s_Book" title="Thain&#39;s Book">Thain&#39;s Book</a>, copied in <a href="/wiki/Fourth_Age" title="Fourth Age">Fo.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Fourth_Age_171" title="Fourth Age 171">171</a>. Thus, the speculation about <a href="/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan" title="Círdan">Círdan</a> still dwelling in <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Mithlond" title="Mithlond">Mithlond</a> cannot be exactly dated to a period before <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Aragorn_II_Elessar" title="Aragorn II Elessar">Elessar</a>&#39;s and <a href="/wiki/Arwen" title="Arwen">Arwen</a>&#39;s death. This means that the Last Ship would have sailed at some unknown date after <a href="/wiki/Fourth_Age" title="Fourth Age">Fo.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Fourth_Age_171" title="Fourth Age 171">171</a>.</span>
