The **Last Debate** was the council that [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) held with [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer), [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan), and [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir) outside [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) on [March 16](/wiki/16_March "16 March"), [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").

## History {#History}

After the [Battle of Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_Pelennor_Fields), Aragorn convened the Last Debate to decide their next course of action. Gandalf told the captains that they would not be able to defeat [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) or his army through force of arms and that the one thing that would is [The One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring). He impressed upon them how important it was that the Ring be destroyed lest Sauron get it. He counselled them to create an army to march on the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) to draw the [Eye of Sauron](/wiki/Eye_of_Sauron) away from [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) and [Sam](/wiki/Sam). Éomer brought up concerns for the defence of Minas Tirith once the army left. In the end, they decided to leave behind 3,000 [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) to attack a very large blocking force stationed on the [West Road](/wiki/West_Road) in [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien); 4,000 who were coming by land from [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir); possibly several thousand who were coming up the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin); and the survivors from the siege of the city. This left the city better defended than it had been before the Battle of Pelennor Fields. Despite the knowledge that they were walking straight into a trap and some fears that Sauron would simply crush them without giving little thought to them, it was agreed that 7000 soldiers would leave Minas Tirith in two days.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Also debated was whether to assail [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) which Gandalf strongly advocated against because of "evil that dwelt in the valley, where the minds of living men would turn to madness and horror" and the risk of drawing the Eye of Sauron too near to Frodo and Sam.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The expeditionary force, known as the [Army of the West](/wiki/Army_of_the_West), numbered 7,000 men, and two days later on [18 March](/wiki/18_March) they left for the Morannon. Of those that left, under [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) there were 2,000 of those who sailed with him up the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin); under [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil) were 3,500 (probably from among the original defenders of the city); under [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) were 500 [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) unhorsed, and another 500 horsed; and lastly another 500 horsed Knights of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth), [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North), and the two sons of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan) and [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir). In total 6000 on foot and 1000 on horse.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
