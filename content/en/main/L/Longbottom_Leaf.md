**Longbottom Leaf** was one of the three varieties of [pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed") recorded as being the most sought-after, the other two being [Old Toby](/wiki/Old_Toby) and [Southern Star](/wiki/Southern_Star).

[Longbottom](/wiki/Longbottom) in the [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing) was the first place in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") where pipe-weed was grown, and so Longbottom Leaf must have been one of the oldest and most established types (if not the original variety). The first Leaf-grower was [Tobold Hornblower](/wiki/Tobold_Hornblower), and his family continued the tradition.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Barrels of Longbottom Leaf were stashed by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) in [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard). [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") who found the barrels in its ruins, noticed the brand-marks of the [Hornblower Family](/wiki/Hornblower_Family) and the date [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1417](/wiki/Third_Age_3017 "Third Age 3017").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
