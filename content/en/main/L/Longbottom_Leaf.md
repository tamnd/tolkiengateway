**Longbottom Leaf** was one of the three varieties of [pipe-weed](../P/Pipe-weed.md) recorded as being the most sought-after, the other two being [Old Toby](../O/Old_Toby.md) and [Southern Star](../S/Southern_Star.md).

[Longbottom](Longbottom.md) in the [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md) was the first place in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) where pipe-weed was grown, and so Longbottom Leaf must have been one of the oldest and most established types (if not the original variety). The first Leaf-grower was [Tobold Hornblower](../T/Tobold_Hornblower.md), and his family continued the tradition.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Barrels of Longbottom Leaf were stashed by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) in [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md). [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) who found the barrels in its ruins, noticed the brand-marks of the [Hornblower Family](../H/Hornblower_Family.md) and the date [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1417](../T/Third_Age_3017.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
