On [29 December](/wiki/29_December) [1966](/wiki/1966), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to James S. Marsh**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** A second letter to Mr Marsh, commenting on his plan to produce a book with [Donald Swann](/wiki/Donald_Swann) (which would become *[The Road Goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")*), and his mutual appreciation of music.
- **Publication:** None.

## Transcript {#Transcript}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Dear Mr Marsh,<br/><br/>Thank you for your letter. Since music touches you, I spare a moment to let you know that I spent a day last week with Donald Swann he <s>is</s> was on a hasty visit to England in the middle of his tour of the U.S. and Canada. We are hoping to produce <a href="/wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)" title="The Road Goes Ever On (book)">a book</a>, the main part of which will be songs from <u><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></u>, with his setting to be published by <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Houghton_Mifflin" title="Houghton Mifflin">Houghton Mifflin</a> of Boston next year. I find his music moving and very close to my own intentions. He is at present very engaged on the difficult task of making a setting for “Errantry”.<br/><br/>With best wishes again,<br/>Yours sincerely<br/>J.R.R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
