On [8 December](../_/8_December.md) [1956](../_/1956.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Anthony Roberts**.<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Replying to a fan, Tolkien explains why the publication of the "*legends of the [First](../F/First_Age.md) and [Second](../S/Second_Age.md) Ages, including those related to the [Silmarils](../S/Silmarils.md) ('[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)')*" is delayed: "*...unfortunately I am still a busy official and a 'professor' with a considerable amount of other things on hand ... I am afraid there are no [hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) in these stories, and not much of every-day reality, and that makes me doubtful about their reception.*"<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Description:** One-page, handwritten letter.<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup>
