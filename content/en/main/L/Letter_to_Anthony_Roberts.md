On [8 December](/wiki/8_December) [1956](/wiki/1956), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Anthony Roberts**.<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Replying to a fan, Tolkien explains why the publication of the "*legends of the [First](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") and [Second](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") Ages, including those related to the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils) ('[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)')*" is delayed: "*...unfortunately I am still a busy official and a 'professor' with a considerable amount of other things on hand ... I am afraid there are no [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") in these stories, and not much of every-day reality, and that makes me doubtful about their reception.*"<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Description:** One-page, handwritten letter.<sup id="cite_ref-TS_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TS-1">[1]</a></sup>
