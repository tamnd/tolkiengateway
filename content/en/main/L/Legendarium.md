The <i><b>legendarium</b></i> is the entirety of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s works concerning his imagined world of [Arda](/wiki/Arda).

Tolkien himself used the term,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and also referred to his "**mythology**" in the same sense.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Expecting publication for *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, and hoping that "The Silmarillion" would be published together, Tolkien envisioned his work (possibly including *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*) as the "**[Saga](/wiki/Golden_Book "Golden Book") of the [Three Jewels](/wiki/Silmarils "Silmarils") and the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power)**".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The term of *legendarium* is not the same as "[canon](/wiki/Canon "Canon")". The legendarium includes all Tolkien's corpus in its entirety, even mutually contradicting versions, both earlier and later stages of its conception.

### Writings of the legendarium {#Writings_of_the_legendarium}

    - *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*
    - *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*

    - *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*
    - *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*
    - *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* (12 volumes)
    - *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*
    - *[Beren and Lúthien](/wiki/Beren_and_L%C3%BAthien)*
    - *[The Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin)*

### Other sources {#Other_sources}

More of Tolkien's vision of his legendarium can be found in [interviews conducted with him](/wiki/Category:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Category:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien"). Hints of lesser value (i.e., hard to verify the authenticity), may also be found in [reminiscences](/wiki/Index:Reminiscences_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Reminiscences of J.R.R. Tolkien"). There is also much material on the topic that remains [unpublished](/wiki/Index:Unpublished_material "Index:Unpublished material"), including many [letters not published in *The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien*](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien").
