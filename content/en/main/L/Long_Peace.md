The **Long Peace** is a term referring to the extended peace in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) in the later [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) during which [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) did not try to break the [Siege of Angband](../S/Siege_of_Angband.md). It began with a failed sortie by [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Return_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-1">[1]</a></sup> in the [year](../C/Coranar.md) [260](../F/First_Age_260.md)<sup id="cite_ref-GA260_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA260-2">[2]</a></sup> and ended with the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Fingolfin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingolfin-3">[3]</a></sup> in [455](../F/First_Age_455.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> 195 years later.

During this period the [Elves of Beleriand](../E/Elves_of_Beleriand.md) prospered, with only minor conflicts with the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md). It was also during the Long Peace that the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) passed into [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) from the [east](../E/Eriador.md).

## History {#History}

The [Siege of Angband](../S/Siege_of_Angband.md) held the forces of [Darkness](../D/Darkness.md) within [Angband](../A/Angband.md). [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) the Worm attempted to break it, managing to drive the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) off [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md), however he was too young, and was pushed back by [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md). It was then decided that Glaurung should wait to mature until ready to strike back.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-1">[1]</a></sup> This long wait allowed for the races of Beleriand to recover and prosper.

The first major event during those years was the arrival of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) into Beleriand, looking for the land of the [Gods](../V/Valar.md) in the [West](../U/Uttermost_West.md). [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) quickly befriended these first [Men](../M/Men.md) and advised them to settle in [Estolad](../E/Estolad.md) in the realm of [Amrod](../A/Amrod.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Around that time, the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md), the [Folk of Marach](../F/Folk_of_Marach.md), and the [Easterlings](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>) crossed over the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) into Beleriand. The Haladin dwelt in [Thargelion](../T/Thargelion.md) as scattered groups until a host of orcs [assaulted](../B/Battle_of_the_Gelion-Ascar_Stockade~66779.md) their territory in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [375](../F/First_Age_375.md). They built [a stockade](../G/Gelion-Ascar_Stockade.md) between the angle of [Gelion](../G/Gelion.md) until [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md)'s army could relieve them. [Haleth](../H/Haleth.md), who had become [Chieftain](../C/Chieftain_of_the_Haladin.md) following the death of her father and brother, declined a fief in his lands and moved on to [Talath Dirnen](../T/Talath_Dirnen.md) to settle in the [Forest of Brethil](../F/Forest_of_Brethil.md).

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [402](../F/First_Age_402.md), [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) attempted to break through the [Pass of Aglon](../P/Pass_of_Aglon.md), but the people of [Bëor](../B/Bëor.md) helped [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) and [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md) threw back the invaders.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> This was the first time Men fought on behalf of the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md). Their head was also given the region of [Ladros](Ladros.md) of [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md).

The Marachians remained in Estolad after the Beorians, becoming close allies with the [House of Fingolfin](../H/House_of_Fingolfin.md) in [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) and then moved to the southern slopes of the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md); during the time of [Hador](../H/Hador.md) in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [416](../F/First_Age_416.md) they were granted permanent fief of [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md).

During this time, Angband also built its forces and Glaurung grew stronger. The period ended when finally the Siege was broken in the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Fingolfin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingolfin-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
