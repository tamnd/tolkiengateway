The **Long Peace** is a term referring to the extended peace in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) in the later [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) during which [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) did not try to break the [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband). It began with a failed sortie by [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung)<sup id="cite_ref-Return_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-1">[1]</a></sup> in the [year](/wiki/Coranar "Coranar") [260](/wiki/First_Age_260 "First Age 260")<sup id="cite_ref-GA260_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA260-2">[2]</a></sup> and ended with the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach)<sup id="cite_ref-Fingolfin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingolfin-3">[3]</a></sup> in [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> 195 years later.

During this period the [Elves of Beleriand](/wiki/Elves_of_Beleriand) prospered, with only minor conflicts with the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). It was also during the Long Peace that the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) passed into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) from the [east](/wiki/Eriador "Eriador").

## History {#History}

The [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband) held the forces of [Darkness](/wiki/Darkness) within [Angband](/wiki/Angband). [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) the Worm attempted to break it, managing to drive the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) off [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen), however he was too young, and was pushed back by [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon). It was then decided that Glaurung should wait to mature until ready to strike back.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-1">[1]</a></sup> This long wait allowed for the races of Beleriand to recover and prosper.

The first major event during those years was the arrival of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) into Beleriand, looking for the land of the [Gods](/wiki/Valar "Valar") in the [West](/wiki/Uttermost_West "Uttermost West"). [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) quickly befriended these first [Men](/wiki/Men) and advised them to settle in [Estolad](/wiki/Estolad) in the realm of [Amrod](/wiki/Amrod).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Around that time, the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin), the [Folk of Marach](/wiki/Folk_of_Marach), and the [Easterlings](</wiki/Easterlings_(First_Age)> "Easterlings (First Age)") crossed over the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) into Beleriand. The Haladin dwelt in [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion) as scattered groups until a host of orcs [assaulted](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Gelion-Ascar_Stockade "Battle of the Gelion-Ascar Stockade") their territory in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [375](/wiki/First_Age_375 "First Age 375"). They built [a stockade](/wiki/Gelion-Ascar_Stockade "Gelion-Ascar Stockade") between the angle of [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion) until [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir)'s army could relieve them. [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth), who had become [Chieftain](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_Haladin "Chieftain of the Haladin") following the death of her father and brother, declined a fief in his lands and moved on to [Talath Dirnen](/wiki/Talath_Dirnen) to settle in the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Forest_of_Brethil).

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [402](/wiki/First_Age_402 "First Age 402"), [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) attempted to break through the [Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Pass_of_Aglon), but the people of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor) helped [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) and [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor) threw back the invaders.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> This was the first time Men fought on behalf of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar). Their head was also given the region of [Ladros](/wiki/Ladros) of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion).

The Marachians remained in Estolad after the Beorians, becoming close allies with the [House of Fingolfin](/wiki/House_of_Fingolfin) in [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) and then moved to the southern slopes of the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin); during the time of [Hador](/wiki/Hador) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [416](/wiki/First_Age_416 "First Age 416") they were granted permanent fief of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min).

During this time, Angband also built its forces and Glaurung grew stronger. The period ended when finally the Siege was broken in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach).<sup id="cite_ref-Fingolfin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingolfin-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
