On [12 November](../_/12_November.md) [1949](../_/1949.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Miss How**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Description:** Tolkien writes that he has "two very large scale works accepted and nearly finished".
