On [12 November](/wiki/12_November) [1949](/wiki/1949), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Miss How**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Description:** Tolkien writes that he has "two very large scale works accepted and nearly finished".
- **Publication:** *[Sotheby's Catalogue of Valuable Autograph Letters, Literary Manuscripts and Historical Documents 21-22 July 1980](/wiki/Sotheby%27s_Catalogue_of_Valuable_Autograph_Letters,_Literary_Manuscripts_and_Historical_Documents_21-22_July_1980)*, p. 338.
