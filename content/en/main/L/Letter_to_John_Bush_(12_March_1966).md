On [12 March](../_/12_March.md) [1966](../_/1966.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to John Bush**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Tolkien thanks a fan for sending him a copy of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Herbert" title="wikipedia:Frank Herbert">Frank Herbert</a>'s novel <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune_(novel)" title="wikipedia:Dune (novel)"><i>Dune</i></a>. Tolkien writes that he dislikes "*Dune* with some intensity, and in that unfortunate case it is much the best and fairest to another author to keep silent and refuse to comment". Tolkien also proposes to return the copy of *Dune*, since he received another copy from a fan in 1965.
- **Publication:** A quotation from the letter was published in *Tolkien's Library: An Annotated Checklist*.
