<i><b>Lugburz</b></i> is the second track of the album [Minas Morgul](</wiki/Minas_Morgul_(album)> "Minas Morgul (album)") by the band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning). The song name is taken from [Lugbúrz](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr "Barad-dûr"), meaning "*dark tower*" in the [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech), referring to [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr). The lyrics directly quote the [incantation](/wiki/Cold_be_hand_and_heart_and_bone, "Cold be hand and heart and bone,") chanted by a [Barrow-wight](/wiki/Barrow-wights "Barrow-wights") who had trapped [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") and [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") in the [Fog on the Barrow-downs](/wiki/Fog_on_the_Barrow-downs) chapter of [The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring).

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Cold be hand and heart and bone, <br/>
and cold be sleep under stone: <br/>
never more to wake on stony bed, <br/>
never, till the sun fails and the moon is dead. <br/>
<br/>
In the black wind the stars shall die, <br/>
and still on gold here let them lie, <br/>
till the dark lord lifts his hand <br/>
over dead sea and withered land <br/>
<br/>
When the winter first begins to bite <br/>
and stones crack in the frosty night, <br/>
when pools are black and trees are bare, <br/>
it is evil in the wild to fare. <br/>
<br/>
To lay down my will upon the Land, Lugburz <br/>
<br/>
No sound disturbs this place of blackened souls <br/>
This winter walls of stone and ice behold thy might <br/>
Again I&#39;m kneeling down to hear these strange tunes of war <br/>
Night, oh beloved night, your wisdom floats into my mind <br/>
and forms my thoughts of Middle-Earth <br/>
to build up a new mighty empire.
</p>
