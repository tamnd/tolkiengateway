<i><b>lacho</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "flame".<sup id="cite_ref-UT2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT2-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CH_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CH-2">[2]</a></sup>

The word is commonly understood as a verbal imperative.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Phrases {#Phrases}

"*Lacho calad! Drego morn*! Flame Light! Flee Night!"<sup id="cite_ref-UT2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT2-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CH_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CH-2">[2]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}

- *[lhach](/wiki/Lhach "Lhach")* ("leaping flame")
