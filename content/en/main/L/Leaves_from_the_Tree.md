<i><b>Leaves from the Tree: J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s Shorter Fiction</b></i> is a collection of the proceedings of the 4th Tolkien Society Workshop, held in Beverley in 1989. It was published by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) in [1991](/wiki/1991) under the auspices of the [Peter Roe Memorial Fund](/wiki/Peter_Roe_Memorial_Fund) in [1991](/wiki/1991).

The publication is notable for including contributions from [John Rateliff](/wiki/John_Rateliff), [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) and [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey). In particular, [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull)'s paper, "Dragons from Andrew Lang's Retelling of Sigurd to Tolkien's Chrysophylax", reproduces an extract from Tolkien's then unpublished [lecture on dragons](</wiki/Dragons_(lecture)> "Dragons (lecture)").
