On [24 January](../_/24_January.md) [1965](../_/1965.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Joan O. Falconer**.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Replying to Falconer's letter of [17 January](../_/17_January.md), Tolkien discusses [Sam Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)'s character in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Excerpts have been published in [*Mythprint* (Vol.8, No.3)](<../M/Mythprint_(Vol.8,_No.3).md>) and in *[Tolkien and The Silmarillion](../T/Tolkien_and_The_Silmarillion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Archive:** The letter is kept at the Raynor Memorial Libraries at [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>
