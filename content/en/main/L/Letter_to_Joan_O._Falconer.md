On [24 January](/wiki/24_January) [1965](/wiki/1965), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Joan O. Falconer**.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Contents:** Replying to Falconer's letter of [17 January](/wiki/17_January), Tolkien discusses [Sam Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee")'s character in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Excerpts have been published in [*Mythprint* (Vol.8, No.3)](</wiki/Mythprint_(Vol.8,_No.3)> "Mythprint (Vol.8, No.3)") and in *[Tolkien and The Silmarillion](/wiki/Tolkien_and_The_Silmarillion)*.<sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Archive:** The letter is kept at the Raynor Memorial Libraries at [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University).<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>
