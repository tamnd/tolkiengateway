On [February](../F/February.md) [1956](../_/1956.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Peter Alford**. It was possibly written in Mid-February [1956](../_/1956.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien gives details of the titles considered for the six books of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** Excerpts appeared in *[Sotheby's Valuable Printed Books and Manuscripts 13 December 2001](../S/Sotheby's_Valuable_Printed_Books_and_Manuscripts_13_December_2001.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Pieter Collier](../P/Pieter_Collier.md) has noted that Peter Alford was a bookbinder, who had written to Tolkien asking for permission to bind his *Lord of the Rings* set with his own sub-titles. Tolkien approved but also detailed his preferred titles:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I think your own choices may stand. The only real divisions are, of course, the &#39;books&#39;. It may interest you to know what the titles were to have been, before the decision to publish in three parts.<i><br/><br/></i>I The First Journey (the Ring sets out.)<i><br/></i>II The Journey of the Nine Companions (the Ring goes South)<i><br/></i>III The Treason of Isengard<i><br/></i>IV The Journey of the Ringbearer (the Ring goes east)<i><br/></i>V The War of the Ring<i><br/></i>VI The End of the Third Age<i><br/><br/></i>The alternative or sub-titles in brackets would have been abandoned, I think.&#34;
</p>
</blockquote>

Alford's set (sold by [Sotheby's](../S/Sotheby's.md) for GBP 32,450 in December 2001) included the following note by Tolkien:

"*The titles of the six Books, the only proper division of The Lord of the Rings were those originally devised before its publication in three parts. Peter Alford has my approval of his own choice of sub-titles on his bindings. J.R.R. Tolkien*".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
