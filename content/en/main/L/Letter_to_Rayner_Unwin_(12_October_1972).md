On [12 October](/wiki/12_October) [1972](/wiki/1972), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin)**.

- **Subject:** The printed appearance of colours of certain pictures in the Ballantine Tolkien Calendar, etc.
- **Publication:** Excerpts from the letter have been published in [*Amon Hen* 70](/wiki/Amon_Hen_70 "Amon Hen 70") (p. 3), in [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien)'s article "'Moria Gate'...Another Look". A shorter part of the letter (although the entry on the letter includes more descriptive information of the contents) was reprinted in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")* (p. 767).
