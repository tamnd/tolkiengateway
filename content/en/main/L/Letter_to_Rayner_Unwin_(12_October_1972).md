On [12 October](../_/12_October.md) [1972](../_/1972.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md)**.

- **Subject:** The printed appearance of colours of certain pictures in the Ballantine Tolkien Calendar, etc.
- **Publication:** Excerpts from the letter have been published in [*Amon Hen* 70](../A/Amon_Hen_70.md) (p. 3), in [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md)'s article "'Moria Gate'...Another Look". A shorter part of the letter (although the entry on the letter includes more descriptive information of the contents) was reprinted in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md)* (p. 767).
