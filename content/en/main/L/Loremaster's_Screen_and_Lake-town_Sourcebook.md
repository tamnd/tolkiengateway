<i><b>Loremaster&#39;s Screen and Lake-town Sourcebook</b></i> is a supplement for [The One Ring](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)") role-playing game.

## Official description {#Official_description}

The Lake-town Guide, written by Francesco Nepitello, presents this fascinating setting in detail, including:

- A guide to Lake-town, its inhabitants and culture
- More information on the Long Marshes and the creatures dwelling within
- New Fellowship Phase activities
- A new playable culture – Men of the Lake

Also included in this product is the heavy-duty card Loremaster’s Screen, which presents useful information for easy reference during a game and features stunning art from The One Ring artist [Jon Hodgson](/wiki/Jon_Hodgson).
