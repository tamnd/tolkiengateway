**Ladros** was a highland region to the northeast of [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md). It was held briefly as a fief by the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md), but lost to [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) in the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) ("Battle of Sudden Flame").

## History {#History}

The region of Ladros seems originally to have been a fief of [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md): at least, his brothers [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md) and [Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md) held "the northern slopes of Dorthonion".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Finrod later assigned this land to [Boromir](<../B/Boromir_(Lord_of_Ladros).md>) of the House of Bëor, whose members had long been loyal in his service. Boromir became [Lord of Ladros](Lord_of_Ladros.md).

Of the House of Bëor, there were three lords of Ladros — Boromir himself, his son [Bregor](../B/Bregor.md), and Bregor's son [Bregolas](../B/Bregolas.md). In Bregolas' time, Morgoth overwhelmed Dorthonion and Ladros in the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), in which Bregolas himself was slain. His brother [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md) and [twelve faithful](../B/Barahir's_outlaws.md) [Bëorings](../B/Bëorings.md) became outlaws in their own land, assaulting Morgoth's forces from their hidden lair at [Tarn Aeluin](../T/Tarn_Aeluin.md) on Ladros' southern borders. They were betrayed by [Gorlim the Unhappy](../G/Gorlim_the_Unhappy.md), (who was himself deceived by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) into divulging the Outlaws' whereabouts.) and all were slain but [Beren](../B/Beren.md), who escaped into the south.

After this time, Ladros fell under the yoke of Morgoth until it was mostly swallowed by the [Great Sea](../B/Belegaer.md) at the end of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), with bits of the land surviving as part of [Tol Fuin](../T/Tol_Fuin.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ladros* is clearly [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), but its meaning is unclear, although it might be a combination of *[lad](Lad.md)* ("plain") + *[ros](../R/Ros.md)* ("spray, rain").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
