**Ladros** was a highland region to the northeast of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion). It was held briefly as a fief by the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor), but lost to [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) ("Battle of Sudden Flame").

## History {#History}

The region of Ladros seems originally to have been a fief of [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod"): at least, his brothers [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod) and [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor) held "the northern slopes of Dorthonion".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Finrod later assigned this land to [Boromir](</wiki/Boromir_(Lord_of_Ladros)> "Boromir (Lord of Ladros)") of the House of Bëor, whose members had long been loyal in his service. Boromir became [Lord of Ladros](/wiki/Lord_of_Ladros).

Of the House of Bëor, there were three lords of Ladros — Boromir himself, his son [Bregor](/wiki/Bregor), and Bregor's son [Bregolas](/wiki/Bregolas). In Bregolas' time, Morgoth overwhelmed Dorthonion and Ladros in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), in which Bregolas himself was slain. His brother [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir) and [twelve faithful](/wiki/Barahir%27s_outlaws "Barahir's outlaws") [Bëorings](/wiki/B%C3%ABorings) became outlaws in their own land, assaulting Morgoth's forces from their hidden lair at [Tarn Aeluin](/wiki/Tarn_Aeluin) on Ladros' southern borders. They were betrayed by [Gorlim the Unhappy](/wiki/Gorlim_the_Unhappy), (who was himself deceived by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) into divulging the Outlaws' whereabouts.) and all were slain but [Beren](/wiki/Beren), who escaped into the south.

After this time, Ladros fell under the yoke of Morgoth until it was mostly swallowed by the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") at the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), with bits of the land surviving as part of [Tol Fuin](/wiki/Tol_Fuin).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ladros* is clearly [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), but its meaning is unclear, although it might be a combination of *[lad](/wiki/Lad "Lad")* ("plain") + *[ros](/wiki/Ros "Ros")* ("spray, rain").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
