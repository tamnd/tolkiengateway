**Latin for the Laity** is the topic and title of a lecture Tolkien gave to his parish of <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=St._Anthony_of_Padua_(Church)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="St. Anthony of Padua (Church) (page does not exist)">St. Anthony of Padua</a> in the late 1950s.<sup id="cite_ref-reference_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reference-1">[1]</a></sup>

According to [Michäel Devaux](../M/Michaël_Devaux.md), Tolkien aimed to help his fellow parishioners better understand the basic [Latin](Latin.md) elements of the Mass and gave various reasons why this would be helpful for their devotions. This lecture shows him as an active member of the parish, and also as having good reasons, which he could articulate, for valuing Latin, emphasizing that his love of the Mass in Latin was not merely nostalgic.<sup id="cite_ref-reference_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reference-1">[1]</a></sup>

At one point in this lecture, Tolkien quotes <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=John_Henry_Newman&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="John Henry Newman (page does not exist)">John Henry Newman</a> (1801-1890), a catholic priest, theologian and cardinal, which is the only explicit evidence of Tolkien actually reading the works of Newman.<sup id="cite_ref-reference_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reference-1">[1]</a></sup>
