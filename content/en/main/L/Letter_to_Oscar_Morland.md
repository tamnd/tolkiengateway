On [9 May](/wiki/9_May) [1967](/wiki/1967), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Oscar Morland**.<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien thanks Morland for sending him copies his privately compiled [indexes for](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Index "The Lord of the Rings Index") *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
- **Description:** Typed letter with signature, one page, 6.75 x 8.75, 76 Sandfield Road, Oxford letterhead.
- **Publication:** A description of the letter was published in [*The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology*](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide") (p. 698).

## Transcription {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Dear Mr. Morland,<br/><br/>Thank you very much for the typed copies of your indexes. I think it is a very generous gift. It will probably be of considerable use to me when I have time to turn to the correction and improvement of the index provided in the revised edition of <i>The Lord of the Rings</i> published last year. It is the only index material I have received that takes in the Appendices, and these references will save me a lot of time. Thank you also for telling me of the pleasure you and your family have found in my works.<br/><br/>Yours sincerely,<br/><br/>J.R.R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
