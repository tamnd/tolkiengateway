**Loëndë** was also known, especially by the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md), as [Mid-year's Day](../M/Mid-year's_Day.md). *Loëndë* was the day that fell in the middle of the year in many of the calendars of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

## Uses in different calendars {#Uses_in_different_calendars}

Since Mid-year's Day in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) was intended to correspond with the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solstice" title="wikipedia:Solstice">summer solstice</a>, then this should apply to *loëndë* in the [Kings' Reckoning](../K/Kings'_Reckoning.md) and [Stewards' Reckoning](../S/Stewards'_Reckoning.md) calendars of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) as well. The Númenórean calendar, every fourth year except the end of the century, loëndë was replaced with two *[Enderi](../E/Enderi.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [New Reckoning](../N/New_Reckoning.md), *loëndë* was the second of the *Enderi* and corresponded with [24 September](../_/24_September.md) in the Shire Calendar.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Loëndë* is a compound of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=End%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Endë (page does not exist)">endë</a></i> “middle” and the suffixal form *-re* of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=R%C3%A9&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ré (page does not exist)">ré</a></i> “day”. The direct translation of *Loëndë* is middle day but it is more often translated as Mid-year's Day.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
