**Loëndë** was also known, especially by the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits), as [Mid-year's Day](/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day). *Loëndë* was the day that fell in the middle of the year in many of the calendars of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

## Uses in different calendars {#Uses_in_different_calendars}

Since Mid-year's Day in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) was intended to correspond with the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solstice" title="wikipedia:Solstice">summer solstice</a>, then this should apply to *loëndë* in the [Kings' Reckoning](/wiki/Kings%27_Reckoning) and [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning) calendars of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) as well. The Númenórean calendar, every fourth year except the end of the century, loëndë was replaced with two *[Enderi](/wiki/Enderi)*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning), *loëndë* was the second of the *Enderi* and corresponded with [24 September](/wiki/24_September) in the Shire Calendar.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Loëndë* is a compound of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=End%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Endë (page does not exist)">endë</a></i> “middle” and the suffixal form *-re* of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=R%C3%A9&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ré (page does not exist)">ré</a></i> “day”. The direct translation of *Loëndë* is middle day but it is more often translated as Mid-year's Day.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
