On [21 March](../_/21_March.md) [1956](../_/1956.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to E. Rasdall**.<sup id="cite_ref-Smith_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Smith-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Publication:** None.
- **Description:** Handwritten note, found inside the dust jacket of a first edition *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* kept at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gelman_Library" title="wikipedia:Gelman Library">Estelle and Melvin Gelman Library</a>'s Special Collections Research Center at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University" title="wikipedia:George Washington University">George Washington University</a>.

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>&#39;It&#39;s a bit of a puzzle as to who [Rasdall] is and how the letter got into the book,&#39; Ms. Kinniff [Gelman Library staff member] said. The book is inscribed with a name and date on the inside cover—Christine Cusnich, August 1954—but nothing is known about Ms. Cusnich, and little is known about Mr. Rasdall, aside from the fact that he was most likely a book collector.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Laura Donnelly-Smith<sup id="cite_ref-Smith_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Smith-1">[1]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

## Content {#Content}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I am so sorry! Your parcel arrived safely, but it arrived at a crowded time, and I was also unwell. In fact, I had to go away to recuperate, and only returned yesterday. It was on my conscience that I had gone off without either returning your books or letting you know. I will send them off, I hope on Friday. Why not at once? Well, because tomorrow <a href="../E/Edith_Tolkien.md" title="Edith Tolkien">my wife</a> and I celebrate our 40th or ruby wedding anniversary, and are preparing for the gathering of our children and children-in-law, which has not occurred complete for many years. I fear it will be a ceremony by <a href="../T/The_Shire.md" title="The Shire">Shire</a> standards ‘brief, moderate, simple, and jejune’ but there may be a speech!<br/><br/>Yours truly, J. R. R. Tolkien<br/><br/>P.S. I have just had a <a href="Letter_184.md" title="Letter 184">letter from a real Sam Gamgee</a> (of Tooting)!
</p>
</blockquote>
