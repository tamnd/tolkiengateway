**Lothlórien** is an instrumental composition by [Enya](../E/Enya.md). It appeared as track nine on her third album *Shepherd Moons* ([1991](../_/1991.md)), and was itself not released on single. It can also be found on disc 2 of the three CD-set *A Box of Dreams*.
