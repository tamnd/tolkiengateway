**Lothlórien** is an instrumental composition by [Enya](/wiki/Enya). It appeared as track nine on her third album *Shepherd Moons* ([1991](/wiki/1991)), and was itself not released on single. It can also be found on disc 2 of the three CD-set *A Box of Dreams*.
