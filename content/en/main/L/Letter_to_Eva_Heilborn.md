On [19 April](/wiki/19_April) [1972](/wiki/1972), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) sent a <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procuration" title="wikipedia:Procuration">per procurationem</a></i> **[letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Eva Heilborn**, a Swedish reader.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Reply to letter of appreciation from Heilborn. Apologies for not being able to offer a longer reply.
- **Description:** Type-written letter (1 page). Page size: 22.5 x 15.5 cm.
- **Publication:** None (catalogue from the auction firm Bukowskis?).

## Transcription {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Dear Miss Heilborn,<br/><br/>Thank you very, very much for your long letter. I was glad to hear that you enjoyed my books so much. I agree with you about illustrations; it is far better to use one&#39;s imagination, as you clearly have done.<br/><br/>I am very busy at the moment as I am moving house, so I hope you will excuse me if I write only a short letter to you.<br/><br/>Again, thank you for your appreciation and I hope that you will continue to get so much enjoyment out of Aragorn, Gandalf, and all the others!<br/><br/>Yours sincerely,<br/>pp. J.R.R. Tolkien
</p>
</blockquote>
