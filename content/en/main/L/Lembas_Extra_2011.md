<i><b>Lembas Extra 2011: Proceedings of the 6th Tolkien Genootschap Unquendor&#39;s Lustrum</b></i> is an issue of the journal *[Lembas Extra](<Lembas_(journal).md>)*. The issue contains twelve articles and poems (all in English), being the proceedings of the Unquendor's Conference hold at Baarlo in [20](../_/20_May.md)-[22 May](../_/22_May.md) [2011](../_/2011.md).

- [Renée Vink](../R/Renée_Vink.md): "Tolkien versus Wagner, Plus Some Dragontail Soup" (pp. 27–39) &amp; "Der Ring des Nibelungen Synopsis" (pp. 40–2)
