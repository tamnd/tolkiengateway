<i><b>Lembas Extra 2011: Proceedings of the 6th Tolkien Genootschap Unquendor&#39;s Lustrum</b></i> is an issue of the journal *[Lembas Extra](</wiki/Lembas_(journal)> "Lembas (journal)")*. The issue contains twelve articles and poems (all in English), being the proceedings of the Unquendor's Conference hold at Baarlo in [20](/wiki/20_May "20 May")-[22 May](/wiki/22_May) [2011](/wiki/2011).
