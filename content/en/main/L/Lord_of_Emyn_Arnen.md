The **Lord of Emyn Arnen** was a title bestowed upon [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), the last ruling [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor), by [King Aragorn Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn_Elessar "Aragorn Elessar") following the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). The lordship included the hills of [Emyn Arnen](/wiki/Emyn_Arnen), located within [South Ithilien](/wiki/South_Ithilien), and served as the seat of the [Prince of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>. The title honored Faramir’s heritage as a descendant of the [House of Húrin](/wiki/House_of_H%C3%BArin) and his new role as a [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor)’s eastern lands during the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

## History {#History}

### Origins of the Emyn Arnen {#Origins_of_the_Emyn_Arnen}

The [Emyn Arnen](/wiki/Emyn_Arnen) were a range of hills in [South Ithilien](/wiki/South_Ithilien), east of the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) and across [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). These hills were historically the homeland of the [House of Húrin](/wiki/House_of_H%C3%BArin), the ruling house of the [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor). Before the Stewards moved their residence to Minas Tirith during the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), Emyn Arnen was the administrative center for their governance.

The hills of Emyn Arnen fell into neglect during Gondor’s long struggle against [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and its servants. Ithilien became depopulated and dangerous due to the rising influence of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), but after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), it was restored as a flourishing region under the leadership of Prince [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Role in the United Kingdom {#Role_in_the_United_Kingdom}

[Faramir](/wiki/Faramir)'s lordship over Emyn Arnen allowed him to oversee the reconstruction and governance of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien). Under his care, [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) was transformed into a region of peace and beauty, know as the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Garden_of_Gondor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Garden of Gondor (page does not exist)">Garden of Gondor</a>. This role reinforced the bond between [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) through Faramir's and [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn)'s marriage.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
