The **Lord of Emyn Arnen** was a title bestowed upon [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md), the last ruling [Steward of Gondor](../S/Steward_of_Gondor.md), by [King Aragorn Elessar](../A/Aragorn_Elessar.md) following the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). The lordship included the hills of [Emyn Arnen](../E/Emyn_Arnen.md), located within [South Ithilien](../S/South_Ithilien.md), and served as the seat of the [Prince of Ithilien](../P/Prince_of_Ithilien.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>. The title honored Faramir’s heritage as a descendant of the [House of Húrin](../H/House_of_Húrin.md) and his new role as a [Steward of Gondor](../S/Steward_of_Gondor.md)’s eastern lands during the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

## History {#History}

### Origins of the Emyn Arnen {#Origins_of_the_Emyn_Arnen}

The [Emyn Arnen](../E/Emyn_Arnen.md) were a range of hills in [South Ithilien](../S/South_Ithilien.md), east of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) and across [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). These hills were historically the homeland of the [House of Húrin](../H/House_of_Húrin.md), the ruling house of the [Steward of Gondor](../S/Steward_of_Gondor.md). Before the Stewards moved their residence to Minas Tirith during the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), Emyn Arnen was the administrative center for their governance.

The hills of Emyn Arnen fell into neglect during Gondor’s long struggle against [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) and its servants. Ithilien became depopulated and dangerous due to the rising influence of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), but after the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), it was restored as a flourishing region under the leadership of Prince [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Role in the United Kingdom {#Role_in_the_United_Kingdom}

[Faramir](../F/Faramir.md)'s lordship over Emyn Arnen allowed him to oversee the reconstruction and governance of [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md). Under his care, [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) was transformed into a region of peace and beauty, know as the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Garden_of_Gondor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Garden of Gondor (page does not exist)">Garden of Gondor</a>. This role reinforced the bond between [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) through Faramir's and [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md)'s marriage.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
