In [December](../D/December.md) [1963](../_/1963.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Jared C. Lobdell](../J/Jared_Lobdell.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien discusses [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) and his qualities as a reader.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A quotation from the letter appeared in Lobdell's article "C.S. Lewis’s Ransom Stories and Their Eighteenth-Century Ancestry" (p. 230), published in *[Word and Story in C.S. Lewis](../W/Word_and_Story_in_C.S._Lewis.md)*.
