In [December](/wiki/December) [1963](/wiki/1963), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Jared C. Lobdell](/wiki/Jared_Lobdell "Jared Lobdell")**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien discusses [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) and his qualities as a reader.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A quotation from the letter appeared in Lobdell's article "C.S. Lewis’s Ransom Stories and Their Eighteenth-Century Ancestry" (p. 230), published in *[Word and Story in C.S. Lewis](/wiki/Word_and_Story_in_C.S._Lewis)*.
