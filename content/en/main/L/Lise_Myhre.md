**Lise Myhre** (born [1 November](/wiki/1_November) [1975](/wiki/1975)) is a Norwegian cartoonist. Her cartoon *Nemi* includes extensive references to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [Middle-earth stories](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").
