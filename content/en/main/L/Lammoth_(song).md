**Lammoth** is the third track of the [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-themed album [Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth) by [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian). The song is the scream of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) after being attacked by [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) for refusing to give her the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils). The region later being known as the "Great Echo", or [Lammoth](/wiki/Lammoth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
