On [5 August](/wiki/5_August) [1957](/wiki/1957), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[two letters](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to Jerome W. Archer**, Director of Libraries at [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Discussion of the invitation to visit the Marquette University the following spring.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** A description of the letters, and quotation from the second letter, appeared in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*.
- **Archive:** Marquette University.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marq_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Excerpt {#Excerpt}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Continuous over-work since 1951 has begun to tell on me; and I am feeling rather exhausted. I have recently been ill; and I am obliged to cancel plans travel this vacation. [...] Leave of absence will, however, I hope, enable me to clear off before next spring some of the things that are now harassing me; and I am now making preliminary plans for the use of the time, and am trying to fix some dates.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>, Second letter to Jerome W. Archer (5 August 1957)</cite></p>
</blockquote>
