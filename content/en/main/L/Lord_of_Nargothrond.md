**Lord of Nargothrond** was the title of the ruler of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), another name of the [King of Nargothrond](/wiki/King_of_Nargothrond).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Note that other minor nobles are also called "lord of Nargothrond": [Guilin](/wiki/Guilin) and/or his sons, [Gelmir](</wiki/Gelmir_(son_of_Guilin)> "Gelmir (son of Guilin)") and [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor) are also mentioned as such.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
