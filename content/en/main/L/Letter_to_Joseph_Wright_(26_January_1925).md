On [26 January](/wiki/26_January) [1925](/wiki/1925), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Joseph Wright](/wiki/Joseph_Wright)**.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Writing on the occasion of Wright's retirement,<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup> Tolkien reveals that it was Wright's works that had introduced him to philology as a schoolboy.<sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Publication:** An extract of the letter was published in *[The Life of Joseph Wright](/wiki/The_Life_of_Joseph_Wright)*, vol. 2, p. 651.<sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Extract {#Extract}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>In the first place I hope that you are not retiring now because of renewed or increased ill-health, but only to enjoy a very long period of well-earned rest and happiness. I hope I may send you my very best wishes, to yourself and to Mrs. Wright, as a grateful disciple (it was your works, that came into my hands by chance as a schoolboy, that first revealed to me the philology I love) to whom you have shown since so many kindnesses.
</p>
</blockquote>
