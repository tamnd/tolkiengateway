**Lórellin** was a tree-shadowed lake in [Lórien](/wiki/L%C3%B3rien_in_Valinor "Lórien in Valinor"), the gardens of [Irmo](/wiki/Irmo). On an island in the lake, Irmo's spouse [Estë](/wiki/Est%C3%AB) sleeps each day.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lórellin* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Dream Pool", from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=L%C3%B3r%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lórë (page does not exist)">lórë</a></i> ("dream") + *[lin](/wiki/Lin "Lin")* ("pool, mere").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
