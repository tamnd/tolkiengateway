On [18 October](/wiki/18_October) [1954](/wiki/1954), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote <b><a href="/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien" title="Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien">a letter</a> to the Rector of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Li%C3%A8ge" title="wikipedia:University of Liège">University of Liège</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:**The letter concerns the festivities of Tolkien's reception of an honorary doctorate at the University of Liège on [October 2nd](/wiki/2_October "2 October"), [1954](/wiki/1954) (he was suggested as a candidate by [Simonne d'Ardenne](/wiki/Simonne_d%27Ardenne)).
- **Publication:** The letter was reprinted in full in the article "Tolkien and Belgium" by [Johan Vanhecke](/wiki/Johan_Vanhecke), published in *[Lembas Extra: Proceedings of the 5th Unquendor Lustrum](/wiki/Lembas_Extra:_Proceedings_of_the_5th_Unquendor_Lustrum)*.
- **Archive**: The letter is kept at the University Archives in Liège.
