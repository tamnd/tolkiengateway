**LINKWI** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") without any given signification.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> It is related to root <a href="/wiki/LINI" title="LINI">LIN<sup>1</sup>-</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *linqe* ("wet")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *lhimp, lhimmid* ("moisten")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
