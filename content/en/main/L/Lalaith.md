**Urwen**, also called **Lalaith**, was the second child of [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) and [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen), and sister of [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) and [Nienor](/wiki/Nienor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Urwen was a happy child and her parents gave her the [nickname](/wiki/Epess%C3%AB "Epessë") "Laughter" (*[Lalaith](</wiki/Lalaith_(word)> "Lalaith (word)")*), from the nearby river, [Nen Lalaith](/wiki/Nen_Lalaith). Her presence in the house of Húrin brought much joy to [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) that she was often compared to Elven children by most people. Túrin, her older brother, loved his sister, but preferred watching her unseen rather than playing with her as she ran and sang. She was three years old when she died of the [Evil Breath](/wiki/Evil_Breath) sent out from [Angband](/wiki/Angband).<sup id="cite_ref-Childhood_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Childhood-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

After recovering of his ill, Túrin was ordered by his nurse to never mention Lalaith again, and speak of Urwen instead. His mother explained laughter had died in their home, blaming the [Enemy](/wiki/Enemy).<sup id="cite_ref-Childhood_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Childhood-2">[2]</a></sup> Urwen's death was especially hard on Túrin, who always looked for her likeness in women ever after,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> which is shown by how he considers [Finduilas](/wiki/Finduilas) to be similar to how he imagined an older Urwen.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Urwen* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), but it is not glossed, although it is clearly the combination of the elements <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=%C3%9Br&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ûr (page does not exist)">ûr</a></i> ("fire, hot") + *[gwenn](/wiki/Gwenn "Gwenn")* ("maiden").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Her nickname, *[lalaith](</wiki/Lalaith_(word)> "Lalaith (word)")*, is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) noun meaning "laughter".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
