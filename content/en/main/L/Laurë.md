## Quenya {#Quenya}

<i><b>laurë</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word glossed as:

1. "gold, not the metal but the colour, what we would should call golden light"<sup id="cite_ref-L230_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L230-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-silmap_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmap-2">[2]</a></sup>
2. "golden colour of sunshine or golden flowers"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>
3. "light of the golden Tree *Laurelin*, gold"<sup id="cite_ref-etym_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-4">[4]</a></sup>
4. "gold"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UTG_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTG-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

In the manuscript "Eldarin Roots and Stems", *laure* derives from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [LAW](/wiki/LAW).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), *laure* derives from root [LÁWAR-](/wiki/LAW#Other_versions "LAW").<sup id="cite_ref-etym_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- <i>Inga<b>laurë</b></i>, "[Inga](/wiki/Vanyar#Etymology "Vanyar")-gold"; the [mother-name](/wiki/Amiless%C3%AB "Amilessë") of [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin) due to his golden hair of his mother's people<sup id="cite_ref-morg_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-morg-6">[6]</a></sup>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Laurelin" title="Laurelin"><b>Laure</b>lin</a></i>, "Song of Gold"; name of one of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees) of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor)<sup id="cite_ref-silmindex_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmindex-7">[7]</a></sup>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien" title="Lothlórien"><b>Laure</b>lindórenan</a></i>, "Valley of Singing Gold";<sup id="cite_ref-UTG_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTG-5">[5]</a></sup> and older name for [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien)<sup id="cite_ref-rotk_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rotk-8">[8]</a></sup>
- <i><b>Laure</b>nandë</i>, "Valley of Gold"; another name for Lothlórien<sup id="cite_ref-UTG_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTG-5">[5]</a></sup>
- *laurië* (plural adjective), "golden"<sup id="cite_ref-rgeo_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rgeo-9">[9]</a></sup>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Laurinqu%C3%AB" title="Laurinquë"><b>laur</b>inquë</a></i>, possible "gold-ful one";<sup id="cite_ref-arda_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-arda-10">[10]</a></sup> a tree in [Hyarrostar](/wiki/Hyarrostar) in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) with yellow flowers<sup id="cite_ref-uttree_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-uttree-11">[11]</a></sup>
- <i>Maka<b>laurë</b></i>, "forging gold"; a prophetic mother-name for [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor)<sup id="cite_ref-pome_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pome-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Qenya {#Qenya}

<i><b>laurë</b></i> is a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) word meaning "gold". The word derives from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") LAURE.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> A related form is *laurina*, "golden".<sup id="cite_ref-bolt_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bolt-14">[14]</a></sup>
