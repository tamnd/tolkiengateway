**Lord of Belegost** was a title for one who ruled over the [Dwarven](../D/Dwarves.md) city of [Belegost](../B/Belegost.md). He was probably descended by one of the [Seven Fathers of the Dwarves](../S/Seven_Fathers_of_the_Dwarves.md). An heirloom of the Lords of Belegost had been the pearl [Nimphelos](../N/Nimphelos.md) given by [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The only known Lord of Belegost was [Azaghâl](../A/Azaghâl.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, [Bodruith](../B/Bodruith.md) was the Lord of the [Dwarves of Belegost](../D/Dwarves_of_Belegost.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> during a [battle in the Thousand Caves](../B/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves.md) against [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md). He was later slain by [Ufedhin](../U/Ufedhin.md), an [Elf of Artanor](../E/Elves_of_Doriath.md), after attempting to kill and steal the [Nauglafring](../N/Nauglafring.md) from him out of greed.
