**Lord of Belegost** was a title for one who ruled over the [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") city of [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost). He was probably descended by one of the [Seven Fathers of the Dwarves](/wiki/Seven_Fathers_of_the_Dwarves). An heirloom of the Lords of Belegost had been the pearl [Nimphelos](/wiki/Nimphelos) given by [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The only known Lord of Belegost was [Azaghâl](/wiki/Azagh%C3%A2l).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, [Bodruith](/wiki/Bodruith) was the Lord of the [Dwarves of Belegost](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Belegost)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> during a [battle in the Thousand Caves](/wiki/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves "Battle in the Thousand Caves") against [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth). He was later slain by [Ufedhin](/wiki/Ufedhin), an [Elf of Artanor](/wiki/Elves_of_Doriath "Elves of Doriath"), after attempting to kill and steal the [Nauglafring](/wiki/Nauglafring) from him out of greed.
