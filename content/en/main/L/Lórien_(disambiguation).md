**Lórien** can refer to:

## Arda {#Arda}

- **[Irmo](/wiki/Irmo)**, the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") who was also referred to as *Lórien*.
    - The gardens of **[Lórien](</wiki/L%C3%B3rien_(Valinor)> "Lórien (Valinor)")** in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), the abode of Irmo.
- The **Kingdom of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien)** in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), often referred to as *Lórien*.
    - [Elves of Lórien](/wiki/Galadhrim "Galadhrim"), the Silvan Elves - or *Galathrim* - who lived there.
    - [King of Lórien](/wiki/King_of_L%C3%B3rien), the former title of the rulers of Lothlórien.
    - [Lord and Lady of Lórien](/wiki/Lord_and_Lady_of_L%C3%B3rien), the titles taken by [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel).

## Books {#Books}

- *[Farewell to Lórien](/wiki/Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien)*, eighth chapter of Book II of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien_(The_Treason_of_Isengard)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Farewell to Lórien (The Treason of Isengard) (page does not exist)">Farewell to Lórien (The Treason of Isengard)</a></i>, fourteenth chapter of *[The Treason of Isengard](/wiki/The_Treason_of_Isengard)*.
- *[The Story Foreseen from Lórien](/wiki/The_Story_Foreseen_from_L%C3%B3rien)*, the sixteenth chapter of *The Treason of Isengard*.
