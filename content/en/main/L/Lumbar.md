**Lumbar** was one of the bright stars placed in the sky by [Varda](/wiki/Varda) at the time of the awakening of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Actually, this "star" is the planet <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn" title="wikipedia:Saturn">Saturn</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lumbar* is an obscure name. It could be [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Shadow Home", as Paul Strack explains, being a compound of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lumb%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lumbë (page does not exist)">lumbë</a></i> ("gloom, shadow") + *[már](/wiki/M%C3%A1r "Már")* ("home").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
