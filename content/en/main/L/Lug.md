**Lug** was an [Orc](../O/Orcs.md) slain by [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), Lug was among those Orcs which assailed the [Gondothlim](../G/Gondothlim.md) alongside the [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md) at the coming of the [Drakes](../D/Dragons.md). Lug was ultimately slain by Tuor, who smote him with his axe, [Dramborleg](../D/Dramborleg.md), which cut his limbs out from beneath him in the process.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2Gondolin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2Gondolin-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lug* or *Lûg*<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> is a [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) name meaning "[snake](../S/Snakes.md)". Its [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) form was *Lôke*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Lug never appeared within any of Tolkien's writing after "[The Fall of Gondolin](<../T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md>)" in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.
