**Lug** was an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") slain by [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), Lug was among those Orcs which assailed the [Gondothlim](/wiki/Gondothlim) alongside the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) at the coming of the [Drakes](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons"). Lug was ultimately slain by Tuor, who smote him with his axe, [Dramborleg](/wiki/Dramborleg), which cut his limbs out from beneath him in the process.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2Gondolin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2Gondolin-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Lug* or *Lûg*<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> is a [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name meaning "[snake](/wiki/Snakes "Snakes")". Its [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) form was *Lôke*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Lug never appeared within any of Tolkien's writing after "[The Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")" in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.
