The **Lord of Lossarnach** was the lord of the flowered vales of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) that lay to the south and west of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). Almost nothing is known of the history and descent of this title, except the name of its holder during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). That was [Forlong the Fat](../F/Forlong_the_Fat.md), who rode to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) with two hundred men from his homeland,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and fell in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Whether Forlong left an heir to inherit his lordship is unknown.
