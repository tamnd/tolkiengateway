On [17 February](/wiki/17_February) [1958](/wiki/1958), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") to Jonathan Wordsworth**.<sup id="cite_ref-note_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Tolkien invites Wordsworth for dinner.
- **Publication:** A short summary was included in [The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Transcription {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>12:45 am Monday 17/2/58<br/>
My dear Wordsworth,<br/>Tuesday Feb. 25.  Dinner at 7:30. I shall be in my room (Fellows 4) from 7 p.m. onwards.  I cannot guarantee any entertainment, but it will be delightful to see you again. Yours  J.R.R.T.
</p>
</blockquote>

## Notes {#Notes}

- Wordsworth added a note in red pen that reads: *'Flattering, don't you think, that he should write even before going to bed!'* <sup id="cite_ref-note_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-note-1">[1]</a></sup>(in reference to the time of writing).
- His son Charles Wordsworth sold the letter in 2006. The money was used to purchase a billiard table.<sup id="cite_ref-note_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-note-1">[1]</a></sup>
