**Lossarnach** was a region in the south of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

Lossarnach was located near the sources of the rivers [Sirith](/wiki/Sirith) and [Erui](/wiki/Erui) in the north-east<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup> of [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin)<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup> south of the eastern end of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> The name Lossarnach or Arnach referred to the valleys in the south of the White Mountains and to their foothills between the rivers [Celos](/wiki/Celos) and Erui.<sup id="cite_ref-VT17_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT17-5">[4]</a></sup> Many flowers grew in its upper valleys. Below is upper valleys were great orchards of fruit trees, which supplied much of the fruit that was required in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). People from Minas Tirith often made trips to Lossarnach to look at the flowers and the blossoms of the fruit trees.<sup id="cite_ref-VT18_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT18-6">[5]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) before the [Siege of Minas Tirith](/wiki/Siege_of_Minas_Tirith), the elderly and most of the women and children were sent to [Tumladen](</wiki/Tumladen_(Gondor)> "Tumladen (Gondor)") and Lossarnach to seek refuge.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> The lord of Lossarnach, old [Forlong](/wiki/Forlong) the Fat, led two hundred men to aid in the defence Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-8">[7]</a></sup> Forlong was killed in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> but many of his men survived and accompanied [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) Elessar on his way to the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate), even though most of them were farmers.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup> It is possible that the mountain-vales, where the ancestors of [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond) came from, were located in Lossarnach.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

Famous people from the region included the wise-woman [Ioreth](/wiki/Ioreth) who served in the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing) of Minas Tirith,<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup> and [Morwen Steelsheen](/wiki/Morwen_Steelsheen), wife of King [Thengel](/wiki/Thengel) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Mark_Fisher_-_Ithilien.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/5/56/Mark_Fisher_-_Ithilien.png/180px-Mark_Fisher_-_Ithilien.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="287" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/5/56/Mark_Fisher_-_Ithilien.png/270px-Mark_Fisher_-_Ithilien.png 1.5x, /w/images/5/56/Mark_Fisher_-_Ithilien.png 2x" data-file-width="302" data-file-height="482"/></a><figcaption>Map of eastern Gondor, by Mark Fisher</figcaption></figure>

*Lossarnach* is a mixed [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and [Pre-Númenórean](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) name. It means "flowery Arnach".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup> The second element, *[Arnach](/wiki/Arnach)* is a Pre-Númenórean name whose origin is forgotten.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup> It is unclear why *[loss](/wiki/Loss "Loss")*, the Sindarin word for "snow", was prefixed to Arnach. It is probable that *loss* referred to the blossoms of the fruit trees. The words for flower, *[loth](/wiki/Loth "Loth")*, and for snow, *loss* are very similar in Sindarin.<sup id="cite_ref-VT18_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT18-6">[5]</a></sup> The [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") settlers, mariners, soldiers and other emigrants, who gave names to the places in Gondor in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), did not know Sindarin well.<sup id="cite_ref-VT17_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT17-5">[4]</a></sup> As a consequence, it is possible that they confused the Sindarin word for flowers with the Sindarin word for snow.

<dl><dd>Lossarnach was an area in the region of Eastern <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>. It was across the river <a href="/wiki/Erui" title="Erui">Erui</a> from Upper <a href="/wiki/Lebennin" title="Lebennin">Lebennin</a> to the west and across the river <a href="/wiki/Anduin" title="Anduin">Anduin</a> from South <a href="/wiki/Ithilien" title="Ithilien">Ithilien</a> to the east. The <a href="/wiki/Rammas_Echor" title="Rammas Echor">Rammas Echor</a> divided it from the <a href="/wiki/Pelennor_Fields" title="Pelennor Fields">Pelennor</a> to the north-east. Settlements in Lossarnach included the capital city of Arnach, <a href="/wiki/Imloth_Melui" title="Imloth Melui">Imloth Melui</a>, <a href="/wiki/Harlond_(Gondor)" title="Harlond (Gondor)">Harlond</a>, and &#34;Ost Norhir&#34;, though the latter two have fallen to the <a href="/wiki/Minas_Morgul" title="Minas Morgul">Morgul</a>-host. The emblem of Lossarnach was three red roses on a white field. Baranor, Captain of the Guard of Arnach, and the Lady Vanyalos, sister of <a href="/wiki/Denethor" title="Denethor">Denethor</a> and wife of <a href="/wiki/Forlong" title="Forlong">Forlong</a>, were in charge of the land when Forlong went to <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>.</dd></dl>
