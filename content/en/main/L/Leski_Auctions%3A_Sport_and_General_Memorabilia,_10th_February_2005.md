<i><b>Leski Auctions: Sport and General Memorabilia, 10th February 2005</b></i> is an auction catalogue from Charles Leski Auctions. The description for Lot 72 includes a full reproduction (photographs) of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [letter to Mr(s?) Souch (8 September 1955)](Letter_to_Mrs_Souch.md).
