**Lord of the Rings: The Search** is a board game based on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, depicting [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") searching for [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom). The game features artwork by [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe).<sup id="cite_ref-Soph_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Soph-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This is a tile based game with a 30-40 minute playing time. Two Hobbits, Sam and Frodo, begin their journey in the Shire and travel through the unknown lands of Middle Earth. Intriguing design with excellent replayability has already made it a bestseller wherever it has been published.<sup id="cite_ref-Soph_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Soph-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>
