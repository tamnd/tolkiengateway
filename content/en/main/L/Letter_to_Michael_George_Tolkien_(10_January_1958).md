On [10 January](../_/10_January.md) [1958](../_/1958.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Michael George Tolkien](../M/Michael_George_Tolkien.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Letter sent on the occasion of Michael's 15th birthday.
- **Description:** Autograph letter, oblong 8vo. 1 p. Tolkien's lettercard stationery with printed Merton letterhead.

## Transcription {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Many Happy Returns of your birthday! I failed to reach the P. Office today, or I should have sent you a small contribution (at the rate of a bob a year!). However, I have asked your father to do so on my behalf. Very much love to you, &amp; I hope next term will prove better than usual.<br/><br/>Your Grandfather
</p>
</blockquote>
