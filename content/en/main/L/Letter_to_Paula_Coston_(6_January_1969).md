On [6 January](/wiki/6_January) [1969](/wiki/1969), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Paula Coston**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- **Description:** 1 page, handwritten.
- **Publication:** A picture of the letter was published in [*Writing Magazine*, August 2014](</wiki/Writing_Magazine_(August_2014)> "Writing Magazine (August 2014)").

<h2><span id="From_the_Magazine.27s_editor"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="From_the_Magazine&#39;s_editor">From the Magazine&#39;s editor</span></h2>

"Paula did tell me she’s always jealously guarded the letters (and mentioned in the article not letting the biographers see them) so I believe this is the first time the contents will have been published anywhere, at least some of them."
