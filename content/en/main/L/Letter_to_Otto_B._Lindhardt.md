On [2 January](/wiki/2_January) [1967](/wiki/1967), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to Otto B. Lindhardt** (of Gyldendals Bibliotek, the then future Danish publishers of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Guidelines for a Danish translation of *The Lord of the Rings*.
- **Publication:** Quotes from the letter were published in *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_A_Reader%27s_Companion)* and in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: I. Chronology](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide "The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide")*.
- **Archive:** Tolkien-[George Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin "Allen and Unwin") archive, [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins).
