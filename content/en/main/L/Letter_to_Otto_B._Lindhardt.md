On [2 January](../_/2_January.md) [1967](../_/1967.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote **[a letter](Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to Otto B. Lindhardt** (of Gyldendals Bibliotek, the then future Danish publishers of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **Subject:** Guidelines for a Danish translation of *The Lord of the Rings*.
- **Publication:** Quotes from the letter were published in *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_A_Reader's_Companion.md)* and in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide: I. Chronology](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md)*.
- **Archive:** Tolkien-[George Allen &amp; Unwin](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md) archive, [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md).
