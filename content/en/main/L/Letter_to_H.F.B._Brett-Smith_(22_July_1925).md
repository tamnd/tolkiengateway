On [22 July](/wiki/22_July) [1925](/wiki/1925), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote **[a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") to** H.F.B. Brett-Smith<b>.<sup id="cite_ref-wheaton_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wheaton-1">[1]</a></sup></b>

- **Description:** Handwritten letter, 2 pages.
- **Subject:** Tolkien is discussing academia at Leeds University and Tolkien's poetry.
- **Publication:** An extract was publish in <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Catalogue_37:_Books_from_a_Private_Library_(with_Additions)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Catalogue 37: Books from a Private Library (with Additions) (page does not exist)">Catalogue 37: Books from a Private Library (with Additions)</a><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Archive:** The original letter is kept at The Marion E. Wade Center.<sup id="cite_ref-wheaton_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wheaton-1">[1]</a></sup>
