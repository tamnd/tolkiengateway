The **Lord and Lady of Lórien**, also **Lord and Lady of the Galadhrim**, often named individually, were [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), and his wife [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) who jointly ruled [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), the realm of the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim).

## History {#History}

The Galadhrim were [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves), who were ruled by the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") [King of Lórien](/wiki/King_of_L%C3%B3rien); after the loss of the last King [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Celeborn and Galadriel came to rule the realm, although they renounced the royal title of "King" and "Queen".

Under them, the realm prospered for a millennium. With the power of [Nenya](/wiki/Nenya), Galadriel kept the land pure and alive, and evil was not permitted to penetrate it. They built [Caras Galadhon](/wiki/Caras_Galadhon) western across the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) where she planted the seeds of the [mallorn](/wiki/Mallorn "Mallorn") trees that was given to her by [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) they received the remnant of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring), and also repulsed several assaults of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) from [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur), until eventually Celeborn counter-attacked, Galadriel threw down its walls, and [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) was cleansed, making the southern part of the forest [a province](/wiki/East_L%C3%B3rien "East Lórien") of their realm.<sup id="cite_ref-great_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the War, Galadriel took a ship to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) and Celeborn ruled the Galadhrim until he grew weary and left for [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), leaving the wooded land.<sup id="cite_ref-great_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-3">[3]</a></sup>
