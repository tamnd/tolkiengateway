The **Lord and Lady of Lórien**, also **Lord and Lady of the Galadhrim**, often named individually, were [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md), and his wife [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) who jointly ruled [Lothlórien](Lothlórien.md), the realm of the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md).

## History {#History}

The Galadhrim were [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md), who were ruled by the [Sindarin](../S/Sindar.md) [King of Lórien](../K/King_of_Lórien.md); after the loss of the last King [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1981](../T/Third_Age_1981.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Celeborn and Galadriel came to rule the realm, although they renounced the royal title of "King" and "Queen".

Under them, the realm prospered for a millennium. With the power of [Nenya](../N/Nenya.md), Galadriel kept the land pure and alive, and evil was not permitted to penetrate it. They built [Caras Galadhon](../C/Caras_Galadhon.md) western across the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) where she planted the seeds of the [mallorn](../M/Mallorn.md) trees that was given to her by [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) they received the remnant of the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md), and also repulsed several assaults of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) from [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md), until eventually Celeborn counter-attacked, Galadriel threw down its walls, and [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) was cleansed, making the southern part of the forest [a province](../E/East_Lórien.md) of their realm.<sup id="cite_ref-great_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the War, Galadriel took a ship to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) and Celeborn ruled the Galadhrim until he grew weary and left for [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), leaving the wooded land.<sup id="cite_ref-great_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-3">[3]</a></sup>
