**Lisa Star** edited the journal *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](../T/Tyalië_Tyelelliéva.md)* until [2001](../_/2001.md). She also used to moderate the Elvish Linguistics mailing list.

Star had criticised the "Elfconners" ([Arden Smith](../A/Arden_Smith.md), [Carl Hostetter](../C/Carl_Hostetter.md), [Patrick Wynne](../P/Patrick_Wynne.md), [Christopher Gilson](../C/Christopher_Gilson.md)) for their publishing methods. On [28 October](../_/28_October.md) [1996](../_/1996.md) she posted in [Tolklang](../T/Tolklang.md) her objections and requested a rapid publication of the original material.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She would later mention in her website, "There is no love lost between Carl Hostetter and me".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The information she provided has sometimes been criticised as inaccurate or imprecise,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> such as the reference to the supposed language "Mørk" by [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

As of [2008](../_/2008.md) Star apparently has withdrawn from the [Tolkien scene](../T/Tolkien_fandom.md) and nobody seemed to be able to contact her.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Her official website received a final update in March [2009](../_/2009.md) before eventually disappearing because of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geocities" title="wikipedia:Geocities">Geocities</a> shutting down.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
