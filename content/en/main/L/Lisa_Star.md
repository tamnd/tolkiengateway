**Lisa Star** edited the journal *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va)* until [2001](/wiki/2001). She also used to moderate the Elvish Linguistics mailing list.

Star had criticised the "Elfconners" ([Arden Smith](/wiki/Arden_Smith), [Carl Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_Hostetter), [Patrick Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_Wynne), [Christopher Gilson](/wiki/Christopher_Gilson)) for their publishing methods. On [28 October](/wiki/28_October) [1996](/wiki/1996) she posted in [Tolklang](/wiki/Tolklang) her objections and requested a rapid publication of the original material.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She would later mention in her website, "There is no love lost between Carl Hostetter and me".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The information she provided has sometimes been criticised as inaccurate or imprecise,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> such as the reference to the supposed language "Mørk" by [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

As of [2008](/wiki/2008) Star apparently has withdrawn from the [Tolkien scene](/wiki/Tolkien_fandom "Tolkien fandom") and nobody seemed to be able to contact her.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Her official website received a final update in March [2009](/wiki/2009) before eventually disappearing because of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geocities" title="wikipedia:Geocities">Geocities</a> shutting down.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
