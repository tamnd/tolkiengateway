The **Lord of the Rings Fanatics Plaza** is a website that hosts forums dedicated to the discussion of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and [Tolkien's](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") works. The original URL *lotrplaza.com* was taken down in the mid-2010s<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and the site remained non-existent until it was revived during the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_Pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 Pandemic">COVID-19 pandemic</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
