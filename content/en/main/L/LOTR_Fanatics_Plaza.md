The **Lord of the Rings Fanatics Plaza** is a website that hosts forums dedicated to the discussion of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) and [Tolkien's](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) works. The original URL *lotrplaza.com* was taken down in the mid-2010s<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and the site remained non-existent until it was revived during the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_Pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 Pandemic">COVID-19 pandemic</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
