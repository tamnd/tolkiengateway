**Olwë** was one of the two kings of the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri), the other being his elder<sup id="cite_ref-PM_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-9">[7]</a></sup> brother [Elwë](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol"), Lord of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). As the king of [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB),<sup id="cite_ref-king_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-king-1">[1]</a></sup> he ruled the Teleri of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), also known as the [Falmari](/wiki/Falmari). He may have had a younger brother, [Elmo](/wiki/Elmo).<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-4">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### In Middle-earth {#In_Middle-earth}

Olwë was born in [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen) and was the younger brother of [Elwë](/wiki/Elw%C3%AB), one of the ambassadors taken by [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) to see the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees). When the ambassadors returned, they urged their peoples to hear the summons of Oromë and go to the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/West" title="West">West</a>. Because their clan was the largest, it was divided in two hosts under the two brothers and together, they led their people.<sup id="cite_ref-capt_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-capt-10">[8]</a></sup>

The [Nelyar](/wiki/Nelyar) were the most unwilling during the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey) and Olwë's host was always the hindmost.<sup id="cite_ref-capt_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-capt-10">[8]</a></sup> [Some](/wiki/Nandor "Nandor") of Olwë's host were terrified by the shadow of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) and preferred to settle by the woodlands of the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-capt_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-capt-10">[8]</a></sup>

During a long wait in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), [Elwë](/wiki/Elw%C3%AB) disappeared.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[9]</a></sup> After years of searching, Olwë grew impatient, and led the greater part of the Teleri to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), on the insistence of the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-6">[4]</a></sup>

### In Aman {#In_Aman}

Olwë and his people dwelt on the island of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) for a while, where the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) could be by the sea whilst also bathing in the light of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor").<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup> These people became the [Falmari](/wiki/Falmari).<sup id="cite_ref-capt_10-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-capt-10">[8]</a></sup> When they finally came to [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar) some centuries later, the [Falmari](/wiki/Falmari), under the lordship of Olwë, began the building of [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB) with the aid of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[11]</a></sup>

His daughter [Eärwen](/wiki/E%C3%A4rwen) married [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin) son of [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB) and later king of the remaining [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor). Besides Eärwen, Olwë had several sons.<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-6">[4]</a></sup>

#### The Kinslaying at Alqualondë {#The_Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë}

When [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) revolted against the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), he attempted to persuade the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) to join him in order to obtain their ships. They were unmoved, wanting no other realm than [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar) and no other lord than Olwë. [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) reproached Olwë and his people as "fainthearted loiterers" and reminded them that the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) had aided them when they first arrived. Olwë rebuked Fëanor's folly and refused to give him any ships of the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri), which they valued as the Noldor did their gems. [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) withdrew but then led his forces to [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB) to take the ships by force, leading to the [first Kinslaying](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB "Kinslaying at Alqualondë"). As the Noldor rowed the stolen vessels northward, Olwë called for [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB) to stop them, but it was not permitted by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[12]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In one manuscript, the [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) form of *Olwë* is said to be <i><b>Olue</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[13]</a></sup> In another manuscript the Telerin form is similar to the Quenya form: *Olwë*.<sup id="cite_ref-Shibboleth_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibboleth-16">[14]</a></sup>

The Falmari sometimes called him <i><b>Volwë</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Shibboleth_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibboleth-16">[14]</a></sup> Another one of his names was <i><b>Ciriáran</b></i> (meaning "mariner king").<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[15]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earlier versions of the [Quenta Silmarillion](/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion), Olwë was named *Elwë* (while his brother Thingol also had the name *Sindo*<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[16]</a></sup>), and there it was Olwë who was one of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Three_Ambassadors&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Three Ambassadors (page does not exist)">Three Ambassadors</a> instead of his brother Thingol.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[17]</a></sup> Neither did the existence of his daughter Eärwen or her marriage to Finarfin emerge yet.
