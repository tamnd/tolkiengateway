**Olwë** was one of the two kings of the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md), the other being his elder<sup id="cite_ref-PM_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-9">[7]</a></sup> brother [Elwë](../T/Thingol.md), Lord of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). As the king of [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md),<sup id="cite_ref-king_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-king-1">[1]</a></sup> he ruled the Teleri of [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), also known as the [Falmari](../F/Falmari.md). He may have had a younger brother, [Elmo](../E/Elmo.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-4">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### In Middle-earth {#In_Middle-earth}

Olwë was born in [Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md) and was the younger brother of [Elwë](../E/Elwë.md), one of the ambassadors taken by [Oromë](Oromë.md) to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) to see the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md). When the ambassadors returned, they urged their peoples to hear the summons of Oromë and go to the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../W/West.md" title="West">West</a>. Because their clan was the largest, it was divided in two hosts under the two brothers and together, they led their people.<sup id="cite_ref-capt_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-capt-10">[8]</a></sup>

The [Nelyar](../N/Nelyar.md) were the most unwilling during the [Great Journey](../G/Great_Journey.md) and Olwë's host was always the hindmost.<sup id="cite_ref-capt_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-capt-10">[8]</a></sup> [Some](../N/Nandor.md) of Olwë's host were terrified by the shadow of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) and preferred to settle by the woodlands of the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-capt_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-capt-10">[8]</a></sup>

During a long wait in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), [Elwë](../E/Elwë.md) disappeared.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[9]</a></sup> After years of searching, Olwë grew impatient, and led the greater part of the Teleri to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), on the insistence of the [Vala](../V/Valar.md) [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-6">[4]</a></sup>

### In Aman {#In_Aman}

Olwë and his people dwelt on the island of [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) for a while, where the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) could be by the sea whilst also bathing in the light of the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup> These people became the [Falmari](../F/Falmari.md).<sup id="cite_ref-capt_10-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-capt-10">[8]</a></sup> When they finally came to [Eldamar](../E/Eldamar.md) some centuries later, the [Falmari](../F/Falmari.md), under the lordship of Olwë, began the building of [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md) with the aid of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[11]</a></sup>

His daughter [Eärwen](../E/Eärwen.md) married [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md) son of [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md) and later king of the remaining [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md). Besides Eärwen, Olwë had several sons.<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-6">[4]</a></sup>

#### The Kinslaying at Alqualondë {#The_Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë}

When [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) revolted against the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), he attempted to persuade the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) to join him in order to obtain their ships. They were unmoved, wanting no other realm than [Eldamar](../E/Eldamar.md) and no other lord than Olwë. [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) reproached Olwë and his people as "fainthearted loiterers" and reminded them that the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) had aided them when they first arrived. Olwë rebuked Fëanor's folly and refused to give him any ships of the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md), which they valued as the Noldor did their gems. [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) withdrew but then led his forces to [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md) to take the ships by force, leading to the [first Kinslaying](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md). As the Noldor rowed the stolen vessels northward, Olwë called for [Ossë](Ossë.md) to stop them, but it was not permitted by the [Valar](../V/Valar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[12]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In one manuscript, the [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) form of *Olwë* is said to be <i><b>Olue</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[13]</a></sup> In another manuscript the Telerin form is similar to the Quenya form: *Olwë*.<sup id="cite_ref-Shibboleth_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibboleth-16">[14]</a></sup>

The Falmari sometimes called him <i><b>Volwë</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Shibboleth_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibboleth-16">[14]</a></sup> Another one of his names was <i><b>Ciriáran</b></i> (meaning "mariner king").<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[15]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earlier versions of the [Quenta Silmarillion](../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion.md), Olwë was named *Elwë* (while his brother Thingol also had the name *Sindo*<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[16]</a></sup>), and there it was Olwë who was one of the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Three_Ambassadors&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Three Ambassadors (page does not exist)">Three Ambassadors</a> instead of his brother Thingol.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[17]</a></sup> Neither did the existence of his daughter Eärwen or her marriage to Finarfin emerge yet.
