**Orthanc** was the impregnable tower of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) built by the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) during the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

The tower of Orthanc was composed of four welded pillars of many-sided stone that stood in the centre of the [Ring of Isengard](../R/Ring_of_Isengard.md) like an island, roughly half a mile from the rim.<sup id="cite_ref-TTRoad_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTRoad-2">[2]</a></sup>

At its peak the tower reached a height of just over five hundred feet. The colour of the rock was a deep, gleaming black. At Orthanc's pinnacle the four piers opened out to form four pinnacles of sharp rock; between these isles of rock there was a narrow, polished floor on which many strange signs were written.<sup id="cite_ref-TTRoad_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTRoad-2">[2]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>There stood a tower of marvellous shape. It was fashioned by the builders of old, who smoothed the Ring of Isengard, and yet it seemed a thing not made by the craft of Men, but riven from the bones of the earth in the ancient torment of the hills.
</p>

## History {#History}

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_Orthanc_in_the_Second_Age.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/dc/Ted_Nasmith_-_Orthanc_in_the_Second_Age.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Orthanc_in_the_Second_Age.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="257" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/dc/Ted_Nasmith_-_Orthanc_in_the_Second_Age.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Orthanc_in_the_Second_Age.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/dc/Ted_Nasmith_-_Orthanc_in_the_Second_Age.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Orthanc_in_the_Second_Age.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1120" data-file-height="960"/></a><figcaption><i>Orthanc in the Second Age</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

The exact date of the construction of Orthanc and the establishment of the fortress of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) is unknown beyond it being made by the Men of Númenor long ago.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Within Orthanc was placed one of the *[Palantíri](../P/Palantíri.md)*, the [Orthanc-stone](Orthanc-stone.md). Thus, Orthanc became one of the seven [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) towers which stored the seeing stones that had been brought from [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SV_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SV-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

The region of [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) was never densely populated; indeed it suffered greatly in the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md) and many inhabitants of Númenórean descent gradually migrated eastward. Eventually the tower of Orthanc itself became deserted, and [its keys](../K/Key_of_Orthanc.md) were removed to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) and held in the keeping of the [Stewards](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UTPalantiri_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTPalantiri-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md),<sup id="cite_ref-UTIsen_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTIsen-6">[6]</a></sup> after the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](../B/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md), the lands of [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) were ceded to the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) and [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md) became the first king of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). Nonetheless Gondor retained control of the fortress of Isengard, and Orthanc remained the third tower of the southern realm. During this time Isengard was manned by a small number of Gondorians led by a hereditary Captain. However the keys of Orthanc were kept by the Stewards of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-UTCirion_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTCirion-7">[7]</a></sup>

Over time the links and correspondence between Isengard and [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) became ever more infrequent; whilst retaining the keys of Orthanc, the Stewards forsook the tower as their thoughts looked eastwards. Eventually the line of Gondorian chieftains failed and the people mingled ever more with the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md). They even allowed them entry into Isengard and eventually the two peoples willingly merged into one. Thus it was that Isengard came under the control of the Dunlendings who led numerous raids on the [Westfold](../W/Westfold.md). Whilst the Rohirrim had not the strength to assail the fortress, the Dunlendings were eventually starved out in the great famine after the [Long Winter](../L/Long_Winter.md). Therefore both [King Fréaláf](../F/Fréaláf.md) and [Steward Beren](<../B/Beren_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) greatly welcomed [Saruman's](../S/Saruman.md) offer to take command of Isengard. Thus it was that, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2759](../T/Third_Age_2759.md),<sup id="cite_ref-AppStewards_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppStewards-8">[8]</a></sup> the wizard was given the keys of Orthanc.<sup id="cite_ref-UTIsen_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTIsen-6">[6]</a></sup>

Saruman began to desire [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) for himself and hoped that it might reveal itself by giving [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) time to re-gain his strength. As head of the [White Council](../W/White_Council.md), Saruman overruled Gandalf's call for an attack on [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2851](../T/Third_Age_2851.md). However, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md), after Saruman discovered that the servants of Sauron were searching for the Ring near [Gladden Fields](../G/Gladden_Fields.md), he agreed to attack Sauron's fortress in [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md). After the last meeting of the White Council in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2953](../T/Third_Age_2953.md), Saruman withdrew to [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) and fortified it. It was in *circa* [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3000](../T/Third_Age_3000.md) that Saruman used the [Orthanc-stone](Orthanc-stone.md) for the first time. Thus it was that Sauron corrupted Saruman's mind and bent it to his will.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB2_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB2-9">[9]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) rode to the foot of Orthanc and there met [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md). It was then that Saruman revealed his true thoughts to Gandalf. He declared his desire to become a ruler of [Men](../M/Men.md) and to join with [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md). But, most importantly, he announced his desire for the One Ring. Gandalf refused to submit to Sauron and Saruman. Subsequently, Saruman had Gandalf taken to the pinnacle of Orthanc where, Saruman schemed, Gandalf would stay until he revealed the location of the One Ring or until Sauron reclaimed it for himself. There Gandalf was trapped and for the first time he saw [wolves](../W/Wolves.md) and [orcs](Orcs.md) in the valley of Isengard below. After many weeks of imprisonment,<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[note 1]</a></sup> Gandalf escaped as [Gwaihir](../G/Gwaihir.md) the Great [Eagle](../E/Eagle.md) came unlooked-for to Orthanc and bore him away.<sup id="cite_ref-FRCouncil_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRCouncil-11">[10]</a></sup>

On [2 March](../_/2_March.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), the [Ents](../E/Ents.md) of [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md) began their [attack on Isengard](../B/Battle_of_Isengard.md). By the next day, the Ents had completely subdued Isengard.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-12">[11]</a></sup>

However, their attacks on Orthanc proved futile because the rock of the tower was too hard for the Ents to damage. Therein Saruman remained.<sup id="cite_ref-TTFlotsam_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTFlotsam-13">[12]</a></sup> On [5 March](../_/5_March.md), [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md), [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md), [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) approached the foot of Orthanc and climbed its stair until they reached the door. There they parleyed with Saruman, who remained inside the tower and spoke to them from a balcony above the door. Saruman refused to co-operate and subsequently Gandalf cast him from the [order of Wizards](../I/Istari.md) and the [White Council](../W/White_Council.md), and broke Saruman's staff. After this confrontation, [Gríma Wormtongue](../G/Gríma.md), who was also inside Orthanc, threw the [*palantír* of Orthanc](Orthanc-stone.md) at the party outside as a parting shot.<sup id="cite_ref-TTVoice_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTVoice-14">[13]</a></sup>

A week before [22 August](../_/22_August.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-12">[11]</a></sup> when [King Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) and company arrived at Orthanc, Saruman had departed Isengard with Gríma Wormtongue. However, before he left, [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md) made him lock the tower and hand over the keys of Orthanc.<sup id="cite_ref-RKPartings_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKPartings-15">[14]</a></sup>

### Fourth Age {#Fourth_Age}

One of [King Elessar's](../A/Aragorn.md) first tasks in the re-ordering of his realm was the restoration of Orthanc. He ordered that the *[palantír](../P/Palantír.md)* recovered from [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) should be returned to the tower. It was then that the tower was searched and many secrets were revealed. It became clear that Saruman had hoarded many ancient treasures and heirlooms. They found that, with the aid of [Wormtongue](../G/Gríma.md), Saruman had acquired jewels and heirlooms of [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md). With the help of [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) a hidden door was found and opened. Inside a casket found on a shelf they found two precious items. One was a small golden case attached to a chain; this was used by [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) to bear [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md). The second item in the casket was the [Elendilmir](../E/Elendilmir.md), the "white star of Elvish crystal upon a fillet of *[mithril](../M/Mithril.md)*" that had been lost since Isildur's demise. Elessar received the Elendilmir with reverence and took it with him as he established his full kingship of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md). It was clear that Saruman had found the remains of Isildur and that he had probably burned the body.<sup id="cite_ref-UTIsen_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTIsen-6">[6]</a></sup>

Orthanc and the lands of Isengard remained part of the [Reunited Kingdom of Arnor and Gondor](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md). However King Elessar granted the [Ents](../E/Ents.md) complete self-governance of the area surrounding Orthanc. This new forest became known as the [Treegarth of Orthanc](../T/Treegarth_of_Orthanc.md).<sup id="cite_ref-RKPartings_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKPartings-15">[14]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Orthanc* displays a curious double etymology (co-existing "by design or chance") since it has a meaning both in the "[language of the Mark of old](../R/Rohanese.md)" (rendered as [Old English](Old_English.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*) and [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s constructed language [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TTRoad_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTRoad-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ui_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ui-16">[15]</a></sup> Tolkien regarded this coincidence as an 'accident'.<sup id="cite_ref-UTIstari_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTIstari-17">[16]</a></sup>

In Old English, *Orthanc* is said to mean "Cunning Mind".<sup id="cite_ref-TTRoad_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTRoad-2">[2]</a></sup> [Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) has noted that Old English *orþanc* as a noun means "original, inborn thought" or "a skilful contrivance or work, artifice, device, design"; as an adjective it means "cunning, skilful".<sup id="cite_ref-Ui_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ui-16">[15]</a></sup> Cf. the meaning of Saruman's name "the one of cunning devices" or "Man of Skill".<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[17]</a></sup>

In the published text, *Orthanc* is said to mean "Mount Fang" in [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TTRoad_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTRoad-2">[2]</a></sup> In other sources, the name is glossed as "forked-height".<sup id="cite_ref-Ui_16-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ui-16">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[18]</a></sup> The name is likely supposed to be Sindarin,<sup id="cite_ref-Ui_16-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ui-16">[15]</a></sup> consisting of *[or](Or.md)* + *[thanc](../T/Thanc.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[19]</a></sup>

### Films {#Films}

<b>2001-03: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Orthanc&#39;s design in the film is quite congruent with the descriptions in the novel, being presented as a tall, dark spire with four pinnacles. The finer details on the tower&#39;s walls seem to be inspired by the artwork of <a href="../A/Alan_Lee.md" title="Alan Lee">Alan Lee</a>, who was one of the concept artists for the films.</dd>
<dd>Orthanc and <a href="../B/Barad-dûr.md" title="Barad-dûr">Barad-dûr</a> are presented as <i>The Two Towers</i> identified in the title of the film. Tolkien himself was never certain about which two towers <i>The Two Towers</i> referred to; in <a href="../L/Letter_140.md" title="Letter 140">Letter 140</a> noted his disapproval of the title itself but also stated that the title was deliberately ambiguous. Indeed it could refer to Orthanc and Barad-dûr, Minas Tirith and Barad-dûr, or Orthanc and Cirith Ungol.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[20]</a></sup> Later Tolkien seemed to settle with Orthanc and Minas Morgul in his original design for the jacket of <i>The Two Towers</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[21]</a></sup> However the producers of <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i> decided that, for theatrical and stylistic purposes, <i>The Two Towers</i> should refer to the towers of Orthanc and Barad-dûr.</dd></dl>

### Games {#Games}

<b>2004: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(video_game).md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Orthanc is the location of the bonus stage &#34;Tower of Orthanc&#34;, where Aragorn, Gimli, or Legolas must fight through twenty floors of various enemies to reach Saruman at the top. This stage can only be unlocked by completing the &#34;Hornburg Courtyard&#34; stage and reaching Level 10 with one of the three playable characters.</dd></dl>

<b>2004: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(video_game).md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Orthanc is part of the scenery in the mission &#34;The Road to Isengard&#34;, visible as Gandalf makes his way to the dam.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[22]</a></sup> It also appears in cutscenes at various stages of the game.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Orthanc is a key feature in the area of <a href="../N/Nan_Curunír.md" title="Nan Curunír">Nan Curunír</a>, where it dominates the land around it, and the <a href="../R/Ring_of_Isengard.md" title="Ring of Isengard">Ring of Isengard</a>. The tower itself is only accessible in certain scenarios, including the raid &#34;The Tower of Orthanc&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[23]</a></sup></dd></dl>
