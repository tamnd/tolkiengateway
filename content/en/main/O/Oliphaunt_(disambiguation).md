**Oliphaunt** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- **[Oliphaunts](/wiki/Oliphaunts)**, the large beasts used by allies of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).
- **[Oliphaunt (poem)](</wiki/Oliphaunt_(poem)>)**, the poem recited by [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee).

## Publications {#Publications}

- [*Oliphaunt* (book)](</wiki/Oliphaunt_(book)> "Oliphaunt (book)"), an illustrated children's book.
- [Oliphaunt (sheet)](</wiki/Oliphaunt_(sheet)>), reprinting Tolkien's poem.
