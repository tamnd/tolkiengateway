**Ormal** was one of the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps) of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), made by [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB), filled by [Varda](/wiki/Varda) and hallowed by [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) in the south of the world while the Valar still dwelt on [Almaren](/wiki/Almaren). It was destroyed by [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") at least five ages before the beginning of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The destruction created the [Sea of Ringil](/wiki/Sea_of_Ringil), south of the [Sea of Helcar](/wiki/Sea_of_Helcar).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name apparently means "Lofty gold"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> or "High-gold".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> Obviously it is composed of the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [roots](/wiki/Root "Root") [OR](/wiki/OR) and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=MAL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MAL (page does not exist)">MAL</a>.
