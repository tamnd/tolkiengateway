**Oxonmoot** is an annual convention organised by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society). It is usually held at a college of the [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford) in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford), England, and was formerly conducted on the weekend closest to [22 September](/wiki/22_September), the shared birthday of [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins). In recent years, however, it has instead been organised over the first weekend of September.<sup id="cite_ref-OM1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OM1-1">[1]</a></sup>

The moot usually includes talks, a quiz, an art show, a masquerade and other entertainments, a visit to [Tolkien's grave](/wiki/Wolvercote_Cemetery "Wolvercote Cemetery") in north Oxford, and last, but not least, the chance to spend a weekend in the company of other fans of Tolkien's work. Most of the event is held in an Oxford college.
