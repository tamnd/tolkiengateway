**Ordláf** was a son of [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) in the early versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").<sup id="cite_ref-Ord_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ord-1">[1]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about him.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ordláf* is in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English), since it was translated by [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine), but no [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) equivalent is given.<sup id="cite_ref-Ord_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ord-1">[1]</a></sup> In modern English, this could mean "Spear-remainder" ("ord" meaning "spear",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and "láf" meaning "remainder"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>).
