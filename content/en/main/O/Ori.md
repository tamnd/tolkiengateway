**Ori** was one of the thirteen [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Thorin's company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") who journeyed to [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") to challenge the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug). After the death of Smaug, Ori lived at the Lonely Mountain<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-2">[2]</a></sup> until he went to [Moria](/wiki/Moria) as part of [Balin's](/wiki/Balin "Balin") ill-fated [attempt](/wiki/Balin%27s_folk "Balin's folk") to re-take the ancient mansions of the Dwarves.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Family and birth {#Family_and_birth}

Ori's parentage is unknown, although he was a remote kinsman of Thorin and a member of the [House of Durin](/wiki/House_of_Durin).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-4">[4]</a></sup> His birth year is also unknown.

However, in [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' account of the quest to Erebor he stated that the brothers [Fíli](/wiki/F%C3%ADli) and [Kíli](/wiki/K%C3%ADli) were the youngest of the thirteen "by some fifty years",<sup id="cite_ref-Hill_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hill-5">[5]</a></sup> and that when Thorin was missing [Balin](/wiki/Balin) was "the eldest left".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Since Kíli was born in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2864](/wiki/Third_Age_2864 "Third Age 2864") and Balin was born in [2763](/wiki/Third_Age_2763 "Third Age 2763"),<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-4">[4]</a></sup> this would put Ori's birth between [2763](/wiki/Third_Age_2763 "Third Age 2763") and c. 2814, and he would have been between c. 127 and 178 years old during the quest to Erebor.

Ori was a member of the [House of Durin](/wiki/House_of_Durin) and probably lived in the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) with [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) after he began living there following the loss of [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in "Thráin").<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-4">[4]</a></sup>

### The Quest of Erebor {#The_Quest_of_Erebor}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-7">[7]</a></sup> Ori was one of the five Dwarves in the fourth group to arrive at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). He wore a grey hood. During the evening when Thorin called for music, Ori (along with [Dori](/wiki/Dori) and [Nori](/wiki/Nori)) played the flute.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

When [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) was telling the story of the company's adventures to [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn), Ori and Nori were the second pair of Dwarves to arrive.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Joona_Kujanen_-_Last_Moments.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/f/f1/Joona_Kujanen_-_Last_Moments.jpg/200px-Joona_Kujanen_-_Last_Moments.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="237" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/f/f1/Joona_Kujanen_-_Last_Moments.jpg/300px-Joona_Kujanen_-_Last_Moments.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/f/f1/Joona_Kujanen_-_Last_Moments.jpg/400px-Joona_Kujanen_-_Last_Moments.jpg 2x" data-file-width="800" data-file-height="946"/></a><figcaption>Joona Kujanen - <i>Last Moments</i></figcaption></figure>

Ori fought in and survived the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies). He was given his share of the treasure by [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot), and made his home in Erebor.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Expedition to Moria {#Expedition_to_Moria}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2989](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-7">[7]</a></sup> Ori (and Óin) had followed Balin with a [company](/wiki/Balin%27s_folk "Balin's folk") of Dwarves in an attempt to reclaim their ancient mansions of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"), which were already occupied by the [Goblins](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> At first the colony did well, but [less than five years later](/wiki/Third_Age_2994 "Third Age 2994") Balin was killed, the Orcs returned and took the [East-gate](/wiki/Great_Gates "Great Gates"), and Óin's group that he took west to the [West-gate](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin "Doors of Durin") was destroyed except for a few.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Mazarbul_page_2.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/a/a7/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Mazarbul_page_2.jpg/300px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Mazarbul_page_2.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="375" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/a/a7/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Mazarbul_page_2.jpg/450px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Mazarbul_page_2.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/a/a7/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Mazarbul_page_2.jpg/600px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Mazarbul_page_2.jpg 2x" data-file-width="686" data-file-height="858"/></a><figcaption>Page from the <a href="/wiki/Book_of_Mazarbul" title="Book of Mazarbul">Book of Mazarbul</a> written by Ori in <a href="/wiki/Tengwar" title="Tengwar">Tengwar</a>.</figcaption></figure>

Ori retreated to the [Chamber of Mazarbul](/wiki/Chamber_of_Mazarbul) where [Balin's tomb](/wiki/Balin%27s_Tomb "Balin's Tomb") was laid. He wrote the last pages in the [Book of Mazarbul](/wiki/Book_of_Mazarbul) before he and the last few remaining Dwarves perished during a [final stand](/wiki/Last_stand_of_Balin%27s_folk "Last stand of Balin's folk").<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-3">[3]</a></sup> As Ori knew there was no hope to escape, he continued writing in the book, hoping that someone would find it, and would know what happened to them.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

When [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) came to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) he met [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in), who delighted in telling the hobbit about the doings of the renewed dwarf-kingdom in the Lonely Mountain. But when Frodo asked about Ori, [Balin](/wiki/Balin), and [Óin](/wiki/%C3%93in) (also members of Thorin and Company), Glóin's face darkened and he said that he did not know. In fact, their fate was largely why the Dwarves had come to Rivendell at that time, to seek advice.<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Legacy {#Legacy}

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) journeyed through [Moria](/wiki/Moria) and discovered the book that contained the records of the expedition. The last lines were written by Ori, as recognized by [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) due to Ori's recognizable large, bold handwriting using [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) (Gimli also said that Ori could write well and speedily).<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Óri is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. The name means "Violent".<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Films {#Films}

<b>1967: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_film)" title="The Hobbit (1967 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1967 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Ori is omitted. <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin Oakenshield</a> only travels with an unnamed watchman and princess <a href="/wiki/Mika_Milovana" title="Mika Milovana">Mika Milovana</a> of Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-Deitch_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deitch-14">[14]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1977: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)" title="The Hobbit (1977 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1977 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Ori&#39;s voice is provided by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Jack_DeLeon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Jack DeLeon (page does not exist)">Jack DeLeon</a>. However, he only ever speaks in unison with the rest of the Dwarves.</dd></dl>

<b>2012-14: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Ori is portrayed by <a href="/wiki/Adam_Brown" title="Adam Brown">Adam Brown</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting-15">[15]</a></sup> A description of Ori in <i>The Hobbit</i> films was released by the studio:</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt"></blockquote></dd></dl>

Younger brother to Nori and Dori, Ori is a talented artist, and can often be found drawing and writing in his journal. It is Ori who chronicles much of the journey through [The Wild](/wiki/The_Wild) to the shores of the [Long Lake](/wiki/Long_Lake) and the slopes of [The Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"). Polite and well-bred, he is used to be being bossed around by his older brother Dori and is usually biddable, although occasionally he can surprise his fellow companions with his courage and determination.

<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/Warner_Bros." title="Warner Bros.">Warner Bros.</a><sup id="cite_ref-HobbitApp_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HobbitApp-16">[16]</a></sup></cite></p>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

<dl><dd>While in the book <a href="/wiki/F%C3%ADli" title="Fíli">Fíli</a> and <a href="/wiki/K%C3%ADli" title="Kíli">Kíli</a> are said to be the youngest of the thirteen Dwarves in Thorin&#39;s company by at least fifty years, Ori is instead the youngest within <i>The Hobbit</i> films. His weapon is a slingshot; In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Fellowship of the Ring">The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>, his skeleton is shown holding the <a href="/wiki/Book_of_Mazarbul" title="Book of Mazarbul">Book of Mazarbul</a> beside <a href="/wiki/Balin%27s_Tomb" title="Balin&#39;s Tomb">Balin&#39;s tomb</a>.</dd></dl>
