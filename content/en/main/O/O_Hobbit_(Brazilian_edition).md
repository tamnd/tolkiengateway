<i><b>O Hobbit</b></i> is the official Brazilian edition of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, translated to Brazillian Portuguese by Lenita Maria Rimoli Esteves and Almiro Pisetta, with revision by [Ronald Kyrmse](../R/Ronald_Kyrmse.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
