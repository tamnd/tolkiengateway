<i><b>Old Words and Names in the Shire</b></i> was a short treatise written by [Merry Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") sometime after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). In this work Merry discussed the kinship with the [language](/wiki/Rohan_language "Rohan language") of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) of such "[shire-words](/wiki/Hobbitish "Hobbitish")" as *[mathom](/wiki/Mathom "Mathom")* and old elements in place names.

This treatise was kept in the library of [Bucklebury](/wiki/Bucklebury), perhaps one of those important works that weren't copied into the [Red Book](/wiki/Red_Book).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
