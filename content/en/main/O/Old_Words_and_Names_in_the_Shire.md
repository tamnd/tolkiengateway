<i><b>Old Words and Names in the Shire</b></i> was a short treatise written by [Merry Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) sometime after the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). In this work Merry discussed the kinship with the [language](../R/Rohan_language.md) of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) of such "[shire-words](../H/Hobbitish.md)" as *[mathom](../M/Mathom.md)* and old elements in place names.

This treatise was kept in the library of [Bucklebury](../B/Bucklebury.md), perhaps one of those important works that weren't copied into the [Red Book](../R/Red_Book.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
