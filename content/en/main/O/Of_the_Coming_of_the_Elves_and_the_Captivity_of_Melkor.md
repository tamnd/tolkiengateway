**Of the Coming of the Elves and the Captivity of Melkor** is the third chapter of the *[Quenta Silmarillion](../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion.md)* section within *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuiviénen.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/54/Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuivi%C3%A9nen.jpg/200px-Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuivi%C3%A9nen.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="302" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/54/Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuivi%C3%A9nen.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuivi%C3%A9nen.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/54/Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuivi%C3%A9nen.jpg/400px-Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuivi%C3%A9nen.jpg 2x" data-file-width="794" data-file-height="1200"/></a><figcaption><i>At Lake Cuiviénen</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

The [Valar](../V/Valar.md) remained in [Aman](../A/Aman.md) under the light of the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md), and only [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md) and [Oromë](Oromë.md) returned to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) at times. From there they brought tidings that those lands were under [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md)'s shadow. Thus many Valar wished to make war on Melkor, especially [Tulkas](../T/Tulkas.md). But [Mandos](../M/Mandos.md) warned them in name of [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) that they could not make war because the [Firstborn](../F/Firstborn.md) were coming in that age. Then [Varda](../V/Varda.md) prepared the world by placing new and more powerful [stars](../S/Stars.md) in the sky to guide the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) in the darkness of Middle-earth. Under the new lights, the Elves awoke at [Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md), but this only became known to the Valar after Oromë came upon them by chance.

In Cuiviénen the Elves invented [languages](../L/Languages.md) and lived in peace, but they were also tormented by Melkor, who sent [evil creatures](../H/Hunter_and_Rider.md) to plague them and instilled in their hearts fears of the [Rider](../H/Hunter_and_Rider.md). It is said that he also captured many Elves in [Utumno](../U/Utumno.md), and corrupted, them creating [Orcs](Orcs.md) in mockery of the Elves. This was said to be the most evil deed of Melkor.

Returning from Middle-earth, Oromë told the Valar of the Elves, and of the shadows that troubled them. Then the Valar went to war against Melkor in the [Battle of the Powers](../B/Battle_of_the_Powers.md), in which the Elves heard a great shaking and were worried but did not take part in the battle. After many battles, Melkor was found cowering in the depths of Utumno, and [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) wrought the chain [Angainor](../A/Angainor.md) to keep him in captivity. He was [chained](../C/Chaining_of_Melkor.md) by Tulkas and imprisoned in the [Halls of Mandos](../H/Halls_of_Mandos.md). However, many of Melkor's servants escaped and hid in [Angband](../A/Angband.md), and [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) was among these.

The Valar sent Oromë to call the Elves to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), and he choose [Ingwë](../I/Ingwë.md), [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md) and [Elwë](../E/Elwë.md) as ambassadors, because many Elves did not trust the Valar. The three ambassadors told their Kindreds about the [Light of Valinor](../L/Light_of_Valinor.md), and the Elves embarked on the journey across Middle-earth, save a few who refused, and became the dark Elves, or the [Avari](../A/Avari.md). Thus began the [Great Journey](../G/Great_Journey.md) and the [Sundering of the Elves](../S/Sundering_of_the_Elves.md). While the Elves crossed Middle-earth, many of the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md), the the most numerous host, fell off along the way, creating new Elven peoples. The Elves eventually reached [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) by the sea. Here more Teleri stayed, for their love of the ocean, or returned to the forests ([Sindar](../S/Sindar.md)). On the shores of the [Great Sea](../G/Great_Sea.md), Oromë left the Elves to seek the counsel of Manwë.
