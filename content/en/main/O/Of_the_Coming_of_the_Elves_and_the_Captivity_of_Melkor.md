**Of the Coming of the Elves and the Captivity of Melkor** is the third chapter of the *[Quenta Silmarillion](/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion)* section within *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuivi%C3%A9nen.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/5/54/Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuivi%C3%A9nen.jpg/200px-Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuivi%C3%A9nen.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="302" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/5/54/Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuivi%C3%A9nen.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuivi%C3%A9nen.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/5/54/Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuivi%C3%A9nen.jpg/400px-Ted_Nasmith_-_At_Lake_Cuivi%C3%A9nen.jpg 2x" data-file-width="794" data-file-height="1200"/></a><figcaption><i>At Lake Cuiviénen</i> by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

The [Valar](/wiki/Valar) remained in [Aman](/wiki/Aman) under the light of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees), and only [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) and [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) returned to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) at times. From there they brought tidings that those lands were under [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor)'s shadow. Thus many Valar wished to make war on Melkor, especially [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas). But [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos) warned them in name of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) that they could not make war because the [Firstborn](/wiki/Firstborn) were coming in that age. Then [Varda](/wiki/Varda) prepared the world by placing new and more powerful [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars") in the sky to guide the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) in the darkness of Middle-earth. Under the new lights, the Elves awoke at [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen), but this only became known to the Valar after Oromë came upon them by chance.

In Cuiviénen the Elves invented [languages](/wiki/Languages "Languages") and lived in peace, but they were also tormented by Melkor, who sent [evil creatures](/wiki/Hunter_and_Rider "Hunter and Rider") to plague them and instilled in their hearts fears of the [Rider](/wiki/Hunter_and_Rider "Hunter and Rider"). It is said that he also captured many Elves in [Utumno](/wiki/Utumno), and corrupted, them creating [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) in mockery of the Elves. This was said to be the most evil deed of Melkor.

Returning from Middle-earth, Oromë told the Valar of the Elves, and of the shadows that troubled them. Then the Valar went to war against Melkor in the [Battle of the Powers](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers), in which the Elves heard a great shaking and were worried but did not take part in the battle. After many battles, Melkor was found cowering in the depths of Utumno, and [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) wrought the chain [Angainor](/wiki/Angainor) to keep him in captivity. He was [chained](/wiki/Chaining_of_Melkor "Chaining of Melkor") by Tulkas and imprisoned in the [Halls of Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos). However, many of Melkor's servants escaped and hid in [Angband](/wiki/Angband), and [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) was among these.

The Valar sent Oromë to call the Elves to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), and he choose [Ingwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB), [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB) and [Elwë](/wiki/Elw%C3%AB) as ambassadors, because many Elves did not trust the Valar. The three ambassadors told their Kindreds about the [Light of Valinor](/wiki/Light_of_Valinor), and the Elves embarked on the journey across Middle-earth, save a few who refused, and became the dark Elves, or the [Avari](/wiki/Avari). Thus began the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey) and the [Sundering of the Elves](/wiki/Sundering_of_the_Elves). While the Elves crossed Middle-earth, many of the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri), the the most numerous host, fell off along the way, creating new Elven peoples. The Elves eventually reached [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) by the sea. Here more Teleri stayed, for their love of the ocean, or returned to the forests ([Sindar](/wiki/Sindar)). On the shores of the [Great Sea](/wiki/Great_Sea), Oromë left the Elves to seek the counsel of Manwë.
