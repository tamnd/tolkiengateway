**O! Wanderers in the shadowed land** is a song sung by [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) when walking through the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest). Its purpose was to encourage the other hobbits to continue on. The trees of the Old Forest did not seem to like the song.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## The poem {#The_poem}

<div class="poem">
<p><i>O! Wanderers in the shadowed land </i><br/>
<i>despair not! For though dark they stand, </i><br/>
<i>all woods there be must end at last, </i><br/>
<i>and see the open sun go past: </i><br/>
<i>the setting sun, the rising sun,</i><br/>
<i>the day&#39;s end, or the day begun. </i><br/>
<i>For east or west all woods must fail... </i>
</p>
</div>

(Frodo's voice faded there leaving the song incomplete)
