**Olo Proudfoot** ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1346](/wiki/Third_Age_2946 "Third Age 2946")—[S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1435](/wiki/Fourth_Age_14 "Fourth Age 14")) was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Olo was the son of [Odo Proudfoot](/wiki/Odo_Proudfoot), and father of [Sancho](/wiki/Sancho_Proudfoot "Sancho Proudfoot"). Together, they attended [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)'s [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Olo Proudfoot owns a farm between <a href="/wiki/Hobbiton" title="Hobbiton">Hobbiton</a> and <a href="/wiki/Bywater" title="Bywater">Bywater</a>. He occupies the role of a crafting vendor in the game and plays no role in the storyline.</dd></dl>
