The **Oldbuck Family** was an ancient family of Shire-hobbits, who went on to become one of the most well-known families of Shire.

## History {#History}

Consisting of mainly [Stoorish](/wiki/Stoors "Stoors") blood, the members of this prosperous family came from the [Marish](/wiki/Marish), where they traced their heritage to one [Bucca of the Marish](/wiki/Bucca_of_the_Marish), the first [Thain](/wiki/Thain) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). Thainship was in the Oldbuck family until [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2340](/wiki/Third_Age_2340 "Third Age 2340") (S.R. 740).

In that year, they settled across the [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) by [Gorhendad Oldbuck](/wiki/Gorhendad_Oldbuck), who later changed their surname to [Brandybuck](/wiki/Brandybuck_Family "Brandybuck Family"), after the river Brandywine.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Oldbuck" is the English translation of the genuine [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) name *[Zaragamba](/wiki/Zaragamba_Family "Zaragamba Family")*, of the same meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The word <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/buck" title="wiktionary:buck">buck</a></i> means "goat" or "deer".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is the ultimate origin of the placename [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) which means "Land of the deer".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
