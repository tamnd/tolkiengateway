The **Oldbuck Family** was an ancient family of Shire-hobbits, who went on to become one of the most well-known families of Shire.

## History {#History}

Consisting of mainly [Stoorish](../S/Stoors.md) blood, the members of this prosperous family came from the [Marish](../M/Marish.md), where they traced their heritage to one [Bucca of the Marish](../B/Bucca_of_the_Marish.md), the first [Thain](../T/Thain.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). Thainship was in the Oldbuck family until [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2340](../T/Third_Age_2340.md) (S.R. 740).

In that year, they settled across the [Brandywine](../B/Brandywine.md) by [Gorhendad Oldbuck](../G/Gorhendad_Oldbuck.md), who later changed their surname to [Brandybuck](../B/Brandybuck_Family~66350.md), after the river Brandywine.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Oldbuck" is the English translation of the genuine [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) name *[Zaragamba](../Z/Zaragamba_Family.md)*, of the same meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The word <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/buck" title="wiktionary:buck">buck</a></i> means "goat" or "deer".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is the ultimate origin of the placename [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md) which means "Land of the deer".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
