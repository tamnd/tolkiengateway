<i><b>Old Mornings Dawn</b></i> is the seventh full-length album by [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning). The album was released on [5 June](/wiki/5_June), [2013](/wiki/2013), through Napalm Records. *Old Mornings Dawn* was released seven years after their previous full-length album *Oath Bound*, which is the longest gap between any Summoning albums. With two bonus tracks contained by the box-set and vinyl versions, this album is Summoning's longest album to date.

The lyrics for four of the tracks are based on the story of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) the mariner, ancestor of the kings of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). The lyrics of two tracks are based on the poems "City of Present Sorrow" and "Town of Dreams", by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). The rest of the lyrics came from unknown writers or were written by the band in a style that fits within the Tolkien universe.
