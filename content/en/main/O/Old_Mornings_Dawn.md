<i><b>Old Mornings Dawn</b></i> is the seventh full-length album by [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md). The album was released on [5 June](../_/5_June.md), [2013](../_/2013.md), through Napalm Records. *Old Mornings Dawn* was released seven years after their previous full-length album *Oath Bound*, which is the longest gap between any Summoning albums. With two bonus tracks contained by the box-set and vinyl versions, this album is Summoning's longest album to date.

The lyrics for four of the tracks are based on the story of [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md) the mariner, ancestor of the kings of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). The lyrics of two tracks are based on the poems "City of Present Sorrow" and "Town of Dreams", by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). The rest of the lyrics came from unknown writers or were written by the band in a style that fits within the Tolkien universe.
