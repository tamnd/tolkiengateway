**Orc-draught**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> was a burning, yet refreshing, liquid force-fed from a flask to [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) by the [Uruk](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai") [Uglúk](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier outline, [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) is said to have recovered his wits in [the Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol "Tower of Cirith Ungol") following [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob)'s sting due to a draught given to him by the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) to counter poison.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

<b><a href="/wiki/1978" title="1978">1978</a>: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>An Orc steals another&#39;s draught and hits him with his mace, throwing him backward. He feeds the draught first to <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Meriadoc" title="Meriadoc">Merry</a> and then to <a href="/wiki/Peregrin_Took" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a>.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/1997" title="1997">1997</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The orc-draught is depicted in the <a href="/wiki/1997" title="1997">1997</a> expansion <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Lidless_Eye" title="Middle-earth: The Lidless Eye">Middle-earth: The Lidless Eye</a></i> on the card <i>Orc-draughts</i> illustrated by Daniel Frazier. The card has the following effects:</dd></dl>

> Discard to give +1 prowess to all characters in bearer’s company until the end of turn.

<b><a href="/wiki/2002" title="2002">2002</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Peregrin_Took" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a> sees an <a href="/wiki/Uruk-hai" title="Uruk-hai">Uruk</a> drinking orc-draught and asks him to give some to <a href="/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a>, as he is sick. <a href="/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk" title="Uglúk">Uglúk</a> yells &#34;Sick is he? Give him some medicine boys!&#34; and the Uruk feeds Merry the orc-draught as he coughs and splutters and Pippin begs him to stop. The Uruks laugh and Uglúk exclaims &#34;Can&#39;t take his draught!&#34;.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2014" title="2014">2014</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor" title="Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor">Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The green fungus <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Orchamarth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Orchamarth (page does not exist)">Orchamarth</a> is said to be one of the primary ingredients in orc-draught.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2015" title="2015">2015</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game">The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The draught is depicted in the <a href="/wiki/2015" title="2015">2015</a> expansion <i>The Uruk-hai Nightmare</i> on the &#34;Orc-draught&#34; card, illustrated by Rafal Hrynkiewicz . The card has the following effects:</dd></dl>

> Peril. Surge.<br>When Revealed: Either discard an ally you control, or increase the pursuit value by 1.<br>Shadow: Exhaust a character you control.

<b><a href="/wiki/2023" title="2023">2023</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth" title="The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth">The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The orc-draught is depicted on the &#34;Orcish Medicine&#34; card, illustrated by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Irvin_Rodriguez" title="Category:Images by Irvin Rodriguez">Irvin Rodriguez</a>. The card has the following effects:</dd></dl>

> Target creature gains your choice of lifelink or indestructible until end of turn.<br>Amass Orcs 1. (Put a +1/-1 counter on an Army you control. It's also an Orc. If you don't control an Army, create a 0/0 black Orc Army creature token first.)
