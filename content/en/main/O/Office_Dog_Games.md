Office Dog have developed and/or published a handful of Tolkien-related board games:

- [The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring – Trick-Taking Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_%E2%80%93_Trick-Taking_Game) (2024)
- [The Hobbit: There and Back Again](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_There_and_Back_Again) (2025)
- [The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers – Trick-Taking Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_%E2%80%93_Trick-Taking_Game) (2026)
