In an older version of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s conception of the [Languages](/wiki/Languages) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) as expounded in the *[Lhammas](/wiki/Lhammas)*, **Oromëan** is the language phylum to which all [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) languages, such as [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), [Kornoldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin) and [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin), belong. It is there contrasted with [Aulëan](/wiki/Aul%C3%ABan), the language phylum of [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul), the language of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves).

The Oromëan languages are so named because their ancestral language supposedly derives from the speech of [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") of the Forest, who was the first of the Valar to meet the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) at [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

This concept was later dropped and it is mentioned that when Oromë found the Elves they already had devised by themselves [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish).
