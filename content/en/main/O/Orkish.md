**Orkish** was a general term for the jumble of languages used by the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), composed from corrupted borrowings from other languages of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). The variations in Orkish between different tribes and types of Orc were so great that it was often useless for communication, and so a standard language was necessary. [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) devised his [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech) for this purpose in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), but in practice the [Common Tongue](/wiki/Westron "Westron") was more normally used.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is said that it was [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) who invented the speech for the Orcs in the first place,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> thus relating to an earlier concept of [Melkian](/wiki/Melkian) family of tongues.
