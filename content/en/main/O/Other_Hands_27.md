<b><i>Other Hands</i> 27</b> is an issue of *[Other Hands](Other_Hands.md)*, a magazine devoted to *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)*. The theme of the issue is "Magic in the Third Age". Also has linguistic contributions by Jesse Dallin and [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md).
