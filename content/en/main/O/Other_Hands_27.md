<b><i>Other Hands</i> 27</b> is an issue of *[Other Hands](/wiki/Other_Hands)*, a magazine devoted to *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*. The theme of the issue is "Magic in the Third Age". Also has linguistic contributions by Jesse Dallin and [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo).
