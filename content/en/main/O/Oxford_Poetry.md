<i><b>Oxford Poetry</b></i> was an annual poetry book published by the [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford).

## Oxford Poetry 1915 (Hardback) {#Oxford_Poetry_1915_(Hardback)}

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Oxford_Poetry_1915_1st_Edition_HB.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/74/Oxford_Poetry_1915_1st_Edition_HB.jpg/50px-Oxford_Poetry_1915_1st_Edition_HB.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="50" height="77" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/7/74/Oxford_Poetry_1915_1st_Edition_HB.jpg/75px-Oxford_Poetry_1915_1st_Edition_HB.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/7/74/Oxford_Poetry_1915_1st_Edition_HB.jpg/100px-Oxford_Poetry_1915_1st_Edition_HB.jpg 2x" data-file-width="325" data-file-height="499"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Ed. G.D.H. Cole &amp; T.W. Earp. 1st Edition 1915. B.H. Blackwell. Hardback. Pg. 279-280

This book contains the poem '[Goblin Feet](/wiki/Goblin_Feet)' (pp. 64–65).

There were most likely three impressions made, the first in 1915 and the second and third being in 1916, although evidence of the third being produced is skeptical.

- Note: Oxford Poetry 1915 also contains verse by [Naomi Mitchison](/wiki/Naomi_Mitchison), who would later become a devotee of Tolkien's works.
