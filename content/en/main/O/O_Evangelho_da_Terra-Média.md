<i><b>O Evangelho da Terra-Média: Leituras Teológico-literárias da Obra de J. R. R. Tolkien</b></i> is a Brazilian academic book that collects many articles about Tolkien and his relationship with theology and philosophy.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The book presents a reading of literary elements in Tolkien&#39;s works. Experienced in the use of tools of analysis and literary criticism, the authors give prominence to an important aspect of Tolkien&#39;s work, not always well perceived: their religious and theological element.
</p>
</blockquote>
