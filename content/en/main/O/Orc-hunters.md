**Orc-hunters**<sup id="cite_ref-identity_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-identity-1">[1]</a></sup> were [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) which excelled in tracking<sup id="cite_ref-shadow_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-2">[2]</a></sup> that roamed the regions of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) under the rule of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) and later [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).

## History {#History}

Sometime in or prior to the year [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [496](/wiki/First_Age_496 "First Age 496") during the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), one particular band of Orc-hunters roamed the region of [Methiriad](/wiki/Methiriad) near the [West March](/wiki/Nivrim "Nivrim") of the [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the year 496 after the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) erased [Nienor](/wiki/Nienor)'s memory, [Mablung](/wiki/Mablung) and his company found her on [Amon Ethir](/wiki/Amon_Ethir) and began to make their way slowly back to Doriath. After many days, they reached a guarded bridge over the [Taeglin](/wiki/Taeglin) and decided to rest there. However, a roaming "band of Orc-hunters" attacked them unawares, causing Nienor to flee into [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) in terror and confusion. When the Orc-hunters pursued her, she outran them and they were overtaken and killed by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-band_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-band-4">[4]</a></sup>

On [16 March](/wiki/16_March) in the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") of the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) spied two Orc-hunters<sup id="cite_ref-identity_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-identity-1">[1]</a></sup> near the [Barad-dûr Road](/wiki/Sauron%27s_Road "Sauron's Road")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> having a disagreement twenty paces away from them regarding their mission. One of them stated that their bosses [Higher Up](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr "Barad-dûr") kept changing their target; from an Elf to a [dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves")-[man](/wiki/Men "Men"), to even "a pack of rebel [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai)" in the context of the [quarrel](/wiki/Battle_of_Cirith_Ungol "Battle of Cirith Ungol") in the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol). As a result of this, he suggested that it could be all three put together in fact. Following the disagreement, the snuffling Orc skillfully killed the larger one with an arrow and ran off to a nearby [Orc-hold](/wiki/Orc-holds "Orc-holds").<sup id="cite_ref-shadow_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a sketch predating the earliest form of the story of [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin), Nienor was driven into some woods by a baneful drink given to her by [Kurúki](/wiki/Kur%C3%BAki),<sup id="cite_ref-Kurúki_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kurúki-6">[6]</a></sup> instead of a group of Orc-hunters<sup id="cite_ref-band_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-band-4">[4]</a></sup>.

In a discarded revision of chapter five of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) is described as a "foul orc-&lt;illegible&gt;" by [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins). [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff) notes that, though the second half of the word is illegible, it was probably "hunter" and not "thing" because the word lacked "a descender at the end".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
