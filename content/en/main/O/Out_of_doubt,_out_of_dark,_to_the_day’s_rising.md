"**Out of doubt, out of dark to the day's rising**" was a song composed by the [Minstrel](/wiki/Minstrels "Minstrels") [Gléowine](/wiki/Gl%C3%A9owine), mourning the death of [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) [King](/wiki/King_of_Rohan "King of Rohan").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was similar to [a poem](/wiki/Out_of_doubt,_out_of_dark_to_the_day%E2%80%99s_rising "Out of doubt, out of dark to the day’s rising") composed by [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) during [the Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields "Battle of the Pelennor Fields").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Text {#Text}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Out of doubt, out of dark, to the day’s rising<br/>
<a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden" title="Théoden">he</a> rode singing in the sun, <a href="/wiki/Herugrim" title="Herugrim">sword</a> unsheathing.<br/>
Hope he rekindled, and in hope ended;<br/>
over death, over dread, over doom lifted<br/>
out of loss, out of life, unto long glory.
</p>
</div>
