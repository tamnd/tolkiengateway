**Orm** was a sea-captain of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Forodwaith" title="Forodwaith">Forodwaith</a> of [i·Ponōrir](/wiki/Pon%C3%B3rir "Ponórir") in an earlier version of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") according to *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Orm led a group of Forodwaith in the siege of [Kortirion](/wiki/Kortirion), a city located in [Leithian](/wiki/Leithian). During an assault, he killed the father of [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine), [Déor](</wiki/D%C3%A9or_(father_of_%C3%86lfwine)> "Déor (father of Ælfwine)"), who was leading a "desperate sally" defending the city.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>
