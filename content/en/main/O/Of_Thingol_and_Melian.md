**Of Thingol and Melian** is the fourth chapter of the *[Quenta Silmarillion](/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion)* section within *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, and is the shortest of all the book.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Elena_Kukanova_-_Nan_Elmoth_-_Elwe_and_Melian.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/9/93/Elena_Kukanova_-_Nan_Elmoth_-_Elwe_and_Melian.jpg/300px-Elena_Kukanova_-_Nan_Elmoth_-_Elwe_and_Melian.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="440" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/9/93/Elena_Kukanova_-_Nan_Elmoth_-_Elwe_and_Melian.jpg/450px-Elena_Kukanova_-_Nan_Elmoth_-_Elwe_and_Melian.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/9/93/Elena_Kukanova_-_Nan_Elmoth_-_Elwe_and_Melian.jpg 2x" data-file-width="490" data-file-height="718"/></a><figcaption><i>Nan Elmoth - Elwe and Melian</i> by <a href="/wiki/Elena_Kukanova" title="Elena Kukanova">Elena Kukanova</a></figcaption></figure>

[Melian](/wiki/Melian), was a [Maia](/wiki/Maia) who dwelt in [Lórien](</wiki/L%C3%B3rien_(Valinor)> "Lórien (Valinor)"). She was the most skilfull singer of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), but she used to visit [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) to fill the silence and teach the birds.

During the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey), the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) lingered in [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand) while the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) were [further to the west](/wiki/Neldoreth "Neldoreth"). [Elwë](/wiki/Elw%C3%AB), the leader of the Teleri while visiting his friend [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB) of the Noldor, was lost in the forest of [Nan Elmoth](/wiki/Nan_Elmoth) and saw Melian dancing. When they saw each other, they remained immobile for the following ages in an ecstatic enchantment. His followers could not find him, so his leadership was taken by [Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB).

In the later years, Elwë was known in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) as *Elu Thingol*. He and Melian became the King and Queen of the Sindar, founding the kingdom of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), the only place in Middle-earth where there was glimpse of the [Light of Valinor](/wiki/Light_of_Valinor).
