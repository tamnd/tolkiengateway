<i><b>Of The Darkening Of Valinor And The Flight Of The Noldor</b></i> is a double sided LP record comprising readings of excerpts from *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, read by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md). Side 1 includes readings of Chapter Eight *[Of the Darkening of Valinor](Of_the_Darkening_of_Valinor.md)* and the start of Chapter Nine *[Of the Flight of the Noldor](Of_the_Flight_of_the_Noldor.md)*. Side 2 completes *Of the Flight of the Noldor*.

## Side One, Read By Christopher Tolkien (28:56) {#Side_One,_Read_By_Christopher_Tolkien_(28:56)}

- **[Of the Darkening of Valinor](Of_the_Darkening_of_Valinor.md)** (Chapter Eight)
- **[Of the Flight of the Noldor](Of_the_Flight_of_the_Noldor.md)** (Chapter Nine) (beginning)

## Side Two, Read By Christopher Tolkien (31:29) {#Side_Two,_Read_By_Christopher_Tolkien_(31:29)}

- **[Of the Flight of the Noldor](Of_the_Flight_of_the_Noldor.md)** (Chapter Nine) (conclusion)
