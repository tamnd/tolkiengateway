<i><b>Of The Darkening Of Valinor And The Flight Of The Noldor</b></i> is a double sided LP record comprising readings of excerpts from *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, read by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien). Side 1 includes readings of Chapter Eight *[Of the Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Of_the_Darkening_of_Valinor)* and the start of Chapter Nine *[Of the Flight of the Noldor](/wiki/Of_the_Flight_of_the_Noldor)*. Side 2 completes *Of the Flight of the Noldor*.

## Side One, Read By Christopher Tolkien (28:56) {#Side_One,_Read_By_Christopher_Tolkien_(28:56)}

- **[Of the Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Of_the_Darkening_of_Valinor)** (Chapter Eight)
- **[Of the Flight of the Noldor](/wiki/Of_the_Flight_of_the_Noldor)** (Chapter Nine) (beginning)

## Side Two, Read By Christopher Tolkien (31:29) {#Side_Two,_Read_By_Christopher_Tolkien_(31:29)}

- **[Of the Flight of the Noldor](/wiki/Of_the_Flight_of_the_Noldor)** (Chapter Nine) (conclusion)
