<i><b>Oliphaunt</b></i> is an illustrated children's book which reprints [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[Oliphaunt](</wiki/Oliphaunt_(poem)> "Oliphaunt (poem)")" from *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*. The illustrations were made by Hank Hinton.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In August [2011](/wiki/2011), a new edition illustrated by Dan McGeehan was published by The Child's World, Inc (<a class="internal mw-magiclink-isbn" href="/wiki/Special:BookSources/9781609731557">ISBN 978-1-60973-155-7</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
