## Quenya {#Quenya}

<i><b>orna</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "tall, high, lofty".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup> Another gloss given is "hasty".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

In "[Words, Phrases and Passages](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_17.md)", the word derives from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [OR, RŌ](OR~34968.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md), Quenya *orna* ("hasty") derives from root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=GOR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="GOR (page does not exist)">GOR</a> ("violence, impetus, haste").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Elvish {#Elvish}

In the *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)* is noted the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) "ancient adjectival form" <i><b>ornā</b></i> ("uprising, tall"), given no designated language.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
