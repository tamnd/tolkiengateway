**Orleg** was a [man](/wiki/Men "Men") of [Túrin's](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin") [outlaw band](/wiki/Gaurwaith "Gaurwaith").

## History {#History}

Orleg was a member of the [Gaurwaith](/wiki/Gaurwaith) or Wolf-men who hunted in the woods of [Taeglin](/wiki/Taeglin). When the group passed south under the leadership of Túrin they came upon a group of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) who were raiding southward. Túrin and Orleg were scouting the land when they were discovered by the Orcs and hunted westward toward the wilds of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond). There Orleg was slain by many arrows but Túrin escaped and it was a long road to return to the outlaw's camp.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
