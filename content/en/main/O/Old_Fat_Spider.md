**Old Fat Spider** is the third track on *[The Hobbit: The Complete Original Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Complete_Original_Soundtrack)*, and was adapted from the poem "[Old fat spider spinning in a tree!](/wiki/Old_fat_spider_spinning_in_a_tree!)" from *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. The song, sung by [Glenn Yarbrough](/wiki/Glenn_Yarbrough), is never actually featured anywhere in the [1977](/wiki/1977) [film adaptation](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)").
