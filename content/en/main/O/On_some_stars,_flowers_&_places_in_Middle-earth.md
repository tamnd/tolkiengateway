**On some stars, flowers &amp; places in Middle-earth** is a small collection of essays on Middle-earth, extracted from previous volumes published by Le Dragon de Brume and translated from French into English.

The volume is available in print, but a full PDF version is also provided on the editor's website.

## Contents {#Contents}

- On the lost tale of Mercury
- On the red star near the Hunter’s Moon
- On Elysian fields of mallos and alfirin
- On a dishevelled dryad
- On flying ships, myths and the Machine
- On the authors &amp; contributors
