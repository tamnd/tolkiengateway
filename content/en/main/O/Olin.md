**Olin** was a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) from [Thorin's Hall](../T/Thorin's_Halls.md) created for *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)*.

When the [Longbeards](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md) left the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md), Olin and a small guard force were left at Thorin's Gate to protect [Thorin's Hall](../T/Thorin's_Halls.md) and report to [Dáin](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> But [Gormr Doursmith](../G/Gormr_Doursmith.md), Steward of the Dourhands, poisoned Olin and killed his men and left him in the Silver Deep Guard House to die.<sup id="cite_ref-Deeds_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deeds-2">[2]</a></sup>

Finally, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3016](../T/Third_Age_3016.md), a company of Dwarves from [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) arrived at Thorin's Gate and the Dwarf player healed Olin with some mushrooms named *Nestadalas*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> When Dwalin, leader of the company, learnt of the [Dourhands](../D/Dourhands.md) treason they were finally driven out of Thorin's Hall.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
