**Olin** was a [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") from [Thorin's Hall](/wiki/Thorin%27s_Halls "Thorin's Halls") created for *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*.

When the [Longbeards](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk "Durin's Folk") left the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains), Olin and a small guard force were left at Thorin's Gate to protect [Thorin's Hall](/wiki/Thorin%27s_Halls "Thorin's Halls") and report to [Dáin](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> But [Gormr Doursmith](/wiki/Gormr_Doursmith), Steward of the Dourhands, poisoned Olin and killed his men and left him in the Silver Deep Guard House to die.<sup id="cite_ref-Deeds_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deeds-2">[2]</a></sup>

Finally, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3016](/wiki/Third_Age_3016 "Third Age 3016"), a company of Dwarves from [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") arrived at Thorin's Gate and the Dwarf player healed Olin with some mushrooms named *Nestadalas*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> When Dwalin, leader of the company, learnt of the [Dourhands](/wiki/Dourhands) treason they were finally driven out of Thorin's Hall.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
