The **Old South Road**, also referred to as just the **South Road**, was an ancient roadway from [Tol Sirion](/wiki/Tol_Sirion) heading south through the [Crossings of Taeglin](/wiki/Crossings_of_Taeglin) and passing along the western edge of the [Moors of the Nibin-noeg](/wiki/Moors_of_the_Nibin-noeg) before reaching [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
