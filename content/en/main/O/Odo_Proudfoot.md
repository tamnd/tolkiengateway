**Odo Proudfoot** ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1304](/wiki/Third_Age_2904 "Third Age 2904") - [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1405](/wiki/Third_Age_3005 "Third Age 3005")<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup>) was a Hobbit of the Shire.

## History {#History}

Odo was the son of [Bodo](/wiki/Bodo_Proudfoot "Bodo Proudfoot") and [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' aunt, [Linda](/wiki/Linda_Proudfoot "Linda Proudfoot")<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup> and gave rise to a line of [Proudfoots](/wiki/Proudfoot_Family "Proudfoot Family") (or, as Odo himself preferred, "Proudfeet").<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup>

Odo was among the guests at the famous [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party) during which he rested his prominently big feet on the table, until Bilbo Baggins suddenly and inexplicably disappeared, and in his surprise he took his feet of the table.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup> At that time, he was an elderly hobbit indeed, being ninety-seven years old. He only lived for a few more years after the Party,<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup> but from him had come at least two generations of descendants, including his mischievous grandson [Sancho](/wiki/Sancho_Proudfoot "Sancho Proudfoot"), who attempted to excavate for treasure in [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Odo* was meaningless in contemporary [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) [Westron](/wiki/Westron). It represented an original name *Oda*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
