**Odo Proudfoot** ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1304](../T/Third_Age_2904.md) - [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1405](../T/Third_Age_3005.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup>) was a Hobbit of the Shire.

## History {#History}

Odo was the son of [Bodo](../B/Bodo_Proudfoot.md) and [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)' aunt, [Linda](../L/Linda_Proudfoot.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup> and gave rise to a line of [Proudfoots](../P/Proudfoot_Family.md) (or, as Odo himself preferred, "Proudfeet").<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup>

Odo was among the guests at the famous [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md) during which he rested his prominently big feet on the table, until Bilbo Baggins suddenly and inexplicably disappeared, and in his surprise he took his feet of the table.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup> At that time, he was an elderly hobbit indeed, being ninety-seven years old. He only lived for a few more years after the Party,<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup> but from him had come at least two generations of descendants, including his mischievous grandson [Sancho](../S/Sancho_Proudfoot.md), who attempted to excavate for treasure in [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Odo* was meaningless in contemporary [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) [Westron](../W/Westron.md). It represented an original name *Oda*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
