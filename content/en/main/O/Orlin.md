**Orlin** was a [Man](/wiki/Man) of [Hisilómë](/wiki/Hithlum "Hithlum") according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in [*The Book of Lost Tales*](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales "The Book of Lost Tales").

He was akin to [Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin") himself through [Mavwin](/wiki/Morwen "Morwen"). When Turambar came to [Brodda's hall](/wiki/Brodda%27s_hall) and murdered [the lord](/wiki/Brodda "Brodda") in vengeance, Orlin was one of the few men present there who tried to seize Turambar, being the only one killed. When [Airin](/wiki/Airin) appeared and the people waited for her judgement, she explained Turambar had indeed commited a grave crime killing someone akin to his mother and in a hall of feast, but that, as it was in his defence, he would only be condemned to exile.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
