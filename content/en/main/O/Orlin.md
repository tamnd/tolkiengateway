**Orlin** was a [Man](../M/Man.md) of [Hisilómë](../H/Hithlum.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in [*The Book of Lost Tales*](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md).

He was akin to [Turambar](../T/Túrin.md) himself through [Mavwin](../M/Morwen.md). When Turambar came to [Brodda's hall](../B/Brodda's_hall.md) and murdered [the lord](../B/Brodda.md) in vengeance, Orlin was one of the few men present there who tried to seize Turambar, being the only one killed. When [Airin](../A/Airin.md) appeared and the people waited for her judgement, she explained Turambar had indeed commited a grave crime killing someone akin to his mother and in a hall of feast, but that, as it was in his defence, he would only be condemned to exile.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
