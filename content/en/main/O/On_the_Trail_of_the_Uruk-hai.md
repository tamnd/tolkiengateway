**On the Trail of the Uruk-hai** is the seventh scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and the ninth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) continue pursuing the fast-moving [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) across the plains of Rohan. Legolas comments on how fast the Uruks are moving, and Gimli struggles with the pursuit.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>They run as if the very whips of their masters were behind them.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Legolas</cite></p>
</blockquote>
