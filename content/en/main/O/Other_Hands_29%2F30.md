<b><i>Other Hands</i> 29/30</b> is a double-issue of *[Other Hands](/wiki/Other_Hands)*, a magazine devoted to *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*. The theme of the issue is "Southern Middle-earth".

The issue also has linguistic contributions by Christian Hass and [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo), and maps by [Bridget Buxton](/wiki/Bridget_Buxton), Eric Dubourg, Wesley J. Frank, [Thomas Morwinsky](/wiki/Thomas_Morwinsky), [Chris Seeman](/wiki/Chris_Seeman).

## Contents {#Contents}

*Article name - (Author's name) \[Publisher's notes\]*

- Editorial - ([Chris Seeman](/wiki/Chris_Seeman)) \[2 pgs\]
- Magic Feature: Words of Command - (Andy Mack) \[concrete game mechanics for WoCs; 3 pgs\]
- An Alternative Continental Map for MERP - ([Thomas Morwinsky](/wiki/Thomas_Morwinsky)) \[notes on the design of the new Endor continent map; 2 pgs + map insert\]
- The Mûmakanril - (Christian Haas) \[cultural background, gazetteer, and development stats; 9 pgs + map insert\]
- Mithril Feature: The Mirror of Fire - ([Chris Seeman](/wiki/Chris_Seeman)) \[2 pgs\]
- The Sisters of Ishtra - (Cory Rushton) \[background and stats for a southron religious order; 4 pgs\]
- Supplement: The Realm of [Bellakar](/wiki/Bellakar) - (Eric Dubourg) \[history for a completely new realm in southern Middle-earth; 56 pgs + map insert\]
