<b><i>Other Hands</i> 29/30</b> is a double-issue of *[Other Hands](Other_Hands.md)*, a magazine devoted to *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)*. The theme of the issue is "Southern Middle-earth".

The issue also has linguistic contributions by Christian Hass and [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md), and maps by [Bridget Buxton](../B/Bridget_Buxton.md), Eric Dubourg, Wesley J. Frank, [Thomas Morwinsky](../T/Thomas_Morwinsky.md), [Chris Seeman](../C/Chris_Seeman.md).
