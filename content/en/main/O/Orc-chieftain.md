A huge **[orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs")-chieftain** attacked the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) and struck [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) with his [spear](/wiki/Spears "Spears") during the [Battle of the Chamber of Mazarbul](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Chamber_of_Mazarbul).

## History {#History}

While travelling through [the Kingdom of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"), the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) were assailed by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) in the [Chamber of Mazarbul](/wiki/Chamber_of_Mazarbul). After the walkers had slain thirteen of the Orcs, the rest of the troop fled, shrieking. Soon after, a fearsome Orc-chieftain, brandishing a spear and shield leaped into the Chamber with his followers. [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) tried to strike him but the Orc turned the his sword with his shield, knocking him to the ground. [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) tried to strike him with [Andúril](/wiki/And%C3%BAril) but the Orc dived under the sword and swiftly charged into the company. He thrusted his spear at [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), which caught the [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") on his right side and hurled him against the wall, pinning him. [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") cried out and hacked the spear's shaft with his [Barrow-blade](/wiki/Barrow-blades "Barrow-blades"), breaking it. The Orc threw down a broken spear-shaft and swept out his [scimitar](/wiki/Scimitar "Scimitar"). Aragorn swung Andúril and struck the chieftain's helm, breaking it and cleaving his head. His followers fled, howling at the sight of their dead chieftain.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

The Chieftain was almost the size of a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men").<sup id="cite_ref-HeightClothing_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HeightClothing-3">[3]</a></sup> He had a broad, swarthy, flat face, eyes like coals and a red tongue.<sup id="cite_ref-Description_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Description-2">[2]</a></sup>

He was well equipped: He wore black mail<sup id="cite_ref-HeightClothing_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HeightClothing-3">[3]</a></sup> and a helm.<sup id="cite_ref-Death_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Death-4">[4]</a></sup> The chieftain was armed with a huge hide shield,<sup id="cite_ref-Shield_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shield-5">[5]</a></sup> a spear<sup id="cite_ref-Description_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Description-2">[2]</a></sup> and a scimitar.<sup id="cite_ref-Death_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Death-4">[4]</a></sup>

<b><a href="/wiki/1978" title="1978">1978</a>: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>After <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> tells <a href="/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="Fellowship of the Ring">the Fellowship</a> to &#34;run for it&#34;, the orc-chieftain gives a war cry and charges. He is alone, without any followers. <a href="/wiki/Boromir" title="Boromir">Boromir</a> tries to stop him but the chieftain blocks him with his shield, which falls to the ground with Boromir. <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> swings at the chieftain with <a href="/wiki/And%C3%BAril" title="Andúril">his sword</a> but the <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orc</a> ducks under it and pushes Aragorn to the ground, before letting out another war cry and throwing his spear at <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a>. The spear strikes Frodo in the centre of his chest, rather than to his right. Aragorn gets up and runs at the Orc, but he is punched in the stomach and on the back of his neck. Aragorn turns and strikes the Orc with his sword on the left of his torso. The chieftain tumbles to the ground and Aragorn slashes him again on his left leg. The orc lies still and Aragorn stabs him once more on the back.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/1989" title="1989">1989</a>: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(comic_book)" title="The Lord of the Rings (comic book)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (comic book)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The orc-chieftain appears in the Chamber, wearing plate armour rather than mail, armed with a spear and shield. <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> warns <a href="/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="Fellowship of the Ring">the Fellowship</a> to be careful, as the chieftain is more dangerous than twenty of his men. He knocks Aragorn over, causing <a href="/wiki/And%C3%BAril" title="Andúril">Andúril</a> to fly out of his hand. Aragorn yells to <a href="/wiki/Boromir" title="Boromir">Boromir</a> that the orc is going after <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a> and, as the comic is loosely based on <a href="/wiki/Ralph_Bakshi" title="Ralph Bakshi">Ralph Bakshi</a>&#39;s <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)">1978 film adaptation</a>, he throws the spear at Frodo rather than stabbing him, as he does in the film. Unlike the film and <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">book</a>, however, Boromir is the one to kill him, breaking his helm and cleaving his head as Aragorn does in the book.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/1993" title="1993">1993</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/Hobitit" title="Hobitit">Hobitit</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The <a href="/wiki/Moria" title="Moria">Moria</a> sequence takes place in episode 6, <i>Lórien</i>. The orc-chieftain attacks in the middle of the battle, not after the Orcs have been driven off, and has no followers. He is not met with any resistance when walking towards <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a> beside other members of <a href="/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="Fellowship of the Ring">the Fellowship</a> yelling Frodo&#39;s name in warning. The chieftain stabs Frodo, but he is not pinned against the wall. <a href="/wiki/Boromir" title="Boromir">Boromir</a> arrives and slashes the chieftain&#39;s face, who moans in agony and crumbles before <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> delivers the finishing blow.</dd></dl>
