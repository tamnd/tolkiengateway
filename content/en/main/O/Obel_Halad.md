**Obel Halad**, also called the **Obel**, **Ephel Brandir**, and the **Ephel**, was the "capital" settlement of the [woodmen](../M/Men_of_Brethil.md) in the centre of the [Forest of Brethil](../F/Forest_of_Brethil.md), the homeland of the [People](../H/House_of_Haleth.md) of [Haleth](../H/Haleth.md). Within this settlement was the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hall_of_the_Chieftains&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hall of the Chieftains (page does not exist)">Hall of the Chieftains</a> or [Warden of Brethil](../H/Halad.md), who was the leader of the land.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Obel Halad lay upon the hill of [Amon Obel](../A/Amon_Obel.md), and is where [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) was taken when he fell ill. It was ruled by [Brandir](../B/Brandir.md) son of [Handir](../H/Handir.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Obel Halad* meant the Fortified Settlement of the Halad.

*Ephel Brandir* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "the encircling fence of Brandir".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier versions, transient names for Ephel Brandir were **[Tavrobel](../T/Tavrobel.md#Tavrobel_in_Brethil)** and **Bar Haleth**, and these can be seen on the [The Second 'Silmarillion' Map](../T/The_Second_'Silmarillion'_Map.md). The location for Tavrobel and Bar Haleth were considerably eastward of where Ephel Brandir would be located.<sup id="cite_ref-woj_c_324_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-woj_c_324-3">[3]</a></sup>

As noted by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md): "Beyond the fact that ‘Tavrobel’ was in the extreme east of Brethil it is not possible to be sure of its site. Bar Haleth was in turn crossed out. It seems certain therefore that this was a transient name for Ephel Brandir, which was marked in subsequently in the centre of Brethil"<sup id="cite_ref-woj_c_324_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-woj_c_324-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the development of the story, "[The Wanderings of Húrin](../T/The_Wanderings_of_Húrin.md)" (a tale that [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) regretfully excluded from *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*), Obel Halad replaced [Ephel Brandir](../E/Ephel_Brandir.md) as the chief settlement of the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Since Ephel Brandir was built upon [Amon Obel](../A/Amon_Obel.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> it is likely that its replacement was also intended to be in the same location.
