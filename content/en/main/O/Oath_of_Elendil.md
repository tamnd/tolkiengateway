After surviving the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor), [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) landed in Middle-earth, proclaiming some words in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). His heir and great-grandson by 39 generations [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") spoke these words again when he took up the [Crown of Gondor](/wiki/Crown_of_Gondor) as [King Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") at the beginning of the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age):

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><b>Et Eärello Endorenna utúlien. Sinomë maruvan ar Hildinyar tenn&#39; Ambar-metta</b>. [...] Out of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Great_Sea" title="Great Sea">Great Sea</a> to <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a> I am come. In this place will I abide, and my heirs, unto the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_End" title="The End">ending of the world</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>

## Portrayals {#Portrayals}

[Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore) used the oath as part of the "[The Great River](</wiki/The_Great_River_(soundtrack)> "The Great River (soundtrack)")" track for *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring")* film. [Viggo Mortensen](/wiki/Viggo_Mortensen) provided the melody for the words in the [track](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(soundtrack)> "The Return of the King (soundtrack)") for the [coronation scene](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(scene)> "The Return of the King (scene)") in *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King")*.
