**Orodlin** was, in the earlier stages of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), the younger son of [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Orodlin* is in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), possibly being a combination of *[orod](/wiki/Orod "Orod")* ("mountain") and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lh%C3%AEn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lhîn (page does not exist)">lhîn</a></i> ("pool").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
