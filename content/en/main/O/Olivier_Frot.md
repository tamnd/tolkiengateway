**Olivier Frot** (born [27 April](/wiki/27_April) [1966](/wiki/1966)) is a French fantasy artist who was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.

While he was originally intended to illustrate more cards, ultimately only his test art for [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) ended up being used.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He also illustrated the cover art for Hexagonal's French edition of the [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [1995](/wiki/1995), he drew a map of the [Fifth Age](/wiki/Fifth_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) to illustrate an article in the French version of [Dragon Magazine](/wiki/Dragon_Magazine).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>
