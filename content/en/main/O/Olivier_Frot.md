**Olivier Frot** (born [27 April](../_/27_April.md) [1966](../_/1966.md)) is a French fantasy artist who was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.

While he was originally intended to illustrate more cards, ultimately only his test art for [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) ended up being used.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He also illustrated the cover art for Hexagonal's French edition of the [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [1995](../_/1995.md), he drew a map of the [Fifth Age](../F/Fifth_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) to illustrate an article in the French version of [Dragon Magazine](../D/Dragon_Magazine.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>
