<i><b>omentie</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "meeting (of pathways)".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- *[o-](../_/Ó-.md)* ("together") + *[mentië](../M/Mentië.md)* ("passage, journey")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Forms {#Forms}

- <i><b>omentielvo</b></i> ("of our meeting")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="../E/Elen_síla_lúmenn&#39;_omentielvo.md" title="Elen síla lúmenn&#39; omentielvo">Elen síla lúmenn&#39; <b>omentie</b>lvo</a></i> = "A star shines on the hour of our meeting"
