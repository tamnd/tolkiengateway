English actor **Orlando Jonathan Blanchard Bloom** (b. [January 13](/wiki/13_January "13 January"), [1977](/wiki/1977)) portrayed [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) in [*The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and [*The Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").

## Awards {#Awards}

- [2002](/wiki/2002) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.cinemaclips.com/PFCS.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">PFCS Award</a></b>, Best Acting Ensemble, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*
- 2002 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.mtv.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">MTV Movie Award</a></b>, Breakthrough Male Performance, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2002 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.empireonline.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Empire Award</a></b>, Best Debut, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- [2003](/wiki/2003) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://ofcs.rottentomatoes.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">OFCS Award</a></b>, Best Ensemble, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*
- 2003 - **PFCS Award**, Best Acting Ensemble, *Peter Jackson's The Two Towers*
- [2004](/wiki/2004) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.bfca.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Critics&#39; Choice Award</a></b>, Best Acting Ensemble, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*
- 2004 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.nbrmp.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">NBR Award</a></b>, Best Acting by an Ensemble, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
- 2004 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.sagawards.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">SAG Award</a></b>, Outstanding Performance by a Cast in a Motion Picture, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
