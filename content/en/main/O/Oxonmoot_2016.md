**Oxonmoot 2016**, the 43rd annual [Oxonmoot](Oxonmoot.md) hosted by [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md), took place [8](../_/8_September.md)-[11 September](../_/11_September.md) at St Antony's College, [Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md). As with [Oxonmoot 2015](Oxonmoot_2015.md), Oxonmoot 2016 was an extra day long.
