**ONO** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md).

**ON/NO** (or **ONO/NŌ**) signifies "beget/be born". **NŌ** (or **NO**) signifies "generation, people, folk, large group regarded as of common ancestry".<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>

While **ONO** shortly is explained as signifying "beget, give birth to", a longer signification is given as "family, tribe or group having a common ancestry, the land or region in which they dwelt".<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup> The latter signification shows the blending with <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=NDOR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NDOR (page does not exist)">NDOR/DORO</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- Quenya: *onta* ("beget"); *nosta* ("be begotten"); *onwe*, *onna* ("child")<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>

- blended with <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=NDOR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NDOR (page does not exist)">NDOR/DORO</a>
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[nóre, nōre](../N/Nóre.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

### NŌ-, ONO- {#NŌ-,_ONO-}

In the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md) appear the related roots NŌ- and ONO-, both signifying "beget". From these roots derive:<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

- NŌ-
    - Quenya: *[nóre](../N/Nóre.md)*; *[nosse](../N/Nossë.md)*
    - [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *nûr* ("race"); *noss* ("clan, family, house")

- NŌ-
- Quenya: *onóro* ("brother"), *onóne* ("sister")
    - [Old Noldorin](../K/Kornoldorin.md): *wanūro*<sup id="cite_ref-VT46_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT46-4">[4]</a></sup>
    - Noldorin: *[gwanur](../G/Gwanur.md)*

- ONO-
    - Quenya: <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Onta-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Onta- (page does not exist)">onta-</a></i>; *onna* ("creature"); *ontaro* ("begetter, parent"); *ontaru*<sup id="cite_ref-VT46_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT46-4">[4]</a></sup> (parents)
    - Noldorin: *odhron* ("parent"); *ed-onna* ("beget"); *ûn* ("creature")

### NŌ- {#NŌ-}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root NŌ-, signifying "become, be born".<sup id="cite_ref-PE12_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE12-5">[5]</a></sup>

### NŌ-, NOWO? (NONO?) {#NŌ-,_NOWO?_(NONO?)}

Also in the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root NŌ-, NOWO? (NONO?), signifying "ahead, in front; after, of time; tomorrow" (one of the derivatives is [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) *nuo* "tomorrow").<sup id="cite_ref-PE12_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE12-5">[5]</a></sup> [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md) therefore connects this early root to the late Quenya word *[noa](../N/Noa.md)* ("tomorrow" or "yesterday").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
