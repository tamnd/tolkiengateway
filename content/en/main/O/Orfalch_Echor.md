The **Orfalch Echor** was the great ravine through the [Dry River](/wiki/Dry_River) of the [Encircling Mountains](/wiki/Encircling_Mountains) by which [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) was approached.

## History {#History}

The Orfalch Echor was the route that [Elemmakil](/wiki/Elemmakil) led [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) through to meet [Ecthelion of the Fountain](/wiki/Ecthelion "Ecthelion"). At its lower end was the First Gate, the [Gate of Wood](/wiki/Gate_of_Wood). The Orfalch continued through the remainder of the [Seven Gates of Gondolin](/wiki/Seven_Gates_of_Gondolin), reaching the Seventh Gate, the [Gate of Steel](/wiki/Gate_of_Steel). At the upper end was a high sward that offered a view of [Tumladen](/wiki/Tumladen) and the city in its center.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During [Húrin's](/wiki/H%C3%BArin "Húrin") [wanderings](/wiki/The_Wanderings_of_H%C3%BArin "The Wanderings of Húrin"), he came upon the Orfalch Echor, only to find the old Way of Escape buried underneath *"a great fall of stones beneath a sheer rock-wall"*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Orfalch Echor* is suggested by Paul Strack to be a combination of [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *or* ("above"), *falch* ("cleft"), and *echor* ("outer circle"), perhaps meaning "High Cleft of the Outer Circle".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")*, the **Way of Escape** was the main entrance to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), and it was not a natural ravine, but was instead built by the [Gondothlim](/wiki/Gondothlim) to be used by those [Noldoli](/wiki/Noldoli) who escaped from [Melko](/wiki/Melko)<sup id="cite_ref-LT_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:163</sup> and as a way of escape in case the city was attacked, as it eventually was. Because of [Meglin's](/wiki/Maeglin "Maeglin") treason, Melko was aware of this pass, so many survivors were killed there by a monster while trying to escape quickly in spite of [Idril's](/wiki/Idril "Idril") warnings.<sup id="cite_ref-LT_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:189</sup>

This way is also known as *Bad Uthwen* in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) and *Uswevandë* in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya).<sup id="cite_ref-LT_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:336</sup>
