**Over-heaven** is a name used by [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It means the same as [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) [Menel](/wiki/Menel) 'Heaven' or [Tarmenel](/wiki/Tarmenel) 'High Heaven'. Over-heaven is a word translated from [Westron](/wiki/Westron) to English, based on [Old Norse](/wiki/Old_Norse) *uphiminn*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
