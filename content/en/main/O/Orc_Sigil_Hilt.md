The **Orc Sigil Hilt**,<sup id="cite_ref-name_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-1">[1]</a></sup> also called the **Hilt**,<sup id="cite_ref-name_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-1">[1]</a></sup> was a sword hilt forged by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) and later rediscovered by the [Men](../M/Men.md) of the [Southlands](../M/Mordor.md) in the late [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md). It was invented by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* television series. It is featured in the [first season](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power_-_Season_1.md) of the series.

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Rings_of_Power_(TV_series)_-_Theo.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/de/The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Theo.jpg/349px-The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Theo.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="349" height="233" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/de/The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Theo.jpg/524px-The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Theo.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/de/The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Theo.jpg/698px-The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Theo.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="683"/></a><figcaption>Theo holding the Oric Sigil Hilt.</figcaption></figure>

The Orc Sigil Hilt was said to be forged by Morgoth in the time when the ancestors of the [Southlanders](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md) swore fealty to him. It was lost for many years until it came into the hands of the Southlander [Waldreg](../W/Waldreg.md), who kept it under a floorboard in his barn. It was eventually found by a young villager named [Theo](../T/Theo.md).

Theo found that the Hilt drank the blood of its wielder. Upon being exposed to this blood, the blade shard magically extended to its full length. While searching for supplies in an empty [Tirharad](../T/Tirharad.md), Theo wielded the blade against the [Orc](Orcs.md) [Vrath](../V/Vrath.md), who recognized the blade and feared it.

Upon inspection by the [Elf](../E/Elves.md) [Arondir](../A/Arondir.md), the Hilt was revealed to be some sort of key, though the elf was not certain of its significance. Arondir then confided in Theo that the [Watchtower of Ostirith](../W/Watchtower_of_Ostirith.md) was of sinister origins, and its walls displayed an engraving depicting the blade.

Tirharad later fell to an Orc army led by [Adar](<../A/Adar_(The_Rings_of_Power).md>). Theo hands the Hilt over to the Orc leader after Adar threatened the life of Theo's mother, [Bronwyn](../B/Bronwyn.md). While a [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) expeditionary force arrived to aid Tirharad, Adar gave the Hilt to Waldreg, who had sworn fealty to him while Adar created and held onto a decoy.

[Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and [Halbrand](../H/Halbrand.md) believed Adar was in possession of the Hilt and pursued him while Waldreg returned the real Hilt to [Ostirith](../W/Watchtower_of_Ostirith.md). Extending the blade to its full length with his blood, Waldreg inserted the Hilt into a keyhole-like opening. After the blade sank to the hilt, he turned it, activating a mechanism that opened the dam by the watchtower. This caused lake waters to flow through the tunnels dug by Adar's captives. The waters reached a cavern inside of [Orodruin](../M/Mount_Doom.md) and caused it to erupt, leading to the ruin of Tirharad.

After serving its purpose, the fate of the Hilt is unknown.
