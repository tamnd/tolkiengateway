**Oiomúrë** was a shoreland region of "great mists" in northern [Aman](../A/Aman.md), near the [Helcaraxë](../H/Helcaraxë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-S9_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S9-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the [Darkening of Valinor](../D/Darkening_of_Valinor.md), [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) and [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md) fled the pursuing [Valar](../V/Valar.md) northwards through the barren and cold land of [Araman](../A/Araman.md), traversing Oiomúrë and the Helcaraxë in order to reach [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-S9_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S9-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) led by [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) during their [Flight](../F/Flight_of_the_Noldor.md) possibly as well passed through Oiomúrë on their march to [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) from [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) through the Helcaraxë.<sup id="cite_ref-S9_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S9-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Arda_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arda-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Origin {#Origin}

[Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md) has suggested that the mists of Oiomúrë may have been caused by "*contact between the [Grinding Ice](../H/Helcaraxë.md) and warmer sea water.*".<sup id="cite_ref-Foster_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foster-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Oiomúrë* is unknown. [Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) has suggested that it is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "Ever-?mist",<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> perhaps containing the element *[oio](Oio.md)* ("ever").<sup id="cite_ref-Arda_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arda-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Foster_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foster-4">[4]</a></sup>
