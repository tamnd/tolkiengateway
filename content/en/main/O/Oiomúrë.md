**Oiomúrë** was a shoreland region of "great mists" in northern [Aman](/wiki/Aman), near the [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-S9_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S9-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the [Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor), [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) and [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) fled the pursuing [Valar](/wiki/Valar) northwards through the barren and cold land of [Araman](/wiki/Araman), traversing Oiomúrë and the Helcaraxë in order to reach [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-S9_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S9-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) led by [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) during their [Flight](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Noldor "Flight of the Noldor") possibly as well passed through Oiomúrë on their march to [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) from [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) through the Helcaraxë.<sup id="cite_ref-S9_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S9-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Arda_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arda-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Origin {#Origin}

[Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) has suggested that the mists of Oiomúrë may have been caused by "*contact between the [Grinding Ice](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB "Helcaraxë") and warmer sea water.*".<sup id="cite_ref-Foster_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foster-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Oiomúrë* is unknown. [Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested that it is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Ever-?mist",<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> perhaps containing the element *[oio](/wiki/Oio "Oio")* ("ever").<sup id="cite_ref-Arda_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arda-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Foster_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foster-4">[4]</a></sup>
