**Old Sindarin** is the conventional name for the hypothetical earliest form of the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) language, gradually mutated from [Common Telerin](../C/Common_Telerin.md).

## History {#History}

The stage of "Old Sindarin" refers to a period where the Sindarin words had not undergone [lenitions](../L/Lenition.md) of their consonants, nor affections of vowels by *[i](../I/I-affection.md)* or *[a](../A/A-affection.md)*, nor <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Apocope&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Apocope (page does not exist)">apocope</a> of final vowels. The first changes that already occurred in Old Sindarin, which made it differentiate from [Common Telerin](../C/Common_Telerin.md), was the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiceless_glottal_fricative" title="wikipedia:Voiceless glottal fricative">aspiration</a> in some sounds; this can be seen in [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Alpa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alpa (page does not exist)">alpa</a></i> but OS <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Alpha&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alpha (page does not exist)">alpha</a></i> &gt; S. *[alph](../A/Alph.md)*. Further changes followed in the process of time.

The OS long vowels of *é*, *á*, *ó* had been changed to other sounds by the time of Sindarin.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

This language was spoken in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) during or after the [Great March](../G/Great_March.md), perhaps by the [Eglath](../E/Eglath.md). However when the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) returned to [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) at the rising of the [Moon](../M/Moon.md), the language had already fully evolved into Sindarin.

It is possible that Old Sindarin was the common ancestor of [Mithrimin](../M/Mithrimin.md), [Falathrin](../F/Falathrin.md) and [Doriathrin](../D/Doriathrin.md).

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In published writing referring to the early legendarium, only the term **[Old Noldorin](Old_Noldorin.md)** was used. In that early conception, it was the language spoken by the [Gnomes](../G/Gnomes.md) in [Kôr](../K/Kôr.md), (in contrast to the later legendarium of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, where the Noldor spoke [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) both in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) and later in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md)). Old Noldorin does show some similarities to Quenya and [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md).

Most of the corpus of ON is given in *[The Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* and their purpose is to illustrate the etymology of some [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) words. Even after [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) renamed "Noldorin" into "Sindarin", almost all rules of etymology and derivation have been retained the same (see "**[Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md)**" for the discussion of some minor differences).

More recent works like *[Quendi and Eldar](../Q/Quendi_and_Eldar.md)* show ancient forms of Sindarin that seem to possess the same structure and to belong to the same evolution as the Old Noldorin of the *Etymologies*. It is therefore possible that an ancient form of Sindarin similar to what Tolkien imagined as "Old Noldorin" did exist in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of his later concepts; but Tolkien did not refer to that stage as a stable, identifiable language and did not go to the process to name it; making "Old Sindarin" to seem a non-canon, but plausible name for it.

Note that in these recent sources, Tolkien provides these forms with an [asterisk](../A/Asterisk.md) as "unattested", suggesting that they belong to an unwritten stage of the language, before the invention of the [Cirth](../C/Cirth.md). This is not the case in the "Old Noldorin" forms of the *Etymologies* where they are unasterisked, supposedly written with [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) in Kôr.
