**Orophin** was a [Silvan Elf](/wiki/Silvan_Elves "Silvan Elves") of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).

## History {#History}

Orophin was the brother of [Haldir](/wiki/Haldir) and [Rúmil](</wiki/R%C3%BAmil_(elf_of_L%C3%B3rien)> "Rúmil (elf of Lórien)"). He was one of the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) who seldom left Lothlórien and spoke but a little of [Westron](/wiki/Westron).

He was one of three wardens of the northern border of Lothlórien met by the [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") as they entered the [Golden Wood](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien")<sup id="cite_ref-ii6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ii6-1">[1]</a></sup> on [15 January](/wiki/15_January) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Some days later, when a band of a hundred [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) were seen passing the [Nimrodel](</wiki/Nimrodel_(river)> "Nimrodel (river)"), the three brothers went ahead and spoke with feigned voices, leading them on into the wood. Orophin ran back to warn the Galadhrim and prepare an ambush and fortify the wood.<sup id="cite_ref-ii6_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ii6-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) identified the name as either [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) or [Silvan](/wiki/Silvan).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> At another point it is described to be "*suitable to [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) sounds and patterns*" although it is "*not clearly etymologizable as Sindarin*".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

One possibility is that *Orophin* means "Mountain Peak", from *[orod](/wiki/Orod "Orod")* and *[pind](/wiki/Pind "Pind")*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
