<i><b>orn</b></i> (plural <i><b>yrn</b></i>) is a noun meaning "[tree](/wiki/Trees "Trees")" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin),<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup> especially a large tree.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
