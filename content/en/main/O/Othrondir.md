**Othrondir** was a [man](/wiki/Men "Men") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) who lived possibly at or near the [Emyn Arnen](/wiki/Emyn_Arnen) during the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).

## History {#History}

Othrondir was an acquaintace of [Borlas](/wiki/Borlas) of [Pen-arduin](/wiki/Pen-arduin), to whom he had told about some rumors concerning a certain restlessness in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). He was also the one who first used the word "canker", evidently referring to secret followers of the "[Dark Tree](/wiki/Dark_Tree)" that was rising during the year [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [220](/wiki/Fourth_Age_220 "Fourth Age 220").<sup id="cite_ref-Canker_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canker-1">[1]</a></sup> under the leadership of someone by the name of [Herumor](</wiki/Herumor_(The_New_Shadow)> "Herumor (The New Shadow)").<sup id="cite_ref-Canker_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canker-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The Sindarin name *Othrondir* means "man of an underground stronghold" (*othrond* 'underground stronghold'<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> + *dîr*<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> 'man').

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name was **Othrondor** in the first manuscript (ms. A) of the story, which was then changed to *Othrondir* in the following manuscripts (mss. B and C).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
