**Oghor-hai** was the name used by the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) to describe that race of [Men](/wiki/Men) otherwise known as the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain) or [Woses](/wiki/Woses). They were believed by the Orcs to possess [magical](/wiki/Magic "Magic") powers, and they therefore feared the Oghor-hai greatly.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}
