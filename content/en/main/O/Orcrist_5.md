<b><i>Orcrist</i> 5</b> <b>/ <i>Tolkien Journal</i> Vol. 4 no. 4</b> is an issue of the journal *[Orcrist](</wiki/Orcrist_(journal)> "Orcrist (journal)")* and the *[Tolkien Journal](/wiki/Tolkien_Journal)* (*Tolkien Journal* whole number 14).

The front cover features the illustration "Mirkwood" by George D. Asdell, and the back cover has the illustration "At the Sign of the Prancing Pony" by Deborah Webster, Sr.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
