The **Old Grange** was a granary located in [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) just down the hill from [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) north of [the Water](/wiki/The_Water "The Water"), west of the [Hobbiton Road](/wiki/Hobbiton_Road).

The Grange was torn down during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) and replaced with rows of tarred sheds.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
