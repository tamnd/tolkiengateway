**Other Hands** was the official magazine devoted to *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*. The magazine was edited by [Chris Seeman](/wiki/Chris_Seeman) and continued to be published until 2001, two years after [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) lost the license to create gaming products related to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s legendarium.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> During its last time of production, *Other Hands* was only published as an internet resource on its web site.
