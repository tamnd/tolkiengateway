<i><b>Om Beowulfsagan</b></i> ("On the Tale of Beowulf") is a [1975](/wiki/1975) Swedish-language collection of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s "[Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics](/wiki/Beowulf:_The_Monsters_and_the_Critics)", "Prefatory Remarks on Prose Translation of 'Beowulf'", and "[English and Welsh](/wiki/English_and_Welsh)". All three pieces were already in print separately, but had not been published together in a collection. [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) was later to include them in [1983](/wiki/1983) in *[The Monsters and the Critics](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics)*.

The book also includes a Foreword by translator [Åke Ohlmarks](/wiki/%C3%85ke_Ohlmarks).

## From the back {#From_the_back}

In his famous essays on the Old English heroic epic *Beowulf*, Tolkien presents a defense of both the hero and the monsters, of both the story and the poetry of the work.

J.R.R. Tolkien, who was Professor of Anglo-Saxon at Oxford, here also shows how much his scholarly achievement inspired the tales of *The Lord of the Rings*.

This is a book for all those who wish to penetrate more deeply into Tolkien's imagination and his interest in language and stories.

<br> <small>[Original text:] I sina berömda essäer om det fornengelska hjälteeposet Beowulf går Tolkien till försvar för både hjälten och odjuren, både sagan och poesin i diktverket. </small>

<small>J.R.R. Tolkien, som var professor i anglosaxiska vid Oxford, visar här också hur mycket hans vetenskapliga gärning inspirerat sagorna om Härskarringen. </small>

<small>En bok för alla dem som vill tränga djupare in i Tolkiens föreställningsvärld och hans intresse för språk och sagor.</small>
