**Oxonmoot 2022**, the 49th annual [Oxonmoot](/wiki/Oxonmoot) hosted by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society), took place [1](/wiki/1_September "1 September")-[4 September](/wiki/4_September) [2022](/wiki/2022). As in the previous year, it was a hybrid event bringing together online delegates with those attending in person at St Ann's College, [Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford").

During the event, [first](/wiki/A_Shadow_of_the_Past "A Shadow of the Past") [two](/wiki/Adrift "Adrift") episodes of [The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power) series were released on 1 September, which influenced the programme, with a watch party of the episodes and several panel discussions about the series.

Speakers included:<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Marcel Aubron-Bülles](/wiki/Marcel_Aubron-B%C3%BClles)
- [John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth)
- [Kristine Larsen](/wiki/Kristine_Larsen)
- [Brian Sibley](/wiki/Brian_Sibley)

Exhibiting artists included:<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Soni Alcorn-Hender](/wiki/Soni_Alcorn-Hender)
- [Anke Eißmann](/wiki/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann)
- [Lída 'Aegeri' Holubová](/wiki/L%C3%ADda_Holubov%C3%A1 "Lída Holubová")
- [Jay Johnstone](/wiki/Jay_Johnstone)
- [Ruth Lacon](/wiki/Ruth_Lacon)
- [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith)
