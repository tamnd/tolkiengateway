**Oxonmoot 2022**, the 49th annual [Oxonmoot](Oxonmoot.md) hosted by [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md), took place [1](../_/1_September.md)-[4 September](../_/4_September.md) [2022](../_/2022.md). As in the previous year, it was a hybrid event bringing together online delegates with those attending in person at St Ann's College, [Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md).

During the event, [first](../A/A_Shadow_of_the_Past.md) [two](../A/Adrift.md) episodes of [The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md) series were released on 1 September, which influenced the programme, with a watch party of the episodes and several panel discussions about the series.

Speakers included:<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Marcel Aubron-Bülles](../M/Marcel_Aubron-Bülles.md)
- [John Garth](../J/John_Garth.md)
- [Kristine Larsen](../K/Kristine_Larsen.md)
- [Brian Sibley](../B/Brian_Sibley.md)

Exhibiting artists included:<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Soni Alcorn-Hender](../S/Soni_Alcorn-Hender.md)
- [Anke Eißmann](../A/Anke_Eißmann.md)
- [Lída 'Aegeri' Holubová](../L/Lída_Holubová.md)
- [Jay Johnstone](../J/Jay_Johnstone.md)
- [Ruth Lacon](../R/Ruth_Lacon.md)
- [Ted Nasmith](../T/Ted_Nasmith.md)
