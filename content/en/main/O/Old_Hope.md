The **Old Hope** was a belief held by a group of [Edain](/wiki/Edain) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). They believed [Eru](/wiki/Eru) will enter within the confines of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) to [heal](/wiki/Arda_Healed "Arda Healed") the [Marring](/wiki/Arda_Marred "Arda Marred") caused by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) sometimes in the [future](/wiki/Later_Ages "Later Ages").

## History {#History}

The group were few, but their number had increased after the coming of the Edain into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), as in the [war of the Eldar](/wiki/Battles_of_Beleriand "Battles of Beleriand") they saw that Morgoth could indeed be defied, as they thought. However, their hope was not grounded on any of the peoples of Arda, but on the One who created [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4) and was beyond it:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>They say that <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Eru" title="Eru">the One</a> will himself come into Arda, and heal <a href="/wiki/Men" title="Men">Men</a> and all the Marring from the beginning to the end. This they say also, or they feign, is a rumour that has come down through years uncounted, even from the days of our undoing.
</p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

During a debate between the [Wise-woman](/wiki/Wise-woman) [Andreth](/wiki/Andreth) and King [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod"), she mentioned those of the "Old Hope" after dealing with *[Estel](/wiki/Estel)* ("<a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Hope" title="Hope">hope</a>"). She denied to be one of them, declaring her incredulity against this belief, but Finrod showed himself more open to it: "If Eru wished to do this, I do not doubt that He would find a way".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the previous draft of the *Athrabeth*, the concept is called "the **Great Hope**", and Andreth declares herself one of the group, while it is Finrod who answers with incredulity.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
