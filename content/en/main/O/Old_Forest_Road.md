The **Old Forest Road**, also called the **Men-i-Naugrim**, or just the **Forest Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-UT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-1">[1]</a></sup> was the main route through the great forest originally known as [Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Mirkwood "Mirkwood") and later as [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood).

## Route {#Route}

The road ran from the [east gate](/wiki/Great_Gates "Great Gates") of the underground dwarven city originally known as [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") and later as Moria north along the eastern side of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) over the upper course of the river <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Gladden" title="Gladden">Gladden</a> to the lowest point where a bridge over the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) could be built and then straight east across the vale of the Anduin and through Mirkwood forest to a bridge across the [River Running](/wiki/River_Running) and then north-east over open land to the iron mines<sup id="cite_ref-NM_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-2">[2]</a></sup> in the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills).<sup id="cite_ref-note_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-note-3">[3]</a></sup> This part of the road eventually fell into disrepair and the [Dwarves of the Iron Hills](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills) lost contact with [Moria](/wiki/Moria). A road that descended from the [Pass of Imladris](/wiki/Pass_of_Imladris) was also part of the Dwarf Road.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The latitude of the eastern part of the road was halfway between the ancient Dwarven meeting-place at [Mount Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad "Gundabad") to the north, and Moria to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The stone-bridge over the Anduin and the first miles of the road through Greenwood were built by the [Longbeard](/wiki/Longbeards "Longbeards") [dwarves of Moria](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Moria "Dwarves of Moria") and the bridge over the River Running was built by [their kin](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills "Dwarves of the Iron Hills") from the Iron Hills in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). The road was completed during the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) when [many dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains "Dwarves of the Blue Mountains") emigrated from the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) to Moria and to the Iron Hills.<sup id="cite_ref-NM_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-2">[2]</a></sup>

The road enjoyed a large amount of traffic until the [War of the Elves and Sauron](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron),<sup id="cite_ref-NM_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-2">[2]</a></sup> which began in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1693](/wiki/Second_Age_1693 "Second Age 1693").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Near the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), probably between [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3431](/wiki/Second_Age_3431 "Second Age 3431") and [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> the stone bridge across the Anduin was specially enlarged and strengthened to carry the armies of the [Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men "Last Alliance of Elves and Men").<sup id="cite_ref-UT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-1">[1]</a></sup>

By the time of the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the stone bridge over the Anduin had been lost and the river was crossed at the [Old Ford](/wiki/Old_Ford). [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) intended to use the Old Forest Road to travel through Mirkwood, but [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn) warned them that the road from the Pass of Imladris to the entrance of the Old Forest Road at the western edge of Mirkwood was often used by [Goblins](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") and that he had heard that the road was overgrown and disused at the eastern end and led to impassable marshes near the River Running where the paths had long been lost.<sup id="cite_ref-lodg_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lodg-9">[9]</a></sup> He instead recommended they use the [Elf-path](/wiki/Elf-path), a secret path made by the [Elves of Mirkwood](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood).

It is possible that the Old Forest Road was repaired and used again after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019");<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> because the host of [Lórien](/wiki/L%C3%B3rien) destroyed [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur), the forest was cleansed and all the wide forest between the [Mountains of Mirkwood](/wiki/Mountains_of_Mirkwood) in the north and the [Narrows of the Forest](/wiki/Narrows_of_the_Forest) in the south was given to the [Beornings](/wiki/Beornings) and the [Woodmen](/wiki/Woodmen) by [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Men-i-Naugrim* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-12">[12]</a></sup> It means "Way of the Dwarves" or "Dwarf Road".<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> It is a compound of *[men](</wiki/Men_(Elvish)> "Men (Elvish)")* ("road, way"), *[i](/wiki/I "I")* ("the") and *[Naugrim](/wiki/Naugrim)* ("dwarves").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-12">[12]</a></sup>
