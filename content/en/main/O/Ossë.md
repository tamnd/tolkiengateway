**Ossë** was a [Maia](../M/Maiar.md) of the [Inner Seas](../I/Inner_Seas.md) associated with [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md).

## Attributes {#Attributes}

Ossë was a vassal of Ulmo, but he loved the winds and preferred the coasts and isles of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md)<sup id="cite_ref-s3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s3-1">[1]</a></sup> over the deeps of his master. There was some darkness and violence in his spirit, presumably after his seduction by [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md), and he enjoyed raging in storms despite the will of Ulmo; only his spouse, [Uinen](../U/Uinen.md), could restrain his wild urges. He obeyed the will of [Mandos](../M/Mandos.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and was a friend of all mariners, but they didn't trust his unpredictable temperament.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It was said that during the early days of [Arda](../A/Arda.md), Ossë turned to [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) for a time, but Uinen brought him back to the loyalty of Ulmo. However, Ossë's wild and wilful nature was still evident despite remaining loyal to Ulmo.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [Great March](../G/Great_March.md) of the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), Ossë was sent to befriend the [Lindar](../L/Lindar.md), the clan of the Eldar who lingered by the shores of [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md) on their way, and he taught them sea-lore and sea-music. When Ulmo returned with the floating island for them to resume their trip to the west, Ossë (who loved the shores of Middle-earth) did not wish to part with his friends; he persuaded some, like [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md), to remain there, at the [Falas](../F/Falas.md) and the [Bay of Balar](../B/Bay_of_Balar.md). These afterwards became the [Falathrim](../F/Falathrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-s3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s3-1">[1]</a></sup>

As Ulmo ferried the floating island with [Olwë](Olwë.md) and his Lindar to [Eldamar](../E/Eldamar.md), they heard Ossë's song and begged Ulmo to stop off the shore, which he did. Ossë fastened the island to the bottom, and it became [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md), the lonely isle off the [Bay of Eldamar](../B/Bay_of_Eldamar.md). However soon the [Falmari](../F/Falmari.md) of the island longed for the splendour and [Light of Valinor](../L/Light_of_Valinor.md) and wished to see their kindred there. Grieving, Ossë submitted to the will of the Valar and Ulmo, and taught them ship-building and offered them [Swans](../S/Swans.md) to draw them to Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-s3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s3-1">[1]</a></sup>

With his master, Ossë often visited [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) and the feet of Mount [Taras](../T/Taras.md) in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), and many [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) were concentrated to that place.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After the [First Kinslaying](../F/First_Kinslaying.md), [Olwë](Olwë.md) called upon Ossë against the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), but he came not, as the Valar did not wish to hinder the [Flight of the Noldor](../F/Flight_of_the_Noldor.md) with violence.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) pronounced the [Doom of the Noldor](../D/Doom_of_the_Noldor~13942.md), Ossë helped guard the shores of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) to keep the ships of the Noldor from reaching [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). His storms had a part in wrecking all the ships that they sent, and only one [Elf](../E/Elves.md) from among their crews survived, by the will of Ulmo: [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) he raised the island of [Elenna](../E/Elenna.md) ([Númenor](../N/Númenor.md)) from the depths of the Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

At least two explanations exist for the name *Ossë*. In *[The Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)*, it is understood as the [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) word *osse* ("terror"), deriving from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) GOS ("dread").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

A later note pertaining to the name instead suggests it is a name of non-Elvish origin, deriving from [Valarin](../V/Valarin.md) *Oš(o)šai* ("spuming, foaming").<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-9">[9]</a></sup> The same later note mentions that Ossë's [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) names were <i><b>Gaerys</b></i> (also containing the S. element *gaer* "dread")<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and <i><b>Yssion</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In older versions of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* as well as the *[Annals of Aman](../A/Annals_of_Aman.md)*, Ossë and his spouse [Uinen](../U/Uinen.md) were Valar in their own right, and often opposed the will of Ulmo.

In [Eriol](../E/Eriol.md)'s [Old English](Old_English.md) translations, Ossë is referred to as *Saefrea* ("Sea-ruler"),<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> reflecting his status as a Vala (Fréa).

In that conception, Ossë is the source of the name *Balar* in place-names such as [Cape Balar](../C/Cape_Balar.md), [Isle of Balar](../I/Isle_of_Balar.md) and [Bay of Balar](../B/Bay_of_Balar.md), which he often visited, and from which all [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) was named.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

Ossë had a large house in [Valmar](../V/Valmar.md) made by [Ónen](../_/Ónen.md) and [Oarni](Oarni.md) from pearls with a flour from sea water and roof from foam. He lived there during congregations of Valar or when the noise of the sea tired him.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> In *[The Tale of the Sun and Moon](../T/The_Tale_of_the_Sun_and_Moon.md)*, it was mentioned that Ossë had green hair and his beard was torn.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

*Ossë* was also the name of a [Tengwa](../T/Tengwa.md) similar to Roman c, which apparently had the value *o*.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> However elsewhere, this letter is named *[Elwë](../E/Elwë.md)* and has the value of *e*. In the [Mode of Beleriand](../M/Mode_of_Beleriand.md) this letter instead has the value *a*.

His name *Falman* from the root FALA (e.g. *falma* "foam") appears in *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>
