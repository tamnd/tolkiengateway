**Ossë** was a [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar") of the [Inner Seas](/wiki/Inner_Seas) associated with [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo).

## Attributes {#Attributes}

Ossë was a vassal of Ulmo, but he loved the winds and preferred the coasts and isles of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth)<sup id="cite_ref-s3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s3-1">[1]</a></sup> over the deeps of his master. There was some darkness and violence in his spirit, presumably after his seduction by [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth"), and he enjoyed raging in storms despite the will of Ulmo; only his spouse, [Uinen](/wiki/Uinen), could restrain his wild urges. He obeyed the will of [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and was a friend of all mariners, but they didn't trust his unpredictable temperament.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It was said that during the early days of [Arda](/wiki/Arda), Ossë turned to [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") for a time, but Uinen brought him back to the loyalty of Ulmo. However, Ossë's wild and wilful nature was still evident despite remaining loyal to Ulmo.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [Great March](/wiki/Great_March) of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), Ossë was sent to befriend the [Lindar](/wiki/Lindar), the clan of the Eldar who lingered by the shores of [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) on their way, and he taught them sea-lore and sea-music. When Ulmo returned with the floating island for them to resume their trip to the west, Ossë (who loved the shores of Middle-earth) did not wish to part with his friends; he persuaded some, like [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan), to remain there, at the [Falas](/wiki/Falas) and the [Bay of Balar](/wiki/Bay_of_Balar). These afterwards became the [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim).<sup id="cite_ref-s3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s3-1">[1]</a></sup>

As Ulmo ferried the floating island with [Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB) and his Lindar to [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar), they heard Ossë's song and begged Ulmo to stop off the shore, which he did. Ossë fastened the island to the bottom, and it became [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), the lonely isle off the [Bay of Eldamar](/wiki/Bay_of_Eldamar). However soon the [Falmari](/wiki/Falmari) of the island longed for the splendour and [Light of Valinor](/wiki/Light_of_Valinor) and wished to see their kindred there. Grieving, Ossë submitted to the will of the Valar and Ulmo, and taught them ship-building and offered them [Swans](/wiki/Swans) to draw them to Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-s3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s3-1">[1]</a></sup>

With his master, Ossë often visited [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast) and the feet of Mount [Taras](/wiki/Taras) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and many [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) were concentrated to that place.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After the [First Kinslaying](/wiki/First_Kinslaying), [Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB) called upon Ossë against the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), but he came not, as the Valar did not wish to hinder the [Flight of the Noldor](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Noldor) with violence.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) pronounced the [Doom of the Noldor](/wiki/Doom_of_the_Noldor), Ossë helped guard the shores of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) to keep the ships of the Noldor from reaching [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). His storms had a part in wrecking all the ships that they sent, and only one [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") from among their crews survived, by the will of Ulmo: [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) he raised the island of [Elenna](/wiki/Elenna) ([Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor)) from the depths of the Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

At least two explanations exist for the name *Ossë*. In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, it is understood as the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) word *osse* ("terror"), deriving from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") GOS ("dread").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

A later note pertaining to the name instead suggests it is a name of non-Elvish origin, deriving from [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) *Oš(o)šai* ("spuming, foaming").<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-9">[9]</a></sup> The same later note mentions that Ossë's [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) names were <i><b>Gaerys</b></i> (also containing the S. element *gaer* "dread")<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and <i><b>Yssion</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In older versions of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* as well as the *[Annals of Aman](/wiki/Annals_of_Aman)*, Ossë and his spouse [Uinen](/wiki/Uinen) were Valar in their own right, and often opposed the will of Ulmo.

In [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol)'s [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) translations, Ossë is referred to as *Saefrea* ("Sea-ruler"),<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> reflecting his status as a Vala (Fréa).

In that conception, Ossë is the source of the name *Balar* in place-names such as [Cape Balar](/wiki/Cape_Balar), [Isle of Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar) and [Bay of Balar](/wiki/Bay_of_Balar), which he often visited, and from which all [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) was named.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

Ossë had a large house in [Valmar](/wiki/Valmar) made by [Ónen](/wiki/%C3%93nen) and [Oarni](/wiki/Oarni) from pearls with a flour from sea water and roof from foam. He lived there during congregations of Valar or when the noise of the sea tired him.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> In *[The Tale of the Sun and Moon](/wiki/The_Tale_of_the_Sun_and_Moon)*, it was mentioned that Ossë had green hair and his beard was torn.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

*Ossë* was also the name of a [Tengwa](/wiki/Tengwa) similar to Roman c, which apparently had the value *o*.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> However elsewhere, this letter is named *[Elwë](/wiki/Elw%C3%AB)* and has the value of *e*. In the [Mode of Beleriand](/wiki/Mode_of_Beleriand) this letter instead has the value *a*.

His name *Falman* from the root FALA (e.g. *falma* "foam") appears in *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One)*.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>
