<i><b>O poveste cu un hobbit</b></i> \["A Story about a Hobbit"\] was the first Romanian-language edition of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, translated by Catinca Ralea and illustrated by [Livia Rusz](../L/Livia_Rusz.md).
