**Overhill** was a small village in the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing), to the north of [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) and [The Hill](/wiki/Hobbiton_Hill "Hobbiton Hill"), marking the end of the short road that ran past [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) and [Bagshot Row](/wiki/Bagshot_Row).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Though the [Boffin Family](/wiki/Boffin_Family) was generally associated with [The Yale](/wiki/The_Yale) in the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), there was at least one Boffin who lived in Overhill: an apparently well-to-do [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") who employed [Sam's](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") cousin [Halfast](/wiki/Halfast_Gamgee "Halfast Gamgee").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other Versions of the Legendarium {#Other_Versions_of_the_Legendarium}

On the original map of the Shire, Overhill was named "Northope".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The village of Overhill is almost entirely surrounded by woods, except for the roads leading in and out. Many hobbits living there are loggers and woodworkers.</dd></dl>
