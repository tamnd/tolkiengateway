The **Orcs of Mordor** or **Mordor-orcs** were the [orc](Orcs.md) footsoldiers of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) within the Black Land of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). They made up the bulk of his hosts in the wars he waged to suborn the [Free Peoples](../F/Free_Peoples.md) throughout the [Second](../S/Second_Age.md) and [Third Ages](../T/Third_Age.md)

## History {#History}

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

After Sauron established himself in Mordor around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1000](../S/Second_Age_1000.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> he took under his command countless Orcs that continued to battle with [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md) over many centuries. The Orcs of Mordor were not simply gathered from the remnants of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s armies, but were also bred anew by Sauron.

When Sauron learned of the [Last Alliance](../L/Last_Alliance.md), he sent many Orcs from Mordor to ambush the Allied forces who would cross the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), but they were too strong and the Orcs hid themselves. That small detachment was eventually forgotten<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and it's possible they mingled with the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md).

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

There were many types in the service of the [Red Eye](../E/Eye_of_Sauron.md), but the largest and fiercest were the [Uruks of Mordor](../U/Uruk-hai.md), black Orcs of tremendous strength and ferocity first seen out of Mordor around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2475](../T/Third_Age_2475.md), assaulting [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md). Ithilien was re-taken by [Steward](../R/Ruling_Stewards.md) [Boromir](<../B/Boromir_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>), but years later Ithilien was infested again by Mordor-orcs driving most of the people west of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> They possibly were secretly bred in Mordor by the [Morgul-lords](../N/Nazgûl.md) at the behest of their master [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2480](../T/Third_Age_2480.md) Sauron started to populate [Moria](../M/Moria.md) with his creatures, and in the same time the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) began to make secret strongholds in the [Hithaeglir](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) so as to bar all the passes into [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

During the rule of the Steward [Egalmoth](<../E/Egalmoth_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>), Orc-wars broke out.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 1]</a></sup>

It seems that Sauron could breed armies of Orcs with enormous speed when they were needed. According to [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), for instance, it had taken just a few years for the Orcs to multiply in Mordor to the vast hordes seen in the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

Few years before the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) the Orcs began multiplying again and disrupted the transportation from [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) to [Osgiliath](Osgiliath.md) along the Anduin.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

In [February](../F/February.md) of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) a group of Mordor-orcs under [Grishnákh](../G/Grishnákh.md) joined forces of the [Orcs of Isengard](Orcs_of_Isengard.md) on the plains of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) during the War of the Ring. Those [raided and captured](../B/Breaking_of_the_Fellowship.md) [Merry](../M/Merry.md) and [Pippin](../P/Pippin.md). Those Orcs then attempted to head east, but returned when they noticed [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) approaching. Eventually they were wiped out by them.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

Orcs made up the backbone of the Eastern hordes during this War, supplemented by Easterlings, Haradrim, and monstrous creatures of Sauron.

Following Sauron's final defeat at the end of the Third Age, Western forces cleared the Black Gate and surrounding hills of Orcs easily without their master to guide them, though it is suggested many escaped into the vast wastes and steppes of Mordor's eastern lands.
