**Old Winyards** was a place in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md)'s [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md),<sup id="cite_ref-nomen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nomen-1">[1]</a></sup> noted for its production of strong wines of the same name.

[Bungo Baggins](../B/Bungo_Baggins.md), who delved [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md), seems to have laid down a large number of bottles of "Old Winyards". His son, [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) gave a present of a dozen bottles to [Rorimac Brandybuck](../R/Rorimac_Brandybuck.md), [Master of Buckland](../M/Master_of_Buckland.md) at the time he left the Shire,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but this still left plenty for his heir [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md). The Old Winyards lasted another seventeen years, until the last drop was drunk by Frodo as he set out on his own adventures.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "the Old Vineyards".<sup id="cite_ref-nomen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nomen-1">[1]</a></sup>

Winyard is a real-life place-name in England, and [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) notes that it descends from [Old English](Old_English.md) before the assimilation to Romance stem *vin-*.<sup id="cite_ref-nomen_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nomen-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
