**Old Winyards** was a place in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")'s [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing),<sup id="cite_ref-nomen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nomen-1">[1]</a></sup> noted for its production of strong wines of the same name.

[Bungo Baggins](/wiki/Bungo_Baggins), who delved [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End), seems to have laid down a large number of bottles of "Old Winyards". His son, [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) gave a present of a dozen bottles to [Rorimac Brandybuck](/wiki/Rorimac_Brandybuck), [Master of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland) at the time he left the Shire,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but this still left plenty for his heir [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"). The Old Winyards lasted another seventeen years, until the last drop was drunk by Frodo as he set out on his own adventures.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "the Old Vineyards".<sup id="cite_ref-nomen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nomen-1">[1]</a></sup>

Winyard is a real-life place-name in England, and [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") notes that it descends from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) before the assimilation to Romance stem *vin-*.<sup id="cite_ref-nomen_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nomen-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
