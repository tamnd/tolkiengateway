**Otho Sackville-Baggins** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2910](/wiki/Third_Age_2910 "Third Age 2910") - [3012](/wiki/Third_Age_3012 "Third Age 3012"), died aged 102) was the husband of [Lobelia Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins) and the founder of the short-lived [Sackville-Baggins Family](/wiki/Sackville-Baggins_Family). He was representative of the greedy and ill-mannered traits of his family.

## History {#History}

Otho was the only known child of [Longo Baggins](/wiki/Longo_Baggins) and [Camellia Sackville](/wiki/Camellia_Sackville).<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup> Through his mother, he was the head of the [Sackville Family](/wiki/Sackville_Family), so he adopted the double surname out of custom.<sup id="cite_ref-Letter214_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letter214-2">[2]</a></sup> Otho had [pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed") plantations in the [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> He married [Lobelia Bracegirdle](/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins "Lobelia Sackville-Baggins") with whom he had a son named [Lotho](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins "Lotho Sackville-Baggins").

He was also second in line to patriarchy of the [Baggins Family](/wiki/Baggins_Family), and would have been [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' heir were it not for his adoption of his cousin [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"). Despite laws dictating that adoption of a member of a different family would not affect headship, Bilbo was of the opinion that Frodo was still deserving of the heirship, as he was a close relative of the same name. Otho objected, though it was ultimately decided that Frodo would become the family's official heir.<sup id="cite_ref-Letter214_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letter214-2">[2]</a></sup> This did not sit well with Otho and his wife, as they both dearly wanted to live in [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).<sup id="cite_ref-Party_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-4">[4]</a></sup>

Nevertheless, Otho was a guest at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party). After Bilbo had vanished in front of the crowd during the celebration, Otho and his wife departed in wrath. The [next day](/wiki/23_September "23 September") he and Lobelia visited Bag End and demanded from [Merry Brandybuck](/wiki/Merry_Brandybuck) to see Frodo and insisted on seeing Bilbo's will, which, unfortunately for him, was valid and clear that Frodo was his legal heir.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-4">[4]</a></sup>

He did not live long enough to see Bag End as he died in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1412](/wiki/Third_Age_3012 "Third Age 3012"); it was six years later when Lobelia eventually did come into possession of Bag End, and moved in with her son.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Otho* was meaningless in contemporary [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) [Westron](/wiki/Westron). It represented an original name *Otha*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
