**Oropher** was one of the [Iathrim](/wiki/Iathrim)<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup> who later established the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm) of the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves) of [Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Greenwood_the_Great) in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). He was killed at the [Battle of Dagorlad](/wiki/Battle_of_Dagorlad) and was succeeded by his son, [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil). Thus, he was the grandfather of [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), a member of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

### Early life {#Early_life}

Oropher was one of the [Iathrim](/wiki/Iathrim) who spent his early life in [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath). Following the destruction of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), Oropher would be among the many [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) who decided to stay in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Move to Greenwood the Great {#Move_to_Greenwood_the_Great}

Among the wave of [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) who left [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) and travelled eastward, before the building of the [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1000](/wiki/Second_Age_1000 "Second Age 1000"),<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-2">[2]</a></sup> Oropher travelled to [Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Greenwood_the_Great), and it was in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750") that he was taken up by the [Elves of the Wood](/wiki/Elves_of_the_Wood) as their king and founded the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-foundation_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-foundation-3">[3]</a></sup>

The few [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) who had come with him were soon merged with the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves), adopting their customs and language and taking names of [Silvan form and style](/wiki/Silvan_Elvish "Silvan Elvish"). Oropher and his household wished to return to a simple existence natural to the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) before they had been disturbed by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup>

Originally, Oropher's realm encompassed the south of [Greenwood](/wiki/Greenwood), with dwellings about the hill of [Amon Lanc](/wiki/Amon_Lanc).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Note_14_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note_14-4">[4]</a></sup> However, throughout the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), he migrated north with his people three times.<sup id="cite_ref-Note_14_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note_14-4">[4]</a></sup> According to one tradition, the first movement was northward beyond the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields), due to his desire to distance himself from the increasing encroachments of the [Dwarves of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm) and his resentment of the intrusions of [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup>

Oropher was also disturbed by the reports of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s rising power and by the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), he dwelt in the western glens of the [Emyn Duir](/wiki/Mountains_of_Mirkwood "Mountains of Mirkwood") and his numerous people lived and roamed in the woods and vales westward as far as [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), north of the ancient [Dwarf-Road](/wiki/Men-i-Naugrim "Men-i-Naugrim").<sup id="cite_ref-Note_14_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note_14-4">[4]</a></sup>

### War of the Last Alliance {#War_of_the_Last_Alliance}

Although Oropher's desire was to distance himself and his people from the affairs of the rest of the world, he could see the danger that [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) posed, and that his defeat would bring about peace in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Thus, he joined the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men), summoning a large army which joined with [Amdír](/wiki/Amd%C3%ADr)'s smaller force of [Elves of Lórien](/wiki/Elves_of_L%C3%B3rien) to create a great host of Silvan Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Silvan Elves were strong and brave, but despite having poor armour and weapons in comparison with the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), Amdír and Oropher were unwilling to submit to the supreme command of [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) and as such they suffered heavy losses in the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance). In the very first assault upon [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), Oropher and other brave and hasty Silvan Elves rushed forward before Gil-galad gave the signal to charge. Oropher himself was killed soon afterwards, whilst two-thirds of his army perished throughout the course of the war. Upon Sauron's defeat the remnants of the army returned to Greenwood under the command of Oropher's son Thranduil, who succeeded his father to kingship.<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name <i><b>Oropher</b></i> probably means 'tall beech-tree'<sup id="cite_ref-EOA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EOA-5">[5]</a></sup> from **oro** meaning 'high' and **fêr** meaning 'beech'.<sup id="cite_ref-Thains_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thains-6">[6]</a></sup>

Oropher hailed from [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) indicating his name could be [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) ([Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin) specifically) but upon merging with the [Elves of the Wood](/wiki/Elves_of_the_Wood), the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) of his household adopted their language and took names of [Silvan form and style](/wiki/Silvan_Elvish "Silvan Elvish") so, it seems likely that his name is styled in [Silvan Elvish](/wiki/Silvan_Elvish).<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_1-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The published [Appendix B](/wiki/Appendix_B) of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, as well as one of its drafts,<sup id="cite_ref-foundation_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-foundation-3">[3]</a></sup> mention only Oropher's son Thranduil as King of the Woodland realm, founded in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750"), suggesting (but not explicitly mentioning) that he was possibly its founder and first king.

Oropher was invented later, while Tolkien was fleshing out the background of Thranduil and the history of the Silvan realms, explicitly clarifying that Thranduil was not the founder of the realm. Oropher was not introduced in more "canonical" narratives, and the drafts exploring his origins were published in *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*.

The first name of Oropher was *Rogner*, such as revealed in [The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
