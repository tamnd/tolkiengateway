**OR** (also **ORO** and **RŌ**) is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "rise, mount". The reverse root is said to be <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=N%C5%AA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NŪ (page does not exist)">NŪ, NDŪ</a> ("sink, go down").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

In a letter, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") noted the root **ŌR/RO**, signifying "rise up, go high".<sup id="cite_ref-L347_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[Rōme(n)](/wiki/R%C3%B3men "Rómen")*; *[ornā](/wiki/Orna "Orna")*; *oro*, *orto* ("mount, mountain"); *oro-* ("up, aloft"); *[órta-](/wiki/Orta- "Orta-")*<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[orod](/wiki/Ered "Ered")*<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

- ?[Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *ornē* ("tree")<sup id="cite_ref-L347_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - Quenya: *[orne](/wiki/Orne "Orne")*<sup id="cite_ref-L347_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - Sindarin: *[orn](/wiki/Orn "Orn")*<sup id="cite_ref-L347_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-2">[2]</a></sup>

Quenya *[óre](/wiki/%C3%93re "Óre")* ("heart (inner mind)", [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) *ōre*, and Sindarin *gûr*, are said to be associated with [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) OR,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> although probably not "semantically connected".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the roots ORO (with the descendant [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) form *oro-* "rise") and RŌ, ROHO (with the descendant Qenya *rōna-* "arise, rise, ascend").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appears the roots ORO- ("up; rise; high") and RŌ- ("rise"). Among the derivatives are:<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

- ORO-
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[óre](/wiki/%C3%93re "Óre")* ("rising"); *[orta-](/wiki/Orta- "Orta-")* ("rise, raise")
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *[or](/wiki/Or "Or")* ("above"); *ortho* ("raise"); *erio* ("rise")
    - ÓROT- ("height, mountain")
        - Quenya: *oron* ("mountain"); *orto* ("mountain-top")
        - Noldorin: *[orod](/wiki/Ered "Ered")*; *[orodrim](/wiki/Orodrim "Orodrim")*
        - [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin): *orth*
    - ÓR-NI- ("high tree")
        - Quenya: *[orne](/wiki/Orne "Orne")*
        - Noldorin: *[orn](/wiki/Orn "Orn")*
        - Doriathrin: *orn*

- RŌ-
    - Quenya: *[rómen](/wiki/R%C3%B3men "Rómen")*; *rómenya* ("eastern"); *róna* ("east")
    - [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin"): *róna* ("east")
        - Noldorin: *[rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn#Etymology "Rhûn")*, *[amrûn](/wiki/Amr%C3%BBn#Noldorin "Amrûn")*
