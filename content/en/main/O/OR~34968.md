**OR** (also **ORO** and **RŌ**) is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "rise, mount". The reverse root is said to be <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=N%C5%AA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NŪ (page does not exist)">NŪ, NDŪ</a> ("sink, go down").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

In a letter, [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted the root **ŌR/RO**, signifying "rise up, go high".<sup id="cite_ref-L347_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[Rōme(n)](../R/Rómen.md)*; *[ornā](Orna.md)*; *oro*, *orto* ("mount, mountain"); *oro-* ("up, aloft"); *[órta-](Orta-.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): *[orod](../E/Ered.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

- ?[Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md): *ornē* ("tree")<sup id="cite_ref-L347_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - Quenya: *[orne](Orne.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-L347_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - Sindarin: *[orn](Orn.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-L347_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-2">[2]</a></sup>

Quenya *[óre](../_/Óre.md)* ("heart (inner mind)", [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) *ōre*, and Sindarin *gûr*, are said to be associated with [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) OR,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> although probably not "semantically connected".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the roots ORO (with the descendant [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) form *oro-* "rise") and RŌ, ROHO (with the descendant Qenya *rōna-* "arise, rise, ascend").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

In the *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* appears the roots ORO- ("up; rise; high") and RŌ- ("rise"). Among the derivatives are:<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

- ORO-
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[óre](../_/Óre.md)* ("rising"); *[orta-](Orta-.md)* ("rise, raise")
    - [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *[or](Or.md)* ("above"); *ortho* ("raise"); *erio* ("rise")
    - ÓROT- ("height, mountain")
        - Quenya: *oron* ("mountain"); *orto* ("mountain-top")
        - Noldorin: *[orod](../E/Ered.md)*; *[orodrim](Orodrim.md)*
        - [Doriathrin](../D/Doriathrin.md): *orth*
    - ÓR-NI- ("high tree")
        - Quenya: *[orne](Orne.md)*
        - Noldorin: *[orn](Orn.md)*
        - Doriathrin: *orn*

- RŌ-
    - Quenya: *[rómen](../R/Rómen.md)*; *rómenya* ("eastern"); *róna* ("east")
    - [Old Noldorin](../K/Kornoldorin.md): *róna* ("east")
        - Noldorin: *[rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md#Etymology)*, *[amrûn](../A/Amrûn.md#Noldorin)*
