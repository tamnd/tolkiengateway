<i><b>Orcs: Revenge of the Ancient</b></i> was a video game developed by Berkeley Systems and slated for publication by [Sierra Entertainment](/wiki/Sierra_Entertainment) in the late 1990s.<sup id="cite_ref-ign1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ign1-1">[1]</a></sup> The game was cancelled on [22 September](/wiki/22_September) [1999](/wiki/1999) due to a restructuring at the company.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The game would have had you controlling an orc chieftain under [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), training a team of orcs using a system that its designers likened to a more sophisticated twist on the mechanics introduced by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Red,_Blue,_and_Yellow" title="wikipedia:Pokémon Red, Blue, and Yellow">the Pokémon games for Game Boy</a>. Players would also have been able to compete against others online.<sup id="cite_ref-ign1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ign1-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
