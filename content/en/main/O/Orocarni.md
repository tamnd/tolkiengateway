The **Orocarni** (also called the **Red Mountains**) was a mountain range in the far north-east of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) made by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) after [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") destroyed the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps).<sup id="cite_ref-SMA4_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SMA4-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

On the western slopes of the Orocarni grew the *[Wild Wood](/wiki/Wild_Wood)*, and near a great waterfall of a river that flowed into the Inland [Sea of Helcar](/wiki/Sea_of_Helcar) lay the bay of [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen), where the first [Elves](/wiki/Elves) woke.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> At their northern edge, the Orocarni came close to the [Ered Engrin](/wiki/Iron_Mountains "Iron Mountains"),<sup id="cite_ref-SMA4_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SMA4-2">[2]</a></sup> similar to [Ered Luin](/wiki/Blue_Mountains "Blue Mountains") in the far north-west.

The locations of the four [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") clans who lived in the East are unknown; they might or might not have resided in the Orocarni. The distance between their mansions in the East and the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), specifically [Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad), was said to be as great or greater than that of Gundabad's distance from the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) in the West.<sup id="cite_ref-Oro_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Oro-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Orocarni* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and translates as 'Red Mountains', being a compound of the prefix *[oro(d)-](/wiki/Ered "Ered")* 'mountain' and the plural of [carnë](/wiki/Carn%C3%AB "Carnë") 'red'.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
