**Ohtar** was a kinsman<sup id="cite_ref-GladdenNote_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GladdenNote-1">[1]</a></sup> and the squire of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Ohtar was apparently a [Dúnadan of Arnor](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-2">[2]</a></sup> and by the first years of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) he was fully trained and experienced and had attained the rank of "Soldier".<sup id="cite_ref-GladdenNote_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GladdenNote-1">[1]</a></sup> He served as the squire of [High King](../H/High_King_of_the_Dúnedain.md) [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-2">[2]</a></sup> and was dear to him.<sup id="cite_ref-GladdenNote_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GladdenNote-1">[1]</a></sup>

He accompanied Isildur's retinue from [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) to [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md), when they were ambushed by [Orcs](Orcs.md) at the north borders of the [Gladden Fields](../G/Gladden_Fields.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-2">[2]</a></sup> on [4 October](../_/4_October.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2](../T/Third_Age_2.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> After the first ambush Isildur gave the shards of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md)'s sword, [Narsil](../N/Narsil.md) and its sheath to Ohtar and ordered him to flee with one companion to save the shards at all cost.<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-2">[2]</a></sup> Ohtar was one of only three survivors of the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](../D/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields.md)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> and brought the shards of Narsil safely back to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) in early [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3](../T/Third_Age_3.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ohtar* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> which means "warrior".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup> It was the title of all fully trained and experienced soldiers who had not yet been granted the rank of *roquen* ("knight"). Ohtar was probably only his title and not his name and Isildur addressed him with this title at the tragic moment of handing him the shards of Narsil and the sheath to hide his feelings by using his formal title.<sup id="cite_ref-GladdenNote_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GladdenNote-1">[1]</a></sup>
