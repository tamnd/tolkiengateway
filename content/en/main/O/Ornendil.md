**Ornendil** was the older<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> son of King [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Eldacar (King of Gondor)") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Ornendil_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ornendil-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1432](/wiki/Third_Age_1432 "Third Age 1432") Ornendil's grandfather, King [Valacar](/wiki/Valacar) died and the civil war of the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife) began,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> because some of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain), especially in the southern provinces refused to accept Eldacar as their new king, who had been born in an alien country and whose mother [Vidumavi](/wiki/Vidumavi) was perceived by them to be of a lesser and alien race. [Castamir the Usurper](/wiki/Castamir "Castamir"), one of the leaders of the rebels against Eldacar, besieged [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath), which Eldacar held for a long time.<sup id="cite_ref-Ornendil_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ornendil-3">[3]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1437](/wiki/Third_Age_1437 "Third Age 1437")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> the forces of Castamir took and burnt Osgiliath. Though [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Eldacar (King of Gondor)") managed to escape to his kinfolk in [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion), Ornendil was captured, and put to death by Castamir.<sup id="cite_ref-Ornendil_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ornendil-3">[3]</a></sup> Ten years later, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1447](/wiki/Third_Age_1447 "Third Age 1447"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Eldacar managed to avenge his son's death by killing Castamir in the [Battle of the Crossings of Erui](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Crossings_of_Erui).<sup id="cite_ref-Ornendil_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ornendil-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Ornendil is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. It means "Lover of Trees".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> It is a compound of [orne](/wiki/Orne "Orne") ("tree") and [-(n)dil](/wiki/-ndil "-ndil") ("lover", "friend").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other verions of the legendarium {#Other_verions_of_the_legendarium}

A late manuscript of what would later become Appendix A mentions that Ornendil was killed in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1446&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1446 (page does not exist)">1446</a> and that his brother Aldamir was the third child of Eldacar, which implies that Ornendil also had a sister.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>
