**Old Noakes** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") from [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater).

## History {#History}

Noakes was a regular at the [Ivy Bush](/wiki/Ivy_Bush), and a friend of [the Gaffer](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee")'s. He was present at his drink just before [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party). Asking the Gaffer about [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), he thought that he must be more than a half [a Brandybuck](/wiki/Brandybuck_Family "Brandybuck Family"), openly wondering why [anyone](/wiki/Drogo_Baggins "Drogo Baggins") should look for [a wife](/wiki/Primula_Brandybuck "Primula Brandybuck") in [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland). When the Gaffer mentioned Frodo's parents' accident, Noakes mentioned he heard they went out after dinner at night and that it was Drogo Baggins' weight which sunk the boat.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Noake(s) or Noke(s) is a real-life English surname, derived probably from the place-name No(a)ke, etymologized as *atten oke* "at the oak".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early draft, Noakes asked about [Bingo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and mentioned that he would come of age on the same day as [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
