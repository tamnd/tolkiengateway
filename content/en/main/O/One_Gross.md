**One Gross** is a measurement equal to 144 of something (a dozen dozens).

One gross was the number of people invited to [Bilbo's Birthday Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md) in order to match the sum of his and [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)'s ages (111 and 33). However, when [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) called them thus, many were insulted as the term "gross" was not used properly on people.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
