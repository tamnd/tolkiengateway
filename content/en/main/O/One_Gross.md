**One Gross** is a measurement equal to 144 of something (a dozen dozens).

One gross was the number of people invited to [Bilbo's Birthday Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") in order to match the sum of his and [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins")'s ages (111 and 33). However, when [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) called them thus, many were insulted as the term "gross" was not used properly on people.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
