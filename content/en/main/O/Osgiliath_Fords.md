The **Osgiliath Fords**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was an area in the city of [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) where some bridges were built. One of them housed the [Dome of Stars](/wiki/Dome_of_Stars).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup> The river they crossed over was the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) a large and mighty river.

Eventually, these fords and bridges were abandoned along with the rest of the city when [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) launched a full scale attack.
