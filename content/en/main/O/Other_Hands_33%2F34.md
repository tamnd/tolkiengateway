<b><i>Other Hands</i> 33/34</b> is a double-issue of *[Other Hands](/wiki/Other_Hands)*, a magazine devoted to *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*. The theme of the issue is "War!"

The issue also contains linguistic contributions by [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo).
