<b><i>Other Hands</i> 33/34</b> is a double-issue of *[Other Hands](Other_Hands.md)*, a magazine devoted to *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)*. The theme of the issue is "War!"

The issue also contains linguistic contributions by [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md).
