**Orgulas Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland).

## History {#History}

Orgulas was the youngest son of [Marmadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Marmadoc_Brandybuck) and [Adaldrida Bolger](/wiki/Adaldrida_Bolger). He had one son, [Gorbulas](/wiki/Gorbulas_Brandybuck "Gorbulas Brandybuck").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Orgulas* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) noted that the adjective *orgulous* ("proud", "haughty"), an anglisized form of a French word corresponding to *orgeilleux in Modern French, is used by Malory in* Le Morte Darthur*. There it is used as a title of a Knight of the Round Table named Bellyance* le Orgulus<i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></i>
