<b><i>Other Hands</i> 31/32</b> is a double-issue of *[Other Hands](Other_Hands.md)*, a magazine devoted to *[MERP](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)*. The theme of the issue is "Evil Races".

The issue also contains linguistic contributions by [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) and [Arden Smith](../A/Arden_R._Smith.md), and maps by Jeff J. Erwin and [Thomas Morwinsky](../T/Thomas_Morwinsky.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

*Article name - (Author's name) \[Publisher's notes\]*

- Editorial - ([Chris Seeman](../C/Chris_Seeman.md)) \[2 pgs\]
- Magic Feature: Heartstrings - (David Wendelken) \[2 new Elven spell lists; 5 pgs\]
- The Lughoth - (George Photopoulos) \[background, layouts and stats for an Angmarean Orc-tribe; 7 pgs\]
- Tol Fuin and the Shadowy Isles - (Jeff J. Erwin) \[gazetteer and map for the islands of lost Beleriand; 8 pgs\]
- Mithril Feature: Mayhem under the Mountain - ([Chris Seeman](../C/Chris_Seeman.md)) \[Orcs of Mount Gundabad; 2 pgs\]
- Using [D&amp;D](../D/Dungeons_and_Dragons.md) 3rd Edition for Middle-earth Campaigns - (Brian McNeilly) \[conversion rules; 6 pgs\]
- The Great Spiders - (Justin Morgan-Davies) \[background and stats for giant spiders; 4 pgs\]
- Supplement: The Eyes of Oclanoc and Other Tales - (John Bliven Morin) \[complete, ready-to-run adventure module set in Lebennin; 16 pgs\]
