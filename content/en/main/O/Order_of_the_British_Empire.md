The **Most Excellent Order of the British Empire** is an order of chivalry in the British honours system, and its most populous, with five classes in civil and military divisions.

Queen Elizabeth II (b.1926 - d.2022) appointed [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) a Commander of the Order of the British Empire (CBE) in the New Year Honours of [1 January](../_/1_January.md) [1972](../_/1972.md) "For services to English Literature".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Tolkien received the insignia of the Order at Buckingham Palace on [28 March](../_/28_March.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) also had been named on the last list of honours earlier in December [1951](../_/1951.md), but he declined.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Other recipients include actors [Christopher Lee](../C/Christopher_Lee.md), [Ian Holm](../I/Ian_Holm.md), [Ian McKellen](../I/Ian_McKellen.md), [Annette Crosbie](../A/Annette_Crosbie.md), [Antony Sher](../A/Antony_Sher.md), [Lenny Henry](../L/Lenny_Henry.md), [Martin Jarvis](../M/Martin_Jarvis.md), [John Hurt](../J/John_Hurt.md), [Billy Connolly](../B/Billy_Connolly.md), [Benedict Cumberbatch](../B/Benedict_Cumberbatch.md), and [Derek Jacobi](../D/Derek_Jacobi.md).
