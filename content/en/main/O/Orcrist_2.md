<b><i>Orcrist</i> 2</b> is an issue of the journal *[Orcrist](</wiki/Orcrist_(journal)> "Orcrist (journal)")*.

The 1st printing had a cover with a drawing of a sword ([Orcrist](/wiki/Orcrist)) by Ivor Rogers. The 2nd printing had cover illustrations by Laura Haglund: the front cover showed a two-headed [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons"); the back cover showed a [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum)-like creature.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
