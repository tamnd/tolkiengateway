<b><i>Orcrist</i> 2</b> is an issue of the journal *[Orcrist](<Orcrist_(journal).md>)*.

The 1st printing had a cover with a drawing of a sword ([Orcrist](Orcrist.md)) by Ivor Rogers. The 2nd printing had cover illustrations by Laura Haglund: the front cover showed a two-headed [dragon](../D/Dragons.md); the back cover showed a [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md)-like creature.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
