The **Overbourn Marshes** were located in the [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing), south of the [Shirebourn](/wiki/Shirebourn) river, where it flowed into the [Brandywine](/wiki/Baranduin "Baranduin") river.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name probably contains [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/ofer#Etymology_2" title="wiktionary:ofer">ofer</a></i> "border, margin, edge" and the element *-bourn*, common in English river- and place-names, referring to the [Shirebourn](/wiki/Shirebourn). However the first element can also be read as "over, across" in the modern sense, therefore the name can be understood as "marshes across the stream".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
