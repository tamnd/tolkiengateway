**Owls** are solitary nocturnal birds of prey. They must have been fairly common in the northern parts of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), for when the [Dwarves](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") sent [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) to spy on the Trolls he was told that if he could not come back he was to "hoot twice like a barn-owl and once like a screech-owl". Thus the Dwarves assumed that Bilbo was familiar with the exact calls that these two types of owls produced.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Much later, on the first night with [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn), Bilbo thought he could hear the hoot of owls in the rafters of the hall.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<h2><span id="Tolkien.27s_life"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Tolkien&#39;s_life">Tolkien&#39;s life</span></h2>

In [1928](/wiki/1928), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) made an illustration of "Owlamoo", an evil owl who appeared in nightmares of his child [Michael](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien "Michael Tolkien").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
