**Odovacar Bolger** ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1336](/wiki/Third_Age_2936 "Third Age 2936")—[S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1431](/wiki/Fourth_Age_10 "Fourth Age 10")) was a [hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Odovacar was the son of [Herugar Bolger](/wiki/Herugar_Bolger) and [Jessamine Boffin](/wiki/Jessamine_Boffin). He became head of the family after his father's death. Odovacar married [Rosamunda Took](/wiki/Rosamunda_Took), and had two children: [Fredegar](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger "Fredegar Bolger") and [Estella](/wiki/Estella_Bolger "Estella Bolger"). They were all present at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Bolger family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The name <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odoacer" title="wikipedia:Odoacer">Odovacar</a> contains *aud* ("wealth") and *vacar* ("watchful").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It was the name of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odoacer" title="wikipedia:Odoacer">a germanic king of Italy</a>.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Odovacar Bolger is a pig farmer living in <a href="/wiki/Budgeford" title="Budgeford">Budgeford</a>. He asks the player to investigate the rumour of a Black Rider seen at his farm at night, but it turns out to be simply another hobbit playing pranks.</dd></dl>
