**Oaths Fulfilled** is the forty-fifth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* and the fifty-fifth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") walk across the battlefield of [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields) and find [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) and [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas). The [King of the Dead](/wiki/King_of_the_Dead) demands to be released, but Gimli objects, "*Very handy in a tight spot these lads, despite the fact they're dead.*" The King of the Dead reminds Aragorn that he had given him his word, and Aragorn honors his promise.
