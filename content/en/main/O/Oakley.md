**Oakley** (in the Vulgar tongue) or **Quercetum** (in Latin) was a neighbouring village of [Ham](/wiki/Ham) where [Farmer Giles](/wiki/Farmer_Giles) lived. When the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons#Other_fiction "Dragons") [Chrysophylax](/wiki/Chrysophylax) came to the [Middle Kingdom](/wiki/Middle_Kingdom) initially he was far away and therefore not a great worry to the farmer. However, when Chrysophylax came to Quercetum and ate sheep, cows, two young people, and the village parson, the folk of Ham became alarmed and called upon Giles to save them.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Oakley* derives from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *acleah* 'oak wood'.<sup id="cite_ref-oakley_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oakley-2">[2]</a></sup> The Latin *Quercetum* (cf. *quercus* 'oak') has the same meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In Oxfordshire there is a village of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakley,_Oxfordshire" title="wikipedia:Oakley, Oxfordshire">Oakley</a> about five miles northeast of [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford) and in about same distance northwest of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Thame&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Thame (page does not exist)">Thame</a> (or [Ham](/wiki/Ham)). Thus it can be said to be the "neighbouring village". The church of Oakley is recorded in 1142.<sup id="cite_ref-oakley_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oakley-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
