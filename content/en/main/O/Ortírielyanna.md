**Ortírielyanna** is a translation of the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub_tuum_praesidium" title="wikipedia:Sub tuum praesidium">Sub tuum praesidium</a></i> into [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) made by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in the 1950s. It was published in [*Vinyar Tengwar*, Number 44](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_44.md), June 2002 in 2002, edited by [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md), [Arden R. Smith](../A/Arden_R._Smith.md), and [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md).

The *Sub tuum praesidium* is the oldest known hymn to [Virgin Mary](../V/Virgin_Mary.md) and is often used in Christian services as a prelude to the Litany of the Blessed Virgin Mary (also known as the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litany_of_the_Blessed_Virgin_Mary" title="wikipedia:Litany of the Blessed Virgin Mary">Litany of Loreto</a>). In 1944, Tolkien recommended his son [Christopher](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) to learn "*the Litany of Loretto (with the prayer Sub tuum praesidium)*" by heart.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was therefore logical that Tolkien created his own [Quenya Litany of Loreto](../T/The_Litany_of_Loreto.md), which immediately follows in the same manuscript as the *Ortírielyanna*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## The prayer {#The_prayer}

The final version of the prayer:

<table style="margin-bottom: 10px;">
<tbody><tr>
<td>
<dl><dd><i>Ortírielyanna rucimme, Aina Eruontari,</i></dd>
<dd><i>alalye nattira arca·ndemmar sangiessemman</i></dd>
<dd><i>ono alye eterúna me illume ilya raxellor</i></dd>
<dd><i>alcarin Vénde ar manaquenta.</i></dd></dl>
</td>
<td>
<dl><dd><dl><dd>We fly to thy patronage, O holy Mother of God;</dd>
<dd>despise not our petitions in our necessities,</dd>
<dd>but deliver us always from all dangers,</dd>
<dd>O glorious and blessed Virgin.</dd></dl></dd></dl>
</td></tr></tbody></table>
