The **Oath of Eorl**, also called the **Oath of Cirion**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and the **Oath of Cirion and Eorl**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>, was the alliance sworn between the nations of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup>

Also agreed was whenever help was needed, the two kingdoms would aid each other.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup> The signal that help was needed was the [Red Arrow](/wiki/Red_Arrow), a symbol of danger. For faster communication between the two allies, the [warning beacons of Gondor](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor") were used. These beacons were placed on seven hills along the northern ridge of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains).

## History {#History}

### The Oath {#The_Oath}

The Oath was first sworn in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510") by [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion), the twelfth Ruling Steward of Gondor, and by [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl), King of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) (later first King of Rohan) following the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant). The Oath stated that the province of [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) would be forever of Rohan, established its borders, and an alliance between the kingdoms. The two rulers swore their oaths on the [Halifirien](/wiki/Halifirien) hill at the site of the [Tomb of Elendil](/wiki/Tomb_of_Elendil).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup>

Eorl spoke his oath in [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese):

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Hear now all peoples who bow not to the Shadow in the East, by the gift of the Lord of the Mundburg we will come to dwell in the land that he names Calenardhon, and therefore I vow in my own name and on behalf of the Éothéod of the North that between us and the Great People of the West there shall be friendship for ever: their enemies shall be our enemies, their need shall be our need, and whatsoever evil, or threat, or assault may come upon them we will aid them to the utmost end of our strength. This vow shall descend to my heirs, all such as may come after me in our new land, and let them keep it in faith unbroken, lest the Shadow fall upon them and they become accursed.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Eorl</cite></p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup>

In addition to his oath, Cirion spoke an invocation in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and the [Common Speech](/wiki/Westron "Westron"), calling the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and [Eru](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar "Ilúvatar") to witness the oaths that were taken:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Vanda sina termaruva Elenna-nóreo alcar enyalien ar Elendil Vorondo voronwë. Nai tiruvantes i hárar mahalmassen mi Númen ar i Eru i or ilyë mahalmar eä tennoio.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Cirion</cite></p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup>

Which in Westron translates to "This oath shall stand in memory of the glory of the Land of the Star, and of the faith of Elendil the Faithful, in the keeping of those who sit upon the thrones of the West and of the One who is above all thrones for ever."<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup>

Eru's name was very rarely invoked in oaths,<sup id="cite_ref-Note_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note-4">[4]</a></sup> and indeed had not been since the time of the [Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup> Among people of Númenórean descent, it was held that only the King could call upon Eru, but Cirion had that authority as Steward and regent for the King.<sup id="cite_ref-Note_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Later history {#Later_history}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2710](/wiki/Third_Age_2710 "Third Age 2710"), King [Déor](/wiki/D%C3%A9or) asked the help of [Steward Egalmoth](</wiki/Egalmoth_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Egalmoth (Steward of Gondor)") to drive off the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) from the occupied [Ring of Isengard](/wiki/Ring_of_Isengard), but the Steward was unable to send help,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> possibly because of a renewed war against the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

During the rule of [Beren](</wiki/Beren_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Beren (Steward of Gondor)"), both nations were overwhelmed; Gondor by the [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar) and Rohan by the Dunlendings, and couldn't help each other. However, after the [Long Winter](/wiki/Long_Winter), as the Rohirrim started to repel the invaders, Gondor sent help from both west and east of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) to assist them.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Peter_Xavier_Price_-_And_They_Clasped_Hand_in_Hand.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/8/8e/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_And_They_Clasped_Hand_in_Hand.jpg/300px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_And_They_Clasped_Hand_in_Hand.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="182" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/8/8e/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_And_They_Clasped_Hand_in_Hand.jpg/450px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_And_They_Clasped_Hand_in_Hand.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/8/8e/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_And_They_Clasped_Hand_in_Hand.jpg/600px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_And_They_Clasped_Hand_in_Hand.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1000" data-file-height="608"/></a><figcaption><i>And they clasped hand in hand</i> by <a href="/wiki/Peter_Xavier_Price" title="Peter Xavier Price">Peter Xavier Price</a></figcaption></figure>

Gondor sent great help to King [Folcwine](/wiki/Folcwine), who reconquered the [west-march](/wiki/West-march "West-march") from the Dunlendings. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2885](/wiki/Third_Age_2885 "Third Age 2885"), when [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) was invaded in great strength, Folcwine sent many men, led by his twin prince sons, to Gondor. With their aid Steward [Túrin II](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_II) won a victory at the [Crossings of Poros](/wiki/Crossings_of_Poros), though the princes [Fastred](</wiki/Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine)> "Fastred (son of Folcwine)") and [Folcred](/wiki/Folcred) were slain.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the [Red Arrow](/wiki/Red_Arrow) was brought to King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) of Rohan by [a messenger of Gondor](/wiki/Hirgon "Hirgon") while [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) was besieged by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), and Théoden rode out with the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Muster_of_Rohan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Muster of Rohan (page does not exist)">Muster of Rohan</a> to the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).

The Oath was renewed by [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) upon their accessions to the thrones of Gondor and Rohan.
