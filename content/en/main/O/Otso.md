<i><b>otso</b></i> is the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word for the number seven.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It comes from the [PQ](../P/PQ.md) [root](../R/Root.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=OT&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="OT (page does not exist)">OT</a>OS/OTOK<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Otso is one of the many words for *karhu* ("bear") in Finnish. Karhu is also the name of the frequent [North Polar Bear](../N/North_Polar_Bear.md) character in *[Letters from Father Christmas](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md)*, which Tolkien wrote to his children during the years 1920-1943.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
