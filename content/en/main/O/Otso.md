<i><b>otso</b></i> is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for the number seven.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It comes from the [PQ](/wiki/PQ) [root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=OT&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="OT (page does not exist)">OT</a>OS/OTOK<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Otso is one of the many words for *karhu* ("bear") in Finnish. Karhu is also the name of the frequent [North Polar Bear](/wiki/North_Polar_Bear) character in *[Letters from Father Christmas](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas)*, which Tolkien wrote to his children during the years 1920-1943.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
