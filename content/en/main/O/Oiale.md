**oiale** is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) adverb meaning "forever, eternally, in eternity,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> everlastingly".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It can also be used as a noun for "everlasting \[?age\]"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word could have be the [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=OY&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="OY (page does not exist)">OY</a> or a stem like *[oio](/wiki/Oio "Oio")* "an endless period", plus the ending *[-lë](/wiki/-l%C3%AB)*.
