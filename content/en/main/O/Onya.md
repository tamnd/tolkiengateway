<i><b>Onya</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word with two possible meanings:

1. A term of address simply meaning "my son". Its only recorded use is between [Meneldur](/wiki/Meneldur) and his son [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion"). However, there is reason to believe that it was more widely used, at least among noble [Men](/wiki/Men). Its opposite was [Atarinya](/wiki/Atarinya), "my father", and we know that this was used by the [Exiles of Númenor](/wiki/Exiles_of_N%C3%BAmenor) long after Meneldur's time. It is therefore reasonable to assume that the word onya survived in use at least into the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), too.
2. A word for [Ent](/wiki/Ent).

Tolkien wrote that the normal word for "child" is *onna* or *onwe*, and that *onya* (which can be used to address either a son or daughter) is formed with the first person possessive suffix, *on-nya* ("my child"), comparable to *atya* ("my father"), and *amya* ("my mother<i>&#34;).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup></i>
