**Oxford** can refer to:

## Locations {#Locations}

- **[Oxford](/wiki/Oxford), a city in the Thames Valley in England.**
    - The [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *alma mater* and where he later worked as a professor.
    - The [Oxford English Dictionary](/wiki/Oxford_English_Dictionary), a publication on which J.R.R. Tolkien worked.

<h2><span id="In_Tolkien.27s_Works"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="In_Tolkien&#39;s_Works">In Tolkien&#39;s Works</span></h2>

- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Oxenford&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Oxenford (page does not exist)">Oxenford</a>, a city in the [Little Kingdom](/wiki/Little_Kingdom), also in the Thames Valley.
    - The [Four Wise Clerks of Oxenford](/wiki/Four_Wise_Clerks_of_Oxenford), characters from *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)*.

## Other Works {#Other_Works}

- *[Oxford Poetry](/wiki/Oxford_Poetry)*, an annual book which published several of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poems
- *[Tolkien's Oxford](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Oxford)*, a [2008](/wiki/2008) book by [Bob Blackham](/wiki/Robert_S._Blackham "Robert S. Blackham").
- "[Tolkien in Oxford](/wiki/Tolkien_in_Oxford)", a documentary.
