**Orofarnë** was one of the [rowans](../R/Rowans.md) of [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md), much beloved by the [Ent](../E/Ents.md) [Bregalad](../Q/Quickbeam.md). It was cut down by [Orcs](Orcs.md) of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md), arousing the anger of this impulsive Ent.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a letter, [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) explained that *orofarnë* means "mountain-dwelling" in High-elven ([Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In another manuscript he glossed *Orofarnë* as "mountain ash", consisting of the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) elements *[oro-](Oron.md#Quenya)* ("hill") + *farnë?/pharne* ("any growing thing or plant").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Editors [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md) and [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md) have made a connection between the [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) word *farnë* ("foliage"; a derivative of the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) PHAS-, also appearing under the rejected [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) PHARAN-) and the name *Orofarnë*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
