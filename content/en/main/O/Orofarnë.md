**Orofarnë** was one of the [rowans](/wiki/Rowans "Rowans") of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest), much beloved by the [Ent](/wiki/Ents "Ents") [Bregalad](/wiki/Quickbeam "Quickbeam"). It was cut down by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), arousing the anger of this impulsive Ent.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a letter, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") explained that *orofarnë* means "mountain-dwelling" in High-elven ([Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In another manuscript he glossed *Orofarnë* as "mountain ash", consisting of the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) elements *[oro-](/wiki/Oron#Quenya "Oron")* ("hill") + *farnë?/pharne* ("any growing thing or plant").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Editors [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) and [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) have made a connection between the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) word *farnë* ("foliage"; a derivative of the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") PHAS-, also appearing under the rejected [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") PHARAN-) and the name *Orofarnë*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
