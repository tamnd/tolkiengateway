**Orchaldor**, the son of [Hatholdir](/wiki/Hatholdir), was descended from the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador). He was a close friend of [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur) and married his elder daughter, [Ailinel](/wiki/Ailinel). The son of Orchaldor and Ailinel was [Soronto](/wiki/Soronto), who desired the kingship. When [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion) created a new [Law of Succession in Númenor](/wiki/Law_of_Succession_in_N%C3%BAmenor), which allowed his daughter [Ancalimë](/wiki/Tar-Ancalim%C3%AB "Tar-Ancalimë") to receive the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Sceptre of Númenor"), the possibility existed that if she refused to rule, died childless, or remained unwed beyond a certain time limit, then Soronto would have become King. As it happened, none of these possibilities came to pass and Tar-Ancalimë served as Númenor's first Ruling Queen.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Orchaldor* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Tall Lord" and is a compound of *orchal* ("tall") and *-dor* ("lord").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
