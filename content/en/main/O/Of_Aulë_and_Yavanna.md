**Of Aulë and Yavanna** is the second chapter of the *[Quenta Silmarillion](/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion)* section within *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Aul%C3%AB_the_Destroyer.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/5/5d/Ted_Nasmith_-_Aul%C3%AB_the_Destroyer.jpg/200px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Aul%C3%AB_the_Destroyer.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="307" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/5/5d/Ted_Nasmith_-_Aul%C3%AB_the_Destroyer.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Aul%C3%AB_the_Destroyer.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/5/5d/Ted_Nasmith_-_Aul%C3%AB_the_Destroyer.jpg/400px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Aul%C3%AB_the_Destroyer.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1041" data-file-height="1600"/></a><figcaption><i>Aulë Prepares to Destroy His Children</i> by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

[Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB), who was the great smith of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), wished to create life who he could teach his craft to. His impatience in waiting for the [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar) to awake got the best of him and he secretly decided to create the [Seven Fathers of the Dwarves](/wiki/Seven_Fathers_of_the_Dwarves). [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) soon learned of Aulë's actions and showed him that the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) had no free will, as Aulë had no power to create new life. Aulë offered his creation to Ilúvatar and felt they should be destroyed; however, Ilúvatar intervened and granted them life of their own. But he gave Aulë one condition: the Dwarves had to rest until the [Awakening of the Elves](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves), whom Eru had chosen to be the Firstborn of his Children.

When [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna), the spouse of Aulë, learned of her husband's deeds, she feared the Dwarves, or even the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Men](/wiki/Men), might harm her [plants](/wiki/Plants "Plants") and [trees](/wiki/Trees "Trees") which she loved. She went to [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) to seek protection. Manwë spoke with her, and while considering her wishes, he saw the [Song of Creation](/wiki/Music_of_the_Ainur "Music of the Ainur") and remembered the [Vision](/wiki/Vision_of_Il%C3%BAvatar "Vision of Ilúvatar") in which Eru told him not to fear, as spirits would also awake to protect what Yavanna held dear. Thus Manwë proclaimed that the [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles) will dwell in the mountains, and the [Shepherds of the Trees](/wiki/Ents "Ents") will walk in the woods.
