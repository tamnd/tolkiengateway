**Of Aulë and Yavanna** is the second chapter of the *[Quenta Silmarillion](../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion.md)* section within *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_Aulë_the_Destroyer.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5d/Ted_Nasmith_-_Aul%C3%AB_the_Destroyer.jpg/200px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Aul%C3%AB_the_Destroyer.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="307" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5d/Ted_Nasmith_-_Aul%C3%AB_the_Destroyer.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Aul%C3%AB_the_Destroyer.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/5d/Ted_Nasmith_-_Aul%C3%AB_the_Destroyer.jpg/400px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Aul%C3%AB_the_Destroyer.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1041" data-file-height="1600"/></a><figcaption><i>Aulë Prepares to Destroy His Children</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

[Aulë](../A/Aulë.md), who was the great smith of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), wished to create life who he could teach his craft to. His impatience in waiting for the [Children of Ilúvatar](../C/Children_of_Ilúvatar.md) to awake got the best of him and he secretly decided to create the [Seven Fathers of the Dwarves](../S/Seven_Fathers_of_the_Dwarves.md). [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md) soon learned of Aulë's actions and showed him that the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) had no free will, as Aulë had no power to create new life. Aulë offered his creation to Ilúvatar and felt they should be destroyed; however, Ilúvatar intervened and granted them life of their own. But he gave Aulë one condition: the Dwarves had to rest until the [Awakening of the Elves](../A/Awakening_of_the_Elves.md), whom Eru had chosen to be the Firstborn of his Children.

When [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md), the spouse of Aulë, learned of her husband's deeds, she feared the Dwarves, or even the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md), might harm her [plants](../P/Plants.md) and [trees](../T/Trees.md) which she loved. She went to [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) to seek protection. Manwë spoke with her, and while considering her wishes, he saw the [Song of Creation](../M/Music_of_the_Ainur.md) and remembered the [Vision](../V/Vision_of_Ilúvatar.md) in which Eru told him not to fear, as spirits would also awake to protect what Yavanna held dear. Thus Manwë proclaimed that the [Eagles](../E/Eagles.md) will dwell in the mountains, and the [Shepherds of the Trees](../E/Ents.md) will walk in the woods.
