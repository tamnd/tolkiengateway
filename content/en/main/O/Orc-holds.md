**Orc-holds** is a term used to refer to underground tunnellings of the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md), found throughout the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) and [Mount Gundabad](../M/Mount_Gundabad.md). From there they caused trouble in the nearby regions, such as [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md), [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) and [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

Mount Gundabad was the capital of Orcs in the North after its first sack in the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md). Major Orkish operations such as battles during the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs.md) and the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> were from it.

[Orkish](Orc.md) holds included [Moria](../M/Moria.md) and the underground [Goblin-town](../G/Goblin-town.md) led by the [Great Goblin](../G/Great_Goblin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
