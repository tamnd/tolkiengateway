**Orc-holds** is a term used to refer to underground tunnellings of the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains), found throughout the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) and [Mount Gundabad](/wiki/Mount_Gundabad). From there they caused trouble in the nearby regions, such as [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) and [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

Mount Gundabad was the capital of Orcs in the North after its first sack in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Major Orkish operations such as battles during the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs) and the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> were from it.

[Orkish](/wiki/Orc "Orc") holds included [Moria](/wiki/Moria) and the underground [Goblin-town](/wiki/Goblin-town) led by the [Great Goblin](/wiki/Great_Goblin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
