**Oatbarton** was a town or village in the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). The [Northway](/wiki/Northway) led from Oatbarton to the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The element *-barton*, common in English place-names ([Old English](/wiki/Old_English): *beretun* "barley-town") means "outlying grange, demesne farm",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> therefore the place was related to a large oat-farm.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2008: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Oatbarton is located within the in-game <a href="/wiki/Evendim" title="Evendim">Evendim</a> region, just outside of the Shire proper, although this is merely a gameplay distinction. It is a farm town, with the largest Northcotton farm located directly to the west of it. <a href="/wiki/Halfast_Gamgee" title="Halfast Gamgee">Halfast Gamgee</a> lives in Oatbarton.</dd></dl>
