**Oatbarton** was a town or village in the [Northfarthing](../N/Northfarthing.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). The [Northway](../N/Northway.md) led from Oatbarton to the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The element *-barton*, common in English place-names ([Old English](Old_English.md): *beretun* "barley-town") means "outlying grange, demesne farm",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> therefore the place was related to a large oat-farm.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2008: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Oatbarton is located within the in-game <a href="../E/Evendim.md" title="Evendim">Evendim</a> region, just outside of the Shire proper, although this is merely a gameplay distinction. It is a farm town, with the largest Northcotton farm located directly to the west of it. <a href="../H/Halfast_Gamgee.md" title="Halfast Gamgee">Halfast Gamgee</a> lives in Oatbarton.</dd></dl>
