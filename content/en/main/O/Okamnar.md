<i><b>okamnar</b></i> is a [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) word meaning "diphthongs".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The singular form "diphthong" is *okamna*.<sup id="cite_ref-VT44_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT44-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word comes from a [PQ](../P/PQ.md) [Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) KAM "bind, join"<sup id="cite_ref-VT44_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT44-2">[2]</a></sup> and contains the prefix *[o-](O-.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}

- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ohlon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ohlon (page does not exist)">ohlon</a></i>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

Earlier versions of the word in different sources are *osamnar* (sg.) or *samnar* (pl.) from a root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=SAM&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SAM (page does not exist)">SAM</a> "unite, join".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Both *osamnar/samnar* and revised *okamnar* date from the late 1930s,<sup id="cite_ref-VT44_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT44-2">[2]</a></sup> with [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ohlon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ohlon (page does not exist)">ohlon</a></i> "diphthong" (pl. *ohloni*) from c. 1959-60.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Tolkien created *okamna* in a note where he decided to delete SAM (and therefore *osamnar*), replacing it with KAM.<sup id="cite_ref-VT44_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT44-2">[2]</a></sup>
