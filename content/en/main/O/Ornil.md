**Ornil** was an [Elf of Nargothrond](../E/Elves_of_Nargothrond.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> who met with [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) after the [Battle of Tumhalad](../B/Battle_of_Tumhalad.md).

## History {#History}

After [Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md)'s death during the [Battle of Tumhalad](../B/Battle_of_Tumhalad.md), Túrin rushed back to [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md), gathering any survivors of the battle in the process, including Ornil. As leaves were falling from trees in a great wind, Ornil compared the changing of the seasons to the [Fall of Nargothrond](../F/Fall_of_Nargothrond.md), causing Túrin's pace to hasten.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about Ornil.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ornil* is in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md). While its meaning is not entirely clear, according to *[Eldamo](../E/Eldamo.md)* it might contain the element *[orn](Orn.md)* ("tree").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
