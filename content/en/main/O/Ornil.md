**Ornil** was an [Elf of Nargothrond](/wiki/Elves_of_Nargothrond "Elves of Nargothrond")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> who met with [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) after the [Battle of Tumhalad](/wiki/Battle_of_Tumhalad).

## History {#History}

After [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor)'s death during the [Battle of Tumhalad](/wiki/Battle_of_Tumhalad), Túrin rushed back to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), gathering any survivors of the battle in the process, including Ornil. As leaves were falling from trees in a great wind, Ornil compared the changing of the seasons to the [Fall of Nargothrond](/wiki/Fall_of_Nargothrond), causing Túrin's pace to hasten.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about Ornil.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ornil* is in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). While its meaning is not entirely clear, according to *[Eldamo](/wiki/Eldamo)* it might contain the element *[orn](/wiki/Orn "Orn")* ("tree").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
