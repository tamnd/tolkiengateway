**odog** is the number seven (7, VII) in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).

In [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), the precursor of Sindarin, "seven" is <i><b>odog</b></i>. The word derives from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") OT- (OTOS, OTOK).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
