<i><b>Oxford Mail</b></i> is a magazine based in [Oxford](Oxford.md), <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=England&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="England (page does not exist)">England</a>.

## Notable issues {#Notable_issues}

### 1956 {#1956}

[15 December](../_/15_December.md) [1956](../_/1956.md) features the article "Deddington Court Now Library" (p. 1) with parts of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s speech held at the opening of the library on [14 December](../_/14_December.md) [1956](../_/1956.md) (slightly different from the quotes appearing in the [*The Banbury Advertiser* 19 December 1956](../T/The_Banbury_Advertiser_19_December_1956.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

### 1957 {#1957}

[11 September](../_/11_September.md) [1957](../_/1957.md) features the article "Fantasy of the Year" (p. 4). This article, reporting from the International Fantasy award luncheon held on [10 September](../_/10_September.md) [1957](../_/1957.md), includes a brief quote by Tolkien who had attended and won the award.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### 1966 {#1966}

The [3 August](../_/3_August.md) [1966](../_/1966.md) issue features an interview by Tolkien to <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=John_Ezard&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="John Ezard (page does not exist)">John Ezard</a> (p. 4).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
