<i><b>Oxford Mail</b></i> is a magazine based in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=England&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="England (page does not exist)">England</a>.

## Notable issues {#Notable_issues}

### 1956 {#1956}

[15 December](/wiki/15_December) [1956](/wiki/1956) features the article "Deddington Court Now Library" (p. 1) with parts of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s speech held at the opening of the library on [14 December](/wiki/14_December) [1956](/wiki/1956) (slightly different from the quotes appearing in the [*The Banbury Advertiser* 19 December 1956](/wiki/The_Banbury_Advertiser_19_December_1956 "The Banbury Advertiser 19 December 1956")).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

### 1957 {#1957}

[11 September](/wiki/11_September) [1957](/wiki/1957) features the article "Fantasy of the Year" (p. 4). This article, reporting from the International Fantasy award luncheon held on [10 September](/wiki/10_September) [1957](/wiki/1957), includes a brief quote by Tolkien who had attended and won the award.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
