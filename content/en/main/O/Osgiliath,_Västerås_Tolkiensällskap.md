**Osgiliath, Västerås Tolkiensällskap** is the Swedish Tolkien Society of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%A4ster%C3%A5s" title="wikipedia:Västerås">Västerås</a>. As of 2024, the association seems to be inactive (<a class="external text" href="http://www.hacke.saeab.se/osgiliath/calendar.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">their calendar</a> doesn't show events past 2009).

Activities in Osgiliath are said to involve live role-playing, sword fighting, studying the works and languages of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), medieval dancing, handicraft, etc.
