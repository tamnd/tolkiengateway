<b><i>Other Hands</i> 23</b> is an issue of *[Other Hands](/wiki/Other_Hands)*, a magazine devoted to *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*. The theme of the issue is "Dragons of the North".

## Contents {#Contents}

- Editorial
- "The Dwarven Engineer" by Carl Brodt \[4 pgs; new profession with spell lists\]
- "A Character Development System for MERP" by James Garriss \[2 pgs; a class-less development system\]
- "Dragons of the North" by [Chris Seeman](/wiki/Chris_Seeman) \[7 pgs; includes 2-pg map of the Grey Mountains and the Withered Heath\]
- "The Knights of Eärendur," an adventure by Eric Dubourg \[8 pgs; set in TA 1409 Eriador\]
- "Rastarin's Log" by [Bridget Buxton](/wiki/Bridget_Buxton) \[4 pgs\]
