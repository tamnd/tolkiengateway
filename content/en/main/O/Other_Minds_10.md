<b><i>Other Minds</i> 10</b> is the tenth issue of *[Other Minds](/wiki/Other_Minds)*, a magazine devoted to *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* and other role playing games set in Middle-earth, published in August 2010.
