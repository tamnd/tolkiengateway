**Old Entish** is the thirty-ninth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and the fiftieth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) turns away from the [Entmoot](/wiki/Entmoot) temporarily to tell [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") that it has been decided they are not [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). Merry asks if they have made a decision regarding [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman), to which Treebeard says "Don't be hasty".

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Hasty? Our friends are out there. They need our help.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Merry to Treebeard</cite></p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

Treebeard gently explains to Merry that it takes a lot of time to say anything in [Old Entish](/wiki/Entish "Entish") and that [Ents](/wiki/Ents) tend not to use the language unless it is worth the time and effort to do so.
