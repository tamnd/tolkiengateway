**Overture (From "The Lord Of The Rings: The War Of The Rohirrim")** is the first track on [*The Lord of the Rings: The War of the Rohirrim* (Original Motion Picture Soundtrack)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_War_of_the_Rohirrim_(Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)> "The Lord of the Rings: The War of the Rohirrim (Original Motion Picture Soundtrack)"). It incorporates the One Ring motif and the Rohan motif from [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore)'s score for [*The Lord of the Rings* film trilogy](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").
