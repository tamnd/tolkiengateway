**Olvar** was a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for the plants and plant-life in the care of [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna). Unlike the *[kelvar](/wiki/Kelvar "Kelvar")*, they are inanimate. [Trees](/wiki/Trees) are the highest of the olvar.

Yavanna had sown seeds early in the [Spring of Arda](/wiki/Spring_of_Arda), and they sprouted and burgeoned quickly; mosses, grasses, great ferns and trees. Flowers and birds "waited still their time in the bosom of Yavanna; but wealth there was of her imagining" and they came later.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> After the destruction of the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps), the [Great Lands](/wiki/Middle-earth "Middle-earth") were left in darkness, and Yavanna protected the olvar by setting them to sleep and they did not grow until light should come again.<sup id="cite_ref-c3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-c3-2">[2]</a></sup> However, there were still kelvar in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor); and in [Nan Elmoth](/wiki/Nan_Elmoth) the trees grew tall while [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) was entranced by [Melian](/wiki/Melian).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After her husband [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) created the [Fathers of the Dwarves](/wiki/Fathers_of_the_Dwarves), she became concerned for the fate of both the [kelvar](/wiki/Kelvar "Kelvar") (animals) and olvar (plants) at the hands of their descendants. She spoke of her fears to [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB), who revealed that [Eru](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar "Ilúvatar") intended that spirits should be sent to dwell among some of the olvar and protect them. This was the ultimate origin of the beings who came to be known as [Ents](/wiki/Ents).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Olvar* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), glossed as "growing things with roots in the earth".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

There is a similarity to other relevant words (perhaps variants) such as *olwa*<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and *olba*<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> which are however glossed as "branch".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, *olwa* is derived from the [root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=G%C3%93LOB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="GÓLOB (page does not exist)">GÓLOB</a>.
