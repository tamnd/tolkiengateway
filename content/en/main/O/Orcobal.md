**Orcobal** was the *"champion of the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs)"* that was slain by [Ecthelion](/wiki/Ecthelion) in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), Orcobal was the champion of those Orcs which assailed the [Gondothlim](/wiki/Gondothlim) alongside the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) at the coming of the [Drakes](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons"). Orcobal was ultimately slain by Ecthelion of the Fountain, who, after killing two goblin captains, *"cleft the head of Orcobal their chiefest champion to his teeth"* before the gates of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-LT2Gondolin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2Gondolin-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Orcobal* is clearly [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), but it is not glossed.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Orcobal never appeared within any of Tolkien's writing after "[The Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")" in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.
