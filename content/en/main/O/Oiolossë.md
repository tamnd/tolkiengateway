<i><b>Oiolossë</b></i> was one of the names of [Taniquetil](/wiki/Taniquetil).<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Oiolossë* (also spelled *Oiolosse*)<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup> is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and means "Ever-snow-white",<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-1">[1]</a></sup> "Everwhite/snowy",<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup> or "Everlasting snow".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety379_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety379-3">[3]</a></sup> The name seems to consist of *[oio](/wiki/Oio "Oio")* "ever" (cf. root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=OY&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="OY (page does not exist)">OY-</a>)<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ety379_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety379-3">[3]</a></sup> and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Losse&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Losse (page does not exist)">losse</a></i> "snow".<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol)'s [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) translations, Oiolossë is referred to as *Sinsnáw, Sinsnǽwen* "Ever-snow".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) equivalent of *Oiolossë* was <i><b><a href="/wiki/Amon_Uilos" title="Amon Uilos">Amon Uilos</a></b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>
