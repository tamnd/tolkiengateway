**Old Toby** was one of the three most notable varieties of [pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed"), grown in the region around [Longbottom](/wiki/Longbottom) in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")'s [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing).<sup id="cite_ref-Pipeweed_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The leaf took its name from the Hobbit who introduced pipe-weed to the Shire, the original "Old Toby", [Tobold Hornblower](/wiki/Tobold_Hornblower), who grew the first true pipe-weed in his gardens in the days of [Isengrim Took II](/wiki/Isengrim_Took_II) about the year [1070](/wiki/Third_Age_2670 "Third Age 2670") of [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning).

How Old Toby came by the plant is not recorded, for to his dying day he would not tell.<sup id="cite_ref-Pipeweed_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pipeweed-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The genuine [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name was <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Zara&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Zara (page does not exist)">zara</a>-Tobi</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-2">[2]</a></sup>
