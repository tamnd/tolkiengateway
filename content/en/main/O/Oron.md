## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>oron</b></i> is an archaic/poetic (†) [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word glossed as "[tree](/wiki/Trees "Trees")".<sup id="cite_ref-PE1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE1-1">[1]</a></sup> [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") also experimented with the form *oronī* ("trees?").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

Although not an attested derivative, the word can likely be connected to [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [ŌR/RO](/wiki/OR "OR") ("rise up, go high").<sup id="cite_ref-L347_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Quenya {#Quenya}

<i><b>oron</b></i> (pl. *oronti*) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "mountain".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [ÓROT](/wiki/OR#Other_versions "OR")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>
