The **Old Guesthouse** or **Sennas Iaur**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was an inn in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). It was located on the first tier, on [Lampwrights' Street](../L/Lampwrights'_Street.md) (*Rath Celerdain*). It was surrounded on three sides by a lawn, and the fourth was open, leading out to the street.

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) met [Bergil](../B/Bergil.md) here. The inn's fate is not known, but it could have been destroyed in the [Siege of Minas Tirith](../S/Siege_of_Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Sennas Iaur* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Old Guesthouse" and is a compound of *sennas* ("guesthouse") and *[iaur](../I/Iaur.md)* ("old").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-3">[3]</a></sup>
