**Overlithe** was the leap day of the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md). It was added as an extra day every four years to accommodate annual deficits between a 365-day calendar and the actual length of a [year](../C/Coranar.md) (which is a little under a quarter of a day longer) except when this year was the last of the century.

Overlithe occurred between [Mid-year's Day](../M/Mid-year's_Day.md) and the second of the two normal [Lithedays](../L/Lithedays.md) (2 Lithe). It had no weekday name, and was treated as a special holiday by the inhabitants of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

A leap year was [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1420](../T/Third_Age_3020.md), and on that Overlithe there was the greatest merrymaking, because of its plentifous harvest and wonderful summer.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
