**Overlithe** was the leap day of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar). It was added as an extra day every four years to accommodate annual deficits between a 365-day calendar and the actual length of a [year](/wiki/Coranar "Coranar") (which is a little under a quarter of a day longer) except when this year was the last of the century.

Overlithe occurred between [Mid-year's Day](/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day) and the second of the two normal [Lithedays](/wiki/Lithedays) (2 Lithe). It had no weekday name, and was treated as a special holiday by the inhabitants of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

A leap year was [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1420](/wiki/Third_Age_3020 "Third Age 3020"), and on that Overlithe there was the greatest merrymaking, because of its plentifous harvest and wonderful summer.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
