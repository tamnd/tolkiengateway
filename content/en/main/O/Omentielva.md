**Omentielva** (also the International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien’s Invented Languages) is a biennial scholarly conference for the invented languages of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). The name of the conference comes from the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word for "meeting", *[omentie](Omentie.md)*. The meetings are all numbered using the appropriate Quenya ordinal. Thus, the first meeting is called Omentielva Minya (literally "first meeting").

The proceedings of the Conference are published as *[Arda Philology](../A/Arda_Philology.md)*.

## Omentielva Minya {#Omentielva_Minya}

The First International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien’s Invented Languages was held from August 4 through August 8, 2005 at the University of Stockholm, Sweden.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Omentielva Tatya {#Omentielva_Tatya}

The Second International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien’s Invented Languages was held from August 8 through August 11, 2007 at Lessius Hogeschool, Antwerp, Belgium.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Omentielva Nelya {#Omentielva_Nelya}

The Third International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien’s Invented Languages was held from August 6 through August 9, 2009 in Whitehaven, England.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Omentielva Cantea {#Omentielva_Cantea}

The Fourth International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien’s Invented Languages was held from August 11 through August 14, 2011 at Universidad Politécnica de Valencia, Spain.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Omentielva Lempea {#Omentielva_Lempea}

The Fifth International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien’s Invented Languages was held from August 8 through August 11, 2013 at Arkadia International Bookshop, Helsinki, Finland.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Omentielva Enquea {#Omentielva_Enquea}

The Sixth International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien’s Invented Languages was held from August 6 through August 9, 2015 at Greisinger Museum, Jenins, Switzerland.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Omentielva Otsea {#Omentielva_Otsea}

The Seventh International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien’s Invented Languages was held from August 10 through August 13, 2017 at California State University's Hayward Campus in the eastern San Francisco Bay area.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Omentielva Toltea {#Omentielva_Toltea}

The Eighth International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien's Invented Languages was held from August 1 through August 4, 2019 at the University of Iceland and Gröndalshús in Reykjavik, Iceland.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Onatsie {#Onatsie}

The Ninth International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien's Invented Languages was originally supposed to be held in August 2021 but had to be postponed; in its place an online discussion called Onatsie was held on August 6 and 7, 2021.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Omentielva Nertea {#Omentielva_Nertea}

The Ninth International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien's Invented Languages was held from August 4 through August 7, 2022 at the Collegium Maius, Jagiellonian University in Kraków and Hotel Załęże in Katowice, Poland.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Omentielva Quainea {#Omentielva_Quainea}

The Tenth International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien's Invented Languages is scheduled to be held in August 2024 in France.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
