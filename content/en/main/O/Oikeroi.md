**Oikeroi** was a [cat](/wiki/Cats "Cats") according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

He was a thane of [Tevildo](/wiki/Tevildo), described as warlike and fierce, but he was killed by [Huan](/wiki/Huan).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Huan gave the coat of Oikeroi to [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien), who used it to disguise [Beren](/wiki/Beren) on their [quest](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril "Quest for the Silmaril") to retrieve a [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) from [Melko](/wiki/Melko). Oikeroi had previously spent a good deal of time about the hall of Melko, allowing Beren to pass unnoticed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In later writings, Beren was disguised in the coat of the great wolf [Draugluin](/wiki/Draugluin), rather than the coat of Oikeroi.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
