**Oikeroi** was a [cat](../C/Cats.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

## History {#History}

He was a thane of [Tevildo](../T/Tevildo.md), described as warlike and fierce, but he was killed by [Huan](../H/Huan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Huan gave the coat of Oikeroi to [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md), who used it to disguise [Beren](../B/Beren.md) on their [quest](../Q/Quest_for_the_Silmaril.md) to retrieve a [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md) from [Melko](../M/Melko.md). Oikeroi had previously spent a good deal of time about the hall of Melko, allowing Beren to pass unnoticed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In later writings, Beren was disguised in the coat of the great wolf [Draugluin](../D/Draugluin.md), rather than the coat of Oikeroi.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
