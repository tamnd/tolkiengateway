**Ost-in-Edhil** ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750") – [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697"), existed for c. 947 years) was the chief city in the elven realm of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion)<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-1">[1]</a></sup> in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).

The [highroad](/wiki/Elven-way "Elven-way") connected Ost-in-Edhil with the [west gate of Moria](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin "Doors of Durin").<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-4">[4]</a></sup> It was the only road of old from the west to [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") and ran along the river [Sirannon](/wiki/Sirannon). Ost-in-Edhil was not within sight of the road for many miles from Moria.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-5">[5]</a></sup> As a consequence, it is possible that Ost-in-Edhil was located near the river Sirannon many miles downstream from its source at the west gate of Moria.

## History {#History}

Around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [700](/wiki/Second_Age_700 "Second Age 700") [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and her husband [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), who were regarded as the Lord and Lady of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) in Eriador founded the elven realm of Eregion. The Elves of Eregion were primarily [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor). [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor), a Noldorin smith who had accompanied Galadriel and Celeborn, and other elven smiths founded the [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-M%C3%ADrdain), a brotherhood of Elven craftsmen.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-1">[1]</a></sup>

Around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750") the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) started to build Ost-in-Edhil.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-1">[1]</a></sup> The Elves of Ost-in-Edhil also built<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> a highroad that connected the city with the west gate of Khazad-dûm.<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [1200](/wiki/Second_Age_1200 "Second Age 1200") a mysterious craftsman named [Annatar](/wiki/Annatar) came to the city. Annatar claimed to have been sent as an emissary by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) to aid the Elves. Although [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) did not trust Annatar and treated him with contempt, Annatar reacted with patience and courtesy, because he wanted to avoid an open conflict with her at the moment. Annatar was more successful to by accepted by the smiths of the Gwaith-i-Mírdain and in secret instructed them in the art of smithing. After some time the Gwaith-i-Mírdain fell under his influence.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-1">[1]</a></sup>

Between [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1350](/wiki/Second_Age_1350 "Second Age 1350") and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1400&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1400 (page does not exist)">1400</a> Annatar persuaded the Gwaith-i-Mírdain to rebel against the rule of Galadriel and Celeborn and to seize control over the elven realm of Eregion. As a consequence Galadriel left Eregion with her daugther [Celebrian](/wiki/Celebrian) and travelled through Khazad-dûm to the elven realm of [Lórinand](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien"). Celeborn did not want to enter Khazad-dûm and stayed in Eregion where he was ignored by Celebrimbor.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the Gwaith-i-Mírdain started to forge the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power) Annatar left Eregion around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1500](/wiki/Second_Age_1500 "Second Age 1500")<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-1">[1]</a></sup> and returned to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). Around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1590](/wiki/Second_Age_1590 "Second Age 1590") Celebrimbor completed the forging of the [three Rings of Power of the Elves](/wiki/Three_Rings "Three Rings").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1600](/wiki/Second_Age_1600 "Second Age 1600") Annatar, who was in truth [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) forged a [master ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") in Mordor with the fire of [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom) to dominate the wearers of the Rings of Power. When Sauron put the master ring on his finger Celebrimbor<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and the other Elves became aware of his plan to be the master of the wearers of the Rings of Power and took their rings off<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-4">[4]</a></sup> and Celebrimbor revolted against Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-1">[1]</a></sup> After Sauron became aware of the revolt of Celebrimbor, he started to gather an army to take the Rings of Power from the Elves of Eregion by force.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1695](/wiki/Second_Age_1695 "Second Age 1695") the forces of Sauron came through [Calenardhon](/wiki/Rohan "Rohan"), invaded Eriador and began the [War of the Elves and Sauron](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron).<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-1">[1]</a></sup> Only two years later, in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697")<sup id="cite_ref-SA1697_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA1697-2">[2]</a></sup> the forces of Sauron [stormed and destroyed Ost-in-Edhil](/wiki/Sack_of_Eregion "Sack of Eregion").<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-1">[1]</a></sup>

After four years,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Sauron and his army were eventually defeated by the Elves with help from the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1701](/wiki/Second_Age_1701 "Second Age 1701") and Sauron fled back to Mordor.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_1-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-1">[1]</a></sup>

Forty-seven centuries later the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) travelled for many miles on the broken and decayed road that once led from Ost-in-Edhil, along the [Sirannon](/wiki/Sirannon), towards the West Gate of [Moria](/wiki/Moria) without seeing the ruins of Ost-in-Edhil.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ost-in-Edhil* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-10">[10]</a></sup> It means "Fortress of the Eldar".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests that the name is a combination of *[ost](/wiki/Ost "Ost")* ("fortress", "fort", "stronghold", "citadel", "fortified town", "enclosure"), the plural *in* of the definite article *[i](/wiki/I "I")* and the plural *Edhil* of *[edhel](/wiki/Edhel "Edhel")* "Elf".<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-10">[10]</a></sup>
