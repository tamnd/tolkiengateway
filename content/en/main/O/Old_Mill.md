The **Old Mill** was the water-mill of [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md), owned and run by the [Sandyman Family](../S/Sandyman_Family.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The Old Mill was a corn mill powered by a large water wheel. It lay directly adjacent to the bridge across [the Water](../T/The_Water.md) and next to the [Old Grange](Old_Grange.md) as well.

## History {#History}

In [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1341](../T/Third_Age_2941.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) ran by the Mill and a mile beyond to get to the [Green Dragon](../G/Green_Dragon~72852.md) to meet [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> When he and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) returned to [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md), they crossed the bridge and passed the Mill.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> By [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1418](../T/Third_Age_3018.md) [Sandyman](../S/Sandyman.md) had been the miller for some time and passed down his craft to his son, [Ted](../T/Ted_Sandyman.md). [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](../L/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins.md) bought the mill, and many other buildings in [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md), before he moved in to [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md).

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/M/Matěj_Čadil_-_Hobbiton_Befouled.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/dd/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Hobbiton_Befouled.jpg/300px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Hobbiton_Befouled.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="436" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/dd/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Hobbiton_Befouled.jpg/450px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Hobbiton_Befouled.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/dd/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Hobbiton_Befouled.jpg/600px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Hobbiton_Befouled.jpg 2x" data-file-width="800" data-file-height="1163"/></a><figcaption>Hobbiton with the New Mill</figcaption></figure>

Shortly after Lotho moved into Bag End, the Mill was knocked down and replaced by a New Mill, operated by Ted Sandyman and several [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md). It was described as "a great brick building straddling the stream, which it fouled with a steaming and stinking overflow." When [Sharkey](../S/Saruman.md) came to [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), all grinding stopped. After Sharkey was driven out, this new brick building was demolished.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the original version of [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md)'s letter to Bilbo the rendezvous point for the beginning of the journey to the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) was the Great Mill.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> This was only changed to the Green Dragon in the page proofs just before the story was published.<sup id="cite_ref-TrollNotes_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TrollNotes-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The [Sarehole Mill](../S/Sarehole_Mill.md) and its millers formed the inspiration for the Old Mill.<sup id="cite_ref-TrollNotes_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TrollNotes-7">[7]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The Mill is a prominent landmark in the Shire, directly south of Bag End. <a href="../T/Ted_Sandyman.md" title="Ted Sandyman">Ted Sandyman</a> can be found outside, but to date he only has lines during some of the seasonal festivals and plays no role in the main story.</dd></dl>
