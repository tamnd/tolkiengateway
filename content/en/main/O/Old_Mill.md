The **Old Mill** was the water-mill of [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), owned and run by the [Sandyman Family](/wiki/Sandyman_Family).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The Old Mill was a corn mill powered by a large water wheel. It lay directly adjacent to the bridge across [the Water](/wiki/The_Water "The Water") and next to the [Old Grange](/wiki/Old_Grange) as well.

## History {#History}

In [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1341](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) ran by the Mill and a mile beyond to get to the [Green Dragon](/wiki/Green_Dragon) to meet [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> When he and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) returned to [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), they crossed the bridge and passed the Mill.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> By [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1418](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") [Sandyman](/wiki/Sandyman) had been the miller for some time and passed down his craft to his son, [Ted](/wiki/Ted_Sandyman "Ted Sandyman"). [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins) bought the mill, and many other buildings in [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), before he moved in to [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).

Shortly after Lotho moved into Bag End, the Mill was knocked down and replaced by a New Mill, operated by Ted Sandyman and several [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians). It was described as "a great brick building straddling the stream, which it fouled with a steaming and stinking overflow." When [Sharkey](/wiki/Saruman "Saruman") came to [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), all grinding stopped. After Sharkey was driven out, this new brick building was demolished.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the original version of [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin)'s letter to Bilbo the rendezvous point for the beginning of the journey to the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) was the Great Mill.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> This was only changed to the Green Dragon in the page proofs just before the story was published.<sup id="cite_ref-TrollNotes_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TrollNotes-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The [Sarehole Mill](/wiki/Sarehole_Mill) and its millers formed the inspiration for the Old Mill.<sup id="cite_ref-TrollNotes_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TrollNotes-7">[7]</a></sup>
