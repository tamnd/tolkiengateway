<i><b>One Ring to Bind Them All: Tolkien&#39;s Mythology</b></i> is a study of the folkloristic and mythic structure of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, applying the theories of scholars Joseph Campbell, Vladimir Propp, and Claude Lévi-Strauss. The book was based on [Anne C. Petty](/wiki/Anne_C._Petty)'s Ph.D. thesis: *The Creative Mythology of J.R.R. Tolkien: A Study of the Mythic Impulse* (Florida State University, 1972; iii, 144 leaves).
