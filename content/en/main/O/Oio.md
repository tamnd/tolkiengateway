<i><b>oio</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word, meaning "ever".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[oiale](/wiki/Oiale "Oiale")* - "everlastingly"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- *[Oiolossë](/wiki/Oioloss%C3%AB)* - "Ever-snow-white" (another name for *[Taniquetil](/wiki/Taniquetil)*)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- *[Oiolairë](/wiki/Oiolair%C3%AB)* - "Ever-summer<i><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></i>
- *[Oiomúrë](/wiki/Oiom%C3%BAr%C3%AB)*<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
