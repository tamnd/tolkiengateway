**Oronzo Cilli** is an Italian independent Tolkien scholar and researcher. He translated the second Italian edition of [The Annotated Hobbit](/wiki/The_Annotated_Hobbit) (Lo Hobbit annotato) in [2004](/wiki/2004).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He gained international notoriety after publishing [J.R.R. Tolkien the Esperantist: Before the Arrival of Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_the_Esperantist:_Before_the_Arrival_of_Bilbo_Baggins) in [2015](/wiki/2015).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> His [2019](/wiki/2019) book [Tolkien's Library: An Annotated Checklist](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Library:_An_Annotated_Checklist) won the [Best Book Award](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society_Awards "The Tolkien Society Awards") from [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) in [2020](/wiki/2020)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and was a finalist for the [Mythopoeic Scholarship Award in Inklings Studies](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society "Mythopoeic Society") in [2021](/wiki/2021).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In [2023](/wiki/2023) he curated the exhibition [Tolkien: Uomo, Professore, Autore](/wiki/TOLKIEN_-_Uomo,_Professore,_Autore "TOLKIEN - Uomo, Professore, Autore") (*"Tolkien: Man, Professor, Author"*).
