The **Old Ford** was the point where the [Old Forest Road](/wiki/Old_Forest_Road) crossed the River [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), about forty miles downriver from the [Carrock](/wiki/Carrock).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In older times, the river was crossed by a stone bridge here, but by the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the bridge had long since disappeared, and the crossing was no more than a simple ford.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2019: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Old Ford is located close to the remains of <i>Hadhodiant</i>, a stone bridge built by the <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a> which merely stands broken rather than being completely gone. On the Western bank is Vegbár, a toll-collecting station of the <a href="/wiki/Beornings" title="Beornings">Beornings</a>, making the Old Ford one and the same with the <a href="/wiki/Ford_of_Carrock" title="Ford of Carrock">Ford of Carrock</a>. Towards the end of the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wargs" title="Wargs">Wargs</a> came down from the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a> but were defeated in the Battle of the Old Ford by the Beornings and the <a href="/wiki/Woodmen" title="Woodmen">Woodmen</a>, who also suffered many casualties. <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a>, <a href="/wiki/Arwen" title="Arwen">Arwen</a> and their household also travelled by the Old Ford on their way to <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> for Arwen&#39;s wedding. Later in the summer of <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3019" title="Third Age 3019">T.A. 3019</a> Prince <a href="/wiki/Durin_VII" title="Durin VII">Durin VII</a> led a campaign to reclaim <a href="/wiki/Gundabad" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a>, and a mission shows dwarven builders construct a wooden replacement for the missing section of the <i>Hadhodiant</i> so as to not be reliant on the Old Ford.</dd></dl>
