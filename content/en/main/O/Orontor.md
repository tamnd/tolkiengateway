**Orontor** was a [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) [man](../M/Men.md) who was the father of [Fíriel](<../F/Fíriel_(The_Lost_Road).md>) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Lost Road](../T/The_Lost_Road.md)*.

## History {#History}

Orontor was a good friend of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md), but at some point in time, he had departed [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). Men speculated that he had gained the displeasure of the king and had fled on a long voyage. However, the truth was that he was on a mysterious mission and would likely never be seen again or be seen again too late.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He left behind his daughter, Fíriel, in Elendil's house where she sang a [sad song](../F/Fíriel's_song.md) from a high window.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of *Oronto* is never given, but it could include the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) element *[oron](Oron.md)* ("mountain"). The name was created in an early step of Quenya development, but it was later used as an example of *anessi* ("given name").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) points out that Orontor's mission, "from which he might never return", could be a precedent of the voyage of [Amandil](../A/Amandil.md) to the West in the final version of *[Akallabêth](../A/Akallabêth.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
