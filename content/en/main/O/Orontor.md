**Orontor** was a [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") [man](/wiki/Men "Men") who was the father of [Fíriel](</wiki/F%C3%ADriel_(The_Lost_Road)> "Fíriel (The Lost Road)") according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)*.

## History {#History}

Orontor was a good friend of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), but at some point in time, he had departed [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). Men speculated that he had gained the displeasure of the king and had fled on a long voyage. However, the truth was that he was on a mysterious mission and would likely never be seen again or be seen again too late.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He left behind his daughter, Fíriel, in Elendil's house where she sang a [sad song](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel%27s_song "Fíriel's song") from a high window.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of *Oronto* is never given, but it could include the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) element *[oron](/wiki/Oron "Oron")* ("mountain"). The name was created in an early step of Quenya development, but it was later used as an example of *anessi* ("given name").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) points out that Orontor's mission, "from which he might never return", could be a precedent of the voyage of [Amandil](/wiki/Amandil) to the West in the final version of *[Akallabêth](/wiki/Akallab%C3%AAth)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
