**Oxford** is the city in which [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) spent a great deal of his life living in. Both *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* were written in North Oxford.

The [Tolkien family](../T/Tolkien_family~43191.md) resided in [20 Northmoor Road](../_/20_Northmoor_Road.md) and in [March](../M/March.md) [1947](../_/1947.md) they moved to [3 Manor Road](../_/3_Manor_Road.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Later legendarium {#Later_legendarium}

Tolkien signed as *[Arcastar](../A/Arcastar.md) Mondósaresse* ("Tolkien in Oxford") in a 1968 letter to the producer of the BBC documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](../T/Tolkien_in_Oxford.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Therefore, <i><b>Mondósar</b></i> is the proper [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name for "Oxford", probably a compound of *[mundo](../M/Mundo.md)* ("bull, ox") and [root](../R/Root.md) THAR.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the abandoned *[The Notion Club Papers](../T/The_Notion_Club_Papers.md)*, the [Notion Club](../N/Notion_Club.md) and its members are based in Oxford.

According to a map of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) annotated by Tolkien, [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md) was at approximately the same latitude as Oxford.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
