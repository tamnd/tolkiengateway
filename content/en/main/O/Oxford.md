**Oxford** is the city in which [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) spent a great deal of his life living in. Both *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* were written in North Oxford.

The [Tolkien family](/wiki/Tolkien_family) resided in [20 Northmoor Road](/wiki/20_Northmoor_Road) and in [March](/wiki/March) [1947](/wiki/1947) they moved to [3 Manor Road](/wiki/3_Manor_Road).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Later legendarium {#Later_legendarium}

Tolkien signed as *[Arcastar](/wiki/Arcastar) Mondósaresse* ("Tolkien in Oxford") in a 1968 letter to the producer of the BBC documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](/wiki/Tolkien_in_Oxford)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Therefore, <i><b>Mondósar</b></i> is the proper [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for "Oxford", probably a compound of *[mundo](/wiki/Mundo "Mundo")* ("bull, ox") and [root](/wiki/Root "Root") THAR.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the abandoned *[The Notion Club Papers](/wiki/The_Notion_Club_Papers)*, the [Notion Club](/wiki/Notion_Club) and its members are based in Oxford.

According to a map of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) annotated by Tolkien, [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) was at approximately the same latitude as Oxford.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
