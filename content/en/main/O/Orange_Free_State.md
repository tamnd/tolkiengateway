**Orange Free State** (Dutch: *Oranje-Vrijstaat*) was an independent <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boer" title="wikipedia:Boer">Boer</a> republic in southern Africa where [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) was born.

Originally founded as *Transorangia* (*Transoranje*) on 17 February 1854 by the *Voortrekkers* (Dutch for "pioneers"), former Dutch colonists of the Cape Colony. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) was born there in [1892](/wiki/1892) as his father, [Arthur Tolkien](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien), headed the [Bloemfontein](/wiki/Bloemfontein) branch of a British bank, but returned to England with his mother at the age of three.

During 1899 and 1902 the Orange Free Country and the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_Republic" title="wikipedia:South African Republic">South African Republic</a>, a Boer Republic in the current South-African provinces <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauteng" title="wikipedia:Gauteng">Gauteng</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limpopo" title="wikipedia:Limpopo">Limpopo</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mpumalanga" title="wikipedia:Mpumalanga">Mpumalanga</a>, fought the Second Boer War with the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Empire" title="wikipedia:British Empire">British Empire</a>. In the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Vereeniging" title="wikipedia:Treaty of Vereeniging">Treaty of Vereeniging</a> it was decided that Orange Free Country would become part of the British Empire as the **Orange River Colony**. In 1910 it joined the Union of South-Africa under its former name, Orange Free Country.

In [1944](/wiki/1944), [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) spent time in Free State Province, training for the Royal Air Force. During this stay he [corresponded](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") extensively with his father about the elder Tolkien's reminiscences of South Africa, as well as newly written chapters of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

Today, the area of the former Orange Free State is now the <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_State" title="wikipedia:Free State">Free State</a></b> (Afrikaans: *Vrystaat*) province of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_South_Africa" title="wikipedia:Republic of South Africa">South Africa</a>.
