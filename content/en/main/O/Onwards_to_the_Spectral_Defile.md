<i><b>Onwards to the Spectral Defile</b></i> was [Cirith Gorgor](</wiki/Cirith_Gorgor_(band)> "Cirith Gorgor (band)")'s first full length album. It was released in [1999](/wiki/1999) on Osmose Records, produced by Cirith Gorgor and E. Hermsen, and recorded at BBA.

## Track listing {#Track_listing}

1. "The Declaration of Our Neverending War"
2. "Winter Embraces Lands Beyond"
3. "Through Burning Wastelands"
4. "Sons of the New Dawn"
5. "A Hymn to the Children of Heimdall"
6. "Darkness Returns"
7. "Wandering Cirith Gorgor"
8. "Ephel Dúath (a Warrior's Tale)"
9. "Shadows over Isengard"
10. "Thorns of Oblivion"
