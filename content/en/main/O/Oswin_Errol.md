**Oswin Errol** was the father of [Alboin Errol](/wiki/Alboin_Errol) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)*.<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Throughout his early life, Oswin was very particular about his name, being annoyed whenever he was mistakenly called **Oswald**, which happened often.<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup>

In his later years, Oswin lived within a "little house in Cornwall" near the western sea with his son, Alboin. One evening, Oswin found his son missing close to his bed-time. Oswin called for him many times to no avail. Upon realizing that his son was not in the play-room of the house, Oswin, searched the small high garden in the back of the house. while discovered his son gazing at the sea while lying atop a low stone wall screened from the the house by a hedge.<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Oswin* ([Old English](/wiki/Old_English): <a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/%C3%93swine_(disambiguation)" title="Óswine (disambiguation)">Óswine</a>) contains the elements *os* ("god") and *wine* ("friend").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Oswin's annoying name *Oswald* ([Old English](/wiki/Old_English)) contains the elements *os* ("god") and *weald* ("power, ruler").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Tolkien eventually abandoned his work on *[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)*, yet Tolkien would reuse the name of "Oswin" for **[Oswin Lowdham](/wiki/Oswin_Lowdham)**, the grandfather of [Alwin Arundel Lowdham](/wiki/Alwin_Arundel_Lowdham) within "[The Notion Club Papers](/wiki/The_Notion_Club_Papers)".
