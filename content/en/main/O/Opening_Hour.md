The **Opening Hour** was the first hour of the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor), during which [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion) began to bloom for the first time before [Laurelin](/wiki/Laurelin) shone. The Opening Hour was not counted in the tale of hours of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), but was the time from which their reckoning, the [Years of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees), [began](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1 "Year of the Trees 1").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
