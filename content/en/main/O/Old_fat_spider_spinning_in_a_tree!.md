"**Old fat spider spinning in a tree!**" is the first line of a poem written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) that was published in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* in [1937](/wiki/1937).<sup id="cite_ref-hobbit_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbit-1">[1]</a></sup>

## First stanza {#First_stanza}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Old fat spider spinning in a tree!<br/>
Old fat spider can’t see me!<br/>
     Attercop! Attercop!<br/>
       Won&#39;t you stop,<br/>
Stop your spinning and look for me?<sup id="cite_ref-hobbit_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbit-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</div>

## History {#History}

[Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) sang this poem to distract the [spiders](/wiki/Spiders "Spiders") while attempting to save [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) and his [companions](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") in [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood).<sup id="cite_ref-hobbit_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbit-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Background {#Background}

Tolkien first wrote the poem on some plot notes before he subsequently made minor changes to it.<sup id="cite_ref-history_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-2">[2]</a></sup>
