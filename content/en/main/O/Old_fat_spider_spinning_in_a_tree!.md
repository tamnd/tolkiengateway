"**Old fat spider spinning in a tree!**" is the first line of a poem written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) that was published in *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* in [1937](../_/1937.md).<sup id="cite_ref-hobbit_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbit-1">[1]</a></sup>

## First stanza {#First_stanza}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Old fat spider spinning in a tree!<br/>
Old fat spider can’t see me!<br/>
     Attercop! Attercop!<br/>
       Won&#39;t you stop,<br/>
Stop your spinning and look for me?<sup id="cite_ref-hobbit_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbit-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</div>

## History {#History}

[Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) sang this poem to distract the [spiders](../S/Spiders.md) while attempting to save [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) and his [companions](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) in [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md).<sup id="cite_ref-hobbit_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbit-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Background {#Background}

Tolkien first wrote the poem on some plot notes before he subsequently made minor changes to it.<sup id="cite_ref-history_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-2">[2]</a></sup>
