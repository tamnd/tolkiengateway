<i><b>ost</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word, meaning "fort, citadel".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Angrenost](/wiki/Isengard "Isengard")* = "Iron Fortress"
- *[Belegost](/wiki/Belegost)* = "Mighty Fortress", [Mickleburg](/wiki/Mickleburg)
- *[Carchost](/wiki/Carchost)*
- *[Narchost](/wiki/Narchost)*
- *[Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath#Etymology)*
- *[Fornost](/wiki/Fornost)* = "Northern Fortress"
