**Oxonmoot 2026**, the 53rd annual [Oxonmoot](Oxonmoot.md) hosted by [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) celebrating the life and works of J.R.R. Tolkien. The event is planned to take place from the afternoon of Thursday [3](../_/3_September.md) until the afternoon of Sunday [6 September](../_/6_September.md) [2026](../_/2026.md) at St Ann's College, [Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md). As in the previous years, it is a hybrid event both in-person and online participation.

The Oxonmoot 2026 t-shirt was designed by [Miriam Ellis](../M/Miriam_Ellis.md). The design incorporates her portraits of Frodo, Sam, Merry, and Pippin, arranged within the four “O” letters of the Oxonmoot logo, with each hobbit’s initials included at the base of the portrait frames.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
