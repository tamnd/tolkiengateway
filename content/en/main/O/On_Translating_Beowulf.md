<i><b>Prefatory Remarks on Prose Translation of &#39;Beowulf&#39;</b></i> is a preface written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) for <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=John_R._Clark_Hall&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="John R. Clark Hall (page does not exist)">John R. Clark Hall</a>'s book *[Beowulf and the Finnsburg Fragment](../B/Beowulf_and_the_Finnsburg_Fragment.md)*, published in [1940](../_/1940.md). It was reprinted as "**On Translating Beowulf**" in *[The Monsters and the Critics and Other Essays](../T/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics_and_Other_Essays.md)* (1983).

In the section "On Metre", Tolkien quotes 19 lines from his own alliterative translation of <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../B/Beowulf.md" title="Beowulf">Beowulf</a></i> (<i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Tale_of_Beewolf_Son_of_Echgethew&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Tale of Beewolf Son of Echgethew (page does not exist)">The Tale of Beewolf Son of Echgethew</a></i>) to explain the metre of the poem. More portions from this work were published in [2024](../_/2024.md) as no. 88 of *[The Collected Poems of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Collected_Poems_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*.
