<i><b>Prefatory Remarks on Prose Translation of &#39;Beowulf&#39;</b></i> is a preface written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) for <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=John_R._Clark_Hall&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="John R. Clark Hall (page does not exist)">John R. Clark Hall</a>'s book *[Beowulf and the Finnsburg Fragment](/wiki/Beowulf_and_the_Finnsburg_Fragment)*, published in [1940](/wiki/1940). It was reprinted as "**On Translating Beowulf**" in *[The Monsters and the Critics and Other Essays](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics_and_Other_Essays)* (1983).

In the section "On Metre", Tolkien quotes 19 lines from his own alliterative translation of <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Beowulf" title="Beowulf">Beowulf</a></i> (<i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Tale_of_Beewolf_Son_of_Echgethew&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Tale of Beewolf Son of Echgethew (page does not exist)">The Tale of Beewolf Son of Echgethew</a></i>) to explain the metre of the poem. More portions from this work were published in [2024](/wiki/2024) as no. 88 of *[The Collected Poems of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Collected_Poems_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)*.
