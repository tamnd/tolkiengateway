<i><b>Over Old Hills</b></i> is the eighth and final track of the album [Dol Guldur](</wiki/Dol_Guldur_(album)> "Dol Guldur (album)") by the band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning). The name [Cottage of Lost Play](/wiki/Cottage_of_Lost_Play) is mentioned several times, and it is assumed that the story of the song takes place in [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa).
