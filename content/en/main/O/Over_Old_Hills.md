<i><b>Over Old Hills</b></i> is the eighth and final track of the album [Dol Guldur](<../D/Dol_Guldur_(album).md>) by the band [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md). The name [Cottage of Lost Play](../C/Cottage_of_Lost_Play.md) is mentioned several times, and it is assumed that the story of the song takes place in [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md).
