<i><b>orne</b></i> is a noun meaning "[tree](../T/Trees.md)" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), especially a tall, slender tree such as an [ash](../A/Ashes.md) or [birch](../B/Birches.md). Compare with *[orn](Orn.md)* in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) has suggested that the form *ornë* (retaining the same meaning) could be valid [Neo-Quenya](../N/Neo-Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [ŌR/RO](OR~34968.md)<sup id="cite_ref-L347_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Ornendil](Ornendil.md)* = "Friend of the Trees"

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): *[orn](Orn.md)*
