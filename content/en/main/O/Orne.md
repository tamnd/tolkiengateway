<i><b>orne</b></i> is a noun meaning "[tree](/wiki/Trees "Trees")" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), especially a tall, slender tree such as an [ash](/wiki/Ashes "Ashes") or [birch](/wiki/Birches "Birches"). Compare with *[orn](/wiki/Orn "Orn")* in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested that the form *ornë* (retaining the same meaning) could be valid [Neo-Quenya](/wiki/Neo-Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [ŌR/RO](/wiki/OR "OR")<sup id="cite_ref-L347_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Ornendil](/wiki/Ornendil)* = "Friend of the Trees"

## Cognates {#Cognates}
