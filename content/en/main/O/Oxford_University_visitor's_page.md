**Oxford University visitor's page** refers to a sheet (8" x 9.5") removed from a visitor's book at the [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford), stamped [20 October](/wiki/20_October) [1911](/wiki/1911). The page features signatures of Oxford University undergraduates, and the names of their respective colleges. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) added his signature about three quarters of the way down the page, and wrote his college, "[Exeter](/wiki/University_of_Oxford#Exeter_College "University of Oxford")" beside his signature.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
