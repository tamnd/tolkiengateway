**Outsiders** was a term used by [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits) to describe all the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) who lived outside [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), including [Bree-hobbits](/wiki/Bree-hobbits). Shire-folk considered all Outsiders dull and uncouth, taking little interest in them.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

Outsiders were scattered across the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands) and significantly more numerous than the people of the Shire realized. Some of Outsiders were wanderers, who didn't settle for long periods of time in any particular place, instead digging holes in banks and staying there only as long as it suited them, while other Outsiders, like the inhabitants of [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land), were as settled, prosperous and civilized as their distant relatives living in the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>.

## Other meanings of the term {#Other_meanings_of_the_term}

In a more general sense the term Outsiders was used by Shire-hobbits to describe all people living outside the Shire. [Bounders](/wiki/Bounders) were guarding the borders of the Shire to prevent nuisance from Outsiders of any kind, great or small<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>.

On the other hand, [Bree-landers](/wiki/Bree-landers) used the term Outsiders to describe all people living outside Bree-land, including hobbits of the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier drafts of the "Lord of the Rings" Tolkien used the term "wild hobbits" to describe Hobbits who lived lonely here and there in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) (outside of the Shire and Bree) and in the eaves of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> "<a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Rangers_(disambiguation)" title="Rangers (disambiguation)">Rangers</a>" was another term synonymous with "wild hobbits".<sup id="cite_ref-:1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-6">[6]</a></sup> In some versions [Trotter](/wiki/Trotter) was considered one of these wild hobbits<sup id="cite_ref-:1_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-6">[6]</a></sup>.
