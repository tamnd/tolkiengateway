## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>orodorim</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "mountain-chain". It is only attested in the compound name <i><a href="/wiki/Thangorodrim" title="Thangorodrim">Thang<b>orodrim</b></a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The word *orodrim* ("range of mountains") is listed by [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) in his [Neo-Sindarin](/wiki/Neo-Elvish "Neo-Elvish") glossary.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

*orodrim* likely consists of *[orod](/wiki/Ered "Ered")* meaning "mountain", and the suffix *-rim* which is a collective plural meaning "a great number, a host".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

- *[emyn](/wiki/Amon "Amon")*, "hills"
- *[ephel](/wiki/Ephel "Ephel")*, "encircling fence"
- Root [OR](/wiki/OR)

## Noldorin {#Noldorin}

<i><b>orodrim</b></i> is the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word for "mountain range".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")*, Noldorin *orodrim* derives from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [ÓROT](/wiki/OR#Other_versions "OR") ("height, mountain").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
