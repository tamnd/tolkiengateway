## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>orodorim</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning "mountain-chain". It is only attested in the compound name <i><a href="../T/Thangorodrim.md" title="Thangorodrim">Thang<b>orodrim</b></a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The word *orodrim* ("range of mountains") is listed by [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) in his [Neo-Sindarin](../N/Neo-Elvish.md) glossary.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

*orodrim* likely consists of *[orod](../E/Ered.md)* meaning "mountain", and the suffix *-rim* which is a collective plural meaning "a great number, a host".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

### See also {#See_also}

- *[ered](../E/Ered.md)*, plural of *orod*
- *[emyn](../A/Amon.md)*, "hills"
- *[ephel](../E/Ephel.md)*, "encircling fence"
- Root [OR](OR~34968.md)

## Noldorin {#Noldorin}

<i><b>orodrim</b></i> is the [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) word for "mountain range".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

In the *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)*, Noldorin *orodrim* derives from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [ÓROT](OR~34968.md#Other_versions) ("height, mountain").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
