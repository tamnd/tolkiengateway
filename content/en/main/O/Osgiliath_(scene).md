**Osgiliath** is the forty-fourth of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and the fifty-fifth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) and the [Rangers of Ithilien](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien) lead [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") to [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath). They see smoke rising from the city, signifying it is under attack by the forces of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring" title="The One Ring">The Ring</a> will not save <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>. It has only the power to destroy. Please... let me go.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Frodo to Faramir</cite></p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

Faramir takes his time to consider what Frodo said before deciding to continue forwards to Osgiliath. The scene ends with Frodo pleading with him to be let go.

## Differences {#Differences}

In the book, [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) did not take [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") to [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath). When he discovered Frodo was carrying [the Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), he told the two [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) he would not try to take it,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and parted with them at [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn), where they went towards the [Morgul Vale](/wiki/Morgul_Vale).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
