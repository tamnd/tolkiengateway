**Orossi** were the [fays](/wiki/Fays "Fays") of the mountains, according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

They arrived to the world with [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) and [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna), along with many other types of fays.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Orossi* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), probably being derived from the root [ORO](/wiki/ORO) ("steepness, rising"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> according to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
