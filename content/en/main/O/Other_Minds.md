<i><b>Other Minds</b></i> is an unofficial, non-profit magazine devoted to role-playing games set in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s imaginary world. The magazine was initiated with the demise of *[Other Hands](/wiki/Other_Hands)*.

*Other Minds* is published in digital format and is edited by [Thomas Morwinsky](/wiki/Thomas_Morwinsky) and [Hawke Robinson](/wiki/Hawke_Robinson).
