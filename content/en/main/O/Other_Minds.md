<i><b>Other Minds</b></i> is an unofficial, non-profit magazine devoted to role-playing games set in [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s imaginary world. The magazine was initiated with the demise of *[Other Hands](Other_Hands.md)*.

*Other Minds* is published in digital format and is edited by [Thomas Morwinsky](../T/Thomas_Morwinsky.md) and [Hawke Robinson](../H/Hawke_Robinson.md).
