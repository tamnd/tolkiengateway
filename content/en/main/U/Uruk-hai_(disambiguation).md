**Uruk-hai** is a [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech) term meaning "*Orc-folk*"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> (sing. **Uruk**, "*Orc"*)<sup id="cite_ref-LotR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LotR-2">[2]</a></sup>. It may refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Uruk-hai/Uruks](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs"), *sensu lato*, the term for the race of Orcs in general,<sup id="cite_ref-LotR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LotR-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> analogous to an English word *Humanity*.
- [Uruk-hai/Uruks](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai"), *sensu stricto*, the term for the special breed of Orcs of great size and strength from [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).<sup id="cite_ref-LotR_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LotR-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Books {#Books}

- *[The Uruk-hai](/wiki/The_Uruk-hai)*, the third chapter of *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*.

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- *[The Uruk-hai (scene)](</wiki/The_Uruk-hai_(scene)>)*, the third scene of the [2002](/wiki/2002) film, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*.
- *[The Uruk-Hai](/wiki/The_Uruk-Hai)*, the fifth track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)*.
