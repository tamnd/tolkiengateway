**Undead** were beings who once had been alive (often [Men](/wiki/Men)) but which had been bestowed with an unnaturally prolonged existence, sometimes by work of necromancy. All such creatures seem to have been associated with Evil.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Undead is a class of monsters, which includes Ghosts, Skeletons, Ghouls, and many others.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007-: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a class="external text" href="http://lorebook.lotro.com/wiki/Category:The_Dead" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Dead</a> is a category of creatures, which includes wights, spirits, skeletons, and many others. A related category is <a class="external text" href="http://lorebook.lotro.com/wiki/Category:Unseen" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Unseen</a>, which includes the Nazgûl and related shadowy figures. One case of a non-Evil undead exists in the ghost of <a href="/wiki/Arvedui" title="Arvedui">Arvedui</a> in <a href="/wiki/Forochel" title="Forochel">Forochel</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>
