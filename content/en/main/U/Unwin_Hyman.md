**Unwin Hyman Ltd.** was a British publishing company known for publishing the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

Unwin Hyman Ltd. was formed in 1986, as a result of the amalgamation of publishers [Allen &amp; Unwin](../A/Allen_&_Unwin.md) and Bell &amp; Hyman. Robin Hyman became chief executive of the company upon its formation, with Allen &amp; Unwin continuing as its Australian-based subsidiary, until the firm was acquired by [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md) in 1990.

HarperCollins has since sold Unwin Hyman's academic book list to Routledge. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
