**Unlight**, also known as the **Cloud of Ungoliant**, was a creation of [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant). It is described as a vast darkness that no light can escape or penetrate. As Ungoliant devoured the [Light of the Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor"), she brought forth the Unlight, and "it seemed not lack, but a thing with being of its own, that made by malice out of Light had the power to pierce the eye, to enter the heart and mind and strangle the very will".<sup id="cite_ref-MR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:289</sup>

## History {#History}

Ungoliant wove this darkness into webs, which covered her lair in [Avathar](/wiki/Avathar). She wove it as a cloak to conceal her and Melkor from the eyes of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) during the [Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-MR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:284</sup> Even [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas), the strongest of the Valar, was unable to break through it when he pursued [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") and [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) from [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> When Ungoliant was denied her full reward from Melkor, she ensnared him in her Unlight. However, the webs were torn asunder by the fire of the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs)' whips, when they came to their masters' aid.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In Valinor, the winds of Manwë rolled back the shadows of Ungoliant to the [sea](/wiki/Sea "Sea").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Though [Ungoliant's descendants](/wiki/Spiders "Spiders") were incapable of producing Unlight, the webs they wove proved formidable, as they were strong enough to entrap even the mightiest warrior, and dark enough to prevent light from entering their lairs.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the cloak that Gwerlum/Gloomweaver creates is one of invisibility rather than darkness.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Later, the webs left in Valinor after the theft were used by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) to cover the [Shadowy Seas](/wiki/Shadowy_Seas) during the Hiding of Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
