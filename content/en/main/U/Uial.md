<i><b>uial</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (and [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)) word meaning "[twilight](/wiki/Twilight "Twilight")".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymoloy {#Etymoloy}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), Noldorin *uial* is mentioned under [roots](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [KAL](/wiki/KAL) and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Y%C5%AA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="YŪ (page does not exist)">YŪ</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Aelin-uial" title="Aelin-uial">Aelin-<b>uial</b></a></i> = "Meres of Twilight"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Emyn_Uial" title="Emyn Uial">Emyn <b>Uial</b></a></i> = "Hills of Evendim"
- <i>Glad<b>uial</b></i> = "Twilight Wood" (other name of [Nan Elmoth](/wiki/Nan_Elmoth))
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Nenuial" title="Nenuial">Nen<b>uial</b></a></i> = "Lake Evendim"

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *úyale*
