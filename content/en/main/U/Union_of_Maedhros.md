The **Union of Maedhros** was a short-lived alliance formed by [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) after the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md). It sought to unite the [Fëanorians](../F/Fëanorians.md) with the other peoples of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), including the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md), the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) and the newly arrived [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md). Due to tensions with the [sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md), [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) and [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) only sent small forces. The alliance was defeated at the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), the Battle of Unnumbered Tears.<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Structure {#Structure}

The Union of Maedhros was divided into two armies in the east and west, led by Maedhros and [High King](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) respectively.

### Eastern army {#Eastern_army}

- Elves of [Himring](../H/Himring.md) ([Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md))
- [Easterlings](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>) under the service of Maedhros ([Bór](../B/Bór.md))
- Easterlings under the service of [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md) ([Ulfang](Ulfang.md))
- [Dwarves of Belegost](../D/Dwarves_of_Belegost.md) ([Azaghâl](../A/Azaghâl.md))

### Western army {#Western_army}

- Noldor of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) ([Fingon](../F/Fingon.md))
- [Men of Dor-lómin](../M/Men_of_Dor-lómin.md) ([Húrin](../H/Húrin.md))
- [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md) ([Haldir](<../H/Haldir_(Chieftain_of_the_Haladin).md>))
- [Elves](../E/Elves_of_Nargothrond.md) of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) ([Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md))
- Some [Falathrim](../F/Falathrim.md)
- [Mablung](../M/Mablung.md) and [Beleg](../B/Beleg.md) from [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md)<sup id="cite_ref-GA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) had lost control over northern [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), and were mostly reduced to enclaves in [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) and [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md), including the hidden [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> When Maedhros heard of the heroic deeds of [Beren](../B/Beren.md) and [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) new hope came to him. Maedhros decided to unite the peoples of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) to assail [Angband](../A/Angband.md).

Elves, Men and Dwarves joined the alliance. The [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) supplied both armed forces and forged weapons. Even to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) came word of the Union and [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) the [Hidden King](../H/Hidden_King.md) secretly made his own preparations. Other Elves, like [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md), refused to join because of the evils of [Celegorm](../C/Celegorm.md) and [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md), while [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) had sworn never to fight alongside the Feanorians; only a few representatives came from Doriath and Nargothrond.

Maedhros' plans were to lead his army (with some assistance from the western army) to [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md) in order to draw out the armies of Morgoth, and then flank with the hidden forces of Fingon that would emerge from the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md).

Initially, the forces managed to clean of evil creatures [West Beleriand](../W/West_Beleriand.md) and [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md), and the final assault was planned for [Midsummer](../M/Midsummer.md). However, Morgoth had time to prepare his forces as Maedhros revealed himself too early, and then [Uldor](Uldor.md), who was an agent of the Enemy, delayed Maedhros' forces by giving false reports of enemy presence. A small force of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) managed to provoke the western army prematurely, instigated by [Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md). As a result, the armies of the Union were themselves outflanked in the ensuing battle, which, despite Turgon's timely and unexpected arrival, would prove disastrous, and would be known as the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-1">[1]</a></sup>
