The **Union of Maedhros** was a short-lived alliance formed by [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) after the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach). It sought to unite the [Fëanorians](/wiki/F%C3%ABanorians) with the other peoples of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), including the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum), the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) and the newly arrived [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings). Due to tensions with the [sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor"), [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) and [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) only sent small forces. The alliance was defeated at the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), the Battle of Unnumbered Tears.<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Structure {#Structure}

The Union of Maedhros was divided into two armies in the east and west, led by Maedhros and [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor "High King of the Noldor") [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) respectively.

### Eastern army {#Eastern_army}

- Elves of [Himring](/wiki/Himring) ([Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros))
- [Easterlings](</wiki/Easterlings_(First_Age)> "Easterlings (First Age)") under the service of Maedhros ([Bór](/wiki/B%C3%B3r))
- Easterlings under the service of [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir) ([Ulfang](/wiki/Ulfang))
- [Dwarves of Belegost](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Belegost) ([Azaghâl](/wiki/Azagh%C3%A2l))

### Western army {#Western_army}

- Noldor of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) ([Fingon](/wiki/Fingon))
- [Men of Dor-lómin](/wiki/Men_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min) ([Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin))
- [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) ([Haldir](</wiki/Haldir_(Chieftain_of_the_Haladin)> "Haldir (Chieftain of the Haladin)"))
- [Elves](/wiki/Elves_of_Nargothrond "Elves of Nargothrond") of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) ([Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor))
- Some [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim)
- [Mablung](/wiki/Mablung) and [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) from [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath)<sup id="cite_ref-GA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) had lost control over northern [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), and were mostly reduced to enclaves in [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) and [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), including the hidden [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> When Maedhros heard of the heroic deeds of [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) new hope came to him. Maedhros decided to unite the peoples of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) to assail [Angband](/wiki/Angband).

Elves, Men and Dwarves joined the alliance. The [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) supplied both armed forces and forged weapons. Even to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) came word of the Union and [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) the [Hidden King](/wiki/Hidden_King) secretly made his own preparations. Other Elves, like [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth), refused to join because of the evils of [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin), while [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) had sworn never to fight alongside the Feanorians; only a few representatives came from Doriath and Nargothrond.

Maedhros' plans were to lead his army (with some assistance from the western army) to [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith) in order to draw out the armies of Morgoth, and then flank with the hidden forces of Fingon that would emerge from the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin).

Initially, the forces managed to clean of evil creatures [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand) and [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), and the final assault was planned for [Midsummer](/wiki/Midsummer). However, Morgoth had time to prepare his forces as Maedhros revealed himself too early, and then [Uldor](/wiki/Uldor), who was an agent of the Enemy, delayed Maedhros' forces by giving false reports of enemy presence. A small force of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) managed to provoke the western army prematurely, instigated by [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor). As a result, the armies of the Union were themselves outflanked in the ensuing battle, which, despite Turgon's timely and unexpected arrival, would prove disastrous, and would be known as the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad).<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-1">[1]</a></sup>
