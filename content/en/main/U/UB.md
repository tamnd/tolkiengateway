**UB** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../R/Root.md) with the sense "consider; have in mind; ponder".<sup id="cite_ref-VT48_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT48-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is the origin of the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) future suffix *-uva*.<sup id="cite_ref-VT48_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT48-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- Quenya: <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=%C3%9Avie&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Úvie (page does not exist)">úvie</a></i> ("considering a matter (with a view to decision)")<sup id="cite_ref-VT48_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT48-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Old Noldorin](../K/Kornoldorin.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=%C5%AAba-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ūba- (page does not exist)">ūba-</a></i> ("to brood on, ponder")<sup id="cite_ref-VT48_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT48-1">[1]</a></sup>
