**Ulmonan** was the name of [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s halls, located at the [bottom](/wiki/Hidden_Half "Hidden Half") of the [Outer Ocean](/wiki/Vaiya "Vaiya"), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ulmonan* is apparently a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) name; the first element is of course *[Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo#Etymology)*, while the second element was, according to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), never explained by [his father](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
