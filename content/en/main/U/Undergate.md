The **Undergate** (also called the under-gate) was a gate with brazen doors that blocked the entrance from the underground side passage that was called the [Under-way](/wiki/Under-way) to the ground floor of the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Choices_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Choices-2">[2]</a></sup>

When [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Sam) first encountered the Under-way they were in darkness and only perceived that the way was blocked. After Frodo had been stung by [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> orcs found Frodo and carried the unconscious Frodo through the Under-way and through the Undergate to the Tower of Cirith Ungol. Sam followed the orcs on the Under-way, but was unable to get through the locked Undergate and was forced to go back to the main tunnel in [Shelob's Lair](/wiki/Shelob%27s_Lair).<sup id="cite_ref-Choices_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Choices-2">[2]</a></sup>
