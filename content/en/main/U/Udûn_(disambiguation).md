**Udûn**, the **Depth**, is a concept usually associated with Hell. It may refer to:

- [Udûn (stronghold)](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(stronghold)>), [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "[Utumno](/wiki/Utumno)", [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s stronghold before the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age); Hell.
    - [Flame of Udûn](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane "Durin's Bane"), the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") of [Moria](/wiki/Moria) destroyed by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf).
- [Udûn (valley)](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(valley)>), a valley in northern [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).

- [Udûn (episode)](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(episode)>), the sixth episode of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*.
