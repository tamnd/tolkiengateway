**Ulmo's warning** was a message given by the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) to [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), who was in turn to deliver it to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon), [King of Gondolin](/wiki/King_of_Gondolin). In the earlier writings it requested that Turgon sally forth to meet [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in open battle, for the Valar would aid their victory, but if he would not go forth to war, then he must abandon Gondolin and lead his people down [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) and seek back to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

For the edition of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) and [Guy Gavriel Kay](/wiki/Guy_Gavriel_Kay) rewrote it to adjust it to the late version: Ulmo's warning announced that the doom of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) was coming, suggesting that they seek to find a way to the [Blessed Realm](/wiki/Blessed_Realm).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Either way, Turgon did not heed this wise counsel, because of his pride, the love of his beautiful city and [Maeglin](/wiki/Maeglin)'s advices.
