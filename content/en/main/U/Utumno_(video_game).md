<i><b>Utumno</b></i> is a dungeon-crawling rogue-like game. Development of the game was started in [1996](../_/1996.md) by [Matt Craighead](../M/Matt_Craighead.md), who was the main designer. *Utumno* was cancelled in [1998](../_/1998.md) when Matt stopped working on the game,<sup id="cite_ref-archive_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-archive-1">[1]</a></sup> but was revived in [2009](../_/2009.md) when another designer decided to work on it.<sup id="cite_ref-archive_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-archive-1">[1]</a></sup> The game is still in development.
