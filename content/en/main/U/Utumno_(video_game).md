<i><b>Utumno</b></i> is a dungeon-crawling rogue-like game. Development of the game was started in [1996](/wiki/1996) by [Matt Craighead](/wiki/Matt_Craighead), who was the main designer. *Utumno* was cancelled in [1998](/wiki/1998) when Matt stopped working on the game,<sup id="cite_ref-archive_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-archive-1">[1]</a></sup> but was revived in [2009](/wiki/2009) when another designer decided to work on it.<sup id="cite_ref-archive_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-archive-1">[1]</a></sup> The game is still in development.
