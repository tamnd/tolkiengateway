**Udûn** was the deep valley in the northwest of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), formed at the meeting of the [Ered Lithui](/wiki/Ered_Lithui) and the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath). Its northern entrance was guarded by the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon) and its southern entrance was guarded by the [Isenmouthe](/wiki/Carach_Angren "Carach Angren").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The fortress [Durthang](/wiki/Durthang) stood in the northern Ephel Duath, on the slopes above Udûn.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Udûn* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), meaning "dark pit, Underworld"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> or "hell".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

It contains two great forges, <i>Anglach</i> in the north and <i>Mornaur</i> in the south, as well as dozens of lesser gear- and forge-works scattered throughout that were busy producing arms for the armies of <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>.</dd></dl>
