**United Artists** was an American film studio and subsidiary of [MGM](../M/MGM.md).

In [1969](../_/1969.md) UA purchased the film rights to *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* from [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) for just over £100 000.<sup id="cite_ref-Times1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Times1-1">[1]</a></sup> It is often repeated that Tolkien sold the rights for a 'pittance' in order to pay a tax bill,<sup id="cite_ref-Times1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Times1-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> in fact the sum he was paid was approximately equivalent to £2.3 million in 2009 and in addition to it he retained a 7.5% royalty interest in any future adaptation (transmitted to the [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md) on his death).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

United Artists commissioned [John Boorman](../J/John_Boorman.md) to write a screenplay of *The Lord of the Rings* in [1970](../_/1970.md), but it was never produced.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In [1976](../_/1976.md) they sold most of their rights to Tolkien's work to [Saul Zaentz Company](../S/Saul_Zaentz.md), who have licensed all subsequent authorized adaptations of *The Hobbit* and *The Lord of the Rings* through [Middle-earth Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md). However, United Artists retained the world distribution rights to film adaptations, until it was purchased by MGM. These are currently exercised by their parent company MGM, which under contract co-financend the [*The Hobbit* film trilogy](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).

On [2 March](../_/2_March.md) [2023](../_/2023.md), United Artists was shut down and it was folded into MGM, possibly due to a string of recent box office bombs.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> <span></span>
