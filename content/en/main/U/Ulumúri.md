The **Ulumúri** are the great white conches of [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) made by [Salmar](/wiki/Salmar).<sup id="cite_ref-s_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s-1">[1]</a></sup> They produce the music of the sea which afterwards [cannot be forgotten](/wiki/Sea-longing "Sea-longing").<sup id="cite_ref-s_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) personally saw Ulmo blowing his horn, which made him see all the waters of the world in a vision:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>And thereupon Ulmo lifted up a mighty horn, and blew upon it a single great note, to which the roaring of the storm was but a windflaw upon a lake.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="/wiki/Unfinished_Tales" title="Unfinished Tales">Unfinished Tales</a></i>, &#34;<a href="/wiki/Of_Tuor_and_his_Coming_to_Gondolin" title="Of Tuor and his Coming to Gondolin">Of Tuor and his Coming to Gondolin</a>&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote>
