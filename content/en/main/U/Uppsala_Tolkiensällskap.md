**Uppsala Tolkiensällskap** is a Swedish Tolkien Society. Based in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala" title="wikipedia:Uppsala">Uppsala</a>, Sweden. It was founded in [1973](/wiki/1973) and is (as of December 2012) the largest Tolkien Society in Sweden, having about 200 active members. The association is also known as Midgårds Fylking (roughly, "The Phalanx of Middle-earth"; "Fylking" also having the connotation of "Fylke", [Åke Ohlmarks](/wiki/%C3%85ke_Ohlmarks)'s Swedish translation in *[Härskarringen](/wiki/H%C3%A4rskarringen)* of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")). Uppsala Tolkiensällskap publishes <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=M%C3%A5nrunan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Månrunan (page does not exist)">Månrunan</a></i> ("The Moon rune"), which is not available to non-members.

Uppsala Tolkiensällskap is not a literary association; it rather focuses on social gatherings and events. Unlike many other Tolkien Societies, Uppsala Tolkiensällskap is a secret and private society. Entering the association is only possible upon recommendation by a member.
