<i><b>unque</b></i> is a noun meaning "a hollow" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/U/Unque.gif.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/b/b2/Unque.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Unque* is also the name in Quenya of the sixteenth letter of the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the fourth letter of the fourth grade or [Tyellë](../T/Tyellë.md), which contains "voiced spirants" and it is the fourth letter in the fourth series of consonants.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-2">[2]</a></sup> In the standard spelling of Quenya this letter represents **NKW** (or transcribed in English as **NQU**)<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> while in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) this letter represents **GHW**. In [Westron](../W/Westron.md) the fourth series is used for the *k*-series, the *[Calmatéma](../C/Calmatéma.md)*, and this letter represents **GH**.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-2">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Agha</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=UNUK&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="UNUK (page does not exist)">UNUK</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
