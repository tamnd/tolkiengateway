<i><b>Ungolianth</b></i> is the seventh track of the album [Minas Morgul](<../M/Minas_Morgul_(album).md>) by the band [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md). The song name is a misspelling of the mother of Spiders, [Ungoliant](Ungoliant.md). The lyrics reference Ungoliant, as well as her dwelling place [Avathar](../A/Avathar.md), and the Dark Lord [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) after he has claimed the [Silmarils](../S/Silmarils.md).

Your odem goes down the green rivers and <br/>
high up to the blue skies, to the wide open forests and <br/>
mountains so high, far away to the unknown and <br/>
at last to destroy the two trees of life on <br/>
the hills of <a href="../E/Ezellohar.md" title="Ezellohar">Ezellohar</a>... <br/>
<br/>
So high my throne, so cold as ice <br/>
it makes my blue eyes turn red <br/>
and this red covers my stronghold <a href="../A/Angband.md" title="Angband">Angband</a> <br/>
as a shadow fog to keep it unseen... <br/>
The Silmaril now covers my crown <br/>
<br/>
Keep away Ungolianth...
