<i><b>Ungolianth</b></i> is the seventh track of the album [Minas Morgul](</wiki/Minas_Morgul_(album)> "Minas Morgul (album)") by the band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning). The song name is a misspelling of the mother of Spiders, [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant). The lyrics reference Ungoliant, as well as her dwelling place [Avathar](/wiki/Avathar), and the Dark Lord [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) after he has claimed the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils).

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Come to me lady of a foreign shore <br/>
to pass on your knowledge to me, <br/>
interrupt these peacefull lands with your odem of pestilence <br/>
Your lifeform is living in the deepest twilight, <br/>
the depths of Avathar. <br/>
<br/>
Your odem goes down the green rivers and <br/>
high up to the blue skies, to the wide open forests and <br/>
mountains so high, far away to the unknown and <br/>
at last to destroy the two trees of life on <br/>
the hills of <a href="/wiki/Ezellohar" title="Ezellohar">Ezellohar</a>... <br/>
<br/>
So high my throne, so cold as ice <br/>
it makes my blue eyes turn red <br/>
and this red covers my stronghold <a href="/wiki/Angband" title="Angband">Angband</a> <br/>
as a shadow fog to keep it unseen... <br/>
The Silmaril now covers my crown <br/>
<br/>
Keep away Ungolianth...
</p>
