<i><b>Urulókë</b></i> (plural *Urulóki*) is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name of the [fire-drakes](/wiki/Fire-drakes "Fire-drakes"), meaning "fire-serpent"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-App_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> or "fire-dragon".<sup id="cite_ref-ety_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ety-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *ur-* ("heat, be hot") + *[lókë](/wiki/L%C3%B3k%C3%AB "Lókë")*<sup id="cite_ref-App_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ety_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ety-4">[4]</a></sup>

In *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, the form *Urulóki* is used both as a singular and plural noun.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

It has been suggested that the spelling according to the Quenya style of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* would be *Urulócë*, pl. *Urulóci*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
