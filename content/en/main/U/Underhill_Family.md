**Underhill** was a common family name amongst [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md). The Underhill Family had branches in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) and in [Bree](../B/Bree.md). In particular, there was a family of [Bree-hobbits](../B/Bree-hobbits.md) who lived in [Staddle](../S/Staddle.md) (on the east side of [Bree-hill](../B/Bree-hill.md)) who had that name.

When [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) made the decision to leave the Shire with [the Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md), [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) said that he must leave the name of "Baggins" behind and gave him the travelling name of "Underhill".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This was perhaps not the best choice, for when "Mr. Underhill" arrived in Bree, there were several Underhills in the common-room at [the Prancing Pony](../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md) and they thought it likely that Frodo was some long-lost cousin of theirs. One of the [Bree-landers](../B/Bree-landers.md), who seemed familiar with the Shire, asked where the Underhills lived and who were their relations.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

When the [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) came north up the [Greenway](../G/Greenway.md) some of them took to thievery and mischief in Bree. During a fight within the town one of the Underhills from Staddle was killed.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
