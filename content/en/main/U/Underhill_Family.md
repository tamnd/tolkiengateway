**Underhill** was a common family name amongst [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits). The Underhill Family had branches in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and in [Bree](/wiki/Bree). In particular, there was a family of [Bree-hobbits](/wiki/Bree-hobbits) who lived in [Staddle](/wiki/Staddle) (on the east side of [Bree-hill](/wiki/Bree-hill)) who had that name.

When [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) made the decision to leave the Shire with [the Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) said that he must leave the name of "Baggins" behind and gave him the travelling name of "Underhill".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This was perhaps not the best choice, for when "Mr. Underhill" arrived in Bree, there were several Underhills in the common-room at [the Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony "The Prancing Pony") and they thought it likely that Frodo was some long-lost cousin of theirs. One of the [Bree-landers](/wiki/Bree-landers), who seemed familiar with the Shire, asked where the Underhills lived and who were their relations.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

When the [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) came north up the [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway) some of them took to thievery and mischief in Bree. During a fight within the town one of the Underhills from Staddle was killed.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
