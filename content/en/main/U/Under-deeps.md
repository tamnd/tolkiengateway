The **Under-deeps** were a vast subterranean network invented by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*, stretching throughout Middle-earth but being particularly associated with [Moria](/wiki/Moria).<sup id="cite_ref-main_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-main-1">[1]</a></sup> Spelled as **Underdeeps**, they reappear in [Decipher](/wiki/Decipher)'s [Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game "The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game"), though seemingly limited to Moria.<sup id="cite_ref-decipher_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-decipher-2">[2]</a></sup>

They were introduced to the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game) in the [Dark Minions](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Dark_Minions "Middle-earth: Dark Minions") expansion, and are also referred to in [Decipher](/wiki/Decipher)'s *[card game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game "The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game")*. <sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History in Middle-earth Role Playing {#History_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing}

The Under-deeps were formed by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) during the [Spring of Arda](/wiki/Spring_of_Arda), by joining various volcanic caverns into a vast network. They spread from [Utumno](/wiki/Utumno) to the [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains) and from [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) to the [Orocarni](/wiki/Orocarni). Morgoth's servants used them as hidden passages to avoid the [Valar](/wiki/Valar). The core of the Under-deeps survived the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), and so do some of Morgoth's servants and creatures, though many of the passages are blocked or hidden.<sup id="cite_ref-main_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-main-1">[1]</a></sup>

- The **Iron Road**, leading from East to West, connected [Uax](/wiki/Urd#Uax "Urd"), Utumno, and [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim)
- The **Oldest Road**, leading from North to South, connected [Carn Dûm](/wiki/Carn_D%C3%BBm), [Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad), Moria, and the [Glittering Caves](/wiki/Glittering_Caves)
- The **Red Road**, leading from North to South, connected Uax and Sammath Helcar
- The **Black Road**, leading from East to West, connected Moria, [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) and [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr)
- The **White Road**, leading from East to West, connected the Glittering Caves and [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow)
- The **Grey Road**, leading from East to West, connected Gundabad and Sammath Maeglom (in the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains))
- The **Gold Road**, leading from North to South, connected Ôlbamarl (in [Khand](/wiki/Khand)) and Laurafelya
- The **Yellow Road**, leading from East to West, connected Sammath Fhûl (in the [Yellow Mountains](/wiki/Yellow_Mountains)) and Sambi Mornya

Of these, the Oldest Road (named so despite being younger than the Iron Road) was the most intact. Moria lay on a junction of this road with the Black Road, and Dwarves believe [Mahal](/wiki/Mahal) chose this on purpose to contest Morgoth's dominance.<sup id="cite_ref-main_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-main-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the Dwarves of Khazad-dûm transformed Hwáin's Well into a mineshaft, hoping to find [mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril"), they inadvertently broke into the Under-deeps. This allowed [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane) to enter Khazad-dûm. Soon after the Dwarves managed to construct a Stone of Sealing to seal off the Under-deeps for good, but it was captured by the Balrog before it could be used. The recovery of this Stone was what finally allowed the Dwarves to retake Khazad-dûm in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History in The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game {#History_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game}

While further excavated by the Dwarves in search of mithril, most of the tunnels and caverns long predated the Dwarves. They also housed many strange creatures awakened by the rise of the Balrog. Old Dwarven outposts meant to defend Khazad-dûm against attacks from the Underdeeps were taken over by Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-decipher_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-decipher-2">[2]</a></sup>
