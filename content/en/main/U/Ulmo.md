**Ulmo** was a [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar"), also known as *King of the Sea*, *Lord of Waters*, and *Dweller of the Deep*.<sup id="cite_ref-UT1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT1-2">[2]</a></sup> One of the mightiest of the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur), he was third in majesty of the [Aratar](/wiki/Aratar), after [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) and [Varda](/wiki/Varda).

At several points Ulmo was notable for setting himself apart from his brethren, and also for being the friendliest to the [Children of Eru](/wiki/Children_of_Eru).

## Attributes {#Attributes}

Ulmo was the Lord of Waters and King of the Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-3">[3]</a></sup> He was the Ainu most deeply instructed in music, and it was said that in water the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) could hear the echo of the [Music of the Ainur](/wiki/Music_of_the_Ainur).<sup id="cite_ref-ai_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ai-4">[4]</a></sup> He lived in the deeps under [Ambar](/wiki/Ambar) who alone saw where he devised his music and whence he governed all waters, bays and rivers.<sup id="cite_ref-s1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-vala_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-3">[3]</a></sup>

From the beginning Manwë had been his closest friend and ally and they came together when the vapors of the water become clouds high in the air, or mists and dew. The two most faithfully served the purpose of Eru.<sup id="cite_ref-ai_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ai-4">[4]</a></sup>

Ulmo was unique among the Valar in several ways; he was alone, taking no spouse among the [Valier](/wiki/Valier); he didn't dwell in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) but in [Ekkaia](/wiki/Ekkaia) from the beginning, preferring the deeps of the seas around and below [Ambar](/wiki/Ambar), and the rivers. He seldom appeared to the councils of his brethren, save in matters of great importance.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-3">[3]</a></sup>

Likewise, he seldomly wore a [fana](/wiki/Fana "Fana") and rather came to the shores unseen or entered firths of the sea and rivers. It was said his spirit was in the very veins of the world. He spoke through the sound of water, and made music with his horns the [Ulumúri](/wiki/Ulum%C3%BAri). But whenever he appeared, he was dreadful and terrible "as a mounting wave that strides to the land"; he wore a dark foam-crested helm and a shimmering silver-green mail, and his voice was deep like the ocean.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-3">[3]</a></sup>

Ulmo cared about Arda and the Children of Eru, and through the veins of the world he kept in touch with them and saw every grief and need, and thus knew more of the goings on with them than even Manwë. Even while the Valar were secluded in Valinor or when the Children were under the wrath of his brethren, Ulmo, alone of the Valar, was the one who never forsook them.<sup id="cite_ref-s1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-vala_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-3">[3]</a></sup> However, it was not done as rebellion towards other Valar, but because it was a role to which he was "appointed ere the making of the World".<sup id="cite_ref-UT1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT1-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Ulmo was very close in friendship to Manwë before [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4) was made, and although Manwë was the most dear to [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar), of all the Ainur Ulmo was most deeply instructed in music. Ulmo with Manwë and [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) were the architects of [Arda](/wiki/Arda).<sup id="cite_ref-ai_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ai-4">[4]</a></sup> Ulmo's vassal [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB), and Ossë's spouse [Uinen](/wiki/Uinen) were, to the Elves, the best known of the [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar). Through them Ulmo would learn much of the elves.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-3">[3]</a></sup>

After Melkor destroyed the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps) and forced the Valar to retreat to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), Ulmo opposed the raising of the [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri) and withdrawal from the [Outer Lands](/wiki/Outer_Lands), leaving them to Melkor's dominion.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Ulmo remained to the Outer Sea, and while his brethren neglected the Outer Lands during the [Sleep of Yavanna](/wiki/Sleep_of_Yavanna), Ulmo kept the earth alive under the darkness.<sup id="cite_ref-s1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-1">[1]</a></sup>

He seldom came to the Councils of [Máhanaxar](/wiki/M%C3%A1hanaxar), such as the time when the Valar were to prepare to [attack](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers "Battle of the Powers") Melkor's fortress of [Utumno](/wiki/Utumno). He also was the chief of a minority of the Valar who believed that the Elves should remain free in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and order and heal the hurts of the land with their skills and grace, but this opinion was overruled.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Nevertheless he eventually helped them get to Aman, because he knew that was what they wanted, and he accepted that. He tore a great piece of land off the shores of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and used it to transport the Eldar to the blessed coasts of Aman. He anchored the island (it was renamed [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa)) in the [Bay of Eldamar](/wiki/Bay_of_Eldamar), which he did because he knew the minds of the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri).

When the time came for Melkor to be given a second chance and was pardoned, Ulmo and [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas) were still distrustful of him, but both obeyed Manwë's bidding.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

During the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) Ulmo protected [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) and [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion) and directed more than any other Vala the [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar). He urged [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) to build [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) and [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) to build [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond).

He chose [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) as his instrument before his birth, by helping [Huor](/wiki/Huor) and [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) reach [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) and telling [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) to accept him, as aid would come from the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador). Years later, he guided [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) directing him to Gondolin as a messenger for Turgon. Despite [Ulmo's warning](/wiki/Ulmo%27s_warning), Turgon chose not to abandon Gondolin. Tuor eventually married Turgon's daughter [Idril](/wiki/Idril) and fathered [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) the [Half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven); Ulmo saved [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing) from the sack of the Havens of [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion), and thus she took the [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmarils "Silmarils") to her husband Eärendil which allowed them to gain admittance to Valinor and plead for their mercy.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ulmo* is said to derive from the [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) <i><b>Ullubōz</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-8">[8]</a></sup>

Alternatively, *Ulmo* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) title, which means "the Pourer", from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ulya-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ulya- (page does not exist)">ulya-</a></i> ("to pour") + *[-mo](/wiki/-mo)* (agentive ending "-er").<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earlier writings his [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name was *Gulma*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Later his Gnomish name was <i><b>Ylmir</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> (see [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor)'s *[The Horns of Ylmir](/wiki/The_Horns_of_Ylmir)*). [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) translations of his name were given as <i><b>Nûron</b></i> and <i><b>Ulu</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

In the [Gnomish Lexicon](/wiki/Gnomish_Lexicon) he is given the name **Vailimo**.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:18</sup>

In [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol)'s [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) translations, Ulmo is referred to by various names: *Garsecges frea* ("Ocean ruler"), *ealwaeter-frea* ("All-waters ruler") or *agendfrea ealra waetera* ("owning lord of all waters").<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

His palace, on the bottom of the [Outer Sea](/wiki/Outer_Sea), was called [Ulmonan](/wiki/Ulmonan).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>
