<i><b>Unto a Long Glory...</b></i> is the seventh track of the album [Dol Guldur](</wiki/Dol_Guldur_(album)> "Dol Guldur (album)") by the band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning). Like the song [The Passing of the Grey Company](</wiki/The_Passing_of_the_Grey_Company_(song)> "The Passing of the Grey Company (song)") from the album [Minas Morgul](</wiki/Minas_Morgul_(album)> "Minas Morgul (album)"), the lyrics reference [Aragorn taking the Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Aragorn_Takes_the_Paths_of_the_Dead "Aragorn Takes the Paths of the Dead"), including the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company), [Stone of Erech](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech), and the [Oathbreakers](/wiki/Oathbreakers).

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>The doom approaches the Dead awaken; <br/>
For the hour is come for the oathbreakers <br/>
At the stone of Erech they shall stand again <br/>
Hear there horn in hills enchants <br/>
Who shall call them <br/>
From grey twilight <br/>
Forgotten ones? <br/>
<br/>
Out of doubt, out of dark <br/>
Hope rekindles, and hope in end <br/>
Over death, over dread <br/>
Over doom lifted <br/>
Out of loss, out of life <br/>
And out of doubt, out of dark <br/>
Over death, over dread <br/>
Out of loss, <br/>
Unto a long glory... <br/>
<br/>
The heir of him to whom the oath they swore <br/>
From the North he shall come <br/>
And he shall pass the door <br/>
To the Paths of the Dead <br/>
<br/>
Mourn not overmuch - mighty was the fallen <br/>
And war now calls us! <br/>
<br/>
And hear there horn in hills ringing <br/>
Who shall call them <br/>
from grey twilight
</p>
