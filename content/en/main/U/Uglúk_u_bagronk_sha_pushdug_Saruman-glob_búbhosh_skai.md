**Uglúk u bagronk sha pushdug Saruman-glob búb-hosh skai** is a phrase in [Black Speech](../B/Black_Speech.md), spoken by an unidentified [Uruk](../O/Orcs.md) of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). It was a phrase used to express a certain contempt in the handling of the prisoners [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), aimed at [Uglúk](Uglúk.md) and the Orcs of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Translation {#Translation}

The phrase is not translated in the text, and in Appendix F it is only identified as "*the more debased form \[of Black Speech\] used by the soldiers of [the Dark Tower](../B/Barad-dûr.md)*".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> However, there exist three different translations of this sentence.

### First translation {#First_translation}

This translation appeared in the draft of Appendix F, published in *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](../T/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md)*. Here, it is translated as "*Uglúk to the cesspool, sha! the dungfilth; the great Saruman-fool, skai!*"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Second translation {#Second_translation}

In a second translation, published in an article by [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md) in [*Vinyar Tengwar* 26](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_26.md), the phrase reads "*Uglúk u bagronk sha pushdug Saruman-glob – búb-hosh skai!*", and the translation "*Uglúk to the dung-pit with stinking Saruman-filth - pig-guts, gah!*". Hostetter identifies the speaker as Grishnákh.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Third translation {#Third_translation}

Yet another translation, published in [*Parma Eldalamberon* 17](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_17.md), is from the late 1950s, and as far as is known, Tolkien's last word on the subject. Here, the sentence is divided into one long sentence and one shorter - only expressing more contempt.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <br>

<dl><dd>&#34;<i>[Ugluk] u bagronk sha pushdug Saruman-glob.</i><br/><i>búbhosh - skai!</i>&#34;</dd></dl>

Which is translated as:<br>

<dl><dd>&#34;<i>Uglúk to torture (chamber) with stinking Saruman-filth.</i><br/><i>Dung-heap. Skai!</i>&#34;.</dd></dl>
