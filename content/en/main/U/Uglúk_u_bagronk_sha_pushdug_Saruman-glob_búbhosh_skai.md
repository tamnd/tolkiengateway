**Uglúk u bagronk sha pushdug Saruman-glob búb-hosh skai** is a phrase in [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech), spoken by an unidentified [Uruk](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). It was a phrase used to express a certain contempt in the handling of the prisoners [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), aimed at [Uglúk](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk) and the Orcs of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Translation {#Translation}

The phrase is not translated in the text, and in Appendix F it is only identified as "*the more debased form \[of Black Speech\] used by the soldiers of [the Dark Tower](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr "Barad-dûr")*".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> However, there exist three different translations of this sentence.

### First translation {#First_translation}

This translation appeared in the draft of Appendix F, published in *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)*. Here, it is translated as "*Uglúk to the cesspool, sha! the dungfilth; the great Saruman-fool, skai!*"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Second translation {#Second_translation}

In a second translation, published in an article by [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) in [*Vinyar Tengwar* 26](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_26 "Vinyar Tengwar 26"), the phrase reads "*Uglúk u bagronk sha pushdug Saruman-glob – búb-hosh skai!*", and the translation "*Uglúk to the dung-pit with stinking Saruman-filth - pig-guts, gah!*". Hostetter identifies the speaker as Grishnákh.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Third translation {#Third_translation}

Yet another translation, published in [*Parma Eldalamberon* 17](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_17 "Parma Eldalamberon 17"), is from the late 1950s, and as far as is known, Tolkien's last word on the subject. Here, the sentence is divided into one long sentence and one shorter - only expressing more contempt.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <br>

<dl><dd>&#34;<i>[Ugluk] u bagronk sha pushdug Saruman-glob.</i><br/><i>búbhosh - skai!</i>&#34;</dd></dl>

Which is translated as:<br>

<dl><dd>&#34;<i>Uglúk to torture (chamber) with stinking Saruman-filth.</i><br/><i>Dung-heap. Skai!</i>&#34;.</dd></dl>
