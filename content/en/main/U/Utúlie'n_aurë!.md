<i><b>Utúlie&#39;n aurë!</b></i> ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") ˈ*The day has come!*ˈ) was the cry of [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) during of the [Fifth Battle of Beleriand](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad "Nirnaeth Arnoediad"), when he saw the armies of his brother [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).

## History {#History}

The west wing of the [Union of Maedhros](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros) was commanded by Fingon, who led the armies of [his house](/wiki/House_of_Fingolfin "House of Fingolfin") and the [Edain](/wiki/Edain). After the treason of the [Easterlings](</wiki/Easterlings_(First_Age)> "Easterlings (First Age)") in the east wing, [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) didn't come from east [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith) as planned, and a dark cloud rose above both [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim) and Fingon's heart. Unexpectedly, Turgon came forth, bringing ten thousand Elves from [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Then when Fingon heard afar the great trumpet of Turgon his brother, the shadow passed and his heart was uplifted, and he shouted aloud: <i><b>Utúlie&#39;n aurë! Aiya Eldalië ar Atanatári, utúlie&#39;n aurë! The day has come! Behold, people of the Eldar and Fathers of Men, the day has come!</b></i> And all those who heard his great voice echo in the hills answered crying: <i><b>Auta i lómë! The night is passing!</b></i>
</p>
</blockquote>

The result of the following battle was tragic: Fingon died and Turgon only could run away after the Men of the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador) sacrificed themselves. However, the hope of the first battle cry was echoed by [Húrin Thalion](/wiki/H%C3%BArin_Thalion) when he remained as the last standing fighter, shouting: *[Aurë entuluva!](/wiki/Aur%C3%AB_entuluva!) Day shall come again!*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
