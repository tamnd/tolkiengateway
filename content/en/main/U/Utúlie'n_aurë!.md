<i><b>Utúlie&#39;n aurë!</b></i> ([Q.](../Q/Quenya.md) ˈ*The day has come!*ˈ) was the cry of [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) during of the [Fifth Battle of Beleriand](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), when he saw the armies of his brother [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).

## History {#History}

The west wing of the [Union of Maedhros](Union_of_Maedhros.md) was commanded by Fingon, who led the armies of [his house](../H/House_of_Fingolfin.md) and the [Edain](../E/Edain.md). After the treason of the [Easterlings](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>) in the east wing, [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) didn't come from east [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md) as planned, and a dark cloud rose above both [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md) and Fingon's heart. Unexpectedly, Turgon came forth, bringing ten thousand Elves from [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Then when Fingon heard afar the great trumpet of Turgon his brother, the shadow passed and his heart was uplifted, and he shouted aloud: <i><b>Utúlie&#39;n aurë! Aiya Eldalië ar Atanatári, utúlie&#39;n aurë! The day has come! Behold, people of the Eldar and Fathers of Men, the day has come!</b></i> And all those who heard his great voice echo in the hills answered crying: <i><b>Auta i lómë! The night is passing!</b></i>
</p>
</blockquote>

The result of the following battle was tragic: Fingon died and Turgon only could run away after the Men of the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md) sacrificed themselves. However, the hope of the first battle cry was echoed by [Húrin Thalion](../H/Húrin_Thalion.md) when he remained as the last standing fighter, shouting: *[Aurë entuluva!](../A/Aurë_entuluva!.md) Day shall come again!*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
