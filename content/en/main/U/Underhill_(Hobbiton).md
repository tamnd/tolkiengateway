**Underhill** was the part of [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md) that lay directly beneath [Hobbiton Hill](../H/Hobbiton_Hill.md). Its most famous [smial](../H/Hobbit-holes.md) was within the Hill itself; [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Obviously Underhill contained [Bagshot Row](../B/Bagshot_Row.md).

As [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) had already learned of *[Baggins](../B/Baggins_family.md)* through [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) and using the name outside of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) would prove dangerous, [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) told [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) to use the name "[Mr. Underhill](../M/Mr._Underhill.md)" after leaving home.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In a draft, Underhill was a separate village. In such a draft, [Bilbo's will](../B/Bilbo's_will.md) mentions "Underhill near Hobbiton".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
