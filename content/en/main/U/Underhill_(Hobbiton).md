**Underhill** was the part of [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) that lay directly beneath [Hobbiton Hill](/wiki/Hobbiton_Hill). Its most famous [smial](/wiki/Hobbit-holes "Hobbit-holes") was within the Hill itself; [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Obviously Underhill contained [Bagshot Row](/wiki/Bagshot_Row).

As [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) had already learned of *[Baggins](/wiki/Baggins_family "Baggins family")* through [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) and using the name outside of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") would prove dangerous, [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) told [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) to use the name "[Mr. Underhill](/wiki/Mr._Underhill)" after leaving home.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In a draft, Underhill was a separate village. In such a draft, [Bilbo's will](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_will) mentions "Underhill near Hobbiton".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
