**Uglúk** was an [Uruk](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai") captain in the service of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). The company of [orcs of Isengard](/wiki/Orcs_of_Isengard "Orcs of Isengard") under his leadership killed [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir), captured [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") and attempted to bring them to [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).

## History {#History}

On [26 February](/wiki/26_February) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") Uglúk was the leader of the [Orc-company](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") that attacked the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) on the slopes of [Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen), slew Boromir and carried off Merry and Pippin.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Orcish_Medicine.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/6/65/Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Orcish_Medicine.jpg/300px-Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Orcish_Medicine.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="234" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/6/65/Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Orcish_Medicine.jpg/450px-Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Orcish_Medicine.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/6/65/Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Orcish_Medicine.jpg/600px-Irvin_Rodriguez_-_Orcish_Medicine.jpg 2x" data-file-width="4000" data-file-height="3126"/></a><figcaption><i>Orcish Medicine</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Irvin_Rodriguez" title="Category:Images by Irvin Rodriguez">Irvin Rodriguez</a></figcaption></figure>

Uglúk and his fellow Isengarders were servants of the [Wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards") [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman), whom he called the Wise and respected him for his giving of man’s-flesh to eat. However, not all of his company were from Isengard – the group contained a contingent of Orcs from [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) led by [Grishnákh](/wiki/Grishn%C3%A1kh) and a party of Northerners from [Moria](/wiki/Moria). Uglúk's orders had been to kill all of the Fellowship except the Halflings, who were to be brought back to Isengard and Saruman alive, captured and unspoiled, as quickly as possible. Grishnákh's orders were the same, except that his destination was to be Lugbúrz ([Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr)) and [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). The Northerners had no orders; they had come from the Mines for vengeance and killing, followed by a quick return to the north.

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Donato_Giancola_-_Swift_Strokes.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/0/06/Donato_Giancola_-_Swift_Strokes.png/300px-Donato_Giancola_-_Swift_Strokes.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="252" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/0/06/Donato_Giancola_-_Swift_Strokes.png 1.5x" data-file-width="400" data-file-height="336"/></a><figcaption><i>Swift Strokes</i> by <a href="/wiki/Donato_Giancola" title="Donato Giancola">Donato Giancola</a></figcaption></figure>

Not long after the capture of the hobbits these conflicting aims led to a brief fight in which some of the Mordor-orcs and Moria-orcs were slain. Uglúk won and was able to impose his will; his Orc-company headed west while Grishnákh's group withdrew.<sup id="cite_ref-Uruk_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Uruk-3">[3]</a></sup>

On the evening of [26 February](/wiki/26_February)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> Uglúk learned that a single horseman of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) had been spotted but had not been stopped. Realizing that pursuit by the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) would swiftly follow he applied medicine on Merry's wound and forced drinks down both hobbits' throats. He then cut their leg bonds so that they could run with the company. When the Northerners objected to his plan to keep on running by daylight Uglúk promised that they would run with him behind them, and he ran at the rear to keep an eye on all of his company.<sup id="cite_ref-Uruk_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Uruk-3">[3]</a></sup>

Despite the fast pace set by Uglúk on [28 February](/wiki/28_February)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> an [Éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored) of mounted [Men](/wiki/Men) began to overtake the Orcs. Near the [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash) most of the Northerners broke away and dashed off towards the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). At the same time Grishnákh returned with a couple of score of fellow Mordor-orcs. Due to the threat from the riders the two Orc-companies combined and headed for the forest. As they sped along they caught up with the Northerners who perforce rejoined Uglúk's command.

Despite all the speed they could muster the Orcs, now numbering two hundred, were surrounded just three furlongs from the eaves of the forest. The hemmed-in Orcs began to grumble against Uglúk until he assured them that reinforcements led by an Orc named [Mauhúr](/wiki/Mauh%C3%BAr) were close at hand. As the night wore on the Riders made a sortie against the Orcs. Uglúk led his Isengarders to stem the attack, which gave Grishnákh the opportunity to attempt to steal away with the two hobbits.<sup id="cite_ref-Uruk_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Uruk-3">[3]</a></sup>

Grishnákh was killed and the hobbits escaped just as Mauhúr tried but failed to break the encirclement. Merry and Pippin fled into the woods as the Éored attacked at dawn on [29 February](/wiki/29_February).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> In the ensuing battle Uglúk almost broke through the Riders with a wedge of soldiers, but he was brought to bay at the very edge of the woods and slain by [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) in a hand-to-hand sword fight.<sup id="cite_ref-Uruk_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Uruk-3">[3]</a></sup>

Uglúk died not knowing that the two hobbits he had captured did not have [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"). As [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) later noted, Uglúk's efforts would unwittingly lead to the downfall of Saruman.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Appearance {#Appearance}

Uglúk was a large black orc.<sup id="cite_ref-Uruk_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Uruk-3">[3]</a></sup> Being an Uruk, he was larger and stronger than the other Orcs and, presumably due to an exhaustive training.<sup id="cite_ref-Uruk_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Uruk-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:449</sup>, he was not bothered by sunlight. Uglúk bore the symbol of the [White Hand](/wiki/White_Hand) of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman). Like the other Isengard Uruk-hai, Uglúk carried a great bow and a short broad-bladed sword.

<b><a href="/wiki/1978" title="1978">1978</a>: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>An <a href="/wiki/Orcs_of_Isengard" title="Orcs of Isengard">Orc of Isengard</a> sharing Uglúk&#39;s dialogue appears in the film. <a href="/wiki/Peregrin_Took" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a> later tells <a href="/wiki/Grishn%C3%A1kh" title="Grishnákh">Grishnákh</a> that &#34;Uglúk will be back any minute&#34;, indicating that the Orc was meant to be Uglúk. Uglúk scolds a group of Orcs for wasting arrows trying to attack the <a href="/wiki/Rohirrim" title="Rohirrim">Rohirrim</a>. He orders <a href="/wiki/Snaga_(orc_of_Isengard)" title="Snaga (orc of Isengard)">Snaga</a> and <a href="/wiki/Lugdush" title="Lugdush">Lugdush</a> to stand guard and bind <a href="/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a> and Pippin&#39;s legs and to refrain from killing them unless the <a href="/wiki/Whiteskins" title="Whiteskins">Whiteskins</a> break through. An <a href="/wiki/Orcs_of_Mordor" title="Orcs of Mordor">Orc of Mordor</a> quarrels with Uglúk, claiming &#34;You and <a href="/wiki/Saruman" title="Saruman">your stinking Wizard</a>. You got us into this. When I tell them in <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a>...&#34;</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/1981" title="1981">1981</a>: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Uglúk was played by <a href="/wiki/Brian_Haines" title="Brian Haines">Brian Haines</a>. Ugluk&#39;s scenes were severely abridged, but his death at the hands of the riders of Rohan can be heard.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/1997" title="1997">1997</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Uglúk is depicted in the <a href="/wiki/1997" title="1997">1997</a> expansion <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Lidless_Eye" title="Middle-earth: The Lidless Eye">Middle-earth: The Lidless Eye</a></i> on two cards, the first of which being <i>Swift Strokes</i> illustrated by <a href="/wiki/Donato_Giancola" title="Donato Giancola">Donato Giancola</a>. The card has the following effects:</dd></dl>

> Warrior receives +1 prowess against one strike and obtains two random values against it, choosing the one to use.

The second is *Orc-draughts*, illustrated by Daniel Frazier which has the following effects:

> Discard to give +1 prowess to all characters in bearer’s company until the end of turn.

<b><a href="/wiki/2002" title="2002">2002</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Uglúk was portrayed by <a href="/wiki/Nathaniel_Lees" title="Nathaniel Lees">Nathaniel Lees</a>. Instead of being the leader of the Uruk-hai that caused Boromir&#39;s death, <a href="/wiki/Lurtz" title="Lurtz">Lurtz</a> is given the role.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2002" title="2002">2002</a>: <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/P%C3%A1n_prste%C5%88ov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series)" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The voice of Uglúk is provided by Pavol Topoľský.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2012" title="2012">2012</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/Guardians_of_Middle-earth" title="Guardians of Middle-earth">Guardians of Middle-earth</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Uglúk is a warrior-type &#34;guardian&#34; with four abilities: <i>Charge of the Uruk-hai</i>, <i>White Hand&#39;s Whip</i>, <i>Fury of Isengard</i> and <i>Orcish Medicine</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2012" title="2012">2012</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Uglúk makes a cameo appearance in a flashback session play. The site of Uglúk&#39;s death has become known as <i>Eomer&#39;s vengeance</i> and his body can be found there where the player&#39;s narration notes that Boromir has been avenged.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2015" title="2015">2015</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game">The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Uglúk is depicted in the <a href="/wiki/2015" title="2015">2015</a> expansion <i>The Uruk-hai Nightmare</i> on the &#34;Orc-draught&#34; card, illustrated by Rafal Hrynkiewicz . The card has the following effects:</dd></dl>

> Peril. Surge.<br>When Revealed: Either discard an ally you control, or increase the pursuit value by 1.<br>Shadow: Exhaust a character you control.

<b><a href="/wiki/2023" title="2023">2023</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth" title="The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth">The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Uglúk is depicted on the <i>Orcish Medicine</i> card, illustrated by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Irvin_Rodriguez" title="Category:Images by Irvin Rodriguez">Irvin Rodriguez</a>. The card has the following effects:</dd></dl>

> Target creature gains your choice of lifelink or indestructible until end of turn.<br>Amass Orcs 1. (Put a +1/-1 counter on an Army you control. It's also an Orc. If you don't control an Army, create a 0/0 black Orc Army creature token first.)
