<i><b>ulgundo</b></i> in [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) means "monster, deformed and hideous creature".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish "Primitive Elvish") form is given as *ulgundô* from ÚLGU.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
