**Urthel** (died [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [460](../F/First_Age_460.md)) was one of [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md)'s [twelve followers](../B/Barahir's_outlaws.md).

## History {#History}

Urthel fought in the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) under the command of [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md). He was part of a small group of [twelve](../B/Barahir's_outlaws.md) [Bëorings](../B/Bëorings.md) loyal to their lord that survived.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The survivors formed a small band and lived as outlaws in [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md), near [Tarn Aeluin](../T/Tarn_Aeluin.md).

They lived in hiding for some time, until [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s agents captured [Gorlim](../G/Gorlim.md) and coerced him into giving the location of their hideaway. [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) were sent to raid it, and succeeded. Urthel and the other outlaws were killed. Only [Beren](../B/Beren.md) survived, as he had been away on an errand.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
