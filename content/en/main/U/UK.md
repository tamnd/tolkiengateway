**UK** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "nasty".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) notes that the root is "*apparently an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onomatopoeia" title="wikipedia:Onomatopoeia">onomatopoeic</a> stem*" and that it is "*probably related to the root [UG](/wiki/UG) 'dislike' (whence Q. úra 'nasty')*".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In *[Eldarin Roots and Stems](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_17 "Parma Eldalamberon 17")* appear the roots "? UK, UKLA" (without any given significations, except for being one of the opposites of [AYA](/wiki/AYA)).<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-3">[3]</a></sup> It has been suggested that "*the queried stem uk in this note is perhaps the same as later UK 'nasty'*".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *ulca*<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-3">[3]</a></sup>
