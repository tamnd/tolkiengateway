**Undertowers** was a dwelling of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) in the [Westmarch](/wiki/Westmarch) during the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age). It was located near the [White Towers](/wiki/White_Towers).

It was at Undertowers that [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee")'s daughter [Elanor Gardner](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner) settled with [Fastred of Greenholm](/wiki/Fastred_of_Greenholm) around [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1462](/wiki/Fourth_Age_41 "Fourth Age 41"). Thus the [Fairbairn Family](/wiki/Fairbairn_Family), the hereditary [Wardens of Westmarch](/wiki/Warden_of_Westmarch "Warden of Westmarch") was founded.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Undertowers was one of the three places of the Shire with the largest libraries and collections of historical documents, the others being [Brandy Hall](/wiki/Brandy_Hall) and [Great Smials](/wiki/Great_Smials). Its library contained the [Red Book of Westmarch](/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch), chronicling the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) and the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
