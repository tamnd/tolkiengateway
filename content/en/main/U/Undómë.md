**Undómë** was one of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md)' names for the hour of star-opening at the end of the day: that is, dusk. Undómë was the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name for this time, with the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) equivalent being [aduial](../A/Aduial.md). There was also a poetic [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) translation, [Evendim](../E/Evendim.md) (that is, the dim light of evening).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This word is the source of [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md)'s surname [Undómiel](Undómiel.md), which translates as 'Evenstar'.
