**Undómë** was one of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves)' names for the hour of star-opening at the end of the day: that is, dusk. Undómë was the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for this time, with the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) equivalent being [aduial](/wiki/Aduial "Aduial"). There was also a poetic [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) translation, [Evendim](/wiki/Evendim) (that is, the dim light of evening).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This word is the source of [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen)'s surname [Undómiel](/wiki/Und%C3%B3miel), which translates as 'Evenstar'.
