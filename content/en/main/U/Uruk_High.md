**Uruk High** is a Tolkien-related heavy/stoner metal band that was formed in 2023 in Varaždin, Croatia. The band's name plays on the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai), and its lyrics mix stoner-metal themes with references to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Their debut album *Isenguardian* (2025) includes songs such as "White Hand Pass", "Warg Rider", "Takin' the Hobbudz to Isengard", "Saruman's Stash" and "Smoky Mines of Moria".

## Members {#Members}

- Tudoom Taman - vocals, guitar
- Dumdumuruk - drums
- Grogoruk - guitar
- Krvoruk - bass
