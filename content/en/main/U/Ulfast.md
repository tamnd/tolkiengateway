**Ulfast** was the second son<sup><a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Ulfast&#39;s_year_of_birth">NB</a></sup> of [Ulfang](/wiki/Ulfang) the *Black*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ulf_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ulf-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After entering [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), Ulfast along with his father and his brothers joined the service of [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir).<sup id="cite_ref-Ulf_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ulf-2">[2]</a></sup>

Ultimately though, his father Ulfang's purpose from the beginning was to eventually betray the [Elves](/wiki/Elves),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> which he and his brothers ultimately did in the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears), under the leadership of [Uldor](/wiki/Uldor) the Accursed - they turned against their allies, attacking them in the rear. Their betrayal proved crucial in the fight against Morgoth's forces, though, and Morgoth was victorious.<sup id="cite_ref-Nirn_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nirn-4">[4]</a></sup>

However, Ulfast, as well as his brother [Ulwarth](/wiki/Ulwarth) were killed by the sons of [Bór](/wiki/B%C3%B3r), who remained loyal to the sons of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) during the battle - while Ulfast's brother Uldor was slain by [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor).<sup id="cite_ref-Nirn_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nirn-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ulfast* was probably not the character's true name, as it seems to be [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Ugly shaggy hair": <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ul-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ul- (page does not exist)">ul-</a></i> ("ugly") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Fast&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fast (page does not exist)">fast</a></i> ("shaggy hair").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup>

A curious thing about the name *Ulfast* is that it remained unchanged from its conception to the later [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Earlier versions of the *[Nirnaeth](/wiki/Nirnaeth)* {#Earlier_versions_of_the_Nirnaeth}

According to an earlier version of the legendarium, instead of being killed by the sons of [Bór](/wiki/B%C3%B3r), both Ulfast and his brother Ulwar survived the Battle of Unnumbered Tears - in fact, they were the ones that slew Bor and his sons in battle.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

However, even though Morgoth pledged better lands to Ulfang, Ulfast's father, he ultimately reneged on his promise and exiled the remainder of his people (under the leadership of Ulfast and Ulwar) to [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum), which was an unfertile and desolate land; Morgoth also forbade them from ever journeying to other lands.

Afterwards, the folk of Ulfast and Ulwar brutally oppressed the remaining native folk of [Hádor](/wiki/House_of_Hador "House of Hador") who were already living there before their arrival.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Ulfast.27s_year_of_birth"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Ulfast&#39;s_year_of_birth">Ulfast&#39;s year of birth</span></h3>

In the appendix of *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings)* in <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Genealogies&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Genealogies (page does not exist)">The Genealogies</a></i> (dating from the early [1930s](/wiki/1930 "1930")), it is stated that Ulfast was born in the year 128.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

When comparing the above date with its associated text *[The Earliest Annals of Beleriand](/wiki/The_Earliest_Annals_of_Beleriand)*, Ulfast was born 35 years before the [Swarthy Men](/wiki/Swarthy_Men) entered Beleriand in the year 163.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

Also, the dates found in *The Earliest Annals of Beleriand* were by and large moved 300 years into the future in *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)* (c. early [1950s](/wiki/1950 "1950")), such that, for example, an event happening in the year 195 in *The Earliest Annals* now happened in the year [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>
