**UR** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") meaning "warmth", "be hot" (compare root [AS](/wiki/AS)).<sup id="cite_ref-Elda_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elda-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ety396_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety396-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *urya-* ("be hot"); *[Úrin](/wiki/Sun#Names_of_the_Sun "Sun")*; *aurĭ-* ("heat, period of sun"); *[aure](/wiki/Aure "Aure")* ("day")<sup id="cite_ref-Elda_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elda-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[aur](/wiki/Aur "Aur")* ("sunlight, day")

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), the meaning of root UR as "be hot" was changed to meaning "wide, large, great", with the derivatives [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *úra* ("large"), *[Úrien](/wiki/E%C3%B6nw%C3%AB "Eönwë")*, and [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *ûr* ("wide").<sup id="cite_ref-Ety396_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety396-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the *[Qenya Lexicon](/wiki/Qenya_Lexicon)* appears the root URU, with the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) derivative *ūrin* ("blazing hot").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
