**Unspoiled Earth 1984** is a calendar that was published by Friends of the Earth (New Zealand) Ltd. with photos of [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) locations by Barney Brewster. The photographs are meant to reflect scenes in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) with Tolkien-themed titles.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
