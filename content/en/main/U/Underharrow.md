**Underharrow** was a hamlet on the banks of the [Snowbourn](/wiki/Snowbourn) river beneath the cliff of [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> in the valley of [Harrowdale](/wiki/Harrowdale) in [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). Located south of [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras), it was a mile upriver from the hamlet [Upbourn](/wiki/Upbourn).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> As the great host of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) passed through the town during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), many women were standing sadly outside their homes. They knew that many of their husbands, fathers, and sons would never return from the battle that was soon at hand.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "under-fane" and refers to the hamlet being in the valley below the [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayals in Adaptations {#Portrayals_in_Adaptations}

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Underharrow is a small town which is the seat of <a href="/wiki/D%C3%BAnhere" title="Dúnhere">Dúnhere</a>, Lord of the <a href="/wiki/Harrowdale" title="Harrowdale">Harrowdale</a>. In his absence at the <a href="/wiki/Fords_of_Isen" title="Fords of Isen">Fords of Isen</a>, it is ruled by his wife Ellen Fremedon, whom the players assists with preparation for allowing <a href="/wiki/Edoras" title="Edoras">Edoras</a> refugees to pass to <a href="/wiki/Dunharrow" title="Dunharrow">Dunharrow</a>.</dd></dl>
