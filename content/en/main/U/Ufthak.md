**Ufthak** was an [Orc](../O/Orcs.md) of the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](../T/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md).

## History {#History}

Ufthak was captured and poisoned by [Shelob](../S/Shelob.md), and encased in her webbings. [Shagrat](../S/Shagrat.md), his commander, saw him hanging there, but made no effort to free him as he was eager to leave the caves without making Shelob angry.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

**2003:** <i><b><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(video_game).md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (video game)">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (video game)</a></b></i>

<dl><dd>Although not explicitly identified by name, a lone Orc can be found in the &#34;Shelob&#39;s Lair&#34; level, fighting for his life against Shelob&#39;s children in a small, web-filled chamber. While it might not truly be Ufthak himself, it may still be a homage to his fate.</dd>
<dd>He can be left to fend for himself, but he will start fighting the player if they choose to save him from his attackers.</dd></dl>
