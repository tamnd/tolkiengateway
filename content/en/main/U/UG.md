**UG** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "dislike".<sup id="cite_ref-VT43_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT43-1">[1]</a></sup> [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md) has suggested that the root is "*probably related*" to [UK](UK.md).<sup id="cite_ref-VT48_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT48-2">[2]</a></sup> Appearing in a late manuscript concerned with negation,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> it has been suggested that the root could be related to the early negative stem <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=UGU&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="UGU (page does not exist)">UGU</a> (yielding the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) derivative *ūmea* "evil").<sup id="cite_ref-VT43_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT43-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}
