**UG** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "dislike".<sup id="cite_ref-VT43_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT43-1">[1]</a></sup> [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) has suggested that the root is "*probably related*" to [UK](/wiki/UK).<sup id="cite_ref-VT48_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT48-2">[2]</a></sup> Appearing in a late manuscript concerned with negation,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> it has been suggested that the root could be related to the early negative stem <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=UGU&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="UGU (page does not exist)">UGU</a> (yielding the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) derivative *ūmea* "evil").<sup id="cite_ref-VT43_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT43-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
