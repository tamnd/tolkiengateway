**Uilos** is one of two [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) names given to the flower referred to by [Men](../M/Men.md) as **[simbelmynë](../S/Simbelmynë.md)** ("Evermind"). This name was used for the flowers that [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) found growing around the [Gate of Silver](../G/Gate_of_Silver.md) on the journey into [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), and is not recorded after the end of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). Texts referring to the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) seem to prefer the Elvish name **[alfirin](../A/Alfirin.md)** for the same white, star-like flower, but whether this signals a change of name across the ages is not known.

The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word *uilos* is not literally equivalent to "Evermind", but rather has a meaning closer to "*Everwhite*". The "Ever-" element in both these names alludes to the fact that the flowers didn't have a particular season, but instead appeared throughout the entire year.
