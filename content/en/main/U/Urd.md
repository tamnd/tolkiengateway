In [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md), **Urd** was a cold and rocky region in the far north of Middle-earth, at the southern edges of the [Iron Mountains](../I/Iron_Mountains.md).

It was inhabited mostly by [Men](../M/Men.md) called the Urdor (Urd. “Sons of Urd”), who spoke Urdorin.<sup id="cite_ref-map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-1">[1]</a></sup> They take their name from their progenitor Urd, a Father of Men who bowed down before [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Urdor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Urdor-2">[2]</a></sup> Some <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Umli&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Umli (page does not exist)">Umli</a>, called half-dwarves by the Urdor who hunted them, also lived in the higher reaches.<sup id="cite_ref-map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Urdor generally hunted and trapped near the taiga Forest of Dír in western Urd, while spending winters in clan centers by the Urd Rush river.<sup id="cite_ref-map_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-1">[1]</a></sup> In times of war, which were near-constant under the rule of [Hoarmûrath](../S/Sixth_of_the_Nazgûl.md), the Urdor had a war-host, largely consisting of war-sleds drawn by snow bears, which was known to the [Lossoth](../L/Lossoth.md) as the *Rekijoukko* (Sled-horde).<sup id="cite_ref-Urdor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Urdor-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Urd, a Father of Men, had bowed down to Morgoth and persecuted those who did not worship his master. Several centuries after Urd's death, his descendants reformed and lived under benevolent matriarchs.<sup id="cite_ref-Urdor_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Urdor-2">[2]</a></sup>

When matriarch Emûrath of Uab, leader of most Urd clans, died in the Umli Wars of [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1962&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1962 (page does not exist)">1962</a>-<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1975&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1975 (page does not exist)">1975</a>, she was succeeded by her daughter Amûrath. Amûrath's brother Hoarmûrath, influenced by his relationship with southern [Avari](../A/Avari.md) elves, wished for the clans to expand militarily, against his sister's wishes. He was ordered into exile, which he refused. After his followers slew his sister, he proclaimed himself the first King of Urd. With the support of elves and war-hungry bands, he got rid of the Urd Priestesses and was acknowledged as Lord of the Urdor in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1992&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1992 (page does not exist)">1992</a>. By [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1997&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1997 (page does not exist)">1997</a>, he had conquered most of the region, and in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1999&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1999 (page does not exist)">1999</a> he turned on his elven allies. Crushing defeats made Hoarmûrath seek help from [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-nazgul_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nazgul-3">[3]</a></sup>

Empowered by a [Ring of Power](../N/Nine_Rings.md), Hoarmûrath defeated the elves in the War in the Woods ([S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2002&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2002 (page does not exist)">2002</a>-<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2053&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2053 (page does not exist)">2053</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-nazgul_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nazgul-3">[3]</a></sup> Under his leadership, Urd also became infamous for the Urdic Invasions targetting the people of Forodwaith, pushing them westward. On Sauron's orders to hunt down all those who refused to acknowledge Sauron's dominion, the Urdor scoured those lands, enslaving or killing those they encountered.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The invasions only stopped after the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md), but would resume after the founding of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1276&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1276 (page does not exist)">1276</a>. It was in this that that an Urdic colony would be established in Angmar as well.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

By [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1640](../T/Third_Age_1640.md), the returned Hoarmûrath had strengthened the Kingdom of Urd again.<sup id="cite_ref-nazgul_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nazgul-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Surrounding lands {#Surrounding_lands}

Beyond Urd were several coastal lands, many of which were sparsely inhabited.

### Angclax {#Angclax}

**Angclax** was a coastal region to the east of Urd and to the south of Uab. Its inhabitants, the Angcla clansmen, were distantly related to the Lossoth.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

### Avikangsdar {#Avikangsdar}

**Avikangsdar** was a cold and mostly uninhabited tundra that once formed part of eastern Angband. It was located to the north-west of Urd and to the west of Uab.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

### Ôm {#Ôm}

**Ôm** was cold land south of Uax with only a few inhabitants.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

### Sharthax {#Sharthax}

**Sharthax** was a coastal region on peninsula southeast of Urd. Its indigenous Shartha tribesman relied on the ocean to survive.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Uab {#Uab}

**Uab** was a mostly uninhabited coastal region north of Angclax. Only a few Umli hunters dwelled there.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> The [Balrog](../B/Balrog.md) [Múar](../M/Múar.md) was trapped here for centuries after the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), until he was freed around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1100&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1100 (page does not exist)">1100</a>. He conquered a large part of the region, until he was finally defeated by Dwarves.<sup id="cite_ref-muar_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-muar-12">[12]</a></sup>

### Uax {#Uax}

**Uax** was a coastal region east of Uab. Beyond Umli hunters, the region was home to ice-trolls and snow-orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> In the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), the Balrog Múar was the Warlord of Uax.<sup id="cite_ref-muar_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-muar-12">[12]</a></sup>

### Uul {#Uul}

**Uul** was a vale formed by a collapsed volcano, south of Sharthax. It was surrounded by hills and taiga.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> <span></span>
