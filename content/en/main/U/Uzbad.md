<i><b>uzbad</b></i> means "lord" in [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is attested in the title *[Uzbad Khazaddûmu](/wiki/Uzbad_Khazadd%C3%BBmu)* ("Lord of Moria"). <span></span>
