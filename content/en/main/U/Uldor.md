**Uldor the Accursed** was the eldest son<sup><a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Uldor&#39;s_year_of_birth">NB</a></sup> of [Ulfang](/wiki/Ulfang) the Black.<sup id="cite_ref-Ulf_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ulf-2">[2]</a></sup>

He was called "the Accursed" most likely due to his role as the leader of the infamous treachery of his [folk](/wiki/Folk_of_Uldor "Folk of Uldor") in the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears).

## History {#History}

After entering [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), Uldor along with his father and his brothers joined the service of [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir),<sup id="cite_ref-Ulf_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ulf-2">[2]</a></sup> and settled in [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion).<sup id="cite_ref-Thar_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thar-1">[1]</a></sup>

Ultimately though, his father Ulfang's purpose from the beginning was to eventually betray the [Elves](/wiki/Elves),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> which his sons ultimately did in the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears), under Uldor's leadership - they turned against their allies, attacking them in the rear. Their betrayal proved crucial in the fight against Morgoth's forces, though, and Morgoth was victorious.<sup id="cite_ref-Nirn_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nirn-4">[4]</a></sup>

However, Uldor's brothers [Ulfast](/wiki/Ulfast) and [Ulwarth](/wiki/Ulwarth) were killed by the sons of [Bór](/wiki/B%C3%B3r), who remained loyal to the sons of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) during the battle - while Uldor himself was slain by [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor).<sup id="cite_ref-Nirn_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nirn-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Uldor* was probably not the character's true name, as it seems to be [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Ugly lord": <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ul-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ul- (page does not exist)">ul-</a></i> ("ugly") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Dor_(Noldorin)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dor (Noldorin) (page does not exist)">dor</a></i> ("king, lord").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup>

A curious thing about the name *Uldor* is that it remained unchanged from its conception to the later [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Earlier versions of the *[Nirnaeth](/wiki/Nirnaeth)* {#Earlier_versions_of_the_Nirnaeth}

According to earlier versions of the legendarium, instead of being killed by Maglor in the Battle of Unnumbered Tears, Uldor was slain by Cranthir (an earlier name for Caranthir) instead.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

It is only in *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)* (c. [1951](/wiki/1951)) that the story of Uldor's death at Maglor's hand appears, as well as the idea that Uldor summoned more Men from east of Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Uldor.27s_year_of_birth"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Uldor&#39;s_year_of_birth">Uldor&#39;s year of birth</span></h3>

In the appendix of *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings)* in <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Genealogies&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Genealogies (page does not exist)">The Genealogies</a></i> (dating from the early [1930s](/wiki/1930 "1930")), it is stated that Uldor was born in the year 125.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

When comparing the above date with its associated text *[The Earliest Annals of Beleriand](/wiki/The_Earliest_Annals_of_Beleriand)*, Uldor was born 38 years before the [Swarthy Men](/wiki/Swarthy_Men) entered Beleriand in the year 163.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>

Also, the dates found in *The Earliest Annals of Beleriand* were by and large moved 300 years into the future in *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)* (c. early [1950s](/wiki/1950 "1950")), such that, for example, an event happening in the year 195 in *The Earliest Annals* now happened in the year [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495").<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup>

### Later legendarium {#Later_legendarium}

In the <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Narn_i_Ch%C3%AEn_H%C3%BArin" title="Narn i Chîn Húrin">Narn i Chîn Húrin</a></i>, an entirely new conception of the Battle of Unnumbered Tears (*Nirnaeth Arnoediad*) emerged.

According to that conception, the eastern army of Maedhros (called *Maedros* in this text) never rendezvoused with the western army of [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) and [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) at all - in other words, they were not simply delayed due to the actions of Uldor, but were completely cut off from the western army by a large host of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and were subsequently routed.

And while the idea of the treachery of the Easterlings was retained, it was only mentioned in passing.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup>
