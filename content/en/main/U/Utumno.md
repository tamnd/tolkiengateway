**Utumno**, or **Udûn**, was a fortress of [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md) in the far north of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). It was the first and greatest of Melkor's citadels, delved in the earliest days. It was the home to hosts of demons, the fallen [Ainur](../A/Ainur.md) who allied with Melkor, and numerous monsters of corruption that were made in mockery of Middle-earth's naturally intended creatures and beings.<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Utumno was built by Melkor after his first arrival in [Arda](../A/Arda.md). The [Valar](../V/Valar.md) had by this time created the [Two Lamps](../T/Two_Lamps.md). Utumno was built under the [Iron Mountains](../I/Iron_Mountains.md), where the light of [Illuin](../I/Illuin.md) and [Ormal](../O/Ormal.md) was dim and cold. Though the Valar did not yet know it, from this place, "the blight of his \[Melkor's\] hatred flowed out thence, and the Spring of Arda was marred."<sup id="cite_ref-Beginning_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beginning-2">[2]</a></sup> Utumno was delved exceedingly deep, with pits filled with fires and great hosts of servants, caverns and vaults "hidden with deceit". As a result the lands of the far north were made desolate, and remained so in the following ages.<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-1">[1]</a></sup>

Melkor used Utumno as his base of operations from [V.Y.](../D/Days_before_days.md) [3400](../V/Valian_Year_3400.md) until [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1090](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1090.md), when the Valar assailed Melkor's fortress.<sup id="cite_ref-P2a_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P2a-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:53</sup><sup id="cite_ref-P2e_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P2e-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:74–5</sup> From there he had destroyed the [Two Lamps](../T/Two_Lamps.md), so that the [Powers](../V/Valar.md) left [Almaren](../A/Almaren.md), their dwelling-place in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), and removed into the West. He then began his corruption of Arda.<sup id="cite_ref-Beginning_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beginning-2">[2]</a></sup> Utumno was also where the first captured [Elves](../E/Elves.md) were taken and the creation of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) began as a mockery of the [Firstborn](../F/Firstborn.md).

Utumno was laid waste in [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1099](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1099.md), in [the war](../B/Battle_of_the_Powers.md) that the Valar waged against Melkor for the sake of the Elves. The [Valar](../V/Valar.md) attacked in full force, and until they broke the gates and unroofed it; Melkor hid in the uttermost pit, but they carried him to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) as their captive . Melkor was [chained](../C/Chaining_of_Melkor.md) held as a prisoner.<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-1">[1]</a></sup>

Melkor had established a second and lesser fortress at the western end of the [Ered Engrin](../I/Iron_Mountains.md) to act as the first line of defence for Utumno from Aman.<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-1">[1]</a></sup> This became [Angband](../A/Angband.md), which was at first held by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md). After the destruction of Utumno, Melkor chose to rebuild and fortify Angband as his lair.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Utumno*, contains the stem *Utumnu-*. An older version of the name, *Utumna*, is said to derive from *Tumna* ([Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), "low-lying, low, profound, deep").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the *[Annals of Aman](../A/Annals_of_Aman.md)*, Tolkien wrote: "Utupnŭ √TUP cover over; hide" and "that stronghold was ever after called Utumno the Deep-hidden";<sup id="cite_ref-P2a_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P2a-3">[3]</a></sup> while in *[The Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)*, the [root](../R/Root.md) is TUB and the original form of the name is given as *Utubnu*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

<i><b>Udûn</b></i> was the less commonly used [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name of Utumno. It meant "dark pit, Underworld"<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> or "hell".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> In *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, this name appears just once, in [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md)'s words to [Durin's Bane](../D/Durin's_Bane.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

It survived, though, as a name for [the northern valley](<Udûn_(valley).md>) of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) that lay behind the [Morannon](../M/Morannon.md). Undoubtedly, [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) chose this name in memory of his ancient master's greatest stronghold.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) was not entirely consistent with the location of Utumno, but it was always located within the northern Middle-earth, in or behind the Iron Mountains.

As noted from one of Tolkien's earlier sketch maps about Utumno from the *[Ambarkanta](../A/Ambarkanta.md)*, Utumno was previously spelled as *Utumna* and was north of the [Iron Mountains](../I/Iron_Mountains.md), towards the western end of the mountain chain.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
