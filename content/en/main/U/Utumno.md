**Utumno**, or **Udûn**, was a fortress of [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor) in the far north of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). It was the first and greatest of Melkor's citadels, delved in the earliest days. It was the home to hosts of demons, the fallen [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) who allied with Melkor, and numerous monsters of corruption that were made in mockery of Middle-earth's naturally intended creatures and beings.<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Utumno was built by Melkor after his first arrival in [Arda](/wiki/Arda). The [Valar](/wiki/Valar) had by this time created the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps). Utumno was built under the [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains), where the light of [Illuin](/wiki/Illuin) and [Ormal](/wiki/Ormal) was dim and cold. Though the Valar did not yet know it, from this place, "the blight of his \[Melkor's\] hatred flowed out thence, and the Spring of Arda was marred."<sup id="cite_ref-Beginning_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beginning-2">[2]</a></sup> Utumno was delved exceedingly deep, with pits filled with fires and great hosts of servants, caverns and vaults "hidden with deceit". As a result the lands of the far north were made desolate, and remained so in the following ages.<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-1">[1]</a></sup>

Melkor used Utumno as his base of operations from [V.Y.](/wiki/Days_before_days "Days before days") [3400](/wiki/Valian_Year_3400 "Valian Year 3400") until [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1090](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1090 "Year of the Trees 1090"), when the Valar assailed Melkor's fortress.<sup id="cite_ref-P2a_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P2a-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:53</sup><sup id="cite_ref-P2e_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P2e-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:74–5</sup> From there he had destroyed the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps), so that the [Powers](/wiki/Valar "Valar") left [Almaren](/wiki/Almaren), their dwelling-place in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and removed into the West. He then began his corruption of Arda.<sup id="cite_ref-Beginning_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beginning-2">[2]</a></sup> Utumno was also where the first captured [Elves](/wiki/Elves) were taken and the creation of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) began as a mockery of the [Firstborn](/wiki/Firstborn).

Utumno was laid waste in [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1099](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1099 "Year of the Trees 1099"), in [the war](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers "Battle of the Powers") that the Valar waged against Melkor for the sake of the Elves. The [Valar](/wiki/Valar) attacked in full force, and until they broke the gates and unroofed it; Melkor hid in the uttermost pit, but they carried him to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) as their captive . Melkor was [chained](/wiki/Chaining_of_Melkor "Chaining of Melkor") held as a prisoner.<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-1">[1]</a></sup>

Melkor had established a second and lesser fortress at the western end of the [Ered Engrin](/wiki/Iron_Mountains "Iron Mountains") to act as the first line of defence for Utumno from Aman.<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-1">[1]</a></sup> This became [Angband](/wiki/Angband), which was at first held by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). After the destruction of Utumno, Melkor chose to rebuild and fortify Angband as his lair.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Utumno*, contains the stem *Utumnu-*. An older version of the name, *Utumna*, is said to derive from *Tumna* ([Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), "low-lying, low, profound, deep").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the *[Annals of Aman](/wiki/Annals_of_Aman)*, Tolkien wrote: "Utupnŭ √TUP cover over; hide" and "that stronghold was ever after called Utumno the Deep-hidden";<sup id="cite_ref-P2a_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P2a-3">[3]</a></sup> while in *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, the [root](/wiki/Root "Root") is TUB and the original form of the name is given as *Utubnu*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

<i><b>Udûn</b></i> was the less commonly used [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name of Utumno. It meant "dark pit, Underworld"<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> or "hell".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> In *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, this name appears just once, in [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)'s words to [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

It survived, though, as a name for [the northern valley](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(valley)> "Udûn (valley)") of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) that lay behind the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon). Undoubtedly, [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) chose this name in memory of his ancient master's greatest stronghold.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") was not entirely consistent with the location of Utumno, but it was always located within the northern Middle-earth, in or behind the Iron Mountains.

As noted from one of Tolkien's earlier sketch maps about Utumno from the *[Ambarkanta](/wiki/Ambarkanta)*, Utumno was previously spelled as *Utumna* and was north of the [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains), towards the western end of the mountain chain.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
