**Uinen** was [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md)'s wife. A [Maia](../M/Maiar.md) of [Ulmo](Ulmo.md), she was known as the **Lady of the Seas**.

## Attributes {#Attributes}

Uinen loved and protected all creatures and weeds that live in the sea and brought calm seas. She could calm the tumultous waves of her husband [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md) and for this she was loved by mariners. She was revered among the [Ainur](../A/Ainur.md) by the [Númenoreans](../N/Númenoreans.md).<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is said that her hair spread through all the surface of water.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Early in the history of [Arda](../A/Arda.md), Ossë was corrupted by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) but [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) pleaded Uinen to persuade him to return to the service of Ulmo.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) of [Olwë](../O/Olwë.md) had much love for Uinen, as she and Ossë had befriended them at the river [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md) during the [Great March](../G/Great_March.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Uinen wept for the Teleri mariners after the kinslaying at [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md) and rose a storm that sunk many [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) on the stolen [Swan-ships](../S/Swan-ships.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Considered a protector and bringer of calm seas by the mariners of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), they would cry to her for help, while their respect for the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) endured.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Guild of Venturers](../G/Guild_of_Venturers.md) were also known as *[Uinendili](Uinendili.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Two explanations exist for the name *Uinen*. In *[The Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)*, the first element came from a root UY-. Several other words under this lemma mean "seaweed", though the name Uinen itself is not explained.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> A later note pertaining to the name instead suggests it is a name of non-Elvish ([Valarin](../V/Valarin.md)) origin of unknown meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) has noted that *Uinen* contains the element *[nen](../N/Nén.md)* ("water").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> It is unknown if he had access to unpublished manuscripts by his father to corroborate this suggestion or if he speculated on the matter.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Annals of Aman](../A/Annals_of_Aman.md)*, Uinen and [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md) were originally counted among the Valar.

In early versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), the character was also named *Ónen*, *Solórë*, or *Ui* (among other names), and was given the title "Queen of [Mermaids](../M/Mermaids.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
