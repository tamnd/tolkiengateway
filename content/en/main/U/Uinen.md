**Uinen** was [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB)'s wife. A [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar") of [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo), she was known as the **Lady of the Seas**.

## Attributes {#Attributes}

Uinen loved and protected all creatures and weeds that live in the sea and brought calm seas. She could calm the tumultous waves of her husband [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB) and for this she was loved by mariners. She was revered among the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) by the [Númenoreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenoreans).<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is said that her hair spread through all the surface of water.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Early in the history of [Arda](/wiki/Arda), Ossë was corrupted by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) but [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) pleaded Uinen to persuade him to return to the service of Ulmo.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) of [Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB) had much love for Uinen, as she and Ossë had befriended them at the river [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) during the [Great March](/wiki/Great_March).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Uinen wept for the Teleri mariners after the kinslaying at [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB) and rose a storm that sunk many [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) on the stolen [Swan-ships](/wiki/Swan-ships).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Considered a protector and bringer of calm seas by the mariners of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), they would cry to her for help, while their respect for the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) endured.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Guild of Venturers](/wiki/Guild_of_Venturers) were also known as *[Uinendili](/wiki/Uinendili)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Two explanations exist for the name *Uinen*. In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, the first element came from a root UY-. Several other words under this lemma mean "seaweed", though the name Uinen itself is not explained.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> A later note pertaining to the name instead suggests it is a name of non-Elvish ([Valarin](/wiki/Valarin)) origin of unknown meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) has noted that *Uinen* contains the element *[nen](/wiki/N%C3%A9n "Nén")* ("water").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> It is unknown if he had access to unpublished manuscripts by his father to corroborate this suggestion or if he speculated on the matter.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Annals of Aman](/wiki/Annals_of_Aman)*, Uinen and [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB) were originally counted among the Valar.

In early versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), the character was also named *Ónen*, *Solórë*, or *Ui* (among other names), and was given the title "Queen of [Mermaids](/wiki/Mermaids)".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
