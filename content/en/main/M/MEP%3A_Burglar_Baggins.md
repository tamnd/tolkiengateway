**MEP: Burglar Baggins** is a jigsaw puzzle in the *[Middle-earth Puzzles](/wiki/Middle-earth_Puzzles)* series, released in 1997 by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises). This puzzle uses art originally created for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.
