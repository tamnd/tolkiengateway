**METW: Grey Havens** is a game card from the set *[Middle-earth: The Wizards](/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Wizards)*, for [MECCG](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game "Middle-earth Collectible Card Game").

<i><b>Note:</b></i> *"Grey Havens" refers to the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").*
