<b><i>Mythlore</i> 26</b> (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/mythlore/vol7/iss4/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Volume 7, Issue 4</a>) is an issue of the *[Mythlore](Mythlore.md)* journal, published by the [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

#### Articles {#Articles}

- Miller, Miriam Y. “The Green Sun: A Study of Color in J.R.R. Tolkien’s The Lord of the Rings.”
- Patterson, Nancy-Lou. “The Host of Heaven: Astrological and Other Images of Divinity in the Fantasies of C.S. Lewis (Part 2).”
- Carter-Day, Deborah. “‘Coinherence’ and ‘The Terrible Good’: A Soul’s Journey to Awareness and Responsibility.”
- Walker, R. C. “The Cartography of Fantasy.”
- Schweitzer, Darrell. “The Novels of Lord Dunsany (Part 2).”

#### Features {#Features}

- GoodKnight, Glen. Editor's Corner.
- Christopher, Joe R. An Inklings Bibliography (15).
- Ellwood, Gracia Fay. “Matters of Grave Import: To Go Gentle.”
- Speth, Lee. “Cavalier Treatment: The Spellmaster of Caerleon.”
- Letters.

#### Reviews {#Reviews}

- *Unfinished Tales of Numenor and Middle-earth.* J. R. R. Tolkien. Ed. by Christopher Tolkien. Reviewed by Paul H. Kocher.
- *The Achievement of C.S. Lewis.* Thomas Howard. Reviewed by Nancy-Lou Patterson.
- *The Silmarillion.* J.R.R. Tolkien. Ed. by Christopher Tolkien. Reviewed by Thomas M. Egan.
