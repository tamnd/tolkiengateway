**Mythcon 42** was the forty-second annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme:** "Monsters, Marvels, and Minstrels: The Rise of Modern Medievalism"
- **Guests of Honor:** [Michael D.C. Drout](Michael_D.C._Drout.md) (professor), and Catherynne M. Valente (fantasy author)
