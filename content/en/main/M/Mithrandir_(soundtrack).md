<i><b>Mithrandir</b></i> is ninth track of [*The Lord Of The Rings - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack*](</wiki/The_Lord_Of_The_Rings_(Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)> "The Lord Of The Rings (Original Motion Picture Soundtrack)"). It serves as a lament for [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf).

The music was written by [Leonard Rosenman](/wiki/Leonard_Rosenman), the lyrics were written by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mark_Fleischer&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mark Fleischer (page does not exist)">Mark Fleischer</a>. A shorter version formed the background music for the twenty-first scene, "[Homage to Gandalf](/wiki/Homage_to_Gandalf)".
