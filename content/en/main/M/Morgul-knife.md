A **Morgul-knife** was a magical dagger. The [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md), the Lord of the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md), used one such Morgul-knife to stab [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) on [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md).

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/C/Campbell_White_-_Morgul-Knife.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/ea/Campbell_White_-_Morgul-Knife.jpg/200px-Campbell_White_-_Morgul-Knife.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="244" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/ea/Campbell_White_-_Morgul-Knife.jpg/300px-Campbell_White_-_Morgul-Knife.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/ea/Campbell_White_-_Morgul-Knife.jpg/400px-Campbell_White_-_Morgul-Knife.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1920" data-file-height="2344"/></a><figcaption><i>Morgul-knife</i> by <a class="external text" href="https://www.campbellwhitedesign.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Campbell White</a></figcaption></figure>

Morgul-knives were magical weapons, which remained in the wound of the victim and turned the victim into a [wraith](../W/Wraiths.md) that was weaker than the Nazgûl and thus under the rule of the Nazgûl and of Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-1">[1]</a></sup>

On 6 October [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> during his journey to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) with [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md), [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) was stabbed by the [Lord of the Nazgûl'](../W/Witch-king.md) with a Morgul-knife on [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md). The Morgul-knife that was used by the Lord of the Nazgûl was long and thin<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-3">[3]</a></sup> and glowed with a pale light.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It had an evil inscription on its hilt that was probably invisible for [Men](Men.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Glorfindel_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glorfindel-5">[5]</a></sup> The point of the Morgul-knife had broken off<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-3">[3]</a></sup> and had remained within the wound and was working inwards threatening to turn Frodo into a wraith.<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-1">[1]</a></sup> When Aragorn managed to drive away the Nazgûl, the Lord of the Nazgûl left behind his Morgul-knife. When dawn came the blade of the Morgul-knife seemed to melt in the growing light of the sun and vanished so that only its hilt was left. Aragorn collected leaves of the herb *[athelas](../A/Athelas.md)* and bathed the wound with water in which the leaves had been soaked. However, *athelas* only had limited power to ease the effects of the wound, because Frodo felt a lessening of the pain and the sense of frozen cold.<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-3">[3]</a></sup>

When they met the Elf [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) on 18 October [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> he shuddered when he touched the hilt, but was able to see the evil inscription on it and to advise Aragorn to touch it as little as possible. Glorfindel declared that his healing skill was not sufficient to heal the wound of this weapon, but Frodo felt a lessening of the chill, an easing of the pain, warmth returned from his shoulder to his hand and the world seemed to grow lighter and he was able to see things better again.<sup id="cite_ref-Glorfindel_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glorfindel-5">[5]</a></sup>

After Frodo arrived in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) on 20 October [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md) [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) was able to locate and remove the shard and to heal the wound in the night before Frodo woke up on October 24 [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-1">[1]</a></sup>

One year after the stabbing with the Morgul-knife<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> the wound in Frodo's shoulder ached and the memory of darkness lay heavily on Frodo. Frodo's eyes seemed to neither see his companions nor the world around him. However, the pain and unease passed by the end of the next day.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

On the second anniversary of the stabbing with the Morgul-knife Frodo fell ill,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> turned very pale and seemed to look far away, but it passed and Frodo was quite normal again on the next day.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

Frodo's eventual departure over the sea to [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) on 29 September [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3021](../T/Third_Age_3021.md)<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> gave him a chance to be fully healed from his wounds.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*[Morgul](Morgul.md)* means "necromancy",<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> "black arts" or "sorcery" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>After the Witch-king stabs Frodo, the blade disappears in an instant, rather than seeming to melt.</dd></dl>

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The enchanted Morgul-knife, wielded by the Ringwraiths, strikes as a short sword with many additional effects on a character.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The &#34;Morgul-knife&#34; modifies a Nazgûl&#39;s prowess, and affects the corruption points of a character. &#34;Morgul-blade&#34; is a Permanent-event card, playable by a Ringwraith or a Ringwraith follower.</dd></dl>

<b>2001-03: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Artist <a href="../J/John_Howe.md" title="John Howe">John Howe</a> made the original design of the daggers of the Ringwraiths.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> They are straight, double-edged daggers with a prominent taper toward the tip. The crossguard is notably turned downward, with a couple of spikes protruding from the center line. It features a small pommel, also with decorative spikes.</dd></dl>
