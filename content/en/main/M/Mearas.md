The **Mearas** was the name given by the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) in [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) for a race of exceptional [horses](/wiki/Horses "Horses").<sup id="cite_ref-Riders_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

The Mearas were strong, wild, and intelligent. They would bear no man but the kings or princes of the Mark (with [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) as a notable exception). Their lifespan was as long as men.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[2]</a></sup>

[Felaróf](/wiki/Felar%C3%B3f) and [Snowmane](/wiki/Snowmane) were white, whereas [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax) was grey.

## History {#History}

According to tradition, the ancestors of the Mearas were brought from the [West](/wiki/Uttermost_West "Uttermost West") by [Béma](/wiki/B%C3%A9ma).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[2]</a></sup> The horses of the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin) were special. During the [Angmar War](/wiki/Angmar_War), the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Host_of_the_West" title="Host of the West">Host of the West</a> included noble [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen) riders, and when they landed at [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) their horses were especially praised.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2501](/wiki/Third_Age_2501 "Third Age 2501")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup> [Léod](/wiki/L%C3%A9od) of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) captured and tried to tame the first of the Mearas, only to be killed when he tried to mount it. His son, [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl), however managed to ride it and named it [Felaróf](/wiki/Felar%C3%B3f). Eorl came with this horse south to [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) and the Mearas were his descendants. Felaróf was said to be able to understand men's speech.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[2]</a></sup>

In [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-6">[4]</a></sup> the greatest of the Mearas, [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax), consented to be ridden by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf).<sup id="cite_ref-Riders_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-1">[1]</a></sup> Gandalf rode Shadowfax throughout the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word Mearas comes from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *mēaras*, simply meaning "horses". Its singular form would be <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/mearh#Old_English" title="wiktionary:mearh">mearh</a></i> or *marh*.

The root *marh* can be seen in the names of the [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen) *[Marhwini](/wiki/Marhwini)* and *[Marhari](/wiki/Marhari)* (which are actually [Gothic](/wiki/Gothic))<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup> as well as *[Marcho](/wiki/Marcho)*.
