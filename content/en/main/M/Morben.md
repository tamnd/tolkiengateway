**Morben** (pl. *Morbin* or *Moerbin*) was a term used amongst [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) (and [Laegil](/wiki/Laegil)) that lived in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). *Morbin* were the [Avari](/wiki/Avari) or anybody else dwelling outside Beleriand or entering Beleriand from outside.<sup id="cite_ref-WJB37X_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJB37X-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Morbin* were usually [elves](/wiki/Elves "Elves") (and other peoples) who were hostile to [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) and [Edain](/wiki/Edain). But the word was at once used of the [Easterlings](</wiki/Easterlings_(First_Age)> "Easterlings (First Age)") who came to the West before the [Battle of Nirnaeth](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad "Nirnaeth Arnoediad").<sup id="cite_ref-WJB37X_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJB37X-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *Morben* derives from [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) words *[morn](/wiki/Morn "Morn")* 'black, dark' and *[pen](/wiki/Pen "Pen")* 'one, somebody, someone'.<sup id="cite_ref-WJA362_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJA362-3">[3]</a></sup> It is equivalent to [Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"). [Moriquendi](/wiki/Moriquendi), but with a different meaning and usage.
