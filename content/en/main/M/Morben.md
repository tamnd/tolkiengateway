**Morben** (pl. *Morbin* or *Moerbin*) was a term used amongst [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) (and [Laegil](../L/Laegil.md)) that lived in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). *Morbin* were the [Avari](../A/Avari.md) or anybody else dwelling outside Beleriand or entering Beleriand from outside.<sup id="cite_ref-WJB37X_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJB37X-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Morbin* were usually [elves](../E/Elves.md) (and other peoples) who were hostile to [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) and [Edain](../E/Edain.md). But the word was at once used of the [Easterlings](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>) who came to the West before the [Battle of Nirnaeth](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJB37X_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJB37X-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *Morben* derives from [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) words *[morn](Morn.md)* 'black, dark' and *[pen](../P/Pen.md)* 'one, somebody, someone'.<sup id="cite_ref-WJA362_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJA362-3">[3]</a></sup> It is equivalent to [Q](../Q/Quenya.md). [Moriquendi](Moriquendi.md), but with a different meaning and usage.
