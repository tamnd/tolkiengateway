**Marhwini** was a [Northman](/wiki/Northmen "Northmen") and a member of the noble family of the [Kingdom of Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion "Rhovanion"). His father [Marhari](/wiki/Marhari) was a descendant of King [Vidugavia](/wiki/Vidugavia) of Rhovanion.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1851](/wiki/Third_Age_1851 "Third Age 1851"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Rhovanion was invaded by a tribe of [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) known as the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders). The soldiers of the Kingdom of Rhovanion, together with the armies of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), met the Wainriders at the [Battle of the Plains](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Plains) in [1856](/wiki/Third_Age_1856 "Third Age 1856"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> which was a disaster. The [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor), [Narmacil II](/wiki/Narmacil_II), was killed and Marhwini's father Marhari died leading the rearguard. Much of the Kingdom of Rhovanion was lost and its people were enslaved. Some Northmen escaped, and Marhwini led one band of refugees to the west.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-4">[4]</a></sup>

The refugees wandered until they reached the untamed land between the eastern bank of the [River Anduin](/wiki/Anduin "Anduin") and the western edge of [Mirkwood Forest](/wiki/Mirkwood "Mirkwood"). Settling there in the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin), the refugees would become the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), the ancestors of the mighty [Riders of Rohan](/wiki/Riders_of_Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1899](/wiki/Third_Age_1899 "Third Age 1899"),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Marhwini warned [Calimehtar](</wiki/Calimehtar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Calimehtar (King of Gondor)") that the Wainriders were plotting to raid [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) over the [Undeeps](/wiki/Undeeps) but the enslaved Northmen also prepared a revolt against the Wainriders. Calimehtar provoked the Wainriders out of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien); the Wainriders came down with all the strength that they could spare, and Calimehtar gave way before them, drawing them away from their homes. At the peak of the battle, horsemen that Calimehtar had sent over the [Undeeps](/wiki/Undeeps) (left unguarded by the enemy) joined with a great [éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored "Éored") led by Marhwini and he drove the Wainriders back to the [East](/wiki/East).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) *Marhwini* is "[Gothic](/wiki/Gothic) in form". The first element of the name is the Gothic word *marh* ("horse"), which corresponds to [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *mearh* whose plural *[mearas](/wiki/Mearas "Mearas")* is used in *The Lord of the Rings*. The second element *wini* ("friend") corresponds to Old English *wine*, which is used in the names of some of the [Kings of the Mark](/wiki/King_of_Rohan "King of Rohan").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) and [Arden R. Smith](/wiki/Arden_R._Smith) suggest that *Marhwini* is early [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) in form.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

David Salo suggests that *Marhwini* means "friend of horses", but could also mean "friend with a horse", in other words a companion-in-arms versed in the equestrian arts.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
