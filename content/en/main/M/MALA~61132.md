**MALA** is a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "crush, squeeze, pulp, hurt, damage". Along with the root [TARA](/wiki/TARA), MALA makes up the words [Maltar](/wiki/Maltar), which is the name of [Aulë’s](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB "Aulë") hammer, and [Malkarauki](/wiki/Malkarauki).<sup id="cite_ref-Malt_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Malt-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Malk_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Malk-2">[2]</a></sup>
