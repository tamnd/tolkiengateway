<i><b>Master of the Rings: The Unauthorized Story Behind J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s &#39;Lord of the Rings&#39;</b></i> is an 80 minute documentary by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Robert_Di_Napoli&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Robert Di Napoli (page does not exist)">Robert Di Napoli</a>, released on [December 4](/wiki/4_December "4 December") [2001](/wiki/2001).

## Contents {#Contents}

The documentary covers such aspects of the Tolkien epic as its literary influences (including *[Beowulf](</wiki/Beowulf_(poem)> "Beowulf (poem)")*), character and plot analysis, inspirations on other arts (such as the progressive rock of the 1970s), and enduring popularity.

Features an art gallery booklet of [the Brothers Hildebrandt](/wiki/The_Brothers_Hildebrandt "The Brothers Hildebrandt") and a music CD (*[Landscapes of Middle-earth](/wiki/Landscapes_of_Middle-earth)*) with <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rick_Wakeman&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rick Wakeman (page does not exist)">Rick Wakeman</a>.
