<i><b>Master of the Rings: The Unauthorized Story Behind J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s &#39;Lord of the Rings&#39;</b></i> is an 80 minute documentary by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Robert_Di_Napoli&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Robert Di Napoli (page does not exist)">Robert Di Napoli</a>, released on [December 4](../_/4_December.md) [2001](../_/2001.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

The documentary covers such aspects of the Tolkien epic as its literary influences (including *[Beowulf](<../B/Beowulf_(poem).md>)*), character and plot analysis, inspirations on other arts (such as the progressive rock of the 1970s), and enduring popularity.

Features an art gallery booklet of [the Brothers Hildebrandt](../T/The_Brothers_Hildebrandt.md) and a music CD (*[Landscapes of Middle-earth](../L/Landscapes_of_Middle-earth.md)*) with <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Rick_Wakeman&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rick Wakeman (page does not exist)">Rick Wakeman</a>.
