<b><i>Mythlore</i> 10</b> (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/mythlore/vol3/iss2/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Volume 3, Issue 2</a>) is an issue of the *[Mythlore](Mythlore.md)* journal, published by the [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md). It is the same as *[Tolkien Journal](../T/Tolkien_Journal.md)* issue 17.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Glen GoodKnight](../G/Glen_GoodKnight.md)'s article "Death and the Desire for Deathlessness: The Counsel of Elrond" contains a [letter dated 17 November 1957 to Dr. Herbert Schiro](../L/Letter_to_Herbert_Schiro.md) (p. 19), in which [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) discusses allegory in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and argues that is about death and deathlessness, not power and dominion.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> This letter was later partially published in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* as [Letter 203](../L/Letter_203.md).
