<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: Mordor</b></i> is a sourcebook supplement for [Games Workshop](/wiki/Games_Workshop)'s *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game)*.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>In the land of Mordor, dread forces gather, ready at Sauron&#39;s command to invade and destroy the lands of the Free peoples. Orcs and trolls mass at the Black Gate, Shelob and Uruk-hai guard the secret pass, while the Nazgul and the mysterious Black Numenoreans march from the fortress of Minas Morgul.
</p><p>This 72-page The Lord of The Rings Sourcebook, contains all of the rules and information you need to field a force from Mordor; as well as detailed background information, hobby advice, accompanying artwork and &#39;Eavy Metal showcase.
</p><p>This is a supplement for Games Workshop&#39;s The Lord of The rings Strategy Battle Game - you will need a copy of the rules in order to play.
</p>
</blockquote>
