**Mentha Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland).

## History {#History}

Mentha was the second child of [Marmadas Brandybuck](/wiki/Marmadas_Brandybuck). She was present at [Bilbo's Birthday Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mentha" title="wikipedia:Mentha">Mentha</a> is the Latin name of the genus "mint".
