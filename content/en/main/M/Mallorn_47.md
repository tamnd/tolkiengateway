<b><i>Mallorn</i> 47</b> is the forty-seventh issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s biannual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Spring [2009](../_/2009.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Cover Art**
    - By: Becky Hitchin
- **Editorial**
    - "The ode goes ever on"
        - By: [Henry Gee](../H/Henry_Gee.md)
- **Letters**
- **Reviews**
    - "The shores of Faery, revisited": *[On Fairy Stories](../T/Tolkien_On_Fairy-stories~23734.md)*
        - By: [David Doughan](../D/David_Doughan.md)
    - "The memetics of culture": *How Tradition Works* by [Michael D.C. Drout](Michael_D.C._Drout.md)
        - By: Johanna Kershaw
    - "Fear and loathing in Middle-earth": *The Mirror Crack'd* edited by Lynn Forest-Hill
        - By: Joel Franz
    - "Ringing ever onwards": *[Proceedings of the 2005 Tolkien Conference](../T/The_Ring_Goes_Ever_On%253A_Proceedings_of_the_Tolkien_2005_Conference.md)*
        - By: [Colin Duriez](../C/Colin_Duriez.md)
    - "Echoes of strange music": *The Darkling and the Lady* by Christine Davidson
        - By: Lynn Forest-Hill
    - "Away with the fairies": *[Tolkien, Race and Cultural History](../T/Tolkien,_Race_and_Cultural_History.md)* by [Dimitra Fimi](../D/Dimitra_Fimi.md)
        - By: [Henry Gee](../H/Henry_Gee.md)
- **Commentary**
    - "The ogre in the dungeon"
        - By: [Alex Lewis](../A/Alex_Lewis.md)
    - "The curious case of [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) and the *[Palantír](../P/Palantír.md)*"
        - By: Jessica Yates
    - "The realm of [Faërie](../F/Faerie.md), and the shadow of Homer in Narnia and [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md)"
        - By: Louis Markos
    - "Real and imaginary history in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*"
        - By: Franco Manni
    - "[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s further indebtedness to Haggard"
        - By: Dale Nelson
- **Poetry**

        - By: Julie Sinclair
    - *[Neldoreth](../N/Neldoreth.md) Revisited*
        - By: Carol Brownlow
    - *The Forest*
        - By: Carol Brownlow
- **Fiction**

        - By: rpg

        - By: Lynn Forest-Hill
- **Well, I'm Back**
