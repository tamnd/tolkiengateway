<i><b>Middle-earth Minstrel: Essays on Music in Tolkien</b></i> is a collection of essays about music in Middle-earth, edited by [Bradford Lee Eden](/wiki/Bradford_Lee_Eden).

- [Jason Fisher](/wiki/Jason_Fisher): "Horns of Dawn: The Tradition of Alliterative Verse in Rohan"

- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Wilkin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Peter Wilkin (page does not exist)">Peter Wilkin</a>: "Ǽfre me strongode longað: Songs of Exile in the Mortal Realms"
- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Danielle_Richards&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Danielle Richards (page does not exist)">Danielle Richards</a>: "J.R.R. Tolkien: A Fortunate Rhythm"
- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Deanna_Delmar_Evans&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Deanna Delmar Evans (page does not exist)">Deanna Delmar Evans</a>: "Tolkien's Unfinished 'Lay of Lúthien' and the Middle English Sir Orfeo"
