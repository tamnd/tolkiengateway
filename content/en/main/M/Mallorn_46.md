<b><i>Mallorn</i> 46</b> is the forty-sixth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s biannual journal *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")*, published in Autumn [2008](/wiki/2008).

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Cover Art**
    - By: [Ruth Lacon](/wiki/Ruth_Lacon)
- **Guest Editorial**
    - "Thoughts on Tolkien"
        - By: Tanith Lee
- **Letters to the Editor**
- **Reviews**
    - "Telling Tales": *Tales Before Narnia* and *[Tales Before Tolkien](/wiki/Tales_Before_Tolkien)*
        - By: Rebecca Addy
    - "Of Baggins, Bladorthin and Pryftan": *[The History of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit)*
        - By: [Charles Noad](/wiki/Charles_Noad)
    - "'W' is for 'Words'": *[The Ring of Words](/wiki/The_Ring_of_Words)*
        - By: Carol Brownlow
- **Commentary**
    - "*The Battle of the Eastern Field*"
        - By: Maggie Burns
- **Poetry**
    - *[The Battle of the Eastern Field](/wiki/The_Battle_of_the_Eastern_Field)*
        - By: [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)
- **Commentary**
    - "'Seven signatures of witnesses in red ink'": Roman Law in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")
        - By: Murray Smith
    - "Teaching and Studying Tolkien"
        - By: [Dimitra Fimi](/wiki/Dimitra_Fimi)
    - "A Dialogue of Worlds": [Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis "C.S. Lewis")'s <i><a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perelandra" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Perelandra</a></i> and <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Lindsay_(novelist)" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Lidsay</a>'s <i><a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Voyage_to_Arcturus" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Voyage to Arcturus</a></i>
        - By: Frank Wilson
    - "Strange Visions of Mountains - The Mountane Motif in Tolkien's Fiction"
        - By: Maria Rafaella Benvenuto
- **Fiction**
    - *Solstice*
        - By: John Gilbey
    - *In the Shadow of the Dragon's Wing*
        - By: Jeff Crook
    - *Calinnen*
        - By: Tanith Lee
- **Well, I'm Back**
    - "Tibbles, Fauna of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor)"
        - By: [David Doughan](/wiki/David_Doughan)
- **Back Cover**
    - By: Lorenzo Daniele

- **Inside Art**
    - By: John Gilbey, [Jef Murray](/wiki/Jef_Murray) and Bonnie Robinson
