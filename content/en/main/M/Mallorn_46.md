<b><i>Mallorn</i> 46</b> is the forty-sixth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s biannual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Autumn [2008](../_/2008.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Cover Art**
    - By: [Ruth Lacon](../R/Ruth_Lacon.md)
- **Guest Editorial**
    - "Thoughts on Tolkien"
        - By: Tanith Lee
- **Letters to the Editor**
- **Reviews**
    - "Telling Tales": *Tales Before Narnia* and *[Tales Before Tolkien](../T/Tales_Before_Tolkien.md)*
        - By: Rebecca Addy
    - "Of Baggins, Bladorthin and Pryftan": *[The History of The Hobbit](../T/The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)*
        - By: [Charles Noad](../C/Charles_Noad.md)
    - "'W' is for 'Words'": *[The Ring of Words](../T/The_Ring_of_Words.md)*
        - By: Carol Brownlow
- **Commentary**
    - "*The Battle of the Eastern Field*"
        - By: Maggie Burns
- **Poetry**
    - *[The Battle of the Eastern Field](../T/The_Battle_of_the_Eastern_Field.md)*
        - By: [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)
- **Commentary**
    - "'Seven signatures of witnesses in red ink'": Roman Law in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md)
        - By: Murray Smith
    - "Teaching and Studying Tolkien"
        - By: [Dimitra Fimi](../D/Dimitra_Fimi.md)
    - "A Dialogue of Worlds": [Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md)'s <i><a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perelandra" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Perelandra</a></i> and <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Lindsay_(novelist)" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Lidsay</a>'s <i><a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Voyage_to_Arcturus" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Voyage to Arcturus</a></i>
        - By: Frank Wilson
    - "Strange Visions of Mountains - The Mountane Motif in Tolkien's Fiction"
        - By: Maria Rafaella Benvenuto
- **Fiction**
    - *Solstice*
        - By: John Gilbey
    - *In the Shadow of the Dragon's Wing*
        - By: Jeff Crook
    - *Calinnen*
        - By: Tanith Lee
- **Well, I'm Back**
    - "Tibbles, Fauna of [Mordor](Mordor.md)"
        - By: [David Doughan](../D/David_Doughan.md)
- **Back Cover**
    - By: Lorenzo Daniele

- **Inside Art**
    - By: John Gilbey, [Jef Murray](../J/Jef_Murray.md) and Bonnie Robinson
