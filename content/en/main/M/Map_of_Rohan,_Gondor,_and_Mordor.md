The **Map of Rohan, Gondor, and Mordor**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> is an unnamed map reproduced in *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)* and in one-volume editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Creation {#Creation}

Tolkien said to [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) that a map of Gondor would be needed for the second and third volumes<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> which would clarify the courses of the characters ([Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) and [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md)) not possible with the [general map](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-L187_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L187-4">[3]</a></sup> He had however by April [1955](../_/1955.md) a lot of trouble displaying the distances correctly, and considered quitting the attempt.<sup id="cite_ref-L162_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L162-5">[4]</a></sup> As he reminisced, it took him

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>many days, the last three virtually without food or bed, to drawing re-scaling and adjusting a large map, at which [Christopher] then worked for 24 hours (6 a.m. to 6 a.m. without bed) in re-drawing just in time.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>J.R.R. Tolkien<sup id="cite_ref-L187_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L187-4">[3]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

More recently the map was redrawn in [1965](../_/1965.md) by [Barbara Remington](../B/Barbara_Remington.md), [1988](../_/1988.md) by [Shelly Shapiro](../S/Shelly_Shapiro.md), and then by [Stephen Raw](../S/Stephen_Raw.md) for the new editions of the *Lord of the Rings* since [1994](../_/1994.md).

<br>

## Description {#Description}

The map is 5 times enlarged from the [general Map of Middle-earth](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md), although C. Tolkien failed to insert the scale for the final drawing.<sup id="cite_ref-L162_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L162-5">[4]</a></sup> It depicts most of the regions where the events of both *The Two Towers* and *The Return of the King* took place, and traversed by the characters.

Unlike the other general or regional maps that were semi-pictorial,<sup id="cite_ref-L161_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L161-6">[5]</a></sup> this one was drawn like a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topographic_map" title="wikipedia:Topographic map">topographic map</a>, with the mountains depicted by detailed <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contour_line" title="wikipedia:Contour line">contour lines</a>, representing elevation.

Its main feature is the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md), surrounded by most of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) (as it was in the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md)), south-eastern [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), and north-western [Mordor](Mordor.md).
