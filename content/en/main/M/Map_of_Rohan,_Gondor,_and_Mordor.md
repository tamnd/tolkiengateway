The **Map of Rohan, Gondor, and Mordor**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> is an unnamed map reproduced in *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)* and in one-volume editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Creation {#Creation}

Tolkien said to [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) that a map of Gondor would be needed for the second and third volumes<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> which would clarify the courses of the characters ([Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) and [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo)) not possible with the [general map](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth "General Map of Middle-earth").<sup id="cite_ref-L187_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L187-4">[3]</a></sup> He had however by April [1955](/wiki/1955) a lot of trouble displaying the distances correctly, and considered quitting the attempt.<sup id="cite_ref-L162_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L162-5">[4]</a></sup> As he reminisced, it took him

More recently the map was redrawn in [1965](/wiki/1965) by [Barbara Remington](/wiki/Barbara_Remington), [1988](/wiki/1988) by [Shelly Shapiro](/wiki/Shelly_Shapiro), and then by [Stephen Raw](/wiki/Stephen_Raw) for the new editions of the *Lord of the Rings* since [1994](/wiki/1994).

<br>

## Description {#Description}

The map is 5 times enlarged from the [general Map of Middle-earth](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth "General Map of Middle-earth"), although C. Tolkien failed to insert the scale for the final drawing.<sup id="cite_ref-L162_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L162-5">[4]</a></sup> It depicts most of the regions where the events of both *The Two Towers* and *The Return of the King* took place, and traversed by the characters.

Unlike the other general or regional maps that were semi-pictorial,<sup id="cite_ref-L161_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L161-6">[5]</a></sup> this one was drawn like a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topographic_map" title="wikipedia:Topographic map">topographic map</a>, with the mountains depicted by detailed <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contour_line" title="wikipedia:Contour line">contour lines</a>, representing elevation.

Its main feature is the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains), surrounded by most of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) (as it was in the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age)), south-eastern [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), and north-western [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).
