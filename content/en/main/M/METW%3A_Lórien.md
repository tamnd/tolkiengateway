<i><b>Note:</b></i> *"Lórien" refers to [Lórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien") of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").*
