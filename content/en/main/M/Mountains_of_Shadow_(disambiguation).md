**Mountains of Shadow** can refer to two mountain ranges:

- The [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin), which bordered [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) and [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim) in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).
- The [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath), which bordered [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) to the south and west.
