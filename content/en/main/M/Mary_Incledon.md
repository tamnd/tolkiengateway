**Frieda <u>Mary</u> Incledon** (1895-1940) was a cousin of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). Her mother Edith Mary ('May') Incledon (née Suffield) was an older sister of [Mabel Tolkien](/wiki/Mabel_Tolkien). Her sister was <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Marjorie_Incledon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Marjorie Incledon (page does not exist)">Marjorie Incledon</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup>

Mary and her sister Marjorie invented the [Animalic](/wiki/Animalic) language and taught some of it to Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Vice_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vice-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:8–9,40;note 18</sup> She also created the [Nevbosh](/wiki/Nevbosh) language with Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Vice_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vice-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:11</sup>

Later on, Mary Incledon became godmother to [John Tolkien](/wiki/John_Tolkien). She lived alone in London after having converted to Roman Catholicism. She died of cancer in 1940 at the age of 45.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Vice_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vice-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:40, note 18</sup>
