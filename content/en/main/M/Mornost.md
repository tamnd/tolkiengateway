In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), **Mornost** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Dark Fortress*) was a citadel of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in the far north of the Fire Tundra in [Forodwaith](/wiki/Northern_Waste "Northern Waste"). After the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), it had been reduced to a ruin.<sup id="cite_ref-fortress_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fortress-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the fortress was destroyed, it caused a collapse of the [Under-deeps](/wiki/Under-deeps) underneath, leading to the formation of the the Rifts of the Under-deeps ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Cirith-i-Nudevyn*, [La.](/wiki/Languages_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing#Labba "Languages in Middle-earth Role Playing") *Sisäänkäytävä Alateihin*), two massive ravines which spanned from Mornost in the north and ended a bit before [Morgoth's Well](/wiki/Durlach "Durlach") in the south. Vile creatures sometimes emerged from the ravines, but most denizens of the Under-deeps preferred the warmth below.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The proper entrance to this part of the Under-deeps was located in the collapsed lowest cellars of Mornost.<sup id="cite_ref-fortress_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fortress-1">[1]</a></sup>

The black stones with which the fortress was made burned like fire when touched by exposed skin, though they provided no actual warmth. The cause of this was attributed to either a specific bane woven into the citadel, or merely the lingering malice of Morgoth.<sup id="cite_ref-fortress_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fortress-1">[1]</a></sup>
