**Martyn Sanderson** ([1938](/wiki/1938)-[2009](/wiki/2009)) was a New Zealand actor who portrayed the [Gate Keeper](/wiki/Harry_Goatleaf "Harry Goatleaf") stationed at the west-gate of [Bree](/wiki/Bree) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.

In June [2007](/wiki/2007), he and fourteen other actors filed <a class="external text" href="https://us.imdb.com/news/ni0058227/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">a lawsuit</a> against [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) over merchandising royalties.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The action was settled out of court in October [2008](/wiki/2008).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
