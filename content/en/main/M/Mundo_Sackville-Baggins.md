**Mundo Sackville-Baggins** is a character created specifically for [The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md).

He was mistakenly identified by the [Blackwolds](../B/Blackwolds.md) to be the Hobbit carrying the Ring<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> that they were looking for and taken to [Bree-land](../B/Bree-land.md) in hopes of selling him to the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md). He was rescued by [Strider](../S/Strider.md) and [Amdir](<../A/Amdir_(Shadows_of_Angmar).md>), in which Amdir was stabbed by a Nazgûl. After the Nazgûl was driven off, they fled to [Archet](../A/Archet.md) to recover. Shortly after, Archet was attacked by the Blackwolds and burned down. In the wake, Amdir was taken by the Nazgûl to become a <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Cargul&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cargul (page does not exist)">Cargul</a>.

When he returned to [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) he requested compensation for his treatment at Archet from the Mayor of [Michel Delving](Michel_Delving.md), [Will Whitfoot](../W/Will_Whitfoot.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

He is the nephew of [Lobelia Sackville-Baggins](../L/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins.md).
