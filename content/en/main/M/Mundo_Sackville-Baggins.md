**Mundo Sackville-Baggins** is a character created specifically for [The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online).

He was mistakenly identified by the [Blackwolds](/wiki/Blackwolds) to be the Hobbit carrying the Ring<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> that they were looking for and taken to [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land) in hopes of selling him to the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl). He was rescued by [Strider](/wiki/Strider) and [Amdir](</wiki/Amdir_(Shadows_of_Angmar)> "Amdir (Shadows of Angmar)"), in which Amdir was stabbed by a Nazgûl. After the Nazgûl was driven off, they fled to [Archet](/wiki/Archet) to recover. Shortly after, Archet was attacked by the Blackwolds and burned down. In the wake, Amdir was taken by the Nazgûl to become a <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Cargul&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cargul (page does not exist)">Cargul</a>.

When he returned to [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") he requested compensation for his treatment at Archet from the Mayor of [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving), [Will Whitfoot](/wiki/Will_Whitfoot).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

He is the nephew of [Lobelia Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins).
