**Mount Doom**, or **Orodruin**, was a volcano in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).

## History {#History}

[Melkor](/wiki/Melkor) created Mount Doom in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), and the name "*Mordor*" may have been given to the surrounding land before Sauron settled there because of its eruptions.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> When [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) chose the land of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) as his dwelling-place in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), Orodruin was the reason for his choice. He "*used the fire that welled there from the heart of the earth in his sorceries and his forging.*" The most famous result of his forging, and in fact the only one we know of for sure, was the One Ring.

The mountain erupted in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3429](/wiki/Second_Age_3429 "Second Age 3429"), signalling Sauron's attack on [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and it took the name [Amon Amarth](/wiki/Amon_Amarth), "Mount Doom".

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2954](/wiki/Third_Age_2954 "Third Age 2954"), Mount Doom reawakened and the last inhabitants of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) terrified fled over [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> From then on it erupted sporadically until the end of the Age.

The [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring)'s [quest](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Ring "Quest for the Ring") in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) was to destroy the Ring at Mount Doom.

## Geography {#Geography}

It stood alone in an empty plain, the [Plateau of Gorgoroth](/wiki/Plateau_of_Gorgoroth) and was connected to the [Dark Tower](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr "Barad-dûr") with [Sauron's Road](/wiki/Sauron%27s_Road), rising about 4500 feet with its base about 3000 feet tall.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-K_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-K-4">[4]</a></sup> The Road approached the east side of the base at a causeway and then wound up like a snake; at that point, the Road seemed damaged by the lava and re-repaired several times.<sup id="cite_ref-K_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-K-4">[4]</a></sup>

Inside its cone, were the [Sammath Naur](/wiki/Cracks_of_Doom "Cracks of Doom") leading to the Crack of Doom, a fiery chasm where [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") was forged.

"Mount Doom" is the [Common Speech](/wiki/Westron "Westron") translation of <i><b>Amon Amarth</b></i> in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor),<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-5">[5]</a></sup> from *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* ("hill")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and *[amarth](/wiki/Amarth "Amarth")* ("fate, doom").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

The name was given because the volcano was linked in ancient and little-understood prophecies with the final end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), when [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") was found again.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-5">[5]</a></sup>

Its original [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name was <i><b>Orodruin</b></i>, glossed as "burning mountain"<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>, "mountain of the red flame"<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and "mountain of blazing fire"<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> The name likely consists of *[orod](/wiki/Ered "Ered")* ("mountain") + *[ruin](/wiki/Ruin "Ruin")* ("fiery red").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Theories {#Theories}

According to [Karen Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Fonstad), Mount Doom was obviously a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/stratovolcano" title="wikipedia:stratovolcano">stratovolcano</a>, composited by alternating layers of ash and lava. Towering at only 4500 ft, it was relatively short.<sup id="cite_ref-K_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-K-4">[4]</a></sup>

<dl><dd><b>1 September: <i><a href="/wiki/A_Shadow_of_the_Past" title="A Shadow of the Past">A Shadow of the Past</a></i>:</b>
<dl><dd>During the late <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">Second Age</a>, Orodruin is shown within the <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Southlands</a> to be a dormant volcano with a snow cap. Its slopes are covered with forests and the surrounding <a href="/wiki/Plateau_of_Gorgoroth" title="Plateau of Gorgoroth">plateau</a> is inhabited by the <a href="/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans" title="Pre-Númenóreans">Southlanders</a>.</dd></dl></dd>
<dd><b>30 September: <i><a href="/wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(episode)" title="Udûn (episode)">Udûn</a></i>:</b>
<dl><dd>After the battle of <a href="/wiki/Tirharad" title="Tirharad">Tirharad</a>, <a href="/wiki/Waldreg" title="Waldreg">Waldreg</a> activates a mechanism at the ruins of the <a href="/wiki/Watchtower_of_Ostirith" title="Watchtower of Ostirith">Watchtower of Ostirith</a>, triggering a flood of water to travel to Orodruin though underground <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orc-made</a> tunnels. The collision between the flood and the fires of Orodruin results in a volcanic eruption, which destroys the Southlands.</dd></dl></dd>
<dd><b>7 October: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Eye" title="The Eye">The Eye</a></i>:</b>
<dl><dd>After the eruption of Orodruin, the Southlanders flee the Plateau of Gorgoroth. During this escape, <a href="/wiki/Ontamo" title="Ontamo">Ontamo</a> is killed, <a href="/wiki/Tar-M%C3%ADriel" title="Tar-Míriel">Queen Regent Míriel</a> is blinded and <a href="/wiki/Isildur" title="Isildur">Isildur</a> is supposedly lost amidst the flames.</dd></dl></dd></dl>
