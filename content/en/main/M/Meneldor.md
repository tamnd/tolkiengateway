**Meneldor** was a [Great Eagle](/wiki/Great_Eagle) of the northern mountains, one of the followers of [Gwaihir the Windlord](/wiki/Gwaihir "Gwaihir").

He was among the squadron of Eagles who flew to the aid of the [Captains of the West](/wiki/Captains_of_the_West) in the last desperate battle before the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). With his lord Gwaihir, and Gwaihir's brother [Landroval](/wiki/Landroval), he flew into the [Black Land](/wiki/Mordor "Mordor") to the rescue of [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), and carried one of these two (we are not told which) out of the destruction of [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Meneldor* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), probably being a combination of *[menel](/wiki/Menel "Menel")* ("the heavens") + *[-dor](/wiki/-dor) ("king")*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
