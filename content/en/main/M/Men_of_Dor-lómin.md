The **Men of Dor-lómin**<sup id="cite_ref-fifth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fifth-1">[1]</a></sup> also **Men of the North** were [Men](Men.md) of the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md), to whom the land of [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) was granted as a hereditary fief by the Kings of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> They were ruled by the [Lord of Dor-lómin](../L/Lord_of_Dor-lómin.md).

In the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md) the Men of Dor-lómin formed a host, led by the brothers [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) and [Huor](../H/Huor.md), that assembled on the right of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md). When the forces of King [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) retreated, the Men of Dor-lómin served as their rearguard. When they were pushed back into the [Fen of Serech](../F/Fen_of_Serech.md) they made a last stand.<sup id="cite_ref-fifth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fifth-1">[1]</a></sup> Afterwards, their lands were given to the [Easterlings](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>), and enslaved by them.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
