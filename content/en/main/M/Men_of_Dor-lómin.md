The **Men of Dor-lómin**<sup id="cite_ref-fifth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fifth-1">[1]</a></sup> also **Men of the North** were [Men](/wiki/Men) of the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador), to whom the land of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) was granted as a hereditary fief by the Kings of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> They were ruled by the [Lord of Dor-lómin](/wiki/Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min).

In the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad) the Men of Dor-lómin formed a host, led by the brothers [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) and [Huor](/wiki/Huor), that assembled on the right of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum). When the forces of King [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) retreated, the Men of Dor-lómin served as their rearguard. When they were pushed back into the [Fen of Serech](/wiki/Fen_of_Serech) they made a last stand.<sup id="cite_ref-fifth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fifth-1">[1]</a></sup> Afterwards, their lands were given to the [Easterlings](</wiki/Easterlings_(First_Age)> "Easterlings (First Age)"), and enslaved by them.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
