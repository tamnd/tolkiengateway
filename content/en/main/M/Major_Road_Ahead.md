**Major Road Ahead** was a hero — a figure in a "long-running family epic" told by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to his children. The stories "revolved around the tireless efforts of Major Road Ahead to prosecute the villain", [Bill Stickers](/wiki/Bill_Stickers).<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The stories about Major Road Ahead and Bill Stickers were only told orally (possibly around [1926](/wiki/1926) and [1930](/wiki/1930)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>), and have thus not been recorded,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> unlike, for example, *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)* and *[Letters from Father Christmas](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas)*.
