The **Mithril-coat**,<sup id="cite_ref-mail_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mail-1">[1]</a></sup> or **Mithril Coat**,<sup id="cite_ref-name_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-2">[2]</a></sup> was a vest of [Dwarf-mail](/wiki/Dwarf-mail) made of [mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril"), owned by [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo) and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).

## History {#History}

The most famous of the items made from *mithril* was the "small **coat of mail**" that had been made for a young elf-prince long before the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor). It was retrieved from the hoard of the dragon [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug), and given to [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) by [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

A "kingly gift" (in [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli)'s estimation), the Mithril-coat was worth more than the entire worth of the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-4">[4]</a></sup> Bilbo kept it as decoration in [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). He later lent it to [Mathom-house](/wiki/Mathom-house) museum in [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> but he took it back before leaving the [Shire](/wiki/Shire) for [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell). [Years later](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), he gave it to his nephew [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Frodo wore it during the [Quest](/wiki/Quest_of_the_Ring "Quest of the Ring") to [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom). It saved Frodo several times; the first when he was nearly skewered by the spear of an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") in the Mines of Moria,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> again shortly before crossing the Bridge of Khazad-dûm,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and a third time when he was struck by an arrow while on the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

The Mithril-coat was later taken by the Orcs who captured Frodo in the pass above [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol),<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> and it was passed on to the Dark Lord's servants at [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr). When the coat was displayed before the hosts of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) at the Gates of Mordor, many despaired, thinking Frodo had been captured or killed, and the Ring taken.<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-12">[12]</a></sup> Gandalf reclaimed it from Sauron's lieutenant<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-12">[12]</a></sup> and was later able to return it to Frodo after the battles were won.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> After the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater), the coat once again saved Frodo's life when [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) attempted to assassinate him at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the original editions of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, Bilbo's mail was of "silvered steel". It was altered to *mithril* in a [1966](/wiki/1966) revision.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hervarar_Saga" title="wikipedia:Hervarar Saga">Hervarar saga</a></i>, which was a cycle dealing with the magic sword *Tyrfing* (and from which Tolkien borrowed, for instance, the names [Dwalin](/wiki/Dwalin) and <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin</a>), the hero Orvar-Odd wore a *silken mailcoat* which nothing could pierce (*Oddr svarar: "ek vil berjask við Angantýr, hann mun gefa stór högg með Tyrfingi, en ek trúi betr skyrtu minni, enn brynju þinni, til hlífðar"*).
