<i><b>Mountain Live: The Road Goes Ever On</b></i> is a live album by the hard rock band **Mountain**, released in 1972. It consists of two tracks recorded at the Woodstock Festival in [1969](/wiki/1969) and two songs recorded live in [1972](/wiki/1972). Other than the title, which is taken from the [poem of the same name](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(song)> "The Road Goes Ever On (song)"), its lyrics have nothing to do with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s work.

## Track listing {#Track_listing}

1. "Long Red"
2. "Waiting To Take You Away"
3. "Crossroader"
4. "Nantucket Sleighride"
