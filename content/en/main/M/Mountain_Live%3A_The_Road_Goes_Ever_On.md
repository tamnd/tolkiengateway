<i><b>Mountain Live: The Road Goes Ever On</b></i> is a live album by the hard rock band **Mountain**, released in 1972. It consists of two tracks recorded at the Woodstock Festival in [1969](../_/1969.md) and two songs recorded live in [1972](../_/1972.md). Other than the title, which is taken from the [poem of the same name](<../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(song).md>), its lyrics have nothing to do with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s work.

## Track listing {#Track_listing}

1. "Long Red"
2. "Waiting To Take You Away"
3. "Crossroader"
4. "Nantucket Sleighride"
