**Middle-earth: Against the Shadow**, released in [1997](../_/1997.md), was the first and only expansion to *[Middle-earth: The Lidless Eye](Middle-earth%253A_The_Lidless_Eye.md)*, the second basic set for [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md), although it also contained hero cards for use with [Middle-earth: The Wizards](Middle-earth%253A_The_Wizards.md).

*Against the Shadow* mostly balances the game by adding hazards against the *minion* player and some support resources for the hero. Notable are the hazard creature versions of the five wizards.

*Against the Shadow* was sold in booster packs of 12 cards (36 boosters per display), featuring 2 rares, 4 uncommons and 6 commons per booster pack. A [corresponding player guide](Middle-earth%253A_Against_the_Shadow_Player_Guide.md) was worked on, though it never reached publication.
