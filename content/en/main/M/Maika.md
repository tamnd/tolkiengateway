<i><b>maika</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "sharp, piercing",<sup id="cite_ref-SApp_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-1">[1]</a></sup> or "blade of a cutting tool or weapon, especially sword-blade".<sup id="cite_ref-VT39_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT39-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") MAK ("cut, hew with a sharp edge")<sup id="cite_ref-VT39_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT39-2">[2]</a></sup>
