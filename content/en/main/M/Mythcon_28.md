**Mythcon 28** was the twenty-eighth annual [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) conference.

- **Theme:** "[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien): The Achievement of His Literary Life"
- **Guests of Honor:** [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) (Tolkien scholars) and [Orson Scott Card](/wiki/Orson_Scott_Card) (fantasy author)
- **Chairman:** [Glen GoodKnight](/wiki/Glen_GoodKnight)
