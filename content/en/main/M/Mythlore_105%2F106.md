<b><i>Mythlore</i> 105/106</b> (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/mythlore/vol27/iss3/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Volume 27, Issue 3/4</a>) is an issue of the *[Mythlore](Mythlore.md)* journal, published by the [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

#### Articles {#Articles}

- Christopher, Joe R. "The Thematic Organization Of *Spirits in Bondage*."
- Hood, Gwenyth. "Heroic Orual and the Tasks of Psyche."
- Kelly, A. Keith, and Michael Livingston. "'A Far Green Country': Tolkien, Paradise, and the End of All Things in Medieval Literature."
- Reiter, Geoffrey. "'Two Sides of the Same Magic': The Dialect of Mortality and Immortality in Peter S. Beagle's *The Last Unicorn*."
- Johnson, Brent D. "Éowyn's Grief."
- Croft, Janet Brennan. "The Education of a Witch: Tiffany Aching, Hermione Granger, and Gendered Magic in Discworld and Potterworld."
- Hawkins, Emma. "Tolkien and Dogs, Just Dogs: in Metaphor and Simile."

#### Features {#Features}

- Croft, Janet Brennan. Editorial.

#### Reviews {#Reviews}

- *Lilith in a New Light: Essays on the George Macdonald Fantasy Novel.* Ed. Lucas H. Harriman. Reviewed by William Gray.
- *Black &amp; White Ogre Country: The Lost Tales of Hilary Tolkien.* Edited by Angela Gardner. Illustrated by Jef Murray. Reviewed by Glen GoodKnight.
- *C.S. Lewis and the Search for Rational Religion.* John Beversluis. Reviewed by Donald T. Williams.
- *Faith and Choice in the Works of Joss Whedon.* K.. Dale Koontz. Reviewed by Amy H. Sturgis.
- *Fritz Leiber, Critical Essays.* Ed. Benjamin Szumskyj. Reviewed by Darrell Schweitzer.
- *Myth and Magic: Art according to the Inklings.* Eduardo Segura and Thomas Honegger. Reviewed by Jason Fisher.
- *From Narnia to a Space Odyssey: The War of Ideas between Arthur C. Clarke And C. S. Lewis.* Ed., and with introduction, by Ryder W. Miller. Reviewed by Joe R. Christopher.
- *The Mirror Crack'd: Fear and Horror in JRR Tolkien's Major Works.* Ed. Lynn Forest-Hill. Reviewed by Edith L. Crowe.
- *Arda Reconstructed: The Creation of the Published Silmarillion.* Douglas Charles Kane. Reviewed by Jason Fisher.
- *Night Operation.* Owen Barfield. Reviewed by David Bratman.
- *Eager Spring.* Owen Barfield. Reviewed by David Bratman.
