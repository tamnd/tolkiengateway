**Mythcon 16** was the sixteenth annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme:** "A Kinship of Dancers: Interplay in the Lives and Works of [Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md), [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), and [Williams](../C/Charles_Williams.md)"
- **Guests of Honor:** Patricia A. McKillip (fantasy author) and Peter Schakel (Lewis scholar)
- **Chairman:** Diana Lynne Pavlac
