**Mede** was the seventh month of the calendar of [Bree](../B/Bree.md). Preceded by the [Summerdays](../S/Summerdays.md), it corresponded to [Afterlithe](../A/Afterlithe.md) of the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md), approximately corresponding to modern [24 June](../_/24_June.md) through [23 July](../_/23_July.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Mede, means "meadow".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
