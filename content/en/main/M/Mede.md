**Mede** was the seventh month of the calendar of [Bree](/wiki/Bree). Preceded by the [Summerdays](/wiki/Summerdays), it corresponded to [Afterlithe](/wiki/Afterlithe) of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), approximately corresponding to modern [24 June](/wiki/24_June) through [23 July](/wiki/23_July).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Mede, means "meadow".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
