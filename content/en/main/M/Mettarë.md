**Mettarë**, meaning "Last-day", immediately preceded [Yestarë](../Y/Yestarë.md) and was the last day of the year in the calendars of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md). In the [Calendar of Imladris](../R/Reckoning_of_Rivendell.md), *Mettarë* fell more or less on [5 April](../_/5_April.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md). In the [Kings' Reckoning](../K/Kings'_Reckoning.md) and [Stewards' Reckoning](../S/Stewards'_Reckoning.md) it corresponded with [1 Yule](../_/1_Yule.md) in the Shire Calendar, around the winter solstice. In the [New Reckoning](../N/New_Reckoning.md) it corresponded with [24 March](../_/24_March.md) in the Shire Calendar.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
