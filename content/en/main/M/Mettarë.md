**Mettarë**, meaning "Last-day", immediately preceded [Yestarë](/wiki/Yestar%C3%AB) and was the last day of the year in the calendars of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain). In the [Calendar of Imladris](/wiki/Reckoning_of_Rivendell "Reckoning of Rivendell"), *Mettarë* fell more or less on [5 April](/wiki/5_April) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar). In the [Kings' Reckoning](/wiki/Kings%27_Reckoning) and [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning) it corresponded with [1 Yule](/wiki/1_Yule) in the Shire Calendar, around the winter solstice. In the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning) it corresponded with [24 March](/wiki/24_March) in the Shire Calendar.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
