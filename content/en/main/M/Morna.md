<i><b>morna</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) adjective meaning "black, dark".<sup id="cite_ref-L297_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L297-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [MOR](/wiki/MOR).<sup id="cite_ref-L297_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L297-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[morn](/wiki/Morn "Morn")* ("black, dark")
