**Mortals** ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") <i><b>Fírimar</b></i> by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) were any of the races of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) who were subject to old age and death (as for example [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) and [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves)), but applied especially to the race of [Men](/wiki/Men). <span></span>
