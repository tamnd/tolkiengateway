**May** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

May was a daughter of [Roper Gamgee](/wiki/Hobson "Hobson").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/may#Etymology_2" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">May</a></i> could refer to the hawthorn blossoms, so called because they blossom in the month May. It was a <a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/May_(disambiguation)" title="May (disambiguation)">common girl&#39;s name</a> in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").
