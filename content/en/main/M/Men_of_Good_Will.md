The term **Men of Good Will** was used by [Elves](/wiki/Elves) during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) to refer to non-[Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") [Men](/wiki/Men) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) who were descended from those who opposed [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-will_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-will-1">[1]</a></sup> The Men of Good Will consisted of tribes "who lived in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) and [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) and the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin) and in the [Great Wood](/wiki/Great_wood "Great wood") and the plains between that and [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and the [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn)."<sup id="cite_ref-will_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-will-1">[1]</a></sup> These tribes included descendants of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) who remained in Middle-earth during the Second Age and dwelled in Eriador instead of sailing to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor); descendants of the Edain's close kin who had never travelled westward beyond the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) during the First Age; "and others of remoter kin".<sup id="cite_ref-will_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-will-1">[1]</a></sup>
