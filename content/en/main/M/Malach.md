**Malach Aradan** was the son and heir of [Marach](/wiki/Marach) of the Third House of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain), and the ancestor of the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador) and its descendants.<sup id="cite_ref-Swest_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Swest-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Malach was born in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [307](/wiki/First_Age_307 "First Age 307"), being the son of Marach and older brother of [Imlach](/wiki/Imlach).<sup id="cite_ref-Hador_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hador-2">[2]</a></sup> He had settled with his folk in [Estolad](/wiki/Estolad), with the people of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor).

The kings of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) welcomed the [Edain](/wiki/Edain), and in response many young men took service with kings and lords of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar). Malach himself left Estolad for [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum), the realm of the Elven High-king [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), where he spent fourteen years ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [322](/wiki/First_Age_322 "First Age 322")-[336](/wiki/First_Age_336 "First Age 336")) with the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), he learned to speak [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), and was given the name <i><b>Aradan</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-Swest_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Swest-1">[1]</a></sup> (Sindarin for "Noble Man"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>).

After his time in Hithlum, Aradan married [Zimrahin](/wiki/Zimrahin) in [337](/wiki/First_Age_337 "First Age 337") who later took the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name of *[Meldis](/wiki/Meldis)*. Zimrahin Meldis bore Malach Aradan two children: the eldest child was an only daughter [Adanel](/wiki/Adanel) and the youngest child was an only son [Magor](/wiki/Magor).<sup id="cite_ref-Hador_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hador-2">[2]</a></sup> Aradan led many of the people of the Third House further west. Some (following Aradan's son, [Magor](/wiki/Magor)) moved into the vales of the southern slopes of the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin).<sup id="cite_ref-Swest_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Swest-1">[1]</a></sup>

He died in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [398](/wiki/First_Age_398 "First Age 398").<sup id="cite_ref-Hador_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hador-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Malach* is a [mannish](/wiki/Taliska "Taliska") name of unknown meaning. *Aradan* was the name given to Malach in [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) when he went to serve the Kings of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor). The name means "Noble Man" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), from *[ara](/wiki/Ara "Ara")-* ("noble") and *[adan](/wiki/Adan "Adan")* ("[man](/wiki/Edain "Edain")").<sup id="cite_ref-Swest_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Swest-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Originally it was [Hador Lórindol](/wiki/Hador "Hador") who led the people of the third house of the Edain across the Ered Luin into Beleriand; when this was changed so that Marach became the earliest leader then Malach was added to the ancestors of Hador.<sup id="cite_ref-Hador_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hador-2">[2]</a></sup>
