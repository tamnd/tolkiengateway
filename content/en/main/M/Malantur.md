**Malantur** was the great-grandson of [Tar-Amandil](../T/Tar-Amandil.md), descended from Tar-Amandil's second son, [Eärendur](<../E/Eärendur_(son_of_Tar-Amandil).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The original [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) royal law of succession stated that the [Sceptre of Númenor](../S/Sceptre_of_Númenor.md) must go to a male heir, either the King's son or, if he had no son, to the nearest kinsman of male descent. During Malantur's life the King was [Tar-Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md), his only child being a daughter, and as the nearest kinsman of male descent, Malantur was the King's presumptive heir.

However, in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [892](../S/Second_Age_892.md) Tar-Aldarion changed the law of succession so that the eldest child of the King, whether a man or a woman, should receive the sceptre. He then named his daughter [Ancalimë](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md) as his heir. The royal Council advised the King to amend the rule such that a female heir could refuse the sceptre, in which case the heir would become the nearest male kinsman of either male or female descent. Tar-Aldarion agreed to this change, so if Ancalimë had refused the sceptre it would have been her cousin [Soronto](../S/Soronto.md) who would have become the ruler of Númenor. Thus Malantur was denied the chance to become Númenor's seventh King.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Though he left descendants, nothing of their lives was recorded.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Malantur* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Golden Lord" and is a combination of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Malina&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Malina (page does not exist)">malina</a></i> ("yellow", "golden") and the suffix <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=-tur&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-tur (page does not exist)">-tur</a></i> ("master", "lord", "ruler").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
