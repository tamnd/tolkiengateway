**Malantur** was the great-grandson of [Tar-Amandil](/wiki/Tar-Amandil), descended from Tar-Amandil's second son, [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(son_of_Tar-Amandil)> "Eärendur (son of Tar-Amandil)").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The original [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") royal law of succession stated that the [Sceptre of Númenor](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor) must go to a male heir, either the King's son or, if he had no son, to the nearest kinsman of male descent. During Malantur's life the King was [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion), his only child being a daughter, and as the nearest kinsman of male descent, Malantur was the King's presumptive heir.

However, in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [892](/wiki/Second_Age_892 "Second Age 892") Tar-Aldarion changed the law of succession so that the eldest child of the King, whether a man or a woman, should receive the sceptre. He then named his daughter [Ancalimë](/wiki/Tar-Ancalim%C3%AB "Tar-Ancalimë") as his heir. The royal Council advised the King to amend the rule such that a female heir could refuse the sceptre, in which case the heir would become the nearest male kinsman of either male or female descent. Tar-Aldarion agreed to this change, so if Ancalimë had refused the sceptre it would have been her cousin [Soronto](/wiki/Soronto) who would have become the ruler of Númenor. Thus Malantur was denied the chance to become Númenor's seventh King.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Though he left descendants, nothing of their lives was recorded.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Malantur* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Golden Lord" and is a combination of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Malina&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Malina (page does not exist)">malina</a></i> ("yellow", "golden") and the suffix <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-tur&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-tur (page does not exist)">-tur</a></i> ("master", "lord", "ruler").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
