<b><i>Mythlore</i> 127</b> (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/mythlore/vol34/iss1/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Volume 34, Issue 1</a>) is an issue of the *[Mythlore](Mythlore.md)* journal, published by the [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

#### Articles {#Articles}

- [Rateliff, John D](../J/John_D._Rateliff.md). "The Lost Letter: Seeking the Keys to William's Arthuriad."
- Branchaw, Sherrylyn. "Tolkien's Philological Philosophy in His Fiction."
- Fitzsimmons, Phillip. "Tales of Anti-Heroes in the Work of J.R.R. Tolkien."
- Bunting, Nancy. "1904: Tolkien, Trauma, and its Anniversaries."
- Lakowski, Romuald I. "A Wilderness of Dragons: Tolkien's Treatment of Dragons in Roverandom and Farmer Giles of Ham."
- [Croft, Janet Brennan](../J/Janet_Brennan_Croft.md). "Noms de Guerre: The Power of Naming in War and Conflict in Middle-earth."
- Agan, Cami. "Hearkening to the Other: A Certeauvian Reading of the Ainulindale."

#### Features {#Features}

- Croft, Janet Brennan. Editorial.
- Ford, Seona. In Memoriam: Barbara Reynolds.
- Christopher, Joe R*. Alice’s* \[Successful\] *Adventures in Wonderland*: An Appreciation of its One Hundred Fifty Years.

#### Reviews {#Reviews}

- *The Collected Poems of C.S. Lewis: A Critical Edition.* Edited by Don W. King. Reviewed by Joe R. Christopher.
- *Tree of Salvation: Yggdrasil and the Cross in the North.* G. Ronald Murphy. Reviewed by Jon Garrad.
- *Anglo-Saxon Community in J.R.R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings.* Deborah A. Higgins. Reviewed by Yvette Kisor.
- *Surprised by the Feminine: A Rereading of C.S. Lewis and Gender.* Monika B. Hilder. Reviewed by Laura Lee Smith.
- *Arda Inhabited: Environmental Relationships in The Lord of The Rings.* Susan Jeffers. Reviewed by Jeremy Larson.
- *Joy: Poet, Seeker, and the Woman Who Captivated C.S. Lewis.* Abigail Santamaria. Reviewed by Crystal Hurd.
- *A Naked Tree: Love Sonnets to C.S. Lewis and Other Poems.* Joy Davidman. Ed. Don W. King. Reviewed by Joe R. Christopher.
- *The Hero Enkidu.* Lewis Turco. Reviewed by Nicholas Birns.
- *Encyclopedia of Goddesses &amp; Heroines.* Patricia Monaghan. Reviewed by Cait Coker.
- *The Oxford Inklings.* Colin Duriez. Reviewed by Crystal Hurd.
- *The Fellowship: The Literary Lives of the Inklings.* Philip Zaleski and Carol Zaleski. Reviewed by Crystal Hurd.
- *The Pilgrim's Regress: Wade Annotated Edition.* C.S. Lewis. Edited and introduced by David C. Downing. Reviewed by Mike Foster.
- *North Wind: A Journal of George MacDonald Studies.* Editors John Pennington and Fernando Soto. Reviewed by Janet Brennan Croft.
- *Supernatural Studies. Special Issue: Television and the Supernatural.* Editor Leah Richards; Guest editor Marisa C. Hayes. Reviewed by Janet Brennan Croft.
- *Journal of Inklings Studies: Theology, Philosophy, Literature. Special Issue: Inklings and the Bible.* Executive editor Judith Wolfe. Reviewed by Janet Brennan Croft.
