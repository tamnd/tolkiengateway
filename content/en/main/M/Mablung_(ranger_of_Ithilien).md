**Mablung** was a [ranger of Ithilien](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien "Rangers of Ithilien") during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

Mablung's family had lived in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) before it was overrun by the forces of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). As such, he was given a place among the Rangers of Ithilien, [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor)'s most trusted forayers.<sup id="cite_ref-Herbs_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Herbs-2">[2]</a></sup>

Together with [Damrod](/wiki/Damrod), [Anborn](/wiki/Anborn) and several others, Mablung served under the [Captain of Gondor](/wiki/Captain_of_Gondor) [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), the [Steward](/wiki/Steward)'s second son. They were sent to intercept a troop of [Southrons](/wiki/Southrons) in March [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), and there, they came across [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee). While standing guard over the [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") with Damrod, he briefly conversed with them. Faramir, in the meantime, went on to ambush the Haradrim.<sup id="cite_ref-Herbs_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Herbs-2">[2]</a></sup>

Mablung and Damrod remained the guards of the Ring-bearer during their short stay at [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn).<sup id="cite_ref-Window_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Window-3">[3]</a></sup>

With his Lord injured, Mablung was given command of the Rangers after the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields). When the [Army of the West](/wiki/Army_of_the_West) marched on the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon), Mablung and his troops scouted the road and discovered an ambush by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) that lay hid in Ithilien. The ambush was quickly turned.<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Mablung was named after [Mablung of the Heavy Hand](/wiki/Mablung "Mablung"), a great Hunter of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-Faramir_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Faramir-5">[5]</a></sup> The name is from [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mab&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mab (page does not exist)">mab</a></i> ("hand") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lung&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lung (page does not exist)">lung</a></i> ("heavy").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> His epithet was a translation of his name<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
