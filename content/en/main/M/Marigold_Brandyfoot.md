**Marigold Brandyfoot** was a [Harfoot](/wiki/Harfoots "Harfoots") who was apart of a caravan led by [Sadoc Burrows](/wiki/Sadoc_Burrows) during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Marigold was invented by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*, where she was portrayed by [Sara Zwangobani](/wiki/Sara_Zwangobani).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Rings_of_Power_(TV_series)_-_Marigold_Brandyfoot_poster.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/b/bd/The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Marigold_Brandyfoot_poster.jpg/200px-The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Marigold_Brandyfoot_poster.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="296" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/b/bd/The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Marigold_Brandyfoot_poster.jpg/300px-The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Marigold_Brandyfoot_poster.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/b/bd/The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Marigold_Brandyfoot_poster.jpg/400px-The_Rings_of_Power_%28TV_series%29_-_Marigold_Brandyfoot_poster.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1382" data-file-height="2047"/></a><figcaption>The <i>Marigold Brandyfoot poster</i> for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a></i></figcaption></figure>

Marigold was born during the latter half of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). After Largo had lost his first wife, [Rose Brandyfoot](/wiki/Rose_Brandyfoot), he met Marigold within a clearing of the [Norfield Glen](/wiki/Norfield_Glen). She helped him move on from his grief, healing him from feeling "*like the wheels broke out from under*" him. Afterwards, they became married, and Marigold took on the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Brandyfoot_family&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Brandyfoot family (page does not exist)">Brandyfoot</a> name. Eventually they had two children together: [Elanor](/wiki/Elanor_Brandyfoot "Elanor Brandyfoot") and [Dilly](/wiki/Dilly_Brandyfoot "Dilly Brandyfoot").

After Largo broke his left ankle when a rope snapped, Marigold grew afraid that that the caravan would be forced to leave them behind on the migration due to the wound. However, Largo attempted to heal her despair by reminding her that she had helped him when he was grieving for Rose Brandyfoot.

Later, when [The Stranger](/wiki/The_Stranger) accidentally destroyed the Harfoot <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Harvest_Festival&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Harvest Festival (page does not exist)">Harvest Festival</a> during [Trailfinder Sadoc's](/wiki/Sadoc_Burrows "Sadoc Burrows") remembrance speech, Elanor's rule breaking was made known, and Sadoc forced the Brandyfoot cart to be put at the back of the caravan during the migration from the [Norfield Glen](/wiki/Norfield_Glen) to the [Grove](/wiki/Grove), dooming them to the very real possibility of being left behind. Despite this, Marigold allows the Stranger to aid them in keeping up with the caravan.

Upon arriving at the shockingly ruined Grove, the Stranger attempts to heal the orchard. Despite this, however, a tree branch broke off, nearly harming Elanor and Dilly, putting Marigold into a panic.

After the Stranger leaves and the [Mystics](/wiki/Mystics) burn the carts to the ground in their pursuit, Marigold, alongside [Sadoc Burrows](/wiki/Sadoc_Burrows), Elanor, and [Poppy Proudfellow](/wiki/Poppy_Proudfellow), leave the Grove to warn the Stranger and seek revenge on the Mystics. After the battle against the Mystics within the [East Bight](/wiki/East_Bight), Marigold and the other <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Brandyfoot_family&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Brandyfoot family (page does not exist)">Brandyfoots</a> say their farewells to Elanor, who decides to accompany the Stranger on a journey to find out more about the [Hermit's Hat](/wiki/Hermit%27s_Hat).
