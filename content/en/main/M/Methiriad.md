**Methiriad**, also known as **Mid-Beleriand**, was a region of [West Beleriand](../W/West_Beleriand.md) that was located between [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) and the river [Narog](../N/Narog.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Methiriad consisted of the following locations:

- [Dor-Cúarthol](../D/Dor-Cúarthol.md)
- [Falls of Sirion](../F/Falls_of_Sirion.md)
- [Gates of Sirion](../G/Gates_of_Sirion.md)
- [Moors of the Nibin-noeg](Moors_of_the_Nibin-noeg.md)
- [Nan-tathren](../N/Nan-tathren.md)
- [Nivrim](../N/Nivrim.md)
- [Talath Dirnen](../T/Talath_Dirnen.md)
- [Vales of Sirion](../V/Vales_of_Sirion.md)

According to [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), both of these names only appear on the [The Second 'Silmarillion' Map](../T/The_Second_'Silmarillion'_Map.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Methiriad* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word, but it is not glossed. As Paul Strack explains, it could mean "End of Two Rivers", from <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Meth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Meth (page does not exist)">Meth</a></i> ("last") + a mutated form of *[Sîr](../S/Sîr.md)* ("river, stream") + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=-ad&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-ad (page does not exist)">-ad</a></i> ("dual").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
