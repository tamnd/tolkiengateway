**Methiriad**, also known as **Mid-Beleriand**, was a region of [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand) that was located between [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) and the river [Narog](/wiki/Narog).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Methiriad consisted of the following locations:

- [Dor-Cúarthol](/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol)
- [Falls of Sirion](/wiki/Falls_of_Sirion)
- [Gates of Sirion](/wiki/Gates_of_Sirion)
- [Moors of the Nibin-noeg](/wiki/Moors_of_the_Nibin-noeg)
- [Nan-tathren](/wiki/Nan-tathren)
- [Nivrim](/wiki/Nivrim)
- [Talath Dirnen](/wiki/Talath_Dirnen)
- [Vales of Sirion](/wiki/Vales_of_Sirion)

According to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), both of these names only appear on the [The Second 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_Second_%27Silmarillion%27_Map).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Methiriad* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word, but it is not glossed. As Paul Strack explains, it could mean "End of Two Rivers", from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Meth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Meth (page does not exist)">Meth</a></i> ("last") + a mutated form of *[Sîr](/wiki/S%C3%AEr)* ("river, stream") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-ad&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-ad (page does not exist)">-ad</a></i> ("dual").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
