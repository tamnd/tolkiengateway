**Middle-earth: The White Hand**, released in [1997](/wiki/1997), was an expansion set for [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).

*The White Hand* further complicates the game by adding a third faction, the corrupted *Fallen-Wizard*, whose player can use both hero and minion resources and characters, although with restrictions.

*The White Hand* was sold in 12 card boosters (36 boosters per display) with 2 rares, 4 uncommons, and 6 commons in each.

## Reception {#Reception}

In a review for *[InQuest](/wiki/InQuest)*, Luke Durok praised the game for doing “something few other CCG expansions have accomplished”, stating that the fallen-wizard mechanics make for a true expansion with “a whole new style of play”. He did find that overall the artwork wasn't as strong as previous expansions.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
