**Menelluin** is the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) name for "cornflower".

It was the emblem of [Idril Celebrindal](/wiki/Idril "Idril"), is stated to be an inlaid plaque saved from [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) and descending through [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) and his son [Elros](/wiki/Elros) and influenced [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") circular designs. The Emblem was taken to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) by [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *menelluin* means "sky-blue"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (from *[menel](/wiki/Menel "Menel")* = "heavens, sky" and *[luinë](/wiki/Luine "Luine")* = "blue").<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
