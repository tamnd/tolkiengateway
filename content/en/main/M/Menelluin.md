**Menelluin** is the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) name for "cornflower".

It was the emblem of [Idril Celebrindal](../I/Idril.md), is stated to be an inlaid plaque saved from [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) and descending through [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md) and his son [Elros](../E/Elros.md) and influenced [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) circular designs. The Emblem was taken to [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) by [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *menelluin* means "sky-blue"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) (from *[menel](Menel.md)* = "heavens, sky" and *[luinë](../L/Luine.md)* = "blue").<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
