The <b><i>Monash Review</i> 3 (1975)</b> features the article "Fantasy that! — a Tolkien original". The article reproduces parts of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem *[Do Worst](../V/Visio_de_Doworst.md)* and describes its background. The same article also includes a transcription and reproduction of [a letter from Tolkien to R.W. Chambers](../L/Letter_to_R.W._Chambers.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
