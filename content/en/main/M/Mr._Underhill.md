**Mr. Underhill** was the name [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) gave to [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) before he set off on his quest during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) had already learned of *[Baggins](../B/Baggins_family.md)* through [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) and using the name outside of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) would prove dangerous, thus a secret identity was formed.

The name was perhaps chosen from the [neighborhood of Underhill](<../U/Underhill_(Hobbiton).md>) where [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md), Frodo's home, belonged.

This was perhaps not the best choice, for when "Mr. Underhill" arrived in Bree, there were several [Underhills](../U/Underhill_Family.md) in the common-room at [the Prancing Pony](../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md) and they thought it likely that Frodo was some long-lost cousin of theirs. One of the [Bree-landers](../B/Bree-landers.md), who seemed familiar with the Shire, asked where the Underhills lived and who were their relations.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
