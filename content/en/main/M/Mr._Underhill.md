**Mr. Underhill** was the name [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) gave to [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) before he set off on his quest during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) had already learned of *[Baggins](/wiki/Baggins_family "Baggins family")* through [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) and using the name outside of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") would prove dangerous, thus a secret identity was formed.

The name was perhaps chosen from the [neighborhood of Underhill](</wiki/Underhill_(Hobbiton)> "Underhill (Hobbiton)") where [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End), Frodo's home, belonged.

This was perhaps not the best choice, for when "Mr. Underhill" arrived in Bree, there were several [Underhills](/wiki/Underhill_Family "Underhill Family") in the common-room at [the Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony "The Prancing Pony") and they thought it likely that Frodo was some long-lost cousin of theirs. One of the [Bree-landers](/wiki/Bree-landers), who seemed familiar with the Shire, asked where the Underhills lived and who were their relations.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
