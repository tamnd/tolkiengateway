<b><i>Mallorn</i> 49</b> is the forty-ninth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s biannual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Spring [2010](../_/2010.md).

- **Editorial**
    - "How do we know what we know?"

- **Letters**
- **Reviews**
    - "A question of source": *[The Epic Realm of Tolkien](../T/The_Epic_Realm_of_Tolkien.md)*

    - "The shape of a story": *Rhetorics of Fantasy*
        - By: Chad Chisholm

        - By: John Gilbey
