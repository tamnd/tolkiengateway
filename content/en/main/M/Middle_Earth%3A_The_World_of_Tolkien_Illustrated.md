<i><b>Middle Earth: The World of Tolkien Illustrated</b></i> is a book with illustrations depicting various scenes from [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*. Each illustration is accompanied by an adapted text by Lin Carter. The book also features a map of [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)'s travels by Wenzel, an introductory text on *The Hobbit* by Carter, and short biographies of Wenzel and Carter.
