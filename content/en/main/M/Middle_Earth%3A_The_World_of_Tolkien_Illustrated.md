<i><b>Middle Earth: The World of Tolkien Illustrated</b></i> is a book with illustrations depicting various scenes from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. Each illustration is accompanied by an adapted text by Lin Carter. The book also features a map of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)'s travels by Wenzel, an introductory text on *The Hobbit* by Carter, and short biographies of Wenzel and Carter.
