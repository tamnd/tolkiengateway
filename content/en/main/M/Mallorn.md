The **mallorn** (pl. **mellyrn**<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:342</sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HCM_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HCM-3">[3]</a></sup>) was a [kind of large tree](/wiki/Fragrant_Trees "Fragrant Trees") growing in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) and the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands).

## Description {#Description}

Mellyrn are trees of immense size, especially when growing in Númenor, where they could reach heights of almost 600 feet (c. 183 meters).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In [Caras Galadhon](/wiki/Caras_Galadhon) in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) the highest of mallorn-trees were nearly 200 feet (c. 61 meters) high and of great girth, having no branches lower than 3 fathoms (c. 5.5 meters) above their roots.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-5">[5]</a></sup>

They are also described as similar to [beeches](/wiki/Beeches "Beeches"): their bark was smooth and silver-grey, and the leaves were green on top and silver underneath; they turned golden in autumn and remained on the tree through the winter to fall to cover the ground in the spring, when new leaves sprouted. Golden flowers bloomed on the branches.<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:335</sup><sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-6">[6]</a></sup>

The trunk of the tree divided to a crown of many branches, and the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) used the space to build *[telain](/wiki/Telain "Telain")* (a sort of platform) or buildings on the top of the trees.<sup id="cite_ref-lor_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lor-7">[7]</a></sup> The leaves also had practical uses, such as for packing rations of *[lembas](/wiki/Lembas "Lembas")*. The Galadhrim used the bark to make *[hithlain](/wiki/Hithlain "Hithlain")*.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-5">[5]</a></sup>

[Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) explained that he could feel the life of the tree when he touched a mallorn in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Decipher_-_Mallorn_Tree.jpg"><img src="/w/images/2/24/Decipher_-_Mallorn_Tree.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="254" height="185" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="254" data-file-height="185"/></a><figcaption><i>Mallorn Tree</i> in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i></figcaption></figure>

Mallorn-trees originally grew in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) and from there were spread to [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) once the Lonely Isle was moved to its final position by [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo). The Elves of Tol Eressëa brought mallorn-trees to the [Men](/wiki/Men) of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) and they grew on the shores around the [Bay of Eldanna](/wiki/Bay_of_Eldanna) in that land.<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-6">[6]</a></sup> Even [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion), the great Ship-King, did not cut down these trees.

Mallorn-nuts were given by Tar-Aldarion to his friend [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), the Noldorin King of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon). The mellyrn did not grow in Lindon, but [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), who was gifted some of the seeds from Gil-galad,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:48</sup> took some nuts with her to [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), where they grew to immense heights (but not as great as the groves of Númenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-6">[6]</a></sup> Lothlórien became known as the *Golden Wood* because of them.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

[Caras Galadhon](/wiki/Caras_Galadhon), the city of Galadriel and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) in Lothlórien, was built in the branches of huge mallorn-trees. The [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") spent the night in a [flet](/wiki/Telain "Telain") in a mallorn-tree nearby, and were later given [lembas](/wiki/Lembas "Lembas") wrapped in mallorn-leaves.<sup id="cite_ref-lor_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lor-7">[7]</a></sup>

Galadriel gave [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) a box of soil containing a single silver mallorn nut.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> After the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) and the [Scouring of the Shire](/wiki/Scouring_of_the_Shire), Sam planted the nut in the [Party Field](/wiki/Party_Field) where the [Party Tree](/wiki/Party_Tree) had stood before its felling. The [Golden Tree](/wiki/Golden_Tree) was the only mallorn in Middle-earth outside of Lórien. When the tree bloomed in the next summer it was said that all the Shire became golden from the flowers.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word comes from *[malt](/wiki/Malt "Malt")* ("gold") + *[orn](/wiki/Orn "Orn")* ("tree"). In [Gondor Sindarin](/wiki/Gondor_Sindarin) the same word was pronounced *Malthorn*.<sup id="cite_ref-VT4227_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT4227-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

<i><b>Malinornë</b></i> is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) translation of *mallorn*.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

## Uses outside the Legendarium {#Uses_outside_the_Legendarium}

By [1956](/wiki/1956) Tolkien had been informed about "a new house far away \[that\] has been called *The Mallorns*" — regrettably, Tolkien noted, as the correct [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) plural would be *mellyrn*.<sup id="cite_ref-HCM_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HCM-3">[3]</a></sup>

*[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")* is also the journal published by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society).
