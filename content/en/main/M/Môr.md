<i><b>môr</b></i> is a noun meaning "darkness, dark, night" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). Compare with *[morë](/wiki/Mor%C3%AB "Morë")* in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).

Examples:

- [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) = "Black Land"
- [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen) = "Dark Maiden"
