<i><b>môr</b></i> is a noun meaning "darkness, dark, night" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md). Compare with *[morë](Morë.md)* in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).

Examples:

- [Mordor](Mordor.md) = "Black Land"
- [Morwen](Morwen.md) = "Dark Maiden"
