**May Gamgee** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

May was the second daughter, and fourth child, of [Hamfast Gamgee](../G/Gaffer_Gamgee.md) and [Bell Goodchild](../B/Bell_Goodchild.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/may#Etymology_2" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">May</a></i> could refer to the hawthorn blossoms, so called because they blossom in the month May. It was a <a class="mw-disambig" href="May_(disambiguation).md" title="May (disambiguation)">common girl&#39;s name</a> in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).
