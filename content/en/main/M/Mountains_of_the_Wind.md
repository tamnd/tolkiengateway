The **Mountains of the Wind** was a range of mountains in the middle of the [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md) to the west of [Hildórien](../H/Hildórien.md) according to the [Ambarkanta](../A/Ambarkanta.md).

## History {#History}

The Mountains of the Wind were thus named because of the strong winds "coming from the East before the Sun". The Mountains of the Wind are no more mentioned in the later versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).

These may correspond to the "mountainous wall" which [Nuin](../N/Nuin.md) eventually crossed after finding a passage which took him to the [Vale of Sleep](../V/Vale_of_Sleep.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [Ambarkanta maps](../A/Ambarkanta_maps.md), the Mountains of the Wind are not shown on Map V, labelled "After the [War of the Gods](../B/Battle_of_the_Powers.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> However, they appear on Map IV<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>, labelled vertically "Mts of the Wind" with two moutain ranges.
