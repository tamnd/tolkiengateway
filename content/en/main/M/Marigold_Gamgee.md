**Marigold Cotton (née Gamgee)** was the youngest child of [Hamfast Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee").

## History {#History}

Like all Gamgees, Marigold was close to the [Cotton Family](/wiki/Cotton_Family). She married [Young Tom Cotton](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Junior "Tolman Cotton Junior"), the eldest son of [Farmer Cotton](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior"). Marigold's brother, [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), married Tom's sister [Rose](/wiki/Rose_Cotton "Rose Cotton").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Sam apparently had a close relationship with her, lamenting the prospect of not seeing her again during his time in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Marigold" (from *Mary* + *gold*) refers to the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tagetes" title="wikipedia:Tagetes">Tagetes</a></i> annual plants of the daisy family.

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") picked the name since it contains "gold" and referring to a golden flower, suggesting the [Fallohide](/wiki/Fallohides "Fallohides") strain in Sam's family — which, increased by the favour of [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), became notable in his children.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>
