**Marroc Brandybuck** was a Hobbit of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland).

## History {#History}

Marroc was the youngest son of [Gormadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gormadoc_Brandybuck) and [Malva Headstrong](/wiki/Malva_Headstrong). He had many descendants.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Marroc* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-3">[3]</a></sup>

A Kngiht of the Round Table named *Marrok* is mentioned in Malory's *Le Morte Darthure*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
