<b><i>Mallorn</i> 59</b> is the fifty-ninth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s annual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Winter [2018](../_/2018.md).

    - "Fairy marriages in Tolkien's works"
        - By: Giovanni C. Costabile
    - "Realism in fantasy: *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* as 'history . . . feigned'"
        - By: Lawrence Krikorian

        - By: Dale Nelson
    - "Beryl, more than just a token?"

    - "The healing of Théoden or 'a glimpse of the Final Victory'"
        - By: Jean Chausse
    - "Checking the Facts"
