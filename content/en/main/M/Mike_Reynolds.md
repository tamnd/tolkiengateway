**Mike Reynold** is a game designer who worked on the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md) and [The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md).

## History {#History}

Reynolds met <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=John_Curtis&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="John Curtis (page does not exist)">John Curtis</a> of Iron Crown Enterprises through a gaming convention they set up together, and was hired as a proofreader for Rolemaster in December 1993. He subsequently co-developed the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md) with [Coleman Charlton](../C/Coleman_Charlton.md), and became its series editor. In that capacity, he wrote out the concepts set forth by the design team into the card text.

After leaving ICE, Reynolds worked for [Decipher](../D/Decipher.md)'s Star Wars trading card game. For a while, he worked as a software engineer for EA games, but returned to Decipher after being laid off, where he then co-designed [The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
