**Miniel** (pl. *Mínil*) ([S](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin"). 'First elf') is the name [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) were called in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is composed of *[min](/wiki/Min "Min")* = "first" and *[el](/wiki/Edhel "Edhel")* = "elf"
