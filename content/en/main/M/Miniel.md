**Miniel** (pl. *Mínil*) ([S](../S/Sindarin.md). 'First elf') is the name [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) were called in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is composed of *[min](Min.md)* = "first" and *[el](../E/Edhel.md)* = "elf"
