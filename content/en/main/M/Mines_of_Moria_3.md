**Mines of Moria 3** is a text-adventure MS-DOS game, released by [Ketchup Games](../K/Ketchup_Games.md) in [1999](../_/1999.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> *Mines of Moria 3* formed the third and final part of the *[Mines of Moria](<Mines_of_Moria_(video_game_series).md>)* trilogy.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> The hero of the trilogy, [Feagwaith](../F/Feagwaith.md), must return to [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and deliver a medicine.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> The game is written by [Adam Roberts](../A/Adam_Roberts.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
