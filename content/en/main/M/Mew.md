**Mew** is a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black-backed_gull" title="wikipedia:Black-backed gull">black-backed gull</a> in *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*. Mew lives on a cliff-face (suggested by [Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond "Wayne G. Hammond") and [Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull "Christina Scull") to be somewhere near <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filey" title="wikipedia:Filey">Filey</a><sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:97</sup>) and is the postman for the sand-sorcerer [Psamathos Psamathides](/wiki/Psamathos_Psamathides).<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:26</sup>

In the story, Mew takes [Rover](/wiki/Rover) to the moon at the request of Psamathos; they fly via Mew's cliff-edge home, over the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isle_of_Dogs" title="wikipedia:Isle of Dogs">Isle of Dogs</a> and see the seas flowing over the [edges of the world](/wiki/Circles_of_the_World "Circles of the World").<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:20</sup> Whilst at the [moon](/wiki/Moon "Moon"), Mew continues to deliver post to the [Man-in-the-Moon](/wiki/Man-in-the-Moon) before - once Roverandom's adventures on the moon are finished - taking Rover (now known as Roverandom) back to Psamathos.<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:55</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"mew" is a term used to describe the noise by a cat or a gull.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
