**Mary Jane Stow** ([1833](/wiki/1833) - [13 February](/wiki/13_February) [1915](/wiki/1915)) was the wife of [John Benjamin Tolkien](/wiki/John_Benjamin_Tolkien) - marrying on [16 February](/wiki/16_February), [1856](/wiki/1856) - and thus [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s paternal grandmother.
