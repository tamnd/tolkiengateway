<b><i>Mallorn</i> 63</b> is the sixty-third issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s annual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Winter [2022](../_/2022.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Front Matter**
    - Editorial: "Let's Talk Adaptations" by Luke Shelton
- **Articles**
    - "‘All that glisters is not gold’: The Ring of Power and the Deception of Simile" by Sara Brown
    - "Middle-earth in South Park: ‘The Return of the Fellowship of the Ring to the Two Towers’ as Parody" by Nick Polk
    - "Saints and Witches: Morwen, Morwenna, and Morgan le Fay" by Clare Moore
- **Notes**
    - "This Ephemeral Beauty: Tolkien’s Legendarium Through Japanese Buddhist Aesthetic Categories" by Robert Steed
    - "Reply to Noad’s Comments on the article, ‘Checking the Facts’" by Nancy Bunting
- **Reviews**
    - *[Law, Government, and Society in J.R.R. Tolkien's Works](../L/Law,_Government,_and_Society_in_J.R.R._Tolkien's_Works.md)* by José Maria Miranda Boto (reviewed by Dominic Nardi)
    - <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tolkien,_Enchantment,_and_Loss&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien, Enchantment, and Loss (page does not exist)">Tolkien, Enchantment, and Loss</a></i> by John Rosegrant (reviewed by Mercury Natis)
    - <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Map_of_Wilderland&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Map of Wilderland (page does not exist)">The Map of Wilderland: Ecocritical Reflections on Tolkien’s Myth of Wilderness</a></i> by Amber Lehning (reviewed by Laura Zanetti and Hélio Parente)
    - *[The Gallant Edith Bratt](../T/The_Gallant_Edith_Bratt.md)* by Nancy Bunting and Seamus Hamill-Keays (reviewed by [Shaun Gunner](../S/Shaun_Gunner.md))
- **Submissions**
    - Letters
    - Errata
    - Guide for Authors
