To flesh out the setting, [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing) introduces many new fantastical types of metal beyond <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Bronze&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bronze (page does not exist)">bronze</a>, [copper](/wiki/Copper "Copper"), [galvorn](/wiki/Galvorn "Galvorn"), [gold](/wiki/Gold "Gold"), [iron](/wiki/Iron "Iron"), [mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril"), [silver](/wiki/Silver "Silver"), and [steel](/wiki/Steel "Steel").

## Adarcer {#Adarcer}

<i><b>Adarcer</b></i> is a white alloy that is described as a fusion of iron, true-coal (glôin), and dark-iron (durang). This strong metal is difficult to work with once forged.<sup id="cite_ref-firstpage_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-firstpage-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Cranor {#Cranor}

<i><b>Cranor</b></i> (“Tree-gold”) is a resin invented by the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Daen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Daen (page does not exist)">Daen</a> of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) that stores static electricity. The extremely hard substance is made using the Sorglasora (“Gold-pine”) sap and the White Lily nectar. It was used to entomb Dunnish Chieftains.<sup id="cite_ref-firstpage_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-firstpage-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Eog {#Eog}

<i><b>Eog</b></i> (<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Dursindarin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dursindarin (page does not exist)">Du.</a> “[Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l)'s Iron” or “Ang Eöl”) is described as a fusion of mithril and durang with unknown other materials. The Elven recipe, thought to originate from the House of Eöl, requires both hot and cold forging. The very hard material can be made in many colours.<sup id="cite_ref-firstpage_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-firstpage-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Ithilnaur {#Ithilnaur}

<i><b>Ithilnaur</b></i> ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Moon-fire”) is an alloy of mithril with other materials such as titanium. It is common in [Moria](/wiki/Moria).<sup id="cite_ref-secondpage_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-secondpage-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Kregora {#Kregora}

<i><b>Kregora</b></i> a very malleable dull-yellow metal, used to line surfaces against spells.<sup id="cite_ref-secondpage_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-secondpage-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Mithrarian {#Mithrarian}

<i><b>Mithrarian</b></i> (“Abyss of High Silver”) is a rare alloy of [eog](#eog), Mithril, and [Celebur](#Other_metals) introduced by [Annatar](/wiki/Annatar). Objects lined with it would float. The [Gwaith-i-Mirdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-Mirdain) were reluctant to work with it, given the nature of the aforementioned Celebur.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Ogamur {#Ogamur}

<i><b>Ogamur</b></i> (S. “From <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gamur&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gamur (page does not exist)">Gamur</a>”) is a Dwarven metal that is very flexible. The recipe, derived from an eastern Dwarf-house, is a closely guarded secret. The [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) are required to purchase it because of this.<sup id="cite_ref-fourthpage_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fourthpage-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Tasarang {#Tasarang}

<i><b>Ogamur</b></i> (S. “Willow-iron”; [W.](/wiki/Westron "Westron") Shalk) is a white metal that resembles white ogamur. It does not stretch. Its chalk-white ore is found below limestone intrusions.<sup id="cite_ref-fourthpage_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fourthpage-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other metals {#Other_metals}

Non-fictional types of material are treated in a similar fashion:<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

- <i><b>Alcam</b></i> (S. “Tin”)
- <i><b>Celebur</b></i> (S. “Burning Silver”) — weak uranium ore
- <i><b>Galnin</b></i> (S. “Shining-white” or Aluminium)
- <i><b>Mithglín</b></i> (S. “Gleaming Grey” or Platinum)
- <i><b>Mithin</b></i> (S. “Pale Grey” or Beryllium)
