<b><i>Mythlore</i> 9</b> (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/mythlore/vol3/iss1/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Volume 3, Issue 1</a>) is an issue of the *[Mythlore](/wiki/Mythlore)* journal, published by the [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society). The issue is the same as *[Tolkien Journal](/wiki/Tolkien_Journal)* issue 16.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

#### Articles {#Articles}

- [All\[a\]n, James](/wiki/James_Allan "James Allan"). “Genesis of *The Lord of the Rings*: A Study of Saga Development,” (Attributed to the pseudonym Dzhimælæn 289PL56-d78; errata list of dropped passages in <a class="external text" href="https://www.mythsoc.org/mythlore/mythlore-12.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Mythlore 12</a>, p. 26) 3.

- Paxson, Diana. “The Holy Grail.” 10-11, 31.
- Christopher, Joe R. “An Introduction to Narnia, Part 4: The Literary Classification of the Chronicles.” 12-15, 27.
- “The Mythopoeic Press: A Proposal.” 18+.
- GoodKnight, Glen and Frederick M. Brenion. “Walter Hooper — A Thank You.” 28+.

#### Features {#Features}

- [Zuber, Bernie](/wiki/Bernie_Zuber "Bernie Zuber"). Across the Brandywine. 16+.
- Missives to Mythlore. 20+.

- Christopher, Joe R. “Tolkien Criticism: An Annotated Checklist.” Review of Tolkien Criticism: An Annotated Checklist compiled by Richard C. West. 26-27.
- GoodKnight, Glen. The Counsel of Elrond. 30.
