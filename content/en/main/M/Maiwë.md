<i><b>maiwë</b></i> (pl. *maiwi*) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) noun meaning "[gull](/wiki/Gulls "Gulls")" (as in "seagull"). Compare with *[aiwë](/wiki/Aiw%C3%AB "Aiwë")* ("small bird").

## Etymology {#Etymology}
