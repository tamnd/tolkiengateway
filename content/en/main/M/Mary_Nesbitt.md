**Mary Nesbitt** (born [9 March](../_/9_March.md) [2001](../_/2001.md)) portrayed [Tilda](../T/Tilda.md), a character created by [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) for [The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md) and [The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md) and a child of [Dale](../D/Dale.md) in [The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md).

## Family {#Family}

- Daughter of [James Nesbitt](../J/James_Nesbitt.md) who portrayed [Bofur](../B/Bofur.md) in [The Hobbit (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)
- Daughter of <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Sonia_Forbes-Adam&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sonia Forbes-Adam (page does not exist)">Sonia Forbes-Adam</a> who portrayed [Belladonna Took](../B/Belladonna_Took.md) in [The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey (extended edition)](<../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey_(extended_edition).md>)
- Younger sister of [Peggy Nesbitt](../P/Peggy_Nesbitt.md) who portrayed [Sigrid](../S/Sigrid.md) in The Hobbit (film series)
