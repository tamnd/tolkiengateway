**Mary Nesbitt** (born [9 March](/wiki/9_March) [2001](/wiki/2001)) portrayed [Tilda](/wiki/Tilda), a character created by [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) for [The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug) and [The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies) and a child of [Dale](/wiki/Dale) in [The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey).

## Family {#Family}

- Daughter of [James Nesbitt](/wiki/James_Nesbitt) who portrayed [Bofur](/wiki/Bofur) in [The Hobbit (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)>)
- Daughter of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sonia_Forbes-Adam&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sonia Forbes-Adam (page does not exist)">Sonia Forbes-Adam</a> who portrayed [Belladonna Took](/wiki/Belladonna_Took) in [The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_(extended_edition)>)
- Younger sister of [Peggy Nesbitt](/wiki/Peggy_Nesbitt) who portrayed [Sigrid](/wiki/Sigrid) in The Hobbit (film series)
