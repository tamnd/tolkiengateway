**MBAR** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) basically meaning "settle, establish", but took on considerable semantic development.<sup id="cite_ref-fate_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fate-1">[1]</a></sup>

In origin, MBAR had the meaning of taking up a firm position in a way that seemed suitable and resolving matters that were in confusion or doubt had arisen; basically to make a decision. It was especially applied to the concept of choosing a region for physical occupation by a family or people and ordering it as "home"; it further developed to include the erection of permanent buildings and dwellings.<sup id="cite_ref-dwell_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwell-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) notes that English "'\[s\]ettle' is in fact, in its various branches of meaning, a very close rendering of the sense of base √MBAR".<sup id="cite_ref-pe17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pe17-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:163-4</sup>

MBAR eventually further evolved into concepts of the world as a whole ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md). [Ambar](../A/Ambar.md) / [S](../S/Sindarin.md). <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Amar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Amar (page does not exist)">Amar</a>) and fate (Q. [umbar](../U/Umbar.md) / S. [amarth](../A/Amarth.md)).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root MBARA, from which was derived [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) *mar* ("dwelling of men, -land, the Earth").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md) appear the root MBAR- signifying "dwell, inhabit" (emended from "dwell, build, inhabit").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
