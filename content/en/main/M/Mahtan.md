**Mahtan** was a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") and the father of [Nerdanel](/wiki/Nerdanel), the wife of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor).<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

A skilled smith in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), Mahtan learned the arts of metal and stone work under the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB), and for this Mahtan was an *[Aulendur](/wiki/Aulendur)* ("Servant of Aulë"). He wore a [copper](/wiki/Copper "Copper") circlet around his head and was known for his fondness for the metal. Thus he was called *Urundil* - 'copper-lover'.<sup id="cite_ref-Name_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Name-2">[2]</a></sup> Mahtan in turn taught Fëanor,<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-1">[1]</a></sup> the greatest of all Elven craftsmen, who—to Mahtan's regret—used this knowledge to forge the first weapons and armour in Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Mahtan had a beard, which was unusual for an Elf, especially one as young as he. Elves could only grow beards from the third [cycle of their lives](/wiki/Elven_life_cycle "Elven life cycle"), while Mahtan was an exception in being only early in his second.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Mahtan* seems to come from the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *mahta-* ("to handle"), a reference to the arts and skills of making.

## Other names {#Other_names}

His devotion to Aulë led to him becoming an <i><b>Aulendur</b></i> ("Servant of Aulë")<sup id="cite_ref-Name_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Name-2">[2]</a></sup> from *[-ndur](/wiki/-ndur)* ("to serve"). The earlier form of this name was Aulendil, which was later used by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) during his treachery to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). His other name <i><b>Urundil</b></i> means "Copper-lover"<sup id="cite_ref-Name_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Name-2">[2]</a></sup> from *urun* ("copper") and *[-ndil](/wiki/-ndil)* ("lover, friend").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> His other [epessë](/wiki/Epess%C3%AB "Epessë") <i><b>Rusco</b></i> means "Fox", a reference to his red-brown hair.<sup id="cite_ref-Sons_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sons-6">[6]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:353</sup>
