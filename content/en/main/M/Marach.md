**Marach** ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [282](/wiki/First_Age_282 "First Age 282")-[376](/wiki/First_Age_376 "First Age 376")<sup id="cite_ref-Hador_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hador-1">[1]</a></sup>) was one of the original leaders of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) when they migrated into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). He was the father of two sons, [Malach Aradan](/wiki/Malach "Malach") the elder, and [Imlach](/wiki/Imlach) the younger. Marach was the great-great-grandfather of [Hador Lórindol](/wiki/Hador "Hador").

## History {#History}

Marach's people were the most numerous of the groups of [Men](/wiki/Men) that moved westward over the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) (the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador)). Due to the size of Marach's host they kept together and moved slowly – so much so that although Marach was originally in the lead he was passed by the people of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor) and the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin). When the Haladin crossed over the mountains they were turned north by the unfriendship of the [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves). However, when the folk of Marach followed the Haladin a year later, marching in ordered companies of tall, warlike folk, the Elves of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand) stayed out of their way.

Marach's people did not tarry in Ossiriand for they had heard that the people of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor) had found a green and fertile land. Marching down the [Dwarf-road](/wiki/Dwarf-road_of_Beleriand "Dwarf-road of Beleriand") they settled to the south and east of the lands of [Baran](/wiki/Baran), son of Bëor, in a region called [Estolad](/wiki/Estolad).

Most of the people of Marach wanted to journey further west, so [Malach Aradan](/wiki/Malach "Malach"), the son of Marach, led them to the southern slopes of [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin). Marach however chose to remain in Estolad until his death.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Originally it was [Hador Lórindol](/wiki/Hador "Hador") who led the people of the third house of the Edain across the Ered Luin into Beleriand; this was changed so that Hador became Marach's descendant of the fourth generation.<sup id="cite_ref-Hador_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hador-1">[1]</a></sup>
