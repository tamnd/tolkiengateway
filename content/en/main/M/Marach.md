**Marach** ([F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [282](../F/First_Age_282.md)-[376](../F/First_Age_376.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Hador_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hador-1">[1]</a></sup>) was one of the original leaders of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) when they migrated into [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). He was the father of two sons, [Malach Aradan](Malach.md) the elder, and [Imlach](../I/Imlach.md) the younger. Marach was the great-great-grandfather of [Hador Lórindol](../H/Hador.md).

## History {#History}

Marach's people were the most numerous of the groups of [Men](Men.md) that moved westward over the [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) (the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md)). Due to the size of Marach's host they kept together and moved slowly – so much so that although Marach was originally in the lead he was passed by the people of [Bëor](../B/Bëor.md) and the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md). When the Haladin crossed over the mountains they were turned north by the unfriendship of the [Green-elves](../G/Green-elves.md). However, when the folk of Marach followed the Haladin a year later, marching in ordered companies of tall, warlike folk, the Elves of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md) stayed out of their way.

Marach's people did not tarry in Ossiriand for they had heard that the people of [Bëor](../B/Bëor.md) had found a green and fertile land. Marching down the [Dwarf-road](../D/Dwarf-road_of_Beleriand.md) they settled to the south and east of the lands of [Baran](../B/Baran.md), son of Bëor, in a region called [Estolad](../E/Estolad.md).

Most of the people of Marach wanted to journey further west, so [Malach Aradan](Malach.md), the son of Marach, led them to the southern slopes of [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md). Marach however chose to remain in Estolad until his death.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
