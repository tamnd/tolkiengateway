<b><i>Mallorn</i> 58</b> is the fifty-eighth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s annual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Winter [2017](../_/2017.md).
