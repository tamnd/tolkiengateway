**Merimas Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland).

## History {#History}

Merimas was the eldest child, and only son, of [Marmadas Brandybuck](/wiki/Marmadas_Brandybuck). He was present at [Bilbo's Birthday Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Merimas* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) suggests that *Merimas* is a variation of the name *[Merimac](/wiki/Merimac_Brandybuck "Merimac Brandybuck")* with an *-as* ending.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The first element, *Meri* also occurrs in *[Meriadoc](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck")*.
