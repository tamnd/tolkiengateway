**Mythcon 7** was the seventh annual [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) conference.

- **Theme:** Arthurian Elements in [Williams](/wiki/Charles_Williams "Charles Williams"), [Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis "C.S. Lewis"), and [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")
- **Guest of Honor:** Thomas Howard (Inklings scholar)
- **Chairman:** Bruce McMenomy
- **Coordinator:** Mary Morman
