<b><i>Mallorn</i> 1</b> is the first issue of the [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Society)'s journal *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")*, published in 1970.

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Editorial**
    - Editorial
        - By Rosemary Pardoe
    - Second Editorial...
        - By Darroll Pardoe
    - Belladonna's Postbag
        - By Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](/wiki/Vera_Chapman)), J.D. Collins, and A.R. Fallone
- **Notes**
    - Heraldry in *The Lord of the Rings*
        - By Sam Long
    - The Importance of Being Gollum
        - By J.D. Collins
    - Brief Notes on Heroic Fantasy
        - By Phil Spencer
    - The Languages of Middle-earth
        - By Bob Borsley
- **Reviews**
    - *[J.R.R.Tolkien](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_(by_Catherine_Stimpson)> "J.R.R. Tolkien (by Catherine Stimpson)")* by Catherine R. Stimpson (Columbia Essays on Modern Writers)
        - Reviewed by Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](/wiki/Vera_Chapman))
- **Creative**
    - Unicorn Poem (unitled)
        - By Rosemary Pardoe
    - Untitled poem ("In the early wind")
        - By Dave Weldrake
    - Illustrations
        - By Santos and by Dick Flinchbaugh
