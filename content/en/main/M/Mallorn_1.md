<b><i>Mallorn</i> 1</b> is the first issue of the [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Society.md)'s journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in 1970.

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Editorial**
    - Editorial
        - By Rosemary Pardoe
    - Second Editorial...
        - By Darroll Pardoe
    - Belladonna's Postbag
        - By Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md)), J.D. Collins, and A.R. Fallone
- **Notes**
    - Heraldry in *The Lord of the Rings*
        - By Sam Long
    - The Importance of Being Gollum
        - By J.D. Collins
    - Brief Notes on Heroic Fantasy
        - By Phil Spencer
    - The Languages of Middle-earth
        - By Bob Borsley
- **Reviews**
    - *[J.R.R.Tolkien](<../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_(by_Catherine_Stimpson).md>)* by Catherine R. Stimpson (Columbia Essays on Modern Writers)
        - Reviewed by Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md))
- **Creative**
    - Unicorn Poem (unitled)
        - By Rosemary Pardoe
    - Untitled poem ("In the early wind")
        - By Dave Weldrake
    - Illustrations
        - By Santos and by Dick Flinchbaugh
