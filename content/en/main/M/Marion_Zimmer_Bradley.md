**Marion Zimmer Bradley** ([3 June](/wiki/3_June) [1930](/wiki/1930) — [25 September](/wiki/25_September) [1999](/wiki/1999)), an American author of fantasy novels, also published some works relating to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and his [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").
