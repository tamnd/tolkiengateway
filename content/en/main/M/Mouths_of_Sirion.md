The **Mouths of Sirion** was the great delta where the [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md), the main river of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), emptied into the sea, in the north-east corner of the [Bay of Balar](../B/Bay_of_Balar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The land about the mouths was named *[Lisgardh](../L/Lisgardh.md)*;<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> it was a region of reeds dense as a forest.<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-6">[6]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Between [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1090](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1090.md) and [1092](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1092.md) the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) assailed [Morgoth](Morgoth.md) in [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> In the upheavals of that time the Sirion was created and its waters first flowed into the Bay of Balar, creating the Mouths of Sirion.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

During the years of Morgoth's captivity the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) spread across [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) and [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) with his mariners explored the coasts.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Doubtless they knew of the place where Sirion met [the Great Sea](../B/Belegaer.md), but no secret refuge was hidden in the reeds until much later, in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [473](../F/First_Age_473.md),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> the year after the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md). That was when [Brithombar](../B/Brithombar.md) and [Eglarest](../E/Eglarest.md) were destroyed by Morgoth's forces and Círdan with the remnants of his people fled to the [Isle of Balar](../I/Isle_of_Balar.md) or to the Mouths of Sirion where they established the secret havens.<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-6">[6]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [506](../F/First_Age_506.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Years_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-11">[11]</a></sup> the [Second Kinslaying](../S/Second_Kinslaying.md) occurred. [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) was destroyed and those few who escaped, including [Elwing](../E/Elwing.md), fled to the Havens of Sirion.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [510](../F/First_Age_510.md) [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) fell and by the next year<sup id="cite_ref-Years_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-11">[11]</a></sup> refugees from that kingdom, including [Idril](../I/Idril.md) and [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), came to the Havens.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> [Drúedain](../D/Drúedain.md) refugees from the fall of the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md) came also to the Mouths of Sirion, reduced to a small number of families of mainly women and children.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

[Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md) wedded Elwing in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [525](../F/First_Age_525.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Years_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-11">[11]</a></sup> and he was accounted the lord of those living in the Havens of Sirion. Eärendil became a friend of Círdan and with his aid built his ship [Vingilot](../V/Vingilot.md), in which he sailed west but was unable to reach [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-15">[15]</a></sup> Having learned of the survival of [Elwing](../E/Elwing.md) and [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md), [Maedhros](Maedhros.md) mustered a troupe to retrieve the [Silmaril](../S/Silmarils.md) that had been [his father's](../F/Fëanor.md). While Earendil was away in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [538](../F/First_Age_538.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Years_11-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-11">[11]</a></sup> the sons of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) attacked and though the forces of the remaining [Sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) were not very large, they ravaged the Havens and slaughtered most of the people. They could not find the Silmaril or Elwing, who went into the [Sea](../B/Belegaer.md) with it, and Círdan and [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) were too late to do anything. Elwing's sons, [Elros](../E/Elros.md) and [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md), had been found by [Maglor](Maglor.md), who raised them out of pity and remorse. Those few who escaped this [Third Kinslaying](../T/Third_Kinslaying.md) went to the Isle of Balar in the ships of Círdan and [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md). From that time on the Havens were in ruins and deserted.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-15">[15]</a></sup>

The chief army of the Valar landed at the Mouths of Sirion during the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md). By the end of the War, most of the survivors of Beleriand gathered at the Mouths and the Isle of Balar, and many of them went to [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) until the Elves were summoned to Valinor.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the text *The List of Names* written during the 1930s, the Mouths of Sirion was called *Eges-sirion*<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>, and this was changed subsequently to *Eges-hirion*. In the same text, Sirion's Haven was called *Siriombar*<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>, and this name does not appear anywhere else.
