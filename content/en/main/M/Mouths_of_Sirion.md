The **Mouths of Sirion** was the great delta where the [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion), the main river of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), emptied into the sea, in the north-east corner of the [Bay of Balar](/wiki/Bay_of_Balar).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The land about the mouths was named *[Lisgardh](/wiki/Lisgardh)*;<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> it was a region of reeds dense as a forest.<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-6">[6]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Between [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1090](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1090 "Year of the Trees 1090") and [1092](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1092 "Year of the Trees 1092") the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) assailed [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> In the upheavals of that time the Sirion was created and its waters first flowed into the Bay of Balar, creating the Mouths of Sirion.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

During the years of Morgoth's captivity the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) spread across [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) with his mariners explored the coasts.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Doubtless they knew of the place where Sirion met [the Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer"), but no secret refuge was hidden in the reeds until much later, in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [473](/wiki/First_Age_473 "First Age 473"),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> the year after the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad). That was when [Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar) and [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest) were destroyed by Morgoth's forces and Círdan with the remnants of his people fled to the [Isle of Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar) or to the Mouths of Sirion where they established the secret havens.<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-6">[6]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [506](/wiki/First_Age_506 "First Age 506")<sup id="cite_ref-Years_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-11">[11]</a></sup> the [Second Kinslaying](/wiki/Second_Kinslaying) occurred. [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) was destroyed and those few who escaped, including [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing), fled to the Havens of Sirion.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [510](/wiki/First_Age_510 "First Age 510") [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) fell and by the next year<sup id="cite_ref-Years_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-11">[11]</a></sup> refugees from that kingdom, including [Idril](/wiki/Idril) and [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), came to the Havens.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain) refugees from the fall of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) came also to the Mouths of Sirion, reduced to a small number of families of mainly women and children.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

[Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) wedded Elwing in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [525](/wiki/First_Age_525 "First Age 525")<sup id="cite_ref-Years_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-11">[11]</a></sup> and he was accounted the lord of those living in the Havens of Sirion. Eärendil became a friend of Círdan and with his aid built his ship [Vingilot](/wiki/Vingilot), in which he sailed west but was unable to reach [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-15">[15]</a></sup> Having learned of the survival of [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing) and [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil), [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) mustered a troupe to retrieve the [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmarils "Silmarils") that had been [his father's](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor "Fëanor"). While Earendil was away in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [538](/wiki/First_Age_538 "First Age 538")<sup id="cite_ref-Years_11-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Years-11">[11]</a></sup> the sons of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) attacked and though the forces of the remaining [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) were not very large, they ravaged the Havens and slaughtered most of the people. They could not find the Silmaril or Elwing, who went into the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") with it, and Círdan and [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) were too late to do anything. Elwing's sons, [Elros](/wiki/Elros) and [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), had been found by [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor), who raised them out of pity and remorse. Those few who escaped this [Third Kinslaying](/wiki/Third_Kinslaying) went to the Isle of Balar in the ships of Círdan and [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad). From that time on the Havens were in ruins and deserted.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-15">[15]</a></sup>

The chief army of the Valar landed at the Mouths of Sirion during the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath). By the end of the War, most of the survivors of Beleriand gathered at the Mouths and the Isle of Balar, and many of them went to [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) until the Elves were summoned to Valinor.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the text *The List of Names* written during the 1930s, the Mouths of Sirion was called *Eges-sirion*<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>, and this was changed subsequently to *Eges-hirion*. In the same text, Sirion's Haven was called *Siriombar*<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>, and this name does not appear anywhere else.
