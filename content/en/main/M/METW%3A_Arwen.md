**METW: Arwen** is a game card from the set *[Middle-earth: The Wizards](/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Wizards)*, for [MECCG](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game "Middle-earth Collectible Card Game").

<i><b>Note:</b></i> *"Arwen" refers to [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").*

## Card text {#Card_text}

<i><b>Unique.</b></i> +7 direct influence only usable against [Aragorn II](/wiki/METW:_Aragorn_II "METW: Aragorn II").

*"The braids of her dark hair were touched by no frost; her white arms and clear face were flawless and smooth, and the light of stars was in her eyes...queenly she looked, and thought and knowledge were in her glance..."* <i><b>-<a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">LotRII</a></b></i>
