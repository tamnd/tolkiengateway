<i><b>mellon</b></i> is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (and [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)) word for "friend".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Didier Willis](/wiki/Didier_Willis) has suggested that the plural form is *mellyn*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Sindarin *mellon* derives from ?[Old Sindarin](/wiki/Old_Sindarin) *melnā* ("dear, beloved"), from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") MEL ("love").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *mellon* derives from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") MEL ("love (as friend)").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
