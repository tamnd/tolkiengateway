<i><b>Minas Tirith Evening-Star</b></i> is a publication by the [American Tolkien Society](/wiki/American_Tolkien_Society).

## History {#History}

The journal began publication in [1967](/wiki/1967), founded and edited by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Philip_W._Helms&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Philip W. Helms (page does not exist)">Philip W. Helms</a>. In [1975](/wiki/1975), it merged with <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Letter-zine&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Letter-zine (page does not exist)">Letter-zine</a></i>, the news letter of the newly formed [American Tolkien Society](/wiki/American_Tolkien_Society), and it is published four times a year to this day.

The journal contains articles, poetry, reviews, fan fiction and artwork, and is devoted solely to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
