**Minas Tirith** was the tower on the island of [Tol Sirion](../T/Tol_Sirion.md) that guarded the [Pass of Sirion](../P/Pass_of_Sirion.md).

Minas Tirith was built around [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [60](../F/First_Age_60.md)<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup> by [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md) to prevent the forces of [Morgoth](Morgoth.md) from invading [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) from the plains of [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md). After Finrod built [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) he handed over control of the watchtower to his kinsman, [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Minas Tirith was captured by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) shortly after the initial onslaught of the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), driving Orodreth out to flee to Nargothrond. Sauron then made it into a watch-tower for Morgoth from which he espied all that passed through the vale. As Tol Sirion became accursed, it was then called **[Tol-in-Gaurhoth](../T/Tol-in-Gaurhoth.md)**, the "Isle of [Werewolves](../W/Werewolves.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-3">[3]</a></sup>

Although the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) regained control of the Pass of Sirion until the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md) ("Battle of Unnumbered Tears"), they did not attempt to rebuild the tower.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Minas Tirith means "Tower of the Guard" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) (from *[minas](Minas.md)* = "tower, citadel" and *[tirith](../T/Tirith.md)* = "watch, guard").

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) within *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, the predecessor of the tower of Minas Tirith was known as the **halls of Tevildo**, a castle on a hilltop that was not terribly distant from [Angamandi](../A/Angband.md). These halls were said to harbour a few mice, fierce and large as rats, that [Tevildo](../T/Tevildo.md) had allowed to dwell in dark holes for his own personal amusement.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:16</sup> During the [Quest for the Silmaril](../Q/Quest_for_the_Silmaril.md), [Beren](../B/Beren.md) was captured by [Melko](Morgoth.md) and was sent to work as a thrall within Tevildo's kitchens, where he was made a scullion<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:16–7</sup> under the watch of [Miaulë](Miaulë.md), Tevildo's cook.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:28</sup> The castle entrance was guarded by the old Doorcat, [Umuiyan](../U/Umuiyan.md), Tevildo's doorkeeper.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:23–5</sup> Once Tevildo was defeated by [Huan](../H/Huan.md), [Tinúviel](../T/Tinúviel.md) used his magical collar and spells to shake the castle, liberating the prisoners and turning Tevildo's servants into normal cats.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:29</sup>
