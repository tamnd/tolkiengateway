**Minas Tirith** was the tower on the island of [Tol Sirion](/wiki/Tol_Sirion) that guarded the [Pass of Sirion](/wiki/Pass_of_Sirion).

Minas Tirith was built around [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [60](/wiki/First_Age_60 "First Age 60")<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup> by [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") to prevent the forces of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) from invading [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) from the plains of [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen). After Finrod built [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) he handed over control of the watchtower to his kinsman, [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Minas Tirith was captured by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) shortly after the initial onslaught of the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), driving Orodreth out to flee to Nargothrond. Sauron then made it into a watch-tower for Morgoth from which he espied all that passed through the vale. As Tol Sirion became accursed, it was then called **[Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth)**, the "Isle of [Werewolves](/wiki/Werewolves)".<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-3">[3]</a></sup>

Although the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) regained control of the Pass of Sirion until the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad) ("Battle of Unnumbered Tears"), they did not attempt to rebuild the tower.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Minas Tirith means "Tower of the Guard" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (from *[minas](/wiki/Minas "Minas")* = "tower, citadel" and *[tirith](/wiki/Tirith "Tirith")* = "watch, guard").

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Tevildo_and_Tin%C3%BAviel.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Ted_Nasmith_-_Tevildo_and_Tin%C3%BAviel.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Tevildo_and_Tin%C3%BAviel.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="397" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/c/c9/Ted_Nasmith_-_Tevildo_and_Tin%C3%BAviel.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Tevildo_and_Tin%C3%BAviel.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/c/c9/Ted_Nasmith_-_Tevildo_and_Tin%C3%BAviel.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Tevildo_and_Tin%C3%BAviel.jpg 2x" data-file-width="816" data-file-height="1080"/></a><figcaption>&#34;<a href="/wiki/Tevildo" title="Tevildo">Tevildo</a> and <a href="/wiki/L%C3%BAthien" title="Lúthien">Tinúviel</a>&#34; by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

According to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") within *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the predecessor of the tower of Minas Tirith was known as the **halls of Tevildo**, a castle on a hilltop that was not terribly distant from [Angamandi](/wiki/Angband "Angband"). These halls were said to harbour a few mice, fierce and large as rats, that [Tevildo](/wiki/Tevildo) had allowed to dwell in dark holes for his own personal amusement.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:16</sup> During the [Quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril), [Beren](/wiki/Beren) was captured by [Melko](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") and was sent to work as a thrall within Tevildo's kitchens, where he was made a scullion<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:16–7</sup> under the watch of [Miaulë](/wiki/Miaul%C3%AB), Tevildo's cook.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:28</sup> The castle entrance was guarded by the old Doorcat, [Umuiyan](/wiki/Umuiyan), Tevildo's doorkeeper.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:23–5</sup> Once Tevildo was defeated by [Huan](/wiki/Huan), [Tinúviel](/wiki/Tin%C3%BAviel) used his magical collar and spells to shake the castle, liberating the prisoners and turning Tevildo's servants into normal cats.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:29</sup>
