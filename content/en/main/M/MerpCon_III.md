**MerpCon III** was the third annual free international convention dedicated to role playing gaming in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). The guest speaker was Doctor Thomas Morwinsky and the event was held in 2007 in Spokane, Washington.
