**Madril** was [Ranger of Ithilien](../R/Rangers_of_Ithilien.md) and [Faramir's](../F/Faramir.md) second in command. Madril was invented by [Warner Bros.](../W/Warner_Bros..md) for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), where he was portrayed by [John Bach](../J/John_Bach.md).

## History {#History}

Madril was a Ranger of some experience. He helped [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) in his ambush of [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md) in [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md), where they also captured [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md). After a short stay at [Henneth Annûn](../H/Henneth_Annûn.md), the company moved on to [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) to reinforce its garrison.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Despite initial doubts, the Orcs held off an attack. Madril sent scouts to [Cair Andros](../C/Cair_Andros.md) to see if there was any enemy activity in the North. However, the Orcs crossed the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) in Osgiliath with boats, and Faramir's men were caught unawares.

The orcs overpowered the Gondorian troops, and at Madril's advice, Faramir called for a retreat. During the call, Madril was badly wounded. He was left behind in the evacuation. As he lay dying, [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(Lieutenant_of_Morgul).md>) stabbed him with a spear, killing him outright.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
