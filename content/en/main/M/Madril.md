**Madril** was [Ranger of Ithilien](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien "Rangers of Ithilien") and [Faramir's](/wiki/Faramir "Faramir") second in command. Madril was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), where he was portrayed by [John Bach](/wiki/John_Bach).

## History {#History}

Madril was a Ranger of some experience. He helped [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) in his ambush of [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien), where they also captured [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"). After a short stay at [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn), the company moved on to [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) to reinforce its garrison.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Despite initial doubts, the Orcs held off an attack. Madril sent scouts to [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros) to see if there was any enemy activity in the North. However, the Orcs crossed the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) in Osgiliath with boats, and Faramir's men were caught unawares.

The orcs overpowered the Gondorian troops, and at Madril's advice, Faramir called for a retreat. During the call, Madril was badly wounded. He was left behind in the evacuation. As he lay dying, [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(Lieutenant_of_Morgul)> "Gothmog (Lieutenant of Morgul)") stabbed him with a spear, killing him outright.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
