**Michael Reisz** (1968) is an American actor and voice actor. He voiced [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game).md>)* game, and also did [Milo Burrows](Milo_Burrows.md) uncredited.
