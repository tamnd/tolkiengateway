**Michael Reisz** (1968) is an American actor and voice actor. He voiced [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)")* game, and also did [Milo Burrows](/wiki/Milo_Burrows) uncredited.
