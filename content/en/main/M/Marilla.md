<i><b>marilla</b></i> is a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) noun for "pearl".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate was *[brithla](/wiki/Brithla "Brithla")*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Silmaril" title="Silmaril">Sil<b>maril</b></a></i> = "Moon Pearl"
