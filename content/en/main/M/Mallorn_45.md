<b><i>Mallorn</i> 45</b> is the forty-fifth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s biannual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Spring [2008](../_/2008.md). Mallorn 45 was the first issue to be published since [Mallorn 44](Mallorn_44.md) in [August](../A/August.md) [2006](../_/2006.md) due to the change of editor and overhaul of the publication; it was the first Mallorn to be published biannually.
