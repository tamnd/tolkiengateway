<b><i>Mallorn</i> 45</b> is the forty-fifth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s biannual journal *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")*, published in Spring [2008](/wiki/2008). Mallorn 45 was the first issue to be published since [Mallorn 44](/wiki/Mallorn_44) in [August](/wiki/August) [2006](/wiki/2006) due to the change of editor and overhaul of the publication; it was the first Mallorn to be published biannually.
