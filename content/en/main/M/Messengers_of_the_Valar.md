The **messengers of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar)** were a group of people who came to the isle of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) from the [West](/wiki/Aman "Aman") in the middle of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). They appeared during the reign of [Tar-Atanamir](/wiki/Tar-Atanamir), the thirteenth [King](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor"). Tar-Atanamir ruled during a time of great upheaval in Númenor; he and most of his people had begun to question the justice of their mortal state and the [Ban of the Valar](/wiki/Ban_of_the_Valar).

The messengers (it is never said whether they were [Elves](/wiki/Elves) or [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar)) explained to the Númenóreans why they could not sail to the [Blessed Realm](/wiki/Blessed_Realm), and urged them not to spurn the [Gift of Men](/wiki/Gift_of_Men), for this would merely make life harder for them. The Númenóreans asked them many questions about the nature of [Men](/wiki/Men) in return. A part of this converse was later recorded in the *[Akallabêth](/wiki/Akallab%C3%AAth)*.

In the end, the greater part of the Númenóreans decided to ignore the advice of the messengers, and drifted away from the friendship of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) and the Valar. This group became known as the [King's Men](/wiki/King%27s_Men), for most of the later Kings of Númenor supported their policies. The small number of Númenóreans who did put their [trust](/wiki/Estel "Estel") in the messengers' words came to call themselves the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
