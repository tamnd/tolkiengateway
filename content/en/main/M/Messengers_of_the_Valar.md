The **messengers of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md)** were a group of people who came to the isle of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) from the [West](../A/Aman.md) in the middle of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md). They appeared during the reign of [Tar-Atanamir](../T/Tar-Atanamir.md), the thirteenth [King](../K/King_of_Númenor.md). Tar-Atanamir ruled during a time of great upheaval in Númenor; he and most of his people had begun to question the justice of their mortal state and the [Ban of the Valar](../B/Ban_of_the_Valar.md).

The messengers (it is never said whether they were [Elves](../E/Elves.md) or [Maiar](Maiar.md)) explained to the Númenóreans why they could not sail to the [Blessed Realm](../B/Blessed_Realm.md), and urged them not to spurn the [Gift of Men](../G/Gift_of_Men.md), for this would merely make life harder for them. The Númenóreans asked them many questions about the nature of [Men](Men.md) in return. A part of this converse was later recorded in the *[Akallabêth](../A/Akallabêth.md)*.

In the end, the greater part of the Númenóreans decided to ignore the advice of the messengers, and drifted away from the friendship of the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) and the Valar. This group became known as the [King's Men](../K/King's_Men.md), for most of the later Kings of Númenor supported their policies. The small number of Númenóreans who did put their [trust](../E/Estel.md) in the messengers' words came to call themselves the [Faithful](../F/Faithful.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
