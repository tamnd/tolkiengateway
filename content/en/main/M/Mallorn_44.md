<b><i>Mallorn</i> 44</b> is the forty-fourth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s annual (as it was then) journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in [August](../A/August.md) [2006](../_/2006.md).

Mallorn 44 was the last issue to be published under the more academic format before the journal was revamped by the new editor, [Henry Gee](../H/Henry_Gee.md); as a result of the change of editor and format, [Mallorn 45](Mallorn_45.md) wasn't published until Spring [2008](../_/2008.md), over 18 months later.
