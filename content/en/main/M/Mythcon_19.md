**Mythcon 19** was the nineteenth annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme:** "Legends for a New Land: Fantasy in America"
- **Guests of Honor:** [Ursula K. Le Guin](../U/Ursula_K._Le_Guin.md) (fantasy author) and Brian Attebery (fantasy scholar)
- **Chairman:** [David Bratman](../D/David_Bratman.md)
