The **Morthond** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) "Black root") was one of the seven rivers of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

## Course {#Course}

The Morthond arose in the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) in a [narrow valley](Mornan.md) that led to the southern entrance of the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-1">[1]</a></sup> The river rose beneath the sheer mountainsides of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md)' southern extent,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and flowed south-west for half its length before turning south-east to join the [Ringló](../R/Ringló.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> near [Cobas Haven](../C/Cobas_Haven.md).<sup id="cite_ref-West_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Near the confluence of the Morthond and the Ringló was the ancient Elf-haven of [Edhellond](../E/Edhellond.md). This refuge had been founded at the beginning of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) by a remnant of [Elves](../E/Elves.md) from [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

At the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the lord of the [Blackroot Vale](../B/Blackroot_Vale.md) was [Duinhir](../D/Duinhir.md). He and his two sons, [Duilin](<../D/Duilin_(son_of_Duinhir).md>) and [Derufin](../D/Derufin.md), marched to [Minas Tirith](Minas_Tirith.md) with five hundred bowmen to aid in the city's defence.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> On [8 March](../_/8_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md), and the [Army of the Dead](../O/Oathbreakers.md) exited from the Paths of the Dead alongside the Morthond.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> They followed the young river before crossing a bridge on their way to the [Stone of Erech](../S/Stone_of_Erech.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Morthond* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name meaning "black-root".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The first element *mor* means "dark" or "black".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> The second element *thond* means "root".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The course and placement of the Morthond changed greatly during the writing of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. In the [First Map of The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) the Morthond was separate from and far west of the Ringló (with [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md) far to the west of the Morthond).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> When [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) developed Outline VI for Book V in *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)* he drew a map with the Morthond east of the Ringló and the Morthond joined the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) near its mouth.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> When Tolkien made the detailed [Second Map of The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Second_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) there were four rivers – [Calenhir](../C/Calenhir.md), Morthond, Kiril, and Ringló – that flowed independently until they all combined just before exiting into Cobas Haven.<sup id="cite_ref-West_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-4">[4]</a></sup>
