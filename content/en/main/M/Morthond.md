The **Morthond** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") "Black root") was one of the seven rivers of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## Course {#Course}

The Morthond arose in the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) in a [narrow valley](/wiki/Mornan "Mornan") that led to the southern entrance of the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead).<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-1">[1]</a></sup> The river rose beneath the sheer mountainsides of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains)' southern extent,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and flowed south-west for half its length before turning south-east to join the [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> near [Cobas Haven](/wiki/Cobas_Haven).<sup id="cite_ref-West_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Near the confluence of the Morthond and the Ringló was the ancient Elf-haven of [Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond). This refuge had been founded at the beginning of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) by a remnant of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) from [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the lord of the [Blackroot Vale](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale) was [Duinhir](/wiki/Duinhir). He and his two sons, [Duilin](</wiki/Duilin_(son_of_Duinhir)> "Duilin (son of Duinhir)") and [Derufin](/wiki/Derufin), marched to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) with five hundred bowmen to aid in the city's defence.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> On [8 March](/wiki/8_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company), and the [Army of the Dead](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") exited from the Paths of the Dead alongside the Morthond.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> They followed the young river before crossing a bridge on their way to the [Stone of Erech](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech).<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Morthond* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "black-root".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The first element *mor* means "dark" or "black".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> The second element *thond* means "root".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The course and placement of the Morthond changed greatly during the writing of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. In the [First Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The First Map of The Lord of the Rings") the Morthond was separate from and far west of the Ringló (with [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth) far to the west of the Morthond).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> When [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) developed Outline VI for Book V in *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)* he drew a map with the Morthond east of the Ringló and the Morthond joined the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) near its mouth.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> When Tolkien made the detailed [Second Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Second_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Second Map of The Lord of the Rings") there were four rivers – [Calenhir](/wiki/Calenhir), Morthond, Kiril, and Ringló – that flowed independently until they all combined just before exiting into Cobas Haven.<sup id="cite_ref-West_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-4">[4]</a></sup>
