**MinasTirith.com** was founded on [May 21](May_21.md), [2000](../_/2000.md) by White Gold Wielder ("WGW"), and was active up until early [2021](../_/2021.md) when it went offline for unknown reasons, making it one of the longest running [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) online communities. The forum was known for their scholarly discussions and tight-knit community.
