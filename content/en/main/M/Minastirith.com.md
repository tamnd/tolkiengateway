**MinasTirith.com** was founded on [May 21](/wiki/May_21), [2000](/wiki/2000) by White Gold Wielder ("WGW"), and was active up until early [2021](/wiki/2021) when it went offline for unknown reasons, making it one of the longest running [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") online communities. The forum was known for their scholarly discussions and tight-knit community.
