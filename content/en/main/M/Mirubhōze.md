<i><b>mirubhōze</b></i> is a [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) word, meaning "a honey wine".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup> The form *mirubhōzē-* is said to be "the beginning of a longer word".<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup>

It contains the element *mirub*/*mirub-* ("wine").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup>
