The **Maiar** (plural for **Maia)** were those [spirits](../S/Spirits.md) which descended into [Ëa](../_/Ëa.md) to assist the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) in the shaping of [Arda](../A/Arda.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> The Maiar and Valar were collectively known as the [Ainur](../A/Ainur.md), though the Maiar were of lesser power and majesty.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Their number is unknown and few were named, and not all took visible shapes in [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Chief among the Maiar were [Eönwë](../E/Eönwë.md), the banner-bearer and herald of [Manwë](Manwë.md)<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> who led the [Host of the West](../H/Host_of_the_Valar.md) in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and [Ilmarë](../I/Ilmarë.md), the handmaid of [Varda](../V/Varda.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">[3]</a></sup>

Also notable among the Maiar were [Arien](../A/Arien.md), a maiden of [Vána](../V/Vána.md), and [Tilion](../T/Tilion.md), a hunter of [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md). Arien guided the vessel of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) through [Ilmen](../I/Ilmen.md) and Tilion guided the [Moon](Moon.md). They were given this task after [Morgoth](Morgoth.md) and [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md) destroyed the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md), [Laurelin](../L/Laurelin.md) and [Telperion](../T/Telperion.md), during the [Darkening of Valinor](../D/Darkening_of_Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Each of the Maiar was associated with one or more particular Vala or Valie. For example, [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md) and [Uinen](../U/Uinen.md), as spirits of the [Sea](../S/Sea.md), were vassals of [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">[3]</a></sup> The Maia [Salmar](../S/Salmar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> - also of Ulmo's people - was the maker of the [Ulumúri](../U/Ulumúri.md), great horns through which Ulmo produced the music of the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> [Melian](Melian.md), who served the Valier Vána and [Estë](../E/Estë.md),<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">[3]</a></sup> would later become wife to [King](../K/King_of_Doriath.md) [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) and mother to [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Mairon](Mairon.md), originally a pupil of [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) the Smith, later became known as the Dark Lord Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Also serving under Aulë's tutelage was [Curunír](../S/Saruman.md),<sup id="cite_ref-:3_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-10">[10]</a></sup> who would later become known as Saruman the White. [Aiwendil](../A/Aiwendil.md), later known as [Radagast](../R/Radagast.md) the Brown, belonged to the people of [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:3_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-10">[10]</a></sup> [Olórin](../G/Gandalf.md), the wisest of the Maiar,<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">[3]</a></sup> was a servant to both [Manwë](Manwë.md) and [Varda](../V/Varda.md) - though he often found himself in the house of [Nienna](../N/Nienna.md), from whom he learned pity and patience.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> He would later be known as Gandalf the Grey.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> These three Maiar would come to be counted among the order of the [*Istari*](../I/Istar.md), more commonly known as the "Wizards", who were sent by the Valar to assist the [free peoples](../F/Free_peoples~19934.md) of Middle-earth in their efforts against Sauron in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:3_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-10">[10]</a></sup>

Of the remaining two Wizards, little is told. Sent by the Vala [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md), they clad themselves in sea-blue and traveled together to the East and came to be called the *[Ithryn Luin](../B/Blue_Wizards.md),* or "Blue Wizards"*.*<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[note 1]</a></sup> Among their many varied names were Alatar and Palando,<sup id="cite_ref-:3_10-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-10">[10]</a></sup> Morinehtar and Rómestámo,<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup> as well as Palacendo and Haimenar.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup> However, they never returned and their later fate is unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup>

The [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md) or *Valaraukar*<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[16]</a></sup> were of a separate class of spirits known as the [Úmaiar](../_/Úmaiar.md) who were corrupted by [Melkor](Morgoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[17]</a></sup> Among these were [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md>) and [Durin's Bane](../D/Durin's_Bane.md).

## Appearance {#Appearance}

The presence of the Maiar in their unclad (invisible) form was revealed by their fragrance.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[18]</a></sup>

However, those of the Maiar which were corrupted by Melkor ([Úmaiar](../_/Úmaiar.md)) emitted a foul stench when invisible, which is why the Úmaiar never appeared unclad when trying to deceive or persuade the Eldar.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[19]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name *Maiar* means "the Beautiful".<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[20]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest forms of the legendarium, such as *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, many beings are said to be among the lesser Ainur, including [dryads](../N/Nandini.md) and [mermaids](Mermaids.md). The concept of the Maiar was introduced in the reworking of the *[Annals of Valinor](../A/Annals_of_Valinor.md)* into the *[Annals of Aman](../A/Annals_of_Aman.md)*, and superseded most references to such beings.<sup id="cite_ref-MRP2b_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MRP2b-22">[21]</a></sup>

[Lungorthin](../L/Lungorthin.md) was an early Balrog, though he did not show up in later writings.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[22]</a></sup>

In a c. [1970](../_/1970.md) text, J.R.R. Tolkien refers to the "mighty speaking eagles" as Maiar:<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[23]</a></sup>

> *...The most notable were those Maiar who took the form of the mighty speaking eagles that we hear of in the legends of the war of the Ñoldor against Melkor, and who remained in the West of Middle-earth until the fall of Sauron and the Dominion of Men, after which they are not heard of again.*
