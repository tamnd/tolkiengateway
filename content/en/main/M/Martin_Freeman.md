<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

**Martin John Christopher Freeman** portrayed [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in *[The Hobbit (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)>)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Announcement_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Announcement-1">[1]</a></sup> The role was previously played by [Ian Holm](/wiki/Ian_Holm) in [*The Lord of the Rings* films](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").

## Casting {#Casting}

As far back as [2007](/wiki/2007) rumors were circulating by fans that Freeman would be an ideal candidate.<sup id="cite_ref-MTV_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MTV-2">[2]</a></sup> On [October 11](/wiki/11_October "11 October"), [2010](/wiki/2010) Freeman confirmed he had to turn down the role due to scheduling conflicts,<sup id="cite_ref-Empire_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Empire-3">[3]</a></sup> however he was officially announced for the role on [October 21](/wiki/21_October "21 October"), 2010.<sup id="cite_ref-Wired_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wired-4">[4]</a></sup>
