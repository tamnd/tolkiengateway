The **Mound of the First Sorrow** was the tomb of [Bruithwir](/wiki/Bruithwir), father of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One "The Book of Lost Tales Part One")*. Its [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name was *Cûm a Gumlaith* or *Cûm a Thegranaithos*.<sup id="cite_ref-LT1VI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1VI-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Following the theft of the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils) by [Melko](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") and his slaying of Bruithwir, Fëanor brooded by the tomb of his father and decided that the [Gnomes](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") should make war against Melko.<sup id="cite_ref-LT1VI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1VI-1">[1]</a></sup>
