**Morwen** was the daughter of [Húrin (Warden of the Keys)](</wiki/H%C3%BArin_(Warden_of_the_Keys)>), in the drafts of the [Appendices](/wiki/Appendices). She was to be wed to [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer), but her character was ultimately abandoned and replaced with that of [Lothíriel](/wiki/Loth%C3%ADriel).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
