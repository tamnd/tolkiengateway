**Mark A. Latham** is a British game designer, writer and editor.

Latham’s professional involvement with Middle-earth began at Games Workshop, where he served as a staff writer and eventually the editor for the partwork magazine *[Battle Games in Middle-earth](../B/Battle_Games_in_Middle-earth.md)*. He later became the editor of *[White Dwarf](../W/White_Dwarf.md)* magazine and the managing editor of Games Workshop’s games development department, overseeing various projects including [The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game](Middle-earth_Strategy_Battle_Game.md), until he left the company to pursue a freelance career.<sup id="cite_ref-About_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-About-1">[1]</a></sup>

Beyond his work for Games Workshop, Latham was a background writer for *[Erebor: The Lonely Mountain](<../E/Erebor_(The_One_Ring_role-playing_game).md>)*, a supplement for *[The One Ring](<../T/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md>)* role-playing game.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
