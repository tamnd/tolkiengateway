**Maria Isabel Morna Braga** (born [1919](../_/1919.md)) was a Portuguese author and translator.

Maria Isabel Bragaa became the first Portuguese translator of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s work, when he contributed for the first Portuguese-language edition of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* ([O Gnomo](../O/O_Gnomo.md)), together with [Mário Braga](Mário_Braga.md).
