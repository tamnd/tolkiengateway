**Maria Isabel Morna Braga** (born [1919](/wiki/1919)) was a Portuguese author and translator.

Maria Isabel Bragaa became the first Portuguese translator of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s work, when he contributed for the first Portuguese-language edition of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* ([O Gnomo](/wiki/O_Gnomo)), together with [Mário Braga](/wiki/M%C3%A1rio_Braga).
