**Messrs Grubb, Grubb and Burrowes** was a notary firm in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) led by two [Grubbs](../G/Grubb.md) and one [Burrowes](../B/Burrowes.md). They were probably lawyers or auctioneers.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Grubb, Grubb and Burrowes oversaw the auction of [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)' estate after the hobbit went missing in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1341](../T/Third_Age_2941.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, Messrs. [Iago Grubb](../I/Iago_Grubb.md) and [Folco Burrowes](../F/Folco_Burrowes.md) are [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)'s (at that stage called Bingo) lawyers and eject [Sancho Proudfoot](../S/Sancho_Proudfoot.md) who was trying to steal from the pantry in the chaos of hobbits trying to collect their parting gifts from Bilbo.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
