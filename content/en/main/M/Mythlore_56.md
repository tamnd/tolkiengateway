<b><i>Mythlore</i> 56</b> (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/mythlore/vol15/iss2/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Volume 15, Issue 2</a>) is an issue of the *[Mythlore](/wiki/Mythlore)* journal, published by the [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) and featuring a cover illustration by [Patrick Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_Wynne).

The article "Narqelion: A Single, Falling Leaf at Sun-fading" by [Paul Nolan Hyde](/wiki/Paul_Nolan_Hyde) (reprinted in *[Vinyar Tengwar 6](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_6)*) includes the full text of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem '[Narqelion](/wiki/Narqelion)' (4 lines were reproduced in [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter)'s *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography)* in 1977). The poem is also reproduced in a computer tengwar font on the lower cover.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
