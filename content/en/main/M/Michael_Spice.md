**Michael Spice** ([May 20](/wiki/20_May "20 May"), [1931](/wiki/1931)-[November 2](/wiki/2_November "2 November"), [1983](/wiki/1983)) voiced the part of [Háma](/wiki/H%C3%A1ma) in [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") broadcasted on the BBC 4. He also did other voices, such as one of the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl).
