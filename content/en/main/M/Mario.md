<i><b>Mario</b></i> is a German illustrated Text-Adventure and parody which is based on *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*. All names are changed.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> It covers chapter 4 and 5 of Book 2 of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
