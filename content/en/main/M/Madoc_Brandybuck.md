**Madoc "Proudneck" Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland).

## History {#History}

Madoc was the son of [Gormadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gormadoc_Brandybuck) and [Malva Headstrong](/wiki/Malva_Headstrong). He married to [Hanna Goldworthy](/wiki/Hanna_Goldworthy) and was the father of [Marmadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Marmadoc_Brandybuck). He served as [Master of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland) between [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1236](/wiki/Third_Age_2836 "Third Age 2836") and [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1277](/wiki/Third_Age_2877 "Third Age 2877").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Madoc* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Madoc* is a [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh) name, which is derived from the element *mad* ("fortunate"). According to a tradition a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madoc" title="wikipedia:Madoc">Madoc</a> who was the son of Owen Gwynedd discovered America around A.D. 1170. A *Madok de la Montayne* is mentioned by Malory in *Le Morte Darthur*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
