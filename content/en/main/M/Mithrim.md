**Mithrim** was a region in the south-east part of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md). It was the area around [Lake Mithrim](../L/Lake_Mithrim.md), surrounded by the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md), and the **[Mountains of Mithrim](Mountains_of_Mithrim.md)**, which separated Mithrim from [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) to the west.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Mithrim's climate was the same as Hithlum's; the air was cool and the winter was cold but it was a fair land.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-2">[2]</a></sup> Swans were frequently seen in the pools of the land.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Mithrim was first inhabited by the [Grey-elves of Mithrim](../E/Elves_of_Mithrim.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Return_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-4">[4]</a></sup> and then also by [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) and [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-5">[5]</a></sup>

Mithrim contained deposits of silver, copper, and tin.<sup id="cite_ref-nature_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nature-6">[6]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Some time before the rising of the [Moon](Moon.md), [Sindarin Elves](../S/Sindar.md) from Beleriand came over [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) and settled in Mithrim.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-4">[4]</a></sup>

When the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) returned to Middle-earth the host of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) camped upon the northern shore of [Lake Mithrim](../L/Lake_Mithrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-4">[4]</a></sup> While here, Fëanor explored the ground and discovered its metals.<sup id="cite_ref-nature_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nature-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Morgoth](Morgoth.md) sent a host of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) through the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) to attack the newcomers and thus upper Mithrim was the scene of the [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](../D/Dagor-nuin-Giliath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-4">[4]</a></sup> Fëanor was killed shortly after when he led an overextended force that was ambushed; the metals in Mithrim contributed greatly to his rashness by trying too soon to conquer the region.<sup id="cite_ref-nature_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nature-6">[6]</a></sup>

The Noldor met the [Elves of Mithrim](../E/Elves_of_Mithrim.md) and learned from them of King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md), and tidings of what had transpired during their stay in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-4">[4]</a></sup>

At the rising of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) the host of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) marched into Mithrim. After approaching [Angband](../A/Angband.md) Fingolfin's host returned to Mithrim and camped upon the northern shore of Lake Mithrim. Due to the rift between Fingolfin's people and the followers of Fëanor, the latter relocated to the southern shores of the lake.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-4">[4]</a></sup>

At a [council in Mithrim](../C/Council_in_Mithrim.md) the princes of the Noldor gathered and, among other things, selected Fingolfin as [High King](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md).

Later in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) Mithrim was ruled by Fingolfin, as it formed the most densely populated part of Hithlum.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-2">[2]</a></sup> However, after the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md) Morgoth overran Mithrim and all of Hithlum and afterwards turned the land over to the [Swarthy Men](../S/Swarthy_Men.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In the winter of the year of the Nirnaeth [Rían](../R/Rían.md) gave birth to [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) in the wilds of Mithrim, where some Grey-elves yet remained. [Annael](../A/Annael.md) of the Elves who dwelt there in the caves of [Androth](../A/Androth.md) fostered Tuor after Rían departed Hithlum.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-5">[5]</a></sup>

All of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) and the lands to its north were deluged in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) at the end of that Age.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Mithrim* is composed of the roots *[mith](Mith.md)* ("grey, light grey") and the mass plural *[rim](../R/Rim.md)* ("great number, host").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The name refers to the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) who first dwelt there.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
