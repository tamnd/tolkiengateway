**Mablung** was an [Elf of Doriath](/wiki/Iathrim "Iathrim") and the chief captain of [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>

He was chiefly remembered in legend for his role in the [the hunt for Carcharoth](/wiki/Hunting_of_the_Wolf "Hunting of the Wolf"), where he retrieved the [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) from [the Wolf](/wiki/Carcharoth "Carcharoth")'s belly.

He also played a tragic role in many events related to [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) such as the altercations between Túrin and [Saeros](/wiki/Saeros), led the guard of [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen) and [Nienor](/wiki/Nienor) before they were lost, and confirmed the identity of Nienor to Túrin before his suicide.

## History {#History}

### Early history {#Early_history}

Mablung is first mentioned at his attendance of the [Mereth Aderthad](/wiki/Mereth_Aderthad) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [20](/wiki/First_Age_20 "First Age 20"), with he and [Daeron](/wiki/Daeron) being the only two messengers bringing the greetings of Thingol.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Hunting of the Wolf {#Hunting_of_the_Wolf}

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [466](/wiki/First_Age_466 "First Age 466"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> during the [Quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril), Thingol learned that his daughter, [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien), was being held in [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) after receiving an embassy sent by [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) declaring that he would wed her. Thinking to make war on Nargothrond to retrieve her, Thingol sent out spies and further learned that Celegorm and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) had since been driven from Nargothrond, but Lúthien had fled and was now missing. Thingol then sent messengers, including Mablung, to [Himring](/wiki/Himring) to summon the aid of the [House of Fëanor](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor) in seeking for her, since it was by their doing she had become lost. These messengers did not complete their task however, as they unexpectedly encountered Carcharoth; the wolf had consumed a Silmaril and, maddened with pain, burst into [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) unhindered by the [Girdle of Melian](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian). Alone of the messengers Mablung escaped and brought the dread tidings to Thingol.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>

At nearly the same time [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and Lúthien returned, and shared their story, including how Carcharoth came to consume the Silmaril after biting off Beren's hand. Shortly after, with Carcharoth continuing to rampage and daily drawing closer to [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth), Thingol organized a great Hunting of the Wolf, accompanied by Mablung, Beren, [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg), and [Huan](/wiki/Huan). During the hunt Carcharoth came upon Thingol and Beren, and was slain, but slew Huan and mortally wounded Beren in turn.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>

Mablung and Beleg then hastened to the King's aid, but in seeing what had been done cast aside their spears and wept. Mablung then cut open the Wolf's belly and found that while Carcharoth's innards had been consumed as though by fire, the hand of Beren still held the Silmaril and remained incorrupt.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup> Mablung attempted to retrieve them, but so great seemed their weight that Mablung's own hand was dragged downward and forced open, letting Beren's hand fall to the ground.<sup id="cite_ref-SMQ13_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SMQ13-4">[4]</a></sup> Beren's hand was then no more, leaving the Silmaril unveiled and filing the forest with its light. Quickly, and in fear, Mablung placed the Silmaril in Beren's remaining hand, with its touch rousing him enough to bid Thingol to receive it.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>

Mablung then helped bear the bodies of Beren and Huan back to Menegroth.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Battle of Unnumbered Tears {#Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears}

Unwilling to have no part in the battle, Thingol gave leave to Mablung to join the [great assault on Morgoth](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad "Nirnaeth Arnoediad") so long as he did not serve the House of the Fëanor; as such he joined himself to the host of [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> At this battle Mablung saved three of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) who fought under [Haldir](</wiki/Haldir_(Chieftain_of_Brethil)> "Haldir (Chieftain of Brethil)"), and healed them of their wounds.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### The death of Saeros {#The_death_of_Saeros}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Saeros%27_Fatal_Leap_2.jpg"><img src="/w/images/6/67/Ted_Nasmith_-_Saeros%27_Fatal_Leap_2.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="298" height="450" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="298" data-file-height="450"/></a><figcaption>&#34;Saeros&#39; Fatal Leap&#34; by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

Mablung became entwined in the Doom of [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin)'s children when he interceded in a confrontation between [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) and [Saeros](/wiki/Saeros) (a counselor to the King) in [Thingol's](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") hall. Mablung's wise warning to Saeros to stay clear of him after he was injured and almost run through for his taunting of Túrin fell on deaf and prideful ears. The following day Saeros waylaid Túrin as he traveled through the woods back to the marches to continue his personal war against the Shadow. Mablung was not privy to the sneak attack Saeros executed, nor to his swift defeat at the hands of Túrin.

Mablung was among a group that was alerted by Saeros' frantic calls as Túrin pursued the now disarmed and stripped elf through the woods (revenge for the taunting Saeros had delivered the night before). Such was Saeros' fear, as he meant to kill Túrin and was certain this was to be returned in kind, that he fled wildly through the woods with Túrin ever behind him.

Only the swiftest could keep up with Túrin, and Mablung was at the forefront of these. Mablung called at Túrin to stop the "Orc-work," but Túrin called back it was only "Orc-play" and was spurned on in his pursuit by Mablung's discourse. Soon after, Saeros attempted to escape his relentless pursuer by leaping across a stream, but instead lost his footing and fell to his death. Turin, who had meant to stop his pursuit at the river, turned and faced his pursuers and, in his pride, refused to defend his actions. Instead, he rejected the King's justice and gave the witnesses the choice of letting him go or slaying him. As their number were too few to take Túrin alive, Mablung let the young man go with a warning that if he were to leave this way a shadow would be over him.

At Túrin's hearing before the King, Mablung defended Túrin for the confrontation in the hall but could give no reason for his hounding of the King's councilor to his death. Beleg, Túrin's greatest friend and brother-in-arms, brought forward a witness who saw Saeros' treacherous attack and thus saved Túrin from the King's doom. Mablung, having heard the full story, expressed his sorrow at his parting words to Túrin.

### The guarding of Morwen and hunt for Nienor {#The_guarding_of_Morwen_and_hunt_for_Nienor}

Mablung was in charge to guard [Morwen Eledhwen](/wiki/Morwen "Morwen"), who had dwelt in Doriath at the time, when she set out to Nargothrond in search of her son [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_Turambar). Unknown to both Mablung and Morwen, [Nienor](/wiki/Nienor) daughter of Morwen, had joined them.

The women were left back upon [Amon Ethir](/wiki/Amon_Ethir) and Mablung set out with a small company to scout Nargothrond as were his orders by Thingol. Yet [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) left Nargothrond and Mablung was unable to prevent them both from getting ensnared in the traps of the [Dragon](/wiki/Dragon). He hid from Glaurung and then searched the sacked Nargothrond, finding no sign of Túrin. Glaurung when returning mocked Mablung, sparing his life, and informing him that he had now lost also Morwen and Nienor.

Eventually Mablung did find Nienor, enchanted by Glaurung, only to lose her again during an attack by [Orc-hunters](/wiki/Orc-hunters). Defeated, Mablung returned to Doriath and due to his failure at Nargothrond he asked Thingol to dismiss him. Thingol judged that the failure hardly was Mablung's fault and that Glaurung was too mighty an enemy for him and kept Mablung in his position. Nevertheless, he spent years afterwards searching for Morwen and Nienor.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Learning that Glaurung was in the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Forest_of_Brethil), Mablung went there, and met Túrin who had just slain the dragon. By admitting Nienor was lost he caused Túrin to realize that Níniel his wife had actually been his sister Nienor. As a consequence, Túrin ran off and Mablung found him shortly after fallen upon his own [sword](/wiki/Gurthang "Gurthang").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

### Later deeds<sup><a href="#Ælfwine_and_Dírhaval">NB</a></sup> {#Later_deedsNB}

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [503](/wiki/First_Age_503 "First Age 503"), during the [attack on Doriath](/wiki/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves "Battle in the Thousand Caves") by the [Dwarves of Nogrod](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Nogrod), Mablung fell<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[note 1]</a></sup> in front of the treasury where the [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr) was kept.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[note 2]</a></sup>

Sometime later, Mablung was cited as being one of the chief sources of information for the poet [Dírhaval](/wiki/D%C3%ADrhaval) who lived in the [Havens of Sirion](/wiki/Havens_of_Sirion) in the days of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name *Mablung* is translated as "with weighted hand". The name might either have been [prophetic](/wiki/Foresight "Foresight") or given as a title after the cutting of the Silmaril.<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-13">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SMQ13_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SMQ13-4">[4]</a></sup> The name is analyzed as [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin), with <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mab&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mab (page does not exist)">mab</a></i> "hand" and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lung&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lung (page does not exist)">lung</a></i> "heavy".<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[12]</a></sup> He is also referred as "Mablung of the Heavy Hand", with his epithet being the translation of his name.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[13]</a></sup>

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) equivalent of *Mablung* appears to have been <i><b>Lungumá</b></i> or <i><b>Lungumaqua</b></i> ("Heavyhand"), containing the element *[má](/wiki/M%C3%A1 "Má")* ("hand").<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-13">[11]</a></sup>

## Names and titles {#Names_and_titles}

Mablung is variously referred to as Thingol's chief warrior<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[14]</a></sup>, "Mablung the Hunter"<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[15]</a></sup>, and the chief huntsman of Thingol.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[16]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Early legendarium {#Early_legendarium}

#### *The Book of Lost Tales* {#The_Book_of_Lost_Tales}

Mablung is first introduced in *[The Tale of Tinúviel](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Tin%C3%BAviel)* as the chief of the king’s thanes during the Hunting of the Wolf. Armed with a spear previously captured in battle with the Orcs, he joined King *Tinwelint* (Thingol), Beren, and Huan. After Karkaras was slain, Mablung retrieved the Silmaril and offered it to Tinwelint, but the King refused it, stating he would not handle it unless given by Beren. As Beren had been sorely wounded by Karkaras, Mablung and Tinwelint bore him back to Tinwelint's halls where Beren briefly awoke. Beren received the Silmaril from Mablung and gave it to Tinwelint.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[17]</a></sup>

The character of Mablung was an emendation from *Tingfang* (son of Tinwelint), who was originally a member of the hunt. As this passage was written after [Tinfang](/wiki/Tinfang) was already stated as becoming lost, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) speculated that [his father](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), noticing the contradiction, then created Mablung as Tingfang's replacement.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[18]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:note 12</sup>

In one version of *[The History of the Exiled Gnomes](/wiki/The_History_of_the_Exiled_Gnomes)* "Mablon the [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorindi "Ilkorindi")" perished serving in the rearguard of [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) during his retreat from the Battle of Unnumbered Tears, standing against a "host of [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs)".<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[19]</a></sup>

Mablung next appears in *[The Nauglafring](/wiki/The_Nauglafring)* where he and Huan joined Tinwelint on a commemorative hunt celebrating the anniversary of the slaying of Karkaras. Initially Tinwelint did not recognize Mablung for the wild growth of his hair, but was soon glad to have him, and Mablung rode beside the king in honour of his deeds at the original great hunt. While Tinwelint's company was resting in mirth and laughter, they were suddenly and unexpectedly encircled by armed Dwarves. They fought bitterly, but eventually Tinwelint and his folks were all slain, with Mablung and the King falling side by side. The Dwarf King, [Naugladur](/wiki/Naugladur), then took Tinwelint's head, only daring to do so after they were dead, as he would not otherwise be so near Tinwelint's sword or Mablung's axe.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[20]</a></sup>

### Later legendarium {#Later_legendarium}

#### *Ælfwine and Dírhaval* {#Ælfwine_and_Dírhaval}

In one version of the introductory note to *Narn i Chîn Húrin*, Mablung is implied to be one of the refugees at the [Havens of Sirion](/wiki/Havens_of_Sirion) in the days of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) (thus surviving both of the attacks on Doriath, contrary to the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion "The Silmarillion")*).

Mablung became one of the primary sources for the Mannish poet [Dírhaval](/wiki/D%C3%ADrhaval) who was composing the lay of the *Children of Húrin*.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[21]</a></sup>

It is unknown if Mablung survived the [Third Kinslaying](/wiki/Third_Kinslaying).

#### *Eldarin Hands, Fingers &amp; Numerals* {#Eldarin_Hands,_Fingers_&_Numerals}

In a very late essay titled "*Eldarin Hands, Fingers &amp; Numerals* and Related Writings", Mablung is said to have been the one to slay Carcharoth after Beren was mortally wounded,<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[22]</a></sup> though the role of Huan in such a scenario was never elaborated on.
