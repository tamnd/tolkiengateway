<b><i>Mythlore</i> 69</b> (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/mythlore/vol18/iss3/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Volume 18, Issue 3</a>) is an issue of the *[Mythlore](/wiki/Mythlore)* journal, published by the [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society).

The article "[Gondolinic Runes](/wiki/Gondolinic_Runes)" by [Paul Nolan Hyde](/wiki/Paul_Nolan_Hyde) includes a facsimile of a manuscript by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) followed by a lengthy analysis (a response to the article was published in [*Mythlore* 70](/wiki/Mythlore_70 "Mythlore 70"), by [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
