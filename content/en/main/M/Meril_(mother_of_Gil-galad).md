**Meril** was the wife of [Felagund](/wiki/Felagund) and the mother of [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), according to a version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") following the completion of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* in c. [1950](/wiki/1950).

She was sent away by Felagund along their son Gil-galad (still a [child](/wiki/Elven_life_cycle#Early_life "Elven life cycle")) to her [own folk](/wiki/Falathrim "Falathrim") in [Eglorest](/wiki/Eglorest) during the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), due to Felagund fearing "that all strong places were doomed to fall at last before the might of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)".<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Meril* is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for [rose](/wiki/Roses "Roses") used as a name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
