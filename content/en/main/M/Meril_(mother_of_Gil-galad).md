**Meril** was the wife of [Felagund](../F/Felagund.md) and the mother of [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), according to a version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) following the completion of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* in c. [1950](../_/1950.md).

She was sent away by Felagund along their son Gil-galad (still a [child](../E/Elven_life_cycle.md#Early_life)) to her [own folk](../F/Falathrim.md) in [Eglorest](../E/Eglorest.md) during the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), due to Felagund fearing "that all strong places were doomed to fall at last before the might of [Morgoth](Morgoth.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Meril* is the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word for [rose](../R/Roses.md) used as a name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### *The War of the Jewels* {#The_War_of_the_Jewels}

Felagund's wife appears again (this time without a name) in a pencilled note near the opening of the *[Beren and Lúthien](../B/Beren_and_Lúthien.md)* chapter of the *[Quenta Silmarillion](<../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road).md>)*:<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-1">[1]</a></sup>

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>But foreseeing evil he commanded Orodreth to send away his son Gilgalad, and wife.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="../T/The_War_of_the_Jewels.md" title="The War of the Jewels">The War of the Jewels</a></i>, &#34;<a href="../T/The_Later_Quenta_Silmarillion.md" title="The Later Quenta Silmarillion">The Later Quenta Silmarillion</a>&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

However, this note was struck out, and somewhat further in the *Beren and Lúthien* chapter (in the part referring to [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md)'s expulsion of [Celegorm](../C/Celegorm.md) and [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md)) there appears another note wherein the unnamed wife<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup> of [Inglor](../F/Finrod.md) "*forsook the folk of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) and went with her son Gilgalad to the [Havens of the Falas](../H/Havens_of_the_Falas.md)*".<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-1">[1]</a></sup>

This has Felagund's wife and son (Gil-galad) only leaving Nargothrond after the expulsion of Celegorm and Curufin (in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [465](../F/First_Age_465.md))<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> or later.

### *The Peoples of Middle-earth* {#The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth}

An otherwise unnamed wife of Finrod appears yet again (though not in any explicit association with Gil-galad) in a note from the late 1960s:<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>During their dwelling in Nargothrond as refugees <a href="../C/Celebrimbor.md" title="Celebrimbor">he</a> had grown to love Finrod and   ^   his wife, and was aghast at the behaviour of <a href="../C/Curufin.md" title="Curufin">his father</a> and would not go with him.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="../T/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md" title="The Peoples of Middle-earth">The Peoples of Middle-earth</a></i>, &#34;<a href="../O/Of_Dwarves_and_Men.md" title="Of Dwarves and Men">Of Dwarves and Men</a>&#34;, note 7</cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>
