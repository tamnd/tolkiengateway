**Money**, specifically minted coin money, was used in the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md) as early as the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).

## History {#History}

In [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), the chief currency was the [Castar](../C/Castar.md); a small silver coin known as the [Tharni](../T/Tharni.md) was worth one-fourth of a Castar.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Azog](../A/Azog.md) threw a money purse at [Nár](../N/Nár.md) containing "few coins of little worth" (which the Dwarves later stuffed in the mouth of Azog's decapitated head after his defeat).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

When [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) rushed from his door to meet [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) and his party of dwarves at the [Green Dragon Inn](../G/Green_Dragon~72852.md), he found himself outside without "a hat, a walking stick, or any *money*, or anything that he usually took with him when he went out".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the death of [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) and the destruction of [Esgaroth](../E/Esgaroth.md) the people were angry at the [Master of Lake-town](Master_of_Lake-town.md) and declared, "We have had enough of the old men and the *money-counters*!" Others took up the cry of "Up with the Bowman (meaning [Bard](../B/Bard.md)) and down with *Moneybags*".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Much of the gold and silver wealth of Dwarf-horde of Smaug appears to have been minted. [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) took home two chests from the horde, one containing gold and the other silver,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and as a result (in addition to his family's pre-existing wealth) lived very comfortably for the next 50 years and was considered quite rich. In addition to the [Hobbits of Bree](../H/Hobbits_of_Bree.md), the [Hobbits of the Shire](../H/Hobbits_of_the_Shire.md) apparently dealt in coin money as well, otherwise they might not have been so eager to search [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) after Bilbo left, looking for secret treasure rooms.

Money was mentioned in relation to the trading town of [Bree](../B/Bree.md). At the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), a pony was considered to be worth about four [silver pennies](../S/Silver_pennies.md). The well-to-do owner of the [Prancing Pony](../P/Prancing_Pony.md), [Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md), considered a loss of 30 silver pennies to be a considerable blow.<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-6">[6]</a></sup> A gold piece was regarded as a particularly extravagant reward for good news.

When [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and the four hobbits were camped on the side of [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) wandered to the edge of the dell. Soon he came running back in fear and said, "I durstn't go outside this dell for any *money*."<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Background Information {#Background_Information}

In *[The Complete Guide to Middle-earth](../T/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth.md)* by [Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md), the entry for "Money" on page 344 makes it a point to say

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><b>contrary to the claims of some</b>, there are references to money in <i>The Lord of the Rings</i>
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>The Complete Guide to Middle-earth<i> - &#34;Money&#34;</i></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

The above phrase suggests that (in the early days of [Tolkien fandom](../T/Tolkien_fandom.md)) there was some belief that coin money used as actual "currency" did not exist in Middle-earth; references to money are scarce, not highlighted and obviously forgettable among the narrative.
