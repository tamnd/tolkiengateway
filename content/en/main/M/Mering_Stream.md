The **Mering Stream**, or <i><b>Glanhír</b></i> in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), was the border river between [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). West of the Mering Stream lay the [Eastfold](/wiki/Eastfold), and to its east lay [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Course {#Course}

The stream rose from a cleft in a northward spur of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains), traversed the [Firienholt](/wiki/Firienholt), which grew about the mountain [Halifirien](/wiki/Halifirien), last of the [Warning beacons of Gondor](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor), then exited to the plain and joined the [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash), where it formed impassable marshes.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:301</sup>

## History {#History}

Before the establishment of the kingdom of Rohan, the line of the Mering Stream was fortified on its between the impassable marshes where it flowed into the Entwash and the bridge where the [road](/wiki/Great_West_Road "Great West Road") ran westward out of the Firienholt.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:301</sup>

In later times it formed the border between Gondor and Rohan, together with the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) and the Mouths of the [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>

On early [March 8](/wiki/March_8) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") crossed the stream during their ride from Rohan to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) in the [War of the ring](/wiki/War_of_the_ring).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On [March 11](/wiki/March_11) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> the riders of Rohan led by their King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) travelled with [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) through the Eastfold on their way to Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In early August [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the funeral escort of King Théoden passed through the Eastfold on its way to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "boundary stream". *Mering* represents [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *māere* or *mēre* ("boundary").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

*Glanhír* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which means "boundary stream".<sup id="cite_ref-UT_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-11">[11]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests that the first element is <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Glan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Glan (page does not exist)">glan(n)</a></i> ("border", "boundary") and that the second element is a [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") form of *[sîr](/wiki/S%C3%AEr "Sîr")* ("river", "stream")".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Mering Stream is only accessible from the <a href="/wiki/Rohan" title="Rohan">Rohan</a> side, as the <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondorian</a> side is blocked by impassable cliffs and reeds. The only way to cross the river is through a guarded ford, which also transitions the player between the <a href="/wiki/Rhovanion" title="Rhovanion">Rhovanion</a> to the <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> in-game landmasses, a transition very noticeable because all of Gondor is covered under the <a href="/wiki/Dawnless_Day" title="Dawnless Day">Dawnless Day</a> at that time, regardless of the daylight conditions on the Rohan side. A Rohirrim town of &#34;Beaconwatch&#34; sits on the bank of the river, with a clear view across into the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Firien_Wood" title="Firien Wood">Firien Wood</a> and <a href="/wiki/Halifirien" title="Halifirien">Halifirien</a>.</dd></dl>
