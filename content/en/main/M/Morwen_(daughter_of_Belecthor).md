**Morwen** was the daughter of [Belecthor I](../B/Belecthor_I.md). She appears in [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md)'s manuscripts for *[The Heirs of Elendil](../T/The_Heirs_of_Elendil.md)* (a document used in constructing the timeline of the Realms in Exile for the [Appendices](../A/Appendices.md)). When Morwen's nephew, the [Steward](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md) [Ecthelion I](../E/Ecthelion_I.md), died childless, her grandson [Egalmoth](<../E/Egalmoth_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) became the 18th Ruling Steward.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Morwen does not appear in the published [Appendices](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Appendices.md) to the [Lord of the Rings](../L/Lord_of_the_Rings.md).

Morwen may have been named after the [Morwen](Morwen.md) of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), who was the daughter of [Baragund](../B/Baragund.md) of the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Morwen* means "Dark Maiden" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) (from *[mor](Mor.md)*, "dark" and the female suffix *[-wen](../_/-wen.md)*, "maiden").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

There are several drafts of the *Heirs of Elendil* manuscripts, identified sequentially with the letters A through D by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md). In a note in Manuscript A (the earliest manuscript), Morwen is the *sister* of Belecthor I and grandmother of Egalmoth, creating a genealogy which has a generation too many. This error likely came as a result of how quickly Tolkien drafted the dates and genealogies in this early manuscript. In Manuscript B, Morwen's role is revised, and she becomes the sister of [Orodreth](<../O/Orodreth_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>), continuing to appear as such in the later manuscripts.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
