**Morwen** was the daughter of [Belecthor I](/wiki/Belecthor_I). She appears in [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien)'s manuscripts for *[The Heirs of Elendil](/wiki/The_Heirs_of_Elendil)* (a document used in constructing the timeline of the Realms in Exile for the [Appendices](/wiki/Appendices)). When Morwen's nephew, the [Steward](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor") [Ecthelion I](/wiki/Ecthelion_I), died childless, her grandson [Egalmoth](</wiki/Egalmoth_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Egalmoth (Steward of Gondor)") became the 18th Ruling Steward.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Morwen does not appear in the published [Appendices](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Appendices "The Lord of the Rings Appendices") to the [Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings).

Morwen may have been named after the [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen) of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), who was the daughter of [Baragund](/wiki/Baragund) of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Morwen* means "Dark Maiden" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (from *[mor](/wiki/Mor "Mor")*, "dark" and the female suffix *[-wen](/wiki/-wen)*, "maiden").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

There are several drafts of the *Heirs of Elendil* manuscripts, identified sequentially with the letters A through D by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien). In a note in Manuscript A (the earliest manuscript), Morwen is the *sister* of Belecthor I and grandmother of Egalmoth, creating a genealogy which has a generation too many. This error likely came as a result of how quickly Tolkien drafted the dates and genealogies in this early manuscript. In Manuscript B, Morwen's role is revised, and she becomes the sister of [Orodreth](</wiki/Orodreth_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Orodreth (Steward of Gondor)"), continuing to appear as such in the later manuscripts.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
