**Makar** was one of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. He is described as a "warrior Vala", along with his sister [Meássë](Meássë.md).

## History {#History}

Before Makar came into [the world](../A/Arda.md), he, along with his sister Meássë, was already a quarrelsome spirit, even going so far as to join [Melko](Melko.md) in his discord during the [Music of the Ainur](Music_of_the_Ainur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

After arriving in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), Makar and Meássë built their dwellings, made of iron, in the north, close to those of [Mandos](Mandos.md) and [Fui](../F/Fui.md). The only Vala who visited his dwelling was [Tulkas](../T/Tulkas.md), who went there to wrestle and fight with Makar, although he did this only to keep his strength intact, for there was no friendship between the two.<sup id="cite_ref-Valar_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valar-1">[1]</a></sup>

Makar, along with his sister, rode often to the [Great Lands](Middle-earth.md), where they hunted wolves and bears in the black mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Valar_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valar-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the Valar decided to seek out Melko and oust him from his dwelling at [Utumna](../U/Utumna.md), Makar adorned them with armour.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> After they brought Melko to Valinor for judgement, he was the only one to speak for him, being displeased with the prospect of peace.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

When the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) awoke, and the Valar invited them to live among the Gods, he spoke against the others and was backed by Meássë, saying that Valinor was built for the Valar, and the Valar only.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Darkening of Valinor](../D/Darkening_of_Valinor.md), he joined the other Valar in pursuit of Melko, who was fleeing north towards his old stronghold. Despite the chase, Melko escaped.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> However, Makar managed to capture and slay two of Melko's servants, returning with a red hand, smeared with blood. He was the only one who had any pleasure at the time, the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md) having been destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><b>Makar</b></i> is a [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) name, meaning "God of battle". Its [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) equivalent is <i><b>Magorn</b></i> or <i><b>Magron</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-App_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-8">[8]</a></sup> He was also called <i><b>Ramandor</b></i> (meaning "the Shouter" in Qenya),<sup id="cite_ref-App_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-8">[8]</a></sup> as well as <i><b>Kostorin</b></i> (or <i><b>Gothonwi</b></i> in Gnomish).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
