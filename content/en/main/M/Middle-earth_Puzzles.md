**Middle-earth Puzzles** (MEP) was a series of jigsaw puzzles released by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) in [1997](/wiki/1997), featuring images by Tolkien-inspired artists.

## Products {#Products}

- (1997) [The Way is Shut](/wiki/MEP:_The_Way_is_Shut "MEP: The Way is Shut")
- (1997) [Burglar Baggins](/wiki/MEP:_Burglar_Baggins "MEP: Burglar Baggins")
- (1997) [Éowyn and the Witch-king](/wiki/MEP:_%C3%89owyn_and_the_Witch-king "MEP: Éowyn and the Witch-king")
- (1997) [An Unexpected Party](/wiki/MEP:_An_Unexpected_Party "MEP: An Unexpected Party")
- (1997) [The Mirror Of Galadriel](/wiki/MEP:_The_Mirror_Of_Galadriel "MEP: The Mirror Of Galadriel")
- (1997) [Map of Middle-earth](/wiki/MEP:_Map_of_Middle-earth "MEP: Map of Middle-earth")
- (1997) [Arwen's Choice](/wiki/MEP:_Arwen%27s_Choice "MEP: Arwen's Choice")
- (199?) [Gandalf's Mark](/wiki/MEP:_Gandalf%27s_Mark "MEP: Gandalf's Mark")

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

**NEW! Middle-earth Puzzles**

This set of 7 beautiful, full-color art puzzles depict some of the most memorable scenes from Tolkien's epic tales of *The Hobbit* and *The Lord of the Rings*. ST # [30501](/wiki/MEP:_The_Way_is_Shut "MEP: The Way is Shut"), [30502](/wiki/MEP:_Burglar_Baggins "MEP: Burglar Baggins"), [30503](/wiki/MEP:_%C3%89owyn_and_the_Witch-king "MEP: Éowyn and the Witch-king") and [31501](/wiki/MEP:_Map_of_Middle-earth "MEP: Map of Middle-earth") use original art created for the award-winning *Middle-earth Collectible Card Game*, and ST # [31001](/wiki/MEP:_An_Unexpected_Party "MEP: An Unexpected Party"), [31002](/wiki/MEP:_The_Mirror_Of_Galadriel "MEP: The Mirror Of Galadriel"), [41002](/wiki/MEP:_Arwen%27s_Choice "MEP: Arwen's Choice") are all original works created by the artists especially for this exclusive puzzle series.
