**Middle-earth Puzzles** (MEP) was a series of jigsaw puzzles released by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) in [1997](../_/1997.md), featuring images by Tolkien-inspired artists.

## Products {#Products}

- (1997) [The Way is Shut](MEP%253A_The_Way_is_Shut.md)
- (1997) [Burglar Baggins](MEP%253A_Burglar_Baggins.md)
- (1997) [Éowyn and the Witch-king](MEP%253A_Éowyn_and_the_Witch-king.md)
- (1997) [An Unexpected Party](MEP%253A_An_Unexpected_Party.md)
- (1997) [The Mirror Of Galadriel](MEP%253A_The_Mirror_Of_Galadriel.md)
- (1997) [Map of Middle-earth](MEP%253A_Map_of_Middle-earth.md)
- (1997) [Arwen's Choice](MEP%253A_Arwen's_Choice.md)
- (199?) [Gandalf's Mark](MEP%253A_Gandalf's_Mark.md)

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

**NEW! Middle-earth Puzzles**

This set of 7 beautiful, full-color art puzzles depict some of the most memorable scenes from Tolkien's epic tales of *The Hobbit* and *The Lord of the Rings*. ST # [30501](MEP%253A_The_Way_is_Shut.md), [30502](MEP%253A_Burglar_Baggins.md), [30503](MEP%253A_Éowyn_and_the_Witch-king.md) and [31501](MEP%253A_Map_of_Middle-earth.md) use original art created for the award-winning *Middle-earth Collectible Card Game*, and ST # [31001](MEP%253A_An_Unexpected_Party.md), [31002](MEP%253A_The_Mirror_Of_Galadriel.md), [41002](MEP%253A_Arwen's_Choice.md) are all original works created by the artists especially for this exclusive puzzle series.
