**Mythcon 17** was the seventeenth annual [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) conference.

- **Theme:** "The Daughters of Beatrice: Women in Fantasy"
- **Guests of Honor:** [Charles de Lint](/wiki/Charles_de_Lint) (fantasy author) and Judith Kollmann (Williams scholar)
- **Chairman:** Sarah Beach and Peter Lowentrout
