<b><i>Mallorn</i> 5</b> is the 5th issue of the [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Society.md)'s journal [Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>), published in 1972. It includes a [short letter from J.R.R. Tolkien](../L/Letter_to_Vera_Chapman_and_the_Tolkien_Society.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Editorial**
    - Pro-Editorial from the Pro-Editor
        - By Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md))
        - Includes a [letter from Tolkien to Vera Chapman and The Tolkien Society](../L/Letter_to_Vera_Chapman_and_the_Tolkien_Society.md) (p. 2).
    - Letters to the Editors
        - By Bob Borsley, Will Haven, Roger Johnson, Tony Fallone, John Abbott, James Ead, Hal Broome, Catherine McEvoy, and Dave Weldrake
    - Belladonna's Postbag
        - By Will Haven, John B. Abbott, and Belladonna Took (Vera Chapman)
- **Notes**
    - Flaws in the Tapestry of *The Lord of the Rings*
        - By John Abbott
    - Shields of Middle-earth
        - By Helen Kerley
- **Creative**
    - Serial: "Sir John de Creke, His Quest" (short story)
        - By Vera Chapman
    - Tir Na Noge (poem)
        - By A.R. Fallone
    - The Professor at Oxford (poem)
        - By Vera Chapman
    - Nazgul (humorous vignettes)
        - By John B. Abbott
- **Book Reviews**
    - *[Bored of the Rings](<../B/Bored_of_the_Rings_(book).md>)* by The Harvard Lampoon
        - Reviewed by Jonathan Brown
    - *The Last Unicorn* by [Peter S. Beagle](../P/Peter_S._Beagle.md)
        - Reviewed by Belladonna Took (Vera Chapman)
    - *The Water of the Wondrous Isles* by [William Morris](../W/William_Morris.md)
        - Reviewed by Belladonna Took (Vera Chapman)
    - *Red Shadows* by [R.E. Howard](../R/Robert_E._Howard.md)
        - Reviewed by Hal Broome
    - *The Whisper of Glocken* by Carol Kendall
        - Reviewed by Rosie Turner
    - *A Wizard of Earthsea* by [Ursula Le Guin](../U/Ursula_K._Le_Guin.md)
        - Reviewed by Hartley Patterson
