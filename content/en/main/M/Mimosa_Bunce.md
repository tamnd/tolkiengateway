**Mimosa Bunce** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Mimosa married [Ponto Baggins](../P/Ponto_Baggins_I.md). They had two children; [Rosa](../R/Rosa_Baggins.md) was born in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1256](../T/Third_Age_2856.md), and [Polo Baggins](../P/Polo_Baggins.md) shortly thereafter.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mimosa" title="wikipedia:Mimosa">Mimosa</a> is a genus of plants (trees, shrubs and herbs) of the legume family.
