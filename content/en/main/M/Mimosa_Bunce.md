**Mimosa Bunce** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Mimosa married [Ponto Baggins](/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_I "Ponto Baggins I"). They had two children; [Rosa](/wiki/Rosa_Baggins "Rosa Baggins") was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1256](/wiki/Third_Age_2856 "Third Age 2856"), and [Polo Baggins](/wiki/Polo_Baggins) shortly thereafter.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mimosa" title="wikipedia:Mimosa">Mimosa</a> is a genus of plants (trees, shrubs and herbs) of the legume family.
