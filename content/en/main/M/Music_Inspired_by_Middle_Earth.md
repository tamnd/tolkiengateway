<i><b>Music Inspired by Middle Earth</b></i> is an album featuring [David Arkenstone](../D/David_Arkenstone.md), released in [2001](../_/2001.md). The music is based on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s epic fantasy novel *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.

## Track listing {#Track_listing}

1. "Prelude: Hobbits from the Shire" – 4:08
2. "The Road to Rivendell" – 3:39
3. "The Quest" – 5:28
4. "Moria" – 5:02
5. "Lothlórien" – 4:18
6. "Galadriel's Mirror" – 4:32
7. "The Riders of Rohan" – 4:24
8. "The Palantír" – 4:50
9. "Arwen and Aragorn" – 3:29
10. "To Isengard" – 4:25
11. "In the Land of Shadow" – 4:22
12. "The Field of Cormallen" – 3:33
13. "The Grey Havens" – 3:37

- All tracks composed by Diane and David Arkenstone
