The **Morgulduin** was a river of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) that began in [Morgul Vale](/wiki/Morgul_Vale) in the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath). It ran westward past [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) until it reached the [Cross-roads](/wiki/Cross-roads) in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien), where its course turned southwest until it entered the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) just south of [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath). Just to the north of the stream was the [road](/wiki/Morgul-road "Morgul-road") from Osgiliath to Minas Morgul.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It is probable that the original name of the river was Ithilduin<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and that it was renamed Morgulduin at the same time as Minas Ithil was renamed Minas Morgul after [its capture](/wiki/Second_Fall_of_Minas_Ithil "Second Fall of Minas Ithil") by the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2002](/wiki/Third_Age_2002 "Third Age 2002").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

On March 9, [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> as [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) were approaching the Cross-roads they heard the voice of the river, which sounded cold and cruel.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) the water from the river was not suitable for drinking.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Near the bridge over the river some way into the Morgul Vale cold vapours issued from the river who sickened Frodo<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> on March 10.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It has been suggested that *Morgulduin* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "black magic river" and consisting of *[mor(n)](/wiki/Morn "Morn")* ("dark", "black"), *[gul](/wiki/G%C3%BBl "Gûl")* ("magic", "necromancy") and *[duin](/wiki/Duin "Duin")* ("river").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

*Ithilduin* is a Sindarin name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Roman Rausch suggests that it means "moon-river" and consists of *[ithil](/wiki/Ithil "Ithil")* ("moon") and *[duin](/wiki/Duin "Duin")* ("river").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Morgulduin originates above the <a href="/wiki/Minas_Morgul" title="Minas Morgul">Minas Morgul</a> in the former cisterns of the city, now corrupted into &#34;Eithel Gwaur&#34;, the Filth-well. Its River-maiden &#34;Rauniel&#34; was killed and devoured by a creature that now lives in and pollutes her domain. The corruption of the river is unmistakable along its course, even after it flows out of the <a href="/wiki/Morgul_Vale" title="Morgul Vale">Morgul Vale</a> into <a href="/wiki/Ithilien" title="Ithilien">Ithilien</a>, with only the mighty current of the <a href="/wiki/Anduin" title="Anduin">Anduin</a> itself able to safely dillute the poisoned waters.</dd></dl>
