## Quenya {#Quenya}

<i><b>mindon</b></i> is a noun meaning "lofty tower" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). Compare with *[minas](/wiki/Minas "Minas")* in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- [Mindon Eldaliéva](/wiki/Mindon_Eldali%C3%A9va) = "(Our) Tower of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar)"

## Sindarin/Noldorin {#Sindarin/Noldorin}

<i><b>mindon</b></i> means "isolated hill ... especially a hill with a watch-tower" in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup> Evidently, this meaning was retained in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) in the contraction *min*, as [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") wrote in his [unfinished index](/wiki/Index_questions "Index questions") that *Min-Rimmon* meant "peak of the Rimmon".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

From [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *minitunda* or *minitaun*, derived from [roots](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=MINI&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MINI (page does not exist)">MINI</a> + <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=TUN&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TUN (page does not exist)">TUN</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples_2}

- [Min-Rimmon](/wiki/Min-Rimmon)
