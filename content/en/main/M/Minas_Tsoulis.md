**Minas Tsoulis** (Greek: Μηνάς Τσούλης) is the author of two books related to the study of [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). He knows a number of languages, including English, French, Italian and Latin. His love for languages led him to the study of Quenya and the subsequent writing of a dictionary and a book of grammar. He is a member of the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](../E/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship.md) and he has also participated in other linguistic conferences.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
