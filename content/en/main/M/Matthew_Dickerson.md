**Matthew T. Dickerson** is an author and professor of Computer Science at Middlebury College in Vermont<sup id="cite_ref-MC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MC-1">[1]</a></sup> who has written multiple articles and books about the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

He has a PhD in computer science from Cornell University and a minor in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) language and literature.<sup id="cite_ref-CCFW_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCFW-2">[2]</a></sup>
