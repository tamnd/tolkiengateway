**Modern English** is the name of the standardized form of the English language that is in use in the [world](../A/Arda.md) today.

## History {#History}

Modern English began its evolution from [Middle English](Middle_English.md) around the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Vowel_Shift" title="wikipedia:Great Vowel Shift">Great Vowel Shift</a> that took place in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a> between the late fifteenth century and the eighteenth century. During this evolution, the first forms of Modern English tend to be referred to as **Early Modern English**, or **Shakespearean English**. As the language became standardised in the mid-to-late eighteenth century, Modern English was born.

[J.R.R. Tolkien's](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writings related to [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md) were said to be translated from [Westron](../W/Westron.md) into Modern English.
