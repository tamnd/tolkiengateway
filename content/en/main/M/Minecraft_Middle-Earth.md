<i><b>Minecraft Middle Earth</b></i> is a fan-built server for <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minecraft" title="wikipedia:Minecraft">Minecraft</a></i>, which tries to recreate [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) in the game.<sup id="cite_ref-pcgamer_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pcgamer-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is one of several Middle-earth related *Minecraft* projects, such as [ArdaCraft](/wiki/ArdaCraft).

## History and development {#History_and_development}

The projected was started on [14 October](/wiki/14_October) [2010](/wiki/2010) by Nicky Vermeersch.<sup id="cite_ref-pcgamer_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pcgamer-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Early on, it was decided to set the game at the time of [Bilbo’s 111th birthday](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party"), [22 September](/wiki/22_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001").<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

The team began with locations such as [Bree](/wiki/Bree) and [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), in a time when many of the tools now used by Minecraft builders were not yet developed.<sup id="cite_ref-pcgamer_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pcgamer-1">[1]</a></sup>

To accommodate as many people as possible, the world can be accessed without any client modifications. For enhanced immersion, the team does provide several resource packs and shaders through its dedicated mod installer. The resource packs feature Human, Dwarven, Elven, Rohan, and Mordor styles.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Tolkiendaysmcme.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/e/ef/Tolkiendaysmcme.png/300px-Tolkiendaysmcme.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="214" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/e/ef/Tolkiendaysmcme.png/450px-Tolkiendaysmcme.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/e/ef/Tolkiendaysmcme.png/600px-Tolkiendaysmcme.png 2x" data-file-width="867" data-file-height="619"/></a><figcaption>The team at the Tolkien Days Convention</figcaption></figure>

As of 2024, MCME has been featured at the [German Tolkien Society's](/wiki/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft_e.V. "Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft e.V.") Tolkien Days three times, as part of its video game tent.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

A monthly magazine keeps fans up to date about the latest changes to the server.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The publication *PCGamesN* considered *Minecraft Middle-Earth* one of the best Minecraft servers.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
