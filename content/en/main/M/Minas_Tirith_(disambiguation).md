**Minas Tirith** means "Tower of the Guard" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (from *[minas](/wiki/Minas "Minas")*, "tower, citadel" and *[tirith](/wiki/Tirith "Tirith")*, "watch, guard") and can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Minas Tirith in Beleriand](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand)> "Minas Tirith (Beleriand)"), a fortress on the isle of [Tol Sirion](/wiki/Tol_Sirion) during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age)
- <b><a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith in Gondor</a>, capital of <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> during the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, originally known as <i>Minas Anor</i></b>

## Chapters {#Chapters}

- *[Minas Tirith (chapter)](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(chapter)>)*, the first chapter of [Book V](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King "The Return of the King") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Minas_Tirith_(The_War_of_the_Ring)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Minas Tirith (The War of the Ring) (page does not exist)">Minas Tirith (The War of the Ring)</a></i>, the third chapter of the third part (also called *Minas Tirith*) of *[The War of the Ring](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Ring)*
