**Minas Tirith** means "Tower of the Guard" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) (from *[minas](Minas.md)*, "tower, citadel" and *[tirith](../T/Tirith.md)*, "watch, guard") and can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Minas Tirith in Beleriand](<Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand).md>), a fortress on the isle of [Tol Sirion](../T/Tol_Sirion.md) during the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md)
- <b><a href="Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith in Gondor</a>, capital of <a href="../G/Gondor.md" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> during the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, originally known as <i>Minas Anor</i></b>

## Chapters {#Chapters}

- *[Minas Tirith (chapter)](<Minas_Tirith_(chapter).md>)*, the first chapter of [Book V](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Minas_Tirith_(The_War_of_the_Ring)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Minas Tirith (The War of the Ring) (page does not exist)">Minas Tirith (The War of the Ring)</a></i>, the third chapter of the third part (also called *Minas Tirith*) of *[The War of the Ring](../T/The_War_of_the_Ring.md)*
