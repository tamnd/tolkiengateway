**Mark Selwyn Hadlow** (1957) is a [New Zealand](../N/New_Zealand.md) actor. He portrayed [Dori](../D/Dori.md) in *[The Hobbit (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)*, as well as [Bert](../B/Bert.md) the [Troll](../T/Troll.md) (through motion capture). He had previously worked with [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) on *Meet the Feebles* and *King Kong*.
