**Mark Selwyn Hadlow** (1957) is a [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) actor. He portrayed [Dori](/wiki/Dori) in *[The Hobbit (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)>)*, as well as [Bert](/wiki/Bert) the [Troll](/wiki/Troll) (through motion capture). He had previously worked with [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) on *Meet the Feebles* and *King Kong*.

## Roles {#Roles}

- [2012](/wiki/2012): *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)* - [Dori](/wiki/Dori)
- [2013](/wiki/2013): *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)* - [Dori](/wiki/Dori)
- [2014](/wiki/2014): *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)* - [Dori](/wiki/Dori)
