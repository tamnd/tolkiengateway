*Middle-earth: Dark Minions*, released in [1996](../_/1996.md), was the second expansion set for *[Middle-earth: The Wizards](Middle-earth%253A_The_Wizards.md)*, the first basic set of [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).

*Dark Minions* adds many powerful hazard cards including more [Undead](../U/Undead.md) and introduces the *agents* - a subtle group of Sauron's servants, concentrating on intrigue and influence. The set also adds an underground network of site cards, the *[Under-deeps](../U/Under-deeps.md)*, which later also becomes The Balrog's realm (for [Middle-earth: The Balrog](Middle-earth%253A_The_Balrog.md)).

*Dark Minions* was sold in booster packs of 15 cards (36 boosters per display), featuring 1 rare, 4 uncommons and 10 commons per booster pack. A [corresponding player guide](Middle-earth%253A_Dark_Minions_Player_Guide.md) was also released.
