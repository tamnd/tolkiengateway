*Middle-earth: Dark Minions*, released in [1996](/wiki/1996), was the second expansion set for *[Middle-earth: The Wizards](/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Wizards)*, the first basic set of [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).

*Dark Minions* adds many powerful hazard cards including more [Undead](/wiki/Undead) and introduces the *agents* - a subtle group of Sauron's servants, concentrating on intrigue and influence. The set also adds an underground network of site cards, the *[Under-deeps](/wiki/Under-deeps)*, which later also becomes The Balrog's realm (for [Middle-earth: The Balrog](/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Balrog)).

*Dark Minions* was sold in booster packs of 15 cards (36 boosters per display), featuring 1 rare, 4 uncommons and 10 commons per booster pack. A [corresponding player guide](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Dark_Minions_Player_Guide "Middle-earth: Dark Minions Player Guide") was also released.
