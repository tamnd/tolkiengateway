**Annie Magdalen Matthews Tolkien** ([4 January](../_/4_January.md) [1905](../_/1905.md) — [1970](../_/1970.md)) was the wife of [Hilary Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md). They were wedded in 1928 in Evesham, Worcestershire, England. They had three children - Gabriel, Julian and Paul. She was the sister-in-law of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
