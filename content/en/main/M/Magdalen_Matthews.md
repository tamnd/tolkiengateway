**Annie Magdalen Matthews Tolkien** ([4 January](/wiki/4_January) [1905](/wiki/1905) — [1970](/wiki/1970)) was the wife of [Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien). They were wedded in 1928 in Evesham, Worcestershire, England. They had three children - Gabriel, Julian and Paul. She was the sister-in-law of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
