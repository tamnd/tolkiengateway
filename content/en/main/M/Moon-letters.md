**Moon-letters**, or **moon-runes**, were a method of writing invented by the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). Moon-letters were ordinary [Runes](/wiki/Runes), but written in such a way that they could only be read when the light of the [moon](/wiki/Moon "Moon") shone behind the writing. Some moon-letters could be read by any moonlight, but it was possible to make the writing even more secret. More sophisticated moon-letters could only be read by the light of the same shape of moon, or at the same time of year, as when they were written.

Moon-letters were written with silver pens.<sup id="cite_ref-Rest_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rest-1">[1]</a></sup> The substance [Ithildin](/wiki/Ithildin), made from *[mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril")*, reflected only starlight and moonlight,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and it seems likely that ithildin, or something very similar, was used by the Dwarves to write their moon-letters.

An example of moon-letters was found by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) on [Thrór's Map](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r%27s_Map) of the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) and surrounding areas that [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) had given [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The moon-letters were of the more cunning type and had been written on a Midsummer's eve under a crescent moon. They said:

<dl><dd>&#34;<i>Stand by the grey stone when the thrush knocks and the setting sun with the last light of <a href="/wiki/Durin%27s_Day" title="Durin&#39;s Day">Durin&#39;s Day</a> will shine upon the key-hole.</i>&#34;<sup id="cite_ref-Rest_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rest-1">[1]</a></sup></dd></dl>
