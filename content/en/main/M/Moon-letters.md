**Moon-letters**, or **moon-runes**, were a method of writing invented by the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md). Moon-letters were ordinary [Runes](../R/Runes.md), but written in such a way that they could only be read when the light of the [moon](Moon.md) shone behind the writing. Some moon-letters could be read by any moonlight, but it was possible to make the writing even more secret. More sophisticated moon-letters could only be read by the light of the same shape of moon, or at the same time of year, as when they were written.

Moon-letters were written with silver pens.<sup id="cite_ref-Rest_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rest-1">[1]</a></sup> The substance [Ithildin](../I/Ithildin.md), made from *[mithril](Mithril.md)*, reflected only starlight and moonlight,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and it seems likely that ithildin, or something very similar, was used by the Dwarves to write their moon-letters.

An example of moon-letters was found by [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) on [Thrór's Map](../T/Thrór's_Map.md) of the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) and surrounding areas that [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) had given [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The moon-letters were of the more cunning type and had been written on a Midsummer's eve under a crescent moon. They said:

<dl><dd>&#34;<i>Stand by the grey stone when the thrush knocks and the setting sun with the last light of <a href="../D/Durin&#39;s_Day.md" title="Durin&#39;s Day">Durin&#39;s Day</a> will shine upon the key-hole.</i>&#34;<sup id="cite_ref-Rest_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rest-1">[1]</a></sup></dd></dl>
