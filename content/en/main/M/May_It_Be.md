**May It Be** is a song composed and sung by [Enya](../E/Enya.md) for [Peter Jackson's](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* with English and [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) lyrics written by longtime Enya collaborator <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roma_Ryan" title="wikipedia:Roma Ryan">Roma Ryan</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-AnnotatedScore_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AnnotatedScore-1">[1]</a></sup> It was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Song in [2002](../_/2002.md).

It is the final track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack.md)* and the eighth track of the third disc of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - The Complete Recordings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_The_Complete_Recordings.md)*.
