**May It Be** is a song composed and sung by [Enya](/wiki/Enya) for [Peter Jackson's](/wiki/Peter_Jackson "Peter Jackson") *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* with English and [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) lyrics written by longtime Enya collaborator <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roma_Ryan" title="wikipedia:Roma Ryan">Roma Ryan</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-AnnotatedScore_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AnnotatedScore-1">[1]</a></sup> It was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Song in [2002](/wiki/2002).

It is the final track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)* and the eighth track of the third disc of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - The Complete Recordings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_The_Complete_Recordings)*.
