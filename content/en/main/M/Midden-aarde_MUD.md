**Midden-aarde MUD** (Dutch: *Middle-earth MUD*) is a Dutch MUD made by "Irmo" and several other programmers. In 2009 the unofficial test version was launched, with gameplay in [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md) as a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md), [Men](Men.md), [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md), [Elf](../E/Elves.md) or [Dúnedain-Ranger](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md). Later that year the project was canceled for a time. But since [2010](../_/2010.md) the game is play-able again. The MUD originally only included [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), but now also includes gameplay in [Bree-land](../B/Bree-land.md), [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and [Ost-in-Edhil](../O/Ost-in-Edhil.md).
