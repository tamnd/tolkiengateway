**Muzgash** was an [orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") serving in the garrison of the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol), under the command of [Shagrat](/wiki/Shagrat). He was caught up in fighting with [Gorbag](/wiki/Gorbag)'s [Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul "Minas Morgul")-[orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") in the Tower; with [Lagduf](/wiki/Lagduf), he attempted to escape, but was shot as he ran through the gate.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Though unidentified, a trio of Uruk-hai are encountered by Samwise Gamgee on the stairs of the tower after the battle. Since only one Uruk (<a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Snaga_(Orc_of_Mordor)" title="Snaga (Orc of Mordor)">Snaga</a>) was encountered by Sam on the stairs in the novel, it is likely that the other two are meant to be <a href="/wiki/Lagduf" title="Lagduf">Lagduf</a> and Muzgash.</dd></dl>
