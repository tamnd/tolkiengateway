**Matthew T. Blessing** (September [1962](/wiki/1962)) is the former Head of the Department of Special Collections and University Archives at [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University). He was responsible for the Tolkien Collection at the university, which contains the largest and rarest Tolkien items in the world, from 1999 to 2012. Since 2012, he has been the <a class="external text" href="https://www.wisconsinhistory.org/Records/Article/CS3543" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">State Archivist and Division Administrator at the Wisconsin Historical Society</a>.
