**METW: Galadriel** is a game card from the set *[Middle-earth: The Wizards](Middle-earth%253A_The_Wizards.md)*, for [MECCG](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).

<i><b>Note:</b></i> *"Galadriel" refers to [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) in [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).*

## Card text {#Card_text}

<i><b>Unique.</b></i> When Galadriel is at Lórien, her controlling player may keep one more card than normal in his hand.

\-3 marshalling points if eliminated.

*"Tall and white and fair...and beautiful beyond enduring, terrible, and worshipful."* <i><b>-<a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">LotRII</a></b></i>
