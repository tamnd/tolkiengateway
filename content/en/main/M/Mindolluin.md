**Mindolluin**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or **Mount Mindolluin**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> was the easternmost mountain of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *Ered Nimrais*).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Mindolluin was a high mountain with a snow-capped peak and high glens, which cast deep purple shadows in the morning light.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> On the eastern side of the mountain a narrow shoulder joined it with the [Hill of Guard](/wiki/Hill_of_Guard)<sup id="cite_ref-MT752_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT752-5">[5]</a></sup> on which the city of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) was built<sup id="cite_ref-N_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-6">[6]</a></sup> at the level of the fifth wall of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-MT752_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT752-5">[5]</a></sup>

At the southern feet of Mount Mindolluin was an ancient steep path , which went up the mountain to a high hallow far above the city where only the [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor) were accustomed to go. Standing in this spot one could look over a precipice to the towers of the city of Minas Tirith far below.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Shortly after [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)'s coronation as Elessar, on [25 June](/wiki/25_June) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[note 1]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) took him by the forgotten path to the high hallow where the newly-crowned king could view the vastness of his realm. When he remarked that the [White Tree of Gondor](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor) in the [Court of the Fountain](</wiki/Court_of_the_Fountain_(Minas_Tirith)> "Court of the Fountain (Minas Tirith)") was still [withered](/wiki/Dead_Tree "Dead Tree"), Gandalf bade him turn around. Opposite the precipice, on an otherwise barren stony slope, one growing thing could be seen: a sapling of the line of [Nimloth](/wiki/Nimloth_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Nimloth of Númenor"). Aragorn removed the tree, brought it to the Citadel and planted it by the fountain in the Court of the Fountain as a sign of rebirth, and for [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) to come to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and be his queen.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Mindolluin* is a Sindarin name,<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-12">[11]</a></sup> which means "Towering blue-head".<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup> [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack) suggests that it is a combination of a shortened form or a root form of *[minas](/wiki/Minas "Minas")* ("tower"), with *[dol](/wiki/Dol "Dol")* ("head") and *[luin](/wiki/Luin "Luin")* ("blue").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-12">[11]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) mentions that the same stem as in *minas* occurs in 'Mindolluin<i>.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup></i>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a draft of *The Lord of the Rings*, the hill was called *Tor-dilluin*<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup> in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-12">[11]</a></sup> before it was changed to *Mindolluin*.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup>
