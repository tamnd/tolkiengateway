**Master Stadle** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") who drank at *[The Prancing Pony](/wiki/Prancing_Pony "Prancing Pony")* in [Third Age 2940](/wiki/Third_Age_2940). [Thorin II Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin") watched as he walked to the bar with a drink and his pipe. He was lifted up to the stool by a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men").

Master Stadle is an original creation for *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)*. He is portrayed by [Kiran Shah](/wiki/Kiran_Shah).
