**Mirrormere** was a dark lake in [Dimrill Dale](/wiki/Dimrill_Dale)<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup> on the east side of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The lake was located less than a mile below the [East-gate](/wiki/Great_Gates "Great Gates") of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"). It was long and oval with a shape like a great spear-head. The deep water of Mirrormere was dark blue like a clear evening sky seen from a lamp-lit room and had a still surface. Close to the southern end of the lake stood a column with a broken top, which was called [Durin's Stone](/wiki/Durin%27s_Stone). A short distance below the lake was a spring with clear ice-cold water that was the source of the river [Silverlode](/wiki/Celebrant "Celebrant")<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

According to the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk), after <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a> had awoken at [Mount Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad "Gundabad") in the north of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), he travelled south until he came upon this lake. He looked down in it and saw a [crown](/wiki/Durin%27s_Crown "Durin's Crown") above his head, being the reflection of stars above. Taking the crown as a sign, he founded the great city of Khazad-dûm beneath the mountains that fed Mirrormere.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> A pillar called [Durin's Stone](/wiki/Durin%27s_Stone) was placed along the banks of the lake to mark the event.<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-3">[3]</a></sup>

On 10 November [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2994](/wiki/Third_Age_2994 "Third Age 2994")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Balin](/wiki/Balin), the [Lord of Moria](/wiki/Lord_of_Moria) was shot from behind by an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") when he went alone to Dimrill Dale to look into Mirrormere.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

During the events that led up to the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), on 15 January [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) passed by the lake after escaping [Moria](/wiki/Moria). Despite their hurry, [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) would not leave without looking in the lake. [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) joined him, and they saw Durin's crown.<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul), the lake was called <i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Kheled-z%C3%A2ram" title="Kheled-zâram">Kheled-zâram</a></b></i><sup id="cite_ref-South_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-3">[3]</a></sup> (probably "glass-pool" or "glass-lake"),<sup id="cite_ref-Words_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Words-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> a combination of *[kheled](/wiki/Kheled "Kheled")* ("glass") and *[zâram](/wiki/Z%C3%A2ram "Zâram")* ("pool", "lake").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name was <i><b>Nen Cenedril</b></i> ("Lake Looking-glass"),<sup id="cite_ref-Words_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Words-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> a combination of *[nen](/wiki/Nen "Nen")* ("lake") and *cenedril* ("looking-glass", literalle "looking-crystal").<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name was <i><b>Angal-limpë</b></i>, containing *angal* ("mirror"). Abandoned versions of this name were *Angal-millë* and *angalailin*.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier draft, [Durin](/wiki/Durin_I "Durin I") is said to have come to [Azanûl](/wiki/Dimrill_Dale "Dimrill Dale") and named its previously "nameless pool".<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>
