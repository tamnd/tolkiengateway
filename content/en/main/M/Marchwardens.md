The **march-wardens**, or **Elf-wardens**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> were [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) ([Iathrim](/wiki/Iathrim)) in [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol)'s army assigned to guard the borders of the forest of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), including such places as [Aelin-uial](/wiki/Aelin-uial). Their chief was [Mablung of the Heavy Hand](/wiki/Mablung "Mablung"). For a while, [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) served with them, fighting alongside his friend [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) in the north-marches to wage war upon the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Fell_Winter" title="Fell Winter">Fell Winter</a>, march-wardens encountered [Elves of Nargothrond](/wiki/Elves_of_Nargothrond) who had survived the disastrous [Battle of Tumhalad](/wiki/Battle_of_Tumhalad) and sought refuge in Doriath. The march-wardens led them to Thingol, and they brought news from [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond). [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen) then fled Doriath seeking news of her son, and Mablung with hardy march-wardens were tasked to bring her back. Unknown to them, [Nienor](/wiki/Nienor) came with them in disguise.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<ul><li><b>Definition:</b> The title <i>Marchwarden</i> refers to &#34;One who is the warden of the marches&#34;. The term <i>march</i> is an archaic and seldom-used word for a border, and a synonym for <i>warden</i> is &#34;guard&#34;; therefore, a march-warden is a border-guard. Other kingdoms in later ages, besides Doriath, likely had Marchwardens as well; but if they did, it is not mentioned in any others of <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">Tolkien&#39;s</a> works. One character, however, who may well have been a Marchwarden, was <a href="/wiki/Haldir" title="Haldir">Haldir</a> of <a href="/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien" title="Lothlórien">Lothlórien</a>, as he identified himself as a border-guard in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Fellowship of the Ring">The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:  <div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395753">.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider{margin-top:1rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem;color:var(--tg-color-subtle);display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:space-between}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider:not(:empty){gap:1rem;white-space:nowrap}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::before,.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::after{content:"";height:1px;width:100%;background-color:var(--tg-border-color-base)}</style><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt"></blockquote></div></li></ul>

"...But there are some of us still who go abroad for the gathering of news and the watching of our enemies, and they speak the languages of other lands. I am one. Haldir is my name."

<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>-Haldir</cite></p>
