<i><b>Mazarbul</b></i> means "written documents, records" in [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word has a probable root \*ZRB, "write, inscribe".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

If so, it also contains a prefix *ma-* which perhaps denotes perfect tense, and the suffix *[-ul](/wiki/-ul)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
