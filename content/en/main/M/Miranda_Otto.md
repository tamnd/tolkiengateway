**Miranda Otto** is an Australian actress who played [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>).

## Roles {#Roles}

- [2024](../_/2024.md): *[The Lord of the Rings: The War of the Rohirrim](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_War_of_the_Rohirrim.md)*: [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) (narrator)
- [2004](../_/2004.md): *DNZ: The Real Middle Earth*: Herself
- [2004](../_/2004.md): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Third_Age.md)*: [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) (archive footage)
- [2003](../_/2003.md): *The Lord of the Rings: The Quest Fulfilled*: Herself
- [2003](../_/2003.md): *Warriors of the Third Age*: Herself
- [2003](../_/2003.md): [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (video game)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(video_game).md>): [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) (archive footage)
- [2003](../_/2003.md): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*: [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md)
- [2002](../_/2002.md): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*: [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md)
