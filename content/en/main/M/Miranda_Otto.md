**Miranda Otto** is an Australian actress who played [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) in [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").

## Roles {#Roles}

- [2024](/wiki/2024): *[The Lord of the Rings: The War of the Rohirrim](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_War_of_the_Rohirrim)*: [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) (narrator)
- [2004](/wiki/2004): *DNZ: The Real Middle Earth*: Herself
- [2004](/wiki/2004): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Third_Age)*: [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) (archive footage)
- [2003](/wiki/2003): *The Lord of the Rings: The Quest Fulfilled*: Herself
- [2003](/wiki/2003): *Warriors of the Third Age*: Herself
- [2003](/wiki/2003): [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (video game)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (video game)"): [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) (archive footage)
- [2003](/wiki/2003): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*: [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn)
- [2002](/wiki/2002): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*: [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn)
