<i><b>mírdain</b></i> is a noun meaning "jewel-smiths" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-App_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-1">[1]</a></sup>

While unattested, the singular form is commonly held to be *mírdan* ("jewel-smith").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The first element in *mírdain* is *[mîr](Mîr.md)* ("jewel").<sup id="cite_ref-App_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-1">[1]</a></sup>
