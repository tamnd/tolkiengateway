**Moripendi** is a [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) term for the [Dark Elves](/wiki/Dark_Elves), specifically the [Avari](/wiki/Avari), since it was not applied by the Teleri to their Eldarin kin in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) ([Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) and [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*[mori-](/wiki/More "More")* means "black, dark", directly from [primitive](/wiki/Primitive "Primitive") <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mori&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mori (page does not exist)">mori</a></i>, [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") [MOR](/wiki/MOR)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> compounded with *[Pendi](/wiki/Pendi)*.

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[Moriquendi](/wiki/Moriquendi)*
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[Morben](/wiki/Morben)*
