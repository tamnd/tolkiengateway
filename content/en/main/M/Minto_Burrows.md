**Minto Burrows** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Minto was a descendant of the [Masters of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland "Master of Buckland") who could trace his ancestry back to [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck). He was the youngest of the four children of [Milo Burrows](/wiki/Milo_Burrows) and [Peony Baggins](/wiki/Peony_Baggins), and when he was five years old he attended Bilbo's famous [Birthday Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") along with the rest of his family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, [Appendix C](/wiki/Appendix_C) has Minto alone of his family *not* marked as having attented Bilbo's party, perhaps because of his young age. In any case, later editions (cf. [50th Anniversary Edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition)> "The Lord of the Rings (50th Anniversary Edition)")) mark him as an antendee.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Minto appears in <a href="/wiki/Hobbiton" title="Hobbiton">Hobbiton</a>, voiced by an uncredited <a href="/wiki/James_Arnold_Taylor" title="James Arnold Taylor">James Arnold Taylor</a>. If a conversation is started with him, he related how <a href="/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins" title="Lobelia Sackville-Baggins">Lobelia Sackville-Baggins</a> had tried to chase him and <a href="/wiki/Sancho_Proudfoot" title="Sancho Proudfoot">Sancho Proudfoot</a> off with her umbrella, and Minto had thrown an apple at her (but missed) in retaliation.</dd></dl>
