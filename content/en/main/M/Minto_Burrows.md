**Minto Burrows** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Minto was a descendant of the [Masters of Buckland](Master_of_Buckland.md) who could trace his ancestry back to [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](../G/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck.md). He was the youngest of the four children of [Milo Burrows](Milo_Burrows.md) and [Peony Baggins](../P/Peony_Baggins.md), and when he was five years old he attended Bilbo's famous [Birthday Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md) along with the rest of his family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, [Appendix C](../A/Appendix_C.md) has Minto alone of his family *not* marked as having attented Bilbo's party, perhaps because of his young age. In any case, later editions (cf. [50th Anniversary Edition](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md>)) mark him as an antendee.

If a conversation is started with him, he related how <a href="../L/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins.md" title="Lobelia Sackville-Baggins">Lobelia Sackville-Baggins</a> had tried to chase him and <a href="../S/Sancho_Proudfoot.md" title="Sancho Proudfoot">Sancho Proudfoot</a> off with her umbrella, and Minto had thrown an apple at her (but missed) in retaliation.</dd></dl>
