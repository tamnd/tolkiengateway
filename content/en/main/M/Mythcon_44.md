**Mythcon 44** was the forty-fourth annual [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) conference.

- **Theme:** "Green and Growing: The Land and Its Inhabitants in Fantasy"
- **Guests of Honor:** [Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson) (Inkling scholar), and Franny Billingsley (fantasy author)
