**Mythcon 44** was the forty-fourth annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme:** "Green and Growing: The Land and Its Inhabitants in Fantasy"
- **Guests of Honor:** [Douglas A. Anderson](../D/Douglas_A._Anderson.md) (Inkling scholar), and Franny Billingsley (fantasy author)
