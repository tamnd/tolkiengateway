The **Moors of the Nibin-noeg** were high uplands that rose between the [Vales of Sirion](/wiki/Vales_of_Sirion) and the [Narog](/wiki/Narog), northeast of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> On the eastern side rose prominence of [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh). The moor was covered in thickets of *[aeglos](</wiki/Aeglos_(plant)> "Aeglos (plant)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The moorlands were associated with the [Nibin-noeg](/wiki/Nibin-noeg) because in ancient times they had been part of their lands. Nargothrond to the west had originally been delved by the Nibin-noeg,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> as had the chambers at the top of Amon Rûdh, known by the Petty-dwarves as *[Sharbhund](/wiki/Sharbhund)* before the coming of the Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the [Second 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_Second_%27Silmarillion%27_Map "The Second 'Silmarillion' Map") the Moors were named **Moors of the Neweglu**.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The name <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Neweglu&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Neweglu (page does not exist)">Neweglu</a></i> does not appear elsewhere, but it is close to the name <i>Neweg, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Newegl%C3%AEn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Neweglîn (page does not exist)">Neweglîn</a></i>, one of the names of the Petty-dwarves found in the *Narn* papers.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
