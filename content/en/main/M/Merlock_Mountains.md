The **Merlock Mountains** are mentioned in the [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md)-poem *[The Mewlips](../T/The_Mewlips.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is unknown if the Merlock Mountains refer to an actual mountain range in [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md), or if they were just an invention by the poet. As the poem describes a location similar to [Mirkwood](Mirkwood.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> it is possible that they were a fictitious version of the [Misty Mountains](Misty_Mountains.md), or what the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) knew about them.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Fans](../T/Tolkien_fandom.md) have speculated that the name *Merlock* was inspired by the name *Morlock* appearing in "Knocking at the Door", an earlier version of the poem (which was perhaps itself inspired by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._G._Wells" title="wikipedia:H. G. Wells">H.G. Wells</a>'s <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morlock" title="wikipedia:Morlock">Morlock</a></i>) <sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
