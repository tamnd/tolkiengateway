<b><i>Mythlore</i> 77</b> (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/mythlore/vol20/iss3/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Volume 20, Issue 3</a>) is an issue of the *[Mythlore](Mythlore.md)* journal, published by the [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

This is one of several short supplemental issues that *Mythlore* published in order to comply with bulk mailing permit regulations. This issue mainly consisted of an editorial by Glen H. GoodKnight and information about the upcoming 1995 Mythopoeic Conference.
