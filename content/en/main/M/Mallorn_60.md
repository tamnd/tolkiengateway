<b><i>Mallorn</i> 60</b> is the sixtieth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s annual journal *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")*, published in Summer [2020](/wiki/2020).

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Cover Art**
    - "Rivendell"
        - By: Emily Austin
- **Editorial**
    - "Starlight in Shadow"
        - By: Luke Shelton
- **Articles**
    - "Scouring the Shire: Panoptic Power and Community Healing in Foucault's 'Ponopticism' and Tolkien's *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*"
        - By: Garrett Van Curen
    - "Not at Home: Liminal Space and Personal Identity in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *Coraline*"
        - By: Kristen McQuinn
    - "Tolkien on Holiday"
        - By: [Andrew Higgins](/wiki/Andrew_Higgins)
    - "In the Moon Gleaming"
        - By: Martin Beech
- **Notes**
    - "The Tolkien Art Index"
        - By: Erik Mueller-Harder
    - "There and Back Again? Tolkien's Brief Visit to Sussex in 1904"
        - By: Mick Henry
    - "A Note on 'A Holy Party: Holiness in *The Hobbi*t'"
        - By: N.J.S. Polk
- **Reviews**
    - *The Heroes of Tolkien*
        - By: Martina Juričková
    - *[Tolkien's Library: An Annotated Checklist](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Library "Tolkien's Library")*
        - By: Jeremy Edmonds

    - *[Tolkien and the Classics](/wiki/Tolkien_and_the_Classics)*
        - By: Tom Hewitt
- **Submissions**
    - Letters to the Editor
    - Guide for Authors
- **Inside Art**
    - By: Tamsin Barlow, Donato Giancola, Jay Johnstone, Breana Melvin, Hannah Polk
