**Melbourne House** was a video game developer. Founded in [1980](/wiki/1980) as **Beam Software**, Melbourne House was first owned by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atari" title="wikipedia:Atari">Atari</a>, and as of [2006](/wiki/2006) by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krome_Studios" title="wikipedia:Krome Studios">Krome Studios</a>. It was closed on 15 October 2010, along with the main Brisbane office. Except games it published aswell books about <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_computer" title="wikipedia:Personal computer">computers</a> and games, by its subsidiary **Melbourne House Publishers, Limited.**.
