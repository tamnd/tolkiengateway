**Måns Björkman Berg** aka **Elros** is a Swedish [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) linguist and member of [Tolkiensällskapet Forodrim](../F/Forodrim.md).

On his webpage, <i><a class="external text" href="http://at.boktypografen.se/index.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Amanye Tenceli: The Writing Systems of Aman</a></i>, Berg specializes on presentation and discussion of writing systems invented by Tolkien ([Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) and [Sarati](../S/Sarati.md)).

Berg also works with graphic design, having, for example, created computer fonts based on Tolkien's writing systems and made the design for the Swedish booklet *[Alviska fraser](../A/Alviska_fraser.md)* ("Elvish Phrases").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
