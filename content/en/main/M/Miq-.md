<i><b>miq-</b></i> is a [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) verb meaning "to kiss".<sup id="cite_ref-PE12_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE12-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) has suggested that the form *miqu-* (retaining the same meaning) could be valid [Neo-Quenya](../N/Neo-Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) MIQI ("kiss")<sup id="cite_ref-PE12_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE12-1">[1]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}
