**Mrs. Maggot** was the wife of the [Farmer Maggot](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot). Their home was the farm [Bamfurlong](/wiki/Bamfurlong) and they had at least two sons and three daughters.

Mrs. Maggot served [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") when her husband hosted them. When the Farmer took the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) to the Ferry, Mrs. Maggot sent with him a basket of [mushrooms](/wiki/Mushrooms "Mushrooms") for Frodo, knowing how much he liked them.<sup id="cite_ref-mushroom_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mushroom-1">[1]</a></sup>
