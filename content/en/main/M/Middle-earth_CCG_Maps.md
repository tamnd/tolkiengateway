<i><b>Middle-earth CCG Maps</b></i> is a map-book for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

In this set you get a 17" X 11.5" map (three-hole drilled and folded to fit in a standard three-ring binder), and a 23.5" X 17" map (includes complete information on which types of cards are playable at each site). Both are printed on a durable plastic-paper that resists stains and normal wear and tear. The full-color interior of this slip cover can be used as an insert cover of a 3-ring binder with clear plastic cover sleeves.

## From the back cover {#From_the_back_cover}

Each of the two full-color maps in this product can be used as an attractive game board and reference tool for players of the *Middle-earth Collectible Card Game*. Use them as playing surfaces, and help players master both region movement and Middle-earth geography.

• Both maps are printed on a durable plastic-paper that resists both stains and normal wear and tear.

• Both maps include all of the regions and sites from *Middle-earth: The Wizards*, *Middle-earth: The Dragons*, and *Middle-earth: Dark Minions*.

• The 17" x 11.5" map is three-hole drilled and folded so that it fits in a standard three-ring binder.

• The 23.5" x 17" map includes complete information concerning which types of cards (e.g., factions, allies, items, etc.) are playable at each site.

• The interior of this slip cover can be used as a full-color insert for the cover of a three-ring binder with clear plastic cover sleeves.
