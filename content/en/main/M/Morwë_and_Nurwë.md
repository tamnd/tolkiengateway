**Morwë** and **Nurwë** were the leaders of two of the kindreds of the [Avari](../A/Avari.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[1]</a></sup> according to a version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) that [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) considered abandoned by [his father](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of the kindreds of Morwë and Nurwë dwelt furthest away from the waters of [Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md). They wandered among the hills and never saw [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) when he first came to them.

During the first [sundering of the Elves](../S/Sundering_of_the_Elves.md), the kindreds of Morwë and Nurwë had refused the summons of Oromë, as they preferred the starlight over the rumor of the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md) in [Aman](../A/Aman.md). Moreover, they feared the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), especially Oromë and his steed [Nahar](../N/Nahar.md), considering Oromë a [demon](../D/Demons.md) "that would devour them" - possibly because of the lies of [Melkor](Melkor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Avar_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Avar-5">[3]</a></sup>

They remained at Cuiviénen with half of the [Tatyar](../T/Tatyar.md) (the second clan, whose other half later became known as the *[Noldor](../N/Noldor.md)*), and a third of the [Nelyar](../N/Nelyar.md) (the third clan, whose other two thirds later became known as the *[Teleri](../T/Teleri.md)* or *[Lindar](../L/Lindar.md)*).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The names *Morwë* and *Nurwë* are in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), but they are not glossed.

*Morwë* might have been composed of the root [MOR](MOR~34024.md)- ("black, dark, darkness") + the suffix *[-wë](../_/-wë.md)*, a common (if not always masculine) element in ancient [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) names.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[5]</a></sup>

*Nurwë*, likewise, contains the suffix *-wë*, but the first element of his name is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[6]</a></sup>
