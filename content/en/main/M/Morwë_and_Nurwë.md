**Morwë** and **Nurwë** were the leaders of two of the kindreds of the [Avari](/wiki/Avari),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[1]</a></sup> according to a version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") that [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) considered abandoned by [his father](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of the kindreds of Morwë and Nurwë dwelt furthest away from the waters of [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen). They wandered among the hills and never saw [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) when he first came to them.

During the first [sundering of the Elves](/wiki/Sundering_of_the_Elves "Sundering of the Elves"), the kindreds of Morwë and Nurwë had refused the summons of Oromë, as they preferred the starlight over the rumor of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor") in [Aman](/wiki/Aman). Moreover, they feared the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), especially Oromë and his steed [Nahar](/wiki/Nahar), considering Oromë a [demon](/wiki/Demons "Demons") "that would devour them" - possibly because of the lies of [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor).<sup id="cite_ref-Avar_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Avar-5">[3]</a></sup>

They remained at Cuiviénen with half of the [Tatyar](/wiki/Tatyar) (the second clan, whose other half later became known as the *[Noldor](/wiki/Noldor)*), and a third of the [Nelyar](/wiki/Nelyar) (the third clan, whose other two thirds later became known as the *[Teleri](/wiki/Teleri)* or *[Lindar](/wiki/Lindar)*).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The names *Morwë* and *Nurwë* are in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), but they are not glossed.

*Morwë* might have been composed of the root [MOR](/wiki/MOR)- ("black, dark, darkness") + the suffix *[-wë](/wiki/-w%C3%AB)*, a common (if not always masculine) element in ancient [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) names.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[5]</a></sup>

*Nurwë*, likewise, contains the suffix *-wë*, but the first element of his name is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[6]</a></sup>
