<i><b>Mithril</b></i>, **Moria-silver**, **silver-steel**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or **true-silver** called by some, was a precious metal highly prized for its strength, light weight, and malleability. The [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) had their own, secret name for it.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Elves](../E/Elves.md) called it *mithril*,<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-2">[2]</a></sup> a name given by the elven mastersmith [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>.

## History {#History}

Mithril was found in the [Misty Mountains](Misty_Mountains.md), in lodes leading north from the underground dwarven city of [Moria](Moria.md) towards [Caradhras](../C/Caradhras.md) and down to darkness.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-2">[2]</a></sup> It was also found on the island of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and probably in [Aman](../A/Aman.md) as well<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-5">[5]</a></sup>.

The sole mithril-vein of the [Misty Mountains](Misty_Mountains.md) made the [Longbeards](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md) of [Moria](Moria.md) wealthy.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-2">[2]</a></sup> Some [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) settled in [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) near the West-gate of [Moria](Moria.md), because they had heard that mithril had been found<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and desired to use it in their crafts. Thanks to trade with the [Dwarves of Moria](../D/Dwarves_of_Khazad-dûm.md), the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) created objects of mithril, perhaps the most notable being [Nenya](../N/Nenya.md), one of the [Three Elven Rings](../T/Three_Rings.md) [of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) of [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) also made an alloy out of mithril called *[ithildin](../I/Ithildin.md)* ("star moon"),<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-2">[2]</a></sup> which was visible only by starlight or moonlight. This was used by the elven mastersmith Celebrimbor for the inlaid decorative designs and writings on the [Doors of Durin](../D/Doors_of_Durin.md) on the West-gate of Moria.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

King [Tar-Telemmaitë](../T/Tar-Telemmaitë.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) received his name, which means silver-handed,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> because he loved silver and always commanded his servants to search for mithril.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

After the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) and the removal of physical [Aman](../A/Aman.md) from the world near the end of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), the mines of [Moria](Moria.md) were the only source of Mithril in the world. Mithril was worth ten times its weight in gold when it could still be mined by the Dwarves.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-2">[2]</a></sup>

Over time, the Dwarves of [Moria](Moria.md) continued to delve deep into the darkness below [Caradhras](../C/Caradhras.md) and in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1980](../T/Third_Age_1980.md)<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> they released a [Balrog](../D/Durin's_Bane.md) from the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md). The [Balrog](../D/Durin's_Bane.md) destroyed the kingdom<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-12">[12]</a></sup> and caused the mining of mithril to stop when the surviving Dwarves<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-12">[12]</a></sup> fled in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1981](../T/Third_Age_1981.md),<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> after which mithril became priceless. The [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) that inhabited [Moria](Moria.md) after the release of the [Balrog](../D/Durin's_Bane.md) did not dare to delve for mithril and gave all the mithril that the dwarves had already mined as tribute to [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), who desired it.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-2">[2]</a></sup>

After [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) became [Lord of the Glittering Caves](../L/Lord_of_the_Glittering_Caves.md), he and his Dwarves forged great gates of *mithril* and steel to replace the [Great Gate](../G/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith.md) of [Minas Tirith](Minas_Tirith.md) which was broken by the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Other mithril creations {#Other_mithril_creations}

- [Vingilótë](../V/Vingilótë.md): The ship of [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md), which he sailed into the sky, making the gleam of truesilver visible to the world as the Evening and Morning Star, was, according to legend, built for him from mithril and from elven-glass.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-5">[5]</a></sup>
- The [Star of Elendil](../S/Star_of_Elendil.md): A gem set on mithril fillet that was an heirloom of the [Lords of Andúnië](../L/Lords_of_Andúnië.md) of Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-15">[15]</a></sup>
    - The second Star of Elendil was created by the smiths of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) after the first one was lost with Isildur in the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](../D/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-15">[15]</a></sup>
- The helmets of the [Guards of the Citadel](../G/Guards_of_the_Citadel.md) of [Minas Tirith](Minas_Tirith.md), considered "heirlooms from the glory of old days".<sup id="cite_ref-MT_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-16">[16]</a></sup>
- The [Mithril-coat](Mithril-coat.md) made by Dwarves and given to [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) as a gift by [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Journey_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-South_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-18">[18]</a></sup>
- The embroidered [crown](../C/Crown_of_Gondor.md) in the [standard of Elendil](../S/Standard_of_Elendil.md), made by [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) for [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), contained mithril.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *mithril* consists of the two [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) words *[mith](Mith.md)* ("grey, light grey") + *[ril](../R/Ril.md)* ("brilliance").<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2024: <i><a href="../T/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="Moria_-_Through_the_Doors_of_Durin.md" title="Moria - Through the Doors of Durin">Moria: Through the Doors of Durin</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Several forms and alloys of mithril are detailed:<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup></dd></dl>

- *Native Mithril:* Raw unrefined nuggets, as soft as copper.
- *True Mithril:* Mithril refined by Dwarves, a secret technique which was also known to or rediscovered by the forges of [Mordor](Mordor.md). It is this form which is used in alloys.
- *Mithril-steel:* An alloy with steel. The uneven mixing led to the mithril forming veins throughout the steel, which made it stronger.
- *Thilevril:* Mithril drawn into thin wires, mostly used for instruments. Orcs repurposed these into whips.
- *Sullied Mithril:* An alloy developed in Mordor from Moria's tribute. When activated by a spoken command, weapons forged from it became very hot or erupted into flames.
