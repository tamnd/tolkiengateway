<i><b>már</b></i> (pl. <i><b>mardi</b></i>) means "dwelling" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:64</sup>

It was used with a defining genitive or in a genitival compound. If added to a personal name, it referred to the residence of the named head of a family and included the adjacent lands attached to permanent buildings and dwellings (ex. [Ingwemar](../I/Ingwemar.md)). When added to the name of a people, it referred to the whole area occupied by them (ex. [Valimar](../V/Valimar.md), [Eldamar](../E/Eldamar.md)). In many cases it thus became synonymous with [nórë](../N/Nórë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-dwell_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwell-2">[2]</a></sup>

There are several specifically defined meanings where it did *not* apply. It was not used in the context of a single person.<sup id="cite_ref-dwell_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwell-2">[2]</a></sup> It was not used to mean a small separate building with a specific function, such as a bake-house or wood-house, which was usually called "<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=K%C3%B6a&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Köa (page does not exist)">köa</a>". It was also not used to mean "house" in the sense of a family of power or authority, which was instead represented by words meaning "kindred".<sup id="cite_ref-dwell_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwell-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:fn3</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [MBAR](MBAR.md) "dwelling, habitation".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Notes {#Notes}

[Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) believes that *már* is unlikely to have the stem-form *mard-* and that the root resulted to **two** words: *már (mar-)* for "home, house", whereas *mar (mard-)* for "earth, world". <cite class="citation cite-web"><span class="cite-web-author"><a href="../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md" title="Helge Fauskanger">Helge Fauskanger</a></span>. <span class="cite-web-article"><a class="external text" href="http://folk.uib.no/hnohf/quen-eng.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">&#34;Quettaparma Quenyallo&#34;</a></span>. <span class="cite-web-website">Arda</span></cite>
