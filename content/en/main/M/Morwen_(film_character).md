**Morwen** is a [woman](../R/Rohirrim.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) who sent her two children to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md). Morwen was invented by [Warner Bros.](../W/Warner_Bros..md) for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), where she was portrayed by [Robyn Malcolm](../R/Robyn_Malcolm.md).

## History {#History}

Morwen was the mother of [Freda](../F/Freda.md) and [Éothain](<../_/Éothain_(film_character).md>), whom she sent to alert [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) that the [Wild Men](../D/Dunlendings.md) were pillaging through [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). Her children were kept at [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) until Théoden led his people to shelter at [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md), where the children were reunited with their mother.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name [Morwen](Morwen.md) is unusual for the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md), being [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) and not [Old English](../O/Old_English.md). Movie producers reasoned that this Elvish name would become popular in Rohan after the coming of [Morwen Steelsheen](Morwen_Steelsheen.md), the [Gondorian](../G/Gondor.md) queen consort of King [Thengel](../T/Thengel.md).
