**Morwen** is a [woman](/wiki/Rohirrim "Rohirrim") of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) who sent her two children to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras). Morwen was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), where she was portrayed by [Robyn Malcolm](/wiki/Robyn_Malcolm).

## History {#History}

Morwen was the mother of [Freda](/wiki/Freda) and [Éothain](</wiki/%C3%89othain_(film_character)> "Éothain (film character)"), whom she sent to alert [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) that the [Wild Men](/wiki/Dunlendings "Dunlendings") were pillaging through [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). Her children were kept at [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) until Théoden led his people to shelter at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep), where the children were reunited with their mother.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen) is unusual for the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), being [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and not [Old English](/wiki/Old_English). Movie producers reasoned that this Elvish name would become popular in Rohan after the coming of [Morwen Steelsheen](/wiki/Morwen_Steelsheen), the [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") queen consort of King [Thengel](/wiki/Thengel).
