**Mikael Åke Persbrandt** (born [25 September](../_/25_September.md) [1963](../_/1963.md)) is a Swedish actor.

He portrayed [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md) in *[The Hobbit (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)*. The rumour that Persbrandt would have a role in [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md)'s *The Hobbit (film series)* first appeared in Swedish magazine <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aftonbladet" title="wikipedia:Aftonbladet">Aftonbladet</a></i> on 3 December 2010.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> On 7 December 2010 it was officially announced that Persbrandt had been cast and that he would portray the character of Beorn.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Persbrandt mentioned in an interview that he was a "fantasy geek" as a child, having read all the books by Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
