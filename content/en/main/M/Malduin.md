**Malduin** was a minor river of northern [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). It flowed southwards from the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) to meet the [Teiglin](../T/Teiglin.md) before that river passed under the eaves of the [Forest of Brethil](../F/Forest_of_Brethil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It means "Golden River" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), from [MAL](MALAT.md) ("gold") + *[duin](../D/Duin.md)* ("river").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
