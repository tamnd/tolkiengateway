<i><b>mírë</b></i> (or *míre*, *mīre*) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word. As a noun, it means "a treasure, a beautiful (precious) thing, especially (but not solely) a gem, jewel";<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup> plural form <i><b>míri</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> As an adjective, it means "precious".<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>
