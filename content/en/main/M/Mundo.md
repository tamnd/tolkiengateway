<i><b>mundo</b></i> is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) word said to mean "[bull](/wiki/Cows "Cows")".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its form *-mund* can create compound words together with prefixes (see Compounds).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is possibly related to the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=MBUD&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MBUD (page does not exist)">MBUD</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Compounds {#Compounds}

- *[Aramund](/wiki/Aramund)* ("Kingly bull")
- *Tarmund* ("Noble bull")
- *Rasmund* ("Horned bull")
- *Turcomund* ("Chief of bulls")
