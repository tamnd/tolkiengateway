<i><b>melme</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) noun meaning "love".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested the form *melmë*, to conform with the spelling of Quenya used in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
