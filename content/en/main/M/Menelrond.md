**Menelrond** was the name of the throne room of [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) in [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth). It was so named because of its huge dome adorned with [silver](/wiki/Silver "Silver") and precious stones, the arrangement of which, thanks to [Melian](/wiki/Melian)'s visions, perfectly respected the position of the stars of the [Dome of Valmar](/wiki/Dome_of_Varda "Dome of Varda").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) translated the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name *Menelrond* as "heaven-dome".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It combines the elements *[menel](/wiki/Menel "Menel")* ("heavens") + *-[rond](/wiki/Rond "Rond")* ("dome").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
