**Marmadoc "Masterful" Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Marmadoc was the son of [Madoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Madoc_Brandybuck) and [Hanna Goldworthy](/wiki/Hanna_Goldworthy). He married [Adaldrida Bolger](/wiki/Adaldrida_Bolger) and had four children; two sons, [Gorbadoc](/wiki/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck "Gorbadoc Brandybuck") and [Orgulas](/wiki/Orgulas_Brandybuck "Orgulas Brandybuck"), and two daughters.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Marmadoc* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) suggests that the name was inspired by the name *Marmaduke*, spelled *Marmaduc* in medieval times, which is derived from Irish *Maelmadoc* ("Servant-(of)-Maedoc"). It is possible that this is a reference to the first name of his father, [Madoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Madoc_Brandybuck), and that the first name of his grandfather [Gormadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gormadoc_Brandybuck) is in turn a reference to the first name of Madoc Brandybuck.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier version of the family tree for the [Brandybucks](/wiki/Brandybucks), his name was *Marmaduc*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
