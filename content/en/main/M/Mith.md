## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>mith</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "grey", "pale grey", or "light grey".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Noldorin {#Noldorin}

<i><b>mith</b></i> is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word meaning "white fog, wet mist" or "grey".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>
