In [Middle-earth Role Playing](Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md), the **Mûmakan**, known to Elves as Ardor ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) “Exalted Land”), was a jungle land to the south of the [Yellow Mountains](../Y/Yellow_Mountains.md) and the north of the Sea of Haragaer.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The history of the Mûmakan was intertwined with the evil [Court of Ardor](../C/Court_of_Ardor.md), which was formed in the late [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) by Elves who had been corrupted by [Morgoth](Morgoth.md). The organization fell into disarray and its leadership fled after the overthrow of Morgoth in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md). In the meantime, [Men](Men.md) had arrived in the region. Around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_100&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 100 (page does not exist)">100</a>, [Koronandë](../K/Koronandë.md) and later [Hathor](../H/Hathor.md) were founded. In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1300&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1300 (page does not exist)">1300</a>, the Númenórean colony [Tantûrak](../T/Tantûrak.md) was founded.<sup id="cite_ref-history_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Magician from the Court of Ardor arrived in Tantûrak around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2000&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2000 (page does not exist)">2000</a>, and inadvertently led to the ouster of [Jí Indûr](../F/Fourth_of_the_Nazgûl.md), who had been the contested king of the erstwhile republic Koronandë. Having fled to the tribes of Mûmakan, with the aid of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s influence, he presented himself as the second-coming of their God-king Amaav a year later. As Jí Amaav II, Indûr united the tribes, and subjugated Gan and parts of [Dûshera](../D/Dûshera.md) (near the Swamp of Geshaan). Further expansion was contested by Númenor and the Court of Ardor. Ultimately, he made a pact with the Magician. With Indûr's support, Tantûrak cut ties with Númenor in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_3000&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 3000 (page does not exist)">3000</a>. Koronandë was thus surrounded by hostile forces, solely spared from complete conquest because of the rivalry between Indûr's Mûmakan Empire and Tantûrak. [Ar-Pharazon](../A/Ar-Pharazon.md) defeated Indûr and reconquered Tantûrak in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3262](../S/Second_Age_3262.md).<sup id="cite_ref-nazgul_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nazgul-3">[3]</a></sup>

When Indûr returned in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1050](../T/Third_Age_1050.md), he slowly worked on rebuilding his empire. By [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1250](../T/Third_Age_1250.md) he had united the tribes into a loose military coalition focused on expansion. Again under the Magician's sway, Tantûrak declared war on Koronandë in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1365&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1365 (page does not exist)">1365</a>. Through Indûr's intervention, a peace was reached in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1372&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1372 (page does not exist)">1372</a>, which increased his influence.<sup id="cite_ref-nazgul_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nazgul-3">[3]</a></sup>

The relative peace of the continent was broken by [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1650&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1650 (page does not exist)">1650</a>, with more and more minor battles having broken out as the population increased. Orcs and wargs became increasingly common sights, and they often raided Dûshera's villages.<sup id="cite_ref-history_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-2">[2]</a></sup>

By [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3000](../T/Third_Age_3000.md), the replacement of the deceased Magician by an agent of Sauron had led to Tantûrak coming under his sway. Tantûrak regularly raided Hathor during this time, and dominated Dûshera.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

### Coasts {#Coasts}

The majority of the Mûmakan coastline consisted of sandy beaches, with the notable exception of the southern tip of the peninsula. That region was rocky, rising to sheer cliffs up to 300 feet high which were honeycombed with caves and grottoes. While the inland regions near the coast generally consisted of wild grass and scattered pines or palms, the jungle foliage occasionally extended almost to the water's edge.

The coastal waters were rich in marine life. Sharks were rare within ten miles of the shore, allowing the inhabitants of Hathor and Koronandë to harvest plentiful fish stocks safely.<sup id="cite_ref-florafauna_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-florafauna-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Rainforest {#Rainforest}

Rainforests covered much of the land, with a dense canopy of trees that grew 100 to 150 feet in height and blocked most sunlight. This lack of sunlight meant that travellers who managed to breach the initial thickets would then encounter a spacious inner area. *Lurakil*, or lord trees, rose above the canopy, growing over 200 feet. This is where Elves and Kirani built tree-houses.

The rainforest fauna included brightly plumaged birds, gliding squirrels, and large constrictor snakes. Predators such as leopards and ocelots were also present but rarely ventured near human settlements.<sup id="cite_ref-florafauna_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-florafauna-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Plains and hills {#Plains_and_hills}

The plains of the Mûmakan were barren compared to the jungle, consisting of endless grass broken only by stunted trees and wild grains. These lands supported herds of gazelles, wild horses, and the region's most famous inhabitants, the [Mûmakil](Mûmakil.md).

The hilly regions, particularly the spines running down the peninsulas, offered a cooler climate and abundant water. Agriculture was possible here: fields of corn and cotton in the south, while wheat was grown in Dûshera, which was a region of independent hill-towns.<sup id="cite_ref-florafauna_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-florafauna-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Swamp of Geshaan {#Swamp_of_Geshaan}

The swamp of Geshaan resembled the rainforest, but it was untamed and treacherous. The unstable ground is often submerged under several feet of stagnant water, and otherwise is filled with fallen trees, huge vines, and overgrown underbrush that make passage nearly impossible.<sup id="cite_ref-florafauna_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-florafauna-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
