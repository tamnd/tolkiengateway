<b><i>Mallorn</i> 2</b> is the second issue of the [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Society.md)'s journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in 1971.

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Editorial**
    - Letters to the Editor
        - By Archie Mercer, Dave Weldrake, Stewart Bailey, A.R. Fallone, Arthur Cruttenden
    - Belladonna's Postbag
        - By Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md)), Hal Broome, Dave Weldrake, Philip Howard, Rosie Turner, and James Ead
    - Editorial
        - By Steven Thomson
- **Notes**
    - The Dragon
        - By Dave Weldrake
    - Llasar Llaes Something and Family
        - By Archie Mercer
    - Hippies or Hobbits?
        - By Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md))
    - On Behalf of the Half-Hippy
        - By A.R. Fallone
- **Reviews**
    - *The Last Unicorn* by [Peter Beagle](../P/Peter_Beagle.md)
        - Reviewed by Alex Holdschmidt
- **Creative**
    - Silver (poem)
        - Author unknown
    - Untitled poem ("Good Boromir, come blow up your horn")
        - By Keith Bridges
    - Illustrations
        - By Brian Talbot and A.R. Fallone
