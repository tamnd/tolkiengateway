**Mythcon 18** was the eighteenth annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme:** "Looking Back From Weathertop: A Fifty Years' Retrospective on [The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)"
- **Guests of Honor:** [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) (Tolkien scholar and executor) and John Bellairs (fantasy author)
- **Chairman:** Gregory G.H. Rihn
