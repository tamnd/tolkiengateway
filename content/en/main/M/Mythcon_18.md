**Mythcon 18** was the eighteenth annual [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) conference.

- **Theme:** "Looking Back From Weathertop: A Fifty Years' Retrospective on [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)"
- **Guests of Honor:** [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) (Tolkien scholar and executor) and John Bellairs (fantasy author)
- **Chairman:** Gregory G.H. Rihn
