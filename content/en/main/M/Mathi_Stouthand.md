**Mathi Stouthand** is a [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") from [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") who accompanied [Dwalin](/wiki/Dwalin) and [Dori](/wiki/Dori) with a company of [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) to investigate Thorin's Gate on [Dáin II](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot")'s order. During Dwalin's investigation he could be found in Frerin's Court.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He sent the player to kill Frostmantle.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He fought in the war between the [Dourhands](/wiki/Dourhands) and [Longbeards](/wiki/Longbeards), who attempted to drive the first out of the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin). After the Longbeards conquered Gondamon Mathi became the lord of Gondamon.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
