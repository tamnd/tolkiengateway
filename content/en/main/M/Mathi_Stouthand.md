**Mathi Stouthand** is a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) from [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) who accompanied [Dwalin](../D/Dwalin.md) and [Dori](../D/Dori.md) with a company of [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) to investigate Thorin's Gate on [Dáin II](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md)'s order. During Dwalin's investigation he could be found in Frerin's Court.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He sent the player to kill Frostmantle.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He fought in the war between the [Dourhands](../D/Dourhands.md) and [Longbeards](../L/Longbeards.md), who attempted to drive the first out of the [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md). After the Longbeards conquered Gondamon Mathi became the lord of Gondamon.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
