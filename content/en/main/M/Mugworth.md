**Mugworth** was the name of a village in [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing), mentioned in a draft version of the eighth chapter of <i><b><a href="/wiki/The_Two_Towers" title="The Two Towers">The Two Towers</a></b></i>, *[The Road to Isengard](/wiki/The_Road_to_Isengard)*.

It was said to be the home of a [hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") called [Elias Tobiasson](/wiki/Tobold_Hornblower#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Tobold Hornblower"), who introduced [Pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed) to [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
