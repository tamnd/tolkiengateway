**Mugworth** was the name of a village in [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md), mentioned in a draft version of the eighth chapter of <i><b><a href="../T/The_Two_Towers.md" title="The Two Towers">The Two Towers</a></b></i>, *[The Road to Isengard](../T/The_Road_to_Isengard.md)*.

It was said to be the home of a [hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) called [Elias Tobiasson](../T/Tobold_Hornblower.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium), who introduced [Pipe-weed](../P/Pipe-weed.md) to [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
