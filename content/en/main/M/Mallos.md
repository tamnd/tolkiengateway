**Mallos** was a flower that grew in the vales of [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin) in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), described as having the shape and colour of a golden bell.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Mallos* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "golden flower",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> apparently consisting of elements related to *[malt](/wiki/Malt "Malt")* ("gold") + *[loth](/wiki/Loth "Loth")*/*[loss](/wiki/Loss "Loss")* ("flower"/"snow").

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor" title="Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor">Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Mallos is a flower which can heal <a href="/wiki/Talion" title="Talion">Talion</a>. It is described as looking similar to <a href="/wiki/Alfirin" title="Alfirin">Alfirin</a>, though Alfirin had a pleasent aroma while Mallos&#39; was musty and skunky. It is said that healers used it in combination with other flowers and its primary usage is in lozenges and draughts, which stopped coughs and soothed throats. It is mentioned that, while native to Gondor, it now grows in <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a>, likely due to the Outcasts (Gondorian Rangers who deserted and now reside in Mordor).</dd></dl>
