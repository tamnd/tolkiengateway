**Monday** (archaic form *Monendei*) was the third day of the week in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar). It was dedicated to the [Moon](/wiki/Moon), representing the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) day *[Orithil](/wiki/Orithil)*.<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the reform of [Isengrim Took II](/wiki/Isengrim_Took_II),<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup> [1 May](/wiki/1_May) and [1 November](/wiki/1_November) always fell on this day.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Monday* represents an archaic [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) form "Monendei", a word preserved in the [Yellowskin](/wiki/Yellowskin). It is dedicated to the [Moon](/wiki/Moon).<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Monday* is translated as "Monday" in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup>
