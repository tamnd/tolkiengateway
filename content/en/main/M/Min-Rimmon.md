**Min-Rimmon** was the fifth of the seven [Beacon-hills](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), which ran west from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) on the northern side of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) that looked down on the plains of [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

Min-Rimmon was the highest peak in a group of crags known as the <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rimmon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rimmon (page does not exist)">Rimmon</a></i>. With [Eilenach](/wiki/Eilenach) and [Amon Dîn](/wiki/Amon_D%C3%AEn), Min-Rimmon was one of the oldest Beacon-hills, set in place before the foundation of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On the night of [7 March](/wiki/7_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), riding eastward on [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax), saw the beacons being lit to call for aid from Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On [12 March](/wiki/12_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden), on his way to Minas Tirith, camped his army of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) under Min-Rimmon.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

After the defeat of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), tidings of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)'s coronation were sent out to "*all parts of Gondor, from Min-Rimmon even to [Pinnath Gelin](/wiki/Pinnath_Gelin) and the far coasts of the sea*".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> This would suggest that there was a settlement of some kind at Min-Rimmon, and that it marked the western boundary of Gondorian settlement in Anórien by the end of the Third Age.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*[Min](/wiki/Mindon "Mindon")* means "peak" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), thus *Min-Rimmon* refers to the "Peak of the Rimmon".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup> "Rimmon" is a name of forgotten origin, coming from the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) before Gondor was established.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
