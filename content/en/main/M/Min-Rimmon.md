**Min-Rimmon** was the fifth of the seven [Beacon-hills](../W/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), which ran west from [Minas Tirith](Minas_Tirith.md) on the northern side of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) that looked down on the plains of [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

Min-Rimmon was the highest peak in a group of crags known as the <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Rimmon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rimmon (page does not exist)">Rimmon</a></i>. With [Eilenach](../E/Eilenach.md) and [Amon Dîn](../A/Amon_Dîn.md), Min-Rimmon was one of the oldest Beacon-hills, set in place before the foundation of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On the night of [7 March](../_/7_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), riding eastward on [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md), saw the beacons being lit to call for aid from Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On [12 March](../_/12_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md), on his way to Minas Tirith, camped his army of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) under Min-Rimmon.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

After the defeat of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), tidings of [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md)'s coronation were sent out to "*all parts of Gondor, from Min-Rimmon even to [Pinnath Gelin](../P/Pinnath_Gelin.md) and the far coasts of the sea*".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> This would suggest that there was a settlement of some kind at Min-Rimmon, and that it marked the western boundary of Gondorian settlement in Anórien by the end of the Third Age.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*[Min](Mindon.md)* means "peak" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), thus *Min-Rimmon* refers to the "Peak of the Rimmon".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup> "Rimmon" is a name of forgotten origin, coming from the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) before Gondor was established.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
