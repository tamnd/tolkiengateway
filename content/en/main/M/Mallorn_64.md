<b><i>Mallorn</i> 64</b> is the sixty-fourth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s annual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Winter [2023](../_/2023.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

<dl><dd><i>Notes marked with a § are from the roundtable &#34;Tolkien and Medieval Constructions of Race&#34; at the 58th International Conference on Medieval Studies at Western Michigan University.</i></dd></dl>

- **Editorial**
    - "In With the New, Preserving the Old" by Luke Shelton
- **Articles**
    - "By the Waters of Anduin We Lay Down and Wept: Tolkien’s *[Akallabêth](../A/Akallabêth.md)* and the Prophetic Imagination" by Tom Emanuel
    - "Back To The Past? Disentangling Elvish Time and Space Reference" by Maria Zielenbach
- **Notes**
    - "Participatory Middle-earth: An Ancient Resonance and the Ruling Ring" by Jason Monroe
    - "*Eruanno* in Tolkien’s *[Aia María](../A/Aia_María.md)*" by Michael Corso
    - "Tolkien’s Lost Lecture on *Hamlet*, Reconstructed" by Jessica Yates
    - "Concerning ‘[Concerning Racism and Tolkien](../A/Amon_Hen_299.md)’" by Clare Moore
    - "The [Squint-eyed Southerner](../S/Squint-eyed_Southerner~64042.md) as a Case Study of Orientalism in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* §" by Mercury Natis
    - "The Problem of White Academia §" by Robin Anne Reid
    - "Tolkien and the [Fallohides](../F/Fallohides.md) §" by Luke Shelton
    - "Notes on a Roundtable: On 'Tolkien and Medieval Constructions of Race' at ICMS 2023 §" by Mariana Rios Maldonado
- **Reviews**
    - <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=J.R.R._Tolkien%E2%80%99s_Utopianism_and_The_Classics&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="J.R.R.     - <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=A_Sense_of_Tales_Untold&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="A Sense of Tales Untold (page does not exist)">A Sense of Tales Untold</a></i> by Peter Grybauskas (reviewed by Rebecca Davis)
    - *[Tweaking Things a Little: Essays on the Epic Fantasy of J.R.R. Tolkien and George R.R. Martin](../T/Tweaking_Things_a_Little.md)* by [Thomas Honegger](../T/Thomas_Honegger.md) (reviewed by Kristine Larsen)
    - <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tolkien_Dogmatics&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien Dogmatics (page does not exist)">Tolkien Dogmatics: Theology through Mythlogy in Middle-earth</a></i> by Austin Freeman (reviewed by Nick Polk)
