**Milo Burrows** was a hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Milo was a grandson of [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck) of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland), who married into the [Baggins Family](/wiki/Baggins_Family) when he wedded [Peony](/wiki/Peony_Baggins "Peony Baggins"), and a nephew of both Bilbo and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins). They had four children - [Mosco](/wiki/Mosco_Burrows "Mosco Burrows"), [Moro](/wiki/Moro_Burrows "Moro Burrows"), [Myrtle](/wiki/Myrtle_Burrows "Myrtle Burrows") and [Minto](/wiki/Minto_Burrows "Minto Burrows") - and all of the family was present at Bilbo's famous [Birthday Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Milo was known for his habit of failing to reply to letters, and because of this he was one of those that found a specially selected gift waiting for him in [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End), after Bilbo's departure: a golden inkpot and pen.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Milo Burrows, voiced by an uncredited <a href="/wiki/Michael_Reisz" title="Michael Reisz">Michael Reisz</a>, lives at Number 4, <a href="/wiki/Bagshot_Row" title="Bagshot Row">Bagshot Row</a>. He expresses pleasure that Frodo is no longer associating with &#34;Elves and Wizards&#34;, causing Frodo to politely make his excuses and leave.</dd></dl>
