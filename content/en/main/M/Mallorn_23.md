<b><i>Mallorn</i> 23</b> is the twenty-third issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s annual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in the summer of [1986](../_/1986.md). The issue includes the poem *[Goblin Feet](../G/Goblin_Feet.md)* by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
