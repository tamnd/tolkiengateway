<b><i>Mallorn</i> 61</b> is the sixty-first issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s annual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Winter [2020](../_/2020.md).
