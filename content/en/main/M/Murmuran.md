**Murmuran** was the hall of [Lórien](/wiki/Irmo "Irmo") in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-cov_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-1">[1]</a></sup>

While many of the Valar had dwellings built in [Valmar](/wiki/Valmar), Lórien dwelt far away, in the south by the feet of the [Mountains of Valinor](/wiki/Mountains_of_Valinor) upon the confines of the realm. His hall was great, and dimly lit, and was built by [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) using mists gathered beyond [Arvalin](/wiki/Arvalin) upon the [Shadowy Seas](/wiki/Shadowy_Seas).<sup id="cite_ref-cov_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-1">[1]</a></sup>

From [Palúrien](/wiki/Pal%C3%BArien) Lórien had received a great wealth of [yewtrees](/wiki/Yewtrees "Yewtrees"), [cedars](/wiki/Cedars "Cedars"), and [pines](/wiki/Pines "Pines"). These formed wide gardens which wandered about his dwelling, winding near even to the feet of [Silpion](/wiki/Silpion). They were full of labyrinths and mazes, and the trees exuded drowsy odours in the dusk which hung over deep pools. [Glowworms](/wiki/Glow-worms "Glow-worms") crept about their borders and Varda had set stars within their depths for the pleasure of Lórien.<sup id="cite_ref-cov_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-1">[1]</a></sup>

Lórien's sprites sang within the gardens, and the scent of nightflowers and the songs of sleepy [nightingales](/wiki/Nightingale "Nightingale") filled them with great loveliness. Also there grew [fumellar](/wiki/Fumellar "Fumellar"), poppies which glowed redly in the dusk, which Lórien used in his enchantments.<sup id="cite_ref-cov_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-1">[1]</a></sup>

Amidmost was [Silindrin](/wiki/Silindrin), set within a ring of shadowy [cypress](/wiki/Cypress "Cypress") towering high. From there Lórien gazed and saw many visions of mystery pass across its face.<sup id="cite_ref-cov_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-1">[1]</a></sup>
