**Murmuran** was the hall of [Lórien](../I/Irmo.md) in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-cov_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-1">[1]</a></sup>

While many of the Valar had dwellings built in [Valmar](../V/Valmar.md), Lórien dwelt far away, in the south by the feet of the [Mountains of Valinor](Mountains_of_Valinor.md) upon the confines of the realm. His hall was great, and dimly lit, and was built by [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) using mists gathered beyond [Arvalin](../A/Arvalin.md) upon the [Shadowy Seas](../S/Shadowy_Seas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-cov_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-1">[1]</a></sup>

From [Palúrien](../P/Palúrien.md) Lórien had received a great wealth of [yewtrees](../Y/Yewtrees.md), [cedars](../C/Cedars.md), and [pines](../P/Pines.md). These formed wide gardens which wandered about his dwelling, winding near even to the feet of [Silpion](../S/Silpion.md). They were full of labyrinths and mazes, and the trees exuded drowsy odours in the dusk which hung over deep pools. [Glowworms](../G/Glow-worms.md) crept about their borders and Varda had set stars within their depths for the pleasure of Lórien.<sup id="cite_ref-cov_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-1">[1]</a></sup>

Lórien's sprites sang within the gardens, and the scent of nightflowers and the songs of sleepy [nightingales](../N/Nightingale.md) filled them with great loveliness. Also there grew [fumellar](../F/Fumellar.md), poppies which glowed redly in the dusk, which Lórien used in his enchantments.<sup id="cite_ref-cov_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-1">[1]</a></sup>

Amidmost was [Silindrin](../S/Silindrin.md), set within a ring of shadowy [cypress](../C/Cypress.md) towering high. From there Lórien gazed and saw many visions of mystery pass across its face.<sup id="cite_ref-cov_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-1">[1]</a></sup>
