**Mablon** the Ilkorin was an [Elf](/wiki/Elf) of the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days). He was noted for his self-sacrifice in the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears) when he challenged a host of [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) to save the remainders of his people.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
