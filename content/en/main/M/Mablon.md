**Mablon** the Ilkorin was an [Elf](../E/Elf.md) of the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md). He was noted for his self-sacrifice in the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](../B/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears.md) when he challenged a host of [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md) to save the remainders of his people.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
