<i><b>more</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "dark(ness)".<sup id="cite_ref-L297_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L297-1">[1]</a></sup>

*more* also perhaps has the same meaning in [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-HF_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HF-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is seen as *mori-* in compounds (see etymology below).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Mori&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mori (page does not exist)">mori</a></i> (the short final *-i* becoming *-e* in [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) stage)<sup id="cite_ref-HF_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HF-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) [MOR](MOR~34024.md).<sup id="cite_ref-L297_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L297-1">[1]</a></sup>
