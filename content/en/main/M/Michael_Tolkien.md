**Michael Hilary Reuel "Mick" Tolkien** ([22 October](/wiki/22_October) [1920](/wiki/1920) - [27 February](/wiki/27_February) [1984](/wiki/1984)) was the second son of [J.R.R.](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") and [Edith Tolkien](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien). Tolkien wrote the story *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)* as a way to console Michael over the loss of his toy dog; Michael appears in the story as **boy Two**.

In the biographical drama film *[Tolkien](</wiki/Tolkien_(film)> "Tolkien (film)")* ([2019](/wiki/2019)), he appears briefly, portrayed by actor Paul Gurcel Escudero.

## Biography {#Biography}

### Youth {#Youth}

Michael owned the puppet on which [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) was based. Like his father, Michael also had **[Atlantis-haunting](/wiki/Atlantis-haunting)** dreams while J.R.R. never mentioned his own to him.

#### *Roverandom* {#Roverandom}

In early September [1925](/wiki/1925), the Tolkien family (then [Ronald](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien"), [John](/wiki/John_Tolkien "John Tolkien"), Michael and a one-year-old [Christopher](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien "Christopher Tolkien")) went on holiday to the seaside resort of Filey in Yorkshire. Although Tolkien had holidayed there previously in [1922](/wiki/1922), Tolkien described Filey as "a very nasty little suburban seaside resort".<sup id="cite_ref-bio_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bio-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:146</sup> During the holiday, Michael lost his beloved black-and-white toy dog on the beach; although the family searched for it, the toy dog could not be found.

To console his son, Tolkien created the story of *Roverandom* to explain the adventures of the dog. Tolkien wrote the story down, based on his own oral version, in [1927](/wiki/1927) and also provided a number of illustrations which have since been published.<sup id="cite_ref-AI_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:77–83</sup> A few years later, Tolkien submitted *Roverandom* for publication to [George Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/George_Allen_%26_Unwin) in [1936](/wiki/1936) and although the book described by [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) as "well written and amusing" it was never considered for publication, perhaps as a result of a desire for a [sequel to *The Hobbit*](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Lord of the Rings").<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:xvii</sup>

Throughout the story, Michael is referred to as "boy Two" as he's Tolkien's second son. In the story, boy Two is brought home a toy dog which he later loses at the beach. He never forgets the dog and later meets Roverandom in the dreamland on the moon. At the end of the story, boy Two is reunited with Roverandom, who is now a real dog.<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:88</sup>

### Later life {#Later_life}

Michael and his family resided at Payables Farm, Woodcote. They hosted his father in late summer [1948](/wiki/1948). While the family was away on holiday, his father found the quiet conditions to complete *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

His grandson is [Royd Tolkien](/wiki/Royd_Tolkien), who had a cameo as a [Ranger](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien "Rangers of Ithilien") in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.
