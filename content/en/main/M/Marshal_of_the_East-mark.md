**Marshal of the [East-mark](../E/East-mark.md)** was a military rank of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md), signifying the commander of the armies of eastern [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). The title was created by the new King [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) after the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). The first Rider to hold the rank was King Eomer's lieutenant, [Elfhelm](../E/Elfhelm.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
