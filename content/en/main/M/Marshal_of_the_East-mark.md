**Marshal of the [East-mark](/wiki/East-mark)** was a military rank of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), signifying the commander of the armies of eastern [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). The title was created by the new King [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). The first Rider to hold the rank was King Eomer's lieutenant, [Elfhelm](/wiki/Elfhelm).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
