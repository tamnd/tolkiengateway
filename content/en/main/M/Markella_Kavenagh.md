**Markella Kavenagh** is an Australian actress. She has portrayed [Elanor "Nori" Brandyfoot](/wiki/Elanor_Brandyfoot "Elanor Brandyfoot") in the *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* series by [Amazon](/wiki/Amazon_Studios "Amazon Studios"). Originally, Kavenagh was cast as "Tyra",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but the character was later renamed to "Nori".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
