**Methedras** ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Last Peak") was the southernmost of "the last major peaks" of the [Misty Mountains](Misty_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Methedras_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Methedras-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Upon its eastern slopes sprang the [Entwash](../E/Entwash.md) River, which flowed into the western edge of [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md), growing about the base of the mountain.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

To the south, Methedras stood above [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) in the valley of [Nan Curunír](../N/Nan_Curunír.md). The sources of the river [Isen](../I/Isen.md) were at the southern end of the [Misty Mountains](Misty_Mountains.md), probably on the southern slopes of Methedras.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> On [3 March](../_/3_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> the [Ents](../E/Ents.md) diverted the waters of the Isen to flow into Isengard, flooding its pits and creating a lake about the tower.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Methedras* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name,<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-8">[8]</a></sup> which means "Last Peak"<sup id="cite_ref-Methedras_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Methedras-2">[2]</a></sup>, It is probably a combination of *[methed](Methed.md)* ("end") and *[ras](../R/Ras.md)* ("horn").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the draft version of "[The Riders of Rohan](../T/The_Riders_of_Rohan.md)", *Methedras* was named *Methen Amon*. This name would be changed to *Methendol* before the final name of *Methedras* was adopted.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<dl><dd>In addition to being prominently visible from within <a href="../I/Isengard.md" title="Isengard">Isengard</a>, the western slope of the mountain is explorable as the &#34;Tâl Methedras&#34; area, home to the &#34;Tûr Morva&#34; village of the <a href="../D/Dunlendings.md" title="Dunlendings">Dunlendings</a>.</dd></dl>
