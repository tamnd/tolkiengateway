**Methedras** ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Last Peak") was the southernmost of "the last major peaks" of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Methedras_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Methedras-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Upon its eastern slopes sprang the [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash) River, which flowed into the western edge of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest), growing about the base of the mountain.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

To the south, Methedras stood above [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) in the valley of [Nan Curunír](/wiki/Nan_Curun%C3%ADr). The sources of the river [Isen](/wiki/Isen) were at the southern end of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), probably on the southern slopes of Methedras.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> On [3 March](/wiki/3_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) diverted the waters of the Isen to flow into Isengard, flooding its pits and creating a lake about the tower.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Methedras* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name,<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-8">[8]</a></sup> which means "Last Peak"<sup id="cite_ref-Methedras_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Methedras-2">[2]</a></sup>, It is probably a combination of *[methed](/wiki/Methed "Methed")* ("end") and *[ras](/wiki/Ras "Ras")* ("horn").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the draft version of "[The Riders of Rohan](/wiki/The_Riders_of_Rohan)", *Methedras* was named *Methen Amon*. This name would be changed to *Methendol* before the final name of *Methedras* was adopted.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
