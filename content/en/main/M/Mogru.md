**Mogru** was a [Man](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md) of [Agar](../A/Agar.md) who held the position of Town-master during the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).

## History {#History}

Mogru was a "fat man with eyes like a lizard", who had much power among his kind.

As he received the ill news of approaching [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) ships from [Tal-Elmar](<../T/Tal-Elmar_(character).md>) and [Hazad Longbeard](../H/Hazad_Longbeard.md), he mocked them and refused to believe such story, for the Númenóreans had not arrived in that land during his lifetime. However, at their request, he walked with difficulty towards a hill from where he could see the [Great Sea](../B/Belegaer.md). By then the ships that Tal-Elmar had seen were too close to the hilly shore to be spotted. Due to the fact that he hated Tal-Elmar and his blunt way of speaking to him, without showing the respect he believed he was deserving, Mogru sent Tal-Elmar ahead to the ships, as a scout.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The fate of Mogru it is not known further.
