**Mogru** was a [Man](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Pre-Númenóreans") of [Agar](/wiki/Agar) who held the position of Town-master during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).

## History {#History}

Mogru was a "fat man with eyes like a lizard", who had much power among his kind.

As he received the ill news of approaching [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") ships from [Tal-Elmar](</wiki/Tal-Elmar_(character)> "Tal-Elmar (character)") and [Hazad Longbeard](/wiki/Hazad_Longbeard), he mocked them and refused to believe such story, for the Númenóreans had not arrived in that land during his lifetime. However, at their request, he walked with difficulty towards a hill from where he could see the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer"). By then the ships that Tal-Elmar had seen were too close to the hilly shore to be spotted. Due to the fact that he hated Tal-Elmar and his blunt way of speaking to him, without showing the respect he believed he was deserving, Mogru sent Tal-Elmar ahead to the ships, as a scout.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The fate of Mogru it is not known further.
