The **Mornan** was a dark valley in the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) where the spring of the source of the [Morthond](Morthond.md) was located.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Mornan was a dark and narrow valley that lay beneath the shadow of two high mountains in the [Ered Nimrais](../W/White_Mountains.md), being located directly to the south of [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mornan_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mornan-2">[2]</a></sup> Above the Mornan was a hidden gateway within some shadowed rocks which lead out from the [Gate of the Dead Men](../G/Gate_of_the_Dead_Men.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mornan_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mornan-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Passing_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-3">[3]</a></sup>

On [8 March](../_/8_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), and the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md) exited the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md) at the Mornan.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> They rode to the [Stone of Erech](../S/Stone_of_Erech.md) where Aragorn, as the heir of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md), called upon the [Oathbreakers](../O/Oathbreakers.md) to follow him and fulfill their pledge.<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md) has suggested that the name *Mornan* consists of *[mor-](Mor.md)* ("dark") + <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../N/Nan.md" title="Nan">nan</a></i> (valley).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
