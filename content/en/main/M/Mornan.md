The **Mornan** was a dark valley in the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) where the spring of the source of the [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond) was located.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Mornan was a dark and narrow valley that lay beneath the shadow of two high mountains in the [Ered Nimrais](/wiki/White_Mountains "White Mountains"), being located directly to the south of [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).<sup id="cite_ref-Mornan_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mornan-2">[2]</a></sup> Above the Mornan was a hidden gateway within some shadowed rocks which lead out from the [Gate of the Dead Men](/wiki/Gate_of_the_Dead_Men).<sup id="cite_ref-Mornan_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mornan-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Passing_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-3">[3]</a></sup>

On [8 March](/wiki/8_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), and the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) exited the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead) at the Mornan.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> They rode to the [Stone of Erech](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech) where Aragorn, as the heir of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur), called upon the [Oathbreakers](/wiki/Oathbreakers) to follow him and fulfill their pledge.<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) has suggested that the name *Mornan* consists of *[mor-](/wiki/Mor "Mor")* ("dark") + <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Nan" title="Nan">nan</a></i> (valley).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
