**Museum Replicas** is a company specialized in creating weapons, armor, jewelery and accessories. The company has a license to create merchandise for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
