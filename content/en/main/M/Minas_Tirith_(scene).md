**Minas Tirith** is the ninth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* and the eleventh scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>). It was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) journey to [Minas Tirith](Minas_Tirith.md) on [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md). They pass the [White Tree](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md) and the [Guards of the Citadel](../G/Guards_of_the_Citadel.md), and Pippin notices that it is dead. Gandalf tells Pippin that [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) is the [Steward](../S/Steward_of_Gondor.md), not the [King](../K/King_of_Gondor.md). He tells Pippin not to say anything at all, but Pippin cannot stay quiet and reveals that [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) gave his life to defend him and [Merry](Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md). Gandalf urges Denethor to prepare for war, but Denethor reveals his bitterness toward the heir to the throne of Gondor, Aragorn.
