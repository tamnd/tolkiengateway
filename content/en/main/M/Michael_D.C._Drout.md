**Michael D.C. Drout** (born [3 May](../_/3_May.md) [1968](../_/1968.md)) is a professor of English and Chair of the English Department at Wheaton College in Norton, Massachusetts.

Along with [Douglas A. Anderson](../D/Douglas_A._Anderson.md) and [Verlyn Flieger](../V/Verlyn_Flieger.md), Drout is a founding editor of *[Tolkien Studies: An Annual Scholarly Review](<../T/Tolkien_Studies_(journal).md>)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He is also a member of the Board of Academic Advisors for [Walking Tree Publishers](../W/Walking_Tree_Publishers.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
