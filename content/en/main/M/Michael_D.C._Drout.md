**Michael D.C. Drout** (born [3 May](/wiki/3_May) [1968](/wiki/1968)) is a professor of English and Chair of the English Department at Wheaton College in Norton, Massachusetts.

Along with [Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson) and [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger), Drout is a founding editor of *[Tolkien Studies: An Annual Scholarly Review](</wiki/Tolkien_Studies_(journal)> "Tolkien Studies (journal)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He is also a member of the Board of Academic Advisors for [Walking Tree Publishers](/wiki/Walking_Tree_Publishers).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
