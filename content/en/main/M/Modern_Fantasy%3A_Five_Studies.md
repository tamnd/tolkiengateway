<i><b>Modern Fantasy: Five Studies</b></i> is a 1975 study of works of fantasy, including essays on authors such as [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), [George MacDonald](/wiki/George_MacDonald), and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mervyn_Peake" title="wikipedia:Mervyn Peake">Mervyn Peake</a>. The author quotes from [a letter](/wiki/Letter_to_C.N._Manlove "Letter to C.N. Manlove") from Tolkien discussing *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* (p. 158).<sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>
