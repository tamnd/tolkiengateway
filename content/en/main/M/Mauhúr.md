**Mauhúr** was an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") in the service of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman).

## History {#History}

Mauhúr was sent to reinforce [Uglúk](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk)'s troop as they returned to [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) with [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"). He found them surrounded by [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer)'s [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) at the edge of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest), from which he led his Orcs in an attack. This attack was a failure: the Rohirrim were able to defeat the Orcs, even with Mauhúr's reinforcements. It is unknown whether Mauhúr and his soldiers were "*killed or driven off*" by the Rohirrim.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

<b><a href="/wiki/1997" title="1997">1997</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Mauhúr is depicted in the <a href="/wiki/1997" title="1997">1997</a> expansion <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_Against_the_Shadow" title="Middle-earth: Against the Shadow">Middle-earth: Against the Shadow</a></i> on the <i>Mauhûr</i> <small>[sic]</small> card illustrated by Omar Rayyan. He can be played as Unique Leader, and his home site is <a href="/wiki/Dol_Guldur" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a>. The card has the following effects:</dd></dl>

> Discard on a body check result of 9. +2 direct influence against Orcs and Orc factions.

<b><a href="/wiki/2002" title="2002">2002</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Mauhúr is portrayed as an <a href="/wiki/Uruk-hai" title="Uruk-hai">Uruk-hai</a> instead of an Orc, and is played by <a href="/wiki/Robbie_Magasiva" title="Robbie Magasiva">Robbie Magasiva</a> and voiced by <a href="/wiki/Andy_Serkis" title="Andy Serkis">Andy Serkis</a>. His remarks on the lack of food enticed <a href="/wiki/Snaga_(orc_of_Isengard)" title="Snaga (orc of Isengard)">Snaga</a>, who proposed eating the captives. Mauhúr was among the Uruks who devoured Snaga&#39;s corpse after the latter was beheaded for his limited discipline.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2012" title="2012">2012</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Mauhúr leads a group of Uruks that split off from <a href="/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk" title="Uglúk">Uglúk</a>&#39;s band and establish a camp called Kufuzg in the Norcrofts of <a href="/wiki/Rohan" title="Rohan">Rohan</a>, where he is found and defeated by the player.</dd></dl>
