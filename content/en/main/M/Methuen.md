**Methuen** is a British publishing company. Its Canadian branch has published several [Tolkien calendars](/wiki/Category:Calendars "Category:Calendars") in Canada. They differ little to none from the international editions by [George Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin "Allen and Unwin").

**Calendars published:**

- 1976: *[The Hobbit Calendar](/wiki/The_Hobbit_Calendar_1976 "The Hobbit Calendar 1976")*
- 1979: *[The Calendar of J.R.R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_1979_Calendar_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(Canada)> "The 1979 Calendar of J.R.R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings (Canada)")*
- 1979: *[The Silmarillion Desk Calendar](/wiki/The_Silmarillion_Desk_Calendar_1979 "The Silmarillion Desk Calendar 1979")*
- 1981: *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar](</wiki/The_1981_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar_-_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(Canada)> "The 1981 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar - The Lord of the Rings (Canada)")*
- 1984: *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar_1984 "The J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar 1984")*
- 1986: *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar](/wiki/The_1986_Tolkien_Calendar "The 1986 Tolkien Calendar")*
