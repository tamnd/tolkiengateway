**Moths** were nocturnal insects of a kind closely related to [butterflies](/wiki/Butterflies "Butterflies"). [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) saw them fluttering about at nightfall when they were trying to find the route to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Curiously, they are also mentioned as dwelling in [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood), where the moths were black or dark grey, and grew to a huge size.<sup id="cite_ref-H8_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H8-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s early conceptions of [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) languages, the words for "moth" are <i><b>malo</b></i> (pl. *malwi*) in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and <i><b>fufril</b></i> in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Tale of Tinúviel](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Tin%C3%BAviel)*, white moths flittered about the head of the dancing [Tinúviel](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Lúthien"), as the evening fell. It is told that Tinúviel "*being a [fairy](/wiki/Fairies "Fairies") minded them not as many of the children of [Men](/wiki/Men) do, although she loved not beetles, and [spiders](/wiki/Spiders "Spiders") will none of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) touch because of [Ungweliantë](/wiki/Ungoliant "Ungoliant")*".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In <a href="/wiki/Peter_Jackson" title="Peter Jackson">Peter Jackson</a>&#39;s movies, moths are somehow associated with the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Eagles" title="Eagles">Eagles</a>; while <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> is trapped on the summit of <a href="/wiki/Orthanc" title="Orthanc">Orthanc</a>, he catches a moth and whispers to it. Later, when confronted by <a href="/wiki/Saruman" title="Saruman">Saruman</a>, the moth reappears; an Eagle (supposedly <a href="/wiki/Gwaihir" title="Gwaihir">Gwaihir</a>) arrives and Gandalf escapes on its back.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Right before the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon" title="Battle of the Morannon">Battle of the Morannon</a>, Gandalf notices a moth flies near him. Then the Eagles appear and help the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Free_Peoples" title="Free Peoples">Free Peoples</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>As <a href="/wiki/Thorin_and_Company" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and Company</a> are trapped in a falling tree by the band of <a href="/wiki/Azog" title="Azog">Azog</a> and their <a href="/wiki/Wargs" title="Wargs">Wargs</a>, Gandalf uses a moth to summon the Eagles to their aid.</dd></dl>
