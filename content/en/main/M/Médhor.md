**Médhor** was a [Silvan elf](/wiki/Silvan_Elves "Silvan Elves") garrisoned at the [Watchtower of Ostirith](/wiki/Watchtower_of_Ostirith) during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Médhor was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) and is portrayed by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Augustus_Prew&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Augustus Prew (page does not exist)">Augustus Prew</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
