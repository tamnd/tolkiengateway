**Michael Alan ("Mike") Foster** (d. [April 12](/wiki/April_12), [2023](/wiki/2023)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) was a retired Professor in English at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois_Central_College" title="wikipedia:Illinois Central College">Illinois Central College</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He had been the North American representative of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) since 1995, and taught a course on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) at Illinois Central College from 1978 until 2005.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
