**Mithe Steps** was a landing-stage at the [Mithe](/wiki/Mithe), the outflow of the [Shirebourn](/wiki/Shirebourn) river. From there a lane ran to [Deephallow](/wiki/Deephallow) and so on to the [Causeway](/wiki/Causeway) road that went through [Rushey](/wiki/Rushey) and [Stock](/wiki/Stock).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) passed the Mithe, he found Mithe Steps empty and "none was there to greet him".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
