**Mithe Steps** was a landing-stage at the [Mithe](Mithe.md), the outflow of the [Shirebourn](../S/Shirebourn.md) river. From there a lane ran to [Deephallow](../D/Deephallow.md) and so on to the [Causeway](../C/Causeway.md) road that went through [Rushey](../R/Rushey.md) and [Stock](../S/Stock.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md) passed the Mithe, he found Mithe Steps empty and "none was there to greet him".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
