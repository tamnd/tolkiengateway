***Middle-earth Role Playing Combat Screen and Reference Sheets*** is a product for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*, 2nd Edition. It replaced the *[Combat Screen](/wiki/Combat_Screen)*.

## Cover/Jacket Text

{{Blockquote|In a role playing game, you assume the role of a character in a "living" novel. Middle-earth Role Playing (MERP) and its companion sourcebooks provide the framework for role playing in the greatest fantasy setting of all time... J.R.R. Tolkien's Middle-earth.

The Middle-earth Role Playing Combat Screen is a must for all MERP gamers! Included are all the MERP tables needed for battles, maneuvering, and action. All this on one 34" x 11" combat screen plus two 17" x 11" reference sheets that can double as screen extenders.}}
