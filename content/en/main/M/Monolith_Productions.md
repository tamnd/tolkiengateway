**Monolith Productions** is an American video game developer, a subsidiary of [Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment](../W/Warner_Bros._Interactive_Entertainment.md) (itself a subsidiary of [Warner Bros.](../W/Warner_Bros..md)) In [2012](../_/2012.md) Monolith released *[Guardians of Middle-earth](../G/Guardians_of_Middle-earth.md)*, while in 2014 they released the action game *[Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](Middle-earth%253A_Shadow_of_Mordor.md)* and in 2017 they released it's sequel, *[Middle-earth: Shadow of War](Middle-earth%253A_Shadow_of_War.md)*. In 2012, Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment merged **Snowblind Studios** into Monolith Productions, which had previously released *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md)*.
