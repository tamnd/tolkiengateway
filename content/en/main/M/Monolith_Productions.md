**Monolith Productions** is an American video game developer, a subsidiary of [Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment](/wiki/Warner_Bros._Interactive_Entertainment) (itself a subsidiary of [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.)) In [2012](/wiki/2012) Monolith released *[Guardians of Middle-earth](/wiki/Guardians_of_Middle-earth)*, while in 2014 they released the action game *[Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor)* and in 2017 they released it's sequel, *[Middle-earth: Shadow of War](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_War)*. In 2012, Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment merged **Snowblind Studios** into Monolith Productions, which had previously released *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North)*.
