The **Mountains of Angmar** were an extension of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) that lay to the west of [Mount Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad "Gundabad"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> home to the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) who ruled from his capital [Carn Dûm](/wiki/Carn_D%C3%BBm) on the western tip of the mountain range.

## History {#History}

The Witch-king came to the Mountains around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300") and established the realm of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> There he gathered [evil men](/wiki/Hill-men "Hill-men"), [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and other evil creatures.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-3">[3]</a></sup> The Witch-king was defeated at the [Battle of Fornost](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> along with all of Angmar's men and orcs west of the mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-3">[3]</a></sup> In [1977](/wiki/Third_Age_1977 "Third Age 1977") [Frumgar](/wiki/Frumgar) and the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> drove off Angmar's remnants east of the mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Orcs probably returned around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2480](/wiki/Third_Age_2480 "Third Age 2480") as they constructed secret strongholds along the Misty Mountain range<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> but were again reduced in numbers by the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> in [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>
