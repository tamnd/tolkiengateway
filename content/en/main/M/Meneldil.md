**Meneldil** ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3318](/wiki/Second_Age_3318 "Second Age 3318")<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> – [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [158](/wiki/Third_Age_158 "Third Age 158"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 281 years), the son of [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) and grandson of [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "High King of the Dúnedain") [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), was the third [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor) and the first to rule in his own name. He reigned 156 years, from [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2](/wiki/Third_Age_2 "Third Age 2") until his death.

## History {#History}

### Early life {#Early_life}

Meneldil was the last man born in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor)<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-3">[3]</a></sup> and was about one year old at the time of the [Downfall](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Downfall of Númenor"). He would have been carried to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) in his father's ship where [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) established a new realm, [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), under their joint leadership.

Meneldil was 111 years old when in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3429](/wiki/Second_Age_3429 "Second Age 3429") [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) sent his army to [attack and capture](/wiki/First_Fall_of_Minas_Ithil "First Fall of Minas Ithil") [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil). It is not known what role Meneldil may have played while his father defended [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) and held the hosts of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) at bay. With the arrival of Elendil and [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad)'s forces, the fortunes of the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) turned, but in the hard fighting Anárion died (in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3440](/wiki/Second_Age_3440 "Second Age 3440")), as did Elendil (in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3441](/wiki/Second_Age_3441 "Second Age 3441")).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Succession to Kingship {#Succession_to_Kingship}

With the deaths of Elendil and Anárion, [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) had no leader and the joint rule in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) was ended. [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) returned to Gondor, assumed the [Elendilmir](/wiki/Elendilmir) as Arnor's king and proclaimed his [lordship](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "High King of the Dúnedain") over all the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain), [North](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_the_North "Dúnedain of the North") and [South](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_the_South "Dúnedain of the South"). While most of Arnor's forces returned to [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) the new King remained in the South to restore order and define Gondor's boundaries. With Meneldil and a company of trusted friends, Isildur rode about the boundaries of Gondor and in Anórien created the [Tomb of Elendil](/wiki/Tomb_of_Elendil) upon the hill of [Eilenaer](/wiki/Halifirien "Halifirien"), thereafter called [Amon Anwar](/wiki/Halifirien "Halifirien").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

When he was ready, he committed the rule of Gondor to Meneldil and departed for [Imladris](/wiki/Rivendell "Rivendell") with his three oldest sons to consult with [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-3">[3]</a></sup> It is said that Meneldil was quite pleased to see his uncle and his sons leave for the North, hoping that they would remain there long.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Then, a month after their departure, Isildur and his three sons were slain in the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-3">[3]</a></sup> Arnor would eventually (in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [10](/wiki/Third_Age_10 "Third Age 10")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>) be ruled by Isildur's fourth son, [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(King_of_Arnor)> "Valandil (King of Arnor)"), but in the South Meneldil assumed full kingship, which was inherited by his descendants. Gondor would not be ruled by an heir of Isildur until the ascension of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn).

Not much is known about his reign other than that a Gondorian scholar wrote the *[Ondonóre Nómesseron Minaþurie](/wiki/Ondon%C3%B3re_N%C3%B3messeron_Mina%C3%BEurie)*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

He was succeeded by his son [Cemendur](</wiki/Cemendur_(King_of_Gondor)> "Cemendur (King of Gondor)").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Meneldil* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Astronomer".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> As Paul Strack explains, it literally means "Lover of the Heavens", being a compound of *[menel](/wiki/Menel "Menel")* ("the heavens") and *[-(n)dil](/wiki/-ndil "-ndil")* ("lover", "friend").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)*, in Section VII "[The Heirs of Elendil](/wiki/The_Heirs_of_Elendil)", [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) explained that there were three manuscripts in the textual history of this section. Manuscript A contained the information about the Southern Line of Gondor, and from its condition it appeared to be the first stage of composition. Manuscript B added the Northern Line, and began as a fair copy of A for the Southern line but became heavily emended. Manuscript B led to Manuscript C, a fine copy (but with later emendations), which led to a final typescript D. In the earlier Manuscripts A and B, Meneldil's date of birth was [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3299](/wiki/Second_Age_3299 "Second Age 3299") instead of [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3318](/wiki/Second_Age_3318 "Second Age 3318"). It was Isildur's son Kiryandil who was born in 3318 and thus was the last man born in Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>
