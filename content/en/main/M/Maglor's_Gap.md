**Maglor's Gap** was the open area between the hills surrounding [Himring](/wiki/Himring) and an extension of the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) just north of [Mount Rerir](/wiki/Rerir "Rerir"). It was the widest break in the northern mountain-fences of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Due to the open terrain it was through this gap that [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) entered East [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) before the [Dagor Aglareb](/wiki/Dagor_Aglareb). In response the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) kept cavalry in the nearby plains, [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir) fortified the mountains to the east of the gap (establishing his base beside Lake [Helevorn](/wiki/Helevorn)), and [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) constructed his stronghold on the Hill of [Himring](/wiki/Himring) to the west.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Most of these preparations came to naught during the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach). The Orcs overwhelmed the Elves' cavalry upon the [Lothlann](/wiki/Lothlann) and the dragon [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) passed through Maglor's Gap to destroy all of the land between the arms of [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion). The fortresses upon Rerir's western slopes were destroyed and [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion) was ravaged. Only Himring was successfully defended, to which Maglor himself fled along with most survivors of the battle in that region.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
