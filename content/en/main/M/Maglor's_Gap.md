**Maglor's Gap** was the open area between the hills surrounding [Himring](../H/Himring.md) and an extension of the [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md) just north of [Mount Rerir](../R/Rerir.md). It was the widest break in the northern mountain-fences of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Due to the open terrain it was through this gap that [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) entered East [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) before the [Dagor Aglareb](../D/Dagor_Aglareb.md). In response the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) kept cavalry in the nearby plains, [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md) fortified the mountains to the east of the gap (establishing his base beside Lake [Helevorn](../H/Helevorn.md)), and [Maedhros](Maedhros.md) constructed his stronghold on the Hill of [Himring](../H/Himring.md) to the west.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Most of these preparations came to naught during the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md). The Orcs overwhelmed the Elves' cavalry upon the [Lothlann](../L/Lothlann.md) and the dragon [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) passed through Maglor's Gap to destroy all of the land between the arms of [Gelion](../G/Gelion.md). The fortresses upon Rerir's western slopes were destroyed and [Thargelion](../T/Thargelion.md) was ravaged. Only Himring was successfully defended, to which Maglor himself fled along with most survivors of the battle in that region.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
