**Massacre at the Fords of Isen** is the seventh scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)"). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The [Riders of Rohan](/wiki/Riders_of_Rohan) come upon the bodies of men of Rohan and horses at the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen). Éomer instructs them to search for [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred), the son of King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden). He recognises the [White Hand](/wiki/White_Hand) of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) painted on the armour of the orcs. They find Théodred badly wounded, but alive, and bring him to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).

## Differences {#Differences}

It is told in [Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales) that Théodred was found near the brink of death by [Elfhelm](/wiki/Elfhelm) and [Grimbold](/wiki/Grimbold) rather than Éomer as happens in this scene. However, Théodred died at the Fords of Isen after asking the two men to "Keep the Fords until Éomer comes".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
