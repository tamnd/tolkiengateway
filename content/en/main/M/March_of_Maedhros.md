The **March of Maedhros** was a region in the northeastern borderlands of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), between [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) and [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md) and south of [Lothlann](../L/Lothlann.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> It was guarded by the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) under the general captaincy of [Maedhros](Maedhros.md). The chief Stronghold of the March of Maedhros was [Himring](../H/Himring.md), a fortress built by the [Sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) after the [Kinslaying](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

The area consisted of a series hills and plains. The hills, most notable in the eastern areas, were surrounding the Hill of [Himring](../H/Himring.md), the capital fortress.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It was fortified by Maedhros after he moved his brothers and their people further east out of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) to avoid any feuds with their kinsmen. He established himself on Himring. It was frequently attacked by the forces of Angband throughout the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) in the Battles of Beleriand, generally because it lacked in natural fortification, until it was finally overrun during the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
