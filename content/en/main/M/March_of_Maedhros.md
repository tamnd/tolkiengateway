The **March of Maedhros** was a region in the northeastern borderlands of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), between [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) and [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) and south of [Lothlann](/wiki/Lothlann).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> It was guarded by the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) under the general captaincy of [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros). The chief Stronghold of the March of Maedhros was [Himring](/wiki/Himring), a fortress built by the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) after the [Kinslaying](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB "Kinslaying at Alqualondë").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

The area consisted of a series hills and plains. The hills, most notable in the eastern areas, were surrounding the Hill of [Himring](/wiki/Himring), the capital fortress.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It was fortified by Maedhros after he moved his brothers and their people further east out of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) to avoid any feuds with their kinsmen. He established himself on Himring. It was frequently attacked by the forces of Angband throughout the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) in the Battles of Beleriand, generally because it lacked in natural fortification, until it was finally overrun during the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
