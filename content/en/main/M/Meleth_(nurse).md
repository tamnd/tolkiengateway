**Meleth** was the nurse of [Eärendel](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendel), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. She took care of him during the first years of his life in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin). She also told him tales about [Melko](/wiki/Melko), which made him cry when the lights of the enemy appeared in his chamber during the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is unknown if she survived with the [exiles](/wiki/Exiles_of_Gondolin "Exiles of Gondolin").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*meleth* is [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) for "love".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
