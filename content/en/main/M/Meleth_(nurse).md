**Meleth** was the nurse of [Eärendel](../E/Eärendel.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. She took care of him during the first years of his life in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). She also told him tales about [Melko](Melko.md), which made him cry when the lights of the enemy appeared in his chamber during the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is unknown if she survived with the [exiles](../E/Exiles_of_Gondolin.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*meleth* is [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) for "love".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
