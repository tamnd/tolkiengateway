<i><b>methed</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "end".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT48_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT48-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) has suggested that *methed* appears to be related to the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) adjective *methen* ("end, final"), and might therefore be derived from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") MET ("end").<sup id="cite_ref-VT48_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT48-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Methed-en-glad](/wiki/Methed-en-glad)* ("the End of the Wood")
- *[Methedras](/wiki/Methedras)* ("last peak")
