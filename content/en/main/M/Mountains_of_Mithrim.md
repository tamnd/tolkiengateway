The **Mountains of Mithrim** or the **Hills of Mithrim** were a range of mountains located in [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> They formed the border between [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim) and [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and thus between the realms of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and his son [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) after the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) settled in Hithlum. The caves of [Androth](/wiki/Androth), where some of the [Grey-elves of Mithrim](/wiki/Elves_of_Mithrim "Elves of Mithrim") dwelt after [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> were probably in the Mountains of Mithrim.
