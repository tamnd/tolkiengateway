**Mat Heathertoes** (died [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")) was a [Man of Bree](/wiki/Bree-men "Bree-men").

## History {#History}

With the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) raging in the South-east, many refugees came up the [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway) to [Bree](/wiki/Bree). Not all these refugees came with the same intentions: a group of [ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians") was ousted from the town near the end of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"). With the help of [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny) and [Harry Goatleaf](/wiki/Harry_Goatleaf), they returned soon after New Year, and a fight ensued. The number of casualties on the side of the ruffians was not recorded, but five of the Bree-folk, three Men and two Hobbits, were killed. Mat Heathertoes was one of them.<sup id="cite_ref-Bound_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bound-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Mat* was a name employed by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") to translate the common (but untranslated) Hobbit name **Matta**.<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-2">[2]</a></sup> The name **Heathertoes** was, like all surnames of the Men of Bree, a plant name,<sup id="cite_ref-Pony_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pony-3">[3]</a></sup> though this seems to have escaped Tolkien when he wrote [a list for translators](/wiki/Guide_to_the_Names_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "Guide to the Names in The Lord of the Rings"). In it he said *Heathertoes* was a Hobbit name, and was given in joke to the unshod Hobbits. While walking, they would gather twigs and moss between their toes.<sup id="cite_ref-nomen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nomen-4">[4]</a></sup> However, Mat was most certainly a Man: given that two of the five were Hobbits,<sup id="cite_ref-Bound_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bound-1">[1]</a></sup> and the last two named victims - [Willie Banks](/wiki/Willie_Banks) and an [Underhill](/wiki/Underhill_Family "Underhill Family") were both positively identified as Hobbits as well.<sup id="cite_ref-Pony_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pony-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-nomen_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nomen-4">[4]</a></sup>
