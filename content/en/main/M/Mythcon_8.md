**Mythcon 8** was the eighth annual [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) conference.

- **Theme:** The "Lesser" Works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)
- **Guest of Honor:** [Richard L. Purtill](/wiki/Richard_L._Purtill) (Inklings scholar and fantasy author)
- **Chairman:** Bruce McMenomy
- **Coordinators:** Andy Howard and Sandi Howard
