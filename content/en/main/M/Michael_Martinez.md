**Michael Martinez** (born [1959](../_/1959.md)) is one of the most well-known [scholars](../T/Tolkienist.md) in regards to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s literary works. He first published *[Parma Endorion](../P/Parma_Endorion.md)* in [1996](../_/1996.md) and has published a vast array of essays over the years. Most of these can be read online via his <a class="external text" href="http://www.merp.com/essays/michael-martinez-1" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">section on MERP.com</a>.
