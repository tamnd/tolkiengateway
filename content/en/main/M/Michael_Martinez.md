**Michael Martinez** (born [1959](/wiki/1959)) is one of the most well-known [scholars](/wiki/Tolkienist "Tolkienist") in regards to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s literary works. He first published *[Parma Endorion](/wiki/Parma_Endorion)* in [1996](/wiki/1996) and has published a vast array of essays over the years. Most of these can be read online via his <a class="external text" href="http://www.merp.com/essays/michael-martinez-1" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">section on MERP.com</a>.
