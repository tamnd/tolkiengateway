<b><i>Mallorn</i> 3</b> is the third issue of the [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Society.md)'s journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in 1971.

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Editorial**
    - Editorial
        - By Steven Thomson
    - Letters to the Editor
        - By Archie Mercer, Dave Weldrake, John Abbott, J.D. Collins, David Pearson, Rosemary Pardoe, Audrey Walton, Hartley Patterson, James Ead, and A.R. Fallone
    - Belladonna's Postbag
        - By Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md)), J.D. Collins, Michael Scott Rohan, James Ead, and Steve Thomson
- **Notes**
    - Some Comparisons
        - By J.D. Collins
    - J.R.R. Tolkien: A Bibliography
        - By John B. Abbott
    - Honey Beer (beer recipe)
        - Ian Shaw
    - The Religion of a Hobbit
        - By Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md))
    - Hippy vs. Hobbit
        - By Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md))
    - Some Thoughts on Hippies and Hobbits
        - By Bob Borsley
- **Creative**
    - Wyngéar (poem)
        - By James Ead
    - Wise Men on Jersey (joke)
        - By Graham England
- **Illustrations**
    - By Brian Talbot and John B. Abbott
