**Mark T. Hooker** is a visiting scholar at Indiana University's Russian and East European Institute (REEI). He is a member of the Board of Academic Advisors for [Walking Tree Publishers](/wiki/Walking_Tree_Publishers),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and in 2012 won [The Fifth Beyond Bree Award](/wiki/Beyond_Bree#Beyond_Bree_Awards "Beyond Bree").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
