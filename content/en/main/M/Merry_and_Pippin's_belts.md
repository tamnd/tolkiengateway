**Merry and Pippin's belts** were the [gifts](/wiki/Gifts_of_Galadriel "Gifts of Galadriel") [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") received from [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel).

Before the [Company of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") left [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), each of its members was presented with a gift by [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel). [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") were given "small silver belts, each with a clasp wrought like a golden flower".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
