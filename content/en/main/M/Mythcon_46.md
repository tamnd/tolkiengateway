**Mythcon 46** was the forty-sixth annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme:** "The Arthurian Mythos"
- **Guests of Honor:** Jo Walton (fantasy author), and [John D. Rateliff](../J/John_D._Rateliff.md) (Inklings and Tolkien scholar)
