**Mythcon 46** was the forty-sixth annual [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) conference.

- **Theme:** "The Arthurian Mythos"
- **Guests of Honor:** Jo Walton (fantasy author), and [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff) (Inklings and Tolkien scholar)
