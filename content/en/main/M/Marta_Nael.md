**Marta Nael** is a Spanish illustrator. As a [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering) artist, she created art for the [Tales of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth "The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth") and [The Hobbit](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit "Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit") expansions.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-plag1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-plag1-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-plag2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-plag2-3">[3]</a></sup>

In May [2026](/wiki/2026), an illustration of the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring) created by [Dan Frazier](/wiki/Dan_Frazier) for The Hobbit expansion was found to have been plagiarized from an earlier illustration by Nael, which was later confirmed by both the artist and [Wizards of the Coast](/wiki/Wizards_of_the_Coast).<sup id="cite_ref-plag1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-plag1-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-plag2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-plag2-3">[3]</a></sup>
