**Marta Nael** is a Spanish illustrator. As a [Magic: The Gathering](Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md) artist, she created art for the [Tales of Middle-earth](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Tales_of_Middle-earth.md) and [The Hobbit](Magic%253A_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit.md) expansions.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-plag1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-plag1-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-plag2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-plag2-3">[3]</a></sup>

In May [2026](../_/2026.md), an illustration of the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md) created by [Dan Frazier](../D/Dan_Frazier.md) for The Hobbit expansion was found to have been plagiarized from an earlier illustration by Nael, which was later confirmed by both the artist and [Wizards of the Coast](../W/Wizards_of_the_Coast.md).<sup id="cite_ref-plag1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-plag1-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-plag2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-plag2-3">[3]</a></sup>
