**Morgul** is an unofficial version of [The Dungeons of Moria](</wiki/The_Dungeons_of_Moria_(video_game)> "The Dungeons of Moria (video game)").<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FAQ_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FAQ-3">[3]</a></sup> Unlike the official versions it takes place in [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul), not in [Moria](/wiki/Moria).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> Your objective is to kill the nine [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl), and so delay the attack on [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FAQ_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FAQ-3">[3]</a></sup> There are [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) and [Half-elves](/wiki/Half-elves) that have shops in [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FAQ_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FAQ-3">[3]</a></sup>
