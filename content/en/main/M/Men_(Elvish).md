## Quenya {#Quenya}

<i><b>men</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "place, spot",<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> or "way".<sup id="cite_ref-App_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), Quenya *men* ("place, spot") derives from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [MEN](/wiki/MEN#Other_versions).<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- *men* ("way"): *[Númen](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen)*/*[Formen](/wiki/Formen)*/*[Rómen](/wiki/R%C3%B3men)*/*[Hyarmen](/wiki/Hyarmen)*<sup id="cite_ref-App_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-2">[2]</a></sup>

### See also {#See_also}

- *[mene](/wiki/Mene "Mene")* (aorist) "goes"

## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>men</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "way, road",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or "us".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples_2}

- *men* ("way, road"): *[Men-i-Naugrim](/wiki/Men-i-Naugrim)*
- *men* ("us"): *[ammen](/wiki/Ammen "Ammen")* ("for us")

## Elvish {#Elvish}

<i><b>men</b></i> is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) (no language given)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup> word meaning "direction, region".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

### Derivatives {#Derivatives}
