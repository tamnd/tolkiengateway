The **Morgai** was a mountain range that ran parallel to the northern [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) on its eastern side.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The Morgai rose on the eastern side of a valley formed by it and the Ephel Dúath to its west. Its ridge line was much lower than that of the Ephel Dúath, and was notched and jagged with fang-like crags.<sup id="cite_ref-Tower899_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower899-2">[2]</a></sup> The valley sloped up gently northward with a stream bed at its floor fed by the two mountain ranges. The stream bed was dry when [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) and [Sam](/wiki/Sam) descended into the valley on their [Quest for Mount Doom](/wiki/Quest_for_Mount_Doom). A track left the [Morgul-road](/wiki/Morgul-road) at the western bridge-end and went down by a long stair to the bottom of the valley. The track went north to lesser posts and strongholds between the Tower of Cirith Ungol and the [Isenmouthe](/wiki/Isenmouthe).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Further north of the point where Frodo and Sam had descended into the valley a streamlet flowed from a gully on the western side. Water also trickled down a source higher up in the valley side and fed there dark pools in the river-bed.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow921_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow921-4">[4]</a></sup>

The Morgai formed the inner wall of the fences of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-Tower899_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower899-2">[2]</a></sup> The road that came over the [Morgul Pass](/wiki/Morgul_Pass) was joined by a road that came winding down from the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol).<sup id="cite_ref-Tower899_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower899-2">[2]</a></sup> A short way after the meeting of those two roads and following a steep incline, the road crossed a bridge of stone over the trough between the Ephel Dúath and the Morgai.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The road then went on through a jagged rift in the Morgai out into the valley of [Gorgoroth](/wiki/Gorgoroth) to [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr).<sup id="cite_ref-Tower899_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower899-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Biology {#Biology}

In the glens of the Morgai grew low scrubby trees, coarse grey grass-tussocks, withered mosses, brambles, some with long thorns or hooked barbs with maggot-ridden buds and briar-thickets. Midges and dun, grey or black flies with an eye-shaped blotch lived in the Morgai. The tops of the Morgai were without vegetation.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow921_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow921-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Morgai* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which means "Black Fence".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-7">[7]</a></sup> It is a combination of *[morn](/wiki/Morn "Morn")* ("black")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-7">[7]</a></sup> and the [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") form of<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> *cai* ("hedge").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
