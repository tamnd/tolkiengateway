**Mighty of the West** was a title used once, in the quotation given above, to refer to [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) the son of [Huor](../H/Huor.md). Its exact meaning is ambiguous: it appears in a passage that discusses Tuor's relationship with his descendants, the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md), and so we have treated it here as an epithet for that division of Mankind.

It might conceivably, though, refer to the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), though it seems unlikely that such a comparison would be made of a mortal Man.
