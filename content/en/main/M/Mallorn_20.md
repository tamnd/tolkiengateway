<b><i>Mallorn</i> 20</b> is the twentieth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s annual journal *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")*, published in September [1983](/wiki/1983).

## Contents {#Contents}

- Cover Illustration
    - By: [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes)
- Editorial
    - By: Jenny Curtis
- Before Puck - the Púkel-men and the *pūca*
    - By: [J.S. Ryan](/wiki/John_Ryan "John Ryan")
- Lay in Reply to the Half-elven's Song
    - By: Stephen Allcroft
- Book Review: *[Finn and Hengest](/wiki/Finn_and_Hengest)*
    - By: Kathleen Herbert
- Art: 'Gandalf meets Radagast'
    - By: Dave Cooper
- The Geography and Economy of Númenor
    - By: Ted Crawford
- *A Doom of Iron*
    - By: Michael Burgess
- Book Review: *[The Monsters and the Critics and other essays](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics "The Monsters and the Critics")*
    - By: [Charles Noad](/wiki/Charles_Noad)
- 'The Tale of Beren and Lúthien'
    - By: Iwan Rhys Morus
- Quenta Findagillo
    - By: [Brin Dunsire](/wiki/Brin_Dunsire)
- *[Mr. Bliss](/wiki/Mr._Bliss)*: The Precursor of a Precursor?
    - By: [Nils Ivar Agøy](/wiki/Nils_Ivar_Ag%C3%B8y)
- Book Review: *[The Road to Middle-Earth](/wiki/The_Road_to_Middle-Earth)*
    - By: Jessica Yates
- *Aragorn's Hymn to Caras Galadhon*
    - By: Steven Quayle
- Guidelines for *Mallorn* contributors
- Where to Write
- [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)

- Inside Art
    - By: Steve Lines, Timo Raittila, Dave Cooper, Tony Curtis
