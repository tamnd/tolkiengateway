The **Mereth Aderthad** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") "Feast of Reuniting") was the great celebratory feast held by [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor "High King of the Noldor") [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) near the [Pools of Ivrin](/wiki/Pools_of_Ivrin) soon after the [Return of the Noldor](/wiki/Return_of_the_Noldor) to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Twenty years after](/wiki/First_Age_20 "First Age 20") the first rising of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> the High King Fingolfin held a great celebratory feast during the spring, close by to the Pools of Ivrin under the [Mountains of Shadow](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin "Ered Wethrin") to the north of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). Its purpose was to unite all of the divided [Elves](/wiki/Elves) in one cause, and ambassadors came from all the peoples and realms of Beleriand. From the eastern [March](/wiki/East_Beleriand "East Beleriand") came warriors lead by [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) and [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor), though none of their [brothers](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor") came. As well as Elves from the separate branches of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), native [Grey-elves](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") were also present, including wanderers of the woods and the [folk](/wiki/Falathrim "Falathrim") of the [Havens](/wiki/Falas "Falas") along with [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan). Ambassadors even came from the [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves), the people of distant [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand) in the east. Yet, from [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), only [Mablung](/wiki/Mablung) and [Daeron](/wiki/Daeron) came with tidings from their [King](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol").<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup>

The feast was hailed as a great success, and the Elves made common cause there against their great enemy to the north. For a few short years after the Mereth Aderthad, Beleriand enjoyed a time of peace and happiness before plunging into the [Wars](/wiki/War_of_the_Great_Jewels "War of the Great Jewels") that would eventually destroy it, though the joy at the feast would be remembered for many years after.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Mereth Aderthad* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Feast of Reuniting", from the word <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mereth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mereth (page does not exist)">mereth</a></i> ("feast"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> and possibly the word <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Aderthad&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aderthad (page does not exist)">aderthad</a></i>.

In the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) phase of the [language](/wiki/Languages "Languages"), *Mereth Aderthad* is glossed as "Feast of Games and Reuniting".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
