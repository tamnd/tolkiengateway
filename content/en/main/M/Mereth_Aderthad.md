The **Mereth Aderthad** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) "Feast of Reuniting") was the great celebratory feast held by [High King](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) near the [Pools of Ivrin](../P/Pools_of_Ivrin.md) soon after the [Return of the Noldor](../R/Return_of_the_Noldor.md) to [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Twenty years after](../F/First_Age_20.md) the first rising of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> the High King Fingolfin held a great celebratory feast during the spring, close by to the Pools of Ivrin under the [Mountains of Shadow](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) to the north of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). Its purpose was to unite all of the divided [Elves](../E/Elves.md) in one cause, and ambassadors came from all the peoples and realms of Beleriand. From the eastern [March](../E/East_Beleriand.md) came warriors lead by [Maedhros](Maedhros.md) and [Maglor](Maglor.md), though none of their [brothers](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) came. As well as Elves from the separate branches of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), native [Grey-elves](../S/Sindar.md) were also present, including wanderers of the woods and the [folk](../F/Falathrim.md) of the [Havens](../F/Falas.md) along with [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md). Ambassadors even came from the [Green-elves](../G/Green-elves.md), the people of distant [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md) in the east. Yet, from [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), only [Mablung](Mablung.md) and [Daeron](../D/Daeron.md) came with tidings from their [King](../T/Thingol.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup>

The feast was hailed as a great success, and the Elves made common cause there against their great enemy to the north. For a few short years after the Mereth Aderthad, Beleriand enjoyed a time of peace and happiness before plunging into the [Wars](../W/War_of_the_Great_Jewels.md) that would eventually destroy it, though the joy at the feast would be remembered for many years after.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Mereth Aderthad* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Feast of Reuniting", from the word <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Mereth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mereth (page does not exist)">mereth</a></i> ("feast"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> and possibly the word <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Aderthad&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aderthad (page does not exist)">aderthad</a></i>.

In the [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) phase of the [language](../L/Languages.md), *Mereth Aderthad* is glossed as "Feast of Games and Reuniting".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
