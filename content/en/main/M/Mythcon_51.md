**Mythcon 51** was the fifty-first annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference. No Mythcon was held in 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic and Mythcon 51 was the first entirely virtual Mython conference.

- **Theme:** "A Virtual "Halfling" Mythcon"
