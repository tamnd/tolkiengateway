<b><i>Mallorn</i> 52</b> is the fifty-first issue of the [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s biannual journal *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")*, published in Autumn [2011](/wiki/2011).

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Cover Art**
    - *A Conversation with Smaug*
        - By: [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith)
- **Editorial**
    - "Consider the context"
        - By: [Nancy Martsch](/wiki/Nancy_Martsch)
- **Letters**
    - "An idea takes root"
        - By: Zak Cramer
- **Reviews**
    - "A doom of diamonds": *The King of Diamonds* by [Simon Tolkien](/wiki/Simon_Tolkien)
        - Reviewed by Mike Foster
    - "The Welsh connection": *[Tolkien and Wales: Language, Literature and Identity](/wiki/Tolkien_and_Wales "Tolkien and Wales")* by Carl Phelpstead
        - Reviewed by Troels Forchhammer
    - "From Middle Ages to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth)": *[The Keys of Middle-Earth: Discovering Medieval Literature through the Fiction of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Keys_of_Middle-earth "The Keys of Middle-earth")* by Stuart D. Lee and Elizabeth Solopova
        - Reviewed by Kastytis Zubovas
    - "The green revolution": *[Ents, Elves, And Eriador: The Environmental Vision of J. R. R. Tolkien](/wiki/Ents,_Elves,_and_Eriador "Ents, Elves, and Eriador")* by [Matthew Dickerson](/wiki/Matthew_Dickerson) and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Jonathan_Evans&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Jonathan Evans (page does not exist)">Jonathan Evans</a>
        - Reviewed by Kusumita P. Pedersen
    - "The lion, the witch and the baffled producers": <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chronicles_of_Narnia:_The_Voyage_of_the_Dawn_Treader" title="wikipedia:The Chronicles of Narnia: The Voyage of the Dawn Treader">The Chronicles of Narnia: The Voyage of the Dawn Treader</a></i>
        - Reviewed by [Chad Chisholm](/wiki/Chad_Chisholm)
    - "A dash of movie magic": <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter_and_the_Deathly_Hallows_%E2%80%93_Part_2" title="wikipedia:Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2">Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows Part 2</a></i>
        - Reviewed by [Henry Gee](/wiki/Henry_Gee)
    - "A trip to Iceland": *Where the Shadows Lie* by Michael Ridpath
        - Reviewed by [Pat Reynolds](/wiki/Patricia_Reynolds "Patricia Reynolds")
- **Commentary**
    - "Casting away treasurers: [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s use of <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Pearl" title="Pearl">Pearl</a></i> in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*"
        - By: Leigh Smith
    - "A woman of valour: [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) in *War and Peace*"
        - By: Catherine Madsen
    - "We hatesses those tricksy numbers: Tolkien, [Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis "C.S. Lewis") and maths anxiety"
        - By: [Kristine Larsen](/wiki/Kristine_Larsen)
    - "Inscriptions and insertions in a first edition of *The Lord of the Rings*"
        - By: James Blake
    - "[Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and Tolkien's treatment of evil"
        - By: David Tneh
- **Fiction**
    - "Werewolf Hollow"
        - By: Mark L. Ridge
- **Well, I'm back**
    - "Did Tolkien support the Canaries"
        - By: Henry Gee
- **Inside Art**
    - By: Lorenzo Daniele, Henry Gee, John Gilbey, [Jef Murray](/wiki/Jef_Murray), Ted Nasmith, Rick J. Ritchie, Colin Sullivan, Sue Wookey
