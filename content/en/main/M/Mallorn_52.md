<b><i>Mallorn</i> 52</b> is the fifty-first issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s biannual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Autumn [2011](../_/2011.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Cover Art**
    - *A Conversation with Smaug*
        - By: [Ted Nasmith](../T/Ted_Nasmith.md)
- **Editorial**
    - "Consider the context"
        - By: [Nancy Martsch](../N/Nancy_Martsch.md)
- **Letters**
    - "An idea takes root"
        - By: Zak Cramer
- **Reviews**
    - "A doom of diamonds": *The King of Diamonds* by [Simon Tolkien](../S/Simon_Tolkien.md)
        - Reviewed by Mike Foster
    - "The Welsh connection": *[Tolkien and Wales: Language, Literature and Identity](../T/Tolkien_and_Wales.md)* by Carl Phelpstead
        - Reviewed by Troels Forchhammer
    - "From Middle Ages to [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md)": *[The Keys of Middle-Earth: Discovering Medieval Literature through the Fiction of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Keys_of_Middle-earth~15408.md)* by Stuart D. Lee and Elizabeth Solopova
        - Reviewed by Kastytis Zubovas
    - "The green revolution": *[Ents, Elves, And Eriador: The Environmental Vision of J. R. R. Tolkien](../E/Ents,_Elves,_and_Eriador.md)* by [Matthew Dickerson](Matthew_Dickerson.md) and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Jonathan_Evans&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Jonathan Evans (page does not exist)">Jonathan Evans</a>
        - Reviewed by Kusumita P. Pedersen
    - "The lion, the witch and the baffled producers": <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chronicles_of_Narnia:_The_Voyage_of_the_Dawn_Treader" title="wikipedia:The Chronicles of Narnia: The Voyage of the Dawn Treader">The Chronicles of Narnia: The Voyage of the Dawn Treader</a></i>
        - Reviewed by [Chad Chisholm](../C/Chad_Chisholm.md)
    - "A dash of movie magic": <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter_and_the_Deathly_Hallows_%E2%80%93_Part_2" title="wikipedia:Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2">Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows Part 2</a></i>
        - Reviewed by [Henry Gee](../H/Henry_Gee.md)
    - "A trip to Iceland": *Where the Shadows Lie* by Michael Ridpath
        - Reviewed by [Pat Reynolds](../P/Patricia_Reynolds.md)
- **Commentary**
    - "Casting away treasurers: [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s use of <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../P/Pearl.md" title="Pearl">Pearl</a></i> in *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*"
        - By: Leigh Smith
    - "A woman of valour: [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) in *War and Peace*"
        - By: Catherine Madsen
    - "We hatesses those tricksy numbers: Tolkien, [Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) and maths anxiety"
        - By: [Kristine Larsen](../K/Kristine_Larsen.md)
    - "Inscriptions and insertions in a first edition of *The Lord of the Rings*"
        - By: James Blake
    - "[Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and Tolkien's treatment of evil"
        - By: David Tneh
- **Fiction**
    - "Werewolf Hollow"
        - By: Mark L. Ridge
- **Well, I'm back**
    - "Did Tolkien support the Canaries"
        - By: Henry Gee
- **Inside Art**
    - By: Lorenzo Daniele, Henry Gee, John Gilbey, [Jef Murray](../J/Jef_Murray.md), Ted Nasmith, Rick J. Ritchie, Colin Sullivan, Sue Wookey
