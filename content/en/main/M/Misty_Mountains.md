The **Misty Mountains**, **Mountains of Mist** or **Towers of Mist**<sup id="cite_ref-s3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s3-1">[1]</a></sup> (<i><b>Hithaeglir</b></i> in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> as a plural; translated also **Line of Misty Peaks**<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>) was a great mountain range that lay between [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) in the west and the Great River [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) in the east.

## Geographic features {#Geographic_features}

In the far north, the Misty Mountains met the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) and the [Mountains of Angmar](/wiki/Mountains_of_Angmar), forming a **T**; The Mountains of Angmar comprising the western "arm", the Grey Mountains the Eastern "arm", and the Misty Mountains the stem.<sup id="cite_ref-map_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-4">[3]</a></sup> They ran 900 miles (1,448 kilometres)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> to the [Gap of Rohan](/wiki/Gap_of_Rohan) in the south, where they approached the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains). The Gap was a valley between the southernmost peak of the Misty Mountains and the northernmost of the White Mountains, creating an important pass.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 2]</a></sup>

The lands around the Misty Mountains included several notable forests, rivers and kingdoms, such as [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar), [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion), [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland), [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Fangorn" title="Fangorn">Fangorn</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-map_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-4">[3]</a></sup> While Elves guarded both sides of the Misty Mountains, few ever crossed over them.

### Important peaks {#Important_peaks}

The northernmost peak of the Misty Mountains can be considered Mount [Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad),<sup id="cite_ref-map_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-4">[3]</a></sup> where according to legend, <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin</a>, eldest of the seven [Fathers of the Dwarves](/wiki/Fathers_of_the_Dwarves), awoke. Later it became a [haven for Orcs](/wiki/Orc-holds "Orc-holds").

The greatest [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") realm in Middle-Earth, [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"), was located at the midpoint of the Misty Mountains. The city was built under three peaks, called the [Mountains of Moria](/wiki/Mountains_of_Moria): Redhorn (*[Caradhras](/wiki/Caradhras)* in Sindarin), Silvertine (*[Celebdil](/wiki/Celebdil)*) and Cloudyhead (*[Fanuidhol](/wiki/Fanuidhol)*).<sup id="cite_ref-south_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-7">[5]</a></sup> Inside Silvertine the Dwarves built the [Endless Stair](/wiki/Endless_Stair), a stairway from the depths of the mountain to its peak.

The southernmost mountain was [Methedras](/wiki/Methedras), which was Sindarin for "Last Peak".<sup id="cite_ref-map_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-4">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Origins {#Origins}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_The_Misty_Mountains.jpeg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/f/f8/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_The_Misty_Mountains.jpeg/300px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_The_Misty_Mountains.jpeg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="191" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/f/f8/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_The_Misty_Mountains.jpeg/450px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_The_Misty_Mountains.jpeg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/f/f8/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_The_Misty_Mountains.jpeg/600px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_The_Misty_Mountains.jpeg 2x" data-file-width="2000" data-file-height="1272"/></a><figcaption>Undated painting of the Misty Mountains by <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. The Misty Mountains were created by the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") during the [Years of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees) as a hindrance for [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), who would hunt his fell creatures. They would later serve as a deterrent for the Elves during the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey), causing some to turn south. The Elves that would not cross the Misty Mountains would become the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor).<sup id="cite_ref-s3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s3-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup>

### Inhabitants {#Inhabitants}

It is said that ancient [bears](/wiki/Bears "Bears") lived in the Mountains until [Giants](/wiki/Giants) arrived and the bears left to the lowlands.<sup id="cite_ref-queer_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-queer-9">[7]</a></sup>

The great Dwarven city of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") (later called the "Black Pit" of [Moria](/wiki/Moria)) was located near the middle of the mountain chain. There [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk) lived for thousands of years with a kingdom which spread as far north as Gundabad and as far east as the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills).

At the southern tip of the Misty Mountains, beneath Methedras, was [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), which was originally a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") fortress but was later given to [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman).

Around [1050](/wiki/Third_Age_1050 "Third Age 1050"), the [Harfoots](/wiki/Harfoots) migrated west across the Misty Mountains, fleeing the ever more numerous Men and the Shadow growing in [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood), thus the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) entered history. Later the other two groups of Hobbits, the [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors) and [Fallohides](/wiki/Fallohides), migrated west as well, until by 2500 no Hobbits could be found east of the mountains.

When the Dwarves were strong, the mountains were generally free of Orcs, but when the [Shadow](/wiki/Shadow) was strong, Orcs bred in Mount Gundabad, in [Goblin-town](/wiki/Goblin-town), later in Moria itself, and everywhere in between. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300") the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains) started increasing and harassing the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves).<sup id="cite_ref-b2_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-b2-10">[8]</a></sup>

The awakening of [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane) drove the Dwarves from their city. It also seems that some Dwarves, either before or after the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs), dwelt on the eastern side of the mountains near Goblin-town.

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2480](/wiki/Third_Age_2480 "Third Age 2480") the Orcs began establishing secret strongholds in the Mountains barring all the passes to [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) from [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion), while Sauron populated Moria with his creatures.<sup id="cite_ref-b2_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-b2-10">[8]</a></sup>

Near the east exit of Goblin-town the Eagles kept an [eyrie](/wiki/Eagle%27s_Eyrie "Eagle's Eyrie") to keep watch on the Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-south_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-7">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-council_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-11">[9]</a></sup>

[Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) believed that it was the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains) that had driven the people of [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn) away, and he promised to return there.<sup id="cite_ref-queer_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-queer-9">[7]</a></sup>

[Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) used the High Pass to cross the Misty Mountains, and witnessed the mysterious [Giants](/wiki/Giants). Later they were captured by the Orcs of Goblin-town.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup>

It seems that Beorn's people returned to the Mountains. Once the Orcs were decimated, the [Beornings](/wiki/Beornings) kept open the passages of the [Ford of Carrock](/wiki/Ford_of_Carrock) and the [High Pass](/wiki/High_Pass) in return for tolls.<sup id="cite_ref-council_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-11">[9]</a></sup>

The [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) faced the challenge to pass the Mountains. They decided to avoid the High Pass or the Gap of Rohan because of the threat of Orc patrols and Saruman's forces. They attempted to cross Redhorn Pass, but a powerful blizzard blocked it, forcing them to journey through Moria. There [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) was lost trying to stop Durin's Bane, and after falling into the abyss, he chased him up to the Dwarven [Endless Stair](/wiki/Endless_Stair) and [fought on the peak](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Peak "Battle of the Peak") of Silvertine.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[11]</a></sup>

It is said that in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) [Durin VII](/wiki/Durin_VII) led the Dwarves back to Moria.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[12]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name of the Misty Mountains, <i><b>hithaeglir</b></i>, consists of *[hîth](/wiki/H%C3%AEth "Hîth")* ("mist") and *aeglir* ("line of peaks").<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[13]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [The Ambarkanta](/wiki/The_Ambarkanta) [maps](/wiki/Ambarkanta_maps "Ambarkanta maps") the Misty Mountains are not present, but the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey) map is present. The Misty Mountains would have to be in the North-Lands, between the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) and [Red Mountains (Orocarni)](/wiki/Orocarni "Orocarni").

In a draft of the chapter *The Council of Elrond*, the Misty Mountains were also named Hithdilias or Eredhithui<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[14]</a></sup>.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1967: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_film)" title="The Hobbit (1967 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1967 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Misty Mountains are renamed the Barricade Mountains, and encircle Mirkwood, which in turn encircles Dale and the Lonely Mountain.</dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Fellowship decides to take the <a href="/wiki/Caradhras" title="Caradhras">Pass of Caradhras</a> over the Misty Mountains after learning that the passage South was being watched. A blizzard caused by Saruman forces the Company to go under the Mountains, through <a href="/wiki/Moria" title="Moria">Moria</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Misty Mountains appear as both a major game region and within other game areas. The region of &#34;Misty Mountains&#34; consists of the part of the mountain range adjacent to Rivendell Valley, including both <a href="/wiki/High_Pass" title="High Pass">High Pass</a> and <a href="/wiki/Goblin-town" title="Goblin-town">Goblin-town</a>. The mountain range in a broader sense can also be accessed from other in-game regions, including the pass Caradhras in <a href="/wiki/Eregion" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>, former settlement of Dwarves under <a href="/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r" title="Thrór">Thrór</a> in eastern <a href="/wiki/Enedwaith" title="Enedwaith">Enedwaith</a>, a village on the slopes of mount <a href="/wiki/Methedras" title="Methedras">Methedras</a> in <a href="/wiki/Dunland" title="Dunland">Dunland</a>, the cliffs of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Zirakzigil" title="Zirakzigil">Zirakzigil</a> in Moria and the eastern slopes of the Mountains in <a href="/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien" title="Lothlórien">Lothlórien</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>While travelling across the Misty Mountains <a href="/wiki/Thorin_and_Company" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and Company</a> are impeded by Stone Giants during a storm. They take refuge in a cave, which leads them to fall into <a href="/wiki/Goblin-town" title="Goblin-town">Goblin-town</a> and are rescued by Gandalf, eventually escaping out onto the East side of the mountain.</dd></dl>
