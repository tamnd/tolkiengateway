**Martin Jarvis** [OBE](/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire "Order of the British Empire") (born [4 August](/wiki/4_August) [1941](/wiki/1941)) is an English actor.

## History {#History}

Jarvis's long acting career has included the role of Jon Forsyte in the critically acclaimed <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Forsyte_Saga#1967_mini-series" title="wikipedia:The Forsyte Saga">miniseries <i>The Forsyte Saga</i></a> and the title character in Dickens's <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Nickleby" title="wikipedia:Nicholas Nickleby">Nicholas Nickleby</a></i>. Many roles followed on stage, screen, and radio. He also served as storyteller on *[Jackanory](/wiki/Jackanory)* for six episodes.
