**Martin Jarvis** [OBE](../O/Order_of_the_British_Empire.md) (born [4 August](../_/4_August.md) [1941](../_/1941.md)) is an English actor.

## History {#History}

Jarvis's long acting career has included the role of Jon Forsyte in the critically acclaimed <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Forsyte_Saga#1967_mini-series" title="wikipedia:The Forsyte Saga">miniseries <i>The Forsyte Saga</i></a> and the title character in Dickens's <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Nickleby" title="wikipedia:Nicholas Nickleby">Nicholas Nickleby</a></i>. Many roles followed on stage, screen, and radio. He also served as storyteller on *[Jackanory](../J/Jackanory.md)* for six episodes.
