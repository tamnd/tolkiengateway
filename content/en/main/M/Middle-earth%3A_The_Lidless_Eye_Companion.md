<i><b>Middle-earth: The Lidless Eye Companion</b></i> is a source-book for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

This *Companion* includes the complete *MELE* rules (in larger print), a complete list of *MELE cards*, scenarios for play, and several full-color NW Middle-earth maps.
