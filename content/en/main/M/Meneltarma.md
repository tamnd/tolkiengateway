**Meneltarma** was a sacred mountain in the centre of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).

## History {#History}

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r413279">.mw-parser-output .t-maintenance{display:inline-flex;margin:0.25rem;padding:0.5rem 0.75rem;box-shadow:var(--tg-border-color-base)0px 0px 0px 1px inset;border-left:4px solid var(--tg-color-surface-3);border-radius:var(--tg-border-radius-small);background-color:var(--tg-color-surface-2);color:var(--tg-color-emphasized);font-size:var(--tg-font-size-x-small);font-weight:var(--tg-font-weight-medium);line-height:var(--line-height-sm)}.mw-parser-output .t-maintenance .ext-floatingui-reference{display:flex;align-items:center;gap:0.5rem}.mw-parser-output .t-maintenance.t-maintenance--yellow{border-left-color:#f59e0b}.mw-parser-output .t-maintenance.t-maintenance--red{border-left-color:#ef4444}.mw-parser-output .t-maintenance.t-maintenance--green{border-left-color:#10b981}.mw-parser-output .t-maintenance .t-maintenance-icon img{filter:var(--filter-invert);opacity:0.6}</style>

The mountain, also known as the **Holy Mountain**, was sacred to the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) as a holy place devoted to [Eru Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar "Ilúvatar"). A requirement of absolute silence was imposed on any who climbed the mountain. The tradition was so strong and universal that even foreigners felt bound by it. Only the King was allowed to utter prayers and thanksgivings to Eru Ilúvatar, during the feasts of [Erukyermë](/wiki/Erukyerm%C3%AB), [Erulaitalë](/wiki/Erulaital%C3%AB) and [Eruhantalë](/wiki/Eruhantal%C3%AB).

Birds and animals did not approach the site, and only the [Eagles of Manwë](/wiki/Eagles "Eagles") were seen circling over the summit. They were also regarded as sacred guardians of the mountain and all the land.

## Geography {#Geography}

Meneltarma was the highest peak of the island. It stood at the centre of the island, geographically in the inland regions called [Mittalmar](/wiki/Mittalmar). It stood three thousand feet (914m) above the surrounding plain.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The base of Meneltarma sloped gently and divided itself into five roots reaching out towards the five divisions of the land. These roots were called [Tarmasundar](/wiki/Tarmasundar), the *Roots of the Pillar*, gentle grass-covered highlands. On the southern slopes were located the tombs of [Kings and Queens of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor"), in a valley called [Noirinan](/wiki/Noirinan).

However, near the summit the slopes became more vertical and could not be ascended easily. The kings later built a spiral road to the peak, beginning at the southern tip of the mountain and winding up to the lip of the summit in the north.

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Jef_Murray_-_At_the_foot_of_the_Meneltarma.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/3/38/Jef_Murray_-_At_the_foot_of_the_Meneltarma.jpg/300px-Jef_Murray_-_At_the_foot_of_the_Meneltarma.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="188" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/3/38/Jef_Murray_-_At_the_foot_of_the_Meneltarma.jpg/450px-Jef_Murray_-_At_the_foot_of_the_Meneltarma.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/3/38/Jef_Murray_-_At_the_foot_of_the_Meneltarma.jpg/600px-Jef_Murray_-_At_the_foot_of_the_Meneltarma.jpg 2x" data-file-width="800" data-file-height="501"/></a><figcaption><i>At the foot of the Meneltarma</i> by <a href="/wiki/Jef_Murray" title="Jef Murray">Jef Murray</a></figcaption></figure>

Meneltarma had a flattened and somehow depressed summit, which was so broad that was able to contain a great multitude during religious ceremonies. However no permanent structure was erected there, and the ceremonies took place in open air. It was said that on a clear day the 'far-sighted' might see [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa).

After the destruction of Númenor in which fire burst from the Meneltarma, some amongst the [Exiles](/wiki/Exiles_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Exiles of Númenor") believed that the [Hallow of Eru](/wiki/Hallow_of_Eru) upon the summit of the mountain remained as an isle in [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer). Some [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) are even said to have set sail trying to reach the isle, from which the legend held that one could catch a glimpse of the unreachable [Aman](/wiki/Aman).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Meneltarma* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Pillar of Heaven",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> from *[menel](/wiki/Menel "Menel")* ("the heavens, firmament, sky") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tarma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tarma (page does not exist)">tarma</a></i> ("pillar").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Among the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) names for the mountain are "very archaic" *hebaensuil*,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>, *Heofonsýl*,<sup id="cite_ref-Edwin_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Edwin-6">[6]</a></sup> and *Ealfæderburg*,<sup id="cite_ref-Edwin_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Edwin-6">[6]</a></sup> with the first two both meaning "Pillar of Heaven" and the last meaning "the mountain of Allfather".

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name for the mountain went through a number of iterations. Tolkien considered *Menelmin, Menelmindo, Menelminda,* and *Menelmindon* before he finally selected *Meneltarma*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
