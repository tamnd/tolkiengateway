The **Map of Wilderland**, depicting the region of [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion), is included in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* (and as plate No. 87 in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist &amp; Illustrator](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Artist_and_Illustrator "J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator")*).<sup id="cite_ref-AH_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AH-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AI_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-2">[2]</a></sup>

The map was drawn by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), and a preliminary version of the map appears as plate No. 84 in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist &amp; Illustrator](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Artist_and_Illustrator "J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator")*.<sup id="cite_ref-AI_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-2">[2]</a></sup> In [1979](/wiki/1979) a coloured version by [H.E. Riddett](/wiki/H.E._Riddett) was published as a poster by [Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin "Allen and Unwin").<sup id="cite_ref-AH_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AH-1">[1]</a></sup>

J.R.R. Tolkien added the river names "Mitheithel = [Hoarwell](/wiki/Hoarwell)", "[Bruinen](/wiki/Bruinen)" with "Loudwater" below it to the river flowing north of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), "Merrill" to the river flowing south of Rivendell, "Rushdown" with "[Rhimdath](/wiki/Rhimdath)" below it and "[R. Gladden](/wiki/Gladden_River "Gladden River")" and the area names "Entish land", "Hoardale" and "[Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields)" in pencil on the Map of Wilderland<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> in one of his copies of *The Hobbit*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>.
