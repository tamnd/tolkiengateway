**Maeglin** is a chapter in the book [The War of the Jewels](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels), eleventh of [The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth) series.

In this chapter, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) describes how his [father](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") wrote the story of [Isfin](/wiki/Aredhel "Aredhel"), or Aredhel, her husband, [Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l), and their son, [Maeglin](/wiki/Maeglin). In this chapter, Christopher Tolkien talked about the manuscript and the process through which the story of Maeglin was constructed.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

After this, Christopher Tolkien breaks down the story, detailing how J. R. R. Tolkien changed the manuscripts, editing names and histories. He notes that much was editing in Eöl's history and how J. R. R. Tolkien had to edit the maps he had previously created.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>
