The **Mound of the Riders** was a burial mound raised for the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) who fell in the [Battles of the Fords of Isen](../B/Battles_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md). It was raised on a bare eyot in the middle of the fords, ringed with stones and set about with spears.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The [First](../F/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md) and [Second](../S/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md) Battles of the Fords happened on [25 February](../_/25_February.md) and [2 March](../_/2_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) respectively. The Rohirrim took heavy losses. Later, [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) gathered all surviving forces he could find. He set some to make the Mound and later to return to protect [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).
