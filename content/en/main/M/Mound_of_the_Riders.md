The **Mound of the Riders** was a burial mound raised for the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) who fell in the [Battles of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Battles_of_the_Fords_of_Isen). It was raised on a bare eyot in the middle of the fords, ringed with stones and set about with spears.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The [First](/wiki/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen "First Battle of the Fords of Isen") and [Second](/wiki/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen "Second Battle of the Fords of Isen") Battles of the Fords happened on [25 February](/wiki/25_February) and [2 March](/wiki/2_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") respectively. The Rohirrim took heavy losses. Later, [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) gathered all surviving forces he could find. He set some to make the Mound and later to return to protect [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).
