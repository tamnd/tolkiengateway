**Minstrels** are those persons who had the ability to compose and sing songs. Although many people sang, and a number created songs, only a few became widely famous for their musical compositions. Known by name:

- [Daeron](/wiki/Daeron) of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), who was the court minstrel of King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Dírhaval](/wiki/D%C3%ADrhaval) of the First Age, who wrote the *[Narn i Chîn Húrin](</wiki/Narn_i_Ch%C3%AEn_H%C3%BArin_(tale)> "Narn i Chîn Húrin (tale)")*, the longest of all the lays to come out of Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Gléowine](/wiki/Gl%C3%A9owine) of the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), was the court minstrel of King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Glirhuin](/wiki/Glirhuin) of the First Age, was a [seer](/wiki/Seer "Seer") and harp-player who composed a song that predicted that the [Stone of the Hapless](/wiki/Stone_of_the_Hapless) would never be defiled or sink beneath the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor) of the First Age, was the second of the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) and was famed as a poet and bard.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- A [minstrel](/wiki/Minstrel_of_Gondor "Minstrel of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) during the late Third Age composed an epic lay recounting the [tale](/wiki/Quest_of_the_Ring "Quest of the Ring") "*[of Frodo of the Nine Fingers and the Ring of Doom](/wiki/Frodo_of_the_Nine_Fingers "Frodo of the Nine Fingers")*".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A poetic [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for "minstrel, reciter" was <i><b>quenso</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, there were named "three most magic players of the Elves": [Dairon](/wiki/Dairon), [Tinfang](/wiki/Tinfang) and [Ivárë](/wiki/Iv%C3%A1r%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In the *[Lay of Leithian](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian)*, the greatest of the minstrels of the Elves are Tinfang Gelion, Maglor and Daeron.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
