**Minstrels** are those persons who had the ability to compose and sing songs. Although many people sang, and a number created songs, only a few became widely famous for their musical compositions. Known by name:

- [Daeron](../D/Daeron.md) of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), who was the court minstrel of King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Dírhaval](../D/Dírhaval.md) of the First Age, who wrote the *[Narn i Chîn Húrin](<../N/Narn_i_Chîn_Húrin_(tale).md>)*, the longest of all the lays to come out of Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Gléowine](../G/Gléowine.md) of the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), was the court minstrel of King [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Glirhuin](../G/Glirhuin.md) of the First Age, was a [seer](../S/Seer.md) and harp-player who composed a song that predicted that the [Stone of the Hapless](../S/Stone_of_the_Hapless.md) would never be defiled or sink beneath the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Maglor](Maglor.md) of the First Age, was the second of the [Sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) and was famed as a poet and bard.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- A [minstrel](Minstrel_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) during the late Third Age composed an epic lay recounting the [tale](../Q/Quest_of_the_Ring.md) "*[of Frodo of the Nine Fingers and the Ring of Doom](../F/Frodo_of_the_Nine_Fingers.md)*".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A poetic [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word for "minstrel, reciter" was <i><b>quenso</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, there were named "three most magic players of the Elves": [Dairon](../D/Dairon.md), [Tinfang](../T/Tinfang.md) and [Ivárë](../I/Ivárë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In the *[Lay of Leithian](../L/Lay_of_Leithian.md)*, the greatest of the minstrels of the Elves are Tinfang Gelion, Maglor and Daeron.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
