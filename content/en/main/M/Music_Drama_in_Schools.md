<i><b>Music Drama in Schools</b></i> is a book edited by Malcolm John, originally published in [1971](/wiki/1971).

Include "A Preparatory School Approach" (pp. 1–19) by [Paul Drayton](/wiki/Paul_Drayton) and [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter) on a [musical dramatization of The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_stage_adaptation)> "The Hobbit (1967 stage adaptation)"). Drayton comments the music with numerous examples of score and Carpenter comments the adaptation and production. Tolkien's reactions are also noted.
