**MEN** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "go",<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup> or "move, proceed (in a direction intended by a person)",<sup id="cite_ref-VT41_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT41-2">[2]</a></sup> or "have as object, (in)tend, proceed, make for, go towards, go".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

In his notes on a poem in *[The Road Goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")*, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") gave the form *men* ("direction, region"). It was from this *men* + *[el](/wiki/El "El")* which was invented the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word *[Menel](/wiki/Menel)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It is not known with certainty if *men* here refers to a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) word (cf. *[Men (Elvish)#Elvish](</wiki/Men_(Elvish)#Elvish> "Men (Elvish)")*) or to a root.

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- *menta* ("send, cause to go, (in a desired direction)"<sup id="cite_ref-VT41_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT41-2">[2]</a></sup>

- *mēn-* ("a way, a going, a mov\[ement\]")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

- See also: Quenya *[mene](/wiki/Mene "Mene")* ("goes")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appears the root MEN-, with the Quenya derivatives *[men](</wiki/Men_(Elvish)#Quenya> "Men (Elvish)")* ("place, spot") and *ména* ("region").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
