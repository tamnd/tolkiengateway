**Middle-earth Network** was a [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)-based online community and network. It included a social networking website, a radio station, and a news website (<a class="external text" href="https://middleearthnews.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">MiddleEarthNews.com</a>),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> which is active as of 2024. <span></span>
