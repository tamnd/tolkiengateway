**METW: Rangers of the North** is a game card from the set *[Middle-earth: The Wizards](Middle-earth%253A_The_Wizards.md)*, for [MECCG](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).

<i><b>Note:</b></i> *"Rangers of the North" refers to the [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) from [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s legendarium.*

## Card text {#Card_text}

<i><b>Unique.</b></i> Playable at [Bree](METW%253A_Bree.md) if the influence check is greater than 9. Standard Modifications: Dúnedain (+1).

*"...an ordered company, armed with spear and bow and sword. They were clad in cloaks of dark grey...pinned upon the left shoulder by a brooch of silver shaped like a rayed star."* <i><b>-LotRV</b></i>
