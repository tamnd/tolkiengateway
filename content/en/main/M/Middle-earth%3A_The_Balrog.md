**Middle-earth: The Balrog**, released in [1998](../_/1998.md), was the last expansion set for [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).

*The Balrog* adds a new sub-faction to the minions, Balrog, who mainly depends on the brute strength of his [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Trolls](../T/Trolls.md). It is argued by the player base that since the production of the game ended after *The Balrog*, the set remains a bit overpowered because it didn't get a "balancing set" published afterwards.

*The Balrog* was sold as two boxes, each containing one deck. Buying both of them would yield a complete set, a format different from all other expansions. Deck 1 (red box) was called *The Shadow-deeps*; deck 2 (green box) was called *Balrog's Host*. Each box contained over half the expansion's cards. Grey bordered reprints of cards from earlier expansions were included to allow playing the decks right out of the box.
