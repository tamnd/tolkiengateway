**Michael Kucharski** is an artist. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.
