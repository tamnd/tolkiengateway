**Mágol** is one of the early languages of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"). It was said to be of Hungarian style and Tolkien worked on it around the years he was writing *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* or earlier. The context in which Tolkien created it is not known, since it is not much connected with his [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

There are at least two papers concerning Mágol. In the earliest one it is referred as "Mágo", and it was said that "Old Mágo" was the language of the children of [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin). The second paper seems a mixture of Hungarian and [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish).<sup id="cite_ref-a_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-a-1">[1]</a></sup> Sometime later he considered making this language [Orkish](/wiki/Orkish); he marked this page with the name "Orcish" but striked it out<sup id="cite_ref-a_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-a-1">[1]</a></sup> which means that he rejected this idea.<sup id="cite_ref-h_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-h-2">[2]</a></sup> However one word from its vocabulary is *[bolg](/wiki/Bolg "Bolg")* meaning "strong".<sup id="cite_ref-h_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-h-2">[2]</a></sup>

In *[The Notion Club Papers](/wiki/The_Notion_Club_Papers)* [Michael G. Ramer](/wiki/Michael_George_Ramer "Michael George Ramer") mentions several Ungric words like *Shomorú* (Hung. *szomorú* 'sad'<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>) 'Saturn',<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> *Dalud dimran*, *Eshil dimzor*,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> a waterfall on the world *[Ellor Eshúrizel](/wiki/Ellor_Esh%C3%BArizel)*,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> *[Gyönyörü, Emberü](/wiki/Green_Ember%C3%BC "Green Emberü")*,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> *Gyürüchill* 'Saturn' <sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
