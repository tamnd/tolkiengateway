<b><i>Mythlore</i> 64</b> (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/mythlore/vol17/iss2/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Volume 17, Issue 2</a>) is an issue of the *[Mythlore](/wiki/Mythlore)* journal, published by the [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society).

## Contents {#Contents}

#### Articles {#Articles}

- Wynne, Patrick. “Sauron Gets Drafted (and Other Thoughts on the Battle of Creativity): 21st Mythopoeic Conference Guest of Honor Address.”
- Thorson, Stephen. “Lewis and Barfield on Imagination.”
- Wynne, Patrick, Tom Loback, Paula DiSante, and Sarah Beach. “‘Feanor Fronts Fingolfin’: Artistic Visions of Four Artists.”
- Byfield, Bruce. “The Imposition of Structure: Archetypes in the Fafhrd and Mouser Series.”
- Houghton, John William. “Commedia as Fairy-story: Eucatastrophe in the Loss of Virgil.”
- Broadwell, Elizabeth. “Essë and Narn: Name, Identity, and Narrative in the Tale of Túrin Turambar.”
- Havard, R. E. “Professor J.R.R. Tolkien: A Personal Memoir.”

#### Features {#Features}

- Christopher, Joe R., Wayne G. Hammond, and Pat Allen Hargis. An Inklings Bibliography (41).
- Hyde, Paul Nolan. “Quenti Lambardillion.”
- Kondratiev, Alexei. “Tales Newly Told.”
- Letters.

#### Reviews {#Reviews}

- *The War of the Ring*. J.R.R Tolkien. Reviewed by Glen GoodKnight.
- *Bilbo's Last Song*. J.R.R Tolkien, Illust. by Pauline Baynes. Reviewed by Nancy-Lou Patterson.
- *Owen Barfield and C.S. Lewis*. Owen Barfield. Reviewed by Nancy-Lou Patterson.
- *A Christian for All Christians: Essays in Honour of C.S. Lewis.* Andrew Walker and James Patrick, Ed.. Reviewed by Nancy-Lou Patterson.
- *The Hobbit.* J.R.R. Tolkien, Illus. by David Wenzel, Adapted by Charles Dixon and Sean Deming. Reviewed by Glen GoodKnight.
- *The Mind of the Maker.* Dorothy L. Sayers. Reviewed by Nancy-Lou Patterson.
- *The Pattern in the Web: The Mythical Poetry of Charles Williams.* Roma A. King, Jr.. Reviewed by Nancy-Lou Patterson.
- *The Land of Narnia*. Biran Sibley, Illus. by Pauline Baynes. Reviewed by Glen GoodKnight.
- *Dangerous By Degrees: Women at Oxford and the Somerville Novelists.* Susan J. Leonardi. Reviewed by Nancy-Lou Patterson.
- *The 1991 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar.* Illust. by John Howe. Reviewed by Bruce Leonard.
