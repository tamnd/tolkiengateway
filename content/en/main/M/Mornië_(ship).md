<br> **Mornië** was the ship of the dead in the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

It plied between [Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos "Halls of Mandos") and [Eruman](/wiki/Eruman),<sup id="cite_ref-GL_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GL-1">[1]</a></sup> and its beaching place was [Hanstovánen](/wiki/Hanstov%C3%A1nen).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> After judging the dead of the race of [Men](/wiki/Men), [Fui](/wiki/Fui) led some to [Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos "Halls of Mandos") or [Angamandi](/wiki/Angamandi), but most of them were sent abroad the black ship Mornië. When the ship was filled, it spread its black sails by itself and sailed south carried by a slow wind. As they pass by, the dead were allowed to have a glimpse of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). Then they reached the great plains of [Arvalin](/wiki/Arvalin), where they wandered or camped in the dusk, being allowed to sing and see the [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars"), waiting until the [Great End](/wiki/Great_End).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Mornië* is [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) for "Black Grief", and its [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate was <i><b>Mornir</b></i>, from *morn* ("dark, black") + *nîr* ("grief").<sup id="cite_ref-GL_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GL-1">[1]</a></sup>
