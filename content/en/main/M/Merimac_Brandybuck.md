**Merimac Brandybuck** was a Hobbit of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland).

## History {#History}

Merimac was the youngest son of [Rorimac Brandybuck](/wiki/Rorimac_Brandybuck) and [Menegilda Goold](/wiki/Menegilda_Goold). He had one son, [Berilac](/wiki/Berilac_Brandybuck "Berilac Brandybuck"), and they were both present at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Merimac* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

The name *Merimac* is, like most male Brandybuck names, Celtic in sound, but apparently meaningless. The first element, *Meri* also occurrs in the name *[Meriadoc](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck")*. <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celtic_onomastics" title="wikipedia:Celtic onomastics">Mac</a></i> is a Celtic word meaning "son", though it is unknown whether Tolkien used it with that meaning in mind.

The entire name also resembles <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merrimac" title="wikipedia:Merrimac">Merrimac</a></i>, a name of a river and several towns in the United States. That word is of Native American origin.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
