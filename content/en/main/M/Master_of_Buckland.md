**Master of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland)** was a hereditary title among the [Bucklander](/wiki/Bucklanders "Bucklanders") [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits). As the head of the [Brandybuck Family](/wiki/Brandybuck_Family), he was also known as the **Master of the [Hall](/wiki/Brandy_Hall "Brandy Hall")**.<sup id="cite_ref-con_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-con-1">[1]</a></sup> Like the Thain and [Warden of Westmarch](/wiki/Warden_of_Westmarch), the Master had only nominal authority.

It was traditional for each of the Masters to have an epithet attached to their names, such as Gorbadoc Brandybuck "Broadbelt", Saradoc Brandybuck "Scattergold", and the most famous, Meriadoc the Magnificent.

## History {#History}

The first Master was [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Gorhendad Oldbuck](/wiki/Gorhendad_Oldbuck), after he left [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and moved to Buckland in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [740](/wiki/Third_Age_2340 "Third Age 2340").<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-2">[2]</a></sup> The holder of the title was accepted as the leader of Buckland, and several farms in the [Marish](/wiki/Marish) as well.<sup id="cite_ref-con_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-con-1">[1]</a></sup> After the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) ([Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [13](/wiki/Fourth_Age_13 "Fourth Age 13")), King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") made the Master of Buckland one of the Mayor Counsellors of the [North-Kingdom](/wiki/Arnor "Arnor").<sup id="cite_ref-Later_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-3">[3]</a></sup>
