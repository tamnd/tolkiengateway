**Matthew Stewart** is a fantasy artist who has created illustrations based on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [Legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium), both independently<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and for official works such as *[The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Card_Game)*,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> *[The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth)*<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> and *[Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit)*.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

Stewart first read *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* when he was in around the sixth grade.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>
