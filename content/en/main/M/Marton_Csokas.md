New Zealand actor **Marton Csokas** (born [June 30th](/wiki/30_June "30 June"), [1966](/wiki/1966)) played [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King")*.

His last name is Hungarian and is pronounced Cho-Kash.
