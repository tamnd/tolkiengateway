**Mosco Burrows** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Mosco was the eldest child of [Milo](/wiki/Milo_Burrows "Milo Burrows") and [Peony Burrows](/wiki/Peony_Baggins "Peony Baggins"). He was present at Bilbo's famous [Birthday Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") at the age of just fourteen.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
