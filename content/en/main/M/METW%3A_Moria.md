**METW: Moria** is a game card from the set *[Middle-earth: The Wizards](Middle-earth%253A_The_Wizards.md)*, for [MECCG](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).

<i><b>Note:</b></i> *"Moria" refers to [Moria](Moria.md) of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).*

## Card text {#Card_text}

- <i><b>Nearest Haven:</b></i> [Lórien](METW%253A_Lórien.md)
- <i><b>Playable:</b></i> Items (minor, major, greater, gold ring)
- <i><b>Automatic-attacks:</b></i> Orcs - 4 strikes with 7 prowess

*"In the pale ray of the wizard's staff...glimpses of stairs and arches, and of other passages and tunnels..."* <i><b>-<a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">LotRII</a></b></i>
