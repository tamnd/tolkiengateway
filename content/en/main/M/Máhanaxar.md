**Máhanaxar**, also called **Rithil-Anamo**,<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:401</sup> was the **Ring of Doom**, probably a hall or court where the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) held their councils.

## History {#History}

The Ring of Doom was located outside the golden western gates of the city of [Valimar](../V/Valimar.md) with the thrones of the Valar that were arranged in circle.<sup id="cite_ref-s1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-2">[2]</a></sup> The evergreen hill of [Ezellohar](../E/Ezellohar.md), upon which [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md) of [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) grew until its [Darkening](../D/Darkening_of_Valinor.md), laid within the Ring.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Valar were sitting on their thrones while watching [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md) chanting, bringing the Two Trees to life upon Ezellohar.<sup id="cite_ref-s1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-2">[2]</a></sup> Ever after, the Powers sat bathed in the light of the Trees to hold their great councils, and here some of the most momentous decisions of [Eä](../E/Eä.md)'s history were made.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Máhanaxar* is how the [Amanyar](../A/Amanyar.md) called the Ring of Doom in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). It contains the word *Máhan* "[chief Vala](../A/Aratar.md)", itself a loan from [Valarin](../V/Valarin.md) *māchanāz*<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:402</sup> "[Authority](../A/Authorities.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:399</sup>

All in all, *Máhanaxar* is said to be derived from [Valarin](../V/Valarin.md) <i><b>Māchananaškad</b></i>, a compound that includes the aforementioned *māchanāz*. It has been suggested that the second element might mean "ring" and is related to [Black Speech](../B/Black_Speech.md) *[nazg](../N/Nazg.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Cf. also [NASAG](../N/NASAG.md).

The word was translated as *Rithil-Anamo* in the Quenya of Aman. The word *rithil*, that probably means "ring", is otherwise unattested. The second word might be the genitive form of an otherwise unattested noun \**anan* (or less probably \**anama*), itself probably derived from [root](../R/Root.md) NAM (cf. [Námo](../N/Námo.md), the name of Mandos).<sup id="cite_ref-arda_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-arda-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other version of the legendarium {#Other_version_of_the_legendarium}

In the [Later *Quenta Silmarillion*](../L/Later_Quenta_Silmarillion.md), as Melkor came through [Aman](../A/Aman.md) and filled it with darkness and dread, he entered the Ring of Doom and cursed it, defiling Manwë's seat of judgement and casting down the thrones of the other Valar.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
