**Máhanaxar**, also called **Rithil-Anamo**,<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:401</sup> was the **Ring of Doom**, probably a hall or court where the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) held their councils.

## History {#History}

The Ring of Doom was located outside the golden western gates of the city of [Valimar](/wiki/Valimar) with the thrones of the Valar that were arranged in circle.<sup id="cite_ref-s1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-2">[2]</a></sup> The evergreen hill of [Ezellohar](/wiki/Ezellohar), upon which [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor") of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) grew until its [Darkening](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor "Darkening of Valinor"), laid within the Ring.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Valar were sitting on their thrones while watching [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) chanting, bringing the Two Trees to life upon Ezellohar.<sup id="cite_ref-s1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-2">[2]</a></sup> Ever after, the Powers sat bathed in the light of the Trees to hold their great councils, and here some of the most momentous decisions of [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4)'s history were made.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Máhanaxar* is how the [Amanyar](/wiki/Amanyar) called the Ring of Doom in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). It contains the word *Máhan* "[chief Vala](/wiki/Aratar "Aratar")", itself a loan from [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) *māchanāz*<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:402</sup> "[Authority](/wiki/Authorities "Authorities")".<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:399</sup>

All in all, *Máhanaxar* is said to be derived from [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) <i><b>Māchananaškad</b></i>, a compound that includes the aforementioned *māchanāz*. It has been suggested that the second element might mean "ring" and is related to [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech) *[nazg](/wiki/Nazg "Nazg")*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Cf. also [NASAG](/wiki/NASAG).

The word was translated as *Rithil-Anamo* in the Quenya of Aman. The word *rithil*, that probably means "ring", is otherwise unattested. The second word might be the genitive form of an otherwise unattested noun \**anan* (or less probably \**anama*), itself probably derived from [root](/wiki/Root "Root") NAM (cf. [Námo](/wiki/N%C3%A1mo), the name of Mandos).<sup id="cite_ref-arda_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-arda-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other version of the legendarium {#Other_version_of_the_legendarium}

In the [Later *Quenta Silmarillion*](/wiki/Later_Quenta_Silmarillion "Later Quenta Silmarillion"), as Melkor came through [Aman](/wiki/Aman) and filled it with darkness and dread, he entered the Ring of Doom and cursed it, defiling Manwë's seat of judgement and casting down the thrones of the other Valar.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
