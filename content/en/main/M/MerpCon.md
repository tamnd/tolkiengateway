<br> **MerpCon**, or **Middle-Earth Role Playing Convention** is an annual convention held in Spokane, Washington. It is not endorsed by [Middle-earth Enterprises](Middle-earth_Enterprises.md), the [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md), and not associated with [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md)' "[MERP](Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)".

## History {#History}

MerpCon is held each year during the last weekend of July. It runs for 3 days from that Friday, through to the end of Sunday (and sometimes a little into the morning of that following Monday). The event is focused on Role Playing Gaming in [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md), using any game system adapted or created for play in Middle-earth or any aspect of Tolkien's universe. The [first MerpCon](MerpCon_I.md) was organised in 2005, followed by a new one each year.

MerpCon was founded by [Hawke Robinson](../H/Hawke_Robinson.md).
