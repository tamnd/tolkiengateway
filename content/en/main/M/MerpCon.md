<br> **MerpCon**, or **Middle-Earth Role Playing Convention** is an annual convention held in Spokane, Washington. It is not endorsed by [Middle-earth Enterprises](/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises), the [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate), and not associated with [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises)' "[MERP](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing "Middle-earth Role Playing")".

## History {#History}

MerpCon is held each year during the last weekend of July. It runs for 3 days from that Friday, through to the end of Sunday (and sometimes a little into the morning of that following Monday). The event is focused on Role Playing Gaming in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), using any game system adapted or created for play in Middle-earth or any aspect of Tolkien's universe. The [first MerpCon](/wiki/MerpCon_I "MerpCon I") was organised in 2005, followed by a new one each year.

MerpCon was founded by [Hawke Robinson](/wiki/Hawke_Robinson).
