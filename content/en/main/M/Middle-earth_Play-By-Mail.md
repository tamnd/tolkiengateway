In **Middle-earth Play-By-Mail** every person has to fill in a form each two or three weeks and mail it to the <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Game_Masters">Game Master</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> Then these forms are processed in the head computer. The results are then sent back to the players.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> Each game has 25 nations, ten who fight for good, ten who are evil and five who are neutral.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> There are three scenarios: one in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") 1650, one in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2950](/wiki/Third_Age_2950 "Third Age 2950") and one in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> *Middle-earth Play-By-Mail* was licensed by the [Tolkien Enterprises](/wiki/Tolkien_Enterprises).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Game Masters {#Game_Masters}

- **USA:** *Game Systems Inc.*
- **UK:** *Gad Games* (till [1995](/wiki/1995)), *[Allsorts PBM Games](/wiki/Allsorts_PBM_Games)* (after [1995](/wiki/1995))
- **Spain:** *Central de JOCS S.L.*
- **Italy:** *DaS Production s.r.l.*
- **Australia:** *Strategic Fantasy Games of Australia*
- **Germany:** *Drachenschmeide*
- **The Netherlands:** *PBM Express (Cancelled)*
