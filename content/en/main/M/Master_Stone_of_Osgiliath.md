The **Master Stone of Osgiliath**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was, presumably, the greatest of the seven *[palantíri](../P/Palantíri.md)* that the [Faithful](../F/Faithful.md) brought to [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md) from [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Palantiri_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantiri-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was also the only *palantír* that could be used to secretly listen to communications between the users of the other *palantíri*.<sup id="cite_ref-Palantiri_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantiri-2">[2]</a></sup>

The *palantír* was held in the [Tower of the Dome](../D/Dome_of_Stars.md) in [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup> the ancient capital of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

While most of the *palantíri* were small and portable, the Stone of Osgiliath was much larger, and could not be lifted by any single man.<sup id="cite_ref-Palantiri_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantiri-2">[2]</a></sup>

However, the greatest of the Seeing-stones was also the first to be lost. After the death of King [Valacar](../V/Valacar.md) a civil war known as the [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md) started between rebels led by [Castamir](../C/Castamir.md), and forces loyal to the new King [Eldacar](<../E/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor).md>) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1432](../T/Third_Age_1432.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-5">[5]</a></sup>

During this civil war in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1437](../T/Third_Age_1437.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-5">[5]</a></sup> the city of Osgiliath was besieged and burned by the rebels, the [Tower of the Dome](../D/Dome_of_Stars.md) of Osgiliath was destroyed and the Stone was lost in the waters of the River [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>
