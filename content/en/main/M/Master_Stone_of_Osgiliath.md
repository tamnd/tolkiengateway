The **Master Stone of Osgiliath**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was, presumably, the greatest of the seven *[palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* that the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) brought to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) from [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Palantiri_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantiri-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was also the only *palantír* that could be used to secretly listen to communications between the users of the other *palantíri*.<sup id="cite_ref-Palantiri_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantiri-2">[2]</a></sup>

The *palantír* was held in the [Tower of the Dome](/wiki/Dome_of_Stars "Dome of Stars") in [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath),<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup> the ancient capital of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

While most of the *palantíri* were small and portable, the Stone of Osgiliath was much larger, and could not be lifted by any single man.<sup id="cite_ref-Palantiri_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantiri-2">[2]</a></sup>

However, the greatest of the Seeing-stones was also the first to be lost. After the death of King [Valacar](/wiki/Valacar) a civil war known as the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife) started between rebels led by [Castamir](/wiki/Castamir), and forces loyal to the new King [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Eldacar (King of Gondor)") in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1432](/wiki/Third_Age_1432 "Third Age 1432").<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-5">[5]</a></sup>

During this civil war in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1437](/wiki/Third_Age_1437 "Third Age 1437"),<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-5">[5]</a></sup> the city of Osgiliath was besieged and burned by the rebels, the [Tower of the Dome](/wiki/Dome_of_Stars "Dome of Stars") of Osgiliath was destroyed and the Stone was lost in the waters of the River [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>
