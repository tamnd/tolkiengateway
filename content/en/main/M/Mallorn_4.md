<b><i>Mallorn</i> 4</b> is the fourth issue of the [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Society.md)'s journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in 1971.

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Editorial**
    - The Editors' Thing
        - By Steven Thomson
    - Letters to the Editors
        - By John B. Abbott, Arthur Crittenden, Su Adler &amp; Helen Kurley, Dave Weldrake, Hal Broome, Bryan Talbot, and A.R. Fallone
    - Competition announcement
        - Task: to prepare a cast list for a hypothetical film adaptation of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*
        - Note: The results of this competition were never announced in subsequent issues.
    - Belladonna's Postbag
        - By Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md)), Malcom Edwards, Dave Weldrake, Jane Keep, Vincent Curran, Cliff Cumming, Hal Broome, Neil L. Burgess, and Hartley Patterson
- **Notes**
    - Tolkien and the Fairies of British Folk Tradition
        - By Dave Weldrake
    - Notes on the Influence of Anglo-Saxon Literature on *The Lord of the Rings*
        - By Bob Borsley
    - Social, Military, and Political Aspects of Rohan
        - By Peter Burley
- **Creative**
    - Ghost of Love (poem)
        - By A.R. Fallone
- **Book reviews**
    - *Lud-in-the-Mist* by Hope Mirrlees
        - Reviewed by Hal Broome
    - *Deryni Rising* by Katherine Kurtz
        - Reviewed by A.R. Fallone
    - The Elric novels by Michael Moorcock
        - Reviewed by Rosie Turner
    - *The Wood Beyond the World* by [William Morris](../W/William_Morris.md)
        - Reviewed by Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md))
- **Illustrations**
    - By Bryan Talbot, John B. Abbott, Roger Nixon, and Hal Broome
