**MEPR: Fatty Bolger** is a [promotional game card](/wiki/Promotional_MECCG_cards "Promotional MECCG cards") from the set *[Middle-earth: The Wizards](/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Wizards)*, for [MECCG](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game "Middle-earth Collectible Card Game"), depicting [Fredegar Bolger](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger).

## Card text {#Card_text}

<i><b>Unique.</b></i> Unless he is one of the starting characters, he may only be brought into play at his home site. All his corruption checks are modified by +1. He can tap to cancel a strike against another Hobbit in his company.

*"Fatty Bolger had not been idle. As soon as he saw the dark shapes creep from the garden, he knew the he must run for it, or perish."* <i><b>-LotRI</b></i>

## Artwork {#Artwork}

The artwork of the card was created by [Christina Wald](/wiki/Christina_Wald).
