**Mythcon 29** was the twenty-ninth annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme: "**[C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md): A Centenary Celebration"
- **Guests of Honor:** Paul F. Ford (Lewis scholar) and [Verlyn Flieger](../V/Verlyn_Flieger.md) (Inklings scholar)
- **Chairman:** [Diana Glyer](../D/Diana_Glyer.md)
