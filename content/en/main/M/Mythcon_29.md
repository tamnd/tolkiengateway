**Mythcon 29** was the twenty-ninth annual [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) conference.

- **Theme: "**[C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis): A Centenary Celebration"
- **Guests of Honor:** Paul F. Ford (Lewis scholar) and [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger) (Inklings scholar)
- **Chairman:** [Diana Glyer](/wiki/Diana_Glyer)
