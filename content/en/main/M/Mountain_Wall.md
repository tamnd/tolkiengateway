The **Mountain Wall** was a term that appears just once, in [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)'s poem *Eärendillinwë* quoted above. The meaning is not completely certain, but it seems to describe the [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri), the [Mountains of Defence](/wiki/Mountains_of_Defence) that ranged along the eastern borders of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).
