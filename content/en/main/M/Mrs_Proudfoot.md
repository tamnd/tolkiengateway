**Mrs. Proudfoot** was [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire) who non-verbally chastised [her husband](/wiki/Everard_Proudfoot "Everard Proudfoot") for being amused by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)'s firework antics.

Mrs. Proudfoot is an original creation for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*. She is portrayed by [Megan Edwards](/wiki/Megan_Edwards).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
