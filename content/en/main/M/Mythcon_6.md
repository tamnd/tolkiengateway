**Mythcon 6** was the sixth annual [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) conference.

- **Theme:** The Fictional Worlds of C.S. Lewis
- **Guests of Honor:** Walter Hooper (Lewis scholar &amp; executor) and Ed Meskys (former Thain of the [Tolkien Society of America](/wiki/Tolkien_Society_of_America))
- **Chairman:** Bruce McMenomy
