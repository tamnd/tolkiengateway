**METW: Aragorn II** is a game card from the set *[Middle-earth: The Wizards](Middle-earth%253A_The_Wizards.md)*, for [MECCG](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).

<i><b>Note:</b></i> *"Aragorn II" refers to the character [Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn.md) from [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).*

## Card text {#Card_text}

<i><b>Unique.</b></i> +2 direct influence against the [Rangers of the North](METW%253A_Rangers_of_the_North.md) faction.

\-3 marshalling points if eliminated.

*"All that is gold does not glitter, Not all those who wander are lost..."* <i><b>-<a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">LotRI</a></b></i>
