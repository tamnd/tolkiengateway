**Molly** was a character in [Smith of Wootton Major](../S/Smith_of_Wootton_Major.md), one of the children who attended [The Feast of Good Children](../T/The_Feast_of_Good_Children.md) during which the [Fay-star](../F/Fay-star.md) was hidden within the [Great Cake](../G/Great_Cake.md). The object from [Faery](../F/Faery.md) was eaten by [Smith Smithson](../S/Smith_Smithson.md).

Years later, [Nokes](../N/Nokes.md), who had been the [Master Cook](Master_Cook.md) at that feast, speculated about who had swallowed the Fay-star. He remembered Molly, the Miller's daughter, claiming that she had been greedy and bolted down her food, and later had become as fat as a sack. [Alf](../A/Alf.md), who had been Nokes' apprentice, disputed this. He noted that Molly had found two trinkets in her slice and therefore could not have eaten the star.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
