**Molly** was a character in [Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major), one of the children who attended [The Feast of Good Children](/wiki/The_Feast_of_Good_Children) during which the [Fay-star](/wiki/Fay-star) was hidden within the [Great Cake](/wiki/Great_Cake). The object from [Faery](/wiki/Faery) was eaten by [Smith Smithson](/wiki/Smith_Smithson).

Years later, [Nokes](/wiki/Nokes), who had been the [Master Cook](/wiki/Master_Cook) at that feast, speculated about who had swallowed the Fay-star. He remembered Molly, the Miller's daughter, claiming that she had been greedy and bolted down her food, and later had become as fat as a sack. [Alf](/wiki/Alf), who had been Nokes' apprentice, disputed this. He noted that Molly had found two trinkets in her slice and therefore could not have eaten the star.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
