<b><i>Mallorn</i> 28</b> is the twenty-eighth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s annual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in September [1991](../_/1991.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- Cover Illustration
    - By: [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md)
- Celtic Influences on the History of the First Age
    - By: Marie Barnfieid
- Frithuwold and the Farmer: *Farmer Giles of Ham* and his place in History
    - By: [Patricia Reynolds](../P/Patricia_Reynolds.md)
- The Quest for Imladris
    - By: Madawc Williams
- Sordid Suburbia
    - By: [Alex Lewis](../A/Alex_Lewis.md)
- *The Hobbit* and *A Midsummer Night's Dream*
    - By: Lisa Hopkins
- Sublime Scenes and Horrid Novels: Milestones along the Road to Middle-earth
    - By: [John Ellison](../J/John_Ellison.md)
- Valaquanta: Of the Energy of the Valar
    - By: Jennifer Coombs and Marc Read
- How to learn Elvish
    - By: [Nancy Martsch](../N/Nancy_Martsch.md)
- Review: *[The War of the Ring](../T/The_War_of_the_Ring.md)*
    - By: [Charles Noad](../C/Charles_Noad.md)
- Review: *[C.S. Lewis: a Biography](<../C/C.S._Lewis%253A_A_Biography_(1990_book).md>)*, by A.N. Wilson
    - By: [Wayne Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md)
- Research Report: Oxonmoot Eccentricity Survey
    - By: Sarah Sturch

- Inside Art
    - By: Denis Gordeyev, [John Ellison](../J/John_Ellison.md), Ian Collier, Ellen Chapman, Alexander Nikolayev, [Ruth Lacon](../R/Ruth_Lacon.md), Steve Lines
