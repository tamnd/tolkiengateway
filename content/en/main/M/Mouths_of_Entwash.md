The **Mouths of Entwash** was a mighty delta formed where the River [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash) met the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). The delta measured a hundred miles from the splitting of the river to its final inflow into [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). Travelers were advised to avoid the marshy lands.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Although the areas west of Anduin belonged to [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), the splitting of Entwash was considered the border, so the delta itself was part of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

So great was the inrush of Entwash that Anduin could not carry all its water, and so a marshland formed on the Anduin's eastern bank, called [Wetwang](/wiki/Wetwang).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
