**Alatar** is a game card from the set *[Middle-earth: The Wizards](Middle-earth%253A_The_Wizards.md)*, for [MECCG](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md). It depicts the [Blue Wizard Alatar](../B/Blue_Wizards.md), as he was also depicted in the [Middle-earth Role Playing](Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md) game.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Card text {#Card_text}

<i><b>Unique.</b></i> During the movement/hazard phase, the number of cards that opponent draws based on Alatar's company's movement is reduced by one. If in a haven when a hazard creature is played on another company, he may join that company and face one of the hazard creature's strikes; he must tap and make a corruption check immediately following the attack.

## Errata {#Errata}

" he must make a corruption check immediately following the attack, and, if untapped, he must tap."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
