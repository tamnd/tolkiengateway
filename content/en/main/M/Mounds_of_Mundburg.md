The **Mounds of Mundburg** was a place near [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) where the dead of the [Battle of Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields "Battle of the Pelennor Fields") were buried. The name of the mounds is the translation of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) into the [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) equivalent *[Mundburg](/wiki/Mundburg)*. The battle and the mounds were celebrated in the *[Song of the Mounds of Mundburg](/wiki/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
