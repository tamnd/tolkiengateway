**Mythcon 39** was the thirty-ninth annual [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) conference.

- **Theme:** "The Valkyrie and the Goddess: The Warrior Woman in Fantasy"
- **Guests of Honor:** [Marjorie J. Burns](/wiki/Marjorie_J._Burns) (scholar) and Sharon Newman (author)
