**Mythcon 39** was the thirty-ninth annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme:** "The Valkyrie and the Goddess: The Warrior Woman in Fantasy"
- **Guests of Honor:** [Marjorie J. Burns](Marjorie_J._Burns.md) (scholar) and Sharon Newman (author)
