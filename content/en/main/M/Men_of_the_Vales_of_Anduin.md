The **Men of the Vales of Anduin** are peoples of [Men](Men.md) who lived to the north of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and acknowledged its authority when the South-Kingdom reached the summit of its power in the days of King <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../H/Hyarmendacil.md" title="Hyarmendacil">Hyarmendacil</a> in the twelfth century T.A.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

As [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md), they most probably descended from the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) like most of the Men of the northern parts of the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It seems that the Men of the Vales of Anduin were ancestors of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md), who, coming from the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md) between the [Carrock](../C/Carrock.md) and the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../G/Gladden.md" title="Gladden">Gladden</a>, moved to the regions near the sources of Anduin during the reign of King [Eärnil II](../E/Eärnil_II.md) in the 21st century T.A.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Their language had some influence on the language of the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) who lived in the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md) in that time.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
