The **Men of the Vales of Anduin** are peoples of [Men](/wiki/Men) who lived to the north of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and acknowledged its authority when the South-Kingdom reached the summit of its power in the days of King <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Hyarmendacil" title="Hyarmendacil">Hyarmendacil</a> in the twelfth century T.A.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

As [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen), they most probably descended from the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) like most of the Men of the northern parts of the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It seems that the Men of the Vales of Anduin were ancestors of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), who, coming from the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin) between the [Carrock](/wiki/Carrock) and the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Gladden" title="Gladden">Gladden</a>, moved to the regions near the sources of Anduin during the reign of King [Eärnil II](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II) in the 21st century T.A.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Their language had some influence on the language of the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) who lived in the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin) in that time.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
