**Marco Maggi** is an Italian tabletop game designer, who often works together with his long-time friend [Francesco Nepitello](/wiki/Francesco_Nepitello). He has worked on games such as the *[War of the Ring](</wiki/War_of_the_Ring_(2004_board_game)> "War of the Ring (2004 board game)")* and the *[The One Ring](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)")* roleplaying game.<sup id="cite_ref-abc_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-abc-1">[1]</a></sup>
