**Marco Maggi** is an Italian tabletop game designer, who often works together with his long-time friend [Francesco Nepitello](../F/Francesco_Nepitello.md). He has worked on games such as the *[War of the Ring](<../W/War_of_the_Ring_(2004_board_game).md>)* and the *[The One Ring](<../T/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md>)* roleplaying game.<sup id="cite_ref-abc_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-abc-1">[1]</a></sup>
