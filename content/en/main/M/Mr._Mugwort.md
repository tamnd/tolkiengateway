**Mr. Mugwort** was a [hobbit](/wiki/Bree-hobbits "Bree-hobbits") of [Bree](/wiki/Bree).

## History {#History}

During [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins)' stay at [The Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony), Mr. Mugwort witnessed Frodo put on [The One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring) and dissapear, and in the ensuing confusion he got into an argument with the innkeeper [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur), not quite believing what he had just seen.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
