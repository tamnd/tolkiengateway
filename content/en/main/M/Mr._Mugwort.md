**Mr. Mugwort** was a [hobbit](../B/Bree-hobbits.md) of [Bree](../B/Bree.md).

## History {#History}

During [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)' stay at [The Prancing Pony](../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md), Mr. Mugwort witnessed Frodo put on [The One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) and dissapear, and in the ensuing confusion he got into an argument with the innkeeper [Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md), not quite believing what he had just seen.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
