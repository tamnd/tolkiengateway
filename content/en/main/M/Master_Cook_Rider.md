**Rob Rider**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the name of a [Master Cook](Master_Cook.md) of [Wootton Major](../W/Wootton_Major.md), the earliest to be mentioned in *[Smith of Wootton Major](../S/Smith_of_Wootton_Major.md)*. It is strongly implied in the work that he explored the world and discovered where the mysterious land of [Faery](../F/Faery.md) lay.

## History {#History}

Although Rider was known only to the people of Wootton Major as a cook, he had a long history of peregrination. Eventually he settled down in the sleepy village of Wootton Major, and held the prestigious title of Master Cook. He married a woman of the village and had a daughter, who married a man named Smith and had a young child called [Smith Smithson](../S/Smith_Smithson.md). One day, however, Rider took a vacation, something unheard of in Wootton Major.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

He was gone for a few months, but during that time he entered the land of Faery, met the [King of Faery](../A/Alf.md), and picked up a [Fay-star](../F/Fay-star.md). He returned with the King of Faery disguised as a youth called Alf; it is not said whether or not Rider knew who the youth was when he first met him, but it is indicated that if not he eventually learned.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Alf became his apprentice. The people noted that after his return he was very merry and sang songs, but were suspicious of his choice of a "foreigner," Alf, as his successor.

Three years later, Rider abruptly told Alf farewell and departed, saying that he was going on a holiday and was not to return.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> He entrusted Alf with the fay-star, wishing that Alf could manage it to pass to his young grandson Smith Smithson, then two years old.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> A man named [Nokes](../N/Nokes.md) was made Great Cook after his departure, but nonetheless Rider got his wish and Alf caused the fay-star to go to his grandchild at [The Feast of Good Children](../T/The_Feast_of_Good_Children.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
