**Mythcon 45** was the forty-fifth annual [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) conference.

- **Theme:** "Where Fantasy Fits"
- **Guests of Honor:** Ursula Vernon (writer and artist), and [Richard C. West](/wiki/Richard_C._West) (Inklings scholar)
