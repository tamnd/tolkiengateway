**Mythcon 45** was the forty-fifth annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme:** "Where Fantasy Fits"
- **Guests of Honor:** Ursula Vernon (writer and artist), and [Richard C. West](../R/Richard_C._West.md) (Inklings scholar)
