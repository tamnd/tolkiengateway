**Master** was a title used for many people, the most prominent being [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil), whose jolly and riddling songs gave him mastery over all things in his domain,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and the two [Dark Lords](/wiki/Dark_Lord "Dark Lord"), whose overwhelming malign sway over fell powers made them overlords over a myriad of willing or unwitting thralls.

Other characters who have also been honoured with this title include:

- [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) was called "little master" by [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and Master by both [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum), the latter case due to Frodo's ownership of Gollum's 'precious': [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The elected civil leader of [Esgaroth](/wiki/Lake-town "Lake-town") upon the Long Lake was called [Master](/wiki/Master_of_Lake-town "Master of Lake-town").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- The Dark Lord [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), as Master to his foul servants, was referred to by this epithet by both the [Wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards") [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and much earlier by [King](</wiki/High_King_(D%C3%BAnedain)> "High King (Dúnedain)") [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur), the latter specifically calling him "Black Master".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
- The [Uruk](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai") captain [Uglúk](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk) used the epithet for his own petty Master, the Wizard [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
