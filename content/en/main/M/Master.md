**Master** was a title used for many people, the most prominent being [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md), whose jolly and riddling songs gave him mastery over all things in his domain,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and the two [Dark Lords](../D/Dark_Lord.md), whose overwhelming malign sway over fell powers made them overlords over a myriad of willing or unwitting thralls.

Other characters who have also been honoured with this title include:

- [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) was called "little master" by [Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and Master by both [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md), the latter case due to Frodo's ownership of Gollum's 'precious': [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The elected civil leader of [Esgaroth](../L/Lake-town.md) upon the Long Lake was called [Master](Master_of_Lake-town.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- The Dark Lord [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), as Master to his foul servants, was referred to by this epithet by both the [Wizard](../W/Wizards.md) [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and much earlier by [King](<../H/High_King_(Dúnedain).md>) [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md), the latter specifically calling him "Black Master".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
- The [Uruk](../U/Uruk-hai.md) captain [Uglúk](../U/Uglúk.md) used the epithet for his own petty Master, the Wizard [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
