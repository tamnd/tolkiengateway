<i><b>Middle-earth Role Playing: a complete system for adventuring in J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s World</b></i> was a core rulebook for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*, 1st Edition. It was later replaced by a [second rulebook using the same stocknumber](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing:_The_Role_Playing_Game_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_World "Middle-earth Role Playing: The Role Playing Game of J.R.R. Tolkien's World") (1986) and the *[Middle-earth Role Playing, 2nd ed.](</wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing_(2nd_edition)> "Middle-earth Role Playing (2nd edition)")* (1993).
