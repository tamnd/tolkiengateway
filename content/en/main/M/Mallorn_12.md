<b><i>Mallorn</i> 12</b> is the twelfth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s annual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in [1978](../_/1978.md).

The issue includes a review "Opinion of Priscilla Tolkien on The Biography" (pp. 5–6) and a reprint of J.R.R. Tolkien's early poem "[The Battle of the Eastern Field](../T/The_Battle_of_the_Eastern_Field.md)" (pp. 24–28).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
