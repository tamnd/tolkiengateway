**Martin Grip** is a Swedish artist who illustrated the chapter images and cover art of the first starter set and [core rulebook](<../C/Core_Rules_(The_One_Ring).md>) of *[The One Ring](<../T/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md>)*’s second edition.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> His style was noted to be a departure from the first edition.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Antonio De Luca](../A/Antonio_De_Luca.md) succeeded him in this role, initially staying close to the style established by Grip.
