<i><b>morn</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) adjective meaning "black, dark".<sup id="cite_ref-L297_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L297-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [MOR](/wiki/MOR).<sup id="cite_ref-L297_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L297-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Lenition {#Lenition}

The [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") form <i><b>vorn</b></i> (*m* &gt; *v*) apparently appears in *[Eryn Vorn](/wiki/Eryn_Vorn)* ("Blackwood").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RClxv_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RClxv-5">[5]</a></sup> It is also likely seen in the compound <i><a href="/wiki/Galvorn" title="Galvorn">gal<b>vorn</b></a></i> and perhaps also in <i><a href="/wiki/Aravorn" title="Aravorn">Ara<b>vorn</b></a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lacho_calad!_Drego_morn!&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lacho calad! Drego morn! (page does not exist)">Drego <b>morn</b>!</a></i> = "Flee night!"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Morben" title="Morben"><b>Mor</b>ben</a></i> = "Dark-folk"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor"><b>Mor</b>dor</a></i> = "Black Land"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Mordu" title="Mordu"><b>Mor</b>du</a></i> = "Black Night"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Morgul" title="Morgul"><b>mor</b>gul</a></i> = "black arts, sorcery"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Mormegil" title="Mormegil"><b>Mor</b>megil</a></i> = "Black Sword"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Moria" title="Moria"><b>Mor</b>ia</a></i> = "Black Chasm"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Mornan" title="Mornan"><b>Mor</b>nan</a></i> = "Dark Vale"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Ras_Morthil" title="Ras Morthil">Ras <b>Mor</b>thil</a></i> = "Black-point Cape"

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the published *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appeared the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word *moru* ("black").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> That spelling, however, was a misreading of the original manuscript, and has been corrected to *morn*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[morna](/wiki/Morna "Morna")* ("black, dark", adjective)
