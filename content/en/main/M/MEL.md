**MEL** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme").<sup id="cite_ref-VT39_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT39-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[méla](/wiki/M%C3%A9la "Méla")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT39_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT39-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) *[māla](/wiki/M%C4%81la "Māla")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT39_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT39-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *Etymologies* appears the root **MEL-**, signifying "love (as friend)". Amongs its derivatives are:

- Quenya *melin* ("dear"), *[melme](/wiki/Melme "Melme")* ("love")
- Noldorin *[mellon](/wiki/Mellon "Mellon")* ("friend")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
