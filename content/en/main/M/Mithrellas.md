**Mithrellas** was a [Silvan Elf](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) who, according the tradition of the House of [Angelimir](../A/Angelimir.md), accompanied [Nimrodel](../N/Nimrodel.md) from [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) on her journey to the southern havens. Mithrellas, it is said, became lost in the woods of [Dor-en-Ernil](../D/Dor-en-Ernil.md), where she was found by [Imrazôr](../I/Imrazôr.md) the [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md), who married her. So [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) blood entered the line of the [Princes of Dol Amroth](../P/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth.md), it was claimed, and her son [Galador](../G/Galador.md) became the first of the long line of Princes. Mithrellas also bore Imrazôr a daughter, [Gilmith](../G/Gilmith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> After the birth, she ran away at night and was never seen again.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Mithrellas* is of unclear meaning, but as suggested by [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md), it is likely to be [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), a combination of *[mithren](Mith.md)* ("grey") + *[las(s)](../L/Lass.md)* ("leaf").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
