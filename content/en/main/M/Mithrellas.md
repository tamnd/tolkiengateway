**Mithrellas** was a [Silvan Elf](/wiki/Silvan_Elves "Silvan Elves") who, according the tradition of the House of [Angelimir](/wiki/Angelimir), accompanied [Nimrodel](/wiki/Nimrodel) from [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) on her journey to the southern havens. Mithrellas, it is said, became lost in the woods of [Dor-en-Ernil](/wiki/Dor-en-Ernil), where she was found by [Imrazôr](/wiki/Imraz%C3%B4r) the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans"), who married her. So [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) blood entered the line of the [Princes of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth "Prince of Dol Amroth"), it was claimed, and her son [Galador](/wiki/Galador) became the first of the long line of Princes. Mithrellas also bore Imrazôr a daughter, [Gilmith](/wiki/Gilmith).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> After the birth, she ran away at night and was never seen again.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Mithrellas* is of unclear meaning, but as suggested by [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo), it is likely to be [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), a combination of *[mithren](/wiki/Mith "Mith")* ("grey") + *[las(s)](/wiki/Lass "Lass")* ("leaf").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
