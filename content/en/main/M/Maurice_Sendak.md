**Maurice Bernard Sendak**<sup id="cite_ref-NYT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NYT-2">[2]</a></sup> ([10 June](/wiki/10_June) [1928](/wiki/1928) - [8 May](/wiki/8_May) [2012](/wiki/2012))<sup id="cite_ref-NYT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NYT-2">[2]</a></sup> was a children's illustrator who was set to illustrate a deluxe edition of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* in the 1960s. He produced an illustration of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), which Tolkien was ambivalent toward, believing the proportions of the characters were off. After suffering a heart attack, Sendak did not return to *The Hobbit* and the edition was cancelled.<sup id="cite_ref-Hammond_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hammond-3">[3]</a></sup> This illustration was published in the *[Tolkien Calendar 2027: The Hobbit 90th Anniversary](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2027 "Tolkien Calendar 2027")*.<sup id="cite_ref-Calendar_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Calendar-4">[4]</a></sup>

An urban myth purports that Sendak produced an illustration of [Wood-elves](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood "Elves of Mirkwood"), which was sent to Tolkien with an incorrect label, identifying them as [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits), leading Tolkien to believe that Sendak did not understand the text and refusing the illustrations. There is no evidence for this illustration or encounter, however.<sup id="cite_ref-Hammond_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hammond-3">[3]</a></sup>
