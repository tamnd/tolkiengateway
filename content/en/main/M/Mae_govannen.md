<i><b>Mae govannen</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) greeting meaning "well met".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is said by [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) when receiving [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
