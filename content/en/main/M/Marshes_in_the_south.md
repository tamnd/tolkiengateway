The **marshes in the south** were likely a collection of smaller marshes or one marsh that lay along the southern border of the [Shire](../S/Shire.md) in the [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md).<sup id="cite_ref-FRHobbits_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRHobbits-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The marshes in the south were likely on or near the [River Brandywine](../B/Baranduin.md). It is unknown if the marshes in the south consisted of multiple marshes or one singular marsh. Regardless, however, they were also roughly fifty leagues (150 miles) south of the [northern moors](../N/North_Moors.md).<sup id="cite_ref-FRHobbits_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRHobbits-1">[1]</a></sup> It is possible that the marshes in the south were actually just the [Overbourn Marshes](../O/Overbourn_Marshes.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but this is unknown.

## History {#History}

In the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1601](../T/Third_Age_1601.md) the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) set out to cross the Brandywine River led by the brothers [Marcho](Marcho.md) and [Blanco](../B/Blanco.md) to live in their new home of the shire. The hobbits had previously agreed with King [Argeleb II](../A/Argeleb_II.md) on the borders of the shire, with the southern border being the marshes in the south.<sup id="cite_ref-FRHobbits_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRHobbits-1">[1]</a></sup>
