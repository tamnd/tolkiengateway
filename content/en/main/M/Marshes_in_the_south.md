The **marshes in the south** were likely a collection of smaller marshes or one marsh that lay along the southern border of the [Shire](/wiki/Shire) in the [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing).<sup id="cite_ref-FRHobbits_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRHobbits-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The marshes in the south were likely on or near the [River Brandywine](/wiki/Baranduin "Baranduin"). It is unknown if the marshes in the south consisted of multiple marshes or one singular marsh. Regardless, however, they were also roughly fifty leagues (150 miles) south of the [northern moors](/wiki/North_Moors "North Moors").<sup id="cite_ref-FRHobbits_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRHobbits-1">[1]</a></sup> It is possible that the marshes in the south were actually just the [Overbourn Marshes](/wiki/Overbourn_Marshes),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but this is unknown.

## History {#History}

In the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1601](/wiki/Third_Age_1601 "Third Age 1601") the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) set out to cross the Brandywine River led by the brothers [Marcho](/wiki/Marcho) and [Blanco](/wiki/Blanco) to live in their new home of the shire. The hobbits had previously agreed with King [Argeleb II](/wiki/Argeleb_II) on the borders of the shire, with the southern border being the marshes in the south.<sup id="cite_ref-FRHobbits_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRHobbits-1">[1]</a></sup>
