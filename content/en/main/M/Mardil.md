**Mardil Voronwë** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [1960](/wiki/Third_Age_1960 "Third Age 1960")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> – [2080](/wiki/Third_Age_2080 "Third Age 2080"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>) was Steward under the kings [Eärnil II](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II) and [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and then became the first Ruling [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor).

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2029](/wiki/Third_Age_2029 "Third Age 2029"), Mardil succeeded his father [Vorondil](/wiki/Vorondil)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> as the Steward of Gondor (a tradition begun with his grandfather [Pelendur](/wiki/Pelendur)).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>.

When Eärnil II died in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2043](/wiki/Third_Age_2043 "Third Age 2043") and Eärnur ascended to the throne, the King of [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) (the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar)) challenged him to single combat. Mardil was able to restrain Eärnur's wrath at that time, but in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2050](/wiki/Third_Age_2050 "Third Age 2050") the King of Morgul renewed his challenge, adding taunts that Eärnur had added the weakness of age to the faint heard of his youth. Mardil was powerless to prevent Eärnur from riding to Minas Morgul; he and his small escort were never heard from again.<sup id="cite_ref-Eärnur_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eärnur-7">[7]</a></sup>

Eärnur had died unmarried with no heirs. Royal descendants had become few and no claimant for the throne could be found of pure [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) blood, or whose claim all would accept, and people were afraid of a new [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife) that would devastate the kingdom. Thus, by default, Mardil began the line of Ruling Stewards of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Eärnur_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eärnur-7">[7]</a></sup>

Each new Stewards took office with the oath "to hold rod and rule in the name of the king, until he shall return". This oath would be sworn by all of the Ruling Stewards.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Mardil received the epithet *Voronwë*, which means "the Steadfast" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-MardilVoronwe_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MardilVoronwe-9">[9]</a></sup> One of his works was to introduce and establish the [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning) in Gondor. He inserted two leap-days in the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2050](/wiki/Third_Age_2050 "Third Age 2050").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> After Mardil the [Stewards of Gondor](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor) no longer took names in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (High-elven<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>), but took names in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) instead.<sup id="cite_ref-MardilVoronwe_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MardilVoronwe-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Mardil* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Devoted to the House" (of the Kings),<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> being a combination of *[mar(da)](/wiki/M%C3%A1r "Már")* ("house") and the suffix *[-(n)dil](/wiki/-ndil "-ndil")* ("friend", "lover", "devoted to").<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

His epithet *Voronwë* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "the Steadfast".<sup id="cite_ref-MardilVoronwe_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MardilVoronwe-9">[9]</a></sup> This could be the noun *voronwë* ("steadfastness")<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> used as a name.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

In the flashback, the messenger of Minas Morgul succeeds in delivering the Witch-king&#39;s second challenge by revealing the <a href="/wiki/Wights" title="Wights">wight</a> of Egalmoth the Lord of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Minas_Ithil" title="Minas Ithil">Minas Ithil</a>, Mardil&#39;s cousin, causing the Steward to turn away in grief and disgust long enough for Eärnur to accept the challenge.</dd></dl>
