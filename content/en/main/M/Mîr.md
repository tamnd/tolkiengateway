<i><b>mîr</b></i> means "a treasure, a jewel" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [MIR](/wiki/MIR)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- [miruvor](/wiki/Miruvor "Miruvor")
- [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr)
- [Aravir](/wiki/Aravir) (?) – with possibly *mîr* lenited to *-vir*.

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[mírë](/wiki/M%C3%ADr%C3%AB "Mírë")*
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) [*míriel*](</wiki/M%C3%ADriel_(Sindarin)> "Míriel (Sindarin)")

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")*, [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *mîr* is said to mean "jewel, precious thing, treasure".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
