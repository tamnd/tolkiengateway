<b><i>Minas Tirith Evening-Star</i> (Volume 8, Number 2)</b> is an issue of the journal *[Minas Tirith Evening-Star](Minas_Tirith_Evening-Star.md)*. It includes the article *An Interview with J.R.R.T.*, which is a transcription of an [interview](../../index/A/Audio_recordings_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) by [Denys Gueroult](../D/Denys_Gueroult.md).

- Note: This interview could be either the [1964 BBC Interview](../_/1964_BBC_Interview.md) or the [1965 BBC Interview](../_/1965_BBC_Interview.md). The confusion arises from the following four different accounts:
    - (1) [Pieter Collier](../P/Pieter_Collier.md) has stated that Dennis Gueroult "from the BBC Radio 4 program 'Now Read On' did broadcast the \[[1964 BBC Interview](../_/1964_BBC_Interview.md)\] for the first time in 1971".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - (2) [Jason Fisher](../J/Jason_Fisher.md) quotes from the booklet of *[The Spoken Word: British Writers](../T/The_Spoken_Word%253A_British_Writers.md)*: "\[The [1965 BBC Interview](../_/1965_BBC_Interview.md)\] was recorded for the BBC Sound Archive and was not transmitted".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - (3) *Tolkienbooks.net: An Illustrated Tolkien Bibliography* says that the interview in Minas Tirith Evening-Star Vol.8, No.2, is "a transcription of an interview with Tolkien by Denys Gueroult in January 1965. First broadcast in January 1971 as part of the BBC Radio 4 book review programme Now Read On."<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - (4) [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) write in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md): Chronology* that the recording of the [1965 BBC Interview](../_/1965_BBC_Interview.md) was "to be placed in the BBC's Recorded Programmes Permanent Library".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Concerning the [1964 BBC Interview](../_/1964_BBC_Interview.md), they write that it was "intended to be a trial run for an official interview to be placed in the BBC's Sound Archives".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
