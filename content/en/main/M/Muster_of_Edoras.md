The **Muster of Edoras** were the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) that could be mustered to war from [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) and the King's Lands, including those from [Harrowdale](../H/Harrowdale.md) in the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md). By tradition, the Muster of Edoras was under the command of the [First Marshal of the Riddermark](../F/First_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark.md). At the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), this tradition had lapsed, partly through the schemes of [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) and [Gríma Wormtongue](../G/Gríma.md). Though there was no First Marshal, the Muster did have a leader in [Elfhelm](../E/Elfhelm.md), though he did not at that time carry the title of Marshal.
