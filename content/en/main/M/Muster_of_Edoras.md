The **Muster of Edoras** were the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) that could be mustered to war from [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) and the King's Lands, including those from [Harrowdale](/wiki/Harrowdale) in the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains). By tradition, the Muster of Edoras was under the command of the [First Marshal of the Riddermark](/wiki/First_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark). At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), this tradition had lapsed, partly through the schemes of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) and [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma"). Though there was no First Marshal, the Muster did have a leader in [Elfhelm](/wiki/Elfhelm), though he did not at that time carry the title of Marshal.
