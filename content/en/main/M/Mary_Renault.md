**Mary Renault**, née Mary Challans, ([4 September](/wiki/4_September) [1905](/wiki/1905) – [13 December](/wiki/13_December) [1983](/wiki/1983)) was an English author of primarily historical novels.

Renault read English at [Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford"), graduating in 1928. She was a student of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> though probably just attending his lectures rather than being tutored by him.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Writing to Charlotte and Denis Plimmer on 8 February 1967, Tolkien says that he has been "recently deeply engaged in the books" of Renault, and mentions in particular her works <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_King_Must_Die" title="wikipedia:The King Must Die">The King Must Die</a></i> and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bull_from_the_Sea" title="wikipedia:The Bull from the Sea">The Bull from the Sea</a></i> (both about <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theseus" title="wikipedia:Theseus">Theseus</a>). Tolkien also tells the Plimmers that Renault has written Tolkien a card of appreciation, "perhaps the piece of 'Fan-mail' that gives me most pleasure".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> And writing to his secretary [Joy Hill](/wiki/Joy_Hill) on 25 August 1967, he mentions the fan-letter again (received "not long ago"), and tells Hill that he wonders how Renault would answer a particular question.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
