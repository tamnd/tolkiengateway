**Mungo Baggins** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Mungo was the eldest child of [Balbo Baggins](/wiki/Balbo_Baggins) and [Berylla Boffin](/wiki/Berylla_Boffin). He married [Laura Grubb](/wiki/Laura_Grubb), and they had five children together: [Bungo](/wiki/Bungo_Baggins "Bungo Baggins"), [Belba](/wiki/Belba_Baggins "Belba Baggins"), [Longo](/wiki/Longo_Baggins "Longo Baggins"), [Linda](/wiki/Linda_Baggins "Linda Baggins") and [Bingo](/wiki/Bingo_Baggins "Bingo Baggins").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
