<i><b>Maps of Middle-earth</b></i> is an accessory for [Decipher](/wiki/Decipher)'s *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game)*.

While the 32-page guide mostly concerns reprinted material, all the maps were newly produced for the game by [Daniel Reeve](/wiki/Daniel_Reeve). While they came folded with the release, members of the [The Lord of the Rings Fan Club](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Fan_Club) (also run by Decipher) could buy them in tubes.<sup id="cite_ref-matthew_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-matthew-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Drawn by renowned illustrator <a href="/wiki/Daniel_Reeve" title="Daniel Reeve">Daniel Reeve</a> – the official cartographer for The Lord of the Rings films – the six 17&#34; x 22&#34; archival quality collector&#39;s maps cover <a href="/wiki/The_Shire" title="The Shire">The Shire</a>, West <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>, East Gondor, <a href="/wiki/Rohan" title="Rohan">Rohan</a>, <a href="/wiki/Eregion" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a>. The boxed set includes the six folded maps and a 32-page guide to the lands of <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a>, including keys to each of the maps.
</p>
</blockquote>

## Background {#Background}

The map of West Gondor shows <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Aerost&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aerost (page does not exist)">Aerost</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Annon_Wain&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Annon Wain (page does not exist)">Annon Wain</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ann%C3%BAlond&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Annúlond (page does not exist)">Annúlond</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Iantras&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Iantras (page does not exist)">Iantras</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=L%C3%B3most&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lómost (page does not exist)">Lómost</a>, [Lond Galen](/wiki/Lond_Galen), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Pinnornost&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Pinnornost (page does not exist)">Pinnornost</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rast_Rhaen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rast Rhaen (page does not exist)">Rast Rhaen</a>, and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Saerthondost&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Saerthondost (page does not exist)">Saerthondost</a>. These locations had first appeared in *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* at the same location.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>. The same is the case for <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Eithel_T%C3%BArin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Eithel Túrin (page does not exist)">Eithel Túrin</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Fanuilond&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fanuilond (page does not exist)">Fanuilond</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Methir&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Methir (page does not exist)">Methir</a>, and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Minas_Brethil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Minas Brethil (page does not exist)">Minas Brethil</a> on the map of East Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> The name of the river <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lh%C3%BAchiril&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lhúchiril (page does not exist)">Lhúchiril</a> in West Gondor was previously used for a river in Arnor.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

[Seregost](/wiki/Seregost), a location depicted on the map of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), would see further use in later adaptations.
