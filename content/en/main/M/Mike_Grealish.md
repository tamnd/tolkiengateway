**Michael Grealish** (born in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gisborne,_New_Zealand" title="wikipedia:Gisborne, New Zealand">Gisborne, New Zealand</a>) is a Kiwi film technician who worked at [Wētā Workshop](/wiki/W%C4%93t%C4%81_Workshop) from 1998 to 2008.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He served as miniature technician for [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and as leather craftsman for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.

While senior leather technician, when [Decipher](/wiki/Decipher), with [Wētā Workshop](/wiki/W%C4%93t%C4%81_Workshop), began producing *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game)* cards depicting aspects omitted from [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") as part of the "Book Cards" label, he portrayed [Erkenbrand](/wiki/Erkenbrand)<sup id="cite_ref-Book_Cards_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Book_Cards-2">[2]</a></sup> in three cards.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> He wore the costume of [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred), as Weta reasoned so little of the armour was seen in the film it would be hard to notice.<sup id="cite_ref-Book_Cards_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Book_Cards-2">[2]</a></sup>
