The **Master-stone** was the chief of all the *[palantíri](../P/Palantíri.md)*, both in [Aman](../A/Aman.md) and [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md), which was held in the [Tower of Avallónë](../T/Tower_of_Avallónë.md) on the [Lonely Isle](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md). It was said that the Seeing-stone in [Elostirion](../E/Elostirion.md) in the [Tower Hills](../T/Tower_Hills.md) could be used to look back along the [Straight Road](../S/Straight_Road.md) and glimpse the Tower.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Stone of Avallónë is variously named the "'Masterstone'" or *Master-stone*. There was also a 'Master Stone' of [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md), the *palantír* of [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md), but this was a lesser Stone than the Masterstone in Avallónë, having power only over other *palantíri* in [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
