The **Master-stone** was the chief of all the *[palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")*, both in [Aman](/wiki/Aman) and [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), which was held in the [Tower of Avallónë](/wiki/Tower_of_Avall%C3%B3n%C3%AB) on the [Lonely Isle](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa "Tol Eressëa"). It was said that the Seeing-stone in [Elostirion](/wiki/Elostirion) in the [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills) could be used to look back along the [Straight Road](/wiki/Straight_Road) and glimpse the Tower.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Stone of Avallónë is variously named the "'Masterstone'" or *Master-stone*. There was also a 'Master Stone' of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), the *palantír* of [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath), but this was a lesser Stone than the Masterstone in Avallónë, having power only over other *palantíri* in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
