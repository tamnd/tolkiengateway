<b>*mēnie</b> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word variously rendered as "determination"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:94</sup>, "impulse"<sup id="cite_ref-impulse_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-impulse-2">[2]</a></sup>, or "device" (in the sense of "something devised or contrived for bringing about some end or result”, or "purpose, intention”)<sup id="cite_ref-impulse_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-impulse-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:note 9</sup>

- [Ermenië](../E/Ermenië.md)

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is thought to be the gerundial form of the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [MEN](MEN~35108.md) ("move", "proceed")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
