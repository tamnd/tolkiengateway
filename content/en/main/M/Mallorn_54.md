<b><i>Mallorn</i> 54</b> is the fifty-forth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in May [2013](../_/2013.md). This is the first issue to return to its annual format and the last issue to be edited by [Henry Gee](../H/Henry_Gee.md).
