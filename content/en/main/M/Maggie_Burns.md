**Maggie Burns** ([1954](../_/1954.md)–[2012](../_/2012.md)) was a British writer and librarian (at the Local Studies and History department of the Central Library in Birmingham, England). Her main interest in Tolkien studies was on the young [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s life in [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md), with a special focus on Tolkien's time at [King Edward's School](../K/King_Edward's_School.md) and the history of the Tolkien and Suffield families. Burns was working on a book about the Tolkien and Suffield families at the time of her death.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
