**Maggie Burns** ([1954](/wiki/1954)–[2012](/wiki/2012)) was a British writer and librarian (at the Local Studies and History department of the Central Library in Birmingham, England). Her main interest in Tolkien studies was on the young [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s life in [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham), with a special focus on Tolkien's time at [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School) and the history of the Tolkien and Suffield families. Burns was working on a book about the Tolkien and Suffield families at the time of her death.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
