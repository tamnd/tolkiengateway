**Mythcon 37** was the thirty-seventh annual [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) conference.

- **Theme:** "The Map &amp; The Territory: Maps and Landscapes in Fantasy"
- **Guests of Honor:** Lois McMaster Bujold (fantasy and SF author) and [Amy Sturgis](/wiki/Amy_H._Sturgis "Amy H. Sturgis") (C.S. Lewis and Native American fantasy scholar)
- **Chairman:** [Janet Croft](/wiki/Janet_Croft)
