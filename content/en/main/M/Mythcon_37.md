**Mythcon 37** was the thirty-seventh annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme:** "The Map &amp; The Territory: Maps and Landscapes in Fantasy"
- **Guests of Honor:** Lois McMaster Bujold (fantasy and SF author) and [Amy Sturgis](../A/Amy_H._Sturgis.md) (C.S. Lewis and Native American fantasy scholar)
- **Chairman:** [Janet Croft](../J/Janet_Croft.md)
