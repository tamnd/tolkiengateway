<i><b>miqe(e)</b></i> is a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) noun meaning "a kiss".<sup id="cite_ref-PE12_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE12-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested that the form *miquë* (retaining the same meaning) could be valid [Neo-Quenya](/wiki/Neo-Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") MIQI ("kiss")<sup id="cite_ref-PE12_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE12-1">[1]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}

- Qenya *[miq-](/wiki/Miq- "Miq-")* ("to kiss", verb)
