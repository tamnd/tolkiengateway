The **massánië** ([S](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin"). <i><b>besain</b></i>) was the name given to an [elven queen](/wiki/Elves "Elves") that kept and distributed [lembas](/wiki/Lembas "Lembas").<sup id="cite_ref-Lembas_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lembas-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the making of lembas, only the [Yavannildi](/wiki/Yavannildi) knew the process and were allowed to handle the grain from the ear to the wafer. As the grain came from Yavanna, only the queen, or the highest among the elven-women of any people, great or small, could keep and distribute the lembas.

The only queens known to share lembas were [Melian](/wiki/Melian) (to [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) and [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin)) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) (to the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring)).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Massánië* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Lady, breadgiver", from *massa* ("bread") + *[anta](/wiki/Anta "Anta")-* (gerund, "give"). The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *besain* means the same.<sup id="cite_ref-Lembas_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lembas-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

According to the *[Oxford English Dictionary](/wiki/Oxford_English_Dictionary)*, "lady" comes from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) <i><a class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/hl%C3%A6fdige" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">hlæfdige</a></i>, from words meaning "loaf" and "kneader".
