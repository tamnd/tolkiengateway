The **Mathom-house** was a museum-like building in [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving) where the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) collected and displayed "[mathoms](/wiki/Mathoms "Mathoms")": items and trinkets for which they had no particular use. It was known to contain a store of [weapons](/wiki/Category:Weapons "Category:Weapons"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and for a time also (between [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") and [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001")) it held [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' [Mithril-coat](/wiki/Mithril-coat).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
