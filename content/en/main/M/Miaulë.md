**Miaulë** was the name of [Tevildo's](/wiki/Tevildo "Tevildo") cook in "[The Tale of Tinúviel](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Tin%C3%BAviel)", according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-tales_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tales-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Miaulë was the cook working within Tevildo's great kitchens, which were widely vaulted, with at least one great fire. She supervised the work of Tevildo's scullions.<sup id="cite_ref-tales_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tales-1">[1]</a></sup>

Upon meeting [Beren](/wiki/Beren) for the first time, Tevildo made him a scullion under the supervision of Miaulë after Beren failed a difficult test. Miaulë's supervision over him was miserable enough that Beren eventually wished that he never saw [Tinúviel](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien "Lúthien").<sup id="cite_ref-tales_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tales-1">[1]</a></sup>

Some time later, when [Umuiyan](/wiki/Umuiyan) led Tinúviel up the cliff, Tevildo brought her to a dining chamber next to the great kitchens of his halls. Tinúviel was able to catch a glimpse of Beren and many cats, likely including Miaulë. When Tinúviel began talking to Tevildo, her voice was heard by Beren, causing him to accidentally drop an unspecified amount of metal and earthware vessels. Miaulë gave him sound scratches as a punishment.<sup id="cite_ref-tales_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tales-1">[1]</a></sup>

An hour later, after the death of [Oikeroi](/wiki/Oikeroi) in the [Withered Dale](/wiki/Withered_Dale), Tevildo tells [Huan](/wiki/Huan) that Beren was likely being punished with Miaulë's scratches, due to the incident earlier.<sup id="cite_ref-tales_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tales-1">[1]</a></sup>

The ultimate fate of Miaulë is not known.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> name *Miaulë* is described by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) alongside *Miaugion* and *Meoita* as "engagingly [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish)-feline names".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is, according to [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) and [Christopher Gilson](/wiki/Christopher_Gilson), likely cognate with [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) *miaulin* ("she-cat"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the feminine form of *miog* ("cat").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
