The **Cambridge Tolkien Society**, also known as the **Minas Tirith Smial**, is a [Cambridge](../C/Cambridge.md)-based smial of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md). The smial was founded in [1983](../_/1983.md) by students of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge" title="wikipedia:University of Cambridge">university</a>, one of them being [Julian C. Bradfield](../J/Julian_C._Bradfield.md). They publish the journal <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Anor_(journal)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Anor (journal) (page does not exist)">Anor</a></i> twice a year, and hold weekly meetings.
