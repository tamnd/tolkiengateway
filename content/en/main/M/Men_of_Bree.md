The **Men of Bree** were [Bree-landers](../B/Bree-landers.md), specifically the [Men](Men.md) who lived around the [Bree-land](../B/Bree-land.md), especially the village of [Bree](../B/Bree.md).

## Origins {#Origins}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a href="Middle_Men.md" title="Middle Men">Middle Men</a></div></div>

Their own tales acknowledged that they descended from the first Men who wandered to the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../W/West.md" title="West">West</a> in the late [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) but their ancestors did not reach [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-sign_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sign-1">[1]</a></sup> In the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) they inhabited the northern [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md), in the land that would be later known as [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md); they were a race of [Pre-Númenóreans](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md), related to the [Men of the Mountains](../O/Oathbreakers.md) and the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md).

During the [Dark Years](../D/Dark_Years.md), some of this folk migrated northward to the dales south of the [Misty Mountains](Misty_Mountains.md). Eventually they continued to the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md) before settling the region around the tall, wooded [Bree-hill](../B/Bree-hill.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Men_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Men-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/M/Matěj_Čadil_-_Bree-land.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/9a/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Bree-land.jpg/300px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Bree-land.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="218" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/9a/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Bree-land.jpg/450px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Bree-land.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/9a/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Bree-land.jpg/600px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Bree-land.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2800" data-file-height="2034"/></a><figcaption><a href="Matěj_Čadil.md" title="Matěj Čadil">Matěj Čadil</a> - <i>Bree-land</i></figcaption></figure>

According to their own tales, the Bree-men were there since the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md), they survived the turmoils, they were still there when the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) came, and would remain for the following millennia.<sup id="cite_ref-sign_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the Kingdom of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) was founded, Bree-land lay inside its borders, and on the road that led to [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md); as such the Men of Bree became subject of Arnor, and adopted [Westron](../W/Westron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Men_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Men-2">[2]</a></sup> Around the Bree-hill, the Men of Bree established the settlement of [Bree](../B/Bree.md), [Staddle](../S/Staddle.md), [Archet](../A/Archet.md) and [Combe](../C/Combe.md).

In about [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1300](../T/Third_Age_1300.md), their land was visited by the [Halflings](../H/Halflings.md) who were fleeing the encroaching darkness to the east.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> They formed a unique society in all [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md) where Men and Hobbits lived beside one another. Thus they were affectionally called the "Big Folk" and the "Little Folk".<sup id="cite_ref-sign_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Men of Bree endured the [Fall of Arnor](../B/Battle_of_Fornost.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md)) and continued living independently, like before its existence.<sup id="cite_ref-sign_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) some Men of Bree like [Bill Ferny](../B/Bill_Ferny.md) and [Harry Goatleaf](../H/Harry_Goatleaf.md) were spies of the [Ringwraiths](../N/Nazgûl.md) and later they joined [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md)'s [ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md).<sup id="cite_ref-sign_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sign-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Culture {#Culture}

In appearance, these [Men](Men.md) were in stature short and broad, and often brown-haired. They were cheerful and provincial. In contrast to other Men, those of Bree were friendly to the other races, Hobbits, [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) and [Elves](../E/Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-sign_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

They seem to have maintained a curious tradition of taking their names from plants and herbs; families of the Men of Bree included [Appledore](../A/Appledore_Family.md), [Ferny](../F/Ferny_Family.md), [Goatleaf](../G/Goatleaf_Family.md), [Heathertoes](../H/Heathertoes_Family.md), [Rushlight](../R/Rushlight_Family.md), [Thistlewool](../T/Thistlewool_Family.md) and [Butterbur](../B/Butterbur_Family.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
