The **Men of Bree** were [Bree-landers](/wiki/Bree-landers), specifically the [Men](/wiki/Men) who lived around the [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land), especially the village of [Bree](/wiki/Bree).

## Origins {#Origins}

Their own tales acknowledged that they descended from the first Men who wandered to the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/West" title="West">West</a> in the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) but their ancestors did not reach [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-sign_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sign-1">[1]</a></sup> In the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) they inhabited the northern [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains), in the land that would be later known as [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon); they were a race of [Pre-Númenóreans](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans), related to the [Men of the Mountains](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") and the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings).

During the [Dark Years](/wiki/Dark_Years), some of this folk migrated northward to the dales south of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). Eventually they continued to the [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs) before settling the region around the tall, wooded [Bree-hill](/wiki/Bree-hill).<sup id="cite_ref-Men_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Men-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Bree-land.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/9/9a/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Bree-land.jpg/300px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Bree-land.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="218" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/9/9a/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Bree-land.jpg/450px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Bree-land.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/9/9a/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Bree-land.jpg/600px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Bree-land.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2800" data-file-height="2034"/></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil" title="Matěj Čadil">Matěj Čadil</a> - <i>Bree-land</i></figcaption></figure>

According to their own tales, the Bree-men were there since the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days), they survived the turmoils, they were still there when the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) came, and would remain for the following millennia.<sup id="cite_ref-sign_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the Kingdom of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) was founded, Bree-land lay inside its borders, and on the road that led to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor); as such the Men of Bree became subject of Arnor, and adopted [Westron](/wiki/Westron).<sup id="cite_ref-Men_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Men-2">[2]</a></sup> Around the Bree-hill, the Men of Bree established the settlement of [Bree](/wiki/Bree), [Staddle](/wiki/Staddle), [Archet](/wiki/Archet) and [Combe](/wiki/Combe).

In about [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300"), their land was visited by the [Halflings](/wiki/Halflings) who were fleeing the encroaching darkness to the east.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> They formed a unique society in all [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) where Men and Hobbits lived beside one another. Thus they were affectionally called the "Big Folk" and the "Little Folk".<sup id="cite_ref-sign_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Men of Bree endured the [Fall of Arnor](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost "Battle of Fornost") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975")) and continued living independently, like before its existence.<sup id="cite_ref-sign_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) some Men of Bree like [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny) and [Harry Goatleaf](/wiki/Harry_Goatleaf) were spies of the [Ringwraiths](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") and later they joined [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s [ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians").<sup id="cite_ref-sign_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sign-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Culture {#Culture}

In appearance, these [Men](/wiki/Men) were in stature short and broad, and often brown-haired. They were cheerful and provincial. In contrast to other Men, those of Bree were friendly to the other races, Hobbits, [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) and [Elves](/wiki/Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-sign_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

They seem to have maintained a curious tradition of taking their names from plants and herbs; families of the Men of Bree included [Appledore](/wiki/Appledore_Family "Appledore Family"), [Ferny](/wiki/Ferny_Family "Ferny Family"), [Goatleaf](/wiki/Goatleaf_Family "Goatleaf Family"), [Heathertoes](/wiki/Heathertoes_Family "Heathertoes Family"), [Rushlight](/wiki/Rushlight_Family "Rushlight Family"), [Thistlewool](/wiki/Thistlewool_Family "Thistlewool Family") and [Butterbur](/wiki/Butterbur_Family "Butterbur Family").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
