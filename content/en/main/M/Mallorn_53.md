<b><i>Mallorn</i> 53</b> is the fifty-third issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s biannual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Spring [2012](../_/2012.md). This was the last issue to be published biannually; the journal returned to its annual format with [*Mallorn* 54](Mallorn_54.md) coming out a year later in Spring [2013](../_/2013.md).
