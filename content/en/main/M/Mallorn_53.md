<b><i>Mallorn</i> 53</b> is the fifty-third issue of the [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s biannual journal *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")*, published in Spring [2012](/wiki/2012). This was the last issue to be published biannually; the journal returned to its annual format with [*Mallorn* 54](/wiki/Mallorn_54 "Mallorn 54") coming out a year later in Spring [2013](/wiki/2013).
