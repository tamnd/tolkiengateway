**Michel Delving** was the chief town of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It was located in the [White Downs](/wiki/White_Downs) that lay within the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing) on the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road), perhaps south to its smaller counterpart, the [Little Delving](/wiki/Little_Delving).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Although located within a hilly region the town had many above-ground houses of wood, brick, or stone.

This large town was unofficially considered to be more or less the Hobbits' "capital".<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Mayor of Michel Delving](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving) (or of the Shire) was the only real official in the Shire, under whom were attached the offices of [Postmaster](/wiki/Postmaster) and [First Shirriff](/wiki/First_Shirriff) for the entire region.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The [Town Hole](/wiki/Town_Hole),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the museum known as the [Mathom-house](/wiki/Mathom-house),<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-2">[2]</a></sup> as well as the storage tunnels later known as [Lockholes](/wiki/Lockholes),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> could be found in Michel Delving.

During the occupation of the Shire, the [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) converted the storage tunnels into prisons. They also used the tunnels (as well as sheds and deserted holes) to store great amounts of foods and beer. On [4 November](/wiki/4_November) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) rode to Michel Delving and released the prisoners from the Lockholes. The Hobbits also appropriated the Ruffians's stores.<sup id="cite_ref-havens_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-havens-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Its name means simply "Large Excavation", being derived from a possible [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") *Micel Delfing*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

*Michel* is derived from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/micel" title="wiktionary:micel">micel</a></i>, meaning "great". Since in Old English *c* is normally pronounced as modern *ch*, here most probably the same pronunciation is kept, while its spelling is modernized. The same element also appears as *mickle*, as in [Mickleburg](/wiki/Mickleburg).
