<b><i>Mythlore</i> 74</b> (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/mythlore/vol19/iss4/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Volume 19, Issue 4</a>) is an issue of the *[Mythlore](Mythlore.md)* journal, published by the [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

#### Articles {#Articles}

- GoodKnight, Glen. “Is Children's Literature Childish?”
- Hood, Gwyneth. “Nature and Technology: Angelic and Sacrificial Strategies in Tolkien's *The Lord of the Rings*.”
- Feimer, Joel N. “Biblical Typology in LeGuin's *The Eye of the Heron*: Character, Structure, and Theme.”
- Branson, David A. “Arthurian Elements in *The Hideous Strength*.”
- Johnson, Michael. “An Ozdessey in Plato.”
- Larsen, Corinne. “The Fourth Branch of the Mabinogi: Structural Analysis Illuminates Character Motivation.”
- Canaan, Howard. “All Hell into His Knapsack: The Spirit of Play in Two Fairy Tales.”
- Hostetter, Carl F., and Wynne, Patrick. “Stone Towers.”
- Lindow, Sandra J. “Touch Magic: The Importance of Teaching Folktales to Emotionally Disturbed, Disabled Readers.”

#### Features {#Features}

- GoodKnight, Glen. Editorial Introduction: In This Issue.
- Christopher, Joe R., and Wayne G. Hammond. An Inklings Bibliography (50).
- Kondratiev, Alexei. “Tales Newly Told.”
- Letters.
- DiSante, Paula. “Artist's Comments.”

#### Reviews {#Reviews}

- *Tolkien's World: Painting of Middle-earth.* Published by HarperCollins. Reviewed by Glen GoodKnight.
- *1994 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar.* Art by Michael Kaluta. Reviewed by Glen GoodKnight.
- *Celtic Calendar 1994*. Art by Patrick Wynne, Laurie Frasier Manifold, and Victoria Palmer. Notes by Alexei Kondratiev. Reviewed by Glen GoodKnight.
- *Dorothy L. Sayers*. Barbara Reynolds. Reviewed by Nancy-Lou Patterson.
- *The Painted Hallway.* Nancy-Lou Patterson. Reviewed by Gracia Fay Ellwood.
