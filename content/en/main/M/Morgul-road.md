The **Morgul-road**,<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> also referred to as the **Morgul-way**,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the **wraith-road**,<sup id="cite_ref-Stairs_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stairs-4">[4]</a></sup> or the **Osgiliath Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-oldname_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oldname-5">[5]</a></sup> was a road in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).

## Course {#Course}

The Morgul-road ran from [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) slightly north of and roughly parallel to the river [Morgulduin](/wiki/Morgulduin)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> to the [Cross-roads](/wiki/Cross-roads) where it crossed the [road](/wiki/Southward_Road "Southward Road") that ran from the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) south to [Harad](/wiki/Harad).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Map_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-8">[8]</a></sup>

It continued eastward for a short distance until it approached a great shoulder of rock at the western end of the mountains on the north side of the [Morgul Vale](/wiki/Morgul_Vale) where it ran southwards along the shoulder until its end where the road turned eastwards again into the Morgul Vale and climbed steeply. After a short time it turned south-eastwards to the bottom of the valley where it crossed a bridge over the river [Morgulduin](/wiki/Morgulduin), and wound up to the gate of the city of [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul), which was some way further east high up on a mountain shoulder on the southern side of the valley.<sup id="cite_ref-Ungol_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ungol-9">[9]</a></sup>

The Morgul-road passed the gate of the city of [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) under the shadow of its walls and went up and up<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-10">[10]</a></sup> east through the valley<sup id="cite_ref-Map_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-8">[8]</a></sup> to the great ravine at the head of the Morgul Vale<sup id="cite_ref-Stairs_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stairs-4">[4]</a></sup> over a dark pass, the Morgul Pass.<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-10">[10]</a></sup> It then descended to the valley between the Ephel Dúath and the Morgai, passed over a bridge of stone,<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-1">[1]</a></sup> and through a jagged rift in the Morgai out into [Gorgoroth](/wiki/Gorgoroth) and on to [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Although it was safer than the more northerly and narrow [Pass of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol "Cirith Ungol") due to avoiding the threat posed by [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob), it was the slower way from Minas Morgul over the mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Choices_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Choices-12">[12]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The road was constructed in the high days of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) to connect [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) with [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul), when they were still called the **Minas Anor** ("Tower of the Sun") and **Minas Ithil** ("Tower of the Moon").<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> It is possible that the part of the road from Minas Morgul over the Morgul Pass to the point below the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol) was constructed when Gondor built the latter tower after the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) to watch [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

On March 9 [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") at dusk<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) reached the Morgul-road at the [Cross-roads](/wiki/Cross-roads).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> On the next day<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> they continue on the road before it crosses the bridge over the Morgulduin and decide to leave the road to climb the [Stairs of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol) to avoid continuing on to Minas Morgul and risking detection.<sup id="cite_ref-Ungol_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ungol-9">[9]</a></sup>

On March 18 and 19 [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Host_of_the_West" title="Host of the West">Host of the West</a> marched on the road from Minas Tirith to the Morgul Vale.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In notes on the chronology **Osgiliath road** is mentioned as another name for the Morgul-road.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Going east from the Crossroads, the Morgul-road turns somewhat to the south and passes through &#34;Annon Morgul&#34;, a great guarded gate at the entrance to the <a href="/wiki/Morgul_Vale" title="Morgul Vale">Morgul Vale</a>. It crosses Morgulduin at the &#34;Gúliant&#34; bridge, which is destroyed by <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> during the passage of the <a href="/wiki/Army_of_the_West" title="Army of the West">Army of the West</a> to make any surpsise assault by the Enemy more difficult. After passing <a href="/wiki/Minas_Morgul" title="Minas Morgul">Minas Morgul</a>, the road begins to climb high into the valley and eventually crosses back onto the northern shore of Morgulduin by a smaller, unnamed bridge. At the <a href="/wiki/Morgul_Pass" title="Morgul Pass">Morgul Pass</a> the road is guarded by a great gate &#34;Fennas Gost&#34;. It the begins to descend down into the valley of &#34;Lhingris&#34; in the <a href="/wiki/Plateau_of_Gorgoroth" title="Plateau of Gorgoroth">Plateau of Gorgoroth</a> and crosses over to the <a href="/wiki/Morgai" title="Morgai">Morgai</a> by a great bridge &#34;Iant Angos&#34;. The road eventually arrives into the plain of &#34;Talath Urui&#34; where it splits into two arms: going north and east of the <a href="/wiki/Mount_Doom" title="Mount Doom">Mount Doom</a>, both eventually lead to <a href="/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr" title="Barad-dûr">Barad-dûr</a>.</dd></dl>
