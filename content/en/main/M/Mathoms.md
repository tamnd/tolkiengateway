**Mathoms** was an old word of the [hobbit-dialect](../H/Hobbitish.md), not recorded as being in use outside [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). It was used to refer to "trinkets" or any item that Hobbits had no particular immediate use for, but were unwilling to throw away.

Mathoms were many of the presents that passed from hand to hand by the [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md) - an important part of their culture - and whose owners did not wish to throw them away. Hobbit houses tended to be crowded with mathoms.

Many mathoms were weapons, useless as there hadn't been any conflict since the [Battle of Greenfields](../B/Battle_of_Greenfields.md) ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1147](../T/Third_Age_2747.md)); weapons were seen mostly as trophies, ornamenting walls or hearths, or stored in the [Mathom-house](Mathom-house.md), a "museum" in [Michel Delving](Michel_Delving.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Mathom* is a word invented by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), constructed from an obsolete [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) word *máðm* "treasure, precious thing".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is used as a rendering of the original [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) word <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kast&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kast (page does not exist)">kast</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
