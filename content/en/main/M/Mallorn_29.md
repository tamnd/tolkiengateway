<b><i>Mallorn</i> 29</b> is the twenty-ninth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s annual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in August [1992](../_/1992.md).
