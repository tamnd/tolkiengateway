**Morgul-wounds** were inflicted by the weapons of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s greatest servants — or, at least, by those of the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl). They carried dark enchantments that cursed the wounds suffered by their victims. The wounds themselves varied; those suffered by Steward [Boromir](</wiki/Boromir_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Boromir (Steward of Gondor)") in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2475](/wiki/Third_Age_2475 "Third Age 2475") left him in crippling pain and shortened his life<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> (he died in [2489](/wiki/Third_Age_2489 "Third Age 2489")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>), while the wound taken by [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") on [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> on [6 October](/wiki/6_October) [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018")<sup id="cite_ref-Great_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-4">[4]</a></sup> threatened to make him a wraith.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On [13 March](/wiki/13_March) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), when [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) was retreating to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-4">[4]</a></sup> he held at bay a mounted soldier of [Harad](/wiki/Harad) as the Nazgûl swirled overhead. Faramir was struck down by a flying dart - it was feared that it was from the Nazgûl and had created a Morgul-wound.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> However, after the great battle [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) examined the wound and found it to be healing. This proved that Faramir had not suffered a Morgul-wound. It had been believed that it was such though due to Faramir's sickness and fever, but Aragorn diagnosed Faramir's condition as due to weariness, grief, wounding, and the [Black Breath](/wiki/Black_Breath).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
