**Morgul-wounds** were inflicted by the weapons of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s greatest servants — or, at least, by those of the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md). They carried dark enchantments that cursed the wounds suffered by their victims. The wounds themselves varied; those suffered by Steward [Boromir](<../B/Boromir_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2475](../T/Third_Age_2475.md) left him in crippling pain and shortened his life<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> (he died in [2489](../T/Third_Age_2489.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>), while the wound taken by [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) on [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> on [6 October](../_/6_October.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-4">[4]</a></sup> threatened to make him a wraith.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On [13 March](../_/13_March.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), when [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) was retreating to [Minas Tirith](Minas_Tirith.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-4">[4]</a></sup> he held at bay a mounted soldier of [Harad](../H/Harad.md) as the Nazgûl swirled overhead. Faramir was struck down by a flying dart - it was feared that it was from the Nazgûl and had created a Morgul-wound.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> However, after the great battle [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) examined the wound and found it to be healing. This proved that Faramir had not suffered a Morgul-wound. It had been believed that it was such though due to Faramir's sickness and fever, but Aragorn diagnosed Faramir's condition as due to weariness, grief, wounding, and the [Black Breath](../B/Black_Breath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
