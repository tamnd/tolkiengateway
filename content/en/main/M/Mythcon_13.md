**Mythcon 13** was the thirteenth annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme:** "Celtic Con": The Celtic Influence on Fantasy Literature
- **Special Guests:** Nancy-Lou Patterson (keynote speaker), [Marion Zimmer Bradley](Marion_Zimmer_Bradley.md) (fantasy author), [Tim Kirk](../T/Tim_Kirk.md) (artist), Katherine Kurtz (fantasy author), Kathryn Lindskoog (Lewis scholar), Ataniel Noel (Tolkien scholar), Paul Edwin Zimmer (fantasy author), [Bernie Zuber](../B/Bernie_Zuber.md) (founder Tolkien Fellowship)
- **Chairman:** Lisa Cowan
