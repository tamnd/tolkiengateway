**Mithlond, Göteborgs Tolkiensällskap** is the Swedish Tolkien Society of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothenburg" title="wikipedia:Gothenburg">Gothenburg</a>. It was founded in [1981](/wiki/1981), hived off from [Forodrim](/wiki/Forodrim). The association publishes <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Elostirion_(magazine)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Elostirion (magazine) (page does not exist)">Elostirion</a></i>.

Mithlond is composed of several guilds, each devoted to a specific task. These inlude languages, art and handicraft, gaming, food, etc.
