The **Men of Dale**,<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>, **Dale-men**<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> or **Bardings**<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> were Men who dwelt in [Dale](/wiki/Dale).

## History {#History}

There was a folk of [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen) by the Mountain [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor) already by [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1856](/wiki/Third_Age_1856 "Third Age 1856"). That year, that folk was joined by other Northmen who fled from the wide plains between [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) and the [River Running](/wiki/River_Running) after the disastrous defeat of the [Battle of the Plains](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Plains).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

More [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen) gradually came up the River Running from the south after King [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) reestablished the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) in Erebor<sup id="cite_ref-Party_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-6">[6]</a></sup> in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2590](/wiki/Third_Age_2590 "Third Age 2590")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>.

There those Northmen built the town of Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-6">[6]</a></sup> The town shared in the prosperity of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) and it was governed by the [Lord of Dale](/wiki/Lord_of_Dale).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770"), during the time of [Girion](/wiki/Girion), [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) descended upon the Dwarf-kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> The Men fielded warriors against him but could not prevent the [Sack of Erebor](/wiki/Sack_of_Erebor). After that, the dragon would crawl out of the [Front Gate](/wiki/Front_Gate) of the Mountain by night and carry away people (especially maidens) to eat. The remaining population soon fled and the deserted city fell into ruins.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-6">[6]</a></sup>

Many went to dwell on the [Long Lake](/wiki/Long_Lake) with the [Lake-men](/wiki/Lake-men), including the last descendant of Girion, [Bard](/wiki/Bard). After the town of Dale was rebuilt by Bard and Bard became the [King of Dale](/wiki/King_of_Dale) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2944](/wiki/Third_Age_2944 "Third Age 2944"),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> its people were known as [Bardings](/wiki/Bardings)<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-1">[1]</a></sup>.

## Culture {#Culture}

The Men of Dale flourished with trade with the [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor).

They spoke [Westron](/wiki/Westron) and their own [language](/wiki/Language_of_Dale "Language of Dale").
