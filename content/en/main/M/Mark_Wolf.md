**Mark J. P. Wolf** is an American author and Professor in Communication. He has written one book about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien): *[Of Words and Worlds: The Imagination and Subcreation of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Of_Words_and_Worlds "Of Words and Worlds")*, which was almost published by Cumberland Press, which went under right before the book came out. He has, however, published *Building Imaginary Worlds; The Theory and History of Subcreation* (2012), and is the editor of *The Routledge Companion to Imaginary Worlds* (2017) and the anthologies *Revisiting Imaginary Worlds* (2017), *Exploring Imaginary Worlds* (2020), *World-Builders on World-Building* (2020), and *Navigating Imaginary Worlds* (forthcoming).
