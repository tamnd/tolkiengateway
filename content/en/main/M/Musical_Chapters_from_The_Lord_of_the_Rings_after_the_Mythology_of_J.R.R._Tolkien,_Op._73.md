*Musical Chapters from The Lord of the Rings after the mythology of J. R. R. Tolkien* is an operatic cycle by composer [Paul Corfield Godfrey](/wiki/Paul_Corfield_Godfrey), written between the years 1967-2023. The composer originally planned a cycle of eleven compositions, two of which were to be devoted to *The Hobbit.* Initially, Godfrey completed only a few of the planned eleven pieces. When the recordings of [The Fall of Gondolin, Op.49 (Opera)](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin,_Op.49_(Opera)>), [Beren and Lúthien, Op. 47 (Opera)](</wiki/Beren_and_L%C3%BAthien,_Op._47_(Opera)>), and [The Children of Húrin, Op. 48 (Opera)](</wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin,_Op._48_(Opera)>) were released and the recordings of [Fëanor, Op. 46 (Opera)](</wiki/F%C3%ABanor,_Op._46_(Opera)>) and [The War of Wrath, Op. 71 (Opera)](</wiki/The_War_of_Wrath,_Op._71_(Opera)>) were being prepared by Volante Opera Productions, Godfrey resumed his work and completed the cycle. The final work included six compositions, rather than the planned eleven, with three dedicated to *The Lord of the Rings* and two to *The Hobbit*.

In 2024, the Tolkien Estate granted the rights to the composer to use the Tolkien texts from [*The Lord of the Rings*](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Lord of the Rings") and [*The Adventures of Tom Bombadil*](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil "The Adventures of Tom Bombadil") to write a version of "[The Sea-Bell](/wiki/The_Sea-Bell)."

This cycle is connected to Godfrey's other works, most notably [*Epic Scenes from The Silmarillion after the mythology of J. R. R. Tolkien (Complete)*](</wiki/Epic_Scenes_from_The_Silmarillion_after_the_mythology_of_J._R._R._Tolkien_(Complete)> "Epic Scenes from The Silmarillion after the mythology of J. R. R. Tolkien (Complete)") and [*Musical Chapters from The Hobbit after the mythology of J. R. R. Tolkien, Op. 8*](/wiki/Musical_Chapters_from_The_Hobbit_after_the_mythology_of_J._R._R._Tolkien,_Op._8 "Musical Chapters from The Hobbit after the mythology of J. R. R. Tolkien, Op. 8").
