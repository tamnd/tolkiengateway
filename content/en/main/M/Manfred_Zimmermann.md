Dr. phil. **Manfred Zimmermann** is a retired German professor and lecturer.

In [1978](/wiki/1978) Zimmermann completed his doctorate in German Philology at the University of Marburg,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> where he worked as a Lecturer at the Institute for Medieval German Philology.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He then worked for over 20 years as an Associate Professor for Medieval German Studies at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMicken_College_of_Arts_and_Sciences_(A%26s)" title="wikipedia:McMicken College of Arts and Sciences (A&amp;s)">McMicken College of Arts and Sciences</a> of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cincinnati" title="wikipedia:University of Cincinnati">University of Cincinnati</a><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> until his retirement in 2014.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
