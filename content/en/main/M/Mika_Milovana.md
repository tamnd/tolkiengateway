**Mika Milovana** was the princess of [Dale](/wiki/Dale) in the [1967 adaptation](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_film)> "The Hobbit (1967 film)") of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.<sup id="cite_ref-video_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-video-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Princess Mika Milovana was one of three who survived the destruction of [Dale](/wiki/Dale) by the dragon [Slag](/wiki/Smaug "Smaug"), the others being a watchman and [General Torin Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin").<sup id="cite_ref-video_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-video-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Princess got angry when [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") refused to accept his prophesied role of the dragon killer. She declared that the dragon killed her father and her people and that she would go alone to the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) if need be. Dismayed that a child should enter into such peril, Bilbo agreed to go.<sup id="cite_ref-video_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-video-1">[1]</a></sup>

After Bilbo had killed Slag, Dale was restored. Bilbo and Mika reigned there together for a time before they returned to [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton).<sup id="cite_ref-video_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-video-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to [Gene Deitch](/wiki/Gene_Deitch), the director of [*The Hobbit* (1967 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_film)> "The Hobbit (1967 film)"), the Princess was named after <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pete_Seeger" title="wikipedia:Pete Seeger">Pete Seeger’s</a> eldest daughter "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mika_Seeger" title="wikipedia:Mika Seeger">Mika</a>". Her second name, "Milovana", 'means a beloved woman' in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_language" title="wikipedia:Czech language">Czech</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
