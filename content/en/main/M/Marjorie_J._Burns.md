**Marjorie Jean Burns** (born [1940](../_/1940.md)) is an English professor at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_State_University" title="wikipedia:Portland State University">Portland State University</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland,_Oregon" title="wikipedia:Portland, Oregon">Portland, Oregon</a>.

Her Ph.D. is from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley" title="wikipedia:University of California, Berkeley">UC Berkeley</a> and focuses on the nineteenth-century British novel. She has been on the faculty of Portland State University for over thirty years, where she teaches courses on nineteenth-century British literature, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), and Norse and Celtic mythology, and more. She has lectured on Tolkien throughout the United States, as well as Australia, Norway, England, and The Netherlands.
