**MIR** (**MĬR-**) is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "precious"<sup id="cite_ref-PE165_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE165-1">[1]</a></sup> or "esteem, value".<sup id="cite_ref-PE37_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE37-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[mírë](/wiki/M%C3%ADr%C3%AB "Mírë")*; *[mĭrya](/wiki/M%C4%ADrya "Mĭrya")*; <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=M%C3%ADrima&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mírima (page does not exist)">mírima</a></i> ("very precious, very lovely (of works of art only)");<sup id="cite_ref-PE165_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE165-1">[1]</a></sup> <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mirwa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mirwa (page does not exist)">mirwa</a></i> ("precious, valuable")<sup id="cite_ref-PE37_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE37-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[mîr](/wiki/M%C3%AEr "Mîr")*

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies") appears the root MIR-, given no direct signification but with the derivatives [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *míre*, [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Old_Noldorin) *mîre* and [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *mîr* ("jewel, precious thing, treasure").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
