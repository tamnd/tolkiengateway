**MIR** (**MĬR-**) is an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "precious"<sup id="cite_ref-PE165_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE165-1">[1]</a></sup> or "esteem, value".<sup id="cite_ref-PE37_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE37-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[mírë](Mírë.md)*; *[mĭrya](Mĭrya.md)*; <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=M%C3%ADrima&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mírima (page does not exist)">mírima</a></i> ("very precious, very lovely (of works of art only)");<sup id="cite_ref-PE165_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE165-1">[1]</a></sup> <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Mirwa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mirwa (page does not exist)">mirwa</a></i> ("precious, valuable")<sup id="cite_ref-PE37_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE37-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): *[mîr](Mîr.md)*

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md) appears the root MIR-, given no direct signification but with the derivatives [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *míre*, [Old Noldorin](../O/Old_Noldorin.md) *mîre* and [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *mîr* ("jewel, precious thing, treasure").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
