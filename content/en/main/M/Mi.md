<i><b>mi</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "in", "within" or "among".

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><b>mi</b> oromardi lissë-miruvóreva</i> = "of the sweet mead **in** lofty halls"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- <i><a href="../A/Aina.md" title="Aina">aina</a> wendë <b>mi</b> wenderon</i> = "Holy <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Virgin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Virgin (page does not exist)">Virgin</a> of virgins"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- <i>aistana elyë i<b>mí</b>ca <a href="../N/Nís.md" title="Nís">nísi</a></i> = "blessed art thou amongst women"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- <i>Alcar <b>mi</b> Tarmenel na <a class="mw-redirect" href="../E/Eru.md" title="Eru">Erun</a> ar mi <a class="mw-redirect" href="../C/Cemen.md" title="Cemen">cemen</a> raine i hínin</i> = "Glory \[be\] to God **in** the Highest, and peace to His people **on** earth".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
