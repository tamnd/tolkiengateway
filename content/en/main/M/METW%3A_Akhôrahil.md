**METW: Akhôrahil** is a game card from the set *[Middle-earth: The Wizards](Middle-earth%253A_The_Wizards.md)*, for [MECCG](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).

- <i><b>Note:</b></i> *Akhôrahil was a name for the fifth [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) originally invented by [ICE](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) in* [Lords of Middle-earth: Vol. II](../L/Lords_of_Middle-earth%253A_Vol._II.md).

## Card text {#Card_text}

<i><b>Unique.</b></i> Nazgûl (5th). May be played as a hazard creature (with one strike) or as a permanent event. As a creature, may also be played keyed to <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=METW:_Harondor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="METW: Harondor (page does not exist)">Harondor</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=METW:_Horse_Plains&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="METW: Horse Plains (page does not exist)">Horse Plains</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=METW:_Gorgoroth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="METW: Gorgoroth (page does not exist)">Gorgoroth</a>, and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=METW:_Khand&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="METW: Khand (page does not exist)">Khand</a>; and may also be played at sites in these regions. If played as a permanent-event, it will remain in play until tapped during the opponent's movement/hazard phase (tapping counts against the hazard limit). When tapped, Akhôrahil becomes a short-event and modifies any one character's body by -1 for the rest of this turn.
