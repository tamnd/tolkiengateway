**Mattocks** were a type of weapon used by the [Dwarves of the Iron Hills](../D/Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills.md) in the [battle of Azanulbizar](../B/Battle_of_Azanulbizar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> and in the [battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> They were heavy and required two hands to use.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Náin](<../N/Náin_(son_of_Grór).md>) used a mattock in the [battle of Azanulbizar](../B/Battle_of_Azanulbizar.md) fighting against [Azog](../A/Azog.md), but his mattock splintered on the stone and Náin himself was killed<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>.<span></span>
