**Mattocks** were a type of weapon used by the [Dwarves of the Iron Hills](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills) in the [battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar "Battle of Azanulbizar")<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> and in the [battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies "Battle of Five Armies").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> They were heavy and required two hands to use.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Náin](</wiki/N%C3%A1in_(son_of_Gr%C3%B3r)> "Náin (son of Grór)") used a mattock in the [battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar "Battle of Azanulbizar") fighting against [Azog](/wiki/Azog), but his mattock splintered on the stone and Náin himself was killed<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>.<span></span>
