**Musical instruments** of different types appear in the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).

## By race {#By_race}

### Valarin {#Valarin}

Among the [Ainur](../A/Ainur.md), reference is made to the trumpets of [Manwë](Manwë.md); [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) is said to have made music upon his great horns of white shell, the **[Ulumúri](../U/Ulumúri.md)**; whereas [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) used his **[Valaróma](../V/Valaróma.md)** while hunting.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Elven {#Elven}

*[There and Back Again](../T/There_and_Back_Again.md)* speaks about "elven-harps",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the harp is indeed frequently associated with the [Elves](../E/Elves.md). Among the elven peoples known to have performed the harp are the [Elves of Mirkwood](../E/Elves_of_Mirkwood.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and possibly the [Nandor](../N/Nandor.md) and [Avari](../A/Avari.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup> When [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) took the [White Ship](../W/White_Ship.md) to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) after the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), he is said to have brought "a silver harp".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> Other instruments known to have been used by Elves include the flute,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup> and <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/trumpet" title="wikipedia:trumpet">trumpets</a></b> (although mostly spoken of being used in battle).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

Searching for [Maedhros](Maedhros.md) in the [Iron Mountains](../I/Iron_Mountains.md), [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) took out "his harp and played a fair song of Valinor".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup> In order to befriend the people of [Bëor](../B/Bëor.md), [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod_Felagund.md) is said to have entered their camp and taken "a rude harp which Bëor had laid aside, and he played music upon it such as mortal ear had never heard".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

In the *[The Book of Lost Tales: Part One](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales%253A_Part_One.md)*, the [Noldoli](../N/Noldoli.md) were said to make "much music for the multitude of their harps and viols was very sweet".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup> During the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Years_of_Double_Mirth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Years of Double Mirth (page does not exist)">Years of Double Mirth</a> festival, the Teleri ([Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md)) played "congregated harps", the Nodoli on "their viols and instruments", and the [Solosimpi](../S/Solosimpi.md) ([Teleri](../T/Teleri.md)) on pipes.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup>

### Mannish {#Mannish}

The [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) are said to have played the harp.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup>

When the people of [Minas Tirith](Minas_Tirith.md) prepared for the arrival of the victorious [Army of the West](../A/Army_of_the_West.md), there came "the harpers that harped most skilfully in all the land" from [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md), and "there were players upon viols and upon flutes and upon horns of silver". As [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) passed the streets of Minas Tirith during his coronation, the [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md) played "music of harp and of viol and of flute".<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup>

The [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) had trumpets,<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[16]</a></sup> although it is unknown if they were used for musical purposes.

[Ælfwine](../_/Ælfwine.md) is said to have played the harp.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[17]</a></sup>

### Hobbitish {#Hobbitish}

At [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md), [hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) were heard playing "'trumpets and <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horn_(instrument)" title="wikipedia:Horn (instrument)">horns</a></b>, <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pipe_(instrument)" title="wikipedia:Pipe (instrument)">pipes</a></b> and flutes, and other musical instruments". Among these other instruments were "musical crackers" (which "contained instruments, small, but of perfect make and enchanting tones") and <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell#Usage_as_musical_instruments" title="wikipedia:Bell">bells</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[18]</a></sup>

### Dwarven {#Dwarven}

Among the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), several types of instruments are recorded in the stories from [Middle-earth](Middle-earth.md). [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) played the following instruments at the [unexpected party](../A/An_Unexpected_Party.md) at [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md):

- <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/fiddle" title="wikipedia:fiddle">fiddle</a></b> (played by [Kíli](../K/Kíli.md) and [Fíli](../F/Fíli.md))
- <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/flute" title="wikipedia:flute">flute</a></b> (played by [Dori](../D/Dori.md), [Nori](../N/Nori.md), and [Ori](../O/Ori.md))
- <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/drum" title="wikipedia:drum">drum</a></b> (played by [Bombur](../B/Bombur.md))
- <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/clarinet" title="wikipedia:clarinet">clarinet</a></b> (played by [Bifur](../B/Bifur.md) and [Bofur](../B/Bofur.md))
- <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/viol" title="wikipedia:viol">viol</a></b> (played by [Dwalin](../D/Dwalin.md) and [Balin](../B/Balin.md))
- a golden <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/harp" title="wikipedia:harp">harp</a></b> (played by [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md))<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[19]</a></sup>

Apparently, other members in Thorin's Company also played the harp, since it is written that the dwarves produced "music of harps and of fiddles" when visiting [Lake-town](../L/Lake-town.md).<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[20]</a></sup> In [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md)'s treasure, Fíli and Kíli found "many golden harps strung with silver": these were [magical](Magic.md) (and apparently made by Dwarves<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[21]</a></sup>), always staying <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musical_tuning" title="wikipedia:Musical tuning">in tune</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[22]</a></sup> The [Dwarves of Khazad-dûm](../D/Dwarves_of_Khazad-dûm.md) were also known to have played the harp and had trumpets.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[23]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md), the word for "music" is *gling*, and "musical" is *glingrin*.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[24]</a></sup> Other words in Gnomish related to musical instruments are:

- *thibin* ("a flute"), *thimpa-* ("to play a flute or whistle"), *thibindon* ("flute-playing").<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[25]</a></sup>
- *tifin* ("a small flute").<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[26]</a></sup>
- *lintha-* ("sound strike or ring bell, etc. play an instrument"), *linthanin* ("playing or music of instruments").<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[27]</a></sup>
- *ross* ("pipe. especially musical") *rogli*, *rothli* ("little pipe. especially musical").<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29">[28]</a></sup>

In the *[The Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)*, the [roots](../S/Sundocarme.md) NGAN-, NGÁNAD- signify "play (on stringed instrument)". From these derive:

- [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) *nande* "a harp"; *nandelle* "little harp"; *nandele* "harping"; *nanda-* "to harp"; *nandaro* "harper"
- [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *gandel*, *gannel* "a harp"; *gannado* or *ganno* "play a harp"<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">[29]</a></sup>

Other words related to music appearing *The Etymologies* include Qenya words *tyalangan* ("harp-player")<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31">[30]</a></sup> and *lindele* ("music").<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32">[31]</a></sup>
