**Musical instruments** of different types appear in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

## By race {#By_race}

### Valarin {#Valarin}

Among the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur), reference is made to the trumpets of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB); [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) is said to have made music upon his great horns of white shell, the **[Ulumúri](/wiki/Ulum%C3%BAri)**; whereas [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) used his **[Valaróma](/wiki/Valar%C3%B3ma)** while hunting.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Elven {#Elven}

*[There and Back Again](/wiki/There_and_Back_Again)* speaks about "elven-harps",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the harp is indeed frequently associated with the [Elves](/wiki/Elves). Among the elven peoples known to have performed the harp are the [Elves of Mirkwood](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and possibly the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor) and [Avari](/wiki/Avari).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup> When [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) took the [White Ship](/wiki/White_Ship) to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), he is said to have brought "a silver harp".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> Other instruments known to have been used by Elves include the flute,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup> and <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/trumpet" title="wikipedia:trumpet">trumpets</a></b> (although mostly spoken of being used in battle).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

Searching for [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) in the [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains), [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) took out "his harp and played a fair song of Valinor".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup> In order to befriend the people of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor), [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod_Felagund) is said to have entered their camp and taken "a rude harp which Bëor had laid aside, and he played music upon it such as mortal ear had never heard".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

In the *[The Book of Lost Tales: Part One](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales:_Part_One)*, the [Noldoli](/wiki/Noldoli) were said to make "much music for the multitude of their harps and viols was very sweet".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup> During the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Years_of_Double_Mirth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Years of Double Mirth (page does not exist)">Years of Double Mirth</a> festival, the Teleri ([Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar)) played "congregated harps", the Nodoli on "their viols and instruments", and the [Solosimpi](/wiki/Solosimpi) ([Teleri](/wiki/Teleri)) on pipes.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup>

### Mannish {#Mannish}

The [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) are said to have played the harp.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup>

When the people of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) prepared for the arrival of the victorious [Army of the West](/wiki/Army_of_the_West), there came "the harpers that harped most skilfully in all the land" from [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth), and "there were players upon viols and upon flutes and upon horns of silver". As [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) passed the streets of Minas Tirith during his coronation, the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) played "music of harp and of viol and of flute".<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup>

The [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) had trumpets,<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[16]</a></sup> although it is unknown if they were used for musical purposes.

[Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine) is said to have played the harp.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[17]</a></sup>

### Hobbitish {#Hobbitish}

At [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party), [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") were heard playing "'trumpets and <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horn_(instrument)" title="wikipedia:Horn (instrument)">horns</a></b>, <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pipe_(instrument)" title="wikipedia:Pipe (instrument)">pipes</a></b> and flutes, and other musical instruments". Among these other instruments were "musical crackers" (which "contained instruments, small, but of perfect make and enchanting tones") and <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell#Usage_as_musical_instruments" title="wikipedia:Bell">bells</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[18]</a></sup>

### Dwarven {#Dwarven}

Among the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), several types of instruments are recorded in the stories from [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) played the following instruments at the [unexpected party](/wiki/An_Unexpected_Party "An Unexpected Party") at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End):

- <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/fiddle" title="wikipedia:fiddle">fiddle</a></b> (played by [Kíli](/wiki/K%C3%ADli) and [Fíli](/wiki/F%C3%ADli))
- <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/flute" title="wikipedia:flute">flute</a></b> (played by [Dori](/wiki/Dori), [Nori](/wiki/Nori), and [Ori](/wiki/Ori))
- <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/drum" title="wikipedia:drum">drum</a></b> (played by [Bombur](/wiki/Bombur))
- <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/clarinet" title="wikipedia:clarinet">clarinet</a></b> (played by [Bifur](/wiki/Bifur) and [Bofur](/wiki/Bofur))
- <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/viol" title="wikipedia:viol">viol</a></b> (played by [Dwalin](/wiki/Dwalin) and [Balin](/wiki/Balin))
- a golden <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/harp" title="wikipedia:harp">harp</a></b> (played by [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin))<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[19]</a></sup>

Apparently, other members in Thorin's Company also played the harp, since it is written that the dwarves produced "music of harps and of fiddles" when visiting [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town).<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[20]</a></sup> In [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug)'s treasure, Fíli and Kíli found "many golden harps strung with silver": these were [magical](/wiki/Magic "Magic") (and apparently made by Dwarves<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[21]</a></sup>), always staying <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musical_tuning" title="wikipedia:Musical tuning">in tune</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[22]</a></sup> The [Dwarves of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm) were also known to have played the harp and had trumpets.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[23]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), the word for "music" is *gling*, and "musical" is *glingrin*.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[24]</a></sup> Other words in Gnomish related to musical instruments are:

- *thibin* ("a flute"), *thimpa-* ("to play a flute or whistle"), *thibindon* ("flute-playing").<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[25]</a></sup>
- *tifin* ("a small flute").<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[26]</a></sup>
- *lintha-* ("sound strike or ring bell, etc. play an instrument"), *linthanin* ("playing or music of instruments").<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[27]</a></sup>
- *ross* ("pipe. especially musical") *rogli*, *rothli* ("little pipe. especially musical").<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29">[28]</a></sup>

In the *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, the [roots](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") NGAN-, NGÁNAD- signify "play (on stringed instrument)". From these derive:

- [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) *nande* "a harp"; *nandelle* "little harp"; *nandele* "harping"; *nanda-* "to harp"; *nandaro* "harper"
- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *gandel*, *gannel* "a harp"; *gannado* or *ganno* "play a harp"<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">[29]</a></sup>

Other words related to music appearing *The Etymologies* include Qenya words *tyalangan* ("harp-player")<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31">[30]</a></sup> and *lindele* ("music").<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32">[31]</a></sup>
