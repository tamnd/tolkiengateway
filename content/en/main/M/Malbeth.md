**Malbeth** was a [seer](../S/Seer.md) and royal counsellor gifted with [foresight](../F/Foresight.md) who is remembered in lore for two prophecies concerning the fate of the descendants of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md).

## History {#History}

Malbeth was probably a [Dúnadan of the North](../D/Dúnedain_of_the_North.md) who appears to have been a royal counsellor to the [King of Arthedain](../K/King_of_Arthedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-guide_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-1">[1]</a></sup> He flourished during the reigns of [Araphant](../A/Araphant.md) and [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

### Last King of Arthedain {#Last_King_of_Arthedain}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1864](../T/Third_Age_1864.md) when the son of [Araphant](../A/Araphant.md) (fourteenth [King](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md) of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md)) was born, Malbeth advised Araphant to name his son *[Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md)*, meaning "*last king*":

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Arvedui you shall call him, for he will be the last in Arthedain. Though a choice will come to the Dúnedain, and if they take the one that seems less hopeful, then your son will change his name and become king of a great realm. If not, then much sorrow and many lives of men shall pass, until the Dúnedain arise and are united again.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="../A/Appendix_A.md" title="Appendix A">Appendix A</a>, Annals of the Kings and Rulers</i>: (iv) Gondor and the Heirs of Anárion</cite></p>
</blockquote>

### Paths of the Dead {#Paths_of_the_Dead}

Malbeth's second prophecy was made in the days of Arvedui (ruled [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1964](../T/Third_Age_1964.md) - [1974](../T/Third_Age_1974.md)), in which the seer foretold [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md)'s passage through the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md) even though the event was over a thousand years in the future:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Over the land there lies a long shadow,<br/>westward reaching wings of darkness.<br/>The Tower trembles; to the tombs of kings<br/>doom approaches. The Dead awaken;<br/>for the hour is come for the oathbreakers:<br/>at the <a href="../S/Stone_of_Erech.md" title="Stone of Erech">Stone of Erech</a> they shall stand again<br/>and hear there a horn in the hills ringing.<br/>Whose shall the horn be? Who shall call them<br/>from the grey twilight, the forgotten people?<br/>The heir of him to whom the oath they swore.<br/>From the North shall he come, need shall drive him:<br/>he shall pass the Door to the Paths of the Dead.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md" title="The Return of the King">The Return of the King</a></i>, &#34;<a href="../T/The_Passing_of_the_Grey_Company.md" title="The Passing of the Grey Company">The Passing of the Grey Company</a>&#34;, &#34;<a href="../O/Over_the_land_there_lies_a_long_shadow.md" title="Over the land there lies a long shadow">Over the land there lies a long shadow</a>&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

Indeed, when [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) was left without a [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md), Arvedui claimed the kingship, both as a [Heir of Isildur](../H/Heir_of_Isildur.md) and as the husband of Gondorian princess [Fíriel](../F/Fíriel.md) who would be entitled to succeed her father to the throne. However the [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md) ruled out his claim ("the one that seems less hopeful") choosing instead [someone](../E/Earnil_II.md) from the [House of Anárion](../H/House_of_Anárion.md), so Arvedui never became "king of a great realm" and didn't change his name. Instead as Malbeth said, there was "much sorrow"; the [Battle of Fornost](../B/Battle_of_Fornost.md) destroyed the North-kingdom, and Arvedui was drowned in the sea soon after, the last king of Arthedain.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

Malbeth's second prophecy was also fulfilled centuries later, during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) when Aragorn (sixteenth [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](../C/Chieftain_of_the_Dúnedain.md)) learned from the *[palantír](../P/Palantíri.md)* that [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) was being threatened from the south by a fleet of [Corsairs](../C/Corsairs.md) from [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md). He needed a route to get to them in haste, and [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md)'s message ("*Bid Aragorn remember the words of the seer, and the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md)*") told him how. Aragorn (with [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) and the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md)) travelled through the Paths of the Dead to the Stone of Erech where Aragorn blew a horn to summon the [Oathbreakers](../O/Oathbreakers.md). He told them he was [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md)'s heir and so they fulfilled their oath and defeated the Corsairs which helped Gondor win the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-company_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-company-4">[3]</a></sup>

Aragorn fulfilled again the first prophecy, where Arvedui had failed; after "much sorrow and many lives of men", Aragorn finally became [High King of the Dúnedain](../H/High_King_of_the_Dúnedain.md) who were "[united again](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md)".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Malbeth* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Golden Word", from *[malt](Malt.md)* ("gold") + [lenited](../L/Lenition.md) *[peth](../P/Peth.md)* ("word, voice").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
