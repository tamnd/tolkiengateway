**Malbeth** was a [seer](/wiki/Seer "Seer") and royal counsellor gifted with [foresight](/wiki/Foresight "Foresight") who is remembered in lore for two prophecies concerning the fate of the descendants of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur).

## History {#History}

Malbeth was probably a [Dúnadan of the North](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_the_North "Dúnedain of the North") who appears to have been a royal counsellor to the [King of Arthedain](/wiki/King_of_Arthedain).<sup id="cite_ref-guide_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-1">[1]</a></sup> He flourished during the reigns of [Araphant](/wiki/Araphant) and [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

### Last King of Arthedain {#Last_King_of_Arthedain}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1864](/wiki/Third_Age_1864 "Third Age 1864") when the son of [Araphant](/wiki/Araphant) (fourteenth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor "Kings of Arnor") of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain)) was born, Malbeth advised Araphant to name his son *[Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui)*, meaning "*last king*":

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Arvedui you shall call him, for he will be the last in Arthedain. Though a choice will come to the Dúnedain, and if they take the one that seems less hopeful, then your son will change his name and become king of a great realm. If not, then much sorrow and many lives of men shall pass, until the Dúnedain arise and are united again.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="/wiki/Appendix_A" title="Appendix A">Appendix A</a>, Annals of the Kings and Rulers</i>: (iv) Gondor and the Heirs of Anárion</cite></p>
</blockquote>

### Paths of the Dead {#Paths_of_the_Dead}

Malbeth's second prophecy was made in the days of Arvedui (ruled [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1964](/wiki/Third_Age_1964 "Third Age 1964") - [1974](/wiki/Third_Age_1974 "Third Age 1974")), in which the seer foretold [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)'s passage through the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead) even though the event was over a thousand years in the future:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Over the land there lies a long shadow,<br/>westward reaching wings of darkness.<br/>The Tower trembles; to the tombs of kings<br/>doom approaches. The Dead awaken;<br/>for the hour is come for the oathbreakers:<br/>at the <a href="/wiki/Stone_of_Erech" title="Stone of Erech">Stone of Erech</a> they shall stand again<br/>and hear there a horn in the hills ringing.<br/>Whose shall the horn be? Who shall call them<br/>from the grey twilight, the forgotten people?<br/>The heir of him to whom the oath they swore.<br/>From the North shall he come, need shall drive him:<br/>he shall pass the Door to the Paths of the Dead.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Return of the King">The Return of the King</a></i>, &#34;<a href="/wiki/The_Passing_of_the_Grey_Company" title="The Passing of the Grey Company">The Passing of the Grey Company</a>&#34;, &#34;<a href="/wiki/Over_the_land_there_lies_a_long_shadow" title="Over the land there lies a long shadow">Over the land there lies a long shadow</a>&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

Indeed, when [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) was left without a [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor"), Arvedui claimed the kingship, both as a [Heir of Isildur](/wiki/Heir_of_Isildur) and as the husband of Gondorian princess [Fíriel](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel) who would be entitled to succeed her father to the throne. However the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) ruled out his claim ("the one that seems less hopeful") choosing instead [someone](/wiki/Earnil_II "Earnil II") from the [House of Anárion](/wiki/House_of_An%C3%A1rion), so Arvedui never became "king of a great realm" and didn't change his name. Instead as Malbeth said, there was "much sorrow"; the [Battle of Fornost](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost) destroyed the North-kingdom, and Arvedui was drowned in the sea soon after, the last king of Arthedain.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

Malbeth's second prophecy was also fulfilled centuries later, during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) when Aragorn (sixteenth [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain)) learned from the *[palantír](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* that [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) was being threatened from the south by a fleet of [Corsairs](/wiki/Corsairs) from [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar). He needed a route to get to them in haste, and [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond)'s message ("*Bid Aragorn remember the words of the seer, and the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead)*") told him how. Aragorn (with [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) and the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company)) travelled through the Paths of the Dead to the Stone of Erech where Aragorn blew a horn to summon the [Oathbreakers](/wiki/Oathbreakers). He told them he was [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur)'s heir and so they fulfilled their oath and defeated the Corsairs which helped Gondor win the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-company_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-company-4">[3]</a></sup>

Aragorn fulfilled again the first prophecy, where Arvedui had failed; after "much sorrow and many lives of men", Aragorn finally became [High King of the Dúnedain](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain) who were "[united again](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom "Reunited Kingdom")".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Malbeth* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Golden Word", from *[malt](/wiki/Malt "Malt")* ("gold") + [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") *[peth](/wiki/Peth "Peth")* ("word, voice").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
