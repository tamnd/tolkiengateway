**Mount Gram** was a mountain inhabited by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).

## History {#History}

Mount Gram was inhabited by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) led by their King [Golfimbul](/wiki/Golfimbul). In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2747](/wiki/Third_Age_2747 "Third Age 2747") they attacked much of northern [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), but were defeated in the [Battle of Greenfields](/wiki/Battle_of_Greenfields).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Location {#Location}

The location of Mount Gram has never been established and the mountain could have been anywhere within the northern [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) region.

The [Tale of Years](/wiki/Appendix_B "Appendix B") mentions that in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2740](/wiki/Third_Age_2740 "Third Age 2740") "Orcs *renew* their invasions of Eriador", obviously referring to [those of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains "Orcs of the Misty Mountains"), mentioned in the immediate previous entries.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> "The [Goblins](/wiki/Orcs#Orcs_and_Goblins "Orcs") of Mount Gram" who invaded the Shire in [2747](/wiki/Third_Age_2747 "Third Age 2747") obviously were a subsequent wave of them.

This must be the reason why [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) considers Gram to have been one of the Misty Mountains, although he does not cite a specific source or reason to do so.<sup id="cite_ref-Foster_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foster-1">[1]</a></sup>

On the other hand, [Karen Wynn Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad) (also without explanation) placed it on a promontory in the [Ettenmoors](/wiki/Ettenmoors).<sup id="cite_ref-Atlas_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Atlas-2">[2]</a></sup>

[John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff) considers the [Norse](/wiki/Norse) name of the mountain (representing [Northern Mannish](/wiki/Northern_Mannish)) and locates it north-eastern of the Shire, suggesting that it refers to the Misty Mountains: "particularly associated with the goblins throughout the *Hobbit*, also seem to be the mountains closest to Bilbo's home". He also considers the possibility that it is another name for Mount [Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest writings of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, Mount Gram was merely "Gram Hill".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology and inspiration {#Etymology_and_inspiration}

[Gram](/wiki/Gram) in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) means "fierce, grim" but it is not certain that the name was intended as Old English.

There is a Mount Gram in Albania.

<b>1988: <i><a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_War_in_Middle_Earth" title="J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s War in Middle Earth">J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s War in Middle Earth</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Mount Gram is located North of the <a href="/wiki/Ettenmoors" title="Ettenmoors">Ettenmoors</a>, West of the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a>, South-west of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Mount_Gundabad" title="Mount Gundabad">Mount Gundabad</a>, and just South of the <a href="/wiki/Mountains_of_Angmar" title="Mountains of Angmar">Mountains of Angmar</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Mount Gram is located in the <a href="/wiki/Ettenmoors" title="Ettenmoors">Ettenmoors</a>, the game&#39;s only Player-versus-Player area. At the foot of it sits &#34;Gramsfoot&#34;, the war-camp of <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a> which is the only stronghold of the Evil players and cannot be entered by the Free Peoples.</dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Tharzog" title="Tharzog">Tharzog</a> is the chieftain of the <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> of Mount Gram.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Ruins_of_the_Lost_Realm" title="Ruins of the Lost Realm">Ruins of the Lost Realm</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Mount Gram is described as a steep horn-like peak on a western arm of the Misty Mountains beyond the Ettenmoors. Before it was infested by Orcs, it was home to Dwarves who built secret tunnels underneath. By <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2965" title="Third Age 2965">2965</a>, the fortress was commanded by an Orc-chieftain named Radgul. He traded ancient Dwarven gold with human bandits in exchange for prisoners, with <a href="/wiki/Hobbits" title="Hobbits">Hobbits</a> fetching higher compensation.<sup id="cite_ref-Gram_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gram-8">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>
