<i><b>mae</b></i> is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for the adverb "well".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The etymology of the word is unknown.

There is a similar [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word *mai* "well".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *Mae govannen*: "well met"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
