**Mrs. Bracegirdle** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) who was one of the attendees of [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md). Mrs. Bracegirdle was invented by [New Line Cinema](../N/New_Line_Cinema.md) for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), where she was portrayed by [Lori Dungey](../L/Lori_Dungey.md).

## History {#History}

Mrs. Bracegirdle was one of the attendees of [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md). When she greets [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md), he is surprised at the number of children she has.

There are two Mr. Bracegirdles known to have attended Bilbo's birthday party, [Bruno](../B/Bruno_Bracegirdle.md) and his son [Hugo](../H/Hugo_Bracegirdle.md). Bruno may have had a wife since he had two children, but he was 88 years old and both of his children were adults. Hugo was 51 years old, though he is not known to have had a wife or children.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
