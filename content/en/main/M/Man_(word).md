<i><b>man</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "who".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Nam%C3%A1ri%C3%AB" title="Namárië">Sí <b>man</b> i yulma nin enquantuva?</a></i> = "**Who** now shall refill the cup for me?"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Markirya" title="Markirya"><b>man</b> cenuva lumbor ahosta?</a></i> = "**Who** shall see the clouds gather?"
