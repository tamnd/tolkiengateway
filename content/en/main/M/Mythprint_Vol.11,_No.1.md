<i><b>Mythprint</b></i> **(Vol.11, No.1)** is an issue of the bulletin *[Mythprint](Mythprint.md)*.

The issue reprints "J.R.R. Tolkien - The Wizard Father" by Michael Tolkien from *The Sunday Telegraph*. The article includes an extract from a letter to Michael, probably dating from the late 1960s, remarking that he was one of the few people that really knew what *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* was about (pp. 3–4).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
