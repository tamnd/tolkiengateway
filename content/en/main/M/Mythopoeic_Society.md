Founded in [1967](../_/1967.md), the **Mythopoeic Society** is a non-profit international literary and educational organization for the study, discussion, and enjoyment of fantastic and mythic literature, especially the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md), and [Charles Williams](../C/Charles_Williams.md). Membership in the Mythopoeic Society is open to all scholars, writers and readers of these literatures. The Mythopoeic Society also hosts a Tolkien and [Inklings](../I/Inklings.md)-focused annual conference called [Mythcon](Mythcon.md).

## Publications {#Publications}

- *[Mythlore](Mythlore.md)*: main journal of the society, includes peer-reviewed articles on mythic and fantastic works.
- *[Mythprint](Mythprint.md)*: newsletter with notices of activities of the society, book reviews and articles.
- *[The Mythic Circle](../T/The_Mythic_Circle.md)*: collection of original fiction and poetry.

## Awards {#Awards}

The Mythopoetic Society promotes several awards devoted to the [mythopoietic ideas](Mythopoeia.md), including both the literary creation and the scholarship studies:

- Alexei Kondratiev Award for the best Student Paper
- Mythopoeic Fantasy Award for Adult Literature
- Mythopoeic Fantasy Award for Children’s Literature
- Mythopoeic Scholarship Award in Inklings Studies
- Mythopoeic Scholarship Award in Myth and Fantasy Studies

From [1971](../_/1971.md) until [1991](../_/1991.md), the *Mythopoeic Scholarship Award* was a single award given for Inklings Studies; the second category of Myth and Fantasy Studies was established in [1992](../_/1992.md).
