Founded in [1967](/wiki/1967), the **Mythopoeic Society** is a non-profit international literary and educational organization for the study, discussion, and enjoyment of fantastic and mythic literature, especially the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), and [Charles Williams](/wiki/Charles_Williams). Membership in the Mythopoeic Society is open to all scholars, writers and readers of these literatures. The Mythopoeic Society also hosts a Tolkien and [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings)-focused annual conference called [Mythcon](/wiki/Mythcon).

## Publications {#Publications}

- *[Mythlore](/wiki/Mythlore)*: main journal of the society, includes peer-reviewed articles on mythic and fantastic works.
- *[Mythprint](/wiki/Mythprint)*: newsletter with notices of activities of the society, book reviews and articles.
- *[The Mythic Circle](/wiki/The_Mythic_Circle)*: collection of original fiction and poetry.

## Awards {#Awards}

The Mythopoetic Society promotes several awards devoted to the [mythopoietic ideas](/wiki/Mythopoeia "Mythopoeia"), including both the literary creation and the scholarship studies:

- Alexei Kondratiev Award for the best Student Paper
- Mythopoeic Fantasy Award for Adult Literature
- Mythopoeic Fantasy Award for Children’s Literature
- Mythopoeic Scholarship Award in Inklings Studies
- Mythopoeic Scholarship Award in Myth and Fantasy Studies

From [1971](/wiki/1971) until [1991](/wiki/1991), the *Mythopoeic Scholarship Award* was a single award given for Inklings Studies; the second category of Myth and Fantasy Studies was established in [1992](/wiki/1992).
