**Manic Miner: The Hobbit** is an unofficial remake of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manic_Miner" title="wikipedia:Manic Miner">Manic Miner</a></i> with combines the story of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* with the gameplay of *Manic Miner*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Spectrum_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Spectrum-3">[3]</a></sup> The game has two versions: the standard version, for people who have experience with *Manic Miner* games, and a beginner's version, for people who are new to *Manic Miner*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> It was released with its companion game *[Jet Set Willy: The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Jet_Set_Willy:_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> The game is freeware and is written by [Andrew Broad](/wiki/Andrew_Broad).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Spectrum_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Spectrum-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
