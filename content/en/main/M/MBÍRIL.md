**MBÍRIL** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme"), a compound of [MIR](/wiki/MIR) and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=RIL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="RIL (page does not exist)">RIL</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *miril* (or *mirilli*; "shining jewel"); *mirilya-* ("glitter")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin): *bril* ("glass, crystal")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
