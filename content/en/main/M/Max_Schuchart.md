**Max Schuchart**, MBE ([August 16](/wiki/16_August "16 August"), [1920](/wiki/1920) - [February 25](/wiki/25_February "25 February"), [2005](/wiki/2005)) was a Dutch poet, journalist and translator. He translated many other English authors, such as Oscar Wilde, Terry Goodkind, Richard Adams, Lord Dunsany, William Horwood, Daniel Defoe and Salman Rushdie.

<h2><span id=".22In_de_Ban_van_de_Ring.22"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="&#34;In_de_Ban_van_de_Ring&#34;">&#34;In de Ban van de Ring&#34;</span></h2>

Schuchart was the first to translate *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* (in [1956](/wiki/1956)-[1957](/wiki/1957)), and the third to translate *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. He was also the first to translate *[Bilbo's Last Song](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Last_Song)* and *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)*. He wrote a short booklet called *[Tolkien: In de Ban van zijn Werk](/wiki/Tolkien:_In_de_Ban_van_zijn_Werk)*, about [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), his works, his visit to The Netherlands in [1958](/wiki/1958) and his involvement with the Dutch translation of *The Lord of the Rings*.

Schuchart revised his translation three times, in [1980](/wiki/1980) (incorporating changes made to the [Second Edition](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings#Publication_History "The Lord of the Rings") of the original and with the [Appendices](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Appendices "The Lord of the Rings Appendices") as a separate volume, including C, the complete text of D, E and F I for the first time), in [1996](/wiki/1996) (this was published [the subsequent year](/wiki/1997 "1997")) and in [2003](/wiki/2003) (including [Appendix F](/wiki/Appendix_F) II for the first time).
