**Max Schuchart**, MBE ([August 16](../_/16_August.md), [1920](../_/1920.md) - [February 25](../_/25_February.md), [2005](../_/2005.md)) was a Dutch poet, journalist and translator. He translated many other English authors, such as Oscar Wilde, Terry Goodkind, Richard Adams, Lord Dunsany, William Horwood, Daniel Defoe and Salman Rushdie.

<h2><span id=".22In_de_Ban_van_de_Ring.22"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="&#34;In_de_Ban_van_de_Ring&#34;">&#34;In de Ban van de Ring&#34;</span></h2>

Schuchart was the first to translate *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* (in [1956](../_/1956.md)-[1957](../_/1957.md)), and the third to translate *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*. He was also the first to translate *[Bilbo's Last Song](../B/Bilbo's_Last_Song.md)* and *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*. He wrote a short booklet called *[Tolkien: In de Ban van zijn Werk](../T/Tolkien%253A_In_de_Ban_van_zijn_Werk.md)*, about [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), his works, his visit to The Netherlands in [1958](../_/1958.md) and his involvement with the Dutch translation of *The Lord of the Rings*.

Schuchart revised his translation three times, in [1980](../_/1980.md) (incorporating changes made to the [Second Edition](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md#Publication_History) of the original and with the [Appendices](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Appendices.md) as a separate volume, including C, the complete text of D, E and F I for the first time), in [1996](../_/1996.md) (this was published [the subsequent year](../_/1997.md)) and in [2003](../_/2003.md) (including [Appendix F](../A/Appendix_F.md) II for the first time).
