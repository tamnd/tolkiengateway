<i><b>Middle-earth: The Dragons Player Guide</b></i> is a source-book for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>With <i><a href="Middle-earth%253A_The_Wizards.md" title="Middle-earth: The Wizards">Middle-earth: The Wizards</a></i>™ you pursue the thrill of adventure in Tolkien&#39;s magical world. Your valiant companies face all hazards deployed by Sauron and his foul servants, while you attempt to wrest the Middle Lands from the Abhorred One&#39;s ever encroaching grasp. Under your guidance the Free Peoples will seize victory, or so you believe, until the dragons of the Withered Heath awake to the Dark Lord&#39;s call.
</p><p><i><a href="Middle-earth%253A_The_Dragons.md" title="Middle-earth: The Dragons">Middle-earth: The Dragons</a></i>™ brings to life these powerful allies of Sauron, as well as the means to fight them. Yet how can any hero hope to survive battle with creatures deformed by Morgoth&#39;s own tainted hand?
</p><p><i>Middle-earth: The Dragons Player Guide</i> provides the knowledge you need to stand and fight these old enemies of Endor.
</p><p>Expanding upon Middle-earth: The Wizards Player Guide, this sourcebook teaches you how to systematically add cards from The Dragons to enhance your own prized deck-building and card-play strategies.
</p><p>Revel in the greater power of cards such as &#34;Smaug Ahunt&#34; and &#34;Prowess of Age.&#34; Create fresh opportunites with &#34;Legendary Hoard&#34; and hoard items. Discover a whole new level of play along with the exciting new strategies available when the Worms of the North invade your game!
</p><p><i>METD Player Guide</i> includes:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Strategy Guide</b>—a powerful, concise reference sure to enhance your deck design and tactical methods with cards from <i>The Dragons</i>.</li>
<li><b>Complete Card Analysis</b>—featuring all 180 <i>METD</i> cards, a black and white photo of each, backgrounds, and strategy tips.</li>
<li><b>Sample Decks</b>—multiple decks created by award-winning players.</li>
<li><b>Tournament Rules and Card Errata</b>—give the nuts and bolts on constructing decks for competitive events.</li>
<li><b>The Card Tables</b>—featuring a <i>METD</i> card list plus important associations between cards at a glance.</li></ul>
</blockquote>
