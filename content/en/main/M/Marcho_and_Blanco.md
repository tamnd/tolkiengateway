**Marcho** and **Blanco** were brothers, [Bree-hobbits](/wiki/Bree-hobbits) of [Fallohide](/wiki/Fallohides "Fallohides") stock who founded [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") in [Third Age 1601](/wiki/Third_Age_1601).<sup id="cite_ref-hobbits_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbits-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-family_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-family-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1601](/wiki/Third_Age_1601 "Third Age 1601"), the two brothers led a group of mostly [Harfoot](/wiki/Harfoots "Harfoots") [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") westwards from [Bree](/wiki/Bree).<sup id="cite_ref-family_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-family-2">[2]</a></sup> They crossed the river [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin) at the [Bridge of Stonebows](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge "Brandywine Bridge"), and with the permission of [King](/wiki/King_of_Arthedain "King of Arthedain") [Argeleb II](/wiki/Argeleb_II) they settled in the royal hunting grounds of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) and founded [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

Soon after (c. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1630](/wiki/Third_Age_1630 "Third Age 1630")) they are joined by [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors), who come up from [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland) and settle in the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Marcho contains the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) element <i>*marh</i> ("horse") and Blanco is derived from Old English *blanca* ("white horse", "grey horse").<sup id="cite_ref-Shippey_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shippey-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marmor_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marmor-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The two brothers are perhaps based on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hengest" title="wikipedia:Hengest">Hengest</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horsa" title="wikipedia:Horsa">Horsa</a>, who brought the Angles, Saxons and Jutes to England. It is noteworthy that both *hengest*<sup id="cite_ref-BT1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BT1-6">[6]</a></sup> and *horsa* mean "stallion" and "horse", respectively, in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English).<sup id="cite_ref-Shippey_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shippey-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Marmor_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marmor-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier typescript version of the Prologue<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and in an earlier manuscript version of the family tree of the Baggins family<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the two Hobbit brothers whe founded the Shire were called Marco and Cavallo.
