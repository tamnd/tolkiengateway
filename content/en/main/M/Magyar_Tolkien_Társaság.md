**Magyar Tolkien Társaság** (Hungarian: **Hungarian Tolkien Society**) is the [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md) in Hungary.

## Publications {#Publications}

HTS started publishing *Lassi Laurië* in the mid 2000s. In 2016 it became an online publication, and it currently has two issues a year.
