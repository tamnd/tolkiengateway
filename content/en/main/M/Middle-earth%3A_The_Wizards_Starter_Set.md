**Middle-earth: The Wizards Starter Set** was a box set aimed at new players of the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Come to Middle-earth as Gandalf the Grey or Saruman the White. Compete with your fellow Wizards for the honor and glory of leading the Free Peoples in their struggles against Sauron, the Dark Lord.
</p><p>This ready-to-play, two-player product contains everything you need to learn to play the award winning <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Wizards" title="Middle-earth: The Wizards">Middle-earth: The Wizards</a></i>.™ Then you can continue to explore Tolkien&#39;s world by using these cards to play again and again—each time facing different challenges and adventures.
</p><p><b>Contains:</b>
</p>
<ul><li>A 60-card ready-to-play &#34;Gandalf&#34; deck <b>and</b> a 60-card ready-to-play &#34;Saruman&#34; deck.</li>
<li>A full-color Middle-earth play map and two full-color player sheets.</li>
<li>An Initial Adventure Guide that leads two players step by step through a detailed sample game.</li>
<li>A short, quick-reference Abbreviated Starter Rules book and a large print Complete Rules book.</li>
<li>Two &#34;burning eye&#34; six-sided dice, two &#34;Hobbit&#34; company markers, and two marshalling point markers.</li>
<li>For 2 players. Ages 12 and up.</li>
<li>Cards provided are a selection of fixed, common and uncommon cards from <i>Middle-earth: The Wizards</i> and <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Dragons" title="Middle-earth: The Dragons">Middle-earth: The Dragons</a></i>™ sets.</li></ul>
</blockquote>
