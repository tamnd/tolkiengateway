<b><i>Mallorn</i> 51</b> is the fifty-first issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s biannual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Spring [2011](../_/2011.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Cover Art**
    - *Horse and Riders*
        - By: Becky Hitchin
- **Editorial**
    - "Ent and sources"
        - By: [Henry Gee](../H/Henry_Gee.md)
- **Reviews**
    - "Modern life": *Restoring Beauty: The Good, the True, and the Beautiful in the Writings of C.S. Lewis*
        - By: Frank Wilson
    - "Scholarship collected": *[Tolkien Studies: An Annual Scholarly Review, Vol. VII](../T/Tolkien_Studies%253A_Volume_7.md)*
        - By: Troels Forchhammer
    - "Keeping the faith": <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toy_Story_3" title="wikipedia:Toy Story 3">Toy Story 3</a></i>
        - By: [Chad Chisholm](../C/Chad_Chisholm.md)
- **Commentary**
    - "<a class="external text" href="http://www.josemanuelferrandez.com/ENguerra.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">J.R.R. Tolkien and the Spanish civil war</a>"
        - By: [José Manuel Ferrández Bru](../J/José_Manuel_Ferrández_Bru.md)
    - "*There and Back Again* and other travel books of the 1930s"
        - By: Dale Nelson
    - "The 'divine passive' in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*"
        - By: Kusumita Pedersen
    - "Ending the dualism of nature and industry in *The Lord of the Rings*"
        - By: Sarah J Sprouse
    - "Tolkien's use of free will versus predestination in *The Lord of the Rings*"
        - By: Bud Scott
- **Poetry**
    - "The Paths of the Dead"
        - By: Tuilinde
- **Fiction**
    - "How many beans make five"
        - By: Elizabeth Counihan
    - "The tale of Aud Sigurdsdottir"
        - By: Diane Forest-Hill
    - "Snicker-Snack and the Queen of Egypt"
        - By: Jeff Crook
- **Well, I'm back**
    - "Delving the [horn-cry](../H/Horn-call_of_Buckland.md) of [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md)"
        - By: Paul H. Vigor
- **Inside Art**
    - By: Bernadette Barnes, Phyllis Berka, Henry Gee, Becky Hitchin, Alan Hyde, Teresa Kirkpatrick, Glendon Mellow, [Jef Murray](../J/Jef_Murray.md), Colin Sullivan
