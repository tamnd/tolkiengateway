**Mark Poole** (born [31 August](../_/31_August.md) [1963](../_/1963.md)) is an American fantasy artist.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup> He was contracted by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create artwork for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.

Poole was one of the original illustrators for *[Magic: The Gathering](Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>, and he illustrated two pieces of alternate art for *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* expansion.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Career {#Career}

After graduating from the University of South Carolina with a degree in fine arts and design in 1986, he worked as an illustrator for advertising and merchandise. By the early 1990s, he had transitioned to fantasy work.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>

Poole works with oil, acrylic and digital media.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
