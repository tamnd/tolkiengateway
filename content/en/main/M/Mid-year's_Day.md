**Mid-year's Day** is the middle day of the year that roughly corresponds with the summer solstice or **Midsummer**.

In the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the [Gondolindrim](/wiki/Gondolindrim) celebrated the [Gates of Summer](/wiki/Gates_of_Summer).

In the context of the [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") calendars, the middle day of the [loa](/wiki/Loa "Loa") (year) is named *[Loëndë](/wiki/Lo%C3%ABnd%C3%AB)* in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), and is the second of the three [Enderi](/wiki/Enderi).

In the context of the [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning), the middle day is the second of the [Lithedays](/wiki/Lithedays), falling between [1 Lithe](/wiki/1_Lithe) and [2 Lithe](/wiki/2_Lithe), and (as the [Overlithe](/wiki/Overlithe)) had no weekday name. In [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), the [Free Fair](/wiki/Free_Fair) is held on the [White Downs](/wiki/White_Downs) during these Lithedays.
