The **mansions of Aulë**,<sup id="cite_ref-S1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S1-1">[1]</a></sup> also called the **halls of Aulë**,<sup id="cite_ref-S5_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S5-2">[2]</a></sup> was the dwelling of [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) in the midst of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), land of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar). In these mansions Aulë laboured long while making "*many beautiful and shapely works both openly and in secret*", creating both the tools and the lore of the craftsmen.<sup id="cite_ref-S1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S1-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is told that [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) and [his sons](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor") often visited the halls of Aulë.<sup id="cite_ref-S5_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S5-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In early versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), Aulë's house, situated in the outskirts of [Valmar](/wiki/Valmar) near its bordering open vale, is said to have consisted of a great court, "*filled with magic webs woven of the light of [Laurelin](/wiki/Laurelin) and the sheen of [Silpion](/wiki/Telperion "Telperion") and the glint of [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars")*"; still other webs were woven of [gold](/wiki/Gold "Gold"), [silver](/wiki/Silver "Silver"), iron, and bronze. This magic web reflected the [Music of the Ainur](/wiki/Music_of_the_Ainur) as it appeared in the mind of [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar). The court also had an area with a "*pool of blue water*" whereabout grew some of every tree that later grew upon the [earth](/wiki/Middle-earth "Middle-earth").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
