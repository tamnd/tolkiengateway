**Mythcon 32** was the thirty-second annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme:** "Many Dimensions: Modern Supernatural Fiction"
- **Guests of Honor:** David Llewellyn Dodds (Williams scholar) and [Peter S. Beagle](../P/Peter_S._Beagle.md) (fantasy author)
- **Chairman:** Eric Rauscher
