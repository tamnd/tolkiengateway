<b><i>Mallorn</i> 55</b> is the fifty-fifth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s annual journal *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")*, published in Winter [2014](/wiki/2014).

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Cover Art**
    - "Balrog"
        - By: [Jef Murray](/wiki/Jef_Murray)
- **Editorial**
    - "And it's Goodbye from me... and Hello from him"
        - By: [Helen Armstrong](/wiki/Helen_Armstrong)
- **Poetry**
    - *[Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) Tinúviel*
        - By: Philip Dodd
    - *Beowulf*
        - By: Kate Perlot
- **Commentary**
    - "Tolkien in [Bloemfontein](/wiki/Bloemfontein)"
        - By: Boris Gorelik
    - "[Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) and the Journey for [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth)"
        - By: Kerry Brooks
    - "'The House of his Spirit Crumbles': A medical consideration of [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir)'s condition on his return from the retreat from [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath), in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*"
        - By: Jennifer Urquart
    - "Reconstructing the Politics of the Dark Age"
        - By: [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey)
    - "'Seeing a Picture Before Us': Tolkien's commentary in [his translation of *Beowulf*](/wiki/Beowulf:_A_Translation_and_Commentary "Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary")"
        - By: [Mark Atherton](/wiki/Mark_Atherton)
    - "Tolkien's Technique of Translation in his Prose *Beowulf*: Literalism and Literariness"
        - By: Britton Brooks
    - "Tolkien and Folklore: *[Sellic Spell](/wiki/Sellic_Spell)* and *[The Lay of Beowulf](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Beowulf)*"
        - By: [Dimitra Fimi](/wiki/Dimitra_Fimi)
    - "Tolkien's Arthurian Twilight: Ancient influences in *[The Fall of Arthur](/wiki/The_Fall_of_Arthur)*"
        - By: Gregory J. Liebau
    - "Friendship in Tolkien's world"
        - By: Martina Juričková
    - "The Human Image and the interrelationship of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Men](/wiki/Men)"
        - By: David Tneh
    - "'Helpful, deprived, insulted, vengeful': Norse Mythology and Tolkien's [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves)"
        - By: Lilian Darvell
- **Reviews**
    - "Votes for [Tauriel](/wiki/Tauriel) - but not the love": *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)*
        - By: [Shaun Gunner](/wiki/Shaun_Gunner)
        - By: Richard Gonsowski
        - By: Sophie Watson
- **Well, I’m back**
    - "On Dwarves and Dwarfs"
        - By: Thor Ewing
- **Inside Art**
    - By: Michael Afford, [Helen Armstrong](/wiki/Helen_Armstrong), [Catherine Karina Chmiel](/wiki/Catherine_Karina_Chmiel) John Cockshaw, [Jenny Dolfen](/wiki/Jenny_Dolfen), [Anke Eissmann](/wiki/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann "Anke Eißmann"), Thor Ewing, Jay Johnstone, [Jef Murray](/wiki/Jef_Murray)
