<b><i>Mallorn</i> 48</b> is the forty-eighth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s biannual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Autumn [2009](../_/2009.md).
