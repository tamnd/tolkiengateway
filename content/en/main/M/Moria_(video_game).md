<dl><dd><i>Not to be confused with the MUD <a href="/wiki/The_Dungeons_of_Moria_(video_game)" title="The Dungeons of Moria (video game)">The Dungeons of Moria (video game)</a></i></dd></dl>

**Moria** is an illustrated, role-playing text-adventure video game set in [Moria](/wiki/Moria).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The objective of the game is to find [Durin's Ring](/wiki/Ring_of_Thr%C3%B3r "Ring of Thrór").<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> Although commercial, it was not licensed by the [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate) or the [Tolkien Enterprises](/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises "Middle-earth Enterprises").<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup>
