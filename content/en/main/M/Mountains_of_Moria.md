The **Mountains of Moria** were [Celebdil](/wiki/Celebdil), [Caradhras](/wiki/Caradhras) and [Fanuidhol](/wiki/Fanuidhol), beneath which [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") was delved by the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The main body of their city (later called [Moria](/wiki/Moria)) seems to have been beneath [Celebdil](/wiki/Celebdil), which they called **Zirakzigil**, and which was crowned by [Durin's Tower](/wiki/Durin%27s_Tower).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
