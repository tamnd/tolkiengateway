<b><i>Mallorn</i> 6</b> is the sixth issue of the Tolkien Society's journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in [1972](../_/1972.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Editorial**
    - Thoughts of Inanity, A Sort of Editorial
        - By Jon M. Harvey
    - Hobbit Mail (letters to the editor)
        - By Dave Weldrake, Sam Long, Archie Mercer, Tony Fallone
- **Notes**
    - Wanderer of the Wilderness
        - By Mike Cruden
    - The Races of Middle-Earth
        - By Archie Mercer
    - The Huntsmen of Fiction
        - By Jon M. Harvey
    - Belladonna Goes to a Party
        - By Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md))
- **Creative**
    - I Wish (poem)
        - By Lisa Conesa
    - Ghost in the Sun (poem)
        - By A.R. Fallone
    - Desolation (poem)
        - By Lisa Conesa
    - On Leaving the Main Sequence (poem)
        - By A.R. Fallone
- **Reviews**
    - *Voyage to Arcturus* by David Lindsay
        - Reviewed by A.R. Fallone
