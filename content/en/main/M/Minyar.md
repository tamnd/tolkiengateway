**Minyar** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya")), or Imillië<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the original name of the first Clan of the Elves, before the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey). The Clan-name, meaning 'Firsts' was derived from the name of the father of the Clan, [Imin](/wiki/Imin) 'One', the first [Elf-father](/wiki/Elf-fathers "Elf-fathers") to awake at [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen).

The other original clans in Cuiviénen were the [Tatyar](/wiki/Tatyar) 'Seconds', and the [Nelyar](/wiki/Nelyar) 'Thirds' according to the Elf-fathers [Tata](/wiki/Tata) 'Two' and [Enel](/wiki/Enel) 'Three'. The Minyar were 14 in number out of the total 144 Elves who awoke at Cuiviénen. They all followed [Ingwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB) in the March and became the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar). None remained behind.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
