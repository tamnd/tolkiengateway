**Minyar** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md)), or Imillië<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the original name of the first Clan of the Elves, before the [Great Journey](../G/Great_Journey.md). The Clan-name, meaning 'Firsts' was derived from the name of the father of the Clan, [Imin](../I/Imin.md) 'One', the first [Elf-father](../E/Elf-fathers.md) to awake at [Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md).

The other original clans in Cuiviénen were the [Tatyar](../T/Tatyar.md) 'Seconds', and the [Nelyar](../N/Nelyar.md) 'Thirds' according to the Elf-fathers [Tata](../T/Tata.md) 'Two' and [Enel](../E/Enel.md) 'Three'. The Minyar were 14 in number out of the total 144 Elves who awoke at Cuiviénen. They all followed [Ingwë](../I/Ingwë.md) in the March and became the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md). None remained behind.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
