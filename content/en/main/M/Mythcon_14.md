**Mythcon 14** was the fourteenth annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme:** "Mythic Structures in [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), [Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md), and [Williams](../C/Charles_Williams.md)"
- **Guests of Honor:** [C.S. Kilby](../C/Clyde_S._Kilby.md) (Inklings scholar &amp; curator) and Stephen R. Donaldson (fantasy author)
- **Chairman:** [Glen GoodKnight](../G/Glen_GoodKnight.md)
