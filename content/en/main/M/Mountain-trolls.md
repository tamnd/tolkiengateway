**Mountain-trolls** were used by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) to wield the great battering-ram, [Grond](</wiki/Grond_(battering_ram)> "Grond (battering ram)"), at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

There is no other known reference to mountain-trolls in the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), so nothing else can be known about these creatures. One can only assume that this breed of [troll](/wiki/Troll "Troll") lived in some of the [mountains](/wiki/Category:Mountains "Category:Mountains") of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and that they were either larger than [hill-trolls](/wiki/Hill-trolls "Hill-trolls") or another name for hill-trolls.
