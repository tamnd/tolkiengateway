**Michael George Reuel Tolkien** (born [11 January](../_/11_January.md) [1943](../_/1943.md)) is the grandson of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), being the son of [Michael H.R. Tolkien](Michael_Tolkien.md). Michael was born in [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md), raised in a rural community in the Chiltern Hills, and then moved several times due to his father's changes of employment.

Michael is a poet and author of several books. He retired in 1994 due to ill health. He has been married three times since 1967 and has two daughters, Catherine and Ruth. Michael is a member of the board of the [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md).
