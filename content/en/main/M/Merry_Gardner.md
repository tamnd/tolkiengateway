**Merry Gardner** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), the second son and fourth child of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton).<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

At the suggestion of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), Sam named one of his children after [Merry Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Merry was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1427](/wiki/Fourth_Age_6 "Fourth Age 6").<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Merry* is a translation of [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) [Westron](/wiki/Westron) (specifically, the Buckland dialect) *Kali*, meaning "jolly, gay".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), the name *Merry* is *Gelir*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
