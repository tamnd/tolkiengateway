**Menel** was an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) (both [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)) name for the regions of [air](</wiki/Vilya_(word)> "Vilya (word)") above [Ambar](/wiki/Ambar#Ambar), a name which is generally meant as "firmament, heaven" or "the heavens".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Sometimes the word is simply synonymous with [the sky](/wiki/Ilmen "Ilmen"), but in other uses it is meant as a more sublime reference to the domain of the [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars") and heavenly bodies.

Menel is a physical place within [Arda](/wiki/Arda), although arguably a sacred one as it is the location of the [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars") of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Elbereth" title="Elbereth">Elbereth</a>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is understood as *[men](</wiki/Men_(Elvish)> "Men (Elvish)")* "towards" + *[el](/wiki/El "El")* "star" meaning "the Direction of the Stars".<sup id="cite_ref-RGEO_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RGEO-2">[2]</a></sup>

The loremaster [Quennar](/wiki/Quennar) is attributed as having devised the word *Menel* ("heavens, firmament").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

While writing *[Átaremma](/wiki/%C3%81taremma)*, Tolkien used *menel* to translate "Heaven", but in the final forms he preferred *ëa han ëa* "beyond [Creation](/wiki/Ea "Ea")"; distinguishing thus *menel* (heaven, as the physical sky) from the metaphysical Heaven.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

Menel is seen in numerous personal and place-names, including [Meneltarma](/wiki/Meneltarma) (the "Pillar of Heaven"), [Meneldil](/wiki/Meneldil), [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur), [Armenelos](/wiki/Armenelos) and [Meneldor](/wiki/Meneldor).

It also appears in *[A Elbereth Gilthoniel](/wiki/A_Elbereth_Gilthoniel)* "*o menel [aglar](/wiki/Aglar "Aglar") [elen](/wiki/Elen "Elen")[ath](/wiki/-ath "-ath")*" ("from the glories of the star-host of heaven").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

[Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") used it in his invocation from [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol): "*o menel [palan](/wiki/Palan "Palan")-[diriel](/wiki/Tir "Tir")*" ("from heaven gazing afar").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

It already existed in [Avarin](/wiki/Avarin), and survived as such in [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) *minal*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>
