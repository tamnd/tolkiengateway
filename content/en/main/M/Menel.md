**Menel** was an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) (both [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)) name for the regions of [air](<../V/Vilya_(word).md>) above [Ambar](../A/Ambar.md#Ambar), a name which is generally meant as "firmament, heaven" or "the heavens".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Sometimes the word is simply synonymous with [the sky](../I/Ilmen.md), but in other uses it is meant as a more sublime reference to the domain of the [stars](../S/Stars.md) and heavenly bodies.

Menel is a physical place within [Arda](../A/Arda.md), although arguably a sacred one as it is the location of the [stars](../S/Stars.md) of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../E/Elbereth.md" title="Elbereth">Elbereth</a>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is understood as *[men](<Men_(Elvish).md>)* "towards" + *[el](../E/El.md)* "star" meaning "the Direction of the Stars".<sup id="cite_ref-RGEO_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RGEO-2">[2]</a></sup>

The loremaster [Quennar](../Q/Quennar.md) is attributed as having devised the word *Menel* ("heavens, firmament").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

While writing *[Átaremma](../_/Átaremma.md)*, Tolkien used *menel* to translate "Heaven", but in the final forms he preferred *ëa han ëa* "beyond [Creation](../E/Ea~22487.md)"; distinguishing thus *menel* (heaven, as the physical sky) from the metaphysical Heaven.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

Menel is seen in numerous personal and place-names, including [Meneltarma](Meneltarma.md) (the "Pillar of Heaven"), [Meneldil](Meneldil.md), [Tar-Meneldur](../T/Tar-Meneldur.md), [Armenelos](../A/Armenelos.md) and [Meneldor](Meneldor.md).

It also appears in *[A Elbereth Gilthoniel](../A/A_Elbereth_Gilthoniel.md)* "*o menel [aglar](../A/Aglar.md) [elen](../E/Elen.md)[ath](../_/-ath.md)*" ("from the glories of the star-host of heaven").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

[Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) used it in his invocation from [Cirith Ungol](../C/Cirith_Ungol.md): "*o menel [palan](../P/Palan.md)-[diriel](../T/Tir.md)*" ("from heaven gazing afar").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

It already existed in [Avarin](../A/Avarin.md), and survived as such in [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) *minal*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>
