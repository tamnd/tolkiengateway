**Magic: The Gathering \| The Hobbit** is an expansion for the [Magic: The Gathering](Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md) collectible card game, which was released on [14 August](../_/14_August.md) [2026](../_/2026.md) under the *Universes Beyond* sub-brand. It was the second Lord of the Rings themed expansion for the card game, the first being *[The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Tales_of_Middle-earth.md)*, which was released in [2023](../_/2023.md).

## History {#History}

The expansion was announced at MagicCon Atlanta 2025.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In May [2026](../_/2026.md), an illustration of the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md) created by [Dan Frazier](../D/Dan_Frazier.md) was found to have been plagiarized from an earlier illustration by [Marta Nael](Marta_Nael.md), which was later confirmed by both the artist and [Wizards of the Coast](../W/Wizards_of_the_Coast.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Products {#Products}

Unlike its predecessor, no proper preconstructed decks were released, though simplified decks aimed at beginners were handed out by game stores.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Boosters and booster boxes came in both regular and collector-oriented varieties, the latter including exclusive cards. A *Bundle* and *Gift Bundle* were also released.<sup id="cite_ref-prodoverview_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prodoverview-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Accessories {#Accessories}

### Dice {#Dice}

In Magic: The Gathering, dice are used to track resources such as life points and counters. Both the *Bundle* and *Gift Bundle* came with oversized themed twenty-sided dice to count lifepoints specifically.<sup id="cite_ref-prodoverview_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prodoverview-7">[7]</a></sup> The pre-release packs came with one of several regular-sized twenty-sided dice, with the same purpose.<sup id="cite_ref-prerelease_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prerelease-8">[8]</a></sup>

Q Workshop released several sets of dice under license from [New Line Cinema](../N/New_Line_Cinema.md), themed after the *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)* film series rather than the card expansion. This set comes with an oversized twenty-sided die, two twelve-sided dice with symbols representing Bilbo and Thorin, a ten-sided die adorned with Gandalf's symbols, and then twelve six-sided dice depicting the remaining Dwarves. The oversized die and the two twelve-sided dice have different colours per set, while the other dice are all identical.<sup id="cite_ref-qw_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-qw-9">[9]</a></sup>
