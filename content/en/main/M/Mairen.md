**Mairen** was the third child and only daughter of [Tar-Amandil](/wiki/Tar-Amandil), the third [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor). Her eldest brother would take up the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre) as [Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil). Almost nothing is known of her life.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Mairen* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it is a feminized form of the adjective *maira* ("admirable", "excellent", "precious", "splendid", "sublime").<sup id="cite_ref-Strack_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strack-2">[2]</a></sup> The original name of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) was *Mairon* ("Admirable"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> which seems to be a masculinized form of the adjective *maira*.<sup id="cite_ref-Strack_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strack-2">[2]</a></sup>
