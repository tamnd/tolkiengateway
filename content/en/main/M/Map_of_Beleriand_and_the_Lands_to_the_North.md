The **Map of Beleriand and the Lands to the North**, appearing in the *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, depicts the region of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).

The map is a redrawing by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) from [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s "[Second 'Silmarillion' map](../T/The_Second_'Silmarillion'_Map.md)", which was divided on four sheets and which was the last map of Beleriand that John Ronald made (the "original" maps, without alterations, were reproduced in *[The Lost Road](../T/The_Lost_Road.md)*).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> However, Tolkien continued to add and change names or features on the map manuscripts; these were reproduced by Christopher in *[The War of the Jewels](../T/The_War_of_the_Jewels.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[A colored version](<../../file/C/Christopher_Tolkien_-_Map_of_Beleriand_(colored_by_H.E._Riddett).jpg.md>) made by [H.E. Riddett](../H/H.E._Riddett.md) is commonly printed in the endpaper of *The Silmarillion* hardback editions.

<br>

## Errors {#Errors}

Christopher Tolkien acknowledged that this map contained separate sources for the rivers [Esgalduin](../E/Esgalduin.md) and [Aros](../A/Aros.md). In the original map, the rivers shared the [same source](../S/Shadowy_Spring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
