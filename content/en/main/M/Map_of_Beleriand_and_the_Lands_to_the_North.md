The **Map of Beleriand and the Lands to the North**, appearing in the *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, depicts the region of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).

The map is a redrawing by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s "[Second 'Silmarillion' map](/wiki/The_Second_%27Silmarillion%27_Map "The Second 'Silmarillion' Map")", which was divided on four sheets and which was the last map of Beleriand that John Ronald made (the "original" maps, without alterations, were reproduced in *[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)*).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> However, Tolkien continued to add and change names or features on the map manuscripts; these were reproduced by Christopher in *[The War of the Jewels](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[A colored version](</wiki/File:Christopher_Tolkien_-_Map_of_Beleriand_(colored_by_H.E._Riddett).jpg> "File:Christopher Tolkien - Map of Beleriand (colored by H.E. Riddett).jpg") made by [H.E. Riddett](/wiki/H.E._Riddett) is commonly printed in the endpaper of *The Silmarillion* hardback editions.

<br>

## Errors {#Errors}

Christopher Tolkien acknowledged that this map contained separate sources for the rivers [Esgalduin](/wiki/Esgalduin) and [Aros](/wiki/Aros). In the original map, the rivers shared the [same source](/wiki/Shadowy_Spring "Shadowy Spring").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
