<b><i>Mythlore</i> 42</b> (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/mythlore/vol11/iss4/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Volume 11, Issue 4</a>) is an issue of the *[Mythlore](/wiki/Mythlore)* journal, published by the [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society).

## Contents {#Contents}

This is the first of several short supplemental issues that *Mythlore* published in order to comply with bulk mailing permit regulations. This issue mainly consisted of an editorial by Glen H. GoodKnight and information about the upcoming 17th Mythopoeic Conference.
