**Mablung** means "Heavy Hand" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mab&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mab (page does not exist)">mab</a>-</i>, "hand" and *[long](/wiki/Long "Long")*, "heavy"), and can refer to:

- **[Mablung](/wiki/Mablung) (died [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [503](/wiki/First_Age_503 "First Age 503")), an [Elven](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") captain of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) killed during a [battle in the Thousand Caves](/wiki/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves "Battle in the Thousand Caves").**
- [Mablung (ranger of Ithilien)](</wiki/Mablung_(ranger_of_Ithilien)>) (fl. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")), a [Ranger of Ithilien](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien "Rangers of Ithilien") during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).
