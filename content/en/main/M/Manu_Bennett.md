**Jonathan "Manu" Bennett** (born [10 October](/wiki/10_October), [1969](/wiki/1969) in Rotorua, New Zealand) is an [Australian](/wiki/Australia "Australia")-[New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) actor who portrayed [Azog](/wiki/Azog) through <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion-capture_acting" title="wikipedia:Motion-capture acting">performance-capture</a> in the [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"). At a young age, Bennet moved to Australia with his mother, a bikini model, and his father, a singer. He went on to attend Merewether High School<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and later returned to New Zealand to attend Te Aute College, where he played on the college rugby team. Bennett then quit rugby to study drama and dance and later moved to Los Angeles after receiving a scholarship to attend the Lee Strasberg Theatre Institute. Bennett has three children with his ex-wife<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Karin Horen<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>.
