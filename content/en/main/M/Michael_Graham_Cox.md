**Michael Graham Cox** ([8 January](/wiki/8_January) [1938](/wiki/1938) - [30 April](/wiki/30_April) [1995](/wiki/1995)) was a British actor who voiced the part of [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"). He would reprise this role in the [*The Lord of the Rings* radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") broadcast on [BBC](/wiki/BBC) Radio 4 in [1981](/wiki/1981).
