<b><i>Mallorn</i> 62</b> is the sixty-second issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s annual journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in Winter [2021](../_/2021.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- **Front Matter**
    - Editorial by Luke Shelton
- **Articles**
    - "A Song of Greater Power: Tolkien’s Construction of Lúthien Tinúviel" by Clare Moore
    - "The Service of Samwise: Heroism, Imagination, and Restoration" by Sarah Shahan
    - "The Red Book and Tolkien’s *The Lord of the Rings*: A Fantasic Uncertainty" by Vincent Ferré and Pauline Loquin
- **Notes**
    - "Tolkien’s Friend Selby" by [Douglas A. Anderson](../D/Douglas_A._Anderson.md)
    - "Hyphens as Sub-Lexical Morphemes in *The Hobbit*" by Sparrow Alden
    - "Can You Tell Me How to Get, How to Get to Watling Street?" by Kristine Larsen
- **Reviews**
    - *[Middle-earth, or There and Back Again](Middle-earth,_or_There_and_Back_Again.md). Cormarë Series #44* by [Łukasz Neubauer](../_/Łukasz_Neubauer.md) (reviewed by Tamsin Barlow and Milton Nye Weatherhead)
    - *[Tolkien and the Classical World](../T/Tolkien_and_the_Classical_World.md). Cormarë Series #45*, ed. Hamish Williams (reviewed by Shawn Marchese)
    - <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Following_the_Formula_in_Beowulf,_%C3%96rvar-Odds_saga,_and_Tolkien&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Following the Formula in Beowulf, Örvar-Odds saga, and Tolkien (page does not exist)">Following the Formula in Beowulf, Örvar-Odds saga, and Tolkien</a></i> by Michael Fox (reviewed by Richard Rohlin)
    - *[Tolkien’s Modern Reading: Middle-earth Beyond the Middle Ages](../T/Tolkien's_Modern_Reading.md)* by [Holly Ordway](../H/Holly_Ordway.md) (reviewed by Putri Prihatini)
- **Submissions**
    - Letters
    - Errata in [Mallorn 60](Mallorn_60.md)
    - Guide for Authors
