**Menegilda Brandybuck, née Goold**, was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Menegilda married [Rorimac Brandybuck](/wiki/Rorimac_Brandybuck), and with him produced two children, [Saradoc](/wiki/Saradoc_Brandybuck "Saradoc Brandybuck") and [Merimac](/wiki/Merimac_Brandybuck "Merimac Brandybuck").

Unlike her husband and sons, she was not present at [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' [Birthday Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name contains *meneg* "much" and *hild* "battle".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
