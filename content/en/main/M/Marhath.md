**Marhath** was the name of the Fourth Master of the [Mark](/wiki/Riddermark "Riddermark"), in the drafts for *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In an earlier text, he held the position of the [Second Master of the Mark](/wiki/Second_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark "Second Marshal of the Riddermark").<sup id="cite_ref-Marshal_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marshal-1">[1]</a></sup>
