**Michael Elsworth** ([June 12](../_/12_June.md), [1932](../_/1932.md)) is an English actor, now living in [New Zealand](../N/New_Zealand.md). He worked with [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) in an uncredited role in <i><a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0116365/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Frighteners</a></i>, and work with him again in Jackson's grand opus. He is credited as the "[Gondorian](../G/Gondorians.md) Archivist" in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, but also appeared uncredited as [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) and doubled for [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) when [Ian McKellen](../I/Ian_McKellen.md) was unavailable.
