**Michael Elsworth** ([June 12](/wiki/12_June "12 June"), [1932](/wiki/1932)) is an English actor, now living in [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand). He worked with [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) in an uncredited role in <i><a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0116365/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Frighteners</a></i>, and work with him again in Jackson's grand opus. He is credited as the "[Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") Archivist" in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, but also appeared uncredited as [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) and doubled for [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) when [Ian McKellen](/wiki/Ian_McKellen) was unavailable.
