**Mewlips** were a legendary race of evil creatures only mentioned in [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") verse. The creatures were likely fictitious, although they could have been inspired by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Living beyond the [Merlock Mountains](/wiki/Merlock_Mountains) and the marsh of [Tode](/wiki/Tode), they are said to have fed on passers-by, collecting their bones in a sack.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) learned that the [Long Marshes](/wiki/Long_Marshes) had grown wider, consuming paths and lost people.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In plot notes that [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) made while writing the story the raftsmen told tales of disappearing rafts, men, and beasts;<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff) suggests that the more ominous line in the notes may indicate that Tolkien had some unpleasantness in mind had [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) gotten into the marshes - perhaps meeting the Mewlips.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><i>Mewlips</i>, described and given statistics in various supplements, are portrayed as undead beings living in swamps and marshes. The race is conceived as having been created by <a href="/wiki/Morgoth" title="Morgoth">Morgoth</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Elder_Days" title="Elder Days">Elder Days</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>
