**Marquette University** is a private Jesuit research university in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, established in 1881.

The university is well known for holding a large number of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s original manuscripts (more than 11,000 pieces). <a class="external text" href="https://www.marquette.edu/library/archives/tolkien.php" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">William Fliss</a> is the Marquette University Archivist who maintains the collection. [Matt Blessing](Matt_Blessing.md) was previously in charge of maintaining the collection (1999-2012).

## History of the collection {#History_of_the_collection}

In late December [1956](../_/1956.md) or early January [1957](../_/1957.md) [William Ready](../W/William_Ready.md), the Director of Libraries at Marquette University, was compiling the University's research-related collections and he asked Bertram Rota, a well-known London bookseller if she could approach J.R.R. Tolkien to see if he was willing to sell the original manuscripts of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. Rota wrote to Tolkien and prior to [January 10th](../_/10_January.md) Tolkien replied.

On [January 10th](../_/10_January.md), Bertram Rota responded to William Ready at Marquette. Tolkien was willing to consider selling his manuscripts but asked what amount the University was willing to offer. He sent a list of *The Hobbit* and *The Lord of the Rings* material he considered selling. Rota suggested to Ready a minimum offer of £1,000, however she would have liked to examine the documents personally before the final decision was made. At last the price was set to £1500.

### The manuscripts {#The_manuscripts}

This arrangement was finalized in May of [1957](../_/1957.md), and a significant amount of material was transferred across the Atlantic that June, with *The Lord of the Rings* material being sent mostly in [1958](../_/1958.md) and later additional material being sent by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) when discovered in his father’s papers.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- More that 1,500 pages (manuscript, typescript, and corrected proofs) for *The Hobbit*, including preliminary dustjacket designs.
- More than 200 pages (manuscript, typescript, and corrected proofs) for *Farmer Giles of Ham*, but not including notes for the foreword and unfinished sequel.
- The complete illustrated manuscript for *Mr. Bliss*, at more than 60 pages.
- More than 9,200 pages of material for *The Lord of the Rings* including the unfinished *Epilogue*, but not including almost all of Tolkien’s artwork.

## Tolkien exhibitions {#Tolkien_exhibitions}

The Marquette University has held a number of [exhibitions focused on Tolkien](../T/Tolkien_exhibitions.md).

- [1983](../_/1983.md) — "The Road Goes Ever On" (conference) and "The Manuscripts of J.R.R.T." (exhibition)
    - Exhibition catalogue — *[The Manuscripts of J.R.R.T.](../T/The_Manuscripts_of_J.R.R.T..md)*

- [1987](../_/1987.md) — "J.R.R.Tolkien: The Hobbit Drawings, Watercolors, and Manuscripts" (exhibition)
    - Exhibition catalogue — *[J.R.R. Tolkien: The Hobbit: Drawings, Watercolors, and Manuscripts](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_The_Hobbit.md)*

- [1987](../_/1987.md) — "Selections from the Marquette J.R.R. Tolkien Collection" (exhibition)
    - Exhibition catalogue — *[Selections from the Marquette J.R.R. Tolkien Collection](../S/Selections_from_the_Marquette_J.R.R._Tolkien_Collection.md)*

- [2004](../_/2004.md) — "The Invented Worlds of J.R.R. Tolkien" (exhibition)
    - Exhibition catalogue — *[The Invented Worlds of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Invented_Worlds_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*

- [2022](../_/2022.md) — "J.R.R. Tolkien: The Art of the Manuscript" (exhibition, collaborate with the Haggerty Museum of Art)
    - Exhibition catalogue — *[J.R.R. Tolkien: The Art of the Manuscript](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_The_Art_of_the_Manuscript.md)*
