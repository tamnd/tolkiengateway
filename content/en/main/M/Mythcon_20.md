**Mythcon 20** was the twentieth annual [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) conference.

- **Theme:** "Mythic Elements in Fantasy"
- **Guests of Honor:** [Guy Gavriel Kay](/wiki/Guy_Gavriel_Kay) (fantasy author) and Raymond H. Thompson (Arthurian scholar)
- **Chairman:** Lynn J.R. Wytenbroek
