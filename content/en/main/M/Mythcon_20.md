**Mythcon 20** was the twentieth annual [Mythopoeic Society](Mythopoeic_Society.md) conference.

- **Theme:** "Mythic Elements in Fantasy"
- **Guests of Honor:** [Guy Gavriel Kay](../G/Guy_Gavriel_Kay.md) (fantasy author) and Raymond H. Thompson (Arthurian scholar)
- **Chairman:** Lynn J.R. Wytenbroek
