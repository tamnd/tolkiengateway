**Methed-en-glad** was a stronghold of [Dor-Cúarthol](/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol) at the edge of the forest south of the [Crossings of the Taeglin](/wiki/Crossings_of_the_Taeglin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The stronghold of Methed-en-glad was one of the many guarded camps and fortresses that were established around the [Echad i Sedryn](/wiki/House_of_Ransom "House of Ransom") during the brief existence of [Dor-Cúarthol](/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol), the realm ruled by the [Two Captains](/wiki/Two_Captains). It was said that from Methed-en-glad, there was a view to the south where the summit of Amon Rûdh could be seen and commands and messages could be received to and from both locations through signals.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Methed-en-glad* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "the End of the Wood", derived from *[methed](/wiki/Methed "Methed")* ("end") + *[en](/wiki/En "En")* ("of the") + *[glad](/wiki/Glad "Glad")* ("wood").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
