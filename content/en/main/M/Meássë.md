**Meássë** was a [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") only appearing in the earliest versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. She is described as the "warrior [goddess](/wiki/Gods "Gods")" and an "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazons" title="wikipedia:Amazons">Amazon</a> with bloody arms". She was the "fierce sister" of [Makar](/wiki/Makar).

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:A-denn_-_Measse.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/b/b6/A-denn_-_Measse.jpg/150px-A-denn_-_Measse.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="150" height="300" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/b/b6/A-denn_-_Measse.jpg/225px-A-denn_-_Measse.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/b/b6/A-denn_-_Measse.jpg/300px-A-denn_-_Measse.jpg 2x" data-file-width="960" data-file-height="1920"/></a><figcaption><i>Measse</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_A-denn" title="Category:Images by A-denn">A-denn</a></figcaption></figure>

Meássë was among the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) who aligned themselves with [Melko](/wiki/Melko) during the [Music of the Ainur](/wiki/Music_of_the_Ainur).<sup id="cite_ref-Valar_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valar-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:67</sup>

Upon their arrival to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), she and her brother Makar made an abode in the northern [Aman](/wiki/Aman), close to the [Halls of Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos).<sup id="cite_ref-Valar_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valar-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:79</sup> She, along with her brother, rode often to the [Great Lands](/wiki/Middle-earth "Middle-earth"), where they hunted wolves and bears in the black mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Valar_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valar-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) awoke, and the Valar invited them to live among the Gods, she backed Makar in his opposition to the rest of them, saying that Valinor was built for the Valar only.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

She joined her brother and the rest of the Valar in the pursuit of Melko and his servants, who were escaping from Valinor after the destruction of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><b>Meássë</b></i> is a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) name, deriving from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=MEHE&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MEHE (page does not exist)">MEHE</a> ("ooze?") and related to the word *mear* ("gore"). Its [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) equivalent is <i><b>Mechos</b></i> or <i><b>Mechothli</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ravi_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ravi-4">[4]</a></sup>

Among her other names was <i><b>Rávi</b></i> (related to *ravennë* "she-[lion](/wiki/Lions "Lions")").,<sup id="cite_ref-Ravi_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ravi-4">[4]</a></sup> as well as <i><b>Tarkil</b></i>, deriving from the root [TARA](/wiki/TARA) ("to batter, thud, beat").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
