**Middle-earth: The Riddles** is an online riddle contest that was made by [Sarassota Collectibles](/wiki/Sarassota_Collectibles) to promote the card game [Middle-earth: The Wizards](/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Wizards).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> At the moment the contest is closed. It was promised that there would come more contests, however fifteen year later there still haven't been any new contests.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> The game was written by [Todd Stevens](/wiki/Todd_Stevens).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
