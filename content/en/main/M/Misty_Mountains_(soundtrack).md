**Misty Mountains** is the fifth track of disc one of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)* and the sixth of the [special edition soundtrack](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack_-_Special_Edition "The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack - Special Edition"). It is based on [the book's](/wiki/The_Hobbit "The Hobbit") "[Far over the misty mountains cold](/wiki/Far_over_the_misty_mountains_cold)".
