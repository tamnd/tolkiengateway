**Melilot Brandybuck** (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1385](/wiki/Third_Age_2985 "Third Age 2985")) was a distant cousin of both [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).

## History {#History}

Melilot was the daughter of [Marmadas Brandybuck](/wiki/Marmadas_Brandybuck).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She was present at [Bilbo's Birthday Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001"), when she was just sixteen years old. The only part she played in the great events of that time was to dance the [springle-ring](/wiki/Springle-ring "Springle-ring") at the party with [Everard Took](/wiki/Everard_Took).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/melilot" title="wikipedia:melilot">Melilot</a></i>, or Sweet Clover, is a grassland plant. The name derives from Greek *μελίλωτος* "honey lotus".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), *Melilot* was a tentative name for the character later known as [Lúthien Tinúviel](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien "Lúthien").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
