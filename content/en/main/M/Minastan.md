**Minastan** was the second son of King [Minardil](/wiki/Minardil) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Minastan's elder brother [Telemnar](/wiki/Telemnar) had sat on their father's throne for just two years when he died with all his heirs in the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Minastan had also died by this time - perhaps he too met his end in the Plague - but he left behind a surviving heir, [Tarondor](</wiki/Tarondor_(King_of_Gondor)> "Tarondor (King of Gondor)"), who became the first King of a new line.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Minastan* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). Its meaning is not glossed, but as Paul Strack explains, it might mean "Smith of the Tower" and be a compound of a shortened form *minas* of [minasse](/wiki/Minasse "Minasse") ("tower") and the suffixal form *-tan* of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tamo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tamo (page does not exist)">tamo</a> ("smith, builder").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
