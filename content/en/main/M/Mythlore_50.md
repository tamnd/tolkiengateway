<b><i>Mythlore</i> 50</b> (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/mythlore/vol13/iss4/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Volume 13, Issue 4</a>) is an issue of the *[Mythlore](/wiki/Mythlore)* journal, published by the [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society).

In a letter published in the journal (p. 41), [L. Sprague de Camp](/wiki/L._Sprague_de_Camp) quotes from [a 1964 letter from Tolkien](/wiki/Letter_to_L._Sprague_de_Camp "Letter to L. Sprague de Camp").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

#### Articles {#Articles}

- Wytenbroe, J. R. “Rites of Passage in The Hobbit.”
- Hyles, Vernon. “On the Nature of Evil: The Cosmic Myths of Lewis, Tolkien and Williams.”
- Musacchio, George. “Elwin Ransom: The Pilgrimage Begins.”
- Croft, Edward. “Where Words Fall Short: Limitations of Language in All Hallows' Eve.”
- Filmer, Kath. “An Allegory Unveiled: A Reading of The Lord of the Rings.”
- Agøy, Nils Ivar. “A Nodal Structure in Tolkien’s Tales of the First Age?”
- Unwin, Rayner, et al. “20 Years and 50 Issues.”
- Schakel, Peter J. “That ‘Hideous Strength’ in Lewis and Orwell: A Comparison and Contrast.”
- Welch, Randy C. “Watership Down: The Individual &amp; Society.”
- Christopher, Joe R. “John Heath-Stubbs’ Artorius and the Influence of Charles Williams.”

#### Poetry {#Poetry}

- Le Guin, Ursula K. “NM.”
- Yolen, Jane. “Amesbury Song.”

#### Features {#Features}

- GoodKnight, Glen. Editor's Opening - Two Decades: Looking Back.
- Christopher, Joe R. An Inklings' Bibliography (31).
- Kondratiev, Alexei. “Tales Newly Told.”
- Hyde, Paul Nolan. “Quenti Lambardillion: A Column on Middle-earth Linguistics.”
- Letters.

- *Daughter of Regals &amp; Other Tales.* Stephen R. Donaldson. Reviewed by Thomas M. Egan.
- *Elfquest — The Blood of Ten Chiefs, Volume One.* Edited by Richard Pini, Robert Asprin, and Lynn Abby. Reviewed by David Bratman.
- *Shadowlands*. Film produced by the BBC and the Episcopal Radio-TV Foundation. Directed by Norman Stone; written by William Nicholson. Reviewed by David Bratman.
- *Charles Williams.* Kathleen Spencer. Reviewed by Nancy-Lou Patterson.
- *Around the Year with C.S. Lewis and His Friends.* Kathryn Lindskoog, compiler. Reviewed by Nancy-Lou Patterson.
