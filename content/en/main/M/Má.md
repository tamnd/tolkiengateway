<i><b>má</b></i> (d. **mát**, pl. **máli**) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "hand".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[PQ](/wiki/PQ) \**maȝ*, [root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=MA%C8%9C&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MAȜ (page does not exist)">MAȜ</a><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

- *máryat*: his/her two hands
- *mannar*: into hands<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- *mánta*: their hand
    - *mántat*: their hands<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Lungum%C3%A1" title="Lungumá">Lungu<b>má</b></a></i> = "Heavyhand"
- <i><a href="/wiki/M%C3%A1tengwi%C3%AB" title="Mátengwië"><b>má</b>tengwië</a></i> = "language of the hands"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Nam%C3%A1ri%C3%AB" title="Namárië">ve fanyar <b>má</b>ryat Elentári ortanë</a></i> = "has uplifted her hands like clouds"
