**meneg** means "thousand" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Possibly from [OS](/wiki/OS) \**meneke* ([root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=MENEK&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MENEK (page does not exist)">MENEK</a>)

## Example {#Example}

- [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth) "Thousand Caves"

## Note {#Note}

It is not clear if the word refers to proverbial thousand, as in "a great number", or to the exact number 1,000.

If it is the latter, we can suppose that if the word refers to the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Duodecimal_system&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Duodecimal system (page does not exist)">duodecimal system</a> of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), their 1,000 is rather 1728 (144 X 12).

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

*Meneg* is a Germanic word meaning "many"
