<i><b>Middle-earth: Visions of a Modern Myth</b></i> is a collection of artwork by [Donato Giancola](/wiki/Donato_Giancola), inspired by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>From the brush of Donato Giancola, one of the world&#39;s most recognized and awarded fantasy artists, comes a book filled with new illustrations that blend his legendary Renaissance craftsmanship with the visions outlined in J.R.R. Tolkein&#39;s [<i>sic!</i>] Middle-Earth [<i>sic!</i>]. Dramatic lighting and deft draftsmanship seemingly culled from the tableaux of great Old Master painters like Caravaggio, Rembrandt and Vermeer explain Donato&#39;s popularity with millions of fans and his numerous Hugo and Chesley Awards for his oil paintings. This long-awaited moving and beautiful voyage through Middle-Earth from a modern master of the arts will be a must-have for eager genre readers everywhere.
</p>
</blockquote>
