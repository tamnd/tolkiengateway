The <b><i>Moose Jaw Times-Herald: Weekend Magazine</i> (Vol. 18, No. 35)</b>, published on [31 August](/wiki/31_August) [1968](/wiki/1968), is a weekend supplement of the Canadian newspaper <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moose_Jaw_Times-Herald" title="wikipedia:Moose Jaw Times-Herald">Moose Jaw Times-Herald</a></i>. It includes a reprint of the [Plimmers' interview](/wiki/The_Man_Who_Understands_Hobbits "The Man Who Understands Hobbits") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) ("The Brains behind Bilbo Baggins", pp. 6–8).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
