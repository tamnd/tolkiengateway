<b><i>Mallorn</i> 7</b> is the seventh issue of the Tolkien Society's journal *[Mallorn](<Mallorn_(journal).md>)*, published in [1973](../_/1973.md). It was the first issue to be published after the death of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- Editorial
    - Well - Er - Um - Ahem!
        - By "The Numenoreans"
    - Letters to the Editor
        - By John Abbott, Jim Allan, Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md)), and Pat McIntosh
- Notes
    - Namarie (obituary of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md))
        - By Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md))
    - The Geology of Middle-Earth
        - By Pat McIntosh
    - The Story of the Silmarillion, Part One
        - By Jim Allan
    - A Vestige of the Shire
        - By Jessica Kemball-Cook
- Creative
    - The Legend of Saint Acrilan (poem)
        - By Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md))
    - Legolas on Anduin (poem)
        - By Pat McIntosh
    - Vuldathoom (short story)
        - By Gordon Larkin
    - Grimhelm's Song (poem)
        - By Pat McIntosh
- Book Reviews
    - *Gold the Man* (also known as *The Mind Behind the Eye*) by Joseph Green
        - Reviewed by Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md))
    - *Stag Boy* by William Rayner
        - Reviewed by Susan Adler
    - *The Tombs of Atuan* by [Ursula Le Guin](../U/Ursula_K._Le_Guin.md)
        - Reviewed by Jessica Kemball-Cook
    - The Stuff of Fantasy—From an Unlikely Source: *Curious Myths of the Middle Ages* by Sabine Baring-Gould
        - Reviewed by Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md))
