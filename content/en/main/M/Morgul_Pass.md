The **Morgul Pass** was a pass in a great ravine at the head of the [Morgul Vale](/wiki/Morgul_Vale) that led over the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Cirith_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirith-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Morgul Pass was also referred to as the "main pass".<sup id="cite_ref-Cirith_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirith-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The **Nameless Pass** was probably another name for the Morgul Pass, because the wraith-road ran from the dead city in a great ravine at the head of the [Morgul Valley](/wiki/Morgul_Vale "Morgul Vale") to the Nameless Pass, which [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) could see from above on the winding stairs that led to the tunnel and the pass of [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol) that was guarded by the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol)<sup id="cite_ref-Cirith_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirith-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WR-4">[4]</a></sup> and because a broad road ran from the Tower of Cirith Ungol down to join the road that came over the Morgul Pass.<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-5">[5]</a></sup> As a consequence, the Nameless Pass was probably not another name for the pass of [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The pass of Cirith Ungol was located in a cleft on the slope of the mountain on the north side of the Morgul pass and could be reached by taking a narrow path that began at a gap in the stone-wall on the left side of the [Morgul-road](/wiki/Morgul-road) near the bridge over the river [Morgulduin](/wiki/Morgulduin), which wound up the mountain on the northern side near the entrance of the [Morgul Vale](/wiki/Morgul_Vale), climbing the [Stairs of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol),<sup id="cite_ref-Stairs_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stairs-7">[7]</a></sup> passing through a tunnel,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and taking a long flight of broad shallow steps to the cleft<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and the crown of the pass, after which the road turned left and plunged steeply down to the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> From a gate in the south-eastern side of the wall that enclosed the courtyard of the tower a broad road ran on the edge of a precipice and then turned southward and wound down to join the [Morgul-road](/wiki/Morgul-road).<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-5">[5]</a></sup>

The [Morgul-road](/wiki/Morgul-road) ran from [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath)<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> to the Morgul Vale. It then crossed a bridge over the river [Morgulduin](/wiki/Morgulduin), wound up to the gate of the city of [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul)<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> and then went on through the great ravine at the head of the Morgul Vale over the Morgul Pass.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirith_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirith-2">[2]</a></sup> It then descended to the valley between the Ephel Dúath and the Morgai, passed over a bridge of stone,<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> and through a jagged rift in the Morgai out into [Gorgoroth](/wiki/Gorgoroth) and on to [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Frodo, [Sam](/wiki/Sam) and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) took the stairs of Cirith Ungol that led to the pass of Cirith Ungol, because they wanted to avoid passing by close to [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) and as a consequence did not use the Morgul Pass.<sup id="cite_ref-Stairs_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stairs-7">[7]</a></sup> After the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields) the army of the West left a strong guard at the Cross-roads to defend it in case enemies came over the Morgul Pass from [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) or came up from the South.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

## Drawing {#Drawing}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) made [a drawing](</wiki/Shelob%27s_Lair_(manuscript)> "Shelob's Lair (manuscript)") that shows the top of the stairs of Cirith Ungol, the entrance to the tunnel with the pass of Cirith Ungol after the exit from the tunnel and the topmost turret of the Tower of Cirith Ungol behind a mountain peak with another broader pass on the right side of the pass of Cirith Ungol and a mountain ridge between the pass of Cirith Ungol and the other broader pass.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

An earlier version of what would later become the chapter The Stairs of Cirith Ungol mentions that Frodo saw the glimmer of the wraith-road that led over the *Morghul Pass* from the city when he looked down from the stairs into the Morgul Vale.<sup id="cite_ref-WR_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WR-4">[4]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The Morgul Pass is guarded by a great gate called &#34;Fennas Gost&#34;. After the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a> Fennas Gost was sealed on the order of <a href="/wiki/Gothmog_(Lieutenant_of_Morgul)" title="Gothmog (Lieutenant of Morgul)">Gothmog</a> who barricaded himself inside <a href="/wiki/Minas_Morgul" title="Minas Morgul">Minas Morgul</a>. It was later forced open by Ithilien rangers of the <a href="/wiki/White_Company" title="White Company">White Company</a>.</dd></dl>
