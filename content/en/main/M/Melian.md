**Melian** the [Maia](/wiki/Maia) was the wife of [Elu Thingol](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol"), mother of [Lúthien Tinúviel](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien "Lúthien"), and **Queen of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath)**.

## Attributes {#Attributes}

Melian served [Vána](/wiki/V%C3%A1na) and [Estë](/wiki/Est%C3%AB). She was said to be akin to [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) the Valië. She is associated with songbirds, and it is said she taught [nightingales](/wiki/Nightingales "Nightingales") how to sing and their music followed her paces. In [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), she dwelt in the [Gardens of Lórien](</wiki/L%C3%B3rien_(Valinor)> "Lórien (Valinor)") tending its trees, and she was the most beautiful, wise and skilled in songs of enchantment of all the people of [Irmo](/wiki/Irmo). However, she journeyed often to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) for she loved the deep shadows of trees and forests.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Thingol_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thingol-7">[5]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the dawn of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) Melian dwelt in the Gardens of Lórien, and taught the [nightingales](/wiki/Nightingales "Nightingales") how to sing. When the lights of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor") mingled, at noon, she sang in Lórien, and it is said that everything stopped, even the bells and fountains of [Valimar](/wiki/Valimar), to enjoy her voice.<sup id="cite_ref-Thingol_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thingol-7">[5]</a></sup>

Around the time of the [Awakening of the Elves](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves), Melian left Valinor after a warning in a dream.<sup id="cite_ref-dream_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dream-8">[6]</a></sup> Melian's love for the trees brought her to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), filling its silence with her and her birds' voices.<sup id="cite_ref-Thingol_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thingol-7">[5]</a></sup> After [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) found the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) in [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen), the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) planned to make [War](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers "Battle of the Powers") against [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup> and during their preparations, they sent Melian to Cuiviénen, and then [a group](/wiki/Five_Guardians "Five Guardians") of great Maiar to guard the Elves. Melian was their leader, the only female spirit among them.<sup id="cite_ref-Five_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Five-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) [marched](/wiki/Great_March "Great March") into the [West](/wiki/Aman "Aman"), in the woods of [Nan Elmoth](/wiki/Nan_Elmoth), Melian came upon [Elwë Singollo](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") of the people of the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri). Elwë was entranced and fell into a swoon at the sight of the Maia Melian, and the two of them stood hand in hand, unable to move or speak for years while the trees grew around them. As a result of his absence, a portion of his followers stayed behind to search for him, while the rest continued on to Valinor under the kingship of [Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB), brother of Elwë.<sup id="cite_ref-Thingol_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thingol-7">[5]</a></sup>

Melian and Thingol recovered and gathered the Elves who stayed behind to look for Elwë, who were named [Eglath](/wiki/Eglath). They founded the kingdom of [Eglador](/wiki/Doriath "Doriath") and ruled as King and Queen of all the Elves in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). Melian was the only known [Ainu](/wiki/Ainur "Ainur") who coupled with one of the [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar), and thus she begot a child, the daughter named Lúthien, who was the fairest of the Children to have ever lived.<sup id="cite_ref-Thingol_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thingol-7">[5]</a></sup> She married the Man [Beren Erchamion](/wiki/Beren "Beren"), and as a result, Melian's blood of the Maiar was passed on to both [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Men](/wiki/Men).<sup id="cite_ref-Quest_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quest-10">[8]</a></sup>

Foreseeing that war was coming to Beleriand, Melian warned her husband Thingol. In turn, Thingol ordered the delving of a strong fortress in their kingdom. This was [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth), the Thousand Caves, and they were aided by the Dwarves. With the return of Morgoth and the flight of [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) from his [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs), the dark spider tried to enter into Thingol's domain, but Melian alone repelled Ungoliant and drove her away.

When war with the Great Enemy, [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), [came at last](/wiki/First_Battle "First Battle") to their land, she used her powers to guard and defend it with a protection called *List Melian*, or "the [Girdle of Melian](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian)", and their kingdom was known as [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), the Land of the Fence. This prevented anyone less powerful than Melian from entering the kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[9]</a></sup> However, with the foresight of a Maia, she predicted that one day someone more powerful would be able to enter. When Beren arrived as foretold, she counselled King Thingol against sending Beren to search for a [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril), which would eventually lead to Doriath's ruin. This was one of many instances in which she proved, through her wisdom and powers of foresight, to be wiser than her husband, and an effective queen of her land. The great evil wolf [Carcharoth](/wiki/Carcharoth) also passed the Girdle, as fate and the power of the Silmaril let him.<sup id="cite_ref-Quest_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quest-10">[8]</a></sup>

In Doriath, she also became a friend and tutor of [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) to whom she taught great lore and wisdom concerning Middle-earth and also the art of [lembas](/wiki/Lembas "Lembas")-baking which she knew from her mistress, Yavanna. She eventually questioned Galadriel on how and why the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) had returned, upon which Galadriel told her only some of the story. Therefore she was the first to glean [the truth](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB "Kinslaying at Alqualondë"), perceiving more than Galadriel was willing to tell, and she warned Thingol against dealing with the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup> After the departure of Lúthien and Beren, she aided [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) and his [mother](/wiki/Morwen "Morwen") and [sister](/wiki/Ni%C3%ABnor "Niënor"). She provided [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) with some way-bread, lembas, for him and the exiled Túrin, showing great favour because never before was lembas given to a Man and seldom was it again. But she also foresaw his doom in his quest for Túrin.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[11]</a></sup> When [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin), father of [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin), returned from his captivity in [Angband](/wiki/Angband), she was the one to lift the dark spell of Morgoth from him.

Thingol's arrogance eventually resulted in his death during a [battle in the Thousand Caves](/wiki/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves "Battle in the Thousand Caves"). Melian then departed from the mortal lands,<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[12]</a></sup> vanishing to Valinor, where she mourned the loss of her husband in the [Halls of Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos) and her daughter to the unknown fate of the [Gift of Men](/wiki/Gift_of_Men). In her absence, Doriath was opened to its enemies, which led to its ultimate demise.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[13]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

She appeared in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)* as *Tindriel* or *Wendelin* ([Qenya](/wiki/Qenya)) and in a [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) dictionary as *Gwendeling* or *Gwendhiling*. In a crossed out note, she had with [Tinwelint](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") a son, [Tinfang](/wiki/Tinfang_Warble "Tinfang Warble"), and a daughter, [Tinúviel](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien "Lúthien").<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[18]</a></sup>

In the early [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") Melian is defined as a fay, making her somewhat more sinister than in her later appearance. This version of her is presented in **The Tale of Tinúviel**, Tolkien's first story of Beren and Lúthien, which was written in archaic English and published in *[The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two)*. In this work she appears in another later narrative, although her character is portrayed as being far weaker and more frail than Melian's final manifestation. She also appeared under several names, such as **Gwenniel**.

[Vëannë](/wiki/V%C3%ABann%C3%AB) and [Ausir](</wiki/Ausir_(boy)> "Ausir (boy)") argued whether she should be called *Wendelin* or *Gwendeling*. In a slightly later version, the name *Melian* appeared. In one variant she was a daughter of [Lórien](/wiki/Irmo "Irmo"). She was found by [Tinwelint](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") while listening to the song of nightingales and ran away with laughter when he tried to touch her hair. Then he fell into a deep slumber, and she kept watch over him while he slept. Later she became his wife and the queen of [Artanor](/wiki/Doriath "Doriath").<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[19]</a></sup>

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) noticed that in this early version her protection was weaker and there was the possibility that enemies would follow the returning Beren and Tinúviel.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[20]</a></sup>

<li id="cite_note-3"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-3">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text"><a href="/wiki/Thingol" title="Thingol">Thingol&#39;s</a> death and Melian&#39;s departure are indeed placed under <a href="/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun" title="Years of the Sun">Y.S.</a> <a href="/wiki/First_Age_503" title="First Age 503">503</a> in the final version of the <a href="/wiki/The_Tale_of_Years_(The_War_of_the_Jewels)" title="The Tale of Years (The War of the Jewels)">The Tale of Years</a> from <i><a href="/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels" title="The War of the Jewels">The War of the Jewels</a></i>, but there Thingol was killed in the battle with the Dwarves. However, according to the narrative introduced after much hesitation into the published <i><a href="/wiki/The_Silmarillion" title="The Silmarillion">Silmarillion</a></i> by <a href="/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien" title="Christopher Tolkien">Christopher Tolkien</a>, it occurred immediately after the reforging of the <a href="/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr" title="Nauglamír">Nauglamír</a>.</span>
</li>
<li id="cite_note-4"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-4">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text"><a href="/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun" title="Years of the Sun">Years of the Sun</a>. Each <a href="/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees" title="Years of the Trees">Year of the Trees</a> is equal to 9.582 Years of the Sun, and the Years of the Trees ended in the <a href="/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1500" title="Year of the Trees 1500">year 1500</a>, while Melian dwelt in <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a> since the <a href="/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1050" title="Year of the Trees 1050">year 1050</a>. She definitely existed in <a href="/wiki/Arda" title="Arda">Arda</a> before this date, but it is unknown if she entered <a href="/wiki/E%C3%A4" title="Eä">Eä</a> exactly at the same time as the <a href="/wiki/Valar" title="Valar">Valar</a> and other <a href="/wiki/Maiar" title="Maiar">Maiar</a> did.</span>
</li>
</ol></div></span>
