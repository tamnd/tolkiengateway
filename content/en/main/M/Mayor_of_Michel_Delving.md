The **Mayor of Michel Delving**, sometimes **Mayor of the Shire** ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): *Condir i-Drann*)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the elected leader of [Michel Delving](Michel_Delving.md), the *de facto* capital of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

The Mayor was chosen by the [hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) every seven years at [Lithe](../L/Lithe.md), during the [Free Fair](../F/Free_Fair.md) on the [White Downs](../W/White_Downs.md). Though the responsibilities of Mayoralty itself amounted to little more than presiding at banquets, the office was attached to that of [Postmaster](../P/Postmaster.md) and [First Shirriff](../F/First_Shirriff.md).<sup id="cite_ref-OrderingShire_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OrderingShire-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

By the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the Mayor of Michel Delving was "the only real official in the Shire", so he was commonly known as the **Mayor of the Shire**<sup id="cite_ref-OrderingShire_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OrderingShire-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [13](../F/Fourth_Age_13.md) ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) 1434) the Mayor of Michel Delving, along with the [Thain](../T/Thain.md) and the [Master of Buckland](Master_of_Buckland.md), was made a Counsellor of the newly re-established [North-kingdom](../A/Arnor.md) by [King](../H/High_King_of_the_Dúnedain.md) [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Mayors of Michel Delving {#Mayors_of_Michel_Delving}

The poem [Perry-the-Winkle](../P/Perry-the-Winkle.md) mentions "[Pott the Mayor](../P/Pott_the_Mayor.md)" in Michel Delving, but it is not clear if this character is based on an actual Hobbit who was a Mayor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Will Whitfoot](../W/Will_Whitfoot.md) was the Mayor of Michel Delving during the War of the Ring. An old and fat hobbit, he was largely regarded as a harmless figure of fun,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> but showed his mettle when in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) he was one of the few hobbits that protested [Lotho Sackville-Baggins'](../L/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins.md) takeover of the Shire on behalf of [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md), and in return was imprisoned in the [Lockholes](../L/Lockholes.md) by Lotho's [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-6">[6]</a></sup> He was released following the [Scouring of the Shire](../S/Scouring_of_the_Shire.md), and [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) acted as his Deputy until he had recovered.<sup id="cite_ref-GreyHavens_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GreyHavens-7">[7]</a></sup>

After Whitfoot, [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) served an unprecedented seven consecutive terms as Mayor, starting in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [6](../F/Fourth_Age_6.md) and ending in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [55](../F/Fourth_Age_55.md) ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) 1427–1476). [Tom Cotton](../T/Tolman_Cotton_Junior.md) briefly acted as his Deputy.<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-3">[3]</a></sup>
