The **Mayor of Michel Delving**, sometimes **Mayor of the Shire** ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *Condir i-Drann*)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the elected leader of [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving), the *de facto* capital of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

The Mayor was chosen by the [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") every seven years at [Lithe](/wiki/Lithe), during the [Free Fair](/wiki/Free_Fair) on the [White Downs](/wiki/White_Downs). Though the responsibilities of Mayoralty itself amounted to little more than presiding at banquets, the office was attached to that of [Postmaster](/wiki/Postmaster) and [First Shirriff](/wiki/First_Shirriff).<sup id="cite_ref-OrderingShire_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OrderingShire-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

By the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the Mayor of Michel Delving was "the only real official in the Shire", so he was commonly known as the **Mayor of the Shire**<sup id="cite_ref-OrderingShire_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OrderingShire-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [13](/wiki/Fourth_Age_13 "Fourth Age 13") ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") 1434) the Mayor of Michel Delving, along with the [Thain](/wiki/Thain) and the [Master of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland), was made a Counsellor of the newly re-established [North-kingdom](/wiki/Arnor "Arnor") by [King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "High King of the Dúnedain") [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn").<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Mayors of Michel Delving {#Mayors_of_Michel_Delving}

The poem [Perry-the-Winkle](/wiki/Perry-the-Winkle) mentions "[Pott the Mayor](/wiki/Pott_the_Mayor)" in Michel Delving, but it is not clear if this character is based on an actual Hobbit who was a Mayor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Will Whitfoot](/wiki/Will_Whitfoot) was the Mayor of Michel Delving during the War of the Ring. An old and fat hobbit, he was largely regarded as a harmless figure of fun,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> but showed his mettle when in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") he was one of the few hobbits that protested [Lotho Sackville-Baggins'](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins "Lotho Sackville-Baggins") takeover of the Shire on behalf of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman), and in return was imprisoned in the [Lockholes](/wiki/Lockholes) by Lotho's [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-6">[6]</a></sup> He was released following the [Scouring of the Shire](/wiki/Scouring_of_the_Shire), and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) acted as his Deputy until he had recovered.<sup id="cite_ref-GreyHavens_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GreyHavens-7">[7]</a></sup>

After Whitfoot, [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) served an unprecedented seven consecutive terms as Mayor, starting in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [6](/wiki/Fourth_Age_6 "Fourth Age 6") and ending in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [55](/wiki/Fourth_Age_55 "Fourth Age 55") ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") 1427–1476). [Tom Cotton](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Junior "Tolman Cotton Junior") briefly acted as his Deputy.<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-3">[3]</a></sup>
