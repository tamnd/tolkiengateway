**Morgul Vale** (<i><b>Imlad Morgul</b></i> in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>), was the valley of the [Morgulduin](/wiki/Morgulduin) in the western [Mountains of Shadow](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath "Ephel Dúath"). At its outflow into [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) stood the fortress of [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) ("Tower of Dark Sorcery"). The name was only used in the later [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), after the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) [captured](/wiki/Second_Fall_of_Minas_Ithil "Second Fall of Minas Ithil") the fortress of [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil) and turned it into the Tower of Dark Sorcery. The only vegetation in the valley were the [Poisoned Meads](/wiki/Poisoned_Meads)<sup id="cite_ref-chronology_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology-2">[2]</a></sup> along the river.

[Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) entered the western end of the valley in order to reach the [stairway](/wiki/Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol "Stairs of Cirith Ungol") to [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol). As the three passed the white bridge Frodo was dazed by the noxious fumes and began running towards the city, but was stopped by Sam and Gollum. As they climbed the stairs they left the reek behind them on the floor of the valley.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Later, when the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Host_of_the_West" title="Host of the West">Host of the West</a> came to and passed the [Cross-roads](/wiki/Cross-roads), a vanguard led by [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) rode to Morgul Vale and there destroyed the bridge and set fire to the Poisoned Meads<sup id="cite_ref-chronology_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology-2">[2]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> After the war was over, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) as King Elessar ordered that Minas Morgul be utterly destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
