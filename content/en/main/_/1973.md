- **[25](25_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Patrick Hunt, who is in prison](../L/Letter_to_Patrick_Hunt.md).

- **[26](26_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Stefan Pettersson](../L/Letter_to_Stefan_Pettersson.md).

## March {#March}

- **[3](3_March.md)** - A [tobacco bill](<../C/Cigar_bill_(3_March_1972).md>) is submitted to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), on which he scribbles a note.
- **[6](6_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 348](../L/Letter_348.md).
- **[8](8_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 348a](../L/Letter_348a.md).
- **[8](8_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 349](../L/Letter_349.md).
- **[24](24_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 349a](../L/Letter_349a.md).

## April {#April}

- **[9](9_April.md)** - [W.H. Lewis](../W/Warren_%2522Warnie%2522_Lewis.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), dies.
- **[20](20_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Dr. Denis Tolhurst](../L/Letter_to_Dr._Denis_Tolhurst.md).
- **[20](20_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to John Higgins](../L/Letter_to_John_Higgins.md).

## May {#May}

- **[16](16_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) takes a holiday to [Bournemouth](../B/Bournemouth.md).
- **[18](18_May.md)** - [R.B. McCallum](../R/R.B._McCallum.md) dies.
- **[24](24_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is [interviewed](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) concerning the election to the Oxford Poetry chair.
- **[24](24_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 350](../L/Letter_350.md).
- **[29](29_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 351](../L/Letter_351.md).
- **[30](30_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Philip Brown](../L/Letter_to_Philip_Brown.md).

## June {#June}

- **[5](5_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 352](../L/Letter_352.md).
- **[5](5_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to James A.H. Murray](../L/Letter_to_James_A.H._Murray.md).
- **[14](14_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Elena Jeronimides](../L/Letter_to_Elena_Jeronimides.md).
- **[14](14_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Bruce Charlton](../L/Letter_to_Bruce_Charlton.md).

## July {#July}

- **[8](8_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Norman Power](../L/Letter_to_Norman_Power.md).
- **[12](12_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is awarded an honorary Doctorate of Letters by the <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">University of Edinburgh</a>.
- **[12](12_July.md)** - Actor [Jørn Benzon](../J/Jørn_Benzon.md) is born.
- **[28](28_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter to Campbell](../L/Letter_to_Campbell.md).

## August {#August}

- **[4](4_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 353](../L/Letter_353.md).
- **[9](9_August.md)** - The [last photograph](../../file/J/Jrrt_1972_tree.jpg.md) of J.R.R. Tolkien is taken by Michael Tolkien, in front of his favourite tree, the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Black_Pine" title="wikipedia:European Black Pine"><i>Pinus Nigra</i></a>, at the botanic garden in [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md).
- **[17](17_August.md)-[20](20_August.md)** - [Mythcon 4](../M/Mythcon_4.md).
- **[24](24_August.md)** - Actress [Grey DeLisle](../G/Grey_DeLisle.md) is born.
- **[25](25_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Glyn Daniel](../L/Letter_to_Glyn_Daniel.md).
- **[28](28_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) takes a holiday to visit friends in [Bournemouth](../B/Bournemouth.md).
- **[29](29_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 354](../L/Letter_354.md), the last letter to be included in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*.
- **[30](30_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) goes to a birthday party for Denis Tolhurst's wife.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) [lunches with Robert Murray](../A/A_Tribute_to_Tolkien.md) (and possibly others).

## September {#September}

- **[2](2_September.md) - After visiting friends in [Bournemouth](../B/Bournemouth.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) becomes ill and dies, at the age of eighty-one. He is buried next to his wife, [Edith](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md).**
- **[6](6_September.md)** - A requiem Mass for [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is held in the Church of St Anthony of Padua in Headington, [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md). Father [Robert Murray](../R/Robert_Murray.md) assists.
- **[29](29_September.md)** - [W.H. Auden](../W/W.H._Auden.md), long time friend of Tolkien, dies.
- **?** - [*Mythprint* (Vol.8, No.3)](<../M/Mythprint_(Vol.8,_No.3).md>), including a [letter from J.R.R. Tolkien to Joan O. Falconer](../L/Letter_to_Joan_O._Falconer.md), is published.
