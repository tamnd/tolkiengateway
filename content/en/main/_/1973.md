[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1973" title="wikipedia:1973">1973</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[14](/wiki/14_January "14 January")** - Reader N.J. Kyle writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Letter_to_N.J._Kyle "Letter to N.J. Kyle").
- **[17](/wiki/17_January "17 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to N.J. Kyle](/wiki/Letter_to_N.J._Kyle "Letter to N.J. Kyle").
- **[24](/wiki/24_January "24 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 347a](/wiki/Letter_347a "Letter 347a").
- **[25](/wiki/25_January "25 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Patrick Hunt, who is in prison](/wiki/Letter_to_Patrick_Hunt "Letter to Patrick Hunt").

## February {#February}

- **[26](/wiki/26_February "26 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Stefan Pettersson](/wiki/Letter_to_Stefan_Pettersson "Letter to Stefan Pettersson").

## March {#March}

- **[3](/wiki/3_March "3 March")** - A [tobacco bill](</wiki/Cigar_bill_(3_March_1972)> "Cigar bill (3 March 1972)") is submitted to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), on which he scribbles a note.
- **[6](/wiki/6_March "6 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 348](/wiki/Letter_348 "Letter 348").
- **[8](/wiki/8_March "8 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 348a](/wiki/Letter_348a "Letter 348a").
- **[8](/wiki/8_March "8 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 349](/wiki/Letter_349).
- **[24](/wiki/24_March "24 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 349a](/wiki/Letter_349a "Letter 349a").

## April {#April}

- **[9](/wiki/9_April "9 April")** - [W.H. Lewis](/wiki/Warren_%22Warnie%22_Lewis "Warren \"Warnie\" Lewis"), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), dies.
- **[20](/wiki/20_April "20 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Dr. Denis Tolhurst](/wiki/Letter_to_Dr._Denis_Tolhurst "Letter to Dr. Denis Tolhurst").
- **[20](/wiki/20_April "20 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to John Higgins](/wiki/Letter_to_John_Higgins "Letter to John Higgins").

## May {#May}

- **[16](/wiki/16_May "16 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) takes a holiday to [Bournemouth](/wiki/Bournemouth).
- **[18](/wiki/18_May "18 May")** - [R.B. McCallum](/wiki/R.B._McCallum) dies.
- **[24](/wiki/24_May "24 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is [interviewed](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") concerning the election to the Oxford Poetry chair.
- **[24](/wiki/24_May "24 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 350](/wiki/Letter_350 "Letter 350").
- **[29](/wiki/29_May "29 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 351](/wiki/Letter_351 "Letter 351").
- **[30](/wiki/30_May "30 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Philip Brown](/wiki/Letter_to_Philip_Brown "Letter to Philip Brown").

## June {#June}

- **[5](/wiki/5_June "5 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 352](/wiki/Letter_352 "Letter 352").
- **[5](/wiki/5_June "5 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to James A.H. Murray](/wiki/Letter_to_James_A.H._Murray "Letter to James A.H. Murray").
- **[14](/wiki/14_June "14 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Elena Jeronimides](/wiki/Letter_to_Elena_Jeronimides "Letter to Elena Jeronimides").
- **[14](/wiki/14_June "14 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Bruce Charlton](/wiki/Letter_to_Bruce_Charlton "Letter to Bruce Charlton").

## July {#July}

- **[8](/wiki/8_July "8 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Norman Power](/wiki/Letter_to_Norman_Power "Letter to Norman Power").
- **[12](/wiki/12_July "12 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is awarded an honorary Doctorate of Letters by the <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">University of Edinburgh</a>.
- **[12](/wiki/12_July "12 July")** - Actor [Jørn Benzon](/wiki/J%C3%B8rn_Benzon) is born.
- **[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter to Campbell](/wiki/Letter_to_Campbell).

## August {#August}

- **[4](/wiki/4_August "4 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 353](/wiki/Letter_353 "Letter 353").
- **[9](/wiki/9_August "9 August")** - The [last photograph](/wiki/File:Jrrt_1972_tree.jpg "File:Jrrt 1972 tree.jpg") of J.R.R. Tolkien is taken by Michael Tolkien, in front of his favourite tree, the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Black_Pine" title="wikipedia:European Black Pine"><i>Pinus Nigra</i></a>, at the botanic garden in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford).
- **[17](/wiki/17_August "17 August")-[20](/wiki/20_August "20 August")** - [Mythcon 4](/wiki/Mythcon_4).
- **[24](/wiki/24_August "24 August")** - Actress [Grey DeLisle](/wiki/Grey_DeLisle) is born.
- **[25](/wiki/25_August "25 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Glyn Daniel](/wiki/Letter_to_Glyn_Daniel "Letter to Glyn Daniel").
- **[28](/wiki/28_August "28 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) takes a holiday to visit friends in [Bournemouth](/wiki/Bournemouth).
- **[29](/wiki/29_August "29 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 354](/wiki/Letter_354 "Letter 354"), the last letter to be included in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)*.
- **[30](/wiki/30_August "30 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) goes to a birthday party for Denis Tolhurst's wife.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) [lunches with Robert Murray](/wiki/A_Tribute_to_Tolkien "A Tribute to Tolkien") (and possibly others).

## September {#September}

- **[2](/wiki/2_September "2 September") - After visiting friends in [Bournemouth](/wiki/Bournemouth), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) becomes ill and dies, at the age of eighty-one. He is buried next to his wife, [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien").**
- **[6](/wiki/6_September "6 September")** - A requiem Mass for [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is held in the Church of St Anthony of Padua in Headington, [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford). Father [Robert Murray](/wiki/Robert_Murray) assists.
- **[29](/wiki/29_September "29 September")** - [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden), long time friend of Tolkien, dies.
- **?** - [*Mythprint* (Vol.8, No.3)](</wiki/Mythprint_(Vol.8,_No.3)> "Mythprint (Vol.8, No.3)"), including a [letter from J.R.R. Tolkien to Joan O. Falconer](/wiki/Letter_to_Joan_O._Falconer "Letter to Joan O. Falconer"), is published.

## November {#November}

- **8** - Actor [Duncan McIntyre](/wiki/Duncan_McIntyre) dies.

## December {#December}

- **?** - Games designer [Nathan Segerlind](/wiki/Nathan_Segerlind) is born.
