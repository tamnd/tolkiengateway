The **[1998](/wiki/1998) Khandlendar compiled by Salman Rushtoes** is a hand-made calendar made and published by Alex Lewis. Featured illustrators include artists with (somewhat unusual) pseudonyms Mustaphaa Goa Ttitt, Renee Neeneerenee, Serena Di'Consuello, Clarence Bywater-Hicks VI and Clara Brandybuck. The word "Khandlendar" is a pun on [Khand](/wiki/Khand), a region south of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).

- Dimensions: 11 5/8 x 16 3/4
- White comb-bound on the top
- String hanger
