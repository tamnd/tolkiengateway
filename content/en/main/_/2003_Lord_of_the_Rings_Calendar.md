**2003 Lord of the Rings Calendar** was produced by the [Italian Tolkien Society](/wiki/Italian_Tolkien_Society) with illustrations by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mark_Addamiano&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mark Addamiano (page does not exist)">Mark Addamiano</a>. The calendar was printed in linoleumgrafica in black and white, limited to only 150 copies. 8 pages total including the front and back covers. Bound by a cord.

- Dimensions: 8 1/4 x 11 3/4 inches
