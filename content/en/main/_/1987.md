[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987" title="wikipedia:1987">1987</a></b>.

## April {#April}

- **[7](/wiki/7_April "7 April")** - Actor [Ismael Cruz Córdova](/wiki/Ismael_Cruz_C%C3%B3rdova) is born.
- **[23](/wiki/23_April "23 April")** - *[Pictures under Discussion](/wiki/Pictures_under_Discussion)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[24](/wiki/24_July "24 July")-[27](/wiki/27_July "27 July")** - [Mythcon 18](/wiki/Mythcon_18).
- **[25](/wiki/25_July "25 July") [Christopher Wiseman](/wiki/Christopher_Wiseman) dies.**

## August {#August}

- <b><a href="/wiki/27_August" title="27 August">27</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings" title="The Lost Road and Other Writings">The Lost Road and Other Writings</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **[28](/wiki/28_August "28 August")** - Actor [John Huston](/wiki/John_Huston) dies.

## September {#September}

- **[25](/wiki/25_September "25 September")-[27](/wiki/27_September "27 September")** - [Oxonmoot 1987](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1987).
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 24](/wiki/Mallorn_24 "Mallorn 24") is published.

## October {#October}

- **[8](/wiki/8_October "8 October")** - [Roger Lancelyn Green](/wiki/Roger_Lancelyn_Green), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") and biographer, dies.
- **[15](/wiki/15_October "15 October")-[16](/wiki/16_October "16 October")** - The <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Storm_of_1987" title="wikipedia:Great Storm of 1987">Great Storm</a>, which had been "[predicted](/wiki/The_Notion_Club_Papers#Trivia "The Notion Club Papers")" by Tolkien in a curious coincidence; in *[The Notion Club Papers](/wiki/The_Notion_Club_Papers)* (written in [1945](/wiki/1945)) Tolkien mentions a Great Storm on Thursday, June 12 of 1987, which however actually occurs during October.

## November {#November}

- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings)* is published in the US.

## December {#December}

- **[24](/wiki/24_December "24 December")** - [Terence Tiller](/wiki/Terence_Tiller), writer of the [1955 radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)")*, dies.*
