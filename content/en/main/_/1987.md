[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987" title="wikipedia:1987">1987</a></b>.

## April {#April}

- **[7](7_April.md)** - Actor [Ismael Cruz Córdova](../I/Ismael_Cruz_Córdova.md) is born.
- **[23](23_April.md)** - *[Pictures under Discussion](../P/Pictures_under_Discussion.md)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[24](24_July.md)-[27](27_July.md)** - [Mythcon 18](../M/Mythcon_18.md).
- **[25](25_July.md) [Christopher Wiseman](../C/Christopher_Wiseman.md) dies.**

## August {#August}

- <b><a href="27_August.md" title="27 August">27</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings.md" title="The Lost Road and Other Writings">The Lost Road and Other Writings</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **[28](28_August.md)** - Actor [John Huston](../J/John_Huston.md) dies.

## September {#September}

- **[25](25_September.md)-[27](27_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1987](../O/Oxonmoot_1987.md).
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 24](../M/Mallorn_24.md) is published.

## October {#October}

- **[8](8_October.md)** - [Roger Lancelyn Green](../R/Roger_Lancelyn_Green.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md) and biographer, dies.
- **[15](15_October.md)-[16](16_October.md)** - The <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Storm_of_1987" title="wikipedia:Great Storm of 1987">Great Storm</a>, which had been "[predicted](../T/The_Notion_Club_Papers.md#Trivia)" by Tolkien in a curious coincidence; in *[The Notion Club Papers](../T/The_Notion_Club_Papers.md)* (written in [1945](1945.md)) Tolkien mentions a Great Storm on Thursday, June 12 of 1987, which however actually occurs during October.

## November {#November}

- **[30](30_November.md)** - *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](../T/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings.md)* is published in the US.

## December {#December}

- **[24](24_December.md)** - [Terence Tiller](../T/Terence_Tiller.md), writer of the [1955 radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>)*, dies.*
