Events that occurred on 27 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1920](/wiki/1920)** - Voice actor [William Conrad](/wiki/William_Conrad) is born.
- **[1936](/wiki/1936)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Professor A. Pompen](/wiki/Letter_to_Aurelius_Pompen "Letter to Aurelius Pompen").
- **[1980](/wiki/1980)** - [Oxonmoot 1980](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1980) at The Turf Tavern.
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - [Oxonmoot 1981](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1981) at The Turf Tavern.
- **[1985](/wiki/1985)** - [Oxonmoot 1985](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1985) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[1987](/wiki/1987)** - [Oxonmoot 1987](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1987) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[1996](/wiki/1996)** - *[Realms of Tolkien](/wiki/Realms_of_Tolkien)* is published.
- **[1998](/wiki/1998)** - [Oxonmoot 1998](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1998) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Exeter College, Oxford">Exeter College</a>.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - *[Parma Eldalamberon](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon)* issue [15](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_15 "Parma Eldalamberon 15") is published.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - [Oxonmoot 2008](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2008) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_Church,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Christ Church, Oxford">Christ Church</a>.
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** - [Oxonmoot 2009](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2009) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Margaret_Hall,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Lady Margaret Hall, Oxford">Lady Margaret Hall</a>.
- **[2011](/wiki/2011)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Rise of Isengard](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Rise_of_Isengard)* expansion is released.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and his companions spend the second night with [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) because of the rain. While discussing with him about [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), Frodo wears it for first time.
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) crosses [Greyflood](/wiki/Greyflood).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
