<i><b>ñguruk</b></i> (or *(ñ)guruk*) is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) word of unknown exact meaning. It was an elaborate word signifying an element of great terror to the [Elves](../E/Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) *(g)ruk* (from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [RUKU](../R/RUKU.md)) + [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [NGUR](../N/NGUR.md)<sup id="cite_ref-AC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AC-1">[1]</a></sup>
