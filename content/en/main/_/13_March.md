Events that occurred on 13 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1956](/wiki/1956)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) receives a letter from a Sam Gamgee – from Tooting (see [Letter 184](/wiki/Letter_184)).
- **[1969](/wiki/1969)** - [Adam Tolkien](/wiki/Adam_Tolkien) is born.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"):
    - [Borondir](/wiki/Borondir) and his companion are on their way in search of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") is stung by [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob) and subsequently captured by the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol).
    - The [Causeway Forts](/wiki/Causeway_Forts) fall and the [Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor) is breached.
    - [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) is wounded while leading the rearguard. He is saved by a sally of the knights of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth) and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf).
    - The [Pelennor](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields "Pelennor Fields") is overrun by the enemies of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).
    - [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) is brought back to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) badly wounded.
    - [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) arrives at [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) and captures [the fleet](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar "Corsairs of Umbar").
    - The [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) travel towards [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). They cross the [Drúadan Forest](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest).
    - [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond) sees a strange light in the tower of Minas Tirith, being [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) confronting [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3020](/wiki/Third_Age_3020 "Third Age 3020"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") gets ill on the anniversary of his poisoning by [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob).<sup id="cite_ref-chief_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") is again ill.<sup id="cite_ref-chief_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-1">[1]</a></sup>
