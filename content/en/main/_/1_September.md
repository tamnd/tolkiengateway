Events that occurred on 1 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1917](1917.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is working on [The Grey Bridge of Tavrobel](../T/The_Grey_Bridge_of_Tavrobel.md) while in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hull#History" title="wikipedia:University of Hull">Brooklands Officers&#39; Hospital</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston_upon_Hull" title="wikipedia:Kingston upon Hull">Kingston upon Hull</a>.
- **[1938](1938.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) goes on holiday to recuperate (see [Letter 33](../L/Letter_33.md)).
- **[1943](1943.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 49b](../L/Letter_49b.md).
- **[1964](1964.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) met [Clyde S. Kilby](../C/Clyde_S._Kilby.md).
- **[1971](1971.md)** - Actor [Dave Wittenberg](../D/Dave_Wittenberg.md) is born.
- **[1977](1977.md)** - *[Cliffs Notes on Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit](../C/Cliffs_Notes_on_Tolkien's_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_and_The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- <b><a href="1989.md" title="1989">1989</a> - <i><a href="../O/Oliphaunt_(book).md" title="Oliphaunt (book)">Oliphaunt</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[2009](2009.md)** - *[The Lord of the Films](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Films.md)* is published.
- **[2011](2011.md)** - *[A Tolkien Tapestry](../A/A_Tolkien_Tapestry.md)* is published.
- **[2011](2011.md)** - *[A Critical Companion to J.R.R. Tolkien: A Literary Reference to His Life and Works](../A/A_Critical_Companion_to_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* is published.
- **[2011](2011.md)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien](<../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_(Holiday_House).md>)*, a children's book from Holiday House, was published.
- **[2016](2016.md)** - *[Songs of Sorrow and Hope: The Art of Jenny Dolfen](../S/Songs_of_Sorrow_and_Hope.md)* is published.
- **[2016](2016.md)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2017](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2017.md)* is published.
- **[2020](2020.md)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2021](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2021.md)* is published.
- **[2022](2022.md)** - The [first](../A/A_Shadow_of_the_Past.md) [two](../A/Adrift.md) episodes of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* are released online.
- **[2022](2022.md)** - A new and revised edition of [The Complete Guide to Middle-earth](../T/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth.md) by [Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md) is released.
