Events that occurred on 1 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1917](/wiki/1917)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is working on [The Grey Bridge of Tavrobel](/wiki/The_Grey_Bridge_of_Tavrobel) while in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hull#History" title="wikipedia:University of Hull">Brooklands Officers&#39; Hospital</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston_upon_Hull" title="wikipedia:Kingston upon Hull">Kingston upon Hull</a>.
- **[1938](/wiki/1938)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) goes on holiday to recuperate (see [Letter 33](/wiki/Letter_33)).
- **[1943](/wiki/1943)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 49b](/wiki/Letter_49b).
- **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) met [Clyde S. Kilby](/wiki/Clyde_S._Kilby).
- **[1971](/wiki/1971)** - Actor [Dave Wittenberg](/wiki/Dave_Wittenberg) is born.
- **[1977](/wiki/1977)** - *[Cliffs Notes on Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit](/wiki/Cliffs_Notes_on_Tolkien%27s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_and_The_Hobbit)* is published.
- <b><a href="/wiki/1989" title="1989">1989</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/Oliphaunt_(book)" title="Oliphaunt (book)">Oliphaunt</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** - *[The Lord of the Films](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Films)* is published.
- **[2011](/wiki/2011)** - *[A Tolkien Tapestry](/wiki/A_Tolkien_Tapestry)* is published.
- **[2011](/wiki/2011)** - *[A Critical Companion to J.R.R. Tolkien: A Literary Reference to His Life and Works](/wiki/A_Critical_Companion_to_J.R.R._Tolkien "A Critical Companion to J.R.R. Tolkien")* is published.
- **[2011](/wiki/2011)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_(Holiday_House)> "J.R.R. Tolkien (Holiday House)")*, a children's book from Holiday House, was published.
- **[2016](/wiki/2016)** - *[Songs of Sorrow and Hope: The Art of Jenny Dolfen](/wiki/Songs_of_Sorrow_and_Hope "Songs of Sorrow and Hope")* is published.
- **[2016](/wiki/2016)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2017](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2017)* is published.
- **[2020](/wiki/2020)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2021](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2021)* is published.
- **[2022](/wiki/2022)** - The [first](/wiki/A_Shadow_of_the_Past "A Shadow of the Past") [two](/wiki/Adrift "Adrift") episodes of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* are released online.
- **[2022](/wiki/2022)** - A new and revised edition of [The Complete Guide to Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth) by [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) is released.
