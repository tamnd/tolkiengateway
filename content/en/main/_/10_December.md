Events that occurred on 10 December.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1824](1824.md)** - Author [George MacDonald](../G/George_MacDonald.md) is born.
- **[1941](1941.md)** - [Baillie Tolkien](../B/Baillie_Tolkien.md) is born.
- **[1944](1944.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 91c](../L/Letter_91c.md).
- **[1947](1947.md)** - Author and satirist [Douglas C. Kenney](../D/Douglas_C._Kenney.md) is born.
- **[1953](1953.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 142a](../L/Letter_142a.md).
- **[1960](1960.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 225](../L/Letter_225.md).
- **[1962](1962.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is [interviewed by John Bowen](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- <b><a href="2001.md" title="2001">2001</a> – <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i> premieres at Odeon Leicester Square, in London.</b>
- **[2013](2013.md)** - *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack.md)* is released.
- **[2025](2025.md)** - Voice actor [Jim Ward](../J/Jim_Ward.md) dies.
