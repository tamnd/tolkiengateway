- **[1824](/wiki/1824)** - Author [George MacDonald](/wiki/George_MacDonald) is born.
- **[1941](/wiki/1941)** - [Baillie Tolkien](/wiki/Baillie_Tolkien) is born.
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 91c](/wiki/Letter_91c "Letter 91c").

- **[1953](/wiki/1953)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 142a](/wiki/Letter_142a).
- **[1960](/wiki/1960)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 225](/wiki/Letter_225).

- <b><a href="/wiki/2001" title="2001">2001</a> – <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i> premieres at Odeon Leicester Square, in London.</b>
