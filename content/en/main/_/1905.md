- **?** - After the death of his mother, the guardianship of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and his brother [Hilary](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien "Hilary Tolkien") is taken over by Father [Francis Xavier Morgan](/wiki/Francis_Xavier_Morgan), a priest of the [Birmingham Oratory](/wiki/Birmingham_Oratory).
