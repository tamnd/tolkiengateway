[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1932" title="wikipedia:1932">1932</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[21](21_February.md)** - Scholar [John Ellison](../J/John_Ellison.md) is born.

## April {#April}

- **[8](8_April.md)** - [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) recommends his brother [Warren](../W/Warren_Lewis.md) in a letter to read [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s translation of *[The Owl and the Nightingale](../T/The_Owl_and_the_Nightingale.md)*.
- **[27](27_April.md)** - Voice actor [Casey Kasem](../C/Casey_Kasem.md) is born.

## May {#May}

- **?** - [*The British Esperantist* (Vol.28)](../T/The_British_Esperantist,_Vol.28,_May_1932.md), including [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s "[A Philologist on Esperanto](../A/A_Philologist_on_Esperanto.md)", is published.
- **[24](24_May.md)** - [R.W. Chambers](../R/R.W._Chambers.md) sends [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) an inscribed copy of *[Beowulf: An Introduction to the Study of the Poem](../B/Beowulf%253A_An_Introduction_to_the_Study_of_the_Poem.md)*.
- **[27](27_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to A.F. Colborn](<../L/Letter_to_A.F._Colborn_(27_May_1932).md>).

## June {#June}

- **[12](12_June.md)** - Actor [Michael Elsworth](../M/Michael_Elsworth.md) is born.
- **[22](22_June.md)** - Voice actress [Prunella Scales](../P/Prunella_Scales.md) is born.

## July {#July}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s essay "[The Name 'Nodens'](../T/The_Name_'Nodens'.md)" is published as an appendix to the *[Report on the Excavation of the Prehistoric, Roman, and Post-Roman Site in Lydney Park, Gloucestershire](../R/Report_on_the_Excavation_of_the_Prehistoric,_Roman,_and_Post-Roman_Site_in_Lydney_Park,_Gloucestershire.md)*.

## August {#August}

- **[9](9_August.md)** - Voice actor [Denys Hawthorne](../D/Denys_Hawthorne.md) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[19](19_October.md)** - Artist [Fritz Möser](../F/Fritz_Möser.md) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[30](30_November.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## December {#December}

- **[9](9_December.md)** - Author [Jan Broberg](../J/Jan_Broberg.md), who were to conduct [an interview](1961_Jan_Broberg.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is born.
- **[23](23_December.md)** - Father Christmas writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **?** - Tolkien's essay *[Sigelwara Land (Part 1)](../S/Sigelwara_Land.md)* is published.
