[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1932" title="wikipedia:1932">1932</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[21](/wiki/21_February "21 February")** - Scholar [John Ellison](/wiki/John_Ellison) is born.

## April {#April}

- **[8](/wiki/8_April "8 April")** - [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) recommends his brother [Warren](/wiki/Warren_Lewis "Warren Lewis") in a letter to read [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s translation of *[The Owl and the Nightingale](/wiki/The_Owl_and_the_Nightingale)*.
- **[27](/wiki/27_April "27 April")** - Voice actor [Casey Kasem](/wiki/Casey_Kasem) is born.

## May {#May}

- **?** - [*The British Esperantist* (Vol.28)](/wiki/The_British_Esperantist,_Vol.28,_May_1932 "The British Esperantist, Vol.28, May 1932"), including [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s "[A Philologist on Esperanto](/wiki/A_Philologist_on_Esperanto)", is published.
- **[24](/wiki/24_May "24 May")** - [R.W. Chambers](/wiki/R.W._Chambers) sends [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) an inscribed copy of *[Beowulf: An Introduction to the Study of the Poem](/wiki/Beowulf:_An_Introduction_to_the_Study_of_the_Poem)*.
- **[27](/wiki/27_May "27 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to A.F. Colborn](</wiki/Letter_to_A.F._Colborn_(27_May_1932)> "Letter to A.F. Colborn (27 May 1932)").

## June {#June}

- **[12](/wiki/12_June "12 June")** - Actor [Michael Elsworth](/wiki/Michael_Elsworth) is born.
- **[22](/wiki/22_June "22 June")** - Voice actress [Prunella Scales](/wiki/Prunella_Scales) is born.

## July {#July}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s essay "[The Name 'Nodens'](/wiki/The_Name_%27Nodens%27)" is published as an appendix to the *[Report on the Excavation of the Prehistoric, Roman, and Post-Roman Site in Lydney Park, Gloucestershire](/wiki/Report_on_the_Excavation_of_the_Prehistoric,_Roman,_and_Post-Roman_Site_in_Lydney_Park,_Gloucestershire)*.

## August {#August}

- **[9](/wiki/9_August "9 August")** - Voice actor [Denys Hawthorne](/wiki/Denys_Hawthorne) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[19](/wiki/19_October "19 October")** - Artist [Fritz Möser](/wiki/Fritz_M%C3%B6ser) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## December {#December}

- **[9](/wiki/9_December "9 December")** - Author [Jan Broberg](/wiki/Jan_Broberg), who were to conduct [an interview](/wiki/1961_Jan_Broberg "1961 Jan Broberg") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is born.
- **[23](/wiki/23_December "23 December")** - Father Christmas writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **?** - Tolkien's essay *[Sigelwara Land (Part 1)](/wiki/Sigelwara_Land "Sigelwara Land")* is published.
