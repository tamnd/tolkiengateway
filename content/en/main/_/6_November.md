Events that occurred on 6 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1877](/wiki/1877)** - Actor [Robert Farquharson](/wiki/Robert_Farquharson) is born.
- **[1924](/wiki/1924)** - Translator [William Auld](/wiki/William_Auld) is born.
- **[1954](/wiki/1954)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to R.W. Burchfield](</wiki/Letter_to_Robert_Burchfield_(6_November_1954)> "Letter to Robert Burchfield (6 November 1954)").
- **[1956](/wiki/1956)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 194](/wiki/Letter_194 "Letter 194").
- **[1956](/wiki/1956)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 194a](/wiki/Letter_194a).
- **[1957](/wiki/1957)** - Actor [Cam Clarke](/wiki/Cam_Clarke) is born.
- **[1969](/wiki/1969)** - Informal date of foundation of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society).
- **[1980](/wiki/1980)** - [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") [Nevill Coghill](/wiki/Nevill_Coghill) dies.
- **[1992](/wiki/1992)** - Actor [Robert Aramayo](/wiki/Robert_Aramayo) is born.
- **[2000](/wiki/2000)** - Author [L. Sprague de Camp](/wiki/L._Sprague_de_Camp) dies.
- **[2001](/wiki/2001)** - "[The Fellowship of the Ring](</wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(Decipher)> "The Fellowship of the Ring (Decipher)")", the first set in *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game)*, is released.
- **[2002](/wiki/2002)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Making of the Movie Trilogy](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Making_of_the_Movie_Trilogy)* is published.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - [Hyarion](/wiki/User:Hyarion "User:Hyarion") purchases the domain [TolkienGateway.net](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway "Tolkien Gateway").
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - *[Peter Jackson: A Film-maker's Journey](/wiki/Peter_Jackson:_A_Film-maker%27s_Journey)* is published.
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - *[Essential Tolkien](/wiki/Essential_Tolkien)* audiobook is released.
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** - The <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook" title="wikipedia:Facebook">Facebook</a> application *[The One Ring](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(Facebook_application)> "The One Ring (Facebook application)")* is launched.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") rest in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").
