Events that occurred on 1 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1939](1939.md)** - Actor [David Weatherley](../D/David_Weatherley.md) is born.
- **[1939](1939.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 36a](../L/Letter_36a.md).
- **[1944](1944.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 56](../L/Letter_56.md).
- **[1948](1948.md)** - Actor [Steven M. Kramer](../S/Steven_M._Kramer.md) is born.
- **[1957](1957.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 195b](../L/Letter_195b.md).
- **[1960](1960.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 222d](../L/Letter_222d.md).
- **[1967](1967.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Hans-Jörg Modlmayr](<../L/Letter_to_Hans-Jörg_Modlmayr_(1_March_1967).md>).
- **[1968](1968.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Miss E. Byrne](<../L/Letter_to_Miss_E._Byrne_(1_March_1968).md>).
- **[2001](2001.md)** - *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](../T/Tyalië_Tyelelliéva.md)* issue [16](../T/Tyalië_Tyelelliéva_16.md) is published.
- **[2007](2007.md)** - *[The Company They Keep](../T/The_Company_They_Keep.md)* is published.
- **[2007](2007.md)** - [Electronic Arts](../E/Electronic_Arts.md) releases *[The Battle for Middle-earth Anthology](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_Anthology.md)*.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Pocket Guide](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_Pocket_Guide.md)* is published.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md): [Volume III: Allies of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Siege_of_Mirkwood.md#Volume_III:_Allies_of_the_King)*, "Oath of the Rangers" is released.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - *Oliphaunt Limited Edition Poster* is published.
- **[2013](2013.md)** - [*Vinyar Tengwar* 50](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_50.md) is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2931](../T/Third_Age_2931.md):
    - [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), son of [Arathorn](../A/Arathorn_II.md), [Heir of Isildur](../H/Heir_of_Isildur.md), is born.
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) begin the passage of the [Dead Marshes](../D/Dead_Marshes.md) at dawn. They temporarily halt and hide from a flying [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md).
    - The [Entmoot](../E/Entmoot.md), which started on [30 February](30_February.md), continues.
    - Aragorn enters Fangorn and meets [Gandalf the White](../G/Gandalf.md). They set out for [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).
    - [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) and his men leave for [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) to ambush the [Southrons](../S/Southrons.md) marching to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).
- [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md):
    - Death of King [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) of the Reunited Kingdom of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md). [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md), son of Elessar and [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md), becomes King.
- [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [121](../F/Fourth_Age_121.md):
    - Death of [Arwen Undómiel](../A/Arwen_Undómiel.md), the Queen of the Reunited Kingdom, at [Cerin Amroth](../C/Cerin_Amroth.md).
