[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009" title="wikipedia:2009">2009</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast).
- **[9](/wiki/9_January "9 January")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Conquest](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Conquest)* is released in Europe.
- **[13](/wiki/13_January "13 January")** - *The Lord of the Rings: Conquest* is released in North America.
- **[26](/wiki/26_January "26 January")** - *[Black &amp; White Ogre Country](/wiki/Black_%26_White_Ogre_Country)* is published.

## March {#March}

- **[15](/wiki/15_March "15 March")** - *[Arda Reconstructed: The Creation of the Published Silmarillion](/wiki/Arda_Reconstructed:_The_Creation_of_the_Published_Silmarillion)* is published.
- **[17](/wiki/17_March "17 March")** - The seventh book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Mines of Moria](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Mines_of_Moria)*, "Leaves of Lórien", is released.

## April {#April}

- **[1](/wiki/1_April "1 April")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: War of the Ring](</wiki/War_of_the_Ring_(2009_book)> "War of the Ring (2009 book)")* is published.
- **[4](/wiki/4_April "4 April")** - The Fourth Grand Council of the [Sociedad Tolkien Peruana](/wiki/Sociedad_Tolkien_Peruana) is held in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lima" title="wikipedia:Lima">Lima, Peru</a>.
- **[19](/wiki/19_April "19 April")** - The first e-Book of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)* are published.
- **[28](/wiki/28_April "28 April")** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien (1997 documentary)](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_(1997_documentary)>)* is released on DVD.

## May {#May}

- **[3](/wiki/3_May "3 May")** - *[The Hunt for Gollum](/wiki/The_Hunt_for_Gollum)*, Chris Bouchard's fan film, is released online.
- **[5](/wiki/5_May "5 May")** - *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudr%C3%BAn)* is published.
- **[7](/wiki/7_May "7 May")** - *[Cinematic Scripture: The Structure and Usage of The Lord of the Rings as Equipment for Living](/wiki/Cinematic_Scripture:_The_Structure_and_Usage_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_as_Equipment_for_Living)* is published.
- **?** - [Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment](/wiki/Warner_Bros._Interactive_Entertainment) takes the license from [Saul Zaentz Company](/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises "Middle-earth Enterprises") to produce video games.

## June {#June}

- **[2](/wiki/2_June "2 June")** - The Deluxe Edition of *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudr%C3%BAn)* is published.
- **[11](/wiki/11_June "11 June")** - *[The Road Goes Ever On and On: The Map of Tolkien's Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_and_On:_The_Map_of_Tolkien%27s_Middle-earth)* is published.
- **[17](/wiki/17_June "17 June")** - *[The Hobbitonian Anthology‎](/wiki/The_Hobbitonian_Anthology "The Hobbitonian Anthology")* is published.
- **[22](/wiki/22_June "22 June")** - *[Tolkien's Bag End](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Bag_End)* is published.
- **[23](/wiki/23_June "23 June")** - The eighth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Mines of Moria](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Mines_of_Moria)*, "Scourge of Khazad-dûm", is released.
- **[30](/wiki/30_June "30 June")** - *[Languages, Myths and History](/wiki/Languages,_Myths_and_History)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[1](/wiki/1_July "1 July")** - The [Tolkien Calendar 2010](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2010) and [Tolkien Diary 2010](/wiki/Tolkien_Diary_2010) are published.
- **[6](/wiki/6_July "6 July")** - *[The Epic Realm of Tolkien: Part One: Beren and Lúthien](/wiki/The_Epic_Realm_of_Tolkien "The Epic Realm of Tolkien")* is published.
- **[17](/wiki/17_July "17 July")-[20](/wiki/20_July "20 July")** - [Mythcon 40](/wiki/Mythcon_40).
- **[21](/wiki/21_July "21 July")** - Artist [Heinz Edelmann](/wiki/Heinz_Edelmann) dies.
- **[24](/wiki/24_July "24 July")-[26](/wiki/26_July "26 July")** - [Tolkien Thing](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing) 2009.

## August {#August}

- **[7](/wiki/7_August "7 August")** - At Montreal's WorldCon, *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* wins the 2009 Prometheus Hall of Fame Award for the best classic literary work of liberty.
- **[6](/wiki/6_August "6 August")-[9](/wiki/9_August "9 August")** - [Omentielva Nelya](/wiki/Omentielva "Omentielva") - 3rd International Conference on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s Invented Languages in Whitehaven, England

## September {#September}

- **[1](/wiki/1_September "1 September")** - *[The Lord of the Films](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Films)* is published.
- **[8](/wiki/8_September "8 September")** - *[Halifirien: The Hallowed Mountain](/wiki/Halifirien:_The_Hallowed_Mountain)* is released online.
- **[8](/wiki/8_September "8 September")** - The [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate), [The Tolkien Trust](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Trust) and [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) settle their lawsuit with [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) and the [Saul Zaentz Company](/wiki/Saul_Zaentz_Company).
- **[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")** - The Frisian translation of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Anne_Tjerk_Popkema&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Anne Tjerk Popkema (page does not exist)">Anne Tjerk Popkema</a>, is published.
- **[25](/wiki/25_September "25 September")-[27](/wiki/27_September "27 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2009](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2009).

## October {#October}

- **[2](/wiki/2_October "2 October")-[4](/wiki/4_October "4 October")** - [Ring\*Con 2009](/wiki/Ring*Con_2009).
- **[3](/wiki/3_October "3 October")** - Author [Gisbert Kranz](/wiki/Gisbert_Kranz) dies.
- **[5](/wiki/5_October "5 October")** - <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_(TV_channel)" title="wikipedia:History (TV channel)">The History Channel</a> airs *[Tolkien's Monsters](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Monsters)*.
- **[14](/wiki/14_October "14 October")** - Actor [Martyn Sanderson](/wiki/Martyn_Sanderson) dies.
- **[15](/wiki/15_October "15 October")-[18](/wiki/18_October "18 October")** - Mereth Aderthad XV, or "EstelCon", in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardona,_Spain" title="wikipedia:Cardona, Spain">Cardona</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.
- **[16](/wiki/16_October "16 October")** - Voice actor [Denys Hawthorne](/wiki/Denys_Hawthorne) dies.
- **[27](/wiki/27_October "27 October")** - Fantasy novel *[The Shadow Dragons](/wiki/The_Shadow_Dragons)*, featuring [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) as a fictional character, is published.

## November {#November}

- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")**- The <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook" title="wikipedia:Facebook">Facebook</a> application *[The One Ring](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(Facebook_application)> "The One Ring (Facebook application)")* is launched.
- **[23](/wiki/23_November "23 November")** - *[Parma Eldalamberon](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon) [#18](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_18 "Parma Eldalamberon 18")* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Siege of Mirkwood](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Siege_of_Mirkwood)* is released in North America.
- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - *[Born of Hope](/wiki/Born_of_Hope)* is released online.
- **[3](/wiki/3_December "3 December")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Siege of Mirkwood](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Siege_of_Mirkwood)* is released in Europe.
- **[22](/wiki/22_December "22 December")** - *[War of the Fantasy Worlds](/wiki/War_of_the_Fantasy_Worlds)* is published.

## ? {#?}

- **(spring)** - [*Mallorn* 47](/wiki/Mallorn_47 "Mallorn 47") is published.
- **(autumn)** - [*Mallorn* 48](/wiki/Mallorn_48 "Mallorn 48") is published.
- **?** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 6](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_6)* is published.
