[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2009" title="wikipedia:2009">2009</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](3_January.md)** - [Birthday Toast](../B/Birthday_Toast.md).
- **[9](9_January.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Conquest](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Conquest.md)* is released in Europe.
- **[13](13_January.md)** - *The Lord of the Rings: Conquest* is released in North America.
- **[26](26_January.md)** - *[Black &amp; White Ogre Country](../B/Black_&_White_Ogre_Country.md)* is published.

## March {#March}

- **[15](15_March.md)** - *[Arda Reconstructed: The Creation of the Published Silmarillion](../A/Arda_Reconstructed%253A_The_Creation_of_the_Published_Silmarillion.md)* is published.
- **[17](17_March.md)** - The seventh book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Mines of Moria](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Mines_of_Moria.md)*, "Leaves of Lórien", is released.

## April {#April}

- **[1](1_April.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: War of the Ring](<../W/War_of_the_Ring_(2009_book).md>)* is published.
- **[4](4_April.md)** - The Fourth Grand Council of the [Sociedad Tolkien Peruana](../S/Sociedad_Tolkien_Peruana.md) is held in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lima" title="wikipedia:Lima">Lima, Peru</a>.
- **[19](19_April.md)** - The first e-Book of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Children of Húrin](../T/The_Children_of_Húrin.md)* are published.
- **[28](28_April.md)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien (1997 documentary)](<../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_(1997_documentary).md>)* is released on DVD.

## May {#May}

- **[3](3_May.md)** - *[The Hunt for Gollum](../T/The_Hunt_for_Gollum.md)*, Chris Bouchard's fan film, is released online.
- **[5](5_May.md)** - *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](../T/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudrún.md)* is published.
- **[7](7_May.md)** - *[Cinematic Scripture: The Structure and Usage of The Lord of the Rings as Equipment for Living](../C/Cinematic_Scripture%253A_The_Structure_and_Usage_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_as_Equipment_for_Living.md)* is published.
- **?** - [Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment](../W/Warner_Bros._Interactive_Entertainment.md) takes the license from [Saul Zaentz Company](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md) to produce video games.

## June {#June}

- **[2](2_June.md)** - The Deluxe Edition of *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](../T/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudrún.md)* is published.
- **[11](11_June.md)** - *[The Road Goes Ever On and On: The Map of Tolkien's Middle-earth](../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_and_On%253A_The_Map_of_Tolkien's_Middle-earth.md)* is published.
- **[17](17_June.md)** - *[The Hobbitonian Anthology‎](../T/The_Hobbitonian_Anthology.md)* is published.
- **[22](22_June.md)** - *[Tolkien's Bag End](../T/Tolkien's_Bag_End.md)* is published.
- **[23](23_June.md)** - The eighth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Mines of Moria](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Mines_of_Moria.md)*, "Scourge of Khazad-dûm", is released.
- **[30](30_June.md)** - *[Languages, Myths and History](../L/Languages,_Myths_and_History.md)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[1](1_July.md)** - The [Tolkien Calendar 2010](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2010.md) and [Tolkien Diary 2010](../T/Tolkien_Diary_2010.md) are published.
- **[6](6_July.md)** - *[The Epic Realm of Tolkien: Part One: Beren and Lúthien](../T/The_Epic_Realm_of_Tolkien.md)* is published.
- **[17](17_July.md)-[20](20_July.md)** - [Mythcon 40](../M/Mythcon_40.md).
- **[21](21_July.md)** - Artist [Heinz Edelmann](../H/Heinz_Edelmann.md) dies.
- **[24](24_July.md)-[26](26_July.md)** - [Tolkien Thing](../T/Tolkien_Thing.md) 2009.

## August {#August}

- **[7](7_August.md)** - At Montreal's WorldCon, *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* wins the 2009 Prometheus Hall of Fame Award for the best classic literary work of liberty.
- **[6](6_August.md)-[9](9_August.md)** - [Omentielva Nelya](../O/Omentielva.md) - 3rd International Conference on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Invented Languages in Whitehaven, England

## September {#September}

- **[1](1_September.md)** - *[The Lord of the Films](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Films.md)* is published.
- **[8](8_September.md)** - *[Halifirien: The Hallowed Mountain](../H/Halifirien%253A_The_Hallowed_Mountain.md)* is released online.
- **[8](8_September.md)** - The [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md), [The Tolkien Trust](../T/The_Tolkien_Trust.md) and [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md) settle their lawsuit with [New Line Cinema](../N/New_Line_Cinema.md) and the [Saul Zaentz Company](../S/Saul_Zaentz_Company.md).
- **[22](22_September.md)** - The Frisian translation of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Anne_Tjerk_Popkema&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Anne Tjerk Popkema (page does not exist)">Anne Tjerk Popkema</a>, is published.
- **[25](25_September.md)-[27](27_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2009](../O/Oxonmoot_2009.md).

## October {#October}

- **[2](2_October.md)-[4](4_October.md)** - [Ring\*Con 2009](../R/Ring%252ACon_2009.md).
- **[3](3_October.md)** - Author [Gisbert Kranz](../G/Gisbert_Kranz.md) dies.
- **[5](5_October.md)** - <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_(TV_channel)" title="wikipedia:History (TV channel)">The History Channel</a> airs *[Tolkien's Monsters](../T/Tolkien's_Monsters.md)*.
- **[14](14_October.md)** - Actor [Martyn Sanderson](../M/Martyn_Sanderson.md) dies.
- **[15](15_October.md)-[18](18_October.md)** - Mereth Aderthad XV, or "EstelCon", in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardona,_Spain" title="wikipedia:Cardona, Spain">Cardona</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.
- **[16](16_October.md)** - Voice actor [Denys Hawthorne](../D/Denys_Hawthorne.md) dies.
- **[27](27_October.md)** - Fantasy novel *[The Shadow Dragons](../T/The_Shadow_Dragons.md)*, featuring [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) as a fictional character, is published.

## November {#November}

- **[6](6_November.md)**- The <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook" title="wikipedia:Facebook">Facebook</a> application *[The One Ring](<../T/The_One_Ring_(Facebook_application).md>)* is launched.
- **[23](23_November.md)** - *[Parma Eldalamberon](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon.md) [#18](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_18.md)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[1](1_December.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Siege of Mirkwood](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Siege_of_Mirkwood.md)* is released in North America.
- **[1](1_December.md)** - *[Born of Hope](../B/Born_of_Hope.md)* is released online.
- **[3](3_December.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Siege of Mirkwood](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Siege_of_Mirkwood.md)* is released in Europe.
- **[22](22_December.md)** - *[War of the Fantasy Worlds](../W/War_of_the_Fantasy_Worlds.md)* is published.
