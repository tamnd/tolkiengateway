[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1933" title="wikipedia:1933">1933</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[4](/wiki/4_February "4 February")** - [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) writes to lifelong friend Arthur Greeves on *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, saying "*Since term began \[on January 15\] I have had a delightful time reading a children's story which Tolkien has just written . . . Whether it is really good (I think it is until the end) is of course another question: still more, whether it will succeed with modern children*" (*They Stand Together*, collected letters from Lewis to Greeves, ed. <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Hooper" title="wikipedia:Walter Hooper">Walter Hooper</a>, No. 183).
- **[24](/wiki/24_February "24 February")** - [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[16](/wiki/16_March "16 March")** - Tolkien writes a [letter to Kenneth Sisam](</wiki/Letter_to_Kenneth_Sisam_(16_March_1933)> "Letter to Kenneth Sisam (16 March 1933)").

## April {#April}

- **[5](/wiki/5_April "5 April")** - Actor [Noel Appleby](/wiki/Noel_Appleby) is born.

## September {#September}

- **?** - Tolkien visits [Sarehole](/wiki/Sarehole), the place of his childhood, and laments the changes he observed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## November {#November}

- **[9](/wiki/9_November "9 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[Errantry](/wiki/Errantry)" is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.

## December {#December}

- **[2](/wiki/2_December "2 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **[21](/wiki/21_December "21 December")** - Father Christmas writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **[21](/wiki/21_December "21 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to R.W. Chambers](/wiki/Letter_to_R.W._Chambers "Letter to R.W. Chambers").
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) gives a decorated manuscript containing the poem "[Visio de Doworst](/wiki/Visio_de_Doworst)" to his friend [R.W. Chambers](/wiki/R.W._Chambers).

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[The Saga of Hrolf Kraki](/wiki/The_Saga_of_Hrolf_Kraki)*, translated by [Stella M. Mills](/wiki/Stella_M._Mills) and dedicated to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **?** - [Michael McStay](/wiki/Michael_McStay), who would voice [Ceorl](/wiki/Ceorl) in [the 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born in Essex, England.
- **?** - [Bernie Zuber](/wiki/Bernie_Zuber), author and artist, is born.
