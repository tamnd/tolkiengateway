[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1933" title="wikipedia:1933">1933</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[4](4_February.md)** - [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) writes to lifelong friend Arthur Greeves on *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, saying "*Since term began \[on January 15\] I have had a delightful time reading a children's story which Tolkien has just written . . . Whether it is really good (I think it is until the end) is of course another question: still more, whether it will succeed with modern children*" (*They Stand Together*, collected letters from Lewis to Greeves, ed. <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Hooper" title="wikipedia:Walter Hooper">Walter Hooper</a>, No. 183).
- **[24](24_February.md)** - [Verlyn Flieger](../V/Verlyn_Flieger.md) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[16](16_March.md)** - Tolkien writes a [letter to Kenneth Sisam](<../L/Letter_to_Kenneth_Sisam_(16_March_1933).md>).

## April {#April}

- **[5](5_April.md)** - Actor [Noel Appleby](../N/Noel_Appleby.md) is born.

## September {#September}

- **?** - Tolkien visits [Sarehole](../S/Sarehole.md), the place of his childhood, and laments the changes he observed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## November {#November}

- **[9](9_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem "[Errantry](../E/Errantry.md)" is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.

## December {#December}

- **[2](2_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[21](21_December.md)** - Father Christmas writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[21](21_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to R.W. Chambers](../L/Letter_to_R.W._Chambers.md).
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) gives a decorated manuscript containing the poem "[Visio de Doworst](../V/Visio_de_Doworst.md)" to his friend [R.W. Chambers](../R/R.W._Chambers.md).

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[The Saga of Hrolf Kraki](../T/The_Saga_of_Hrolf_Kraki.md)*, translated by [Stella M. Mills](../S/Stella_M._Mills.md) and dedicated to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.
- **?** - [Michael McStay](../M/Michael_McStay.md), who would voice [Ceorl](../C/Ceorl.md) in [the 1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), is born in Essex, England.
- **?** - [Bernie Zuber](../B/Bernie_Zuber.md), author and artist, is born.
