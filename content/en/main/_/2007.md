[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2007" title="wikipedia:2007">2007</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast).
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 203](/wiki/Amon_Hen_203)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[2](/wiki/2_February "2 February")** - The production of the video game *[The Lord of the Rings: The White Council](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_White_Council)* is put on hold indefinitely; it would later be officially cancelled.

## March {#March}

- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - *[The Company They Keep](/wiki/The_Company_They_Keep)* is published.
- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - [Electronic Arts](/wiki/Electronic_Arts) releases *[The Battle for Middle-earth Anthology](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_Anthology "The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth Anthology")*.
- **[9](/wiki/9_March "9 March")-[11](/wiki/11_March "11 March")** - The third "[The One Ring Celebration](/wiki/The_One_Ring_Celebration)" is held in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burbank,_California" title="wikipedia:Burbank, California">Burbank, California</a>.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 204](/wiki/Amon_Hen_204)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[15](/wiki/15_April "15 April")** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 4](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_4)* is published.
- <b><a href="/wiki/17_April" title="17 April">17</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin" title="The Children of Húrin">The Children of Húrin</a></i> is published</b>.
- **[24](/wiki/24_April "24 April")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)* is released.
- **[30](/wiki/30_April "30 April")** - *[Roots and Branches](/wiki/Roots_and_Branches)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **[1](/wiki/1_May "1 May")** - *[The History of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit)* Volume 1 is published.
- **[9](/wiki/9_May "9 May")** - *[Quest for Middle-earth](/wiki/Quest_for_Middle-earth)* is published.
- **[15](/wiki/15_May "15 May")** - Artist [Angus McBride](/wiki/Angus_McBride) dies.
- **[16](/wiki/16_May "16 May")** - The Deluxe Edition of *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)* is published.
- **[17](/wiki/17_May "17 May")** - Actor [Noel Appleby](/wiki/Noel_Appleby) dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 205](/wiki/Amon_Hen_205)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[4](/wiki/4_June "4 June")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Official Stage Companion](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Official_Stage_Companion)* is published.
- **[12](/wiki/12_June "12 June")** - The fan film *[The Peril to the Shire](/wiki/The_Peril_to_the_Shire)* premieres to a private audience.
- **[14](/wiki/14_June "14 June")** - The ninth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*, "Shores of Evendim", is released.
- **[19](/wiki/19_June "19 June")** - *[The Lord of the Rings (musical)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical)>)* premieres at the Theatre Royal Drury Lane in London.
- **[26](/wiki/26_June "26 June")** - *[Tree of Tales](/wiki/Tree_of_Tales)* is published.
- **?** - *[Vinyar Tengwar 49](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_49)* is published.
- **?** - *[Inside Language](/wiki/Inside_Language)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[13](/wiki/13_July "13 July")-[15](/wiki/15_July "15 July")** - [Tolkien Thing IX](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing "Tolkien Thing")
- **[27](/wiki/27_July "27 July")-[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")** - [MerpCon III](/wiki/MerpCon_III).
- **?** - *[Parma Eldalamberon](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon)* 17 is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 206](/wiki/Amon_Hen_206)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[3](/wiki/3_August "3 August")-[6](/wiki/6_August "6 August")** - [Mythcon 38](/wiki/Mythcon_38).
- **[4](/wiki/4_August "4 August")** - Author [Timothy J. Wheeler](/wiki/Timothy_J._Wheeler) dies.
- **[8](/wiki/8_August "8 August")-[11](/wiki/11_August "11 August")** - [Omentielva Tatya](/wiki/Omentielva "Omentielva"), the second international conference on Tolkien' s invented languages
- **[21](/wiki/21_August "21 August")** - The tenth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*, "The City of Kings", is released in North America.
- **[24](/wiki/24_August "24 August")** - The tenth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*, "The City of Kings", is released in Europe.
- **[25](/wiki/25_August "25 August")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s Australian Seminar 2007 in Sydney, [Australia](/wiki/Australia).
- **[27](/wiki/27_August "27 August")** - *[The Frodo Franchise](/wiki/The_Frodo_Franchise)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[10](/wiki/10_September "10 September")** - *["from mind to mind": Robert Browning and J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/From_Mind_to_Mind "From Mind to Mind")* is published.
- **[14](/wiki/14_September "14 September")-[16](/wiki/16_September "16 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2007](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2007).
- **[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")** - *[The History of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit)* is published in the United States.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 207](/wiki/Amon_Hen_207)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[1](/wiki/1_October "1 October")** - The second and expanded edition of *[Tolkien On Fairy-stories](/wiki/Tolkien_On_Fairy-stories)* is published.
- **[1](/wiki/1_October "1 October")** – The redesigned slipcased edition of *[Mr. Bliss](/wiki/Mr._Bliss)* is published.
- **[5](/wiki/5_October "5 October")-[7](/wiki/7_October "7 October")** - *Cair Andros*, a feast of the [Ellinikós Sýllogos Philon Tólkin](/wiki/Ellinik%C3%B3s_S%C3%BDllogos_Philon_T%C3%B3lkin) (or "The Prancing Pony").
- **[12](/wiki/12_October "12 October")-[15](/wiki/15_October "15 October")** - [Ring\*Con 2007](/wiki/Ring*Con_2007).
- **[24](/wiki/24_October "24 October")** - The eleventh book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*, "Prisoner of the Free Peoples" is released in North America.
- **[25](/wiki/25_October "25 October")** - The eleventh book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*, "Prisoner of the Free Peoples", is released in Europe.

## November {#November}

- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - *[Essential Tolkien](/wiki/Essential_Tolkien)* audiobook is released.
- **[15](/wiki/15_November "15 November")** - *[Myth and Magic: Art according to the Inklings](/wiki/Myth_and_Magic:_Art_according_to_the_Inklings)* is published.
- **[19](/wiki/19_November "19 November")** - *[The Children of Húrin Signed Limited Edition](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin_Signed_Limited_Edition)* is published.
- **[20](/wiki/20_November "20 November")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King - The Complete Recordings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_-_The_Complete_Recordings)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 208](/wiki/Amon_Hen_208)* is published.
- ? - The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: Mordor is published

## December {#December}

- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - *[Chesterton and Tolkien as Theologians](/wiki/Chesterton_and_Tolkien_as_Theologians)* is published.
- **[18](/wiki/18_December "18 December")** - [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) and [MGM](/wiki/MGM) announce the development of [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").
