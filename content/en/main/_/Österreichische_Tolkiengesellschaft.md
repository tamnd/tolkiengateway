**Österreichische Tolkiengesellschaft**, ÖTG, (Austrian Tolkien Society) is the [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies") in Austria.

## History {#History}

ÖTG was founded on 20 September 2002.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> ÖTG organizes national events annually and also has a number of local chapters throughout Austria. ÖTG typically attends the Oxonmoot as well as other events in neighbouring countries (Germany, Italy and Slovenia).

## Publications {#Publications}

ÖTG publishes *Halfirien*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> 25 issues have appeared through 2011.
