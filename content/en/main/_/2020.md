[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020" title="wikipedia:2020">2020</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](3_January.md)** - [Birthday Toast](../B/Birthday_Toast.md#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[16](16_January.md) - [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), third son of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), dies at the age of ninety-five.**
- **[23](../J/January_23.md)** - [Barbara Remington](../B/Barbara_Remington.md), cover artist on some American editions, dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 281](../A/Amon_Hen_281.md)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[7](../F/February_7.md)** - Actor [Orson Bean](../O/Orson_Bean.md) dies.
- **[16](../F/February_16.md)** - la BnF's exhibition "<a class="external text" href="https://www.bnf.fr/fr" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien, voyage en Terre du Milieu</a>" closes.

## March {#March}

- **[14](14_March.md)** - Filming for the first season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* begins.
- **[25](25_March.md)** - [Tolkien Reading Day](../T/Tolkien_Reading_Day.md).
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 282](../A/Amon_Hen_282.md)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[15](15_April.md)** - [Sean Arnold](../S/Sean_Arnold.md), who would play [Odo Proudfoot](../O/Odo_Proudfoot.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1981 radio series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), dies.
- **[16](16_April.md)** - Animator [Gene Deitch](../G/Gene_Deitch.md), director of [*The Hobbit* (1967 film)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1967_film).md>) dies.
- **[23](23_April.md)** - Actor [Bruce Allpress](../B/Bruce_Allpress.md) dies.
- **[30](30_April.md)** - The deluxe edition of *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight: with Pearl and Sir Orfeo](../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl_and_Sir_Orfeo.md#2020_Deluxe_edition)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **?** - *[Amon Hen 283](../A/Amon_Hen_283.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 60](../M/Mallorn_60.md)* is published.

## June {#June}

- <b><a href="9_June.md" title="9 June">9</a> - <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../T/The_Worlds_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_The_Places_that_Inspired_Middle-earth.md" title="The Worlds of J.R.R. Tolkien: The Places that Inspired Middle-earth">The Worlds of J.R.R. Tolkien: The Places that Inspired Middle-earth</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[19](19_June.md)** - [Ian Holm](../I/Ian_Holm.md), actor who played [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and voiced [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), dies.
- **[25](25_June.md)** - A new illustrated edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[4](4_July.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) Seminar is held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.
- **[19](19_July.md)** - *[Middle-earth, or There and Back Again](../M/Middle-earth,_or_There_and_Back_Again.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 284](../A/Amon_Hen_284.md)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[1](1_September.md)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2021](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2021.md)* is published.
- **[4](4_September.md)** - A second edition of *[The First and Second Ages](../T/The_First_and_Second_Ages.md)* is published.
- **[18](18_September.md) - [20](20_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2020](../O/Oxonmoot_2020.md) held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 285](../A/Amon_Hen_285.md)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[1](1_October.md)** - A new illustrated edition of *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)* is published.
- **[20](20_October.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: War of Three Peaks](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_War_of_Three_Peaks.md)* is released online.
- **[22](22_October.md)** - American author [Richard A. Lupoff](../R/Richard_A._Lupoff.md) dies.
- **[29](29_October.md)** - The Centenary Edition of *[Letters from Father Christmas](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md)* is published.

## November {#November}

- **?** - *[Amon Hen 286](../A/Amon_Hen_286.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 61](../M/Mallorn_61.md)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[2](2_December.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)* and *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)* film series are released in remastered 4k edition on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultra_HD_Blu-ray" title="wikipedia:Ultra HD Blu-ray">Ultra HD Blu-ray</a>.
- **[15](15_December.md)** - The [Lambengolmor mailing list](<../L/Lambengolmor_(mailing_list).md>) is closed down.
