[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020" title="wikipedia:2020">2020</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[16](/wiki/16_January "16 January") - [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), third son of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), dies at the age of ninety-five.**
- **[23](/wiki/January_23 "January 23")** - [Barbara Remington](/wiki/Barbara_Remington), cover artist on some American editions, dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 281](/wiki/Amon_Hen_281)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[7](/wiki/February_7 "February 7")** - Actor [Orson Bean](/wiki/Orson_Bean) dies.
- **[16](/wiki/February_16 "February 16")** - la BnF's exhibition "<a class="external text" href="https://www.bnf.fr/fr" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien, voyage en Terre du Milieu</a>" closes.

## March {#March}

- **[14](/wiki/14_March "14 March")** - Filming for the first season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* begins.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 282](/wiki/Amon_Hen_282)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[15](/wiki/15_April "15 April")** - [Sean Arnold](/wiki/Sean_Arnold), who would play [Odo Proudfoot](/wiki/Odo_Proudfoot) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1981 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), dies.
- **[16](/wiki/16_April "16 April")** - Animator [Gene Deitch](/wiki/Gene_Deitch), director of [*The Hobbit* (1967 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_film)> "The Hobbit (1967 film)") dies.
- **[23](/wiki/23_April "23 April")** - Actor [Bruce Allpress](/wiki/Bruce_Allpress) dies.
- **[30](/wiki/30_April "30 April")** - The deluxe edition of *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight: with Pearl and Sir Orfeo](/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl_and_Sir_Orfeo#2020_Deluxe_edition "Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, Pearl and Sir Orfeo")* is published.

## May {#May}

- **?** - *[Amon Hen 283](/wiki/Amon_Hen_283)* is published.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 60](/wiki/Mallorn_60)* is published.

## June {#June}

- <b><a href="/wiki/9_June" title="9 June">9</a> - <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_Worlds_of_J.R.R._Tolkien:_The_Places_that_Inspired_Middle-earth" title="The Worlds of J.R.R. Tolkien: The Places that Inspired Middle-earth">The Worlds of J.R.R. Tolkien: The Places that Inspired Middle-earth</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[19](/wiki/19_June "19 June")** - [Ian Holm](/wiki/Ian_Holm), actor who played [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and voiced [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), dies.
- **[25](/wiki/25_June "25 June")** - A new illustrated edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[4](/wiki/4_July "4 July")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) Seminar is held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.
- **[19](/wiki/19_July "19 July")** - *[Middle-earth, or There and Back Again](/wiki/Middle-earth,_or_There_and_Back_Again)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 284](/wiki/Amon_Hen_284)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[1](/wiki/1_September "1 September")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2021](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2021)* is published.
- **[4](/wiki/4_September "4 September")** - A second edition of *[The First and Second Ages](/wiki/The_First_and_Second_Ages)* is published.
- **[18](/wiki/18_September "18 September") - [20](/wiki/20_September "20 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2020](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2020) held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 285](/wiki/Amon_Hen_285)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[1](/wiki/1_October "1 October")** - A new illustrated edition of *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)* is published.
- **[20](/wiki/20_October "20 October")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: War of Three Peaks](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_War_of_Three_Peaks)* is released online.
- **[22](/wiki/22_October "22 October")** - American author [Richard A. Lupoff](/wiki/Richard_A._Lupoff) dies.
- **[29](/wiki/29_October "29 October")** - The Centenary Edition of *[Letters from Father Christmas](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas)* is published.

## November {#November}

- **?** - *[Amon Hen 286](/wiki/Amon_Hen_286)* is published.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 61](/wiki/Mallorn_61)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[2](/wiki/2_December "2 December")** - *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)")* and *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")* film series are released in remastered 4k edition on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultra_HD_Blu-ray" title="wikipedia:Ultra HD Blu-ray">Ultra HD Blu-ray</a>.
- **[15](/wiki/15_December "15 December")** - The [Lambengolmor mailing list](</wiki/Lambengolmor_(mailing_list)> "Lambengolmor (mailing list)") is closed down.
