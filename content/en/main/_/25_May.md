- **[1972](1972.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 337](../L/Letter_337.md).

- **[2023](2023.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Gollum](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Gollum.md)* is released.
