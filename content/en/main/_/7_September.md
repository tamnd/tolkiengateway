Events that occurred on 7 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1924](/wiki/1924)** - Composer [Leonard Rosenman](/wiki/Leonard_Rosenman) is born.
- **[1926](/wiki/1926)** - Voice actor [Don Messick](/wiki/Don_Messick) is born.
- **[1937](/wiki/1937)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to George Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Letter_to_George_Allen_and_Unwin "Letter to George Allen and Unwin") concerning the promotion of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.
- **[1955](/wiki/1955)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 168](/wiki/Letter_168).
- **[1957](/wiki/1957)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 201](/wiki/Letter_201).
- **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 262](/wiki/Letter_262).
- **[1970](/wiki/1970)** – The [first Mythcon](/wiki/Mythcon_1 "Mythcon 1") is held at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Mudd_College" title="wikipedia:Harvey Mudd College">Harvey Mudd Collegein</a> <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont,_California" title="wikipedia:Claremont, California">Claremont</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1975](/wiki/1975)** - [Oxonmoot 1975](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1975) at the Welsh Poney, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford" title="wikipedia:Oxford">Oxford</a>.
- <b><a href="/wiki/1989" title="1989">1989</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Treason_of_Isengard" title="The Treason of Isengard">The Treason of Isengard</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **[1998](/wiki/1998)** – [Elfling](/wiki/Elfling) goes online.
- **[2000](/wiki/2000)** - The Brazilian Tolkien society [Conselho Branco, Sociedade Tolkien](/wiki/Conselho_Branco,_Sociedade_Tolkien) is founded.
- **[2017](/wiki/2017)** - The second edition of *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) is held captive by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
