[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2015" title="wikipedia:2015">2015</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[10](/wiki/10_January "10 January") - [11](/wiki/11_January "11 January")** - [Mythmoot](/wiki/Mythmoot) III.
- **[15](/wiki/15_January "15 January")** - *[A Brief History of The Hobbit](/wiki/A_Brief_History_of_The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[16](/wiki/16_January "16 January")** - *[Perilous and Fair](/wiki/Perilous_and_Fair)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 251](/wiki/Amon_Hen_251)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[14](/wiki/14_February "14 February")** - Actor [Alan Howard](/wiki/Alan_Howard) dies.
- **[26](/wiki/26_February "26 February")** - *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)* pocket edition is published.
- **[27](/wiki/27_February "27 February")** - Actor and singer [Leonard Nimoy](/wiki/Leonard_Nimoy) dies.

## March {#March}

- **[5](/wiki/5_March "5 March")** - Artist and linguist [Tom Loback](/wiki/Tom_Loback) dies.
- **[12](/wiki/12_March "12 March")** - [Terry Pratchett](/wiki/Terry_Pratchett) dies.
- **[24](/wiki/24_March "24 March")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)* is released for digital download.
- **[24](/wiki/24_March "24 March")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)* is released on Blu-ray and DVD in the USA.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 252](/wiki/Amon_Hen_252)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[20](/wiki/20_April "20 April")** - Voice actor [Peter Howell](/wiki/Peter_Howell) dies.
- **[20](/wiki/20_April "20 April")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)* is released on Blu-ray and DVD in the UK.
- **[27](/wiki/27_April "27 April")** - Cinematographer [Andrew Lesnie](/wiki/Andrew_Lesnie) dies.

## May {#May}

- **[15](/wiki/15_May "15 May")** - Voice actor [John Stephenson](/wiki/John_Stephenson) dies.
- **[21](/wiki/21_May "21 May")** - *[Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary](/wiki/Beowulf:_A_Translation_and_Commentary)* by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), edited by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), is published in paperback.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 253](/wiki/Amon_Hen_253)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[7](/wiki/7_June "7 June")** - Actor [Christopher Lee](/wiki/Christopher_Lee) dies.
- **[12](/wiki/12_June "12 June")** - [*Parma Eldalamberon* 22](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_22 "Parma Eldalamberon 22") is published.

## July {#July}

- **[4](/wiki/4_July "4 July")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) Seminar at the Hilton, [Leeds](/wiki/Leeds).
- **[7](/wiki/7_July "7 July")** - *[Representations of Nature in Middle-earth](/wiki/Representations_of_Nature_in_Middle-earth)* is published.
- **[21](/wiki/21_July "21 July")** – Actor [Theodore Bikel](/wiki/Theodore_Bikel) dies.
- **[30](/wiki/30_July "30 July")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2016](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2016)* is published.
- **[31](/wiki/31_July "31 July") - [3 Aug.](/wiki/3_August "3 August")** - [Mythcon 46](/wiki/Mythcon_46) in Hotel Elegante, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_Springs,_Colorado" title="wikipedia:Colorado Springs, Colorado">Colorado Springs</a>.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 254](/wiki/Amon_Hen_254)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[3](/wiki/3_August "3 August")** - [Jef Murray](/wiki/Jef_Murray) dies.
- **[6](/wiki/6_August "6 August")-[9](/wiki/9_August "9 August")** - [Omentielva Enquea](/wiki/Omentielva "Omentielva") - Sixth International Conference on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s Invented Languages in [Greisinger Museum](/wiki/Greisinger_Museum), Switzerland.
- **[11](/wiki/11_August "11 August")** - Actor [Richard Whiteside](/wiki/Richard_Whiteside) dies.
- **[27](/wiki/27_August "27 August")** - <i><b><a href="/wiki/The_Story_of_Kullervo" title="The Story of Kullervo">The Story of Kullervo</a></b></i> is published in the UK.
- **[31](/wiki/31_August "31 August")** - *[Journeys &amp; Destinations](/wiki/Journeys_%26_Destinations)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[5](/wiki/5_September "5 September")-[6](/wiki/6_September "6 September")** - <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Middle-earth_Festival&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Middle-earth Festival (page does not exist)">Middle-earth Festival</a> at [Sarehole Mill](/wiki/Sarehole_Mill), [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham).
- **[10](/wiki/10_September "10 September")-[13](/wiki/13_September "13 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2015](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2015) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>, [Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford").
- **[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")** - [Arne Zettersten](/wiki/Arne_Zettersten), colleague and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 255](/wiki/Amon_Hen_255)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[6](/wiki/6_October "6 October")** - [Árpád Göncz](/wiki/%C3%81rp%C3%A1d_G%C3%B6ncz) dies.
- **[8](/wiki/8_October "8 October")** - *[The Art of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Art_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is published in the UK.
- **[13](/wiki/13_October "13 October")** - *[The Art of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Art_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is published in the US.
- **[20](/wiki/20_October "20 October")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)* [Extended Edition](</wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies_(extended_edition)> "The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies (extended edition)") is released for digital download.

## November {#November}

- **[5](/wiki/5_November "5 November")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies: Chronicles: The Art of War](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies:_Chronicles:_The_Art_of_War)* is published.
- **[17](/wiki/17_November "17 November")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)* [Extended Edition](</wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies_(extended_edition)> "The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies (extended edition)") is released on Blu-ray and DVD in Canada and the USA.
- **[18](/wiki/18_November "18 November")** - *[The Tolkien Treasury](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Treasury)* is published.
- **[23](/wiki/23_November "23 November")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)* [Extended Edition](</wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies_(extended_edition)> "The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies (extended edition)") is released on Blu-ray and DVD in the UK.
- **?** - *[Fan Phenomena: The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Fan_Phenomena:_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 256](/wiki/Amon_Hen_256)* is published.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 56](/wiki/Mallorn_56)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[13](/wiki/13_December "13 December")** - Author [Daniel Grotta](/wiki/Daniel_Grotta) dies.
