Events that occurred on 7 August

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1952](/wiki/1952)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to George and Moira Sayer](</wiki/Letter_to_George_and_Moira_Sayer_(7_August_1952)> "Letter to George and Moira Sayer (7 August 1952)").
- **[1954](/wiki/1954)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 148](/wiki/Letter_148).
- **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 259](/wiki/Letter_259).
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - [Mythcon 12](/wiki/Mythcon_12) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mills_College" title="wikipedia:Mills College">Mills College</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland,_California" title="wikipedia:Oakland, California">Oakland</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1982](/wiki/1982)** - The fourth episode of the new version of the [1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[1994](/wiki/1994)** - [Mythcon 25](/wiki/Mythcon_25) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_University" title="wikipedia:American University">American University</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C." title="wikipedia:Washington, D.C.">Washington, D.C.</a>
- **[1995](/wiki/1995)** - [Mythcon 26](/wiki/Mythcon_26) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California" title="wikipedia:Berkeley, California">Berkeley</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - <a class="external text" href="http://omentielva.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Minya</a> - 1st International Conference on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s Invented Languages in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm" title="wikipedia:Stockholm">Stockholm</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden" title="wikipedia:Sweden">Sweden</a>.
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - [Mythcon 37](/wiki/Mythcon_37) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma" title="wikipedia:University of Oklahoma">University of Oklahoma</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman,_Oklahoma" title="wikipedia:Norman, Oklahoma">Norman</a>.
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** - At Montreal's <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worldcon" title="wikipedia:Worldcon">WorldCon</a>, *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* wins the 2009 Prometheus Hall of Fame Award for the best classic literary work of liberty.
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** - <a class="external text" href="http://omentielva.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Nelya</a> - 3rd International Conference on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s Invented Languages in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitehaven" title="wikipedia:Whitehaven">Whitehaven</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>.
- **[2015](/wiki/2015)** - [Omentielva Enquea](/wiki/Omentielva "Omentielva") - Sixth International Conference on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s Invented Languages in [Greisinger Museum](/wiki/Greisinger_Museum), Switzerland.
- **[2019](/wiki/2019)** - *[Tolkien and the Classics](/wiki/Tolkien_and_the_Classics)* is published.
