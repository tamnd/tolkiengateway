Events that occurred on 23 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1923](/wiki/1923)** - [*Bulletin of the Modern Humanities Research Association* 20](/wiki/Bulletin_of_the_Modern_Humanities_Research_Association_20 "Bulletin of the Modern Humanities Research Association 20"), containing an obituary written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **[1937](/wiki/1937)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 18](/wiki/Letter_18).
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 86](/wiki/Letter_86).
- **[1958](/wiki/1958)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 212a](/wiki/Letter_212a "Letter 212a").
- **[1999](/wiki/1999)** - [Hiswelókë](/wiki/Hiswel%C3%B3k%C3%AB)'s [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) dictionary begins.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - *[The Spoken Word: British Writers](/wiki/The_Spoken_Word:_British_Writers)*, featuring an [interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is released.
- **[2014](/wiki/2014)** - Writer and actor [Bernard Mayes](/wiki/Bernard_Mayes) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") recovers from his wound in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell). [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) finds the blade shard in his shoulder and removes it.
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
