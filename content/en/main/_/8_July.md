Events that occurred on 8 July.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1915](/wiki/1915)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the poem *[The Shores of Elfland](/wiki/The_Shores_of_Elfland)*.
- **[1953](/wiki/1953)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 137e](/wiki/Letter_137e).
- **[1963](/wiki/1963)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s paper "[English and Welsh](/wiki/English_and_Welsh)" is published in *[Angles and Britons: O'Donnell Lectures](/wiki/Angles_and_Britons "Angles and Britons")*.
- **[1969](/wiki/1969)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is diagnosed with an inflamed or diseased gall-bladder. Dr. Denis Tolhurst forbids him the consummation of fats (notably butter) and, even worse, alcohol.
- **[1973](/wiki/1973)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Norman Power](/wiki/Letter_to_Norman_Power "Letter to Norman Power").
- **[1999](/wiki/1999)** - The first [Tolkien Thing](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing) is held in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne" title="wikipedia:Cologne">Cologne</a>.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2011](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2011)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
