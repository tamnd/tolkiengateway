<i><b>-l</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) suffix that has three meanings.

## -l<sup>1</sup> {#-l1}

Used as an imperious or familiar form.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Longer form *-lye*.

## -l(ye)<sup>2</sup> {#-l(ye)2}

Used as a singular pronoun suffix.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## -l<sup>3</sup> {#-l3}

Used as a suffix meaning you (courteous form). Longer form *-lye*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
