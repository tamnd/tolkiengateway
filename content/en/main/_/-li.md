<i><b>-li</b></i> is a partitive plural ending in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), indicating "many, some, a lot of".<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:62</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Root](/wiki/Root) LI-, meaning "many", from which <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Li%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lië (page does not exist)">lië</a></i> also comes.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Compare *-lai* in *[Kinn-lai](/wiki/Kinn-lai)*.

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i>vanimá<b>li</b>on</i> = genitive "of beautiful children"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- i <i><a href="/wiki/Falma" title="Falma">falma</a>-<b>li</b>-nna-r</i> = "the foam wave-many-towards-pl. ending"<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:127</sup>
