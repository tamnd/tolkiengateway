Events that occurred on 9 February.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1921](1921.md)** - Author [Gisbert Kranz](../G/Gisbert_Kranz.md) is born.
- **[1928](1928.md)** - Artist [Frank Frazetta](../F/Frank_Frazetta.md) is born.
- **[1953](1953.md)** - Actor [Ciarán Hinds](../C/Ciarán_Hinds.md) is born.
- **[1960](1960.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 222a](../L/Letter_222a.md).
- **[1968](1968.md)** - The last day of filming [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) for the documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](../T/Tolkien_in_Oxford.md)*.
- **[1972](1972.md)** - Voice actor [Crispin Freeman](../C/Crispin_Freeman.md) is born.
- **[1980](1980.md)** - Voice actor [Heron Carvic](../H/Heron_Carvic.md) dies.
- **[1991](1991.md)** - First official meeting of the [Sociedad Tolkien Española](../S/Sociedad_Tolkien_Española.md).
- **[2008](2008.md)** - "C.S. Lewis, the Inklings, and the Call to Christian Community" conference, featuring [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md).
