Events that occurred on 7 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1955](1955.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 163](../L/Letter_163.md).
- **[1957](1957.md)** - [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md) acquires many of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s manuscripts for £1,500 (<a class="external text" href="https://www.thisismoney.co.uk/money/bills/article-1633409/Historic-inflation-calculator-value-money-changed-1900.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">worth £<i>£50,714</i> in 2025</a>).
- **[1964](1964.md)** - Actor [Brian Talbot](../B/Brian_Talbot.md) is born.
- **[1972](1972.md)** – Actor [Karl Urban](../K/Karl_Urban.md) is born.
- **[1981](1981.md)** - The fourteenth episode of the [1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- **[1999](1999.md)** - *[Tolkien: A Celebration: Collected Writings on a Literary Legacy](../T/Tolkien%253A_A_Celebration.md)* is published.
- **[2008](2008.md)** - [Forodrim](../F/Forodrim.md)'s 36th birthday celebration.
- **[2015](2015.md)** - Actor [Christopher Lee](../C/Christopher_Lee.md) dies.
