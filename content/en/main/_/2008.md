[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008" title="wikipedia:2008">2008</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](1_January.md)** - Fantasy novel *[The Search for the Red Dragon](../T/The_Search_for_the_Red_Dragon.md)*, featuring [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) as a fictional character, is published.
- **[3](3_January.md)** - [Birthday Toast](../B/Birthday_Toast.md).
- **[3](3_January.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s Birthday Dinner.
- **[5](5_January.md)** - <a class="external text" href="http://entertainment.timesonline.co.uk/tol/arts_and_entertainment/books/article3127837.ece" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Times</a> ranks [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) the sixth-best post-war British writer.
- **[14](14_January.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Ultimate Critical Review](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Ultimate_Critical_Review.md)* is released.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 209](../A/Amon_Hen_209.md)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[6](6_February.md)** - Artist [John Alvin](../J/John_Alvin.md) dies.
- **[7](7_February.md)-[9](9_February.md)** - *[C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md), the [Inklings](../I/Inklings.md), and the Call to Christian Community* conference, featuring [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md).
- **[11](11_February.md)** - The [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md), [The Tolkien Trust](../T/The_Tolkien_Trust.md) and [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md) file a lawsuit against [New Line Cinema](../N/New_Line_Cinema.md) and the [Saul Zaentz Company](../S/Saul_Zaentz_Company.md).
- **[13](13_February.md)** - The twelfth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Shadows_of_Angmar.md)*, "The Ashen Wastes", is released in North America.
- **[14](14_February.md)** - The twelfth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Shadows_of_Angmar.md)*, "The Ashen Wastes", is released in Europe.
- **[15](15_February.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings and the Western Narrative Tradition](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_and_the_Western_Narrative_Tradition.md)* is published

## March {#March}

- **[4](4_March.md)** - Composer [Leonard Rosenman](../L/Leonard_Rosenman.md) dies.
- **[9](9_March.md)** - *[Tolkien's Shorter Works](../T/Tolkien's_Shorter_Works.md)* is published.
- **[10](10_March.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is elected as the "Best Foreign Book of All Time" in the Netherlands.
- **[11](11_March.md)** - *[Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings – Sources of Inspiration](../T/Tolkien's_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_–_Sources_of_Inspiration.md)* is published.
- **[25](25_March.md)** - [Tolkien Reading Day](../T/Tolkien_Reading_Day.md).
- **[31](31_March.md)** - *[Tolkien's Gedling](../T/Tolkien's_Gedling.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 210](../A/Amon_Hen_210.md)* is published.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 45](../M/Mallorn_45.md) is published.

## April {#April}

- **[24](24_April.md)** - [Guillermo del Toro](../G/Guillermo_del_Toro.md) is announced as the director of *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)* film adaptation and it's proposed sequel.
- **[24](24_April.md)** - The thirteenth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Shadows_of_Angmar.md)*, "Doom of the Last-King", is released in North America.
- **[28](28_April.md)** - *[Tolkien - min vän Ronald och hans världar](../T/Tolkien_-_min_vän_Ronald_och_hans_världar.md)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **[1](1_May.md)** - *[Of Words and Worlds](../O/Of_Words_and_Worlds.md)* is published.
- **[1](1_May.md)** - The thirteenth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Shadows_of_Angmar.md)*, "Doom of the Last-King", is released in Europe.
- **[10](10_May.md)** - [Frank Frazetta](../F/Frank_Frazetta.md), who created illustrations for [The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md) and [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) dies.
- **[23](23_May.md)** - The [RiffTrax](../R/RiffTrax.md) commentary of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* is released online.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 211](../A/Amon_Hen_211.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Lembas Extra: Proceedings of the 5th Unquendor Lustrum](../L/Lembas_Extra%253A_Proceedings_of_the_5th_Unquendor_Lustrum.md)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[5](5_June.md)-[8](8_June.md)** - [Forodrim](../F/Forodrim.md)'s 36th birthday celebration.
- **[10](10_June.md)** - Actor [David Brierly](../D/David_Brierly.md) dies.

## July {#July}

- **[1](1_July.md)** - *[Tolkien On Fairy-stories](../T/Tolkien_On_Fairy-stories~23734.md)* is published.
- **[10](10_July.md)-[13](13_July.md)** - [Tolkien Thing X](../T/Tolkien_Thing.md).
- **[22](22_July.md)** - The fourteenth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Shadows_of_Angmar.md)*, "The Ring-forges of Eregion", is released in North America.
- **[24](24_July.md)** - The fourteenth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Shadows_of_Angmar.md)*, "The Ring-forges of Eregion", is released in Europe.
- **?** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 5](../T/Tolkien_Studies%253A_Volume_5.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 212](../A/Amon_Hen_212.md)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[1](1_August.md)** - [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md), Tolkien's favourite artist, dies.
- **[15](15_August.md)-[18](18_August.md)** - [Mythcon 39](../M/Mythcon_39.md).

## September {#September}

- **[8](8_September.md)** - *[Tolkien's Oxford](../T/Tolkien's_Oxford.md)* is published.
- **[25](25_September.md)-[28](28_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2008](../O/Oxonmoot_2008.md).
- **?** - The 2008 edition of *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 213](../A/Amon_Hen_213.md)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[7](7_October.md)** - The [radio series of *Tales from the Perilous Realm*](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>) is re-released in the US.
- **[21](21_October.md)** - Fantasy novel *[The Indigo King](../T/The_Indigo_King.md)*, featuring [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) as a fictional character, is published.
- **[23](23_October.md)** - *[The Spoken Word: British Writers](../T/The_Spoken_Word%253A_British_Writers.md)*, featuring an interview with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is released.

## November {#November}

- **[1](1_November.md)** - *[The Tolkien Name in History](../T/The_Tolkien_Name_in_History.md)* is published.
- **[12](12_November.md)** - *[Fantasy, Myth and the Measure of Truth: Tales of Pullman, Lewis, Tolkien, MacDonald and Hoffmann](../F/Fantasy,_Myth_and_the_Measure_of_Truth.md)* is published.
- **[18](18_November.md)** - The fifteenth and final book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Shadows_of_Angmar.md)*, "Daughter of Strife", is released.
- **[18](18_November.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Mines of Moria](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Mines_of_Moria.md)* is released.
- **[20](20_November.md)** - *[Tolkien, Race and Cultural History: From Fairies to Hobbits](../T/Tolkien,_Race_and_Cultural_History.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 214](../A/Amon_Hen_214.md)* is published.
