[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008" title="wikipedia:2008">2008</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** - Fantasy novel *[The Search for the Red Dragon](/wiki/The_Search_for_the_Red_Dragon)*, featuring [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) as a fictional character, is published.
- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast).
- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s Birthday Dinner.
- **[5](/wiki/5_January "5 January")** - <a class="external text" href="http://entertainment.timesonline.co.uk/tol/arts_and_entertainment/books/article3127837.ece" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Times</a> ranks [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) the sixth-best post-war British writer.
- **[14](/wiki/14_January "14 January")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Ultimate Critical Review](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Ultimate_Critical_Review)* is released.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 209](/wiki/Amon_Hen_209)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[6](/wiki/6_February "6 February")** - Artist [John Alvin](/wiki/John_Alvin) dies.
- **[7](/wiki/7_February "7 February")-[9](/wiki/9_February "9 February")** - *[C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings), and the Call to Christian Community* conference, featuring [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey).
- **[11](/wiki/11_February "11 February")** - The [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate), [The Tolkien Trust](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Trust) and [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) file a lawsuit against [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) and the [Saul Zaentz Company](/wiki/Saul_Zaentz_Company).
- **[13](/wiki/13_February "13 February")** - The twelfth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*, "The Ashen Wastes", is released in North America.
- **[14](/wiki/14_February "14 February")** - The twelfth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*, "The Ashen Wastes", is released in Europe.
- **[15](/wiki/15_February "15 February")** - *[The Lord of the Rings and the Western Narrative Tradition](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_and_the_Western_Narrative_Tradition)* is published

## March {#March}

- **[4](/wiki/4_March "4 March")** - Composer [Leonard Rosenman](/wiki/Leonard_Rosenman) dies.
- **[9](/wiki/9_March "9 March")** - *[Tolkien's Shorter Works](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Shorter_Works)* is published.
- **[10](/wiki/10_March "10 March")** - *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is elected as the "Best Foreign Book of All Time" in the Netherlands.
- **[11](/wiki/11_March "11 March")** - *[Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings – Sources of Inspiration](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_%E2%80%93_Sources_of_Inspiration)* is published.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- **[31](/wiki/31_March "31 March")** - *[Tolkien's Gedling](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Gedling)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 210](/wiki/Amon_Hen_210)* is published.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 45](/wiki/Mallorn_45 "Mallorn 45") is published.

## April {#April}

- **[24](/wiki/24_April "24 April")** - [Guillermo del Toro](/wiki/Guillermo_del_Toro) is announced as the director of *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")* film adaptation and it's proposed sequel.
- **[24](/wiki/24_April "24 April")** - The thirteenth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*, "Doom of the Last-King", is released in North America.
- **[28](/wiki/28_April "28 April")** - *[Tolkien - min vän Ronald och hans världar](/wiki/Tolkien_-_min_v%C3%A4n_Ronald_och_hans_v%C3%A4rldar)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **[1](/wiki/1_May "1 May")** - *[Of Words and Worlds](/wiki/Of_Words_and_Worlds)* is published.
- **[1](/wiki/1_May "1 May")** - The thirteenth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*, "Doom of the Last-King", is released in Europe.
- **[10](/wiki/10_May "10 May")** - [Frank Frazetta](/wiki/Frank_Frazetta), who created illustrations for [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit) and [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) dies.
- **[23](/wiki/23_May "23 May")** - The [RiffTrax](/wiki/RiffTrax) commentary of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* is released online.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 211](/wiki/Amon_Hen_211)* is published.
- **?** - *[Lembas Extra: Proceedings of the 5th Unquendor Lustrum](/wiki/Lembas_Extra:_Proceedings_of_the_5th_Unquendor_Lustrum)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[5](/wiki/5_June "5 June")-[8](/wiki/8_June "8 June")** - [Forodrim](/wiki/Forodrim)'s 36th birthday celebration.
- **[10](/wiki/10_June "10 June")** - Actor [David Brierly](/wiki/David_Brierly) dies.

## July {#July}

- **[1](/wiki/1_July "1 July")** - *[Tolkien On Fairy-stories](/wiki/Tolkien_On_Fairy-stories)* is published.
- **[10](/wiki/10_July "10 July")-[13](/wiki/13_July "13 July")** - [Tolkien Thing X](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing "Tolkien Thing").
- **[22](/wiki/22_July "22 July")** - The fourteenth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*, "The Ring-forges of Eregion", is released in North America.
- **[24](/wiki/24_July "24 July")** - The fourteenth book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*, "The Ring-forges of Eregion", is released in Europe.
- **?** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 5](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_5)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 212](/wiki/Amon_Hen_212)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[1](/wiki/1_August "1 August")** - [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes), Tolkien's favourite artist, dies.
- **[15](/wiki/15_August "15 August")-[18](/wiki/18_August "18 August")** - [Mythcon 39](/wiki/Mythcon_39).

## September {#September}

- **[8](/wiki/8_September "8 September")** - *[Tolkien's Oxford](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Oxford)* is published.
- **[25](/wiki/25_September "25 September")-[28](/wiki/28_September "28 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2008](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2008).
- **?** - The 2008 edition of *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](/wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 213](/wiki/Amon_Hen_213)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[7](/wiki/7_October "7 October")** - The [radio series of *Tales from the Perilous Realm*](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)") is re-released in the US.
- **[21](/wiki/21_October "21 October")** - Fantasy novel *[The Indigo King](/wiki/The_Indigo_King)*, featuring [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) as a fictional character, is published.
- **[23](/wiki/23_October "23 October")** - *[The Spoken Word: British Writers](/wiki/The_Spoken_Word:_British_Writers)*, featuring an interview with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is released.

## November {#November}

- **[1](/wiki/1_November "1 November")** - *[The Tolkien Name in History](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Name_in_History)* is published.
- **[12](/wiki/12_November "12 November")** - *[Fantasy, Myth and the Measure of Truth: Tales of Pullman, Lewis, Tolkien, MacDonald and Hoffmann](/wiki/Fantasy,_Myth_and_the_Measure_of_Truth "Fantasy, Myth and the Measure of Truth")* is published.
- **[18](/wiki/18_November "18 November")** - The fifteenth and final book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*, "Daughter of Strife", is released.
- **[18](/wiki/18_November "18 November")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Mines of Moria](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Mines_of_Moria)* is released.
- **[20](/wiki/20_November "20 November")** - *[Tolkien, Race and Cultural History: From Fairies to Hobbits](/wiki/Tolkien,_Race_and_Cultural_History "Tolkien, Race and Cultural History")* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 214](/wiki/Amon_Hen_214)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[29](/wiki/29_December "29 December")** - Artist [Kay Woollard](/wiki/Kay_Woollard) dies.

## ? {#?}
