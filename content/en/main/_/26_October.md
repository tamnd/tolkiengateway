Events that occurred on 26 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1916](/wiki/1916)** - While in reserve, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s battalion is inspected by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Haig" title="wikipedia:Douglas Haig">Sir Douglas Haig</a>, the British commander, at the [Battle of the Somme](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme).
- **[1945](/wiki/1945)** - [Jane Chance](/wiki/Jane_Chance) is born.
- **[1958](/wiki/1958)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to L.M. Cutts](/wiki/Letter_to_L.M._Cutts "Letter to L.M. Cutts").
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - Finding himself short of a speech subject, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) reads *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)* to a room of about 800 people.
- **[1994](/wiki/1994)** - *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va)* issue [2](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va_2 "Tyalië Tyelelliéva 2") is published.
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - [RingCon](/wiki/RingCon) III in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tacoma,_Washington" title="wikipedia:Tacoma, Washington">Tacoma, Washington</a>.
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - Scholar [Angus McIntosh](/wiki/Angus_McIntosh), pupil and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), dies.
- **[2017](/wiki/2017)** - [Roger Garland](/wiki/Roger_Garland) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") rest in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").
