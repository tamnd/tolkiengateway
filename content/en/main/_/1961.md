[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1961" title="wikipedia:1961">1961</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[4](/wiki/4_January "4 January")** - Actor [Graham McTavish](/wiki/Graham_McTavish) is born.
- **[5](/wiki/5_January "5 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 227](/wiki/Letter_227).
- **[5](/wiki/5_January "5 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mme Ossendrijver](</wiki/Letter_to_Madam_Ossendrijver_(5_January_1961)> "Letter to Madam Ossendrijver (5 January 1961)").
- **[24](/wiki/24_January "24 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 228](/wiki/Letter_228).

## February {#February}

- **[12](/wiki/12_February "12 February")** - [Joseph Pearce](/wiki/Joseph_Pearce) is born.
- **[13](/wiki/13_February "13 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 228a](/wiki/Letter_228a "Letter 228a").
- **[23](/wiki/23_February "23 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 229](/wiki/Letter_229).
- **[28](/wiki/28_February "28 February")** - [Mark Ferguson](/wiki/Mark_Ferguson), who would play [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, is born.

## April {#April}

- **[10](/wiki/10_April "10 April")** - Actress [Lorraine Ashbourne](/wiki/Lorraine_Ashbourne) is born.
- **[28](/wiki/28_April "28 April")** - [Jamieson Price](/wiki/Jamieson_Price), who would voice [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring)*, is born.

## May {#May}

- **[6](/wiki/6_May "6 May")** - Actor [Wally Wingert](/wiki/Wally_Wingert) is born.
- **[9](/wiki/9_May "9 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mr. Hart](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Hart "Letter to Mr Hart").
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) spends a Sunday with a fan club at Queen's College in [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge) (as the meeting happened mid-month, it was most probably on Sunday, the [14th](/wiki/14_May "14 May") of May).
- **[17](/wiki/17_May "17 May")** - Singer [Enya](/wiki/Enya) is born.
- **[23](/wiki/23_May "23 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Rayner Unwin](</wiki/Letter_to_Rayner_Unwin_(23_May_1961)> "Letter to Rayner Unwin (23 May 1961)").

## June {#June}

- **[1](/wiki/1_June "1 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Jan Broberg](/wiki/Letter_to_Jan_Broberg "Letter to Jan Broberg").
- **[8](/wiki/8_June "8 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 230](/wiki/Letter_230 "Letter 230").

## August {#August}

- **[21](/wiki/21_August "21 August")** - An [interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) ("[Den besynnerlige professor Tolkien](/wiki/Den_besynnerlige_professor_Tolkien)") is published in Swedish.

## October {#October}

- **[31](/wiki/31_October "31 October")** - Director [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) is born in Pukerua Bay, [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand).
