[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976" title="wikipedia:1976">1976</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[25](/wiki/25_January "25 January")** - [Hilary Arthur Reuel](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien "Hilary Tolkien"), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s brother, dies.

## April {#April}

- **[4](/wiki/4_April "4 April")** - [Gervase Mathew](/wiki/Gervase_Mathew), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), dies.
- **?** - *[Tolkien and The Silmarillion](/wiki/Tolkien_and_The_Silmarillion)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **[28](/wiki/28_May "28 May")** - Actor [Liam O'Brien](/wiki/Liam_O%27Brien) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[29](/wiki/29_June "29 June")** - Actor [Bret McKenzie](/wiki/Bret_McKenzie) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[2](/wiki/2_July "2 July")** - Actor [Alexander Lindsay](/wiki/Alexander_Lindsay) is born.
- **[19](/wiki/19_July "19 July")** - Actor [Benedict Cumberbatch](/wiki/Benedict_Cumberbatch) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[13](/wiki/13_August "13 August")-[16](/wiki/16_August "16 August")** - [Mythcon 7](/wiki/Mythcon_7).

## September {#September}

- **[2](/wiki/2_September "2 September")** - *[The Father Christmas Letters](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas")* is first published in the UK.
- **[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")** - Actor [Sala Baker](/wiki/Sala_Baker) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[1](/wiki/1_October "1 October")-[3](/wiki/3_October "3 October")** - [Oxonmoot 1976](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1976).
- **[19](/wiki/19_October "19 October")** - *[The Father Christmas Letters](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas")* is published in the US.

## November {#November}

- **?** - [*Fantastic* November 1976](/wiki/Fantastic_November_1976 "Fantastic November 1976"), featuring an article by [L. Sprague de Camp](/wiki/L._Sprague_de_Camp) about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.

## December {#December}

- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - Actor [Dean O'Gorman](/wiki/Dean_O%27Gorman) is born.
- **[8](/wiki/8_December "8 December")** - Actor [Dominic Monaghan](/wiki/Dominic_Monaghan) is born.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Milton Waldman](/wiki/Milton_Waldman) dies.

- **?** - *[The Tolkien Companion](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Companion)* is published.

- **?** - [*Mallorn* 10](/wiki/Mallorn_10 "Mallorn 10") is published.
