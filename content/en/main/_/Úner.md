**Úner**, 'Noman', was the man [Ancalimë](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md) said she would 'prefer above all others' when [Hallacar](../H/Hallacar.md) proposed to her after wooing her under the pseudonym [Mámandil](../M/Mámandil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Ancalimë meant that she would not like to marry any man.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The elements of the name are *[ú-](Ú-.md)*, a negative prefix,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and *[nér](../N/Nér.md)* "(adult) man".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
