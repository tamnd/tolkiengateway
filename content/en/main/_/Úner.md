**Úner**, 'Noman', was the man [Ancalimë](/wiki/Tar-Ancalim%C3%AB "Tar-Ancalimë") said she would 'prefer above all others' when [Hallacar](/wiki/Hallacar) proposed to her after wooing her under the pseudonym [Mámandil](/wiki/M%C3%A1mandil).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Ancalimë meant that she would not like to marry any man.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The elements of the name are *[ú-](/wiki/%C3%9A- "Ú-")*, a negative prefix,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and *[nér](/wiki/N%C3%A9r "Nér")* "(adult) man".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
