<i><b>Палантир</b></i> (English: *The Palantír Journal*) is a Russian literary and academic journal published annually by the [St Petersburg Tolkien Society](/wiki/St_Petersburg_Tolkien_Society).

The journal was founded in [April](/wiki/April) [1997](/wiki/1997) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg" title="wikipedia:Saint Petersburg">St Petersburg</a>. It is published annually, usually twice a month in spring and autumn. Since 1997, there were 91 issues.
