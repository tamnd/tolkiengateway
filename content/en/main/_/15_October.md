Events that occurred on 15 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **1844** - Philosopher and author [Friedrich Nietzsche](/wiki/Friedrich_Nietzsche) is born.
- **[1931](/wiki/1931)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[Progress in Bimble Town](/wiki/Progress_in_Bimble_Town)" is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.
- **[1937](/wiki/1937)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 17](/wiki/Letter_17).
- **[1953](/wiki/1953)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 141a](/wiki/Letter_141a).
- **[1959](/wiki/1959)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 220](/wiki/Letter_220).
- **[1963](/wiki/1963)** – While dining, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) saves the life of an old lady who nearly choked on a whiting bone.
- **[1999](/wiki/1999)** - Author [Barbara Strachey](/wiki/Barbara_Strachey) dies in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford).
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - [Ring\*Con 2007](/wiki/Ring*Con_2007) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulda" title="wikipedia:Fulda">Fulda</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany" title="wikipedia:Germany">Germany</a>.
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** - Mereth Aderthad XV, or "EstelCon", in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardona,_Spain" title="wikipedia:Cardona, Spain">Cardona</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) is officially announced as the director of [The Hobbit film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").
- **[2012](/wiki/2012)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Riders of Rohan](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Riders_of_Rohan)* expansion is released.
- **[2023](/wiki/2023)** - [The Lord of the Rings (musical)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical)>) ends at the Watermill Theatre in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newbury,_Berkshire" title="wikipedia:Newbury, Berkshire">Newbury</a>, West Berkshire.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - With [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") severely wounded, [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") and the [four hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") travel slowly towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) hurries towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) after the [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") attacked him on [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
