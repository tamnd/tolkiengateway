[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002" title="wikipedia:2002">2002</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** - *[A Tolkien Miscellany](/wiki/A_Tolkien_Miscellany)* is published.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 43](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_43) is published.

## March {#March}

- **[6](/wiki/6_March "6 March")** - Fan site <a class="external text" href="http://www.councilofelrond.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Council of Elrond</a> goes online.
- **[24](/wiki/24_March "24 March")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* wins four Oscars, out of thirteen nominations, at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/74th_Academy_Awards" title="wikipedia:74th Academy Awards">74th Academy Awards</a>.

## May {#May}

- **[29](/wiki/29_May "29 May")** - The mailing list *[Lambengolmor](</wiki/Lambengolmor_(mailing_list)> "Lambengolmor (mailing list)")* is launched.

## June {#June}

- **[6](/wiki/6_June "6 June")** - At the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_MTV_Movie_Awards" title="wikipedia:2002 MTV Movie Awards">MTV Movie Awards</a>, *[Lord of the Piercing](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Piercing)* is aired.
- **[12](/wiki/12_June "12 June")** - *[The Art of The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Art_of_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* is published.
- **[18](/wiki/18_June "18 June")** - *[The Standard Deviants: Rings, Kings and Things](/wiki/The_Standard_Deviants:_Rings,_Kings_and_Things)* is released.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 44](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_44) is published.

## July {#July}

- **[12](/wiki/12_July "12 July")** - [Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](/wiki/Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski), scholar and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), dies.
- **[22](/wiki/22_July "22 July")** - Actor [Alexander John](/wiki/Alexander_John) dies.
- **[25](/wiki/25_July "25 July")** - *[The Uncharted Realms of Tolkien](/wiki/The_Uncharted_Realms_of_Tolkien)* is published.
- **[26](/wiki/26_July "26 July")-[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")** - [Mythcon 33](/wiki/Mythcon_33).

## August {#August}

- <b><a href="/wiki/5_August" title="5 August">5</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth_Index" title="The History of Middle-earth Index">The History of Middle-earth Index</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[8](/wiki/8_August "8 August")** - [Tolkien Thing IV](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing "Tolkien Thing").
- **[16](/wiki/16_August "16 August")** - *[The Annotated Hobbit: Revised and Expanded Edition](/wiki/The_Annotated_Hobbit:_Revised_and_Expanded_Edition)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[13](/wiki/13_September "13 September")-[15](/wiki/15_September "15 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2002](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2002).
- **[24](/wiki/24_September "24 September")** - The Xbox and GBA ports of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (video game)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)") are published in the US.
- **[26](/wiki/26_September "26 September")** - [I.L. Gordon](/wiki/I.L._Gordon) dies.

## October {#October}

- **[5](/wiki/5_October "5 October")** - The [Ellinikós Sýllogos Philon Tólkin](/wiki/Ellinik%C3%B3s_S%C3%BDllogos_Philon_T%C3%B3lkin) is founded.
- **[16](/wiki/16_October "16 October")** - The PS2 port of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (video game)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)") is published in the US.
- **[22](/wiki/22_October "22 October")** - The PC port of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (video game)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)") is published in the US.

## November {#November}

- **[2](/wiki/2_November "2 November")** - The symposium "J.R.R. Tolkien: The Man Behind The Lord of the Rings" is held at the <a class="external text" href="http://acmrs.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Arizona Center for Medieval and Renaissance Studies</a>. [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey), [Randel Helms](/wiki/Randel_Helms) and Ron Newcomer speak.
- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Making of the Movie Trilogy](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Making_of_the_Movie_Trilogy)* is published.
- **[12](/wiki/12_November "12 November")** - [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* Extended Edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)") is released on DVD.
- **[13](/wiki/13_November "13 November")** - The *South Park* episode "[The Return of the Fellowship of the Ring to the Two Towers](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_to_the_Two_Towers)" is televised.
- **[29](/wiki/29_November "29 November")** - [John Justin](/wiki/John_Justin), who played [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) in [*The Hobbit* (1968 radio series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)"), dies.
- **?** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien's Sanctifying Myth: Understanding Middle-earth](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_Sanctifying_Myth "J.R.R. Tolkien's Sanctifying Myth")* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[5](/wiki/5_December "5 December")** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)** premieres at Ziegfeld Theatre, in New York City.
- **[6](/wiki/6_December "6 December")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers Photo Guide](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_Photo_Guide)* is published.
- **[10](/wiki/10_December "10 December")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)* is released.
- **[18](/wiki/18_December "18 December")** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)** is released in USA.
- **[19](/wiki/19_December "19 December")** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)** is released in New Zealand.
- **?** - [Dragon Flame](/wiki/Dragon_Flame) is released.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[At Dawn in Rivendell](/wiki/At_Dawn_in_Rivendell)*, the third album of the [Tolkien Ensemble](/wiki/Tolkien_Ensemble), is published.
- **?** - The [audiobook](/wiki/Audiobooks "Audiobooks") of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, read by [Rob Inglis](/wiki/Rob_Inglis), is published.
