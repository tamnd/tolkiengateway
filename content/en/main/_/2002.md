[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002" title="wikipedia:2002">2002</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](1_January.md)** - *[A Tolkien Miscellany](../A/A_Tolkien_Miscellany.md)* is published.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 43](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_43.md) is published.

## March {#March}

- **[6](6_March.md)** - Fan site <a class="external text" href="http://www.councilofelrond.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Council of Elrond</a> goes online.
- **[24](24_March.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* wins four Oscars, out of thirteen nominations, at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/74th_Academy_Awards" title="wikipedia:74th Academy Awards">74th Academy Awards</a>.

## May {#May}

- **[29](29_May.md)** - The mailing list *[Lambengolmor](<../L/Lambengolmor_(mailing_list).md>)* is launched.

## June {#June}

- **[6](6_June.md)** - At the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_MTV_Movie_Awards" title="wikipedia:2002 MTV Movie Awards">MTV Movie Awards</a>, *[Lord of the Piercing](../L/Lord_of_the_Piercing.md)* is aired.
- **[12](12_June.md)** - *[The Art of The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Art_of_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* is published.
- **[18](18_June.md)** - *[The Standard Deviants: Rings, Kings and Things](../T/The_Standard_Deviants%253A_Rings,_Kings_and_Things.md)* is released.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 44](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_44.md) is published.

## July {#July}

- **[12](12_July.md)** - [Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](../P/Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski.md), scholar and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), dies.
- **[22](22_July.md)** - Actor [Alexander John](../A/Alexander_John.md) dies.
- **[25](25_July.md)** - *[The Uncharted Realms of Tolkien](../T/The_Uncharted_Realms_of_Tolkien.md)* is published.
- **[26](26_July.md)-[29](29_July.md)** - [Mythcon 33](../M/Mythcon_33.md).

## August {#August}

- <b><a href="5_August.md" title="5 August">5</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth_Index.md" title="The History of Middle-earth Index">The History of Middle-earth Index</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[8](8_August.md)** - [Tolkien Thing IV](../T/Tolkien_Thing.md).
- **[16](16_August.md)** - *[The Annotated Hobbit: Revised and Expanded Edition](../T/The_Annotated_Hobbit%253A_Revised_and_Expanded_Edition.md)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[13](13_September.md)-[15](15_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2002](../O/Oxonmoot_2002.md).
- **[24](24_September.md)** - The Xbox and GBA ports of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (video game)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game).md>) are published in the US.
- **[26](26_September.md)** - [I.L. Gordon](../I/I.L._Gordon.md) dies.

## October {#October}

- **[5](5_October.md)** - The [Ellinikós Sýllogos Philon Tólkin](../E/Ellinikós_Sýllogos_Philon_Tólkin.md) is founded.
- **[16](16_October.md)** - The PS2 port of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (video game)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game).md>) is published in the US.
- **[22](22_October.md)** - The PC port of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (video game)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game).md>) is published in the US.

## November {#November}

- **[2](2_November.md)** - The symposium "J.R.R. Tolkien: The Man Behind The Lord of the Rings" is held at the <a class="external text" href="http://acmrs.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Arizona Center for Medieval and Renaissance Studies</a>. [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md), [Randel Helms](../R/Randel_Helms.md) and Ron Newcomer speak.
- **[6](6_November.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Making of the Movie Trilogy](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Making_of_the_Movie_Trilogy.md)* is published.
- **[12](12_November.md)** - [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* Extended Edition](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition).md>) is released on DVD.
- **[13](13_November.md)** - The *South Park* episode "[The Return of the Fellowship of the Ring to the Two Towers](../T/The_Return_of_the_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_to_the_Two_Towers.md)" is televised.
- **[29](29_November.md)** - [John Justin](../J/John_Justin.md), who played [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) in [*The Hobbit* (1968 radio series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>), dies.
- **?** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien's Sanctifying Myth: Understanding Middle-earth](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_Sanctifying_Myth.md)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[5](5_December.md)** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)** premieres at Ziegfeld Theatre, in New York City.
- **[6](6_December.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers Photo Guide](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_Photo_Guide.md)* is published.
- **[10](10_December.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack.md)* is released.
- **[18](18_December.md)** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)** is released in USA.
- **[19](19_December.md)** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)** is released in New Zealand.
- **?** - [Dragon Flame](../D/Dragon_Flame.md) is released.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[At Dawn in Rivendell](../A/At_Dawn_in_Rivendell.md)*, the third album of the [Tolkien Ensemble](../T/Tolkien_Ensemble.md), is published.
- **?** - The [audiobook](../A/Audiobooks.md) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, read by [Rob Inglis](../R/Rob_Inglis.md), is published.
