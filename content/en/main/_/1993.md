## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - The [Danish Tolkien Society, Imladris](/wiki/Imladris,_Danmarks_Tolkienforening "Imladris, Danmarks Tolkienforening") is founded.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 27](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_27) is published.

## March {#March}

- **[29](/wiki/29_March "29 March")** - [Hobitit](/wiki/Hobitit) is aired on YLE.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 28](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_28) is published.

## April {#April}

- **?** - [Other Hands 1](/wiki/Other_Hands_1) is published.

## May {#May}

- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 29](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_29) is published.

## July {#July}

- **[30](/wiki/30_July "30 July")** - [Mythcon 24](/wiki/Mythcon_24) begins.
- **[31](/wiki/31_July "31 July")** - [Roland Adlerberth](/wiki/Roland_Adlerberth) dies.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 30](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_30) is published.

## August {#August}

- **[2](/wiki/2_August "2 August")** - [Mythcon 24](/wiki/Mythcon_24) ends.

## September {#September}

- **[17](/wiki/17_September "17 September")-[19](/wiki/19_September "19 September")** - [Oxonmoot 1993](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1993).
- <b><a href="/wiki/23_September" title="23 September">23</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/Morgoth%27s_Ring" title="Morgoth&#39;s Ring">Morgoth&#39;s Ring</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 30](/wiki/Mallorn_30 "Mallorn 30") is published.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 31](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_31) is published.

## November {#November}

- **[15](/wiki/15_November "15 November")** - *[Poems by J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Poems_by_J.R.R._Tolkien:_Volume_I-III "Poems by J.R.R. Tolkien: Volume I-III")* is published.
- **[27](/wiki/27_November "27 November")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Magyar_Tolkien_Tarsasag&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Magyar Tolkien Tarsasag (page does not exist)">Magyar Tolkien Tarsasag</a></i>, the Hungarian [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies"), is founded.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 32](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_32) is published.

## December {#December}

- **[14](/wiki/14_December "14 December")** - *[Morgoth's Ring](/wiki/Morgoth%27s_Ring)* is published in the US.
