[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1941" title="wikipedia:1941">1941</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](1_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) resigns as Governor of [King Edward's School](../K/King_Edward's_School.md), [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md).
- **[2](2_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 41](../L/Letter_41.md).
- **[12](12_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 42](../L/Letter_42.md).
- **[26](26_January.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 42a](../L/Letter_42a.md).

## February {#February}

- **[1](1_February.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 42b](../L/Letter_42b.md).

## March {#March}

- **[6](6_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) starts writing [letter 43](../L/Letter_43.md).
- **[12](12_March.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 43a](../L/Letter_43a.md).
- **[18](18_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 44](../L/Letter_44.md).

## April {#April}

- **[30](30_April.md)** - [Sean Arnold](../S/Sean_Arnold.md), who would play [Odo Proudfoot](../O/Odo_Proudfoot.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1981 radio series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), is born.

## June {#June}

- **[9](9_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 45](../L/Letter_45.md).

## August {#August}

- **[4](4_August.md)** - [Martin Jarvis](../M/Martin_Jarvis.md), who would voice [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: Conquest](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Conquest.md)*, is born.

## October {#October}

- **[1](1_October.md)** - Author [Glen GoodKnight](../G/Glen_GoodKnight.md) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[18](18_November.md)** - [David Hemmings](../D/David_Hemmings.md), who would play [Bergil](../B/Bergil.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1955 radio series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>), is born.
- **[26](26_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 46](../L/Letter_46.md).

## December {#December}

- **[10](10_December.md)** - [Baillie Tolkien](../B/Baillie_Tolkien.md) is born.
- **[22](22_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[24](24_December.md)** - Father Christmas writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Ian Mune](../I/Ian_Mune.md), who played a [bounder](../S/Shirriffs.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, is born.
