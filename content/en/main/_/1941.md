[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1941" title="wikipedia:1941">1941</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) resigns as Governor of [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School), [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham).
- **[2](/wiki/2_January "2 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 41](/wiki/Letter_41 "Letter 41").
- **[12](/wiki/12_January "12 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 42](/wiki/Letter_42 "Letter 42").
- **[26](/wiki/26_January "26 January")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 42a](/wiki/Letter_42a).

## February {#February}

- **[1](/wiki/1_February "1 February")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 42b](/wiki/Letter_42b).

## March {#March}

- **[6](/wiki/6_March "6 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) starts writing [letter 43](/wiki/Letter_43 "Letter 43").
- **[12](/wiki/12_March "12 March")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 43a](/wiki/Letter_43a).
- **[18](/wiki/18_March "18 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 44](/wiki/Letter_44 "Letter 44").

## April {#April}

- **[30](/wiki/30_April "30 April")** - [Sean Arnold](/wiki/Sean_Arnold), who would play [Odo Proudfoot](/wiki/Odo_Proudfoot) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1981 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born.

## June {#June}

- **[9](/wiki/9_June "9 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 45](/wiki/Letter_45 "Letter 45").

## August {#August}

- **[4](/wiki/4_August "4 August")** - [Martin Jarvis](/wiki/Martin_Jarvis), who would voice [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in *[The Lord of the Rings: Conquest](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Conquest)*, is born.

## October {#October}

- **[1](/wiki/1_October "1 October")** - Author [Glen GoodKnight](/wiki/Glen_GoodKnight) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[18](/wiki/18_November "18 November")** - [David Hemmings](/wiki/David_Hemmings), who would play [Bergil](/wiki/Bergil) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1955 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)"), is born.
- **[26](/wiki/26_November "26 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 46](/wiki/Letter_46 "Letter 46").

## December {#December}

- **[10](/wiki/10_December "10 December")** - [Baillie Tolkien](/wiki/Baillie_Tolkien) is born.
- **[22](/wiki/22_December "22 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **[24](/wiki/24_December "24 December")** - Father Christmas writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Ian Mune](/wiki/Ian_Mune), who played a [bounder](/wiki/Shirriffs "Shirriffs") in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, is born.

## Note {#Note}

While writing *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, Tolkien modelled the [lunar](/wiki/Moon "Moon") phases of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") on those of this year, with an offset of +5/6 days.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
