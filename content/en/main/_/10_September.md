Events that occurred on 10 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1950](1950.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) returns from a stay at [his son](../M/Michael_Tolkien.md)'s cottage where he made some final revisions to *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* (see [Letter 134](../L/Letter_134.md)).
- **[1950](1950.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 129](../L/Letter_129.md).
- **[1951](1951.md)** - First day of the Congrès Internationale de Philologie Moderne at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Li%C3%A8ge" title="wikipedia:University of Liège">University of Liège</a>, a conference in which [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) delivers the paper "[Middle English 'Losenger'](../M/Middle_English_'Losenger'.md)".
- **[1957](1957.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wins the [International Fantasy Award](../T/The_Bookseller,_14_September_1957.md).
- **[1959](1959.md)** - Actor [Jim Meskimen](../J/Jim_Meskimen.md) is born.
- **[1964](1964.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 263](../L/Letter_263.md).
- **[2007](2007.md)** - *["from mind to mind": Robert Browning and J.R.R. Tolkien](../F/From_Mind_to_Mind.md)* is published.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - Bulgarian Tolkien Society's 6th Tolkien-Fest in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia" title="wikipedia:Sofia">Sofia</a>.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)* goes free-to-play for the general public in North America.
- **[2015](2015.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2015](../O/Oxonmoot_2015.md) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2016](2016.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2016](../O/Oxonmoot_2016.md) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) is held captive by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
