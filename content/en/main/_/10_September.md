Events that occurred on 10 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1950](/wiki/1950)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) returns from a stay at [his son](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien "Michael Tolkien")'s cottage where he made some final revisions to *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* (see [Letter 134](/wiki/Letter_134)).
- **[1950](/wiki/1950)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 129](/wiki/Letter_129).
- **[1951](/wiki/1951)** - First day of the Congrès Internationale de Philologie Moderne at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Li%C3%A8ge" title="wikipedia:University of Liège">University of Liège</a>, a conference in which [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) delivers the paper "[Middle English 'Losenger'](/wiki/Middle_English_%27Losenger%27)".
- **[1957](/wiki/1957)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wins the [International Fantasy Award](/wiki/The_Bookseller,_14_September_1957 "The Bookseller, 14 September 1957").
- **[1959](/wiki/1959)** - Actor [Jim Meskimen](/wiki/Jim_Meskimen) is born.
- **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 263](/wiki/Letter_263).
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - *["from mind to mind": Robert Browning and J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/From_Mind_to_Mind "From Mind to Mind")* is published.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - Bulgarian Tolkien Society's 6th Tolkien-Fest in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia" title="wikipedia:Sofia">Sofia</a>.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)* goes free-to-play for the general public in North America.
- **[2015](/wiki/2015)** - [Oxonmoot 2015](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2015) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2016](/wiki/2016)** - [Oxonmoot 2016](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2016) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) is held captive by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
