[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1909" title="wikipedia:1909">1909</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[29](29_January.md)** - [Richard E. Blackwelder](../R/Richard_E._Blackwelder.md), Tolkien collector, is born.

## June {#June}

- **[10](10_June.md)** - Around this date [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) composes the [Book of the Foxrook](../B/Book_of_the_Foxrook.md).

## August {#August}

- **[20](20_August.md)** - [André Morell](../A/André_Morell.md), who would play [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>), is born.

## Autumn {#Autumn}

- [Father Morgan](../F/Francis_Xavier_Morgan.md) discovers the romance between [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [Edith Bratt](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md), which he fears may lead to early marriage and ruin Tolkien's career prospects.
- Tolkien fails to win an [Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md) scholarship.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Elaine Griffiths](../E/Elaine_Griffiths.md), friend and pupil of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is born.
