[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1909" title="wikipedia:1909">1909</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[29](/wiki/29_January "29 January")** - [Richard E. Blackwelder](/wiki/Richard_E._Blackwelder), Tolkien collector, is born.

## June {#June}

- **[10](/wiki/10_June "10 June")** - Around this date [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) composes the [Book of the Foxrook](/wiki/Book_of_the_Foxrook).

## August {#August}

- **[20](/wiki/20_August "20 August")** - [André Morell](/wiki/Andr%C3%A9_Morell), who would play [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"), is born.

## Autumn {#Autumn}

- [Father Morgan](/wiki/Francis_Xavier_Morgan "Francis Xavier Morgan") discovers the romance between [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [Edith Bratt](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien"), which he fears may lead to early marriage and ruin Tolkien's career prospects.
- Tolkien fails to win an [Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford") scholarship.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Elaine Griffiths](/wiki/Elaine_Griffiths), friend and pupil of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is born.
