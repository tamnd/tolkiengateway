Events that occurred on 11 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1901](/wiki/1901)** - [Dorothy Whitelock](/wiki/Dorothy_Whitelock), later colleague of Tolkien, is born.
- **[1906](/wiki/1906)** - Actor and comedian [Theodore Isidore Gottlieb](/wiki/Theodore_Isidore_Gottlieb) is born.
- **[1918](/wiki/1918)** - [The War](/wiki/World_War_I "World War I") comes to an end in a cease-fire.
- **[1919](/wiki/1919)** - The Ministry of Pensions sends a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), on which he wrote a draft of *[The Ruined Enchanter: A Fairy Ballad](/wiki/The_Ruined_Enchanter "The Ruined Enchanter")*.
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - Actor [Martyn Read](/wiki/Martyn_Read) is born.
- <b><a href="/wiki/1954" title="1954">1954</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Two_Towers" title="The Two Towers">The Two Towers</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[1956](/wiki/1956)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mr Britten](</wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Britten_(11_November_1956)> "Letter to Mr Britten (11 November 1956)").
- **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 265](/wiki/Letter_265).
- **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Clyde S. Kilby](</wiki/Letter_to_Clyde_S._Kilby_(11_November_1964)> "Letter to Clyde S. Kilby (11 November 1964)").
- **[1993](/wiki/1993)** - *[Poems by J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Poems_by_J.R.R._Tolkien:_Volume_I-III "Poems by J.R.R. Tolkien: Volume I-III")* is published.
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - *[The Hobbit (2003 video game)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)>)* is released in the USA.
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - [Ring\*Con 2006](/wiki/Ring*Con_2006) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulda" title="wikipedia:Fulda">Fulda</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany" title="wikipedia:Germany">Germany</a>.
- **[2011](/wiki/2011)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North)* is released in Spain.
- **[2014](/wiki/2014)** - The half-hour documentary *[Tolkien's Great War](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Great_War)* by Elliot and Zander Weaver is released.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[2026](/wiki/2026)** - Season 3 of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* will release.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2994](/wiki/Third_Age_2994 "Third Age 2994"):
    - A Dwarf (perhaps [Ori](/wiki/Ori)) writes in the [Book of Mazarbul](/wiki/Book_of_Mazarbul) about [Balin](/wiki/Balin)'s death the previous day.
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") rest in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").
    - Full moon<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
