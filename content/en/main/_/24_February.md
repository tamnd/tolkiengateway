    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) carry the boats through a passage above [Sarn Gebir](../S/Sarn_Gebir.md) and continue rowing after Sarn Gebir.
    - [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md) sees [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) wandering in [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md).
