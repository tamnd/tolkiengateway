Events that occurred on 1 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1840](/wiki/1840)** - [George Tolkien](/wiki/George_Tolkien) dies.
- **[1914](/wiki/1914)** - [George Sayer](/wiki/George_Sayer), friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is born.
- **[1917](/wiki/1917)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is considered fit for general service again.
- **[1917](/wiki/1917)** - [Edith Tolkien](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien) writes her final letter to her husband at Thirtle Bridge army camp, near Withernsea from her lodgings in Hornsea.
- **[1946](/wiki/1946)** - Actor [Brian Cox](/wiki/Brian_Cox) is born.
- **[1961](/wiki/1961)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Jan Broberg](/wiki/Letter_to_Jan_Broberg "Letter to Jan Broberg").
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Royston F. Porthouse](/wiki/Letter_to_Royston_F._Porthouse "Letter to Royston F. Porthouse").
- **[1984](/wiki/1984)** - *[Moria: The Dwarven City](/wiki/Moria:_The_Dwarven_City)* is published.
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - *[Tolkien and the Great War](/wiki/Tolkien_and_the_Great_War)* is released in paperback in the US.
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 3](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_3)* is published.
- <b><a href="/wiki/2017" title="2017">2017</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/Beren_and_L%C3%BAthien" title="Beren and Lúthien">Beren and Lúthien</a></i> is published in the UK and Europe.</b>
- <b><a href="/wiki/2018" title="2018">2018</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/Tolkien:_Maker_of_Middle-earth" title="Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth">Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth</a></i></b> and *[Tolkien: Treasures](/wiki/Tolkien:_Treasures)* are published, accompanying the exhibition "Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth" by [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library).
- **[2025](/wiki/2025)** - [Shaun Gunner](/wiki/Shaun_Gunner) is appointed Chief Executive Officer of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society), having previously served over 12 years as Chair.<sup id="cite_ref-CEO_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CEO-1">[1]</a></sup>
