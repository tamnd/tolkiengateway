Events that occurred on 8 January.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1913](1913.md)** - Engagement of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [Edith Bratt](../E/Edith_Bratt.md)
- **[1938](1938.md)** - Voice actor [Michael Graham Cox](../M/Michael_Graham_Cox.md) is born.
- **[1944](1944.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 54](../L/Letter_54.md).
- **[1968](1968.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien reads over *[The New Shadow](../T/The_New_Shadow.md)* and most likely starts typing a final typescript.
- **[1971](1971.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 319](../L/Letter_319.md).
- **[2004](2004.md)** - *[The Return of the King](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(video_game).md>)* video game is released in Japan.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) reach [Hollin](../E/Eregion.md). *[Crebain](../C/Crebain.md)* appear.
