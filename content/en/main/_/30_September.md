## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1923](/wiki/1923)** - Musician [Donald Swann](/wiki/Donald_Swann) is born.
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 82](/wiki/Letter_82).
- **[1946](/wiki/1946)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 106](/wiki/Letter_106).
- **[1947](/wiki/1947)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 111a](/wiki/Letter_111a).
- **[1947](/wiki/1947)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Rosemary](</wiki/Letter_to_Rosemary_(30_September_1947)> "Letter to Rosemary (30 September 1947)").
- **[1955](/wiki/1955)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 170](/wiki/Letter_170).
- **[1963](/wiki/1963)** - Music producer [Carvin Knowles](/wiki/Carvin_Knowles) is born.
- **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - Actor [Bernard Rebel](/wiki/Bernard_Rebel) dies.
- **[1979](/wiki/1979)** - [Oxonmoot 1979](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1979) at the Eastgate Hotel.
- **[1997](/wiki/1997)** - The album *[An Evening in Rivendell](/wiki/An_Evening_in_Rivendell)* is released.
- **[2000](/wiki/2000)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien and His Literary Resonances](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_and_His_Literary_Resonances)* is published.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - Jubilee for [Mithlond](/wiki/Mithlond,_G%C3%B6teborgs_Tolkiens%C3%A4llskap "Mithlond, Göteborgs Tolkiensällskap"), the Gothenburg Tolkien Society of Sweden.
- **[2014](/wiki/2014)** - *[Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor)* is released in North America.
- **[2017](/wiki/2017)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Romanticist and Poet](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Romanticist_and_Poet)* is published.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[2022](/wiki/2022)** - The episode [Udûn](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(episode)> "Udûn (episode)") of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* is released online.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow) is raided by three [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") in the early hours and [Fatty Bolger](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger "Fredegar Bolger") alerts the village. The Black Riders run away.
    - The [Inn](/wiki/Prancing_Pony "Prancing Pony") at [Bree](/wiki/Bree) is simultaneously raided but the Black Riders find only pillows in the Hobbits' beds. The ponies are lost.
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and his companions buy [Bill the Pony](/wiki/Bill_the_Pony) from [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny) and leave Bree together with [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn").
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) comes to [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow) and sees Frodo's cloak outside. Losing hope, he reaches Bree at night. He meets [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur) who apologises and tells him the Hobbits left with Strider.
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
