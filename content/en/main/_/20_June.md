Events that occurred on 20 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1934](1934.md)** – Artist [Heinz Edelmann](../H/Heinz_Edelmann.md) is born.
- **[2012](2012.md)** – *[Wagner and Tolkien: Mythmakers](../W/Wagner_and_Tolkien.md)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) [attacks](../S/Sauron's_attack_on_Osgiliath.md) [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md). [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) and [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) defend the bridge till its destruction.
    - About the same time [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) is attacked and [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) escapes. The [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) begins.
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - New Moon<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
