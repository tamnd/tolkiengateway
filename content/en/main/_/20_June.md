Events that occurred on 20 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1934](/wiki/1934)** – Artist [Heinz Edelmann](/wiki/Heinz_Edelmann) is born.
- **[2012](/wiki/2012)** – *[Wagner and Tolkien: Mythmakers](/wiki/Wagner_and_Tolkien "Wagner and Tolkien")* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) [attacks](/wiki/Sauron%27s_attack_on_Osgiliath "Sauron's attack on Osgiliath") [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath). [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) and [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) defend the bridge till its destruction.
    - About the same time [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) is attacked and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) escapes. The [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) begins.
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
