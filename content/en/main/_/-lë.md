<i><b>-lë</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) suffix that has four meanings.

## -lë<sup>1</sup> {#-lë1}

Typically used to derive verbal nouns.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- **hortalë** = "speeding, urging" (**horta-** = "to speed, urge"; <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=KHOR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KHOR (page does not exist)">KHOR</a>)
- **intyalë** = "imagination"; lit. "guessing, supposing" (**intya-** = "to guess, suppose"; <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=INK&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="INK (page does not exist)">INK</a>)
- **vestalë** = "wedding" (**vesta-** = "to wed"; <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=BES&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="BES (page does not exist)">BES</a>)

## -lë<sup>2</sup> {#-lë2}

Some verbal nouns may be formed directly from the stem when it ends in a vowel.

### Examples {#Examples_2}

- **tailë** = "lengthening" ("to extent, make longer"; <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=TAY&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TAY (page does not exist)">TAY</a> or <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=TAI&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TAI (page does not exist)">TAI</a>)
- **cuilë** = "life, being alive" ("come to life"; <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=KUY&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KUY (page does not exist)">KUY</a>)

## -lë<sup>3</sup> {#-lë3}

In the case of basic stems ending in a consonant, the ending -lë may be added to a nasal-infixed form of them.

### Examples {#Examples_3}

- **mancalë** = "commerce" (**manca-** = "to trade"; derived from <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=MBAKH&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MBAKH (page does not exist)">MBAKH</a> "exchange")
- **quentalë** = "account, history" ("speak"; [KWET](../K/KWET.md))

## -lë<sup>4</sup> {#-lë4}

The ending -lë is also used to derive concrete nouns from an adjective.

### Examples {#Examples_4}

- **oialë** = "everlasting \[?age\]" (**oia** = "everlasting"; <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=OY&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="OY (page does not exist)">OY</a>?)
- **aicalë** = "a peak" (**aica** = "sharp"; [AYAK](../A/AYAK.md))
- **meryalë** = "holiday" (**merya** = "festive"; <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=MBER&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MBER (page does not exist)">MBER</a>)
