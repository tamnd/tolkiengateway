Events that occurred on 2 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1916](/wiki/1916)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 4](/wiki/Letter_4).
- **[1935](/wiki/1935)** - British actor [Stephen Thorne](/wiki/Stephen_Thorne) is born.
- **[1955](/wiki/1955)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Derrick Parnum](/wiki/Letter_to_Derrick_Parnum "Letter to Derrick Parnum").
- **[1956](/wiki/1956)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) examines [Brian Miller](/wiki/Brian_Miller)'s thesis on the [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) *Dame Sirith*.
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is interviewed for the article "[The Hobbit-forming World of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Hobbit-forming_World_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)".
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II)* is released on PC and Xbox 360.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) come to the end of the [Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes).
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) comes to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) and heals [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden). [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) is released and Théoden holds [his sword](/wiki/Herugrim "Herugrim") again.
    - [Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma") is expelled and later seen leading Orcs northward.
    - Gandalf is named lord of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) and [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax) passes officially to him. The [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) ride west against [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman).
    - [Second Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen), [Erkenbrand](/wiki/Erkenbrand) is defeated.
    - [Entmoot](/wiki/Entmoot) ends in afternoon.
    - The [Ents](/wiki/Ents) march on [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) and reach it at night.
