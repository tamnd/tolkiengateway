Events that occurred on 2 January

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1917](/wiki/1917)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to the War Office](/wiki/Letter_to_the_War_Office "Letter to the War Office").
- **[1929](/wiki/1929)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to E.V. Gordon](</wiki/Letter_to_E.V._Gordon_(2_January_1929)> "Letter to E.V. Gordon (2 January 1929)").
- **[1941](/wiki/1941)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 41](/wiki/Letter_41).
- **[1965](/wiki/1965)** - The thirteenth episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[1967](/wiki/1967)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Otto B. Lindhardt](/wiki/Letter_to_Otto_B._Lindhardt "Letter to Otto B. Lindhardt").
- **[1967](/wiki/1967)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Peyton Moss](/wiki/Letter_to_Peyton_Moss "Letter to Peyton Moss").
- **[1969](/wiki/1969)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 308](/wiki/Letter_308).
- **[1969](/wiki/1969)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 309](/wiki/Letter_309).
- **[1972](/wiki/1972)** – In honour of his 80th birthday the following day, [*The Sunday Times Magazine*](</wiki/The_Sunday_Times_Magazine_(2_January_1972)> "The Sunday Times Magazine (2 January 1972)") runs an interview of [William Cater](/wiki/William_Cater) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), "The Lord of the Legends".
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - *[The Art of The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Art_of_The_Return_of_the_King)* is published.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Complete Visual Companion](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Complete_Visual_Companion)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- Conclusion of the six-day [Yuletide](/wiki/Yuletide) festival in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) travels south along the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).
