Events that occurred on 17 July.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1912](/wiki/1912)** - Author [Barbara Strachey](/wiki/Barbara_Strachey) is born.
- **[1918](/wiki/1918)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) attends a medical board examination in Kingston-upon-Hull.
- **[1971](/wiki/1971)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 325](/wiki/Letter_325).
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - *[The Tolkien Quiz Book](</wiki/The_Tolkien_Quiz_Book_(by_Robinson_and_Wilson)> "The Tolkien Quiz Book (by Robinson and Wilson)")*, the book by Robinson and Wilson, is published.
- **[1982](/wiki/1982)** - The first episode of the new version of the [1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[1985](/wiki/1985)** - [Robert Havard](/wiki/Robert_Havard), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), dies.
- **[1998](/wiki/1998)** - Author [Paul H. Kocher](/wiki/Paul_H._Kocher) dies.
- **[1998](/wiki/1998)** - [Mythcon 29](/wiki/Mythcon_29) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheaton,_Illinois" title="wikipedia:Wheaton, Illinois">Wheaton, Illinois</a>.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - [The Lord of the Rings Symphony](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle" title="wikipedia:Seattle">Seattle</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_(state)" title="wikipedia:Washington (state)">Washington</a>.
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** - [Mythcon 40](/wiki/Mythcon_40) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles" title="wikipedia:Los Angeles">Los Angeles</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - [Tolkien Thing](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing) 2010 in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Hersfeld" title="wikipedia:Bad Hersfeld">Bad Hersfeld</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany" title="wikipedia:Germany">Germany</a>.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - [Mythcon 41](/wiki/Mythcon_41) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas" title="wikipedia:Dallas">Dallas</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas" title="wikipedia:Texas">Texas</a>.
- **[2011](/wiki/2011)** - [Tolkien Thing](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing) 2011 in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breuberg" title="wikipedia:Breuberg">Breuberg</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany" title="wikipedia:Germany">Germany</a>.
- **[2011](/wiki/2011)** - [Mythcon 42](/wiki/Mythcon_42) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albuquerque,_New_Mexico" title="wikipedia:Albuquerque, New Mexico">Albuquerque, New Mexico</a>.
