Events that occurred on 11 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1913](/wiki/1913)** - Actor [John Bryning](/wiki/John_Bryning) is born.
- **[1918](/wiki/1918)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is officially released from Brooklands Officers' Hospital, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kingston-upon-Hull&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kingston-upon-Hull (page does not exist)">Kingston-upon-Hull</a>.
- **[1945](/wiki/1945)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 103](/wiki/Letter_103).
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 289b](/wiki/Letter_289b).
- **[1979](/wiki/1979)** - The *[Jackanory](/wiki/Jackanory)* episode "The Clouds Burst" is aired on the [BBC](/wiki/BBC).
- **[1996](/wiki/1996)** - *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va)* issue [9](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va_9 "Tyalië Tyelelliéva 9") is published.
- **[1999](/wiki/1999)** - <b>Principal photography for <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a> begins.</b>
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - Chilean Tolkien Society's Mereth Oronnad.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - At [Vinyamar](/wiki/Vinyamar), [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) speaks to [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and instructs him to go to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) to deliver a message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - With [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") severely wounded, [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") and the [four hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") travel slowly towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).
    - [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) drives three [Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") off the [Last Bridge](/wiki/Last_Bridge) of [Mitheithel](/wiki/Mitheithel) and pursues them westward. He meets two others who flee southward. He leaves a beryl on the Bridge. He starts looking for the travellers.
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) hurries towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) after the [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") attacked him on [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
