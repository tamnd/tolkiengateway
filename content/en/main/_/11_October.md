Events that occurred on 11 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1913](1913.md)** - Actor [John Bryning](../J/John_Bryning.md) is born.
- **[1918](1918.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is officially released from Brooklands Officers' Hospital, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kingston-upon-Hull&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kingston-upon-Hull (page does not exist)">Kingston-upon-Hull</a>.
- **[1945](1945.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 103](../L/Letter_103.md).
- **[1966](1966.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 289b](../L/Letter_289b.md).
- **[1979](1979.md)** - The *[Jackanory](../J/Jackanory.md)* episode "The Clouds Burst" is aired on the [BBC](../B/BBC.md).
- **[1996](1996.md)** - *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](../T/Tyalië_Tyelelliéva.md)* issue [9](../T/Tyalië_Tyelelliéva_9.md) is published.
- **[1999](1999.md)** - <b>Principal photography for <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a> begins.</b>
- **[2010](2010.md)** - Chilean Tolkien Society's Mereth Oronnad.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md):
    - At [Vinyamar](../V/Vinyamar.md), [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) speaks to [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) and instructs him to go to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) to deliver a message to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - With [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) severely wounded, [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) and the [four hobbits](../T/Travellers.md) travel slowly towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).
    - [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) drives three [Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) off the [Last Bridge](../L/Last_Bridge.md) of [Mitheithel](../M/Mitheithel.md) and pursues them westward. He meets two others who flee southward. He leaves a beryl on the Bridge. He starts looking for the travellers.
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) hurries towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) after the [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) attacked him on [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
