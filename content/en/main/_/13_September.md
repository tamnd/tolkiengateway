Events that occurred on 13 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1943](/wiki/1943)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 49d](/wiki/Letter_49d).
- **[1951](/wiki/1951)** - Final day of the <a class="external text" href="https://donum.uliege.be/bitstream/2268.1/14083/1/PHL_129_Essais.pdf" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Congrès Internationale de Philologie Moderne</a> at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Li%C3%A8ge" title="wikipedia:University of Liège">University of Liège</a>, a conference in which [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) delivers the paper "[Middle English 'Losenger'](/wiki/Middle_English_%27Losenger%27)".
- **[1951](/wiki/1951)** - Actor [Bruce Phillips](/wiki/Bruce_Phillips) is born.
- **[1974](/wiki/1974)** - The first [Oxonmoot](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1974 "Oxonmoot 1974") is held at The Welsh Poney, [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford).
- **[1992](/wiki/1992)** - The second part of the [1992 radio series](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)") of *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)* is aired on [BBC Radio 5](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[1996](/wiki/1996)** - Actor [Sadwyn Brophy](/wiki/Sadwyn_Brophy) is born.
- **[2002](/wiki/2002)** - [Oxonmoot 2002](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2002) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Antony%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Antony&#39;s College, Oxford">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2014](/wiki/2014)** - [Oxonmoot 2014](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2014) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Margaret_Hall,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Lady Margaret Hall, Oxford">Lady Margaret Hall</a>.
- **[2015](/wiki/2015)** - [Oxonmoot 2015](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2015) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2019](/wiki/2019)** - [Oxonmoot 2019](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2019) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) is held captive by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) depart for [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) by way of the [Redhorn Pass](/wiki/Redhorn_Pass); the rest of the escort set out for [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
