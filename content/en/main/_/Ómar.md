**Ómar**, also called **Amillo**, was a [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

He was considered to be the youngest of all the Valar, and was the last of them to come into the world, singing songs as he entered.<sup id="cite_ref-III_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-III-2">[2]</a></sup> Ómar knew all [languages](/wiki/Languages "Languages") and all songs.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In [Valmar](/wiki/Valmar) he would sing to the harp-playing of his brother, [Salmar](/wiki/Salmar), and his voice was the best of all voices;<sup id="cite_ref-Valmar_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valmar-4">[4]</a></sup> other times, he would be trilling in the gardens of [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), where [Nielíqui](/wiki/Niel%C3%ADqui) Oromë's daughter danced about its woods.<sup id="cite_ref-Valmar_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valmar-4">[4]</a></sup>

From Ómar and [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) the [Teleri](/wiki/Vanyar "Vanyar")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> learned much of poetry and song.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><b>Ómar</b></i> and <i><b>Amillo</b></i> are [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) names. The former is related to Qenya *ōma* "voice",<sup id="cite_ref-Pei_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pei-7">[6]</a></sup> while the latter is glossed as "one of the Happy Folk; Hilary", where *Hilary* derives from Latin *hilaris* "cheerful".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) equivalents of his names are <i><b>Ûmor</b></i> and <i><b>Gamlos</b></i> (likely meaning "one who shouts with joy").<sup id="cite_ref-Pei_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pei-7">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early, rejected entry in *[Qenyaqetsa](/wiki/Qenyaqetsa)*, it is said that Amillo, together with his brother [Noldorin](/wiki/Salmar "Salmar") and the Vala [Erinti](/wiki/Erinti) left [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) to live in [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), being the only three of the Valar that have done so.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>
