**Ómar**, also called **Amillo**, was a [Vala](../V/Valar.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

## History {#History}

He was considered to be the youngest of all the Valar, and was the last of them to come into the world, singing songs as he entered.<sup id="cite_ref-III_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-III-2">[2]</a></sup> Ómar knew all [languages](../L/Languages.md) and all songs.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In [Valmar](../V/Valmar.md) he would sing to the harp-playing of his brother, [Salmar](../S/Salmar.md), and his voice was the best of all voices;<sup id="cite_ref-Valmar_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valmar-4">[4]</a></sup> other times, he would be trilling in the gardens of [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md), where [Nielíqui](../N/Nielíqui.md) Oromë's daughter danced about its woods.<sup id="cite_ref-Valmar_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valmar-4">[4]</a></sup>

From Ómar and [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) the [Teleri](../V/Vanyar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> learned much of poetry and song.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><b>Ómar</b></i> and <i><b>Amillo</b></i> are [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) names. The former is related to Qenya *ōma* "voice",<sup id="cite_ref-Pei_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pei-7">[6]</a></sup> while the latter is glossed as "one of the Happy Folk; Hilary", where *Hilary* derives from Latin *hilaris* "cheerful".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

The [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) equivalents of his names are <i><b>Ûmor</b></i> and <i><b>Gamlos</b></i> (likely meaning "one who shouts with joy").<sup id="cite_ref-Pei_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pei-7">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early, rejected entry in *[Qenyaqetsa](../Q/Qenyaqetsa.md)*, it is said that Amillo, together with his brother [Noldorin](../S/Salmar.md) and the Vala [Erinti](../E/Erinti.md) left [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) to live in [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md), being the only three of the Valar that have done so.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>
