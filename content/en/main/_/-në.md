<i><b>-në</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) suffix that has two meanings.

## -në<sup>1</sup> {#-në1}

Used for denoting a set of something.

### Examples {#Examples}

- **carcanë** = "row of teeth" (**carca** = "tooth"\|[KARAK](../K/KARAK.md))

## -në<sup>2</sup> {#-në2}

Evidently a nominal counterpart of the adjectival ending **-na**.

### Examples {#Examples_2}

- **carnë** = "(round) loaf" (**carna** = "round"\| <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=KOR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KOR (page does not exist)">KOR</a>)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **sarnë** = "strong place" (**sarna** = "of stone"\| <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=SAR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SAR (page does not exist)">SAR</a>?)
