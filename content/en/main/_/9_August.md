Events that occurred on 9 August

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1914](1914.md)** - Artist [Tove Jansson](../T/Tove_Jansson.md) is born.
- **[1923](1923.md)** - Voice actor [John Stephenson](../J/John_Stephenson.md) is born.
- **[1932](1932.md)** - Voice actor [Denys Hawthorne](../D/Denys_Hawthorne.md) is born.
- **[1934](1934.md)** - <a class="external text" href="http://www.tolkiensociety.org/blog/2015/07/1934-sketch-of-tolkien-discovered/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien&#39;s portrait</a> by artist F.A. Farrell is published in the Australian Catholic journal *The Advocate*.
- **[1945](1945.md)** – As <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_bombings_of_Hiroshima_and_Nagasaki" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">the bombs</a> are dropped on Hiroshima and Nagasaki, [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) [denounces these tactics as "utter folly"](../L/Letter_102.md).
- **[1973](1973.md)** - The [last known photograph](../../file/J/Jrrt_1972_tree.jpg.md) of J.R.R. Tolkien is taken, in front of his favourite tree, the [*Pinus Nigra*](../P/Pines.md#Inspiration) (named [Laocoon](../L/Laocoon.md)), at the Botanic Garden in [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md).
- **[1981](1981.md)** - The twenty-third episode of the [1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- **[1981](1981.md)** - [Mythcon 12](../M/Mythcon_12.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mills_College" title="wikipedia:Mills College">Mills College</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland,_California" title="wikipedia:Oakland, California">Oakland</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1986](1986.md)** - [Mythcon 17](../M/Mythcon_17.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University" title="wikipedia:California State University">California State University</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach,_California" title="wikipedia:Long Beach, California">Long Beach</a>.
- **[1997](1997.md)** - [Mythcon 28](../M/Mythcon_28.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pepperdine_University" title="wikipedia:Pepperdine University">Pepperdine University</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malibu,_California" title="wikipedia:Malibu, California">Malibu</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[2005](2005.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Symphony](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony.md)* plays in Herodion, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece" title="wikipedia:Greece">Greece</a>.
- **[2007](2007.md)** - <a class="external text" href="http://omentielva.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Tatya</a> - 2nd International Conference on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Invented Languages in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antwerp" title="wikipedia:Antwerp">Antwerp</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium" title="wikipedia:Belgium">Belgium</a>.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - <a class="external text" href="http://omentielva.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Nelya</a> - 3rd International Conference on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Invented Languages in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitehaven" title="wikipedia:Whitehaven">Whitehaven</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>.
- **[2013](2013.md)** - [Omentielva Lempea](../O/Omentielva.md) - 5th International Conference on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Invented Languages in Helsinki, Finland.
- **[2014](2014.md)** - [Mythcon 45](../M/Mythcon_45.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheaton_College_(Massachusetts)" title="wikipedia:Wheaton College (Massachusetts)">Wheaton College</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts" title="wikipedia:Massachusetts">Massachusetts</a>.
- **[2015](2015.md)** - [Omentielva Enquea](../O/Omentielva.md) - Sixth International Conference on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Invented Languages in [Greisinger Museum](../G/Greisinger_Museum.md), Switzerland.
- **[2022](2022.md)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2023](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2023.md)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) is held captive by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
