**Írimë** was a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves"). She was the third child (and second daughter) of [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB), [King of the Noldor](/wiki/King_of_the_Noldor), and [Indis](/wiki/Indis), his second wife. She was the sister of [Findis](/wiki/Findis), [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), and [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin), as well as the half-sister of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor).<sup id="cite_ref-Shib_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shib-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Little is known about Írimë, except that during the [Exile of the Noldor](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor) she went to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) across the [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB) with her elder brother, Fingolfin, whom she was close to, while the rest of her siblings remained in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-Shib_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shib-1">[1]</a></sup> Írimë's fate is unknown; she may have been slain or returned to [Aman](/wiki/Aman) after the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Írimë* is clearly [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), probably a feminized form of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=%C3%8Drima&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Írima (page does not exist)">írima</a></i> ("desirable, lovely").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Her [mother-name](/wiki/Amiless%C3%AB "Amilessë") was <i><b>Lalwendë</b></i>, or in shortened form <i><b>Lalwen</b></i>, and she was generally known by this name. This short name was not changed in Middle-earth, as it fit [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-Shib_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shib-1">[1]</a></sup> The Sindarin version of this name would have been <i><b>Glaðwen</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the same lines of *[The Shibboleth of Fëanor](/wiki/The_Shibboleth_of_F%C3%ABanor)*, [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) contradictorily gave both *Írimë* and <i><b>Írien</b></i> as names for this character. His son [Christopher](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien "Christopher Tolkien") explains his father could have been influenced by the previous genealogies.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The first introduction of Írimë in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") was during the composition of [the Later *Quenta Silmarillion*](/wiki/The_Later_Quenta_Silmarillion "The Later Quenta Silmarillion"), in which Finwë also had another daughter called [Faniel](/wiki/Faniel).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In a following revision, her name was changed to *Finvain*,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> but was soon changed again to *Írimë* or *Írien* as explained above.<sup id="cite_ref-Shib_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shib-1">[1]</a></sup>
