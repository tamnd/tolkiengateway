**Öshül-küllösh** was a waterfall on [Ellor Eshúrizel](/wiki/Ellor_Esh%C3%BArizel) according to [Michael George Ramer](/wiki/Michael_George_Ramer) in *[The Notion Club Papers](/wiki/The_Notion_Club_Papers)*.<sup id="cite_ref-History_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-History-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Nicholas Guildford](/wiki/Nicholas_Guildford) reported in the [Notion Club Papers](/wiki/Notion_Club_Papers) that [Alwin Arundel Lowdham](/wiki/Alwin_Arundel_Lowdham) had claimed that after the meeting, Ramer had revealed to him that he had thought that Öshül-küllösh meant "*Falling Water*".<sup id="cite_ref-History_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-History-1">[1]</a></sup>
