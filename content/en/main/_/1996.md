[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1996" title="wikipedia:1996">1996</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[5](/wiki/5_January "5 January")** - English actor [Maxim Baldry](/wiki/Maxim_Baldry) is born.
- **[19](/wiki/19_January "19 January")** - The first concert of the [Tolkien Ensemble](/wiki/Tolkien_Ensemble) at Gjorslev Castle, Denmark.

## March {#March}

- **[6](/wiki/6_March "6 March")** - Actor [Simon Cadell](/wiki/Simon_Cadell) dies.
- **[22](/wiki/22_March "22 March")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 8 is published.

## April {#April}

- **?** - [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) agrees with Harvey Weinstein to be involved in the production of a film project based on [Tolkien's](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") works.

## May {#May}

- **[3](/wiki/3_May "3 May")** - Author and academic [Charles Moorman](/wiki/Charles_Moorman) dies.
- **[14](/wiki/14_May "14 May")** - [Vera Chapman](/wiki/Vera_Chapman) dies.

## July {#July}

- **[21](/wiki/21_July "21 July")** - Actor [Wolfe Morris](/wiki/Wolfe_Morris) dies.
- **[26](/wiki/26_July "26 July")-[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")** - [Mythcon 27](/wiki/Mythcon_27).

## August {#August}

- **?** - [Grenadier Models Inc.](/wiki/Grenadier_Models_Inc.), which had released metal miniatures based on *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, ceases to exist.

## September {#September}

- <b><a href="/wiki/2_September" title="2 September">2</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth" title="The Peoples of Middle-earth">The Peoples of Middle-earth</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[2](/wiki/2_September "2 September")** - *[A Tolkien Book of Days](/wiki/A_Tolkien_Book_of_Days)* is published.
- **[13](/wiki/13_September "13 September")** - Actor [Sadwyn Brophy](/wiki/Sadwyn_Brophy) is born.
- **[20](/wiki/20_September "20 September")-[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")** - [Oxonmoot 1996](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1996).
- **[27](/wiki/27_September "27 September")** - *[Realms of Tolkien](/wiki/Realms_of_Tolkien)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[11](/wiki/11_October "11 October")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 9 is published.

## November {#November}

- **[27](/wiki/27_November "27 November")** - Actress [Alexandra Astin](/wiki/Alexandra_Astin) is born.

## December {#December}

- **[6](/wiki/6_December "6 December")** - *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)* is published in the US.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 34](/wiki/Mallorn_34 "Mallorn 34") is published.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Elaine Griffiths](/wiki/Elaine_Griffiths), friend and pupil of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), dies.
- **?** - *[Parma Endorion](/wiki/Parma_Endorion)* is published.
- **?** - *[Middle-earth: The Dragons](/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Dragons)* card game is released.
- **?** - *[Middle-earth: Dark Minions](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Dark_Minions)* card game is released.
- **?** - *[Travel and Communication in Tolkien's Worlds](/wiki/Travel_and_Communication_in_Tolkien%27s_Worlds)* is published.
