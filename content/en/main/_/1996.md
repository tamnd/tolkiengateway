## January {#January}

- **[5](5_January.md)** - English actor [Maxim Baldry](../M/Maxim_Baldry.md) is born.
- **[19](19_January.md)** - The first concert of the [Tolkien Ensemble](../T/Tolkien_Ensemble.md) at Gjorslev Castle, Denmark.

## March {#March}

- **[6](6_March.md)** - Actor [Simon Cadell](../S/Simon_Cadell.md) dies.
- **[22](22_March.md)** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](../T/Tyalie_Tyelellieva.md)* issue 8 is published.

## April {#April}

- **?** - [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) agrees with Harvey Weinstein to be involved in the production of a film project based on [Tolkien's](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) works.

## May {#May}

- **[3](3_May.md)** - Author and academic [Charles Moorman](../C/Charles_Moorman.md) dies.
- **[14](14_May.md)** - [Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md) dies.

## July {#July}

- **[21](21_July.md)** - Actor [Wolfe Morris](../W/Wolfe_Morris.md) dies.
- **[26](26_July.md)-[29](29_July.md)** - [Mythcon 27](../M/Mythcon_27.md).

## August {#August}

- **?** - [Grenadier Models Inc.](../G/Grenadier_Models_Inc..md), which had released metal miniatures based on *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, ceases to exist.

## September {#September}

- <b><a href="2_September.md" title="2 September">2</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md" title="The Peoples of Middle-earth">The Peoples of Middle-earth</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[2](2_September.md)** - *[A Tolkien Book of Days](../A/A_Tolkien_Book_of_Days.md)* is published.
- **[13](13_September.md)** - Actor [Sadwyn Brophy](../S/Sadwyn_Brophy.md) is born.
- **[20](20_September.md)-[22](22_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1996](../O/Oxonmoot_1996.md).
- **[27](27_September.md)** - *[Realms of Tolkien](../R/Realms_of_Tolkien.md)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[11](11_October.md)** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](../T/Tyalie_Tyelellieva.md)* issue 9 is published.

## November {#November}

- **[27](27_November.md)** - Actress [Alexandra Astin](../A/Alexandra_Astin.md) is born.

## December {#December}

- **[6](6_December.md)** - *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](../T/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md)* is published in the US.
