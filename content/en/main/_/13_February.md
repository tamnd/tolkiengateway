Events that occurred on 13 February.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1883](/wiki/1883)** - The composer [Richard Wagner](/wiki/Richard_Wagner) dies.
- **[1908](/wiki/1908)** - Voice actor [Patrick Barr](/wiki/Patrick_Barr) is born.
- **[1923](/wiki/1923)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 6](/wiki/Letter_6).
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [John Tolkien](/wiki/John_Tolkien) is made an Exorcist and Acolyte in a ceremony at Stonyhurst.
- **[1961](/wiki/1961)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 228a](/wiki/Letter_228a "Letter 228a").
- **[1971](/wiki/1971)** - [Rachel Tolkien](/wiki/Rachel_Tolkien) is born.
- **[1979](/wiki/1979)** - *[The Tolkien Quiz Book](</wiki/The_Tolkien_Quiz_Book_(by_Bart_Andrews)> "The Tolkien Quiz Book (by Bart Andrews)")* by Andrews and Zuber is published.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online): [Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar "The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar")*, "The Ashen Wastes" is released in North America.
- **[2021](/wiki/2021)** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) Seminar is held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.

    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) rests in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).
