## January {#January}

- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 43](/wiki/Amon_Hen_43 "Amon Hen 43") is published.
- **?** - *[Quettar](/wiki/Quettar)* is founded.

## February {#February}

- **[29](/wiki/29_February "29 February")** - *[A Reader's Guide to The Silmarillion](/wiki/A_Reader%27s_Guide_to_The_Silmarillion)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **[11](/wiki/11_May "11 May")** - [The Return of the King (1980 film)](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)>) is released on VHS in the United States.
- **[22](/wiki/22_May "22 May")** - Actress [Nazanin Boniadi](/wiki/Nazanin_Boniadi) is born.
- **[28](/wiki/28_May "28 May")** - Second edition of *[The Languages of Tolkien's Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Languages_of_Tolkien%27s_Middle-earth)* is published.
- **[29](/wiki/29_May "29 May")** - *[Poems and Stories](/wiki/Poems_and_Stories)* is published.
- **[29](/wiki/29_May "29 May")** - Actor [Adam Brown](/wiki/Adam_Brown) is born.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 44](/wiki/Amon_Hen_44 "Amon Hen 44") is published.

## July {#July}

- **[25](/wiki/25_July "25 July")-[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - [Mythcon 11](/wiki/Mythcon_11).
- **[26](/wiki/26_July "26 July")-[3 Aug](/wiki/3_August "3 August")** - At the Griffoni Film Festival, [Ralph Bakshi](/wiki/Ralph_Bakshi) wins the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giffoni_Film_Festival" title="wikipedia:Giffoni Film Festival">Golden Gryphon</a> for *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)")*.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 45](/wiki/Amon_Hen_45 "Amon Hen 45") is published.

- **[31](/wiki/31_August "31 August")** - [*Amon Hen* 46](/wiki/Amon_Hen_46 "Amon Hen 46") is published.

## September {#September}

- **[26](/wiki/26_September "26 September")-[28](/wiki/28_September "28 September")** - [Oxonmoot 1980](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1980).

- **?** - *[A Walk Through the Shire](/wiki/A_Walk_Through_the_Shire)* is published.

- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 47](/wiki/Amon_Hen_47 "Amon Hen 47") is published.
