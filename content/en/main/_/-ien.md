<i><b>-ien</b></i> or <i><b>-iën</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) suffix meaning "daughter".

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/L%C3%BAthien#Etymology" title="Lúthien">Lúth<b>ien</b></a></i> = "Daughter of Spells", "Enchantress"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Arien#Etymology" title="Arien">Ar<b>ien</b></a></i> = "Maiden of Sunlight"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}

The unrelated toponymic ending *[-ien](/wiki/-nd "-nd")*.
