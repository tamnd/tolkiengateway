The <i><b>1976 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar</b></i> was the official Tolkien calendar published by [Ballantine Books](/wiki/Ballantine_Books) in [1975](/wiki/1975). It features art by [The Brothers Hildebrandt](/wiki/The_Brothers_Hildebrandt) based on *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
