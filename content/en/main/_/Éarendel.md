<i><b>Éarendel</b></i>, was the name of [Éadwine](/wiki/%C3%89adwine)'s ship. The ship, along with Éadwine, disappeared in the year [878 AD](/wiki/Seventh_Age_878 "Seventh Age 878"), and no trace of both was ever found.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

<i><b>The Éarendel</b></i> was the name of a ship in [1947](/wiki/1947), on which [Edwin](/wiki/Edwin_Lowdham "Edwin Lowdham"), along with three other sailors, went off into <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_ocean" title="wikipedia:Atlantic ocean">Atlantic</a> and never returned.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
