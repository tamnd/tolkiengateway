<i><b>-ndur, -nur</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) suffix added to names to indicate faithful service or servitude.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=N%C3%BAro&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Núro (page does not exist)">-n(d)úro</a></i> "servant", [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=ND%C5%AA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NDŪ (page does not exist)">NDŪ</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Arandur" title="Arandur">Ara<b>ndur</b></a></i> = "[Steward](/wiki/Steward)" (literally "King-servant")
- <i><a href="/wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)" title="Eärendur (Lord of Andúnië)">Eäre<b>ndur</b></a>/<a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Earnur" title="Earnur">Ear<b>nur</b></a></i> = "Servant of the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer"), mariner"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Elendur_(son_of_Isildur)" title="Elendur (son of Isildur)">Ele<b>ndur</b></a></i> = "Servant of the Elves"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Cemendur_(son_of_Axantur)" title="Cemendur (son of Axantur)">Ceme<b>ndur</b></a></i> = "Servant of the Earth" (i.e. farmer?)
- <i><a href="/wiki/Isildur" title="Isildur">Isil<b>dur</b></a></i> = "Devoted to the [Moon](/wiki/Moon)"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Meneldur" title="Meneldur">Mene<b>ldur</b></a></i> = "Servant of [Heaven](/wiki/Heaven), astronomer"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Pelendur" title="Pelendur">Pele<b>ndur</b></a></i> = "Servant of the Border"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Valandur" title="Valandur">Vala<b>ndur</b></a></i> = "Servant of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar)"
