[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1913" title="wikipedia:1913">1913</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](3_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s twenty-first birthday.
- **[3](3_January.md)** - Tolkien [contacts](<../L/Letter_to_Edith_Bratt_(3_January_1913).md>) [Edith Bratt](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md), who subsequently breaks off her engagement with another man.
- **[7](7_January.md)** - Actor [Francis de Wolff](../F/Francis_de_Wolff.md) is born.
- **[8](8_January.md)** - Engagement of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [Edith Bratt](../E/Edith_Bratt.md)
- **[21](21_January.md)** - Actor [Heron Carvic](../H/Heron_Carvic.md) is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) begins [his diary](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_diaries.md) "JRRT and [EMB](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) in account together, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad_maiorem_Dei_gloriam" title="wikipedia:Ad maiorem Dei gloriam">AMDG</a>".

## February {#February}

- **[1](1_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a postcard to Edith Bratt](<../L/Letter_to_Edith_Bratt_(1_February_1913).md>).

## May {#May}

- **[16](16_May.md)** – [Norman Davis](../N/Norman_Davis.md) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[11](11_October.md)** - Actor [John Bryning](../J/John_Bryning.md) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[19](19_November.md)** - *[Exeter College Smoker](<../E/Exeter_College_Smoker_(1913).md>)*, featuring a cover illustration by J.R.R. Tolkien, is printed.

## December {#December}

- **[1](1_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is elected president of Stapledon Society, a student body at Exeter College.
- **[16](16_December.md)** – In an annual Christmas rugby match at [King Edward's School](../K/King_Edward's_School.md), alumni at [Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md) and [Cambridge](../C/Cambridge.md) play against the School's First XV. This year, the alumni [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), [Christopher Wiseman](../C/Christopher_Wiseman.md), [T.K. Barnsley](../T/Thomas_Kenneth_Barnsley.md), [Robert Gilson](../R/Robert_Gilson.md), [G.B. Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md) and [Sidney Barrowclough](../S/Sidney_Barrowclough.md) lose 14-10.
- **?** - First stanza of *[Valedictory](../V/Valedictory.md)* is published in the [Stapeldon Magazine](../S/Stapeldon_Magazine.md).
- **?** - "Oxford Letter", [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s report on former King Edward's pupils at [Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md), is published in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](../K/King_Edward's_School_Chronicle.md)*.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien takes a Second Class in exams and transfers from classics to English, with emphasis in philology, and formally studies Old Norse.
