[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1913" title="wikipedia:1913">1913</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s twenty-first birthday.
- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - Tolkien [contacts](</wiki/Letter_to_Edith_Bratt_(3_January_1913)> "Letter to Edith Bratt (3 January 1913)") [Edith Bratt](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien"), who subsequently breaks off her engagement with another man.
- **[7](/wiki/7_January "7 January")** - Actor [Francis de Wolff](/wiki/Francis_de_Wolff) is born.
- **[8](/wiki/8_January "8 January")** - Engagement of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [Edith Bratt](/wiki/Edith_Bratt)
- **[21](/wiki/21_January "21 January")** - Actor [Heron Carvic](/wiki/Heron_Carvic) is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) begins [his diary](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_diaries "J.R.R. Tolkien's diaries") "JRRT and [EMB](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien") in account together, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad_maiorem_Dei_gloriam" title="wikipedia:Ad maiorem Dei gloriam">AMDG</a>".

## February {#February}

- **[1](/wiki/1_February "1 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a postcard to Edith Bratt](</wiki/Letter_to_Edith_Bratt_(1_February_1913)> "Letter to Edith Bratt (1 February 1913)").

## May {#May}

- **[16](/wiki/16_May "16 May")** – [Norman Davis](/wiki/Norman_Davis) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[11](/wiki/11_October "11 October")** - Actor [John Bryning](/wiki/John_Bryning) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[19](/wiki/19_November "19 November")** - *[Exeter College Smoker](</wiki/Exeter_College_Smoker_(1913)> "Exeter College Smoker (1913)")*, featuring a cover illustration by J.R.R. Tolkien, is printed.

## December {#December}

- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is elected president of Stapledon Society, a student body at Exeter College.
- **[16](/wiki/16_December "16 December")** – In an annual Christmas rugby match at [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School), alumni at [Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford") and [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge) play against the School's First XV. This year, the alumni [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), [Christopher Wiseman](/wiki/Christopher_Wiseman), [T.K. Barnsley](/wiki/Thomas_Kenneth_Barnsley "Thomas Kenneth Barnsley"), [Robert Gilson](/wiki/Robert_Gilson), [G.B. Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith "Geoffrey Bache Smith") and [Sidney Barrowclough](/wiki/Sidney_Barrowclough) lose 14-10.
- **?** - First stanza of *[Valedictory](/wiki/Valedictory)* is published in the [Stapeldon Magazine](/wiki/Stapeldon_Magazine).
- **?** - "Oxford Letter", [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s report on former King Edward's pupils at [Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford"), is published in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School_Chronicle)*.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien takes a Second Class in exams and transfers from classics to English, with emphasis in philology, and formally studies Old Norse.
