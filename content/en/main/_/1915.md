[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1915" title="wikipedia:1915">1915</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[13](13_February.md)** - [Mary Jane Stow](../M/Mary_Jane_Stow.md) dies.

## March {#March}

- **?** - Earliest "evidence" of Tolkien working on "fairy language".

## April {#April}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes the poem *[Goblin Feet](../G/Goblin_Feet.md)*.

## June {#June}

- **[10](10_June.md)** - Exams start at [Oxford University](../O/Oxford_University.md). Because of [the war](../G/Great_War.md), only Tolkien and 24 others remain in the entire University. Tolkien passes.
- **[28](28_June.md)** - Tolkien applies at the Oxford recruiting office to join the army.
- **[28](28_June.md)** - [Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](../P/Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski.md), scholar and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is born.
- **?** - Tolkien is awarded First Class Honours degree in English Language and Literature.

## July {#July}

- **[15](15_July.md)** - Tolkien is commissioned as a second lieutenant of the 13th Battalion Lancashire Fusiliers, and begins training at Bedford.

## September {#September}

- **[25](25_September.md)** - The Big Four of the [T.C.B.S.](../T/T.C.B.S..md) - Tolkien, [Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md), [Wiseman](../C/Christopher_Wiseman.md) and [Gilson](../R/Robert_Gilson.md) - have their last meeting.

## October {#October}

- **[4](4_October.md)** - Translator [Tore Zetterholm](../T/Tore_Zetterholm.md) is born.

## November {#November}

- **?** - Tolkien writes his earliest version of *[The Trees of Kortirion](../T/The_Trees_of_Kortirion.md)*.
- **[21](21_November.md)** - G.B. Smith is shipped to France.
- **[26](26_November.md)** - Tolkien writes [Letter 3](../L/Letter_3.md) with a copy of *Kortirion* to [Edith Bratt](../E/Edith_Bratt.md) from Rugeley Camp, Staffordshire.

## December {#December}

- **?** - Tolkien might have written the poem *[Habbanan beneath the Stars](../E/Eruman_beneath_the_Stars.md)*.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - The poem "[Goblin Feet](../G/Goblin_Feet.md)" is published in *[Oxford Poetry 1915](../O/Oxford_Poetry.md)*.
