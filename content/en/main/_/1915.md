[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1915" title="wikipedia:1915">1915</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[13](/wiki/13_February "13 February")** - [Mary Jane Stow](/wiki/Mary_Jane_Stow) dies.

## March {#March}

- **?** - Earliest "evidence" of Tolkien working on "fairy language".

## April {#April}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the poem *[Goblin Feet](/wiki/Goblin_Feet)*.

## June {#June}

- **[10](/wiki/10_June "10 June")** - Exams start at [Oxford University](/wiki/Oxford_University). Because of [the war](/wiki/Great_War "Great War"), only Tolkien and 24 others remain in the entire University. Tolkien passes.
- **[28](/wiki/28_June "28 June")** - Tolkien applies at the Oxford recruiting office to join the army.
- **[28](/wiki/28_June "28 June")** - [Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](/wiki/Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski), scholar and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is born.
- **?** - Tolkien is awarded First Class Honours degree in English Language and Literature.

## July {#July}

- **[15](/wiki/15_July "15 July")** - Tolkien is commissioned as a second lieutenant of the 13th Battalion Lancashire Fusiliers, and begins training at Bedford.

## September {#September}

- **[25](/wiki/25_September "25 September")** - The Big Four of the [T.C.B.S.](/wiki/T.C.B.S.) - Tolkien, [Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith "Geoffrey Bache Smith"), [Wiseman](/wiki/Christopher_Wiseman "Christopher Wiseman") and [Gilson](/wiki/Robert_Gilson "Robert Gilson") - have their last meeting.

## October {#October}

- **[4](/wiki/4_October "4 October")** - Translator [Tore Zetterholm](/wiki/Tore_Zetterholm) is born.

## November {#November}

- **?** - Tolkien writes his earliest version of *[The Trees of Kortirion](/wiki/The_Trees_of_Kortirion)*.
- **[21](/wiki/21_November "21 November")** - G.B. Smith is shipped to France.
- **[26](/wiki/26_November "26 November")** - Tolkien writes [Letter 3](/wiki/Letter_3) with a copy of *Kortirion* to [Edith Bratt](/wiki/Edith_Bratt) from Rugeley Camp, Staffordshire.

## December {#December}

- **?** - Tolkien might have written the poem *[Habbanan beneath the Stars](/wiki/Eruman_beneath_the_Stars "Eruman beneath the Stars")*.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - The poem "[Goblin Feet](/wiki/Goblin_Feet)" is published in *[Oxford Poetry 1915](/wiki/Oxford_Poetry "Oxford Poetry")*.
