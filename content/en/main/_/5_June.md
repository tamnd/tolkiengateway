Events that occurred on 5 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1946](/wiki/1946)** - Actor [John Bach](/wiki/John_Bach) is born.
- **[1953](/wiki/1953)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 137b](/wiki/Letter_137b).
- **[1953](/wiki/1953)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 137c](/wiki/Letter_137c).
- **[1955](/wiki/1955)** – Harvey Breit writes an article on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") in the *New York Times Book Review*. Breit tells of "Dr. [Tolkein](/wiki/Tolkein)", and contains many more errors. Tolkien later writes [an angry letter about it](/wiki/Letter_165 "Letter 165").
- **[1959](/wiki/1959)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) delivers the [Valedictory Address to the University of Oxford](/wiki/Valedictory_Address_to_the_University_of_Oxford).
- **[1973](/wiki/1973)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 352](/wiki/Letter_352).
- **[1973](/wiki/1973)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to James A.H. Murray](/wiki/Letter_to_James_A.H._Murray "Letter to James A.H. Murray").
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - [Andy Serkis](/wiki/Andy_Serkis) wins the <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Movie_Awards" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">MTV Movie Award</a> for "Best Animated Character", for his role as [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum). His <a class="external text" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wdebYE103sA" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">immortal acceptance speech</a> would win the [2004](/wiki/2004) <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Award" title="wikipedia:Hugo Award">Hugo Award</a> for "Best Dramatic Presentation, Short Form".
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - [The Lord of the Rings Symphony](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta" title="wikipedia:Atlanta">Atlanta</a> Symphony Hall, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_(U.S._state)" title="wikipedia:Georgia (U.S. state)">Georgia</a>.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - [Forodrim](/wiki/Forodrim)'s 36th birthday celebration.
