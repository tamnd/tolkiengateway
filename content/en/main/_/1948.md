[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948" title="wikipedia:1948">1948</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[7](/wiki/7_January "7 January")** - [Jarion Monroe](/wiki/Jarion_Monroe), who would voice the [King of the Dead](/wiki/King_of_the_Dead) in *[EA's The Return of the King](/wiki/EA%27s_The_Return_of_the_King)*, is born.
- **[18](/wiki/18_January "18 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to J.L.N. O'Loughlin](</wiki/Letter_to_J.L.N._O%27Loughlin_(18_January_1948)> "Letter to J.L.N. O'Loughlin (18 January 1948)").
- **[18](/wiki/18_January "18 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [to Rosemary](</wiki/Letter_to_Rosemary_(18_January_1948)> "Letter to Rosemary (18 January 1948)")
- **[25](/wiki/25_January "25 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 113](/wiki/Letter_113 "Letter 113").

## February {#February}

- **[2](/wiki/2_January "2 January")** - [Nils-Lennart Johannesson](/wiki/Nils-Lennart_Johannesson) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - Actor [Steven M. Kramer](/wiki/Steven_M._Kramer) is born.

## April {#April}

- **[7](/wiki/7_April "7 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 114](/wiki/Letter_114 "Letter 114").

## June {#June}

- **[15](/wiki/15_June "15 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 115](/wiki/Letter_115 "Letter 115").

## August {#August}

- **[5](/wiki/5_August "5 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 116](/wiki/Letter_116 "Letter 116").
- **[20](/wiki/20_August "20 August")** - [John Noble](/wiki/John_Noble), who would play [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.

## September {#September}

- **[8](/wiki/8_September "8 September")** - Artist [Michael Hague](/wiki/Michael_Hague) is born.
- **[14](/wiki/14_September "14 September")** - Around that date Tolkien completes the drafts of the final parts of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* at the home of his son, [Michael](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien "Michael Tolkien").
- **?** - Tolkien visits [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge) to view the *Ancrene Wisse* manuscript.

## October {#October}

- **[14](/wiki/14_October "14 October")** - Narrator [Gerard Murphy](/wiki/Gerard_Murphy) is born.
- **[31](/wiki/31_October "31 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 117](/wiki/Letter_117 "Letter 117").
- **?** - [Katharine Farrer](/wiki/Katharine_Farrer) sends a letter to Tolkien expressing her opinions about the *[Ainulindalë](</wiki/Ainulindal%C3%AB_(Morgoth%27s_Ring)> "Ainulindalë (Morgoth's Ring)")*.

## November {#November}

- **[24](/wiki/24_November "24 November")** - Artist [John Alvin](/wiki/John_Alvin) is born.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Dick Plotz](/wiki/Dick_Plotz) is born.
- **?** - *[Studia Neophilologica Vol. 20, Nos. 1-2](/wiki/Studia_Neophilologica_Vol._20,_Nos._1-2)*, containing an essay by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [S.R.T.O. d'Ardenne](/wiki/Simonne_d%27Ardenne "Simonne d'Ardenne"), is published.
