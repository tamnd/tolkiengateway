## January {#January}

- **[7](7_January.md)** - [Jarion Monroe](../J/Jarion_Monroe.md), who would voice the [King of the Dead](../K/King_of_the_Dead.md) in *[EA's The Return of the King](../E/EA's_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*, is born.
- **[18](18_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to J.L.N. O'Loughlin](<../L/Letter_to_J.L.N._O'Loughlin_(18_January_1948).md>).
- **[18](18_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [to Rosemary](<../L/Letter_to_Rosemary_(18_January_1948).md>)
- **[25](25_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 113](../L/Letter_113.md).

## February {#February}

- **[2](2_January.md)** - [Nils-Lennart Johannesson](../N/Nils-Lennart_Johannesson.md) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[1](1_March.md)** - Actor [Steven M. Kramer](../S/Steven_M._Kramer.md) is born.

## April {#April}

- **[7](7_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 114](../L/Letter_114.md).

## June {#June}

- **[15](15_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 115](../L/Letter_115.md).

## August {#August}

- **[5](5_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 116](../L/Letter_116.md).
- **[20](20_August.md)** - [John Noble](../J/John_Noble.md), who would play [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.

## September {#September}

- **[8](8_September.md)** - Artist [Michael Hague](../M/Michael_Hague.md) is born.
- **[14](14_September.md)** - Around that date Tolkien completes the drafts of the final parts of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* at the home of his son, [Michael](../M/Michael_Tolkien.md).
- **?** - Tolkien visits [Cambridge](../C/Cambridge.md) to view the *Ancrene Wisse* manuscript.

- **[14](14_October.md)** - Narrator [Gerard Murphy](../G/Gerard_Murphy.md) is born.

- **?** - [Katharine Farrer](../K/Katharine_Farrer.md) sends a letter to Tolkien expressing her opinions about the *[Ainulindalë](<../A/Ainulindalë_(Morgoth's_Ring).md>)*.

## November {#November}
