**-il** is a little-attested ending in the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) language,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> used sometimes to denote feminine gender. Its masculine counterpart is *[-on](-on.md)*.

Its presence causes [i-affection](../I/I-affection.md) to the vowels of the stem (cf. *[brannon](../B/Brannon.md)* and <i><a href="../B/Brennil.md" title="Brennil">br<b>e</b>nn<b>i</b>l</a></i>).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Possibly it derives from a [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) ending \**-illê*. This is supported by several [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) words which end in *-illë* with Sindarin cognates in *-il*; most notably compare the Quenya <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Amille&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Amille (page does not exist)">amille</a></i> "mother" and the Sindarin <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Emil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Emil (page does not exist)">emil</a></i>.

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Emil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Emil (page does not exist)">emil</a></i> "mother"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Odhril&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Odhril (page does not exist)">odhril</a></i> "parent (referring to mother)"
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Melethril&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Melethril (page does not exist)">melethril</a></i> "lover"<sup id="cite_ref-E_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E-3">[2]</a></sup>
- *[brennil](../B/Brennil.md)* "lady"<sup id="cite_ref-E_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E-3">[2]</a></sup>
- *[hiril](../H/Hiril.md)* "lady"<sup id="cite_ref-E_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E-3">[2]</a></sup>
- <i><a href="../T/Thuringwethil.md" title="Thuringwethil">Thuringweth<b>il</b></a></i> "secret-shadow **woman**"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

In at least one case, *-il* is said to be a diminutive ending; compare how the masculine ending *-on* can sometimes refer to "great" things.

- *[niphredil](../N/Niphredil.md)* "little pallor" ([niphred](../N/Niphred.md))<sup id="cite_ref-E_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E-3">[2]</a></sup>
- *[ernil](../E/Ernil.md)* "prince" (perhaps understood as \*"little noble"; <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Arn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Arn (page does not exist)">arn</a>)
