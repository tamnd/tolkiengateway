**-il** is a little-attested ending in the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) language,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> used sometimes to denote feminine gender. Its masculine counterpart is *[-on](/wiki/-on)*.

Its presence causes [i-affection](/wiki/I-affection "I-affection") to the vowels of the stem (cf. *[brannon](/wiki/Brannon "Brannon")* and <i><a href="/wiki/Brennil" title="Brennil">br<b>e</b>nn<b>i</b>l</a></i>).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Possibly it derives from a [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) ending \**-illê*. This is supported by several [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) words which end in *-illë* with Sindarin cognates in *-il*; most notably compare the Quenya <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Amille&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Amille (page does not exist)">amille</a></i> "mother" and the Sindarin <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Emil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Emil (page does not exist)">emil</a></i>.

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Emil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Emil (page does not exist)">emil</a></i> "mother"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Odhril&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Odhril (page does not exist)">odhril</a></i> "parent (referring to mother)"
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Melethril&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Melethril (page does not exist)">melethril</a></i> "lover"<sup id="cite_ref-E_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E-3">[2]</a></sup>
- *[brennil](/wiki/Brennil "Brennil")* "lady"<sup id="cite_ref-E_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E-3">[2]</a></sup>
- *[hiril](/wiki/Hiril "Hiril")* "lady"<sup id="cite_ref-E_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E-3">[2]</a></sup>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Thuringwethil" title="Thuringwethil">Thuringweth<b>il</b></a></i> "secret-shadow **woman**"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

In at least one case, *-il* is said to be a diminutive ending; compare how the masculine ending *-on* can sometimes refer to "great" things.

- *[niphredil](/wiki/Niphredil "Niphredil")* "little pallor" ([niphred](/wiki/Niphred "Niphred"))<sup id="cite_ref-E_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E-3">[2]</a></sup>
- *[ernil](/wiki/Ernil "Ernil")* "prince" (perhaps understood as \*"little noble"; <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Arn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Arn (page does not exist)">arn</a>)
