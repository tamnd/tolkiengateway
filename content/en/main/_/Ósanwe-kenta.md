The <i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="Ósanwe.md" title="Ósanwe">Ósanwe</a>-<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Centa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Centa (page does not exist)">kenta</a></b></i>, or "Enquiry into the Communication of Thought", is a summary by an unnamed editor of a longer treatise by [Pengolodh](../P/Pengolodh.md) of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).

The subject-matter is <i><b><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Sanwe-latya&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sanwe-latya (page does not exist)">sanwe-latya</a></b></i> ([Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "thought-opening", but better translated as "telepathy"). Pengolodh included it as an appendix to the *[Lhammas](../L/Lhammas.md)* because of the implications of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tengwesta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tengwesta (page does not exist)">tengwesta</a></i> ("grammar, language") on thought-transmission, i.e. with the development of language, telepathy became more difficult and all but fell out of use among the "[incarnated](../I/Incarnate.md)" (those beings with *[hröa](../H/Hröa.md)*).

## Real world history {#Real_world_history}

The essay was written in c. [1959](1959.md)-[60](1960.md) by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) as a typescript of eight pages, separately paginated, numbered 1-8, and appended to the major work *[Quendi and Eldar](../Q/Quendi_and_Eldar.md)*. The existence of this essay was revealed by Christopher Tolkien in [Morgoth's Ring](../M/Morgoth's_Ring.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> published in [1993](1993.md). It was published and edited by [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md) for the first time in [*Vinyar Tengwar*, Number 39](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_39.md), July 1998. From the same editor, the essay was later included as a chapter of *[The Nature of Middle-earth](../T/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth.md)* ([2021](2021.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> A related and short manuscript, "Etymological Notes on the *Ósanwe-kenta*", was published in [*Vinyar Tengwar*, Number 41](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_41.md), July 2000. This latest essay is mostly a list of linguistic roots.
