Events that occurred on 9 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1922](/wiki/1922)** - [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s favourite artist, [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes), is born.
- **[1943](/wiki/1943)** - Renowned scholar [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey) is born.
- **[1954](/wiki/1954)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 149](/wiki/Letter_149).
- **[1954](/wiki/1954)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to the Rector of the University of Liège](</wiki/Letter_to_Li%C3%A8ge_University_(9_September_1954)> "Letter to Liège University (9 September 1954)").
- **[2016](/wiki/2016)** - [Oxonmoot 2016](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2016) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2022](/wiki/2022)** - The episode [Adar](</wiki/Adar_(episode)> "Adar (episode)") of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* is released online.
- **[2023](/wiki/2023)** - Actor [Peter McKenzie](/wiki/Peter_McKenzie) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) is held captive by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
