[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1920" title="wikipedia:1920">1920</a></b>.

## June {#June}

- **[11](/wiki/11_June "11 June")** - German translator [Walter Scherf](/wiki/Walter_Scherf) is born.
- **[22](/wiki/22_June "22 June")** - Voice acting legend [Paul Frees](/wiki/Paul_Frees), who would voice several characters in the [Rankin/Bass](/wiki/Rankin/Bass) movies, is born.
- **?** - "[The Happy Mariners](/wiki/The_Happy_Mariners)" is published in *[The Stapeldon Magazine](/wiki/The_Stapeldon_Magazine)*.

## July {#July}

- **[21](/wiki/21_July "21 July")** - Tolkien is appointed to Readership in English Language at the [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds).

## August {#August}

- **[16](/wiki/16_August "16 August")** - [Max Schuchart](/wiki/Max_Schuchart), who would be the first to translate *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, is born.
- **[19](/wiki/19_August "19 August")** - Composer [Paul Kont](/wiki/Paul_Kont) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[27](/wiki/27_September "27 September")** - [William Conrad](/wiki/William_Conrad), voice actor who portrayed [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) in [*The Return of the King* (1980 film)](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)"), is born.

## October {#October}

- **[8](/wiki/8_October "8 October")** - [Nils Werenskiold](/wiki/Nils_Werenskiold), the first Norwegian translator of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, is born.
- **[10](/wiki/10_October "10 October")** - [Victor Platt](/wiki/Victor_Platt), who would voice [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1955 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)"), is born.
- **[22](/wiki/22_October "22 October")** - [Michael Tolkien](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien), Tolkien's second son, is born.
- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")** - [Norman Bird](/wiki/Norman_Bird), who would voice [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"), is born.

## December {#December}

- **[22](/wiki/22_December "22 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[The Book of Fairy Poetry](/wiki/The_Book_of_Fairy_Poetry)* is published.
