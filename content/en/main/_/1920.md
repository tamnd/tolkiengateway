[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1920" title="wikipedia:1920">1920</a></b>.

## June {#June}

- **[11](11_June.md)** - German translator [Walter Scherf](../W/Walter_Scherf.md) is born.
- **[22](22_June.md)** - Voice acting legend [Paul Frees](../P/Paul_Frees.md), who would voice several characters in the [Rankin/Bass](../R/Rankin%252FBass.md) movies, is born.
- **?** - "[The Happy Mariners](../T/The_Happy_Mariners.md)" is published in *[The Stapeldon Magazine](../T/The_Stapeldon_Magazine.md)*.

## July {#July}

- **[21](21_July.md)** - Tolkien is appointed to Readership in English Language at the [University of Leeds](../U/University_of_Leeds.md).

## August {#August}

- **[16](16_August.md)** - [Max Schuchart](../M/Max_Schuchart.md), who would be the first to translate *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, is born.
- **[19](19_August.md)** - Composer [Paul Kont](../P/Paul_Kont.md) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[27](27_September.md)** - [William Conrad](../W/William_Conrad.md), voice actor who portrayed [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) in [*The Return of the King* (1980 film)](<../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md>), is born.

## October {#October}

- **[8](8_October.md)** - [Nils Werenskiold](../N/Nils_Werenskiold.md), the first Norwegian translator of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, is born.
- **[10](10_October.md)** - [Victor Platt](../V/Victor_Platt.md), who would voice [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1955 radio series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>), is born.
- **[22](22_October.md)** - [Michael Tolkien](../M/Michael_Tolkien.md), Tolkien's second son, is born.
- **[30](30_October.md)** - [Norman Bird](../N/Norman_Bird.md), who would voice [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>), is born.

## December {#December}

- **[22](22_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[The Book of Fairy Poetry](../T/The_Book_of_Fairy_Poetry.md)* is published.
