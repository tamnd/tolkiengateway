Events that occurred on 19 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1916](/wiki/1916)** - Writer [Terence Tiller](/wiki/Terence_Tiller) is born.
- **[1931](/wiki/1931)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) and [Hugo Dyson](/wiki/Hugo_Dyson) have dinner, followed by a conversation that ran until 4 a.m. [the following morning](/wiki/20_September "20 September") that was essential for Lewis's conversion to [Christianity](/wiki/Christianity).
- **[1943](/wiki/1943)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 49e](/wiki/Letter_49e).
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 80a](/wiki/Letter_80a).
- **[1956](/wiki/1956)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Terence Tiller](</wiki/Letter_to_Terence_Tiller_(19_September_1956)> "Letter to Terence Tiller (19 September 1956)").
- **[1962](/wiki/1962)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Alina Dadlez](</wiki/Letter_to_Alina_Dadlez_(19_September_1962)> "Letter to Alina Dadlez (19 September 1962)").
- **[1965](/wiki/1965)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 277a](/wiki/Letter_277a "Letter 277a").
- **[1982](/wiki/1982)** - The article "[The Return of Tolkien](/wiki/The_Sunday_Times_Magazine_19_September_1982 "The Sunday Times Magazine 19 September 1982")" is published in *The Sunday Times Magazine*.
- **[1986](/wiki/1986)** - [Oxonmoot 1986](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1986) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[1993](/wiki/1993)** - [Oxonmoot 1993](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1993) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:University College, Oxford">University College</a>.
- **[1997](/wiki/1997)** - [Oxonmoot 1997](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1997) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Exeter College, Oxford">Exeter College</a>.
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - [Oxonmoot 2003](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2003) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Hugh%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Hugh&#39;s College, Oxford">St Hugh&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - [Oxonmoot 2004](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2004) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Hilda%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Hilda&#39;s College, Oxford">St Hilda&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - [The Lord of the Rings Symphony](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony) at Mortensen Hall in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartford,_Connecticut" title="wikipedia:Hartford, Connecticut">Hartford</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut" title="wikipedia:Connecticut">Connecticut</a>.
- **[2020](/wiki/2020)** - [Oxonmoot 2020](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2020) held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) arrives at [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) but is not allowed to enter.
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
