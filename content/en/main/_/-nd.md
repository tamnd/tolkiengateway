<i><b>-nd</b></i> is a toponymical ending in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), an ending "commonly used in the names of regions or countries". Vowels could be attached to the ending, yielding the forms *-and*, *-end*, *-ond*. In names such as *[Rohan](../R/Rohan.md)*, the *-d* was dropped, due to its lack of pronunciation in speech (this also occurred in *[Anorien](../A/Anorien.md)* and <i><a href="../I/Ithilien.md" title="Ithilien">Ithilien</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup></i>

The form also appears as *-ian(d)*, *-iann*, *-ion*, *-ien*.<sup id="cite_ref-PE_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*-ian(d)* and ultimately *-ien* are reduced from *-iand(a)*, *-iend(e)*,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and said to be derived from [PQ](../P/PQ.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Yand%C4%93&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Yandē (page does not exist)">yandē</a></i> "a wide region, or country".<sup id="cite_ref-PE_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-3">[3]</a></sup>

*-(i)on* appears in later-formed names and is in origin distinct from the above. It is explained to be from root [YAN](../Y/YAN.md)A- and/or root YONO.<sup id="cite_ref-PE_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

Relevant endings don't appear in other languages such as [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) save perhaps the name [Hildórien](../H/Hildórien.md) and [Calacirian](../C/Calacirian.md) (from *Kalakiryande*).<sup id="cite_ref-RGEO_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RGEO-5">[5]</a></sup> However there is the [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) name *Valariande*<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and *Ossiriande*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The book *[The Road Goes Ever On](<../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book).md>)* gives the Quenya name of Lothlórien as <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=L%C3%B3riende&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lóriende (page does not exist)">Lóriende</a></i>.

The above hint that in Quenya the endings were preserved as *-iande* and *-iende*, without loss of final *-e*. On the other hand, *Valariande* is perhaps non-canon as early Qenya (but consistent to the later rules of phonology); as for *Lóriende*, it seems to be constructed after the Sindarin name and can not be indicative of Quenya etymology.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
