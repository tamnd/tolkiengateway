[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1931" title="wikipedia:1931">1931</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[25](25_February.md)** - [Fraser Kerr](../F/Fraser_Kerr.md), who would voice [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>), is born.

## March {#March}

- **[16](16_March.md)** - [Anthony Kenny](../A/Anthony_Kenny.md) is born.
- **[26](26_March.md)** - [Leonard Nimoy](../L/Leonard_Nimoy.md), who would reach cult status both as Dr. Spock and with *[The Ballad of Bilbo Baggins](../T/The_Ballad_of_Bilbo_Baggins.md)*, is born.

## May {#May}

- **[11](11_May.md)** - Artist [Angus McBride](../A/Angus_McBride.md) is born.
- **[20](20_May.md)** - Actor [Michael Spice](../M/Michael_Spice.md) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[14](14_July.md)** - [Robert Stephens](../R/Robert_Stephens.md), who would voice [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) in [the radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), is born.

## September {#September}

- **[12](12_September.md)** - [Ian Holm](../I/Ian_Holm.md), who would voice [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) in the [BBC radio adaptation](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) and play [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.
- **[19](19_September.md)** - [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) invited [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [Hugo Dyson](../H/Hugo_Dyson.md) to dine with him in Magdalen. A conversation followed that ran until 4 a.m. [the following morning](20_September.md) that was essential to Lewis's conversion to [Christianity](../C/Christianity.md).
- **[25](25_September.md)** - [Peter Woodthorpe](../P/Peter_Woodthorpe.md), who would voice [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) in both [The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>), and [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) abandons *[The Lay of Leithian](../T/The_Lay_of_Leithian.md)* and sends it to [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) for review.

## October {#October}

- **[15](15_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem "[Progress in Bimble Town](../P/Progress_in_Bimble_Town.md)" is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.
- **[30](30_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is appointed supervisor for B.Litt student [A.F. Colborn](../A/A.F._Colborn.md).
- **[31](31_October.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## December {#December}

- **[23](23_December.md)** - Father Christmas writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- Gabriel Tolkien, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s first nephew is born.
