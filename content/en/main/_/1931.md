[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1931" title="wikipedia:1931">1931</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[25](/wiki/25_February "25 February")** - [Fraser Kerr](/wiki/Fraser_Kerr), who would voice [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"), is born.

## March {#March}

- **[16](/wiki/16_March "16 March")** - [Anthony Kenny](/wiki/Anthony_Kenny) is born.
- **[26](/wiki/26_March "26 March")** - [Leonard Nimoy](/wiki/Leonard_Nimoy), who would reach cult status both as Dr. Spock and with *[The Ballad of Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Bilbo_Baggins)*, is born.

## May {#May}

- **[11](/wiki/11_May "11 May")** - Artist [Angus McBride](/wiki/Angus_McBride) is born.
- **[20](/wiki/20_May "20 May")** - Actor [Michael Spice](/wiki/Michael_Spice) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[14](/wiki/14_July "14 July")** - [Robert Stephens](/wiki/Robert_Stephens), who would voice [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in [the radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born.

## September {#September}

- **[12](/wiki/12_September "12 September")** - [Ian Holm](/wiki/Ian_Holm), who would voice [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) in the [BBC radio adaptation](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") and play [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.
- **[19](/wiki/19_September "19 September")** - [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) invited [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [Hugo Dyson](/wiki/Hugo_Dyson) to dine with him in Magdalen. A conversation followed that ran until 4 a.m. [the following morning](/wiki/20_September "20 September") that was essential to Lewis's conversion to [Christianity](/wiki/Christianity).
- **[25](/wiki/25_September "25 September")** - [Peter Woodthorpe](/wiki/Peter_Woodthorpe), who would voice [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) in both [The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)>), and [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) abandons *[The Lay of Leithian](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Leithian)* and sends it to [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) for review.

## October {#October}

- **[15](/wiki/15_October "15 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[Progress in Bimble Town](/wiki/Progress_in_Bimble_Town)" is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.
- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is appointed supervisor for B.Litt student [A.F. Colborn](/wiki/A.F._Colborn).
- **[31](/wiki/31_October "31 October")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## December {#December}

- **[23](/wiki/23_December "23 December")** - Father Christmas writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- Gabriel Tolkien, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s first nephew is born.
