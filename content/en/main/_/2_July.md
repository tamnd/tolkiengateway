Events that occurred on 2 July.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1962](1962.md)** – [J.R.R.](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [Edith Tolkien](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) go on a trip to [Bournemouth](../B/Bournemouth.md).
- **[1966](1966.md)** - An issue of *[Saturday Evening Post](<../S/Saturday_Evening_Post_(2_July_1966).md>)* is published, containing the [an interview with Tolkien](../T/The_Hobbit-forming_World_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- **[1972](1972.md)** - [Mythcon 3](../M/Mythcon_3.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach,_California" title="wikipedia:Long Beach, California">Long Beach, California</a>.
- **[1976](1976.md)** - Actor [Alexander Lindsay](../A/Alexander_Lindsay.md) is born.
- **[2003](2003.md)** - "Ents of Fangorn", the sixth set in *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md)*, is released.
- **[2004](2004.md)** - [The Lord of the Rings Symphony](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony.md) at Jones Hall in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston" title="wikipedia:Houston">Houston</a>.
- **[2006](2006.md)** - [The Gathering of the Fellowship](../T/The_Gathering_of_the_Fellowship.md) convention in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto" title="wikipedia:Toronto">Toronto</a>.
- **[2006](2006.md)** - [Pieter Collier](../P/Pieter_Collier.md) is awarded the *1st Annual [Northeast Tolkien Society](../N/North_East_Tolkien_Society.md) Imperishable Flame Award 2006* for his immense collection.
- **[2020](2020.md)** - [Andy Serkis](../A/Andy_Serkis.md) publishes *The Hobbit* in audiobook form. It is now available on Audible.
