Events that occurred on 2 July.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1962](/wiki/1962)** – [J.R.R.](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") and [Edith Tolkien](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien) go on a trip to [Bournemouth](/wiki/Bournemouth).
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - An issue of *[Saturday Evening Post](</wiki/Saturday_Evening_Post_(2_July_1966)> "Saturday Evening Post (2 July 1966)")* is published, containing the [an interview with Tolkien](/wiki/The_Hobbit-forming_World_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "The Hobbit-forming World of J.R.R. Tolkien").
- **[1972](/wiki/1972)** - [Mythcon 3](/wiki/Mythcon_3) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach,_California" title="wikipedia:Long Beach, California">Long Beach, California</a>.
- **[1976](/wiki/1976)** - Actor [Alexander Lindsay](/wiki/Alexander_Lindsay) is born.
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - "Ents of Fangorn", the sixth set in *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game)*, is released.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - [The Lord of the Rings Symphony](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony) at Jones Hall in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston" title="wikipedia:Houston">Houston</a>.
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - [The Gathering of the Fellowship](/wiki/The_Gathering_of_the_Fellowship) convention in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto" title="wikipedia:Toronto">Toronto</a>.
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - [Pieter Collier](/wiki/Pieter_Collier) is awarded the *1st Annual [Northeast Tolkien Society](/wiki/North_East_Tolkien_Society "North East Tolkien Society") Imperishable Flame Award 2006* for his immense collection.
- **[2020](/wiki/2020)** - [Andy Serkis](/wiki/Andy_Serkis) publishes *The Hobbit* in audiobook form. It is now available on Audible.
