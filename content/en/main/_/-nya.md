<i><b>-nya</b></i> is a pronominal [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) suffix, 1st person singular possessive, "my"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> (e.g. **tatanya** = "my father").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> This ending seems to prefer **i** as its connecting vowel where one its needed, cf. **Anarinya** "my sun",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> so also in **hildinyar**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
