[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1906" title="wikipedia:1906">1906</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[16](/wiki/16_February "16 February")** - [Colin Hardie](/wiki/Colin_Hardie), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), is born.

## August {#August}

- **[5](/wiki/5_August "5 August")** - [John Huston](/wiki/John_Huston), who would voice [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)")* and *[The Return of the King](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)")*, is born.

## November {#November}

- **[11](/wiki/11_November "11 November")** - [Theodore Isidore Gottlieb](/wiki/Theodore_Isidore_Gottlieb), who would voice [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) in [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)") and *[The Return of the King](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)")*, is born.

## December {#December}

- **[5](/wiki/5_December "5 December")** - [Otto Preminger](/wiki/Otto_Preminger), who would play the [Elvenking](/wiki/Thranduil "Thranduil") in [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)"), is born.
