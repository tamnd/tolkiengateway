[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1945" title="wikipedia:1945">1945</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[7](/wiki/7_January "7 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 94a](/wiki/Letter_94a).
- **[9](/wiki/9_January "9 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 94b](/wiki/Letter_94b).
- **[15](/wiki/15_January "15 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 94c](/wiki/Letter_94c).
- **[18](/wiki/18_January "18 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 95](/wiki/Letter_95).
- **[21](/wiki/21_January "21 January")** - [Martin Shaw](/wiki/Martin_Shaw) is born.
- **[30](/wiki/30_January "30 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 96](/wiki/Letter_96).
- **?** - *[Leaf by Niggle](/wiki/Leaf_by_Niggle)* is published in the *[The Dublin Review](</wiki/The_Dublin_Review_(January_1945)> "The Dublin Review (January 1945)")*.

## February {#February}

- **[10](/wiki/10_February "10 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) starts writing [a letter to the *Catholic Herald*](/wiki/The_Name_Coventry "The Name Coventry").
- **[11](/wiki/11_February "11 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) finishes writing [a letter to the *Catholic Herald*](/wiki/The_Name_Coventry "The Name Coventry").
- **[11](/wiki/11_February "11 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 97](/wiki/Letter_97).
- **[17](/wiki/17_February "17 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 97a](/wiki/Letter_97a).
- **[20](/wiki/20_February "20 February")** - Tolkien joins the Oxford Dante Society.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[23](/wiki/23_February "23 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 97b](/wiki/Letter_97b).
- **[23](/wiki/23_February "23 February")** - A letter by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), "[The Name Coventry](/wiki/The_Name_Coventry)", is published in the *[Catholic Herald](</wiki/The_Catholic_Herald_(23_February_1945)> "The Catholic Herald (23 February 1945)")*.

## March {#March}

- **[12](/wiki/12_March "12 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 97c](/wiki/Letter_97c).
- **18?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 98](/wiki/Letter_98).
- **?** - [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) returns to England after serving in [South Africa](/wiki/South_Africa).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## April {#April}

- **[18](/wiki/18_April "18 April")** - [Karen Wynn Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad), amateur cartographer, is born.
- **[29](/wiki/29_April "29 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 98a](/wiki/Letter_98a).

## May {#May}

- **[15](/wiki/15_May "15 May")** - [Charles Williams](/wiki/Charles_Williams), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), dies.
- **[15](/wiki/15_May "15 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 99](/wiki/Letter_99 "Letter 99").
- **[29](/wiki/29_May "29 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 100](/wiki/Letter_100 "Letter 100").

## June {#June}

- **[2](/wiki/2_June "2 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Maegraith](/wiki/Letter_to_Maegraith "Letter to Maegraith").
- **[3](/wiki/3_June "3 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 101](/wiki/Letter_101 "Letter 101").
- **[23](/wiki/23_June "23 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is elected Merton Professor of English Language and Literature.

## August {#August}

- **[9](/wiki/9_August "9 August")** - As <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_bombings_of_Hiroshima_and_Nagasaki" title="wikipedia:Atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki">the bombs</a> are dropped on Hiroshima and Nagasaki, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") [denounces](/wiki/Letter_102 "Letter 102") these tactics as "utter folly".
- **[18](/wiki/18_August "18 August")** - [Eric Rücker Eddison](/wiki/Eric_R%C3%BCcker_Eddison) dies.

## September {#September}

- **[17](/wiki/17_September "17 September")** - [Bruce Spence](/wiki/Bruce_Spence), who would play the [Mouth of Sauron](/wiki/Mouth_of_Sauron) in the extended edition of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*, is born.

## October {#October}

- **?** - J.R.R. Tolkien is made a Fellow of Merton College.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **[7](/wiki/7_October "7 October")** - Author [Hal Colebatch](/wiki/Hal_Colebatch) is born.
- **[9](/wiki/9_October "9 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 102a](/wiki/Letter_102a).
- **[11](/wiki/11_October "11 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 103](/wiki/Letter_103 "Letter 103").
- **[22](/wiki/22_October "22 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 104](/wiki/Letter_104 "Letter 104").
- **[26](/wiki/26_October "26 October")** - Author [Jane Chance](/wiki/Jane_Chance) is born.

## December {#December}

- **[2](/wiki/2_December "2 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) submits *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth Beorhthelm's Son](/wiki/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth "The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth")* for publication.
- **?** - *[The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun)* is published in the *Welsh Review*.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien states he is putting *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* "before all else".
- **?** - Writer [Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) is born.
- **?** - Actor [Jeffrey Thomas](/wiki/Jeffrey_Thomas) is born.
