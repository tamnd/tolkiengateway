[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1945" title="wikipedia:1945">1945</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[7](7_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 94a](../L/Letter_94a.md).
- **[9](9_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 94b](../L/Letter_94b.md).
- **[15](15_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 94c](../L/Letter_94c.md).
- **[18](18_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 95](../L/Letter_95.md).
- **[21](21_January.md)** - [Martin Shaw](../M/Martin_Shaw.md) is born.
- **[30](30_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 96](../L/Letter_96.md).
- **?** - *[Leaf by Niggle](../L/Leaf_by_Niggle.md)* is published in the *[The Dublin Review](<../T/The_Dublin_Review_(January_1945).md>)*.

## February {#February}

- **[10](10_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) starts writing [a letter to the *Catholic Herald*](../T/The_Name_Coventry.md).
- **[11](11_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) finishes writing [a letter to the *Catholic Herald*](../T/The_Name_Coventry.md).
- **[11](11_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 97](../L/Letter_97.md).
- **[17](17_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 97a](../L/Letter_97a.md).
- **[20](20_February.md)** - Tolkien joins the Oxford Dante Society.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[23](23_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 97b](../L/Letter_97b.md).
- **[23](23_February.md)** - A letter by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), "[The Name Coventry](../T/The_Name_Coventry.md)", is published in the *[Catholic Herald](<../T/The_Catholic_Herald_(23_February_1945).md>)*.

## March {#March}

- **[12](12_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 97c](../L/Letter_97c.md).
- **18?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 98](../L/Letter_98.md).
- **?** - [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) returns to England after serving in [South Africa](../S/South_Africa.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## April {#April}

- **[18](18_April.md)** - [Karen Wynn Fonstad](../K/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad.md), amateur cartographer, is born.
- **[29](29_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 98a](../L/Letter_98a.md).

## May {#May}

- **[15](15_May.md)** - [Charles Williams](../C/Charles_Williams.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), dies.
- **[15](15_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 99](../L/Letter_99.md).
- **[29](29_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 100](../L/Letter_100.md).

## June {#June}

- **[2](2_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Maegraith](../L/Letter_to_Maegraith.md).
- **[3](3_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 101](../L/Letter_101.md).
- **[23](23_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is elected Merton Professor of English Language and Literature.

## August {#August}

- **[9](9_August.md)** - As <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_bombings_of_Hiroshima_and_Nagasaki" title="wikipedia:Atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki">the bombs</a> are dropped on Hiroshima and Nagasaki, [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) [denounces](../L/Letter_102.md) these tactics as "utter folly".
- **[18](18_August.md)** - [Eric Rücker Eddison](../E/Eric_Rücker_Eddison.md) dies.

## September {#September}

- **[17](17_September.md)** - [Bruce Spence](../B/Bruce_Spence.md), who would play the [Mouth of Sauron](../M/Mouth_of_Sauron.md) in the extended edition of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*, is born.

## October {#October}

- **?** - J.R.R. Tolkien is made a Fellow of Merton College.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **[7](7_October.md)** - Author [Hal Colebatch](../H/Hal_Colebatch.md) is born.
- **[9](9_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 102a](../L/Letter_102a.md).
- **[11](11_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 103](../L/Letter_103.md).
- **[22](22_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 104](../L/Letter_104.md).
- **[26](26_October.md)** - Author [Jane Chance](../J/Jane_Chance.md) is born.

## December {#December}

- **[2](2_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) submits *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth Beorhthelm's Son](../T/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth.md)* for publication.
- **?** - *[The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun](../T/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun.md)* is published in the *Welsh Review*.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien states he is putting *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* "before all else".
- **?** - Writer [Jim Allan](../J/Jim_Allan.md) is born.
- **?** - Actor [Jeffrey Thomas](../J/Jeffrey_Thomas.md) is born.
