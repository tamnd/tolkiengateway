- **[1980](/wiki/1980)** - [Mythcon 11](/wiki/Mythcon_11) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nevada,_Reno" title="wikipedia:University of Nevada, Reno">University of Nevada</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reno,_Nevada" title="wikipedia:Reno, Nevada">Reno</a>.

- **[1991](/wiki/1991)** - [Mythcon 22](/wiki/Mythcon_22) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego" title="wikipedia:San Diego">San Diego</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
