Events that occurred on 31 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1855](1855.md)** - [Joseph Wright](../J/Joseph_Wright.md) is born.
- **[1914](1914.md)** - The Big Four - [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), [Wiseman](../C/Christopher_Wiseman.md), [Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md) and [Gilson](../R/Robert_Gilson.md) - plan to meet at [Cambridge](../C/Cambridge.md), and all but Tolkien come. Now under the lead of [Barnsley](../T/Thomas_Kenneth_Barnsley.md) and [Barrowclough](../S/Sidney_Barrowclough.md), the [T.C.B.S.](../T/T.C.B.S..md) begins to crumble.
- **[1931](1931.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[1943](1943.md)** - Author [J.E.A. Tyler](../J/J.E.A._Tyler.md) is born.
- **[1948](1948.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 117](../L/Letter_117.md).
- **[1954](1954.md)** – "[The Hero Is a Hobbit](../T/The_Hero_Is_a_Hobbit.md)", a review by [W.H. Auden](../W/W.H._Auden.md) of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, is published.
- **[1961](1961.md)** - Director [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) is born in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pukerua_Bay" title="wikipedia:Pukerua Bay">Pukerua Bay</a>, [New Zealand](../N/New_Zealand.md).
- **[1964](1964.md)** - The fourth episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- <b><a href="1967.md" title="1967">1967</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book).md" title="The Road Goes Ever On (book)">The Road Goes Ever On</a></i> is first published in the United States.</b>
- **[1970](1970.md)** - Actor [Nolan North](../N/Nolan_North.md) is born.
- **[1974](1974.md)** - Artist [Randy Gallegos](../R/Randy_Gallegos.md) is born.
- **[2001](2001.md)** - *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](../T/Tyalië_Tyelelliéva.md)* issue [17](../T/Tyalië_Tyelelliéva_17.md) is published.
- **[2005](2005.md)** - [RingCon](../R/RingCon.md) V at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SeaTac,_Washington" title="wikipedia:SeaTac, Washington">SeaTac, Washington</a>.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - Mereth Aderthad XVI, or "EstelCon" of the Spanish Tolkien Society in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia" title="wikipedia:Valencia">Valencia</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.
