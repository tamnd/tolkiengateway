Events that occurred on 31 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1855](/wiki/1855)** - [Joseph Wright](/wiki/Joseph_Wright) is born.
- **[1914](/wiki/1914)** - The Big Four - [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), [Wiseman](/wiki/Christopher_Wiseman "Christopher Wiseman"), [Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith "Geoffrey Bache Smith") and [Gilson](/wiki/Robert_Gilson "Robert Gilson") - plan to meet at [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge), and all but Tolkien come. Now under the lead of [Barnsley](/wiki/Thomas_Kenneth_Barnsley "Thomas Kenneth Barnsley") and [Barrowclough](/wiki/Sidney_Barrowclough "Sidney Barrowclough"), the [T.C.B.S.](/wiki/T.C.B.S.) begins to crumble.
- **[1931](/wiki/1931)** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **[1943](/wiki/1943)** - Author [J.E.A. Tyler](/wiki/J.E.A._Tyler) is born.
- **[1948](/wiki/1948)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 117](/wiki/Letter_117).
- **[1954](/wiki/1954)** – "[The Hero Is a Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hero_Is_a_Hobbit)", a review by [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden) of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, is published.
- **[1961](/wiki/1961)** - Director [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) is born in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pukerua_Bay" title="wikipedia:Pukerua Bay">Pukerua Bay</a>, [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand).
- **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - The fourth episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- <b><a href="/wiki/1967" title="1967">1967</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)" title="The Road Goes Ever On (book)">The Road Goes Ever On</a></i> is first published in the United States.</b>
- **[1970](/wiki/1970)** - Actor [Nolan North](/wiki/Nolan_North) is born.
- **[1974](/wiki/1974)** - Artist [Randy Gallegos](/wiki/Randy_Gallegos) is born.
- **[2001](/wiki/2001)** - *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va)* issue [17](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va_17 "Tyalië Tyelelliéva 17") is published.
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - [RingCon](/wiki/RingCon) V at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SeaTac,_Washington" title="wikipedia:SeaTac, Washington">SeaTac, Washington</a>.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - Mereth Aderthad XVI, or "EstelCon" of the Spanish Tolkien Society in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia" title="wikipedia:Valencia">Valencia</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.
