[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021" title="wikipedia:2021">2021</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](3_January.md)** - [Birthday Toast](../B/Birthday_Toast.md#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[12](12_January.md)** - A synopsis for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* series is released.
- **[23](23_January.md)** - *[Tolkien and the Classical World](../T/Tolkien_and_the_Classical_World.md)* is published.
- **[25](25_January.md)** - *[Tolkien's Modern Reading](../T/Tolkien's_Modern_Reading.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 287](../A/Amon_Hen_287.md)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[13](13_February.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) Seminar on the theme of "Twenty-first Century Receptions of Tolkien" is held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.

## March {#March}

- **[18](18_March.md)** - A new illustrated edition of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* is published.
- **[25](25_March.md)** - [Tolkien Reading Day](../T/Tolkien_Reading_Day.md).

## April {#April}

- **?** - *[Amon Hen 288](../A/Amon_Hen_288.md)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **[10](10_May.md)** - [Houghton Miffin](../H/Houghton_Mifflin_Harcourt.md) is bought by [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md).
- **[27](27_May.md)** - A new paperback edition of *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight: with Pearl and Sir Orfeo](../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl_and_Sir_Orfeo.md#2020_Deluxe_edition)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[10](10_June.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The War of the Rohirrim](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_War_of_the_Rohirrim.md)* is officially announced.
- **[10](10_June.md)** - *[The Science of Middle-earth](<../T/The_Science_of_Middle-earth_(2021_book).md>)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 289](../A/Amon_Hen_289.md)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[3](3_July.md) - [4](4_July.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) Seminar on the theme of "Tolkien and Diversity" is held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.
- **[29](29_July.md)** - *[Switzerland in Tolkien's Middle-Earth](../S/Switzerland_in_Tolkien's_Middle-Earth.md)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[3](3_August.md)** - Filming for the first season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* is wrapped.
- **[10](10_August.md)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2022](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2022.md)* is published.
- **[19](19_August.md)** - *[The Gallant Edith Bratt](../T/The_Gallant_Edith_Bratt.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 290](../A/Amon_Hen_290.md)* is published.

## September {#September}

- <b><a href="2_September.md" title="2 September">2</a> - <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../T/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth%253A_Late_Writings_on_the_Lands,_Inhabitants,_and_Metaphysics_of_Middle-earth.md" title="The Nature of Middle-earth: Late Writings on the Lands, Inhabitants, and Metaphysics of Middle-earth">The Nature of Middle-earth: Late Writings on the Lands, Inhabitants, and Metaphysics of Middle-earth</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[2](2_September.md) - [5](5_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2021](../O/Oxonmoot_2021.md) held online and at <a class="external text" href="https://www.st-annes.ox.ac.uk/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Anne&#39;s College, Oxford</a>.
- **[16](16_September.md)** - A new audiobook of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* narrated by [Andy Serkis](../A/Andy_Serkis.md) is released digitally.
- **[23](23_September.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Rise to War](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Rise_to_War.md)* is released online.

## October {#October}

- **[14](14_October.md)** - A new edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is published, including for the first time 30 images and sketches by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- **[14](14_October.md)** - A new audiobook of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* narrated by [Andy Serkis](../A/Andy_Serkis.md) is released physically.
- **[30](30_October.md)** - [Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game](../M/Middle-earth_Strategy_Battle_Game.md): Fall of the Necromancer is released.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 291](../A/Amon_Hen_291.md)* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[6](6_November.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) Seminar on the theme of "Translating and Illustrating Tolkien" is held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.
- **[10](10_November.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Fate of Gundabad](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Fate_of_Gundabad.md)* expansion is released.
- **[11](11_November.md)** - *[Pictures by J.R.R. Tolkien](../P/Pictures_by_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* is republished.

## December {#December}

- **[2](2_December.md)** - British actor [Antony Sher](../A/Antony_Sher.md) dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 292](../A/Amon_Hen_292.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 62](../M/Mallorn_62.md)* is publsihed.
