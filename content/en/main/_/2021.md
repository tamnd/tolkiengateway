[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021" title="wikipedia:2021">2021</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[12](/wiki/12_January "12 January")** - A synopsis for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* series is released.
- **[23](/wiki/23_January "23 January")** - *[Tolkien and the Classical World](/wiki/Tolkien_and_the_Classical_World)* is published.
- **[25](/wiki/25_January "25 January")** - *[Tolkien's Modern Reading](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Modern_Reading)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 287](/wiki/Amon_Hen_287)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[13](/wiki/13_February "13 February")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) Seminar on the theme of "Twenty-first Century Receptions of Tolkien" is held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.

## March {#March}

- **[18](/wiki/18_March "18 March")** - A new illustrated edition of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* is published.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).

## April {#April}

- **?** - *[Amon Hen 288](/wiki/Amon_Hen_288)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **[10](/wiki/10_May "10 May")** - [Houghton Miffin](/wiki/Houghton_Mifflin_Harcourt "Houghton Mifflin Harcourt") is bought by [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins).
- **[27](/wiki/27_May "27 May")** - A new paperback edition of *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight: with Pearl and Sir Orfeo](/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl_and_Sir_Orfeo#2020_Deluxe_edition "Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, Pearl and Sir Orfeo")* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[10](/wiki/10_June "10 June")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The War of the Rohirrim](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_War_of_the_Rohirrim)* is officially announced.
- **[10](/wiki/10_June "10 June")** - *[The Science of Middle-earth](</wiki/The_Science_of_Middle-earth_(2021_book)> "The Science of Middle-earth (2021 book)")* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 289](/wiki/Amon_Hen_289)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[3](/wiki/3_July "3 July") - [4](/wiki/4_July "4 July")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) Seminar on the theme of "Tolkien and Diversity" is held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.
- **[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")** - *[Switzerland in Tolkien's Middle-Earth](/wiki/Switzerland_in_Tolkien%27s_Middle-Earth)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[3](/wiki/3_August "3 August")** - Filming for the first season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* is wrapped.
- **[10](/wiki/10_August "10 August")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2022](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2022)* is published.
- **[19](/wiki/19_August "19 August")** - *[The Gallant Edith Bratt](/wiki/The_Gallant_Edith_Bratt)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 290](/wiki/Amon_Hen_290)* is published.

## September {#September}

- <b><a href="/wiki/2_September" title="2 September">2</a> - <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth:_Late_Writings_on_the_Lands,_Inhabitants,_and_Metaphysics_of_Middle-earth" title="The Nature of Middle-earth: Late Writings on the Lands, Inhabitants, and Metaphysics of Middle-earth">The Nature of Middle-earth: Late Writings on the Lands, Inhabitants, and Metaphysics of Middle-earth</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[2](/wiki/2_September "2 September") - [5](/wiki/5_September "5 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2021](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2021) held online and at <a class="external text" href="https://www.st-annes.ox.ac.uk/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Anne&#39;s College, Oxford</a>.
- **[16](/wiki/16_September "16 September")** - A new audiobook of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* narrated by [Andy Serkis](/wiki/Andy_Serkis) is released digitally.
- **[23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Rise to War](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Rise_to_War)* is released online.

## October {#October}

- **[14](/wiki/14_October "14 October")** - A new edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is published, including for the first time 30 images and sketches by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- **[14](/wiki/14_October "14 October")** - A new audiobook of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* narrated by [Andy Serkis](/wiki/Andy_Serkis) is released physically.
- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")** - [Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Strategy_Battle_Game): Fall of the Necromancer is released.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 291](/wiki/Amon_Hen_291)* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) Seminar on the theme of "Translating and Illustrating Tolkien" is held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.
- **[10](/wiki/10_November "10 November")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Fate of Gundabad](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Fate_of_Gundabad)* expansion is released.
- **[11](/wiki/11_November "11 November")** - *[Pictures by J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Pictures_by_J.R.R._Tolkien)* is republished.

## December {#December}

- **[2](/wiki/2_December "2 December")** - British actor [Antony Sher](/wiki/Antony_Sher) dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 292](/wiki/Amon_Hen_292)* is published.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 62](/wiki/Mallorn_62)* is publsihed.
