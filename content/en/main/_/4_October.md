Events that occurred on 4 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1915](/wiki/1915)** - Translator [Tore Zetterholm](/wiki/Tore_Zetterholm) is born.
- **[1953](/wiki/1953)** - Actor [Chris Fairbank](/wiki/Chris_Fairbank) is born.
- **[1961](/wiki/1961)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 231](/wiki/Letter_231).
- **[1965](/wiki/1965)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Agatha Carew Hunt](/wiki/Letter_to_Agatha_Carew_Hunt "Letter to Agatha Carew Hunt").
- **[1979](/wiki/1979)** - The *[Jackanory](/wiki/Jackanory)* episode "Out of the Frying-Pan and Into the Fire" is aired on the [BBC](/wiki/BBC).
- **[1999](/wiki/1999)** - *[Poems from The Hobbit](/wiki/Poems_from_The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** - [Ring\*Con 2009](/wiki/Ring*Con_2009) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn" title="wikipedia:Bonn">Bonn</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany" title="wikipedia:Germany">Germany</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - Together with [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") the [four hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") travel towards [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop).
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) hurries towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) after the [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") attacked him on [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
