[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1969" title="wikipedia:1969">1969</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[2](/wiki/2_January "2 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes letters [308](/wiki/Letter_308 "Letter 308") and [309](/wiki/Letter_309 "Letter 309").
- **[6](/wiki/6_January "6 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Paula Coston](</wiki/Letter_to_Paula_Coston_(6_January_1969)> "Letter to Paula Coston (6 January 1969)").
- **[11](/wiki/11_January "11 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 309a](/wiki/Letter_309a "Letter 309a").
- **[27](/wiki/27_January "27 January")** - Actor and author [Cliff Broadway](/wiki/Cliff_Broadway) is born.
- **?** - The first issue of [Mythlore](/wiki/Mythlore_1 "Mythlore 1") is published.

## February {#February}

- **[3](/wiki/3_February "3 February")** - [Shane Rangi](/wiki/Shane_Rangi), who would play several characters and lead the stunt crew of [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.
- **?** - [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo), Tolkien Linguist, is born.

## March {#March}

- **[6](/wiki/6_March "6 March")** - Scholar [Gergely Nagy](/wiki/Gergely_Nagy) is born.
- **[13](/wiki/13_March "13 March")** - [Adam Tolkien](/wiki/Adam_Tolkien) is born.
- **[15](/wiki/15_March "15 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Amy Ronald](</wiki/Letter_to_Amy_Ronald_(15_March_1969)> "Letter to Amy Ronald (15 March 1969)").
- **[20](/wiki/20_March "20 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Amy Ronald](</wiki/Letter_to_Amy_Ronald_(20_March_1969)> "Letter to Amy Ronald (20 March 1969)").
- **?** - *[Tolkien: A Look Behind The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Tolkien:_A_Look_Behind_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[8](/wiki/8_April "8 April")** - Actor [Ian Hughes](/wiki/Ian_Hughes) is born.
- **?** - [Mythlore 2](/wiki/Mythlore_2) is published.

## May {#May}

- **[7](/wiki/7_May "7 May")** - Producer [Rick Porras](/wiki/Rick_Porras) is born.
- **[14](/wiki/14_May "14 May")** - [Cate Blanchett](/wiki/Cate_Blanchett), who would play [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.
- **[20](/wiki/20_May "20 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 310](/wiki/Letter_310 "Letter 310").

## June {#June}

- **[30](/wiki/30_June "30 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Paul Bibire](/wiki/Letter_to_Paul_Bibire "Letter to Paul Bibire").

## July {#July}

- **[8](/wiki/8_July "8 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is diagnosed with an inflamed or diseased gall-bladder. Dr. Denis Tolhurst forbids him the consummation of fats (notably butter) and, in the eyes of the patient even worse, alcohol.
- **[16](/wiki/16_July "16 July")** - [Royd Tolkien](/wiki/Royd_Tolkien) is born.
- **[19](/wiki/19_July "19 July")** - Voice actress [Courtenay Taylor](/wiki/Courtenay_Taylor) is born.
- **[22](/wiki/22_July "22 July")** - Actor [James Arnold Taylor](/wiki/James_Arnold_Taylor) is born.
- **[31](/wiki/31_July "31 July")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 311](/wiki/Letter_311).

## August {#August}

- **[1](/wiki/1_August "1 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mr Burrows](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Burrows "Letter to Mr Burrows").
- **[5](/wiki/5_August "5 August")** - Actress [Kim Mai Guest](/wiki/Kim_Mai_Guest) is born.
- **[6](/wiki/6_August "6 August")** - [Chris Edgerly](/wiki/Chris_Edgerly), who would voice various characters in [EA](/wiki/Electronic_Arts "Electronic Arts") video games, is born.

## September {#September}

- **[29](/wiki/29_September "29 September")** - Artist [Warren Mahy](/wiki/Warren_Mahy) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[1](/wiki/1_October "1 October")** - Actor [Charles Edwards](/wiki/Charles_Edwards) is born.
- **?** - The anthology *[The Young Magicians](/wiki/The_Young_Magicians)*, which includes two poems by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **?** - *[‎J.R.R. Tolkien](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_(by_Catherine_Stimpson)> "J.R.R. Tolkien (by Catherine Stimpson)")*, a study by Catherine R. Stimpson, is published.‎
- **[10](/wiki/10_October "10 October")** - Actor [Manu Bennett](/wiki/Manu_Bennett) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - Informal date of foundation of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society).
- **[7](/wiki/7_November "7 November")** - [Vera Chapman](/wiki/Vera_Chapman) announces the foundation of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) in the *New Statesman*.
- **[16](/wiki/16_November "16 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 312](/wiki/Letter_312 "Letter 312").
- **[25](/wiki/25_November "25 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 313](/wiki/Letter_313 "Letter 313").

## December {#December}

- **[15](/wiki/15_December "15 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 314](/wiki/Letter_314 "Letter 314").

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Charles Leslie Wrenn](/wiki/Charles_Leslie_Wrenn), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), dies.
- **?** - *[Bored of the Rings](</wiki/Bored_of_the_Rings_(book)> "Bored of the Rings (book)")* is published.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) sells the film, games, and merchandising rights of the books [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit) and [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) for $250,000 to [United Artists](/wiki/United_Artists).
- **?** - First deluxe, one-volume edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* by [Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Allen_%26_Unwin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
