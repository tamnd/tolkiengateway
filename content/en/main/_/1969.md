[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1969" title="wikipedia:1969">1969</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[2](2_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes letters [308](../L/Letter_308.md) and [309](../L/Letter_309.md).
- **[6](6_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Paula Coston](<../L/Letter_to_Paula_Coston_(6_January_1969).md>).
- **[11](11_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 309a](../L/Letter_309a.md).
- **[27](27_January.md)** - Actor and author [Cliff Broadway](../C/Cliff_Broadway.md) is born.
- **?** - The first issue of [Mythlore](../M/Mythlore_1.md) is published.

## February {#February}

- **[3](3_February.md)** - [Shane Rangi](../S/Shane_Rangi.md), who would play several characters and lead the stunt crew of [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.
- **?** - [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md), Tolkien Linguist, is born.

## March {#March}

- **[6](6_March.md)** - Scholar [Gergely Nagy](../G/Gergely_Nagy.md) is born.
- **[13](13_March.md)** - [Adam Tolkien](../A/Adam_Tolkien.md) is born.
- **[15](15_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Amy Ronald](<../L/Letter_to_Amy_Ronald_(15_March_1969).md>).
- **[20](20_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Amy Ronald](<../L/Letter_to_Amy_Ronald_(20_March_1969).md>).
- **?** - *[Tolkien: A Look Behind The Lord of the Rings](../T/Tolkien%253A_A_Look_Behind_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[8](8_April.md)** - Actor [Ian Hughes](../I/Ian_Hughes.md) is born.
- **?** - [Mythlore 2](../M/Mythlore_2.md) is published.

## May {#May}

- **[7](7_May.md)** - Producer [Rick Porras](../R/Rick_Porras.md) is born.
- **[14](14_May.md)** - [Cate Blanchett](../C/Cate_Blanchett.md), who would play [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.
- **[20](20_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 310](../L/Letter_310.md).

## June {#June}

- **[30](30_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Paul Bibire](../L/Letter_to_Paul_Bibire.md).

## July {#July}

- **[8](8_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is diagnosed with an inflamed or diseased gall-bladder. Dr. Denis Tolhurst forbids him the consummation of fats (notably butter) and, in the eyes of the patient even worse, alcohol.
- **[16](16_July.md)** - [Royd Tolkien](../R/Royd_Tolkien.md) is born.
- **[19](19_July.md)** - Voice actress [Courtenay Taylor](../C/Courtenay_Taylor.md) is born.
- **[22](22_July.md)** - Actor [James Arnold Taylor](../J/James_Arnold_Taylor.md) is born.
- **[31](31_July.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 311](../L/Letter_311.md).

## August {#August}

- **[1](1_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mr Burrows](../L/Letter_to_Mr_Burrows.md).
- **[5](5_August.md)** - Actress [Kim Mai Guest](../K/Kim_Mai_Guest.md) is born.
- **[6](6_August.md)** - [Chris Edgerly](../C/Chris_Edgerly.md), who would voice various characters in [EA](../E/Electronic_Arts.md) video games, is born.

## September {#September}

- **[29](29_September.md)** - Artist [Warren Mahy](../W/Warren_Mahy.md) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[1](1_October.md)** - Actor [Charles Edwards](../C/Charles_Edwards.md) is born.
- **?** - The anthology *[The Young Magicians](../T/The_Young_Magicians.md)*, which includes two poems by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.
- **?** - *[‎J.R.R. Tolkien](<../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_(by_Catherine_Stimpson).md>)*, a study by Catherine R. Stimpson, is published.‎
- **[10](10_October.md)** - Actor [Manu Bennett](../M/Manu_Bennett.md) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[6](6_November.md)** - Informal date of foundation of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md).
- **[7](7_November.md)** - [Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md) announces the foundation of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) in the *New Statesman*.
- **[16](16_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 312](../L/Letter_312.md).
- **[25](25_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 313](../L/Letter_313.md).

## December {#December}

- **[15](15_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 314](../L/Letter_314.md).

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Charles Leslie Wrenn](../C/Charles_Leslie_Wrenn.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), dies.
- **?** - *[Bored of the Rings](<../B/Bored_of_the_Rings_(book).md>)* is published.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) sells the film, games, and merchandising rights of the books [The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md) and [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) for $250,000 to [United Artists](../U/United_Artists.md).
- **?** - First deluxe, one-volume edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* by [Allen &amp; Unwin](../A/Allen_&_Unwin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
