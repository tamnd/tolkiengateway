[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1902" title="wikipedia:1902">1902</a></b>.

## Early {#Early}

- **?** - [Mabel Tolkien](../M/Mabel_Suffield.md) moves her family, from <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=King%27s_Heath&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="King&#39;s Heath (page does not exist)">King&#39;s Heath</a> to a house in <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Edgbaston&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Edgbaston (page does not exist)">Edgbaston</a> next door to the [Birmingham Oratory](../B/Birmingham_Oratory.md) and the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Grammar_School_of_St._Philip&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Grammar School of St. Philip (page does not exist)">Grammar School of St. Philip</a>. To save money, Mabel removes the boys from [King Edward's School](../K/King_Edward's_School.md) and enrolls them in St. Philips.

## April {#April}

- **[9](9_April.md)** - [Lord David Cecil](../L/Lord_David_Cecil.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), is born.
- **[13](13_April.md)** - [Godfrey Kenton](../G/Godfrey_Kenton.md), the voice of [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and [Mablung](<../M/Mablung_(ranger_of_Ithilien).md>) in the [1955 radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>)*, is born.*

## June {#June}

- **[11](11_June.md)** - Illustrator [Eric Fraser](../E/Eric_Fraser.md) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[26](26_September.md)** - [Clyde S. Kilby](../C/Clyde_S._Kilby.md), literature professor, is born.
