[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1902" title="wikipedia:1902">1902</a></b>.

## Early {#Early}

- **?** - [Mabel Tolkien](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield "Mabel Suffield") moves her family, from <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=King%27s_Heath&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="King&#39;s Heath (page does not exist)">King&#39;s Heath</a> to a house in <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Edgbaston&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Edgbaston (page does not exist)">Edgbaston</a> next door to the [Birmingham Oratory](/wiki/Birmingham_Oratory) and the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Grammar_School_of_St._Philip&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Grammar School of St. Philip (page does not exist)">Grammar School of St. Philip</a>. To save money, Mabel removes the boys from [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School) and enrolls them in St. Philips.

## April {#April}

- **[9](/wiki/9_April "9 April")** - [Lord David Cecil](/wiki/Lord_David_Cecil), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), is born.
- **[13](/wiki/13_April "13 April")** - [Godfrey Kenton](/wiki/Godfrey_Kenton), the voice of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and [Mablung](</wiki/Mablung_(ranger_of_Ithilien)> "Mablung (ranger of Ithilien)") in the [1955 radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)")*, is born.*

## June {#June}

- **[11](/wiki/11_June "11 June")** - Illustrator [Eric Fraser](/wiki/Eric_Fraser) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[26](/wiki/26_September "26 September")** - [Clyde S. Kilby](/wiki/Clyde_S._Kilby), literature professor, is born.
