<i><b>-lto</b></i> is a pronominal [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) suffix meaning "they", occurring in *Fíriel's Song* (**meldielto** "they are beloved" and **cárielto** "they made").<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> Also, **tulielto** "they have come".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
