**ÁLAT-** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "large, great in size".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *alta*; prefix:<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> *alat-*

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *Alataire* (see [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer#Etymology))
