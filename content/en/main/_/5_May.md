Events that occurred on 5 May.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1917](/wiki/1917)** - [Edith Tolkien](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien) writes her first letter to her husband at Thirtle Bridge army camp, near Withernsea from her new lodgings in Hornsea. The letter was re-directed to Hornsea Musketry Camp.
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - Actor [John Rhys-Davies](/wiki/John_Rhys-Davies) is born.
- **[1947](/wiki/1947)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 107a](/wiki/Letter_107a).
- **[1958](/wiki/1958)** - Author [James Branch Cabell](/wiki/James_Branch_Cabell) dies.
- **[1960](/wiki/1960)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 222f](/wiki/Letter_222f "Letter 222f").
- <b><a href="/wiki/1977" title="1977">1977</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography" title="J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography">J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography</a></i> is first published in the UK.</b>
- <b><a href="/wiki/2009" title="2009">2009</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudr%C3%BAn" title="The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún">The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - The <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America%27s_Next_Top_Model" title="wikipedia:America&#39;s Next Top Model">America&#39;s Next Top Model</a></i> episode "[Hobbits vs. Models](/wiki/Hobbits_vs._Models)" premières on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_CW_Television_Network" title="wikipedia:The CW Television Network">The CW</a>.
- **[2011](/wiki/2011)** - *[English and Medieval Studies Presented to J.R.R. Tolkien on the Occasion of his Seventieth Birthday](/wiki/English_and_Medieval_Studies_Presented_to_J.R.R._Tolkien_on_the_Occasion_of_his_Seventieth_Birthday)* is made available as a print-on-demand publication from [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins).
- **[2024](/wiki/2024)** - Actor [Bernard Hill](/wiki/Bernard_Hill), who portrayed [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) in [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), dies.
