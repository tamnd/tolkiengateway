Events that occurred on 4 July.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1966](1966.md)** – *[The Jerusalem Bible](../T/The_Jerusalem_Bible.md)* is published.
- **[1972](1972.md)** – [Letter 339](../L/Letter_339.md) is published in the *Daily Telegraph*.
- **[1972](1972.md)** - [Mythcon 3](../M/Mythcon_3.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach,_California" title="wikipedia:Long Beach, California">Long Beach, California</a> ends.
- **[2006](2006.md)** - [The Gathering of the Fellowship](../T/The_Gathering_of_the_Fellowship.md) convention in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto" title="wikipedia:Toronto">Toronto</a>.
- **[2015](2015.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) Seminar at the Hilton, [Leeds](../L/Leeds.md).
- **[2020](2020.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) Seminar is held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) sets out from [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) to ask for counsel at [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).
