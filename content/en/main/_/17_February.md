Events that occurred on 17 February.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1894](1894.md)** - [Hilary Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s brother, is born.
- **[1919](1919.md)** - Hilary Tolkien returns home after [the War](../W/World_War_I.md).
- **[1934](1934.md)** - Actor [Barry Humphries](../B/Barry_Humphries.md) is born.
- **[1938](1938.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 23](../L/Letter_23.md).
- **[1945](1945.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 97a](../L/Letter_97a.md).
- **[1958](1958.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes to [Wordsworth](<../L/Letter_to_Wordsworth_(17_February_1958).md>).
- **[1963](1963.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Julie Cape](../L/Letter_to_Julie_Cape.md).
- **[1978](1978.md)** - Actor [Rory Michael Kinnear](../R/Rory_Michael_Kinnear.md) is born.
- **[1979](1979.md)** - Composer [Bear McCreary](../B/Bear_McCreary.md) is born.
- **[1991](1991.md)** – The third day of the first "Colloquium on the Languages of Middle-earth" at the house of [E.L.F.](../E/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship.md) member [Bill Welden](../B/Bill_Welden.md).
- **[1991](1991.md)** – Singer [Ed Sheeran](../E/Ed_Sheeran.md) is born.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) travels south down the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).
    - [Gwaihir](../G/Gwaihir.md) bears [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) to [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).
