<i><b>-nna</b></i> means "to, upon" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), allative ending in **cilyanna**, **Endorenna**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> **Elendilenna**, **Elenna**,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> **númenna**, **númenórenna**, **rómenna**, **tielyanna**. Plural <i><b>-nnar</b></i> in **mannar**, **valannar**.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> <span></span>
