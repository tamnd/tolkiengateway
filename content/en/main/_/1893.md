[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1893" title="wikipedia:1893">1893</a></b>.

## Summer (southern hemisphere) {#Summer_(southern_hemisphere)}

- [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) spends the cool hours in the family garden usually watching his father plant vines or trees.
- Ronald is bitten by a tarantula and runs away in terror, his nurse grabs him and sucks the poison out.
- He shows interest in drawing, usually scribbling with pencil and paper.
- He is bothered by teething and his health suffers in the heat.

## April {#April}

- **[8](8_April.md)** - [Vincent Trought](../V/Vincent_Trought.md), a member of the [T.C.B.S.](../T/T.C.B.S..md), is born.
- **[20](20_April.md)** - [Christopher Wiseman](../C/Christopher_Wiseman.md), the only of Tolkien's friends that survived World War I, is born.

## October {#October}

- **[25](25_October.md)** - [Robert Gilson](../R/Robert_Gilson.md), childhood friend of Tolkien, is born.
