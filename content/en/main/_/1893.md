[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1893" title="wikipedia:1893">1893</a></b>.

## Summer (southern hemisphere) {#Summer_(southern_hemisphere)}

- [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) spends the cool hours in the family garden usually watching his father plant vines or trees.
- Ronald is bitten by a tarantula and runs away in terror, his nurse grabs him and sucks the poison out.
- He shows interest in drawing, usually scribbling with pencil and paper.
- He is bothered by teething and his health suffers in the heat.

## April {#April}

- **[8](/wiki/8_April "8 April")** - [Vincent Trought](/wiki/Vincent_Trought), a member of the [T.C.B.S.](/wiki/T.C.B.S.), is born.
- **[20](/wiki/20_April "20 April")** - [Christopher Wiseman](/wiki/Christopher_Wiseman), the only of Tolkien's friends that survived World War I, is born.

## October {#October}

- **[25](/wiki/25_October "25 October")** - [Robert Gilson](/wiki/Robert_Gilson), childhood friend of Tolkien, is born.
