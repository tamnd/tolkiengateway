<i><b>-vëa</b></i> is an adjectival ending with the specific meaning "-like" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). The plural is <i><b>-vië</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- **elvëa** = "starlike" (**él** = "star"; the long é in él becomes short before the cluster *lv*)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
