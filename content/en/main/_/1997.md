[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997" title="wikipedia:1997">1997</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[6](6_January.md)** - A new edition of *[The Monsters and the Critics](../T/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics.md)* is published.

## March {#March}

- **[20](20_March.md)** - Translator [Britt G. Hallqvist](../B/Britt_G._Hallqvist.md) dies.

## April {#April}

- **[25](25_April.md)** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](../T/Tyalie_Tyelellieva.md)* issue 10 is published.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 38](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_38.md) is published.

## May {#May}

- **[9](9_May.md)-[11](11_May.md)** - [Bree Moot 3](../B/Bree_Moot_3.md).

## July {#July}

- **[23](23_July.md)** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](../T/Tyalie_Tyelellieva.md)* issue 11 is published.

## August {#August}

- **[1](1_August.md)** - The German Tolkien Society [Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft e.V.](../D/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft_e.V..md) is founded.
- **[8](8_August.md)-[11](11_August.md)** - [Mythcon 28](../M/Mythcon_28.md).
- **[18](18_August.md)** - The audiobook *[Pearl and Sir Orfeo](<../P/Pearl_and_Sir_Orfeo_(audiobook).md>)* by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), read by Terry Jones, is published.
- **?** - Production begins for *[The Lord of the Rings (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)*.

## September {#September}

- **[19](19_September.md)-[21](21_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1997](../O/Oxonmoot_1997.md).
- **[30](30_September.md)** - The album *[An Evening in Rivendell](../A/An_Evening_in_Rivendell.md)* is released.
- **?** - *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* [audiobook](../A/Audiobooks.md) topped the bestseller list for audiobooks for children.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 35](../M/Mallorn_35.md) is published.

## October {#October}

- **[20](20_October.md)** - Actor [John Bell](../J/John_Bell.md) is born.
- **[24](24_October.md)** - [Don Messick](../D/Don_Messick.md), who voiced [Balin](../B/Balin.md) and the [Lord of the Eagles](../G/Gwaihir.md) in [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>), dies.

## November {#November}

- **[13](13_November.md)** - [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) sends a parcel containing <a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0116344/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">&#34;Forgotten Silver&#34;</a> and <a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0110005/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">&#34;Heavenly Creatures&#34;</a>, and a letter explaining [an upcoming movie project](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) to [Alan Lee](../A/Alan_Lee.md).
- **[13](13_November.md)** - [The Brazilian Tolkien Society](../T/The_Brazilian_Tolkien_Society.md) is founded.

## December {#December}

- **[14](14_December.md)** - [Owen Barfield](../O/Owen_Barfield.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), dies.
