[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997" title="wikipedia:1997">1997</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[6](/wiki/6_January "6 January")** - A new edition of *[The Monsters and the Critics](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics)* is published.

## March {#March}

- **[20](/wiki/20_March "20 March")** - Translator [Britt G. Hallqvist](/wiki/Britt_G._Hallqvist) dies.

## April {#April}

- **[25](/wiki/25_April "25 April")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 10 is published.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 38](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_38) is published.

## May {#May}

- **[9](/wiki/9_May "9 May")-[11](/wiki/11_May "11 May")** - [Bree Moot 3](/wiki/Bree_Moot_3).

## July {#July}

- **[23](/wiki/23_July "23 July")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 11 is published.

## August {#August}

- **[1](/wiki/1_August "1 August")** - The German Tolkien Society [Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft e.V.](/wiki/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft_e.V.) is founded.
- **[8](/wiki/8_August "8 August")-[11](/wiki/11_August "11 August")** - [Mythcon 28](/wiki/Mythcon_28).
- **[18](/wiki/18_August "18 August")** - The audiobook *[Pearl and Sir Orfeo](</wiki/Pearl_and_Sir_Orfeo_(audiobook)> "Pearl and Sir Orfeo (audiobook)")* by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), read by Terry Jones, is published.
- **?** - Production begins for *[The Lord of the Rings (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)>)*.

## September {#September}

- **[19](/wiki/19_September "19 September")-[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")** - [Oxonmoot 1997](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1997).
- **[30](/wiki/30_September "30 September")** - The album *[An Evening in Rivendell](/wiki/An_Evening_in_Rivendell)* is released.
- **?** - *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* [audiobook](/wiki/Audiobooks "Audiobooks") topped the bestseller list for audiobooks for children.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 35](/wiki/Mallorn_35 "Mallorn 35") is published.

## October {#October}

- **[20](/wiki/20_October "20 October")** - Actor [John Bell](/wiki/John_Bell) is born.
- **[24](/wiki/24_October "24 October")** - [Don Messick](/wiki/Don_Messick), who voiced [Balin](/wiki/Balin) and the [Lord of the Eagles](/wiki/Gwaihir "Gwaihir") in [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)"), dies.

## November {#November}

- **[13](/wiki/13_November "13 November")** - [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) sends a parcel containing <a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0116344/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">&#34;Forgotten Silver&#34;</a> and <a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0110005/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">&#34;Heavenly Creatures&#34;</a>, and a letter explaining [an upcoming movie project](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") to [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee).
- **[13](/wiki/13_November "13 November")** - [The Brazilian Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Brazilian_Tolkien_Society) is founded.

## December {#December}

- **[14](/wiki/14_December "14 December")** - [Owen Barfield](/wiki/Owen_Barfield), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), dies.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](/wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm)* is published.
- **?** - *[A Question of Time](/wiki/A_Question_of_Time)* is published.
- **?** - *[News from the Shire and Beyond - Studies on Tolkien](/wiki/News_from_the_Shire_and_Beyond_-_Studies_on_Tolkien)*, the first book of the [Cormarë Series](/wiki/Cormar%C3%AB_Series), is published.
- **?** - [*The Hobbit* (1968 radio series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)") is released on CD.
- **?** - *[The Hobbit Companion](/wiki/The_Hobbit_Companion)* is published.
- **?** - Artist [Maret Kernumees](/wiki/Maret_Kernumees) dies.
- **?** - The [Middle-earth Puzzles](/wiki/Middle-earth_Puzzles) are released.
- **?** - *[Middle-earth: The Lidless Eye](/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Lidless_Eye)* card game is released.
- **?** - *[Middle-earth: Against the Shadow](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Against_the_Shadow)* card game is released.
- **?** - *[Middle-earth: The White Hand](/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_White_Hand)* card game is released.
- **?** - *[Hands of the Healer](/wiki/Hands_of_the_Healer)* is published.
