<i><b>-mo</b></i> is a masculine agentive suffix in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Isilmo" title="Isilmo">Isil<b>mo</b></a></i> = "Man of the Moon"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Ulmo" title="Ulmo">Ul<b>mo</b></a></i> = "He who pours"<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Cirya" title="Cirya">Cirya</a><b>mo</b></i> = "Mariner"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Tar-Meneldur" title="Tar-Meneldur">Elentir<b>mo</b></a></i> = "Star Watcher"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
