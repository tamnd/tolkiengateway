[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1963" title="wikipedia:1963">1963</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[11](/wiki/11_January "11 January")** - Actor [Jason Connery](/wiki/Jason_Connery) is born.

## February {#February}

- **[17](/wiki/17_February "17 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Julie Cape](/wiki/Letter_to_Julie_Cape "Letter to Julie Cape").
- **[20](/wiki/20_February "20 February")** - Author [Rainer Nagel](/wiki/Rainer_Nagel) is born.
- **[21](/wiki/21_February "21 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mrs Gill](/wiki/Letter_to_Mrs_Gill "Letter to Mrs Gill").
- **[22](/wiki/22_February "22 February")** - [Jane Neave](/wiki/Jane_Neave), aunt of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), dies.

## March {#March}

- **[10](/wiki/10_March "10 March")** - Actor [Steve Staley](/wiki/Steve_Staley) is born.
- **[11](/wiki/11_March "11 March")** - Producer [Zane Weiner](/wiki/Zane_Weiner) is born.
- **[23](/wiki/23_March "23 March")** - Mr. and Mrs. Kloos write [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_and_Mrs_Kloos "Letter to Mr and Mrs Kloos").

## April {#April}

- **[6](/wiki/6_April "6 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to a couple he met on holiday](/wiki/Letter_to_%22My_Dear_People%22 "Letter to \"My Dear People\"").
- **[18](/wiki/18_April "18 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) starts writing [a letter to Mr. and Mrs. Kloos](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_and_Mrs_Kloos "Letter to Mr and Mrs Kloos").

## May {#May}

- **[6](/wiki/6_May "6 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) finishes writing [a letter to Mr. and Mrs. Kloos](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_and_Mrs_Kloos "Letter to Mr and Mrs Kloos").

## June {#June}

- **[9](/wiki/9_June "9 June")** - Actress [Megan Edwards](/wiki/Megan_Edwards) is born.
- **[25](/wiki/25_June "25 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 245](/wiki/Letter_245 "Letter 245").

## July {#July}

- **[8](/wiki/8_July "8 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s paper "[English and Welsh](/wiki/English_and_Welsh)" is published in *[Angles and Britons: O'Donnell Lectures](/wiki/Angles_and_Britons "Angles and Britons")*, a collection of lectures from the University of Wales.

## August {#August}

- **[25](/wiki/25_August "25 August")** - Actress [Brianne Siddall](/wiki/Brianne_Siddall) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[18](/wiki/18_September "18 September")** - Writer [Gary Russell](/wiki/Gary_Russell) is born.
- **[20](/wiki/20_September "20 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 247](/wiki/Letter_247 "Letter 247").
- **[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) starts writing [a letter to Eileen Elgar](</wiki/Letter_to_Eileen_Elgar_(September_1963)> "Letter to Eileen Elgar (September 1963)").
- **[25](/wiki/25_September "25 September")** - Actor [Mikael Persbrandt](/wiki/Mikael_Persbrandt) is born.
- **[30](/wiki/30_September "30 September")** - Music producer [Carvin Knowles](/wiki/Carvin_Knowles) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[3](/wiki/3_October "3 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) posts [a letter to Eileen Elgar](</wiki/Letter_to_Eileen_Elgar_(September_1963)> "Letter to Eileen Elgar (September 1963)").
- **[5](/wiki/5_October "5 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 248](/wiki/Letter_248 "Letter 248").
- **[15](/wiki/15_October "15 October")** - While dining, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) saves the life of an old lady who nearly choked on a whiting-bone.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[16](/wiki/16_October "16 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 249](/wiki/Letter_249 "Letter 249").
- **[20](/wiki/20_October "20 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 249a](/wiki/Letter_249a "Letter 249a").
- **[21](/wiki/21_October "21 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mrs. Munby](/wiki/Letter_to_Mrs_Munby "Letter to Mrs Munby").
- **[29](/wiki/29_October "29 October")** - Actor [Jed Brophy](/wiki/Jed_Brophy) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[1](/wiki/1_November "1 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 250](/wiki/Letter_250 "Letter 250").
- **[10](/wiki/10_November "10 November")** - *[Hobbits and Heroes](</wiki/The_Sunday_Telegraph_(10_November_1963)> "The Sunday Telegraph (10 November 1963)")*, which includes extracts of an interview with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published in *The Sunday Telegraph*.
- **[22](/wiki/22_November "22 November")** - [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) dies.
- **[26](/wiki/26_November "26 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), [James Dundas-Grant](/wiki/James_Dundas-Grant), [Robert Havard](/wiki/Robert_Havard), [Owen Barfield](/wiki/Owen_Barfield) and [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) attend the funeral of C.S. Lewis.
- **[28](/wiki/28_November "28 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to George Sayer](</wiki/Letter_to_George_Sayer_(28_November_1963)> "Letter to George Sayer (28 November 1963)").

## December {#December}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Jared C. Lobdell](/wiki/Letter_to_Jared_C._Lobdell "Letter to Jared C. Lobdell").
- **[4](/wiki/4_December "4 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to a Miss Clark](/wiki/Letter_to_Miss_Clark "Letter to Miss Clark").
- **[16](/wiki/16_December "16 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Baronne A. Baeyens](/wiki/Letter_to_Baronne_A._Baeyens "Letter to Baronne A. Baeyens").
- **[18](/wiki/18_December "18 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Jonathan Hepworth](/wiki/Letter_to_Jonathan_Hepworth "Letter to Jonathan Hepworth").
- **[23](/wiki/23_December "23 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 253](/wiki/Letter_253 "Letter 253").

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)* is published in the US.
