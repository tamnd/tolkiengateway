[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1963" title="wikipedia:1963">1963</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[11](11_January.md)** - Actor [Jason Connery](../J/Jason_Connery.md) is born.

## February {#February}

- **[17](17_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Julie Cape](../L/Letter_to_Julie_Cape.md).
- **[20](20_February.md)** - Author [Rainer Nagel](../R/Rainer_Nagel.md) is born.
- **[21](21_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mrs Gill](../L/Letter_to_Mrs_Gill.md).
- **[22](22_February.md)** - [Jane Neave](../J/Jane_Neave.md), aunt of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), dies.

## March {#March}

- **[10](10_March.md)** - Actor [Steve Staley](../S/Steve_Staley.md) is born.
- **[11](11_March.md)** - Producer [Zane Weiner](../Z/Zane_Weiner.md) is born.
- **[23](23_March.md)** - Mr. and Mrs. Kloos write [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](../L/Letter_to_Mr_and_Mrs_Kloos.md).

## April {#April}

- **[6](6_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to a couple he met on holiday](../L/Letter_to_%2522My_Dear_People%2522.md).
- **[18](18_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) starts writing [a letter to Mr. and Mrs. Kloos](../L/Letter_to_Mr_and_Mrs_Kloos.md).

## May {#May}

- **[6](6_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) finishes writing [a letter to Mr. and Mrs. Kloos](../L/Letter_to_Mr_and_Mrs_Kloos.md).

## June {#June}

- **[9](9_June.md)** - Actress [Megan Edwards](../M/Megan_Edwards.md) is born.
- **[25](25_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 245](../L/Letter_245.md).

## July {#July}

- **[8](8_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s paper "[English and Welsh](../E/English_and_Welsh.md)" is published in *[Angles and Britons: O'Donnell Lectures](../A/Angles_and_Britons.md)*, a collection of lectures from the University of Wales.

## August {#August}

- **[25](25_August.md)** - Actress [Brianne Siddall](../B/Brianne_Siddall.md) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[18](18_September.md)** - Writer [Gary Russell](../G/Gary_Russell.md) is born.
- **[20](20_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 247](../L/Letter_247.md).
- **[22](22_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) starts writing [a letter to Eileen Elgar](<../L/Letter_to_Eileen_Elgar_(September_1963).md>).
- **[25](25_September.md)** - Actor [Mikael Persbrandt](../M/Mikael_Persbrandt.md) is born.
- **[30](30_September.md)** - Music producer [Carvin Knowles](../C/Carvin_Knowles.md) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[3](3_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) posts [a letter to Eileen Elgar](<../L/Letter_to_Eileen_Elgar_(September_1963).md>).
- **[5](5_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 248](../L/Letter_248.md).
- **[15](15_October.md)** - While dining, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) saves the life of an old lady who nearly choked on a whiting-bone.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[16](16_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 249](../L/Letter_249.md).
- **[20](20_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 249a](../L/Letter_249a.md).
- **[21](21_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mrs. Munby](../L/Letter_to_Mrs_Munby.md).
- **[29](29_October.md)** - Actor [Jed Brophy](../J/Jed_Brophy.md) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[1](1_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 250](../L/Letter_250.md).
- **[10](10_November.md)** - *[Hobbits and Heroes](<../T/The_Sunday_Telegraph_(10_November_1963).md>)*, which includes extracts of an interview with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published in *The Sunday Telegraph*.
- **[22](22_November.md)** - [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) dies.
- **[26](26_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), [James Dundas-Grant](../J/James_Dundas-Grant.md), [Robert Havard](../R/Robert_Havard.md), [Owen Barfield](../O/Owen_Barfield.md) and [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) attend the funeral of C.S. Lewis.
- **[28](28_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to George Sayer](<../L/Letter_to_George_Sayer_(28_November_1963).md>).

## December {#December}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Jared C. Lobdell](../L/Letter_to_Jared_C._Lobdell.md).
- **[4](4_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to a Miss Clark](../L/Letter_to_Miss_Clark.md).
- **[16](16_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Baronne A. Baeyens](../L/Letter_to_Baronne_A._Baeyens.md).
- **[18](18_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Jonathan Hepworth](../L/Letter_to_Jonathan_Hepworth.md).
- **[23](23_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 253](../L/Letter_253.md).

## ? {#?}
