Events that occurred on 22 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1911](1911.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and several other cadets of [King Edward's School](../K/King_Edward's_School.md)'s Officer Training Corps line the route at the Coronation of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_V_of_the_United_Kingdom" title="wikipedia:George V of the United Kingdom">George V</a>. Tolkien stands near Buckingham Palace.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[1920](1920.md)** - Voice actor [Paul Frees](../P/Paul_Frees.md) is born.
- **[1932](1932.md)** - Actress [Prunella Scales](../P/Prunella_Scales.md) is born.
- **[1952](1952.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 133](../L/Letter_133.md).
- **[1953](1953.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 137d](../L/Letter_137d.md).
- **[2007](2007.md)** - "Age's End", the seventeenth and last set in *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md)*, is released.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - *[Tolkien's Bag End](../T/Tolkien's_Bag_End.md)* is published.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - The [RiffTrax](../R/RiffTrax.md) commentary of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* is released online.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2942](../T/Third_Age_2942.md):
    - [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) returns to [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and learns he has been declared dead.
