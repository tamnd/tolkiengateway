[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1918" title="wikipedia:1918">1918</a></b>.

## Early {#Early}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) recovers from his trench fever to the degree that he can do home service. He is promoted to full lieutenant.

## February {#February}

- **[14](/wiki/14_February "14 February")** - Producer [William Lawrence Snyder](/wiki/William_Lawrence_Snyder) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[2](/wiki/2_November "2 November")** - [Roger Lancelyn Green](/wiki/Roger_Lancelyn_Green), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), is born.
- **[5](/wiki/5_November "5 November")** - [Alan Tilvern](/wiki/Alan_Tilvern), who would voice the ["Innkeeper"](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur "Barliman Butterbur") in [The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)>), is born.
- **[11](/wiki/11_November "11 November")** - [The War](/wiki/World_War_I "World War I") comes to an end.
- **?** - Tolkien is accepted as a junior staff member of the *New English Dictionary*, later called the *[Oxford English Dictionary](/wiki/Oxford_English_Dictionary)*. He worked with words beginning with the letter "w".

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[A Spring Harvest](/wiki/A_Spring_Harvest)*, containing a foreword by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
