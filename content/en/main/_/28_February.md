Events that occurred on 28 February.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1911](1911.md)** - [J.A.W. Bennett](../J/J.A.W._Bennett.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), born in [New Zealand](../N/New_Zealand.md).
- **[1949](1949.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 119](../L/Letter_119.md).
- **[1950](1950.md)** - Artist [Roger Garland](../R/Roger_Garland.md) is born.
- **[1961](1961.md)** - Actor [Mark Ferguson](../M/Mark_Ferguson.md) is born.
- **[2022](2022.md)** - [Priscilla Tolkien](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md), only daughter of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - [Grishnákh](../G/Grishnákh.md) returns.
    - [Éomer](Éomer.md) overtakes the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) just outside [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md). Many Orcs under [Mauhúr](../M/Mauhúr.md) come. Fifteen Rohirrim, including [Gárulf](../G/Gárulf.md), fall.
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) try to find a way through the [Emyn Muil](../E/Emyn_Muil.md).
    - A fragment of the [Great Horn](../G/Great_Horn.md) is recovered from the fens of [Entwash](../E/Entwash.md).
