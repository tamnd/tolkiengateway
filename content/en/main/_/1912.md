[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1912" title="wikipedia:1912">1912</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[20](/wiki/20_January "20 January")** - [Vincent Trought](/wiki/Vincent_Trought), a member of the [T.C.B.S.](/wiki/T.C.B.S.), dies. He had been severely ill since the autumn.

## April {#April}

- **[5](/wiki/5_April "5 April")** - [John Le Mesurier](/wiki/John_Le_Mesurier), who would voice [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") in the [1981 radio adaptation of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born.

## May {#May}

- **[18](/wiki/18_May "18 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) [pays his Oxford Union Library fees](/wiki/File:Oxford_Union_Society_Library_ledger_-_Tolkien_1912_fee.jpg "File:Oxford Union Society Library ledger - Tolkien 1912 fee.jpg").
- **[31](/wiki/31_May "31 May")** - German translator [Margaret Carroux](/wiki/Margaret_Carroux) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[17](/wiki/17_July "17 July")** - Author [Barbara Strachey](/wiki/Barbara_Strachey) is born.

## December {#December}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) creates the illustration *[End of the World](/wiki/End_of_the_World)*.

## Unknown {#Unknown}

- The [Apolausticks](/wiki/Apolausticks) are founded at [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford).
