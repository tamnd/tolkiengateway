[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1912" title="wikipedia:1912">1912</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[20](20_January.md)** - [Vincent Trought](../V/Vincent_Trought.md), a member of the [T.C.B.S.](../T/T.C.B.S..md), dies. He had been severely ill since the autumn.

## April {#April}

- **[5](5_April.md)** - [John Le Mesurier](../J/John_Le_Mesurier.md), who would voice [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) in the [1981 radio adaptation of *The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), is born.

## May {#May}

- **[18](18_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) [pays his Oxford Union Library fees](../../file/O/Oxford_Union_Society_Library_ledger_-_Tolkien_1912_fee.jpg.md).
- **[31](31_May.md)** - German translator [Margaret Carroux](../M/Margaret_Carroux.md) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[17](17_July.md)** - Author [Barbara Strachey](../B/Barbara_Strachey.md) is born.

## December {#December}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) creates the illustration *[End of the World](../E/End_of_the_World.md)*.

## Unknown {#Unknown}

- The [Apolausticks](../A/Apolausticks.md) are founded at [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md).
