## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - From midnight, [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) legally passes to the ownership of the [Sackville-Baggins Family](/wiki/Sackville-Baggins_Family).<sup id="cite_ref-three_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-three-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") have breakfast, and after 10 they continue their walk along the road, up and down the slopes of the [Green Hill Country](/wiki/Green_Hill_Country) until the top of a steep bank. They stop for lunch.<sup id="cite_ref-three_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-three-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) crosses the [Isen](/wiki/Isen).
    - [Khamûl](/wiki/Kham%C3%BBl) rides on the [Stock Road](/wiki/Stock_Road) and senses the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring); uncertain because of the bright daylight, he leaves and waits until the night.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - The hobbits stop to hide from the [Black Rider](/wiki/Black_Rider). They continue walking parallel to the road. They join the road around sunset.
    - They hide in a tree-stump and have a light meal and rest, and resume by nightfall.
    - [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor "Gildor") and [his companions](/wiki/Wandering_Companies "Wandering Companies") return from pilgrimage to the *[palantír](/wiki/Elostirion-stone "Elostirion-stone")* of the [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills) to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).
    - Khamûl senses the ring again and pursues Frodo.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - Frodo, Sam and Pippin encounter the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) near [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End); he flees because of the approaching Elves' [chanting](/wiki/Elven_Hymn_to_Elbereth "Elven Hymn to Elbereth"). The Hobbits camp with the Elves.
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
