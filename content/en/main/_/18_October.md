Events that occurred on 18 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1894](1894.md)** - [Geoffrey Bache Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md), member of the [T.C.B.S.](../T/T.C.B.S..md), is born.
- **[1946](1946.md)** - Composer [Howard Shore](../H/Howard_Shore.md) is born.
- **[1954](1954.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to the Recteur of the University of Liège](<../L/Letter_to_Liège_University_(18_October_1954).md>).
- **[1966](1966.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 289d](../L/Letter_289d.md).
- **[1986](1986.md)** - Scholar [Clyde S. Kilby](../C/Clyde_S._Kilby.md) dies.
- **[1979](1979.md)** - *[The Middle-earth Quiz Book](../T/The_Middle-earth_Quiz_Book.md)* is published.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - Mereth Aderthad XV, or "EstelCon", in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardona,_Spain" title="wikipedia:Cardona, Spain">Carodna</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md):
    - [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), guided by [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md), travels to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) in order to deliver [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md)'s message to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - With [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) severely wounded, [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) and the [four hobbits](../T/Travellers.md) travel slowly towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md). They find [Bilbo's Trolls](../W/William,_Tom,_and_Bert.md) and soon after the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md). There they are found by [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) at dusk.
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) reaches [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) from the North and sends [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md) back to [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
