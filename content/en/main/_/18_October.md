Events that occurred on 18 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1894](/wiki/1894)** - [Geoffrey Bache Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith), member of the [T.C.B.S.](/wiki/T.C.B.S.), is born.
- **[1946](/wiki/1946)** - Composer [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore) is born.
- **[1954](/wiki/1954)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to the Recteur of the University of Liège](</wiki/Letter_to_Li%C3%A8ge_University_(18_October_1954)> "Letter to Liège University (18 October 1954)").
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 289d](/wiki/Letter_289d "Letter 289d").
- **[1986](/wiki/1986)** - Scholar [Clyde S. Kilby](/wiki/Clyde_S._Kilby) dies.
- **[1979](/wiki/1979)** - *[The Middle-earth Quiz Book](/wiki/The_Middle-earth_Quiz_Book)* is published.
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** - Mereth Aderthad XV, or "EstelCon", in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardona,_Spain" title="wikipedia:Cardona, Spain">Carodna</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - With [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") severely wounded, [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") and the [four hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") travel slowly towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell). They find [Bilbo's Trolls](/wiki/William,_Tom,_and_Bert "William, Tom, and Bert") and soon after the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road). There they are found by [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) at dusk.
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) reaches [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) from the North and sends [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax) back to [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
