**"Where is the Horse and the Rider?"** is the thirty-sixth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and the forty-sixth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Gamling](/wiki/Gamling) readies [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) for battle, helping him put on his armour. As he does so, he reassures the king that the men of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) will follow him, no matter what. The scene is interspersed with a montage of other shots, including the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) marching towards [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep), and men and boys of Rohan readying themselves for war.
