<noinclude>{{month|November}}

Events that occurred on 1 November.

## On Earth

</noinclude>

* **[1897](/wiki/1897)** - Novelist, poet and letter-receiver [Naomi Mitchison](/wiki/Naomi_Mitchison) is born.

* **[1910](/wiki/1910)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) visits [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge) for a football match.

* **[1922](/wiki/1922)** - Actor [John Westbrook](/wiki/John_Westbrook) is born.

* **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 88a](/wiki/Letter_88a).

* **[1963](/wiki/1963)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 250](/wiki/Letter_250).

* **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - Artist [Jay Johnstone](/wiki/Jay_Johnstone) is born.

* **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - Actor [Daran Norris](/wiki/Daran_Norris) is born.

* **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) conducts an [interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien) with [Richard Plotz](/wiki/Dick_Plotz).

* **[1970](/wiki/1970)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 317](/wiki/Letter_317).

* **[1971](/wiki/1971)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 330](/wiki/Letter_330).

* **[1972](/wiki/1972)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s desk is [sold at Sotheby's](/wiki/Sotheby's_Fine_English_and_Continental_Furniture_Bronzes,_Textiles_and_Works_of_Art_1825-1930_and_French_Animalier_Bronzes).

* **[1975](/wiki/1975)** - Cartoonist [Lise Myhre](/wiki/Lise_Myhre) is born.

* **[1977](/wiki/1977)** - [Ted Johnstone](/wiki/Ted_Johnstone) dies.

* **[1979](/wiki/1979) - *[Pictures by J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Pictures_by_J.R.R._Tolkien)* is first published in the UK.**

* **[1990](/wiki/1990)** – [Tolklang](/wiki/Tolklang) is founded by [Julian C. Bradfield](/wiki/Julian_C._Bradfield).

* **[1999](/wiki/1999)** - *[George Allen and Unwin: A Remembrancer](/wiki/George_Allen_and_Unwin:_A_Remembrancer)* is published.

* **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - *[The Tolkien Name in History](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Name_in_History)* is published.

* **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - Mereth Aderthad XVI, or "EstelCon" of the Spanish Tolkien Society in [Valenica](/wiki/wikipedia:Valenica), [Spain](/wiki/wikipedia:Spain).

* **[2011](/wiki/2011)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North)* is released in North America.

* **[2012](/wiki/2012)** - *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Miscellany](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Miscellany)* is published.

* **[2025](/wiki/2025)** - Author and sholar [Colin Duriez](/wiki/Colin_Duriez) dies.<noinclude>
