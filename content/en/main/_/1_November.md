Events that occurred on 1 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1897](1897.md)** - Novelist, poet and letter-receiver [Naomi Mitchison](../N/Naomi_Mitchison.md) is born.
- **[1910](1910.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) visits [Cambridge](../C/Cambridge.md) for a football match.
- **[1922](1922.md)** - Actor [John Westbrook](../J/John_Westbrook.md) is born.
- **[1944](1944.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 88a](../L/Letter_88a.md).
- **[1963](1963.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 250](../L/Letter_250.md).
- **[1964](1964.md)** - Actor [Daran Norris](../D/Daran_Norris.md) is born.
- **[1966](1966.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) conducts an [interview](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) with [Richard Plotz](../D/Dick_Plotz.md).
- **[1970](1970.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 317](../L/Letter_317.md).
- **[1971](1971.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 330](../L/Letter_330.md).
- **[1972](1972.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s desk is [sold at Sotheby's](../S/Sotheby's_Fine_English_and_Continental_Furniture_Bronzes,_Textiles_and_Works_of_Art_1825-1930_and_French_Animalier_Bronzes.md).
- **[1975](1975.md)** - Cartoonist [Lise Myhre](../L/Lise_Myhre.md) is born.
- **[1977](1977.md)** - [Ted Johnstone](../T/Ted_Johnstone.md) dies.
- <b><a href="1979.md" title="1979">1979</a> - <i><a href="../P/Pictures_by_J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="Pictures by J.R.R. Tolkien">Pictures by J.R.R. Tolkien</a></i> is first published in the UK.</b>
- **[1990](1990.md)** – [Tolklang](../T/Tolklang.md) is founded by [Julian C. Bradfield](../J/Julian_C._Bradfield.md).
- **[1999](1999.md)** - *[George Allen and Unwin: A Remembrancer](../G/George_Allen_and_Unwin%253A_A_Remembrancer.md)* is published.
- **[2008](2008.md)** - *[The Tolkien Name in History](../T/The_Tolkien_Name_in_History.md)* is published.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - Mereth Aderthad XVI, or "EstelCon" of the Spanish Tolkien Society in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valenica" title="wikipedia:Valenica">Valenica</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.
- **[2011](2011.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md)* is released in North America.
- **[2012](2012.md)** - *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Miscellany](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Miscellany.md)* is published.
- **[2025](2025.md)** - Author and sholar [Colin Duriez](../C/Colin_Duriez.md) dies.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md):
    - [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), guided by [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md), travels to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) in order to deliver [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md)'s message to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) rest in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - At their arrival in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), the [four Hobbits](../T/Travellers.md) are arrested at [Frogmorton](../F/Frogmorton.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> by the [First Eastfarthing Troop](../F/First_Eastfarthing_Troop.md) and taken to the [Shirriff-house](../S/Shirriff-house.md).
