<i><b>...Where the Shadows Lie</b></i> is the first full-length album by the epic metal band [Battlelore](/wiki/Battlelore). It was released in [August 13](/wiki/13_August "13 August"), [2002](/wiki/2002). Its cover features the painting *Morgoth and High King of Noldor*, by [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith), used with permission. It had good reviews and fan support. No singles were released from the album however a video was filmed for the song "Journey To Undying Lands" and this was used to promote the band throughout Europe.
