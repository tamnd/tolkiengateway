Events that occurred on 13 April.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1902](/wiki/1902)** - Voice actor [Godfrey Kenton](/wiki/Godfrey_Kenton) is born.
- **[1937](/wiki/1937)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 12](/wiki/Letter_12).
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 60](/wiki/Letter_60).
- **[1970](/wiki/1970)** - [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey) receives a [letter](/wiki/Letter_to_Tom_Shippey "Letter to Tom Shippey") from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) after giving a speech at a Tolkien-themed convention.
- **[2000](/wiki/2000)** - Another edition of *[Tolkien: The Illustrated Encyclopedia](/wiki/Tolkien:_The_Illustrated_Encyclopedia)* is published.
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - German translator [Wolfgang Krege](/wiki/Wolfgang_Krege) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"):
    - [Eorl the Young](/wiki/Eorl "Eorl") and the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) travel towards the [Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Field_of_Celebrant).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - In the morning [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) examines [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins")'s Ring and determines from its inscription that it is [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"). He tells [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") of the [Ring's history](/wiki/The_One_Ring#History "The One Ring") and Frodo volunteers to leave [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") in order to secure the Ring.
    - [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) is caught by Gandalf eavesdropping, and is (happily) obliged to accompany Frodo.
