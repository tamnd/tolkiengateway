<i><b>-duinë</b></i> is a rare [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) suffix used for rivers (of large volume).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*-duinë* derives from [Common Elvish](../C/Common_Elvish.md) *duinē* ("large river, (liable to flood surrounding land)"), from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) DUY (stem *dui-* "flow (in volume)").<sup id="cite_ref-VT48_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT48-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HMN_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HMN-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Anduinë](../A/Anduinë.md)* “Long River”<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- *[Nunduinë](../N/Nunduinë.md)* “West River”<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
