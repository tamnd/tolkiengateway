Events that occurred on 12 August

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1911](/wiki/1911)** - Voice actor [Felix Felton](/wiki/Felix_Felton) is born.
- **[1916](/wiki/1916)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 5](/wiki/Letter_5).
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 78](/wiki/Letter_78).
- **[1955](/wiki/1955)** - During the holiday trip to Italy, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and his daughter [Priscilla](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien "Priscilla Tolkien") attend High Mass at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_di_Santa_Chiara" title="wikipedia:Basilica di Santa Chiara">Basilica di Santa Chiara in Assisi</a>.
- **[1959](/wiki/1959)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 216](/wiki/Letter_216).
- **[1978](/wiki/1978)** - [Mythcon 9](/wiki/Mythcon_9) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Sacramento,_California" title="wikipedia:West Sacramento, California">West Sacramento</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1983](/wiki/1983)** - [Mythcon 14](/wiki/Mythcon_14) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scripps_College" title="wikipedia:Scripps College">Scripps College</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont,_California" title="wikipedia:Claremont, California">Claremont</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1984](/wiki/1984)** - [Mythcon 15](/wiki/Mythcon_15) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mills_College" title="wikipedia:Mills College">Mills College</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland,_California" title="wikipedia:Oakland, California">Oakland</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - Voice actor [Peter Woodthorpe](/wiki/Peter_Woodthorpe) dies.
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s [Tolkien 2005](/wiki/Tolkien_2005) conference in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham" title="wikipedia:Birmingham">Birmingham</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>.
- **[2011](/wiki/2011)** - <a class="external text" href="http://omentielva.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Cantea</a> - 4th International Conference on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s Invented Languages in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia" title="wikipedia:Valencia">Valencia</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.
- **[2017](/wiki/2017)** - [Omentielva Otsea](/wiki/Omentielva "Omentielva") - the Seventh International Conference on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s Invented Languages at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_East_Bay" title="wikipedia:California State University, East Bay">California State University, East Bay</a>.
- **[2025](/wiki/2025)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2026](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2026)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) is held captive by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
