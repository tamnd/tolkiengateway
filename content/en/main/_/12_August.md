Events that occurred on 12 August

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1911](1911.md)** - Voice actor [Felix Felton](../F/Felix_Felton.md) is born.
- **[1916](1916.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 5](../L/Letter_5.md).
- **[1944](1944.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 78](../L/Letter_78.md).
- **[1955](1955.md)** - During the holiday trip to Italy, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and his daughter [Priscilla](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md) attend High Mass at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_di_Santa_Chiara" title="wikipedia:Basilica di Santa Chiara">Basilica di Santa Chiara in Assisi</a>.
- **[1959](1959.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 216](../L/Letter_216.md).
- **[1978](1978.md)** - [Mythcon 9](../M/Mythcon_9.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Sacramento,_California" title="wikipedia:West Sacramento, California">West Sacramento</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1983](1983.md)** - [Mythcon 14](../M/Mythcon_14.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scripps_College" title="wikipedia:Scripps College">Scripps College</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont,_California" title="wikipedia:Claremont, California">Claremont</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1984](1984.md)** - [Mythcon 15](../M/Mythcon_15.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mills_College" title="wikipedia:Mills College">Mills College</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland,_California" title="wikipedia:Oakland, California">Oakland</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[2004](2004.md)** - Voice actor [Peter Woodthorpe](../P/Peter_Woodthorpe.md) dies.
- **[2005](2005.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s [Tolkien 2005](../T/Tolkien_2005.md) conference in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham" title="wikipedia:Birmingham">Birmingham</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>.
- **[2011](2011.md)** - <a class="external text" href="http://omentielva.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Cantea</a> - 4th International Conference on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Invented Languages in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia" title="wikipedia:Valencia">Valencia</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.
- **[2017](2017.md)** - [Omentielva Otsea](../O/Omentielva.md) - the Seventh International Conference on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Invented Languages at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_East_Bay" title="wikipedia:California State University, East Bay">California State University, East Bay</a>.
- **[2025](2025.md)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2026](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2026.md)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) is held captive by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
