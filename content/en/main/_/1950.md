[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950" title="wikipedia:1950">1950</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[5](/wiki/5_February "5 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 123](/wiki/Letter_123).
- **[24](/wiki/24_February "24 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 124](/wiki/Letter_124 "Letter 124").
- **[28](/wiki/28_February "28 February")** - Artist [Roger Garland](/wiki/Roger_Garland) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[6](/wiki/6_March "6 March")** - [Stanley Unwin](/wiki/Stanley_Unwin) writes to Tolkien about dividing the *Lord of the Rings* in volumes; he also reports that *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)* had not sold as well as hoped.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[10](/wiki/10_March "10 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes letters [125](/wiki/Letter_125 "Letter 125") and [126](/wiki/Letter_126 "Letter 126").
- **[10](/wiki/10_March "10 March")** - [Stephen Oliver](/wiki/Stephen_Oliver) is born.
- **[18](/wiki/18_March "18 March")** - [Brad Dourif](/wiki/Brad_Dourif), who would play [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma") in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.

## April {#April}

- **[3](/wiki/3_April "3 April")** - Stanley Unwin writes to Tolkien considering again the cost for printing and the problem of publishing *Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **[14](/wiki/14_April "14 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 127](/wiki/Letter_127) presenting an ultimatum to Unwin about publishing *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)* with the *Lord of the Rings*.
- **[17](/wiki/17_April "17 April")** - Stanley Unwin writes to Tolkien, refusing to publish the *Silmarillion* without checking the final work.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **?** - After Stanley Unwin's refusal of publishing the [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings), Professor Tolkien went to [Milton Waldman](/wiki/Milton_Waldman), but was turned down again, because he felt they were too long.

## May {#May}

- **[May](/wiki/May)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) moves into [99 Holywell Street](/wiki/99_Holywell_Street).

## July {#July}

- **[19](/wiki/19_July "19 July")** - [Simon Cadell](/wiki/Simon_Cadell), voice actor who portrayed [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) in the [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") is born.

## August {#August}

- **[1](/wiki/1_August "1 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 128](/wiki/Letter_128 "Letter 128").

## September {#September}

- **[4](/wiki/4_September "4 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 128a](/wiki/Letter_128a).
- **[10](/wiki/10_September "10 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 129](/wiki/Letter_129 "Letter 129").
- **[14](/wiki/14_September "14 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 130](/wiki/Letter_130 "Letter 130").

## October {#October}

- **[9](/wiki/9_October "9 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 130a](/wiki/Letter_130a).

## November {#November}

- **[4](/wiki/4_November "4 November")** - [John Vickery](/wiki/John_Vickery), who would play several roles in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1979 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1979 radio series)"), is born.
- **[22](/wiki/22_November "22 November")** - Artist [David T. Wenzel](/wiki/David_T._Wenzel) is born.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien works on *[The Lay of Leithian Recommenced](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Leithian_Recommenced)*.
