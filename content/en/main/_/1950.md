[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950" title="wikipedia:1950">1950</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[5](5_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 123](../L/Letter_123.md).
- **[24](24_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 124](../L/Letter_124.md).
- **[28](28_February.md)** - Artist [Roger Garland](../R/Roger_Garland.md) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[6](6_March.md)** - [Stanley Unwin](../S/Stanley_Unwin.md) writes to Tolkien about dividing the *Lord of the Rings* in volumes; he also reports that *[Farmer Giles of Ham](../F/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md)* had not sold as well as hoped.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[10](10_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes letters [125](../L/Letter_125.md) and [126](../L/Letter_126.md).
- **[10](10_March.md)** - [Stephen Oliver](../S/Stephen_Oliver.md) is born.
- **[18](18_March.md)** - [Brad Dourif](../B/Brad_Dourif.md), who would play [Gríma Wormtongue](../G/Gríma.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.

## April {#April}

- **[3](3_April.md)** - Stanley Unwin writes to Tolkien considering again the cost for printing and the problem of publishing *Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **[14](14_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 127](../L/Letter_127.md) presenting an ultimatum to Unwin about publishing *[Silmarillion](../S/Silmarillion.md)* with the *Lord of the Rings*.
- **[17](17_April.md)** - Stanley Unwin writes to Tolkien, refusing to publish the *Silmarillion* without checking the final work.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **?** - After Stanley Unwin's refusal of publishing the [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md), Professor Tolkien went to [Milton Waldman](../M/Milton_Waldman.md), but was turned down again, because he felt they were too long.

## May {#May}

- **[May](../M/May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) moves into [99 Holywell Street](99_Holywell_Street.md).

## July {#July}

- **[19](19_July.md)** - [Simon Cadell](../S/Simon_Cadell.md), voice actor who portrayed [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) in the [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[1](1_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 128](../L/Letter_128.md).

## September {#September}

- **[4](4_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 128a](../L/Letter_128a.md).
- **[10](10_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 129](../L/Letter_129.md).
- **[14](14_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 130](../L/Letter_130.md).

## October {#October}

- **[9](9_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 130a](../L/Letter_130a.md).

## November {#November}

- **[4](4_November.md)** - [John Vickery](../J/John_Vickery.md), who would play several roles in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1979 radio series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series).md>), is born.
- **[22](22_November.md)** - Artist [David T. Wenzel](../D/David_T._Wenzel.md) is born.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien works on *[The Lay of Leithian Recommenced](../T/The_Lay_of_Leithian_Recommenced.md)*.
