[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025" title="wikipedia:2025">2025</a></b>.

## March {#March}

- **25** - *[Tales of the Shire: A "The Lord of the Rings" Game](../T/Tales_of_the_Shire%253A_A_%2522The_Lord_of_the_Rings%2522_Game.md)* is released.

## May {#May}

- **[31](31_May.md)** - After 12 years in the role, [Shaun Gunner](../S/Shaun_Gunner.md) steps down as Chair of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) to become its first Chief Executive Officer. He is succeeded as Acting Chair by Daniel Helen.<sup id="cite_ref-CEO_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CEO-1">[1]</a></sup>

## June {#June}

- **[1](1_June.md)** - [Shaun Gunner](../S/Shaun_Gunner.md) is appointed Chief Executive Officer of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md), having previously served over 12 years as Chair.<sup id="cite_ref-CEO_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CEO-1">[1]</a></sup>

## August {#August}

- **[12](12_August.md)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2026](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2026.md)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[12](12_September.md)** - Actor [Sean Astin](../S/Sean_Astin.md) elected President of the <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAG-AFTRA" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Screen Actors Guild–American Federation of Television and Radio Artists</a> (SAG-AFTRA)
- **[16](16_September.md)** - Release of the revised and expanded edition of *[The Hobbit: A Graphic Novel](<../T/The_Hobbit_(comic_book).md>)*

## October {#October}

- <b><a href="9_October.md" title="9 October">9</a> - Release of <i><a href="../T/The_Bovadium_Fragments%253A_together_with_The_Origin_of_Bovadium.md" title="The Bovadium Fragments: together with The Origin of Bovadium">The Bovadium Fragments: together with The Origin of Bovadium</a></i>.</b>
- **[23](23_October.md)** - Release of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Doomed_to_Die:_An_A-Z_of_Death_in_Tolkien&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Doomed to Die: An A-Z of Death in Tolkien (page does not exist)">Doomed to Die: An A-Z of Death in Tolkien</a></i>.

## November {#November}

- **[11](11_November.md)** - Release of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Doomed_to_Die:_An_A-Z_of_Death_in_Tolkien&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Doomed to Die: An A-Z of Death in Tolkien (page does not exist)">Doomed to Die: An A-Z of Death in Tolkien</a></i> in the U.S.
- **[18](18_November.md)** - Release of *[The Bovadium Fragments: together with The Origin of Bovadium](../T/The_Bovadium_Fragments%253A_together_with_The_Origin_of_Bovadium.md)* in the U.S.
