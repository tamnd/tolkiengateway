[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025" title="wikipedia:2025">2025</a></b>.

## March {#March}

- **25** - *[Tales of the Shire: A "The Lord of the Rings" Game](/wiki/Tales_of_the_Shire:_A_%22The_Lord_of_the_Rings%22_Game)* is released.

## May {#May}

- **[31](/wiki/31_May "31 May")** - After 12 years in the role, [Shaun Gunner](/wiki/Shaun_Gunner) steps down as Chair of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) to become its first Chief Executive Officer. He is succeeded as Acting Chair by Daniel Helen.<sup id="cite_ref-CEO_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CEO-1">[1]</a></sup>

## June {#June}

- **[1](/wiki/1_June "1 June")** - [Shaun Gunner](/wiki/Shaun_Gunner) is appointed Chief Executive Officer of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society), having previously served over 12 years as Chair.<sup id="cite_ref-CEO_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CEO-1">[1]</a></sup>

## August {#August}

- **[12](/wiki/12_August "12 August")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2026](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2026)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[12](/wiki/12_September "12 September")** - Actor [Sean Astin](/wiki/Sean_Astin) elected President of the <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAG-AFTRA" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Screen Actors Guild–American Federation of Television and Radio Artists</a> (SAG-AFTRA)
- **[16](/wiki/16_September "16 September")** - Release of the revised and expanded edition of *[The Hobbit: A Graphic Novel](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(comic_book)> "The Hobbit (comic book)")*

## October {#October}

- <b><a href="/wiki/9_October" title="9 October">9</a> - Release of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Bovadium_Fragments:_together_with_The_Origin_of_Bovadium" title="The Bovadium Fragments: together with The Origin of Bovadium">The Bovadium Fragments: together with The Origin of Bovadium</a></i>.</b>
- **[23](/wiki/23_October "23 October")** - Release of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Doomed_to_Die:_An_A-Z_of_Death_in_Tolkien&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Doomed to Die: An A-Z of Death in Tolkien (page does not exist)">Doomed to Die: An A-Z of Death in Tolkien</a></i>.

## November {#November}

- **[11](/wiki/11_November "11 November")** - Release of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Doomed_to_Die:_An_A-Z_of_Death_in_Tolkien&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Doomed to Die: An A-Z of Death in Tolkien (page does not exist)">Doomed to Die: An A-Z of Death in Tolkien</a></i> in the U.S.
- **[18](/wiki/18_November "18 November")** - Release of *[The Bovadium Fragments: together with The Origin of Bovadium](/wiki/The_Bovadium_Fragments:_together_with_The_Origin_of_Bovadium)* in the U.S.
