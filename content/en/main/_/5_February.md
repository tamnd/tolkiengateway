Events that occurred on 5 February

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1937](1937.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 11](../L/Letter_11.md).
- **[1950](1950.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 123](../L/Letter_123.md).
- **[1956](1956.md)** - Dan Wickenden gives a positive review of *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)* in the *New York Herald Tribune* titled "Notable Allegorical Trilogy Comes to Triumphant End".
- **[1959](1959.md)** - [Peter Emshwiller](../P/Peter_Emshwiller.md), who would provide the voice of [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md)*, is born.
- **[1968](1968.md)** - The first day of filming [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) for the documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](../T/Tolkien_in_Oxford.md)*.
- **[2004](2004.md)** - [Andy Serkis](../A/Andy_Serkis.md) wins an <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_Awards" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Empire Award</a> in the category *Best British Actor*, for his role as [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>).

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) rests in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).
