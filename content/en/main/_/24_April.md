Events that occurred on 24 April.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 63](/wiki/Letter_63).
- **[1957](/wiki/1957)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 196a](/wiki/Letter_196a).
- **[1962](/wiki/1962)** - Author and editor [Henry Gee](/wiki/Henry_Gee) is born.
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)* is released.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - [Guillermo del Toro](/wiki/Guillermo_del_Toro) is officially announced as the director of *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")* film adaptation and its proposed sequel.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online): [Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar "The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar")*, "Doom of the Last-King" is released in North America.
- **[2014](/wiki/2014)** - [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) announces that the third *[Hobbit film](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")* is to be renamed from *There and Back Again* to *[The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies "The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies")*.
- **[2018](/wiki/2018)** - [Robert Murray](/wiki/Robert_Murray) dies.
