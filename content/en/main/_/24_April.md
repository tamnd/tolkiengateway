Events that occurred on 24 April.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1944](1944.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 63](../L/Letter_63.md).
- **[1957](1957.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 196a](../L/Letter_196a.md).
- **[1962](1962.md)** - Author and editor [Henry Gee](../H/Henry_Gee.md) is born.
- **[2007](2007.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Shadows_of_Angmar.md)* is released.
- **[2008](2008.md)** - [Guillermo del Toro](../G/Guillermo_del_Toro.md) is officially announced as the director of *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)* film adaptation and its proposed sequel.
- **[2008](2008.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md): [Shadows of Angmar](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Shadows_of_Angmar.md)*, "Doom of the Last-King" is released in North America.
- **[2014](2014.md)** - [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) announces that the third *[Hobbit film](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)* is to be renamed from *There and Back Again* to *[The Battle of the Five Armies](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md)*.
- **[2018](2018.md)** - [Robert Murray](../R/Robert_Murray.md) dies.
