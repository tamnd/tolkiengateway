Events that occurred on 28 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1916](1916.md)** – After being diagnosed with <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trench_fever" title="wikipedia:Trench fever">trench fever</a> the previous day, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is hospitalized.
- **[1944](1944.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 88](../L/Letter_88.md).
- **[1958](1958.md)** - [C. S. Lewis](../C/C._S._Lewis.md) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- **[1966](1966.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 290](../L/Letter_290.md).
- **[1968](1968.md)** - Actor [Stephen Hunter](../S/Stephen_Hunter.md) is born.
- **[1971](1971.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Joy Hill](<../L/Letter_to_Joy_Hill_(28_October_1971).md>).
- <b><a href="1988.md" title="1988">1988</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_Annotated_Hobbit.md" title="The Annotated Hobbit">The Annotated Hobbit</a></i> is first published in United States.</b>
- **[2006](2006.md)** - Author [J.E.A. Tyler](../J/J.E.A._Tyler.md) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md):
    - [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), guided by [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md), travels to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) in order to deliver [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md)'s message to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) rest in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - Nightfall: [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and the [four Hobbits](../T/Travellers.md) reach [Bree](../B/Bree.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and find out much has changed since they last were there.
