[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000" title="wikipedia:2000">2000</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](1_January.md)** - Actor [Leon Wadham](../L/Leon_Wadham.md) is born.
- **[30](30_January.md)** - *[Tolkien's Legendarium](../T/Tolkien's_Legendarium.md)* is published.
- **[30](30_January.md)** - Actress [Markella Kavenagh](../M/Markella_Kavenagh.md) is born.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 161](../A/Amon_Hen_161.md)* is published.
- **?** - [Other Hands 28](../O/Other_Hands_28.md) is published.

## March {#March}

- **[19](19_March.md)** - Actor [Fraser Kerr](../F/Fraser_Kerr.md) dies.
- **[29](29_March.md)** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](../T/Tyalie_Tyelellieva.md)* issue 14 is published.

## May {#May}

- **[21](21_May.md)** - [Minastirith.com](../M/Minastirith.com.md) is founded.

## June {#June}

- **[28](28_June.md)** - The [Tolkien Ensemble](../T/Tolkien_Ensemble.md) publish their second album, *[A Night in Rivendell](../A/A_Night_in_Rivendell.md)*.

## July {#July}

- **[20](20_July.md)** - The second [Tolkien Thing](../T/Tolkien_Thing.md) is held.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 41](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_41.md) is published.
- **?** - [Other Hands 29/30](../O/Other_Hands_29%252F30.md) is published.

## August {#August}

- **[18](18_August.md)-[21](21_August.md)** - [Mythcon 31](../M/Mythcon_31.md).

## September {#September}

- **[5](5_September.md)** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](../T/Tyalie_Tyelellieva.md)* issue 15 is published.
- **[22](22_September.md)-[24](24_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2000](../O/Oxonmoot_2000.md).
- **[30](30_September.md)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien and His Literary Resonances](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_and_His_Literary_Resonances.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[The End of the Third Age](../T/The_End_of_the_Third_Age.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Author of the Century](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_Author_of_the_Century.md)* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[6](6_November.md)** - Author [L. Sprague de Camp](../L/L._Sprague_de_Camp.md) dies.
- **[23](23_November.md)** - [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md), the first reader of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, dies.

## December {#December}

- **[22](22_December.md)** - Principal photography for [The Lord of the Rings (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) ends.
- **[23](23_December.md)** - Actor [Billy Barty](../B/Billy_Barty.md) dies.
- **[26](26_December.md)** - Actor [Michael Deacon](../M/Michael_Deacon.md) dies.
- **[26](26_December.md)** - Composer [Paul Kont](../P/Paul_Kont.md) dies.
