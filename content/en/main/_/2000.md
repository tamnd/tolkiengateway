[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2000" title="wikipedia:2000">2000</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** - Actor [Leon Wadham](/wiki/Leon_Wadham) is born.
- **[30](/wiki/30_January "30 January")** - *[Tolkien's Legendarium](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Legendarium)* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_January "30 January")** - Actress [Markella Kavenagh](/wiki/Markella_Kavenagh) is born.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 161](/wiki/Amon_Hen_161)* is published.
- **?** - [Other Hands 28](/wiki/Other_Hands_28) is published.

## March {#March}

- **[19](/wiki/19_March "19 March")** - Actor [Fraser Kerr](/wiki/Fraser_Kerr) dies.
- **[29](/wiki/29_March "29 March")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 14 is published.

## May {#May}

- **[21](/wiki/21_May "21 May")** - [Minastirith.com](/wiki/Minastirith.com) is founded.

## June {#June}

- **[28](/wiki/28_June "28 June")** - The [Tolkien Ensemble](/wiki/Tolkien_Ensemble) publish their second album, *[A Night in Rivendell](/wiki/A_Night_in_Rivendell)*.

## July {#July}

- **[20](/wiki/20_July "20 July")** - The second [Tolkien Thing](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing) is held.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 41](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_41) is published.
- **?** - [Other Hands 29/30](/wiki/Other_Hands_29/30) is published.

## August {#August}

- **[18](/wiki/18_August "18 August")-[21](/wiki/21_August "21 August")** - [Mythcon 31](/wiki/Mythcon_31).

## September {#September}

- **[5](/wiki/5_September "5 September")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 15 is published.
- **[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")-[24](/wiki/24_September "24 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2000](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2000).
- **[30](/wiki/30_September "30 September")** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien and His Literary Resonances](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_and_His_Literary_Resonances)* is published.
- **?** - *[The End of the Third Age](/wiki/The_End_of_the_Third_Age)* is published.
- **?** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Author of the Century](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Author_of_the_Century)* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - Author [L. Sprague de Camp](/wiki/L._Sprague_de_Camp) dies.
- **[23](/wiki/23_November "23 November")** - [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin), the first reader of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, dies.

## December {#December}

- **[22](/wiki/22_December "22 December")** - Principal photography for [The Lord of the Rings (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)>) ends.
- **[23](/wiki/23_December "23 December")** - Actor [Billy Barty](/wiki/Billy_Barty) dies.
- **[26](/wiki/26_December "26 December")** - Actor [Michael Deacon](/wiki/Michael_Deacon) dies.
- **[26](/wiki/26_December "26 December")** - Composer [Paul Kont](/wiki/Paul_Kont) dies.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[Lord of the Rings (board game)](</wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings_(board_game)>)* is released.
- **?** - The first trailer *[The Lord of the Rings film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)")* is released online.
