**1 Yule** was the last day in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), after [30 December](/wiki/30_December), marking the end of the year.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> Due to the [Shire-reform](/wiki/Shire-reform) the Yuledays always fell on the same days of the week. 1 Yule was always a [Highday](/wiki/Highday) (Friday).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

Events on 1 Yule:

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) travels south along the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).
