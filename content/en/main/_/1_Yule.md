**1 Yule** was the last day in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md), after [30 December](30_December.md), marking the end of the year.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> Due to the [Shire-reform](../S/Shire-reform.md) the Yuledays always fell on the same days of the week. 1 Yule was always a [Highday](../H/Highday.md) (Friday).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

Events on 1 Yule:

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) travels south along the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).
