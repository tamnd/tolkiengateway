Events that occurred on 23 August

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1925](1925.md)** - The first recorded date in the writing of the [Lay of Leithian](../L/Lay_of_Leithian.md), at Line 557.
- **[1974](1974.md)** - [Mythcon 5](../M/Mythcon_5.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scripps_College" title="wikipedia:Scripps College">Scripps College</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont,_California" title="wikipedia:Claremont, California">Claremont</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1981](1981.md)** - The twenty-fifth episode of the [1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- <b><a href="1990.md" title="1990">1990</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_War_of_the_Ring.md" title="The War of the Ring">The War of the Ring</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **[1992](1992.md)** - The second part of the [1992 radio series](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>) of *[Farmer Giles of Ham](../F/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 5](../B/BBC.md).
- **[1992](1992.md)** - [Tolkien Centenary Conference](../T/Tolkien_Centenary_Conference.md), a combination of [Oxonmoot](../O/Oxonmoot.md) 1992 and [Mythcon](../M/Mythcon.md) 23, at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keble_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Keble College, Oxford">Keble College, Oxford</a>.
- **[2006](2006.md)** - [The Oxford Tolkien Conference](../T/The_Oxford_Tolkien_Conference.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Exeter College, Oxford">Exeter College, Oxford</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) is held captive by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
