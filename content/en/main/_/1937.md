[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1937" title="wikipedia:1937">1937</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - Actor [Norman Forsey](/wiki/Norman_Forsey) is born.
- **[4](/wiki/4_January "4 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 9](/wiki/Letter_9).
- **[7](/wiki/7_January "7 January")** - At the request of his father, young [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) writes a book report on *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)*. This is the first of many positive critiques.
- **[16](/wiki/16_January "16 January")** - Artist [Klaus Ensikat](/wiki/Klaus_Ensikat) is born.
- **[17](/wiki/17_January "17 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 10](/wiki/Letter_10).

## February {#February}

- **[4](/wiki/4_February "4 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[The Dragon's Visit](/wiki/The_Dragon%27s_Visit)" is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.
- **[5](/wiki/5_February "5 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 11](/wiki/Letter_11).
- **[12](/wiki/12_February "12 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 11a](/wiki/Letter_11a).
- **[18](/wiki/18_February "18 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[Knocking at the Door](/wiki/The_Mewlips "The Mewlips")" is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.

## March {#March}

- **[4](/wiki/4_March "4 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[Iúmonna Gold Galdre Bewunden](/wiki/I%C3%BAmonna_Gold_Galdre_Bewunden)" is republished in the *Oxford Magazine*.
- **[6](/wiki/6_March "6 March")** - [Michael Deacon](/wiki/Michael_Deacon), who would voice [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma") in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"), is born.
- **[10](/wiki/10_March "10 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 11b](/wiki/Letter_11b).

## April {#April}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 11c](/wiki/Letter_11c).
- **[13](/wiki/13_April "13 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 12](/wiki/Letter_12 "Letter 12").
- **[15](/wiki/15_April "15 April")** - Charles Furth writes [a letter to J.R.R.Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Charles_Furth_(25_April_1937)> "Letter to Charles Furth (25 April 1937)").
- **[20](/wiki/20_April "20 April")** - [George Takei](/wiki/George_Takei), who travelled the world as narrator of [Johan de Meij](/wiki/Johan_de_Meij)'s *[Symphony No. 1, "The Lord of the Rings"](/wiki/Symphony_No._1,_%22The_Lord_of_the_Rings%22)*, is born.
- **[25](/wiki/25_April "25 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Charles Furth](</wiki/Letter_to_Charles_Furth_(25_April_1937)> "Letter to Charles Furth (25 April 1937)").

## May {#May}

- **[13](/wiki/13_May "13 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 13](/wiki/Letter_13 "Letter 13").
- **[28](/wiki/28_May "28 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 14](/wiki/Letter_14 "Letter 14").

## June {#June}

- **[10](/wiki/10_June "10 June")** - *[The Battle of Maldon](</wiki/The_Battle_of_Maldon_(E.V._Gordon)> "The Battle of Maldon (E.V. Gordon)")*, edited by [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon) and with contribution by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **[25](/wiki/25_June "25 June")** - Author [Timothy J. Wheeler](/wiki/Timothy_J._Wheeler) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[1](/wiki/1_July "1 July")** - *[Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics](/wiki/Beowulf:_The_Monsters_and_the_Critics)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[1](/wiki/1_August "1 August")** - [Ian Hogg](/wiki/Ian_Hogg), who would voice [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) in [BBC](/wiki/BBC)'s radio series of *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)")*, is born.
- **[5](/wiki/5_August "5 August")** - [Alan Howard](/wiki/Alan_Howard), the voice for [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") in [The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring) and [The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King"), is born.
- **[31](/wiki/31_August "31 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 15](/wiki/Letter_15 "Letter 15").

## September {#September}

- **[7](/wiki/7_September "7 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to George Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Letter_to_George_Allen_and_Unwin "Letter to George Allen and Unwin") concerning the promotion of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and mentioning his friend [Wilfred Rowland Childe](/wiki/Wilfred_Rowland_Childe).
- **[17](/wiki/17_September "17 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 15a](/wiki/Letter_15a).
- **[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")** - <b><i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Simonne d'Ardenne](</wiki/Letter_to_Simonne_d%27Ardenne_(21_September_1937)> "Letter to Simonne d'Ardenne (21 September 1937)").
- **[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) signs copies of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and sends them to [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon), [Elaine Griffiths](/wiki/Elaine_Griffiths), Helen Buckhurst, [Stella Mills](/wiki/Stella_Mills), [Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien), [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) and [Katherine Kilbride](/wiki/Katherine_Kilbride).
- **[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Jane Neave](</wiki/Letter_to_Jane_Neave_(22_September_1937)> "Letter to Jane Neave (22 September 1937)").
- **[28](/wiki/28_September "28 September")** - [Donald Gee](/wiki/Donald_Gee), who would voice [Radagast](/wiki/Radagast) in [1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born.

## October {#October}

- **[2](/wiki/2_October "2 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 15b](/wiki/Letter_15b).
- **[2](/wiki/2_October "2 October")** - [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) publishes [a review](/wiki/A_World_for_Children "A World for Children") of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* in *The Times Literary Supplement*.
- **[3](/wiki/3_October "3 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 16](/wiki/Letter_16 "Letter 16").
- **[8](/wiki/8_October "8 October")** - [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) reviews *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* in *The Times*.
- **[9](/wiki/9_October "9 October")** - [Brian Blessed](/wiki/Brian_Blessed), who would play [Farmer Giles](/wiki/Farmer_Giles) in [*Tales from the Perilous Realm* (1992 radio series)](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)"), is born.
- **[15](/wiki/15_October "15 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 17](/wiki/Letter_17 "Letter 17").
- **[23](/wiki/23_October "23 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 18](/wiki/Letter_18 "Letter 18").

## November {#November}

- **[15](/wiki/15_November "15 November")** - Tolkien submits to [Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Allen_%26_Unwin) a bundle of manuscripts including *[The Lay of Leithian](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Leithian)* ("Long Poem"); *[Quenta Silmarillion](</wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road)> "Quenta Silmarillion (Lost Road)")*, *[Ainulindalë](</wiki/Ainulindal%C3%AB_(Lost_Road)> "Ainulindalë (Lost Road)")*, *[Ambarkanta](/wiki/Ambarkanta)*, and *[The Fall of Númenor (chapter)](</wiki/The_Fall_of_N%C3%BAmenor_(chapter)>)* ("The Gnomes Material"); [The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road); and *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)* and *[Mr. Bliss](/wiki/Mr._Bliss)*. They also discuss *[Beowulf](/wiki/Beowulf_and_the_Finnsburg_Fragment "Beowulf and the Finnsburg Fragment")*.
- **[22](/wiki/22_November "22 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 18a](/wiki/Letter_18a "Letter 18a").
- **[28](/wiki/28_November "28 November")** - G.E. Selby writes [a letter to J.R.R.Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_G.E._Selby_(14_December_1937)> "Letter to G.E. Selby (14 December 1937)").
- **[29](/wiki/29_November "29 November")** - [Jared Lobdell](/wiki/Jared_Lobdell) is born.

## December {#December}

- **[13](/wiki/13_December "13 December")** - [Arthur Ransome writes a letter](/wiki/Letter_to_Arthur_Ransome "Letter to Arthur Ransome") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- **[14](/wiki/14_December "14 December")-[15](/wiki/15_December "15 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to G.E. Selby](</wiki/Letter_to_G.E._Selby_(14_December_1937)> "Letter to G.E. Selby (14 December 1937)").
- **[15](/wiki/15_December "15 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Arthur Ransome](/wiki/Letter_to_Arthur_Ransome "Letter to Arthur Ransome").
- **15** - [Stanley Unwin](/wiki/Stanley_Unwin) writes to Tolkien that his "Silmarillion" is not fit for a book by itself, but rather "wonderful material" to inspire more books like *The Hobbit*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[16](/wiki/16_December "16 December")-[19](/wiki/19_December "19 December")** - Tolkien starts writing the first chapter of the "New Hobbit", which will later become *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
- **[16](/wiki/16_December "16 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 19](/wiki/Letter_19 "Letter 19").
- **[17](/wiki/17_December "17 December")** - Charles Furth writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Charles_Furth_(19_December_1937)> "Letter to Charles Furth (19 December 1937)").
- **[19](/wiki/19_December "19 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Charles Furth (Letter 20)](/wiki/Letter_20 "Letter 20").
- **[25](/wiki/25_December "25 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien submits *[The Father Christmas Letters](/wiki/The_Father_Christmas_Letters)* and a draft of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* for publication but they are rejected.
- **?** - Author [Jared Lobdell](/wiki/Jared_Lobdell) is born.
