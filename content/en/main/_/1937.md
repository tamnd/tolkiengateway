[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1937" title="wikipedia:1937">1937</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](3_January.md)** - Actor [Norman Forsey](../N/Norman_Forsey.md) is born.
- **[4](4_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 9](../L/Letter_9.md).
- **[7](7_January.md)** - At the request of his father, young [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) writes a book report on *[Farmer Giles of Ham](../F/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md)*. This is the first of many positive critiques.
- **[16](16_January.md)** - Artist [Klaus Ensikat](../K/Klaus_Ensikat.md) is born.
- **[17](17_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 10](../L/Letter_10.md).

## February {#February}

- **[4](4_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem "[The Dragon's Visit](../T/The_Dragon's_Visit.md)" is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.
- **[5](5_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 11](../L/Letter_11.md).
- **[12](12_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 11a](../L/Letter_11a.md).
- **[18](18_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem "[Knocking at the Door](../T/The_Mewlips.md)" is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.

## March {#March}

- **[4](4_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem "[Iúmonna Gold Galdre Bewunden](../I/Iúmonna_Gold_Galdre_Bewunden.md)" is republished in the *Oxford Magazine*.
- **[6](6_March.md)** - [Michael Deacon](../M/Michael_Deacon.md), who would voice [Gríma Wormtongue](../G/Gríma.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>), is born.
- **[10](10_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 11b](../L/Letter_11b.md).

## April {#April}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 11c](../L/Letter_11c.md).
- **[13](13_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 12](../L/Letter_12.md).
- **[15](15_April.md)** - Charles Furth writes [a letter to J.R.R.Tolkien](<../L/Letter_to_Charles_Furth_(25_April_1937).md>).
- **[20](20_April.md)** - [George Takei](../G/George_Takei.md), who travelled the world as narrator of [Johan de Meij](../J/Johan_de_Meij.md)'s *[Symphony No. 1, "The Lord of the Rings"](../S/Symphony_No._1,_%2522The_Lord_of_the_Rings%2522.md)*, is born.
- **[25](25_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Charles Furth](<../L/Letter_to_Charles_Furth_(25_April_1937).md>).

## May {#May}

- **[13](13_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 13](../L/Letter_13.md).
- **[28](28_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 14](../L/Letter_14.md).

## June {#June}

- **[10](10_June.md)** - *[The Battle of Maldon](<../T/The_Battle_of_Maldon_(E.V._Gordon).md>)*, edited by [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md) and with contribution by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.
- **[25](25_June.md)** - Author [Timothy J. Wheeler](../T/Timothy_J._Wheeler.md) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[1](1_July.md)** - *[Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics](../B/Beowulf%253A_The_Monsters_and_the_Critics.md)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[1](1_August.md)** - [Ian Hogg](../I/Ian_Hogg.md), who would voice [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md) in [BBC](../B/BBC.md)'s radio series of *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>)*, is born.
- **[5](5_August.md)** - [Alan Howard](../A/Alan_Howard.md), the voice for [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) in [The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) and [The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md), is born.
- **[31](31_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 15](../L/Letter_15.md).

## September {#September}

- **[7](7_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to George Allen &amp; Unwin](../L/Letter_to_George_Allen_and_Unwin.md) concerning the promotion of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and mentioning his friend [Wilfred Rowland Childe](../W/Wilfred_Rowland_Childe.md).
- **[17](17_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 15a](../L/Letter_15a.md).
- **[21](21_September.md)** - <b><i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit.md" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[21](21_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Simonne d'Ardenne](<../L/Letter_to_Simonne_d'Ardenne_(21_September_1937).md>).
- **[21](21_September.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) signs copies of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and sends them to [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md), [Elaine Griffiths](../E/Elaine_Griffiths.md), Helen Buckhurst, [Stella Mills](../S/Stella_Mills.md), [Hilary Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md), [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) and [Katherine Kilbride](../K/Katherine_Kilbride.md).
- **[22](22_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Jane Neave](<../L/Letter_to_Jane_Neave_(22_September_1937).md>).
- **[28](28_September.md)** - [Donald Gee](../D/Donald_Gee.md), who would voice [Radagast](../R/Radagast.md) in [1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), is born.

## October {#October}

- **[2](2_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 15b](../L/Letter_15b.md).
- **[2](2_October.md)** - [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) publishes [a review](../A/A_World_for_Children.md) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* in *The Times Literary Supplement*.
- **[3](3_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 16](../L/Letter_16.md).
- **[8](8_October.md)** - [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) reviews *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* in *The Times*.
- **[9](9_October.md)** - [Brian Blessed](../B/Brian_Blessed.md), who would play [Farmer Giles](../F/Farmer_Giles.md) in [*Tales from the Perilous Realm* (1992 radio series)](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>), is born.
- **[15](15_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 17](../L/Letter_17.md).
- **[23](23_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 18](../L/Letter_18.md).

## November {#November}

- **[15](15_November.md)** - Tolkien submits to [Allen &amp; Unwin](../A/Allen_&_Unwin.md) a bundle of manuscripts including *[The Lay of Leithian](../T/The_Lay_of_Leithian.md)* ("Long Poem"); *[Quenta Silmarillion](<../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road).md>)*, *[Ainulindalë](<../A/Ainulindalë_(Lost_Road).md>)*, *[Ambarkanta](../A/Ambarkanta.md)*, and *[The Fall of Númenor (chapter)](<../T/The_Fall_of_Númenor_(chapter).md>)* ("The Gnomes Material"); [The Lost Road](../T/The_Lost_Road.md); and *[Farmer Giles of Ham](../F/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md)* and *[Mr. Bliss](../M/Mr._Bliss.md)*. They also discuss *[Beowulf](../B/Beowulf_and_the_Finnsburg_Fragment.md)*.
- **[22](22_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 18a](../L/Letter_18a.md).
- **[28](28_November.md)** - G.E. Selby writes [a letter to J.R.R.Tolkien](<../L/Letter_to_G.E._Selby_(14_December_1937).md>).
- **[29](29_November.md)** - [Jared Lobdell](../J/Jared_Lobdell.md) is born.

## December {#December}

- **[13](13_December.md)** - [Arthur Ransome writes a letter](../L/Letter_to_Arthur_Ransome.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- **[14](14_December.md)-[15](15_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to G.E. Selby](<../L/Letter_to_G.E._Selby_(14_December_1937).md>).
- **[15](15_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Arthur Ransome](../L/Letter_to_Arthur_Ransome.md).
- **15** - [Stanley Unwin](../S/Stanley_Unwin.md) writes to Tolkien that his "Silmarillion" is not fit for a book by itself, but rather "wonderful material" to inspire more books like *The Hobbit*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[16](16_December.md)-[19](19_December.md)** - Tolkien starts writing the first chapter of the "New Hobbit", which will later become *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.
- **[16](16_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 19](../L/Letter_19.md).
- **[17](17_December.md)** - Charles Furth writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](<../L/Letter_to_Charles_Furth_(19_December_1937).md>).
- **[19](19_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Charles Furth (Letter 20)](../L/Letter_20.md).
- **[25](25_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien submits *[The Father Christmas Letters](../T/The_Father_Christmas_Letters.md)* and a draft of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* for publication but they are rejected.
- **?** - Author [Jared Lobdell](../J/Jared_Lobdell.md) is born.
