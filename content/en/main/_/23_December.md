Events that occurred on 23 December.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1924](1924.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[1925](1925.md)** - [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) is born.
- **[1931](1931.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[1932](1932.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[1934](1934.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to G.H. Cowling](../L/Letter_to_G.H._Cowling.md).
- **[1936](1936.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[1940](1940.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[1956](1956.md)** - The sixth and final episode of [1956 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* airs on the [BBC](../B/BBC.md) Third Programme.
- **[1963](1963.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 253](../L/Letter_253.md).
- **[2000](2000.md)** - Actor [Billy Barty](../B/Billy_Barty.md) dies.
- **[2003](2003.md)** - *[Parma Eldalamberon](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon.md)* issue [14](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_14.md) is published.
