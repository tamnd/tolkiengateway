[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018" title="wikipedia:2018">2018</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](3_January.md)** - [Birthday Toast](../B/Birthday_Toast.md#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[24](24_January.md)** - [Ursula K. Le Guin](../U/Ursula_K._Le_Guin.md) dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 269](../A/Amon_Hen_269.md)* is published.

## March {#March}

- **[22](22_March.md)** - *[Pagan Saints in Middle-earth](../P/Pagan_Saints_in_Middle-earth.md)* is published.
- **[25](25_March.md)** - [Tolkien Reading Day](../T/Tolkien_Reading_Day.md).
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 270](../A/Amon_Hen_270.md)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[10](10_April.md)** - [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md) announce that *[The Fall of Gondolin](../T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin.md)* will be published on [30 August](30_August.md) 2018.
- **[24](24_April.md)** - [Robert Murray](../R/Robert_Murray.md) dies.
- **[30](30_April.md)** - *[Poetry and Song in the works of J.R.R. Tolkien](../P/Poetry_and_Song_in_the_works_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **[3](3_May.md)** - *[Beren and Lúthien](../B/Beren_and_Lúthien.md)* is published in paperback.
- **[31](31_May.md)** - *[The Hobbit Facsimile Gift Edition](../T/The_Hobbit_Facsimile_Gift_Edition.md)* is published in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom" title="wikipedia:United Kingdom">UK</a>
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 271](../A/Amon_Hen_271.md)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[1](1_June.md)** - [Bodleian Library](../B/Bodleian_Library.md)'s exhibition "Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth" begins. The companion books *[Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth](../T/Tolkien%253A_Maker_of_Middle-earth.md)* and *[Tolkien: Treasures](../T/Tolkien%253A_Treasures.md)* will also be published.
- **[5](5_June.md)** - *Uncle Curro. J.R.R. Tolkien's Spanish Connection* is published, a translation of *[La Conexion Española de J.R.R. Tolkien](../L/La_Conexion_Española_de_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*.
- **[11](11_June.md)** - *Where the Shadows Lie: A Jungian Interpretation of Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[1](1_July.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) Seminar at the Hilton, [Leeds](../L/Leeds.md).
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 272](../A/Amon_Hen_272.md)* is published.

## August {#August}

- <b><a href="30_August.md" title="30 August">30</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin.md" title="The Fall of Gondolin">The Fall of Gondolin</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[30](30_August.md)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2019](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2019.md)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[20](20_September.md) - [23](23_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2018](../O/Oxonmoot_2018.md) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 273](../A/Amon_Hen_273.md)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[28](28_October.md)** - [Bodleian Library](../B/Bodleian_Library.md)'s exhibition "Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth" ends.
