[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018" title="wikipedia:2018">2018</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[24](/wiki/24_January "24 January")** - [Ursula K. Le Guin](/wiki/Ursula_K._Le_Guin) dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 269](/wiki/Amon_Hen_269)* is published.

## March {#March}

- **[22](/wiki/22_March "22 March")** - *[Pagan Saints in Middle-earth](/wiki/Pagan_Saints_in_Middle-earth)* is published.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 270](/wiki/Amon_Hen_270)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[10](/wiki/10_April "10 April")** - [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) announce that *[The Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin)* will be published on [30 August](/wiki/30_August) 2018.
- **[24](/wiki/24_April "24 April")** - [Robert Murray](/wiki/Robert_Murray) dies.
- **[30](/wiki/30_April "30 April")** - *[Poetry and Song in the works of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Poetry_and_Song_in_the_works_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **[3](/wiki/3_May "3 May")** - *[Beren and Lúthien](/wiki/Beren_and_L%C3%BAthien)* is published in paperback.
- **[31](/wiki/31_May "31 May")** - *[The Hobbit Facsimile Gift Edition](/wiki/The_Hobbit_Facsimile_Gift_Edition)* is published in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom" title="wikipedia:United Kingdom">UK</a>
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 271](/wiki/Amon_Hen_271)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[1](/wiki/1_June "1 June")** - [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library)'s exhibition "Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth" begins. The companion books *[Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth](/wiki/Tolkien:_Maker_of_Middle-earth)* and *[Tolkien: Treasures](/wiki/Tolkien:_Treasures)* will also be published.
- **[5](/wiki/5_June "5 June")** - *Uncle Curro. J.R.R. Tolkien's Spanish Connection* is published, a translation of *[La Conexion Española de J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/La_Conexion_Espa%C3%B1ola_de_J.R.R._Tolkien)*.
- **[11](/wiki/11_June "11 June")** - *Where the Shadows Lie: A Jungian Interpretation of Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[1](/wiki/1_July "1 July")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) Seminar at the Hilton, [Leeds](/wiki/Leeds).
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 272](/wiki/Amon_Hen_272)* is published.

## August {#August}

- <b><a href="/wiki/30_August" title="30 August">30</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin" title="The Fall of Gondolin">The Fall of Gondolin</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[30](/wiki/30_August "30 August")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2019](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2019)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[20](/wiki/20_September "20 September") - [23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2018](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2018) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 273](/wiki/Amon_Hen_273)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[4](/wiki/4_October "4 October")** - *[A Middle-earth Traveller](/wiki/A_Middle-earth_Traveller)* is published.
- **[28](/wiki/28_October "28 October")** - [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library)'s exhibition "Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth" ends.

## November {#November}

- **?** - *[Amon Hen 274](/wiki/Amon_Hen_274)* is published.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - American writer [Rod Jellema](/wiki/Rod_Jellema) dies.
