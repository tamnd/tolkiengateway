**99 Holywell Street** in Oxford was the home of [Ronald](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien"), and [Priscilla Tolkien](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien) from [May](/wiki/May) [1950](/wiki/1950) to [30 March](/wiki/30_March) [1953](/wiki/1953).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) also lived here with [Faith Tolkien](/wiki/Faith_Tolkien) from [1954](/wiki/1954).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-3">[3]</a></sup>
