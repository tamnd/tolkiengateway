[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003" title="wikipedia:2003">2003</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** - The website [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway) is created by [Hyarion](/wiki/User:Hyarion "User:Hyarion").
- **[9](/wiki/9_January "9 January")** - The Brazilian Tolkien society [Heren Hyarmen](/wiki/Heren_Hyarmen) changes its name to "Heren Hyarmeno".

## March {#March}

- **[11](/wiki/11_March "11 March")** - *[At Dawn in Rivendell](/wiki/At_Dawn_in_Rivendell)*, the third album of [Tolkien Ensemble](/wiki/Tolkien_Ensemble) is released (it was recorded in the previous year). It features recitations by [Christopher Lee](/wiki/Christopher_Lee).
- **[23](/wiki/23_March "23 March")** - [The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers) wins two Oscars, out of six nominations, at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/75th_Academy_Awards" title="wikipedia:75th Academy Awards">75th Academy Awards</a>.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - The first [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).

## April {#April}

- **[1](/wiki/1_April "1 April")** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: His Life and Works](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_(Library_of_Great_Authors)> "J.R.R. Tolkien (Library of Great Authors)")* is published.
- **[15](/wiki/15_April "15 April")** - *[The Art of The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Art_of_The_Two_Towers)* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_April "30 April")** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography (Greenwood Biographies)](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography_(Greenwood_Biographies)>)* by [Leslie Ellen Jones](/wiki/Leslie_Ellen_Jones) is published.

## May {#May}

- **[5](/wiki/5_May "5 May")** - Artist [Ben Lee](/wiki/Ben_Lee) dies of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchenne_muscular_dystrophy" title="wikipedia:Duchenne muscular dystrophy">Duchenne</a>.

## June {#June}

- **[5](/wiki/5_June "5 June")** - [Andy Serkis](/wiki/Andy_Serkis) wins an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Movie_Awards" title="wikipedia:MTV Movie Awards">MTV Movie Award</a>, category "Best Animated Character", for his role as [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum). His <a class="external text" href="http://youtube.com/watch?v=Uj411At8VRI" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">immortal acceptance speech</a> would win a Hugo in [2004](/wiki/2004).
- **[15](/wiki/15_June "15 June")** - [Philip Stone](/wiki/Philip_Stone), who voiced [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"), dies.
- **[16](/wiki/16_June "16 June")** - *[British Writers: Classics, Volume II](/wiki/British_Writers:_Classics,_Volume_II)* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_June "30 June")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: Shadow and Flame](/wiki/Shadow_and_Flame "Shadow and Flame")* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[10](/wiki/10_July "10 July")** - [Tolkien Thing V](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing "Tolkien Thing").
- **[25](/wiki/25_July "25 July")-[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - [Mythcon 34](/wiki/Mythcon_34).
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 41](/wiki/Mallorn_41 "Mallorn 41") is published.

## August {#August}

- **[4](/wiki/4_August "4 August")** - *[The Lord of the Rings and Philosophy: One Book to Rule Them All](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_and_Philosophy "The Lord of the Rings and Philosophy")* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[18](/wiki/18_September "18 September")-[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2003](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2003).
- **[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")** - The [Eleventy-First Birthday Party](/wiki/Eleventy-First_Birthday_Party) is held in Frankenmuth, Michigan.
- **?** - *[The Origins of Tolkien's Middle-earth For Dummies](/wiki/The_Origins_of_Tolkien%27s_Middle-earth_For_Dummies)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[1](/wiki/1_October "1 October")** - *[The Gospel According to Tolkien](/wiki/The_Gospel_According_to_Tolkien)* is published.
- <b><a href="/wiki/20_October" title="20 October">20</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/Tolkien_and_the_Great_War" title="Tolkien and the Great War">Tolkien and the Great War</a></i> is published.</b>
- **?** - *[Tolkien and C.S. Lewis: The Gift of Friendship](/wiki/Tolkien_and_C.S._Lewis "Tolkien and C.S. Lewis")* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[4](/wiki/4_November "4 November")** - *[The Essential J.R.R. Tolkien Sourcebook](/wiki/The_Essential_J.R.R._Tolkien_Sourcebook)* is published.
- **[4](/wiki/4_November "4 November")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring)* is released in the USA.
- **[10](/wiki/10_November "10 November")** - *[Secret Fire: The Spiritual Vision of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Secret_Fire:_The_Spiritual_Vision_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)* is published.
- **[11](/wiki/11_November "11 November")** - [The Hobbit (2003 video game)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)>) is released in the USA.
- **[14](/wiki/14_November "14 November")** - *[Tolkien Through Russian Eyes](/wiki/Tolkien_Through_Russian_Eyes)* is published.
- **[18](/wiki/18_November "18 November")** - [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* Extended Edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)") is released on DVD.
- **[25](/wiki/25_November "25 November")** - [The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack) is released.
- **[26](/wiki/26_November "26 November")** - [Gordon Reid](/wiki/Gordon_Reid), who provided several voices in [*The Lord of the Rings* radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), dies.
- **[28](/wiki/28_November "28 November")** - *[The Hobbit (2003 video game)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)>)* is released in Europe.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 45](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_45) is published.

## December {#December}

- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)** premieres in Embassy Theatre of [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand).
- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - *[Gollum: How We Made Movie Magic](/wiki/Gollum:_How_We_Made_Movie_Magic)* is published.
- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - *[Tolkien: A Cultural Phenomenon](/wiki/Tolkien:_A_Cultural_Phenomenon)* is published.
- **[3](/wiki/3_December "3 December")** - Actor [David Hemmings](/wiki/David_Hemmings) dies.
- **[17](/wiki/17_December "17 December")** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)** is released in USA.
- **[17](/wiki/17_December "17 December")** - Actor [Alan Tilvern](/wiki/Alan_Tilvern) dies.
- **[18](/wiki/18_December "18 December")** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)** is released in New Zealand.
- **[23](/wiki/23_December "23 December")** - [*Parma Eldalamberon* 14](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_14 "Parma Eldalamberon 14") is published.
