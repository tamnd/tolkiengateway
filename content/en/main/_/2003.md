[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003" title="wikipedia:2003">2003</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](1_January.md)** - The website [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) is created by [Hyarion](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Hyarion).
- **[9](9_January.md)** - The Brazilian Tolkien society [Heren Hyarmen](../H/Heren_Hyarmen.md) changes its name to "Heren Hyarmeno".

## March {#March}

- **[11](11_March.md)** - *[At Dawn in Rivendell](../A/At_Dawn_in_Rivendell.md)*, the third album of [Tolkien Ensemble](../T/Tolkien_Ensemble.md) is released (it was recorded in the previous year). It features recitations by [Christopher Lee](../C/Christopher_Lee.md).
- **[23](23_March.md)** - [The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md) wins two Oscars, out of six nominations, at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/75th_Academy_Awards" title="wikipedia:75th Academy Awards">75th Academy Awards</a>.
- **[25](25_March.md)** - The first [Tolkien Reading Day](../T/Tolkien_Reading_Day.md).

## April {#April}

- **[1](1_April.md)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: His Life and Works](<../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_(Library_of_Great_Authors).md>)* is published.
- **[15](15_April.md)** - *[The Art of The Two Towers](../T/The_Art_of_The_Two_Towers.md)* is published.
- **[30](30_April.md)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography (Greenwood Biographies)](<../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_A_Biography_(Greenwood_Biographies).md>)* by [Leslie Ellen Jones](../L/Leslie_Ellen_Jones.md) is published.

## May {#May}

- **[5](5_May.md)** - Artist [Ben Lee](../B/Ben_Lee.md) dies of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchenne_muscular_dystrophy" title="wikipedia:Duchenne muscular dystrophy">Duchenne</a>.

## June {#June}

- **[5](5_June.md)** - [Andy Serkis](../A/Andy_Serkis.md) wins an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MTV_Movie_Awards" title="wikipedia:MTV Movie Awards">MTV Movie Award</a>, category "Best Animated Character", for his role as [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md). His <a class="external text" href="http://youtube.com/watch?v=Uj411At8VRI" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">immortal acceptance speech</a> would win a Hugo in [2004](2004.md).
- **[15](15_June.md)** - [Philip Stone](../P/Philip_Stone.md), who voiced [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>), dies.
- **[16](16_June.md)** - *[British Writers: Classics, Volume II](../B/British_Writers%253A_Classics,_Volume_II.md)* is published.
- **[30](30_June.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: Shadow and Flame](../S/Shadow_and_Flame.md)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[10](10_July.md)** - [Tolkien Thing V](../T/Tolkien_Thing.md).
- **[25](25_July.md)-[28](28_July.md)** - [Mythcon 34](../M/Mythcon_34.md).
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 41](../M/Mallorn_41.md) is published.

## August {#August}

- **[4](4_August.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings and Philosophy: One Book to Rule Them All](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_and_Philosophy.md)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[18](18_September.md)-[21](21_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2003](../O/Oxonmoot_2003.md).
- **[22](22_September.md)** - The [Eleventy-First Birthday Party](../E/Eleventy-First_Birthday_Party.md) is held in Frankenmuth, Michigan.
- **?** - *[The Origins of Tolkien's Middle-earth For Dummies](../T/The_Origins_of_Tolkien's_Middle-earth_For_Dummies.md)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[1](1_October.md)** - *[The Gospel According to Tolkien](../T/The_Gospel_According_to_Tolkien.md)* is published.
- <b><a href="20_October.md" title="20 October">20</a> - <i><a href="../T/Tolkien_and_the_Great_War.md" title="Tolkien and the Great War">Tolkien and the Great War</a></i> is published.</b>
- **?** - *[Tolkien and C.S. Lewis: The Gift of Friendship](../T/Tolkien_and_C.S._Lewis.md)* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[4](4_November.md)** - *[The Essential J.R.R. Tolkien Sourcebook](../T/The_Essential_J.R.R._Tolkien_Sourcebook.md)* is published.
- **[4](4_November.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_of_the_Ring.md)* is released in the USA.
- **[10](10_November.md)** - *[Secret Fire: The Spiritual Vision of J.R.R. Tolkien](../S/Secret_Fire%253A_The_Spiritual_Vision_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* is published.
- **[11](11_November.md)** - [The Hobbit (2003 video game)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game).md>) is released in the USA.
- **[14](14_November.md)** - *[Tolkien Through Russian Eyes](../T/Tolkien_Through_Russian_Eyes.md)* is published.
- **[18](18_November.md)** - [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* Extended Edition](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition).md>) is released on DVD.
- **[25](25_November.md)** - [The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack.md) is released.
- **[26](26_November.md)** - [Gordon Reid](../G/Gordon_Reid.md), who provided several voices in [*The Lord of the Rings* radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), dies.
- **[28](28_November.md)** - *[The Hobbit (2003 video game)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game).md>)* is released in Europe.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 45](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_45.md) is published.

## December {#December}

- **[1](1_December.md)** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)** premieres in Embassy Theatre of [New Zealand](../N/New_Zealand.md).
- **[1](1_December.md)** - *[Gollum: How We Made Movie Magic](../G/Gollum%253A_How_We_Made_Movie_Magic.md)* is published.
- **[1](1_December.md)** - *[Tolkien: A Cultural Phenomenon](../T/Tolkien%253A_A_Cultural_Phenomenon.md)* is published.
- **[3](3_December.md)** - Actor [David Hemmings](../D/David_Hemmings.md) dies.
- **[17](17_December.md)** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)** is released in USA.
- **[17](17_December.md)** - Actor [Alan Tilvern](../A/Alan_Tilvern.md) dies.
- **[18](18_December.md)** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)** is released in New Zealand.
- **[23](23_December.md)** - [*Parma Eldalamberon* 14](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_14.md) is published.
