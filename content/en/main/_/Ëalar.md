<i><b>ëalar</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word which referred to such [spirits](/wiki/Spirits "Spirits") who did not require a body in order to be complete. *Eälar* are contrasted with *[fëar](/wiki/F%C3%ABar "Fëar")*, which are the souls of beings whose natural state is to be [incarnated](/wiki/Incarnate "Incarnate") in a body.<sup id="cite_ref-MR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is the plural of Quenya *ëala* ("being"),<sup id="cite_ref-MR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-1">[1]</a></sup> and seems to be related to the Quenya verb *[ëa](</wiki/Ea_(verb)> "Ea (verb)")* ("be, exist").

It has been suggested that *-la* is the present participle ending of verbs (cf. "-ing"; but Quenya *-la* only forms adjectival participles).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
