Events that occurred on 5 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1970](/wiki/1970)** – The [first Mythcon](/wiki/Mythcon_1 "Mythcon 1") is held at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Mudd_College" title="wikipedia:Harvey Mudd College">Harvey Mudd College</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont,_California" title="wikipedia:Claremont, California">Claremont</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1971](/wiki/1971)** - [Mythcon 2](/wiki/Mythcon_2) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Barbara,_California" title="wikipedia:Santa Barbara, California">Santa Barbara</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1975](/wiki/1975)** - [Oxonmoot 1975](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1975) at the Welsh Poney, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford" title="wikipedia:Oxford">Oxford</a>.
- **[2000](/wiki/2000)** - *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va)* issue [15](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va_15 "Tyalië Tyelelliéva 15") is published.
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - A version of *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)* is released, edited by [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger).
- **[2019](/wiki/2019)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2020](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2020)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2](/wiki/Third_Age_2 "Third Age 2"):
    - After the victory over [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and his stay in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) sets out for [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) is held captive by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
