[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998" title="wikipedia:1998">1998</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- <b><a href="/wiki/5_January" title="5 January">5</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/Roverandom" title="Roverandom">Roverandom</a></i>, edited by <a href="/wiki/Christina_Scull" title="Christina Scull">Christina Scull</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond" title="Wayne G. Hammond">Wayne G. Hammond</a>, is published in the UK.</b>

## March {#March}

- **?** - Actor [John Bryning](/wiki/John_Bryning) dies.

## April {#April}

- **[4](/wiki/4_April "4 April")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 12 is published.
- <b><a href="/wiki/15_April" title="15 April">15</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/Roverandom" title="Roverandom">Roverandom</a></i>, edited by <a href="/wiki/Christina_Scull" title="Christina Scull">Christina Scull</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond" title="Wayne G. Hammond">Wayne G. Hammond</a>, is published in the US.</b>
- **[27](/wiki/27_April "27 April")** - Actor [Godfrey Kenton](/wiki/Godfrey_Kenton) dies.

## June {#June}

- **[3](/wiki/3_June "3 June")** - Producer [William Lawrence Snyder](/wiki/William_Lawrence_Snyder) dies.
- **?** - *[CarolinaCon Regional MECCG Newsletter](/wiki/CarolinaCon_Regional_MECCG_Newsletter)* issue 1 is published.

## July {#July}

- **[15](/wiki/15_July "15 July")-[20](/wiki/20_July "20 July")** - [Mythcon 29](/wiki/Mythcon_29).
- **[17](/wiki/17_July "17 July")** - Author [Paul H. Kocher](/wiki/Paul_H._Kocher) dies.
- **[21](/wiki/21_July "21 July")** - The Royal Mail published a new set of stamps, "Magical Worlds: Classic Fantasy Books for Children" with art by Peter Malone. One of the stamps features [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") and [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug).
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 39](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_39) is published.
- **?** - *[Parma Eldalamberon](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon)* issue [12](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_12 "Parma Eldalamberon 12") is published.

## August {#August}

- **[24](/wiki/24_August "24 August")** - *[CarolinaCon Regional MECCG Newsletter](/wiki/CarolinaCon_Regional_MECCG_Newsletter)* issue 2 is published.

## September {#September}

- **[7](/wiki/7_September "7 September")** - [Elfling](/wiki/Elfling) is founded.
- **[25](/wiki/25_September "25 September")-[27](/wiki/27_September "27 September")** - [Oxonmoot 1998](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1998).
- **?** - *[CarolinaCon Regional MECCG Newsletter](/wiki/CarolinaCon_Regional_MECCG_Newsletter)* issue 3 is published.

## October {#October}

- **[3](/wiki/3_October "3 October")** - Actor [Roddy McDowall](/wiki/Roddy_McDowall) dies.
- **[5](/wiki/5_October "5 October")** - *[Tolkien: Man and Myth](/wiki/Tolkien:_Man_and_Myth)* is published.
- **[17](/wiki/17_October "17 October")** - [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") [Colin Hardie](/wiki/Colin_Hardie) dies.

## November {#November}

- **?** - [*Mallorn* 36](/wiki/Mallorn_36 "Mallorn 36") is published.

## December {#December}

- **[3](/wiki/3_December "3 December")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 13 is published.

## ? {#?}
