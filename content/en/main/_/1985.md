[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985" title="wikipedia:1985">1985</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[12](/wiki/January_12 "January 12")** - Actress [Cynthia Addai-Robinson](/wiki/Cynthia_Addai-Robinson) is born.

## March {#March}

- **?** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien's Letters to Rhona Beare](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_Letters_to_Rhona_Beare)* is published.
- **?** - Gary Gygax publishes the essay "*[The influence of J.R.R. Tolkien on the D&amp;D and AD&amp;D games](/wiki/The_influence_of_J.R.R._Tolkien_on_the_D%26D_and_AD%26D_games)*" in *Dragon* magazine.

## April {#April}

- **[25](/wiki/25_April "25 April")** - *[A Path from Rome: An Autobiography](/wiki/A_Path_from_Rome "A Path from Rome")*, which includes a [reminiscence of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Index:Reminiscences_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Reminiscences of J.R.R. Tolkien"), is published.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 22](/wiki/Mallorn_22 "Mallorn 22") is published.

## May {#May}

- **?** - *[Masterpieces of Terror and the Supernatural](/wiki/Masterpieces_of_Terror_and_the_Supernatural)*, including [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [first edition of *Riddles in the Dark*](/wiki/Riddles_in_the_Dark#First_Edition "Riddles in the Dark"), is published.
- **?** - [*Beyond Bree* May 1985](/wiki/Beyond_Bree_May_1985 "Beyond Bree May 1985"), including [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s '[Concerning ... 'The Hoard'](/wiki/Concerning_..._%27The_Hoard%27) and [Kinship of the Half-elven](/wiki/Kinship_of_the_Half-elven), is published.

## June {#June}

- **[24](/wiki/24_June "24 June")** - Actor [Valentine Dyall](/wiki/Valentine_Dyall) dies.
- **?** - [*Beyond Bree* June 1985](/wiki/Beyond_Bree_June_1985 "Beyond Bree June 1985"), including [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s '[Kinship of the Half-elven](/wiki/Kinship_of_the_Half-elven)', is published.

## July {#July}

- **[17](/wiki/17_July "17 July") - [Robert Havard](/wiki/Robert_Havard), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), dies.**
- **[26](/wiki/26_July "26 July")-[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")** - [Mythcon 16](/wiki/Mythcon_16).
- **?** - *[Some Light on Middle-earth](/wiki/Some_Light_on_Middle-earth)* is published.

## August {#August}

- <b><a href="/wiki/22_August" title="22 August">22</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lays_of_Beleriand" title="The Lays of Beleriand">The Lays of Beleriand</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **[29](/wiki/29_August "29 August")** - Actor [Patrick Barr](/wiki/Patrick_Barr) dies.

## September {#September}

- **[27](/wiki/27_September "27 September")-[29](/wiki/29_September "29 September")** - [Oxonmoot 1985](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1985).

## October {#October}

- **?** - [*Beyond Bree* October 1985](/wiki/Beyond_Bree_October_1985 "Beyond Bree October 1985") is published.

## November {#November}

- **[20](/wiki/20_November "20 November")** - *[The Lays of Beleriand](/wiki/The_Lays_of_Beleriand)* is published in the US.

## December {#December}

- **[5](/wiki/December_5 "December 5")** - Artist [Matěj Čadil](/wiki/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil) is born.
