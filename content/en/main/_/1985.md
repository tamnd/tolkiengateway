- **?** - [*Beyond Bree* May 1985](../B/Beyond_Bree_May_1985.md), including [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s '[Concerning ... 'The Hoard'](../C/Concerning_..._'The_Hoard'.md) and [Kinship of the Half-elven](../K/Kinship_of_the_Half-elven.md), is published.
