Events that occurred on 11 February.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1945](/wiki/1945)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 97](/wiki/Letter_97).
- **[1952](/wiki/1952)** - Actor [Paul Norell](/wiki/Paul_Norell) is born.
- **[1953](/wiki/1953)** - [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) is born.
- **[1994](/wiki/1994)** - Voice actor [William Conrad](/wiki/William_Conrad) dies.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - The [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate), [The Tolkien Trust](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Trust) and [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) file a lawsuit against [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) and the [Saul Zaentz Company](/wiki/Saul_Zaentz_Company).

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- The beginning of [Coirë](/wiki/Coir%C3%AB) and [Echuir](/wiki/Echuir).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) rests in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).
