[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013" title="wikipedia:2013">2013</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 239](/wiki/Amon_Hen_239 "Amon Hen 239") is published.

## March {#March}

- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - [*Vinyar Tengwar* 50](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_50 "Vinyar Tengwar 50") is published.
- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")**-**[3](/wiki/3_March "3 March")** - Conference on Tolkien and His Works at Valparaiso University.
- **[19](/wiki/19_March "19 March")** - *[La Conexion Española de J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/La_Conexion_Espa%C3%B1ola_de_J.R.R._Tolkien)* is published in Spain.
- **[21](/wiki/21_March "21 March")** - *[The Hobbit: Armies of The Third Age](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_Armies_of_The_Third_Age)* is released online.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- **[30](/wiki/30_March "30 March")** - HobbitCon, at the Hotel Maritim in Bonn, starts.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 240](/wiki/Amon_Hen_240 "Amon Hen 240") is published.

## April {#April}

- **[1](/wiki/1_April "1 April")** - HobbitCon, at the Hotel Maritim in Bonn, ends.
- **[8](/wiki/8_April "8 April")** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)* is released on DVD.
- **[20](/wiki/20_April "20 April")** - Artist [Quinton Hoover](/wiki/Quinton_Hoover) dies.
- **[23](/wiki/23_April "23 April")** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: Chronicles: Creatures &amp; Characters](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey:_Chronicles:_Creatures_%26_Characters)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **[11](/wiki/11_May "11 May") - [12](/wiki/12_May "12 May")** - <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Middle-earth_Weekend&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Middle-earth Weekend (page does not exist)">Middle-earth Weekend</a> at [Sarehole Mill](/wiki/Sarehole_Mill), [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham).
- **[21](/wiki/21_May "21 May")** - <b><i><a href="/wiki/The_Fall_of_Arthur" title="The Fall of Arthur">The Fall of Arthur</a></i> by <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> is published.</b>
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 241](/wiki/Amon_Hen_241 "Amon Hen 241") is published.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 54](/wiki/Mallorn_54 "Mallorn 54") is published.

## July {#July}

- **[11](/wiki/11_July "11 July") - [14](/wiki/14_July "14 July")** - [Tolkien Thing](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing) 2013.
- **[12](/wiki/12_July "12 July") - [15](/wiki/15_July "15 July")** - [Mythcon 44](/wiki/Mythcon_44) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University" title="wikipedia:Michigan State University">Michigan State University</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Lansing,_Michigan" title="wikipedia:East Lansing, Michigan">East Lansing</a>.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 242](/wiki/Amon_Hen_242 "Amon Hen 242") is published.

## August {#August}

- **[8](/wiki/8_August "8 August") - [11](/wiki/11_August "11 August")** - [Omentielva Lempea](/wiki/Omentielva "Omentielva") - 5th International Conference on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s Invented Languages in Helsinki, Finland.
- **[26](/wiki/26_August "26 August")** - Narrator [Gerard Murphy](/wiki/Gerard_Murphy) dies.
- **[29](/wiki/29_August "29 August")** - *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug: Annual 2014](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug:_Annual_2014)* is published.
- **[29](/wiki/29_August "29 August")** - *[Guardians of Middle-earth](/wiki/Guardians_of_Middle-earth)* is released on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Windows" title="wikipedia:Microsoft Windows">Windows</a>.

- **[2](/wiki/2_September "2 September")** - [*Parma Eldalamberon* 21](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_21 "Parma Eldalamberon 21") is published.
- **[6](/wiki/6_September "6 September") - [8](/wiki/8_September "8 September")** - The [Greisinger Museum](/wiki/Greisinger_Museum) has its Opening Ceremony.
- **[12](/wiki/12_September "12 September")** - *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, illustrated by [Jemima Catlin](/wiki/Jemima_Catlin), is published.
- **[12](/wiki/12_September "12 September")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2014](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2014)* is published.
- **[20](/wiki/20_September "20 September") - [22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2013](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2013) at Lady Margaret Hall.
- **[26](/wiki/26_September "26 September")** - *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)* pocket edition is published.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 243](/wiki/Amon_Hen_243 "Amon Hen 243") is published.

- **[5](/wiki/5_November "5 November")** - *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug: Activity Book](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug:_Activity_Book)* is published.
- **[5](/wiki/5_November "5 November")** - *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug: The Movie Storybook](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug:_The_Movie_Storybook)* is published.

- **[5](/wiki/5_November "5 November")** - *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug: Visual Companion](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug:_Visual_Companion)* is published.
- **[7](/wiki/7_November "7 November")** - *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug: Ultimate Sticker Collection](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug:_Ultimate_Sticker_Collection)* is published.

- **[11](/wiki/11_November "11 November")** - [*The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey* Extended Edition](</wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_(extended_edition)> "The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey (extended edition)") is released on DVD.
- **[20](/wiki/20_November "20 November")** - [The Lord of the Rings Online: Helm's Deep](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Helm%27s_Deep) is released online.
- **[22](/wiki/22_November "22 November")** - <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lewis_and_Tolkien:_The_Lost_Road&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lewis and Tolkien: The Lost Road (page does not exist)">Lewis and Tolkien: The Lost Road</a> is aired on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[26](/wiki/26_November "26 November")** - *[The Wobbit](/wiki/The_Wobbit)* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tolkien:_the_Forest_and_the_City&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien: the Forest and the City (page does not exist)">Tolkien: the Forest and the City</a> is published.</i>
- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Unofficial_Middle-Earth_Monsters%27_Guide&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Unofficial Middle-Earth Monsters&#39; Guide (page does not exist)">The Unofficial Middle-Earth Monsters&#39; Guide</a></i> is published.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 244](/wiki/Amon_Hen_244 "Amon Hen 244") is published.
