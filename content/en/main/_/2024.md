[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024" title="wikipedia:2024">2024</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** - All of [Tolkien's writings](/wiki/Index:Writings_by_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Writings by J.R.R. Tolkien") that were published during his lifetime enters in public domain in countries where copyright expires in 50 years after the author's death, in countries of Africa and Asia such as in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt" title="wikipedia:Egypt">Egypt</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China" title="wikipedia:China">China</a>, and in other countries such us [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus" title="wikipedia:Belarus">Belarus</a>.
- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).

## February {#February}

- **[23](/wiki/23_February "23 February")** - It is announced that the online videogame [The Lord of the Rings: Heroes of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Heroes_of_Middle-earth) will be discontinued online.

## March {#March}

- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- **[28](/wiki/28_March "28 March")** - Release of [Christopher Tolkien Centenary Boxed Sets](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien_Centenary_Boxed_Sets) #2

## May {#May}

- **[5](/wiki/5_May "5 May")** - Actor [Bernard Hill](/wiki/Bernard_Hill), who portrayed [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), dies.
- **[24](/wiki/24_May "24 May")** - The online videogame [The Lord of the Rings: Heroes of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Heroes_of_Middle-earth) is discontinued online.

## July {#July}

- **[4](/wiki/4_July "4 July")** - Release of [Christopher Tolkien Centenary Boxed Sets](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien_Centenary_Boxed_Sets) #3
- **[18](/wiki/18_July "18 July")** - Release of [Tolkien Calendar 2025](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2025)

## August {#August}

- **[13](/wiki/13_August "13 August")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2025](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2025)* is published.
- **[15](/wiki/15_August "15 August")** - Release of [Christopher Tolkien Centenary Boxed Sets](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien_Centenary_Boxed_Sets) #4
- **[29](/wiki/29_August "29 August")** - The second season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* is released.
- **[29](/wiki/29_August "29 August") - [1 September](/wiki/1_September)** - [Oxonmoot 2024](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2024) is held.

## September {#September}

- <b><a href="/wiki/12_September" title="12 September">12</a> - Release of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Collected_Poems_of_J.R.R._Tolkien" title="The Collected Poems of J.R.R. Tolkien">The Collected Poems of J.R.R. Tolkien</a></i>.</b>

## December {#December}

- **[13](/wiki/13_December "13 December")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The War of the Rohirrim](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_War_of_the_Rohirrim)* is released in cinemas.<sup id="cite_ref-ReleaseDate2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReleaseDate2-1">[1]</a></sup>
