[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1940" title="wikipedia:1940">1940</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[4](/wiki/4_January "4 January")** - [David Collings](/wiki/David_Collings), who would voice the part of [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) in [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born.
- **[22](/wiki/22_January "22 January")** - [John Hurt](/wiki/John_Hurt), who would voice [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"), is born.

## March {#March}

- **[6](/wiki/6_March "6 March")** - [Hans-Jörg Modlmayr](/wiki/Hans-J%C3%B6rg_Modlmayr) is born.
- **[7](/wiki/7_March "7 March")** - [Richard O'Callaghan](/wiki/Richard_O%27Callaghan), who would portray [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) in [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born.
- **[30](/wiki/30_March "30 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 38](/wiki/Letter_38).

## April {#April}

- **[16](/wiki/16_April "16 April")** - Artist [Margrethe II of Denmark](/wiki/Margrethe_II_of_Denmark) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[12](/wiki/12_July "12 July")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 38a](/wiki/Letter_38a).
- **[16](/wiki/16_July "16 July")** - *[Beowulf and the Finnsburg Fragment](/wiki/Beowulf_and_the_Finnsburg_Fragment)*, with contributions from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - Actor [Phil Proctor](/wiki/Phil_Proctor) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[26](/wiki/26_August "26 August")-[27](/wiki/27_August "27 August")** - After a hiatus, Tolkien evidently returns to the writing of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, writing a new outline for the "New Plot".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## September {#September}

- **[29](/wiki/29_September "29 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 39](/wiki/Letter_39 "Letter 39").

## October {#October}

- **[6](/wiki/6_October "6 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 40](/wiki/Letter_40 "Letter 40").
- **[20](/wiki/20_October "20 October")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 40a](/wiki/Letter_40a).

## December {#December}

- **[23](/wiki/23_December "23 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **[24](/wiki/24_December "24 December")** - Father Christmas writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **?** - [Marjorie J. Burns](/wiki/Marjorie_J._Burns), English professor and expert on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is born.
