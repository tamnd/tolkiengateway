[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1940" title="wikipedia:1940">1940</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[4](4_January.md)** - [David Collings](../D/David_Collings.md), who would voice the part of [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) in [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), is born.
- **[22](22_January.md)** - [John Hurt](../J/John_Hurt.md), who would voice [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>), is born.

## March {#March}

- **[6](6_March.md)** - [Hans-Jörg Modlmayr](../H/Hans-Jörg_Modlmayr.md) is born.
- **[7](7_March.md)** - [Richard O'Callaghan](../R/Richard_O'Callaghan.md), who would portray [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) in [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), is born.
- **[30](30_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 38](../L/Letter_38.md).

## April {#April}

- **[16](16_April.md)** - Artist [Margrethe II of Denmark](../M/Margrethe_II_of_Denmark.md) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[12](12_July.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 38a](../L/Letter_38a.md).
- **[16](16_July.md)** - *[Beowulf and the Finnsburg Fragment](../B/Beowulf_and_the_Finnsburg_Fragment.md)*, with contributions from [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.
- **[28](28_July.md)** - Actor [Phil Proctor](../P/Phil_Proctor.md) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[26](26_August.md)-[27](27_August.md)** - After a hiatus, Tolkien evidently returns to the writing of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, writing a new outline for the "New Plot".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## September {#September}

- **[29](29_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 39](../L/Letter_39.md).

## October {#October}

- **[6](6_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 40](../L/Letter_40.md).
- **[20](20_October.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 40a](../L/Letter_40a.md).

## December {#December}

- **[23](23_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[24](24_December.md)** - Father Christmas writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **?** - [Marjorie J. Burns](../M/Marjorie_J._Burns.md), English professor and expert on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is born.
