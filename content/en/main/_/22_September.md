### Cultural significance {#Cultural_significance}

As this day was the birthday of the [Ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearer) Frodo Baggins it became significant to the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning) after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring); corresponding to 30 [Yavannië](/wiki/Yavanni%C3%AB), it was made a festival in honor of Frodo. ## Notes {#Notes}

As Frodo and Sam ride through the Shire in 3021, the text tells us that "the Moon went westward" above them.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> According to astronomy, on that date of that year, no moon should have been visible.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Tolkien fans celebrate Frodo's birthday as <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hobbit_Day&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hobbit Day (page does not exist)">Hobbit Day</a>, after a suggestion by the [American Tolkien Society](/wiki/American_Tolkien_Society); the week containing 22 September each year is called <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tolkien_Week&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien Week (page does not exist)">Tolkien Week</a>.

Despite this, how the [Shire Reckoning](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning), and consequently the date of Bilbo's and Frodo's birthday, corresponds with our calendar is a matter of debate. [Appendix D](/wiki/Appendix_D) says that our New Year's Day ([1 January](/wiki/1_January)) corresponds "more or less" to the Shire's "[9 January](/wiki/9_January)", and in standard years our [14 September](/wiki/14_September) corresponds to the Shire's "22 September" (256 days later). However, Appendix D also says that the Shire calendar's "[Midyear's Day](/wiki/Midyear%27s_Day)" is "intended to correspond as nearly as possible to the summer solstice." In the Shire calendar, 22 [Halimath](/wiki/Halimath) is 83 days after Midyear's Day. If we take the summer solstice to be our [June 21](/wiki/June_21), then Bilbo's and Frodo's birthday must be 83 days later, which is our [September 12](/wiki/September_12).
