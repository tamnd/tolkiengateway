[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010" title="wikipedia:2010">2010</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](3_January.md)** - [Birthday Toast](../B/Birthday_Toast.md).
- **[12](12_January.md)** - Actress [Elizabeth Moody](../E/Elizabeth_Moody.md) dies.
- **[13](13_January.md)** - *The Power of Tolkien's Prose: Middle-Earth's Magical Style* by Steve Walker is published.
- **[18](18_January.md)** - *[Mirkwood: A Novel About JRR Tolkien](../M/Mirkwood%253A_A_Novel_About_JRR_Tolkien.md)* by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Steve_Hillard&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Steve Hillard (page does not exist)">Steve Hillard</a> is released.
- **[20](20_January.md)** - [Cubicle 7 Entertainment](../C/Cubicle_7_Entertainment.md) announces the forth-coming release of *[The One Ring: The Lord of the Rings Role-playing Game](../T/The_One_Ring%253A_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Role-playing_Game.md)*.
- **?** - [Amon Hen 221](../A/Amon_Hen_221.md) is published.

## February {#February}

- **[12](12_February.md)** - *[A Tolkien English Glossary](../A/A_Tolkien_English_Glossary.md)* is published.

## March {#March}

- **[1](1_March.md)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Pocket Guide](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_Pocket_Guide.md)* is published.
- **[1](1_March.md)** - *Oliphaunt Limited Edition Poster* is published.
- **[1](1_March.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md): [Volume III: Allies of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Siege_of_Mirkwood.md#Volume_III:_Allies_of_the_King)*, "Oath of the Rangers" is released.
- **[4](4_March.md)** - *[The History of Middle-earth](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* is re-released in hardcover.
- **[6](6_March.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s [Smials](../S/Smials.md#In_real_world) Day in [Leeds](../L/Leeds.md).
- **[25](25_March.md)** - [Tolkien Reading Day](../T/Tolkien_Reading_Day.md).
- **?** - [Amon Hen 222](../A/Amon_Hen_222.md) is published.

## April {#April}

- **[1](1_April.md)** - *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](../T/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudrún.md)* is released in paperback.
- **[1](1_April.md)** - *[West of the Mountains, East of the Sea](../W/West_of_the_Mountains,_East_of_the_Sea.md)* is published.
- **[17](17_April.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) holds its AGM in Brighton, UK.
- **[17](17_April.md)** - *Tolkien Enterprises* change it's name to *[Middle-earth Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md)*.
- **[21](21_April.md)** - *[Middle-earth Minstrel](../M/Middle-earth_Minstrel.md)* is published.
- **[23](23_April.md)** - [Bo Hansson](../B/Bo_Hansson.md), famous for his music inspired by *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, dies.
- **[23](23_April.md) - [25](25_April.md)** - [DTG](../D/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft_e.V..md)'s Tolkien Seminar 2010.
- **[26](26_April.md)** - [Simon Tolkien](../S/Simon_Tolkien.md) publishes the article "[In My Grandfather's Footsteps](../I/In_My_Grandfather's_Footsteps.md)".

## May {#May}

- **[5](5_May.md)** - The <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America%27s_Next_Top_Model" title="wikipedia:America&#39;s Next Top Model">America&#39;s Next Top Model</a></i> episode "[Hobbits vs. Models](../H/Hobbits_vs._Models.md)" premieres on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_CW_Television_Network" title="wikipedia:The CW Television Network">The CW</a>.
- **[15](15_May.md) - [16](16_May.md)** - Middle-earth Weekend, Sarehole Mill, [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md).
- **[22](22_May.md)** - "Tolkien and Philosophy" seminar in Modena, Italy.
- **[28](28_May.md)** - Scholar [Alexei Kondratiev](../A/Alexei_Kondratiev.md) dies.
- **[30](30_May.md)** - [Guillermo del Toro](../G/Guillermo_del_Toro.md) departs from [The Hobbit film](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>) adaptation.
- **?** - [Amon Hen 223](../A/Amon_Hen_223.md) is published.

## June {#June}

- **[9](9_June.md)** - [Midway Games](../M/Midway_Games.md) is closed, because of bankruptcy.
- **[22](22_June.md)** - The [RiffTrax](../R/RiffTrax.md) commentary of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* is released online.
- **[26](26_June.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s Northern Hemisphere seminar: "Tolkien &amp; [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md)".
- **26 - [27](27_June.md)** - Danish Society's Bree Summer Banquet.

## July {#July}

- **[8](8_July.md)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2011](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2011.md)* is published.
- **[15](15_July.md)-[18](18_July.md)** - [Tolkien Thing](../T/Tolkien_Thing.md) 2010.
- **[16](16_July.md)-[19](19_July.md)** - [Mythcon 41](../M/Mythcon_41.md) in Dallas, Texas.
- **?** - [Amon Hen 224](../A/Amon_Hen_224.md) is published.

## August {#August}

- **[3](3_August.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Card_Game.md)* is announced.
- **[13](13_August.md)-[16](16_August.md)** - ["Edge of the Wild"](<../E/Edge_of_the_Wild_(Tolkien_art_exhibit).md>), Tolkien art exhibition in Moreton-in-Marsh.
- **[28](28_August.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s Southern Hemisphere seminar: "Tolkien's Odysseys - Middle-earth and Beyond"
- **early** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 7](../T/Tolkien_Studies%253A_Volume_7.md)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[2](2_September.md)** - *[There and Back Again: The Map of Tolkien's The Hobbit](../T/There_and_Back_Again%253A_The_Map_of_Tolkien's_The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[8](8_September.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online:](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md) [Volume III: Allies of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Siege_of_Mirkwood.md#Volume_III:_Allies_of_the_King)*, "Ride of the Grey Company" is released in North America, which includes free to play for current and former subscribers.
- **[10](10_September.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)* goes free to play for the general public in North America.
- **10 - [12](12_September.md)** - Bulgarian Tolkien Society's 6th Tolkien-Fest in Sofia.
- **[14](14_September.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn's Quest](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Aragorn's_Quest.md)* is released in North America.
- **[24](24_September.md)-[26](26_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2010](../O/Oxonmoot_2010.md) at Lady Margaret Hall.
- **24-26** - Lasse Lanta Autumn Meeting of Austrian Tolkien Society.
- **[26](26_September.md)** - Decentennial Celebration and Hobbit Birthdays, [North East Tolkien Society](../N/North_East_Tolkien_Society.md)
- **[28](28_September.md)** - *[The Music of the Lord of the Rings Films](../T/The_Music_of_the_Lord_of_the_Rings_Films.md)* is published.
- **[30](30_September.md) - [Oct. 3](3_October.md)** - Jubilee for [Mithlond](../M/Mithlond,_Göteborgs_Tolkiensällskap.md), the Gothenburg Tolkien Society of Sweden.
- **?** - [Amon Hen 225](../A/Amon_Hen_225.md) is published.

## October {#October}

- **[1](1_October.md)** - *[Middle-earth: Visions of a Modern Myth](../M/Middle-earth%253A_Visions_of_a_Modern_Myth.md)* by [Donato Giancola](../D/Donato_Giancola.md) is published.
- **[6](6_October.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn's Quest](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Aragorn's_Quest.md)* is released in [Australia](../A/Australia.md).
- **[9](9_October.md) - [11](11_October.md)** - Chilean Tolkien Society's Mereth Oronnad.
- [10](10_October.md) - [Minecraft Middle-Earth](../M/Minecraft_Middle-Earth.md) begins building.
- **[15](15_October.md)** – [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) is officially announced as the director of [The Hobbit film series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).
- **[19](19_October.md)** - *[The Dragon's Apprentice](../T/The_Dragon's_Apprentice.md)* is published.
- **[25](25_October.md)** – German translator [Walter Scherf](../W/Walter_Scherf.md) dies.
- **[29](29_October.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn's Quest](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Aragorn's_Quest.md)* is released in Europe.
- **29 - [1 Nov.](1_November.md)** - Mereth Aderthad/EstelConXVI Annual Convention of the Spanish Tolkien Society in Valenica, [Spain](../S/Spain.md).
- **[30](30_October.md)** - *[Looking for the King: An Inklings Novel](../L/Looking_for_the_King.md)* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[2](2_November.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online:](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md) [Volume III: Allies of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Siege_of_Mirkwood.md#Volume_III:_Allies_of_the_King)*, "Ride of the Grey Company" is released in Europe.
- **[2](2_November.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)* goes free-to-play for the general public in Europe.
- **[3](3_November.md)** - Author [Glen GoodKnight](../G/Glen_GoodKnight.md) dies.
- **[15](15_November.md)** - [*Parma Eldalamberon*, issue 19](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_19.md) is published.
- **?** - [Amon Hen 226](../A/Amon_Hen_226.md) is published.
- **?** - [Update 1: Journey to Winter-Home](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md#Update_1:_Journey_to_Winter-Home), the first update of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)*, is released.
