[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010" title="wikipedia:2010">2010</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast).
- **[12](/wiki/12_January "12 January")** - Actress [Elizabeth Moody](/wiki/Elizabeth_Moody) dies.
- **[13](/wiki/13_January "13 January")** - *The Power of Tolkien's Prose: Middle-Earth's Magical Style* by Steve Walker is published.
- **[18](/wiki/18_January "18 January")** - *[Mirkwood: A Novel About JRR Tolkien](/wiki/Mirkwood:_A_Novel_About_JRR_Tolkien)* by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Steve_Hillard&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Steve Hillard (page does not exist)">Steve Hillard</a> is released.
- **[20](/wiki/20_January "20 January")** - [Cubicle 7 Entertainment](/wiki/Cubicle_7_Entertainment) announces the forth-coming release of *[The One Ring: The Lord of the Rings Role-playing Game](/wiki/The_One_Ring:_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Role-playing_Game)*.
- **?** - [Amon Hen 221](/wiki/Amon_Hen_221) is published.

## February {#February}

- **[12](/wiki/12_February "12 February")** - *[A Tolkien English Glossary](/wiki/A_Tolkien_English_Glossary)* is published.

## March {#March}

- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Pocket Guide](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Pocket_Guide)* is published.
- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - *Oliphaunt Limited Edition Poster* is published.
- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online): [Volume III: Allies of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Siege_of_Mirkwood#Volume_III:_Allies_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings Online: Siege of Mirkwood")*, "Oath of the Rangers" is released.
- **[4](/wiki/4_March "4 March")** - *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* is re-released in hardcover.
- **[6](/wiki/6_March "6 March")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s [Smials](/wiki/Smials#In_real_world) Day in [Leeds](/wiki/Leeds).
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- **?** - [Amon Hen 222](/wiki/Amon_Hen_222) is published.

## April {#April}

- **[1](/wiki/1_April "1 April")** - *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudr%C3%BAn)* is released in paperback.
- **[1](/wiki/1_April "1 April")** - *[West of the Mountains, East of the Sea](/wiki/West_of_the_Mountains,_East_of_the_Sea)* is published.
- **[17](/wiki/17_April "17 April")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) holds its AGM in Brighton, UK.
- **[17](/wiki/17_April "17 April")** - *Tolkien Enterprises* change it's name to *[Middle-earth Enterprises](/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises)*.
- **[21](/wiki/21_April "21 April")** - *[Middle-earth Minstrel](/wiki/Middle-earth_Minstrel)* is published.
- **[23](/wiki/23_April "23 April")** - [Bo Hansson](/wiki/Bo_Hansson), famous for his music inspired by *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, dies.
- **[23](/wiki/23_April "23 April") - [25](/wiki/25_April "25 April")** - [DTG](/wiki/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft_e.V. "Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft e.V.")'s Tolkien Seminar 2010.
- **[26](/wiki/26_April "26 April")** - [Simon Tolkien](/wiki/Simon_Tolkien) publishes the article "[In My Grandfather's Footsteps](/wiki/In_My_Grandfather%27s_Footsteps)".

## May {#May}

- **[5](/wiki/5_May "5 May")** - The <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America%27s_Next_Top_Model" title="wikipedia:America&#39;s Next Top Model">America&#39;s Next Top Model</a></i> episode "[Hobbits vs. Models](/wiki/Hobbits_vs._Models)" premieres on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_CW_Television_Network" title="wikipedia:The CW Television Network">The CW</a>.
- **[15](/wiki/15_May "15 May") - [16](/wiki/16_May "16 May")** - Middle-earth Weekend, Sarehole Mill, [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham).
- **[22](/wiki/22_May "22 May")** - "Tolkien and Philosophy" seminar in Modena, Italy.
- **[28](/wiki/28_May "28 May")** - Scholar [Alexei Kondratiev](/wiki/Alexei_Kondratiev) dies.
- **[30](/wiki/30_May "30 May")** - [Guillermo del Toro](/wiki/Guillermo_del_Toro) departs from [The Hobbit film](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)") adaptation.
- **?** - [Amon Hen 223](/wiki/Amon_Hen_223) is published.

## June {#June}

- **[9](/wiki/9_June "9 June")** - [Midway Games](/wiki/Midway_Games) is closed, because of bankruptcy.
- **[22](/wiki/22_June "22 June")** - The [RiffTrax](/wiki/RiffTrax) commentary of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* is released online.
- **[26](/wiki/26_June "26 June")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s Northern Hemisphere seminar: "Tolkien &amp; [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham)".
- **26 - [27](/wiki/27_June "27 June")** - Danish Society's Bree Summer Banquet.

## July {#July}

- **[8](/wiki/8_July "8 July")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2011](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2011)* is published.
- **[15](/wiki/15_July "15 July")-[18](/wiki/18_July "18 July")** - [Tolkien Thing](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing) 2010.
- **[16](/wiki/16_July "16 July")-[19](/wiki/19_July "19 July")** - [Mythcon 41](/wiki/Mythcon_41) in Dallas, Texas.
- **?** - [Amon Hen 224](/wiki/Amon_Hen_224) is published.

## August {#August}

- **[3](/wiki/3_August "3 August")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Card_Game)* is announced.
- **[13](/wiki/13_August "13 August")-[16](/wiki/16_August "16 August")** - ["Edge of the Wild"](</wiki/Edge_of_the_Wild_(Tolkien_art_exhibit)> "Edge of the Wild (Tolkien art exhibit)"), Tolkien art exhibition in Moreton-in-Marsh.
- **[28](/wiki/28_August "28 August")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s Southern Hemisphere seminar: "Tolkien's Odysseys - Middle-earth and Beyond"
- **early** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 7](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_7)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[2](/wiki/2_September "2 September")** - *[There and Back Again: The Map of Tolkien's The Hobbit](/wiki/There_and_Back_Again:_The_Map_of_Tolkien%27s_The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[8](/wiki/8_September "8 September")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online:](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online "The Lord of the Rings Online") [Volume III: Allies of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Siege_of_Mirkwood#Volume_III:_Allies_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings Online: Siege of Mirkwood")*, "Ride of the Grey Company" is released in North America, which includes free to play for current and former subscribers.
- **[10](/wiki/10_September "10 September")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)* goes free to play for the general public in North America.
- **10 - [12](/wiki/12_September "12 September")** - Bulgarian Tolkien Society's 6th Tolkien-Fest in Sofia.
- **[14](/wiki/14_September "14 September")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn's Quest](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Aragorn%27s_Quest)* is released in North America.
- **[24](/wiki/24_September "24 September")-[26](/wiki/26_September "26 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2010](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2010) at Lady Margaret Hall.
- **24-26** - Lasse Lanta Autumn Meeting of Austrian Tolkien Society.
- **[26](/wiki/26_September "26 September")** - Decentennial Celebration and Hobbit Birthdays, [North East Tolkien Society](/wiki/North_East_Tolkien_Society)
- **[28](/wiki/28_September "28 September")** - *[The Music of the Lord of the Rings Films](/wiki/The_Music_of_the_Lord_of_the_Rings_Films)* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_September "30 September") - [Oct. 3](/wiki/3_October "3 October")** - Jubilee for [Mithlond](/wiki/Mithlond,_G%C3%B6teborgs_Tolkiens%C3%A4llskap "Mithlond, Göteborgs Tolkiensällskap"), the Gothenburg Tolkien Society of Sweden.
- **?** - [Amon Hen 225](/wiki/Amon_Hen_225) is published.

## October {#October}

- **[1](/wiki/1_October "1 October")** - *[Middle-earth: Visions of a Modern Myth](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Visions_of_a_Modern_Myth)* by [Donato Giancola](/wiki/Donato_Giancola) is published.
- **[6](/wiki/6_October "6 October")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn's Quest](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Aragorn%27s_Quest)* is released in [Australia](/wiki/Australia).
- **[9](/wiki/9_October "9 October") - [11](/wiki/11_October "11 October")** - Chilean Tolkien Society's Mereth Oronnad.
- [10](/wiki/10_October "10 October") - [Minecraft Middle-Earth](/wiki/Minecraft_Middle-Earth) begins building.
- **[15](/wiki/15_October "15 October")** – [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) is officially announced as the director of [The Hobbit film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").
- **[19](/wiki/19_October "19 October")** - *[The Dragon's Apprentice](/wiki/The_Dragon%27s_Apprentice)* is published.
- **[25](/wiki/25_October "25 October")** – German translator [Walter Scherf](/wiki/Walter_Scherf) dies.
- **[29](/wiki/29_October "29 October")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn's Quest](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Aragorn%27s_Quest)* is released in Europe.
- **29 - [1 Nov.](/wiki/1_November "1 November")** - Mereth Aderthad/EstelConXVI Annual Convention of the Spanish Tolkien Society in Valenica, [Spain](/wiki/Spain).
- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")** - *[Looking for the King: An Inklings Novel](/wiki/Looking_for_the_King "Looking for the King")* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[2](/wiki/2_November "2 November")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online:](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online "The Lord of the Rings Online") [Volume III: Allies of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Siege_of_Mirkwood#Volume_III:_Allies_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings Online: Siege of Mirkwood")*, "Ride of the Grey Company" is released in Europe.
- **[2](/wiki/2_November "2 November")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)* goes free-to-play for the general public in Europe.
- **[3](/wiki/3_November "3 November")** - Author [Glen GoodKnight](/wiki/Glen_GoodKnight) dies.
- **[15](/wiki/15_November "15 November")** - [*Parma Eldalamberon*, issue 19](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_19 "Parma Eldalamberon 19") is published.
- **?** - [Amon Hen 226](/wiki/Amon_Hen_226) is published.
- **?** - [Update 1: Journey to Winter-Home](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online#Update_1:_Journey_to_Winter-Home "The Lord of the Rings Online"), the first update of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*, is released.

## December {#December}

- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - *[Middle-earth and Beyond: Essays on the World of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Middle-earth_and_Beyond "Middle-earth and Beyond")* is published.
- **?** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien (Christian Encounters)](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_(Christian_Encounters)>)* is published.
