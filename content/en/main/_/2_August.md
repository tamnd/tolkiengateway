Events that occurred on 2 August

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1946](/wiki/1946)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](</wiki/Letter_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(2_August_1946)> "Letter to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski (2 August 1946)").
- **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 258](/wiki/Letter_258).
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is interviewed by [William Cater](/wiki/William_Cater).
- **[1968](/wiki/1968)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Robert Havard](/wiki/Letter_to_Robert_Havard "Letter to Robert Havard").
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - The twenty-second episode of the [1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[1993](/wiki/1993)** - [Mythcon 24](/wiki/Mythcon_24) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota" title="wikipedia:University of Minnesota">University of Minnesota</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis" title="wikipedia:Minneapolis">Minneapolis</a>.
- **[1999](/wiki/1999)** - [Mythcon 30](/wiki/Mythcon_30) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milwaukee" title="wikipedia:Milwaukee">Milwaukee</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wisconsin" title="wikipedia:Wisconsin">Wisconsin</a>.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - [Mythcon 35](/wiki/Mythcon_35) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan" title="wikipedia:University of Michigan">University of Michigan</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Arbor,_Michigan" title="wikipedia:Ann Arbor, Michigan">Ann Arbor</a>.
- **[2015](/wiki/2015)** - [Mythcon 46](/wiki/Mythcon_46) in Hotel Elegante, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_Springs,_Colorado" title="wikipedia:Colorado Springs, Colorado">Colorado Springs</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) is held captive by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - The funeral procession of king [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) travels toward [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).
