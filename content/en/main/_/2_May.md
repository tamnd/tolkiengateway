Events that occurred on 2 May.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1924](/wiki/1924)** - Voice actor [Theodore Bikel](/wiki/Theodore_Bikel) is born.
- **[1955](/wiki/1955)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Miss Turnbull](</wiki/Letter_to_Miss_Turnbull_(2_May_1955)> "Letter to Miss Turnbull (2 May 1955)").
- **[1968](/wiki/1968)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 302](/wiki/Letter_302).
- **[1970](/wiki/1970)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is awarded an honorary Doctorate of Letters by the <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">University of Nottingham</a>.
- **[1978](/wiki/1978)** - [Jonathan Harding](/wiki/Jonathan_Harding) is born.
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - [*Amon Hen* 50](/wiki/Amon_Hen_50 "Amon Hen 50") is published.
- **[1995](/wiki/1995)** - Voice actor [Michael Hordern](/wiki/Michael_Hordern) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2942](/wiki/Third_Age_2942 "Third Age 2942"):
    - [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") wakes up in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) before dawn. The Elves are singing loud and clear under his open window.
