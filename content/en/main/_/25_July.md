Events that occurred on 25 July

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1938](/wiki/1938)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 29](/wiki/Letter_29).
- **[1938](/wiki/1938)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) sends an angry [letter](/wiki/Letter_30 "Letter 30") to a German publisher, after being asked whether he was <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aryan" title="wikipedia:Aryan">Aryan</a> or not.
- **[1979](/wiki/1979)** - [Bryan Cogman](/wiki/Bryan_Cogman) is born.
- **[1980](/wiki/1980)** - [Mythcon 11](/wiki/Mythcon_11) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nevada,_Reno" title="wikipedia:University of Nevada, Reno">University of Nevada</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reno,_Nevada" title="wikipedia:Reno, Nevada">Reno</a>.
- **[1987](/wiki/1987)** - [Mythcon 18](/wiki/Mythcon_18) at [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University), <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milwaukee" title="wikipedia:Milwaukee">Milwaukee</a>.
- **[1991](/wiki/1991)** - Voice actor [Brian Haines](/wiki/Brian_Haines) dies.
- **[2002](/wiki/2002)** - *[The Uncharted Realms of Tolkien](/wiki/The_Uncharted_Realms_of_Tolkien)* is published.
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - [Mythcon 34](/wiki/Mythcon_34) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville,_Tennessee" title="wikipedia:Nashville, Tennessee">Nashville</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee" title="wikipedia:Tennessee">Tennessee</a>.
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - *[Tolkien's Mighty Pen](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Mighty_Pen)* is published.
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** - [Tolkien Thing](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing) 2009 in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rieneck" title="wikipedia:Rieneck">Rieneck</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany" title="wikipedia:Germany">Germany</a>.
- **[2023](/wiki/2023)** - [The Lord of the Rings (musical)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical)>) opens at the Watermill Theatre in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newbury,_Berkshire" title="wikipedia:Newbury, Berkshire">Newbury</a>, West Berkshire.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) is held captive by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - The funeral procession of king [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) travels toward [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras)
