Events that occurred on 30 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1920](/wiki/1920)** - Actor [Norman Bird](/wiki/Norman_Bird) is born.
- **[1931](/wiki/1931)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is appointed supervisor for B.Litt student [A.F. Colborn](/wiki/A.F._Colborn).
- **[1936](/wiki/1936)** - [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin), aged 10, writes a "[Report on the Hobbit](/wiki/Report_on_the_Hobbit)".
- **[1965](/wiki/1965)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to his grandson Michael George](</wiki/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(30_October_1965)> "Letter to Michael George Tolkien (30 October 1965)"), published as [Letter 279](/wiki/Letter_279).
- **[1965](/wiki/1965)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to J.L.N. O'Loughlin](</wiki/Letter_to_J.L.N._O%27Loughlin_(30_October_1965)> "Letter to J.L.N. O'Loughlin (30 October 1965)").
- **[1995](/wiki/1995)** - Actor [Andy Pessoa](/wiki/Andy_Pessoa) is born.
- **[2001](/wiki/2001)** - The parody book *[The Roadkill of Middle Earth](/wiki/The_Roadkill_of_Middle_Earth)* is published.
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - [RingCon](/wiki/RingCon) V at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SeaTac,_Washington" title="wikipedia:SeaTac, Washington">SeaTac, Washington</a>.
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - *[Finding God in The Hobbit](/wiki/Finding_God_in_The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - Mereth Aderthad XVI, or "EstelCon" of the Spanish Tolkien Society in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valenica" title="wikipedia:Valenica">Valenica</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - *[Looking for the King: An Inklings Novel](/wiki/Looking_for_the_King "Looking for the King")* is published.
- **[2014](/wiki/2014)** - [The Hobbit *and Tolkien's Mythology*](/wiki/The_Hobbit_and_Tolkien%27s_Mythology "The Hobbit and Tolkien's Mythology") is published.
- **[2014](/wiki/2014)** - *[Arda Inhabited](/wiki/Arda_Inhabited)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") rest in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and the [four Hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") leave [Bree](/wiki/Bree).<sup id="cite_ref-chief_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-1">[1]</a></sup> Gandalf leaves to visit [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil).
    - Night: The Travellers come to the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge).<sup id="cite_ref-chief_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - Full moon<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
