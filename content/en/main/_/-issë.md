<i><b>-issë</b></i> is a feminine agental suffix in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i>mel<b>issë</b></i> = "lover" (*mel-* = "to love"; [MEL](/wiki/MEL))<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- *[Írissë](/wiki/%C3%8Driss%C3%AB)* = “Desirable Lady” (*írë* = "desire")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}
