[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1953" title="wikipedia:1953">1953</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[29](29_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 135c](../L/Letter_135c.md).

## February {#February}

- **[9](9_February.md)** - Actor [Ciarán Hinds](../C/Ciarán_Hinds.md) is born.
- **[11](11_February.md)** - [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[11](11_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 135d](../L/Letter_135d.md).
- **[24](24_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) meets [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) in [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and then writes [letter 136](../L/Letter_136.md) to his father, [Stanley Unwin](../S/Stanley_Unwin.md).
- **[30](30_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [Edith](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) move to [76 Sandfield Road](76_Sandfield_Road.md).

## April {#April}

- **[11](11_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 137](../L/Letter_137.md).
- **[15](15_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) gives the [W.P. Ker Lecture](../W/W.P._Ker_Lecture.md) at Glasgow University on the religious themes adultery, temptation and repentance in <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight.md" title="Sir Gawain and the Green Knight">Sir Gawain and the Green Knight</a></i>.
- **[21](21_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 137a](../L/Letter_137a.md).

## June {#June}

- **[5](5_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 137b](../L/Letter_137b.md).
- **[5](5_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 137c](../L/Letter_137c.md).
- **[11](11_June.md)** - *[Pearl](<../P/Pearl_(E.V._Gordon).md>)*, edited by [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md) and including a contribution by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.
- **[22](22_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 137d](../L/Letter_137d.md).
- **[24](24_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to George Sayer](<../L/Letter_to_George_Sayer_(24_June_1953).md>).
- **[27](27_June.md)** - Actress [Lorri Holt](../L/Lorri_Holt.md) is born.
- **?** - [Christopher Gilson](../C/Christopher_Gilson.md), linguist, is born.

## July {#July}

- **? (mid-month)** - J.R.R. Tolkien receives galley-proofs of the first volume from [Allen and Unwin](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md); he realised that some words have been over-corrected (dwarves to dwarfs, elvish to elfish, further to farther, and worst of all, elven to elfin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **[8](8_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 137e](../L/Letter_137e.md).
- **[28](28_July.md)** - Rayner Unwin presses Tolkien to choose titles for each volume for *Lord of the Rings*; he erroneously proposes that the general title should belong to the first volume.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## August {#August}

- **[4](4_August.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 138](../L/Letter_138.md).
- **[8](8_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 139](../L/Letter_139.md).
- **[15](15_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Ronald Eames](../L/Letter_to_Ronald_Eames.md).
- **[17](17_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) [agrees](../L/Letter_140.md) with [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) on the volume titles for the book: they will be *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md)* and (reluctantly) *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.
- **[31](31_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 140a](../L/Letter_140a.md).

## September {#September}

- **[3](3_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 140b](../L/Letter_140b.md).
- **[8](8_September.md)** - Actor [John McGlynn](../J/John_McGlynn.md) is born.
- **[23](23_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 140c](../L/Letter_140c.md).

## October {#October}

- **[4](4_October.md)** - Actor [Chris Fairbank](../C/Chris_Fairbank.md) is born.
- **[9](9_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 141](../L/Letter_141.md).
- **[15](15_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 141a](../L/Letter_141a.md).
- **?** - *[Essays and Studies 1953](../E/Essays_and_Studies_1953.md)*, including *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth Beorhthelm's Son](../T/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth_Beorhthelm's_Son.md)* by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.

## November {#November}

- **[19](19_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 141b](../L/Letter_141b.md).
- **[23](23_November.md)** - Composer [Johan de Meij](../J/Johan_de_Meij.md) is born.
- **[30](30_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 141c](../L/Letter_141c.md).

## December {#December}

- **[1](1_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 141d](../L/Letter_141d.md).
- **[2](2_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 142](../L/Letter_142.md).
- **[4](4_December.md)** - An issue of the magazine *[Radio Times](../R/Radio_Times_4_December_1953.md)* featuring an article by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.
- **[5](5_December.md)** - Actor [Christopher Guard](../C/Christopher_Guard.md) is born.
- **[10](10_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 142a](../L/Letter_142a.md).
- **[28](28_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Rayner Unwin](<../L/Letter_to_Rayner_Unwin_(28_December_1953).md>).
- **[29](29_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) visits [Allen &amp; Unwin](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_Street" title="wikipedia:Museum Street">Museum Street</a> in London.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 135b](../L/Letter_135b.md).
- **?** - *[Essais de philologie moderne (1951)](<../E/Essais_de_philologie_moderne_(1951).md>)*, including the essay "[Middle English 'Losenger'](../M/Middle_English_'Losenger'.md)" by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.
- **Late** - [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) draws [General Map of Middle-earth](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
