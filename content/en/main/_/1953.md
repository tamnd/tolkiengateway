[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1953" title="wikipedia:1953">1953</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[29](/wiki/29_January "29 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 135c](/wiki/Letter_135c).

## February {#February}

- **[9](/wiki/9_February "9 February")** - Actor [Ciarán Hinds](/wiki/Ciar%C3%A1n_Hinds) is born.
- **[11](/wiki/11_February "11 February")** - [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[11](/wiki/11_March "11 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 135d](/wiki/Letter_135d).
- **[24](/wiki/24_March "24 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) meets [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and then writes [letter 136](/wiki/Letter_136 "Letter 136") to his father, [Stanley Unwin](/wiki/Stanley_Unwin).
- **[30](/wiki/30_March "30 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien") move to [76 Sandfield Road](/wiki/76_Sandfield_Road).

## April {#April}

- **[11](/wiki/11_April "11 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 137](/wiki/Letter_137 "Letter 137").
- **[15](/wiki/15_April "15 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) gives the [W.P. Ker Lecture](/wiki/W.P._Ker_Lecture) at Glasgow University on the religious themes adultery, temptation and repentance in <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight" title="Sir Gawain and the Green Knight">Sir Gawain and the Green Knight</a></i>.
- **[21](/wiki/21_April "21 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 137a](/wiki/Letter_137a).

## June {#June}

- **[5](/wiki/5_June "5 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 137b](/wiki/Letter_137b).
- **[5](/wiki/5_June "5 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 137c](/wiki/Letter_137c).
- **[11](/wiki/11_June "11 June")** - *[Pearl](</wiki/Pearl_(E.V._Gordon)> "Pearl (E.V. Gordon)")*, edited by [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon) and including a contribution by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **[22](/wiki/22_June "22 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 137d](/wiki/Letter_137d).
- **[24](/wiki/24_June "24 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to George Sayer](</wiki/Letter_to_George_Sayer_(24_June_1953)> "Letter to George Sayer (24 June 1953)").
- **[27](/wiki/27_June "27 June")** - Actress [Lorri Holt](/wiki/Lorri_Holt) is born.
- **?** - [Christopher Gilson](/wiki/Christopher_Gilson), linguist, is born.

## July {#July}

- **? (mid-month)** - J.R.R. Tolkien receives galley-proofs of the first volume from [Allen and Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin); he realised that some words have been over-corrected (dwarves to dwarfs, elvish to elfish, further to farther, and worst of all, elven to elfin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **[8](/wiki/8_July "8 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 137e](/wiki/Letter_137e).
- **[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - Rayner Unwin presses Tolkien to choose titles for each volume for *Lord of the Rings*; he erroneously proposes that the general title should belong to the first volume.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## August {#August}

- **[4](/wiki/4_August "4 August")** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 138](/wiki/Letter_138).
- **[8](/wiki/8_August "8 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 139](/wiki/Letter_139 "Letter 139").
- **[15](/wiki/15_August "15 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Ronald Eames](/wiki/Letter_to_Ronald_Eames "Letter to Ronald Eames").
- **[17](/wiki/17_August "17 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) [agrees](/wiki/Letter_140 "Letter 140") with [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) on the volume titles for the book: they will be *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)* and (reluctantly) *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*.
- **[31](/wiki/31_August "31 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 140a](/wiki/Letter_140a).

## September {#September}

- **[3](/wiki/3_September "3 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 140b](/wiki/Letter_140b).
- **[8](/wiki/8_September "8 September")** - Actor [John McGlynn](/wiki/John_McGlynn) is born.
- **[23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 140c](/wiki/Letter_140c).

## October {#October}

- **[4](/wiki/4_October "4 October")** - Actor [Chris Fairbank](/wiki/Chris_Fairbank) is born.
- **[9](/wiki/9_October "9 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 141](/wiki/Letter_141 "Letter 141").
- **[15](/wiki/15_October "15 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 141a](/wiki/Letter_141a).
- **?** - *[Essays and Studies 1953](/wiki/Essays_and_Studies_1953)*, including *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth Beorhthelm's Son](/wiki/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth_Beorhthelm%27s_Son)* by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.

## November {#November}

- **[19](/wiki/19_November "19 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 141b](/wiki/Letter_141b).
- **[23](/wiki/23_November "23 November")** - Composer [Johan de Meij](/wiki/Johan_de_Meij) is born.
- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 141c](/wiki/Letter_141c).

## December {#December}

- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 141d](/wiki/Letter_141d).
- **[2](/wiki/2_December "2 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 142](/wiki/Letter_142 "Letter 142").
- **[4](/wiki/4_December "4 December")** - An issue of the magazine *[Radio Times](/wiki/Radio_Times_4_December_1953 "Radio Times 4 December 1953")* featuring an article by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **[5](/wiki/5_December "5 December")** - Actor [Christopher Guard](/wiki/Christopher_Guard) is born.
- **[10](/wiki/10_December "10 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 142a](/wiki/Letter_142a).
- **[28](/wiki/28_December "28 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Rayner Unwin](</wiki/Letter_to_Rayner_Unwin_(28_December_1953)> "Letter to Rayner Unwin (28 December 1953)").
- **[29](/wiki/29_December "29 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) visits [Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin "Allen and Unwin") at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_Street" title="wikipedia:Museum Street">Museum Street</a> in London.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 135b](/wiki/Letter_135b).
- **?** - *[Essais de philologie moderne (1951)](</wiki/Essais_de_philologie_moderne_(1951)>)*, including the essay "[Middle English 'Losenger'](/wiki/Middle_English_%27Losenger%27)" by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **Late** - [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) draws [General Map of Middle-earth](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
