Events that occurred on 16 March

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1837](1837.md)** - [Mary Wall](../M/Mary_Wall.md) dies.
- **[1916](1916.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) receives his degree from the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md).
- **[1921](1921.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is appointed to membership of the Faculty of Arts and the Board of the Faculty Arts at the [University of Leeds](../L/Leeds_University.md).
- **[1931](1931.md)** - Philosopher [Anthony Kenny](../A/Anthony_Kenny.md) is born.
- **[1933](1933.md)** - Tolkien writes [a letter to Kenneth Sisam](<../L/Letter_to_Kenneth_Sisam_(16_March_1933).md>).
- **[1949](1949.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 120](../L/Letter_120.md).
- **[1954](1954.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 143a](../L/Letter_143a.md).
- **[1964](1964.md)** - Artist [Quinton Hoover](../Q/Quinton_Hoover.md) is born.
- **[1967](1967.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Hans-Jörg Modlmayr](<../L/Letter_to_Hans-Jörg_Modlmayr_(16_March_1967).md>).
- **[1972](1972.md)** - Tolkien writes [Letter 333](../L/Letter_333.md).
- **[2004](2004.md)** - *[The World of Tolkien](../T/The_World_of_Tolkien.md)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md):
    - [Borondir](../B/Borondir.md) is on his way in search of the [Éothéod](Éothéod.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - Mustering of the [Host of the West](../A/Army_of_the_West.md). [Debate](../L/Last_Debate.md) of the commanders outside [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) travel northward along the [Morgai](../M/Morgai.md). They look out over the camp to [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md).
