[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1939" title="wikipedia:1939">1939</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[23](/wiki/23_January "23 January")** - [The Brothers Hildebrandt](/wiki/The_Brothers_Hildebrandt) are born.

## February {#February}

- **[1](/wiki/1_February "1 February")** - [Wolfgang Krege](/wiki/Wolfgang_Krege), German translator, is born.
- **[2](/wiki/2_February "2 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 35](/wiki/Letter_35).
- **[10](/wiki/10_February "10 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 36](/wiki/Letter_36).

## March {#March}

- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - [David Weatherley](/wiki/David_Weatherley), who would play [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, is born.
- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 36a](/wiki/Letter_36a).
- **[8](/wiki/8_March "8 March")** - Tolkien delivers his lecture "[On Fairy-Stories](/wiki/On_Fairy-Stories)" at St. Andrew's University, Scotland.
- **[27](/wiki/27_March "27 March")**-**[29](/wiki/29_March "29 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) completes three days' training with the top-secret Government Code and Cypher School (GCCS) at their London headquarters.

## April {#April}

- **[20](/wiki/20_April "20 April")** - Writer and screenwriter [Peter S. Beagle](/wiki/Peter_S._Beagle) is born.

## May {#May}

- **[25](/wiki/25_May "25 May")** - [Ian McKellen](/wiki/Ian_McKellen), who would play [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.

## June {#June}

- **[16](/wiki/16_June "16 June")** - [Ted Johnstone](/wiki/Ted_Johnstone) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[6](/wiki/6_September "6 September")** - [Gordon Reid](/wiki/Gordon_Reid), who would provide the voices of several characters in [*The Lord of the Rings* radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born.
- **[15](/wiki/15_September "15 September")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 36b](/wiki/Letter_36b).

## October {#October}

- **[29](/wiki/29_October "29 October")** - Actress [Elizabeth Moody](/wiki/Elizabeth_Moody) is born.

## December {#December}

- **[19](/wiki/19_December "19 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 37](/wiki/Letter_37 "Letter 37").
- **[24](/wiki/24_December "24 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[The Reeve's Tale: Version Prepared for Recitation at the 'Summer Diversions'](/wiki/The_Reeve%27s_Tale "The Reeve's Tale")* is published.
- **?** - *[Rehabilitations and Other Essays](/wiki/Rehabilitations_and_Other_Essays)* by [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), which includes references to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
