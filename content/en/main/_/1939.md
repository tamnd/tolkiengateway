[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1939" title="wikipedia:1939">1939</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[23](23_January.md)** - [The Brothers Hildebrandt](../T/The_Brothers_Hildebrandt.md) are born.

## February {#February}

- **[1](1_February.md)** - [Wolfgang Krege](../W/Wolfgang_Krege.md), German translator, is born.
- **[2](2_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 35](../L/Letter_35.md).
- **[10](10_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 36](../L/Letter_36.md).

## March {#March}

- **[1](1_March.md)** - [David Weatherley](../D/David_Weatherley.md), who would play [Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, is born.
- **[1](1_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 36a](../L/Letter_36a.md).
- **[8](8_March.md)** - Tolkien delivers his lecture "[On Fairy-Stories](../O/On_Fairy-Stories.md)" at St. Andrew's University, Scotland.
- **[27](27_March.md)**-**[29](29_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) completes three days' training with the top-secret Government Code and Cypher School (GCCS) at their London headquarters.

## April {#April}

- **[20](20_April.md)** - Writer and screenwriter [Peter S. Beagle](../P/Peter_S._Beagle.md) is born.

## May {#May}

- **[25](25_May.md)** - [Ian McKellen](../I/Ian_McKellen.md), who would play [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.

## June {#June}

- **[16](16_June.md)** - [Ted Johnstone](../T/Ted_Johnstone.md) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[6](6_September.md)** - [Gordon Reid](../G/Gordon_Reid.md), who would provide the voices of several characters in [*The Lord of the Rings* radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), is born.
- **[15](15_September.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 36b](../L/Letter_36b.md).

## October {#October}

- **[29](29_October.md)** - Actress [Elizabeth Moody](../E/Elizabeth_Moody.md) is born.

## December {#December}

- **[19](19_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 37](../L/Letter_37.md).
- **[24](24_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}
