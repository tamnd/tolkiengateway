Events that occurred on 28 August

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1898](/wiki/1898)** - [R.B. McCallum](/wiki/R.B._McCallum), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") is born
- **[1967](/wiki/1967)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to a Mr Joukes](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Joukes "Letter to Mr Joukes").
- **[1968](/wiki/1968)** - Actor [Billy Boyd](/wiki/Billy_Boyd) is born.
- **[1973](/wiki/1973)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) takes a holiday to visit friends in [Bournemouth](/wiki/Bournemouth). He would die there [5 days](/wiki/September_2 "September 2") later.
- **[1977](/wiki/1977)** - [Mythcon 8](/wiki/Mythcon_8) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_San_Diego" title="wikipedia:University of San Diego">University of San Diego</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey) reviews *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)* for *The Times Literary Supplement*.
- **[1982](/wiki/1982)** - The seventh episode of the new version of the [1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[1987](/wiki/1987)** - Voice actor [John Huston](/wiki/John_Huston) dies.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s Southern Hemisphere seminar: "Tolkien's Odysseys - Middle-earth and Beyond".
- **[2014](/wiki/2014)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2015](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2015)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) is held captive by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and the [four Hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") overtake [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) and [Wormtongue](/wiki/Wormtongue). While Frodo and his friends go to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), Saruman heads straight for [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
