Events that occurred on 29 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1939](1939.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) does the third and final day of his training with the top-secret Government Code and Cypher School (GCCS) at their London headquarters.
- **[1972](1972.md)** - Actor [Samuel Hazeldine](../S/Samuel_Hazeldine.md) is born.
- **[1981](1981.md)** - The fourth episode of the [1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- **[2000](2000.md)** - *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](../T/Tyalië_Tyelelliéva.md)* issue [14](../T/Tyalië_Tyelelliéva_14.md) is published.
- **[2012](2012.md)** - Author [Jan Broberg](../J/Jan_Broberg.md) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) leaves [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) for [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), rescued out of the fire, rest in [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).
