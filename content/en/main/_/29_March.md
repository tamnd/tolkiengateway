Events that occurred on 29 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1939](/wiki/1939)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) does the third and final day of his training with the top-secret Government Code and Cypher School (GCCS) at their London headquarters.
- **[1972](/wiki/1972)** - Actor [Samuel Hazeldine](/wiki/Samuel_Hazeldine) is born.
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - The fourth episode of the [1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[2000](/wiki/2000)** - *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va)* issue [14](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va_14 "Tyalië Tyelelliéva 14") is published.
- **[2012](/wiki/2012)** - Author [Jan Broberg](/wiki/Jan_Broberg) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) leaves [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) for [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), rescued out of the fire, rest in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).
