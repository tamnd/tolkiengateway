## January {#January}

- **[22](/wiki/22_January "22 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 143](/wiki/Letter_143).

Tolkien to provide suggestions for the dust-jacket designs for the soon-to-be-published *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.<sup id="cite_ref-ai_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ai-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rc_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. li</sup>

## May {#May}

- **[3](/wiki/3_May "3 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 144a](/wiki/Letter_144a).
- **[8](/wiki/8_May "8 May")–[15](/wiki/15_May "15 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is in [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge) as an elector for the chair of Medieval and Renaissance English, a post designed for [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis).
- **[13](/wiki/13_May "13 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 145](/wiki/Letter_145 "Letter 145").
- **[17](/wiki/17_May "17 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Henry Willink](/wiki/Letter_to_Henry_Willink "Letter to Henry Willink").
- **[22](/wiki/22_May "22 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) has a formal dinner in Cambridge.
- **[26](/wiki/26_May "26 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Letter_to_W.N._Beard_(26_May_1954)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Letter to W.N. Beard (26 May 1954) (page does not exist)">a letter to W.N. Beard</a>.

- **[10](/wiki/10_June "10 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 146a](/wiki/Letter_146a).
- **[15](/wiki/15_June "15 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 147](/wiki/Letter_147 "Letter 147").

## July {#July}

- **[20](/wiki/20_July "20 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) receives a D. Litt. from University College Dublin.
- **[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")** - **[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring), the first volume of [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings), is published**.

## August {#August}

- **[7](/wiki/7_August "7 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 148](/wiki/Letter_148 "Letter 148").
- **[14](/wiki/14_August "14 August")** - [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) [reviews *The Fellowship of the Ring*](/wiki/The_Gods_Return_to_Earth "The Gods Return to Earth") for *Time and Tide*.
- **[18](/wiki/18_August "18 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 148a](/wiki/Letter_148a).
- **[21](/wiki/21_August "21 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Nevill Coghill](/wiki/Letter_to_Nevill_Coghill "Letter to Nevill Coghill").

## September {#September}

- **[9](/wiki/9_September "9 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 149](/wiki/Letter_149 "Letter 149").
- **[9](/wiki/9_September "9 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to the Rector of the University of Liège](</wiki/Letter_to_Li%C3%A8ge_University_(9_September_1954)> "Letter to Liège University (9 September 1954)").
- **[18](/wiki/18_September "18 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes letters [150](/wiki/Letter_150 "Letter 150") and [151](/wiki/Letter_151 "Letter 151").
- **[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 152](/wiki/Letter_152 "Letter 152").
- **[25](/wiki/25_September "25 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 154](/wiki/Letter_154 "Letter 154").

## October {#October}

- **[2](/wiki/2_October "2 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) receives an honorary doctorate from the University of Liège.
- **[18](/wiki/18_October "18 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to the Rector of the University of Liège](</wiki/Letter_to_Li%C3%A8ge_University_(18_October_1954)> "Letter to Liège University (18 October 1954)").
- **[19](/wiki/19_October "19 October")** - Actor [Ken Stott](/wiki/Ken_Stott) is born.
- **[21](/wiki/21_October "21 October")** - The American edition of *The Fellowship of the Ring* is published by the [Houghton Mifflin Company](/wiki/Houghton_Mifflin_Company).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **[31](/wiki/31_October "31 October")** – "[The Hero Is a Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hero_Is_a_Hobbit)", a review by [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden) of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, is published.

## November {#November}

- **[4](/wiki/4_November "4 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 156](/wiki/Letter_156 "Letter 156").
- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to R.W. Burchfield](</wiki/Letter_to_Robert_Burchfield_(6_November_1954)> "Letter to Robert Burchfield (6 November 1954)").
- **[7](/wiki/7_November "7 November")** - [Guy Gavriel Kay](/wiki/Guy_Gavriel_Kay), who would assist [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) with the writing of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, is born.
- **[11](/wiki/11_November "11 November")** - **[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers), the second volume of [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings), is published**.
- **[17](/wiki/17_November "17 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Rayner Heppenstall](</wiki/Letter_to_Rayner_Heppenstall_(17_November_1954)> "Letter to Rayner Heppenstall (17 November 1954)").
- **[22](/wiki/22_November "22 November")** - Rayner Heppenstall writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Rayner_Heppenstall_(1-2_December_1954)> "Letter to Rayner Heppenstall (1-2 December 1954)").
- **[27](/wiki/27_November "27 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 157](/wiki/Letter_157 "Letter 157").

## December {#December}

- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) starts writing [a letter to Rayner Heppenstall](</wiki/Letter_to_Rayner_Heppenstall_(1-2_December_1954)> "Letter to Rayner Heppenstall (1-2 December 1954)").
- **[2](/wiki/2_December "2 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 158](/wiki/Letter_158 "Letter 158").
- **[2](/wiki/2_December "2 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) finishes writing [a letter to Rayner Heppenstall](</wiki/Letter_to_Rayner_Heppenstall_(1-2_December_1954)> "Letter to Rayner Heppenstall (1-2 December 1954)").
- **[3](/wiki/3_December "3 December")** - The [BBC Third Programme](/wiki/BBC "BBC") broadcasts the radio play of *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth](</wiki/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth,_Beorhthelm%27s_Son_(radio_play)> "The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth, Beorhthelm's Son (radio play)")*.
- **[13](/wiki/13_December "13 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Rayner Heppenstall](</wiki/Letter_to_Rayner_Heppenstall_(13_December_1954)> "Letter to Rayner Heppenstall (13 December 1954)").
- **[14](/wiki/14_December "14 December")** - [James Horan](/wiki/James_Horan), who would voice several characters in video games, is born.
- **[20](/wiki/20_December "20 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 158a](/wiki/Letter_158a).
- **[25](/wiki/25_December "25 December")** - Singer [Annie Lennox](/wiki/Annie_Lennox) is born.
