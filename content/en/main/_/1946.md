[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1946" title="wikipedia:1946">1946</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[10](/wiki/10_February "10 February")** - [John Tolkien](/wiki/John_Tolkien) becomes a priest.
- **[21](/wiki/21_February "21 February")** - [Anthony Daniels](/wiki/Anthony_Daniels), who would play [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"), is born.

## April {#April}

- **[14](/wiki/14_April "14 April")** - The review "[Research v. Literature](</wiki/Sunday_Times_(14_April_1946)> "Sunday Times (14 April 1946)")" by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is published.
- **[29](/wiki/29_April "29 April")** - [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter), Tolkien's biographer, is born.

## May {#May}

- **[1](/wiki/1_May "1 May")** - [Nellie Bellflower](/wiki/Nellie_Bellflower), who would play [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) in [*The Return of the King* (1980 film)](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)"), is born in Phoenix, Arizona.

## June {#June}

- **[1](/wiki/1_June "1 June")** - Actor [Brian Cox](/wiki/Brian_Cox) is born.
- **[5](/wiki/5_June "5 June")** - [John Bach](/wiki/John_Bach), who would play [Madril](/wiki/Madril) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.

## July {#July}

- **[7](/wiki/7_July "7 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a postcard to G.E. Selby](</wiki/Letter_to_G.E._Selby_(7_July_1946)> "Letter to G.E. Selby (7 July 1946)").
- **[21](/wiki/21_July "21 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 105](/wiki/Letter_105 "Letter 105").
- **[23](/wiki/23_July "23 July")** - Philologist [G.H. Cowling](/wiki/G.H._Cowling) dies.

## August {#August}

- **[2](/wiki/2_August "2 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](</wiki/Letter_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(2_August_1946)> "Letter to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski (2 August 1946)").

## September {#September}

- **[30](/wiki/30_September "30 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 106](/wiki/Letter_106 "Letter 106").

## October {#October}

- **[16](/wiki/16_October "16 October")** - [Percy Bates](/wiki/Percy_Bates) dies.
- **[18](/wiki/18_October "18 October")** - Composer [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[4](/wiki/4_November "4 November")** - [John Callen](/wiki/John_Callen), who would play [Óin](/wiki/%C3%93in) in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), is born.

## December {#December}

- **[7](/wiki/7_December "7 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 107](/wiki/Letter_107 "Letter 107").

## Other {#Other}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) works on the [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) language.
