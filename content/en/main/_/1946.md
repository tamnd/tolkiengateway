[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1946" title="wikipedia:1946">1946</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[10](10_February.md)** - [John Tolkien](../J/John_Tolkien.md) becomes a priest.
- **[21](21_February.md)** - [Anthony Daniels](../A/Anthony_Daniels.md), who would play [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>), is born.

## April {#April}

- **[14](14_April.md)** - The review "[Research v. Literature](<../S/Sunday_Times_(14_April_1946).md>)" by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is published.
- **[29](29_April.md)** - [Humphrey Carpenter](../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md), Tolkien's biographer, is born.

## May {#May}

- **[1](1_May.md)** - [Nellie Bellflower](../N/Nellie_Bellflower.md), who would play [Éowyn](Éowyn.md) in [*The Return of the King* (1980 film)](<../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md>), is born in Phoenix, Arizona.

## June {#June}

- **[1](1_June.md)** - Actor [Brian Cox](../B/Brian_Cox.md) is born.
- **[5](5_June.md)** - [John Bach](../J/John_Bach.md), who would play [Madril](../M/Madril.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.

## July {#July}

- **[7](7_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a postcard to G.E. Selby](<../L/Letter_to_G.E._Selby_(7_July_1946).md>).
- **[21](21_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 105](../L/Letter_105.md).
- **[23](23_July.md)** - Philologist [G.H. Cowling](../G/G.H._Cowling.md) dies.

## August {#August}

- **[2](2_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](<../L/Letter_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(2_August_1946).md>).

## September {#September}

- **[30](30_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 106](../L/Letter_106.md).

## October {#October}

- **[16](16_October.md)** - [Percy Bates](../P/Percy_Bates.md) dies.
- **[18](18_October.md)** - Composer [Howard Shore](../H/Howard_Shore.md) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[4](4_November.md)** - [John Callen](../J/John_Callen.md), who would play [Óin](Óin.md) in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>), is born.

## December {#December}

- **[7](7_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 107](../L/Letter_107.md).

## Other {#Other}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) works on the [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) language.
