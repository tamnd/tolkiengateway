<i><b>únat</b></i> (pl. <i><b>únati</b></i>) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) noun meaning "a thing impossible to be or to be done". The term refers to physical laws established by [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) that are impossible to break. The *únati* contrast with the *[axani](/wiki/Axan "Axan")*, which are laws established by Ilúvatar that lesser beings should not, but can, disobey. [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor) refused to accept any *axani* as binding upon him, but he was unable to violate the *únati* much to his frustration. Since the *únati* remained, they were a perpetual reminder of the existence of Eru and His invincibility, which fed Melkor's unceasing and unappeasable rage.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word consists of the prefix *ú-*, meaning "not, un-, in- (usually with bad sense)", and *nat*, meaning "thing".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
