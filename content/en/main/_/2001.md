[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001" title="wikipedia:2001">2001</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - The [Peruvian Tolkien Society](/wiki/Peruvian_Tolkien_Society) is founded.
- **[17](/wiki/17_January "17 January")** - [Richard E. Blackwelder](/wiki/Richard_E._Blackwelder), Tolkien collector, dies.
- **?** - [Other Hands 31/32](/wiki/Other_Hands_31/32) is published.

## February {#February}

- **[26](/wiki/26_February "26 February")** - Philologist [Brian Miller](/wiki/Brian_Miller), student and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), dies.

## March {#March}

- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 16 is published.

## April {#April}

- **[5](/wiki/5_April "5 April")** - Actor [Theodore Isidore Gottlieb](/wiki/Theodore_Isidore_Gottlieb) dies.
- **[28](/wiki/28_April "28 April")** - Actor [Paul Daneman](/wiki/Paul_Daneman) dies.

## May {#May}

- **[11](/wiki/11_May "11 May")** - *[Kinder Surprise](/wiki/Kinder_Surprise)* begins its *Lord of the Rings* promotion in Germany.

## June {#June}

- **[25](/wiki/25_June "25 June")** - Actress [Pauline Letts](/wiki/Pauline_Letts) dies.
- **[27](/wiki/27_June "27 June")** - Artist [Tove Jansson](/wiki/Tove_Jansson) dies.

## July {#July}

- **[5](/wiki/5_July "5 July")** - [Tolkien Thing III](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing "Tolkien Thing").
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 42](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_42) is published.
- **?** - [Other Hands 33/34](/wiki/Other_Hands_33/34) is published.

## August {#August}

- **[3](/wiki/3_August "3 August")-[6](/wiki/6_August "6 August")** - [Mythcon 32](/wiki/Mythcon_32).

## September {#September}

- **[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")-[23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2001](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2001).

## October {#October}

- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")**- The parody book *[The Roadkill of Middle Earth](/wiki/The_Roadkill_of_Middle_Earth)* is published.
- **[31](/wiki/31_October "31 October")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 17 is published.

## November {#November}

- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - [Decipher](/wiki/Decipher) publishes the first set of "[Decipher Cards](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game "The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game")", *The Fellowship of the Ring*.
- **[9](/wiki/9_November "9 November")** - Translator [Tore Zetterholm](/wiki/Tore_Zetterholm) dies.
- **[10](/wiki/10_November "10 November")** - The premiere of the first episode of the Slovak radio serial adaptation *[Pán prsteňov](</wiki/P%C3%A1n_prste%C5%88ov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series)> "Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)")*.
- **[19](/wiki/19_November "19 November")** - *[Meditations on Middle-earth](/wiki/Meditations_on_Middle-earth)* is published.
- **[20](/wiki/20_November "20 November")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)* is released in public.
- **[27](/wiki/27_November "27 November")** - *[Music Inspired by Middle Earth](/wiki/Music_Inspired_by_Middle_Earth)*, a musical album featuring many artists, including [David Arkenstone](/wiki/David_Arkenstone), is released.
- **[29](/wiki/29_November "29 November")** - *[Quest for the Ring](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Ring)* airs on FOX TV.
- **?** - *[Finding God in The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Finding_God_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[4](/wiki/4_December "4 December")** - The documentary *[Master of the Rings](/wiki/Master_of_the_Rings:_The_Unauthorized_Story_Behind_J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_%27Lord_of_the_Rings%27 "Master of the Rings: The Unauthorized Story Behind J.R.R. Tolkien's 'Lord of the Rings'")* is released.
- **[10](/wiki/10_December "10 December")** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)** premieres in Odeon Leicester Square, London.
- **[15](/wiki/15_December "15 December")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 18 is published.
- **[15](/wiki/15_December "15 December")** - Conclusion of the first series (season) of the Slovak radio serial adaptation *[Pán prsteňov](</wiki/P%C3%A1n_prste%C5%88ov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series)> "Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)")*.
- **[19](/wiki/19_December "19 December")** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)** is released in cinemas world-wide.
- **?** - *[Tolkien: Man and Myth](/wiki/Tolkien:_Man_and_Myth)* is published.
