[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001" title="wikipedia:2001">2001</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](3_January.md)** - The [Peruvian Tolkien Society](../P/Peruvian_Tolkien_Society.md) is founded.
- **[17](17_January.md)** - [Richard E. Blackwelder](../R/Richard_E._Blackwelder.md), Tolkien collector, dies.
- **?** - [Other Hands 31/32](../O/Other_Hands_31%252F32.md) is published.

## February {#February}

- **[26](26_February.md)** - Philologist [Brian Miller](../B/Brian_Miller.md), student and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), dies.

## March {#March}

- **[1](1_March.md)** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](../T/Tyalie_Tyelellieva.md)* issue 16 is published.

## April {#April}

- **[5](5_April.md)** - Actor [Theodore Isidore Gottlieb](../T/Theodore_Isidore_Gottlieb.md) dies.
- **[28](28_April.md)** - Actor [Paul Daneman](../P/Paul_Daneman.md) dies.

## May {#May}

- **[11](11_May.md)** - *[Kinder Surprise](../K/Kinder_Surprise.md)* begins its *Lord of the Rings* promotion in Germany.

## June {#June}

- **[25](25_June.md)** - Actress [Pauline Letts](../P/Pauline_Letts.md) dies.
- **[27](27_June.md)** - Artist [Tove Jansson](../T/Tove_Jansson.md) dies.

## July {#July}

- **[5](5_July.md)** - [Tolkien Thing III](../T/Tolkien_Thing.md).
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 42](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_42.md) is published.
- **?** - [Other Hands 33/34](../O/Other_Hands_33%252F34.md) is published.

## August {#August}

- **[3](3_August.md)-[6](6_August.md)** - [Mythcon 32](../M/Mythcon_32.md).

## September {#September}

- **[21](21_September.md)-[23](23_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2001](../O/Oxonmoot_2001.md).

## October {#October}

- **[30](30_October.md)**- The parody book *[The Roadkill of Middle Earth](../T/The_Roadkill_of_Middle_Earth.md)* is published.
- **[31](31_October.md)** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](../T/Tyalie_Tyelellieva.md)* issue 17 is published.

## November {#November}

- **[6](6_November.md)** - [Decipher](../D/Decipher.md) publishes the first set of "[Decipher Cards](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md)", *The Fellowship of the Ring*.
- **[9](9_November.md)** - Translator [Tore Zetterholm](../T/Tore_Zetterholm.md) dies.
- **[10](10_November.md)** - The premiere of the first episode of the Slovak radio serial adaptation *[Pán prsteňov](<../P/Pán_prsteňov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series).md>)*.
- **[19](19_November.md)** - *[Meditations on Middle-earth](../M/Meditations_on_Middle-earth.md)* is published.
- **[20](20_November.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack.md)* is released in public.
- **[27](27_November.md)** - *[Music Inspired by Middle Earth](../M/Music_Inspired_by_Middle_Earth.md)*, a musical album featuring many artists, including [David Arkenstone](../D/David_Arkenstone.md), is released.
- **[29](29_November.md)** - *[Quest for the Ring](../Q/Quest_for_the_Ring.md)* airs on FOX TV.
- **?** - *[Finding God in The Lord of the Rings](../F/Finding_God_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[4](4_December.md)** - The documentary *[Master of the Rings](../M/Master_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Unauthorized_Story_Behind_J.R.R._Tolkien's_'Lord_of_the_Rings'.md)* is released.
- **[10](10_December.md)** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)** premieres in Odeon Leicester Square, London.
- **[15](15_December.md)** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](../T/Tyalie_Tyelellieva.md)* issue 18 is published.
- **[15](15_December.md)** - Conclusion of the first series (season) of the Slovak radio serial adaptation *[Pán prsteňov](<../P/Pán_prsteňov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series).md>)*.
- **[19](19_December.md)** - **[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)** is released in cinemas world-wide.
- **?** - *[Tolkien: Man and Myth](../T/Tolkien%253A_Man_and_Myth.md)* is published.
