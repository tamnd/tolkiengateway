[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1917" title="wikipedia:1917">1917</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[2](/wiki/2_January "2 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to the War Office](/wiki/Letter_to_the_War_Office "Letter to the War Office").
- **[12](/wiki/12_January "12 January")** - [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s leave of absence (to recover from his <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/trench_fever" title="wikipedia:trench fever">trench fever</a>) ends, but he is far from recovered.
- **[23](/wiki/23_January "23 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) faces a Medical Board in [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham), but he is not deemed fit to return to [war](/wiki/World_War_I "World War I").

## February {#February}

- **[27](/wiki/27_February "27 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) faces his third Medical Board, and again his leave is extended.

## April {#April}

- **[15](/wiki/15_April "15 April")** - Voice actor [Hans Conried](/wiki/Hans_Conried) is born.
- **[18](/wiki/18_April "18 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [Christopher Wiseman](/wiki/Christopher_Wiseman), the Great Twin Brethren, meet in Harrogate, and discuss the fate of the [Barrovians](/wiki/T.C.B.S. "T.C.B.S.") and the future of the Society.
- **[19](/wiki/19_April "19 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), in revalidation, is assigned to the Humber Garrison, but is not fit enough.
- Around that time, Tolkien conceives the story of [Beren and Lúthien](/wiki/Beren_and_L%C3%BAthien) in a small wood near Roos in Holderness.
- **[29](/wiki/29_April "29 April")** - [William Squire](/wiki/William_Squire), who would play [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in [The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)>), is born.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## May {#May}

- **[1](/wiki/1_May "1 May")** - Actress [Pauline Letts](/wiki/Pauline_Letts) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[1](/wiki/1_June "1 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is considered fit for service again, but he is not reassigned to the Lancashire Fusilliers.
- **[18](/wiki/18_June "18 June")** - [Richard Boone](/wiki/Richard_Boone), who would voice [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) in [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)"), is born.
- **[29](/wiki/29_June "29 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) succumbs to gastritis while at Brocton Camp.

## July {#July}

- **?** - [Thomas Kenneth Barnsley](/wiki/Thomas_Kenneth_Barnsley) is killed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## August {#August}

- **[1](/wiki/1_August "1 August")** - ["Tea Cake" Barnsley](/wiki/Thomas_Kenneth_Barnsley "Thomas Kenneth Barnsley") is killed in Ypres, Belgium.

## October {#October}

- **[13](/wiki/13_October "13 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is found unfit for duty for six months, but able to do a desk job.

## November {#November}

- **[16](/wiki/16_November "16 November")** - The Tolkien's first son, [John](/wiki/John_Tolkien "John Tolkien"), is born.
- **[24](/wiki/24_November "24 November")** - Actor [John Justin](/wiki/John_Justin) is born.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) begins work on *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One)*.
