[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1917" title="wikipedia:1917">1917</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[2](2_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to the War Office](../L/Letter_to_the_War_Office.md).
- **[12](12_January.md)** - [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s leave of absence (to recover from his <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/trench_fever" title="wikipedia:trench fever">trench fever</a>) ends, but he is far from recovered.
- **[23](23_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) faces a Medical Board in [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md), but he is not deemed fit to return to [war](../W/World_War_I.md).

## February {#February}

- **[27](27_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) faces his third Medical Board, and again his leave is extended.

## April {#April}

- **[15](15_April.md)** - Voice actor [Hans Conried](../H/Hans_Conried.md) is born.
- **[18](18_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [Christopher Wiseman](../C/Christopher_Wiseman.md), the Great Twin Brethren, meet in Harrogate, and discuss the fate of the [Barrovians](../T/T.C.B.S..md) and the future of the Society.
- **[19](19_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), in revalidation, is assigned to the Humber Garrison, but is not fit enough.
- Around that time, Tolkien conceives the story of [Beren and Lúthien](../B/Beren_and_Lúthien.md) in a small wood near Roos in Holderness.
- **[29](29_April.md)** - [William Squire](../W/William_Squire.md), who would play [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) in [The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>), is born.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## May {#May}

- **[1](1_May.md)** - Actress [Pauline Letts](../P/Pauline_Letts.md) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[1](1_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is considered fit for service again, but he is not reassigned to the Lancashire Fusilliers.
- **[18](18_June.md)** - [Richard Boone](../R/Richard_Boone.md), who would voice [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) in [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>), is born.
- **[29](29_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) succumbs to gastritis while at Brocton Camp.

## July {#July}

- **?** - [Thomas Kenneth Barnsley](../T/Thomas_Kenneth_Barnsley.md) is killed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## August {#August}

- **[1](1_August.md)** - ["Tea Cake" Barnsley](../T/Thomas_Kenneth_Barnsley.md) is killed in Ypres, Belgium.

## October {#October}

- **[13](13_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is found unfit for duty for six months, but able to do a desk job.

## November {#November}

- **[16](16_November.md)** - The Tolkien's first son, [John](../J/John_Tolkien.md), is born.
- **[24](24_November.md)** - Actor [John Justin](../J/John_Justin.md) is born.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) begins work on *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One.md)*.
