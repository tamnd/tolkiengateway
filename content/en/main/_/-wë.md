<i><b>-wë</b></i> (or *-we*) is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) suffix used mainly in masculine names more usual in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). It is translated as "man" or simply "person".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJAD1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJAD1-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

It was seen in names of the Elves of [Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md) and the [Great March](../G/Great_March.md): [Morwë](../M/Morwë.md), [Nurwë](../N/Nurwë.md), [Ingwë](../I/Ingwë.md), [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md), [Elwë](../E/Elwë.md), [Olwë](../O/Olwë.md), [Nowë](../N/Nowë.md), and [Lenwë](../L/Lenwë.md).

The suffix appears frequently in other Quenya names of the First Age, such as [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md), generally but not exclusively masculine.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The ending was also seen in [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md),<sup id="cite_ref-WJAD1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJAD1-2">[2]</a></sup> and perhaps also in [Eönwë](../E/Eönwë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The element, having the archaic sense of "person",<sup id="cite_ref-finwe_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-finwe-6">[6]</a></sup> can also rarely be found in feminine names, such as [Elenwë](../E/Elenwë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md), *-we* derives from the [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) ending <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=-wego&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-wego (page does not exist)">-wego</a></i> (from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=WEG&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="WEG (page does not exist)">WEG</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the later text "[The Shibboleth of Fëanor](../T/The_Shibboleth_of_Fëanor.md)", the ending derives from the stem <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=EWE&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="EWE (page does not exist)">EWE</a>, which is also an ancient word for "person".<sup id="cite_ref-finwe_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-finwe-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

The element is seen in names of [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) form which in turn perhaps represents [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md). The name ending did not seem to survive in later generations or in other languages; an exception is the element *-u* seen only in the name *[Elu](../E/Elu.md)* (from \**Elwego*) who was better known as [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md). The Elf *Nowë* became better known as [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

Olwë became [Olue](../O/Olue.md) in [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md). Lenwë was known as [Denweg](../D/Denweg.md).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
