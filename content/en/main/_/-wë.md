<i><b>-wë</b></i> (or *-we*) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) suffix used mainly in masculine names more usual in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). It is translated as "man" or simply "person".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJAD1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJAD1-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

It was seen in names of the Elves of [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen) and the [Great March](/wiki/Great_March): [Morwë](/wiki/Morw%C3%AB), [Nurwë](/wiki/Nurw%C3%AB), [Ingwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB), [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB), [Elwë](/wiki/Elw%C3%AB), [Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB), [Nowë](/wiki/Now%C3%AB), and [Lenwë](/wiki/Lenw%C3%AB).

The suffix appears frequently in other Quenya names of the First Age, such as [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), generally but not exclusively masculine.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The ending was also seen in [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB),<sup id="cite_ref-WJAD1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJAD1-2">[2]</a></sup> and perhaps also in [Eönwë](/wiki/E%C3%B6nw%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The element, having the archaic sense of "person",<sup id="cite_ref-finwe_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-finwe-6">[6]</a></sup> can also rarely be found in feminine names, such as [Elenwë](/wiki/Elenw%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), *-we* derives from the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) ending <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-wego&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-wego (page does not exist)">-wego</a></i> (from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=WEG&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="WEG (page does not exist)">WEG</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the later text "[The Shibboleth of Fëanor](/wiki/The_Shibboleth_of_F%C3%ABanor)", the ending derives from the stem <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=EWE&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="EWE (page does not exist)">EWE</a>, which is also an ancient word for "person".<sup id="cite_ref-finwe_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-finwe-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

The element is seen in names of [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) form which in turn perhaps represents [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin). The name ending did not seem to survive in later generations or in other languages; an exception is the element *-u* seen only in the name *[Elu](/wiki/Elu)* (from \**Elwego*) who was better known as [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). The Elf *Nowë* became better known as [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

Olwë became [Olue](/wiki/Olue) in [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin). Lenwë was known as [Denweg](/wiki/Denweg).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
