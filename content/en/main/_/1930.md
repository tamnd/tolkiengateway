[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930" title="wikipedia:1930">1930</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[12](12_January.md)** - Musician [Glenn Yarbrough](../G/Glenn_Yarbrough.md) is born.

## February {#February}

- **[27](27_February.md)** - [Joseph Wright](../J/Joseph_Wright.md), former tutor of Tolkien, dies.

## April {#April}

- **[18](18_April.md)** - [Clive Revill](../C/Clive_Revill.md), who would voice [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) in the [2003 video game](<../T/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game).md>) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, is born.

## May {#May}

- **[27](27_May.md)** - The University Press of Oxford sends [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) a copy of [*A Middle English Reader*](../L/List_of_books_in_Tolkien's_library.md#A_Middle_English_Reader)*.*
- **[29](29_May.md)** - "[The Oxford English School](../T/The_Oxford_English_School.md)", an essay by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.

## June {#June}

- **[3](3_June.md)** - Author [Marion Zimmer Bradley](../M/Marion_Zimmer_Bradley.md) is born.
- **[9](9_June.md)** - Author [Lin Carter](../L/Lin_Carter.md) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[25](25_August.md)** - [Bruce Allpress](../B/Bruce_Allpress.md), who would play Aldor in [The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md), is born.

## September {#September}

- **[23](23_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) completes a manuscript of [The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun](../T/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun.md)
- **[28](28_September.md)** - Illustrator [Livia Rusz](../L/Livia_Rusz.md) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[28](28_November.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## December {#December}

- **[24](24_December.md)** - Father Christmas writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) completes a full draft of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, which is later printed in *[The Shaping of Middle-earth](../T/The_Shaping_of_Middle-earth.md)*.
- **?** - *[War in Heaven](../W/War_in_Heaven.md)*, first novel of [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md) [Charles Williams](../C/Charles_Williams.md), is published.
