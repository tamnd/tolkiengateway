[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1930" title="wikipedia:1930">1930</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[12](/wiki/12_January "12 January")** - Musician [Glenn Yarbrough](/wiki/Glenn_Yarbrough) is born.

## February {#February}

- **[27](/wiki/27_February "27 February")** - [Joseph Wright](/wiki/Joseph_Wright), former tutor of Tolkien, dies.

## April {#April}

- **[18](/wiki/18_April "18 April")** - [Clive Revill](/wiki/Clive_Revill), who would voice [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) in the [2003 video game](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)> "The Hobbit (2003 video game)") of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, is born.

## May {#May}

- **[27](/wiki/27_May "27 May")** - The University Press of Oxford sends [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) a copy of [*A Middle English Reader*](/wiki/List_of_books_in_Tolkien%27s_library#A_Middle_English_Reader "List of books in Tolkien's library")*.*
- **[29](/wiki/29_May "29 May")** - "[The Oxford English School](/wiki/The_Oxford_English_School)", an essay by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.

## June {#June}

- **[3](/wiki/3_June "3 June")** - Author [Marion Zimmer Bradley](/wiki/Marion_Zimmer_Bradley) is born.
- **[9](/wiki/9_June "9 June")** - Author [Lin Carter](/wiki/Lin_Carter) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[25](/wiki/25_August "25 August")** - [Bruce Allpress](/wiki/Bruce_Allpress), who would play Aldor in [The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers), is born.

## September {#September}

- **[23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) completes a manuscript of [The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun)
- **[28](/wiki/28_September "28 September")** - Illustrator [Livia Rusz](/wiki/Livia_Rusz) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[28](/wiki/28_November "28 November")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## December {#December}

- **[24](/wiki/24_December "24 December")** - Father Christmas writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) completes a full draft of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, which is later printed in *[The Shaping of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Shaping_of_Middle-earth)*.
- **?** - *[War in Heaven](/wiki/War_in_Heaven)*, first novel of [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") [Charles Williams](/wiki/Charles_Williams), is published.
