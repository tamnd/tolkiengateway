[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012" title="wikipedia:2012">2012</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](3_January.md)** - [Birthday Toast](../B/Birthday_Toast.md#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[19](19_January.md)** - *Do Balrogs Have Wings?* by Andrew Rilstone is published.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 233](../A/Amon_Hen_233.md) is published.

## February {#February}

- **[25](25_February.md)** - *[Tolkien and Wagner: The Ring and Der Ring](../T/Tolkien_and_Wagner.md)* is published.

## March {#March}

- **[8](8_March.md)** - Author [Robert Giddings](../R/Robert_Giddings.md) dies.
- **[12](12_March.md)** - [Update 6: Shores of the Great River](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md#Update_6:_Shores_of_the_Great_River), the sixth free update of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)*, is released.
- **[20](20_March.md)** - *Makers Of The Middle Ages* by Richard Utz and Elizabeth Emery is published.
- **[25](25_March.md)** - [Tolkien Reading Day](../T/Tolkien_Reading_Day.md).
- **[25](25_March.md)** - *Pipe Smoking In Middle Earth* by Mark Irwin is published.
- **[29](29_March.md)** - Author [Jan Broberg](../J/Jan_Broberg.md) dies.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 234](../A/Amon_Hen_234.md) is published.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 53](../M/Mallorn_53.md) is published.

## April {#April}

- **[19](19_April.md)** - *[Tolkien in East Yorkshire 1917 - 1918](../T/Tolkien_in_East_Yorkshire_1917_-_1918.md)* by Phil Mathison is published.
- **[27](27_April.md) - [29](29_April.md)** - [Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft](../D/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft.md) 9th Seminar.

## May {#May}

- **[4](4_May.md)** - *[JRR Tolkien: The True Lord of the Rings](../J/JRR_Tolkien%253A_The_True_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, a graphic novel, is published.
- **[19](19_May.md) - [20](20_May.md)** - <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Middle-earth_Weekend&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Middle-earth Weekend (page does not exist)">Middle-earth Weekend</a> at [Sarehole Mill](../S/Sarehole_Mill.md), [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md).
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 235](../A/Amon_Hen_235.md) is published.

## June {#June}

- **[2](2_June.md) - [3](3_June.md)** - [Unquendor](../U/Unquendor.md) and [Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft](../D/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft.md)'s International Tolkien Day.
- **[7](7_June.md)** - *Pocket Posh J.R.R. Tolkien* by The Puzzle Society is published.
- **[9](9_June.md)** - *[The Broken Scythe: Death and Immortality in the Works of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Broken_Scythe.md)* is published.
- **[17](17_June.md)** - *The Hobbit Novel Guide* by Shan C. Gillard is published.
- **[20](20_June.md)** - *[Wagner and Tolkien: Mythmakers](../W/Wagner_and_Tolkien.md)* is published.
- **[24](24_June.md)** - Actor [James Grout](../J/James_Grout.md) dies.
- **[26](26_June.md)** - *[Tolkien and Welsh](../T/Tolkien_and_Welsh.md)* by [Mark T. Hooker](../M/Mark_T._Hooker.md) is published.

## July {#July}

- **[6](6_July.md)** - Principal photography of <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md" title="The Hobbit (film series)">The Hobbit<i> films</i></a> ends.</i>
- **[12](12_July.md) - [15](15_July.md)** - [Tolkien Thing](../T/Tolkien_Thing.md) 2012.
- **[15](15_July.md)** - *The International Relations of Middle-Earth* by Abigail E. Ruane and Patrick James is published.
- **[26](26_July.md)** - *[The Saga of Hrolf Kraki](../T/The_Saga_of_Hrolf_Kraki.md)*, translated by [Stella M. Mills](../S/Stella_M._Mills.md), is published in a new edition, with a Foreword by [Priscilla Tolkien](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md).
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 236](../A/Amon_Hen_236.md) is published.

## August {#August}

- **[3](3_August.md)** - [*Parma Eldalamberon* 20: *The Qenya Alphabet*](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_20.md) by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is published.
- **[3](3_August.md) - [6](6_August.md)** - [Mythcon 43](../M/Mythcon_43.md) at University of California, Berkeley.
- **[11](11_August.md)** - *[Hobbit to Hero](../H/Hobbit_to_Hero.md)* by Elizabeth M. Stephen is published.
- **[16](16_August.md) - [20](20_August.md)** - [The Return of the Ring](../T/The_Return_of_the_Ring.md), [Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) conference at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loughborough_University" title="wikipedia:Loughborough University">Loughborough University</a>.
- **[30](30_August.md)** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: Annual 2013](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey%253A_Annual_2013.md)* is published.
- **[30](30_August.md)** - [Tolkien Calendar 2013](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2013.md) is published.
- **?** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 9](../T/Tolkien_Studies%253A_Volume_9.md)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[1](1_September.md)** - *There and Back Again: J. R. R. Tolkien and the Origins of the Hobbit* by Mark Atherton is published.
- **[5](5_September.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Riders of Rohan](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Riders_of_Rohan.md)* is released.
- **[12](12_September.md)** - An updated edition of [Charles Dixon](../C/Charles_Dixon.md) and [David T. Wenzel](../D/David_T._Wenzel.md)'s *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(comic_book).md>)* is published.
- **[13](13_September.md)** - *Hobbitus Ille*, the Latin translation of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, is published.
- **[14](14_September.md)** - An article about [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and the artist [Mary Fairburn](../M/Mary_Fairburn.md) is published in *[Times Literary Supplement](../T/Times_Literary_Supplement.md)*.
- **[21](21_September.md) - [23](23_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2012](../O/Oxonmoot_2012.md) at Lady Margaret Hall.
- **[22](22_September.md)** - *[Exploring J.R.R. Tolkien's The Hobbit](../E/Exploring_J.R.R._Tolkien's_The_Hobbit.md)* by [Corey Olsen](../C/Corey_Olsen.md) is published.
- **[26](26_September.md)** - *[Tolkien's World: A Guide to the Peoples and Places of Middle-Earth](<../T/Tolkien's_World_(by_Gareth_Hanrahan).md>)* by [Gareth Hanrahan](../G/Gareth_Hanrahan.md) and Peter McKinstry is published.
- **[27](27_September.md)** - A revised edition of *[Letters from Father Christmas](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md)* is published.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 237](../A/Amon_Hen_237.md) is published.

- **[1](1_October.md)** - <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=On_the_Shoulders_of_Hobbits:_The_Road_to_Virtue_with_Tolkien_and_Lewis&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="On the Shoulders of Hobbits: The Road to Virtue with Tolkien and Lewis (page does not exist)">On the Shoulders of Hobbits: The Road to Virtue with Tolkien and Lewis</a></i> by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Louis_Markos&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Louis Markos (page does not exist)">Louis Markos</a> is published.
- **[3](3_October.md)** - *[The Hobbit and Philosophy](../T/The_Hobbit_and_Philosophy.md)* by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Gregory_Bassham&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gregory Bassham (page does not exist)">Gregory Bassham</a> and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Eric_Bronson&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Eric Bronson (page does not exist)">Eric Bronson</a> is published.

- **[30](30_October.md)** - <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=J.R.R._Tolkien:_The_Making_of_a_Legend&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="J.R.R. ## November {#November}

- **[1](1_November.md)** - *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Miscellany](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Miscellany.md)* by [Robert S. Blackham](../R/Robert_S._Blackham.md) is published.
- **[6](6_November.md)** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: The Movie Storybook](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey%253A_The_Movie_Storybook.md)* by Paddy Kempshall is published.
- **[6](6_November.md)** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: Official Movie Guide](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey%253A_Official_Movie_Guide.md)* by [Brian Sibley](../B/Brian_Sibley.md) is published.
- **[6](6_November.md)** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: Visual Companion](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey%253A_Visual_Companion.md)* by [Jude Fisher](../J/Jude_Fisher.md) is published.
- **[6](6_November.md)** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: The World of Hobbits](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey%253A_The_World_of_Hobbits.md)* by Paddy Kempshall is published.
- **[8](8_November.md)** - *[The Hobbit: Kingdoms of Middle-earth](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_Kingdoms_of_Middle-earth.md)* is released online.
- **[13](13_November.md)** - *The Tolkien Years of the Brothers Hildebrandt* is published.
- **[17](17_November.md) - [18](18_November.md)** - [Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft](../D/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft.md)'s The Hobbit Party.
- **[19](19_November.md)** - The [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md), [The Tolkien Trust](../T/The_Tolkien_Trust.md) and [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md) file another [lawsuit](../L/Lawsuits.md) with [New Line Cinema](../N/New_Line_Cinema.md) and the [Saul Zaentz Company](../S/Saul_Zaentz_Company.md) over merchandising rights.
- **[27](27_November.md)** - *[The Hobbits: The Many Lives of Bilbo, Frodo, Sam, Merry and Pippin](../T/The_Hobbits.md)* is published.
- **[30](30_November.md)** - *Amazing &amp; Extraordinary Facts: J R R Tolkien* by [Colin Duriez](../C/Colin_Duriez.md) is published.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 238](../A/Amon_Hen_238.md) is published.

- **[4](4_December.md)** - *[Guardians of Middle-earth](../G/Guardians_of_Middle-earth.md)* is released on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_3" title="wikipedia:PlayStation 3">PlayStation 3</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_360" title="wikipedia:Xbox 360">Xbox 360</a>.

- <b><a href="12_December.md" title="12 December">12</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i> is released in New Zealand.</b>
- <b><a href="13_December.md" title="13 December">13</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i> is released in Europe.</b>
