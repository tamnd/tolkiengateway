[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2012" title="wikipedia:2012">2012</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[19](/wiki/19_January "19 January")** - *Do Balrogs Have Wings?* by Andrew Rilstone is published.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 233](/wiki/Amon_Hen_233 "Amon Hen 233") is published.

## February {#February}

- **[25](/wiki/25_February "25 February")** - *[Tolkien and Wagner: The Ring and Der Ring](/wiki/Tolkien_and_Wagner "Tolkien and Wagner")* is published.

## March {#March}

- **[8](/wiki/8_March "8 March")** - Author [Robert Giddings](/wiki/Robert_Giddings) dies.
- **[12](/wiki/12_March "12 March")** - [Update 6: Shores of the Great River](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online#Update_6:_Shores_of_the_Great_River "The Lord of the Rings Online"), the sixth free update of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*, is released.
- **[20](/wiki/20_March "20 March")** - *Makers Of The Middle Ages* by Richard Utz and Elizabeth Emery is published.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - *Pipe Smoking In Middle Earth* by Mark Irwin is published.
- **[29](/wiki/29_March "29 March")** - Author [Jan Broberg](/wiki/Jan_Broberg) dies.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 234](/wiki/Amon_Hen_234 "Amon Hen 234") is published.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 53](/wiki/Mallorn_53 "Mallorn 53") is published.

## April {#April}

- **[19](/wiki/19_April "19 April")** - *[Tolkien in East Yorkshire 1917 - 1918](/wiki/Tolkien_in_East_Yorkshire_1917_-_1918)* by Phil Mathison is published.
- **[27](/wiki/27_April "27 April") - [29](/wiki/29_April "29 April")** - [Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft](/wiki/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft) 9th Seminar.

## May {#May}

- **[4](/wiki/4_May "4 May")** - *[JRR Tolkien: The True Lord of the Rings](/wiki/JRR_Tolkien:_The_True_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, a graphic novel, is published.
- **[19](/wiki/19_May "19 May") - [20](/wiki/20_May "20 May")** - <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Middle-earth_Weekend&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Middle-earth Weekend (page does not exist)">Middle-earth Weekend</a> at [Sarehole Mill](/wiki/Sarehole_Mill), [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham).
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 235](/wiki/Amon_Hen_235 "Amon Hen 235") is published.

## June {#June}

- **[2](/wiki/2_June "2 June") - [3](/wiki/3_June "3 June")** - [Unquendor](/wiki/Unquendor) and [Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft](/wiki/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft)'s International Tolkien Day.
- **[7](/wiki/7_June "7 June")** - *Pocket Posh J.R.R. Tolkien* by The Puzzle Society is published.
- **[9](/wiki/9_June "9 June")** - *[The Broken Scythe: Death and Immortality in the Works of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Broken_Scythe "The Broken Scythe")* is published.
- **[17](/wiki/17_June "17 June")** - *The Hobbit Novel Guide* by Shan C. Gillard is published.
- **[20](/wiki/20_June "20 June")** - *[Wagner and Tolkien: Mythmakers](/wiki/Wagner_and_Tolkien "Wagner and Tolkien")* is published.
- **[24](/wiki/24_June "24 June")** - Actor [James Grout](/wiki/James_Grout) dies.
- **[26](/wiki/26_June "26 June")** - *[Tolkien and Welsh](/wiki/Tolkien_and_Welsh)* by [Mark T. Hooker](/wiki/Mark_T._Hooker) is published.

## July {#July}

- **[6](/wiki/6_July "6 July")** - Principal photography of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)">The Hobbit<i> films</i></a> ends.</i>
- **[12](/wiki/12_July "12 July") - [15](/wiki/15_July "15 July")** - [Tolkien Thing](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing) 2012.
- **[15](/wiki/15_July "15 July")** - *The International Relations of Middle-Earth* by Abigail E. Ruane and Patrick James is published.
- **[26](/wiki/26_July "26 July")** - *[The Saga of Hrolf Kraki](/wiki/The_Saga_of_Hrolf_Kraki)*, translated by [Stella M. Mills](/wiki/Stella_M._Mills), is published in a new edition, with a Foreword by [Priscilla Tolkien](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien).
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 236](/wiki/Amon_Hen_236 "Amon Hen 236") is published.

## August {#August}

- **[3](/wiki/3_August "3 August")** - [*Parma Eldalamberon* 20: *The Qenya Alphabet*](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_20 "Parma Eldalamberon 20") by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is published.
- **[3](/wiki/3_August "3 August") - [6](/wiki/6_August "6 August")** - [Mythcon 43](/wiki/Mythcon_43) at University of California, Berkeley.
- **[11](/wiki/11_August "11 August")** - *[Hobbit to Hero](/wiki/Hobbit_to_Hero)* by Elizabeth M. Stephen is published.
- **[16](/wiki/16_August "16 August") - [20](/wiki/20_August "20 August")** - [The Return of the Ring](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_Ring), [Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society "The Tolkien Society") conference at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loughborough_University" title="wikipedia:Loughborough University">Loughborough University</a>.
- **[30](/wiki/30_August "30 August")** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: Annual 2013](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey:_Annual_2013)* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_August "30 August")** - [Tolkien Calendar 2013](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2013) is published.
- **?** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 9](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_9)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[1](/wiki/1_September "1 September")** - *There and Back Again: J. R. R. Tolkien and the Origins of the Hobbit* by Mark Atherton is published.
- **[5](/wiki/5_September "5 September")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Riders of Rohan](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Riders_of_Rohan)* is released.
- **[12](/wiki/12_September "12 September")** - An updated edition of [Charles Dixon](/wiki/Charles_Dixon) and [David T. Wenzel](/wiki/David_T._Wenzel)'s *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(comic_book)> "The Hobbit (comic book)")* is published.
- **[13](/wiki/13_September "13 September")** - *Hobbitus Ille*, the Latin translation of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, is published.
- **[14](/wiki/14_September "14 September")** - An article about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and the artist [Mary Fairburn](/wiki/Mary_Fairburn) is published in *[Times Literary Supplement](/wiki/Times_Literary_Supplement)*.
- **[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September") - [23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2012](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2012) at Lady Margaret Hall.
- **[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")** - *[Exploring J.R.R. Tolkien's The Hobbit](/wiki/Exploring_J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_The_Hobbit)* by [Corey Olsen](/wiki/Corey_Olsen) is published.
- **[26](/wiki/26_September "26 September")** - *[Tolkien's World: A Guide to the Peoples and Places of Middle-Earth](</wiki/Tolkien%27s_World_(by_Gareth_Hanrahan)> "Tolkien's World (by Gareth Hanrahan)")* by [Gareth Hanrahan](/wiki/Gareth_Hanrahan) and Peter McKinstry is published.
- **[27](/wiki/27_September "27 September")** - A revised edition of *[Letters from Father Christmas](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas)* is published.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 237](/wiki/Amon_Hen_237 "Amon Hen 237") is published.

## October {#October}

- **[1](/wiki/1_October "1 October")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=A_Hobbit_Journey:_Discovering_the_Enchantment_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_Middle-Earth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="A Hobbit Journey: Discovering the Enchantment of J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s Middle-Earth (page does not exist)">A Hobbit Journey: Discovering the Enchantment of J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s Middle-Earth</a></i> by [Matthew Dickerson](/wiki/Matthew_Dickerson) is published.
- **[1](/wiki/1_October "1 October")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=On_the_Shoulders_of_Hobbits:_The_Road_to_Virtue_with_Tolkien_and_Lewis&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="On the Shoulders of Hobbits: The Road to Virtue with Tolkien and Lewis (page does not exist)">On the Shoulders of Hobbits: The Road to Virtue with Tolkien and Lewis</a></i> by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Louis_Markos&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Louis Markos (page does not exist)">Louis Markos</a> is published.
- **[3](/wiki/3_October "3 October")** - *[The Hobbit and Philosophy](/wiki/The_Hobbit_and_Philosophy)* by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gregory_Bassham&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gregory Bassham (page does not exist)">Gregory Bassham</a> and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Eric_Bronson&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Eric Bronson (page does not exist)">Eric Bronson</a> is published.
- **[18](/wiki/18_October "18 October")** - *A Brief Guide to J. R. R. Tolkien* by Nigel Cawthorne is published.
- **[25](/wiki/25_October "25 October")** - *What Would Bilbo Do? The Unofficial Hobbit Handbook* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=J.R.R._Tolkien:_The_Making_of_a_Legend&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="J.R.R. Tolkien: The Making of a Legend (page does not exist)">J.R.R. Tolkien: The Making of a Legend</a></i> by [Colin Duriez](/wiki/Colin_Duriez) is published.
- **?** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Christian_World_of_the_Hobbit&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Christian World of the Hobbit (page does not exist)">The Christian World of the Hobbit</a></i> is published.

## November {#November}

- **[1](/wiki/1_November "1 November")** - *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Miscellany](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Miscellany)* by [Robert S. Blackham](/wiki/Robert_S._Blackham) is published.
- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: The Movie Storybook](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey:_The_Movie_Storybook)* by Paddy Kempshall is published.
- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: Official Movie Guide](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey:_Official_Movie_Guide)* by [Brian Sibley](/wiki/Brian_Sibley) is published.
- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: Visual Companion](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey:_Visual_Companion)* by [Jude Fisher](/wiki/Jude_Fisher) is published.
- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: The World of Hobbits](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey:_The_World_of_Hobbits)* by Paddy Kempshall is published.
- **[8](/wiki/8_November "8 November")** - *[The Hobbit: Kingdoms of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_Kingdoms_of_Middle-earth)* is released online.
- **[13](/wiki/13_November "13 November")** - *The Tolkien Years of the Brothers Hildebrandt* is published.
- **[17](/wiki/17_November "17 November") - [18](/wiki/18_November "18 November")** - [Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft](/wiki/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft)'s The Hobbit Party.
- **[19](/wiki/19_November "19 November")** - The [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate), [The Tolkien Trust](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Trust) and [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) file another [lawsuit](/wiki/Lawsuits "Lawsuits") with [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) and the [Saul Zaentz Company](/wiki/Saul_Zaentz_Company) over merchandising rights.
- **[27](/wiki/27_November "27 November")** - *[The Hobbits: The Many Lives of Bilbo, Frodo, Sam, Merry and Pippin](/wiki/The_Hobbits "The Hobbits")* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - *Amazing &amp; Extraordinary Facts: J R R Tolkien* by [Colin Duriez](/wiki/Colin_Duriez) is published.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 238](/wiki/Amon_Hen_238 "Amon Hen 238") is published.

## December {#December}

- **[4](/wiki/4_December "4 December")** - *[Guardians of Middle-earth](/wiki/Guardians_of_Middle-earth)* is released on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_3" title="wikipedia:PlayStation 3">PlayStation 3</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_360" title="wikipedia:Xbox 360">Xbox 360</a>.
- **[11](/wiki/11_December "11 December")** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)* is released
- <b><a href="/wiki/12_December" title="12 December">12</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i> is released in New Zealand.</b>
- <b><a href="/wiki/13_December" title="13 December">13</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i> is released in Europe.</b>
- <b><a href="/wiki/14_December" title="14 December">14</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i> is released in India and North America.</b>
- **[14](/wiki/14_December "14 December")** - *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: Chronicles: Art &amp; Design](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey:_Chronicles:_Art_%26_Design)* is published.
- **[20](/wiki/20_December "20 December")** - *Shamanic Odyssey: Homer, Tolkien, and the Visionary Experience* by Robert Tindall and Susana Bustos is published.
- <b><a href="/wiki/26_December" title="26 December">26</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i> is released in Australia.</b>
- **?** - [The Hobbit Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/The_Hobbit_Strategy_Battle_Game) is released.
