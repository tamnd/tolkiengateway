Events that occurred on 21 July.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1920](1920.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is appointed to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reader_(academic_rank)" title="wikipedia:Reader (academic rank)">Readership</a> in English Language at the [University of Leeds](../L/Leeds_University.md).
- **[1938](1938.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to A.F. Colborn](<../L/Letter_to_A.F._Colborn_(21_July_1938).md>).
- **[1946](1946.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 105](../L/Letter_105.md).
- **[1962](1962.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 239a](../L/Letter_239a.md).
- **[1965](1965.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 273](../L/Letter_273.md).
- **[1967](1967.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 296](../L/Letter_296.md).
- **[1996](1996.md)** – Actor [Wolfe Morris](../W/Wolfe_Morris.md) dies.
- **[1998](1998.md)** – The Royal Mail published a new set of stamps, "Magical Worlds: Classic Fantasy Books for Children" with art by Peter Malone. One of the stamps features [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md).
- **[2009](2009.md)** – Artist [Heinz Edelmann](../H/Heinz_Edelmann.md) dies.
- **[2011](2011.md)** – *[Light Beyond All Shadow: Religious Experience in Tolkien's Work](../L/Light_Beyond_All_Shadow.md)* is published.
- **[2015](2015.md)** – Actor [Theodore Bikel](../T/Theodore_Bikel.md) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) is held captive by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The funeral procession of king [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) travels toward [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md)
