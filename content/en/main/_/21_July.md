Events that occurred on 21 July.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1920](/wiki/1920)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is appointed to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reader_(academic_rank)" title="wikipedia:Reader (academic rank)">Readership</a> in English Language at the [University of Leeds](/wiki/Leeds_University "Leeds University").
- **[1938](/wiki/1938)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to A.F. Colborn](</wiki/Letter_to_A.F._Colborn_(21_July_1938)> "Letter to A.F. Colborn (21 July 1938)").
- **[1946](/wiki/1946)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 105](/wiki/Letter_105).
- **[1962](/wiki/1962)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 239a](/wiki/Letter_239a "Letter 239a").
- **[1965](/wiki/1965)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 273](/wiki/Letter_273).
- **[1967](/wiki/1967)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 296](/wiki/Letter_296).
- **[1996](/wiki/1996)** – Actor [Wolfe Morris](/wiki/Wolfe_Morris) dies.
- **[1998](/wiki/1998)** – The Royal Mail published a new set of stamps, "Magical Worlds: Classic Fantasy Books for Children" with art by Peter Malone. One of the stamps features [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") and [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug).
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** – Artist [Heinz Edelmann](/wiki/Heinz_Edelmann) dies.
- **[2011](/wiki/2011)** – *[Light Beyond All Shadow: Religious Experience in Tolkien's Work](/wiki/Light_Beyond_All_Shadow "Light Beyond All Shadow")* is published.
- **[2015](/wiki/2015)** – Actor [Theodore Bikel](/wiki/Theodore_Bikel) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) is held captive by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - The funeral procession of king [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) travels toward [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras)
