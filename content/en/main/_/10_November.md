Events that occurred on 10 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1943](1943.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 51b](../L/Letter_51b.md).
- **[1955](1955.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 174](../L/Letter_174.md).
- **[1958](1958.md)** - [C. S. Lewis](../C/C._S._Lewis.md) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- **[1963](1963.md)** - *[Hobbits and Heroes](<../T/The_Sunday_Telegraph_(10_November_1963).md>)*, which includes extracts of an interview with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published in *The Sunday Telegraph*.
- **[1968](1968.md)** - *The Gathering of the Clouds*, the seventh episode of the [1968 radio series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, is aired on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).
- **[2003](2003.md)** - *[Secret Fire: The Spiritual Vision of J.R.R. Tolkien](../S/Secret_Fire%253A_The_Spiritual_Vision_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* is published.
- **[2006](2006.md)** - [Ring\*Con 2006](../R/Ring%252ACon_2006.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulda" title="wikipedia:Fulda">Fulda</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany" title="wikipedia:Germany">Germany</a>.
- **[2011](2011.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md)* is released in France.
- **[2021](2021.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Fate of Gundabad](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Fate_of_Gundabad.md)* expansion is released.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md):
    - [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), guided by [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md), travels to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) in order to deliver [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md)'s message to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2994](../T/Third_Age_2994.md):
    - [Balin](../B/Balin.md), Lord of [Moria](../M/Moria.md), is killed by an Orc archer as he stood to watch in [Mirrormere](../M/Mirrormere.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) rest in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).
