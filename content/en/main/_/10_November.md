Events that occurred on 10 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1943](/wiki/1943)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 51b](/wiki/Letter_51b).
- **[1955](/wiki/1955)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 174](/wiki/Letter_174).
- **[1958](/wiki/1958)** - [C. S. Lewis](/wiki/C._S._Lewis) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- **[1963](/wiki/1963)** - *[Hobbits and Heroes](</wiki/The_Sunday_Telegraph_(10_November_1963)> "The Sunday Telegraph (10 November 1963)")*, which includes extracts of an interview with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published in *The Sunday Telegraph*.
- **[1968](/wiki/1968)** - *The Gathering of the Clouds*, the seventh episode of the [1968 radio series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)") of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, is aired on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - *[Secret Fire: The Spiritual Vision of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Secret_Fire:_The_Spiritual_Vision_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)* is published.
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - [Ring\*Con 2006](/wiki/Ring*Con_2006) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulda" title="wikipedia:Fulda">Fulda</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany" title="wikipedia:Germany">Germany</a>.
- **[2011](/wiki/2011)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North)* is released in France.
- **[2021](/wiki/2021)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Fate of Gundabad](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Fate_of_Gundabad)* expansion is released.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2994](/wiki/Third_Age_2994 "Third Age 2994"):
    - [Balin](/wiki/Balin), Lord of [Moria](/wiki/Moria), is killed by an Orc archer as he stood to watch in [Mirrormere](/wiki/Mirrormere).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") rest in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").
