<i><b>-ima</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) suffix meaning "-able". (e.g. *mátima* edible) This suffix is noted "-ima" whence a lengthening of the *sundóma* in the examples *mátima* and *nótima*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
