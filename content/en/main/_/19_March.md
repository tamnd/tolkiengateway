Events that occurred on 19 March

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1884](1884.md)** - Philologist [Elias Lönnrot](../E/Elias_Lönnrot.md), who compiled the *[Kalevala](../K/Kalevala.md)*, dies.
- **[1918](1918.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) attends a medical board examination. 9th Royal Defence Corps at Easington is disbanded and Tolkien is to be based at Thirtle Bridge again.
- **[1956](1956.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 185](../L/Letter_185.md).
- **[1989](1989.md)** - [Heren Hyarmen](../H/Heren_Hyarmen.md) is founded by [Ronald Kyrmse](../R/Ronald_Kyrmse.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Paulo" title="wikipedia:São Paulo">São Paulo</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil" title="wikipedia:Brazil">Brazil</a>. On [9 January](9_January.md) [2003](2003.md), the name was changed to "Heren Hyarmeno", the correct [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) genitive to mean "Order of the South".
- **[2000](2000.md)** - Voice actor [Fraser Kerr](../F/Fraser_Kerr.md) dies.
- **[2013](2013.md)** - *[La Conexion Española de J.R.R. Tolkien](../L/La_Conexion_Española_de_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* is published in Spain.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md):
    - [Borondir](../B/Borondir.md) is on his way in search of the [Éothéod](Éothéod.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - [The Host of the West](../A/Army_of_the_West.md) reaches the [Morgul Vale](../M/Morgul_Vale.md). The Infantry joins Cavalry.
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) escape the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and begin their journey along the road to [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md).
