Events that occurred on 19 March

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1884](/wiki/1884)** - Philologist [Elias Lönnrot](/wiki/Elias_L%C3%B6nnrot), who compiled the *[Kalevala](/wiki/Kalevala)*, dies.
- **[1918](/wiki/1918)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) attends a medical board examination. 9th Royal Defence Corps at Easington is disbanded and Tolkien is to be based at Thirtle Bridge again.
- **[1956](/wiki/1956)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 185](/wiki/Letter_185).
- **[1989](/wiki/1989)** - [Heren Hyarmen](/wiki/Heren_Hyarmen) is founded by [Ronald Kyrmse](/wiki/Ronald_Kyrmse) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Paulo" title="wikipedia:São Paulo">São Paulo</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil" title="wikipedia:Brazil">Brazil</a>. On [9 January](/wiki/9_January) [2003](/wiki/2003), the name was changed to "Heren Hyarmeno", the correct [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) genitive to mean "Order of the South".
- **[2000](/wiki/2000)** - Voice actor [Fraser Kerr](/wiki/Fraser_Kerr) dies.
- **[2013](/wiki/2013)** - *[La Conexion Española de J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/La_Conexion_Espa%C3%B1ola_de_J.R.R._Tolkien)* is published in Spain.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"):
    - [Borondir](/wiki/Borondir) is on his way in search of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [The Host of the West](/wiki/Army_of_the_West "Army of the West") reaches the [Morgul Vale](/wiki/Morgul_Vale). The Infantry joins Cavalry.
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") escape the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and begin their journey along the road to [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr).
