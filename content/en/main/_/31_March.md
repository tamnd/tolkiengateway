Events that occurred on 31 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1979](1979.md)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Scholar and Storyteller](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_Scholar_and_Storyteller.md)* is published.
- **[2006](2006.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings 1954-2004: Scholarship in Honor of Richard E. Blackwelder](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_1954-2004.md)* is published.
- **[2008](2008.md)** - *[Tolkien's Gedling](../T/Tolkien's_Gedling.md)* is published.
- **[2008](2008.md)** - [Stormfront Studios](../S/Stormfront_Studios.md), developers of the [2002](2002.md) video game *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(video_game).md>)*, ceases trading.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

*Note: Months of the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) only have 30 days, so no events have nor will happen on the 31st.*
