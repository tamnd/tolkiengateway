Events that occurred on 31 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1979](/wiki/1979)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Scholar and Storyteller](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Scholar_and_Storyteller)* is published.
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - *[The Lord of the Rings 1954-2004: Scholarship in Honor of Richard E. Blackwelder](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_1954-2004 "The Lord of the Rings 1954-2004")* is published.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - *[Tolkien's Gedling](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Gedling)* is published.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - [Stormfront Studios](/wiki/Stormfront_Studios), developers of the [2002](/wiki/2002) video game *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (video game)")*, ceases trading.
