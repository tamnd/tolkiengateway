<i><b>-uva</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) future tense ending.

A final -a drops out before the ending -uva is added; quanta- "fill", future tense quantuva "shall fill".

## Examples {#Examples}

- lait**uva**lmet = "we shall bless them"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- tir**uva**ntes = “they will guard it”
- enquant**uva** = "shall refill"
- cen**uva** = "Shall see"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- entul**uva** = "Come again" <sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
