**Éothain** is a [boy](../R/Rohirrim.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) who was sent to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) with his sister. Éothain was invented by [Warner Bros.](../W/Warner_Bros..md) for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), where he was portrayed by [Sam Comery](../S/Sam_Comery.md).

## History {#History}

Éothain and his younger sister [Freda](../F/Freda.md) were sent by their mother [Morwen](<../M/Morwen_(film_character).md>) to alert [King Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) that the [Wild Men](../D/Dunlendings.md) were pillaging through [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). Éothain and Freda were kept safe at [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) until Théoden led his people to [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md), where they were reunited with their mother.
