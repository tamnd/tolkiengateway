Events that occurred on 5 March

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1959](1959.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to William Ready](<../L/Letter_to_William_Ready_(5_March_1959).md>).
- **[1964](1964.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Eileen Elgar](<../L/Letter_to_Eileen_Elgar_(5_March_1964).md>) (see [Letter 255](../L/Letter_255.md)) and encloses the manuscripts [Concerning ... 'The Hoard'](../C/Concerning_..._'The_Hoard'.md) and [Kinship of the Half-elven](../K/Kinship_of_the_Half-elven.md).
- **[1967](1967.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 294a](../L/Letter_294a.md).
- **[1971](1971.md)** - Voice actor [Yuri Lowenthal](../Y/Yuri_Lowenthal.md) is born.
- **[1983](1983.md)** - Actor [Owain Arthur](../O/Owain_Arthur.md) is born.
- **[2004](2004.md)** - The first day of "[RingCon IV](../R/RingCon.md)" in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle" title="wikipedia:Seattle">Seattle</a>.
- **[2015](2015.md)** - Artist and linguist [Tom Loback](../T/Tom_Loback.md) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - [Wormtongue](../G/Gríma.md) reaches [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) at morn.
    - [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) and company reach [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) at noon. They have a parley with [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) and camp at [Dol Baran](../D/Dol_Baran.md).
    - [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) looks into the [Orthanc-stone](../O/Orthanc-stone.md) and a short time later a [winged Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) passes over the camp. In response, [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) sets out with the Hobbit for [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) hides in sight of the [Morannon](../M/Morannon.md), and leaves at dusk.
