## February {#February}

- **[21](/wiki/21_February "21 February")** - [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden), friend of Tolkien, is born.

## April {#April}

- **[23](/wiki/23_April "23 April")** - Author [Paul H. Kocher](/wiki/Paul_H._Kocher) is born.

## May {#May}

- **[9](/wiki/9_May "9 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) receives the book *[Dictionary in Englysshe and Welshe](/wiki/List_of_books_in_Tolkien%27s_library#Dictionary_in_Englysshe_and_Welshe "List of books in Tolkien's library")*.

## November {#November}

- **[27](/wiki/27_November "27 November")** - Author [L. Sprague de Camp](/wiki/L._Sprague_de_Camp) is born.

## December {#December}

- **[13](/wiki/13_December "13 December")** - [Ebba-Margareta von Freymann](/wiki/Ebba-Margareta_von_Freymann), a German poetess who would translate the poems in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* for [Margaret Carroux](/wiki/Margaret_Carroux), is born.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Actor [Duncan McIntyre](/wiki/Duncan_McIntyre) is born.
- **?** - Scholar [I.L. Gordon](/wiki/I.L._Gordon) is born.
- **Autumn** - Tolkien starts creating the [Naffarin](/wiki/Naffarin) language.
