[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1967" title="wikipedia:1967">1967</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **Early** - [Joy Hill](/wiki/Joy_Hill) sends [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) a copy of *[The Children's Treasury of Literature](/wiki/The_Children%27s_Treasury_of_Literature)*.
- **[2](/wiki/2_January "2 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Otto B. Lindhardt](/wiki/Letter_to_Otto_B._Lindhardt "Letter to Otto B. Lindhardt").
- **[2](/wiki/2_January "2 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Peyton Moss](/wiki/Letter_to_Peyton_Moss "Letter to Peyton Moss").
- **[5](/wiki/5_January "5 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Joy Hill](</wiki/Letter_to_Joy_Hill_(5_January_1967)> "Letter to Joy Hill (5 January 1967)").
- **[11](/wiki/11_January "11 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 293b](/wiki/Letter_293b "Letter 293b").
- **[15](/wiki/15_January "15 January")** - [Philip Norman's interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) - is published in *[The Sunday Times Magazine](/wiki/The_Sunday_Times_Magazine_15_January_1967 "The Sunday Times Magazine 15 January 1967")* and in *[The New York Times Magazine](/wiki/The_New_York_Times_Magazine_15_January_1967 "The New York Times Magazine 15 January 1967")*.

## February {#February}

- **[8](/wiki/8_February "8 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 294](/wiki/Letter_294).
- **[8](/wiki/8_February "8 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to C.N. Manlove](/wiki/Letter_to_C.N._Manlove "Letter to C.N. Manlove").
- **[8](/wiki/8_February "8 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to David L. Sands](/wiki/Letter_to_David_L._Sands "Letter to David L. Sands").
- **[20](/wiki/20_February "20 February")** - Actress [Katherine Soucie](/wiki/Kath_Soucie "Kath Soucie") is born.
- **?** - [L. Sprague de Camp](/wiki/L._Sprague_de_Camp) visits [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

## March {#March}

- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Hans-Jörg Modlmayr](</wiki/Letter_to_Hans-J%C3%B6rg_Modlmayr_(1_March_1967)> "Letter to Hans-Jörg Modlmayr (1 March 1967)").
- **[5](/wiki/5_March "5 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 294a](/wiki/Letter_294a "Letter 294a").
- **[9](/wiki/9_March "9 March")** - Actor [Neil Kaplan](/wiki/Neil_Kaplan) is born.
- **[16](/wiki/16_March "16 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Hans-Jörg Modlmayr](</wiki/Letter_to_Hans-J%C3%B6rg_Modlmayr_(16_March_1967)> "Letter to Hans-Jörg Modlmayr (16 March 1967)").
- **[20](/wiki/20_March "20 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 294b](/wiki/Letter_294b "Letter 294b").
- **[21](/wiki/21_March "21 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Letter_to_Humphrey_Carpenter "Letter to Humphrey Carpenter").
- **[24](/wiki/24_March "24 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 295](/wiki/Letter_295).
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - *The Scotsman* publishes an interview of William Foster with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

## April {#April}

- **[12](/wiki/12_April "12 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 295a](/wiki/Letter_295a "Letter 295a").
- **[17](/wiki/17_April "17 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to J.D. Gilbert](/wiki/Letter_to_J.D._Gilbert "Letter to J.D. Gilbert").
- **?[21](/wiki/21_April "21 April")-[28](/wiki/28_April "28 April")** - [William Ready](/wiki/William_Ready) [visits J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Letter_to_John_Leyerle "Letter to John Leyerle") sometime between these dates.
- **[28](/wiki/28_April "28 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to John Leyerle](/wiki/Letter_to_John_Leyerle "Letter to John Leyerle").

## May {#May}

- **[9](/wiki/9_May "9 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Oscar Morland](/wiki/Letter_to_Oscar_Morland "Letter to Oscar Morland").
- **[15](/wiki/15_May "15 May")** - Linguist [Ivan Derzhanski](/wiki/Ivan_Derzhanski) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[12](/wiki/12_June "12 June")** - [Donald Swann](/wiki/Donald_Swann) and [William Elvin](/wiki/William_Elvin) record the music for *[The Road goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")*.
- **[15](/wiki/15_June "15 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) records verses for the record *[Poems and Songs of Middle Earth](/wiki/Poems_and_Songs_of_Middle_Earth)*.
- **[29](/wiki/29_June "29 June")** - [Gene Deitch](/wiki/Gene_Deitch)'s [adaptation](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_film)> "The Hobbit (1967 film)") of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is shown to a limited audience in New York City.

## July {#July}

- **[12](/wiki/12_July "12 July")-[13](/wiki/13_July "13 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 295b](/wiki/Letter_295b "Letter 295b").
- **[21](/wiki/21_July "21 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 296](/wiki/Letter_296 "Letter 296").
- **[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - *[The Ballad of Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/The_Ballad_of_Bilbo_Baggins)* is performed by [Leonard Nimoy](/wiki/Leonard_Nimoy) in the TV-show <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malibu_U" title="wikipedia:Malibu U">Malibu U</a></i>.

## August {#August}

- **[1](/wiki/1_August "1 August")** - Actor [Cameron Rhodes](/wiki/Cameron_Rhodes) is born.
- **[28](/wiki/28_August "28 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to a Mr Joukes](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Joukes "Letter to Mr Joukes").
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 297](/wiki/Letter_297 "Letter 297").

## September {#September}

- **[11](/wiki/11_September "11 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 298](/wiki/Letter_298 "Letter 298").
- **[18](/wiki/18_September "18 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Elsie Honeybourne](</wiki/Letter_to_Elsie_Honeybourne_(18_September_1967)> "Letter to Elsie Honeybourne (18 September 1967)").

## October {#October}

- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Letter_to_Joy_Hill_(30_October_1967)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Letter to Joy Hill (30 October 1967) (page does not exist)">a letter to Joy Hill</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[31](/wiki/31_October "31 October")** - *[The Road Goes Ever On, A Song Cycle](/wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On,_A_Song_Cycle)* is first published in the United States.

## November {#November}

- **[9](/wiki/9_November "9 November")** - *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)* is first published in the UK.

## December {#December}

- **[3](/wiki/3_December "3 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 298a](/wiki/Letter_298a "Letter 298a").
- **[4](/wiki/4_December "4 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Roger Verhulst](</wiki/Letter_to_Roger_Verhulst_(4_December_1967)> "Letter to Roger Verhulst (4 December 1967)").
- **[7](/wiki/7_December "7 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Hans-Jörg Modlmayr](</wiki/Letter_to_Hans-J%C3%B6rg_Modlmayr_(7_December_1967)> "Letter to Hans-Jörg Modlmayr (7 December 1967)").
- **[12](/wiki/12_December "12 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 299](/wiki/Letter_299 "Letter 299").
- **[13](/wiki/13_December "13 December")** - [Margaret Carroux](/wiki/Margaret_Carroux) comes to meet [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford) to talk about her German translation.
- **[14](/wiki/14_December "14 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Rosfrith Murray](/wiki/Letter_to_Rosfrith_Murray "Letter to Rosfrith Murray").
- **[16](/wiki/16_December "16 December")** - [Miranda Otto](/wiki/Miranda_Otto), who portrayed [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.
- **[17](/wiki/17_December "17 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and his wife went to see *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_stage_adaptation)> "The Hobbit (1967 stage adaptation)")* musical performance.
- **[27](/wiki/27_December "27 December")** – Artist [Josef Madlener](/wiki/Josef_Madlener) dies.
- **[30](/wiki/30_December "30 December")** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Letter_to_Donald_Swann_(30_December_1967)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Letter to Donald Swann (30 December 1967) (page does not exist)">a letter to Donald Swann</a>.
