[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1967" title="wikipedia:1967">1967</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **Early** - [Joy Hill](../J/Joy_Hill.md) sends [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) a copy of *[The Children's Treasury of Literature](../T/The_Children's_Treasury_of_Literature.md)*.
- **[2](2_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Otto B. Lindhardt](../L/Letter_to_Otto_B._Lindhardt.md).
- **[2](2_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Peyton Moss](../L/Letter_to_Peyton_Moss.md).
- **[5](5_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Joy Hill](<../L/Letter_to_Joy_Hill_(5_January_1967).md>).
- **[11](11_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 293b](../L/Letter_293b.md).
- **[15](15_January.md)** - [Philip Norman's interview](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) - is published in *[The Sunday Times Magazine](../T/The_Sunday_Times_Magazine_15_January_1967.md)* and in *[The New York Times Magazine](../T/The_New_York_Times_Magazine_15_January_1967.md)*.

## February {#February}

- **[8](8_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to C.N. Manlove](../L/Letter_to_C.N._Manlove.md).
- **[8](8_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to David L. Sands](../L/Letter_to_David_L._Sands.md).
- **[20](20_February.md)** - Actress [Katherine Soucie](../K/Kath_Soucie.md) is born.
- **?** - [L. Sprague de Camp](../L/L._Sprague_de_Camp.md) visits [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

- **[1](1_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Hans-Jörg Modlmayr](<../L/Letter_to_Hans-Jörg_Modlmayr_(1_March_1967).md>).

- **[21](21_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Humphrey Carpenter](../L/Letter_to_Humphrey_Carpenter.md).
- **[24](24_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 295](../L/Letter_295.md).
- **[25](25_March.md)** - *The Scotsman* publishes an interview of William Foster with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

- **[12](12_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 295a](../L/Letter_295a.md).
- **[17](17_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to J.D. Gilbert](../L/Letter_to_J.D._Gilbert.md).
- **?[21](21_April.md)-[28](28_April.md)** - [William Ready](../W/William_Ready.md) [visits J.R.R. Tolkien](../L/Letter_to_John_Leyerle.md) sometime between these dates.
- **[28](28_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to John Leyerle](../L/Letter_to_John_Leyerle.md).

## May {#May}

- **[9](9_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Oscar Morland](../L/Letter_to_Oscar_Morland.md).
- **[15](15_May.md)** - Linguist [Ivan Derzhanski](../I/Ivan_Derzhanski.md) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[12](12_June.md)** - [Donald Swann](../D/Donald_Swann.md) and [William Elvin](../W/William_Elvin.md) record the music for *[The Road goes Ever On](<../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book).md>)*.
- **[15](15_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) records verses for the record *[Poems and Songs of Middle Earth](../P/Poems_and_Songs_of_Middle_Earth.md)*.
- **[29](29_June.md)** - [Gene Deitch](../G/Gene_Deitch.md)'s [adaptation](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1967_film).md>) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is shown to a limited audience in New York City.

## July {#July}

- **[12](12_July.md)-[13](13_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 295b](../L/Letter_295b.md).
- **[21](21_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 296](../L/Letter_296.md).
- **[28](28_July.md)** - *[The Ballad of Bilbo Baggins](../T/The_Ballad_of_Bilbo_Baggins.md)* is performed by [Leonard Nimoy](../L/Leonard_Nimoy.md) in the TV-show <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malibu_U" title="wikipedia:Malibu U">Malibu U</a></i>.

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 297](../L/Letter_297.md).

- **[13](13_December.md)** - [Margaret Carroux](../M/Margaret_Carroux.md) comes to meet [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md) to talk about her German translation.
- **[14](14_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Rosfrith Murray](../L/Letter_to_Rosfrith_Murray.md).
- **[16](16_December.md)** - [Miranda Otto](../M/Miranda_Otto.md), who portrayed [Éowyn](Éowyn.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.
- **[17](17_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and his wife went to see *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1967_stage_adaptation).md>)* musical performance.
- **[27](27_December.md)** – Artist [Josef Madlener](../J/Josef_Madlener.md) dies.
- **[30](30_December.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Letter_to_Donald_Swann_(30_December_1967)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Letter to Donald Swann (30 December 1967) (page does not exist)">a letter to Donald Swann</a>.
