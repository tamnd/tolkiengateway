Events that occurred on 14 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1905](/wiki/1905)** - [Gervase Mathew](/wiki/Gervase_Mathew), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") is born.
- **[1925](/wiki/1925)** - [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") [John Wain](/wiki/John_Wain) is born.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Mines of Moria](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Mines_of_Moria)* is announced.
- **[2020](/wiki/2020)** - Filming for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* begins.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"):
    - [Borondir](/wiki/Borondir) is on his way in search of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") finds [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") in the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol).
    - [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) is [besieged](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor "Siege of Gondor").
    - [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) sails towards [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith)
    - The [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) travel towards [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). Led by the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain) they reach the [Grey Wood](/wiki/Grey_Wood).

        - [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) cross the [Carnen](/wiki/Carnen) and push [King](/wiki/King_of_Dale "King of Dale") [Brand](/wiki/Brand) back to [Dale](/wiki/Dale).
        - [Men of Dale](/wiki/Men_of_Dale) and [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor) fight a great battle at the feet of [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor).
