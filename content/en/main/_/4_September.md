Events that occurred on 4 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1918](1918.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) attends a medical board examination in Kingston-upon-Hull.
- **[1936](1936.md)** - German translator [Hans J. Schütz](../H/Hans_J._Schütz.md) is born
- **[1950](1950.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 128a](../L/Letter_128a.md).
- **[1957](1957.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 200a](../L/Letter_200a.md).
- **[1957](1957.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) meets with some American producers interested in making an animated version of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* (see [Letter 201](../L/Letter_201.md)).
- **[1960](1960.md)** - The first organized Tolkien Club, [The Fellowship of the Ring](<../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(fangroup).md>), becomes formally constituted.
- **[1968](1968.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mary Fairburn](<../L/Letter_to_Mary_Fairburn_(4_September_1968).md>).
- **[1970](1970.md)** – The [first Mythcon](../M/Mythcon_1.md) is held at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Mudd_College" title="wikipedia:Harvey Mudd College">Harvey Mudd College</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont,_California" title="wikipedia:Claremont, California">Claremont</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1971](1971.md)** - [Mythcon 2](../M/Mythcon_2.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Barbara,_California" title="wikipedia:Santa Barbara, California">Santa Barbara</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1982](1982.md)** - The eighth episode of the new version of the [1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- **[2020](2020.md)** - A second edition of *[The First and Second Ages](../T/The_First_and_Second_Ages.md)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) is held captive by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
