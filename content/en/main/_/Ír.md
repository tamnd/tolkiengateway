**Ír** was an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_people" title="wikipedia:Irish people">Irish</a> king, according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ire_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ire-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Ír was the father of [Íring](Íring.md), who was commissioned by an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Saxons" title="wikipedia:Anglo-Saxons">Anglo-Saxon</a> king [Watol](../W/Watol.md) to build a road through his lands, which he was able to do in seven years. However, Watol's sons, the [Wætlingas](../W/Wætlingas.md), resented Íring's arrogance, and wagered that they could build an even better road in only three years.

King Ír, who was allied with the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) of the island of [Íwerin](Íwerin.md) (i.e. <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland" title="wikipedia:Ireland">Ireland</a>), however, sent "all kinds of secret evils" at his son's behest, in order to stop the Wætlingas from reaching their goal.<sup id="cite_ref-Ire_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ire-1">[1]</a></sup>
