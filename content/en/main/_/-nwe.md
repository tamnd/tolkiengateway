<i><b>-nwe</b></i> is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) suffix that has two meanings.

## -nwe<sup>1</sup> {#-nwe1}

It is equivalent to the English suffix *-ness*. This can be seen in [Manwe](../M/Manwe.md) ("blessedness") and [Voronwe](../V/Voronwe.md) ("steadfastness").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## -nwe<sup>2</sup> {#-nwe2}

It is a dual exclusive pronoun suffix meaning "we, both of us".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> This can be seen in [ósanwe](Ósanwe.md) (lit. "thinking together") and inwe ("ourselves").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
