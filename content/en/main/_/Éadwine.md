**Éadwine** was a [Man](/wiki/Man) living in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Saxon_England" title="wikipedia:Anglo-Saxon England">Anglo-Saxon England</a> in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9th_century" title="wikipedia:9th century">9th century AD</a>. He was the father of the famous seafarer [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine), the first mortal to find the [Straight Road](/wiki/Straight_Road) in thousands of years.<sup id="cite_ref-Ead_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ead-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Éadwine was an experienced seaman, who often told stories of strange, faraway lands to his young son Ælfwine. However, around the year [878 AD](/wiki/Seventh_Age_878 "Seventh Age 878"), when Ælfwine was still a child, Éadwine took his ship [Éarendel](/wiki/%C3%89arendel) out to the sea and never returned. Around the same time, Éadwine's wife fled to her kin in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornwall" title="wikipedia:Cornwall">West Wales</a>, taking their son with her.<sup id="cite_ref-Ead_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ead-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Éadwine* is in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) and consists of the elements *ead* ("wealth, fortune") + *wine* ("friend").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Its modern English cognate is <i><b>Edwin</b></i>, seen in the name of [Edwin Lowdham](/wiki/Edwin_Lowdham), the father of [Alwin Lowdham](/wiki/Alwin_Lowdham), one of the main characters in [The Notion Club Papers](/wiki/The_Notion_Club_Papers).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to the early [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the father of Ælfwine was a man named [Déor](</wiki/D%C3%A9or_(father_of_%C3%86lfwine)> "Déor (father of Ælfwine)").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In *[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)*, Éadwine wasn't the name of Ælfwine's father but rather that of [his son](</wiki/E%C3%A4dwine_(son_of_%C3%86lfwine)> "Eädwine (son of Ælfwine)").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
