[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1924" title="wikipedia:1924">1924</a></b>.

## April {#April}

- **[14](/wiki/14_April "14 April")** - [Philip Stone](/wiki/Philip_Stone), who would voice [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"), is born.

## May {#May}

- **[2](/wiki/2_May "2 May")** - [Theodore Bikel](/wiki/Theodore_Bikel), who would voice [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in [*The Return of the King* (1980 film)](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)"), is born.
- **?** - Actor [William Johnson](/wiki/William_Johnson) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[3](/wiki/3_July "3 July")** - Artist [Kay Woollard](/wiki/Kay_Woollard) is born.
- **[16](/wiki/16_July "16 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is appointed Professor of English Language at the University of Leeds.
- **[19](/wiki/19_July "19 July")** - Director [Arthur Rankin Jr.](/wiki/Arthur_Rankin_Jr.) is born.
- **[31](/wiki/31_July "31 July")** - Actor [Garard Green](/wiki/Garard_Green) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[8](/wiki/8_August "8 August")** - [Gene Deitch](/wiki/Gene_Deitch), who would make a short 12 minute [adaptation](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_film)> "The Hobbit (1967 film)") of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, is born.

## September {#September}

- **[7](/wiki/7_September "7 September")** - Composer [Leonard Rosenman](/wiki/Leonard_Rosenman) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[1](/wiki/1_October "1 October")** - Tolkien begins his Professorship.
- **[14](/wiki/14_October "14 October")** - Philologist [Brian Miller](/wiki/Brian_Miller), student and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is born.
- **[25](/wiki/25_October "25 October")** - Actor [Billy Barty](/wiki/Billy_Barty) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - Translator [William Auld](/wiki/William_Auld) is born.
- **[21](/wiki/21_November "21 November")** - **[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) is born in Leeds, England**.

## December {#December}

- **[23](/wiki/23_December "23 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien's poems "[An Evening in Tavrobel](/wiki/An_Evening_in_Tavrobel)", "[The Lonely Isle](/wiki/The_Lonely_Isle)", and "[The Princess Ní](/wiki/The_Princess_N%C3%AD)" are published in *[Leeds University Verse 1914-24](/wiki/Leeds_University_Verse_1914-24)*.
- **?** - [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") tells his son [John](/wiki/John_Tolkien "John Tolkien") the story "[The Orgog](/wiki/The_Orgog)" when he cannot sleep.
