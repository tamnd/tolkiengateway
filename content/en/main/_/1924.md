[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1924" title="wikipedia:1924">1924</a></b>.

## April {#April}

- **[14](14_April.md)** - [Philip Stone](../P/Philip_Stone.md), who would voice [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>), is born.

## May {#May}

- **[2](2_May.md)** - [Theodore Bikel](../T/Theodore_Bikel.md), who would voice [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) in [*The Return of the King* (1980 film)](<../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md>), is born.
- **?** - Actor [William Johnson](../W/William_Johnson.md) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[3](3_July.md)** - Artist [Kay Woollard](../K/Kay_Woollard.md) is born.
- **[16](16_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is appointed Professor of English Language at the University of Leeds.
- **[19](19_July.md)** - Director [Arthur Rankin Jr.](../A/Arthur_Rankin_Jr..md) is born.
- **[31](31_July.md)** - Actor [Garard Green](../G/Garard_Green.md) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[8](8_August.md)** - [Gene Deitch](../G/Gene_Deitch.md), who would make a short 12 minute [adaptation](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1967_film).md>) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, is born.

## September {#September}

- **[7](7_September.md)** - Composer [Leonard Rosenman](../L/Leonard_Rosenman.md) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[1](1_October.md)** - Tolkien begins his Professorship.
- **[14](14_October.md)** - Philologist [Brian Miller](../B/Brian_Miller.md), student and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is born.
- **[25](25_October.md)** - Actor [Billy Barty](../B/Billy_Barty.md) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[6](6_November.md)** - Translator [William Auld](../W/William_Auld.md) is born.
- **[21](21_November.md)** - **[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) is born in Leeds, England**.

## December {#December}

- **[23](23_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien's poems "[An Evening in Tavrobel](../A/An_Evening_in_Tavrobel.md)", "[The Lonely Isle](../T/The_Lonely_Isle.md)", and "[The Princess Ní](../T/The_Princess_Ní.md)" are published in *[Leeds University Verse 1914-24](../L/Leeds_University_Verse_1914-24.md)*.
- **?** - [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) tells his son [John](../J/John_Tolkien.md) the story "[The Orgog](../T/The_Orgog.md)" when he cannot sleep.
