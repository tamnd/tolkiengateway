[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023" title="wikipedia:2023">2023</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](3_January.md)** - [Birthday Toast](../B/Birthday_Toast.md#Tolkien_Society_tradition).

## March {#March}

- **[25](25_March.md)** - [Tolkien Reading Day](../T/Tolkien_Reading_Day.md).
- <b><a href="30_March.md" title="30 March">30</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_Battle_of_Maldon%253A_together_with_The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth.md" title="The Battle of Maldon: together with The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth">The Battle of Maldon: together with The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>

## April {#April}

- **[22](22_April.md)** - Australian actor [Barry Humphries](../B/Barry_Humphries.md) dies.

## May {#May}

- **[10](10_May.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Heroes of Middle-earth](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Heroes_of_Middle-earth.md)* is released online.
- **[25](25_May.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Gollum](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Gollum.md)* is released.

## June {#June}

- **[6](6_June.md)** - *[The Battle of Maldon: together with The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth](../T/The_Battle_of_Maldon%253A_together_with_The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth.md)* is published in the US.
- **[21](21_June.md)** - Filming for the second season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* is wrapped.

## July {#July}

- **[2](2_July.md)** - The 2023 [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Society.md) seminar, takes place at The Hilton Leeds City and it is held Online.
- **[25](25_July.md)** - [The Lord of the Rings (musical)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical).md>) opens at the Watermill Theatre in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newbury,_Berkshire" title="wikipedia:Newbury, Berkshire">Newbury</a>, West Berkshire.

## August {#August}

<ul><li><b><a href="15_August.md" title="15 August">15</a></b> - <i><a href="../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2024.md" title="Tolkien Calendar 2024">Tolkien Calendar 2024</a></i> is published.</li>
<li><b><a href="18_August.md" title="18 August">18</a></b> - Game developer <a href="../W/Wendell_Lariston_Hill,_Jr..md" title="Wendell Lariston Hill, Jr.">Wendell Lariston Hill, Jr.</a> dies.</li>
<li><b><a href="31_August.md" title="31 August">31</a> - <a href="3_September.md" title="3 September">3 September</a></b> - <a href="../O/Oxonmoot_2023.md" title="Oxonmoot 2023">Oxonmoot 2023</a> is held.</li>
<li class="mw-empty-elt"></li></ul>

## September {#September}

- **[2](2_September.md)** - *[Tolkien's Faith](../T/Tolkien's_Faith.md)* is published.
- **[9](9_September.md)** - Actor [Peter McKenzie](../P/Peter_McKenzie.md) dies.

## October {#October}

- **[15](15_October.md)** - [The Lord of the Rings (musical)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical).md>) ends at the Watermill Theatre in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newbury,_Berkshire" title="wikipedia:Newbury, Berkshire">Newbury</a>, West Berkshire.
- **[24](24_October.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Return_to_Moria.md)* is released on PC.

## November {#November}

- **[8](8_November.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Corsairs of Umbar](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Corsairs_of_Umbar.md)* is released online.
- **[9](9_November.md)** - A revised and expanded edition of *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* is published in the UK.

## December {#December}

- **[7](7_December.md)** - [Christopher Tolkien Centenary Boxed Sets](../C/Christopher_Tolkien_Centenary_Boxed_Sets.md) #1 is released
