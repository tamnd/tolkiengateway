[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023" title="wikipedia:2023">2023</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).

## March {#March}

- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- <b><a href="/wiki/30_March" title="30 March">30</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Battle_of_Maldon:_together_with_The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth" title="The Battle of Maldon: together with The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth">The Battle of Maldon: together with The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>

## April {#April}

- **[22](/wiki/22_April "22 April")** - Australian actor [Barry Humphries](/wiki/Barry_Humphries) dies.

## May {#May}

- **[10](/wiki/10_May "10 May")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Heroes of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Heroes_of_Middle-earth)* is released online.
- **[25](/wiki/25_May "25 May")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Gollum](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Gollum)* is released.

## June {#June}

- **[6](/wiki/6_June "6 June")** - *[The Battle of Maldon: together with The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth](/wiki/The_Battle_of_Maldon:_together_with_The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth)* is published in the US.
- **[21](/wiki/21_June "21 June")** - Filming for the second season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* is wrapped.

## July {#July}

- **[2](/wiki/2_July "2 July")** - The 2023 [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Society) seminar, takes place at The Hilton Leeds City and it is held Online.
- **[25](/wiki/25_July "25 July")** - [The Lord of the Rings (musical)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical)>) opens at the Watermill Theatre in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newbury,_Berkshire" title="wikipedia:Newbury, Berkshire">Newbury</a>, West Berkshire.

## August {#August}

<ul><li><b><a href="/wiki/15_August" title="15 August">15</a></b> - <i><a href="/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2024" title="Tolkien Calendar 2024">Tolkien Calendar 2024</a></i> is published.</li>
<li><b><a href="/wiki/18_August" title="18 August">18</a></b> - Game developer <a href="/wiki/Wendell_Lariston_Hill,_Jr." title="Wendell Lariston Hill, Jr.">Wendell Lariston Hill, Jr.</a> dies.</li>
<li><b><a href="/wiki/31_August" title="31 August">31</a> - <a href="/wiki/3_September" title="3 September">3 September</a></b> - <a href="/wiki/Oxonmoot_2023" title="Oxonmoot 2023">Oxonmoot 2023</a> is held.</li>
<li class="mw-empty-elt"></li></ul>

## September {#September}

- **[2](/wiki/2_September "2 September")** - *[Tolkien's Faith](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Faith)* is published.
- **[9](/wiki/9_September "9 September")** - Actor [Peter McKenzie](/wiki/Peter_McKenzie) dies.

## October {#October}

- **[15](/wiki/15_October "15 October")** - [The Lord of the Rings (musical)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical)>) ends at the Watermill Theatre in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newbury,_Berkshire" title="wikipedia:Newbury, Berkshire">Newbury</a>, West Berkshire.
- **[24](/wiki/24_October "24 October")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Return_to_Moria)* is released on PC.

## November {#November}

- **[8](/wiki/8_November "8 November")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Corsairs_of_Umbar)* is released online.
- **[9](/wiki/9_November "9 November")** - A revised and expanded edition of *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)* is published in the UK.

## December {#December}

- **[7](/wiki/7_December "7 December")** - [Christopher Tolkien Centenary Boxed Sets](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien_Centenary_Boxed_Sets) #1 is released
