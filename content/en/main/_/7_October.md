Events that occurred on 7 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1945](1945.md)** - Author [Hal Colebatch](../H/Hal_Colebatch.md) is born.
- **[1983](1983.md)** - Amateur actress [Hannah Wood](../H/Hannah_Wood.md) is born.
- **[1984](1984.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1984](../O/Oxonmoot_1984.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[2007](2007.md)** - *Cair Andros*, a feast of the [Ellinikós Sýllogos Philon Tólkin](../E/Ellinikós_Sýllogos_Philon_Tólkin.md).
- **[2008](2008.md)** - The [1992 radio series](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>) of *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm.md)* is re-released in the US.
- **[2019](2019.md)** - *[Music in Tolkien's Work and Beyond](../M/Music_in_Tolkien's_Work_and_Beyond.md)* is published.
- **[2022](2022.md)** - The episode [The Eye](../T/The_Eye.md) of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* is released online.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - With [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) severely wounded, [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) and the [four hobbits](../T/Travellers.md) travel slowly towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) hurries towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) after the [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) attacked him on [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
