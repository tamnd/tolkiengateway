Events that occurred on 7 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1945](/wiki/1945)** - Author [Hal Colebatch](/wiki/Hal_Colebatch) is born.
- **[1983](/wiki/1983)** - Amateur actress [Hannah Wood](/wiki/Hannah_Wood) is born.
- **[1984](/wiki/1984)** - [Oxonmoot 1984](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1984) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - *Cair Andros*, a feast of the [Ellinikós Sýllogos Philon Tólkin](/wiki/Ellinik%C3%B3s_S%C3%BDllogos_Philon_T%C3%B3lkin).
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - The [1992 radio series](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)") of *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](/wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm)* is re-released in the US.
- **[2019](/wiki/2019)** - *[Music in Tolkien's Work and Beyond](/wiki/Music_in_Tolkien%27s_Work_and_Beyond)* is published.
- **[2022](/wiki/2022)** - The episode [The Eye](/wiki/The_Eye) of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* is released online.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - With [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") severely wounded, [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") and the [four hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") travel slowly towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) hurries towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) after the [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") attacked him on [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
