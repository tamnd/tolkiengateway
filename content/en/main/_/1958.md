[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958" title="wikipedia:1958">1958</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[10](10_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to his grandson Michael George Tolkien](<../L/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(10_January_1958).md>).
- **[29](29_January.md)** - Actor [Alister Duncan](../A/Alister_Duncan.md) is born.

## February {#February}

- **[17](17_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes to [Wordsworth](<../L/Letter_to_Wordsworth_(17_February_1958).md>).
- **[21](21_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 205](../L/Letter_205.md).

## March {#March}

- **[18](18_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 205a](../L/Letter_205a.md).
- **[28](28_March.md)** - Actor [Stephen Ure](../S/Stephen_Ure.md) is born.
- **[28](28_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is invited to attend a ["Hobbit Dinner" in Rotterdam](%2522Hobbit_Dinner%2522_in_Rotterdam.md).

## April {#April}

- **[2](2_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Peter Alford](<../L/Letter_to_Peter_Alford_(2_April_1958).md>).
- **[2](2_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 205b](../L/Letter_205b.md).
- **[3](3_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), signing as "[Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md)", sends [a letter to a Dutchman](../L/Letter_to_an_unknown_Dutchman.md).
- **[8](8_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes letters [206](../L/Letter_206.md) and [207](../L/Letter_207.md).
- **[10](10_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 208](../L/Letter_208.md).
- **[12](12_April.md)** - [Tony Oliver](../T/Tony_Oliver.md), who would voice [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_of_the_Ring.md)*, is born.

## May {#May}

- **[4](4_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 209](../L/Letter_209.md).
- **[5](5_May.md)** - Author [James Branch Cabell](../J/James_Branch_Cabell.md) dies.
- **[23](23_May.md)** - Actor [William Kircher](../W/William_Kircher.md) is born.
- **[26](26_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is driven at high speed to ABC Television's studio at Aston, Birmingham.

## June {#June}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 210](../L/Letter_210.md).

## August {#August}

- **[29](29_August.md)** - British actor [Lenny Henry](../L/Lenny_Henry.md) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[14](14_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 211](../L/Letter_211.md).
- **[20](20_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Misses E. Jones and J. Taylor](../L/Letter_to_Misses_E._Jones_and_J._Taylor.md).
- **[20](20_October.md)** - [Viggo Mortensen](../V/Viggo_Mortensen.md), who would play [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.
- **[23](23_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 212a](../L/Letter_212a.md).
- **[25](25_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 213](../L/Letter_213.md).
- **[26](26_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to L.M. Cutts](../L/Letter_to_L.M._Cutts.md).
- **[28](28_October.md)** - [C. S. Lewis](../C/C._S._Lewis.md) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

## November {#November}

- **[2](2_November.md)** - [Taum Santoski](../T/Taum_Santoski.md), Tolkien linguist, is born.
- **[10](10_November.md)** - [C. S. Lewis](../C/C._S._Lewis.md) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- **[30](30_November.md)** - Actor [Gareth Jones](../G/Gareth_Jones.md) dies.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Actress [Kathryn Hurlbutt](../K/Kathryn_Hurlbutt.md) is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [two letters to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](<../L/Letters_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(1958).md>).
- **?** - *[The Old English Apollonius of Tyre](../T/The_Old_English_Apollonius_of_Tyre.md)*, containing a prefatory note by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.
- **?** - [John D. Rateliff](../J/John_D._Rateliff.md), editor and author, is born.
- **?** - [Edith Tolkien](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) smashes her left arm in the garden, one of the reasons why that year became frustrating for the Tolkiens, preventing the completion of [Letter 214](../L/Letter_214.md) and further work on *the Silmarillion*.
