[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958" title="wikipedia:1958">1958</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[10](/wiki/10_January "10 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to his grandson Michael George Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(10_January_1958)> "Letter to Michael George Tolkien (10 January 1958)").
- **[29](/wiki/29_January "29 January")** - Actor [Alister Duncan](/wiki/Alister_Duncan) is born.

## February {#February}

- **[17](/wiki/17_February "17 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes to [Wordsworth](</wiki/Letter_to_Wordsworth_(17_February_1958)> "Letter to Wordsworth (17 February 1958)").
- **[21](/wiki/21_February "21 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 205](/wiki/Letter_205).

## March {#March}

- **[18](/wiki/18_March "18 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 205a](/wiki/Letter_205a "Letter 205a").
- **[28](/wiki/28_March "28 March")** - Actor [Stephen Ure](/wiki/Stephen_Ure) is born.
- **[28](/wiki/28_March "28 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is invited to attend a ["Hobbit Dinner" in Rotterdam](/wiki/%22Hobbit_Dinner%22_in_Rotterdam).

## April {#April}

- **[2](/wiki/2_April "2 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Peter Alford](</wiki/Letter_to_Peter_Alford_(2_April_1958)> "Letter to Peter Alford (2 April 1958)").
- **[2](/wiki/2_April "2 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 205b](/wiki/Letter_205b).
- **[3](/wiki/3_April "3 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), signing as "[Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)", sends [a letter to a Dutchman](/wiki/Letter_to_an_unknown_Dutchman "Letter to an unknown Dutchman").
- **[8](/wiki/8_April "8 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes letters [206](/wiki/Letter_206 "Letter 206") and [207](/wiki/Letter_207 "Letter 207").
- **[10](/wiki/10_April "10 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 208](/wiki/Letter_208 "Letter 208").
- **[12](/wiki/12_April "12 April")** - [Tony Oliver](/wiki/Tony_Oliver), who would voice [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") in *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring)*, is born.

## May {#May}

- **[4](/wiki/4_May "4 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 209](/wiki/Letter_209 "Letter 209").
- **[5](/wiki/5_May "5 May")** - Author [James Branch Cabell](/wiki/James_Branch_Cabell) dies.
- **[23](/wiki/23_May "23 May")** - Actor [William Kircher](/wiki/William_Kircher) is born.
- **[26](/wiki/26_May "26 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is driven at high speed to ABC Television's studio at Aston, Birmingham.

## June {#June}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 210](/wiki/Letter_210 "Letter 210").

## August {#August}

- **[29](/wiki/29_August "29 August")** - British actor [Lenny Henry](/wiki/Lenny_Henry) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[14](/wiki/14_October "14 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 211](/wiki/Letter_211 "Letter 211").
- **[20](/wiki/20_October "20 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Misses E. Jones and J. Taylor](/wiki/Letter_to_Misses_E._Jones_and_J._Taylor "Letter to Misses E. Jones and J. Taylor").
- **[20](/wiki/20_October "20 October")** - [Viggo Mortensen](/wiki/Viggo_Mortensen), who would play [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.
- **[23](/wiki/23_October "23 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 212a](/wiki/Letter_212a "Letter 212a").
- **[25](/wiki/25_October "25 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 213](/wiki/Letter_213 "Letter 213").
- **[26](/wiki/26_October "26 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to L.M. Cutts](/wiki/Letter_to_L.M._Cutts "Letter to L.M. Cutts").
- **[28](/wiki/28_October "28 October")** - [C. S. Lewis](/wiki/C._S._Lewis) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

## November {#November}

- **[2](/wiki/2_November "2 November")** - [Taum Santoski](/wiki/Taum_Santoski), Tolkien linguist, is born.
- **[10](/wiki/10_November "10 November")** - [C. S. Lewis](/wiki/C._S._Lewis) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - Actor [Gareth Jones](/wiki/Gareth_Jones) dies.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Actress [Kathryn Hurlbutt](/wiki/Kathryn_Hurlbutt) is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [two letters to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](</wiki/Letters_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(1958)> "Letters to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski (1958)").
- **?** - *[The Old English Apollonius of Tyre](/wiki/The_Old_English_Apollonius_of_Tyre)*, containing a prefatory note by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **?** - [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff), editor and author, is born.
- **?** - [Edith Tolkien](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien) smashes her left arm in the garden, one of the reasons why that year became frustrating for the Tolkiens, preventing the completion of [Letter 214](/wiki/Letter_214) and further work on *the Silmarillion*.
- **?** - In order to pay his taxes, Tolkien offers some of his manuscripts to [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University) for the sum of £1250.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
