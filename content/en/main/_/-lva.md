<i><b>-lva</b></i> is a pronominal ending in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) meaning "our", of two persons where one addresses the other.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Only attested in the genitive <i><b>-lvo</b></i> in the word **omentielvo**. For this reason it has been argued<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>who?</i></a>]</sup> that the ending may actually be \**-lwa*, since there is some evidence that **wo** would change to **vo** in Quenya and the **w** of \***-lwa** could be derived from the dual ending <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-u&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-u (page does not exist)">-u</a></i>.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
