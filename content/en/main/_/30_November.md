Events that occurred on 30 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1932](1932.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[1947](1947.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 112](../L/Letter_112.md).
- **[1953](1953.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 141c](../L/Letter_141c.md).
- **[1955](1955.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 175](../L/Letter_175.md).
- **[1958](1958.md)** - Actor [Gareth Jones](../G/Gareth_Jones.md) dies.
- **[1964](1964.md)** - *[El hobito](../E/El_hobito.md)*, the first translation into Spanish of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, is published.
- **[1966](1966.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) conducts an [interview](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md), published as "[The Man Who Understands Hobbits](../T/The_Man_Who_Understands_Hobbits.md)".
- **[1966](1966.md)** - *[Letters to an American Lady](../L/Letters_to_an_American_Lady.md)* is released.
- **[1972](1972.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 345](../L/Letter_345.md).
- <b><a href="1987.md" title="1987">1987</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings.md" title="The Lost Road and Other Writings">The Lost Road and Other Writings</a></i> is published in the US.</b>
- <b><a href="1989.md" title="1989">1989</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_Treason_of_Isengard.md" title="The Treason of Isengard">The Treason of Isengard</a></i> is published in the US.</b>
- **[1989](1989.md)** - *[Oliphaunt](<../O/Oliphaunt_(book).md>)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) rest in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).
