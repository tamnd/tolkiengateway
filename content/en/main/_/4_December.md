Events that occurred on 4 December.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 91b](/wiki/Letter_91b "Letter 91b").
- **[1947](/wiki/1947)** - *[Essays Presented to Charles Williams](/wiki/Essays_Presented_to_Charles_Williams)*, including "[On Fairy-Stories](/wiki/On_Fairy-Stories)", is published.
- **[1953](/wiki/1953)** - An issue of the magazine *[Radio Times](/wiki/Radio_Times_4_December_1953 "Radio Times 4 December 1953")* featuring [an article](/wiki/A_Fourteenth-Century_Romance "A Fourteenth-Century Romance") by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **[1955](/wiki/1955)** - The fourth episode of [1955 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)") of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* airs on the [BBC](/wiki/BBC) Third Programme.
- **[1963](/wiki/1963)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to a Miss Clark](/wiki/Letter_to_Miss_Clark "Letter to Miss Clark").
- **[1967](/wiki/1967)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Roger Verhulst](</wiki/Letter_to_Roger_Verhulst_(4_December_1967)> "Letter to Roger Verhulst (4 December 1967)").
- **[1999](/wiki/1999)** - Voice actress [Gaia Wise](/wiki/Gaia_Wise) is born.
- **[2001](/wiki/2001)** - The documentary *[Master of the Rings](/wiki/Master_of_the_Rings:_The_Unauthorized_Story_Behind_J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_%27Lord_of_the_Rings%27 "Master of the Rings: The Unauthorized Story Behind J.R.R. Tolkien's 'Lord of the Rings'")* is released.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - [The Lord of the Rings Symphony](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony) plays in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newark,_New_Jersey" title="wikipedia:Newark, New Jersey">Newark, New Jersey</a>.
- **[2012](/wiki/2012)** - *[Guardians of Middle-earth](/wiki/Guardians_of_Middle-earth)* is released on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_3" title="wikipedia:PlayStation 3">PlayStation 3</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_360" title="wikipedia:Xbox 360">Xbox 360</a>.
- **[2014](/wiki/2014)** - <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=JRR_Tolkien,_le_seigneur_des_%C3%A9crivains&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="JRR Tolkien, le seigneur des écrivains (page does not exist)">JRR Tolkien, le seigneur des écrivains</a> (La Grande Librairie) is aired.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") rest in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").
