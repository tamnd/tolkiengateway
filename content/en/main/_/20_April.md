## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1893](1893.md)** - [Christopher Wiseman](../C/Christopher_Wiseman.md), the only of Tolkien's friends that survived World War I, is born.
- **[1937](1937.md)** - Narrator [George Takei](../G/George_Takei.md) is born.
- **[1939](1939.md)** - Writer and screenwriter [Peter S. Beagle](../P/Peter_S._Beagle.md) is born.
- **[1943](1943.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 48](../L/Letter_48.md).
- **[1964](1964.md)** - Actor [Andy Serkis](../A/Andy_Serkis.md) is born.
- **[1973](1973.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Dr. Denis Tolhurst](../L/Letter_to_Dr._Denis_Tolhurst.md).
- **[1973](1973.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to John Higgins](../L/Letter_to_John_Higgins.md).
- **[1999](1999.md)** - Actor [Elan Garfias](../E/Elan_Garfias.md) is born.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)* developer [Turbine](../T/Turbine.md) is bought by [Warner Bros.](../W/Warner_Bros..md)
- **[2011](2011.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Card_Game.md)* was released.
- **[2013](2013.md)** - Artist [Quinton Hoover](../Q/Quinton_Hoover.md) dies.
- **[2015](2015.md)** - Voice actor [Peter Howell](../P/Peter_Howell.md) dies.
