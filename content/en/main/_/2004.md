[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004" title="wikipedia:2004">2004</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[2](2_January.md)** - *[The Art of The Return of the King](../T/The_Art_of_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* is published.
- **[2](2_January.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Complete Visual Companion](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Complete_Visual_Companion.md)* is published.
- **[5](5_January.md)** - Graphic novel *[Heaven's War](../H/Heaven's_War.md)* is published.
- **[7](7_January.md)** - Dutch translator [Willy Wielek-Berg](../W/Willy_Wielek-Berg.md) dies.
- **[28](28_January.md)** - *[The Two Towers Anthology](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md)* is released by [Decipher](../D/Decipher.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## February {#February}

- **[5](5_February.md)** - [Andy Serkis](../A/Andy_Serkis.md) wins an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_Awards" title="wikipedia:Empire Awards">Empire Award</a> in the category *Best British Actor*, for his role as [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>).
- **[22](22_February.md)** - At the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10th_Screen_Actors_Guild_Awards" title="wikipedia:10th Screen Actors Guild Awards">10th Screen Actors Guild Awards</a>, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* wins the award for "Outstanding Performance by a Cast in a Motion Picture".
- **[29](29_February.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* wins 11 Oscars at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/76th_Academy_Awards" title="wikipedia:76th Academy Awards">76th Academy Awards</a>.

## March {#March}

- **[16](16_March.md)** - *[The World of Tolkien](../T/The_World_of_Tolkien.md)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[30](30_April.md)** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 1](../T/Tolkien_Studies%253A_Volume_1.md)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **[1](1_May.md)** - *[There and Back Again: In the Footsteps of J.R.R. Tolkien's England](../T/There_and_Back_Again%253A_In_the_Footsteps_of_J.R.R._Tolkien's_England.md)* is published.
- **[15](15_May.md)-[16](16_May.md)** - <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tolkien_Weekend_2004&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien Weekend 2004 (page does not exist)">Tolkien Weekend 2004</a> at [Sarehole Mill](../S/Sarehole_Mill.md).
- **[24](24_May.md)** - *[Understanding The Lord of the Rings](../U/Understanding_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[3](3_June.md)-[5](5_June.md)** - [Lord of the Rings Symphony](../L/Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony.md) in Atlanta, GA, Atlanta Symphony Hall
- **[30](30_June.md)** - *[War and the Works of J.R.R. Tolkien](../W/War_and_the_Works_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[1](1_July.md)-[2](2_July.md)** - Lord of the Rings Symphony in Houston, TX, Jones Hall.
- **[10](10_July.md)** - German translator Hans J. Schütz dies.
- **[13](13_July.md)** - Lord of the Rings Symphony in Philadelphia, PA, Mann Center
- **[13](13_July.md)-[15](15_July.md)** - [Tolkien Thing VI](../T/Tolkien_Thing.md)
- **[15](15_July.md)-[17](17_July.md)** - Lord of the Rings Symphony in Seattle, WA, S.Mark Taper Auditorium/Benaroya Hall
- **[29](29_July.md)-[31](31_July.md)** - Lord of the Rings Symphony in Pittsburgh, PA, Heinz Hall
- **[30](30_July.md)** - [Mythcon 35](../M/Mythcon_35.md) begins.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 46](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_46.md) is published.
- **?** - *[Translating Tolkien: Text and Film](../T/Translating_Tolkien%253A_Text_and_Film.md)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[2](2_August.md)** - [Mythcon 35](../M/Mythcon_35.md) ends.
- **[12](12_August.md)** - [Peter Woodthorpe](../P/Peter_Woodthorpe.md), who voiced [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) in both [The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>) as well as [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), dies.

## September {#September}

- **[15](15_September.md)** - A new Swedish translation of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, made by [Erik Andersson](../E/Erik_Andersson.md), is published.
- **[17](17_September.md)-[19](19_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2004](../O/Oxonmoot_2004.md).
- **[18](18_September.md)-[19](19_September.md)** - *Lord of the Rings Symphony* in Hartford, CT, Mortensen Hall.
- **[23](23_September.md)** - *[There and Back Again: An Actor's Tale](../T/There_and_Back_Again%253A_An_Actor's_Tale.md)*, in which [Sean Astin](../S/Sean_Astin.md) describes the shooting of [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) from his perspective, is published.
- **[27](27_September.md)** - [Parma Eldalamberon 15](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_15.md) is published.
- **[28](28_September.md)** - The parody book *[The Sillymarillion](../T/The_Sillymarillion.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Peter Jackson: From Gore to Mordor](../P/Peter_Jackson%253A_From_Gore_to_Mordor.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[When a Fan Hits the Shit: The Rise and Fall of a Phony Charity](../W/When_a_Fan_Hits_the_Shit.md)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[8](8_October.md)-[9](9_October.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Symphony](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony.md)* in Chicago, IL, Auditorium Theatre.
- **[12](12_October.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Symphony](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony.md)* in Milwaukee, WI.
- <b><a href="21_October.md" title="21 October">21</a> - The <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (50th Anniversary Edition)">50th Anniversary Edition</a> of <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> is published.</b>

## November {#November}

- **[2](2_November.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Third_Age.md)* is released in North America.
- **[5](5_November.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Third_Age.md)* is released in Europe.
- **[6](6_November.md)** - [Hyarion](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Hyarion) purchases the domain [TolkienGateway.net](../../project/A/About.md)
- **[8](8_November.md)** - [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md)'s *[A Gateway to Sindarin](../A/A_Gateway_to_Sindarin.md)* is published.
- **?** - [Mithril Armoury](../M/Mithril_Armoury.md) files for <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapter_7,_Title_11,_United_States_Code" title="wikipedia:Chapter 7, Title 11, United States Code">Chapter 7</a> bankruptcy.

## December {#December}

- **[3](3_December.md)-[4](4_December.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Symphony](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony.md)* plays in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newark,_New_Jersey" title="wikipedia:Newark, New Jersey">Newark, New Jersey</a>.
- **[14](14_December.md)** - [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>) is released on DVD.
- **[22](22_December.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Third_Age.md)* is released in Japan.
- **[26](26_December.md)** - Actor [Garard Green](../G/Garard_Green.md) dies.
