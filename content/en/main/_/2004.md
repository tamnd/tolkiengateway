[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004" title="wikipedia:2004">2004</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[2](/wiki/2_January "2 January")** - *[The Art of The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Art_of_The_Return_of_the_King)* is published.
- **[2](/wiki/2_January "2 January")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Complete Visual Companion](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Complete_Visual_Companion)* is published.
- **[5](/wiki/5_January "5 January")** - Graphic novel *[Heaven's War](/wiki/Heaven%27s_War)* is published.
- **[7](/wiki/7_January "7 January")** - Dutch translator [Willy Wielek-Berg](/wiki/Willy_Wielek-Berg) dies.
- **[28](/wiki/28_January "28 January")** - *[The Two Towers Anthology](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game "The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game")* is released by [Decipher](/wiki/Decipher).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## February {#February}

- **[5](/wiki/5_February "5 February")** - [Andy Serkis](/wiki/Andy_Serkis) wins an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_Awards" title="wikipedia:Empire Awards">Empire Award</a> in the category *Best British Actor*, for his role as [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").
- **[22](/wiki/22_February "22 February")** - At the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10th_Screen_Actors_Guild_Awards" title="wikipedia:10th Screen Actors Guild Awards">10th Screen Actors Guild Awards</a>, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* wins the award for "Outstanding Performance by a Cast in a Motion Picture".
- **[29](/wiki/29_February "29 February")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* wins 11 Oscars at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/76th_Academy_Awards" title="wikipedia:76th Academy Awards">76th Academy Awards</a>.

## March {#March}

- **[16](/wiki/16_March "16 March")** - *[The World of Tolkien](/wiki/The_World_of_Tolkien)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[30](/wiki/30_April "30 April")** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 1](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_1)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **[1](/wiki/1_May "1 May")** - *[There and Back Again: In the Footsteps of J.R.R. Tolkien's England](/wiki/There_and_Back_Again:_In_the_Footsteps_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_England)* is published.
- **[15](/wiki/15_May "15 May")-[16](/wiki/16_May "16 May")** - <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tolkien_Weekend_2004&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien Weekend 2004 (page does not exist)">Tolkien Weekend 2004</a> at [Sarehole Mill](/wiki/Sarehole_Mill).
- **[24](/wiki/24_May "24 May")** - *[Understanding The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Understanding_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[3](/wiki/3_June "3 June")-[5](/wiki/5_June "5 June")** - [Lord of the Rings Symphony](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony) in Atlanta, GA, Atlanta Symphony Hall
- **[30](/wiki/30_June "30 June")** - *[War and the Works of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/War_and_the_Works_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[1](/wiki/1_July "1 July")-[2](/wiki/2_July "2 July")** - Lord of the Rings Symphony in Houston, TX, Jones Hall.
- **[10](/wiki/10_July "10 July")** - German translator Hans J. Schütz dies.
- **[13](/wiki/13_July "13 July")** - Lord of the Rings Symphony in Philadelphia, PA, Mann Center
- **[13](/wiki/13_July "13 July")-[15](/wiki/15_July "15 July")** - [Tolkien Thing VI](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing "Tolkien Thing")
- **[15](/wiki/15_July "15 July")-[17](/wiki/17_July "17 July")** - Lord of the Rings Symphony in Seattle, WA, S.Mark Taper Auditorium/Benaroya Hall
- **[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")-[31](/wiki/31_July "31 July")** - Lord of the Rings Symphony in Pittsburgh, PA, Heinz Hall
- **[30](/wiki/30_July "30 July")** - [Mythcon 35](/wiki/Mythcon_35) begins.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 46](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_46) is published.
- **?** - *[Translating Tolkien: Text and Film](/wiki/Translating_Tolkien:_Text_and_Film)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[2](/wiki/2_August "2 August")** - [Mythcon 35](/wiki/Mythcon_35) ends.
- **[12](/wiki/12_August "12 August")** - [Peter Woodthorpe](/wiki/Peter_Woodthorpe), who voiced [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) in both [The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)>) as well as [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), dies.

## September {#September}

- **[15](/wiki/15_September "15 September")** - A new Swedish translation of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, made by [Erik Andersson](/wiki/Erik_Andersson), is published.
- **[17](/wiki/17_September "17 September")-[19](/wiki/19_September "19 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2004](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2004).
- **[18](/wiki/18_September "18 September")-[19](/wiki/19_September "19 September")** - *Lord of the Rings Symphony* in Hartford, CT, Mortensen Hall.
- **[23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")** - *[There and Back Again: An Actor's Tale](/wiki/There_and_Back_Again:_An_Actor%27s_Tale)*, in which [Sean Astin](/wiki/Sean_Astin) describes the shooting of [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") from his perspective, is published.
- **[27](/wiki/27_September "27 September")** - [Parma Eldalamberon 15](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_15) is published.
- **[28](/wiki/28_September "28 September")** - The parody book *[The Sillymarillion](/wiki/The_Sillymarillion)* is published.
- **?** - *[Peter Jackson: From Gore to Mordor](/wiki/Peter_Jackson:_From_Gore_to_Mordor)* is published.
- **?** - *[When a Fan Hits the Shit: The Rise and Fall of a Phony Charity](/wiki/When_a_Fan_Hits_the_Shit "When a Fan Hits the Shit")* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[8](/wiki/8_October "8 October")-[9](/wiki/9_October "9 October")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Symphony](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony)* in Chicago, IL, Auditorium Theatre.
- **[12](/wiki/12_October "12 October")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Symphony](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony)* in Milwaukee, WI.
- <b><a href="/wiki/21_October" title="21 October">21</a> - The <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition)" title="The Lord of the Rings (50th Anniversary Edition)">50th Anniversary Edition</a> of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> is published.</b>

## November {#November}

- **[2](/wiki/2_November "2 November")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Third_Age)* is released in North America.
- **[5](/wiki/5_November "5 November")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Third_Age)* is released in Europe.
- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - [Hyarion](/wiki/User:Hyarion "User:Hyarion") purchases the domain [TolkienGateway.net](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:About "Tolkien Gateway:About")
- **[8](/wiki/8_November "8 November")** - [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo)'s *[A Gateway to Sindarin](/wiki/A_Gateway_to_Sindarin)* is published.
- **?** - [Mithril Armoury](/wiki/Mithril_Armoury) files for <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapter_7,_Title_11,_United_States_Code" title="wikipedia:Chapter 7, Title 11, United States Code">Chapter 7</a> bankruptcy.

## December {#December}

- **[3](/wiki/3_December "3 December")-[4](/wiki/4_December "4 December")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Symphony](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony)* plays in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newark,_New_Jersey" title="wikipedia:Newark, New Jersey">Newark, New Jersey</a>.
- **[14](/wiki/14_December "14 December")** - [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)") is released on DVD.
- **[22](/wiki/22_December "22 December")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Third_Age)* is released in Japan.
- **[26](/wiki/26_December "26 December")** - Actor [Garard Green](/wiki/Garard_Green) dies.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[2005 An Elvish Travel Calendar](/wiki/2005_An_Elvish_Travel_Calendar)* is published.
- **?** - *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: Siege of Gondor](</wiki/Siege_of_Gondor_(book)> "Siege of Gondor (book)")* is published.
- **?** - *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: The Battle of Pelennor Fields](/wiki/The_Battle_of_Pelennor_Fields "The Battle of Pelennor Fields")* is published.
- **?** - *[Tolkien's Golem](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Golem)* is published.
