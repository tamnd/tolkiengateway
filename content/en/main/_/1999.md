## January {#January}

- **[11](/wiki/11_January "11 January")** - Novelist and poet [Naomi Mitchison](/wiki/Naomi_Mitchison) dies.
- **?** - *[CarolinaCon Regional MECCG Newsletter](/wiki/CarolinaCon_Regional_MECCG_Newsletter)* issue 4 is published.

## April {#April}

- **[20](/wiki/20_April "20 April")** - Actor [Elan Garfias](/wiki/Elan_Garfias) is born.
- **[26](/wiki/26_April "26 April")** - Fansite [TheOneRing.net](/wiki/TheOneRing.net) goes online.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 40](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_40) is published.

## May {#May}

- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 157](/wiki/Amon_Hen_157 "Amon Hen 157") is published.

## June {#June}

- **[7](/wiki/7_June "7 June")** - *[Tolkien: A Celebration: Collected Writings on a Literary Legacy](/wiki/Tolkien:_A_Celebration "Tolkien: A Celebration")* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[8](/wiki/8_July "8 July")** - The first [Tolkien Thing](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing) is held.
- **[30](/wiki/30_July "30 July")** - [Mythcon 30](/wiki/Mythcon_30) begins.
- **?** - [Other Hands 26](/wiki/Other_Hands_26) is published.

## August {#August}

- **[2](/wiki/2_August "2 August")** [Mythcon 30](/wiki/Mythcon_30) ends.
- **?** - *[CarolinaCon Regional MECCG Newsletter](/wiki/CarolinaCon_Regional_MECCG_Newsletter)* issue 5 is published.

## September {#September}

- **[24](/wiki/24_September "24 September")-[26](/wiki/26_September "26 September")** - [Oxonmoot 1999](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1999).
- **[25](/wiki/25_September "25 September")** - Author [Marion Zimmer Bradley](/wiki/Marion_Zimmer_Bradley) dies.

## October {#October}

- **[1](/wiki/1_October "1 October")** - [Noel Johnson](/wiki/Noel_Johnson), who played [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1955 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)"), dies.
- **[4](/wiki/4_October "4 October")** - *[Poems from The Hobbit](/wiki/Poems_from_The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[11](/wiki/11_October "11 October")** - Principal photography for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") starts.
- **[15](/wiki/15_October "15 October")** - Author [Barbara Strachey](/wiki/Barbara_Strachey) dies in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford).
- **?** - *[CarolinaCon Regional MECCG Newsletter](/wiki/CarolinaCon_Regional_MECCG_Newsletter)* issue 6 is published.
- **?** - [Other Hands 27](/wiki/Other_Hands_27) is published.

## November {#November}

- **[1](/wiki/1_November "1 November")** - *[George Allen and Unwin: A Remembrancer](/wiki/George_Allen_and_Unwin:_A_Remembrancer)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[4](/wiki/4_December "4 December")** - Voice actress [Gaia Wise](/wiki/Gaia_Wise) is born.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 37](/wiki/Mallorn_37 "Mallorn 37") is published.
