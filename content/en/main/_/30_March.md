Events that occurred on 30 March

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1936](1936.md)** - C.A. Furth (of [George Allen &amp; Unwin Ltd](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md)) writes a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien.
- **[1940](1940.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 38](../L/Letter_38.md).
- **[1953](1953.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [Edith](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) move to [76 Sandfield Road](76_Sandfield_Road.md).
- **[1968](1968.md)** – The documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](../T/Tolkien_in_Oxford.md)* is aired on the [BBC](../B/BBC.md).
- **[1972](1972.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 334](../L/Letter_334.md).
- **[2005](2005.md)** - *[Unsung Heroes of The Lord of the Rings](../U/Unsung_Heroes_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is published.
- <b><a href="2023.md" title="2023">2023</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_Battle_of_Maldon%253A_together_with_The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth.md" title="The Battle of Maldon: together with The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth">The Battle of Maldon: together with The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
