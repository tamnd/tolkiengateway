<i><b>-ssë</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) suffix that has two meanings.

## -ssë<sup>1</sup> {#-ssë1}

Meaning (singular) *in,* *on* or *at* and can also be written as <i><b>-essë</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

<dl><dd><b>Plural</b>: <i>-ssen</i> or <i>-issen</i> for consonant endings.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup></dd>
<dd><b>Partitive Plural</b>: <i>-lissen</i> or <i>-ilissen</i> for consonant endings.</dd>
<dd><b>Dual</b>: <i>-tsë</i></dd></dl>

## -ssë<sup>2</sup> {#-ssë2}

To denote abstract or locality

### Examples {#Examples}

- **[Vala](/wiki/Vala)** = "angelic power, god" (**valassë** = "divinity"\|[BAL](/wiki/BAL))<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **laiqua** = "green" (**laiquassë** = "greenness")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **hande** = "intelligent" (**handessë** = "intelligence"\|[KHAN](/wiki/KHAN))<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **hópa** = "harbour" (**hopassë** = "harbourage"\|[KHOP](/wiki/KHOP)) <sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
- **findë** = "hair" (**findessë** = "head of hair")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
