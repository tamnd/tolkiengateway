[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1949" title="wikipedia:1949">1949</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[28](/wiki/28_January "28 January")** - The letter "[Cardinal Mindszenty](/wiki/Cardinal_Mindszenty)", co-signed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published in *The Times*.

## February {#February}

- **[15](/wiki/15_February "15 February")** - Scholar [Alexei Kondratiev](/wiki/Alexei_Kondratiev) is born.
- **[28](/wiki/28_February "28 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 119](/wiki/Letter_119).

## March {#March}

- **[11](/wiki/11_March "11 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Miss Turnbull](</wiki/Letter_to_Miss_Turnbull_(11_March_1949)> "Letter to Miss Turnbull (11 March 1949)").
- **[16](/wiki/16_March "16 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 120](/wiki/Letter_120 "Letter 120").
- **[20](/wiki/20_March "20 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) visits <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hugh_Brogan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hugh Brogan (page does not exist)">Hugh Brogan</a> in [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge).

## April {#April}

- **[9](/wiki/9_April "9 April")** - [Sorcha Cusack](/wiki/Sorcha_Cusack), who would play [Goldberry](/wiki/Goldberry) in [*Tales from the Perilous Realm* (1992 radio series)](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)"), is born.

## June {#June}

- **[4](/wiki/4_June "4 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Letter_to_Pauline_Baynes "Letter to Pauline Baynes").

## July {#July}

- **[13](/wiki/13_July "13 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 121](/wiki/Letter_121 "Letter 121").
- **[14](/wiki/14_July "14 July")** - Tolkien author and screenwriter [Brian Sibley](/wiki/Brian_Sibley) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[6](/wiki/6_September "6 September")** - Author [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[20](/wiki/20_October "20 October")** - *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)* is published.
- **?** - A fair, revised typescript of the finished *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is made a copy.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## November {#November}

- **[12](/wiki/12_November "12 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Miss How](/wiki/Letter_to_Miss_How "Letter to Miss How").

## December {#December}

- **[12](/wiki/12_December "12 December")** - [Bill Nighy](/wiki/Bill_Nighy), who would play [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) in [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born.
- **[18](/wiki/18_December "18 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 122](/wiki/Letter_122 "Letter 122").
- **[20](/wiki/20_December "20 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 122a](/wiki/Letter_122a "Letter 122a").

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) gets his B.A. English at Trinity College, [Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford").
- **Autumn** - Tolkien is introduced to editor [Milton Waldman](/wiki/Milton_Waldman).
