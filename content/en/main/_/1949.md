[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1949" title="wikipedia:1949">1949</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[28](28_January.md)** - The letter "[Cardinal Mindszenty](../C/Cardinal_Mindszenty.md)", co-signed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published in *The Times*.

## February {#February}

- **[15](15_February.md)** - Scholar [Alexei Kondratiev](../A/Alexei_Kondratiev.md) is born.
- **[28](28_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 119](../L/Letter_119.md).

## March {#March}

- **[11](11_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Miss Turnbull](<../L/Letter_to_Miss_Turnbull_(11_March_1949).md>).
- **[16](16_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 120](../L/Letter_120.md).
- **[20](20_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) visits <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hugh_Brogan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hugh Brogan (page does not exist)">Hugh Brogan</a> in [Cambridge](../C/Cambridge.md).

## April {#April}

- **[9](9_April.md)** - [Sorcha Cusack](../S/Sorcha_Cusack.md), who would play [Goldberry](../G/Goldberry.md) in [*Tales from the Perilous Realm* (1992 radio series)](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>), is born.

## June {#June}

- **[4](4_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Pauline Baynes](../L/Letter_to_Pauline_Baynes.md).

## July {#July}

- **[13](13_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 121](../L/Letter_121.md).
- **[14](14_July.md)** - Tolkien author and screenwriter [Brian Sibley](../B/Brian_Sibley.md) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[6](6_September.md)** - Author [Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[20](20_October.md)** - *[Farmer Giles of Ham](../F/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md)* is published.
- **?** - A fair, revised typescript of the finished *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is made a copy.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## November {#November}

- **[12](12_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Miss How](../L/Letter_to_Miss_How.md).

## December {#December}

- **[12](12_December.md)** - [Bill Nighy](../B/Bill_Nighy.md), who would play [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) in [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), is born.
- **[18](18_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 122](../L/Letter_122.md).
- **[20](20_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 122a](../L/Letter_122a.md).

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) gets his B.A. English at Trinity College, [Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md).
- **Autumn** - Tolkien is introduced to editor [Milton Waldman](../M/Milton_Waldman.md).
