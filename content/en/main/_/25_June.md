Events that occurred on 25 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1926](1926.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a letter to W.G. Harding.
- **[1937](1937.md)** - Author [Timothy J. Wheeler](../T/Timothy_J._Wheeler.md) is born.
- **[1957](1957.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 200](../L/Letter_200.md).
- **[1963](1963.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 245](../L/Letter_245.md).
- **[1989](1989.md)** – The first meeting of members of the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](../E/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship.md) takes place at the residence of [Bill Welden](../B/Bill_Welden.md).
- **[2001](2001.md)** - Actress [Pauline Letts](../P/Pauline_Letts.md) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - [Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn.md) finds the sapling of the [White Tree](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
