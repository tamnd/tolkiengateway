## March {#March}

- **[2](/wiki/2_March "2 March")** - British actor [Stephen Thorne](/wiki/Stephen_Thorne) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[11](/wiki/11_June "11 June")** - [Father Morgan](/wiki/Francis_Xavier_Morgan "Francis Xavier Morgan"), the caretaker of [Ronald](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") and [Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien), dies.
- **[29](/wiki/29_June "29 June")** - Author [Robert Giddings](/wiki/Robert_Giddings) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[5](/wiki/5_July "5 July")** - [Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien) marries Magdalen Matthews.

## September {#September}

- **[16](/wiki/16_September "16 September")** - Animator [Jules Bass](/wiki/Jules_Bass), half of the [Rankin/Bass](/wiki/Rankin/Bass) production team, is born.

## December {#December}

- **[24](/wiki/24_December "24 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}
