**Írensaga** was a tall peak in the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains), notable for its saw-toothed<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-1">[1]</a></sup> crest.<sup id="cite_ref-N_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-2">[2]</a></sup>

In between Írensaga, [Starkhorn](/wiki/Starkhorn) and [Dwimorberg](/wiki/Dwimorberg) lay [Harrowdale](/wiki/Harrowdale).<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-1">[1]</a></sup> [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") saw this mountain on [9 March](/wiki/9_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") when King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) came to [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) after the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Írensaga* is a name in the [language of Rohan](/wiki/Rohanese "Rohanese"),<sup id="cite_ref-N_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-2">[2]</a></sup> "translated" into [Old English](/wiki/Old_English),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> which means "iron-saw"<sup id="cite_ref-N_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-2">[2]</a></sup> or "Ironsaw"<sup id="cite_ref-Roads_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roads-5">[5]</a></sup> with reference to its serrated ridge, crest.<sup id="cite_ref-N_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-2">[2]</a></sup>

In earlier drafts, Írensaga lacked an accent.<sup id="cite_ref-Roads_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roads-5">[5]</a></sup>
