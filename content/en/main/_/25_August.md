Events that occurred on 25 August

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1900](1900.md)** - Author and philosopher [Friedrich Nietzsche](../F/Friedrich_Nietzsche.md) dies.
- **[1930](1930.md)** - Actor [Bruce Allpress](../B/Bruce_Allpress.md) is born.
- **[1947](1947.md)** - Artist [Michael Kaluta](../M/Michael_Kaluta.md) is born.
- **[1957](1957.md)** - Author [Ian Brodie](../I/Ian_Brodie.md) is born.
- **[1963](1963.md)** - Actress [Brianne Siddall](../B/Brianne_Siddall.md) is born.
- **[1966](1966.md)** - [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and suggests that *[The End of Bovadium](../T/The_End_of_Bovadium.md)* be published in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Oxford_Magazine" title="wikipedia:The Oxford Magazine">The Oxford Magazine</a></i>.
- **[1971](1971.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 327](../L/Letter_327.md).
- **[1973](1973.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Glyn Daniel](../L/Letter_to_Glyn_Daniel.md)
- **[1974](1974.md)** - [Mythcon 5](../M/Mythcon_5.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scripps_College" title="wikipedia:Scripps College">Scripps College</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont,_California" title="wikipedia:Claremont, California">Claremont</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- <b><a href="1988.md" title="1988">1988</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_Return_of_the_Shadow.md" title="The Return of the Shadow">The Return of the Shadow</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **[2003](2003.md)** - Starter decks of "The Countdown Collection", a special set of [The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md), are released.
- **[2006](2006.md)** - [The Oxford Tolkien Conference](../T/The_Oxford_Tolkien_Conference.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Exeter College, Oxford">Exeter College, Oxford</a>.
- **[2007](2007.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s Australian Seminar 2007 in Sydney, [Australia](../A/Australia.md).
- **[2010](2010.md)** - *[A middle English vocabulary](<../A/A_middle_English_vocabulary_(2010).md>)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) is held captive by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
