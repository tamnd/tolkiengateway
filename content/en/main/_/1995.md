[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1995" title="wikipedia:1995">1995</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** - *[Proceedings of the J.R.R. Tolkien Centenary Conference](/wiki/Proceedings_of_the_J.R.R._Tolkien_Centenary_Conference)* is published.
- **[11](/wiki/11_January "11 January")** - Actor [Peter Pratt](/wiki/Peter_Pratt) dies.

## February {#February}

- **[6](/wiki/6_February "6 February")** - Actor [Frank Duncan](/wiki/Frank_Duncan) dies.
- **[7](/wiki/7_February "7 February")** - Actor [Tom Glynn-Carney](/wiki/Tom_Glynn-Carney) is born.
- **[16](/wiki/16_February "16 February")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 4 is published.

## April {#April}

- **[21](/wiki/21_April "21 April")** - *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva issue 5](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va_5 "Tyalië Tyelelliéva 5")* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_April "30 April")** - Actor [Michael Graham Cox](/wiki/Michael_Graham_Cox) dies.

## May {#May}

- **[2](/wiki/2_May "2 May")** - Actor [Michael Hordern](/wiki/Michael_Hordern) dies.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 133](/wiki/Amon_Hen_133 "Amon Hen 133") is published.

## August {#August}

- **[4](/wiki/4_August "4 August")-[7](/wiki/7_August "7 August")** - [Mythcon 26](/wiki/Mythcon_26).
- **[8](/wiki/8_August "8 August")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 6 is published.
- **[8](/wiki/8_August "8 August")** - *[Parma Eldalamberon](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon)* issue [11](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_11 "Parma Eldalamberon 11") is published.

## September {#September}

- **[15](/wiki/15_September "15 September")-[17](/wiki/17_September "17 September")** - [Oxonmoot 1995](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1995).
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 32](/wiki/Mallorn_32 "Mallorn 32") is published.

## October {#October}

- **[27](/wiki/27_October "27 October")** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Artist_and_Illustrator)* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")** - Actor [Andy Pessoa](/wiki/Andy_Pessoa) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[12](/wiki/12_November "12 November")** - Actor [Robert Stephens](/wiki/Robert_Stephens) dies.
- **[20](/wiki/20_November "20 November")** - [Ebba-Margareta von Freymann](/wiki/Ebba-Margareta_von_Freymann), a German poetess who translated most of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s songs and poems into German, dies.
- **[22](/wiki/22_November "22 November")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 7 is published.

## December {#December}

- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien (Writers and Their Work)](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_(Writers_and_Their_Work)>)* is published.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 37](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_37) is published.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - The [Tolkien Ensemble](/wiki/Tolkien_Ensemble) is founded.
