Events that occurred on 12 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1925](/wiki/1925)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to a Mr. Ashton](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Ashton "Letter to Mr Ashton").
- **[1932](/wiki/1932)** - Actor [Michael Elsworth](/wiki/Michael_Elsworth) is born.
- **[1967](/wiki/1967)** - [Donald Swann](/wiki/Donald_Swann) and [William Elvin](/wiki/William_Elvin) record the music for *[The Road Goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")*.
- **[2002](/wiki/2002)** - *[The Art of The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Art_of_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* is published.
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - The fan film *[The Peril to the Shire](/wiki/The_Peril_to_the_Shire)* premières to a private audience.
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online): Shores of Evendim* patch is released.
- **[2015](/wiki/2015)** - [*Parma Eldalamberon* 22](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_22 "Parma Eldalamberon 22") is published.
