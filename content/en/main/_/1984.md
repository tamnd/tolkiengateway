[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1984" title="wikipedia:1984">1984</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[24](/wiki/24_February "24 February")** - *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One)*, the first book of *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)*, is published in the US.
- **[27](/wiki/27_February "27 February")** - [Michael Tolkien](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s second son, dies.
- **?** - *[Fantasists on Fantasy: A Collection of Critical Reflections by Eighteen Masters of the Art](/wiki/Fantasists_on_Fantasy "Fantasists on Fantasy")*, including two works by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.

## April {#April}

- **[1](/wiki/1_April "1 April")** - *[The Monsters and the Critics](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics)* is published in the US.
- **[18](/wiki/18_April "18 April")** - [Francis de Wolff](/wiki/Francis_de_Wolff), who voiced [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) in [the 1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)"), dies.
- **?** - *[Bree and the Barrow-Downs](/wiki/Bree_and_the_Barrow-Downs)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[1](/wiki/1_June "1 June")** - *[Moria: The Dwarven City](/wiki/Moria:_The_Dwarven_City)* is published.
- **[6](/wiki/6_June "6 June")** - [Åke Ohlmarks](/wiki/%C3%85ke_Ohlmarks), Swedish translator, dies.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 21](/wiki/Mallorn_21 "Mallorn 21") is published.

## August {#August}

- **[10](/wiki/10_August "10 August")-[13](/wiki/13_August "13 August")** - [Mythcon 15](/wiki/Mythcon_15).
- <b><a href="/wiki/16_August" title="16 August">16</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales:_Part_Two" title="The Book of Lost Tales: Part Two">The Book of Lost Tales: Part Two</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>

## October {#October}

- **[5](/wiki/5_October "5 October")-[7](/wiki/7_October "7 October")** - [Oxonmoot 1984](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1984).

## November {#November}

- **[26](/wiki/26_November "26 November")** - *[The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two)* is published in the US.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 70](/wiki/Amon_Hen_70 "Amon Hen 70") is published.

## December {#December}

- **?** - [*Beyond Bree* December 1984](/wiki/Beyond_Bree_December_1984 "Beyond Bree December 1984") is published.

## ? {#?}

- *?* - [Jane Johnson](/wiki/Jude_Fisher "Jude Fisher") becomes Publisher of the [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") list at [Allen and Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin), [Unwin Hyman](/wiki/Unwin_Hyman) and [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins).
- **?** - *[Rangers of the North (book)](</wiki/Rangers_of_the_North_(book)>)* is published.

- **?** - *[The Tower of Cirith Ungol and Shelob's Lair](/wiki/The_Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol_and_Shelob%27s_Lair)* is published.
- **?** - *[Hillmen of the Trollshaws](/wiki/Hillmen_of_the_Trollshaws)* is published.
