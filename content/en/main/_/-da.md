<i><b>-da</b></i> is a [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish) suffix for the passive product of an action.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Used to denoting the passive result of the action, and when used substantivally a single product of this. Tolkien notes that "*dā* (variant of *nā*)" and "in practice words denoting products are found made with either *dā* or *tā* (lengthening owing to the influence of *dā*)"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Quenya examples {#Quenya_examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Yulda" title="Yulda">yul<b>da</b></a></i> = "draught"
- <i>car<b>da</b></i> = "deed"
