[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1900" title="wikipedia:1900">1900</a></b>.

## June {#June}

- **?** - [Mabel Tolkien](../M/Mabel_Suffield.md) and her sister May Incledon are received into the Church of Rome, bringing about the wrath and opposition of their strongly Baptist relatives.

## August {#August}

- **[25](25_August.md)** - [Friedrich Nietzsche](../F/Friedrich_Nietzsche.md), whose works have been compared to those of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in scholarly articles, dies.

## September {#September}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is admitted into [King Edward's School](../K/King_Edward's_School.md) after passing the entrance exam.

## Late {#Late}

- **?** - The Tolkiens move from [Sarehole](../S/Sarehole.md) to <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Moseley&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Moseley (page does not exist)">Moseley</a> to be closer to [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md) and [King Edward's School](../K/King_Edward's_School.md).
