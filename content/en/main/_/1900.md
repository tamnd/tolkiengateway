[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1900" title="wikipedia:1900">1900</a></b>.

## June {#June}

- **?** - [Mabel Tolkien](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield "Mabel Suffield") and her sister May Incledon are received into the Church of Rome, bringing about the wrath and opposition of their strongly Baptist relatives.

## August {#August}

- **[25](/wiki/25_August "25 August")** - [Friedrich Nietzsche](/wiki/Friedrich_Nietzsche), whose works have been compared to those of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in scholarly articles, dies.

## September {#September}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is admitted into [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School) after passing the entrance exam.

## Late {#Late}

- **?** - The Tolkiens move from [Sarehole](/wiki/Sarehole) to <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Moseley&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Moseley (page does not exist)">Moseley</a> to be closer to [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham) and [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School).
