Events that occurred on 24 July

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1915](1915.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes the poem *[The Happy Mariners](../T/The_Happy_Mariners.md)*.
- **[1916](1916.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s second five-day front-line duty begins.
- **[1938](1938.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 31](../L/Letter_31.md).
- **[1968](1968.md)** - Artist [Colleen Doran](../C/Colleen_Doran.md) is born.
- **[1971](1971.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 326](../L/Letter_326.md).
- **[1982](1982.md)** - The second episode of the new version of the [1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- **[1987](1987.md)** - [Mythcon 18](../M/Mythcon_18.md) at [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md), <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milwaukee" title="wikipedia:Milwaukee">Milwaukee</a>.
- **[2008](2008.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md): [Shadows of Angmar](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Shadows_of_Angmar.md)*, "The Ring-forges of Eregion" is released in Europe.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - [Tolkien Thing](../T/Tolkien_Thing.md) 2009 in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rieneck" title="wikipedia:Rieneck">Rieneck</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany" title="wikipedia:Germany">Germany</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) is held captive by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The funeral procession of king [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) travels toward [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md)
