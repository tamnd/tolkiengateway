**ÑGUR** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "death".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ety_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)): *gûr* ("death")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *nuru* ("death")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-2">[2]</a></sup>
