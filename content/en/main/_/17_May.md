- **[1954](1954.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Henry Willink](../L/Letter_to_Henry_Willink.md).
- **[1961](1961.md)** – Singer [Enya](../E/Enya.md) is born.
- **[1981](1981.md)** - The eleventh episode of the [1981 radio adaptation](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- **[2007](2007.md)** - New Zealand actor [Noel Appleby](../N/Noel_Appleby.md) dies.
