Events that occurred on 5 January.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1925](1925.md)** - Voice actor [Wolfe Morris](../W/Wolfe_Morris.md) is born.
- **[1956](1956.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 179c](../L/Letter_179c.md).
- **[1961](1961.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 227](../L/Letter_227.md).
- **[1961](1961.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mme Ossendrijver](<../L/Letter_to_Madam_Ossendrijver_(5_January_1961).md>).
- **[1967](1967.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Joy Hill](<../L/Letter_to_Joy_Hill_(5_January_1967).md>).
- **[1982](1982.md)** - Voice actor [Hans Conried](../H/Hans_Conried.md) dies.
- **[1989](1989.md)** - *[The Return of the Shadow](../T/The_Return_of_the_Shadow.md)* is published in the US.<b></b>
- **[1996](1996.md)** - Actor [Maxim Baldry](../M/Maxim_Baldry.md) is born.
- **[1998](1998.md)** - *[Roverandom](../R/Roverandom.md)* is published in the UK.<b></b>
- **[2004](2004.md)** - Graphic novel *[Heaven's War](../H/Heaven's_War.md)* is published.
- **[2008](2008.md)** - <a class="external text" href="https://web.archive.org/web/20080511204023/http://entertainment.timesonline.co.uk/tol/arts_and_entertainment/books/article3127837.ece" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Times</a> ranks [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) the sixth-best post-war British writer.
