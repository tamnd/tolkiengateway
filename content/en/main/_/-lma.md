<i><b>-lma</b></i> pronominal [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) ending meaning "our", attested (with the genitive ending *[-o](/wiki/-o)* that displaces final *-a*) in the word *omentielmo* "of our meeting". This "our" is plural inclusive.

[Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") (it seems) improperly used it in the greeting "a star shines on the hour of our meeting": since he and the Elf he was speaking to constituted only two persons, he should have used the *dual* inclusive instead. The mistake (which was corrected in the second edition) is now widely regarded as a typo. The correct form **omentielvo** occurs in some of the manuscripts of the Red Book.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
