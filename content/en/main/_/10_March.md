Events that occurred on 10 March

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1937](1937.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 11b](../L/Letter_11b.md).
- **[1950](1950.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes Letters [125](../L/Letter_125.md) and [126](../L/Letter_126.md).
- **[1950](1950.md)** - [Stephen Oliver](../S/Stephen_Oliver.md) is born.
- **[1963](1963.md)** - Voice actor [Steve Staley](../S/Steve_Staley.md) is born.
- **[1981](1981.md)** - [*Amon Hen* 49](../A/Amon_Hen_49.md) is published.
- **[2004](2004.md)** - "Siege of Gondor", the eighth set in *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md)* is released.
- **[2007](2007.md)** - "[The One Ring Celebration](../T/The_One_Ring_Celebration.md) 2007" in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burbank,_California" title="wikipedia:Burbank, California">Burbank, California</a>.
- **[2008](2008.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is elected "Best Foreign Book of All Time" in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands" title="wikipedia:Netherlands">Netherlands</a>.
- **[2026](2026.md)** - Artist [Michael Hague](../M/Michael_Hague.md) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md):
    - [Borondir](../B/Borondir.md) and his companion depart from [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) in search of the [Éothéod](Éothéod.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The [Great Signal](../G/Great_Signal.md).
    - The [Dawnless Day](../D/Dawnless_Day.md).
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) pass the [Cross-roads](../C/Cross-roads.md), and see the [Morgul-host](../M/Minas_Morgul.md) set forth, towards [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).
    - The Muster of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) takes place. The [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) ride from [Harrowdale](../H/Harrowdale.md).
    - [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) is rescued by [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) from the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) outside the gates of [the City](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). He meets [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) and reports to [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md).
    - [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), leading the [Dead Men](../D/Dead_Men.md) and the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md), crosses the river [Ringló](../R/Ringló.md).
    - An army of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) from the [Morannon](../M/Morannon.md) takes [Cair Andros](../C/Cair_Andros.md) and passes into [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md).
