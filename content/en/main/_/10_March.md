Events that occurred on 10 March

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1937](/wiki/1937)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 11b](/wiki/Letter_11b).
- **[1950](/wiki/1950)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes Letters [125](/wiki/Letter_125 "Letter 125") and [126](/wiki/Letter_126 "Letter 126").
- **[1950](/wiki/1950)** - [Stephen Oliver](/wiki/Stephen_Oliver) is born.
- **[1963](/wiki/1963)** - Voice actor [Steve Staley](/wiki/Steve_Staley) is born.
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - [*Amon Hen* 49](/wiki/Amon_Hen_49 "Amon Hen 49") is published.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - "Siege of Gondor", the eighth set in *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game)* is released.
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - "[The One Ring Celebration](/wiki/The_One_Ring_Celebration) 2007" in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burbank,_California" title="wikipedia:Burbank, California">Burbank, California</a>.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is elected "Best Foreign Book of All Time" in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands" title="wikipedia:Netherlands">Netherlands</a>.
- **[2026](/wiki/2026)** - Artist [Michael Hague](/wiki/Michael_Hague) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"):
    - [Borondir](/wiki/Borondir) and his companion depart from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) in search of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - The [Great Signal](/wiki/Great_Signal).
    - The [Dawnless Day](/wiki/Dawnless_Day).
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) pass the [Cross-roads](/wiki/Cross-roads), and see the [Morgul-host](/wiki/Minas_Morgul "Minas Morgul") set forth, towards [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).
    - The Muster of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) takes place. The [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) ride from [Harrowdale](/wiki/Harrowdale).
    - [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) is rescued by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) from the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) outside the gates of [the City](/wiki/Minas_Tirith "Minas Tirith"). He meets [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") and reports to [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor).
    - [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), leading the [Dead Men](/wiki/Dead_Men) and the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company), crosses the river [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3).
    - An army of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) from the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon) takes [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros) and passes into [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien).
