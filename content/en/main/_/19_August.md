Events that occurred on 19 August

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1916](/wiki/1916)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [G.B. Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith "Geoffrey Bache Smith") meet at Acheux.
- **[1920](/wiki/1920)** - Composer [Paul Kont](/wiki/Paul_Kont) is born.
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 289a](/wiki/Letter_289a "Letter 289a").
- **[1973](/wiki/1973)** - [Mythcon 4](/wiki/Mythcon_4) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Barbara,_California" title="wikipedia:Santa Barbara, California">Santa Barbara</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1991](/wiki/1991)** - Scholar [Taum Santoski](/wiki/Taum_Santoski) dies of cancer at the age of 32.
- **[1992](/wiki/1992)** - [Tolkien Centenary Conference](/wiki/Tolkien_Centenary_Conference), a combination of [Oxonmoot](/wiki/Oxonmoot) 1992 and [Mythcon](/wiki/Mythcon) 23, at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keble_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Keble College, Oxford">Keble College, Oxford</a>.
- **[2000](/wiki/2000)** - [Mythcon 31](/wiki/Mythcon_31) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaii" title="wikipedia:Hawaii">Hawaii</a>.
- **[2012](/wiki/2012)** - [The Return of the Ring](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_Ring), [Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society "The Tolkien Society") conference at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loughborough_University" title="wikipedia:Loughborough University">Loughborough University</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>.
- **[2022](/wiki/2022)** - [The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power (Season One: Amazon Original Series Soundtrack)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_(Season_One:_Amazon_Original_Series_Soundtrack)>) is released.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) is held captive by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) visit the [Glittering Caves](/wiki/Glittering_Caves).
