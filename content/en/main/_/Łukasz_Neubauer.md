**Łukasz Neubauer** is a [Tolkien scholar](/wiki/Tolkien_studies "Tolkien studies") from Poland. He received his PhD in English philology from the University of Łódź. He is a researcher and lecturer at the University of Koszalin, Poland, where he teaches courses on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) literature, and Arthurian romances. In addition to publishing numerous articles on medieval, as well as [Christian](/wiki/Christianity "Christianity"), influences and resonances in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
