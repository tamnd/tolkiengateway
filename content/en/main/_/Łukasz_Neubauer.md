**Łukasz Neubauer** is a [Tolkien scholar](../T/Tolkien_studies.md) from Poland. He received his PhD in English philology from the University of Łódź. He is a researcher and lecturer at the University of Koszalin, Poland, where he teaches courses on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) literature, and Arthurian romances. In addition to publishing numerous articles on medieval, as well as [Christian](../C/Christianity.md), influences and resonances in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.
