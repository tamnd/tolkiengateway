## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>în</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word appearing in the [King's Letter](/wiki/King%27s_Letter).<sup id="cite_ref-SD_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SD-1">[1]</a></sup> The word is commonly understood to be a pronoun meaning "his" (referring to the subject).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Phrases {#Phrases}

"<i>Ar e aníra ennas suilannad mhellyn <b>în</b> phain</i><sup id="cite_ref-SD_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SD-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Noldorin {#Noldorin}

<i><b>în</b></i> is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word meaning "year".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") YEN ("year")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Cognates {#Cognates}
