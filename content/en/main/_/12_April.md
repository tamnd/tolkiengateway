Events that occurred on 12 April.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1956](/wiki/1956)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to a Mr. Earle](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Earle "Letter to Mr Earle").
- **[1958](/wiki/1958)** - Voice actor [Tony Oliver](/wiki/Tony_Oliver) is born.
- **[1962](/wiki/1962)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 237](/wiki/Letter_237).
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mother Mary Anthony](/wiki/Letter_to_Mother_Mary_Anthony "Letter to Mother Mary Anthony").
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to James Ridolfo](/wiki/Letter_to_James_Ridolfo "Letter to James Ridolfo").
- **[1967](/wiki/1967)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 295a](/wiki/Letter_295a "Letter 295a").
- **[1970](/wiki/1970)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Tom Shippey](/wiki/Letter_to_Tom_Shippey "Letter to Tom Shippey").
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - The sixth episode of [the 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"):
    - [Eorl the Young](/wiki/Eorl "Eorl") and the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) travel towards the [Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Field_of_Celebrant).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Ted Sandyman](/wiki/Ted_Sandyman) are in *[The Green Dragon](/wiki/The_Green_Dragon)* and gossip about the strange news.<sup id="cite_ref-shadow_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - At the evening, the weather was clearing after a rain. Samwise returns to [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), at the same time [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) arrives after a nine-year absence.<sup id="cite_ref-shadow_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - Gandalf warns [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) that [his ring](/wiki/One_Ring "One Ring") is dangerous, mentions [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) to him, but says no more until the morning.<sup id="cite_ref-shadow_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-1">[1]</a></sup>
