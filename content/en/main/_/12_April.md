Events that occurred on 12 April.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1956](1956.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to a Mr. Earle](../L/Letter_to_Mr_Earle.md).
- **[1958](1958.md)** - Voice actor [Tony Oliver](../T/Tony_Oliver.md) is born.
- **[1962](1962.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 237](../L/Letter_237.md).
- **[1966](1966.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mother Mary Anthony](../L/Letter_to_Mother_Mary_Anthony.md).
- **[1966](1966.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to James Ridolfo](../L/Letter_to_James_Ridolfo.md).
- **[1967](1967.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 295a](../L/Letter_295a.md).
- **[1970](1970.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Tom Shippey](../L/Letter_to_Tom_Shippey.md).
- **[1981](1981.md)** - The sixth episode of [the 1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md):
    - [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl.md) and the [Éothéod](Éothéod.md) travel towards the [Field of Celebrant](../F/Field_of_Celebrant.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Ted Sandyman](../T/Ted_Sandyman.md) are in *[The Green Dragon](../T/The_Green_Dragon.md)* and gossip about the strange news.<sup id="cite_ref-shadow_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - At the evening, the weather was clearing after a rain. Samwise returns to [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md), at the same time [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) arrives after a nine-year absence.<sup id="cite_ref-shadow_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - Gandalf warns [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) that [his ring](../O/One_Ring.md) is dangerous, mentions [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) to him, but says no more until the morning.<sup id="cite_ref-shadow_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-1">[1]</a></sup>
