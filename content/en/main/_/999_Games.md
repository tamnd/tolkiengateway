**999 Games** is a Dutch board game publisher. Originally founded in [1990](/wiki/1990) as a small mail order company, its distribution of [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering) led to massive expansion.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> 999 Games publishes Dutch translations of various card and board games based on Tolkien's works.
