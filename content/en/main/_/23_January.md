Events that occurred on 23 January.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1917](1917.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) faces a Medical Board in [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md), but he is not deemed fit to return to [war](../W/World_War_I.md).
- **[1939](1939.md)** - [The Brothers Hildebrandt](../T/The_Brothers_Hildebrandt.md) are born.
- **[2020](2020.md)** - [Barbara Remington](../B/Barbara_Remington.md), cover artist on some American editions, dies.
- **[2021](2021.md)** - *[Tolkien and the Classical World](../T/Tolkien_and_the_Classical_World.md)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) rests in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) pursues [the Balrog](../D/Durin's_Bane.md) to the peak of [Celebdil](../C/Celebdil.md).
