Events that occurred on 21 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1925](/wiki/1925)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) submits his resignation from [Leeds](/wiki/Leeds_University "Leeds University"), his employment will officially end on [31 December](/wiki/31_December) 1925.
- **[1926](/wiki/1926)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Willard G. Harding](</wiki/Letter_to_Willard_G._Harding_(21_October_1926)> "Letter to Willard G. Harding (21 October 1926)").
- **[1929](/wiki/1929)** – Author [Ursula K. Le Guin](/wiki/Ursula_K._Le_Guin) born.
- **[1936](/wiki/1936)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 8c](/wiki/Letter_8c).
- **[1952](/wiki/1952)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 134a](/wiki/Letter_134a).
- **[1955](/wiki/1955)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) presents his paper [English and Welsh](/wiki/English_and_Welsh).
- **[1963](/wiki/1963)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mrs. Munby](/wiki/Letter_to_Mrs_Munby "Letter to Mrs Munby").
- **[1972](/wiki/1972)** – Actor [Felix Felton](/wiki/Felix_Felton) dies.
- <b><a href="/wiki/2004" title="2004">2004</a> – The <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition)" title="The Lord of the Rings (50th Anniversary Edition)">50th Anniversary Edition</a> of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** – Fantasy novel *[The Indigo King](/wiki/The_Indigo_King)*, featuring [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) as a fictional character, is published.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** – In a press release, it is confirmed that [Martin Freeman](/wiki/Martin_Freeman) will play [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") recovers from his wound in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
