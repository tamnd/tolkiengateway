[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1938" title="wikipedia:1938">1938</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](1_January.md)** - In a series of lectures aimed at children, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) lectures on *[Dragons](<../D/Dragons_(lecture).md>)* in the [University](../U/University_of_Oxford.md) Museum.
- **[8](8_January.md)** - [Michael Graham Cox](../M/Michael_Graham_Cox.md), who would voice [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>) and [the 1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), is born.
- **[10](10_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) goes to London to rehearse his talk at the BBC, [four days later](14_January.md).
- **[14](14_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) gives a talk about *Anglo-Saxon Verse* on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).

## February {#February}

- **[1](1_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 21](../L/Letter_21.md).
- **[4](4_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 22](../L/Letter_22.md).
- **[14](14_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) gives an invitational speech at the Lovelace Society, the essay club of Worcester College, [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md). He reads them *The Legend of Worming Hall*, a story that would become *[Farmer Giles of Ham](../F/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md)*. [Norman Power](../N/Norman_Power.md) is present at the speech.
- **[17](17_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 23](../L/Letter_23.md).
- **[18](18_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 24](../L/Letter_24.md).
- **[20](20_February.md)** - In response to an article in *The Observer* of [January 16](16_January.md), a [letter](../L/Letter_25.md) by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is published, explaining the name and inception of the intriguing hero, which would, according to the *Observer*, *"save many research students so very much trouble in the generations to come."*
- **[24](24_February.md)** - Actor [Martyn Sanderson](../M/Martyn_Sanderson.md) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[4](4_March.md)** - Tolkien writes [Letter 26](../L/Letter_26.md).
- **[26](26_March.md)** - "[There's magic in The Hobbit](../T/There's_magic_in_The_Hobbit.md)", [Houghton Mifflin](../H/Houghton_Mifflin.md)'s advertisement for *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, is printed in *Publishers Weekly*.

## June {#June}

- **[4](4_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 28](../L/Letter_28.md).

## July {#July}

- **[21](21_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to A.F. Colborn](<../L/Letter_to_A.F._Colborn_(21_July_1938).md>).
- **[24](24_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 31](../L/Letter_31.md).
- **[25](25_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 29](../L/Letter_29.md).
- **[25](25_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) sends an angry [letter](../L/Letter_30.md) to a German publisher, after being asked whether he was Aryan or not.
- **[27](27_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 32](../L/Letter_32.md).
- **[29](29_July.md)** - [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md) dies.

## August {#August}

- **[31](31_August.md)** - Tolkien submits the finished version of *[Farmer Giles of Ham](../F/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md)* to [Allen and Unwin](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md), but it is rejected.
- **[31](31_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 33](../L/Letter_33.md).

## September {#September}

- **? (last days)** - Tolkien writes "[Ancient History](../T/The_Shadow_of_the_Past.md)" and inserts it as the third chapter of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## October {#October}

- **[13](13_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 34](../L/Letter_34.md).
- **[22](22_October.md)** - [Derek Jacobi](../D/Derek_Jacobi.md), who narrated several [audiobooks](../A/Audiobooks.md), is born.
- **[27](27_October.md)** - [Lascelles Abercrombie](../L/Lascelles_Abercrombie.md) dies.
- **[29](29_October.md)** - [Ralph Bakshi](../R/Ralph_Bakshi.md), who would direct [the animated movie](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>) in [1978](1978.md), is born.

## December {#December}

- **? (early)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) completes writing "Chapter 12" of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* (eventually *[Many Meetings](../M/Many_Meetings.md)* that would be the 13th chapter<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>), but is unable to return to writing for a couple of months.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **[24](24_December.md)** - Tolkien writes [a postcard to Miss Kilbride](../L/Letter_to_Miss_Kilbride.md).
- **[25](25_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to Tolkien's children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published in the United States and receives the New York Herald Tribune award as best children's book of the season.
- **?** - *[The Oxford Book of Greek Verse in Translation](../T/The_Oxford_Book_of_Greek_Verse_in_Translation.md)*, to which [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) helped with an Introduction, is published.
