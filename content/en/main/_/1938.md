[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1938" title="wikipedia:1938">1938</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** - In a series of lectures aimed at children, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) lectures on *[Dragons](</wiki/Dragons_(lecture)> "Dragons (lecture)")* in the [University](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford") Museum.
- **[8](/wiki/8_January "8 January")** - [Michael Graham Cox](/wiki/Michael_Graham_Cox), who would voice [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)") and [the 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born.
- **[10](/wiki/10_January "10 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) goes to London to rehearse his talk at the BBC, [four days later](/wiki/14_January "14 January").
- **[14](/wiki/14_January "14 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) gives a talk about *Anglo-Saxon Verse* on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC").

## February {#February}

- **[1](/wiki/1_February "1 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 21](/wiki/Letter_21).
- **[4](/wiki/4_February "4 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 22](/wiki/Letter_22 "Letter 22").
- **[14](/wiki/14_February "14 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) gives an invitational speech at the Lovelace Society, the essay club of Worcester College, [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford). He reads them *The Legend of Worming Hall*, a story that would become *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)*. [Norman Power](/wiki/Norman_Power) is present at the speech.
- **[17](/wiki/17_February "17 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 23](/wiki/Letter_23).
- **[18](/wiki/18_February "18 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 24](/wiki/Letter_24).
- **[20](/wiki/20_February "20 February")** - In response to an article in *The Observer* of [January 16](/wiki/16_January "16 January"), a [letter](/wiki/Letter_25 "Letter 25") by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is published, explaining the name and inception of the intriguing hero, which would, according to the *Observer*, *"save many research students so very much trouble in the generations to come."*
- **[24](/wiki/24_February "24 February")** - Actor [Martyn Sanderson](/wiki/Martyn_Sanderson) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[4](/wiki/4_March "4 March")** - Tolkien writes [Letter 26](/wiki/Letter_26).
- **[26](/wiki/26_March "26 March")** - "[There's magic in The Hobbit](/wiki/There%27s_magic_in_The_Hobbit)", [Houghton Mifflin](/wiki/Houghton_Mifflin)'s advertisement for *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, is printed in *Publishers Weekly*.

## June {#June}

- **[4](/wiki/4_June "4 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 28](/wiki/Letter_28 "Letter 28").

## July {#July}

- **[21](/wiki/21_July "21 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to A.F. Colborn](</wiki/Letter_to_A.F._Colborn_(21_July_1938)> "Letter to A.F. Colborn (21 July 1938)").
- **[24](/wiki/24_July "24 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 31](/wiki/Letter_31 "Letter 31").
- **[25](/wiki/25_July "25 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 29](/wiki/Letter_29 "Letter 29").
- **[25](/wiki/25_July "25 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) sends an angry [letter](/wiki/Letter_30 "Letter 30") to a German publisher, after being asked whether he was Aryan or not.
- **[27](/wiki/27_July "27 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 32](/wiki/Letter_32 "Letter 32").
- **[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")** - [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon) dies.

## August {#August}

- **[31](/wiki/31_August "31 August")** - Tolkien submits the finished version of *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)* to [Allen and Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin), but it is rejected.
- **[31](/wiki/31_August "31 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 33](/wiki/Letter_33 "Letter 33").

## September {#September}

- **? (last days)** - Tolkien writes "[Ancient History](/wiki/The_Shadow_of_the_Past "The Shadow of the Past")" and inserts it as the third chapter of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## October {#October}

- **[13](/wiki/13_October "13 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 34](/wiki/Letter_34 "Letter 34").
- **[22](/wiki/22_October "22 October")** - [Derek Jacobi](/wiki/Derek_Jacobi), who narrated several [audiobooks](/wiki/Audiobooks "Audiobooks"), is born.
- **[27](/wiki/27_October "27 October")** - [Lascelles Abercrombie](/wiki/Lascelles_Abercrombie) dies.
- **[29](/wiki/29_October "29 October")** - [Ralph Bakshi](/wiki/Ralph_Bakshi), who would direct [the animated movie](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)") in [1978](/wiki/1978), is born.

## December {#December}

- **? (early)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) completes writing "Chapter 12" of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* (eventually *[Many Meetings](/wiki/Many_Meetings)* that would be the 13th chapter<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>), but is unable to return to writing for a couple of months.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **[24](/wiki/24_December "24 December")** - Tolkien writes [a postcard to Miss Kilbride](/wiki/Letter_to_Miss_Kilbride "Letter to Miss Kilbride").
- **[25](/wiki/25_December "25 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to Tolkien's children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published in the United States and receives the New York Herald Tribune award as best children's book of the season.
- **?** - *[The Oxford Book of Greek Verse in Translation](/wiki/The_Oxford_Book_of_Greek_Verse_in_Translation)*, to which [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) helped with an Introduction, is published.
