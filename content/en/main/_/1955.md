[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1955" title="wikipedia:1955">1955</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[15](/wiki/15_February "15 February")** - Tolkien leaves the Oxford Dante Society.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## March {#March}

- **[2](/wiki/2_March "2 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Derrick Parnum](/wiki/Letter_to_Derrick_Parnum "Letter to Derrick Parnum").
- **[3](/wiki/3_March "3 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 159](/wiki/Letter_159).
- **[6](/wiki/6_March "6 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 160](/wiki/Letter_160 "Letter 160").

## April {#April}

- **[14](/wiki/14_April "14 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 161](/wiki/Letter_161 "Letter 161").
- **[18](/wiki/18_April "18 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 162](/wiki/Letter_162 "Letter 162").
- **[18](/wiki/18_April "18 April")** - [Grant Major](/wiki/Grant_Major) is born.
- **[21](/wiki/21_April "21 April")** - The American edition of *The Two Towers* is published by the [Houghton Mifflin Company](/wiki/Houghton_Mifflin_Harcourt "Houghton Mifflin Harcourt").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **?** - [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) draws *[Map of Rohan, Gondor, and Mordor](/wiki/Map_of_Rohan,_Gondor,_and_Mordor)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## May {#May}

- **[2](/wiki/2_May "2 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to a Miss Turnbull](</wiki/Letter_to_Miss_Turnbull_(2_May_1955)> "Letter to Miss Turnbull (2 May 1955)").
- **[12](/wiki/12_May "12 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Rayner Unwin](</wiki/Letter_to_Rayner_Unwin_(12_May_1955)> "Letter to Rayner Unwin (12 May 1955)").
- **[19](/wiki/19_May "19 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to a Miss Turnbull](</wiki/Letter_to_Miss_Turnbull_(19_May_1955)> "Letter to Miss Turnbull (19 May 1955)").
- **[26](/wiki/26_May "26 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 162a](/wiki/Letter_162a).

## June {#June}

- **[5](/wiki/5_June "5 June")** - Harvey Breit writes an article on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") in the *New York Times Book Review*. Breit tells of "Dr. [Tolkein](/wiki/Tolkein)", and the article contains many more errors. Tolkien sends an angry letter to him.
- **[7](/wiki/7_June "7 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 163](/wiki/Letter_163 "Letter 163").
- **[13](/wiki/13_June "13 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Deirdre Levinson](</wiki/Letter_to_Deirdre_Levinson_(13_June_1955)> "Letter to Deirdre Levinson (13 June 1955)").
- **[29](/wiki/29_June "29 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 164](/wiki/Letter_164 "Letter 164").
- **[29](/wiki/29_June "29 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the Preface to *[The Ancrene Riwle](/wiki/The_Ancrene_Riwle)*.
- **[30](/wiki/30_June "30 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 165](/wiki/Letter_165 "Letter 165").

## July {#July}

- **[5](/wiki/5_July "5 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 165a](/wiki/Letter_165a).
- **[15](/wiki/15_July "15 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 165b](/wiki/Letter_165b).
- **[22](/wiki/22_July "22 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 166](/wiki/Letter_166 "Letter 166").
- **[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mrs Dixey](/wiki/Letter_to_Mrs_Dixey "Letter to Mrs Dixey").
- **[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Jennifer Brookes-Smith](</wiki/Letter_to_Jennifer_Brookes-Smith_(28_July_1955)> "Letter to Jennifer Brookes-Smith (28 July 1955)").
- **[30](/wiki/30_July "30 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and his daughter [Priscilla](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien "Priscilla Tolkien") visit Italy, and he writes several entries in [his diary](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_diaries "J.R.R. Tolkien's diaries") concerning the trip.

## August {#August}

- **[12](/wiki/12_August "12 August")** - During the holiday trip to Italy, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and his daughter [Priscilla](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien "Priscilla Tolkien") attend High Mass at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_di_Santa_Chiara" title="wikipedia:Basilica di Santa Chiara">Basilica di Santa Chiara in Assisi</a>.
- **[15](/wiki/15_August "15 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 167](/wiki/Letter_167 "Letter 167").
- **[22](/wiki/22_August "22 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to John Roberts](/wiki/Letter_to_John_Roberts "Letter to John Roberts").

## September {#September}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the draft of [letter 171](/wiki/Letter_171 "Letter 171").
- **[7](/wiki/7_September "7 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 168](/wiki/Letter_168 "Letter 168").
- **[8](/wiki/8_September "8 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 168a](/wiki/Letter_168a).
- **[11](/wiki/11_September "11 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 169](/wiki/Letter_169 "Letter 169").
- **[18](/wiki/18_September "18 September")** - [Bob Papenbrook](/wiki/Bob_Papenbrook), who would voice [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) in the video game [The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring), is born.
- **[30](/wiki/30_September "30 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 170](/wiki/Letter_170 "Letter 170").

## October {#October}

- **[12](/wiki/12_October "12 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 172](/wiki/Letter_172 "Letter 172").
- **[20](/wiki/20_October "20 October")** - **[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King), the third and final volume of [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings), is published**.
- **[21](/wiki/21_October "21 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) presents his paper [English and Welsh](/wiki/English_and_Welsh).
- **[24](/wiki/24_October "24 October")** - Tolkien writes [letter 173](/wiki/Letter_173 "Letter 173").
- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")** - Radio broadcast about Tolkien's works. Tolkien misses it because he is on a visit in [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge), in a house that has no radio.

## November {#November}

- **[10](/wiki/10_November "10 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 174](/wiki/Letter_174 "Letter 174").
- **[25](/wiki/25_November "25 November")** - [Bruce Hopkins](/wiki/Bruce_Hopkins), who would play [Gamling](/wiki/Gamling) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.
- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 175](/wiki/Letter_175 "Letter 175").
- **?** - The first episode of the [first radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)") *is aired on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC").*
- **?** - *[The Ancrene Riwle](/wiki/The_Ancrene_Riwle)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[2](/wiki/2_December "2 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Elizabeth Jennings](/wiki/Letter_to_Elizabeth_Jennings "Letter to Elizabeth Jennings").
- **[3](/wiki/3_December "3 December")** - Tolkien's poem *[Imram](/wiki/Imram)* is published in *[Time and Tide](</wiki/Time_and_Tide_(3_December_1955)> "Time and Tide (3 December 1955)")*.
- **[8](/wiki/8_December "8 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes letters [176](/wiki/Letter_176 "Letter 176") and [177](/wiki/Letter_177 "Letter 177").
- **[12](/wiki/12_December "12 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 178](/wiki/Letter_178 "Letter 178").
- **[12](/wiki/12_December "12 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to David Masson](</wiki/Letter_to_David_Masson_(12_December_1955)> "Letter to David Masson (12 December 1955)").
- **[14](/wiki/14_December "14 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 179](/wiki/Letter_179 "Letter 179").
- **[16](/wiki/16_December "16 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mr Capan](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Capan "Letter to Mr Capan").
- **[20](/wiki/20_December "20 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 179a](/wiki/Letter_179a).
- **[20](/wiki/20_December "20 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 179b](/wiki/Letter_179b).
- **[21](/wiki/21_December "21 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mrs Rachael Feichtmeir](/wiki/Letter_to_Mrs_Rachael_Feichtmeir "Letter to Mrs Rachael Feichtmeir").

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Pete Fenlon](/wiki/Pete_Fenlon) is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [an 8-page draft letter to Mr David Masson](</wiki/Letter_to_David_Masson_(1955)> "Letter to David Masson (1955)").
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to August Closs](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien#1950 "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien").
- **?late** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to G.E. Selby](</wiki/Letter_to_G.E._Selby_(1955/6)> "Letter to G.E. Selby (1955/6)").
