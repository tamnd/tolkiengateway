[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1955" title="wikipedia:1955">1955</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[15](15_February.md)** - Tolkien leaves the Oxford Dante Society.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## March {#March}

- **[2](2_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Derrick Parnum](../L/Letter_to_Derrick_Parnum.md).
- **[3](3_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 159](../L/Letter_159.md).
- **[6](6_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 160](../L/Letter_160.md).

## April {#April}

- **[14](14_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 161](../L/Letter_161.md).
- **[18](18_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 162](../L/Letter_162.md).
- **[18](18_April.md)** - [Grant Major](../G/Grant_Major.md) is born.
- **[21](21_April.md)** - The American edition of *The Two Towers* is published by the [Houghton Mifflin Company](../H/Houghton_Mifflin_Harcourt.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **?** - [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) draws *[Map of Rohan, Gondor, and Mordor](../M/Map_of_Rohan,_Gondor,_and_Mordor.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## May {#May}

- **[2](2_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to a Miss Turnbull](<../L/Letter_to_Miss_Turnbull_(2_May_1955).md>).
- **[12](12_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Rayner Unwin](<../L/Letter_to_Rayner_Unwin_(12_May_1955).md>).
- **[19](19_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to a Miss Turnbull](<../L/Letter_to_Miss_Turnbull_(19_May_1955).md>).
- **[26](26_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 162a](../L/Letter_162a.md).

## June {#June}

- **[5](5_June.md)** - Harvey Breit writes an article on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in the *New York Times Book Review*. Breit tells of "Dr. [Tolkein](../T/Tolkein.md)", and the article contains many more errors. Tolkien sends an angry letter to him.
- **[7](7_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 163](../L/Letter_163.md).
- **[13](13_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Deirdre Levinson](<../L/Letter_to_Deirdre_Levinson_(13_June_1955).md>).
- **[29](29_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 164](../L/Letter_164.md).
- **[29](29_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes the Preface to *[The Ancrene Riwle](../T/The_Ancrene_Riwle.md)*.
- **[30](30_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 165](../L/Letter_165.md).

## July {#July}

- **[5](5_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 165a](../L/Letter_165a.md).
- **[15](15_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 165b](../L/Letter_165b.md).
- **[22](22_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 166](../L/Letter_166.md).
- **[28](28_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mrs Dixey](../L/Letter_to_Mrs_Dixey.md).
- **[28](28_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Jennifer Brookes-Smith](<../L/Letter_to_Jennifer_Brookes-Smith_(28_July_1955).md>).
- **[30](30_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and his daughter [Priscilla](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md) visit Italy, and he writes several entries in [his diary](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_diaries.md) concerning the trip.

## August {#August}

- **[12](12_August.md)** - During the holiday trip to Italy, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and his daughter [Priscilla](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md) attend High Mass at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_di_Santa_Chiara" title="wikipedia:Basilica di Santa Chiara">Basilica di Santa Chiara in Assisi</a>.
- **[15](15_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 167](../L/Letter_167.md).
- **[22](22_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to John Roberts](../L/Letter_to_John_Roberts.md).

## September {#September}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes the draft of [letter 171](../L/Letter_171.md).
- **[7](7_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 168](../L/Letter_168.md).
- **[8](8_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 168a](../L/Letter_168a.md).
- **[11](11_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 169](../L/Letter_169.md).
- **[18](18_September.md)** - [Bob Papenbrook](../B/Bob_Papenbrook.md), who would voice [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) in the video game [The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_of_the_Ring.md), is born.
- **[30](30_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 170](../L/Letter_170.md).

## October {#October}

- **[12](12_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 172](../L/Letter_172.md).
- **[20](20_October.md)** - **[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md), the third and final volume of [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md), is published**.
- **[21](21_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) presents his paper [English and Welsh](../E/English_and_Welsh.md).
- **[24](24_October.md)** - Tolkien writes [letter 173](../L/Letter_173.md).
- **[30](30_October.md)** - Radio broadcast about Tolkien's works. Tolkien misses it because he is on a visit in [Cambridge](../C/Cambridge.md), in a house that has no radio.

## November {#November}

- **[10](10_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 174](../L/Letter_174.md).
- **[25](25_November.md)** - [Bruce Hopkins](../B/Bruce_Hopkins.md), who would play [Gamling](../G/Gamling.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.
- **[30](30_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 175](../L/Letter_175.md).
- **?** - The first episode of the [first radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>) *is aired on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).*
- **?** - *[The Ancrene Riwle](../T/The_Ancrene_Riwle.md)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[2](2_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Elizabeth Jennings](../L/Letter_to_Elizabeth_Jennings.md).
- **[3](3_December.md)** - Tolkien's poem *[Imram](../I/Imram.md)* is published in *[Time and Tide](<../T/Time_and_Tide_(3_December_1955).md>)*.
- **[8](8_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes letters [176](../L/Letter_176.md) and [177](../L/Letter_177.md).
- **[12](12_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 178](../L/Letter_178.md).
- **[12](12_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to David Masson](<../L/Letter_to_David_Masson_(12_December_1955).md>).
- **[14](14_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 179](../L/Letter_179.md).
- **[16](16_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mr Capan](../L/Letter_to_Mr_Capan.md).
- **[20](20_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 179a](../L/Letter_179a.md).
- **[20](20_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 179b](../L/Letter_179b.md).
- **[21](21_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mrs Rachael Feichtmeir](../L/Letter_to_Mrs_Rachael_Feichtmeir.md).
