[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1916" title="wikipedia:1916">1916</a></b>.

## Early {#Early}

- **?** - Being a linguist, Tolkien decides to specialise in signalling, and learns Morse code.

## March {#March}

- **[2](2_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 4](../L/Letter_4.md).
- **[16](16_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) receives his degree at the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md).
- **[22](22_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) marries [Edith Bratt](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md).

## June {#June}

- **[2](2_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) receives orders to report in Folkestone, and prepare for embarking.
- **[4](4_June.md)** - Tolkien leaves for France.
- **[27](27_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) leaves <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89taples" title="wikipedia:Étaples">Étaples</a>, and goes to the [Somme front](../B/Battle_of_the_Somme.md).
- **?** - Tolkien might have written the poem *[Habbanan beneath the Stars](../E/Eruman_beneath_the_Stars.md)*.

## July {#July}

- **[1](1_July.md)** - One of the bloodiest battles in recent history, the [Battle of the Somme](../B/Battle_of_the_Somme.md), starts. [Robert Gilson](../R/Robert_Gilson.md) is hit by a shell at La Boiselle on the first day; one of the first of the 360,000 British casualties of that battle.
- **[14](14_July.md)** - Tolkien's C-company is sent into action at the Somme front.
- **[22](22_July.md)** - [Ralph "the Baby" Payton](../R/Ralph_Payton.md), one of the [T.C.B.S.](../T/T.C.B.S..md), is killed [in action](../W/World_War_I.md).

## August {#August}

- **[12](12_August.md)** - Tolkien writes [Letter 5](../L/Letter_5.md).
- **[19](19_August.md)** - Tolkien and [G.B. Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md) meet at Acheux.

## September {#September}

- **[13](13_September.md)-[24](24_September.md)** - Revises the poem <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Mermaid%27s_Flute_from_%27The_Lay_of_Earendel%27&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Mermaid&#39;s Flute from &#39;The Lay of Earendel&#39; (page does not exist)">The Mermaid&#39;s Flute</a> and composes [Consolatrix Afflictorium](../S/Stella_Vespertina.md), and [Stella Vespertina](../S/Stella_Vespertina.md)
- **[19](19_September.md)** - [Terence Tiller](../T/Terence_Tiller.md), writer of the [1955 radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>)*, is born.*

## October {#October}

- **[26](26_October.md)** - While in reserve, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s battalion is inspected by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Haig" title="wikipedia:Douglas Haig">Sir Doulgas Haig</a>, the British commander at the [Battle of the Somme](../B/Battle_of_the_Somme.md).
- **[27](27_October.md)** - Tolkien is struck by "pyrexia of unknown origin": <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/trench_fever" title="wikipedia:trench fever">trench fever</a>.
- **[31](31_October.md)** - Canon [Norman Power](../N/Norman_Power.md), an acquaintance of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is born.

## November {#November}

- **[8](8_November.md)** - Tolkien is repatriated, and is sent to a hospital in [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md).
- **[22](22_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) signs [a form for the War Office](../W/War_Office_form_22_November_1916.md), confirming his return to England from France due to trench fever.
- **[29](29_November.md)** - [G.B. Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md) is hit by shrapnel, and wounded on his right arm and thigh.

## December {#December}

- **[3](3_December.md)** - G.B. Smith dies of gas-gangrene in his wounds. The news doesn't reach Tolkien until the 16th.
- **[16](16_December.md)-[25](25_December.md)** - Tolkien recovers, and he and Edith visit Great Haywood.
- **[28](28_December.md)** - [Noel Johnson](../N/Noel_Johnson.md), who would play [Éomer](Éomer.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1955 radio series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>), is born.
