[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1916" title="wikipedia:1916">1916</a></b>.

## Early {#Early}

- **?** - Being a linguist, Tolkien decides to specialise in signalling, and learns Morse code.

## March {#March}

- **[2](/wiki/2_March "2 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 4](/wiki/Letter_4).
- **[16](/wiki/16_March "16 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) receives his degree at the [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford).
- **[22](/wiki/22_March "22 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) marries [Edith Bratt](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien").

## June {#June}

- **[2](/wiki/2_June "2 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) receives orders to report in Folkestone, and prepare for embarking.
- **[4](/wiki/4_June "4 June")** - Tolkien leaves for France.
- **[27](/wiki/27_June "27 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) leaves <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89taples" title="wikipedia:Étaples">Étaples</a>, and goes to the [Somme front](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme "Battle of the Somme").
- **?** - Tolkien might have written the poem *[Habbanan beneath the Stars](/wiki/Eruman_beneath_the_Stars "Eruman beneath the Stars")*.

## July {#July}

- **[1](/wiki/1_July "1 July")** - One of the bloodiest battles in recent history, the [Battle of the Somme](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme), starts. [Robert Gilson](/wiki/Robert_Gilson) is hit by a shell at La Boiselle on the first day; one of the first of the 360,000 British casualties of that battle.
- **[14](/wiki/14_July "14 July")** - Tolkien's C-company is sent into action at the Somme front.
- **[22](/wiki/22_July "22 July")** - [Ralph "the Baby" Payton](/wiki/Ralph_Payton "Ralph Payton"), one of the [T.C.B.S.](/wiki/T.C.B.S.), is killed [in action](/wiki/World_War_I "World War I").

## August {#August}

- **[12](/wiki/12_August "12 August")** - Tolkien writes [Letter 5](/wiki/Letter_5).
- **[19](/wiki/19_August "19 August")** - Tolkien and [G.B. Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith "Geoffrey Bache Smith") meet at Acheux.

## September {#September}

- **[13](/wiki/13_September "13 September")-[24](/wiki/24_September "24 September")** - Revises the poem <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Mermaid%27s_Flute_from_%27The_Lay_of_Earendel%27&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Mermaid&#39;s Flute from &#39;The Lay of Earendel&#39; (page does not exist)">The Mermaid&#39;s Flute</a> and composes [Consolatrix Afflictorium](/wiki/Stella_Vespertina "Stella Vespertina"), and [Stella Vespertina](/wiki/Stella_Vespertina)
- **[19](/wiki/19_September "19 September")** - [Terence Tiller](/wiki/Terence_Tiller), writer of the [1955 radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)")*, is born.*

## October {#October}

- **[26](/wiki/26_October "26 October")** - While in reserve, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s battalion is inspected by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Haig" title="wikipedia:Douglas Haig">Sir Doulgas Haig</a>, the British commander at the [Battle of the Somme](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme).
- **[27](/wiki/27_October "27 October")** - Tolkien is struck by "pyrexia of unknown origin": <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/trench_fever" title="wikipedia:trench fever">trench fever</a>.
- **[31](/wiki/31_October "31 October")** - Canon [Norman Power](/wiki/Norman_Power), an acquaintance of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is born.

## November {#November}

- **[8](/wiki/8_November "8 November")** - Tolkien is repatriated, and is sent to a hospital in [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham).
- **[22](/wiki/22_November "22 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) signs [a form for the War Office](/wiki/War_Office_form_22_November_1916 "War Office form 22 November 1916"), confirming his return to England from France due to trench fever.
- **[29](/wiki/29_November "29 November")** - [G.B. Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith "Geoffrey Bache Smith") is hit by shrapnel, and wounded on his right arm and thigh.

## December {#December}

- **[3](/wiki/3_December "3 December")** - G.B. Smith dies of gas-gangrene in his wounds. The news doesn't reach Tolkien until the 16th.
- **[16](/wiki/16_December "16 December")-[25](/wiki/25_December "25 December")** - Tolkien recovers, and he and Edith visit Great Haywood.
- **[28](/wiki/28_December "28 December")** - [Noel Johnson](/wiki/Noel_Johnson), who would play [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1955 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)"), is born.
