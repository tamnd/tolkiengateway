[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951" title="wikipedia:1951">1951</a></b>.

## April {#April}

- **[2](2_April.md)** - Marriage of [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) and [Faith Faulconbridge](../F/Faith_Faulconbridge.md).

## May {#May}

- **[12](12_May.md)** - Tolkien completes the tale of Beren and Lúthien in a new version of the *[Quenta Silmarillion](<../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road).md>)*.
- **[21](21_May.md)** - Artist [Angelo Montanini](../A/Angelo_Montanini.md) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[10](10_September.md)**-**[13](13_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) attends the Congrès Internationale de Philologie Moderne at the University of Liège, where he delivers the paper "[Middle English 'Losenger'](../M/Middle_English_'Losenger'.md)".
- **[13](13_September.md)** - [Bruce Phillips](../B/Bruce_Phillips.md), who would play [Grimbold](../G/Grimbold.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*, is born.

## October {#October}

- Tolkien's arm suffers "a terrible bout" of fibrositis and neuritis.<sup id="cite_ref-l133_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l133-1">[1]</a></sup>

## November {#November}

- **[19](19_November.md)** - [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) writes to Tolkien expressing his interest in seeing the *Silmarillion*.<sup id="cite_ref-l133_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l133-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[25](25_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Iona and Peter Opie](../L/Letter_to_Iona_and_Peter_Opie.md).

## Late {#Late}

- **?** (probably fall) - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes the [famous letter](../L/Letter_to_Milton_Waldman.md) to [Milton Waldman](../M/Milton_Waldman.md) ([Letter 131](../L/Letter_131.md)) explaining what his [Legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) is about, hoping to get *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* published. His attempt is unsuccessful.
- **?** - Tolkien is working on several things, such as *[Of Tuor and his Coming to Gondolin](../O/Of_Tuor_and_his_Coming_to_Gondolin.md)*, the [Grey Annals](../G/Grey_Annals.md) and the [Annals of Aman](../A/Annals_of_Aman.md).

## Unknown {#Unknown}

- **?** - Second edition of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
