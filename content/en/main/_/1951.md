[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1951" title="wikipedia:1951">1951</a></b>.

## April {#April}

- **[2](/wiki/2_April "2 April")** - Marriage of [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) and [Faith Faulconbridge](/wiki/Faith_Faulconbridge).

## May {#May}

- **[12](/wiki/12_May "12 May")** - Tolkien completes the tale of Beren and Lúthien in a new version of the *[Quenta Silmarillion](</wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road)> "Quenta Silmarillion (Lost Road)")*.
- **[21](/wiki/21_May "21 May")** - Artist [Angelo Montanini](/wiki/Angelo_Montanini) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[10](/wiki/10_September "10 September")**-**[13](/wiki/13_September "13 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) attends the Congrès Internationale de Philologie Moderne at the University of Liège, where he delivers the paper "[Middle English 'Losenger'](/wiki/Middle_English_%27Losenger%27)".
- **[13](/wiki/13_September "13 September")** - [Bruce Phillips](/wiki/Bruce_Phillips), who would play [Grimbold](/wiki/Grimbold) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*, is born.

## October {#October}

- Tolkien's arm suffers "a terrible bout" of fibrositis and neuritis.<sup id="cite_ref-l133_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l133-1">[1]</a></sup>

## November {#November}

- **[19](/wiki/19_November "19 November")** - [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) writes to Tolkien expressing his interest in seeing the *Silmarillion*.<sup id="cite_ref-l133_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l133-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[25](/wiki/25_November "25 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Iona and Peter Opie](/wiki/Letter_to_Iona_and_Peter_Opie "Letter to Iona and Peter Opie").

## Late {#Late}

- **?** (probably fall) - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the [famous letter](/wiki/Letter_to_Milton_Waldman "Letter to Milton Waldman") to [Milton Waldman](/wiki/Milton_Waldman) ([Letter 131](/wiki/Letter_131)) explaining what his [Legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium) is about, hoping to get *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* published. His attempt is unsuccessful.
- **?** - Tolkien is working on several things, such as *[Of Tuor and his Coming to Gondolin](/wiki/Of_Tuor_and_his_Coming_to_Gondolin)*, the [Grey Annals](/wiki/Grey_Annals) and the [Annals of Aman](/wiki/Annals_of_Aman).

## Unknown {#Unknown}

- **?** - Second edition of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
