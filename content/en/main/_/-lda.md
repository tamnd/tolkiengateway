<i><b>-lda</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) suffix that has five meanings.

## -lda<sup>1</sup> {#-lda1}

Used as a plural possessive suffix.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>examples needed</i></a>]</sup>

## -lda<sup>2</sup> {#-lda2}

Used as a plural possessive suffix. Tolkien hesitated on the origin of *-lda*, first considering it as "a comp\[arative\] suffix" but then noting "Or could it be an idiom to add pronoun to adjective = thou beautiful. -lda, thy, suffix of 2nd person singular."<sup id="cite_ref-p55_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p55-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>examples needed</i></a>]</sup>

## -lda<sup>3</sup> {#-lda3}

Used as a plural possessive suffix meaning your (polite).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> (e.g. **meletyalda** "your majesty")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## -lda<sup>4</sup> {#-lda4}

Used as a plural/dual possessive suffix. Probably an imperious or familiar form.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>examples needed</i></a>]</sup>

## -lda<sup>5</sup> {#-lda5}

Used as a comp\[arative\] suffix.<sup id="cite_ref-p55_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p55-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>examples needed</i></a>]</sup> <span></span>
