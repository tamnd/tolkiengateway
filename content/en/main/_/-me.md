<i><b>-më</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) suffix which usually denotes abstract, or at least rather intangible, things.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="../I/Isilmë.md#Etymology" title="Isilmë">Isil<b>më</b></a></i> = "moonlight"<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../M/Melmë.md" title="Melmë">mel<b>më</b></a></i> = "love" ("to love"; [MEL](../M/MEL.md))<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
- <i><a href="../N/Nilmë.md" title="Nilmë">nil<b>më</b></a></i> = "friendship" ("friend"; <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=NIL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NIL (page does not exist)">NIL</a>)<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
- <i>tel<b>më</b></i> = "cover" ("hood"; <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=TEL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TEL (page does not exist)">TEL</a>)<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
- <i>qual<b>më</b></i> = "agony, death" ("to die in pain"; <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=KWAL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KWAL (page does not exist)">KWAL</a>)<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
