Events that occurred on 27 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1899](1899.md)** - Actor [Gerik Schjelderup](../G/Gerik_Schjelderup.md) is born.
- **[1907](1907.md)** - Author [L. Sprague de Camp](../L/L._Sprague_de_Camp.md) is born.
- **[1914](1914.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) reads *[The Voyage of Eärendel the Evening Star](../T/The_Voyage_of_Eärendel_the_Evening_Star.md)* at a poorly attended meeting of [Exeter College](../O/Oxford_University.md)'s Essay Club.
- **[1914](1914.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 2](../L/Letter_2.md).
- **[1954](1954.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 157](../L/Letter_157.md).
- **[1977](1977.md)** - The [1977 animated version](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is broadcast in the United States.
- **[1993](1993.md)** - Magyar Tolkien Tarsasag, the Hungarian [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md), is founded.
- **[1996](1996.md)** - Actress [Alexandra Astin](../A/Alexandra_Astin.md) is born.
- **[2001](2001.md)** - *[Music Inspired by Middle Earth](../M/Music_Inspired_by_Middle_Earth.md)*, a musical album featuring many artists, including [David Arkenstone](../D/David_Arkenstone.md), is released.
- **[2006](2006.md)** - Actor [Anthony Jackson](../A/Anthony_Jackson.md) dies.
- **[2012](2012.md)** - *[The Hobbits: The Many Lives of Bilbo, Frodo, Sam, Merry and Pippin](../T/The_Hobbits.md)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) rest in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).
