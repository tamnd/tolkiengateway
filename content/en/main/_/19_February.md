- **[1952](1952.md)** - Actor [Geoff Morrell](../G/Geoff_Morrell.md) is born.
- **[1990](1990.md)** - Actor [Luke Pasqualino](../L/Luke_Pasqualino.md) is born.
- **[2006](2006.md)** - Actor [Wi Kuki Kaa](../W/Wi_Kuki_Kaa.md) dies.

    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) travels south down the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md). [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) spots [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) on his log.
