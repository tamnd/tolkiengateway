<i><b>ósanwe</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "interchange of thought".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Ósanwe* is an inherent ability that all [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) and [Incarnates](/wiki/Incarnates) possess, however the power is lessened by having a body or *[hröa](/wiki/Hr%C3%B6a "Hröa")*, so it is generally weaker for [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and especially for [Men](/wiki/Men). All minds are "equal in status, though they differ in capacity and strength." Language can also become an impediment to the development of *ósanwe*, since it is easier for Incarnates to use which can lead to neglect.

A mind can only be communicated to with *ósanwe* if it is open (*sanwe-latya*, "thought opening, telepathy"). Any mind can be willed closed, and this is a barrier that cannot be overcome by anything or anyone, not even by [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor) applying all his power and will.

The ability for communication can be strengthened through various means: from affinity between the persons communicating, urgency on the part of the person sending the thought, or their authority lending effectiveness, allowing thought to pass through bodies more easily.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>When the <a href="/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="Fellowship of the Ring">Fellowship of the Ring</a> arrives in <a href="/wiki/Caras_Galadhon" title="Caras Galadhon">Caras Galadhon</a>, <a href="/wiki/Galadriel" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a> telepathically talks to them about the fate of <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> and specifically greets <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Frodo" title="Frodo">Frodo</a>. She talks to the latter again in the same way about the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/One_Ring" title="One Ring">One Ring</a> after he looked into the <a href="/wiki/Mirror_of_Galadriel" title="Mirror of Galadriel">Mirror of Galadriel</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Before the beginning of the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg" title="Battle of the Hornburg">Battle of the Hornburg</a>, Galadriel and <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a> have a long-distance communication about the danger awaiting <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> and the others there, and send a troop of Elves to fight with them.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>When <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Pippin" title="Pippin">Pippin</a> looks into the palantír of Isengard, he is able to close off his mind to Sauron and prevent any information about the One Ring from reaching him.</dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>During the meeting of the <a href="/wiki/White_Council" title="White Council">White Council</a> at <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a> Galadriel greets <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> telepathically, and they continue to communicate while <a href="/wiki/Saruman" title="Saruman">Saruman</a> is talking.</dd></dl>
