[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992" title="wikipedia:1992">1992</a></b>. Because this year marked the 100th birthday of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), it is often referred to as the **Centenary year** or **Centennial Year**.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - Hundredth birthday of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - On this special day, the [Finnish Tolkien Society](/wiki/Finnish_Tolkien_Society) is founded.
- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - The premiere of the first episode of German radio serial adaptation *[Der Herr der Ringe](</wiki/Der_Herr_der_Ringe_(1992_German_radio_series)> "Der Herr der Ringe (1992 German radio series)")*, a co-production of public radio broadcasters SWR (Südwestrundfunk) and WDR (Westdeutscher Rundfunk), on Tolkien's 100th birthday.
- <b><a href="/wiki/6_January" title="6 January">6</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/Sauron_Defeated" title="Sauron Defeated">Sauron Defeated</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **[6](/wiki/6_January "6 January")** - *[The Tolkien Family Album](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Family_Album)* is published.
- **[11](/wiki/11_January "11 January")-[12](/wiki/12_January "12 January")** - On the occasion of the centenary, an International Tolkien Symposium is held in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aachen" title="wikipedia:Aachen">Aachen, Germany</a>.
- **?** - [*Vinyar Tengwar* 21](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_21 "Vinyar Tengwar 21") is published.
- **?** - [Beyond Bree J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar 1992](/wiki/Beyond_Bree_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar_1992) is released.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 113](/wiki/Amon_Hen_113 "Amon Hen 113") is published.

## February {#February}

- **[10](/wiki/10_February "10 February")** - Actor [Charlie Vickers](/wiki/Charlie_Vickers) is born.

## March {#March}

- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 22](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_22) is published.

## April {#April}

- **[29](/wiki/29_April "29 April")** - [Stephen Oliver](/wiki/Stephen_Oliver) dies.
- **?** - Conclusion of the German radio serial adaptation *[Der Herr der Ringe](</wiki/Der_Herr_der_Ringe_(1992_German_radio_series)> "Der Herr der Ringe (1992 German radio series)")*.

## May {#May}

- **[22](/wiki/22_May "22 May")-[24](/wiki/24_May "24 May")** - [The Tolkien Phenomenon](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Phenomenon) is held.
- **?** - [*Vinyar Tengwar* 23](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_23 "Vinyar Tengwar 23") is published.

## July {#July}

- **?** - [*Vinyar Tengwar* 24](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_24 "Vinyar Tengwar 24") is published.

## August {#August}

- **[16](/wiki/16_August "16 August")** - The first part of the [radio adaptation of *Farmer Giles of Ham*](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)") is aired on [BBC 5](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[16](/wiki/16_August "16 August")-[24](/wiki/24_August "24 August")** - [Tolkien Centenary Conference](/wiki/Tolkien_Centenary_Conference), a combination of [Oxonmoot](/wiki/Oxonmoot) and [Mythcon](/wiki/Mythcon).
- **[23](/wiki/23_August "23 August")** - The second part of the [radio adaptation of *Farmer Giles of Ham*](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)") is aired on [BBC5](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[30](/wiki/30_August "30 August")** - The [radio adaptation of *Smith of Wootton Major*](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)") is aired on [BBC 5](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **?** - *[The Making of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Making_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* by [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) is published.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 29](/wiki/Mallorn_29 "Mallorn 29") is published.

## September {#September}

- **[6](/wiki/6_September "6 September")** - The first part of the [radio adaptation of *The Adventures of Tom Bombadil*](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)") is aired on [BBC5](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[13](/wiki/13_September "13 September")** - The second part of the [radio adaptation of *The Adventures of Tom Bombadil*](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)") is aired on [BBC5](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[20](/wiki/20_September "20 September")** - The [radio adaptation of *Leaf by Niggle*](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)") is aired on [BBC5](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")** - The Danish [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies") [Tolkiens Følge](/wiki/Tolkiens_F%C3%B8lge) is founded.
- **?** - [*Vinyar Tengwar* 25](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_25 "Vinyar Tengwar 25") is published.

## October {#October}

- **[27](/wiki/27_October "27 October")** - *[Sauron Defeated](/wiki/Sauron_Defeated)* is published in the US.
- **[27](/wiki/27_October "27 October")** - *[Pictures by J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Pictures_by_J.R.R._Tolkien)* (revised edition) is published.

## November {#November}

- [6](/wiki/6_November "6 November") - [Robert Aramayo](/wiki/Robert_Aramayo) is born.
- **?** - [*Vinyar Tengwar* 26](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_26 "Vinyar Tengwar 26") is published.

## Fall {#Fall}

- **?** - *[The Tolkien Collector](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Collector)* is founded.
