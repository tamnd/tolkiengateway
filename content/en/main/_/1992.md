[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1992" title="wikipedia:1992">1992</a></b>. Because this year marked the 100th birthday of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), it is often referred to as the **Centenary year** or **Centennial Year**.

## January {#January}

- **[3](3_January.md)** - Hundredth birthday of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- **[3](3_January.md)** - On this special day, the [Finnish Tolkien Society](../F/Finnish_Tolkien_Society.md) is founded.
- **[3](3_January.md)** - The premiere of the first episode of German radio serial adaptation *[Der Herr der Ringe](<../D/Der_Herr_der_Ringe_(1992_German_radio_series).md>)*, a co-production of public radio broadcasters SWR (Südwestrundfunk) and WDR (Westdeutscher Rundfunk), on Tolkien's 100th birthday.
- <b><a href="6_January.md" title="6 January">6</a> - <i><a href="../S/Sauron_Defeated.md" title="Sauron Defeated">Sauron Defeated</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **[6](6_January.md)** - *[The Tolkien Family Album](../T/The_Tolkien_Family_Album.md)* is published.
- **[11](11_January.md)-[12](12_January.md)** - On the occasion of the centenary, an International Tolkien Symposium is held in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aachen" title="wikipedia:Aachen">Aachen, Germany</a>.
- **?** - [*Vinyar Tengwar* 21](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_21.md) is published.
- **?** - [Beyond Bree J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar 1992](../B/Beyond_Bree_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar_1992.md) is released.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 113](../A/Amon_Hen_113.md) is published.

## February {#February}

- **[10](10_February.md)** - Actor [Charlie Vickers](../C/Charlie_Vickers.md) is born.

- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 22](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_22.md) is published.

- **?** - Conclusion of the German radio serial adaptation *[Der Herr der Ringe](<../D/Der_Herr_der_Ringe_(1992_German_radio_series).md>)*.

- **?** - [*Vinyar Tengwar* 23](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_23.md) is published.

## July {#July}

## August {#August}

- **[16](16_August.md)** - The first part of the [radio adaptation of *Farmer Giles of Ham*](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>) is aired on [BBC 5](../B/BBC.md).
- **[16](16_August.md)-[24](24_August.md)** - [Tolkien Centenary Conference](../T/Tolkien_Centenary_Conference.md), a combination of [Oxonmoot](../O/Oxonmoot.md) and [Mythcon](../M/Mythcon.md).
- **[23](23_August.md)** - The second part of the [radio adaptation of *Farmer Giles of Ham*](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>) is aired on [BBC5](../B/BBC.md).
- **[30](30_August.md)** - The [radio adaptation of *Smith of Wootton Major*](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>) is aired on [BBC 5](../B/BBC.md).
- **?** - *[The Making of The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Making_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* by [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) is published.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 29](../M/Mallorn_29.md) is published.

## September {#September}

- **[6](6_September.md)** - The first part of the [radio adaptation of *The Adventures of Tom Bombadil*](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>) is aired on [BBC5](../B/BBC.md).
- **[13](13_September.md)** - The second part of the [radio adaptation of *The Adventures of Tom Bombadil*](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>) is aired on [BBC5](../B/BBC.md).
- **[20](20_September.md)** - The [radio adaptation of *Leaf by Niggle*](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>) is aired on [BBC5](../B/BBC.md).
- **[22](22_September.md)** - The Danish [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md) [Tolkiens Følge](../T/Tolkiens_Følge.md) is founded.
- **?** - [*Vinyar Tengwar* 25](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_25.md) is published.
