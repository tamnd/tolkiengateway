<i><b>-n</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) suffix that has three meanings.

## -n<sup>1</sup> {#-n1}

Used as a dative ending to indicate the indirect object of a sentence. For example in <i><b>nin</b></i> "for me" <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and '''<i><b>enyalien</b></i> "in memory of".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## -n<sup>2</sup> {#-n2}

**-n** or **-nye** is a pronominal ending, 1st person singular "I". It is used to indicate an action is being completed by the speaker. For example, **-n** is used as a modifier in *Elendil's Oath,* to form <i><b>utúlien</b></i> "I am come" from <i><b>utúlie</b></i> "has come".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## -n<sup>3</sup> {#-n3}

Used as a plural sign used of the cases.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>examples needed</i></a>]</sup>
