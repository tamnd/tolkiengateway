Events that occurred on 25 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1915](1915.md)** - The Big Four of the [T.C.B.S.](../T/T.C.B.S..md) - [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), [Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md), [Wiseman](../C/Christopher_Wiseman.md) and [Gilson](../R/Robert_Gilson.md) - have their last meeting.
- **[1927](1927.md)** - Producer and interviewer [Denys Gueroult](../D/Denys_Gueroult.md) is born.
- **[1931](1931.md)** - Voice actor [Peter Woodthorpe](../P/Peter_Woodthorpe.md) is born.
- **[1954](1954.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 154](../L/Letter_154.md).
- **[1963](1963.md)** - Actor [Mikael Persbrandt](../M/Mikael_Persbrandt.md) is born.
- **[1977](1977.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1977](../O/Oxonmoot_1977.md) at The Welsh Pony.
- **[1977](1977.md)** - William Carter publishes "The filial duty of Christopher Tolkien" in *Sunday Times Magazine*.
- **[1981](1981.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1981](../O/Oxonmoot_1981.md) at The Turf Tavern.
- **[1982](1982.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1982](../O/Oxonmoot_1982.md) at the Cotswold Lodge Hotel.
- **[1982](1982.md)** - The eleventh episode of the new version of the [1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- **[1983](1983.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1983](../O/Oxonmoot_1983.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[1987](1987.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1987](../O/Oxonmoot_1987.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[1988](1988.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1988](../O/Oxonmoot_1988.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[1998](1998.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1998](../O/Oxonmoot_1998.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Exeter College, Oxford">Exeter College</a>.
- **[1999](1999.md)** - Author [Marion Zimmer Bradley](../M/Marion_Zimmer_Bradley.md) dies.
- **[1999](1999.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1999](../O/Oxonmoot_1999.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Exeter College, Oxford">Exeter College</a>.
- **[2008](2008.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2008](../O/Oxonmoot_2008.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_Church,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Christ Church, Oxford">Christ Church</a>.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2009](../O/Oxonmoot_2009.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Margaret_Hall,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Lady Margaret Hall, Oxford">Lady Margaret Hall</a>.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2010](../O/Oxonmoot_2010.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Margaret_Hall,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Lady Margaret Hall, Oxford">Lady Margaret Hall</a>.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - Lasse Lanta Autumn Meeting of the Austrian Tolkien Society.
- **[2011](2011.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2011](../O/Oxonmoot_2011.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Margaret_Hall,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Lady Margaret Hall, Oxford">Lady Margaret Hall</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - After the departure of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md), [Khamûl](../K/Khamûl.md) resumes his search for the Ring.<sup id="cite_ref-RC164_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC164-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - The hobbits have breakfast with bread, fruit and drinks the [High Elves](../H/High_Elves.md) left for them. Frodo decides to continue to [Bucklebury](../B/Bucklebury.md) by cutting through [Green Hill Country](../G/Green_Hill_Country.md) and [the Marish](../T/The_Marish.md).<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - They are blocked by the [Stock-brook](../S/Stock-brook.md), until they see a [Black Rider](../B/Black_Riders.md) for a third time, behind them; they are forced to dive into the bushes by the bank and continue their way along it.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - They wade the stream and run into a wood; rain falls over the Marish. At mid-day they have a lunch beside a sheltered [elm](../E/Elms.md).<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - Unable to find Frodo, Khamûl summons another [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) from [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-RC164_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC164-1">[1]</a></sup> The two evil cries disturb the hobbits who are resting.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - The two Ringwraiths go eastwards. Khamûl<sup id="cite_ref-RC164_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC164-1">[1]</a></sup> interrogates [Farmer Maggot](../F/Farmer_Maggot.md) about "Baggins", but he doesn't cooperate.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup> Khamûl sends his companion towards the [Overbourn Marshes](../O/Overbourn_Marshes.md) while himself goes towards the [Brandywine Bridge](../B/Brandywine_Bridge.md).<sup id="cite_ref-RC164_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC164-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - Leaving the wood, the hobbits see that they have turned too much to the south; adjusting their course to the [Bucklebury Ferry](../B/Bucklebury_Ferry.md), they reach a turnip field outside Maggot's [estates](../B/Bamfurlong.md) in the afternoon.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - After a dinner, Maggot takes the hobbits with his waggon to the [Ferry](../B/Bucklebury_Ferry.md). They meet [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) looking for them.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup> Khamûl also arrives at the [Bucklebury Ferry](../B/Bucklebury_Ferry.md) but too late.<sup id="cite_ref-RC164_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC164-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - They reach [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md) in the night. Frodo reveals them his plans but they are already known. In the night he dreams about the trees of the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) hurries towards [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) on [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
