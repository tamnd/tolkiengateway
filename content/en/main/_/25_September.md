## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - After the departure of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), [Khamûl](/wiki/Kham%C3%BBl) resumes his search for the Ring.<sup id="cite_ref-RC164_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC164-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - The hobbits have breakfast with bread, fruit and drinks the [High Elves](/wiki/High_Elves) left for them. Frodo decides to continue to [Bucklebury](/wiki/Bucklebury) by cutting through [Green Hill Country](/wiki/Green_Hill_Country) and [the Marish](/wiki/The_Marish "The Marish").<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - They are blocked by the [Stock-brook](/wiki/Stock-brook), until they see a [Black Rider](/wiki/Black_Riders "Black Riders") for a third time, behind them; they are forced to dive into the bushes by the bank and continue their way along it.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - They wade the stream and run into a wood; rain falls over the Marish. At mid-day they have a lunch beside a sheltered [elm](/wiki/Elms "Elms").<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - Unable to find Frodo, Khamûl summons another [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) from [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur).<sup id="cite_ref-RC164_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC164-1">[1]</a></sup> The two evil cries disturb the hobbits who are resting.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - The two Ringwraiths go eastwards. Khamûl<sup id="cite_ref-RC164_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC164-1">[1]</a></sup> interrogates [Farmer Maggot](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot) about "Baggins", but he doesn't cooperate.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup> Khamûl sends his companion towards the [Overbourn Marshes](/wiki/Overbourn_Marshes) while himself goes towards the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge).<sup id="cite_ref-RC164_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC164-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - Leaving the wood, the hobbits see that they have turned too much to the south; adjusting their course to the [Bucklebury Ferry](/wiki/Bucklebury_Ferry), they reach a turnip field outside Maggot's [estates](/wiki/Bamfurlong "Bamfurlong") in the afternoon.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - After a dinner, Maggot takes the hobbits with his waggon to the [Ferry](/wiki/Bucklebury_Ferry "Bucklebury Ferry"). They meet [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") looking for them.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[2]</a></sup> Khamûl also arrives at the [Bucklebury Ferry](/wiki/Bucklebury_Ferry) but too late.<sup id="cite_ref-RC164_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC164-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - They reach [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow) in the night. Frodo reveals them his plans but they are already known. In the night he dreams about the trees of the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) hurries towards [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire) on [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
