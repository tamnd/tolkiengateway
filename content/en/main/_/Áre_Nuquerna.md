<i><b>Áre Nuquerna</b></i> is the name in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) of the thirty-second letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In form it is the thirty-first letter ([Áre](/wiki/%C3%81re)) inverted. It is mostly used as a variation, especially in modes employing superimposed *[tehtar](/wiki/Tehtar "Tehtar")*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and it represents **Z**. The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Oza</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
