[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977" title="wikipedia:1977">1977</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[13](13_January.md)** - Actor [Orlando Bloom](../O/Orlando_Bloom.md) is born.
- **[17](17_January.md)** - [Adam Fox](../A/Adam_Fox.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), dies.

## February {#February}

- **[14](14_February.md)** - *[The Mythology of Middle-earth](../T/The_Mythology_of_Middle-earth.md)*, [Ruth S. Noel](../R/Ruth_S._Noel.md)'s first book on Tolkien's works, is published.

## April {#April}

- **[1](1_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children found [The Tolkien Trust](../T/The_Tolkien_Trust.md) charity.

## May {#May}

- <b><a href="5_May.md" title="5 May">5</a> - <i><a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_A_Biography.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography">J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography</a></i> is first published in the UK.</b>
- **[16](16_May.md)** - Singer [Emiliana Torrini](../E/Emiliana_Torrini.md) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[11](11_June.md)** - The first [Wisconsin Ring Game](../W/Wisconsin_Ring_Game.md) is held at <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_Dodge_State_Park" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Governor Dodge State Park</a> in southern Wisconsin.
- **[29](29_June.md)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_A_Biography.md)* is published in the US.

## July {#July}

- **[1](1_July.md)** - Actress [Liv Tyler](../L/Liv_Tyler.md) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[26](26_August.md)-[29](29_August.md)** - [Mythcon 8](../M/Mythcon_8.md).

## September {#September}

- **[1](1_September.md)** - *[Cliffs Notes on Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit](../C/Cliffs_Notes_on_Tolkien's_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_and_The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[15](15_September.md)** - **[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md) is published by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md)**.
- **[23](23_September.md)-[25](25_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1977](../O/Oxonmoot_1977.md).

## November {#November}

- **[1](1_November.md)** - [Ted Johnstone](../T/Ted_Johnstone.md) dies.
- **[27](27_November.md)** - [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>) is released on VHS in the United States.

## December {#December}

- **[18](18_December.md)** - Actor [Cyril Ritchard](../C/Cyril_Ritchard.md) dies.
