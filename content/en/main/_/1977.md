[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977" title="wikipedia:1977">1977</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[13](/wiki/13_January "13 January")** - Actor [Orlando Bloom](/wiki/Orlando_Bloom) is born.
- **[17](/wiki/17_January "17 January")** - [Adam Fox](/wiki/Adam_Fox), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), dies.

## February {#February}

- **[14](/wiki/14_February "14 February")** - *[The Mythology of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Mythology_of_Middle-earth)*, [Ruth S. Noel](/wiki/Ruth_S._Noel)'s first book on Tolkien's works, is published.

## April {#April}

- **[1](/wiki/1_April "1 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children found [The Tolkien Trust](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Trust) charity.

## May {#May}

- <b><a href="/wiki/5_May" title="5 May">5</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography" title="J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography">J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography</a></i> is first published in the UK.</b>
- **[16](/wiki/16_May "16 May")** - Singer [Emiliana Torrini](/wiki/Emiliana_Torrini) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[11](/wiki/11_June "11 June")** - The first [Wisconsin Ring Game](/wiki/Wisconsin_Ring_Game) is held at <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_Dodge_State_Park" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Governor Dodge State Park</a> in southern Wisconsin.
- **[29](/wiki/29_June "29 June")** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography)* is published in the US.

## July {#July}

- **[1](/wiki/1_July "1 July")** - Actress [Liv Tyler](/wiki/Liv_Tyler) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[26](/wiki/26_August "26 August")-[29](/wiki/29_August "29 August")** - [Mythcon 8](/wiki/Mythcon_8).

## September {#September}

- **[1](/wiki/1_September "1 September")** - *[Cliffs Notes on Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit](/wiki/Cliffs_Notes_on_Tolkien%27s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_and_The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[15](/wiki/15_September "15 September")** - **[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion) is published by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien)**.
- **[23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")-[25](/wiki/25_September "25 September")** - [Oxonmoot 1977](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1977).

## November {#November}

- **[1](/wiki/1_November "1 November")** - [Ted Johnstone](/wiki/Ted_Johnstone) dies.
- **[27](/wiki/27_November "27 November")** - [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)") is released on VHS in the United States.

## December {#December}

- **[18](/wiki/18_December "18 December")** - Actor [Cyril Ritchard](/wiki/Cyril_Ritchard) dies.
