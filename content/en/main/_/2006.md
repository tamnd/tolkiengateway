[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006" title="wikipedia:2006">2006</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[20](20_January.md)** - The second *[The One Ring Celebration](../T/The_One_Ring_Celebration.md)* is held in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasadena,_California" title="wikipedia:Pasadena, California">Pasadena</a>.
- **[31](31_January.md)** - *[In Elven Lands](../I/In_Elven_Lands.md)* is released.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 197](../A/Amon_Hen_197.md)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[4](4_February.md)** - Previews of [The Lord of the Rings (musical)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical).md>) open in Toronto.
- **[19](19_February.md)** - Death of [Wi Kuki Kaa](../W/Wi_Kuki_Kaa.md), actor originally cast as [Ghân-buri-Ghân](../G/Ghân-buri-Ghân.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 47](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_47.md) is published.

## March {#March}

- **[2](2_March.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md)* is published.
- **[17](17_March.md)** - [Bob Papenbrook](../B/Bob_Papenbrook.md), who voiced [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) in the video game *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_of_the_Ring.md)*, dies.
- **[23](23_March.md)** - [The Lord of the Rings (musical)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical).md>) at Toronto has its press opening.
- **[31](31_March.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings 1954-2004: Scholarship in Honor of Richard E. Blackwelder](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_1954-2004.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 198](../A/Amon_Hen_198.md)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[7](7_April.md)** - The [Tolkien Ensemble](../T/Tolkien_Ensemble.md) publish their collected work, *[Complete Songs and Poems](../C/Complete_Songs_and_Poems.md)*.
- **[10](10_April.md)** - *[Fellowship!](../F/Fellowship!.md)*, an independent musical parody of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, wins the <a class="external text" href="http://www.laweekly.com/index.php?option=com_lawcontent&amp;task=view&amp;id=13103&amp;Itemid=47" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">LA Weekly Theater Award</a> for *Musical of the Year*.

## May {#May}

- **[9](9_May.md)** - *[A Tolkienian Mathomium](../A/A_Tolkienian_Mathomium.md)* is published.
- **[19](19_May.md)** - *[The Ring of Words](../T/The_Ring_of_Words.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 199](../A/Amon_Hen_199.md)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[1](1_June.md)** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 3](../T/Tolkien_Studies%253A_Volume_3.md)* is published.
- **[11](11_June.md)** - Tim Hildebrandt, one of the renowned [brothers Hildebrandt](../B/Brothers_Hildebrandt.md), dies.

## July {#July}

- **[1](1_July.md)-[4](4_July.md)** - [The Gathering of the Fellowship 2006](../T/The_Gathering_of_the_Fellowship_2006.md) takes place in Toronto.
- **[2](2_July.md)** - [Pieter Collier](../P/Pieter_Collier.md) is awarded the *1st Annual Northeast Tolkien Society Imperishable Flame Award 2006* for his immense collection.
- **[13](13_July.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The White Council](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_White_Council.md)* is announced.
- **[14](14_July.md)-[16](16_July.md)** - [Tolkien Thing VIII](../T/Tolkien_Thing.md).
- **[28](28_July.md)-[30](30_July.md)** - ELF 2006.
- **[29](29_July.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: Legions of Middle-earth](../L/LOTRSBG%253A_Legions_of_Middle-earth.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 200](../A/Amon_Hen_200.md)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[4](4_August.md)-[7](7_August.md)** - [Mythcon 37](../M/Mythcon_37.md).
- **[21](21_August.md)-[25](25_August.md)** - The Oxford Tolkien Conference at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Exeter College, Oxford">Exeter College, Oxford</a>.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 44](../M/Mallorn_44.md)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[11](11_September.md)** - Translator [William Auld](../W/William_Auld.md) dies.
- **[15](15_September.md)-[17](17_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2006](../O/Oxonmoot_2006.md).
- **[18](18_September.md)** - *[Children of Húrin](../C/Children_of_Húrin.md)* is announced.
- **[26](26_September.md)** - Fantasy novel *[Here, There Be Dragons](../H/Here,_There_Be_Dragons.md)*, featuring [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) as a fictional character, is published.
- **[29](29_September.md)** - *[Tolkien and Modernity 1](../T/Tolkien_and_Modernity_1.md)* and *[Tolkien and Modernity 2](../T/Tolkien_and_Modernity_2.md)* are published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 201](../A/Amon_Hen_201.md)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[10](10_October.md)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien Encyclopedia: Scholarship and Critical Assessment](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_Encyclopedia%253A_Scholarship_and_Critical_Assessment.md)* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[6](6_November.md)** - *[Peter Jackson: A Film-maker's Journey](../P/Peter_Jackson%253A_A_Film-maker's_Journey.md)* is published.
- **[10](10_November.md)-[12](12_November.md)** - [Ring\*Con 2006](../R/Ring%252ACon_2006.md).
- **[21](21_November.md)** - The [RiffTrax](../R/RiffTrax.md) commentary of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* is released online.
- **[27](27_November.md)** - [Anthony Jackson](../A/Anthony_Jackson.md), the Tale Bearer in the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>), dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 202](../A/Amon_Hen_202.md)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 48](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_48.md) is published.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Audiobooks](../A/Audiobooks.md) of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md)* and *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)*, read by [Rob Inglis](../R/Rob_Inglis.md), are published.
- **?** - Actor [Tyroe Muhafidin](../T/Tyroe_Muhafidin.md) is born.
