[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006" title="wikipedia:2006">2006</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[20](/wiki/20_January "20 January")** - The second *[The One Ring Celebration](/wiki/The_One_Ring_Celebration)* is held in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasadena,_California" title="wikipedia:Pasadena, California">Pasadena</a>.
- **[31](/wiki/31_January "31 January")** - *[In Elven Lands](/wiki/In_Elven_Lands)* is released.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 197](/wiki/Amon_Hen_197)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[4](/wiki/4_February "4 February")** - Previews of [The Lord of the Rings (musical)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical)>) open in Toronto.
- **[19](/wiki/19_February "19 February")** - Death of [Wi Kuki Kaa](/wiki/Wi_Kuki_Kaa), actor originally cast as [Ghân-buri-Ghân](/wiki/Gh%C3%A2n-buri-Gh%C3%A2n) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 47](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_47) is published.

## March {#March}

- **[2](/wiki/2_March "2 March")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II)* is published.
- **[17](/wiki/17_March "17 March")** - [Bob Papenbrook](/wiki/Bob_Papenbrook), who voiced [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) in the video game *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring)*, dies.
- **[23](/wiki/23_March "23 March")** - [The Lord of the Rings (musical)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical)>) at Toronto has its press opening.
- **[31](/wiki/31_March "31 March")** - *[The Lord of the Rings 1954-2004: Scholarship in Honor of Richard E. Blackwelder](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_1954-2004 "The Lord of the Rings 1954-2004")* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 198](/wiki/Amon_Hen_198)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[7](/wiki/7_April "7 April")** - The [Tolkien Ensemble](/wiki/Tolkien_Ensemble) publish their collected work, *[Complete Songs and Poems](/wiki/Complete_Songs_and_Poems)*.
- **[10](/wiki/10_April "10 April")** - *[Fellowship!](/wiki/Fellowship!)*, an independent musical parody of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, wins the <a class="external text" href="http://www.laweekly.com/index.php?option=com_lawcontent&amp;task=view&amp;id=13103&amp;Itemid=47" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">LA Weekly Theater Award</a> for *Musical of the Year*.

## May {#May}

- **[9](/wiki/9_May "9 May")** - *[A Tolkienian Mathomium](/wiki/A_Tolkienian_Mathomium)* is published.
- **[19](/wiki/19_May "19 May")** - *[The Ring of Words](/wiki/The_Ring_of_Words)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 199](/wiki/Amon_Hen_199)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[1](/wiki/1_June "1 June")** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 3](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_3)* is published.
- **[11](/wiki/11_June "11 June")** - Tim Hildebrandt, one of the renowned [brothers Hildebrandt](/wiki/Brothers_Hildebrandt "Brothers Hildebrandt"), dies.

## July {#July}

- **[1](/wiki/1_July "1 July")-[4](/wiki/4_July "4 July")** - [The Gathering of the Fellowship 2006](/wiki/The_Gathering_of_the_Fellowship_2006) takes place in Toronto.
- **[2](/wiki/2_July "2 July")** - [Pieter Collier](/wiki/Pieter_Collier) is awarded the *1st Annual Northeast Tolkien Society Imperishable Flame Award 2006* for his immense collection.
- **[13](/wiki/13_July "13 July")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The White Council](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_White_Council)* is announced.
- **[14](/wiki/14_July "14 July")-[16](/wiki/16_July "16 July")** - [Tolkien Thing VIII](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing "Tolkien Thing").
- **[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")-[30](/wiki/30_July "30 July")** - ELF 2006.
- **[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: Legions of Middle-earth](/wiki/LOTRSBG:_Legions_of_Middle-earth "LOTRSBG: Legions of Middle-earth")* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 200](/wiki/Amon_Hen_200)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[4](/wiki/4_August "4 August")-[7](/wiki/7_August "7 August")** - [Mythcon 37](/wiki/Mythcon_37).
- **[21](/wiki/21_August "21 August")-[25](/wiki/25_August "25 August")** - The Oxford Tolkien Conference at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Exeter College, Oxford">Exeter College, Oxford</a>.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 44](/wiki/Mallorn_44)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[11](/wiki/11_September "11 September")** - Translator [William Auld](/wiki/William_Auld) dies.
- **[15](/wiki/15_September "15 September")-[17](/wiki/17_September "17 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2006](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2006).
- **[18](/wiki/18_September "18 September")** - *[Children of Húrin](/wiki/Children_of_H%C3%BArin)* is announced.
- **[26](/wiki/26_September "26 September")** - Fantasy novel *[Here, There Be Dragons](/wiki/Here,_There_Be_Dragons)*, featuring [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) as a fictional character, is published.
- **[29](/wiki/29_September "29 September")** - *[Tolkien and Modernity 1](/wiki/Tolkien_and_Modernity_1)* and *[Tolkien and Modernity 2](/wiki/Tolkien_and_Modernity_2)* are published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 201](/wiki/Amon_Hen_201)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[10](/wiki/10_October "10 October")** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien Encyclopedia: Scholarship and Critical Assessment](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_Encyclopedia:_Scholarship_and_Critical_Assessment)* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[6](/wiki/6_November "6 November")** - *[Peter Jackson: A Film-maker's Journey](/wiki/Peter_Jackson:_A_Film-maker%27s_Journey)* is published.
- **[10](/wiki/10_November "10 November")-[12](/wiki/12_November "12 November")** - [Ring\*Con 2006](/wiki/Ring*Con_2006).
- **[21](/wiki/21_November "21 November")** - The [RiffTrax](/wiki/RiffTrax) commentary of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* is released online.
- **[27](/wiki/27_November "27 November")** - [Anthony Jackson](/wiki/Anthony_Jackson), the Tale Bearer in the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)"), dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 202](/wiki/Amon_Hen_202)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 48](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_48) is published.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Audiobooks](/wiki/Audiobooks) of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)* and *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*, read by [Rob Inglis](/wiki/Rob_Inglis), are published.
- **?** - Actor [Tyroe Muhafidin](/wiki/Tyroe_Muhafidin) is born.
