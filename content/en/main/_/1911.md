[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1911" title="wikipedia:1911">1911</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the "Debating Society Report" in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School_Chronicle)*.
- **[28](/wiki/28_February "28 February")** - [J.A.W. Bennett](/wiki/J.A.W._Bennett), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), born in [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand).

## March {#March}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s first poem, *[The Battle of the Eastern Field](/wiki/The_Battle_of_the_Eastern_Field)*, and the report "[Acta Senatus](/wiki/Acta_Senatus)", are published in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School_Chronicle)*.

## June {#June}

- **[3](/wiki/3_June "3 June")** - Swedish translator [Åke Ohlmarks](/wiki/%C3%85ke_Ohlmarks) is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the "Debating Society Report" and the Editorial in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School_Chronicle)*.

## July {#July}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the Editorial in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School_Chronicle)*.

## Summer {#Summer}

- **?** - The Tolkiens go to holiday in [Switzerland](/wiki/Switzerland), where J.R.R. (alledgedly) finds a postcard of *[Der Berggeist](/wiki/Der_Berggeist)*.
- Tolkien with a party of 12 wander on foot in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauterbrunnen" title="wikipedia:Lauterbrunnen">Lauterbrunnen</a>. Vivid memories of that adventure would form the basis for [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## August {#August}

- **[12](/wiki/12_August "12 August")** - [Felix Felton](/wiki/Felix_Felton), who would play [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), [the Black Captain](/wiki/Witch-king "Witch-king") and [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in [The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)>), is born.

## October {#October}

- **[3](/wiki/3_October "3 October")** - [Michael Hordern](/wiki/Michael_Hordern), who would voice [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in the [1981](/wiki/1981) [radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born.
- **[20](/wiki/20_October "20 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) signs a [Oxford University visitor's page](/wiki/Oxford_University_visitor%27s_page).

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) creates the *[Tea Club and Barrovian Society](/wiki/T.C.B.S. "T.C.B.S.")* at grammar school.
