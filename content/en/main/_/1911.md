[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1911" title="wikipedia:1911">1911</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes the "Debating Society Report" in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](../K/King_Edward's_School_Chronicle.md)*.
- **[28](28_February.md)** - [J.A.W. Bennett](../J/J.A.W._Bennett.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), born in [New Zealand](../N/New_Zealand.md).

## March {#March}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s first poem, *[The Battle of the Eastern Field](../T/The_Battle_of_the_Eastern_Field.md)*, and the report "[Acta Senatus](../A/Acta_Senatus.md)", are published in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](../K/King_Edward's_School_Chronicle.md)*.

## June {#June}

- **[3](3_June.md)** - Swedish translator [Åke Ohlmarks](Åke_Ohlmarks.md) is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes the "Debating Society Report" and the Editorial in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](../K/King_Edward's_School_Chronicle.md)*.

## July {#July}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes the Editorial in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](../K/King_Edward's_School_Chronicle.md)*.

## Summer {#Summer}

- **?** - The Tolkiens go to holiday in [Switzerland](../S/Switzerland.md), where J.R.R. (alledgedly) finds a postcard of *[Der Berggeist](../D/Der_Berggeist.md)*.
- Tolkien with a party of 12 wander on foot in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauterbrunnen" title="wikipedia:Lauterbrunnen">Lauterbrunnen</a>. Vivid memories of that adventure would form the basis for [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) in *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## August {#August}

- **[12](12_August.md)** - [Felix Felton](../F/Felix_Felton.md), who would play [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md), [the Black Captain](../W/Witch-king.md) and [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) in [The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>), is born.

## October {#October}

- **[3](3_October.md)** - [Michael Hordern](../M/Michael_Hordern.md), who would voice [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) in the [1981](1981.md) [radio series of *The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), is born.
- **[20](20_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) signs a [Oxford University visitor's page](../O/Oxford_University_visitor's_page.md).

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) creates the *[Tea Club and Barrovian Society](../T/T.C.B.S..md)* at grammar school.
