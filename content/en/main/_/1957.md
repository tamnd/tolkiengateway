[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1957" title="wikipedia:1957">1957</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[15](/wiki/15_January "15 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is [interviewed](/wiki/1957_%22Carnival_of_Books%22_Interview "1957 \"Carnival of Books\" Interview") by Ruth Harshaw for the NBC radio program "Carnival of Books".

## February {#February}

- **[3](/wiki/3_February "3 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 195a](/wiki/Letter_195a "Letter 195a").

## March {#March}

- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 195b](/wiki/Letter_195b "Letter 195b").
- **[21](/wiki/21_March "21 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 196](/wiki/Letter_196 "Letter 196").

## April {#April}

- **[9](/wiki/9_April "9 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mr Hayward](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Hayward "Letter to Mr Hayward").
- **[23](/wiki/23_April "23 April")** - Poet [Roy Campbell](/wiki/Roy_Campbell) dies.
- **[24](/wiki/24_April "24 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 196a](/wiki/Letter_196a).

## May {#May}

- **[7](/wiki/7_May "7 May")** - Tolkien visits [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge) to view the *Ancrene Wisse* manuscript.
- **[9](/wiki/9_May "9 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 197](/wiki/Letter_197 "Letter 197").

## June {#June}

- **[7](/wiki/7_June "7 June")** - [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University) acquires many of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s manuscripts for 1,500 pounds.
- **[9](/wiki/9_June "9 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to William Ready](</wiki/Letter_to_William_Ready_(9_June_1957)> "Letter to William Ready (9 June 1957)").
- **[24](/wiki/24_June "24 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 199](/wiki/Letter_199 "Letter 199").
- **[25](/wiki/25_June "25 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 200](/wiki/Letter_200 "Letter 200").
- **[26](/wiki/26_June "26 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Deirdre Levinson](</wiki/Letter_to_Deirdre_Levinson_(26_June_1957)> "Letter to Deirdre Levinson (26 June 1957)").

## August {#August}

- **[5](/wiki/5_August "5 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [two letters to Jerome W. Archer](/wiki/Letters_to_Jerome_W._Archer "Letters to Jerome W. Archer"), Director of Libraries at [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University).
- **[21](/wiki/21_August "21 August")** - Artist [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe) is born.
- **[24](/wiki/24_August "24 August")** - Actor [Stephen Fry](/wiki/Stephen_Fry) is born.
- **[25](/wiki/25_August "25 August")** - Author [Ian Brodie](/wiki/Ian_Brodie) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[2](/wiki/2_September "2 September")** - Professor [William Craigie](/wiki/William_Craigie), [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s predecessor as [Rawlinson and Bosworth Professor of Anglo-Saxon](/wiki/Rawlinson_and_Bosworth_Professor_of_Anglo-Saxon), dies.
- **[4](/wiki/4_September "4 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 200a](/wiki/Letter_200a "Letter 200a").
- **[7](/wiki/7_September "7 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 201](/wiki/Letter_201 "Letter 201").
- **[10](/wiki/10_September "10 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wins the [International Fantasy Award](/wiki/The_Bookseller,_14_September_1957 "The Bookseller, 14 September 1957").
- **[11](/wiki/11_September "11 September")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [letter 202](/wiki/Letter_202 "Letter 202").
- **[11](/wiki/11_September "11 September")** - The [*Oxford Mail*](/wiki/Oxford_Mail,_11_September_1957 "Oxford Mail, 11 September 1957") reports from the International Fantasy Award, won by Tolkien.
- **[14](/wiki/14_September "14 September")** - [*The Bookseller*](/wiki/The_Bookseller,_14_September_1957 "The Bookseller, 14 September 1957") reports from the International Fantasy Award, won by Tolkien.

- **?** - Scholar [Christopher Mitchell](/wiki/Christopher_Mitchell) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[9/10](/wiki/9_November "9 November")** - At midnight, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](</wiki/Letter_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(9/10_November_1957)> "Letter to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski (9/10 November 1957)").
- **[17](/wiki/17_November "17 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 203](/wiki/Letter_203 "Letter 203").
- **[17](/wiki/17_November "17 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](</wiki/Letter_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(17_November_1957)> "Letter to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski (17 November 1957)").

## December {#December}

- **[7](/wiki/7_December "7 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 204](/wiki/Letter_204 "Letter 204").
- **[13](/wiki/13_December "13 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 204a](/wiki/Letter_204a "Letter 204a").
