- **[15](15_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is [interviewed](1957_%2522Carnival_of_Books%2522_Interview.md) by Ruth Harshaw for the NBC radio program "Carnival of Books".

- **[9](9_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to William Ready](<../L/Letter_to_William_Ready_(9_June_1957).md>).

- **[5](5_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [two letters to Jerome W. Archer](../L/Letters_to_Jerome_W._Archer.md), Director of Libraries at [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md).

- **[24](24_August.md)** - Actor [Stephen Fry](../S/Stephen_Fry.md) is born.
- **[25](25_August.md)** - Author [Ian Brodie](../I/Ian_Brodie.md) is born.

- **[11](11_September.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [letter 202](../L/Letter_202.md).
- **[11](11_September.md)** - The [*Oxford Mail*](../O/Oxford_Mail,_11_September_1957.md) reports from the International Fantasy Award, won by Tolkien.
- **[14](14_September.md)** - [*The Bookseller*](../T/The_Bookseller,_14_September_1957.md) reports from the International Fantasy Award, won by Tolkien.
