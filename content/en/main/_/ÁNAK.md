**ÁNAK** and **NAK** are [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [roots](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "bite".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ank%C4%81&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ankā (page does not exist)">an-kā</a></i> ("jaw, row of teeth")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[anca](/wiki/Anca "Anca")*
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)): *[anc](/wiki/Anc "Anc")*

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nakma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nakma (page does not exist)">nakma</a></i> ("jaw")
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nangwa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nangwa (page does not exist)">nangwa</a></i>
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Naew&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Naew (page does not exist)">naew</a></i>

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nak-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nak- (page does not exist)">nak-</a></i> ("bite"); *[nahta](/wiki/Nahta "Nahta")* ("a bite")
- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nag-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nag- (page does not exist)">nag-</a></i> ("bite"); <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Naeth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Naeth (page does not exist)">naeth</a></i> ("biting, gnashing of teeth")
