## January {#January}

- **[4](/wiki/4_January "4 January")** - [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter), [biographer](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography "J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography") and [editor](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") dies.
- **[14](/wiki/14_January "14 January")-[16](/wiki/16_January "16 January")** - [The One Ring Celebration](/wiki/The_One_Ring_Celebration).
- **?** - [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway)'s [IRC channel](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Chat "Tolkien Gateway:Chat") goes online.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 191](/wiki/Amon_Hen_191)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[25](/wiki/25_February "25 February")** - Dutch translator [Max Schuchart](/wiki/Max_Schuchart) dies.
- **?** - *[Vinyar Tengwar 47](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_47)* is published.
- **?** - *[Walking with Bilbo](/wiki/Walking_with_Bilbo)* is published.

## March {#March}

- **[11](/wiki/11_March "11 March")** - Cartographer [Karen Wynn Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad) dies.
- **[28](/wiki/28_March "28 March")** - The parody book *[The Soddit](/wiki/The_Soddit)* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_March "30 March")** - *[Unsung Heroes of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Unsung_Heroes_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 192](/wiki/Amon_Hen_192)* is published.

- **[13](/wiki/13_April "13 April")** - German translator [Wolfgang Krege](/wiki/Wolfgang_Krege) dies.
- **[15](/wiki/15_April "15 April")** - *[Reconsidering Tolkien](/wiki/Reconsidering_Tolkien)* is published.
- **[22](/wiki/22_April "22 April")** - [Norman Bird](/wiki/Norman_Bird), who played [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"), dies.
- **[30](/wiki/30_April "30 April")** - *[Interrupted Music: The Making of Tolkien's Mythology](/wiki/Interrupted_Music:_The_Making_of_Tolkien%27s_Mythology)* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_April "30 April")** - *[The Forsaken Realm of Tolkien](/wiki/The_Forsaken_Realm_of_Tolkien)* is published.
- **?** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 2](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_2)* is published.
- **?** - *[Reconsidering Tolkien](/wiki/Reconsidering_Tolkien)* is published.

- **?** - *[Amon Hen 193](/wiki/Amon_Hen_193)* is published.

- **[9](/wiki/9_August "9 August")-[10](/wiki/10_August "10 August")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Symphony](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony)* plays in Herodion, Greece.
- **[10](/wiki/10_August "10 August")** - *[The Tolkien Society Guide to Oxford](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society_Guide_to_Oxford)* is published.
- **[11](/wiki/11_August "11 August")-[15](/wiki/15_August "15 August")** - The convention [Tolkien 2005](/wiki/Tolkien_2005) is held.

- **[20](/wiki/20_October "20 October")** - [George Sayer](/wiki/George_Sayer), friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), dies.
- **[25](/wiki/25_October "25 October")** - The [Tolkien Ensemble](/wiki/Tolkien_Ensemble) publish their fourth album, *[Leaving Rivendell](/wiki/Leaving_Rivendell)*.
- **[26](/wiki/26_October "26 October")** - Scholar [Angus McIntosh](/wiki/Angus_McIntosh), pupil and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), dies.

- **?** - *[The Lord of the Rings Sketchbook](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Sketchbook)* is published.
