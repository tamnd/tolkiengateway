Events that occurred on 31 May.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1912](/wiki/1912)** - German translator [Margaret Carroux](/wiki/Margaret_Carroux) is born.
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 72](/wiki/Letter_72).
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - The thirteenth episode of the [1981 radio adaptation](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[2018](/wiki/2018)** - *[The Hobbit Facsimile Gift Edition](/wiki/The_Hobbit_Facsimile_Gift_Edition)* is published in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom" title="wikipedia:United Kingdom">UK</a>.
- **[2025](/wiki/2025)** - After 12 years in the role, [Shaun Gunner](/wiki/Shaun_Gunner) steps down as Chair of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) to become its first Chief Executive Officer. He is succeeded as Acting Chair by [Daniel Helen](/wiki/Daniel_Helen).<sup id="cite_ref-CEO_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CEO-1">[1]</a></sup>
