Events that occurred on 31 May.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1912](1912.md)** - German translator [Margaret Carroux](../M/Margaret_Carroux.md) is born.
- **[1944](1944.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 72](../L/Letter_72.md).
- **[1981](1981.md)** - The thirteenth episode of the [1981 radio adaptation](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- **[2018](2018.md)** - *[The Hobbit Facsimile Gift Edition](../T/The_Hobbit_Facsimile_Gift_Edition.md)* is published in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom" title="wikipedia:United Kingdom">UK</a>.
- **[2025](2025.md)** - After 12 years in the role, [Shaun Gunner](../S/Shaun_Gunner.md) steps down as Chair of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) to become its first Chief Executive Officer. He is succeeded as Acting Chair by [Daniel Helen](../D/Daniel_Helen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CEO_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CEO-1">[1]</a></sup>

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

*Note: Months of the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) only have 30 days, so no events have nor will happen on the 31st.*<span></span>
