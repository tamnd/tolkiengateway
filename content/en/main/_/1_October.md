Events that occurred on 1 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1914](/wiki/1914)** - [Donald A. Wollheim](/wiki/Donald_A._Wollheim) is born.
- **[1924](/wiki/1924)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) begins his Professorship of English Language at the [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds).
- **[1941](/wiki/1941)** - Author [Glen GoodKnight](/wiki/Glen_GoodKnight) is born.
- **[1969](/wiki/1969)** - Actor [Charles Edwards](/wiki/Charles_Edwards) is born.
- **[1976](/wiki/1976)** - [Oxonmoot 1976](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1976) at The Welsh Pony.
- **[1979](/wiki/1979)** - The *[Jackanory](/wiki/Jackanory)* episode "An Unexpected Party" is aired on the [BBC](/wiki/BBC).
- **[1999](/wiki/1999)** - Voice actor [Noel Johnson](/wiki/Noel_Johnson) dies.
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - *[The Gospel According to Tolkien](/wiki/The_Gospel_According_to_Tolkien)* is published.
- <b><a href="/wiki/2007" title="2007">2007</a> - The second and expanded edition of <i><a href="/wiki/Tolkien_On_Fairy-stories" title="Tolkien On Fairy-stories">Tolkien On Fairy-stories</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** – The redesigned slipcased edition of *[Mr. Bliss](/wiki/Mr._Bliss)* is published.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - *[Middle-earth: Visions of a Modern Myth](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Visions_of_a_Modern_Myth)* is published.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - Jubilee for [Mithlond, the Gothenburg Tolkien Society](/wiki/Mithlond,_G%C3%B6teborgs_Tolkiens%C3%A4llskap "Mithlond, Göteborgs Tolkiensällskap") of Sweden.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - Together with [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") the [four hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") travel towards [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop).
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) leaves [Bree](/wiki/Bree).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
