[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1919" title="wikipedia:1919">1919</a></b>.

## Early {#Early}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) begins his two years of work on the [Oxford English Dictionary](../O/Oxford_English_Dictionary.md).

## January {#January}

- **[1](1_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) starts a new [diary](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_diaries.md), mainly written in the [Alphabet of Rúmil](../S/Sarati.md).

## February {#February}

- **[17](17_February.md)** - [Hilary Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md) returns home after [the War](../W/World_War_I.md).

## June {#June}

- **[14](14_June.md)** - Dutch translator [Willy Wielek-Berg](../W/Willy_Wielek-Berg.md) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[15](15_July.md)** - Tolkien is officially released from service, well a year after the war's end.

## September {#September}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) acquires a copy of the Early English Text Society edition of *[Morte Arthure](../L/List_of_books_in_Tolkien's_library.md#Morte_Arthure)*.

## November {#November}

- **[11](11_November.md)** - The Ministry of Pensions sends a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), on which he wrote a draft of *[The Ruined Enchanter: A Fairy Ballad](../T/The_Ruined_Enchanter.md)*.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien receives his M.A. from [Exeter College](../U/University_of_Oxford.md).
