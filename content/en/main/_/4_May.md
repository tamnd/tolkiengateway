Events that occurred on 4 May.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 65](/wiki/Letter_65).
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - While writing on *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) sees a new character arrive on the scene. He later [comments](/wiki/Letter_66 "Letter 66"): "*(I am sure I did not invent him, I did not even want him, though I like him, but there he came walking into the woods of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien)): [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), brother of [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir)*".
- **[1958](/wiki/1958)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 209](/wiki/Letter_209).
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - Actor [Trystan Gravelle](/wiki/Trystan_Gravelle) is born.
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - Artist [Ben Lee](/wiki/Ben_Lee) dies.
- **[2012](/wiki/2012)** - *[JRR Tolkien: The True Lord of the Rings](/wiki/JRR_Tolkien:_The_True_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, a graphic novel, is published.
