Events that occurred on 20 December.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1928](1928.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[1949](1949.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 122a](../L/Letter_122a.md).
- **[1954](1954.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 158a](../L/Letter_158a.md).
- **[1955](1955.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 179a](../L/Letter_179a.md).
- **[1955](1955.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 179b](../L/Letter_179b.md).
- **[1956](1956.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Deirdre Levinson](<../L/Letter_to_Deirdre_Levinson_(20_December_1956).md>).
- **[1971](1971.md)** - Actor [Michael Yurchak](../M/Michael_Yurchak.md) is born.
- **[1974](1974.md)** - Actor [Nathan Clark](../N/Nathan_Clark.md) is born.
- **[2002](2002.md)** - [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md) publishes "The Plot Unravels", a review of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* in *The Times Literary Supplement*.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) rests in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and prepare for their [quest](../Q/Quest_of_the_Ring.md) to destroy [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).
