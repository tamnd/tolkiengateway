Events that occurred on 26 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1957](/wiki/1957)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Deirdre Levinson](</wiki/Letter_to_Deirdre_Levinson_(26_June_1957)> "Letter to Deirdre Levinson (26 June 1957)").
- **[1968](/wiki/1968)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 305](/wiki/Letter_305).
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - *[Tree of Tales](/wiki/Tree_of_Tales)* is published.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - [Simon Tolkien](/wiki/Simon_Tolkien) publishes the article "[In My Grandfather's Footsteps](/wiki/In_My_Grandfather%27s_Footsteps)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s Northern Hemisphere seminar: "Tolkien &amp; [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham)".
- **[2012](/wiki/2012)** - *[Tolkien and Welsh](/wiki/Tolkien_and_Welsh)* by [Mark T. Hooker](/wiki/Mark_T._Hooker) is published.
- **[2016](/wiki/2016)** - Scholar [John Ellison](/wiki/John_Ellison) dies.
