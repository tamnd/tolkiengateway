Events that occurred on 26 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1957](1957.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Deirdre Levinson](<../L/Letter_to_Deirdre_Levinson_(26_June_1957).md>).
- **[1968](1968.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 305](../L/Letter_305.md).
- **[2007](2007.md)** - *[Tree of Tales](../T/Tree_of_Tales.md)* is published.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - [Simon Tolkien](../S/Simon_Tolkien.md) publishes the article "[In My Grandfather's Footsteps](../I/In_My_Grandfather's_Footsteps.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[2010](2010.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s Northern Hemisphere seminar: "Tolkien &amp; [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md)".
- **[2012](2012.md)** - *[Tolkien and Welsh](../T/Tolkien_and_Welsh.md)* by [Mark T. Hooker](../M/Mark_T._Hooker.md) is published.
- **[2016](2016.md)** - Scholar [John Ellison](../J/John_Ellison.md) dies.
