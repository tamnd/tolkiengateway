**Úrimë** (*Urui* in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)) was the eighth month of the year in the [King's Reckoning](../K/King's_Reckoning.md) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](../S/Stewards'_Reckoning.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), following [Cermië](../C/Cermië.md), and preceding [Yavannië](../Y/Yavannië.md). In the [New Reckoning](../N/New_Reckoning.md) it was the fifth month of the year.

*Úrimë* in the Kings' Reckoning (and for the most part in the Stewards' Reckoning) corresponded to [Wedmath](../W/Wedmath.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) (approximately modern [24 July](24_July.md) through [22 August](22_August.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The first element of *Urui* is *Ur-*, which is derived from Sindarin *ûr* "fire, heat".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The second element *-ui* is a common adjectival ending,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> also present in other Sindarin names of the months.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
