**Úrimë** (*Urui* in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)) was the eighth month of the year in the [King's Reckoning](/wiki/King%27s_Reckoning) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), following [Cermië](/wiki/Cermi%C3%AB), and preceding [Yavannië](/wiki/Yavanni%C3%AB). In the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning) it was the fifth month of the year.

*Úrimë* in the Kings' Reckoning (and for the most part in the Stewards' Reckoning) corresponded to [Wedmath](/wiki/Wedmath) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) (approximately modern [24 July](/wiki/24_July) through [22 August](/wiki/22_August)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The first element of *Urui* is *Ur-*, which is derived from Sindarin *ûr* "fire, heat".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The second element *-ui* is a common adjectival ending,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> also present in other Sindarin names of the months.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
