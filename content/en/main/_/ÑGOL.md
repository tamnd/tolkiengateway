<br> **ÑGOL** is an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "dark-hued, dark-brown".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17169_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17169-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md) appears the roots ÑGOL, signifying "wise, wisdom, be wise", and ÑGOLOD, signifying "one of the wise folk, Gnome".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety377_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety377-2">[2]</a></sup>
