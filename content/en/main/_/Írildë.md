**Írildë** was the daughter of [Cemendur](<../C/Cemendur_(son_of_Axantur).md>), a nobleman of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). Her nephew [Hallacar](../H/Hallacar.md) would marry [Tar-Ancalimë](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md), the first [Ruling Queen of Númenor](../R/Ruling_Queen_of_Númenor.md), making her an aunt of the royal family. Almost nothing is known of her life.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Írildë* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), and it could be a combination of *írë* ("desire") + -ldë (feminine agent).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> *Irildë* was a Qenya/Quenya form of *[Idril](../I/Idril.md)*.
