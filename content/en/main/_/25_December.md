Events that occurred on 25 December.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1903](/wiki/1903)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) takes his First Communion.
- **[1914](/wiki/1914)** – The last meeting of the [T.C.B.S.](/wiki/T.C.B.S.) takes place in London before [Geoffrey Bache Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith), [Robert Gilson](/wiki/Robert_Gilson), [Thomas Kenneth Barnsley](/wiki/Thomas_Kenneth_Barnsley) and [Christopher Wiseman](/wiki/Christopher_Wiseman) join the [Great War](/wiki/World_War_I "World War I").
- **[1925](/wiki/1925)** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **[1928](/wiki/1928)** - Father Christmas writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **[1937](/wiki/1937)** - Father Christmas writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **[1938](/wiki/1938)** - Father Christmas writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **[1943](/wiki/1943)** - Father Christmas writes his last letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **[1954](/wiki/1954)** - Singer [Annie Lennox](/wiki/Annie_Lennox) is born.
- **[1971](/wiki/1971)** - *[Attacks of Taste](/wiki/Attacks_of_Taste)*, with a contribution by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - *[Reading The Lord of the Rings: New Writings on Tolkien's Classic](/wiki/Reading_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "Reading The Lord of the Rings")* is published.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

At first the Fellowship was to depart on [24 November](/wiki/24_November) but later Tolkien changed the date.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:xlv</sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") noted in *[Nomenclature](/wiki/Guide_to_the_Names_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "Guide to the Names in The Lord of the Rings")* that the date the Fellowship departed from Rivendell was purposefully chosen.

However, he mentioned later to Henry Resnick that it was an "unimportant date" that "occurred strictly on accident" but he let it "to show that this was not a christian myth anyhow".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
