[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1929" title="wikipedia:1929">1929</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[2](2_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to E.V. Gordon](<../L/Letter_to_E.V._Gordon_(2_January_1929).md>).

## February {#February}

- **[4](4_February.md)** - Actor [Noel Appleby](../N/Noel_Appleby.md) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[18](18_June.md)** - [Priscilla Tolkien](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s only daughter, is born.
- **[23](23_June.md)** - [Barbara Remington](../B/Barbara_Remington.md), cover artist on some American editions, is born.

## July {#July}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) makes a recording for the *[Linguaphone Conversational Course](../L/Linguaphone_Conversational_Course.md)*.

## October {#October}

- **[10](10_October.md)** - [Bernard Mayes](../B/Bernard_Mayes.md), the adapter and voice of [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1979 radio series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series).md>) and [*The Hobbit* (1979 radio series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1979_radio_series).md>), is born.

## December {#December}

- **[7](7_December.md)** - [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) sent a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) informing tha have read [The Gest of Beren and Lúthien](../T/The_Gest_of_Beren_and_Lúthien.md).
- **[26](26_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[Essays And Studies (vol. 14, 1929)](<../E/Essays_and_Studies_(1929).md>)*, including the essay '[Ancrene Wisse and Hali Meiðhad](../A/Ancrene_Wisse_and_Hali_Meiðhad.md)' by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.
- **?** - [Daphne Castell](../D/Daphne_Castell.md) is born.
