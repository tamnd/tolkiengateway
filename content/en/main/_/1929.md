[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1929" title="wikipedia:1929">1929</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[2](/wiki/2_January "2 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to E.V. Gordon](</wiki/Letter_to_E.V._Gordon_(2_January_1929)> "Letter to E.V. Gordon (2 January 1929)").

## February {#February}

- **[4](/wiki/4_February "4 February")** - Actor [Noel Appleby](/wiki/Noel_Appleby) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[18](/wiki/18_June "18 June")** - [Priscilla Tolkien](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s only daughter, is born.
- **[23](/wiki/23_June "23 June")** - [Barbara Remington](/wiki/Barbara_Remington), cover artist on some American editions, is born.

## July {#July}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) makes a recording for the *[Linguaphone Conversational Course](/wiki/Linguaphone_Conversational_Course)*.

## October {#October}

- **[10](/wiki/10_October "10 October")** - [Bernard Mayes](/wiki/Bernard_Mayes), the adapter and voice of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1979 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1979 radio series)") and [*The Hobbit* (1979 radio series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1979_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1979 radio series)"), is born.

## December {#December}

- **[7](/wiki/7_December "7 December")** - [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) sent a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) informing tha have read [The Gest of Beren and Lúthien](/wiki/The_Gest_of_Beren_and_L%C3%BAthien).
- **[26](/wiki/26_December "26 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[Essays And Studies (vol. 14, 1929)](</wiki/Essays_and_Studies_(1929)> "Essays and Studies (1929)")*, including the essay '[Ancrene Wisse and Hali Meiðhad](/wiki/Ancrene_Wisse_and_Hali_Mei%C3%B0had)' by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **?** - [Daphne Castell](/wiki/Daphne_Castell) is born.
