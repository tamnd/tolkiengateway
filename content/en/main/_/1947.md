[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1947" title="wikipedia:1947">1947</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[10](/wiki/10_January "10 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to A.W. Riddle](</wiki/Letter_to_A.W._Riddle_(10_January_1947)> "Letter to A.W. Riddle (10 January 1947)").

## March {#March}

- **?** - J.R.R. Tolkien, [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Bratt "Edith Bratt") and [Priscilla](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien "Priscilla Tolkien") move from [20 Northmoor Road](/wiki/20_Northmoor_Road) to [3 Manor Road](/wiki/3_Manor_Road), [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## May {#May}

- **[5](/wiki/5_May "5 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 107a](/wiki/Letter_107a).

## June {#June}

- **[3](/wiki/3_June "3 June")** - Actor [Matthew Locricchio](/wiki/Matthew_Locricchio) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[5](/wiki/5_July "5 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 108](/wiki/Letter_108 "Letter 108").
- **[19](/wiki/19_July "19 July")** - Author and scholar [Colin Duriez](/wiki/Colin_Duriez) is born.
- **[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) reports on the first version of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.
- **[31](/wiki/31_July "31 July")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 109](/wiki/Letter_109).

## August {#August}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) makes the drawing *[New Lodge, Stonyhurst](/wiki/New_Lodge,_Stonyhurst)*.
- **[20](/wiki/20_August "20 August")** - Artist [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee) is born.
- **[25](/wiki/25_August "25 August")** - Artist [Michael Kaluta](/wiki/Michael_Kaluta) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[20](/wiki/20_September "20 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 110](/wiki/Letter_110 "Letter 110").
- **[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 111](/wiki/Letter_111 "Letter 111").
- **[26](/wiki/26_September "26 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Jennifer Brookes-Smith](</wiki/Letter_to_Jennifer_Brookes-Smith_(26_September_1947)> "Letter to Jennifer Brookes-Smith (26 September 1947)").
- **[26](/wiki/26_September "26 September")** - Actor [Graham Faulkner](/wiki/Graham_Faulkner) is born.
- **[30](/wiki/30_September "30 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 111a](/wiki/Letter_111a).
- **[30](/wiki/30_September "30 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Rosemary](</wiki/Letter_to_Rosemary_(30_September_1947)> "Letter to Rosemary (30 September 1947)").
- **?** - Tolkien, a fellow of Merton College, visits the college's estates in [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge).

## October {#October}

- **[2](/wiki/2_October "2 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to A.W. Riddle](</wiki/Letter_to_A.W._Riddle_(2_October_1947)> "Letter to A.W. Riddle (2 October 1947)").
- **[14](/wiki/14_October "14 October")** - Author [David Day](/wiki/David_Day) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 112](/wiki/Letter_112 "Letter 112").

## December {#December}

- **[4](/wiki/4_December "4 December")** - *[Essays Presented to Charles Williams](/wiki/Essays_Presented_to_Charles_Williams)*, including "[On Fairy-Stories](/wiki/On_Fairy-Stories)", is published.
- **[10](/wiki/10_December "10 December")** - [Douglas C. Kenney](/wiki/Douglas_C._Kenney), co-author of *[Bored of the Rings](</wiki/Bored_of_the_Rings_(book)> "Bored of the Rings (book)")*, is born.
- **[17](/wiki/17_December "17 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Malcolm Knox](/wiki/Letter_to_Malcolm_Knox "Letter to Malcolm Knox").
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [S.R.T.O. d'Ardenne](/wiki/Simonne_d%27Ardenne "Simonne d'Ardenne")'s essay "'iþþlen' in *Sawles Warde*" is published in *[English Studies](</wiki/English_Studies_(December_1947)> "English Studies (December 1947)")*.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - The first translation of one of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s works, *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* (as *[Hompen](/wiki/Hompen)*), is published.
- **?** - [Ruth S. Noel](/wiki/Ruth_S._Noel) is born.
- **?** - [Peter Kjærulff](/wiki/Peter_Kj%C3%A6rulff) is born.
