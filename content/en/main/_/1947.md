[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1947" title="wikipedia:1947">1947</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[10](10_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to A.W. Riddle](<../L/Letter_to_A.W._Riddle_(10_January_1947).md>).

## March {#March}

- **?** - J.R.R. Tolkien, [Edith](../E/Edith_Bratt.md) and [Priscilla](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md) move from [20 Northmoor Road](20_Northmoor_Road.md) to [3 Manor Road](3_Manor_Road.md), [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## May {#May}

- **[5](5_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 107a](../L/Letter_107a.md).

## June {#June}

- **[3](3_June.md)** - Actor [Matthew Locricchio](../M/Matthew_Locricchio.md) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[5](5_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 108](../L/Letter_108.md).
- **[19](19_July.md)** - Author and scholar [Colin Duriez](../C/Colin_Duriez.md) is born.
- **[28](28_July.md)** - [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) reports on the first version of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.
- **[31](31_July.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 109](../L/Letter_109.md).

## August {#August}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) makes the drawing *[New Lodge, Stonyhurst](../N/New_Lodge,_Stonyhurst.md)*.
- **[20](20_August.md)** - Artist [Alan Lee](../A/Alan_Lee.md) is born.
- **[25](25_August.md)** - Artist [Michael Kaluta](../M/Michael_Kaluta.md) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[20](20_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 110](../L/Letter_110.md).
- **[21](21_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 111](../L/Letter_111.md).
- **[26](26_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Jennifer Brookes-Smith](<../L/Letter_to_Jennifer_Brookes-Smith_(26_September_1947).md>).
- **[26](26_September.md)** - Actor [Graham Faulkner](../G/Graham_Faulkner.md) is born.
- **[30](30_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 111a](../L/Letter_111a.md).
- **[30](30_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Rosemary](<../L/Letter_to_Rosemary_(30_September_1947).md>).
- **?** - Tolkien, a fellow of Merton College, visits the college's estates in [Cambridge](../C/Cambridge.md).

## October {#October}

- **[2](2_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to A.W. Riddle](<../L/Letter_to_A.W._Riddle_(2_October_1947).md>).
- **[14](14_October.md)** - Author [David Day](../D/David_Day.md) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[30](30_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 112](../L/Letter_112.md).

## December {#December}

- **[4](4_December.md)** - *[Essays Presented to Charles Williams](../E/Essays_Presented_to_Charles_Williams.md)*, including "[On Fairy-Stories](../O/On_Fairy-Stories.md)", is published.
- **[10](10_December.md)** - [Douglas C. Kenney](../D/Douglas_C._Kenney.md), co-author of *[Bored of the Rings](<../B/Bored_of_the_Rings_(book).md>)*, is born.
- **[17](17_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Malcolm Knox](../L/Letter_to_Malcolm_Knox.md).
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [S.R.T.O. d'Ardenne](../S/Simonne_d'Ardenne.md)'s essay "'iþþlen' in *Sawles Warde*" is published in *[English Studies](<../E/English_Studies_(December_1947).md>)*.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - The first translation of one of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s works, *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* (as *[Hompen](../H/Hompen.md)*), is published.
- **?** - [Ruth S. Noel](../R/Ruth_S._Noel.md) is born.
- **?** - [Peter Kjærulff](../P/Peter_Kjærulff.md) is born.
