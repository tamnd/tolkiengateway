**Éadgifu** was the mother of [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine), from whom he has heard stories of the "islands of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/West" title="West">West</a>",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in the text called *[Ælfwine of England](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine_of_England)* from *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Eng_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eng-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Éadgifu was born in [Lionesse](/wiki/Lionesse), a region of [Lúthien](/wiki/Luthany "Luthany"), and was called as such by [Déor](</wiki/D%C3%A9or_(father_of_%C3%86lfwine)> "Déor (father of Ælfwine)") her husband - even though that was not her original name.

She had an unquenchable longing for the sea while she dwelt in the inland city of [Kortirion](/wiki/Kortirion#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> with Déor, a trait which was inherited by her son Ælfwine. The [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of Lionesse, with whom she was friendly with, sent messengers to his birth, presumably to give their congratulations.

Éadgifu died during the siege of Kortirion by the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Forodwaith_(The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Forodwaith (The Book of Lost Tales) (page does not exist)">Forodwaith</a> (i.e. <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikings" title="wikipedia:Vikings">Vikings</a>), and "her fair form lay unhonoured in [Mindon Gwar](/wiki/Mindon_Gwar)".<sup id="cite_ref-Eng_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eng-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Éadgifu* is in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English), being comprised of *éad* ("blessedness") + *gifu* ("gift").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier text from *The Book of Lost Tales*, *Éadgifu* was the Old English name for [Naimi](/wiki/Naimi), the Elven wife of [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
