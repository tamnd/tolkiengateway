Events that occurred on 18 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1916](/wiki/1916)** - The [Battle of the Somme](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme) ends. Neither side wins. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is officially stricken off as a casualty by his Battalion.
- **[1941](/wiki/1941)** - Actor [David Hemmings](/wiki/David_Hemmings) is born.
- **[1976](/wiki/1976)** - Voice actor [Dominic Armato](/wiki/Dominic_Armato) is born.
- **[1980](/wiki/1980)** - *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)* is published in the USA.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online): Daughter of Strife*, patch is released.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Mines of Moria](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Mines_of_Moria)* expansion is released.
- **[2015](/wiki/2015)** - *[The Tolkien Treasury](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Treasury)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) travel up the [Dry River](/wiki/Dry_River) and approach [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") rest in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").
