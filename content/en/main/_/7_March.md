Events that occurred on 7 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1940](1940.md)** - Voice actor [Richard O'Callaghan](../R/Richard_O'Callaghan.md) is born.
- **[1956](1956.md)** - William Galbraith writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](../L/Letter_to_William_Galbraith.md).
- **[2004](2004.md)** - The third day of "[RingCon IV](../R/RingCon.md)" in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle" title="wikipedia:Seattle">Seattle</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) leads a raid against the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md) in [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).
    - He finds [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and takes them to [Henneth Annûn](../H/Henneth_Annûn.md). He learns about Frodo carrying [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) but does not try to take it.
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) travels with [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) along the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) towards [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).
    - [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) reaches [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md) at nightfall.
    - [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) and the [éoherë](Éoherë.md) travel towards [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md).
    - Full moon<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wanted this night to have a full moon (seen both by Frodo and Pippin, and the muster of Rohan) and adjusted other dates and routes around it. At first, Tolkien thought that [Time](../T/Time.md) "froze" while the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) stayed in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md), and in the first drafts of *[The Forbidden Pool](../T/The_Forbidden_Pool.md)*, Frodo was witnessing the full moon on the night of [6 February](6_February.md). Later Tolkien changed his mind about Lothlórien, pushing *The Forbidden Pool* a month later, and also invented the [Shire Reckoning](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md). To accommodate the full moons, and the new calendar, he adjusted the Fellowship's departure from Lothlórien on [16 February](16_February.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In one of his time-schemes Tolkien used to keep track of his writing, he noted "Total eclipse at 10.30" for the night of 7–8 March, possibly after the 2 March eclipse of [1942](1942.md) (on which year he based the lunar phases). However the eclipse didn't pass into the story.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
