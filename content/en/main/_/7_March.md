Events that occurred on 7 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1940](/wiki/1940)** - Voice actor [Richard O'Callaghan](/wiki/Richard_O%27Callaghan) is born.
- **[1956](/wiki/1956)** - William Galbraith writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Letter_to_William_Galbraith "Letter to William Galbraith").
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - The third day of "[RingCon IV](/wiki/RingCon "RingCon")" in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle" title="wikipedia:Seattle">Seattle</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) leads a raid against the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).
    - He finds [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") and takes them to [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn). He learns about Frodo carrying [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") but does not try to take it.
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) travels with [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") along the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) towards [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).
    - [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) reaches [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) at nightfall.
    - [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and the [éoherë](/wiki/%C3%89oher%C3%AB "Éoherë") travel towards [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow).
    - Full moon<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wanted this night to have a full moon (seen both by Frodo and Pippin, and the muster of Rohan) and adjusted other dates and routes around it. At first, Tolkien thought that [Time](/wiki/Time) "froze" while the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) stayed in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), and in the first drafts of *[The Forbidden Pool](/wiki/The_Forbidden_Pool)*, Frodo was witnessing the full moon on the night of [6 February](/wiki/6_February). Later Tolkien changed his mind about Lothlórien, pushing *The Forbidden Pool* a month later, and also invented the [Shire Reckoning](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning). To accommodate the full moons, and the new calendar, he adjusted the Fellowship's departure from Lothlórien on [16 February](/wiki/16_February).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In one of his time-schemes Tolkien used to keep track of his writing, he noted "Total eclipse at 10.30" for the night of 7–8 March, possibly after the 2 March eclipse of [1942](/wiki/1942) (on which year he based the lunar phases). However the eclipse didn't pass into the story.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
