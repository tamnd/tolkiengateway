Events that occurred on 6 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1901](/wiki/1901)** - Actor [Bernard Rebel](/wiki/Bernard_Rebel) is born.
- **[1940](/wiki/1940)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 40](/wiki/Letter_40).
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 83](/wiki/Letter_83).
- **[1968](/wiki/1968)** - *Out of the Frying-pan into the Fire*, the second episode of the [1968 radio series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)") of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, is aired on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[1984](/wiki/1984)** - [Oxonmoot 1984](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1984) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- <b><a href="/wiki/2006" title="2006">2006</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide" title="The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide">The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - *Cair Andros*, a feast of the [Ellinikós Sýllogos Philon Tólkin](/wiki/Ellinik%C3%B3s_S%C3%BDllogos_Philon_T%C3%B3lkin).
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn's Quest](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Aragorn%27s_Quest)* is released in [Australia](/wiki/Australia).
- **[2015](/wiki/2015)** - [Árpád Göncz](/wiki/%C3%81rp%C3%A1d_G%C3%B6ncz) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - The travellers climb [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop) at noon. They find [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)'s stone.
    - The camp under Weathertop is raided at night, and [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") is attacked by the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) who attempts to hit his heart with a Morgul blade that will make him a wraith. Frodo wears the Ring and is wounded on the shoulder.
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) hurries towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) after the [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") attacked him on [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - At the crossing of the [Ford of Bruinen](/wiki/Ford_of_Bruinen), Frodo feels the first return of pain from his stab wound.<sup id="cite_ref-chief_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3020](/wiki/Third_Age_3020 "Third Age 3020"):
    - Frodo feels the pain return again.<sup id="cite_ref-chief_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021"):
    - [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") returns to [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) on his faithful pony, [Bill](/wiki/Bill_the_Pony "Bill the Pony"), after seeing off [many of his friends](/wiki/White_Ship "White Ship") at the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens).<sup id="cite_ref-chief_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-1">[1]</a></sup>
