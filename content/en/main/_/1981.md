## January {#January}

- **[10](/wiki/10_January "10 January")** - Actor [Richard Boone](/wiki/Richard_Boone) dies.
- **[28](/wiki/28_January "28 January")** - Actor [Elijah Wood](/wiki/Elijah_Wood) is born.
- **[29](/wiki/29_January "29 January")** - [J.A.W. Bennett](/wiki/J.A.W._Bennett), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), dies.
