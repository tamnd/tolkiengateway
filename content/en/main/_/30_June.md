Events that occurred on 30 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1955](1955.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 165](../L/Letter_165.md).
- **[1966](1966.md)** - Actor [Marton Csokas](../M/Marton_Csokas.md) is born.
- **[1969](1969.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Paul Bibire](../L/Letter_to_Paul_Bibire.md)
- **[1972](1972.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 339](../L/Letter_339.md), published in *The Daily Telegraph* as "[Beautiful place because trees are loved](../L/Letter_339.md)".
- **[1972](1972.md)** - [Mythcon 3](../M/Mythcon_3.md) begins in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach,_California" title="wikipedia:Long Beach, California">Long Beach, California</a>.
- **[2003](2003.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: Shadow and Flame](../S/Shadow_and_Flame.md)* is published.
- **[2004](2004.md)** - *[War and the Works of J.R.R. Tolkien](../W/War_and_the_Works_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* is published.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - *[Languages, Myths and History](../L/Languages,_Myths_and_History.md)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - About this time, [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) on his way to [Bree](../B/Bree.md) encounters [Radagast](../R/Radagast.md), who informs him that the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) are abroad.
