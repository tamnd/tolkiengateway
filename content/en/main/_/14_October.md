Events that occurred on 14 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1924](1924.md)** - Philologist [Brian Miller](../B/Brian_Miller.md), student and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is born.
- **[1947](1947.md)** - Author [David Day](../D/David_Day.md) is born.
- **[1948](1948.md)** - Narrator [Gerard Murphy](../G/Gerard_Murphy.md) is born.
- **[1958](1958.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 211](../L/Letter_211.md).
- **[1966](1966.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 289c](../L/Letter_289c.md).
- **[2005](2005.md)** - Author and artist [Bernie Zuber](../B/Bernie_Zuber.md) dies.
- **[2007](2007.md)** - [Ring\*Con 2007](../R/Ring%252ACon_2007.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulda" title="wikipedia:Fulda">Fulda</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany" title="wikipedia:Germany">Germany</a>.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - Actor [Martyn Sanderson](../M/Martyn_Sanderson.md) dies.
- **[2022](2022.md)** - The episode [Alloyed](../A/Alloyed.md) of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* is released online.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md):
    - [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), guided by [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md), travels to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) in order to deliver [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md)'s message to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - With [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) severely wounded, [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) and the [four hobbits](../T/Travellers.md) travel slowly towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) hurries towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) after the [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) attacked him on [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
