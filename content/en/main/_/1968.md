[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968" title="wikipedia:1968">1968</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[4](4_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Ken Jackson](<../L/Letter_to_Ken_Jackson_(4_January_1968).md>).
- **[8](8_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) reads over *[The New Shadow](../T/The_New_Shadow.md)* and most likely starts typing a final typescript.
- **[29](29_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Ken Jackson](<../L/Letter_to_Ken_Jackson_(29_January_1968).md>).

## February {#February}

- **[5](5_February.md)-[9](9_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is filmed for the documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](../T/Tolkien_in_Oxford.md)*.
- **[20](20_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 300](../L/Letter_300.md).
- **[29](29_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 301](../L/Letter_301.md).

## March {#March}

- **[1](1_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Miss E. Byrne](<../L/Letter_to_Miss_E._Byrne_(1_March_1968).md>).
- **[17](17_March.md)** - Author [Nataša Tučev](../N/Nataša_Tučev.md) is born.
- **[20](20_March.md)** - Actor and stuntman [Lawrence Makoare](../L/Lawrence_Makoare.md) is born.
- **[21](21_March.md)** - Actor [Greg Ellis](../G/Greg_Ellis.md) is born.
- **[22](22_March.md)** - *The Daily Telegraph* publishes an interview of Charlotte and Denis Plimmer with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), in the article "[The Man Who Understands Hobbits](../T/The_Man_Who_Understands_Hobbits.md)".
- **[25](25_March.md)** - [Michael Reisz](../M/Michael_Reisz.md) , who would voice [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game).md>)*, is born.
- **[30](30_March.md)** – The documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](../T/Tolkien_in_Oxford.md)* is aired on the [BBC](../B/BBC.md).
- **?** - *[The Road Goes Ever On](<../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book).md>)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[15](15_April.md)** - Actor [Richard Whiteside](../R/Richard_Whiteside.md) is born.

## May {#May}

- **[2](2_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 302](../L/Letter_302.md).
- **[3](3_May.md)** - Author [Michael D.C. Drout](../M/Michael_D.C._Drout.md) is born.
- **[6](6_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 303](../L/Letter_303.md).
- **[24](24_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to George Burke Johnston](../L/Letter_to_George_Burke_Johnston.md).
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mary Fairburn](<../L/Letter_to_Mary_Fairburn_(May_1968).md>).
- **?** - *[The Tolkien Relation](../T/The_Tolkien_Relation.md)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[4](4_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 304](../L/Letter_304.md).
- **[17](17_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) falls down the stairs of [76 Sandfield Road](76_Sandfield_Road.md) and breaks his leg.
- **[26](26_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 305](../L/Letter_305.md).

## July {#July}

- **Mid-July** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [Edith](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) move from [76 Sandfield Road](76_Sandfield_Road.md) to [Poole](../W/Woodridings.md), near [Bournemouth](../B/Bournemouth.md).
- **[24](24_July.md)** - Artist [Colleen Doran](../C/Colleen_Doran.md) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[2](2_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Robert Havard](../L/Letter_to_Robert_Havard.md).
- **[28](28_August.md)** - [Billy Boyd](../B/Billy_Boyd.md), who would play [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.
- **[30](30_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 305a](../L/Letter_305a.md).
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Ingrid Pridgeon](../L/Letter_to_Ingrid_Pridgeon.md).

## September {#September}

- **[4](4_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mary Fairburn](<../L/Letter_to_Mary_Fairburn_(4_September_1968).md>).
- **[29](29_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Billy Callahan](../L/Letter_to_Billy_Callahan.md).
- **[29](29_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Letter_to_Margaret_Carroux&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Letter to Margaret Carroux (page does not exist)">letter to</a> [Margaret Carroux](../M/Margaret_Carroux.md).
- **[29](29_September.md)** - *An Unexpected Party*, the first episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>), is aired on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).

## October {#October}

- **[6](6_October.md)** - *Out of the Frying-pan into the Fire*, the second episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>), is aired on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).
- **[9](9_October.md)** - Video game writer [František Fuka](../F/František_Fuka.md) is born.
- **[10](10_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mary Fairburn](<../L/Letter_to_Mary_Fairburn_(10_October_1968).md>).
- **[13](13_October.md)** - *Riddles in the Dark*, the third episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>), is aired on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md). [Stanley Unwin](../S/Stanley_Unwin.md) dies.
- **[16](16_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Niall Hoskin](../L/Letter_to_Niall_Hoskin.md).
- **[20](20_October.md)** - *Strange Lodgings*, the fourth episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>), is aired on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).
- **[27](27_October.md)** - *Barrels out of Bond*, the fifth episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>)*, is aired on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).*
- **[28](28_October.md)** - [Stephen Hunter](../S/Stephen_Hunter.md), who would play [Bombur](../B/Bombur.md) in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>), is born.

## November {#November}

- **[3](3_November.md)** - *A Warm Welcome*, the sixth episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>)*, is aired on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).*
- **[4](4_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mary Fairburn](<../L/Letter_to_Mary_Fairburn_(4_November_1968).md>).
- **[10](10_November.md)** - *The Gathering of the Clouds*, the seventh episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>)*, is aired on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).*
- **[14](14_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 307](../L/Letter_307.md).
- **[17](17_November.md)** - *The Clouds Burst*, the eighth and last episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>)*, is aired on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).*
- **On or before [29](29_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) has his secretary, [Joy Hill](../J/Joy_Hill.md), compile a list of unpublished works that includes *[The End of Bovadium](../T/The_End_of_Bovadium.md)*.

## December {#December}

- **[2](2_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter](<../L/Letter_to_Rayner_Unwin_(2_December_1968).md>) to [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md), commenting that, though *[The End of Bovadium](../T/The_End_of_Bovadium.md)* was on the list of unpublished works, he had "no intention of publishing" it to keep it from preventing him from working on *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.
- **[29](29_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 307a](../L/Letter_307a.md).

## ? {#?}

- **Late Summer** - [*Tolkien Journal* (Vol.3, No.3)](<../T/Tolkien_Journal_(Vol.3,_No.3).md>) is published.
- **?** - Tolkien subscribes to an appeal for the restoration of the [Sarehole Mill](../S/Sarehole_Mill.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **?** - After the death of his [father](../S/Stanley_Unwin.md), [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) becomes chairman of [Allen and Unwin](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md).
- **?** - [The Tolkien Society of Sweden](../T/The_Tolkien_Society_of_Sweden.md), the first European [Tolkien society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md), is founded.
- **?** - *[Tolkien and the Critics: Essays on J.R.R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings](../T/Tolkien_and_the_Critics.md)* is published.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mr David Masson](../L/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md#1960s).
- **?** - [Ken Rudolph Calendar 1969](../K/Ken_Rudolph_Calendar_1969.md) is released.
