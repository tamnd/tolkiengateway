[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968" title="wikipedia:1968">1968</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[4](/wiki/4_January "4 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Ken Jackson](</wiki/Letter_to_Ken_Jackson_(4_January_1968)> "Letter to Ken Jackson (4 January 1968)").
- **[8](/wiki/8_January "8 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) reads over *[The New Shadow](/wiki/The_New_Shadow)* and most likely starts typing a final typescript.
- **[29](/wiki/29_January "29 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Ken Jackson](</wiki/Letter_to_Ken_Jackson_(29_January_1968)> "Letter to Ken Jackson (29 January 1968)").

## February {#February}

- **[5](/wiki/5_February "5 February")-[9](/wiki/9_February "9 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is filmed for the documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](/wiki/Tolkien_in_Oxford)*.
- **[20](/wiki/20_February "20 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 300](/wiki/Letter_300 "Letter 300").
- **[29](/wiki/29_February "29 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 301](/wiki/Letter_301).

## March {#March}

- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Miss E. Byrne](</wiki/Letter_to_Miss_E._Byrne_(1_March_1968)> "Letter to Miss E. Byrne (1 March 1968)").
- **[17](/wiki/17_March "17 March")** - Author [Nataša Tučev](/wiki/Nata%C5%A1a_Tu%C4%8Dev) is born.
- **[20](/wiki/20_March "20 March")** - Actor and stuntman [Lawrence Makoare](/wiki/Lawrence_Makoare) is born.
- **[21](/wiki/21_March "21 March")** - Actor [Greg Ellis](/wiki/Greg_Ellis) is born.
- **[22](/wiki/22_March "22 March")** - *The Daily Telegraph* publishes an interview of Charlotte and Denis Plimmer with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), in the article "[The Man Who Understands Hobbits](/wiki/The_Man_Who_Understands_Hobbits)".
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Michael Reisz](/wiki/Michael_Reisz) , who would voice [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)")*, is born.
- **[30](/wiki/30_March "30 March")** – The documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](/wiki/Tolkien_in_Oxford)* is aired on the [BBC](/wiki/BBC).
- **?** - *[The Road Goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[15](/wiki/15_April "15 April")** - Actor [Richard Whiteside](/wiki/Richard_Whiteside) is born.

## May {#May}

- **[2](/wiki/2_May "2 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 302](/wiki/Letter_302 "Letter 302").
- **[3](/wiki/3_May "3 May")** - Author [Michael D.C. Drout](/wiki/Michael_D.C._Drout) is born.
- **[6](/wiki/6_May "6 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 303](/wiki/Letter_303 "Letter 303").
- **[24](/wiki/24_May "24 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to George Burke Johnston](/wiki/Letter_to_George_Burke_Johnston "Letter to George Burke Johnston").
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mary Fairburn](</wiki/Letter_to_Mary_Fairburn_(May_1968)> "Letter to Mary Fairburn (May 1968)").
- **?** - *[The Tolkien Relation](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Relation)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[4](/wiki/4_June "4 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 304](/wiki/Letter_304 "Letter 304").
- **[17](/wiki/17_June "17 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) falls down the stairs of [76 Sandfield Road](/wiki/76_Sandfield_Road) and breaks his leg.
- **[26](/wiki/26_June "26 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 305](/wiki/Letter_305 "Letter 305").

## July {#July}

- **Mid-July** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien") move from [76 Sandfield Road](/wiki/76_Sandfield_Road) to [Poole](/wiki/Woodridings "Woodridings"), near [Bournemouth](/wiki/Bournemouth).
- **[24](/wiki/24_July "24 July")** - Artist [Colleen Doran](/wiki/Colleen_Doran) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[2](/wiki/2_August "2 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Robert Havard](/wiki/Letter_to_Robert_Havard "Letter to Robert Havard").
- **[28](/wiki/28_August "28 August")** - [Billy Boyd](/wiki/Billy_Boyd), who would play [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.
- **[30](/wiki/30_August "30 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 305a](/wiki/Letter_305a "Letter 305a").
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Ingrid Pridgeon](/wiki/Letter_to_Ingrid_Pridgeon "Letter to Ingrid Pridgeon").

## September {#September}

- **[4](/wiki/4_September "4 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mary Fairburn](</wiki/Letter_to_Mary_Fairburn_(4_September_1968)> "Letter to Mary Fairburn (4 September 1968)").
- **[29](/wiki/29_September "29 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Billy Callahan](/wiki/Letter_to_Billy_Callahan "Letter to Billy Callahan").
- **[29](/wiki/29_September "29 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Letter_to_Margaret_Carroux&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Letter to Margaret Carroux (page does not exist)">letter to</a> [Margaret Carroux](/wiki/Margaret_Carroux).
- **[29](/wiki/29_September "29 September")** - *An Unexpected Party*, the first episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)"), is aired on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC").

## October {#October}

- **[6](/wiki/6_October "6 October")** - *Out of the Frying-pan into the Fire*, the second episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)"), is aired on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[9](/wiki/9_October "9 October")** - Video game writer [František Fuka](/wiki/Franti%C5%A1ek_Fuka) is born.
- **[10](/wiki/10_October "10 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mary Fairburn](</wiki/Letter_to_Mary_Fairburn_(10_October_1968)> "Letter to Mary Fairburn (10 October 1968)").
- **[13](/wiki/13_October "13 October")** - *Riddles in the Dark*, the third episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)"), is aired on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC"). [Stanley Unwin](/wiki/Stanley_Unwin) dies.
- **[16](/wiki/16_October "16 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Niall Hoskin](/wiki/Letter_to_Niall_Hoskin "Letter to Niall Hoskin").
- **[20](/wiki/20_October "20 October")** - *Strange Lodgings*, the fourth episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)"), is aired on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[27](/wiki/27_October "27 October")** - *Barrels out of Bond*, the fifth episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)")*, is aired on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC").*
- **[28](/wiki/28_October "28 October")** - [Stephen Hunter](/wiki/Stephen_Hunter), who would play [Bombur](/wiki/Bombur) in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), is born.

## November {#November}

- **[3](/wiki/3_November "3 November")** - *A Warm Welcome*, the sixth episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)")*, is aired on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC").*
- **[4](/wiki/4_November "4 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mary Fairburn](</wiki/Letter_to_Mary_Fairburn_(4_November_1968)> "Letter to Mary Fairburn (4 November 1968)").
- **[10](/wiki/10_November "10 November")** - *The Gathering of the Clouds*, the seventh episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)")*, is aired on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC").*
- **[14](/wiki/14_November "14 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 307](/wiki/Letter_307 "Letter 307").
- **[17](/wiki/17_November "17 November")** - *The Clouds Burst*, the eighth and last episode of the [1968 radio series of *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)")*, is aired on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC").*
- **On or before [29](/wiki/29_November "29 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) has his secretary, [Joy Hill](/wiki/Joy_Hill), compile a list of unpublished works that includes *[The End of Bovadium](/wiki/The_End_of_Bovadium)*.

## December {#December}

- **[2](/wiki/2_December "2 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter](</wiki/Letter_to_Rayner_Unwin_(2_December_1968)> "Letter to Rayner Unwin (2 December 1968)") to [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin), commenting that, though *[The End of Bovadium](/wiki/The_End_of_Bovadium)* was on the list of unpublished works, he had "no intention of publishing" it to keep it from preventing him from working on *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.
- **[29](/wiki/29_December "29 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 307a](/wiki/Letter_307a "Letter 307a").

## ? {#?}

- **Late Summer** - [*Tolkien Journal* (Vol.3, No.3)](</wiki/Tolkien_Journal_(Vol.3,_No.3)> "Tolkien Journal (Vol.3, No.3)") is published.
- **?** - Tolkien subscribes to an appeal for the restoration of the [Sarehole Mill](/wiki/Sarehole_Mill).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **?** - After the death of his [father](/wiki/Stanley_Unwin "Stanley Unwin"), [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) becomes chairman of [Allen and Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin).
- **?** - [The Tolkien Society of Sweden](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society_of_Sweden), the first European [Tolkien society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies"), is founded.
- **?** - *[Tolkien and the Critics: Essays on J.R.R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Tolkien_and_the_Critics "Tolkien and the Critics")* is published.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mr David Masson](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien#1960s "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien").
- **?** - [Ken Rudolph Calendar 1969](/wiki/Ken_Rudolph_Calendar_1969) is released.
