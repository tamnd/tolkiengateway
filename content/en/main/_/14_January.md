Events that occurred on 14 January.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1936](1936.md)** - In his Preface to *[Roman Britain and the English Settlements](../R/Roman_Britain_and_the_English_Settlements.md)*, R.G. Collingwood on this date writes about [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s contribution to the book.
- **[1938](1938.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) gives a talk about *Anglo-Saxon Verse* on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).
- **[1956](1956.md)** - Tolkien writes [Letter 180](../L/Letter_180.md).
- **[1956](1956.md)** - Tolkien writes a [a letter to Peter Alford](<../L/Letter_to_Peter_Alford_(14_January_1956).md>).
- **[1973](1973.md)** - Reader N.J. Kyle writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](../L/Letter_to_N.J._Kyle.md).
- **[2005](2005.md)** - The first "[The One Ring Celebration](../T/The_One_Ring_Celebration.md)" starts.
- **[2008](2008.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Ultimate Critical Review](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Ultimate_Critical_Review.md)* is released.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) travel through [Moria](../M/Moria.md) from mid-morning to evening. They spend the night in [Hall Twenty-one](../T/Twenty-first_Hall.md).
