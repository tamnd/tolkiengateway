Events that occurred on 14 January.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1936](/wiki/1936)** - In his Preface to *[Roman Britain and the English Settlements](/wiki/Roman_Britain_and_the_English_Settlements)*, R.G. Collingwood on this date writes about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s contribution to the book.
- **[1938](/wiki/1938)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) gives a talk about *Anglo-Saxon Verse* on [BBC Radio](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[1956](/wiki/1956)** - Tolkien writes [Letter 180](/wiki/Letter_180).
- **[1956](/wiki/1956)** - Tolkien writes a [a letter to Peter Alford](</wiki/Letter_to_Peter_Alford_(14_January_1956)> "Letter to Peter Alford (14 January 1956)").
- **[1973](/wiki/1973)** - Reader N.J. Kyle writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Letter_to_N.J._Kyle "Letter to N.J. Kyle").
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - The first "[The One Ring Celebration](/wiki/The_One_Ring_Celebration)" starts.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Ultimate Critical Review](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Ultimate_Critical_Review)* is released.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) travel through [Moria](/wiki/Moria) from mid-morning to evening. They spend the night in [Hall Twenty-one](/wiki/Twenty-first_Hall "Twenty-first Hall").
