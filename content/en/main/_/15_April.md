Events that occurred on 15 April.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1917](/wiki/1917)** - Voice actor [Hans Conried](/wiki/Hans_Conried) is born.
- **[1937](/wiki/1937)** - Charles Furth writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Charles_Furth_(25_April_1937)> "Letter to Charles Furth (25 April 1937)").
- **[1953](/wiki/1953)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) gives the [W.P. Ker Lecture](/wiki/W.P._Ker_Lecture) at Glasgow University on the religious themes adultery, temptation and repentance in <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight" title="Sir Gawain and the Green Knight">Sir Gawain and the Green Knight</a></i>.
- **[1954](/wiki/1954)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 143e](/wiki/Letter_143e).
- **[1962](/wiki/1962)** - Voice actor [Tom Kane](/wiki/Tom_Kane) is born.
- **[1968](/wiki/1968)** - Actor [Richard Whiteside](/wiki/Richard_Whiteside) is born.
- **[1979](/wiki/1979)** - Actor [Luke Evans](/wiki/Luke_Evans) is born.
- **[1998](/wiki/1998)** - *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)* is published in the US.<b></b>
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - *[The Art of The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Art_of_The_Two_Towers)* is published.
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - *[Reconsidering Tolkien](/wiki/Reconsidering_Tolkien)* is published.
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - *[Tolkien Studies: Volume 4](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_4)* is published.
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - The first day of [The Children of Húrin Release Party](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin_Release_Party) is hosted by [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway).
- **[2020](/wiki/2020)** - Voice actor [Sean Arnold](/wiki/Sean_Arnold) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"):
    - [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant):
        - The [Balchoth](/wiki/Balchoth) and a great host of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) attack the inferior host of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) on the [Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Field_of_Celebrant).
        - All seems lost for Gondor until the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) under leadership of [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) come to its aid.
        - Death of [Borondir Udalraph](/wiki/Borondir "Borondir").
        - Together with the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) is victorious and [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion), steward of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), gives [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) to [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) which becomes the later [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).
