## January {#January}

- **[20](20_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) starts supervising [Brian Miller](../B/Brian_Miller.md)'s work on the [Middle English](../M/Middle_English.md) *Dame Sirith*.

## February {#February}

- **[7](7_February.md)** - The [Inklings](../I/Inklings.md) have dinner at [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md)' house, in the company of Dr. Firor of John Hopkins University. The evening was described by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) as *"like a glimpse of old times: quiet and rational (since [Hugo](../H/Hugo_Dyson.md) was not asked)."*
- **[10](10_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 132](../L/Letter_132.md).
- **[11](11_February.md)** - Actor [Paul Norell](../P/Paul_Norell.md) is born.
- **[19](19_February.md)** - Actor [Geoff Morrell](../G/Geoff_Morrell.md) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[23](23_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 132a](../L/Letter_132a.md).

## April {#April}

- **[30](30_April.md)** - Brazilian translator [Ronald Kyrmse](../R/Ronald_Kyrmse.md) is born.

## May {#May}

- **[27](27_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) acts as host to the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Oxford_Dante_Society&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Oxford Dante Society (page does not exist)">Oxford Dante Society</a>.

## June {#June}

- **?** - [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) writes a letter to Tolkien, enquiring about *[Errantry](../E/Errantry.md)* and the publication efforts of his works.
- **[22](22_June.md)** - After being turned down by [Collins](../H/HarperCollins.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) [writes](../L/Letter_133.md) to [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md), saying he would prefer to have just the *Lord of the Rings* published than nothing at all.

## July {#July}

- **[1](1_July.md)** - [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) replies to Tolkien, expressing his interest in reading the finished *Lord of the Rings* and discussing the publishing of the *[Silmarillion](../S/Silmarillion.md)* later,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> ending their earlier dispute.
- **[1](1_July.md)** - Musician [David Arkenstone](../D/David_Arkenstone.md) is born.
- **?** - Actor [Brian George](../B/Brian_George.md) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[7](7_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to George and Moira Sayer](<../L/Letter_to_George_and_Moira_Sayer_(7_August_1952).md>).
- **[10](10_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Moira Sayer](<../L/Letter_to_Moira_Sayer_(10_August_1952).md>).
- **[11](11_August.md)–[18](18_August.md)** - Tolkien visits [Cambridge](../C/Cambridge.md) to view the *Ancrene Wisse* manuscript.
- **[29](29_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 134](../L/Letter_134.md).
- **[30](30_August.md)** - Translator [Helmut W. Pesch](../H/Helmut_W._Pesch.md) is born.
- **Late** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) visits [George Sayer](../G/George_Sayer.md). Sayer makes [an audio recording](1952_tape_recording.md) of Tolkien singing and reading excerpts from his works (e.g., [Namárië](../N/Namárië.md)).

## October {#October}

- **[17](17_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is appointed supervisor of [T.P. Dunning](../T/T.P._Dunning.md)'s thesis.
- **[21](21_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 134a](../L/Letter_134a.md).
- **[24](24_October.md)** - Tolkien writes [letter 135](../L/Letter_135.md).

## November {#November}

- **[12](12_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 135a](../L/Letter_135a.md).
- **[13](13_November.md)** - [C. S. Lewis](../C/C._S._Lewis.md) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

## December {#December}

- **[28](28_December.md)** - Game developer [Wendell Lariston Hill, Jr.](../W/Wendell_Lariston_Hill,_Jr..md) is born

## ? {#?}

- **?** (Fall) - Retreating to [Michael Tolkien](../M/Michael_Tolkien.md)'s home at Woodcote, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) rereads, revises and corrects *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **?** - Artist [Stephen Raw](../S/Stephen_Raw.md), who would be commissioned to redraw [the maps](../../category/I/Images_by_Stephen_Raw.md) for the revised [1994](1994.md) edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, is born.
- **?** - [Ballantine Books](../B/Ballantine_Books.md) is founded.
