## January {#January}

- **[20](/wiki/20_January "20 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) starts supervising [Brian Miller](/wiki/Brian_Miller)'s work on the [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) *Dame Sirith*.

## February {#February}

- **[7](/wiki/7_February "7 February")** - The [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings) have dinner at [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis)' house, in the company of Dr. Firor of John Hopkins University. The evening was described by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") as *"like a glimpse of old times: quiet and rational (since [Hugo](/wiki/Hugo_Dyson "Hugo Dyson") was not asked)."*
- **[10](/wiki/10_February "10 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 132](/wiki/Letter_132 "Letter 132").
- **[11](/wiki/11_February "11 February")** - Actor [Paul Norell](/wiki/Paul_Norell) is born.
- **[19](/wiki/19_February "19 February")** - Actor [Geoff Morrell](/wiki/Geoff_Morrell) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[23](/wiki/23_March "23 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 132a](/wiki/Letter_132a).

## April {#April}

- **[30](/wiki/30_April "30 April")** - Brazilian translator [Ronald Kyrmse](/wiki/Ronald_Kyrmse) is born.

## May {#May}

- **[27](/wiki/27_April "27 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) acts as host to the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Oxford_Dante_Society&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Oxford Dante Society (page does not exist)">Oxford Dante Society</a>.

## June {#June}

- **?** - [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) writes a letter to Tolkien, enquiring about *[Errantry](/wiki/Errantry)* and the publication efforts of his works.
- **[22](/wiki/22_June "22 June")** - After being turned down by [Collins](/wiki/HarperCollins "HarperCollins"), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) [writes](/wiki/Letter_133 "Letter 133") to [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin), saying he would prefer to have just the *Lord of the Rings* published than nothing at all.

## July {#July}

- **[1](/wiki/1_July "1 July")** - [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) replies to Tolkien, expressing his interest in reading the finished *Lord of the Rings* and discussing the publishing of the *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)* later,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> ending their earlier dispute.
- **[1](/wiki/1_July "1 July")** - Musician [David Arkenstone](/wiki/David_Arkenstone) is born.
- **?** - Actor [Brian George](/wiki/Brian_George) is born.

## August {#August}

- **[7](/wiki/7_August "7 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to George and Moira Sayer](</wiki/Letter_to_George_and_Moira_Sayer_(7_August_1952)> "Letter to George and Moira Sayer (7 August 1952)").
- **[10](/wiki/10_August "10 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Moira Sayer](</wiki/Letter_to_Moira_Sayer_(10_August_1952)> "Letter to Moira Sayer (10 August 1952)").
- **[11](/wiki/11_August "11 August")–[18](/wiki/18_August "18 August")** - Tolkien visits [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge) to view the *Ancrene Wisse* manuscript.
- **[29](/wiki/29_August "29 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 134](/wiki/Letter_134 "Letter 134").
- **[30](/wiki/30_August "30 August")** - Translator [Helmut W. Pesch](/wiki/Helmut_W._Pesch) is born.
- **Late** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) visits [George Sayer](/wiki/George_Sayer). Sayer makes [an audio recording](/wiki/1952_tape_recording "1952 tape recording") of Tolkien singing and reading excerpts from his works (e.g., [Namárië](/wiki/Nam%C3%A1ri%C3%AB)).

## October {#October}

- **[17](/wiki/17_October "17 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is appointed supervisor of [T.P. Dunning](/wiki/T.P._Dunning)'s thesis.
- **[21](/wiki/21_October "21 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 134a](/wiki/Letter_134a).
- **[24](/wiki/24_October "24 October")** - Tolkien writes [letter 135](/wiki/Letter_135 "Letter 135").

## November {#November}

- **[12](/wiki/12_November "12 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 135a](/wiki/Letter_135a).
- **[13](/wiki/13_November "13 November")** - [C. S. Lewis](/wiki/C._S._Lewis) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

## December {#December}

- **[28](/wiki/28_December "28 December")** - Game developer [Wendell Lariston Hill, Jr.](/wiki/Wendell_Lariston_Hill,_Jr.) is born

## ? {#?}

- **?** (Fall) - Retreating to [Michael Tolkien](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien)'s home at Woodcote, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) rereads, revises and corrects *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **?** - Artist [Stephen Raw](/wiki/Stephen_Raw), who would be commissioned to redraw [the maps](/wiki/Category:Images_by_Stephen_Raw "Category:Images by Stephen Raw") for the revised [1994](/wiki/1994) edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, is born.
- **?** - [Ballantine Books](/wiki/Ballantine_Books) is founded.
