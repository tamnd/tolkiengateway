Events that occurred on 3 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1943](1943.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 51a](../L/Letter_51a.md).
- **[1968](1968.md)** - *A Warm Welcome*, the sixth episode of the [1968 radio series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, is aired on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).
- **[2004](2004.md)** - "Shadows", the eleventh set in *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md)*, is released.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - Author [Glen GoodKnight](../G/Glen_GoodKnight.md) dies.
- <b><a href="2016.md" title="2016">2016</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun.md" title="The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun">The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[2016](2016.md)** - The <a class="external text" href="https://www.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/home" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Bodleian Library</a> presented the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodley_Medal" title="wikipedia:Bodley Medal">Bodley Medal</a> to [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), the Library's highest honour.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md):
    - [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), guided by [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md), travels to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) in order to deliver [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md)'s message to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) rest in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).
    - First quarter<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
        - Passing of [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) and death of [Gríma Wormtongue](../G/Gríma.md)
        - End of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md)
