<i><b>-ro</b></i> or <i><b>-r</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) affix, used to create an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agentive_ending" title="wikipedia:Agentive ending">agentive ending</a> (to create a noun from a verb; compare English *speaker*/*speak*).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HF_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HF-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[ista-](/wiki/Ista- "Ista-")* ("know") / <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Istari" title="Istari">ista<b>r</b></a></i> ("wizard", "knower")<sup id="cite_ref-HF_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HF-2">[2]</a></sup>
- *envinyata-* ("renew"; unattested)<sup id="cite_ref-HF_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HF-2">[2]</a></sup> / <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Envinyatar" title="Envinyatar">Envinyata<b>r</b></a></i> ("Renewer")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
