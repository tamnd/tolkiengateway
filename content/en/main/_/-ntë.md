<i><b>-ntë</b></i> is a pronominal [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) ending, inflexion of 3 plural ("they") when no subject is previously mentioned. (Seen in *Círion's Oath*.)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
