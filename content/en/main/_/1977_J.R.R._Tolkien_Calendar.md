The <i><b>1977 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar</b></i> was the official Tolkien calendar published by [Ballantine Books](/wiki/Ballantine_Books) in [1976](/wiki/1976). It features art by [The Brothers Hildebrandt](/wiki/The_Brothers_Hildebrandt) based on *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
