**Éomund** was the high ranking captain of the [Éothéod](Éothéod.md).

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md), he rode with his lord [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl.md) to the aid of the [Gondorians](../G/Gondor.md) in the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](../B/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md). After the victory there against the [Balchoth](../B/Balchoth.md), Éomund witnessed the [Oath](../O/Oath_of_Eorl.md) Eorl vowed to [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md), the [Steward of Gondor](../S/Steward_of_Gondor.md), and helped organise the newly founded [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The first element, *eoh*, is a frequently used Old English translation of genuine [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md) *logo-*, "(war)horse". The second element means "protection, protector". <span></span>
