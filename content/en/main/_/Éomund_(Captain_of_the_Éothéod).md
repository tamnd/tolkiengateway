**Éomund** was the high ranking captain of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od).

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"), he rode with his lord [Eorl the Young](/wiki/Eorl "Eorl") to the aid of the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") in the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant). After the victory there against the [Balchoth](/wiki/Balchoth), Éomund witnessed the [Oath](/wiki/Oath_of_Eorl "Oath of Eorl") Eorl vowed to [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion), the [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor), and helped organise the newly founded [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The first element, *eoh*, is a frequently used Old English translation of genuine [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) *logo-*, "(war)horse". The second element means "protection, protector". <span></span>
