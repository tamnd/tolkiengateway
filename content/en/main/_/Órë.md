<i><b>óre</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning:

1. "heart (inner mind)"<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
2. "to urge"<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
3. "rising" (noun)<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[2]</a></sup>

### Other forms {#Other_forms}

- (as noun) *órenya* "my heart"<sup id="cite_ref-VT41:11_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT41:11-4">[3]</a></sup>
- (as verb) *Óre* "(it) urges"<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- *Óre nin* "I am urged"<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

In the manuscript "[Notes on Óre](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_41 "Vinyar Tengwar 41")", *óre* ("heart (inner mind)") derives from the [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=%C6%B7OR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ƷOR (page does not exist)">ƷOR</a>. The word is also said to be associated with the root [OR](/wiki/OR), although probably not "semantically connected".<sup id="cite_ref-VT41:11_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT41:11-4">[3]</a></sup>

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), the word *óre* ("rising") derives from root [ORO](/wiki/OR#Other_versions "OR").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Tengwa {#Tengwa}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ore.gif"><img src="/w/images/4/48/Ore.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Óre* is also the name in Quenya of the twenty-first letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the first letter of the sixth grade or [Tyellë](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB), which contains "semi-vocalic" consonants and it is the sixth letter in the dental or *t*-series of consonants, the *[Tincotéma](/wiki/Tincot%C3%A9ma)*. In most modes this letter represents weak or untrilled **R** (usually used medially or at the end of words).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> However, in the [Mode of Beleriand](/wiki/Mode_of_Beleriand), as in the inscription on the [West-gate](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin "Doors of Durin") of [Moria](/wiki/Moria), this letter represents **N**.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Ar</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>
