**Óswine**, the **Prince of Gwar**, was the ruler of the city of [Kortirion](/wiki/Kortirion#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> in [Lúthien](/wiki/Luthany#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Luthany") (i.e. <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in the text called *[Ælfwine of England](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine_of_England)* from *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Osw_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Osw-3">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Óswine was a "lover of songs", and was friendly to the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) that still remained in Lúthien, congregating mostly in the lands surrounding Kortirion, called [Alalminórë](/wiki/Alalmin%C3%B3r%C3%AB) or the "Land of [Elms](/wiki/Elms)". In his city also dwelt [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine), who would later discover [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), as well as his parents, the [minstrel](/wiki/Minstrels "Minstrels") [Déor](</wiki/D%C3%A9or_(father_of_%C3%86lfwine)> "Déor (father of Ælfwine)") and [Éadgifu](/wiki/%C3%89adgifu) of [Lionesse](/wiki/Lionesse).

He was killed during the siege of Kortirion by the fierce people called the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Forodwaith_(The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Forodwaith (The Book of Lost Tales) (page does not exist)">Forodwaith</a> (that is, the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikings" title="wikipedia:Vikings">Vikings</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-Osw_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Osw-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Óswine* is in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English), consisting of the elements *ós* ("god") + *wine* ("friend").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

For the explanation of the name *Gwar* in Óswine's title, *Prince of Gwar*, see: [Kortirion#Etymology and names](/wiki/Kortirion#Etymology_and_names "Kortirion").
