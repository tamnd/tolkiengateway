**Óswine**, the **Prince of Gwar**, was the ruler of the city of [Kortirion](../K/Kortirion.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> in [Lúthien](../L/Luthany.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium) (i.e. <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in the text called *[Ælfwine of England](Ælfwine_of_England.md)* from *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Osw_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Osw-3">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Óswine was a "lover of songs", and was friendly to the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) that still remained in Lúthien, congregating mostly in the lands surrounding Kortirion, called [Alalminórë](../A/Alalminórë.md) or the "Land of [Elms](../E/Elms.md)". In his city also dwelt [Ælfwine](Ælfwine.md), who would later discover [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md), as well as his parents, the [minstrel](../M/Minstrels.md) [Déor](<../D/Déor_(father_of_Ælfwine).md>) and [Éadgifu](Éadgifu.md) of [Lionesse](../L/Lionesse.md).

He was killed during the siege of Kortirion by the fierce people called the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Forodwaith_(The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Forodwaith (The Book of Lost Tales) (page does not exist)">Forodwaith</a> (that is, the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vikings" title="wikipedia:Vikings">Vikings</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-Osw_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Osw-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Óswine* is in [Old English](../O/Old_English.md), consisting of the elements *ós* ("god") + *wine* ("friend").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

For the explanation of the name *Gwar* in Óswine's title, *Prince of Gwar*, see: [Kortirion#Etymology and names](../K/Kortirion.md#Etymology_and_names).
