[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1926" title="wikipedia:1926">1926</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** – [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon) replaces [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in the Chair of English Language at the University of Leeds.

## March {#March}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s "Philology: General Works" is published in *[The Year's Work in English Studies](/wiki/The_Year%27s_Work_in_English_Studies)*.

## April {#April}

- **[29](/wiki/29_April "29 April")** - [Leonard Fenton](/wiki/Leonard_Fenton), who would voice [the Elvenking](/wiki/Thranduil "Thranduil") in the radio play of *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)")* and [Daddy Twofoot](/wiki/Daddy_Twofoot) in *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)")*, is born.

## May {#May}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) meets [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), who will have a lasting impact on his works.

## June {#June}

- **[25](/wiki/25_June "25 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a letter to W.G. Harding.

## September {#September}

- **[7](/wiki/7_September "7 September")** - [Don Messick](/wiki/Don_Messick), who would voice [Balin](/wiki/Balin) and the [Lord of the Eagles](/wiki/Gwaihir "Gwaihir") in [The Hobbit (1977 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)>), is born.

## October {#October}

- **[21](/wiki/21_October "21 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Willard G. Harding](</wiki/Letter_to_Willard_G._Harding_(21_October_1926)> "Letter to Willard G. Harding (21 October 1926)").

## December {#December}

- **[26](/wiki/26_December "26 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Coalbiters&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Coalbiters (page does not exist)">The Coalbiters</a> is formed, a faculty club who read Old Norse.
