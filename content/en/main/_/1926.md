[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1926" title="wikipedia:1926">1926</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](1_January.md)** – [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md) replaces [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in the Chair of English Language at the University of Leeds.

## March {#March}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s "Philology: General Works" is published in *[The Year's Work in English Studies](../T/The_Year's_Work_in_English_Studies.md)*.

## April {#April}

- **[29](29_April.md)** - [Leonard Fenton](../L/Leonard_Fenton.md), who would voice [the Elvenking](../T/Thranduil.md) in the radio play of *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>)* and [Daddy Twofoot](../D/Daddy_Twofoot.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>)*, is born.

## May {#May}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) meets [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md), who will have a lasting impact on his works.

## June {#June}

- **[25](25_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a letter to W.G. Harding.

## September {#September}

- **[7](7_September.md)** - [Don Messick](../D/Don_Messick.md), who would voice [Balin](../B/Balin.md) and the [Lord of the Eagles](../G/Gwaihir.md) in [The Hobbit (1977 film)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>), is born.

## October {#October}

- **[21](21_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Willard G. Harding](<../L/Letter_to_Willard_G._Harding_(21_October_1926).md>).

## December {#December}

- **[26](26_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Coalbiters&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Coalbiters (page does not exist)">The Coalbiters</a> is formed, a faculty club who read Old Norse.
