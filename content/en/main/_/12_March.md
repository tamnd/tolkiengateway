Events that occurred on 12 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1927](1927.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Ronald Ashton](../L/Letter_to_Ronald_Ashton.md).
- **[1941](1941.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 43a](../L/Letter_43a.md).
- **[1945](1945.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 97c](../L/Letter_97c.md).
- **[1958](1958.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) receives [Index questions](../I/Index_questions.md) from [Nancy Smith](../N/Nancy_Smith.md).
- **[1966](1966.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to John Bush](<../L/Letter_to_John_Bush_(12_March_1966).md>).
- **[2003](2003.md)** - "Battle of Helm's Deep", the fifth set in *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md)*, is released.
- **[2012](2012.md)** - [Update 6: Shores of the Great River](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md#Update_6:_Shores_of_the_Great_River), the sixth free update of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)*, is released.
- **[2015](2015.md)** - [Terry Pratchett](../T/Terry_Pratchett.md) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md):
    - [Borondir](../B/Borondir.md) and his companion are on their way in search of the [Éothéod](Éothéod.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) leads [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) into [Shelob's Lair](../S/Shelob's_Lair.md).
    - [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) has to retreat to the [Causeway Forts](../C/Causeway_Forts.md).
    - The [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) travel towards [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). They camp under [Min-Rimmon](../M/Min-Rimmon.md).
    - [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) drives the enemy towards [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md).
    - The [Ents](../E/Ents.md) overcome the invaders of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).
