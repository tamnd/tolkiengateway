[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1943" title="wikipedia:1943">1943</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[11](/wiki/11_January "11 January")** - [Michael George Tolkien](/wiki/Michael_George_Tolkien), grandson of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is born.
- **[29](/wiki/29_January "29 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to J.L.N. O'Loughlin](</wiki/Letter_to_J.L.N._O%27Loughlin_(29_January_1943)> "Letter to J.L.N. O'Loughlin (29 January 1943)").

## April {#April}

- **[10](/wiki/10_April "10 April")** - [Bo Hansson](/wiki/Bo_Hansson), who will become famous for his music inspired by *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, is born.
- **[20](/wiki/20_April "20 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 48](/wiki/Letter_48 "Letter 48").

## July {#July}

- **?** - [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) joins the Royal Air Force.
- **[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 49a](/wiki/Letter_49a).

## August {#August}

- **[3](/wiki/3_August "3 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Leila Keene and Pat Kirke](/wiki/Letter_to_Leila_Keene_and_Pat_Kirke "Letter to Leila Keene and Pat Kirke").
- **[10](/wiki/10_August "10 August")** - Actor [Peter McKenzie](/wiki/Peter_McKenzie) is born.
- **[16](/wiki/16_August "16 August")** - Actress [Sharon Baird](/wiki/Sharon_Baird) is born.
- **[20](/wiki/20_August "20 August")** - Actor [Sylvester McCoy](/wiki/Sylvester_McCoy) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[1](/wiki/1_September "1 September")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 49b](/wiki/Letter_49b).
- **[8](/wiki/8_September "8 September")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 49c](/wiki/Letter_49c).
- **[9](/wiki/9_September "9 September")** - Renowned scholar [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey) is born.
- **[13](/wiki/13_September "13 September")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 49d](/wiki/Letter_49d).
- **[19](/wiki/19_September "19 September")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 49e](/wiki/Letter_49e).

## October {#October}

- **[25](/wiki/25_October "25 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 50](/wiki/Letter_50 "Letter 50").
- **[27](/wiki/27_October "27 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 51](/wiki/Letter_51 "Letter 51").
- **[31](/wiki/31_October "31 October")** - Writer [J.E.A. Tyler](/wiki/J.E.A._Tyler) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[3](/wiki/3_November "3 November")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 51a](/wiki/Letter_51a).
- **[10](/wiki/10_November "10 November")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 51b](/wiki/Letter_51b).
- **[29](/wiki/29_November "29 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 52](/wiki/Letter_52 "Letter 52").
- **Late November** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the poem *[A Closed Letter to Andrea Charicoryides Surnamed Polygrapheus, Logothete of the Theme of Geodesia in the Empire, Bard of the Court of Camelot, Malleus Malitiarium, Inclinga Sum Sometimes Known as Charles Williams](/wiki/A_Closed_Letter_to_Andrea_Charicoryides_Surnamed_Polygrapheus,_Logothete_of_the_Theme_of_Geodesia_in_the_Empire,_Bard_of_the_Court_of_Camelot,_Malleus_Malitiarium,_Inclinga_Sum_Sometimes_Known_as_Charles_Williams)*.

## December {#December}

- **[9](/wiki/9_December "9 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 53](/wiki/Letter_53 "Letter 53").
- **[15](/wiki/15_December "15 December")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 53a](/wiki/Letter_53a).
- **[25](/wiki/25_December "25 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes his last letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **[31](/wiki/31_December "31 December")** - Actor [Victor Raider-Wexler](/wiki/Victor_Raider-Wexler) is born.

## Unknown {#Unknown}

- **Early**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> - [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) draws a large-scale map of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") that would many years later be the basis for *[A Part of the Shire](/wiki/A_Part_of_the_Shire)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>
