The **ñaltalma** was an [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") device of communication, used for signalling from afar, reflecting the light of the [sun](/wiki/Sun "Sun") or the clear [moon](/wiki/Moon "Moon").

## History {#History}

Legends said that the device, as many other such inventions, was invented by [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) himself, brought to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) by the [exiled Noldor](/wiki/Exiled_Noldor "Exiled Noldor"), and adopted by the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar). However it is probably far older, as there is no definite record that it was one of the many things the Sindar learned from the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor).<sup id="cite_ref-Ñaltalma_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ñaltalma-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

In the later [Ages](/wiki/Ages), the history of its origins, as well as details of its form and usage, have been lost from records.<sup id="cite_ref-Ñaltalma_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ñaltalma-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

Details about the device's form and usage are lost in history. It can be discerned at least that it evidently was portable (could be carried in pocket or pouch) and had a bright surface to use the light of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) or the clear [Moon](/wiki/Moon), and direct its flashes to an intended watcher; however the flashes it generated/reflected were not visible by [Men](/wiki/Men) (at least from distance).<sup id="cite_ref-Ñaltalma_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ñaltalma-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ñaltalma* is not glossed, but it is clearly [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), containing the element *[ñalta](/wiki/%C3%91alta "Ñalta")* ("radiance, glittering reflection").

Its [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name was <i><b>glathralvas</b></i> ("flashing glass/crystal").<sup id="cite_ref-Ñaltalma_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ñaltalma-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) describes the object as a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heliograph" title="wikipedia:Heliograph">heliograph</a>, a common communication device in the British Army. As [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) comments, Tolkien had to be well familiarized with the heliograph, as he trained in military signalling and became Battalion Signalling Officer in [1916](/wiki/1916), although the own manual of his training described the heliograph as not suitable for Great Britain's atmosphere.<sup id="cite_ref-Ñaltalma_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ñaltalma-1">[1]</a></sup>
