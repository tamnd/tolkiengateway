**3 Manor Road** in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford) was the home of [Ronald](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien"), and [Priscilla Tolkien](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien) from [1947](/wiki/1947)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> to [1950](/wiki/1950). They moved to this "tiny terraced house",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> owned by [Merton College](/wiki/Merton_College), when their previous home, the house at [20 Northmoor Road](/wiki/20_Northmoor_Road) became too big and costly to maintain after the boys had moved out.

But quite soon they found their new home too small: there was no room that Tolkien could use as a study. Tolkien stated that he typed out *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* (once) and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* (twice) while sitting on a bed in the attic.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Merton College promised to offer Tolkien a larger house as soon as one became available. In 1950 the Tolkiens moved to [99 Holywell Street](/wiki/99_Holywell_Street).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Theologian <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Farrer" title="wikipedia:Austin Farrer">Austin Farrer</a> and his wife [Katharine](/wiki/Katharine_Farrer "Katharine Farrer") lived in number 7 of the same street,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and after the Tolkiens moved, Ronald kept correspondence with them, especially with Katharine.
