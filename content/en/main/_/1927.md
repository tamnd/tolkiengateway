[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1927" title="wikipedia:1927">1927</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s "Philology: General Works" is published in *[The Year's Work in English Studies](../T/The_Year's_Work_in_English_Studies.md)*.

## March {#March}

- **[12](12_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Ronald Ashton](../L/Letter_to_Ronald_Ashton.md).

## June {#June}

- **?** - *[Adventures in Unnatural History and Medieval Metres](../A/Adventures_in_Unnatural_History_and_Medieval_Metres,_being_the_Freaks_of_Fisiologus.md)* is published in *The Stapeldon Magazine*.

## September {#September}

- **[25](25_September.md)** - Producer and interviewer [Denys Gueroult](../D/Denys_Gueroult.md) is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) paints a [coiled dragon](../../file/J/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Dragon.jpg.md) derived from the poem *[Beowulf](<../B/Beowulf_(poem).md>)*.
- **?** - *[The Marvellous Land of Snergs](../T/The_Marvellous_Land_of_Snergs.md)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[22](22_October.md)** - [James Grout](../J/James_Grout.md), who would voice [Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) and [Nokes](../N/Nokes.md) in [the *Smith of Wootton Major* episode](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>), is born.

## December {#December}

- **[21](21_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[The Nameless Land](../T/The_Song_of_Ælfwine.md)*, a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published in *[Realities: An Anthology of Verse](../R/Realities%253A_An_Anthology_of_Verse.md)*
- **?** - [Edith Tolkien](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) gives her husband [a desk](../D/Desk_certificate_27_July_1972.md), on which he will write *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.
