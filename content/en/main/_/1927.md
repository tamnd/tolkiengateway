[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1927" title="wikipedia:1927">1927</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s "Philology: General Works" is published in *[The Year's Work in English Studies](/wiki/The_Year%27s_Work_in_English_Studies)*.

## March {#March}

- **[12](/wiki/12_March "12 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Ronald Ashton](/wiki/Letter_to_Ronald_Ashton "Letter to Ronald Ashton").

## June {#June}

- **?** - *[Adventures in Unnatural History and Medieval Metres](/wiki/Adventures_in_Unnatural_History_and_Medieval_Metres,_being_the_Freaks_of_Fisiologus "Adventures in Unnatural History and Medieval Metres, being the Freaks of Fisiologus")* is published in *The Stapeldon Magazine*.

## September {#September}

- **[25](/wiki/25_September "25 September")** - Producer and interviewer [Denys Gueroult](/wiki/Denys_Gueroult) is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) paints a [coiled dragon](/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Dragon.jpg "File:J.R.R. Tolkien - Dragon.jpg") derived from the poem *[Beowulf](</wiki/Beowulf_(poem)> "Beowulf (poem)")*.
- **?** - *[The Marvellous Land of Snergs](/wiki/The_Marvellous_Land_of_Snergs)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[22](/wiki/22_October "22 October")** - [James Grout](/wiki/James_Grout), who would voice [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur) in [*The Lord of the Rings* radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") and [Nokes](/wiki/Nokes) in [the *Smith of Wootton Major* episode](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)"), is born.

## December {#December}

- **[21](/wiki/21_December "21 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[The Nameless Land](/wiki/The_Song_of_%C3%86lfwine "The Song of Ælfwine")*, a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published in *[Realities: An Anthology of Verse](/wiki/Realities:_An_Anthology_of_Verse)*
- **?** - [Edith Tolkien](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien) gives her husband [a desk](/wiki/Desk_certificate_27_July_1972 "Desk certificate 27 July 1972"), on which he will write *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
