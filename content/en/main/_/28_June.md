Events that occurred on 28 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1915](1915.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) applies at the [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md) recruiting office to join the [army](../W/World_War_I.md).
- **[1915](1915.md)** - Scholar and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) [Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](../P/Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski.md) is born.
- **[1922](1922.md)** - Actor [Erik Bauersfeld](../E/Erik_Bauersfeld.md) is born.
- **[1919](1919.md)** - The Treaty of Versailles is signed and the First World War officially ends.
- **[1981](1981.md)** - The seventeenth episode of [the 1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- **[1982](1982.md)** - Artist [Ray Dillon](../R/Ray_Dillon.md) is born.
- **[2000](2000.md)** – The [Tolkien Ensemble](../T/Tolkien_Ensemble.md) publish their second album, *[A Night in Rivendell](../A/A_Night_in_Rivendell.md)*.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - [*The Inklings of Oxford: C. S. Lewis, J. R. R. Tolkien, and Their Friends*](../T/The_Inklings_of_Oxford.md) is published.
