Events that occurred on 24 May.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1932](1932.md)** - [R.W. Chambers](../R/R.W._Chambers.md) sends [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) an inscribed copy of *[Beowulf: An Introduction to the Study of the Poem](../B/Beowulf%253A_An_Introduction_to_the_Study_of_the_Poem.md)*.
- **[1968](1968.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to George Burke Johnston](../L/Letter_to_George_Burke_Johnston.md).
- **[1973](1973.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is [interviewed](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) concerning the election to the Oxford Poetry chair.
- **[1973](1973.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 350](../L/Letter_350.md).
- **[1981](1981.md)** - The twelfth episode of the [1981 radio adaptation](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- **[1992](1992.md)** - [The Tolkien Phenomenon](../T/The_Tolkien_Phenomenon.md) ends in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turku" title="wikipedia:Turku">Turku</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland" title="wikipedia:Finland">Finland</a>.
- **[1994](1994.md)** - [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md) [John Wain](../J/John_Wain.md) dies.
- **[2004](2004.md)** - *[Understanding The Lord of the Rings](../U/Understanding_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is published.
- **[2014](2014.md)** - *[Beowulf Launch Party](../B/Beowulf_Launch_Party.md) hosted by [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) and [Middle-earth Network](../M/Middle-earth_Network.md) with support from Tolkien Brasil and The Tolkienist.*
- **[2024](2024.md)** - The online videogame *[The Lord of the Rings: Heroes of Middle-earth](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Heroes_of_Middle-earth.md)* is discontinued online.
