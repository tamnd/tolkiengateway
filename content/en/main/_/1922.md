[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1922" title="wikipedia:1922">1922</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes an entry in [his diary](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_diaries.md) on the coming of [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md) to the [University of Leeds](../U/University_of_Leeds.md).

## February {#February}

- **[10](10_February.md)** - [Árpád Göncz](Árpád_Göncz.md), translator and former President of Hungary, is born.

## May {#May}

- **[11](11_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s *[A Middle English Vocabulary](../A/A_Middle_English_Vocabulary.md)* is published.
- **[27](27_May.md)** - Actor [Christopher Lee](../C/Christopher_Lee.md) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[28](28_June.md)** - Actor [Erik Bauersfeld](../E/Erik_Bauersfeld.md) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[28](28_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) agrees to review *[Beowulf: An Introduction to the Study of the Poem](../B/Beowulf%253A_An_Introduction_to_the_Study_of_the_Poem.md)*.

## September {#September}

- **[9](9_September.md)** - [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s favourite artist, [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md), is born.

## November {#November}

- **[1](1_November.md)** - Actor [John Westbrook](../J/John_Westbrook.md) is born.

## December {#December}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem *[The Clerkes Compleinte](../T/The_Clerkes_Compleinte.md)* is published in [*The Gryphon*, Vol.4 No.3](../T/The_Gryphon,_Vol.4_No.3.md).

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien begins work on the new edition of *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight](<../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(edition).md>)* with [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md).
