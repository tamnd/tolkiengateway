[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1922" title="wikipedia:1922">1922</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes an entry in [his diary](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_diaries "J.R.R. Tolkien's diaries") on the coming of [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon) to the [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds).

## February {#February}

- **[10](/wiki/10_February "10 February")** - [Árpád Göncz](/wiki/%C3%81rp%C3%A1d_G%C3%B6ncz), translator and former President of Hungary, is born.

## May {#May}

- **[11](/wiki/11_May "11 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[A Middle English Vocabulary](/wiki/A_Middle_English_Vocabulary)* is published.
- **[27](/wiki/27_May "27 May")** - Actor [Christopher Lee](/wiki/Christopher_Lee) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[28](/wiki/28_June "28 June")** - Actor [Erik Bauersfeld](/wiki/Erik_Bauersfeld) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) agrees to review *[Beowulf: An Introduction to the Study of the Poem](/wiki/Beowulf:_An_Introduction_to_the_Study_of_the_Poem)*.

## September {#September}

- **[9](/wiki/9_September "9 September")** - [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s favourite artist, [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes), is born.

## November {#November}

- **[1](/wiki/1_November "1 November")** - Actor [John Westbrook](/wiki/John_Westbrook) is born.

## December {#December}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem *[The Clerkes Compleinte](/wiki/The_Clerkes_Compleinte)* is published in [*The Gryphon*, Vol.4 No.3](/wiki/The_Gryphon,_Vol.4_No.3 "The Gryphon, Vol.4 No.3").

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien begins work on the new edition of *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight](</wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(edition)> "Sir Gawain and the Green Knight (edition)")* with [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon).
