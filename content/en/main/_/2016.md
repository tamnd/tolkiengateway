## August {#August}

- **[11](11_August.md)** - Musician [Glenn Yarbrough](../G/Glenn_Yarbrough.md) dies.

- **[1](1_September.md)** - *[Songs of Sorrow and Hope: The Art of Jenny Dolfen](../S/Songs_of_Sorrow_and_Hope.md)* is published.
- **[1](1_September.md)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2017](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2017.md)* is published.
- **[3](3_September.md)-[4](4_September.md)** - <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Middle-earth_Festival&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Middle-earth Festival (page does not exist)">Middle-earth Festival</a> at [Sarehole Mill](../S/Sarehole_Mill.md), [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md).

- **[22](22_September.md)** - <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Hobbit_Facsimile_First_Edition&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Hobbit Facsimile First Edition (page does not exist)">The Hobbit Facsimile First Edition</a></i> is published.
