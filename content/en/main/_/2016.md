[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016" title="wikipedia:2016">2016</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 257](/wiki/Amon_Hen_257)* is published.

## March {#March}

- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 258](/wiki/Amon_Hen_258)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[3](/wiki/3_April "3 April")** - Actor [Erik Bauersfeld](/wiki/Erik_Bauersfeld) dies.
- **[5](/wiki/5_April "5 April")** - *[The Story of Kullervo](/wiki/The_Story_of_Kullervo)* is published in the USA.
- **[7](/wiki/7_April "7 April")** - *[A Secret Vice](</wiki/A_Secret_Vice_(book)> "A Secret Vice (book)")*, a extended edition of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s lecture, is published.

## May {#May}

- **[31](/wiki/31_May "31 May")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Movie Trilogy Coloring Book](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Movie_Trilogy_Coloring_Book)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 259](/wiki/Amon_Hen_259)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[9](/wiki/9_June "9 June")** - *[The Return Of The Ring: Proceedings of the Tolkien Society Conference 2012](/wiki/The_Return_Of_The_Ring:_Proceedings_of_the_Tolkien_Society_Conference_2012)* is published.
- **[26](/wiki/26_June "26 June")** - Scholar [John Ellison](/wiki/John_Ellison) dies.

## July {#July}

- **[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - *[Leaf by Niggle](/wiki/Leaf_by_Niggle)*, is published in its own volume.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 260](/wiki/Amon_Hen_260)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[11](/wiki/11_August "11 August")** - Musician [Glenn Yarbrough](/wiki/Glenn_Yarbrough) dies.

## September {#September}

- **[1](/wiki/1_September "1 September")** - *[Songs of Sorrow and Hope: The Art of Jenny Dolfen](/wiki/Songs_of_Sorrow_and_Hope "Songs of Sorrow and Hope")* is published.
- **[1](/wiki/1_September "1 September")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2017](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2017)* is published.
- **[3](/wiki/3_September "3 September")-[4](/wiki/4_September "4 September")** - <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Middle-earth_Festival&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Middle-earth Festival (page does not exist)">Middle-earth Festival</a> at [Sarehole Mill](/wiki/Sarehole_Mill), [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham).
- **[8](/wiki/8_September "8 September") - [11](/wiki/11_September "11 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2016](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2016) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Hobbit_Facsimile_First_Edition&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Hobbit Facsimile First Edition (page does not exist)">The Hobbit Facsimile First Edition</a></i> is published.
- **[23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")** - Actor [William Johnson](/wiki/William_Johnson) dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 261](/wiki/Amon_Hen_261)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[19](/wiki/19_October "19 October")** - The posthumous publication, *[Beren and Lúthien](/wiki/Beren_and_L%C3%BAthien)*, edited by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), is announced for publication on [4 May](/wiki/4_May) [2017](/wiki/2017).

## November {#November}

- **[3](/wiki/3_November "3 November")** - *[The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun)* is published.
- **[3](/wiki/3_November "3 November")** - The Bodleian Library presented the Bodley Medal to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), the Library's highest honour.
- **[24](/wiki/24_November "24 November")** - *[Laughter in Middle-earth](/wiki/Laughter_in_Middle-earth)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 262](/wiki/Amon_Hen_262)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=A_Well_of_Wonder&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="A Well of Wonder (page does not exist)">A Well of Wonder: C. S. Lewis, J. R. R. Tolkien, and The Inklings</a></i> is published.
- **[6](/wiki/6_December "6 December")** - Actor [Peter Vaughan](/wiki/Peter_Vaughan) dies.
- **[19](/wiki/19_December "19 December")** - [Daybreak Game Company](/wiki/Daybreak_Game_Company) becomes the new publisher of [The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online).
