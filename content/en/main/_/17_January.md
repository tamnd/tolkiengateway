Events that occurred on 17 January.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1937](1937.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 10](../L/Letter_10.md).
- **[1964](1964.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mrs. A. Mountfield](../L/Letter_to_Mrs_A._Mountfield.md).
- **[1965](1965.md)** - Joan O. Falconer writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](../L/Letter_to_Joan_O._Falconer.md).
- **[1973](1973.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to N.J. Kyle](../L/Letter_to_N.J._Kyle.md).
- **[1977](1977.md)** - [Adam Fox](../A/Adam_Fox.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), dies.
- **[1982](1982.md)** - *[Mr. Bliss](../M/Mr._Bliss.md)* is published in the US.
- **[1983](1983.md)** - Actor [Ryan Gage](../R/Ryan_Gage.md) is born.
- **[2001](2001.md)** - Tolkien collector [Richard E. Blackwelder](../R/Richard_E._Blackwelder.md) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) pass the [Silverlode](../S/Silverlode.md) and reach the [Naith](../N/Naith.md) and [Cerin Amroth](../C/Cerin_Amroth.md).
    - The [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) following them are killed or flee to the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).
    - The Fellowship comes to [Caras Galadhon](../C/Caras_Galadhon.md) in the evening.
