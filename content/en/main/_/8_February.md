## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 254a](/wiki/Letter_254a).
- **[1967](/wiki/1967)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 294](/wiki/Letter_294).
- **[1967](/wiki/1967)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to C.N. Manlove](/wiki/Letter_to_C.N._Manlove "Letter to C.N. Manlove").
- **[1967](/wiki/1967)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to David L. Sands](/wiki/Letter_to_David_L._Sands "Letter to David L. Sands").
- **[1968](/wiki/1968)** - The fourth day of filming [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) for the documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](/wiki/Tolkien_in_Oxford)*.
- **[2001](/wiki/2001)** - [*A Tolkien Treasury* (miniature edition)](</wiki/A_Tolkien_Treasury_(miniature_edition)> "A Tolkien Treasury (miniature edition)") is released.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - "[C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings), and the Call to Christian Community" conference, featuring [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey).
