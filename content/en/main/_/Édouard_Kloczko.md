**Édouard J. Kloczko** or **Laurifindil** ([August 22](22_August.md), [1963](1963.md)), born in Lviv of Ukraine, is a linguist and translator.

He is well known in [Tolkien fandom](../T/Tolkien_fandom.md), especially in [France](../F/France.md), concerning his works about the [languages](../L/Languages.md) of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

In [1985](1985.md) he founded "la Fée" ("Faculté des études elfiques"), which means "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairy" title="wikipedia:Fairy">fairy</a>" in English; it was disbanded in [1993](1993.md).
