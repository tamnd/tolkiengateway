**Édouard J. Kloczko** or **Laurifindil** ([August 22](/wiki/22_August "22 August"), [1963](/wiki/1963)), born in Lviv of Ukraine, is a linguist and translator.

He is well known in [Tolkien fandom](/wiki/Tolkien_fandom), especially in [France](/wiki/France), concerning his works about the [languages](/wiki/Languages "Languages") of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

In [1985](/wiki/1985) he founded "la Fée" ("Faculté des études elfiques"), which means "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairy" title="wikipedia:Fairy">fairy</a>" in English; it was disbanded in [1993](/wiki/1993).
