<i><b>-ath</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) collective plural suffix,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> meaning all of the group to which it is affixed. It originated as a dual, and is sometimes still used as such.

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Argonath" title="Argonath">Argon<b>ath</b></a></i> (used as a dual)
- <i><a href="/wiki/Cirith_Thoronath" title="Cirith Thoronath">Cirith Thoron<b>ath</b></a></i>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Dagor_Dagorath" title="Dagor Dagorath">Dagor Dagor<b>ath</b></a></i>
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Dr%C3%BAath" title="Drúath">Drú<b>ath</b></a></i>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Ennor#Compounds" title="Ennor">ennor<b>ath</b></a></i>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Eglath" title="Eglath">Egl<b>ath</b></a></i>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Giliath" title="Giliath">gili<b>ath</b></a></i>
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Periannath" title="Periannath">Periann<b>ath</b></a></i>
- <i><a href="/wiki/Taur-im-Duinath" title="Taur-im-Duinath">Taur-im-Duin<b>ath</b></a></i> (again dual, referring to a forest between two rivers [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) and [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion))
