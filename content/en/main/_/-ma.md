**-ma** is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) nominal (concretizing) ending denoting a thing (usually object) having something to do with the root meaning; either having its properties or being produced by the verbal action in question; or even being a tool used to accomplish it.

In general, *-ma* seems to be stuck to naked [roots](../R/Root.md) or stems to produce the nouns. Eg. <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=RIL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="RIL (page does not exist)">RIL</a> &gt; <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Rilma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rilma (page does not exist)">rilma</a></i> "glittering light"; verb stems <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Par-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Par- (page does not exist)">par-</a></i> "to learn" &gt; *[parma](../P/Parma.md)* "book". However, full nouns can also be suffixed with *-ma* to produce another object: Eg. <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=L%C3%ADco&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Líco (page does not exist)">líco</a></i> "wax" &gt; <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=L%C3%ADcuma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lícuma (page does not exist)">lícuma</a></i> "candle".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*-ma* derives from [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=-m%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-mâ (page does not exist)">-mâ</a></i> frequent in the names of implements.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Such PQ nouns have yielded Quenya -ma nouns, for example <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tuim%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tuimâ (page does not exist)">tuimâ</a></i> &gt; <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tuima&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tuima (page does not exist)">tuima</a></i> or <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Sjalm%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sjalmâ (page does not exist)">sjalmâ</a></i> &gt; *[hyalma](../H/Hyalma.md)*.

In some phonological environments, *-ma* appears in allomorphs in later Quenya: Nouns in *-wa* or *-ba* might conceal a primitive *-mâ*. For example PQ <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Labm%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Labmâ (page does not exist)">labmâ</a></i> &gt; Q <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lamba&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lamba (page does not exist)">lamba</a></i> (metathesis of *bm* to *mb*) or PQ <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Takm%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Takmâ (page does not exist)">takmâ</a></i> &gt; Q <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tangwa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tangwa (page does not exist)">tangwa</a></i> (nasalization and strengthening of *k* before *m* and velarization of *m*).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is obviously related to similar endings like *[-mo](-mo.md)* (which indicates a person or doer) or *[-me](-me.md)* (which form a more abstract noun) which behave and operate similarly. For example <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Alma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alma (page does not exist)">alma</a></i> "wealth" vs <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Alme&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alme (page does not exist)">alme</a></i> "blessing", *[carma](../C/Carma.md)* "tool" vs <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Carme&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Carme (page does not exist)">carme</a></i> "art".

## Other possible examples {#Other_possible_examples}

Many Quenya nouns might end in *-ma* however that does not indicate the existence of a suffixed *-ma* proper ending. For example, the word *[ráma](../R/Ráma.md)* derives from a root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=RAM&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="RAM (page does not exist)">RAM</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Therefore it is not analyzed as \*\**rá-ma* but as *rám-a*.

There are other nouns of such ambiguous etymology, as for example <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lamma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lamma (page does not exist)">lamma</a></i> "sound" from root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=LAM&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LAM (page does not exist)">LAM</a>. It could be analyzed as LAM + *-ma* but also the double *mm* might simply be the result of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Antoryame&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Antoryame (page does not exist)">antoryame</a></i> (q.v.) of the radical consonant LA**M**.

Nouns of ambiguous or unknown etymology that might contain *-ma* are:

<i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Cauma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cauma (page does not exist)">cauma</a></i> "protection", <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Emma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Emma (page does not exist)">emma</a></i> "picture", <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Erma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Erma (page does not exist)">erma</a></i> "physical mater, <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Farma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Farma (page does not exist)">farma</a></i>, <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lamma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lamma (page does not exist)">lamma</a></i> "sound", <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Namma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Namma (page does not exist)">namma</a></i> "claw, talon", <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Orma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Orma (page does not exist)">orma</a></i> "physical matter", <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Querma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Querma (page does not exist)">querma</a></i> "spinning wheel", *[síma](../S/Síma.md)* "mind, imagination", <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Solma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Solma (page does not exist)">solma</a></i> (also <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=S%C3%B3l&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sól (page does not exist)">sól</a>) "helmet", <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=S%C3%BAma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Súma (page does not exist)">súma</a></i> "hollow cavity"

## Cognates {#Cognates}

Because of [lenition](../L/Lenition.md) and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Apocope&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Apocope (page does not exist)">apocope</a> of final vowels, all endings *-ma*, *-me* and *-mo* have been reduced to a final *-v* in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) which doesn't seem to work as a productive suffix. In some cases it even has disappeared according to the phonological environment. For example Q. *tengwa* &gt; S. <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=T%C3%AAw&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Têw (page does not exist)">têw</a></i>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

*-ma* is a frequent ending of Greek neutral nouns, such as *thelema*, *schema*, *stigma*, *enigma* etc.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
