## May {#May}

- **[4](/wiki/4_May "4 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 65](/wiki/Letter_65 "Letter 65").
- **[4](/wiki/4_May "4 May")** - While writing on *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) sees a new character arrive on the scene. He later [comments](/wiki/Letter_66 "Letter 66"): *"(I am sure I did not invent him, I did not even want him, though I like him, but there he came walking into the woods of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien)): [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), brother of [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir)"*.
- **[5](/wiki/5_May "5 May")** - [John Rhys-Davies](/wiki/John_Rhys-Davies), who would play [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) and voice [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.
- **[6](/wiki/6_May "6 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 66](/wiki/Letter_66 "Letter 66").
- **[11](/wiki/11_May "11 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 67](/wiki/Letter_67 "Letter 67").

- **[14](/wiki/14_May "14 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 69](/wiki/Letter_69 "Letter 69").
- **[21](/wiki/21_May "21 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 70](/wiki/Letter_70 "Letter 70").
- **[25](/wiki/25_May "25 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 71](/wiki/Letter_71 "Letter 71").
