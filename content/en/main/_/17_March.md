Events that occurred on 17 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1960](1960.md)** - [Jef Murray](../J/Jef_Murray.md) is born.
- **[1968](1968.md)** - Author [Nataša Tučev](../N/Nataša_Tučev.md) is born.
- **[1989](1989.md)** - Actress [Morfydd Clark](../M/Morfydd_Clark.md) is born.
- **[2006](2006.md)** - Voice actor [Bob Papenbrook](../B/Bob_Papenbrook.md) dies.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md): [Mines of Moria](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Mines_of_Moria.md#Synopsis)*, "Leaves of Lórien" is released.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md):
    - [Borondir](../B/Borondir.md) is on his way in search of the [Éothéod](Éothéod.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - [Battle of Dale](../B/Battle_of_Dale.md):
        - [King Brand](../B/Brand.md) and [King Dain Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) die.
        - Many [Dwarves of Erebor](../D/Dwarves_of_Erebor.md) and [Men of Dale](../M/Men_of_Dale.md) flee into [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) and are besieged.
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) travel northward along the [Morgai](../M/Morgai.md).
    - [Shagrat](../S/Shagrat.md) brings [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)'s cloak, mail shirt and sword to [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md), where he is slain.
