[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019" title="wikipedia:2019">2019</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[25](/wiki/25_January "25 January")** - Morgan Library's exhibition "<a class="external text" href="http://www.themorgan.org/exhibitions/tolkien" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth</a>" opens.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 275](/wiki/Amon_Hen_275)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[25](/wiki/25_February "25 February")** - *[Sub-creating Arda](/wiki/Sub-creating_Arda)* is published.

## March {#March}

- **[22](/wiki/22_March "22 March")** - [Jared Lobdell](/wiki/Jared_Lobdell) dies.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - *[Tolkien the Pagan?](/wiki/Tolkien_the_Pagan%3F)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 276](/wiki/Amon_Hen_276)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[9](/wiki/9_April "9 April")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Middle-Earth:_Journeys_in_Myth_and_Legend&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Middle-Earth: Journeys in Myth and Legend (page does not exist)">Middle-Earth: Journeys in Myth and Legend</a></i> is published.

## May {#May}

- **[3](/wiki/3_May "3 May")** - The biographical film, *[Tolkien](</wiki/Tolkien_(film)> "Tolkien (film)")*, is released in the United Kingdom.
- **[10](/wiki/10_May "10 May")** - The biographical film, *[Tolkien](</wiki/Tolkien_(film)> "Tolkien (film)")*, is released in the United States.
- **[12](/wiki/12_May "12 May")** - Morgan Library's exhibition "<a class="external text" href="http://www.themorgan.org/exhibitions/tolkien" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth</a>" closes.
- **[26](/wiki/26_May "26 May")** - British actor [Stephen Thorne](/wiki/Stephen_Thorne) dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 277](/wiki/Amon_Hen_277)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **?** - *[Amon Hen 278](/wiki/Amon_Hen_278)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[7](/wiki/7_August "7 August")** - *[Tolkien and the Classics](/wiki/Tolkien_and_the_Classics)* is published.
- **[7](/wiki/7_August "7 August")-[11](/wiki/11_August "11 August")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s [Tolkien 2019](/wiki/Tolkien_2019) event in Birmingham, UK.
- **[26](/wiki/26_August "26 August")** - Actor [Ray Henwood](/wiki/Ray_Henwood) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[5](/wiki/5_September "5 September")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2020](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2020)* is published.
- **[13](/wiki/13_September "13 September") - [15](/wiki/15_September "15 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2019](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2019) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")** - *["Something Has Gone Crack"](/wiki/%22Something_Has_Gone_Crack%22)* is published.
- <b><a href="/wiki/26_September" title="26 September">26</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Lost_Chaucer" title="Tolkien&#39;s Lost Chaucer">Tolkien&#39;s Lost Chaucer</a></i> is published.</b>
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 279](/wiki/Amon_Hen_279)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[7](/wiki/7_October "7 October")** - *[Music in Tolkien's Work and Beyond](/wiki/Music_in_Tolkien%27s_Work_and_Beyond)* is published.
- **[22](/wiki/22_October "22 October")** - la BnF's exhibition "<a class="external text" href="http://www.bnf.fr" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien, voyage en Terre du Milieu</a>" opens.

## November {#November}

- **[5](/wiki/5_November "5 November")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Minas Morgul](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Minas_Morgul)* is released online.
- **[14](/wiki/14_November "14 November")** - *[The Hobbit Sketchbook](/wiki/The_Hobbit_Sketchbook)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 280](/wiki/Amon_Hen_280)* is published.

## Unknown {#Unknown}
