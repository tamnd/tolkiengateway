Events that occurred on 14 August

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1954](1954.md)** - [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) [reviews *The Fellowship of the Ring*](../T/The_Gods_Return_to_Earth.md) for *Time and Tide*.
- **[1972](1972.md)** - Richard Jeffery sends a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) (see [Letter 347](../L/Letter_347.md)).
- **[1976](1976.md)** - [Mythcon 7](../M/Mythcon_7.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacramento,_California" title="wikipedia:Sacramento, California">Sacramento</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1982](1982.md)** - The fifth episode of the new version of the [1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- **[1982](1982.md)** - [Dorothy Whitelock](../D/Dorothy_Whitelock.md), former colleague of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), dies.
- **[1982](1982.md)** - [Mythcon 13](../M/Mythcon_13.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chapman_University" title="wikipedia:Chapman University">Chapman College</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange,_California" title="wikipedia:Orange, California">Orange</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1983](1983.md)** - [Mythcon 14](../M/Mythcon_14.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scripps_College" title="wikipedia:Scripps College">Scripps College</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont,_California" title="wikipedia:Claremont, California">Claremont</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[2005](2005.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s [Tolkien 2005](../T/Tolkien_2005.md) conference in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham" title="wikipedia:Birmingham">Birmingham</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - "[Edge of the Wild](<../E/Edge_of_the_Wild_(Tolkien_art_exhibit).md>)", Tolkien art exhibition in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moreton-in-Marsh" title="wikipedia:Moreton-in-Marsh">Moreton-in-Marsh</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>.
- **[2011](2011.md)** - <a class="external text" href="http://omentielva.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Cantea</a> - 4th International Conference on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Invented Languages in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia" title="wikipedia:Valencia">Valencia</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) is held captive by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - After the funeral of [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md), [Éomer](Éomer.md)'s guests take leave. [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) bids farewell to her father and brothers. [Éomer](Éomer.md) and [Éowyn](Éowyn.md) give [Merry](../M/Merry.md) a great horn.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
