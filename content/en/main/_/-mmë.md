<i><b>-mmë</b></i> is a pronominal [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) ending, exclusive "we".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i>va<b>mmë</b></i> "we won't".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
