## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 315](/wiki/Letter_315).
- **[7](/wiki/7_January "7 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to his grandson Michael George Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(7_January_1970)> "Letter to Michael George Tolkien (7 January 1970)").

## March {#March}

- **[4](/wiki/4_March "4 March")** - Actor [William Keen](/wiki/William_Keen) is born.

## April {#April}

- **[12](/wiki/12_April "12 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Tom Shippey](/wiki/Letter_to_Tom_Shippey "Letter to Tom Shippey").

## May {#May}

- **[2](/wiki/2_May "2 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is awarded an honorary Doctorate of Letters by the <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nottingham" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">University of Nottingham</a>.

- **[6](/wiki/6_June "6 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 315a](/wiki/Letter_315a "Letter 315a").

- **[26](/wiki/26_August "26 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 315b](/wiki/Letter_315b "Letter 315b").

- **[4](/wiki/4_September "4 September")-[7](/wiki/7_September "7 September")** - The first [Mythcon](/wiki/Mythcon_1 "Mythcon 1") is held at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Mudd_College" title="wikipedia:Harvey Mudd College">Harvey Mudd College</a>.
- **[11](/wiki/11_September "11 September")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [letter 316](/wiki/Letter_316 "Letter 316").
