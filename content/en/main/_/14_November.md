Events that occurred on 14 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1904](/wiki/1904)** – At the age of 34, [Mabel Tolkien](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield "Mabel Suffield") dies after six days in a diabetic coma.
- **[1907](/wiki/1907)** - Swedish author [Astrid Lindgren](/wiki/Astrid_Lindgren) is born.
- **[1955](/wiki/1955)** - The first episode of [1955 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)") of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* airs on the [BBC](/wiki/BBC) Third Programme.
- **[1962](/wiki/1962)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to H. Cotton Minchin](</wiki/Letter_to_H._Cotton_Minchin_(14_November_1962)> "Letter to H. Cotton Minchin (14 November 1962)").
- **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - The sixth episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[1968](/wiki/1968)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 307](/wiki/Letter_307).
- <b><a href="/wiki/1986" title="1986">1986</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_Shaping_of_Middle-earth" title="The Shaping of Middle-earth">The Shaping of Middle-earth</a></i> is published in the US.</b>
- <b><a href="/wiki/1990" title="1990">1990</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_War_of_the_Ring" title="The War of the Ring">The War of the Ring</a></i> is published in the US.</b>
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - *[Tolkien Through Russian Eyes](/wiki/Tolkien_Through_Russian_Eyes)* is published.
- **[2019](/wiki/2019)** - *[The Hobbit Sketchbook](/wiki/The_Hobbit_Sketchbook)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") rest in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").
