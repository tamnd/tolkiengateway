Events that occurred on 27 April.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1915](/wiki/1915)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes *[You and Me / and the Cottage of Lost Play](/wiki/The_Little_House_of_Lost_Play:_Mar_Vanwa_Tyali%C3%A9va "The Little House of Lost Play: Mar Vanwa Tyaliéva")*.
- **[1932](/wiki/1932)** - Voice actor [Casey Kasem](/wiki/Casey_Kasem) is born.
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 286](/wiki/Letter_286).
- **[1998](/wiki/1998)** - Voice actor [Godfrey Kenton](/wiki/Godfrey_Kenton) dies.
- **[2015](/wiki/2015)** - Cinematographer [Andrew Lesnie](/wiki/Andrew_Lesnie) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"):
    - [Thirteen dwarves](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) arrive at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") learns of the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) and [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) and agrees to accompany the [dwarves](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) on their adventure.
