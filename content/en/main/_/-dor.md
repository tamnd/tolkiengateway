**-dor** is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) suffix,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> meaning "king, lord".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> As seen in earlier [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), the word is actually <i><b>taur</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> but only the [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") form is seen in examples.

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Arador" title="Arador">Ara<b>dor</b></a></i> = "Noble lord"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Celebrindor" title="Celebrindor">Celebrin<b>dor</b></a></i> = "Silver lord"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Gildor" title="Gildor">Gil<b>dor</b></a></i> = "Star lord"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Gwindor" title="Gwindor">Gwin<b>dor</b></a></i> = "New lord"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Meneldor" title="Meneldor">Menel<b>dor</b></a></i> = "Lord of the Heavens"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Thorondor" title="Thorondor">Thoron<b>dor</b></a></i> = "King of Eagles"
