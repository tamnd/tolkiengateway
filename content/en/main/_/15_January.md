Events that occurred on 15 January.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1945](1945.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 94c](../L/Letter_94c.md).
- **[1957](1957.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is [interviewed](1957_%2522Carnival_of_Books%2522_Interview.md) by Ruth Harshaw for the NBC radio program "Carnival of Books".
- **[1965](1965.md)** - Actor [James Nesbitt](../J/James_Nesbitt.md) is born.
- **[1967](1967.md)** - [Philip Norman's interview](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is published in *[The Sunday Times Magazine](../T/The_Sunday_Times_Magazine_15_January_1967.md)* and in *[The New York Times Magazine](../T/The_New_York_Times_Magazine_15_January_1967.md)*.
- **[1970](1970.md)** - Musician [Charles Deenen](../C/Charles_Deenen.md) is born.
- **[1972](1972.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) holds a general meeting and becomes formally constituted.
- **[2005](2005.md)** - The second day of the first "[The One Ring Celebration](../T/The_One_Ring_Celebration.md)".
- **[2015](2015.md)** - *[A Brief History of The Hobbit](../A/A_Brief_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) find the [Chamber of Mazarbul](../C/Chamber_of_Mazarbul.md) and [Balin's Tomb](../B/Balin's_Tomb.md). [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) takes the [Book of Mazarbul](../B/Book_of_Mazarbul.md).
    - Attack of the Orcs and the [Balrog](../B/Balrogs.md). [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) destroys the [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](../B/Bridge_of_Khazad-dûm.md) and falls with [Durin's Bane](../D/Durin's_Bane.md).
    - The rest of the [Fellowship](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) exits [Moria](../M/Moria.md) and enters [Dimrill Dale](../D/Dimrill_Dale.md).
    - [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md), [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) gaze in [Mirrormere](../M/Mirrormere.md).
    - [Haldir](../H/Haldir.md), [Rúmil](<../R/Rúmil_(elf_of_Lórien).md>) and [Orophin](../O/Orophin.md) meet the Fellowship. They sleep on flets.
    - [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) pass through [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).
