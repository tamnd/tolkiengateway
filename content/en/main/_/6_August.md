Events that occurred on 6 August.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1965](1965.md)** - Linguist [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md) is born.
- **[1966](1966.md)** - [*Glasgow Herald* (6 August 1966)](<../G/Glasgow_Herald_(6_August_1966).md>), including an [interview](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) by [Daphne Castell](../D/Daphne_Castell.md), is published.
- **[1969](1969.md)** - Voice actor [Chris Edgerly](../C/Chris_Edgerly.md) is born.
- **[1990](1990.md)** - [Mythcon 21](../M/Mythcon_21.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University" title="wikipedia:California State University">California State University</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Beach,_California" title="wikipedia:Long Beach, California">Long Beach</a>.
- **[1994](1994.md)** - [Mythcon 25](../M/Mythcon_25.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_University" title="wikipedia:American University">American University</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C." title="wikipedia:Washington, D.C.">Washington, D.C.</a>
- **[1995](1995.md)** - [Mythcon 26](../M/Mythcon_26.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California" title="wikipedia:Berkeley, California">Berkeley</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[2001](2001.md)** - [Mythcon 32](../M/Mythcon_32.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California" title="wikipedia:Berkeley, California">Berkeley</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[2002](2002.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* is released on DVD and VHS.
- **[2005](2005.md)** - <a class="external text" href="http://omentielva.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Minya</a> - 1st International Conference on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Invented Languages in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm" title="wikipedia:Stockholm">Stockholm</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden" title="wikipedia:Sweden">Sweden</a>.
- **[2006](2006.md)** - [Mythcon 37](../M/Mythcon_37.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma" title="wikipedia:University of Oklahoma">University of Oklahoma</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman,_Oklahoma" title="wikipedia:Norman, Oklahoma">Norman</a>.
- **[2007](2007.md)** - [Mythcon 38](../M/Mythcon_38.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California" title="wikipedia:Berkeley, California">Berkeley</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - <a class="external text" href="http://omentielva.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Nelya</a> - 3rd International Conference on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Invented Languages in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitehaven" title="wikipedia:Whitehaven">Whitehaven</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>.
- **[2012](2012.md)** - [Mythcon 43](../M/Mythcon_43.md) at University of California, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California" title="wikipedia:Berkeley, California">Berkeley</a>.
- **[2015](2015.md)** - [Omentielva Enquea](../O/Omentielva.md) - Sixth International Conference on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Invented Languages in [Greisinger Museum](../G/Greisinger_Museum.md), Switzerland.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) is held captive by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The funeral procession of king [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) travels toward [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).
