Events that occurred on 11 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1918](1918.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is transferred to Savoy Convalescent Hospital, Blackpool, west coast of England.
- **[1951](1951.md)** - Second day of the Congrès Internationale de Philologie Moderne at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Li%C3%A8ge" title="wikipedia:University of Liège">University of Liège</a>, a conference in which [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) delivers the paper "[Middle English 'Losenger'](../M/Middle_English_'Losenger'.md)".
- **[1955](1955.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 169](../L/Letter_169.md).
- **[1957](1957.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 202](../L/Letter_202.md).
- **[1957](1957.md)** - The [*Oxford Mail*](../O/Oxford_Mail,_11_September_1957.md) reports from the International Fantasy Award, won by Tolkien.
- **[1959](1959.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 217](../L/Letter_217.md).
- **[1964](1964.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 264](../L/Letter_264.md).
- **[1967](1967.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 298](../L/Letter_298.md).
- **[1970](1970.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 316](../L/Letter_316.md).
- **[1982](1982.md)** - The ninth episode of the new version of the [1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- **[2006](2006.md)** - Translator [William Auld](../W/William_Auld.md) dies.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Bulgarian_Tolkien_Society&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bulgarian Tolkien Society (page does not exist)">Bulgarian Tolkien Society</a>'s 6th Tolkien-Fest in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia" title="wikipedia:Sofia">Sofia</a>.
- **[2014](2014.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2014](../O/Oxonmoot_2014.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Margaret_Hall,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Lady Margaret Hall, Oxford">Lady Margaret Hall</a>.
- **[2015](2015.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2015](../O/Oxonmoot_2015.md) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2016](2016.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2016](../O/Oxonmoot_2016.md) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
