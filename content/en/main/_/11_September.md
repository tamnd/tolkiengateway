Events that occurred on 11 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1918](/wiki/1918)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is transferred to Savoy Convalescent Hospital, Blackpool, west coast of England.
- **[1951](/wiki/1951)** - Second day of the Congrès Internationale de Philologie Moderne at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Li%C3%A8ge" title="wikipedia:University of Liège">University of Liège</a>, a conference in which [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) delivers the paper "[Middle English 'Losenger'](/wiki/Middle_English_%27Losenger%27)".
- **[1955](/wiki/1955)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 169](/wiki/Letter_169).
- **[1957](/wiki/1957)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 202](/wiki/Letter_202).
- **[1957](/wiki/1957)** - The [*Oxford Mail*](/wiki/Oxford_Mail,_11_September_1957 "Oxford Mail, 11 September 1957") reports from the International Fantasy Award, won by Tolkien.
- **[1959](/wiki/1959)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 217](/wiki/Letter_217).
- **[1964](/wiki/1964)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 264](/wiki/Letter_264).
- **[1967](/wiki/1967)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 298](/wiki/Letter_298).
- **[1970](/wiki/1970)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 316](/wiki/Letter_316).
- **[1982](/wiki/1982)** - The ninth episode of the new version of the [1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - Translator [William Auld](/wiki/William_Auld) dies.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Bulgarian_Tolkien_Society&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bulgarian Tolkien Society (page does not exist)">Bulgarian Tolkien Society</a>'s 6th Tolkien-Fest in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia" title="wikipedia:Sofia">Sofia</a>.
- **[2014](/wiki/2014)** - [Oxonmoot 2014](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2014) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Margaret_Hall,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Lady Margaret Hall, Oxford">Lady Margaret Hall</a>.
- **[2015](/wiki/2015)** - [Oxonmoot 2015](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2015) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2016](/wiki/2016)** - [Oxonmoot 2016](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2016) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
