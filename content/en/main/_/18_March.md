Events that occurred on 18 March

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1941](1941.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 44](../L/Letter_44.md).
- **[1950](1950.md)** - Actor [Brad Dourif](../B/Brad_Dourif.md) is born.
- **[1956](1956.md)** - Tolkien writes [a letter to a Mr Sam Gamgee](../L/Letter_184.md).
- **[1958](1958.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 205a](../L/Letter_205a.md).
- **[1971](1971.md)** - Tolkien writes [Letter 322](../L/Letter_322.md).
- **[1989](1989.md)** - Actress [Lily Collins](../L/Lily_Collins.md) is born.
- **[2005](2005.md)** - "Black Rider", the twelfth set in *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md)*, is released.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md)* is announced for release in [2011](2011.md).

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md):
    - [Borondir](../B/Borondir.md) is on his way in search of the [Éothéod](Éothéod.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The [Army of the West](../A/Army_of_the_West.md) marches from [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). After [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md), they end the day march but a vanguard continues to the [Cross-roads](../C/Cross-roads.md). [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) reclaims the land and restores the defiled statue of the King.
    - [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) march on the road from [Durthang](../D/Durthang.md) to [Udûn](<../U/Udûn_(valley).md>).
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Sam_Gamgee.md) come in sight of the [Isenmouthe](../C/Carach_Angren.md). They are overtaken by the orc-band.
