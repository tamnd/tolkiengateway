Events that occurred on 20 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1784](1784.md)** – [George Tolkien](../G/George_Tolkien.md) is born.
- **[1911](1911.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) signs a [Oxford University visitor's page](../O/Oxford_University_visitor's_page.md).
- **[1940](1940.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 40a](../L/Letter_40a.md).
- <b><a href="1949.md" title="1949">1949</a> – <i><a href="../F/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md" title="Farmer Giles of Ham">Farmer Giles of Ham</a></i> is published.</b>
- <b><a href="1955.md" title="1955">1955</a> – <i><a href="../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md" title="The Return of the King">The Return of the King</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[1958](1958.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Misses E. Jones and J. Taylor](../L/Letter_to_Misses_E._Jones_and_J._Taylor.md).
- **[1958](1958.md)** – Actor [Viggo Mortensen](../V/Viggo_Mortensen.md) is born.
- **[1963](1963.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 249a](../L/Letter_249a.md).
- **[1965](1965.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 278](../L/Letter_278.md).
- **[1965](1965.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter](<../L/Letter_to_Timothy_J._Wheeler_(20_October_1965).md>) to [Timothy J. Wheeler](../T/Timothy_J._Wheeler.md).
- **[1968](1968.md)** – *Strange Lodgings*, the fourth episode of the [1968 radio series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, is aired on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).
- <b><a href="1994.md" title="1994">1994</a> - <i><a href="../T/The_War_of_the_Jewels.md" title="The War of the Jewels">The War of the Jewels</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **[1997](1997.md)** - Actor [John Bell](../J/John_Bell.md) is born.
- <b><a href="2003.md" title="2003">2003</a> - <i><a href="../T/Tolkien_and_the_Great_War.md" title="Tolkien and the Great War">Tolkien and the Great War</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[2005](2005.md)** – [George Sayer](../G/George_Sayer.md), friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), dies.
- **[2020](2020.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: War of Three Peaks](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_War_of_Three_Peaks.md)* expansion is released.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md):
    - [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), guided by [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md), travels to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) in order to deliver [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md)'s message to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md), [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) and the [four hobbits](../T/Travellers.md) reach the [Ford of Bruinen](../F/Ford_of_Bruinen.md) where they are attacked by [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md). [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) reaches the other riverside but the Riders are cast back by the water, their horses drown and their robes are destroyed.
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
