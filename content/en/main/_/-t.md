<i><b>-t</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) suffix that has three meanings.

## -t<sup>1</sup> {#-t1}

Used as a dual ending, denoting a *pair* of something, e.g. <i>márya<b>t</b></i> ("her \[pair of\] hands").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## -t<sup>2</sup> {#-t2}

A pronominal ending meaning "them", seen in the word <i>laituvalme<b>t</b></i> ("we shall bless them"). Also independent word **te**, like in <i>a laita <b>të</b></i> ("praise them").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## -t<sup>3</sup> {#-t3}

Used as a reduced pronominal affix of the 2nd person, "you" (singular). Found in *heca* ("be gone, stand aside"): <i>heca<b>t</b></i> (singular) and *hecal* (plural).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
