[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1965" title="wikipedia:1965">1965</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[2](2_January.md)** - The thirteenth episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[6](6_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) receives a fan letter from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iris_Murdoch" title="wikipedia:Iris Murdoch">Iris Murdoch</a>.
- **[6](6_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to his grandson Michael George](<../L/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(6_January_1965).md>), published as [Letter 266](../L/Letter_266.md).
- **[9](9_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) starts writing [Letter 267](../L/Letter_267.md).
- **[9](9_January.md)** - The fourteenth episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[15](15_January.md)** - Actor [James Nesbitt](../J/James_Nesbitt.md) is born.
- **[16](16_January.md)** - The fifteenth and last episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[17](17_January.md)** - Joan O. Falconer writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](../L/Letter_to_Joan_O._Falconer.md).
- **[19](19_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 268](../L/Letter_268.md).
- **[20](20_January.md)** - The [1965 BBC Interview](1965_BBC_Interview.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is made.
- **[24](24_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Joan O. Falconer](../L/Letter_to_Joan_O._Falconer.md).

## February {#February}

- **?** - The [Tolkien Society of America](../T/Tolkien_Society_of_America.md) is founded.

## March {#March}

- **[15](15_March.md)** - [Robyn Malcolm](../R/Robyn_Malcolm.md), who would play [Morwen](<../M/Morwen_(film_character).md>) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*, is born.
- **?** - *[Tree and Leaf](../T/Tree_and_Leaf.md)* is published in the US.

## April {#April}

- **[28](28_April.md)** - Actor [Steven Jay Blum](../S/Steven_Jay_Blum.md) is born.

## May {#May}

- **[12](12_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 269](../L/Letter_269.md).
- **[20](20_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 270](../L/Letter_270.md).
- **[25](25_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 271](../L/Letter_271.md).
- **[28](28_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [to an unknown reader](<../L/Letter_to_unknown_recipient_(28_May_1965).md>).
- **?** - [Ace Books](../A/Ace_Books.md) publishes an unauthorized paperback edition of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* in the United States.

## July {#July}

- **[20](20_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 272](../L/Letter_272.md).
- **[21](21_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 273](../L/Letter_273.md).
- **[28](28_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 274](../L/Letter_274.md).
- **?** - [Ace Books](../A/Ace_Books.md) publishes an unauthorized paperback edition of *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md)* in the United States.
- **?** - [Ace Books](../A/Ace_Books.md) publishes an unauthorized paperback edition of *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)* in the United States.
- **[31](31_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 274a](../L/Letter_274a.md).

## August {#August}

- **[3](3_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to The Librarian at Marquette](../L/Letter_to_The_Librarian_at_Marquette.md)
- **[4](4_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 275](../L/Letter_275.md).
- **[6](6_August.md)** - [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md), renowned Tolkien linguist, is born.
- **[24](24_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 275a](../L/Letter_275a.md).
- **[26](26_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Paula Coston](<../L/Letter_to_Paula_Coston_(26_August_1965).md>)
- **?** - Tolkien notes the best solution to the problem of [Gil-galad's](../G/Gil-galad.md) [parentage](../G/Gil-galad.md#Parentage) is to shift him into the son of [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md).
- Between July and September, Tolkien makes revisions and corrections to *The Lord of the Rings* and sends them to [Houghton Mifflin](../H/Houghton_Mifflin.md) for a revised edition.

## September {#September}

- **[12](12_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes letters [276](../L/Letter_276.md) and [277](../L/Letter_277.md).
- **[16](16_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to his grandson Michael George Tolkien](<../L/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(16_September_1965).md>).
- **[19](19_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 277a](../L/Letter_277a.md).
- **[21](21_September.md)** - [David Wenham](../D/David_Wenham.md), who played [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.
- **[29](29_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Sterling E. Lanier](<../L/Letter_to_Sterling_E._Lanier_(29_September_1965).md>).

## October {#October}

- **[4](4_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Agatha Carew Hunt](../L/Letter_to_Agatha_Carew_Hunt.md).
- **[20](20_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 278](../L/Letter_278.md).
- **[30](30_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to his grandson Michael George](<../L/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(30_October_1965).md>), published as [Letter 279](../L/Letter_279.md).
- **[30](30_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to J.L.N. O'Loughlin](<../L/Letter_to_J.L.N._O'Loughlin_(30_October_1965).md>).
- **?** - The poems "[Once upon a Time](../O/Once_upon_a_Time.md)" and "[The Dragon's Visit](../T/The_Dragon's_Visit.md)" by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) are published in *[Winter's Tales for Children 1](../W/Winter's_Tales_for_Children_1.md)*.
- **?** - [Ballantine Books](../B/Ballantine_Books.md) publishes an authorized paperback edition of *The Lord of the Rings* incorporating Tolkien's recent revisions.

## November {#November}

- **[8](8_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 280](../L/Letter_280.md).

## December {#December}

- **[6](6_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to G.S. Rigby Jr.](../L/Letter_to_G.S._Rigby_Jr..md).
- **[9](9_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to William McCullam](../L/Letter_to_William_McCullam.md).
- **[9](9_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Jane Dixon](../L/Letter_to_Jane_Dixon.md).
- **[9](9_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Miss Jaworski](../L/Letter_to_Miss_Jaworski.md).
