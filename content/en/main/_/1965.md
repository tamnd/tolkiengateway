[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1965" title="wikipedia:1965">1965</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[2](/wiki/2_January "2 January")** - The thirteenth episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[6](/wiki/6_January "6 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) receives a fan letter from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iris_Murdoch" title="wikipedia:Iris Murdoch">Iris Murdoch</a>.
- **[6](/wiki/6_January "6 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to his grandson Michael George](</wiki/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(6_January_1965)> "Letter to Michael George Tolkien (6 January 1965)"), published as [Letter 266](/wiki/Letter_266).
- **[9](/wiki/9_January "9 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) starts writing [Letter 267](/wiki/Letter_267).
- **[9](/wiki/9_January "9 January")** - The fourteenth episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[15](/wiki/15_January "15 January")** - Actor [James Nesbitt](/wiki/James_Nesbitt) is born.
- **[16](/wiki/16_January "16 January")** - The fifteenth and last episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[17](/wiki/17_January "17 January")** - Joan O. Falconer writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Letter_to_Joan_O._Falconer "Letter to Joan O. Falconer").
- **[19](/wiki/19_January "19 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 268](/wiki/Letter_268).
- **[20](/wiki/20_January "20 January")** - The [1965 BBC Interview](/wiki/1965_BBC_Interview) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is made.
- **[24](/wiki/24_January "24 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Joan O. Falconer](/wiki/Letter_to_Joan_O._Falconer "Letter to Joan O. Falconer").

## February {#February}

- **?** - The [Tolkien Society of America](/wiki/Tolkien_Society_of_America) is founded.

## March {#March}

- **[15](/wiki/15_March "15 March")** - [Robyn Malcolm](/wiki/Robyn_Malcolm), who would play [Morwen](</wiki/Morwen_(film_character)> "Morwen (film character)") in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*, is born.
- **?** - *[Tree and Leaf](/wiki/Tree_and_Leaf)* is published in the US.

## April {#April}

- **[28](/wiki/28_April "28 April")** - Actor [Steven Jay Blum](/wiki/Steven_Jay_Blum) is born.

## May {#May}

- **[12](/wiki/12_May "12 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 269](/wiki/Letter_269 "Letter 269").
- **[20](/wiki/20_May "20 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 270](/wiki/Letter_270 "Letter 270").
- **[25](/wiki/25_May "25 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 271](/wiki/Letter_271 "Letter 271").
- **[28](/wiki/28_May "28 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [to an unknown reader](</wiki/Letter_to_unknown_recipient_(28_May_1965)> "Letter to unknown recipient (28 May 1965)").
- **?** - [Ace Books](/wiki/Ace_Books) publishes an unauthorized paperback edition of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* in the United States.

## July {#July}

- **[20](/wiki/20_July "20 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 272](/wiki/Letter_272 "Letter 272").
- **[21](/wiki/21_July "21 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 273](/wiki/Letter_273 "Letter 273").
- **[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 274](/wiki/Letter_274 "Letter 274").
- **?** - [Ace Books](/wiki/Ace_Books) publishes an unauthorized paperback edition of *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)* in the United States.
- **?** - [Ace Books](/wiki/Ace_Books) publishes an unauthorized paperback edition of *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)* in the United States.
- **[31](/wiki/31_July "31 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 274a](/wiki/Letter_274a "Letter 274a").

## August {#August}

- **[3](/wiki/3_August "3 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to The Librarian at Marquette](/wiki/Letter_to_The_Librarian_at_Marquette "Letter to The Librarian at Marquette")
- **[4](/wiki/4_August "4 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 275](/wiki/Letter_275 "Letter 275").
- **[6](/wiki/6_August "6 August")** - [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter), renowned Tolkien linguist, is born.
- **[24](/wiki/24_August "24 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 275a](/wiki/Letter_275a "Letter 275a").
- **[26](/wiki/26_August "26 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Paula Coston](</wiki/Letter_to_Paula_Coston_(26_August_1965)> "Letter to Paula Coston (26 August 1965)")
- **?** - Tolkien notes the best solution to the problem of [Gil-galad's](/wiki/Gil-galad "Gil-galad") [parentage](/wiki/Gil-galad#Parentage "Gil-galad") is to shift him into the son of [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth).
- Between July and September, Tolkien makes revisions and corrections to *The Lord of the Rings* and sends them to [Houghton Mifflin](/wiki/Houghton_Mifflin) for a revised edition.

## September {#September}

- **[12](/wiki/12_September "12 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes letters [276](/wiki/Letter_276 "Letter 276") and [277](/wiki/Letter_277 "Letter 277").
- **[16](/wiki/16_September "16 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to his grandson Michael George Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(16_September_1965)> "Letter to Michael George Tolkien (16 September 1965)").
- **[19](/wiki/19_September "19 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 277a](/wiki/Letter_277a "Letter 277a").
- **[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")** - [David Wenham](/wiki/David_Wenham), who played [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.
- **[29](/wiki/29_September "29 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Sterling E. Lanier](</wiki/Letter_to_Sterling_E._Lanier_(29_September_1965)> "Letter to Sterling E. Lanier (29 September 1965)").

## October {#October}

- **[4](/wiki/4_October "4 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Agatha Carew Hunt](/wiki/Letter_to_Agatha_Carew_Hunt "Letter to Agatha Carew Hunt").
- **[20](/wiki/20_October "20 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 278](/wiki/Letter_278 "Letter 278").
- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to his grandson Michael George](</wiki/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(30_October_1965)> "Letter to Michael George Tolkien (30 October 1965)"), published as [Letter 279](/wiki/Letter_279).
- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to J.L.N. O'Loughlin](</wiki/Letter_to_J.L.N._O%27Loughlin_(30_October_1965)> "Letter to J.L.N. O'Loughlin (30 October 1965)").
- **?** - The poems "[Once upon a Time](/wiki/Once_upon_a_Time)" and "[The Dragon's Visit](/wiki/The_Dragon%27s_Visit)" by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) are published in *[Winter's Tales for Children 1](/wiki/Winter%27s_Tales_for_Children_1)*.
- **?** - [Ballantine Books](/wiki/Ballantine_Books) publishes an authorized paperback edition of *The Lord of the Rings* incorporating Tolkien's recent revisions.

## November {#November}

- **[8](/wiki/8_November "8 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 280](/wiki/Letter_280 "Letter 280").

## December {#December}

- **[6](/wiki/6_December "6 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to G.S. Rigby Jr.](/wiki/Letter_to_G.S._Rigby_Jr. "Letter to G.S. Rigby Jr.").
- **[9](/wiki/9_December "9 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to William McCullam](/wiki/Letter_to_William_McCullam "Letter to William McCullam").
- **[9](/wiki/9_December "9 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Jane Dixon](/wiki/Letter_to_Jane_Dixon "Letter to Jane Dixon").
- **[9](/wiki/9_December "9 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Miss Jaworski](/wiki/Letter_to_Miss_Jaworski "Letter to Miss Jaworski").
- **[9](/wiki/9_December "9 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Christine Jones](/wiki/Letter_to_Christine_Jones "Letter to Christine Jones").
- **[15](/wiki/15_December "15 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 281](/wiki/Letter_281 "Letter 281").
- **[18](/wiki/18_December "18 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 282](/wiki/Letter_282 "Letter 282").
- **[27](/wiki/27_December "27 December")** - [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden) talks at a meeting of the [New York Tolkien Society](/wiki/North_East_Tolkien_Society "North East Tolkien Society").
- **[29](/wiki/29_December "29 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 282a](/wiki/Letter_282a "Letter 282a").
