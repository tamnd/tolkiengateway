[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1910" title="wikipedia:1910">1910</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** - An entry is written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in the first of [his preserved diaries](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_diaries "J.R.R. Tolkien's diaries").

## April {#April}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) sees Peter Pan at a Birmingham theatre, about which he writes in [his diary](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_diaries "J.R.R. Tolkien's diaries").

## May {#May}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the poem [The Dale-lands](/wiki/The_Dale-lands).

## November {#November}

- **[1](/wiki/1_November "1 November")** - J.R.R. Tolkien visits [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge) for a football match.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the "Debating Society Report" in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School_Chronicle)*.

## December {#December}

- **[17](/wiki/17_December "17 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is awarded an Open Classical Exhibition for classical languages to [Exeter College](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford").
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the "Debating Society Report" in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School_Chronicle)*.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Father Morgan](/wiki/Francis_Xavier_Morgan "Francis Xavier Morgan") moves the boys.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is forbidden from contacting [Edith Bratt](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien") until he is twenty-one. She leaves for [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham).
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) speaks [Gothic](/wiki/Gothic) and [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) in a school debate.
