## January {#January}

- **[1](1_January.md)** - An entry is written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in the first of [his preserved diaries](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_diaries.md).

## April {#April}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) sees Peter Pan at a Birmingham theatre, about which he writes in [his diary](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_diaries.md).

## May {#May}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes the poem [The Dale-lands](../T/The_Dale-lands.md).

## November {#November}

- **[1](1_November.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien visits [Cambridge](../C/Cambridge.md) for a football match.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes the "Debating Society Report" in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](../K/King_Edward's_School_Chronicle.md)*.

## December {#December}

- **[17](17_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is awarded an Open Classical Exhibition for classical languages to [Exeter College](../U/University_of_Oxford.md).
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes the "Debating Society Report" in *[King Edward's School Chronicle](../K/King_Edward's_School_Chronicle.md)*.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Father Morgan](../F/Francis_Xavier_Morgan.md) moves the boys.
