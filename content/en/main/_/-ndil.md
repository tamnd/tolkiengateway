<i><b>-ndil, -nil, -dil</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) suffix added to names to indicate friendship, love, or devotion to an object, idea, grouping, course, occupation or spirit ([Ainur](/wiki/Ainur)) to which one is devoted for its own sake<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From the longer form -(n)dildo,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> from [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NIL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NIL (page does not exist)">NIL</a>/<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NDIL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NDIL (page does not exist)">NDIL</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

*[nildo](/wiki/Nildo "Nildo")* means "friend".

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil)/<a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_(disambiguation)" title="Eärnil (disambiguation)">Eärnil</a> = "Devoted to the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer")"
- [Amandil](/wiki/Tar-Amandil "Tar-Amandil") = "Friend of [Aman](/wiki/Aman)"
- [Ornendil](/wiki/Ornendil) = "Friend of the [Trees](/wiki/Trees)"
- [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)> "Valandil (Lord of Andúnië)") = "Devoted to the [Valar](/wiki/Valar)"
- [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) = "Friend of the stars", also understood as "[Elf-friend](/wiki/Elf-friend)"
- \*[Vardilmë](/wiki/Vardilm%C3%AB) = Possibly female form meaning "Friend of [Varda](/wiki/Varda)"
- <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Aulendil" title="Aulendil">Aulendil</a> = "Friend of [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB)"
- It also appears in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[Edennil](/wiki/Edennil)* (adan) and *[Enerdhil](/wiki/Enerdhil)* (anar)
