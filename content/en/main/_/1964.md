[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964" title="wikipedia:1964">1964</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **First or second week**: [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) invites [Walter Hooper](/wiki/Walter_Hooper) to his house. They talk mostly about [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), who recently had died.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[9](/wiki/9_January "9 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 254](/wiki/Letter_254).
- **[10](/wiki/10_January "10 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to the Royal Society of Literature](/wiki/Letter_to_the_Royal_Society_of_Literature "Letter to the Royal Society of Literature").
- **[17](/wiki/17_January "17 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mrs. A. Mountfield](/wiki/Letter_to_Mrs_A._Mountfield "Letter to Mrs A. Mountfield").
- **[18](/wiki/18_January "18 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Justin Arundale](/wiki/Letter_to_Justin_Arundale "Letter to Justin Arundale").
- **[20](/wiki/20_January "20 January")** - The [1965 BBC Interview](/wiki/1965_BBC_Interview) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is made.
- **[20](/wiki/20_January "20 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) starts writing [a letter to Professor Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](</wiki/Letter_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(20%E2%80%9326_January_1964)> "Letter to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski (20–26 January 1964)").
- **[26](/wiki/26_January "26 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) finishes writing [a letter to Professor Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](</wiki/Letter_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(20%E2%80%9326_January_1964)> "Letter to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski (20–26 January 1964)").

## February {#February}

- **[8](/wiki/8_February "8 February")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 254a](/wiki/Letter_254a).

## March {#March}

- **[5](/wiki/5_March "5 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Eileen Elgar](</wiki/Letter_to_Eileen_Elgar_(5_March_1964)> "Letter to Eileen Elgar (5 March 1964)") (of which a part was published as [letter 255](/wiki/Letter_255 "Letter 255")) and encloses the manuscripts [Concerning ... 'The Hoard'](/wiki/Concerning_..._%27The_Hoard%27) and [Kinship of the Half-elven](/wiki/Kinship_of_the_Half-elven).
- **[16](/wiki/16_March "16 March")** - Artist [Quinton Hoover](/wiki/Quinton_Hoover) is born.

## April {#April}

- **?** - [*I, Palantir*, issue 3](</wiki/I,_Palantir_(April_1964)> "I, Palantir (April 1964)") is published.
- **[20](/wiki/20_April "20 April")** - Actor [Andy Serkis](/wiki/Andy_Serkis) is born.

## May {#May}

- **[13](/wiki/13_May "13 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 256](/wiki/Letter_256 "Letter 256").
- **[28](/wiki/28_May "28 May")** - *[Tree and Leaf](/wiki/Tree_and_Leaf)* is first published in the UK.
- **[30](/wiki/30_May "30 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [Letter to Jane T. Sibley](/wiki/Letter_to_Jane_T._Sibley)

## June {#June}

- **[6](/wiki/6_June "6 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Sue Parman](/wiki/Letter_to_Sue_Parman "Letter to Sue Parman").
- **[7](/wiki/7_June "7 June")** - Actor [Brian Talbot](/wiki/Brian_Talbot) is born.
- **[13](/wiki/13_June "13 June")-[15](/wiki/15_June "15 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [a letter to W.R. Matthews](/wiki/Letter_to_W.R._Matthews "Letter to W.R. Matthews").

## July {#July}

- **[10](/wiki/10_July "10 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Alan White](/wiki/Letter_to_Alan_White "Letter to Alan White").
- **[15](/wiki/15_July "15 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) sends [a letter to Miss J.L. Curry](/wiki/Letter_to_Miss_J.L._Curry "Letter to Miss J.L. Curry").
- **[16](/wiki/16_July "16 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 257](/wiki/Letter_257 "Letter 257").
- **?** - [L. Sprague de Camp](/wiki/L._Sprague_de_Camp) sends [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") a copy of his anthology *[Swords &amp; Sorcery](/wiki/Swords_%26_Sorcery)*.

## August {#August}

- **[2](/wiki/2_August "2 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 258](/wiki/Letter_258 "Letter 258").
- **[7](/wiki/7_August "7 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 259](/wiki/Letter_259 "Letter 259").
- **[10](/wiki/10_August "10 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Carole Ward](/wiki/Letter_to_Carole_Ward "Letter to Carole Ward").
- **[16](/wiki/16_August "16 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 260](/wiki/Letter_260 "Letter 260").
- **[30](/wiki/30_August "30 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 261](/wiki/Letter_261 "Letter 261").
- **[30](/wiki/30_August "30 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to L. Sprague de Camp](/wiki/Letter_to_L._Sprague_de_Camp "Letter to L. Sprague de Camp").

## September {#September}

- **[1](/wiki/1_September "1 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) met [Clyde S. Kilby](/wiki/Clyde_S._Kilby)
- **[7](/wiki/7_September "7 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 262](/wiki/Letter_262 "Letter 262").
- **[10](/wiki/10_September "10 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 263](/wiki/Letter_263 "Letter 263").
- **[11](/wiki/11_September "11 September")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [letter 264](/wiki/Letter_264 "Letter 264").
- **[30](/wiki/30_September "30 September")** - Actor [Bernard Rebel](/wiki/Bernard_Rebel) dies.

## October {#October}

- **[9](/wiki/9_October "9 October")** - [Guillermo del Toro](/wiki/Guillermo_del_Toro), co-writer of the upcoming *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")* duology, is born.
- **[10](/wiki/10_October "10 October")** - The first episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[10](/wiki/10_October "10 October")** - Actor [Quinton Flynn](/wiki/Quinton_Flynn) is born.
- **[17](/wiki/17_October "17 October")** - The second episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[24](/wiki/24_October "24 October")** - The third episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[31](/wiki/31_October "31 October")** - The fourth episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[1](/wiki/1_November "1 November")** - Actor [Daran Norris](/wiki/Daran_Norris) is born.
- **[7](/wiki/7_November "7 November")** - The fifth episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[11](/wiki/11_November "11 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 265](/wiki/Letter_265 "Letter 265").
- **[11](/wiki/11_November "11 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Clyde S. Kilby](</wiki/Letter_to_Clyde_S._Kilby_(11_November_1964)> "Letter to Clyde S. Kilby (11 November 1964)").
- **[14](/wiki/14_November "14 November")** - The sixth episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[21](/wiki/21_November "21 November")** - The seventh episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[24](/wiki/24_November "24 November")** - Robert Graves, [Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford")'s Professor of Poetry, introduces [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ava_Gardner" title="wikipedia:Ava Gardner">Ava Gardner</a>. They had never heard of one another, and both did not know why the other was famous.
- **[26](/wiki/26_November "26 November")** - The [1964 BBC Interview](/wiki/1964_BBC_Interview) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) was made.
- **[28](/wiki/28_November "28 November")** - The eighth episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - *[El hobito](/wiki/El_hobito)*, the first translation into Spanish of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, is published.

## December {#December}

- **[5](/wiki/5_December "5 December")** - The ninth episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[12](/wiki/12_December "12 December")** - The tenth episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[19](/wiki/19_December "19 December")** - The eleventh episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[26](/wiki/26_December "26 December")** - The twelfth episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[31](/wiki/31_December "31 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a letter to [Ingeborg Korff and her pupils](/wiki/Letter_to_Ingeborg_Korff "Letter to Ingeborg Korff").

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the essay "[The Ulsterior Motive](/wiki/The_Ulsterior_Motive)".
- **?** - Author [Chris Smith](/wiki/Chris_Smith) is born.
