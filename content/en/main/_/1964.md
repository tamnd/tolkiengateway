## January

* **First or second week**: [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) invites [Walter Hooper](/wiki/Walter_Hooper) to his house. They talk mostly about [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), who recently had died.

> Reference: [Joseph Pearce](/wiki/Joseph_Pearce), "Tolkien and C.S. Lewis: An Interview with Walter Hooper", in *[Tolkien: A Celebration](/wiki/Tolkien:_A_Celebration)*, pp. 190-1

* **[9](/wiki/9_January)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 254](/wiki/Letter_254).

* **[10](/wiki/10_January)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to the Royal Society of Literature](/wiki/Letter_to_the_Royal_Society_of_Literature).

* **[17](/wiki/17_January)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mrs. A. Mountfield](/wiki/Letter_to_Mrs_A._Mountfield).

* **[18](/wiki/18_January)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Justin Arundale](/wiki/Letter_to_Justin_Arundale).

* **[20](/wiki/20_January)** - The [1965 BBC Interview](/wiki/1965_BBC_Interview) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is made.

* **[20](/wiki/20_January)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) starts writing [a letter to Professor Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](/wiki/Letter_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(20–26_January_1964)).

* **[26](/wiki/26_January)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) finishes writing [a letter to Professor Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](/wiki/Letter_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(20–26_January_1964)).
