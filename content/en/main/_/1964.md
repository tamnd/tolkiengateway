[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964" title="wikipedia:1964">1964</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **First or second week**: [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) invites [Walter Hooper](../W/Walter_Hooper.md) to his house. They talk mostly about [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md), who recently had died.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[9](9_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 254](../L/Letter_254.md).
- **[10](10_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to the Royal Society of Literature](../L/Letter_to_the_Royal_Society_of_Literature.md).
- **[17](17_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mrs. A. Mountfield](../L/Letter_to_Mrs_A._Mountfield.md).
- **[18](18_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Justin Arundale](../L/Letter_to_Justin_Arundale.md).
- **[20](20_January.md)** - The [1965 BBC Interview](1965_BBC_Interview.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is made.
- **[20](20_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) starts writing [a letter to Professor Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](<../L/Letter_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(20–26_January_1964).md>).
- **[26](26_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) finishes writing [a letter to Professor Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](<../L/Letter_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(20–26_January_1964).md>).

## February {#February}

- **[8](8_February.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 254a](../L/Letter_254a.md).

## March {#March}

- **[5](5_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Eileen Elgar](<../L/Letter_to_Eileen_Elgar_(5_March_1964).md>) (of which a part was published as [letter 255](../L/Letter_255.md)) and encloses the manuscripts [Concerning ... 'The Hoard'](../C/Concerning_..._'The_Hoard'.md) and [Kinship of the Half-elven](../K/Kinship_of_the_Half-elven.md).
- **[16](16_March.md)** - Artist [Quinton Hoover](../Q/Quinton_Hoover.md) is born.

## April {#April}

- **?** - [*I, Palantir*, issue 3](<../I/I,_Palantir_(April_1964).md>) is published.
- **[20](20_April.md)** - Actor [Andy Serkis](../A/Andy_Serkis.md) is born.

## May {#May}

- **[13](13_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 256](../L/Letter_256.md).
- **[28](28_May.md)** - *[Tree and Leaf](../T/Tree_and_Leaf.md)* is first published in the UK.
- **[30](30_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [Letter to Jane T. Sibley](../L/Letter_to_Jane_T._Sibley.md)

## June {#June}

- **[6](6_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Sue Parman](../L/Letter_to_Sue_Parman.md).
- **[7](7_June.md)** - Actor [Brian Talbot](../B/Brian_Talbot.md) is born.
- **[13](13_June.md)-[15](15_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [a letter to W.R. Matthews](../L/Letter_to_W.R._Matthews.md).

## July {#July}

- **[10](10_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Alan White](../L/Letter_to_Alan_White.md).
- **[15](15_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) sends [a letter to Miss J.L. Curry](../L/Letter_to_Miss_J.L._Curry.md).
- **[16](16_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 257](../L/Letter_257.md).
- **?** - [L. Sprague de Camp](../L/L._Sprague_de_Camp.md) sends [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) a copy of his anthology *[Swords &amp; Sorcery](../S/Swords_&_Sorcery.md)*.

## August {#August}

- **[2](2_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 258](../L/Letter_258.md).
- **[7](7_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 259](../L/Letter_259.md).
- **[10](10_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Carole Ward](../L/Letter_to_Carole_Ward.md).
- **[16](16_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 260](../L/Letter_260.md).
- **[30](30_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 261](../L/Letter_261.md).
- **[30](30_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to L. Sprague de Camp](../L/Letter_to_L._Sprague_de_Camp.md).

## September {#September}

- **[1](1_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) met [Clyde S. Kilby](../C/Clyde_S._Kilby.md)
- **[7](7_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 262](../L/Letter_262.md).
- **[10](10_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 263](../L/Letter_263.md).
- **[11](11_September.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [letter 264](../L/Letter_264.md).
- **[30](30_September.md)** - Actor [Bernard Rebel](../B/Bernard_Rebel.md) dies.

## October {#October}

- **[9](9_October.md)** - [Guillermo del Toro](../G/Guillermo_del_Toro.md), co-writer of the upcoming *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)* duology, is born.
- **[10](10_October.md)** - The first episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[10](10_October.md)** - Actor [Quinton Flynn](../Q/Quinton_Flynn.md) is born.
- **[17](17_October.md)** - The second episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[24](24_October.md)** - The third episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[31](31_October.md)** - The fourth episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[1](1_November.md)** - Actor [Daran Norris](../D/Daran_Norris.md) is born.
- **[7](7_November.md)** - The fifth episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[11](11_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 265](../L/Letter_265.md).
- **[11](11_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Clyde S. Kilby](<../L/Letter_to_Clyde_S._Kilby_(11_November_1964).md>).
- **[14](14_November.md)** - The sixth episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[21](21_November.md)** - The seventh episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[24](24_November.md)** - Robert Graves, [Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md)'s Professor of Poetry, introduces [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ava_Gardner" title="wikipedia:Ava Gardner">Ava Gardner</a>. They had never heard of one another, and both did not know why the other was famous.
- **[26](26_November.md)** - The [1964 BBC Interview](1964_BBC_Interview.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) was made.
- **[28](28_November.md)** - The eighth episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[30](30_November.md)** - *[El hobito](../E/El_hobito.md)*, the first translation into Spanish of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, is published.

## December {#December}

- **[5](5_December.md)** - The ninth episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[12](12_December.md)** - The tenth episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[19](19_December.md)** - The eleventh episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[26](26_December.md)** - The twelfth episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[31](31_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a letter to [Ingeborg Korff and her pupils](../L/Letter_to_Ingeborg_Korff.md).
