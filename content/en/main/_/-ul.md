**-ul** is a suffix in [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) meaning "of".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is a part of the word [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul), <i>(Language) <b>of</b> the <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a></i>. It also appears in [Balin](/wiki/Balin) [Fundinul](/wiki/Fundin "Fundin") [uzbad](/wiki/Uzbad "Uzbad") [Khazad-dûmu](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm "Khazad-dûm"), <i>Balin, (son) <b>of</b> Fundin, Lord of Moria</i>.
