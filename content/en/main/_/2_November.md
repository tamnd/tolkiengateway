Events that occurred on 2 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1918](/wiki/1918)** – [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") [Roger Lancelyn Green](/wiki/Roger_Lancelyn_Green) is born.
- **[1956](/wiki/1956)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 193](/wiki/Letter_193).
- **[1958](/wiki/1958)** - [Taum Santoski](/wiki/Taum_Santoski), Tolkien linguist, is born.
- **[1959](/wiki/1959)** - Actor [Peter Mullan](/wiki/Peter_Mullan) is born.
- **[1962](/wiki/1962)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 241a](/wiki/Letter_241a "Letter 241a").
- **[1983](/wiki/1983)** - Actor [Michael Spice](/wiki/Michael_Spice) dies.
- **[1986](/wiki/1986)** - Voice actor [Paul Frees](/wiki/Paul_Frees) dies.
- **[1990](/wiki/1990)** - [Donald A. Wollheim](/wiki/Donald_A._Wollheim) dies.
- **[2002](/wiki/2002)** - The symposium "J.R.R. Tolkien: The Man Behind The Lord of the Rings" is held at the <a class="external text" href="http://acmrs.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Arizona Center for Medieval and Renaissance Studies</a>. [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey), [Randel Helms](/wiki/Randel_Helms) and Ron Newcomer speak.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Third_Age)* is released in North America.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online:](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online "The Lord of the Rings Online") [Volume III: Allies of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Siege_of_Mirkwood#Volume_III:_Allies_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings Online: Siege of Mirkwood")*, "Ride of the Grey Company" is released in Europe.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)* goes free-to-play for the general public in Europe.
- **[2011](/wiki/2011)** - *[The Loss and the Silence: Aspects of Modernism in the Works of C.S. Lewis, J.R.R. Tolkien &amp; Charles Williams](/wiki/The_Loss_and_the_Silence "The Loss and the Silence")* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") rest in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - The [Travellers](/wiki/Travellers) come to [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater) and [rouse](/wiki/Horn-call_of_Buckland "Horn-call of Buckland") the [Shire-folk](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits").
- The above event was commemorated in [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) by sounding the [Horn of the Mark](/wiki/Horn_of_the_Mark) and other festivities.
