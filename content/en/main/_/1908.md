## Early {#Early}

- **?** - [Ronald](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") and [Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien) move to [37 Duchess Road](/wiki/37_Duchess_Road), behind the [Birmingham Oratory](/wiki/Birmingham_Oratory), into a room let by a Mrs. Faulkner.

## February {#February}

- **[13](/wiki/13_February "13 February")** - [Patrick Barr](/wiki/Patrick_Barr), who would voice [Gamling](/wiki/Gamling) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1981 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) begins his first term at [Oxford University](/wiki/Oxford_University).
