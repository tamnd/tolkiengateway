[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2014" title="wikipedia:2014">2014</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Saul Zaentz](/wiki/Saul_Zaentz) dies.
- **[30](/wiki/30_January "30 January")** - Director [Arthur Rankin, Jr.](/wiki/Arthur_Rankin,_Jr.) dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 245](/wiki/Amon_Hen_245)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[27](/wiki/27_February "27 February")** - *[The Fall of Arthur](/wiki/The_Fall_of_Arthur)* is published in paperback.
- **[27](/wiki/27_February "27 February")** - *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)* pocket edition is published.

## March {#March}

- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - *[Anglo-Saxon Community in J.R.R. Tolkien's the Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Anglo-Saxon_Community_in_J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_the_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is published.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 246](/wiki/Amon_Hen_246)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[3](/wiki/3_April "3 April")** - *[Smaug: Unleashing the Dragon](/wiki/Smaug:_Unleashing_the_Dragon)* is published.
- **[5](/wiki/5_April "5 April")** - *Words of Westernesse: Adûnaic and Westron* is published.
- **[5](/wiki/5_April "5 April")** - *The Moon in The Hobbit* is published.
- **[5](/wiki/5_April "5 April")** - *The indigenous peoples of Eriador and Gondor* is published.
- **[7](/wiki/7_April "7 April")** - *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)* is released on Blu-ray and DVD in the UK.
- **[8](/wiki/8_April "8 April")** - *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)* is released on Blu-ray and DVD in the USA.
- **[19](/wiki/19_April "19 April") - [21](/wiki/21_April "21 April")** - <a class="external text" href="http://www.hobbitcon.de/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Hobbitcon 2</a>.
- **[24](/wiki/24_April "24 April")** - [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) announces that the third *[Hobbit film](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")* will be renamed to *[The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies "The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies")*.
- **[30](/wiki/30_April "30 April")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hither_Shore_10&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hither Shore 10 (page does not exist)">Hither Shore 10</a></i> is published.

## May {#May}

- **[22](/wiki/22_May "22 May")** - <b><i><a href="/wiki/Beowulf:_A_Translation_and_Commentary" title="Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary">Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary</a></i> by <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>, edited by <a href="/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien" title="Christopher Tolkien">Christopher Tolkien</a>, is published.</b>
- **[24](/wiki/24_May "24 May")** - [Beowulf Launch Party](/wiki/Beowulf_Launch_Party) hosted by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) and [Middle-earth Network](/wiki/Middle-earth_Network) with support from <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tolkien_Brasil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien Brasil (page does not exist)">Tolkien Brasil</a> and [The Tolkienist](/wiki/Marcel_B%C3%BClles "Marcel Bülles").
- **[28](/wiki/28_May "28 May")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Middle-Earth_in_Magic_Mirror_Maps&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Middle-Earth in Magic Mirror Maps (page does not exist)">Middle-Earth in Magic Mirror Maps</a></i> is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_May "30 May")** - *[Tolkien in the New Century: Essays in Honor of Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tolkien_in_the_New_Century "Tolkien in the New Century")* is published.
- **?** - *A Companion to J. R. R. Tolkien* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 247](/wiki/Amon_Hen_247)* is published.

## June {#June}

- **[6](/wiki/6_June "6 June")** - *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug: Chronicles: Cloaks &amp; Daggers](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug:_Chronicles:_Cloaks_%26_Daggers)* is published.
- **[15](/wiki/15_June "15 June")** - Voice actor [Casey Kasem](/wiki/Casey_Kasem) dies.
- **[19](/wiki/19_June "19 June")** - A new 60th Anniversary Edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is published.
- **[19](/wiki/19_June "19 June")** - An updated edition of *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_A_Reader%27s_Companion)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[18](/wiki/18_July "18 July") - [20](/wiki/20_July "20 July")** - [Tolkien Thing](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing) 2014.
- **[26](/wiki/26_July "26 July")** - [Laocoon](/wiki/Laocoon), one of Tolkien's favourite trees, lost two limbs. Subsequently, the Oxford Botanic Garden decided that the tree must be felled for safety reasons.
- **[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")** - Producer and interviewer [Denys Gueroult](/wiki/Denys_Gueroult) dies.

## August {#August}

- **[8](/wiki/8_August "8 August") - [11](/wiki/11_August "11 August")** - [Mythcon 45](/wiki/Mythcon_45) in Wheaton College, Norton.
- **[14](/wiki/14_August "14 August")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) Seminar as part of WorldCon, London.
- **[14](/wiki/14_August "14 August")** - *[Tolkien On Fairy-stories](/wiki/Tolkien_On_Fairy-stories)* is published in paperback.
- **[28](/wiki/28_August "28 August")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2015](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2015)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 248](/wiki/Amon_Hen_248)* is published.

## September {#September}

- **[11](/wiki/11_September "11 September")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies: Annual 2015](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies:_Annual_2015)* is published.
- **[11](/wiki/11_September "11 September") - [14](/wiki/14_September "14 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2014](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2014) at Lady Margaret Hall.
- **[12](/wiki/12_September "12 September")** - *[Tolkien at Exeter College: How an Oxford undergraduate created Middle-earth](/wiki/Tolkien_at_Exeter_College "Tolkien at Exeter College")* by [John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth) is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_September "30 September")** - [Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor) is released in North America.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 249](/wiki/Amon_Hen_249)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[3](/wiki/3_October "3 October")** - *[Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor)* is released in Europe.
- **[9](/wiki/9_October "9 October")** - *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)* pocket edition is published.
- **[13](/wiki/13_October "13 October")** - *[Journal of Inklings Studies, vol.4 no.2](/wiki/Journal_of_Inklings_Studies,_vol.4_no.2)*, including a translation of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s *[The Book of Jonah](/wiki/The_Book_of_Jonah)*, is published.
- **[21](/wiki/21_October "21 October")** - *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug - Extended Edition](</wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_(extended_edition)> "The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug (extended edition)")* is released for digital download.
- **[23](/wiki/23_October "23 October")** - Writer and actor [Bernard Mayes](/wiki/Bernard_Mayes) dies.
- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")** - [The Hobbit *and Tolkien's Mythology*](/wiki/The_Hobbit_and_Tolkien%27s_Mythology "The Hobbit and Tolkien's Mythology") is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")** - *[Arda Inhabited](/wiki/Arda_Inhabited)* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[3](/wiki/3_November "3 November")** - [*The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug* Extended Edition](</wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_(extended_edition)> "The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug (extended edition)") is released on Blu-ray and DVD in the UK.
- **[4](/wiki/4_November "4 November")** - [*The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug* Extended Edition](</wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_(extended_edition)> "The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug (extended edition)") is released on Blu-ray and DVD in the USA.
- **[11](/wiki/11_November "11 November")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies: Activity Book](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies:_Activity_Book)* is published.
- **[15](/wiki/15_November "15 November")** - *[War of Words: Soldier-Poets of the Somme](/wiki/War_of_Words:_Soldier-Poets_of_the_Somme)* is aired.
- **[15](/wiki/15_November "15 November")** - *[ArdaCraft](/wiki/ArdaCraft)* begins building Middle-earth.
- **[20](/wiki/20_November "20 November")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies: The Movie Storybook](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies:_The_Movie_Storybook)* is published.
- **[20](/wiki/20_November "20 November")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies: Official Movie Guide](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies:_Official_Movie_Guide)* is published.
- **[20](/wiki/20_November "20 November")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies: Visual Companion](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies:_Visual_Companion)* is published.
- **[20](/wiki/20_November "20 November")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Hobbit_Trilogy_Location_Guide&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Hobbit Trilogy Location Guide (page does not exist)">The Hobbit Trilogy Location Guide</a></i> is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - *[Tolkien's Great War (documentary)](</wiki/Tolkien%27s_Great_War_(documentary)>)* by Zander and Elliot Weaver is released.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 250](/wiki/Amon_Hen_250)* is published.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 55](/wiki/Mallorn_55)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)* premières in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leicester_Square" title="wikipedia:Leicester Square">Leicester Square</a>, London.
- **[4](/wiki/4_December "4 December")** - <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=JRR_Tolkien,_le_seigneur_des_%C3%A9crivains&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="JRR Tolkien, le seigneur des écrivains (page does not exist)">JRR Tolkien, le seigneur des écrivains</a> (La Grande Librairie) is aired.
- **[5](/wiki/5_December "5 December")** - *[Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor)* wins one Game Award, out of three nominations, at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_Awards_2014" title="wikipedia:The Game Awards 2014">2014 The Game Awards</a>.
- **[8](/wiki/8_December "8 December")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack - Special Edition](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack_-_Special_Edition)* is released.
- **[11](/wiki/11_December "11 December")** - <b><i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i> is released in New Zealand.</b>
- **[12](/wiki/12_December "12 December")** - <b><i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i> is released in the United Kingdom.</b>
- **[17](/wiki/17_December "17 December")** - <b><i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i> is released in the United States.</b>
- **[17](/wiki/17_December "17 December")** - *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies: Chronicles: Art &amp; Design](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies:_Chronicles:_Art_%26_Design)* is published.
