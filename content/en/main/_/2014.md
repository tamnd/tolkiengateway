## June {#June}

- **[6](6_June.md)** - *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug: Chronicles: Cloaks &amp; Daggers](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug%253A_Chronicles%253A_Cloaks_&_Daggers.md)* is published.

- **[18](18_July.md) - [20](20_July.md)** - [Tolkien Thing](../T/Tolkien_Thing.md) 2014.

- **[29](29_July.md)** - Producer and interviewer [Denys Gueroult](../D/Denys_Gueroult.md) dies.
