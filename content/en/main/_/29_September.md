Events that occurred on 29 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1940](1940.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 39](../L/Letter_39.md).
- **[1965](1965.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Sterling E. Lanier](<../L/Letter_to_Sterling_E._Lanier_(29_September_1965).md>).
- **[1968](1968.md)** - *An Unexpected Party*, the first episode of the [1968 radio series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, is aired on [BBC Radio](../B/BBC.md).
- **[1968](1968.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Billy Callahan](../L/Letter_to_Billy_Callahan.md).
- **[1968](1968.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Letter_to_Margaret_Carroux&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Letter to Margaret Carroux (page does not exist)">letter to</a> [Margaret Carroux](../M/Margaret_Carroux.md).
- **[1969](1969.md)** - Artist [Warren Mahy](../W/Warren_Mahy.md) is born.
- **[1973](1973.md)** - Author [W.H. Auden](../W/W.H._Auden.md) dies.
- **[1979](1979.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1979](../O/Oxonmoot_1979.md) at the Eastgate Hotel.
- **[1979](1979.md)** - Scholars [Humphrey Carpenter](../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md), [George Sayer](../G/George_Sayer.md) and [Clyde S. Kilby](../C/Clyde_S._Kilby.md) deliver a public discussion on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheaton_College_(Illinois)" title="wikipedia:Wheaton College (Illinois)">Wheaton College</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois" title="wikipedia:Illinois">Illinois</a> (published as "[A Dialogue](../A/A_Dialogue%253A_Discussion_by_Humphrey_Carpenter,_Professor_George_Sayer_and_Dr._Clyde_S._Kilby.md)").
- **[1985](1985.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1985](../O/Oxonmoot_1985.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[2006](2006.md)** - *[Tolkien and Modernity 1](../T/Tolkien_and_Modernity_1.md)* and *[Tolkien and Modernity 2](../T/Tolkien_and_Modernity_2.md)* are published.
- **[2011](2011.md)** - A new hardback edition of *[Mr. Bliss](../M/Mr._Bliss.md)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - Gandalf visits the [Gaffer](../G/Gaffer_Gamgee.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
    - [Harry Goatleaf](../H/Harry_Goatleaf.md) lets [Black Riders](../B/Black_Rider.md) into [Bree](../B/Bree.md).<sup id="cite_ref-timeline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timeline-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) hides behind the hedge on the Road west of Bree and eavesdrops on "[Mr. Underhill](../M/Mr._Underhill.md)". [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and his companions reach [Bree](../B/Bree.md) at night and Harry lets them in. Strider follows climbing over the gate.<sup id="cite_ref-timeline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timeline-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - The hobbits stay at the *[Prancing Pony](../P/Prancing_Pony.md)*. [Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md) takes them to the parlor for dinner.<sup id="cite_ref-timeline_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timeline-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - Butterbur doesn't let Strider interrupt Frodo, and stays in the Commons room; [Bill Ferny](../B/Bill_Ferny.md) is also there. After dinner, the hobbits, join, while Merry stays in the parlor.<sup id="cite_ref-timeline_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timeline-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - Pippin interacts with the people in the room while Strider finally talks to Frodo. Merry is going for a walk.<sup id="cite_ref-timeline_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timeline-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - In the inn Frodo arouses attention by his sudden [disappearance](../U/Unseen.md) by wearing the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md). Seeing this, Bill leaves for his home. Meanwhile Merry sees a Black Rider outside.<sup id="cite_ref-timeline_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timeline-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - Strider warns Frodo to be more careful and in the parlor he reveals himself as being [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md)'s friend. Meanwhile Merry follows the Rider to Bill's house, where Bill tells about Frodo's disappearance.<sup id="cite_ref-timeline_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timeline-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md) finally gives Frodo [Gandalf's letter](../G/Gandalf's_letter.md) and sends [Nob](../N/Nob.md) to find Merry. Harry recognises Merry and two Riders go to take him, while one goes to notify the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md); Merry faints because of the [Black Breath](../B/Black_Breath.md).
    - Nob finds Merry and shouts at people attempting to carry him away.<sup id="cite_ref-timeline_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timeline-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - Merry returns to the parlor. Anticipating an attack, the hobbits's rooms are abandoned while Strider and Nob create disguises.<sup id="cite_ref-timeline_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timeline-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3021](../T/Third_Age_3021.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md), [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) leave [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) on the [White Ship](../W/White_Ship.md) and pass over the Sea.
