<i><b>úvanimo</b></i>, or *úvano*, means "a monster, corrupt or evil creature" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PE150_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE150-1">[1]</a></sup> The word *úvanimo* also appears in the *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)*, given the meaning "monster (creature of [Melko](../M/Morgoth.md))" (wrongly rendered as capitalized "*Úvanimor*" in the published text).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Such creatures that were identified by this term include Monsters, [Giants](../G/Giants.md), and [Ogres](../O/Ogres.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
