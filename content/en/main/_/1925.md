[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1925" title="wikipedia:1925">1925</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[5](5_January.md)** - [Wolfe Morris](../W/Wolfe_Morris.md), who would voice of [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) in the 1968 radio series of *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>)*, is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s "Philology: General Works" is published in *[The Year's Work in English Studies](../T/The_Year's_Work_in_English_Studies.md)*.

## February {#February}

- **[2](2_February.md)** - Brother [Joseph Power](../J/Joseph_Power.md) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[14](14_March.md)** - [John Wain](../J/John_Wain.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), is born.

## April {#April}

- **[23](23_April.md)** - *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight](<../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(edition).md>)* is first published.
- **?** - "[Some Contributions to Middle-English Lexicography](../S/Some_Contributions_to_Middle-English_Lexicography.md)", an essay by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.

## June {#June}

- **[6](6_June.md)** - Actor [Gareth Jones](../G/Gareth_Jones.md) is born.
- **[8](8_June.md)** - Father [Robert Murray](../R/Robert_Murray.md), friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and his family, is born.
- **[12](12_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to a Mr. Ashton](../L/Letter_to_Mr_Ashton.md).
- **[27](27_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 7](../L/Letter_7.md).
- **?** - Tolkien's poem *[Light as Leaf on Lindentree](../L/Light_as_Leaf_on_Lindentree.md)* is published in *The Gryphon*.

## July {#July}

- **[22](22_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 8](../L/Letter_8.md).
- **[22](22_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to H.F.B. Brett-Smith](<../L/Letter_to_H.F.B._Brett-Smith_(22_July_1925).md>).
- **?** - *[The Devil's Coach-Horses](../T/The_Devil's_Coach-Horses.md)*, an essay by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.

## August {#August}

- **[8](8_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to H.F.B. Brett-Smith](<../L/Letter_to_H.F.B._Brett-Smith_(8_August_1925).md>).
- **[23](23_August.md)** - The first recorded date in the writing of the *[Lay of Leithian](../L/Lay_of_Leithian.md)*, at Line 557.

## October {#October}

- **[21](21_October.md)** - Tolkien submits his resignation from [Leeds](../U/University_of_Leeds.md), his employment officially ended [December 31](31_December.md), 1925.
- **[29](29_October.md)** - [Paul Daneman](../P/Paul_Daneman.md), who would voice [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) in the 1968 radio series of *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>)*, is born.

## December {#December}

- **[23](23_December.md)** - [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) is born.
- **[25](25_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien moves from Leeds to [Pembroke College](../P/Pembroke_College.md) of [Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md) where he is elected Rawlinson and Bosworth Professor of Anglo-Saxon.
- **?** - *[The Flight of the Noldoli from Valinor](../T/The_Flight_of_the_Noldoli_from_Valinor.md)* is published.
- **?** - The journal *[The Transactions of the Honourable Society of Cymmrodorion Session 1923-24](../T/The_Transactions_of_the_Honourable_Society_of_Cymmrodorion_Session_1923-24.md)*, which includes an essay with a contribution by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) as translator, is published.
- **?** - Author and academic [Charles Moorman](../C/Charles_Moorman.md) is born.
