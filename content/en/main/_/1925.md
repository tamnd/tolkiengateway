[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1925" title="wikipedia:1925">1925</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[5](/wiki/5_January "5 January")** - [Wolfe Morris](/wiki/Wolfe_Morris), who would voice of [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) in the 1968 radio series of *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)")*, is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s "Philology: General Works" is published in *[The Year's Work in English Studies](/wiki/The_Year%27s_Work_in_English_Studies)*.

## February {#February}

- **[2](/wiki/2_February "2 February")** - Brother [Joseph Power](/wiki/Joseph_Power) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[14](/wiki/14_March "14 March")** - [John Wain](/wiki/John_Wain), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), is born.

## April {#April}

- **[23](/wiki/23_April "23 April")** - *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight](</wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(edition)> "Sir Gawain and the Green Knight (edition)")* is first published.
- **?** - "[Some Contributions to Middle-English Lexicography](/wiki/Some_Contributions_to_Middle-English_Lexicography)", an essay by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.

## June {#June}

- **[6](/wiki/6_June "6 June")** - Actor [Gareth Jones](/wiki/Gareth_Jones) is born.
- **[8](/wiki/8_June "8 June")** - Father [Robert Murray](/wiki/Robert_Murray), friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and his family, is born.
- **[12](/wiki/12_June "12 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to a Mr. Ashton](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Ashton "Letter to Mr Ashton").
- **[27](/wiki/27_June "27 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 7](/wiki/Letter_7 "Letter 7").
- **?** - Tolkien's poem *[Light as Leaf on Lindentree](/wiki/Light_as_Leaf_on_Lindentree)* is published in *The Gryphon*.

## July {#July}

- **[22](/wiki/22_July "22 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 8](/wiki/Letter_8 "Letter 8").
- **[22](/wiki/22_July "22 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to H.F.B. Brett-Smith](</wiki/Letter_to_H.F.B._Brett-Smith_(22_July_1925)> "Letter to H.F.B. Brett-Smith (22 July 1925)").
- **?** - *[The Devil's Coach-Horses](/wiki/The_Devil%27s_Coach-Horses)*, an essay by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.

## August {#August}

- **[8](/wiki/8_August "8 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to H.F.B. Brett-Smith](</wiki/Letter_to_H.F.B._Brett-Smith_(8_August_1925)> "Letter to H.F.B. Brett-Smith (8 August 1925)").
- **[23](/wiki/23_August "23 August")** - The first recorded date in the writing of the *[Lay of Leithian](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian)*, at Line 557.

## October {#October}

- **[21](/wiki/21_October "21 October")** - Tolkien submits his resignation from [Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds "University of Leeds"), his employment officially ended [December 31](/wiki/31_December "31 December"), 1925.
- **[29](/wiki/29_October "29 October")** - [Paul Daneman](/wiki/Paul_Daneman), who would voice [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in the 1968 radio series of *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)")*, is born.

## December {#December}

- **[23](/wiki/23_December "23 December")** - [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) is born.
- **[25](/wiki/25_December "25 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien moves from Leeds to [Pembroke College](/wiki/Pembroke_College) of [Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford") where he is elected Rawlinson and Bosworth Professor of Anglo-Saxon.
- **?** - *[The Flight of the Noldoli from Valinor](/wiki/The_Flight_of_the_Noldoli_from_Valinor)* is published.
- **?** - The journal *[The Transactions of the Honourable Society of Cymmrodorion Session 1923-24](/wiki/The_Transactions_of_the_Honourable_Society_of_Cymmrodorion_Session_1923-24)*, which includes an essay with a contribution by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) as translator, is published.
- **?** - Author and academic [Charles Moorman](/wiki/Charles_Moorman) is born.
