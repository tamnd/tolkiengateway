**Éothain** was a rider of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).

## History {#History}

Éothain was a member of [Éomer](Éomer.md)'s [éored](Éored.md) that overran the [orcs](../O/Orcs.md) who had captured [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) at the fringe of [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md), and encountered [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) on [30 February](30_February.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md). Éothain scoffed at the idea that [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) even existed, believing them to be nothing but [a child's tale](../H/Holbytla.md), and argued against the gift of horses to [the three companions](../T/Three_Hunters.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Éothain* is a modernized [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name, which means "Horse-soldier". It contains the Old English element *Éoh* ("warhorse", "charger") and the modernized Old English element *[thegn](../T/Thain.md)* (in a military context "soldier").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is a "translation" of a [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md) name into Old English that was never recorded, though the first element is likely translated from Rohanese *lô*/*loh*, "war-horse".

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In one of the last drafts of [The Riders of Rohan](../T/The_Riders_of_Rohan.md), Éothain was Éomer's squire,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> whereas in a later draft of [The Ride of the Rohirrim](../T/The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.md), he was the captain of Éomer's household éored.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> No reference to Éothain's rank in Éomer's éored is made in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.
