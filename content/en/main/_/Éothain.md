**Éothain** was a rider of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

## History {#History}

Éothain was a member of [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer)'s [éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored "Éored") that overran the [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") who had captured [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") at the fringe of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest), and encountered [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) on [30 February](/wiki/30_February), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"). Éothain scoffed at the idea that [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) even existed, believing them to be nothing but [a child's tale](/wiki/Holbytla "Holbytla"), and argued against the gift of horses to [the three companions](/wiki/Three_Hunters "Three Hunters").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Éothain* is a modernized [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name, which means "Horse-soldier". It contains the Old English element *Éoh* ("warhorse", "charger") and the modernized Old English element *[thegn](/wiki/Thain "Thain")* (in a military context "soldier").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is a "translation" of a [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) name into Old English that was never recorded, though the first element is likely translated from Rohanese *lô*/*loh*, "war-horse".

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In one of the last drafts of [The Riders of Rohan](/wiki/The_Riders_of_Rohan), Éothain was Éomer's squire,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> whereas in a later draft of [The Ride of the Rohirrim](/wiki/The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim), he was the captain of Éomer's household éored.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> No reference to Éothain's rank in Éomer's éored is made in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
