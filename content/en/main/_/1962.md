## August {#August}

- **[1](1_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 240](../L/Letter_240.md).
- **[31](31_August.md)** - Actor [Dee Bradley Baker](../D/Dee_Bradley_Baker.md) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[8](8_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) begins writing [letter 241](../L/Letter_241.md).
- **[14](14_September.md)** - An Argentinian publisher [writes to J.R.R. Tolkien](<../L/Letter_to_Alina_Dadlez_(19_September_1962).md>) about a translation into Spanish of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* (*[El hobito](../E/El_hobito.md)*).
- **[17](17_September.md)** - [Alina Dadlez](../A/Alina_Dadlez.md) writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](<../L/Letter_to_Alina_Dadlez_(19_September_1962).md>).

- **[20](20_September.md)** - Cartoonist [Bill Amend](../B/Bill_Amend.md) is born.
- **?** - [Matt Blessing](../M/Matt_Blessing.md), the head of [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md) archives, is born.

- **[13](13_October.md)** - [Tim Tolkien](../T/Tim_Tolkien.md) is born.

- **[2](2_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 241a](../L/Letter_241a.md).
- **[14](14_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to H. Cotton Minchin](<../L/Letter_to_H._Cotton_Minchin_(14_November_1962).md>).

- **[22](22_November.md)** – A review in *The Listener* calls *The Adventures of Tom Bombadil* "close to genius".
- **[23](23_November.md)** - The literary supplement of *The Times* runs a review of *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](../T/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md)*.
