[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962" title="wikipedia:1962">1962</a></b>.

## April {#April}

- **[12](/wiki/12_April "12 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 237](/wiki/Letter_237 "Letter 237").
- **[15](/wiki/15_April "15 April")** - Actor [Tom Kane](/wiki/Tom_Kane) is born.
- **[24](/wiki/24_April "24 April")** - [Henry Gee](/wiki/Henry_Gee) is born.
- **?** - Actress [Tatyana Yassukovich](/wiki/Tatyana_Yassukovich) is born.

## July {#July}

- **[2](/wiki/2_July "2 July")** - [J.R.R.](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") and [Edith Tolkien](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien) make a trip to [Bournemouth](/wiki/Bournemouth).
- **[3](/wiki/3_July "3 July")** - [Alina Dadlez](/wiki/Alina_Dadlez) writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Alina_Dadlez_(20_July_1962)> "Letter to Alina Dadlez (20 July 1962)").
- **[18](/wiki/18_July "18 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 238](/wiki/Letter_238 "Letter 238").
- **[20](/wiki/20_July "20 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 239](/wiki/Letter_239 "Letter 239").
- **[20](/wiki/20_July "20 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Alina Dadlez](</wiki/Letter_to_Alina_Dadlez_(20_July_1962)> "Letter to Alina Dadlez (20 July 1962)").
- **[21](/wiki/21_July "21 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 239a](/wiki/Letter_239a "Letter 239a").

## August {#August}

- **[1](/wiki/1_August "1 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 240](/wiki/Letter_240 "Letter 240").
- **[31](/wiki/31_August "31 August")** - Actor [Dee Bradley Baker](/wiki/Dee_Bradley_Baker) is born.

## September {#September}

- **[8](/wiki/8_September "8 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) begins writing [letter 241](/wiki/Letter_241 "Letter 241").
- **[14](/wiki/14_September "14 September")** - An Argentinian publisher [writes to J.R.R. Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Alina_Dadlez_(19_September_1962)> "Letter to Alina Dadlez (19 September 1962)") about a translation into Spanish of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* (*[El hobito](/wiki/El_hobito)*).
- **[17](/wiki/17_September "17 September")** - [Alina Dadlez](/wiki/Alina_Dadlez) writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Alina_Dadlez_(19_September_1962)> "Letter to Alina Dadlez (19 September 1962)").
- **[19](/wiki/19_September "19 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Alina Dadlez](</wiki/Letter_to_Alina_Dadlez_(19_September_1962)> "Letter to Alina Dadlez (19 September 1962)").
- **[20](/wiki/20_September "20 September")** - Cartoonist [Bill Amend](/wiki/Bill_Amend) is born.
- **?** - [Matt Blessing](/wiki/Matt_Blessing), the head of [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University) archives, is born.

## October {#October}

- **[13](/wiki/13_October "13 October")** - [Tim Tolkien](/wiki/Tim_Tolkien) is born.
- **[22](/wiki/22_October "22 October")** - *[O Gnomo](/wiki/O_Gnomo)*, the first Portuguese translation of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, is published.

## November {#November}

- **[2](/wiki/2_November "2 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 241a](/wiki/Letter_241a "Letter 241a").
- **[14](/wiki/14_November "14 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to H. Cotton Minchin](</wiki/Letter_to_H._Cotton_Minchin_(14_November_1962)> "Letter to H. Cotton Minchin (14 November 1962)").
- **[19](/wiki/19_November "19 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 241b](/wiki/Letter_241b "Letter 241b").
- **[20](/wiki/20_November "20 November")** - [C. S. Lewis](/wiki/C._S._Lewis) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- **[22](/wiki/22_November "22 November")** - *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)* is published.
- **[22](/wiki/22_November "22 November")** – A review in *The Listener* calls *The Adventures of Tom Bombadil* "close to genius".
- **[23](/wiki/23_November "23 November")** - The literary supplement of *The Times* runs a review of *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*.
- **[28](/wiki/28_November "28 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 242](/wiki/Letter_242 "Letter 242").

## December {#December}

- **[7](/wiki/7_December "7 December")** - *[Ancrene Wisse](/wiki/Ancrene_Wisse)*, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s edition of a [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) manuscript, is published.
- **[10](/wiki/10_December "10 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is [interviewed by John Bowen](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien").
- **[16](/wiki/16_December "16 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 242a](/wiki/Letter_242a "Letter 242a").
- **[19](/wiki/19_December "19 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 243](/wiki/Letter_243 "Letter 243").
