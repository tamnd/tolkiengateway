[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1936" title="wikipedia:1936">1936</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[14](14_January.md)** - In his Preface to *[Roman Britain and the English Settlements](../R/Roman_Britain_and_the_English_Settlements.md)*, R.G. Collingwood on this date writes about [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s contribution to the book.

## March {#March}

- **[30](30_March.md)** - C.A. Furth (of [George Allen &amp; Unwin Ltd](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md)) writes a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien.

## April {#April}

- **[28](28_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes the manuscript "[Primitive Quendian: Final Consonants](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_21.md)".

## May {#May}

- **[17](17_May.md)** - Swedish author [Lars Gustafsson](../L/Lars_Gustafsson.md), who were to conduct an [interview](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is born.

## June {#June}

- **[14](14_June.md)** - Author [G.K. Chesterton](../G/G.K._Chesterton.md) dies.

## August {#August}

- **[10](10_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 8b](../L/Letter_8b.md).

## September {#September}

- **[4](4_September.md)** - German translator [Hans J. Schütz](../H/Hans_J._Schütz.md) is born.
- **[27](27_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Professor A. Pompen](../L/Letter_to_Aurelius_Pompen.md).

## October {#October}

- **[3](3_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) finishes retyping *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.
- **[10](10_October.md)** - [Stanley Unwin](../S/Stanley_Unwin.md) reads the typescript of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.
- **[17](17_October.md)** - [David Buck](../D/David_Buck.md), who would voice [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>), is born.
- **[21](21_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 8c](../L/Letter_8c.md).
- **[30](30_October.md)** - [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md), aged 10, writes a "[Report on the Hobbit](../R/Report_on_the_Hobbit.md)".

## November {#November}

- **[25](25_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) delivers the lecture "[Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics](../B/Beowulf%253A_The_Monsters_and_the_Critics.md)" to the British Academy.

## December {#December}

- **[17](17_December.md)** - [Marian Diamond](../M/Marian_Diamond.md), who would voice [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1981 radio series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>), is born.
- **[23](23_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Allen and Unwin](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md) read the manuscript of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and suggest that he complete it.
- **?** - *[Songs for the Philologists](../S/Songs_for_the_Philologists.md)* is published.
- **?** - Illustrator [Peter Chuklev](../P/Peter_Chuklev.md) is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s secretary [Joy Hill](../J/Joy_Hill.md) is born.
