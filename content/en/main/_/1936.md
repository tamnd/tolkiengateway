[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1936" title="wikipedia:1936">1936</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[14](/wiki/14_January "14 January")** - In his Preface to *[Roman Britain and the English Settlements](/wiki/Roman_Britain_and_the_English_Settlements)*, R.G. Collingwood on this date writes about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s contribution to the book.

## March {#March}

- **[30](/wiki/30_March "30 March")** - C.A. Furth (of [George Allen &amp; Unwin Ltd](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin "Allen and Unwin")) writes a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien.

## April {#April}

- **[28](/wiki/28_April "28 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes the manuscript "[Primitive Quendian: Final Consonants](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_21 "Parma Eldalamberon 21")".

## May {#May}

- **[17](/wiki/17_May "17 May")** - Swedish author [Lars Gustafsson](/wiki/Lars_Gustafsson), who were to conduct an [interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is born.

## June {#June}

- **[14](/wiki/14_June "14 June")** - Author [G.K. Chesterton](/wiki/G.K._Chesterton) dies.

## August {#August}

- **[10](/wiki/10_August "10 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 8b](/wiki/Letter_8b).

## September {#September}

- **[4](/wiki/4_September "4 September")** - German translator [Hans J. Schütz](/wiki/Hans_J._Sch%C3%BCtz) is born.
- **[27](/wiki/27_September "27 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Professor A. Pompen](/wiki/Letter_to_Aurelius_Pompen "Letter to Aurelius Pompen").

## October {#October}

- **[3](/wiki/3_October "3 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) finishes retyping *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.
- **[10](/wiki/10_October "10 October")** - [Stanley Unwin](/wiki/Stanley_Unwin) reads the typescript of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.
- **[17](/wiki/17_October "17 October")** - [David Buck](/wiki/David_Buck), who would voice [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"), is born.
- **[21](/wiki/21_October "21 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 8c](/wiki/Letter_8c).
- **[30](/wiki/30_October "30 October")** - [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin), aged 10, writes a "[Report on the Hobbit](/wiki/Report_on_the_Hobbit)".

## November {#November}

- **[25](/wiki/25_November "25 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) delivers the lecture "[Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics](/wiki/Beowulf:_The_Monsters_and_the_Critics)" to the British Academy.

## December {#December}

- **[17](/wiki/17_December "17 December")** - [Marian Diamond](/wiki/Marian_Diamond), who would voice [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1981 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"), is born.
- **[23](/wiki/23_December "23 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Allen and Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin) read the manuscript of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and suggest that he complete it.
- **?** - *[Songs for the Philologists](/wiki/Songs_for_the_Philologists)* is published.
- **?** - Illustrator [Peter Chuklev](/wiki/Peter_Chuklev) is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s secretary [Joy Hill](/wiki/Joy_Hill) is born.
