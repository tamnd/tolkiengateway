Events that occurred on 15 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1892](/wiki/1892)** - [Arthur](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien "Arthur Tolkien"), [Mabel](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield "Mabel Suffield"), two household servants, and the nurse holding [Ronald](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") pose for a picture in the garden at Bank House. The photograph is used in Tolkien Christmas cards sent to family and friends. The image is reproduced in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography)* and *[The Tolkien Family Album](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Family_Album)*.
- **[1937](/wiki/1937)** - Tolkien submits to [Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Allen_%26_Unwin) a bundle of manuscripts including *[The Lay of Leithian](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Leithian)* ("Long Poem"); *[Quenta Silmarillion](</wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road)> "Quenta Silmarillion (Lost Road)")*, *[Ainulindalë](</wiki/Ainulindal%C3%AB_(Lost_Road)> "Ainulindalë (Lost Road)")*, *[Ambarkanta](/wiki/Ambarkanta)*, and *[The Fall of Númenor (chapter)](</wiki/The_Fall_of_N%C3%BAmenor_(chapter)>)* ("The Gnomes Material"); [The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road); and *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)* and *[Mr. Bliss](/wiki/Mr._Bliss)*. They also discuss *[Beowulf](/wiki/Beowulf_and_the_Finnsburg_Fragment "Beowulf and the Finnsburg Fragment")*.
- **[1961](/wiki/1961)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 233](/wiki/Letter_233).
- **[1961](/wiki/1961)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Jane Neave](</wiki/Letter_to_Jane_Neave_(15_November_1961)> "Letter to Jane Neave (15 November 1961)").
- <b><a href="/wiki/1978" title="1978">1978</a> - The <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)">1978 animated film</a> of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> premières in the United States.</b>
- **[1983](/wiki/1983)** - Actor [John Le Mesurier](/wiki/John_Le_Mesurier) dies.
- **[1983](/wiki/1983)** - Illustrator [Eric Fraser](/wiki/Eric_Fraser) dies.
- **[1991](/wiki/1991)** - *[After the King: Stories In Honor of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/After_the_King "After the King")* is published.
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - *[Myth and Magic: Art according to the Inklings](/wiki/Myth_and_Magic:_Art_according_to_the_Inklings)* is published.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - *[Parma Eldalamberon](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon)* issue [19](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_19 "Parma Eldalamberon 19") is published.
- **[2014](/wiki/2014)** - *[War of Words: Soldier-Poets of the Somme](/wiki/War_of_Words:_Soldier-Poets_of_the_Somme)* is aired.
- **[2014](/wiki/2014)** - *[ArdaCraft](/wiki/ArdaCraft)* begins building Middle-earth.
- <b><a href="/wiki/2022" title="2022">2022</a> - <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_Fall_of_N%C3%BAmenor_(book)" title="The Fall of Númenor (book)">The Fall of Númenor</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[2022](/wiki/2022)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Before the Shadow](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Before_the_Shadow)* is released online.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") rest in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").
