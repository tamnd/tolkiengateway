Events that occurred on 19 January.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1918](1918.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) attends a medical board examination. He is based with 9th Royal Defence Corps at Easington.
- **[1965](1965.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 268](../L/Letter_268.md).
- **[1996](1996.md)** - The [Tolkien Ensemble](../T/Tolkien_Ensemble.md) have their first concert at Gjorslev Castle, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark" title="wikipedia:Denmark">Denmark</a>.
- **[2022](2022.md)** - The official title of [The Lord of the Rings tv series](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md) is announced.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) rests in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).
