Events that occurred on 21 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1915](/wiki/1915)** – [G.B. Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith "Geoffrey Bache Smith") is shipped to France.
- **[1924](/wiki/1924) – [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) is born.**
- **[1964](/wiki/1964)** – The seventh episode of the [Princess](/wiki/Princess)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[1972](/wiki/1972)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 343](/wiki/Letter_343).
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** – The [RiffTrax](/wiki/RiffTrax) commentary of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* is released online.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") rest in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").
