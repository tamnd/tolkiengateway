Events that occurred on 21 November.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1915](1915.md)** – [G.B. Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md) is shipped to France.
- **[1924](1924.md) – [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) is born.**
- **[1964](1964.md)** – The seventh episode of the [Princess](../P/Princess.md)-serialization of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* is published.
- **[1972](1972.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 343](../L/Letter_343.md).
- **[2006](2006.md)** – The [RiffTrax](../R/RiffTrax.md) commentary of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* is released online.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) rest in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and prepare for their quest to destroy [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).
