[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974" title="wikipedia:1974">1974</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[16](/wiki/16_February "16 February")** - On the advice of [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) appoints [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) as Honorary President in perpetuo.
- **[20](/wiki/20_February "20 February")** - Artist and linguist [Ryszard Derdziński](/wiki/Ryszard_Derdzi%C5%84ski) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[21](/wiki/21_March "21 March")** - Actor [Joseph Mawle](/wiki/Joseph_Mawle) is born.

## May {#May}

- **?** - *[Tolkien's World](</wiki/Tolkien%27s_World_(1974)> "Tolkien's World (1974)")*, by [Randel Helms](/wiki/Randel_Helms), is published.

## August {#August}

- **[16](/wiki/16_August "16 August")** - Tolkien scholar [Corey Olsen](/wiki/Corey_Olsen) is born.
- **[23](/wiki/23_August "23 August")-[26](/wiki/26_August "26 August")** - [Mythcon 5](/wiki/Mythcon_5).

## September {#September}

- **[13](/wiki/13_September "13 September")-[15](/wiki/15_September "15 September")** - The first [Oxonmoot](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1974 "Oxonmoot 1974") is held in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford).

## October {#October}

- **[31](/wiki/31_October "31 October")** - Artist [Randy Gallegos](/wiki/Randy_Gallegos) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[26](/wiki/26_November "26 November")** - *[Bilbo's Last Song](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Last_Song)* is published as a poster.

## December {#December}

- **[20](/wiki/20_December "20 December")** - Actor [Nathan Clark](/wiki/Nathan_Clark) is born.
