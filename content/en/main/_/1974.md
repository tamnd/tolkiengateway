- **[16](16_February.md)** - On the advice of [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) appoints [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) as Honorary President in perpetuo.

- **[21](21_March.md)** - Actor [Joseph Mawle](../J/Joseph_Mawle.md) is born.
