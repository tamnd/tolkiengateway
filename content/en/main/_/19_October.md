Events that occurred on 19 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1932](1932.md)** - Artist [Fritz Möser](../F/Fritz_Möser.md) is born.
- **[1954](1954.md)** - Actor [Ken Stott](../K/Ken_Stott.md) is born.
- **[1971](1971.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) receives a letter from [William Cater](../W/William_Cater.md) (see [Letter 330](../L/Letter_330.md)).
- **[1976](1976.md)** - *[The Father Christmas Letters](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md)* is published in the US.<b></b>
- **[2005](2005.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Sketchbook](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Sketchbook.md)* is published.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - *[The Dragon's Apprentice](../T/The_Dragon's_Apprentice.md)* is published.
- **[2011](2011.md)** - Brother [Joseph Power](../J/Joseph_Power.md) dies.
- **[2016](2016.md)** - The posthumous publication, *[Beren and Lúthien](../B/Beren_and_Lúthien.md)*, edited by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), is announced for publication on [4 May](4_May.md) [2017](2017.md).

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md):
    - [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), guided by [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md), travels to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) in order to deliver [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md)'s message to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md):
    - [Durin's Day](../D/Durin's_Day.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) hurries [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) and the [four hobbits](../T/Travellers.md) along and they cover a great distance towards the [Ford of Bruinen](../F/Ford_of_Bruinen.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
    - Last quarter<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
