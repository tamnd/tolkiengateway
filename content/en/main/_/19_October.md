Events that occurred on 19 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1932](/wiki/1932)** - Artist [Fritz Möser](/wiki/Fritz_M%C3%B6ser) is born.
- **[1954](/wiki/1954)** - Actor [Ken Stott](/wiki/Ken_Stott) is born.
- **[1971](/wiki/1971)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) receives a letter from [William Cater](/wiki/William_Cater) (see [Letter 330](/wiki/Letter_330)).
- **[1976](/wiki/1976)** - *[The Father Christmas Letters](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas")* is published in the US.<b></b>
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Sketchbook](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Sketchbook)* is published.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - *[The Dragon's Apprentice](/wiki/The_Dragon%27s_Apprentice)* is published.
- **[2011](/wiki/2011)** - Brother [Joseph Power](/wiki/Joseph_Power) dies.
- **[2016](/wiki/2016)** - The posthumous publication, *[Beren and Lúthien](/wiki/Beren_and_L%C3%BAthien)*, edited by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), is announced for publication on [4 May](/wiki/4_May) [2017](/wiki/2017).

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"):
    - [Durin's Day](/wiki/Durin%27s_Day).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) hurries [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") and the [four hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") along and they cover a great distance towards the [Ford of Bruinen](/wiki/Ford_of_Bruinen).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
    - Last quarter<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
