Events that occurred on 18 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1905](/wiki/1905)** - Author [George MacDonald](/wiki/George_MacDonald) dies.
- **[1954](/wiki/1954)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes letters [150](/wiki/Letter_150 "Letter 150") and [151](/wiki/Letter_151 "Letter 151").
- **[1954](/wiki/1954)** - [Naomi Mitchison](/wiki/Naomi_Mitchison) published a review of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* in the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Statesman" title="wikipedia:New Statesman">New Statesman</a></i>.
- **[1955](/wiki/1955)** - Voice actor [Bob Papenbrook](/wiki/Bob_Papenbrook) is born.
- **[1963](/wiki/1963)** - Writer [Gary Russell](/wiki/Gary_Russell) is born.
- **[1967](/wiki/1967)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Elsie Honeybourne](</wiki/Letter_to_Elsie_Honeybourne_(18_September_1967)> "Letter to Elsie Honeybourne (18 September 1967)").
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - Extracts from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s "[The Name Coventry](/wiki/The_Name_Coventry)" is published in *[The Catholic Herald](</wiki/The_Catholic_Herald_(18_September_1981)> "The Catholic Herald (18 September 1981)")*.
- **[1982](/wiki/1982)** - The tenth episode of the new version of the [1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[1993](/wiki/1993)** - [Oxonmoot 1993](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1993) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:University College, Oxford">University College</a>.
- **[1994](/wiki/1994)** - [Oxonmoot 1994](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1994) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:University College, Oxford">University College</a>.
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - [Oxonmoot 2003](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2003) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Hugh%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Hugh&#39;s College, Oxford">St Hugh&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - [Oxonmoot 2004](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2004) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Hilda%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Hilda&#39;s College, Oxford">St Hilda&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - [The Lord of the Rings Symphony](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony) at Mortensen Hall in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartford,_Connecticut" title="wikipedia:Hartford, Connecticut">Hartford</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut" title="wikipedia:Connecticut">Connecticut</a>.
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - [Oxonmoot 2005](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2005) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Peter%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Peter&#39;s College, Oxford">St Peter&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)* is announced.
- **[2020](/wiki/2020)** - [Oxonmoot 2020](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2020) held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir) rescues [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) from the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc) in the early hours.
    - The [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") cross the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen).
    - The Black Riders visit [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) who lies that Gandalf has "confessed", and gives them directions to [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
