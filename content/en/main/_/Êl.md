<i><b>êl</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "[star](/wiki/Star "Star")". It is archaic and poetic, only recorded being used in verses.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [EL](/wiki/EL)
