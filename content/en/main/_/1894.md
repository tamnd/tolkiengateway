[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1894" title="wikipedia:1894">1894</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[17](/wiki/17_February "17 February")** - [Hilary Arthur Reuel Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien "Hilary Tolkien"), younger brother to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is born in [Bloemfontein](/wiki/Bloemfontein), South Africa.

## October {#October}

- **[18](/wiki/18_October "18 October")** - [Geoffrey Bache Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith), childhood friend of Tolkien, is born.

## November {#November}

- [Mabel](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield "Mabel Suffield"), [Hilary](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien "Hilary Tolkien"), and [Ronald](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") take a train to the coast near Cape Town so Ronald can be in the cooler air.
- After they return home a trip to England is decided as [Mabel](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield "Mabel Suffield") dislikes the hot climate and the [Bloemfontein](/wiki/Bloemfontein) lifestyle, even though [Arthur](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien "Arthur Tolkien") is happy with it.

## Christmas {#Christmas}

- "My first Christmas memory is of blazing sun, drawn curtains and a drooping eucalyptus" ([Letter 163](/wiki/Letter_163))
