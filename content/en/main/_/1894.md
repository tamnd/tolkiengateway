[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1894" title="wikipedia:1894">1894</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[17](17_February.md)** - [Hilary Arthur Reuel Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md), younger brother to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is born in [Bloemfontein](../B/Bloemfontein.md), South Africa.

## October {#October}

- **[18](18_October.md)** - [Geoffrey Bache Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md), childhood friend of Tolkien, is born.

## November {#November}

- [Mabel](../M/Mabel_Suffield.md), [Hilary](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md), and [Ronald](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) take a train to the coast near Cape Town so Ronald can be in the cooler air.
- After they return home a trip to England is decided as [Mabel](../M/Mabel_Suffield.md) dislikes the hot climate and the [Bloemfontein](../B/Bloemfontein.md) lifestyle, even though [Arthur](../A/Arthur_Tolkien.md) is happy with it.

## Christmas {#Christmas}

- "My first Christmas memory is of blazing sun, drawn curtains and a drooping eucalyptus" ([Letter 163](../L/Letter_163.md))
