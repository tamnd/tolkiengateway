Events that occurred on 22 January.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1928](1928.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) reads a paper, *[The Chill Barbarians of the North](../T/The_Chill_Barbarians_of_the_North.md)*, at a meeting of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Newman_Society" title="wikipedia:Oxford University Newman Society">Newman Society</a>, in Oxford.
- **[1940](1940.md)** - Actor [John Hurt](../J/John_Hurt.md) is born.
- **[1954](1954.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 143](../L/Letter_143.md).
- **[1956](1956.md)** - The *New York Times Book Review* publishes [W.H. Auden](../W/W.H._Auden.md)'s review of *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)*, "[At the End of the Quest, Victory](../A/At_the_End_of_the_Quest,_Victory.md)".
- **[2003](2003.md)** - Tolkien's eldest son [John Francis Reuel Tolkien](../J/John_Tolkien.md) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) rests in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).
    - [Uglúk](../U/Uglúk.md)'s troop reach [Moria](../M/Moria.md), and gather a group of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) there.
