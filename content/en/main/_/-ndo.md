<i><b>-ndo</b></i> is a masculine agental suffix.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:190</sup> The feminine counterpart is <i><b>-ndë</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:69</sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i>meli<b>ndo</b></i> = "lover"
- <i>coli<b>ndo</b></i> = "bearer"
