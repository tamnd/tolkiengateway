Events that occurred on 22 February.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1963](1963.md)** - [Jane Neave](../J/Jane_Neave.md), aunt of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), dies.
- **[1984](1984.md)** - *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One.md)* is published in the US.<b></b>
- **[2004](2004.md)** - At the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10th_Screen_Actors_Guild_Awards" title="wikipedia:10th Screen Actors Guild Awards">10th Screen Actors Guild Awards</a>, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* wins the award for "Outstanding Performance by a Cast in a Motion Picture". The award is accepted by [Bernard Hill](../B/Bernard_Hill.md).

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) travels south down the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).
    - [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) sees [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) and talks to [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) about him.
    - At night, [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md) arrives at [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).
    - New Moon<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Notes {#Notes}

In the text we are told that "*the white rind of the new Moon could be seen glimmering in the remote lakes*", which puts the actual new Moon rather earlier than astronomy would calculate.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
