## February {#February}

- **[9](/wiki/9_February "9 February")** - Author [Gisbert Kranz](/wiki/Gisbert_Kranz) is born.
- **[12](/wiki/12_February "12 February")** - [Saul Zaentz](/wiki/Saul_Zaentz), producer and movie rights holder of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, is born.

## March {#March}

- **[16](/wiki/16_March "16 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is appointed to membership of the Faculty of Arts and the Board of the Faculty Arts at the [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds).

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[Fourteenth Century Verse and Prose](/wiki/Fourteenth_Century_Verse_and_Prose)* is published, but without Tolkien's *[A Middle English Vocabulary](/wiki/A_Middle_English_Vocabulary)*.
- **?** - Author [Alan Bliss](/wiki/Alan_Bliss) is born.
