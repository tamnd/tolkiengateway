Events that occurred on 5 April.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1912](/wiki/1912)** - Actor [John Le Mesurier](/wiki/John_Le_Mesurier) is born.
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 59](/wiki/Letter_59).
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - The fifth episode of [the 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[2001](/wiki/2001)** - Actor [Theodore Isidore Gottlieb](/wiki/Theodore_Isidore_Gottlieb) dies of pneumonia.
- **[2016](/wiki/2016)** - *[The Story of Kullervo](/wiki/The_Story_of_Kullervo)* is published in the USA.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), rescued out of the fire, rest in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).
