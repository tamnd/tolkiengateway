Events that occurred on 13 January.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1960](1960.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) sends [a letter to Elizabeth Jones](../L/Letter_to_Elizabeth_Jones.md).
- **[1977](1977.md)** - Actor [Orlando Bloom](../O/Orlando_Bloom.md) is born.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Conquest](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Conquest.md)* is released in North America.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - *[The Power of Tolkien's Prose: Middle-Earth's Magical Style](../T/The_Power_of_Tolkien's_Prose%253A_Middle-Earth's_Magical_Style.md)* by Steve Walker is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) is attacked a second time by a pack of [Wargs](../W/Wargs.md) in the early hours.
    - They reach the [West-gate of Moria](../D/Doors_of_Durin.md) at nightfall. The [Watcher](../W/Watcher_in_the_Water.md) attacks and jams the West-gate.
    - [Bill the Pony](../B/Bill_the_Pony.md) leaves.
    - [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) begins to trail the [Ring-bearer](../R/Ring-bearer.md).

## Notes {#Notes}

According to astronomy, the moon on that day of 3019, would have been a waning gibbous, which rises after midnight (and thus early on [January 14](14_January.md)) until it passes over the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) and shine on the *western* side where the Gate was.

So the [Tale of Years](../A/Appendix_B.md) must be incorrect when it has the Company enter Moria before midnight on the 13th.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
