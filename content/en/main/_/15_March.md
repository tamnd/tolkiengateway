Events that occurred on 15 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1901](/wiki/1901)** - [Robert Havard](/wiki/Robert_Havard), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), is born.
- **[1942](/wiki/1942)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to John Kettle](/wiki/Letter_to_John_Kettle "Letter to John Kettle").
- **[1965](/wiki/1965)** - Actress [Robyn Malcolm](/wiki/Robyn_Malcolm) is born.
- **[1969](/wiki/1969)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Amy Ronald](</wiki/Letter_to_Amy_Ronald_(15_March_1969)> "Letter to Amy Ronald (15 March 1969)").
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - The second episode of [the 1981 radio adaptation](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** - *[Arda Reconstructed: The Creation of the Published Silmarillion](/wiki/Arda_Reconstructed:_The_Creation_of_the_Published_Silmarillion)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"):
    - [Borondir](/wiki/Borondir) is on his way in search of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) meets [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) at [the Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony "The Prancing Pony"), and they devise a plan to rid [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") of [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - In the early morning the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) breaks the [Gates](/wiki/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith "Great Gate of Minas Tirith") of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).
    - [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) burns himself on a pyre. [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) is rescued by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond) and brought to the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing).
    - The horns of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) echo in the sides of [Mindolluin](/wiki/Mindolluin) at cockcrow.
    - [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields):
        - Death of [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden).
        - [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) slays the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) with the assistance of [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck).
        - [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) arrives on [the battlefield](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields "Pelennor Fields") and raises the [standard of Elendil](/wiki/Standard_of_Elendil "Standard of Elendil") woven by [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen).
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") escape the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol) and begin their journey north along the [Morgai](/wiki/Morgai).
    - The [Battle under the trees](/wiki/Battle_under_the_trees) in [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) takes place. [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) drives back the enemy from [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur).
    - Second [assault](/wiki/Assaults_on_Lothl%C3%B3rien "Assaults on Lothlórien") on [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).
