Events that occurred on 15 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1901](1901.md)** - [Robert Havard](../R/Robert_Havard.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), is born.
- **[1942](1942.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to John Kettle](../L/Letter_to_John_Kettle.md).
- **[1965](1965.md)** - Actress [Robyn Malcolm](../R/Robyn_Malcolm.md) is born.
- **[1969](1969.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Amy Ronald](<../L/Letter_to_Amy_Ronald_(15_March_1969).md>).
- **[1981](1981.md)** - The second episode of [the 1981 radio adaptation](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](../B/BBC.md).
- **[2009](2009.md)** - *[Arda Reconstructed: The Creation of the Published Silmarillion](../A/Arda_Reconstructed%253A_The_Creation_of_the_Published_Silmarillion.md)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md):
    - [Borondir](../B/Borondir.md) is on his way in search of the [Éothéod](Éothéod.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) meets [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) at [the Prancing Pony](../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md), and they devise a plan to rid [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) of [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - In the early morning the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) breaks the [Gates](../G/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith.md) of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).
    - [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) burns himself on a pyre. [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) is rescued by [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Beregond](../B/Beregond.md) and brought to the [Houses of Healing](../H/Houses_of_Healing.md).
    - The horns of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) echo in the sides of [Mindolluin](../M/Mindolluin.md) at cockcrow.
    - [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md):
        - Death of [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md).
        - [Éowyn](Éowyn.md) slays the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) with the assistance of [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md).
        - [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) arrives on [the battlefield](../P/Pelennor_Fields.md) and raises the [standard of Elendil](../S/Standard_of_Elendil.md) woven by [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md).
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) escape the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](../T/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md) and begin their journey north along the [Morgai](../M/Morgai.md).
    - The [Battle under the trees](../B/Battle_under_the_trees.md) in [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) takes place. [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) drives back the enemy from [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md).
    - Second [assault](../A/Assaults_on_Lothlórien.md) on [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).
