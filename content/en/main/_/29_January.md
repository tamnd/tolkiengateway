Events that occurred on 29 January

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1909](/wiki/1909)** - Author and collector [Richard E. Blackwelder](/wiki/Richard_E._Blackwelder) is born.
- **[1943](/wiki/1943)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to J.L.N. O'Loughlin](</wiki/Letter_to_J.L.N._O%27Loughlin_(29_January_1943)> "Letter to J.L.N. O'Loughlin (29 January 1943)").
- **[1953](/wiki/1953)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 135c](/wiki/Letter_135c).
- **[1954](/wiki/1954)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to the Rector of the University of Liège](</wiki/Letter_to_Li%C3%A8ge_University_(29_January_1954)> "Letter to Liège University (29 January 1954)").
- **[1958](/wiki/1958)** - Actor [Alister Duncan](/wiki/Alister_Duncan) is born.
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 283b](/wiki/Letter_283b "Letter 283b").
- **[1968](/wiki/1968)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Ken Jackson](</wiki/Letter_to_Ken_Jackson_(29_January_1968)> "Letter to Ken Jackson (29 January 1968)").
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - [J.A.W. Bennett](/wiki/J.A.W._Bennett), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), dies.
