<i><b>-rya</b></i> is a pronominal [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) ending meaning "his, her"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in **máryat** "her hands", **ómaryo** "of her voice" (genitive of \***ómarya** "her voice"), **súmaryassë** "in her bosom" (locative of **súmarya** "her bosom"); for the meaning "his" cf. **coarya** "his house".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
