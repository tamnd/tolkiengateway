[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1960" title="wikipedia:1960">1960</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[13](/wiki/13_January "13 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) sends [a letter to Elizabeth Jones](/wiki/Letter_to_Elizabeth_Jones "Letter to Elizabeth Jones").

## February {#February}

- **[9](/wiki/9_February "9 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 222a](/wiki/Letter_222a "Letter 222a").
- **[12](/wiki/12_February "12 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 222b](/wiki/Letter_222b "Letter 222b").
- **[29](/wiki/29_February "29 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 222c](/wiki/Letter_222c "Letter 222c").

## March {#March}

- **[1](/wiki/1_March "1 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 222d](/wiki/Letter_222d "Letter 222d").
- **[17](/wiki/17_March "17 March")** - [Jef Murray](/wiki/Jef_Murray) is born.

## April {#April}

- **[4](/wiki/4_April "4 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 222e](/wiki/Letter_222e "Letter 222e").
- **[4](/wiki/4_April "4 April")** - [Hugo Weaving](/wiki/Hugo_Weaving), who would play [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.

## May {#May}

- **[5](/wiki/5_May "5 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 222f](/wiki/Letter_222f "Letter 222f").
- **?** - [*Triode* 18](/wiki/Triode_18 "Triode 18"), including [a letter by J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Letter_to_the_editor_of_Triode "Letter to the editor of Triode"), is published.

## July {#July}

- **[7](/wiki/7_July "7 July")** - [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") writes [a letter](/wiki/Letter_to_H.W._Donner "Letter to H.W. Donner") to [H.W. Donner](/wiki/H.W._Donner).
- **[9](/wiki/9_July "9 July")** - [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") has a formal dinner in [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge).
- **[31](/wiki/31_July "31 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 223](/wiki/Letter_223 "Letter 223").

## September {#September}

- **[4](/wiki/4_September "4 September")** - [The Fellowship of the Ring](</wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(fangroup)> "The Fellowship of the Ring (fangroup)") becomes formally constituted.
- **[12](/wiki/12_September "12 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 224](/wiki/Letter_224 "Letter 224").
- **[23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")** - [Jason Carter](/wiki/Jason_Carter), who would voice [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) in *[EA's The Battle for Middle-earth II](/wiki/EA%27s_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II)* and *[The Rise of the Witch-king](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II:_The_Rise_of_the_Witch-king "The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II: The Rise of the Witch-king")*, is born.

## October {#October}

- **[25](/wiki/25_October "25 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) seeks (through his secretary <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Elisabeth_Lumsden&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Elisabeth Lumsden (page does not exist)">Elisabeth Lumsden</a>) to publish "a sort of satirical fantasy") believed to be *[The End of Bovadium](/wiki/The_End_of_Bovadium)* in the magazine <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_and_Tide_(magazine)" title="wikipedia:Time and Tide (magazine)">Time and Tide</a></i>.

## December {#December}

- **[10](/wiki/10_December "10 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 225](/wiki/Letter_225 "Letter 225").
- **[14](/wiki/14_December "14 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) draws [Idril Celebrindal's heraldic device](</wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Idril_Celebrindal_heraldic_device_(I).jpg> "File:J.R.R. Tolkien - Idril Celebrindal heraldic device (I).jpg").
- **[31](/wiki/31_December "31 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 226](/wiki/Letter_226 "Letter 226").

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [I.L. Gordon](/wiki/I.L._Gordon) publishes an edition of the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) poem *[The Seafarer](</wiki/The_Seafarer_(I.L._Gordon)> "The Seafarer (I.L. Gordon)")*, to which [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) contributed.
- **?** - *[The Four Loves](/wiki/The_Four_Loves)*, a book by [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) with a reference to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **?** - Actor [Peter Hambleton](/wiki/Peter_Hambleton) is born.
