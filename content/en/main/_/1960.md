## January {#January}

- **[13](13_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) sends [a letter to Elizabeth Jones](../L/Letter_to_Elizabeth_Jones.md).

## February {#February}

- **[9](9_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 222a](../L/Letter_222a.md).
- **[12](12_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 222b](../L/Letter_222b.md).
- **[29](29_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 222c](../L/Letter_222c.md).

## March {#March}

- **[1](1_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 222d](../L/Letter_222d.md).
- **[17](17_March.md)** - [Jef Murray](../J/Jef_Murray.md) is born.

## April {#April}

- **[4](4_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 222e](../L/Letter_222e.md).
- **[4](4_April.md)** - [Hugo Weaving](../H/Hugo_Weaving.md), who would play [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.

## May {#May}

- **[5](5_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 222f](../L/Letter_222f.md).
- **?** - [*Triode* 18](../T/Triode_18.md), including [a letter by J.R.R. Tolkien](../L/Letter_to_the_editor_of_Triode.md), is published.

## July {#July}

- **[7](7_July.md)** - [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter](../L/Letter_to_H.W._Donner.md) to [H.W. Donner](../H/H.W._Donner.md).
- **[9](9_July.md)** - [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) has a formal dinner in [Cambridge](../C/Cambridge.md).
- **[31](31_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 223](../L/Letter_223.md).

## September {#September}

- **[4](4_September.md)** - [The Fellowship of the Ring](<../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(fangroup).md>) becomes formally constituted.
- **[12](12_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 224](../L/Letter_224.md).
- **[23](23_September.md)** - [Jason Carter](../J/Jason_Carter.md), who would voice [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) in *[EA's The Battle for Middle-earth II](../E/EA's_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md)* and *[The Rise of the Witch-king](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II%253A_The_Rise_of_the_Witch-king.md)*, is born.

## October {#October}

- **[25](25_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) seeks (through his secretary <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Elisabeth_Lumsden&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Elisabeth Lumsden (page does not exist)">Elisabeth Lumsden</a>) to publish "a sort of satirical fantasy") believed to be *[The End of Bovadium](../T/The_End_of_Bovadium.md)* in the magazine <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_and_Tide_(magazine)" title="wikipedia:Time and Tide (magazine)">Time and Tide</a></i>.

## December {#December}

- **[10](10_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 225](../L/Letter_225.md).
- **[14](14_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) draws [Idril Celebrindal's heraldic device](<../../file/J/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Idril_Celebrindal_heraldic_device_(I).jpg.md>).
- **[31](31_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 226](../L/Letter_226.md).

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[The Four Loves](../T/The_Four_Loves.md)*, a book by [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) with a reference to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.
- **?** - Actor [Peter Hambleton](../P/Peter_Hambleton.md) is born.
