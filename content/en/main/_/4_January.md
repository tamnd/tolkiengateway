## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1892](/wiki/1892)** - [Arthur Tolkien](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien) sends a letter to his mother about [his new son](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien").
- **[1937](/wiki/1937)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 9](/wiki/Letter_9).
- **[1940](/wiki/1940)** - Voice actor [David Collings](/wiki/David_Collings) is born.
- **[1961](/wiki/1961)** - Actor [Graham McTavish](/wiki/Graham_McTavish) is born.
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - Author and linguist [Arden R. Smith](/wiki/Arden_R._Smith) is born.
- **[1968](/wiki/1968)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Ken Jackson](</wiki/Letter_to_Ken_Jackson_(4_January_1968)> "Letter to Ken Jackson (4 January 1968)").
- **[1991](/wiki/1991)** - Actress [Olivia Tennet](/wiki/Olivia_Tennet) is born.
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - [Biographer](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography "J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography") and [editor](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien") [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter) dies.
