**Otr** was a [demon](../D/Demon.md) "in otter's form" who appears briefly in *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](../T/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudrún.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Otr was the son of <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hreidmar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hreidmar (page does not exist)">Hreidmar</a>, a [demon](../D/Demon.md), and the brother of <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=F%C3%A1fnir&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fáfnir (page does not exist)">Fáfnir</a> and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Regin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Regin (page does not exist)">Regin</a>. He thought [silver](../S/Silver.md) [salmon](../S/Salmon.md) were sweet to eat. One day, when he was fishing at the "black waters" of <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Andvari&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Andvari (page does not exist)">Andvari</a>'s falls, he was killed with a stone by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Loki&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Loki (page does not exist)">Loki</a> before being stripped and flayed. Loki and the other gods later brought Otr's body to Hreidmar’s halls to trade for housing.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>
