[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972" title="wikipedia:1972">1972</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](1_January.md)** - Actress [Jennifer Hale](../J/Jennifer_Hale.md) is born.
- **[2](2_January.md)** - In honour of his 80th birthday the following day, [*The Sunday Times Magazine*](<../T/The_Sunday_Times_Magazine_(2_January_1972).md>) runs an interview of [William Cater](../W/William_Cater.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), "The Lord of the Legends".
- **[3](3_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) celebrates his 80th birthday.
- **[3](3_January.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) wishes J.R.R. Tolkien a happy 80th birthday in *The Times.*
- **[3](3_January.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) sends Tolkien a jar of tobacco as a birthday present.
- **[15](15_January.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) holds a general meeting and becomes formally constituted.
- **[24](24_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 332](../L/Letter_332.md).
- **[30](30_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to his grandson Michael George Tolkien](<../L/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(30_January_1972).md>).
