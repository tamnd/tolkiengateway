[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972" title="wikipedia:1972">1972</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** - Actress [Jennifer Hale](/wiki/Jennifer_Hale) is born.
- **[2](/wiki/2_January "2 January")** - In honour of his 80th birthday the following day, [*The Sunday Times Magazine*](</wiki/The_Sunday_Times_Magazine_(2_January_1972)> "The Sunday Times Magazine (2 January 1972)") runs an interview of [William Cater](/wiki/William_Cater) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), "The Lord of the Legends".
- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) celebrates his 80th birthday.
- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) wishes J.R.R. Tolkien a happy 80th birthday in *The Times.*
- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) sends Tolkien a jar of tobacco as a birthday present.
- **[15](/wiki/15_January "15 January")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) holds a general meeting and becomes formally constituted.
- **[24](/wiki/24_January "24 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 332](/wiki/Letter_332).
- **[30](/wiki/30_January "30 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to his grandson Michael George Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(30_January_1972)> "Letter to Michael George Tolkien (30 January 1972)").

## February {#February}

- **[2](/wiki/2_February "2 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Letter_to_Dorothy_Wood_(2_February_1972)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Letter to Dorothy Wood (2 February 1972) (page does not exist)">a letter to Dorothy Wood</a>.
- **[2](/wiki/2_February "2 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Edmund R. Meskys](</wiki/Letter_to_Edmund_R._Meskys_(2_February_1972)> "Letter to Edmund R. Meskys (2 February 1972)")
- **[6](/wiki/6_February "6 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Vera Chapman and the Tolkien Society](/wiki/Letter_to_Vera_Chapman_and_the_Tolkien_Society "Letter to Vera Chapman and the Tolkien Society").
- **[9](/wiki/9_February "9 February")** - Actor [Crispin Freeman](/wiki/Crispin_Freeman) is born.
- **[16](/wiki/16_February "16 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Miss Fay Darrington](</wiki/Letter_to_Fay_Darrington_(16_February_1972)> "Letter to Fay Darrington (16 February 1972)").
- **[26](/wiki/26_February "26 February")** - Actor [Keith Ferguson](/wiki/Keith_Ferguson) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[16](/wiki/16_March "16 March")** - Tolkien writes [letter 333](/wiki/Letter_333 "Letter 333").
- **[27](/wiki/27_March "27 March")** - [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) organizes a party in celebration of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s CBE the [following day](/wiki/28_March "28 March").
- **[28](/wiki/28_March "28 March")** - At Buckingham Palace, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is appointed a [CBE](/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire "Order of the British Empire") by Queen Elizabeth II.

- **[27](/wiki/27_June "27 June")** - [Vera Chapman](/wiki/Vera_Chapman) meets [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). He agrees to become [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s Honorary President.
