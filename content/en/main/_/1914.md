[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1914" title="wikipedia:1914">1914</a></b>.

## Early {#Early}

- **?** - [Edith Bratt](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) converts to Catholicism and becomes engaged to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

## January {#January}

- **[6](6_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) begins working on *[The Book of Ishness](../T/The_Book_of_Ishness.md)*.
- **[10](10_January.md)** - Scholar [Angus McIntosh](../A/Angus_McIntosh.md), pupil and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is born.
- **[30](30_January.md)** - The [Chequers Clubbe](../C/Chequers_Clubbe.md), founded by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is given permission to organize dinners.
- **?** - *[Rules of the Exeter College Essay Club](../R/Rules_of_the_Exeter_College_Essay_Club.md)*, signed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is printed.

## February {#February}

- **[14](14_February.md)** - Translator [Britt G. Hallqvist](../B/Britt_G._Hallqvist.md) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[1](1_June.md)** - [George Sayer](../G/George_Sayer.md), friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is born.
- **[18](18_June.md)** - The [Chequers Clubbe](../C/Chequers_Clubbe.md), founded by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), meet to have dinner.

## August {#August}

- **[4](4_August.md)** - The British Empire declares [war](../W/World_War_I.md) on Germany, following tension and conflict on the mainland in July. [George Allen &amp; Unwin](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md)'s offices are opened in London by [Stanley Unwin](../S/Stanley_Unwin.md).
- **[9](9_August.md)** - Artist [Tove Jansson](../T/Tove_Jansson.md) is born.
- **?** - [Hilary Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md) joins the army as a bugler.
- **?** - [Geoffrey Bache Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md) and [Robert Gilson](../R/Robert_Gilson.md) join the army. [Christopher Wiseman](../C/Christopher_Wiseman.md) joins the navy instead.

## September {#September}

- **[16](16_September.md)** - Author and librarian [William Ready](../W/William_Ready.md) is born.
- **[24](24_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes *[The Voyage of Eärendel the Evening Star](../T/The_Voyage_of_Eärendel_the_Evening_Star.md)*.

## October {#October}

- **[1](1_October.md)** - [Donald A. Wollheim](../D/Donald_A._Wollheim.md) is born.
- **?** - Tolkien, still undergraduate, is training at the Officer's Training Corps. He writes [Letter 1](../L/Letter_1.md) to his fiancée [Edith Bratt](../E/Edith_Bratt.md).

## November {#November}

- **[27](../N/November_27.md)** - Tolkien writes [Letter 2](../L/Letter_2.md).

## December {#December}

- **[22](22_December.md)** - Tolkien writes [Ferrum et Sanguis: 1914](../F/Ferrum_et_Sanguis%253A_1914.md).
- **[25](25_December.md)** - The [T.C.B.S.](../T/T.C.B.S..md) meet in London.

## ? {#?}

- **Spring** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is awarded the Skeat Prize for English by the [Exeter College](../U/University_of_Oxford.md#Exeter_College). He spends the £5 prize money on buying [four books](../L/List_of_books_in_Tolkien's_library.md).
- **?** - Artist [Horus Engels](../H/Horus_Engels.md) is born.
