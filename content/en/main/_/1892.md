[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1892" title="wikipedia:1892">1892</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - **[John Ronald Reuel Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") is born to [Arthur](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien "Arthur Tolkien") and [Mabel Tolkien](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield "Mabel Suffield") at Bank House, Maitland Street, in [Bloemfontein](/wiki/Bloemfontein), South Africa**.
- **[4](/wiki/4_January "4 January")** - [Arthur Tolkien](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien) sends a letter to his mother about his new son.
- **[31](/wiki/31_January "31 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is christened in the Anglican Cathedral in Bloemfontein.

## November {#November}

- **[15](/wiki/15_November "15 November")** - Arthur, Mabel, two household servants, and the nurse holding Ronald pose for a picture in the garden at Bank House. The photograph is used in Tolkien Christmas cards sent to family and friends. The image is reproduced in [J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography) and [The Tolkien Family Album](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Family_Album).
