Events that occurred on 20 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1886](/wiki/1886)** – [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") [Charles Williams](/wiki/Charles_Williams) is born.
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 80b](/wiki/Letter_80b).
- **[1947](/wiki/1947)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 110](/wiki/Letter_110).
- **[1948](/wiki/1948)** – Author [George R. R. Martin](/wiki/George_R._R._Martin) is born.
- **[1962](/wiki/1962)** – Cartoonist [Bill Amend](/wiki/Bill_Amend) is born.
- **[1963](/wiki/1963)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 247](/wiki/Letter_247).
- <b><a href="/wiki/1982" title="1982">1982</a> – <i><a href="/wiki/Mr._Bliss" title="Mr. Bliss">Mr. Bliss</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **[1986](/wiki/1986)** – [Oxonmoot 1986](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1986) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[1991](/wiki/1991)** – [Oxonmoot 1991](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1991) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:University College, Oxford">University College</a>.
- **[1992](/wiki/1992)** – The [1992 radio adaptation](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)") of *[Leaf by Niggle](/wiki/Leaf_by_Niggle)* is aired on [BBC Radio 5](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[1996](/wiki/1996)** – [Oxonmoot 1996](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1996) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:University College, Oxford">University College</a>.
- **[1997](/wiki/1997)** – [Oxonmoot 1997](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1997) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Exeter College, Oxford">Exeter College</a>.
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** – [Oxonmoot 2003](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2003) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Hugh%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Hugh&#39;s College, Oxford">St Hugh&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2013](/wiki/2013)** – [Oxonmoot 2013](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2013) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Margaret_Hall,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Lady Margaret Hall, Oxford">Lady Margaret Hall</a>.
- **[2018](/wiki/2018)** - [Oxonmoot 2018](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2018) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Antony%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Antony&#39;s College, Oxford">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2020](/wiki/2020)** - [Oxonmoot 2020](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2020) held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) gains entrance to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) and warns them about [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman). [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) tells him 'Take any horse, only be gone ere tomorrow is old!'.
    - Two covered carts leave [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) for [Frodo's](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") new house in [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow). They convey the furniture and goods that Frodo has not sold by way of the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
