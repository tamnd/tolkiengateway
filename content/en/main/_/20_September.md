Events that occurred on 20 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1886](1886.md)** – [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md) [Charles Williams](../C/Charles_Williams.md) is born.
- **[1944](1944.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 80b](../L/Letter_80b.md).
- **[1947](1947.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 110](../L/Letter_110.md).
- **[1948](1948.md)** – Author [George R. R. Martin](../G/George_R._R._Martin.md) is born.
- **[1962](1962.md)** – Cartoonist [Bill Amend](../B/Bill_Amend.md) is born.
- **[1963](1963.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 247](../L/Letter_247.md).
- <b><a href="1982.md" title="1982">1982</a> – <i><a href="../M/Mr._Bliss.md" title="Mr. Bliss">Mr. Bliss</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **[1986](1986.md)** – [Oxonmoot 1986](../O/Oxonmoot_1986.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[1991](1991.md)** – [Oxonmoot 1991](../O/Oxonmoot_1991.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:University College, Oxford">University College</a>.
- **[1992](1992.md)** – The [1992 radio adaptation](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>) of *[Leaf by Niggle](../L/Leaf_by_Niggle.md)* is aired on [BBC Radio 5](../B/BBC.md).
- **[1996](1996.md)** – [Oxonmoot 1996](../O/Oxonmoot_1996.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:University College, Oxford">University College</a>.
- **[1997](1997.md)** – [Oxonmoot 1997](../O/Oxonmoot_1997.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exeter_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Exeter College, Oxford">Exeter College</a>.
- **[2003](2003.md)** – [Oxonmoot 2003](../O/Oxonmoot_2003.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Hugh%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Hugh&#39;s College, Oxford">St Hugh&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2013](2013.md)** – [Oxonmoot 2013](../O/Oxonmoot_2013.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Margaret_Hall,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Lady Margaret Hall, Oxford">Lady Margaret Hall</a>.
- **[2018](2018.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2018](../O/Oxonmoot_2018.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Antony%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Antony&#39;s College, Oxford">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2020](2020.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2020](../O/Oxonmoot_2020.md) held online due to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic">Covid-19 pandemic</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) gains entrance to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) and warns them about [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md). [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) tells him 'Take any horse, only be gone ere tomorrow is old!'.
    - Two covered carts leave [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) for [Frodo's](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) new house in [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md). They convey the furniture and goods that Frodo has not sold by way of the [Brandywine Bridge](../B/Brandywine_Bridge.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
