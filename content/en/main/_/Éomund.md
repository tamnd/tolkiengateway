**Éomund** was the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lord_of_Aldburg&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lord of Aldburg (page does not exist)">Lord of Aldburg</a> and a [Marshal of the Mark](/wiki/Marshal_of_the_Mark).

## History {#History}

Éomund was a descendant of [Eofor](/wiki/Eofor), the third son of Brego the second king of Rohan, and dwelt in [Aldburg](/wiki/Aldburg) in the [Eastfold](/wiki/Eastfold).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), during his travels in Rohan, made the acquaintance of Éomund.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He married [Théodwyn](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odwyn), the sister of the later King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden). Their children were [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) and [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn), who were each to play a great part in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

He was the marshal in charge of the east marches, active in defending the horse-herds of the region against increasing raids by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). He was killed in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3002](/wiki/Third_Age_3002 "Third Age 3002"), while pursuing such a raid, by a large force that lay in ambush in the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil). Théodwyn grew ill and died shortly after, and their children were raised by Théoden.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Éomund* is made up of [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/eoh" title="wiktionary:eoh">eoh</a></i> ("war-horse, charger") + <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/mund#Old_English" title="wiktionary:mund">mund</a></i> ("protector").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The name is appropriate to his role as the guardian of the horses of the Eastfold.
