[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2017" title="wikipedia:2017">2017</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[27](/wiki/27_January "27 January")** - Actor [John Hurt](/wiki/John_Hurt) dies.
- **[30](/wiki/30_January "30 January")** - Actor [Victor Platt](/wiki/Victor_Platt) dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 263](/wiki/Amon_Hen_263)* is published.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 57](/wiki/Mallorn_57)* is published.

## February {#February}

- **[2](/wiki/2_February "2 February")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Hobbit_Movie_Trilogy_Colouring_Book&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Hobbit Movie Trilogy Colouring Book (page does not exist)">The Hobbit Movie Trilogy Colouring Book</a></i> is published.

## March {#March}

- **[14](/wiki/14_March "14 March")** - [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library) announce the book *[Tolkien: The Maker of Middle-earth](/wiki/Tolkien:_Maker_of_Middle-earth "Tolkien: Maker of Middle-earth")* to go alongside their forthcoming exhibition.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day). [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) hosts a storytelling of *[Mr. Bliss](/wiki/Mr._Bliss)* at <a class="external text" href="http://www.storymuseum.org.uk/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Story Museum</a>, [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford).
- **[28](/wiki/28_March "28 March")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=John_Ronald%27s_Dragons:_The_Story_of_J.R.R._Tolkien&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="John Ronald&#39;s Dragons: The Story of J.R.R. Tolkien (page does not exist)">John Ronald&#39;s Dragons: The Story of J.R.R. Tolkien</a></i> is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 264](/wiki/Amon_Hen_264)* is published.

## May {#May}

- **?** - *[Amon Hen 265](/wiki/Amon_Hen_265)* is published.

## June {#June}

- <b><a href="/wiki/1_June" title="1 June">1</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/Beren_and_L%C3%BAthien" title="Beren and Lúthien">Beren and Lúthien</a></i> is published.</b>

## July {#July}

- **[2](/wiki/2_July "2 July")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) Seminar at the Hilton, [Leeds](/wiki/Leeds).
- **[3](/wiki/3_July "3 July")** - A [lawsuit](/wiki/Lawsuits "Lawsuits") was settled between [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) and [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate).
- **[14](/wiki/14_July "14 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien the Esperantist: Before the Arrival of Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_the_Esperantist:_Before_the_Arrival_of_Bilbo_Baggins) is published.
- **[21](/wiki/21_July "21 July")** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Romanticist and Poet](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Romanticist_and_Poet)* is published.
- **[31](/wiki/31_July "31 July")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Mordor](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Mordor)* is released online.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 266](/wiki/Amon_Hen_266)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[10](/wiki/10_August "10 August")-[13](/wiki/13_August "13 August")** - [Omentielva Otsea](/wiki/Omentielva "Omentielva") - the Seventh International Conference on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s Invented Languages at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_East_Bay" title="wikipedia:California State University, East Bay">California State University, East Bay</a>.
- **[31](/wiki/31_August "31 August")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2018](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2018)* is published.
- **?** - [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) resigns from his appointment as director of the [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate).

## September {#September}

- **[15](/wiki/15_September "15 September")** - The revised edition of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Aragorn:_J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_Undervalued_Hero&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aragorn: J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s Undervalued Hero (page does not exist)">Aragorn: J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s Undervalued Hero</a></i> is published.
- **[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")** - The deluxe boxed set edition of *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* is published.
- **[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September") - [24](/wiki/24_September "24 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2017](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2017) at <a class="external text" href="http://www.sant.ox.ac.uk" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[30](/wiki/30_September "30 September")** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Romanticist and Poet](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Romanticist_and_Poet)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 267](/wiki/Amon_Hen_267)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[8](/wiki/8_October "8 October")** - *[Binding Them All](/wiki/Binding_Them_All)* is published.
- **[9](/wiki/9_October "9 October")** - Filming began on [*Tolkien* (film)](</wiki/Tolkien_(film)> "Tolkien (film)"), directed by [Dome Karukoski](/wiki/Dome_Karukoski) and written by [David Gleeson](/wiki/David_Gleeson).
- **[10](/wiki/10_October "10 October")** - *[Middle-earth: Shadow of War](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_War)* is released.
- **[12](/wiki/12_October "12 October")** - *[Flora of Middle-Earth: Plants of J.R.R. Tolkien's Legendarium](/wiki/Flora_of_Middle-Earth "Flora of Middle-Earth")* is published.
- **[19](/wiki/19_October "19 October")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Middle-earth:_From_Script_to_Screen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Middle-earth: From Script to Screen (page does not exist)">Middle-earth: From Script to Screen: Building the World of The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit</a></i> is published.
- **[19](/wiki/19_October "19 October")** - *[The Middle-earth Treasury](/wiki/The_Middle-earth_Treasury)* is published.
- **[24](/wiki/24_October "24 October")** - [Faith Faulconbridge](/wiki/Faith_Faulconbridge) dies.
- **[26](/wiki/26_October "26 October")** - [Roger Garland](/wiki/Roger_Garland) dies.

## November {#November}

- **[2](/wiki/2_November "2 November")** - The second edition of *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide)* is published.
- **[13](/wiki/13_November "13 November")** - [Amazon](/wiki/Amazon.com_Incorporated "Amazon.com Incorporated") acquires the global television rights for the books [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) and [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit) from [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate).
- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Sweet_and_the_Bitter&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Sweet and the Bitter (page does not exist)">The Sweet and the Bitter: Death and Dying in J. R. R. Tolkien&#39;s the Lord of the Rings</a></i> is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 268](/wiki/Amon_Hen_268)* is published.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 58](/wiki/Mallorn_58)* is published.

## December {#December}

- **[1](/wiki/1_December "1 December")** - *[Death and Immortality in Middle-earth](/wiki/Death_and_Immortality_in_Middle-earth)* is published.
- **[17](/wiki/17_December "17 December")** - *["There Would Always Be a Fairy Tale": Essays on Tolkien's Middle-earth](/wiki/There_Would_Always_Be_a_Fairy_Tale "There Would Always Be a Fairy Tale")* is published.
- **[22](/wiki/22_December "22 December")** - *[The Inklings and King Arthur](/wiki/The_Inklings_and_King_Arthur)* is published
- **?** - *[The Flame Imperishable: Tolkien, St. Thomas, and the Metaphysics of Faerie](/wiki/The_Flame_Imperishable:_Tolkien,_St._Thomas,_and_the_Metaphysics_of_Faerie)* is published.
