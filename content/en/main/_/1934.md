[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1934" title="wikipedia:1934">1934</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[10](/wiki/10_January "10 January")** - [Arne Zettersten](/wiki/Arne_Zettersten), colleague and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is born.
- **[18](/wiki/18_January "18 January")** - The poem *[Looney](/wiki/Looney)* is published.
- **[21](/wiki/21_January "21 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 8a](/wiki/Letter_8a).

## February {#February}

- **[12](/wiki/12_February "12 February")** - Actress [Annette Crosbie](/wiki/Annette_Crosbie) is born.
- **[15](/wiki/15_February "15 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](</wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil_(poem)> "The Adventures of Tom Bombadil (poem)")" is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.
- **[17](/wiki/17_February "17 February")** - Actor [Barry Humphries](/wiki/Barry_Humphries) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem *[Firiel](</wiki/Firiel_(poem)> "Firiel (poem)")* is published within [*The Chronicle of the Convents of the Sacred Heart* (Volume IV)](</wiki/The_Chronicle_of_the_Convents_of_the_Sacred_Heart_(Volume_IV)> "The Chronicle of the Convents of the Sacred Heart (Volume IV)").

## June {#June}

- **[20](/wiki/20_June "20 June")** - Artist [Heinz Edelmann](/wiki/Heinz_Edelmann) is born.
- **?** - The second half of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s essay "[Sigelwara Land](/wiki/Sigelwara_Land)" is published in *Medium Aevum*.

## August {#August}

- **[9](/wiki/9_August "9 August")** - <a class="external text" href="http://www.tolkiensociety.org/blog/2015/07/1934-sketch-of-tolkien-discovered/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien&#39;s portrait</a> by artist F.A. Farrell is published in the Australian Catholic journal *The Advocate*.
- **[16](/wiki/16_August "16 August")** - Author [Diana Wynne Jones](/wiki/Diana_Wynne_Jones) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[20](/wiki/20_November "20 November")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a letter to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien).

## December {#December}

- **[23](/wiki/23_December "23 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to G.H. Cowling](/wiki/Letter_to_G.H._Cowling "Letter to G.H. Cowling").
- **[24](/wiki/24_December "24 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - *[Chaucer as a Philologist: The Reeve's Tale](/wiki/Chaucer_as_a_Philologist:_The_Reeve%27s_Tale)* is published.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) receives the two-year Leverhulme Research Fellowship.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s "Chaucer as a Philologist" is published in *Transactions of the Philological Society*.
- **?** - Artist [Maret Kernumees](/wiki/Maret_Kernumees) is born.
- **?** - Artist [Cor Blok](/wiki/Cor_Blok) is born.
