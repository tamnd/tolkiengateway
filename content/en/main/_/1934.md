[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1934" title="wikipedia:1934">1934</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[10](10_January.md)** - [Arne Zettersten](../A/Arne_Zettersten.md), colleague and friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is born.
- **[18](18_January.md)** - The poem *[Looney](../L/Looney.md)* is published.
- **[21](21_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 8a](../L/Letter_8a.md).

## February {#February}

- **[12](12_February.md)** - Actress [Annette Crosbie](../A/Annette_Crosbie.md) is born.
- **[15](15_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem "[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](<../T/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil_(poem).md>)" is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.
- **[17](17_February.md)** - Actor [Barry Humphries](../B/Barry_Humphries.md) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[25](25_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem *[Firiel](<../F/Firiel_(poem).md>)* is published within [*The Chronicle of the Convents of the Sacred Heart* (Volume IV)](<../T/The_Chronicle_of_the_Convents_of_the_Sacred_Heart_(Volume_IV).md>).

## June {#June}

- **[20](20_June.md)** - Artist [Heinz Edelmann](../H/Heinz_Edelmann.md) is born.
- **?** - The second half of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s essay "[Sigelwara Land](../S/Sigelwara_Land.md)" is published in *Medium Aevum*.

## August {#August}

- **[9](9_August.md)** - <a class="external text" href="http://www.tolkiensociety.org/blog/2015/07/1934-sketch-of-tolkien-discovered/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien&#39;s portrait</a> by artist F.A. Farrell is published in the Australian Catholic journal *The Advocate*.
- **[16](16_August.md)** - Author [Diana Wynne Jones](../D/Diana_Wynne_Jones.md) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[20](20_November.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a letter to [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md).

## December {#December}

- **[23](23_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to G.H. Cowling](../L/Letter_to_G.H._Cowling.md).
- **[24](24_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Artist [Maret Kernumees](../M/Maret_Kernumees.md) is born.
