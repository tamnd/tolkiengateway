[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022" title="wikipedia:2022">2022</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](3_January.md)** - [Birthday Toast](../B/Birthday_Toast.md#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[20](20_January.md)** - The official title of [*The Lord of the Rings* television series](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md) is announced.

## February {#February}

- **[13](13_February.md)** - The first official trailer of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* is released online.
- **[28](28_February.md)** - [Priscilla Tolkien](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md) dies.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 293](../A/Amon_Hen_293.md) is published.

## March {#March}

- **[17](17_March.md)** - [Amazon](../A/Amazon.com_Incorporated.md) completes the acquisition of [MGM](../M/MGM.md).
- **[22](22_March.md)** - The second edition of [*The One Ring*™ RPG](<../T/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md>) is officially released.
- **[25](25_March.md)** - [Tolkien Reading Day](../T/Tolkien_Reading_Day.md).

## April {#April}

- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 294](../A/Amon_Hen_294.md) is published.

## May {#May}

- **[14](14_May.md)** - *Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game: Defence of the North* is released.

## June {#June}

- **[24](24_June.md)** - *[The Great Tales Never End: Essays in Memory of Christopher Tolkien](../T/The_Great_Tales_Never_End.md)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[14](14_July.md)** - The second official teaser trailer of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* is released.
- **[28](28_July.md)** - Actor [Bernard Cribbins](../B/Bernard_Cribbins.md) dies.
- <b><a href="31_July.md" title="31 July">31</a> -<i><a href="../T/The_Chronology_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Chronology of The Lord of the Rings">The Chronology of The Lord of the Rings</a></i> is published.</b>

## August {#August}

- **[9](9_August.md)** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2023](../T/Tolkien_Calendar_2023.md)* is published.
- **[19](19_August.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power (Season One: Amazon Original Series Soundtrack)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power_(Season_One%253A_Amazon_Original_Series_Soundtrack).md>)* is released online.
- **[23](23_August.md)** - The third and final trailer of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* is released online.
- **?** - [Embracer Group](../E/Embracer_Group.md) enters into an agreement to buy [Middle-earth Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md) from the Saul Zaentz Company.

## September {#September}

- **[1](1_September.md) - [4](4_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2022](../O/Oxonmoot_2022.md) is held online at <a class="external text" href="https://www.st-annes.ox.ac.uk/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Anne&#39;s College</a>, [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md).
- <b><a href="1_September.md" title="1 September">1</a> - Episode one, &#34;<a href="../A/A_Shadow_of_the_Past.md" title="A Shadow of the Past">A Shadow of the Past</a>&#34;, and episode two, &#34;<a href="../A/Adrift.md" title="Adrift">Adrift</a>&#34;, of <i>The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</i> are simultaneously released online.</b>
- **[1](1_September.md)** - A new and revised edition of *[The Complete Guide to Middle-earth](../T/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth.md)* by [Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md) is published.
- **[9](9_September.md)** - The episode "[Adar](../A/Adar.md)" of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* is released online.
- **[16](16_September.md)** - The episode "[The Great Wave](../T/The_Great_Wave.md)" of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* is released online.
- **[23](23_September.md)** - The episode "[Partings](../P/Partings.md)" of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* is released online.
- **[30](30_September.md)** - The episode "[Udûn](<../U/Udûn_(episode).md>)" of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* is released online.

## October {#October}

- **[3](3_October.md)** - Production for the second season of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* series begins in the United Kingdom.
- **[7](7_October.md)** - The episode "[The Eye](../T/The_Eye.md)" of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* is released online.
- **[14](14_October.md)** - The episode "[Alloyed](../A/Alloyed.md)" of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* is released online.
- **[25](25_October.md)** - American animator [Jules Bass](../J/Jules_Bass.md) dies.

## November {#November}

- <b><a href="15_November.md" title="15 November">15</a> - <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../T/The_Fall_of_Númenor_and_Other_Tales_from_the_Second_Age_of_Middle-earth.md" title="The Fall of Númenor and Other Tales from the Second Age of Middle-earth">The Fall of Númenor and Other Tales from the Second Age of Middle-earth</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[15](15_November.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Before the Shadow](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Before_the_Shadow.md)* is released online.

## December {#December}

- **[14](14_December.md)** - Most of the production team of the second season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* is revealed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 63](../M/Mallorn_63.md) is published.
