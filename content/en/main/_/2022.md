[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022" title="wikipedia:2022">2022</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **[20](/wiki/20_January "20 January")** - The official title of [*The Lord of the Rings* television series](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power") is announced.

## February {#February}

- **[13](/wiki/13_February "13 February")** - The first official trailer of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* is released online.
- **[28](/wiki/28_February "28 February")** - [Priscilla Tolkien](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien) dies.
- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 293](/wiki/Amon_Hen_293 "Amon Hen 293") is published.

## March {#March}

- **[17](/wiki/17_March "17 March")** - [Amazon](/wiki/Amazon.com_Incorporated "Amazon.com Incorporated") completes the acquisition of [MGM](/wiki/MGM).
- **[22](/wiki/22_March "22 March")** - The second edition of [*The One Ring*™ RPG](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)") is officially released.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).

## April {#April}

- **?** - [*Amon Hen* 294](/wiki/Amon_Hen_294 "Amon Hen 294") is published.

## May {#May}

- **[14](/wiki/14_May "14 May")** - *Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game: Defence of the North* is released.

## June {#June}

- **[24](/wiki/24_June "24 June")** - *[The Great Tales Never End: Essays in Memory of Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/The_Great_Tales_Never_End "The Great Tales Never End")* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[14](/wiki/14_July "14 July")** - The second official teaser trailer of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* is released.
- **[28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - Actor [Bernard Cribbins](/wiki/Bernard_Cribbins) dies.
- <b><a href="/wiki/31_July" title="31 July">31</a> -<i><a href="/wiki/The_Chronology_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Chronology of The Lord of the Rings">The Chronology of The Lord of the Rings</a></i> is published.</b>

## August {#August}

- **[9](/wiki/9_August "9 August")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2023](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2023)* is published.
- **[19](/wiki/19_August "19 August")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power (Season One: Amazon Original Series Soundtrack)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_(Season_One:_Amazon_Original_Series_Soundtrack)>)* is released online.
- **[23](/wiki/23_August "23 August")** - The third and final trailer of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* is released online.
- **?** - [Embracer Group](/wiki/Embracer_Group) enters into an agreement to buy [Middle-earth Enterprises](/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises) from the Saul Zaentz Company.

## September {#September}

- **[1](/wiki/1_September "1 September") - [4](/wiki/4_September "4 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2022](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2022) is held online at <a class="external text" href="https://www.st-annes.ox.ac.uk/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St Anne&#39;s College</a>, [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford).
- <b><a href="/wiki/1_September" title="1 September">1</a> - Episode one, &#34;<a href="/wiki/A_Shadow_of_the_Past" title="A Shadow of the Past">A Shadow of the Past</a>&#34;, and episode two, &#34;<a href="/wiki/Adrift" title="Adrift">Adrift</a>&#34;, of <i>The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</i> are simultaneously released online.</b>
- **[1](/wiki/1_September "1 September")** - A new and revised edition of *[The Complete Guide to Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth)* by [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) is published.
- **[9](/wiki/9_September "9 September")** - The episode "[Adar](/wiki/Adar)" of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* is released online.
- **[16](/wiki/16_September "16 September")** - The episode "[The Great Wave](/wiki/The_Great_Wave)" of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* is released online.
- **[23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")** - The episode "[Partings](/wiki/Partings)" of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* is released online.
- **[30](/wiki/30_September "30 September")** - The episode "[Udûn](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(episode)> "Udûn (episode)")" of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* is released online.

## October {#October}

- **[3](/wiki/3_October "3 October")** - Production for the second season of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* series begins in the United Kingdom.
- **[7](/wiki/7_October "7 October")** - The episode "[The Eye](/wiki/The_Eye)" of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* is released online.
- **[14](/wiki/14_October "14 October")** - The episode "[Alloyed](/wiki/Alloyed)" of *The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* is released online.
- **[25](/wiki/25_October "25 October")** - American animator [Jules Bass](/wiki/Jules_Bass) dies.

## November {#November}

- <b><a href="/wiki/15_November" title="15 November">15</a> - <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_Fall_of_N%C3%BAmenor_and_Other_Tales_from_the_Second_Age_of_Middle-earth" title="The Fall of Númenor and Other Tales from the Second Age of Middle-earth">The Fall of Númenor and Other Tales from the Second Age of Middle-earth</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[15](/wiki/15_November "15 November")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Before the Shadow](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Before_the_Shadow)* is released online.

## December {#December}

- **[14](/wiki/14_December "14 December")** - Most of the production team of the second season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* is revealed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 63](/wiki/Mallorn_63 "Mallorn 63") is published.
