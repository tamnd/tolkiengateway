<b>Éomer <i>Éadig</i></b> of the [House of Eorl](/wiki/House_of_Eorl) was the eighteenth [King of Rohan](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan") and first of the Third Line. He was the son of [Théodwyn](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odwyn), sister of King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), and [Éomund](/wiki/%C3%89omund), a Chief Marshal of the [Riddermark](/wiki/Riddermark). After he and his sister [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) were orphaned, Théoden adopted them as his own children.<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_A_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix_A-5">[5]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Youth {#Youth}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Anna_Lee_-_Young_%C3%89omer_and_%C3%89owyn.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/75/Anna_Lee_-_Young_%C3%89omer_and_%C3%89owyn.jpg/300px-Anna_Lee_-_Young_%C3%89omer_and_%C3%89owyn.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="451" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/7/75/Anna_Lee_-_Young_%C3%89omer_and_%C3%89owyn.jpg/450px-Anna_Lee_-_Young_%C3%89omer_and_%C3%89owyn.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/7/75/Anna_Lee_-_Young_%C3%89omer_and_%C3%89owyn.jpg 2x" data-file-width="472" data-file-height="709"/></a><figcaption>Anna Lee - <i>Young Éomer and Éowyn</i></figcaption></figure>

Éomer was born in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2991](/wiki/Third_Age_2991 "Third Age 2991") as son of [Éomund](/wiki/%C3%89omund) and [Théodwyn](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odwyn). When he was eleven years old, his father was slain by [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") raiders in the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil). His mother fell ill and died soon after. His uncle [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) adopted him and his sister [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) as his own children and they went to live at [Meduseld](/wiki/Meduseld).

Éomer grew strong and tall and became a valiant and skilled warrior and rider. Théoden made him [Third Marshal of the Riddermark](/wiki/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark) and he was in command of the [East-mark](/wiki/East-mark) like his father. He had his home at [Aldburg](/wiki/Aldburg) in the [Folde](/wiki/Folde), one of the oldest towns of Rohan. With his [Éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored) he defended Rohan mainly against Orcs from [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_A_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix_A-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Struggle against Saruman {#Struggle_against_Saruman}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:John_Howe_-_Eomer_2.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/f/f1/John_Howe_-_Eomer_2.jpg/200px-John_Howe_-_Eomer_2.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="501" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/f/f1/John_Howe_-_Eomer_2.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="244" data-file-height="611"/></a><figcaption>&#34;Éomer&#34; by <a href="/wiki/John_Howe" title="John Howe">John Howe</a></figcaption></figure>

But Rohan's enemies were not only at the borders but also within. [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma"), the King's advisor, was secretly a spy of the wizard [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman), who dwelled at [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) and had become a servant of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). Gríma, in secret, worked to weaken Théoden and his kingdom and Théoden fell deeply under his influence. Éomer fell into the King's disfavor, but Gríma did not manage to destroy Éomer's loyalty to Théoden; nor to his cousin, Prince [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred), whom Éomer regarded as his brother.

In the summer of [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir), son of [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor"), crossed Rohan on his way to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell). Éomer learned from him about the dream of [Isildur's Bane](/wiki/Isildur%27s_Bane). Soon after September, evil Men, Orcs and Wargs in Saruman's service began to fight openly against the Rohirrim. Saruman claimed lordship over Rohan and blocked the [Gap of Rohan](/wiki/Gap_of_Rohan).

The chief obstacles to an easy conquest of Rohan by Saruman were Théodred and Éomer. So came the first great battle in the conflict, the [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen), on [25 February](/wiki/25_February) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"). After untiring assaults on Théodred's position, the Prince was finally killed, leaving Éomer as the only obstacle.

The [day after](/wiki/26_February "26 February") the battle, Èomer gained knowledge of a troop of Orcs entering Rohan from [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) to the east. Unbeknownst to Éomer, they were the same Orcs that had taken [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) captive. Suspecting an alliance between Saruman and Sauron, he wanted to hunt them down. Under the strong influence of Gríma, Théoden forbade Éomer to pursue the Orcs because Edoras would be left unguarded. But Éomer neglected the order since he already suspected Gríma's treachery, and [at around midnight](/wiki/27_February "27 February") he set out from [Eastfold](/wiki/Eastfold) with his [Éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored). At the end of [the next day](/wiki/28_February "28 February") they caught sight of the Orcs at the border of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest).<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_B_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix_B-6">[6]</a></sup> They surrounded them and remained watchful throughout the night. With several small attacks they weakened the troop until another Orc band from Isengard arrived. The Rohirrim defeated them and surrounded the first troop even closer.

With the first light of the new day the Rohirrim proceeded to attack. They killed all the Orcs and Éomer himself slew their leader [Uglúk](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk). The [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") meanwhile were able to flee into Fangorn Forest. Fifteen men and 12 horses did not survive the assault. They were buried in a mound encircled with fifteen spears.<sup id="cite_ref-Uruk-hai_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Uruk-hai-7">[7]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Riders_of_Rohan.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/f/f3/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Riders_of_Rohan.jpg/250px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Riders_of_Rohan.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="331" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/f/f3/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Riders_of_Rohan.jpg/375px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Riders_of_Rohan.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/f/f3/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Riders_of_Rohan.jpg/500px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Riders_of_Rohan.jpg 2x" data-file-width="653" data-file-height="864"/></a><figcaption><i>The Riders of Rohan</i> by <a href="/wiki/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann" title="Anke Eißmann">Anke Eißmann</a></figcaption></figure>

On the way back to Edoras, the Rohirrim met [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), and [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas). Éomer demanded their names and business. Fighting almost broke almost out between them, but Aragorn prevented this. He then told Éomer his true name and showed him his sword, [Andúril](/wiki/And%C3%BAril). Éomer now listened to the story of their journey, and learned about the losses of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir). When Aragorn revealed that they had tracked the band of Orcs all the way from the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) in the hope of rescuing the hobbits, Éomer was astonished and gave Aragorn the name "Wingfoot", recognizing the difficulty of crossing so much terrain in so little time. He revealed that his men had destroyed the band of Orcs, and that it was fruitless to go searching for his friends. He instead urged Aragorn to return with him to Edoras to help the people of Rohan in their need, but Aragorn refused and insisted that he be able to continue his search for the hobbits. Although he was counteracting the law of Rohan by letting Aragorn go free, Éomer decided to trust him and he even lent him and his companions two horses, [Hasufel](/wiki/Hasufel) and [Arod](/wiki/Arod), to help them in their search, but he asked them to prove his trust by bringing them back to Edoras when their search was over.<sup id="cite_ref-Riders_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-8">[8]</a></sup>

Upon his return to Edoras, Éomer threatened Gríma, but Gríma arrested him and accused him of breaking the law because he had pursued the Orcs and had allowed strangers to roam freely without Théoden's permission.

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:John_Howe_-_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/6/6d/John_Howe_-_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg/500px-John_Howe_-_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="500" height="237" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/6/6d/John_Howe_-_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg/750px-John_Howe_-_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/6/6d/John_Howe_-_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg/1000px-John_Howe_-_Helm%27s_Deep.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1274" data-file-height="603"/></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/John_Howe" title="John Howe">John Howe</a> - <i>Helms Deep</i></figcaption></figure>

Éomer was released after Gandalf healed Théoden from the influence of Gríma. He was delighted to see that his king was recovering quickly from his weakness. Now Gríma's treachery was revealed, but due to Gandalf's intervening he was not killed, but rather expelled from the [Golden Hall](/wiki/Meduseld "Meduseld"). Théoden, now realizing the situation, decided to lead the Rohirrim into battle against Saruman instantly. The [Three Hunters](/wiki/Three_Hunters) and Gandalf accompanied them when they left Edoras the same day.<sup id="cite_ref-King_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-King-9">[9]</a></sup>

The following day they met the scout [Ceorl](/wiki/Ceorl) who had been sent out by [Erkenbrand](/wiki/Erkenbrand) to warn Éomer. He was surprised to come across King Théoden's army riding north and brought news that Saruman had let loose his forces and that the Rohirrim had been forced to retreat across the Isen with great losses. These tidings caused Théoden to reconsider his strategy. After consulting with Gandalf he decided to ride to join Erkenbrand at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep) while the wizard left them, saying that he would meet them there.

They arrived at Helm's Deep on the night of [3 March](/wiki/3_March) and Éomer oversaw the preparations for the great battle. In the middle of the night, the siege began, and when battering rams attacked the gates, Éomer and Aragorn led a sortie through a passage on the side of the Hornburg that drove the enemies threatening the doors away. In the course of this Gimli saved Éomer's life when he killed two Orcs who had ambushed Éomer. The battle continued throughout the night and ever more attackers scaled the walls with large ladders. Aragorn and Éomer had to repeatedly move the defenders, who were getting weary, to repel the Orcs. With the help of a device of Saruman's the Orcs caused an explosion under the wall that made a wide hole in it. From then on the attackers could not be stopped and the defenders were separated. Éomer and Gimli retreated to the [Glittering Caves](/wiki/Glittering_Caves) whereas Aragorn and Legolas defended the Hornburg.

The battle seemed lost when at dawn Gandalf returned with Erkenbrand and 1000 riders from the Westfold. Together they drove the enemies out of the deep and into a forest of [Huorns](/wiki/Huorns) that had arrived during the night and from which no Orcs returned.<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-10">[10]</a></sup> After the end of the battle Éomer accompanied his king to Isengard.<sup id="cite_ref-Road_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Road-11">[11]</a></sup> There they met Saruman who tried to enthral Théoden with his magic voice, but both Éomer and Théoden saw through him and his dark designs. Théoden said that Rohan would have no peace until Saruman and his works had perished. Gandalf then broke Saruman's staff and cast him from the Order of [Wizards](/wiki/Wizards) and the [White Council](/wiki/White_Council).<sup id="cite_ref-Voice_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Voice-12">[12]</a></sup>

### The Battle of the Pelennor Fields {#The_Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields}

On [5 March](/wiki/5_March) the company set out to Helm's Deep. While they rested in the night Pippin took the *[palantír](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* [of Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc-stone "Orthanc-stone") from the sleeping Gandalf, looked in it, and was confronted by Sauron. Knowing the peril Pippin was in, Gandalf said farewell to the others and departed with Pippin towards [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-Palantir_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantir-13">[13]</a></sup> The Three Hunters and the Rohirrim continued their journey towards Helm's Deep where they arrived on [6 March](/wiki/6_March). Now Aragorn looked into the palantír and revealed himself to Sauron. Because of what he glimpsed in the *palantír*, he left the Rohirrim, and took the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead) with the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company). Éomer was afraid of never seeing him again, but Aragorn predicted that they would "meet again, though all the hosts of Mordor should stand between." before setting out.<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-14">[14]</a></sup>

Éomer accompanied his king to the muster of [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) where he met his sister Éowyn again. Then the [Red Arrow](/wiki/Red_Arrow) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), which requested Rohan's aid, was brought by a messenger and the Rohhirim set out in great haste to Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-15">[15]</a></sup> They used narrow mountain paths and on [13 March](/wiki/13_March) reached Drúadan Forest where they met [Ghân-buri-Ghân](/wiki/Gh%C3%A2n-buri-Gh%C3%A2n), the chief of the Drúedain. He agreed to lead the Rohirrim by a secret road past the Orc hosts that laid siege to Minas Tirith.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Per_Sj%C3%B6gren_-_The_Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/6/64/Per_Sj%C3%B6gren_-_The_Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.jpg/300px-Per_Sj%C3%B6gren_-_The_Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="221" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/6/64/Per_Sj%C3%B6gren_-_The_Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.jpg/450px-Per_Sj%C3%B6gren_-_The_Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/6/64/Per_Sj%C3%B6gren_-_The_Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.jpg/600px-Per_Sj%C3%B6gren_-_The_Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1100" data-file-height="812"/></a><figcaption>Per Sjögren - <i>The Battle of the Pelennor Fields</i></figcaption></figure>

The Rohirrim arrived on the battleground of the [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields) at dawn on [15 March](/wiki/15_March). Éomer led the first Éored that should follow the king's banner in the centre.<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-16">[16]</a></sup> The Rohirrim were strong and filled with wrath and many enemies were slain. The Rohirrim went hither and thither at their will and so Éomer was only brought to Théoden when the king was already mortally wounded. Before his death Théoden hailed Éomer as King of the Mark and Éomer and those who stood by wept. When Éomer recognized Éowyn lying nearby, a cold fury rose in him and without taking counsel or waiting for the approach of the men of the City he led his men across the field, slaying all enemies in their path. As they charged he shouted out "Death! Ride, ride to ruin and the world's ending!".

But soon fortune turned against Éomer and he and his men were encircled. Just in time came the aid of the horsemen of Gondor, among them [Hirluin of the Green Hills](/wiki/Hirluin "Hirluin") and [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil) prince of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth). But ever more enemies came from [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath), and when the black ships of the [Corsairs](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar "Corsairs of Umbar") came up the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), the battle seemed lost. Éomer rallied the Rohirrim for a last fight against the Corsairs when he realized the standard of Aragorn unfurling on the first ship.

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Abe_Papakhian_-_Eomer_and_Aragorn_(colour).jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/a/a3/Abe_Papakhian_-_Eomer_and_Aragorn_%28colour%29.jpg/300px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Eomer_and_Aragorn_%28colour%29.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="199" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/a/a3/Abe_Papakhian_-_Eomer_and_Aragorn_%28colour%29.jpg/450px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Eomer_and_Aragorn_%28colour%29.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/a/a3/Abe_Papakhian_-_Eomer_and_Aragorn_%28colour%29.jpg/600px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Eomer_and_Aragorn_%28colour%29.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1280" data-file-height="849"/></a><figcaption>Abe Papakhian - <i>Éomer and Aragorn</i></figcaption></figure>

The enemies fled when they saw that their own ships were filled with foes and so Éomer met Aragorn in the midst of the battlefield as Aragorn had predicted.<sup id="cite_ref-Pelennor_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pelennor-17">[17]</a></sup>

The battle ended in victory and Éomer learned that Éowyn was not dead, but lay gravely ill in the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing). He also learned that the contact with the [Lord of the Nazgûl](/wiki/Witch-king "Witch-king") was not the only reason for her sickness but that also her despair had worsened it. Aragorn managed to heal Éowyn and she awoke at her brother's call. Meriadoc Brandybuck was also healed and Éomer made him a Knight of the Riddermark in return for his aid for Éowyn and Théoden.<sup id="cite_ref-Healing_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Healing-18">[18]</a></sup>

The next day, Éomer took part in the debate of the Captains of the West. There Gandalf told that their hope now lay with [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), who had gone to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) to destroy [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") wrought by Sauron. Gandalf counselled that they should ride to the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) in order to force Sauron to empty his land and take the Dark Lord's attention away from his search for the Ring, giving the Ring-bearers their only chance. Aragorn agreed and Éomer followed his friend in return for his aid in their time of need.<sup id="cite_ref-Debate_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Debate-19">[19]</a></sup>

On [18 March](/wiki/18_March) the [Host of the West](/wiki/Army_of_the_West "Army of the West"), Gandalf, the Three Hunters, and Éomer among them, set out towards the Black Gate. They [fought](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon "Battle of the Morannon") there on [25 March](/wiki/25_March) and withstood the forces of the enemy until the Ring was destroyed and Sauron and his servants and works perished.<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-20">[20]</a></sup>

### King of the Mark {#King_of_the_Mark}

After the great victory, Aragorn set up his camp on the [Field of Cormallen](/wiki/Field_of_Cormallen) in [North Ithilien](/wiki/North_Ithilien) and Éomer took part in the celebrations there.<sup id="cite_ref-Cormallen_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cormallen-21">[21]</a></sup> Together with the other Captains of the West he returned to Minas Tirith and attended the coronation of Aragorn on [1 May](/wiki/1_May). A week later he renewed the [Oath of Eorl](/wiki/Oath_of_Eorl) for Aragorn and both swore everlasting friendship between Rohan and Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-22">[22]</a></sup> On [8 May](/wiki/8_May) he, with his sister, set out to Rohan, for there was much to organize and to set in order.<sup id="cite_ref-chief_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-23">[23]</a></sup>

Éomer returned to Minas Tirith on [18 July](/wiki/18_July) and set out the [next day](/wiki/19_July "19 July"), bringing Théoden's body back to Edoras. On [10 August](/wiki/10_August) the funeral of Théoden took place<sup id="cite_ref-chief_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-23">[23]</a></sup> and Éomer announced the betrothal of Éowyn to [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), the [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor") and [Prince of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien). When the feast was over, Éomer and Éowyn gave Merry the name [Holdwine](/wiki/Holdwine), "loyal friend," of the Mark, and the ancient [Horn of the Mark](/wiki/Horn_of_the_Mark), an heirloom of the [House of Eorl](/wiki/House_of_Eorl).<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-4">[4]</a></sup>

King Éomer discarded the old offices of [Second](/wiki/Second_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark "Second Marshal of the Riddermark") and [Third Marshals of Rohan](/wiki/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark "Third Marshal of the Riddermark") and instead created the positions of [Marshal of the West-mark](/wiki/Marshal_of_the_West-mark) (the first of whom was [Erkenbrand](/wiki/Erkenbrand)) and [Marshal of the East-mark](/wiki/Marshal_of_the_East-mark) (the first of whom was [Elfhelm](/wiki/Elfhelm)).<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Jenny_Dolfen_-_Sons_of_Rohan.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/d/dd/Jenny_Dolfen_-_Sons_of_Rohan.jpg/300px-Jenny_Dolfen_-_Sons_of_Rohan.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="413" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/d/dd/Jenny_Dolfen_-_Sons_of_Rohan.jpg/450px-Jenny_Dolfen_-_Sons_of_Rohan.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/d/dd/Jenny_Dolfen_-_Sons_of_Rohan.jpg 2x" data-file-width="500" data-file-height="689"/></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Jenny_Dolfen" title="Jenny Dolfen">Jenny Dolfen</a> - <i>Sons of Rohan</i></figcaption></figure>

But though Sauron had perished, the [King of the West](/wiki/King_of_the_West) had many enemies to destroy before the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/White_Tree" title="White Tree">White Tree</a> could grow in peace. So Éomer often fulfilled the [Oath of Eorl](/wiki/Oath_of_Eorl) and went with King Elessar far into the [East](/wiki/East) and <a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/South_(disambiguation)" title="South (disambiguation)">South</a> of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). He was known as **Éomer Éadig**, or "the Blessed", because during his reign Rohan recovered from the hurts of the War and became a rich and fruitful land again. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021") he wedded Princess [Lothíriel](/wiki/Loth%C3%ADriel), daughter of Prince [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil) of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth), whom he had met during his stay in Gondor, and she bore him a son [Elfwine](/wiki/Elfwine) the Fair.<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_A_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix_A-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup>

He kept his friendship with his knight Merry Brandybuck and when he became [Master of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland) in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [11](/wiki/Fourth_Age_11 "Fourth Age 11"), the King sent him great gifts, as did his sister from [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).<sup id="cite_ref-later_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-later-26">[26]</a></sup>

In [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [63](/wiki/Fourth_Age_63 "Fourth Age 63") Éomer, presumably feeling death coming near him, asked Master [Holdwine](/wiki/Holdwine) to visit him one last time. Both Merry and his friend Pippin came to Rohan and stayed with him until the autumn of that year, when King Éomer died.<sup id="cite_ref-later_26-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-later-26">[26]</a></sup>

King Éomer had reigned for 65 years, longer than any other King of Rohan save [Aldor the Old](/wiki/Aldor_the_Old). His son Elfwine the Fair became King of Rohan.

## Personality {#Personality}

He was tall, strong, and possessed of great passion and also was considered a valiant and splendid warrior and a discerning and decent man. Éomer was "of like height with [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)" (which was probably 6 ft, 6in.<sup id="cite_ref-RC229_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC229-3">[3]</a></sup>), due to his grandmother [Morwen Steelsheen](/wiki/Morwen_Steelsheen), who was of [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") descent.<sup id="cite_ref-RC367_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC367-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Weapon {#Weapon}

Éomer's sword was called [Gúthwinë](/wiki/G%C3%BAthwin%C3%AB),<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-10">[10]</a></sup> which is [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) for "battle-friend".<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[27]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

His name is likely derived from the Anglo-Saxon words *eoh* ("war-horse"), and *mǣre*, ("grand, excellent, famous").<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[28]</a></sup> Éo- sounds like "eh-ah" with the "ah" just barely pronounced.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29">[29]</a></sup>

He adopted the epithet *Éadig* during his kingship, which is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) word meaning "happy, wealthy" and usually translated as "blessed", used for saints in medieval literature.<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">[30]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

Éomer was very tall.<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31">[31]</a></sup> He had a beard<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32">[32]</a></sup> and clear bright eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33">[33]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

*Eomer* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name appearing in *[Beowulf](</wiki/Beowulf_(poem)> "Beowulf (poem)")*. Eomer is also an ancestor of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offa_of_Mercia" title="wikipedia:Offa of Mercia">Offa of Mercia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34">[34]</a></sup>

<b>1978: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Éomer and his army are shown slaying <a href="/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk" title="Uglúk">Uglúk</a>&#39;s band of Orcs, and later arriving at the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg" title="Battle of the Hornburg">Battle of the Hornburg</a> alongside <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> to help turn the tide (Éomer taking the place of <a href="/wiki/Erkenbrand" title="Erkenbrand">Erkenbrand</a>, in that regard). Though his name is mentioned by other characters, he is never officially identified and does not have any lines — nor is any animation superimposed over the rotoscoped live-action.</dd></dl>

<b>1979: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1979 radio series)">The Mind&#39;s Eye&#39;s The Lord of the Rings</a></i>:</b>

<b>1980: <a href="/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)"><i>The Return of the King</i> (1980 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>An unnamed Man (who has no lines) flanks Théoden leading into the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>, is later present during the <a href="/wiki/Last_Debate" title="Last Debate">Last Debate</a>, and eventually flanks Aragorn as the mustered forces march to the <a href="/wiki/Black_Gate" title="Black Gate">Black Gate</a>.
