[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966" title="wikipedia:1966">1966</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[4](4_January.md)** - Author and linguist [Arden R. Smith](../A/Arden_R._Smith.md) is born.
- **[6](6_January.md)** - [A.R. Rahman](../A/A.R._Rahman.md), composer of *[The Lord of the Rings (musical)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical).md>)*, is born.
- **[7](7_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 283](../L/Letter_283.md).
- **[7](7_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Roger Shaw](../L/Letter_to_Roger_Shaw.md).
- **[10](10_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Timothy J. Wheeler](<../L/Letter_to_Timothy_J._Wheeler_(10_January_1966).md>).
- **[11](11_January.md)** - [Robert Farquharson](../R/Robert_Farquharson.md), who played [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) and [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1955 radio series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>), dies.
- **[12](12_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to David Hicks](../L/Letter_to_David_Hicks.md).
- **[12](12_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 283a](../L/Letter_283a.md).
- **[29](29_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 283b](../L/Letter_283b.md).

## February {#February}

- **[10](10_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Donald A. Wollheim](../L/Letter_to_Donald_A._Wollheim.md).
- **[23](23_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 284](../L/Letter_284.md).

## March {#March}

- **?[2](2_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is interviewed for the article "[The Hobbit-forming World of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Hobbit-forming_World_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)".
- **[9](9_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Roger Verhulst](<../L/Letter_to_Roger_Verhulst_(9_March_1966).md>).
- **[11](11_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 284a](../L/Letter_284a.md).
- **[12](12_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to John Bush](<../L/Letter_to_John_Bush_(12_March_1966).md>).
- **[20](20_March.md)** - Director [Kenji Kamiyama](../K/Kenji_Kamiyama.md) is born.
- **[21](21_March.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to George Sayer](<../L/Letter_to_George_Sayer_(21_March_1966).md>).

## April {#April}

- **[8](8_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 285](../L/Letter_285.md).
- **[8](8_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Roger Verhulst](<../L/Letter_to_Roger_Verhulst_(8_April_1966).md>).
- **[12](12_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mother Mary Anthony](../L/Letter_to_Mother_Mary_Anthony.md).
- **[12](12_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to James Ridolfo](../L/Letter_to_James_Ridolfo.md).
- **[14](14_April.md)** - Actor [Lloyd Owen](../L/Lloyd_Owen.md) is born.
- **[19](19_April.md)** - [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](<../L/Letter_to_Rayner_Unwin_(25_April_1966).md>).
- **[25](25_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Rayner Unwin](<../L/Letter_to_Rayner_Unwin_(25_April_1966).md>).
- **[27](27_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 286](../L/Letter_286.md).

## May {#May}

- **[3](3_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Roger Verhulst](<../L/Letter_to_Roger_Verhulst_(3_May_1966).md>).
- **[6](6_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Martin Snapp](../L/Letter_to_Martin_M._Snapp.md).
- **[10](10_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes letters [287](../L/Letter_287.md) and [288](../L/Letter_288.md).
- **[30](30_May.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to Nancy Smith](<../L/Letter_to_Nancy_Smith_(30_May_1966).md>).

## June {#June}

- **[1](1_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Royston F. Porthouse](../L/Letter_to_Royston_F._Porthouse.md).
- **[9](9_June.md)** - [John Garth](../J/John_Garth.md) is born.
- **[30](30_June.md)** - [Marton Csokas](../M/Marton_Csokas.md), who would play [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), is born.

## July {#July}

- **[2](2_July.md)** - An issue of *[Saturday Evening Post](<../S/Saturday_Evening_Post_(2_July_1966).md>)* is published, containing the [an interview with Tolkien](../T/The_Hobbit-forming_World_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- **[4](4_July.md)** – *[The Jerusalem Bible](../T/The_Jerusalem_Bible.md)* is published.
- **[23](23_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) has a formal dinner in [Cambridge](../C/Cambridge.md).
- **[27](27_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) receives the unexpected visitors [J.S. Ryan](../J/John_Ryan.md) and [Hans-Jörg Modlmayr](../H/Hans-Jörg_Modlmayr.md).
- **[29](29_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Michael Tolkien](<../L/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(29_July_1966).md>), partly published as [letter 289](../L/Letter_289.md).
- **[29](29_July.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Max Rouslin](../L/Letter_to_Max_Rouslin.md).

## August {#August}

- **[2](2_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is interviewed by [William Cater](../W/William_Cater.md).
- **[6](6_August.md)** - [*Glasgow Herald* (6 August 1966)](<../G/Glasgow_Herald_(6_August_1966).md>), including an [interview](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) by [Daphne Castell](../D/Daphne_Castell.md), is published.
- **[10](10_August.md)** - [André Sogliuzzo](../A/André_Sogliuzzo.md), who would provide many voices for Tolkien-themed [video games](../../category/V/Video_games.md), is born.
- **[11](11_August.md)** - [*The Christian Science Monitor* (11 August 1966)](<../T/The_Christian_Science_Monitor_(11_August_1966).md>), including an [interview](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) by [Daphne Castell](../D/Daphne_Castell.md), is published.
- **[19](../A/August_19.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 289a](../L/Letter_289a.md).
- **[24](24_August.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) lends [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) a reading-copy of *[The End of Bovadium](../T/The_End_of_Bovadium.md)*.
- **[25](25_August.md)** - [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and suggests that *[The End of Bovadium](../T/The_End_of_Bovadium.md)* be published in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Oxford_Magazine" title="wikipedia:The Oxford Magazine">The Oxford Magazine</a></i>.
- **?** - The magazine *[Rally - August, 1966](../R/Rally_-_August,_1966.md)*, with an essay on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) by [Jared Lobdell](../J/Jared_Lobdell.md), is published.

## September {#September}

- **[12](12_September.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Rose MacNamara](../L/Letter_to_Rose_MacNamara.md).
- **?** - *[The Tolkien Reader](../T/The_Tolkien_Reader.md)* is published in the United States.

## October {#October}

- **[11](11_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 289b](../L/Letter_289b.md).
- **[12](12_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Kenneth I. Rasmussen, Jr.](../L/Letter_to_Kenneth_I._Rasmussen,_Jr..md).
- **[14](14_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 289c](../L/Letter_289c.md).
- **[16](16_October.md)** - Actress [Mary Elizabeth McGlynn](../M/Mary_Elizabeth_McGlynn.md) is born.
- **[17](17_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to schoolchildren](../L/Letter_to_a_Primary_School_in_Acocks_Green.md).
- **[18](18_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 289d](../L/Letter_289d.md).
- **[26](26_October.md)** - Finding himself short of a speech subject, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) reads *[Smith of Wootton Major](../S/Smith_of_Wootton_Major.md)* to a room of about 800 people.
- <b><a href="27_October.md" title="27 October">27</a> - The second edition of <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[28](28_October.md)** - Tolkien writes [letter 290](../L/Letter_290.md).
- **?** - The article "[Tolkien on Tolkien](../T/Tolkien_on_Tolkien.md)" is published in *Diplomat*.

## November {#November}

- **[1](1_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) conducts an [interview](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) with [Richard Plotz](../D/Dick_Plotz.md).
- **[7](7_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 290a](../L/Letter_290a.md).
- **[22](22_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 291](../L/Letter_291.md).
- **[24](24_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Harry C. Bauer](../L/Letter_to_Harry_C._Bauer.md).
- **[30](30_November.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) conducts an [interview](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md), published as "[The Man Who Understands Hobbits](../T/The_Man_Who_Understands_Hobbits.md)".
- **[30](30_November.md)** - *[Letters to an American Lady](../L/Letters_to_an_American_Lady.md)* is released.
- **?** - [*New Worlds* Vol. 50, No. 168](../N/New_Worlds_Vol._50,_No._168.md), including an [interview](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) by [Daphne Castell](../D/Daphne_Castell.md), is published.

## December {#December}

- **[8](8_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a [letter to James S. Marsh](<../L/Letter_to_James_S._Marsh_(8_December_1966).md>).
- **[12](12_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 292](../L/Letter_292.md).
- **[14](14_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) inscribes a copy of the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hobitto_no_B%C5%8Dken&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hobitto no Bōken (page does not exist)">Japanese <i>Hobbit</i></a> to Father Hugh Maycock, and [includes a brief note](../L/Letter_to_Father_Hugh_Maycock.md).
- **[15](15_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) signs three American editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* for Allen.
- **[29](29_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes a second [letter to James S. Marsh](<../L/Letter_to_James_S._Marsh_(29_December_1966).md>).
- **[29](29_December.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 293](../L/Letter_293.md).
