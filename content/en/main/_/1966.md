[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1966" title="wikipedia:1966">1966</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[4](/wiki/4_January "4 January")** - Author and linguist [Arden R. Smith](/wiki/Arden_R._Smith) is born.
- **[6](/wiki/6_January "6 January")** - [A.R. Rahman](/wiki/A.R._Rahman), composer of *[The Lord of the Rings (musical)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical)>)*, is born.
- **[7](/wiki/7_January "7 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 283](/wiki/Letter_283).
- **[7](/wiki/7_January "7 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Roger Shaw](/wiki/Letter_to_Roger_Shaw "Letter to Roger Shaw").
- **[10](/wiki/10_January "10 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Timothy J. Wheeler](</wiki/Letter_to_Timothy_J._Wheeler_(10_January_1966)> "Letter to Timothy J. Wheeler (10 January 1966)").
- **[11](/wiki/11_January "11 January")** - [Robert Farquharson](/wiki/Robert_Farquharson), who played [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) and [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1955 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)"), dies.
- **[12](/wiki/12_January "12 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to David Hicks](/wiki/Letter_to_David_Hicks "Letter to David Hicks").
- **[12](/wiki/12_January "12 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 283a](/wiki/Letter_283a "Letter 283a").
- **[29](/wiki/29_January "29 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 283b](/wiki/Letter_283b "Letter 283b").

## February {#February}

- **[10](/wiki/10_February "10 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Donald A. Wollheim](/wiki/Letter_to_Donald_A._Wollheim "Letter to Donald A. Wollheim").
- **[23](/wiki/23_February "23 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 284](/wiki/Letter_284).

## March {#March}

- **?[2](/wiki/2_March "2 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is interviewed for the article "[The Hobbit-forming World of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Hobbit-forming_World_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)".
- **[9](/wiki/9_March "9 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Roger Verhulst](</wiki/Letter_to_Roger_Verhulst_(9_March_1966)> "Letter to Roger Verhulst (9 March 1966)").
- **[11](/wiki/11_March "11 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 284a](/wiki/Letter_284a "Letter 284a").
- **[12](/wiki/12_March "12 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to John Bush](</wiki/Letter_to_John_Bush_(12_March_1966)> "Letter to John Bush (12 March 1966)").
- **[20](/wiki/20_March "20 March")** - Director [Kenji Kamiyama](/wiki/Kenji_Kamiyama) is born.
- **[21](/wiki/21_March "21 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to George Sayer](</wiki/Letter_to_George_Sayer_(21_March_1966)> "Letter to George Sayer (21 March 1966)").

## April {#April}

- **[8](/wiki/8_April "8 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 285](/wiki/Letter_285 "Letter 285").
- **[8](/wiki/8_April "8 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Roger Verhulst](</wiki/Letter_to_Roger_Verhulst_(8_April_1966)> "Letter to Roger Verhulst (8 April 1966)").
- **[12](/wiki/12_April "12 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mother Mary Anthony](/wiki/Letter_to_Mother_Mary_Anthony "Letter to Mother Mary Anthony").
- **[12](/wiki/12_April "12 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to James Ridolfo](/wiki/Letter_to_James_Ridolfo "Letter to James Ridolfo").
- **[14](/wiki/14_April "14 April")** - Actor [Lloyd Owen](/wiki/Lloyd_Owen) is born.
- **[19](/wiki/19_April "19 April")** - [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Rayner_Unwin_(25_April_1966)> "Letter to Rayner Unwin (25 April 1966)").
- **[25](/wiki/25_April "25 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Rayner Unwin](</wiki/Letter_to_Rayner_Unwin_(25_April_1966)> "Letter to Rayner Unwin (25 April 1966)").
- **[27](/wiki/27_April "27 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 286](/wiki/Letter_286 "Letter 286").

## May {#May}

- **[3](/wiki/3_May "3 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Roger Verhulst](</wiki/Letter_to_Roger_Verhulst_(3_May_1966)> "Letter to Roger Verhulst (3 May 1966)").
- **[6](/wiki/6_May "6 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Martin Snapp](/wiki/Letter_to_Martin_M._Snapp "Letter to Martin M. Snapp").
- **[10](/wiki/10_May "10 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes letters [287](/wiki/Letter_287 "Letter 287") and [288](/wiki/Letter_288 "Letter 288").
- **[30](/wiki/30_May "30 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to Nancy Smith](</wiki/Letter_to_Nancy_Smith_(30_May_1966)> "Letter to Nancy Smith (30 May 1966)").

## June {#June}

- **[1](/wiki/1_June "1 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Royston F. Porthouse](/wiki/Letter_to_Royston_F._Porthouse "Letter to Royston F. Porthouse").
- **[9](/wiki/9_June "9 June")** - [John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth) is born.
- **[30](/wiki/30_June "30 June")** - [Marton Csokas](/wiki/Marton_Csokas), who would play [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.

## July {#July}

- **[2](/wiki/2_July "2 July")** - An issue of *[Saturday Evening Post](</wiki/Saturday_Evening_Post_(2_July_1966)> "Saturday Evening Post (2 July 1966)")* is published, containing the [an interview with Tolkien](/wiki/The_Hobbit-forming_World_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "The Hobbit-forming World of J.R.R. Tolkien").
- **[4](/wiki/4_July "4 July")** – *[The Jerusalem Bible](/wiki/The_Jerusalem_Bible)* is published.
- **[23](/wiki/23_July "23 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) has a formal dinner in [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge).
- **[27](/wiki/27_July "27 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) receives the unexpected visitors [J.S. Ryan](/wiki/John_Ryan "John Ryan") and [Hans-Jörg Modlmayr](/wiki/Hans-J%C3%B6rg_Modlmayr).
- **[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Michael Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Michael_George_Tolkien_(29_July_1966)> "Letter to Michael George Tolkien (29 July 1966)"), partly published as [letter 289](/wiki/Letter_289 "Letter 289").
- **[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Max Rouslin](/wiki/Letter_to_Max_Rouslin "Letter to Max Rouslin").

## August {#August}

- **[2](/wiki/2_August "2 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is interviewed by [William Cater](/wiki/William_Cater).
- **[6](/wiki/6_August "6 August")** - [*Glasgow Herald* (6 August 1966)](</wiki/Glasgow_Herald_(6_August_1966)> "Glasgow Herald (6 August 1966)"), including an [interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) by [Daphne Castell](/wiki/Daphne_Castell), is published.
- **[10](/wiki/10_August "10 August")** - [André Sogliuzzo](/wiki/Andr%C3%A9_Sogliuzzo), who would provide many voices for Tolkien-themed [video games](/wiki/Category:Video_games "Category:Video games"), is born.
- **[11](/wiki/11_August "11 August")** - [*The Christian Science Monitor* (11 August 1966)](</wiki/The_Christian_Science_Monitor_(11_August_1966)> "The Christian Science Monitor (11 August 1966)"), including an [interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) by [Daphne Castell](/wiki/Daphne_Castell), is published.
- **[19](/wiki/August_19 "August 19")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 289a](/wiki/Letter_289a "Letter 289a").
- **[24](/wiki/24_August "24 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) lends [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) a reading-copy of *[The End of Bovadium](/wiki/The_End_of_Bovadium)*.
- **[25](/wiki/25_August "25 August")** - [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and suggests that *[The End of Bovadium](/wiki/The_End_of_Bovadium)* be published in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Oxford_Magazine" title="wikipedia:The Oxford Magazine">The Oxford Magazine</a></i>.
- **?** - The magazine *[Rally - August, 1966](/wiki/Rally_-_August,_1966)*, with an essay on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) by [Jared Lobdell](/wiki/Jared_Lobdell), is published.

## September {#September}

- **[12](/wiki/12_September "12 September")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Rose MacNamara](/wiki/Letter_to_Rose_MacNamara "Letter to Rose MacNamara").
- **?** - *[The Tolkien Reader](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Reader)* is published in the United States.

## October {#October}

- **[11](/wiki/11_October "11 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 289b](/wiki/Letter_289b).
- **[12](/wiki/12_October "12 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Kenneth I. Rasmussen, Jr.](/wiki/Letter_to_Kenneth_I._Rasmussen,_Jr. "Letter to Kenneth I. Rasmussen, Jr.").
- **[14](/wiki/14_October "14 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 289c](/wiki/Letter_289c "Letter 289c").
- **[16](/wiki/16_October "16 October")** - Actress [Mary Elizabeth McGlynn](/wiki/Mary_Elizabeth_McGlynn) is born.
- **[17](/wiki/17_October "17 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to schoolchildren](/wiki/Letter_to_a_Primary_School_in_Acocks_Green "Letter to a Primary School in Acocks Green").
- **[18](/wiki/18_October "18 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 289d](/wiki/Letter_289d "Letter 289d").
- **[26](/wiki/26_October "26 October")** - Finding himself short of a speech subject, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) reads *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)* to a room of about 800 people.
- <b><a href="/wiki/27_October" title="27 October">27</a> - The second edition of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> is published.</b>
- **[28](/wiki/28_October "28 October")** - Tolkien writes [letter 290](/wiki/Letter_290 "Letter 290").
- **?** - The article "[Tolkien on Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien_on_Tolkien)" is published in *Diplomat*.

## November {#November}

- **[1](/wiki/1_November "1 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) conducts an [interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") with [Richard Plotz](/wiki/Dick_Plotz "Dick Plotz").
- **[7](/wiki/7_November "7 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 290a](/wiki/Letter_290a).
- **[22](/wiki/22_November "22 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 291](/wiki/Letter_291 "Letter 291").
- **[24](/wiki/24_November "24 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Harry C. Bauer](/wiki/Letter_to_Harry_C._Bauer "Letter to Harry C. Bauer").
- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) conducts an [interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien"), published as "[The Man Who Understands Hobbits](/wiki/The_Man_Who_Understands_Hobbits)".
- **[30](/wiki/30_November "30 November")** - *[Letters to an American Lady](/wiki/Letters_to_an_American_Lady)* is released.
- **?** - [*New Worlds* Vol. 50, No. 168](/wiki/New_Worlds_Vol._50,_No._168 "New Worlds Vol. 50, No. 168"), including an [interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) by [Daphne Castell](/wiki/Daphne_Castell), is published.

## December {#December}

- **[8](/wiki/8_December "8 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a [letter to James S. Marsh](</wiki/Letter_to_James_S._Marsh_(8_December_1966)> "Letter to James S. Marsh (8 December 1966)").
- **[12](/wiki/12_December "12 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 292](/wiki/Letter_292 "Letter 292").
- **[14](/wiki/14_December "14 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) inscribes a copy of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hobitto_no_B%C5%8Dken&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hobitto no Bōken (page does not exist)">Japanese <i>Hobbit</i></a> to Father Hugh Maycock, and [includes a brief note](/wiki/Letter_to_Father_Hugh_Maycock "Letter to Father Hugh Maycock").
- **[15](/wiki/15_December "15 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) signs three American editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* for Allen.
- **[29](/wiki/29_December "29 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes a second [letter to James S. Marsh](</wiki/Letter_to_James_S._Marsh_(29_December_1966)> "Letter to James S. Marsh (29 December 1966)").
- **[29](/wiki/29_December "29 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 293](/wiki/Letter_293 "Letter 293").
