Events that occurred on 23 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1930](1930.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) completes a manuscript of [The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun](../T/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun.md)
- **[1944](1944.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) starts writing [Letter 81](../L/Letter_81.md).
- **[1953](1953.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 140c](../L/Letter_140c.md).
- **[1960](1960.md)** - Voice actor [Jason Carter](../J/Jason_Carter.md) is born.
- **[1977](1977.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1977](../O/Oxonmoot_1977.md) at The Welsh Pony.
- **[1978](1978.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1978](../O/Oxonmoot_1978.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[1983](1983.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1983](../O/Oxonmoot_1983.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[1988](1988.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1988](../O/Oxonmoot_1988.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[1989](1989.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1989](../O/Oxonmoot_1989.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[1990](1990.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1990](../O/Oxonmoot_1990.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- <b><a href="1993.md" title="1993">1993</a> - <i><a href="../M/Morgoth&#39;s_Ring.md" title="Morgoth&#39;s Ring">Morgoth&#39;s Ring</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **[2000](2000.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2000](../O/Oxonmoot_2000.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Antony%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Antony&#39;s College, Oxford">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2001](2001.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2001](../O/Oxonmoot_2001.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Antony%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Antony&#39;s College, Oxford">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2004](2004.md)** - *[There and Back Again: An Actor's Tale](../T/There_and_Back_Again%253A_An_Actor's_Tale.md)* is published.
- **[2011](2011.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2011](../O/Oxonmoot_2011.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Margaret_Hall,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Lady Margaret Hall, Oxford">Lady Margaret Hall</a>.
- **[2012](2012.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2012](../O/Oxonmoot_2012.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Margaret_Hall,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Lady Margaret Hall, Oxford">Lady Margaret Hall</a>.
- **[2016](2016.md)** - Actor [William Johnson](../W/William_Johnson.md) dies.
- **[2017](2017.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2017](../O/Oxonmoot_2017.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Antony%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Antony&#39;s College, Oxford">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2018](2018.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2018](../O/Oxonmoot_2018.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Antony%27s_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:St Antony&#39;s College, Oxford">St Antony&#39;s College</a>.
- **[2019](2019.md)** - *["Something Has Gone Crack"](%2522Something_Has_Gone_Crack%2522.md)* is published.
- **[2021](2021.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: Rise to War](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Rise_to_War.md)* is released online.
- **[2022](2022.md)** - The episode [Partings](../P/Partings.md) of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* is released online.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3001](../T/Third_Age_3001.md):
    - The day after [the Birthday Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md), [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) manages the inheritance of [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)'s belongings and distributes his parting gifts.
    - The rumor spreads crowds of hobbits come to loot [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) and seek treasure; including the [Sackville-Bagginses](../S/Sackville-Bagginses.md) who also demand to see Bilbo's will.
    - At the evening [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) visits Frodo, warning him not to use [the Ring](../O/One_Ring.md) and keep it secret. He changes his plans<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and begins seeking [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - Four [Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) enter [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) before dawn. The others pursue the [Rangers](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) eastward, and then return to watch the [Greenway](../G/Greenway.md).
    - [Merry Brandybuck](../M/Merry_Brandybuck.md) loads a cart with the remainder of [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md)'s luggage and leaves for [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md) with [Fatty Bolger](../F/Fatty_Bolger.md).<sup id="cite_ref-comp_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-comp-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - After lunch [Folco Boffin](../F/Folco_Boffin.md) leaves Frodo with Pippin. [Lobelia](../L/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins.md) visits Bag End with [Lotho](../L/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins.md) to inspect.<sup id="cite_ref-comp_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-comp-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - [Khamûl](../K/Khamûl.md) comes to [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md) at nightfall and interrogates [Gaffer Gamgee](../G/Gaffer_Gamgee.md), who directs him to [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md). Frodo leaves with Sam and Pippin.<sup id="cite_ref-comp_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-comp-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - The three hobbits walk west of Hobbiton, traverse [the Water](../T/The_Water.md), and after supper they join the road leading to [Bucklebury](../B/Bucklebury.md). They camp in a <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Fir&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fir (page does not exist)">fir</a>-wood.<sup id="cite_ref-comp_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-comp-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - After taming [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md), [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) leaves [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) and heads for [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
