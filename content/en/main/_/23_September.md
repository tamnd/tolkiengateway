## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001"):
    - The day after [the Birthday Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party"), [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") manages the inheritance of [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins")'s belongings and distributes his parting gifts.
    - The rumor spreads crowds of hobbits come to loot [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) and seek treasure; including the [Sackville-Bagginses](/wiki/Sackville-Bagginses) who also demand to see Bilbo's will.
    - At the evening [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) visits Frodo, warning him not to use [the Ring](/wiki/One_Ring "One Ring") and keep it secret. He changes his plans<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and begins seeking [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - Four [Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") enter [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") before dawn. The others pursue the [Rangers](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North") eastward, and then return to watch the [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway).
    - [Merry Brandybuck](/wiki/Merry_Brandybuck) loads a cart with the remainder of [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo)'s luggage and leaves for [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow) with [Fatty Bolger](/wiki/Fatty_Bolger).<sup id="cite_ref-comp_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-comp-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - After lunch [Folco Boffin](/wiki/Folco_Boffin) leaves Frodo with Pippin. [Lobelia](/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins "Lobelia Sackville-Baggins") visits Bag End with [Lotho](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins "Lotho Sackville-Baggins") to inspect.<sup id="cite_ref-comp_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-comp-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - [Khamûl](/wiki/Kham%C3%BBl) comes to [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) at nightfall and interrogates [Gaffer Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee), who directs him to [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland). Frodo leaves with Sam and Pippin.<sup id="cite_ref-comp_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-comp-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - The three hobbits walk west of Hobbiton, traverse [the Water](/wiki/The_Water "The Water"), and after supper they join the road leading to [Bucklebury](/wiki/Bucklebury). They camp in a <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Fir&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fir (page does not exist)">fir</a>-wood.<sup id="cite_ref-comp_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-comp-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - After taming [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) leaves [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) and heads for [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
