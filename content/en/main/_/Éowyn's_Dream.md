**Éowyn's Dream** is the seventh scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)"). This scene was not included in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) wanders the [Golden Hall](/wiki/Golden_Hall) at night. He walks past [Pippin](/wiki/Pippin) who, after tossing and turning, awakes.

Aragorn comes upon [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) asleep, and cover her with a blanket. She wakes and tells him of her dream in which she saw *"a great wave, climbing over green lands and above the hills"*, upon which she was standing. Aragorn comforts her in her distress, before she falls asleep again.

## Differences {#Differences}

The dream Éowyn speaks to Aragorn of in this scene is had by [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) in the books. This occurs in the chapter "[The Steward and the King](/wiki/The_Steward_and_the_King)", after his time in the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing). While Éowyn and he stand together watching [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) at the time of the destruction of the ring, he speaks to her of Númenor, and "*of the great dark wave climbing over the green lands and above the hills*". He tells her he often dreams of it.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
