**Éowyn's Dream** is the seventh scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>). This scene was not included in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) wanders the [Golden Hall](../G/Golden_Hall.md) at night. He walks past [Pippin](../P/Pippin.md) who, after tossing and turning, awakes.

Aragorn comes upon [Éowyn](Éowyn.md) asleep, and cover her with a blanket. She wakes and tells him of her dream in which she saw *"a great wave, climbing over green lands and above the hills"*, upon which she was standing. Aragorn comforts her in her distress, before she falls asleep again.

## Differences {#Differences}

The dream Éowyn speaks to Aragorn of in this scene is had by [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) in the books. This occurs in the chapter "[The Steward and the King](../T/The_Steward_and_the_King.md)", after his time in the [Houses of Healing](../H/Houses_of_Healing.md). While Éowyn and he stand together watching [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) at the time of the destruction of the ring, he speaks to her of Númenor, and "*of the great dark wave climbing over the green lands and above the hills*". He tells her he often dreams of it.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
