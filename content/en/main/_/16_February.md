Events that occurred on 16 February.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1856](1856.md)** - The wedding of [John Benjamin Tolkien](../J/John_Benjamin_Tolkien.md) and [Mary Jane Stow](../M/Mary_Jane_Stow.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s paternal grandparents.
- **[1903](1903.md)** - Actor [Norman Shelley](../N/Norman_Shelley.md) is born.
- **[1906](1906.md)** - [Colin Hardie](../C/Colin_Hardie.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), is born.
- **[1948](1948.md)** - [Nils-Lennart Johannesson](../N/Nils-Lennart_Johannesson.md) is born.
- **[1972](1972.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Miss Fay Darrington](<../L/Letter_to_Fay_Darrington_(16_February_1972).md>).
- **[1974](1974.md)** - On the advice of [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) appoints J.R.R. Tolkien as Honorary President in perpetuo.
- **[1991](1991.md)** – The second day of the first "Colloquium on the Languages of Middle-earth" at the house of [E.L.F.](../E/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship.md) member [Bill Welden](../B/Bill_Welden.md).
- **[1995](1995.md)** - *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](../T/Tyalië_Tyelelliéva.md)* issue [4](../T/Tyalië_Tyelelliéva_4.md) is published.
- **[2020](2020.md)** - la BnF's exhibition "<a class="external text" href="http://www.bnf.fr" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien, voyage en Terre du Milieu</a>" closes.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - Farewell to [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md). [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) gives the [Fellowship](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) gifts on their departure.
    - They travel south over the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) by boat while [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) in hiding observes and follows them.
