**Óin** was one of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Thorin's company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") who set out to reclaim [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") from [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug). Later, he followed [Balin](/wiki/Balin) in an ill-fated attempt to retake [Moria](/wiki/Moria).

## History {#History}

Óin was the son of [Gróin](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3in) and older brother of [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in), both of the [House of Durin](/wiki/House_of_Durin).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup>

### The Quest of Erebor {#The_Quest_of_Erebor}

Óin was one of the five Dwarves in the fourth group to arrive at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). He wore a brown hood.<sup id="cite_ref-An_Unexpected_Party_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-An_Unexpected_Party-1">[1]</a></sup>

Óin and Glóin were noted for their ability to start fires, although both failed on the night that the company met the [trolls](/wiki/Trolls "Trolls"). In their frustration they began to quarrel and eventually to fight, which was the incident that prompted the company to head for the fire seen far off on a hill.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the glade where the wolves came to gather, Óin was one of five Dwarves who climbed up to fairly comfortable seats in a huge pine tree.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

When [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) was telling the story of the company's adventures to [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn), Óin and Glóin were the fifth pair of Dwarves to show up.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Óin fought in and survived the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies). He was given his share of the treasure by [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot), and made his home in Erebor.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Expedition to Moria {#Expedition_to_Moria}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2989](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Óin had followed Balin with a [company](/wiki/Balin%27s_folk "Balin's folk") of Dwarves in an attempt to reclaim their ancient mansions of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"), which were occupied by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> At first the colony did well, but on [November 10](/wiki/November_10), [2994](/wiki/Third_Age_2994 "Third Age 2994") Balin was killed, and the Orcs returned. They took the [East-gate](/wiki/Great_Gates "Great Gates")/[First Hall](/wiki/First_Hall) then the [Second Hall](/wiki/Second_Hall) after a [fierce battle](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Second_Hall "Battle of the Second Hall") which Óin likely fought in.

Óin went with a party to find escape via the [West-gate](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin "Doors of Durin") but they found the water up to the doors and he was taken by the [Watcher in the Water](/wiki/Watcher_in_the_Water). Of his party only four returned five days after they had left, and soon thereafter all the remaining colonists were killed in a [final stand](/wiki/Last_stand_of_Balin%27s_folk "Last stand of Balin's folk") in the [Chamber of Mazarbul](/wiki/Chamber_of_Mazarbul).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Their fate was unknown to their kinsmen and their silence was largely why [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) went to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) at that time, to seek news from [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond). When [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) came to Rivendell, Glóin delighted in telling him about the doings of the renewed dwarf-kingdom. But when Frodo asked about Óin, Balin, and Ori, Glóin's face darkened and he said that he did not know.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Oinn* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. His name means "Shy".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

<b>2012-14: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Óin was portrayed by <a href="/wiki/John_Callen" title="John Callen">John Callen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting-13">[13]</a></sup> A description of Óin in <i>The Hobbit</i> films was released by the studio:</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt"></blockquote></dd></dl>

Along with his brother Gloin, Oin is a distant cousin of Thorin Oakenshield. A brave [Northern Dwarf](/wiki/Longbeards "Longbeards"), Oin joins [The Company of Thorin Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") out of a sense of loyalty to his kin, and also because he has a substantial sum of money invested in the venture. Well read with an enquiring mind, Oin is the healer among the Company, often applying an herbal salve of his own invention – which has since come to be known as ‘ointment’ after its maker.

<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/Warner_Bros." title="Warner Bros.">Warner Bros.</a><sup id="cite_ref-HobbitApp_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HobbitApp-14">[14]</a></sup></cite></p>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

<dl><dd>Perhaps the oldest of the thirteen Dwarves after Balin, Óin is also hard of hearing, as he carries an ear trumpet. When the company was captured in <a href="/wiki/Goblin-town" title="Goblin-town">Goblin-town</a>, the ear trumpet got trodden upon by a <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Goblin</a>, though Óin was mostly able to bend it back into its original shape after their escape. Due to the fact that he was the group&#39;s de facto medicine man, he opts to stay behind in <a href="/wiki/Lake-town" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a> along with <a href="/wiki/F%C3%ADli" title="Fíli">Fíli</a> to look after <a href="/wiki/K%C3%ADli" title="Kíli">Kíli</a> when <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin</a> deems him too ill to accompany the group on the final leg of their quest up to the Lonely Mountain due to having been struck by a poisoned arrow. Óin is in awe of <a href="/wiki/Tauriel" title="Tauriel">Tauriel&#39;s</a> Elvish healing powers when she ultimately heals Kíli&#39;s wound with <a href="/wiki/Athelas" title="Athelas">a<i>thelas</i></a></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The companion book <i>The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey: A Visual Companion</i> adds that Óin acted as a midwife among the dwarves. Among the infants he delivered was his nephew Gimli, though rumor suggests that Gimli slipped from the grip of his uncle and landed on his head.</dd></dl>

Bróin and Zigilburk are eventually discovered by the player, allowing the dwarves to pay proper respect to Óin&#39;s remains.</dd></dl>
