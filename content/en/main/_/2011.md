- **[21](21_March.md)** - [Update 2: Echoes of the Dead](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md#Update_2:_Echoes_of_the_Dead), the second update of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)*, is released in North America.
- **[24](24_March.md)** - [Update 2: Echoes of the Dead](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md#Update_2:_Echoes_of_the_Dead), the second update of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)*, is released in Europe.
- **[25](25_March.md)** - [Tolkien Reading Day](../T/Tolkien_Reading_Day.md).
- **[26](26_March.md)** - <a class="external text" href="http://3rdcome.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">3rd Conference on Middle-earth</a>.
- **[26](26_March.md)** - Author [Diana Wynne Jones](../D/Diana_Wynne_Jones.md) dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 228](../A/Amon_Hen_228.md)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[20](20_April.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Card_Game.md)* was released.
- **[29](29_April.md)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien's Double Worlds and Creative Process](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_Double_Worlds_and_Creative_Process.md)* by [Arne Zettersten](../A/Arne_Zettersten.md) was released.
- **[30](30_April.md)** - *[Tolkien and Wales: Language, Literature and Identity](../T/Tolkien_and_Wales.md)* by [Carl Phelpstead](../C/Carl_Phelpstead.md) was published.

- **[5](5_May.md)** - *[English and Medieval Studies Presented to J.R.R. Tolkien on the Occasion of his Seventieth Birthday](../E/English_and_Medieval_Studies_Presented_to_J.R.R._Tolkien_on_the_Occasion_of_his_Seventieth_Birthday.md)* is made available as a print-on-demand publication from [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md).
- **[20](20_May.md) - [22](22_May.md)** - [Unquendor](../U/Unquendor.md)'s Lustrumfeest.
- **[21](21_May.md) - [22](22_May.md)** - [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s Middle-earth Weekend in Sarehole, [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md).
- **[23](23_May.md)** - [Update 3: Lost Legends of Eriador](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md#Update_3:_Lost_Legends_of_Eriador), the third update of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)* is released.
- **?** - [*Tolkien Studies*: Volume 8](../T/Tolkien_Studies%253A_Volume_8.md) is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 229](../A/Amon_Hen_229.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 51](../M/Mallorn_51.md)* is published.

- **[31](31_July.md)** - *[Picturing Tolkien](../P/Picturing_Tolkien.md)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 230](../A/Amon_Hen_230.md)* is published.

- **[11](11_August.md) - [14](14_August.md)** - <a class="external text" href="http://omentielva.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Cantea</a> - 4th International Conference on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Invented Languages in Valencia, Spain.
- **[24](24_August.md)** - *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md)* is released for PlayStation 3, Xbox 360 and PC in North America.

- **[1](1_September.md)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien](<../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_(Holiday_House).md>)*, a children's book from Holiday House, was published.
