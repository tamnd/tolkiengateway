[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011" title="wikipedia:2011">2011</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[3](/wiki/3_January "3 January")** - [Birthday Toast](/wiki/Birthday_Toast#Tolkien_Society_tradition).
- **?** - [Amon Hen 227](/wiki/Amon_Hen_227) is published.

## March {#March}

- **[21](/wiki/21_March "21 March")** - Filming begins on [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").
- **[21](/wiki/21_March "21 March")** - [Update 2: Echoes of the Dead](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online#Update_2:_Echoes_of_the_Dead "The Lord of the Rings Online"), the second update of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*, is released in North America.
- **[24](/wiki/24_March "24 March")** - [Update 2: Echoes of the Dead](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online#Update_2:_Echoes_of_the_Dead "The Lord of the Rings Online"), the second update of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*, is released in Europe.
- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - [Tolkien Reading Day](/wiki/Tolkien_Reading_Day).
- **[26](/wiki/26_March "26 March")** - <a class="external text" href="http://3rdcome.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">3rd Conference on Middle-earth</a>.
- **[26](/wiki/26_March "26 March")** - Author [Diana Wynne Jones](/wiki/Diana_Wynne_Jones) dies.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 228](/wiki/Amon_Hen_228)* is published.

## April {#April}

- **[20](/wiki/20_April "20 April")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Card_Game)* was released.
- **[29](/wiki/29_April "29 April")** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien's Double Worlds and Creative Process](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_Double_Worlds_and_Creative_Process)* by [Arne Zettersten](/wiki/Arne_Zettersten) was released.
- **[30](/wiki/30_April "30 April")** - *[Tolkien and Wales: Language, Literature and Identity](/wiki/Tolkien_and_Wales "Tolkien and Wales")* by [Carl Phelpstead](/wiki/Carl_Phelpstead) was published.

## May {#May}

- **[5](/wiki/5_May "5 May")** - *[English and Medieval Studies Presented to J.R.R. Tolkien on the Occasion of his Seventieth Birthday](/wiki/English_and_Medieval_Studies_Presented_to_J.R.R._Tolkien_on_the_Occasion_of_his_Seventieth_Birthday)* is made available as a print-on-demand publication from [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins).
- **[20](/wiki/20_May "20 May") - [22](/wiki/22_May "22 May")** - [Unquendor](/wiki/Unquendor)'s Lustrumfeest.
- **[21](/wiki/21_May "21 May") - [22](/wiki/22_May "22 May")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s Middle-earth Weekend in Sarehole, [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham).
- **[23](/wiki/23_May "23 May")** - [Update 3: Lost Legends of Eriador](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online#Update_3:_Lost_Legends_of_Eriador "The Lord of the Rings Online"), the third update of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)* is released.
- **?** - [*Tolkien Studies*: Volume 8](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_8 "Tolkien Studies: Volume 8") is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 229](/wiki/Amon_Hen_229)* is published.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 51](/wiki/Mallorn_51)* is published.

## July {#July}

- **[1](/wiki/1_July "1 July")** - *[Tolkien Calendar 2012](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2012)* is published.
- **[5](/wiki/5_July "5 July")** - A revised printing of [*Parma Eldalamberon* 12](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_12 "Parma Eldalamberon 12") is released.
- **[7](/wiki/7_July "7 July")** - [*Beowulf and the Critics*: Revised Second Edition](/wiki/Beowulf_and_the_Critics "Beowulf and the Critics") is published.
- **[14](/wiki/14_July "14 July")-[17](/wiki/17_July "17 July")** - [Tolkien Thing](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing) 2011.
- **[15](/wiki/15_July "15 July") - [18](/wiki/18_July "18 July")** - [Mythcon 42](/wiki/Mythcon_42) in Albuquerque, New Mexico
- **[21](/wiki/21_July "21 July")** - *[Light Beyond All Shadow: Religious Experience in Tolkien's Work](/wiki/Light_Beyond_All_Shadow "Light Beyond All Shadow")* is published.
- **[30](/wiki/30_July "30 July")** - [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s seminar in York.
- **[31](/wiki/31_July "31 July")** - *[Picturing Tolkien](/wiki/Picturing_Tolkien)* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 230](/wiki/Amon_Hen_230)* is published.

## August {#August}

- **[11](/wiki/11_August "11 August") - [14](/wiki/14_August "14 August")** - <a class="external text" href="http://omentielva.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Cantea</a> - 4th International Conference on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s Invented Languages in Valencia, Spain.
- **[24](/wiki/24_August "24 August")** - *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North)* is released for PlayStation 3, Xbox 360 and PC in North America.
- **?** - *[Tolkien and the Study of His Sources](/wiki/Tolkien_and_the_Study_of_His_Sources)* is published.
- **(?)** - [*The One Ring* (role-playing game)](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)") is released.
- **?** - The illustrated children's book *[Oliphaunt](</wiki/Oliphaunt_(book)> "Oliphaunt (book)")* is published in a new edition.

## September {#September}

- **[1](/wiki/1_September "1 September")** - *[A Tolkien Tapestry](/wiki/A_Tolkien_Tapestry)* is published.
- **[1](/wiki/1_September "1 September")** - *[A Critical Companion to J.R.R. Tolkien: A Literary Reference to His Life and Works](/wiki/A_Critical_Companion_to_J.R.R._Tolkien "A Critical Companion to J.R.R. Tolkien")* is published.
- **[1](/wiki/1_September "1 September")** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_(Holiday_House)> "J.R.R. Tolkien (Holiday House)")*, a children's book from Holiday House, was published.
- **[23](/wiki/23_September "23 September")-[25](/wiki/25_September "25 September")** - [Oxonmoot 2011](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2011) at Lady Margaret Hall.
- **[27](/wiki/27_September "27 September")** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Rise of Isengard](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Rise_of_Isengard)* is released.
- **[29](/wiki/29_September "29 September")** - A new hardback edition of *[Mr. Bliss](/wiki/Mr._Bliss)* is released.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 231](/wiki/Amon_Hen_231)* is published.

## October {#October}

- **[19](/wiki/19_October "19 October")** - Brother [Joseph Power](/wiki/Joseph_Power) dies.
- **[27](/wiki/27_October "27 October")** - A revised updated single-edition version of *[The History of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[27](/wiki/27_October "27 October")** - *[The Art of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Art_of_The_Hobbit)* is published.
- **[27](/wiki/27_October "27 October")** - *[From Elvish to Klingon: Exploring Invented Languages](/wiki/From_Elvish_to_Klingon "From Elvish to Klingon")* is published.

## November {#November}

- **[2](/wiki/2_November "2 November")** - *[The Loss and the Silence: Aspects of Modernism in the Works of C.S. Lewis, J.R.R. Tolkien &amp; Charles Williams](/wiki/The_Loss_and_the_Silence "The Loss and the Silence")* is published.
- **?** - *[Amon Hen 232](/wiki/Amon_Hen_232)* is published.
- **?** - *[Mallorn 52](/wiki/Mallorn_52)* is published.
