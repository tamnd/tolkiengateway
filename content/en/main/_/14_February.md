Events that occurred on 14 February.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1896](/wiki/1896)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) dictates [a letter to his father](/wiki/Letter_to_Arthur_Tolkien "Letter to Arthur Tolkien").
- **[1896](/wiki/1896)** - [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon), a later colleague of Tolkien, is born.
- **[1914](/wiki/1914)** - Translator [Britt G. Hallqvist](/wiki/Britt_G._Hallqvist) is born.
- **[1918](/wiki/1918)** - Producer [William Lawrence Snyder](/wiki/William_Lawrence_Snyder) is born.
- **[1938](/wiki/1938)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) gives an invitational speech at the Lovelace Society, the essay club of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Worcester College, Oxford">Worcester College, Oxford.</a> He reads them *The Legend of Worming Hall*, a story that would become *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)*.
- **[1977](/wiki/1977)** - *[The Mythology of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Mythology_of_Middle-earth)* is published.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online): [Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar "The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar")*, "The Ashen Wastes" is released in Europe.
- **[2015](/wiki/2015)** - Actor [Alan Howard](/wiki/Alan_Howard) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) rests in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>

<li id="cite_note-1"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-1">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text">In editions prior to the <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition)" title="The Lord of the Rings (50th Anniversary Edition)">50th Anniversary Edition</a></i>, the <a href="/wiki/Appendix_B" title="Appendix B">Tale of Years</a> mentions two events on this day in <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3019" title="Third Age 3019">3019</a>: <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> returning to life, and the <a href="/wiki/Mirror_of_Galadriel" title="Mirror of Galadriel">Mirror of Galadriel</a>-sequence. Since it is clear from the narrative that the latter event occurred one day before departure on <a href="/wiki/16_February" title="16 February">16 February</a>, this causes a problem in the chronology. <a href="/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond" title="Wayne G. Hammond">Hammond</a> and <a href="/wiki/Christina_Scull" title="Christina Scull">Scull</a> decided to fix the Tale of Years so that the <a href="/wiki/Mirror_of_Galadriel" title="Mirror of Galadriel">Mirror of Galadriel</a>-sequence happened on <a href="/wiki/15_February" title="15 February">15 February</a>. The return to life of Gandalf was also moved to this date, for reasons unclear.</span>
