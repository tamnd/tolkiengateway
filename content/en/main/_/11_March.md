Events that occurred on 11 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1949](/wiki/1949)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Miss Turnbull](</wiki/Letter_to_Miss_Turnbull_(11_March_1949)> "Letter to Miss Turnbull (11 March 1949)").
- **[1953](/wiki/1953)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 135d](/wiki/Letter_135d).
- **[1963](/wiki/1963)** - Producer [Zane Weiner](/wiki/Zane_Weiner) is born.
- **[1966](/wiki/1966)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 284a](/wiki/Letter_284a "Letter 284a").
- **[1989](/wiki/1989)** - Actor [John Wyse](/wiki/John_Wyse) dies.
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - *[At Dawn in Rivendell](/wiki/At_Dawn_in_Rivendell)*, the third album of [Tolkien Ensemble](/wiki/Tolkien_Ensemble), is released (it was recorded in the previous year). It features recitations by [Christopher Lee](/wiki/Christopher_Lee).
- **[2005](/wiki/2005)** - Cartographer [Karen Wynn Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad) dies from the complications of breast cancer.
- **[2007](/wiki/2007)** - "[The One Ring Celebration](/wiki/The_One_Ring_Celebration) 2007" in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burbank,_California" title="wikipedia:Burbank, California">Burbank, California</a>.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - *[Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings – Sources of Inspiration](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_%E2%80%93_Sources_of_Inspiration)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"):
    - [Borondir](/wiki/Borondir) and his companions are on their way in search of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) visits [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob), but when he sees [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") asleep he nearly decides not to pursue his plan to recover [the Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").
    - [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) sends [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) to [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath).
    - [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) with the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) and the [Dead Men](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") reaches [Linhir](/wiki/Linhir) and crosses into [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin).
    - [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) bids [Angbor](/wiki/Angbor) of [Lamedon](/wiki/Lamedon) to follow him to [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir), where "[Isildur's Heir](/wiki/Heir_of_Isildur "Heir of Isildur") has need of him".
    - The [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) travel towards [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).
    - [Eastern](/wiki/Eastemnet "Eastemnet") [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) is invaded from the north.
    - Orcs of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) [assail](/wiki/Assaults_on_Lothl%C3%B3rien "Assaults on Lothlórien") [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), but they are beaten back.
