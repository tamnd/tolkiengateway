Events that occurred on 12 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1944](1944.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 84](../L/Letter_84.md).
- **[1944](1944.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) sends *[Leaf by Niggle](../L/Leaf_by_Niggle.md)* to the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_Review_(Catholic_periodical)" title="wikipedia:Dublin Review (Catholic periodical)">Dublin Review</a></i>, which hosts a short-story competition. It was published in the January [1945](1945.md) issue.
- **[1955](1955.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 172](../L/Letter_172.md).
- **[1966](1966.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Kenneth I. Rasmussen, Jr.](../L/Letter_to_Kenneth_I._Rasmussen,_Jr..md).
- **[1972](1972.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Rayner Unwin](<../L/Letter_to_Rayner_Unwin_(12_October_1972).md>).
- **[1979](1979.md)** - The *[Jackanory](../J/Jackanory.md)* episode "The Last Stage" is aired on the [BBC](../B/BBC.md).
- **[2004](2004.md)** - [The Lord of the Rings Symphony](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Symphony.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milwaukee" title="wikipedia:Milwaukee">Milwaukee</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wisconsin" title="wikipedia:Wisconsin">Wisconsin</a>.
- **[2007](2007.md)** - [Ring\*Con 2007](../R/Ring%252ACon_2007.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulda" title="wikipedia:Fulda">Fulda</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany" title="wikipedia:Germany">Germany</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md):
    - [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) meets [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md) who agrees to show Tuor the way to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). The pair set out on their journey.
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - With [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) severely wounded, [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) and the [four hobbits](../T/Travellers.md) travel slowly towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) hurries towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) after the [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) attacked him on [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
