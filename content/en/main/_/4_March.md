Events that occurred on 4 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1914](1914.md)** - [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) submits an essay on his favourite poet, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Thompson" title="wikipedia:Francis Thompson">Francis Thompson</a>, to the [Oxford](../O/Oxford_University.md) Essay Club.
- **[1937](1937.md)** - Tolkien's poem "[Iúmonna Gold Galdre Bewunden](../I/Iúmonna_Gold_Galdre_Bewunden.md)" is re-published in *The Oxford Magazine*.
- **[1938](1938.md)** - Tolkien writes [Letter 26](../L/Letter_26.md).
- **[1959](1959.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 214b](../L/Letter_214b.md).
- **[1970](1970.md)** - Actor [William Keen](../W/William_Keen.md) is born.
- **[1988](1988.md)** - [Shaun Gunner](../S/Shaun_Gunner.md) is born.
- **[2008](2008.md)** - Composer [Leonard Rosenman](../L/Leonard_Rosenman.md) dies.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - *[The History of Middle-earth](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* is re-released in hardcover.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Erkenbrand](../E/Erkenbrand.md) show up and finish the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md).
    - [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) set out for [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) with a guard of twenty Rohirrim. They pass the tombs of the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md).
    - The River [Isen](../I/Isen.md) is restored to its original course.
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) reach the slag-mounds on the edge of the [Desolation of the Morannon](../D/Desolation_of_the_Morannon.md).
