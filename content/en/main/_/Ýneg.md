**ýneg** is the number twelve (12, XII) in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). While the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) had a duodecimal system, [Men](/wiki/Men) did not, and didn't use this term.

The word itself is formed out of an earlier \*<i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Yuneke&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Yuneke (page does not exist)">yuneke</a></i>, meaning, "two sixes".
