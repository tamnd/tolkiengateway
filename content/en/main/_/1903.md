[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1903" title="wikipedia:1903">1903</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[16](/wiki/16_February "16 February")** - [Norman Shelley](/wiki/Norman_Shelley), who would voice [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) in [the first radio adaptation of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1956_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1956 radio series)"), is born.

## December {#December}

- **Christmas** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) takes his First Communion, and buys a copy of *[Chambers's Etymological Dictionary](/wiki/List_of_books_in_Tolkien%27s_library#Chambers's_Etymological_Dictionary "List of books in Tolkien's library")*.

## Autumn {#Autumn}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wins a Foundation Scholarship to [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School), and returns there to continue his studies.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [Stella M. Mills](/wiki/Stella_M._Mills), student under [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is born.
