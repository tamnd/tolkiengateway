Events that occurred on 16 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1917](1917.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) leaves Brooklands Officers' Hospital in Hull for Thirtle Bridge camp.
- **[1944](1944.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 85](../L/Letter_85.md).
- **[1946](1946.md)** - [Percy Bates](../P/Percy_Bates.md) dies.
- **[1959](1959.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [Letter 219](../L/Letter_219.md).
- **[1963](1963.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 249](../L/Letter_249.md).
- **[1966](1966.md)** - Actress [Mary Elizabeth McGlynn](../M/Mary_Elizabeth_McGlynn.md) is born.
- **[1968](1968.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Niall Hoskin](../L/Letter_to_Niall_Hoskin.md).
- **[2002](2002.md)** - The PlayStation 2 version of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game).md>)* are released in North America.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - Mereth Aderthad XV, or "EstelCon", in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardona,_Spain" title="wikipedia:Cardona, Spain">Cardona</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - Voice actor [Denys Hawthorne](../D/Denys_Hawthorne.md) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md):
    - [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), guided by [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md), travels to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) in order to deliver [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md)'s message to [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - With [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) severely wounded, [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) and the [four hobbits](../T/Travellers.md) travel slowly towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).
    - [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) finds their trail and follows them over the [Last Bridge](../L/Last_Bridge.md).
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) hurries towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) after the [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) attacked him on [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
