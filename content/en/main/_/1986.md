## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** - [Lord David Cecil](/wiki/Lord_David_Cecil), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), dies.

## March {#March}

- **[25](/wiki/25_March "25 March")** - The Swiss [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies") [Eredain](/wiki/Eredain) is founded.

## April {#April}

- **[23](/wiki/23_April "23 April")** - Actor [Otto Preminger](/wiki/Otto_Preminger) dies.

## July {#July}

- **[16](/wiki/16_July "16 July")** - Actor [Calum Gittins](/wiki/Calum_Gittins) is born.
- **?** - [*Beyond Bree* July 1986](/wiki/Beyond_Bree_July_1986 "Beyond Bree July 1986"), including two pages from one of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s annotated copies of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, is published.

- **[8](/wiki/8_August "8 August")-[11](/wiki/11_August "11 August")** - [Mythcon 17](/wiki/Mythcon_17).

## September {#September}

- **[19](/wiki/19_September "19 September")-[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")** - [Oxonmoot 1986](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1986)

## October {#October}

- **[18](/wiki/18_October "18 October")** - Scholar [Clyde S. Kilby](/wiki/Clyde_S._Kilby) dies.
- **[27](/wiki/27_October "27 October")** - Artist [Jemima Catlin](/wiki/Jemima_Catlin) is born.

## November {#November}

- **[2](/wiki/2_November "2 November")** - Voice acting legend [Paul Frees](/wiki/Paul_Frees) dies.
- **[14](/wiki/14_November "14 November")** - *[The Shaping of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Shaping_of_Middle-earth)* is published in the US.

## December {#December}

- **?** - [*Beyond Bree* December 1986](/wiki/Beyond_Bree_December_1986 "Beyond Bree December 1986"), including a quoted statement by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
