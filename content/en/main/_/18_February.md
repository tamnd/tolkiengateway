Events that occurred on 18 February.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1857](/wiki/1857)** - [Arthur Tolkien](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien) is born on Handsworth, Staffordshire, England.
- **[1937](/wiki/1937)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[Knocking at the Door](/wiki/The_Mewlips "The Mewlips")" is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.
- **[1938](/wiki/1938)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 24](/wiki/Letter_24).
- **[1944](/wiki/1944)** - Narrator [Anthony Jackson](/wiki/Anthony_Jackson) is born.
- **[1991](/wiki/1991)** – The final day of the first "Colloquium on the Languages of Middle-earth" at the house of [E.L.F.](/wiki/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship "Elvish Linguistic Fellowship") member [Bill Welden](/wiki/Bill_Welden).

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) travels south down the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) follows them on a log.
