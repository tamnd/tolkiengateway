[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1928" title="wikipedia:1928">1928</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[12](/wiki/12_January "12 January")** - *[A New Glossary of the Dialect of the Huddersfield District](/wiki/A_New_Glossary_of_the_Dialect_of_the_Huddersfield_District)*, containing a [Foreword](/wiki/Foreword_to_A_New_Glossary_of_the_Dialect_of_the_Huddersfield_District "Foreword to A New Glossary of the Dialect of the Huddersfield District") by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **[22](/wiki/22_January "22 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) reads a paper, [The Chill Barbarians of the North](/wiki/The_Chill_Barbarians_of_the_North), at a meeting of the Newman Society, in Oxford.

## February {#February}

- <b><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=9_Febuary&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="9 Febuary (page does not exist)">9</a></b> - [Frank Frazetta](/wiki/Frank_Frazetta), who would create illustrations for [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit) and [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[27](/wiki/27_March "27 March")-[28](/wiki/28_March "28 March")** - [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") works on the latter part of [Canto IV](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_IV "Lay of Leithian Canto IV") of *[Lay of Leithian](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian)*.

## April {#April}

- **[7](/wiki/7_April "7 April")** - *The Tablet* reports about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s paper "[The Chill Barbarians of the North](/wiki/The_Chill_Barbarians_of_the_North)".

## June {#June}

- **[6](/wiki/6_June "6 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is [invited to a dinner](/wiki/The_Meaning_of_Everything "The Meaning of Everything") marking the completion of the *[Oxford English Dictionary](/wiki/Oxford_English_Dictionary)*.

## July {#July}

- **[22](/wiki/22_July "22 July")** - [Orson Bean](/wiki/Orson_Bean), who would voice [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)") and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) (as well as Bilbo) in *[The Return of the King](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)")*, is born.

## September {#September}

- **[17](/wiki/17_September "17 September")** - [Roddy McDowall](/wiki/Roddy_McDowall), who would voice [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) in [*The Return of the King* (1980 film)](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)"), is born.

## December {#December}

- **[20](/wiki/20_December "20 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **[25](/wiki/25_December "25 December")** - Father Christmas writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.
- **[29](/wiki/29_December "29 December")** - [Bernard Cribbins](/wiki/Bernard_Cribbins), who would narrate several episodes of [Jackanory](/wiki/Jackanory)'s telling of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, is born.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien assists Sir Mortimer Wheeler in the unearthing of a Roman Asclepieion at Lydney Park, Gloucestershire.
- **?** - Tolkien draws "[Owlamoo](/wiki/Owlamoo)".
