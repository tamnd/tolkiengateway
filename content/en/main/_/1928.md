[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1928" title="wikipedia:1928">1928</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[12](12_January.md)** - *[A New Glossary of the Dialect of the Huddersfield District](../A/A_New_Glossary_of_the_Dialect_of_the_Huddersfield_District.md)*, containing a [Foreword](../F/Foreword_to_A_New_Glossary_of_the_Dialect_of_the_Huddersfield_District.md) by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.
- **[22](22_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) reads a paper, [The Chill Barbarians of the North](../T/The_Chill_Barbarians_of_the_North.md), at a meeting of the Newman Society, in Oxford.

## February {#February}

- <b><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=9_Febuary&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="9 Febuary (page does not exist)">9</a></b> - [Frank Frazetta](../F/Frank_Frazetta.md), who would create illustrations for [The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md) and [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[27](27_March.md)-[28](28_March.md)** - [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) works on the latter part of [Canto IV](../L/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_IV.md) of *[Lay of Leithian](../L/Lay_of_Leithian.md)*.

## April {#April}

- **[7](7_April.md)** - *The Tablet* reports about [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s paper "[The Chill Barbarians of the North](../T/The_Chill_Barbarians_of_the_North.md)".

## June {#June}

- **[6](6_June.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) is [invited to a dinner](../T/The_Meaning_of_Everything.md) marking the completion of the *[Oxford English Dictionary](../O/Oxford_English_Dictionary.md)*.

## July {#July}

- **[22](22_July.md)** - [Orson Bean](../O/Orson_Bean.md), who would voice [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) in [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>) and [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) (as well as Bilbo) in *[The Return of the King](<../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md>)*, is born.

## September {#September}

- **[17](17_September.md)** - [Roddy McDowall](../R/Roddy_McDowall.md), who would voice [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) in [*The Return of the King* (1980 film)](<../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md>), is born.

## December {#December}

- **[20](20_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[25](25_December.md)** - Father Christmas writes a letter to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[29](29_December.md)** - [Bernard Cribbins](../B/Bernard_Cribbins.md), who would narrate several episodes of [Jackanory](../J/Jackanory.md)'s telling of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, is born.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - Tolkien assists Sir Mortimer Wheeler in the unearthing of a Roman Asclepieion at Lydney Park, Gloucestershire.
- **?** - Tolkien draws "[Owlamoo](../O/Owlamoo.md)".
