**22 Northmoor Road** was the home of the Tolkien family for four years, from [7 January](/wiki/7_January) [1926](/wiki/1926) to [14 January](/wiki/14_January) [1930](/wiki/1930), until they "decided to move in over the fence" according to [John](/wiki/John_Tolkien "John Tolkien") and [Priscilla Tolkien](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien) in [The Tolkien Family Album](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Family_Album).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
