**22 Northmoor Road** was the home of the Tolkien family for four years, from [7 January](7_January.md) [1926](1926.md) to [14 January](14_January.md) [1930](1930.md), until they "decided to move in over the fence" according to [John](../J/John_Tolkien.md) and [Priscilla Tolkien](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md) in [The Tolkien Family Album](../T/The_Tolkien_Family_Album.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-1">[1]</a></sup>
