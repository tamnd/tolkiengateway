Events that occurred on 28 September.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1930](1930.md)** - Illustrator [Livia Rusz](../L/Livia_Rusz.md) is born.
- **[1937](1937.md)** - Voice actor [Donald Gee](../D/Donald_Gee.md) is born.
- **[1973](1973.md)** - The <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.nationalreview.com/2005/10/tolkien-rip-guy-davenport/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">National Review</a></i> publishes an article on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s death.
- **[1979](1979.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1979](../O/Oxonmoot_1979.md) at the Eastgate Hotel.
- **[1980](1980.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1980](../O/Oxonmoot_1980.md) at The Turf Tavern.
- **[1985](1985.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1985](../O/Oxonmoot_1985.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Town_Hall" title="wikipedia:Oxford Town Hall">Oxford Town Hall</a>.
- **[2004](2004.md)** - The parody book *[The Sillymarillion](../T/The_Sillymarillion.md)* is published.
- **[2008](2008.md)** - [Oxonmoot 2008](../O/Oxonmoot_2008.md) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_Church,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Christ Church, Oxford">Christ Church</a>.
- **[2010](2010.md)** - *[The Music of the Lord of the Rings Films](../T/The_Music_of_the_Lord_of_the_Rings_Films.md)*, by [Doug Adams](../D/Doug_Adams.md), is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and his companions traverse the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md) and are captured by a [Barrow-wight](../B/Barrow-wights.md). [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md) rescues them, finds and renames their five ponies, gives them weapons and suggests to visit the [Prancing Pony](../P/Prancing_Pony.md) at [Bree](../B/Bree.md).
    - [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) reaches [Sarn Ford](../S/Sarn_Ford.md).
    - [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) travels towards [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to ask for counsel.
    - At night [Khamûl](../K/Khamûl.md) and the [Ringwraith](../R/Ringwraiths.md) from [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) arrive at [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md); Khamûl sends the [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) Ringwraith to collect the Ringwraith who guarded the [Brandywine Bridge](../B/Brandywine_Bridge.md) and the horses that they had left behind. The three Ringwraiths then watch Crickhollow through the night until the next morning.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
