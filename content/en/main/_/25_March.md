Events that occurred on 25 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1934](1934.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem *[Firiel](<../F/Firiel_(poem).md>)* is published within [*The Chronicle of the Convents of the Sacred Heart* (Volume IV)](<../T/The_Chronicle_of_the_Convents_of_the_Sacred_Heart_(Volume_IV).md>).
- **[1967](1967.md)** - *The Scotsman* publishes an interview of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) by William Foster.
- **[1968](1968.md)** - Actor [Michael Reisz](../M/Michael_Reisz.md) is born.
- **[1979](1979.md)** - Actor [Lee Pace](../L/Lee_Pace.md) is born.
- **[1986](1986.md)** - The Swiss [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md) [Eredain](../E/Eredain.md) is founded.
- Since **[2003](2003.md)** - [Tolkien Reading Day](../T/Tolkien_Reading_Day.md).
- **[2014](2014.md)** - *[Anglo-Saxon Community in J.R.R. Tolkien's the Lord of the Rings](../A/Anglo-Saxon_Community_in_J.R.R._Tolkien's_the_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is published.
- **[2015](2015.md)** - [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md) launches a new version of the website *tolkienestate.com*, including many articles.
- **[2019](2019.md)** - *[Tolkien the Pagan?](../T/Tolkien_the_Pagan%253F.md)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md):
    - [Borondir](../B/Borondir.md) reaches the [Éothéod](Éothéod.md) and delivers them the message of [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md), [Steward of Gondor](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The Host of the West is surrounded by enemies on the Slag-hills.
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) reach the [Sammath Naur](../C/Cracks_of_Doom.md). [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) seizes the [Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) and falls in the [Cracks of Doom](../C/Cracks_of_Doom.md).
    - Downfall of [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md) and passing of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).
    - [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) and [Samwise](../S/Samwise.md) are borne away by [Gwaihir](../G/Gwaihir.md) and his brothers.
    - [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) and [Éowyn](Éowyn.md) watch from the walls of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) as the shadow is lifted from [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).
    - An eagle brings news to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) about the fall of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).
    - Beginning of the New Year in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3020](../T/Third_Age_3020.md):
    - [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) returns to the [Shire](../S/Shire.md) from forestry work; Frodo has been ill, but he has already recovered and says nothing about himself to Samwise.
- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3021](../T/Third_Age_3021.md):
    - Beginning of the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md) in the reckoning of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-chief_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Elanor the Fair](../E/Elanor_Gardner.md), daughter of Samwise, is born.<sup id="cite_ref-chief_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted in *[Nomenclature](../G/Guide_to_the_Names_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* that the date the Ring was destroyed was purposefully chosen. It coincides with the feast of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annunciation" title="wikipedia:Annunciation">Annunciation</a> or <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Day" title="wikipedia:Lady Day">Lady Day</a>, in which the Savior entered the world. Also of note, Tolkien likely chose the 25th of March as the date of the destruction of the One Ring (showing the influence of his [Catholic faith](../C/Christianity.md)) as this is widely believed to have been the day of the death of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_Christ" title="wikipedia:Jesus Christ">Jesus Christ</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
