[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1994" title="wikipedia:1994">1994</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[28](/wiki/28_January "28 January")** - Actress [Ema Horvath](/wiki/Ema_Horvath) is born.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 33](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_33) is published.

## February {#February}

- **[1](/wiki/1_February "1 February")** - *[Tolkien's Ring](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Ring)* is published.
- **[11](/wiki/11_February "11 February")** - Actor [William Conrad](/wiki/William_Conrad) dies.

## March {#March}

- **[23](/wiki/23_March "23 March")** - [Donald Swann](/wiki/Donald_Swann), composer of *[The Road Goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")*, dies.
- **?** - [*Vinyar Tengwar* 34](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_34 "Vinyar Tengwar 34") is published.
- **?** - *[The Tolkien Collector](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Collector)* Issue 6 is published.

## May {#May}

- **[24](/wiki/24_May "24 May")** - [John Wain](/wiki/John_Wain), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), dies.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 35](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_35) is published.

## July {#July}

- **[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 1 is published.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 36](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_36) is published.

## August {#August}

- **[5](/wiki/5_August "5 August")-[8](/wiki/8_August "8 August")** - [Mythcon 25](/wiki/Mythcon_25).

## September {#September}

- **[16](/wiki/16_September "16 September")-[18](/wiki/18_September "18 September")** - [Oxonmoot 1994](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1994).

## October {#October}

- <b><a href="/wiki/20_October" title="20 October">20</a> - <i><a href="/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels" title="The War of the Jewels">The War of the Jewels</a></i> is published in the UK.</b>
- **[26](/wiki/26_October "26 October")** - *[Tyalië Tyelelliéva](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va)* issue [2](/wiki/Tyali%C3%AB_Tyelelli%C3%A9va_2 "Tyalië Tyelelliéva 2") is published.
- **?** - [The Lord of the Rings: Volume 1](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Volume_1) is released on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNES" title="wikipedia:SNES">SNES</a>.

## December {#December}

- **[6](/wiki/6_December "6 December")** - *[The War of the Jewels](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels)* is published in the US.
- **[29](/wiki/29_December "29 December")** - *[Tyalie Tyelellieva](/wiki/Tyalie_Tyelellieva)* issue 3 is published.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 31](/wiki/Mallorn_31 "Mallorn 31") is published.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar 1995](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar_1995) is released.
- **?** - [Leonid Korablev Calendar 1995](/wiki/Leonid_Korablev_Calendar_1995) is released.
- **?** - *[Northwestern Middle-earth Map Set](/wiki/Northwestern_Middle-earth_Map_Set)* is published.
- **?** - [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins), now Tolkien's primary publisher, resets the text of *The Lord of the Rings* in digital form, introducing many errors.
