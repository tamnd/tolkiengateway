Events that occurred on 21 December.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1927](1927.md)** – [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[1933](1933.md)** – [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.
- **[1933](1933.md)** – [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to R.W. Chambers](../L/Letter_to_R.W._Chambers.md).
- **[1955](1955.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Mrs. Rachael Feichtmeir](../L/Letter_to_Mrs_Rachael_Feichtmeir.md).
- **[2001](2001.md)** – [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md) published "Temptations for All Time", a review of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* for *The Times Literary Supplement*.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) rests in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and prepare for their [quest](../Q/Quest_of_the_Ring.md) to destroy [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).
