Events that occurred on 22 October.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1920](/wiki/1920)** - [Michael Tolkien](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s second son, is born.
- **[1927](/wiki/1927)** - Actor [James Grout](/wiki/James_Grout) is born.
- **[1938](/wiki/1938)** - Actor and narrator [Derek Jacobi](/wiki/Derek_Jacobi) is born.
- **[1945](/wiki/1945)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 104](/wiki/Letter_104).
- **[1955](/wiki/1955)** - [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) wrote "[The Dethronement of Power](/wiki/The_Dethronement_of_Power)", a review of *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)* and *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)* published in <i><a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_and_Tide_(magazine)" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Time and Tide</a></i>.
- **[1962](/wiki/1962)** - *[O Gnomo](/wiki/O_Gnomo)*, the first Portuguese translation of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, is published.
- **[2002](/wiki/2002)** - The PC version of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)")* is released in North America.
- **[2019](/wiki/2019)** - <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibliothèque_nationale_de_France" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">la BnF</a>'s exhibition "<a class="external text" href="https://www.bnf.fr/fr/agenda/tolkien-voyage-en-terre-du-milieu" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien, voyage en Terre du Milieu</a>" opens.
- **[2020](/wiki/2020)** - American author [Richard A. Lupoff](/wiki/Richard_A._Lupoff) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"):
    - [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), guided by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), travels to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in order to deliver [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s message to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") recovers from his wound in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
