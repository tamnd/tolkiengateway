[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991" title="wikipedia:1991">1991</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[4](/wiki/4_January "4 January")** - Actress [Olivia Tennet](/wiki/Olivia_Tennet) is born.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 15](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_15) is published.

## February {#February}

- **[9](/wiki/9_February "9 February")** - First official meeting of the [Sociedad Tolkien Española](/wiki/Sociedad_Tolkien_Espa%C3%B1ola).
- **[15](/wiki/15_February "15 February")-[18](/wiki/18_February "18 February")** - The first *Colloquium on the Languages of Middle-earth* starts at the house of [E.L.F.](/wiki/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship "Elvish Linguistic Fellowship") member [Bill Welden](/wiki/Bill_Welden).
- **[17](/wiki/17_February "17 February")** - Singer [Ed Sheeran](/wiki/Ed_Sheeran) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[9](/wiki/9_March "9 March")** - Norwegian translator [Nils Werenskiold](/wiki/Nils_Werenskiold) dies.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 16](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_16) is published.

## April {#April}

- **[10](/wiki/10_April "10 April")** - *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth)*, revised edition, is published.

## May {#May}

- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 17](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_17) is published.

## July {#July}

- **[22](/wiki/22_July "22 July")** - [Margaret Carroux](/wiki/Margaret_Carroux), the translator of the first German *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*-translation, dies.
- **[25](/wiki/25_July "25 July")** - Actor [Brian Haines](/wiki/Brian_Haines) dies.
- **[26](/wiki/26_July "26 July")-[29](/wiki/29_July "29 July")** - [Mythcon 22](/wiki/Mythcon_22).
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 18](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_18) is published.

## August {#August}

- **[3](/wiki/3_August "3 August")** - [Priscilla Tolkien](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien) speaks on "J.R.R. Tolkien: Scholar and Storyteller" at the Martin Luther King Memorial Library in Washington, D.C.
- **[19](/wiki/19_August "19 August")** - [Taum Santoski](/wiki/Taum_Santoski), who assisted [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) with *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* and wrote much of the base of *[The History of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit)*, dies of cancer at the age of 32.

## September {#September}

- **[20](/wiki/20_September "20 September")-[22](/wiki/22_September "22 September")** - [Oxonmoot 1991](/wiki/Oxonmoot_1991).
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 19](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_19) is published.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 28](/wiki/Mallorn_28 "Mallorn 28") is published.

## October {#October}

- **?** - [Elendor](/wiki/Elendor), the oldest Multi-User Shared Hallucination (MUSH) server, is established.

## November {#November}

- **[15](/wiki/15_November "15 November")** - *[After the King: Stories In Honor of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/After_the_King "After the King")* is published.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 20](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_20) is published.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s secretary [Joy Hill](/wiki/Joy_Hill) dies.
- **?** - The [Sociedad Tolkien Española](/wiki/Sociedad_Tolkien_Espa%C3%B1ola) is founded.
- **?** - [Tolkien - The Centenary Calendar 1992](/wiki/Tolkien_-_The_Centenary_Calendar_1992) is released.
- **?** - [The 1992 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar](/wiki/The_1992_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar) is released.
- **?** - *[The Tolkien Diary 1992](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Diary_1992)* is published.
