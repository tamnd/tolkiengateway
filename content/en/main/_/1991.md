[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991" title="wikipedia:1991">1991</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[4](4_January.md)** - Actress [Olivia Tennet](../O/Olivia_Tennet.md) is born.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 15](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_15.md) is published.

## February {#February}

- **[9](9_February.md)** - First official meeting of the [Sociedad Tolkien Española](../S/Sociedad_Tolkien_Española.md).
- **[15](15_February.md)-[18](18_February.md)** - The first *Colloquium on the Languages of Middle-earth* starts at the house of [E.L.F.](../E/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship.md) member [Bill Welden](../B/Bill_Welden.md).
- **[17](17_February.md)** - Singer [Ed Sheeran](../E/Ed_Sheeran.md) is born.

## March {#March}

- **[9](9_March.md)** - Norwegian translator [Nils Werenskiold](../N/Nils_Werenskiold.md) dies.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 16](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_16.md) is published.

## April {#April}

- **[10](10_April.md)** - *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](../T/The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth.md)*, revised edition, is published.

## May {#May}

- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 17](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_17.md) is published.

## July {#July}

- **[22](22_July.md)** - [Margaret Carroux](../M/Margaret_Carroux.md), the translator of the first German *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*-translation, dies.
- **[25](25_July.md)** - Actor [Brian Haines](../B/Brian_Haines.md) dies.
- **[26](26_July.md)-[29](29_July.md)** - [Mythcon 22](../M/Mythcon_22.md).
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 18](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_18.md) is published.

## August {#August}

- **[3](3_August.md)** - [Priscilla Tolkien](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md) speaks on "J.R.R. Tolkien: Scholar and Storyteller" at the Martin Luther King Memorial Library in Washington, D.C.
- **[19](19_August.md)** - [Taum Santoski](../T/Taum_Santoski.md), who assisted [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) with *[The History of Middle-earth](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* and wrote much of the base of *[The History of The Hobbit](../T/The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)*, dies of cancer at the age of 32.

## September {#September}

- **[20](20_September.md)-[22](22_September.md)** - [Oxonmoot 1991](../O/Oxonmoot_1991.md).
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 19](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_19.md) is published.
- **?** - [*Mallorn* 28](../M/Mallorn_28.md) is published.

## October {#October}

- **?** - [Elendor](../E/Elendor.md), the oldest Multi-User Shared Hallucination (MUSH) server, is established.

## November {#November}

- **[15](15_November.md)** - *[After the King: Stories In Honor of J.R.R. Tolkien](../A/After_the_King.md)* is published.
- **?** - [Vinyar Tengwar 20](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_20.md) is published.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s secretary [Joy Hill](../J/Joy_Hill.md) dies.
- **?** - The [Sociedad Tolkien Española](../S/Sociedad_Tolkien_Española.md) is founded.
- **?** - [Tolkien - The Centenary Calendar 1992](../T/Tolkien_-_The_Centenary_Calendar_1992.md) is released.
- **?** - [The 1992 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar](../T/The_1992_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar.md) is released.
- **?** - *[The Tolkien Diary 1992](../T/The_Tolkien_Diary_1992.md)* is published.
