Events that occurred on 1 April.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1977](/wiki/1977)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children found [The Tolkien Trust](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Trust) charity.
- **[1984](/wiki/1984)** - *[The Monsters and the Critics](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics)* is published in the US.<b></b>
- **[2003](/wiki/2003)** - *[J.R.R. Tolkien: His Life and Works](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_(Library_of_Great_Authors)> "J.R.R. Tolkien (Library of Great Authors)")* is published.
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - *[The Roots of Tolkien's Middle Earth](/wiki/The_Roots_of_Tolkien%27s_Middle_Earth)* is published.
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** - *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: War of the Ring](</wiki/War_of_the_Ring_(2009_book)> "War of the Ring (2009 book)")* is published.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudr%C3%BAn)* is released in paperback.
- **[2010](/wiki/2010)** - *[West of the Mountains, East of the Sea](/wiki/West_of_the_Mountains,_East_of_the_Sea)* is published.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), rescued out of the fire, rest in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).
