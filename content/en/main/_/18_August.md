Events that occurred on 18 August

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1945](/wiki/1945)** - [Eric Rücker Eddison](/wiki/Eric_R%C3%BCcker_Eddison) dies.
- **[1954](/wiki/1954)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 148a](/wiki/Letter_148a).
- **[1973](/wiki/1973)** - [Mythcon 4](/wiki/Mythcon_4) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Barbara,_California" title="wikipedia:Santa Barbara, California">Santa Barbara</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1975](/wiki/1975)** - [Mythcon 6](/wiki/Mythcon_6) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scripps_College" title="wikipedia:Scripps College">Scripps College</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont,_California" title="wikipedia:Claremont, California">Claremont</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California" title="wikipedia:California">California</a>.
- **[1992](/wiki/1992)** - [Tolkien Centenary Conference](/wiki/Tolkien_Centenary_Conference), a combination of [Oxonmoot](/wiki/Oxonmoot) 1992 and [Mythcon](/wiki/Mythcon) 23, at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keble_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Keble College, Oxford">Keble College, Oxford</a>.
- **[1997](/wiki/1997)** - The audiobook *[Pearl and Sir Orfeo](</wiki/Pearl_and_Sir_Orfeo_(audiobook)> "Pearl and Sir Orfeo (audiobook)")* by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), read by Terry Jones, is published.
- **[2000](/wiki/2000)** - [Mythcon 31](/wiki/Mythcon_31) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaii" title="wikipedia:Hawaii">Hawaii</a>.
- **[2008](/wiki/2008)** - [Mythcon 39](/wiki/Mythcon_39) at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Connecticut_State_University" title="wikipedia:Central Connecticut State University">Central Connecticut State University</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Britain,_Connecticut" title="wikipedia:New Britain, Connecticut">New Britain</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut" title="wikipedia:Connecticut">Connecticut</a>.
- **[2012](/wiki/2012)** - [The Return of the Ring](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_Ring), [Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society "The Tolkien Society") conference at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loughborough_University" title="wikipedia:Loughborough University">Loughborough University</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>.
- **[2023](/wiki/2023)** - Game developer [Wendell Lariston Hill, Jr.](/wiki/Wendell_Lariston_Hill,_Jr.) dies.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"):
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) is held captive by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) on the pinnacle of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).
    - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) travels towards [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to ask for counsel.
- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - A riding of [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), the [Hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers"), and many more, make camp at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
