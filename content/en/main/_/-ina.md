<i><b>-ina</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) suffix that has two meanings.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:68</sup>

## -ina<sup>1</sup> {#-ina1}

Used as an aorist suffix. This use is only mentioned in a marginal note and no examples are given.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:68</sup>

## -ina<sup>2</sup> {#-ina2}

Used as an adjectival suffix.

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i>cal<b>ina</b></i> = lit. 'illuminated' "sunny, light" (*[cala](/wiki/Cala "Cala")*, [KAL](/wiki/KAL) light)<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:153</sup>
- <i>lamb<b>ina</b></i> = "of tongue, spoken with tongue" (*[lambe](/wiki/Lambe "Lambe")* = "tongue")<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:46</sup><sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:123</sup>
- <i>lat<b>ina</b></i> = "used of freedom of movement, of things not encumbered with obstacles" (<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=LAT&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LAT (page does not exist)">LAT</a> open, unenclosed, free to entry)<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:159</sup>
- <i>ler<b>ina</b></i> = "not guarded, reserved, made fast or owned" (<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=LER&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LER (page does not exist)">LER</a> free (of moveable or moving things), able to move as willed, unimpeded, unharmed, loose, not fixed fast or static)<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:160</sup>
- <i>ra<b>ina</b></i> = "smiling, gracious, sweet faced" (<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=RAYA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="RAYA (page does not exist)">RAYA</a> smile, show pleasure or favour in facial expression)<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:182</sup>
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Elerr%C3%ADna" title="Elerrína">Elerr<b>ína</b></a></i> = "Crowned of Stars"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
