[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1923" title="wikipedia:1923">1923</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[Iúmonna Gold Galdre Bewunden](/wiki/I%C3%BAmonna_Gold_Galdre_Bewunden)" is published in *The Gryphon*.

## February {#February}

- **[1](/wiki/1_February "1 February")** - [Frank Duncan](/wiki/Frank_Duncan), who would play <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Torhthelm&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Torhthelm (page does not exist)">Torhthelm</a> in *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth, Beorhthelm's Son (radio play)](</wiki/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth,_Beorhthelm%27s_Son_(radio_play)>)* and [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Halbarad](/wiki/Halbarad) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1955 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)"), is born.
- **[13](/wiki/13_February "13 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 6](/wiki/Letter_6).

## March {#March}

- **[21](/wiki/21_March "21 March")** - [Peter Pratt](/wiki/Peter_Pratt), who would play [Balin](/wiki/Balin) in [*The Hobbit* (1968 radio series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)"), is born.

## April {#April}

- **[4](/wiki/4_April "4 April")** - Actor [Peter Vaughan](/wiki/Peter_Vaughan) is born.
- **[26](/wiki/26_April "26 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s review "[Holy Maidenhood](/wiki/Holy_Maidenhood)" is published in *The Times Literary Supplement*.

## June {#June}

- **?** - The collection *[A Northern Venture](/wiki/A_Northern_Venture)*, which includes "[Enigmata Saxonica Nuper Inventa Duo](/wiki/Enigmata_Saxonica_Nuper_Inventa_Duo)" by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.

## August {#August}

- **[9](/wiki/9_August "9 August")** - [John Stephenson](/wiki/John_Stephenson), who would voice [Dori](/wiki/Dori), the [Great Goblin](/wiki/Great_Goblin) and [Bard the Bowman](/wiki/Bard "Bard") in the animated version of *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)")*, is born.

## September {#September}

- **[21](/wiki/21_September "21 September")** - [Roland Adlerberth](/wiki/Roland_Adlerberth) is born.
- **[30](/wiki/30_September "30 September")** - Musician [Donald Swann](/wiki/Donald_Swann) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[17](/wiki/17_October "17 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) receives an increase in salary from £600 p.a. to £700 p.a.
- **[23](/wiki/23_October "23 October")** - [*Bulletin of the Modern Humanities Research Association, number 20*](/wiki/Bulletin_of_the_Modern_Humanities_Research_Association_20 "Bulletin of the Modern Humanities Research Association 20"), containing <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Henry_Bradley,_3_Dec.,_1845-23_May,_1923&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Henry Bradley, 3 Dec., 1845-23 May, 1923 (page does not exist)">an obituary</a> written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.

## December {#December}

- **[24](/wiki/24_December "24 December")** - [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) writes a [letter](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas") to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **(Spring)** - *[The Microcosm, Vol.8 No.1](/wiki/The_Microcosm,_Vol.8_No.1)*, including the poem *[The City of the Gods](/wiki/The_City_of_the_Gods)* by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **(Autumn)** - Tolkien publishes the poem *[The Cat and the Fiddle](/wiki/The_Man_in_the_Moon_Stayed_Up_Too_Late "The Man in the Moon Stayed Up Too Late")*.
