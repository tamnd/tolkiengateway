[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1923" title="wikipedia:1923">1923</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem "[Iúmonna Gold Galdre Bewunden](../I/Iúmonna_Gold_Galdre_Bewunden.md)" is published in *The Gryphon*.

## February {#February}

- **[1](1_February.md)** - [Frank Duncan](../F/Frank_Duncan.md), who would play <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Torhthelm&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Torhthelm (page does not exist)">Torhthelm</a> in *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth, Beorhthelm's Son (radio play)](<../T/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth,_Beorhthelm's_Son_(radio_play).md>)* and [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) and [Halbarad](../H/Halbarad.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1955 radio series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>), is born.
- **[13](13_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 6](../L/Letter_6.md).

## March {#March}

- **[21](21_March.md)** - [Peter Pratt](../P/Peter_Pratt.md), who would play [Balin](../B/Balin.md) in [*The Hobbit* (1968 radio series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>), is born.

## April {#April}

- **[4](4_April.md)** - Actor [Peter Vaughan](../P/Peter_Vaughan.md) is born.
- **[26](26_April.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s review "[Holy Maidenhood](../H/Holy_Maidenhood.md)" is published in *The Times Literary Supplement*.

## June {#June}

- **?** - The collection *[A Northern Venture](../A/A_Northern_Venture.md)*, which includes "[Enigmata Saxonica Nuper Inventa Duo](../E/Enigmata_Saxonica_Nuper_Inventa_Duo.md)" by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.

## August {#August}

- **[9](9_August.md)** - [John Stephenson](../J/John_Stephenson.md), who would voice [Dori](../D/Dori.md), the [Great Goblin](../G/Great_Goblin.md) and [Bard the Bowman](../B/Bard.md) in the animated version of *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>)*, is born.

## September {#September}

- **[21](21_September.md)** - [Roland Adlerberth](../R/Roland_Adlerberth.md) is born.
- **[30](30_September.md)** - Musician [Donald Swann](../D/Donald_Swann.md) is born.

## October {#October}

- **[17](17_October.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) receives an increase in salary from £600 p.a. to £700 p.a.
- **[23](23_October.md)** - [*Bulletin of the Modern Humanities Research Association, number 20*](../B/Bulletin_of_the_Modern_Humanities_Research_Association_20.md), containing <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Henry_Bradley,_3_Dec.,_1845-23_May,_1923&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Henry Bradley, 3 Dec., 1845-23 May, 1923 (page does not exist)">an obituary</a> written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.

## December {#December}

- **[24](24_December.md)** - [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) writes a [letter](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s children.

## ? {#?}

- **(Spring)** - *[The Microcosm, Vol.8 No.1](../T/The_Microcosm,_Vol.8_No.1.md)*, including the poem *[The City of the Gods](../T/The_City_of_the_Gods.md)* by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.
- **(Autumn)** - Tolkien publishes the poem *[The Cat and the Fiddle](../T/The_Man_in_the_Moon_Stayed_Up_Too_Late.md)*.
