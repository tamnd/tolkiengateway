**Óswine** was the father of [Éadwine](/wiki/%C3%89adwine) and thus a grandfather of [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine) the Mariner.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Óswine* is in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English). It contains the elements *os* ("god") + *wine* ("friend").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
