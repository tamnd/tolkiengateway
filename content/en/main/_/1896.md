[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1896" title="wikipedia:1896">1896</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[14](14_February.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) dictates [a letter to his father](../L/Letter_to_Arthur_Tolkien.md).
- **[14](14_February.md)** - [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md), a later colleague of Tolkien, is born.
- **[15](15_February.md)** - [Arthur Tolkien](../A/Arthur_Tolkien.md) dies in [Bloemfontein](../B/Bloemfontein.md), as a result of a severe hemorrhage suffered the previous day. He had been sick for many months with rheumatic fever, and never made the trip to [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md) to see his family.
- **?** - [Mabel Tolkien](../M/Mabel_Suffield.md) and her family move from Birmingham to the hamlet of [Sarehole](../S/Sarehole.md).

## April {#April}

- **[7](7_April.md)** - [Hugo Dyson](../H/Hugo_Dyson.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), is born.

## August {#August}

- **[1](1_August.md)** - [John Benjamin Tolkien](../J/John_Benjamin_Tolkien.md), grandfather of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), dies.

## October {#October}

- **[3](3_October.md)** - [William Morris](../W/William_Morris.md), artist and author who influenced [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), dies.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [James Dundas-Grant](../J/James_Dundas-Grant.md), [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md), is born.
