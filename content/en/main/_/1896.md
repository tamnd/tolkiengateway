[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1896" title="wikipedia:1896">1896</a></b>.

## February {#February}

- **[14](/wiki/14_February "14 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) dictates [a letter to his father](/wiki/Letter_to_Arthur_Tolkien "Letter to Arthur Tolkien").
- **[14](/wiki/14_February "14 February")** - [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon), a later colleague of Tolkien, is born.
- **[15](/wiki/15_February "15 February")** - [Arthur Tolkien](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien) dies in [Bloemfontein](/wiki/Bloemfontein), as a result of a severe hemorrhage suffered the previous day. He had been sick for many months with rheumatic fever, and never made the trip to [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham) to see his family.
- **?** - [Mabel Tolkien](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield "Mabel Suffield") and her family move from Birmingham to the hamlet of [Sarehole](/wiki/Sarehole).

## April {#April}

- **[7](/wiki/7_April "7 April")** - [Hugo Dyson](/wiki/Hugo_Dyson), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), is born.

## August {#August}

- **[1](/wiki/1_August "1 August")** - [John Benjamin Tolkien](/wiki/John_Benjamin_Tolkien), grandfather of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), dies.

## October {#October}

- **[3](/wiki/3_October "3 October")** - [William Morris](/wiki/William_Morris), artist and author who influenced [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), dies.

## ? {#?}

- **?** - [James Dundas-Grant](/wiki/James_Dundas-Grant), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), is born.
