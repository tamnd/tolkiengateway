Events that occurred on 4 February

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1929](1929.md)** - Actor [Noel Appleby](../N/Noel_Appleby.md) is born.
- **[1933](1933.md)** - [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) writes to lifelong friend Arthur Greeves about *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.
- **[1937](1937.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s poem "[The Dragon's Visit](../T/The_Dragon's_Visit.md)" is published in *The Oxford Magazine*.
- **[1938](1938.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 22](../L/Letter_22.md).
- **[1959](1959.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 214a](../L/Letter_214a.md).
- **[1971](1971.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [Letter 321](../L/Letter_321.md).
- **[2006](2006.md)** - Previews of [The Lord of the Rings (musical)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical).md>) open in Toronto.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md):
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) rests in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).
