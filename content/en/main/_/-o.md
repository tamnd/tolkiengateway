<i><b>-o</b></i> (pl. **-ion** and **-ron**) is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) element that marks the genitive of nouns. <sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is derived from the element <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=HO&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="HO (page does not exist)">HO</a> "from" used as an enclitic and eventually as a suffix.

## Examples {#Examples}

- **Oromëo:** "coming from Orome, of Orome" <b><sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup></b>
- **Elenna-nórëo:** "of the Land of the Star" <sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- **Rithil-Anamo**"Doom Ring" <sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- **Rúmilo**"of Rúmil" <sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **aran Lestanórëo**"King of Doriath"<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>
