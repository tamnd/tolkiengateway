## May {#May}

- **[8](/wiki/8_May "8 May")** - [Vera Chapman](/wiki/Vera_Chapman), who will later found [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society), is born.

## August {#August}

- **[28](/wiki/28_August "28 August")** - [R.B. McCallum](/wiki/R.B._McCallum), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") is born

## November {#November}

- **[9](/wiki/9_November "9 November")** - [Owen Barfield](/wiki/Owen_Barfield), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), is born.
- **[29](/wiki/29_November "29 November")** - [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), who will play an immense part in the development of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s works, is born.
