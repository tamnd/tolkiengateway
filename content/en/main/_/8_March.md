Events that occurred on 8 March.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- <b><a href="/wiki/1939" title="1939">1939</a> - <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> gives the lecture <i><a href="/wiki/On_Fairy-Stories" title="On Fairy-Stories">On Fairy-Stories</a></i>.</b>
- **[1941](/wiki/1941)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) finishes writing [Letter 43](/wiki/Letter_43).
- **[1956](/wiki/1956)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to William Galbraith](/wiki/Letter_to_William_Galbraith "Letter to William Galbraith").
- **[1973](/wiki/1973)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 348a](/wiki/Letter_348a).
- **[1973](/wiki/1973)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 349](/wiki/Letter_349).
- **[1981](/wiki/1981)** - The first episode of [the 1981 radio adaptation](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is aired on [BBC Radio 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
- **[2004](/wiki/2004)** - The final day of "[RingCon IV](/wiki/RingCon "RingCon")" in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle" title="wikipedia:Seattle">Seattle</a>.

## In Arda {#In_Arda}

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"):
    - [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") leave [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn) and say farewell to [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir). [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) comes with them.
    - [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) travels with [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") along the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) towards [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).
    - [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) take the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead) at daybreak. They reach the [Stone of Erech](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech) at midnight.
    - [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and the [éoherë](/wiki/%C3%89oher%C3%AB "Éoherë") travel towards [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow).
