Events that occurred on 11 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1902](1902.md)** - Illustrator [Eric Fraser](../E/Eric_Fraser.md) is born.
- **[1920](1920.md)** - German translator [Walter Scherf](../W/Walter_Scherf.md) is born.
- **[1935](1935.md)** – Guardian and "second father" of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) [Father Francis](../F/Francis_Xavier_Morgan.md) dies.
- **[1953](1953.md)** - *[Pearl](<../P/Pearl_(E.V._Gordon).md>)*, edited by [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md) and including a contribution by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), is published.
- **[1972](1972.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Robert Burchfield](<../L/Letter_to_Robert_Burchfield_(11_June_1972).md>).
- **[1975](1975.md)** - Actor [John Tui](../J/John_Tui.md) is born.
- **[1977](1977.md)** - The first [Wisconsin Ring Game](../W/Wisconsin_Ring_Game.md) is held at <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_Dodge_State_Park" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Governor Dodge State Park</a> in southern Wisconsin.
- **[2006](2006.md)** - Artist [Tim Hildebrandt](../T/The_Brothers_Hildebrandt.md) dies.
- **[2009](2009.md)** - *[The Road Goes Ever On and On: The Map of Tolkien's Middle-earth](../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_and_On%253A_The_Map_of_Tolkien's_Middle-earth.md)* is published.
