Events that occurred on 11 June.

## On Earth {#On_Earth}

- **[1902](/wiki/1902)** - Illustrator [Eric Fraser](/wiki/Eric_Fraser) is born.
- **[1920](/wiki/1920)** - German translator [Walter Scherf](/wiki/Walter_Scherf) is born.
- **[1935](/wiki/1935)** – Guardian and "second father" of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) [Father Francis](/wiki/Francis_Xavier_Morgan "Francis Xavier Morgan") dies.
- **[1953](/wiki/1953)** - *[Pearl](</wiki/Pearl_(E.V._Gordon)> "Pearl (E.V. Gordon)")*, edited by [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon) and including a contribution by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), is published.
- **[1972](/wiki/1972)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Robert Burchfield](</wiki/Letter_to_Robert_Burchfield_(11_June_1972)> "Letter to Robert Burchfield (11 June 1972)").
- **[1975](/wiki/1975)** - Actor [John Tui](/wiki/John_Tui) is born.
- **[1977](/wiki/1977)** - The first [Wisconsin Ring Game](/wiki/Wisconsin_Ring_Game) is held at <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_Dodge_State_Park" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Governor Dodge State Park</a> in southern Wisconsin.
- **[2006](/wiki/2006)** - Artist [Tim Hildebrandt](/wiki/The_Brothers_Hildebrandt "The Brothers Hildebrandt") dies.
- **[2009](/wiki/2009)** - *[The Road Goes Ever On and On: The Map of Tolkien's Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_and_On:_The_Map_of_Tolkien%27s_Middle-earth)* is published.
