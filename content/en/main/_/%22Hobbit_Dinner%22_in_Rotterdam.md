The **Hobbit-maaltijd**<sup id="cite_ref-205b_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-205b-2">[2]</a></sup> refers to an event in which [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) participated on [28 March](/wiki/28_March) [1958](/wiki/1958). Tolkien had been invited to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotterdam" title="wikipedia:Rotterdam">Rotterdam</a> for a "Hobbit Dinner" by his Dutch publisher Het Spectrum and the bookseller Voorhoeve &amp; Dietrich.<sup id="cite_ref-Huff_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Huff-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Rene_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rene-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Background {#Background}

<h3><span id="The_.22Hobbit-maaltijd.22"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_&#34;Hobbit-maaltijd&#34;">The &#34;Hobbit-maaltijd&#34;</span></h3>

The dinner was held at an assembly hall, the Flevo-hall, part of a building called the Twaalf Provinciën Huis, that no longer exists. Author <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hella_Haasse" title="wikipedia:Hella Haasse">Hella Haasse</a> was one of the guests at the large dinner.<sup id="cite_ref-Rene_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rene-4">[4]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Tolkien.27s_speech"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Tolkien&#39;s_speech">Tolkien&#39;s speech</span></h3>

Tolkien delivered his brief speech in English and Dutch, in the middle of which is a poem "[Twenty years have flowed away down the long river](/wiki/Twenty_years_have_flowed_away_down_the_long_river)", in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and English. His finishing statement is quoted in [Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter "Humphrey Carpenter")'s *[Biography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography "J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography")*.<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [1993](/wiki/1993), an audio recording of the speech was discovered by [René van Rossenberg](/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_van_Rossenberg) (which remains unreleased), who later in [1995](/wiki/1995) published an article in *[Mythlore 80](/wiki/Mythlore_80)* detailing the events of that night. (<a class="external text" href="https://dc.swosu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=2162&amp;context=mythlore" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">see here</a>)

An excerpt of the recording appeared in an online article in [2014](/wiki/2014).<sup id="cite_ref-Huff_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Huff-3">[3]</a></sup>

Beside the oral deliverance Tolkien also prepared a written manuscript for the speech, which contains three versions of the Quenya poem. In [2024](/wiki/2024), part of the speech and the full texts of the poem are now published as entry 182 in *[The Collected Poems of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Collected_Poems_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)* (2024).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
