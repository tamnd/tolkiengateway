**59 St John Street**, [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md), was [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s undergraduate lodgings from [1914](1914.md) to [1915](1915.md) whilst he was studying at the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md). There he wrote a [poem about Eärendel](../T/The_Bidding_of_the_Minstrel.md) in the winter of 1914<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and months later [You and Me / and the Cottage of Lost Play\|other](../T/The_Little_House_of_Lost_Play%253A_Mar_Vanwa_Tyaliéva.md) about the [Cottage of Lost Play](../C/Cottage_of_Lost_Play.md), on [27](27_April.md)-[28 April](28_April.md), 1915.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
