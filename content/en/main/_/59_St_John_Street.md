**59 St John Street**, [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford), was [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s undergraduate lodgings from [1914](/wiki/1914) to [1915](/wiki/1915) whilst he was studying at the [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford). There he wrote a [poem about Eärendel](/wiki/The_Bidding_of_the_Minstrel "The Bidding of the Minstrel") in the winter of 1914<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and months later [You and Me / and the Cottage of Lost Play\|other](/wiki/The_Little_House_of_Lost_Play:_Mar_Vanwa_Tyali%C3%A9va "The Little House of Lost Play: Mar Vanwa Tyaliéva") about the [Cottage of Lost Play](/wiki/Cottage_of_Lost_Play), on [27](/wiki/27_April "27 April")-[28 April](/wiki/28_April), 1915.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
