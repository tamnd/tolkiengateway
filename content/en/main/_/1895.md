## April {#April}

- **?** - [Mabel Tolkien](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield "Mabel Suffield") boarded the SS Guelph and returned to [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham), England with her two children, [John](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") and [Hilary](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien "Hilary Tolkien"). [Arthur Tolkien](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien) stayed in South Africa with plans to join his family when time permits.

## June {#June}

- **[16](/wiki/16_June "16 June")** - [Warren "Warnie" Lewis](/wiki/Warren_%22Warnie%22_Lewis), [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings"), is born.
