## January {#January}

- **[1](1_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to a fan](../L/Letter_to_Mr_Polet.md).
- **[5](5_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 179c](../L/Letter_179c.md).
- **[5](5_January.md)** - The American edition of *The Return of the King* is published by the [Houghton Mifflin Company](../H/Houghton_Mifflin_Harcourt.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[5](5_January.md)** - [Doris Elizabeth Sykes](<../L/Letter_to_Doris_Elizabeth_Sykes_(28_January_1956).md>) sends illustrations to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- **[14](14_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [letter 180](../L/Letter_180.md).
- **[14](14_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Peter Alford](<../L/Letter_to_Peter_Alford_(14_January_1956).md>).
- **[22](22_January.md)** - The *New York Times Book Review* publishes [W.H. Auden](../W/W.H._Auden.md)'s review of *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)*: "[At the End of the Quest, Victory](../A/At_the_End_of_the_Quest,_Victory.md)".
- **[24](24_January.md)** - [Ted Nasmith](../T/Ted_Nasmith.md), Canadian artist and illustrator is born.
- **[28](28_January.md)** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Doris Elizabeth Sykes](<../L/Letter_to_Doris_Elizabeth_Sykes_(28_January_1956).md>).
- **?** - [Diana Glyer](../D/Diana_Glyer.md) is born.

## February {#February}

- **[5](5_February.md)** - Dan Wickenden gives a positive review of [The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md) in the *New York Herald Tribune* titled "Notable Allegorical Trilogy Comes to Triumphant End".
- **?Mid-February** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) writes [a letter to Peter Alford](<../L/Letter_to_Peter_Alford_(February_1956).md>).
