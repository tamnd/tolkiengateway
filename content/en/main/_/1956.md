[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956" title="wikipedia:1956">1956</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[1](/wiki/1_January "1 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to a fan](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Polet "Letter to Mr Polet").
- **[5](/wiki/5_January "5 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 179c](/wiki/Letter_179c "Letter 179c").
- **[5](/wiki/5_January "5 January")** - The American edition of *The Return of the King* is published by the [Houghton Mifflin Company](/wiki/Houghton_Mifflin_Harcourt "Houghton Mifflin Harcourt").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[5](/wiki/5_January "5 January")** - [Doris Elizabeth Sykes](</wiki/Letter_to_Doris_Elizabeth_Sykes_(28_January_1956)> "Letter to Doris Elizabeth Sykes (28 January 1956)") sends illustrations to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- **[14](/wiki/14_January "14 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 180](/wiki/Letter_180 "Letter 180").
- **[14](/wiki/14_January "14 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Peter Alford](</wiki/Letter_to_Peter_Alford_(14_January_1956)> "Letter to Peter Alford (14 January 1956)").
- **[22](/wiki/22_January "22 January")** - The *New York Times Book Review* publishes [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden)'s review of *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*: "[At the End of the Quest, Victory](/wiki/At_the_End_of_the_Quest,_Victory)".
- **[24](/wiki/24_January "24 January")** - [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith), Canadian artist and illustrator is born.
- **[28](/wiki/28_January "28 January")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Doris Elizabeth Sykes](</wiki/Letter_to_Doris_Elizabeth_Sykes_(28_January_1956)> "Letter to Doris Elizabeth Sykes (28 January 1956)").
- **?** - [Diana Glyer](/wiki/Diana_Glyer) is born.

## February {#February}

- **[5](/wiki/5_February "5 February")** - Dan Wickenden gives a positive review of [The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King) in the *New York Herald Tribune* titled "Notable Allegorical Trilogy Comes to Triumphant End".
- **?Mid-February** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Peter Alford](</wiki/Letter_to_Peter_Alford_(February_1956)> "Letter to Peter Alford (February 1956)").

## March {#March}

- **[2](/wiki/2_March "2 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) examines [Brian Miller](/wiki/Brian_Miller)'s thesis on the [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) *Dame Sirith*.
- **[7](/wiki/7_March "7 March")** - William Galbraith writes [a letter to J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Letter_to_William_Galbraith "Letter to William Galbraith").
- **[8](/wiki/8_March "8 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to William Galbraith](/wiki/Letter_to_William_Galbraith "Letter to William Galbraith").
- **[13](/wiki/13_March "13 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) receives a letter from a Sam Gamgee – from Tooting.
- **[18](/wiki/18_March "18 March")** - Tolkien writes a letter to a Mr Sam Gamgee ([letter 184](/wiki/Letter_184 "Letter 184")).
- **[19](/wiki/19_March "19 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 185](/wiki/Letter_185 "Letter 185").
- **[21](/wiki/21_March "21 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to E. Rasdall](/wiki/Letter_to_E._Rasdall "Letter to E. Rasdall").
- **[28](/wiki/28_March "28 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Patricia Kirke](</wiki/Letter_to_Patricia_Kirke_(28_March_1956)> "Letter to Patricia Kirke (28 March 1956)").

- **[3](/wiki/3_April "3 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 188a](/wiki/Letter_188a "Letter 188a").
- **[8](/wiki/8_April "8 April")** - [Jim Piddock](/wiki/Jim_Piddock), who would voice [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) in [Vivendi](/wiki/Vivendi)'s game *[The Fellowship of the Ring](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)")*, is born.

- **[12](/wiki/12_April "12 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to a Mr. Earle](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Earle "Letter to Mr Earle").
- **[28](/wiki/28_April "28 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Deirdre Levinson](</wiki/Letter_to_Deirdre_Levinson_(28_April_1956)> "Letter to Deirdre Levinson (28 April 1956)").

## June {#June}

- **[18](/wiki/18_June "18 June")** - [Dominic Guard](/wiki/Dominic_Guard), who would voice [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"), is born.

- **[16](/wiki/16_December "16 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Mr Britten](</wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Britten_(16_December_1956)> "Letter to Mr Britten (16 December 1956)").
