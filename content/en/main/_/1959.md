[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related events that took place in <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1959" title="wikipedia:1959">1959</a></b>.

## January {#January}

- **[10](/wiki/10_January "10 January")** - Screenwriter [Fran Walsh](/wiki/Fran_Walsh) is born.
- **[12](/wiki/12_January "12 January")** - [Simon Tolkien](/wiki/Simon_Tolkien) is born.

## February {#February}

- **[4](/wiki/4_February "4 February")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 214a](/wiki/Letter_214a "Letter 214a").
- **[5](/wiki/5_February "5 February")** - [Peter Emshwiller](/wiki/Peter_Emshwiller), who would provide the voice of [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II)*, is born.

## March {#March}

- **[4](/wiki/4_March "4 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 214b](/wiki/Letter_214b "Letter 214b").
- **[5](/wiki/5_March "5 March")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to William Ready](</wiki/Letter_to_William_Ready_(5_March_1959)> "Letter to William Ready (5 March 1959)").
- **[20](/wiki/20_March "20 March")** - Game designer [Anders Blixt](/wiki/Anders_Blixt) is born.

## April {#April}

- **[17](/wiki/17_April "17 April")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 215](/wiki/Letter_215 "Letter 215").
- **[17](/wiki/17_April "17 April")** - [Sean Bean](/wiki/Sean_Bean), who would play [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), is born.

## May {#May}

- **[26](/wiki/26_May "26 May")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Letter_to_Miss_Robinson&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Letter to Miss Robinson (page does not exist)">a letter to Miss Robinson</a>.
- **[30](/wiki/30_May "30 May")** - Swedish-French director [Charlotte Brändström](/wiki/Charlotte_Br%C3%A4ndstr%C3%B6m) is born.

## June {#June}

- **[5](/wiki/5_June "5 June")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) delivers the [Valedictory Address to the University of Oxford](/wiki/Valedictory_Address_to_the_University_of_Oxford).

## July {#July}

- **[16](/wiki/16_July "16 July")** - [Bob Joles](/wiki/Bob_Joles), who would voice [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) and [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot) in various [EA](/wiki/Electronic_Arts "Electronic Arts") video games, is born.

## August {#August}

- **[1](/wiki/1_August "1 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) serves the first Mass of [Robert Murray](/wiki/Robert_Murray).
- **[4](/wiki/4_August "4 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 215a](/wiki/Letter_215a "Letter 215a").
- **[12](/wiki/12_August "12 August")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 216](/wiki/Letter_216 "Letter 216").

## September {#September}

- **[10](/wiki/10_September "10 September")** - Actor [Jim Meskimen](/wiki/Jim_Meskimen) is born.
- **[11](/wiki/11_September "11 September")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [letter 217](/wiki/Letter_217 "Letter 217").

## October {#October}

- **[9](/wiki/9_October "9 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 218](/wiki/Letter_218 "Letter 218").
- **[15](/wiki/15_October "15 October")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 220](/wiki/Letter_220 "Letter 220").
- **[16](/wiki/16_October "16 October")** - J.R.R. Tolkien writes [letter 219](/wiki/Letter_219 "Letter 219").

## November {#November}

- **[2](/wiki/2_November "2 November")** - Actor [Peter Mullan](/wiki/Peter_Mullan) is born.
- **[8](/wiki/8_November "8 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [Letter 220a](/wiki/Letter_220a).
- **[24](/wiki/24_November "24 November")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 221](/wiki/Letter_221 "Letter 221").
- **?'** - The poem "<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Passing_of_the_Elven-kind&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Passing of the Elven-kind (page does not exist)">The Passing of the Elven-kind</a>" by Ted Johnstone, the oldest known published [Tolkien fan faction](/wiki/Fanfiction "Fanfiction"), is published.

## December {#December}

- **[9](/wiki/9_December "9 December")** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [letter 222](/wiki/Letter_222 "Letter 222").
- **[29](/wiki/29_December "29 December")** - [Brian Sergent](/wiki/Brian_Sergent), who would play [Ted Sandyman](/wiki/Ted_Sandyman) in the extended edition of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, is born.
- **?** - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) writes [a letter to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski](</wiki/Letter_to_Przemyslaw_Mroczkowski_(December_1959)> "Letter to Przemyslaw Mroczkowski (December 1959)").
