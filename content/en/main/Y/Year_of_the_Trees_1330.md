**Year of the Trees 1330** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1330**) is the 1330th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1330 was also **V.Y. 4830** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) first seen in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), according to the annals that were made in Doriath.<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) hires [Dwarven](../D/Dwarves.md) smiths - including [Telchar](../T/Telchar.md) of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) - to forge weapons for the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md). Thingol's armoury is now full of spears, axes, helms and armour.<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup>
