<i><b>yanta</b></i> is a noun meaning "bridge" or "yoke" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Yanta.gif"><img src="/w/images/2/22/Yanta.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Yanta* is the name in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) of the thirty-fifth letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> In most modes it is used as a consonant and represents **Y**.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> However, in the [Mode of Beleriand](/wiki/Mode_of_Beleriand), as seen as in the inscription on the [West-gate](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin "Doors of Durin") of [Moria](/wiki/Moria), *Yanta* represents **E**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Ai</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<br>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=YAT&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="YAT (page does not exist)">YAT</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
