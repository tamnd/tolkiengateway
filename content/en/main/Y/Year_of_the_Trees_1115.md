**Year of the Trees 1115** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1115**) is the 1115th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1115 was also **V.Y. 4615** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) pass through [a great forest](../M/Mirkwood.md) and encounter the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) and the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md). [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) leads the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) and the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) across the river and the [Towers of Mist](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) but the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) are unwilling.<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Some of the Teleri are led southwards along the Great River by [Lenwë](../L/Lenwë.md) and later become known as the [Nandor](../N/Nandor.md). This is the Second [Sundering of the Elves](../S/Sundering_of_the_Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
