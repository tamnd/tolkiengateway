The **Younger Children of Ilúvatar** were [Men](/wiki/Men), who awoke many thousands of years after the Elder Children, the [Elves](/wiki/Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) the Elf [Gwindor](/wiki/Gwindor) rebuked [Finduilas](/wiki/Finduilas) for weeping, stating that it was not fit for the [Elder Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Elder_Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar) to wed the Younger.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
