The **Younger Children of Ilúvatar** were [Men](../M/Men.md), who awoke many thousands of years after the Elder Children, the [Elves](../E/Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) the Elf [Gwindor](../G/Gwindor.md) rebuked [Finduilas](../F/Finduilas.md) for weeping, stating that it was not fit for the [Elder Children of Ilúvatar](../E/Elder_Children_of_Ilúvatar.md) to wed the Younger.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
