**Year of the Trees 1140** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1140**) is the 1140th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1140 was also **V.Y. 4640** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Tirion](../T/Tirion.md) is full-wrought and the tower of [Ingwë](../I/Ingwë.md) - [Mindon Eldaliéva](../M/Mindon_Eldaliéva.md) - is built. However, many of the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) yearn for the light of the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md) and move to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) and live amongst the [Valar](../V/Valar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
