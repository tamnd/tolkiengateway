The **Yavannildi** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) *Ivonwin*) were the [elven-women](../E/Elves.md) that knew and kept the secrets to making [lembas](../L/Lembas.md), an art learned from the [Valar](../V/Valar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-oflembas_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oflembas-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

According to [Pengolodh](../P/Pengolodh.md), only the Yavannildi knew the process of making lembas and were allowed to handle the grain from the ear of the corn that would become the wafer. However, as the grain came from Yavanna, only the [Massánië](../M/Massánië.md), or the highest among the elven-women, could keep and distribute the lembas.<sup id="cite_ref-oflembas_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oflembas-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is said that "in Elvish legend", lembas was "a gift from [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) and [Varda](../V/Varda.md)" for the [Great Journey](../G/Great_Journey.md) that was brought to the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) by [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md), who taught the art to the **Bread-women** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) *hávanissi*) by [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) , though Bread-women was possibly just another name for the Yavannildi.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Yavannildi* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "Maidens of [Yavanna](Yavanna.md)", from *[nildë](../N/Nilmo.md)* ("friend").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) form *Ivonwin* means the same, from *[Ivon](../I/Ivon.md)* (the Sindarin name for [Yavanna](Yavanna.md)) + [lenited](../L/Lenition.md) plural *[gwend](../G/Gwend.md)* ("maiden").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the second text of the chapter "The Making of *Lembas*" in *[The Nature of Middle-earth](../T/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth.md)*, Oromë brought the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Western_Corn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Western Corn (page does not exist)">Western Corn</a> seed that was used in the making of lembas rather than the bread itself. In the text, he taught the art to the **[Three Elderwomen of the Elves](../T/Three_Elderwomen_of_the_Elves.md)**, rather than the Yavannildi. Due to the dimmed sunlight,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> the Western Corn slowly diminished until the Elves had none upon entering [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). However, the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) [brought](../R/Return_of_the_Noldor.md) a new batch of Western Corn during their [flight](../E/Exile_of_the_Noldor.md) from [Aman](../A/Aman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>
