**Year of the Trees 1149** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1149**) is the 1149th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1149 was also **V.Y. 4649** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md) bids [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) to bring the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md); however [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md) is grieved and persuades some to stay.<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) establishes the havens of [Brithombar](../B/Brithombar.md) and [Eglarest](../E/Eglarest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
