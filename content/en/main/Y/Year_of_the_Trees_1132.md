**Year of the Trees 1132** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1132**) is the 1132nd [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1132 was also **V.Y. 4632** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) moves the isle of [Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) to the [Bay of Balar](../B/Bay_of_Balar.md). Ulmo summons the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) to embark on the isle and leave for [Aman](../A/Aman.md); only the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) and the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) hear the summons and they leave [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) without the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Part of the island breaks off and remains in the Bay, becoming the [Isle of Balar](../I/Isle_of_Balar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md), in the east of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), search for their lord [Elwë](../T/Thingol.md). Unsuccessful - and discovering that [Ingwë](../I/Ingwë.md) and [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md) had already departed - they make Elwë's brother [Olwë](../O/Olwë.md) their lord. They push onto the shores and are befriended by [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md) and [Uinen](../U/Uinen.md) and become lovers of the seas.<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
