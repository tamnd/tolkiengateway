**Yávien** was the only daughter and oldest child of [Nolondil](../N/Nolondil.md), the brother of [Tar-Amandil](../T/Tar-Amandil.md), [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md). Almost nothing is known of her life.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Yávien* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it is a compound of *[yávë](Yávë.md)* ("fruit") and the feminine suffix *[ien](../I/Ien.md)* (feminine ending, feminine patronymic, "-daughter").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
