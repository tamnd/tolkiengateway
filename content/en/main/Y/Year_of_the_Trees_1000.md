**Year of the Trees 1000** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1000**) is the 1000th [year of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") of [Arda](/wiki/Arda). Year of the Trees 1000 was also **V.Y. 4500** in [Valian Years](/wiki/Valian_Years).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Valar](/wiki/Valar) hold council, for [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) and [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) report that [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") might rise once more in power. They discuss the forthcomingness of the [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar) and whether to bring light to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Over the next fifty years, [Varda](/wiki/Varda) uses the light from [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion) to fashion new stars, and organize them (and those already wrought) into constellations. The greatest of these constellations was the [Menelmacar](/wiki/Menelmacar).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
- About some time before the [Awakening](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves "Awakening of the Elves") of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) secretly creates the 7 [Fathers](/wiki/Fathers_of_the_Dwarves "Fathers of the Dwarves") of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) and their 6 spouses. [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) adopts them and they are put to sleep.<sup id="cite_ref-Dwarves_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwarves-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other version of the legendarium {#Other_version_of_the_legendarium}

- An alternate date for the [Awakening](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves "Awakening of the Elves") of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), the Firstborn [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar), at the shores of [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen), according to the [Round World version](/wiki/Round_World_version_of_the_legendarium "Round World version of the legendarium") of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>
- The [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
