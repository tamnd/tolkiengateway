**Year of the Trees 1000** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1000**) is the 1000th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1000 was also **V.Y. 4500** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Valar](../V/Valar.md) hold council, for [Yavanna](Yavanna.md) and [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) report that [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) might rise once more in power. They discuss the forthcomingness of the [Children of Ilúvatar](../C/Children_of_Ilúvatar.md) and whether to bring light to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Over the next fifty years, [Varda](../V/Varda.md) uses the light from [Telperion](../T/Telperion.md) to fashion new stars, and organize them (and those already wrought) into constellations. The greatest of these constellations was the [Menelmacar](../M/Menelmacar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
- About some time before the [Awakening](../A/Awakening_of_the_Elves.md) of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md), [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) secretly creates the 7 [Fathers](../F/Fathers_of_the_Dwarves.md) of the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) and their 6 spouses. [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md) adopts them and they are put to sleep.<sup id="cite_ref-Dwarves_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwarves-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other version of the legendarium {#Other_version_of_the_legendarium}

- An alternate date for the [Awakening](../A/Awakening_of_the_Elves.md) of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md), the Firstborn [Children of Ilúvatar](../C/Children_of_Ilúvatar.md), at the shores of [Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md), according to the [Round World version](../R/Round_World_version_of_the_legendarium.md) of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>
- The [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
