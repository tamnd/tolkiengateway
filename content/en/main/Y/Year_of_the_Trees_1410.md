**Year of the Trees 1410** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1410**) is the 1410th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1410 was also **V.Y. 4910** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) is allowed to move freely around [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). He starts feigning friendship with the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) and spreading lies amongst the [Elves](../E/Elves.md); the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) are wary of him, but the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) are eager to receive his knowledge.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
