<i><b>yulda</b></i>, pl. <i><b>yuldar</b></i>, is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "drink" or "draught".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><b>yulda</b></i> comes from the [root](../R/Root.md) YUL (*drink*)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and the suffix [-da](../_/-da.md) (*the passive product of an action*).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
