**Year of the Trees 1** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1**) is the 1st [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1 was also **V.Y. 3501** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- After [Yavanna](Yavanna.md) grew the [Two Trees of Valinor](../T/Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md) on the hilltop [Ezellohar](../E/Ezellohar.md) to give a [new light](../L/Light_of_Valinor.md) to Arda, [Telperion](../T/Telperion.md) blooms alone at the [Opening Hour](../O/Opening_Hour.md); on the second hour, the [First Day](../F/First_Day.md) comes, and the [Count of Time](../T/Time.md) begins with it.<sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- For one thousand Years of the Trees the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) dwell in bliss in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) beyond the [Mountains of Aman](../P/Pelóri.md), while all [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) lay in a [twilight](../T/Twilight.md) under the [Stars](../S/Stars.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Annals_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Over the next many ages [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) sleeps in [Utumno](../U/Utumno.md), and [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) hunts down the Enemy's monstrosities throughout Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Annals_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-3">[3]</a></sup> Melkor raises the [Hithaeglir](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) in an attempt to hinder his efforts.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
