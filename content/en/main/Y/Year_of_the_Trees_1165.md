**Year of the Trees 1165** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1165**) is the 1165th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1165 was also **V.Y. 4665** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The last of the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) depart from [Tirion](../T/Tirion.md). The [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) live in Tirion alone and their friendships are with the [Teleri](../F/Falmari.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
