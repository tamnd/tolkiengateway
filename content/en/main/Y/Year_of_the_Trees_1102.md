**Year of the Trees 1102** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1102**) is the 1102nd [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1102 was also **V.Y. 4602** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Elves](../E/Elves.md) are unwilling to heed the summons of [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) and leave [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) for [Aman](../A/Aman.md). Therefore, Oromë invites three chieftains to join him on a visit to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md): [Ingwë](../I/Ingwë.md), [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md) and [Elwë](../T/Thingol.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
- After speaking to the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and seeing the light of the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md), the three chieftains see the awe and splendour of Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
