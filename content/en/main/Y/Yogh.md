**Yogh** (**Ȝ ȝ**) is a letter of Middle English literature. Note that sometimes it is written with a three (3) when the font is not available.

The Middle English sound *Ȝ* was derived from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *g*. It was a sound that doesn't exist any more in English language since it is now assimilated with *y* or isn't pronounced at all.

## Ȝ in Elvish {#Ȝ_in_Elvish}

### Primitive Quendian {#Primitive_Quendian}

Tolkien used *Ȝ* in *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)* to describe the sound *gh*. The *Ȝ* is seen most often in [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [Roots](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") such as MAȜ, KUȜ, TEȜ. This sound however was assimilated to other sounds.

Before unvoiced consonants it would evolve into *ch*: *maȝtâ* (MAȜ) evolved to [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) *machtâ*.

Final, after *u*, it would evolve into *w*: *kuȝ* (KUȜ) &gt; *kuw*

### Later Ȝ {#Later_Ȝ}

The sound *gh* would return once more in [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish). Like in the case of English, Primitive Quendian *g* was simplified to *gh* in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), in most cases). For example the word *galadâ* "tree" evolved into \**ghaldâ/ȝaldâ* in the Quenyan branch.

Once more, the *gh* sound could be heard in Elvish, and it survived long enough to be written in [Sarati](/wiki/Sarati) before disappearing again. The word *[alda](/wiki/Alda "Alda")* was still spelt *ȝalda* with the letter <em style="font: normal 1.7em/1.5em &#39;Tirion Sarati&#39;">&#39;</em> representing *gh*. It was known by tradition among the [loremasters](/wiki/Loremasters "Loremasters")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

When *gh* was lost once more, the Sarat <em style="font: normal 1.7em/1.5em &#39;Tirion Sarati&#39;">&#39;</em> was eventually used for a carrier. It was inherited as a carrier in the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) and later was replaced by the letter [Anna](/wiki/Anna), to indicate an assuming “vanished” *gh* wherever words begun with a vowel.

This usage tried to explain the relation of words between Noldorin words starting with a vowel where Telerin had *g-* (cf. Quenya *[alda](/wiki/Alda "Alda")* vs. [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) *galla*), but did not explain all the cases of words without an initial consonant (*alca*, according to this, should be spelled \*<span class="tg-tengwar tg-tengwar--tengwar_eldamar">hDjaE</span><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395196">@font-face{font-family:"TemplateStyles-TengwarEldamar";src:local("Tengwar Eldarmar"),url("https://tolkiengateway.net/w/resources/assets/fonts/tengwar-eldamar.woff2")format("woff2");font-style:normal;font-weight:normal}.mw-parser-output .tg-tengwar--tengwar_eldamar{font-family:"TemplateStyles-TengwarEldamar"}</style> that time, although it had never been <i>**galka</i> before).

## Other languages {#Other_languages}

Note that in other languages, like [Orkish](/wiki/Orkish), Tolkien wrote this sound as *gh*.

In [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar), *ȝ/gh* is written with the letter [Anca](/wiki/Anca) or Unquë.
