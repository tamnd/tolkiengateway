**Yulemath** was the last month of the calendars of [Bree](/wiki/Bree) and [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing), corresponding to [Foreyule](/wiki/Foreyule) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar). It was the thirty days immediately preceding the holiday of [Yule](/wiki/Yule), approximately corresponding to modern [21 November](/wiki/21_November) through [20 December](/wiki/20_December).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
