<i><b>yúyo</b></i> (<i><b>yú-</b></i>) is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) adjetive which translates to "both".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In the only example we have, the noun following *yúyo* receives no plural or dual marker.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It's unclear if it also could be used as a noun or an adverb.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) YŪ- ("two, both")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Example {#Example}

- <i>mahtanë <b>yúyo</b> <a href="../M/Má.md" title="Má">má</a> véla</i> = "wield both hands alike"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
