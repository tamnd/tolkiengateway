**Year of the Trees 1075** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1075**) is the 1075th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1075 was also **V.Y. 4575** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- An alternate date for the [Awakening](../A/Awakening_of_Men.md) of [Men](../M/Men.md), the Secondborn [Children of Ilúvatar](../C/Children_of_Ilúvatar.md), in [Hildórien](../H/Hildórien.md), according to the [Round World version](../R/Round_World_version_of_the_legendarium.md) of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
