**Year of the Trees 1075** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1075**) is the 1075th [year of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Arda](/wiki/Arda). Year of the Trees 1075 was also **V.Y. 4575** in [Valian Years](/wiki/Valian_Years).

Notable events in this year include:

- An alternate date for the [Awakening](/wiki/Awakening_of_Men "Awakening of Men") of [Men](/wiki/Men), the Secondborn [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar), in [Hildórien](/wiki/Hild%C3%B3rien), according to the [Round World version](/wiki/Round_World_version_of_the_legendarium "Round World version of the legendarium") of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
