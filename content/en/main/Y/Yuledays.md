The **Yuledays** or **Yule-tide** were the midwinter festival days that marked the end of one year and the beginning of the next. They were celebrated in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>, but also by the [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen) of [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor); the word "Yule" was recognisable in Gondor as a 'northern name' for the midwinter festival.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the [Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Five_Armies), [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn) summoned many Northmern to [his home](/wiki/Beorn%27s_Hall "Beorn's Hall") to feast. [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo) and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) were also there, on their way to the Shire and passed the Yule-tide ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")—[2942](/wiki/Third_Age_2942 "Third Age 2942")).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) there was a six-day Yuletide festival ([29 Foreyule](/wiki/29_December "29 December") to [2 Afteryule](/wiki/2_January "2 January")); at their center were the two [Yule](/wiki/Yule)-days that fell outside the formal 'months' of the calendar, between [Foreyule](/wiki/Foreyule) and [Afteryule](/wiki/Afteryule).<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>
