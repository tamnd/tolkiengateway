The **Yuledays** or **Yule-tide** were the midwinter festival days that marked the end of one year and the beginning of the next. They were celebrated in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md)<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>, but also by the [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md) of [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md); the word "Yule" was recognisable in Gondor as a 'northern name' for the midwinter festival.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the [Battle of the Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md), [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md) summoned many Northmern to [his home](../B/Beorn's_Hall.md) to feast. [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo.md) and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) were also there, on their way to the Shire and passed the Yule-tide ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md)—[2942](../T/Third_Age_2942.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) there was a six-day Yuletide festival ([29 Foreyule](../_/29_December.md) to [2 Afteryule](../_/2_January.md)); at their center were the two [Yule](Yule.md)-days that fell outside the formal 'months' of the calendar, between [Foreyule](../F/Foreyule.md) and [Afteryule](../A/Afteryule.md).<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>
