**Year of the Trees 1479** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1479**) is the 1479th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1479 was also **V.Y. 4979** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- *Possible* birth of [Idril](../I/Idril.md) in [Aman](../A/Aman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
