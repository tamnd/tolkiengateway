**Year of the Trees 1280** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1280**) is the 1280th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1280 was also **V.Y. 4780** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Marriage of [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md) and [Eärwen](../E/Eärwen.md). A great feast is held in the land of the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
