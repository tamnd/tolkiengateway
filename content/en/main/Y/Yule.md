**Yule** was the [Northern Mannish](../N/Northern_Mannish.md) name of the midwinter festival, observed around the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md). The days around Yule were the Yule-tide.

In the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md), Yule referred to two days (the last of the previous and the first of the new year). Due to a peculiarity of the Calendar, the 2 Yuledays were "between" and outside the formal months of [Foreyule](../F/Foreyule.md) and [Afteryule](../A/Afteryule.md). They always fell on the same days of the week: the last day of the year, [1 Yule](../_/1_Yule.md), was always a [Highday](../H/Highday.md) (Friday), while the first day of the following year, [2 Yule](../_/2_Yule.md), was always a [Sterday](../S/Sterday.md) (Saturday).<sup id="cite_ref-Shire_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shire-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

Around them, the six-day festival of [Yuletide](Yuledays.md) was held, running from [29](../_/29_December.md) [Foreyule](../F/Foreyule.md) through [2](../_/2_January.md) [Afteryule](../A/Afteryule.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

Yule represents a "translation" of a [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) word, but the word itself doesn't occur in [Westron](../W/Westron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-4">[3]</a></sup>

However it's possible that it derives from a form of [Northern Mannish](../N/Northern_Mannish.md) and later used in [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md). Since [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md) of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) were in part [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md), the word was recognisable as a 'northern name' for the midwinter festival.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-4">[3]</a></sup>

A [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word for "Yule and the beginning of [Sun](../S/Sun.md)'s return" was <i><b>Amanar</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md), one of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s early conceptions of an [Elven](../E/Elvish.md) language, the word for "Yule(night), i.e. Log-night" is *durufui*. *Tanfui* means "Yule night".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
