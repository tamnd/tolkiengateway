**Year of the Trees 1495** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1495**) is the 1495th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1495 was also **V.Y. 4995** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) and [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md) cross the [Pelóri](../P/Pelóri.md). They go to [the Green Mound](../E/Ezellohar.md) and destroy the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md). [Aman](../A/Aman.md) is covered in darkness,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-2">[2]</a></sup> thus beginning the [Long Night](../L/Long_Night.md).
- Melkor goes to [Formenos](../F/Formenos.md), kills [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md) and steals the [Silmarils](../S/Silmarils.md). Finwë's son, [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md), names Melkor "[Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-S_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- After his [father's](../F/Finwë.md) death, [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) becomes the [King of the Noldor](../K/King_of_the_Noldor.md) though this claim was not acknowledged by all the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md); many took [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) as their lord immediately after the loss of [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-S_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Morgoth and Ungoliant cross the [Pelóri](../P/Pelóri.md), pass through [Araman](../A/Araman.md), and go over the [Helcaraxë](../H/Helcaraxë.md) to the north of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md) demands the [Silmarils](../S/Silmarils.md) and attacks Morgoth; the [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md) come to Morgoth's aid and Ungoliant flees to [Nan Dungortheb](../N/Nan_Dungortheb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Morgoth goes to [Angband](../A/Angband.md), rears up [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md), and fills his fortress with [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md). He affixes the Silmarils to his [Iron Crown](../I/Iron_Crown.md) and declares himself King of the World.<sup id="cite_ref-S_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Fëanor stirs the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) to leave [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) and seek revenge - Fëanor and [his sons](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) swear [an Oath](../O/Oath_of_Fëanor.md) pledging to reclaim the Silmarils. The [Flight of the Noldor](../F/Flight_of_the_Noldor.md) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-S_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Flight_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Fëanor goes to [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md) to persuade the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) to join the Noldor in exile. The Teleri refuse so the Noldor engage the Teleri in battle and steal their ships in the [First Kinslaying](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-4">[4]</a></sup>
