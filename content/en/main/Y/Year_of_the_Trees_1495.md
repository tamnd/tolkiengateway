**Year of the Trees 1495** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1495**) is the 1495th [year of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Arda](/wiki/Arda). Year of the Trees 1495 was also **V.Y. 4995** in [Valian Years](/wiki/Valian_Years).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") and [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) cross the [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri). They go to [the Green Mound](/wiki/Ezellohar "Ezellohar") and destroy the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees). [Aman](/wiki/Aman) is covered in darkness,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-2">[2]</a></sup> thus beginning the [Long Night](/wiki/Long_Night).
- Melkor goes to [Formenos](/wiki/Formenos), kills [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB) and steals the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils). Finwë's son, [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), names Melkor "[Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)".<sup id="cite_ref-S_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- After his [father's](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB "Finwë") death, [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) becomes the [King of the Noldor](/wiki/King_of_the_Noldor) though this claim was not acknowledged by all the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor); many took [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) as their lord immediately after the loss of [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-S_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Morgoth and Ungoliant cross the [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri), pass through [Araman](/wiki/Araman), and go over the [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB) to the north of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) demands the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils) and attacks Morgoth; the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) come to Morgoth's aid and Ungoliant flees to [Nan Dungortheb](/wiki/Nan_Dungortheb).<sup id="cite_ref-AA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Morgoth goes to [Angband](/wiki/Angband), rears up [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim), and fills his fortress with [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). He affixes the Silmarils to his [Iron Crown](/wiki/Iron_Crown) and declares himself King of the World.<sup id="cite_ref-S_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Fëanor stirs the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) to leave [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) and seek revenge - Fëanor and [his sons](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor") swear [an Oath](/wiki/Oath_of_F%C3%ABanor "Oath of Fëanor") pledging to reclaim the Silmarils. The [Flight of the Noldor](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Noldor) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-S_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Flight_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Fëanor goes to [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB) to persuade the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) to join the Noldor in exile. The Teleri refuse so the Noldor engage the Teleri in battle and steal their ships in the [First Kinslaying](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB "Kinslaying at Alqualondë").<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-4">[4]</a></sup>
