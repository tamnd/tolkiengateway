**Yazneg** was a [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") who was the initial lieutenant of [Azog](/wiki/Azog)'s pack. Yazneg was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for [*The Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), where he was portrayed by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Rawls_(actor)" title="wikipedia:John Rawls (actor)">John Rawls</a>.

## History {#History}

After spotting the Company of [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) camping out in the [Lone-lands](/wiki/Lone-lands), Yazneg sent [Fimbul](/wiki/Fimbul) to inform Azog that they found Thorin and his Company. Later, the Orc troop catches up with Thorin near [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), but are distracted by [Radagast](/wiki/Radagast) and his rabbit sled. However, during the chase, one of the hunters spotted where Thorin and his Company were hiding. The orcs then encircle the dwarves and attempt to attack them, but are forced to retreat due to an unexpected attack by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and his [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") forces. Yazneg and Fimbul return empty-handed to Weathertop, where Azog ultimately throws Yazneg to the pack's [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs) to feast upon, as an example for the rest not to fail him.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Yazneg is not a name created by Tolkien and its derivation isn't explained within the films. However [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo), who worked as a linguistic consultant for [*The Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), provides an Orkish wordlist on his blog which glosses *yaz* as "name" and *-neg* as "without".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

Yazneg's appearance was derived from early concept designs for Azog in the film that were never used.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
