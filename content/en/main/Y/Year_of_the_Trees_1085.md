**Year of the Trees 1085** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1085**) is the 1085th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1085 was also **V.Y. 4585** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) discovers the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) near the shores of [Helcar](../S/Sea_of_Helcar.md); he names them the Eldar, the People of the Stars. As a result of [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md)'s spies and whispers, some Eldar are afraid of Oromë and flee as he approaches.<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
- About this time, it is possible that the first [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) are created, being corrupted from Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
