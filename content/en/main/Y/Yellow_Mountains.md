The **Yellow Mountains** were a range of mountains to the south-east of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) within the [Southlands](/wiki/Dark_Land "Dark Land"), a southern continuation of the [Red Mountains](/wiki/Orocarni "Orocarni"). Both ranges were created when the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) thrust the centre of Middle-earth to the east as extra protection for [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [Ambarkanta maps](/wiki/Ambarkanta_maps), the Yellow Mountains are not shown on Map V, labelled *"After the [War of the Gods](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers "Battle of the Powers")"*.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1989: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="/wiki/Shadow_in_the_South" title="Shadow in the South">Shadow in the South</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>The Yellow Mountains (<a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Orolanari</i>, <a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Ered Laranor</i>) were volcanic mountains with black and yellow landforms. They were said to rise to heights of up to 10,000 feet. When <a href="/wiki/Ormal" title="Ormal">Ormal</a> fell, the mountains were swept to the south and east, forming the spine of the utter South.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The mountains&#39; caverns and caves were home to the Mablâd, local <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a> who named themselves after the earth&#39;s steam (Mabûl). They guarded the wealth of the mountains: <a href="/wiki/Silver" title="Silver">silver</a>, <a href="/wiki/Stones_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing#Laen" title="Stones in Middle-earth Role Playing">laen</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Metals_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing#Eog" title="Metals in Middle-earth Role Playing">eog</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-south_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-2">[2]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>There were five notable passes through the Yellow Mountains:</dd></dl>

1. The Elornan Cut, which was the safe northernmost pass, located near the headwaters of the river Sireshan.
2. The Pel Stairs, a fog-covered defile prone to rockfalls, situated between Mirëdor and Pel.
3. Trenth Walk, also known as the Pass of Ages, which was a safe hewn trail between Alsarias and Trenth, passing along the Mablâd stronghold Blackfame. Passage required a toll to be paid.
4. Cirith Celiant (S. “Silverbridge Pass”, Pel *Fâdaraja Cupita*) which followed the river Celiant.
5. The Aeluin Crossing, the southernmost route, which actually cut between the mountains and high foothills.<sup id="cite_ref-south_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-2">[2]</a></sup>
