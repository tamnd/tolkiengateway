**YAN** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "vast, huge"; YAN and **YAD** are also given the signification "wide". The root also appears in the forms YANA, YĂN.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *yāna, yanda*<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *iaun* ("roomy, wide, extensive"); [*-iann*, *-ion*](/wiki/-nd "-nd"); *iand*<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root DẎṆTṆ, with the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) derivative *yanta-* ("enlarge, increase, add to").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
