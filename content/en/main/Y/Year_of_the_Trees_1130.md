**Year of the Trees 1130** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1130**) is the 1130th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1130 was also **V.Y. 4630** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Elwë](../T/Thingol.md), returning from a meeting with [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md), meets [Melian](../M/Melian.md) in [Nan Elmoth](../N/Nan_Elmoth.md) and falls into a trance.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
