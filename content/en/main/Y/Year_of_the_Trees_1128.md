**Year of the Trees 1128** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1128**) is the 1128th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1128 was also **V.Y. 4628** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Being led by [Elwë](../T/Thingol.md), the host of the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) come at last to [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). They dwell for a time in [the region east of](../O/Ossiriand.md) the river [Gelion](../G/Gelion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
