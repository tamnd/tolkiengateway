**Year of the Trees 1500** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1500**) is the 1500th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1500 was also **V.Y. 5000** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Valar](../V/Valar.md) complete the creation of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) and the [Moon](../M/Moon.md), which will be guided by the [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md) [Arien](../A/Arien.md) and [Tilion](../T/Tilion.md), respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-Narsilion_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narsilion-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The Valar put great towers and sentinels around the [Calacirya](../C/Calacirya.md). From now, Valinor became [hidden](../H/Hiding_of_Valinor.md) with the [Enchanted Isles](../E/Enchanted_Isles.md) set about [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Narsilion_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narsilion-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- The Moon rises above the World for the first time; Tilion traverses the heavens seven times before the coming of the Sun.<sup id="cite_ref-Narsilion_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narsilion-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- At the first moonrise,<sup id="cite_ref-Narsilion_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narsilion-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-2">[2]</a></sup> the host of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) set foot upon [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) after crossing the [Helcaraxë](../H/Helcaraxë.md) with grievous losses.<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Helcaraxe_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helcaraxe-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
    - Death of [Turgon's](../T/Turgon.md) wife and [Idril's](../I/Idril.md) mother [Elenwë](../E/Elenwë.md) in the icy waters of Helcaraxë.<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Helcaraxe_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helcaraxe-5">[5]</a></sup>
- [Battle of the Lammoth](../B/Battle_of_the_Lammoth.md) is fought and Fingolfin's youngest son [Argon](../A/Argon.md) is slain.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
