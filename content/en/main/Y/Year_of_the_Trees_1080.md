**Year of the Trees 1080** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1080**) is the 1080th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1080 was also **V.Y. 4580** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- About this time the spies of [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) discover the [Quendi](../Q/Quendi.md) and afflict them.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The following years, elven legends tell about evil [spirits](../S/Spirits.md) such as the [Hunter and the Rider](../H/Hunter_and_Rider.md) who harass the Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
