The **Years of the Trees**, also called the **Days of Bliss (of Valinor)** were the long years that lay between the founding of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) and its [Darkening](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor "Darkening of Valinor"). During this time, Valinor was lit by the light of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor"), but [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) lay in the [Great Darkness](/wiki/Great_Darkness).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

For the most of its part, the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) was reckoned in the Years of the Trees,<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:51</sup><sup id="cite_ref-Nature_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nature-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:123</sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> which were [later](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor "Darkening of Valinor") replaced by much shorter [Years of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun).<sup id="cite_ref-Atani_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Atani-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:HelenKei_-_Trees_of_Valinor.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/7b/HelenKei_-_Trees_of_Valinor.jpg/300px-HelenKei_-_Trees_of_Valinor.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="176" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/7/7b/HelenKei_-_Trees_of_Valinor.jpg/450px-HelenKei_-_Trees_of_Valinor.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/7/7b/HelenKei_-_Trees_of_Valinor.jpg/600px-HelenKei_-_Trees_of_Valinor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="602"/></a><figcaption><i>Trees of Valinor</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_HelenKei" title="Category:Images by HelenKei">HelenKei</a></figcaption></figure>

Long before the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) awoke, [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) created the Two Trees of Valinor to give light to realm of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar). The Trees first flowered after 3500 Valian Years (i.e. nearly 35,000 ordinary years) had passed.<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:51</sup> This period began at the [Opening Hour](/wiki/Opening_Hour), when [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion) first started to bloom alone; on the second hour, the [First Day](/wiki/First_Day) begun, and with it, the beginning of the [Count of Time](/wiki/Time "Time").<sup id="cite_ref-BegDays_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BegDays-8">[8]</a></sup>

So began a reign of peace in Valinor, but Middle-earth was lit by starlight alone, and [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") worked in the depths of [Utumno](/wiki/Utumno) in the north of the World and its dominion expanded to the south, while [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) was appointed lieutenant in [Angband](/wiki/Angband), anticipating an assault from the West.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> It is not told how long this time lasted, just that 'the ages drew on'; this period would have lasted roughly 10,500 years. It was during this period that [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) made the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), and at [Eru](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar "Ilúvatar")'s direction, set them to sleep until the creation of the [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars") and the [Awakening of the Elves](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves). The latter event marked the beginning of the First Age.<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:51</sup><sup id="cite_ref-Nature_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nature-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:123</sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Orom%C3%AB_espies_the_first_Elves.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/7d/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Orom%C3%AB_espies_the_first_Elves.jpg/300px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Orom%C3%AB_espies_the_first_Elves.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="168" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/7/7d/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Orom%C3%AB_espies_the_first_Elves.jpg/450px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Orom%C3%AB_espies_the_first_Elves.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/7/7d/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Orom%C3%AB_espies_the_first_Elves.jpg/600px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Orom%C3%AB_espies_the_first_Elves.jpg 2x" data-file-width="850" data-file-height="475"/></a><figcaption><i>Oromë espies the first Elves</i> by <a href="/wiki/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann" title="Anke Eißmann">Anke Eißmann</a></figcaption></figure>

When [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) discovered that the Elves had awoken at [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen), great changes came about. The Valar began the [Battle of the Powers](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers) against [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth"); Utumno was destroyed and Melkor brought in chains to Valinor. The Valar also summoned the Elves to dwell in their land, and many answered this summons.

From the time the three kindreds of the Elves were gathered began the **Noontide of the Blessed Realm**.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> A period of three ages (about 2,900 years) followed. [Melkor was imprisoned](/wiki/Chaining_of_Melkor "Chaining of Melkor") in the [Halls of Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos), and the Valar and [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) dwelt together in the light of the Trees. In the darkness of Middle-earth, the Dark Elves who had not journeyed to Valinor still dwelt, and the Fathers of the Dwarves stirred. Men would not appear until some time after the end of the Years of the Trees.

These Years came to an end when [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) released Melkor from his imprisonment. For a time, the [Dark Lord](/wiki/Dark_Lord) pretended friendship with the Eldar, but he turned back to the darkness. After shining for 1495 Valian Years,<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:51</sup> the trees were destroyed by [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant). During the [Long Night](/wiki/Long_Night), they stole the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils) and fled back to the north of Middle-earth. Seeking revenge, [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) led a great part of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) out of Valinor and back to Beleriand.

So the Years of the Trees came to an end. At this time, the Valar made the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and [Moon](/wiki/Moon) to give light to the World, and after 5000 Valian Years had passed,<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:51</sup> the [Years of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun) fo the First Age began.<sup id="cite_ref-Atani_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Atani-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In Tolkien's latest writings where Arda was a [round world](/wiki/Round_World_version_of_the_Silmarillion "Round World version of the Silmarillion") from its beginning, the Two Trees held the light of the untainted Sun rather than that of the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps), which did not ever truly exist. In this version of Arda's history, rather than an absence of a Sun or Moon, the darkness that covered Arda was instead due to a great shadow designed by Melkor to spread over the northern lands of Middle-earth, blotting out the light of the Moon and stars and causing day to be a dim twilight at full.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>
