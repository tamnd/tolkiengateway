<i><b>yávë</b></i> means "fruit" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=YAB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="YAB (page does not exist)">YAB</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Yavanna#Etymology" title="Yavanna"><b>Yav</b>anna</a></i> = "Giver of fruits"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Y%C3%A1vi%C3%AB" title="Yávië"><b>yáv</b>ië</a></i> = "autumn, harvest"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Aia_Mar%C3%ADa" title="Aia María">aistana i <b>yávë</b> mónalyo</a></i> = "blessed is the fruit of thy womb"
