**Year of the Trees 1161** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1161**) is the 1161st [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1161 was also **V.Y. 4661** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Falmari](../F/Falmari.md), under the teachings of [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md), build ships pulled by swans and leave [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) for [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
