The **Years of the Sun** was the time after the first rising of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) until the present.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The unit of time of the Years of the Sun was the [*coranar*](/wiki/Coranar "Coranar") ("sun-round"), also referred to as a [*loa*](/wiki/Loa "Loa") ("growth"). Both terms equate to 365 days, 5 hours, 48 minutes, 46 seconds.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
