**Year of the Trees 1498** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1498**) is the 1498th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1498 was also **V.Y. 4998** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The chaining of [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) upon [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Fortification of a Fëanorian camp at [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>
