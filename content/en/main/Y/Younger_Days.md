The **Younger Days** were those that succeeded the [Middle Days](/wiki/Middle_Days) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). This term was applied to the years of the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) (and [beyond it](/wiki/Later_Ages "Later Ages")) following after the [Downfall of Barad-dûr](/wiki/Downfall_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr). It was a time when the [Dominion of Men](/wiki/Dominion_of_Men) at last came into its full extent.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
