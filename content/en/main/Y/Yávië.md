**Yávië** is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for the season of autumn, called [Iavas](/wiki/Iavas) in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). This was also the name of the third season in the [Calendar of Imladris](/wiki/Calendar_of_Imladris), 54 days which fell more or less between [10 August](/wiki/10_August) and [3 October](/wiki/3_October) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
