**Year of the Trees 1350** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1350**) is the 1350th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1350 was also **V.Y. 4850** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Denethor](<../D/Denethor_(king_of_the_Laiquendi).md>), son of [Lenwë](../L/Lenwë.md), leads a part of the [Nandor](../N/Nandor.md) into [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md) and is welcomed by [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
