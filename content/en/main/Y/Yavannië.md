**Yavannië** ([Ivanneth](/wiki/Ivanneth) in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)) was the ninth month of the year in the [King's Reckoning](/wiki/King%27s_Reckoning) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), following [Úrimë](/wiki/%C3%9Arim%C3%AB), and preceding the month of [Narquelië](/wiki/Narqueli%C3%AB). In the Stewards' Reckoning, *Yavannië* preceded the autumn feast of [Yáviérë](/wiki/Y%C3%A1vi%C3%A9r%C3%AB). In the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning), *Yavannië* was the sixth month of the year (still following *Úrimë*) and preceded the New Reckoning's *[Enderi](/wiki/Enderi)*.<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Yavannië* in the Kings' Reckoning (and for the most part in the Stewards' Reckoning) corresponded to [Halimath](/wiki/Halimath) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar)<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup> (approximately modern [23 August](/wiki/23_August) through [21 September](/wiki/21_September)). In the New Reckoning, 30 Yavannië corresponded with the birthday of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), [22 Halimath](/wiki/22_September "22 September") in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), and was made a festival.<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>
