**Yavannië** ([Ivanneth](../I/Ivanneth.md) in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)) was the ninth month of the year in the [King's Reckoning](../K/King's_Reckoning.md) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](../S/Stewards'_Reckoning.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), following [Úrimë](../_/Úrimë.md), and preceding the month of [Narquelië](../N/Narquelië.md). In the Stewards' Reckoning, *Yavannië* preceded the autumn feast of [Yáviérë](Yáviérë.md). In the [New Reckoning](../N/New_Reckoning.md), *Yavannië* was the sixth month of the year (still following *Úrimë*) and preceded the New Reckoning's *[Enderi](../E/Enderi.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Yavannië* in the Kings' Reckoning (and for the most part in the Stewards' Reckoning) corresponded to [Halimath](../H/Halimath.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup> (approximately modern [23 August](../_/23_August.md) through [21 September](../_/21_September.md)). In the New Reckoning, 30 Yavannië corresponded with the birthday of [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [22 Halimath](../_/22_September.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md), and was made a festival.<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>
