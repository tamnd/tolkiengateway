The **yellow-fanged guard**<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> was an [orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") in the company of orcs that kidnapped [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"). He had yellow fangs and a black [knife](/wiki/Knives "Knives")<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup> with a long sharp<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">[3]</a></sup> steel<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> blade, jugged like a saw-edge.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Two or more orc were guarding the captured [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits"). These orcs spoke with each other in [their own tongue,](/wiki/Orkish "Orkish") but used [Common Speech](/wiki/Westron "Westron") when talking to Pippin. The yellow-fanged guard threatened Pippin with this knife.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup> He also cursed the [Isengarders](/wiki/Orcs_of_Isengard "Orcs of Isengard") and their leader [Uglúk](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk) both in [Common Speech](/wiki/Westron "Westron") and in a debased form of [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech).<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-5">[5]</a></sup> [His curse](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk_u_bagronk_sha_pushdug_Saruman-glob_b%C3%BAbhosh_skai "Uglúk u bagronk sha pushdug Saruman-glob búbhosh skai") is one of the two known phrases in Black Speech.

Soon a quarrel arose among the orcs about their future route and the fate of the prisoners.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The yellow-fanged guard joined the fray<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and was cut down by broad-bladed swords of the followers of Uglúk.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> His body fell on top of Pippin,<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> who managed to cut the ropes tying his arms using the knife that the orc still held in his dead hand.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">[3]</a></sup> Four more orcs were slain by Uglúk<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> and other Isengarders<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">[3]</a></sup> during that quarrel.

## Origin {#Origin}

It is unclear whether the yellow-fanged guard was a [Mordor Orc](/wiki/Orcs_of_Mordor "Orcs of Mordor") or an [Orc of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains "Orcs of the Misty Mountains"). [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) himself in the [Appendix F](/wiki/Appendix_F) identified him as "the Mordor-orc" and as a soldier of the [Dark Tower](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr "Barad-dûr") whose captain was [Grishnákh](/wiki/Grishn%C3%A1kh).<sup id="cite_ref-:3_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-5">[5]</a></sup> On the other hand, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) called some or all of the five orcs slain in that quarrel "Northern Orcs from far away".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> However, considering that the Fellowship previously encountered black [Uruks](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai") of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) in [Moria](/wiki/Moria),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> both of these statements may be true in the same time.
