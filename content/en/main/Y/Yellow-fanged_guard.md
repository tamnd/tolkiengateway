The **yellow-fanged guard**<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> was an [orc](../O/Orcs.md) in the company of orcs that kidnapped [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md). He had yellow fangs and a black [knife](../K/Knives.md)<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup> with a long sharp<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">[3]</a></sup> steel<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> blade, jugged like a saw-edge.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Two or more orc were guarding the captured [hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md). These orcs spoke with each other in [their own tongue,](../O/Orkish.md) but used [Common Speech](../W/Westron.md) when talking to Pippin. The yellow-fanged guard threatened Pippin with this knife.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup> He also cursed the [Isengarders](../O/Orcs_of_Isengard.md) and their leader [Uglúk](../U/Uglúk.md) both in [Common Speech](../W/Westron.md) and in a debased form of [Black Speech](../B/Black_Speech.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-5">[5]</a></sup> [His curse](../U/Uglúk_u_bagronk_sha_pushdug_Saruman-glob_búbhosh_skai.md) is one of the two known phrases in Black Speech.

Soon a quarrel arose among the orcs about their future route and the fate of the prisoners.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The yellow-fanged guard joined the fray<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and was cut down by broad-bladed swords of the followers of Uglúk.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> His body fell on top of Pippin,<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> who managed to cut the ropes tying his arms using the knife that the orc still held in his dead hand.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">[3]</a></sup> Four more orcs were slain by Uglúk<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> and other Isengarders<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">[3]</a></sup> during that quarrel.

## Origin {#Origin}

It is unclear whether the yellow-fanged guard was a [Mordor Orc](../O/Orcs_of_Mordor.md) or an [Orc of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md). [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) himself in the [Appendix F](../A/Appendix_F.md) identified him as "the Mordor-orc" and as a soldier of the [Dark Tower](../B/Barad-dûr.md) whose captain was [Grishnákh](../G/Grishnákh.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:3_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-5">[5]</a></sup> On the other hand, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) called some or all of the five orcs slain in that quarrel "Northern Orcs from far away".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> However, considering that the Fellowship previously encountered black [Uruks](../U/Uruk-hai.md) of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) in [Moria](../M/Moria.md),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> both of these statements may be true in the same time.
