**Year of the Trees 1105** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1105**) is the 1105th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1105 was also **V.Y. 4605** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Many [Elves](../E/Elves.md) heed the summons of [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md), and passing the [Sea of Helcar](../S/Sea_of_Helcar.md), begin the [Great March](../G/Great_Journey.md) to [Aman](../A/Aman.md). The three clans willing were ever known as the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) and they were: the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) led by [Ingwë](../I/Ingwë.md); the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) led by [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md); and the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) led by [Elwë](../T/Thingol.md) and [Olwë](../O/Olwë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Some Elves - possibly led by [Morwë](../M/Morwë.md) and [Nurwë](../N/Nurwë.md) - chose to stay behind and became known as the [Avari](../A/Avari.md), the Unwilling. This was the First [Sundering of the Elves](../S/Sundering_of_the_Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
