**Year of the Trees 1133** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1133**) is the 1133rd [year of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Arda](/wiki/Arda). Year of the Trees 1133 was also **V.Y. 4633** in [Valian Years](/wiki/Valian_Years).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) and the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) reach [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) and establish [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar). The [Valar](/wiki/Valar) open the rift of [Calacirya](/wiki/Calacirya) in the [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri) so that the [Light of Valinor](/wiki/Light_of_Valinor) reaches Eldamar. The [Elves](/wiki/Elves) begin the building of [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion) on the hill of [Túna](/wiki/T%C3%BAna) inside Calacirya.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
