**Year of the Trees 1250** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1250**) is the 1250th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1250 was also **V.Y. 4750** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) creates the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md). He begins to study the craft of gems.<sup id="cite_ref-AA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) enter [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) and meet the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md). Both peoples befriend each other.<sup id="cite_ref-AA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
