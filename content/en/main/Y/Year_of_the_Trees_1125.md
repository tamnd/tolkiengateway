**Year of the Trees 1125** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1125**) is the 1125th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1125 was also **V.Y. 4625** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) and the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) cross the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) and reach [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). Passing over the [Vale of Sirion](../V/Vale_of_Sirion.md) they reach the shores of the [Great Sea](../B/Belegaer.md) and become afraid. [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) returns to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The [Grey Annals](../G/Grey_Annals.md) date the arrival of the Noldor and Vanyar in Beleriand to 1115 and make no mention of the Second Sundering. [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) notes in the preface to the Annals of Aman: "The dates of the annals of the Years of the Trees were changed very frequently – in some cases there are as many as six substitutions – and I give only the final form." Thus, one of the two dates seems to be a remnant of an earlier draft.
