**Yews** were a [tree](../T/Tree.md) whose wood was used for bows. [Bard](../B/Bard.md) had a bow of such wood<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>, and so did the [Woodmen](../W/Woodmen.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>, and the [Orcs of Isengard](../O/Orcs_of_Isengard.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During [Berúthiel](../B/Berúthiel.md)'s time as the [wife](../Q/Queen_of_Gondor.md) of [King](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) [Tarannon](../T/Tarannon.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), she decorated the gardens of the [King's House](../K/King's_House.md) in [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) "with tormented sculptures beneath [cypresses](../C/Cypresses.md) and yews".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
