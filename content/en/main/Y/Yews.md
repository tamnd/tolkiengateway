**Yews** were a [tree](/wiki/Tree "Tree") whose wood was used for bows. [Bard](/wiki/Bard) had a bow of such wood<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>, and so did the [Woodmen](/wiki/Woodmen)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>, and the [Orcs of Isengard](/wiki/Orcs_of_Isengard).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During [Berúthiel](/wiki/Ber%C3%BAthiel)'s time as the [wife](/wiki/Queen_of_Gondor "Queen of Gondor") of [King](/wiki/King_of_Gondor "King of Gondor") [Tarannon](/wiki/Tarannon) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), she decorated the gardens of the [King's House](/wiki/King%27s_House) in [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) "with tormented sculptures beneath [cypresses](/wiki/Cypresses "Cypresses") and yews".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
