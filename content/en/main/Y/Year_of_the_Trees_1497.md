**Year of the Trees 1497** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1497**) is the 1497th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1497 was also **V.Y. 4997** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) sends armies of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) to attack the [Elves](../S/Sindar.md) of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). The [First Battle of Beleriand](../F/First_Battle.md); the [Havens of the Falas](../H/Havens_of_the_Falas.md) are besieged.<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Sindar_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindar-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Death of [Denethor](<../D/Denethor_(son_of_Lenwë).md>) at [Amon Ereb](../A/Amon_Ereb.md). His people go into hiding in [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md) and are known as the [Laiquendi](../L/Laiquendi.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Sindar_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindar-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Melian casts a [Girdle](../G/Girdle_of_Melian.md) over her realm; it becomes known as [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Sindar_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindar-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Arrival of the Host of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) at the [Firth of Drengist](../F/Firth_of_Drengist.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Flight_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-4">[4]</a></sup> This occurred some seven sun years after Melian's raising the Girdle.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- The burning of the ships at [Losgar](../L/Losgar.md); the host of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) are forced to cross the [Helcaraxë](../H/Helcaraxë.md) to reach [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Flight_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Establishment of the camp at the shores of the [Lake Mithrim](../L/Lake_Mithrim.md) by the [Fëanorians](../H/House_of_Fëanor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Return_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-6">[6]</a></sup>
- Second battle of Beleriand: [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](../D/Dagor-nuin-Giliath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Return_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-6">[6]</a></sup>
- Death of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) at hands of [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md>) and the [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Return_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-6">[6]</a></sup>
- [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) feigns to treat with [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), but is attacked and captured.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
