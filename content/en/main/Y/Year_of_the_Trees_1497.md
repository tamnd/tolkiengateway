**Year of the Trees 1497** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1497**) is the 1497th [year of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Arda](/wiki/Arda). Year of the Trees 1497 was also **V.Y. 4997** in [Valian Years](/wiki/Valian_Years).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) sends armies of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) to attack the [Elves](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). The [First Battle of Beleriand](/wiki/First_Battle "First Battle"); the [Havens of the Falas](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas) are besieged.<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Sindar_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindar-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Death of [Denethor](</wiki/Denethor_(son_of_Lenw%C3%AB)> "Denethor (son of Lenwë)") at [Amon Ereb](/wiki/Amon_Ereb). His people go into hiding in [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand) and are known as the [Laiquendi](/wiki/Laiquendi).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Sindar_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindar-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Melian casts a [Girdle](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian "Girdle of Melian") over her realm; it becomes known as [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Sindar_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindar-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Arrival of the Host of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) at the [Firth of Drengist](/wiki/Firth_of_Drengist).<sup id="cite_ref-AA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Flight_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-4">[4]</a></sup> This occurred some seven sun years after Melian's raising the Girdle.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- The burning of the ships at [Losgar](/wiki/Losgar); the host of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) are forced to cross the [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB) to reach [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-AA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AA-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Flight_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Establishment of the camp at the shores of the [Lake Mithrim](/wiki/Lake_Mithrim) by the [Fëanorians](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor "House of Fëanor").<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Return_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-6">[6]</a></sup>
- Second battle of Beleriand: [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](/wiki/Dagor-nuin-Giliath).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Return_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-6">[6]</a></sup>
- Death of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) at hands of [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)> "Gothmog (balrog)") and the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs).<sup id="cite_ref-GA_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Return_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-6">[6]</a></sup>
- [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) feigns to treat with [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), but is attacked and captured.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
