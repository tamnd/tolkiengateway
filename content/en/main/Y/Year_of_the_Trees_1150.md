**Year of the Trees 1150** (abbreviated to **Y.T. 1150**) is the 1150th [year of the Trees](Years_of_the_Trees.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Year of the Trees 1150 was also **V.Y. 4650** in [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Olwë](../O/Olwë.md) finally decides to move to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) and embarks on [Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) with a great host of the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The folk of [Elwë](../T/Thingol.md) are still unwilling to depart - they remain with their lord and become known as the [Eglath](../E/Eglath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
