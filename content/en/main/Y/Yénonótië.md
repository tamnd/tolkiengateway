The <i><b>Yénonótië</b></i> was a great work by the [Loremaster](/wiki/Lambengolmor "Lambengolmor") [Quennar](/wiki/Quennar) in which he explained the chronology of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar). It included discussions on the lengths of [Valian Years](/wiki/Valian_Years), the ages of the Valar, and the [Years of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun) of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). Some of the material in the *Yénonótië* was later quoted by [Pengolodh](/wiki/Pengolodh) in his enlargement of the *[Annals of Aman](/wiki/The_Annals_of_Aman "The Annals of Aman")*.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Yénonótië* means "Reckoning of Years" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (from the words *[yén](/wiki/Y%C3%A9n "Yén")* '[long year](/wiki/Valian_year "Valian year")' and *onótië*, 'reckoning').
