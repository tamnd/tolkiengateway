The <i><b>Yénonótië</b></i> was a great work by the [Loremaster](../L/Lambengolmor.md) [Quennar](../Q/Quennar.md) in which he explained the chronology of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md). It included discussions on the lengths of [Valian Years](../V/Valian_Years.md), the ages of the Valar, and the [Years of the Sun](Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). Some of the material in the *Yénonótië* was later quoted by [Pengolodh](../P/Pengolodh.md) in his enlargement of the *[Annals of Aman](../T/The_Annals_of_Aman.md)*.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Yénonótië* means "Reckoning of Years" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) (from the words *[yén](Yén.md)* '[long year](../V/Valian_year.md)' and *onótië*, 'reckoning').
