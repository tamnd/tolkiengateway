<i><b>Dreams &amp; Gifts: The Art of Angelo Montanini</b></i> was an exhibition of artwork by [Angelo Montanini](/wiki/Angelo_Montanini). The exhibition, curated by Davide Martini, was held in Florence, Italy in [2007](/wiki/2007).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Most illustrations are inspired by the works of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), although a few have themes from other fantasy worlds. An accompanying book was released the same year, which was printed in 700 copies.
