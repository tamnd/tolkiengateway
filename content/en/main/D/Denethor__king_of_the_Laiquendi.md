**Denethor** was the son of [Lenwë](/wiki/Lenwë), the original leader of the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor).

## History

Daunted by the towering peaks of the [Hithaeglir](/wiki/Hithaeglir), Lenwë, his son Denethor, and other [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) forsook the [Great March](/wiki/Great_March) of the Elves to the [West](/wiki/Valinor) and passed southward down the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). Much later Denethor would lead a part of those people, the Nandor, west into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).

> Reference: {{S|Captivity}}

In the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age),

> Reference: {{TI|Breaking}}, p. 375, note 7

centuries before the first rising of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun), the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and other foul creatures of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) began to harass the Nandor east of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains). Hearing of the power and protection of the Elf King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol), Denethor led many of his people over the Blue Mountains into Beleriand.  They settled in [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand) and Denethor became their king. They were called the *[Laiquendi](/wiki/Laiquendi)* ([Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Green-elves").

Years later, in {{YT|1497}}, [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) loosed an army on Beleriand, beginning the [First Battle](/wiki/First_Battle_of_Beleriand) in the [Wars of Beleriand](/wiki/Battles_of_Beleriand).

> Reference: {{GA|36}}

The lightly-armed Laiquendi were driven back to the great hill of [Amon Ereb](/wiki/Amon_Ereb), where Denethor was slain.

> Reference: {{GA|39}}

While the Laiquendi survived, they became a reclusive people, seldom taking up arms, and never again naming a king.

> Reference: {{S|Sindar}}

## Etymology

{{Pronounce|Denethor.mp3|Ardamir}}

In an explanation of the name of [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor#Etymology) (who was named after this elf), the Steward of Gondor, it is stated that *Denethor* is [Nandorin](/wiki/Nandorin) fit into the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) sound range, and is said to mean "Lithe and lank".

> Reference: {{WJ|Author}}, Note 17

In the earlier *[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)*, the name Denethor is said to contain the word element *Dani*.

> Reference: {{LR|Etymologies}} entry DAN-

According to *[The Lhammas](/wiki/The_Lhammas)*, it derives from [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish) *Ndani-thārō*, meaning "Saviour of the Dani", as he was the one who led them to the West. Other derivatives of this name are: *Daintáro* in [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin), *Nanisáro* in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), *Dainthor* in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) and *Denipor* in [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin).

> Reference: {{LR|Lhammas}}
