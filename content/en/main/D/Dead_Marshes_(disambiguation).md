**Dead Marshes** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- The **[Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes)**, the wetlands that lay north-west of the [Dagorlad](/wiki/Dagorlad) and where [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo), [Sam](/wiki/Sam) and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) found passage on their way to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- [The Dead Marshes](/wiki/The_Dead_Marshes), the eleventh track of disc one of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers - The Complete Recordings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_-_The_Complete_Recordings)* by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore).
- [Dagorlad and the Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dagorlad_and_the_Dead_Marshes) is a module (in the Adventure-series) for [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), 1st Edition.
