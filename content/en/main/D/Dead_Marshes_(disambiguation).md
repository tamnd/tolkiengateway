**Dead Marshes** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- The **[Dead Marshes](Dead_Marshes.md)**, the wetlands that lay north-west of the [Dagorlad](Dagorlad.md) and where [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md), [Sam](../S/Sam.md) and [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) found passage on their way to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- [The Dead Marshes](../T/The_Dead_Marshes.md), the eleventh track of disc one of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers - The Complete Recordings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_-_The_Complete_Recordings.md)* by [Howard Shore](../H/Howard_Shore.md).
- [Dagorlad and the Dead Marshes](Dagorlad_and_the_Dead_Marshes.md) is a module (in the Adventure-series) for [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md), 1st Edition.
