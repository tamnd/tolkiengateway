**Dean Lance O'Gorman** ([1 December](../_/1_December.md), [1976](../_/1976.md)) is a New Zealand actor. He portrayed [Fíli](../F/Fíli.md) in the [*The Hobbit* (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting1-1">[1]</a></sup> O'Gorman joined the cast on [30 April](../_/30_April.md) [2011](../_/2011.md) after Rob Kazinsky, who was originally cast as Fíli, left the production for personal reasons.<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting1-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting2-2">[2]</a></sup>
