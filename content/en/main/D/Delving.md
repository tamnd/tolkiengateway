**Delving** was a town in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") that appeared in *[Perry-the-Winkle](/wiki/Perry-the-Winkle)*, a nursery rhyme<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-1">[1]</a></sup> by [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)<sup id="cite_ref-preface_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-preface-2">[2]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-Perry_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Perry-3">[3]</a></sup>

It was perhaps a fictionalization of [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving).<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a possible [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") form *Delfing*. It means "digging".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
