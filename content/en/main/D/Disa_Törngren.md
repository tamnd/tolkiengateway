**Disa Törngren** was a Swedish publisher, translator and author. Among her literary achievements was the co-authoring of *Hexikon: En sagolik uppslagsbok* (1981; \["Hexicon: An Encyclopedia of Fairy-tales"\]).

Disa Törngren's son, Erland Törngren, has described how his mother (then working as Head of the fiction department of the publishing house Geber), having heard about the success of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* in England, contacted [Stanley Unwin](/wiki/Stanley_Unwin) during the later 1950s in order to negotiate the Swedish publication rights.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> On behalf of Almqvist &amp; Wiksell/Gebers Förlag, Törngren wrote on 3 July 1956 to [Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/George_Allen_and_Unwin "George Allen and Unwin") that [Åke Ohlmarks](/wiki/%C3%85ke_Ohlmarks) had been chosen as the translator of *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In 1978, her translation of [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter)'s *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography)* was published. The poems included in the book were translated by Åke Ohlmarks.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
