The **Deeping-coomb** was a deep valley on the northern side of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) that led up to [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep) and the castle of the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Deeping-coomb was a green valley whose grass covered slopes lapped the mounting hills.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Its mouth was five leagues (15 miles<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>) from the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The Deeping-coomb was the site of an important battle, the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg), during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name has the sense the "deep valley belonging to the *Deep*" to which it led.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

A *Coomb* is a "deep valley", occurring frequently in English place-names in the forms *-comb*, *-cumb*, *-combe*, etc.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
