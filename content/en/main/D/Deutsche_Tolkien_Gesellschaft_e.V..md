The **Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft e.V.** (shortened **DTG**) (German: "German Tolkien Society (incorporated society)") is the German [Tolkien society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md).

## History {#History}

The DTG was founded on [1 August](../_/1_August.md), [1997](../_/1997.md) as a literary association. Since [1998](../_/1998.md) the domicile of the DTG is in Cologne. It steadily grew up to its current member count of about 1500 people, especially from Germany or German-speaking countries.

## Gatherings {#Gatherings}

The DTG organises a number of Tolkien events:

- *[Tolkien Thing](../T/Tolkien_Thing.md)* ("Tolkien Moot") - A meeting of the DTG members. (once a year)
- *Tolkien Tage* ("Tolkien Days") - A weekend with seminars, lectures, games, performances and other activities for all Tolkien-fans.
- *Tolkien Seminar* (since [2004](../_/2004.md)) - Somekind of academic convention, where german Tolkien-experts lecture and then discuss with the other participants a common theme, based on Tolkien's works. (once a year)
- *Tolkien Stammtisch* ("Tolkien regulars' table") - A regional event. The meetings ("Stammtische") take place regularly (usually once a month). The *Tolkien Stammtisch* is something like a regional representation of the DTG and allows potential members the first contact with the association.

## Publications {#Publications}

Two times a year the DTG publishes its association magazine *Der Flammifer von Westernis* (including an English Summary). The most important publication is the scientific journal *[Hither Shore](<../H/Hither_Shore_(journal).md>)* (bilingual).
