**Dogs** were animals in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), similar to [Foxes](/wiki/Foxes) and [Wolves](/wiki/Wolves), but domesticated.

The Races of Middle-earth had domesticated and employed dogs, with the notable exception of [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) who didn't have good relations with animals.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) had domesticated dogs, like [Farmer Maggot](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot) who owned three to guard [his field](/wiki/Bamfurlong "Bamfurlong").

Dogs bred for hunting were [Hounds](/wiki/Hounds). The greatest of them all was [Huan](/wiki/Huan), the Hound of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), the word for "dog" is *huo*, and the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) cognate of the same meaning is *hû*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") also experimented with other, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onomatopoeia" title="wikipedia:Onomatopoeia">onomatopoetic</a> names: Quenya *roa*, *grā*, and *wā(v)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), the early version of Noldorin, "dog" is also *hû*, and the additional cognate *huil* means "bitch". The word *saur* means "hound, wild dog".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

[Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) also had its own terms for a dog, specifically *raba* in "common" gender singular ("dog", as a species/creature), the masculine singular *rabô* ("male dog"), the feminine singular *rabê* ("female dog", "bitch"), the plural *rabî* ("dogs"), and the dual (pair-plural) *rabât* ("a pair/duo of dogs"). There are also known declensed forms in the non-nominative cases, namely *raban* in the singular subjective case, *rabīm* in the plural subjective case, and *rabu-* in the objective case.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

At the end of the scene [The Account of Isildur](/wiki/The_Account_of_Isildur) a dog is seen barking then backing into a [hobbit-hole](/wiki/Hobbit-hole "Hobbit-hole") as a [Black Rider](/wiki/Black_Rider) approaches.

<dl><dd>Dogs are found throughout Middle-earth, especially in the Shire. Lore-masters can also have a non-combat dog pet.</dd></dl>
