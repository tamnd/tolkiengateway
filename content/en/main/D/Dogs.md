**Dogs** were animals in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), similar to [Foxes](../F/Foxes.md) and [Wolves](../W/Wolves.md), but domesticated.

The Races of Middle-earth had domesticated and employed dogs, with the notable exception of [Dwarves](Dwarves.md) who didn't have good relations with animals.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) had domesticated dogs, like [Farmer Maggot](../F/Farmer_Maggot.md) who owned three to guard [his field](../B/Bamfurlong.md).

Dogs bred for hunting were [Hounds](../H/Hounds.md). The greatest of them all was [Huan](../H/Huan.md), the Hound of [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), the word for "dog" is *huo*, and the [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) cognate of the same meaning is *hû*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) also experimented with other, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onomatopoeia" title="wikipedia:Onomatopoeia">onomatopoetic</a> names: Quenya *roa*, *grā*, and *wā(v)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md), the early version of Noldorin, "dog" is also *hû*, and the additional cognate *huil* means "bitch". The word *saur* means "hound, wild dog".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

[Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) also had its own terms for a dog, specifically *raba* in "common" gender singular ("dog", as a species/creature), the masculine singular *rabô* ("male dog"), the feminine singular *rabê* ("female dog", "bitch"), the plural *rabî* ("dogs"), and the dual (pair-plural) *rabât* ("a pair/duo of dogs"). There are also known declensed forms in the non-nominative cases, namely *raban* in the singular subjective case, *rabīm* in the plural subjective case, and *rabu-* in the objective case.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

At the end of the scene [The Account of Isildur](../T/The_Account_of_Isildur.md) a dog is seen barking then backing into a [hobbit-hole](../H/Hobbit-hole.md) as a [Black Rider](../B/Black_Rider.md) approaches.
