English actor **Dominic Monaghan** (born 1976 in Berlin, Germany) played [Meriadoc "Merry" Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") in [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"). He is also the narrator of [Ringers: Lord of the Fans](/wiki/Ringers:_Lord_of_the_Fans), a documentary about the influence of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in popular culture. In 2021, he and fellow *Lord of the Rings* actor [Billy Boyd](/wiki/Billy_Boyd) started the *[The Friendship Onion](/wiki/The_Friendship_Onion)* podcast, which includes stories from their time making the films.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
