**Drúwaith Iaur** also called the **Old Púkel-wilderness**<sup id="cite_ref-FurtherNotes_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FurtherNotes-1">[1]</a></sup> or **Old Púkel-land**<sup id="cite_ref-Note13_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note13-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> was a region of dark woods<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-4">[4]</a></sup> in the mountains of the promontory of [Andrast](/wiki/Andrast)<sup id="cite_ref-FurtherNotes_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FurtherNotes-1">[1]</a></sup> (a southwestern outlier of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains)) on their northern<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and northwestern side<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>.

Drúwaith Iaur was one of two regions in the White Mountains where the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain) lived. They settled there in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) after they had crossed the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) (probably near [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros)) from the east and continued to live there secretly at the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-FurtherNotes_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FurtherNotes-1">[1]</a></sup> Drúwaith Iaur was never occupied by the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans),<sup id="cite_ref-FurtherNotes_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FurtherNotes-1">[1]</a></sup> not considered to be a part of the realm of Gondor,<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-4">[4]</a></sup> not inhabited by Men of Gondor or of Rohan and seldom visited by them<sup id="cite_ref-FurtherNotes_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FurtherNotes-1">[1]</a></sup>. After the [Battles of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Battles_of_the_Fords_of_Isen) the Drúedain came out of the caves where they lived and attacked a remnant of the forces of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) that had been driven from the fords to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-Note13_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note13-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Drúwaith Iaur* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, consisting of *drú* ("wild"), *[waith](/wiki/Waith "Waith")* ("folk, land")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and *[iaur](/wiki/Iaur "Iaur")* ("old").<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-4">[4]</a></sup> In this name the word *Iaur* does not mean "original" but "former".<sup id="cite_ref-FurtherNotes_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FurtherNotes-1">[1]</a></sup>

It thus could be translated as **The old wilderness of the Drû-folk**<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>, **Old Púkel-wilderness**<sup id="cite_ref-FurtherNotes_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FurtherNotes-1">[1]</a></sup> or **Old Púkel-land**<sup id="cite_ref-Note13_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note13-2">[2]</a></sup>.
