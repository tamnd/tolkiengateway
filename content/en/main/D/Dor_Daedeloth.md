**Dor Daedeloth** was the land of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

## Geography {#Geography}

Dor Daedeloth was one of the three large regions of western Middle-earth, along with [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum). It lay all around the fortress of [Angband](/wiki/Angband) and on both sides of the [Ered Engrin](/wiki/Ered_Engrin). Its borders were the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin) to the west, the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) to the east, and the [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), the [Pass of Sirion](/wiki/Pass_of_Sirion) and the [March of Maedhros](/wiki/March_of_Maedhros) to the south.

The Ered Engrin, an absolutely unclimbable chain, split the region in two separate parts. The southern part was [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen), the great grassy plain north of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), later to become the desert of [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith) after the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) had utterly burned it. The northern part was an unnamed region, cold and vast.

## History {#History}

It was here, north of the mountains, that the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and other creatures of Morgoth lived and bred.<sup id="cite_ref-S13_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S13-1">[1]</a></sup>

The march of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) in the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) was halted there, when [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) was mortally wounded by the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs). The Noldor then encircled the land (at least in the south), starting the [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband).<sup id="cite_ref-S13_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S13-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion "The Silmarillion")*, the name *Dor Daedeloth* is translated as "Land of the Shadow of Horror".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In other sources it is spelled as <i><b>Dor-Daedeloth</b></i>, and translated as "Land of Great Dread".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) notes that the name appears only in the chapter *[Of the Return of the Noldor](/wiki/Of_the_Return_of_the_Noldor)* and is not mentioned again after the [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

<i><b>Dor-Daideloth</b></i> was the name used for the region in early versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s final writings, the name was changed on the primary map to <i><b>Dor-na-Daerachas</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
