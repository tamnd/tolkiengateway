**Dor Daedeloth** was the land of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

Dor Daedeloth was one of the three large regions of western Middle-earth, along with [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) and [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md). It lay all around the fortress of [Angband](../A/Angband.md) and on both sides of the [Ered Engrin](../E/Ered_Engrin.md). Its borders were the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) to the west, the [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md) to the east, and the [Dorthonion](Dorthonion.md), the [Pass of Sirion](../P/Pass_of_Sirion.md) and the [March of Maedhros](../M/March_of_Maedhros.md) to the south.

The Ered Engrin, an absolutely unclimbable chain, split the region in two separate parts. The southern part was [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md), the great grassy plain north of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), later to become the desert of [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md) after the [Dagor Bragollach](Dagor_Bragollach.md) had utterly burned it. The northern part was an unnamed region, cold and vast.

## History {#History}

It was here, north of the mountains, that the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and other creatures of Morgoth lived and bred.<sup id="cite_ref-S13_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S13-1">[1]</a></sup>

The march of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) in the late [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) was halted there, when [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) was mortally wounded by the [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md). The Noldor then encircled the land (at least in the south), starting the [Siege of Angband](../S/Siege_of_Angband.md).<sup id="cite_ref-S13_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S13-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the published *[Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, the name *Dor Daedeloth* is translated as "Land of the Shadow of Horror".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In other sources it is spelled as <i><b>Dor-Daedeloth</b></i>, and translated as "Land of Great Dread".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md) notes that the name appears only in the chapter *[Of the Return of the Noldor](../O/Of_the_Return_of_the_Noldor.md)* and is not mentioned again after the [Siege of Angband](../S/Siege_of_Angband.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

<i><b>Dor-Daideloth</b></i> was the name used for the region in early versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s final writings, the name was changed on the primary map to <i><b>Dor-na-Daerachas</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
