<i><b>Digging Potatoes, Growing Trees</b></i> is a collection of the after-dinner speeches held at the Annual Dinners of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md). There have been three volumes of *Digging Potatoes, Growing Trees* covering speeches from 1980 to 2000. *Digging Potatoes, Growing Trees* has been published by [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) as volumes 5, 6 and 11 in the [Peter Roe Memorial Fund](../P/Peter_Roe_Memorial_Fund.md) series.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><a href="../R/Rayner_Unwin.md" title="Rayner Unwin">Rayner Unwin</a>&#39;s address from 1990 covers the plans for future Tolkien publications. From 1991, <a href="../T/Tom_Shippey.md" title="Tom Shippey">Tom Shippey</a> examines Tolkien&#39;s position as a writer amongst his contemporaries. <a href="../C/Colin_Duriez.md" title="Colin Duriez">Colin Duriez</a> in 1992 considered the friendship of Tolkien and C S Lewis. In 1995, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Joan_Anne_Tolkien&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Joan Anne Tolkien (page does not exist)">Joan Anne Tolkien</a>, J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s granddaughter related some of her memories of her grandfather. <a href="../R/René_van_Rossenberg.md" title="René van Rossenberg">René van Rossenberg</a>, who spoke in 1996 discusses the growth of Tolkien Societies in Europe and how Tolkien is appreciated by those for whom English is not their first language. In 1997, <a href="../C/Colin_Duriez.md" title="Colin Duriez">Colin Duriez</a> returned to discuss how both Tolkien and Lewis placed humanity and creativity at the centre of their work.
</p>
</blockquote>

## Volume 1 {#Volume_1}

- [1980](../_/1980.md): [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md), "Inspiration and Invention, or Where Tolkien Got Stuck"
- [1983](../_/1983.md): [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md), "...I thought of the incident of Zeebrugge which nobody wrote about at all..."
- [1986](../_/1986.md): Geraldine Harris, "...'Growing trees' when I should have been 'digging potatoes'..."

## Volume 2 {#Volume_2}

- [1990](../_/1990.md): [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md), "'How many more volumes in this [series](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)?' I don't know, [Christopher](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) doesn't know"
- [1991](../_/1991.md): [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md), "What have these people got in common? One thing... they had all been shot at"
- [1992](../_/1992.md): [Colin Duriez](../C/Colin_Duriez.md), "'Art has been verified...' The friendship of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md)"
- [1995](../_/1995.md): [Joan Anne Tolkien](../T/Tolkien_Family.md), "...asked many times - and it still surprises me - 'Did you know your grandfather?'"
- [1996](../_/1996.md): [René van Rossenberg](../R/René_van_Rossenberg.md), "...When I made a mental picture of [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), I saw dykes, windmills, rectangular tulip-fields"
- [1997](../_/1997.md): [Colin Duriez](../C/Colin_Duriez.md), "Tolkien and the Old West"

## Volume 3 {#Volume_3}

- [1995](../_/1995.md): Charles A. Coulombe, "The Ethics of Escape"
- [1998](../_/1998.md): [Michael Tolkien](../M/Michael_George_Tolkien.md), "Eden, Fall, Exile and Beyond"
- [1999](../_/1999.md): Michael Scott Rohan, "Whether it was genetic or cultural or religious disposition, Tolkien loved music"
- [2000](../_/2000.md): Patrick Curry, "On Hobbits and Elves: or Took and Baggins Again"
