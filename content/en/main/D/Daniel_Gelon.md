**Daniel Gelon** (born [16 January](/wiki/16_January) [1967](/wiki/1967)) is an American fantasy artist. He often illustrated for [Wizards of the Coast](/wiki/Wizards_of_the_Coast) properties such as [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering) and [Dungeons and Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup> In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.

He initially worked in ink, then moved on to watercolour, gouache, acrylics and eventually digital art. He is married to fellow artist [Heather Hudson](/wiki/Heather_Hudson).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
