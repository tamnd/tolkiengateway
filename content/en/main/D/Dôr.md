<i><b>dôr</b></i> or <i><b>dor</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) noun meaning "land, dwelling-place". Compare with *[nórë](../N/Nórë.md)* in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="Dor_Caranthir.md" title="Dor Caranthir"><b>Dor</b> Caranthir</a></i> = "Land of Caranthir"
- <i><a href="Dor-Cúarthol.md" title="Dor-Cúarthol"><b>Dor</b>-Cúarthol</a></i> = "Land of Bow and Helm"
- <i><a href="Doriath.md" title="Doriath"><b>Dor</b>iath</a></i> = "Fenced Land"
- <i><a href="Dorthonion.md" title="Dorthonion"><b>Dor</b>thonion</a></i> = "Land of Pines"
- <i><a href="Dorwinion.md" title="Dorwinion"><b>Dor</b>winion</a></i> = "Young-land country"
- <i><a href="../E/Eriador.md" title="Eriador">Eria<b>dor</b></a></i> = "Lonely Land"
- <i><a href="../G/Gondor.md" title="Gondor">Gon<b>dor</b></a> = &#34;</i>Stone Land*"*
- <i><a href="../M/Mordor.md" title="Mordor">Mor<b>dor</b></a> = &#34;</i>Dark Land; Black Land*"*
