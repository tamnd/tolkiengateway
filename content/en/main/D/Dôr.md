<i><b>dôr</b></i> or <i><b>dor</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) noun meaning "land, dwelling-place". Compare with *[nórë](/wiki/N%C3%B3r%C3%AB "Nórë")* in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Dor_Caranthir" title="Dor Caranthir"><b>Dor</b> Caranthir</a></i> = "Land of Caranthir"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol" title="Dor-Cúarthol"><b>Dor</b>-Cúarthol</a></i> = "Land of Bow and Helm"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Doriath" title="Doriath"><b>Dor</b>iath</a></i> = "Fenced Land"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Dorthonion" title="Dorthonion"><b>Dor</b>thonion</a></i> = "Land of Pines"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Dorwinion" title="Dorwinion"><b>Dor</b>winion</a></i> = "Young-land country"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Eriador" title="Eriador">Eria<b>dor</b></a></i> = "Lonely Land"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gon<b>dor</b></a> = &#34;</i>Stone Land*"*
- <i><a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mor<b>dor</b></a> = &#34;</i>Dark Land; Black Land*"*
