**Dora Baggins** was the aunt of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).

## History {#History}

Her parents were [Fosco Baggins](/wiki/Fosco_Baggins) and [Ruby Bolger](/wiki/Ruby_Bolger). She had two younger brothers, [Dudo Baggins](/wiki/Dudo_Baggins) and [Drogo Baggins](/wiki/Drogo_Baggins), the father of Frodo.<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup>

Dora enjoyed writing letters filled with advice to her relations, and in memory of this correspondence [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) gave her a waste-paper basket when he left [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> She attended his [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") at the age of 99. Dora Baggins died five years later in [1406](/wiki/Third_Age_3006 "Third Age 3006").<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Dora* was meaningless in contemporary [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) [Westron](/wiki/Westron). It represented an original name *Doro* or *Dore*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
