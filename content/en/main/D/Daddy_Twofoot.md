**Daddy Twofoot** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") in the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

## History {#History}

Daddy Twofoot lived in a hole on [Bagshot Row](/wiki/Bagshot_Row) in [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), right next door to [Hamfast Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee") and his family. He was a regular at the [Ivy Bush Inn](/wiki/Ivy_Bush "Ivy Bush"), where he was one particular evening in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001"), when Hamfast Gamgee entertained his fellow patrons with his knowledge of [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) and "[Mad Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins")". During the conversation, Dad seconded [Old Noakes](/wiki/Old_Noakes)'s disdain for the [Bucklanders](/wiki/Bucklanders), considering them peculiar strangers.<sup id="cite_ref-ALEP_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ALEP-1">[1]</a></sup>

<b>1981: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Daddy Twofoot is voiced by <a href="/wiki/Leonard_Fenton" title="Leonard Fenton">Leonard Fenton</a>. He is present in the Ivy Bush before <a href="/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party" title="Bilbo&#39;s Farewell Party">Bilbo&#39;s Birthday Party</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1992: <a href="/wiki/Der_Herr_der_Ringe_(1992_German_radio_series)" title="Der Herr der Ringe (1992 German radio series)"><i>Der Herr der Ringe</i> (1992 German radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Daddy Twofoot is played by Hans Edgar Stecher.</dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Daddy Twofoot&#39;s place at the conversation (which is moved to the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Green_Dragon" title="Green Dragon">Green Dragon</a>) is taken over by <a href="/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee" title="Samwise Gamgee">Sam</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> A <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Decipher_Card" title="Decipher Card">Decipher Card</a> of Daddy Twofoot identifies him as one of the Hobbits seen in the preceding scene, <i><a href="/wiki/Concerning_Hobbits_(scene)" title="Concerning Hobbits (scene)">Concerning Hobbits</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Daddy Twofoot lives at Number Two, <a href="/wiki/Bagshot_Row" title="Bagshot Row">Bagshot Row</a>, and can be seen sitting outside the Green Dragon. No actor is specified for the part.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Found at his residence not far from <a href="/wiki/Bag_End" title="Bag End">Bag End</a>, Daddy Twofoot plays no role in the story and only provides a few lines during a seasonal festival quest.</dd></dl>
