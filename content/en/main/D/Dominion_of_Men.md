The **Dominion of Men** refers to the fact that [Men](/wiki/Men) remained the only speaking race on [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), inheriting its mastery from the ancient [Elves](/wiki/Elves). For this they were sometimes called "The Usurpers" by the Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The Dominion specifically referred to the advent of the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) and beyond.

## History {#History}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395753"/><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>These were the fading years of the Eldar. [...] they attempted nothing new, living in memory of the past. The Dwarves hid themselves in deep places, guarding their hoards.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_Tale_of_Years" title="The Tale of Years">The Tale of Years</a>: The Third Age</i></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395753"/></div>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

The Dominion of Men and the fading of the [First-born](/wiki/First-born) were foreseen by the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) in the [Vision of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Vision_of_Il%C3%BAvatar), before the Creation of [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Therefore [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar) knew about the coming of this changing of the World, but they could not know its result.

These times began in the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the "Fading Years" for the Elves, when most left Middle-earth and sailed to [the West](/wiki/Aman "Aman"). This was the case for example, of the Elvish realm of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth), which was abandoned by the Elves before becoming a realm of [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Meanwhile, other races, such as [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and [Ents](/wiki/Ents),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> saw their numbers dwindling. The Dominion of Men was exemplified in the extent of the [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) language of [Westron](/wiki/Westron), which by the end of the Age was adopted by most of the other speaking races of the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

By the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the Dominion of Men was already projected. [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) saw it as an opportunity and prepared [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s rule for the [Younger Days](/wiki/Younger_Days). He attempted to invite Gandalf to join this Power.<sup id="cite_ref-council_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-8">[8]</a></sup>

[Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) spoke of the Dominion of Men when he and Aragorn stood in the high hallow on [Mindolluin](/wiki/Mindolluin), where there grew a sapling of [Nimloth](/wiki/Nimloth_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Nimloth of Númenor").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)'s ascent to the Throne of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom) marked that new era.

The Dominion of Men officially began with the destruction of [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), when the [Three Rings](/wiki/Three_Rings) lost their powers of preserving the lore and beauty as understood by the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) and their [Keepers](/wiki/Keepers_of_the_Three_Rings "Keepers of the Three Rings") passed over the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") on the [White Ship](/wiki/White_Ship). <span></span>
