The **Dominion of Men** refers to the fact that [Men](../M/Men.md) remained the only speaking race on [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), inheriting its mastery from the ancient [Elves](../E/Elves.md). For this they were sometimes called "The Usurpers" by the Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The Dominion specifically referred to the advent of the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md) and beyond.

## History {#History}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>These were the fading years of the Eldar. [...] they attempted nothing new, living in memory of the past. The Dwarves hid themselves in deep places, guarding their hoards.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../T/The_Tale_of_Years.md" title="The Tale of Years">The Tale of Years</a>: The Third Age</i></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

The Dominion of Men and the fading of the [First-born](../F/First-born.md) were foreseen by the [Ainur](../A/Ainur.md) in the [Vision of Ilúvatar](../V/Vision_of_Ilúvatar.md), before the Creation of [Eä](../E/Eä.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Therefore [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md) knew about the coming of this changing of the World, but they could not know its result.

These times began in the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the "Fading Years" for the Elves, when most left Middle-earth and sailed to [the West](../A/Aman.md). This was the case for example, of the Elvish realm of [Dol Amroth](Dol_Amroth.md), which was abandoned by the Elves before becoming a realm of [Dúnedain](Dúnedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Meanwhile, other races, such as [Dwarves](Dwarves.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and [Ents](../E/Ents.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> saw their numbers dwindling. The Dominion of Men was exemplified in the extent of the [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) language of [Westron](../W/Westron.md), which by the end of the Age was adopted by most of the other speaking races of the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

By the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the Dominion of Men was already projected. [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) saw it as an opportunity and prepared [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s rule for the [Younger Days](../Y/Younger_Days.md). He attempted to invite Gandalf to join this Power.<sup id="cite_ref-council_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-8">[8]</a></sup>

[Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) spoke of the Dominion of Men when he and Aragorn stood in the high hallow on [Mindolluin](../M/Mindolluin.md), where there grew a sapling of [Nimloth](../N/Nimloth_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md)'s ascent to the Throne of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md) marked that new era.

The Dominion of Men officially began with the destruction of [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md), when the [Three Rings](../T/Three_Rings.md) lost their powers of preserving the lore and beauty as understood by the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) and their [Keepers](../K/Keepers_of_the_Three_Rings.md) passed over the [Sea](../B/Belegaer.md) on the [White Ship](../W/White_Ship.md). <span></span>
