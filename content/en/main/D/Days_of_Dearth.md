The **Days of Dearth** was the period of famine in [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) following the [Long Winter](../L/Long_Winter.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-1">[1]</a></sup> a bitterly cold winter (lasting from [November](../N/November.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2758](../T/Third_Age_2758.md) to March [2759](../T/Third_Age_2759.md))<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup> in which many thousands of [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) died. The subsequent famine continued into the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2760](../T/Third_Age_2760.md).
