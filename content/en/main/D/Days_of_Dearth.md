The **Days of Dearth** was the period of famine in [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire) following the [Long Winter](/wiki/Long_Winter),<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-1">[1]</a></sup> a bitterly cold winter (lasting from [November](/wiki/November) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758") to March [2759](/wiki/Third_Age_2759 "Third Age 2759"))<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup> in which many thousands of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) died. The subsequent famine continued into the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2760](/wiki/Third_Age_2760 "Third Age 2760").
