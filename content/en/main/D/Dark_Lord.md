The **Dark Lord**, **Dark Power** or **the Enemy** were names given to the two major foes of the [Free Peoples](../F/Free_Peoples.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md): **[Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)**, the mighty rebel [Vala](../V/Valar.md), and **[Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)**, the great subversive [Maia](../M/Maia.md).

## History {#History}

When Morgoth ruled in [Utumno](../U/Utumno.md), he took the [form](../F/Fana.md) of a dark lord, tall and terrible. Thereafter, before approaching [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md) after escaping from the Valar's service, he took the same form, in which he would remain for ever.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Once in [Angband](../A/Angband.md), he forged an [Iron Crown](../I/Iron_Crown.md) and proclaimed himself "[King of the World](../K/King_of_the_World.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He was, though, often called **Dark Lord** since then.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In the Third Age, [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md) will remember him by this title.

In the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), his former lieutenant Sauron followed in his footsteps, becoming his successor. He first tried to subdue the Free Peoples with the [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md), disguised as [Annatar](../A/Annatar.md), but after being discovered, he began conquering Middle-earth with military power, also taking pretentious titles like "Lord of the Earth" as its self-styled ruler. Since then, though, he was often called **Dark Power** or **Dark Lord**,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> especially by [Men](../M/Men.md), who avoid the mere uttering of his name.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
