The **Dark Lord**, **Dark Power** or **the Enemy** were names given to the two major foes of the [Free Peoples](/wiki/Free_Peoples) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth): **[Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)**, the mighty rebel [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar"), and **[Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)**, the great subversive [Maia](/wiki/Maia).

## History {#History}

When Morgoth ruled in [Utumno](/wiki/Utumno), he took the [form](/wiki/Fana "Fana") of a dark lord, tall and terrible. Thereafter, before approaching [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) after escaping from the Valar's service, he took the same form, in which he would remain for ever.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Once in [Angband](/wiki/Angband), he forged an [Iron Crown](/wiki/Iron_Crown) and proclaimed himself "[King of the World](/wiki/King_of_the_World)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He was, though, often called **Dark Lord** since then.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In the Third Age, [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) will remember him by this title.

In the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), his former lieutenant Sauron followed in his footsteps, becoming his successor. He first tried to subdue the Free Peoples with the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power), disguised as [Annatar](/wiki/Annatar), but after being discovered, he began conquering Middle-earth with military power, also taking pretentious titles like "Lord of the Earth" as its self-styled ruler. Since then, though, he was often called **Dark Power** or **Dark Lord**,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> especially by [Men](/wiki/Men), who avoid the mere uttering of his name.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
