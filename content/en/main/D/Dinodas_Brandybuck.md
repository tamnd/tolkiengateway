**Dinodas Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Dinodas was the sixth child and youngest son of [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck) and [Mirabella Took](/wiki/Mirabella_Took). He attended [Bilbo's](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Dinodas* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) suggests that the name *Dinodas* is a rearrangement of the name *[Dodinas](/wiki/Dodinas_Brandybuck "Dodinas Brandybuck")*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

*Dodynas* and *Dinodes* are both alternate spellings of the Table Knight Sir Dodinas le Savage.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier versions of the family tree of the Brandybuck family he was called *Priamus*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
