**Dinodas Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Dinodas was the sixth child and youngest son of [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](../G/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Mirabella Took](../M/Mirabella_Took.md). He attended [Bilbo's](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) [Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Dinodas* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](../C/Celtic.md) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](../J/Jim_Allan.md) suggests that the name *Dinodas* is a rearrangement of the name *[Dodinas](Dodinas_Brandybuck.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

*Dodynas* and *Dinodes* are both alternate spellings of the Table Knight Sir Dodinas le Savage.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier versions of the family tree of the Brandybuck family he was called *Priamus*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
