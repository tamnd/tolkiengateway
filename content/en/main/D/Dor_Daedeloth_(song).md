<i><b>Dor Daedeloth</b></i> is the eleventh and final track of the album [Minas Morgul](<../M/Minas_Morgul_(album).md>) by the band [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md). The song name is taken from [Dor Daedeloth](Dor_Daedeloth.md), the land of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md)*.* The lyrics reference the region itself, the [Iron Mountains](../I/Iron_Mountains.md), and the [Mountains of Terror](../E/Ered_Gorgoroth.md).

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>My heart for your word, Ered Engrin, <br/>
and watch your folk, tired and small <br/>
all hope lies in its last breath... <br/>
You won&#39;t fear us, for we won&#39;t bring you anything <br/>
but a fading spirit, <br/>
the essence of my frost cries... <br/>
<br/>
frost and ice destroy... <br/>
<br/>
My heart for your word, Dor Daedeloth, <br/>
You have seen the journey of time, and let us, <br/>
half mortal beings, take part of the truth <br/>
and the solitude of mankind, forewer... <br/>
<br/>
Wherever we go, wherever we stand, silence <br/>
covers the blood soaken land, <br/>
and so we pass the mountains of Ered Gorgoroth. <br/>
<br/>
Five shillvets are crossing the plains <br/>
to the wide open forests of the west <br/>
on and on they are travelling, in <br/>
the direction of Hithlum <br/>
called Nebelland, that made me <br/>
asking for eternity...
</p>
