**Denis Bridoux** is an author and member of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society). Born in France, his interest in Tolkien led him to studying English in university and eventually moving to West Yorkshire. As an organizer of the Amon Sûl Smial, he edited the [Weathertop](</wiki/Weathertop_(journal)> "Weathertop (journal)") and [Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)") journals.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
