The **Dwarves of the Blue Mountains** were the various [Dwarven](Dwarves.md) cultures that settled the [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md).

## History {#History}

### Elder Days {#Elder_Days}

According to Dwarvish traditions the ancestors of the kindreds of dwarves of the [Firebeards](../F/Firebeards.md) and [Broadbeams](../B/Broadbeams.md) awoke in the north of the Blue Mountains<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) after the [Awakening of the Elves](../A/Awakening_of_the_Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is presumed that these two kindreds of dwarves built the great underground Dwarven cities of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) and [Belegost](../B/Belegost.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Dwarves_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwarves-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

The Dwarves built their great mansions in the mountains, commanding the only passes. These had probably been built even before the arrival of the Eldar into Beleriand on their [Great Journey](../G/Great_Journey.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

From their cities, the Dwarves of the Blue Mountains came down into [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) to trade with the Elves. These Dwarves were friends to the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Sindar_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindar-6">[5]</a></sup> and later allies of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> They played a great part in the making of [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md)'s halls at [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md), and later aided in the delving of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) beside [Narog](../N/Narog.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-8">[7]</a></sup> [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) rewarded them with treasures he brought from [Tirion](../T/Tirion.md). It was during this time that the Dwarves of the Blue Mountains created the [Nauglamír](../N/Nauglamír.md) for [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md), the most renowned of their works of the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-8">[7]</a></sup>

After [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s return to Middle-earth, the Dwarves were loosely [allied](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md) with the Elves in the [Wars of Beleriand](../W/Wars_of_Beleriand.md) that followed. They fought in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), where [Azaghâl](../A/Azaghâl.md) of [Belegost](../B/Belegost.md) drove [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) from the field but was himself slain. After the battle, relations between the Elves and Dwarves cooled; the [Dwarves of Nogrod](Dwarves_of_Nogrod.md) slew Thingol and stole the [Nauglamír](../N/Nauglamír.md), and after this time, enmity and mistrust grew between the two peoples.

### Later History {#Later_History}

The cities of Nogrod and Belegost were ruined when [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md) was broken in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md). As a result, around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [40](../S/Second_Age_40.md) many dwarves left Nogroad and Belegost and moved to [Khazad-dûm](../K/Khazad-dûm.md)<sup id="cite_ref-B1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B1-9">[8]</a></sup> bringing their lore and craft with them,<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-10">[9]</a></sup> however, there always remained some Dwarves on the eastern side of the Blue Mountains in days afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

After the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs.md), [Thráin II](../T/Thráin_II.md) and his people established a [new realm-in-exile](../T/Thorin's_Halls.md) in the northern Ered Luin beyond the [Little Lune](../L/Little_Lune.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-10">[9]</a></sup> His people prospered in a fashion and were swelled in numbers by many of the wandering folk of Durin and they built many fair houses in the hills.<sup id="cite_ref-Dwarves_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwarves-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Durin_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-10">[9]</a></sup> Years later, after King [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) went on the [Quest of Erebor](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md) and was killed in the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md), the [Kingdom under the Mountain](../K/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain.md) was re-established by the new king [Dáin II Ironfoot](Dáin_Ironfoot.md). Many of the people of Durin's folk went to him and it became a great and prosperous kingdom once again.

Even later in the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md), Dwarves still lived in the east side of the Blue Mountains, especially south of the [Gulf of Lune](../G/Gulf_of_Lune.md) where they had active mines, and still travelled east along the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>

<b>2013: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>While <a href="../T/Thorin_and_Company.md" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and Company</a> are lost in <a href="../M/Mirkwood.md" title="Mirkwood">Mirkwood</a>, <a href="../B/Bofur.md" title="Bofur">Bofur</a> finds a dwarven tobacco pouch on the ground, which he recognizes as one from the Dwarves of the Blue Mountains. <a href="../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo</a> tells him that it is his own pouch that fell earlier, and they are going on circles.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>It is possible for Dwarf player characters to choose the Blue Mountains as their background, though this is only for aesthetic purposes and has no impact on the game&#39;s story or mechanics.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The Blue Mountain dwarves in the late Third Age are among the first non-player characters beginning Dwarf and Elf characters meet, and consist of both the noble Longbeards of Thorin&#39;s Hall and the villainous &#34;<a href="Dourhands.md" title="Dourhands">Dourhands</a>&#34;, renegades from the two Blue Mountain Houses. A later update introduced the &#34;Narfanghoth&#34; and the &#34;Landorrim&#34;, the game&#39;s representations of the Firebeards and Broadbeams.</dd></dl>
