The **Dwarves of the Blue Mountains** were the various [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") cultures that settled the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin).

## History {#History}

### Elder Days {#Elder_Days}

According to Dwarvish traditions the ancestors of the kindreds of dwarves of the [Firebeards](/wiki/Firebeards) and [Broadbeams](/wiki/Broadbeams) awoke in the north of the Blue Mountains<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) after the [Awakening of the Elves](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is presumed that these two kindreds of dwarves built the great underground Dwarven cities of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) and [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost).<sup id="cite_ref-Dwarves_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwarves-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

The Dwarves built their great mansions in the mountains, commanding the only passes. These had probably been built even before the arrival of the Eldar into Beleriand on their [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

From their cities, the Dwarves of the Blue Mountains came down into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) to trade with the Elves. These Dwarves were friends to the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar)<sup id="cite_ref-Sindar_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindar-6">[5]</a></sup> and later allies of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> They played a great part in the making of [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol)'s halls at [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth), and later aided in the delving of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) beside [Narog](/wiki/Narog).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-8">[7]</a></sup> [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) rewarded them with treasures he brought from [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion). It was during this time that the Dwarves of the Blue Mountains created the [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr) for [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod), the most renowned of their works of the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-8">[7]</a></sup>

After [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s return to Middle-earth, the Dwarves were loosely [allied](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros "Union of Maedhros") with the Elves in the [Wars of Beleriand](/wiki/Wars_of_Beleriand) that followed. They fought in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), where [Azaghâl](/wiki/Azagh%C3%A2l) of [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost) drove [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) from the field but was himself slain. After the battle, relations between the Elves and Dwarves cooled; the [Dwarves of Nogrod](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Nogrod) slew Thingol and stole the [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr), and after this time, enmity and mistrust grew between the two peoples.

### Later History {#Later_History}

The cities of Nogrod and Belegost were ruined when [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim) was broken in the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath). As a result, around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [40](/wiki/Second_Age_40 "Second Age 40") many dwarves left Nogroad and Belegost and moved to [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm)<sup id="cite_ref-B1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B1-9">[8]</a></sup> bringing their lore and craft with them,<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-10">[9]</a></sup> however, there always remained some Dwarves on the eastern side of the Blue Mountains in days afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

After the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs), [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_II) and his people established a [new realm-in-exile](/wiki/Thorin%27s_Halls "Thorin's Halls") in the northern Ered Luin beyond the [Little Lune](/wiki/Little_Lune).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-10">[9]</a></sup> His people prospered in a fashion and were swelled in numbers by many of the wandering folk of Durin and they built many fair houses in the hills.<sup id="cite_ref-Dwarves_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwarves-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Durin_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-10">[9]</a></sup> Years later, after King [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) went on the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor) and was killed in the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies), the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) was re-established by the new king [Dáin II Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot"). Many of the people of Durin's folk went to him and it became a great and prosperous kingdom once again.

Even later in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), Dwarves still lived in the east side of the Blue Mountains, especially south of the [Gulf of Lune](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune) where they had active mines, and still travelled east along the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The Blue Mountain dwarves in the late Third Age are among the first non-player characters beginning Dwarf and Elf characters meet, and consist of both the noble Longbeards of Thorin&#39;s Hall and the villainous &#34;<a href="/wiki/Dourhands" title="Dourhands">Dourhands</a>&#34;, renegades from the two Blue Mountain Houses. A later update introduced the &#34;Narfanghoth&#34; and the &#34;Landorrim&#34;, the game&#39;s representations of the Firebeards and Broadbeams.</dd></dl>
