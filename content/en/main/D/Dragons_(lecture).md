<i><b>Dragons</b></i>, also called **Lecture on Dragons** or **Essay on Dragons**, is a lecture by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). It is referenced as "Ms. Tolk. A61. fols. 98-125" in the Tolkien's papers conserved at the [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library).

The lecture was originally given on [1 January](/wiki/1_January) [1938](/wiki/1938) to children at the [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford) Museum. Tolkien described two kinds of dragons, "'creeping' and 'winged' but, in general, large, deadly, coiling serpent-creatures".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Extended extracts were published by [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) in *[Leaves from the Tree](/wiki/Leaves_from_the_Tree)* ([1991](/wiki/1991)), by [Wayne Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_Hammond) and Christina Scull in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Artist_and_Illustrator)* ([1995](/wiki/1995)) and in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide)* ([2006](/wiki/2006)), and by [John Rateliff](/wiki/John_Rateliff) in *[The History of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit)* ([2007](/wiki/2007)). In [2018](/wiki/2018), the lecture was published in full, included in *[The Hobbit Facsimile Gift Edition](/wiki/The_Hobbit_Facsimile_Gift_Edition)*. It is also included in an 88-paged [booklet](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(booklet)> "The Hobbit (booklet)") that comes with the [2023](/wiki/2023) hardcover <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Illustrated_by_the_Author&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Illustrated by the Author (page does not exist)">Illustrated by the Author</a> deluxe edition.
