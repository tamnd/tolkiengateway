<i><b>Dragons</b></i>, also called **Lecture on Dragons** or **Essay on Dragons**, is a lecture by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). It is referenced as "Ms. Tolk. A61. fols. 98-125" in the Tolkien's papers conserved at the [Bodleian Library](../B/Bodleian_Library.md).

The lecture was originally given on [1 January](../_/1_January.md) [1938](../_/1938.md) to children at the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md) Museum. Tolkien described two kinds of dragons, "'creeping' and 'winged' but, in general, large, deadly, coiling serpent-creatures".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Extended extracts were published by [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) in *[Leaves from the Tree](../L/Leaves_from_the_Tree.md)* ([1991](../_/1991.md)), by [Wayne Hammond](../W/Wayne_Hammond.md) and Christina Scull in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_Artist_and_Illustrator.md)* ([1995](../_/1995.md)) and in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md)* ([2006](../_/2006.md)), and by [John Rateliff](../J/John_Rateliff.md) in *[The History of The Hobbit](../T/The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)* ([2007](../_/2007.md)). In [2018](../_/2018.md), the lecture was published in full, included in *[The Hobbit Facsimile Gift Edition](../T/The_Hobbit_Facsimile_Gift_Edition.md)*. It is also included in an 88-paged [booklet](<../T/The_Hobbit_(booklet).md>) that comes with the [2023](../_/2023.md) hardcover <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Illustrated_by_the_Author&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Illustrated by the Author (page does not exist)">Illustrated by the Author</a> deluxe edition.
