**Dodinas Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Dodinas was the fourth child of [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck) and [Mirabella Took](/wiki/Mirabella_Took), born between [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1308](/wiki/Third_Age_2908 "Third Age 2908") and [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1313](/wiki/Third_Age_2913 "Third Age 2913"). He seems to have died before [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001") as he was not present at [Bilbo's](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Dodinas* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

In Malory's *Le Morte Darthur* a Knight of the Round Table is called *Dodynas le Saveage*. In editions with modernized spelling he is called *Dodinas*. He is called *Doddinaual de Sauage* in *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight](</wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(poem)> "Sir Gawain and the Green Knight (poem)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
