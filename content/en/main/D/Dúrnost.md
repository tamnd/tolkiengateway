**Dúrnost** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Dark Fortress*) was a fortress that was located within [Forodwaith](/wiki/Northern_Waste "Northern Waste"). Dúrnost was invented by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*.

## History {#History}

After the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) and the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) took up residency at the fortress of Dúrnost deep within the region of [Forodwaith](/wiki/Northern_Waste "Northern Waste"), far to the north of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). He sought to create a power over flesh derived from the [Unseen](/wiki/Unseen) world. As such, Sauron issued forth a call and many of [Morgoth's](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") former servants answered. Among whom, [Adar](</wiki/Adar_(The_Rings_of_Power)> "Adar (The Rings of Power)") was one of them.

During this time at Dúrnost, Sauron conducted experiments on many of the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) in order to tap into the [Unseen](/wiki/Unseen) world. Unfortunately, Adar eventually became became disillusioned with the Dark Lord's frequent sacrifices of the Orcs to create a power over flesh. As a result, Adar rebelled against Sauron, attaining the belief that he split him open. Dúrnost became abandoned sometime after this, save only for at least one [Hristorco](/wiki/Snow-trolls "Snow-trolls").

During the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) lead a company of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) to investigate Dúrnost deep within Forodwaith, the last location marked on her map where Sauron could possibly be hiding.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> It was only when the company gave up hope and [Thondir](/wiki/Thondir) confronted her to get her to leave, that Dúrnost was revealed in a lightning storm.

While investigating the inside of Dúrnost, Galadriel comes upon a filled in door leading to an anvil bearing the same sigil as the one that Sauron had engraved onto [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod), proving without a doubt that Sauron certainly once operated within Dúrnost long ago. Unfortunately, the investigation was cut short when the company was ambushed by a Hristorco that was stalking them within Dúrnost. After the slaying of the Hristorco, Galadriel attempts to resume her expedition further north. However, the other elves, led by Thondir, laid their swords down, refusing to go any further north and Galadriel was forced to return to the [High King Gil-Galad](/wiki/Gil-galad "Gil-galad").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dúrnost* is clearly meant to be [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), a combination of *[dúr](/wiki/D%C3%BBr "Dûr")* ("dark, sombre") + *[ost](/wiki/Ost "Ost")* ("fort, fortress, citadel").
