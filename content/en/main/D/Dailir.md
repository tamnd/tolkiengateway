**Dailir** was the name of the arrow used by [Beleg Strongbow](/wiki/Beleg "Beleg") with his bow, [Belthronding](/wiki/Belthronding). After shooting this arrow Beleg always found it unharmed. However, it was broken at last when Beleg fell upon it when he was carrying [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin).<sup id="cite_ref-Dailir_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dailir-1">[1]</a></sup> This arrow is only featured in the first version of *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*dailir* is glossed "cleaver" in the Noldorin Word-lists, and is related to the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) verb *daila-* ("to cleave") and the noun *dail* ("axe-blade"). The *-ir* suffix creates an "agent noun" (such as adding 'r' to 'bite' in English produces 'biter').<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
