**David Leslie Edward Hemmings** ([November 18](/wiki/18_November "18 November"), [1941](/wiki/1941) - [December 3](/wiki/3_December "3 December"), [2003](/wiki/2003)) was a British actor. In later life, he was known for his distinctive eyebrows and booming voice, in films such as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gladiator_(2000_film)" title="wikipedia:Gladiator (2000 film)">Gladiator</a></i>, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangs_of_New_York" title="wikipedia:Gangs of New York">Gangs of New York</a></i> and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_League_of_Extraordinary_Gentlemen_(film)" title="wikipedia:The League of Extraordinary Gentlemen (film)">The League of Extraordinary Gentlemen</a></i>.

In his youth, however, he was a boy soprano, and at the age of 14, he provided the voice of [Bergil](/wiki/Bergil) in [the 1956 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)") of *The Lord of the Rings*.
