The **Desolation of the Dragon**,<sup id="cite_ref-Doorstep_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doorstep-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Fire_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-2">[2]</a></sup> also known as the **Desolation of Smaug**,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> was a bleak and barren desert around [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") and [Dale](/wiki/Dale), charred and blackened by the burning breath of [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) in the days of the dragon Smaug.<sup id="cite_ref-Doorstep_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doorstep-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The borders of the Desolation harboured some birds,<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-2">[2]</a></sup> but there was little grass, neither trees nor bushes, only broken and blackened stumps at its heart.<sup id="cite_ref-Doorstep_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doorstep-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The area that later became known as the Desolation of the Dragon had been green and fair before the arrival of the dragon<sup id="cite_ref-Doorstep_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doorstep-1">[1]</a></sup> and probably encompassed arable lands to produce food for the city of Dale.

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770") the dragon Smaug attacked Erebor and destroyed the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) and the city of Dale<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and probably started to create the Desolation of the Dragon by burning the landscape with his fiery breath.

After [Bard](/wiki/Bard) killed Smaug in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> the desolation recovered, the town of Dale was rebuilt in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2944](/wiki/Third_Age_2944 "Third Age 2944"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> the fields in the valley of the [River Running](/wiki/River_Running) were tilled again and the former desolation was filled with birds and blossoms in spring and with fruits in autumn.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
