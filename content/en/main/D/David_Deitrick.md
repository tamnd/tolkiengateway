**David Deitrick** (born [15 May](/wiki/15_May) [1953](/wiki/1953)) is an American artist. In the 1980s and 1990s, he illustrated for countless games and magazines. In that capacity, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to make card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.

He is married to fellow artist [Lori Deitrick](/wiki/Lori_Deitrick).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
