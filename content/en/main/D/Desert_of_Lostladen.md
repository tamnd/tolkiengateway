The **Desert of Lostladen** was a region between [Nurn](../N/Nurn.md) and [Near Harad](../N/Near_Harad.md), just south of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). Nothing else is known of the desert, and the name is only found pencilled on a map made for *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In an early sketch of the chapter "[The Last Debate](../T/The_Last_Debate.md)", the region was called the Lothland desert. [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) has therefore noted that the name *Lostladen* is likely related to the names *[Lothlann](../L/Lothlann.md)*/*Lothland* found in the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md) (cf. [roots](../S/Sundocarme.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=LAD&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LAD (page does not exist)">LAD</a> and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=LUS&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LUS (page does not exist)">LUS</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
