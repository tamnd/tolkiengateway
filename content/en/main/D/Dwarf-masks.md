**Dwarf-masks** were great gilded helmets made in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) by the [Dwarves of the Blue Mountains](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains).

## History {#History}

The [Dwarves of Belegost](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Belegost) appeared within the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad "Nirnaeth Arnoediad") wearing masks they were resistant to fire, as according to their custom.<sup id="cite_ref-fifth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fifth-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Elves of Nargothrond](/wiki/Elves_of_Nargothrond) were in possession of dwarf-masks, as well as [dwarf-mail](/wiki/Dwarf-mail "Dwarf-mail"); [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) [Mormegil](/wiki/Mormegil) found an all-gilded mask within the armories of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), which he wore before going to battle.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> At the [Battle of Tumhalad](/wiki/Battle_of_Tumhalad) the dwarf-mask protected him from [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
