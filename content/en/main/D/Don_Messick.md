**Donald Messick** ([September 7](/wiki/7_September "7 September"), [1926](/wiki/1926) - [October 24](/wiki/24_October "24 October"), [1997](/wiki/1997)) was an American voice actor. A legend in voice acting, Messick is remembered for iconic roles such as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scooby-Doo_(character)" title="wikipedia:Scooby-Doo (character)">Scooby-Doo</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papa_Smurf" title="wikipedia:Papa Smurf">Papa Smurf</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Droopy" title="wikipedia:Droopy">Droopy</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atom_Ant" title="wikipedia:Atom Ant">Atom Ant</a> and a variety of animals (a market now owned by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Welker" title="wikipedia:Frank Welker">Frank Welker</a> and [Dee Bradley Baker](/wiki/Dee_Bradley_Baker)).
