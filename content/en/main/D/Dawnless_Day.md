The **Dawnless Day**, also known as the **Storm of Mordor**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and **Darkness Unescapable**,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> refers to [10 March](../_/10_March.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-3">[3]</a></sup> when fumes from [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) veiled the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) in an attempt by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) to discourage or misguide his enemies. The veil of [Darkness](Darkness.md) blacked out most of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-5">[5]</a></sup> though some beams of light could pass through it.

Along with the Darkness, a host from [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md) set forth toward [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), as witnessed by [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) who passed the [Cross-roads](../C/Cross-roads.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Later [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) from the [Morannon](../M/Morannon.md) took [Cair Andros](../C/Cair_Andros.md) and passed into [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Darkness was also observed by the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) who were mustering their Riders and rode from [Harrowdale](../H/Harrowdale.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-5">[5]</a></sup>

On this day [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) was rescued by [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Imrahil](../I/Imrahil.md) outside the [Great Gate of Minas Tirith](../G/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith.md) and [Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn.md) led the [Dead Men](../O/Oathbreakers.md) and the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md), across the river [Ringló](../R/Ringló.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Darkness was eventually driven away by a south-west wind<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 1]</a></sup> on the morning of [March 15](../_/15_March.md),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup> when [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and his companions came to the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Dawnless Day sprawls over four in-game regions, including all of <a href="../G/Gondor.md" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> east of <a href="../C/Calembel.md" title="Calembel">Calembel</a>, <a href="../A/Anórien.md" title="Anórien">Anórien</a>, South <a href="../I/Ithilien.md" title="Ithilien">Ithilien</a>, <a href="../O/Osgiliath.md" title="Osgiliath">Osgiliath</a> and <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> itself. The sky in those regions is permanently darkened, with in-game day being near-indistinguishable from the nighttime. The common folk of Gondor whom the player meets during this time are almost all in despair about what this new misfortune means for them.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In 2023, the game added another version of Gondor (sans Anórien) chronologically set after the end of the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, allowing the players to experience its lands without the Dawnless Day&#39;s oppressive presence.</dd></dl>
