The **Dawnless Day**, also known as the **Storm of Mordor**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and **Darkness Unescapable**,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> refers to [10 March](/wiki/10_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-3">[3]</a></sup> when fumes from [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) veiled the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) in an attempt by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) to discourage or misguide his enemies. The veil of [Darkness](/wiki/Darkness) blacked out most of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan),<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-5">[5]</a></sup> though some beams of light could pass through it.

Along with the Darkness, a host from [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) set forth toward [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), as witnessed by [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) who passed the [Cross-roads](/wiki/Cross-roads).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Later [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) from the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon) took [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros) and passed into [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Darkness was also observed by the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) who were mustering their Riders and rode from [Harrowdale](/wiki/Harrowdale).<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-5">[5]</a></sup>

On this day [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) was rescued by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil) outside the [Great Gate of Minas Tirith](/wiki/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith) and [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") led the [Dead Men](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") and the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company), across the river [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Darkness was eventually driven away by a south-west wind<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 1]</a></sup> on the morning of [March 15](/wiki/15_March "15 March"),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup> when [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and his companions came to the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Dawnless Day sprawls over four in-game regions, including all of <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> east of <a href="/wiki/Calembel" title="Calembel">Calembel</a>, <a href="/wiki/An%C3%B3rien" title="Anórien">Anórien</a>, South <a href="/wiki/Ithilien" title="Ithilien">Ithilien</a>, <a href="/wiki/Osgiliath" title="Osgiliath">Osgiliath</a> and <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> itself. The sky in those regions is permanently darkened, with in-game day being near-indistinguishable from the nighttime. The common folk of Gondor whom the player meets during this time are almost all in despair about what this new misfortune means for them.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In 2023, the game added another version of Gondor (sans Anórien) chronologically set after the end of the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, allowing the players to experience its lands without the Dawnless Day&#39;s oppressive presence.</dd></dl>
