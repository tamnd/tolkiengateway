The **Dwarves of Nogrod** were the [Dwarven](Dwarves.md) people who dwelled at [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md), one of the two dwarven cities in the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). Along with their close [kinsmen](Dwarves_of_Belegost.md) in [Belegost](../B/Belegost.md), they were among the [Dwarves of the Blue Mountains](Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains.md).

## History {#History}

During the [Years of the Trees](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) the western [Fathers of the Dwarves](../F/Fathers_of_the_Dwarves.md) awoke from beneath the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) creating the lines of the [Broadbeams](../B/Broadbeams.md) and the [Firebeards](../F/Firebeards.md). These two houses built the great Dwarven cities of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) and [Belegost](../B/Belegost.md) in the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>

They were initially friendly to the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of Beleriand, renowned as craftsmen and jewellers, such as [Gamil Zirak](../G/Gamil_Zirak.md) whose works were to be found in the treasuries of King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) of [Doriath](Doriath.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> and [Telchar](../T/Telchar.md) known for the forging of weapons. Dwarves from the Blue Mountains, perhaps also those of Nogrod, aided the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) in the building of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-doriath_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-doriath-5">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

Dwarves of Nogrod, as well as their kinsmen of Belegost, participated in the [Union of Maedhros](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md) supplying them with soldiers and weapons.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

Some craftsmen from Nogrod stayed in [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md) and were asked by King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) of [Doriath](Doriath.md) to set the [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md) in the [Nauglamir](../N/Nauglamir.md). But they coveted the Nauglamir and killed Thingol in his treasury, and fled, only to be slaughtered by the avenging Elves who killed all but two. The survivors returned to Nogrod, and lied to their kinsmen saying that Thingol refused to pay the craftsmen for their work and killed them.

Although the [Dwarves of Belegost](Dwarves_of_Belegost.md) refused to aid them, the Dwarves of Nogrod formed a great host which passed over [Sarn Athrad](../S/Sarn_Athrad.md) intimidating the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) who fled before them. In the [sack of Menegroth](../B/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves.md), they ravaged the Caves and Thingol's treasures. But on their return home they were hunted down by [Beren Erchamion](../B/Beren.md) and killed by his army of [Green-elves](../G/Green-elves.md) and a group of [Ents](../E/Ents.md) at the [Battle of Sarn Athrad](../B/Battle_of_Sarn_Athrad.md) as they were crossing the river.<sup id="cite_ref-doriath_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-doriath-5">[4]</a></sup>

After the end of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [40](../S/Second_Age_40.md), the power and wealth of Khazad-dûm was much increased; for it was enriched by many people and much lore and craft when the ancient cities of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) and [Belegost](../B/Belegost.md) in the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) were ruined in the breaking of [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup> However, there always remained some Dwarves on the eastern side of the Blue Mountains in days afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>
