The **Days before days** were three thousand and five hundred [Valian Years](/wiki/Valian_Years) that passed ere the measurement of time first known to the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) began with the flowering of the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Days before days began shortly after the [Music of the Ainur](/wiki/Music_of_the_Ainur), when the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) descended into [Arda](/wiki/Arda) after labouring in the vast halls of [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4) for ages uncounted and forgotten.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> When they entered the world destined to be the habitation for the [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar), it was still lifeless and had no distinct geographical features. However, [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") claimed Arda for himself alone, and there were the [First War](/wiki/First_War) that ravaged until the coming of [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas) whose prowess urged Melkor to retreat.

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Steven_White_Jr._-_Early_Arda.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/5/52/Steven_White_Jr._-_Early_Arda.jpg/250px-Steven_White_Jr._-_Early_Arda.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="200" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/5/52/Steven_White_Jr._-_Early_Arda.jpg/375px-Steven_White_Jr._-_Early_Arda.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/5/52/Steven_White_Jr._-_Early_Arda.jpg 2x" data-file-width="500" data-file-height="400"/></a><figcaption><i>Early Arda</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Steven_White_Jr." title="Category:Images by Steven White Jr.">Steven White Jr.</a></figcaption></figure>

When the Valar finally repaired damage made by Melkor, they chose the initial shape of Arda; it was of a symmetrical continent lit by the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps): one in the continent's north, and one in the south, made out of the misty light that veiled the barren ground. The Valar concentrated this light into two colossal vessels, [Illuin](/wiki/Illuin) and [Ormal](/wiki/Ormal). Then [Aulë the Smith](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB "Aulë") forged great towers, one in the north, [Helcar](/wiki/Helcar), and another in the south, [Ringil](</wiki/Ringil_(tower)> "Ringil (tower)"). In the middle, where the light of the Lamps mingled, the Valar dwelt at the island of [Almaren](/wiki/Almaren), their first kingdom. Thus the [Spring of Arda](/wiki/Spring_of_Arda) flourished upon the Earth.

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:John_Howe_-_The_Making_of_Middle-earth.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/1/19/John_Howe_-_The_Making_of_Middle-earth.jpg/250px-John_Howe_-_The_Making_of_Middle-earth.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="148" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/1/19/John_Howe_-_The_Making_of_Middle-earth.jpg/375px-John_Howe_-_The_Making_of_Middle-earth.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/1/19/John_Howe_-_The_Making_of_Middle-earth.jpg/500px-John_Howe_-_The_Making_of_Middle-earth.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2083" data-file-height="1233"/></a><figcaption><i>The Making of Middle-earth</i> by <a href="/wiki/John_Howe" title="John Howe">John Howe</a></figcaption></figure>

The Two Lamps shone until they were destroyed by an assault of Melkor. Arda was again darkened, and the Lamps' fall spoiled the perfect symmetry of Arda's surface, and thus the Spring came to an end. Separate continents appeared: [Aman](/wiki/Aman) in the far West, [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), or *Endor*, in the centre from the north to south, and the [Land of the Sun](/wiki/Land_of_the_Sun) in the far East. At the site of the northern Lamp the inland [Sea of Helcar](/wiki/Sea_of_Helcar) later formed, of which [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen) was a bay. After that the reckoning of the [Years of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees) started, when [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) grew the Two Trees in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). However, Middle-earth was still covered in [darkness](/wiki/Darkness "Darkness") and Melkor dominated it residing in his dreadful stronghold [Utumno](/wiki/Utumno).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [J.R.R. Tolkien's](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") latest writings in which Arda was a [round world](/wiki/Round_World_version_of_the_Silmarillion "Round World version of the Silmarillion") from its beginning, the Sun and Moon (and the Two Trees for a time) had always given light to Arda, and the Two Lamps never existed.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

In [David Day](/wiki/David_Day)'s books the period was [fanonically](/wiki/Fanon "Fanon") misnamed "Ages/Years of the Lamps".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> However, J.R.R. Tolkien himself never used these terms.
