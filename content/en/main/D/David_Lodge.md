## Roles {#Roles}

- [2004](/wiki/2004): *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring)*: [Grishnakh](/wiki/Grishnakh), Rohan Captain, Monster Warrior
