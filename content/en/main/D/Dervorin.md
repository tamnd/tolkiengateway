**Dervorin** was the son of the lord of the [Ringló Vale](../R/Ringló_Vale.md).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the prelude of the [siege of Minas Tirith](../S/Siege_of_Gondor.md) on [9 March](../_/9_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Gondorian](../G/Gondorian.md) lords of the [Outlands](../O/Outlands.md) arrived with their troops at [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) to aid in the defence of the City. Dervorin came to Minas Tirith on foot, leading three hundred men.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup>

The actions of Dervorin during the siege of Minas Tirith and the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md) are not mentioned. His name was not mentioned in the list of lords and sons of lords of Gondor, of Rohan or of the [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) who died in the Battle of the Pelennor Fields.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In addition, it is not mentioned if Dervorin went with the [Army of the West](../A/Army_of_the_West.md) to the [Black Gate](../B/Black_Gate.md) of Mordor and particpated in the [Battle of the Morannon](../B/Battle_of_the_Morannon.md) and if he survived the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) and returned to the Ringló Vale.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[David Salo](David_Salo.md) and [Paul Strack](../P/Paul_Strack.md) explain that *Dervorin* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name of unclear meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> According to David Salo, the first element of the name may contain *deru*, possibly from [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) \**nderwā* and the root NDER, which means "manly".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
