**Denethor** can refer to three characters:

- [Denethor](</wiki/Denethor_(Nando)> "Denethor (Nando)"), son of [Lenwë](/wiki/Lenw%C3%AB) and leader of the [Laiquendi](/wiki/Laiquendi) who was killed in the [First Battle of Beleriand](/wiki/First_Battle_of_Beleriand).
- [Denethor I](/wiki/Denethor_I) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2375](/wiki/Third_Age_2375 "Third Age 2375") - [2477](/wiki/Third_Age_2477 "Third Age 2477")), tenth [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).
- **[Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2930](/wiki/Third_Age_2930 "Third Age 2930")- [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")), twenty-sixth Ruling Steward of Gondor during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), and father of [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) and [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir).**
