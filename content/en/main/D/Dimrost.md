**Dimrost**, also known as **Nen Girith**, was a cascading waterfall on the stream of [Celebros](../C/Celebros.md), where it flowed down to meet the River [Taeglin](../T/Taeglin.md) on the borders of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The falls raised a fine spray into the air, from which they took their name, which means "Rainy Stair". Above Dimrost, the [Men](../M/Men.md) of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md) had constructed a wooden bridge to cross the Celebros.

From the bridge, a wide view could be seen, showing the [Ravines of Taeglin](../R/Ravines_of_Taeglin.md) two miles distant.<sup id="cite_ref-Nienor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nienor-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When [Níniel](../N/Nienor.md) was first brought to Brethil, when she saw that view from the bridge, she started shivering uncontrollably - so much so that the name of Dimrost was changed to *Nen Girith*, the "Shuddering Water".<sup id="cite_ref-Nienor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nienor-2">[2]</a></sup> It was only later that the cause of her fear was discovered: the Ravines she had seen from the bridge would be the place of her tragic death.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dimrost* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Rainy Stair", being a combination of *[dim](Dim.md)* ("stair") + *[rost](../R/Rost.md)* ("rain").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

*Nen Girith* is a combination of *[nen](../N/Nen.md)* ("water") and *[girith](../G/Girith.md)* ("shuddering").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, the waterfall is called the **Silver Bowl**, and it has the same use in the narrative, although here Níniel is not sick when first approaches the place, and its name is not changed because of that.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The name **Silver Bowl** appears also on the north part of [The First 'Silmarillion' Map](../T/The_First_'Silmarillion'_Map.md), associated to the River [Taeglin](../T/Taeglin.md) and not to the [Celebros](../C/Celebros.md)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>.
