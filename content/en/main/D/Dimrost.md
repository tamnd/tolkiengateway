**Dimrost**, also known as **Nen Girith**, was a cascading waterfall on the stream of [Celebros](/wiki/Celebros), where it flowed down to meet the River [Taeglin](/wiki/Taeglin) on the borders of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The falls raised a fine spray into the air, from which they took their name, which means "Rainy Stair". Above Dimrost, the [Men](/wiki/Men) of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) had constructed a wooden bridge to cross the Celebros.

From the bridge, a wide view could be seen, showing the [Ravines of Taeglin](/wiki/Ravines_of_Taeglin) two miles distant.<sup id="cite_ref-Nienor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nienor-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When [Níniel](/wiki/Nienor "Nienor") was first brought to Brethil, when she saw that view from the bridge, she started shivering uncontrollably - so much so that the name of Dimrost was changed to *Nen Girith*, the "Shuddering Water".<sup id="cite_ref-Nienor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nienor-2">[2]</a></sup> It was only later that the cause of her fear was discovered: the Ravines she had seen from the bridge would be the place of her tragic death.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dimrost* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Rainy Stair", being a combination of *[dim](/wiki/Dim "Dim")* ("stair") + *[rost](/wiki/Rost "Rost")* ("rain").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

*Nen Girith* is a combination of *[nen](/wiki/Nen "Nen")* ("water") and *[girith](/wiki/Girith "Girith")* ("shuddering").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the waterfall is called the **Silver Bowl**, and it has the same use in the narrative, although here Níniel is not sick when first approaches the place, and its name is not changed because of that.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The name **Silver Bowl** appears also on the north part of [The First 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_First_%27Silmarillion%27_Map), associated to the River [Taeglin](/wiki/Taeglin) and not to the [Celebros](/wiki/Celebros)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>.
