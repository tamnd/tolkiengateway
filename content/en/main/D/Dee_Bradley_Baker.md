**Dee Bradley Baker** (1962) is an American voice actor and he is the go-to-guy when it comes to animal sounds. He is considered to be one of the most versatile and requested actors in Los Angeles.

## Roles {#Roles}

- [2014](/wiki/2014): *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)* - Creature voices
- [2006](/wiki/2006): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II)* - [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum), orcs, cattle
- [2004](/wiki/2004): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth)* - Orcs, cattle
- [2003](/wiki/2003): [*The Hobbit* (2003 video game)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)> "The Hobbit (2003 video game)") - Additional voices
