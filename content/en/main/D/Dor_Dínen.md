**Dor Dínen**, the **Silent Land**, was a small, roughly triangular region to the east of [Nan Dungortheb](/wiki/Nan_Dungortheb) that lay between the upper courses of the rivers [Esgalduin](/wiki/Esgalduin) and [Aros](/wiki/Aros). It was crossed by a road passing from the stone bridge of [Iant Iaur](/wiki/Iant_Iaur) on Esgalduin to the ford of the [Arossiach](/wiki/Arossiach) on Aros. Its southern edge met the forests of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath). The Arossiach led to the northern marches of [Himlad](/wiki/Himlad) to the east.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dor Dínen* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and comes from *[dor](/wiki/Dor "Dor")* "land" and *[dínen](/wiki/D%C3%ADnen "Dínen")* "silent".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Earlier versions of the legendarium {#Earlier_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a passage of the text *Maeglin* written in *c.* 1970, *Dor Dínen* was spelled <i><b>Dor Dhínen</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>. <span></span>
