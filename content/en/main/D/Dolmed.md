**Mount Dolmed** was a mountain in the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Blue_Mountains "Blue Mountains").

## History {#History}

Mount Dolmed loomed over the only known pass from [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). According to the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), it was here that the founders of the [Broadbeams](/wiki/Broadbeams) and the [Firebeards](/wiki/Firebeards) established the Dwarven cities of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) and [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost).

The two cities were established on the eastern side of the mountain. A little to the northeast of Dolmed was Belegost, and a little to the southeast was Nogrod.

In [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1497](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1497 "Year of the Trees 1497"), the [First Battle of Beleriand](/wiki/First_Battle_of_Beleriand) was fought between the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and the forces of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth). In the end, the Elves were victorious, and the surviving [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) fled east toward the Ered Luin. There they were met and annihilated by the Dwarves.

Almost five hundred years later, the [battle in the Thousand Caves](/wiki/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves "Battle in the Thousand Caves") occurred between the [Elves of Doriath](/wiki/Elves_of_Doriath) and the [Dwarves of Nogrod](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Nogrod). In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [503](/wiki/First_Age_503 "First Age 503"), after returning from their victory in [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth), the Dwarves were [ambushed](/wiki/Battle_of_Sarn_Athrad "Battle of Sarn Athrad") and slaughtered at [Sarn Athrad](/wiki/Sarn_Athrad). After the battle, surviving Dwarves climbed the slopes of Mount Dolmed and were waylaid and slain by [Ents](/wiki/Ents).

## Fate {#Fate}

Some [Tolkienists](/wiki/Tolkienists) such as [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) and [Karen Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Fonstad) speculate that after the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), Dolmed was destroyed when the Ered Luin were broken and the [Gulf of Lune](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune) broke through it.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

However, [Ronald E. Kyrmse](/wiki/Ronald_Kyrmse "Ronald Kyrmse")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and later [Didier Willis](/wiki/Didier_Willis)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> independently noted a prominent unidentified mountain exactly on the location of Dolmed in the map of Beleriand as well as Mount [Rerir](/wiki/Rerir) to the north. This, coupled with a reinterpretation of Tolkien's maps, shows that Dolmed at least partially survived the devastation.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dolmed* contains the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) words *[dol](/wiki/Dol "Dol")* ("head") and *mêd* (wet)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>. The word *dol* is used often in Sindarin to refer to hills.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early version, the mountain was called *Mount Dolm*<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>.
