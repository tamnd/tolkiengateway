**Dale** can each refer to:

- **[Dale](/wiki/Dale)**, a valley in [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) that was also the name of a realm and a town.
- **[East Dales](/wiki/Eastfold "Eastfold")**, a region of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).
- **[Men of Dale](/wiki/Men_of_Dale)**, the inhabitants of Dale.
- **[Lord of Dale](/wiki/Lord_of_Dale)**, a [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) title.
- **[King of Dale](/wiki/King_of_Dale)**, a Mannish title.
- **[Language of Dale](/wiki/Language_of_Dale)**, a [Northern Mannish](/wiki/Northern_Mannish) language.
- **[Battle of Dale](/wiki/Battle_of_Dale)**, a battle during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/The_Dale-lands" title="The Dale-lands">The Dale-lands</a></b></i>, a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
