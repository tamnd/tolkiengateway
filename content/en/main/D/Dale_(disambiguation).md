**Dale** can each refer to:

- **[Dale](Dale.md)**, a valley in [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) that was also the name of a realm and a town.
- **[East Dales](../E/Eastfold.md)**, a region of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).
- **[Men of Dale](../M/Men_of_Dale.md)**, the inhabitants of Dale.
- **[Lord of Dale](../L/Lord_of_Dale.md)**, a [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) title.
- **[King of Dale](../K/King_of_Dale.md)**, a Mannish title.
- **[Language of Dale](../L/Language_of_Dale.md)**, a [Northern Mannish](../N/Northern_Mannish.md) language.
- **[Battle of Dale](../B/Battle_of_Dale.md)**, a battle during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).
- <i><b><a href="../T/The_Dale-lands.md" title="The Dale-lands">The Dale-lands</a></b></i>, a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
