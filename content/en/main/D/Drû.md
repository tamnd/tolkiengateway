**Drû** (pl. **Drúin**, coll. **Drúath**) is the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name for the mysterious people of [Men](../M/Men.md) who became known as the [Woses](../W/Woses.md) in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).

## Usage of plural {#Usage_of_plural}

- *Drúin* is the simple plural of the word.
- The collective *Drúath* was modified as early as the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) when it became known that they were enemies of the orcs. Later used to refer to a large number of the Drúedain.
- *[Drúedain](Drúedain.md)* refers to the race as a whole.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

This Sindarin word comes from *[Drughu](Drughu.md)* the [Drúedain](Drúedain.md)'s own name for themselves.

Since [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) languages don't have *gh* it was derived as *drughu &gt; dru'u &gt; druu &gt; drû*
