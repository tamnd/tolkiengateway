**Drû** (pl. **Drúin**, coll. **Drúath**) is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for the mysterious people of [Men](/wiki/Men) who became known as the [Woses](/wiki/Woses) in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

## Usage of plural {#Usage_of_plural}

- *Drúin* is the simple plural of the word.
- The collective *Drúath* was modified as early as the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) when it became known that they were enemies of the orcs. Later used to refer to a large number of the Drúedain.
- *[Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain)* refers to the race as a whole.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

This Sindarin word comes from *[Drughu](/wiki/Drughu)* the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain)'s own name for themselves.

Since [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) languages don't have *gh* it was derived as *drughu &gt; dru'u &gt; druu &gt; drû*
