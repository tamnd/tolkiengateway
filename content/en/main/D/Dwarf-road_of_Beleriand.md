The **Dwarf-road** was the road built by the [Dwarves of the Blue Mountains](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains) as a trade route to the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-1">[1]</a></sup> From the cities of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) and [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> it passed under the shoulders of [Mount Dolmed](/wiki/Dolmed "Dolmed")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and then ran westward along the north bank of the [River Ascar](/wiki/Ascar "Ascar") for many miles, and entered [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand) where it crossed the [River Gelion](/wiki/Gelion "Gelion") at the Ford of [Sarn Athrad](/wiki/Sarn_Athrad).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod_Felagund) used the Dwarf-road to cross the Gelion at Sarn Athrad before crossing the upper part of the River [Ascar](/wiki/Ascar) just before he discovered the first [Men](/wiki/Men) (the people of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor)) to come west beyond the [Ered Lindon](/wiki/Blue_Mountains "Blue Mountains"). Soon other Men were using the Dwarf-road to enter the western lands: First came the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin), who turned northward into [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion). Next, [Marach](/wiki/Marach) brought his people down the Dwarf-road to settle south and east of the dwellings of [Baran](/wiki/Baran) son of Bëor.

In time [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), deciding to assail these Men, sent an Orc-raid to the east that then used the passes of the Dwarf-road to fall upon the Haladin.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the sack of [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth) the Dwarves were marching east upon the Dwarf-road when they were attacked in the [Battle of Sarn Athrad](/wiki/Battle_of_Sarn_Athrad).<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-1">[1]</a></sup>
