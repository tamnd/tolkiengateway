The **Dwarf-road** was the road built by the [Dwarves of the Blue Mountains](Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains.md) as a trade route to the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) and [Doriath](Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-1">[1]</a></sup> From the cities of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) and [Belegost](../B/Belegost.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> it passed under the shoulders of [Mount Dolmed](Dolmed.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and then ran westward along the north bank of the [River Ascar](../A/Ascar.md) for many miles, and entered [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md) where it crossed the [River Gelion](../G/Gelion.md) at the Ford of [Sarn Athrad](../S/Sarn_Athrad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod_Felagund.md) used the Dwarf-road to cross the Gelion at Sarn Athrad before crossing the upper part of the River [Ascar](../A/Ascar.md) just before he discovered the first [Men](../M/Men.md) (the people of [Bëor](../B/Bëor.md)) to come west beyond the [Ered Lindon](../B/Blue_Mountains.md). Soon other Men were using the Dwarf-road to enter the western lands: First came the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md), who turned northward into [Thargelion](../T/Thargelion.md). Next, [Marach](../M/Marach.md) brought his people down the Dwarf-road to settle south and east of the dwellings of [Baran](../B/Baran.md) son of Bëor.

In time [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), deciding to assail these Men, sent an Orc-raid to the east that then used the passes of the Dwarf-road to fall upon the Haladin.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the sack of [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md) the Dwarves were marching east upon the Dwarf-road when they were attacked in the [Battle of Sarn Athrad](../B/Battle_of_Sarn_Athrad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-1">[1]</a></sup>
