**Dwarrowdelf** is an epic/atmospheric black metal band from Southampton, England. The solo project of the musician Tom O'Dell, it takes its name from the Dwarrowdelf, the Westron name for [Khazad-dûm](../K/Khazad-dûm.md) (Moria). Its 2018 album *The Sons of Fëanor* devotes a track to each of the seven sons of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md).
