**Daisy Gamgee** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2972](/wiki/Third_Age_2972 "Third Age 2972") - ?) was the oldest daughter of [Hamfast Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee") and [Bell Goodchild](/wiki/Bell_Gamgee "Bell Gamgee"). She had two older brothers, [Hamson Gamgee](/wiki/Hamson_Gamgee), and [Halfred Gamgee](/wiki/Halfred_Gamgee) as well as three younger siblings: [May Gamgee](/wiki/May_Gamgee), [Marigold Gamgee](/wiki/Marigold_Gamgee), and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), the companion of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy" title="wikipedia:Daisy">Daisy</a>, a flower name, derives from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *dæges eage* "day's eye".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) version of the name was <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Eirien&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Eirien (page does not exist)">Eirien</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
