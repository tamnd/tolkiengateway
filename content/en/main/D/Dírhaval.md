**Dírhaval** was a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min)<sup id="cite_ref-Index_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-2">[2]</a></sup> of the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador) who lived among the exiles and refugees at the [Havens of Sirion](/wiki/Havens_of_Sirion) during the closing century of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), at the time of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil).

## History {#History}

Dírhaval was known as a [poet](/wiki/Minstrels "Minstrels"), though he only produced a single poem in the [Minlamad thent/estent](/wiki/Minlamad_thent/estent), one of the three Elvish [Verse Modes](/wiki/Verse_Modes). At the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion), he drew upon “all the tidings and lore” <sup id="cite_ref-WJII_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJII-1">[1]</a></sup> of the people of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min), [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), and other lands of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). Dirhaval also learned from people such as [Mablung](/wiki/Mablung) and [Andvír](/wiki/Andv%C3%ADr). He compiled and wrote it all in the *[Narn i Chîn Húrin](</wiki/Narn_i_Ch%C3%AEn_H%C3%BArin_(tale)> "Narn i Chîn Húrin (tale)")* ("Tale of the Children of Húrin"), telling of the tragedy of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s curse on [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin)'s children: [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) and [Nienor](/wiki/Nienor). It was written in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), in which he had great skill, and it was the longest of all the lays to come out of Beleriand.

Dírhaval did not write other poems or lays, as the [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) [captured](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril "Quest for the Silmaril") by [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) was in the keeping of [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing): soon the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) launched an attack on the exiles in the Havens of Sirion to claim it. Dírhaval was one of those who fell in the [Third Kinslaying](/wiki/Third_Kinslaying).<sup id="cite_ref-WJII_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJII-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UT_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first draft of the introductory note, Tolkien's original spelling of the name *Dírhaval* was **Dírhavel**.<sup id="cite_ref-WJII_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJII-1">[1]</a></sup>

Christopher Tolkien used the earlier spelling within the [Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales), but notes in [The War of the Jewels](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels) that the name *Dírhavel* should have been spelled as *Dírhaval* since the latter spelling was the latest variation of the name.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
