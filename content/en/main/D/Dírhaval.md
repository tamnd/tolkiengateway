**Dírhaval** was a [Man](../M/Men.md) of [Dor-lómin](Dor-lómin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Index_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-2">[2]</a></sup> of the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md) who lived among the exiles and refugees at the [Havens of Sirion](../H/Havens_of_Sirion.md) during the closing century of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), at the time of [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md).

## History {#History}

Dírhaval was known as a [poet](../M/Minstrels.md), though he only produced a single poem in the [Minlamad thent/estent](../M/Minlamad_thent%252Festent.md), one of the three Elvish [Verse Modes](../V/Verse_Modes.md). At the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md), he drew upon “all the tidings and lore” <sup id="cite_ref-WJII_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJII-1">[1]</a></sup> of the people of [Dor-lómin](Dor-lómin.md), [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md), [Doriath](Doriath.md), and other lands of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). Dirhaval also learned from people such as [Mablung](../M/Mablung.md) and [Andvír](../A/Andvír.md). He compiled and wrote it all in the *[Narn i Chîn Húrin](<../N/Narn_i_Chîn_Húrin_(tale).md>)* ("Tale of the Children of Húrin"), telling of the tragedy of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s curse on [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md)'s children: [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) and [Nienor](../N/Nienor.md). It was written in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), in which he had great skill, and it was the longest of all the lays to come out of Beleriand.

Dírhaval did not write other poems or lays, as the [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md) [captured](../Q/Quest_for_the_Silmaril.md) by [Beren](../B/Beren.md) and [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) was in the keeping of [Elwing](../E/Elwing.md): soon the [Sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) launched an attack on the exiles in the Havens of Sirion to claim it. Dírhaval was one of those who fell in the [Third Kinslaying](../T/Third_Kinslaying.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJII_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJII-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UT_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first draft of the introductory note, Tolkien's original spelling of the name *Dírhaval* was **Dírhavel**.<sup id="cite_ref-WJII_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJII-1">[1]</a></sup>

Christopher Tolkien used the earlier spelling within the [Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md), but notes in [The War of the Jewels](../T/The_War_of_the_Jewels.md) that the name *Dírhavel* should have been spelled as *Dírhaval* since the latter spelling was the latest variation of the name.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
