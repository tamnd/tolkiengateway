The **Dark Door**, also called the **Gate of the Dead Men**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and the **Gate of the Dead**,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> was the entrance to the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md) beneath the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md). Through this entrance in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2570](../T/Third_Age_2570.md) [Baldor](../B/Baldor.md) son of [Brego](../B/Brego.md) passed never to return.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> On [8 March](../_/8_March.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) through the door [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and his companions passed during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) to stir the [Dead Men of Dunharrow](../O/Oathbreakers.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
