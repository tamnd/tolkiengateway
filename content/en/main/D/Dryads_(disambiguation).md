**Dryads** can refer to:

- [Nandini](/wiki/Nandini), the [fays](/wiki/Fays "Fays") referred to as the "dryads" in a text associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.
- [Tavaro(n)/Tavaril](/wiki/Tavari#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Tavari"), the terms mentioned in *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)* (1930s), given as the male and female equivalents of "dryad", respectively.
