<i><b>Draksjukan. Mytiska fantasier hos Tolkien, Wagner och de Vries</b></i> ("Dragons-sickness: Mythical Fantasies of Tolkien, Wagner and de Vries") is a Swedish-language study of texts by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), [Richard Wagner](/wiki/Richard_Wagner) and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_de_Vries" title="wikipedia:Jan de Vries">Jan de Vries</a>, focusing on their use of similar mythological elements.
