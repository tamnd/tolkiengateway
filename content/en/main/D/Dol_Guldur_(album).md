<i><b>Dol Guldur</b></i> is the third full-length studio album by [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning) and was released in [1997](/wiki/1997). The name is a reference to [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur), the stronghold of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) located in the south of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood). [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is credited for the lyrics alongside P.K. of the band Abigor.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> The cover artwork was adapted from the painting *"<a class="external text" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Category:Images_by_Roger_Garland#/media/File:Roger_Garland_-_Middle-earth.jpg" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Middle Earth</a>"* by [Roger Garland](/wiki/Roger_Garland) and the Ottenstein reservoir showing the ruins of Lichtenfels Castle.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>
