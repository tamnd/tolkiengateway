**Dream Cards** are new fan-made cards for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).

The Council of Elrond, a player-run organization set up as the new world coordinating body for the game after it ceased publication, has released several standardized sets of such cards. Following the example of [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises), these draw inspiration from the books and from [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-cardnum_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cardnum-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Council of Elrond sets {#Council_of_Elrond_sets}

### Firstborn {#Firstborn}

<i><b>Firstborn</b></i> is a set focusing on the Elves. Its expansion symbol is <span typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Certh_3.svg"><img src="/w/images/6/6a/Certh_3.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="18" height="27" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="32" data-file-height="48"/></a></span>.

New characters, items and locations introduced include [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor), [Aranrúth](/wiki/Aranr%C3%BAth), Ascarnil (character introduced in [Empire of the Witch-king](/wiki/Empire_of_the_Witch-king)), [Bladorthin](/wiki/Bladorthin), [Caras Amarth](/wiki/Caras_Amarth), the [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-M%C3%ADrdain), [Rúmil](</wiki/R%C3%BAmil_(elf_of_L%C3%B3rien)> "Rúmil (elf of Lórien)"), and Tharudan (character introduced in the [Northwestern Middle-earth Gazetteer](/wiki/Northwestern_Middle-earth_Gazetteer)).<sup id="cite_ref-cardnum_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cardnum-2">[2]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Durin.27s_Folk"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Durin&#39;s_Folk">Durin&#39;s Folk</span></h3>

**Durin's Folk** is a set focusing on the Dwarves. Its expansion symbol is <span typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Certh_20.svg"><img src="/w/images/c/c6/Certh_20.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="18" height="27" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="32" data-file-height="48"/></a></span>.

New characters, items and locations introduced include [Amon Lanc](/wiki/Amon_Lanc), Bar-en-Ibûn (location introduced in [Raiders of Cardolan](/wiki/Raiders_of_Cardolan)), [Barak-shathûr](/wiki/Dimrill_Dale#Portrayal_in_adaptations "Dimrill Dale"), [Borin](/wiki/Borin), [Carc](/wiki/Carc), [Dís](/wiki/D%C3%ADs), [Flói](/wiki/Fl%C3%B3i), [Frár](/wiki/Fr%C3%A1r), [Frerin](/wiki/Frerin), [Galion](/wiki/Galion), the [Long Marshes](/wiki/Long_Marshes), [Lóni](/wiki/L%C3%B3ni), [Norin](/wiki/Norin), and the Ruins of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod).<sup id="cite_ref-cardnum_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cardnum-2">[2]</a></sup>

### The Necromancer {#The_Necromancer}

<i><b>The Necromancer</b></i> is a set focusing on Sauron and the Nazgûl. Its expansion symbol is <span typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Certh_22.svg"><img src="/w/images/9/95/Certh_22.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="18" height="27" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="32" data-file-height="48"/></a></span>.

New characters, items and locations introduced include Barad-wath (location introduced in [Gorgoroth](</wiki/Gorgoroth_(book)> "Gorgoroth (book)")), [Durthang](/wiki/Durthang), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Barad-wath&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Barad-wath (page does not exist)">Barad-wath</a>, Gaurhir (character introduced in Gorgoroth), Gûrthlug (character introduced in Gorgoroth), Leardinoth (character introduced in [Brigands of Mirkwood](/wiki/Brigands_of_Mirkwood)), [Núrnen](/wiki/Sea_of_N%C3%BArnen "Sea of Núrnen"), and Zurtak (character introduced in [The Necromancer's Lieutenant](/wiki/The_Necromancer%27s_Lieutenant)).<sup id="cite_ref-cardnum_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cardnum-2">[2]</a></sup>

### The Northern Waste {#The_Northern_Waste}

<i><b>The Northern Waste</b></i> is a set inspired by the [The Northern Waste](/wiki/The_Northern_Waste) and the cards it included. Its expansion symbol is <span typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Certh_44.svg"><img src="/w/images/7/70/Certh_44.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="18" height="27" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="32" data-file-height="48"/></a></span>.

It introduces characters and locations from the sourcebook.<sup id="cite_ref-overview_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-overview-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-cardnum_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cardnum-2">[2]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Morgoth.27s_Legacy"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Morgoth&#39;s_Legacy">Morgoth&#39;s Legacy</span></h3>

<i><b>Morgoth&#39;s Legacy</b></i> is a set inspired by the remnants of Morgoth's armies and fortresses in the [Northern Waste](/wiki/Northern_Waste). Its expansion symbol is <span typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Certh_31.svg"><img src="/w/images/6/69/Certh_31.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="18" height="27" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="32" data-file-height="48"/></a></span>.

It introduces a new Balrog, [Durlach](/wiki/Durlach).<sup id="cite_ref-overview_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-overview-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-cardnum_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cardnum-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
