**Dream Cards** are new fan-made cards for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).

The Council of Elrond, a player-run organization set up as the new world coordinating body for the game after it ceased publication, has released several standardized sets of such cards. Following the example of [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md), these draw inspiration from the books and from [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-cardnum_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cardnum-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Council of Elrond sets {#Council_of_Elrond_sets}

### Firstborn {#Firstborn}

<i><b>Firstborn</b></i> is a set focusing on the Elves. Its expansion symbol is <span typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/C/Certh_3.svg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/6/6a/Certh_3.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="18" height="27" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="32" data-file-height="48"/></a></span>.

New characters, items and locations introduced include [Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md), [Aranrúth](../A/Aranrúth.md), Ascarnil (character introduced in [Empire of the Witch-king](../E/Empire_of_the_Witch-king.md)), [Bladorthin](../B/Bladorthin.md), [Caras Amarth](../C/Caras_Amarth.md), the [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](../G/Gwaith-i-Mírdain.md), [Rúmil](<../R/Rúmil_(elf_of_Lórien).md>), and Tharudan (character introduced in the [Northwestern Middle-earth Gazetteer](../N/Northwestern_Middle-earth_Gazetteer.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-cardnum_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cardnum-2">[2]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Durin.27s_Folk"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Durin&#39;s_Folk">Durin&#39;s Folk</span></h3>

**Durin's Folk** is a set focusing on the Dwarves. Its expansion symbol is <span typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/C/Certh_20.svg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c6/Certh_20.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="18" height="27" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="32" data-file-height="48"/></a></span>.

New characters, items and locations introduced include [Amon Lanc](../A/Amon_Lanc.md), Bar-en-Ibûn (location introduced in [Raiders of Cardolan](../R/Raiders_of_Cardolan.md)), [Barak-shathûr](Dimrill_Dale.md#Portrayal_in_adaptations), [Borin](../B/Borin.md), [Carc](../C/Carc.md), [Dís](Dís.md), [Flói](../F/Flói.md), [Frár](../F/Frár.md), [Frerin](../F/Frerin.md), [Galion](../G/Galion.md), the [Long Marshes](../L/Long_Marshes.md), [Lóni](../L/Lóni.md), [Norin](../N/Norin.md), and the Ruins of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md).<sup id="cite_ref-cardnum_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cardnum-2">[2]</a></sup>

### The Necromancer {#The_Necromancer}

<i><b>The Necromancer</b></i> is a set focusing on Sauron and the Nazgûl. Its expansion symbol is <span typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/C/Certh_22.svg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/9/95/Certh_22.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="18" height="27" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="32" data-file-height="48"/></a></span>.

New characters, items and locations introduced include Barad-wath (location introduced in [Gorgoroth](<../G/Gorgoroth_(book).md>)), [Durthang](Durthang.md), <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Barad-wath&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Barad-wath (page does not exist)">Barad-wath</a>, Gaurhir (character introduced in Gorgoroth), Gûrthlug (character introduced in Gorgoroth), Leardinoth (character introduced in [Brigands of Mirkwood](../B/Brigands_of_Mirkwood.md)), [Núrnen](../S/Sea_of_Núrnen.md), and Zurtak (character introduced in [The Necromancer's Lieutenant](../T/The_Necromancer's_Lieutenant.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-cardnum_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cardnum-2">[2]</a></sup>

### The Northern Waste {#The_Northern_Waste}

<i><b>The Northern Waste</b></i> is a set inspired by the [The Northern Waste](../T/The_Northern_Waste.md) and the cards it included. Its expansion symbol is <span typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/C/Certh_44.svg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/7/70/Certh_44.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="18" height="27" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="32" data-file-height="48"/></a></span>.

It introduces characters and locations from the sourcebook.<sup id="cite_ref-overview_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-overview-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-cardnum_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cardnum-2">[2]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Morgoth.27s_Legacy"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Morgoth&#39;s_Legacy">Morgoth&#39;s Legacy</span></h3>

<i><b>Morgoth&#39;s Legacy</b></i> is a set inspired by the remnants of Morgoth's armies and fortresses in the [Northern Waste](../N/Northern_Waste.md). Its expansion symbol is <span typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/C/Certh_31.svg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/6/69/Certh_31.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="18" height="27" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="32" data-file-height="48"/></a></span>.

It introduces a new Balrog, [Durlach](Durlach.md).<sup id="cite_ref-overview_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-overview-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-cardnum_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cardnum-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
