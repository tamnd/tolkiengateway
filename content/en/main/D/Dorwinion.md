**Dorwinion** was a land which lay on the north-western shores of the [Sea of Rhûn](../S/Sea_of_Rhûn.md) south of the [River Running](../R/River_Running.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In Dorwinion a heady wine was made, which was strong enough to let even [Elves](../E/Elves.md) get drunk and fall asleep.

## History {#History}

In the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) [King](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) [Turambar](<../T/Turambar_(King_of_Gondor).md>) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) defeated the [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) and conquered a large territory in the [East](../E/East.md), so the lands that were or would be Dorwinion probably became part of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> By the time of [Hyarmendacil I](../H/Hyarmendacil_I.md) Gondor had reached its greatest extent in all its history and extended east to the Sea of Rhûn.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1248](../T/Third_Age_1248.md) King [Minalcar](../M/Minalcar.md) and [Vidugavia](../V/Vidugavia.md) of the [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md) also campaigned in the lands between Rhovanion and the Sea of Rhûn.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In the following centuries the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) had hit those lands,<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-6">[6]</a></sup> although it is not known how much Dorwinion was affected.

During the [war](../W/Wainrider%252FBalchoth_War.md) with the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) ([1856](../T/Third_Age_1856.md)), Gondor abandoned its territories east of the river [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> thus possibly excluding Dorwinion from its withdrawing borders. It is possible that Gondor continued to no longer extend east of the Anduin to the Sea of Rhûn, because Steward [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md) of Gondor sent scouts and spies to the area between [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) and [Dagorlad](Dagorlad.md) and put a few men in the old forts along the Anduin, because he was always concerned about the threat of invasion from the North via the wide lands from the [Brown Lands](../B/Brown_Lands.md) to the Sea of Rhûn.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

By [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md) the heady wine for the feasts of [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) came from the great gardens of Dorwinion.<sup id="cite_ref-Barrels_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Barrels-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Inhabitants {#Inhabitants}

Despite its location in the outskirts of [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md), the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name Dorwinion for the country indicated that it had been given this name by Elves or [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md) whose influence reached as far north as the lands between the River Running and the river [Carnen](../C/Carnen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Atani_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Atani-11">[11]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/L/Lori_Deitrick_-_Men_of_Dorwinion.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/59/Lori_Deitrick_-_Men_of_Dorwinion.jpg/200px-Lori_Deitrick_-_Men_of_Dorwinion.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="168" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/5/59/Lori_Deitrick_-_Men_of_Dorwinion.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="298" data-file-height="250"/></a><figcaption><i>Men of Dorwinion</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Lori_Deitrick.md" title="Category:Images by Lori Deitrick">Lori Deitrick</a></figcaption></figure>

It is not known who inhabited Dorwinion. It is possible that it was inhabited by [Men](../M/Men.md). Since the late [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), many Men wandered or settled the empty lands between the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md), the [Greenwood](../G/Greenwood.md) and the Sea of Rhûn.<sup id="cite_ref-Atani_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Atani-11">[11]</a></sup> The wine of the great gardens of Dorwinion for the Elves of the [Woodland Realm](../W/Woodland_Realm.md), especially for their king and other goods were brought from far away from their kinsfolk in the South or from the vineyards of Men in distant lands.<sup id="cite_ref-Barrels_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Barrels-10">[10]</a></sup> Dorwinion was located a long distance away from the Woodland Realm south of the River Running where the River Running flowed into the Sea of Rhûn.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> Trade goods came up the River Running and were carted past the falls at the southern end of the [Long Lake](../L/Long_Lake.md) and Men came from the South to the shoreward end of the bridge that connected [Lake-town](../L/Lake-town.md) with the shore of the Long Lake to take some empty barrels that had been returned by the Elves with them or to fill some with goods to be transported back up the [Forest River](../F/Forest_River.md) to the [Elvenking's Halls](../E/Elvenking's_Halls.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Welcome_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Welcome-12">[12]</a></sup> Dorwinion was located south of Lake-town so Men that came from the South may have come from Dorwinion.

It is also possible that it was inhabited by [Nandor](../N/Nandor.md) or [Avari](../A/Avari.md) [Elves](../E/Elves.md) who had later learned the cultivation of vines from people who spoke [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), because its name was a testimony to the spread of Sindarin and the cultivation of vines was originally not known to the Nandor or Avari<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-13">[13]</a></sup> and because the Nandor were a group of the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) who stayed long on the shores of the Sea of Rhûn.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> However, in the beginning of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) before the building of [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md) many of the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) left [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) and went eastwards and some founded realms in the forests far away where their people were mostly [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md),<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> but there is no forest in Dorwinion.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Dorwinion* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name, which means "Young-land country" or "Land of Gwinion".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-13">[13]</a></sup> [Paul Strack](../P/Paul_Strack.md) suggests that it is a combination of *[dôr](Dôr.md)* ("country") and *Gwinion* ("Young-land")<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> and that the latter is a combination of *gwain* ("young") and the suffix *-ion* ("land").<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) commented that its "Sindarin name" was "a testimony to the spread of Sindarin: in this case expectable since the cultivation of vines was not known originally to the Nandor or Avari".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_13-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

<i>Dor<b>v</b>inion</i> or *Dor-Winion* is mentioned in the *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](../T/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md)*; its wine was famous among the [Dwarves of Nogrod](Dwarves_of_Nogrod.md) and [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md). It is said there to lie in the "Burning south" (of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md)),<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> which might suggest it was a different "Dorwinion", or may just have referred to the fact it came from the more southern lands of [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) by way of the [Dwarf-road of Beleriand](Dwarf-road_of_Beleriand.md).

While writing the [Quenta Silmarillion](../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion.md), Tolkien once mentioned Dorwinion as a location of [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> Tolkien reused the name and the wines in *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, establishing thus that it is somewhere in or near [Wilderland](../R/Rhovanion.md).

In annotations to [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md), Tolkien suggested the name *Mildor* meaning "Land of wines", and it was struck out.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Based on the meaning of its name, [John Rateliff](../J/John_Rateliff.md) suggests that the name is a reference to the Irish legend of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%ADr_na_n%C3%93g" title="wikipedia:Tír na nÓg">Tír na nÓg</a> "Land of the Young". However this similarity rather applies to the earlier phase where Dorwinion was mentioned as a part of Tol Eressea.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>While Dorwinion itself does not appear in the game, <a href="../E/Easterlings.md" title="Easterlings">Easterlings</a> from that land are encountered as refugees in the <a href="Dale.md" title="Dale">Dale</a>-lands. The Men of Dorwinion are said to have fled from a disaster in the East that occurred following the downfall of <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>. They are notably more friendly towards the Westlands than other Easterling tribes and have had trade relations in the past with Dale and the Woodland Realm. No explanation is given for Dorwinion&#39;s Sindarin name or whether Elves also live in the region.</dd></dl>
