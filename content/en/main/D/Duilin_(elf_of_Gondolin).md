**Duilin** was an [Elf](/wiki/Elf)-lord of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

He was the chief of the [folk of the Swallow](/wiki/Folk_of_the_Swallow "Folk of the Swallow"). He was the "swiftest of all men to run and leap",<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:173</sup> and the surest of the archers of the [Eldalië](/wiki/Eldali%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup>

When the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin) began, [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)> "Gothmog (balrog)") led [Melko](/wiki/Melko)'s armies to the northern gate, but the folks of the Swallow and [of the Arch](/wiki/House_of_the_Heavenly_Arch "House of the Heavenly Arch") were in the northern walls and began pouring arrows from the there.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:176</sup> As he shot, Duilin perished falling from the battlements after being smitten by a fiery bolt of the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) from outside the city.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:178</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

His name means "Swallow" in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish); its [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) form was <i><b>Tuilindo</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup>
