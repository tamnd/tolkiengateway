**Dáin I** was the King of [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk) in the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Grey_Mountains "Dwarves of the Grey Mountains").<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Dáin was the son of King [Náin II](/wiki/N%C3%A1in_II), and he had a younger brother [Borin](/wiki/Borin). He had three children: [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r), [Frór](/wiki/Fr%C3%B3r), and [Grór](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3r).

Dáin flourished during the period when the [House of Durin](/wiki/House_of_Durin) was seated in the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains). He succeeded his father when the [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons) of the north had declared [war against](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons "War of the Dwarves and Dragons") the [Dwarves of the Grey Mountains](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Grey_Mountains). Dáin ruled his people for only four years, who were troubled by increasing attacks on his [halls](/wiki/D%C3%A1in%27s_Halls "Dáin's Halls") until he met his premature end when both he and his second son, Frór, were killed by a great [cold-drake](/wiki/Cold-drakes "Cold-drakes") at the gates of his hall.

After his death, his sons Thrór and Grór led most of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of Durin's folk east. Thrór, along with Borin his uncle, led [one group](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor "Dwarves of Erebor") back to the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain), while Grór founded [his own realm](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills "Dwarves of the Iron Hills") in the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[2]</a></sup>
