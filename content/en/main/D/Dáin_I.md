**Dáin I** was the King of [Durin's folk](Durin's_folk.md) in the [Grey Mountains](Dwarves_of_the_Grey_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Dáin was the son of King [Náin II](../N/Náin_II.md), and he had a younger brother [Borin](../B/Borin.md). He had three children: [Thrór](../T/Thrór.md), [Frór](../F/Frór.md), and [Grór](../G/Grór.md).

Dáin flourished during the period when the [House of Durin](../H/House_of_Durin.md) was seated in the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md). He succeeded his father when the [Dragons](Dragons.md) of the north had declared [war against](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons.md) the [Dwarves of the Grey Mountains](Dwarves_of_the_Grey_Mountains.md). Dáin ruled his people for only four years, who were troubled by increasing attacks on his [halls](Dáin's_Halls~64102.md) until he met his premature end when both he and his second son, Frór, were killed by a great [cold-drake](../C/Cold-drakes.md) at the gates of his hall.

After his death, his sons Thrór and Grór led most of the [Dwarves](Dwarves.md) of Durin's folk east. Thrór, along with Borin his uncle, led [one group](Dwarves_of_Erebor.md) back to the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md), while Grór founded [his own realm](Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills.md) in the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[2]</a></sup>
