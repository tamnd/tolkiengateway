**Darth Gandalf** is a Youtube channel about Tolkien subjects. He makes long and short-form video essays on [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

He has a series called *War in Middle-earth*, where he talks about all the (big) conflicts that ever happened in Arda. Another series of his is called *Middle-earth Mysteries*, where he talks about things that Tolkien left open, such as the [Blue Wizards](../B/Blue_Wizards.md).

He has also said that he uses the [Tolkien Gateway](../T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) a lot.
