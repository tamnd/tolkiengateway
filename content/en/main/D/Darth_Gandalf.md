**Darth Gandalf** is a Youtube channel about Tolkien subjects. He makes long and short-form video essays on [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

He has a series called *War in Middle-earth*, where he talks about all the (big) conflicts that ever happened in Arda. Another series of his is called *Middle-earth Mysteries*, where he talks about things that Tolkien left open, such as the [Blue Wizards](/wiki/Blue_Wizards).

He has also said that he uses the [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway) a lot.
