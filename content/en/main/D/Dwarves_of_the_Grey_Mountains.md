The **Dwarves of the Grey Mountains** were the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) who lived in the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) in northern [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

## History {#History}

### The Earliest Settlements {#The_Earliest_Settlements}

In the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days), the Longbeards established mansions in [Moria](/wiki/Moria) and the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills), and they considered the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains), which lay between these mansions, to be within their territory. Some [Men](/wiki/Men)—mostly related to the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador) of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand)—settled between the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) and [Greenwood](/wiki/Greenwood), and they allied with the [Longbeards](/wiki/Longbeards) against Morgoth's [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-relations_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-relations-2">[2]</a></sup> This alliance ended in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) after [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) destroyed [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion), which prompted the Longbeards to seal Moria. During this time, Orcs took control of the Grey Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-relations_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-relations-2">[2]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="The_Awakening_of_Durin.27s_Bane"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Awakening_of_Durin&#39;s_Bane">The Awakening of Durin&#39;s Bane</span></h3>

Durin's folk started to gather in the Grey Mountains in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981") after [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") was awakened in their ancestral home of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm), which its people fled in fear. Another portion of [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk) lead by King [Thráin I](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_I) established the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) founded the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1999](/wiki/Third_Age_1999 "Third Age 1999")<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup> at [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain").

Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2000](/wiki/Third_Age_2000 "Third Age 2000"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> many centuries before [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) established a kingdom in the Ered Mithrin, [Scatha](/wiki/Scatha), a mighty [Long-worm](/wiki/Long-worms "Long-worms") of the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) and one of the greatest [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons) to infest that range of the north, had plundered treasure from the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) and kept it in his hoard. He was slain by [Fram](/wiki/Fram) son of [Frumgar](/wiki/Frumgar) in the early days of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od). His recovered hoard was the subject of great dispute between the [Men of the Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od "Éothéod") and the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of that region, who claimed the hoard as their own. Fram rebuked their claim, and sent them the teeth of the dragon, with the words, "Jewels such as these you will not match in your treasuries, for they are hard to come by." It was rumoured that for this insult the Dwarves killed him.<sup id="cite_ref-App|Eorl_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|Eorl-1">[1]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="The_Seat_of_Durin.27s_Realm"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Seat_of_Durin&#39;s_Realm">The Seat of Durin&#39;s Realm</span></h3>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2210](/wiki/Third_Age_2210 "Third Age 2210") King [Thorin I](/wiki/Thorin_I), learning that most of his people were gathering in the Grey Mountains, left the Lonely Mountain to join the Dwarves to the north, for those mountains were rich and little explored.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup>

### The War of the Dwarves and Dragons {#The_War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons}

For many years, Durin's folk prospered in the Ered Mithrin but, the dragons of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Forodwaith" title="Forodwaith">Forodwaith</a> eventually multiplied and became strong, and in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2570](/wiki/Third_Age_2570 "Third Age 2570")<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-5">[5]</a></sup> the Dragons made war on the dwarves, sacking and plundering their halls. The Dwarves held out for around twenty years, but finally in [2589](/wiki/Third_Age_2589 "Third Age 2589")<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-5">[5]</a></sup> the Dragons attacked the halls of King [Dáin I](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I). King Dáin, and his second son [Frór](/wiki/Fr%C3%B3r), were killed by a [Cold-drake](/wiki/Cold-drake) outside his door to his halls.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup>

Following the death of their king, most of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) abandoned the Grey Mountains. In [2590](/wiki/Third_Age_2590 "Third Age 2590"),<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-5">[5]</a></sup> King [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) and his uncle [Borin](/wiki/Borin) returned to the Erebor with the [Arkenstone](/wiki/Arkenstone) to re-establish the Kingdom under the Mountain. However, Thrór's younger brother [Grór](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3r) led others to the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Later History {#Later_History}

There still was a remnant of the Dwarves in the Ered Mithrin after the core of the population left.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup>
