**David Thorn Wenzel** is an American illustrator of comics and children's books. He is best known, perhaps, for his graphic novel visualization of *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(comic_book).md>)*.

## History {#History}

Wenzel was born in upstate New York in [1950](../_/1950.md). In his junior year ([1971](../_/1971.md)), he held a Tolkien art exhibition ("a hobbit exhibition") in Joeseloff Gallery. He graduated from the Hartford Art School.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [18 May](../_/18_May.md) [2025](../_/2025.md), Wenzel stated in an Instagram post that the expanded edition of the Hobbit graphic novel includes "40 new paintings for the story and a back section with additional artwork and notes on" his creative process.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
