**Dorothy Grace Wood** (née Mountain), called "**Ding**" by close relatives, was a cousin of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) (her mother was <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Grace_Mountain&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Grace Mountain (page does not exist)">Grace Mountain</a>). As a child, J.R.R. Tolkien spent several holidays with his paternal aunt in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northumberland" title="wikipedia:Northumberland">Northumberland</a>, and in later life he renewed the friendship with Dorothy.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
