<i><b>Drawings by Tolkien</b></i> is the catalogue of a [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) exhibition of the same name, held by Ashmolean Museum, Oxford, in [1976](/wiki/1976)-[1977](/wiki/1977). The catalogue shows that 82 items were displayed on exhibition, but very few of them were reproduced in the booklet.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was also printed by The National Book League (<a class="internal mw-magiclink-isbn" href="/wiki/Special:BookSources/0853532516">ISBN 0853532516</a>).
