**Doom** first appeared as a bonus track on the 2015 [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian) album <i><a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyond_the_Red_Mirror" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Beyond the Red Mirror</a></i>. In 2018 it appeared as the first bonus track on the remaster of Blind Guardian's [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-themed album *[Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth)* with some changed lyrics.
