**Dorthonion** was a forested highland region in the north of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) which spanned sixty leagues from west to east.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The hilly north-eastern section was called **[Ladros](/wiki/Ladros)**. Dorthonion was walled-in by the [Echoriath](/wiki/Echoriad "Echoriad") ("Encircling Mountains") in the west and the [Ered Gorgoroth](/wiki/Ered_Gorgoroth) ("Mountains of Terror") in the south.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup>

From the plains of [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen) to the north, Dorthonion rose gradually until it became a bleak land of tarns and bare tors near the precipices of the Ered Gorgoroth. In the north and the west the land was covered with great forests of pines.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-4">[4]</a></sup> The rivers [Rivil](/wiki/Rivil) and [Mindeb](/wiki/Mindeb) had their sources in these uplands, as did the lake [Aeluin](/wiki/Aeluin).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup> In the centre of the land was a tall peak called [Foen](/wiki/Foen).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The northern highlands, such as Dorthonion, were raised during the battles of the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) during the [Battle of the Powers](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) gave permission to the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) to settle the northern lands of Beleriand in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [6](/wiki/First_Age_6 "First Age 6").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> North Dorthonion was ruled by the Elf-lords [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod) and [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor) and Ladros was ruled by [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod).<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-4">[4]</a></sup>

Later Finrod and his people removed to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) in the south, whilst Angrod and Aegnor ruled over those of their folk who still dwelt in Dorthonion, as vassals of their brother.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-4">[4]</a></sup> Ladros was then granted by [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor) [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) to the House of Bëor as a fiefdom.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455"), Dorthonion was overrun by the armies of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) ("Battle of Sudden Flame") and thereafter was called **[Taur-nu-Fuin](</wiki/Taur-nu-Fuin_(Dorthonion)> "Taur-nu-Fuin (Dorthonion)")** ("Forest under Nightshade").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir) refused to forsake Dorthonion and remained with twelve companions near [Tarn Aeluin](/wiki/Tarn_Aeluin). Through the treachery of [Gorlim](/wiki/Gorlim) nearly all of this band were killed, save for the son of [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir), [Beren](/wiki/Beren). Beren followed the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) who had killed his father to [Rivil's Well](/wiki/Rivil%27s_Well). He then killed the Orc captain and recovered the [Ring of Barahir](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir). Eventually he was pursued so closely that Beren fled from Dorthonion over the [Ered Gorgoroth](/wiki/Ered_Gorgoroth).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

Many, many years later, on [29 February](/wiki/29_February), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) chanted to [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") as he carried them through [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest) and he mentioned "*To the pine-trees upon the highland of Dorthonion I climbed in the Winter.*"<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

*Dorthonion* means "Land of the Pine Trees" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), from *[dôr](/wiki/D%C3%B4r "Dôr")* ("land, dwelling-place") + *[thôn](/wiki/Th%C3%B4n "Thôn")* ("pine tree").<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

In a philological fragment of uncertain date it is stated that Dorthonion was also called <i><b>Taur-no-Foen</b></i>, the "Forest of the Foen".<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

Earlier forms of the name were *Taur-na-Danion*,  *Taur-na-Thanion*, and *Dorthanion*.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:note 20</sup>
