**Diari dalla Terza Era** (Italian: **Third Age Journals**) is a [fan film](/wiki/Category:Fan_films "Category:Fan films") that was made released in July 2013. The film can be downloaded online for free.

Diari dalla Terza Era takes place between the events of [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) and [The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion) specifically in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975") during the fall of Kingdom of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar).

The film follows a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") known as "Il Campione" (Italian:The Champion) who pursues the [Witch-King of Angmar](/wiki/Witch-King "Witch-King") despite [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel)'s prophecy which states:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>He will not return to this land.  Far off yet is his doom, and not by the hand of man will he fall.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Glorfindel, <i><a href="/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Return of the King">The Return of the King</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Appendix_A" title="Appendix A">Appendix A</a> (iv)</cite></p>
</blockquote>

## Production {#Production}

Diari dalla Terza Era is based on [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) and [The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion), specifically the chapter [Of the Rings of Power and the Third Age](/wiki/Of_the_Rings_of_Power_and_the_Third_Age). It was an independent, non-profit production shot in April and May 2013 with a budget of 1,400 euros.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Filming took place in The Castle of Piandimeleto, Monte Catria, Monte Acuto, Parco di Cesane in Urbino and Gola del Furlo.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
