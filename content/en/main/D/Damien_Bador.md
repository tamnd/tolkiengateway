**Damien Bador** is a French engineer, specialist in [languages](/wiki/Languages "Languages") and tongues in Middle-earth. He has written numerous essays on the invented languages of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). He is also translator and editor. He is the editor of [a special of the fanzine *L'arc et le Heaume*](</wiki/L%27Arc_et_le_Heaume_(Special_Issue)_3> "L'Arc et le Heaume (Special Issue) 3") dedicated to the [Catholic](/wiki/Christianity "Christianity") prayers translated in [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish).
