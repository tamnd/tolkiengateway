**Derufin** was one of the two tall sons of [Duinhir](/wiki/Duinhir), lord of the [Blackroot Vale](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale) at the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

With five hundred archers of their people, Derufin and his brother [Duilin](</wiki/Duilin_(son_of_Duinhir)> "Duilin (son of Duinhir)") travelled to the aid of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), far to the east of their own lands that lay under the shadow of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> There, the brothers fought together in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), and both were lost in an assault on the mighty [Mûmakil](/wiki/M%C3%BBmakil).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
