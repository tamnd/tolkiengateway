**Dry River** was the name given to the dry bed of what had once been a tributary of the River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) rising in the [Encircling Mountains](/wiki/Encircling_Mountains). It had joined the Sirion just north of the [Ford of Brithiach](/wiki/Ford_of_Brithiach).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> At the northern end of its gully lay the entrance to the Hidden City of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In primeval times (before the awakening of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves)) the Dry River was filled with water; it drained a lake in the Encircling Mountains. Once the lake was gone the lake became the valley of [Tumladen](/wiki/Tumladen) and the bed of the Dry River provided a defensible pathway into the hidden area.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Aredhel](/wiki/Aredhel) and her son [Maeglin](/wiki/Maeglin), fleeing [Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l), sought and found their way up the Dry River into Gondolin in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [400](/wiki/First_Age_400 "First Age 400").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Unbeknownst to them Eöl followed them, also found the Dry River, and tried to enter Tumladen by stealth. He was captured and brought to King [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

King Turgon led an army ten thousand strong down the Dry River and up the [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) in [472](/wiki/First_Age_472 "First Age 472")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> where they participated in the *[Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad)*. When the battle went against the Elves and Men [Huor](/wiki/Huor) and [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) held back the forces of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) to allow Turgon to escape. Turgon's army marched to the Dry River and vanished into the mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In [496](/wiki/First_Age_496 "First Age 496")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) led [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) to the Dry River and thence through the [Seven Gates](/wiki/Seven_Gates_of_Gondolin "Seven Gates of Gondolin") to Gondolin.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Tuor did not leave Gondolin by way of the Dry River; when the city fell he, [Idril](/wiki/Idril), [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil), and other refugees escaped by the dreadful [Cirith Thoronath](/wiki/Cirith_Thoronath).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

After being released by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [500](/wiki/First_Age_500 "First Age 500"), Húrin wandered into [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum). In the succeeding year<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> he attempted to find access to Gondolin, but by then the Dry River entrance was blocked.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
