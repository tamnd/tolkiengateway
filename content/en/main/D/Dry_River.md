**Dry River** was the name given to the dry bed of what had once been a tributary of the River [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md) rising in the [Encircling Mountains](../E/Encircling_Mountains.md). It had joined the Sirion just north of the [Ford of Brithiach](../F/Ford_of_Brithiach.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> At the northern end of its gully lay the entrance to the Hidden City of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In primeval times (before the awakening of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md)) the Dry River was filled with water; it drained a lake in the Encircling Mountains. Once the lake was gone the lake became the valley of [Tumladen](../T/Tumladen.md) and the bed of the Dry River provided a defensible pathway into the hidden area.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Aredhel](../A/Aredhel.md) and her son [Maeglin](../M/Maeglin.md), fleeing [Eöl](../E/Eöl.md), sought and found their way up the Dry River into Gondolin in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [400](../F/First_Age_400.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Unbeknownst to them Eöl followed them, also found the Dry River, and tried to enter Tumladen by stealth. He was captured and brought to King [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

King Turgon led an army ten thousand strong down the Dry River and up the [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md) in [472](../F/First_Age_472.md)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> where they participated in the *[Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md)*. When the battle went against the Elves and Men [Huor](../H/Huor.md) and [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) held back the forces of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) to allow Turgon to escape. Turgon's army marched to the Dry River and vanished into the mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In [496](../F/First_Age_496.md)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md) led [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) to the Dry River and thence through the [Seven Gates](../S/Seven_Gates_of_Gondolin.md) to Gondolin.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Tuor did not leave Gondolin by way of the Dry River; when the city fell he, [Idril](../I/Idril.md), [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md), and other refugees escaped by the dreadful [Cirith Thoronath](../C/Cirith_Thoronath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

After being released by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [500](../F/First_Age_500.md), Húrin wandered into [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md). In the succeeding year<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> he attempted to find access to Gondolin, but by then the Dry River entrance was blocked.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
