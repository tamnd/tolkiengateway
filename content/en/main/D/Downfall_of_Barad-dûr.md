The **Downfall of Barad-dûr** refers to the destruction of [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr) as a result [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") being unmade.

## History {#History}

[Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) began construction of Barad-dûr around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1000](/wiki/Second_Age_1000 "Second Age 1000"). Around [1600](/wiki/Second_Age_1600 "Second Age 1600"), he secretly forged the One Ring in the fires of [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom) and completed his fortress after 600 years of construction with the power of the Ring.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> The One Ring was said to be the foundation of Barad-dûr, this great tower of Sauron could only stand whilst the power of the One Ring endured. When [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) cut the One Ring off Sauron's hand in the [Battle of Dagorlad](/wiki/Battle_of_Dagorlad) the tower was levelled, but with Isildur's refusal to destroy the ring, the foundations remained unbroken.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Sauron rebuilt Barad-dûr in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2951](/wiki/Third_Age_2951 "Third Age 2951") after being driven out of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> On [25 March](/wiki/25_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), the One Ring fell into the [Cracks of Doom](/wiki/Cracks_of_Doom) whilst in the clutches of the creature [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum). Barad-dûr was utterly destroyed,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) and [Towers of the Teeth](/wiki/Towers_of_the_Teeth) falling with it.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
