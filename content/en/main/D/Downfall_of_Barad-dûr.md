The **Downfall of Barad-dûr** refers to the destruction of [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md) as a result [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) being unmade.

## History {#History}

[Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) began construction of Barad-dûr around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1000](../S/Second_Age_1000.md). Around [1600](../S/Second_Age_1600.md), he secretly forged the One Ring in the fires of [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md) and completed his fortress after 600 years of construction with the power of the Ring.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> The One Ring was said to be the foundation of Barad-dûr, this great tower of Sauron could only stand whilst the power of the One Ring endured. When [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) cut the One Ring off Sauron's hand in the [Battle of Dagorlad](../B/Battle_of_Dagorlad.md) the tower was levelled, but with Isildur's refusal to destroy the ring, the foundations remained unbroken.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Sauron rebuilt Barad-dûr in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2951](../T/Third_Age_2951.md) after being driven out of [Dol Guldur](Dol_Guldur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> On [25 March](../_/25_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), the One Ring fell into the [Cracks of Doom](../C/Cracks_of_Doom.md) whilst in the clutches of the creature [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md). Barad-dûr was utterly destroyed,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the [Black Gate](../B/Black_Gate.md) and [Towers of the Teeth](../T/Towers_of_the_Teeth.md) falling with it.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
