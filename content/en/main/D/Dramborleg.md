The **Axe of Tuor** was the great axe that [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) favoured over any sword.

## History {#History}

Tuor fought with the axe during the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), taking it with him when he escaped the downfall of the city. His descendants later took it with them to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) as an heirloom. It was lost during the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor) because only the [Ring of Barahir](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir) was said to have survived.<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the Axe of Tuor was called **Dramborleg**, meaning "Thudder-Sharp" in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), though it was also known as *Drambor*. The axe was named so because with it, Tuor "smote both a heavy dint as of a club and cleft as a sword";<sup id="cite_ref-Name_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Name-2">[2]</a></sup> he slew three [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) and some [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) named [Othrod](/wiki/Othrod), [Balcmeg](/wiki/Balcmeg), and [Lug](/wiki/Lug).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognates of Dramborleg were <i><b>Tarambolaika</b></i> or <i><b>Tarambor</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Name_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Name-2">[2]</a></sup>
