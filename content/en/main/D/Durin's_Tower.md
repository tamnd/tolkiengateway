**Durin's Tower** was the tower upon the [Silvertine](../C/Celebdil.md), one of the three peaks that stood above the [Dwarven](Dwarves.md) city of [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md).

## History {#History}

It was built by the [Dwarves](Dwarves.md) in ancient times on the peak of the Silvertine, which they called [Zirakzigil](../C/Celebdil.md) - one of the three [Mountains of Moria](../M/Mountains_of_Moria.md) under which lay the realm of Khazad-dûm. The tower was carved out of the living rock of the mountain and stood upon a high eyrie. The [Endless Stair](../E/Endless_Stair.md) led to the tower from the foundations of the mountain.<sup id="cite_ref-White_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-White-1">[1]</a></sup>

By the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), Durin's Tower had long been lost. Some Dwarves believed it had been destroyed, while others thought it was only a legend. On [23 January](../_/23_January.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and the [Balrog](Durin's_Bane.md) climbed the Endless Stair to Durin's Tower. Upon the Silvertine they [fought](../B/Battle_of_the_Peak.md) until Gandalf defeated the [demon](../B/Balrogs.md) [two days later](../_/25_January.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> During the battle, the tower was destroyed and the entrance to the stair was blocked.<sup id="cite_ref-White_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-White-1">[1]</a></sup>
