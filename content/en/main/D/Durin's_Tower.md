**Durin's Tower** was the tower upon the [Silvertine](/wiki/Celebdil "Celebdil"), one of the three peaks that stood above the [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") city of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria").

## History {#History}

It was built by the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) in ancient times on the peak of the Silvertine, which they called [Zirakzigil](/wiki/Celebdil "Celebdil") - one of the three [Mountains of Moria](/wiki/Mountains_of_Moria) under which lay the realm of Khazad-dûm. The tower was carved out of the living rock of the mountain and stood upon a high eyrie. The [Endless Stair](/wiki/Endless_Stair) led to the tower from the foundations of the mountain.<sup id="cite_ref-White_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-White-1">[1]</a></sup>

By the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), Durin's Tower had long been lost. Some Dwarves believed it had been destroyed, while others thought it was only a legend. On [23 January](/wiki/23_January) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and the [Balrog](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane "Durin's Bane") climbed the Endless Stair to Durin's Tower. Upon the Silvertine they [fought](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Peak "Battle of the Peak") until Gandalf defeated the [demon](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") [two days later](/wiki/25_January "25 January").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> During the battle, the tower was destroyed and the entrance to the stair was blocked.<sup id="cite_ref-White_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-White-1">[1]</a></sup>
