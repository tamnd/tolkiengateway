**Donato Giancola** (born [7 March](/wiki/7_March) [1967](/wiki/1967)), professionally simply known as **Donato**, is an American artist specializing in science-fiction and fantasy illustrations, especially [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related depictions. *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* were the first books he read for pleasure and "they left an impression so deep on my psyche that they are forever a standard to which other novels are compared." His proudest achievement artistically has been [The Hobbit: Explusion](/wiki/File:Donato_Giancola_-_The_Hobbit_-_Expulsion.jpg "File:Donato Giancola - The Hobbit - Expulsion.jpg").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-2">[2]</a></sup>

His artwork [“Frodo’s Inheritance”](/wiki/File:Donato_Giancola_-_Frodo%27s_Inheritance.png "File:Donato Giancola - Frodo's Inheritance.png") won the Best Artwork in The Tolkien Society Awards 2024.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
