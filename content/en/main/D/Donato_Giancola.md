**Donato Giancola** (born [7 March](../_/7_March.md) [1967](../_/1967.md)), professionally simply known as **Donato**, is an American artist specializing in science-fiction and fantasy illustrations, especially [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related depictions. *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* were the first books he read for pleasure and "they left an impression so deep on my psyche that they are forever a standard to which other novels are compared." His proudest achievement artistically has been [The Hobbit: Explusion](../../file/D/Donato_Giancola_-_The_Hobbit_-_Expulsion.jpg.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-2">[2]</a></sup>

His artwork [“Frodo’s Inheritance”](../../file/D/Donato_Giancola_-_Frodo's_Inheritance.png.md) won the Best Artwork in The Tolkien Society Awards 2024.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
