**Dimholt**, was a small fir-wood of dark trees within [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow), outside the entrance to the [Dark Door](/wiki/Dark_Door).

The Dimholt was traversed by a double row of standing stones that marked an ancient roadway leading away into the east, towards the [Dwimorberg](/wiki/Dwimorberg) and the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead). Deep within a wood of black trees stood a single great stone to mark the way, and beyond that, among the roots of the Dwimorberg, lay the Dark Door of [The Dead](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is supposed to be [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) and the element *dim* has the archaic sense "obscure, secret". *Holt* means "wood" in occasional poetic use (also seen in the name *[Everholt](/wiki/Everholt)*), with a cognate in German "Holz" meaning 'wood, lumber'.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>
