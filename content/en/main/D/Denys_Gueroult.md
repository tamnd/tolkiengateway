**Denys Gueroult** [MBE](/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire "Order of the British Empire") ([25 September](/wiki/25_September) [1927](/wiki/1927)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> - [29 July](/wiki/29_July) [2014](/wiki/2014)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) was a [BBC](/wiki/BBC) producer responsible for a [recorded interview](/wiki/Index:Audio_recordings_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Audio recordings of J.R.R. Tolkien") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in [January](/wiki/January) [1965](/wiki/1965), known as *[An Interview with J.R.R.T.](/wiki/An_Interview_with_J.R.R.T.)*

"Denys Gueroult" is the spelling used in *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide)* and [*Minas Tirith Evening-Star* (Volume 8, Number 2)](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_Evening-Star_(Volume_8,_Number_2)> "Minas Tirith Evening-Star (Volume 8, Number 2)"). Variant spellings in different works are: Denis, Dennis, Denys, David, Gueroult, Gerrolt, Gerroult. In the archives of the [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University), the name is spelt "Denis Guerouit".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
