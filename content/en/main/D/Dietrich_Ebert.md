**Dietrich Ebert** (born [1948](../_/1948.md)) is a German designer and illustrator.

Dietrich Ebert was born in St. Peter in the German state of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schleswig-Holstein" title="wikipedia:Schleswig-Holstein">Schleswig-Holstein</a>. As a graphic designer Dietrich Ebert works for several advertising agencies and industrial concerns. Since more than twenty-five years Ebert designs book covers for the publishing company [Klett-Cotta](../K/Klett-Cotta.md). In [1985](../_/1985.md) Klett-Cotta published with *[Der Tolkien Kalender 1986](<../T/The_Tolkien_Calendar_1986_(German).md>)* a German Tolkien Calendar with 12 color illustrations of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) made by Dietrich Ebert. Dietrich Ebert lives and works in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reutlingen" title="wikipedia:Reutlingen">Reutlingen</a>, Germany.

## Tolkien related cover designs and illustrations {#Tolkien_related_cover_designs_and_illustrations}

The following German editions of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)’s works were designed or illustrated by Dietrich Ebert:

- *Die Abenteuer des Tom Bombadil* (*[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](../T/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md)*) by J.R.R. Tolkien, Klett-Cotta [1984](../_/1984.md).
- *Nachrichten aus Mittelerde* (*[Unfinished Tales of Númenor and Middle-earth](../U/Unfinished_Tales_of_Númenor_and_Middle-earth.md)*) by J.R.R. Tolkien (Christopher Tolkien), Klett-Cotta [1996](../_/1996.md).
- *Das Silmarillion* (*[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*) by J.R.R. Tolkien ([Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md)), Klett-Cotta [1997](../_/1997.md).
- *Das Buch der Verschollenen Geschichten* (*[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*) (Part 1 and 2) by J.R.R. Tolkien (Christopher Tolkien), Klett-Cotta [1999](../_/1999.md).
- *Der Herr der Ringe* (*[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*) by J. R. R. Tolkien, in one volume (red edition), Klett-Cotta [2000](../_/2000.md).
- *Der Herr der Ringe* by J.R.R. Tolkien, in three volumes (green edition), Klett-Cotta [2000](../_/2000.md).
- *Der Herr der Ringe – Anhänge und Register* (*[Appendices](../A/Appendices.md)*), Klett-Cotta [2000](../_/2000.md).
- *Briefe* (*[Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](../L/Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*) published by Christopher Tolkien and [Humphrey Carpenter](../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md), Klett-Cotta [2003](../_/2003.md).
- *Der Herr der Ringe* by J.R.R. Tolkien, special edition (leather edition), Klett-Cotta [2003](../_/2003.md).
