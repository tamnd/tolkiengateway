**Dietrich Ebert** (born [1948](/wiki/1948)) is a German designer and illustrator.

Dietrich Ebert was born in St. Peter in the German state of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schleswig-Holstein" title="wikipedia:Schleswig-Holstein">Schleswig-Holstein</a>. As a graphic designer Dietrich Ebert works for several advertising agencies and industrial concerns. Since more than twenty-five years Ebert designs book covers for the publishing company [Klett-Cotta](/wiki/Klett-Cotta). In [1985](/wiki/1985) Klett-Cotta published with *[Der Tolkien Kalender 1986](</wiki/The_Tolkien_Calendar_1986_(German)> "The Tolkien Calendar 1986 (German)")* a German Tolkien Calendar with 12 color illustrations of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) made by Dietrich Ebert. Dietrich Ebert lives and works in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reutlingen" title="wikipedia:Reutlingen">Reutlingen</a>, Germany.

## Tolkien related cover designs and illustrations {#Tolkien_related_cover_designs_and_illustrations}

The following German editions of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)’s works were designed or illustrated by Dietrich Ebert:

- *Die Abenteuer des Tom Bombadil* (*[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*) by J.R.R. Tolkien, Klett-Cotta [1984](/wiki/1984).
- *Nachrichten aus Mittelerde* (*[Unfinished Tales of Númenor and Middle-earth](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales_of_N%C3%BAmenor_and_Middle-earth)*) by J.R.R. Tolkien (Christopher Tolkien), Klett-Cotta [1996](/wiki/1996).
- *Das Silmarillion* (*[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*) by J.R.R. Tolkien ([Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien)), Klett-Cotta [1997](/wiki/1997).
- *Das Buch der Verschollenen Geschichten* (*[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*) (Part 1 and 2) by J.R.R. Tolkien (Christopher Tolkien), Klett-Cotta [1999](/wiki/1999).
- *Der Herr der Ringe* (*[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*) by J. R. R. Tolkien, in one volume (red edition), Klett-Cotta [2000](/wiki/2000).
- *Der Herr der Ringe* by J.R.R. Tolkien, in three volumes (green edition), Klett-Cotta [2000](/wiki/2000).
- *Der Herr der Ringe – Anhänge und Register* (*[Appendices](/wiki/Appendices)*), Klett-Cotta [2000](/wiki/2000).
- *Briefe* (*[Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)*) published by Christopher Tolkien and [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter), Klett-Cotta [2003](/wiki/2003).
- *Der Herr der Ringe* by J.R.R. Tolkien, special edition (leather edition), Klett-Cotta [2003](/wiki/2003).
