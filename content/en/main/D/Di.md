<i><b>di</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) preposition meaning "beneath, under".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Example {#Example}

- <i><a href="/wiki/A_Elbereth_Gilthoniel" title="A Elbereth Gilthoniel">le nallon sí <b>di&#39;</b>nguruthos</a></i> = "here overwhelmed in dread of Death I cry"
