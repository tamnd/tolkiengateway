**David Elkan** is a [British](/wiki/United_Kingdom "United Kingdom") author. He wrote the book *[A Guide to Playing The Hobbit](/wiki/A_Guide_to_Playing_The_Hobbit)*, which guides and helps players of [the video game adaptation of *The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1982_video_game)> "The Hobbit (1982 video game)") through the game.
