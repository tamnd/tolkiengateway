**David Elkan** is a [British](../U/United_Kingdom.md) author. He wrote the book *[A Guide to Playing The Hobbit](../A/A_Guide_to_Playing_The_Hobbit.md)*, which guides and helps players of [the video game adaptation of *The Hobbit*](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1982_video_game).md>) through the game.
