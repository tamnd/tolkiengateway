The **Dimrill Stair** was the name given to the southeastern part of the [Redhorn Gate](/wiki/Redhorn_Gate), leading down into [Dimrill Dale](/wiki/Dimrill_Dale). It was named the Dimrill Stair because of the sequence of waterfalls that flowed down beside the pass, which ultimately led down into the lake of [Mirrormere](/wiki/Mirrormere).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2008: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Dimrill Stair is a prominent waterfall dropping into the <a href="/wiki/Mirrormere" title="Mirrormere">Mirrormere</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2024: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Moria_-_Through_the_Doors_of_Durin" title="Moria - Through the Doors of Durin">Moria: Through the Doors of Durin</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Prior to the construction of West Moria and the roads beneath the mountain, the Dimrill Stair served as the primary trade highway between Khazad-dûm and Elves and Men. At its foot stood the ruins of the Second Age fortress of Dimrill Watch, which had even sheltered migrating <a href="/wiki/Woodmen" title="Woodmen">Woodmen</a> at some point. The mithril key to the Dimrill Door, a secret entrance at the 17th pillar of the stair, was captured by Orcs and sent as tribute to <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>. By <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2965" title="Third Age 2965">2965</a>, Orcs of <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a> regularly used this secret entrance in their attempts to capture Moria.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> An iron copy of this key was found by Flonar in the archives of the <a href="/wiki/Blue_Mountains" title="Blue Mountains">Blue Mountains</a>, though it too was acquired by Orcs when they captured Flonar in 2965.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>
