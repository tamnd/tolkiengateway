**Drogo Baggins** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). He was the father of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).

## History {#History}

Drogo Baggins was the second child, and oldest son, of [Fosco Baggins](/wiki/Fosco_Baggins) and [Ruby Bolger](/wiki/Ruby_Bolger), born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1308](/wiki/Third_Age_2908 "Third Age 2908"). He married [Primula Brandybuck](/wiki/Primula_Brandybuck), and had one child, [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

A regular and respectable [Shire-hobbit](/wiki/Shire-hobbit), fond of good food, he often visited his generous father-in-law [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck) in [Brandy Hall](/wiki/Brandy_Hall), and seemed to adopt the curious customs of the [Bucklanders](/wiki/Bucklanders), like boating. This was the cause of his irregular death, as during one of his visits to his in-laws in [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> he drowned in a boating accident with his wife Primula. Frodo was still very young and he was taken under the custody of his uncle, [Rorimac Brandybuck](/wiki/Rorimac_Brandybuck).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Drogo* was meaningless in contemporary [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) [Westron](/wiki/Westron). It represented an original name *Droga*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
