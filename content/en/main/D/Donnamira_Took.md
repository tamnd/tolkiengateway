**Donnamira Boffin, née Took**, was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Donnamira was one of [Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took")'s younger children, born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1256](/wiki/Third_Age_2856 "Third Age 2856").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She married [Hugo Boffin](/wiki/Hugo_Boffin) and their children were [Jago](/wiki/Jago_Boffin "Jago Boffin") and [Jessamine](/wiki/Jessamine_Boffin "Jessamine Boffin").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Donnamira's name participates in a circular wordplay with the names of her sisters, [Bella**donna**](/wiki/Belladonna_Took "Belladonna Took") and [**Mira**bella](/wiki/Mirabella_Took "Mirabella Took"), all Italian names.
