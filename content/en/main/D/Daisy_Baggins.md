**Daisy Boffin, née Baggins**, was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Daisy was the daughter of [Dudo Baggins](/wiki/Dudo_Baggins), born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1350](/wiki/Third_Age_2950 "Third Age 2950").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She married [Griffo Boffin](/wiki/Griffo_Boffin) and had a child, [Tosto Boffin](/wiki/Tosto_Boffin) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1388](/wiki/Third_Age_2988 "Third Age 2988"). They were all guests at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Daisy&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Daisy (page does not exist)">Daisy</a>, a flower name, derives from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *dæges eage* "day's eye".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007 <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Daisy Boffin normally appears in <a href="/wiki/Bywater" title="Bywater">Bywater</a> as a vendor with no unique lines. During the annual Yule Festival, she is found at the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Party_Tree" title="Party Tree">Party Tree</a> where she asks the player for assistance with celebration preparations.</dd></dl>
