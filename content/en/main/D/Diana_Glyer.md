**Dr. Diana Pavlac Glyer** ([January](/wiki/January) [1956](/wiki/1956)) is a professor in the Department of English and in the Honors College at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azusa_Pacific_University" title="wikipedia:Azusa Pacific University">Azusa Pacific University</a>. Dr. Glyer received her Ph.D. from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago" title="wikipedia:University of Illinois at Chicago">University of Illinois at Chicago</a> and now lives in California.

## Life {#Life}

Glyer was introduced to the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings) in high school. She read *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, and became interested in the friendship of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis). She would spend twenty years researching their relationship, focusing mainly on completeness of primary sources.

In 1997, Glyer received the *[Clyde S. Kilby](/wiki/Clyde_S._Kilby) Research Grant*. This endowed award is presented annually by the Steering Committee of the <a class="external text" href="http://www.wheaton.edu/wadecenter/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Marion E. Wade Center</a> to a scholar engaged in a publishable project related to one of the seven authors on which the Center focuses. The intention of the award is both to recognize scholarly contributions and also to assist the work of those who use the resources of the Wade Center. This grant allowed her to publish <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Company_They_Keep:_C.S._Lewis_and_J.R.R._Tolkien_as_Writers_in_Community&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Company They Keep: C.S. Lewis and J.R.R. Tolkien as Writers in Community (page does not exist)">The Company They Keep: C.S. Lewis and J.R.R. Tolkien as Writers in Community</a></i> in [2006](/wiki/2006).
