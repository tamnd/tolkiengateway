**Dwaling** was a village in the far Northern parts of the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), just north of the hills of [Scary](/wiki/Scary).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Dwaling seems to be a dialectal form of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/dwelling" title="wiktionary:dwelling">dwelling</a></i>.

In a note to translators, Tolkien said, but without making clear if it was his intended meaning, that *Dwaling* "should be" a regular toponymic formation of a settlement's name, "the descendants of *Dwale*". *Dwale* would be a nick-name of one particular Hobbit, and the name was derived from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *dwale*, "dull".<sup id="cite_ref-lviii_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lviii-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is notable that *dull* and *dwale* ([Gothic](/wiki/Gothic): *dwals* "fool") are cognates of Tol- in [**Tol**kien](/wiki/Tolkien "Tolkien") (German: <i><b>toll</b>kühn</i>, "foolhardy").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Tolkien himself has translated his own name into Gothic as *Dwalakōneis*.<sup id="cite_ref-lviii_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lviii-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2008: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dwaling  is the northernmost settlement of the Shire, located within the in-game <a href="/wiki/Evendim" title="Evendim">Evendim</a> region. It is the first Shire settlement to be overrun by southern ruffians, who have already moved in and began pusing out the Hobbits by October of <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3018" title="Third Age 3018">Third Age 3018</a>.</dd></dl>
