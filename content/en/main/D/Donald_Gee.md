**Donald Gee** ([September 28](/wiki/28_September "28 September"), [1937](/wiki/1937) - [January 14](/wiki/14_January "14 January"), [2022](/wiki/2022)) was an English actor. He provided the voice of [Radagast](/wiki/Radagast) in [the 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") broadcasted on [BBC 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
