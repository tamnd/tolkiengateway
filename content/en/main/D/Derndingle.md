**Derndingle** was a hollow in the south of [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md), used by the [Ents](../E/Ents.md) from time immemorial as the place where they met in [Entmoot](../E/Entmoot.md). In shape it was a wide round depression, surrounded by a dark green hedge of evergreen trees. The hollow itself was clear of trees, except for three tall silver [birches](../B/Birches.md) that grew in its centre.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name Derndingle is [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) in origin but of course comes from an old version of [Westron](../W/Westron.md) when [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md) still remembered the [Ents](../E/Ents.md).

A *dingle* is a "tree-shadowed dell". The archaic element *dern* (also seen in *[Dernhelm](Dernhelm.md)*) means "secret, hidden".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
