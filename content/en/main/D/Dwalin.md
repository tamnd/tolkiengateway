**Dwalin** was a [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") of the [House of Durin](/wiki/House_of_Durin). He was the son of [Fundin](/wiki/Fundin) and younger brother of [Balin](/wiki/Balin). Alongside Balin, Dwalin embarked on two expeditions to reclaim his people's lost homeland, the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain), from the dragon [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug); first a failed one, led by King [Thráin](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in), then a successful one, where he was a member of [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company). Dwalin survived the adventure and the subsequent [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies), and lived in wealth and splendour in the Lonely Mountain well into the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).

## History {#History}

### Early history {#Early_history}

Two years before Dwalin was born, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770"), the dragon Smaug attacked the kingdom of Erebor,<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> and destroyed the settlement and the nearby city of [Dale](/wiki/Dale), taking the gold of the mountain as his own. In the aftermath of destruction, King [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) and a small group of his surviving kinsmen headed south while other [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor) escaped to the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-4">[4]</a></sup> Because of this, Dwalin was born in exile, possibly in [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland) to [Fundin](/wiki/Fundin) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2772](/wiki/Third_Age_2772 "Third Age 2772") nine years after his brother, Balin.

### War of the Dwarves and Orcs {#War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs}

After years of living in Dunland, a despondent Thrór made a journey to Moria, where an orc cheiftan, [Azog](/wiki/Azog), slaughtered him. In retaliation, [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_II), now King of Durin's Folk, waged war. A 27-year-old Dwalin participated in the [Battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar), where, amid battle, Fundin and several other kinsman were slain. Despite this, the war was won. After the victory, Thráin was counselled by [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot) not to enter [Khazad-Dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"), so, Thráin and his son [Thorin Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin") settled in the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Blue_Mountains "Blue Mountains").<sup id="cite_ref-Line_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-5">[5]</a></sup> At some point, Dwalin and Balin joined Thráin in the Blue Mountains, along with the other survivors of the war.

<h3><span id="King_Thr.C3.A1in.27s_expedition"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="King_Thráin&#39;s_expedition">King Thráin&#39;s expedition</span></h3>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2841](/wiki/Third_Age_2841 "Third Age 2841"), Dwalin, Balin and a few other dwarves accompanied Thráin when he left the Ered Luin to reclaim his lost homeland, Erebor. The small party was dogged by the emissaries of the dark lord, [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), because Thráin carried one of the seven dwarven rings of power. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2845](/wiki/Third_Age_2845 "Third Age 2845"), Thráin and the rest of the company were separated by a black rain in the eaves of the dark forest, [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood). Unbeknonwst to Dwalin and the company, Thráin had been captured by Sauron. After a fruitless search for their leader the Dwarves gave up and returned to inform Thorin of the mision's failure, who became the new King of Durin's Folk.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Quest for Erebor {#Quest_for_Erebor}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Adlpictures_-_Dwalin.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/1/11/Adlpictures_-_Dwalin.jpg/200px-Adlpictures_-_Dwalin.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="283" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/1/11/Adlpictures_-_Dwalin.jpg/300px-Adlpictures_-_Dwalin.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/1/11/Adlpictures_-_Dwalin.jpg/400px-Adlpictures_-_Dwalin.jpg 2x" data-file-width="900" data-file-height="1273"/></a><figcaption><i>Dwalin</i> by Adlpictures</figcaption></figure>

A century later, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"), Dwalin and Balin joined Thorin's expedition to reclaim the Lonely Mountain. Per the instructions of the wizard, [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), they were invited to a [smial](/wiki/Smial "Smial") named [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End), which is located in the [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), to form their plan. Dwalin was the first dwarf to arrive, alone, when the unexpected party began at the hobbit, which was owned by [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins). He was wearing a dark-green hood and walked in as if expected and hung his hood on one of Bilbo's pegs. A greatly surprised hobbit invited Dwalin to tea. Soon, Balin arrived and he and Dwalin sat talking while Bilbo kept answering the door for the arrival of more and more Dwarves. Later, when Bilbo griped aloud in his kitchen about the lack of help in setting out refreshments, Dwalin and Balin were the first dwarves to lend a hand. Later still, when Thorin called for music, Dwalin and Balin said that they forgot their instruments on the porch and are sent by Thorin to bring his instrument as well. When they returned; Dwalin and Balin brought viols as large as themselves and Thorin's harp.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-1">[1]</a></sup>

The next day, the company met at the [Green Dragon](/wiki/Green_Dragon) Inn. When Bilbo told the company that he had forgotten to bring his hat or his pocket-handkerchief, Dwalin gave him his spare dark-green hood and cloak to keep in place of a hat, but told him that he would "*have to manage without pocket-handkerchiefs, and a good many other things before the journey's end*". Soon afterward, Gandalf arrived and brought several of Bilbo's assortments.

In [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), at the [Trollshaws](/wiki/Trollshaws), Dwalin along with the entire company were captured by [Trolls](/wiki/Trolls) after Bilbo went to explore a fire. The three trolls: [William](/wiki/William), [Bert](/wiki/Bert) and <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Tom" title="Tom">Tom</a> soon began to argue among themselves, and Gandalf managed to save them. Shortly afterward, the company located and looted the nearby troll cave.

Later on, the company arrived at [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and stayed with the [half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven "Half-elven") lord [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) for about 14 days with food and something for everyone. On one night Elrond disclosed the swords Thorin and Gandalf found in the troll-hoard as [Orcrist](/wiki/Orcrist) and [Glamdring](/wiki/Glamdring). Elrond also read the moon letters of the map of Thror, which read: *"Stand by the grey stone when the thrush knocks, and the setting sun with the last light of Durin's Day will shine upon the key-hole.*"

After seeking refuge from a storm in a cave which was, unbeknownst to them the [Front Porch](/wiki/Front_Porch) of [Goblin-town](/wiki/Goblin-town), they were captured by [Goblins](/wiki/Goblins). They were saved by Gandalf, and Bilbo, fleeing after his encounter with [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum), also escaped. The company were then chased by [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs) and they rushed into the trees. Dwalin and Balin managed to swarm up a tall slender fir that had few branches for sitting, and they were later then saved by the [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles) of the [Great Eagle](/wiki/Great_Eagle).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The next day, when the company arrived at [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn)'s hall after the eagles left them on the [Carrock](/wiki/Carrock), Dwalin and Balin were the third pair of Dwarves to appear during Gandalf's story, trying to intrigue the skin-changer. They were later served well in dinner and breakfast and were also given ponies to ride until they arrived at the forest of Mirkwood, the same place where Thráin was lost nearly 100 years before.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

At the [Enchanted River](/wiki/Enchanted_River) in Mirkwood, Dwalin was in the last boatload to cross with [Bombur](/wiki/Bombur). He exited the boat just before a deer charged down the path and caused Bombur to fall in the water, which put him in an enchanted sleep for six days, and when he woke up, he had forgotten the whole journey. Later, after the Dwarves had been captured by the [spiders](/wiki/Spiders "Spiders") and rescued by Bilbo, it was Dwalin who first realised that Thorin was missing,<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-8">[8]</a></sup> for at that point, though unknown to the Dwarves, Thorin had been captured by the Wood-Elves.

At the opening of the barrels after the escape from the halls of the [Elvenking](/wiki/Thranduil "Thranduil"), Dwalin and Balin were two of the most unhappy Dwarves and did nothing to help pull out other Dwarves as they arrived in [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town), where they were greeted extremely fondly.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Afterwards, though the [Master of Lake-town](/wiki/Master_of_Lake-town) didn't believe that Thorin was truly the [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain), they left for the mountain. When the last week of autumn arrived it was Dwalin who complained the most about Bilbo's inactivity. He felt that Bilbo, with his "invisible ring", ought to go through the [Front Gate](/wiki/Front_Gate) of Erebor and spy things out.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

After [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) went to destroy Lake-town, the dwarves entered and Thorin's search for the [Arkenstone](/wiki/Arkenstone) began. Dwalin later fought in and survived the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies) along with several others from the company (all but Thorin and Fíli and Kíli). Afterwards he received his fourteenth share and chose to live in Erebor under the rule of Thorin's cousin and new king after his death, [Dáin II](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_II).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

### Later life {#Later_life}

Dwalin lived in great wealth in Erebor after the Battle of the Five Armies. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2989](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989"), his brother Balin set out on a quest to reclaim [Moria](/wiki/Moria) though Dáin was against it. He set out with others (such as [Óin](/wiki/%C3%93in) and [Ori](/wiki/Ori)). For about 5 years messages came from Moria and it seemed good, until suddenly news stopped (though unknown to him, the colony was destroyed by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs)).

As Bilbo packed some oddments in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001") before departing the Shire, Bilbo brought Dwalin's green hood he had given him, though the colour had worn out. Dwalin was one of seven of the ten Dwarves of Thorin's company who were still living in Erebor when [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) came to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), as reported by [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in) before the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> It is possible that he fought in the [Battle of Dale](/wiki/Battle_of_Dale) against Sauron's forces, the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings), under King Dáin (though the king was slain). Dwalin lived until [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [91](/wiki/Fourth_Age_91 "Fourth Age 91"), passing at the age of 340,<sup id="cite_ref-Line_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In [Norse mythology](/wiki/Norse_mythology), *Dvalinn* is a chief Dwarf.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> The origin of the name is uncertain; suggested etymologies are "one lying in a trance",<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> "numb" and "dawdler".<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> Both words, *dvalen* ("to sleep") *dvelja* ("to delay") derive from the same root.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the early version of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, it was Dwalin rather than Balin who spotted the Trolls' fire in the woods (and it was stated that Dwalin was always the Dwarves' look-out).<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> This contradicted the fact that Balin was the Dwarf who was looking for Bilbo at the inn at the start of the adventure<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> and Tolkien eventually made Balin the permanent look-out. However, Dwalin remained the first Dwarf to arrive at Bag End<sup id="cite_ref-Party_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-1">[1]</a></sup> (what would be expected of the Company's look-out). Dwalin was also the first to notice that Thorin was missing after the battle with the spiders,<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-8">[8]</a></sup> a testament to his observational abilities.

### Films {#Films}

<b>1967: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_film)" title="The Hobbit (1967 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1967 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dwalin is omitted. <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin Oakenshield</a> only travels with an unnamed guard and the princess of Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-Deitch_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deitch-19">[19]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1977: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)" title="The Hobbit (1977 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1977 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dwalin does not have a blue beard, but a white one with a long nose and a green hood (looking as Balin, though he wears a red hood and glasses). He speaks only one line on his own (by <a href="/wiki/Paul_Frees" title="Paul Frees">Paul Frees</a>), when the Dwarves are in front of the <a href="/wiki/Back_Door" title="Back Door">Back Door</a> saying&#34; &#34;Well, what now?&#34;; otherwise, he only speaks in unison with the rest of the Company though it is unknown whether he survived the Battle of the Five Armies, as seven dwarves passed away during the battle.</dd></dl>

<b>2012-14: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dwalin is played by <a href="/wiki/Graham_McTavish" title="Graham McTavish">Graham McTavish</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting-20">[20]</a></sup> A description of Dwalin in <i>The Hobbit</i> films was released by the studio:</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt"></blockquote></dd></dl>

Renowned as a great Warrior, Dwalin speaks his mind plainly and is not one to suffer fools lightly. Fiercely loyal, he is Thorin Oakenshield’s staunchest supporter with an unshakeable belief in his friend’s leadership. A powerful and bruising fighter, Dwalin has a natural tendency to distrust anyone who is not a Dwarf and is particularly suspicious of Elves. Proud, brave and one of the tallest of the Dwarves, Dwalin will bow to none but those who have earned his respect.

### Games {#Games}

<b>1982: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1982_video_game)" title="The Hobbit (1982 video game)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1982 video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dwalin is omitted; Thorin is the only companion of the player, <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2003: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)" title="The Hobbit (2003 video game)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (2003 video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dwalin is a blue-bearded Dwarf, who is not seen in the game (other than cut-scenes) until <a href="/wiki/Lake-town" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3016" title="Third Age 3016">3016</a>, during the default introduction for new Dwarf and Elf player characters, Dwalin leads a party of dwarves from the <a href="/wiki/Lonely_Mountain" title="Lonely Mountain">Lonely Mountain</a> to <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Thorin%27s_Halls" title="Thorin&#39;s Halls">Thorin&#39;s Hall</a> to investigate why the Steward appointed by Thorin, Gormr Doursmith of the <a href="/wiki/Dourhands" title="Dourhands">Dourhands</a>, has ceased sending tribute to <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Erebor" title="Erebor">Erebor</a>. After the Dourhands&#39; treachery is revealed and they are expelled, Dwalin becomes the new Steward of Thorin&#39;s Hall and maintains this position through the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Following the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Siege_of_Erebor" title="Siege of Erebor">Siege of Erebor</a>, Dwalin travels to Erebor to pay respect to the new king <a href="/wiki/Thorin_Stonehelm" title="Thorin Stonehelm">Thorin Stonehelm</a> and, along with the other surviving dwarves of the <a href="/wiki/Thorin_and_Company" title="Thorin and Company">Company</a>, visits the tomb of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Thorin_Oakenshield" title="Thorin Oakenshield">Thorin Oakenshield</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2021: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Rise_to_War" title="The Lord of the Rings: Rise to War">The Lord of the Rings: Rise to War</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>If the player chooses the Erebor faction at the start of the game, Dwalin will be received as part of the game&#39;s tutorial.</dd></dl>
