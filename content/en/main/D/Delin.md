**Delin** son of [Gelmir](../F/Finwë.md#Other_version_of_the_legendarium) was a [Gnome](../G/Gnome.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in a prose fragment following *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Prose_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prose-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/Q/Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/87/Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg/300px-Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="146" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/87/Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg/450px-Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/87/Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg/600px-Quentandil_-_Gnome_Banners.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1533" data-file-height="744"/></a><figcaption><i>Gnome Banners</i> by <a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Quentandil" title="User:Quentandil">Quentandil</a></figcaption></figure>

After the [Gnomes](../G/Gnomes.md) returned to the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md) to wage war with [Melko](../M/Melko.md), Delin led one of the three great armies of his father Gelmir. His standard (banner) was that of a "green beech-leaf upon silver diapered with golden flowers", and his army preferred spears over other weapons.<sup id="cite_ref-Prose_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prose-1">[1]</a></sup>

Beyond this point in the story, nothing else is known about him.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Delin* seems to be in [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md), but its meaning is uncertain.
