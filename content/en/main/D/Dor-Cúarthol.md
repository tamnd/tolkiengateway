**Dor-Cúarthol** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Land of Bow and Helm*) was the name given to the country ruled by [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) and [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) from their lair atop [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The country was situated "between [Teiglin](/wiki/Taeglin "Taeglin") and the [west march](/wiki/Nivrim "Nivrim") of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath)" and likely the [Narog](/wiki/Narog) and [Andram](/wiki/Andram). The name comes from Beleg's [great bow](/wiki/Belthronding "Belthronding") and Túrin's [ancestral helm](/wiki/Dragon-helm_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min "Dragon-helm of Dor-lómin").<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Built within Amon Rûdh was the [home](/wiki/Bar-en-Nibin-noeg "Bar-en-Nibin-noeg") of the last [Petty-dwarves](/wiki/Petty-dwarves), [Mîm](/wiki/M%C3%AEm) and his sons [Ibun](/wiki/Ibun) and [Khîm](/wiki/Kh%C3%AEm). When Túrin and the outlaws known as the [Gaurwaith](/wiki/Gaurwaith) encountered Mîm in the wild they captured him, but Mîm ransomed his home to the Gaurwaith for his life. One day in the midst of winter, Beleg of Doriath, having been seeking out his old friend, brought to him the [Helm](/wiki/Dragon-helm_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min "Dragon-helm of Dor-lómin") of [his ancestors](/wiki/House_of_Hador "House of Hador"), and joined the outlaws. That spring, Túrin took up his helm and went among his company in hunting down Orcs and even though they outnumbered the Gaurwaith greatly, the valour of Túrin and the bowmanship of Beleg made them seem like a host.<sup id="cite_ref-land_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-land-4">[4]</a></sup>

The deeds of the company were soon heard all throughout [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), in [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth) and even [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), declaring that "the Helm and Bow that had fallen in [Dimbar](/wiki/Dimbar) had arisen again beyond hope".<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-3">[3]</a></sup> Many leaderless and dispossessed wanderers took heart and came to seek them and Túrin gladly received them, though Beleg suggested that he not permit any one to enter into Amon Rûdh, now renamed as Echad i Sedryn, which only the [Old Company](/wiki/Gaurwaith "Gaurwaith") of the Gaurwaith and the [Petty-dwarves](/wiki/Petty-dwarves) could enter. Thus, many guarded camps and forts were established around Amon Rûdh from the eastern forest to the highlands and the southern reaches. The most prominent of these settlements were known as [Methed-en-glad](/wiki/Methed-en-glad) and [Bar-erib](/wiki/Bar-erib).<sup id="cite_ref-land_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-land-4">[4]</a></sup>

Soon after, the following of Túrin became a great force to be reckoned with, resulting in [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) pulling back and making feint attacks instead. The knowledge of this feat reached even as far as [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), causing [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) to send messengers to offer the Two Captains any aid other than in arms if they should feel so inclined.<sup id="cite_ref-land_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-land-4">[4]</a></sup>

Upon his success against [Angband](/wiki/Angband), Túrin named the land between the Taeglin and Doriath as *Dor-Cúarthol*, taking on the name *[Gorthol](/wiki/Gorthol)* and claiming the lordship of the land for himself.<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-3">[3]</a></sup>

The realm of Dor-Cúarthol lasted for two more years, barring the [southward road](</wiki/Old_South_Road_(Beleriand)> "Old South Road (Beleriand)") from the forces of Angband,<sup id="cite_ref-land_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-land-4">[4]</a></sup> but it was not to last. For the fame of the Two Captains had reached [Angband](/wiki/Angband), and perceiving that "Gorthol" was indeed Túrin, the son of [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin), and fearing that he would overcome his doom, Morgoth sent his most skilled spies to watch the lands around the hill, but not make any move against it. Yet the presence of the Orcs was known to Mîm, who went out foraging for [wild roots](/wiki/Earth-bread "Earth-bread") with his son, Ibun. However, his true intention was to betray Dor-Cúarthol to the Orc-captain so as to rid Amon Rûdh of the Old Company.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> With the Orcs insisting on taking Ibun hostage, Mîm led them to the Echad within Amon Rûdh and the Orcs [sacked](/wiki/Sack_of_Bar-en-Danwedh "Sack of Bar-en-Danwedh") it. Thus, Dor-Cúarthol was no more, for Beleg was left for dead, Túrin was made a captive,<sup id="cite_ref-land_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-land-4">[4]</a></sup> and of the Old Company, only [Andvír](/wiki/Andv%C3%ADr), the son of [Andróg](/wiki/Andr%C3%B3g), survived.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dor-Cúarthol* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Land of Bow and Helm",<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup> from *[dôr](/wiki/D%C3%B4r "Dôr")* ("land") + *[cú](/wiki/C%C3%BA "Cú")* ("bow") + *[a](/wiki/A "A")* ("and") + *[thôl](/wiki/Th%C3%B4l "Thôl")* ("helm").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup>
