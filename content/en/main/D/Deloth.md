<i><b>deloth</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "abhorrence".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) <i>deloth/<b>delos</b></i> ("abhorrence, detestation, loathing") comes from [roots](/wiki/Root "Root") DYEL + GOS.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Dor_Daedeloth" title="Dor Daedeloth">Dor Dae<b>deloth</b></a></i> = "Land of Great Dread"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Dagor_Delothrin" title="Dagor Delothrin">Dagor <b>Deloth</b>rin</a></i> = "Terrible Battle"
