**Sir Derek George Jacobi** [CBE](/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire "Order of the British Empire") (born [22 October](/wiki/22_October) [1938](/wiki/1938)) is an English actor. Though already an acclaimed performer on stage, he gained star status in [1976](/wiki/1976), as the titular character in the [BBC](/wiki/BBC)'s classic series *I, Claudius*, which also proved successful for [Brian Blessed](/wiki/Brian_Blessed), [John Rhys-Davies](/wiki/John_Rhys-Davies), [John Hurt](/wiki/John_Hurt), [Christopher Guard](/wiki/Christopher_Guard) and [Bernard Hill](/wiki/Bernard_Hill). Noted for his outstanding voice, Jacobi provided narration for many audiobooks. He narrated Mary Shelley's *Frankenstein*, Homer's *Iliad*, and several of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s lesser known works.
