**Sir Derek George Jacobi** [CBE](../O/Order_of_the_British_Empire.md) (born [22 October](../_/22_October.md) [1938](../_/1938.md)) is an English actor. Though already an acclaimed performer on stage, he gained star status in [1976](../_/1976.md), as the titular character in the [BBC](../B/BBC.md)'s classic series *I, Claudius*, which also proved successful for [Brian Blessed](../B/Brian_Blessed.md), [John Rhys-Davies](../J/John_Rhys-Davies.md), [John Hurt](../J/John_Hurt.md), [Christopher Guard](../C/Christopher_Guard.md) and [Bernard Hill](../B/Bernard_Hill.md). Noted for his outstanding voice, Jacobi provided narration for many audiobooks. He narrated Mary Shelley's *Frankenstein*, Homer's *Iliad*, and several of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s lesser known works.
