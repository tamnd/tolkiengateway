The **Desolation of the Morannon** was the dead land that lay just before the gates of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) south of the [Dagorlad](Dagorlad.md). The terrible barrenness of the landscape was due to the labours of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s slaves, who had so defiled the land that it was diseased beyond repair. It was a foul place of horror for absolutely nothing grew in the polluted soil. Pools of water were choked with ash and muds, slag-mounds of crushed and powered rock abounded, and cones of earth fire-blasted and stained with poison rose as if they were part of a graveyard.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md) the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) and men of [Khand](../K/Khand.md) defeated the northern army of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and killed King [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) of Gondor on a hill in the Desolation of the Morannon,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> on which King [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) would later fight the forces of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), led by [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md), reached the Desolation of the Morannon on [4 March](../_/4_March.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md). The hobbits spent [the next day](../_/5_March.md) hiding in sight of the Gate, and at dusk they resumed their route.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../H/Host_of_the_West.md" title="Host of the West">Host of the West</a> camped in the Desolation of the Morannon on [24 March](../_/24_March.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) and fought against the forces of Sauron on the slag-hills in the Desolation of the Morannon on [25 March](../_/25_March.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
