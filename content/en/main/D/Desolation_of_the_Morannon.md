The **Desolation of the Morannon** was the dead land that lay just before the gates of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) south of the [Dagorlad](/wiki/Dagorlad). The terrible barrenness of the landscape was due to the labours of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s slaves, who had so defiled the land that it was diseased beyond repair. It was a foul place of horror for absolutely nothing grew in the polluted soil. Pools of water were choked with ash and muds, slag-mounds of crushed and powered rock abounded, and cones of earth fire-blasted and stained with poison rose as if they were part of a graveyard.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944") the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) and men of [Khand](/wiki/Khand) defeated the northern army of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and killed King [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) of Gondor on a hill in the Desolation of the Morannon,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> on which King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") would later fight the forces of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), led by [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum), reached the Desolation of the Morannon on [4 March](/wiki/4_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"). The hobbits spent [the next day](/wiki/5_March "5 March") hiding in sight of the Gate, and at dusk they resumed their route.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Host_of_the_West" title="Host of the West">Host of the West</a> camped in the Desolation of the Morannon on [24 March](/wiki/24_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") and fought against the forces of Sauron on the slag-hills in the Desolation of the Morannon on [25 March](/wiki/25_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) participated in the Battle of the Somme in 1916<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and remembered miles of seething tortured earth as described in the chapter about the approach to Mordor.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
