The **Dwimorberg** or **Haunted Mountain** was a mountain east of the [Firienfeld](../F/Firienfeld.md) in [Dunharrow](Dunharrow.md)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> in the centre of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Dwimorberg was brooding and black. Its lower slopes were covered with sombre pines, which included the [Dimholt](Dimholt.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> a wood of dark trees.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In a hollow place in front of the root of the mountain stood a single mighty standing stone. Beyond in a deep glen in the wall of rock in a deep glen was the [Dark Door](Dark_Door.md), which led inside the Dwimorberg to the haunted [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The folk of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) said that the [wraiths](../W/Wraiths.md) of the [Oathbreakers](../O/Oathbreakers.md) haunted the mountain, prevented the living to use the Paths of the Dead and came out like shadows into the [Harrowdale](../H/Harrowdale.md) at times of great unquiet and coming death.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "Phantom Mountain" or "Haunted Mountain"<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> in [Old English](../O/Old_English.md), representing the [Rohan language](../R/Rohan_language.md),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> from <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/dwimor#Old_English" title="wiktionary:dwimor">dwimor</a></i> ("phantom", "ghost") and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/beorg#Old_English" title="wiktionary:beorg">beorg</a></i> ("mountain").

The first element *dwimor* is also seen in the names *[Dwimordene](Dwimordene.md)* ([Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md))<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and *[Dwimmerlaik](Dwimmerlaik.md)* (given to the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md)) in the language of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
