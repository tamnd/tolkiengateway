The **Daemon** was popularly<sup id="cite_ref-telegraph_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-telegraph-1">[1]</a></sup> believed<sup id="cite_ref-telegraph_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-telegraph-1">[1]</a></sup> to be a [demon](/wiki/Demon "Demon") who manufactured machines called Motores "in his [secret workshops](/wiki/Phrontisterium "Phrontisterium")"<sup id="cite_ref-telegraph_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-telegraph-1">[1]</a></sup> at [Vaccipratum](/wiki/Vaccipratum) to block "the streets, asphyxiate the inhabitants, and finally explode".<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-2">[2]</a></sup> The people of [Bovadium](/wiki/Bovadium) later worshipped his<sup id="cite_ref-telegraph_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-telegraph-1">[1]</a></sup> Motores "and the traffic jams blocking the roads in and around" Bovadium.<sup id="cite_ref-satire_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-satire-3">[3]</a></sup> As a consequence, the city was destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Vaccipratum* is a Latin name meaning "cow pasture",<sup id="cite_ref-telegraph_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-telegraph-1">[1]</a></sup> "Cowley",<sup id="cite_ref-telegraph_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-telegraph-1">[1]</a></sup> or "cow-meadow".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter)'s [1977](/wiki/1977) [biography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography "J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography") of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), it is mentioned in a summary of *[The Bovadium Fragments](/wiki/The_End_of_Bovadium "The End of Bovadium")* that the "Daemon of Vaccipratum" is "a reference to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_Nuffield" title="wikipedia:Lord Nuffield">Lord Nuffield</a> and his motor-works at Cowley".<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-2">[2]</a></sup>
