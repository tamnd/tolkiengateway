**Durin's Day** is a rare event noted by [Dwarves](Dwarves.md).

The new year of their calendar was the last cycle of the [Moon](../M/Moon.md) to begin in autumn, or in [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md)'s words "the first day of the last moon of Autumn on the threshold of Winter".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When on this day both the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) and Moon may be seen in the sky together, it is called **Durin's Day**.

The [Moon-letters](../M/Moon-letters.md) written on [Thrór's Map](../T/Thrór's_Map.md) predicted that on Durin's Day the last light of the Sun as night fell would reveal the [secret door](../B/Back_Door.md) into the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md).

## Calculation {#Calculation}

Unlike *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) left no precise timetable for the events of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*. However, in *[The History of The Hobbit](../T/The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)*, Tolkien set [19 October](../_/19_October.md) as the date of that year's Durin's Day.<sup id="cite_ref-The_History_of_The_Hobbit_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-The_History_of_The_Hobbit-2">[2]</a></sup>

Before the *History* some calculations were made. According to [Andreas Möhn](../A/Andreas_Möhn.md), the Durin's Day of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md) occurred on [22 October](../_/22_October.md) of the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md). This is based in the assumption that the orbital period of the moon, as described in *The Lord of the Rings*, was about 20 minutes shorter/faster than today.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Karen Wynn Fonstad](../K/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad.md) made a rough estimation based purely on the traveling times and distances, without any astronomical calculations. She puts the Durin's Day on [30 October](../_/30_October.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
