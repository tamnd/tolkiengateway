**Durin's Day** is a rare event noted by [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves).

The new year of their calendar was the last cycle of the [Moon](/wiki/Moon) to begin in autumn, or in [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin)'s words "the first day of the last moon of Autumn on the threshold of Winter".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When on this day both the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and Moon may be seen in the sky together, it is called **Durin's Day**.

The [Moon-letters](/wiki/Moon-letters) written on [Thrór's Map](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r%27s_Map) predicted that on Durin's Day the last light of the Sun as night fell would reveal the [secret door](/wiki/Back_Door "Back Door") into the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain).

## Calculation {#Calculation}

Unlike *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") left no precise timetable for the events of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. However, in *[The History of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit)*, Tolkien set [19 October](/wiki/19_October) as the date of that year's Durin's Day.<sup id="cite_ref-The_History_of_The_Hobbit_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-The_History_of_The_Hobbit-2">[2]</a></sup>

Before the *History* some calculations were made. According to [Andreas Möhn](/wiki/Andreas_M%C3%B6hn), the Durin's Day of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") occurred on [22 October](/wiki/22_October) of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar). This is based in the assumption that the orbital period of the moon, as described in *The Lord of the Rings*, was about 20 minutes shorter/faster than today.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Karen Wynn Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad) made a rough estimation based purely on the traveling times and distances, without any astronomical calculations. She puts the Durin's Day on [30 October](/wiki/30_October).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
