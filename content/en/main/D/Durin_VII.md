**Durin VII**, also known as **Durin the Last**,<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-5">[3]</a></sup> was the last of the six reincarnations of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a>.

## History {#History}

The actions of Durin VII were prophesied long before he was born; his birth was apparently prophesied upon the accession of [Dáin II](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_II) after the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[4]</a></sup>

Durin VII was the direct descendant of [Thorin III Stonehelm](/wiki/Thorin_III_Stonehelm) (according to some sources, he was his son).<sup id="cite_ref-Making_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Making-2">[1]</a></sup>

He led [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk) from [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor) back to [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") and restored the ancient kingdom sometime after [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [171](/wiki/Fourth_Age_171 "Fourth Age 171"). There they remained "until the world grew old and the Dwarves failed and the days of Durin's race were ended."<sup id="cite_ref-Making_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Making-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

One account states that the name *Durin* was a word for "king" in the language of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Men_of_the_North" title="Men of the North">Men of the North</a> in the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-relations_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-relations-7">[5]</a></sup>

Another account states that *Durinn* is one of the Dwarfs in the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. The name means "Sleepy".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2018: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Prince Durin is the son of King <a href="/wiki/Thorin_Stonehelm" title="Thorin Stonehelm">Thorin III Stonehelm</a>. In the game&#39;s storyline other Dwarves have been given that name and yet proved not to be the Father of the Longbeards returned, so a major part of his motivation is to prove whether he truly is or will be Durin VII.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>At the onset of the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, Thorin III send Durin to the <a href="/wiki/Iron_Hills" title="Iron Hills">Iron Hills</a> for his safety, where to his frustration the Prince had sat out the entire war in relative comfort. Seeking to prove himself, he follows a group of the <a href="/wiki/Ironfists" title="Ironfists">Zhélruka</a> dwarves on the pilgrimage to <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ered_Mithrin" title="Ered Mithrin">Ered Mithrin</a>, where they believe their old strongholds can be reclaimed following <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>&#39;s downfall. Despite his son disobeying his direct order, King Thorin puts his trust in him and sends to him both warriors from <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Erebor" title="Erebor">Erebor</a> for reinforcements and the Red Axe of <a href="/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot" title="Dáin Ironfoot">Dáin Ironfoot</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The dwarves reclaim Skarháld, a fortress outpost of <a href="/wiki/D%C3%A1in%27s_hall" title="Dáin&#39;s hall">Dáin&#39;s halls</a>, but Durin sets his sights on a grander prize. Boosted by <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Longbeard" title="Longbeard">Longbeard</a> reinforements coming from <a href="/wiki/Eriador" title="Eriador">Eriador</a> to <a href="/wiki/Iron_Hills" title="Iron Hills">Iron Hills</a>, Durin marches on <a href="/wiki/Gundabad" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a> itself which is now ruled by Gorgar, son of <a href="/wiki/Bolg" title="Bolg">Bolg</a>. In summer of <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3019" title="Third Age 3019">T.A. 3019</a>, the &#34;War of Three Peaks&#34; breaks out, in which the dwarves emerge victorious due to the <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">orcs</a> being undermined by both <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmarim</a> cultists and the <a href="/wiki/Hobgoblins" title="Hobgoblins">Hobgoblins</a> from the North. Deep beneath Gundabad, Durin has an experience that causes him to remember his previous lives and confidently assert himself as Durin the Deathless returned.</dd></dl>
