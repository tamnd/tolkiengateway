<i><b>Dungeon Adventure</b></i> is a text-adventure made by [Pete](/wiki/Pete_Austin "Pete Austin"), [Mike](/wiki/Mike_Austin "Mike Austin") and [Nick Austin](/wiki/Nick_Austin).<sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FAQ_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FAQ-3">[3]</a></sup> It covers the events of *[the Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Lord of the Rings")* and is the third and last part of *[The Middle-Earth Trilogy](/wiki/The_Middle-Earth_Trilogy)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-4">[4]</a></sup> Later, all names from [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s work were changed and it was published together with *[Colossal Adventure](/wiki/Colossal_Adventure)* and *[Adventure Quest](/wiki/Adventure_Quest)* as *[The Jewels of Darkness](/wiki/Jewels_of_Darkness "Jewels of Darkness")* by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecomsoft#Firebird" title="wikipedia:Telecomsoft">Firebird</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecomsoft#Rainbird" title="wikipedia:Telecomsoft">Rainbird Software</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FAQ_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FAQ-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Story {#Story}

After the defeat of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and the destruction of [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr), players must collect the Dark Lord's remaining treasures in the Tower's dark dungeons, where Sauron and his minions await.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
