**Dissipate** (Chinese: 消散) is an epic black metal band from Beijing, China. Its 2020 EP *Mbelekôrolindalë* takes its name from a Quenya construction meaning "the music of [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md)" (compare the *[Ainulindalë](../A/Ainulindalë.md)*), and its songs are named after events such as the [Dagor Bragollach](Dagor_Bragollach.md) and the [Darkening of Valinor](Darkening_of_Valinor.md).
