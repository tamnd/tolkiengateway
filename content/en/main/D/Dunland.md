**Dunland** was a country near the western slopes of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) at their southern end.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was located east of the [Old South Road](/wiki/Old_South_Road), south of the river [Glanduin](/wiki/Glanduin) and north of the river [Isen](/wiki/Isen) in the region of [Enedwaith](/wiki/Enedwaith).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Far from the centres of population of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), its inhabitants at times included the [Men](/wiki/Men) known as [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings)<sup id="cite_ref-OfMen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfMen-3">[3]</a></sup> as well as [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors)<sup id="cite_ref-TA1150_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1150-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA1630_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1630-5">[5]</a></sup> and [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin1073_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin1073-6">[6]</a></sup>

Dunland consisted of hills at the feet of the Misty Mountains, open country and wood. Northern Dunland was a green, pleasant and uninhabited country.<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-7">[7]</a></sup> At the end of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) some Dunlendings were herdsmen.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### First Men {#First_Men}

In the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), Dunland first acquired a significant population of [Men](/wiki/Men) when those who had dwelt in the forests of Enedwaith south of the [Gwathló](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3) fled from the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) after they began to cut down all of the trees.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

When the kingdoms of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) were established early in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) the land of Enedwaith (and the region of Dunland) were largely ignored, although the inhabitants were nominally subjects of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Stay of the Stoors {#Stay_of_the_Stoors}

About [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1150](/wiki/Third_Age_1150 "Third Age 1150"), the Hobbit-breed known as the [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors) left their early homeland in the upper vales of the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) and crossed the [Redhorn Pass](/wiki/Caradhras#The_Redhorn_Gate "Caradhras"). Some settled in the [Angle](/wiki/Angle) and others travelled down the [Loudwater](/wiki/Bruinen "Bruinen") and settled in Dunland.<sup id="cite_ref-TA1150_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1150-4">[4]</a></sup> While the Stoors in the Angle vacated that area in [1356](/wiki/Third_Age_1356 "Third Age 1356"), those living in Dunland remained until around [1630](/wiki/Third_Age_1630 "Third Age 1630") when they migrated to the newly founded [Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-TA1630_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1630-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Middle Years of the Third Age {#Middle_Years_of_the_Third_Age}

The [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) swept through the north-west of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) in the years [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636")<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> - [1637](/wiki/Third_Age_1637 "Third Age 1637"). Dunland suffered, but to a lesser extent than in other regions due to their self-isolation. After the end of the royal line in Gondor the Dunlendings ceased to be subjects of the realm. During the years of the [Watchful Peace](/wiki/Watchful_Peace) ([1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975") – [2050](/wiki/Third_Age_2050 "Third Age 2050")), as the people of [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) dwindled, the Dunlendings began drifting across the [Isen](/wiki/Isen).<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-10">[10]</a></sup>

The expansion of the Dunlendings to the southeast of Dunland was checked when the new realm of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) was established in [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510").<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> In the subsequent centuries there was tension between the Dunlendings and the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim).<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Dwarves {#Dwarves}

After [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770"),<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> Dwarves fleeing the [Sack of Erebor](/wiki/Sack_of_Erebor) settled in Dunland, from where [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) departed when he and his companion [Nár](/wiki/N%C3%A1r) journeyed to [Moria](/wiki/Moria) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2790](/wiki/Third_Age_2790 "Third Age 2790").<sup id="cite_ref-Durin1073_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin1073-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA2790_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2790-14">[14]</a></sup> After the [Battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2799](/wiki/Third_Age_2799 "Third Age 2799"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA2799_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2799-15">[15]</a></sup> provoked by the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs)' brutal slaying of Thrór<sup id="cite_ref-Durin1073_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin1073-6">[6]</a></sup> in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2790](/wiki/Third_Age_2790 "Third Age 2790"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA2790_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2790-14">[14]</a></sup> [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in "Thráin") and [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) led the remnants of their followers back to Dunland. They left soon after, to eventually settle in the east of the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) beyond the river [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2802](/wiki/Third_Age_2802 "Third Age 2802").<sup id="cite_ref-TA2799_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2799-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

### Later Third Age {#Later_Third_Age}

[Beren's](</wiki/Beren_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Beren (Steward of Gondor)") decision to trust Saruman however had severe consequences, as before and around the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the Wizard inflamed the Dunlendings' grievance and enmity to the Rohirrim<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> and concentrated a great military force which besieged them at the [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep).<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> After the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg), the Rohirrim allowed the surviving Dunlendings to return to their homes. The Rohirrim required that all hostilities cease, and that the Dunlendings retreat behind the [Isen](/wiki/Isen) river again.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

After the War the four Hobbits, [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck"), and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), with the company of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), and others journeyed through Dunland on their way home. While traversing the region they met two beggars, Saruman and [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma").<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Dunland* contains the element *dunn*.<sup id="cite_ref-OfMen_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfMen-3">[3]</a></sup> The English adjective *dun* means "dark, dusky, dull-hued".<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> This name was given by Rohirrim of neighbouring [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) after arriving in nearby [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) in the later [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age). The element *dunn* has no relation to the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word *[dûn](/wiki/D%C3%BBn "Dûn")* ("west").<sup id="cite_ref-OfMen_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfMen-3">[3]</a></sup>
