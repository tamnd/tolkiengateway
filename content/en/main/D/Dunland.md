**Dunland** was a country near the western slopes of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) at their southern end.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was located east of the [Old South Road](../O/Old_South_Road.md), south of the river [Glanduin](../G/Glanduin.md) and north of the river [Isen](../I/Isen.md) in the region of [Enedwaith](../E/Enedwaith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Far from the centres of population of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), its inhabitants at times included the [Men](../M/Men.md) known as [Dunlendings](Dunlendings.md)<sup id="cite_ref-OfMen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfMen-3">[3]</a></sup> as well as [Stoors](../S/Stoors.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA1150_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1150-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA1630_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1630-5">[5]</a></sup> and [Dwarves](Dwarves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin1073_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin1073-6">[6]</a></sup>

Dunland consisted of hills at the feet of the Misty Mountains, open country and wood. Northern Dunland was a green, pleasant and uninhabited country.<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-7">[7]</a></sup> At the end of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) some Dunlendings were herdsmen.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### First Men {#First_Men}

In the early [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), Dunland first acquired a significant population of [Men](../M/Men.md) when those who had dwelt in the forests of Enedwaith south of the [Gwathló](../G/Gwathló.md) fled from the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) after they began to cut down all of the trees.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

When the kingdoms of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) were established early in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) the land of Enedwaith (and the region of Dunland) were largely ignored, although the inhabitants were nominally subjects of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Stay of the Stoors {#Stay_of_the_Stoors}

About [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1150](../T/Third_Age_1150.md), the Hobbit-breed known as the [Stoors](../S/Stoors.md) left their early homeland in the upper vales of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) and crossed the [Redhorn Pass](../C/Caradhras.md#The_Redhorn_Gate). Some settled in the [Angle](../A/Angle.md) and others travelled down the [Loudwater](../B/Bruinen.md) and settled in Dunland.<sup id="cite_ref-TA1150_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1150-4">[4]</a></sup> While the Stoors in the Angle vacated that area in [1356](../T/Third_Age_1356.md), those living in Dunland remained until around [1630](../T/Third_Age_1630.md) when they migrated to the newly founded [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA1630_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1630-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Middle Years of the Third Age {#Middle_Years_of_the_Third_Age}

The [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) swept through the north-west of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) in the years [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md)<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> - [1637](../T/Third_Age_1637.md). Dunland suffered, but to a lesser extent than in other regions due to their self-isolation. After the end of the royal line in Gondor the Dunlendings ceased to be subjects of the realm. During the years of the [Watchful Peace](../W/Watchful_Peace.md) ([1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md) – [2050](../T/Third_Age_2050.md)), as the people of [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) dwindled, the Dunlendings began drifting across the [Isen](../I/Isen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-10">[10]</a></sup>

The expansion of the Dunlendings to the southeast of Dunland was checked when the new realm of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) was established in [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> In the subsequent centuries there was tension between the Dunlendings and the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Dwarves {#Dwarves}

After [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2770](../T/Third_Age_2770.md),<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> Dwarves fleeing the [Sack of Erebor](../S/Sack_of_Erebor.md) settled in Dunland, from where [Thrór](../T/Thrór.md) departed when he and his companion [Nár](../N/Nár.md) journeyed to [Moria](../M/Moria.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2790](../T/Third_Age_2790.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin1073_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin1073-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA2790_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2790-14">[14]</a></sup> After the [Battle of Azanulbizar](../B/Battle_of_Azanulbizar.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2799](../T/Third_Age_2799.md),<sup id="cite_ref-TA2799_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2799-15">[15]</a></sup> provoked by the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md)' brutal slaying of Thrór<sup id="cite_ref-Durin1073_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin1073-6">[6]</a></sup> in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2790](../T/Third_Age_2790.md),<sup id="cite_ref-TA2790_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2790-14">[14]</a></sup> [Thráin II](../T/Thráin.md) and [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) led the remnants of their followers back to Dunland. They left soon after, to eventually settle in the east of the [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md) beyond the river [Lhûn](../L/Lhûn.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2802](../T/Third_Age_2802.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA2799_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2799-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

### Later Third Age {#Later_Third_Age}

[Beren's](<../B/Beren_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) decision to trust Saruman however had severe consequences, as before and around the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the Wizard inflamed the Dunlendings' grievance and enmity to the Rohirrim<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> and concentrated a great military force which besieged them at the [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md).<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> After the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md), the Rohirrim allowed the surviving Dunlendings to return to their homes. The Rohirrim required that all hostilities cease, and that the Dunlendings retreat behind the [Isen](../I/Isen.md) river again.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

After the War the four Hobbits, [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md), and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), with the company of [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md), [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md), and others journeyed through Dunland on their way home. While traversing the region they met two beggars, Saruman and [Gríma Wormtongue](../G/Gríma.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Dunland* contains the element *dunn*.<sup id="cite_ref-OfMen_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfMen-3">[3]</a></sup> The English adjective *dun* means "dark, dusky, dull-hued".<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> This name was given by Rohirrim of neighbouring [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) after arriving in nearby [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) in the later [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md). The element *dunn* has no relation to the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word *[dûn](Dûn.md)* ("west").<sup id="cite_ref-OfMen_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfMen-3">[3]</a></sup>
