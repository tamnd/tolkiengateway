**Déorwine** was the Chief of the [King's Riders](../K/King's_Riders.md) during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

## History {#History}

Déorwine replaced Háma in the post when the latter was killed at [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md). He and six of the men under his command (<a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../H/Harding.md" title="Harding">Harding</a>, [Guthláf](../G/Guthláf.md), [Herefara](../H/Herefara.md), [Herubrand](../H/Herubrand.md), <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../H/Horn.md" title="Horn">Horn</a> and [Fastred](<../F/Fastred_(rider_of_Rohan).md>)) were slain at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Déorwine* is an [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name, which means "brave-friend"<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup> or "fierce-friend"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>. It probably contains the Old English elements <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/deor#Old_English" title="wiktionary:deor">dēor</a></i> ("brave", "bold (as a wild beast)") and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/wine#Old_English" title="wiktionary:wine">wine</a></i> ("friend").<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup>
