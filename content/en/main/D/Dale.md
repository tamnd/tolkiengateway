**Dale** was a valley between two arms of the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>, a town of the [Men of Dale](/wiki/Men_of_Dale) in that valley<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and a realm of the [king](/wiki/King_of_Dale "King of Dale") of those Men<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-1">[1]</a></sup>.

## Description {#Description}

The valley of Dale lay between the south-western and south-eastern arms of the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain). The [River Running](/wiki/River_Running) sprang from beneath the mountain and issued from the [Front Gate](/wiki/Front_Gate) of the [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) in the mountain wall, forming a waterfall that fell into the valley below. The river flowed briefly south and then made a great eastward loop. After almost completing its loop, the river flowed west for a while and then continued to flow south to the [Long Lake](/wiki/Long_Lake). The town of Dale was located near the southern end of the valley nestled within the eastward loop of the river on its western side.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

An old road ran from the Front Gate of the Lonely Mountain along the eastern bank of the River Running down into the valley and then led to the east towards the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills). A road branched off to the west to a bridge at the beginning of the eastward bend of the river where it crossed over the River Running. From the western end of the bridge, one road ran east to the town of Dale and another road ran in the direction of [Ravenhill](/wiki/Ravenhill).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Dale was known as a merry town that traded, mainly in food-supplies, for the skills and craft-pieces of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor "Dwarves of Erebor"). Fathers from Dale paid handsome amounts to the Dwarves to take their sons as apprentices, and the town of Dale became famous for wonderful and magical toys. Trade flowed up and down the River Running to and from [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town) on the Long Lake and beyond.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-9">[9]</a></sup> The town was also renowned for its bells.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Foundation {#Foundation}

There is no record of when Men first settled in the valley of Dale. It is implied that Men were already living in or near Dale and were joined by refugees of the [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen) after the latter were defeated by the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) in the [Battle of the Plains](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Plains) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1856](/wiki/Third_Age_1856 "Third Age 1856").<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2590](/wiki/Third_Age_2590 "Third Age 2590"),<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> King [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) reestablished the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) in the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain)<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-14">[14]</a></sup>. The Dwarven kingdom prospered and in those days, [Men](/wiki/Men) living to the south gradually spread up the River Running as far as the valley and built the town of Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-9">[9]</a></sup> The town shared in the prosperity of the Dwarves<sup id="cite_ref-Party_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-9">[9]</a></sup> and it was initially governed by the [Lord of Dale](/wiki/Lord_of_Dale), of whom the last was named [Girion](/wiki/Girion)<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-15">[15]</a></sup>.

### Destruction {#Destruction}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770"),<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> the dragon [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) attacked the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) and the town of Dale<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-14">[14]</a></sup>. Although the [Men of Dale](/wiki/Men_of_Dale) fielded warriors against the monster, they could not prevent him from killing or scattering the Dwarves and then occupying the Lonely Mountain. After the attack the dragon would crawl out of the [Front Gate](/wiki/Front_Gate) of the Mountain by night and carry away people (especially maidens) from Dale to eat. The remaining population soon fled and the deserted town fell into ruins.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-9">[9]</a></sup> Among the survivors of the ruin of Dale were Girion's wife and child who escaped down the River Running.<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-15">[15]</a></sup>

### Re-establishment {#Re-establishment}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), a member of [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) sneaked into the Lonely Mountain causing the angered dragon to attack<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> and destroy [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town) in the [Long Lake](/wiki/Long_Lake)<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-15">[15]</a></sup>. During the defence of Lake-town [Bard](/wiki/Bard), a descendant of Girion, managed to kill Smaug with the [black arrow](/wiki/Black_arrow "Black arrow") from his yew bow.<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_15-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-15">[15]</a></sup>

Three years after the killing of Smaug, Bard rebuilt Dale and became the [King of Dale](/wiki/King_of_Dale).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> Dale soon again enjoyed prosperity. Men from the Long Lake, the South, and the West came to Dale and the whole valley became tilled again. [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town) was rebuilt and grew wealthy from trade up and down the River Running.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> The people of Dale became known as the [Bardings](/wiki/Bardings), after their new ruler.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup>

Bard was succeeded by his son, [Bain](/wiki/Bain) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2977](/wiki/Third_Age_2977 "Third Age 2977").<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup>

### Later history {#Later_history}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3007](/wiki/Third_Age_3007 "Third Age 3007") [Brand](/wiki/Brand), the son of Bain, became the King of Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup> During his reign his realm extended far south and east of [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town).<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-1">[1]</a></sup> The eastern border of his realm is not recorded, but it may have been as far east as the river [Carnen](/wiki/Carnen).<sup id="cite_ref-Council_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-25">[25]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Great_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-26">[26]</a></sup>

In [3017](/wiki/Third_Age_3017 "Third Age 3017"), a messenger from [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) came to the [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor) and they found out that messengers from Mordor had also come to King Brand in Dale. Despite words offering [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s friendship, the Dark Lord's forces began gathering on the eastern borders of the realm of King Brand.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_25-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-25">[25]</a></sup> About the same time that [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) was attacked (on [15 March](/wiki/15_March) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")), a host of Sauron's allies, including [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings), which had been a threat to the borders of the realm of Dale for a long time, crossed the river Carnen and drove Brand back to Dale. On [17 March](/wiki/17_March), the [Battle of Dale](/wiki/Battle_of_Dale) began. King Brand and King [Dáin II Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_II_Ironfoot) were both killed and the [Men of Dale](/wiki/Men_of_Dale) and the Dwarves were driven into the Mountain. The enemy could not take the gates and a siege began.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[27]</a></sup> However, when news of the [fall of Sauron](/wiki/Downfall_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr "Downfall of Barad-dûr") came the enemy forces lost heart,<sup id="cite_ref-Great_26-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-26">[26]</a></sup> and on [27 March](/wiki/27_March)<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[28]</a></sup> the besieged Dwarves and Men burst forth from the mountain and the new King of Dale, [Bard II](/wiki/Bard_II), and the new [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain), [Thorin III Stonehelm](/wiki/Thorin_Stonehelm "Thorin Stonehelm"), drove the enemy away and the Easterlings troubled the kingdom of Dale no more<sup id="cite_ref-Great_26-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-26">[26]</a></sup>.

King Bard II sent an ambassador to the crowning of King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"). The kingdom of Dale remained independent but in friendship with [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and under the protection and crown of the [King of the West](/wiki/King_of_the_West).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_26-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-26">[26]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is possible that Dale received its name, because of its location in a valley.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29">[29]</a></sup> The word *dale* from Old English *dael* means "valley".<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">[30]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

A late draft of what would later become the chapter "[Many Meetings](/wiki/Many_Meetings)" mentions that the realm of Brand "included Esgaroth, and much land to the south of the great falls".<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31">[31]</a></sup>

In two plot notes for *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, Girion is referred to as "king of Dale".<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32">[32]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33">[33]</a></sup> In earlier versions of what would later become the chapters "[Inside Information](/wiki/Inside_Information)" and "[Fire and Water](/wiki/Fire_and_Water)", Girion is already referred to as "lord of Dale".<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34">[34]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35">[35]</a></sup>

<b>2006: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The City of Dale appears in the <i>Erebor</i> map, just south-east of the mountain itself. It appears to contain several houses, taverns and bridges, as well as a statue of a man. The city is built around the mountain&#39;s river.</dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The city appears briefly in the prologue of the film, when the dragon Smaug attacks Erebor.</dd></dl>

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The ruined city of Dale appears briefly when <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Thorin_and_Company" title="Thorin and Company">Dwarves</a> are heading for <a href="/wiki/Lonely_Mountain" title="Lonely Mountain">Erebor</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>After the death of <a href="/wiki/Smaug" title="Smaug">Smaug</a>, the villagers of <a href="/wiki/Lake-town" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a> seek refuge in the ruined city. During the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies" title="Battle of Five Armies">Battle of Five Armies</a>, they hide inside Dale while the <a href="/wiki/Lake-men" title="Lake-men">Lake-men</a> combat the enemy forces. When the <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orc</a> army attacks the ruined city, Bard and the Lake-towners run to defend it, leaving the <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Elves" title="Elves">Elves</a> to protect the gates of Erebor. The Elves later come to join the <a href="/wiki/Men" title="Men">Men</a> in defending the city.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Dale.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/9/9f/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Dale.jpg/300px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Dale.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="169" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/9/9f/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Dale.jpg/450px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Dale.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/9/9f/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Dale.jpg/600px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Dale.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1920" data-file-height="1080"/></a><figcaption>Dale in <i>The Lord of the Rings Online</i></figcaption></figure>

<dl><dd>Dale was originally introduced as a standalone instance taking place during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Dale" title="Battle of Dale">Battle of Dale</a>. Several years later, a proper explorable version of the city and the surrounding lands were added as part of the &#34;Strongholds of the North&#34; update. Still rebuilding after the recent battle, Dale is now ruled by King <a href="/wiki/Bard_II" title="Bard II">Bard II</a> and his mother, Queen Regent Erna. A large statue of <a href="/wiki/Bard" title="Bard">Bard</a> the Bowman stands at the northern entrance of the city.</dd></dl>
