**Dúnhere** was the chieftain of the folk of [Harrowdale](/wiki/Harrowdale), and a valiant captain who fought in the [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen) and the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).

## History {#History}

Dúnhere was a nephew of [Erkenbrand](/wiki/Erkenbrand).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He was the chieftain of the folk of [Harrowdale](/wiki/Harrowdale) and the lord of Harrowdale.<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-2">[2]</a></sup>

He served under [Grimbold](/wiki/Grimbold) in the [Battles of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Battles_of_the_Fords_of_Isen). Grimbold placed all the riders for whom horses were available under the command of Dúnhere to cover the retreat of his forces to the south in the night after the [Second Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Dúnhere did not accompany [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and Erkenbrand to the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg), but went to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) instead. [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) counselled him and the other captains to assemble the riders of Rohan in Harrowdale and to await King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) there, rather than in the open fields around Edoras, and to light as few fires as possible. Several days later, [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and the rest of the riders mustered there for the ride to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-2">[2]</a></sup>

Dúnhere was slain at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields). He was remembered in the [Song of the Mounds of Mundburg](/wiki/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dúnhere* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name, which means "hill-warrior".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> It contains the Old English elements <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/dun#Old_English" title="wiktionary:dun">dūn</a></i> ("hill") and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/here#Old_English" title="wiktionary:here">here</a></i> ("army", "host").<sup id="cite_ref-RC_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dúnhere is the Lord of <a href="/wiki/Dunharrow" title="Dunharrow">Dunharrow</a> and a nearby small village of <a href="/wiki/Underharrow" title="Underharrow">Underharrow</a>. He left his wife Ellen Fremedon in charge before departing with <a href="/wiki/Erkenbrand" title="Erkenbrand">Erkenbrand</a> for the Fords. The player sees Dúnhere shortly before the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Battle_of_Helm%27s_Deep" title="Battle of Helm&#39;s Deep">battle of Helm&#39;s Deep</a> but not does get a chance to interact with him. They do not meet properly until the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Druadan_Forest" title="Druadan Forest">Druadan Forest</a> on the day before the Rohirrim army arrives to <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>. Dúnhere is slain by an arrow during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>.</dd></dl>
