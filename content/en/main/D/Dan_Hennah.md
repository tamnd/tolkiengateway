**Dan Hennah** served as the production designer for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"). He also portrayed the [Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took") in the [extended edition](</wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_(extended_edition)> "The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey (extended edition)") of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)*.

## Awards {#Awards}

- [2004](/wiki/2004) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.oscars.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Academy Award</a></b>, Best Art Direction-Set Decoration, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*
- [2013](/wiki/2013) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.saturnawards.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Saturn Award</a></b>, Best Production Design, *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)*
- [2014](/wiki/2014) - **Saturn Award**, Best Production Design, *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)*
