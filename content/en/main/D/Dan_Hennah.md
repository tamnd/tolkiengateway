**Dan Hennah** served as the production designer for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) and [*The Hobbit* (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>). He also portrayed the [Old Took](../G/Gerontius_Took.md) in the [extended edition](<../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey_(extended_edition).md>) of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md)*.

## Awards {#Awards}

- [2004](../_/2004.md) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.oscars.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Academy Award</a></b>, Best Art Direction-Set Decoration, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*
- [2013](../_/2013.md) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.saturnawards.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Saturn Award</a></b>, Best Production Design, *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md)*
- [2014](../_/2014.md) - **Saturn Award**, Best Production Design, *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md)*
