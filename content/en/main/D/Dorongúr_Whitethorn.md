**Dorongúr Whitethorn** is an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) and one of the original inhabitants of [Edhelion](../E/Edhelion.md). He fought in the Battle of Edhelion and defended the gate against [Dourhands](Dourhands.md) and [Goblins](../G/Goblins.md). Eventually a [Snow-troll](../S/Snow-troll.md) destroyed the gate and attempted to kill Dorongúr and his companions, including the player, but they were rescued by [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) who killed the Troll.<sup id="cite_ref-Refuge_of_Edhelion_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Refuge_of_Edhelion-1">[1]</a></sup>

Dorongúr led Elrond and the player to the place where they saw that [Skorgrím Dourhand](../S/Skorgrím_Dourhand.md) and two [Dourhands](Dourhands.md) had surrounded [Talagan Silvertongue](../T/Talagan_Silvertongue.md), the lord of Edhelion. Talagan Silvertongue, in attempt to protect Edhelion's relics, destroys the entrance of the palace in which not only the Dourhands die, but Talagan himself too.<sup id="cite_ref-Refuge_of_Edhelion_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Refuge_of_Edhelion-1">[1]</a></sup> Dorongúr and the survivors of Edhelion leave the city, on Elrond's counsel. Unlike many of the other survivors Dorongúr does not sail to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) but remains in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Eventually after six centuries Dorongúr leads a company of Elves, against the counsel of Elrond, to Edhelion and with the aid of [Dorollas](../L/List_of_minor_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_characters_in_the_Ered_Luin.md#Dorollas) and the player attempts to restore the city.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> But the city is befouled by many evils and soon was attacked by a group of Goblins.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Eventually it was discovered that the Goblins were allied with [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) and the [Dourhands](Dourhands.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> who wanted to revive [Skorgrím Dourhand](../S/Skorgrím_Dourhand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Dorongúr leads the Elves and a company of [Longbeards](../L/Longbeards.md) in war against the Goblins and the Dourhands and drive them from <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Thorin%27s_Gate&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Thorin&#39;s Gate (page does not exist)">Thorin&#39;s Gate</a>, while [Dwalin](Dwalin.md) and the player try to stop the reviving of Skorgrím, but they fail.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Dorongúr gives attempt to restore Edhelion up and leads the Elves to the city [Duillond](Duillond.md), where he becomes lord.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
