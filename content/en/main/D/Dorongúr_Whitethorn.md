**Dorongúr Whitethorn** is an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") and one of the original inhabitants of [Edhelion](/wiki/Edhelion). He fought in the Battle of Edhelion and defended the gate against [Dourhands](/wiki/Dourhands) and [Goblins](/wiki/Goblins). Eventually a [Snow-troll](/wiki/Snow-troll) destroyed the gate and attempted to kill Dorongúr and his companions, including the player, but they were rescued by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) who killed the Troll.<sup id="cite_ref-Refuge_of_Edhelion_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Refuge_of_Edhelion-1">[1]</a></sup>

Dorongúr led Elrond and the player to the place where they saw that [Skorgrím Dourhand](/wiki/Skorgr%C3%ADm_Dourhand) and two [Dourhands](/wiki/Dourhands) had surrounded [Talagan Silvertongue](/wiki/Talagan_Silvertongue), the lord of Edhelion. Talagan Silvertongue, in attempt to protect Edhelion's relics, destroys the entrance of the palace in which not only the Dourhands die, but Talagan himself too.<sup id="cite_ref-Refuge_of_Edhelion_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Refuge_of_Edhelion-1">[1]</a></sup> Dorongúr and the survivors of Edhelion leave the city, on Elrond's counsel. Unlike many of the other survivors Dorongúr does not sail to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) but remains in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Eventually after six centuries Dorongúr leads a company of Elves, against the counsel of Elrond, to Edhelion and with the aid of [Dorollas](/wiki/List_of_minor_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_characters_in_the_Ered_Luin#Dorollas "List of minor The Lord of the Rings Online characters in the Ered Luin") and the player attempts to restore the city.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> But the city is befouled by many evils and soon was attacked by a group of Goblins.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Eventually it was discovered that the Goblins were allied with [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) and the [Dourhands](/wiki/Dourhands),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> who wanted to revive [Skorgrím Dourhand](/wiki/Skorgr%C3%ADm_Dourhand).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Dorongúr leads the Elves and a company of [Longbeards](/wiki/Longbeards) in war against the Goblins and the Dourhands and drive them from <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Thorin%27s_Gate&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Thorin&#39;s Gate (page does not exist)">Thorin&#39;s Gate</a>, while [Dwalin](/wiki/Dwalin) and the player try to stop the reviving of Skorgrím, but they fail.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Dorongúr gives attempt to restore Edhelion up and leads the Elves to the city [Duillond](/wiki/Duillond), where he becomes lord.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
