The **Dwarves of Erebor**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Cirth_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirth-3">[3]</a></sup>, **Folk under the Mountain**<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or **Dwarf-folk of Erebor**<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> were those who dwelt in the halls of [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md), beneath the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md).

## History {#History}

### Kingdom {#Kingdom}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/9d/Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="375" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/9d/Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/9d/Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg 2x" data-file-width="960" data-file-height="1200"/></a><figcaption><a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a> - Heart of the Mountain</figcaption></figure>

[Thráin I](../T/Thráin_I.md) established the [Kingdom under the Mountain](../K/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain.md) fleeing from Moria. During his time the [Arkenstone](../A/Arkenstone.md) was discovered, the most prized possession of the Kingdom. His son, [Thorin I](../T/Thorin_I.md), later became [King under the Mountain](../K/King_under_the_Mountain.md) but after seeing the wealth of the [Dwarves of the Grey Mountains](Dwarves_of_the_Grey_Mountains.md), he abandoned Erebor.

When the [Cold-drakes](../C/Cold-drakes.md) invaded the Grey Mountains, they drove most of the Dwarves out. [Thrór](../T/Thrór.md), a descendant of Thorin I, took some of his people and flew the Grey Mountains for Erebor. His brother [Grór](../G/Grór.md) continued east with a great following of Durin's folk to the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md) while himself refounded the kingdom of Erebor in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2590](../T/Third_Age_2590.md).

### Second Kingdom {#Second_Kingdom}

The Dwarves gained the friendship of the [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md) along the [Celduin](../C/Celduin.md) and had much trade of goods, beautiful trinkets, and weapons with the town of [Dale](Dale.md). The Dwarves of Erebor also had much traffic of ores with their kinsmen in the Iron Hills and the region had peace and prosperity for many years.

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/D/David_T._Wenzel_-_Dwarf-woman.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e0/David_T._Wenzel_-_Dwarf-woman.jpg/300px-David_T._Wenzel_-_Dwarf-woman.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="271" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/e/e0/David_T._Wenzel_-_Dwarf-woman.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="391" data-file-height="353"/></a><figcaption>The Dwarves flee Erebor and Dale</figcaption></figure>

The Dwarves were properous, and made great wealth for themselves until they were dispossessed of their immense wealth by [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) the Dragon, who flew south from the [Ered Mithrin](../G/Grey_Mountains.md) and [sacked](../S/Sack_of_Erebor.md) their kingdom in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2770](../T/Third_Age_2770.md).

The Dwarves reclaimed their inheritance many years later under the leadership of [Thorin II](../T/Thorin.md). But it was the descendants of [Dáin II Ironfoot](Dáin_Ironfoot.md) that ruled there ever after.

### Third Kingdom {#Third_Kingdom}

After the restoration thanks to [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md), the Dwarves became again rich and prosperous under Dáin's rule, as the Dwarves exceeded their ancestors in masonry and mining. Dale was rebuilt as a kingdom and they had close friendship with the [Elves of Mirkwood](../E/Elves_of_Mirkwood.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Balin](../B/Balin.md) and other Dwarves set out to [reclaim Moria](../B/Balin's_Colony~64118.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2989](../T/Third_Age_2989.md). Dáin did not give his blessing and could not dissuade them.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-7">[7]</a></sup> It was only later learned that the colony was destroyed after five years.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), a messenger came from [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md), enquiring about "[Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)" from "[Shire](../T/The_Shire.md)", promising the last three [Dwarf-rings](../S/Seven_Rings.md) to Dáin. The King asked time to think and he sent [Glóin](../G/Glóin.md) and his son [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) to [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) for council, and to warn Bilbo.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-7">[7]</a></sup>

The [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) reached the North; King [Brand](../B/Brand.md) fighting the [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) retreated from the River [Carnen](../C/Carnen.md) and was given aid by Dwarves in the [Battle of Dale](../B/Battle_of_Dale.md). After three days of fighting Dwarves and Men were driven back, and Brand was slain, followed by Dáin.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Several days later the Easterlings learned that the War was lost. When the new King [Thorin III Stonehelm](../T/Thorin_Stonehelm.md), son of Dáin, and Brand's [Bard II](../B/Bard_II.md) saw this they came out of the mountain and routed their enemies.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-10">[10]</a></sup>

Little is known of the reign of Thorin III, other than the Dwarves helped rebuild the cities of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) and the fortress of [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md). Some of the Dwarves colonized the [Glittering Caves](../G/Glittering_Caves.md).

## Language and writing {#Language_and_writing}

The Dwarves of Erebor spoke [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md) but used the [Language of Dale](../L/Language_of_Dale.md) for their public names.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

Coming from Moria, the Dwarves brought with them the [Angerthas Moria](../A/Angerthas_Moria.md), which however was modified further. For some reason some [runes](../R/Runes.md) of Angerthas Moria were reverted back to their [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) values in [Angerthas](../A/Angerthas.md). The resulting variation was known as [Angerthas Erebor](../A/Angerthas_Erebor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirth_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirth-3">[3]</a></sup>
