The **Dwarves of Erebor**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Cirth_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirth-3">[3]</a></sup>, **Folk under the Mountain**<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or **Dwarf-folk of Erebor**<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> were those who dwelt in the halls of [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"), beneath the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain).

## History {#History}

### Kingdom {#Kingdom}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/9/9d/Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="375" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/9/9d/Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/9/9d/Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Heart_of_the_Mountain.jpg 2x" data-file-width="960" data-file-height="1200"/></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a> - Heart of the Mountain</figcaption></figure>

[Thráin I](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_I) established the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) fleeing from Moria. During his time the [Arkenstone](/wiki/Arkenstone) was discovered, the most prized possession of the Kingdom. His son, [Thorin I](/wiki/Thorin_I), later became [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain) but after seeing the wealth of the [Dwarves of the Grey Mountains](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Grey_Mountains), he abandoned Erebor.

When the [Cold-drakes](/wiki/Cold-drakes) invaded the Grey Mountains, they drove most of the Dwarves out. [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r), a descendant of Thorin I, took some of his people and flew the Grey Mountains for Erebor. His brother [Grór](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3r) continued east with a great following of Durin's folk to the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) while himself refounded the kingdom of Erebor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2590](/wiki/Third_Age_2590 "Third Age 2590").

### Second Kingdom {#Second_Kingdom}

The Dwarves gained the friendship of the [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen) along the [Celduin](/wiki/Celduin) and had much trade of goods, beautiful trinkets, and weapons with the town of [Dale](/wiki/Dale). The Dwarves of Erebor also had much traffic of ores with their kinsmen in the Iron Hills and the region had peace and prosperity for many years.

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:David_T._Wenzel_-_Dwarf-woman.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/e/e0/David_T._Wenzel_-_Dwarf-woman.jpg/300px-David_T._Wenzel_-_Dwarf-woman.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="271" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/e/e0/David_T._Wenzel_-_Dwarf-woman.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="391" data-file-height="353"/></a><figcaption>The Dwarves flee Erebor and Dale</figcaption></figure>

The Dwarves were properous, and made great wealth for themselves until they were dispossessed of their immense wealth by [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) the Dragon, who flew south from the [Ered Mithrin](/wiki/Grey_Mountains "Grey Mountains") and [sacked](/wiki/Sack_of_Erebor "Sack of Erebor") their kingdom in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770").

The Dwarves reclaimed their inheritance many years later under the leadership of [Thorin II](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin"). But it was the descendants of [Dáin II Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot") that ruled there ever after.

### Third Kingdom {#Third_Kingdom}

After the restoration thanks to [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company), the Dwarves became again rich and prosperous under Dáin's rule, as the Dwarves exceeded their ancestors in masonry and mining. Dale was rebuilt as a kingdom and they had close friendship with the [Elves of Mirkwood](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Balin](/wiki/Balin) and other Dwarves set out to [reclaim Moria](/wiki/Balin%27s_Colony "Balin's Colony") in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2989](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989"). Dáin did not give his blessing and could not dissuade them.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-7">[7]</a></sup> It was only later learned that the colony was destroyed after five years.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), a messenger came from [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), enquiring about "[Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins")" from "[Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")", promising the last three [Dwarf-rings](/wiki/Seven_Rings "Seven Rings") to Dáin. The King asked time to think and he sent [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in) and his son [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) to [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) for council, and to warn Bilbo.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-7">[7]</a></sup>

The [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) reached the North; King [Brand](/wiki/Brand) fighting the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) retreated from the River [Carnen](/wiki/Carnen) and was given aid by Dwarves in the [Battle of Dale](/wiki/Battle_of_Dale). After three days of fighting Dwarves and Men were driven back, and Brand was slain, followed by Dáin.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Several days later the Easterlings learned that the War was lost. When the new King [Thorin III Stonehelm](/wiki/Thorin_Stonehelm "Thorin Stonehelm"), son of Dáin, and Brand's [Bard II](/wiki/Bard_II) saw this they came out of the mountain and routed their enemies.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-10">[10]</a></sup>

Little is known of the reign of Thorin III, other than the Dwarves helped rebuild the cities of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) and the fortress of [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep). Some of the Dwarves colonized the [Glittering Caves](/wiki/Glittering_Caves).

## Language and writing {#Language_and_writing}

The Dwarves of Erebor spoke [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) but used the [Language of Dale](/wiki/Language_of_Dale) for their public names.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

Coming from Moria, the Dwarves brought with them the [Angerthas Moria](/wiki/Angerthas_Moria), which however was modified further. For some reason some [runes](/wiki/Runes "Runes") of Angerthas Moria were reverted back to their [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) values in [Angerthas](/wiki/Angerthas). The resulting variation was known as [Angerthas Erebor](/wiki/Angerthas_Erebor).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirth_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirth-3">[3]</a></sup>
