**Durin's Crown** a constellation of [Seven Stars](/wiki/Seven_Stars) mentioned in the lore of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). According to the legend, it appeared as a crown above <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin</a>'s reflection in [Mirrormere](/wiki/Mirrormere) despite the fact it was day; he saw it as a good omen and built [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") in the valley of [Azanulbizar](/wiki/Azanulbizar). [Durin's Stone](/wiki/Durin%27s_Stone) was set to commemorate the location and the event.

Ages later, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") looked in the lake, but whether they saw the crown is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Identity {#Identity}

The form of the constellation is not known. It's possible that the seven stars on [Doors of Durin](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin) around the crown signify the constellation. [Valacirca](/wiki/Valacirca) is also a constellation of seven stars.

Steve Gardner concluded that it could also have been <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auriga_(constellation)" title="wikipedia:Auriga (constellation)">Auriga</a> or the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corona_Borealis" title="wikipedia:Corona Borealis">Northern Crown</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Since the constellation was a miraculous event, it's also possible that its stars were only a vision, and not an actual constellation set on [menel](/wiki/Menel "Menel"). <span></span>
