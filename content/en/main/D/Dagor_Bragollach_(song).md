<i><b>Dagor Bragollach</b></i> is the eighth track of the album [Minas Morgul](<../M/Minas_Morgul_(album).md>) by the band [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md). The song name is taken from the [Dagor Bragollach](Dagor_Bragollach.md), the fourth great battle of the [Wars of Beleriand](../W/Wars_of_Beleriand.md), fought between the forces of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) and the league of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md). The lyrics directly reference the battle itself, and the destruction of [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md).

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>In the year 455 it began, the battle of rapid fire. <br/>
Announced threw flames, <br/>
more deadly then the third battle. <br/>
Streams of fire flowing out of Thangorodrim and burned Ardgalen <br/>
And with the flame a see of orcs arived, <br/>
and balrogs and Glaurung the dragon. <br/>
Having received all his might now Dor Thonion broke down first under this onset <br/>
Angrod and aegnor where killed and their folk enslaved. <br/>
The riders of maglor where sacrifices of the flames. <br/>
On the planes of Lothlann victory on our side the triumph of Morgoth.
</p>
</div>
