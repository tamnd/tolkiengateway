<i><b>duin</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "large river".<sup id="cite_ref-HMN_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HMN-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*duin* derives from [Common Elvish](/wiki/Common_Elvish) *duinē* ("large river, (liable to flood surrounding land)"), from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") DUY (stem *dui-* "flow (in volume)").<sup id="cite_ref-HMN_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HMN-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT48_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT48-2">[2]</a></sup>
