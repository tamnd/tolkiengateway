The **Dark Years** were the long years of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) when the races of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), especially the [Men](../M/Men.md), suffered under the domination of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).

## Background {#Background}

After the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) was defeated and judged by the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), but his lieutenant [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) escaped judgement. The [Edain](../E/Edain.md) had been offered the isle of [Elenna](../E/Elenna.md) and flourished there. While the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and the [Dwarves](Dwarves.md) retreated in their respective realms, outside them, "Middle-earth went backward and light and wisdom faded".<sup id="cite_ref-Ak_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ak-1">[1]</a></sup> According to the *[Tale of Years](../T/Tale_of_Years.md)*, the dates that concern events in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), unlike [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), are often uncertain.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Middle Men](../M/Middle_Men.md) who did not heed the summons of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and Morgoth fell back into a primitive state, having no contact with the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) or the [Edain](../E/Edain.md). Instead, some of the [evil Men](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>) who escaped the destruction of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) came to the east and became their Kings. For this the Middle Men were neglected by the Valar.<sup id="cite_ref-Ak_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ak-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was thanks to the [Entwives](../E/Entwives.md) that some Mannish folk learned agriculture, and these Men honoured them.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The primitive [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md) of [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) were defenceless against the remnants of Orcs, but they were provided with metal weapons by the [Longbeards](../L/Longbeards.md), who made an [Alliance](../A/Alliance_of_Dwarves_and_Men.md) with them.<sup id="cite_ref-pmrel_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pmrel-4">[4]</a></sup>

Around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [600](../S/Second_Age_600.md) the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) sent ships to Middle-earth and established contact with the Middle Men and started teaching them agriculture, stonecraft and smithying. But it was around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [882](../S/Second_Age_882.md) when King [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) [warned](../G/Gil-galad's_letter.md) the Númenóreans that an evil power was taking shape in the [East](../E/East.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Indeed, [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) was ever at work gathering [Men of Darkness](../M/Men_of_Darkness.md) in the east and the south,<sup id="cite_ref-rings_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-7">[7]</a></sup> some of whom had been of old corrupted by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-pmrel_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pmrel-4">[4]</a></sup> He established himself in [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1000](../S/Second_Age_1000.md), and he fortified the land with the [Black Gate](../B/Black_Gate.md) and the armies of Men whom he corrupted and enslaved. He also built [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md) near [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md).

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7c/Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg/300px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="233" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7c/Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg/450px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7c/Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg/600px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Death_of_Celebrimbor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="792" data-file-height="616"/></a><figcaption>Abe Papakhian - <i>Death of Celebrimbor</i></figcaption></figure>

Sauron attempted to corrupt the prosperous [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md), persuading them to create the [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md). When the Elves rejected him, they fought [a bloody conflict against him](../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md), but Sauron destroyed [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) and devastated much of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md), pushing the Elves back to the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md), while the [Dwarves](Dwarves.md) retreated to [Moria](../M/Moria.md).

## The Dark Years begin {#The_Dark_Years_begin}

Wearing a fair and wise guise for the Men who worshipped him as "Lord of the Earth", Sauron now dominated most of the [East](../E/East.md) and the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../S/South.md" title="South">South</a>, and even much of the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md). He gathered all the evil servants of his former master to him, and the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) greatly multiplied under him. Sauron ruled with force and fear, and it was during this time that he started to be known as the Dark Lord and the Enemy by those who saw his power spreading over the world. Not allowing any freedom or rivalry, he desired to destroy the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) as well as Númenor. The Elves remembered those years as the "Days of Flight" due to Sauron hunting them, and they fled to [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md), where Sauron could not enter, and some even passed over the [Sea](../S/Sea.md) to the [Uttermost West](../U/Uttermost_West.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-7">[7]</a></sup>

Other peoples retreated to the mountains or the woods, in fear.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-7">[7]</a></sup> Reinforced and commanded by his servants, the Orcs invaded the mountains of the [Longbeards](../L/Longbeards.md) again. [Gundabad](../G/Gundabad.md) was re-taken, the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md) infested and [Moria](../M/Moria.md) lost contact with the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md). This also broke their Alliance with the Northmen, who also during those times had war against the Orcs and savage tribes of [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md). The war scattered them and the diminished numbers who remained there were impoverished, living in caves or on the borders of [Greenwood](../G/Greenwood.md).<sup id="cite_ref-pmrel_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pmrel-4">[4]</a></sup>

Some of the [Men of the](../M/Men_of_the_Mountains.md) [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) feared Sauron and started worshipping him.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Others of their folk migrated northward to the southern dales of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Men_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Men-9">[9]</a></sup>

But then the powerful Númenóreans responded to the Elves' plea and sent ships and armies to their support. The alliance, after heavy fighting, managed to reclaim Eriador and pushed the Dark Lord back to Mordor with a handful of Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-7">[7]</a></sup>

Although the war was ended, this was not the end of the Dark Days. Sauron directed his power over the far south and east conquering and dominating the savage tribes.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

### Shadow over Númenor {#Shadow_over_Númenor}

Meanwhile Númenor fell under the [Shadow](../S/Shadow.md) and the colonists started to oppress the primitive Men of the Westlands, demanding tribute of goods and wealth.<sup id="cite_ref-Ak_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ak-1">[1]</a></sup> Some indigenous folk of Middle-earth were afraid of these [Black Númenóreans](../B/Black_Númenóreans.md) and their ships who intended at some point to conquer the land of [Agar](../A/Agar.md) and slay its people.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

Sauron turned his attention to the west once more, declaring himself "King of Men", starting against the Númenórean colonists and their ports. The arrogant Númenóreans, who were traditionally the "Kings of Men", saw him as their chief competitor, and once more came against him. King [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md) took Sauron as a captive to [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), further corrupting the Númenóreans, who continued to enslave the primitive Men and even used them as human sacrifices. This corruption eventually resulted in the [Downfall of Númenor](Downfall_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ak_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ak-1">[1]</a></sup>

Sauron's spirit escaped to Middle-earth, and returned to Mordor, where he slowly rebuilt his strength. Unable to assume a fair form, he started to rule through terror and force.

## Fall of Sauron {#Fall_of_Sauron}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_-_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Last_Alliance.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d9/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_-_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Last_Alliance.jpg/300px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_-_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Last_Alliance.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="125" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d9/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_-_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Last_Alliance.jpg/450px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_-_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Last_Alliance.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d9/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_-_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Last_Alliance.jpg/600px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_-_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Last_Alliance.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1920" data-file-height="800"/></a><figcaption>Dúnedain attack in Peter Jackson&#39;s <i>Fellowship of the Ring</i></figcaption></figure>

The [Faithful](../F/Faithful.md) who escaped founded [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) in Middle-earth and gathered Mannish peoples under them, including the [Men of the Mountains](../O/Oathbreakers.md) who formerly worshipped Sauron. Sauron still considered the Númenóreans his most hated enemies and he launched a pre-emptive attack against them. The Númenóreans formed a [Last Alliance](../L/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men.md) with the [Elves of Lindon](../E/Elves_of_Lindon.md) and fought Sauron in the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md), where finally Sauron perished.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-7">[7]</a></sup>

Sauron's defeat ended the Dark Years and the Second Age, and allowed the Númenórean realms to flourish during the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).

## Other names {#Other_names}

Other names of this period were the **Black Years**, **Days of Flight** for the Elves who fled to the Sea,<sup id="cite_ref-rings_7-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-7">[7]</a></sup> and **Dark Days**.<sup id="cite_ref-Foster_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foster-13">[13]</a></sup>

At one point [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) mentions the **Accursed Years** to the [Oathbreakers](../O/Oathbreakers.md), which [Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md) identifies as another name of that period.<sup id="cite_ref-Foster_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foster-13">[13]</a></sup> On the other hand, the [Encyclopedia of Arda](../E/Encyclopedia_of_Arda.md) considers it a "*term of uncertain meaning*", probably referring to the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) when the Oathbreakers were cursed to roam the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
