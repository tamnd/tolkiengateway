**Daniel Frazier** (born [28 September](../_/28_September.md) [1945](../_/1945.md)) is an American artist. After illustrating his first cover for [Dragon Magazine](Dragon_Magazine.md) in 1991, he became a full-time illustrator. Some earlier work for [Wizards of the Coast](../W/Wizards_of_the_Coast.md) led him to become a prominent artist for [Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup> In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.

In May [2026](../_/2026.md), an illustration of the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md) created by Frazier for [Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit.md) was found to have been plagiarized from an earlier illustration by [Marta Nael](../M/Marta_Nael.md), which was later confirmed by both the artist and [Wizards of the Coast](../W/Wizards_of_the_Coast.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
