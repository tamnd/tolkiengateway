The **Doors of Felagund** were doors that guarded the entrance to [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md), raised by its builder [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md). They were set in the face of a cliff above the river Narog, and could be approached only by a narrow pathway across the cliff<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>, making Nargothrond secure from enemy attack.

After Felagund's death, [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) came to dwell in Nargothrond, and he persuaded King [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) to change this secretive policy, and to build a [great bridge](../B/Bridge_of_Nargothrond.md) before the Doors of Felagund, so that his armies could more easily pass across the [Narog](../N/Narog.md). This was a dire error, because it gave [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s forces a means to approach the Doors. After the [Battle of Tumhalad](../B/Battle_of_Tumhalad.md), [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) the Dragon came against the Doors of Felagund and destroyed them, allowing the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) to sack the once-mighty citadel.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
