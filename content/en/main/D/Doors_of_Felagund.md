The **Doors of Felagund** were doors that guarded the entrance to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), raised by its builder [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod"). They were set in the face of a cliff above the river Narog, and could be approached only by a narrow pathway across the cliff<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>, making Nargothrond secure from enemy attack.

After Felagund's death, [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) came to dwell in Nargothrond, and he persuaded King [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) to change this secretive policy, and to build a [great bridge](/wiki/Bridge_of_Nargothrond "Bridge of Nargothrond") before the Doors of Felagund, so that his armies could more easily pass across the [Narog](/wiki/Narog). This was a dire error, because it gave [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s forces a means to approach the Doors. After the [Battle of Tumhalad](/wiki/Battle_of_Tumhalad), [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) the Dragon came against the Doors of Felagund and destroyed them, allowing the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) to sack the once-mighty citadel.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
