**Daniel Falconer** is a [Weta Workshop](/wiki/Weta_Workshop) designer who has worked on both [*The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and [*The Hobbit* films](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"). He has also authored various [Weta Workshop](/wiki/Weta_Workshop) publications.
