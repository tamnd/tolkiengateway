The **Dome of Stars** was a great domed hall in the city [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the capital of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), where the chief *[palantír](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")*, the [Master Stone of Osgiliath](/wiki/Master_Stone_of_Osgiliath) was kept.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The **Tower of the Dome of Osgiliath** was destroyed during the civil war of the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife) in the [siege and Burning](/wiki/Burning_of_Osgiliath "Burning of Osgiliath") of Osgiliath and the palantír was lost in the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1437](/wiki/Third_Age_1437 "Third Age 1437").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Dome of Stars stands in the northern section of the city. After <a href="/wiki/Sauron%27s_attack_on_Osgiliath" title="Sauron&#39;s attack on Osgiliath">Sauron&#39;s attack on Osgiliath</a> in <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3018" title="Third Age 3018">T.A. 3018</a>, his forces threw down the bridge leading to the Dome of Stars from the west, transforming it into their stronghold. Later during the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, the Dome serves as perch for one of the <a href="/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl" title="Nazgûl">Nazgûl</a> whom the players confront in the palantír chamber during a group instance. After the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Morgul-host&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Morgul-host (page does not exist)">Morgul-host</a> moves over to the <a href="/wiki/Pelennor_Fields" title="Pelennor Fields">Pelennor Fields</a>, some of the <a href="/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien" title="Rangers of Ithilien">Rangers of Ithilien</a> who remained in hiding inside the city&#39;s culverts and became cut off from the rest of <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>&#39;s forces, sneak into the Dome of Stars and observe the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">battle of the Pelennor Fields</a> from its top.</dd></dl>
