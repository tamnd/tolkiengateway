**Douglas Carrel** is a Scottish illustrator. He illustrated several scenes and characters from [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit), including a picture of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) for the documentary *Real Wizards: The Search For Harry's Ancestors*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and trolls for an excerpt in *Fantasy Tales*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Some of those illustrations were later reused in [Das Geheimnis der Ringe](/wiki/Das_Geheimnis_der_Ringe).

He also drew the cover illustrations for [The Soddit](/wiki/The_Soddit) and [The Sellamillion](/wiki/The_Sellamillion).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
