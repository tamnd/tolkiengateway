**Douglas Carrel** is a Scottish illustrator. He illustrated several scenes and characters from [The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md), including a picture of [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) for the documentary *Real Wizards: The Search For Harry's Ancestors*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and trolls for an excerpt in *Fantasy Tales*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Some of those illustrations were later reused in [Das Geheimnis der Ringe](Das_Geheimnis_der_Ringe.md).

He also drew the cover illustrations for [The Soddit](../T/The_Soddit.md) and [The Sellamillion](../T/The_Sellamillion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
