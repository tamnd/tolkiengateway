<i><b>Dragon Magazine</b></i> was an official magazine for the [Dungeons &amp; Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons "Dungeons and Dragons") role-playing game. It launched in [1976](/wiki/1976) and ran until [2013](/wiki/2013). Throughout its run, it was published by [TSR](/wiki/TSR), [Wizards of the Coast](/wiki/Wizards_of_the_Coast), and Paizo under various forms. Despite its primary focus, *Dragon Magazine* also served as a broader fantasy periodical, reviewing other role-playing games, fantasy literature, and various related properties. Notably, [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing) often utilized the magazine's back cover for its advertisements.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

    - A critical response to Cummings' letter by Ray Rahman

    - *Life — at least, Tim Hildebrandt’s vision of life — unwinds at an unrelenting 24 frames a second* (About the art of [Tim Hildebrandt](/wiki/Tim_Hildebrandt))

    - *[The influence of J.R.R. Tolkien on the D&amp;D and AD&amp;D games](/wiki/The_influence_of_J.R.R._Tolkien_on_the_D%26D_and_AD%26D_games)* by Gary Gygax

    - Review of *[Mirkwood](</wiki/Mirkwood_(MERP)> "Mirkwood (MERP)")* (MERP) by Rick Swan
- [1996](/wiki/1996): *Dragon* #239 (October)
    - Review of *[Mirkwood](</wiki/Mirkwood_(MERP)> "Mirkwood (MERP)")* (MERP) by Rick Swan
