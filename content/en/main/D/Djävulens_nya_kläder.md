<i><b>Djävulens nya kläder: essäer</b></i> (*The Devil's New Clothes: Essays*) is a Swedish-language book written by the academic philosopher and teacher Erik Ryding. One of the essays (pp. 121–137) deals with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien): "Trollkarlens Återkomst" ("The Return of the Wizard"). The essay is a response to a newspaper article, "Tolkien och tiden" ("Tolkien and Time"), published in *Sydsvenska Dagbladet Snällposten* (Malmö), 26 January 1961.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Tolkien's publisher in Sweden, AWE/Gebers, sent him a copy of *Djävulens nya kläder*. Apparently, Tolkien was able to read the essay in Swedish.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
