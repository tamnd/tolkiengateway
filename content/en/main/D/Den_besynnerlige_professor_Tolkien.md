**Den besynnerlige professor Tolkien** ("The Peculiar Professor Tolkien" or "Tolkien, the Peculiar Professor") is the title of an interview with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) conducted by [Lars Gustafsson](/wiki/Lars_Gustafsson). The article was published in Swedish in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dagens_Nyheter" title="wikipedia:Dagens Nyheter">Dagens Nyheter</a></i>, on [August 21](/wiki/21_August "21 August"), [1961](/wiki/1961).

The interview is more lengthy than [Jan Broberg](/wiki/Jan_Broberg)'s [interview in the same year](/wiki/1961_Jan_Broberg "1961 Jan Broberg"). [Åke Ohlmarks](/wiki/%C3%85ke_Ohlmarks) makes a reference to the interview in his book *[Tolkiens arv](/wiki/Tolkiens_arv)*:

"*Lars Gustafsson \[...\] recounted in 1961 that \[Tolkien's\] house in Headington was chock-full of large piles of manuscripts*"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

An English version of the interview, translated by John-Henri Holmberg and edited and with an introduction by [Morgan Thomsen](/wiki/Morgan_Thomsen) and [Shaun Gunner](/wiki/Shaun_Gunner), was published in 2013 in the journal [*Hither Shore* 9](/wiki/Hither_Shore_9 "Hither Shore 9").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
