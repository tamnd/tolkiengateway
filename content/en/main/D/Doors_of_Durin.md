<p><br/>
</p><p>The <b>Doors of Durin</b>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> also known as the <b>West-gate</b>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-August3018_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-August3018-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-January3019_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-January3019-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> the <b>West-door</b>,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> <b>Elven Door</b>,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> or <b>Hollin Gate</b><sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-8">[8]</a></sup> were built into the <a href="../W/Walls_of_Moria.md" title="Walls of Moria">Walls of Moria</a> in the dark cliffs of the <a href="../C/Celebdil.md" title="Celebdil">Silvertine</a>, and formed the western entrance to the great <a href="Dwarves.md" title="Dwarves">Dwarven</a> city of <a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Khazad-dûm</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span></h2>
<p>The main entrance to <a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Khazad-dûm</a> was the <a href="../G/Great_Gates.md" title="Great Gates">Great Gates</a> in <a href="Dimrill_Dale.md" title="Dimrill Dale">Dimrill Dale</a>, east of the <a href="../M/Misty_Mountains.md" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a>. During the <a href="../S/Second_Age.md" title="Second Age">Second Age</a>, it was decided to open a way to the west side of the Silvertine, which would facilitate contact and cooperation with the <a href="../E/Elves.md" title="Elves">Elven</a> realm of <a class="mw-redirect" href="../H/Hollin.md" title="Hollin">Hollin</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Dark_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dark-9">[9]</a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Anke_Eißmann_-_The_Doors_of_Durin.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e5/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Doors_of_Durin.jpg/300px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Doors_of_Durin.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="421" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e5/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Doors_of_Durin.jpg/450px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Doors_of_Durin.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/e/e5/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_The_Doors_of_Durin.jpg 2x" data-file-width="510" data-file-height="716"/></a><figcaption><i>The Doors of Durin</i> by <a href="../A/Anke_Eißmann.md" title="Anke Eißmann">Anke Eißmann</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<p>The <b>Elven Door</b> was constructed in cooperation between <a href="Dwarves.md" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a> and <a href="../E/Elves.md" title="Elves">Elves</a>, sometime between the founding of Eregion in <a href="../S/Second_Age.md" title="Second Age">S.A.</a> <a href="../S/Second_Age_750.md" title="Second Age 750">750</a><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and the descruction of Eregion in <a href="../S/Second_Age_1697.md" title="Second Age 1697">1697</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-11">[11]</a></sup> It was the two greatest craftsmen of the Second Age, the <a href="../N/Noldor.md" title="Noldor">Elf</a> and Lord of Eregion, <a href="../C/Celebrimbor.md" title="Celebrimbor">Celebrimbor</a>, and the <a href="Dwarves.md" title="Dwarves">dwarf</a> <a href="../N/Narvi.md" title="Narvi">Narvi</a> who built the Doors.<sup id="cite_ref-Dark_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dark-9">[9]</a></sup>
</p><p>These were the days before the <a href="Dark_Years.md" title="Dark Years">Dark Years</a> of <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>&#39;s dominion in <a href="../M/Middle-earth.md" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a>, and the friendship between Elven and Dwarven kingdoms was a rare and special event. During this peaceful time the Doors stood open, allowing unfettered trade. But during the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md" title="War of the Elves and Sauron">War of the Elves and Sauron</a> (in <a href="../S/Second_Age.md" title="Second Age">S.A.</a> <a href="../S/Second_Age_1697.md" title="Second Age 1697">1697</a>) the Doors were sealed shut after <a href="../S/Sack_of_Eregion.md" title="Sack of Eregion">Hollin fell</a> to Sauron&#39;s forces.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-11">[11]</a></sup>
</p><p>When Khazad-dûm was abandoned in <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_1981.md" title="Third Age 1981">1981</a><sup id="cite_ref-TA_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-12">[12]</a></sup> the way of opening the Doors was forgotten.
</p><p>At some point between <a href="../T/Third_Age_2845.md" title="Third Age 2845">2845</a> and <a href="../T/Third_Age_2950.md" title="Third Age 2950">2950</a> the Wizard <a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> entered the city looking for King <a href="../T/Thráin.md" title="Thráin">Thráin II</a> who had disappeared on journey to Erebor. After his search the Wizard exited through the Doors; however this experience did not help him know how to open the doors from the outside.<sup id="cite_ref-Dark_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dark-9">[9]</a></sup>
</p><p>In <a href="../T/Third_Age_2994.md" title="Third Age 2994">2994</a>, during the settling of <a class="mw-redirect" href="../B/Balin&#39;s_Colony~64118.md" title="Balin&#39;s Colony">Balin&#39;s Colony</a>, they were attacked by an onslaught of <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">orcs</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-12">[12]</a></sup> <a href="../_/Óin.md" title="Óin">Óin</a> led a group to the west side of the city hoping to find escape through the <b>West-gate</b>, but instead he found the water up to the doors where the <a href="../W/Watcher_in_the_Water.md" title="Watcher in the Water">Watcher in the Water</a> killed him. The Dwarves were trapped, and all perished.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-8">[8]</a></sup>
</p><p>In <a href="../A/August.md" title="August">August</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_3018.md" title="Third Age 3018">3018</a> <a href="../G/Gollum.md" title="Gollum">Gollum</a> took refuge in Moria; but when he had at last discovered the way to the Doors he could not get out.<sup id="cite_ref-August3018_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-August3018-3">[3]</a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_Password_Into_Moria.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/f/f9/Ted_Nasmith_-_Password_Into_Moria.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="274" height="450" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="274" data-file-height="450"/></a><figcaption><i>Password Into Moria</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<p>On <a href="../_/13_January.md" title="13 January">13 January</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_3019.md" title="Third Age 3019">3019</a><sup id="cite_ref-January3019_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-January3019-4">[4]</a></sup> the <a href="../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="Fellowship of the Ring">Fellowship of the Ring</a> entered Moria through the Doors, but initially Gandalf could not find out the password to open them. <a href="../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry Brandybuck</a> unknowingly gave Gandalf the answer by asking, &#34;What does it mean by <i>speak, friend, and enter</i>?&#34; When Gandalf realized that the correct translation was &#34;Say friend and enter&#34; he sprang up, laughed, and said &#34;<i><a href="../M/Mellon.md" title="Mellon">Mellon</a></i>&#34;, which means &#34;friend&#34; in <a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">Sindarin</a>, and the Doors opened. Shortly thereafter, the <a href="../W/Watcher_in_the_Water.md" title="Watcher in the Water">Watcher in the Water</a> attacked the Fellowship and shut the Doors behind them.<sup id="cite_ref-Dark_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dark-9">[9]</a></sup> Afterwards <a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Khazad-dûm</a> was resettled by Dwarves; it is unknown if the Doors of Durin were ever repaired.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Appearance">Appearance</span></h2>
<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/D/Donato_Giancola_-_The_Doors_of_Moria.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/9b/Donato_Giancola_-_The_Doors_of_Moria.jpg/300px-Donato_Giancola_-_The_Doors_of_Moria.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="226" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/9b/Donato_Giancola_-_The_Doors_of_Moria.jpg/450px-Donato_Giancola_-_The_Doors_of_Moria.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/9b/Donato_Giancola_-_The_Doors_of_Moria.jpg/600px-Donato_Giancola_-_The_Doors_of_Moria.jpg 2x" data-file-width="3000" data-file-height="2257"/></a><figcaption><i>The Doors of Moria</i> by <a href="Donato_Giancola.md" title="Donato Giancola">Donato Giancola</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<p>They were fashioned as flush doors, the jambs invisible to the eye, and matched so perfectly with the mountain rock that when closed the Doors could not be seen. The slabs were made by Narvi out of a grey material stronger than stone, and inlaid by Celebrimbor with <i><a href="../I/Ithildin.md" title="Ithildin">ithildin</a></i>, which could only be seen in starlight and moonlight.
</p><p>When visible, the fine silver-like inlay showed a hammer and anvil (the emblems of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="Durin.md" title="Durin">Durin</a>), a crown and <a href="../S/Seven_Stars.md" title="Seven Stars">Seven Stars</a> (probably <a href="Durin&#39;s_Crown.md" title="Durin&#39;s Crown">Durin&#39;s Crown</a>), two trees surmounted by crescent moons (probably symbolizing the <a href="../G/Galathilion.md" title="Galathilion">Tree of the High Elves</a>), and a single star (the emblem of the <a href="../H/House_of_Fëanor.md" title="House of Fëanor">House of Fëanor</a>). On the top left and right corners there were the <a href="../T/Tengwar.md" title="Tengwar">tengwar</a> <i><a href="../C/Calma.md" title="Calma">Calma</a></i> (C) and <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../_/Óre.md" title="Óre">Óre</a></i> (N) standing for Celebrimbor and Narvi; between their feet was an <i><a href="../A/Ando.md" title="Ando">Ando</a></i> (D) for Durin.
</p><p>The inscription on the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archivolt" title="wikipedia:Archivolt">archivolt</a> read:
</p>
<blockquote><p><i>&#34;Ennyn Durin Aran Moria. Pedo Mellon a Minno. Im Narvi hain echant. Celebrimbor o Eregion teithant i thiw hin.&#34;</i></p></blockquote>
<p>As Gandalf first translated it to the other Walkers:
</p>
<blockquote><p>&#34;The Doors of Durin, Lord of Moria. Speak, friend, and enter. I, Narvi, made them. Celebrimbor of Hollin drew these signs.&#34;</p></blockquote>
<p>Actually, the inscription gave the password that would cause the Doors to swing open. Gandalf&#39;s translation is correct, but the proper translation of the second sentence, also correct, is &#34;Say &#39;friend&#39; and enter.&#34;<sup id="cite_ref-Dark_9-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dark-9">[9]</a></sup>
</p><p>From the inside the Doors could be opened by simply pushing against them, though it usually took the thrust of two to do so. When Moria was inhabited by the Dwarves they kept doorwards inside who would help open the Doors and see that only those with permission could pass.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="A_possible_inconsistency">A possible inconsistency</span></h2>
<p>The name <i><a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Moria</a></i> means &#34;Black Chasm&#34; in <a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">Sindarin</a> and was said to have been given by the Elves &#34;without love&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-Trans_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Trans-14">[14]</a></sup> Some statements seem to imply that the name Moria for Khazad-dûm was only widely used after it fell to <a href="Durin&#39;s_Bane.md" title="Durin&#39;s Bane">Durin&#39;s Bane</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> although others suggest the name was merely given by the Elves as a result of their lack of love for underground dwellings.<sup id="cite_ref-Trans_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Trans-14">[14]</a></sup> The possibly derogatory nature of this name has led some to question why it appears on doors that were made in a time of friendship between Elves and Dwarves,<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> but Tolkien has never said that Dwarves find it derogatory and they use it at the end of the Third Age, even talking about its wonders.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> For a more detailed discussion of potential explanations, see <a href="../P/Possible_inconsistencies_in_the_legendarium.md#Doors_of_Durin" title="Possible inconsistencies in the legendarium">Possible inconsistencies in the legendarium</a>.
</p>
