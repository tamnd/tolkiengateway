**Drúadan** was a [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) [language](/wiki/Languages "Languages") that was spoken by the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

The language of the Drúedain was completely alien to [Westron](/wiki/Westron) speakers.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It sounded throaty and strange to [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The word *gorgûn* was possibly derived from the Elvish [words](/wiki/RUKU "RUKU") for [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-AC_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AC-4">[4]</a></sup>

The voices of the Drúedain have been described as "deep and guttural".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

- [Drúadan words](/wiki/Category:Dr%C3%BAadan_words "Category:Drúadan words")

## Other names {#Other_names}

J.R.R. Tolkien simply referred to the language as the "tongue of the Druedain" in an essay that was written in the years 1959-60.<sup id="cite_ref-AC_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AC-4">[4]</a></sup>

Scholars and fans have often used the unattested name <i><b>Drúedainic</b></i> to refer to the same tongue.<sup id="cite_ref-Fauskanger_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fauskanger-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The tongue of the Woses is called <i>Pûkael</i>, said to be &#34;ideally suited to their voices and... alien to that of other Men&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Pûkael is nearly impossible for other people to pronounce, and the Woses are not willing to teach it.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup></dd></dl>
