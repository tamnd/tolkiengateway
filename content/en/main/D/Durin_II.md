**Durin II** (lived sometime during the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) or early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age)<sup id="cite_ref-AppDurin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppDurin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>) was a [King of Durin's folk](/wiki/King_of_Durin%27s_folk) and [King of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Kings_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm "Kings of Khazad-dûm").

## History {#History}

Like all Durins after <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin I</a> he was given the name of the first [Father of the Dwarves](/wiki/Seven_Fathers_of_the_Dwarves "Seven Fathers of the Dwarves") because he greatly resembled him in both appearance and manner. Indeed it was believed among the Dwarves that he was the reincarnation of Durin I, though whether this is possible is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-AppDurin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppDurin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

One account states that the name *Durin* was a word for "king" in the language of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Men_of_the_North" title="Men of the North">Men of the North</a> in the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-relations_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-relations-3">[2]</a></sup>

Another account states that *Durinn* is one of the Dwarfs in the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. The name means "Sleepy".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Theories {#Theories}

[Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster), author of *[The Complete Guide to Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth)*, has suggested that this is the Durin named on the [Doors of Durin](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin) of Moria, built around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

He ruled in <a href="/wiki/Gundabad" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a> during the early <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">Second Age</a>. He invited the <a href="/wiki/Ironfists" title="Ironfists">Zhelruka</a> dwarves to dwell in Gundabad after their own ancestral hall was destroyed during the late <a href="/wiki/First_Age" title="First Age">First Age</a>, but the refugees have never made it to Gundabad, becoming lost in <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ered_Mithrin" title="Ered Mithrin">Ered Mithrin</a> along their way.</dd></dl>
