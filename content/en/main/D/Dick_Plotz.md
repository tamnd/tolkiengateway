**Richard "Dick" Plotz** ([1948](/wiki/1948) - [2024](/wiki/2024))<sup id="cite_ref-obit_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-obit-1">[1]</a></sup> was the founder of the [Tolkien Society of America](/wiki/Tolkien_Society_of_America).

## Biography {#Biography}

Plotz became involved in the [Tolkien community](/wiki/Fandom "Fandom") in the 1960s. In [1965](/wiki/1965) he formed the [Tolkien Society of America](/wiki/Tolkien_Society_of_America) (Which merged with the [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) in [1972](/wiki/1972)).<sup id="cite_ref-obit_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-obit-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He says it began after he saw writing in [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) on the wall of a subway station. He responded back in Elvish and became part of a conversation in Elvish written on various subway station walls. After becoming curious as to who was doing the writing he wrote the date and location of the first Tolkien Society meeting. Early attendees of these meetings include [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden) and [Deborah Rogers](/wiki/Deborah_Rogers).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-obitbratman_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-obitbratman-4">[4]</a></sup>

Plotz corresponded with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and visited him at his home twice.<sup id="cite_ref-obit_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-obit-1">[1]</a></sup> He received a letter from Tolkien ([Letter 276](/wiki/Letter_276)) regarding his opinions on The Tolkien Society and answering questions Plotz has asked about his books.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> He interviewed Tolkien for the [January 1967 issue of *Seventeen*](/wiki/Seventeen "Seventeen"). He also is known for a letter he received from Tolkien ([Letter 293a](/wiki/Letter_293a)) specifying the declension of the noun in late Quenya (hence called "[The Plotz Declension](/wiki/The_Plotz_Declension)")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
