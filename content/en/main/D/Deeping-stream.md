The **Deeping-stream** was a river in the west of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).

## Course {#Course}

The Deeping-stream flowed from the gorge on the northern side of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) known as [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> through a culvert in the [Deeping Wall](Deeping_Wall.md) that blocked the entrance to Helm's Deep, then around the feet of the rock where the [Hornburg](../H/Hornburg.md) stood, before flowing to the north into the [Deeping-coomb](Deeping-coomb.md) and then out into the [Westfold Vale](../W/Westfold_Vale.md)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> South of the bottom of the coomb<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the Deeping-stream ran beside the [Deeping-road](Deeping-road.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The Deeping-road ran northeast,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> but it is not known into which river the Deeping-stream flowed. It is possible that if flowed northwestward into the river [Isen](../I/Isen.md), which is the nearest river from the northern end of the Westfold Vale. However, it is also possible that it flowed northeastwards into the river [Entwash](../E/Entwash.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The element Deeping in the name Deeping-stream means that the stream belongs to the deep,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> out of which it flows.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an untitled drawing of Helm's Deep by J.R.R. Tolkien the line of the Deeping-stream seems to flow westwards after leaving the Deeping-coomb.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2013: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Deeping-stream originates inside the <a href="../G/Glittering_Caves.md" title="Glittering Caves">Glittering Caves</a> and forms a small pool with an island in the middle before flowing out through the <a href="Deeping_Wall.md" title="Deeping Wall">Deeping Wall</a>. After flowing into <a href="../W/Westfold.md" title="Westfold">Westfold</a>, the stream turns east and joins the river <i>Swiftwater</i>, originating in the <a href="../W/White_Mountains.md" title="White Mountains">White Mountains</a>, before eventually flowing into <a href="../E/Entwash.md" title="Entwash">Entwash</a>.</dd></dl>
