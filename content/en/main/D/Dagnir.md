**Dagnir** (died [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [460](../F/First_Age_460.md)) was one of [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md)'s [twelve followers](../B/Barahir's_outlaws.md).

## History {#History}

Dagnir fought in the [Dagor Bragollach](Dagor_Bragollach.md) under the command [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md). He was part of a small group of [twelve men](../B/Barahir's_outlaws.md) loyal to their lord that survived.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The survivors formed a small band and lived as outlaws in [Dorthonion](Dorthonion.md), near [Tarn Aeluin](../T/Tarn_Aeluin.md).

They lived in hiding for some time, until [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s agents captured [Gorlim](../G/Gorlim.md) and coerced him into giving the location of their hideaway. [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) were sent to raid it, and succeeded. Dagnir and the other outlaws were killed. Only [Beren](../B/Beren.md) survived, as he had been away on an errand.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name *Dagnir* means "slayer" (or "bane") from <i><a href="Dag.md" title="Dag">dag</a> + (n)<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=D%C3%AEr&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dîr (page does not exist)">dîr</a></i>, cf. [Dagnir Glaurunga](Dagnir_Glaurunga.md) "'Glaurung's Bane".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
