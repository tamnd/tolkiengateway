The **Dark Guard**, also known as the **Outer Guard**, was a division of [Gondolindrim](../G/Gondolindrim.md) that guarded the [Outer Gate](../G/Gate_of_Wood.md) of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) in the dark cavern that preceded it. They were the first Gondolindrim to confront those who attempted to enter the city through the [Seven Gates](../S/Seven_Gates_of_Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SilmMaeglin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SilmMaeglin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the year [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [400](../F/First_Age_400.md), [Aredhel](../A/Aredhel.md), returning to Gondolin with her son [Maeglin](../M/Maeglin.md), was happily received by the Dark Guard. However, [Eöl](../E/Eöl.md) had followed them from a distance, discovering the secret entrance into the city. The Dark Guard was taken by surprise when Eöl revealed his presence to them while traversing the [Orfalch Echor](../O/Orfalch_Echor.md) by stealth. After he claimed that Aredhel was his wife, the Guard brought him before [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) in the [Tower of the King](../T/Tower_of_the_King.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SilmMaeglin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SilmMaeglin-1">[1]</a></sup>

Years later, the Dark Guard was approached by [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) and [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md) in their cavern in front of the Outer Gate, the Gate of Wood. Their captain, [Elemmakil](../E/Elemmakil.md), was amazed that his friend Voronwë would bring a mortal [Man](../M/Man.md) with him. He announced that he should, by law, slay him. Voronwë and Tuor answered that they came at the command of [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md). As Elemmakil guided the two through the Seven Gates, they passed the guard divisions of the other gates.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-2">[2]</a></sup>

On the inner side of the [Last Gate](../G/Gate_of_Steel.md), an unknown number of Elves representing the Dark Guard were stationed at the high sward overlooking [Tumladen](../T/Tumladen.md). They stood with an army of soldiers that contained representatives from each of the Seven Gates.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-2">[2]</a></sup>
