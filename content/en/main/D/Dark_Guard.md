The **Dark Guard**, also known as the **Outer Guard**, was a division of [Gondolindrim](/wiki/Gondolindrim) that guarded the [Outer Gate](/wiki/Gate_of_Wood "Gate of Wood") of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in the dark cavern that preceded it. They were the first Gondolindrim to confront those who attempted to enter the city through the [Seven Gates](/wiki/Seven_Gates_of_Gondolin "Seven Gates of Gondolin").<sup id="cite_ref-SilmMaeglin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SilmMaeglin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the year [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [400](/wiki/First_Age_400 "First Age 400"), [Aredhel](/wiki/Aredhel), returning to Gondolin with her son [Maeglin](/wiki/Maeglin), was happily received by the Dark Guard. However, [Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l) had followed them from a distance, discovering the secret entrance into the city. The Dark Guard was taken by surprise when Eöl revealed his presence to them while traversing the [Orfalch Echor](/wiki/Orfalch_Echor) by stealth. After he claimed that Aredhel was his wife, the Guard brought him before [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) in the [Tower of the King](/wiki/Tower_of_the_King).<sup id="cite_ref-SilmMaeglin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SilmMaeglin-1">[1]</a></sup>

Years later, the Dark Guard was approached by [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) in their cavern in front of the Outer Gate, the Gate of Wood. Their captain, [Elemmakil](/wiki/Elemmakil), was amazed that his friend Voronwë would bring a mortal [Man](/wiki/Man) with him. He announced that he should, by law, slay him. Voronwë and Tuor answered that they came at the command of [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo). As Elemmakil guided the two through the Seven Gates, they passed the guard divisions of the other gates.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-2">[2]</a></sup>

On the inner side of the [Last Gate](/wiki/Gate_of_Steel "Gate of Steel"), an unknown number of Elves representing the Dark Guard were stationed at the high sward overlooking [Tumladen](/wiki/Tumladen). They stood with an army of soldiers that contained representatives from each of the Seven Gates.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-2">[2]</a></sup>
