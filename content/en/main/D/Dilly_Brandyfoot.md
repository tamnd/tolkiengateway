**Dilly Brandyfoot** was a [Harfoot](../H/Harfoots.md) of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md). Her character was invented for *[The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* television series by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md), where she was portrayed by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Beau_Cassidy&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Beau Cassidy (page does not exist)">Beau Cassidy</a>.

## History {#History}

Dilly is the daughter of [Largo Brandyfoot](../L/Largo_Brandyfoot.md), the stepdaughter of [Marigold Brandyfoot](../M/Marigold_Brandyfoot.md), and the sister of [Elanor "Nori" Brandyfoot](../E/Elanor_Brandyfoot.md).

She gathers berries away from the camp along with other children, though she and Nori spot a mysterious paw print. Dilly is told that it is simply a dog, and is told to go back home, though Nori and her friend, [Poppy Proudfellow](../P/Poppy_Proudfellow.md), note that it is actually the print of a <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../W/Wolf.md" title="Wolf">wolf</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
