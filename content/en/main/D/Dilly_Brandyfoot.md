**Dilly Brandyfoot** was a [Harfoot](/wiki/Harfoots "Harfoots") of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Her character was invented for *[The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power")* television series by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios), where she was portrayed by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Beau_Cassidy&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Beau Cassidy (page does not exist)">Beau Cassidy</a>.

## History {#History}

Dilly is the daughter of [Largo Brandyfoot](/wiki/Largo_Brandyfoot), the stepdaughter of [Marigold Brandyfoot](/wiki/Marigold_Brandyfoot), and the sister of [Elanor "Nori" Brandyfoot](/wiki/Elanor_Brandyfoot "Elanor Brandyfoot").

She gathers berries away from the camp along with other children, though she and Nori spot a mysterious paw print. Dilly is told that it is simply a dog, and is told to go back home, though Nori and her friend, [Poppy Proudfellow](/wiki/Poppy_Proudfellow), note that it is actually the print of a <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Wolf" title="Wolf">wolf</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
