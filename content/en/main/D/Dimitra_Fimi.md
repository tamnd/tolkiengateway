**Dimitra Fimi** (born [2 June](/wiki/2_June) [1978](/wiki/1978)) is Professor of Fantasy and Children's Literature at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow" title="wikipedia:University of Glasgow">University of Glasgow</a>. She teaches and researches on many fantasy authors. She is also co-Director of the Centre for Fantasy and the Fantastic at Glasgow. Fimi is currently on the [Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)") editorial team.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
