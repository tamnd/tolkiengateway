**Dorlas** was a [Man](../M/Men.md) of the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

## History {#History}

Dorlas was a man of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-2">[2]</a></sup> whose son was [Avranc](../A/Avranc.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Avranc1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Avranc1-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the [Crossings of Taeglin](../C/Crossings_of_Taeglin.md), he and several other woodmen waylaid an [Orc](../O/Orc.md)-host leading captive [Elves of Nargothrond](../E/Elves_of_Nargothrond.md). They had intended to rescue the captives, but the Orcs slew their prisoners and pinned [Finduilas](../F/Finduilas.md) to a tree with a spear.<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-2">[2]</a></sup>

Later he and his woodmen were surrounded by Orcs. While [Túrin Turambar](../T/Túrin.md) was in search of Finduilas he came upon them and rescued them, and Dorlas told him of Finduilas' death. Dorlas and his men led Túrin to where the woodmen buried Finduilas, the [Haudh-en-Elleth](../H/Haudh-en-Elleth.md), and Túrin collapsed in grief. Dorlas then had Túrin carried to their stockade upon [Amon Obel](../A/Amon_Obel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-2">[2]</a></sup>

Later, when [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) sent orcs to attack the woodmen, Túrin refused fight because of his promise to [Níniel](../N/Níniel.md) not to go to battle unless their homes were assailed. Dorlas upbraided Túrin for his inaction; Túrin then led a company of the [Men of Brethil](../M/Men_of_Brethil.md) who defeated the orcs. This action revealed to Glaurung where Túrin lived. Glaurung then sought to assail Brethil, and Túrin called for volunteers to go fight the dragon. Only Dorlas stepped forward, and he chastised his people and spoke ill of [Brandir](../B/Brandir.md), who could not go on account of his lameness. [Hunthor](../H/Hunthor.md) then volunteered to go in his stead.<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-2">[2]</a></sup>

However, when Túrin, Hunthor, and Dorlas came to the ravines of [Taeglin](../T/Taeglin.md), Dorlas was overwhelmed with fear and would not cross. Abandoning his companions, he hid in the forest, but Brandir found him.<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-2">[2]</a></sup> In anger, Dorlas struck out at Brandir, but was himself slain, for Brandir drew his sword and killed him .<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name of *Dorlas* was changed to that of *Darlas*, in a later note.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
