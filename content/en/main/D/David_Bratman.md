**David Stephen Bratman** (born [1957](/wiki/1957)), an independent university library consultant, is a member of the [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) for which he has been editor of the magazine *[Mythprint](/wiki/Mythprint)*, awards administrator, member of the board of directors, and chair of the annual conference. He has also written a large number of books reviews and compiled bibliographies and reader guides to the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), and other [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings). He is furthermore a member of the editorial board of *[Tolkien Studies](</wiki/Tolkien_Studies_(journal)> "Tolkien Studies (journal)")*.

David Bratman lives in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunnyvale,_Californa" title="wikipedia:Sunnyvale, Californa">San Jose, Californa</a>, with his wife and two cats.
