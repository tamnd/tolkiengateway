**David Stephen Bratman** (born [1957](../_/1957.md)), an independent university library consultant, is a member of the [Mythopoeic Society](../M/Mythopoeic_Society.md) for which he has been editor of the magazine *[Mythprint](../M/Mythprint.md)*, awards administrator, member of the board of directors, and chair of the annual conference. He has also written a large number of books reviews and compiled bibliographies and reader guides to the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md), and other [Inklings](../I/Inklings.md). He is furthermore a member of the editorial board of *[Tolkien Studies](<../T/Tolkien_Studies_(journal).md>)*.

David Bratman lives in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunnyvale,_Californa" title="wikipedia:Sunnyvale, Californa">San Jose, Californa</a>, with his wife and two cats.
