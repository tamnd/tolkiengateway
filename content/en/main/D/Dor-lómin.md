**Dor-lómin** was a region in the southwestern part of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum), bordered in the east by the [Mountains of Mithrim](/wiki/Mountains_of_Mithrim), and in the north by the river which formed the [Rainbow Cleft](/wiki/Cirith_Ninniach "Cirith Ninniach"), also known as *[Annon-in-Gelydh](/wiki/Annon-in-Gelydh)* ("Gate of the Noldor").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It was first colonized by the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) shortly after they arrived in Middle-earth, and for a long time was ruled by [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) son of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> before he took over as High King of the Noldor after his father was killed.

By this time the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) known as the [House of Marach](/wiki/House_of_Hador "House of Hador") had entered [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), and Hador Lórindol had "entered the household of Fingolfin in his youth and was loved by the King."<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Fingolfin granted him the land of Dor-lómin as a fief, and he gathered most of his kin into that land.

[Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin)'s house was built in its southeastern corner near the mountain known as *[Amon Darthir](/wiki/Amon_Darthir)*, where the river *[Nen Lalaith](/wiki/Nen_Lalaith)* began.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) son of [Huor](/wiki/Huor) was fostered by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Androth](/wiki/Androth) in the Mountains of Mithrim, east of Dor-lómin.<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the *[Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad)* ("Battle of Unnumbered Tears"), the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador) was destroyed or scattered, and [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) dwelt in Dor-lómin.<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-5">[5]</a></sup>

Like the rest of Hithlum, it was destroyed during the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath).<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dor-lómin* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "Land of Echoes" based on *Dor*, which means "land",<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:435</sup> and *Lómin*, which means "echoing", as in Eyrd Lómin (or [Ered Lómin](/wiki/Ered_L%C3%B3min))<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-6">[6]</a></sup> "the Echoing Mountains".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:176</sup> Its [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name was <i><b>Lóminórë</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:145</sup>
