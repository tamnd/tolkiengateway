**Dor-lómin** was a region in the southwestern part of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md), bordered in the east by the [Mountains of Mithrim](../M/Mountains_of_Mithrim.md), and in the north by the river which formed the [Rainbow Cleft](../C/Cirith_Ninniach.md), also known as *[Annon-in-Gelydh](../A/Annon-in-Gelydh.md)* ("Gate of the Noldor").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It was first colonized by the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) shortly after they arrived in Middle-earth, and for a long time was ruled by [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) son of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> before he took over as High King of the Noldor after his father was killed.

By this time the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) known as the [House of Marach](../H/House_of_Hador.md) had entered [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), and Hador Lórindol had "entered the household of Fingolfin in his youth and was loved by the King."<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Fingolfin granted him the land of Dor-lómin as a fief, and he gathered most of his kin into that land.

[Húrin](../H/Húrin.md)'s house was built in its southeastern corner near the mountain known as *[Amon Darthir](../A/Amon_Darthir.md)*, where the river *[Nen Lalaith](../N/Nen_Lalaith.md)* began.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) son of [Huor](../H/Huor.md) was fostered by the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of [Androth](../A/Androth.md) in the Mountains of Mithrim, east of Dor-lómin.<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the *[Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md)* ("Battle of Unnumbered Tears"), the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md) was destroyed or scattered, and [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) dwelt in Dor-lómin.<sup id="cite_ref-Turin_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Turin-5">[5]</a></sup>

Like the rest of Hithlum, it was destroyed during the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dor-lómin* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name meaning "Land of Echoes" based on *Dor*, which means "land",<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:435</sup> and *Lómin*, which means "echoing", as in Eyrd Lómin (or [Ered Lómin](../E/Ered_Lómin.md))<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-6">[6]</a></sup> "the Echoing Mountains".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:176</sup> Its [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name was <i><b>Lóminórë</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:145</sup>
