**David Harvey** (born [1946](/wiki/1946)) is a New Zealander judge who won the 1980 <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_New_Zealand" title="wikipedia:Television New Zealand">TVNZ</a> Mastermind Quiz contest with *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* as his specialist subject. He went on to win the International Mastermind competition with the same subject in 1981.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
