**Deeping-road** was a short road which diverged from the main horse-road (the [Great Road](/wiki/Great_Road)) to the north from [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep) and led from there to the [Deeping-coomb](/wiki/Deeping-coomb) and [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The road is marked in [The West of Middle-earth at the End of the Third Age](/wiki/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age) as a short stretch from north-east to south-west but without a name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Deeping-road splits from the <a href="/wiki/Great_West_Road" title="Great West Road">Great West Road</a> at the village of &#34;Marton&#34; and runs south towards <a href="/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep" title="Helm&#39;s Deep">Helm&#39;s Deep</a>.</dd></dl>
