**Dimbar** was an empty land in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).

## Geography {#Geography}

Dimbar formed a wedge between the [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) and the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Brethil "Brethil") in the west and the [Mindeb](/wiki/Mindeb) and [Neldoreth](/wiki/Neldoreth) in the east (and [Nan Dungortheb](/wiki/Nan_Dungortheb) in the northeast). The main road, which crossed the Sirion at the [Ford of Brithiach](/wiki/Ford_of_Brithiach) to [Himring](/wiki/Himring), cut Dimbar in half; to the north of the road the land climbed to the vales of the [Crissaegrim](/wiki/Crissaegrim).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

No settlements or inhabitants were mentioned in Dimbar.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During a battle with [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs"), [Huor](/wiki/Huor) and [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin), at a very young age, became lost and desperate in a mist in Dimbar. They were found by [Thorondor](/wiki/Thorondor), and would be the first mortals to see [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) from <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Taur-nu-Fuin" title="Taur-nu-Fuin">Taur-nu-Fuin</a> built a road through [Anach](/wiki/Anach) and the land was overrun by Orcs. [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin") and [Beleg Strongbow](/wiki/Beleg "Beleg") would lead raids against them.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) had freed [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin), he came to the land of Dimbar. There he stood before the silent cliffs of the [Echoriath](/wiki/Echoriath) and cried out for [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) to hear him. But Húrin had been followed and was heard, and his words told Morgoth where Turgon dwelt.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dimbar* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) from *[dem](/wiki/Dem "Dem")* ("sad")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and *[-mbar](/wiki/-mbar)* ("dwellingplace, home").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> <span></span>
