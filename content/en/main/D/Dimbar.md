**Dimbar** was an empty land in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

Dimbar formed a wedge between the [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md) and the [Forest of Brethil](../B/Brethil.md) in the west and the [Mindeb](../M/Mindeb.md) and [Neldoreth](../N/Neldoreth.md) in the east (and [Nan Dungortheb](../N/Nan_Dungortheb.md) in the northeast). The main road, which crossed the Sirion at the [Ford of Brithiach](../F/Ford_of_Brithiach.md) to [Himring](../H/Himring.md), cut Dimbar in half; to the north of the road the land climbed to the vales of the [Crissaegrim](../C/Crissaegrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

No settlements or inhabitants were mentioned in Dimbar.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During a battle with [orcs](../O/Orcs.md), [Huor](../H/Huor.md) and [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md), at a very young age, became lost and desperate in a mist in Dimbar. They were found by [Thorondor](../T/Thorondor.md), and would be the first mortals to see [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) from <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../T/Taur-nu-Fuin.md" title="Taur-nu-Fuin">Taur-nu-Fuin</a> built a road through [Anach](../A/Anach.md) and the land was overrun by Orcs. [Túrin Turambar](../T/Túrin.md) and [Beleg Strongbow](../B/Beleg.md) would lead raids against them.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) had freed [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md), he came to the land of Dimbar. There he stood before the silent cliffs of the [Echoriath](../E/Echoriath.md) and cried out for [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) to hear him. But Húrin had been followed and was heard, and his words told Morgoth where Turgon dwelt.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dimbar* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) from *[dem](Dem.md)* ("sad")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and *[-mbar](../_/-mbar.md)* ("dwellingplace, home").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> <span></span>
