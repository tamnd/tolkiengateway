**Dwimmerlaik** was a name given to the [Lord of the Nazgûl](../W/Witch-king.md) by [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) (as [Dernhelm](Dernhelm.md)) when she confronted him during the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

By extension, it is possible that *Dwimmerlaik* is how the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) called the [Wraiths](../W/Wraiths.md) or rumors of the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) in general.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *dwimmerlaik* is said to mean "work of necromancy, [spectre](../S/Spectres.md)" in [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) and [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) have suggested that *dwimmerlaik* derives from [Middle English](../M/Middle_English.md) *dweomer*, [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/gedwimor" title="wiktionary:gedwimor">(gwe)dwimor</a></i>, *-er* ("illusion, phantom") + Middle English *-layk*, *-laik* ("play").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> See also <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="Dwimordene.md" title="Dwimordene"><b>Dwimor</b>dene</a></i>, <i><a href="Dwimorberg.md" title="Dwimorberg"><b>Dwimor</b>berg</a></i>; [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) is also referred to as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/dwimmer-crafty" title="wiktionary:dwimmer-crafty"><b>dwimmer</b>-crafty</a></i> by [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md).

It is not clear if the word refers to a generic creature of the Rohanese folklore, such as the [holbytlan](../H/Holbytla.md) or a [phantom](../P/Phantoms.md); therefore when Éowyn confronted the Witch-king she likely rather identified him with one, than coining the word on that moment.

[David Day](David_Day.md) in *[A Tolkien Bestiary](../A/A_Tolkien_Bestiary.md)* considered that a Dwimmerlaik is any creature of Rohanese superstitious folklore that includes [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Ents](../E/Ents.md). However, other than the appearance of the root *dwimor-* in the abovementioned names, there is no indication in Tolkien's writings that the word pre-existed or that it was used for other races.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
