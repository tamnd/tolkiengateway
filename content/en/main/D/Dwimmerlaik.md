**Dwimmerlaik** was a name given to the [Lord of the Nazgûl](/wiki/Witch-king "Witch-king") by [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) (as [Dernhelm](/wiki/Dernhelm)) when she confronted him during the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

By extension, it is possible that *Dwimmerlaik* is how the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) called the [Wraiths](/wiki/Wraiths) or rumors of the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) in general.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *dwimmerlaik* is said to mean "work of necromancy, [spectre](/wiki/Spectres "Spectres")" in [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) and [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) have suggested that *dwimmerlaik* derives from [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) *dweomer*, [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/gedwimor" title="wiktionary:gedwimor">(gwe)dwimor</a></i>, *-er* ("illusion, phantom") + Middle English *-layk*, *-laik* ("play").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> See also <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Dwimordene" title="Dwimordene"><b>Dwimor</b>dene</a></i>, <i><a href="/wiki/Dwimorberg" title="Dwimorberg"><b>Dwimor</b>berg</a></i>; [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) is also referred to as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/dwimmer-crafty" title="wiktionary:dwimmer-crafty"><b>dwimmer</b>-crafty</a></i> by [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer).

It is not clear if the word refers to a generic creature of the Rohanese folklore, such as the [holbytlan](/wiki/Holbytla "Holbytla") or a [phantom](/wiki/Phantoms "Phantoms"); therefore when Éowyn confronted the Witch-king she likely rather identified him with one, than coining the word on that moment.

[David Day](/wiki/David_Day) in *[A Tolkien Bestiary](/wiki/A_Tolkien_Bestiary)* considered that a Dwimmerlaik is any creature of Rohanese superstitious folklore that includes [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Ents](/wiki/Ents). However, other than the appearance of the root *dwimor-* in the abovementioned names, there is no indication in Tolkien's writings that the word pre-existed or that it was used for other races.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
