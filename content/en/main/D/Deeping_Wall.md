The **Deeping Wall** was the solid stone wall, some twenty feet in height and thick enough that four men could walk abreast along the top, that defended the refuges of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep). It lay beneath the castle of the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg), to which it was connected by a flight of steps.<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-1">[1]</a></sup> In the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg) (on [3 March](/wiki/3_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) it was breached for the only time in its history by the [Fire of Orthanc](/wiki/Fire_of_Orthanc) that was used by the besieging army of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings), but they were soon driven back and defeated.<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-1">[1]</a></sup>
