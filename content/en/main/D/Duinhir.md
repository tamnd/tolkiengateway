**Duinhir** was lord of the [Blackroot Vale](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale) at the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

On [10 March](/wiki/10_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup> Duinhir led a contingent of five hundred bowmen as well as his two sons, [Duilin](</wiki/Duilin_(son_of_Duinhir)> "Duilin (son of Duinhir)") and [Derufin](/wiki/Derufin), into [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) to fight in the upcoming battle.<sup id="cite_ref-Minas_Tirith_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Minas_Tirith-1">[1]</a></sup> Sadly, Lord Duinhir lost both of his sons in the fray (on [15 March](/wiki/15_March)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-2">[2]</a></sup>) when they were trampled by *[mûmakil](/wiki/M%C3%BBmakil "Mûmakil")* while attempting to shoot out the monsters' eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Duinhir* means "River-lord" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, the predecessor to Duinhir was named **Inram the Tall**, who was a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorian) from the [vale of Morthond](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale "Blackroot Vale"). He was mentioned in a sketch as one of the leaders of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) that came to the aid of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Inram had at least <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Duilin" title="Duilin">two</a> [sons](/wiki/Derufin "Derufin") who had dark hair and grey eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Despite not featuring in <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> film series</a>, Decipher produced a card depicting the character.</dd></dl>

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Duinhir is the Lord of the <a href="/wiki/Blackroot_Vale" title="Blackroot Vale">Blackroot Vale</a> with the seat in the town of Morlad. Duinhir is absolutely broken when both of his sons die during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a> but nevertheless joins the march of the Host of the West towards the <a href="/wiki/Black_Gate" title="Black Gate">Black Gate</a>. Upon entering the <a href="/wiki/Dagorlad" title="Dagorlad">Dagorlad</a>, his will fails completely and he becomes one of the faint-hearted, men who leave the main host with <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>&#39;s permission. Duinhir then leads those men towards <a href="/wiki/Cair_Andros" title="Cair Andros">Cair Andros</a>, where they liberate the island from the forces of <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a>.</dd></dl>
