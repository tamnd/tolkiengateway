**Dáin II** or **Dáin Ironfoot** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) 2767 – 3019, aged 252 years) was the [Lord of the Iron Hills](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Iron_Hills) and [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain). In his life, he fought in the [Battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar), the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies) and the [Battle of Dale](/wiki/Battle_of_Dale). He was renowned as a warrior across Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-Erebor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Erebor-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Early life {#Early_life}

Dáin was born in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2767](/wiki/Third_Age_2767 "Third Age 2767") in the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills), the son of [Náin](</wiki/N%C3%A1in_(son_of_Gr%C3%B3r)> "Náin (son of Grór)"), during the reign of his grandfather [Grór](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3r).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

### War of the Dwarves and Orcs {#War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Liz_Danforth_-_Dain_and_Azog.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/5/59/Liz_Danforth_-_Dain_and_Azog.png/200px-Liz_Danforth_-_Dain_and_Azog.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="238" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/5/59/Liz_Danforth_-_Dain_and_Azog.png/300px-Liz_Danforth_-_Dain_and_Azog.png 1.5x, /w/images/5/59/Liz_Danforth_-_Dain_and_Azog.png 2x" data-file-width="317" data-file-height="378"/></a><figcaption>Liz Danforth - <i>Dáin and Azog</i></figcaption></figure>

After the murder of [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) by [Azog](/wiki/Azog) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2790](/wiki/Third_Age_2790 "Third Age 2790"), his son [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in "Thráin") swore revenge. He summoned all available Dwarves, and sacked every Orc stronghold in the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), battling his way to [Moria](/wiki/Moria). There, the [Battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar) raged before the gate of the ancient Dwarf stronghold.

[Náin](</wiki/N%C3%A1in_(son_of_Gr%C3%B3r)> "Náin (son of Grór)") and his son Dáin arrived late in the battle, but proved pivotal. Fresh and well equipped, they drove through the Orc forces, until at last Náin confronted Azog. But Náin was weary and filled with anger, and Azog strong and agile. He darted from the dwarf's blows, and when the warrior stumbled, Azog hewed and broke his neck. However, all across the valley, his troops were in retreat. He tried to escape back inside, but he was followed by Dáin who with his red axe, hewed off his head before the doors of the East-Gate. He was a very young Dwarf at thirty-two at the time, and it was heralded as a magnificent feat.

After the battle King [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in "Thráin") wanted to enter Moria as his own. But Dáin dissuaded him, because he alone looked past the gate into Moria, and had the wisdom to know that it was impossible for Durin's folk to return at that time because of [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Lord of the Iron Hills {#Lord_of_the_Iron_Hills}

Dáin returned to the Halls of Grór with his people, and with his father dead, became [Lord of the Iron Hills](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Iron_Hills) at his grandfather's death in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2805](/wiki/Third_Age_2805 "Third Age 2805"). He had one son, [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin_Stonehelm "Thorin Stonehelm"), born in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2866](/wiki/Third_Age_2866 "Third Age 2866").<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

During his reign his people became the strongest in [Wilderland](/wiki/Rhovanion "Rhovanion"), being the only realm capable to resist [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) if he dared come North and retake the realm of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) and the northern passes of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Erebor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Erebor-2">[2]</a></sup>

### The Battle of Five Armies {#The_Battle_of_Five_Armies}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Angus_McBride_-_Dain_II.jpg"><img src="/w/images/1/14/Angus_McBride_-_Dain_II.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="298" height="253" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="298" data-file-height="253"/></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Angus_McBride" title="Angus McBride">Angus McBride</a> - <i>Dáin</i></figcaption></figure>

Nearly a hundred years later, King [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin), the son of [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in "Thráin"), set out on a journey to reclaim [Erebor, the Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"), which had been usurped by the [Dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The dragon was slain, though by [Bard](/wiki/Bard) of [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town) and not by Thorin's company. Thorin claimed the entire hoard and proclaimed himself [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain), but both Bard and the Elven king [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) longed for compensation of their suffering caused by the Dragon. Because his company of thirteen could not hold the mountain, Thorin sent for Dáin.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Dáin responded to Thorin's call and set out with more than five hundred dwarves, well armed and experienced. They were originally intent on driving off the Elves and Men, but [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) arrived to inform them of the approaching Orcs and [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs). They all united against the common enemy, and so was fought the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies) in the valley of [Dale](/wiki/Dale).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Thorin was killed in the battle, as were his sister-sons [Kíli](/wiki/K%C3%ADli) and [Fíli](/wiki/F%C3%ADli). As his "rightful heir," Dáin succeeded Thorin as [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain), and King of all of Durin's folk.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### King under the Mountain {#King_under_the_Mountain}

During his reign as king, he became exceedingly rich and his people were very prosperous. The town of [Dale](/wiki/Dale) was rebuilt and its border stretched far to the South and East and the friendship between the [Elves of Mirkwood](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood), [Men of Dale](/wiki/Men_of_Dale), and [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor) was close, especially between the Men and Dwarves. For the [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor) now exceeded all their ancestors in the art of masonry and mining, and created many beautiful things in Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

But not all were content. [Balin](/wiki/Balin) set out with several others to reclaim Moria in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2989](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989"). Dáin did not give the expedition his blessing, and could not dissuade them.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-8">[8]</a></sup> It was only later learned the colony was destroyed after five years.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Messenger.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/9/9f/Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Messenger.jpg/300px-Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Messenger.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="162" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/9/9f/Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Messenger.jpg/450px-Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Messenger.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/9/9f/Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Messenger.jpg/600px-Ralph_Damiani_-_The_Messenger.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1920" data-file-height="1037"/></a><figcaption><i>Ralph Damiani</i> - &#34;The Messenger&#34;</figcaption></figure>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), a messenger came from Mordor, enquiring about a creature named "[Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins")" from "[Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")". In return for information Dáin would be given the last three [Dwarf-rings](/wiki/Dwarf-rings). Dáin told him to give him time to think and for many weeks he pondered until he sent [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in) and his son [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) to [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) for counsel, and to warn Bilbo of the enemy looking for him.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-8">[8]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Steamey_-_King_Brand_and_King_D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/3/3f/Steamey_-_King_Brand_and_King_D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot.jpg/300px-Steamey_-_King_Brand_and_King_D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="533" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/3/3f/Steamey_-_King_Brand_and_King_D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="450" data-file-height="800"/></a><figcaption>Steamey - <i>King Brand and King Dáin Ironfoot</i></figcaption></figure>

After many months war came to Erebor in the [Battle of Dale](/wiki/Battle_of_Dale). King [Brand](/wiki/Brand) of Dale was at war with [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) along the River [Carnen](/wiki/Carnen), but they defeated him and he retreated back to Dale where he was given aid by Dáin and an army of Dwarves. After three days of fighting the Dwarves and Men were driven back, and Brand was slain. But defending his body stood Dáin, who at over 250 years old was not feeble, and still could wield his axe with great skill. But his skill was not enough and he was slain as well before the gates of Erebor.<sup id="cite_ref-Erebor_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Erebor-2">[2]</a></sup>

Several days later the Easterlings learned that the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields) was lost and they were afraid. When the new King [Thorin III Stonehelm](/wiki/Thorin_Stonehelm "Thorin Stonehelm"), son of Dáin, and Brand's son [Bard II](/wiki/Bard_II) saw this they came out of the mountain and routed their enemies.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-10">[10]</a></sup>

*Dáinn* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. His name most likely means "deadlike".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

The epithet *[Ironfoot](/wiki/Ironfoot)* remains unexplained in the published writings of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). However, [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff) has pointed out that an explanation is likely found in the original manuscript of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, where it is stated that the shoes of the Dwarves of the Iron Hills were of iron.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

### Films {#Films}

<b>1977: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)" title="The Hobbit (1977 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1977 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dáin is said to come to Thorin&#39;s aid, but he is never individually identified. It&#39;s possible that he&#39;s the Dwarf who lays <a href="/wiki/Orcrist" title="Orcrist">Orcrist</a> on Thorin&#39;s body after he dies, as that Dwarf does not resemble any of the other 12 members of Thorin&#39;s company.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dáin Ironfoot is played by <a href="/wiki/Billy_Connolly" title="Billy Connolly">Billy Connolly</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-BBCCasting1_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBCCasting1-14">[14]</a></sup> After the <a href="/wiki/Elves" title="Elves">Elves</a> of Mirkwood occupy <a href="/wiki/Dale" title="Dale">Dale</a>, <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin</a> secretly sends out a raven to Dáin. The next day, just as Thranduil is about to start his attack, Dáin arrives unexpectedly at <a href="/wiki/Lonely_Mountain" title="Lonely Mountain">Erebor</a> mounted on a large, armoured boar and leading an army of <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a>. He also wields a red war hammer instead of a red axe. He tells the Elves and <a href="/wiki/Men" title="Men">Men</a> to withdraw or face his attack. After trading insults with Thranduil, the Dwarves and Elves begin attacking each other, but the <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orc</a> Army suddenly arrives, and Dáin leads his Dwarves to attack the Orcs instead. Following their example, the Elves join the Dwarves, fighting side by side with them. When <a href="/wiki/Thorin_and_Company" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and Company</a> charge out of the mountain, Dáin and his army rally behind them and drive back the Orcs from the mountain. Later he and Thorin embrace in the midst of the battle. His coronation as the new <a href="/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain" title="King under the Mountain">King under the Mountain</a> is only shown in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies_(extended_edition)" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies (extended edition)">Extended Edition</a> of the film.</dd></dl>

### Games {#Games}

<dl><dd><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Weta_Workshop" title="Weta Workshop">Weta</a> made a special card for Dáin.</dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dáin, along with King <a href="/wiki/Brand" title="Brand">Brand</a> of Dale, makes a brief appearance in the raid &#34;Flight to the Lonely Mountain&#34;, where they brief the player before the assault of the <a href="/wiki/Easterlings" title="Easterlings">Easterlings</a> begin. After the conclusion of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Siege_of_Erebor" title="Siege of Erebor">Siege of Erebor</a>, his tomb can be found in &#34;Mazal Akrâz&#34;, the &#34;Chamber of Glory&#34;, nearby those of <a href="/wiki/F%C3%ADli" title="Fíli">Fíli</a>, <a href="/wiki/K%C3%ADli" title="Kíli">Kíli</a>, and his cousin <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Thorin_Oakenshield" title="Thorin Oakenshield">Thorin Oakenshield</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>A young Dáin is encountered in a flashback story of the Battle of Azanulbizar along with his father and grandfather. He is a close friend of &#34;Hermáth Stormhammer&#34;, the hero from whose point of view the player experiences the battle. After slaying Azog, Dáin briefly ventures beyond the East-gate into Moria and, after recovering from the fright of seeing Durin&#39;s Bane, tells Hermáth and the player the story of the fall of Khazad-dûm.</dd></dl>
