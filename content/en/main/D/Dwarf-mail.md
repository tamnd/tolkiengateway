**Dwarf-mail** refers to the shirts of linked steel rings (mail armour, mail or maille for short). It is classed as *dwarf*-mail, as in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) fashioned the finest steel that [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) had ever seen. In [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost), the famous Dwarf-mail of linked rings was first made.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Many of these shirts of dwarf-mail came into the possession of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). One such shirt and [dwarf-mask](/wiki/Dwarf-masks "Dwarf-masks") was gifted to [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) [Mormegil](/wiki/Mormegil) during his time in [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
