**Douglas Chaffee** ([24 January](/wiki/24_January) [1936](/wiki/1936)–[26 April](/wiki/26_April) [2011](/wiki/2011)) was an American artist who first rose to prominence as a space age illustrator, as head of IBM's art department. Later he became a freelancer, illustrating many books and games. [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) contracted him to make artwork for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
