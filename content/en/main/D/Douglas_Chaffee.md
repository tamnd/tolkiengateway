**Douglas Chaffee** ([24 January](../_/24_January.md) [1936](../_/1936.md)–[26 April](../_/26_April.md) [2011](../_/2011.md)) was an American artist who first rose to prominence as a space age illustrator, as head of IBM's art department. Later he became a freelancer, illustrating many books and games. [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) contracted him to make artwork for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
