The **Dagor Bragollach** ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for **"Battle of Sudden Flame"**) was the fourth great battle of the [Wars of Beleriand](../W/Wars_of_Beleriand.md), fought between the forces of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) and the league of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md).

## Background {#Background}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a href="Dagor_Aglareb.md" title="Dagor Aglareb">Dagor Aglareb</a></div></div>

After the [Dagor Aglareb](Dagor_Aglareb.md), where [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) was defeated by the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), the [Siege of Angband](../S/Siege_of_Angband.md) was established.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> For about four hundred years there was relative [peace](../L/Long_Peace.md) in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). The Elves built their cities and strongholds across the land and they were strengthened by the coming of [Men](../E/Edain.md) to Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [422](../F/First_Age_422.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) considered a new assault upon [Angband](../A/Angband.md), as Morgoth had been contained rather than wholly defeated, and the threat of new dangers devised by him was present in Fingolfin's mind. [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md) and [Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md) were like-minded, but most of the Noldor, especially the [Sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md), were content with the present peaceful state of affairs and were reluctant to pay the deadly cost such an attack would incur. Thus, no attack was made, and Morgoth was left alone to continue secretly building his strength until his preparations were completed in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [455](../F/First_Age_455.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Battle {#Battle}

On a winter's night, Morgoth sent out rivers of flame from [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md), and the [Mountains of Iron](../I/Iron_Mountains.md) spewed fires and deadly fumes. The flames consumed the plain of [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md), which was renamed [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md). Many Elves perished as they tried to escape the fire, and the confusion caused by the flame and smoke hindered the defenders that watched Angband. Then Morgoth sent forth his armies, led by [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md), first of the [Urulóki](../U/Urulóki.md). Behind Glaurung came [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md) and multitudes of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), and they slaughtered anyone they came across.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-5">[5]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/E/Elena_Kukanova_-_Dagor_Bragollach.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7d/Elena_Kukanova_-_Dagor_Bragollach.jpg/200px-Elena_Kukanova_-_Dagor_Bragollach.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="269" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7d/Elena_Kukanova_-_Dagor_Bragollach.jpg/300px-Elena_Kukanova_-_Dagor_Bragollach.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7d/Elena_Kukanova_-_Dagor_Bragollach.jpg/400px-Elena_Kukanova_-_Dagor_Bragollach.jpg 2x" data-file-width="521" data-file-height="700"/></a><figcaption><i>Dagor Bragollach</i> by <a href="../E/Elena_Kukanova.md" title="Elena Kukanova">Elena Kukanova</a></figcaption></figure>

The highlands of [Dorthonion](Dorthonion.md) bore much of the assault and were overrun. [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md) and [Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md) were slain, and many of [Bëor's folk](../H/House_of_Bëor.md) fell, including their lord [Bregolas](../B/Bregolas.md). King [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md) came north from [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) with an army, but Finrod with a small company was surrounded at the [Fen of Serech](../F/Fen_of_Serech.md) and cut off from his people. Finrod would have been killed or captured, but was saved by [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md) and his [twelve followers](../X/XII_Bëorings.md), who was fighting in western [Dorthonion](Dorthonion.md) near the [Pass of Sirion](../P/Pass_of_Sirion.md). They managed to cut through with great loss and Finrod and his folk came back to Nargothrond, while Barahir continued defending Dorthonion. It was this deed that later earned Barahir the ring of Finrod, which would become known as the [Ring of Barahir](../R/Ring_of_Barahir.md), along with a vow of abiding friendship.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the north-west [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) and [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) marched with their host to aid the sons of Finarfin, but they were driven back to the fortresses in the [mountains](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md). [Hador](../H/Hador.md), the [lord of Dor-lómin](../L/Lord_of_Dor-lómin.md), and his younger son [Gundor](../G/Gundor.md) were slain defending the rearguard of Fingolfin. In the mountain fortresses there was a fierce battle between Morgoth's forces and the Elves and [Men of the North](../H/House_of_Hador.md). Hithlum was successfully defended against the Orcs and Balrogs, but Fingolfin became separated from his people during the battle.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-5">[5]</a></sup>

The [March of Maedhros](../M/March_of_Maedhros.md) was assaulted heavily by the armies of Morgoth. With great loss, the hosts of Morgoth [forced](../B/Battle_of_the_Pass_of_Aglon.md) the [Pass of Aglon](../P/Pass_of_Aglon.md), and [Celegorm](../C/Celegorm.md) and [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md) fled with their people to Nargothrond. [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) was later able to reclose the Pass, but the horsemen of the people of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) were overwhelmed on the plain of [Lothlann](../L/Lothlann.md) and [Maglor's Gap](../M/Maglor's_Gap.md) was taken. Glaurung came there and burned the land between the arms of [Gelion](../G/Gelion.md). With the gap taken, the Orcs moved south and captured [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md)'s fortress in [Mount Rerir](../M/Mount_Rerir.md). [Thargelion](../T/Thargelion.md) was ravaged and [Lake Helevorn](../L/Lake_Helevorn.md) defiled. The Orcs then crossed over Gelion into [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md). Maglor fled to join Maedhros at Himring while Caranthir fled south with Amrod and Amras to [Amon Ereb](../A/Amon_Ereb.md), which they defended with the help of the [Green-elves](../G/Green-elves.md), and the Orcs did not come into Taur-im-Duinath or Ossiriand. Maedhros fought valiantly against the Orcs with the help of Maglor and the people of Dorthonion and the east marches, preventing Morgoth from taking the fortress on [Himring](../H/Himring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-5">[5]</a></sup>

War did not completely cease in Beleriand afterwards, but the Battle was considered to have ended with the lessening of Morgoth's onslaught at the coming of spring.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_5-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

Thus the Siege of Angband was broken, and the forces of Morgoth were able to roam at will throughout the north. His enemies were decimated, and most of the [Grey-elves](../G/Grey-elves.md) fled south to [Doriath](Doriath.md), [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md), [Falas](../F/Falas.md) and [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md). However, Morgoth could not completely destroy his foes as they scattered and hid in secret places, and he had himself been wounded.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_5-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-5">[5]</a></sup>

Morgoth's power extended over the [Northlands](../N/Northlands.md), but a few folk of [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md) remained, fighting determinedly for their lands and refusing to retreat from the attacking forces. Morgoth relentlessly sought to kill them until very few remained. So great was his wrath against them that Dorthonion was turned into a twisted land of dread, renamed <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../T/Taur-nu-Fuin.md" title="Taur-nu-Fuin">Taur-nu-Fuin</a>, and it was filled with such dark enchantment that even Orcs would not enter it unless need drove them. The women and children of Barahir's folk at last fled to [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md), some joining the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md) there, and others continuing to [Dor-lómin](Dor-lómin.md) and joining the [people](../H/House_of_Hador.md) of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../G/Galdor.md" title="Galdor">Galdor</a>. The remaining men lived as outlaws in Dorthonion, until all were slain save [Beren](../B/Beren.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_5-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-5">[5]</a></sup>

The sons of Fëanor were scattered and driven from their lands, except for Maedhros, who still held Himring. Celegorm and Curufin travelled to Nargothrond after [being defeated](../B/Battle_of_the_Pass_of_Aglon.md) in the [Pass of Aglon](../P/Pass_of_Aglon.md). For a time, the Noldor defended the [Pass of Sirion](../P/Pass_of_Sirion.md) and [Minas Tirith](<../M/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand).md>), but after the fall of Fingolfin, Sauron came and took Minas Tirith for Morgoth, turning it into a place of evil known as [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](../T/Tol-in-Gaurhoth.md). Using this pass, the servants of Morgoth came farther into Beleriand and surrounded Doriath. Yet Nargothrond and Gondolin remained hidden from Morgoth, and as the Elves began to recover their losses, southern Beleriand had peace for a time.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_5-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-5">[5]</a></sup>

### The fall of Fingolfin {#The_fall_of_Fingolfin}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="../F/Fall_of_Fingolfin.md" title="Fall of Fingolfin">Fall of Fingolfin</a></b></div></div>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/K/Kimberly_-_Fingolfin_sketch.jpeg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/ba/Kimberly_-_Fingolfin_sketch.jpeg/300px-Kimberly_-_Fingolfin_sketch.jpeg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="222" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/ba/Kimberly_-_Fingolfin_sketch.jpeg/450px-Kimberly_-_Fingolfin_sketch.jpeg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/ba/Kimberly_-_Fingolfin_sketch.jpeg/600px-Kimberly_-_Fingolfin_sketch.jpeg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="757"/></a><figcaption><i>Fingolfin&#39;s Wrath</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Kimberly.md" title="Category:Images by Kimberly">Kimberly</a></figcaption></figure>

After a year of fighting,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> when [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md), the [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md), saw what he believed to be the utter ruin of the Noldor, he rode in anger across the dust of Anfauglith and challenged Morgoth to single combat. At the doors of [Angband](../A/Angband.md) itself they fought a great duel. Fingolfin's sword, [Ringil](../R/Ringil.md), wounded Morgoth seven times. Yet he was felled by Morgoth's hammer, [Grond](<../G/Grond_(Hammer_of_the_Underworld).md>), and crushed by Morgoth's mighty foot. Fingolfin's body was saved by [Thorondor](../T/Thorondor.md) King of Eagles and brought into the mountaintops north of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). There [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) his son built a [cairn](../F/Fingolfin's_Cairn.md) over his body.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_5-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-5">[5]</a></sup>

*Dagor Bragollach* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Battle of Sudden Flame".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> It contains the elements *[dagor](Dagor.md)* ("battle") + *[bragol](../B/Bragol.md)* ("quick, sudden") + *[lach](../L/Lach.md)* ("flame").

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The earliest concept of the battle appears in the unfinished poem *[The Lay of Leithian](../T/The_Lay_of_Leithian.md)*. Although the account is brief, some elements of it remain in later writings such as [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md) saving Felagund and the deaths of [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md) and <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../E/Egnor.md" title="Egnor">Egnor</a> (Aegnor).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> In the *[Sketch of the Mythology](../S/Sketch_of_the_Mythology.md)* where Tolkien started to piece together the legends, the battle appears briefly once more. However, Christopher commented that the new material that appears in later writings may have already been in the mind of his father when he wrote the *Sketch*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](../Q/Quenta_Noldorinwa.md)* there is a huge expansion of material in this version compared to the *Sketch*, of which many elements appear in *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-QNINE_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QNINE-10">[10]</a></sup> It was originally labelled as the second battle, but was later changed to the third battle in the [Wars of Beleriand](../W/Wars_of_Beleriand.md), partly due to the inclusion of the [Dagor Aglareb](Dagor_Aglareb.md) as the second battle.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

The naming of the battle went through many revisions in the writing. The battle was not named in early writings until the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](../Q/Quenta_Noldorinwa.md)*, where it is called the **Battle of Sudden Flame**.<sup id="cite_ref-QNINE_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QNINE-10">[10]</a></sup> It was later changed to **Battle of Sudden Fire**<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> and for the first time given an Elvish name, **Dagor Hurbreged**,<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> then changed to **Dagor Húr-Breged**.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> The battle was referred to as **Dagor Vreged-úr** in the *[Quenta Silmarillion](<../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road).md>)*.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> In the latest versions it takes its final form **Dagor Bragollach**, **Battle of Sudden Flame**.<sup id="cite_ref-GADB_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GADB-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>
