**Doderic Brandybuck** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Doderic was a son of [Seredic Brandybuck](/wiki/Seredic_Brandybuck) and [Hilda Bracegirdle](/wiki/Hilda_Bracegirdle). He attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Doderic* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) suggests that the name *Doderic* is a Germanic form of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theuderic" title="wikipedia:Theuderic">Theuderic</a> with the variatons <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodoric_the_Great" title="wikipedia:Theodoric the Great">Theodoric</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dietrich_von_Bern" title="wikipedia:Dietrich von Bern">Dietrich</a> and is a combination of the elements *theud* ("people") and *ric* ("rule", "reign").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier version of the family tree of the Brandybuck family he had the name <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roderic" title="wikipedia:Roderic">Roderic</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the name of a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visigoths" title="wikipedia:Visigoths">Visigothic</a> king of Spain between A.D. 710 and 711.
