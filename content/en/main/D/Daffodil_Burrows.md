**Daffodil Burrows** was a [Harfoot](/wiki/Harfoots "Harfoots") who was once a part of a Harfoot caravan during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Daffodil was invented by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*.

## History {#History}

Not much is known about Daffodil Burrows. At one point in time during the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), she was lost to [Wolves](/wiki/Wolves) during one of the seasonal migrations, leaving her husband, [Sadoc Burrows](/wiki/Sadoc_Burrows), to grieve for her in spirit during the remembrance ceremony of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Harvest_Festival&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Harvest Festival (page does not exist)">Harvest Festival</a>.

Later, as the sun rose after Sadoc is mortally wounded by the [Nomad](/wiki/Mystics "Mystics") at the [East Bight](/wiki/East_Bight), he died believing in his heart that he would soon see his wife, Daffodil Burrows, again.
