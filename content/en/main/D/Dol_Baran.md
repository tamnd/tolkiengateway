**Dol Baran** was the last southernmost hill of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Palantir_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

Dol Baran was round, crowned with heather with green lower slopes. It was located southwest of the southwestern end of [Nan Curunir](../N/Nan_Curunir.md) about a mile west of the road that ran from [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) to the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Palantir_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the visit to [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) the party accompanying King [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) camped in a dale on its southern side for the night<sup id="cite_ref-Palantir_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup> on [5 March](../_/5_March.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was here that [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) looked into the [Palantír of Orthanc](../P/Palantír_of_Orthanc.md) and later that a [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) flew over on its way to see [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Palantir_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dol Baran* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests that it means "Bare Hill".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is a compound of *[dol](Dol.md)* ("head", "hill") and *[paran](../P/Paran.md)* ("naked", "bare", "smooth", "shaven") referring to its lack of trees.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
