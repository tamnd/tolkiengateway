**Dol Baran** was the last southernmost hill of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Palantir_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

Dol Baran was round, crowned with heather with green lower slopes. It was located southwest of the southwestern end of [Nan Curunir](/wiki/Nan_Curunir) about a mile west of the road that ran from [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) to the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen).<sup id="cite_ref-Palantir_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the visit to [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) the party accompanying King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) camped in a dale on its southern side for the night<sup id="cite_ref-Palantir_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup> on [5 March](/wiki/5_March).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was here that [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) looked into the [Palantír of Orthanc](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADr_of_Orthanc) and later that a [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) flew over on its way to see [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)<sup id="cite_ref-Palantir_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dol Baran* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests that it means "Bare Hill".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is a compound of *[dol](/wiki/Dol "Dol")* ("head", "hill") and *[paran](/wiki/Paran "Paran")* ("naked", "bare", "smooth", "shaven") referring to its lack of trees.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
