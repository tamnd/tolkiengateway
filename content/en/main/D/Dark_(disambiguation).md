**Dark** can refer to more than one concept:

- **[Darkness](Darkness.md)**, a weapon and concept used by the [Dark Lords](Dark_Lord.md).
- **[Darkness Unescapable](Darkness_Unescapable.md)**, a name for the darkness during the [Dawnless Day](Dawnless_Day.md).
- **[Dark Lord](Dark_Lord.md)**, a name referring to [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) and [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).
- <b><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Dark_Throne&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dark Throne (page does not exist)">Dark Throne</a></b>, the term used to refer to the Throne of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) and used to describe Morgoth's power and forces.
- **[Outer Dark](../O/Outer_Dark.md)**, the [Void](../V/Void.md).
- <b><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Everlasting_Darkness&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Everlasting Darkness (page does not exist)">Everlasting Darkness</a></b>, a term referred to in the [Oath of Fëanor](../O/Oath_of_Fëanor.md).
- "**[A Knife in the Dark](../A/A_Knife_in_the_Dark.md)**", the eleventh chapter of the first book in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.
- "**[A Journey in the Dark](../A/A_Journey_in_the_Dark.md)**", the fourth chapter of the second book in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.
- **[Dark Door](Dark_Door.md)**, the entrance to the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md).
- **[Dark Gate](Dark_Gate.md)**, the [Gate of Wood](../G/Gate_of_Wood.md).
- **[Dark Guard](Dark_Guard.md)**, a division of [Gondolindrim](../G/Gondolindrim.md).
- <b><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Dark_Kings&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dark Kings (page does not exist)">Dark Kings</a></b>, some kings in the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).
- **[Dark Plague](Dark_Plague.md)**, a name for the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md).
- **[Dark Land](Dark_Land.md)**, a continent south of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).
- **[Dark Tree](Dark_Tree.md)**, an abstract term in *[The New Shadow](../T/The_New_Shadow.md)*.
- **[Dark Years](Dark_Years.md)**, a time during the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).
- **[Dark Elf](Dark_Elf.md)**, a title of [Eöl](../E/Eöl.md).
- "**[Riddles in the Dark](../R/Riddles_in_the_Dark.md)**", the fifth chapter of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.
- <i><b><a href="Dark.md" title="Dark">Dark</a></b></i>, a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- <i><b><a href="Dark_Are_the_Clouds_about_the_North.md" title="Dark Are the Clouds about the North">Dark Are the Clouds about the North</a></b></i>, a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- <b><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Huorn-dark&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Huorn-dark (page does not exist)">Huorn-dark</a></b>, the shrouding darkness of the [Huorns](../H/Huorns.md).
- **[Men of Darkness](../M/Men_of_Darkness.md)**, were [Men](../M/Men.md) in the east of Middle-earth who fell to evil.
- **[Nether Darkness](../N/Nether_Darkness.md)**, a place in the far south of [Arda](../A/Arda.md).
- "**[From dark Dunharrow in the dim morning](../F/From_dark_Dunharrow_in_the_dim_morning.md)**", the first line in a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- **[Darkening of Valinor](Darkening_of_Valinor.md)**, the death of the [Two Trees of Valinor](../T/Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md).
- "**[Of the Darkening of Valinor](../O/Of_the_Darkening_of_Valinor.md)**", the eighth chapter of the *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.
- "**[Out of doubt, out of dark to the day’s rising](../O/Out_of_doubt,_out_of_dark_to_the_day’s_rising.md)**", the first line in a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- "**[Out of doubt, out of dark, to the day’s rising](../O/Out_of_doubt,_out_of_dark,_to_the_day’s_rising.md)**", the first line in a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- "**[Under the mountain dark and tall](../U/Under_the_mountain_dark_and_tall.md)**", the first line in a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
- **[Voice of the Dark](../V/Voice_of_the_Dark.md)**, a name for [Eru](../E/Eru.md) by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).
- <b><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Dark_Marches&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dark Marches (page does not exist)">Dark Marches</a></b>, a place in [Faery](../F/Faery.md).
