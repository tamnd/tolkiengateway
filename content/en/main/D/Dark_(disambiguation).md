**Dark** can refer to more than one concept:

- **[Darkness](/wiki/Darkness)**, a weapon and concept used by the [Dark Lords](/wiki/Dark_Lord "Dark Lord").
- **[Darkness Unescapable](/wiki/Darkness_Unescapable)**, a name for the darkness during the [Dawnless Day](/wiki/Dawnless_Day).
- **[Dark Lord](/wiki/Dark_Lord)**, a name referring to [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) and [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).
- <b><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Dark_Throne&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dark Throne (page does not exist)">Dark Throne</a></b>, the term used to refer to the Throne of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) and used to describe Morgoth's power and forces.
- **[Outer Dark](/wiki/Outer_Dark)**, the [Void](/wiki/Void).
- <b><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Everlasting_Darkness&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Everlasting Darkness (page does not exist)">Everlasting Darkness</a></b>, a term referred to in the [Oath of Fëanor](/wiki/Oath_of_F%C3%ABanor).
- "**[A Knife in the Dark](/wiki/A_Knife_in_the_Dark)**", the eleventh chapter of the first book in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.
- "**[A Journey in the Dark](/wiki/A_Journey_in_the_Dark)**", the fourth chapter of the second book in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.
- **[Dark Door](/wiki/Dark_Door)**, the entrance to the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead).
- **[Dark Gate](/wiki/Dark_Gate)**, the [Gate of Wood](/wiki/Gate_of_Wood).
- **[Dark Guard](/wiki/Dark_Guard)**, a division of [Gondolindrim](/wiki/Gondolindrim).
- <b><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Dark_Kings&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dark Kings (page does not exist)">Dark Kings</a></b>, some kings in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).
- **[Dark Plague](/wiki/Dark_Plague)**, a name for the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague).
- **[Dark Land](/wiki/Dark_Land)**, a continent south of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).
- **[Dark Tree](/wiki/Dark_Tree)**, an abstract term in *[The New Shadow](/wiki/The_New_Shadow)*.
- **[Dark Years](/wiki/Dark_Years)**, a time during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).
- **[Dark Elf](/wiki/Dark_Elf)**, a title of [Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l).
- "**[Riddles in the Dark](/wiki/Riddles_in_the_Dark)**", the fifth chapter of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Dark" title="Dark">Dark</a></b></i>, a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Dark_Are_the_Clouds_about_the_North" title="Dark Are the Clouds about the North">Dark Are the Clouds about the North</a></b></i>, a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- <b><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Huorn-dark&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Huorn-dark (page does not exist)">Huorn-dark</a></b>, the shrouding darkness of the [Huorns](/wiki/Huorns).
- **[Men of Darkness](/wiki/Men_of_Darkness)**, were [Men](/wiki/Men) in the east of Middle-earth who fell to evil.
- **[Nether Darkness](/wiki/Nether_Darkness)**, a place in the far south of [Arda](/wiki/Arda).
- "**[From dark Dunharrow in the dim morning](/wiki/From_dark_Dunharrow_in_the_dim_morning)**", the first line in a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- **[Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor)**, the death of the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor).
- "**[Of the Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Of_the_Darkening_of_Valinor)**", the eighth chapter of the *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.
- "**[Out of doubt, out of dark to the day’s rising](/wiki/Out_of_doubt,_out_of_dark_to_the_day%E2%80%99s_rising)**", the first line in a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- "**[Out of doubt, out of dark, to the day’s rising](/wiki/Out_of_doubt,_out_of_dark,_to_the_day%E2%80%99s_rising)**", the first line in a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- "**[Under the mountain dark and tall](/wiki/Under_the_mountain_dark_and_tall)**", the first line in a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
- **[Voice of the Dark](/wiki/Voice_of_the_Dark)**, a name for [Eru](/wiki/Eru) by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).
- <b><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Dark_Marches&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dark Marches (page does not exist)">Dark Marches</a></b>, a place in [Faery](/wiki/Faery).

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- [Dark Wizard](/wiki/Dark_Wizard), a [Wizard](/wiki/Order_of_Wizards "Order of Wizards") in [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn).
- "[A Knife in the Dark (scene)](</wiki/A_Knife_in_the_Dark_(scene)>)", the fifteenth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and the nineteenth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)").
- "[A Knife In The Dark (soundtrack)](</wiki/A_Knife_In_The_Dark_(soundtrack)>)", the seventh track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)* by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore).
- "[A Journey in the Dark (scene)](</wiki/A_Journey_in_the_Dark_(scene)>)", the twenty-eighth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and the thirty-fourth of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)").
- "[A Journey In The Dark (soundtrack)](</wiki/A_Journey_In_The_Dark_(soundtrack)>)", the twelfth track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)* by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore).
- "[Riddles in the Dark (soundtrack)](</wiki/Riddles_in_the_Dark_(soundtrack)>)", the eighth track of disc two of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)* and the [special edition soundtrack](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack_-_Special_Edition "The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack - Special Edition"), by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore).
- *[Middle-earth: Dark Minions](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Dark_Minions)*, the second expansion set for *[Middle-earth: The Wizards](/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Wizards)*.
- *[Middle-earth: Dark Minions Player Guide](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Dark_Minions_Player_Guide)*, a source-book for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).
- *[Dark Mage of Rhudaur](/wiki/Dark_Mage_of_Rhudaur)*, a supplement for the first edition of *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*.
- *[Denizens of the Dark Wood](/wiki/Denizens_of_the_Dark_Wood)*, a module for the first edition of *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*.
- *[The Dark Elf](/wiki/The_Dark_Elf)*, the fourteenth track of [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian)'s Tolkien themed album, [Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth).
- *[A Dark Passage](/wiki/A_Dark_Passage)*, the twenty-first track of [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian)'s Tolkien themed album, [Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth).
- *[Jewels of Darkness](/wiki/Jewels_of_Darkness)*, a trilogy of games by [Level 9 Computing](/wiki/Level_9_Computing).
- *[Darker than the Darkness](/wiki/Darker_than_the_Darkness)*, a set of two additional adventures for the *[Lord of the Rings Adventure Game](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings_Adventure_Game)*.
- *[The Darkening of Mirkwood](/wiki/The_Darkening_of_Mirkwood)*, a supplement for [The One Ring](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)") role-playing game.

## Other {#Other}

- [Riddles in the Dark (podcast)](</wiki/Riddles_in_the_Dark_(podcast)>), a podcast hosted by [Corey Olsen](/wiki/Corey_Olsen), Dave Kale, and Trish Lambert that was released from 2012 to 2015 about the [*The Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").
- *[Ring of Darkness](/wiki/Ring_of_Darkness)* ("Кольцо Тьмы"), a Russian-language fantasy series set in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) 300 years after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), written by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Perumov" title="wikipedia:Nick Perumov">Nick Perumov</a>.
- *[The Dark Powers of Tolkien](/wiki/The_Dark_Powers_of_Tolkien)*, a book written by [David Day](/wiki/David_Day), published in [2018](/wiki/2018).
