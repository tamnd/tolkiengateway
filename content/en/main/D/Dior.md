**Dior** was the son of [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien), and the grandson and heir to the throne of [Elu Thingol](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol"), [King of Doriath](/wiki/King_of_Doriath). Dior was the first of the [Peredhil](/wiki/Peredhil).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Dior was born on the island of [Tol Galen](/wiki/Tol_Galen) in [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-4">[4]</a></sup> When he was 27, he married [Nimloth of Doriath](/wiki/Nimloth_of_Doriath),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> and took her back to live by the [Lanthir Lamath](/wiki/Lanthir_Lamath) waterfall at the base of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains). There, they had three children: [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing), [Eluréd](/wiki/Elur%C3%A9d), and [Elurín](/wiki/Elur%C3%ADn).

After [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) was slain by the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) who coveted the [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr), Dior with his father and a host of [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves) of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand) journeyed swiftly north to the river [Ascar](/wiki/Ascar) and fought the [Dwarves of Nogrod](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Nogrod) in the [Battle of Sarn Athrad](/wiki/Battle_of_Sarn_Athrad).<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-6">[5]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [503](/wiki/First_Age_503 "First Age 503"), he and his family went to [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth) and restored the realm of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath). After the death of his parents, a lord of the [Laiquendi](/wiki/Laiquendi) came to [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) bringing the [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr), which was worn by his mother. After mourning, he decided to wear it himself. When he placed the [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr) on himself, it sat lightly and he appeared as the fairest of three living races: [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar), [Elves](/wiki/Elves), and [Men](/wiki/Men). This news came to the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor), who came to [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) with their followers, consumed by the [Oath of Fëanor](/wiki/Oath_of_F%C3%ABanor).<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-6">[5]</a></sup>

Dior managed to kill [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm), and [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) were also slain, but the kingdom of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) was destroyed. Dior and Nimloth were slain during the [Second Kinslaying](/wiki/Second_Kinslaying), along with anyone trying to prevent the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) from gaining the [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril).<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-6">[5]</a></sup>

After the attack, the servants of [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) left [Eluréd](/wiki/Elur%C3%A9d) and [Elurín](/wiki/Elur%C3%ADn) in the forest to die. [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing), however, carrying the [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr), escaped with the remnant of the [Elves of Doriath](/wiki/Iathrim "Iathrim") to the [Havens of Sirion](/wiki/Havens_of_Sirion).<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-6">[5]</a></sup> Years later, she would wed [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) and together they would seek aid from [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), the name *Dior* is said to mean "successor" in [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin). The name derives from [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *ndeuro* ("follower, successor"), from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NDEW&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NDEW (page does not exist)">NDEW</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Dior was first called <i><b>Aranel</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-4">[4]</a></sup> probably [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for ("Elf King").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> Later he was called <i><b>Eluchíl</b></i> ("Heir of Elu") and **the Fair**.<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### The Book of Lost Tales {#The_Book_of_Lost_Tales}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, a name of Dior was <i><b><a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Ausir_(disambiguation)" title="Ausir (disambiguation)">Ausir</a></b></i> ([Gn.](/wiki/Gnomish "Gnomish") "The Wealthy").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Dior.27s_Fate"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Dior&#39;s_Fate">Dior&#39;s Fate</span></h3>

In an older version of the *Quenta Silmarillion*, [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) spoke this judgment to [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil):

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>To Eärendel I remit the ban, and the peril that he took upon himself out of love for the Two Kindreds shall not fall on him; neither shall it fall upon Elwing who entered into peril for love of Eärendel: save only in this: they shall not ever walk again among Elves or Men in the Outer Lands. Now all those who have the blood of mortal Men, in whatever part, great or small, are mortal, unless other doom be granted to them; but in this matter the power of doom is given to me.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings" title="The Lost Road and Other Writings">The Lost Road and Other Writings</a></i>, Part Two: Valinor and Middle-earth before The Lord of the Rings, Quenta Silmarillion</cite></p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) interprets it as that Dior would inherit his father's mortality, irrespective of his mother's nature and choice:<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>§9 It is to be observed that according to the judgement of Manwë Dior Thingol’s Heir, son of Beren, was mortal irrespective of the choice of his mother.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings" title="The Lost Road and Other Writings">The Lost Road and Other Writings</a></i>, Part Two: Valinor and Middle-earth before The Lord of the Rings, The Conclusion Of The Quenta Silmarillion, §9</cite></p>
</blockquote>
