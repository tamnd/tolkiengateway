<i><b>dol</b></i> in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) means "head" and also poetically "hill" instead of *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From [OS](/wiki/OS) \**dola*, [PQ](/wiki/PQ) [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NDOL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NDOL (page does not exist)">NDOL</a><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
