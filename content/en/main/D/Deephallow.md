**Deephallow** was a village on [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md)'s eastern border, built on the banks of the River [Brandywine](../B/Brandywine.md), just north to the point where [Shirebourn](../S/Shirebourn.md) flowed into the river. Immediately across Brandywine was [Haysend](../H/Haysend.md), the southernmost point of [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted that "is not clear in etymology (not meant to be - not all names are!)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[David Salo](David_Salo.md) suggested that the name represents a speculative [Old Hobbitish](../O/Old_English.md) form \**Deophealh* "deep <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/haugh" title="wiktionary:haugh">haugh</a>". A haugh meant first "angle, corner, secluded place" and later "land by a river", consistent to the location of the village near the wedge formed by Brandywine and Shirebourn.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The same element is also seen in "[Woodhall](../W/Woodhall.md)".

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

Although Deephallow is never mentioned in the text, there was a mention of it in a draft of *[A Short Cut to Mushrooms](../A/A_Short_Cut_to_Mushrooms.md)*, as the place where the [Causeway](../C/Causeway.md) leads.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
