<i><b>Dungeons &amp; Dragons</b></i> is a popular fantasy role-playing game that was first published by [TSR](../T/TSR.md) in [1974](../_/1974.md). While unrelated to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s works, it took inspiration from it. Since [1997](../_/1997.md) it is owned and published by [Wizards of the Coast](../W/Wizards_of_the_Coast.md), who also publish the card game *[Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md)*.

Branching off from war games as the first published tabletop role-playing game, it paved the way for later games such as *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)*. Until [2013](../_/2013.md), [Dragon Magazine](Dragon_Magazine.md) served as one of its official periodicals.

## Inspiration from Tolkien {#Inspiration_from_Tolkien}

*Dungeons &amp; Dragons*, especially in its early days, borrowed liberally from various sources.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <sup id="cite_ref-Dragon95_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dragon95-2">[2]</a></sup> Its use of terms from Tolkien's works specifically came under scrutiny in [1977](../_/1977.md) when TSR was threatened with a lawsuit by Elan Merchandising or [Tolkien Enterprises](../T/Tolkien_Enterprises.md). Their primary objection concerned TSR's board game *[The Battle of the Five Armies](<../B/Battle_of_the_Five_Armies_(board_game).md>)*, though they also targeted terminology within *Dungeons &amp; Dragons*, leading to some name changes in subsequent releases after the suit was settled:

- *[Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md)* to "[halfling](../H/Halflings.md)"
- *[Ent](../E/Ents.md)* to "treant"
- *[Balrog](../B/Balrogs.md)* to "Type VI demon \[balor\]"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Other names, like [Elves](../E/Elves.md), [Half-elves](../H/Half-elven.md), [Dwarves](Dwarves.md) and [Dragons](Dragons.md), are so common, and not necessarily related to Tolkien's works, that they still stand.

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

*[Adventures in Middle-earth](../A/Adventures_in_Middle-earth.md)* and its successor *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying.md)* are licensed role-playing games for 5th edition *Dungeons &amp; Dragons*.

Fans also adapted older editions to the setting as the *Eä d20 RPG*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
