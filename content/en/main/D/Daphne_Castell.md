**Daphne R. Castell** ([1929](../_/1929.md)<sup id="cite_ref-FJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FJ-1">[1]</a></sup> - [3 August](../_/3_August.md) [1983](../_/1983.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) was a British science-fiction writer and librarian. During the 1950s at Oxford University, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) supervised her thesis about Thomas Chatterton, which was never completed.<sup id="cite_ref-FJ_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FJ-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CGC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CGC-3">[3]</a></sup> She married Malcolm Cloke in 1955.<sup id="cite_ref-FJ_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FJ-1">[1]</a></sup>

In late spring or early summer 1966, Castell conducted an [interview with Tolkien](../../index/I/Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) for the magazine *New Worlds*.<sup id="cite_ref-CGC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CGC-3">[3]</a></sup>
