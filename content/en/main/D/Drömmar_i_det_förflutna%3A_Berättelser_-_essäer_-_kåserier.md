<i><b>Drömmar i det förflutna: Berättelser - essäer - kåserier</b></i> (Swedish: *Dreams in the Past: Tales - Essays - Causeries*) is a Swedish-language book by Erik Lundberg. It includes the essay "Den vita stjärnan bortom Ephel Duath" ("The White Star above [Ephel Duath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath "Ephel Dúath")"), pp. 155-\[164\]. The essay is a reprint of the magazine article "Den vita stjärnan över Ephel Duath: en studie över J.R.R. Tolkiens Sagan om Ringen" ("The White Star beyond Ephel Duath: A Study of J.R.R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings"), published in *Studiekamraten* 62 (1980, nr 8, pp. 9–10).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
