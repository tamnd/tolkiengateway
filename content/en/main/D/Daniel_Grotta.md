**Daniel T. Grotta** ([1944](../_/1944.md)—[13 December](../_/13_December.md) [2015](../_/2015.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>), sometimes credited as **Daniel Grotta-Kurska**, was a freelance american journalist. In [1976](../_/1976.md), Grotta published the first biography of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). He gave many speeches on Tolkien, and appeared on both radio and TV shows discussing Tolkien and his work.
