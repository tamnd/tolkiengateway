**Daniel T. Grotta** ([1944](/wiki/1944)—[13 December](/wiki/13_December) [2015](/wiki/2015)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>), sometimes credited as **Daniel Grotta-Kurska**, was a freelance american journalist. In [1976](/wiki/1976), Grotta published the first biography of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). He gave many speeches on Tolkien, and appeared on both radio and TV shows discussing Tolkien and his work.
