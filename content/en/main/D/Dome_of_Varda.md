The **Dome of Varda** was, in a [later version](/wiki/Round_World_version_of_the_Silmarillion "Round World version of the Silmarillion") of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), a false firmament above [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was made by [Varda](/wiki/Varda) to protect the airs of Valinor from [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth"), keeping out his spirits and spies. It was spangled with [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars") which were in fact very brilliant sparks. In consequence, the [sun](/wiki/Sun "Sun")-day was not taken account in the reckoning of time by the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) dwelling in [Aman](/wiki/Aman).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> This Dome inspired King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) to build his throne room, called *[Menelrond](/wiki/Menelrond)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The Dome of Varda was also called "the Great Dome of Valmar" ; or *Nur-menel*, the lesser firmament, by opposition to *[Tar-menel](/wiki/Tarmenel "Tarmenel")*, the true firmament.

The expression can be also found in the plural form, as "Domes of Varda",<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> but it's not clear what these "domes" mean<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> (possibly a confusion with the "silver domes of Valmar"<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>).
