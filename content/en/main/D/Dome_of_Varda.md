The **Dome of Varda** was, in a [later version](../R/Round_World_version_of_the_Silmarillion.md) of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), a false firmament above [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was made by [Varda](../V/Varda.md) to protect the airs of Valinor from [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md), keeping out his spirits and spies. It was spangled with [stars](../S/Stars.md) which were in fact very brilliant sparks. In consequence, the [sun](../S/Sun.md)-day was not taken account in the reckoning of time by the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) dwelling in [Aman](../A/Aman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> This Dome inspired King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) to build his throne room, called *[Menelrond](../M/Menelrond.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The Dome of Varda was also called "the Great Dome of Valmar" ; or *Nur-menel*, the lesser firmament, by opposition to *[Tar-menel](../T/Tarmenel.md)*, the true firmament.

The expression can be also found in the plural form, as "Domes of Varda",<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> but it's not clear what these "domes" mean<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> (possibly a confusion with the "silver domes of Valmar"<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>).
