**Dwarf-road** refers to two distinctly-different roads:

- [Dwarf-road of Beleriand](/wiki/Dwarf-road_of_Beleriand), built by the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) to trade with the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).
- Dwarf-road of Mirkwood, also known as the [Old Forest Road](/wiki/Old_Forest_Road), built by [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) to travel between their kingdoms.
