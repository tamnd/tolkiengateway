**Daniel Horne** (born [3 June](/wiki/3_June) [1960](/wiki/1960)) is an American artist. Starting in 1986, he began freelancing as an illustrator for roleplaying games, often illustrating for [Dungeons and Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons) and [Dragon Magazine](/wiki/Dragon_Magazine). He was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create illustrations for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* and to make card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.

In the late 1990s, his interests shifted to doll-making. After an 28 inch Gandalf doll he made was nominated for a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chesley_Awards" title="wikipedia:Chesley Awards">Chesley Award</a> in [1997](/wiki/1997), he became a designer for [Middle-earth Toys](/wiki/Middle-earth_Toys).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
