**Daniel Horne** (born [3 June](../_/3_June.md) [1960](../_/1960.md)) is an American artist. Starting in 1986, he began freelancing as an illustrator for roleplaying games, often illustrating for [Dungeons and Dragons](Dungeons_and_Dragons.md) and [Dragon Magazine](Dragon_Magazine.md). He was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create illustrations for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)* and to make card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.

In the late 1990s, his interests shifted to doll-making. After an 28 inch Gandalf doll he made was nominated for a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chesley_Awards" title="wikipedia:Chesley Awards">Chesley Award</a> in [1997](../_/1997.md), he became a designer for [Middle-earth Toys](../M/Middle-earth_Toys.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
