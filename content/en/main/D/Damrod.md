**Damrod** was a [ranger of Ithilien](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien "Rangers of Ithilien").

## History {#History}

Damrod was the companion of [Mablung](</wiki/Mablung_(ranger_of_Ithilien)> "Mablung (ranger of Ithilien)") and a trusted guard of [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir). Together he and Mablung watched [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") during the ambush of the [Men of Harad](/wiki/Haradrim "Haradrim") in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien). While they waited before the battle Damrod told the hobbits of the Enemy's dealings with the [Southrons](/wiki/Haradrim "Haradrim"). Damrod had little hope; he believed "the days of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) are numbered, and the walls of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) are doomed".<sup id="cite_ref-herbs_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-herbs-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the ambush Damrod and Mablung continued to serve as watch-guards over the hobbits as the Rangers journeyed to [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Mablung and Damrod are first met in <a href="/wiki/Osgiliath" title="Osgiliath">Osgiliath</a> culverts, hiding from the enemy forces above. Upon entering the Wastes of Morannon, Damrod is chosen to lead the faint-hearted soldiers relieved by <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>, now commanded by <a href="/wiki/Duinhir" title="Duinhir">Duinhir</a>, towards <a href="/wiki/Cair_Andros" title="Cair Andros">Cair Andros</a> through <a href="/wiki/Ithilien" title="Ithilien">Ithilien</a>. He later leads a group of Rangers inside <a href="/wiki/Minas_Morgul" title="Minas Morgul">Minas Morgul</a> itself, using the confusion among enemy forces to sneak between the guard towers beneath the enemy&#39;s notice.</dd></dl>
