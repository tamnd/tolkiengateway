**Damrod** was a [ranger of Ithilien](../R/Rangers_of_Ithilien.md).

## History {#History}

Damrod was the companion of [Mablung](<../M/Mablung_(ranger_of_Ithilien).md>) and a trusted guard of [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md). Together he and Mablung watched [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) during the ambush of the [Men of Harad](../H/Haradrim.md) in [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md). While they waited before the battle Damrod told the hobbits of the Enemy's dealings with the [Southrons](../H/Haradrim.md). Damrod had little hope; he believed "the days of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) are numbered, and the walls of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) are doomed".<sup id="cite_ref-herbs_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-herbs-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the ambush Damrod and Mablung continued to serve as watch-guards over the hobbits as the Rangers journeyed to [Henneth Annûn](../H/Henneth_Annûn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
