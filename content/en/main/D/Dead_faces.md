The **dead faces**, also called **Corpse-candles**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or **candles of corpses**, were apparitions of [ghostly](../G/Ghosts.md) faces of dead [Elven](../E/Elves.md), [Mannish](../M/Men.md), and [Orcish](../O/Orcs.md) warriors in the [Mere of Dead Faces](../M/Mere_of_Dead_Faces.md) in the [Dead Marshes](Dead_Marshes.md). The faces, rotting and twisted, with weed caught in their hair, appeared in dark waters being lit by pale and fell lights, as from unseen candles.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) told [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Sam_Gamgee.md) that there was a [great battle](../B/Battle_of_Dagorlad.md) long ago in which Men, Elves and Orcs fought for months at the [Black Gates](../B/Black_Gate.md) and that the marshes had expanded since the battle and had swallowed up the graves of the warriors who fell in the battle. How the dead warriors came to be transformed into such [undead](../U/Undead.md) beings is unknown. Sam said that the Dead cannot be really there, because the battle was an age ago and asked if the presence of dead Men, Elves and Orcs in the marshes was some "devilry hatched in the Dark Land", likely referring to [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s dark arts in the nearby [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A *corpse candle* is defined as a "luminous appearance, resembling the flame of a candle, sometimes seen in churchyards and other damp places, superstitiously regarded as portending death".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

For a [poem](../T/The_Last_Ark.md), [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) created the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) phrase *ve loikolíkuma*, meaning "a corpse-candle".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In an earlier version of the same poem, the [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) phrase was *ve kaivo-kalma*, meaning "as a corpse-candle".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Corpse Candles and Corpse lanterns are seen as two variants of the same basic type of creature. The latter are said to be more powerful and intelligent, capable of a &#34;more alluring web&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> A related creature are the corpse-lights, which haunt underground areas of the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Cards depicting these creatures include &#34;Corpse-candle&#34; and &#34;Wisp of Pale Sheen&#34;.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2002.md" title="2002">2002</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>While Frodo in the book only claims to have <i>seen</i> the dead faces, <a href="../P/Peter_Jackson.md" title="Peter Jackson">Peter Jackson</a>&#39;s film version includes a dramatic twist: Frodo falls into the water as under a spell from the dead.</dd></dl>

<b>2002-5: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game">The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The faces of the dead are classified as phantoms, a subdivision of ghosts.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Corpse-candles are a form of the Dead, floating balls of light that haunt marshes and grave-sites, swarming around the player.</dd></dl>
