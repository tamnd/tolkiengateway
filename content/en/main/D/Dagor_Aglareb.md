The **Dagor Aglareb** (**Glorious Battle**) was the third great battle in the [Wars of Beleriand](/wiki/Wars_of_Beleriand), and a resounding victory for the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) over [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).

It was the first major battle since Morgoth's forces [had been annihilated](/wiki/Dagor-nuin-Giliath "Dagor-nuin-Giliath") nearly a hundred years prior by the [House of Fëanor](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor). Part of the reason for the long delay was that Morgoth himself had been in the [East](/wiki/East), corrupting [Men](/wiki/Men) after learning of their arising following the first emergence of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun); concerned by the growing union of the Eldar, however, he returned to [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).

The battle began suddenly with earthquakes, fire, and a multi-front assault by Morgoth's forces. These forces were repelled though, and ultimately destroyed on the plains of [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen) by a united force of the Eldar led by [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros).

As a result of this battle the Eldar tightened their leaguer, establishing the long [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband). Morgoth, in turn, adopted more insidious methods, seeking to capture Eldar alive to coerce from them knowledge and skill, and to foment dissension between their kingdoms. As Morgoth's attention became fully set on the Eldar, several tribes of Men were able to escape from his worship, wondering West and eventually becoming the [Edain](/wiki/Edain).

## Background {#Background}

In [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1495](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1495 "Year of the Trees 1495"), Melkor ended the [Days of Bliss](/wiki/Days_of_Bliss) and ushered in the [Long Night](/wiki/Long_Night) when he brought about the [death of the Two Trees](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor "Darkening of Valinor"), murdered [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB) the [King of the Noldor](/wiki/King_of_the_Noldor), and stole the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmaril "Silmaril"). Morgoth, as Melkor was thereafter called, then returned to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and re-established himself within his northern fortress of [Angband](/wiki/Angband).<sup id="cite_ref-flight_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-flight-1">[1]</a></sup>

Soon after, in [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1497](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1497 "Year of the Trees 1497"), Morgoth launched his [first great assault](/wiki/First_Battle_of_Beleriand "First Battle of Beleriand") against the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) under King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol). While his eastern host was destroyed by Thingol, aided by the [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves) and [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains "Dwarves of the Blue Mountains"), his western host captured most of [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand), save the [Havens of the Falas](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas) which were put to siege. Thingol thereafter withdrew what people he could into his realm, and Queen [Melian](/wiki/Melian) established a [fence of enchantment](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian "Girdle of Melian") about it, thus both protecting and isolating them.<sup id="cite_ref-sindar_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sindar-2">[2]</a></sup>

In that same year however, the first of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), the host of Fëanor, [returned to Middle-earth](/wiki/Return_of_the_Noldor "Return of the Noldor"), having pursued Morgoth from [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). Morgoth rallied his forces and [launched a surprise attack](/wiki/Dagor-nuin-Giliath "Dagor-nuin-Giliath") against the Noldor encampment at [Lake Mithrim](/wiki/Lake_Mithrim), but though he far outnumbered them, his [Orcs](/wiki/Orc "Orc") were no match against [Elves who had beheld the Light](/wiki/Calaquendi "Calaquendi"), and were driven back with great slaughter. [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), in his wrath however, drew far ahead of the van of his host in pursuit of the fleeing Orcs, and was surrounded and mortally wounded when Morgoth's forces turned to bay, joined by [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrog "Balrog") issued from Angband to aid them. Fëanor's eldest son, Maedhros, was further captured following a feigned parlay.<sup id="cite_ref-return_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-return-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1500](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1500 "Year of the Trees 1500") a second, even greater, host of the Noldor under Fingolfin arrived in Middle-earth, having crossed the [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB). At this time also came the first rising of the [Moon](/wiki/Moon) and [Sun](/wiki/Sun), the later of which dismayed Morgoth and his servants, causing him to withdraw them and send forth a great reek and dark cloud to hide his lands from its light.<sup id="cite_ref-return_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-return-3">[3]</a></sup>

Following the first dawn of the Sun, Morgoth's spies brought him tidings of the [arising of Men](/wiki/Awakening_of_Men "Awakening of Men") in the East. So great did Morgoth deem this matter that he himself secretly departed Angband under shadow, leaving command of the War to his lieutenant [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> While Morgoth's purpose was ever to corrupt or destroy, with Men he also sought by fear and lies to make them the foes of the Eldar, and to then bring them out of the East against Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-ga80_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga80-5">[5]</a></sup>

The forces of the Eldar, meanwhile, strengthened in union. In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [5](/wiki/First_Age_5 "First Age 5") Maedhros was rescued by [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon), thus healing the feud that divided the [house of Fingolfin](/wiki/House_of_Fingolfin "House of Fingolfin") and that of Fëanor,<sup id="cite_ref-return_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-return-3">[3]</a></sup> and in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [7](/wiki/First_Age_7 "First Age 7"), at a [council of the Noldor](/wiki/Council_in_Mithrim "Council in Mithrim"), Fingolfin was chosen as their [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor "High King of the Noldor")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> with the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) subsequently relocating to [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [20](/wiki/First_Age_20 "First Age 20"), Fingolfin held a [great council and high feast](/wiki/Mereth_Aderthad "Mereth Aderthad") to which came many chieftains and representatives from not only the Noldor, but also of the Sindar and even the Green-elves of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand); here many councils were taken in good will and oaths were sworn of league and friendship.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Hearing tidings of the growing union and might of the Eldar, Morgoth returned to Angband from the East, leaving behind but few servants, and those of less might and cunning.<sup id="cite_ref-ga80_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga80-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Battle {#Battle}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Thomas_Rouillard_-_Duel.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/5/5f/Thomas_Rouillard_-_Duel.jpg/300px-Thomas_Rouillard_-_Duel.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="140" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/5/5f/Thomas_Rouillard_-_Duel.jpg/450px-Thomas_Rouillard_-_Duel.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/5/5f/Thomas_Rouillard_-_Duel.jpg/600px-Thomas_Rouillard_-_Duel.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1052" data-file-height="491"/></a><figcaption><i>Duel</i> by <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Thomas_Rouillard" title="Thomas Rouillard">Thomas Rouillard</a></figcaption></figure>

Morgoth, believing the reports of his spies that the lords of the Eldar were wandering abroad with little thought of war, made trial against their strength and watchfulness. In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [60](/wiki/First_Age_60 "First Age 60"), with little warning, there were suddenly earthquakes in the North, and flames erupted from fissures in the earth, and the [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains) vomited flame, and a great army of Orcs poured forth from Angband.<sup id="cite_ref-ga77_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga77-9">[9]</a></sup>

While his main host assaulted [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion),<sup id="cite_ref-qs102_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-qs102-10">[10]</a></sup> another thrust down in the west through the [Vale of Sirion](/wiki/Vale_of_Sirion),<sup id="cite_ref-ga77_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga77-9">[9]</a></sup> and still other forces passed through [Maglor's Gap](/wiki/Maglor%27s_Gap) in the east.<sup id="cite_ref-qs102_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-qs102-10">[10]</a></sup> These Orcs scattered throughout Beleriand,<sup id="cite_ref-ga77_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga77-9">[9]</a></sup> with bands straying abroad far and wide doing great evil.<sup id="cite_ref-qs102_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-qs102-10">[10]</a></sup>

But the Eldar were more prepared than Morgoth thought, and Fingolfin and Maedhros gathered swiftly a great force of both Noldor and Sindar. While others sought out and destroyed the wandering Orcs within Beleriand, Fingolfin and Maedhros themselves fell against and repelled the main host at Dorthonion.<sup id="cite_ref-qs102_10-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-qs102-10">[10]</a></sup> The Orcs were driven back into retreat across the [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen) where Fingolfin and Maedhros pursued them and, even within sight of [Angband's gates](/wiki/Gates_of_Angband "Gates of Angband"), surrounded and destroyed them.<sup id="cite_ref-qs102_10-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-qs102-10">[10]</a></sup>

While this was a great victory for the Eldar, they also took it as a warning, and drew closer their leaguer, setting the Siege of Angband.<sup id="cite_ref-ga78_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga78-11">[11]</a></sup> [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) in the west was held by Fingolfin and Fingon, and East Beleriand south of the [March of Maedhros](/wiki/March_of_Maedhros) by the Sons of Fëanor; the lands between were held by the [House of Finarfin](/wiki/House_of_Finarfin), and in the [Pass of Sirion](/wiki/Pass_of_Sirion), on the island of [Tol Sirion](/wiki/Tol_Sirion), [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) established his [great watchtower](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand)> "Minas Tirith (Beleriand)").<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Such was their watch that Fingolfin boasted, save by treason among themselves, Morgoth could never again escape nor come upon them unawares.<sup id="cite_ref-ga78_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga78-11">[11]</a></sup>

Yet neither could the Noldor take Angband, nor recover the Silmarils, nor even keep Morgoth fully fenced. From his position at [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim), the Iron Mountains curved northward to either side and made for an impassable barrier, and thus in the frozen wilderness to his rear, where the Eldar were restrained from keeping watch, his spies and messengers came and went by devious routes into Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-ga78_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga78-11">[11]</a></sup>

Following his defeat, Morgoth now desired to sow fear and disunion among the Eldar, and he bade the Orcs to take alive any that they could. Thus, some among the Eldar were seized by robber bands in the woods, or in over rash pursuit of their foes, and brought back to Angband where they were put to torment and duress. From them, Morgoth was able to make use of their lore and skills, and wrung the tidings and counsels of his enemies, and learned much of what befell since the [rebellion of the Noldor](/wiki/Rebellion_of_the_Noldor "Rebellion of the Noldor"), and rejoiced in such seeds of dissension. Some of them were even so [daunted by the terror of his eyes](/wiki/Spell_of_Bottomless_Dread "Spell of Bottomless Dread") that they required no further chains, doing his will wherever they might be, and these he released to work treason among their kin; thus, the Elves became afraid of all who claimed escape from Angband.<sup id="cite_ref-ga812_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga812-13">[13]</a></sup>

It was not long before dark tales began to whisper among the Sindar against the Noldor, for Morgoth chose them for the first assault of his malice, and spread evil truth enhanced and poisoned with lies. By [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [67](/wiki/First_Age_67 "First Age 67"), these tales reached Círdan, and he in turn informed Thingol. Thingol, after learning the full truth from [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod), responded by banning the use of [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) within Beleriand, holding those who use it to be [slayers of kin](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB "Kinslaying at Alqualondë") unrepentant.<sup id="cite_ref-ga100107_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga100107-14">[14]</a></sup>

Even during the Siege, war never wholly ceased, as Morgoth would ever make trial and thrusts against his enemies.<sup id="cite_ref-ga78_11-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga78-11">[11]</a></sup> These were, however, restricted mostly to Orc raids and affrays on the north-marches, with the largest and most dangerous being [an attempted assault to the rear of Hithlum](/wiki/Assaults_on_Hithlum#During_the_Siege_of_Angband "Assaults on Hithlum") in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [155](/wiki/First_Age_155 "First Age 155").<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

As the growing strength of the Eldar, before the battle, caused Morgoth to return from the East to Beleriand, his designs to subjugate Men were not wholly achieved.<sup id="cite_ref-ga80_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga80-5">[5]</a></sup> Following the battle, with Morgoth being restrained by the Noldor, his thought was bent to their ruin, and he gave little heed to anything else in Middle-earth. Men were therefore able to wax and multiply, and some among them had converse with the [Dark Elves](/wiki/Avari "Avari") from whom they learned much, including language and rumour of the Blessed Realms in the West. From among these Men came those who repented, and rebelled against the Dark Power, and were in turn hunted and oppressed by those that worshipped it; they thus wondered westwards, fleeing from the darkness which Morgoth had ensnared them.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dagor Aglareb* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Glorious Battle",<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> it contains the elements *[dagor](/wiki/Dagor "Dagor")* ("battle") + *[aglar](/wiki/Aglar "Aglar")* ("glory") + *-eb* (adjective suffix).<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

In old English this battle was called **Hréþgúþ**, with "hréþ" meaning "glory".<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

What would become the Dagor Aglareb is first described in the *"The first version of The Earliest Annals of Beleriand"*, under the year 50. While it still starts with earthquakes, rather than a concentrated assault it is described as Orc-raids, which are driven back in the year 51 with the Siege being then established.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

In *"The second version of the earliest Annals of Beleriand"*, this became a determinate event. Still taking place in the year 51, the driving back of Orc-raids now includes the destruction of an Orc army upon Bladorion (Ard-galen), and it is titled as the "Second Battle" along with being named as "Dagor Aglareb".<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup>

In *"[The Later Annals of Beleriand](/wiki/The_Later_Annals_of_Beleriand)"*, the date goes through an emendation from year 51 to year 60.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup>

In the 1930s *"[Quenta Silmarillion](</wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road)> "Quenta Silmarillion (Lost Road)")"*, the battle takes it's mostly final form. Specific new elements include the Orcs invading Beleriand both through the Pass of Sirion in the west and Maglor's Gap in the east; and of others dealing with the wandering Orcs while Fingolfin and Maedhros confronted the main host in the plains.<sup id="cite_ref-qs102_10-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-qs102-10">[10]</a></sup>

In *"[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)"*, Dagor Aglareb becomes the Third Battle of Beleriand. While an army of Orcs passing through the Vale of Sirion is still included, there is no mention of Orcs passing through Maglor's Gap.<sup id="cite_ref-ga77_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga77-9">[9]</a></sup> The largest additions, however, are not to the battle itself, but the surrounding events of Morgoth himself being in the East and returning just prior to the battle, and of his new orders following it to take Eldar as captives.
