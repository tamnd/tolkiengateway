**Dol Guldur** is an atmospheric black/doom metal band from Taborton, New York. The band takes its name from [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur), the fortress of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in southern [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood). Its 2022 EP *I* includes songs named after [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) and [Anglachel](/wiki/Anglachel).

## Members {#Members}

- Malodor - guitars
- Fågnër - vocals, bass, keyboards
