**Duilin** was one of the two sons of [Duinhir](/wiki/Duinhir), the lord of [Blackroot Vale](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale).

## History {#History}

With his brother [Derufin](/wiki/Derufin), Duilin followed his father to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and there they fought in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields). The Men of the distant valley of [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond) had great skill as bowmen, and so Duilin and Derufin led their archers against the [mûmakil](/wiki/M%C3%BBmakil "Mûmakil"), attempting to shoot out the beasts' eyes. Brave as they were, both the brothers were lost in that battle, trampled by their gigantic foes.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of *Duilin* is unclear. Both in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) and in the later [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) the name meant "swallow",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> but in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) it could be "Singer by the River" or "Spring-Singer", containing the same element *[duin](/wiki/Duin "Duin")* "river" as in the name of his father.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
