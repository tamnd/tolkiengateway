**Didier Willis** ([25 April](/wiki/25_April), [1971](/wiki/1971)), a French IT engineer, is President of Le Dragon de Brume, a non-profit Tolkien association.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Before that, he wrote several articles and studies on Arda in the French <a class="external text" href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/e-zine" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">e-zine</a> *[Hiswelókë](/wiki/Hiswel%C3%B3k%C3%AB)*. Amongs other things, he has compiled the <a class="external text" href="http://www.jrrvf.com/hisweloke/sindar/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Hiswelókë&#39;s Sindarin Dictionary</a> as well as the (now unmaintained) [Dragon Flame](/wiki/Dragon_Flame) application.
