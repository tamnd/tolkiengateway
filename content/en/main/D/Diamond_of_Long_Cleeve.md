**Diamond of Long Cleeve** was a [hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Diamond came from [Long Cleeve](/wiki/Long_Cleeve), a village in the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing). She married [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1427](/wiki/Fourth_Age_6 "Fourth Age 6"), and had one son, [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir_Took_I "Faramir Took I"), named after [Prince](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien "Prince of Ithilien") [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Took_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond" title="wikipedia:Diamond">Diamonds</a></i> are precious jewels<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> The jewel name supports the possibility that she was of a high family, such as the [North-tooks](/wiki/North-took_Family "North-took Family"), descendants of [Bandobras Took](/wiki/Bandobras_Took).
