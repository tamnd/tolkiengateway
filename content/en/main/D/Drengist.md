The **Firth of Drengist**, or just **Drengist**, was a firth in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). The [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md) broke through the [Ered Lómin](../E/Ered_Lómin.md) and ended in the [Cirith Ninniach](../C/Cirith_Ninniach.md). The lands it separated were [Lammoth](../L/Lammoth.md) to the north, and [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Outside the firth was the little-known island of [Ened](../E/Ened.md).

## History {#History}

[Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) and the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) landed at the Firth of Drengist, and their ships were burned at [Losgar](../L/Losgar.md), which was located at the outlet of the bay.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> From there, Fëanor and his host pierced into [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Years later, the first of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s [Assaults on Hithlum](../A/Assaults_on_Hithlum.md) was countered here by [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md), driving the Orcs into the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GA115_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA115-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In an early manuscript by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), *Drengist* is glossed as the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) equivalent of [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *Nearufléot*. The latter name is said by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) to consist of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/nearu" title="wiktionary:nearu">nearu</a></i> ("narrow") + <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/fleet#English" title="wiktionary:fleet">fléot</a></i> ("arm of the sea, estuary, firth").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The Elvish etymology of the name *Drengist* itself, however, appears to have been nowhere explained by Tolkien.
