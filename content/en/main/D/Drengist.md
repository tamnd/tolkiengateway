The **Firth of Drengist**, or just **Drengist**, was a firth in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). The [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) broke through the [Ered Lómin](/wiki/Ered_L%C3%B3min) and ended in the [Cirith Ninniach](/wiki/Cirith_Ninniach). The lands it separated were [Lammoth](/wiki/Lammoth) to the north, and [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast) to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Outside the firth was the little-known island of [Ened](/wiki/Ened).

## History {#History}

[Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) and the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) landed at the Firth of Drengist, and their ships were burned at [Losgar](/wiki/Losgar), which was located at the outlet of the bay.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> From there, Fëanor and his host pierced into [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Years later, the first of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s [Assaults on Hithlum](/wiki/Assaults_on_Hithlum) was countered here by [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), driving the Orcs into the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GA115_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA115-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In an early manuscript by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), *Drengist* is glossed as the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) equivalent of [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *Nearufléot*. The latter name is said by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) to consist of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/nearu" title="wiktionary:nearu">nearu</a></i> ("narrow") + <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/fleet#English" title="wiktionary:fleet">fléot</a></i> ("arm of the sea, estuary, firth").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The Elvish etymology of the name *Drengist* itself, however, appears to have been nowhere explained by Tolkien.
