**Dagorlad** was the dusty plain that lay to the northwest of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), immediately below the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) of the Morannon.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This was the site of several important battles in [Gondor's](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") history, most notably the defeat of [Sauron's](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron") forces in the famous [Battle of Dagorlad](/wiki/Battle_of_Dagorlad) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> King [Calimehtar](</wiki/Calimehtar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Calimehtar (King of Gondor)")'s victory over the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1899](/wiki/Third_Age_1899 "Third Age 1899"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and the [Battle of the Morannon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon) on [25 March](/wiki/25_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
