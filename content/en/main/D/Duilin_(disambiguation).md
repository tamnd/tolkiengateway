**Duilin** can refer to:

- [Duilin (son of Duinhir)](<Duilin_(son_of_Duinhir).md>), a [Gondorian](../G/Gondorians.md) soldier who lived during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) and died in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).
- [Duilin (elf of Gondolin)](<Duilin_(elf_of_Gondolin).md>), a lord who led the [House of the Swallow](../H/House_of_the_Swallow.md) in the [Lost Tale](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md) of "[The Fall of Gondolin](<../T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md>)".
- [Duilin](<Duilin_(father_of_Saelon).md>), the father of [Saelon](../S/Saelon.md), a character from the *[The New Shadow](../T/The_New_Shadow.md)*.
- The [nightingale](../N/Nightingales.md), or "dusk-singer", which is *duilinn* in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).
