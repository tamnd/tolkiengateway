**Duilin** can refer to:

- [Duilin (son of Duinhir)](</wiki/Duilin_(son_of_Duinhir)>), a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") soldier who lived during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) and died in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).
- [Duilin (elf of Gondolin)](</wiki/Duilin_(elf_of_Gondolin)>), a lord who led the [House of the Swallow](/wiki/House_of_the_Swallow) in the [Lost Tale](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales "The Book of Lost Tales") of "[The Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")".
- [Duilin](</wiki/Duilin_(father_of_Saelon)> "Duilin (father of Saelon)"), the father of [Saelon](/wiki/Saelon), a character from the *[The New Shadow](/wiki/The_New_Shadow)*.
- The [nightingale](/wiki/Nightingales "Nightingales"), or "dusk-singer", which is *duilinn* in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).
