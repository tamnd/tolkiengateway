**Dernhelm** was the alias taken by [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) when she broke the command of her uncle [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md). As Dernhelm she befriended [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) who was under the service of Théoden, and failed to recognize her. Together they rode [Windfola](../W/Windfola.md) to the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was as Dernhelm that she first challenged the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "hidden protector" in [Old English](../O/Old_English.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> (see [Helm](../H/Helm.md)).

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1980: <a href="../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)"><i>The Return of the King</i> (1980 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Éowyn does not make use of the name, only dressing like a man. Her hair is tucked underneath an open-faced helmet, which she removes while confronting the Witch-king.</dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Here Éowyn does introduce herself as Dernhelm, and is stated to wear a helmet with a visor that obscures her face.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the movie, the name is not used, but Éowyn&#39;s helmet partly covers her face. As one of the riders grasps Merry to put him on the horse as they ride for the battle, Merry smiles, immediately recognizing Éowyn from the rider&#39;s voice.</dd></dl>
