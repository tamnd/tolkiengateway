**Deer** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) *[Aras](../A/Aras.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) were graceful creatures found throughout [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Deer were loved by the [Vala](../V/Valar.md) [Nessa](../N/Nessa.md), and were said to follow her as she travelled through wild lands.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) the [Teiglin](../T/Teiglin.md) River flowed through a gorge west of the forest of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> At the narrowest part of the gorge was *[Cabed-en-Aras](../C/Cabed-en-Aras.md)*, "The Deer's Leap", where once a deer leaped across the river to escape a huntsman of [Haleth](../H/Haleth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) were in the process of crossing the [Enchanted River](../E/Enchanted_River.md) when a dark-coloured deer bowled into the Dwarves. Although [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) shot the hart with an arrow as it leaped there was no recovering the venison for the boat they were using floated away. Worse, the commotion caused [Bombur](../B/Bombur.md) to fall into the water, which caused him to fall asleep for days after he was rescued. Soon after, a [white deer](../W/White_deer.md) and her snowy fawns appeared on the path. The dwarves shot arrows at them but none hit and the deer vanished.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), the adjective <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ross&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ross (page does not exist)">ross</a></i> was often used to describe the copper colour of red deer.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

The Sindarin term <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Rhovan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rhovan (page does not exist)">rhovan</a></i> ("wild")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-8">[8]</a></sup> referred to large beasts, especially the great red deer who lived in the Vales of Anduin and western Mirkwood.<sup id="cite_ref-Rhoven_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rhoven-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the Extended Edition of the film, while the hobbits camp at the edge of the <a href="../M/Midgewater_Marshes.md" title="Midgewater Marshes">Midgewater Marshes</a>, Aragorn leaves them for a while and returns at night, carrying a small deer he had hunted and caught. The dead deer used for filming the scene was bought from a New Zealand farm raising fallow deer as livestock for meat.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Deer are found in many places of Middle-earth. They are typically not aggressive, but some will fight if the player threatens them. Weaker deer will run away when attacked.</dd></dl>
