**Deer** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *[Aras](/wiki/Aras)*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) were graceful creatures found throughout [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Deer were loved by the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Nessa](/wiki/Nessa), and were said to follow her as she travelled through wild lands.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) the [Teiglin](/wiki/Teiglin) River flowed through a gorge west of the forest of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> At the narrowest part of the gorge was *[Cabed-en-Aras](/wiki/Cabed-en-Aras)*, "The Deer's Leap", where once a deer leaped across the river to escape a huntsman of [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) were in the process of crossing the [Enchanted River](/wiki/Enchanted_River) when a dark-coloured deer bowled into the Dwarves. Although [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) shot the hart with an arrow as it leaped there was no recovering the venison for the boat they were using floated away. Worse, the commotion caused [Bombur](/wiki/Bombur) to fall into the water, which caused him to fall asleep for days after he was rescued. Soon after, a [white deer](/wiki/White_deer "White deer") and her snowy fawns appeared on the path. The dwarves shot arrows at them but none hit and the deer vanished.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), the adjective <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ross&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ross (page does not exist)">ross</a></i> was often used to describe the copper colour of red deer.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

The Sindarin term <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rhovan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rhovan (page does not exist)">rhovan</a></i> ("wild")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-8">[8]</a></sup> referred to large beasts, especially the great red deer who lived in the Vales of Anduin and western Mirkwood.<sup id="cite_ref-Rhoven_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rhoven-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the Extended Edition of the film, while the hobbits camp at the edge of the <a href="/wiki/Midgewater_Marshes" title="Midgewater Marshes">Midgewater Marshes</a>, Aragorn leaves them for a while and returns at night, carrying a small deer he had hunted and caught. The dead deer used for filming the scene was bought from a New Zealand farm raising fallow deer as livestock for meat.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Deer are found in many places of Middle-earth. They are typically not aggressive, but some will fight if the player threatens them. Weaker deer will run away when attacked.</dd></dl>
