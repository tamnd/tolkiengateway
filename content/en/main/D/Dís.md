**Dís** (born [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [2760](/wiki/Third_Age_2760 "Third Age 2760")) was a [female](/wiki/Dwarf-women "Dwarf-women") [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") of the royal line of [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk).

## History {#History}

Dís was the daughter of King [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in "Thráin") and younger sister of [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) and [Frerin](/wiki/Frerin). Like her brothers, she was born in [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) and was driven into exile in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770") when the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) sacked the kingdom. She was the mother of [Fíli](/wiki/F%C3%ADli) and [Kíli](/wiki/K%C3%ADli).

Although female Dwarves were rarely seen by outsiders, or mentioned in Dwarven records, Dís was an exceptional case: her sons were killed heroically defending their mortally wounded uncle Thorin in the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) has suggested that *Dís* is derived from [Old Norse](/wiki/Old_Norse) *-dís* ("sister") found in many Nordic names (such as *Herdís*, *Hjördís*, etc).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<b>2018: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Although very old, Dís is still alive during the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>. She holds the honorary position of the Keeper of <a href="/wiki/Ravenhill" title="Ravenhill">Ravenhill</a>, where she tends to ravens now led by Arcah, Daughter of Croär. After the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Dale" title="Battle of Dale">Battle of Dale</a> she and the surviving companions of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Thorin_Oakenshield" title="Thorin Oakenshield">Thorin Oakenshield</a> pay respect to tombs of her brother and sons, which are now next to that of King <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Dain_Ironfoot" title="Dain Ironfoot">Dain Ironfoot</a>.</dd></dl>
