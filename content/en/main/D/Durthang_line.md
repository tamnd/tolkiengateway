The **Durthang line** was a group of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) that dwelled at the [Orc-hold](/wiki/Orc-holds "Orc-holds") of [Durthang](/wiki/Durthang) in the northern [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) before being called to fight at the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon) in the valley of [Udûn](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(valley)> "Udûn (valley)") during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It is possible that "the road-builders of old" who made the road that led to the [Isenmouthe](/wiki/Carach_Angren "Carach Angren") in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) built Durthang and dwelled there.<sup id="cite_ref-built_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-built-2">[2]</a></sup> It is possible that Durthang may have been originally inhabited by [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians).<sup id="cite_ref-sauron_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sauron-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) inhabited several fortresses overlooking the entrance to Mordor in the aftermath of the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) like the [Towers of the Teeth](/wiki/Towers_of_the_Teeth)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> After the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) devastated Gondor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> the watch on Mordor was neglected until, by [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1640](/wiki/Third_Age_1640 "Third Age 1640"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> "the fortresses that guarded the passes were unmanned" because of a lack of troops.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> It is possible that this included Durthang.<sup id="cite_ref-sauron_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sauron-3">[3]</a></sup> It is possible that Durthang was later reoccupied and manned by troops from Gondor,<sup id="cite_ref-sauron_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sauron-3">[3]</a></sup> because in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944") when king [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) of Gondor and both his sons fell in a battle "north of the [Morannon](/wiki/Black_Gate "Black Gate")"<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> by the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> the Morannon and the Towers of the Teeth were still manned by troops from Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1980](/wiki/Third_Age_1980 "Third Age 1980") when the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) came to Mordor and gathered the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) there,<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> it is possible that Orcs<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-1">[1]</a></sup> occupied Durthang.<sup id="cite_ref-sauron_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sauron-3">[3]</a></sup> At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) Durthang was an [Orc-hold](/wiki/Orc-holds "Orc-holds").<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-1">[1]</a></sup>

On Sunday,<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> [18 March](/wiki/18_March) of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-march_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-march-14">[14]</a></sup> [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) caught a glimpse of Durthang when they came to the road that led away from "that orc-tower" to the Isenmouthe.<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-1">[1]</a></sup> After resting "under overhanging stone in sight of Durthang"<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> until that evening,<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> Frodo and Sam began to head down the road, which they found was not very broad.<sup id="cite_ref-begin_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-begin-17">[17]</a></sup> After traveling for twelve miles and deciding to take a rest for a few minutes,<sup id="cite_ref-begin_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-begin-17">[17]</a></sup> a company of Orcs "going at a great pace" from Durthang<sup id="cite_ref-journey_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-journey-18">[18]</a></sup> passed them by.<sup id="cite_ref-built_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-built-2">[2]</a></sup> The Orcs in the front of the line "bore torches" and "were a gang of smaller breeds" who only wanted to finish the march and "escape the whip", being forced to fight for Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-built_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-built-2">[2]</a></sup> To prevent the company from seeing their feet, Sam and Frodo lowered their orc-shields.<sup id="cite_ref-built_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-built-2">[2]</a></sup> When one of the two "large fierce *uruks*" that lead them mistook the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) for deserters from the garrison at [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol), the [slave-driver](/wiki/Slave-drivers "Slave-drivers") forced them to march along with the company to the Black Gate.<sup id="cite_ref-built_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-built-2">[2]</a></sup> In the "early hours"<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> "before dawn"<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> on [19 March](/wiki/19_March),<sup id="cite_ref-march_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-march-14">[14]</a></sup> after marching for "some miles" eastward near [Udûn](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(valley)> "Udûn (valley)") in Mordor, they came to a "road-meeting" where "several companies came together" and clashed, each trying to get to the Black Gate first.<sup id="cite_ref-journey_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-journey-18">[18]</a></sup> When "a troop of heavy-armed *uruks* from Barad-dûr charged into the Durthang line", Sam saw the chance for him and Frodo to slip away in the confusion.<sup id="cite_ref-journey_18-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-journey-18">[18]</a></sup> It is unknown what became of the Durthang line.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[Sauron Defeated](/wiki/Sauron_Defeated)*, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) noted that, apart from "minor details", the Orc-band from Durthang that Frodo and Sam encounter at the end of "[The Land of Shadow](/wiki/The_Land_of_Shadow)" chapter remain much the same in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s original draft.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> In this draft, the two "large fierce *uruks*" are further specified as "the fighting-orcs", which Christopher Tolkien suggested might have been the first usage of the word *uruk* (not *Uruk-hai*).<sup id="cite_ref-notes_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-notes-22">[22]</a></sup> In draft notes on the chronology, Tolkien originally wrote that Frodo and Sam saw Durthang on [17 March](/wiki/17_March) and took "the road from Durthang" on the "night of March 17-18" before joining "the orc-company".<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup> Tolkien changed this to that of the published version.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

On [17 October](/wiki/17_October) of [2005](/wiki/2005), [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) deduced in *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_A_Reader%27s_Companion)* that Sam and Frodo having to lower their shields to prevent the Orcs from seeing their feet heavily implies that Sam "did not bring any footwear to Frodo in Cirith Ungol" because Hobbits usually don't have footwear.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup> Hammond and Scull also compare Sam's experience with the Orcs to the effort put on a "typical foot soldier" in an army as well as how, during the [First World War](/wiki/World_War_I "World War I"), Tolkien had a certain distance to cover in "lengthy marches" despite not being expected "to carry his kit" at such times because he was an officer.<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[26]</a></sup>

Ugrukhôr let the Thandrim leave Mordor, only to have archers slay them all as soon as they passed the Black Gate, save one he spared to report these events to Gondor: Halannon the Survivor.</dd></dl>
