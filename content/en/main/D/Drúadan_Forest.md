**Drúadan Forest** was in the fief of [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien), in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), lying north of the eastern end of the [Ered Nimrais](/wiki/White_Mountains "White Mountains"). Located near or in the Forest were three of [Gondor's warning beacons](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor"): To the west was [Nardol](/wiki/Nardol), within the woods was [Eilenach](/wiki/Eilenach), and just east of the woods was [Amon Dîn](/wiki/Amon_D%C3%AEn). Skirting the northern edge of Drúadan Forest was the great road that connected Gondor to [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), while within the southern part of the forest was the long-abandoned road through [Stonewain Valley](/wiki/Stonewain_Valley).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was avoided by the people of Gondor and [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), who believed it was haunted: in reality the forest was home to a small enclave of [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain), or *Woses*, who only survived in these woods and in the [Drúwaith Iaur](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAwaith_Iaur).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) were on their way to support [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), and a host of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) was found by scouts three miles west of Amon Dîn upon the [Great West Road](/wiki/Great_West_Road) and moving toward [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden)'s forces. A leader of the Woses, [Ghân-buri-Ghân](/wiki/Gh%C3%A2n-buri-Gh%C3%A2n) came forward and offered to lead the Riders by secret paths through the forest so they could avoid the Orcs. On [13 March](/wiki/13_March)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the Woses led the Rider of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) across the forgotten road down [Stonewain Valley](/wiki/Stonewain_Valley) and [the next day](/wiki/14_March "14 March") they reached the [Grey Wood](/wiki/Grey_Wood), and were able to come to [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields) undetected.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In exchange for this help the forest was later given to the Drúedain as a protected enclave inside the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom) of Gondor and Arnor by [King Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Drúadan* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. It means "Wose" or "wild man".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> It is a compound of *[drû](/wiki/Dr%C3%BB "Drû")* ("Wose") and *[adan](/wiki/Adan "Adan")* ("Man (as a race)".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
