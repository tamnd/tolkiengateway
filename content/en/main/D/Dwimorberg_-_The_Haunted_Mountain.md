**Dwimorberg - The Haunted Mountain** is the twenty-sixth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* and the thirty-third scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)"). It was extended in the latter edition.

## Differences {#Differences}

- The line *"The very warmth of my blood seems stolen away,"* spoken by Gimli in this scene is taken from a different context. In the books, it is [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond) who says this in a conversation with [Pippin](/wiki/Pippin); this occurs after they each hear the faint cry of a [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) while discussing the imminent threat of the destruction of Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
