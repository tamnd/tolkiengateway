**Durin IV** was a King of [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk) who ruled the great [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") city of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"), and the fourth king to have the name Durin. Like his father he bore a [Ring of Power](/wiki/Ring_of_Thr%C3%B3r "Ring of Thrór").<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Like all Durins after <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin I</a>, he was given the name of the first of the [Seven Fathers of the Dwarves](/wiki/Seven_Fathers_of_the_Dwarves) because he greatly resembled him in both appearance and manner. Indeed it was believed among the Dwarves that he was the reincarnation of Durin I, though whether this is possible is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

One account states that the name *Durin* was a word for "king" in the language of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Men_of_the_North" title="Men of the North">Men of the North</a> in the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-relations_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-relations-2">[2]</a></sup>

Another account states that *Durinn* is one of the Dwarfs in the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. The name means "Sleepy".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Durin IV was the reigning King of the Longbeards during the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance" title="War of the Last Alliance">War of the Last Alliance</a>. He and the &#34;Thíuda&#34;, the <a href="/wiki/Northmen" title="Northmen">Northmen</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">Second Age</a>, were tasked with defeating a mighty horde of <a href="/wiki/Easterlings" title="Easterlings">Easterlings</a>. They were defeated and retreated to rejoin the rest of the Alliance on the besieged plain of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Gorgoroth" title="Gorgoroth">Gorgoroth</a>.</dd></dl>
