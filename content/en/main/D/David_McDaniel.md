**David Edward McDaniel** ([16 June](../_/16_June.md) [1939](../_/1939.md) – [1 November](../_/1_November.md) [1977](../_/1977.md)) known by pseudonym **Ted Johnstone**, was a United States science fiction author, that founded and organized the first Tolkien fan group, "[The Fellowship of the Ring](<../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(fangroup).md>)" at Pittcon, the 1960 Worldcon. They published four issues of the fanzine [i-Palantir](../I/I-Palantir.md) before the organization disbanded.
