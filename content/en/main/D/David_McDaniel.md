**David Edward McDaniel** ([16 June](/wiki/16_June) [1939](/wiki/1939) – [1 November](/wiki/1_November) [1977](/wiki/1977)) known by pseudonym **Ted Johnstone**, was a United States science fiction author, that founded and organized the first Tolkien fan group, "[The Fellowship of the Ring](</wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(fangroup)> "The Fellowship of the Ring (fangroup)")" at Pittcon, the 1960 Worldcon. They published four issues of the fanzine [i-Palantir](/wiki/I-Palantir "I-Palantir") before the organization disbanded.
