In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), **Dûshera** was a land in the southwest of Middle-earth south of the central [Yellow Mountains](/wiki/Yellow_Mountains) between the Bay of Ûsakan to the west and the river Koros to the east.<sup id="cite_ref-Dûshera_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dûshera-1">[1]</a></sup>

Dûshera was a warm, wet, hilly land that was criss-crossed by streams and punctuated with lakes and swamps.<sup id="cite_ref-Dûshera_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dûshera-1">[1]</a></sup>

Dûshera was a sparsely settled land<sup id="cite_ref-Ardor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ardor-2">[2]</a></sup> that was inhabited by various peoples, most notably Mûmakanin-speaking fisherfolk along the coasts and Chailuk-speaking [Woses](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain "Drúedain") in the remote hills with the Nan Koros dominated by Mûmakani<sup id="cite_ref-Dûshera_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dûshera-1">[1]</a></sup>. Men with a ruddy complexion lived near the perimeter of the Pel Dûshera, a lake in the northwestern part of Dûshera, and in small independent hill towns along the base of the low mountains that flank the area. The inhabitants of the towns grew corn and wheat with some having been able to export some wheat to Elves and Hathorians in the south to river traders who were by majority Elves or Kirani.<sup id="cite_ref-Ardor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ardor-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the mid [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) the borders of Dûshera were near the western edge of the rainforest in the west, the river Koros in the east and in the south along the hills that ran from the rainforest in the southwest almost to the swamp of Geeshaan in the southeast.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Dûshera seems to have also been inhabited by [Black Númenóreans](/wiki/Black_N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) and seems to have included a kingdom that was ruled by Black Númenóreans.<sup id="cite_ref-Kingdom_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kingdom-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1327&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1327 (page does not exist)">1327</a>, Uthcû, a Black Númenórean was born as the heir to the throne of a kingdom as the oldest son of Drecca, a Black Númenórean Prince in Dûshera. After having been informed by his arms instructor that his younger brother Jenthar had discovered that Uthcû had bribed his arms instructor to avoid unreasonable amounts of time in the training yard and that Jenthar intended to inform their father, the sixteen year old Uthcû killed his arms instructor and Jenthar with a dagger, but had to flee by horse when one of his father's guards saw him standing over the still form of his brother with a bloody dagger in his hand.<sup id="cite_ref-Kingdom_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kingdom-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1357&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1357 (page does not exist)">1357</a> Uthcû left the bejewelled courts of Sarûl, the capital of the Black Númenórean realm of [Tantûrak](/wiki/Tant%C3%BBrak) to join the [Army of the Southern Dragon](/wiki/Army_of_the_Southern_Dragon) of the [Ringwraith](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") [Akhôrahil](/wiki/Fifth_of_the_Nazg%C3%BBl "Fifth of the Nazgûl"). He quickly rose through the ranks and had become a general by [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1367&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1367 (page does not exist)">1367</a><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1641&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1641 (page does not exist)">1641</a> he received a [lesser ring of power](/wiki/Lesser_rings "Lesser rings") that prolonged his live<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> from Akhôrahil<sup id="cite_ref-TA1641_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1641-7">[7]</a></sup>. After that,<sup id="cite_ref-TA1641_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1641-7">[7]</a></sup> Akhôrahil returned to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) at a request from [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>. The Army of the Soutnern Dragon led by Uthcû conquered Tulwang in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1657&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1657 (page does not exist)">1657</a> and Ciryatandor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1802&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1802 (page does not exist)">1802</a> where Uthcû used Barad Carannûn as his capital. Ten years later, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1812&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1812 (page does not exist)">1812</a> Uthcû broke his ties with his liege Akhôrahil, but was killed three years later in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1815&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1815 (page does not exist)">1815</a> when Sangarunya, a general loyal to Akhôrahil, led the eastern part of the Army of the Soutern dragon in a surprise attack on Barad Carannûn.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
