**Denethor** was the son of [Lenwë](../L/Lenwë.md), the original leader of the [Nandor](../N/Nandor.md).

## History {#History}

Daunted by the towering peaks of the [Hithaeglir](../H/Hithaeglir.md), Lenwë, his son Denethor, and other [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) forsook the [Great March](../G/Great_March.md) of the Elves to the [West](../V/Valinor.md) and passed southward down the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md). Much later Denethor would lead a part of those people, the Nandor, west into [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> centuries before the first rising of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md), the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and other foul creatures of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) began to harass the Nandor east of the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md). Hearing of the power and protection of the Elf King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md), Denethor led many of his people over the Blue Mountains into Beleriand. They settled in [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md) and Denethor became their king. They were called the *[Laiquendi](../L/Laiquendi.md)* ([Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "Green-elves").

Years later, in [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1497](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1497.md), [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) loosed an army on Beleriand, beginning the [First Battle](../F/First_Battle_of_Beleriand.md) in the [Wars of Beleriand](../B/Battles_of_Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The lightly-armed Laiquendi were driven back to the great hill of [Amon Ereb](../A/Amon_Ereb.md), where Denethor was slain.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> While the Laiquendi survived, they became a reclusive people, seldom taking up arms, and never again naming a king.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In an explanation of the name of [Denethor II](Denethor.md#Etymology) (who was named after this elf), the Steward of Gondor, it is stated that *Denethor* is [Nandorin](../N/Nandorin.md) fit into the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) sound range, and is said to mean "Lithe and lank".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the earlier *[The Lost Road](../T/The_Lost_Road.md)*, the name Denethor is said to contain the word element *Dani*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> According to *[The Lhammas](../T/The_Lhammas.md)*, it derives from [Primitive Elvish](../P/Primitive_Elvish.md) *Ndani-thārō*, meaning "Saviour of the Dani", as he was the one who led them to the West. Other derivatives of this name are: *Daintáro* in [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md), *Nanisáro* in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), *Dainthor* in [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) and *Denipor* in [Doriathrin](Doriathrin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
