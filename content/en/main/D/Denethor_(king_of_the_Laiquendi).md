**Denethor** was the son of [Lenwë](/wiki/Lenw%C3%AB), the original leader of the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor).

## History {#History}

Daunted by the towering peaks of the [Hithaeglir](/wiki/Hithaeglir), Lenwë, his son Denethor, and other [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) forsook the [Great March](/wiki/Great_March) of the Elves to the [West](/wiki/Valinor "Valinor") and passed southward down the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). Much later Denethor would lead a part of those people, the Nandor, west into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> centuries before the first rising of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun), the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and other foul creatures of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) began to harass the Nandor east of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains). Hearing of the power and protection of the Elf King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol), Denethor led many of his people over the Blue Mountains into Beleriand. They settled in [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand) and Denethor became their king. They were called the *[Laiquendi](/wiki/Laiquendi)* ([Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Green-elves").

Years later, in [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1497](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1497 "Year of the Trees 1497"), [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) loosed an army on Beleriand, beginning the [First Battle](/wiki/First_Battle_of_Beleriand "First Battle of Beleriand") in the [Wars of Beleriand](/wiki/Battles_of_Beleriand "Battles of Beleriand").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The lightly-armed Laiquendi were driven back to the great hill of [Amon Ereb](/wiki/Amon_Ereb), where Denethor was slain.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> While the Laiquendi survived, they became a reclusive people, seldom taking up arms, and never again naming a king.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In an explanation of the name of [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor#Etymology "Denethor") (who was named after this elf), the Steward of Gondor, it is stated that *Denethor* is [Nandorin](/wiki/Nandorin) fit into the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) sound range, and is said to mean "Lithe and lank".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the earlier *[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)*, the name Denethor is said to contain the word element *Dani*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> According to *[The Lhammas](/wiki/The_Lhammas)*, it derives from [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish) *Ndani-thārō*, meaning "Saviour of the Dani", as he was the one who led them to the West. Other derivatives of this name are: *Daintáro* in [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin), *Nanisáro* in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), *Dainthor* in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) and *Denipor* in [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
