**Desk certificate 27 July 1972** refers to a certificate of authenticity written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) on [27 July](/wiki/27_July) [1972](/wiki/1972) for the [sale at Sotheby's](/wiki/Sotheby%27s_Fine_English_and_Continental_Furniture_Bronzes,_Textiles_and_Works_of_Art_1825-1930_and_French_Animalier_Bronzes "Sotheby's Fine English and Continental Furniture Bronzes, Textiles and Works of Art 1825-1930 and French Animalier Bronzes") of his personal desk. The statement gives a brief history of the desk, stating that *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and some of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* were written on it, and notes that the desk has been presented to *Help the Aged* in memory of [Edith Tolkien](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien) (who gave the desk to Tolkien in [1927](/wiki/1927)).<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The manuscript is held at the J.R.R. Tolkien Manuscripts collections at the [Marion E. Wade Center](/wiki/Marion_E._Wade_Center) (where also the desk is now found).<sup id="cite_ref-C_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-C-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
