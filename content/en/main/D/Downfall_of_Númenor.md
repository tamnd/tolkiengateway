The **Downfall of Númenor**, also called the **Fall** or **Drowning of Númenor**, was the utter destruction of the island of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), a divine punishment by [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md) to the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) for breaking the [Ban of the Valar](../B/Ban_of_the_Valar.md). [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md), the [king of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md), was coerced by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) to assault the [Undying Lands](../U/Undying_Lands.md) which ultimately led to the island's destruction and death of most of its people in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3319](../S/Second_Age_3319.md).

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

During the early years of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), [Morgoth's](../M/Morgoth.md) greatest servant [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), had grown mighty in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), ruling through terror, force, and persuasion, Sauron dominated the peoples of Middle-earth in the south and east. After the forging of the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md) Sauron launched an invasion of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and [destroyed Eregion](../S/Sack_of_Eregion.md). [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) dispatched an immense military force to Middle-earth to aid the [Elves of Lindon](../E/Elves_of_Lindon.md) and after a period of heavy fighting, [defeated](../B/Battle_of_the_Gwathló.md) Sauron and his hosts.<sup id="cite_ref-akallabeth_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup>

Sauron returned to Mordor to rebuild his strength and having tasted the power of conquest and victory in arms, the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) began establishing settlements along the coasts and oppressed the Men of Middle-earth. Many began to speak openly of their envy of the Eldar's immortality and in defiance of the [Ban](../B/Ban_of_the_Valar.md). It appeared to the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) that the shadow of Morgoth had returned to plague their hearts. Emissaries of the Valar came to Númenor, seeking to remind them that death was not given as a punishment but the people of Númenor did not heed their words and later became split into two factions; the [King's Men](../K/King's_Men.md), those loyal to the King and prone to oppose the restraints upon Númenor, and the [Faithful](../F/Faithful.md), those who remained true to their friendship with the Eldar and loyalty to the Valar. As time went on, Númenor continued to grow in wealth and might, yet somehow its people's bliss was diminished.<sup id="cite_ref-akallabeth_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Adviser to the King {#Adviser_to_the_King}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Turner_Mohan_-_The_King&#39;s_Councelor.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/6a/Turner_Mohan_-_The_King%27s_Councelor.jpg/300px-Turner_Mohan_-_The_King%27s_Councelor.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="398" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/6a/Turner_Mohan_-_The_King%27s_Councelor.jpg/450px-Turner_Mohan_-_The_King%27s_Councelor.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/6a/Turner_Mohan_-_The_King%27s_Councelor.jpg/600px-Turner_Mohan_-_The_King%27s_Councelor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="900" data-file-height="1195"/></a><figcaption><i>The King&#39;s Councillor</i> by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Turner_Mohan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Turner Mohan (page does not exist)">Turner Mohan</a></figcaption></figure>

After returning to the height of his power, Sauron began attacking Númenórean cities by the coasts and took on the titles '**Lord of the Earth'** and '**King of Men'**. [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md) the King, received word of his rise in power angered by Sauron's pride the Númenóreans swiftly came once more to Middle-earth with a great military force to confront Sauron a final time. So mighty were they that Sauron's forces fled from them with barely a fight. Sauron realising he could not defeat the Númenóreans militarily allowed himself to be captured, and begged falsely for mercy. The Númenóreans brought him back as a prisoner.<sup id="cite_ref-akallabeth_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup>

Through Sauron's cunning and persuasion he rose from prisoner to adviser for the King. Convincing many officials to worship Morgoth, they quickly became corrupted to his side. Under Sauron's influence the Númenóreans prospered but became more nefarious, the Men of Middle-earth were hunted and enslaved and brought back to their temples to be slain cruelly. The White tree [Nimloth](<../N/Nimloth_(tree).md>) was cut down and its wood was used to light the first fire on the altar of a mighty temple built in [Armenelos](../A/Armenelos.md). Recognising the corruption of the council [Amandil](../A/Amandil.md), the [lord of Andúnië](../L/Lords_of_Andúnië.md), withdrew to [Rómenna](../R/Rómenna.md) summoning more of the [Faithful](../F/Faithful.md) to follow him.<sup id="cite_ref-akallabeth_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup>

The shadow of death soon loomed over Ar-Pharazôn and fearing death and old age the king was prompted by Sauron to make war upon the Valar, seize the Undying Lands and take immortality by force and so preparations began in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3310](../S/Second_Age_3310.md) of a great armada for the invasion.<sup id="cite_ref-APSA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-APSA-3">[3]</a></sup> Amandil was aware of the king's machinations and called his son [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) revealing his plan to sail west in secret, imitating their forefather [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md), and seek mercy from the Valar for what was to come, as Amandil perceived there was no turning back from the Númenórean's course of war and anticipated their ruin. Amandil then departed along with three servants he held close, sailing east then going about towards the west and was never seen or heard of again. Following the advice of his father Elendil prepared for the ruin of Númenor, gathering more of the Faithful and prepared their ships that were anchored in the east coast, storing their heirlooms and goods.<sup id="cite_ref-akallabeth_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Drowning of Númenor {#Drowning_of_Númenor}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/J/John_Howe_-_The_Drowning_of_Numenor.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/82/John_Howe_-_The_Drowning_of_Numenor.jpg/300px-John_Howe_-_The_Drowning_of_Numenor.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="299" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/82/John_Howe_-_The_Drowning_of_Numenor.jpg/450px-John_Howe_-_The_Drowning_of_Numenor.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/8/82/John_Howe_-_The_Drowning_of_Numenor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="534" data-file-height="533"/></a><figcaption><i>The Drowning of Númenor</i> by <a href="../J/John_Howe.md" title="John Howe">John Howe</a></figcaption></figure>

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a href="../C/Changing_of_the_World.md" title="Changing of the World">Changing of the World</a></div></div>

During these days the weather in Númenor grew restless, the skies darkened, and the storms wrecked the ships of the Númenóreans. Then a great cloud shaped as an eagle loomed over the island and beneath its wings lightning struck the land. Few of the Númenóreans repented but many defied the warning and Sauron stood in the Temple and was unscathed from the lightning. In [3319](../S/Second_Age_3319.md), Ar-Pharazôn's armada was complete,<sup id="cite_ref-APSA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-APSA-3">[3]</a></sup> known as the [Great Armament](../G/Great_Armament.md), Ar-Pharazôn boarded his flagship [Alcarondas](../A/Alcarondas.md) ahead of his fleet and departed for [Aman](../A/Aman.md). Sauron remained behind. The fleet finally arrived on the shores of Aman and Ar-Pharazôn's host made their camp about [Túna](../T/Túna.md).<sup id="cite_ref-akallabeth_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Valar feared that the Númenorean army could wreak havoc in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> but they were forbidden from killing or otherwise using force against Men.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md), chief of the Valar, thus called upon [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md), who opened a massive chasm in the sea between Númenor and Aman which swallowed the ships in the Great Armament of Ar-Pharazôn. Ar-Pharazon himself, along with his host that had landed on Aman, was 'buried under falling hills' and would remain in the [Caves of the Forgotten](../C/Caves_of_the_Forgotten.md) until the [Last Battle](../L/Last_Battle.md). Númenor was covered by great waves and sank into the abyss, killing its inhabitants, including the body of Sauron, which robbed him his ability to assume fair and charming forms. Ilúvatar broke and [changed the world](../C/Changing_of_the_World.md), changing [Arda](../A/Arda.md)'s shape from flat to round and taking [Aman](../A/Aman.md) and [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) from the [Circles of the World](../C/Circles_of_the_World.md) forever, so that mortal sailors would not reach the Undying Lands again.<sup id="cite_ref-akallabeth_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

The Faithful managed to escape the catastrophe, led by Elendil and his two sons [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md) they fled Númenor on nine ships. Among their possessions were the [Palantíri](../P/Palantíri.md), the [Ring of Barahir](../R/Ring_of_Barahir.md), a [silver rod](../S/Sceptre_of_Annúminas.md), [Narsil](../N/Narsil.md) and a scion of Nimloth which grew from a fruit Isildur saved before the tree was cut down. They landed in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), but due to the upheaval caused by the destruction of Númenor their ships were separated, Elendil landed in Lindon while Isildur and Anárion were borne to the south. The followers of Elendil united the colonists who were in the settlements of those lands among the [Middle Men](../M/Middle_Men.md) and established two kingdoms which came to be known as the [Realms in Exile](../R/Realms_in_Exile.md): [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) in the south, and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) in the north.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-7">[7]</a></sup> The few Númenóreans endeavoured to preserve the culture of Númenor in their new kingdoms, [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) the language of Númenor, was used in the realms (even before the Downfall) and later it evolved into [Westron](../W/Westron.md) or the Common Speech, burrowing some words from [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The sadness and the shock from the loss of their island home always remained in the hearts of the Númenóreans and their descendants.<sup id="cite_ref-akallabeth_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup>

Other Númenóreans survived that were of the [King's Men](../K/King's_Men.md), they were already in Middle-earth before the Downfall and established other realms in exile to the south; of these the [Haven of Umbar](../U/Umbar.md) was the chief and continued to reign the lands there. They later became known as the [Black Númenóreans](../B/Black_Númenóreans.md)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and continued to serve Sauron, whom had also survived the Downfall, although greatly diminished and bereft of shape returned to Middle-earth with [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) to continue troubling its inhabitants.<sup id="cite_ref-akallabeth_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup>

[New lands](../N/New_lands.md) formed in the West to replace the absence of Aman; islands rose and hills were formed. The coastline of Middle-earth also felt the effects of the cataclysm. In some places the coasts retreated, and in others they had advanced. [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) in particular suffered great loss from the advance of the coasts, whereas the coasts had retreated to the east and south of the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md), putting [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) much further inland than it had been and nearly destroying [Tolfalas](../T/Tolfalas.md). As the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) found new courses along the new coasts, the [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md) formed.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

After its fall Númenor was called *[Akallabêth](../A/Akallabêth.md)* or *[Atalantë](../A/Atalantë.md)* in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), meaning "the Downfallen", *[Mar-nu-Falmar](../M/Mar-nu-Falmar.md)* ("Land under the Waves").

Sauron's plan to destroy Númenor had been overwhelmingly successful, and though he too was caught by the flooding, he escaped, and at some point, returned to Middle-earth, believing himself to now be fully unopposed.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The manner of Sauron's arrival to Númenor took different forms in earlier versions of the story. In *The Fall of Númenor*, Sȗr (an earlier name for Sauron) came to Númenor in the 'likeness of a great bird' and preached the return of Morgoth.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> In a later version, the King of Númenor sends ships to Middle-earth after hearing the rise of Sauron and summons him to do homage.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> The element of the king sending an armed host appears later in *The Drowning of Anadûnê* where he smithies weapons of war.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> This would later be expanded to what closely resembles what is in the *Akallabêth*.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

In earlier versions of the story Ar-Pharazôn did not set foot on Aman with his army, instead he remained aboard his ship along with his queen [Istar](../T/Tar-Míriel.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium) (an earlier name of Tar-Míriel) and both perished when the sea swallowed the fleet.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> This was also the only instance in the texts that mentions Tar-Míriel accompanying Ar-Pharazôn to Aman, whereas in later writings she remained on the island.
