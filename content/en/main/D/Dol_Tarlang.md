**Dol Tarlang** was the northernmost of three peaks (the other two being [Cûl Bîn](/wiki/C%C3%BBl_B%C3%AEn) and [Cûl Veleg](/wiki/C%C3%BBl_Veleg)) located in the pass of [Tarlang's Neck](/wiki/Tarlang%27s_Neck). The three mountains formed a spur on the southern side of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains).

According to legend, Dol Tarlang was in origin the head of the [giant](/wiki/Giants "Giants") [Tarlang](/wiki/Tarlang).<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Dol Tarlang* is glossed as "Tarlang's Head",<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup> consisting of *[dol](/wiki/Dol "Dol")* ("head") and *[Tarlang](/wiki/Tarlang#Etymology)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
