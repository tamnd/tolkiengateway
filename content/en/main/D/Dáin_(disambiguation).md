**Dáin** can refer to two [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves):

- [Dáin I](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2440](/wiki/Third_Age_2440 "Third Age 2440") - [2589](/wiki/Third_Age_2589 "Third Age 2589")), [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Durin%27s_Folk "Kings of Durin's Folk") of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) who was killed by a [cold-drake](/wiki/Cold-drake "Cold-drake").
- [Dáin II *Ironfoot*](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2767](/wiki/Third_Age_2767 "Third Age 2767") - [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")), [Lord of the Iron Hills](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Iron_Hills) who became King of Durin's Folk following the death of [Thorin II *Oakenshield*](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin").
