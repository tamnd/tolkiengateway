**Durin's Stone** was a pillar that stood on the banks of [Mirrormere](/wiki/Mirrormere) lake near the [East-gate](/wiki/Great_Gates "Great Gates") of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria").

According to the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk), it was built on the site where <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a> looked down in the lake and saw [a crown](/wiki/Durin%27s_Crown "Durin's Crown") above his head; its purpose was to mark the event. It was ornamented with [runes](/wiki/Angerthas_Moria "Angerthas Moria"). By the later [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) the stone was broken, cracked and heavily weathered.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Balin](/wiki/Balin) was slain on that spot in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2994](/wiki/Third_Age_2994 "Third Age 2994").
