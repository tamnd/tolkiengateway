**Durin's Axe** was a great heirloom of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves).

## History {#History}

The axe remained lost in [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") after it was deserted in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981"). It was found again by [Balin's](/wiki/Balin "Balin") [expedition](/wiki/Balin%27s_Colony "Balin's Colony"), but lost again in [2994](/wiki/Third_Age_2994 "Third Age 2994"), when his colony was destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2023: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Return_to_Moria" title="The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria">The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The axe is broken into several fragments that must be recovered by the player in order for the axe to be reforged.</dd></dl>

<b>2024: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Moria_-_Through_the_Doors_of_Durin" title="Moria - Through the Doors of Durin">Moria: Through the Doors of Durin</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2965" title="Third Age 2965">2965</a>, the axe was still in the Lower Armoury of the <a href="/wiki/Third_Deep" title="Third Deep">Third Deep</a>, which was locked with a special key.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup></dd></dl>
