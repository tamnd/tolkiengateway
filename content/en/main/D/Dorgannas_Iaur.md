<i><b>Dorgannas Iaur</b></i> was a book of lore written by [Torhir Ifant](/wiki/Torhir_Ifant). The book contained an "account of the shapes of the lands of old", and [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine) quoted from it in his translation of the *[Quenta Silmarillion](/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion)* to clarify the placing of the realms of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MB-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dorgannas Iaur* is likely a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, apparently meaning "shaping of the lands of old" and consisting of *[dor](/wiki/Dor "Dor")* ("land") + *cannas* ("shaping") + *[iaur](/wiki/Iaur "Iaur")* ("old").<sup id="cite_ref-MB_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MB-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-DW_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DW-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
