**Dírhael** was a [Dúnadan](Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md) that was descended from [Aranarth](../A/Aranarth.md), the first [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and the maternal grandfather of [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Dírhael was married to [Ivorwen](../I/Ivorwen.md) and had a daughter called [Gilraen](../G/Gilraen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-2">[2]</a></sup> They lived in a hidden refuge in the wilds of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Arwen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arwen-3">[3]</a></sup> possibly in the [Angle](../A/Angle.md).

When [Arathorn](../A/Arathorn_II.md), the son of the [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md) [Arador](../A/Arador.md), wished to wed [Gilraen](../G/Gilraen.md), Gilraen's father Dírhael was against this marriage, because Gilraen was still too young for marriage according to the customs of the Dúnedain and he foresaw that Arathorn would be Chieftain soon and would only have a short life.

Ivorwen, however, also had the gift of foresight, and persuaded her husband to allow the marriage by saying that the days were darkening before the storm, and that [hope](../E/Estel.md) might be born for the Dúnedain if Arathorn wedded Gilraen now instead of delaying the marriage.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2929](../T/Third_Age_2929.md) his daughter Gilraen married Arathorn.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Arador was slain one year after the marriage in [2930](../T/TA.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and Dírhael's son-in-law became Chieftain of the Dúnedain.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> One year later, in [2931](../T/TA.md),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), the son of Arathorn and Gilraen was born. Only two years after the birth of Aragorn, Arathorn was likewise slain in [2933](../T/TA.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Dírhael* is in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md). Its meaning is not glossed, but [Paul Strack](../P/Paul_Strack.md) suggests it means "Wise Man" according to [David Salo](David_Salo.md), being a compound of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=D%C3%AEr&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dîr (page does not exist)">dîr</a></i> ("man") and *hael*, a [lenited](../L/Lenition.md) form of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Sael&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sael (page does not exist)">sael</a></i> ("wise").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earlier manuscript versions of what would later become Appendix A and Appendix B, Dírhael's name was *Dirhael*, before being changed to *Dirhoel*,<sup id="cite_ref-Arwen_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arwen-3">[3]</a></sup> and finally to *Dírhael*.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

Earlier manuscript versions of what would later become Appendix A mention that he was a descendant of Arathorn I "by a younger branch",<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> or that he we was of the blood of Isildur, but not of the "right line of the Heirs".<sup id="cite_ref-Arwen_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arwen-3">[3]</a></sup> Based on the ages of Gilraen, Arathorn II and Arador, it is possible that Dírhael was the great-grandson of Arathorn I.
