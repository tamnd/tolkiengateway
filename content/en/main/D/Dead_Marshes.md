The **Dead Marshes** were reeking wetlands that lay north-west of the [Dagorlad](/wiki/Dagorlad) and south-east of the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil). The marshes were an endless network of pools and soft mires filled with water-courses, and in the dark waters could be seen [the dead](/wiki/Dead_faces "Dead faces") from battles of long ago.<sup id="cite_ref-Passage_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passage-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:John_Howe_-_The_Battle_of_Dagorlad.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/8/87/John_Howe_-_The_Battle_of_Dagorlad.jpg/300px-John_Howe_-_The_Battle_of_Dagorlad.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="139" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/8/87/John_Howe_-_The_Battle_of_Dagorlad.jpg/450px-John_Howe_-_The_Battle_of_Dagorlad.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/8/87/John_Howe_-_The_Battle_of_Dagorlad.jpg/600px-John_Howe_-_The_Battle_of_Dagorlad.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1198" data-file-height="554"/></a><figcaption><i>The Battle of Dagorlad</i> by <a href="/wiki/John_Howe" title="John Howe">John Howe</a></figcaption></figure>

In the year [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434"), the host of the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men) fought the forces of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) in the [Battle of Dagorlad](/wiki/Battle_of_Dagorlad).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> During the battle on the plains, more than half of the Elves of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) under the command of King [Malgalad](/wiki/Malgalad) were driven into the Dead Marshes.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> After the battle, many of the slain were buried outside of the marshy area, but over time (in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age)) the Marshes had grown and swallowed the graves.<sup id="cite_ref-Passage_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passage-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944"), King [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) was caught by a surprise attack by the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) upon the Dagorlad. When the King and his guard were destroyed, many of the confused soldiers of Gondor were driven by the attackers into the Dead Marshes.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> However, after [Eärnil II](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II) won the [Battle of the Camp](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Camp), those of the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) who were not slain in the fight were themselves driven into the Dead Marshes and there perished.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3017](/wiki/Third_Age_3017 "Third Age 3017"), [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) found and captured [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) along the skirts of the Dead Marshes<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and brought him to [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood).

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Inger_Edelfeldt_-_The_Dead_Marshes.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/6/62/Inger_Edelfeldt_-_The_Dead_Marshes.jpg/300px-Inger_Edelfeldt_-_The_Dead_Marshes.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="426" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/6/62/Inger_Edelfeldt_-_The_Dead_Marshes.jpg/450px-Inger_Edelfeldt_-_The_Dead_Marshes.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/6/62/Inger_Edelfeldt_-_The_Dead_Marshes.jpg/600px-Inger_Edelfeldt_-_The_Dead_Marshes.jpg 2x" data-file-width="997" data-file-height="1417"/></a><figcaption><i>The Dead Marshes</i> by <a href="/wiki/Inger_Edelfeldt" title="Inger Edelfeldt">Inger Edelfeldt</a></figcaption></figure>

After [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") had met Gollum in the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) (on [29 February](/wiki/29_February) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-Great_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-7">[7]</a></sup>), he revealed that he had found a hidden way across the Marshes.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The three took Gollum’s passage, and on [1 March](/wiki/1_March)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-7">[7]</a></sup> started crossing the Marshes, where they saw candles and lights dancing about. Frodo was mesmerized by the lights and tried to reach out and touch the faces of the dead at the bottom of the marshes. Gollum told them that the dead could not be touched, suggesting that he had once tried to eat them.<sup id="cite_ref-Passage_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passage-1">[1]</a></sup> They completed the passage [the next day](/wiki/March_2 "March 2") and continued their way to the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)*, Tolkien speculated that the description of the Dead Marshes may have been based on his personal experience in [World War I](/wiki/World_War_I), specifically, the [Battle of the Somme](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> When it rained, blast craters in no-man's land would become a series of pools or lakes with bodies of dead soldiers, from both sides, floating in them.
