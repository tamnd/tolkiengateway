The **Dead Tree** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1640](../T/Third_Age_1640.md) - [2872](../T/Third_Age_2872.md); the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snag_(ecology)" title="wikipedia:Snag (ecology)">snag</a> stood [for 147 years](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)) was planted by King [Tarondor](<../T/Tarondor_(King_of_Gondor).md>). It was the third and last [White Tree of Gondor](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md). It died at the same time as [Belecthor II](../B/Belecthor_II.md) the twenty-first [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

For lack of a seedling, the Dead Tree was left standing in the [Court of the Fountain](<../C/Court_of_the_Fountain_(Minas_Tirith).md>) of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) "until the King comes".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It stood throughout the following centuries and the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). That was until King [Aragorn Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) found a new seedling on [Mount Mindolluin](../M/Mindolluin.md) in 3019.

When it was replaced, it was removed from the court but was placed in the [Tombs of the Kings](../R/Rath_Dínen.md) with all the honour that would normally be accorded a fallen [monarch](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<b>2003: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The film shows the dead tree standing in the court. A close up reveals that it is flowering again during the <a href="../S/Siege_of_Gondor.md" title="Siege of Gondor">Siege of Gondor</a>, specifically the scene where <a href="Denethor.md" title="Denethor">Denethor</a> is preparing to cremate himself and <a href="../F/Faramir.md" title="Faramir">Faramir</a>. During the coronation of Aragorn, the tree is seen fully blossomed.</dd></dl>
