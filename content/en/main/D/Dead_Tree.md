The **Dead Tree** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1640](/wiki/Third_Age_1640 "Third Age 1640") - [2872](/wiki/Third_Age_2872 "Third Age 2872"); the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snag_(ecology)" title="wikipedia:Snag (ecology)">snag</a> stood [for 147 years](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")) was planted by King [Tarondor](</wiki/Tarondor_(King_of_Gondor)> "Tarondor (King of Gondor)"). It was the third and last [White Tree of Gondor](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor). It died at the same time as [Belecthor II](/wiki/Belecthor_II) the twenty-first [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

For lack of a seedling, the Dead Tree was left standing in the [Court of the Fountain](</wiki/Court_of_the_Fountain_(Minas_Tirith)> "Court of the Fountain (Minas Tirith)") of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) "until the King comes".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It stood throughout the following centuries and the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). That was until King [Aragorn Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") found a new seedling on [Mount Mindolluin](/wiki/Mindolluin "Mindolluin") in 3019.

When it was replaced, it was removed from the court but was placed in the [Tombs of the Kings](/wiki/Rath_D%C3%ADnen "Rath Dínen") with all the honour that would normally be accorded a fallen [monarch](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The film shows the dead tree standing in the court. A close up reveals that it is flowering again during the <a href="/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor" title="Siege of Gondor">Siege of Gondor</a>, specifically the scene where <a href="/wiki/Denethor" title="Denethor">Denethor</a> is preparing to cremate himself and <a href="/wiki/Faramir" title="Faramir">Faramir</a>. During the coronation of Aragorn, the tree is seen fully blossomed.</dd></dl>
