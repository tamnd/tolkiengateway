The **Drúedain of Númenor** were the descendants of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain_of_Beleriand "Drúedain of Beleriand") survivors at the [Havens of Sirion](/wiki/Havens_of_Sirion) who were allowed to remove to the newly-made island of [Elenna](/wiki/Elenna) (i.e. [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor)) at the beginning of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-Drugu_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drugu-1">[1]</a></sup>

Once settled in Númenor they thrived again and took no part in any wars.<sup id="cite_ref-Drugu_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drugu-1">[1]</a></sup>

According to a copy of *[The Mariner's Wife](/wiki/Aldarion_and_Erendis "Aldarion and Erendis")*, an old Númenórean story of some of their first voyages back to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) preserved in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), there was a note by the scribe which described the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain) of the household of [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion) (the sixth [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor)) begging him to cease his voyages to Middle-earth, since they foreboded that evil would come of them.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Drugu_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drugu-1">[1]</a></sup>

However, since they did not succeed in doing so, from then onwards the Drúedain began leaving Númenor for Middle-earth (despite their fear of the sea) until none were left at the island by the time [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) escaped the [Downfall](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Downfall of Númenor") in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3319](/wiki/Second_Age_3319 "Second Age 3319")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-Drugu_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drugu-1">[1]</a></sup>

The last of them fled Númenor when [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n) brought [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) there in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3262](/wiki/Second_Age_3262 "Second Age 3262")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-Drugu_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drugu-1">[1]</a></sup>
