The **Drúedain of Númenor** were the descendants of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) [Drúedain](Drúedain_of_Beleriand.md) survivors at the [Havens of Sirion](../H/Havens_of_Sirion.md) who were allowed to remove to the newly-made island of [Elenna](../E/Elenna.md) (i.e. [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md)) at the beginning of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Drugu_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drugu-1">[1]</a></sup>

Once settled in Númenor they thrived again and took no part in any wars.<sup id="cite_ref-Drugu_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drugu-1">[1]</a></sup>

According to a copy of *[The Mariner's Wife](../A/Aldarion_and_Erendis.md)*, an old Númenórean story of some of their first voyages back to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) preserved in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), there was a note by the scribe which described the [Drúedain](Drúedain.md) of the household of [Tar-Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) (the sixth [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md)) begging him to cease his voyages to Middle-earth, since they foreboded that evil would come of them.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Drugu_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drugu-1">[1]</a></sup>

However, since they did not succeed in doing so, from then onwards the Drúedain began leaving Númenor for Middle-earth (despite their fear of the sea) until none were left at the island by the time [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) escaped the [Downfall](Downfall_of_Númenor.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3319](../S/Second_Age_3319.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-Drugu_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drugu-1">[1]</a></sup>

The last of them fled Númenor when [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md) brought [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) there in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3262](../S/Second_Age_3262.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-Drugu_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drugu-1">[1]</a></sup>
