**Dagorath** is a raw black metal band from Talcahuano, Chile. The solo project of the musician Nigromante, it takes its name from the [Dagor Dagorath](Dagor_Dagorath.md), the prophesied final battle of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). Its 2023 compilation *Ondolindë* takes its title from the Quenya name for [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).
