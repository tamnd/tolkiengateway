**Dagorath** is a raw black metal band from Talcahuano, Chile. The solo project of the musician Nigromante, it takes its name from the [Dagor Dagorath](/wiki/Dagor_Dagorath), the prophesied final battle of [Arda](/wiki/Arda). Its 2023 compilation *Ondolindë* takes its title from the Quenya name for [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).
