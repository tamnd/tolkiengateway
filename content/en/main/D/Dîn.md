<i><b>dîn</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word with three meanings:

1. *silence*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
2. *opening, gap, pass in mountains*
3. a soft mutation of *[tîn](/wiki/T%C3%AEn "Tîn")*

- *[Dor Dínen](/wiki/Dor_D%C3%ADnen)*
- *[Rath Dínen](/wiki/Rath_D%C3%ADnen)*
- *[Amon Dîn](/wiki/Amon_D%C3%AEn)*
