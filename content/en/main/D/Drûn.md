**Drûn** was a region in [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), located north of [Tarn Aeluin](/wiki/Tarn_Aeluin) and west of [Ladros](/wiki/Ladros).<sup id="cite_ref-Drun_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drun-1">[1]</a></sup>

The region was marked on [the Second 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_Second_%27Silmarillion%27_Map "The Second 'Silmarillion' Map"),<sup id="cite_ref-Drun_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Drun-1">[1]</a></sup> on which the [map of Beleriand](/wiki/Map_of_Beleriand_and_the_Lands_to_the_North "Map of Beleriand and the Lands to the North") in the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)* was based upon.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Apart from the mention of a "*fire in Drûn*" in the [revised *Lay of Leithian*](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Leithian_Recommenced "The Lay of Leithian Recommenced"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> nothing else is known about the region.
