**Princess Disa** is the wife of [Prince Durin IV](/wiki/Durin_IV "Durin IV"). Disa was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios), where she is portrayed by [Sophia Nomvete](/wiki/Sophia_Nomvete).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When Disa reminded [Durin IV](/wiki/Durin_IV) how he will inherit [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm) from [his father](/wiki/Durin_III "Durin III"), Durin IV remarked that it was just another reason to count his blessings, before reminiscing on how he married her and not [Margid Rustborin](/wiki/Margid_Rustborin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Controversy {#Controversy}

Disa was the first female dwarf revealed from the show and she was portrayed with little facial hair. Some fans took offense because they believed [Dwarf-women](/wiki/Dwarf-women) have full beards.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Sophia Nomvete responded to this, explaining that Disa does have facial hair, although it is less prominent than most male dwarves, as she only has sideburns.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
