**Decipher, Inc.** is a gaming company, founded in [1983](/wiki/1983). Their first games were puzzles (hence the name) and board games, but they later settled on trading card games. After losing the rights to *Star Wars* in 2001, they quickly found a replacement: [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"). Their popular *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game)* - usually referred to as "Decipher Cards" - lasted until [2007](/wiki/2007), when their license expired.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

During this time, Decipher also ran the *[The Lord of the Rings Fan Club](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Fan_Club)*, and in [2002](/wiki/2002) they published *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game)*.
