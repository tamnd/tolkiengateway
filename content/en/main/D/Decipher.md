**Decipher, Inc.** is a gaming company, founded in [1983](../_/1983.md). Their first games were puzzles (hence the name) and board games, but they later settled on trading card games. After losing the rights to *Star Wars* in 2001, they quickly found a replacement: [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>). Their popular *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md)* - usually referred to as "Decipher Cards" - lasted until [2007](../_/2007.md), when their license expired.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

During this time, Decipher also ran the *[The Lord of the Rings Fan Club](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Fan_Club.md)*, and in [2002](../_/2002.md) they published *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game.md)*.
