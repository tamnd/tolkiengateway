**Dáin's hall** was the dwelling of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) in the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains), the name coming from the [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves")-king [Dáin I](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Little is known about this hall save that in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2589](/wiki/Third_Age_2589 "Third Age 2589"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Dáin](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I "Dáin I") was killed by a great [Cold-Drake](/wiki/Cold-drakes "Cold-drakes") along with his son [Frór](/wiki/Fr%C3%B3r) at the entrance to the hall.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup> A year later, most of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) (though not all)<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup> abandoned the mountains, and left for [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") and the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) lead by [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) and [Grór](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3r), respectively, [Dáin](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I "Dáin I")'s other two sons.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2018: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Founded by <a href="/wiki/Thorin_I" title="Thorin I">Thorin I</a> it was known as &#34;Grárik&#34; or the &#34;Grey Kingdom&#34;. Its capital was &#34;Thikil-gundu, the Steel Keep&#34;, a fortress which pre-dated the kingdom and was also claimed by the &#34;Zhélruka&#34; dwarves of the <a href="/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn" title="Rhûn">East</a>.</dd></dl>
