**Dáin's hall** was the dwelling of [Durin's Folk](Durin's_Folk~15256.md) in the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md), the name coming from the [Dwarf](Dwarves.md)-king [Dáin I](Dáin_I.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Little is known about this hall save that in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2589](../T/Third_Age_2589.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Dáin](Dáin_I.md) was killed by a great [Cold-Drake](../C/Cold-drakes.md) along with his son [Frór](../F/Frór.md) at the entrance to the hall.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup> A year later, most of [Durin's Folk](Durin's_Folk~15256.md) (though not all)<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup> abandoned the mountains, and left for [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) and the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md) lead by [Thrór](../T/Thrór.md) and [Grór](../G/Grór.md), respectively, [Dáin](Dáin_I.md)'s other two sons.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2018: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Founded by <a href="../T/Thorin_I.md" title="Thorin I">Thorin I</a> it was known as &#34;Grárik&#34; or the &#34;Grey Kingdom&#34;. Its capital was &#34;Thikil-gundu, the Steel Keep&#34;, a fortress which pre-dated the kingdom and was also claimed by the &#34;Zhélruka&#34; dwarves of the <a href="../R/Rhûn.md" title="Rhûn">East</a>.</dd></dl>
