**Daedalic Entertainment GmbH** is a German videogame publisher and developer and it is based in Hamburg.In 2022, it was acquired by [Nacon](/wiki/Nacon) company.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the publishing company of the videogame [The Lord of the Rings: Gollum](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Gollum).
