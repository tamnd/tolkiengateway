**Durlach** was a [Balrog](../B/Balrog.md) of [Angband](../A/Angband.md) invented by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)*. While he was described as one of the mightiest beings to still inhabit the [Northern Waste](../N/Northern_Waste.md) by the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), he was spell-bound to Morgoth's Well after the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-bio_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bio-2">[2]</a></sup>

Durlach often appeared as a giant shimmering red-and-yellow serpent with slitted eyes, though he was also able to appear in the shape of a demon.<sup id="cite_ref-desc_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-desc-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Morgoth's Well ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) “Eithel Morgoth”) was a volcanic basin that had been shaped into the forge and armoury of [Angband](../A/Angband.md).<sup id="cite_ref-well_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-well-4">[4]</a></sup> During the destruction of Angband, the enthralled elf Hirgon bound Durlach to the pit of lava before perishing.<sup id="cite_ref-desc_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-desc-3">[3]</a></sup> While the Well's fortifications were destroyed in the War of Wrath, the caldera of the forge remained. Its remaining tunnels and pits reached into the Under-forges of the [Under-deeps](../U/Under-deeps.md).<sup id="cite_ref-well_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-well-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On a mission to cleanse the Northern Waste from [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s taint by planting trees, Hirgon's brother was slain by Durlach in Morgoth's Well.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> His companions set up a tomb for him on the slopes of the Well.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

The lake of lava in the Well slowly rose during the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), and the spell that bound Durlach to the Well would be broken if it ever overflowed.<sup id="cite_ref-bio_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bio-2">[2]</a></sup>
