**Déor** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) 2644 – 2718, aged 74 years) was the seventh [King of Rohan](/wiki/King_of_Rohan).

## History {#History}

Déor was born during the rule of his great-great-grandfather, [Aldor](/wiki/Aldor) the Old. He himself became king at age 55, following the death of [his father](/wiki/Goldwine "Goldwine"). During Déor's kingship the peace of Rohan (which had begun during [Aldor](/wiki/Aldor)'s reign) came to an end - the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) took to raiding the herds and studs of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) over the River [Isen](/wiki/Isen).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

When it became clear that the raiders were coming from near [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2710](/wiki/Third_Age_2710 "Third Age 2710") King Déor led an expedition to the north. He found and defeated a host of Dunlendings, but discovered that [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) was held by hostile forces. Secure in the fortress they could not be dislodged and [Egalmoth](</wiki/Egalmoth_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Egalmoth (Steward of Gondor)") could not send help. Instead Déor kept a strong force of riders in the north of the [Westfold](/wiki/Westfold).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Déor's reign lasted nineteen years. He was succeeded as King by his son, [Gram](/wiki/Gram).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Déor means "brave" or "bold" in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
