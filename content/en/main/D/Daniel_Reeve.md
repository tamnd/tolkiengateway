**Daniel Reeve** is an artist, calligrapher, and cartographer who worked on [*The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")* films, as well as *[The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power")* television series.

Once a computer programmer, Reeve was a Tolkien fan and hobbyist who experimented with [Elvish calligraphy](/wiki/Tengwar "Tengwar") and maps. During the filming of the Trilogy, he was hired to produce all the maps, inscriptions, scrolls and other props that appear on-screen. Now Reeve works as a freelance artist.

He has produced several Tolkien-inspired [fonts](/wiki/Fonts "Fonts") and maps that appear in paraphernalia of the Lord of the Rings franchise. His maps were a part of *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game)*.

Reeve also worked in the production of the film adaptations of [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis)'s *[The Chronicles of Narnia](/wiki/The_Chronicles_of_Narnia)*.
