**Daniel Reeve** is an artist, calligrapher, and cartographer who worked on [*The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) and *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)* films, as well as *[The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* television series.

Once a computer programmer, Reeve was a Tolkien fan and hobbyist who experimented with [Elvish calligraphy](../T/Tengwar.md) and maps. During the filming of the Trilogy, he was hired to produce all the maps, inscriptions, scrolls and other props that appear on-screen. Now Reeve works as a freelance artist.

He has produced several Tolkien-inspired [fonts](../F/Fonts.md) and maps that appear in paraphernalia of the Lord of the Rings franchise. His maps were a part of *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game.md)*.

Reeve also worked in the production of the film adaptations of [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md)'s *[The Chronicles of Narnia](../T/The_Chronicles_of_Narnia.md)*.
