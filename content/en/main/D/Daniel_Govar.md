**Daniel Govar** has created numerous [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-inspired illustrations. He also co-founded and illustrated the website <a class="external text" href="https://web.archive.org/web/20060202005148/http://www.thereandbackagain.net/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">There And Back Again</a>, a Lord of the Rings site dedicated to visualizing the world created by J.R.R. Tolkien. (no longer available).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

He holds a degree in Imaging and Digital Art from UMBC with an emphasis on interactivity, animation, traditional film, and illustration.
