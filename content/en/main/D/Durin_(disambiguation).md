**Durin** is a dwarf-name from the Icelandic saga [Völuspá](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1), where it is written as *Durinn*; in old Norse *durin* means "warden" or "guard". As the father of the [Longbeards](/wiki/Longbeards), the name **Durin** has been used frequently within the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

## Characters {#Characters}

- **[Durin I](/wiki/Durin_I), "the Deathless", one of the seven [Fathers of the Dwarves](/wiki/Fathers_of_the_Dwarves).**
- [Durin II](/wiki/Durin_II)
- [Durin III](/wiki/Durin_III), given a [Ring of Power](/wiki/Ring_of_Thr%C3%B3r "Ring of Thrór") by [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor).
- [Durin IV](/wiki/Durin_IV)
- [Durin V](/wiki/Durin_V)
- [Durin VI](/wiki/Durin_VI), slain by [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane).
- [Durin VII](/wiki/Durin_VII), "the Last", reclaimed [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).

- [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane), the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") of [Moria](/wiki/Moria) killed by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf).

## People {#People}

- [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk), the most famous [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") clan of [Tolkien's](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").
    - [Kings of Durin's Folk](/wiki/Kings_of_Durin%27s_Folk), the title held by Durin I and his successors who ruled over Durin's Folk.

## Objects {#Objects}

- [Durin's Axe](/wiki/Durin%27s_Axe), an heirloom of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk).
- [Durin's Stone](/wiki/Durin%27s_Stone), a stone pillar on the shore of [Mirrormere](/wiki/Mirrormere).

## Places {#Places}

- [Doors of Durin](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin), the entrance to [Moria](/wiki/Moria), opened by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.
- [Durin's Tower](/wiki/Durin%27s_Tower), a Dwarven tower built into [Celebdil](/wiki/Celebdil) above [Moria](/wiki/Moria).

## Events {#Events}

- [Durin's Day](/wiki/Durin%27s_Day), the first day of the [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") calendar.

## Constellation {#Constellation}

- [Durin's Crown](/wiki/Durin%27s_Crown), a constellation, often thought to be the [Valacirca](/wiki/Valacirca).
