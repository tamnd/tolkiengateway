**Durin** is a dwarf-name from the Icelandic saga [Völuspá](../V/Völuspá.md), where it is written as *Durinn*; in old Norse *durin* means "warden" or "guard". As the father of the [Longbeards](../L/Longbeards.md), the name **Durin** has been used frequently within the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).

## Characters {#Characters}

- **[Durin I](Durin_I.md), "the Deathless", one of the seven [Fathers of the Dwarves](../F/Fathers_of_the_Dwarves.md).**
- [Durin II](Durin_II.md)
- [Durin III](Durin_III.md), given a [Ring of Power](../R/Ring_of_Thrór.md) by [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md).
- [Durin IV](Durin_IV.md)
- [Durin V](Durin_V.md)
- [Durin VI](Durin_VI.md), slain by [Durin's Bane](Durin's_Bane.md).
- [Durin VII](Durin_VII.md), "the Last", reclaimed [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md) in the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md).

- [Durin's Bane](Durin's_Bane.md), the [Balrog](../B/Balrogs.md) of [Moria](../M/Moria.md) killed by [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md).
