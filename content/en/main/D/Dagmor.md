**Dagmor** was a sword that belonged to [Beren Erchamion](/wiki/Beren "Beren").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It means "Dark Slayer" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), from *[dag](/wiki/Dag "Dag")-* ("slay") and *[môr](/wiki/M%C3%B4r "Môr")* ("darkness, dark, night").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
