**David Collings** ([4 June](/wiki/4_June) [1940](/wiki/1940) - [23 March](/wiki/23_March) [2020](/wiki/2020)) was a British actor. He voiced the part of [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) in [*The Lord of the Rings* 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") broadcast on the BBC 4.
