**David Collings** ([4 June](../_/4_June.md) [1940](../_/1940.md) - [23 March](../_/23_March.md) [2020](../_/2020.md)) was a British actor. He voiced the part of [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* 1981 radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) broadcast on the BBC 4.
