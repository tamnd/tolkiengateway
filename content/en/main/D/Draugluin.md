**Draugluin** was the first [Werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf).<sup id="cite_ref-Draug_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Draug-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Bred from [wolves](/wiki/Wolves "Wolves") and inhabited with an evil spirit imprisoned by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) himself, Draugluin was the sire of all Werewolves of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and dwelled with his master in [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth), the former watchtower of [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod"), which he aided to conquer.

He was slain by [Huan](/wiki/Huan) during the [Quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril), though he informed Sauron that Huan was present. [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) used his pelt to sneak into [Angband](/wiki/Angband).<sup id="cite_ref-Draug_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Draug-2">[2]</a></sup>

Draugluin was the sire of [Carcharoth](/wiki/Carcharoth) and many other Werewolves. It is probable the [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs) of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) were descended from him.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

The name *Draugluin* means "blue wolf" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), from *[draug](/wiki/Draug "Draug")* ("wolf") + *[luin](/wiki/Luin "Luin")* ("blue"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> in reference to the color of his pelt.
