<i><b>Drughu</b></i> is the word in the [Drúadan language](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_language) for the [Woses](/wiki/Woses) (that is, their own name for themselves).<sup id="cite_ref-UTnote_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTnote-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *Drû* &gt; *[Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain)*<sup id="cite_ref-UTnote_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTnote-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *Rú* &gt; *[Rúatan](/wiki/R%C3%BAatani "Rúatani")*<sup id="cite_ref-UTnote_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTnote-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Pre-Númenórean](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean): *drûg*<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
