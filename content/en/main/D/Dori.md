**Dori** was a [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarf) of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk). He was the brother of [Nori](/wiki/Nori) and [Ori](/wiki/Ori), and a remote kinsman of [Thorin Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin"). Alongside his brothers, Dori was a member of [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company), who, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"), partook in a successful expedition to the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) to reclaim the lands from the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug).

Proclaimed by the [hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), the company's burglar, as a "decent fellow," thrice Dori aided him during the expedition to Erebor: during the company's escape from the [goblins](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") in the tunnels of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), carrying him on his back as they fled; during their escape from [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs), climbing down a tree, allowing Bilbo to scramble up him into said tree; and when the company were carried off by the [eagles](/wiki/Eagles "Eagles"), wherein Bilbo grabbed onto Dori's legs. Upon the conclusion of the quest, Dori lived with his share of the treasure in the Lonely Mountain into the final years of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

## History {#History}

### Family and birth {#Family_and_birth}

Dori's parentage is unknown, although he was a member of the [House of Durin](/wiki/House_of_Durin) and a remote kinsman of [Thorin Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin").<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> He was also the brother of [Nori](/wiki/Nori), and a cousin of [Ori](/wiki/Ori).<sup id="cite_ref-Person_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Person-1">[1]</a></sup> His birth year is also unknown.

However, in [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' account of the quest to Erebor he stated that the brothers [Fíli](/wiki/F%C3%ADli) and [Kíli](/wiki/K%C3%ADli) were the youngest of the thirteen "by some fifty years",<sup id="cite_ref-Hill_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hill-3">[3]</a></sup> and that when Thorin was missing [Balin](/wiki/Balin) was "the eldest left".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Since Kíli was born in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2864](/wiki/Third_Age_2864 "Third Age 2864") and Balin was born in [2763](/wiki/Third_Age_2763 "Third Age 2763"),<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> this would put Dori's birth between [2763](/wiki/Third_Age_2763 "Third Age 2763") and c. 2814, and he would have been between c. 127 and 178 years old during the quest to Erebor.

Thorin stated that Dori was the strongest of the Dwarves in the company.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Dori (and Nori) shared Bilbo's opinion that meals should be plentiful and frequent.,<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-6">[6]</a></sup> and also it is said that Dori was "a decent fellow despite his grumbling".

### The Quest of Erebor {#The_Quest_of_Erebor}

When Bilbo first met Dori (in the fourth group of Dwarves to arrive at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) alongside Nori and Ori and another set of brothers, [Óin](/wiki/%C3%93in) and [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in)) he was wearing a purple hood along with a silver belt, same as his brother Nori. During the evening when Thorin called for music, Dori (along with Nori and Ori) played the flute.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Bilbo proclaimed Dori to be "really a decent fellow"<sup id="cite_ref-Pan_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pan-8">[8]</a></sup> because thrice Dori assisted Bilbo when the hobbit might otherwise have been killed: First, Dori carried Bilbo in the tunnels of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) during the escape from the goblins, but unfortunately dropped him which led to the hobbit's encounter with Gollum.<sup id="cite_ref-Hill_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hill-3">[3]</a></sup> Upon Nori's discovery that Bilbo was gone the other Dwarves blamed Dori for losing their burglar, especially the wizard [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) arguing with the dwarf. Second, during the escape from the [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs), Dori climbed down from a tree, which allowed Bilbo to scramble up Dori and into the tree. Lastly, Bilbo grabbed Dori's legs as they were carried off by an eagle, saving them from a fire lit by Goblins, much to both Bilbo and Dori's dismay crying "My legs! My poor legs!".<sup id="cite_ref-Pan_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pan-8">[8]</a></sup>

When [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) was telling the story of the company's adventures to [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn), Dori and Thorin were the first two Dwarves to show up.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Thorin also notes that Dori is the strongest of their company. Upon being pulled out of the barrel after escaping the [Elvenking](/wiki/Thranduil "Thranduil")'s halls and arrived in [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town), Dori, along with several others was one of the Dwarves who was waterlogged and seemed only half alive.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

Dori would later fight in and survive the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies). He was given his share of the treasure by [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot), the new king under the mountain after Thorin's death at the battle by [Bolg](/wiki/Bolg), and made his home in Erebor alongside his other companions that survived the battle and would live in great wealth under King Dáin.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

### Later life {#Later_life}

Dori lived a wealthy life after the quest to reclaim the Lonely Mountain. His date of death is unknown but he was still alive during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), as mentioned by [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in) before the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018").<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-12">[12]</a></sup> He probably fought in the [Battle of Dale](/wiki/Battle_of_Dale) against [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s forces, the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings), along with the other members of the company still alive.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Dóri* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. His name most likely means "Borer" or "auger-man".<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

On the wet night before meeting the Trolls the prepublication version had Bombur and Bofur failing to light a fire and quarreling about it, with Dori and Nori getting into a fight.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> In the published version Tolkien added a comment that [Óin](/wiki/%C3%93in) and [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in) were especially good at making a fire, made them the two who could not do it that night, and had them start fighting.<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Films {#Films}

<b>1967: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_film)" title="The Hobbit (1967 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1967 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dori is omitted. <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin Oakenshield</a> only travels with an unnamed guard and the princess of Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-Deitch_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deitch-15">[15]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1977: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)" title="The Hobbit (1977 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1977 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dori is voiced by <a href="/wiki/John_Stephenson" title="John Stephenson">John Stephenson</a>. Apart from Thorin, he is one of only three of the other Dwarves who speaks with any regularity (<a href="/wiki/Balin" title="Balin">Balin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Bombur" title="Bombur">Bombur</a> being the others). As in the book, he sometimes carries <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo</a> on his back. He is the one to discover the Hobbit is missing during their escape from <a href="/wiki/Goblin-town" title="Goblin-town">Goblin-town</a>. Bilbo also holds onto his leg (much to his chagrin) while the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Eagles" title="Eagles">Eagles</a> are flying the Company to safety. Later, after reclaiming Erebor, you see the dwarves celebrating as Dori jokingly says about him being king under the mountain, though Thorin dismisses the dwarf&#39;s joke. During the Battle of Five Armies you see Dori, stabbed (making him possibly one of the dwarves that passed away during the battle).</dd></dl>

<b>2012-14: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dori is played by <a href="/wiki/Mark_Hadlow" title="Mark Hadlow">Mark Hadlow</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting-16">[16]</a></sup> A description of Dori in <i>The Hobbit</i> films was released by the studio:</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt"></blockquote></dd></dl>

A distant relative of Thorin Oakenshield, Dori is the eldest amongst his brothers Ori and Nori. He is the strongest in The Company of Dwarves, and spends much of his time watching out for Ori, making sure he hasn’t caught a chill or gotten himself killed by Wargs or Goblins. Eternally pessimistic, with a natural tendency to expect the worst, Dori is nonetheless quite prepared to risk life and limb in order to get the job done.

### Games {#Games}

<b>1982: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1982_video_game)" title="The Hobbit (1982 video game)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1982 video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dori is omitted; Thorin is the only companion of the player, <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2003: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)" title="The Hobbit (2003 video game)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (2003 video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dori has a long brown beard without moustache. He does not appear in the game until <a href="/wiki/Lake-town" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a>, though he can be seen in the fir tree with Bilbo, much like the scene in the book.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dori is a non-playable character, who is the official &#34;High Hoard Seeker&#34; of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Erebor" title="Erebor">Erebor</a>. He is captured along with the gems he was bringing to the Dwarves of Othrikar by the Dourhands. The player must rescue him in Vol I, Book III, Chapter 4 &#34;Freeing Dori&#34;. He also makes an appearance during the Dwarven Introduction quest line, at Thorin&#39;s Gate. Much later, after the events of <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>&#39;s Fall, he is found in various places in Erebor, and leads an expedition into the <a href="/wiki/Grey_Mountains" title="Grey Mountains">Ered Mithrin</a> to save his brother <a href="/wiki/Nori" title="Nori">Nori</a>.</dd></dl>
