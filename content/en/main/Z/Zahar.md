<i><b>zahar</b></i> appears to be a [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md) word, only attested in the compound name *[Tumunzahar](../T/Tumunzahar.md)*. The whole name is translated "Hollowbold",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Magnus Åberg](../M/Magnus_Åberg.md) has suggested that the element *zahar* means "hollow".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
