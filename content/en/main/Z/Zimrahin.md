**Zimrahin** was a woman from the [Folk of Marach](../F/Folk_of_Marach.md).

She wedded [Malach Aradan](../M/Malach.md) in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [337](../F/First_Age_337.md), after his return from fourteen years in [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md). After marriage she took the Elvish name of *Meldis* and became the mother of [Adanel](../A/Adanel.md) and [Magor](../M/Magor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJwest_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJwest-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Zimrahin* is obviously a [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) name of the Folk of Marach. The name seems to contain the later [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) element *[zimra](Zimra.md)* "jewel", and possibly means "jewel child". Adûnaic would emerge as a descendant of the language Zimhrahin was speaking.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<br> *Meldis* is the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name of Zimrahin. The meaning of the name is "friend".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The character only appears in *The 'Folk of Marach'* family tree, publichsed in *[The War of the Jewels](../T/The_War_of_the_Jewels.md)*.

In the first conception of the table, there was a note for multiple children that said, "Others not concerned in these Chronicles". In version two of the table, this became one name, a third child caled "Sael..th" that was changed to "[Saelon](<../S/Saelon_(daughter_of_Malach).md>)"; finally the name and birth-date were struck out.<sup id="cite_ref-WJwest_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJwest-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
