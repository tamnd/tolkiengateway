**Zamîn**, called "mother Zamîn" by the boy [Îbal](../_/Îbal.md), was a servant in the [White House](../W/White_House.md) of [Erendis](../E/Erendis.md). As such she was a companion of Erendis' daughter, [Ancalimë](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md), who first learned of her father's name ([Aldarion](../A/Aldarion.md)) from the old country woman.

When Ancalimë had grown to adulthood and become the heir of the king (upon Tar-Aldarion's reformulation of the [Law of Succession in Númenor](../L/Law_of_Succession_in_Númenor.md)) she was beset by suitors coming to [Emerië](../E/Emerië.md). Zamîn aided the [Emerwen Aranel](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md), or "Princess Shepherdess" to go into hiding on a farm bordering the lands of [Hallatan](../H/Hallatan.md) of [Hyarastorni](../H/Hyarastorni.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Zamîn's name appears to be [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md), but its meaning is impossible to determine. <span></span>
