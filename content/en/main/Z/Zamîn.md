**Zamîn**, called "mother Zamîn" by the boy [Îbal](/wiki/%C3%8Ebal), was a servant in the [White House](/wiki/White_House) of [Erendis](/wiki/Erendis). As such she was a companion of Erendis' daughter, [Ancalimë](/wiki/Tar-Ancalim%C3%AB "Tar-Ancalimë"), who first learned of her father's name ([Aldarion](/wiki/Aldarion)) from the old country woman.

When Ancalimë had grown to adulthood and become the heir of the king (upon Tar-Aldarion's reformulation of the [Law of Succession in Númenor](/wiki/Law_of_Succession_in_N%C3%BAmenor)) she was beset by suitors coming to [Emerië](/wiki/Emeri%C3%AB). Zamîn aided the [Emerwen Aranel](/wiki/Tar-Ancalim%C3%AB "Tar-Ancalimë"), or "Princess Shepherdess" to go into hiding on a farm bordering the lands of [Hallatan](/wiki/Hallatan) of [Hyarastorni](/wiki/Hyarastorni).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Zamîn's name appears to be [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic), but its meaning is impossible to determine. <span></span>
