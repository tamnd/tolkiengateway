**Zimraphel**, title of [Ar-Zimraphel](/wiki/Ar-Zimraphel), a translation of [Míriel](/wiki/Tar-M%C3%ADriel "Tar-Míriel") into [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It means "jewel-daughter".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> *[Zimra](/wiki/Zimir "Zimir")* means, disputably, *jewel*, while <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Phel&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Phel (page does not exist)">phel</a></i> is possibly *daughter*.
