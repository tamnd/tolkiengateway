**Zane David Weiner** ([March 11](../_/11_March.md), [1963](../_/1963.md)) served as a unit production manager for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) and was a producer for [*The Hobbit* (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).

## Roles {#Roles}

- **[2013](../_/2013.md)**: *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md)* - One of the "<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Laketown_Spies&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Laketown Spies (page does not exist)">Laketown Spies</a>"

## Awards {#Awards}

- [2004](../_/2004.md) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.producersguild.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">PGA Award</a></b>, Outstanding Directorial Achievement in Motion Pictures, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*
- [2014](../_/2014.md) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.empireonline.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Empire Award</a></b>, Best Sci-Fi/Fantasy, *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md)*
- [2014](../_/2014.md) - <b> <a class="external text" href="http://heartlandfilm.org/festival/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Heartland Film Festival</a></b>, Truly Moving Picture Award, *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md)*
- 2014 - <b> <a class="external text" href="http://www.producersguild.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">PGA Awards</a></b>, Vanguard Award, [*The Hobbit* (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)
