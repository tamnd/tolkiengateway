**Celegorm** was a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") prince and one of the [seven sons](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor") of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor). Along with all the other sons of Fëanor, he swore the [Oath of Fëanor](/wiki/Oath_of_F%C3%ABanor), joined the [exile of the Noldor](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor"), and settled in [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand).

Celegorm was a great huntsman, and was a friend of the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB). From Oromë he learned great skill of birds and beasts, and could understand a number of their languages. He was further given by Oromë the great hound [Huan](/wiki/Huan).

Celegorm was almost always in the company of his brother [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin), and the pair are most notable for their role as antagonists in the story *[Of Beren and Lúthien](/wiki/Of_Beren_and_L%C3%BAthien)* where they attempted to usurp the kingdom of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), and to force [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) into marriage to coerce a political alliance with the kingdom of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).

Their misdeeds resulted in weakening the [Union of Maedhros](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros), which contributed to the disastrous outcome of the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad). They were eventually killed when the sons of Fëanor [attacked Doriath](/wiki/Second_Kinslaying "Second Kinslaying") in an effort to claim the [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) held there.

## History {#History}

### Early life and exile {#Early_life_and_exile}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:ToastedBuckwheat_-_Grandson_of_M%C3%ADriel.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/f/f0/ToastedBuckwheat_-_Grandson_of_M%C3%ADriel.jpg/300px-ToastedBuckwheat_-_Grandson_of_M%C3%ADriel.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="434" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/f/f0/ToastedBuckwheat_-_Grandson_of_M%C3%ADriel.jpg/450px-ToastedBuckwheat_-_Grandson_of_M%C3%ADriel.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/f/f0/ToastedBuckwheat_-_Grandson_of_M%C3%ADriel.jpg/600px-ToastedBuckwheat_-_Grandson_of_M%C3%ADriel.jpg 2x" data-file-width="900" data-file-height="1303"/></a><figcaption><i>Grandson of Míriel</i> by ToastedBuckwheat</figcaption></figure>

Celegorm was a Noldorin prince, the third of the seven sons of Fëanor and [Nerdanel](/wiki/Nerdanel), born in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) during the Time of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees). Celegorm was a hunter, and a friend of Oromë. He often followed the Oromë’s horn<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and, while his father and brothers were frequently guests at the [halls of Aulë](/wiki/Halls_of_Aul%C3%AB "Halls of Aulë"), Celegorm instead went to the [house of Oromë](/wiki/House_of_Orom%C3%AB "House of Oromë") where he obtained great knowledge of birds and beasts and learned all their tongues.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Oromë further gifted the great wolfhound Huan to Celegorm.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Elena_Kukanova_-_The_Years_of_Youth.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/f/fc/Elena_Kukanova_-_The_Years_of_Youth.jpg/300px-Elena_Kukanova_-_The_Years_of_Youth.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="151" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/f/fc/Elena_Kukanova_-_The_Years_of_Youth.jpg/450px-Elena_Kukanova_-_The_Years_of_Youth.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/f/fc/Elena_Kukanova_-_The_Years_of_Youth.jpg/600px-Elena_Kukanova_-_The_Years_of_Youth.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="516"/></a><figcaption><i>The Years of Youth</i> by Elena Kukanova</figcaption></figure>

During the unrest of the Noldor, Fëanor forged "fell swords" for his sons,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and they followed him to [Formenos](/wiki/Formenos) when he was banished from [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion).<sup id="cite_ref-Unrest_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Unrest-5">[5]</a></sup> When [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) killed [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB) and stole Fëanor's beloved [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils), they all took the terrible [Oath of Fëanor](/wiki/Oath_of_F%C3%ABanor) to recover the jewels, participated in the [First Kinslaying](/wiki/First_Kinslaying) at [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB) to obtain the [Falmari](/wiki/Falmari) ships, and abandoned the host of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) when they sailed to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-6">[6]</a></sup>

From their landing place at the [Firth of Drengist](/wiki/Firth_of_Drengist), Fëanor's host traveled north and encamped on the northern shore of [Lake Mithrim](/wiki/Lake_Mithrim) where they were attacked unaware by a host of Morgoth. In [the battle that followed](/wiki/Dagor-nuin-Giliath "Dagor-nuin-Giliath"), Fëanor's forces were swiftly victorious, and pursued the routed orcs over the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Mountains_of_Shadow" title="Mountains of Shadow">Mountains of Shadow</a> into [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen).<sup id="cite_ref-Giliath_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Giliath-7">[7]</a></sup>

Other armies of Morgoth, which had been besieging the [Havens of the Falas](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas), came north in an attempt to aid the routed forces, but they were intercepted by Celegorm and a part of the Elven-host at the hills near [Eithel Sirion](/wiki/Eithel_Sirion) and driven into the [Fen of Serech](/wiki/Fen_of_Serech).<sup id="cite_ref-Giliath_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Giliath-7">[7]</a></sup>

Though Morgoth's assaulting forces were destroyed,<sup id="cite_ref-Giliath_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Giliath-7">[7]</a></sup> Fëanor recklessly drew far ahead of his host in pursuit of the remnant orcs, and was ambushed and mortally wounded at the confines of [Dor Daedeloth](/wiki/Dor_Daedeloth). He was rescued by his sons, and just before his death he laid it on them to uphold their oath and to avenge him. Shortly after, [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) was taken captive after feigning to treat with Morgoth.<sup id="cite_ref-S|Return_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S|Return-8">[8]</a></sup>

The host of Fëanor remained at northern shore of Lake Mithrim until the arrival of Fingolfin's host with [first sunrise](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [1](/wiki/First_Age_1 "First Age 1") which prompted them to relocate to the southern shore of Lake Mithrim to avoid open hostilities with the larger host of Fingolfin.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [5](/wiki/First_Age_5 "First Age 5") Maedhros was rescued by [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and waived his claim of [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor) to Fingolfin before relocating the House of Fëanor to [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand)<sup id="cite_ref-S|Return_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S|Return-8">[8]</a></sup> in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [7](/wiki/First_Age_7 "First Age 7").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

### Establishment in Beleriand {#Establishment_in_Beleriand}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Marya_Filatova_-_Celegorm_in_Himlad.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/c/cf/Marya_Filatova_-_Celegorm_in_Himlad.jpg/300px-Marya_Filatova_-_Celegorm_in_Himlad.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="150" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/c/cf/Marya_Filatova_-_Celegorm_in_Himlad.jpg/450px-Marya_Filatova_-_Celegorm_in_Himlad.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/c/cf/Marya_Filatova_-_Celegorm_in_Himlad.jpg/600px-Marya_Filatova_-_Celegorm_in_Himlad.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1200" data-file-height="599"/></a><figcaption><i>Celegorm in Himlad</i> by Marya Filatova</figcaption></figure>

Celegorm and his brother Curufin held and fortified the realm of [Himlad](/wiki/Himlad), including the [Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Pass_of_Aglon). This was situated between King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol)'s realm of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), Maedhros' realm at [Himring](/wiki/Himring), and [Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l)'s realm of [Nan Elmoth](/wiki/Nan_Elmoth).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [316](/wiki/First_Age_316 "First Age 316"), while Celegorm and Curufin were riding with [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir) in [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion), his cousin [Aredhel](/wiki/Aredhel) arrived at Himlad.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-13">[13]</a></sup> Before they return, Aredhel disappeared, having unknowingly crossing into Nam Elmoth during her restless wandering. Celegorm and Aredhel never shown or said to meet again before her death.

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455") Morgoth broke the [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband) with a [massive assault](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach "Dagor Bragollach") against the Noldor. Celegorm and Curufin held strong forces, with many horsed archers, behind Aglon, but they were overthrown<sup id="cite_ref-GA|148_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA|148-14">[14]</a></sup> and the [Pass of Aglon was forced](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pass_of_Aglon "Battle of the Pass of Aglon"), though with great cost to the hosts of Morgoth.<sup id="cite_ref-Fingolfin_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingolfin-15">[15]</a></sup> Celegorm and Curufin hardly escaped, and passed westward along the north borders of Doriath with such mounted following as they could save, and came at length to the [vale of Sirion](/wiki/Vale_of_Sirion "Vale of Sirion").<sup id="cite_ref-GA|148_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA|148-14">[14]</a></sup>

### Overpowerful guests in Nargothrond {#Overpowerful_guests_in_Nargothrond}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/4/45/Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg/300px-Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="410" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/4/45/Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg/450px-Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/4/45/Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg/600px-Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2882" data-file-height="3934"/></a><figcaption>&#34;Tyelkormo and Curufinwe&#34; by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Tuuliky" title="Category:Images by Tuuliky">Tuuliky</a></figcaption></figure>

Following Morgoth's victories against the House of Finarfin, and scattering of the people of Fëanor, he sent his greatest servant [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) to assault [Minas Tirith](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand)> "Minas Tirith (Beleriand)") on [Tol Sirion](/wiki/Tol_Sirion) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [457](/wiki/First_Age_457 "First Age 457").<sup id="cite_ref-Fingolfin_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingolfin-15">[15]</a></sup> Sauron was able to besiege and take the fortress, driving [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) out where he would have been slain if not for the arrival of Curufin and Celegorm with their riders and other forces they could gather. They fought fiercely, allowing Orodreth to escape to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), before they too had to fall back and join him.<sup id="cite_ref-Orodreth_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Orodreth-16">[16]</a></sup> The people of the city harboured their Fëanorian brethren,<sup id="cite_ref-Fingolfin_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingolfin-15">[15]</a></sup> and the grief between Houses [of Fëanor](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor "House of Fëanor") and [of Finarfin](/wiki/House_of_Finarfin "House of Finarfin") was put aside for a time.<sup id="cite_ref-Orodreth_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Orodreth-16">[16]</a></sup> While there, Celegorm and Curufin showed their cousin [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod), King of Nargothrond, friendship in every need.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-17">[17]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [465](/wiki/First_Age_465 "First Age 465"), [Beren](/wiki/Beren) came to Nargothrond to ask for aid in his [quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril "Quest for the Silmaril") as Finrod had previously sworn an oath to Beren's father [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir) to render whatsoever service was asked in hour of need to him or to any of his kin.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> Bound by this oath, Finrod summoned his people and declared that he would aid Beren and requested the help of his chieftains. Celegorm and Curufin, their own Oath now reawakened, both spoke against Finrod. Celegorm, drawing his sword, spoke first and declared that the Silmarils belonged only to Fëanor's sons, and that even should the quest be achieved they would slay any that kept the Silmaril or gave it to any hands but their own. Curufin spoke next, more softly but with no less power, of war and the ruin of Nargothrond, and instilled such fear in the hearts of the Elves that they refused to obey their king or go into open battle until the time of Túrin, forgetting all bonds of kinship. In their hearts, Celegorm and Curufin thought to send Finrod to his death so that they could usurp his throne. Finrod, forsaken, then gave his crown to Orodreth to govern in his stead and left with Beren and only ten other companions. With Finrod gone, Celegorm and Curufin further swayed the hearts of the people of Nargothrond, and Orodreth had no power to withstand them. The ultimate goal of the brothers was to take control of all Elf-kingdoms before then seeking the Silmarils.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-17">[17]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Mysilvergreen_-_Luthien_and_Celegorm_in_Nargothrond.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/e/e7/Mysilvergreen_-_Luthien_and_Celegorm_in_Nargothrond.jpg/300px-Mysilvergreen_-_Luthien_and_Celegorm_in_Nargothrond.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="234" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/e/e7/Mysilvergreen_-_Luthien_and_Celegorm_in_Nargothrond.jpg/450px-Mysilvergreen_-_Luthien_and_Celegorm_in_Nargothrond.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/e/e7/Mysilvergreen_-_Luthien_and_Celegorm_in_Nargothrond.jpg/600px-Mysilvergreen_-_Luthien_and_Celegorm_in_Nargothrond.jpg 2x" data-file-width="900" data-file-height="701"/></a><figcaption><i>Luthien and Celegorm in Nargothrond</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Mysilvergreen" title="Category:Images by Mysilvergreen">Mysilvergreen</a></figcaption></figure>

While hunting Sauron's [wolves](/wiki/Wolves "Wolves") in the [Guarded Plain](/wiki/Guarded_Plain), Celegorm, Curufin, and Huan came across [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien), daughter of [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) the King of Doriath, searching for Beren. Feigning to help her, they brought her to Nargothrond and then kept her captive. Celegorm had become enamored with her, and intended to wed Lúthien to coerce Thingol into an alliance.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

With the aid of Huan, however, Lúthien escaped and the two managed to rescue Beren along with many Elf prisoners from Sauron. When these Elves, along with Huan, returned to Nargothrond the people learned of Finrod's death and Lúthien's great deeds and turned against the brothers, now perceiving their motives as treachery. Returned to power, Orodreth would not let Celegorm and Curufin be slain, and instead expelled them from Nargothrond. None went with them; even Curufin's son, Celebrimbor, repudiated his deeds and remained behind. Huan, however, still followed his master Celegorm.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-17">[17]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Beren%27s_Leap.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/c/c2/Ted_Nasmith_-_Beren%27s_Leap.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Beren%27s_Leap.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="203" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/c/c2/Ted_Nasmith_-_Beren%27s_Leap.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="450" data-file-height="304"/></a><figcaption><i>Beren&#39;s Leap</i> by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

Leaving Nargothrond, the two brothers rode towards [Himring](/wiki/Himring) where Maedhros dwelt, and encountered Lúthien and Beren on the way. While Celegorm charged at Beren, Curufin, being a strong and cunning horseman, captured Lúthien. As Curufin passed by, he was caught from behind at the throat by the [Leap of Beren](/wiki/Leap_of_Beren), and hurled to the ground. Beren then proceeded to throttle Curufin while Celegorm was held at bay by Huan who had finally forsook the service of Celegorm. Lúthien forbid Beren to slay Curufin, so instead Beren stripped Curufin of his gear and weapons, including his knife Angrist, before flinging him away. Beren further claimed Curufin's horse, bidding Curufin to walk back to his kinsfolk. Curufin cursed Beren to "Go hence unto a swift and bitter death" before joining Celegorm on his horse and they made as if to ride away. Filled with shame and malice, Curufin took Celegorm's bow and shot two arrows at Lúthien; Huan caught the first in his mouth while the second hit Beren in the breast when he sprang in front of Lúthien. Huan then pursued Celegorm and Curufin, and they fled in fear.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_17-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-17">[17]</a></sup>

### Later deeds in Beleriand {#Later_deeds_in_Beleriand}

The evil deeds of Celegorm and Curufin impeded the [Union of Maedhros](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros) against Morgoth. Because of them, Thingol and Orodreth would not join save some token forces that would not be restrained.<sup id="cite_ref-Nirnaeth_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nirnaeth-20">[20]</a></sup> Additionally, the wounding of Beren caused only half of the [House of Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth) that dwelt in [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) to come forth, and they joined [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) and [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) in the west instead of Maedhros.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> In retaliation, Celegorm and Curufin vowed openly to slay Thingol and destroy his people if they were victorious and the Silmaril (now in Thingol's possession) was not then freely surrendered to them.<sup id="cite_ref-Nirnaeth_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nirnaeth-20">[20]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [472](/wiki/First_Age_472 "First Age 472") the Union of Maedhros launched their [disastrous assault on Morgoth](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad "Nirnaeth Arnoediad"). During the battle, their host was scattered and all of the sons of Fëanor were wounded, though none slain. Gathering a remnant of their forces, they managed to hew their way out, escaping towards [Mount Dolmed](/wiki/Mount_Dolmed). In the aftermath of the battle, the sons of Fëanor were greatly diminished, and took to a woodland life, mingling with the [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves) of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand).<sup id="cite_ref-Nirnaeth_20-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nirnaeth-20">[20]</a></sup>

The sons of Fëanor learned of the [F.A. 495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495") [Fall of Nargothrond](/wiki/Fall_of_Nargothrond), and while Maedhros was dismayed, Celegorm and Curufin were not displeased.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup>

[A Silmaril](/wiki/Nauglamir "Nauglamir") was in the possession of Thingol's heir, King [Dior](/wiki/Dior) and Celegorm stirred his brothers to [attack Doriath](/wiki/Second_Kinslaying "Second Kinslaying") to seize it. In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [506](/wiki/First_Age_506 "First Age 506") the Sons of Fëanor attacked and Celegorm died at the hands of Dior himself, before he was also killed. His brothers Curufin and Caranthir were also killed in this battle.<sup id="cite_ref-doriath_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-doriath-23">[23]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Celegorm* is not glossed, but being the [Sindarized](/wiki/Sindarized) version of the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name *Tyelkormo*, it would mean "Hasty-riser".<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-24">[24]</a></sup> It is actually [North Sindarin](/wiki/North_Sindarin), as it keeps the final *m*.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup>

In the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) phase of the name, it was rendered as <i><b>Celegorn</b></i>, presumably meaning "Swift-impetuous".<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[26]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Celegorm's [father-name](/wiki/Father-name "Father-name") was <i><b>Turcafinwë</b></i> ("Strong, powerful (in body) Finwë"), being <i><b>Turko</b></i> the short form.<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_24-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-24">[24]</a></sup>

His [mother-name](/wiki/Amiless%C3%AB "Amilessë") was <i><b>Tyelkormo</b></i> ("Hasty-riser"), a reference to his quick temper and his habit of leaping up when angered. *[Tyelka](/wiki/Tyelka)* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "hasty".<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_24-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-24">[24]</a></sup> An earlier form of the Quenya version of *Celegorm* was <i><b>Celecormë</b></i>, related with *[Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB)*.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[27]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier drafts, both Celegorm and Curufin present in Himald when Aredhel arrived. She expressed to them that she escaped from [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) of her own will and was glad to dwell with them and be free. The brothers considered sending a message to her brother King [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) to inform him, but delayed as she was happy, the route was perilous, and Turgon would have demanded her immediate return.

In [The Nauglafring](/wiki/The_Nauglafring), from the [second volume](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two "The Book of Lost Tales Part Two") of *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)*, he is "pierced with a hundred arrows" and dies beside Cranthor (Caranthir) in the attack on Dior's realm.<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[28]</a></sup>

In [Lay of Leithian Canto X](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_X), from the [third volume](/wiki/The_Lays_of_Beleriand "The Lays of Beleriand") of *The History of Middle-earth*, it is Celegorm, instead of Curufin, who fires the two arrows at Lúthien. His weapons are described as a "bow of yew with gold wire bound" and "a dwarvish dart and cruelly hooked"<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29">[29]</a></sup>

Celegorm's hair color is often referred to as being blonde in early versions of the legendarium. In the [Quenta Silmarillion](</wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road)> "Quenta Silmarillion (Lost Road)"), from the [fifth volume](/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings "The Lost Road and Other Writings") of *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)*, Christopher Tolkien summarizes as follows:<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">[30]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The QS version(s) of ‘Beren and Lúthien’ are thus to be found in chapter 19 of the published work, and are not given here; but significant points in which the QS text(s) were altered editorially must be mentioned. I list these in order of their occurrence, with references to the pages of The Silmarillion (hardback edition).
...
Then Celegorm arose amid the throng (p. 169). In QS this is followed by ‘golden was his long hair’. In the Lay at this point (line 1844) Celegorm has ‘gleaming hair’; his Old English name was Cynegrim Fægerfeax (‘Fair-hair’), IV. 213. The phrase was removed in The Silmarillion text on account of the dark hair of the Noldorin princes other than in ‘the golden house of Finarfin’ (see I. 44); but he remains ‘Celegorm the fair’ in The Silmarillion p. 60.
</p>
</blockquote>
