**Challenge Decks** was a line of 10 preconstructed decks for [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game) products. Each deck consisted of 110 grey-bordered cards.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><b>We&#39;ve Built Some Killer Decks!</b>
</p><p>Each of the ten pre-constructed 110 card decks:
</p>
<ul><li>Themed to one of five Wizards or five Ringwraiths</li>
<li>Contains 3 rare cards from the MECCG series</li>
<li>Features sites, hazards, resources, characters, and a sideboard</li>
<li>Tuned for competitive or tournament play</li>
<li>Includes cards from <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Wizards" title="Middle-earth: The Wizards">Middle-earth: the Wizards</a>™ thru <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_White_Hand" title="Middle-earth: The White Hand">Middle-earth: the White Hand</a>™</li>
<li>Available early Spring ’98</li></ul>
</blockquote>
