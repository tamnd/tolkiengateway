**Chithing** was the fourth month of the year according to the calendars of [Bree](/wiki/Bree) and the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing), approximately equivalent to modern April. It corresponds to [Astron](/wiki/Astron) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Chithing* represents *ciðing* "germinating".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
