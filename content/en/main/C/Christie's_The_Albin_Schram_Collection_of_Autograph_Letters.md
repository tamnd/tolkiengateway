<i><b>Christie&#39;s The Albin Schram Collection of Autograph Letters</b></i> is an auction catalogue which includes extracts (lot 187, p. 115) from a two page letter from [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md) (see *[Letter to Pauline Baynes](../L/Letter_to_Pauline_Baynes.md)*). The second page of the letter is reproduced in a photograph.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
