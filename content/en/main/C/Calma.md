<i><b>calma</b></i> is a noun meaning "lamp" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/C/Calma.gif.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/4/49/Calma.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Calma* is also the name in Quenya of the third letter of the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the third letter of the first grade or [Tyellë](../T/Tyellë.md), which contains "voiceless stops" and it is the first letter in the third series of consonants. For written Quenya or [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), the third series is used for the *k*-series, the *[Calmatéma](Calmatéma.md)* and *Calma* represents **K** or "hard" **C**. In [Westron](../W/Westron.md) the third series is used for a variety of consonantal sounds and this letter represents **CH** as in "church".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Ché</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [KAL](../K/KAL.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
