**Celandine Brandybuck** was the third and youngest child of [Seredic Brandybuck](../S/Seredic_Brandybuck.md) and [Hilda Bracegirdle](../H/Hilda_Bracegirdle.md).

## History {#History}

Celandine was descended from the line of the [Masters of the Hall of Buckland](../M/Master_of_Buckland.md): specifically, from [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](../G/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck.md). Along with her parents and her brothers [Doderic](../D/Doderic_Brandybuck.md) and [Ilberic](../I/Ilberic_Brandybuck.md), she was present at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celandine" title="wikipedia:Celandine">Celandine</a> is the name of three different flowers (two poppies and one buttercup). The name derives from the Greek, via Latin, *χελιδώνι* (*chelidoni*) meaning "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/swallow" title="wikipedia:swallow">swallow</a>".
