**Celandine Brandybuck** was the third and youngest child of [Seredic Brandybuck](/wiki/Seredic_Brandybuck) and [Hilda Bracegirdle](/wiki/Hilda_Bracegirdle).

## History {#History}

Celandine was descended from the line of the [Masters of the Hall of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland "Master of Buckland"): specifically, from [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck). Along with her parents and her brothers [Doderic](/wiki/Doderic_Brandybuck "Doderic Brandybuck") and [Ilberic](/wiki/Ilberic_Brandybuck "Ilberic Brandybuck"), she was present at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celandine" title="wikipedia:Celandine">Celandine</a> is the name of three different flowers (two poppies and one buttercup). The name derives from the Greek, via Latin, *χελιδώνι* (*chelidoni*) meaning "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/swallow" title="wikipedia:swallow">swallow</a>".
