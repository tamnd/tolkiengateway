## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This is the true story of C.S. Lewis - one of the greatest writers of the twentieth century - whose books for children and adults have become much-loved classics.<br/>
Part of the story of C.S. Lewis has been made famous through the film &#39;Shadowlands&#39;. Here this fascinating man&#39;s entire life-story is told by those who knew him personally.<br/>
C.S. Lewis was born in Belfast in 1898 and was sent to England for a public school education with his elder brother, Warren. Lewis exhibited a genius for imagination and perception from his earliest years. Brought up in a Christian household, Lewis lost his faith in his teenage years but was to regain it, with reluctance, as a fellow at Magdalen College, Oxford. His faith subsequently influenced his writings. He became a vigorous champion of the Christian faith through classics such as Mere Christianity and through his BBC broadcasts.<br/>
His Chronicles of Narnia became children&#39;s classics and he was deluged with correspondence from his young readers.<br/>
In his latter years he unexpectedly fell deeply in love with a divorced American, Joy Greshem, and married her, only to suffer the devastation of her death a few years later. C.S. Lewis died in 1963 at his home in Oxford.<br/>
During his lifetime C.S. Lewis suggested to his friend, Roger Lancelyn Green, who was a fellow English scholar, that he would undertake his biography one day. After Lewis&#39;s death in 1963 Lancelyn Green and Walter Hooper were approached by several of Lewis&#39;s friends, to write the biography. Warren Lewis, brother to Jack, contributed a great deal to the writing. The authors had at their disposal a vast collection of letters and diaries, as well as the recollections of many surviving family members and friends.<br/>
Walter Hooper has enhanced the original text with additional material to provide a new, expanded edition which all C.S. Lewis fans will be keen to own.
</p>
</blockquote>
