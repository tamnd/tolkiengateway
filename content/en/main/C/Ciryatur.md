**Ciryatur** was the commander of the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") fleet sent by [Minastir](/wiki/Tar-Minastir "Tar-Minastir"), the [heir](/wiki/King%27s_Heir "King's Heir")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Delay_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Delay-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> of Queen [Tar-Telperiën](/wiki/Tar-Telperi%C3%ABn), to aid [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) in the [War of the Elves and Sauron](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron), which arrived in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1700](/wiki/Second_Age_1700 "Second Age 1700").<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-4">[3]</a></sup>

Ciryatur had divided his forces and sent a great part of them directly to<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-4">[3]</a></sup> [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> When the fleet arrived in Lindon, Gil-galad’s [Elves](/wiki/Elves) had been beaten back to the river [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) and were holding the line of the river together with a Númenórean force that had been stationed on the shores of Lindon earlier after Gil-Galad feared that Sauron would invade Eriador. Reinforced by the fresh forces from Númenor the Elves of Lindon and the Númenoreans defeated the forces of Sauron at the river Lhûn and drove them back to the south-east. The forces of Sauron were further decimated in a battle at the [Sarn Ford](/wiki/Sarn_Ford) at the river [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin).<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-4">[3]</a></sup>

Sauron was driven further to the south-east to [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad) at the crossings of the river [Gwathló](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3) where he was reinforced by more of his troops that had come at his orders from the south-east. But Ciryatur had sent a strong force to the small Númenórean harbour of [Vinyalondë](/wiki/Vinyalond%C3%AB)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> at the mouth of the [Gwathló](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3). This second Númenórean force appeared in the rear of Sauron's army at the Gwathló. In the ensuing [Battle of the Gwathló](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Gwathl%C3%B3), the host of Sauron was completely routed and Sauron himself barely escaped. A final battle in eastern [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) stripped Sauron of all but a tiny bodyguard. The Dark Lord retreated into [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and plotted vengeance upon the Númenóreans.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ciryatur* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests that it means "Ship Master", being a compound of *[cirya](/wiki/Cirya "Cirya")* ("ship") + suffix *[tur](/wiki/Tur "Tur")* ("master")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> — an appropriate name for an admiral. It is in fact possible that this is the name of his position rather than his real name (cf. [Ohtar](/wiki/Ohtar)).
