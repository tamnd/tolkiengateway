**Curufin** was a [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) prince and one of the [seven sons](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md). Along with the other sons of Fëanor, he swore the [Oath of Fëanor](../O/Oath_of_Fëanor.md), joined the [Exile of the Noldor](../E/Exile_of_the_Noldor.md), and settled in [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md).

He was Fëanor's favorite son, and resembled him greatly in skill, temperament, and appearance. He was also the father of [Celebrimbor](Celebrimbor.md), master [jewel-smith](../G/Gwaith-i-Mírdain.md) of [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md), who forged the [three](../T/Three_Rings.md) Elvish [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md).

Curufin was almost always in the company of his brother [Celegorm](Celegorm.md), and the pair are most notable for their role as antagonists in the story of *[Beren and Lúthien](../B/Beren_and_Lúthien.md)* where they made foiled attempts to usurp the kingdom of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md), force [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) into marriage to coerce a political alliance with the kingdom of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), and even attacked [Beren](../B/Beren.md) and Lúthien directly.

Their misdeeds resulted in the weakening of the [Union of Maedhros](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md), which contributed to the disastrous outcome of the fifth battle of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md) (*Battle of Unnumbered Tears*).

Curufin, alongside his brothers Celegorm and [Caranthir](Caranthir.md), was eventually killed when he and the rest of his brothers [attacked Doriath](../S/Second_Kinslaying.md) in an effort to reclaim the [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md) held there.

## History {#History}

### Life in Valinor {#Life_in_Valinor}

**Curufin** was a Noldorin prince, one of the seven sons of Fëanor and [Nerdanel](../N/Nerdanel.md), born in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) during the Time of the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md). He was Fëanor's favorite son, and he greatly resembled Fëanor in temper, talents, and appearance.<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-2">[2]</a></sup> At some point he married and had a son, Celebrimbor.

Fëanor and his sons abode seldom in one place for long, but travelled far and wide upon the confines of Valinor, going even to the borders of the Dark and the cold shores of the [Outer Sea](../O/Outer_Sea.md), seeking the unknown. Often they were guests in the halls of [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-S|Princes_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S|Princes-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [unrest of the Noldor](../U/Unrest_of_the_Noldor.md) and the strife between Fëanor and his half-brother [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md), Fëanor was banished from [Tirion](../T/Tirion.md) and took up residence in [Formenos](../F/Formenos.md); he was followed by his father, King [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md), and his sons.<sup id="cite_ref-Unrest_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Unrest-4">[4]</a></sup>

Along with Finwë, the sons of Fëanor did not attend the [high feast](../H/High_feast.md) and were out riding when Formenos was attacked by [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md) and [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md) after the pair had [killed the Two Trees](../D/Darkening_of_Valinor.md). They attempted to return, but as they approached they were thrown from their horses and lay upon their faces, blind and without strength. Once the cloud passed and they could move again, they entered Formenos and found Finwë dead, the house ravaged, and the Silmarils stolen.

They then sped to the [Ring of Doom](../R/Ring_of_Doom.md) where [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) informed [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) and, unknowingly, also Fëanor who kept himself hidden during the conversation. Fëanor reacted by cursing both Melkor and the summons of Manwë, and then fleeing into the night; the sons of Fëanor followed him, dismayed that he might slay himself.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Fëanor and his sons soon returned to Tirion where during a [great speech by Fëanor](../S/Speech_of_Fëanor.md), they took a [terrible and blasphemous oath](../O/Oath_of_Fëanor.md) of enmity and vengeance against all or any who dares to claim any part or right in the Silmarils, and led most of the Noldor into exile.

To obtain the ships needed to pursue Morgoth into [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), Fëanor's host seized them from the [Falmari](../F/Falmari.md), [killing many when they resisted](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md), and thus came under the [Doom of Mandos](../D/Doom_of_Mandos.md).

After a long march northwards, the Noldor drew close to the [Helcaraxë](../H/Helcaraxë.md), a land bridge deemed impassable, and the narrow strait between Aman and Middle-earth they intended to sail. With too few ships remaining to ferry all the Noldor at once, and the fear of treachery between their houses, Fëanor and his sons stole away in them, abandoning the host of Fingolfin.<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-6">[6]</a></sup>

While Curufin's son Celebrimbor joined him in exile, his wife had refused to take part in the rebellion of Fëanor and remained in Aman with the people of [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-DwarvesNotes_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DwarvesNotes-7">[7]</a></sup>

### Return to Middle-earth {#Return_to_Middle-earth}

After landing at the [Firth of Drengist](../F/Firth_of_Drengist.md), and burning the ships to prevent any from sailing back, Fëanor's host traveled north and encamped on the northern shore of [Lake Mithrim](../L/Lake_Mithrim.md) where they were attacked unaware by a host of Morgoth. In [the battle that followed](../D/Dagor-nuin-Giliath.md), Fëanor's forces were swiftly victorious, and pursued the routed orcs over the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../M/Mountains_of_Shadow.md" title="Mountains of Shadow">Mountains of Shadow</a> into [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-8">[8]</a></sup>

Though Morgoth's assaulting forces were destroyed, Fëanor recklessly drew far ahead of his host in pursuit of the remnant orcs, and was ambushed and mortally wounded at the confines of [Dor Daedeloth](../D/Dor_Daedeloth.md). He was rescued by his sons, and just before his death he laid it on them to uphold their oath and to avenge him. Shortly after, Maedhros was taken captive after feigning to treat with Morgoth.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-8">[8]</a></sup>

The host of Fëanor remained at northern shore of Lake Mithrim until the arrival of Fingolfin's host, having successfully crossed the Helcaraxë, with the [first sunrise](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [1](../F/First_Age_1.md). To avoid open hostilities with Fingolfin's larger forces, the remaining sons of Fëanor relocated to the southern shore of Lake Mithrim.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

The hatred between the houses began to be assuaged in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [5](../F/First_Age_5.md), when Maedhros was rescued by Fingolfin's son [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md), and two years later the Noldor held a [great common council](Council_in_Mithrim.md) where Fingolfin was chosen to be [overlord of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md). Maedhros subsequently waived his claim to the title, and shortly after relocated the House of Fëanor to [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md). In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [20](../F/First_Age_20.md), Fingolfin held the great [Feast of Reuniting](../M/Mereth_Aderthad.md) where the Elven houses, both Noldor and [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), pledged common cause against Morgoth.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Establishment in Himlad {#Establishment_in_Himlad}

Curufin and his brother Celegorm held and fortified the realm of [Himlad](../H/Himlad.md), including the [Pass of Aglon](../P/Pass_of_Aglon.md). This was situated between King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md)'s realm of Doriath, Maedhros' realm at [Himring](../H/Himring.md), and [Eöl](../E/Eöl.md)'s realm of [Nan Elmoth](../N/Nan_Elmoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

Curufin had a good relationship with the Dwarves,<sup id="cite_ref-Khuzdul_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Khuzdul-12">[12]</a></sup> and he bore the knife [Angrist](../A/Angrist.md), made by the dwarf [Telchar](../T/Telchar.md) of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md), which he wore sheathless by his side.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-13">[13]</a></sup>

Eöl, however, Curufin loved not at all. Curufin was aware of Eöl's resentment towards the Noldor, and that Eöl was using his friendship with the [Dwarves of the Blue Mountains](../D/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains.md), which Curufin was jealous of, to stir up unfriendliness against them. Regardless, Eöl was still allowed to cross through East Beleriand, though a constant watch was placed on Nan Elmoth as they mistrusted his doings and goings.<sup id="cite_ref-Eol_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eol-14">[14]</a></sup>

### Encounters with Aredhel and Eöl {#Encounters_with_Aredhel_and_Eöl}

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [316](../F/First_Age_316.md), while Celegorm and Curufin were riding with [Caranthir](Caranthir.md) in [Thargelion](../T/Thargelion.md), their cousin [Aredhel](../A/Aredhel.md), daughter of Fingolfin, arrived at Himlad. The people of Celegorm welcomed her and bade her stay among them with honour until their lord’s return.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-15">[15]</a></sup>

She expressed to Curufin, and later to Celegorm of whom she was most fond, that she escaped from [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) of her own will and was glad to dwell with them and be free. The brothers considered sending a message to her brother King [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) to inform him, but delayed as the route was extremely perilous, she was happy, and Turgon would have demanded her immediate return. Before they could make up their minds though, Aredhel disappeared, having unknowingly crossed into Nam Elmoth during her restless wandering. It was a long time before they knew what happened to her, but they eventually learned once their scouts starting sighting her visiting the borders of Nan Elmoth, or straying beyond them. They chose not to report this to Turgon to avoid his wrath.<sup id="cite_ref-Eol_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eol-14">[14]</a></sup>

Nearly a century later, in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [400](../F/First_Age_400.md), Curufin's scouts reported to him of Aredhel and [Maeglin](../M/Maeglin.md) crossing through Himlad to the [Fords of Aros](../F/Fords_of_Aros.md) and, suspecting strange deeds, he came south from Aglon and encamped near the Fords himself. His riders soon waylaid Eöl, also now crossing into Himlad, and they brought Eöl to him.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-15">[15]</a></sup> Curufin had long known that Eöl had taken Aredhel to wife and shrewdly guessed that she was now seeking to escape from him at last.<sup id="cite_ref-Eol_14-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eol-14">[14]</a></sup>

Curufin greeted Eöl with insults and open contempt. He greatly desired to slay Eöl, something that was easily within his power, and none would suspect him, or even much miss Eöl at all, but he restrained from doing so. Eldarin law would have deemed this as murder as Eldar were forbidden to slay one another in revenge for any grievance, Eöl had responded to Curufin with courtesy, was not believed to be under the shadow of Morgoth, and was crossing through on an errand of distress, not mischief.<sup id="cite_ref-Eol_14-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eol-14">[14]</a></sup> Curufin ultimately gave Eöl leave to depart and even counseled Eöl to return to his dwelling and cease to pursue those who loved him no longer, or else never to return again.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-15">[15]</a></sup>

### Retreat to Nargothrond {#Retreat_to_Nargothrond}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/V/Victoria_Clare_-_The_Battle_of_Aglon_Pass.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/b2/Victoria_Clare_-_The_Battle_of_Aglon_Pass.jpg/300px-Victoria_Clare_-_The_Battle_of_Aglon_Pass.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="212" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/b2/Victoria_Clare_-_The_Battle_of_Aglon_Pass.jpg/450px-Victoria_Clare_-_The_Battle_of_Aglon_Pass.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/b/b2/Victoria_Clare_-_The_Battle_of_Aglon_Pass.jpg/600px-Victoria_Clare_-_The_Battle_of_Aglon_Pass.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1200" data-file-height="846"/></a><figcaption><i>The Battle of Aglon Pass</i> by <a class="mw-redirect" href="../V/Victoria_Clare.md" title="Victoria Clare">Victoria Clare</a></figcaption></figure>

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [455](../F/First_Age_455.md), Morgoth broke the [Siege of Angband](../S/Siege_of_Angband.md) with a [massive assault](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) against the Noldor. Celegorm and Curufin held strong forces, with many horsed archers, behind Aglon, but they were overthrown<sup id="cite_ref-rout_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rout-16">[16]</a></sup> and the Pass of Aglon was taken, though with great cost to the hosts of Morgoth.<sup id="cite_ref-Fingolfin_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingolfin-17">[17]</a></sup> Celegorm and Curufin hardly escaped, and passed westward along the north borders of Doriath, with such mounted following as they could save, and came at length to the [vale of Sirion](../V/Vale_of_Sirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rout_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rout-16">[16]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [457](../F/First_Age_457.md) Morgoth sent a great force, under the command of his lieutenant [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), to capture the tower [Minis Tirith](<../M/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand).md>), which guarded the [Pass of Sirion](../P/Pass_of_Sirion.md). The fortress was eventually taken and its lord, [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md), was driven out where he would have been slain if not for the intervention of Celegorm and Curufin; they arrived with their riders and other such forces they could gather and fought fiercely, allowing Orodreth to escape and retreat to [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Orodreth_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Orodreth-18">[18]</a></sup>

Shortly after, Celegorm and Curufin also had to withdraw, and they were harboured in Nargothrond gratefully, the griefs between the [House of Finarfin](../H/House_of_Finarfin.md) and House of Fëanor now forgotten.<sup id="cite_ref-Orodreth_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Orodreth-18">[18]</a></sup> The people of Celegorm swelled the strength of Nargothrond<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> and Celegorm and Curufin grew to strong power, leading many of them.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-13">[13]</a></sup> To their cousin [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md), King of Nargothrond, they showed friendship in every need.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_13-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-13">[13]</a></sup>

### The Oath awakened {#The_Oath_awakened}

<p>For the Silmarils are cursed with an oath of hatred, and he that even names them in desire moves a great power from slumber; and the sons of Fëanor would lay all the Elf-kingdoms in ruin rather than suffer any other than themselves to win or possess a Silmaril, for the Oath drives them.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../F/Finrod.md" title="Finrod">Finrod</a>, <i><a href="../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion.md" title="Quenta Silmarillion">Quenta Silmarillion</a></i>, &#34;<a href="../O/Of_Beren_and_Lúthien.md" title="Of Beren and Lúthien">Of Beren and Lúthien</a>&#34;</cite></p>

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [465](../F/First_Age_465.md),<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> [Beren](../B/Beren.md) arrived at Nargothrond seeking aid on his [quest to retrieve a Silmaril](../Q/Quest_for_the_Silmaril.md) from Morgoth to present to King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) of Doriath; this was the bride-price named by Thingol for Beren to wed his daughter, [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md). Finrod's life had previously been saved by Beren's father, [Barahir](../B/Barahir.md), and in gratitude he had sworn an oath of abiding friendship and aid in every need to Barahir and his kin.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_13-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-13">[13]</a></sup>

Bound by this oath Finrod summoned his people and spoke to them of Barahir's deeds in saving him, declared that he would aid Beren, and requested the help of his chieftains. Many of Finrod's people were stirred by his speech to once more go to battle until Celegorm and Curufin, with their own Oath awakened, spoke against it. Celegorm drew his sword and, with flaming eyes, spoke wild and potent words of the wrath that would be incurred on Nargothrond and Doriath should the quest be achieved; the sons of Fëanor would slay any that either kept a Silmaril, or gave one to any hands but their own. Curufin spoke next, more softly but with no less power, of the dread of Morgoth and put such fear into the hearts of the people that they refused open war for the next thirty years until the time of [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> Among Finrod's people only his ten most faithful companions remained willing to join him, and he left his crown to Orodreth to govern in his stead.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_13-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-13">[13]</a></sup> Before departing Finrod had a final terse exchange with Celegorm where he gave a foresight that a Silmaril would be retrieved, but it would never go to the hands of the sons of Fëanor; to other keeping would the bride-price of Lúthien be delivered.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup>

Sometime after, evil [wolves](../W/Wolves.md) started to cross the river [Narog](../N/Narog.md) and entering the [Guarded Plain](../G/Guarded_Plain.md), leading Celegorm and Curufin to hunt them, joined by their riders and [Huan](../H/Huan.md), Celegorm's great hound of Valinor. While on the hunt, Curufin secretly spoke to Celegorm of Orodreth being a "dullard slow" and laid out a plot where they could usurp the throne of Nagrothrond in the event of Finrod's death. With no rumour or tidings of Finrod, Curufin suggested that Celegorm should feign another hunt, but with the actual intent to seek out Finrod; through this they would then either confirm his death, or, if by some grace he was alive and bearing a Silmaril, they could kill him and reclaim it before he returned. Either way, the throne would then be theirs as the eldest house among the Noldor. Celegorm listened, but said nothing.<sup id="cite_ref-Canto8_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canto8-23">[23]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:213</sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/4/45/Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg/300px-Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="410" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/4/45/Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg/450px-Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/4/45/Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg/600px-Tuuliky_-_Tyelkormo_and_Curufinwe.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2882" data-file-height="3934"/></a><figcaption><i>Tyelkormo and Curufinwe</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Tuuliky.md" title="Category:Images by Tuuliky">Tuuliky</a></figcaption></figure>

On the third day of their hunt, they had reached near their borders to the west of Doriath and Huan discovered Lúthien and brought her before Celegorm and Curufin. She revealed who she was and of her escape from Doriath in order to rescue Beren, whom she had learned from the visions of her mother [Melian](../M/Melian.md) was being held prisoner in [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](../T/Tol-in-Gaurhoth.md), Finrod's former fortress of Minas Tirith now occupied by Sauron. Celegorm, immediately enamoured by her beauty, and Curufin both listened, and gave no indication of their awareness of Beren or his quest. Once Lúthien finished, she besought them for their aid. In private counsel, Curufin explained to Celegorm that they now knew that Finrod had been taken prisoner, where the wolves were coming from, and what answer he should give to Lúthien. Celegorm then addressed Lúthien and told her that their hunting party wasn't adequate to assault such a fortress, but that if she came back with them to Nargothrond, they would then devise aid to rescue Beren.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_13-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Canto8_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canto8-23">[23]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:214–6</sup>

Once they returned to Nargothrond however, instead of assisting Lúthien they took her captive. Their hearts had turned to becoming the mightiest of the Noldor princes and making all Elf-kingdoms obedient to the House of Fëanor; only from that position of strength would they then seek the Silmarils. With Finrod to perish in Sauron's dungeons, and Thingol to be coerced into assenting a wedding of Lúthien to Celegorm, they would advance in such power as they would then both usurp Nargothrond and force Doriath into an alliance. Orodreth, though he was aware the purposes of the brothers, was powerless to hinder them as the people of Nargothrond were under their sway and would not heed him.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_13-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-13">[13]</a></sup>

### Designs foiled {#Designs_foiled}

These treacherous plans were soon laid bare. With the aid of Huan, Lúthien escaped and the pair succeeded in rescuing Beren along with many Elf prisoners from Tol-in-Gaurhoth. When these Elves returned to Nargothrond the people learned of Finrod's death and Lúthien's great deeds and now perceived the brothers to be guided by treachery rather than fear of Morgoth in abandoning Finrod. Additionally, Celegorm had previously dispatched secret messages to Doriath to urge Thingol's suit, which also hinted that he would leave Finrod to die and take the throne of Nargothond;<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup> Thingol in wrath had sent their letters back by his own servants to Orodreth.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup>

Though Celegorm attempted to still the clamours of the people, they grew to calls for the brothers to be slain. Brought before Orodreth, Celegorm stood unbowed with scorn and a menacing anger, while Curufin smiled, though wary-eyed and hand on the haft of his knife. Curufin then laughed and, addressing Orodreth as "Sir Steward", noted that they were not accustomed to standing in his hall and requested to know what he sought of them.<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[26]</a></sup> Orodreth, speaking coldly, stated that they now stood before the king and that they were banished from Nargothrond, and there would never again be love between the realm and the sons of Fëanor. Celegorm and Curufin soon departed and they went alone as none of their people would any longer follow them; even Curufin's son, Celebrimbor, repudiated his fathers deeds and remained behind. Huan, however, still followed his master Celegorm.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_13-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-13">[13]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_Beren&#39;s_Leap.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/c2/Ted_Nasmith_-_Beren%27s_Leap.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Beren%27s_Leap.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="203" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c2/Ted_Nasmith_-_Beren%27s_Leap.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="450" data-file-height="304"/></a><figcaption><i>Beren&#39;s Leap</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

Leaving Nargothrond, the two brothers rode towards [Himring](../H/Himring.md) where Maedhros dwelt. They chanced upon Lúthien and Beren on the way, and charged at them. While Celegorm sought to ride Beren down, Curufin grabbed Lúthien and lifted her to his saddle. Curufin was, however, caught from behind at the throat by the [Leap of Beren](../L/Leap_of_Beren.md), and hurled to the ground where Beren then proceeded to throttle him. Celegorm was prevented from assisting Curufin as Huan finally turned from his service and held him at bay. Curufin's life was spared when Lúthien forbid Beren to slay him, so instead Beren stripped Curufin of his gear and weapons, including his knife Angrist, before flinging him away; he further claimed Curufin's horse for the service of Lúthien, telling Curufin to walk back to his kinsfolk. Curufin cursed Beren before joining Celegorm on his horse and they made as if to ride away. Filled with shame and malice though, Curufin took Celegorm's bow and shot two arrows at Lúthien; Huan caught the first in his mouth while the second hit Beren in the breast when he sprang in front of her. Huan then pursued Celegorm and Curufin, and they fled in fear.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_13-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-13">[13]</a></sup>

### Later deeds in Beleriand {#Later_deeds_in_Beleriand}

The evil deeds of Celegorm and Curufin impeded the Union of Maedhros against Morgoth. Because of them, Thingol and Orodreth would not join save some token forces that would not be restrained.<sup id="cite_ref-Nirnaeth_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nirnaeth-27">[27]</a></sup> Additionally, the wounding of Beren caused only half of the [House of Haleth](../H/House_of_Haleth.md) that dwelt in [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md) to come forth, and they joined Fingon and Turgon in the west instead of Maedhros.<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[28]</a></sup> In retaliation, Celegorm and Curufin vowed openly to slay Thingol and destroy his people if they were victorious and the Silmaril (now in Thingol's possession) was not then freely surrendered to them.<sup id="cite_ref-Nirnaeth_27-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nirnaeth-27">[27]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [472](../F/First_Age_472.md), the Union of Maedhros launched their [disastrous assault on Morgoth](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md). During the battle, their host was scattered and all of the sons of Fëanor were wounded, though none slain. Gathering a remnant of their forces, they managed to hew their way out, and escaped towards [Mount Dolmed](../M/Mount_Dolmed.md). In the aftermath of the battle, the sons of Fëanor were greatly diminished, and took to a woodland life, mingling with the [Green-elves](../G/Green-elves.md) of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Nirnaeth_27-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nirnaeth-27">[27]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [503](../F/First_Age_503.md), [Dior](../D/Dior.md), son of Beren and Lúthien, became king of Doriath following Thingol's death in a dispute with the Dwarves over the great treasure, the [Necklace of the Dwarves](../N/Nauglamír.md) bearing a Silmaril, and Doraith's subsequent [ransacking by the Dwarves](../B/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves.md). Dior wore the Silmaril openly, and used its power to restore the realm. In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [505](../F/First_Age_505.md) the sons of Fëanor learned of the Silmaril in Doriath and, initially restrained by Maedhros, sent a message to Dior demanding its return, to which Dior sent no response. In late [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [506](../F/First_Age_506.md), Celegorm roused the brothers and in winter they [attacked Doriath](../S/Second_Kinslaying.md). While the Fëanorian's ended up holding the field, it was a hollow victory: they took heavy losses, including the deaths of Celegorm (by Dior's hand), Curufin, and Caranthir; and the Silmaril escaped, now borne by Dior's daughter [Elwing](../E/Elwing.md).<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29">[29]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

Curufin was Fëanor's favorite son and resembled Fëanor very much in face.<sup id="cite_ref-Favson_30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Favson-30">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:435</sup> He alone showed in some degree the same temper and talents, and was the chief inheritor of his father’s skills.<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31">[31]</a></sup> He was known to be of perilous mood,<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_15-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-15">[15]</a></sup> and his vindictiveness could be seen in his attempt to kill Lúthien after his shame and malice from being bested by Beren,<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_13-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-13">[13]</a></sup> and later being not displeased when learning of the [Fall of Nargothrond](../F/Fall_of_Nargothrond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32">[32]</a></sup>

At the burning of the ships in [Losgar](../L/Losgar.md), Curufin is the only specifically named individual of the "few of those most close to Fëanor in obedience" who was aroused by Fëanor in setting them aflame. Of all the sons of Fëanor, Curufin is the only one noted as not preferring to use his mother-name.<sup id="cite_ref-Favson_30-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Favson-30">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:437–8</sup>

In Beleriand, Curufin, along with Maglor, were the only among Fëanor's sons interested the linguistic changes of the various dialects of [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) and the development of theories concerning [Primitive Eldarin](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) and its known decedents.<sup id="cite_ref-Khuzdul_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Khuzdul-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Favson_30-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Favson-30">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:421–2</sup> He was most interested in [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md), the language of the Dwarves, and it was from him that the [loremasters](../L/Loremaster.md) obtained what knowledge they had of it.<sup id="cite_ref-Khuzdul_12-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Khuzdul-12">[12]</a></sup>

In relation to Celegorm, Christopher Tolkien notes that Curufin is *"the more longheaded schemer, standing behind his brother and prompting him - it is clear ... that Celegorm has some authority — or is felt by Curufin to have some authority — that Curufin lacks"*<sup id="cite_ref-Canto8_23-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canto8-23">[23]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:247</sup> and that Curufin was *"the wickeder (as he was certainly also the cleverer) of the brothers"*.<sup id="cite_ref-Canto10_33-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canto10-33">[33]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:275</sup> A margin note on the passage of their wolf-hunt states that *"it is Curufin who put evil into Celegorm's heart"*.<sup id="cite_ref-Canto8_23-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canto8-23">[23]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:247</sup>

As noted by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), *"The meeting between Eöl and Curufin (if not too long an interruption) is good, since it shows (as is desirable) Curufin, too often the villain (especially in the Tale of Tinúviel), in a better and more honourable light - though still one of dangerous mood and contemptuous speech."*<sup id="cite_ref-Eol_14-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eol-14">[14]</a></sup>

Curufin is described as being a strong and cunning horseman.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_13-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-13">[13]</a></sup>

Several items were known to be in Curufin's possession. While in Valinor, Fëanor, in his secret forge, "tempered fell swords for himself and for his sons", along with "tall helms with plumes of red".<sup id="cite_ref-Unrest_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Unrest-4">[4]</a></sup> Curufin carried the Dwarven knife [Angrist](../A/Angrist.md), forged by Telchar, until it was despoiled from him by Beren along with his other gear, weapons, and coat of mail.<sup id="cite_ref-Canto10_33-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canto10-33">[33]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:237</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Curufin* is the [Sindarized](../S/Sindarized.md) form of *Curufinwë*, being a compound of *[curu](Curu.md)* ("skill") and *fin* (Sindarized "Finwë").<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34">[34]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Curufin had the sobriquet of "the crafty".<sup id="cite_ref-S|Princes_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S|Princes-3">[3]</a></sup>

Curufin's [father-name](../F/Father-name.md) was <i><b><a href="Curufinwë.md" title="Curufinwë">Curufinwë</a></b></i><sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35">[note 1]</a></sup> ("Skillful Finwë"), the same father-name given to Fëanor. Because Curufin was Fëanor's favorite son, he chose to give him the same name. The short form of this name was <i><b>Curvo</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36">[note 2]</a></sup>

His [mother-name](../A/Amilessë.md) was <i><b>Atarincë</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37">[note 3]</a></sup> ("Little Father"),<sup id="cite_ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38">[note 4]</a></sup> referring initially to his physical resemblance to his father, and later also found in his mental likeness.<sup id="cite_ref-Favson_30-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Favson-30">[30]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:436</sup>

In *Ælfwine’s translation of the Quenta into Old English; Old English equivalents of Elvish names*, Curufin's name is given as <i><b>Cyrefinn Facensearo</b></i> and defined as "\[Curufin the Crafty. O.E. cyre 'choice', facen 'deceit, guile, wickedness' (a word of wholly bad meaning); searu 'skill, cunning' (also with bad meaning, 'plot, snare, treachery'); facensearu 'treachery'.\]"<sup id="cite_ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39">[35]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, in the tale of *[The Nauglafring](../T/The_Nauglafring.md)*, Curufin goes as a representative of the sons of Fëanor to Dior to bid him to turn over the Silmaril, set within the [Nauglafring](../N/Nauglafring.md), in his possession. Dior refuses on the grounds that he could not endure such a despoiling of the Nauglafring, to which Curufin then states that the Nauglafring unbroken must then be given over. This rises Dior to anger, who bids Curufin to be gone and to instead seek the other two Silmarils still with Melko (Morgoth). As in *The Silmarillion*, the sons of Fëanor then attack Dior's realm, but instead of Celegorm, Caranthir, and Curufin being killed in the battle, here it is Maglor, Dinithel (Amras), Celegorm, and Cranthor (Caranthir).<sup id="cite_ref-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-40">[36]</a></sup>

In [third volume](../T/The_Lays_of_Beleriand.md) of *The History of Middle-earth*, there are many differences in both the events and portrayals of Celegorm and Curufin from the published *[Silmarillion](../S/Silmarillion.md)*.

- In *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](../T/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md)* Christopher Tolkien summarizes the multiple shifts of the founding of Nargothrond of which include Celegorm and Curufin founding it after the Nirnaeth Arnoediad, and another where they found it after they are routed when Morgoth [breaks the Leaguer of Angband](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), and continue to remain in the east after their host is defeated in the Nirnaeth Arnoediad.<sup id="cite_ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41">[37]</a></sup>
- In *[Lay of Leithian Canto VIII](../L/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_VIII.md)* when Curufin and Celegorm come across Lúthien, it is Curufin instead of Celegorm who is enchanted by her.<sup id="cite_ref-Canto8_23-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canto8-23">[23]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:215–7</sup>
- In *[Lay of Leithian Canto X](../L/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_X.md)* some of their people ("such folk as still them followed") join them in exile from Nargothrond,<sup id="cite_ref-Canto10_33-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canto10-33">[33]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:234</sup> and after their foiled attack on Beren and Lúthien it is Celegorm instead of Curufin who curses Beren and fires the arrows.<sup id="cite_ref-Canto10_33-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canto10-33">[33]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:238</sup>

Christopher Tolkien further notes that the *"subtleties in the relationship between Celegorm and Curufin are passed over in the prose version (The Silmarillion pp. 172–3), and there is no suggestion that Curufin was the more sinister of the pair, and the prime mover in their machinations."*<sup id="cite_ref-Canto8_23-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canto8-23">[23]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:221</sup>

In *[The Earliest Annals of Valinor](../T/The_Earliest_Annals_of_Valinor.md)*, from the [fourth volume](../T/The_Shaping_of_Middle-earth.md) of *The History of Middle-earth*, Orodreth, [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md), and Egnor ([Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md)) are stated to have a great friendship with Celegorm and Curufin in Aman,<sup id="cite_ref-42" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-42">[38]</a></sup> and through that love were even uniquely granted passage to sail with the House of Fëanor after the ships were seized.<sup id="cite_ref-43" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-43">[39]</a></sup>

In *[Maeglin](<../M/Maeglin_(chapter).md>)*, from the [eleventh volume](../T/The_War_of_the_Jewels.md) of *The History of Middle-earth*, Celegorm and Curufin more directly assist Aredhel and Maeglin in their escape from Eöl: Celegorm first provides them with swift horses, and the brothers then waylaid the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) and its ford over [Aros](../A/Aros.md), denying its use to Eöl and delaying him until he escaped them that night in the darkness.<sup id="cite_ref-44" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-44">[40]</a></sup>

In *[The Tale of Years](<../T/The_Tale_of_Years_(The_War_of_the_Jewels).md>)* from the same volume, it was Celegorm and Curufin instead of [Beren](../B/Beren.md) who intercepted and defeated the Dwarves of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) at the [Battle of Sarn Athrad](../B/Battle_of_Sarn_Athrad.md), and were greatly angered to find that a desired Silmaril was not within their treasure looted from the [battle](../B/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves.md) in the [Thousand Caves](../M/Menegroth.md). However, [Christopher Tolkien](Christopher_Tolkien.md) comments that this idea was evidently abandoned by his father, since the older idea of Beren fighting the Dwarves and being aided by the [Ents](../E/Ents.md) appeared again in the [Letter 247](../L/Letter_247.md).<sup id="cite_ref-45" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-45">[41]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-46" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-46">[42]</a></sup>

While in the published *Silmarillion* Curufin is recorded as the fifth son of Fëanor, in *[The Shibboleth of Fëanor](../T/The_Shibboleth_of_Fëanor.md)* he is the fourth.<sup id="cite_ref-Khuzdul_12-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Khuzdul-12">[12]</a></sup>
