In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), **Caras Celairnen** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Town by the Brilliant Water”), also *Gobel Calarnen*, was a town near the junction of the rivers [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) and Uialduin.<sup id="cite_ref-p42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p42-1">[1]</a></sup>

A monastery of Arthadan scholars, named Tol Lamfirith ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Muted-voice Isle”), was located ten miles to the north.<sup id="cite_ref-p43_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p43-2">[2]</a></sup> After the fall of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), it became an important hidden outpost of the [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North).<sup id="cite_ref-p44_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p44-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Early in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the Dwarves of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) established a trading post at the river junction. Their fort was destroyed in aftermath of the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath). A shared need for a river port led to [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) commissioning [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk) to rebuild the settlement as Caras Celairnen.<sup id="cite_ref-p42_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p42-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance), [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) granted Caras Celairnen to King [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(King_of_Arnor)> "Valandil (King of Arnor)") of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), who in turn granted the settlement and its surrounding vale to his son Silivnir, who was married to an Elf. The settlement received a charter that granted it a large degree of autonomy. When the charter was renewed in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_740&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 740 (page does not exist)">740</a>, aligning some of its laws with Arnorian precedent, more Dúnedain moved to Caras Celairnen and became the largest part of its population.<sup id="cite_ref-p42_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p42-1">[1]</a></sup>

Silivnir's descendants continued to rule the vale, though over time the town's council usurped much of their authority.<sup id="cite_ref-p43_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p43-2">[2]</a></sup> When prince [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur)'s fleet arrived at [Mithlond](/wiki/Mithlond), it was found to be too poorly connected, so forces were brought to Caras Celairnen with smaller vessels. With most rulers of Arthedain having perished, a knight of Gondor held the port for twenty years, until another heir of Silivnir was found.<sup id="cite_ref-p44_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p44-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the remainder of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), Caras Celairnen remained a quiet town, mostly notable for its market where Rivermen, Dwarves, and Hobbits traded. Elves slowly withdrew from the port during this time, and ships from Gondor became rarer and rarer.<sup id="cite_ref-p44_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p44-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the early [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") elevated Curudur Silivnirion to a lord of the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom), and the town began to flourish again as goods passed through on their way to [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas).<sup id="cite_ref-p44_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p44-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
