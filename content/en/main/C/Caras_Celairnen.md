In [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md), **Caras Celairnen** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) “Town by the Brilliant Water”), also *Gobel Calarnen*, was a town near the junction of the rivers [Lhûn](../L/Lhûn.md) and Uialduin.<sup id="cite_ref-p42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p42-1">[1]</a></sup>

A monastery of Arthadan scholars, named Tol Lamfirith ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) “Muted-voice Isle”), was located ten miles to the north.<sup id="cite_ref-p43_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p43-2">[2]</a></sup> After the fall of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md), it became an important hidden outpost of the [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md).<sup id="cite_ref-p44_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p44-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Early in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), the Dwarves of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) established a trading post at the river junction. Their fort was destroyed in aftermath of the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md). A shared need for a river port led to [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) commissioning [Durin's folk](../D/Durin's_folk.md) to rebuild the settlement as Caras Celairnen.<sup id="cite_ref-p42_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p42-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md), [Círdan](Círdan.md) granted Caras Celairnen to King [Valandil](<../V/Valandil_(King_of_Arnor).md>) of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md), who in turn granted the settlement and its surrounding vale to his son Silivnir, who was married to an Elf. The settlement received a charter that granted it a large degree of autonomy. When the charter was renewed in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_740&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 740 (page does not exist)">740</a>, aligning some of its laws with Arnorian precedent, more Dúnedain moved to Caras Celairnen and became the largest part of its population.<sup id="cite_ref-p42_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p42-1">[1]</a></sup>

Silivnir's descendants continued to rule the vale, though over time the town's council usurped much of their authority.<sup id="cite_ref-p43_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p43-2">[2]</a></sup> When prince [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md)'s fleet arrived at [Mithlond](../M/Mithlond.md), it was found to be too poorly connected, so forces were brought to Caras Celairnen with smaller vessels. With most rulers of Arthedain having perished, a knight of Gondor held the port for twenty years, until another heir of Silivnir was found.<sup id="cite_ref-p44_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p44-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the remainder of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), Caras Celairnen remained a quiet town, mostly notable for its market where Rivermen, Dwarves, and Hobbits traded. Elves slowly withdrew from the port during this time, and ships from Gondor became rarer and rarer.<sup id="cite_ref-p44_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p44-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the early [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md), King [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) elevated Curudur Silivnirion to a lord of the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md), and the town began to flourish again as goods passed through on their way to [Annúminas](../A/Annúminas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-p44_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p44-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
