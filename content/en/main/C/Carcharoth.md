**Carcharoth** was bred from the foul breed of [Draugluin](/wiki/Draugluin), the first [Werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf), and fed with [elvish](/wiki/Elves "Elves") and [mannish](/wiki/Men "Men") flesh by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) himself. He was the greatest, most powerful wolf to ever live. Carcharoth was set as a guard on the [Gates of Angband](/wiki/Gates_of_Angband), and later he mortally wounded both [Huan](/wiki/Huan), the Hound of Valinor, and [Beren](/wiki/Beren).<sup id="cite_ref-S19_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S19-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After hearing of the Fall of [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth), where Huan had defeated [Draugluin](/wiki/Draugluin) and [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), Morgoth decided to create a werewolf to kill Huan, who could only be killed by the greatest wolf to have ever lived. Carcharoth became involved with the [Quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril) when Beren and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) had to pass him on their way into [Angband](/wiki/Angband). He stopped them, suspicious of Draugluin, (Beren in disguise) as he had heard of Draugluin's death. Lúthien enchanted him with her magic, but on their way out Carcharoth attacked before Lúthien could enchant him again. Beren held out the captured [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) in an attempt to stay the beast, but Carcharoth bit off Beren's hand at the wrist with Silmaril and all.

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Huan%27s_Leap_2.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/7a/Ted_Nasmith_-_Huan%27s_Leap_2.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Huan%27s_Leap_2.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="201" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/7/7a/Ted_Nasmith_-_Huan%27s_Leap_2.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="450" data-file-height="301"/></a><figcaption><i>Huan&#39;s Leap</i> by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

The Silmaril burnt away Carcharoth's insides, and he became crazed with pain. A terror to [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar), Men and [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) alike, he passed south through [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), consumed with an overpowering thirst. From this comes his name of **Anfauglir**, meaning 'Jaws of Thirst'. He arrived in [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) where Beren, King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol), [Beleg Cúthalion](/wiki/Beleg "Beleg") and [Mablung](/wiki/Mablung) joined with [Huan the Hound](/wiki/Huan_the_Hound) in the [Hunting of the Wolf](/wiki/Hunting_of_the_Wolf).

At last, he was discovered within the borders of Doriath itself, where he fought his last fight with Huan: the wolf and the hound took one another's lives in that combat.

Both Beren and Huan were mortally wounded. When Mablung cut open the belly of the beast, he found there the Silmaril with Beren's hand still around it, but when he touched the flesh it was no more.<sup id="cite_ref-S19_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S19-1">[1]</a></sup>

The name is translated as Red Maw;<sup id="cite_ref-S19_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S19-1">[1]</a></sup> the recogniseable [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) elements are *[car(a)n](/wiki/Caran "Caran")* ("red") as well as <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Carach&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Carach (page does not exist)">carach</a></i> ("jaw")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> or <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Carch&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Carch (page does not exist)">carch</a> ("fang").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Based on the above, perhaps the name can be analyzed into the unattested words \**carn* (an alternate version of *caran*) and \**caroth* ("maw"), the latter ending with the augmentative suffix *-oth* also seen in *[Nogoth](/wiki/Nogoth)* "(Big) Dwarf" = *[naug](/wiki/Naug "Naug")* + *oth*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> ("maw" = "big jaw").

Note that the final letter of <i>car(a)<b>n</b></i> + the initial letter of <i><b>c</b>arach/oth-</i> produce the sound **ch** (IPA: <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">x</span>) with the phenomenon called [nasal mutation](/wiki/Nasal_mutation "Nasal mutation").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

His later name *Anfauglir*, is probably composed of Sindarin *[and](/wiki/And "And")* ("long") + *[faug](/wiki/Faug "Faug")* ("thirst") + *lîr* ("jaws").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Originally Carcharoth's name was <i><b>Karkaras</b></i>, the Knife-fang, who was the father of wolves. He was a great grey wolf.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

<i><b>Borosaith</b></i> meaning "Everhungry" is another name for Carcharoth, and it is said to be used in old songs.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

For the creation of Carcharoth, [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) was clearly influenced by the wolf <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fenrir" title="wikipedia:Fenrir">Fenrir</a> in [Norse mythology](/wiki/Norse_mythology), who bit off <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%BDr" title="wikipedia:Týr">Týr</a>'s hand, and was also tormented by ceaseless pains.
