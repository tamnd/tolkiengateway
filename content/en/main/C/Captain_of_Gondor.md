The **Captain of Gondor** was a military title and position of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

Prince [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md) held the title Captain of Gondor leading the [Host of the West](<../H/Host_of_the_West_(Arnor).md>) against [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) in the [Battle of Fornost](../B/Battle_of_Fornost.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

A millennium later, [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) also held the title, as the leader of the [Rangers of Ithilien](../R/Rangers_of_Ithilien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
