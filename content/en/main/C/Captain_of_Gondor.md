The **Captain of Gondor** was a military title and position of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

Prince [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur) held the title Captain of Gondor leading the [Host of the West](</wiki/Host_of_the_West_(Arnor)> "Host of the West (Arnor)") against [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) in the [Battle of Fornost](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

A millennium later, [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) also held the title, as the leader of the [Rangers of Ithilien](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
