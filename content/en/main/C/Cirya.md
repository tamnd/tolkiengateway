<i><b>cirya</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "ship"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or "cleft, pass".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Both meanings come from the [root](../R/Root.md) *KIR*, related with "cut, cleave, pass swiftly through".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="../A/Arciryas.md" title="Arciryas">Ar<b>cirya</b>s</a></i> = "Noble Ship-(?)"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="Ciryaher.md" title="Ciryaher"><b>Cirya</b>her</a></i> = "Ship-lord"
- <i><a href="Ciryandil.md" title="Ciryandil"><b>Cirya</b>ndil</a></i> = "Ship-friend"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="Ciryatan.md" title="Ciryatan"><b>Cirya</b>tan</a></i> = "Shipbuilder"
- <i><a href="Ciryatur.md" title="Ciryatur"><b>Cirya</b>tur</a></i> = "Ship-master"
- <i><a href="../T/Tarciryan.md" title="Tarciryan">Tar<b>cirya</b>n</a></i> = "High Ship-(?)"

- <i><a href="Calacirya.md" title="Calacirya">Cala<b>cirya</b></a></i> = "Pass of Light"
