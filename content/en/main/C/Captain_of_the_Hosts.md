**Captain of the Hosts** was a military title of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), perhaps the supreme military position in the Kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The title is mentioned alongside the name of Prince [Tarannon](/wiki/Tarannon) who, in this capacity, extended the realm along the shores. As [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor), he later took the name "Falastur".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is possible that the position was held by the [King's Heir](/wiki/King%27s_Heir) or another member of the [House of Anárion](/wiki/House_of_An%C3%A1rion).
