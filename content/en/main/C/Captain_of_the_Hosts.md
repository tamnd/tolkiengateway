**Captain of the Hosts** was a military title of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), perhaps the supreme military position in the Kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The title is mentioned alongside the name of Prince [Tarannon](../T/Tarannon.md) who, in this capacity, extended the realm along the shores. As [King of Gondor](../K/King_of_Gondor.md), he later took the name "Falastur".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is possible that the position was held by the [King's Heir](../K/King's_Heir.md) or another member of the [House of Anárion](../H/House_of_Anárion.md).
