The **Cirith Ninniach**, the "Rainbow Cleft", was a thin, rocky pass through the *[Ered Lómin](../E/Ered_Lómin.md)* ("Echoing Mountains") in the far northwest of Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Cirith Ninniach connected the land of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) with the [Firth of Drengist](../F/Firth_of_Drengist.md) which led to the [Great Sea](../B/Belegaer.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> Although not explicitly stated, when [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) led his followers up the Firth and into Hithlum they must have found and used Cirith Ninniach to achieve their passage.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During King [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md)'s stay in [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) the [Gate of the Noldor](../G/Gate_of_the_Noldor.md), a dark tunnel under the mountains, was built which opened into the pass. Centuries later, after Turgon and his people had abandoned the region for [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), Tuor found and used both the Gate of the Noldor and Cirith Ninniach to escape from Hithlum.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Tuor found the Cirith Ninniach by following a stream that flowed from [Dor-Lómin](../D/Dor-Lómin~15272.md) to the [Ered Lómin](../E/Ered_Lómin.md) and then disappeared into a great arch in a wall of rock. At first he though that there was no passage but then he saw two [Elves](../E/Elves.md) issue from the archway. [Gelmir](<../G/Gelmir_(messenger_of_Círdan).md>) and [Arminas](../A/Arminas.md) they were, and they told him that he had found the [Gate of the Noldor](../G/Gate_of_the_Noldor.md). The two Elves led Tuor into the tunnel beside the stream and down long flights of steps. They left Tuor beside a waterfall and he continued until he emerged into a narrow ravine that ran straight into the West. In the pass the stream rushed over falls and wove rainbows; thus he named the ravine Cirith Ninniach.

Tuor traveled in the pass for four days, watching the stream turn into a river fed by shimmering falls that descended the walls of Cirith Ninniach. The pass ended when the river met the sea in the narrow Firth of Drengist.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Cirith Ninniach* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), glossed as "Rainbow Cleft" in the narrative, but it could literally mean "Pass of the Slender Crossing", from *[cirith](Cirith.md)* ("cleft, pass"), *[ninn](../N/Ninn.md)* + [iach](../I/Iach.md) *("ford, crossing").*

Early [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) names for the pass were *Cris Ilbranteloth* ("Gully of the Rainbow Roof") and *Glorfalc* ("Golden Cleft").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [The Quenta](../T/The_Quenta.md), the original name of the *Rainbow-cleft* was *Cris-Ilfing*<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>, emended to *Kirith Helvin*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>. In that version the river, that led Tuor to *Cris-Ilfing* seems to be flowed underground directly from [Lake Mithrim](../L/Lake_Mithrim.md)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>.

In our world, the name *Ilfing* is the earliest known form of name of <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbl%C4%85g_(river)" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Elbląg River</a> from today's Poland, as described in narrative of Anglosaxon voyager Wulfstan included in <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_English_Orosius" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"><i>Old English Orosius</i></a>.
