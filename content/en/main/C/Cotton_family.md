The **Cotton family** was a working class family of [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md).

## Origin {#Origin}

[Cottar](Cottar.md), the founder of the Cotton clan, was born some two hundred years before the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). It was his grandson, [Holman](../H/Holman_Cotton.md), who was the first to take the surname Cotton. "Long Hom", as Holman was known, is also the first of the Cottons to be explicitly connected with the village of [Bywater](../B/Bywater.md), which remained the seat of the family from that time on.<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-1">[1]</a></sup>

Holman's son [Tolman](../T/Tolman_Cotton_Senior.md) farmed the lands to the south of Bywater, and it was he who made the family one of the most important in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). He played a significant part in the decisive [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md), the most important step in the recovery of the Shire from [Sharkey's Men](../S/Sharkey's_Men.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and his daughter [Rose](../R/Rose_Cotton.md) married [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) in the following spring.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Cotton* is a real-life English surname and means "cottage town".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Thus the name could represent a possible [Old Hobbitish](../O/Old_English.md) *Cottun* "town with cottages".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

It is also a translation of the [Westron](../W/Westron.md) <i><b>Hlothran</b></i>, associated with the name *[Hlothram](Cotman.md#Etymology)*. Hlothran was "a fairly common village-name in the Shire",<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> though none is mentioned (or noteworthy enough) on any map.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) chose to couple the Cottons with the [Gamgees](../G/Gamgee_family.md) because of *Gamgee cotton* (also known as *Gamgee tissue*), a surgical tissue he heard of as a child. It was invented by <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sampson_Gamgee" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">J. Sampson Gamgee</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
