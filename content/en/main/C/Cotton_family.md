The **Cotton family** was a working class family of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits).

## Origin {#Origin}

[Cottar](/wiki/Cottar), the founder of the Cotton clan, was born some two hundred years before the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). It was his grandson, [Holman](/wiki/Holman_Cotton "Holman Cotton"), who was the first to take the surname Cotton. "Long Hom", as Holman was known, is also the first of the Cottons to be explicitly connected with the village of [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater), which remained the seat of the family from that time on.<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-1">[1]</a></sup>

Holman's son [Tolman](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior") farmed the lands to the south of Bywater, and it was he who made the family one of the most important in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). He played a significant part in the decisive [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater), the most important step in the recovery of the Shire from [Sharkey's Men](/wiki/Sharkey%27s_Men),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and his daughter [Rose](/wiki/Rose_Cotton "Rose Cotton") married [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) in the following spring.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Cotton* is a real-life English surname and means "cottage town".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Thus the name could represent a possible [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") *Cottun* "town with cottages".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

It is also a translation of the [Westron](/wiki/Westron) <i><b>Hlothran</b></i>, associated with the name *[Hlothram](/wiki/Cotman#Etymology "Cotman")*. Hlothran was "a fairly common village-name in the Shire",<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> though none is mentioned (or noteworthy enough) on any map.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) chose to couple the Cottons with the [Gamgees](/wiki/Gamgee_family "Gamgee family") because of *Gamgee cotton* (also known as *Gamgee tissue*), a surgical tissue he heard of as a child. It was invented by <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sampson_Gamgee" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">J. Sampson Gamgee</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
