**CDC Games** (Chinese: 中华网游戏集团) is a Chinese company, specialized in developing and publishing video games. In 2006 CDC Games made an agreement with [Turbine](/wiki/Turbine), to published [The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online) in China. They released a beta, for which you had to pay to get access. Though spending $10.000.000 on the game, they still hadn't published anything in [2011](/wiki/2011). Turbine saw this as a contract breach and terminated the agreement.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
