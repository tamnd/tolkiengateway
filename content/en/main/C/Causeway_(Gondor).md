The **Causeway** in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) was the walled<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> road that ran west from [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) to the [Causeway Forts](Causeway_Forts.md) in the [Rammas Echor](../R/Rammas_Echor.md) that surrounded the [Pelennor Fields](../P/Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-gate_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gate-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It is possible that the Causeway was built before the Causeway Forts.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

On [12 March](../_/12_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> the attacking <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Morgul-host&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Morgul-host (page does not exist)">Morgul-host</a><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> that was led by the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) and outnumbered the defenders ten times managed to cross the Anduin on a large number of floats and barges that they had secretly built on the eastern side of Osgiliath so that Faramir retreated up the Causeway and rallied his men to the Causeway Forts.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In the early hours of [13 March](../_/13_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the attackers wrecked the Causeway Forts and blasted breaches in the Rammas Echor.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

On [18 March](../_/18_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../H/Host_of_the_West.md" title="Host of the West">Host of the West</a><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> left [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) and passed "down the great road to the Causeway" to Osgiliath.<sup id="cite_ref-gate_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gate-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The causeway was originally unnamed when it first appears in an earlier manuscript of what would later become the chapter "[Minas Tirith](<../M/Minas_Tirith_(chapter).md>)".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> In an earlier manuscript of what would later become the chapter "[The Siege of Gondor](../T/The_Siege_of_Gondor.md)", the Causeway Forts are described as guarding "the entrance of the causeway into the townlands" and Faramir is said to "have paid dearly for the causeway" instead of "for the crossing".<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> The name of the road was first capitalized as Causeway in an earlier version of the chapter "[The Last Debate](../T/The_Last_Debate.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
