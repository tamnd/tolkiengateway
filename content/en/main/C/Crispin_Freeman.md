**Crispin McDougal Freeman** ([February 9](/wiki/9_February "9 February"), [1972](/wiki/1972)) is an American voice actor, who is a recognized voice in anime dubbing. His most recognized voice is that of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alucard_(Hellsing)" title="wikipedia:Alucard (Hellsing)">Alucard</a> in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hellsing_(TV_series)" title="wikipedia:Hellsing (TV series)">Hellsing</a></i>.

Freeman is a Tolkien fan, and during a visit to Anime Conventions in [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand), he visited [Mount Sunday](/wiki/Mount_Sunday).

## Roles {#Roles}

- [2011](/wiki/2011): *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North)* - Unspecified
- [2009](/wiki/2009): *[The Lord of the Rings: Conquest](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Conquest)* - [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), Young Male [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits")
- [2006](/wiki/2006): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II)* - Additional voices
- [2005](/wiki/2005): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth)* - Additional voices
- [2003](/wiki/2003): *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring)* - [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), Orc Slasher
