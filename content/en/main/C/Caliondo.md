**Caliondo** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌkaliˈondo]</span>) was a [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) of royal blood, and was the son of [Eärendur](<../E/Eärendur_(son_of_Tar-Amandil).md>), who was the younger son of [King](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) [Tar-Amandil](../T/Tar-Amandil.md). He himself had one son, [Malantur](../M/Malantur.md). Almost nothing is known of his life.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Caliondo* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Helge Fauskanger suggests it may be a longer form of *[Calion](Calion.md)*, unless it contains *[ondo](../O/Ondo.md)* ("rock").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The meaning of *Calion* (the Quenya name of [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md)) is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Son of Light" and is a compound of *[cala](Cala.md)* ("light") and the patronymic suffix *[-ion](../_/-ion.md)* ("-son", masculine patronymic).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests *Caliondo* is a combination of *[cala](Cala.md)* ("light") and the masculine agantal suffix *[-ndo](../_/-ndo.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
