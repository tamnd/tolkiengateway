**Caliondo** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌkaliˈondo]</span>) was a [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") of royal blood, and was the son of [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(son_of_Tar-Amandil)> "Eärendur (son of Tar-Amandil)"), who was the younger son of [King](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") [Tar-Amandil](/wiki/Tar-Amandil). He himself had one son, [Malantur](/wiki/Malantur). Almost nothing is known of his life.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Caliondo* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Helge Fauskanger suggests it may be a longer form of *[Calion](/wiki/Calion)*, unless it contains *[ondo](/wiki/Ondo "Ondo")* ("rock").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The meaning of *Calion* (the Quenya name of [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n)) is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Son of Light" and is a compound of *[cala](/wiki/Cala "Cala")* ("light") and the patronymic suffix *[-ion](/wiki/-ion)* ("-son", masculine patronymic).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests *Caliondo* is a combination of *[cala](/wiki/Cala "Cala")* ("light") and the masculine agantal suffix *[-ndo](/wiki/-ndo)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
