**Carn Dûm** was the chief fortress of the realm of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and the seat of its king.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It was located at the northwestern end of the [Mountains of Angmar](../M/Mountains_of_Angmar.md), the north-western extension of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1300](../T/Third_Age_1300.md), [Sauron's](../S/Sauron.md) [shadow](../S/Shadow.md) became stronger and evil things multiplied, heralded by the reappearance of the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The lord of the Nazgûl came north and established the evil realm of Angmar between the [Ettenmoors](../E/Ettenmoors.md) and the [Mountains of Angmar](../M/Mountains_of_Angmar.md) on both sides of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), because he intended to destroy the [Dúnedain of the North](../D/Dúnedain_of_the_North.md) and to exploit the disunity of the splinter realms of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md), [Cardolan](Cardolan.md) and [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md). He gathered many evil men, Orcs and other fell creatures in Angmar and became known as the Witch-king of Angmar.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> It is not known if Carn Dûm already existed when the Witch-king established the realm of Angmar or if it was built during his reign.

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1409](../T/Third_Age_1409.md) a large army from Angmar invaded and devastated Cardolan, but a remnant of the Dúnedain of Cardolan defended the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md) or fled to the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The [last prince of Cardolan](../L/Last_prince_of_Cardolan.md) was slain in that year and was said to have been buried in the [barrow](../B/Barrows.md) in the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md) in which [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) were trapped by a [Barrow-wight](../B/Barrow-wight.md) almost 1700 years later.<sup id="cite_ref-ArgelebII_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ArgelebII-8">[8]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> the combined armies of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) crossed the river [Lune](../L/Lune.md) and defeated the forces of Angmar in a great battle on the plain between [Lake Evendim](../L/Lake_Evendim.md) and the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md). The [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) of Angmar fled northwards towards Angmar with a remnant of his forces, but but his forces were overtaken by the cavalry of Gondor led by prince [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md) and by forces led by the Elf-lord [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) who came north from Rivendell before they could reach the shelter of Carn Dûm. The Witch-king was able to flee, but the remaining forces of Angmar were utterly defeated and no man or orc of the realm of Angmar remained west of the [Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> As a consequence, it is possible that Carn Dûm was destroyed. It is also possible that Carn Dûm was repopulated after [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2480](../T/Third_Age_2480.md), when Orcs began making secret strongholds in the Misty Mountains,.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

On [September 28](../S/September_28.md) of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md)<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> while Meriadoc Brandybuck was trapped with Frodo Baggins by a Barrow-wight in a barrow in the Barrow-downs, he had a dream that he and others were attacked and defeated by the men of Carn Dûm at night and that his heart had been pierced with a spear.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> It is possible that he dreamt of the experiences of the last prince of Cardolan during the last hours of his life.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

The etymology of *Carn Dûm* is uncertain, but it is often interpreted as a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name.

In early manuscripts of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, *Carndoom* and later *Caron-dûn* (both meaning "Red Valley") appear as early names of [Dimrill Dale](../D/Dimrill_Dale.md).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> Paul Strack favours these early terms as [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) precursors of Sindarin *Carn Dûm*.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) also favours the Sindarin interpretation, giving it as "Red Valley", a compound of *[carn](Caran.md)* + *[tûm](../T/Tum.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

Another suggestion, is that the name roughly translates as "mountain fortress" in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaelic" title="wikipedia:Gaelic">Gaelic</a>; although Tolkien uses [Celtic](Celtic.md) elements for some translated [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) names, this would have been the only instance where he uses Gaelic. It is unknown whether this is just coincidence or not.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Carn Dûm is the capital of <a href="../A/Angmar.md" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>. The campaign begins with its construction, and it is besieged once by <a href="../G/Glorfindel.md" title="Glorfindel">Glorfindel</a>. Carn Dûm is also playable in the skirmish section of the game.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Carn Dum is located in the game&#39;s region of Angmar and was both the main focus of storyline and the final end-game dungeon for the game&#39;s original release. It is ruled by Angmar&#39;s shadow that has returned to this city in the form of the False King <a class="mw-redirect" href="../M/Mordirith.md" title="Mordirith">Mordirith</a>, Steward of Angmar.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Carn Dum is the final level in the game. It is ruled by <a href="../A/Agandaûr.md" title="Agandaûr">Agandaûr</a>, a descendant of the <a href="../B/Black_Númenóreans.md" title="Black Númenóreans">Black Númenóreans</a> and a servant of <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>.</dd></dl>
