**Carn Dûm** was the chief fortress of the realm of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and the seat of its king.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It was located at the northwestern end of the [Mountains of Angmar](/wiki/Mountains_of_Angmar), the north-western extension of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300"), [Sauron's](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron") [shadow](/wiki/Shadow "Shadow") became stronger and evil things multiplied, heralded by the reappearance of the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The lord of the Nazgûl came north and established the evil realm of Angmar between the [Ettenmoors](/wiki/Ettenmoors) and the [Mountains of Angmar](/wiki/Mountains_of_Angmar) on both sides of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), because he intended to destroy the [Dúnedain of the North](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_the_North) and to exploit the disunity of the splinter realms of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan) and [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur). He gathered many evil men, Orcs and other fell creatures in Angmar and became known as the Witch-king of Angmar.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> It is not known if Carn Dûm already existed when the Witch-king established the realm of Angmar or if it was built during his reign.

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1409](/wiki/Third_Age_1409 "Third Age 1409") a large army from Angmar invaded and devastated Cardolan, but a remnant of the Dúnedain of Cardolan defended the [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs) or fled to the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The [last prince of Cardolan](/wiki/Last_prince_of_Cardolan "Last prince of Cardolan") was slain in that year and was said to have been buried in the [barrow](/wiki/Barrows "Barrows") in the [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs) in which [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) were trapped by a [Barrow-wight](/wiki/Barrow-wight) almost 1700 years later.<sup id="cite_ref-ArgelebII_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ArgelebII-8">[8]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975"),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> the combined armies of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) and [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) crossed the river [Lune](/wiki/Lune) and defeated the forces of Angmar in a great battle on the plain between [Lake Evendim](/wiki/Lake_Evendim) and the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs). The [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) of Angmar fled northwards towards Angmar with a remnant of his forces, but but his forces were overtaken by the cavalry of Gondor led by prince [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur) and by forces led by the Elf-lord [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) who came north from Rivendell before they could reach the shelter of Carn Dûm. The Witch-king was able to flee, but the remaining forces of Angmar were utterly defeated and no man or orc of the realm of Angmar remained west of the [Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains "Misty Mountains").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> As a consequence, it is possible that Carn Dûm was destroyed. It is also possible that Carn Dûm was repopulated after [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2480](/wiki/Third_Age_2480 "Third Age 2480"), when Orcs began making secret strongholds in the Misty Mountains,.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

On [September 28](/wiki/September_28) of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018")<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> while Meriadoc Brandybuck was trapped with Frodo Baggins by a Barrow-wight in a barrow in the Barrow-downs, he had a dream that he and others were attacked and defeated by the men of Carn Dûm at night and that his heart had been pierced with a spear.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> It is possible that he dreamt of the experiences of the last prince of Cardolan during the last hours of his life.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

The etymology of *Carn Dûm* is uncertain, but it is often interpreted as a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.

In early manuscripts of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, *Carndoom* and later *Caron-dûn* (both meaning "Red Valley") appear as early names of [Dimrill Dale](/wiki/Dimrill_Dale).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> Paul Strack favours these early terms as [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) precursors of Sindarin *Carn Dûm*.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) also favours the Sindarin interpretation, giving it as "Red Valley", a compound of *[carn](/wiki/Caran "Caran")* + *[tûm](/wiki/Tum "Tum")*.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

Another suggestion, is that the name roughly translates as "mountain fortress" in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaelic" title="wikipedia:Gaelic">Gaelic</a>; although Tolkien uses [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) elements for some translated [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) names, this would have been the only instance where he uses Gaelic. It is unknown whether this is just coincidence or not.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Carn Dûm is the capital of <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>. The campaign begins with its construction, and it is besieged once by <a href="/wiki/Glorfindel" title="Glorfindel">Glorfindel</a>. Carn Dûm is also playable in the skirmish section of the game.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Carn Dum is located in the game&#39;s region of Angmar and was both the main focus of storyline and the final end-game dungeon for the game&#39;s original release. <dl><dd>Carn Dum is the final level in the game. It is ruled by <a href="/wiki/Aganda%C3%BBr" title="Agandaûr">Agandaûr</a>, a descendant of the <a href="/wiki/Black_N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans" title="Black Númenóreans">Black Númenóreans</a> and a servant of <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>.</dd></dl>
