**Carnimírië** was one of the [rowans](../R/Rowans.md) of [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md), much beloved by the [Ent](../E/Ent.md) [Bregalad](../Q/Quickbeam.md). It was cut down by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md), arousing the anger of this impulsive Ent.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a letter, [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) explained that *carnemírië* means "with adornment of red jewels" in High-elven ([Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In another manuscript he explained the name as the abstract form of *carni-mírea* ("red-jeweled").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
