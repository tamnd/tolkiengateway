**Carnimírië** was one of the [rowans](/wiki/Rowans "Rowans") of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest), much beloved by the [Ent](/wiki/Ent) [Bregalad](/wiki/Quickbeam "Quickbeam"). It was cut down by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), arousing the anger of this impulsive Ent.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a letter, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") explained that *carnemírië* means "with adornment of red jewels" in High-elven ([Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In another manuscript he explained the name as the abstract form of *carni-mírea* ("red-jeweled").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
