**Cerin Amroth** was a hill in the heart of the ancient Elven realm of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).

## Geography {#Geography}

The slopes of the hill were covered with grass and with the flowers *[elanor](</wiki/Elanor_(flower)> "Elanor (flower)")* and *[niphredil](/wiki/Niphredil "Niphredil")*. On the top of the hill grew two circles of trees: an outer circle of leafless trees with snow-white bark and an inner circle of *[mallorn-trees](/wiki/Mallorn "Mallorn")*. In the centre of the inner circle stood a very tall tree with a white [flet](/wiki/Flet "Flet") built high amid its branches.<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/72/Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png/200px-Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="295" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/7/72/Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png/300px-Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/7/72/Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png/400px-Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png 2x" data-file-width="1483" data-file-height="2184"/></a><figcaption><i>Arwen and Aragorn plight their troth on Cerin Amroth</i> by <a href="/wiki/Stephen_Walsh" title="Stephen Walsh">Stephen Walsh</a></figcaption></figure>

The hill received its name, Cerin Amroth ("Amroth's Mound"), in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) when [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth), the [King of Lórien](/wiki/King_of_L%C3%B3rien), lived in the high trees in the fashion of the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves) on the hill. The main function of the flet on the hill was to watch the fortress of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The hill retained its name after Amroth left his kingdom with his lover [Nimrodel](/wiki/Nimrodel) and many other Elves of Lothlórien in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

On the evening of Midsummer<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2980](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980") [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) promised to marry each other on Cerin Amroth.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On 17 January [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring), except [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) who had fallen off the [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm), briefly visited Cerin Amroth. [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) and [Sam](/wiki/Sam) climbed up to the flet with [Haldir](/wiki/Haldir) and were able to see much of the land of Lothlórien, as well as southern [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) with the hill of Dol Guldur in its centre.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

After the death of Aragorn on 1 March [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [120](/wiki/Fourth_Age_120 "Fourth Age 120"),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Arwen went to Lothlórien, where she lived alone for a few months, eventually surrendering her life on Cerin Amroth before the beginning of spring. Her green grave was on Cerin Amroth.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
