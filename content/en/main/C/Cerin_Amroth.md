**Cerin Amroth** was a hill in the heart of the ancient Elven realm of [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

The slopes of the hill were covered with grass and with the flowers *[elanor](<../E/Elanor_(flower).md>)* and *[niphredil](../N/Niphredil.md)*. On the top of the hill grew two circles of trees: an outer circle of leafless trees with snow-white bark and an inner circle of *[mallorn-trees](../M/Mallorn.md)*. In the centre of the inner circle stood a very tall tree with a white [flet](../F/Flet.md) built high amid its branches.<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/S/Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/72/Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png/200px-Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="295" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/72/Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png/300px-Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/72/Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png/400px-Stephen_Walsh_-_Arwen_and_Aragorn_plight_their_troth_on_Cerin_Amroth.png 2x" data-file-width="1483" data-file-height="2184"/></a><figcaption><i>Arwen and Aragorn plight their troth on Cerin Amroth</i> by <a href="../S/Stephen_Walsh.md" title="Stephen Walsh">Stephen Walsh</a></figcaption></figure>

The hill received its name, Cerin Amroth ("Amroth's Mound"), in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) when [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md), the [King of Lórien](../K/King_of_Lórien.md), lived in the high trees in the fashion of the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) on the hill. The main function of the flet on the hill was to watch the fortress of [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The hill retained its name after Amroth left his kingdom with his lover [Nimrodel](../N/Nimrodel.md) and many other Elves of Lothlórien in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1981](../T/Third_Age_1981.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

On the evening of Midsummer<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2980](../T/Third_Age_2980.md) [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) promised to marry each other on Cerin Amroth.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On 17 January [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md), except [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) who had fallen off the [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](../B/Bridge_of_Khazad-dûm.md), briefly visited Cerin Amroth. [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) and [Sam](../S/Sam.md) climbed up to the flet with [Haldir](../H/Haldir.md) and were able to see much of the land of Lothlórien, as well as southern [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) with the hill of Dol Guldur in its centre.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

After the death of Aragorn on 1 March [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Arwen went to Lothlórien, where she lived alone for a few months, eventually surrendering her life on Cerin Amroth before the beginning of spring. Her green grave was on Cerin Amroth.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
