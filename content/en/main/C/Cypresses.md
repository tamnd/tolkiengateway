**Cypresses** were a type of tree that grew in the northern marches of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

During [Berúthiel](/wiki/Ber%C3%BAthiel)'s time as the [wife](/wiki/Queen_of_Gondor "Queen of Gondor") of [King](/wiki/King_of_Gondor "King of Gondor") [Tarannon](/wiki/Tarannon) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), she decorated the gardens of the [King's House](/wiki/King%27s_House) in [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) "with tormented sculptures beneath cypresses and [yews](/wiki/Yews "Yews")".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1994: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="/wiki/Treasures_of_Middle-earth_(2nd_edition)" title="Treasures of Middle-earth (2nd edition)">Treasures of Middle-earth</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>A type of cypress, <i><b>Black Cypress</b></i>, is described as the largest evergreen growing in southeastern <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a>, growing up to 200 ft. Its trunk was black and the scale-like leaves were dark green. Its hard to work wood was used for shipbuilding.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>
