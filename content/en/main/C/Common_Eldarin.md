**Common Eldarin** is the tongue of the ancient [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) of the [Great March](/wiki/Great_March). It is the <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_common_ancestor" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">last common ancestor</a> of all known [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) languages (broken into [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and [Common Telerin](/wiki/Common_Telerin) branches).

## Grammatical note {#Grammatical_note}

Common Eldarin presented the ending of -r as a plural marker of uninflected verbal stems.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Origin {#Origin}

Common [Eldarin](/wiki/Eldarin) is an evolution of [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian), the original language of all [Quendi](/wiki/Quendi) (or Elves), developing after the Eldar left [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen) for [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). As the years passed, the language of those traveling Elves became diverged from the one spoken before the March. By the time they reached [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), dialects of Common Eldarin had already begun to develop.

Since the Eldar dominate the annals of the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days), all the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) languages that are described in detail come from this branch. The [Avarin](/wiki/Avarin) languages followed a wholly different evolution, distinct from Common Eldarin, and the changes presented here may not have applied during the evolution of those languages.

At least at one point, the changes reflected coexisting alternative forms of PQ. For example, the pairs of endings <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-%C3%B4&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-ô (page does not exist)">-ô</a></i> (m), <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-%C3%AA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-ê (page does not exist)">-ê</a></i> (f) and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-%C3%BB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-û (page does not exist)">-û</a></i> (m), <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-%C3%AE&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-î (page does not exist)">-î</a></i> (f). This is mirrored in the evolution of short *-u* becoming *-o* and the short *-i* becoming *-e*.

## Evolution from Primitive Quendian {#Evolution_from_Primitive_Quendian}

During the Great March, minor changes to the Primitive Quendian brought the later Eldarin languages. These changes had to do with several developments of the word-forms, usually normalization. For example:

<dd>PQ: <i>-<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=H%C3%B4&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hô (page does not exist)">hô</a></i> &gt; CE: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-%C3%B4&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-ô (page does not exist)">-ô</a></i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>&#34;Difficult&#34; consonant clusters, usually those with nasals (like <i>bm</i> and <i>dn</i>), were rearranged</dt>
<dd>PQ: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Labm%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Labmâ (page does not exist)">labmâ</a></i> (root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=LAB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LAB (page does not exist)">LAB</a>) &gt; CE: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lamb%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lambâ (page does not exist)">lambâ</a></i></dd>
<dd>PQ: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Stabn%C3%AA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Stabnê (page does not exist)">stabnê</a></i> (<a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB" title="Sundocarmë">Root</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=STAB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="STAB (page does not exist)">STAB</a>) &gt; CE <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Stamb%C3%AA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Stambê (page does not exist)">stambê</a></i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt><i>ȝt</i> &gt; <i>cht</i></dt>
<dd>PQ: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ma%C8%9Dt%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maȝtâ (page does not exist)">maȝtâ</a></i> (root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=MA%C8%9C&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MAȜ (page does not exist)">MAȜ</a>) &gt; CE: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Maht%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mahtâ (page does not exist)">mahtâ</a></i> (pronounced <i>machta</i>)</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Final short <i>-a, -e, -o</i> are <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Apocope&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Apocope (page does not exist)">lost</a>, sometimes producing a long monosyllable as their trace</dt>
<dd>PQ: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nd%C3%AAro&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ndêro (page does not exist)">ndêro</a></i> &gt; CE: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nd%C3%A6%5Er&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ndæ^r (page does not exist)">ndæ̂r</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup></dd>
<dd>(<i><a href="/wiki/Kwende" title="Kwende">kwende</a></i> &gt; <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Quend%C3%AB" title="Quendë">quendë</a></i> is an exception)</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Final short <i>-i, -u</i> became <i>-e, -o</i></dt>
<dd>PQ: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ma%C8%9Diti&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maȝiti (page does not exist)">maȝiti</a></i> &gt; CE: *<i>maȝite</i></dd>
<dd>PQ: <i><a href="/wiki/RAK" title="RAK">ranku</a></i> &gt; CE: *<i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ranko" title="Ranko">ranko</a></i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Other minor changes</dt>
<dd>PQ: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Wo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Wo (page does not exist)">wo</a></i> &gt; CE: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Wa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Wa (page does not exist)">wa</a></i></dd></dl>

<dd>CE: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Maht%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mahtâ (page does not exist)">mahtâ</a></i> &gt; <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mahta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mahta (page does not exist)">mahta</a></i></dd>
<dd>CE: <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lamb%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lambâ (page does not exist)">lambâ</a></i> &gt; <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lamba&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lamba (page does not exist)">lamba</a></i></dd></dl>

At some point, dialects separated the uniformity of that language, and produced the [Common Telerin](/wiki/Common_Telerin) branch. <span></span>
