<i><b>calen</b></i> (plural <i><b>celin</b></i>) is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for "green".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) counterpart is *calina*.
