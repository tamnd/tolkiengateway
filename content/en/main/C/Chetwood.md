The **Chetwood** was the woodlands that lay east and south of the town of [Bree](/wiki/Bree),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> some forty miles east of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-2">[2]</a></sup>. The village of [Archet](/wiki/Archet) was built on its edge.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Prior to the settlement of the Shire many Hobbits lived in Bree and in the Chetwood nearby.<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-2">[2]</a></sup> When [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") led the four hobbits out of Bree they spent three days in the Chetwood before descending to the [Midgewater Marshes](/wiki/Midgewater_Marshes).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

**Chetwood** is modelled on a relic of British nomenclature.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The first element *chet* (also found in *[Archet](/wiki/Archet#Etymology)*) is of [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) origin and means "wood". The second element is the english word *wood* with the same meaning as the first element.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-6">[6]</a></sup> The first element is seen in the [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh) word <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/coed#Welsh" title="wiktionary:coed">coed</a></i> for "wood".<sup id="cite_ref-etym_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey) mentions <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chetwode" title="wikipedia:Chetwode">Chetwode</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> a village in Buckinghamshire close to the border of Oxfordshire, whose existence was first recorded in the year 949 as *Cetwuda*.

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Chetwood consisted of three areas in the region of <a href="/wiki/Bree-land" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a>. Chetwood South was located around the <a href="/wiki/East_Road" title="East Road">East Road</a> south of <a href="/wiki/Staddle" title="Staddle">Staddle</a>, south-east of <a href="/wiki/Bree" title="Bree">Bree</a> and south-west of the <a href="/wiki/Midgewater_Marshes" title="Midgewater Marshes">Midgewater Marshes</a>. Chetwood North was north of the marsh, between <a href="/wiki/Combe" title="Combe">Combe</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Weather_Hills" title="Weather Hills">Weather Hills</a>. The Far Chetwood lay in the northeast of Bree-land beyond the <a href="/wiki/Archet" title="Archet">Archet</a> Dale, south of the lake of Nen Harn on the border with the <a href="/wiki/North_Downs" title="North Downs">North Downs</a>. Early in the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, bandits of the Blackwold gang lived in the woods.</dd></dl>
