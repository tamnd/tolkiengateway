**Calmacil** (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈkalmakil]</span>) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name, meaning "Sword of Light". It can refer to two Kings of [Men](/wiki/Men):

- [Tar-Calmacil](/wiki/Tar-Calmacil) ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [2516](/wiki/Second_Age_2516 "Second Age 2516") - [2825](/wiki/Second_Age_2825 "Second Age 2825")), the eighteenth [King](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) who was the first King to adopt an [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) regnal name.
- [Calmacil](</wiki/Calmacil_(King_of_Gondor)> "Calmacil (King of Gondor)") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1058](/wiki/Third_Age_1058 "Third Age 1058") - [1304](/wiki/Third_Age_1304 "Third Age 1304")), the eighteenth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).
