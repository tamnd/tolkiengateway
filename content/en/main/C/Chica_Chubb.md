**Chica Baggins, née Chubb**, was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbit) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Chica was the daughter of the head of the Chubb family. As her family name would have become lost after her marriage to [Bingo Baggins](/wiki/Bingo_Baggins), her offspring received a double surname,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Chubb-Baggins](/wiki/Chubb-Baggins). Their son [Falco](/wiki/Falco_Chubb-Baggins "Falco Chubb-Baggins") was the first to bear that name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
