**Cambridge** is a town in England, known for its **University of Cambridge**.

## Tolkien and Cambridge {#Tolkien_and_Cambridge}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is known to have visited Cambridge at least once in his youth, for a [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School) 1st XV football match against the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Leys_School" title="wikipedia:The Leys School">Leys School</a> on [1 November](/wiki/1_November) [1910](/wiki/1910).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

As a fellow of Merton College he visited the college's estates in Cambridge in September [1947](/wiki/1947). These included Merton Hall and Pythagoras Hall.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In this area (Northampton Street) the former site of the Merton Arms pub still carries the <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Merton_College_Oxford_Coat_Of_Arms.svg" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Merton College Coat Of Arms</a>.

He later visited Corpus Christi College in Cambridge on a number of occasions to view the manuscript of Ancrene Wisse: September [1948](/wiki/1948), 11–18 August [1952](/wiki/1952) (see also a photograph of Tolkien at the Garden House Hotel during this period, “The Laughing Philosopher” <a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkienestate.com/life/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien Estate website</a>), October–November [1952](/wiki/1952), December [1956](/wiki/1956), 7 May [1957](/wiki/1957).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Between the 8 and 15 May [1954](/wiki/1954), Tolkien was in Cambridge as an elector for the chair of Medieval and Renaissance English, a post designed for [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis). Although Lewis was initially reluctant to take the post, he eventually accepted.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Tolkien is recorded as having dined formally a couple of times <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad_eundem_degree" title="wikipedia:Ad eundem degree">ad eundem</a></i> in Cambridge, 22 May [1954](/wiki/1954), 9 July [1960](/wiki/1960) and 23 July [1966](/wiki/1966);<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> less formally, he dined or stayed with individuals in Cambridge: he dined with Hugh Brogan on 20 March [1949](/wiki/1949) (where he fell down the stairs);<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> at a house with no radio 30 October [1955](/wiki/1955) (so he missed a discussion of his works);<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> staying in Cambridge at the end of July [1960](/wiki/1960);<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and with Glyn Daniel in August [1973](/wiki/1973)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In mid-May [1961](/wiki/1961), one of Tolkien's visits to Cambridge included a Sunday spent with a fan club at Queen's College.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
