The **Captain of the Haven** was the title of a person in [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md) during the rule of [Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Ecthelion II](../E/Ecthelion_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

Sometime between [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2957](../T/Third_Age_2957.md) and [2980](../T/Third_Age_2980.md), [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) came to Gondor in disguise under the name *[Thorongil](../T/Thorongil.md)* and served Steward Ecthelion II.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> During that time he often counselled him that the rebels in [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md) were a danger to the southern fiefs of Gondor. In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2980](../T/Third_Age_2980.md) Ecthelion II permitted Thorongil to attack Umbar with a small fleet. Thorongil arrived with his fleet unnoticed at night and burnt a large part of the ships of the unprepared Corsairs. Thorongil himself overthrew the Captain of the Haven in a battle on the quays before he retreated with his fleet with small losses.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
