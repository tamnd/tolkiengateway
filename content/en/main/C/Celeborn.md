**Celeborn** was one of the noblest of the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-RGEONotes_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RGEONotes-5">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TimeScales_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TimeScales-6">[5]</a></sup> — who wedded the Lady [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) of the [House of Finarfin](../H/House_of_Finarfin.md) and with her, he remained in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) after the end of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

## History {#History}

### Early life {#Early_life}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/L/Līga_Kļaviņa_-_Love_at_First_Sight.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a8/L%C4%ABga_K%C4%BCavi%C5%86a_-_Love_at_First_Sight.jpg/300px-L%C4%ABga_K%C4%BCavi%C5%86a_-_Love_at_First_Sight.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="224" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a8/L%C4%ABga_K%C4%BCavi%C5%86a_-_Love_at_First_Sight.jpg/450px-L%C4%ABga_K%C4%BCavi%C5%86a_-_Love_at_First_Sight.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a8/L%C4%ABga_K%C4%BCavi%C5%86a_-_Love_at_First_Sight.jpg/600px-L%C4%ABga_K%C4%BCavi%C5%86a_-_Love_at_First_Sight.jpg 2x" data-file-width="900" data-file-height="673"/></a><figcaption><i>Love at First Sight</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Līga_Kļaviņa.md" title="Category:Images by Līga Kļaviņa">Līga Kļaviņa</a></figcaption></figure>

Celeborn was a [Sindarin](../S/Sindar.md) prince of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Return_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-7">[6]</a></sup> being the grandson of [Elmo](../E/Elmo.md),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup> the brother of [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md); thus, he was the grand-nephew of the [King of Doriath](../K/King_of_Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[8]</a></sup>

In the late [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) came to Doriath as guests of Thingol. There, Celeborn and Galadriel met, fell in love, and were soon betrothed. Galadriel remained in Doriath with Celeborn after [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) went to the [Caverns of Narog](Caverns_of_Narog.md) to establish the stronghold of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-7">[6]</a></sup>

For the rest of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), Celeborn and Galadriel are not mentioned to have played any significant role in the general course of events of the Age, while their relatives, both [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) and [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), did. By the [Fall of Nargothrond](../F/Fall_of_Nargothrond.md) in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md), Galadriel passed over the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) so it seems likely that Celeborn followed her although this is not known for certain.<sup id="cite_ref-mirror_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mirror-4">[3]</a></sup>

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

After the [fall of Beleriand](../W/War_of_wrath~38556.md), Celeborn and Galadriel lived in [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) for some time. Celeborn ruled the fief of [Harlindon](../H/Harlindon.md), which was composed mostly of [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), under [High King](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

[Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and Celeborn crossed into [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) with many [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), and [Green-elves](../G/Green-elves.md) in their following. For a while, they dwelt in the country about [Lake Nenuial](../L/Lake_Nenuial.md), ruling the Eldar in Eriador, including the wandering companies of the native Nandor. Probably around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [300](../S/Second_Age_300.md), a daughter was born to Galadriel and Celeborn, named [Celebrían](Celebrían.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TimeScales_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TimeScales-6">[5]</a></sup> They departed for [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) and arrived there by [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [750](../S/Second_Age_750.md).<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup> It was ruled by [Celebrimbor](Celebrimbor.md), grandson of Fëanor and the distant half-cousin of Galadriel.

It was only sometime between [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1350](../S/Second_Age_1350.md) and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1400&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1400 (page does not exist)">1400</a><sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[8]</a></sup> that [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) crossed the [Hithaeglir](../H/Hithaeglir.md) through [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md) and relocated there with their daughter Celebrían, becoming great among the [Wood-elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[8]</a></sup> Celeborn decided to stay in [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) due to his enmity towards the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves_of_Khazad-dûm.md).<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[8]</a></sup> It is said that Celeborn fought in the [Sack of Eregion](../S/Sack_of_Eregion.md) leading the remnants of Eregion out of the battle. He and [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) narrowly escaped to a dell, where the latter founded [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[8]</a></sup>

After the [War of the Elves and Sauron](../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md), Galadriel passed again through [Moria](../M/Moria.md) with [Celebrían](Celebrían.md) and came to [Imladris](../I/Imladris.md), seeking Celeborn.<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[8]</a></sup> There she found him, and there they dwelt together for a long time.<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[8]</a></sup> Some time later, [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and Celeborn departed from Imladris and went to the little-inhabited lands between the mouth of the [Gwathló](../G/Gwathló.md) and [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[8]</a></sup> There they dwelt in [Belfalas](../B/Belfalas.md), at the place that was afterwards called [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md); and their company was swelled by [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) from [Lórinand](../L/Lórinand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_9-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-9">[8]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/R/Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Swan-Boat_of_Galadriel.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/af/Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Swan-Boat_of_Galadriel.jpg/300px-Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Swan-Boat_of_Galadriel.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="282" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/af/Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Swan-Boat_of_Galadriel.jpg/450px-Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Swan-Boat_of_Galadriel.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/af/Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Swan-Boat_of_Galadriel.jpg/600px-Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Swan-Boat_of_Galadriel.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1279" data-file-height="1201"/></a><figcaption><i>The Swan-Boat of Galadriel</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Rowena_Morrill.md" title="Category:Images by Rowena Morrill">Rowena Morrill</a></figcaption></figure>

After long journeys in [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md), from [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and the borders of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) to [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) in the [north](../W/Woodland_Realm.md), Celeborn and Galadriel passed over the mountains to [Imladris](../I/Imladris.md), and there dwelt for many years.<sup id="cite_ref-AaN_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AaN-12">[11]</a></sup> It was there, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [109](../T/Third_Age_109.md),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-13">[12]</a></sup> that his daughter [Celebrían](Celebrían.md) wedded [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) Half-elven of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).

When [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md), the [King of Lórien](../K/King_of_Lórien.md), perished in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1981](../T/Third_Age_1981.md), Celeborn and Galadriel took up the rule of Lindórinand jointly, and were called the [Lord and Lady](../L/Lord_and_Lady_of_Galadhrim.md) of Lothlórien, the new name for Lindórinand, and together, they built [Caras Galadhon](Caras_Galadhon.md).

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), Lothlórien received the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md), composed of various travellers on the [quest](../Q/Quest_of_the_Ring.md) to destroy [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md). Celeborn and Galadriel offered advice and boats for the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), sending them on their way. After the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) repelled the forces of [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) three times, Celeborn led the forces of the Galadhrim across the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) and [took the fortress](../F/Fall_of_Dol_Guldur.md). Galadriel threw down its walls and purified the forest.<sup id="cite_ref-great_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-14">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-chief_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-15">[14]</a></sup>

On [6 April](../_/6_April.md),<sup id="cite_ref-chief_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-15">[14]</a></sup> that was the [Elven day of New Year](../Y/Yestarë.md), [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) met with Celeborn in the midst of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) and renamed it *[Eryn Lasgalen](../E/Eryn_Lasgalen.md)*, "The Wood of the Green Leaves". With the forest now cleansed, it was divided among the Elves and Men; Celeborn took all the forest south of the [Narrows](../N/Narrows.md) and established [East Lórien](../E/East_Lórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-great_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-14">[13]</a></sup>

Celeborn attended the wedding of [Aragorn II Elessar](../A/Aragorn_II_Elessar.md) and his granddaughter [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md), and on the journey to return, he bade a fond farewell to [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md) as well. He and Galadriel escorted [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) until the [Mountains of Moria](../M/Mountains_of_Moria.md), and on [13 September](../_/13_September.md) they turned to return to [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-chief_15-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-15">[14]</a></sup>

### Later history {#Later_history}

After Galadriel's departure, it is believed that Celeborn relocated to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to reside with [Elladan](../E/Elladan.md), [Elrohir](../E/Elrohir.md), and some of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), having also grown weary of [East Lórien](../E/East_Lórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-prologue_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prologue-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-great_14-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-14">[13]</a></sup>

At some unknown date (possibly between [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md) and [171](../F/Fourth_Age_171.md)),<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[note 2]</a></sup> he sought the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) and sailed west aboard [Last Ship](../L/Last_Ship.md) with his mighty kinsman [Cirdan the Shipwright](Cirdan_the_Shipwright.md), but when he did so, he took with him the last memory of the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md) in Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-prologue_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prologue-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Celeborn* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name, consisting of *[celeb](Celeb.md)* ("silver") + a derivative of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Orn%C4%81&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ornā (page does not exist)">ornā</a></i> ("tall"). *Teleporno*, his original [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) name, has an identical meaning. The name Teleporno was "Sindarized" as *Celeborn*: [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) *[telpe](../T/Telepi.md)* ("silver") (Quenya *[tyelpe](../T/Telpe.md)*) was transformed to Sindarin *celeb*.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[15]</a></sup> Teleporno's name gained an additional [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) form, **[Telporno](../T/Telporno.md)** (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[telˈporno]</span>).<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[16]</a></sup>

He was given the epithet "the Wise" by Galadriel.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[17]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### As a Sinda {#As_a_Sinda}

Before the summer of 1950 in an earlier version of the [Appendix on Languages](../T/The_Appendix_on_Languages.md) it was mentioned that the native language of Celeborn was *[Telerian](../T/Telerin.md)*, the native language of the Teleri who remained behind in the north-west of Middle-earth, of the Elves of Lórien and of the elf-kingdom in Northern Mirkwood of [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md), and that in the household of [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) also *[Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md)* was spoken, because she was a Noldo.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[18]</a></sup> It thus appears that Celeborn was a wood-elf in this earlier version. In a later version of the Appendix on Languages following a major revision of 1951, it is first mentioned that Celeborn was a Sinda (Grey-elf) and that most of the people of Lórien were of a woodland race.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[19]</a></sup> The text in the final version of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* only mentions that Celeborn was a kinsman of [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md).<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[20]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[21]</a></sup> *The Lord of the Rings* does not mention the ancestry of Celeborn and if he was a Sinda or a Teler from [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). The text in *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* also only mentions that Celeborn was a *kinsman of Thingol* and that Galadriel stayed in [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), because Celeborn lived there and did not accompany her brother [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) to [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Return_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-7">[6]</a></sup> and that Celeborn was a *prince of Doriath*<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[22]</a></sup> and calls him *Celeborn of Doriath*.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[23]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[24]</a></sup> *The Silmarillion* leaves the ancestry of Celeborn open and there is no mention if he was a Sinda or Teler, only implying that Galadriel met Celeborn in Doriath.

In a table with the descendants of Thingol from December 1959 Thingol's younger brother [Elmo](../E/Elmo.md) has a son [Galadhon](../G/Galadhon.md) who has two sons, Celeborn and [Galathil](../G/Galathil.md), and a daughter, [Nimloth](../N/Nimloth_of_Doriath.md), who married King [Dior](../D/Dior.md). However, on the same table Nimloth also appears as the daughter of Galathil. It looks as if the second thought of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) was that Nimloth was the daughter of Galathil and the niece of Celeborn.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[25]</a></sup> The genealogy according to this second thought was included in [Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md).<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[26]</a></sup> According to that table, Celeborn is thus the greatnephew of Thingol and second cousin of Galadriel.

In a text written in 1965, Celeborn is born in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) and he is a descendant of [Elmo](../E/Elmo.md).<sup id="cite_ref-elfage_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-elfage-29">[27]</a></sup> In a rejected note he is the father of Amroth, and was left with his son after his (first) wife stole away and deserted them. Tolkien further noted that while remarriage seldom happened, it could occur in the time of such divided feelings as of the end of the First Age.<sup id="cite_ref-elfage_29-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-elfage-29">[27]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:note 6</sup>

Only a note in <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On.md" title="The Road Goes Ever On">The Road Goes Ever On</a>, which was published in 1967 during J.R.R. Tolkien's lifetime, strictly mentions that Celeborn was a Sinda.<sup id="cite_ref-RGEONotes_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RGEONotes-5">[4]</a></sup>

### As a Teler {#As_a_Teler}

According to an alternate history of Celeborn and Galadriel in [Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md), Celeborn was a [Teler](../T/Teler.md) of noble rank in [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md),<sup id="cite_ref-AAm_30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AAm-30">[28]</a></sup> born **[Teleporno](../T/Teleporno.md)**,<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31">[29]</a></sup> grandson of [Olwë](../O/Olwë.md), prince of [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md) (thus he was a kinsman of [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-GC_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GC-32">[30]</a></sup> Only nine months before his death, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) still was of the opinion that Celeborn was only a kinsman of Thingol afar off, because Celeborn came from Valinor and that his orginal name was *Telporno*.<sup id="cite_ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33">[31]</a></sup>

In one c. [1969](../_/1969.md) version of the legendarium, Celeborn was the son of a [Telerin](../F/Falmari.md) [Elf](../E/Elf.md) called [Gilitīro](../G/Gilitīro.md).<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34">[32]</a></sup>

[Artanis](../G/Galadriel.md), Olwë's granddaughter and a [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) princess of great beauty, often spent long visits to Alqualondë and her mother’s kin. During this time the two fell in love, and Teleporno gave her the romantic epithet *[Alatáriel](../A/Alatáriel.md)*, meaning "Maiden Crowned with Radiant Garland".<sup id="cite_ref-Shib_35-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shib-35">[33]</a></sup>

Later, Teleporno and Artanis helped to defend Alqualondë from the Noldor during the [First Kinslaying](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AAm_30-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AAm-30">[28]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GC_32-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GC-32">[30]</a></sup> Nevertheless, Artanis’s heart was fired by the stories of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) of the open lands across the [sea](../B/Belegaer.md). She urged Teleporno to take a ship eastward to [Endor](../E/Endor.md), and he gave in. In doing so the two fell under the [Ban of the Valar](../B/Ban_of_the_Valar.md).

In Beleriand, Teleporno and Artanis were greeted by [Elu Thingol](../T/Thingol.md), [King of Doriath](../K/King_of_Doriath.md). Teleporno eventually "[Sindarized](../S/Sindarization.md)" his name to Celeborn; Artanis Sindarinized the epithet Celeborn had given her, and Alatáriel became Galadriel.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>On the other hand, the making of Celeborn into a Telerin Elf of Aman contradicts not only statements in <i>The Silmarillion</i>, but also those cited already from The Road Goes Ever On and Appendix B to The Lord of the Rings”
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="Christopher_Tolkien.md" title="Christopher Tolkien">Christopher Tolkien</a>, &#34;<a href="../T/The_History_of_Galadriel_and_Celeborn.md" title="The History of Galadriel and Celeborn">The History of Galadriel and Celeborn</a>&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote>

Celeborn as a Telerin prince seems a late development. This altered origin was not adopted into the published *[Silmarillion](../S/Silmarillion.md)*, primarily because it contradicted published [canon](Canon.md), and was not incorporated in Tolkien's drafts of the earlier parts, and therefore would have necessitated a major rewrite by [Christopher Tolkien](Christopher_Tolkien.md) and [Guy Gavriel Kay](../G/Guy_Gavriel_Kay.md) to account for a separate departure of Galadriel. It is unknown whether Tolkien would have revised the story or would have felt bound by published writings.

Celeborn's Telerin ancestry would have made the couple first cousins. *[Morgoth's Ring](../M/Morgoth's_Ring.md)* states that among the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) first cousins might marry, but seldom did so or desired to do so.<sup id="cite_ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36">[34]</a></sup> Furthermore, the published *Silmarillion* states "Eldar wedded not with kin so near," in reference to [Maeglin](../M/Maeglin.md)'s attraction to his first cousin [Idril](../I/Idril.md).<sup id="cite_ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37">[35]</a></sup>

<b><a href="../_/1978.md" title="1978">1978</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)">The Lord of the Rings</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Celeborn only has a brief, role and speaks only one line. He appeared beside <a href="../G/Galadriel.md" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a> for a brief shot. Galadriel pronounces his name &#34;Seleborn&#34;.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/1981.md" title="1981">1981</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)">The Lord of the Rings</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Celeborn was voiced by <a href="../S/Simon_Cadell.md" title="Simon Cadell">Simon Cadell</a>.  Unusually, virtually the whole dialogue Celeborn speaks in the book is utilised in the radio adaptation.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/1992.md" title="1992">1992</a>: <a href="../D/Der_Herr_der_Ringe_(1992_German_radio_series).md" title="Der Herr der Ringe (1992 German radio series)"><i>Der Herr der Ringe</i> (1992 German radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Celeborn is played by Wolfgang Hinze.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2001.md" title="2001">2001</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Celeborn is played by <a href="../M/Marton_Csokas.md" title="Marton Csokas">Marton Csokas</a>.  The role was extremely small in the theatrical version, but expanded slightly in the Extended Edition of <i>The Fellowship of the Ring</i>.  In that version, he participates in giving gifts to the Fellowship as they are leaving Lórien.  Notably, he gives <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> a dagger and warns him of the dangers the Fellowship will face while sailing down the <a href="../A/Anduin.md" title="Anduin">Anduin</a>.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2002.md" title="2002">2002</a>: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game).md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Celeborn makes a brief appearance in Lothlórien.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2003.md" title="2003">2003</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Celeborn leaves for Valinor with Galadriel and the other Ringbearers, rather than remaining behind as in the book.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2006.md" title="2006">2006</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Celeborn only appears during the &#34;Evil&#34; campaign, when he is slain by the Forces of Sauron.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2007.md" title="2007">2007</a>-: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>After the fall of <a href="../T/Thangorodrim.md" title="Thangorodrim">Thangorodrim</a>, Celeborn spends the early years of the <a href="../S/Second_Age.md" title="Second Age">Second Age</a> hunting down the surviving servants of <a href="../M/Morgoth.md" title="Morgoth">Morgoth</a>. He encountered <a href="../S/Shelob.md" title="Shelob">Shelob</a> in <a href="../E/Ephel_Dúath.md" title="Ephel Dúath">Ephel Dúath</a> and eventually was able to trap her by placing Light of <a href="../E/Eärendil.md" title="Eärendil">Eärendil</a> at the exits of <a href="../S/Shelob&#39;s_Lair.md" title="Shelob&#39;s Lair">Shelob&#39;s Lair</a>, trapping her inside for many centuries. :During an extended flashback depicting the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md" title="War of the Last Alliance">War of the Last Alliance</a>, Celeborn can be found in Echad-in-Edhil in <a href="../M/Mordor.md" title="Mordor">Mordor</a> next to <a href="../A/Amroth.md" title="Amroth">Amroth</a>, Lord of <a class="mw-redirect" href="../L/Lórien.md" title="Lórien">Lórien</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>During the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, Celeborn can be found next to <a href="../G/Galadriel.md" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a> on a main talan in <a href="Caras_Galadhon.md" title="Caras Galadhon">Caras Galadhon</a>, from where he negotiates with the Dwarves of the Iron Garrison, sends the player to combat the ancient evil in the depths of <a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Moria</a> and organizes a military assault against <a href="../D/Dol_Guldur.md" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a> in <a href="../M/Mirkwood.md" title="Mirkwood">Mirkwood</a>. After the fall of the Dark Tower, Celeborn meets with <a href="../T/Thranduil.md" title="Thranduil">Thranduil</a> to rename Mirkwood <a href="../E/Eryn_Lasgalen.md" title="Eryn Lasgalen">Eryn Lasgalen</a> and give the middle section of it to the <a href="../W/Woodmen.md" title="Woodmen">Woodmen</a>. Celeborn and Galadriel then travel to <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> to attend the Great Wedding of <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> and <a href="../A/Arwen.md" title="Arwen">Arwen</a>, after which Celeborn ventures into the <a href="../M/Morgul_Vale.md" title="Morgul Vale">Morgul Vale</a> once again to finish <a href="../S/Shelob.md" title="Shelob">Shelob</a> for good and leads a group including <a href="../L/Legolas.md" title="Legolas">Legolas</a>, <a href="../M/Mablung_(ranger_of_Ithilien).md" title="Mablung (ranger of Ithilien)">Mablung</a> and the player inside her Lair, Torech Ungol.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2014.md" title="2014">2014</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies%253A_Chronicles%253A_Art_&amp;_Design.md" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies: Chronicles: Art &amp; Design">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies: Chronicles: Art &amp; Design</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In a concept for <i>The Battle of the Five Armies</i> film, Celeborn and a force of Lothlórien elves would have joined in the <a href="../W/White_Council.md" title="White Council">White Council</a>’s assault on Dol Guldur.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2022.md" title="2022">2022</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a></i>: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power_-_Season_1.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power - Season 1">Season 1</a></b>

<dl><dd>In episode 7, <i><a href="../T/The_Eye.md" title="The Eye">The Eye</a></i>, Celeborn is mentioned by <a href="../G/Galadriel.md" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a> in a conversation with <a href="../T/Theo.md" title="Theo">Theo</a>, telling him that they met after Celeborn saw her dancing in a glade filled with flowers. Later, when he marched off the join the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../W/Wars_of_Beleriand.md" title="Wars of Beleriand">war</a> against <a href="../M/Morgoth.md" title="Morgoth">Morgoth</a>, Galadriel chided him that his armour did not fit properly and called him a silver clam.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Jamie_Campbell_Bower&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Jamie Campbell Bower (page does not exist)">Jamie Campbell Bower</a> portrays Celeborn. The character appears in flashback sequences of <a href="../G/Galadriel.md" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a> and he, dancing in the forest with <a href="../E/Elves.md" title="Elves">Elves</a> and embracing.
