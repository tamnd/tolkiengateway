<i><b>chwind</b></i>, alternatively <i><b>whinn</b></i>, are [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) words meaning "[birch](/wiki/Birches "Birches")" (the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) equivalent was *[hwinde](/wiki/Hwinde "Hwinde")*). The words were used in an 'ordinary non-mythological' sense, in contrast to the word *[brethil](</wiki/Brethil_(Sindarin_word)> "Brethil (Sindarin word)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
