**Chris Crawshaw** was the seventh Chairman of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md). Serving for nearly 12 years from [21 April](../_/21_April.md) [1996](../_/1996.md) until [19 April](../_/19_April.md) [2008](../_/2008.md), Crawshaw is the Society's second longest-serving Chairman. During her chairmanship, Crawshaw oversaw the large [Tolkien 2005](../T/Tolkien_2005.md) event and the expansion in the Society's membership following the release of *[The Lord of the Rings](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)* films.
