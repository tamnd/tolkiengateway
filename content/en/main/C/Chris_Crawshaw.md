**Chris Crawshaw** was the seventh Chairman of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society). Serving for nearly 12 years from [21 April](/wiki/21_April) [1996](/wiki/1996) until [19 April](/wiki/19_April) [2008](/wiki/2008), Crawshaw is the Society's second longest-serving Chairman. During her chairmanship, Crawshaw oversaw the large [Tolkien 2005](/wiki/Tolkien_2005) event and the expansion in the Society's membership following the release of *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)")* films.
