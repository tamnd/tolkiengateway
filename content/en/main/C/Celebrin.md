**celebrin** is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) adjective meaning "like silver (in hue or worth)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is composed of the noun *[celeb](/wiki/Celeb "Celeb")* ("silver") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-ren&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-ren (page does not exist)">-ren</a></i>, an adjective suffix.<sup id="cite_ref-Eld_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eld-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Telperin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Telperin (page does not exist)">telperin</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-Eld_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eld-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Words similar to, and with a similar meaning appear all the way to the earliest [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"):

- In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), a very early precursor to Sindarin associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, there appear the adjectives <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Celebriol&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Celebriol (page does not exist)">celebriol</a></i> and *celebrin*, meaning "like silver" and "of silver", respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- In [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), a somewhat later precursor to Sindarin, there appears the adjective *[celebren](/wiki/Celebren "Celebren")*, likewise meaning "of silver, like silver".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
