**Christopher Luke Wiseman** ([20 April](../_/20_April.md) [1893](../_/1893.md) Edgbaston - [25 July](../_/25_July.md) [1987](../_/1987.md) Milford-on-Sea) was the only friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) who survived the [First World War](../W/World_War_I.md), during which he served as an Instructor Lieutenant in the Royal Navy.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He went on to become Headmaster of Queens College, Taunton.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

He became friends with Tolkien whilst studying at King Edward's School, Birmingham, which they attended from [1905](../_/1905.md). Together with Tolkien and their mutual friends [Robert Gilson](../R/Robert_Gilson.md) and [Geoffrey Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md) they formed the [T.C.B.S.](../T/T.C.B.S..md) and he hosted at least one meeting at his London home in [1914](../_/1914.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

It was after Wiseman whom Tolkien's son was named [Christopher](Christopher_Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In biographical drama about J.R.R. Tolkien, called [Tolkien](<../T/Tolkien_(film).md>), he was portrayed by actor [Tom Glynn-Carney](../T/Tom_Glynn-Carney.md).
