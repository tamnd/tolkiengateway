**Christopher Luke Wiseman** ([20 April](/wiki/20_April) [1893](/wiki/1893) Edgbaston - [25 July](/wiki/25_July) [1987](/wiki/1987) Milford-on-Sea) was the only friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) who survived the [First World War](/wiki/World_War_I "World War I"), during which he served as an Instructor Lieutenant in the Royal Navy.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He went on to become Headmaster of Queens College, Taunton.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

He became friends with Tolkien whilst studying at King Edward's School, Birmingham, which they attended from [1905](/wiki/1905). Together with Tolkien and their mutual friends [Robert Gilson](/wiki/Robert_Gilson) and [Geoffrey Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith "Geoffrey Bache Smith") they formed the [T.C.B.S.](/wiki/T.C.B.S.) and he hosted at least one meeting at his London home in [1914](/wiki/1914).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

It was after Wiseman whom Tolkien's son was named [Christopher](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien "Christopher Tolkien").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In biographical drama about J.R.R. Tolkien, called [Tolkien](</wiki/Tolkien_(film)> "Tolkien (film)"), he was portrayed by actor [Tom Glynn-Carney](/wiki/Tom_Glynn-Carney).
