**Christopher Edgerly** ([August 6](../_/6_August.md), [1969](../_/1969.md)) is an American comedian and voice actor. He was hired to replace [Viggo Mortensen](../V/Viggo_Mortensen.md), who had walked out because of compensation issues, in [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (video game)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(video_game).md>). He went on to voice Aragorn in *[The Battle for Middle-earth Anthology](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_Anthology.md)*, and provided the voice of the ranger Elagost in [EA](../E/Electronic_Arts.md)'s alternative [fellowship](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)-game *[The Third Age](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Third_Age.md)*.
