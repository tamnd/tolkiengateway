**Christopher Edgerly** ([August 6](/wiki/6_August "6 August"), [1969](/wiki/1969)) is an American comedian and voice actor. He was hired to replace [Viggo Mortensen](/wiki/Viggo_Mortensen), who had walked out because of compensation issues, in [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (video game)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (video game)"). He went on to voice Aragorn in *[The Battle for Middle-earth Anthology](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_Anthology "The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth Anthology")*, and provided the voice of the ranger Elagost in [EA](/wiki/Electronic_Arts "Electronic Arts")'s alternative [fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring")-game *[The Third Age](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Third_Age "The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age")*.
