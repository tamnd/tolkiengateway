**Catherine Élise "Cate" Blanchett** (born 1969) played [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) in both [*The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), and [*The Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").

## Filmography {#Filmography}

- [2001](/wiki/2001): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* - [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel)
- [2002](/wiki/2002): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* - [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel)
- [2003](/wiki/2003): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* - [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel)
- [2012](/wiki/2012): *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)* - [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel)
- [2013](/wiki/2013): *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)* - [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel)
- [2014](/wiki/2014): *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)* - [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel)
