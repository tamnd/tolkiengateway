**Catherine Élise "Cate" Blanchett** (born 1969) played [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) in both [*The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), and [*The Hobbit* film series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).

## Filmography {#Filmography}

- [2001](../_/2001.md): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* - [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md)
- [2002](../_/2002.md): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* - [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md)
- [2003](../_/2003.md): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* - [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md)
- [2012](../_/2012.md): *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md)* - [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md)
- [2013](../_/2013.md): *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md)* - [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md)
- [2014](../_/2014.md): *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md)* - [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md)
