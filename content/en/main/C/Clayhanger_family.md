## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Clayhanger* is an English placename, the name of three hamlets in England (one in West Midlands, one in Cheshire and one in Devon). It comes from [OE](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") *clǣghangra*, "Clayey wooded slope".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> As a family name, it is best known from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Burnett" title="wikipedia:Arnold Burnett">Arnold Burnett</a>'s <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Clayhanger_Family" title="wikipedia:The Clayhanger Family"><i>Clayhanger</i> novels</a> published between 1910 and 1918. The name would likely be used for a family that resides on a clayey wooded slope.
