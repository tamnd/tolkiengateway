**Celbaros** was a town in [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Celb_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Celb-1">[1]</a></sup> It was most likely identified by [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) with <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheltenham" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Cheltenham</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Celb_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Celb-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Celbaros* is in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md). While the meaning of the name isn't entirely clear, the second element seems to be <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Baros&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Baros (page does not exist)">baros</a></i> ("hamlet").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Celbaros only appears in one of the drawings of the three early heraldic devices made by Tolkien (the other two depict [Cortirion](Cortirion.md) and [Tavrobel](../T/Tavrobel.md)), however, unlike the others, Celbaros was never incorporated into the mythology.<sup id="cite_ref-Celb_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Celb-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is likely that Celbaros was identified with the English town of Cheltenham, where J.R.R. Tolkien and [Edith Bratt](../E/Edith_Bratt.md) were reunited on [January 8](../J/January_8.md), [1913](../_/1913.md) - and after a separation of three years, Tolkien asked Edith to marry him.<sup id="cite_ref-Celb_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Celb-1">[1]</a></sup>
