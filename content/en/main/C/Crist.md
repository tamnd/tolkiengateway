<i><b>crist</b></i> means "cleaver, sword" in [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Didier Willis](../D/Didier_Willis.md) has suggested that *crist* (retaining the same meaning) could be valid [Neo-Sindarin](../N/Neo-Elvish.md).<sup id="cite_ref-DW_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DW-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md): KIRÍS ("cut")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
