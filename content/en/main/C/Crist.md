<i><b>crist</b></i> means "cleaver, sword" in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Didier Willis](/wiki/Didier_Willis) has suggested that *crist* (retaining the same meaning) could be valid [Neo-Sindarin](/wiki/Neo-Elvish "Neo-Elvish").<sup id="cite_ref-DW_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DW-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme"): KIRÍS ("cut")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
