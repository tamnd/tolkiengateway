The **Cliff House** was the house of [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md).

## Description {#Description}

The Cliff House was as the name suggests, a house on a cliff. It is located near the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=North_Pole&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="North Pole (page does not exist)">North Pole</a>. The house has a cellar built into the cliffs.<sup id="cite_ref-l25_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l25-1">[1]</a></sup> The house had a garden that was tended by the [Snow Man](../S/Snow_Man.md) but little grew except snowdrops and frost-ferns.<sup id="cite_ref-l26_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l26-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Father Christmas moved to the Cliff House in November or December of [1925](../_/1925.md). He had previously lived at the [Christmas House](Christmas_House.md) but was forced to move after the [North Polar Bear](../N/North_Polar_Bear.md) had climbed the roof to try to rescue Father Christmas' hood that had blown up by the wind and had fallen through the roof and made a hole in the living room.<sup id="cite_ref-l25_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l25-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [1932](../_/1932.md), for several days Father Christmas and the [North Polar Bear](../N/North_Polar_Bear.md) found items in the cellar missing and in disarray, originally blaming [Paksu](../P/Paksu.md) and [Valkotukka](../V/Valkotukka.md). When they came down to discover all the items from the main cellar missing, the North Polar Bear said he smelled [Goblin](../O/Orcs.md#Other_writings) and they discovered a tunnel. The tunnel was too small for them to fit through but after going to the caves to confer with [Mr Cave Bear](../M/Mr_Cave_Bear.md) they discovered a tunnel built by Goblins leading from the caves to the Cliff House's cellar.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [1934](../_/1934.md), Paksu and Valkotukka stole Father Christmas's paints and painted over the white walls of the cellar.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
