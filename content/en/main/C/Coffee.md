**Coffee** was a beverage known at least by the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) and the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) in the Third Age. When [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) entertained [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) in April [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"), he had to set a big jug of coffee in the hearth, because one of the five dwarves who entered together ([Dori](/wiki/Dori), [Ori](/wiki/Ori), [Nori](/wiki/Nori), [Óin](/wiki/%C3%93in), and [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in)) asked for it.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
