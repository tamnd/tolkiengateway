**Coffee** was a beverage known at least by the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) and the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) in the Third Age. When [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) entertained [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) in [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) in April [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md), he had to set a big jug of coffee in the hearth, because one of the five dwarves who entered together ([Dori](../D/Dori.md), [Ori](../O/Ori.md), [Nori](../N/Nori.md), [Óin](../_/Óin.md), and [Glóin](../G/Glóin.md)) asked for it.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
