**Cormarë** ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya")**Ringday**) was a festival honoring the birthday of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), destroyer of [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").

## History {#History}

In [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) the [King Elessar](/wiki/King_Elessar)'s [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning) calendar, 30 [Yavannië](/wiki/Yavanni%C3%AB) was made a festival in honor of the [Ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearer), since it corresponded with Frodo's birthday, [22 September](/wiki/22_September) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar). Except in leap-years 30 Yavannië corresponded with 21 September in the Shire Calendar (due to its [Overlithe](/wiki/Overlithe) leap-day at midsummer), so the New Reckoning's leap-day was inserted between *Yavannië* and its [Enderi](/wiki/Enderi) and Frodo's birthday feast was doubled in leap-years.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It's not clear if the last day of the month of *Yavannië* was still referred to as "30 Yavannië", or if this day, and the leap-day, were simply called *Cormarë*.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Cormarë* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word glossed as "Ringday", apparently being a compound of *[corma](/wiki/Corma "Corma")* ("ring")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the suffixal form of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=R%C3%A9&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ré (page does not exist)">ré</a></i> ("day").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Outside the legendarium {#Outside_the_legendarium}

22 September is a popular "ceremonial" date for founding [Tolkien societies](/wiki/Category:Tolkien_Societies "Category:Tolkien Societies"). [Unquendor](/wiki/Unquendor) (the Netherlands), [Eredain](/wiki/Eredain) (Switzerland), [Imladris](/wiki/Imladris,_Danmarks_Tolkienforening "Imladris, Danmarks Tolkienforening") (Denmark) and several others were founded on this day. Another ceremonial day is [3 January](/wiki/3_January), the birthday of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

Among [fans](/wiki/Tolkien_fandom "Tolkien fandom"), 22 September is also commonly observed as the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hobbit_Day&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hobbit Day (page does not exist)">Hobbit Day</a>.
