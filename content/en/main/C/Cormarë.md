**Cormarë** ([Q.](../Q/Quenya.md)**Ringday**) was a festival honoring the birthday of [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), destroyer of [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).

## History {#History}

In [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) the [King Elessar](../K/King_Elessar.md)'s [New Reckoning](../N/New_Reckoning.md) calendar, 30 [Yavannië](../Y/Yavannië.md) was made a festival in honor of the [Ring-bearer](../R/Ring-bearer.md), since it corresponded with Frodo's birthday, [22 September](../_/22_September.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md). Except in leap-years 30 Yavannië corresponded with 21 September in the Shire Calendar (due to its [Overlithe](../O/Overlithe.md) leap-day at midsummer), so the New Reckoning's leap-day was inserted between *Yavannië* and its [Enderi](../E/Enderi.md) and Frodo's birthday feast was doubled in leap-years.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It's not clear if the last day of the month of *Yavannië* was still referred to as "30 Yavannië", or if this day, and the leap-day, were simply called *Cormarë*.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Cormarë* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word glossed as "Ringday", apparently being a compound of *[corma](Corma.md)* ("ring")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the suffixal form of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=R%C3%A9&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ré (page does not exist)">ré</a></i> ("day").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Outside the legendarium {#Outside_the_legendarium}

22 September is a popular "ceremonial" date for founding [Tolkien societies](../../category/T/Tolkien_Societies.md). [Unquendor](../U/Unquendor.md) (the Netherlands), [Eredain](../E/Eredain.md) (Switzerland), [Imladris](../I/Imladris,_Danmarks_Tolkienforening.md) (Denmark) and several others were founded on this day. Another ceremonial day is [3 January](../_/3_January.md), the birthday of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

Among [fans](../T/Tolkien_fandom.md), 22 September is also commonly observed as the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hobbit_Day&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hobbit Day (page does not exist)">Hobbit Day</a>.
