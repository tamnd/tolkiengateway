**Cross-roads of the Fallen King** is the thirteenth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)"). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) continues to lead [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) and [Sam](/wiki/Sam) towards [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). Frodo voices his concerns to Sam that he may not come back from this journey, which Sam dismisses as "morbid thinking", saying that they are going *there and back again*, just like [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo) did.

The three come across the statue of a king, defiled and decapitated, its head replaced by a large boulder. As they walk past the stone head on the ground, the sun briefly comes out, illuminating the king's head, crowned with flowers lit up in the sunlight.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Mr Frodo, look! The King has got a crown again.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Sam</cite></p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

The moment passes as clouds cover the sun again, and Gollum urges them to continue.

## Differences {#Differences}

The second half of this scene is very closely aligned with the book. The statue of the king resembles that which Frodo and Sam encounter in the chapter [Journey to the Cross-Roads](/wiki/Journey_to_the_Cross-Roads). In the glow of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun), Frodo sees "a huge sitting figure, still and solemn as the great stone kings of [Argonath](/wiki/Argonath)... Its head was gone, and in its place was set in mockery a round rough-hewn stone". A minor difference is that, in the source material, it is Frodo who notices the king's head and says *"Look! The king has got a crown again!"* to Sam.<sup id="cite_ref-TT_Journey_p._702_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT_Journey_p._702-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Notes {#Notes}

In the book, the king's crown is described as being "a coronal of silver and gold".<sup id="cite_ref-TT_Journey_p._702_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT_Journey_p._702-1">[1]</a></sup> This is the name of the piece of music from *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King - The Complete Recordings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_-_The_Complete_Recordings)* which plays over this scene.
