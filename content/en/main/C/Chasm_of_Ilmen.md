The **Chasm of Ilmen** ([Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md): *Ilmen-assa*)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> is the gap between the edge of [the World](../A/Ambar.md) and [Vaiya](../V/Vaiya.md). It is filled with the air of [Ilmen](../I/Ilmen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-fashion_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fashion-2">[2]</a></sup>

Where the interior seas fell down the Chasm of Ilmen, their waterfalls made bridges of ice which close the chasm. The ice extended to all [Vaiya](../V/Vaiya.md) and even the [Ilurambar](../I/Ilurambar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-fashion_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fashion-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Tilion](../T/Tilion.md) who guides the [Moon](../M/Moon.md) plunges in the chasm between the western shores of the earth and Vaiya, the Outer Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-fashion_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fashion-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LRQ_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LRQ-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Christopher Tolkien](Christopher_Tolkien.md) notes that in the [Ambarkanta](../A/Ambarkanta.md), as well as an earlier unfinished version of the Silmarillion (c. 1937),<sup id="cite_ref-LRQ_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LRQ-3">[3]</a></sup> there is a chasm *between* the Earth and the Outer Sea (=Vaiya), into which Tilion guides the Moon. However for the published [Silmarillion](../S/Silmarillion.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> he preferred a later passage (c. 1951)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> where [Tilion](../T/Tilion.md) plunges into an unnamed chasm which is *beyond* the Outer Sea (=[Ekkaia](../E/Ekkaia.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> In a later note, Christopher accepts that although his father wrote that, it is not what he intended, and the Chasm lies before the Outer Sea, rather than beyond it.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> <span></span>
