**Cemendur** (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[keˈmendur]</span>) is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Servant of the [Earth](/wiki/Ambar "Ambar")" (from *[kemen](/wiki/Kemen "Kemen")*, "earth" and *[-ndur](/wiki/-ndur)*, "servant") and may refer to:

- [Cemendur](</wiki/Cemendur_(son_of_Axantur)> "Cemendur (son of Axantur)") (born [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [575](/wiki/Second_Age_575 "Second Age 575")), son of [Axantur](/wiki/Axantur) and a minor [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") royal.
- [Cemendur](</wiki/Cemendur_(King_of_Gondor)> "Cemendur (King of Gondor)") ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3399](/wiki/Second_Age_3399 "Second Age 3399") - [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [238](/wiki/Third_Age_238 "Third Age 238")), the fourth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).
