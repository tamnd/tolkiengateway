**Cemendur** (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[keˈmendur]</span>) is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "Servant of the [Earth](../A/Ambar.md)" (from *[kemen](../K/Kemen.md)*, "earth" and *[-ndur](../_/-ndur.md)*, "servant") and may refer to:

- [Cemendur](<Cemendur_(son_of_Axantur).md>) (born [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [575](../S/Second_Age_575.md)), son of [Axantur](../A/Axantur.md) and a minor [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) royal.
- [Cemendur](<Cemendur_(King_of_Gondor).md>) ([S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3399](../S/Second_Age_3399.md) - [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [238](../T/Third_Age_238.md)), the fourth [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).
