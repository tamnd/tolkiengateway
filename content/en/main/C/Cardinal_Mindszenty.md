**"Cardinal Mindszenty"** is the title of [a letter](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Letters not published in The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien"), co-signed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), sent to the editor of *The Times* (published on [28 January](/wiki/28_January) [1949](/wiki/1949); p. 5). The letter, written to protest against the arrest in 1949 of Cardinal <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%B3zsef_Mindszenty" title="wikipedia:József Mindszenty">József Mindszenty</a> of Hungary, is signed by the Honorary President and nine Honorary Vice Presidents (among which Tolkien is included) of <a class="external text" href="http://www.newman.org.uk/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">the Newman Association</a>.

The letter appears to have been largely unknown among Tolkien scholars, until it was discovered by [Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson) in [2011](/wiki/2011). Anderson comments that "*Tolkien's affiliation with the Newman Society was previously undocumented*", and finds it noteworthy that Tolkien during his busy 1940s managed to "*volunteer work for the Association devoted to John Henry Newman, founder of the [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham) Oratory where Tolkien himself had been educated as a boy*".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
