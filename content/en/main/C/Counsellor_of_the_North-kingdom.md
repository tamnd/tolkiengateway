**Counsellor of the North-kingdom** was a title given to some members of the royal council of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) established after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

After his accession, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) established a council in [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), and in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [13](/wiki/Fourth_Age_13 "Fourth Age 13"), he chose three Counsellors of the North-kingdom from the people of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland).<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-1">[1]</a></sup> These Counsellors were those appointed to the positions of the [Thain](/wiki/Thain) and [Mayor of the Shire](/wiki/Mayor_of_the_Shire), and the [Master of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland).<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the time of the appointment, these offices were held by [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) I, [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), and [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck), and they would remain in these posts for the nearly fifty years until [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [61](/wiki/Fourth_Age_61 "Fourth Age 61"), when [Sam Gamgee](/wiki/Sam_Gamgee) was said to have passed over the [Sea](/wiki/Sea).<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-1">[1]</a></sup> Two years later, [Merry](/wiki/Merry) and [Pippin](/wiki/Pippin) resigned their offices to their sons and removed to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-1">[1]</a></sup> It is unknown if they were succeded as Counsellors by their heirs.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
