**Counsellor of the North-kingdom** was a title given to some members of the royal council of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) established after the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

## History {#History}

After his accession, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) established a council in [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md), and in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [13](../F/Fourth_Age_13.md), he chose three Counsellors of the North-kingdom from the people of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) and [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-1">[1]</a></sup> These Counsellors were those appointed to the positions of the [Thain](../T/Thain.md) and [Mayor of the Shire](../M/Mayor_of_the_Shire.md), and the [Master of Buckland](../M/Master_of_Buckland.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the time of the appointment, these offices were held by [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) I, [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), and [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md), and they would remain in these posts for the nearly fifty years until [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [61](../F/Fourth_Age_61.md), when [Sam Gamgee](../S/Sam_Gamgee.md) was said to have passed over the [Sea](../S/Sea.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-1">[1]</a></sup> Two years later, [Merry](../M/Merry.md) and [Pippin](../P/Pippin.md) resigned their offices to their sons and removed to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-1">[1]</a></sup> It is unknown if they were succeded as Counsellors by their heirs.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
