In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), **Ciryatanor**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> or **Anbalukkhôr**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> was a [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor "Númenor") colony that turned into an independent Kingdom allied with [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) under the reign of [Akhôrahil the Ringwraith](/wiki/Fifth_of_the_Nazg%C3%BBl "Fifth of the Nazgûl"). Its capital was *Marath Carnadúnê* ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") “Tower of the Red Sunset”, [S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Barad Carannûn*).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> The Black Númenóreans of Ciryatanor were probably referred to as Ciryedain.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

The colony was founded as a Númenórean client-kingdom by Ciryamir in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1904&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1904 (page does not exist)">1904</a>, at the northern flank of the [Yellow Mountains](/wiki/Yellow_Mountains), and was named after [Tar-Ciryatan](/wiki/Tar-Ciryatan). In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1919&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1919 (page does not exist)">1919</a>, Ciryamir's son Akhôrahil ascended to the throne after having influenced his father into suicide a year earlier. As the self-declared Storm King, he expanded the kingdom along the Yellow Mountains through various conquests in [Sîrayn](/wiki/S%C3%AErayn): Chennacatt in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1929&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1929 (page does not exist)">1929</a>, Isra in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1933&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1933 (page does not exist)">1933</a>, and Kirmiesra in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1979&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1979 (page does not exist)">1979</a>. Pushing through Harshandat, his armies had reached the Bay of Ormal by [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1999&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1999 (page does not exist)">1999</a>.

The Storm King renounced his allegiance to Númenor in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2250&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2250 (page does not exist)">2250</a>. The army of [Tar-Ancalimon](/wiki/Tar-Ancalimon) landed in Tulwang in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [2280](/wiki/Second_Age_2280 "Second Age 2280"). The Storm King's armies were defeated and his capital was razed. Akhôrahil himself fled to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-GreaterHarad_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GreaterHarad-5">[4]</a></sup> It is unclear what happened to Ciryatanor until it is mentioned again in the Third Age.

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

After [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1050](/wiki/Third_Age_1050 "Third Age 1050"), Akhôrahil moved into the fortress of [Ny Chennacatt](/wiki/Ny_Chennacatt), hoping to reclaim his former kingdom. He assembled a new army, the [Army of the Southern Dragon](/wiki/Army_of_the_Southern_Dragon), and secured the allegiance of Orc-lords from the Yellow Mountains and traitors to the Free Peoples of Greater Harad.<sup id="cite_ref-GreaterHarad_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GreaterHarad-5">[4]</a></sup> This army, headed by [Utchû](/wiki/Army_of_the_Southern_Dragon#Utchû "Army of the Southern Dragon"), launched campaigns against Ciryatanor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1660&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1660 (page does not exist)">1660</a> and [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1705&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1705 (page does not exist)">1705</a>, and it is finally conquered in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1802&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1802 (page does not exist)">1802</a>. Barad Carannûn is the capital, so presumably it had been rebuilt since its razing. Utchû turned on his liege twelve years later. The new Army of the Southern Dragon attacked Utchû's Ciryatanor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1815&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1815 (page does not exist)">1815</a>, and a year later, Akhôrahil visited the city for the last time to reorganize its leadership.

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_2013&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 2013 (page does not exist)">2013</a>, a civil war split the kingdoms of Tulwang and Ciryatanor into tiny petty kingdoms. The [Sun Lord](/wiki/Far_Harad#Portrayal_in_adaptations "Far Harad") Pon Ifta conquered Ciryatanor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_2205&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 2205 (page does not exist)">2205</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

## Fandom {#Fandom}

In fan projects elaborating on Greater Harad, such as the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=New_Notion_Club_Archives&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="New Notion Club Archives (page does not exist)">New Notion Club Archives</a> and [Realms in Exile](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Realms_in_Exile "The Lord of the Rings: Realms in Exile"), the kingdom is instead known as **An-Balkûmagân**, with the reasoning being that Ciryatanor is not a name the [King's Men](/wiki/King%27s_Men) would use and that Anbalukkhôr is not a entirely correct equivalent either.
