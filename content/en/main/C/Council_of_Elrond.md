<p>The <b>Council of Elrond</b> was a council led by <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a>, held on <a href="/wiki/25_October" title="25 October">25 October</a> of <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3018" title="Third Age 3018">3018</a><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>. The attendees were called together at the essential moment by fate, each thinking they were guided only by circumstance.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-2">[2]</a></sup> The main purpose of the council was to discuss what should be done with <a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring" title="The One Ring">the One Ring</a>, which was brought there by the <a href="/wiki/Ring-bearers" title="Ring-bearers">Ring-bearer</a> <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo Baggins</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-2">[2]</a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Attendees">Attendees</span></h2>
<p>The following people are known to have attended the Council:
</p>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a>. As convener of the council and de facto ruler of Rivendell, Elrond headed the Council.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a>, one of the <a href="/wiki/Wizards" title="Wizards">Wizards</a>, was one of the <a href="/wiki/Wise" title="Wise">Wise</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo Baggins</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Ring-bearers" title="Ring-bearers">Ring-bearer</a>, came to Rivendell on the advice of Gandalf.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a>, a former Ring-bearer, and the &#34;Ring-finder&#34;.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Glorfindel" title="Glorfindel">Glorfindel</a>, a <a href="/wiki/Noldor" title="Noldor">Noldo</a> of Rivendell who had aided Frodo earlier on his travel to Rivendell.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Erestor" title="Erestor">Erestor</a>, and several unnamed advisers of Elrond&#39;s household.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>, a <a href="/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North" title="Rangers of the North">Ranger</a>, <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain" title="Chieftains of the Dúnedain">Chieftain of the Dúnedain</a> and <a href="/wiki/Heir_of_Isildur" title="Heir of Isildur">Heir of Isildur</a>, who had been Frodo&#39;s travel companion, guide and aide since <a href="/wiki/Bree" title="Bree">Bree</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Boromir" title="Boromir">Boromir</a>, son of the <a href="/wiki/Denethor" title="Denethor">Steward of Gondor</a>. His brother <a href="/wiki/Faramir" title="Faramir">Faramir</a> had a <a href="/wiki/Seek_for_the_Sword_that_was_broken" title="Seek for the Sword that was broken">prophetic dream</a> thrice, and he himself once. It spoke of &#34;<a href="/wiki/Narsil" title="Narsil">the Sword that was broken</a>, &#34;<a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Imladris</a>&#34;, &#34;<a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Isildur%27s_Bane" title="Isildur&#39;s Bane">Isildur&#39;s Bane</a>&#34; and a &#34;<a href="/wiki/Hobbits" title="Hobbits">Halfling</a>&#34;. In order to find the meaning of the dream, Boromir went to &#34;Imladris&#34; - Rivendell.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in" title="Glóin">Glóin</a> and his son <a href="/wiki/Gimli" title="Gimli">Gimli</a> were sent by <a href="/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot" title="Dáin Ironfoot">Dáin II Ironfoot</a> to warn Bilbo about a messenger from <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a> who appeared at the gate of <a href="/wiki/Lonely_Mountain" title="Lonely Mountain">Erebor</a>, and asked for information of the One Ring. As king <a href="/wiki/Brand" title="Brand">Brand</a> of <a href="/wiki/Dale" title="Dale">Dale</a> was also encountered, Dáin decided to send messengers to Rivendell. They also came to seek news of what happened to <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Balin%27s_Colony" title="Balin&#39;s Colony">Balin&#39;s Colony</a>, which entered <a href="/wiki/Moria" title="Moria">Moria</a> thirty years before.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Legolas" title="Legolas">Legolas</a>, was sent by his father <a href="/wiki/Thranduil" title="Thranduil">Thranduil</a> with ill news. <a href="/wiki/Gollum" title="Gollum">Gollum</a>, put in the custody of the <a href="/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood" title="Elves of Mirkwood">Elves of Mirkwood</a> by Aragorn, managed to escape after a surprise attack of <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs</a>.</li>
<li><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Galdor_of_the_Havens" title="Galdor of the Havens">Galdor of the Havens</a>, a messenger of <a href="/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan" title="Círdan">Círdan</a> who just happened to be present at the time. His role in the Council was no larger than asking a couple of questions.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span></h2>
<p>At the Council, Gandalf, Elrond and Bilbo told the history of the Ring to all. At hearing the potential of &#34;Isildur&#39;s Bane&#34;, Boromir proposed to use the Ring to defend his native <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>, and overthrow <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>, its creator. Gandalf and Elrond were against it, and stated that the Ring was the essence of Sauron, and thus would corrupt and ultimately destroy whoever tried to use it. To dissolve Galdor&#39;s doubts, Gandalf spoke about the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ring_Verse" title="Ring Verse">Ring Verse</a> that appeared on the Ring, and recited it in <a href="/wiki/Black_Speech" title="Black Speech">Black Speech</a>, angering Elrond in the process.
</p>
<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/2/2b/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg/250px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="143" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/2/2b/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg/375px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/2/2b/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg/500px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg 2x" data-file-width="700" data-file-height="399"/></a><figcaption><i>The Lord of Rivendell</i> by <a href="/wiki/Peter_Xavier_Price" title="Peter Xavier Price">Peter Xavier Price</a></figcaption></figure>
<p>The fate of the Ring was considered. The Ring was indestructible as it could be unmade only where it was created, in the <a href="/wiki/Cracks_of_Doom" title="Cracks of Doom">Crack of Doom</a> in <a href="/wiki/Mount_Doom" title="Mount Doom">Mount Doom</a>, and several solutions were proposed. It was suggested to give it to <a href="/wiki/Tom_Bombadil" title="Tom Bombadil">Tom Bombadil</a> who could not be corrupted, or to send it to <a href="/wiki/Valinor" title="Valinor">Valinor</a> under the guardianship of the <a href="/wiki/Valar" title="Valar">Valar</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Calaquendi" title="Calaquendi">High Elves</a>. But it was deemed that the Ring could not be hidden or disposed, as it would remain dangerous for the generations to come. 
</p><p>Eventually it was decided that the only way to be free of Sauron and the Ring was to cast the Ring where it was made.
</p><p>It then became necessary to decide who should take responsibility for such a perilous undertaking. Bilbo offered to finish the job he had started, but Gandalf told him he could not take back the Ring due to having possessed it for some time. Eventually Frodo surprised everyone including himself by telling them he would take the Ring. After some thought Elrond agreed. At this point Sam emerged from hiding and demanded to accompany Frodo, and since it was hardly possible to separate them Elrond agreed.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-2">[2]</a></sup>
</p><p>Who would further accompany the &#34;<a href="/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="Fellowship of the Ring">Company of the Ring</a>&#34; was the subject of some debate for the following two months, although it was clear that Gandalf, Aragorn, Boromir, Gimli and Legolas would join them. Elrond opted for two more; &#34;Nine walkers&#34; would counter the &#34;<a href="/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl" title="Nazgûl">Nine Riders</a>&#34;. He originally intended to send two of his household, such as Glorfindel, but Gandalf opted to include <a href="/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a> and <a href="/wiki/Peregrin_Took" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a> instead, as he deemed friendship and loyalty more important than strength.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
</p>

</p>
<dl><dd>The Council of Elrond begins without introduction in the sixth episode. Glóin, Galdor and Erestor are omitted.</dd></dl>

<p><b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>
</p>
<dl><dd>The attendants are described as being &#34;summoned&#34;, and in the extended edition of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Two Towers</a></i>, it is explicitly stated that the Council was called because the Ring was found; Boromir was ordered by his father to claim it. This is in contrast to the original text, where fate seems to bring them together.</dd></dl>
<dl><dd>The process of the council is significantly shortened. There are no or minimal references to the history of the Second Age, the Rings of Power, the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">current War</a>, the politics of the races and the characters&#39; backgrounds. The only characters who speak are the protagonists who will later form the <a href="/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="Fellowship of the Ring">Company of the Ring</a>. Unrelated characters (such as Bilbo, Erestor or Glóin) are absent or don&#39;t speak at all. According to <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">Decipher cards</a>, some of the non-speaking participants are tradesmen from <a href="/wiki/Dale" title="Dale">Dale</a> and <a href="/wiki/Lake-town" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a>. <a href="/wiki/Figwit" title="Figwit">Figwit</a> is also present.</dd></dl>
<dl><dd>The brief council is shadowed by sudden chaos and arguing over who would take the responsibility of the Ring. It is seen that the fight is inflamed by the Ring&#39;s evil influence as it sits in the middle of the court. Gandalf puts an end by reciting the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ring_Verse" title="Ring Verse">Ring Verse</a>, darkening the area and quieting everyone shouting.</dd></dl>
<dl><dd>The composition of the Company of the Ring was decided at the council. Sam was not the only one eavesdropping; Merry and Pippin were with him doing the same.</dd></dl>
<p><b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>
</p>
<dl><dd>Only Elrond, Legolas, Gimli, Boromir, Aragorn, Gandalf and Frodo are mentioned, though the other three Hobbits are also present. The first seven are quickly chosen, but as Elrond tells of two great warriors, Merry and Pippin barge in.</dd></dl>
