<i><b>Creating The Lord of the Rings Symphony</b></i> is a [2004](../_/2004.md) documentary which includes excerpts of live concert footage, commentary by Howard Shore, and the illustrations of Alan Lee and John Howe.

This documentary can also be found on *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* Platinum series Special Extended Edition Collector's Gift Set.
