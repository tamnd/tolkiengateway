In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), **Cameth Brin** ([Bl.](/wiki/Languages_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing#Blarm "Languages in Middle-earth Role Playing") “Twisted Hill”) was a massive, fortified hill in the [Trollshaws](/wiki/Trollshaws). The fortress was once the capital of [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur).<sup id="cite_ref-travguide_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-travguide-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Cameth Brin rose above *Glin a-Creag* ([Du.](/wiki/Dunlendish "Dunlendish") “Valley of the Rock”), a fertile valley in the otherwise rocky Trollshaws. [Petty-dwarves](/wiki/Petty-dwarves) established halls in the hills. Despite the fertility of the valley, the hill was later shunned by Men because it was inhabited by evil spirits who were called the Ta-Fa-Lisch (“Ghosts of the Little People”).<sup id="cite_ref-travguide_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-travguide-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-land2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-land2-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_164&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 164 (page does not exist)">164</a>, a [Hillman](/wiki/Hillmen "Hillmen") High Chief named Mong-Finn launched a rebellion against [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor). Desperate for power, he entered Cameth Brin alone to forge an alliance with the Ta-Fa-Lisch. His rebellion failed, and the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) gained control of the hill, cleansing the spirits within. [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Arnor)> "Eldacar (King of Arnor)") expanded the hill into a fortress. The construction was complete in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [339](/wiki/Third_Age_339 "Third Age 339"), after which it became the capital of the province of Rhudaur.<sup id="cite_ref-travguide_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-travguide-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Eldacar also had barracks constructed at the base of hill, named Tanoth Brin (Bl. “Beneath the Hill”).<sup id="cite_ref-travguide_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-travguide-1">[1]</a></sup>

When Rhudaur falls under the rule of a Hillman usurper, Cameth Brin is besieged by King Celervellon of [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan). In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1235&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1235 (page does not exist)">1235</a>, in what would later be termed the “Disaster of Cameth Brin”, Celervellon's army was surprised by Orcs from [Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad). While the army survived, the King, his sons and other rulers were slain.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>

After the 18th century of the Third Age, the fortress was abandoned.<sup id="cite_ref-travguide_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-travguide-1">[1]</a></sup>
