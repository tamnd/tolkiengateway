<i><b>Chaucer as a Philologist: The Reeve&#39;s Tale</b></i> is an essay by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), published in *[Transactions of the Philological Society](../T/Transactions_of_the_Philological_Society_1934.md)* for [1934](../_/1934.md).

The essay was delivered at a meeting of the Philological Society in Oxford on [16 May](../_/16_May.md) [1931](../_/1931.md). Tolkien discusses <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Chaucer" title="wikipedia:Geoffrey Chaucer">Chaucer</a>'s use of the Northern Middle English dialect as a linguistic joke in "The Reeve's Tale", part of the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Canterbury_Tales" title="wikipedia:The Canterbury Tales">Canterbury Tales</a></i>.

It was reprinted in [2008](../_/2008.md) in *[Tolkien Studies 5](../T/Tolkien_Studies_5.md)*. It was also reprinted in [2024](../_/2024.md) in *[Tolkien on Chaucer, 1913-1959](../T/Tolkien_on_Chaucer,_1913-1959.md)*, but incorporating Tolkien's own corrections.
