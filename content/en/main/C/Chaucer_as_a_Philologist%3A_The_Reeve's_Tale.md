<i><b>Chaucer as a Philologist: The Reeve&#39;s Tale</b></i> is an essay by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), published in *[Transactions of the Philological Society](/wiki/Transactions_of_the_Philological_Society_1934 "Transactions of the Philological Society 1934")* for [1934](/wiki/1934).

The essay was delivered at a meeting of the Philological Society in Oxford on [16 May](/wiki/16_May) [1931](/wiki/1931). Tolkien discusses <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Chaucer" title="wikipedia:Geoffrey Chaucer">Chaucer</a>'s use of the Northern Middle English dialect as a linguistic joke in "The Reeve's Tale", part of the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Canterbury_Tales" title="wikipedia:The Canterbury Tales">Canterbury Tales</a></i>.

It was reprinted in [2008](/wiki/2008) in *[Tolkien Studies 5](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies_5)*. It was also reprinted in [2024](/wiki/2024) in *[Tolkien on Chaucer, 1913-1959](/wiki/Tolkien_on_Chaucer,_1913-1959)*, but incorporating Tolkien's own corrections.
