<i><b>caran</b></i> is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for "red". Compare with its [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) equivalent, *[Carnë](/wiki/Carn%C3%AB)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir)* = "Red-face"
- *[Caradhras](/wiki/Caradhras)* (*caran* + *[ras](/wiki/Ras "Ras")*, with assimilation) = "Redhorn"

Possible examples are

- *[Carcharoth](/wiki/Carcharoth)* (\**carn* + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Carach&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Carach (page does not exist)">carach</a></i>, with liquid mutation) = "[Red Maw](/wiki/Red_Maw)"
- *[Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan)* (\**carn* + *[dol](/wiki/Dol "Dol")* + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-and&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-and (page does not exist)">-and</a></i>) = "country of red hills"
- *[Carn Dûm](/wiki/Carn_D%C3%BBm)*
