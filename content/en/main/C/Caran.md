<i><b>caran</b></i> is the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word for "red". Compare with its [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) equivalent, *[Carnë](Carnë.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Caranthir](Caranthir.md)* = "Red-face"
- *[Caradhras](Caradhras.md)* (*caran* + *[ras](../R/Ras.md)*, with assimilation) = "Redhorn"

Possible examples are

- *[Carcharoth](Carcharoth.md)* (\**carn* + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Carach&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Carach (page does not exist)">carach</a></i>, with liquid mutation) = "[Red Maw](../R/Red_Maw.md)"
- *[Cardolan](Cardolan.md)* (\**carn* + *[dol](../D/Dol.md)* + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=-and&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-and (page does not exist)">-and</a></i>) = "country of red hills"
- *[Carn Dûm](Carn_Dûm.md)*
