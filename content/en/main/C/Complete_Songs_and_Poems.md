<i><b>Complete Songs &amp; Poems</b></i> is a compilation album by the [Tolkien Ensemble](/wiki/Tolkien_Ensemble). It features all 69 songs which had been released on their 4 previous albums. The collection also includes a 112-page booklet.

The cover artwork was illustrated by [Margrethe II of Denmark](/wiki/Margrethe_II_of_Denmark).
