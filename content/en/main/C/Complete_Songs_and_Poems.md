<i><b>Complete Songs &amp; Poems</b></i> is a compilation album by the [Tolkien Ensemble](../T/Tolkien_Ensemble.md). It features all 69 songs which had been released on their 4 previous albums. The collection also includes a 112-page booklet.

The cover artwork was illustrated by [Margrethe II of Denmark](../M/Margrethe_II_of_Denmark.md).
