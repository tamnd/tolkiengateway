**caer** and **pae** are words for the number ten (10, X) in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).

In [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md), the precursor of Sindarin, "ten" is <i><b>caer</b></i>. The word derives from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) KAYAN-, KAYAR-.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
