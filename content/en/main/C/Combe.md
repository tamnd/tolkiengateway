**Combe** was a village in [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land).<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> It was located northeast of the village [Bree](/wiki/Bree) north of the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road)<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-2">[2]</a></sup> in a deep valley a little further eastward from [Staddle](/wiki/Staddle),<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> which was on the gentler south-eastern slopes of [Bree-hill](/wiki/Bree-hill).<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) left the [Prancing Pony](/wiki/Prancing_Pony) inn in Bree in the morning of [30 September](/wiki/30_September), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> even many inhabitants from Combe were crowded on the road in Bree to watch their departure.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> On that day having walked for some miles on the East Road, Aragorn and the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) could see rising smoke in a deep hollow north of the East Road that indicated where Combe was located.<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *Coomb* means a deep (but usually not very large) valley and occurs as an element in place-names spelt *-comb*, *-cumb* or *-combe*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The name *Combe* (*Coomb*) has been modelled on a relic of a "British" (i.e. Celtic) name that was chosen for its meaning, because the survival of traces of the older language of the Bree-men resembled the survival of [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) elements in England.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggests that *Combe* means "valley" and that it is a Celtic name, which is derived from British <i>*kumba</i> and [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh) *cwm*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

The real-life [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh) place-name <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cwm,_Blaenau_Gwent" title="wikipedia:Cwm, Blaenau Gwent">Cwm</a></i> means "valley".
