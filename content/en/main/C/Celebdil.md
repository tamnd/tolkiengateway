**Celebdil**, also known as **Silvertine** and **Zirakzigil**, was one of the three peaks that stood above the [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") city of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"). The other two were [Caradhras](/wiki/Caradhras) (*Redhorn*) and [Fanuidhol](/wiki/Fanuidhol) (*Cloudyhead*).

Celebdil was the southernmost of the [Mountains of Moria](/wiki/Mountains_of_Moria) and the [Endless Stair](/wiki/Endless_Stair) led from the deepest halls of Khazad-dûm up to [Durin's Tower](/wiki/Durin%27s_Tower) on the peak of the mountain. There the [Battle of the Peak](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Peak) between [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane) took place.<sup id="cite_ref-White_Rider_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-White_Rider-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Celebdil* is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name of [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) *Zirakzigil*, translated as the mountain Silvertine. *Celebdil* contains *[celeb](/wiki/Celeb "Celeb")* ("silver") and a derivative of the [root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") [TIL](/wiki/TIL).<sup id="cite_ref-S_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Silvertine* contains *tine*, "spike, sharp horn".<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Tolkien wrote to his son [Michael](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien "Michael Tolkien") that on a trip to Switzerland in his youth he had regretted leaving a view of two mountains: the Jungfrau and "the *Silberhorn* sharp against dark blue: the *Silvertine (Celebdil)* of my dreams."<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
