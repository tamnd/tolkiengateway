**Colleen Doran** ([July 24](/wiki/24_July "24 July"), [1968](/wiki/1968)) is an American artist and fantasy writer. In addition to creating over 200 [Topps Masterpieces](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Masterpieces "The Lord of the Rings Masterpieces"), she has also appeared at various [Ring\*Cons](/wiki/Ring*Con "Ring*Con"), [ORCs](/wiki/The_One_Ring_Celebration "The One Ring Celebration") and other conventions. She also appeared in *[Ringers: Lord of the Fans](/wiki/Ringers:_Lord_of_the_Fans)*.
