**Colleen Doran** ([July 24](../_/24_July.md), [1968](../_/1968.md)) is an American artist and fantasy writer. In addition to creating over 200 [Topps Masterpieces](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Masterpieces.md), she has also appeared at various [Ring\*Cons](../R/Ring%252ACon.md), [ORCs](../T/The_One_Ring_Celebration.md) and other conventions. She also appeared in *[Ringers: Lord of the Fans](../R/Ringers%253A_Lord_of_the_Fans.md)*.
