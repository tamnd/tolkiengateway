The **Chequers Clubbe** was a dining society at [Exeter College](/wiki/University_of_Oxford#Exeter_College "University of Oxford"), founded in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilary_term" title="wikipedia:Hilary term">Hilary Term</a> [1914](/wiki/1914) by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and Colin Cullis, and given permission by the Sub-Rector on [30 January](/wiki/30_January) 1914. Many of the members had likely previously been members of the [Apolausticks](/wiki/Apolausticks), another club founded by Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Tolkien provided the menu artwork (see *[Ye Chequers Clubbe Binge](/wiki/Ye_Chequers_Clubbe_Binge)*) for a club dinner, held on [June 18](/wiki/18_June "18 June"), [1914](/wiki/1914).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
