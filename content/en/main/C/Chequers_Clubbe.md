The **Chequers Clubbe** was a dining society at [Exeter College](../U/University_of_Oxford.md#Exeter_College), founded in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilary_term" title="wikipedia:Hilary term">Hilary Term</a> [1914](../_/1914.md) by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and Colin Cullis, and given permission by the Sub-Rector on [30 January](../_/30_January.md) 1914. Many of the members had likely previously been members of the [Apolausticks](../A/Apolausticks.md), another club founded by Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Tolkien provided the menu artwork (see *[Ye Chequers Clubbe Binge](../Y/Ye_Chequers_Clubbe_Binge.md)*) for a club dinner, held on [June 18](../_/18_June.md), [1914](../_/1914.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
