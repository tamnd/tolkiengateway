**Carvin Knowles** (born [30 September](/wiki/30_September) [1963](/wiki/1963)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) is an American composer, producer and artist, living in New Zealand. He took a BMA in Music Composition from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma" title="wikipedia:University of Oklahoma">University of Oklahoma</a> in 1988. In 2006, he produced and wrote songs for the [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-inspired album *[In Elven Lands](/wiki/In_Elven_Lands)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
