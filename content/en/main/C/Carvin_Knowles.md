**Carvin Knowles** (born [30 September](../_/30_September.md) [1963](../_/1963.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) is an American composer, producer and artist, living in New Zealand. He took a BMA in Music Composition from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma" title="wikipedia:University of Oklahoma">University of Oklahoma</a> in 1988. In 2006, he produced and wrote songs for the [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-inspired album *[In Elven Lands](../I/In_Elven_Lands.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
