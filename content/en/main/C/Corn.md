**Corn** is a general name for various cereal crops, barley, rye, wheat and oats. It should not be confused with <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">maize</a>.

## History {#History}

Corn was one of the creations of [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Vala_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vala-2">[2]</a></sup> Corn grew in [Aman](../A/Aman.md) which had in it the strong life of the Blessed Realms. This corn grew swiftly and needed only a little sunlight to ripen, and if it was sown at any season (save in frost), it soon sprouted. This corn was brought to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) although it did not thrive and would not endure northern winds that came from [Angband](../A/Angband.md). None were permitted to handle this grain, save the elven-maidens of Yavanna, the [Yavannildi](../Y/Yavannildi.md), who also knew the art of making [lembas](../L/Lembas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

According to [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md), [Men](../M/Men.md) learned the cultivation of corn by the [Entwives](../E/Entwives.md) of the [Brown Lands](../B/Brown_Lands.md), and they were greatly praised for it. That information, however, passed into legend.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Corn was also introduced to [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), who learned to cultivate it. During the [Ban of the Valar](../B/Ban_of_the_Valar.md), the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) spread their knowledge of corn and agriculture throughout [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), and so, this crop was introduced to the [Middle Men](../M/Middle_Men.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Aka_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aka-5">[5]</a></sup>

The area that would later be known as [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) was known for its fertility. The [Kings of Arnor](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md) made many farmlands there, and aside from wine, corn was the main produce. Though the land was deserted by the time they came to live there, the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) continued used their land for similar crop.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-6">[6]</a></sup> In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md) as he prepared to leave the Shire, [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) noticed that due to the fine weather in the summer and autumn "the corn was tall and full".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
