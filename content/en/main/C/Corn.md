**Corn** is a general name for various cereal crops, barley, rye, wheat and oats. It should not be confused with <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maize" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">maize</a>.

## History {#History}

Corn was one of the creations of [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna).<sup id="cite_ref-Vala_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vala-2">[2]</a></sup> Corn grew in [Aman](/wiki/Aman) which had in it the strong life of the Blessed Realms. This corn grew swiftly and needed only a little sunlight to ripen, and if it was sown at any season (save in frost), it soon sprouted. This corn was brought to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) although it did not thrive and would not endure northern winds that came from [Angband](/wiki/Angband). None were permitted to handle this grain, save the elven-maidens of Yavanna, the [Yavannildi](/wiki/Yavannildi), who also knew the art of making [lembas](/wiki/Lembas "Lembas").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

According to [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard), [Men](/wiki/Men) learned the cultivation of corn by the [Entwives](/wiki/Entwives) of the [Brown Lands](/wiki/Brown_Lands), and they were greatly praised for it. That information, however, passed into legend.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Corn was also introduced to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), who learned to cultivate it. During the [Ban of the Valar](/wiki/Ban_of_the_Valar), the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) spread their knowledge of corn and agriculture throughout [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and so, this crop was introduced to the [Middle Men](/wiki/Middle_Men).<sup id="cite_ref-Aka_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aka-5">[5]</a></sup>

The area that would later be known as [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") was known for its fertility. The [Kings of Arnor](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor) made many farmlands there, and aside from wine, corn was the main produce. Though the land was deserted by the time they came to live there, the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) continued used their land for similar crop.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-6">[6]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") as he prepared to leave the Shire, [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") noticed that due to the fine weather in the summer and autumn "the corn was tall and full".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
