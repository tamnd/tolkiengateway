**Crows** are black omnivorous birds sometimes associated with battles due to their taste for carrion.

## History {#History}

Crows were known of in [Aman](/wiki/Aman), with [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) applying "jail-crow" as a term of insult against [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The crows encountered by [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and the [Dwarves](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") during the quest to [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> were mostly harmless. During the scouting expedition to the [Front Gate](/wiki/Front_Gate) of the Lonely Mountain the crows were considered "ominous" and [Balin](/wiki/Balin) distrusted them,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> though later he called them merely "nasty suspicious-looking creatures at that, and rude as well".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The most feared variety in the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) was the large type known as *[crebain](/wiki/Crebain "Crebain")*. On [8 January](/wiki/8_January) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") the [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") entered [Hollin](/wiki/Hollin)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-5">[5]</a></sup> and were spied upon by these large crows from [Fangorn](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest "Fangorn Forest") and [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) were aware that [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) made use of *crebain*; on [30 February](/wiki/30_February)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-5">[5]</a></sup> [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) told [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) that Saruman's "birds of ill omen are abroad in the sky".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Crows may actually just be another name for Ravens as they looked and acted exactly the same. (Balin certainly distinguished between them, preferring Ravens over Crows.)

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for "crow" is *quáko*, derived from [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *k(a)wāk*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Another Quenya word for "crow" is *korko*, and the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) cognate of the same meaning is *corch*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), one of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s very early conceptions of an [Elven](/wiki/Elvish "Elvish") language, the word for "crow" is *crunc* (pl. *crunghin*).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

[Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) also had its own terms for a crow, specifically *khô* in "common" gender singular ("crow", as a species/creature), the plural *khôi* ("crows"), and the dual (pair-plural) *khôwat* ("a pair of crows"). Tolkien's notes on Adûnaic vocabulary and grammar also indicate that the more archaic forms of the term were likely *khaw*, later *khâw*, eventually evolving into *khâu* (with the Adûnaic diphtong *âu*), while the plural of these older forms would apparently be *khâwî(m)*. However, both *khâu* and *khâwî(m)* would likely be declensions in the subjective case of the noun, rather than in the nominative case. The objective case of the noun would be *khôwu*.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s plot notes for the second phase of writing *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, there was no distinction between crows and ravens as ominous birds.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> When the scouting party went to view the [Front Gate](/wiki/Front_Gate) [Balin](/wiki/Balin) did not like the ravens nearby, stating that they looked "like spies of evil".<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> However, when the Company needed to learn of the death of [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) Tolkien "rehabilitated" the ravens, making them friendly to the Dwarves, and replaced all negative raven-references with crows.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>
