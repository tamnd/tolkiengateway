**Cûm-nan-Arasaith**, the "Mound of Avarice", is the mound where many Elves and Dwarves were laid to rest in Doriath after the battle in [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The mound was named **Cûm an-Idrisaith** in *Lost Tales*.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2-1">[1]</a></sup>
