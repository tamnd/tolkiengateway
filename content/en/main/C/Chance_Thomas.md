**Chance Thomas** is a composer and producer of original music for video games. He has been [Sierra Online](/wiki/Sierra_Entertainment "Sierra Entertainment")'s music director as well as [Electronic Arts](/wiki/Electronic_Arts)', and has as such worked on many Tolkien games. In 1998 he was hired to compose the music for *[Middle-earth Online](/wiki/Middle-earth_Online)*, a project that would eventually become *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*.
