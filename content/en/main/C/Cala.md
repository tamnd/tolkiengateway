<i><b>cala</b></i> is a Quenya word meaning "light".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The form *cálë* is attested in the *[Oilima Markirya](../O/Oilima_Markirya.md)* poem.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Root](../R/Root.md) [KAL](../K/KAL.md)

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="Calacirya.md" title="Calacirya"><b>Cala</b>cirya</a></i> = "Pass of Light"
- <i><a href="Calaquendi.md" title="Calaquendi"><b>Cala</b>quendi</a></i> = "Light Elves"
