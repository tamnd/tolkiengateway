<i><b>cilya</b></i> is a noun meaning "chasm, cleft, gorge" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*cilya* derives from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=KIL-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KIL- (page does not exist)">KIL-</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Calacirya" title="Calacirya">Cala<b>cirya</b></a></i> = "Cleft of Light"
- <i>eari ullier i <b>kilyanna</b></i> = "the seas poured into the chasm"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
