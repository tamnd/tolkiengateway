**Cerin Erain**, a smial of the [Forodrim](../F/Forodrim.md), is located in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala" title="wikipedia:Uppsala">Uppsala</a>. Cerin Erain was founded in November [1997](../_/1997.md), and its activities are governed under the law of Forodrim. As of 2009, the association had 15 active members.

The activities of Cerin Erain vary, and have included gaming events, social gatherings, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yule" title="wikipedia:Yule">Yule</a> parties, etc.
