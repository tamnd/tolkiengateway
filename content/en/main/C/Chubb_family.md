The **Chubb family** was a family of [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits), of whom several were present at [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Their main connection with the [Baggins](/wiki/Baggins_family "Baggins family") family was through Bilbo's grandfather, the [Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took"), whose wife [Adamanta Chubb](/wiki/Adamanta_Chubb) was mother to no fewer than twelve children, including Bilbo's mother [Belladonna](/wiki/Belladonna_Took "Belladonna Took").

Marriage into the [Took](/wiki/Took) and [Baggins](/wiki/Baggins) families, as well as the usage of exotic flower names and jewels, suggest that the Chubbs were of higher class.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Chubb is associated with the word 'chubby', round and fat in bodily shape, like Hobbits usually are.

Chubb is a real-life English surname.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>
