The unofficial name **Christopher Tolkien Centenary Boxed Sets** refers to a series of hardcover books published by [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) from [2023](/wiki/2023) to [2026](/wiki/2026).

The publication of the series coincides with the centenary celebration of [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) (1924-2020), the literary executor of his father [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). All books included in sets 1–6 of this series were edited by Christopher; the 5 books contained in set #7 were edited by others.

The series includes 7 boxed sets, each containing 3-5 volumes. The volumes are bound in hardcover, featuring double-sided dustjackets, which can be removed and put back in reverse. Set #1 - #4 are the "[legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium")" books, including *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*, and 12 *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* volumes. Their dustjackets reproduce on one side the original covers of the first hardcover editions, on the other side the illustrated covers of the first paperback editions, by [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe).

Next is a box titled **"Myths and Legends" (no. 1)**, containing 4 books all deal with the old legends: *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, Pearl, and Sir Orfeo](/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl,_and_Sir_Orfeo)*, *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudr%C3%BAn)*, *[The Fall of Arthur](/wiki/The_Fall_of_Arthur)*, and *[Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary](/wiki/Beowulf:_A_Translation_and_Commentary)*.

The three **"Great Tales"** were also re-issued in this format, being the first time they were published in a single box in UK by HarperCollins. Unlike the other boxes, these do not have the reversible dustjackets.

The **"Myths and Legends" (no. 2)** continues on the inclusion of more works by J.R.R. Tolkien: *[Finn and Hengest](/wiki/Finn_and_Hengest)*, *[The Story of Kullervo](/wiki/The_Story_of_Kullervo)*, *[The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun)*, *[The Battle of Maldon](/wiki/The_Battle_of_Maldon:_together_with_The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth "The Battle of Maldon: together with The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth")*. Most notably, it also contains *[The Old English Exodus](/wiki/The_Old_English_Exodus)*, which had never seen a reprint since its 1982 publication.
