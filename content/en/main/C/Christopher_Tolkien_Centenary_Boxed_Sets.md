The unofficial name **Christopher Tolkien Centenary Boxed Sets** refers to a series of hardcover books published by [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md) from [2023](../_/2023.md) to [2026](../_/2026.md).

The publication of the series coincides with the centenary celebration of [Christopher Tolkien](Christopher_Tolkien.md) (1924-2020), the literary executor of his father [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). All books included in sets 1–6 of this series were edited by Christopher; the 5 books contained in set #7 were edited by others.

The series includes 7 boxed sets, each containing 3-5 volumes. The volumes are bound in hardcover, featuring double-sided dustjackets, which can be removed and put back in reverse. Set #1 - #4 are the "[legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md)" books, including *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*, and 12 *[The History of Middle-earth](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* volumes. Their dustjackets reproduce on one side the original covers of the first hardcover editions, on the other side the illustrated covers of the first paperback editions, by [John Howe](../J/John_Howe.md).

Next is a box titled **"Myths and Legends" (no. 1)**, containing 4 books all deal with the old legends: *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, Pearl, and Sir Orfeo](../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl,_and_Sir_Orfeo.md)*, *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](../T/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudrún.md)*, *[The Fall of Arthur](../T/The_Fall_of_Arthur.md)*, and *[Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary](../B/Beowulf%253A_A_Translation_and_Commentary.md)*.

The three **"Great Tales"** were also re-issued in this format, being the first time they were published in a single box in UK by HarperCollins. Unlike the other boxes, these do not have the reversible dustjackets.

The **"Myths and Legends" (no. 2)** continues on the inclusion of more works by J.R.R. Tolkien: *[Finn and Hengest](../F/Finn_and_Hengest.md)*, *[The Story of Kullervo](../T/The_Story_of_Kullervo.md)*, *[The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun](../T/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun.md)*, *[The Battle of Maldon](../T/The_Battle_of_Maldon%253A_together_with_The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth.md)*. Most notably, it also contains *[The Old English Exodus](../T/The_Old_English_Exodus.md)*, which had never seen a reprint since its 1982 publication.
