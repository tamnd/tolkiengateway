**Cirith Ungol** means "Spider's Cleft" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol), a pass through the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) into [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).
- The [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol), a tower above Cirith Ungol in which [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) was imprisoned.
- The [Stairs of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol), the stairs which led from [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) to Cirith Ungol.
- The [Battle of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Battle_of_Cirith_Ungol), a battle between two groups of [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") which resulted in [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins")'s rescue.

## Chapters {#Chapters}

- *[The Stairs of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/The_Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol)*, the eighth chapter of the fourth book in *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*.
- *[The Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/The_Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol)*, the first chapter of the sixth book in *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*.
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kirith_Ungol&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kirith Ungol (page does not exist)">Kirith Ungol</a></i>, the eighth chapter of Part II of *[The War of the Ring](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Ring)*.
