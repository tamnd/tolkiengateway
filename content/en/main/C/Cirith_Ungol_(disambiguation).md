**Cirith Ungol** means "Spider's Cleft" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) and can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Cirith Ungol](Cirith_Ungol.md), a pass through the [Ephel Dúath](../E/Ephel_Dúath.md) into [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).
- The [Tower of Cirith Ungol](../T/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md), a tower above Cirith Ungol in which [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) was imprisoned.
- The [Stairs of Cirith Ungol](../S/Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol.md), the stairs which led from [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md) to Cirith Ungol.
- The [Battle of Cirith Ungol](../B/Battle_of_Cirith_Ungol.md), a battle between two groups of [orcs](../O/Orcs.md) which resulted in [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)'s rescue.

## Chapters {#Chapters}

- *[The Stairs of Cirith Ungol](../T/The_Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol.md)*, the eighth chapter of the fourth book in *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md)*.
- *[The Tower of Cirith Ungol](../T/The_Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md)*, the first chapter of the sixth book in *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kirith_Ungol&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kirith Ungol (page does not exist)">Kirith Ungol</a></i>, the eighth chapter of Part II of *[The War of the Ring](../T/The_War_of_the_Ring.md)*.
