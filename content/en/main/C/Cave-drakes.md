**Cave-drakes** are a type of [Dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") that appear in several adaptations of [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings).

## Middle-earth Role Playing {#Middle-earth_Role_Playing}

In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), **Cave-drakes** were a lesser variety of Dragons descended from [Cold-drakes](/wiki/Cold-drakes). They were wingless and lacked any breath weapon, while also being smaller than other Dragons.

Like greater Dragons, they were capable of speech. They were however much less intelligent, and also prone to lying. Cave-drakes were very aggressive, even to others of their own kind.

One specific breed of Cave-drakes were the Cave Worms, limbless and slimy creatures who dwelt in the [Under-deeps](/wiki/Under-deeps).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Both Cave-drakes and Cave Worms appear in the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game), which was also published by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game {#Middle-earth_Strategy_Battle_Game}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:SBG_-_Cave_Drake.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/2/2d/SBG_-_Cave_Drake.png/300px-SBG_-_Cave_Drake.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="215" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/2/2d/SBG_-_Cave_Drake.png/450px-SBG_-_Cave_Drake.png 1.5x, /w/images/2/2d/SBG_-_Cave_Drake.png 2x" data-file-width="524" data-file-height="376"/></a><figcaption>A miniature Cave Drake</figcaption></figure>

In the [Middle-earth Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Strategy_Battle_Game), **Cave Drakes** were subterranean dragons who dwelt under mountain ranges. They were known to viciously defend their nests, making them the natural enemies of the Dwarves who delved those mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
