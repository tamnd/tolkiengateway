**Capalini** were most likely the [fays](/wiki/Fays "Fays") of springs, according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Cap_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cap-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Capalini* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), probably being related to the word <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kapalinda_(Qenya_word)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kapalinda (Qenya word) (page does not exist)">kapalinda</a></i> ("spring of water"),<sup id="cite_ref-Cap_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cap-1">[1]</a></sup> with *kapalinda* being derived from the root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=KAPA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KAPA (page does not exist)">KAPA</a> ("leap, spring").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
