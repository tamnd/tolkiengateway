**Cemendur** was the son of [Axantur](/wiki/Axantur) and a member of a minor branch of [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") royalty. He had two children, a daughter [Írildë](/wiki/%C3%8Drild%C3%AB) and a son [Hallatan](/wiki/Hallatan). Hallatan would later become the father of [Hallacar](/wiki/Hallacar), the husband of [Tar-Ancalimë](/wiki/Tar-Ancalim%C3%AB), the first [Ruling Queen of Númenor](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Cemendur* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Servant of the Earth" and is a compound of *[kemen](/wiki/Kemen "Kemen")* ("earth") and *[-(n)dur](/wiki/Dur "Dur")* ("servant").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
