**Cemendur** was the son of [Axantur](../A/Axantur.md) and a member of a minor branch of [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) royalty. He had two children, a daughter [Írildë](../_/Írildë.md) and a son [Hallatan](../H/Hallatan.md). Hallatan would later become the father of [Hallacar](../H/Hallacar.md), the husband of [Tar-Ancalimë](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md), the first [Ruling Queen of Númenor](../R/Ruling_Queen_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Cemendur* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Servant of the Earth" and is a compound of *[kemen](../K/Kemen.md)* ("earth") and *[-(n)dur](../D/Dur.md)* ("servant").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
