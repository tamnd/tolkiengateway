**Copper** was a metal familiar to the smiths of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). [Mahtan](/wiki/Mahtan), the father of [Nerdanel](/wiki/Nerdanel) (the wife of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor)), prized this metal above gold. He wore a band of copper about his head and was known as *[Urundil](/wiki/Urundil)* or "copper-lover".<sup id="cite_ref-Note61_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note61-1">[1]</a></sup> There were objects of copper and bronze in the hoards of the [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From the root [RUN](/wiki/RUN) meaning "red, glowing" came the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word *Urun* for copper.<sup id="cite_ref-Note61_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note61-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Copper was one of the six metals used by [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) to create the alloy [Tilkal](/wiki/Tilkal). It was used to forge the great chain [Angainor](/wiki/Angainor) to bind [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth"). In a footnote, it was explained that "tilkal" was an acronym formed from the initial letters for each of the six metals. The first letter, 't' came from *tambë*, which was apparently the word for "copper".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1994: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="/wiki/Treasures_of_Middle-earth_(2nd_edition)" title="Treasures of Middle-earth (2nd edition)">Treasures of Middle-earth</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Copper is given the <a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">Sindarin</a> name <i>Paer</i> in the book. It was common in <a href="/wiki/Eriador" title="Eriador">Eriador</a>, especially <a href="/wiki/Rhudaur" title="Rhudaur">Rhudaur</a>. In <a href="/wiki/Moria" title="Moria">Moria</a> it was used to line basins and waterthroughs, while Men used it for pots, pans, gutters, roofing, wiring and even statues. In <a href="/wiki/Tharbad" title="Tharbad">Tharbad</a> dwarf-sourced copper was used for coinage before the wars with <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>. Only the <a href="/wiki/Noldor" title="Noldor">Noldor</a> are noted as regarding the metal highly.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Copper ore can be mined by apprentice prospectors in <a href="/wiki/Swanfleet" title="Swanfleet">Swanfleet</a>, <a href="/wiki/Blue_Mountains" title="Blue Mountains">Ered Luin</a>, <a href="/wiki/The_Shire" title="The Shire">the Shire</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Bree-land" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>
