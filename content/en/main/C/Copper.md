**Copper** was a metal familiar to the smiths of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). [Mahtan](../M/Mahtan.md), the father of [Nerdanel](../N/Nerdanel.md) (the wife of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md)), prized this metal above gold. He wore a band of copper about his head and was known as *[Urundil](../U/Urundil.md)* or "copper-lover".<sup id="cite_ref-Note61_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note61-1">[1]</a></sup> There were objects of copper and bronze in the hoards of the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From the root [RUN](../R/RUN.md) meaning "red, glowing" came the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word *Urun* for copper.<sup id="cite_ref-Note61_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note61-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Copper was one of the six metals used by [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) to create the alloy [Tilkal](../T/Tilkal.md). It was used to forge the great chain [Angainor](../A/Angainor.md) to bind [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md). In a footnote, it was explained that "tilkal" was an acronym formed from the initial letters for each of the six metals. The first letter, 't' came from *tambë*, which was apparently the word for "copper".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<b>1994: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="../T/Treasures_of_Middle-earth_(2nd_edition).md" title="Treasures of Middle-earth (2nd edition)">Treasures of Middle-earth</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Copper is given the <a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">Sindarin</a> name <i>Paer</i> in the book. It was common in <a href="../E/Eriador.md" title="Eriador">Eriador</a>, especially <a href="../R/Rhudaur.md" title="Rhudaur">Rhudaur</a>. In <a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Moria</a> it was used to line basins and waterthroughs, while Men used it for pots, pans, gutters, roofing, wiring and even statues. In <a href="../T/Tharbad.md" title="Tharbad">Tharbad</a> dwarf-sourced copper was used for coinage before the wars with <a href="../A/Angmar.md" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>. Only the <a href="../N/Noldor.md" title="Noldor">Noldor</a> are noted as regarding the metal highly.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>
