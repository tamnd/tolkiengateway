The **Corsair wars** were a long, predominately naval struggle between the [Kings](/wiki/King_of_Gondor "King of Gondor") and [Stewards of Gondor](/wiki/Ruling_Stewards "Ruling Stewards") and the rebellious [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar).

## Background {#Background}

The fortress haven of [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) was founded by Númenórean colonists during the Second Age during the peak of Númenorean power and eventually fell under the control of the [King's Men](/wiki/King%27s_Men). These King's Men eventually became the [Black Númenóreans](/wiki/Black_N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) who submitted to Sauron and served him gladly in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-window_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-window-3">[3]</a></sup> Even after the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor), the Black Númenóreans held control of Umbar and showed great antipathy to the [Exiles of Númenor](/wiki/Exiles_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup>

The hatred between Umbar and Gondor simmered for a while; however, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [933](/wiki/Third_Age_933 "Third Age 933"), [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") [Eärnil I](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_I) captured [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar), driving the lords of Umbar into exile. 82 years later, in [1015](/wiki/Third_Age_1015 "Third Age 1015"), these lords lead the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) to re-take the fortress and slew King [Ciryandil](/wiki/Ciryandil), son of Eärnil I. This caused his son to conquer [Harad](/wiki/Harad) and eventually take the name [Hyarmendacil I](/wiki/Hyarmendacil_I) and Umbar became a great haven of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="/wiki/Kin-strife" title="Kin-strife">Kin-strife</a></b></div></div>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r398211"/>

Umbar was securely held by Gondor until the aftermath of the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife) in [1447](/wiki/Third_Age_1447 "Third Age 1447"). The great civil conflict between [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Eldacar (King of Gondor)") and the usurper [Castamir](/wiki/Castamir) resulted in Castamir's death and his sons fleeing to Umbar. There they established an independent lordship in opposition to Gondor and continued to clash with Gondor for centuries to come.<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Conflicts {#Conflicts}

Since [1448](/wiki/Third_Age_1448 "Third Age 1448"), the people who fled with the descendants of Castamir became known as the "[Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar)" causing Gondor to lose power over the southern realm and in regular conflict with it. Umbar welcomed enemies of Gondor, contested the possession of the fief [South Gondor](/wiki/South_Gondor), and absorbed tributaries to Gondor in Harad.<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [1540](/wiki/Third_Age_1540 "Third Age 1540"), King [Aldamir](/wiki/Aldamir) was slain in battle against the Corsairs who allied with the kings of [Harad](/wiki/Harad), but was avenged by his son [Hyarmendacil II](/wiki/Hyarmendacil_II) in [1551](/wiki/Third_Age_1551 "Third Age 1551").<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [1634](/wiki/Third_Age_1634 "Third Age 1634"), learning through spies that King [Minardil](/wiki/Minardil) was at [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir), Castamir's great-grandsons [Angamaitë](/wiki/Angamait%C3%AB) and [Sangahyando](/wiki/Sangahyando), and thus the leaders of the Corsairs, raided the haven and killed the King. His heir [Telemnar](/wiki/Telemnar) prepared a fleet against Umbar but could not retaliate as the kingdom was ravaged by the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) of [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636").<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-4">[4]</a></sup>

By [1810](/wiki/Third_Age_1810 "Third Age 1810") the Corsairs raided as far as [Anfalas](/wiki/Anfalas), and Minardil's great-grandnephew [Telumehtar](/wiki/Telumehtar) punished them. In an attack, he took the fortress and haven of the Corsairs, killing Castamir's last descendants. After this victory, he renamed himself [Umbardacil](/wiki/Umbardacil) and Umbar fell under the power of Gondor once more.<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup>

Gondorian control of Umbar lasted for another 46 years until the attacks of the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders), in which Umbar was again lost and fell under the power of the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim).<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup>

In some unknown conflict against the Corsairs in [2746](/wiki/Third_Age_2746 "Third Age 2746"), the fifteenth [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth) was killed.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-4">[4]</a></sup>

After nearly a century of preparation, the Corsairs of Umbar assembled three strong fleets, which they sent northward to harry the coasts of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor); one of these fleets passed so far north that it reached the mouths of the river [Isen](/wiki/Isen). These attacks were launched during the [Long Winter](/wiki/Long_Winter), but in the following spring, [Steward](/wiki/Steward) [Beregond](</wiki/Beregond_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Beregond (Steward of Gondor)") was able to launch his own fleet, driving the Corsairs of Umbar back.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-6">[6]</a></sup>

29 years later, Umbar's fleet was largely destroyed when [Thorongil](/wiki/Thorongil), in the service of the [Ecthelion II](/wiki/Ecthelion_II), led a task force south and burned them, overthrowing the [Captain of the Haven](/wiki/Captain_of_the_Haven) in the process.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-6">[6]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Umbar had not fully recovered from [surprise attack](/wiki/Surprise_Attack_on_Umbar "Surprise Attack on Umbar"), but could still send a massive force: 50 "great ships" and a number of smaller vessels "beyond count" to raid the coastlands of Gondor and draw off major forces from the defence of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-Debate_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Debate-7">[7]</a></sup> They were once again defeated by [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Angbor](/wiki/Angbor), and the [Army of the Dead](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers").

It was only during the reign of [High King](</wiki/High_King_(D%C3%BAnedain)> "High King (Dúnedain)") [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn_II_Elessar "Aragorn II Elessar") that the [Corsairs](/wiki/Corsairs) were completely subdued, thus putting an end to the centuries-long conflict and the rebel status of [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar).<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
