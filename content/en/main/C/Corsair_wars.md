The **Corsair wars** were a long, predominately naval struggle between the [Kings](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) and [Stewards of Gondor](../R/Ruling_Stewards.md) and the rebellious [Corsairs of Umbar](Corsairs_of_Umbar.md).

## Background {#Background}

The fortress haven of [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md) was founded by Númenórean colonists during the Second Age during the peak of Númenorean power and eventually fell under the control of the [King's Men](../K/King's_Men.md). These King's Men eventually became the [Black Númenóreans](../B/Black_Númenóreans.md) who submitted to Sauron and served him gladly in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-window_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-window-3">[3]</a></sup> Even after the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md), the Black Númenóreans held control of Umbar and showed great antipathy to the [Exiles of Númenor](../E/Exiles_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup>

The hatred between Umbar and Gondor simmered for a while; however, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [933](../T/Third_Age_933.md), [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Eärnil I](../E/Eärnil_I.md) captured [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md), driving the lords of Umbar into exile. 82 years later, in [1015](../T/Third_Age_1015.md), these lords lead the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md) to re-take the fortress and slew King [Ciryandil](Ciryandil.md), son of Eärnil I. This caused his son to conquer [Harad](../H/Harad.md) and eventually take the name [Hyarmendacil I](../H/Hyarmendacil_I.md) and Umbar became a great haven of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="../K/Kin-strife~3650.md" title="Kin-strife">Kin-strife</a></b></div></div>

Umbar was securely held by Gondor until the aftermath of the [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md) in [1447](../T/Third_Age_1447.md). The great civil conflict between [Eldacar](<../E/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor).md>) and the usurper [Castamir](Castamir.md) resulted in Castamir's death and his sons fleeing to Umbar. There they established an independent lordship in opposition to Gondor and continued to clash with Gondor for centuries to come.<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Conflicts {#Conflicts}

Since [1448](../T/Third_Age_1448.md), the people who fled with the descendants of Castamir became known as the "[Corsairs of Umbar](Corsairs_of_Umbar.md)" causing Gondor to lose power over the southern realm and in regular conflict with it. Umbar welcomed enemies of Gondor, contested the possession of the fief [South Gondor](../S/South_Gondor.md), and absorbed tributaries to Gondor in Harad.<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [1540](../T/Third_Age_1540.md), King [Aldamir](../A/Aldamir.md) was slain in battle against the Corsairs who allied with the kings of [Harad](../H/Harad.md), but was avenged by his son [Hyarmendacil II](../H/Hyarmendacil_II.md) in [1551](../T/Third_Age_1551.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [1634](../T/Third_Age_1634.md), learning through spies that King [Minardil](../M/Minardil.md) was at [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md), Castamir's great-grandsons [Angamaitë](../A/Angamaitë.md) and [Sangahyando](../S/Sangahyando.md), and thus the leaders of the Corsairs, raided the haven and killed the King. His heir [Telemnar](../T/Telemnar.md) prepared a fleet against Umbar but could not retaliate as the kingdom was ravaged by the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) of [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md).<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-4">[4]</a></sup>

By [1810](../T/Third_Age_1810.md) the Corsairs raided as far as [Anfalas](../A/Anfalas.md), and Minardil's great-grandnephew [Telumehtar](../T/Telumehtar.md) punished them. In an attack, he took the fortress and haven of the Corsairs, killing Castamir's last descendants. After this victory, he renamed himself [Umbardacil](../U/Umbardacil.md) and Umbar fell under the power of Gondor once more.<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup>

Gondorian control of Umbar lasted for another 46 years until the attacks of the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md), in which Umbar was again lost and fell under the power of the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup>

In some unknown conflict against the Corsairs in [2746](../T/Third_Age_2746.md), the fifteenth [Prince of Dol Amroth](../P/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth.md) was killed.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-4">[4]</a></sup>

After nearly a century of preparation, the Corsairs of Umbar assembled three strong fleets, which they sent northward to harry the coasts of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md); one of these fleets passed so far north that it reached the mouths of the river [Isen](../I/Isen.md). These attacks were launched during the [Long Winter](../L/Long_Winter.md), but in the following spring, [Steward](../S/Steward.md) [Beregond](<../B/Beregond_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) was able to launch his own fleet, driving the Corsairs of Umbar back.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-6">[6]</a></sup>

29 years later, Umbar's fleet was largely destroyed when [Thorongil](../T/Thorongil.md), in the service of the [Ecthelion II](../E/Ecthelion_II.md), led a task force south and burned them, overthrowing the [Captain of the Haven](Captain_of_the_Haven.md) in the process.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-6">[6]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), Umbar had not fully recovered from [surprise attack](../S/Surprise_Attack_on_Umbar~64111.md), but could still send a massive force: 50 "great ships" and a number of smaller vessels "beyond count" to raid the coastlands of Gondor and draw off major forces from the defence of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Debate_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Debate-7">[7]</a></sup> They were once again defeated by [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Angbor](../A/Angbor.md), and the [Army of the Dead](../O/Oathbreakers.md).

It was only during the reign of [High King](<../H/High_King_(Dúnedain).md>) [Elessar](../A/Aragorn_II_Elessar.md) that the [Corsairs](Corsairs.md) were completely subdued, thus putting an end to the centuries-long conflict and the rebel status of [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-annals_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-1">[1]</a></sup>
