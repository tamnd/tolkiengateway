**Celepharn** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [979](../T/Third_Age_979.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> - [1191](../T/Third_Age_1191.md),<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup> died aged 212) was the fourth [King of Arthedain](../K/King_of_Arthedain.md).

## History {#History}

Celepharn was the son of [Mallor](../M/Mallor.md) and the father of [Celebrindor](Celebrindor.md); he came to the throne of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md) upon the death of his father in [1110](../T/Third_Age_1110.md).<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup>

During his rule, circa [1150](../T/Third_Age_1150.md), more *[Periannath](../P/Periannath.md)* (mostly [Fallohides](../F/Fallohides.md) and [Stoors](../S/Stoors.md)) passed over the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) and entered [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and the lands of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md). The Stoors settled mainly in [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md) or the [Angle](../A/Angle_of_Eriador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Celepharn's rule coincided with that of [Hyarmendacil I](../H/Hyarmendacil_I.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), under whom the Southern Kingdom reached the height of its power.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Celepharn ruled Arthedain for eighty-one years, and was succeeded by his son, Celebrindor, upon his death in [1191](../T/Third_Age_1191.md).<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The etymology of Celepharn's name is unknown. [Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md) categorizes the name as [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) proposes that it might be composed of *[celeb](Celeb.md)* ("silver") + [lenited](../L/Lenition.md)) *[sarn](../S/Sarn.md)* ("stone");<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> in this case, *ph* should not represent *f*, but two sounds pronounced separately as "celep-harn".

Earlier, Anthony Bryant speculated that the second element is a derivative of the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) PHAR ("reach, go all the way, suffice"), thus meaning something like "enough silver" or "sufficient silver".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
