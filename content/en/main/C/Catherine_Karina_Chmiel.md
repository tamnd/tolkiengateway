**Katarzyna Karina Chmiel-Gugulska** is a Polish artist and illustrator. At university, she studied philosophy and art. Chmiel is a member of the [*Parmadili*, the Tolkien Section of the Silesian Fantasy Club](../S/Sekcja_Tolkienowska_ŚKF.md). Her essays and artwork have appeared in the *Parmadili* 's almanac, *[Aiglos](<../A/Aiglos_(journal).md>)*, and in the journal *[Other Minds](../O/Other_Minds.md)*. She has also provided concept art for *[Born of Hope](../B/Born_of_Hope.md)*.
