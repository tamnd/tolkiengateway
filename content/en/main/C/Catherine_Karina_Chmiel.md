**Katarzyna Karina Chmiel-Gugulska** is a Polish artist and illustrator. At university, she studied philosophy and art. Chmiel is a member of the [*Parmadili*, the Tolkien Section of the Silesian Fantasy Club](/wiki/Sekcja_Tolkienowska_%C5%9AKF "Sekcja Tolkienowska ŚKF"). Her essays and artwork have appeared in the *Parmadili* 's almanac, *[Aiglos](</wiki/Aiglos_(journal)> "Aiglos (journal)")*, and in the journal *[Other Minds](/wiki/Other_Minds)*. She has also provided concept art for *[Born of Hope](/wiki/Born_of_Hope)*.
