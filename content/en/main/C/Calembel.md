**Calembel** was a town in the region of [Lamedon](/wiki/Lamedon) in northern [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). It was located on a small hill near the road between [Erech](/wiki/Erech) and [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> near the fords of the river [Ciril](/wiki/Ciril).<sup id="cite_ref-Company_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Company-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [9 March](/wiki/9_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) and the [Army of the Dead](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") departed from Erech, crossed the fords over the river [Ciril](/wiki/Ciril) and arrived at Calembel. The fords over the river Ciril and the town of Calembel were deserted, because many men had gone to fight in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) and the remaining population had fled to the hills after they heard a rumour that the [King of the Dead](/wiki/King_of_the_Dead) was coming.<sup id="cite_ref-Company_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Company-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Calembel* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-4">[4]</a></sup> It means "Greenham".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Paul Strack and David Salo suggest that it is a compound of *[calen](/wiki/Calen "Calen")* ("green") and the [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") form *bel* of *[pêl](/wiki/P%C3%AAl "Pêl")* ("fenced field", "fence").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Calembel is the seat of Lord <a href="/wiki/Angbor" title="Angbor">Angbor</a>, who was charged by Prince <a href="/wiki/Imrahil" title="Imrahil">Imrahil</a> with protecting the land from the Corsairs of <a href="/wiki/Umbar" title="Umbar">Umbar</a> when the other Lords of Gondor rode to the defence of <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>. Deciding to make a stand against the Corsairs in <a href="/wiki/Linhir" title="Linhir">Linhir</a>, Angbor left his son Drambor in charge of Calembel, which served as a refuge for inhabitants of the nearby village of Lothgobel when it was attacked by the Corsairs.</dd></dl>
