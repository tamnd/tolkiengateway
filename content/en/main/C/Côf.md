<i><b>côf</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "bay".<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas#Other_names" title="Bay of Belfalas"><b>Côf</b> Belfalas</a></i> ("Bay of Belfalas")<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early text called the <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Noldorin_Word-lists&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Noldorin Word-lists (page does not exist)">Noldorin Word-lists</a></i>, there appears the word <i><b>cú</b></i> ("bay, cove").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
