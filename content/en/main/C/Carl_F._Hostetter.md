**Carl Franklin Hostetter** ([August 6](/wiki/6_August "6 August"), [1965](/wiki/1965)) is a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA" title="wikipedia:NASA">NASA</a> computer scientist and [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") scholar who is a key figure of the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](/wiki/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship).

## Principal writing and editing {#Principal_writing_and_editing}

Hostetter is the author of numerous articles on [Tolkienian linguistics](/wiki/Languages "Languages"), and the editor of two journals on the subject, *[Vinyar Tengwar](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar)* (print) and *[Tengwestië](</wiki/Tengwesti%C3%AB_(journal)> "Tengwestië (journal)")* (online).

Together with [Christopher Gilson](/wiki/Christopher_Gilson), [Arden R. Smith](/wiki/Arden_R._Smith), [Bill Welden](/wiki/Bill_Welden), and [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) (called by some the "<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Elfconner&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Elfconner (page does not exist)">Elfconners</a>"), he has been engaged by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) in the ordering, editing, and publishing of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s writings concerning his invented languages, which have been published as *[Parma Eldalamberon](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon)* numbers 11 through 22 (and continuing) and in *Vinyar Tengwar*.

Hostetter edited *[The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth)*, which includes unpublished writings by Tolkien, as well as writings originally published in *Vinyar Tengwar* and *[La Feuille de la Compagnie 3](/wiki/La_Feuille_de_la_Compagnie_3)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Mailing-lists {#Mailing-lists}

Hostetter has also contributed to various mailing-lists dedicated to Tolkien's languages, including [Tolklang](/wiki/Tolklang) and [Elfling](/wiki/Elfling). After a (still ongoing) dispute with [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) and [Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger),<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> he set up [Lambengolmor](</wiki/Lambengolmor_(mailing_list)> "Lambengolmor (mailing list)") and Elfling-d.
