**Carl Franklin Hostetter** ([August 6](../_/6_August.md), [1965](../_/1965.md)) is a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA" title="wikipedia:NASA">NASA</a> computer scientist and [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) scholar who is a key figure of the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](../E/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship.md).

## Principal writing and editing {#Principal_writing_and_editing}

Hostetter is the author of numerous articles on [Tolkienian linguistics](../L/Languages.md), and the editor of two journals on the subject, *[Vinyar Tengwar](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar.md)* (print) and *[Tengwestië](<../T/Tengwestië_(journal).md>)* (online).

Together with [Christopher Gilson](Christopher_Gilson.md), [Arden R. Smith](../A/Arden_R._Smith.md), [Bill Welden](../B/Bill_Welden.md), and [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md) (called by some the "<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Elfconner&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Elfconner (page does not exist)">Elfconners</a>"), he has been engaged by [Christopher Tolkien](Christopher_Tolkien.md) in the ordering, editing, and publishing of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s writings concerning his invented languages, which have been published as *[Parma Eldalamberon](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon.md)* numbers 11 through 22 (and continuing) and in *Vinyar Tengwar*.

Hostetter edited *[The Nature of Middle-earth](../T/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth.md)*, which includes unpublished writings by Tolkien, as well as writings originally published in *Vinyar Tengwar* and *[La Feuille de la Compagnie 3](../L/La_Feuille_de_la_Compagnie_3.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Mailing-lists {#Mailing-lists}

Hostetter has also contributed to various mailing-lists dedicated to Tolkien's languages, including [Tolklang](../T/Tolklang.md) and [Elfling](../E/Elfling.md). After a (still ongoing) dispute with [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) and [Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md),<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> he set up [Lambengolmor](<../L/Lambengolmor_(mailing_list).md>) and Elfling-d.
