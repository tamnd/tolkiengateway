<i><b>corma</b></i> is suggested as a [Neo-Quenya](/wiki/Neo-Elvish "Neo-Elvish") word meaning "ring".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is only attested in the compounds <i><b>Cormacolindor</b></i> ("[Ring-bearers](/wiki/Ring-bearers)"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> *[Cormarë](/wiki/Cormar%C3%AB)* ("Ringday"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and <i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Lord_of_the_Ring#Elvish_translation" title="Lord of the Ring">Cormaron</a></b></i> ("of the Rings").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
