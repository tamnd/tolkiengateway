**Camel** is an English progressive rock band, formed in 1971.

In the 1974 album "Mirage" they included a three-part song called "[Nimrodel / Procession / White Rider](/wiki/Nimrodel_/_Procession_/_White_Rider)". The first two parts are instrumental and the last part is a short description of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf).
