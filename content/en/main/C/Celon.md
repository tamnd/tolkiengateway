**Celon** was a tributary of the [Aros](../A/Aros.md) that rose in the highlands around [Himring](../H/Himring.md) and flowed south-westwards until it met the Aros on the southern borders of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md). The woods of [Nan Elmoth](../N/Nan_Elmoth.md) lay on its south-eastern bank, and the river marked the boundary between [Himlad](../H/Himlad.md) and [Estolad](../E/Estolad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The Celon was not a great river and was referred to as a "slender stream".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the years of darkness before the [return of the Noldor](../R/Return_of_the_Noldor.md) the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) established camps between Celon and [Gelion](../G/Gelion.md), from which they plundered widely throughout [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> After the Noldor had returned they created new realms; on the north flank of the Celon [Celegorm](Celegorm.md) and [Curufin](Curufin.md) established themselves in [Himlad](../H/Himlad.md) while to the south lay the domains of [Amrod](../A/Amrod.md) and [Amros](../A/Amros.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

When the people of [Bëor](../B/Bëor.md) came to Beleriand, [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod_Felagund.md) advised them to gather and settle upon the east banks of the Celon. Thereafter the land was called [Estolad](../E/Estolad.md), which meant the "Encampment".<sup id="cite_ref-West_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the death of [Haldad](../H/Haldad.md) and the rescue of the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md) by [Caranthir](Caranthir.md), the new leader, [Haleth](../H/Haleth.md), led her people to Estolad where they dwelt for a time. Later though she removed her people westward to the [Forest of Brethil](../F/Forest_of_Brethil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-West_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) the Orcs returned, coming down the Celon to encompass Doriath.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Celon* means the "stream flowing down from heights".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The name was derived from *et-kelē*, the "issue of water, spring".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In later writings, Tolkien decided that the name of the Celon was "*too hackneyed a river-name*" and that **Limhir** was "*more suitable for the river*" with the prefix *Lim-* being "*repeated in [L.R.](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) as were not unnaturally other names from [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md)*".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
