**Celon** was a tributary of the [Aros](/wiki/Aros) that rose in the highlands around [Himring](/wiki/Himring) and flowed south-westwards until it met the Aros on the southern borders of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath). The woods of [Nan Elmoth](/wiki/Nan_Elmoth) lay on its south-eastern bank, and the river marked the boundary between [Himlad](/wiki/Himlad) and [Estolad](/wiki/Estolad).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The Celon was not a great river and was referred to as a "slender stream".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the years of darkness before the [return of the Noldor](/wiki/Return_of_the_Noldor "Return of the Noldor") the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) established camps between Celon and [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion), from which they plundered widely throughout [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> After the Noldor had returned they created new realms; on the north flank of the Celon [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) established themselves in [Himlad](/wiki/Himlad) while to the south lay the domains of [Amrod](/wiki/Amrod) and [Amros](/wiki/Amros).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

When the people of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor) came to Beleriand, [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod_Felagund) advised them to gather and settle upon the east banks of the Celon. Thereafter the land was called [Estolad](/wiki/Estolad), which meant the "Encampment".<sup id="cite_ref-West_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the death of [Haldad](/wiki/Haldad) and the rescue of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) by [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir), the new leader, [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth), led her people to Estolad where they dwelt for a time. Later though she removed her people westward to the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Forest_of_Brethil).<sup id="cite_ref-West_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) the Orcs returned, coming down the Celon to encompass Doriath.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Celon* means the "stream flowing down from heights".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The name was derived from *et-kelē*, the "issue of water, spring".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In later writings, Tolkien decided that the name of the Celon was "*too hackneyed a river-name*" and that **Limhir** was "*more suitable for the river*" with the prefix *Lim-* being "*repeated in [L.R.](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Lord of the Rings") as were not unnaturally other names from [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand)*".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
