**Clamavi de Profundis** is a musical group inspired by religious and classical music as well as fantasy literature, among other genres. They compose original music in addition to performing folk, religious, and other songs. Their music is usually placed on their YouTube channel. Some of Clamavi de Profundis' music is Tolkien-inspired, and they have written their own musical compositions for songs and poems by [J. R. R. Tolkien](../J/J._R._R._Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
