The **Causeway** was the road in the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) that ran from [Deephallow](/wiki/Deephallow) north through [Rushey](/wiki/Rushey) and [Stock](/wiki/Stock) and ended at the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The road was high banked and ran parallel to the [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) opposite of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland). [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") rode in [Farmer Maggot](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot)'s wagon on the part of the Causeway that stretched five or more miles from Maggot's lane to the [Bucklebury Ferry](/wiki/Bucklebury_Ferry).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
