The **Captain of Morgoth** in the west during the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad) was the leader of a host of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) from [Angband](/wiki/Angband) that assailed [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) at [Eithel Sirion](/wiki/Eithel_Sirion).<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Captain of Morgoth had been commanded to ensure that Fingon's force was brought into the battle as early as possible using whatever means necessary so that Fingon could not have the opportunity to outflank [Morgoth's](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") main army.

When his Orcs marched up to the [Eithel Sirion](/wiki/Eithel_Sirion) within eyesight of [Barad Eithel](/wiki/Barad_Eithel), the Captain of Morgoth sent riders with [tokens of parley](</wiki/Gelmir_(son_of_Guilin)> "Gelmir (son of Guilin)") to the walls of the outworks. Once there, the heralds of the riders hewed off Gelmir's limbs and left him to die.

While the heralds and the riders were perished in the charge of [Gwindor's](/wiki/Gwindor "Gwindor") [company](/wiki/Elves "Elves"), it is unknown if the Captain of Morgoth was slain as well.<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-1">[1]</a></sup>
