**Cold-drakes** were dragons that were unable to breath fire, but instead could possess an icy "cold breath" which froze people and induced fear.<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-1">[1]</a></sup> [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) bred and used cold-drakes in the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). After the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), some cold-drakes were found in the [waste](/wiki/Northern_Waste "Northern Waste") north of the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains).

As the millennia passed, their numbers grew, until they became a serious threat in the later centuries of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) to the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) that mined the Grey Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-3">[3]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2589](/wiki/Third_Age_2589 "Third Age 2589"), [Dáin I](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I), [King of Durin's Folk](/wiki/Kings_of_Durin%27s_Folk "Kings of Durin's Folk"), and his second son [Frór](/wiki/Fr%C3%B3r) were slain at the gates of their [hall](/wiki/D%C3%A1in%27s_Halls "Dáin's Halls") by a cold-drake.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-3">[3]</a></sup> The attacks of these fearsome creatures persuaded the Dwarves to migrate eastwards from the Grey Mountains, and it was soon afterward that their [realms](/wiki/Dwarf_realms "Dwarf realms") in the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) and at [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") were established.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2018: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Cold-drakes appear throughout the northern lands of <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a>, particularly in the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ered_Mithrin" title="Ered Mithrin">Ered Mithrin</a> where they serve &#34;Hrímil Frost-Heart&#34;, a dragon who consumed one of the dwarven <a href="/wiki/Rings_of_Power" title="Rings of Power">Rings of Power</a>. Hrímil&#39;s mightiest spawn, the cold-drake &#34;Vethúg Wintermind&#34;, was the one to slay King <a href="/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I" title="Dáin I">Dáin I</a> and his son <a href="/wiki/Fr%C3%B3r" title="Frór">Frór</a> in Thikil-gundu, &#34;The Steel Keep&#34; (otherwise known as <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/D%C3%A1in%27s_Halls" title="Dáin&#39;s Halls">Dáin&#39;s Halls</a>). The game&#39;s cold-drakes often project icy breath, even though the exact nature of the &#34;cold breath&#34;<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-1">[1]</a></sup> of Tolkien&#39;s cold-drakes is simply not known.</dd></dl>
