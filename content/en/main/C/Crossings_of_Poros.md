The **Crossings of Poros**,<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> also known as the **Crossing of Poros**<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> or **Fords of the Poros**,<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-3">[3]</a></sup> marked the point where the [Harad Road](/wiki/Harad_Road) passed over the river [Poros](/wiki/Poros) from [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) to [South Gondor](/wiki/South_Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Fords first entered history in the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944"), when the enemies of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) made an alliance. [King](/wiki/King_of_Gondor "King of Gondor") [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) of Gondor knew that the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) in the north and his enemies in the south were preparing to attack Gondor. As a consequence, he divided his forces into a northern army and a southern army and left the southern army under the command of [Eärnil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II "Eärnil II"), a member of the royal house and a descendant of King [Telumehtar](/wiki/Telumehtar) to deal with the enemies approaching from [Near Harad](/wiki/Near_Harad)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> in the south. Eärnil intentionally left the Fords undefended and positioned his troops in a camp about forty miles north of the Fords in [South Ithilien](/wiki/South_Ithilien)<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-3">[3]</a></sup> and achieved a great victory in South Ithilien and destroyed the army of the Harad, which had crossed the river Poros.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the days of [Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Stewards "Ruling Stewards") [Túrin II](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_II) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) had occupied [South Gondor](/wiki/South_Gondor) and there were many armed conflicts along the river Poros.<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2885](/wiki/Third_Age_2885 "Third Age 2885") emissaries of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) stirred up the Haradrim who crossed the Poros invaded Ithilien<sup id="cite_ref-TA2885_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2885-6">[6]</a></sup> with a large army.<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> When King [Folcwine](/wiki/Folcwine) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) heard of the invasion he fulfilled the [Oath of Eorl](/wiki/Oath_of_Eorl)<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> and sent many men led by his twin sons [Folcred](/wiki/Folcred) and [Fastred](</wiki/Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine)> "Fastred (son of Folcwine)") to help Túrin II.<sup id="cite_ref-Folcwine_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folcwine-7">[7]</a></sup>

Túrin II defeated the Haradrim invaders with the help of the troops from Rohan in a [battle](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Crossings_of_Poros "Battle of the Crossings of Poros") at the Crossings of Poros,<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> but Folcred and Fastred fell side by side in battle in Ithilien.<sup id="cite_ref-Folcwine_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folcwine-7">[7]</a></sup> They were buried together high upon the shore of the river Poros according to the customs of their [people](/wiki/Rohirrim "Rohirrim") in a burial mound, which was called *[Haudh in Gwanûr](/wiki/Haudh_in_Gwan%C3%BBr)*. The mound stood there for a long time and the enemies of Gondor were afraid to pass the mound.<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup>
