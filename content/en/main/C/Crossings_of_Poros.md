The **Crossings of Poros**,<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> also known as the **Crossing of Poros**<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> or **Fords of the Poros**,<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-3">[3]</a></sup> marked the point where the [Harad Road](../H/Harad_Road.md) passed over the river [Poros](../P/Poros.md) from [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) to [South Gondor](../S/South_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Fords first entered history in the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md), when the enemies of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) made an alliance. [King](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) of Gondor knew that the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) in the north and his enemies in the south were preparing to attack Gondor. As a consequence, he divided his forces into a northern army and a southern army and left the southern army under the command of [Eärnil](../E/Eärnil_II.md), a member of the royal house and a descendant of King [Telumehtar](../T/Telumehtar.md) to deal with the enemies approaching from [Near Harad](../N/Near_Harad.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> in the south. Eärnil intentionally left the Fords undefended and positioned his troops in a camp about forty miles north of the Fords in [South Ithilien](../S/South_Ithilien.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-3">[3]</a></sup> and achieved a great victory in South Ithilien and destroyed the army of the Harad, which had crossed the river Poros.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the days of [Steward](../R/Ruling_Stewards.md) [Túrin II](../T/Túrin_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md) had occupied [South Gondor](../S/South_Gondor.md) and there were many armed conflicts along the river Poros.<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2885](../T/Third_Age_2885.md) emissaries of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) stirred up the Haradrim who crossed the Poros invaded Ithilien<sup id="cite_ref-TA2885_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2885-6">[6]</a></sup> with a large army.<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> When King [Folcwine](../F/Folcwine.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) heard of the invasion he fulfilled the [Oath of Eorl](../O/Oath_of_Eorl.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> and sent many men led by his twin sons [Folcred](../F/Folcred.md) and [Fastred](<../F/Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine).md>) to help Túrin II.<sup id="cite_ref-Folcwine_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folcwine-7">[7]</a></sup>

Túrin II defeated the Haradrim invaders with the help of the troops from Rohan in a [battle](../B/Battle_of_the_Crossings_of_Poros.md) at the Crossings of Poros,<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> but Folcred and Fastred fell side by side in battle in Ithilien.<sup id="cite_ref-Folcwine_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folcwine-7">[7]</a></sup> They were buried together high upon the shore of the river Poros according to the customs of their [people](../R/Rohirrim.md) in a burial mound, which was called *[Haudh in Gwanûr](../H/Haudh_in_Gwanûr.md)*. The mound stood there for a long time and the enemies of Gondor were afraid to pass the mound.<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup>
