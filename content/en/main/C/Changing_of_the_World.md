The **Changing of the World** was the event in which [Arda](../A/Arda.md) was transformed from a flat world into a [round planet](../B/Bent_World.md). It occurred in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3319](../S/Second_Age_3319.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> when [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md) utterly [destroyed](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) the island of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) due to the deeds of [its people](../N/Númenóreans.md).

## History {#History}

When the [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) [King](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md) attacked [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) relinquished their dominion over [Arda](../A/Arda.md) and called upon Eru for help. Ilúvatar responded by creating an enormous rift between Númenor and the [Blessed Realm](../A/Aman.md), into which Ar-Pharazôn's [fleet](../G/Great_Armament.md) and, ultimately, his land were swept. Meanwhile, the flat world bent back upon itself and met at this rift. The final result was a round planet of which [Aman](../A/Aman.md) and [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) were no longer a physical part. After the Changing of the World, only the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) could find the [Straight Way](../S/Straight_Road.md) and reach the ancient [West](../A/Aman.md) by ship.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a class="mw-redirect" href="../R/Round_World_version_of_the_Silmarillion.md" title="Round World version of the Silmarillion">Round World version of the Silmarillion</a></div></div>

In Tolkien's latest writings published in the later volumes of *[The History of Middle-earth](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)*, Arda was a round world from its beginning, and the idea of a flat world was instead an invention of the Númenóreans. Thus, in this alternate account, the world would not need to be bent at the time of Númenor's downfall and Aman's removal from the physical world.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
