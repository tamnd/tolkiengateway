<b><i>Carandaith</i> Vol. 1, No. 2</b> is an issue of the fanzine [Carandaith](/wiki/Carandaith), published by the [Australian Tolkien Society](/wiki/Australian_Tolkien_Society). The magazine reprints the article "The Realms of Tolkien" by [Daphne Castell](/wiki/Daphne_Castell) from [*New Worlds* Vol. 50, No. 168](/wiki/New_Worlds_Vol._50,_No._168 "New Worlds Vol. 50, No. 168"), which includes extracts from [an interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
