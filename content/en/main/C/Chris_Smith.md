**Chris Smith** ([1964](../_/1964.md)) is the [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) editor of [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md), since [1999](../_/1999.md). He edited several books about [Peter Jackson's films](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) by [Gary Russell](../G/Gary_Russell.md) and [Jude Fisher](../J/Jude_Fisher.md), but is most famous for *[The Lord of the Rings: Weapons and Warfare](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Weapons_and_Warfare.md)*, a book containing a lot of information about the weaponry used in [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md)'s films.
