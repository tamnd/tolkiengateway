**Chris Smith** ([1964](/wiki/1964)) is the [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") editor of [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins), since [1999](/wiki/1999). He edited several books about [Peter Jackson's films](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") by [Gary Russell](/wiki/Gary_Russell) and [Jude Fisher](/wiki/Jude_Fisher), but is most famous for *[The Lord of the Rings: Weapons and Warfare](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Weapons_and_Warfare)*, a book containing a lot of information about the weaponry used in [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson)'s films.
