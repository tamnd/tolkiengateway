**caras** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) word which means "moated fortress" (in [Silvan Elvish](/wiki/Silvan_Elvish)) or "city" (in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)/[Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)).

The proper meaning of this word was "a built fort or dwelling surrounded by bulwarks"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup> or a "circular earthwall with dike".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It derives from [PQ](/wiki/PQ) \**karasse*, [root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") √<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=KAR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KAR (page does not exist)">KAR</a> "make, build".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
