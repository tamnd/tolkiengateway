**Camels** were animals native to the [Southern lands](../../category/S/Southern_lands.md), and thus appear illustrated on [a map of Middle-earth](<../../file/P/Pauline_Baynes_-_A_Map_of_Middle-earth_(color)_2.jpg.md>).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> Otherwise, these animals only appear in linguistic writings dating from the early versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PE12_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE12-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE14_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE14-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) name of "a camel" is <i><b>ulun(t)</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-PE12_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE12-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE14_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE14-3">[2]</a></sup>

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Camels, level 4 animals, are called <i>devevi</i> (sing. <i>deve</i>) in Apysaic (a language of south-western Endor). There are both one- and two-humped variants of the beast.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> Staibir is the name of a War Camel appearing in an adventure set in Southwestern Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1987-: <i><a href="../M/Mithril_Miniatures.md" title="Mithril Miniatures">Mithril Miniatures</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>A camel was released as part of the 32mm scale figure &#34;Haradrian Champion on War Camel&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2001-: <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game">The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Camels are used as mounts by the Mahud Raiders of the barren lands of the Nafarat.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>
