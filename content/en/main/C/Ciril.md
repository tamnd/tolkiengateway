The river **Ciril** also spelled <i><b>Kiril</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup> was counted as third of the [Seven Rivers of Gondor](/wiki/Rivers_of_Gondor "Rivers of Gondor").

It arose in an isolated peak south of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) in the north of the [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") region of [Lamedon](/wiki/Lamedon), and flowed south past the township of [Calembel](/wiki/Calembel) until it met the river [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3), and as part of that river flowed towards [Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

On [9 March](/wiki/9_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) led [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company), and the [Oathbreakers](/wiki/Oathbreakers) across the Ciril on the way to [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), the name *Ciril*/*Kiril* could have derived from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") KIR ("cut"), although this is said to be an uncertain etymology.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Ciril pours from the <a href="/wiki/White_Mountains" title="White Mountains">White Mountains</a> into the marshland of &#34;Tathrendalf&#34; from which it eventually emerges as a single stream. It passes <a href="/wiki/Calembel" title="Calembel">Calembel</a> with its farmlands and the beacon of &#34;Maegond&#34; before joining the <a href="/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3" title="Ringló">Ringló</a>.</dd></dl>
