The river **Ciril** also spelled <i><b>Kiril</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup> was counted as third of the [Seven Rivers of Gondor](../R/Rivers_of_Gondor.md).

It arose in an isolated peak south of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) in the north of the [Gondorian](../G/Gondor.md) region of [Lamedon](../L/Lamedon.md), and flowed south past the township of [Calembel](Calembel.md) until it met the river [Ringló](../R/Ringló.md), and as part of that river flowed towards [Edhellond](../E/Edhellond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

On [9 March](../_/9_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) led [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md), and the [Oathbreakers](../O/Oathbreakers.md) across the Ciril on the way to [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), the name *Ciril*/*Kiril* could have derived from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) KIR ("cut"), although this is said to be an uncertain etymology.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Ciril pours from the <a href="../W/White_Mountains.md" title="White Mountains">White Mountains</a> into the marshland of &#34;Tathrendalf&#34; from which it eventually emerges as a single stream. It passes <a href="Calembel.md" title="Calembel">Calembel</a> with its farmlands and the beacon of &#34;Maegond&#34; before joining the <a href="../R/Ringló.md" title="Ringló">Ringló</a>.</dd></dl>
