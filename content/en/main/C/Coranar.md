**Coranar** meaning literally "sun-round", a name given by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) to a single [solar year](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun").<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Valar](/wiki/Valar) intended a Year of the Sun to be exactly 350 full days, being a 1/10 fraction of a [Year of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees"). However the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and [Moon](/wiki/Moon) were slower than expected in their passage than the Valar had intended, and in one coranar, the Sun turns 365.25 times instead of 350, or approximately 1/9.58 of a Year of the Trees.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Although the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) (and the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits)) divided the coranar in 365 days, the extra hours (5 hours, 48 minutes, 46 seconds) accumulated a deficit after centuries; to remedy this, some calendars included a "leap-year" every four years (see: [enderi](/wiki/Enderi "Enderi"), [Overlithe](/wiki/Overlithe)).

144 coranari made a [yén](/wiki/Y%C3%A9n "Yén") which was the basic unit of time for the [immortal](/wiki/Immortality "Immortality") [Elves](/wiki/Elves).
