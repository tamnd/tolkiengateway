**Coranar** meaning literally "sun-round", a name given by the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) to a single [solar year](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md).<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Valar](../V/Valar.md) intended a Year of the Sun to be exactly 350 full days, being a 1/10 fraction of a [Year of the Trees](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md). However the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) and [Moon](../M/Moon.md) were slower than expected in their passage than the Valar had intended, and in one coranar, the Sun turns 365.25 times instead of 350, or approximately 1/9.58 of a Year of the Trees.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Although the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) (and the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md)) divided the coranar in 365 days, the extra hours (5 hours, 48 minutes, 46 seconds) accumulated a deficit after centuries; to remedy this, some calendars included a "leap-year" every four years (see: [enderi](../E/Enderi.md), [Overlithe](../O/Overlithe.md)).

144 coranari made a [yén](../Y/Yén.md) which was the basic unit of time for the [immortal](../I/Immortality.md) [Elves](../E/Elves.md).
