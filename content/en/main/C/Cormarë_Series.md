The <i><b>Cormarë Series</b></i> is a series of scholarly books and essays by [Walking Tree Publishers](/wiki/Walking_Tree_Publishers). The series is named after *[Cormarë](/wiki/Cormar%C3%AB)*, or "Ring-day", [September 22th](/wiki/22_September "22 September"). This name was chosen because the initiative for this series came on the 10th anniversity of the Swiss [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies") *[Eredain](/wiki/Eredain)* (founders of Walking Tree Publishers), who named their Anniversary meetings (in 1996, 2001 and 2006) "Cormarë", even though it fell on [March 25](/wiki/25_March "25 March").
