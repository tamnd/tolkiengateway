**Carl Phelpstead** is a Professor of English Literature at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University" title="wikipedia:Cardiff University">Cardiff University</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales" title="wikipedia:Wales">Wales</a>. His research and teaching focus on Old Norse-Icelandic literature, Old and Middle English literature, and modern medievalism.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
