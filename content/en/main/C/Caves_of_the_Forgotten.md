The **Caves of the Forgotten** were a possibly fictitious cave complex believed to have been formed out of the hills that buried the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") invaders of the [Blessed Realm](/wiki/Aman "Aman").<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Seduced by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), the [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor), [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n) was persuaded that he had the power to take the lands of [Aman](/wiki/Aman) from the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) themselves. He set out with a [great fleet](/wiki/Great_Armament "Great Armament"), landed at the Blessed Realm and a host of the Númenóreans camped near the hill of [Túna](/wiki/T%C3%BAna) in the [Calacirya](/wiki/Calacirya) of the [Pelóri Mountains](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri "Pelóri").<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-1">[1]</a></sup>

At that time [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB), the Elder King, gave up his power over [Arda](/wiki/Arda) and called on [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar), who then [broke the world](/wiki/Changing_of_the_World "Changing of the World"). Their fleets and even their [island](/wiki/Elenna "Elenna") home were into an abyss that opened between [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) and the Blessed Realm.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-1">[1]</a></sup>

Meanwhile, Ar-Pharazôn and his mortal warriors were buried beneath falling hills. Yet, they are said to lie imprisoned within the Caves of the Forgotten, awaiting the day of the [Last Battle](/wiki/Last_Battle) when they will be released from their caves.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-1">[1]</a></sup> However, the part that they have to play in the battle remains a mystery.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first version of the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor), both "[Angor](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n "Ar-Pharazôn") the mighty and [Istar](/wiki/Tar-M%C3%ADriel "Tar-Míriel") his queen fell like stars into the dark, and they perished out of all knowledge", suggesting that they remained on the ships and fell with them into the great abyss and drowned. Only the mortal warriors that went on land were buried beneath fallen hills, "where legend saith that they lie imprisoned in the **Forgotten Caves** until the day of Doom and the [Last Battle](/wiki/Last_Battle)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In the second version, the name of the caves was changed to the "Caves of the Forgotten".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The name and the narrative concerning the Caves of the Forgotten remained substantially unchanged within the third version,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> yet were not mentioned at all within any of the three versions of *[The Drowning of Anadûnê](/wiki/The_Drowning_of_Anad%C3%BBn%C3%AA)*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
