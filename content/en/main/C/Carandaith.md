<i><b>Carandaith</b></i> was an Australian fanzine published by [Australian Tolkien Society](/wiki/Australian_Tolkien_Society) between 1968 and 1972. It was edited by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Paul_Novitski&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Paul Novitski (page does not exist)">Paul Novitski</a> (under the pen name Alpajpuri).
