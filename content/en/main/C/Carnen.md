**Carnen** — [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for **Redwater** — was a [river](/wiki/Category:Rivers "Category:Rivers") in the north-east of Middle-earth.

## Course {#Course}

The Carnen flowed from its source in the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) about 250 miles to the south until it flowed into the [Celduin](/wiki/River_Running "River Running"), which flowed into the [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It roughly formed the border between the "The North" (the northwestern region of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth)) and the vast "East" (the area called [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Between the time when [Thráin I](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_I) founded the dwarf-kingdom in the Lonely Mountain (in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1999](/wiki/Third_Age_1999 "Third Age 1999")) and the arrival of [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) (in [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770"))<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen) living between the Celduin and Carnen grew strong and repelled all [enemies from the East](/wiki/Easterlings "Easterlings"). It is possible that this military success was the result of the acquisition of weapons and armour from the [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-4">[4]</a></sup> The area became depopulated after the arrival of the dragon but again filled with Northmen after Smaug's demise.

For a long time [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings), which were allied with [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) threatened the borders of the [Kingdom of Dale](/wiki/Kingdom_of_Dale). On 14 March [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) an army of these Easterlings host of Sauron crossed the Carnen and drove the forces of King [Brand](/wiki/Brand) back to [Dale](/wiki/Dale). A great battle between the army of the Easterlings and the army of King Brand and their allies the Dwarves of Erebor lasted for three days at the feet of the [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"). On 17 March [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> the Easterlings defeated their opponents and killed King Brand and King [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot). The surviving men of Dale and Dwarves retreated into the Lonely Mountain and were besieged by the Easterlings. The Easterlings were distressed when they received news that Sauron's army had been defeated in the south. On 27 March [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> the defenders of the Lonely Mountain attacked and defeated the Easterlings. The surviving Easterlings feld back to the East and did no longer trouble Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Carnen* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name,<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-9">[9]</a></sup> which means "Redwater".<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests that it is a combination of *[caran](/wiki/Caran "Caran")* ("red") and *[nen](/wiki/Nen "Nen")* ("water").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-9">[9]</a></sup>

It is possible that the river received its name, because the riverbed consisted of sediments, which contained iron from the Iron hills which gave the river a rusty reddish colour.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2018: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The origins of river Carnen can be found in the Ironfold region. It begins as six streams flowing into the Iron Hills from the nearby mountains, all shallow and rust-brown in colour. Across the Iron Hills, the streams eventually join into a single river, which then plummets down through several waterfalls into the area called &#34;the Barding-lands&#34;, clearing it of sediment before it flows further to the south.</dd></dl>
