**Ciryandil** or **Kiryandil** can refer to:

- [Ciryandil](/wiki/Ciryandil), the fourteenth [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor).
- [Kiryandil](</wiki/Kiryandil_(son_of_Siriondil)> "Kiryandil (son of Siriondil)"), the father of King [Eärnil II](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II), according to an abandoned draft of the *[Appendices](/wiki/Appendices)*.
- [Kiryandil](</wiki/Elendur_(son_of_Isildur)> "Elendur (son of Isildur)"), an early version of Elendur, the eldest son of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur), according to an abandoned draft of the *Appendices*.
