<i><b>Christie&#39;s 20th Century Books and Manuscripts 16 November 2001</b></i> is an auction catalogue from Christie's (London). Two Tolkien-related lots are included:

- The description for Lot 20 includes extracts from a [letter to George Sayer dated 28 November 1963](<../L/Letter_to_George_Sayer_(28_November_1963).md>).

- The description for Lot 21 includes extracts from letters to George Sayer and his wife Moira, dating from [7 August 1952](<../L/Letter_to_George_and_Moira_Sayer_(7_August_1952).md>) (also [to Moira Sayer, 10 August 1952](<../L/Letter_to_Moira_Sayer_(10_August_1952).md>)) through to [11 December 1971](<../L/Letter_to_George_and_Moira_Sayer_(11_December_1971).md>). The lot also includes proof copies (corrected by Tolkien) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and [*Essays and Studies* (1953)](<../E/Essays_and_Studies_(1953).md>), together with various first edition copies of books by Tolkien.

Part of the 1963 letter and an annotated page from the proof copy of *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)* are reproduced in photographs.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
