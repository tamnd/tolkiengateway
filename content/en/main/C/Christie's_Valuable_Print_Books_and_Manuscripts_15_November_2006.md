<i><b>Christie&#39;s Valuable Print Books and Manuscripts 15 November 2006</b></i> is an auction catalogue from Christie's. Lot 152 (p. 116) consists of a 1966/1967/1967 set of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* inscribed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), together with a page of examples of [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) script and notes.

The inscribed copy of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* is the copy that Tolkien is seen to inscribe in the BBC's 1968 documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](/wiki/Tolkien_in_Oxford)*. The [tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar "Tengwar") inscription reads: *Elen síla lúmenna omentielvo* ("A star shines upon the hour of our meeting").

The page of tengwar examples repeats the title of the documentary three times: the first is a transliteration of English, while the second and third are translations into [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) - [Arcastar](/wiki/Arcastar) Mondósaresse. The three lines of tengwar are accompanied by explanatory notes.

Both the inscription and the page of examples and notes are reproduced in a photograph.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
