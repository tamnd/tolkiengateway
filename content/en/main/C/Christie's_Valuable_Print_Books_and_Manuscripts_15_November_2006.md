<i><b>Christie&#39;s Valuable Print Books and Manuscripts 15 November 2006</b></i> is an auction catalogue from Christie's. Lot 152 (p. 116) consists of a 1966/1967/1967 set of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* inscribed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), together with a page of examples of [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) script and notes.

The inscribed copy of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* is the copy that Tolkien is seen to inscribe in the BBC's 1968 documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](../T/Tolkien_in_Oxford.md)*. The [tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) inscription reads: *Elen síla lúmenna omentielvo* ("A star shines upon the hour of our meeting").

The page of tengwar examples repeats the title of the documentary three times: the first is a transliteration of English, while the second and third are translations into [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) - [Arcastar](../A/Arcastar.md) Mondósaresse. The three lines of tengwar are accompanied by explanatory notes.

Both the inscription and the page of examples and notes are reproduced in a photograph.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
