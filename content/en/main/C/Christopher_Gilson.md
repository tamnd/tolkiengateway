**Christopher John Gilson** (June [1953](/wiki/1953)) is a leading member in the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](/wiki/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship), and the chief editor of *[Parma Eldalamberon](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon)*. Together with [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter), [Arden R. Smith](/wiki/Arden_R._Smith), [Bill Welden](/wiki/Bill_Welden) and [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) he is in the *[Vinyar Tengwar](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar)* review committee.
