**Christopher John Gilson** (June [1953](../_/1953.md)) is a leading member in the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](../E/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship.md), and the chief editor of *[Parma Eldalamberon](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon.md)*. Together with [Carl F. Hostetter](Carl_F._Hostetter.md), [Arden R. Smith](../A/Arden_R._Smith.md), [Bill Welden](../B/Bill_Welden.md) and [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md) he is in the *[Vinyar Tengwar](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar.md)* review committee.
