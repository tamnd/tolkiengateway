**Captain of Ships** was a title of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) that was held by [Castamir](/wiki/Castamir), the grandson of [Calimehtar](</wiki/Calimehtar_(son_of_Calmacil)> "Calimehtar (son of Calmacil)"), the younger brother of king [Rómendacil II](/wiki/R%C3%B3mendacil_II).<sup id="cite_ref-Eldacar_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldacar-1">[1]</a></sup>

Castamir was the Captain of Ships during the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife). His position allowed him to find supporters from the havens of [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar), [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) and the coastal lands of Gondor, and to overthrow the rightful King <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Eldacar" title="Eldacar">Eldacar</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldacar_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldacar-1">[1]</a></sup>
