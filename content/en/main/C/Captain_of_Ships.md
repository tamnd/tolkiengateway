**Captain of Ships** was a title of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) that was held by [Castamir](Castamir.md), the grandson of [Calimehtar](<Calimehtar_(son_of_Calmacil).md>), the younger brother of king [Rómendacil II](../R/Rómendacil_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eldacar_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldacar-1">[1]</a></sup>

Castamir was the Captain of Ships during the [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md). His position allowed him to find supporters from the havens of [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md), [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) and the coastal lands of Gondor, and to overthrow the rightful King <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../E/Eldacar.md" title="Eldacar">Eldacar</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldacar_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldacar-1">[1]</a></sup>
