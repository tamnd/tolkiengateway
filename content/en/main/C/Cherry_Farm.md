<i><b>Cherry Farm</b></i> is an unpublished <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farce" title="wikipedia:Farce">farce</a> written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and performed for his <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Incledon_family&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Incledon family (page does not exist)">Incledon relatives</a> during part of his vacation at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnt_Green" title="wikipedia:Barnt Green">Barnt Green</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcestershire" title="wikipedia:Worcestershire">Worcestershire</a><sup id="cite_ref-guide_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-1">[1]</a></sup> as the seasonal theatrical<sup id="cite_ref-drama_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-drama-2">[2]</a></sup> on [Christmas](/wiki/Christmas) in [1911](/wiki/1911).<sup id="cite_ref-chronology_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology-3">[3]</a></sup>
