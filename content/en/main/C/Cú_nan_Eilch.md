**Cú nan Eilch** was an unspecified place, probably in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in a prose fragment following *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Eil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eil-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Following the [darkening of Valinor](../D/Darkening_of_Valinor.md) and the unrest of the [Gnomes](../G/Gnomes.md), the [Solosimpi](../S/Solosimpi.md) refused to go with the Gnomes in pursuit of [Melko](../M/Melko.md). In response, [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) threatened to "march to Cú nan Eilch", after which the Gnomes took the ships of the Solosimpi by force.<sup id="cite_ref-Eil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eil-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Cú nan Eilch* is in [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md), but its meaning was never specified.

However, in a text called the <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Noldorin_Word-lists&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Noldorin Word-lists (page does not exist)">Noldorin Word-lists</a></i>, there appears the word *[cú](Côf.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium)* ("bay, cove");<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and in the earlier <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Gnomish_Lexicon_Slips&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gnomish Lexicon Slips (page does not exist)">Gnomish Lexicon Slips</a></i>, the word <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Iaun&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Iaun (page does not exist)">alch</a></i> ("shrine") is given, with *eilch* (or *elcin*) being its plural form.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
