In [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md), **Chey Sart** was grasslands region surrounding the black-peaked volcano Ulk Chey Sart, to the west of the Ered Harmal mountains and [Khand](../K/Khand.md).

It was the center of the Chey culture, as well as the kingdom of [Ren the Unclean](../E/Eighth_of_the_Nazgûl.md).<sup id="cite_ref-map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After a plague in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1998&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1998 (page does not exist)">1998</a> left illusionist Ren with a twisted mind, he dubbed himself Fire King and Son of Ulk Chey Sart. Gaining followers, he had subjugated the Chey territories by [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2000&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2000 (page does not exist)">2000</a>. Unbelievers among the Chey tribes were purged.<sup id="cite_ref-nazgul_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nazgul-2">[2]</a></sup>

The First Chey Expansion ([S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2155&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2155 (page does not exist)">2155</a>-<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2693&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2693 (page does not exist)">2693</a>) saw the Fire King expanding his empire into to the lands of Dalpygis, Khargagis Ahar, Heb Aaraan, and Orgothraath. The Second Chey Expansion ([S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [2899](../S/Second_Age_2899.md)-[3261](../S/Second_Age_3261.md)) then expanded into Vaag, Acaana, and western Gaathgykarkan. With the help of the [Variags](../V/Variags.md) of [Ûvatha](../N/Ninth_of_the_Nazgûl.md), Ren also conquered the the rest of Kargagis Ahar and Rycolis.<sup id="cite_ref-nazgul_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nazgul-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md), the empire waned and split back into tribes. The Chey often faced Variag and Nûriag oppression. After Ren returned to Middle-earth in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1050](../T/Third_Age_1050.md), he returned to his volcano, which he expanded into an underground citadel. From there, he once again started subjugating the tribes. He had achieved this by [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1264&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1264 (page does not exist)">1264</a>. The two periods during which Ren reigned in Chey Sart, from [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1264&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1264 (page does not exist)">1264</a> to [1640](../T/Third_Age_1640.md) and from [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2063](../T/Third_Age_2063.md) to [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md) were times of prosperity and expansion for the Chey.<sup id="cite_ref-nazgul_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nazgul-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
