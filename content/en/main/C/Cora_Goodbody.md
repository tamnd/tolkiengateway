**Cora Bolger, née Goodbody**, was a Hobbit of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Cora married [Rudolph Bolger](../R/Rudolph_Bolger.md). They had one son, [Fastolph](../F/Fastolph_Bolger.md), born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1210](../T/Third_Age_2810.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Cora* was meaningless in contemporary [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) [Westron](../W/Westron.md). It represented an original name *Coro* or *Core*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
