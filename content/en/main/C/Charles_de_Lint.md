**Charles de Lint** (born [December 22](/wiki/22_December "22 December"), [1951](/wiki/1951)) is a Canadian author who primarily writes fantasy fiction.

In 1986, de Lint was Guest of Honour at [Mythcon 17](/wiki/Mythcon_17).
