**Cortney Skinner** is an American fantasy artist. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create art for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).
