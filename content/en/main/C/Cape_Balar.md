**Cape Balar** was the small cape that lay to the south-west of [Arvernien](../A/Arvernien.md), and marked the northern limit of the [Bay of Balar](../B/Bay_of_Balar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [The Second 'Silmarillion' Map](../T/The_Second_'Silmarillion'_Map.md), a later addition by Tolkien shows the place name "Ship-havens" on the small bay formed to the east of Cape Balar<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>.
