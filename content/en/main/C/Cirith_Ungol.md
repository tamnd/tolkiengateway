**Cirith Ungol** was a pass through the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) that led into [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).

## Appearance {#Appearance}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Kirith_Ungol.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/70/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Kirith_Ungol.jpg/200px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Kirith_Ungol.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="142" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/7/70/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Kirith_Ungol.jpg/300px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Kirith_Ungol.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/7/70/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Kirith_Ungol.jpg/400px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Kirith_Ungol.jpg 2x" data-file-width="730" data-file-height="519"/></a><figcaption>&#34;Kirith Ungol&#34; by <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a></figcaption></figure>

The pass was located in a cleft on the slope of the mountain on the north-east side of the [Morgul Vale](/wiki/Morgul_Vale),<sup id="cite_ref-Pool_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pool-1">[1]</a></sup> above and to the north of the [Morgul Pass](/wiki/Morgul_Pass).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It could be reached by taking a narrow path that began at a gap in the stone-wall on the left side of the [Morgul-road](/wiki/Morgul-road) near the bridge over the river [Morgulduin](/wiki/Morgulduin), which wound up the mountain on the northern side near the entrance of the [Morgul Vale](/wiki/Morgul_Vale), climbing the [Stairs of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol),<sup id="cite_ref-Stairs_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stairs-3">[3]</a></sup> passing through a tunnel,<sup id="cite_ref-Lair_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lair-4">[4]</a></sup> and taking a long flight of broad, shallow steps to the cleft<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> with the crown of the pass after which the path turned left and plunged steeply down to the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> From a gate in the south-eastern side of the wall that enclosed the courtyard of the tower a broad road ran on the edge of a precipice and then turned southward and wound down to join the [Morgul-road](/wiki/Morgul-road).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Despite the danger posed by Shelob, it was the swiftest path from [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) over the mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Sketch_of_cleft_in_the_mountains.jpeg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/b/bd/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Sketch_of_cleft_in_the_mountains.jpeg/150px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Sketch_of_cleft_in_the_mountains.jpeg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="150" height="115" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/b/bd/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Sketch_of_cleft_in_the_mountains.jpeg/225px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Sketch_of_cleft_in_the_mountains.jpeg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/b/bd/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Sketch_of_cleft_in_the_mountains.jpeg/300px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Sketch_of_cleft_in_the_mountains.jpeg 2x" data-file-width="653" data-file-height="500"/></a><figcaption>Sketch of cleft in the mountains by <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a></figcaption></figure>

The pass of Cirith Ungol was probably named after the giant [spider](/wiki/Spiders "Spiders") [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob), who dwelt there.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> It was also referred to as the "high pass".<sup id="cite_ref-Pool_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pool-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Before the War of the Ring {#Before_the_War_of_the_Ring}

The pass was first called <i><b>Cirith Dúath</b></i> ("Shadow Cleft"). This name was used during the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance), when [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) sent his sons [Aratan](/wiki/Aratan) and [Ciryon](/wiki/Ciryon) to [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil) to prevent the forces of Sauron from escaping through the pass.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> After the War of the Last Alliance, the [Men of Gondor](/wiki/Men_of_Gondor) built a tower at the eastern end of Cirith Dúath as an eastern outpost of the defences of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) to keep enemies in Mordor. This tower was later called the Tower of Cirith Ungol.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

*Cirith Ungol* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. It means "Cleft of the Spider". It contains the elements *[cirith](/wiki/Cirith "Cirith")* ("cleft", "a narrow passage cut through earth or rock (like a railway cutting)") and *[ungol](/wiki/Spiders#Names "Spiders")* ("spider").<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier drafts Cirith Ungol was called Kirith Ungol and was guarded by Minas Morgul instead of the Tower of Cirith Ungol. It was first the main pass into Mordor or a cleft near the main pass and was located where [Cirith Gorgor](/wiki/Cirith_Gorgor) is located in the final version.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> Later it became a high pass that was first on the south side<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> and finally on the north side of the Morgul vale.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>
