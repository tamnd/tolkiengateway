**Colombo Hornblower** was one of the recipients of [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo)'s gifts (a barometer) during his birthday party, in the drafts for *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> His name was initially *Cosimo Chubb*, before he ultimately became a [Hornblower](/wiki/Hornblower).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
