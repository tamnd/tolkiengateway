The **Crown of Gondor**, also called the **winged crown**,<sup id="cite_ref-App|Tale_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|Tale-1">[1]</a></sup> **White Crown**, **Silver Crown** and the **crown of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md)**,<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[2]</a></sup> was the ceremonial headgear of the [Kings](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), and was used as a symbol of the Kingship.

## Description {#Description}

It is said that the original crown was a plain [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) war-helm.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-3">[3]</a></sup> However a later crown replaced it, described as a tall, jewelled and winged helm, not unlike the helms of the [Guards of the Citadel](../G/Guards_of_the_Citadel.md), but taller and all white, with wings resembling those of a sea-bird wrought of pearl and silver. Seven gems of adamant were set in the circlet. On its summit was a jewel, the light of which went up like a flame to represent Anarion.<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/C/Crown_of_Gondor_(3D_render).png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/c7/Crown_of_Gondor_%283D_render%29.png/300px-Crown_of_Gondor_%283D_render%29.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="424" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/c7/Crown_of_Gondor_%283D_render%29.png/450px-Crown_of_Gondor_%283D_render%29.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/c7/Crown_of_Gondor_%283D_render%29.png/600px-Crown_of_Gondor_%283D_render%29.png 2x" data-file-width="733" data-file-height="1037"/></a><figcaption><i>Crown of Gondor</i> by <a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Grond" title="User:Grond">Grond</a>, designed to match Tolkien&#39;s illustration.</figcaption></figure>

The original crown of Gondor was said to be a plain Númenórean war-helm, the one worn by the Númenórean Kings in the [Battle of Dagorlad](../B/Battle_of_Dagorlad.md); as [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md)'s helm was crushed when he was killed, the war-helm of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) was used for the crowning of the Kings of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the days of [Atanatar II](../A/Atanatar_II.md) Alcarin this was replaced by a jewelled helm.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-3">[3]</a></sup>

Before replying to the challenge of the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md), [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md) put the Crown of Elendil<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> in the lap of King [Eärnil II](../E/Eärnil_II.md) in the [houses of the dead](../H/Hallows.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[2]</a></sup> and it remained there even after his loss, for nearly a thousand years.

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/C/Crown_of_gondor.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/39/Crown_of_gondor.png/100px-Crown_of_gondor.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="100" height="133" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/39/Crown_of_gondor.png/150px-Crown_of_gondor.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/39/Crown_of_gondor.png/200px-Crown_of_gondor.png 2x" data-file-width="426" data-file-height="568"/></a><figcaption><i>Crown of gondor</i> by <a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Grond" title="User:Grond">Grond</a>, designed to match Tolkien&#39;s illustration.</figcaption></figure>

On [1 May](../_/1_May.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> the rightful heir, [Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn.md) [Telcontar](../H/House_of_Telcontar.md), came forward to receive the crown. The tradition had been for a new king to receive the crown from his father before he died or to obtain it from his father's tomb. Since this was not possible, [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md), [Steward of Gondor](../S/Steward_of_Gondor.md), brought forth the ancient crown and gave it to Aragorn. After chanting "*Et Eärello Endorenna utúlien. Sinome maruvan ar Hildinyar tenn' Ambar-metta!*" Aragorn surprised the crowd by returning the crown to Faramir. At his request, [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) took the crown and bore it to [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), who placed it on King Elessar's head with the blessing, "Now come the days of the King, and may they be blessed while the thrones of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) endure!"<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the year [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> Aragorn went to the [House of the Kings](../H/House_of_the_Kings.md) to lay down his life. In that place he bade farewell to his son [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md) and, following tradition, gave him the Crown of Gondor and the [Sceptre of Annúminas](../S/Sceptre_of_Annúminas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-App|Tale_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|Tale-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) described it in his letters as being similar to the <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedjet" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">crown of the Pharaohs of Egypt</a>. He writes "I think the crown of Gondor (the S. Kingdom) was very tall, like that of Egypt, but with wings attached, not set straight back but at an angle."<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>
