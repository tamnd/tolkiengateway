**Cermië** (<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Cerveth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cerveth (page does not exist)">Cerveth</a> in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)) was the seventh month of the year in the [King's Reckoning](../K/King's_Reckoning.md) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](../S/Stewards'_Reckoning.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), following [Loëndë](../L/Loëndë.md), and preceding [Úrimë](../_/Úrimë.md). In the [New Reckoning](../N/New_Reckoning.md) it was the fourth month of the year, immediately following [Nárië](../N/Nárië.md).

*Cermië* in the Kings' Reckoning and in the Stewards' Reckoning corresponded, for the most part, to [Afterlithe](../A/Afterlithe.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) (approximately modern [24 June](../_/24_June.md) through [23 July](../_/23_July.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
