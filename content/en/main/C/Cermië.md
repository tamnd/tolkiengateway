**Cermië** (<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Cerveth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cerveth (page does not exist)">Cerveth</a> in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)) was the seventh month of the year in the [King's Reckoning](/wiki/King%27s_Reckoning) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), following [Loëndë](/wiki/Lo%C3%ABnd%C3%AB), and preceding [Úrimë](/wiki/%C3%9Arim%C3%AB). In the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning) it was the fourth month of the year, immediately following [Nárië](/wiki/N%C3%A1ri%C3%AB).

*Cermië* in the Kings' Reckoning and in the Stewards' Reckoning corresponded, for the most part, to [Afterlithe](/wiki/Afterlithe) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) (approximately modern [24 June](/wiki/24_June) through [23 July](/wiki/23_July)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
