**The One Ring: Roleplaying in the world of Lord of the Rings**, referred to in product listings as **The One Ring: Core Rules**, is the core rulebook of the second edition of the [*The One Ring* Roleplaying Game](<../T/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md>). For the initial release on Kickstarter, Free League also offered an exclusive collector's edition wrapped in fake red leather.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

A second edition core rulebook was first announced under [Cubicle 7 Entertainment](Cubicle_7_Entertainment.md) as the *[The One Ring – The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](../T/The_One_Ring_–_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game.md)*, with a largely different set of artists.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Enter the world of Middle-earth with The One Ring™, the official tabletop roleplaying game based on the works of J.R.R. Tolkien. Designed specifically to evoke the atmosphere of The Hobbit™ and The Lord of the Rings™, the game contains rules for creating heroes and sending them off on adventures in a land threatened by the growing Shadow.
</p><p>This beautifully illustrated, full-colour hardcover manual presents Middle-earth as it was twenty years after Bilbo Baggins’ remarkable disappearance and unexpected return. The great events narrated in The Lord of the Rings™ are still fifty years away, leaving plenty of room to narrate stories worthy of an epic.
</p><p>Now in its second edition, The One Ring™ comes with updated and revised rules, a new visual style, and a focus on the land of Eriador — the Lone-lands west of the Misty Mountains. Among its key features are:
</p>
<ul><li>Rules for travelling across the land (Journey), facing frightening foes (Combat), and meeting the personalities of Middle-earth (Council).</li>
<li>Thorough advice for the Loremaster on how to bring Middle-earth to life, including rules for magical treasure, the Shadow, and the Eye of Mordor.</li>
<li>In-depth information on six Patrons — individuals sponsoring the adventures of the Player-heroes — including Bilbo Baggins and Gandalf the Grey.</li>
<li>A bestiary containing a spread of adversaries, from lowly Orc Soldiers and Highway-robbers, to monstrous Cave-Trolls and Barrow-Wights.</li>
<li>Rules to create your very own Nameless Things — unknowable beings from the dark corners of the world.</li>
<li>A complete Landmark adventure, <i>The Star of the Mist</i>, with extra support for new Loremasters, in the form of tutorial advice throughout.</li></ul>
</blockquote>

## In other languages {#In_other_languages}

For the Polish localization, a unique green collector's edition was released by publisher Black Monk.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
