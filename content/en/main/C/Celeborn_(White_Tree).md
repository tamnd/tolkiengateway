**Celeborn** was the name of the **White Tree** that flourished in [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa). It was a seedling of the tree [Galathilion](/wiki/Galathilion), which in turn had been made in image of [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion), the eldest of the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Celeborn was an ancestor to [Nimloth of Númenor](/wiki/Nimloth_of_N%C3%BAmenor)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> which is in turn ancestor to the [White Trees of Gondor](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor "White Tree of Gondor").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*[Celeb](/wiki/Celeb)* is Sindarin for "silver", while *[orn](/wiki/Orn "Orn")* is "tree"; its name means "Tree of Silver".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
