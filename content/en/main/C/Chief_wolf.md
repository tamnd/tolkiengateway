The **chief wolf** was a great grey [Warg](/wiki/Wargs "Wargs") who led a pack which attacked [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) in the woods east of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) in the [year 2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

## History {#History}

### Early life {#Early_life}

The [Warg](/wiki/Wargs "Wargs") pack led by the chief wolf often collaborated with the [Goblins](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") of [Goblin-town](/wiki/Goblin-town), assisting them in their raids and sharing in the plunder. In [year 2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the Goblins and Wargs planned to meet in the woods east of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) to attack some of the villages established by [Men](/wiki/Men) from the South.<sup id="cite_ref-Pan_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pan-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Attack on the company {#Attack_on_the_company}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Allison_Theus_-_Great_Gray_Wolf.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/9/9c/Allison_Theus_-_Great_Gray_Wolf.jpg/180px-Allison_Theus_-_Great_Gray_Wolf.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="203" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/9/9c/Allison_Theus_-_Great_Gray_Wolf.jpg/270px-Allison_Theus_-_Great_Gray_Wolf.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/9/9c/Allison_Theus_-_Great_Gray_Wolf.jpg 2x" data-file-width="308" data-file-height="348"/></a><figcaption><i>Great Gray Wolf</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Allison_Theus" title="Category:Images by Allison Theus">Allison Theus</a></figcaption></figure>

During [Third Age 2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941), the Warg and his pack of hundreds<sup id="cite_ref-Language_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Language-1">[1]</a></sup> went to meet with the Goblins of [Goblin-town](/wiki/Goblin-town) at a glade surrounded by trees, intending to raid the [Men](/wiki/Men) of the South. The [Goblins](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") were delayed due to the death of their leader, the [Great Goblin](/wiki/Great_Goblin). While waiting, the Wargs discovered a [group of Dwarves](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") who had escaped from their allies at Goblin-town. Letting out a howl, the pack chased the Company to the meeting-place glade and the dwarves climbed up the trees surrounding the glade to escape. After the Wargs sniffed each tree to determine whether there was anybody in them, they gathered in a circle around the chief wolf. The chief wolf spoke to his companions in the [language of the Wargs](/wiki/Wolf-language "Wolf-language") of wicked and terrible things. He reminded them of the Wargs' pact with the Goblins and their plan to attack the Men of the South. Intermittently, the Wargs would simultaneously howl in response.<sup id="cite_ref-Pan_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pan-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:David_T._Wenzel_-_Eagle_Rescue.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/2/2b/David_T._Wenzel_-_Eagle_Rescue.jpg/170px-David_T._Wenzel_-_Eagle_Rescue.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="170" height="260" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/2/2b/David_T._Wenzel_-_Eagle_Rescue.jpg/255px-David_T._Wenzel_-_Eagle_Rescue.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/2/2b/David_T._Wenzel_-_Eagle_Rescue.jpg 2x" data-file-width="327" data-file-height="500"/></a><figcaption><i>The Eagles&#39; rescue</i> by <a href="/wiki/David_T._Wenzel" title="David T. Wenzel">David T. Wenzel</a></figcaption></figure>

[Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), the [Istari](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards") of the company, begun gathering pine-cones, setting them alight and flinging them at the Wargs. One of the largest ones directly hit the grey chief on the nose and he lept ten feet in the air begun running around the circle and snapping, even at the other Wargs, in anger. The Dwarves begun cheering while the Wargs flipped themselves onto their backs and extinguished the fires on their backs. The Wargs began leaping up and snapping at the Dwarves, cursing at them in their language. Soon, the Goblins arrived to the meeting spot and devised a plan to force the company from the trees. The Goblins stacked fern and brushwood at the trunks of the trees and begun fanning the flame nearest to the Company's tree, adding leaves to fuel it. The Wargs watched as the Goblins danced and [sung](/wiki/Fifteen_birds_in_five_fir-trees "Fifteen birds in five fir-trees") in celebration. Just before the tree collapsed, the [Lord of the Eagles](/wiki/Great_Eagle "Great Eagle") and his servants swooped down and picked up the Company in their talons. The Goblins and Wargs scattered far and wide in the woods in an attempt to catch up, the Goblins tossing spears at the Eagles, though it was no help as the Eagles ultimately escaped to the [Eyrie](/wiki/Great_Shelf "Great Shelf").<sup id="cite_ref-Pan_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pan-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Searching for the Company {#Searching_for_the_Company}

In the coming days, [Goblin](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") patrols hunted for the Dwarves fiercely with the [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs), still furious due to the death of the [Great Goblin](/wiki/Great_Goblin) and the burning of the chief wolf's nose.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early drafts, [Bladorthin](/wiki/Gandalf "Gandalf") "may have understood" the [Wolf-language](/wiki/Wolf-language) spoken by the chief wolf, whist in the final text Gandalf explicitly "understood it".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The chief wolf is depicted on the &#34;Great Gray Wolf&#34; card, illustrated by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Allison_Theus" title="Category:Images by Allison Theus">Allison Theus</a>. The card has the following effects:<blockquote><p>Forced: If Great Gray Wolf is dealt a shadow card with a riddle, return all Creature enemies to the staging area at the end of the combat phase.<br/>Riddle: The first player names a card type, sphere and cost, shuffles his deck, then discards the top 3 cards. For each of those cards that matches all three items, place 1 progress token on stage 2.</p></blockquote></dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2012" title="2012">2012</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Unlike in the book, in which the <a href="/wiki/Wargs" title="Wargs">Wargs</a>, alone, attack <a href="/wiki/Thorin_and_Company" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and Company</a> before being joined by the <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Goblins</a>, a separate group of Orcs, distinct from those from <a href="/wiki/Goblin-town" title="Goblin-town">Goblin-town</a>, attack the Company while riding Wargs. <a href="/wiki/Azog" title="Azog">Azog the Defiler</a>, leader of the group, rides a great white Warg, known as the <b>Warg Matriarch</b>.</dd></dl>

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Hobbit_(film_series)_-_Warg_Matriarch_and_Azog_2.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/1/18/The_Hobbit_%28film_series%29_-_Warg_Matriarch_and_Azog_2.png/250px-The_Hobbit_%28film_series%29_-_Warg_Matriarch_and_Azog_2.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="157" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/1/18/The_Hobbit_%28film_series%29_-_Warg_Matriarch_and_Azog_2.png/375px-The_Hobbit_%28film_series%29_-_Warg_Matriarch_and_Azog_2.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/1/18/The_Hobbit_%28film_series%29_-_Warg_Matriarch_and_Azog_2.png/500px-The_Hobbit_%28film_series%29_-_Warg_Matriarch_and_Azog_2.png 2x" data-file-width="1258" data-file-height="791"/></a><figcaption>The Warg Matriarch&#39;s has dark fur striations that mimic the layered design of Azog&#39;s armor.</figcaption></figure>

<dl><dd>The Warg Matriarch was the mother of the Gundabad wargs of Azog&#39;s pack of <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs</a>. She was known to get along well with Azog, as Azog was often seen stroking and feeding her throughout his hunt for <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The Warg Matriarch was seen when <a href="/wiki/Yazneg" title="Yazneg">Yazneg</a> and <a href="/wiki/Fimbul" title="Fimbul">Fimbul</a> returned to <a href="/wiki/Weathertop" title="Weathertop">Weathertop</a> to report their failure in capturing <a href="/wiki/Thorin_and_Company" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and Company</a>. The Matriarch was also present when Bolg reported to Azog about his encounter with <a href="/wiki/Legolas" title="Legolas">Legolas</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tauriel" title="Tauriel">Tauriel</a> in <a href="/wiki/Lake-town" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a>. She was also seen accompanying her master to <a href="/wiki/Dol_Guldur" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a>. She was last seen when Azog led an orc army toward <a href="/wiki/Lonely_Mountain" title="Lonely Mountain">Erebor</a>. It is unclear whether she survived the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies" title="Battle of Five Armies">Battle of Five Armies</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The Warg Matriarch is distinguishable from other Wargs by her large size and white fur coat. Elements of her appearance echo the visual design of Azog, as she also has several visible facial scars in addition to dark striations in her fur that mimic the appearance of Azog&#39;s armor.</dd></dl>
