**Cwén** was the first wife of [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Cwe_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cwe-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Cwén married Eriol (then called *Ottor*) after he settled on the island of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heligoland" title="wikipedia:Heligoland">Heligoland</a> in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Sea" title="wikipedia:North Sea">North Sea</a>, in the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands).

Together, they had two sons called [Hengest](/wiki/Hengest) and [Horsa](/wiki/Horsa), named so in honour of their grandfather [Eoh](/wiki/Eoh) (since the names share a similar meaning).

Sometime after, while Hengest and Horsa were still children, Cwén died, prompting Eriol to abandon the Great Lands and seek for [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa).<sup id="cite_ref-Cwe_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cwe-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Cwén* is in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English), meaning "woman, wife".<sup id="cite_ref-Cwe_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cwe-1">[1]</a></sup>
