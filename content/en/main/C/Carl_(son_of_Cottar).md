**Carl** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Carl was the second son of [Cottar](/wiki/Cottar), the younger brother of [Cotman](/wiki/Cotman).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Carl* is a common English first name, from the Anglo-Saxon concept <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churl" title="wikipedia:Churl">Churl</a></i>, a free peasant.
