**Carl** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Carl was the second son of [Cottar](Cottar.md), the younger brother of [Cotman](Cotman.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Carl* is a common English first name, from the Anglo-Saxon concept <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churl" title="wikipedia:Churl">Churl</a></i>, a free peasant.
