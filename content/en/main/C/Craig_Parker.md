**Craig Parker** (born [1970](/wiki/1970)) played [Haldir](/wiki/Haldir) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers")*. He also provided the voices of [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(Lieutenant_of_Morgul)> "Gothmog (Lieutenant of Morgul)") and [Guritz](/wiki/Guritz) in *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King")*, though he was not credited for these.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In June [2007](/wiki/2007), he and 14 other actors filed <a class="external text" href="https://us.imdb.com/news/ni0058227/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">a lawsuit</a> against [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) over merchandising royalties.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The action was settled out of court in October [2008](/wiki/2008).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
