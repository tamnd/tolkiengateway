**Basil Chad Chisholm** ([27 July](../_/27_July.md) [1978](../_/1978.md)) is an Assistant Professor of English at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Wesleyan_University" title="wikipedia:Southern Wesleyan University">Southern Wesleyan University</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central,_South_Carolina" title="wikipedia:Central, South Carolina">Central, South Carolina</a>, though he has also taught at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Arlington" title="wikipedia:University of Texas at Arlington">University of Texas at Arlington</a> and at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rust_College" title="wikipedia:Rust College">Rust College</a> (Holly Springs, Mississippi) where he was named lecturer of the year by the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Methodist_Church" title="wikipedia:United Methodist Church">United Methodist</a> Board of Higher Education in 2008. Chisholm has written several articles on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), [C.S. Lewis](C.S._Lewis.md), and [the Inklings](../T/The_Inklings.md). Much of his present scholarship springs from <a class="external text" href="http://www.redmonky.net/utpa/4324/poulakos.pdf" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">John Poulakos&#39;s theory of rhetorical possibility</a>, and Chisholm is working on a book that uses C.S. Lewis' ideas on children's storytelling to develop a theory of possibility to reexamine classical children's literature. In 2013, Chisholm was awarded a dissertation fellowship to complete his dissertation on "rhetoric-oriented approaches to juvenile texts,” which he successfully defended in November of that year.
