**Catapults** were siege engines used for casting missiles.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the [siege of Minas Tirith](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor "Siege of Gondor") the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Morgul-host&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Morgul-host (page does not exist)">Morgul-host</a><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> led by the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) set up great catapults,<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">[3]</a></sup> which were used to throw into the first circle of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) both flaming missiles<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> to cause fires<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4">[4]</a></sup> and severed heads of fallen [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians)<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4">[4]</a></sup> to cause dread and despair.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Gondorians also had similar engines upon the [walls](/wiki/City_Wall "City Wall") of Minas Tirith, but they were smaller and couldn't reach catapults of the enemy.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">[3]</a></sup> It is possible that these Gondorian catapults were later used to crush or set aflame siege towers used by the besiegers.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Morgul-host uses catapults, operated by <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> and <a href="/wiki/Trolls" title="Trolls">Trolls</a>. They launch both severed heads of Gondorian soldiers and flaming missiles (as in the book), but also stone missiles, able to damage the walls of Minas Tirith.</dd>
<dd>Gondorians use <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trebuchet" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">trebuchets</a>, which launch pieces of broken walls instead of dedicated missiles. They are used successfully to destroy several siege towers of the enemy.</dd></dl>
