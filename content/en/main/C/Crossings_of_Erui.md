The **Crossings of Erui** or <i><b>Ethraid Erui</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> were fords in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## History {#History}

Some fifty miles south of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), the main road to [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) crossed the river [Erui](/wiki/Erui).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife) of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1447](/wiki/Third_Age_1447 "Third Age 1447"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Eldacar (King of Gondor)") battled [Castamir the Usurper](/wiki/Castamir "Castamir") at the crossing. Eldacar won and killed his foe, but many Gondorians fell that day.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Crossings of Erui were found in Eastern Gondor, and connected Upper <a href="/wiki/Lebennin" title="Lebennin">Lebennin</a> to <a href="/wiki/Lossarnach" title="Lossarnach">Lossarnach</a>. During the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, the ford was haunted by ancient <a href="/wiki/Gondorians" title="Gondorians">Gondorian</a> armour animated by water under the command of the angry River-maid the Lone Lady, preventing the invading <a href="/wiki/Haradrim" title="Haradrim">Haradrim</a> from crossing. The Lone Lady animated the long-lost armour of <a href="/wiki/Castamir" title="Castamir">Castamir</a> himself before being calmed by her sisters.</dd></dl>
