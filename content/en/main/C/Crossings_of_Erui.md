The **Crossings of Erui** or <i><b>Ethraid Erui</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> were fords in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

## History {#History}

Some fifty miles south of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), the main road to [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) crossed the river [Erui](../E/Erui.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In the [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md) of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1447](../T/Third_Age_1447.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Eldacar](<../E/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor).md>) battled [Castamir the Usurper](Castamir.md) at the crossing. Eldacar won and killed his foe, but many Gondorians fell that day.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Crossings of Erui were found in Eastern Gondor, and connected Upper <a href="../L/Lebennin.md" title="Lebennin">Lebennin</a> to <a href="../L/Lossarnach.md" title="Lossarnach">Lossarnach</a>. During the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, the ford was haunted by ancient <a href="../G/Gondorians.md" title="Gondorians">Gondorian</a> armour animated by water under the command of the angry River-maid the Lone Lady, preventing the invading <a href="../H/Haradrim.md" title="Haradrim">Haradrim</a> from crossing. The Lone Lady animated the long-lost armour of <a href="Castamir.md" title="Castamir">Castamir</a> himself before being calmed by her sisters.</dd></dl>
