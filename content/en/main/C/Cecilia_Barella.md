**Cecilia Barella** is a member of the [Italian Tolkien Society](/wiki/Italian_Tolkien_Society). Barella is also the editor of *A Tolkien Webliography*, a blog that tries to document Tolkien's texts on the net. As of July 2010, however, the work on the blog seems to be abandoned (no update since January 2008).
