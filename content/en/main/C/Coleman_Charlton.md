**Coleman Charlton** is an American table-top game designer. He was one of the founders of [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises), and designed the *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* game system<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the late 1990s, Coleman Charlton and [Pete Fenlon](/wiki/Pete_Fenlon), his long-time colleague from ICE, joined the Mayfair Game company.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
