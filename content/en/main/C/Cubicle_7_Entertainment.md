**Cubicle 7 Entertainment** is a British-based publisher and events company, specialising in the games industry. Founded in [2006](/wiki/2006), by Angus Abranson and Dominic McDowall-Thomas, the company has published role-playing games from a list of licensed and home-grown properties. On [20 January](/wiki/20_January) [2010](/wiki/2010), Cubicle announced the release of *[The One Ring: The Lord of the Rings Role-playing Game](/wiki/The_One_Ring:_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Role-playing_Game)*.

On [27 July](/wiki/27_July) [2016](/wiki/2016), Cubicle announced the release of *[Adventures in Middle-earth](/wiki/Adventures_in_Middle-earth)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Announce_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Announce-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [27 November](/wiki/27_November) [2019](/wiki/2019), the company announced that, by the first half of [2020](/wiki/2020), they would cease publishing The One Ring and Adventures in Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-TORE_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TORE-2">[2]</a></sup>
