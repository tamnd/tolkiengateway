When the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) explored the [Chamber of Mazarbul](/wiki/Chamber_of_Mazarbul) in [Moria](/wiki/Moria), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) mentioned that he had seen one or more **cave-trolls** in the passage outside. The huge arm and shoulder with a dark skin of greenish scales and the great flat toeless foot that appeared in the gap in the barred door of the chamber<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-1">[1]</a></sup> presumably belonged to this cave-troll<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>. After [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) had stabbed the foot with [Sting](/wiki/Sting), black blood dripped from the blade to the floor and smoked there.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-1">[1]</a></sup> Later during the flight of the fellowship through Moria, two trolls appeared in the [Second Hall](/wiki/Second_Hall) of Moria,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> but it is not recorded if they were cave-trolls.

Mentioned only once in the written records of [Arda](/wiki/Arda), nothing else is known of these creatures. One can only assume that this breed of [troll](/wiki/Troll "Troll") lived in some of the caves of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). The fact that Gandalf was able to recognise the creature he encountered in Moria specifically as a cave-troll strongly suggests that this appearance was shared by others of their kind.
