When the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) explored the [Chamber of Mazarbul](Chamber_of_Mazarbul.md) in [Moria](../M/Moria.md), [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) mentioned that he had seen one or more **cave-trolls** in the passage outside. The huge arm and shoulder with a dark skin of greenish scales and the great flat toeless foot that appeared in the gap in the barred door of the chamber<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-1">[1]</a></sup> presumably belonged to this cave-troll<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>. After [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) had stabbed the foot with [Sting](../S/Sting.md), black blood dripped from the blade to the floor and smoked there.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-1">[1]</a></sup> Later during the flight of the fellowship through Moria, two trolls appeared in the [Second Hall](../S/Second_Hall.md) of Moria,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> but it is not recorded if they were cave-trolls.

Mentioned only once in the written records of [Arda](../A/Arda.md), nothing else is known of these creatures. One can only assume that this breed of [troll](../T/Troll.md) lived in some of the caves of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). The fact that Gandalf was able to recognise the creature he encountered in Moria specifically as a cave-troll strongly suggests that this appearance was shared by others of their kind.
