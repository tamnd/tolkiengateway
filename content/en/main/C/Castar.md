The **Castar** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Mirian*) was the chief currency used within [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). A smaller coin was the *[tharni](/wiki/Tharni "Tharni")* or <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Canath&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Canath (page does not exist)">canath</a></i>, a silver coin worth a fourth of the *castar*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Westron](/wiki/Westron) *castar* bears a striking resemblance to the [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) word <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kast&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kast (page does not exist)">kast</a></i> and [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kastu&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kastu (page does not exist)">kastu</a></i>, which mean "[mathom](/wiki/Mathom "Mathom")".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Sindarin *mirian* seems to be derived from *[mîr](/wiki/M%C3%AEr "Mîr")*, "jewel".

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor" title="Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor">Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>The Castar, also known as “Gondorian Coin”, was described as being widely used throughout Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Mirian was described as a distinct coin that was once the currency of <a href="/wiki/Eregion" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Doors_of_Durin" title="Doors of Durin">Doors of Durin</a> and the great forge of Khazad-dûm featured on the coins in honour of the Dwarves who provided the <a href="/wiki/Mithril" title="Mithril">mithril</a> to make them. The coins became rare relics after <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> destroyed most of them.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>
