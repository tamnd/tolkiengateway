In *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*, **Caras Amarth** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “City of Doom”) was the second capital of [Elves of Mirkwood](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood), built by [Oropher](/wiki/Oropher) in the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). It mainly consisted of a massive underground palace carved out of a hill.<sup id="cite_ref-desc_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-desc-1">[1]</a></sup> After the construction of [Aradhryand](/wiki/Elvenking%27s_Halls "Elvenking's Halls") between [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1050](/wiki/Third_Age_1050 "Third Age 1050")-[1100](/wiki/Third_Age_1100 "Third Age 1100"), the court moved away from Caras Amarth.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> By [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1640](/wiki/Third_Age_1640 "Third Age 1640"), it has been reduced to ruins.<sup id="cite_ref-desc_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-desc-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Elf-path](/wiki/Elf-path) passed by the ruins.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
