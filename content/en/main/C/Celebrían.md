**Celebrían** was an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves")-lady, the daughter of [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn)<sup id="cite_ref-TA109_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA109-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the wife of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond)<sup id="cite_ref-TA109_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA109-1">[1]</a></sup>. She was the mother of [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) and the twins [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir) and [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan).<sup id="cite_ref-Children_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Children-3">[3]</a></sup> Her daughter would later marry [Aragorn Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"), hero of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and become the Queen of the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>.

## History {#History}

Celebrían was born early in the Second Age, likely in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [300](/wiki/Second_Age_300 "Second Age 300"), when Galadriel refused to return to [Eressëa](/wiki/Eress%C3%ABa), and passed over the [Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains "Blue Mountains").<sup id="cite_ref-Scales_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scales-6">[6]</a></sup> Celebrían lived in [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion), and later moved to [Lórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien") with her mother in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1350](/wiki/Second_Age_1350 "Second Age 1350"). In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1701](/wiki/Second_Age_1701 "Second Age 1701"), she went to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) with Galadriel searching for Celeborn, where she first met Lord Elrond, who loved her in secret.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-7">[7]</a></sup> Their marriage was delayed by the wars against [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:135</sup> though they would eventually wed in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [109](/wiki/Third_Age_109 "Third Age 109").<sup id="cite_ref-TA109_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA109-1">[1]</a></sup> Their first children, the twins [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan) and [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir), were born in [130](/wiki/Third_Age_130 "Third Age 130"), and their daughter [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) in [241](/wiki/Third_Age_241 "Third Age 241").<sup id="cite_ref-Children_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Children-3">[3]</a></sup> She had inherited the [Elfstone](/wiki/Elfstone) from her mother, which was later given to Arwen, her daughter.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2509](/wiki/Third_Age_2509 "Third Age 2509"), on a trip to [Lórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien") to visit her parents, she was waylaid by [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains) in the [Redhorn Pass](/wiki/Redhorn_Pass). She was captured and tormented, receiving a poisoned wound. Her sons rescued her and she was physically healed by Elrond, but never fully recovered in mind or spirit, and no longer wished to stay in Middle-earth. She left for the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) and passed over the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") the following year.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

She was followed by Elrond and her mother Galadriel, who eventually took the [White Ship](/wiki/White_Ship) in the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) and also passed the [Great Sea](/wiki/Great_Sea) to the [Undying Lands](/wiki/Undying_Lands),<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> where they presumably reunited with her. Later Celeborn and possibly her sons, Elladan and Elrohir, also went to the Grey Havens to sail to the Undying Lands,<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> and presumably reunited with Celebrían.

*Celebrían* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>, which means "Silver queen"<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>. It is probably a compound of *[celeb](/wiki/Celeb "Celeb")* ("silver") and *rían* ("queen").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

J.R.R. Tolkien tried to define the birth-year of Celebrían in different manuscripts, specially those dealing with chronology and Elven growth. Here are the dates of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), presented in chronological order as Tolkien considered them: [300](/wiki/Second_Age_300 "Second Age 300") (circa),<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> 300 ("probably"),<sup id="cite_ref-Scales_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scales-6">[6]</a></sup> 260 or [350](/wiki/Second_Age_350 "Second Age 350"),<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> [850](/wiki/Second_Age_850 "Second Age 850") (a proposal while calculating her age),<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> 9 (circa) or 45.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

In "[Concerning Galadriel and Celeborn](/wiki/Concerning_Galadriel_and_Celeborn)" (the only narrative dealing with a complete history of Middle-earth during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age)), both Celebrían and [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth) are children of Galadriel and Celeborn,<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-7">[7]</a></sup> but in later texts J.R.R. Tolkien gave Amroth a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") origin.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

In the drafts of *[The Tale of Years](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Years)* of the Third Age, Elrond married Celebrían c. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") 2500 and their children were born in the same century. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2600](/wiki/Third_Age_2600 "Third Age 2600"), she was slain by Orcs instead of captured.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-21">[21]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:226</sup> The marriage was later changed to 2300, and her capture and departure to 2509.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-21">[21]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:234–6</sup>
