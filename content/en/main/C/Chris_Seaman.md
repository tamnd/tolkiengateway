**Chris Seaman** is an illustrator. As a *[Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md)* artist, he created a postcard for the *[Tales of Middle-earth](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Tales_of_Middle-earth.md)* expansion and a piece of alternate art for *[The Hobbit](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit.md)* expansion.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
