**Cirith Gorgor** was a ravine between the mountain ranges of the [Ered Lithui](../E/Ered_Lithui.md) and the [Ephel Dúath](../E/Ephel_Dúath.md)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> in the north-west of the valley of [Udûn](<../U/Udûn_(valley).md>) in [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was the main entrance to Mordor and was sealed by the [Black Gate](../B/Black_Gate.md) and guarded by the two [Towers of the Teeth](../T/Towers_of_the_Teeth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Cirith Gorgor is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name,<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> which is translated as "Haunted Pass".<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Gate_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-3">[3]</a></sup> It is a combination of *[cirith](Cirith.md)* ("cleft", "ravine", "defile") and *[gorgor](../G/Gorgor.md)* ("terror", "haunting fear")".<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup>
