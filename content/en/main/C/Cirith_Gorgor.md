**Cirith Gorgor** was a ravine between the mountain ranges of the [Ered Lithui](/wiki/Ered_Lithui) and the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> in the north-west of the valley of [Udûn](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(valley)> "Udûn (valley)") in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was the main entrance to Mordor and was sealed by the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) and guarded by the two [Towers of the Teeth](/wiki/Towers_of_the_Teeth).<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Cirith Gorgor is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name,<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> which is translated as "Haunted Pass".<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Gate_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-3">[3]</a></sup> It is a combination of *[cirith](/wiki/Cirith "Cirith")* ("cleft", "ravine", "defile") and *[gorgor](/wiki/Gorgor "Gorgor")* ("terror", "haunting fear")".<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup>
