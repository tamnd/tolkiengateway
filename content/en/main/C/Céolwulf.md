**Céolwulf** was the father of [Tréowine](/wiki/Tr%C3%A9owine), a close friend of the famous mariner [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Céolwulf* is in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English). It might be derived from the elements *ceol* ("keel") + *wulf* ("wolf").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In one of the manuscripts of *[The Notion Club Papers](/wiki/The_Notion_Club_Papers)*, during the recital of the *[Lay of King Sheave](/wiki/Lay_of_King_Sheave)* in <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Edward_the_Elder&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Edward the Elder (page does not exist)">Edward the Elder</a>'s court, it wasn't Ælfwine or Tréowine who recited the poem, but rather one Céolwulf (who wasn't the father of Tréowine at that point).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
