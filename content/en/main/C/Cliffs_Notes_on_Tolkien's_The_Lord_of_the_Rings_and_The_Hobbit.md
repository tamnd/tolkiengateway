<b><i>Cliffs Notes on Tolkien&#39;s </i>The Lord of the Rings<i> and </i>The Hobbit</b> is a book in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CliffsNotes" title="wikipedia:CliffsNotes">CliffsNotes</a> series of books. The series aims to provide student study guides of noted literature.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

CliffsNotes on Lord of the Rings follows [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)'s adventures in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) through [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md), [The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md), and [The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md). When the [hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) Frodo receives [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md), forged by the evil [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), he joins [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md), [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md), and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) on a dangerous quest to cast the ring into the fires of [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md). Lord of the Rings was pivotal in the development of the fantasy genre as it appears today.
