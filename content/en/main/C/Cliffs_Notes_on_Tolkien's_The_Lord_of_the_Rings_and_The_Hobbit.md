<b><i>Cliffs Notes on Tolkien&#39;s </i>The Lord of the Rings<i> and </i>The Hobbit</b> is a book in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CliffsNotes" title="wikipedia:CliffsNotes">CliffsNotes</a> series of books. The series aims to provide student study guides of noted literature.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

CliffsNotes on Lord of the Rings follows [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins")'s adventures in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) through [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring), [The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers), and [The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King). When the [hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") Frodo receives [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), forged by the evil [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), he joins [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck"), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir), and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) on a dangerous quest to cast the ring into the fires of [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom). Lord of the Rings was pivotal in the development of the fantasy genre as it appears today.
