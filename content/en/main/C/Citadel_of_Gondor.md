The **Citadel of Minas Tirith**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UIHighCourt_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UIHighCourt-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> **Citadel**<sup id="cite_ref-MT_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:752</sup> or **High City**<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:972</sup> was the topmost seventh walled circle of the city of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UIHighCourt_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UIHighCourt-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

The Citadel was a strong fortress<sup id="cite_ref-MT_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:761</sup> on the top of the [Hill of Guard](../H/Hill_of_Guard.md), on which the city of Minas Tirith was built,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> 700 feet above the [Great Gate of Minas Tirith](../G/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:752</sup> It consisted of the topmost seventh wall in the shape of a circle with seven towers at equal distances along the wall, of which one tower stood next to the gate of the Citadel. On its eastern side was a battlement on the top of a pier of rock, which looked like a ship-keel and rose from the ground behind the Great Gate on the lowest circle of walls of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-Plan_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Plan-8">[8]</a></sup>

The entrance to the Citadel was on the east side and was carved into the rock. A sloping lamp-lit tunnel led up to the seventh gate, which was guarded by the [Guards of the Citadel](../G/Guards_of_the_Citadel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:752–3</sup>

The [Tower of Ecthelion](../T/Tower_of_Ecthelion.md)<sup id="cite_ref-MT_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:751</sup> and the [Hall of the Kings](../H/Hall_of_the_Kings.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:968</sup> were in the centre of the Citadel.<sup id="cite_ref-Plan_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Plan-8">[8]</a></sup> In front of (i.e. east of) the Tower of Ecthelion was<sup id="cite_ref-Plan_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Plan-8">[8]</a></sup> the [Court of the Fountain](<Court_of_the_Fountain_(Minas_Tirith).md>) where the [White Tree](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md) had grown and died<sup id="cite_ref-MT_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:753</sup> and grew again after King [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) had found and planted a seedling of it.<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:971</sup> Behind (i.e. west of) the Tower of Ecthelion stood<sup id="cite_ref-Plan_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Plan-8">[8]</a></sup> the [King's House](../K/King's_House.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> To the right of (i.e. north of) the Tower of Ecthelion was<sup id="cite_ref-Plan_8-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Plan-8">[8]</a></sup> the [Great Hall of Feasts](../G/Great_Hall_of_Feasts.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-10">[10]</a></sup> The Citadel also contained barracks for the Guard, a storehouse and buttery for the Guard,<sup id="cite_ref-MT_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:762</sup> a mess for the Guard in a small hall on the north side,<sup id="cite_ref-MT_4-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:772</sup> a house for guests close to the walls on the north side,<sup id="cite_ref-MT_4-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:759</sup> armories<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> and deep vaults.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

It is possible that [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md), the new [King of Rohan](../K/King_of_Rohan.md), referred to the city of Minas Tirith in general and not to the Citadel of Minas Tirith as the topmost seventh circle of the city, when he said that the dead [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md), his uncle and former King of Rohan, lay in the "Citadel of Gondor".<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[note 1]</a></sup> At the time of his statement the body of Théoden had been laid on a bed of state in front of the dais in the Hall of the Kings<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup> and was later laid in a tomb in the [Hallows](../H/Hallows.md) of Minas Tirith,<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:969</sup><sup id="cite_ref-Partings_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-10">[10]</a></sup> which were located behind (i.e. west of) the fifth circle of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_4-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:752</sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a preliminary draft of what would later become the chapter "[The Steward and the King](../T/The_Steward_and_the_King.md)" it is mentioned that the house in which the Companions of the Ring lived together with Gandalf was in the citadel.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup> In an earlier version of what would later become the chapter "[Many Partings](../M/Many_Partings.md)", Merethrond is said to be in the citadel. A page for this passage contains a plan of the citadel, which shows the shape of the wall, the locations of the towers, of the White Tower and the Hall of the Kings, of the Court of the Fountain, of the King's House and of the Hall of Feasts and outlines of other buildings.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[16]</a></sup>
