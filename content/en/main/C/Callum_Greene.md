**Callum Greene** ([22 February](/wiki/22_February), [1971](/wiki/1971)) is a British producer. He was credited as executive producer in *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)* and he is one of the executive producers of the first season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* series by [Amazon](/wiki/Amazon.com_Incorporated "Amazon.com Incorporated").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
