The **Cushing Memorial Library and Archives collection** refers to a collection of [sixteen philological books in German](/wiki/List_of_books_in_Tolkien%27s_library "List of books in Tolkien's library") from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s personal library and two full [pages and four fragments of notes and two letters](/wiki/Index:Unpublished_material "Index:Unpublished material"), held at the Cushing Memorial Library and Archives at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A%26M_University" title="wikipedia:Texas A&amp;M University">Texas A&amp;M University</a>.

Fifteen of the books are inscribed with Tolkien's name and date, and a number of these contain his annotations. Madeline J. Keyser provided a description of parts of the material in the article "Sixteen Philological Books and Notes from the Library of J.R.R. Tolkien" (appearing on the website [Tolkien Library](/wiki/Tolkien_Library)).<sup id="cite_ref-Sixteen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sixteen-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) and [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) have analyzed portions of the material posted by Keyser, specifically the fragments consisting of an "invented [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish), or Elvish-influenced, language".<sup id="cite_ref-Hostetter_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hostetter-2">[2]</a></sup>
