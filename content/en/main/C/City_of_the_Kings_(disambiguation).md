The **City of the Kings** was a name used for two royal cities of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) at different times in history and can refer to:

- **[Armenelos](/wiki/Armenelos)**, the capital of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), which was the seat of the island's rulers throughout the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).
- **[Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith)**, city of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), originally named [Minas Anor](/wiki/Minas_Anor) before the [twenty-seventh King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor"), [Tarondor](</wiki/Tarondor_(King_of_Gondor)> "Tarondor (King of Gondor)"), moved the royal seat of Gondor from [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) to Minas Anor in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).
