**Corey Olsen** (born [16 August](/wiki/16_August) [1974](/wiki/1974)) is a professor and podcaster known for promoting and analyzing the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). He was formerly a professor at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_College" title="wikipedia:Washington College">Washington College</a> in Chestertown, Maryland, USA, where he taught courses in J.R.R. Tolkien and medieval literature, specializing in Chaucer and Sir Thomas Malory.

In the summer of 2009, he launched his website and podcast *[The Tolkien Professor](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Professor)*, on which he is posting a detailed lecture series on Tolkien's works, as well as responses to readers' questions and discussions with other Tolkien readers.

He is president of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Signum_University&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Signum University (page does not exist)">Signum University</a>, which holds online courses on Tolkien and other literary subjects, as well as its free counterpart, [Mythgard Institute](/wiki/Mythgard_Institute).
