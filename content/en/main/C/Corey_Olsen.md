**Corey Olsen** (born [16 August](../_/16_August.md) [1974](../_/1974.md)) is a professor and podcaster known for promoting and analyzing the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). He was formerly a professor at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_College" title="wikipedia:Washington College">Washington College</a> in Chestertown, Maryland, USA, where he taught courses in J.R.R. Tolkien and medieval literature, specializing in Chaucer and Sir Thomas Malory.

In the summer of 2009, he launched his website and podcast *[The Tolkien Professor](../T/The_Tolkien_Professor.md)*, on which he is posting a detailed lecture series on Tolkien's works, as well as responses to readers' questions and discussions with other Tolkien readers.

He is president of <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Signum_University&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Signum University (page does not exist)">Signum University</a>, which holds online courses on Tolkien and other literary subjects, as well as its free counterpart, [Mythgard Institute](../M/Mythgard_Institute.md).
