**Calenhad** was the sixth and penultimate penultimate [Beacon-hill](../W/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), which ran west from [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) on the northern side of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) that looked down on the plains of [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> It was located to south-east of [Firienholt](../F/Firienholt.md).

## Description {#Description}

Calenhad was a small, green, treeless hill, slightly bigger and taller than [Erelas](../E/Erelas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup> Its beacon was seldom lit and its lighting during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) indicated great urgency.

## Hitstory {#Hitstory}

On the night of [7 March](../_/7_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), riding eastward on [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md), saw the beacons being lit to call for aid from Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is suggested in the [Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md) that Calenhad probably means "green space" from the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) (*[calen](Calen.md)* "green" + *sad* ([lenited](../L/Lenition.md) to *had*) "place, spot").<sup id="cite_ref-UT_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-5">[5]</a></sup> In "[The Rivers and Beacon-hills of Gondor](../T/The_Rivers_and_Beacon-hills_of_Gondor~21248.md)", however, [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) suggested that the second element may be *sâdh* "sward, turf".<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>
