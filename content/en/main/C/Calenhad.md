**Calenhad** was the sixth and penultimate penultimate [Beacon-hill](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), which ran west from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) on the northern side of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) that looked down on the plains of [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> It was located to south-east of [Firienholt](/wiki/Firienholt).

## Description {#Description}

Calenhad was a small, green, treeless hill, slightly bigger and taller than [Erelas](/wiki/Erelas).<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup> Its beacon was seldom lit and its lighting during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) indicated great urgency.

## Hitstory {#Hitstory}

On the night of [7 March](/wiki/7_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), riding eastward on [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax), saw the beacons being lit to call for aid from Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is suggested in the [Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales) that Calenhad probably means "green space" from the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (*[calen](/wiki/Calen "Calen")* "green" + *sad* ([lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") to *had*) "place, spot").<sup id="cite_ref-UT_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-5">[5]</a></sup> In "[The Rivers and Beacon-hills of Gondor](/wiki/The_Rivers_and_Beacon-hills_of_Gondor)", however, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") suggested that the second element may be *sâdh* "sward, turf".<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>
