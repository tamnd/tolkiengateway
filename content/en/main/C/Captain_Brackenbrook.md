**Captain Brackenbrook** is the leader of [Archet](/wiki/Archet)'s garrison.<sup id="cite_ref-Captain_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captain-1">[1]</a></sup> Captain Brackenbrook is mostly found in the Mad Badger Inn, from where he commands the garrison.<sup id="cite_ref-Captain_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captain-1">[1]</a></sup> He was killed in the Assault of Archet.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> After his death his son, [Jon](/wiki/Jon_Brackenbrook "Jon Brackenbrook"), succeeds him as captain.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
