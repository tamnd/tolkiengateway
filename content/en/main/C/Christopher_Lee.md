**Sir Christopher Frank Carandini Lee** [CBE](/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire "Order of the British Empire") ([1922](/wiki/1922)-[2015](/wiki/2015))<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was an English actor, who portrayed [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) in [*The Lord of the Rings* film trilogy](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), [*The Hobbit* film trilogy](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), and read the [*The Children of Húrin* audiobook](</wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin_(audiobook)> "The Children of Húrin (audiobook)").

## Life {#Life}

Lee had a long history with [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s fiction; he read *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* after leaving the Royal Air Force in [1945](/wiki/1945), and since *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* came out, he read all Tolkien's books once a year. Lee also had the experience of actually meeting Tolkien in person (making him the only individual involved in the film trilogies to do so) while visiting [The Eagle and Child](/wiki/The_Eagle_and_Child) during the 1950s:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>We were sitting there talking and drinking beer, and someone said, &#34;Oh, look who walked in.&#34; It was Professor Tolkien, and I nearly fell off my chair. I didn&#39;t even know he was alive. He was a benign looking man, smoking a pipe, walking in, an English countryman with earth under his feet. And he was a genius, a man of incredible intellectual knowledge. He knew somebody in our group. He (the man in the group) said &#34;Oh Professor, Professor...&#34; And he came over. And each one of us, well I knelt of course, each one of us said &#34;how do you do?&#34; And I just said &#34;Ho.. How.. How...&#34;
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>

### Filming The Lord of The Rings {#Filming_The_Lord_of_The_Rings}

In his youth, Lee had envisioned himself playing Gandalf, though he realized he was too old for that by the time his agent called about a potential part in [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson)'s adaptation. He was nonetheless very enthusiastic about taking part. Though he doesn't remember what he read during the casting process, he was later told Jackson specifically wanted him for the role of Saruman beforehand.<sup id="cite_ref-TORNinterview_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TORNinterview-3">[3]</a></sup> Lee had never been in a movie with the actual Gandalf, [Sir Ian McKellen](/wiki/Ian_McKellen "Ian McKellen"), but the two quickly became friends, being the oldest actors on the set (though Lee was 17 years older). When McKellen was cast as Gandalf, Lee was 78 years old and McKellen was 61.

Lee shot most of his scenes in [Wellington](/wiki/Wellington), in the main studio, but also shot one scene in Wellington's national park. He visited [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) four times, the longest time being ten weeks. He later did some post-synching in London.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

While jet-lagged, Lee broke his hand smashing it against a wall.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> Several shots of him in the finished films show him carefully hiding this bandaged left hand.

### Other projects {#Other_projects}

Known for his booming voice, Christopher Lee has sung operas, and performed with the [Tolkien Ensemble](/wiki/Tolkien_Ensemble) on their CDs *[At Dawn in Rivendell](/wiki/At_Dawn_in_Rivendell)* and *[Leaving Rivendell](/wiki/Leaving_Rivendell)*. He sang the role of [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard), as well as reciting numerous other poems. Beyond purely Tolkien-based music, he also worked with metal band *Rhapsody of Fire*, playing a *Wizard-king* character in several albums, possibly inspired by his role as Saruman.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Lee has recounted his life and his connections with Tolkien's work in the foreword to [Chris Smith](/wiki/Chris_Smith)'s *[The Lord of the Rings: Weapons and Warfare](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Weapons_and_Warfare)*, and in chapter 74, titled "Spellbinder", of his autobiography, *Lord of Misrule*.

Lee agreed to reprise his role as Saruman for [*The Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)") on the condition that, due to his age, he did not have to fly out to New Zealand to be filmed. As a result all of his close-up his scenes as Saruman in this film where shot in London and not in New Zealand, while his stunt scenes where shot in New Zealand with a body double.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

I&#39;m still an enormous fan - I read The Lord of the Rings every year.
</p>
