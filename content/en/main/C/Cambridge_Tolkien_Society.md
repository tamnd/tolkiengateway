**The Cambridge Tolkien Society**, CTS, is a [Tolkien society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies") of [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge), England founded in 1983.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Publications {#Publications}

CTS has published its magazine *Anor*<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> since it was founded, continuing to publish new issues once or twice a year. Back issues can be accessed for free through their website.
