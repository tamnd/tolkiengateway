## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>can-</b></i> means "cry out, shout, call" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme"): [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) [KAN](/wiki/KAN)<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Telerin {#Telerin}

<i><b>can-</b></i> means "cry aloud, call" or "to summons or name a person" in [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme"): [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) [KAN](/wiki/KAN)<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
