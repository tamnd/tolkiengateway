**Cruachan** is an Irish group that began as a Tolkien folk concept band named [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) and released several songs related to the legendarium.

After changing their name to Cruachan, they became known for combining black metal and Irish folk to create Folk Metal. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
