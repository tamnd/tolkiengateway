**Cruachan** is an Irish group that began as a Tolkien folk concept band named [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) and released several songs related to the legendarium.

After changing their name to Cruachan, they became known for combining black metal and Irish folk to create Folk Metal. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Members {#Members}

- Keith Fay – vocals, guitar, keyboards, bodhrán, mandolin, percussion, bouzouki (1992–present)
- Joe Farrell – bass (2020–present); drums (1999–2007)
- Audrey Trainor – violin (2020–present)
- David Quinn – guitar (2020–present)
- Sterling Paterson – drums (2026 –present)
