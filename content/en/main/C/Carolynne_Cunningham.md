**Carolynne Cunningham** served as an assistant director for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and was a producer for [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").

## Awards {#Awards}

- [2004](/wiki/2004) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.dga.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">DGA Award</a></b>, Outstanding Directorial Achievement in Motion Pictures, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*
- [2014](/wiki/2014) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.empireonline.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Empire Award</a></b>, Best Sci-Fi/Fantasy, *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)*
- 2014 - <b> <a class="external text" href="http://heartlandfilm.org/festival/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Heartland Film Festival</a></b>, Truly Moving Picture Award, *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)*
- 2014 - <b> <a class="external text" href="http://www.producersguild.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">PGA Awards</a></b>, Vanguard Award, [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")
