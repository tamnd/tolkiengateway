**Calenardhon** was a green region north of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains), which became a province of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and later became known as [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

## History {#History}

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [882](/wiki/Second_Age_882 "Second Age 882"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), the king of the [elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") realm of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) and [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) warned [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur), the [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor) in a letter that a servant of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) was arising in the East and that he thought that he would invade the Westlands from the East through the gap between the southern end of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) and the White Mountains in Calenardhon, that they at least had to defend Eriador along the long rivers west of the Misty Mountains and asked Tar-Meneldur for men to help to strengthen the haven of [Vinyalondë](/wiki/Vinyalond%C3%AB) so that it could accomodate the great ships of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1695](/wiki/Second_Age_1695 "Second Age 1695"), this servant, [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) came with a great army through Calenardhon and invaded Eriador in the [War of the Elves and Sauron](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron). When Sauron's army was utterly defeated in the [Battle of the Gwathló](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Gwathl%C3%B3) with the help of a Númenorean army which had landed at the haven of Vinyalondë at the mouth of the river [Gwathló](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3) Sauron only narrowly escaped with a small force, but he was again attacked in the east of Calenardhon and fled back with only a bodyguard of troops to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1437](/wiki/Third_Age_1437 "Third Age 1437"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> during the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife) when <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Eldacar" title="Eldacar">Eldacar</a>, the deposed [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor) returned to Gondor from his refuge in [Wilderland](/wiki/Wilderland) with a great army of [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen) who had served Gondor and of [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) of the northern parts of Gondor, folk from the province of Calenardhon gathered to him.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

From [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636") to [1637](/wiki/Third_Age_1637 "Third Age 1637") during the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) the people of Calenhardhon suffered like all the peoples of the rest of Gondor and [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

From [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2063](/wiki/Third_Age_2063 "Third Age 2063") to [2460](/wiki/Third_Age_2460 "Third Age 2460") the population of Calenardhon declined when a part of it moved to the east to hold the line of the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). The Gondorian garrisons of the fortresses of [Aglarond](/wiki/Hornburg "Hornburg") and [Angrenost](/wiki/Isengard "Isengard") were not renewed and the forts were left in the care of local hereditary chieftains. The subjects of those chieftains became more mixed in blood, because the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) were not hindered to migrate over the river [Isen](/wiki/Isen) into Calenardhon.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

During the rule of the [Steward](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor "Steward of Gondor") [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion), [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) was attacked by the [Balchoth](/wiki/Balchoth) and by Orcs from the Misty Mountains, and Calenardhon was overrun by these invaders. Cirion sent messages for help to Gondor's old allies, the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), to help defend Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-stewards_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stewards-8">[8]</a></sup>

[Eorl the Young](/wiki/Eorl_the_Young) answered the call, and helped Gondor defeat the Balchoth and the Orcs at the [Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Field_of_Celebrant). In gratitude, Cirion granted all Calenardhon to the Éothéod.<sup id="cite_ref-stewards_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stewards-8">[8]</a></sup>

After the Éothéod settled in Calenardhon, the land was called [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) in Gondor<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and the Mark of the Riders,<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> the [Mark](/wiki/Mark)<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> or the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Riddermark" title="Riddermark">Riddermark</a> by the Éothéod.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

<i><b>Calenardhon</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name,<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-17">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GS_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GS-18">[18]</a></sup> which means "green province"<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> or "the (great) green region".<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> The initial element is clearly *[calen](/wiki/Calen "Calen")* ("green"),<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-17">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GS_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GS-18">[18]</a></sup> the second element is probably <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gardh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gardh (page does not exist)">gardh</a></i> ("region")<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-17">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GS_18-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GS-18">[18]</a></sup> and the final element is the suffix *[on(d)](/wiki/-nd "-nd")* ("region").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_17-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-17">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GS_18-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GS-18">[18]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup>

Earlier names used by Tolkien for this region were <i><a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Elenarda_(disambiguation)" title="Elenarda (disambiguation)">Elenarda</a></i> &gt; *Kalen(arda)* &gt; *Kalinarda* &gt; *Calenardan* &gt; *Calenardon*.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup>
