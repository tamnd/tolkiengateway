The **Corsairs of Umbar** were pirates that were based in the [Haven of Umbar](../U/Umbar.md).

## History {#History}

During the [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md), the defeated rebels of [Castamir](Castamir.md) fled Gondor to Umbar — by this time Umbar became the hated enemy of Gondor and a welcome refuge for its enemies. Ever since they claimed [South Gondor](../H/Harondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-1">[1]</a></sup>

They allied with the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md) against Gondor; in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1540](../T/Third_Age_1540.md) King [Aldamir](../A/Aldamir.md) was slain during that war.<sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Angamaite](../A/Angamaite.md) and [Sangahyando](../S/Sangahyando.md), the descendants of Castamir, led the Corsairs to ravage [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md), killing King [Minardil](../M/Minardil.md) who was there ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1634](../T/Third_Age_1634.md)). In the following years, Gondor suffered by the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) and Corsairs raided the Gondorian [coasts](../O/Outlands.md) up to [Anfalas](../A/Anfalas.md), until [Umbardacil](../U/Umbardacil.md) avenged Minardil's death, slew the last descendants of Castamir, drove away the Corsairs and retook Umbar ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1810](../T/Third_Age_1810.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup>

Eventually, Umbar was taken by the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the reign of Steward Cirion ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2489](../T/Third_Age_2489.md)-[2567](../T/Third_Age_2567.md)),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the Corsairs of Umbar attacked the coasts of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2746](../T/Third_Age_2746.md), the Corsairs were involved in a conflict with the fifteenth [Prince of Dol Amroth](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) and killed him.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Corsairs took a long time to prepare a great fleet. In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2758](../T/Third_Age_2758.md) three fleets sailed from Umbar and [Harad](../H/Harad.md) and landed at many places along the coasts of Gondor and even at the mouth of the river [Isen](../I/Isen.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-6">[6]</a></sup> and in the mouth of the river [Lefnui](../L/Lefnui.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-7">[7]</a></sup> The troops from Umbar and Harad supported the Dunlendings who were led by Wulf to invade Rohan from the west over the river Isen and down from Isengard.<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-7">[7]</a></sup> Before the spring of [2759](../T/Third_Age_2759.md), [Beregond](<../B/Beregond_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) defeated the Corsairs of Umbar and the Men of Harad that had invaded Gondor and subsequently sent troops to Rohan to help the Rohirrim to defeat the invaders.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-6">[6]</a></sup> As a result the Dunleandings were driven from Rohan and from Isengard.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In the time of [Ecthelion II](../E/Ecthelion_II.md), a man under his service known as [Thorongil](../T/Thorongil.md), warned him that the Corsairs were a great peril for the [Southern Fiefs](../S/Southern_Fiefs.md). With a small fleet, Thorongil made a [surprise attack](../S/Surprise_Attack_on_Umbar~64111.md), burning a great part of their ships and overthrowing the [Captain of the Haven](Captain_of_the_Haven.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-9">[9]</a></sup>

During the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), a fleet of Corsairs was raiding [Lebennin](../L/Lebennin.md) when [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) captured their ships<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and rowed them to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) to relieve the siege of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-pelennor_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pelennor-11">[11]</a></sup>

It is possible that the threat posed by the Corsairs of Umbar to the coastlands of Gondor and to sea traffic was completely subdued during the reign of King Elessar<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> in a war between the forces of Gondor led by King Elessar supported by the cavalry of the Rohan led by King Éomer on the distant fields of the South.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Culture {#Culture}

The corsairs's fleet included <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dromon" title="wikipedia:Dromon">dromunds</a>, and ships with deep hulls and many oars and with black sails.<sup id="cite_ref-pelennor_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pelennor-11">[11]</a></sup> Others were recognizable by their red sails, adorned with a black star or eye.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

In the course of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) Westron had become the native language of nearly all [Men](../M/Men.md) that lived within the borders of the old kingdom of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), including all along the coasts from Umbar northward and inland as far as the [Ephel Dúath](../E/Ephel_Dúath.md). At the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) Westron was still the native tongue in this area.<sup id="cite_ref-F1_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-F1-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) described in his [notes for the translation of names in *The Lord of the Rings* into other languages](../G/Guide_to_the_Names_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) that the Corsairs "are imagined as similar to the Mediterranean corsairs: sea-robbers with fortified bases".<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

John Bowers suggests that the Corsairs of Umbar were inspired by the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbary_Pirates" title="wikipedia:Barbary Pirates">Pirates of the Barbary Coast</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> These pirates included the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sal%C3%A9_Rovers" title="wikipedia:Salé Rovers">pirates</a> of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Sal%C3%A9" title="wikipedia:Republic of Salé">Salé</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morocco" title="wikipedia:Morocco">Morocco</a>. In the 17th century thousands of muslims or former muslims fled from Spain to Salé where they established a republic that engaged in piracy and made raids in Spain and even on the coasts of England where they took members of the popluation with them as slaves.

Myriam Librán-Moreno seems to see parallels between the Corsairs of Umbar and the conquest of Crete by the Arabs in A.D. 824, who established an emirate there with Crete becoming a center of Arab pirates in the Mediterranean that caused hardships for the Byzantine Empire.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

J.R.R. Tolkien wrote on [his son's map of Middle-earth](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md) for [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md) that Umbar and the City of the Corsairs are approximately at the latitude of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus" title="wikipedia:Cyprus">Cyprus</a> and that [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) is approximately at the latitude of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenna" title="wikipedia:Ravenna">Ravenna</a>, but more to the east near <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgrade" title="wikipedia:Belgrade">Belgrade</a>. He wrote that these references are so that Pauline Baynes can roughly judge the climate and the fauna and flora for her [map of Middle-earth](../A/A_Map_of_Middle-earth.md). In addition, he wrote in a [letter to Charlotte and Denis Plimmer](../L/Letter_294.md) that the city of [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) is approximately at the latitude of ancient <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troy" title="wikipedia:Troy">Troy</a>. Cyprus is approximately on the same latidue as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carthage" title="wikipedia:Carthage">Carthage</a>, an ancient city in northern Africa at the Mediterranean Sea and a historic enemy of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire" title="wikipedia:Roman Empire">Roman Empire</a>, and as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangier" title="wikipedia:Tangier">Tangier</a>, an ancient city in northwest Africa at the southern entrance to the Mediterranean Sea.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2006: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Corsairs are from the <a href="../M/Mordor.md" title="Mordor">Mordor</a> faction, and are equipped with knives and fire-bombs.</dd></dl>

<b>2023: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Corsairs_of_Umbar.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online: Corsairs of Umbar">The Lord of the Rings Online: Corsairs of Umbar</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Corsairs were led by four brothers who falsely call themselves the Heirs of <a href="Castamir.md" title="Castamir">Castamir</a>. These were Azruthor, Dolgimil, Azgarzôr, and the eldest Balakhôr the Scourge. The player negotiated with a Corsair named Jajax, who ended up siding with the player against the Heirs and their followers.</dd></dl>
