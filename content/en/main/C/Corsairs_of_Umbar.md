The **Corsairs of Umbar** were pirates that were based in the [Haven of Umbar](/wiki/Umbar "Umbar").

## History {#History}

During the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife), the defeated rebels of [Castamir](/wiki/Castamir) fled Gondor to Umbar — by this time Umbar became the hated enemy of Gondor and a welcome refuge for its enemies. Ever since they claimed [South Gondor](/wiki/Harondor "Harondor").<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-1">[1]</a></sup>

They allied with the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) against Gondor; in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1540](/wiki/Third_Age_1540 "Third Age 1540") King [Aldamir](/wiki/Aldamir) was slain during that war.<sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Angamaite](/wiki/Angamaite) and [Sangahyando](/wiki/Sangahyando), the descendants of Castamir, led the Corsairs to ravage [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir), killing King [Minardil](/wiki/Minardil) who was there ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1634](/wiki/Third_Age_1634 "Third Age 1634")). In the following years, Gondor suffered by the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) and Corsairs raided the Gondorian [coasts](/wiki/Outlands "Outlands") up to [Anfalas](/wiki/Anfalas), until [Umbardacil](/wiki/Umbardacil) avenged Minardil's death, slew the last descendants of Castamir, drove away the Corsairs and retook Umbar ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1810](/wiki/Third_Age_1810 "Third Age 1810")).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup>

Eventually, Umbar was taken by the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the reign of Steward Cirion ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2489](/wiki/Third_Age_2489 "Third Age 2489")-[2567](/wiki/Third_Age_2567 "Third Age 2567")),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the Corsairs of Umbar attacked the coasts of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2746](/wiki/Third_Age_2746 "Third Age 2746"), the Corsairs were involved in a conflict with the fifteenth [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth "Princes of Dol Amroth") and killed him.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Corsairs took a long time to prepare a great fleet. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758") three fleets sailed from Umbar and [Harad](/wiki/Harad) and landed at many places along the coasts of Gondor and even at the mouth of the river [Isen](/wiki/Isen)<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-6">[6]</a></sup> and in the mouth of the river [Lefnui](/wiki/Lefnui).<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-7">[7]</a></sup> The troops from Umbar and Harad supported the Dunlendings who were led by Wulf to invade Rohan from the west over the river Isen and down from Isengard.<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-7">[7]</a></sup> Before the spring of [2759](/wiki/Third_Age_2759 "Third Age 2759"), [Beregond](</wiki/Beregond_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Beregond (Steward of Gondor)") defeated the Corsairs of Umbar and the Men of Harad that had invaded Gondor and subsequently sent troops to Rohan to help the Rohirrim to defeat the invaders.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-6">[6]</a></sup> As a result the Dunleandings were driven from Rohan and from Isengard.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In the time of [Ecthelion II](/wiki/Ecthelion_II), a man under his service known as [Thorongil](/wiki/Thorongil), warned him that the Corsairs were a great peril for the [Southern Fiefs](/wiki/Southern_Fiefs). With a small fleet, Thorongil made a [surprise attack](/wiki/Surprise_Attack_on_Umbar "Surprise Attack on Umbar"), burning a great part of their ships and overthrowing the [Captain of the Haven](/wiki/Captain_of_the_Haven).<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-9">[9]</a></sup>

During the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), a fleet of Corsairs was raiding [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin) when [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) captured their ships<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and rowed them to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) to relieve the siege of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-pelennor_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pelennor-11">[11]</a></sup>

It is possible that the threat posed by the Corsairs of Umbar to the coastlands of Gondor and to sea traffic was completely subdued during the reign of King Elessar<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> in a war between the forces of Gondor led by King Elessar supported by the cavalry of the Rohan led by King Éomer on the distant fields of the South.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Culture {#Culture}

The corsairs's fleet included <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dromon" title="wikipedia:Dromon">dromunds</a>, and ships with deep hulls and many oars and with black sails.<sup id="cite_ref-pelennor_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pelennor-11">[11]</a></sup> Others were recognizable by their red sails, adorned with a black star or eye.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

In the course of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) Westron had become the native language of nearly all [Men](/wiki/Men) that lived within the borders of the old kingdom of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), including all along the coasts from Umbar northward and inland as far as the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath). At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) Westron was still the native tongue in this area.<sup id="cite_ref-F1_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-F1-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) described in his [notes for the translation of names in *The Lord of the Rings* into other languages](/wiki/Guide_to_the_Names_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "Guide to the Names in The Lord of the Rings") that the Corsairs "are imagined as similar to the Mediterranean corsairs: sea-robbers with fortified bases".<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

John Bowers suggests that the Corsairs of Umbar were inspired by the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbary_Pirates" title="wikipedia:Barbary Pirates">Pirates of the Barbary Coast</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> These pirates included the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sal%C3%A9_Rovers" title="wikipedia:Salé Rovers">pirates</a> of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Sal%C3%A9" title="wikipedia:Republic of Salé">Salé</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morocco" title="wikipedia:Morocco">Morocco</a>. In the 17th century thousands of muslims or former muslims fled from Spain to Salé where they established a republic that engaged in piracy and made raids in Spain and even on the coasts of England where they took members of the popluation with them as slaves.

Myriam Librán-Moreno seems to see parallels between the Corsairs of Umbar and the conquest of Crete by the Arabs in A.D. 824, who established an emirate there with Crete becoming a center of Arab pirates in the Mediterranean that caused hardships for the Byzantine Empire.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

J.R.R. Tolkien wrote on [his son's map of Middle-earth](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth "General Map of Middle-earth") for [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes) that Umbar and the City of the Corsairs are approximately at the latitude of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus" title="wikipedia:Cyprus">Cyprus</a> and that [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) is approximately at the latitude of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenna" title="wikipedia:Ravenna">Ravenna</a>, but more to the east near <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgrade" title="wikipedia:Belgrade">Belgrade</a>. He wrote that these references are so that Pauline Baynes can roughly judge the climate and the fauna and flora for her [map of Middle-earth](/wiki/A_Map_of_Middle-earth "A Map of Middle-earth"). In addition, he wrote in a [letter to Charlotte and Denis Plimmer](/wiki/Letter_294 "Letter 294") that the city of [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) is approximately at the latitude of ancient <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troy" title="wikipedia:Troy">Troy</a>. Cyprus is approximately on the same latidue as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carthage" title="wikipedia:Carthage">Carthage</a>, an ancient city in northern Africa at the Mediterranean Sea and a historic enemy of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire" title="wikipedia:Roman Empire">Roman Empire</a>, and as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangier" title="wikipedia:Tangier">Tangier</a>, an ancient city in northwest Africa at the southern entrance to the Mediterranean Sea.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2006: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Corsairs are from the <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a> faction, and are equipped with knives and fire-bombs.</dd></dl>

<b>2023: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Corsairs_of_Umbar" title="The Lord of the Rings Online: Corsairs of Umbar">The Lord of the Rings Online: Corsairs of Umbar</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Corsairs were led by four brothers who falsely call themselves the Heirs of <a href="/wiki/Castamir" title="Castamir">Castamir</a>. These were Azruthor, Dolgimil, Azgarzôr, and the eldest Balakhôr the Scourge. The player negotiated with a Corsair named Jajax, who ended up siding with the player against the Heirs and their followers.</dd></dl>
