**Cair Andros** was a large river-island in the [Anduin River](/wiki/Anduin "Anduin") that resembled a ship, hence the name.

## Geography {#Geography}

The island itself was long and narrow, like a ship, a little over ten miles in length.<sup id="cite_ref-MapGondor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MapGondor-1">[1]</a></sup> Its northern end of sharp rocks, like a "high prow" of a ship, split the waters of the upstream Anduin in a bubbling foam, and this gave the island its name.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-2">[2]</a></sup> Cair Andros was covered with trees.<sup id="cite_ref-Cormallen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cormallen-3">[3]</a></sup>

The island was located to the east of the land of [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien) and about fifty miles<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> north of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-MapGondor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MapGondor-1">[1]</a></sup> Because of its location it was of strategic importance, and was one of the only practical means for an army to cross the river<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> (the others were the bridge of [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) and, until the formation of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), the [Undeeps](/wiki/Undeeps)). As such, the island housed [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorian) fortifications at various points in history.

## History {#History}

Historians in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) believed that the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain) were the first men who crossed the Anduin in the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). They believed that they came from lands south of Mordor, turned north into Ithilien and probably crossed the Anduin near Cair Andros before settling in the vales of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) and the wooded lands at their northern feet.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Upon the establishment of the realm of Gondor, the island grew in strategic importance. Gondor took steps to fortify the island, too. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1248](/wiki/Third_Age_1248 "Third Age 1248") [Minalcar](/wiki/Minalcar), [regent](/wiki/Regent_of_Gondor "Regent of Gondor") to King [Narmacil I](/wiki/Narmacil_I), defeated the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) and upon his return fortified the west bank of the Anduin up to the [Limlight](/wiki/Limlight),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> which may have included the fortification of Cair Andros.

The island was also manned at the time of King [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher)'s fall<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> in [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

After the granting of [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) to the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510") (who created the realm of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan))<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Cair Andros remained a vulnerable crossing-point for the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) who took Cair Andros' risk seriously. [Amon Dîn](/wiki/Amon_D%C3%AEn), the first of the [Beacon-hills](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor"), was set up originally to keep watch over any attempt by enemies to cross the Anduin at or near Cair Andros and to warn the citizens of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) with its beacon.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

Centuries later, [Túrin II](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_II) fortified the island again in about the year [2901](/wiki/Third_Age_2901 "Third Age 2901")<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> to protect Anórien.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

On the same day that the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) rode from [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) with the armies that would [besiege Minas Tirith](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor "Siege of Gondor"), Sauron released a smaller force (more than 6,000 Orcs and Men) from the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon). They overwhelmed the Gondorian defences of Cair Andros on [10 March](/wiki/10_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") and used the island to cross into Anórien.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> This force blocked the eastward progress of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) as they rode to Gondor's aid.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

However, the Rohirrim used the secret ride down [Stonewain Valley](/wiki/Stonewain_Valley)<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> and, after the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), three thousand riders of the Rohirrim under the command of [Elfhelm](/wiki/Elfhelm)<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> chased that force down and pushed them back out of Anórien so that it fled towards Cair Andros.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> Apparently the island was held by that force for some days.<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-19">[19]</a></sup>

On [23 March](/wiki/23_March),<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> in his march on Mordor, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) gave a leave to one thousand scared young men from Rohan and [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach) from his main army, with orders to retake the island if necessary.<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-19">[19]</a></sup> They must have succeeded, because many ships lay by its shores, as glimpsed by [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") from the [Field of Cormallen](/wiki/Field_of_Cormallen) on [8 April](/wiki/8_April).<sup id="cite_ref-Cormallen_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cormallen-3">[3]</a></sup> After many days of camping in the Field of Cormallen, the Captains of the West went aboard the ship with all their men and sailed from Cair Andros down Anduin to Osgiliath.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Cair Andros* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Ship of Long Foam",<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-2">[2]</a></sup> from *[cair](/wiki/Cirya "Cirya")* ("ship") + *[and](/wiki/And "And")* ("long") + *[ros](/wiki/Ros "Ros")* ("foam").<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup>
