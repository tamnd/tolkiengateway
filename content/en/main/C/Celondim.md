**Celondim** is a city in the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) in the game *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online).*

Here [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") players are dropped once they have completed the tutorial.

## History {#History}

Celondim is city located at the Sout of the Blue Mountains along the river [Lune](/wiki/Lune). Originally, it was a port to ferry wood to [Mithlond](/wiki/Mithlond) to build ships, but now there is one of the bigger cities in the area. [Duillond](/wiki/Duillond), to the north, is smaller and it is founded by people of [Edhelion](/wiki/Edhelion), because after [Skorgrím](/wiki/Skorgr%C3%ADm) destroyed their city they relocated to [Gondamon](/wiki/Gondamon), which they have left for Duillond to dwell nearer to the [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) and the Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Celondim means "sad stream flowing down from heights", because [celon](/wiki/Celon#Etymology "Celon") means the "stream flowing down from heights"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and [dim](/wiki/Dim "Dim") means "sad".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
