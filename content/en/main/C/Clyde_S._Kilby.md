**Dr. Clyde Samuel Kilby** ([September 26](../_/26_September.md), [1902](../_/1902.md) - [October 18](../_/18_October.md), [1986](../_/1986.md)) was an American author and professor. Kilby was the founder of the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md), and an influential scholar in [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md) studies. In his honour, the Clyde S. Kilby Award for Inkling Studies was issued (one notable winner is [Colin Duriez](Colin_Duriez.md)), and also the Clyde S. Kilby Reasearch Grant ([Diana Pavlac Glyer](../D/Diana_Pavlac_Glyer.md) is a recipient).
