**Dr. Clyde Samuel Kilby** ([September 26](/wiki/26_September "26 September"), [1902](/wiki/1902) - [October 18](/wiki/18_October "18 October"), [1986](/wiki/1986)) was an American author and professor. Kilby was the founder of the [Marion E. Wade Center](/wiki/Marion_E._Wade_Center), and an influential scholar in [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") studies. In his honour, the Clyde S. Kilby Award for Inkling Studies was issued (one notable winner is [Colin Duriez](/wiki/Colin_Duriez)), and also the Clyde S. Kilby Reasearch Grant ([Diana Pavlac Glyer](/wiki/Diana_Pavlac_Glyer) is a recipient).
