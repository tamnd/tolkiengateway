**Calimehtar** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") "Light-warrior", pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌkaliˈmeçtar]</span> or in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌkaliˈmeʃtar]</span>) can refer to two [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians):

- [Calimehtar](</wiki/Calimehtar_(son_of_Calmacil)> "Calimehtar (son of Calmacil)") (born after [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1126](/wiki/Third_Age_1126 "Third Age 1126")), younger brother of [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") [Rómendacil II](/wiki/R%C3%B3mendacil_II), and grandfather of [Castamir the Usurper](/wiki/Castamir "Castamir").
- [Calimehtar](</wiki/Calimehtar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Calimehtar (King of Gondor)") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1736](/wiki/Third_Age_1736 "Third Age 1736") - [1936](/wiki/Third_Age_1936 "Third Age 1936")), thirtieth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) who defeated the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders).
