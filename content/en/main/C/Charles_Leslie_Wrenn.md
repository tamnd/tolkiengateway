**Charles Leslie Wrenn** ([1895](/wiki/1895)–[1969](/wiki/1969)) was a fellow [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). He became [Rawlinson and Bosworth Professor of Anglo-Saxon](/wiki/Rawlinson_and_Bosworth_Professor_of_Anglo-Saxon) at the [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford) in [1945](/wiki/1945), the successor in the chair to J.R.R. Tolkien, a post he held until 1963.
