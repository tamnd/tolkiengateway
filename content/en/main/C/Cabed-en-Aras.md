**Cabed Naeramarth**, originally known as **Cabed-en-Aras**, was a deep, narrow gorge near [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) through which the river [Taeglin](/wiki/Taeglin) ran.

## History {#History}

Its name was given when once a deer leaped across to escape from some huntsmen. It was just upriver or north of the inflow of the [Celebros](/wiki/Celebros) into Taeglin. On its eastern side was a sheer cliff some forty feet tall with a crown of trees. The western side was less sheer and lower, with hanging trees and bushes. The waters of the Taeglin rushed through the narrow defile, which was filled with rocks that a person might ford, at great peril.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Nienor%27s_Despair.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/0/0b/Ted_Nasmith_-_Nienor%27s_Despair.jpg/150px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Nienor%27s_Despair.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="150" height="231" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/0/0b/Ted_Nasmith_-_Nienor%27s_Despair.jpg/225px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Nienor%27s_Despair.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/0/0b/Ted_Nasmith_-_Nienor%27s_Despair.jpg 2x" data-file-width="292" data-file-height="450"/></a><figcaption><i>Nienor&#39;s Despair</i> by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

[Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) led [Dorlas](/wiki/Dorlas) and [Hunthor](/wiki/Hunthor) to the gorge with the intent of attacking the dragon [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) as he straddled the gap. Dorlas led the party down to the water on the east side, but could not find the courage to cross the fearsome waters. Túrin and Hunthor pressed on, ascending the western side to make their attack. The heat and stench of Glaurung nearly overcame Túrin, who would have fallen into the river had Hunthor not steadied him. However, a stone dislodged by the passing of the dragon smote Hunthor's head and he was lost in the waters. Túrin came up under Glaurung and plunged his sword [Gurthang](/wiki/Gurthang) into the pale belly of the beast.

Later Túrin and Glaurung, both seemingly dead, were found by [Nienor](/wiki/Nienor). Nienor dressed Túrin's wound (his hand had been blackened by Glaurung's blood) and cried out for him to come back to her. Her cry aroused the dragon one last time, who revealed that Túrin, her husband, was also her brother. In horror, Nienor ran to the brink of the Cabed-en-Aras and cast herself into the ravine, which was later named *Cabed Naeramarth*, the Leap of Dreadful Doom. The spot was later considered haunted, and even animals did not come there.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Cabed-en-Aras* is translated from "Leap of the Deer" from *[cabed](/wiki/Cabed "Cabed")* ("leap") + *[en](/wiki/En "En")* ("of") + *[aras](/wiki/Aras "Aras")* ("deer").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

*Cabed Naeramarth*, meaning "Leap of Dreadful Doom"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> from *[amarth](/wiki/Amarth "Amarth")* ("doom").
