**Charlie Vickers** is an Australian actor. He portrayed [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in the [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) television series *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He was unaware about the character’s true identity until the filming of the [third episode](</wiki/Adar_(episode)> "Adar (episode)").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
