The **cats of Queen [Berúthiel](/wiki/Ber%C3%BAthiel)** were a group of [Cats](/wiki/Cats) that the [Queen](/wiki/Queen_of_Gondor "Queen of Gondor") to [Tarannon Falastur](/wiki/Tarannon_Falastur) kept as slaves.

## History {#History}

In the late ninth and possibly early tenth century of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), [Berúthiel](/wiki/Ber%C3%BAthiel), the wife of the twelfth [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor), loathed cats, though they became attracted to her for precisely that reason. They followed her around, and eventually she took advantage of their company by enslaving and torturing them for amusement.<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-1">[1]</a></sup> Of the [Cats](/wiki/Cats) that she kept<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-1">[1]</a></sup> as her slaves: there were ten of them: nine black and one white.<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-2">[2]</a></sup> Berúthiel trained the cats to go out on evil errands throughout the night to spy on her enemies or to terrify them,<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-1">[1]</a></sup> in order to discover the dark secrets of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), so that she knew the things that men wished the most to keep hidden.<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-2">[2]</a></sup> She sent the white cat to spy on the black ones to torment them. Berúthiel was able to converse with them or read their memories. Her cats were infamous among the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians). All were afraid of them, did not dare to touch them, and cursed whenever they saw them.<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-2">[2]</a></sup>

Somehow, the childlessness of Tarannon was associated with Berúthiel's cats, though the way in which the cats had affected Tarannon was unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Istari_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-2">[2]</a></sup>

Eventually, Tarannon exiled Berúthiel and her cats from Gondor and they were set adrift at sea before a north wind on a ship alone that "*was last seen flying past Umbar under a sickle moon, with a cat at the masthead and another as a figure-head on the prow*"<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-2">[2]</a></sup> and Berúthiel went back to live in the inland city,<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-1">[1]</a></sup> likely still with her cats.

## Legacy {#Legacy}

Despite their erasure from the [Book of the Kings](/wiki/Book_of_the_Kings), the cats of Queen Berúthiel were so notorious that they were held in the memory of [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) for centuries; [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) alluded to them more than 2,000 years after Berúthiel's exile.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier version of what would later become the chapter [A Journey in the Dark](/wiki/A_Journey_in_the_Dark), it was the narrator rather than Aragorn II who mentioned the cats of Queen Berúthiel. This first version of the sentence referred simply to cats in general with no reference to Berúthiel. The second version of the sentence referred to "<i>the <b>cat of Benish Armon</b>,</i>" before the reference was changed to "<i>the <b>cats of Queen Tamar</b>,</i>" with a few slight revisions regarding Berúthiel's name.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<dl><dd>While the cats of Queen Berúthiel do not make direct appearances, there is a type of enemy that the player faces called <b>Spying Cats</b>. They appear during the undead <a href="/wiki/Tarannon_Falastur" title="Tarannon Falastur">Tarannon Falastur</a> fight in the Silent Street instance. Should the player attack Tarannon, the cats give off the &#34;Watchful Spies&#34; effect which reflects 50% of the damage back onto the player.</dd></dl>
