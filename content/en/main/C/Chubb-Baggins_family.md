The **Chubb-Baggins family** was a family created by the wedding of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' uncle [Bingo Baggins](/wiki/Bingo_Baggins) to [Chica Chubb](/wiki/Chica_Chubb). Double surnames were not uncommon in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), and were used when the daughter of the head of the family married. This way, the family name would be preserved.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Genealogy {#Genealogy}

The Chubb-Bagginses were part of the [Baggins family](/wiki/Baggins_family).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The figures after the names are those of birth (and death where that is recorded). Names in *italics* signify those who attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party) on [22 September](/wiki/22_September) [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001").
