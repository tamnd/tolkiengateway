The **Chubb-Baggins family** was a family created by the wedding of [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)' uncle [Bingo Baggins](../B/Bingo_Baggins.md) to [Chica Chubb](Chica_Chubb.md). Double surnames were not uncommon in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), and were used when the daughter of the head of the family married. This way, the family name would be preserved.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Genealogy {#Genealogy}

The Chubb-Bagginses were part of the [Baggins family](../B/Baggins_family.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The figures after the names are those of birth (and death where that is recorded). Names in *italics* signify those who attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md) on [22 September](../_/22_September.md) [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1401](../T/Third_Age_3001.md).
