**Carl "Nibs" Cotton** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and the youngest child of [Tolman (Farmer) Cotton](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior").

## History {#History}

Born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1389](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Carl and his siblings spent much time in [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater), and would often swim in the [Bywater Pool](/wiki/Bywater_Pool) with the Gamgee children.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He had not yet come of age when [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey)'s [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) entered the Shire, so his father ordered him to guard the house with a pitch fork while his father and brothers fought in the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Carl was probably named after his ancestor, [Carl, son of Cottar](</wiki/Carl_(son_of_Cottar)> "Carl (son of Cottar)"). The nickname "Nibs" has been a term of mock respect in historical English.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
