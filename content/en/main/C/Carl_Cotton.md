**Carl "Nibs" Cotton** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) and the youngest child of [Tolman (Farmer) Cotton](../T/Tolman_Cotton_Senior.md).

## History {#History}

Born in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1389](../T/Third_Age_2989.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Carl and his siblings spent much time in [Bywater](../B/Bywater.md), and would often swim in the [Bywater Pool](../B/Bywater_Pool.md) with the Gamgee children.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He had not yet come of age when [Sharkey](../S/Sharkey.md)'s [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) entered the Shire, so his father ordered him to guard the house with a pitch fork while his father and brothers fought in the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Carl was probably named after his ancestor, [Carl, son of Cottar](<Carl_(son_of_Cottar).md>). The nickname "Nibs" has been a term of mock respect in historical English.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
