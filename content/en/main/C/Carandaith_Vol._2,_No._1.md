<b><i>Carandaith</i> Vol. 2, No. 1</b> is an issue of the magazine [Carandaith](/wiki/Carandaith), published by the [Australian Tolkien Society](/wiki/Australian_Tolkien_Society). A letter from [Joy Hill](/wiki/Joy_Hill) (of [George Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin "Allen and Unwin")) includes an extract from [a letter sent by Tolkien](</wiki/Letter_to_Joy_Hill_(June_1969)> "Letter to Joy Hill (June 1969)") discussing a film adaptation of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* (cf. p. 67).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <br>
