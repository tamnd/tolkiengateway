<b><i>Carandaith</i> Vol. 2, No. 1</b> is an issue of the magazine [Carandaith](Carandaith.md), published by the [Australian Tolkien Society](../A/Australian_Tolkien_Society.md). A letter from [Joy Hill](../J/Joy_Hill.md) (of [George Allen &amp; Unwin](../A/Allen_and_Unwin.md)) includes an extract from [a letter sent by Tolkien](<../L/Letter_to_Joy_Hill_(June_1969).md>) discussing a film adaptation of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* (cf. p. 67).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <br>
