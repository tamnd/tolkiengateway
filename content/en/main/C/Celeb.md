<i><b>celeb</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "[silver](/wiki/Silver "Silver")".<sup id="cite_ref-L347_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SApp_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the early pre-cursors of Sindarin (the languages [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) and [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish)), *celeb* also signified "silver".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Other forms of the word include Noldorin *celebren* ("of silver"<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup> or "like silver"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>) and Sindarin *celebrin* ("like silver").<sup id="cite_ref-SApp_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-3">[3]</a></sup> [Didier Willis](/wiki/Didier_Willis) has suggested that the Noldorin form *celevon* means "of silver".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
