<i><b>Christie&#39;s Fine Printed Books and Manuscripts 19 June 2012</b></i> is an auction catalogue from Christie's. The catalogue includes the following sales of letters by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien):

- Lot 252: letter from Tolkien to Elena Jeronimides (see [Letter to Elena Jeronimides](/wiki/Letter_to_Elena_Jeronimides)).
- Lot 251: letter from Tolkien to Roger Verhulst (see [Letter to Roger Verhulst (9 March 1966)](</wiki/Letter_to_Roger_Verhulst_(9_March_1966)>)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
