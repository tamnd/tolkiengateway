**Carrots** was a character in an unfinished and unpublished children's<sup id="cite_ref-chronology_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology-1">[1]</a></sup> tale by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-2">[2]</a></sup>

When the Tolkien family had moved in [1924](/wiki/1924)<sup id="cite_ref-chronology_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology-1">[1]</a></sup> to 2 Darnley Road<sup id="cite_ref-chronology_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology-1">[1]</a></sup> in Leeds, [John Tolkien](/wiki/John_Tolkien) recalled that he often had difficulty falling asleep and his father would come to his bedside and tell stories to him. One of these tales was about a boy named Carrots, who had red hair and climbed "into a cuckoo clock" to go off on a series of strange adventures.<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-2">[2]</a></sup>
