<i><b>Creating Gollum</b></i> is a collectible DVD and book set containing information on how [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) was created and portrayed for [The Lord of the Rings Film Series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").

## History {#History}

*Creating Gollum* was released on [1 January](/wiki/1_January) [2003](/wiki/2003) by [New Line Home Entertainment](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema "New Line Cinema"). The Disc is titled *Capturing Movie Memories: The Gollum "Sméagol" Collectible.* A <a class="external text" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w_Z7YUyCEGE" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">digital portrayal</a> of the DVD was posted on YouTube by "nature video" on [11 December](/wiki/11_December) [2013](/wiki/2013).
