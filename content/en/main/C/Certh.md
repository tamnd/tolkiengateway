**Certh** is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for *[rune](/wiki/Runes "Runes")*. The plural is *[cirth](/wiki/Cirth "Cirth")*.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Old Sindarin](/wiki/Old_Sindarin) \**kirta*, root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=KIR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KIR (page does not exist)">KIR</a> "cut"

## See also {#See_also}

Words derived from *certh* include *[certhas](/wiki/Certhas "Certhas")*, "runic alphabet".
