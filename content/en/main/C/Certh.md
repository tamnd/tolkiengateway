**Certh** is the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word for *[rune](../R/Runes.md)*. The plural is *[cirth](Cirth.md)*.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Old Sindarin](../O/Old_Sindarin.md) \**kirta*, root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=KIR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KIR (page does not exist)">KIR</a> "cut"

## See also {#See_also}

Words derived from *certh* include *[certhas](Certhas.md)*, "runic alphabet".
