**Culumalda** was a tree with hanging yellow blossoms that grew in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien), and especially in the woods around the [Field of Cormallen](/wiki/Field_of_Cormallen).<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SApp_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Culumalda* was intended to be the source of the name *[Cormallen](/wiki/Cormallen#Etymology)*.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SApp_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) connects *culumalda* to the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) stems *[cul-](/wiki/KUL "KUL")* ("golden-red") and *[mal-](/wiki/Mal- "Mal-")* ("gold").<sup id="cite_ref-SApp_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested that *culumalda* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), consisting of *[culuma](/wiki/Kuluma "Kuluma")* ("orange") + *[alda](/wiki/Alda "Alda")* ("tree").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") indicated that the tree was "prob\[ably\] a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/laburnum" title="wikipedia:laburnum">laburnum</a>".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup>
