**Cresses** were a type of plant, that grew along streams in [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The specific cress encountered in Rohan was likely watercress.<sup id="cite_ref-flora_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-flora-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1984-1997: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i></b>

<dl><dd><i><b>Golden Cress</b></i> (<a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Melbis</i>, <a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Malquessi</i>), also known as <i>Gold Chute</i>, was a fernlike riverside herb, found almost exclusively in the Vale of <a href="/wiki/Erech" title="Erech">Erech</a> and <a href="/wiki/Blackroot_Vale" title="Blackroot Vale">Blackroot Vale</a>. Prepared into ointment, it helped heal bleeding wounds. <sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>
