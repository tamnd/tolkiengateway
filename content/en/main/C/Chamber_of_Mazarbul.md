The **Chamber of Mazarbul** was the old chamber of records of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"). Probably built during the earlier years of Khazad-dûm, it was later used as a base by [Balin](/wiki/Balin) when he began his ill-fated attempt at recolonization in the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

*[Mazarbul](</wiki/Mazarbul_(word)> "Mazarbul (word)")* means "records" in [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> So the "Chamber of Mazarbul" means the "Chamber of Records". This name is similar to the [Book of Records](/wiki/Book_of_Mazarbul "Book of Mazarbul") found in that chamber.

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Angus_McBride_-_Moria.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/9/9f/Angus_McBride_-_Moria.jpg/300px-Angus_McBride_-_Moria.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="354" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/9/9f/Angus_McBride_-_Moria.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="424" data-file-height="500"/></a><figcaption><i>Moria</i> by <a href="/wiki/Angus_McBride" title="Angus McBride">Angus McBride</a></figcaption></figure>

The chamber itself was probably built at around the same time as the [Twenty-first Hall](/wiki/Twenty-first_Hall) in the Seventh Level. This would have been slightly later than the earliest Dwarven delvings, which were to be found in the lower levels near the [Great Gates](/wiki/Great_Gates).

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2989](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989"), Balin led a group of Dwarves to recolonize [Moria](/wiki/Moria). Balin chose the Twenty-first Hall as his headquarters and set up his seat in the Chamber of Mazarbul. When Balin was killed a few years later, he was laid to rest in a [tomb](/wiki/Balin%27s_Tomb "Balin's Tomb") inside the chamber. It was there where the remnants of the colony made their last stand, and perished.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-3">[3]</a></sup>

The [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") found the chamber thirty years after Balin came to Moria and it was here that Gandalf found the [Book of Mazarbul](/wiki/Book_of_Mazarbul), a record of Balin's recolonization efforts. While still in the chamber, the Fellowship was attacked by a band of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). After a [brief fight](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Chamber_of_Mazarbul "Battle of the Chamber of Mazarbul"), they retreated through the east door. [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) attempted to guard the door against the [Balrog](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane "Durin's Bane"), until the roof of the Chamber collapsed.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Geography and appearance {#Geography_and_appearance}

The chamber was located to the right of a pathway that branched off the north end of the Twenty-first hall. When the Fellowship found the chamber as they passed through Moria, [Balin's Tomb](/wiki/Balin%27s_Tomb) was located inside it, and a bright shaft of sunlight streamed in from outside the mountain to land directly on the tomb.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-2">[2]</a></sup>

The chamber was large, square, and dimly lit.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-2">[2]</a></sup> There were two stone doors leading into it (one entrance from the Twenty-first Hall, one from the stair tunnels that the Fellowship later used to flee the Balrog). Many deep recesses were cut into the chamber rock containing chests that had been looted by the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) inhabiting Moria. A deep dust had fallen upon the entire room by the time the Fellowship entered it, and the remains of a long-abandoned battle site were to be seen strewn across the floor.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-3">[3]</a></sup>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In Peter Jackson&#39;s The Fellowship of the Ring (<a href="/wiki/Balin%27s_Tomb_(scene)" title="Balin&#39;s Tomb (scene)">Scene 35: Balin&#39;s Tomb</a> and <a href="/wiki/The_Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm_(scene)" title="The Bridge of Khazad-dûm (scene)">Scene 36: The Bridge of Khazad-dûm</a>), the Chamber of Mazarbul is presented much as it is described in the books, with a few notable exceptions.
<ul><li>It is placed in the center of the Twenty-first Hall, rather than on the right of a corridor running off the hall to the north.</li>
<li>There is only one proper door explicitly shown leading into the chamber, rather than the two described in the book. This door is also made of wood in the film, rather than stone. There is, however, one smashed doorway out of the back of the chamber, with the window illuminating Balin&#39;s tomb above it, which the Fellowship flee out of.</li>
<li>A well is also introduced into the layout, directly below the shaft of light. This well was found in the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Well-chamber&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Well-chamber (page does not exist)">Well-chamber</a><sup id="cite_ref-chronology_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology-4">[4]</a></sup> of the crossroads in the book, but was transplanted here to serve greater dramatic purpose.</li></ul></dd></dl>
