<i><b>Christie&#39;s 20th Century Books and Manuscripts 2 December 2003</b></i> is an auction catalogue from Christie's. It includes the following Tolkien-related lots:<sup id="cite_ref-TB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup>

- Lot 36 (p. 24): - A letter to [Jennifer Brookes-Smith (26 September 1947)](</wiki/Letter_to_Jennifer_Brookes-Smith_(26_September_1947)> "Letter to Jennifer Brookes-Smith (26 September 1947)"). One page of the letter is reproduced in a photograph.
- Lot 37 (p. 25): A letter to [Jennifer Brookes-Smith (28 July 1955)](</wiki/Letter_to_Jennifer_Brookes-Smith_(28_July_1955)> "Letter to Jennifer Brookes-Smith (28 July 1955)").
- Lot 38 (pp. 25–6): A letter to [Jane Neave (15 November 1961)](</wiki/Letter_to_Jane_Neave_(15_November_1961)> "Letter to Jane Neave (15 November 1961)"). The first page of the letter is reproduced in a photograph.
- Lot 39 (p. 26): This lot consists of a typed copy of the poem [Princess Mee](/wiki/Little_Princess_Mee "Little Princess Mee"), together with [Letter 234](/wiki/Letter_234).
