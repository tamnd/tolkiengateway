**Colin John Rudd** is a British musician and singer who has released numerous songs of poems from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s works. He has performed at Oxford University for [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) and currently has a collection of 9 songs relating to Tolkien, published in [2001](/wiki/2001) as <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Songs_of_JRR_Tolkien&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Songs of JRR Tolkien (page does not exist)">Songs of JRR Tolkien</a></i>. Since then he has published 28 Tolkien related songs on his YouTube channel; full list below.
