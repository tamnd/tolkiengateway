**Caras Gaer** was "a strange shrine"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> that acted as the [Dark Wizard](/wiki/Dark_Wizard)'s stronghold in western [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn) in the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Caras Gaer was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios). It appeared in the second season of the series.

## History {#History}

Caras Gaer served as the staging ground for the Dark Wizard's schemes in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). From here he ordered the [Gaudrim](/wiki/Gaudrim) and [Mystics](/wiki/Mystics) to intercept and capture [The Stranger](/wiki/The_Stranger).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Caras Gaer* consists of the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) words *caras* ("a fort or dwelling surrounded by bulwarks")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and gaer ("awful, fearful").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
