The **Cross-roads of the Fallen King** was the point in the land of [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) where the north-south [Harad Road](../H/Harad_Road.md) was crossed by the east-west road from [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) to [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md) (later [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Originally maintained by the [Men of Gondor](../G/Gondorians.md), who grew tall trees and built a statue of a [king](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md), the Cross-roads had effectively fallen under the control of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) at the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) had befouled the statue; its head had been knocked off and replaced by a stone effigy.

On their way to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md), [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), and [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) came to the Cross-roads around [10 March](../_/10_March.md). They were in a belt of trees of vast size. Next to the road leading to [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) they found the defiled statue, but seeing its fallen head gave Frodo hope, for nature crowned it with flowers.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Sometime later, in the evening of [18 March](../_/18_March.md), a vanguard of [Army of the West](../A/Army_of_the_West.md) passed by the Cross-roads. [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) ordered a fanfare of trumpeteers and ceremoniously reclaimed the lands in the name of the Lords of Gondor. Soldiers broke the defiling stone-head and restored the statue also washing the scrawls away.

The [next day](../M/March_19.md) they were joined by the main Host. Before leaving, they stationed a strong force of archers there, to guard against any attack from the East or South, and resumed their course towards [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inconsistencies {#Inconsistencies}

The statue of the Fallen King is said to have a carven beard in the text.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> However, in a note published in *[The Nature of Middle-earth](../T/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth.md)*, Tolkien stated that the kingly line of Númenor (and subsequently Gondor and Arnor) would be unable to grow beards due to their Elvish ancestry.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
