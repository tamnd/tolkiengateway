The **Cross-roads of the Fallen King** was the point in the land of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) where the north-south [Harad Road](/wiki/Harad_Road) was crossed by the east-west road from [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) to [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil) (later [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Originally maintained by the [Men of Gondor](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians"), who grew tall trees and built a statue of a [king](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor"), the Cross-roads had effectively fallen under the control of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) at the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) had befouled the statue; its head had been knocked off and replaced by a stone effigy.

On their way to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) came to the Cross-roads around [10 March](/wiki/10_March). They were in a belt of trees of vast size. Next to the road leading to [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) they found the defiled statue, but seeing its fallen head gave Frodo hope, for nature crowned it with flowers.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Sometime later, in the evening of [18 March](/wiki/18_March), a vanguard of [Army of the West](/wiki/Army_of_the_West) passed by the Cross-roads. [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) ordered a fanfare of trumpeteers and ceremoniously reclaimed the lands in the name of the Lords of Gondor. Soldiers broke the defiling stone-head and restored the statue also washing the scrawls away.

The [next day](/wiki/March_19 "March 19") they were joined by the main Host. Before leaving, they stationed a strong force of archers there, to guard against any attack from the East or South, and resumed their course towards [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inconsistencies {#Inconsistencies}

The statue of the Fallen King is said to have a carven beard in the text.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> However, in a note published in *[The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth)*, Tolkien stated that the kingly line of Númenor (and subsequently Gondor and Arnor) would be unable to grow beards due to their Elvish ancestry.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
