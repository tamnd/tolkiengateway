<p><br/>
<b>Cuiviénen</b> was a land on the shores of the inland <a href="/wiki/Sea_of_Helcar" title="Sea of Helcar">Sea of Helcar</a> in the far east of <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a> where the first <a href="/wiki/Elves" title="Elves">Elves</a> <a href="/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves" title="Awakening of the Elves">awoke</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
<!-- -->
<!-- -->
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar" title="Ilúvatar">Ilúvatar</a> <a href="/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves" title="Awakening of the Elves">awoke</a> the first <a href="/wiki/Elves" title="Elves">Elves</a>, the <i><a href="/wiki/Quendi" title="Quendi">Quendi</a></i> as they came to call themselves, by Cuiviénen in <a href="/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1050" title="Year of the Trees 1050">1050th</a> <a href="/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees" title="Years of the Trees">Year of the Trees</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and thus the <a href="/wiki/First_Age" title="First Age">First Age</a> at last began.<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> From the first they were divided into three groups: the <a href="/wiki/Minyar" title="Minyar">Minyar</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tatyar" title="Tatyar">Tatyar</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Nelyar" title="Nelyar">Nelyar</a>. They dwelt in Cuiviénen for more than fifty <a href="/wiki/Valian_Years" title="Valian Years">Valian Years</a> before the first <a href="/wiki/Sundering_of_the_Elves" title="Sundering of the Elves">sundering</a>.  Many of the Elves, particularly of the Minyar and Tatyar, journeyed west to <a href="/wiki/Valinor" title="Valinor">Valinor</a> and, if they did not tarry in Middle-earth, saw its light and became known as the <a href="/wiki/Eldar" title="Eldar">Eldar</a>. Those who remained were called the <a href="/wiki/Avari" title="Avari">Avari</a>, the unwilling, for they did not desire to see the beauty of that land, but preferred the starlight of Cuiviénen. After the <a href="/wiki/War_of_Wrath" title="War of Wrath">War of Wrath</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Changing_of_the_World" title="Changing of the World">Changing of the World</a>, Cuiviénen was destroyed and could no longer be sought out by the Eldar.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Geography">Geography</span></h2>
<p>Cuiviénen lay on the shores of a bay on the inland <a href="/wiki/Sea_of_Helcar" title="Sea of Helcar">Sea of Helcar</a>&#39;s eastern end, at the foot of <a href="/wiki/Orocarni" title="Orocarni">Orocarni</a> near the <a href="/wiki/Wild_Wood" title="Wild Wood">Wild Wood</a>. Located in the central regions of Middle-earth, Cuiviénen was far to the west of where the first Men later awoke in <a href="/wiki/Hild%C3%B3rien" title="Hildórien">Hildórien</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> It was approximately 2,000 miles east of <a href="/wiki/Beleriand" title="Beleriand">Beleriand</a>&#39;s shoreline with <a href="/wiki/Belegaer" title="Belegaer">Belegaer</a> at <a href="/wiki/Eglarest" title="Eglarest">Eglarest</a>, as the crow flies, and it was about a 450 mile march east or southeast of the inland <a href="/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn" title="Sea of Rhûn">Sea of Rhûn</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> On the east side of the lake near which the Elves woke was a great cliff from which a waterfall came down. Near the lake was a green valley, a second larger valley, a third deeper and wider valley, a grove of birches by a stream and a firwood on a hill-side.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
</p>

<p>The <a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Quenya</a> name <i>Cuiviénen</i> means &#34;Water of Awakening&#34;,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> from <i><a href="/wiki/Cuivi%C3%AB" title="Cuivië">cuivië</a></i> (&#34;awakening&#34;) + <i><a href="/wiki/Nen" title="Nen">nen</a></i> (&#34;water&#34;).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Noldorin" title="Noldorin">Noldorin</a>/<a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">Sindarin</a> cognate was <i><b>Nen Echui</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
</p>
