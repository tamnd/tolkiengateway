The **Chieftain of the Dúnedain**, also known as the **Chieftain of the North**, was the hereditary title of the rulers of the [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North).

## History {#History}

The title was created following the final destruction of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1974](/wiki/Third_Age_1974 "Third Age 1974") by the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar). The heir to the throne of Arthedain, [Aranarth](/wiki/Aranarth) son of [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui), in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1976](/wiki/Third_Age_1976 "Third Age 1976") chose not to claim the kingship and instead ruled the remnants of his people as Chieftain.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The Chieftains were raised in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), where the heirlooms of the [House of Isildur](/wiki/House_of_Isildur), were also kept.

They were descendants of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) through the kings of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) through [Fíriel](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel);<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> as such they regarded themselves as the legitimate heirs to both [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). All the Chieftains took a name in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The Chieftains continued to take the kingly prefix of *[aran](/wiki/Aran "Aran")* or *[ar(a)-](/wiki/Ara "Ara")* ("high king"), to signify their royal heritage and their right to rule all the former lands of Arnor.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> When the line of [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) failed, the Chieftains considered themselves the heirs of Anárion through their ancestor [Fíriel](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel); none of the Chieftains ever forgot Arvedui's claim to the throne.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-8">[8]</a></sup>

The people of Arthedain became known as the [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North), a wandering people quite content with living in secret. Due to the destruction of Angmar, and the [Watchful Peace](/wiki/Watchful_Peace), which followed after the [Wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards") [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) drove [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) out of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur), there was little evil in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) and attacks by the enemy were few and far between.

It is notable that most of the Chieftains lived out their full life-span and their longevity did not decrease as much as that of the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

[Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") became the last Chieftain when he reclaimed the title of [High King of the Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "High King of the Dúnedain").
