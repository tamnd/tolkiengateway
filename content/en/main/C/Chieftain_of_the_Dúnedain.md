The **Chieftain of the Dúnedain**, also known as the **Chieftain of the North**, was the hereditary title of the rulers of the [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md).

## History {#History}

The title was created following the final destruction of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1974](../T/Third_Age_1974.md) by the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md). The heir to the throne of Arthedain, [Aranarth](../A/Aranarth.md) son of [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md), in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1976](../T/Third_Age_1976.md) chose not to claim the kingship and instead ruled the remnants of his people as Chieftain.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The Chieftains were raised in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), where the heirlooms of the [House of Isildur](../H/House_of_Isildur.md), were also kept.

They were descendants of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) through the kings of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md) through [Fíriel](../F/Fíriel.md);<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> as such they regarded themselves as the legitimate heirs to both [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md). All the Chieftains took a name in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The Chieftains continued to take the kingly prefix of *[aran](../A/Aran.md)* or *[ar(a)-](../A/Ara.md)* ("high king"), to signify their royal heritage and their right to rule all the former lands of Arnor.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> When the line of [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md) failed, the Chieftains considered themselves the heirs of Anárion through their ancestor [Fíriel](../F/Fíriel.md); none of the Chieftains ever forgot Arvedui's claim to the throne.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-8">[8]</a></sup>

The people of Arthedain became known as the [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md), a wandering people quite content with living in secret. Due to the destruction of Angmar, and the [Watchful Peace](../W/Watchful_Peace.md), which followed after the [Wizard](../W/Wizards.md) [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) drove [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) out of [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md), there was little evil in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and attacks by the enemy were few and far between.

It is notable that most of the Chieftains lived out their full life-span and their longevity did not decrease as much as that of the [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

[Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn.md) became the last Chieftain when he reclaimed the title of [High King of the Reunited Kingdom](../H/High_King_of_the_Dúnedain.md).
