**Calenhir** was a river in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) that flowed east from the [Pinnath Gelin](/wiki/Pinnath_Gelin) into the [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond). On the "working map" that [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) used while writing *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* the river was drawn on the map but only named in the list of the "Rivers of Gondor".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This river was removed from the maps of the published *Lord of the Rings*<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Its name seems to mean "Green River" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), from *[calen](/wiki/Calen "Calen")* ("green") + [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") *[sîr](/wiki/S%C3%AEr "Sîr")* ("river").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Calenhir belongs to the region of <a href="/wiki/Anfalas" title="Anfalas">Anfalas</a>, the in-game boundaries of <a href="/wiki/Pinnath_Gelin" title="Pinnath Gelin">Pinnath Gelin</a> beginning just slightly north of its origin.</dd></dl>
