- **[Canto I](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_I "Lay of Leithian Canto I")** - concerning [Elu Thingol](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") and [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath)
- **[Canto II](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_II "Lay of Leithian Canto II")** - concerning [Barahir the Bold](/wiki/Barahir "Barahir"), the treachery of [Gorlim](/wiki/Gorlim), his death, and [Beren Erchamion](/wiki/Beren "Beren")'s vow for revenge
- **[Canto III](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_III "Lay of Leithian Canto III")** - concerning the meeting of Beren and [Lúthien Tinúviel](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien "Lúthien")
- **[Canto IV](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_IV "Lay of Leithian Canto IV")** - concerning Beren's capture and the bride-price of Lúthien as layed down by Thingol: a [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmarils "Silmarils")
- **[Canto V](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_V "Lay of Leithian Canto V")** - concerning Lúthien in Doriath and her escape
- **[Canto VI](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_VI "Lay of Leithian Canto VI")** - concerning Beren in [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), with [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") and the scheming of [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin)
- **[Canto VII](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_VII "Lay of Leithian Canto VII")** - concerning the battle of minds between Finrod and [Thû](/wiki/Th%C3%BB), and the fall of Finrod
- **[Canto VIII](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_VIII "Lay of Leithian Canto VIII")** - concerning Celegorm and Curufin's capture of Lúthien, and her meeting with [Huan](/wiki/Huan)
- **[Canto IX](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_IX "Lay of Leithian Canto IX")** - concerning Huan's battle with Thû, the destruction of [Wizard's Isle](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth "Tol-in-Gaurhoth"), and the freeing of Beren
- **[Canto X](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_X "Lay of Leithian Canto X")** - concerning the second parting of Beren and Lúthien and the battle with Celegorm and Curufin
- **[Canto XI](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_XI "Lay of Leithian Canto XI")** - concerning the reunion of Beren and Lúthien and the approach to [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim)
- **[Canto XII](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_XII "Lay of Leithian Canto XII")** - concerning the battle of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) and the enchanting of [Carcharoth](/wiki/Carcharoth)
- **[Canto XIII](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_XIII "Lay of Leithian Canto XIII")** - concerning Lúthien's dance before Morgoth, his enchantment, and the stealing of the Silmaril
- **[Canto XIV](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian_Canto_XIV "Lay of Leithian Canto XIV")** - concerning the flight of Beren and Lúthien and the rage of Carcharoth
