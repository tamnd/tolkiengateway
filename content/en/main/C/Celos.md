The **Celos** was a lesser river or stream that flowed out of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) into the [Gondorian](../G/Gondorians.md) land of [Lebennin](../L/Lebennin.md), a little to the west of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). It was a tributary of the larger River [Sirith](../S/Sirith.md).

## Location {#Location}

The smaller-scale map that accompanies *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)* shows the Celos flowing into the Sirith from the east,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> while the large-scale contour map of Gondor shows it flowing from the west.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> A brief reference in *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)* suggests that the Celos was the next river eastward from the [Gilrain](../G/Gilrain.md), which would make the large-scale map correct.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup> The mistake was carried over to *[The West of Middle-earth at the End of the Third Age](../T/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age.md)* for the *Unfinished Tales*.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-5">[4]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](Christopher_Tolkien.md) noted the error of the small-scale map in *[The Treason of Isengard](../T/The_Treason_of_Isengard.md)*; the correct location of the Celos is thus being the western tributary.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

Later prints of the *Unfinished Tales* (but not all) corrected the labels.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name *Celos* derives from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [KELU-](../K/KELU.md) ("flow out swiftly") + ending *-sse*, *-ssa*. The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) cognate is *kelussë* ("freshet, water falling out swiftly from a rocky spring").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The river Celos divides <a href="../L/Lebennin.md" title="Lebennin">Lebennin</a> into Lower <a href="../L/Lebennin.md" title="Lebennin">Lebennin</a> located in the region of Central <a href="../G/Gondor.md" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> and Upper Lebennin in the region of Eastern Gondor. The river <a href="../S/Sirith.md" title="Sirith">Sirith</a> joined it to the east, below the <a href="../W/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor.md" title="Warning beacons of Gondor">beacon</a> of &#34;Sirthanc&#34;. The village of &#34;Erynos&#34; stood across from the mouth of the Sirith, on the western bank of the juncture. South of the meeting of the rivers the combined river flowed south through the city of <a href="../P/Pelargir.md" title="Pelargir">Pelargir</a> and into the <a href="../A/Anduin.md" title="Anduin">Anduin</a>. The Celos was the domain of the lighthearted River-maid &#34;Silverfroth&#34;, one of the Five Sisters, though she and her sisters were widely considered a myth by the time of the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>.</dd></dl>
