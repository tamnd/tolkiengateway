**Caras Galadhon** was the city and fortress of the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-FR-II6_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR-II6-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Overview {#Overview}

Caras Galadhon was in the [Naith](/wiki/Naith) of Lothlórien, in the [angle](/wiki/Egladil "Egladil") between the rivers [Celebrant](/wiki/Celebrant) and [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) known as [Egladil](/wiki/Egladil).<sup id="cite_ref-FR-II6_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR-II6-1">[1]</a></sup>

The city was located on a green hill surrounded by a fosse and a wall whose gates lay to the south-west. The inhabitants dwelt in large [flets](/wiki/Telain "Telain") in the trees, reached by white ladders. On the top of the hill, in the greatest of trees, were the house of Celeborn and Galadriel.<sup id="cite_ref-FR-II6_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR-II6-1">[1]</a></sup> In the same tree were the highest point in the land.<sup id="cite_ref-UT-6_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-6-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The city was founded in the second millennium of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) due to the growing threat of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur).<sup id="cite_ref-UT-6_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-6-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) was brought through Lothlórien to Caras Galadhon, and there met the lords of the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim). They stayed in the city for a month, recovering from their journey through [Moria](/wiki/Moria).

In the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), after Galadriel had gone into the [West](/wiki/Straight_Road "Straight Road"), Caras Galadhon was ruled by a lonely Celeborn as lord of Lothlórien which now spanned both sides of the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). Later he left for [Imladris](/wiki/Imladris) as well, and few Galadhrim remained in Lórien; Caras Galadhon became an empty place.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Caras Galadhon* is said to be [Silvan Elvish](/wiki/Silvan_Elvish).<sup id="cite_ref-UT-6_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-6-2">[2]</a></sup> However, the word *[caras](/wiki/Caras "Caras")* is also attested as [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), being adapted from the [native speech](/wiki/Nandorin "Nandorin") of the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor)<sup id="cite_ref-Ngelaidh_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ngelaidh-4">[4]</a></sup>. Tolkien mentions the proper meaning of this word was "a built fort or dwelling surrounded by bulwarks", deriving from the root √CAR "make, build",<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> or a "circular earthwall with dike".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The city would be named <i><b>Caras (i)Ngelelaið</b></i> In pure Sindarin form.<sup id="cite_ref-Ngelaidh_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ngelaidh-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 1]</a></sup>

*Galadhon* can be interpreted as genitive plural "of trees", showing a case ending not existing in Sindarin but known from [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s earliest version of Sindarin, called [Goldogrin](/wiki/Gnomish "Gnomish"), also possessed this ending. In that case, Caras Galadhon means "moated fortress of the trees".

The name could also refer in some way to [Galadhon](/wiki/Galadhon), whom Tolkien conceived to be the father of [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn). In that case, the name would mean \*"Galadhon's fortress".

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first edition of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, Caras Galadhon was misspelled <i><b>Caras Galadon</b></i>.

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Caras Galadhon is the main inhabited area in Lothlórien, where players cannot enter until they prove their friendship to Galadhrim. The map and general layout of the city is replicated from the map in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth" title="The Atlas of Middle-earth">The Atlas of Middle-earth</a></i>. The city hosts most major gameplay facilities and is composed of a large number of flets on different levels, connected by multiple ladders and staircases. It is rather hard to navigate and thus several quests consist solely of directing the player to various point of interest, then challenging him to find them again. The Company Pavilion, Galadriel&#39;s Garden and the House of Celeborn are also located there.</dd></dl>
