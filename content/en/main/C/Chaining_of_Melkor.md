The **Chaining of Melkor** refers to the defeat of [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md) in the [Battle of the Powers](../B/Battle_of_the_Powers.md), which ended with him being bound by the great chain [Angainor](../A/Angainor.md), and his subsequent imprisonment in the [Halls of Mandos](../H/Halls_of_Mandos.md).

This period lasted for three ages, at which point [he was released](../U/Unchaining_of_Melkor.md).

## Background {#Background}

Since the [Music of the Ainur](../M/Music_of_the_Ainur.md), Melkor had been in contest with his brethren. In the [Days before days](../D/Days_before_days.md), after descending into [Arda](../A/Arda.md), Melkor [fought a long war](../F/First_War.md) against the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), led by [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md), until he was eventually driven off by the arrival of [Tulkas](../T/Tulkas.md).

The Valar then constructed the [Two Lamps](../T/Two_Lamps.md) and Arda entered a [period of peace and flourishing](../S/Spring_of_Arda.md). Melkor, gathering other spirits to his side, returned in secret however, and eventually threw down the Lamps. The Valar then retreated to [Aman](../A/Aman.md), leaving Melkor near uncontested mastery over [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Once the Valar learned of the [Awakening of the Elves](../A/Awakening_of_the_Elves.md) within Middle-earth, they resolved to regain mastery over Arda, and launched a direct assault against Melkor.

## The chaining {#The_chaining}

Eventually, the Valar confronted Melkor directly within the deeps of his fortress [Utumno](../U/Utumno.md) where he was defeated and, by Tulkas, bound with [Angainor](../A/Angainor.md). Melkor was carried by to Valinor and sentenced to a long term of incarceration in the Halls of Mandos by [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md), his brother and the chief of the Valar.<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the end of the third age of his captivity, Manwë pardoned Melkor and released him from his bondage,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but he turned back to darkness, destroying the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md) and fleeing back to his [strongholds in the north](../A/Angband.md) of Middle-earth with the stolen [Silmarils](../S/Silmarils.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
