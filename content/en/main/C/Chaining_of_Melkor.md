The **Chaining of Melkor** refers to the defeat of [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor) in the [Battle of the Powers](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers), which ended with him being bound by the great chain [Angainor](/wiki/Angainor), and his subsequent imprisonment in the [Halls of Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos).

This period lasted for three ages, at which point [he was released](/wiki/Unchaining_of_Melkor "Unchaining of Melkor").

## Background {#Background}

Since the [Music of the Ainur](/wiki/Music_of_the_Ainur), Melkor had been in contest with his brethren. In the [Days before days](/wiki/Days_before_days), after descending into [Arda](/wiki/Arda), Melkor [fought a long war](/wiki/First_War "First War") against the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), led by [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB), until he was eventually driven off by the arrival of [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas).

The Valar then constructed the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps) and Arda entered a [period of peace and flourishing](/wiki/Spring_of_Arda "Spring of Arda"). Melkor, gathering other spirits to his side, returned in secret however, and eventually threw down the Lamps. The Valar then retreated to [Aman](/wiki/Aman), leaving Melkor near uncontested mastery over [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Once the Valar learned of the [Awakening of the Elves](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves) within Middle-earth, they resolved to regain mastery over Arda, and launched a direct assault against Melkor.

## The chaining {#The_chaining}

Eventually, the Valar confronted Melkor directly within the deeps of his fortress [Utumno](/wiki/Utumno) where he was defeated and, by Tulkas, bound with [Angainor](/wiki/Angainor). Melkor was carried by to Valinor and sentenced to a long term of incarceration in the Halls of Mandos by [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB), his brother and the chief of the Valar.<sup id="cite_ref-Captivity_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captivity-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the end of the third age of his captivity, Manwë pardoned Melkor and released him from his bondage,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but he turned back to darkness, destroying the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor") and fleeing back to his [strongholds in the north](/wiki/Angband "Angband") of Middle-earth with the stolen [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
