**Cobas Haven** was an inlet that opened onto the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas) between [Anfalas](/wiki/Anfalas) to the north and the promontory of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth) to the south. The river [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond) (with the gathered waters of the [Ciril](/wiki/Ciril) and the [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3)) emptied into Cobas Haven from the northeast.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The haven of [Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond), southwest of the confluence of the Morthond and Ringló, had been established by Sindarin Elves who had fled [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). In the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) found the shores of the Bay of Belfalas uninhabited except for the small settlement of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) still living in this haven.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The last of these Elves departed in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In order to reach the open ocean, all of the departing Elves would have had to traverse the waters of Cobas Haven.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) has suggested that his father derived the name *Cobas* from [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) *[Kópas](/wiki/K%C3%B3pa "Kópa")* ("Haven").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
