**Clifford Broadway** ([January 27](../_/27_January.md), [1969](../_/1969.md)), also going by the internet handle **"Quickbeam"**, is a writer, actor, [Tolkien fan](../T/Tolkien_fandom.md) and staff member of [TheOneRing.net](../T/TheOneRing.net.md) since [1999](../_/1999.md).

Active as an actor for most of his life, Broadway has played [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) in the Xbox version of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (video game)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game).md>), hosted TORn's Oscar parties of [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), [The One Ring Celebrations](../T/The_One_Ring_Celebration.md) and various DragonCons. He also played a bit role as a soldier of Gondor in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.
