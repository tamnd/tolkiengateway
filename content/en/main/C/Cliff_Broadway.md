**Clifford Broadway** ([January 27](/wiki/27_January "27 January"), [1969](/wiki/1969)), also going by the internet handle **"Quickbeam"**, is a writer, actor, [Tolkien fan](/wiki/Tolkien_fandom "Tolkien fandom") and staff member of [TheOneRing.net](/wiki/TheOneRing.net) since [1999](/wiki/1999).

Active as an actor for most of his life, Broadway has played [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) in the Xbox version of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (video game)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"), hosted TORn's Oscar parties of [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), [The One Ring Celebrations](/wiki/The_One_Ring_Celebration "The One Ring Celebration") and various DragonCons. He also played a bit role as a soldier of Gondor in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.
