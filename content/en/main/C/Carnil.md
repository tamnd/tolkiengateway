**Carnil** was a bright red star<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> created by [Varda](/wiki/Varda) in preparation for the coming of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Actually, this "star" is the planet <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mars" title="wikipedia:Mars">Mars</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Carnil* is probably [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Red Star", by Paul Strack explains, being a compound of *[carnë](/wiki/Carn%C3%AB "Carnë")* ("red") + *[el](/wiki/El "El")* ("star").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
