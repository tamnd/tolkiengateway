**Calimmacil** was a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") nobleman.

## History {#History}

Calimmacil was the son of [Arciryas](/wiki/Arciryas), and therefore a nephew of [Narmacil II](/wiki/Narmacil_II) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1684](/wiki/Third_Age_1684 "Third Age 1684")-[1856](/wiki/Third_Age_1856 "Third Age 1856")), the 29th [King of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor").

Calimmacil had a son, [Siriondil](</wiki/Siriondil_(son_of_Calimmacil)> "Siriondil (son of Calimmacil)"), and a grandson, [Eärnil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II "Eärnil II").

Nothing is known about the life of Calimmacil, who enters history because after King [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) and his sons were slain in battle, his grandson Eärnil was chosen to succeed Ondoher to the throne.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Calimmacil* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). It is not glossed, but as Paul Strack explains, it probably means "Bright Sword", from *[calima](/wiki/Kalima "Kalima")* ("bright") and *[macil](/wiki/Macil "Macil")* ("sword").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
