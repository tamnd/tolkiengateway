## Other writings {#Other_writings}

In Fragment II of *[The End of Bovadium](/wiki/The_End_of_Bovadium)*<sup id="cite_ref-CGL_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CGL-1">[1]</a></sup>, it is mentioned that other cities besides *[Bovadium](/wiki/Bovadium)* had been experiencing troubles from *[Motores Bi](/wiki/Motores_Bi)*; the **Capital City** is described as having "become an almost solid mass of *Motores*". During the debates between the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Northerners" title="Northerners">Northerners</a> and the [Southerners](/wiki/Southerners), the Minister choose not to do anything proposed by either side and would have proceeded with his own plan had he not gotten trapped in the Capital City and died.<sup id="cite_ref-FII_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FII-2">[2]</a></sup>
