## Other writings {#Other_writings}

In Fragment II of *[The End of Bovadium](../T/The_End_of_Bovadium.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-CGL_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CGL-1">[1]</a></sup>, it is mentioned that other cities besides *[Bovadium](../B/Bovadium.md)* had been experiencing troubles from *[Motores Bi](../M/Motores_Bi.md)*; the **Capital City** is described as having "become an almost solid mass of *Motores*". During the debates between the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../N/Northerners.md" title="Northerners">Northerners</a> and the [Southerners](../S/Southerners.md), the Minister choose not to do anything proposed by either side and would have proceeded with his own plan had he not gotten trapped in the Capital City and died.<sup id="cite_ref-FII_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FII-2">[2]</a></sup>
