<i><b>Christie&#39;s Fine Printed Books and Manuscripts 24 May 2002</b></i> is an auction catalogue which includes three reproductions of letters from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to [Nancy Smith](/wiki/Nancy_Smith) and a joint manuscript ("[Index questions](/wiki/Index_questions)").

- Lot 409 (pp. 278–9): "[Index questions](/wiki/Index_questions)" and [Letter to Nancy Smith (27 May 1958)](</wiki/Letter_to_Nancy_Smith_(27_May_1958)>)
- Lot 410 (pp. 280–1): [Letter to Nancy Smith (Christmas 1963)](</wiki/Letter_to_Nancy_Smith_(Christmas_1963)>)
- Lot 411 (p. 282): [Letter to Nancy Smith (30 May 1966)](</wiki/Letter_to_Nancy_Smith_(30_May_1966)>)
