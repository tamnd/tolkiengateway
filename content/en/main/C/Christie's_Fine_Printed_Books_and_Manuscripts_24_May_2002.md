<i><b>Christie&#39;s Fine Printed Books and Manuscripts 24 May 2002</b></i> is an auction catalogue which includes three reproductions of letters from [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Nancy Smith](../N/Nancy_Smith.md) and a joint manuscript ("[Index questions](../I/Index_questions.md)").

- Lot 409 (pp. 278–9): "[Index questions](../I/Index_questions.md)" and [Letter to Nancy Smith (27 May 1958)](<../L/Letter_to_Nancy_Smith_(27_May_1958).md>)
- Lot 410 (pp. 280–1): [Letter to Nancy Smith (Christmas 1963)](<../L/Letter_to_Nancy_Smith_(Christmas_1963).md>)
- Lot 411 (p. 282): [Letter to Nancy Smith (30 May 1966)](<../L/Letter_to_Nancy_Smith_(30_May_1966).md>)
