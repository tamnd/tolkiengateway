**Chris Seeman** is Associate Professor of Theology at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walsh_University" title="wikipedia:Walsh University">Walsh University</a> in North Canton, Ohio. He has written both scholarly articles and gaming material concerned with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s works. Seeman is also the creator of the web site *The Tolkien Music List*.
