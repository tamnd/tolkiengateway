**Chariots** were used by [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) for transportation and for war.<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the invasion of the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) the chieftains of the Wainriders fought in chariots.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944") in the battle known as the [Disaster of the Morannon](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Morannon) the vanguard of the Wainriders and their allies from [Khand](/wiki/Khand), which consisted of war-chariots of the Wainriders and a force of cavalry, surprised the main force of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), which was led by [King](/wiki/King_of_Gondor "King of Gondor") [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher). The enemy vanguard crashed into the disordered line of the main force of Gondor, killed King Ondoher and his son [Artamir](/wiki/Artamir) with almost all of the king's Guard, and pushed the [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") [soldiers](/wiki/Gondorian_Military_Forces "Gondorian Military Forces") back, dispersing and pursuing many of them to the west into the [Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes).<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-1">[1]</a></sup>

Later in the Third Age, on [26 February](/wiki/26_February) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) had a vision of the chariots of chieftains coming from the [East](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn "Rhûn") when he sat on the [Seat of Seeing](/wiki/Seat_of_Seeing) on [Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen), the Hill of the Eye.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It is unknown whether these chariots were used in battle or for transportation.
