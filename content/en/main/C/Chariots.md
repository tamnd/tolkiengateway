**Chariots** were used by [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) for transportation and for war.<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the invasion of the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) the chieftains of the Wainriders fought in chariots.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md) in the battle known as the [Disaster of the Morannon](../D/Disaster_of_the_Morannon.md) the vanguard of the Wainriders and their allies from [Khand](../K/Khand.md), which consisted of war-chariots of the Wainriders and a force of cavalry, surprised the main force of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), which was led by [King](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md). The enemy vanguard crashed into the disordered line of the main force of Gondor, killed King Ondoher and his son [Artamir](../A/Artamir.md) with almost all of the king's Guard, and pushed the [Gondorian](../G/Gondorians.md) [soldiers](../G/Gondorian_Military_Forces.md) back, dispersing and pursuing many of them to the west into the [Dead Marshes](../D/Dead_Marshes.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-1">[1]</a></sup>

Later in the Third Age, on [26 February](../_/26_February.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) had a vision of the chariots of chieftains coming from the [East](../R/Rhûn.md) when he sat on the [Seat of Seeing](../S/Seat_of_Seeing.md) on [Amon Hen](../A/Amon_Hen.md), the Hill of the Eye.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It is unknown whether these chariots were used in battle or for transportation.
