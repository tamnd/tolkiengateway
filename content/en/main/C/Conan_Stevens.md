**Mark "Conan" Stevens** portrayed a Gundabad Orc in *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)* and in *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)*. In the third film he is credited as [Keeper of the Dungeons](/wiki/Keeper_of_the_Dungeons). He was originally cast as [Azog](/wiki/Azog), Bolg's father, but switched roles during filming.<sup id="cite_ref-TORNBolg_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TORNBolg-1">[1]</a></sup> After that, he was cast to portray [Bolg](/wiki/Bolg) in the [*The Hobbit* film trilogy](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)") through prosthetic makeup but later he was replaced by [Lawrence Makoare](/wiki/Lawrence_Makoare), who portrayed him through CGI and performance capture in the later film.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> However, Stevens was still credited as Bolg in the ending credits of *An Unexpected Journey*.
