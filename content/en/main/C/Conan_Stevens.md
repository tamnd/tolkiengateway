**Mark "Conan" Stevens** portrayed a Gundabad Orc in *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md)* and in *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md)*. In the third film he is credited as [Keeper of the Dungeons](../K/Keeper_of_the_Dungeons.md). He was originally cast as [Azog](../A/Azog.md), Bolg's father, but switched roles during filming.<sup id="cite_ref-TORNBolg_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TORNBolg-1">[1]</a></sup> After that, he was cast to portray [Bolg](../B/Bolg.md) in the [*The Hobbit* film trilogy](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>) through prosthetic makeup but later he was replaced by [Lawrence Makoare](../L/Lawrence_Makoare.md), who portrayed him through CGI and performance capture in the later film.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> However, Stevens was still credited as Bolg in the ending credits of *An Unexpected Journey*.
