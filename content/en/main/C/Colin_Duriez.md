**Colin Duriez** ([19 July](../_/19_July.md) [1947](../_/1947.md)—[1 November](../_/1_November.md) [2025](../_/2025.md)<sup id="cite_ref-death_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-death-1">[1]</a></sup>) was the author of several books and articles relating to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and the [Inklings](../I/Inklings.md).
