**Colin Duriez** ([19 July](/wiki/19_July) [1947](/wiki/1947)—[1 November](/wiki/1_November) [2025](/wiki/2025)<sup id="cite_ref-death_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-death-1">[1]</a></sup>) was the author of several books and articles relating to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings).
