**Council of the Rings** is a Tolkien content creator that hosts an online community of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) fans on YouTube, Reddit, Discord, Instagram and Twitter. On his YouTube channel various topics around J.R.R. Tolkien and his works are covered. The channel is especially known for its reviews of [The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md) series, his own Second age series, the community posts and the commissioned artworks created by [Ralph Damiani](../R/Ralph_Damiani.md).

Council of the Rings is also known under a different alias, Macbeth of Gondor, which is a different YouTube channel run by the same content creator. On that channel development and news around [Kingdoms of Arda](../K/Kingdoms_of_Arda.md) is shared with the public. Kingdoms of Arda is a [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) mod for Mount &amp; Blade II: Bannerlord. Macbeth is currently an active member of the development and head of the Scene Department. In the past he has also worked on other The Lord of the Rings mods for other games such as; Minecraft, Mount &amp; Blade: Warband, Europa Universalis IV, Crusader Kings II and Crusader Kings III.
