**Carandrian** was an [Ent](/wiki/Ent) of [Quickbeam](/wiki/Quickbeam)'s folk, according to the draft version of the chapter *[Flotsam and Jetsam](/wiki/Flotsam_and_Jetsam)*.<sup id="cite_ref-War_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-War-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Carandrian was a friend of Quickbeam, and one of the Ents that took part in the [Battle of Isengard](/wiki/Battle_of_Isengard); during the battle he was injured by an axe, which enraged the rest of the Ents.<sup id="cite_ref-War_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-War-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Carandrian* is in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin). Its meaning is unclear, but it might contain the element *[caran](/wiki/Caran "Caran")* ("red"), according to [Roman Rausch](/wiki/Roman_Rausch).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
