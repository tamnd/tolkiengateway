**Carandrian** was an [Ent](../E/Ent.md) of [Quickbeam](../Q/Quickbeam.md)'s folk, according to the draft version of the chapter *[Flotsam and Jetsam](../F/Flotsam_and_Jetsam.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-War_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-War-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Carandrian was a friend of Quickbeam, and one of the Ents that took part in the [Battle of Isengard](../B/Battle_of_Isengard.md); during the battle he was injured by an axe, which enraged the rest of the Ents.<sup id="cite_ref-War_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-War-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Carandrian* is in [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md). Its meaning is unclear, but it might contain the element *[caran](Caran.md)* ("red"), according to [Roman Rausch](../R/Roman_Rausch.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
