**Clovers** were a family of flowering plants. Cockscomb, purple, and white honey-smelling clovers grew at the edge of [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn)'s [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees")-pastures.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1984-1997: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>The books describe two types of clover:</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Bitterlove</b></i>  (<a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Dolgened</i>, <a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Nullacenië</i>) was a type of four-leaved clover, with small pink flowers. Its fresh leaves could be boiled to produce a sensory-enhancing steam, which allowed both Elves and Men to see as well in the dark as Dwarves could. Dwarves, however, would have their vision reduced.<sup id="cite_ref-healer_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-healer-2">[2]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Black Clover</b></i> (<a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Mornast</i>, <a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Mornasto</i>) was a rare and dangerous clover in the higher valley of <a href="/wiki/Lebennin" title="Lebennin">Lebennin</a>. Its pollen gave a burning sensation to the lungs, followed by coughing and vomiting. The <a href="/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain" title="Drúedain">Drúedain</a> used it in a poison paste.<sup id="cite_ref-healer_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-healer-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>
