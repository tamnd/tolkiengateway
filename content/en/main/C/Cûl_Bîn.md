**Cûl Bîn** was the southernmost of three peaks (the other two being [Dol Tarlang](/wiki/Dol_Tarlang) and [Cûl Veleg](/wiki/C%C3%BBl_Veleg)) located in the pass of [Tarlang's Neck](/wiki/Tarlang%27s_Neck). The three mountains formed a spur on the southern side of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains).

According to legend, Cûl Bîn was in origin part of the load of rocks carried by the [giant](/wiki/Giants "Giants") [Tarlang](/wiki/Tarlang).<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Cûl Bîn* is glossed as "Little Load",<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup> consisting of *[cûl](/wiki/C%C3%BBl "Cûl")* ("load") and *[bîn](/wiki/P%C3%AEn "Pîn")* ("little", from *[pîn](/wiki/P%C3%AEn "Pîn")*).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Cùl Bin(neadh), in Scottish Gaelic, means "behind \[a non-specific / group of\] mountain(s)", the subject of which could of course be another mountain.
