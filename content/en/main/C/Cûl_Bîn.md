**Cûl Bîn** was the southernmost of three peaks (the other two being [Dol Tarlang](../D/Dol_Tarlang.md) and [Cûl Veleg](Cûl_Veleg.md)) located in the pass of [Tarlang's Neck](../T/Tarlang's_Neck.md). The three mountains formed a spur on the southern side of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md).

According to legend, Cûl Bîn was in origin part of the load of rocks carried by the [giant](../G/Giants.md) [Tarlang](../T/Tarlang.md).<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Cûl Bîn* is glossed as "Little Load",<sup id="cite_ref-N_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-1">[1]</a></sup> consisting of *[cûl](Cûl.md)* ("load") and *[bîn](../P/Pîn.md)* ("little", from *[pîn](../P/Pîn.md)*).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Cùl Bin(neadh), in Scottish Gaelic, means "behind \[a non-specific / group of\] mountain(s)", the subject of which could of course be another mountain.
