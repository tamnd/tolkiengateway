## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="Cúthalion.md" title="Cúthalion"><b>Cú</b>thalion</a></i> = "Strongbow"
- <i><a href="../D/Dor-Cúarthol.md" title="Dor-Cúarthol">Dor-<b>Cú</b>arthol</a></i> = "Land of Bow and Helm"
- <i><a href="../L/Laer_Cú_Beleg.md" title="Laer Cú Beleg">Laer <b>Cú</b> Beleg</a></i> = "Song of the Great Bow"
