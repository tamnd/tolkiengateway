The **Captains of the [Outlands](/wiki/Outlands)** were those princes and other leaders of the [Southern Fiefs](/wiki/Southern_Fiefs) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) who led their troops to the defence of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

Altogether, the Captains led over two thousand soldiers to Minas Tirith. Among their ranks were

- [Forlong](/wiki/Forlong), the Lord of [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach), with two hundred well-armed men bearing battle-axes
- [Dervorin](/wiki/Dervorin), son of the lord of the [Ringló Vale](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3_Vale), with three hundred men on foot
- [Duinhir](/wiki/Duinhir) and his sons [Duilin](</wiki/Duilin_(son_of_Duinhir)> "Duilin (son of Duinhir)") and [Derufin](/wiki/Derufin) from the uplands of [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond), leading five hundred bowmen
- [Golasgil](/wiki/Golasgil), the lord of [Anfalas](/wiki/Anfalas), "a long line" of with many sorts of men scantily equipped except the men of the household of Golasgil himself
- [Hirluin the Fair](/wiki/Hirluin "Hirluin") from [Pinnath Gelin](/wiki/Pinnath_Gelin) with three hundred "gallant green-clad men"
- [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil), Prince of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth), with a company of knights in full armour and seven hundred men-of-arms<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
