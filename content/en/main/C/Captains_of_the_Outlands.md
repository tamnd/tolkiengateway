The **Captains of the [Outlands](../O/Outlands.md)** were those princes and other leaders of the [Southern Fiefs](../S/Southern_Fiefs.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) who led their troops to the defence of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) in the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

## History {#History}

Altogether, the Captains led over two thousand soldiers to Minas Tirith. Among their ranks were

- [Forlong](../F/Forlong.md), the Lord of [Lossarnach](../L/Lossarnach.md), with two hundred well-armed men bearing battle-axes
- [Dervorin](../D/Dervorin.md), son of the lord of the [Ringló Vale](../R/Ringló_Vale.md), with three hundred men on foot
- [Duinhir](../D/Duinhir.md) and his sons [Duilin](<../D/Duilin_(son_of_Duinhir).md>) and [Derufin](../D/Derufin.md) from the uplands of [Morthond](../M/Morthond.md), leading five hundred bowmen
- [Golasgil](../G/Golasgil.md), the lord of [Anfalas](../A/Anfalas.md), "a long line" of with many sorts of men scantily equipped except the men of the household of Golasgil himself
- [Hirluin the Fair](../H/Hirluin.md) from [Pinnath Gelin](../P/Pinnath_Gelin.md) with three hundred "gallant green-clad men"
- [Imrahil](../I/Imrahil.md), Prince of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md), with a company of knights in full armour and seven hundred men-of-arms<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
