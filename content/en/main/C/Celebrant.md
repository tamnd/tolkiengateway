**Celebrant** (translated as **Silverlode**) was a stream rising in the eastern [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) from springs some way below the the [Mirrormere](/wiki/Mirrormere) in the [Dimrill Dale](/wiki/Dimrill_Dale). It then ran through [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) where it was met by the [Nimrodel](</wiki/Nimrodel_(river)> "Nimrodel (river)") river, and emptied into the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2994](/wiki/Third_Age_2994 "Third Age 2994"), an army of orcs came up the Silverlode and wiped out the colony of Dwarves dwelling in [Moria](/wiki/Moria).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Around [November](/wiki/November) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan) and [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir), sent by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) to a secret errand, passed down the Silverlode into a "strange country" and were the last to return to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), in [December](/wiki/December).<sup id="cite_ref-south_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) followed this river when they travelled from Moria to Lothlórien.<sup id="cite_ref-south_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-south-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Celebrant* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, consisting of *[celeb](/wiki/Celeb "Celeb")* ("silver") + *[rant](/wiki/Rant "Rant")* ("course").<sup id="cite_ref-S_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-3">[3]</a></sup>

The element *lode* in *Silverlode* means "course, water-channel".<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-4">[4]</a></sup>

These names were also a translation of <i><b><a href="/wiki/Kibil-n%C3%A2la" title="Kibil-nâla">Kibil-nâla</a></b></i> in [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul), the language of [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). An earlier name is also given as *Zigilnâd*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

On the [The First Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings), the river was named "Blackroot", then "Redway"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>. The two names were strucked out on the map.
