The **Causeway Forts**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or **Guard-towers**<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup> were two towers with battlements on each side of the north-eastern<sup id="cite_ref-MT_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-3">[3]</a></sup> gate of the [Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor) from which a [Causeway](</wiki/Causeway_(Gondor)> "Causeway (Gondor)") ran over the flat land to the city of [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-4">[4]</a></sup> On the inside of the Rammas Echor the road ran for four leagues (twelve miles)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> over the [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields) to the [Great Gate of Minas Tirith](/wiki/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [10 March](/wiki/10_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond), a member of the [Guards of the Citadel](/wiki/Guards_of_the_Citadel), returned to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) from the Causeway Forts to which he had been sent from the city over the Pelennor Fields.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup>

On the following day, on [11 March](/wiki/11_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> the [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor) [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) sent his younger son [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) to [defend](/wiki/Faramir%27s_defence_of_Osgiliath "Faramir's defence of Osgiliath") the crossing over the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) at Osgiliath against the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Morgul-host&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Morgul-host (page does not exist)">Morgul-host</a> to decimate the attackers before retreating back to Minas Tirith.

On [12 March](/wiki/12_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the attacking Morgul-host that was led by the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) and outnumbered the defenders ten times managed to cross the Anduin on a large number of floats and barges that they had secretly built on the eastern side of Osgiliath so that Faramir retreated and rallied his men to the Causeway Forts.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

However, in the early hours of [13 March](/wiki/13_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> the attackers wrecked the Causeway Forts and blasted breaches in the Rammas Echor. The defenders had to retreat over the Pelennor Fields to the Great Gate of Minas Tirith with Faramir leading the rearguard. When cavalry from [Harad](/wiki/Harad) overtook the retreating men and caused them to rout, the entire remaining cavalry of Minas Tirith charged from the city, attacked the enemy forces and escorted the retreating men including Faramir, who had been shot with a dart during a fight with a mounted champion of Harad, back to the city. Only two thirds of the defenders including Faramir made it back to Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier manuscript of what would later become the chapter [The Siege of Gondor](/wiki/The_Siege_of_Gondor) the Causeway Forts are called *Bereth Ondrath*.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests that *Bereth Ondrath* is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) name, which means "Towers of the Stone Road".<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-13">[13]</a></sup> It is likely a combination of the plural of *[barad](/wiki/Barad "Barad")* ("tower"), the [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") form of *[gond](/wiki/Gond "Gond")* ("stone") and *[rath](/wiki/Rath "Rath")* ("course", "street").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Causeway Forts guard the way west out of the city. In the version of the city that act as the player-versus-monster-player map, they are the stronghold of the Free Peoples and cannot be accessed by the enemy players.</dd></dl>
