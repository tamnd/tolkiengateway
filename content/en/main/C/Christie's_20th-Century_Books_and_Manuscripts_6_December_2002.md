<i><b>Christie&#39;s 20th-Century Books and Manuscripts 6 December 2002</b></i> is an auction catalogue from Christie's (London). Two Tolkien-related lots are included:

- Lot 26 (p. 20) - Quotes from and describes two letters to A.W. Riddle dated [10 January](<../L/Letter_to_A.W._Riddle_(10_January_1947).md>) and [2 October](<../L/Letter_to_A.W._Riddle_(2_October_1947).md>) [1947](../_/1947.md). Two pages from the letters are reproduced in a photograph.
- Lot 29 (p. 21) - Quotes from and describes a letter to [Carole Ward dated 10 August 1964](../L/Letter_to_Carole_Ward.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
