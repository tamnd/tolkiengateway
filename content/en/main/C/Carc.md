**Carc** was one of the [ravens](/wiki/Ravens "Ravens") who lived on [Ravenhill](/wiki/Ravenhill), one of the foothills of [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"), the Lonely Mountain. Carc seems to have been the chief of those birds, and dwelt with his wife above the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves)' guard-chamber on the hill. [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) recalled him as being wise and famous at the time the Dwarves were driven out of [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") by [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug). When Thorin returned to the Lonely Mountain 171 years later, Carc was dead, but the ravens remained, led by his son [Roäc](/wiki/Ro%C3%A4c).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Carc* is likely an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onomatopoeia" title="wikipedia:Onomatopoeia">onomatopoeic</a> name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
