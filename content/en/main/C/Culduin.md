The **Culduin** is the river in [Nurn](/wiki/Nurn) in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) that flows from the eastern part of the west-to-east running section of the southern [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) mountains northwards into the [Sea of Nurnen](/wiki/Sea_of_Nurnen) on its southern shore. The river is unnamed on the [map](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth "General Map of Middle-earth") that was drawn by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) for publication with *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* based on a [map](/wiki/The_Third_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Third Map of The Lord of the Rings") that was drawn by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

## Chronology and Creation {#Chronology_and_Creation}

It first appeared on a merchandise map of Mordor for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* that was drawn by [Daniel Reeve](/wiki/Daniel_Reeve)<sup id="cite_ref-reeve-site_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-site-1">[1]</a></sup> in 2003<sup id="cite_ref-reeve-mail_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-mail-2">[2]</a></sup>.

## Fan works {#Fan_works}

The fansite [Thelandofshadow.com](/wiki/Thelandofshadow.com) took on several of the names introduced by Reeve, and expanded on them. The Culduin was described as follows:<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The smallest of the four rivers that flowed into the Sea of Núrnen. This river flowed across the south of Nurn into the snland sea. It was a small river that flowed down from the mountains of Ered Glamhoth. It was dry much of the year, but flowed like a raging storm when the rains fell.
</p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

The information from The Land of Shadow was incorporated in a slightly modified form into the fan made *Mordor Gazetteer* of the Middle-earth Role Playing (MERP) fan modules group.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Culduin* seems to be a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which means "Loderiver". It seems to contain the elements *[cûl](/wiki/C%C3%BBl "Cûl")* ("load")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and *[duin](/wiki/Duin "Duin")* ("(large) river")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>.
