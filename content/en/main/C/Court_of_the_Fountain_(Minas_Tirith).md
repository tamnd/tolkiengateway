The **Court of the Fountain** was part of the highest level in the citadel of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), near the steps to the [Tower of Ecthelion](../T/Tower_of_Ecthelion.md). This courtyard held the [White Tree of Gondor](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md) and was guarded by the [Guards of the Citadel](../G/Guards_of_the_Citadel.md). It was paved with white stones except around a fountain, where a sward of green lay. The [dead White Tree](../D/Dead_Tree.md), seen by [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) as he hurried after [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), drooped over the fountain.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The Court of the Fountain is represented in three different versions of the in-game <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>. Prior to the <a href="../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a> it houses the dead White Tree, modeled after Professor Tolkien&#39;s favorite tree in the Oxford Botanical Gardens. After King <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../E/Elessar.md" title="Elessar">Elessar</a> finds the new sapling, the old tree is removed and the sapling is planted down during a formal ceremony in which the player participates. During the royal wedding of Elessar and <a href="../A/Arwen.md" title="Arwen">Arwen</a> on <a href="../M/Mid-year&#39;s_Day.md" title="Mid-year&#39;s Day">Midsummer Day</a>, the Court is the center of the festivities.</dd></dl>
