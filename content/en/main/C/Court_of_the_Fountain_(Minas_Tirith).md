The **Court of the Fountain** was part of the highest level in the citadel of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), near the steps to the [Tower of Ecthelion](/wiki/Tower_of_Ecthelion). This courtyard held the [White Tree of Gondor](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor) and was guarded by the [Guards of the Citadel](/wiki/Guards_of_the_Citadel). It was paved with white stones except around a fountain, where a sward of green lay. The [dead White Tree](/wiki/Dead_Tree "Dead Tree"), seen by [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") as he hurried after [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), drooped over the fountain.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Court of the Fountain is represented in three different versions of the in-game <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>. Prior to the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a> it houses the dead White Tree, modeled after Professor Tolkien&#39;s favorite tree in the Oxford Botanical Gardens. After King <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Elessar" title="Elessar">Elessar</a> finds the new sapling, the old tree is removed and the sapling is planted down during a formal ceremony in which the player participates. During the royal wedding of Elessar and <a href="/wiki/Arwen" title="Arwen">Arwen</a> on <a href="/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day" title="Mid-year&#39;s Day">Midsummer Day</a>, the Court is the center of the festivities.</dd></dl>
