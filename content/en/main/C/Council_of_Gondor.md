The **Council of Gondor**, also known as the **Great Council of Gondor**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> was composed of the Lords of the Fiefs and the Captains of the Forces in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), the advisors to the ruler of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) who were the [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor) and later the [Ruling Stewards](/wiki/Ruling_Stewards).<sup id="cite_ref-politics_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-politics-2">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor) governed the realm with the frame of ancient law, of which he was administrator (and interpreter) but not the lawmaker. He consulted the Council in debatable matters of importance domestic, or external.<sup id="cite_ref-politics_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-politics-2">[1]</a></sup>

The Council took authority over the realm after the loss of [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) left it kingless in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> and it acted under the control of [Steward](/wiki/Steward) [Pelendur](/wiki/Pelendur). In [1945](/wiki/Third_Age_1945 "Third Age 1945"), [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), whose wife was [Ondoher's daughter](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel "Fíriel"), invoked the [Law of Succession in Númenor](/wiki/Law_of_Succession_in_N%C3%BAmenor) to claim the throne of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor); the Council voted against his claim, in favour of [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher)'s distant relative, [Eärnil II](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

In all debatable matters of importance domestic, or external, however, even [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor_II "Denethor II") held Council, and at least listened to what the Lords of the Fiefs and the Captains of the Forces had to say.<sup id="cite_ref-politics_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-politics-2">[1]</a></sup> As the [Siege of Gondor](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor) approached, [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor") called the Council together on [11 March](/wiki/11_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> and it advised him to make no stroke of war against the enemy due to the threat from the south.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

[Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) re-established the Great Council of Gondor, and in that [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), who remained by inheritance the [Steward](/wiki/Steward) (or representative of the King during his absence abroad, or sickness, or between his death and the accession of his heir) was his chief counsellor.<sup id="cite_ref-politics_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-politics-2">[1]</a></sup>

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Council consists of the Wardens and the Captains of the City. The Wardens are those of the Burg, of the Coffers, of the Green, of the <a href="/wiki/Warden_of_the_Houses_of_Healing" title="Warden of the Houses of Healing">Houses of Healing</a>, of the Houses of Lore, <a href="/wiki/Warden_of_the_Keys" title="Warden of the Keys">of the Keys</a>, of the Laws, of the Stores and of the Wells. The Captains are those of the Garrison, the Citadel, the Out-Wall, the South-Gate and of the Tidings. The Council recommences under King <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Elessar</a> afer the end of the war.</dd></dl>
