**Charles E. Noad** (1 September 1947-[13 July](../_/13_July.md) [2023](../_/2023.md))<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a computer programmer, a proofreader and writer. He proofread [The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md), *[The History of Middle-earth](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* series, and [John D. Rateliff](../J/John_D._Rateliff.md)'s *[The History of The Hobbit](../T/The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-plaza_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-plaza-2">[2]</a></sup>
