**Charles E. Noad** (1 September 1947-[13 July](/wiki/13_July) [2023](/wiki/2023))<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a computer programmer, a proofreader and writer. He proofread [The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien), *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* series, and [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff)'s *[The History of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit)*.<sup id="cite_ref-plaza_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-plaza-2">[2]</a></sup>
