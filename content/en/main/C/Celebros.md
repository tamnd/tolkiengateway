**Celebros** was a stream of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), a tributary to [Taeglin](/wiki/Taeglin) that flowed through [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) forest.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It included the fall [Dimrost](/wiki/Dimrost)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> which was renamed [Nen Girith](/wiki/Nen_Girith), the "Shuddering Water", when the resting [Nienor](/wiki/Nienor) became cold and could not be warmed or comforted.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) was slain by [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) near the confluence of this stream with Taeglin, which was also near to the spot where Nienor committed suicide by jumping into Taeglin.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Celebros* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), from *[celeb](/wiki/Celeb "Celeb")* ("silver") and *[ros](/wiki/Ros "Ros")* ("foam").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
