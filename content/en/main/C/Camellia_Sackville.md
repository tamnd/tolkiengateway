**Camellia Sackville** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Camellia was daughter of the head of the [Sackville Family](../S/Sackville_Family.md). She married [Longo Baggins](../L/Longo_Baggins.md), and had one son, [Otho](../O/Otho_Sackville-Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The double surname [Sackville-Baggins](../S/Sackville-Baggins.md) was bestowed on her son, after the death of Camellia's father, as he was the eldest grandson, and became the new head.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camellia" title="wikipedia:Camellia">Camellia</a> is a group of Asian plants.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early sketches of the Family Trees, Camellia was said to be from [Michel Delving](../M/Michel_Delving.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
