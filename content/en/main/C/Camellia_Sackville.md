**Camellia Sackville** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Camellia was daughter of the head of the [Sackville Family](/wiki/Sackville_Family). She married [Longo Baggins](/wiki/Longo_Baggins), and had one son, [Otho](/wiki/Otho_Sackville-Baggins "Otho Sackville-Baggins").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The double surname [Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Sackville-Baggins) was bestowed on her son, after the death of Camellia's father, as he was the eldest grandson, and became the new head.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camellia" title="wikipedia:Camellia">Camellia</a> is a group of Asian plants.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early sketches of the Family Trees, Camellia was said to be from [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
