**Kemal Amin "Casey" Kasem** ([April 27](/wiki/27_April "27 April"), [1932](/wiki/1932) - [June 15](/wiki/15_June "15 June"), [2014](/wiki/2014)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>), played [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") in [The Return of the King (1980 film)](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)>). Kasem, a renowned voice actor, is perhaps best known as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaggy_Rogers" title="wikipedia:Shaggy Rogers">Shaggy</a> in *Scooby Doo*.
