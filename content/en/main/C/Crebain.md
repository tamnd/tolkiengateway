<i><b>Crebain</b></i> were large birds of the [crow-kind](/wiki/Crows "Crows"), native to [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland) and [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest).

## History {#History}

The [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) encountered various flocks of *crebain* on their arrival in [Hollin](/wiki/Hollin). Fearing that they were used as spies by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman), the Fellowship spent the entire day in hiding, without a campfire.<sup id="cite_ref-RGS_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RGS-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Crebain* is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) plural of *craban*. The word was derived from [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) tongues.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The plural form was known from the *Lord of the Rings* while the singular form was published in [2007](/wiki/2007); even before the publication of this singular, *craban* was the generally assumed form by [students](/wiki/Tolkienists "Tolkienists") of [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), although the forms *croban* or *creban* were possible as well.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The similarity between *craban* and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proto-Germanic" title="wikipedia:Proto-Germanic">Proto-Germanic</a> \**hrabanaz*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and its derivatives, such as *raven*, has been noted by several writers.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>A flock of <i>Crebain</i> &#34;from <a href="/wiki/Dunland" title="Dunland">Dunland</a>&#34; appears when the Fellowship is in Hollin. Though <a href="/wiki/Gimli" title="Gimli">Gimli</a> at first dismisses it as a cloud, <a href="/wiki/Boromir" title="Boromir">Boromir</a> notices them going too fast, and against the wind. The Fellowship hide as the birds swoop by.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The <i>crebain</i> are revealed to have indeed been spies when they report to Saruman at <a href="/wiki/Isengard" title="Isengard">Isengard</a> later.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>
