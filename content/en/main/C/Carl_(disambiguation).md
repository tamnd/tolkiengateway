**Carl** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

Potentially connected to the [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) **[Ceorl](/wiki/Ceorl)**, **Carl** was used by several Hobbits in the ancestry of the [Cotton Family](/wiki/Cotton_Family).

- [Carl](</wiki/Carl_(son_of_Cottar)> "Carl (son of Cottar)") (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1263](/wiki/Third_Age_2863 "Third Age 2863")), the second son of [Cottar](/wiki/Cottar).
- [Carl "Nibs" Cotton](/wiki/Carl_Cotton "Carl Cotton") (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1389](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989")), youngest son of [Tolman Cotton Senior](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior).
- [Sandyman](/wiki/Sandyman) the miller, who in [*The Hobbit* (2003 video game)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)> "The Hobbit (2003 video game)") was called "Carl Sandyman".

## Real world {#Real_world}

**Carl** and **Karl** are German versions of **Charles**.

- Carl Groves, lead vocalist of *[Glass Hammer](/wiki/Glass_Hammer)*
- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Carl_Hague&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Carl Hague (page does not exist)">Carl Hague</a>, actor who voiced [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1979 radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1979 radio series)").
- [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter), linguist and editor of *[Vinyar Tengwar](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar)*.
- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Carl_Lundgren&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Carl Lundgren (page does not exist)">Carl Lundgren</a>, artist.
- [Carl Phelpstead](/wiki/Carl_Phelpstead), author.
- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Carl_Willner&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Carl Willner (page does not exist)">Carl Willner</a>, author.
- [Karl Urban](/wiki/Karl_Urban), actor who played [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").
