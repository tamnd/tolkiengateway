**Carl** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

Potentially connected to the [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md) **[Ceorl](Ceorl.md)**, **Carl** was used by several Hobbits in the ancestry of the [Cotton Family](Cotton_Family~66362.md).

- [Carl](<Carl_(son_of_Cottar).md>) (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1263](../T/Third_Age_2863.md)), the second son of [Cottar](Cottar.md).
- [Carl "Nibs" Cotton](Carl_Cotton.md) (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1389](../T/Third_Age_2989.md)), youngest son of [Tolman Cotton Senior](../T/Tolman_Cotton_Senior.md).
- [Sandyman](../S/Sandyman.md) the miller, who in [*The Hobbit* (2003 video game)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game).md>) was called "Carl Sandyman".

## Real world {#Real_world}

**Carl** and **Karl** are German versions of **Charles**.

- [Carl F. Hostetter](Carl_F._Hostetter.md), linguist and editor of *[Vinyar Tengwar](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar.md)*.
