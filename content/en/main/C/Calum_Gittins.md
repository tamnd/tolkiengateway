**Calum Gittins** ([July 16](../_/16_July.md), [1986](../_/1986.md)) is the son of [Philippa Boyens](../P/Philippa_Boyens.md). He played [Haleth](<../H/Haleth_(film_character).md>), the movie-only son of [Háma](../H/Háma.md), in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*.

His lines were later dubbed, as his own voice had matured too much in the time between initial shooting and pick-ups.
