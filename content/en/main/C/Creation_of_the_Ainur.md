The **creation of the Ainur** was a great event prior to the creation of [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4), in which [Eru Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar "Ilúvatar") created the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) ("Holy Ones"), his servants.

## History {#History}

Ilúvatar made the Ainur "in the beginning",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> before the creation of [Time](/wiki/Time), in the [Timeless Halls](/wiki/Timeless_Halls) that he had prepared for them.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> As offspring of his thought, at first each one only "comprehended that part of the mind of Ilúvatar from which he came". After this, He propounded to them themes of music, which later would initiate the [Music of the Ainur](/wiki/Music_of_the_Ainur) and the Creation of the World.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first version of the story, it is specified that Ilúvatar "sang into being the Ainur" and thereafter he made their dwellings in the [void](/wiki/Void "Void").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The creation of a spiritual hierarchy resembles the creation of angels by the Judeo-Chistian God. However, it also implies ideas previous to [Christianity](/wiki/Christianity), as [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger) explains:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The concept of offspring from One suggests a sort of Pythagorean divisibility of a unity into component parts without disminution of the whole. The Ainur are powers or principalities emanating directly from the godhead and are developed in the text as aspects of his nature.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>
