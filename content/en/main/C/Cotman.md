**Cotman** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits").

## History {#History}

Cotman was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1260](/wiki/Third_Age_2860 "Third Age 2860"), the son of Cottar. He had one brother named [Carl](</wiki/Carl_(son_of_Cottar)> "Carl (son of Cottar)"). They were ancestors of the [Cottons](/wiki/Cotton_Family "Cotton Family") of [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater).

He married [Rose](</wiki/Rose_(daughter_of_Holman)> "Rose (daughter of Holman)"), the daughter of [Holman the greenhanded](/wiki/Holman_the_greenhanded), and their son (also called [Holman](/wiki/Holman_Cotton "Holman Cotton")) was the first Hobbit to use the family-name of Cotton.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Cotman* is a translation of [Westron](/wiki/Westron) <i><b>Hlothram</b></i>, derived from *[hlothram(a)](</wiki/Hlothram(a)> "Hlothram(a)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Cotman* is also a real-life surname, of similar meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
