**Ilmen** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈilmen]</span>) was a part of the atmosphere that surrounded the world of [Arda](../A/Arda.md), the region of airs where the [stars](../S/Stars.md) are.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Flesh cannot survive the airs of Ilmen unaided.<sup id="cite_ref-Ak_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ak-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), [Varda](../V/Varda.md) gave power to the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) and [Moon](../M/Moon.md) to traverse the lower regions of Ilmen, and, being closer than the stars, they would outshine them. Initially they voyaged upon appointed courses above the [girdle of Arda](../G/Girdle_of_Arda.md) from [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) unto the [East](../E/East.md) and back, so that they would meet at the middle of each day above the middle of the earth. At some point [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) sent <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Spirits_of_shadow&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Spirits of shadow (page does not exist)">spirits of shadow</a> against [Tilion](../T/Tilion.md) and after a strife beneath the stars, he defeated them.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the [Changing of the World](../C/Changing_of_the_World.md), the [Straight Road](../S/Straight_Road.md) can traverse Ilmen and reaches [Tol Eressea](../T/Tol_Eressea.md) and [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ak_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ak-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) has noted that *Ilmen* contains the stem *ilm-* and is related to such words as *[Ilmarë](Ilmarë.md)* and *[Ilmarin](Ilmarin.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the *[Etymologies](../E/Etymologies.md)* however, Ilmen is derived from the [root](../R/Root.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=GIL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="GIL (page does not exist)">GIL</a> signifying "the region above air where stars are".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> See also *[-men](<../M/Men_(Elvish).md>)*

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the early schematic titled *[I Vene Kemen](I_Vene_Kemen.md)*, the middle layer of airs was marked as *Ilwe* ([Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md)).

In the [Ambarkanta](../A/Ambarkanta.md), Tolkien initially wrote it as Silma, which then was changed to Ilma and finally Ilmen. In those texts, Ilmen formed the outer layer of normal air, enclosing the inner air, [Vista](../V/Vista.md), and itself enclosed by [Vaiya](../V/Vaiya.md) beyond. According to the [Diagram II](../../file/J/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Ambarkanta_Diagram_II.jpg.md), Ilmen is narrower along <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../N/North.md" title="North">North</a> and <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../S/South.md" title="South">South</a>, allowing some ice mountains to be formed on the surface of Vaiya. As Valinor lays outside Vista, its main air is Ilmen which is breathed by the [Valar](../V/Valar.md); it is cleansed and purified by the [Light of Valinor](../L/Light_of_Valinor.md), and the celestial bodies, preventing it to be darkened into mists and shadows. It is said that descending from Vista one may land on the earth, but from Ilmen one may land on Valinor.

Ilmen fills the gap between the edge of the world and Vaiya, and thus encloses Ambar above and below. The waters of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) are a compound of Vaiya and Ilmen, which [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) blends under the [Earthroots](../M/Martalmar.md).

Ilmen is the level of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md), the [Moon](../M/Moon.md) and the [Stars](../S/Stars.md) so it was also called *Tinwe-malle* (star-street) and <i><a class="mw-disambig" href="../E/Elenarda_(disambiguation).md" title="Elenarda (disambiguation)">Elenarda</a></i> (Stellar Kingdom).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The [Moon](../M/Moon.md) passed through Ilmen on its way around the world, plunging down the [Chasm of Ilmen](../C/Chasm_of_Ilmen.md) on its way back.

In [Diagram III](../../file/J/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Ambarkanta_Diagram_III.jpg.md) which shows the round Earth after the Changing, Ilmen is an upper layer of air (still between Vista and Vaiya) showing Valinor and Eresea there, with Valinor at the boundary with Vaiya. <span></span>
