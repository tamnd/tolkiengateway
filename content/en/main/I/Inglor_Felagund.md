**Inglor Felagund** was an older, rejected name for [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md). It makes a relic appearance in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, in the form of <i><a href="../G/Gildor.md" title="Gildor">Gildor <b>Inglor</b>ion</a></i>.

## Finrod {#Finrod}

In earlier versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), until the era of the first edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, the name *Finrod* was given to the character later known as [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md). [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md) was at the time known as Inglor Felagund.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The name however was survived in the patronym *Inglorion*. The character with the name *Inglorion* in *The Fellowship of the Ring* [Gildor Inglorion](../G/Gildor.md), although a fellow Noldo, was unrelated to Finrod and certainly not his son. Finrod, as seen in *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, was unmarried and childless because his lover, [Amarië](../A/Amarië.md) of the Vanyar, did not follow him to Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Inglor* in the early appearances was interpreted as "heart-gold", from the [roots](../S/Sundocarmë.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=ID-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ID- (page does not exist)">ID-</a> and [LAW](../L/LAW.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

At one point, Tolkien interpreted *Inglor* as a hypothetical [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name of Felagund's father [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md), a translation of his Quenya [mother-name](../A/Amilessë.md) <i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="Ingalaurë.md" title="Ingalaurë">Ingalaurë</a></b></i> ("Top Gold"), but "this was not actually applied to Finarfin who never came to Beleriand".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
