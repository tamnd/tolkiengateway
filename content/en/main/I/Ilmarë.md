**Ilmarë** was one of the chiefs of the [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar), and the handmaiden to Queen [Varda](/wiki/Varda).

She is only mentioned briefly in the *[Valaquenta](/wiki/Valaquenta)*, where Ilmarë is described along with the Maia [Eönwë](/wiki/E%C3%B6nw%C3%AB) who was the banner-bearer and herald of King [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Her name may be related to one of the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) words for "Starlight".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Christopher Tolkien has noted that *Ilmarë* is related to such words as *[Ilmen](/wiki/Ilmen)* and *[Ilmarin](/wiki/Ilmarin)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to the early stages of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the character that would become Ilmarë was called **Erinti**. She was the daughter of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) and Varda, but the concept of the Valar having [children](/wiki/Valarindi "Valarindi") was abandoned and Ilmarë became Varda's handmaiden.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

She appears in the *[Qenyaqetsa](/wiki/Qenyaqetsa)*, in a rejected early entry. There she is described as:

But, as [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) points out, her dwelling in Tol Eressëa in a *korin* was eventually taken over by the character of [Meril-i-Turinqi](/wiki/Meril-i-Turinqi), and Erinti became the daughter of Manwë and Varda.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Early etymology {#Early_etymology}

The name <i><b>Erinti</b></i> is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

She was also called <i><b>Lotessë</b></i> (or <i><b>Lotissë</b></i>), being comprised of Qenya <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=L%C3%B3te&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lóte (page does not exist)">lóte</a></i> ("flower") and the feminine suffix *-ssë*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Another one of her names was <i><b>Akairis</b></i> (simply meaning "bride" in Qenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Aka_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aka-5">[5]</a></sup>

Among her earlier, rejected names was <i><b>Helinyetillë</b></i> (glossed as "Eyes of Heartsease"<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>), a combination of Qenya <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Helin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Helin (page does not exist)">helin</a></i> ("pansy") and some variation of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Y%C3%A9ta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Yéta (page does not exist)">yéta</a>-</i> ("look at"), later used as a name for [Nessa](/wiki/Nessa).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> An early, likewise rejected name <i><b>Venestë</b></i> appears next to *Lotesse* in the Qenya Lexicon, but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-Aka_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aka-5">[5]</a></sup>
