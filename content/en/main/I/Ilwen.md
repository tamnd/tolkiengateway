**Ilwen** was the wife of [Ingwë](Ingwë.md), King of the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md).

## History {#History}

Ilwen was born at [Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md), likely to the more junior generations of the [Minyar](../M/Minyar.md). She wed [Ingwë](Ingwë.md), who was a direct male-line descendant of [Imin](Imin.md), from eldest son to eldest son. The couple were wed and had several children at Cuiviénen by the time [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) found the [Quendi](../Q/Quendi.md) and invited them to [Aman](../A/Aman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ilion_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ilion-1">[1]</a></sup>

Ilwen probably accompanied Ingwë and the rest of the Vanyar to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), as it is said that Ingwë begat many children in the bliss of Aman, as noted by [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md) when he complained to the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) about his widowhood following the death of [Míriel](../M/Míriel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to *[Eldamo](../E/Eldamo.md)*, the name *Ilwen* seems to be in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
