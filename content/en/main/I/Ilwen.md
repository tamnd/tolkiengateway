**Ilwen** was the wife of [Ingwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB), King of the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar).

## History {#History}

Ilwen was born at [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen), likely to the more junior generations of the [Minyar](/wiki/Minyar). She wed [Ingwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB), who was a direct male-line descendant of [Imin](/wiki/Imin), from eldest son to eldest son. The couple were wed and had several children at Cuiviénen by the time [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) found the [Quendi](/wiki/Quendi) and invited them to [Aman](/wiki/Aman).<sup id="cite_ref-Ilion_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ilion-1">[1]</a></sup>

Ilwen probably accompanied Ingwë and the rest of the Vanyar to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), as it is said that Ingwë begat many children in the bliss of Aman, as noted by [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB) when he complained to the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) about his widowhood following the death of [Míriel](/wiki/M%C3%ADriel).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to *[Eldamo](/wiki/Eldamo)*, the name *Ilwen* seems to be in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
