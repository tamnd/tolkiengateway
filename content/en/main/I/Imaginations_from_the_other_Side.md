<i><b>Imaginations from the Other Side</b></i> is the lead track of the album of the same name by [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian). It is the only song in the album that mentions the work of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
