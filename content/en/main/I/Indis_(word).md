**indis** is a word in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) which means "great or valiant woman",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> but is also translated as "bride" or "wife".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is most notably the name of [Finwë's second wife](Indis.md).

## Earlier words for bride / wife {#Earlier_words_for_bride_/_wife}

Other words in the earlier lexicons, such as [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), which mean "bride" or "wife":

- *acairis*
    - Acairis was another name for *Erinti*, the character that became [Ilmarë](Ilmarë.md)
- *vestani*
- *herivesti*

## See also {#See_also}

- *Indis i·Ciryamo*: the Quenya translation of "[The Mariner’s Wife](../A/Aldarion_and_Erendis%253A_The_Mariner's_Wife.md)"
- *[Nessa](../N/Nessa.md)*: also known as *Indis* in the earlier legendarium
