The **Isen**, called <i><b>Sîr Angren</b></i> in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-WifeNote_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WifeNote-1">[1]</a></sup>, was a river that formed the western border of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) from its junction with the [Adorn](../A/Adorn.md) to the outer fences of the fortress of [Isengard](Isengard.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>.

## Course {#Course}

The source of the river was in the southern [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), first flowing south through [Nan Curunír](../N/Nan_Curunír.md) and across the [Gap of Rohan](../G/Gap_of_Rohan.md) to the edge of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md), where it abruptly bent west and ran to the sea of [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md). At a distance of about 50 [lári](../L/Lár.md) west of the [Gap of Rohan](../G/Gap_of_Rohan.md), the Isen was joined by its tributary river, the [Adorn](../A/Adorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [North-South Road](../G/Greenway.md) ended on the western shore of the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> There, the Isen passed in two arms around a large eyot. The Fords of Isen were the only place south of [Isengard](Isengard.md) where large armies, escpecially if they were mounted or heavily armed, could cross over the Isen.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> About a mile east of the gates of Isengard, a bridge had been built over the Isen where it ran through a very deep channel.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the Isen and the [Adorn](../A/Adorn.md) formed the western boundary of the Kingdom of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), but the triangle of land between Isen, Adorn, and the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) was a contested land, claimed by the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) as well as the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md) during certain periods of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

The Isen formed a natural boundary in the [Gap of Rohan](../G/Gap_of_Rohan.md), and was only crossable at Isengard or at the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md), where the Rohirrim fought a number of great battles against the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md) and [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md)'s [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) in the late Third Age.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

The mouth of Isen was the northernmost landing point of the fleets<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-9">[9]</a></sup> of the [Corsairs](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-10">[10]</a></sup> from [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md) and [Harad](../H/Harad.md) that assailed the coasts of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2758](../T/Third_Age_2758.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-9">[9]</a></sup> and aided the Dunlendings against [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-10">[10]</a></sup>.

When the [Ents](../E/Ents.md) attacked [Isengard](Isengard.md), they dammed the Isen and other springs and streams in [Nan Curunír](../N/Nan_Curunír.md), and dug trenches to divert the river's flow, before breaking the dams and temporarily drowning all of Isengard.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Isen* represents the [Westron](../W/Westron.md) name of the river, albeit archaic in form. It is an old variant form of English "iron". The river's name *Isen* is derived from (the first element of) *[Isengard](Isengard.md)*, the fortress at its source.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-12">[12]</a></sup>

The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-13">[13]</a></sup> name of the river was *[Sîr](../S/Sîr.md)* *[Angren](../A/Anga.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-WifeNote_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WifeNote-1">[1]</a></sup> which means "River of Iron"<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-13">[13]</a></sup>. It is probable that the river's name in Sindarin was also derived from the first element of *Angrenost*, the Sindarin name of the fortress at its source.
