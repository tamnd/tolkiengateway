The **Isen**, called <i><b>Sîr Angren</b></i> in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)<sup id="cite_ref-WifeNote_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WifeNote-1">[1]</a></sup>, was a river that formed the western border of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) from its junction with the [Adorn](/wiki/Adorn) to the outer fences of the fortress of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>.

## Course {#Course}

The source of the river was in the southern [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), first flowing south through [Nan Curunír](/wiki/Nan_Curun%C3%ADr) and across the [Gap of Rohan](/wiki/Gap_of_Rohan) to the edge of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains), where it abruptly bent west and ran to the sea of [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer). At a distance of about 50 [lári](/wiki/L%C3%A1r "Lár") west of the [Gap of Rohan](/wiki/Gap_of_Rohan), the Isen was joined by its tributary river, the [Adorn](/wiki/Adorn).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [North-South Road](/wiki/Greenway "Greenway") ended on the western shore of the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> There, the Isen passed in two arms around a large eyot. The Fords of Isen were the only place south of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) where large armies, escpecially if they were mounted or heavily armed, could cross over the Isen.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> About a mile east of the gates of Isengard, a bridge had been built over the Isen where it ran through a very deep channel.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the Isen and the [Adorn](/wiki/Adorn) formed the western boundary of the Kingdom of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), but the triangle of land between Isen, Adorn, and the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) was a contested land, claimed by the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) as well as the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) during certain periods of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

The Isen formed a natural boundary in the [Gap of Rohan](/wiki/Gap_of_Rohan), and was only crossable at Isengard or at the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen), where the Rohirrim fought a number of great battles against the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) and [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) in the late Third Age.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

The mouth of Isen was the northernmost landing point of the fleets<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-9">[9]</a></sup> of the [Corsairs](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar "Corsairs of Umbar")<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-10">[10]</a></sup> from [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) and [Harad](/wiki/Harad) that assailed the coasts of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758")<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-9">[9]</a></sup> and aided the Dunlendings against [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan)<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-10">[10]</a></sup>.

When the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) attacked [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), they dammed the Isen and other springs and streams in [Nan Curunír](/wiki/Nan_Curun%C3%ADr), and dug trenches to divert the river's flow, before breaking the dams and temporarily drowning all of Isengard.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Isen* represents the [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name of the river, albeit archaic in form. It is an old variant form of English "iron". The river's name *Isen* is derived from (the first element of) *[Isengard](/wiki/Isengard)*, the fortress at its source.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-12">[12]</a></sup>

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-13">[13]</a></sup> name of the river was *[Sîr](/wiki/S%C3%AEr)* *[Angren](/wiki/Anga "Anga")*,<sup id="cite_ref-WifeNote_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WifeNote-1">[1]</a></sup> which means "River of Iron"<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-13">[13]</a></sup>. It is probable that the river's name in Sindarin was also derived from the first element of *Angrenost*, the Sindarin name of the fortress at its source.
