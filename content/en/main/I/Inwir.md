The **Inwir** was the royal clan of the [Teleri](/wiki/Vanyar#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Vanyar"), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. They were the kindred, or house, of [Inwë](/wiki/Inw%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Inwir* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), meaning the "kindred of Inwë" according to [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Its [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate was <i><b>Imrim</b></i>, also appearing as <i><b>Imrin</b></i>, and it contains the plural suffix *-[rim](/wiki/Rim "Rim")*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
