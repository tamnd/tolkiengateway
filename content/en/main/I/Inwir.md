The **Inwir** was the royal clan of the [Teleri](../V/Vanyar.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. They were the kindred, or house, of [Inwë](Inwë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Inwir* is in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), meaning the "kindred of Inwë" according to [Paul Strack](../P/Paul_Strack.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Its [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) cognate was <i><b>Imrim</b></i>, also appearing as <i><b>Imrin</b></i>, and it contains the plural suffix *-[rim](../R/Rim.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
