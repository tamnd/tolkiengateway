**Isilmë** was the second daughter of [Tar-Elendil](../T/Tar-Elendil.md), fourth [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md). Because of the law of <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnatic_primogeniture" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">agnatic primogeniture</a> her younger brother would take up the [Sceptre](../S/Sceptre.md) as [Tar-Meneldur](../T/Tar-Meneldur.md). Almost nothing is known of her life.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *isilmë* means 'moonlight' in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) (from *[isil](Isil.md)* = "moon" and *[-më](../_/-me.md)* = "denotes abstract or intangible things").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
