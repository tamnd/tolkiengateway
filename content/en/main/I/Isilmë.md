**Isilmë** was the second daughter of [Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil), fourth [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor). Because of the law of <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnatic_primogeniture" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">agnatic primogeniture</a> her younger brother would take up the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre) as [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur). Almost nothing is known of her life.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *isilmë* means 'moonlight' in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (from *[isil](/wiki/Isil "Isil")* = "moon" and *[-më](/wiki/-me "-me")* = "denotes abstract or intangible things").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
