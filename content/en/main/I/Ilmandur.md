**Ilmandur** was supposed to be eldest of the three sons of Elendil, who only appears in the third version of the manuscript of "[The Council of Elrond](../T/The_Council_of_Elrond.md)" where [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) tells how the sons of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) governed the three cities of Gondor, but Tolkien struck out the name in pencil.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

According to [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), Ilmandur was probably meant to be the lord of [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md), as [Ilmen](Ilmen.md) in his name means the realm of stars, *giliath* in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), present in the name Osgiliath.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
