**Ilmandur** was supposed to be eldest of the three sons of Elendil, who only appears in the third version of the manuscript of "[The Council of Elrond](/wiki/The_Council_of_Elrond)" where [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) tells how the sons of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) governed the three cities of Gondor, but Tolkien struck out the name in pencil.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

According to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), Ilmandur was probably meant to be the lord of [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath), as [Ilmen](/wiki/Ilmen) in his name means the realm of stars, *giliath* in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), present in the name Osgiliath.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
