<b><i>I, Palantir</i> (April 1964), issue 3</b> is an issue of the fanzine *[I-Palantir](/wiki/I-Palantir)*.

## Contents {#Contents}

- "[The Parting of Arwen](/wiki/The_Parting_of_Arwen)" by Elfride Rivers, a pseudonym of [Marion Zimmer Bradley](/wiki/Marion_Zimmer_Bradley)
- "Haiku Portraits" by Don Studebaker, Ted Johnstone, and others (only one Haiku, in [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) and English)
- "Ethical Patterns in *The Lord of the Rings*" by Patricia Meyer Spacks (reprinted from *Critique* 3:1)
- "No Monroe in Lothlorien" by Arthur Weir (reprinted from *Triode* #17, with some of the comments it drew there, including [those made by Tolkien in a letter to the editor of *Triode*](/wiki/Letter_to_the_editor_of_Triode "Letter to the editor of Triode"))
- "Hobbits and Heroes" by Anthony Curtis (short article with extracts from [interview with J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien"), reprinted from [the Sunday Telegraph, November 10, 1963](</wiki/The_Sunday_Telegraph_(10_November_1963)> "The Sunday Telegraph (10 November 1963)"))<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
