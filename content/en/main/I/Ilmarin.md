**Ilmarin** referred to the mansions of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) and [Varda](/wiki/Varda).<sup id="cite_ref-SI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SI-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

The <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dome" title="wikipedia:Dome">domed</a> halls<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> situated on the summit of [Taniquetil](/wiki/Taniquetil), the highest peak of the world, from where Manwë here set his throne, and [spirits](/wiki/Spirits "Spirits") shaped like [hawks](/wiki/Hawks "Hawks") and [eagles](/wiki/Eagles "Eagles") constantly came with news of events in [Arda](/wiki/Arda). Manwë and Varda "*could look out across the Earth even into the [East](/wiki/East)*".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>When Manwë there ascends his throne and looks forth, if Varda is beside him, he sees further than all other eyes, through mist, and through darkness, and over the leagues of the sea. And if Manwë is with her, Varda hears more clearly than all other ears the sound of voices that cry from east to west, from the hills and the valleys, and from the dark places that Melkor has made upon Earth.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/Valaquenta" title="Valaquenta">Valaquenta</a>: Of the Valar</cite></p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

During the [high feast](/wiki/High_feast "High feast") before the [Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor), the [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar), [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar), and [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) sang before Manwë and Varda in their halls.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ilmarin* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> for "mansion of the high airs".<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) has noted that *Ilmarin* is related to such words as *[Ilmarë](/wiki/Ilmar%C3%AB)* and *[Ilmen](/wiki/Ilmen)*.<sup id="cite_ref-SI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SI-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the poem [Namárië](/wiki/Nam%C3%A1ri%C3%AB), the dwelling of Manwë and Varda is given the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name <i><b>oromardi</b></i> ("lofty halls").<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE16_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE16-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other uses {#Other_uses}

In [one poem](/wiki/Galadriel%27s_Song "Galadriel's Song"), the name *Ilmarin* is used for [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor),<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-7">[7]</a></sup> and in the *[Eärendillinwë](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendillinw%C3%AB)* the name "Hill of Ilmarin" refers to [Oiolossë](/wiki/Oioloss%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Ilmarin and the throne of Manwë could be inspired by [Norse Mythology](/wiki/Norse_Mythology): the gods have a dwelling called <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valaskj%C3%A1lf" title="wikipedia:Valaskjálf">Valaskjálf</a> in Valhalla, in which Odin has his magic throne, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hlidskj%C3%A1lf" title="wikipedia:Hlidskjálf">Hlidskjálf</a>:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Another great abode is there, which is named Valaskjálf; Odin possesses that dwelling; the gods made it and thatched it with sheer silver, and in this hall is the Hlidskjálf, the high-seat so called. Whenever Allfather sits in that seat, he surveys all lands.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i>Gylfaginning</i>, §17</cite></p>
</blockquote>
