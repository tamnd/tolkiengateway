The **Iron Hills** were a range of great hills in the north-east of [Wilderland](/wiki/Rhovanion "Rhovanion").<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> The Hills were the source of the river [Carnen](/wiki/Carnen)<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> and rich in iron<sup id="cite_ref-Relations_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Relations-2">[2]</a></sup>. It was also home to a [Dwarf-realm](/wiki/Dwarf_realms "Dwarf realms") of the [Longbeards](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk "Durin's Folk").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Although the Iron Hills were rich in iron, they were probably not in [gold](/wiki/Gold "Gold"); the [Dwarves of the Iron Hills](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills), although prosperous, were not as wealthy as their other cousins (e.g. the [Kingdom Under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_Under_the_Mountain)).<sup id="cite_ref-Foster_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foster-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Early history {#Early_history}

The [Longbeard](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk "Durin's Folk") [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") colonized the Iron Hills in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> The Hills were their primary source of iron-ore,<sup id="cite_ref-Relations_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Relations-2">[2]</a></sup> and so the [Dwarf-road of Mirkwood](/wiki/Old_Forest_Road "Old Forest Road") ran north-east to the Hills for the dwarf-traders and merchants between Khazad-dûm and the Hills.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

Also in the First Age, some Men who migrated out of the east—"laggard kindred" of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain)—chose to settle in the lands between the Iron Hills and Mirkwood.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

After [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) destroyed [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the Longbeards sealed Khazad-dûm and Orcs took control of the northern Misty Mountains and the Grey Mountains. This cut off communications between the Iron Hills and Khazad-dûm for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981") the last of the Dwarves living in Khazad-dûm fled from the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") who had slain kings [Durin VI](/wiki/Durin_VI) and [Náin I](/wiki/N%C3%A1in_I).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> Most of those who escaped wandered to the north. [Thráin I](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_I), the son of Náin I, moved to the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) and established the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) there.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup> His son [Thorin I](/wiki/Thorin_I) moved from there to the far north to the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) where most of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) had settled, because the mountains were rich and had hardly been explored.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

In [2570](/wiki/Third_Age_2570 "Third Age 2570") dragons began [afflicting the dwarves in the north](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons "War of the Dwarves and Dragons") and in [2589](/wiki/Third_Age_2589 "Third Age 2589") King [Dáin I](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I) was slain by a [cold-drake](/wiki/Cold-drakes "Cold-drakes") outside [his hall](/wiki/D%C3%A1in%27s_Halls "Dáin's Halls") in the Grey Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup> A short time afterwards most of Durin's Folk left the Grey Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Grór_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grór-13">[12]</a></sup> In [2590](/wiki/Third_Age_2590 "Third Age 2590")<sup id="cite_ref-TA2590_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2590-14">[13]</a></sup> [Grór](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3r), the youngest son of Dáin,<sup id="cite_ref-Line_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-15">[14]</a></sup> left the Grey Mountains with many followers and established a Lordship in the Iron Hills<sup id="cite_ref-Grór_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grór-13">[12]</a></sup>. In the same year,<sup id="cite_ref-TA2590_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2590-14">[13]</a></sup> [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r), the oldest son<sup id="cite_ref-Line_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-15">[14]</a></sup> and heir of Dáin, and the rest of the Dwarves of the Grey Mountains moved back to the Lonely Mountain<sup id="cite_ref-Thrór_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thrór-16">[15]</a></sup>.

Over the next few centuries a large quantity of ore was traded between the Dwarves of Erebor and the Dwarves of the Iron Hills,<sup id="cite_ref-Thrór_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thrór-16">[15]</a></sup> until the dragon [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) [destroyed](/wiki/Sack_of_Erebor "Sack of Erebor") the Kingdom under the Mountain and scattered the Longbeards who dwelt there<sup id="cite_ref-Thrór_16-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thrór-16">[15]</a></sup> in [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770")<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[16]</a></sup>. Most of the survivors subsequently went to their kinsmen in the Iron Hills.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[17]</a></sup>

In [2799](/wiki/Third_Age_2799 "Third Age 2799")<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[18]</a></sup> the final battle of the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs) [occurred in the Dimrill Dale](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar "Battle of Azanulbizar"). When reinforcements, led by [Náin](</wiki/N%C3%A1in_(son_of_Gr%C3%B3r)> "Náin (son of Grór)"), arrived from the Iron Hills the battle turned in the favour of the dwarves. With the war over, Dáin led his people home where, after the death of Grór, he became the new [Lord of the Iron Hills](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Iron_Hills).<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[19]</a></sup>

On March 15,[T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) knew already that [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) intended to attack [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) as soon as he felt to be strong enough. He also knew that only the Dwarves of the Iron Hills were there to oppose any attempt from the East to occupy [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) and the northern passes over the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[20]</a></sup>

In [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[21]</a></sup> Lord [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot) led his warriors in the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies) near Erebor<sup id="cite_ref-Dáin_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dáin-23">[22]</a></sup>. The battle was won, but Thorin was slain and Dáin became the new [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain) and [King of the Longbeards](/wiki/Kings_of_Durin%27s_Folk "Kings of Durin's Folk") as Dáin II. He relocated to Erebor and re-established the Kingdom under the Mountain.<sup id="cite_ref-Dáin_23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dáin-23">[22]</a></sup> The Iron Hills passed out of history after this relocation, but probably were not wholly deserted.<sup id="cite_ref-Foster_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foster-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2018: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Iron Hills are an area in the region of &#34;The Dwarf-holds&#34;, ruled by &#34;Lord Náin the Slakeless&#34;, brother of King <a href="/wiki/Thorin_Stonehelm" title="Thorin Stonehelm">Thorin Stonehelm</a>. The hills and the scrub-land connecting to the <a href="/wiki/Dale" title="Dale">Dale</a>-lands are also known collectively as the &#34;Ironfold&#34;. Settled by &#34;Farin Blackmattock&#34; in the First Age, the capital &#34;Járnfast&#34; was destroyed by the fall of <a href="/wiki/Thangorodrim" title="Thangorodrim">Thangorodrim</a> and afterwards rebuilt. During the time it features in the game, Járnfast has been damaged by tremors caused by <a href="/wiki/Mount_Doom" title="Mount Doom">Mount Doom</a>&#39;s recent eruption. It is also the site of a seal that confines a horde of <a href="/wiki/Nameless_things" title="Nameless things">nameless things</a> in &#34;The Howling Pit&#34; below.</dd></dl>
