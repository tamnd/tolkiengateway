**Iminyë** was the first spouse to the [Elf-fathers](/wiki/Elf-fathers) to be [awakened](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves "Awakening of the Elves") at the bay of [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen). She lay by [Imin](/wiki/Imin), the first elf to be awakened. Iminyë later married Imin.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She was one of the [Three Elderwomen of the Elves](/wiki/Three_Elderwomen_of_the_Elves), who [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) taught the art of making [lembas](/wiki/Lembas "Lembas").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
