The **Isle of Seabirds** was a location in the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. It was located in the northern waters, not far from [Falasquil](../F/Falasquil.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Earendel_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendel-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:255</sup> being the place where "all the birds of all waters come at whiles".<sup id="cite_ref-Earendel_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendel-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:253</sup> [Eärendel](../E/Eärendel.md) sailed there with [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md) after learning that [Elwing](../E/Elwing.md) had been transformed into a seabird. He hoped to find her there among the birds, but she looked for him elsewhere. He dwelt there for "three times seven years" until he sailed in his last travel to the margin of the world.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendel_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendel-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:255</sup> In the last outline of "[The Tale of Eärendel](../T/The_Tale_of_Eärendel.md)", his waiting is successful, as Elwing comes to him as a seamew.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendel_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendel-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:260</sup> The isle had a tower, but it is not said if Eärendel built it.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendel_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendel-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:264</sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The concept of the isle, as well as the northern tower, survived in later versions, so in *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* [Elwing's tower](../E/Elwing's_tower.md) is described with a similar role: "*Therefore there was built for her a white tower northward upon the borders of the Sundering Seas; and thither at times all the sea-birds of the earth repaired.*"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
