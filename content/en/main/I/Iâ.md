<i><b>iâ</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning "void, abyss".<sup id="cite_ref-L_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-App_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is a descendant form of *yagā*, deriving from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) YAG.<sup id="cite_ref-L_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L-1">[1]</a></sup>
