**Ian J. Brodie** MNZM ([August 25](../_/25_August.md), [1957](../_/1957.md)) is a professional writer and photographer, who travels around the world to capture iconic landscapes from movies on camera. One of his most successful works is *[The Lord of the Rings Location Guidebook](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Location_Guidebook.md)*, which sold a record 320,000 copies in his native New Zealand alone. A revised edition and an extended, coffeetable edition were also published. Brodie also did research for the New Zealand documentary <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=DNZ:_The_Real_Middle_Earth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="DNZ: The Real Middle Earth (page does not exist)">DNZ: The Real Middle Earth</a></i>.

Brodie was awarded *Membership of the New Zealand Order of Merit* for services to the [New Zealand](../N/New_Zealand.md) tourism industry, which blossomed thanks to his book.

He also cameoed as a Gondorian breadseller in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.
