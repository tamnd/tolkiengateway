**Ian J. Brodie** MNZM ([August 25](/wiki/25_August "25 August"), [1957](/wiki/1957)) is a professional writer and photographer, who travels around the world to capture iconic landscapes from movies on camera. One of his most successful works is *[The Lord of the Rings Location Guidebook](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Location_Guidebook)*, which sold a record 320,000 copies in his native New Zealand alone. A revised edition and an extended, coffeetable edition were also published. Brodie also did research for the New Zealand documentary <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=DNZ:_The_Real_Middle_Earth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="DNZ: The Real Middle Earth (page does not exist)">DNZ: The Real Middle Earth</a></i>.

Brodie was awarded *Membership of the New Zealand Order of Merit* for services to the [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) tourism industry, which blossomed thanks to his book.

He also cameoed as a Gondorian breadseller in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.
