**Iorbad** was a [Southlander](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Pre-Númenóreans") village that was located within the [Southlands](/wiki/Mordor "Mordor") of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) during the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Iorbad was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios).

## History {#History}

During the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the inhabitants of Iorbad were the last to flee to the Watchtower of Ostirith in order to be protected from the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) that were lead by [Adar](/wiki/Adar). The village was presumably destroyed during the [eruption](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(episode)> "Udûn (episode)") of [Orodruin](/wiki/Mount_Doom "Mount Doom") if it was not already destroyed by Orcs as [Hordern](/wiki/Hordern) was.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Iorbad* is most likely a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning *old pathway*, being possibly comprised of the elements *[Iaur](/wiki/Iaur)* ("old") and *[Bâd](/wiki/B%C3%A2d)* ("pathway").
