**Iorbad** was a [Southlander](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md) village that was located within the [Southlands](../M/Mordor.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) during the late [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md). Iorbad was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md).

## History {#History}

During the late [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), the inhabitants of Iorbad were the last to flee to the Watchtower of Ostirith in order to be protected from the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) that were lead by [Adar](../A/Adar.md). The village was presumably destroyed during the [eruption](<../U/Udûn_(episode).md>) of [Orodruin](../M/Mount_Doom.md) if it was not already destroyed by Orcs as [Hordern](../H/Hordern.md) was.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Iorbad* is most likely a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning *old pathway*, being possibly comprised of the elements *[Iaur](Iaur.md)* ("old") and *[Bâd](../B/Bâd.md)* ("pathway").
