**Ivies** were a type of plant. They grew on trees in [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and on rock-chimneys along the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1984-1997: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i></b>

<dl><dd><i><b>Poison Ivy</b></i> (<a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Nelhaew</i>, <a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Neldesangwa</i>), also called <i>Rigána</i>, was a type of ivy described in the game&#39;s books, which grew in deciduous forests. Its oily leaves caused rashes. <sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>
