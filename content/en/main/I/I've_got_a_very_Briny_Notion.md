"**I've got a very Briny Notion**" is the first line of a poem written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) around [1945](../_/1945.md) that is sung by [Alwin Arundel Lowdham](../A/Alwin_Arundel_Lowdham.md) in *[The Notion Club Papers](../T/The_Notion_Club_Papers.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

According to <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Night_65&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Night 65 (page does not exist)">Night 65</a><sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup> in *[The Notion Club Papers](../T/The_Notion_Club_Papers.md)* that were discovered by [Howard Green](../H/Howard_Green.md) in <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Seventh_Age_2012&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Seventh Age 2012 (page does not exist)">2012</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Foreword_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foreword-2">[2]</a></sup> all the main members of the [Notion Club](../N/Notion_Club.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Foreword_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foreword-2">[2]</a></sup> attended a meeting held in [Philip Frankley](../P/Philip_Frankley.md)'s rooms at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Queen%27s_College_(Oxford)" title="wikipedia:The Queen&#39;s College (Oxford)">Queen&#39;s</a> on [8 May](../_/8_May.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Seventh_Age_1987&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Seventh Age 1987 (page does not exist)">1987</a>. After around the first hour of this meeting, [Alwin Arundel Lowdham](../A/Alwin_Arundel_Lowdham.md) arrived at around 9:30 pm, singing:<sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p><span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 1em;">I&#39;ve got a very Briny Notion</span><br/>
<span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 2em;">To drink myself to sleep.<sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup></span>
</p>
</div>

Afterwards, Lowdham was quite restless, refusing to sit down. During the next thirty minutes or so, Lowdham would occasionally burst into attempts at continuing his song, seldom getting further than the aforementioned first two lines:<sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p><span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 1em;">Bring me my bowl, my magic potion!</span><br/>
<span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 2em;">Tonight I’m diving deep.</span><br/>
<span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 3em;">down! down! down!</span><br/>
<span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 2em;">Down where the dream-<a href="../F/Fish.md" title="Fish">fish</a> go.<sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup></span>
</p>
</div>

The other members, notably [Michael George Ramer](../M/Michael_George_Ramer.md), did not take it well. As a result, Lowdham's singing intervals became less frequent. At around 10:00 pm, the conversation, led by Frankley and Lowdham, turned to neologisms.<sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup>

<span></span>
