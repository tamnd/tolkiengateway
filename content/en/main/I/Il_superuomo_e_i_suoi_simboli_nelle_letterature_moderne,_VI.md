**Il superuomo e i suoi simboli nelle letterature moderne, VI** (*The Superman and its Symbols in the Modern Literature*) is an Italian-language book.

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) is treated in the contribution by Carla Giannone: "Il superuomo di Tolkien" ("The Superman of Tolkien"), pp. 139–150.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
