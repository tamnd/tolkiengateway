The **Isle of Balar** was a refugee camp for the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) and [Edain](/wiki/Edain) of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).

The Isle of Balar was located in the great [Bay of Balar](/wiki/Bay_of_Balar) to the south of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> According to legend, it was the eastern tip of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), which had broken off when [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) ferried the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) to [Aman](/wiki/Aman).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Great numbers of pearls could be found in the shallow waters about the island.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Before the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), [Elves of Nargothrond](/wiki/Elves_of_Nargothrond), with ships built by the [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim), explored the Isle of Balar, looking to it as a potential safe haven in time of need.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Some companies of the [Gondolindrim](/wiki/Gondolindrim) had also been sent there by [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) to build ships and send messengers to [Aman](/wiki/Aman), but none of those ships ever arrived there and few of them returned.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the [Fall of the Falas](/wiki/Fall_of_the_Falas), some Falathrim survivors escaped the [Havens](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas "Havens of the Falas") on ships, with [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) and [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad); they reached Balar, where they established a safe refuge, keeping also a foothold of ships at the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion). Hearing this, Turgon once more sent messengers there asking for Círdan's help. Círdan built seven ships to send to the West, but no news of their fate came to Balar; and only one Elf, [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB), survived and returned to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The two refugee camps remained in close contact, and [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) assisted [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) of [Arvernien](/wiki/Arvernien) in building the ship [Vingilot](/wiki/Vingilot) ([Vingilótë](/wiki/Vingil%C3%B3t%C3%AB)), with which he sought for [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), to ask for the pardon of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Many of the Elves dwelling in the Isle of Balar went to [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) until the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) were summoned to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). It is unknown whether or not the Isle of Balar survived beyond the First Age.
