The **Isle of Balar** was a refugee camp for the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) and [Edain](../E/Edain.md) of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).

The Isle of Balar was located in the great [Bay of Balar](../B/Bay_of_Balar.md) to the south of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> According to legend, it was the eastern tip of [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md), which had broken off when [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) ferried the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) to [Aman](../A/Aman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Great numbers of pearls could be found in the shallow waters about the island.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Before the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), [Elves of Nargothrond](../E/Elves_of_Nargothrond.md), with ships built by the [Falathrim](../F/Falathrim.md), explored the Isle of Balar, looking to it as a potential safe haven in time of need.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Some companies of the [Gondolindrim](../G/Gondolindrim.md) had also been sent there by [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) to build ships and send messengers to [Aman](../A/Aman.md), but none of those ships ever arrived there and few of them returned.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the [Fall of the Falas](../F/Fall_of_the_Falas.md), some Falathrim survivors escaped the [Havens](../H/Havens_of_the_Falas.md) on ships, with [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) and [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md); they reached Balar, where they established a safe refuge, keeping also a foothold of ships at the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md). Hearing this, Turgon once more sent messengers there asking for Círdan's help. Círdan built seven ships to send to the West, but no news of their fate came to Balar; and only one Elf, [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md), survived and returned to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The two refugee camps remained in close contact, and [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) assisted [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md) of [Arvernien](../A/Arvernien.md) in building the ship [Vingilot](../V/Vingilot.md) ([Vingilótë](../V/Vingilótë.md)), with which he sought for [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), to ask for the pardon of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Many of the Elves dwelling in the Isle of Balar went to [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) until the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) were summoned to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). It is unknown whether or not the Isle of Balar survived beyond the First Age.
