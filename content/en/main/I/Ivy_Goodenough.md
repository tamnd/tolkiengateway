**Ivy Boffin, née Goodenough**, was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Ivy was the wife of [Buffo Boffin](/wiki/Buffo_Boffin) and mother of [Bosco](/wiki/Bosco_Boffin "Bosco Boffin") ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1167](/wiki/Third_Age_2767 "Third Age 2767")-[S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1258](/wiki/Third_Age_2858 "Third Age 2858")), [Basso](/wiki/Basso_Boffin "Basso Boffin") (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1169](/wiki/Third_Age_2769 "Third Age 2769")), [Briffo](/wiki/Briffo_Boffin "Briffo Boffin") (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1170](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770")), and [Berylla](/wiki/Berylla_Baggins "Berylla Baggins") (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1172](/wiki/Third_Age_2772 "Third Age 2772")).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
