**Ivy Boffin, née Goodenough**, was a Hobbit of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Ivy was the wife of [Buffo Boffin](../B/Buffo_Boffin.md) and mother of [Bosco](../B/Bosco_Boffin.md) ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1167](../T/Third_Age_2767.md)-[S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1258](../T/Third_Age_2858.md)), [Basso](../B/Basso_Boffin.md) (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1169](../T/Third_Age_2769.md)), [Briffo](../B/Briffo_Boffin.md) (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1170](../T/Third_Age_2770.md)), and [Berylla](../B/Berylla_Baggins.md) (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1172](../T/Third_Age_2772.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
