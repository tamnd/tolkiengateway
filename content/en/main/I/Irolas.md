**Irolas** was a [Man](../G/Gondorians.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) and a servant

Irolas is an original creation for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* film. He is portrayed by [Ian Hughes](Ian_Hughes.md).

## History {#History}

Irolas and other Gondorian soldiers watched as Gandalf rode out on [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md), protecting [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) and his troops who were fleeing from [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) by repelling the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) with his staff. Irolas greeted Faramir and the soldiers at the gate of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). He praised the wisdom of [Denethor II](../D/Denethor.md), claiming he had foreseen the [Orc](../O/Orcs.md) attack on Osgiliath. [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) rebutted "Foreseen and done nothing".

After Faramir returned after his assault on Osgiliath, Irolas hurried his gravely injured body through Minas Tirith. He was brought to the court and Denethor emerged, asking whether Faramir had fallen. Irolas told him that they were outnumbered and that none had survived.

Dumbfounded, Irolas witnessed the total breakdown of Denethor as he ordered everyone to abandon their posts.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In the commentary of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* ([Extended Edition](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>)) , it is stated that he was originally to be called [Beregond](../B/Beregond.md), but as the role was reduced, [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) saw little use in still calling him Beregond.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> On a [Decipher Card](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md), Irolas is identified as [Imrahil](Imrahil.md) instead.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
