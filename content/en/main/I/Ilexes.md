<dl><dd>Wood of this tree can be foraged by supreme foresters in <a href="/wiki/Eregion" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien" title="Lothlórien">Lothlórien</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mirkwood" title="Mirkwood">Mirkwood</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Enedwaith" title="Enedwaith">Enedwaith</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>
