**Indor** was the father of [Peleg](/wiki/Peleg) and a grandfather of [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Indor* is in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), most likely being the word *indor* ("master of house, lord") used as a personal name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Its [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognate is also *Indor*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the later legendarium, according to *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*, **[Indor](/wiki/Indor)** was the name of [Aerin](/wiki/Aerin)'s father.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
