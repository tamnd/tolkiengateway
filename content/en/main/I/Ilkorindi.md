The **Ilkorindi** (also **Ilkorins**) was an Elven race according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. It was applied to those [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who were "not of [Kôr](/wiki/K%C3%B4r)" (cf. *[Úmanyar](/wiki/%C3%9Amanyar)* "not of [Aman](/wiki/Aman)") and it referred to the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who stayed in the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands). Their language was [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In later versions of the legendarium, the Ilkorindi seemingly became the [Umanyar](/wiki/Umanyar), specifically the [Avari](/wiki/Avari), [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor) and [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) Elves described in the later Silmarillion.
