The **Ilkorindi** (also **Ilkorins**) was an Elven race according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. It was applied to those [Elves](../E/Elves.md) who were "not of [Kôr](../K/Kôr.md)" (cf. *[Úmanyar](../_/Úmanyar.md)* "not of [Aman](../A/Aman.md)") and it referred to the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) who stayed in the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md). Their language was [Ilkorin](Ilkorin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In later versions of the legendarium, the Ilkorindi seemingly became the [Umanyar](../U/Umanyar.md), specifically the [Avari](../A/Avari.md), [Nandor](../N/Nandor.md) and [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) Elves described in the later Silmarillion.
