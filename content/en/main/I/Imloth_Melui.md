**Imloth Melui** was a valley in [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Roses](/wiki/Roses) grew in Imloth Melui.<sup id="cite_ref-Houses_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Houses-3">[3]</a></sup> People from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) often travelled to the upper valleys of Lossarnach to see the flowers and the blossoms of the fruit trees.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup> [Ioreth](/wiki/Ioreth) was probably raised in Imloth Melui before she travelled to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-Houses_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Houses-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Imloth Melui* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-5">[5]</a></sup> for "sweet flower-valley"<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup> or "lovely flowery vale".<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> It is a compound of *Imloth* ("flowery vale") and *Melui* ("lovely").<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> The element *Imloth* seems to be a compound of *im* ("valley") and *[loth](/wiki/Loth "Loth")* ("flower").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Located in the <a href="/wiki/Lossarnach" title="Lossarnach">Lossarnach</a> area of Eastern <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>, the valley of Imloth Melui also contains a town of the same time. The river <a href="/wiki/Erui" title="Erui">Erui</a> flows through the valley, dividing the city in two. During the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a> it hosts many refugees from the White City, primarily women, children and the elderly. In Lord <a href="/wiki/Forlong" title="Forlong">Forlong</a>&#39;s absence it was ruled by his wife Lady Vanyalos, the sister of Steward <a href="/wiki/Denethor" title="Denethor">Denethor</a>.</dd></dl>
