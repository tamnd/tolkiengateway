**Isengrim Took I** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) who held the position of [Thain](../T/Thain.md) some time between [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [740](../T/Third_Age_2340.md) and [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1083](../T/Third_Age_2683.md).

## History {#History}

There are no direct records of Isengrim I but he was evidently the namesake of [Isengrim II](Isengrim_Took_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-App|Took_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|Took-1">[1]</a></sup> Isengrim I's reign as Thain came between the beginning of the Took line of Thains in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [740](../T/Third_Age_2340.md) and the reign of Isengrim II, which began in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1083](../T/Third_Age_2683.md), after which there were no gaps in the Took family's genealogy.<sup id="cite_ref-App|B2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|B2-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Isengrim* is of [Anglo-Saxon](../O/Old_English.md) origin and comes from *isen* meaning "iron" and *grim* meaning "fierce".

*Isengrim* is the name of a wolf who appears in many medieval epics, including the story of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reynard_the_Fox" title="wikipedia:Reynard the Fox">Reynard the Fox</a> which originated in French folklore.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
