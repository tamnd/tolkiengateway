**Iavas** was the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for the season of autumn. This was also the name of the third season in the [Calendar of Imladris](/wiki/Reckoning_of_Rivendell "Reckoning of Rivendell"); 54 days which fell more or less between [10 August](/wiki/10_August) and [3 October](/wiki/3_October) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word contains a possible Sindarin word for fruit plus the abstract ending <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-as&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-as (page does not exist)">-as</a></i>.

The actual word for fruit is not known but it's most likely \**iaf* (cf. Q. <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Y%C3%A1ve" title="Yáve">yáve</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Yava&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Yava (page does not exist)">yava</a></i>)

Possible [OS](/wiki/OS) form \**jabasse*

## Cognates {#Cognates}

Called [Yávië](/wiki/Y%C3%A1vi%C3%AB) in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).
