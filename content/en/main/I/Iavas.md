**Iavas** was the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name for the season of autumn. This was also the name of the third season in the [Calendar of Imladris](../R/Reckoning_of_Rivendell.md); 54 days which fell more or less between [10 August](../_/10_August.md) and [3 October](../_/3_October.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word contains a possible Sindarin word for fruit plus the abstract ending <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=-as&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-as (page does not exist)">-as</a></i>.

The actual word for fruit is not known but it's most likely \**iaf* (cf. Q. <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="../Y/Yáve.md" title="Yáve">yáve</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Yava&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Yava (page does not exist)">yava</a></i>)

Possible [OS](../O/OS.md) form \**jabasse*

## Cognates {#Cognates}

Called [Yávië](../Y/Yávië.md) in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).
