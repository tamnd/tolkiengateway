**Ingwion**<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Gil_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gil-5">[4]</a></sup> was the son of [Ingwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB), the [High King of the Elves](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Elves), and the leader of the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) in the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath).<sup id="cite_ref-Lat_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lat-6">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Van_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Van-7">[6]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Elena_Kukanova_-_War_of_Wrath.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/d/d6/Elena_Kukanova_-_War_of_Wrath.jpg/200px-Elena_Kukanova_-_War_of_Wrath.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="125" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/d/d6/Elena_Kukanova_-_War_of_Wrath.jpg/300px-Elena_Kukanova_-_War_of_Wrath.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/d/d6/Elena_Kukanova_-_War_of_Wrath.jpg/400px-Elena_Kukanova_-_War_of_Wrath.jpg 2x" data-file-width="800" data-file-height="498"/></a><figcaption><i>War of Wrath</i> by <a href="/wiki/Elena_Kukanova" title="Elena Kukanova">Elena Kukanova</a></figcaption></figure>

Ingwion was born at [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen) before the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey) to Ingwë and his wife [Ilwen](/wiki/Ilwen).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Nom_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nom-1">[1]</a></sup>

Several thousand years later, he led the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) of the [Host of the West](/wiki/Host_of_the_Valar "Host of the Valar") to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) to fight in the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) at the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-Van_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Van-7">[6]</a></sup>

Nothing else is known about his fate, either during the war, or after it.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ingwion* is in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), and is a compound of the name of his father *Ingwë* + the Quenya patronymic suffix *[-ion](/wiki/-ion)* ("son of"), which is translated as "Son of Ingwë".<sup id="cite_ref-Iwe_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Iwe-9">[7]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The name *Ingwion* was changed from an earlier form *Ingwiel*,<sup id="cite_ref-Lat_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lat-6">[5]</a></sup> which was itself changed from *Ingwil*.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[8]</a></sup> The meanings of these earlier forms are unclear, but they all contain the name of his father Ingwë as one of their elements.<sup id="cite_ref-Iwe_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Iwe-9">[7]</a></sup>

However, in *[The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth)*, from a text dating c. [1959](/wiki/1959), the form <i><b>Ingwil</b></i> reappears again.<sup id="cite_ref-Nom_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### *The Book of Lost Tales* {#The_Book_of_Lost_Tales}

The earliest literary precursor of the character of Ingwion was called *Ingil*, according to the early version of the legendarium in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. For more information, see: **[Ingil](/wiki/Ingil)**.

### *The Later Annals of Beleriand* {#The_Later_Annals_of_Beleriand}

According to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") from the *[Later Annals of Beleriand](/wiki/The_Later_Annals_of_Beleriand "The Later Annals of Beleriand")* (late 1930s), Ingwion's (in that text he was called *Ingwiel*) role in the War of Wrath was slightly more fleshed out than in the later texts.

There, Ingwiel, the "prince of all the Elves", was the commander of the force that landed and [captured](/wiki/Battle_of_Eglarest "Battle of Eglarest") the haven of [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest) (in the text it was called *Eglorest*) in one of the first battles of the war, driving away the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) from the shore.<sup id="cite_ref-Elf_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elf-12">[10]</a></sup>
