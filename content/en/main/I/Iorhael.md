**Iorhael** was a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name that [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") used for [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> a translation of [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name *[Maura](/wiki/Maura)*.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning is "old-wise" and comes from *[iaur](/wiki/Iaur "Iaur")* and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sael&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sael (page does not exist)">sael</a></i>.
