**Iorhael** was a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name that [Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn.md) used for [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> a translation of [Westron](../W/Westron.md) name *[Maura](../M/Maura.md)*.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning is "old-wise" and comes from *[iaur](Iaur.md)* and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Sael&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sael (page does not exist)">sael</a></i>.
