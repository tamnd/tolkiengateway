<b>Sir Ian Murray McKellen <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Companions_of_Honour" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">CH</a></b> [CBE](/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire "Order of the British Empire") (born 25 May 1939) played [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in [*The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and [*The Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").

He also wrote forewords to [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee)'s *[The Lord of the Rings Sketchbook](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Sketchbook)* and [Brian Sibley](/wiki/Brian_Sibley)'s *[The Lord of the Rings: The Making of the Movie Trilogy](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Making_of_the_Movie_Trilogy)*.
