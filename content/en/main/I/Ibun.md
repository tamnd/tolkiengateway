**Ibun** was the son of [Mîm](/wiki/M%C3%AEm) and brother of [Khîm](/wiki/Kh%C3%AEm), the last of the [Petty-dwarves](/wiki/Petty-dwarves).<sup id="cite_ref-Mim_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mim-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Ibun lived with his family at [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh). They were seen by the [Gaurwaith](/wiki/Gaurwaith) as they snuck past them carrying heavy sacks. Mîm was captured, and the two brothers fled into the mist. As they ran, Khîm was shot by a wild arrow from the outlaws, but Ibun survived, and shared his father's [House of Ransom](/wiki/House_of_Ransom) with [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) and the outlaws.<sup id="cite_ref-Mim_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mim-1">[1]</a></sup>

One winter, the two [Petty-dwarves](/wiki/Petty-dwarves) went out in search of roots, and were captured by a band of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). To save his life Mîm betrayed Túrin to the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), but Ibun's fate is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Much later, in [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), Mîm told [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin), "I am the last of my people",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> meaning that he outlived both his sons; Ibun either lost his life at the hands of the Orcs, or in some later unrecorded mishap.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

No definition of <i><b>Ibun</b></i> is provided, though it is thought to be [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul).

It is very similar to *Ibn*, the Arabic word for "son".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
