**Ibun** was the son of [Mîm](../M/Mîm.md) and brother of [Khîm](../K/Khîm.md), the last of the [Petty-dwarves](../P/Petty-dwarves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mim_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mim-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Ibun lived with his family at [Amon Rûdh](../A/Amon_Rûdh.md). They were seen by the [Gaurwaith](../G/Gaurwaith.md) as they snuck past them carrying heavy sacks. Mîm was captured, and the two brothers fled into the mist. As they ran, Khîm was shot by a wild arrow from the outlaws, but Ibun survived, and shared his father's [House of Ransom](../H/House_of_Ransom.md) with [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) and the outlaws.<sup id="cite_ref-Mim_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mim-1">[1]</a></sup>

One winter, the two [Petty-dwarves](../P/Petty-dwarves.md) went out in search of roots, and were captured by a band of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md). To save his life Mîm betrayed Túrin to the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), but Ibun's fate is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Much later, in [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md), Mîm told [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md), "I am the last of my people",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> meaning that he outlived both his sons; Ibun either lost his life at the hands of the Orcs, or in some later unrecorded mishap.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

No definition of <i><b>Ibun</b></i> is provided, though it is thought to be [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md).

It is very similar to *Ibn*, the Arabic word for "son".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
