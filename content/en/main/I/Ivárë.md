**Ivárë** was a famous [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") [minstrel](/wiki/Minstrels "Minstrels"), named along with [Tinfang Warble](/wiki/Tinfang_Warble) and [Dairon](/wiki/Daeron "Daeron") as one of the "three most magic players of the Elves", according to the early [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the *[Gnomish Lexicon](/wiki/Gnomish_Lexicon)* he is called a "piper of the sea",<sup id="cite_ref-Ior_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ior-2">[2]</a></sup> possibly indicating that he was one of the [Solosimpi](/wiki/Solosimpi), a group of Elves who in the later legendarium became the [Teleri](/wiki/Falmari "Falmari") of [Aman](/wiki/Aman).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ivárë* is [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> Its [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate is <i><b>Ior</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ior_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ior-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Lay of Leithian](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Leithian)*, the three greatest Elven minstrels are mentioned again as Tinfang, Dairon and [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor); *Maglor* was a later change from an earlier draft, in which he replaced Ivárë.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
