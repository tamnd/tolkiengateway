**Ivárë** was a famous [Elven](../E/Elves.md) [minstrel](../M/Minstrels.md), named along with [Tinfang Warble](../T/Tinfang_Warble.md) and [Dairon](../D/Daeron.md) as one of the "three most magic players of the Elves", according to the early [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the *[Gnomish Lexicon](../G/Gnomish_Lexicon.md)* he is called a "piper of the sea",<sup id="cite_ref-Ior_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ior-2">[2]</a></sup> possibly indicating that he was one of the [Solosimpi](../S/Solosimpi.md), a group of Elves who in the later legendarium became the [Teleri](../F/Falmari.md) of [Aman](../A/Aman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ivárë* is [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> Its [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) cognate is <i><b>Ior</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ior_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ior-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Lay of Leithian](../T/The_Lay_of_Leithian.md)*, the three greatest Elven minstrels are mentioned again as Tinfang, Dairon and [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md); *Maglor* was a later change from an earlier draft, in which he replaced Ivárë.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
