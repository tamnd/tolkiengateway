**Isengrim Took II** was the twenty-second [Thain of the Shire](../T/Thain.md).

## History {#History}

Isengrim II became Thain in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2683](../T/Third_Age_2683.md) and begun to excavate the [Great Smials](../G/Great_Smials.md) in [Green Hill Country](../G/Green_Hill_Country.md), which would be the Thain's seat for generations to come.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

During his service the Shire-reform was established in the [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md), after which every year of the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) would always begin in [Sterday](../S/Sterday.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During this period also [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) reckoned that [pipe-weed](../P/Pipe-weed.md) was first cultivated in the [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md) by [Tobold Hornblower](../T/Tobold_Hornblower.md) <sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Thainship passed on to his son, [Isumbras III](Isumbras_Took_III.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Isengrim" is of [Anglo-Saxon](../O/Old_English.md) origin and comes from *isen* meaning "iron" and *grim* meaning "fierce".

"Isengrim" is the name of a wolf who appears in many medieval epics, including the story of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reynard_the_Fox" title="wikipedia:Reynard the Fox">Reynard the Fox</a> which originated in French folklore.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-5">[5]</a></sup>
