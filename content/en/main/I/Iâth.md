<i><b>iâth</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word and means "fence".<sup id="cite_ref-silmap_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmap-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Doriath#Etymology" title="Doriath">Dor<b>iath</b></a></i> = "Land of the Fence"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Echoriath#Etymology" title="Echoriath">Echor<b>iath</b></a></i> = "Encircling Fence"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Iathrim" title="Iathrim"><b>Iath</b>rim</a></i> = "People of the Fence"
