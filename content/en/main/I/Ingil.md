**Ingil** was the son of [Inwë](/wiki/Inw%C3%AB), the King of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Kor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Cortirion.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/5/50/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Cortirion.png/200px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Cortirion.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="164" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/5/50/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Cortirion.png/300px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Cortirion.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/5/50/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Cortirion.png/400px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Cortirion.png 2x" data-file-width="742" data-file-height="607"/></a><figcaption><i>Cortirion</i> by <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>, depicting the Tower of Ingil</figcaption></figure>

Ingil belonged to the [Inwir](/wiki/Inwir), the royal clan of the [Teleri](/wiki/Vanyar#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Vanyar"), descendants and kin of Ingil's father Inwë,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> who perished leading the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Kôr](/wiki/K%C3%B4r) in the [expedition](/wiki/War_of_Wrath#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "War of Wrath") to the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands) to free the enslaved [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) after the [fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin "Fall of Gondolin"), against the wishes of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar).<sup id="cite_ref-Mer_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mer-3">[3]</a></sup> Due to this, the Elves were forbidden to return to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and instead relocated to [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), where Ingil (who also participated in the war against [Melko](/wiki/Melko)) became their leader.<sup id="cite_ref-Kor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kor-1">[1]</a></sup>

There, he founded the city of [Koromas](/wiki/Koromas) in the [middle](/wiki/Alalmin%C3%B3r%C3%AB "Alalminórë") of the island, and built a [great tower](/wiki/Tirin_na_Gilweth "Tirin na Gilweth"), from which the city's other name, *Kortirion*, came from.<sup id="cite_ref-Kor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kor-1">[1]</a></sup> The island of Tol Eressëa also became known as *[Ingilnórë](/wiki/Ingiln%C3%B3r%C3%AB)*,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup> in honour of Ingil.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

His granddaughter, [Meril-i-Turinqi](/wiki/Meril-i-Turinqi), later became the Lady of Tol Eressëa, possibly after Ingil's eventual return to Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-Mer_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mer-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ingil* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya). It contains the earlier name of his father Inwë, *[Ing](/wiki/Ing)* + the suffix *-'il*, possibly derived from the early primitive Elvish root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=GILI&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="GILI (page does not exist)">GILI</a>, meaning "shine (white)".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate of *Ingil* is <i><b>Gilweth</b></i>, with <i><b>Githilma</b></i> (changed from earlier *Githlon*<sup id="cite_ref-Gno_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gno-10">[8]</a></sup>) being the Gnomish equivalent of his other name <i><b>Isilmo</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[9]</a></sup>

Other names for the character include: (Qenya) <i><b>Ingilmo</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-Qen_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Qen-12">[10]</a></sup> <i><b>Ilwir</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[11]</a></sup> *Veril* (a name which was afterwards applied to his granddaughter, Meril), and (Gnomish) <i><b>Githlim</b></i> (changed from *Ithlim*),<sup id="cite_ref-Qen_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Qen-12">[10]</a></sup> <i><b>Gwethil</b></i> and <i><b>Ithlin</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Nam_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nam-14">[12]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Ingil and Telimektar {#Ingil_and_Telimektar}

In one of the outlines for the continuation of *The Book of Lost Tales*, there appears an entirely different conception of Ingil's fate.

There, it is said that, when Melko broke free of his second imprisonment (after Inwë's expedition to the Great Lands), he went to [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) in order to sow dissension between the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) and the [Solosimpi](/wiki/Solosimpi), who sent for help to Valinor. However, the only aid that came from the Valar was that of [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas), who in secret has sent his son [Telimektar](/wiki/Telimektar) to Tol Eressëa to aid them.

There, Telimektar, with the help of Ingil, surprised Melko and wounded him, and chased him up the [Pine of Tavrobel](/wiki/Pine_of_Tavrobel) all the way to [Ilwë](/wiki/Ilw%C3%AB). However, Melko kept attacking the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and the [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars"), and in the end caused the death of [Urwendi](/wiki/Urwendi) the steerer of the Sun-vessel.

Finally, the Valar decided to cut down the great Pine, which left Melko stranded out of the world.

However, Ingil and Telimektar had to remain in the sky, guarding ceaselessly until Melko's return in the [Great End](/wiki/Great_End). There, [Varda](/wiki/Varda) periodically gives Telimektar stars in order to replace the ones Melko destroys and to remind the world of the Valar's watch - and the constellation of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orion_(constellation)" title="wikipedia:Orion (constellation)">Orion</a> is afterwards seen as Telimektar's image in the sky.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[13]</a></sup>

After Ingil's "ascent into heaven, when in likeness of a great [bee](/wiki/Bees "Bees") bearing honey of flame he followed [Daimort](/wiki/Daimort)",<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[note 3]</a></sup> he acquired the (Gnomish) name <i><b>Gil</b></i>, which was also used as the name of the star <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirius" title="wikipedia:Sirius">Sirius</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Gno_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gno-10">[8]</a></sup> The image of Ingil in the sky, Sirius, was also known as [Nielluin](/wiki/Nielluin).<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[14]</a></sup>

### Later legendarium {#Later_legendarium}

In the later versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), the character evolved to become **[Ingwion](/wiki/Ingwion)**, the son of [Ingwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB).
