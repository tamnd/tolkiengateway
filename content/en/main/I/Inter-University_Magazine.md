The <i><b>Inter-University Magazine</b></i> was a Catholic journal, associated with the Jesuit college of the [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford).<sup id="cite_ref-IUM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IUM-1">[1]</a></sup>

Among [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s manuscripts is found a note about publishing his poem "[Tinfang Warble](</wiki/Tinfang_Warble_(poem)> "Tinfang Warble (poem)")" in the "I.U.M\[agazine\]".<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-2">[2]</a></sup> Another poem published by Tolkien in this magazine was "[The Grey Bridge of Tavrobel](/wiki/The_Grey_Bridge_of_Tavrobel)" (in the same or in another issue).<sup id="cite_ref-IUM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IUM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth) identified the acronym as likely referring to the *Inter-University Magazine*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> According to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), the publication of the former poem took place in [1927](/wiki/1927).<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-2">[2]</a></sup> However, he has not been able to retrace how this year was established, and [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) have therefore suggested "a less absolute publication date" for both poems, to sometime between ?[1925](/wiki/1925) and ?[1927](/wiki/1927).<sup id="cite_ref-IUM_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IUM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> As to date, no physical copy of the journal containing any of the poems has been found.<sup id="cite_ref-IUM_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IUM-1">[1]</a></sup>
