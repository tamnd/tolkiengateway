The **Ithil-stone** was the *[palantír](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* that stood in [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur)'s ancient fortress of [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil), placed there soon after the founding of the city. The stone was captured by the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2002](/wiki/Third_Age_2002 "Third Age 2002") when the city [fell to them](/wiki/Second_Fall_of_Minas_Ithil "Second Fall of Minas Ithil")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and became known as [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul). The stone was used by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) to entrap [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) and deceive [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is likely that the stone was destroyed in the fall of [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr).
