**Ioreth** was a woman of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

Ioreth was originally from [Imloth Melui](/wiki/Imloth_Melui) in [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach), but moved to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) and worked in the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing). By [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") she was the oldest woman working there and was well versed in old lore. Upon seeing the illness-stricken [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), she said that she wished there was still a king in Gondor, remarking "For it is said in old lore: *The hands of the king are the hands of a healer*." [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) heard this, and brought [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) to the Houses of Healing, where he was able to heal many of the sick. Ioreth was very talkative, which annoyed Aragorn and Gandalf.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ioreth* means "old woman" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin); *[iaur](/wiki/Iaur "Iaur")* ("old") plus feminine ending <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-eth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-eth (page does not exist)">-eth</a></i>. In draft, Tolkien had spelt her name *Yoreth*.<sup id="cite_ref-Joukes_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Joukes-2">[2]</a></sup>

Like "[Gamling](/wiki/Gamling)", it was specifically chosen to suit the character of the old nurse.<sup id="cite_ref-Joukes_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Joukes-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1956: <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1956_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1956 radio series)">The Lord of the Rings (1956 radio series)</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Prunella_Scales" title="Prunella Scales">Prunella Scales</a> is credited as Ioreth in the fifth episode of the second run, &#34;Minas Tirith and Mount Doom&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-RT1727_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RT1727-3">[3]</a></sup> As Scales was in her twenties at the time, it is unknown to what extent the &#34;old lady&#34; aspect was used.</dd></dl>

<b>1979: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1979 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1979 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Pat_Franklyn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Pat Franklyn (page does not exist)">Pat Franklyn</a> is credited as &#34;The Old Woman&#34;. She is not referred to as Ioreth at any time, but her lines are lifted from Ioreth&#39;s.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Pauline_Letts" title="Pauline Letts">Pauline Letts</a> plays Ioreth.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> in the absence of <a href="/wiki/Bergil" title="Bergil">Bergil</a> she is the one who provides Aragorn with the leaves he uses in his ministrations.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/P%C3%A1n_prste%C5%88ov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series)" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The voice of Ioreth is provided by Vilma Jamnická.</dd></dl>

<b>2015<i>: </i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a><i>:</i></b><i></i>

<dl><dd>Ioreth is found in the Houses of Healing after the battle, where she asks the player to assist her with mixing herbs and delivering medicine to the wounded.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>She also found in the High Hall of the Houses of Healing during the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Midsummer" title="Midsummer">Midsummer</a> Festivals, where she asks you to scare off the noisy revellers and the dispose of the empty bottles.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>
