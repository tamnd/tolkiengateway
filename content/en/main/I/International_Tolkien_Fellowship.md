The **International Tolkien Fellowship** is a loose group of [Tolkien Societies](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md) around the globe which started during the convergence of Societies in [Oxonmoot](../O/Oxonmoot.md). The Societies who form the ITF seek to communicate with each other, share their news, announce their activities and maintain the interest of the [Tolkien fandom](../T/Tolkien_fandom.md).

Presently it has the form of a facebook group which seeks to provide a comprehensive list of worldwide activities. It published the list of events in [Amon Hen](<../A/Amon_Hen_(journal).md>).
