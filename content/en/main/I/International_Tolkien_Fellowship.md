The **International Tolkien Fellowship** is a loose group of [Tolkien Societies](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies) around the globe which started during the convergence of Societies in [Oxonmoot](/wiki/Oxonmoot). The Societies who form the ITF seek to communicate with each other, share their news, announce their activities and maintain the interest of the [Tolkien fandom](/wiki/Tolkien_fandom).

Presently it has the form of a facebook group which seeks to provide a comprehensive list of worldwide activities. It published the list of events in [Amon Hen](</wiki/Amon_Hen_(journal)> "Amon Hen (journal)").
