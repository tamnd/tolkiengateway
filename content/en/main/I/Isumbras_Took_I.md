**Isumbras Took** was a the thirteenth [Thain](../T/Thain.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Isumbras was elected Thain in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [740](../T/Third_Age_2340.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> after [Gorhendad Oldbuck](../G/Gorhendad_Oldbuck.md) relocated to [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> From then on, the Thain was a title held by the [Took Family](../T/Took_Family.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Took family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The name *Isumbras* is of [Anglo-Saxon](../O/Old_English.md) and French origin and means "iron-arm". It contains the Anglo-Saxon and Middle High German element *[isen](Isen.md)* ("iron") and the French element *bras* ("arm").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) likely got the name from <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Isumbras" title="wikipedia:Sir Isumbras">Sir Isumbras</a></i>, an English medieval romance about a man who suffers a lifetime of hardship but who finds happiness in his latter years.
