The **Esgaliant** (or **Iant Iaur** as it became known in later days) was a bridge over the [Esgalduin](/wiki/Esgalduin).

The bridge was great and made of stone,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> part of the [great road](</wiki/East_Road_(Beleriand)> "East Road (Beleriand)") that ran north of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) and connected [Brithiach](/wiki/Brithiach) to [Arossiach](/wiki/Arossiach).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The **Bridge of Esgalduin** was made long before [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) came to [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), perhaps by [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After terror entered the [Ered Gorgoroth](/wiki/Ered_Gorgoroth) and the land to its south became deserted, the road and bridge fell into decay, and it was referred to as Iant Iaur, the Old Bridge.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Iant Iaur* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Old Bridge", from *[iant](/wiki/Iant "Iant")* ("bridge") and *[iaur](/wiki/Iaur "Iaur")* ("old"). Therefore *Esgal-iant* clearly means "Bridge of Esgalduin".<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
