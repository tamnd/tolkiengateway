The **Iron Mountains** or **Ered Engrin** were an immense mountain range in the far north of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), stretching almost from one end of the continent to the other, though falling short of the sea on either side.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Of old the Iron Mountains came close to touching the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) (*Ered Luin*) of the West and the Red Mountains (*[Orocarni](../O/Orocarni.md)*) of the East,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but in the wars between the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) the mountain range was distorted. Melkor himself created the mountains as a fence for his citadel of [Utumno](../U/Utumno.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Melkor's great fortresses of [Angband](../A/Angband.md) and [Utumno](../U/Utumno.md) were built in the mountains. North of the range lay the regions of ever-lasting cold.

After the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) the Iron Mountains were broken and disappeared, at least the part of their length that lay directly north of Beleriand.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

[Karen Wynn Fonstad](../K/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad.md), in her book *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](../T/The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth.md)*, imagined that remnants of the Iron Mountains after the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) could perhaps be all the northern mountain ranges of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md): the [Mountains of Angmar](../M/Mountains_of_Angmar.md), the [Ered Mithrin](../G/Grey_Mountains.md), and the [Iron Hills](Iron_Hills.md), still occupied by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Dragons](../D/Dragons.md) until the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> However, this depiction predates publication of *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](../T/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md)*, where it is revealed that the Grey Mountains and Iron Hills existed in the First Age independently of the Iron Mountains. It was during this age that the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md) first colonized the Iron Hills. They also considered the Grey Mountains, which lay between Khazad-dûm and the Iron Hills, to be part of their territory.<sup id="cite_ref-relations_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-relations-5">[5]</a></sup>
