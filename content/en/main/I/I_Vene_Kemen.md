<i><b>I Vene Kemen</b></i> is the title of an early drawing by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). The illustration is a "map" of the world, envisioned in the form of a ship, as conceived in the earliest version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-1">[1]</a></sup> Locations and concepts featured in the drawing include:

- *Nūme* = "<a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/West" title="West">West</a>"
- *[Valinor](/wiki/Valinor)*
- *[Taniquetil](/wiki/Taniquetil)*
- *Harmalin*, later *[Arvalin](/wiki/Arvalin)*
- *i aldas* = "[The Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor")"
- *(T?)oros valinoriva*, likely referring to the [Mountains of Valinor](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri "Pelóri")
- *Tolli Kimpelear*, likely referring to the [Twilit Isles](/wiki/Twilit_Isles)
- *[Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa)* = "The Lonely Isle"
- *I Tolli Kuruvar* = "The [Magic Isles](/wiki/Magic_Isles)"
- *Haloisi Velike* = "The [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer")"
- *Ô* = "The [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer")"
- *I Nori Landar*, likely meaning "The [Great Lands](/wiki/Middle-earth "Middle-earth")"
- *Koivienéni*, later *[Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen)*
- *[Palisor](/wiki/Palisor)*
- *Sil* = "[Moon](/wiki/Moon)"
- *Ûr* = "[Sun](/wiki/Sun)"
- *Luvier* = "Clouds"
- *Oronto* = "East"
- *[Vaitya](/wiki/Vaitya)*, *[Ilwë](/wiki/Ilw%C3%AB)* and *[Vilna](/wiki/Vilna)*, layers of air
- *[Ulmonan](/wiki/Ulmonan)*
- *[Uin](/wiki/Uin)*
- *Vai* or *Neni Erùmear* = "[Outermost Waters](/wiki/Ekkaia "Ekkaia")"

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) has suggested that *I Vene Kemen* is a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) name perhaps meaning "The Shape of the Earth" or "The Vessel of the Earth".<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-1">[1]</a></sup> The element *vene* is likely related to the root VENE ("shape, cut out, scoop") and its derivative *venë* ("small boat, vessel, dish"). For the element *kemen*, the *[Qenya Lexicon](/wiki/Qenya_Lexicon)* contains the words *kemi* ("earth, soil") and *kemen* ("soil"), derived from the root KEME.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
