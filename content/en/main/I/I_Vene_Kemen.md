<i><b>I Vene Kemen</b></i> is the title of an early drawing by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). The illustration is a "map" of the world, envisioned in the form of a ship, as conceived in the earliest version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-1">[1]</a></sup> Locations and concepts featured in the drawing include:

- *Nūme* = "<a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../W/West.md" title="West">West</a>"
- *[Valinor](../V/Valinor.md)*
- *[Taniquetil](../T/Taniquetil.md)*
- *Harmalin*, later *[Arvalin](../A/Arvalin.md)*
- *i aldas* = "[The Trees](../T/Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md)"
- *(T?)oros valinoriva*, likely referring to the [Mountains of Valinor](../P/Pelóri.md)
- *Tolli Kimpelear*, likely referring to the [Twilit Isles](../T/Twilit_Isles.md)
- *[Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md)* = "The Lonely Isle"
- *I Tolli Kuruvar* = "The [Magic Isles](../M/Magic_Isles.md)"
- *Haloisi Velike* = "The [Great Sea](../B/Belegaer.md)"
- *Ô* = "The [Sea](../B/Belegaer.md)"
- *I Nori Landar*, likely meaning "The [Great Lands](../M/Middle-earth.md)"
- *Koivienéni*, later *[Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md)*
- *[Palisor](../P/Palisor.md)*
- *Sil* = "[Moon](../M/Moon.md)"
- *Ûr* = "[Sun](../S/Sun.md)"
- *Luvier* = "Clouds"
- *Oronto* = "East"
- *[Vaitya](../V/Vaitya.md)*, *[Ilwë](Ilwë.md)* and *[Vilna](../V/Vilna.md)*, layers of air
- *[Ulmonan](../U/Ulmonan.md)*
- *[Uin](../U/Uin.md)*
- *Vai* or *Neni Erùmear* = "[Outermost Waters](../E/Ekkaia.md)"

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) has suggested that *I Vene Kemen* is a [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) name perhaps meaning "The Shape of the Earth" or "The Vessel of the Earth".<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-1">[1]</a></sup> The element *vene* is likely related to the root VENE ("shape, cut out, scoop") and its derivative *venë* ("small boat, vessel, dish"). For the element *kemen*, the *[Qenya Lexicon](../Q/Qenya_Lexicon.md)* contains the words *kemi* ("earth, soil") and *kemen* ("soil"), derived from the root KEME.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
