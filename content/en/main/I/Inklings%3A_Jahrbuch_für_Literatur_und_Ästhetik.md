<i><b>Inklings: Jahrbuch für Literatur und Ästhetik</b></i> (English: "Annual Review of Literature and Aesthetics") is a journal focussing on studies of fantastic literature and the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings). It has been published by the Inklings Gesellschaft on a regular basis since [1983](/wiki/1983), and contains articles both in German and English.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
