<i><b>InQuest</b></i>, later known as **InQuest Gamer**, was a magazine about trading card games. In that capacity, it often covered the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md) and [The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md).
