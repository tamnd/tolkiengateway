<i><b>InQuest</b></i>, later known as **InQuest Gamer**, was a magazine about trading card games. In that capacity, it often covered the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game) and [The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game).
