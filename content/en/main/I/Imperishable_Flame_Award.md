**The Imperishable Flame Awards**, named after the concept of the [Flame Imperishable](/wiki/Flame_Imperishable), are presented by the [North East Tolkien Society](/wiki/North_East_Tolkien_Society) in recognition of Scholarship, Creativity and the building of fan community resources celebrating and honoring [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and The [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings).

<br> The 2006/2007 winners were:

<dl><dt>Category 1 - Tolkien/Inklings Scholarship</dt>
<dd><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Diana_Pavlac_Glyer" title="Diana Pavlac Glyer">Diana Pavlac Glyer</a> for <i><a href="/wiki/The_Company_They_Keep" title="The Company They Keep">The Company They Keep: C.S. Lewis, J.R.R. Tolkien as Writers in Community</a></i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Category 2 - Tolkien/Inklings Inspired Creativity</dt>
<dd><a href="/wiki/James_Dunning" title="James Dunning">James Dunning</a> for 40 Years of Tolkien Art</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Category 3 - Tolkien/Inkling Fan Community Award</dt>
<dd><a href="/wiki/Pieter_Collier" title="Pieter Collier">Pieter Collier</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Hyarion" title="Hyarion">Hyarion</a> for Organizing <i>The Children Of Hurin Release Party</i></dd></dl>
