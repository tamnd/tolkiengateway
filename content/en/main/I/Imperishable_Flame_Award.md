**The Imperishable Flame Awards**, named after the concept of the [Flame Imperishable](../F/Flame_Imperishable.md), are presented by the [North East Tolkien Society](../N/North_East_Tolkien_Society.md) in recognition of Scholarship, Creativity and the building of fan community resources celebrating and honoring [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and The [Inklings](Inklings.md).

<br> The 2006/2007 winners were:

<dd><a class="mw-redirect" href="../D/Diana_Pavlac_Glyer.md" title="Diana Pavlac Glyer">Diana Pavlac Glyer</a> for <i><a href="../T/The_Company_They_Keep.md" title="The Company They Keep">The Company They Keep: C.S. Lewis, J.R.R. Tolkien as Writers in Community</a></i></dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Category 2 - Tolkien/Inklings Inspired Creativity</dt>
<dd><a href="../J/James_Dunning.md" title="James Dunning">James Dunning</a> for 40 Years of Tolkien Art</dd></dl>

<dl><dt>Category 3 - Tolkien/Inkling Fan Community Award</dt>
<dd><a href="../P/Pieter_Collier.md" title="Pieter Collier">Pieter Collier</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="../H/Hyarion.md" title="Hyarion">Hyarion</a> for Organizing <i>The Children Of Hurin Release Party</i></dd></dl>
