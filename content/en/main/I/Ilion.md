**Ilion** was the father of [Ingwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB), who would later become the King of the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar). He was one of the [Minyar](/wiki/Minyar), a direct descendant of [Imin](/wiki/Imin) and [Iminyë](/wiki/Iminy%C3%AB) from eldest son to eldest son, belonging to the fourth generation of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) (i.e. a great-grandson of Imin).<sup id="cite_ref-Ilion_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ilion-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is unknown if he joined the [Great March](/wiki/Great_March) or remained in [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ilion* seems to be in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), and may mean "all-son", from the [root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=IL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="IL (page does not exist)">IL</a> ("all") + -*[ion](/wiki/Ion "Ion")* (masculine patronymic).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
