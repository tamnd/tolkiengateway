<i><b>inzil</b></i> is an [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) word meaning "flower, lily".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*inzil* is similar to [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ini%C3%B0il&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Iniðil (page does not exist)">iniðil</a></i> and [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Indil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Indil (page does not exist)">indil</a></i> ("lily, or other large single flower").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Inzilad%C3%BBn" title="Inziladûn"><b>Inzil</b>adûn</a></i> = "Flower of the West"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Inzilb%C3%AAth" title="Inzilbêth"><b>Inzil</b>bêth</a></i> = "Flower-word"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Rothinzil" title="Rothinzil">Roth<b>inzil</b></a></i> = "Foam-flower"
