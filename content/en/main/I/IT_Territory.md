**IT Territory** (Russian: Территория ИТ) is a Russian company, which is specialized in developing and publishing MMO's. They published [The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md) in Russia and maintain its' servers ([Fornost](../F/Fornost.md#Portrayal_in_Adaptations) and [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md#Portrayal_in_Adaptations)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
