**Isumbras Took II** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) who held the position of [Thain](../T/Thain.md) some time between [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [740](../T/Third_Age_2340.md) and [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1083](../T/Third_Age_2683.md).

## History {#History}

There are no direct records of Isumbras II, but he evidently existed between his namesake [Isumbras I](Isumbras_Took_I.md), who founded the Took line of Thains in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [740](../T/Third_Age_2340.md), and the later [Isumbras III](Isumbras_Took_III.md).<sup id="cite_ref-App|Took_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|Took-1">[1]</a></sup> Isumbras II's reign could have occurred no later than [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1083](../T/Third_Age_2683.md), when [Isengrim II](Isengrim_Took_II.md) became Thain, since there were no subsequent gaps in the Took family's genealogy.<sup id="cite_ref-App|B2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|B2-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Isumbras" is of French and [Anglo-Saxon](../O/Old_English.md) origin and means "iron-arm". It comes from the French *bras* meaning "arm", and the Anglo-Saxon and Middle High German word *[isen](Isen.md)* which means "iron".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) possibly got the name from <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Isumbras" title="wikipedia:Sir Isumbras">Sir Isumbras</a></i>, an English medieval romance about a man who suffers a lifetime of hardship but who finds happiness in his latter years.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
