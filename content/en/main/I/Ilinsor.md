**Ilinsor** was a [Vala](/wiki/Vala) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. He was a *[Súru](/wiki/Sylphs "Sylphs")*, one of the servants of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) and [Varda](/wiki/Varda), and later chosen as a steersman of the [Moon](/wiki/Moon) for [Silmo](/wiki/Silmo) could not do it. The greater brightness of the Sun made him envious, therefore he chased the stars.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
