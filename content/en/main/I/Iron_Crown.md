The **Iron Crown** was the crown forged by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) to hold the three [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils).

## History {#History}

### First Age {#First_Age}

The Iron Crown was forged by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in the year [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1495](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1495 "Year of the Trees 1495") on his return to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) to hold the three [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils). He bore it throughout the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), and would not remove it, although it was a great burden to him.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [466](/wiki/First_Age_466 "First Age 466"), [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) cast a spell of sleep upon Morgoth, and as he fell to the ground, the Iron Crown rolled away. [Beren](/wiki/Beren) retrieved a Silmaril from it using the knife [Angrist](/wiki/Angrist). He attempted to take a second, but the knife broke, and Morgoth stirred.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> After Morgoth's defeat by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) in the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), the crown was beaten into a collar for his neck.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

Ages later, [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) mentioned the "Iron Crown in [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim)" to [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) while mentioning [Beren](/wiki/Beren)'s renowned [Quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<b><a href="/wiki/2024" title="2024">2024</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a></i>: <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_-_Season_2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power - Season 2 (page does not exist)">Season 2</a></b>

<dl><dd>Following the defeat of <a href="/wiki/Morgoth" title="Morgoth">Morgoth</a>, <a href="/wiki/Adar_(The_Rings_of_Power)" title="Adar (The Rings of Power)">Adar</a> was present Sauron re-fired the Crown to fit him, removing the spots for <a href="/wiki/Silmarils" title="Silmarils">Silmarils</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Sauron attempted to crown himself leader of the <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orc</a> forces but the Orcs believed him to be inferior to Morgoth. Adar stabbed him with the Crown and the Orcs swarmed and temporarily killed Sauron, choosing to follow Adar instead.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>
