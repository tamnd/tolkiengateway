**Sir Ian Barry Mune** (b. [1941](/wiki/1941)) played a [bounder](/wiki/Shirriffs "Shirriffs") (specifically, the one whose head was cut off by a [Ringwraith](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl")) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*. He also served as an additional second unit director; he describes one of his works as "\[i\]t was the bit where the hobbits were being dragged from the burning tavern by the orcs"<sup><a class="external autonumber" href="http://www.pukeariki.com/en/stories/arts/titokowarufilm.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[1]</a></sup>, which made it into the trailer for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*. It is unclear as to what scene he is referring; no scene matching his description is in the trailer. He could be referring to a scene shot for the [The Mirror of Galadriel](</wiki/The_Mirror_of_Galadriel_(scene)> "The Mirror of Galadriel (scene)") scene in *The Fellowship of the Ring*.

In June [2007](/wiki/2007), he and 14 other actors filed <a class="external text" href="https://us.imdb.com/news/ni0058227/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">a lawsuit</a> against [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) over merchandising royalties.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The action was settled out of court in October [2008](/wiki/2008).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
