**Idril's secret way** was a tunnel under [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) that [Idril](/wiki/Idril), [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), and their allies built in secret, and it was through this that the survivors of the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin) escaped.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
