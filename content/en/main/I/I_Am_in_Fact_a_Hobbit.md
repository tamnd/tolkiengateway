## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Whether you&#39;ve been a fan for years or you&#39;ve just recently been hooked by the blockbuster <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)">Lord of the Rings movies</a>, &#34;I Am in Fact a Hobbit&#34; is an excellent starting point into the life and work of <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>. This indispensable and concise introduction to the career of Tolkien includes: a biographical chapter about the man who was a brilliant Oxford professor and <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Catholic Christian</a>, loving father and devoted husband and close friend of <a href="/wiki/C.S._Lewis" title="C.S. Lewis">C.S. Lewis</a>; overviews and discussions of his best-selling popular works such as <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a> and <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a>, his often overlooked academic works and his children&#39;s books such as <a href="/wiki/Roverandom" title="Roverandom">Roverandom</a> and <a href="/wiki/Mr._Bliss" title="Mr. Bliss">Mr. Bliss</a>; a detailed chronology of the important events and times of his life and career; an extensive listing of his works, both published and unpublished; and a resource bibliography of the best works about him.
</p>
</blockquote>
