**Sir Ian Holm Cuthbert** [CBE](/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire "Order of the British Empire") ([12 September](/wiki/12_September) [1931](/wiki/1931) - [19 June](/wiki/19_June), [2020](/wiki/2020)) voiced [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) in the BBC radio adaptation of *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)")*. Years later he played [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), and Old Bilbo in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").

Ian Holm was the father of [Harry Holm](/wiki/Harry_Holm), who played [Elanor Fairbairns](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner "Elanor Gardner") in the 1981 radio series.
