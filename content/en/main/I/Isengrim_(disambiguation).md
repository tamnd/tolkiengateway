**Isengrim** is a name born by three [Thains](/wiki/Thain "Thain") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), all from the [Took Family](/wiki/Took_Family):

- [Isengrim Took I](/wiki/Isengrim_Took_I), one of the eight unnamed Thains of the Took line, who reigned sometime between [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [740](/wiki/Third_Age_2340 "Third Age 2340") and [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1083](/wiki/Third_Age_2683 "Third Age 2683").
- [Isengrim Took II](/wiki/Isengrim_Took_II) ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1020](/wiki/Third_Age_2620 "Third Age 2620") - [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1122](/wiki/Third_Age_2722 "Third Age 2722")), twenty-second Thain of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") who begins the excavation of [Great Smials](/wiki/Great_Smials).
- [Isengrim Took III](/wiki/Isengrim_Took_III) ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1232](/wiki/Third_Age_2832 "Third Age 2832") - [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1330](/wiki/Third_Age_2930 "Third Age 2930")), eldest son of the [Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took") and twenty-seventh Thain of the Shire.
