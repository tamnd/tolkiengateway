**Isengrim** is a name born by three [Thains](../T/Thain.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), all from the [Took Family](../T/Took_Family.md):

- [Isengrim Took I](Isengrim_Took_I.md), one of the eight unnamed Thains of the Took line, who reigned sometime between [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [740](../T/Third_Age_2340.md) and [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1083](../T/Third_Age_2683.md).
- [Isengrim Took II](Isengrim_Took_II.md) ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1020](../T/Third_Age_2620.md) - [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1122](../T/Third_Age_2722.md)), twenty-second Thain of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) who begins the excavation of [Great Smials](../G/Great_Smials.md).
- [Isengrim Took III](Isengrim_Took_III.md) ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1232](../T/Third_Age_2832.md) - [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1330](../T/Third_Age_2930.md)), eldest son of the [Old Took](../G/Gerontius_Took.md) and twenty-seventh Thain of the Shire.
