**Inzilbêth** was a [Queen](/wiki/Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Queen of Númenor") of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).

## History {#History}

She was the daughter of [Lindórië](/wiki/Lind%C3%B3ri%C3%AB), who was the sister of [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)> "Eärendur (Lord of Andúnië)"), the fifteenth [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië"),<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup> and a descendant of [Tar-Calmacil](/wiki/Tar-Calmacil).<sup id="cite_ref-Ar-Gimilzor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ar-Gimilzor-3">[3]</a></sup>

Her family was secretly [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) and she had been taught by her mother. As such, when [Ar-Gimilzôr](/wiki/Ar-Gimilz%C3%B4r) asked her for marriage, she refused but Ar-Gimilzôr did not allow his wishes to be denied.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup> There was no love between Inzilbêth and her husband or between her sons. Her elder son [Inziladûn](/wiki/Inzilad%C3%BBn), who would take the royal title of [Tar-Palantir](/wiki/Tar-Palantir), followed her teachings rather than his father's when he became King of Númenor. Her younger son [Gimilkhâd](/wiki/Gimilkh%C3%A2d) however, would follow in the footsteps of his father.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ar-Gimilzor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ar-Gimilzor-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Inzilbêth* is an [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Flower-word" and is a compound of *[inzil](/wiki/Inzil "Inzil")* ("flower") and *[bêth](/wiki/B%C3%AAth "Bêth")* ("word").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
