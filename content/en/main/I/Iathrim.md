The **Iathrim** or **Elves of Doriath** were those [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) that lived in the broad forests of central [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), [Neldoreth](/wiki/Neldoreth), and [Region](/wiki/Forest_of_Region "Forest of Region"), under the Kingship of [Elu Thingol](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") and later [Dior Eluchíl](/wiki/Dior "Dior"). These people, whose [Queen](/wiki/Melian "Melian") was of the order of the [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar) and whose King was the High King of the Sindar, were held to be the highest and noblest of the [Grey-elven](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") kindred.

The [Marchwardens](/wiki/Marchwardens) were those Elves assigned to guarding the borders of the forest of Doriath.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the [Ruin of Doriath](/wiki/Ruin_of_Doriath), [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing) led the survivors to the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Iathrim* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "People of the Fence", as is a compound of *[iath](/wiki/Iath "Iath")* ("fence") and the class-plural suffix *-[rim](/wiki/Rim "Rim")*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales "The Book of Lost Tales") elves of [Artanor](/wiki/Artanor) (Doriath) are called Woodland Elves, as well as Woodelves, Elves of the wood(land) or Elves of the forest.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> On the [First 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_First_%27Silmarillion%27_Map "The First 'Silmarillion' Map") elves of Doriath are called [Wood-elves](/wiki/Wood-elves).
