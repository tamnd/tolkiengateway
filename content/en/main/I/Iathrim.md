The **Iathrim** or **Elves of Doriath** were those [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) that lived in the broad forests of central [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), [Neldoreth](../N/Neldoreth.md), and [Region](../F/Forest_of_Region.md), under the Kingship of [Elu Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) and later [Dior Eluchíl](../D/Dior.md). These people, whose [Queen](../M/Melian.md) was of the order of the [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md) and whose King was the High King of the Sindar, were held to be the highest and noblest of the [Grey-elven](../S/Sindar.md) kindred.

The [Marchwardens](../M/Marchwardens.md) were those Elves assigned to guarding the borders of the forest of Doriath.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the [Ruin of Doriath](../R/Ruin_of_Doriath.md), [Elwing](../E/Elwing.md) led the survivors to the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Iathrim* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "People of the Fence", as is a compound of *[iath](Iath.md)* ("fence") and the class-plural suffix *-[rim](../R/Rim.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md) elves of [Artanor](../A/Artanor.md) (Doriath) are called Woodland Elves, as well as Woodelves, Elves of the wood(land) or Elves of the forest.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> On the [First 'Silmarillion' Map](../T/The_First_'Silmarillion'_Map.md) elves of Doriath are called [Wood-elves](../W/Wood-elves.md).
