**Irmo**, commonly called *[Lórien](</wiki/L%C3%B3rien_(Valinor)> "Lórien (Valinor)")* after his place of dwelling, was a [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") who was known as "the master of visions and dreams."

## History {#History}

Irmo was the younger brother of [Námo](/wiki/Mandos "Mandos"), who was also more commonly known by the name of his dwelling place, *[Mandos](/wiki/Mandos)*. Irmo and Námo were known collectively as the [Fëanturi](/wiki/F%C3%ABanturi), the masters of spirits.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup> Their sister was [Nienna](/wiki/Nienna), the [Valie](/wiki/Valar "Valar") of pity and sorrow.

Irmo lived in his gardens with his spouse [Estë](/wiki/Est%C3%AB) with their dwelling lying west of [Valimar](/wiki/Valimar).

Irmo tended to and provided refuge to the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of Valinor in Lórien, where he would guide the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) through visions and dreams.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup> When [Míriel](/wiki/M%C3%ADriel) lost strength after giving birth to [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), she was brought to Lórien for rest. Her spirit later departed to [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos) and refused to return.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

When the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and [Moon](/wiki/Moon) were created, [Varda](/wiki/Varda) purposed that the two vessels should journey in [Ilmen](/wiki/Ilmen) and mingle their light as the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees) once had. However, Irmo and Estë asked her to reconsider as "sleep and rest had been banished from the Earth."<sup id="cite_ref-Sun_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sun-3">[3]</a></sup> Therefore, Varda changed her council and created twilight.<sup id="cite_ref-Sun_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sun-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Irmo* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name meaning "Desirer" or "Master of Desire".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> His common name *Lórien* ([Quenya](/wiki/Quenya); <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈloːri.en]</span>) means "Land of Dreams".

In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, <i><b>Lhuien</b></i> is given as a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) cognate of *Lórien*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol)'s [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) translations, Irmo is referred to as *Swefnfréa* "Dream-ruler".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In early versions of the legendarium, this Vala (variously spelt <i><b>Lorien</b></i> and <i><b>Lôrien</b></i>) was given various surnames: [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) <i><b>Olofantur</b></i> (the element *fantur*, a derivative of the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") FANA, refers to "visions, dreams, falling asleep"),<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-7">[7]</a></sup> Qenya <i><b>Fulmur</b></i> (probably from the root FUMU, "sleep"), and [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) <i><b>Losfan</b></i> (consisting of *oloth* "a dream, apparition, vision" + ending *-fan*, thus *(o)loth-fan* &gt; *Losfan*).<sup id="cite_ref-PE14_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE14-8">[8]</a></sup> Gnomish renderings of his first name included <i><b>Glurim</b></i> (containing the element *lûr* "slumber") and <i><b>Lûriel</b></i> or <i><b>Lúriel</b></i> (&gt; <i><b>Lúrin</b></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE14_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE14-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### The Book of Lost Tales {#The_Book_of_Lost_Tales}

In [The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales), <i><b>Lórien Olofántur</b></i> (*Fantur of Dreams*) was the lord of dreams and imaginings, and loved twilight and flittering shadows, and sweet scents borne upon evening winds. He was one of two [Fánturi](/wiki/F%C3%A1nturi), with the other being his brother *Vefántur Mandos* (*Fantur of Death*).<sup id="cite_ref-cov_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-10">[10]</a></sup>

He arrived in Middle-earth in the fifth wave of Valar, along with [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas), Mandos, *Fui Nienna* (spouse of Mandos), and [Vána](/wiki/V%C3%A1na) (spouse of Oromë). The earlier waves were Melko; then Manwë, Varda, and their folk; then Aulë, Yavanna, Ulmo, and their folk (though only [Salmar](/wiki/Salmar) accompanied Ulmo); then Ossë, Ónen, and their troops. The later waves were [Makar](/wiki/Makar) and [Meássë](/wiki/Me%C3%A1ss%C3%AB); and then lastly [Ómar](/wiki/%C3%93mar).<sup id="cite_ref-cov_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-10">[10]</a></sup>

Following the destruction of the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps), Aulë fashioned two great cauldrons to hold the remaining light in the world: [Kulullin](/wiki/Kulullin) for the gold, and [Silindrin](/wiki/Silindrin) for the silver, They then dug two great pits in preparation for the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees). In the second pit was cast three huge pearls found by Ossë, and a small star cast by Varda, and it was then covered with foams and white mists and sprinkled lightly with earth. Lórien then sat near and whispered swift noiseless words while his sprites played half-heard tunes beside him. The Valar then poured upon it silver light from Silindrin.<sup id="cite_ref-cov_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-10">[10]</a></sup>

Through the efforts of Yavanna, the Two Trees then sprang up. On beholding the silver tree, [Silpion](/wiki/Silpion), Lórien could not contain his joy. When Yavanna instructed that the dew of the trees must be gathered, and then used to water the trees themselves, Lórien bade [Silmo](/wiki/Silmo), a youth he loved, to the task of refreshing Silpion.<sup id="cite_ref-cov_10-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-10">[10]</a></sup>

While many of the Valar had dwellings built in [Valmar](/wiki/Valmar), Lórien dwelt far to the south in a placed he called [Murmuran](/wiki/Murmuran). Around his abode were wide gardens, which contained many types of trees and [fumellar](/wiki/Fumellar "Fumellar"), poppies which Lórien used in his enchantments. These gardens stretched even to Silindrin, where Lórien would gaze and see many visions of mystery.<sup id="cite_ref-cov_10-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cov-10">[10]</a></sup>
