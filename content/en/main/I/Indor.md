## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [earliest versions](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales "The Book of Lost Tales") of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), **Indor** was the father of [Peleg](/wiki/Peleg), in turn the father of [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor). Therefore like Tuor he probably belonged to the [House of the Swan](/wiki/House_of_the_Swan).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* Tuor's grandfather is [Galdor the Tall](</wiki/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min)> "Galdor (Lord of Dor-lómin)").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
