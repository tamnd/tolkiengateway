**Ilkorin**, a language [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) wrote in the earliest versions of his [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), was the tongue of the [Ilkorindi](/wiki/Ilkorindi).

In this early setting, Ilkorin was [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic)-flavored. When the [Noldoli](/wiki/Noldoli) returned to the Great Lands, their [language](/wiki/Old_Noldorin "Old Noldorin") was much influenced by the phonology of Ilkorin and evolved to the amalgamated language known as [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin).

In the later writings, the language was replaced by [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and [Avarin](/wiki/Avarin). In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, some names (for example, the rivernames of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand)) are said to be of Ilkorin origin. However, in later notes, some of those names were renamed or given another etymology.

In this same phase, [North Sindarin](/wiki/North_Sindarin) was conceived, which assumed linguistic features originating conceptually from Ilkorin.
