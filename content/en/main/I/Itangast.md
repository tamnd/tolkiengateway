In *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)* and the *[Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*, **Itangast**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> was a [Fire-drake](../F/Fire-drake.md) in the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md). He is described as the deadliest and smartest of his kin in the Grey Mountains. As such only [Scatha](../S/Scatha.md) and [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) are noted as his peers. He had blue-tipped black scales and long silvery horns.

His lair was situated at Gold Hill, which was a knoll full of valuable ores. It was also a strategic site overlooking the Cirith Auris, a pass used to travel between [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) and the [Northern Waste](../N/Northern_Waste.md).<sup id="cite_ref-creatures_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-creatures-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> The only known treasure in his hoard is *Sulthol* ([Q.](../Q/Quenya.md) “Wind Helm”), a helmet made by [Edain](../E/Edain.md) who lived northwest of [Dale](../D/Dale.md) in the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-treasures_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-treasures-5">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

He got the title of “Guest-eater” after inviting neighbouring [Northman](../N/Northmen.md) chiefs to a banquet, and then killing them instead. This allowed him to focus on the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), who had sent seven failed expeditions to conquer Gold Hill. The Northman too tried to vanquish the dragon thrice, for his lair was originally a sacred burial ground of theirs.<sup id="cite_ref-creatures_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-creatures-2">[1]</a></sup>

Itangast lost the mating duels to claim [Leucaruth](../L/Leucaruth.md), which began a fierce rivalry with the victor Throkmaw, though Itangast later got his revenge by severely wounding his rival.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
