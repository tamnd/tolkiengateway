In *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* and the *[Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game "Middle-earth Collectible Card Game")*, **Itangast**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> was a [Fire-drake](/wiki/Fire-drake) in the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains). He is described as the deadliest and smartest of his kin in the Grey Mountains. As such only [Scatha](/wiki/Scatha) and [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) are noted as his peers. He had blue-tipped black scales and long silvery horns.

His lair was situated at Gold Hill, which was a knoll full of valuable ores. It was also a strategic site overlooking the Cirith Auris, a pass used to travel between [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) and the [Northern Waste](/wiki/Northern_Waste).<sup id="cite_ref-creatures_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-creatures-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> The only known treasure in his hoard is *Sulthol* ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") “Wind Helm”), a helmet made by [Edain](/wiki/Edain) who lived northwest of [Dale](/wiki/Dale) in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-treasures_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-treasures-5">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

He got the title of “Guest-eater” after inviting neighbouring [Northman](/wiki/Northmen "Northmen") chiefs to a banquet, and then killing them instead. This allowed him to focus on the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), who had sent seven failed expeditions to conquer Gold Hill. The Northman too tried to vanquish the dragon thrice, for his lair was originally a sacred burial ground of theirs.<sup id="cite_ref-creatures_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-creatures-2">[1]</a></sup>

Itangast lost the mating duels to claim [Leucaruth](/wiki/Leucaruth), which began a fierce rivalry with the victor Throkmaw, though Itangast later got his revenge by severely wounding his rival.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
