**Ivan A. Derzhanski** (born [15 May](../_/15_May.md) [1967](../_/1967.md)) is a Bulgarian mathematician and linguist. Since [1999](../_/1999.md), he is a member of the Review Panel of *[Vinyar Tengwar](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
