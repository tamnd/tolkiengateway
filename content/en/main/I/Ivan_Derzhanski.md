**Ivan A. Derzhanski** (born [15 May](/wiki/15_May) [1967](/wiki/1967)) is a Bulgarian mathematician and linguist. Since [1999](/wiki/1999), he is a member of the Review Panel of *[Vinyar Tengwar](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
