**Illuin** was one of the [Two Lamps](../T/Two_Lamps.md) of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), made by [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md), filled by [Varda](../V/Varda.md) and hallowed by [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) in ancient days to bring light to the world. It was set up on the mount [Helcar](../H/Helcar.md) in the northern part of Middle-earth while the other Lamp, [Ormal](../O/Ormal.md) was set up in the south. It was destroyed by [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md), and where it fell it created the inland [Sea of Helcar](../S/Sea_of_Helcar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of *Illuin* is unclear,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but it could be a combination of *[il-](Ilmen.md)* ("sky") and *[luin](../L/Luin.md)* ("blue").

Because of its apparent etymology as "blue light" (whereas its pair referred to "gold" light), it is possible that Illuin was a precursor of [Telperion](../T/Telperion.md), the silver one of the [Two Trees of Valinor](../T/Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md), and of the [Moon](../M/Moon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
