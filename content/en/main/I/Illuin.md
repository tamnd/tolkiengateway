**Illuin** was one of the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps) of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), made by [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB), filled by [Varda](/wiki/Varda) and hallowed by [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) in ancient days to bring light to the world. It was set up on the mount [Helcar](/wiki/Helcar) in the northern part of Middle-earth while the other Lamp, [Ormal](/wiki/Ormal) was set up in the south. It was destroyed by [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth"), and where it fell it created the inland [Sea of Helcar](/wiki/Sea_of_Helcar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of *Illuin* is unclear,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but it could be a combination of *[il-](/wiki/Ilmen "Ilmen")* ("sky") and *[luin](/wiki/Luin "Luin")* ("blue").

Because of its apparent etymology as "blue light" (whereas its pair referred to "gold" light), it is possible that Illuin was a precursor of [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion), the silver one of the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor), and of the [Moon](/wiki/Moon).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
