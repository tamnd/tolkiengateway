**Imrazôr**, known as the **Númenórean**, was a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") nobleman of [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas) at the end of the second millennium of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age). He was said to have wedded an [Elf-maid](/wiki/Elves "Elves") named [Mithrellas](/wiki/Mithrellas), a wandering companion of [Nimrodel](/wiki/Nimrodel). His son [Galador](/wiki/Galador) was the first [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name is not known, however it includes [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) *zôr* which means "flame" or "foam".

The first element *imra* (perhaps from \**imar*) is not translated but it also appears in the name of his descendant, [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil) and perhaps <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Im%C3%A2r&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Imâr (page does not exist)">Imâr</a>.

Imrazôr, who seems to have retained the usage of Adûnaic in his House, was also known as the **Númenórean**. Why a Gondorian noble of the Third Age would be called the "Númenórean" is not made clear from the context.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
