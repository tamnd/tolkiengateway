<i><b>iôr</b></i> is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word meaning "course".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Didier Willis](/wiki/Didier_Willis) has suggested that *iôr* is a noun.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) has suggested that the form *ŷr* (retaining the same meaning) could be valid [Neo-Sindarin](/wiki/Neo-Elvish "Neo-Elvish").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Old_Noldorin) *yura*, [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=YUR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="YUR (page does not exist)">YUR</a> ("run")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)/[Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *[rant](/wiki/Rant "Rant")* ("course")
