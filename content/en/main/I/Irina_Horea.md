**Irina Horea** is a Romanian translator for British and American literature and director of the USR (Romanian Writer's Union). She is known for the high quality of her work ever since her first translation appeared, in [1977](/wiki/1977), a poetry volume by <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Wakoski" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Diane Wakosky</a> and <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Shapiro" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Karl Shapiro</a>. From there on she translated over 35 books, among them works by acclaimed authors as <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Bailey" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Paul Bailey</a>, <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Golding" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">William Golding</a>, <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Irving" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">John Irving</a> and <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McEwan" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ian McEwan</a>. She is also a correspondent for the Romanian edition of *Lettre Internationale*.

## J.R.R. Tolkien books translated by Irina Horea {#J.R.R._Tolkien_books_translated_by_Irina_Horea}

For the RAO house of publishing Irina Horea translated the following works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) :

- [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit) (*Hobbitul*)
- [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) (*Stapanul Inelelor*)
    - [The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring) (*Fratia Inelului*)
    - [The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers) (*Cele Doua Turnuri*)
    - [The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King) (*Intoarcerea Regelui*)
- [The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion) (*Silmarillion*)
- [Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales) (*Povesti Neterminate*)
- [The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin) (*Copiii lui Húrin*)
