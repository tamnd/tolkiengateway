<i><b>In Hollow Halls Beneath the Fells</b></i> is the third track of the album [Let Mortal Heroes Sing Your Fame](../L/Let_Mortal_Heroes_Sing_Your_Fame.md) by the band [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md). The song name and lyrics are taken from the poem [Far over the misty mountains cold](../F/Far_over_the_misty_mountains_cold.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Far over the misty mountains cold<br/>
To dungeons deep and carvens old<br/>
We must away ere break of day<br/>
To seek the pale enchanted gold<br/>
<br/>
The pines were roaring on the height<br/>
The winds were moaning in the night<br/>
The fire was read, it flaming spread<br/>
The trees like torches blazed with light<br/>
<br/>
The dwarves of yore made mighty spells<br/>
While hammers fell like ringing bells<br/>
In places deep where dark things sleep<br/>
In hollow halls beneath the fells<br/>
<br/>
For ancient kind and elvish lord<br/>
There many a gleaming golden hoard<br/>
They shaped and wrought, and light they caught<br/>
To hide in gems on hilt of sword
</p>
</div>
