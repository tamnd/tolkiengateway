**Isilmo** was the younger brother of [Queen](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Ruling Queen of Númenor") [Tar-Telperiën](/wiki/Tar-Telperi%C3%ABn). He had a son named [Minastir](/wiki/Minastir). Tar-Telperiën outlived Isilmo, but she refused to wed and had no children. Thus, Tar-Telperiën was succeeded by Isilmo's son, her nephew, who became [Tar-Minastir](/wiki/Tar-Minastir).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Telperien_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Telperien-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Isilmo* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it is a compound of *[Isil](/wiki/Moon#Other_names "Moon")* ("moon") and the agental suffix *[-mo](/wiki/-mo)* ("one", "anyone", "someone", "somebody").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
