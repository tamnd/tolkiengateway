Iron was a commonly used metal in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), though it was perhaps most infamously used in the [Iron Crown](/wiki/Iron_Crown). In [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) it was called [ang](/wiki/Ang "Ang").

The gate of [High Hay](/wiki/High_Hay) has thick-set iron bars.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Orcs of Isengard](/wiki/Orcs_of_Isengard) wore iron helmets.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1994: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="/wiki/Treasures_of_Middle-earth_(2nd_edition)" title="Treasures of Middle-earth (2nd edition)">Treasures of Middle-earth</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Much is made of the purity of iron in the book. Pure iron is silver-white and easiest to work with, though it is rare even <a href="/wiki/Moria" title="Moria">Moria</a>, while more common varieties are darker and stronger. The Dwarves considered anything that&#39;s “virtually pure” iron to be iron. The Elves primarily used iron to make <a href="/wiki/Steel" title="Steel">steel</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Barrow-iron ore can be mined by journeyman prospectors in <a href="/wiki/Cardolan" title="Cardolan">Cardolan</a>, <a href="/wiki/Blue_Mountains" title="Blue Mountains">Ered Luin</a>, <a href="/wiki/The_Shire" title="The Shire">the Shire</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bree-land" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Lone-lands" title="Lone-lands">Lone-lands</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/North_Downs" title="North Downs">North Downs</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Rich iron ore can be mined by expert prospectors in the Lone-lands, the North Downs, <a href="/wiki/Lake_Evendim" title="Lake Evendim">Evendim</a>, Bree-land, and <a href="/wiki/The_Yondershire" title="The Yondershire">the Yondershire</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Dwarf-iron ore can be mined by artisan prospectors in the <a href="/wiki/Trollshaws" title="Trollshaws">Trollshaws</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a>, <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>, Evendim, and the Lone-lands.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Ancient iron ore can be mined by master prospectors in the Misty Mountains, Angmar, <a href="/wiki/Forochel" title="Forochel">Forochel</a>, <a href="/wiki/Eregion" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>, and Bree-land.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>
