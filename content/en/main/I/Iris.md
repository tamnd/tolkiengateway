**Iris** was a colourful and distinctive flower. [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") found it growing in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and the [Gladden River](/wiki/Gladden_River) and the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields) took their name from a variety of this flower.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Notes {#Notes}

The "iris-swords" mentioned in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* are a reference to its thin, pointed leaves. In [Letter 297](/wiki/Letter_297), [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") identifies the flower as the <i><a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iris_pseudacorus" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Iris pseudacorus</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
