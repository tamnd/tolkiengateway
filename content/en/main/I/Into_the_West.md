**Into the West** is a song written by [Fran Walsh](/wiki/Fran_Walsh), [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore) and [Annie Lennox](/wiki/Annie_Lennox), and performed by Lennox for [Peter Jackson's](/wiki/Peter_Jackson "Peter Jackson") *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.

It was the nineteenth and final track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)* and formed part of [Days of the Ring](/wiki/Days_of_the_Ring), track seven of disc four of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King - The Complete Recordings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_-_The_Complete_Recordings)*.

The song was conceived as a bittersweet [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") lament for those who have sailed across the [Sundering Seas](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer").

In the commentaries and documentaries accompanying the [extended DVD edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)") of the movie, director [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) explained that the song was partially inspired by the premature death from cancer of 17-year-old [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) filmmaker <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Duncan" title="wikipedia:Cameron Duncan">Cameron Duncan</a> whose work had impressed Jackson and his team. The first public performance of the song was at Duncan's funeral.

The song won the Academy Award for *Achievement in music written for motion pictures (Original song)* at the 76th Academy Awards, one of the movie's eleven Oscars.
