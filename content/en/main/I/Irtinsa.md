**Lake Irtinsa** was a body of water in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

It was built by the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) following the creation of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) and [Moon](../M/Moon.md), and was located just south of [Valmar](../V/Valmar.md). It was surrounded by dark [yews](../Y/Yews.md) set as a maze, and its walls were made of "silver and white marbles". It was also where the Vala [Lórien](Irmo.md) hoarded the dew of the [Rose of Silpion](../M/Moon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
