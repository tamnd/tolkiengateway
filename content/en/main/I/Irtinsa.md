**Lake Irtinsa** was a body of water in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

It was built by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) following the creation of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and [Moon](/wiki/Moon), and was located just south of [Valmar](/wiki/Valmar). It was surrounded by dark [yews](/wiki/Yews "Yews") set as a maze, and its walls were made of "silver and white marbles". It was also where the Vala [Lórien](/wiki/Irmo "Irmo") hoarded the dew of the [Rose of Silpion](/wiki/Moon "Moon").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
