**Ilberic Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Ilberic was the second son of [Seredic Brandybuck](../S/Seredic_Brandybuck.md) and [Hilda Bracegirdle](../H/Hilda_Bracegirdle.md), born in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1391](../T/Third_Age_2991.md). In [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1401](../T/Third_Age_3001.md) little Ilberic attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md) with his family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Ilberic* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](../C/Celtic.md) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](../J/Jim_Allan.md) suggests that the name *Ilberic* seems to be Germanic. It is a variaton of the name *Alberic* and contains the elements *alb* ("[Elf](../E/Elves.md)") and *ric* ("reign", "rule"). <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberic" title="wikipedia:Alberic">Alberic</a> is a dwarf king in medieval German heroic literature who appears in [Richard Wagner](../R/Richard_Wagner.md)'s [Ring cycle](../D/Der_Ring_des_Nibelungen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier version of the family tree for the [Brandybucks](../B/Brandybucks.md), the name of Ilberic was <i><b>Alberic</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
