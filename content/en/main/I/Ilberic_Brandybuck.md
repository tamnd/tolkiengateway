**Ilberic Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Ilberic was the second son of [Seredic Brandybuck](/wiki/Seredic_Brandybuck) and [Hilda Bracegirdle](/wiki/Hilda_Bracegirdle), born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1391](/wiki/Third_Age_2991 "Third Age 2991"). In [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001") little Ilberic attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party) with his family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Ilberic* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) suggests that the name *Ilberic* seems to be Germanic. It is a variaton of the name *Alberic* and contains the elements *alb* ("[Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves")") and *ric* ("reign", "rule"). <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberic" title="wikipedia:Alberic">Alberic</a> is a dwarf king in medieval German heroic literature who appears in [Richard Wagner](/wiki/Richard_Wagner)'s [Ring cycle](/wiki/Der_Ring_des_Nibelungen "Der Ring des Nibelungen").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier version of the family tree for the [Brandybucks](/wiki/Brandybucks), the name of Ilberic was <i><b>Alberic</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
