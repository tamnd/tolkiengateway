**Iago Grubb** was one of [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md)'s lawyers in *[The Return of the Shadow](../T/The_Return_of_the_Shadow.md)* (at that stage of the writing Frodo was called Bingo). Iago, along with Bingo's other lawyer [Folco Burrowes](../F/Folco_Burrowes.md), eject [Sancho Proudfoot](../S/Sancho_Proudfoot.md) as he tries to excavate the pantry at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Shadow_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shadow-1">[1]</a></sup>
