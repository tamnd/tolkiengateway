**In the Willow-meads of Tasarinan** is a song that was sung by [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) on [29 February](/wiki/29_February) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> as he strode through [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest), taking [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") to his home of [Wellinghall](/wiki/Wellinghall) at the foot of the mountain [Methedras](/wiki/Methedras). Treebeard had been telling the hobbits of the "broad days" in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) when the forests were much more extensive and after a bit of silence began chanting this song as he walked.

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic;margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>In the willow-meads of <a href="/wiki/Nan-tathren" title="Nan-tathren">Tasarinan</a> I walked in the Spring.<br/>
Ah! the sight and the smell of the Spring in Nan-tasarion!<br/>
And I said that was good.<br/>
I wandered in Summer in the elm-woods of <a href="/wiki/Ossiriand" title="Ossiriand">Ossiriand</a>.<br/>
Ah! the light and the music in the Summer by the Seven Rivers of Ossir!<br/>
And I thought that was best.<br/>
To the beeches of <a href="/wiki/Forest_of_Neldoreth" title="Forest of Neldoreth">Neldoreth</a> I came in the Autumn.<br/>
Ah! the gold and the red and the sighing of leaves in the Autumn in Taur-na-neldor!<br/>
It was more than my desire.<br/>
To the pine-trees upon the highland of <a href="/wiki/Dorthonion" title="Dorthonion">Dorthonion</a> I climbed in the Winter.<br/>
Ah! the wind and the whiteness and the black branches of Winter upon <a href="/wiki/Orod-na-Th%C3%B4n" title="Orod-na-Thôn">Orod-na-Thôn</a>!<br/>
My voice went up and sang in the sky.<br/>
And now all those lands lie under the wave,<br/>
And I walk in <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ambar%C3%B3na" title="Ambaróna">Ambarona</a>, in <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Tauremorna" title="Tauremorna">Tauremorna</a>, in <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Aldal%C3%B3m%C3%AB" title="Aldalómë">Aldalómë</a>,<br/>
In my own land, in the country of Fangorn,<br/>
Where the roots are long,<br/>
And the years lie thicker than the leaves<br/>
In Tauremornalómë.
</p>
</div>

After finishing this song Treebeard walked on silently through the silent woods.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

[Donald Swann](/wiki/Donald_Swann) wrote music for this song, which can be found in the book *[The Road Goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")*;<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> it is also found on the accompanying CD of the latest version.
