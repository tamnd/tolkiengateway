**Inklings** can refer to:

## Real-world {#Real-world}

- The **[Inklings](Inklings.md)**, a literary group which included [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md).
- [Category:Inklings](../../category/I/Inklings.md), listing people associated with the Inklings.
- [The Founders' Inklings](../T/The_Founders'_Inklings.md), podcast produced by [Middle-earth Network Radio](../M/Middle-earth_Network.md).
