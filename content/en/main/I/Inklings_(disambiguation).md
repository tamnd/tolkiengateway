**Inklings** can refer to:

## Real-world {#Real-world}

- The **[Inklings](/wiki/Inklings)**, a literary group which included [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis).
- [Category:Inklings](/wiki/Category:Inklings), listing people associated with the Inklings.
- [The Founders' Inklings](/wiki/The_Founders%27_Inklings), podcast produced by [Middle-earth Network Radio](/wiki/Middle-earth_Network "Middle-earth Network").

### Books {#Books}

- <i><b><a href="/wiki/The_Inklings_(book)" title="The Inklings (book)">The Inklings: C.S. Lewis, J.R.R. Tolkien, Charles Williams and Their Friends</a></b></i>, a [1978](/wiki/1978) book by [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter).
- [The Inklings of Oxford](/wiki/The_Inklings_of_Oxford), a [2009](/wiki/2009) book by Harry Lee Poe.
- *[Inklings: Jahrbuch für Literatur und Ästhetik](/wiki/Inklings:_Jahrbuch_f%C3%BCr_Literatur_und_%C3%84sthetik)*, a bi-lingual (German/English) journal.

- *[Myth and Magic: Art according to the Inklings](/wiki/Myth_and_Magic:_Art_according_to_the_Inklings)*, a [2007](/wiki/2007) book in the *[Cormarë Series](/wiki/Cormar%C3%AB_Series)*.
