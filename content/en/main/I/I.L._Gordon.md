**Ida Lilian Gordon** (*née* Pickles, b. [1907](../_/1907.md) - [26 September](../_/26_September.md) [2002](../_/2002.md)) was a scholar and teacher at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester" title="wikipedia:University of Manchester">University of Manchester</a>. She studied at the [University of Leeds](../U/University_of_Leeds.md) (B.A. honours 1928, Ph.D. 1930) under [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md), whom she married in 1930.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [1960](../_/1960.md), Ida Gordon published *[The Seafarer](<../T/The_Seafarer_(I.L._Gordon).md>)*, an edition of the [Old English poem](<../T/The_Seafarer_(poem).md>). This edition was based on a work originally done by E.V. Gordon and [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
