**Ida Lilian Gordon** (*née* Pickles, b. [1907](/wiki/1907) - [26 September](/wiki/26_September) [2002](/wiki/2002)) was a scholar and teacher at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester" title="wikipedia:University of Manchester">University of Manchester</a>. She studied at the [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds) (B.A. honours 1928, Ph.D. 1930) under [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon), whom she married in 1930.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [1960](/wiki/1960), Ida Gordon published *[The Seafarer](</wiki/The_Seafarer_(I.L._Gordon)> "The Seafarer (I.L. Gordon)")*, an edition of the [Old English poem](</wiki/The_Seafarer_(poem)> "The Seafarer (poem)"). This edition was based on a work originally done by E.V. Gordon and [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
