**Isengard** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- **[Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), the fortress inhabited by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) in the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).**
- The [Battle of Isengard](/wiki/Battle_of_Isengard), a battle fought during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).
- [Gate of Isengard](/wiki/Gate_of_Isengard)
- [Ring of Isengard](/wiki/Ring_of_Isengard)

## Books and Chapters {#Books_and_Chapters}

- *[The Treason of Isengard](/wiki/The_Treason_of_Isengard)*, the seventh volume in *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* series by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), edited by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien).
- *The Treason of Isengard*, the title of Book III of [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings), published within *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*.
- "[The Road to Isengard](/wiki/The_Road_to_Isengard)", the eighth chapter of the third book in *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*.

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

### Games {#Games}

- *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Rise of Isengard](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Rise_of_Isengard)*, a [2011](/wiki/2011) expansion for *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*.
- *[Isengard (LOTRRPG)](</wiki/Isengard_(LOTRRPG)>)*, a [2005](/wiki/2005) supplement for *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game)*.
- *[The Lord of the Rings: The Treason of Isengard](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Treason_of_Isengard)*, a cancelled sequel to the [2002](/wiki/2002) [Vivendi](/wiki/Vivendi) video game *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)")*.
- *[A Spy In Isengard](/wiki/A_Spy_In_Isengard)*, a [1988](/wiki/1988) game book in the *[Middle-earth Quest](/wiki/Middle-earth_Quest)* series.
- *[Isengard and Northern Gondor](/wiki/Isengard_and_Northern_Gondor)*, a [1983](/wiki/1983)/[1987](/wiki/1987) supplement for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*.
