## Books and Chapters {#Books_and_Chapters}

- *[The Treason of Isengard](../T/The_Treason_of_Isengard.md)*, the seventh volume in *[The History of Middle-earth](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* series by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), edited by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md).
- *The Treason of Isengard*, the title of Book III of [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md), published within *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md)*.
- "[The Road to Isengard](../T/The_Road_to_Isengard.md)", the eighth chapter of the third book in *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md)*.
