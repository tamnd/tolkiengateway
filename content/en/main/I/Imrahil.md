**Imrahil** was the twenty-second [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth).

## History {#History}

### Early life {#Early_life}

Imrahil was the only son of [Adrahil](/wiki/Adrahil) of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth). He had two older sisters, [Ivriniel](/wiki/Ivriniel) and [Finduilas](</wiki/Finduilas_(wife_of_Denethor)> "Finduilas (wife of Denethor)"). After his father's death, he became Prince in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3010](/wiki/Third_Age_3010 "Third Age 3010"). Imrahil had four children: [Elphir](/wiki/Elphir), [Erchirion](/wiki/Erchirion), [Amrothos](/wiki/Amrothos) and [Lothíriel](/wiki/Loth%C3%ADriel).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:John_Howe_-_Prince_Imrahil.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/2/22/John_Howe_-_Prince_Imrahil.jpg/120px-John_Howe_-_Prince_Imrahil.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="120" height="305" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/2/22/John_Howe_-_Prince_Imrahil.jpg/180px-John_Howe_-_Prince_Imrahil.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/2/22/John_Howe_-_Prince_Imrahil.jpg/240px-John_Howe_-_Prince_Imrahil.jpg 2x" data-file-width="244" data-file-height="621"/></a><figcaption><i>Prince Imrahil</i> by <a href="/wiki/John_Howe" title="John Howe">John Howe</a></figcaption></figure>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Imrahil came to the aid of his brother-in-law, [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor"). On [9 March](/wiki/9_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), he came into [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) with a company of knights and seven hundred soldiers.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-2">[2]</a></sup> On [13 March](/wiki/13_March), as [Southrons](/wiki/Southrons) harassed [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), Imrahil and his knights, with [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), formed a sortie, and Imrahil himself saved his sister-son from his pursuers.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Faramir received a poisonous wound, and Denethor fell to madness. Being the highest ranking officer in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) fit for battle, Imrahil took command, and quickly passed it on to Gandalf.

Two days later, the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields) raged before the city. Imrahil ventured outside the city, and witnessed the funeral procession of [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden). Here he beheld [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn), and wondered that Rohan would send even women in Gondor's great need. Though Théoden had passed, Imrahil recognised that Éowyn still lived (though she was near death), and ordered that she be taken to the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing).

Because [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) and his riders were outnumbered, Imrahil rode into battle, together with [Húrin the Tall](</wiki/H%C3%BArin_(Warden_of_the_Keys)> "Húrin (Warden of the Keys)"), [Forlong of Lossarnach](/wiki/Forlong "Forlong") and [Hirluin the Fair](/wiki/Hirluin "Hirluin"), and they were soon joined by [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) who landed at [Harlond](/wiki/Harlond_in_Gondor "Harlond in Gondor"). Imrahil continued East, driving the frightened [Variags](/wiki/Variags) and [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") away.

Imrahil survived the battle unscathed, and together with Éomer and Aragorn he came to the [Great Gate of Minas Tirith](/wiki/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-4">[4]</a></sup> Imrahil recognized that Aragorn was the rightful King, but he agreed that it was wise for Aragorn to wait to enter the city, because he knew Denethor was strong-willed and proud.

When Imrahil learned that Denethor was dead and Faramir dying, he suggested that Aragorn be summoned, remembering that the Kings of old were great healers. Aragorn came at Gandalf's request to heal the wounded Faramir, Éowyn, and [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck"), but he declared that Prince Imrahil should rule the City until Faramir awoke, in effect granting Imrahil the temporary position of Steward. Though Aragorn also declared that Gandalf should lead them in all things for the remaining days of the war, to which Imrahil agreed.

On [16 March](/wiki/16_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) recognized that Imrahil had elven-blood when he looked at him during their meeting in Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> On the same day Imrahil participated in the [debate](/wiki/Last_Debate "Last Debate") of the [Captains of the West](/wiki/Captains_of_the_West). Gandalf proposed that they march to the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon) to distract Sauron's attention from [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") the Ring-bearer. Aragorn agreed to this advice and said that he did not yet claim to command any man, so the others should choose if they wanted to follow it. Imrahil said that he considered Aragorn to be his liege-lord, regardless whether Aragorn claimed it or not and that he would follow him. Imrahil also stated that he stood in the place of the [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor) and advised that some troops should remain to protect the City. In the end it was decided that an army of 7,000 would ride forth. Imrahil laughed at the thought.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Húrin the Tall](/wiki/H%C3%BArin_the_Tall) was left behind in command.

The <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Host_of_the_West" title="Host of the West">Host of the West</a> left Minas Tirith on [18 March](/wiki/18_March). Bypassing [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) and marching North, the heralds announced the coming of King Elessar at Imrahil's advice. When Sauron's forces emerged from the Black Gate on March 25, Imrahil stood on the front line with his men. They fought the [Battle of the Morannon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon) until [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") was destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Prince Imrahil was present at the celebrations of the [field of Cormallen](/wiki/Field_of_Cormallen "Field of Cormallen"), and the coronation of Aragorn as King Elessar, and rode with the funeral procession of King Théoden to Rohan, remaining in [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) after the King's burial on [August 10](/wiki/August_10). Imrahil and Éomer became great friends, and in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021") Éomer wed Imrahil's daughter [Lothíriel](/wiki/Loth%C3%ADriel).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

### Later life {#Later_life}

After the War of the Ring, Prince Imrahil and his nephew [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), [Prince of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien), were King Elessar's chief commanders. Imrahil was also part of the Great [Council of Gondor](/wiki/Council_of_Gondor), and remained an advisor of the King.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Prince Imrahil died in the year [34](/wiki/Fourth_Age_34 "Fourth Age 34") of the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), and was succeed by his eldest son [Elphir](/wiki/Elphir), who continued the line of Princes.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Imrahil* is an [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Its meaning is not glossed.

The second element *-hil*, also seen in the name *[Adrahil](/wiki/Adrahil)*, is perhaps related to the [Westron](/wiki/Westron) ending *-kil*<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[note 1]</a></sup> and perhaps ultimately related to [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) "follower" ([KHIL](/wiki/KHILI#Other_versions "KHILI")).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LR_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-14">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup>

Considering the Elvish root KHIL, [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) and [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) have proposed as a possible meaning "Heir of Imra" with the first element *Imra* being the same as the first element in the name [Imrazôr](/wiki/Imraz%C3%B4r) and the second element *-hil* ("heir").<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Imrahil is not mentioned by name. However, in the film&#39;s version of the <a href="/wiki/Last_Debate" title="Last Debate">Last Debate</a>, <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> are joined by individuals who, based on the book scene, can be inferred to be Imrahil, <a href="/wiki/%C3%89omer" title="Éomer">Éomer</a>, <a href="/wiki/Elladan_and_Elrohir" title="Elladan and Elrohir">Elladan, and Elrohir</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>1992: <a href="/wiki/Der_Herr_der_Ringe_(1992_German_radio_series)" title="Der Herr der Ringe (1992 German radio series)"><i>Der Herr der Ringe</i> (1992 German radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Imrahil is played by Gert Andresen.</dd></dl>

**2004**: [The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game):

<dl><dd>Imrahil&#39;s card in 2004&#39;s <i>Siege of Gondor</i> expansion depicts him as the Prince of Dol Amoroth and a knight of Gondor at <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Imrahil is featured prominently in the main storyline and is first met within the gates of <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>. He is close to <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Mithrandir</a> and has his trust, so the Wizard often leaves him and the player to take care of certain problems while he is too preoccupied elsewhere.</dd></dl>
