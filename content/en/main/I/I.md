<i><b>i</b></i> (plural <i><b>in</b></i> in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)) is the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) article "the" and relative pronoun "who".

## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

### Mutations {#Mutations}

In Sindarin, the singular form causes [lenition](../L/Lenition.md) to the following word. For example *[tâl](../T/Tâl.md)* "foot" &gt; <i>i <b>d</b>âl</i> "the foot".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The plural form causes [nasal mutation](../N/Nasal_mutation.md), and the final -*n* is assimilated. For example *[Periain](../P/Perian.md)* "[Halflings](../H/Halflings.md)" &gt; <i>i<b>n+P</b>eriain</i> &gt; <i>i-<b>Ph</b>eriain</i> "the Halflings".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

(The affection is usually noted with a hyphen or a dot between article and word.)

### Related forms {#Related_forms}

These Sindarin prepositions contain the suffixed article *-in*:

- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Nan_(preposition)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nan (preposition) (page does not exist)">nan</a></i> "to the"
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ben&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ben (page does not exist)">ben</a></i> "according to the"
- *[nuin](../N/Nuin.md)* "under the"
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Erin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Erin (page does not exist)">erin</a></i> "on the"
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Uin_(preposition)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Uin (preposition) (page does not exist)">uin</a></i> "of the"

The above combined articles when before a word, cause a peculiar phenomenon of lenition different from the above.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## As a relative pronoun {#As_a_relative_pronoun}

In both Quenya and Sindarin, *i* is used for the relative pronoun "who" or a conjunction "that", introducing a secondary phrase. In Quenya it is usually the plural form of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ya&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ya (page does not exist)">ya</a>/<a href="../Y/Ye.md" title="Ye">ye</a></i> but can also be used in the Singular.

For example:

- <i><a href="../P/Perhael.md" title="Perhael">Perhael</a>, <b>i</b> <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Sennui&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sennui (page does not exist)">sennui</a> &#34;<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Panthael&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Panthael (page does not exist)">Panthael</a>&#34; <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Esta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Esta (page does not exist)">estathar</a> <a href="../A/Aen.md" title="Aen">aen</a></i> "[Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), **who** ought to be called 'Fullwise'"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- <i><a href="Ilúvatar.md" title="Ilúvatar">Eru</a> <b>i</b> <a href="../O/Or.md" title="Or">or</a> <a href="Ilya.md" title="Ilya">ilye</a> <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Mahalma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mahalma (page does not exist)">mahalmar</a> <a href="../E/Ea_(verb).md" title="Ea (verb)">ea</a></i> "the One, **who** is above all thrones"

In Quenya, it can also be declined as a noun:

- <i><b>illon</b> <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Cam&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cam (page does not exist)">camnelyes</a></i> "**from whom** you received it?"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
