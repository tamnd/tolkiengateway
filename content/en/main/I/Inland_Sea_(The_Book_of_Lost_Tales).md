The **Inland Sea** was the name of a large sea in the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands), later known as the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean" title="wikipedia:Mediterranean">Mediterranean</a>, according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Med_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Med-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The sea was also called <i><b>Wendelsæ</b></i> in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English).<sup id="cite_ref-Med_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Med-1">[1]</a></sup>
