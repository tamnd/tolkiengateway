The **Icebay of Forochel** was an immense body of water whose southern reach lay between the northern tips of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) and [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) and extended for hundreds of miles to the northeast. The mouth of the bay was in the southwest, between the Blue Mountains and the Cape of [Forochel](/wiki/Forochel), which separated most of the bay from [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer).<sup id="cite_ref-UTMap_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTMap-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

A long time ago before a people known as the [Forodwaith](</wiki/Forodwaith_(people)> "Forodwaith (people)"), who were used to endure the bitter cold of the realm of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) settled in the far north. In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) a remnant of these people was known as the [Lossoth](/wiki/Lossoth) or the Snowmen of Forochel. The Lossoth lived on the Cape of Forochel most of the time, but they also camped on the southern shore of the bay at the feet of the Blue Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1974](/wiki/Third_Age_1974 "Third Age 1974") the kingdom of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) was overrun by the forces of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar). King [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) fled to the north, first to the [Far Downs](/wiki/Far_Downs), then near the northern end of the Blue Mountains and finally, driven by hunger, to a camp of the Lossoth at the shore of the bay. Partly from fear and partly from pity these people fed and sheltered the king and his men. Then, in March<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-2">[2]</a></sup> of [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> a ship from [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) reached the Icebay to take Arvedui and his party onboard. After they set sail a storm arose which crushed the ship in the ice and the king, his men, and all the mariners perished in the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

On Christopher Tolkien's [General Map of Middle-earth](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth) the label "Ice Bay of Forochel" was placed in the water in the smaller southern lobe of the bay.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> When [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) redrew his map, he placed the label on the shore just below the smaller southern lobe of the bay and added an arrow after the label that pointed to the larger northeast part of the bay.<sup id="cite_ref-UTMap_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTMap-1">[1]</a></sup> Christopher Tolkien stated that he had little doubt that the water with the label "Ice Bay of Forochel" on his original map was only a small part of the full immense Bay of Forochel.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The full extent of the Icebay is shown on J.R.R. Tolkien's [The First Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings) and on Map IA that was redrawn by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) based on this map and published in *[The Treason of Isengard](/wiki/The_Treason_of_Isengard)*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
