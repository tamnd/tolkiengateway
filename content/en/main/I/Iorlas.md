**Iorlas** was a man of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## History {#History}

Iorlas was an uncle of [Bergil](/wiki/Bergil), either [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond)'s brother or brother-in-law. When [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") mentioned to Bergil he was 29 years old, this surprised him, as his uncle Iorlas was also 29, and nigh twice as tall as the Hobbit.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Iorlas* could either mean "Old Leaf" or "Old Joy" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).
