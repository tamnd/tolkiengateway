**Ithilien** was a region and fiefdom of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## Geography {#Geography}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Ithilien.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/a/ac/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Ithilien.jpg/300px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Ithilien.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="211" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/a/ac/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Ithilien.jpg/450px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Ithilien.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/a/ac/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Ithilien.jpg/600px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Ithilien.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1600" data-file-height="1123"/></a><figcaption><i>Ithilien</i> by <a href="/wiki/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil" title="Matěj Čadil">Matěj Čadil</a></figcaption></figure>

Ithilien was a region of Gondor for most of its history. Its borders were probably the [Nindalf](/wiki/Nindalf) in the north,<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) in the east, the river [Poros](/wiki/Poros) in the south, and the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) in the west.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-MapRohan_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MapRohan-3">[3]</a></sup> The region was further divided into [North](/wiki/North_Ithilien "North Ithilien") and [South Ithilien](/wiki/South_Ithilien). The division was probably at the river [Morgulduin](/wiki/Morgulduin), or at the east-west-road from [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) to [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul).<sup id="cite_ref-MapRohan_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MapRohan-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Emyn Arnen](/wiki/Emyn_Arnen) were located in South Ithilien.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-4">[4]</a></sup>

Ithilien was a sheltered region. It was shielded from the east by the Ephel Dúath, protected from the north by the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil), and it was open to the southern airs and the moist winds from the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-Herbs_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Herbs-5">[5]</a></sup>

Small woods of resinous trees, fir, [cedar](/wiki/Cedars "Cedars"), [cypress](/wiki/Cypresses "Cypresses"), groves and thickets of tamarisk and terebinth, olive trees, bay,<sup id="cite_ref-Herbs_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Herbs-5">[5]</a></sup> great [ilexes](/wiki/Ilexes "Ilexes") of huge girth, ash-trees and giant oaks<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-6">[6]</a></sup> grew in Ithilien. There were also junipers, myrtles, thymes, sages of many kinds, marjorams, parsleys, and many herbs of forms and scents beyond the garden-lore of Sam. In addition, there were saxifrages, stonecrops, primeroles, [anemones](/wiki/Anemones "Anemones"), filbert-brakes, [asphodel](/wiki/Asphodel "Asphodel"), [lilies](/wiki/Lilies "Lilies"), roses, [irises](/wiki/Iris "Iris"), [water-lilies](/wiki/Water-lilies "Water-lilies"), briars, [eglantines](/wiki/Eglantine "Eglantine") and trailing clematises.<sup id="cite_ref-Herbs_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Herbs-5">[5]</a></sup> Furthermore, grass, celandine and acres populous with the leaves of woodland hyacinths<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-6">[6]</a></sup> grew in Ithilien.

[Lebethron](/wiki/Lebethron)<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-6">[6]</a></sup> and [Culumalda](/wiki/Culumalda) grew especially in the woods around the [Field of Cormallen](/wiki/Field_of_Cormallen).<sup id="cite_ref-RC_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SApp_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-8">[8]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Early history {#Early_history}

Historians in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) believed that, in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the [Woses](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain "Drúedain") reached the region and were the first to cross the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) (probably near [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros)).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Old records preserved in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) indicate that the name *Arnen* was once used for the larger part of the region during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup> When [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) and his sons founded the [Realms in Exile](/wiki/Realms_in_Exile), Ithilien took its name after his son, [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomenclature_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomenclature-10">[10]</a></sup> Ithilien was the territory of Isildur and was governed from [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil)<sup id="cite_ref-Ithilien_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ithilien-11">[11]</a></sup> [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil), the Tower of the Moon, had been built as a strong place that could threaten [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). In Minas Ithil stood the house of Isildur.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> It was [briefly captured](/wiki/First_Fall_of_Minas_Ithil "First Fall of Minas Ithil") by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3429](/wiki/Second_Age_3429 "Second Age 3429"),<sup id="cite_ref-SA_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-13">[13]</a></sup> but was restored later. The [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol) was built by Gondor as an eastern outpost of the defences of Ithilien after the Last Alliance.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> Ithilien was a fair and prosperous land during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) and the first part of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) when Gondor was strong and Mordor deserted.

### Invasions by Easterlings and Haradrim {#Invasions_by_Easterlings_and_Haradrim}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [490](/wiki/Third_Age_490 "Third Age 490") during the reign of King [Ostoher](/wiki/Ostoher), [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) invaded Gondor, probably through northern Ithilien. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [500](/wiki/Third_Age_500 "Third Age 500"), his son, [Tarostar](/wiki/Tarostar), defeated them and drove them out of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Ostoher_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ostoher-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-16">[16]</a></sup>

The [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol) was also defending Ithilien from attacks from Sauron's remaining servants and stopped them from returning to Mordor, guarding the high pass over the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath). Gondor occupied the fortress until [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636") when the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) killed large parts of the population. During the plague, most of the people of [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) fled the city for the western dales or the woods of Ithilien.

After the disastrous [Battle of the Plains](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Plains) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1856](/wiki/Third_Age_1856 "Third Age 1856") the Gondorian territories north of Ithilien and east of the river Anduin were abandoned by Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-17">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Narmacil_II_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narmacil_II-18">[18]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1899](/wiki/Third_Age_1899 "Third Age 1899"), Marhwini warned [King Calimehtar](</wiki/Calimehtar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Calimehtar (King of Gondor)") that the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) were plotting to raid [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) over the [Undeeps](/wiki/Undeeps). Calimehtar, therefore, provoked the Wainriders out of Ithilien, and his horsemen, joined by a large [éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored "Éored") led by [Marhwini](/wiki/Marhwini), drove the Wainriders back.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-19">[19]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Calimehtar_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Calimehtar-20">[20]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944"), [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) from [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn) made an alliance with the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim), and a dual attack on Ithilien was launched. While the Wainriders assaulted Ithilien from the north, the armies of the Haradrim crossed the [Poros](/wiki/Poros) and invaded [South Ithilien](/wiki/South_Ithilien). The Gondorian [Captain](/wiki/Captain_of_the_Southern_Army "Captain of the Southern Army") [Eärnil](/wiki/Earnil_II "Earnil II") leading Gondor's inferior [Southern Army](/wiki/Southern_Army), defeated the Haradrim, and then turned north, [took](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Camp "Battle of the Camp") the Wainriders by surprise and pushed many of them into the swamplands of the [Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes).<sup id="cite_ref-Ondoher_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ondoher-21">[21]</a></sup>

### Desertion {#Desertion}

When Minas Ithil was captured by Mordor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2002](/wiki/Third_Age_2002 "Third Age 2002") after a three year [long siege](/wiki/Second_Fall_of_Minas_Ithil "Second Fall of Minas Ithil"), it was renamed [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul), the Tower of Black Sorcery, a place of dread, and many of the people that still remained in Ithilien deserted it.<sup id="cite_ref-Ondoher_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ondoher-21">[21]</a></sup>

During the [Watchful Peace](/wiki/Watchful_Peace), the lords of Morgul had secretly bred the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai), and in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2475](/wiki/Third_Age_2475 "Third Age 2475") these creatures assailed and overran Ithilien and captured [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath). [Boromir](</wiki/Boromir_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Boromir (Steward of Gondor)") defeated the host of Morgul and regained Ithilien. However, from that time on no people dwelt in the ruined city of Osgiliath.<sup id="cite_ref-stew_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-22">[22]</a></sup>

[Amon Dîn](/wiki/Amon_D%C3%AEn) was used as a fortified outpost in [Anorien](/wiki/Anorien), and it kept watch over the passage into North Ithilien from [Dagorlad](/wiki/Dagorlad), as well as any attempted crossings of the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) near [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-19">[19]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup>

In the days of Steward [Túrin II](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_II), Ithilien was infested by [Mordor-orcs](/wiki/Mordor-orcs) and more people fled west over the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin); only the hardiest people remained in the land.<sup id="cite_ref-stew_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-22">[22]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2885](/wiki/Third_Age_2885 "Third Age 2885"), Ithilien was invaded in great strength by [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) that had occupied [South Gondor](/wiki/South_Gondor). With the aid of King [Folcwine](/wiki/Folcwine) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), Túrin II won a victory at the [Crossings of Poros](/wiki/Crossings_of_Poros), though the princes [Fastred](</wiki/Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine)> "Fastred (son of Folcwine)") and [Folcred](/wiki/Folcred) were slain.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-16">[16]</a></sup>

Most of the remaining people of Ithilien fled across the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) to escape further attacks by [Uruks](/wiki/Uruks) from Mordor ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2901](/wiki/Third_Age_2901 "Third Age 2901")),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_16-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-16">[16]</a></sup> but Túrin II still kept scouts there, operating out of secret refuges such as [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn). [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) returned to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2951](/wiki/Third_Age_2951 "Third Age 2951"), and the last remaining people of Ithilien fled over the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) when [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom) erupted in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2954](/wiki/Third_Age_2954 "Third Age 2954").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_16-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-16">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-stew_22-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-22">[22]</a></sup> The [Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor) was constructed as part of the necessary defences of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) after Ithilien fell under the shadow of the Enemy, probably during the days of Steward [Ecthelion II](/wiki/Ecthelion_II).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup> The descendants of the people who had dwelt in Ithilien formed the corps of the [Rangers of Ithilien](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien).<sup id="cite_ref-Herbs_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Herbs-5">[5]</a></sup>

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), in early March [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) the son of the Steward [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor), was leading the [Rangers of Ithilien](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien) to ambush Haradrim who would pass through Ithilien.<sup id="cite_ref-Herbs_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Herbs-5">[5]</a></sup> After that [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) led [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) through Ithilien on their way to [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol) and into Mordor, and Faramir allowed them to continue when he was satisfied they were not agents of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:John_Howe_-_The_Mumakil_of_Harad.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/2/2a/John_Howe_-_The_Mumakil_of_Harad.jpg/200px-John_Howe_-_The_Mumakil_of_Harad.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="128" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/2/2a/John_Howe_-_The_Mumakil_of_Harad.jpg/300px-John_Howe_-_The_Mumakil_of_Harad.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/2/2a/John_Howe_-_The_Mumakil_of_Harad.jpg/400px-John_Howe_-_The_Mumakil_of_Harad.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1294" data-file-height="830"/></a><figcaption><i>The Mumakil of Harad</i> by <a href="/wiki/John_Howe" title="John Howe">John Howe</a></figcaption></figure>

When the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Host_of_the_West" title="Host of the West">Host of the West</a> marched on the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon), their scouts, men from [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn) led by [Mablung](</wiki/Mablung_(ranger_of_Ithilien)> "Mablung (ranger of Ithilien)") discovered an ambush by Orcs and Easterlings that lay hidden in Ithilien at the same place where Faramir and his men had previously ambushed the men of Harad. The ambush was quickly turned when horsemen bypassed the Orcs and Easterlings and attacked them on their flank and from behind.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup>

### Later history {#Later_history}

After the fall of Sauron Faramir retained the title of Steward. In addition, King Aragorn gave Ithilien to Faramir to be his princedom and ordered him to live in [Emyn Arnen](/wiki/Emyn_Arnen) as the [Prince of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien). Minas Morgul was destroyed and not repopulated.<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[26]</a></sup> Faramir married [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) after the funeral of King Théoden in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-partings_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-partings-27">[27]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-chief_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chief-28">[28]</a></sup> As the march-warden of Gondor in its main eastern outpost the Prince of Ithilien had the duty to rehabilitate the lost territory and to clear it from Orcs and outlaws.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29">[29]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">[30]</a></sup>

After the fall of Sauron, Legolas brought south some of the [Elves of Mirkwood](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood) where they dwelt in Ithilien, it becoming once again the fairest country in all the Westlands.<sup id="cite_ref-durin_31-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-durin-31">[31]</a></sup> Legolas devised gardens for the house of Faramir and Éowyn in Emyn Arnen.<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32">[32]</a></sup> After King Aragorn died, Legolas built a grey ship and sailed from Ithilien to the West, reportedly taking Gimli with him.<sup id="cite_ref-durin_31-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-durin-31">[31]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33">[33]</a></sup>

*Ithilien* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. It means "land of the moon".<sup id="cite_ref-Ithilien_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ithilien-11">[11]</a></sup> It is a compound of [Ithil](/wiki/Ithil) ("Moon")<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34">[34]</a></sup> and the plural form *[ien](/wiki/-nd "-nd")* of the place name suffix *-ian(d)*.<sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35">[35]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36">[36]</a></sup>

The name does not refer to the moon or the climate, but is related to the 'heraldic' name of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur), the son of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil). It is the counterpart of [Anorien](/wiki/Anorien), the "Land of the Sun".<sup id="cite_ref-Nomenclature_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomenclature-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b><a href="/wiki/1987" title="1987">1987</a>-<a href="/wiki/1996" title="1996">96</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The <i><a href="/wiki/Sea-Lords_of_Gondor" title="Sea-Lords of Gondor">Sea-Lords of Gondor</a> - Pelargir and Lebennin</i> campaign module contains a detailed map that includes Ithilien.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The <i><a href="/wiki/Southern_Gondor:_The_Land" title="Southern Gondor: The Land">Southern Gondor: The Land</a></i> module contains information about settlements and sites, the history, geography, flora and fauna, inhabitants, politics and power and warcraft of Harithilien. It is mentioned that the Emyn Arnen define the boundary dividing Harithilien (South Ithilien) from Forithilien (North Ithilien).</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In the <i><a href="/wiki/The_Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol_and_Shelob%27s_Lair" title="The Tower of Cirith Ungol and Shelob&#39;s Lair">The Tower of Cirith Ungol and Shelob&#39;s Lair</a></i> module there are five adventures that take place in the Tower of Cirith Ungol, Shelob&#39;s Lair and the surrounding area. It is mentioned that the river Ithilduin (later renamed to Morgulduin) divides Ithilien into Northern and Southern provinces.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In the <i><a href="/wiki/Ghosts_of_the_Southern_Anduin" title="Ghosts of the Southern Anduin">Ghosts of the Southern Anduin</a></i> adventure module there are four adventures that take place in and around the town of Bar-en-Tinnen in Ithilien (renamed to Bar-en-Dinnen in <i>Southern Gondor: The Land</i>).</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In the <i><a href="/wiki/Gates_of_Mordor_(book)" title="Gates of Mordor (book)">Gates of Mordor</a></i> adventure module there are two adventures that take place in North Ithilien and one adventure that takes place in <a href="/wiki/Durthang" title="Durthang">Durthang</a> in the northern Ephel Dúath.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The <i><a href="/wiki/Minas_Ithil_(book)" title="Minas Ithil (book)">Minas Ithil</a></i> city module contains detailed information about Minas Ithil (later renamed to Minas Morgul) in T.A. 1640 and three adventures that take place in Minas Ithil.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><a href="/wiki/The_Kin-strife" title="The Kin-strife">The Kin-strife</a> campaign module contains detailed information about Minas Ithil (later renamed to Minas Morgul) during the time of the <a href="/wiki/Kin-strife" title="Kin-strife">Kin-strife</a> and three adventures that take place in Minas Ithil.</i></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Ithilien is split into two game regions, each covering the portion located either north or south of the Cross-roads. Unlike other similar cases in the game, the separation is chronological as well as geographical: South Ithilien is story-wise set before the <a href="/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor" title="Siege of Gondor">Siege of Gondor</a> begins in full, while North Ithilien is set after the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>, with the <a href="/wiki/Army_of_the_West" title="Army of the West">Host of the West</a> marching towards the <a href="/wiki/Black_Gate" title="Black Gate">Black Gate</a>.</dd></dl>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ithilien_(South)_map.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/6/68/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ithilien_%28South%29_map.jpg/180px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ithilien_%28South%29_map.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="135" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/6/68/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ithilien_%28South%29_map.jpg/270px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ithilien_%28South%29_map.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/6/68/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ithilien_%28South%29_map.jpg/360px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ithilien_%28South%29_map.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="768"/></a><figcaption>Map of South Ithilien in<i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i></figcaption></figure>

In the South Ithilien, the main stronghold of the [Rangers of Ithilien](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien) is *Bâr Hurin* commanded by [Anborn](/wiki/Anborn), which is later overrun during the war. In the North Ithilien, *Bâr Aroth* and *Bâr Tarnaher* are similarly overrun, while *Aelin Veren* was only briefly passed by and has since been reclaimed from the Enemy. The secrecy of [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn) has been abandoned, as it is used to support the Host of the West during its march and would serve no further purpose should its mission fail.
