**Isengrim Took III** was the twenty-seventh [Thain of the Shire](/wiki/Thain "Thain").

## History {#History}

The eldest son of the [Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took"), Isengrim's rule was noticeably short. Due to his father's longevity, he was already near his nineties when he became [Thain](/wiki/Thain), and ruled for ten years before his death. As he had no children of his own, his brother [Isumbras](/wiki/Isumbras_Took_IV "Isumbras Took IV") became the new Thain.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Isengrim" is of [Anglo-Saxon](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") origin and comes from *isen* meaning "iron" and *grim* meaning "fierce".

It is likely Isengrim is named after an earlier <a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Isengrim_(disambiguation)" title="Isengrim (disambiguation)">Isengrim</a>, but it is notable that "Isengrim" is the name of a wolf who appears in many medieval epics, including the story of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reynard_the_Fox" title="wikipedia:Reynard the Fox">Reynard the Fox</a> which originated in French folklore.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>
