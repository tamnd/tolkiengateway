**The Idaho and Montana Tolkien Society**, also known as **Skies of Rohan**, is a [Tolkien society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies") in north-west of the United States. In 2011 and 2012 they held none of their own events, so the status of the society is currently unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
