<i><b>ilya</b></i> (pl. *ilyë* or *ilye*) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "all, every; the whole; each, all of a particular group of things".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Nam_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nam-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ety_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UT_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- "<i><a href="/wiki/Ar" title="Ar">ar</a> <b>ilyë</b> <a href="/wiki/Ti%C3%AB" title="Tië">tier</a> undulávë <a href="/wiki/Lumbul%C3%AB" title="Lumbulë">lumbulë</a></i>;" ("and **all** paths are drowned deep in shadow;)"<sup id="cite_ref-Nam_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nam-2">[2]</a></sup>
- "...<i><a href="/wiki/I" title="I">i</a> <a href="/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar" title="Ilúvatar">Eru</a> i <a href="/wiki/Or" title="Or">or</a> <b>ilyë</b> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mahalma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mahalma (page does not exist)">mahalmar</a> <a href="/wiki/Ea_(verb)" title="Ea (verb)">eä</a> <a href="/wiki/Tennoio" title="Tennoio">tennoio</a></i>" ("the One who is above **all** thrones for ever")<sup id="cite_ref-UT_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-6">[6]</a></sup>
