<i><b>iaur</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) adjective meaning "old, ancient, original, former".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=YA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="YA (page does not exist)">YA</a><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Aran_Einior" title="Aran Einior">Aran Ein<b>ior</b></a></i> = "Elder King"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Dr%C3%BAwaith_Iaur" title="Drúwaith Iaur">Drúwaith <b>Iaur</b></a></i> = "Old Púkel land"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Tom_Bombadil" title="Tom Bombadil"><b>Iar</b>wain</a></i> = "Old-young"
- *[Ioreth](/wiki/Ioreth)* = "Old Woman"
- *[Iorhael](/wiki/Iorhael)* = "Frodo" (lit. "Old Wise")
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Sennas_Iaur" title="Sennas Iaur">Sennas <b>Iaur</b></a></i> = "Old Guesthouse"

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) notes that *iâr* in [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh) means "a hen", while *hen* means "old" in Welsh.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[yára](/wiki/Y%C3%A1ra "Yára")*
