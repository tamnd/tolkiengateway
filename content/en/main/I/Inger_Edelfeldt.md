**Inger Edelfeldt** (born [1956](/wiki/1956)) is a Swedish author, translator and artist who has created numerous [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related illustrations, including illustrations for the [Tolkien Calendar 1985](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_1985), [The 1985 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar](/wiki/The_1985_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar), and the [Tolkien Calendar 1998](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_1998). Her [version](/wiki/File:Inger_Edelfeldt_-_Gollum.jpg "File:Inger Edelfeldt - Gollum.jpg") of [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) is actually herself, staring in a mirror.

Perhaps inspired by the fiction works of Tolkien, Edelfeldt wrote *Missne och Robin* (1980), her only book which can be included in the genre of fantasy<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
