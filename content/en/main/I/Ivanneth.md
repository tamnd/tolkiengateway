**Ivanneth** was the name among the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) for the ninth month of the year, more commonly known in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) by its [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) title, [Yavannië](../Y/Yavannië.md), and approximately equivalent to modern September.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The month of Ivanneth in the second year of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) is historically important as the time when [Isildur](Isildur.md) set out from [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) to ride for the [North-kingdom](../A/Arnor.md) of the Dúnedain. Before the month was out, he and his companions were set upon by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) out of the autumn-coloured trees of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md), and the [Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) Isildur bore was lost in the [Great River](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Its name comes from the words *[yávë](../Y/Yávë.md)* ("fruit") and *[anna](../A/Anna.md)* ("gift"), a reference to the harvests of autumn, and connected to the name of [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md), the [Vala](../V/Valar.md) of earth and growth. <span></span>
