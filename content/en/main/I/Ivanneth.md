**Ivanneth** was the name among the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) for the ninth month of the year, more commonly known in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) by its [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) title, [Yavannië](/wiki/Yavanni%C3%AB), and approximately equivalent to modern September.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The month of Ivanneth in the second year of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) is historically important as the time when [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) set out from [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) to ride for the [North-kingdom](/wiki/Arnor "Arnor") of the Dúnedain. Before the month was out, he and his companions were set upon by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) out of the autumn-coloured trees of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood), and the [Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") Isildur bore was lost in the [Great River](/wiki/Anduin "Anduin").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Its name comes from the words *[yávë](/wiki/Y%C3%A1v%C3%AB "Yávë")* ("fruit") and *[anna](/wiki/Anna "Anna")* ("gift"), a reference to the harvests of autumn, and connected to the name of [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna), the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") of earth and growth. <span></span>
