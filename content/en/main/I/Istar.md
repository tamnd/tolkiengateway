**Istar** (pl. <i><b>Istari</b></i>) A [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning approximately "*Wise One*", translated as "*[Wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards")*",<sup id="cite_ref-UT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-1">[1]</a></sup> or "those who know".<sup id="cite_ref-L156_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L156-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word comes from the verb *[ista-](/wiki/Ista- "Ista-")* ("to know"), and possibly the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agentive_ending" title="wikipedia:Agentive ending">agentive ending</a> *[-ro](/wiki/-ro)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

- *Istarion*: "of Wizards"<sup id="cite_ref-UT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-1">[1]</a></sup> (<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genitive_case" title="wikipedia:Genitive case">genitive plural</a>)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[ithron](/wiki/Ithron "Ithron")*<sup id="cite_ref-UT_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Heren Istarion](/wiki/Order_of_Wizards "Order of Wizards")*<sup id="cite_ref-UT_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Istar**, the original, discarded, name of [Tar-Míriel](/wiki/Tar-M%C3%ADriel)
