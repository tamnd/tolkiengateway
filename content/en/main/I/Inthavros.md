**Inthavros** was the "forest palace" of [Tavros](</wiki/Tavros_(wood-fay)> "Tavros (wood-fay)"), the chief [wood-fay](/wiki/Tavari "Tavari"), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Int_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Int-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Inthavros* is in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish). It consists of the element <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=In-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="In- (page does not exist)">in-</a></i> ("house of") and the proper name *Tavros*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

An earlier name for the place was *Intavros*.<sup id="cite_ref-Int_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Int-1">[1]</a></sup>
