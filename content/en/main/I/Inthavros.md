**Inthavros** was the "forest palace" of [Tavros](<../T/Tavros_(wood-fay).md>), the chief [wood-fay](../T/Tavari.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Int_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Int-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Inthavros* is in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md). It consists of the element <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=In-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="In- (page does not exist)">in-</a></i> ("house of") and the proper name *Tavros*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

An earlier name for the place was *Intavros*.<sup id="cite_ref-Int_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Int-1">[1]</a></sup>
