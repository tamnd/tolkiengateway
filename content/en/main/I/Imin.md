**Imin** was the first of the [Elf-fathers](/wiki/Elf-fathers) to [awake](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves "Awakening of the Elves") at [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen), and the forefather of the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar). His [spouse](/wiki/Three_Elderwomen_of_the_Elves "Three Elderwomen of the Elves") was [Iminyë](/wiki/Iminy%C3%AB).

## History {#History}

Imin and [Iminyë](/wiki/Iminy%C3%AB) wandered with the two other Fathers and their spouses, [Tata](/wiki/Tata) and [Tatië](/wiki/Tati%C3%AB), [Enel](/wiki/Enel) and [Enelyë](/wiki/Enely%C3%AB), until they found a group of 12 sleeping Elves which he took with him. That clan was named after him [Minyar](/wiki/Minyar), the "Firsts".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [Cuivienyarna](/wiki/Cuivienyarna), Imin is portrayed as arrogant, due to his being the first Elf to awake. In the process he is seen to be seeking more Elven followers for his clan, and lastly being left with the fewest.

Thereafter, he refused to take the other Elves that they found, hoping to find some group larger than the ones claimed by the other Fathers. However, despite his expectations they found none other and his clan remained with only 14 members.

The clan was later named [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> He also founded the [House of Imin](/wiki/House_of_Imin).<sup id="cite_ref-Father_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Father-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the [Debate of the Quendi](/wiki/Debate_of_the_Quendi), Imin disagreed with the departure from Cuiviénen and, claiming the title of "Father of All Quendi" (*Ilquendatar*), asserted that he had the right of decision.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
