<i><b>Ithildin</b></i> was a substance made by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) out of the metal *[mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril")* and used by the [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-M%C3%ADrdain) in constructions such as gateways. Ithildin could only be seen by the [reflected light](/wiki/Silm%C3%AB "Silmë") of the [Moon](/wiki/Moon) and [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars"), and even then remained hidden until a "[magic](/wiki/Magic "Magic")" word was said. The designs on the [Doors of Durin](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin) were made from this substance.<sup id="cite_ref-FR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) translated *ithildin* as "starmoon".<sup id="cite_ref-FR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") stated that *ithildin* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, meaning "moon-star(light"), "moonlight" or "starlight". The word contains the elements *[Ithil](/wiki/Moon#Other_names "Moon")* ("moon") + *[tin/tîn](/wiki/T%C3%AEn#Noun "Tîn")* ("spark; star; twinkle of stars"). He noted that the correct Sindarin form should be *ithildim*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2024: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Moria_-_Through_the_Doors_of_Durin" title="Moria - Through the Doors of Durin">Moria: Through the Doors of Durin</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Ithildin could be made into an ink, used by Elven lords and Dwarven kings to write secret moon-letters. Additionally, a related substance to ithildin called Erceleb is introduced. Instead of reflecting starlight, it absorbed it. Dwarves marked hidden doors with Erceleb, as the metal glowed brightly when awakened by specific words. Intense moonlight could also make Erceleb record words, which would be repeated when awakened.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>
