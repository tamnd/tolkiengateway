**Imlach** (born [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [310](/wiki/First_Age_310 "First Age 310")) was a son of [Marach](/wiki/Marach), founder of the [House of Marach](/wiki/House_of_Hador "House of Hador"), and the younger brother of [Malach Aradan](/wiki/Malach "Malach").<sup id="cite_ref-Hador_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hador-1">[1]</a></sup> History records almost nothing about his life, except that his son [Amlach](/wiki/Amlach) was among the leaders of dissent at [Estolad](/wiki/Estolad).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
