**Illustrations to J.R.R. Tolkien** is an art book illustrated and printed by Don "D." West ([1945](../_/1945.md)-[2015](../_/2015.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was published in [1971](../_/1971.md) and was limited to 450 hand-colored copies.
