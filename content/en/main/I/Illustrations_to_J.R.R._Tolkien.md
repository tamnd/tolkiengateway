**Illustrations to J.R.R. Tolkien** is an art book illustrated and printed by Don "D." West ([1945](/wiki/1945)-[2015](/wiki/2015)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was published in [1971](/wiki/1971) and was limited to 450 hand-colored copies.
