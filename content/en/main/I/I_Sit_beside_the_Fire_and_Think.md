**I Sit beside the Fire and Think** is a song by [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md), which he sang softly in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) on [24 December](../_/24_December.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the evening before the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) set out upon their quest. Bilbo sang the song in the presence of [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), after giving Frodo the [Mithril-coat](../M/Mithril-coat.md) and [Sting](../S/Sting.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The song is a contemplative piece, sung by a now-aging hobbit recalling past events that ends in anticipation of hearing returning friends.

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic;margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>I sit beside the fire and think<br/>
of all that I have seen<br/>
of meadow-flowers and butterflies<br/>
in summers that have been;<br/>
 <br/>
Of yellow leaves and gossamer<br/>
in autumns that there were,<br/>
with morning mist and silver sun<br/>
and wind upon my hair.<br/>
 <br/>
I sit beside the fire and think<br/>
of how the world will be<br/>
when winter comes without a spring<br/>
that I shall ever see.<br/>
 <br/>
For still there are so many things<br/>
that I have never seen:<br/>
in every wood in every spring<br/>
there is a different green.<br/>
 <br/>
I sit beside the fire and think<br/>
of people long ago<br/>
and people who will see a world<br/>
that I shall never know.<br/>
 <br/>
But all the while I sit and think<br/>
of times there were before,<br/>
I listen for returning feet<br/>
and voices at the door.
</p>
</div>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

[Donald Swann](../D/Donald_Swann.md) wrote music for this song, which can be found in the book *[The Road Goes Ever On](<../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book).md>)*;<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> it is also found on the accompanying CD of the latest version.

[Summoning](../S/Summoning.md) used the poem for lyrics in a song called "A Distant Flame Before The Sun", from the [1999](../_/1999.md) album *[Stronghold](../S/Stronghold.md)*.

Inspired by this poem, [Burzum](<../B/Burzum_(band).md>) wrote the lyrics of the song "Beholding the Daughters of the Firmament".

[Clamavi De Profundis](../C/Clamavi_De_Profundis.md) wrote music for this song and released it as a memorial for Christopher Tolkien.
