<i><b>imbë</b></i> (or <i><b>imbe</b></i>) is a word with two different meanings.

## Quenya {#Quenya}

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word <i><b>imbë</b></i> (or <i><b>imbe</b></i>) means "between".<sup id="cite_ref-RGEO_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RGEO-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

"<i><a href="/wiki/I" title="I">i</a> falmalinnar <b>imbë</b> met, ar hísië</i>" ('on the foaming waves **between** us, and mist')<sup id="cite_ref-RGEO_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RGEO-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Elvish {#Elvish}

The word <i><b>imbe</b></i>, in an unidentified [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) language, is said to mean "in(wards)".<sup id="cite_ref-VT_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested that the word likely is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
