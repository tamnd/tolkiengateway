**Ilu** is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word that means "all, everything, the world". As a cosmological term it includes everything that exists, the created being ([Eä](../E/Eä.md)) but also [God](../G/God.md) and the [spirits](../S/Spirits.md) who created it, and all [souls](../F/Fëa.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the early concept of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), *Ilu* was the name of "the World", signifying *both* the inhabited world and also the whole universe.

Ilu is everything that exists, and comprises of the habitable world proper ([Ambar](../A/Ambar.md)), with its layers of air and skies, the [Sun](../S/Sun.md), the [Moon](../M/Moon.md) and the [stars](../S/Stars.md). All these are enveloped in the outer layer of air, [Vaiya](../V/Vaiya.md).

The boundaries of Ilu are the [Ilurambar](Ilurambar.md), the "shell" that encloses Ilu, separating Vaiya from [kúma](../K/Kúma.md) and non-existence.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

This concept evolved in a different cosmology in *[The Later Quenta Silmarillion](../T/The_Later_Quenta_Silmarillion.md)*, where new concepts are introduced: The habitable world is named *[Arda](../A/Arda.md)*, more or less equivalent to both [Ambar](../A/Ambar.md) and Ilu; the whole existence, or created universe, is named *[Eä](../E/Eä.md)*, which, unlike the former Ilu, is vast and might include other worlds other than Arda.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *Ilu* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and means "universe", and is an alternative form of *[ilúvë](Ilúvë.md)*. They come from the [root](../R/Root.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=IL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="IL (page does not exist)">IL</a> meaning "all".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Ilu does not appear in the published [Silmarillion](../S/Silmarillion.md), but the alternate form *[ilúvë](Ilúvë.md)* survives, as in the name *[Ilúvatar](Ilúvatar.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Christopher Gilson](../C/Christopher_Gilson.md) has noted that the name *Ilu* is presumably inspired by the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akkadian" title="wikipedia:Akkadian">Akkadian</a> word *ilu* ("gods").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> <span></span>
