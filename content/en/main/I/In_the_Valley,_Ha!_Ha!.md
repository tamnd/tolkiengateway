**In the Valley, Ha! Ha!** is the second track on *[The Hobbit: The Complete Original Soundtrack](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Complete_Original_Soundtrack.md)*, with its reprise being the twelfth track. They were respectively adapted from the poems "[O! What are you doing,](../O/O!_What_are_you_doing,.md)" and "[The dragon is withered](../T/The_dragon_is_withered.md)" from *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*. While sung by [Glenn Yarbrough](../G/Glenn_Yarbrough.md) on the soundtrack, the version that appears in the [1977](../_/1977.md) [film adaptation](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>) is sung by the cast members. The reprise, however, is sung by the cast members in both the film *and* on the soundtrack.

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Oh, what are you doing,<br/>
And where are you going?<br/>
Your <a href="../P/Ponies.md" title="Ponies">ponies</a> need shoeing,<br/>
The river is flowing!<br/>
Oh, tra-la-la-lally<br/>
Here down in <a href="../R/Rivendell.md" title="Rivendell">the valley</a>, ha! ha!<br/>
<br/>
Oh, where are you going<br/>
With beards all a-wagging?<br/>
No knowing, no knowing,<br/>
What brings <a href="../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md" title="Bilbo Baggins">Mr. Baggins</a>,<br/>
And <a href="../B/Balin.md" title="Balin">Balin</a> and <a href="../D/Dwalin.md" title="Dwalin">Dwalin</a><br/>
In June? in the valley, ha! ha!<br/>
<br/>
Oh, will you be staying,<br/>
Or will you be flying?<br/>
Your ponies are straying!<br/>
The daylight is dying!<br/>
<br/>
To fly would be folly,<br/>
To stay would be jolly.<br/>
Tra-lil-lil-lil-lolly, ha! ha!<br/>
<br/>
Oh, what are you doing,<br/>
And where are you going?<br/>
Your ponies need shoeing,<br/>
The river is flowing!<br/>
Oh, tra-la-la-lally<br/>
Here down in the valley, ha! ha!
</p>
</div>

**REPRISE:**

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p><a href="../S/Smaug.md" title="Smaug">The dragon</a> is withered,<br/>
His bones are now crumbled.<br/>
His armour is shrivered,<br/>
His splendour is humbled!<br/>
Oh, tra-la-la-lally<br/>
Here down in the valley, ha! ha!<br/>
<br/>
Though sword shall be rusted,<br/>
And throne and crown perish<br/>
With strength that <a href="../M/Men.md" title="Men">Men</a> trusted,<br/>
And wealth that they cherish,<br/>
Oh, tra-la-la-lally<br/>
Here down in the valley, ha! ha!<br/>
<br/>
The stars are far brighter<br/>
Than gems without measure,<br/>
The moon is far whiter<br/>
Than silver in treasure<br/>
<br/>
The leaves are yet swinging,<br/>
And <a href="../E/Elves.md" title="Elves">Elves</a> are yet singing,<br/>
Come back to the valley, ha! ha!<br/>
<br/>
Oh, where are you going,<br/>
So late in returning?<br/>
The river is flowing,<br/>
The stars are all burning!<br/>
Oh, tra-la-la-lally<br/>
Here down in the valley, ha! ha!
</p>
</div>
