**In the Valley, Ha! Ha!** is the second track on *[The Hobbit: The Complete Original Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Complete_Original_Soundtrack)*, with its reprise being the twelfth track. They were respectively adapted from the poems "[O! What are you doing,](/wiki/O!_What_are_you_doing,)" and "[The dragon is withered](/wiki/The_dragon_is_withered)" from *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. While sung by [Glenn Yarbrough](/wiki/Glenn_Yarbrough) on the soundtrack, the version that appears in the [1977](/wiki/1977) [film adaptation](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)") is sung by the cast members. The reprise, however, is sung by the cast members in both the film *and* on the soundtrack.

<p>Oh, what are you doing,<br/>
And where are you going?<br/>
Your <a href="/wiki/Ponies" title="Ponies">ponies</a> need shoeing,<br/>
The river is flowing!<br/>
Oh, tra-la-la-lally<br/>
Here down in <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">the valley</a>, ha! ha!<br/>
<br/>
Oh, where are you going<br/>
With beards all a-wagging?<br/>
No knowing, no knowing,<br/>
What brings <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Mr. Baggins</a>,<br/>
And <a href="/wiki/Balin" title="Balin">Balin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Dwalin" title="Dwalin">Dwalin</a><br/>
In June? in the valley, ha! ha!<br/>
<br/>
Oh, will you be staying,<br/>
Or will you be flying?<br/>
Your ponies are straying!<br/>
The daylight is dying!<br/>
<br/>
To fly would be folly,<br/>
To stay would be jolly.<br/>
Tra-lil-lil-lil-lolly, ha! ha!<br/>
<br/>
Oh, what are you doing,<br/>
And where are you going?<br/>
Your ponies need shoeing,<br/>
The river is flowing!<br/>
Oh, tra-la-la-lally<br/>
Here down in the valley, ha! ha!
</p>
</div>

**REPRISE:**

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p><a href="/wiki/Smaug" title="Smaug">The dragon</a> is withered,<br/>
His bones are now crumbled.<br/>
His armour is shrivered,<br/>
His splendour is humbled!<br/>
Oh, tra-la-la-lally<br/>
Here down in the valley, ha! ha!<br/>
<br/>
Though sword shall be rusted,<br/>
And throne and crown perish<br/>
With strength that <a href="/wiki/Men" title="Men">Men</a> trusted,<br/>
And wealth that they cherish,<br/>
Oh, tra-la-la-lally<br/>
Here down in the valley, ha! ha!<br/>
<br/>
The stars are far brighter<br/>
Than gems without measure,<br/>
The moon is far whiter<br/>
Than silver in treasure<br/>
<br/>
The leaves are yet swinging,<br/>
And <a href="/wiki/Elves" title="Elves">Elves</a> are yet singing,<br/>
Come back to the valley, ha! ha!<br/>
<br/>
Oh, where are you going,<br/>
So late in returning?<br/>
The river is flowing,<br/>
The stars are all burning!<br/>
Oh, tra-la-la-lally<br/>
Here down in the valley, ha! ha!
</p>
