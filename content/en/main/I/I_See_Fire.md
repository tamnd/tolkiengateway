**I See Fire** is a song, written and performed by [Ed Sheeran](../E/Ed_Sheeran.md), that features in the end credits of *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md)*. It is also the fourteenth track of the second disc of both *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack.md)* and [special edition soundtrack](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack_-_Special_Edition.md).
