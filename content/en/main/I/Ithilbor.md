**Ithilbor** was the chief of the [Guest-elves](../G/Guest-elves.md), and a member of [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md)'s council.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Nothing else is known about him, except that he was the father of [Saeros](../S/Saeros.md), a Green-elf of [Arthórien](../A/Arthórien.md) who resided long in Thingol's court in [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

His name is not given an explanation but is apparently composed of the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) words *[Ithil](Ithil.md)* + *-[paur](../P/Paur.md)*, which translates as "Moon-fist".
