**Ithilbor** was the chief of the [Guest-elves](/wiki/Guest-elves), and a member of [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol)'s council.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Nothing else is known about him, except that he was the father of [Saeros](/wiki/Saeros), a Green-elf of [Arthórien](/wiki/Arth%C3%B3rien) who resided long in Thingol's court in [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

His name is not given an explanation but is apparently composed of the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) words *[Ithil](/wiki/Ithil)* + *-[paur](/wiki/Paur "Paur")*, which translates as "Moon-fist".
