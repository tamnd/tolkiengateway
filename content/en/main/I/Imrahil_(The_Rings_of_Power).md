**Imrahil** was a [Man](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) during the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Imrahil was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) and is portrayed by an unknown actor.

## History {#History}

Imrahil was a cadet in training to become a mariner of the [Sea Guard](/wiki/Guild_of_Venturers "Guild of Venturers") in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). He is the son of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sail_Master&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sail Master (page does not exist)">Sail Master</a> and was a cadet in training to become a part of the [Sea Guard](/wiki/Guild_of_Venturers "Guild of Venturers") in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). He was a friend of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur), [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(The_Rings_of_Power)> "Valandil (The Rings of Power)") and [Ontamo](/wiki/Ontamo). During one cadet training session, Imrahil made a mistake that led him to go flying off the ship, clinging to the rope that he was supposed to be responsible for. Fortunately for Imrahil, Isildur reacted fast and called the Valandil and Ontamo over to help real Imrahil back onto the ship. Though Imrahil suffered no wounds, he was dismissed from the Sea Guard recruitment training. When the ship returned to Númenor, Ontamo expressed pity for Imrahil while Valandil remarked that Imrahil's father, the Sail Master, did not look pleased. Coincidentally, this was nine days before the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sea_Trial&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sea Trial (page does not exist)">Sea Trial</a>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Imrahil* is said to be of [Númenórean](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic "Adûnaic") origin,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> though its exact meaning is uncertain.
