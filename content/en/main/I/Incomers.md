**Incomers** refers to the [Easterling](</wiki/Easterlings_(First_Age)> "Easterlings (First Age)") usurpers who ruled the land of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) granted them by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) after their treachery in the of [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad "Nirnaeth Arnoediad"). It was a term used by the remnant of the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador) left as serfs and thralls under the tyranny of [Brodda](/wiki/Brodda) of the House of [Ulfang](/wiki/Ulfang).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Lady [Aerin](/wiki/Aerin) used this term in her meeting with [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) [Mormegil](/wiki/Mormegil) after his sack of the Hall of Brodda.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name *Incomers* was also used in a rejected version of the legend of [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth) and [Nimrodel](/wiki/Nimrodel), apparently referring to [Elves](/wiki/Elves) from lost [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) which entered [Lórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
