**In Dwimordene, in Lórien** was sung by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in [Meduseld](/wiki/Meduseld) on [2 March](/wiki/2_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> as an answer to [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma")'s insulting claim that "webs of deceit were ever woven in [Dwimordene](/wiki/Dwimordene)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Song {#Song}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>In Dwimordene, in Lórien<br/>
Seldom have walked the feet of Men,<br/>
Few mortal eyes have seen the light<br/>
That lies there ever, long and bright.<br/>
Galadriel! Galadriel!<br/>
Clear is the water of your well;<br/>
White is the star in your white hand;<br/>
Unmarred, unstained is leaf and land<br/>
In Dwimordene, in Lórien<br/>
More fair than thoughts of Mortal Men.
</p>
</div>
