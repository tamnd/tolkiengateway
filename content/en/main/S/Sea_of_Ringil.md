The **Sea of Ringil** was an inland sea south of the [Sea of Helcar](Sea_of_Helcar.md). It was created by the fall of the Lamp [Ormal](../O/Ormal.md), which was atop the tower known as [Ringil](<../R/Ringil_(tower).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-5b_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5b-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5e_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5e-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the tumult of the [Battle of the Powers](../B/Battle_of_the_Powers.md), it grew in size and "became a great sea flowing north-eastward and joining by straights both the [Western](../B/Belegaer.md) and [Eastern Seas](../E/East_Sea.md)"<sup id="cite_ref-5b_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5b-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5e_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5e-2">[2]</a></sup> and divided Middle-earth into two landmasses, the southern of which was called the [Dark Land](../D/Dark_Land.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
