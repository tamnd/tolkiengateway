The **Sea of Ringil** was an inland sea south of the [Sea of Helcar](/wiki/Sea_of_Helcar). It was created by the fall of the Lamp [Ormal](/wiki/Ormal), which was atop the tower known as [Ringil](</wiki/Ringil_(tower)> "Ringil (tower)").<sup id="cite_ref-5b_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5b-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5e_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5e-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the tumult of the [Battle of the Powers](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers), it grew in size and "became a great sea flowing north-eastward and joining by straights both the [Western](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") and [Eastern Seas](/wiki/East_Sea "East Sea")"<sup id="cite_ref-5b_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5b-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5e_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5e-2">[2]</a></sup> and divided Middle-earth into two landmasses, the southern of which was called the [Dark Land](/wiki/Dark_Land).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
