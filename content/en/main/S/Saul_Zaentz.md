**Saul Zaentz** ([12 February](/wiki/12_February), [1921](/wiki/1921)-[3 January](/wiki/3_January), [2014](/wiki/2014)) was an American film producer. He was the founder of the *Saul Zaentz Company*. [Middle-earth Enterprises](/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises), that owns the worldwide film, stage, and merchandise rights to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, until [2022](/wiki/2022), was a division of the Saul Zaentz Company and it was too created by Zaentz. He acquired the film, stage, and merchandise rights of these two books from [United Artists](/wiki/United_Artists) in [1976](/wiki/1976).

He was credited as the producer of [The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)>).
