**Saul Zaentz** ([12 February](../_/12_February.md), [1921](../_/1921.md)-[3 January](../_/3_January.md), [2014](../_/2014.md)) was an American film producer. He was the founder of the *Saul Zaentz Company*. [Middle-earth Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md), that owns the worldwide film, stage, and merchandise rights to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, until [2022](../_/2022.md), was a division of the Saul Zaentz Company and it was too created by Zaentz. He acquired the film, stage, and merchandise rights of these two books from [United Artists](../U/United_Artists.md) in [1976](../_/1976.md).

He was credited as the producer of [The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>).
