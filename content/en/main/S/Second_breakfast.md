**Second breakfast** was a meal taken by [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) on the morning he set off on the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor).<sup id="cite_ref-Roast_Mutton_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roast_Mutton-1">[1]</a></sup> It was possibly one of the six meals a day eaten by [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>As <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Strider</a> leads the <a href="/wiki/Travellers" title="Travellers">Travellers</a> to <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Hobbits" title="Hobbits">Hobbits</a> begin to unpack their cooking supplies. Strider tells them that they won&#39;t stop until night. <a href="/wiki/Peregrin_Took" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a> asks when they will take breakfast and Strider reminds him that they&#39;ve already eaten it. Pippin replies &#34;Well, we&#39;ve had one, yes. What about second breakfast?&#34;. Strider turns around and continues walking. <a href="/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a> says &#34;Don&#39;t think he knows about second breakfast, Pip&#34;.</dd></dl>
