<i><b>SEVEN 28</b></i> is the twenty-eighth issue of the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md)'s annual journal *[SEVEN](SEVEN.md)*, published in [2011](../_/2011.md).<sup id="cite_ref-jstor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-jstor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "C.S. Lewis's 'Easley Fragment'"
    - By: David C. Downing and Bruce R. Johnson
- "G.K. Chesterton's Portrait of George MacDonald"
    - By: Daniel Gabelman
- "Dorothy L. Sayers and the Creative Reader"
    - By: Chris Willerton
- "Tolkien's Beautiful Sorrow"
    - By: Michael David Elam
- "Sayers and the Somersham Pageant"
    - By: Martin Ferguson Smith
- "Two Essays on George MacDonald," with an introduction by Daniel Gabelman
    - By: G.K. Chesteron
- "Quality or Quantity? A Response to Justin Barrett's Quantitative Analysis of Planet Narnia"
    - By: Michael Ward<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
