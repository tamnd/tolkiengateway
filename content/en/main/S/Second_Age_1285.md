**Second Age 1285** (abbreviated to **S.A. 1285**) is the 1285th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Tar-Ancalimë](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
