**Steven Jay Blum** ([April 28](/wiki/28_April "28 April"), [1965](/wiki/1965)) is an American voice actor. His most recognized roles are that of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverine_(comics)" title="wikipedia:Wolverine (comics)">Wolverine</a>, and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spike_Spiegel" title="wikipedia:Spike Spiegel">Spike Spiegel</a> in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cowboy_Bebop" title="wikipedia:Cowboy Bebop">Cowboy Bebop</a></i>. He is currently married to [Mary Elizabeth McGlynn](/wiki/Mary_Elizabeth_McGlynn),who is also a voice actress.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Roles {#Roles}

- [2014](/wiki/2014): *[Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor)*: [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)
- [2011](/wiki/2011): *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North)*: Unspecified
- [2010](/wiki/2010): *[The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn's Quest](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Aragorn%27s_Quest)*: Unspecified
- [2006](/wiki/2006): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II)*: Unspecified
- [2003](/wiki/2003): *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring)*: [Varin](/wiki/Varin), Gondor Swordsman, Dwarf Shieldbreaker
