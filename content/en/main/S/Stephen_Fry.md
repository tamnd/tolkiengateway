**Stephen John Fry** (b. [August 24](/wiki/24_August "24 August"), [1957](/wiki/1957)) is an English actor, screenwriter, author, playwright, journalist, poet, comedian, television presenter and film director. He portrayed the [Master of Lake-town](/wiki/Master_of_Lake-town) in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").
