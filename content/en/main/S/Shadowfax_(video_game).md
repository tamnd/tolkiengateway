**Shadowfax** is a commercial, unlicensed arcade action video game released in [1982](/wiki/1982).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup> The primary designer was [Mike Singleton](/wiki/Mike_Singleton), who later worked on [Interplay](/wiki/Interplay_Productions "Interplay Productions")'s *[J.R.R. Tolkien's War in Middle Earth](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_War_in_Middle_Earth)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Design {#Design}

The graphics of the moving horses came from *Computer Race*, an earlier game by Mike Singleton (which was cancelled due to the United Kingdom's betting laws), and were in fact taken from the photos of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eadweard_Muybridge" title="wikipedia:Eadweard Muybridge">Eadweard Muybridge</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-interview_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-interview-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Gameplay {#Gameplay}

The player is [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), riding on [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax); the objective is to kill the chasing [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) with lightning.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
