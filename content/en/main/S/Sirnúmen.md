**Sirnúmen** was the dwelling place of the [Noldoli](../N/Noldoli.md) in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) after they were banished from their original home of [Kôr](../K/Kôr.md), and the place from which [Melko](../M/Melko.md) stole the [Silmarils](Silmarils.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

## History {#History}

[Melko](../M/Melko.md), jealous of the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md), especially the [Noldoli](../N/Noldoli.md) and their skills in making beautiful things, sought to spread lies and discord among them while they dwelt in their city of Kôr, and told lies to [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) regarding them. The [Noldoli](../N/Noldoli.md) sent an embassy to Manwë as well, speaking against Melko and his lies. Manwë chided both, sending Melko away to [Mandos](../H/Halls_of_Mandos.md), and instructing Aulë to find a new place for the Noldoli to dwell, fearing that their discontent would spread among the others at Kôr. The happiness of the Noldoli was greatly diminished then, both as a result of Melko's treachery and of the loss of their home.<sup id="cite_ref-Theft_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Theft-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:141–2</sup>

The place the Noldoli chose to build their new home was situated alongside the small stream [Híri](../H/Híri.md), which plunged underground north of the roots of [Silpion](Silpion.md). There was a rock-ringed dale there, where the Noldoli made caves in the walls and stored their precious gems and metals. They considered it their temporary home, always believing that they would one day return to Kôr.<sup id="cite_ref-Theft_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Theft-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:142</sup>

They remained there until the great feast called [Samírien](Samírien.md), which occurred every twenty-one years, was approaching. For the celebration, all of the Eldar fared to [Valmar](../V/Valmar.md), including those that dwelt at Sirnúmen.<sup id="cite_ref-Theft_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Theft-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:143</sup> On the third day of that feast, Melko, knowing that Sirnúmen would be emptied, crept there with many of his dark accomplices. He was surprised to find that the Noldoli, grown wary, had left guards to protect their treasury despite the custom of the feast. Melko and his followers slew the guards, one of which was [Bruithwir](../B/Bruithwir.md), the father of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md), and plundered the gems kept there, including the [Silmarils](Silmarils.md) wrought by Fëanor.<sup id="cite_ref-Theft_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Theft-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:144–5</sup>

Returning to Sirnúmen, the Noldoli found their guards slain and were distraught, though Manwë told them that such a tragedy would not have befallen them if they had not broken tradition and left some of their number behind. When a messenger of Melko arrived at Valmar, riders were sent to Sirnúmen to summon the Elves that dwelt there, as the matter concerned them. Manwë sent a message to Melko banishing him from Valinor forever.<sup id="cite_ref-Theft_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Theft-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:146–8</sup> Then [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) spoke to Manwë on behalf of the Noldoli, and Manwë granted the Noldoli permission to return to Kôr if they wished. Many did so, but Fëanor and a few folk remained in Sirnúmen. There Fëanor brooded, and his discontent grew, until at last he went to Kôr, speaking against the Valar and urging the Noldoli to leave Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-Theft_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Theft-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:149–50</sup>

Later, Melko and [Gwerlum](../G/Gwerlum.md), sneaking into Valinor with the intent of destroying the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md), were briefly hindered by [Daurin](../D/Daurin.md), one of the Noldoli from Sirnúmen. Melko slew Daurin and finished destroying the trees, while those that had witnessed Daurin's death fled to alert the Valar of what they had seen.<sup id="cite_ref-Theft_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Theft-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:153</sup> Following the destruction of the Two Trees, Fëanor succeeded in persuading his people to arm themselves and [leave Valinor](../F/Flight_of_the_Noldor.md). However, they left many of their youngest children in Kôr and Sirnúmen.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Sirnúmen* is [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), apparently derived from root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=SIRI&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SIRI (page does not exist)">SIRI</a> ("flow") + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=N%C3%BAm%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Númë (page does not exist)">númë</a></i> ("West").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In his commentary, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) translates it as 'Western Stream'.<sup id="cite_ref-VIn_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VIn-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:157</sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In its first two occurrences in the text, the name was originally written *Numessir* and was changed to *Sirnúmen*. In all later occurrences, *Sirnúmen* was the first form written.<sup id="cite_ref-VIn_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VIn-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:155</sup>

Referencing this same location, while composing the *Lost Tales*, [J. R. R. Tolkien](../J/J._R._R._Tolkien.md) wrote in a separate notebook that, "The river of the second rocky dwelling of the Gnomes in Valinor was *[kelusindi](../K/Kelusindi.md)* and the spring at its source *[kapalinda](../K/Kapalinda.md)*."<sup id="cite_ref-VIn_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VIn-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:157</sup>

Christopher Tolkien notes that Sirnúmen is the precursor of [Formenos](../F/Formenos.md),<sup id="cite_ref-VIn_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VIn-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:158</sup> the location in the later [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) where Fëanor and a number of his [people](../N/Noldor.md) dwell when they are exiled from [Tirion](../T/Tirion.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and where Fëanor's [father](../F/Finwë.md) is slain by [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md) during the theft of the Silmarils.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
