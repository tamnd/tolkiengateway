An [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs"), addressed as **Snaga**, was a scout under the command of [Uglúk](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk), and part of a company who was bringing [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) and [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) to [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard). He was killed when [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer)'s riders attacked their camp.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

**Snaga** was a contemptuous term for the lesser [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), used especially among the larger and stronger [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai). It comes from a word in the [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech) meaning 'slave' or 'servant'.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
