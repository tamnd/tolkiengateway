**Solosimpi** ("Pipers of the Shores") were an Elven race in the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. They are the predecessors of the [Falmari](../F/Falmari.md) in later versions, while the name *[Teleri](../T/Teleri.md)* was given to the clan of [Elves](../E/Elves.md) known as the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md).

## History {#History}

Their leader was [Tinwelint](../T/Tinwelint.md) but after he was lost, they chose [Ellu Melemno](../E/Ellu.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Solosimpi dwelt in [Kopas Alqaluntë](../K/Kopas_Alqaluntë.md) but many were [slaughtered](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md) by the [Gnomes](../G/Gnomes.md) under [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Their preferred weapon was the [bow](../B/Bow.md) and arrow, in contrast to the spears of the [Teleri](../V/Vanyar.md) and the swords of the Gnomes.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
