**Solosimpi** ("Pipers of the Shores") were an Elven race in the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. They are the predecessors of the [Falmari](/wiki/Falmari) in later versions, while the name *[Teleri](/wiki/Teleri)* was given to the clan of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) known as the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar).

## History {#History}

Their leader was [Tinwelint](/wiki/Tinwelint) but after he was lost, they chose [Ellu Melemno](/wiki/Ellu "Ellu").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Solosimpi dwelt in [Kopas Alqaluntë](/wiki/Kopas_Alqalunt%C3%AB) but many were [slaughtered](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB "Kinslaying at Alqualondë") by the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) under [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Their preferred weapon was the [bow](/wiki/Bow "Bow") and arrow, in contrast to the spears of the [Teleri](/wiki/Vanyar "Vanyar") and the swords of the Gnomes.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
