**Stephen Ure** (born [March 28](/wiki/28_March "28 March"), [1958](/wiki/1958)) is an Australian actor who played [Grishnákh](/wiki/Grishn%C3%A1kh) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*, [Gorbag](/wiki/Gorbag) in *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King")*, [Grinnah](/wiki/Grinnah) in *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)*, and [Fimbul](/wiki/Fimbul) in both *An Unexpected Journey* and *[The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug "The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug")*.

In June [2007](/wiki/2007), he and 14 other actors filed <a class="external text" href="https://us.imdb.com/news/ni0058227/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">a lawsuit</a> against [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) over merchandising royalties.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The action was settled out of court in October [2008](/wiki/2008).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
