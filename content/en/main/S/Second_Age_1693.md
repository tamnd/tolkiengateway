**Second Age 1693** (abbreviated to **S.A. 1693**) is the 1693rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [War of the Elves and Sauron](../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-AppSA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppSA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The [Three Rings](../T/Three_Rings.md) are hidden.<sup id="cite_ref-AppSA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppSA-1">[1]</a></sup>
