**Sauron's assault on Osgiliath** occurred on 20 June, [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), and was later recognized as the beginning of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

For many years (since [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2901](/wiki/Third_Age_2901 "Third Age 2901")) the land of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) had been held by Sauron's forces, but he had not breached the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) to attack deeper into Gondor, biding his time as he marshaled his strength.<sup id="cite_ref-hunt_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hunt-2">[2]</a></sup> By the end of the Age, Sauron had gathered all he could from the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power) and started searching for [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") in [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2951](/wiki/Third_Age_2951 "Third Age 2951") he declared himself openly, gathered forces and fortified [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [3009](/wiki/Third_Age_3009 "Third Age 3009") [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) was captured after he had ventured into [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and the Dark Lord learned that his Ring was in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), a location unknown to him. Gollum was released from Mordor in [3017](/wiki/Third_Age_3017 "Third Age 3017") and was soon taken by [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in the [Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup> His capture alarmed Sauron, causing him to send the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) out to attack the deserted ruins of [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) and hunt for the Ring. Sauron had two purposes in making this assault: First, to test the strength and preparedness of [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) (which was found to be greater than he hoped); and second (and more importantly) to make the appearance of the Nazgûl seem to be only part of his war policy against [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), in order to conceal from the [Wise](/wiki/Wise) their mission to hunt for [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").<sup id="cite_ref-hunt_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hunt-2">[2]</a></sup>

Days before the attack, the sons of Denethor, [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) and [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), had a [prophetic dream](/wiki/Seek_for_the_Sword_that_was_broken "Seek for the Sword that was broken").<sup id="cite_ref-council_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Battle {#Battle}

At the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond), [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) recounted this battle as a sudden attack in which the men of Gondor were outnumbered by the forces of Mordor with their allies, the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) and [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim). However, he attributed Gondor's defeat to a power that had not been felt before, issuing from a great black horseman. Only a remnant of Gondor's eastern force survived. The last bridge across the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) was defended by a company led by Boromir and [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) until the structure was destroyed. Only the two brothers and two others survived by swimming.<sup id="cite_ref-council_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the eastern portion of Osgiliath was taken and the bridge broken, Sauron broke off the assault, ordering the Nazgûl to begin their search for the Ring.<sup id="cite_ref-hunt_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hunt-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

[Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) was raided on the same day, probably in a scheduled attack, as the captured [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) found an opportunity to escape during the chaos.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB3_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB3-1">[1]</a></sup>

The battle allowed the Black Riders to set forth and search for the One Ring in the west.

News of the defeat and the [Black Shadow](/wiki/Black_Shadow) reached [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) and the borders of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") by the end of [June](/wiki/June). [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) had an uneasy feeling when he got those messages and when he talked with refugees from the South he felt that they had a fear of which they didn't talk.<sup id="cite_ref-council_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-3">[3]</a></sup>

It was the first conflict of the War of the Ring. Sauron found Denethor's forces stronger than he expected.<sup id="cite_ref-hunt_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hunt-2">[2]</a></sup> For months thereafter Sauron continued to amass his forces in Mordor preparing for a full assault against Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> However when he discovered the [Heir of Isildur](/wiki/Heir_of_Isildur), his plans were altered<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and proceeded hastily with his ready forces; [another skirmish occurred in Osgiliath](/wiki/Faramir%27s_defence_of_Osgiliath "Faramir's defence of Osgiliath") leading up to the [Siege of Gondor](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor).
