"**Seek for the Sword that was broken:**" was a [rhyme](/wiki/Poems_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "Poems in The Lord of the Rings") that appeared in dreams to [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) and later his brother [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir).<sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Poem excerpt {#Poem_excerpt}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p><span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 1em;">Seek for the <a href="/wiki/Narsil" title="Narsil">Sword that was broken</a>:</span><br/>
<span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 2em;">In <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Imladris</a> it dwells;</span><br/>
<span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 1em;">There shall be counsels taken</span><br/>
<span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 2em;">Stronger than <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Morgul-spells" title="Morgul-spells">Morgul-spells</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup></span>
</p>
</div>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_After_the_dream.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/d/d9/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_After_the_dream.jpg/300px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_After_the_dream.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="172" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/d/d9/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_After_the_dream.jpg/450px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_After_the_dream.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/d/d9/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_After_the_dream.jpg/600px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_After_the_dream.jpg 2x" data-file-width="838" data-file-height="480"/></a><figcaption><i>After the dream</i> by <a href="/wiki/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann" title="Anke Eißmann">Anke Eißmann</a></figcaption></figure>

When Faramir had the dream, he did not speak of it. He had the dream twice again, and his brother had it once. Neither knew what exactly the "Doom" and "Isildur's Bane" referred to, nor where "Imladris" was. Their [father](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor") told Boromir that Imladris was where Lord [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) dwelt, and the road being too dangerous, Boromir took it upon himself to follow this lead.<sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Background {#Background}

In a rough outline of the Council of Elrond written in [August](/wiki/August) of [1940](/wiki/1940), the following words allude to the poem:<sup id="cite_ref-treason_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-treason-2">[2]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Prophecies had been spoken. The Broken Sword should be reforged. Our wise men said the Broken Sword was in Rivendell.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Boromir at the <a href="/wiki/Council_of_Elrond" title="Council of Elrond">Council of Elrond</a><sup id="cite_ref-treason_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-treason-2">[2]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

The earliest form of the "dream-verse" was lost along with the page containing it. As a consequence, it cannot be known when [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) first wrote the poem.<sup id="cite_ref-treason_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-treason-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [September](/wiki/September) of [2024](/wiki/2024), the poem was reprinted near the end of [entry 156](/wiki/Riddle_of_Strider "Riddle of Strider") in *[The Collected Poems of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Collected_Poems_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)*.<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1981: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)">The Lord of the Rings</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The song is sung by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Matthew_Vine&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Matthew Vine (page does not exist)">Matthew Vine</a> in a high-pitched voice at the Council of Elrond. It is the reason for Boromir&#39;s coming to Rivendell, and it is explained by <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> and <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a>.</dd></dl>
