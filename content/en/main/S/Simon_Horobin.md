Dr. **Simon C.P. Horobin** is a Reader of Medieval English Language and Tutorial Fellow at [Magdalen College, Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md). Before that, he served as lecturer in English Language at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow" title="wikipedia:University of Glasgow">University of Glasgow</a>. His main field of study is Middle English, and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Chaucer" title="wikipedia:Geoffrey Chaucer">Chaucer</a> in particular.
