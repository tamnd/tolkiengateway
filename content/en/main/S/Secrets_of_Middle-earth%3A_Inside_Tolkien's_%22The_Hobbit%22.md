**Secrets of Middle-earth: Inside Tolkien's "The Hobbit"** is a [2003](/wiki/2003) documentary directed, written and conducted by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Bob_Carruthers&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bob Carruthers (page does not exist)">Bob Carruthers</a>. It features art by [The Brothers Hildebrandt](/wiki/The_Brothers_Hildebrandt), music by [Mostly Autumn](/wiki/Mostly_Autumn), and interview segments with [Priscilla Tolkien](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien), [John Tolkien](/wiki/John_Tolkien), [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter), [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin), [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey) and [Tim Hildebrandt](/wiki/Tim_Hildebrandt).
