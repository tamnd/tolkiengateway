**Sam's garden box** was the [gift](../G/Gifts_of_Galadriel.md) [Samwise Gamgee](Samwise_Gamgee.md) received from [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md).

## History {#History}

Before the [Company of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) left [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md), [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) presented each of them with gifts. To [Sam](Samwise_Gamgee.md) she gave a little box of plain grey wood, unadorned save for a single silver rune upon the lid.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It contained earth from Galadriel's orchard, the earth was like grey dust, soft and fine, and in the middle of which was a seed, like a small nut with a silver shell.

After the [Scouring of the Shire](Scouring_of_the_Shire.md), Sam planted saplings in all the places where specially beautiful and beloved trees had been destroyed, and he put a grain of the precious dust in the soil at the root of each. The little silver nut he planted in the [Party Field](../P/Party_Field.md) to replace the [Party Tree](../P/Party_Tree.md), and the nut grew into the [Golden Tree](../G/Golden_Tree.md): "the only Mallorn west of the [Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) and east of the [Sea](../B/Belegaer.md), and one of the finest in the world".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
