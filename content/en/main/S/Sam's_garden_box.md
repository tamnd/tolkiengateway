**Sam's garden box** was the [gift](/wiki/Gifts_of_Galadriel "Gifts of Galadriel") [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) received from [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel).

## History {#History}

Before the [Company of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") left [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) presented each of them with gifts. To [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") she gave a little box of plain grey wood, unadorned save for a single silver rune upon the lid.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It contained earth from Galadriel's orchard, the earth was like grey dust, soft and fine, and in the middle of which was a seed, like a small nut with a silver shell.

After the [Scouring of the Shire](/wiki/Scouring_of_the_Shire), Sam planted saplings in all the places where specially beautiful and beloved trees had been destroyed, and he put a grain of the precious dust in the soil at the root of each. The little silver nut he planted in the [Party Field](/wiki/Party_Field) to replace the [Party Tree](/wiki/Party_Tree), and the nut grew into the [Golden Tree](/wiki/Golden_Tree): "the only Mallorn west of the [Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains "Misty Mountains") and east of the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer"), and one of the finest in the world".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
