**Swords** were long-bladed tools and weapons used by many different peoples of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

## By race {#By_race}

### Ainur {#Ainur}

[Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) wielded [a sword](/wiki/Sword_of_Manw%C3%AB "Sword of Manwë") during the [War of the Powers](/wiki/War_of_the_Powers). It was later given to his herald [Eönwë](/wiki/E%C3%B6nw%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) also took to wielding the elven sword [Glamdring](/wiki/Glamdring) after coming into possession of it.<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-2">[2]</a></sup> [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane) also carried a flaming sword.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Elves {#Elves}

The [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) began forging swords in response to the lies spread among them by [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor). [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) fashioned several for himself and his [sons](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor"), notably using his own to threaten his half-brother [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) with death.<sup id="cite_ref-Silmarils_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Silmarils-4">[4]</a></sup> The host of the Noldor also wielded swords in the [Kinslaying at Alqualondë](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB), with the opposing [Falmari](/wiki/Falmari) lacking any such implements.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Among the most notable blades of the Noldor was [Ringil](/wiki/Ringil), the blade of Fingolfin, which he wielded in his [clash](/wiki/Fall_of_Fingolfin "Fall of Fingolfin") with Morgoth.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Fearing the return of Melkor, King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) of the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) sought the aid of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) and [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost). These provided the elves of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) with many weapons, including swords.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindar_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindar-7">[7]</a></sup> [Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l), who had learned the craft of the Dwarven smiths<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>, fashioned two swords, [Anglachel](/wiki/Anglachel) and [Anguirel](/wiki/Anguirel), from the iron of a meteorite. Anglachel was given to King Thingol as payment for Eöl's being allowed to dwell in [Nan Elmoth](/wiki/Nan_Elmoth). Thingol's own sword was [Aranrúth](/wiki/Aranr%C3%BAth).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Eöl retained ownership of Anguirel until it was stolen from him by his son [Maeglin](/wiki/Maeglin) and taken to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Gondolin was also home for a time to the mate swords Glamdring and [Orcrist](/wiki/Orcrist)<sup id="cite_ref-South_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-11">[11]</a></sup>, the former being the weapon of King [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) himself.<sup id="cite_ref-shortrest_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shortrest-12">[12]</a></sup> Turgon would also leave a sword behind in [Vinyamar](/wiki/Vinyamar) at the bidding of [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) as one of the tokens that would identify a messenger sent by the Lord of the Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

### Dwarves {#Dwarves}

Due to their war-like ways, the Dwarves forged and wielded swords from early in their history.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindar_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindar-7">[7]</a></sup> The sword [Narsil](/wiki/Narsil) was forged by the Dwarf [Telchar](/wiki/Telchar)<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> [Thorin Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin_Oakenshield) also took to wielding the elven sword [Orcrist](/wiki/Orcrist) after coming into possession of it.<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Men {#Men}

[Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) would take up the sword left in Vinyamar by Turgon after being chosen by Ulmo as his messenger.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

During the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), Aranrúth was possessed by the [Kings of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor"), who descended from king Thingol through [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing) the mother of [Elros](/wiki/Elros). It did not survive the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

[Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) carried a sword of lesser lineage but similar design to [Andúril](/wiki/And%C3%BAril).<sup id="cite_ref-South_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-11">[11]</a></sup>

Instead of curved scimitars that were usually used by Orcs, the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) loyal to [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) had short, broad-bladed swords.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

### Trolls {#Trolls}

Gandalf discerned that Glamdring and Orcrist had not been made by trolls.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>
