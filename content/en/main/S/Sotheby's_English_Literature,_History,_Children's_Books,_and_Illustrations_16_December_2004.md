<i><b>Sotheby&#39;s English Literature, History, Children&#39;s Books, and Illustrations 16 December 2004</b></i> is an auction catalogue from [Sotheby's](/wiki/Sotheby%27s) (London).

- The description for Lot 394 (p. 274) includes quotes from an unpublished letter from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to schoolchildren (cf. [Letter to a Primary School in Acocks Green](/wiki/Letter_to_a_Primary_School_in_Acocks_Green)).
- The description for Lot 395 (p. 274) quotes a brief inscription by Tolkien to [George and Moira Sayer](/wiki/George_Sayer "George Sayer").
