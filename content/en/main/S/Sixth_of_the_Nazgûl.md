The **Sixth of the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md)** was never differentiated in Tolkien's works, but several adaptations did attribute individual traits to this specific Nazgûl.

[Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) constructed an elaborate non-[canonical](../C/Canon.md) backstory for [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md), identifying the character as *Hoarmûrath*, a backstory which was also used in the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ICE_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ICE-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Decipher](../D/Decipher.md), for their [Trading Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md), instead limited this individualization to naming the character *[Úlairë](../N/Nazgûl.md#Other_names) Enquëa* ([Q.](../Q/Quenya.md) Sixth Nazgûl), and assigning the epithet of *Black Threat*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

While some other adaptations also individualized the Nine, none are known to have assigned specific numbers.

## Backstory in Middle-earth Role Playing {#Backstory_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing}

**Hoarmûrath** was born in [S.A.](Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1954&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1954 (page does not exist)">1954</a> in the [Forest of Dír](../U/Urd.md), among the Urdar clans of hunters and trappers. His mother was their matriarch until her death in war, after which his sister became queen. As the queen’s brother, Hoarmûrath held a high position within his society.

His close relationship with the [Avari](../A/Avari.md) of the south, who taught him magic and power, put him at odds with Urd traditions. When he proposed to wage war for territory, his sister instead ordered him exiled. A struggle ensued, and Hoarmûrath's retainers slew his sister. To avoid punishment, he declared himself the first King of Urd. With the aid of the Avari, he crushed all opposition and became the Lord of the Urdar in [S.A.](Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1992&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1992 (page does not exist)">1992</a>.

Hoarmûrath expanded his dominion rapidly, , ruling much of the land between the Northern Seas, but his alliance with the Avari soured when he coveted their immortality and wealth. In [S.A.](Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1999&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1999 (page does not exist)">1999</a>, he turned on them, but the Avari were victorious in two great battles. In desperation, Hoarmûrath sought help from [Sauron](Sauron.md). The Dark Lord sent [Khamûl](../K/Khamûl.md) with a [Ring of Power](../N/Nine_Rings.md). In [S.A.](Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2000&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2000 (page does not exist)">2000</a>, Hoarmûrath accepted and became the sixth King to fall under Sauron's sway.

Invigorated by his Ring, Hoarmûrath led a renewed and successful campaign against the Avari. He styled himself as the Lord of the North and ruled as a client-king for Sauron for over two hundred years. In [S.A.](Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2250&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2250 (page does not exist)">2250</a>, having secured his kingdom and his succession, he was summoned to Mordor by Sauron to help prepare to counter the growing might of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). For the next thousand years, he oversaw the construction of defences around Udûn, including the Gates of Mordor, though he still visited his own kingdom.

He fled back to Urd after Númenor's invasion in [S.A.](Second_Age.md) [3261](Second_Age_3261.md) but returned to Mordor after the [Downfall](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md). He led campaigns in Rhovanion and commanded the northern flank in the invasion of Ithilien. He passed into shadows as Sauron was overthrown in the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md).

Hoarmûrath reappeared around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1050](../T/Third_Age_1050.md), and by [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1640](../T/Third_Age_1640.md) had rebuilt his kingdom. He joined the other Nazgûl in Mordor and made his lair in the abandoned castle of [Durthang](../D/Durthang.md). He later participated in the capture of [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Morgul.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2002](../T/Third_Age_2002.md).

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), he rode with the Black Riders in search of the [One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md). He was one of the Ringwraiths who fought in the [Battle of Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), later joining the remaining wraiths at the [Battle of the Morannon](../B/Battle_of_the_Morannon.md), where he perished with Sauron's defeat.<sup id="cite_ref-ICE_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ICE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Weaponry in Middle-earth Role Playing {#Weaponry_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing}

Beyond a [Morgul-knife](../M/Morgul-knife.md), the Ringwraith is noted to have possessed the following weapons:

- **Snow Hammer**: A war mattock made of clear [laen](Stones_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md#Laen), inlaid with drake-horn.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **Hue Changer**: A [dírwooden](../W/Woods_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md#Dírwood) spear which can change colour.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
