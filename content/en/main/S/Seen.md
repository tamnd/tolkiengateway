All things in [Arda](../A/Arda.md) fall into the realm of either the Seen or the [Unseen](../U/Unseen.md).

The Seen are material objects and living, breathing creatures. However strong personalities like [High Elves](../C/Calaquendi.md) had also a different form in the Unseen realm.
