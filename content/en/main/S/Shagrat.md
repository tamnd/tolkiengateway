**Shagrat** was an [Uruk](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai") commanding the garrison of the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

During a patrol to [Shelob's Lair](/wiki/Shelob%27s_Lair), Shagrat's company encountered a troop of Orcs from [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) led by [Gorbag](/wiki/Gorbag). The two did not get along, but before things escalated, they noted something out of place: a [strange small creature](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), all wound up in [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob)'s webs. The discovery of what appeared to be an undersized [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") led the two commanders to compare their notes; they concluded that there must have been another nearby. But they could not find this "great Elf warrior".

Turmoil rose again amongst the group when Gorbag and Shagrat disagreed with what they had to do with the prisoner. Shagrat's orders from [above](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron") were simple: all personal effects were to be sent to [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr). Shagrat did not trust many of his own troops, and none of Gorbag's, so he locked up the prisoner in the top chamber of the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Gorbag coveted the [Mithril-coat](/wiki/Mithril-coat) Frodo wore and attempted to usurp Shagrat's rule, but lost to Shagrat's superior numbers. Soon, Gorbag's faction was pressed back. Shagrat eventually killed [Gorbag](/wiki/Gorbag) and ran off to Sauron with the prisoner's belongings.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He reached [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr) three days later, on [17 March](/wiki/17_March).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> After Shagrat brought him the news and items, Sauron slew him.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Shagrat is simply referred to in-game as a &#34;large angry Orc&#34;. He is carrying a long red knife and a black bundle. He can be killed by Sam.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2003" title="2003">2003</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Tait&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Peter Tait (page does not exist)">Peter Tait</a> played Shagrat. In this adaptation, it is Shagrat and not Gorbag who covets the <i><a href="/wiki/Mithril#The_Mithril_Coat" title="Mithril">mithril</a></i> shirt and starts the fight. In the <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)">Extended Edition</a>, he is shown escaping with the <i>mithril</i> shirt, which is then presented later by the <a href="/wiki/Mouth_of_Sauron" title="Mouth of Sauron">Mouth of Sauron</a> to the Captains of the West. It is unclear if this means Shagrat had changed his mind and brought the shirt to <a href="/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr" title="Barad-dûr">Barad-dûr</a>, or if he was waylaid by Orcs loyal to Sauron during his escape, and the shirt was confiscated.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2008" title="2008">2008</a>: <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Lego_The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="Lego The Lord of the Rings">Lego The Lord of the Rings</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In this adaptation, Shagrat first appears on a ledge cackling overlooking <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo Baggins</a> and <a href="/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee" title="Samwise Gamgee">Samwise Gamgee</a> after Gollum&#39;s initial betrayal. During the very next cutscene, he strikes, pinning Sam to the ground to prevent him from warning Frodo about <a href="/wiki/Shelob" title="Shelob">Shelob</a>. Despite this, he is a playable character during the <a href="/wiki/Shelob" title="Shelob">Shelob</a> boss fight solely for the purposes of making a solo moment in the films multiplayer. Similar to the films, it is Shagrat and not Gorbag who covets the <i><a href="/wiki/Mithril#The_Mithril_Coat" title="Mithril">mithril</a></i> shirt and starts the fight in the <a href="/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol" title="Tower of Cirith Ungol">Tower of Cirith Ungol</a>.</dd></dl>
