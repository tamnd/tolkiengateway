**Second Age 700** (abbreviated to **S.A. 700**) is the 700th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Tar-Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md), sixth [King](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) and eldest child and only son of [Tar-Meneldur](../T/Tar-Meneldur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Kings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kings-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Birth of [Írildë](../_/Írildë.md), daughter of [Cemendur](<../C/Cemendur_(son_of_Axantur).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-earlier_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-earlier-2">[2]</a></sup>

## In other versions of the legendarium {#In_other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)* it is stated that "*[Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) therefore went eastwards, about the year 700 of the Second Age, and established the (primarily but by no means solely) [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) realm of Eregion.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-3">[3]</a></sup> This contradicts the information published in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* which gives the founding of [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) in the year 750.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB1-4">[4]</a></sup> It is possible to reconcile the texts if 700 is seen as the year of their moving eastwards with 750 as the year of the foundation of Eregion; the canonicity of this, however, is unknown.
