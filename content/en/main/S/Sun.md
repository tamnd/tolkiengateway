**Anar**, the **Sun**, is the source of daylight, rising in the [East](../E/East.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and sinking in the <a class="mw-disambig" href="../W/West_(disambiguation).md" title="West (disambiguation)">West</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [Years of the Trees](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md), [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) was lit for many thousands of years by the light of the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md), [Telperion](../T/Telperion.md) the Silver and [Laurelin](../L/Laurelin.md) the Gold. When these were destroyed by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) and [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md), [Arda](../A/Arda.md) was plunged into darkness. Through the power of [Varda](../V/Varda.md) and [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md), though, [Laurelin](../L/Laurelin.md) produced a single fiery fruit before it died. This golden fire was set in a vessel made by [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) and his people, and steered into the sky by the [Maia](../M/Maiar.md) [Arien](../A/Arien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-OfSun_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfSun-3">[3]</a></sup>

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_First_Dawn_of_the_Sun.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a9/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_First_Dawn_of_the_Sun.jpg/200px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_First_Dawn_of_the_Sun.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="256" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a9/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_First_Dawn_of_the_Sun.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_First_Dawn_of_the_Sun.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a9/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_First_Dawn_of_the_Sun.jpg/400px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_First_Dawn_of_the_Sun.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1200" data-file-height="1538"/></a><figcaption><i>The First Dawn of the Sun</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

[Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) was in the West of the World, and so the first sunrise was in the west, not the east. Originally, [Arien](../A/Arien.md) was to have steered Anar ceaselessly from west to east and back again, always remaining in the sky, but the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) changed this counsel, so that each evening Anar would descend into the distant western seas, and re-emerge each morning in the east.<sup id="cite_ref-OfSun_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfSun-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Sun was seen by the Elves as a sign for the awakening of [Men](../M/Men.md), and they valued the [Moon](../M/Moon.md) higher.<sup id="cite_ref-OfSun_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfSun-3">[3]</a></sup> [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s creatures, the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), feared the Sun, and with the exception of the [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md), they did not travel while it was in the sky.

The [Trolls](../T/Trolls.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) feared the Sun even more, and with great reason: they turned to stone under its light. Only the later [Olog-hai](../O/Olog-hai.md) were able to move under the Sun.

## Other names {#Other_names}

<dl><dd><i>For the <a href="../D/Dwarves.md" title="Dwarves">Dwarf</a> see <a href="../A/Anar_(dwarf).md" title="Anar (dwarf)">Anar (dwarf)</a></i></dd></dl>

Names of the Sun amongst the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) included:

- <i><b>Anar</b></i> (derived from the [root](Sundocarmë.md) [ANÁR](../A/ANÁR.md)<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>) or **The Fire-golden**,<sup id="cite_ref-OfSun_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfSun-3">[3]</a></sup> a name given to it by the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md).
- <i><b>Anor</b></i> (derived from the [root](Sundocarmë.md) [ANÁR](../A/ANÁR.md)<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>), the common name in [Sindarin](Sindarin.md), as seen in *[Minas Anor](../M/Minas_Anor.md)*, the [Gondorian](../G/Gondor.md) province of [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md), and *[elanor](../E/Elanor.md)* ("Sun-star").
- <i><b>Vása</b></i> ("the Consumer"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>), or **Heart of Fire**, a name given by the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) as it marked the waning of the Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-OfSun_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfSun-3">[3]</a></sup>
- <i><b>Aþâraigas</b></i> ("appointed heat"), its in [Valarin](../V/Valarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
- <i><b>Kalantar</b></i> ("Light-giver").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
- <i><b>Ūri</b></i>, is one of the [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) words for the sun, giving the "later" forms ([Westron](../W/Westron.md)?) **Uir**/**Ŷr**.<sup id="cite_ref-adun_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-adun-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:306</sup> There is also the personified name **Ūrī** ("[Lady of the Sun](../L/Lady_of_the_Sun.md)").<sup id="cite_ref-adun_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-adun-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:426</sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

A poetic name for the Sun was *The Daystar*, and [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) referred to it as *The Yellow Face*.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Gates_of_Morning.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/4/4a/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Gates_of_Morning.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Gates_of_Morning.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="199" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/4/4a/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Gates_of_Morning.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="450" data-file-height="299"/></a><figcaption><i>The Gates of Morn</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

In the early versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) as described in *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One.md)*, the Sun was described in great detail as an immense island of fire. It was also said there that the youth [Tilion](../T/Tilion.md), who guided the Moon, was said to secretly be in love with Arien, and that because he steered the Moon too close to the Sun the Moon was burned.

In some early versions of *The Silmarillion*, it is prophecised that in [the End](../T/The_End.md) Morgoth will destroy the Sun and Moon.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

In an unwritten revision of the Silmarillion, Morgoth at one point was infatuated with Arien, and wanted to claim her as his wife: he is at one point even described as ravishing her, so she abandoned her body and 'died': the Sun after this for a while left its course, burning a large part of [Arda](../A/Arda.md) the world (apparently creating the deserts of Far [Harad](../H/Harad.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

In the [Round World](../R/Round_World_version_of_the_Silmarillion.md) version of the legendarium, the Sun and the Moon were not the fruit of the Two Trees, but actually preceded the creation of the Trees. Instead, the Trees preserved the light of the Sun before it was tainted by Melkor when he ravished Arien.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>
