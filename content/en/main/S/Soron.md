**Soron** (archaic **þoron**) means "[eagle](../E/Eagles.md)" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The form *soron* may only be the form of this word used in compounds. As an independent word, Tolkien wrote it as **sorno** in a letter from 1972.<sup id="cite_ref-let_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-let-2">[2]</a></sup> Earlier, in the Etymologies of the 1930s, *soron* "eagle" appeared beside an alternate form **sorne**, but its plural form *sorni* ("eagles") indicated that *sorne* was the common form.<sup id="cite_ref-etym_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

This word derives from the [primitive](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) **þoronō**<sup id="cite_ref-let_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-let-2">[2]</a></sup> from the [root](Sundocarme.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=THORON&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="THORON (page does not exist)">THORON</a> "eagle", an extension of the root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=THOR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="THOR (page does not exist)">THOR</a> "come swooping down".<sup id="cite_ref-etym_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-3">[3]</a></sup> In the development of Quenya words from [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md), a short vowel that follows another vowel of the same quality was lost,<sup id="cite_ref-let_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-let-2">[2]</a></sup> so that primitive *þoronō* developed into *þorno* and then *sorno*.
