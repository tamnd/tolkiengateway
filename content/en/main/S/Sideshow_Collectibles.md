**Sideshow Collectibles** is a company that specializes in high end collectibles such as polystone statues, Premium Format figures, 12 inch figures and has many areas of speciality items. Sideshow has a very large collection of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* products, however most of the line that was created from [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") films are now unavailable.

Sideshow teamed up with [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) and [Weta Workshop](/wiki/Weta_Workshop) to create an array of almost 200 unique statues, busts, medallions, and environments that were based on [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings).
