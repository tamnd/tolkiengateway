**Sideshow Collectibles** is a company that specializes in high end collectibles such as polystone statues, Premium Format figures, 12 inch figures and has many areas of speciality items. Sideshow has a very large collection of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* products, however most of the line that was created from [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) films are now unavailable.

Sideshow teamed up with [New Line Cinema](../N/New_Line_Cinema.md) and [Weta Workshop](../W/Weta_Workshop.md) to create an array of almost 200 unique statues, busts, medallions, and environments that were based on [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md).
