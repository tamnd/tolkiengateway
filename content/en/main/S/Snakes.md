**Snakes**, sometimes referred to as [serpents](Serpents.md), were reptilian creatures, often associated with the forces of darkness, and especially with the [Dark Lords](../D/Dark_Lord.md).

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), "snake" is *[ango](../A/Ango.md)* or *[lókë](../L/Lókë.md)*. [Sindarin](Sindarin.md) has *[lhûg](../L/Lhûg.md)*.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md), *lingos* means "snake" and *ling* means "small snake".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
