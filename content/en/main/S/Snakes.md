**Snakes**, sometimes referred to as [serpents](/wiki/Serpents "Serpents"), were reptilian creatures, often associated with the forces of darkness, and especially with the [Dark Lords](/wiki/Dark_Lord "Dark Lord").

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), "snake" is *[ango](/wiki/Ango "Ango")* or *[lókë](/wiki/L%C3%B3k%C3%AB "Lókë")*. [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) has *[lhûg](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBg "Lhûg")*.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), *lingos* means "snake" and *ling* means "small snake".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
