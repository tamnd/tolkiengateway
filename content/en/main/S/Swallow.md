**Swallows** are a type of bird. They are referenced frequently within the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

- In *[The Tale of Tinúviel](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Tin%C3%BAviel)*, it is noted that [Timpinen](/wiki/Timpinen) would depart the area around the [Cottage of Lost Play](/wiki/Cottage_of_Lost_Play) in a grey magic boat before autumn, and the swallows drew him far away.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:7</sup>
- In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), the word for swallow is *"<a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Duilin" title="Duilin">duilin</a>"*, with the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognate being *"[tuilindo](/wiki/Tuilindo "Tuilindo")"* which translates to "spring-singer"
