**Second Age 771** (abbreviated to **S.A. 771**) is the 771st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Erendis](../E/Erendis.md), wife of [Tar-Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Aldarion_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aldarion-1">[1]</a></sup>
