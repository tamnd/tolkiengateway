**Second Age 32** (abbreviated to **S.A. 32**) is the 32nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- All the remaining [Edain](../E/Edain.md) (including a few [Drúedain](../D/Drúedain.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) finally settle on the isle of [Elenna](../E/Elenna.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> where the kingdom of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) is established.<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Elros](../E/Elros.md), son of [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md), becomes the first [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) and thus receives his royal name **Tar-Minyatur**.<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
