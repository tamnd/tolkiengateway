The **Stair Falls** were the falls beneath the [West-gate of Moria](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin "Doors of Durin"), where the Gate Stream of the [Sirannon](/wiki/Sirannon) made a waterfall some thirty feet in height before flowing out across the grasslands of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion). The falls took their name from the stair carved beside them in the rock of the cliff.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
