**Sagan om Ringen** is a Swedish TV movie adaptation of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*. The film follows [Frodo's](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") journey from [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") to the Fellowship's departure from [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Notably, the film was produced due to the success of [Bo Hansson](/wiki/Bo_Hansson)'s [Sagan om Ringen](</wiki/Sagan_om_Ringen_(album)> "Sagan om Ringen (album)") or Music Inspired by Lord of the Rings. Hansson created the music for the film.

Scenes with actors were filmed in front of a blue screen and the actors were composited into hand-painted backgrounds, created by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Linblom&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Peter Linblom (page does not exist)">Peter Linblom</a>, for the final product.

## Differences from the source material {#Differences_from_the_source_material}

### Part 1 {#Part_1}

- [Bilbo's](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") [party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") is a dinner attended by only the central [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf). Bilbo does not give a speech. Rather he disappears in the middle of dinner without drawing attention to himself.
- [The One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring) is closer in size to a large bracelet.
- [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) is given the title *alvkungen* or *The [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") King.* The book gives him the title *Lord of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).*
- Gandalf does not leave the Shire following the Birthday Party, but sets the Hobbits off on their journey.
- [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") immediately sets off with [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") and [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck"). There is no [Conspiracy](/wiki/Conspirators "Conspirators") or plan to make others believe Frodo is moving [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow).
- [Fatty Bolger](/wiki/Fatty_Bolger) and [Folco Boffin](/wiki/Folco_Boffin) are omitted.
- The Hobbits meet a company of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who drive off the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl), but no one Elf is identified, meaning [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor "Gildor") is omitted.
- The [Bamfurlong](/wiki/Bamfurlong) is omitted.
- The Hobbits are rescued by [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) and visit [his house](</wiki/Underhill_(house)> "Underhill (house)"). [Goldberry](/wiki/Goldberry) is omitted.
- The [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs) are omitted.
- The Hobbits enter *[The Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony)* and immediately meet [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"). They find Gandalf's letter (though [Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur "Barliman Butterbur") is omitted) and set off with Strider. All other events in [Bree](/wiki/Bree) are omitted.

### Part 2 {#Part_2}

- The end of the Flight to [the Ford](/wiki/Ford_of_Bruinen "Ford of Bruinen") is truncated. [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) do not interact and the Riders immediately attempt to cross the Ford, before being washed away.
- Most of the events in Rivendell besides the party, [the Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond "Council of Elrond") and the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) setting out, are omitted.
- The only individuals seen in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) are the attendees of the Council of Elrond. [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in) is not physically seen but is still present at the council and the narrator relates what he said. [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) is not present, despite appearing in the previous episode. [Erestor](/wiki/Erestor) and [Galdor of the Havens](</wiki/Galdor_(elf_of_the_Havens)> "Galdor (elf of the Havens)") are not present.
- [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") attend the Council of Elrond, with no mention made that they were not invited.
- [Radagast the Brown](/wiki/Radagast "Radagast") is not seen or directly mentioned, though Gandalf says he was "tricked" into meeting with [Saruman the White](/wiki/Saruman "Saruman"), implying the encounter with Radagast took place off-screen.
- The circumstances of the aforementioned off-screen encounter with Radagast must be slightly different, however, as Gandalf gets the attention of [Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir) by sending "a message with the birds", rather than Gwaihir coming to [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc) because of Radagast's message.
- [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) is omitted. Despite the narrator mentioning that the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) has nine members, only eight are listed.
- Despite [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny)'s omission in Episode 1, it is said that "Sam stood by the Pony", presumably [Bill the Pony](/wiki/Bill_the_Pony).
