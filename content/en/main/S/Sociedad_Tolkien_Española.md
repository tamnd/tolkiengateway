The **Sociedad Tolkien Española** (Spanish: "Spanish Tolkien Society"), or **STE**, is a [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md) based in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain" title="wikipedia:Spain">Spain</a>.

## History {#History}

The Society was founded in [1991](../_/1991.md) in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elche" title="wikipedia:Elche">Elche</a>, in view of the coming [Centenary](../_/1992.md), having the first official meeting on [9 February](../_/9_February.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its first chairman was [José Manuel Ferrández Bru](../J/José_Manuel_Ferrández_Bru.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Although the Executive Committee and its seat is placed in a particular locality, there are many local delegations called "smials".

Ever since [2015](../_/2015.md), an official [podcast](../P/Podcast.md) releases monthly, called "<a class="external text" href="https://www.sociedadtolkien.org/podcast/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Regreso a Hobbiton</a>".

## Publications {#Publications}

The Society publishes the journal <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.sociedadtolkien.org/revista-estel/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Estel</a></i> twice a year. As of 2023, 99 issues have been published.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The journal <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.sociedadtolkien.org/blog/category/publicaciones/revista-nolme/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Nolmë</a></i>, more academic, is published irregularly.

## Awards {#Awards}

Four awards are given out every year:

- "<a class="external text" href="https://www.sociedadtolkien.org/premio-de-arte-niggle/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Niggle</a>", artisanship award, with the categories "Pictoral arts and illustration" and "Other arts".
- "<a class="external text" href="https://www.sociedadtolkien.org/premio-ensayo-aelfwine/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ælfwine</a>", essay award (open to English submissions), with the categories "Academic essay" and "Literary-humanities essay".
- "<a class="external text" href="https://www.sociedadtolkien.org/premio-relato-gandalf/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Gandalf</a>", story award (the oldest, awarded since 1995).
- "<a class="external text" href="https://www.sociedadtolkien.org/premios-bilbo/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Bilbo</a>", micro-story award.
