<i><b>Sotheby&#39;s The Library of Richard Manney 11 October 1991</b></i> is an auction catalogue from [Sotheby's](Sotheby's.md). Lot 298 was a first edition of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* with an inscription in [tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> The inscription is reprinted and discussed by [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md) in [*Vinyar Tengwar* No.21](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_21.md) and by [Nancy Martsch](../N/Nancy_Martsch.md) in issue [No.22](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_22.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>
