<i><b>Sotheby&#39;s The Library of Richard Manney 11 October 1991</b></i> is an auction catalogue from [Sotheby's](/wiki/Sotheby%27s). Lot 298 was a first edition of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* with an inscription in [tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar "Tengwar") by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> The inscription is reprinted and discussed by [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) in [*Vinyar Tengwar* No.21](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_21 "Vinyar Tengwar 21") and by [Nancy Martsch](/wiki/Nancy_Martsch) in issue [No.22](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_22 "Vinyar Tengwar 22").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>
