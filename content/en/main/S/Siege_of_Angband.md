The **Siege of Angband** was the 400-year leaguer of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) around the fortress of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in the last six centuries of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), which began following the [Dagor Aglareb](/wiki/Dagor_Aglareb). The Noldor and their allies possessed insufficient force to assault [Angband](/wiki/Angband) directly.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the west [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) guarded the pass over [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin) into [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) from [Barad Eithel](/wiki/Barad_Eithel), while [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) and [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) held the pass of Sirion from [Minas Tirith](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand)> "Minas Tirith (Beleriand)") and [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod) with [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor) held the northern slopes of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) to the borders of the [Aglon](/wiki/Pass_of_Aglon "Pass of Aglon"). In the east the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) held watch; [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) held the land between the rivers [Aros](/wiki/Aros) and [Celon](/wiki/Celon), [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) had his stronghold on [Himring](/wiki/Himring) while he guarded the Marches north of that hill and the plains between the rivers Celon and [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion), [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor) kept watch over the northern hills of the river Gelion, and [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir) in the shadows of [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was during the Siege that [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon)'s people left [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast) to build [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Other Elven cities, [Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar) and [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest) were also established by the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) on [Falas](/wiki/Falas), and [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), the realm of [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod). The siege was incomplete, as Morgoth was still capable of sending out various forces of orcs to harass the elves, such as the attack of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum); but the Noldor were grown strong enough, that these task forces posed no significant threat.

About 200 years into the Siege, [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) came forth from Angband, and although he was yet young and not fully grown, many fled in terror. However, Fingon led a group of archers on horseback against him, and Glaurung was forced to return to Angband. After the defeat of Glaurung, a period called [Long Peace](/wiki/Long_Peace) began, and [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) prospered greatly during this time.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Men](/wiki/Men) of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) entered Beleriand and were followed by the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin).

Years after the [Battle of the Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pass_of_Aglon), the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Forest_of_Brethil) was given to the Haladin. A final band of Men, the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador), came to Beleriand; they were given the lands of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min).

After about four hundred years, the Siege was broken in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
