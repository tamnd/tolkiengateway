The **Siege of Angband** was the 400-year leaguer of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) around the fortress of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) in the last six centuries of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), which began following the [Dagor Aglareb](../D/Dagor_Aglareb.md). The Noldor and their allies possessed insufficient force to assault [Angband](../A/Angband.md) directly.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the west [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) and [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) guarded the pass over [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) into [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) from [Barad Eithel](../B/Barad_Eithel.md), while [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) and [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) held the pass of Sirion from [Minas Tirith](<../M/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand).md>) and [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md) with [Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md) held the northern slopes of [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) to the borders of the [Aglon](../P/Pass_of_Aglon.md). In the east the [Sons of Fëanor](Sons_of_Fëanor.md) held watch; [Celegorm](../C/Celegorm.md) and [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md) held the land between the rivers [Aros](../A/Aros.md) and [Celon](../C/Celon.md), [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) had his stronghold on [Himring](../H/Himring.md) while he guarded the Marches north of that hill and the plains between the rivers Celon and [Gelion](../G/Gelion.md), [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md) kept watch over the northern hills of the river Gelion, and [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md) in the shadows of [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was during the Siege that [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md)'s people left [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) to build [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Other Elven cities, [Brithombar](../B/Brithombar.md) and [Eglarest](../E/Eglarest.md) were also established by the [Sindar](Sindar.md) on [Falas](../F/Falas.md), and [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md), the realm of [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md). The siege was incomplete, as Morgoth was still capable of sending out various forces of orcs to harass the elves, such as the attack of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md); but the Noldor were grown strong enough, that these task forces posed no significant threat.

About 200 years into the Siege, [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) came forth from Angband, and although he was yet young and not fully grown, many fled in terror. However, Fingon led a group of archers on horseback against him, and Glaurung was forced to return to Angband. After the defeat of Glaurung, a period called [Long Peace](../L/Long_Peace.md) began, and [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) prospered greatly during this time.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Men](../M/Men.md) of the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md) entered Beleriand and were followed by the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md).

Years after the [Battle of the Pass of Aglon](../B/Battle_of_the_Pass_of_Aglon.md), the [Forest of Brethil](../F/Forest_of_Brethil.md) was given to the Haladin. A final band of Men, the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md), came to Beleriand; they were given the lands of [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md).

After about four hundred years, the Siege was broken in the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
