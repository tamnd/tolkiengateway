"**Silver flow the streams from Celos to Erui**" is a poem sung by [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) in the chapter "[The Last Debate](/wiki/The_Last_Debate)".

## Text {#Text}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Silver flow the streams from <a href="/wiki/Celos" title="Celos">Celos</a> to <a href="/wiki/Erui" title="Erui">Erui</a><br/>
In the green fields of <a href="/wiki/Lebennin" title="Lebennin">Lebennin</a>!<br/>
Tall grows the grass there. In the wind from the <a href="/wiki/Belegaer" title="Belegaer">Sea</a><br/>
The white lilies sway,<br/>
And the golden bells are shaken of <a href="/wiki/Mallos" title="Mallos">mallos</a> and <a href="/wiki/Alfirin" title="Alfirin">alfirin</a><br/>
In the green fields of Lebennin,<br/>
In the wind from the Sea!
</p>
</div>
