The **Second Age**, often abbreviated to **S.A.**, began after the destruction of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), and the execution and subsequent expulsion of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) from [Eä](../E/Eä.md) into the [Void](../V/Void.md).

The founding of [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) (from the remnants of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md)) and the creation of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), the two major [Elvish](../E/Elves.md) and [Mannish](../M/Men.md) kingdoms of the Age, respectively, took place soon afterwards.

## Elvish realms {#Elvish_realms}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/S/Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_&amp;_Numenor.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/2d/Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_%26_Numenor.jpg/300px-Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_%26_Numenor.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="240" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/2d/Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_%26_Numenor.jpg/450px-Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_%26_Numenor.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/2d/Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_%26_Numenor.jpg/600px-Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_%26_Numenor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2000" data-file-height="1600"/></a><figcaption><i>Middle-earth &amp; Númenor</i> by <a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Smeagol" title="User:Smeagol">Steven White Jr</a></figcaption></figure>

[Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) was an Elvish realm ruled by [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), last [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) in Middle-earth. It lay in what remained of Beleriand, between the mountains of [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md) and the [Great Sea](../B/Belegaer.md). From its [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) Elven ships sailed daily to [the West](../A/Aman.md). Imladris, or [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), was founded by [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) about halfway through the Age.

In the early part of the Second Age many [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) Elves journeyed East of the Ered Luin and established the realm of [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) near the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md). These Elves, led by [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md) son of [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md), became close friends with the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md). Together they made great endeavours in smithcraft, until the skill of the Elves reached a height never to be matched again. This attracted the attention of [Sauron](Sauron.md), who tricked the Elves into making the [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md) by his aid. When the Elves perceived that he desired to rule them with [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md), they resisted, and Sauron waged war against them. In this war, Eregion was destroyed and Celebrimbor killed.

## Mannish realms {#Mannish_realms}

The Second Age was the age of the glory of Númenor, the great island kingdom that was created for the descendants of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) in the first years of the Age, and as the years passed, their power grew until it surpassed that of any nation of [Men](../M/Men.md), before or since. They sailed east, exploring Middle-earth and founding great cities there. Eventually, their power and pride became so great that they challenged the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) themselves, and were destroyed.

Meanwhile, in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) there were [Dark Years](../D/Dark_Years.md), in which Men lived in simple tribal societies or were under the domination of Sauron. The men who served Sauron revered him as a god and feared him greatly. In the beginning of the Second Age, the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) attempted to help the Men of Middle-earth, but eventually desired to dominate them.

The Downfall of Númenor was not the end of the Second Age; a remnant of the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) led by [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) escaped its wreck and founded kingdoms in Middle-earth: [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) in the north and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) in the south. Along with [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), they formed a mighty alliance, the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](../L/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men.md), and marched on [Sauron](Sauron.md), who had grown great again, assailing the Dark Tower of [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md). With the first overthrow of Sauron by the Alliance, the Second Age came to its end.
