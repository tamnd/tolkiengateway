The **Second Age**, often abbreviated to **S.A.**, began after the destruction of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) in the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), and the execution and subsequent expulsion of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) from [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4) into the [Void](/wiki/Void).

The founding of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) (from the remnants of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand)) and the creation of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), the two major [Elvish](/wiki/Elves "Elves") and [Mannish](/wiki/Men "Men") kingdoms of the Age, respectively, took place soon afterwards.

## Elvish realms {#Elvish_realms}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_%26_Numenor.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/2/2d/Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_%26_Numenor.jpg/300px-Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_%26_Numenor.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="240" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/2/2d/Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_%26_Numenor.jpg/450px-Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_%26_Numenor.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/2/2d/Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_%26_Numenor.jpg/600px-Steven_White_Jr._-_Middle-earth_%26_Numenor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2000" data-file-height="1600"/></a><figcaption><i>Middle-earth &amp; Númenor</i> by <a href="/wiki/User:Smeagol" title="User:Smeagol">Steven White Jr</a></figcaption></figure>

[Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) was an Elvish realm ruled by [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), last [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor) in Middle-earth. It lay in what remained of Beleriand, between the mountains of [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) and the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer"). From its [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) Elven ships sailed daily to [the West](/wiki/Aman "Aman"). Imladris, or [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), was founded by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) about halfway through the Age.

In the early part of the Second Age many [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) Elves journeyed East of the Ered Luin and established the realm of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) near the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). These Elves, led by [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor) son of [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin), became close friends with the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"). Together they made great endeavours in smithcraft, until the skill of the Elves reached a height never to be matched again. This attracted the attention of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), who tricked the Elves into making the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power) by his aid. When the Elves perceived that he desired to rule them with [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), they resisted, and Sauron waged war against them. In this war, Eregion was destroyed and Celebrimbor killed.

## Mannish realms {#Mannish_realms}

The Second Age was the age of the glory of Númenor, the great island kingdom that was created for the descendants of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) in the first years of the Age, and as the years passed, their power grew until it surpassed that of any nation of [Men](/wiki/Men), before or since. They sailed east, exploring Middle-earth and founding great cities there. Eventually, their power and pride became so great that they challenged the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) themselves, and were destroyed.

Meanwhile, in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) there were [Dark Years](/wiki/Dark_Years), in which Men lived in simple tribal societies or were under the domination of Sauron. The men who served Sauron revered him as a god and feared him greatly. In the beginning of the Second Age, the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) attempted to help the Men of Middle-earth, but eventually desired to dominate them.

The Downfall of Númenor was not the end of the Second Age; a remnant of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) led by [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) escaped its wreck and founded kingdoms in Middle-earth: [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) in the north and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in the south. Along with [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), they formed a mighty alliance, the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men), and marched on [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), who had grown great again, assailing the Dark Tower of [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr). With the first overthrow of Sauron by the Alliance, the Second Age came to its end.
