**Swans** were waterfowl of either black or white colour,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> though usually the latter. It said that swans were especially abundant in the *Nîn-in-Eilph* ([Swanfleet](Swanfleet.md)), a wetland region in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Anke_Eißmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/3d/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg/300px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="228" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/3d/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg/450px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/3d/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg/600px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg 2x" data-file-width="850" data-file-height="645"/></a><figcaption><i>Following the Swans</i> by <a href="../A/Anke_Eißmann.md" title="Anke Eißmann">Anke Eißmann</a></figcaption></figure>

The swan was an important bird in the tales of the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md). The [Maia](../M/Maiar.md) [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md) sent "*many strong-winged swans*" to draw the ships of the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) over [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md) to [Aman](../A/Aman.md) without help of the winds. The Teleri (who were later called by the epithet the *Swanherds*)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> thus especially came to revere swans, and named their city [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md) (the Swanhaven) and built their [ships in swan-form](Swan-ships.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Ossë's master [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md), the [Vala](../V/Vala.md), sent seven great swans to guide [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) to [Vinyamar](../V/Vinyamar.md), where he found a shield which had "an emblem of a white swan's wing". Tuor already loved these birds, having been fostered at [Mithrim](../M/Mithrim.md), where swans seem to have been plentiful (and Tuor's foster-father, [Annael](../A/Annael.md) and [his folk](../E/Elves_of_Mithrim.md), had the swan as a symbol). Departing from Vinyamar, Ulmo's seven swans approached Tuor and offered him one feather each, which Tuor set in the crest of his helm.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

The Teleri were not alone in using the swan-form in ship-building: the prow of [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md)'s ship [Vingilótë](../V/Vingilótë.md) "*was fashioned like a swan*",<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and the Elves of [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) made a [Swan-ship](Swan-ships.md), so well crafted that it resembled a real, giant swan.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_Lórien.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7b/Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="210" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7b/Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/7b/Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1600" data-file-height="1119"/></a><figcaption><i>Farewell to Lórien</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

In the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the symbol of the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Swan-knights&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Swan-knights (page does not exist)">swan-knights</a> of [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md) was a banner, silver upon blue, bearing a "token of the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ship_and_the_Silver_Swan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ship and the Silver Swan (page does not exist)">Ship and the Silver Swan</a>", and their mariners used swan-prowed ships.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Sindarin](Sindarin.md), the name for "swan" was *[alph](../A/Alph.md)* (pl. *eilph*), as in [Nîn-in-Eilph](../N/Nîn-in-Eilph.md). The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognate" title="wikipedia:Cognate">cognate</a> was *[alqua](../A/Alqua.md)*, as in [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md), and the [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) was <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Alpa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alpa (page does not exist)">alpa</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

In Tolkien's early tongues [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md) and [Danian](../D/Danian.md), the name for "swan" was *alch* and *ealc*, respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> Other early conceptions include [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) *alfa* or *alfuil* and [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) *alqe*.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

A fragment of an [unfinished story](<../T/Tal-Elmar_(chapter).md>) mentioned a possibly fictitious race known as the [Swans of Gorbelgod](Swans_of_Gorbelgod.md).<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

In an early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), more details about the use of swans in symbolism and crafting are given:

- Reminiscent of the Elven [Swan-ships](Swan-ships.md), [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) seems to have been the first to make "*great vessels like to the bodies of swans*".<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-19">[19]</a></sup>
- It is also said [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) used a "*boat with a prow fashioned like to the neck of a swan*" on [Lake Mithrim](../L/Lake_Mithrim.md), and that Ulmo apparently sent three swans to guide him out of Mithrim (much reminding of the later device of the "seven swans"). Later, the wing of a white swan became the symbol for Tuor and his company ([House of the Wing](../H/House_of_the_Wing.md)) in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In a poem about [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md), it is said that Tom "*wore in his tall hat a swan-wing feather*".<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) explained in a letter to [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md) that the swan, (traditionally the <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swan#Role_in_culture" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">property of the King</a>), indicated a "*special friendship with Tom*", the swans being among the creatures "*who looked for the return of their rightful Lord, the true King*".<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup> Perhaps this perceived nobility of the swans explains why Tolkien used the bird as a symbol for the princely line of Dol Amroth, and why the swans were described as allies of the [Ainur](../A/Ainur.md) and [Elves](../E/Elves.md).

A glimpse of Tolkien's childhood experience with swans has also been described:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>...they [Ronald and Hilary] would scamper away from the yard, and run round to a place behind the mill [at <a class="mw-redirect" href="Sarehole.md" title="Sarehole">Sarehole</a>] where there was a silent pool with swans swimming on it.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md" title="Humphrey Carpenter">Humphrey Carpenter</a>, <i><a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_A_Biography.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography">J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography</a></i></cite></p>
</blockquote>
