**Swans** were waterfowl of either black or white colour,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> though usually the latter. It said that swans were especially abundant in the *Nîn-in-Eilph* ([Swanfleet](/wiki/Swanfleet)), a wetland region in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/3/3d/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg/300px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="228" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/3/3d/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg/450px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/3/3d/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg/600px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Following_The_Swans.jpeg 2x" data-file-width="850" data-file-height="645"/></a><figcaption><i>Following the Swans</i> by <a href="/wiki/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann" title="Anke Eißmann">Anke Eißmann</a></figcaption></figure>

The swan was an important bird in the tales of the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days). The [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar") [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB) sent "*many strong-winged swans*" to draw the ships of the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) over [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) to [Aman](/wiki/Aman) without help of the winds. The Teleri (who were later called by the epithet the *Swanherds*)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> thus especially came to revere swans, and named their city [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB) (the Swanhaven) and built their [ships in swan-form](/wiki/Swan-ships "Swan-ships").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Ossë's master [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo), the [Vala](/wiki/Vala), sent seven great swans to guide [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) to [Vinyamar](/wiki/Vinyamar), where he found a shield which had "an emblem of a white swan's wing". Tuor already loved these birds, having been fostered at [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim), where swans seem to have been plentiful (and Tuor's foster-father, [Annael](/wiki/Annael) and [his folk](/wiki/Elves_of_Mithrim "Elves of Mithrim"), had the swan as a symbol). Departing from Vinyamar, Ulmo's seven swans approached Tuor and offered him one feather each, which Tuor set in the crest of his helm.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

The Teleri were not alone in using the swan-form in ship-building: the prow of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil)'s ship [Vingilótë](/wiki/Vingil%C3%B3t%C3%AB) "*was fashioned like a swan*",<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and the Elves of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) made a [Swan-ship](/wiki/Swan-ships "Swan-ships"), so well crafted that it resembled a real, giant swan.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/7b/Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="210" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/7/7b/Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/7/7b/Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Farewell_to_L%C3%B3rien.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1600" data-file-height="1119"/></a><figcaption><i>Farewell to Lórien</i> by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the symbol of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Swan-knights&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Swan-knights (page does not exist)">swan-knights</a> of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth) was a banner, silver upon blue, bearing a "token of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ship_and_the_Silver_Swan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ship and the Silver Swan (page does not exist)">Ship and the Silver Swan</a>", and their mariners used swan-prowed ships.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), the name for "swan" was *[alph](/wiki/Alph "Alph")* (pl. *eilph*), as in [Nîn-in-Eilph](/wiki/N%C3%AEn-in-Eilph). The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognate" title="wikipedia:Cognate">cognate</a> was *[alqua](/wiki/Alqua "Alqua")*, as in [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB), and the [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) was <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Alpa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alpa (page does not exist)">alpa</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

In Tolkien's early tongues [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin) and [Danian](/wiki/Danian), the name for "swan" was *alch* and *ealc*, respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> Other early conceptions include [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) *alfa* or *alfuil* and [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) *alqe*.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

A fragment of an [unfinished story](</wiki/Tal-Elmar_(chapter)> "Tal-Elmar (chapter)") mentioned a possibly fictitious race known as the [Swans of Gorbelgod](/wiki/Swans_of_Gorbelgod).<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

In an early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), more details about the use of swans in symbolism and crafting are given:

- Reminiscent of the Elven [Swan-ships](/wiki/Swan-ships), [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) seems to have been the first to make "*great vessels like to the bodies of swans*".<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-19">[19]</a></sup>
- It is also said [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) used a "*boat with a prow fashioned like to the neck of a swan*" on [Lake Mithrim](/wiki/Lake_Mithrim), and that Ulmo apparently sent three swans to guide him out of Mithrim (much reminding of the later device of the "seven swans"). Later, the wing of a white swan became the symbol for Tuor and his company ([House of the Wing](/wiki/House_of_the_Wing)) in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In a poem about [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil), it is said that Tom "*wore in his tall hat a swan-wing feather*".<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) explained in a letter to [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes) that the swan, (traditionally the <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swan#Role_in_culture" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">property of the King</a>), indicated a "*special friendship with Tom*", the swans being among the creatures "*who looked for the return of their rightful Lord, the true King*".<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup> Perhaps this perceived nobility of the swans explains why Tolkien used the bird as a symbol for the princely line of Dol Amroth, and why the swans were described as allies of the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) and [Elves](/wiki/Elves).

A glimpse of Tolkien's childhood experience with swans has also been described:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>...they [Ronald and Hilary] would scamper away from the yard, and run round to a place behind the mill [at <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Sarehole" title="Sarehole">Sarehole</a>] where there was a silent pool with swans swimming on it.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter" title="Humphrey Carpenter">Humphrey Carpenter</a>, <i><a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography" title="J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography">J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography</a></i></cite></p>
</blockquote>
