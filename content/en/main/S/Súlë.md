<i><b>súlë</b></i> (older <i><b>thúlë</b></i> or <i><b>þúlë</b></i>) is a noun meaning "spirit" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Thule.gif"><img src="/w/images/0/02/Thule.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Súlë* is also the name in Quenya of the ninth letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the first letter of the third grade or [Tyellë](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB), which contains "voiceless spirants" and it is the third letter in the dental or *t*-series of consonants, the *[Tincotéma](/wiki/Tincot%C3%A9ma)*. In all modes except for later Quenya, *Súlë* represents **TH**.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Thó</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<br>

## History {#History}

The original name of *Súlë* was *þúlë* and in Quenya it had the value **TH** or **þ**. The alteration of its value occurred among the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) while in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). The change angered [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) for he felt it was an insult to change the pronunciation of the [mother-name](/wiki/Mother-name "Mother-name") of his mother [Míriel](/wiki/M%C3%ADriel), which was *[þerindë](/wiki/%C3%9Eerind%C3%AB "Þerindë")*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup> However, the mutation of **þ** to **S** continued and eventually this letter was renamed *Súlë* and in Quenya only has the value **S** (and is only used instead of letters 29 or 30 when the original sound in a word was "þ").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=TH%C5%AA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="THŪ (page does not exist)">THŪ</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
