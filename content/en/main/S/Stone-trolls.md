**Stone-trolls** were a race of [trolls](/wiki/Trolls "Trolls") in the service of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). Not much is known of these creatures, except that they inhabited the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and that they spoke a "debased form of the [Common Speech](/wiki/Westron "Westron")".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is possible that they were not actually living beings, but mere counterfeits made by [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor), and they would return to their stone images once exposed to the light of the Sun.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[William, Tom, and Bert](/wiki/William,_Tom,_and_Bert) — the trolls encountered by [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and his companions on their journey to [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") — were likely of this kind, as they spoke [Westron](/wiki/Westron).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RF_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RF-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), [Sam Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") composed the humorous poem "[The Stone Troll](/wiki/The_Stone_Troll)", about <a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Tom_(disambiguation)" title="Tom (disambiguation)">Tom</a> visiting an old troll gnawing on the bones of [Tim](/wiki/Tim).<sup id="cite_ref-RF_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RF-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The three trolls attempt to trap and carry the Dwarves in bags. This is reminiscent to the scene in *[Beowulf](</wiki/Beowulf_(poem)> "Beowulf (poem)")* where Grendel carries his human prey in a glove.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> <span></span>
