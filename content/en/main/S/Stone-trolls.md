**Stone-trolls** were a race of [trolls](../T/Trolls.md) in the service of [Sauron](Sauron.md). Not much is known of these creatures, except that they inhabited the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) and that they spoke a "debased form of the [Common Speech](../W/Westron.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is possible that they were not actually living beings, but mere counterfeits made by [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md), and they would return to their stone images once exposed to the light of the Sun.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[William, Tom, and Bert](../W/William,_Tom,_and_Bert.md) — the trolls encountered by [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and his companions on their journey to [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) — were likely of this kind, as they spoke [Westron](../W/Westron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RF_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RF-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), [Sam Gamgee](Samwise_Gamgee.md) composed the humorous poem "[The Stone Troll](../T/The_Stone_Troll.md)", about <a class="mw-disambig" href="../T/Tom_(disambiguation).md" title="Tom (disambiguation)">Tom</a> visiting an old troll gnawing on the bones of [Tim](../T/Tim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-RF_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RF-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The three trolls attempt to trap and carry the Dwarves in bags. This is reminiscent to the scene in *[Beowulf](<../B/Beowulf_(poem).md>)* where Grendel carries his human prey in a glove.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> <span></span>
