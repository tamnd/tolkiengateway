The **Simbelmyne Calendar 1998** features 13 black and white illustrations by [Karolina Stopa](/wiki/Karolina_Stopa) and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Arkadiusz_Kubala&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Arkadiusz Kubala (page does not exist)">Arkadiusz Kubala</a>. Gregorian calendar compared with the reckonings of [Imladris](/wiki/Reckoning_of_Rivendell "Reckoning of Rivendell"), [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland), [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). Moon phases are displayed. From the [Polish Tolkien Society](/wiki/Polish_Tolkien_Society).

- Dimensions: 8 3/8 x 11 5/8 inches
- Clear plastic cover; stapled
