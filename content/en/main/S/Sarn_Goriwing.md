In *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* and the *[Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game "Middle-earth Collectible Card Game")*, **Sarn Goriwing** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Abhorrent Spray's Stone”) was a black spire in [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood), built on the waterfall Goriwing, near the source of the [Enchanted River](/wiki/Enchanted_River). By [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1640](/wiki/Third_Age_1640 "Third Age 1640"), it had become the home of Lhachglin the Animist, a servant of [the Necromancer](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron") of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur).

The tower was 90 feet high and made of “glassy igneous rock”. Its entrance hall was adorned by a mosaic of the [Lidless Eye](/wiki/Lidless_Eye), while its throneroom housed a “Living Throne” made of a twisted tree<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> as well the Forest Orb, which was a minor seeing stone that only covered Mirkwood.

During's Lhachglin's rule, Sarn Goriwing was protected by Slyardach, who was one the Dindae ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Silent Shadows”)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 2]</a></sup>, as well as a guard of 30 [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai). The area surrounding it was planted with vines, *Yavin Girith* ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Shuddering Fruits”), whose leaves emitted a fine mist which could make unsuspecting travellers fall asleep.<sup id="cite_ref-maindescription_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-maindescription-3">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[2]</a></sup>
