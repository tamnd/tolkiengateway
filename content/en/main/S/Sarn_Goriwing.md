In *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)* and the *[Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*, **Sarn Goriwing** ([S.](Sindarin.md) “Abhorrent Spray's Stone”) was a black spire in [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md), built on the waterfall Goriwing, near the source of the [Enchanted River](../E/Enchanted_River.md). By [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1640](../T/Third_Age_1640.md), it had become the home of Lhachglin the Animist, a servant of [the Necromancer](Sauron.md) of [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md).

The tower was 90 feet high and made of “glassy igneous rock”. Its entrance hall was adorned by a mosaic of the [Lidless Eye](../L/Lidless_Eye.md), while its throneroom housed a “Living Throne” made of a twisted tree<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> as well the Forest Orb, which was a minor seeing stone that only covered Mirkwood.

During's Lhachglin's rule, Sarn Goriwing was protected by Slyardach, who was one the Dindae ([S.](Sindarin.md) “Silent Shadows”)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 2]</a></sup>, as well as a guard of 30 [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md). The area surrounding it was planted with vines, *Yavin Girith* ([S.](Sindarin.md) “Shuddering Fruits”), whose leaves emitted a fine mist which could make unsuspecting travellers fall asleep.<sup id="cite_ref-maindescription_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-maindescription-3">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[2]</a></sup>
