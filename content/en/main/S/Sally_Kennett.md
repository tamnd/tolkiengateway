**Sally Kennett** was the ninth Chairman of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society). She was appointed on [8 January](/wiki/8_January) [2009](/wiki/2009), before being elected on [26 April](/wiki/26_April) later that year. Serving for over four years, Kennett stood down at The Tolkien Society's AGM on [20 April](/wiki/20_April) [2013](/wiki/2013) and was replaced by [Shaun Gunner](/wiki/Shaun_Gunner). From 1998 to 2009 Kennett served as the Tolkien Society's secretary and contributed to *[Amon Hen](</wiki/Amon_Hen_(journal)> "Amon Hen (journal)")* through the regular columns "Sally's Snippets" and "Behind Glass Doors". During her time as both Chairman and Secretary she also served as the Tolkien Society's Librarian.
