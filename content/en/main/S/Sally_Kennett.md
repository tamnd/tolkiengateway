**Sally Kennett** was the ninth Chairman of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md). She was appointed on [8 January](../_/8_January.md) [2009](../_/2009.md), before being elected on [26 April](../_/26_April.md) later that year. Serving for over four years, Kennett stood down at The Tolkien Society's AGM on [20 April](../_/20_April.md) [2013](../_/2013.md) and was replaced by [Shaun Gunner](Shaun_Gunner.md). From 1998 to 2009 Kennett served as the Tolkien Society's secretary and contributed to *[Amon Hen](<../A/Amon_Hen_(journal).md>)* through the regular columns "Sally's Snippets" and "Behind Glass Doors". During her time as both Chairman and Secretary she also served as the Tolkien Society's Librarian.
