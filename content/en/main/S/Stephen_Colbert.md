**Stephen Tyrone Colbert** is an American political satirist, writer, actor, comedian, and television host of Comedy Central's *The Colbert Report* and CBS's *The Late Show with Stephen Colbert*. He had a cameo in *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)* as one of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Laketown_Spies&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Laketown Spies (page does not exist)">Laketown Spies</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Tolkien scholar [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff) has suggested that Colbert has offered a formerly unrecorded piece of scholarly insight into *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, by the latter's suggestion that "*[Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) knew [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) would fail in his quest to destroy the [Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), because he'd seen with his own eyes that Frodo could not throw the ring into his own fire at [Bag-End](/wiki/Bag-End)*".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
