**Stephen Tyrone Colbert** is an American political satirist, writer, actor, comedian, and television host of Comedy Central's *The Colbert Report* and CBS's *The Late Show with Stephen Colbert*. He had a cameo in *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md)* as one of the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Laketown_Spies&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Laketown Spies (page does not exist)">Laketown Spies</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Tolkien scholar [John D. Rateliff](../J/John_D._Rateliff.md) has suggested that Colbert has offered a formerly unrecorded piece of scholarly insight into *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, by the latter's suggestion that "*[Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) knew [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) would fail in his quest to destroy the [Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md), because he'd seen with his own eyes that Frodo could not throw the ring into his own fire at [Bag-End](../B/Bag-End.md)*".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
