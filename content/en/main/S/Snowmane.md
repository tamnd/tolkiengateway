**Snowmane** was the [steed](/wiki/Horses "Horses") of [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden), [King of Rohan](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan"). His sire was [Lightfoot](/wiki/Lightfoot)<sup id="cite_ref-v6_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-v6-1">[1]</a></sup> and was possibly one of the [Mearas](/wiki/Mearas).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Théoden rode with him to the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields). The steed was killed when struck with a black dart by the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king), and he fell on the King, mortally wounding him.<sup id="cite_ref-v6_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-v6-1">[1]</a></sup>

Snowmane, also referred to as *Théoden's Bane* and *Master's Bane*, was buried in the hollow where he fell which became known as [Snowmane's Howe](/wiki/Snowmane%27s_Howe).<sup id="cite_ref-v6_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-v6-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Snowmane* is the Modern English form of [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) (which represents [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese)) *Snāw-mana*. The "proper" Rohanese name was thus *Snawmana*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<b>1980<i>: </i><a href="/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)">The Return of the King</a><i>:</i></b><i></i>

<dl><dd>Snowmane is startled by a black cloud coming from <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a>, triggered by <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a>&#39;s use of <a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring" title="The One Ring">The One Ring</a>, and bucks <a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden" title="Théoden">Théoden</a> off his back. Théoden dies from impact with the ground.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden" title="Théoden">Théoden</a> rides Snowmane to <a href="/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep" title="Helm&#39;s Deep">Helm&#39;s Deep</a>. Théoden does not ride Snowmane during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg" title="Battle of the Hornburg">Battle of the Hornburg</a> and instead rides a different, brown horse.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden" title="Théoden">Théoden</a> rides Snowmane during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>. He gives his speech and charges into the battle on him. During the battle, the <a href="/wiki/Witch-king" title="Witch-king">Witch-king</a>&#39;s <a href="/wiki/Fell_beasts" title="Fell beasts">Fell Beast</a> bites Snowmane and throws him away with <a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden" title="Théoden">Théoden</a> still on his back, crushing him. Snowmane lies inanimate, presumably dead.</dd></dl>

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Snowmane accompanied Théoden to the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg" title="Battle of the Hornburg">Battle of the Hornburg</a>, and was ridden on the final charge out of the fortress. He fell on the <a href="/wiki/Pelennor_Fields" title="Pelennor Fields">Pelennor Fields</a> much as described in the book.</dd></dl>
