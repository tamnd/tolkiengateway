**Saradas Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Saradas was the third child and second son of [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](../G/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck.md), and had one son, [Seredic](Seredic_Brandybuck.md). He was present at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Saradas* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](../C/Celtic.md) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](../J/Jim_Allan.md) suggests that the name *Saradas* is a variation of the name *[Saradoc](Saradoc_Brandybuck.md)* with an *-as* ending like in *Marmadas* and *Merimas*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
