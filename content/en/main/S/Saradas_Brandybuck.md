**Saradas Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Saradas was the third child and second son of [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck), and had one son, [Seredic](/wiki/Seredic_Brandybuck "Seredic Brandybuck"). He was present at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Saradas* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) suggests that the name *Saradas* is a variation of the name *[Saradoc](/wiki/Saradoc_Brandybuck "Saradoc Brandybuck")* with an *-as* ending like in *Marmadas* and *Merimas*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
