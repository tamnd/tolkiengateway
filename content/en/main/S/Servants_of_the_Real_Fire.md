In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), the **Servants of the Real Fire** were a cult active in the [Dominions of the Seven](/wiki/Dominions_of_the_Seven) and surrounding regions, though they were most prominent in Pel.<ref name="beliefs">{{ICE|3900}}, pp. 25-26

## Beliefs

Rejecting the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and the [Music of the Ainur](/wiki/Music_of_the_Ainur), they instead believed that [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Ilúvatar) (Alûva) and [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) (Malkôra) were two deities of equal power fighting for control of [Arda](/wiki/Arda).

While Ilúvatar was in power in the Beginning Days

> Reference: Before recorded time

, Morgoth ruled during the Middle Days

> Reference: The First and Second Ages

. The Third Age was seen as the Later Days to the cult, during which the two deities were in their final battle.

The cult consisted of two Orders, the Golden Flame and Blue Flame, emphasizing Ilúvatar and Morgoth respectively. Depending on their rank, adherents showed their allegiance through tattoos or a bronze choker depicting the two flames, with the flame of their specific order being upright.

> Reference: == History==

Its core scriptures were first unveiled in {{TA|1449}} by Arcastur of Pel, the cult's first prophet. <ref name="timeline"/>

Between {{TA|2850}} and {{TA|2890}}, they took control of Mag and [Tumag](/wiki/Tumag).<ref>{{ICE|3900}}, p. 10
