The **Stock Road** was a road in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

It begun from the village of [Stock](Stock.md), passed through the [Woody End](../W/Woody_End.md) and the [Green Hills](../G/Green_Hills.md).

At the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) ([22 September](../_/22_September.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3021](../T/Third_Age_3021.md)), [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise Gamgee](Samwise_Gamgee.md) met the [Keepers of the Three Rings](../K/Keepers_of_the_Three_Rings.md) in Woody End, waiting on the Stock Road.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Identification {#Identification}

The road is not marked on the map *[A Part of the Shire](../A/A_Part_of_the_Shire.md)*. However there is an unnamed road joining [Tuckborough](../T/Tuckborough.md) with Stock through Green Hill Country and Woody End. [Karen Fonstad](../K/Karen_Fonstad.md) identifies this road as the Stock Road.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

According to [Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md), the Stock Road joined the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) west of [Bywater](../B/Bywater.md) but no such road does so on said map.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
