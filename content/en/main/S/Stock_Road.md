The **Stock Road** was a road in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

It begun from the village of [Stock](/wiki/Stock), passed through the [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End) and the [Green Hills](/wiki/Green_Hills).

At the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) ([22 September](/wiki/22_September), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021")), [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) met the [Keepers of the Three Rings](/wiki/Keepers_of_the_Three_Rings) in Woody End, waiting on the Stock Road.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Identification {#Identification}

The road is not marked on the map *[A Part of the Shire](/wiki/A_Part_of_the_Shire)*. However there is an unnamed road joining [Tuckborough](/wiki/Tuckborough) with Stock through Green Hill Country and Woody End. [Karen Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Fonstad) identifies this road as the Stock Road.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

According to [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster), the Stock Road joined the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) west of [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater) but no such road does so on said map.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
