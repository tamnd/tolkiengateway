**Sylvester McCoy** (b. [August 20](/wiki/20_August "20 August"), [1943](/wiki/1943) as **Percy James Patrick Kent-Smith**) is a Scottish actor. He is best known for playing the seventh incarnation of The Doctor in the *Doctor Who* series. He portrayed [Radagast](/wiki/Radagast) in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").
