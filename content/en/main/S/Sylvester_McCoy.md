**Sylvester McCoy** (b. [August 20](../_/20_August.md), [1943](../_/1943.md) as **Percy James Patrick Kent-Smith**) is a Scottish actor. He is best known for playing the seventh incarnation of The Doctor in the *Doctor Who* series. He portrayed [Radagast](../R/Radagast.md) in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).
