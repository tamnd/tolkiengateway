**Siril** was the greatest of the few rivers on the island of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It rose in the valley of [Noirinan](/wiki/Noirinan) beneath [Meneltarma](/wiki/Meneltarma), and flowed directly southwards to enter the sea at the fishing port of [Nindamos](/wiki/Nindamos), on the borders of the [Hyarnustar](/wiki/Hyarnustar) and the [Hyarrostar](/wiki/Hyarrostar). In its lower course it became a slow and winding stream, meeting the sea among wide marshes and reedy flats.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *siril* means "rivulet".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>
